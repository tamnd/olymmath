Let $n>0$ be an integer. Each of the $n$ girls in a group is the only one who knows a certain piece of gossip. To share their information, they call each other two at a time, but each time, only one speaks and the other just listens to all the information her friend tells her.

Determine the minimum number of phone calls sufficient for each girl to know all the gossip.

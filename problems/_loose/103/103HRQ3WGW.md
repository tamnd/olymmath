Let $P$ be a polynomial of degree $n$ such that for all $k \in\{0,1, \cdots, n\}$, the $k$-th derivative $P^{(k)}$ has as many roots as its degree $\left(P^{(0)}=P \text{ and } P^{(1)}=P', \text{ etc.}\right)$. Let $S_{k}$ be the sum of the roots of $P^{(k)}$. Show that $\left(S_{k}\right)_{0 \leqslant k \leqslant n}$ is an arithmetic sequence.

Let the lengths of the three sides of a triangle be $a, b, c$, the medians to these sides be $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}$, and the diameter of the circumcircle be $D$. Prove: $\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}}{m_{c}}+\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}}{m_{a}}+\frac{c^{2}+a^{2}}{m_{b}} \leqslant 6 D$.

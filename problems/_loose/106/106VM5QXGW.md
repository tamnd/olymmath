Let $P$ be an odd prime. Consider the set $\{1,2, \cdots, 2 p\}$ and its subsets $A$ that satisfy the following two conditions:
(1) $|A|=p$;
(2) The sum of all elements in $A$ is divisible by $p$. Find the number of all such subsets $A$.

Let $S$ be the area of a triangle, $p$ its semiperimeter, $r$ the radius of its inscribed circle and $a, b, c$ its sides. Prove that:

a) $3 \sqrt{3} r^{2} \leq S \leq \frac{p^{2}}{3 \sqrt{3}}$

b) $S \leq \frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{4 \sqrt{3}}$.

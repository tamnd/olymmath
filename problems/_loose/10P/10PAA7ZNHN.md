Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $H_{1}, H_{2}$, and $H_{3}$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B$, and $C$ respectively. The incircle of $ABC$ touches $(BC), (CA), (AB)$ at $T_{1}, T_{2}$, and $T_{3}$ respectively. Let $l_{1}, l_{2}$, and $l_{3}$ be the lines symmetric to $\left(H_{2} H_{3}\right), H_{3} H_{1}, H_{1} H_{2}$ with respect to $\left(T_{2} T_{3}\right), \left(T_{3} T_{1}\right)$, and $T_{1} T_{2}$ respectively. Show that the lines $l_{1}, l_{2}, l_{3}$ determine a triangle whose vertices lie on the incircle of $ABC$.

Each of the 10 people is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. Each of them thought of some natural number. Then the first said: "My number is greater than 1", the second said: "My number is greater than 2", ..., the tenth said: "My number is greater than 10". After that, they, in a different order, said (each one phrase): "My number is less than 1", "My number is less than 2", ..., "My number is less than 10". What is the maximum number of knights that could have been among these 10 people? (O. Podlipsky)

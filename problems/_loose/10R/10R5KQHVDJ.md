17-105 Let the lengths of the sides of $\triangle ABC$ be $a, b, c$, and $c \leqslant b \leqslant a$. Through an interior point $P$, the lines connecting $P$ to $A, B, C$ intersect the opposite sides at $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, respectively. Let
$$
s=A A^{\prime}+B B^{\prime}+C C^{\prime},
$$

then for all positions of $P$, $s$ is less than
(A) $2 a+b$.
(B) $2 a+c$.
(C) $2 b+c$.
(D) $a+2 b$.
(E) $a+b+c$.

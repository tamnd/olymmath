In the triangle $ABC$, the circle circumscribed around the triangle is intersected for the second time by the angle bisector of the angle at $A$ at point $D$, and by the external angle bisector of the angle at $A$ at point $D_{1}$. The projection of $D$ onto the line $AB$ is $E$, and the projection of $D_{1}$ onto the line $AB$ is $E_{1}$. Prove that $AE$ is equal to half the sum of the lengths of sides $AB$ and $AC$, and $AE_{1}$ is equal to half the absolute value of the difference of the lengths of sides $AB$ and $AC$.

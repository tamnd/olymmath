The circumcircle of $\triangle BOC$ intersects lines $AC$ and $AB$ for the second time at $D$ and $E$ respectively. Suppose that $AQ$ , $BC$ , and $DE$ are concurrent. If $OD=3$ and $OE=7$ , compute $AQ$ .

On 19 cards, the numbers $15,16,17, \ldots, 33$ are written respectively (one number per card). Members of the math club Vasya, Petya, and Misha decided to divide all these cards among themselves so that each of them gets at least one card and no one ends up with a pair of cards where the difference between the numbers is odd. How many ways are there to distribute the cards?

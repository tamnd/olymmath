It is known that there is an aviation service between two countries, and there is exactly 1 one-way flight between any two cities belonging to these two countries, but each city has flights to some cities in the other country. Try to prove that from this, 4 cities $A, B, C, D$ can be found such that it is possible to fly directly from $A$ to $B$, from $B$ to $C$, from $C$ to $D$, and from $D$ to $A$.

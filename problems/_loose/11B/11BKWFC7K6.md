Given a triangle $A B C$ and some point $T$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $T$ to the lines $A B$ and $A C$ respectively, and let $R$ and $S$ be the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $A$ to the lines $T C$ and $T B$ respectively. Prove that the intersection point $X$ of the lines $P R$ and $Q S$ lies on the line $B C$.

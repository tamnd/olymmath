The students Eva, Renate, Monika, Ingrid, Jürgen, Hans, and Gerd have lined up in order of their height.

The tallest stands at the front, and of two equally tall students, the one whose first name starts with a letter that comes earlier in the alphabet stands in front of the other. The following is known:

(1) It is true that Ingrid is 2 cm shorter than Monika.

(2) It is false that Eva does not have the same height as Gerd.

(3) It is not true that none of these students is shorter than Hans.

(4) It is true that Jürgen is shorter than Ingrid but taller than Hans.

(5) It is false that Hans is taller than Monika.

(6) It is not false that Monika is 2 cm taller than Gerd and also taller than Jürgen.

It is to be determined:

a) Which students are of the same height?

b) What is the order of the first names in which the students have lined up? (Start with the tallest student.)

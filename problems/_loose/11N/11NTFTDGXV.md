For positive real numbers $p$ and $q$, it is known that each of the equations $x^{2} + p x + q = 0$ and $x^{2} + q x + p = 0$ has two distinct real roots. Prove that the equation $x^{2} + (p+q) x + 2(p+q) = 0$ also has two distinct real roots.

Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{m}=1(m>0)$ passes through the point $A\left(1, \frac{3}{2}\right)$. Can we find two points $E, F$ on the ellipse $C$ such that: the slopes of the three sides of $\triangle A E F$ form an arithmetic sequence, and the lines containing sides $A E$ and $A F$ are both tangent to the circle $(x-2)^{2}+\left(y-\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2}=r^{2}(r>0)$? If they exist, find the corresponding value of $r$; if not, explain the reason.

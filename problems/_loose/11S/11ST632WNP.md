Given the polynomial $A(x)=1+3 x+5 x^{2}+7 x^{3}+\ldots+199 x^{99}$. Is it possible, by rearranging the coefficients in it, to obtain a polynomial $B(x)=b_{0}+b_{1} x+b_{2} x^{2}+b_{3} x^{3}+\ldots+b_{99} x^{99}$ such that for all natural numbers $k \geqslant 2$ the difference $A(k)-B(k)$ is not divisible by 199?

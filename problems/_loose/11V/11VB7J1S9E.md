Prove that the following numbers are irrational:

a) $\sqrt{p}$, where $p$ is a prime number;

b) $\sqrt{p_{1} \ldots p_{k}}$, where $p_{1}, \ldots, p_{k}$ are distinct prime numbers;

c) $\sqrt{\frac{p_{1} \ldots p_{k}}{p_{k+1} \ldots p_{n}}}$, where $p_{1}, \ldots, p_{n}$ are distinct prime numbers.

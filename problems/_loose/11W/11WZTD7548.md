In a sequence of positive integers, an inversion is a pair of positions where the element in the leftmost position is greater than the element in the rightmost position. For example, the sequence 2,5,3,1,3 has 5 inversions: between the first and the fourth position, between the second and all subsequent positions, and finally, between the third and the fourth. Among all sequences of positive integers whose sum of elements is $n$, what is the maximum number of inversions if

a) $n=7$?

b) $n=2019$?

Note: The sequences of positive integers considered in this problem can have more than 5 elements.

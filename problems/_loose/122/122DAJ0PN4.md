The tennis association assigns numbers to tennis players based on their skill levels: the strongest player is ranked No. 1, the next is No. 2, and so on. Suppose that when two players whose number difference is greater than 2 compete, the player with the smaller number always wins. Now, the 1024 strongest players are participating in a singles tournament. The tournament is conducted on an elimination basis: participants are divided into pairs according to the draw results, and the two players in each pair play one match, with the winner advancing to the next round. Thus, after each round of matches, the number of players is halved, and after 10 rounds of matches, a champion is produced. What is the maximum possible number of the champion?

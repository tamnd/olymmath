Let $n \equiv 1(\bmod 4)$ and $n>1, P=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}$ be any permutation of $\{1,2,3, \cdots, n\}$, and $k_{p}$ denote the largest index $k$ such that the following inequality holds,
$$
a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{k}<a_{k+1}+a_{k+2}+\cdots+a_{n} \text {. }
$$

Find the sum of $k_{p}$ for all possible different permutations $P$.

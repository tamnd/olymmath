3 Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute triangle, and $P, Q$ be points on side $B C$. Take point $C_{1}$ such that the convex quadrilateral $A P B C_{1}$ has a circumcircle, $Q C_{1} / / C A$, and $C_{1}$ is on the opposite side of line $A B$ from $Q$. Take point $B_{1}$ such that the convex quadrilateral $A P C B_{1}$ has a circumcircle, $Q B_{1} / / B A$, and $B_{1}$ is on the opposite side of line $A C$ from $Q$. Prove: $B_{1}, C_{1}, P, Q$ are concyclic.

Consider a sequence of words , consisting of the letters $A$ and $B$ .
The first word in the sequence is " $A$ " . The k-th word i s obtained from the $(k-1)$ -th by means of the following transformation : each $A$ is substituted by $AAB$ , and each $B$ is substituted by $A$ . It is easily seen that every word is an initial part of the next word. The initial parts of these words coincide to give a sequence of letters $AABAABAAA BAABAAB...$ (a) In which place of this sequence is the $1000$ -th letter $A$ ?
(b ) Prove that this sequence is not periodic.

(V . Galperin , Moscows)

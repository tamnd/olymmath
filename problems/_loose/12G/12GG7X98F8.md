A square chessboard-like table consists of 15 by 15 fields. One of the two diagonals of the square is called $d$. In each of the 225 fields of the table, one of the numbers 1 to 15 can be entered such that the following requirements (1) and (2) are met:

(1) Each (horizontal) row contains each of the 15 numbers exactly once.

(2) For any two fields that are symmetric with respect to $d$, the numbers in these fields are equal.

For each entry, the sum of the numbers in the 15 fields that are crossed by the diagonal $d$ can be formed.

Prove that this sum is uniquely determined by the conditions (1) and (2), and determine this sum!

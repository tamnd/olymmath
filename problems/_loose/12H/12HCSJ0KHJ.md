a) The measures of the angles $A, B, C, D$ of the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ are directly proportional to four consecutive natural numbers. Prove that $ABCD$ is a trapezoid.

b) Let $O$ be the intersection point of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of the trapezoid $ABCD, AB \| CD, AB > CD$. Through the point $O$ we draw the line $OM$ parallel to the side $AD$, with $M \in AB$, and denote by $N$ the symmetric point of $M$ with respect to the midpoint of the side $AB$. Prove that $ON \| BC$.

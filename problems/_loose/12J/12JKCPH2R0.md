In the equation

$$
x^{2022}-2 x^{2021}-3 x^{2020}-\ldots-2022 x-2023=0
$$

the coefficients of all powers of $x$, except the highest, can be permuted in any way. Can such a permutation ensure that the equation has at least two positive roots?

Find the minimum value of the constant $\lambda$ such that for all positive integers $n$ and any positive real numbers $x_{k}(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$ whose sum is 1, we have
$$
\lambda \prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1-x_{k}\right) \geqslant 1-\sum_{k=1}^{n} x_{k}^{2} .
$$

Given a circle $\odot O$ and a point $P$ not on $\odot O$, two moving lines $l$ and $l^{\prime}$ through $P$ intersect $\odot O$ at points $X$ and $Y$, $X^{\prime}$ and $Y^{\prime}$ respectively. Prove that the line connecting the centers of the circumcircles of $\triangle P X Y^{\prime}$ and $\triangle P X^{\prime} Y$ passes through a fixed point. ${ }^{[2]}$
(2013, Romanian National Team Selection Exam)

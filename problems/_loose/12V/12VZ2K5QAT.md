Taking any vertex of a tetrahedron as the vertex, and any median triangle (a triangle with one side being the line segment connecting the midpoints of a pair of edges, and another vertex being the midpoint of an edge) as the base, all such tetrahedra have equal volumes, and each is equal to one-eighth of the volume of the original tetrahedron.

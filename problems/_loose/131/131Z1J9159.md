Let positive real numbers $u, v, w$ satisfy $u+v+w+\sqrt{u \tau w}=4$.
Prove: $\sqrt{\frac{v w}{u}}+\sqrt{\frac{u w}{v}}+\sqrt{\frac{u v}{w}} \geqslant u+v+w$.

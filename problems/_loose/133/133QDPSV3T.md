Let's prove that if $a, b, c, d$ are real numbers, then

$$
\begin{gathered}
(a+c)^{2}(b+d)^{2} \geq 2\left(a b^{2} c+b c^{2} d+c d^{2} a+d a^{2} b+4 a b c d\right) \\
(a+c)^{2}(b+d)^{2} \geq 4 b c(c d+d a+a b)
\end{gathered}
$$

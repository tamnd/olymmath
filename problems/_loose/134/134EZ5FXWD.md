$$
\begin{array}{ll}
13 \cdot 15 \text { If } a>1, \text { and let } & \\
p=\sqrt{1993 a}-\sqrt{1993 a-1}, & q=\sqrt{1993 a-1}-\sqrt{1993 a}, \\
r=\sqrt{1993 a}-\sqrt{1993 a+1}, & s=\sqrt{1993 a+1}-\sqrt{1993 a} .
\end{array}
$$

Then the smallest value among $p, q, r, s$ is
(A) $p$.
(B) $q$.
(C) $r$.
(D) $s$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1993)

Prove the following Ramanujan identities:
a) $\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{2}-1}=\sqrt[3]{\frac{1}{9}}-\sqrt[3]{\frac{2}{9}}+\sqrt[3]{\frac{4}{9}}$

b) $\sqrt{\sqrt[3]{5}-\sqrt[3]{4}}=\frac{1}{3}(\sqrt[3]{2}+\sqrt[3]{20}-\sqrt[3]{25})$;

c) $\sqrt[6]{7 \sqrt[3]{20}-19}=\sqrt[3]{\frac{5}{3}}-\sqrt[3]{\frac{2}{3}}$

d) $\sqrt[4]{\frac{3+2 \sqrt[4]{5}}{3-2 \sqrt[4]{5}}}=\frac{\sqrt[4]{5}+1}{\sqrt[4]{5}-1}$

e) $\sqrt{\sqrt[3]{28}-\sqrt[3]{27}}=\frac{1}{3}(\sqrt[3]{98}-\sqrt[3]{28}-1)$;

f) $\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[5]{\frac{32}{5}}-\sqrt[5]{\frac{27}{5}}}=\sqrt[5]{\frac{1}{25}}+\sqrt[5]{\frac{3}{25}}-\sqrt[5]{\frac{9}{25}}$

a) Prove that the quadrilateral $AKZL$ is an isosceles trapezoid
b) Prove that $2S_{ABKZ}=S_{ABCD}$ c) If quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a square of side $a$ , calculate the area of the isosceles trapezoid $AKZL$ in terms of side $BC=a$

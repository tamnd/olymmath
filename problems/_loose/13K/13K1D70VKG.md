The remainder of $ n $ modulo $ p $ is denoted by $ (n)_p $. Let $ p $ and $ q $ be distinct odd primes, and let $ a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_q $ be natural numbers. Prove that there exists a natural number $ m $ with $ (m, p) = 1 $ and indices $ i \neq j $ such that:
$$
(ma_i)_p \equiv (ma_j)_p \pmod{q}.
$$

In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AB < AC$, $O$ and $G$ are the circumcenter and centroid of $\triangle ABC$ respectively, and $D$ is the midpoint of side $BC$. Point $E$ lies on the circle with diameter $BC$, such that $AE \perp BC$, and $A$ and $E$ are on the same side of $BC$. Extend $EG$ to intersect $OD$ at point $F$. Draw lines through $F$ parallel to $OB$ and $OC$, intersecting $BC$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. Let $\Gamma$ be the circle passing through points $B$ and $C$ and tangent to $OB$ and $OC$. It is known that there exist points $M$ and $N$ on segments $AB$ and $AC$ respectively, such that $MK \perp BC$ and $NL \perp BC$. Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle AMN$ is tangent to circle $\Gamma$.

Let the three sides of $\triangle A B C$ be $a, b, c$, and let point $P$ be any point on the plane of $\triangle A B C$. Prove:
$$
\frac{P B \cdot P C}{b c}+\frac{P C \cdot P A}{c a}+\frac{P A \cdot P B}{a b} \geqslant 1 .
$$

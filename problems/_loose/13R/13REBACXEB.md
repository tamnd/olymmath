Quadratic trinomials $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ are such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& f(2) \\
& g(2)
\end{aligned}=\begin{aligned}
& f(3) \\
& g(3)
\end{aligned}=2
$$

Find $f(1)$, given that $g(1)=2, f(5)=7$ and $g(5)=2$.

Let $P, Q, R$ be points on the edges $AC, AD, AB$ or their extensions of tetrahedron $ABCD$, and let $E, F$ be points on $BC$ such that $BE = EF = FC$. The lines $AE, AF$ intersect $RP$ at points $G, H$ respectively. Let the volumes of tetrahedra $APQR$ and $AGHQ$ be denoted by $V, V_{1}$ respectively. Prove that $V \geqslant 3 V_{1}$, and the equality holds if and only if $RP \parallel BC$ or $RP$ coincides with $BC$.

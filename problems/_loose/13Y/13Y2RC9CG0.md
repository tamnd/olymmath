Find the largest number $m$ such that for any positive numbers $a, b$, and $c$, the sum of which is 1, the inequality

$$
\sqrt{\frac{a b}{c+a b}}+\sqrt{\frac{b c}{a+b c}}+\sqrt{\frac{c a}{b+c a}} \geqslant m
$$

holds.

(l. Emelyanov)

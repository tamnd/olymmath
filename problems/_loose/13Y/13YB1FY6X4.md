Given that $F$ is a focus, $P, Q$ are two moving points on a conic section, and $\angle P F Q=2 \delta$ (where $\delta$ is an acute angle). Prove: ( I ) The locus of the intersection point of the lines through points $P, Q$ is a conic section with $F$ as a focus; (III) the line $P Q$ is always tangent to a conic section with $F$ as a focus.

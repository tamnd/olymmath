Let's draw external normals $a', b', c'$ from the vertex $S$ of the trihedral angle $Sabc$ to the faces $bSc, cSa,$ and $aSb$ respectively: we obtain a new trihedral angle $Sa'b'c'$, which is called the polar angle relative to the angle $Sabc$. Prove: a) the trihedral angle polar to $Sa'b'c'$ is the original angle $Sabc$; b) the measure of the plane angle $aSb$ of the given trihedral angle, together with the measure of the dihedral angle at the edge $Sc'$ in the polar angle, sums to $180^\circ$.

A glazier has a piece of glass in the shape of a triangle. Using his knowledge of geometry, he knows that he could cut a circle of radius $r$ from it. Prove that, for any natural number $n$, from the triangular piece he can obtain $n^{2}$ circles of radius $\frac{r}{n}$ (assuming that perfect cuts can always be made).

Prove the existence of points symmetric to each other relative to a given plane: if a plane $\alpha$ and a point $A$ outside it are given, then there exists a unique point $A^{\prime}$ on the other side of the plane $\alpha$ that satisfies the conditions: a) $\left(A A^{\prime}\right) \perp \alpha$; b) $\left(A A^{\prime}\right) \cap \alpha=S$ and at the same time $\overrightarrow{S A^{\prime}}=-\overrightarrow{S A}$. Prove that the plane $\alpha$ is the set of points equidistant from points $A$ and $A^{\prime}$ [The mapping of space in which each point $A$ is mapped to the point $A^{\prime}$ in the described manner is called symmetry relative to the plane $\alpha$ (or reflection in the plane $\alpha$)].

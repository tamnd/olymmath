Provide all quadruples $\left(z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4}\right)$ of two-digit numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4}$ that have the following properties. For each quadruple:

(1) $z_{1} \cdot z_{2}=z_{3} \cdot z_{4}$,

(2) $z_{3}$ is obtained by reading $z_{1}$ backwards,

(3) $z_{4}$ is obtained by reading $z_{2}$ backwards, (Example $24 \cdot 63=42 \cdot 36$ )

(4) Among the four digits of $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$, there are no two that are the same,

(5) $z_{1}$ is the smallest of the four numbers.

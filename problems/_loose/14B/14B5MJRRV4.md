Júlia is training for math olympiads. One day she decides to divide 2014 by each of the positive integer divisors of 2015. For each division, she writes the quotient in her notebook and the remainder on a blackboard.

(a) Let's help Júlia. Write down the eight positive integer divisors of 2015.

(b) For each divisor, perform the division and write a list of the quotients and another list of the remainders obtained.

(c) When she finishes, Júlia notices a great "coincidence": the numbers written in the notebook were the same as those on the blackboard, just written in a different order. Is it a coincidence? Show that, for any number $ n $ Júlia chooses, if she calculates the quotient and the remainder of the division of $ n-1 $ by each of the divisors of $ n $, the numbers in the notebook and on the blackboard will be exactly the same, possibly just written in a different order.

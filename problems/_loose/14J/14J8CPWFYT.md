Two regular 10-sided polygons are given, with a natural number written at each vertex of each polygon.

It is known that the sum of the numbers on each 10-sided polygon is 99. Prove that on both polygons, it is possible to mark several consecutive vertices (but not all) such that the sums of the marked numbers are the same.

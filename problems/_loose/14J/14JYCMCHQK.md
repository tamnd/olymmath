For positive integers $m$ and $n$ we define $T(m,n) = gcd \left(m, \frac{n}{gcd(m,n)} \right)$ (a) Prove that there are infinitely many pairs $(m,n)$ of positive integers for which $T(m,n) > 1$ and $T(n,m) > 1$ .
(b) Do there exist positive integers $m,n$ such that $T(m,n) = T(n,m) > 1$ ?

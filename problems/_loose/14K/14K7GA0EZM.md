Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \cdots$ be a sequence of positive real numbers, satisfying the condition $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} \geqslant \sqrt{n}$ for all $n \geqslant 1$.

Prove: For all $n \geqslant 1, \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}^{2}>\frac{1}{4}$ $\left(1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}\right)$.

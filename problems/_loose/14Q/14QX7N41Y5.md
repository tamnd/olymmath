Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be a continuous function. Define a sequence of functions $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ in the following way:
$$f_0(x)=f(x), f_{n+1}(x)=\int^x_0f_n(t)\text dt, n=0,1,2,\ldots.$$Prove that if $f_n(1)=0$ for all $n$, then $f(x)\equiv0$.

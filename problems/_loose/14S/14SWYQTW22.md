$x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ is a sequence, with the general term $x_{n}=\frac{n}{n+a}$, where $a$ is a natural number. Try to prove: for a natural number $n$, the term $x_{n}$ of the sequence can always be expressed as the product of two other terms.
(1986 Federal Republic of Germany Competition Problem)

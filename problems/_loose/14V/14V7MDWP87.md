In the interior of parallelogram $ABCD$, let us draw lines through point $P$ parallel to the sides of the parallelogram, and let these lines intersect the sides $AB, BC, CD$, and $DA$ of the parallelogram at points $Q, R, S$, and $T$ respectively. Reflect point $P$ over the center of parallelogram $ABCD$ and denote the reflection of $P$ by $P'$. Prove that the area of the resulting parallelogram $APCP'$ is equal to the difference in the areas of parallelograms $PRBQ$ and $PSDT$.

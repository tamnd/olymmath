The quadratic functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ are with real coefficients and have the following property: if the number $g(x)$ is integer for a positive $x$, then the number $f(x)$ is integer too. Prove that there are such integers $m$ and $n$, that $f(x)=m g(x)+n$ for all real $x$.

Ana and Bobi are traveling to an archipelago with 2009 islands. Some of the islands are connected by two-way ferry routes. During the trip, Ana and Bobi play the following game. Ana chooses the first island where the helicopter will land. The journey continues by water, with Bobi and Ana alternately choosing the next (unvisited so far) island in the route (now Bobi is first). Any two consecutive islands in the route must be connected by a ferry route. Whoever cannot choose the next island loses. Prove that in this game Ana has a winning strategy.

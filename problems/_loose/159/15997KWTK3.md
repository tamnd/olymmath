Let $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{2 n+3}$ be $2 n+3$ points on a plane, where no three points are collinear and no four points are concyclic. A circle is drawn through any three points such that the remaining $2 n$ points are equally divided into $n$ points inside and $n$ points outside the circle. The number of such circles is denoted as $K$. Prove that
$$
K>\frac{1}{\pi} C_{2 n+3}^{2} .
$$

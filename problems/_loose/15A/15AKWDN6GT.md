A line not perpendicular to the $x$-axis intersects the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ at points $A, B$ ($A, B$ are not in the same quadrant). The directrix of the parabola intersects the $x$-axis at point $N$. It is known that $\angle A N B$ is bisected by the $x$-axis. Prove that the line segment $A B$ passes through the focus of the parabola.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $K$ a point on side $AC$, different from $A$ and $C$, $M$ a point on side $BC$, different from $B$ and $C$, and let $X$ be the intersection point of $KB$ and $MA$. Prove that it is not possible for the equalities $\overline{KX}=\overline{XB}$ and $\overline{MX}=\overline{XA}$ to both hold.

${ }^{+}$ Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be graph invariants that take positive integer values. Restate the following two statements in propositional form and prove that they are equivalent:
(i) $\alpha$ has a function of $\beta$ as an upper bound;
(ii) When $\alpha$ is sufficiently large, $\beta$ is forced to increase.
Prove that the following proposition is not equivalent to (i) and (ii), and suggest a small modification to make it equivalent to (i) and (ii):
(iii) $\beta$ has a function of $\alpha$ as a lower bound.

In a $13 \cdot 3$ space, there is a convex polyhedron, all of whose vertices are located at integer points (each vertex has three integer coordinates). Additionally, there are no other integer points inside the polyhedron, on its faces, or on its edges.
Prove that the number of vertices of the polyhedron does not exceed 8.
(40th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1977)

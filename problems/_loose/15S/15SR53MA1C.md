Triangle $A B C$ with $A B=22, B C=19, C A=13$ is given.

a) If $M$ is the center of gravity of $\triangle A B C$, prove that $A M^{2}+C M^{2}=B M^{2}$.

b) Find the locus of points $P$ from the plane of $\triangle A B C$, for which $A P^{2}+C P^{2}=B P^{2}$.

c) Find the minimal and maximal values of $B P$, if $A P^{2}+C P^{2}=B P^{2}$.

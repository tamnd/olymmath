Let $m$ be a positive integer. The positive integer $a$ is called a *golden residue* modulo $m$ if $\gcd(a,m)=1$ and $x^x \equiv a \pmod m$ has a solution for $x$ . Given a positive integer $n$ , suppose that $a$ is a golden residue modulo $n^n$ . Show that $a$ is also a golden residue modulo $n^{n^n}$ .

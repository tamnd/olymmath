Cubes. As is known, the entire space can be filled with equal cubes. At each vertex, eight cubes will meet. Therefore, it is possible - by appropriately cutting off the vertices of the cubes and gluing the adjacent cut parts into one body - to fill the space with regular octahedra (eight-faced bodies) and the bodies remaining from the cubes. What will these bodies be? If we maximally increase the octahedra, what part of the space will they occupy?

Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle in which $A B = A C$. On the circumcircle of this triangle, take a point $D$ on the smallest arc joining $A$ to $B$; finally, take a point $E$ on the line $(A D)$, outside the segment $[A D]$, such that points $A$ and $E$ are in the same half-plane limited by the line $(B C)$. The circumcircle of triangle $B D E$ intersects the line $(A B)$ at $F$; show that the lines $(E F)$ and $(B C)$ are parallel.

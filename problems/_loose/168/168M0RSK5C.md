How many polynomials $p(x)$ with real coefficients, of degree between 1 and 2020 (inclusive), exist such that there is a real number $\alpha$ for which the equation $p(x)^{2}=p\left(x^{2}\right)+\alpha p(x)$ holds for every real number $x$?
(a) Suppose that $n=k^{2}$ is a perfect square. Prove that the number of positive divisors of $n$ strictly less than $k$ is equal to the number of divisors of $n$ strictly greater than $k$.

(b) Suppose that $n=k^{2}$ is a perfect square. Prove that $n$ has at most $2 k-1$ positive divisors.

(c) Find all positive integers $k$ such that $k^{2}$ has exactly $2 k-1$ positive divisors.

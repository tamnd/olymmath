Given a convex hexagon with vertices $A, B, C, D, E$, and $F$. Let the centroid of triangle $ABC$ be $B_{1}$, the centroid of triangle $BCD$ be $C_{1}$, the centroid of triangle $CDE$ be $D_{1}$, the centroid of triangle $DEF$ be $E_{1}$, the centroid of triangle $EFA$ be $F_{1}$, and the centroid of triangle $FAB$ be $A_{1}$. Prove that the hexagon $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1} E_{1} F_{1}$ is centrally symmetric.

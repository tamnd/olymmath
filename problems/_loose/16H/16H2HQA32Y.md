At each vertex of a triangle, a non-negative number is written, and the sum of all these numbers is 3000. Each number is replaced by the arithmetic mean of the numbers at the adjacent vertices, and this operation is repeated 10 times. Prove that after this, each of the numbers will be less than 1002.

$103^{**}$. Given 1967 numbers

$$
x_{1}=0, x_{2}, x_{3}, \ldots, x_{1966}, x_{1967}=0
$$

such that

$$
x_{i}=\frac{x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}}{4}+1
$$

for any $i=2, \ldots, 1966$. Prove that
a) $0 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant 2$ for $i=0,1,2, \ldots, 1967$;

b) $x_{1}=x_{1967}, x_{2}=x_{1966}, x_{3}=x_{1965}, \ldots$;

c) the number $x_{984}$ is the largest among the numbers $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{1967}$ and $x_{1}<x_{2}<\cdots<x_{984}$.

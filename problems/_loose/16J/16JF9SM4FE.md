Given the quadratic equation $x^{2}+p x+q=0$, with roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$. Find the equation whose roots are: 1) $x_{1}+\frac{1}{x_{1}}$ and $x_{2}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}$, 2) $\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}$ and $\frac{x_{2}}{x_{1}}$, 3) $\frac{p}{2}$ more than the roots of the given equation.

Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle with centroid $K$. We draw a line $e$ through $K$ that intersects the lines $A B, B C, C A$ at points $C_{1}, A_{1}, B_{1}$, respectively, distinct from the vertices. Circles are drawn around these intersection points with radii $C_{1} C, A_{1} A, B_{1} B$, respectively. It is to be proven that there are two points in the plane through which all three circles pass. - What is the geometric locus of these common points as the line $e$ rotates around $K$?

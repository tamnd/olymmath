On a parking lot, there are cars. Among them, there are cars of the brands "Toyota", "Honda", "Skoda", as well as cars of other brands. It is known that the number of non-"Honda" cars is one and a half times the number of non-red cars; the number of non-"Skoda" cars is one and a half times the number of non-yellow cars; finally, the number of non-"Toyota" cars is half the number of red and yellow cars combined. Prove that the number of "Toyota" cars is not less than the number of "Honda" and "Skoda" cars together. (A. Solynin)

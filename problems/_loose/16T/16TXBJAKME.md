Given $\triangle A B C$ with circumcenter and incenter $O, I$ respectively, the incircle touches sides $B C, C A, A B$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively. Line $D E$ intersects $A B$ at point $Q$, and $D F$ intersects $A C$ at point $P$. The midpoints of segments $P E$ and $Q F$ are $M, N$ respectively. Prove: $M N \perp I O$.

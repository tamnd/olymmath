(A) $\frac{13}{18}$.
(B) $\frac{18}{13}$.
(C) $-\frac{13}{18}$.
(D) $-\frac{18}{13}$.
(Shenyang City, Liaoning Province, China "Yucai Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

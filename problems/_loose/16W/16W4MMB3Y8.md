Let the prime factorization of $n$ be $p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}} \ldots p_{t}^{\alpha_{t}}$. Let $r_{1}, r_{2}, \ldots, r_{m}$ denote the positive integers less than $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$. Show that

$$
r_{1}^{2}+r_{2}^{2}+\cdots+r_{m}^{2}=\frac{m}{3}\left(n^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(-1)^{t} p_{1} p_{2} \ldots p_{t}\right)
$$

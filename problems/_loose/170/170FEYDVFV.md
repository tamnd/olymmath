Given is $\triangle A B C$ in which the angle at vertex $A$ is obtuse and $A B \neq A C$. The angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects side $B C$ and the circumcircle at points $D$ and $E$, respectively. Let the circles with diameters $B C$ and $D E$ intersect at points $K$ and $L$. Prove that the point symmetric to point $A$ with respect to line $B C$ lies on line $K L$.

At the evening party, $n(\geqslant 2)$ pairs of young men and women dance together. Suppose that no man has danced with all the women, and each woman has danced with at least one man. Prove that there must be two men $b_{1}, b_{2}$ and two women $g_{1}, g_{2}$, such that $b_{1}$ has danced with $g_{1}$, $b_{2}$ has danced with $g_{2}$, but $b_{1}$ has not danced with $g_{2}$, and $b_{2}$ has not danced with $g_{1}$.

Determine all integers $n$ satisfying the following property: if $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ are integers whose sum is not divisible by $n$, then there exists a permutation $\left(b_{i}\right)_{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}$ of $\left(a_{i}\right)_{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}$ such that $n$ divides $b_{1}+2 b_{2}+\cdots+n b_{n}$.

If the value of the infinite sum
$$\frac{1}{2^2-1^2}+\frac{1}{4^2-2^2}+\frac{1}{8^2-4^2}+\frac{1}{16^2-8^2}+\dots.$$
can be expressed as $\frac{a}{b}$ for relatively prime positive integers $a,b,$ evaluate $a+b.$

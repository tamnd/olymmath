For positive numbers $x$ and $y$, it is known that

$$
\frac{1}{1+x+x^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+y+y^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+x+y}=1
$$

What values can the product $x y$ take? List all possible options and prove that there are no others.

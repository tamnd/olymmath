The grandfather of Baron K. F. I. von Münchhausen built a square castle, divided it into 9 square halls, and placed the arsenal in the central hall. The father of the baron divided each of the 8 remaining halls into 9 equal square foyers and arranged winter gardens in all the central foyers. The baron himself divided each of the 64 free foyers into 9 equal square rooms and arranged a pool in each of the central rooms, making the rest living rooms. The baron boasts that he managed to visit all the living rooms, being in each one only once, and returned to the starting point (a door was made in each wall between two adjacent living rooms). Could the baron's words be true?

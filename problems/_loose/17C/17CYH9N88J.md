Let $a, b, c$ be distinct natural numbers and let $r, s, t$ be natural numbers such that:

$$
a b+1=r^{2}, a c+1=s^{2}, b c+1=t^{2}
$$

Prove that not all three fractions $\frac{r s}{t}, \frac{r t}{s}, \frac{s t}{r}$ can be natural numbers.

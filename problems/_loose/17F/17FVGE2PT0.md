Prove that for all positive integers $a, b, c$ the following inequality holds:
$$\frac{a + b}{a + c}+\frac{b + c}{b + a}+\frac{c + a}{c + b} \le \frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{a}$$

Given a cube $A B C D-A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ with edge length $1$, and $E$ is the midpoint of $D_{1} C_{1}$, find the following:
(1) Find the distance between the skew lines $D_{1} B$ and $A_{1} E$;
(2) Find the distance from $B_{1}$ to the plane $A_{1} B E$;
(3) Find the distance from $D_{1} C$ to the plane $A_{1} B E$;
(4) Find the distance between the planes $A_{1} D B$ and $D_{1} C B_{1}$.

$A$ chess piece is placed on some square in an $n \times n(n \geqslant 2)$ square chessboard. It then makes alternately "straight" and "diagonal" moves. "Straight" means to a square having a common side with the original square. "Diagonal" means to a square which has exactly one point in common with the original square. Find all $n$ for which there exists a sequence of moves, starting by a "diagonal" move from the original square, such that the piece passes through all squares of the chessboard, and through each square exactly once.

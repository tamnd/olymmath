Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive numbers whose sum is 1, and let $n>2$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a_{2} a_{3} \ldots a_{n}}{a_{1}+n-2}+\frac{a_{1} a_{3} a_{4} \ldots a_{n}}{a_{2}+n-2}+\ldots+\frac{a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{n-1}}{a_{n}+n-2} \leq \frac{1}{(n-1)^{2}}
$$

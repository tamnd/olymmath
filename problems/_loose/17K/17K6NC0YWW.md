Consider a transformation $ f$ which maps the point $ (x,\ y)$ to $ (3x + y,\ - 2x)$ in the $ x - y$ plane. Denote the image of $ P$ as $ f(P)$. Define the lines $ l_0,\ l_1,\ \cdots$ by $ f$ as follows.

$ (a)$ $ l_0: 3x + 2y = 1$.

$ (b)$ Let $ l_{n + 1}$ be the line which $ f(P)$ draws when the point $ P$ moves on $ l_n$.

Denote $ l_n$ as $ a_nx + b_ny = 1$ by linear expression.

(1) Express $ a_{n + 1},\ b_{n + 1}$ in terms of $ a_n,\ b_n$.

(2) Let $ D_n$ be the domain by which the inequality $ a_nx + b_ny > 1$ determines. Draw the domain of the points which are contained in all of $ D_0,\ D_1,\ D_2,\ \cdots$.

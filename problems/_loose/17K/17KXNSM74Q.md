A semicircle is divided into $2 n+1$ equal arcs by points $A_{0}, A_{1}, \ldots, A_{2 n+1}$ ( $A_{0}$ and $A_{2 n+1}$ are the endpoints of the semicircle), $O$ is the center of the semicircle. Prove that the lines $A_{1} A_{2 n}$, $A_{2} A_{2 n-1}, \ldots, A_{n} A_{n+1}$ form segments with the lines $O A_{n}$ and $O A_{n+1}$, the sum of the lengths of which is equal to the radius of the circle.

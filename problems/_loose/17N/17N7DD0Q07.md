The number $a\in \{1,2,…,9\}$ is written on the board. Agnes and Ben take turns writing any digit from $1$ to $9$ in any place of the number on the board and thus obtain a new number. The game ends if any player manages to write a digit in such a way that the resulting number is the square of a natural number. That player wins the game. For each $a = 1$ , $2$ , $…$ , $9$ , determine whether any player has a winning strategy, and if so, which one. (Agnes starts the game.)

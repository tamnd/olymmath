At Ivan's birthday, $3 n$ guests arrived. Ivan has $3 n$ cylinders with letters A, B, and V written on top, with $n$ of each type. Ivan wants to organize a ball: put the cylinders on the guests and arrange them in circles (one or more) such that the length of each circle is divisible by $3$, and when looking at any circle from above, it reads ABAVAV...ABV clockwise. Prove that Ivan can organize the ball in exactly $(3n)!$ different ways. (Cylinders with the same letter are indistinguishable; all guests are different.)

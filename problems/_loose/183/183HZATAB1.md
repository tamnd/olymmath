In the vertices $A B C \triangle$, draw tangents to the circumcircle of the triangle. These tangents form a triangle whose vertices opposite to $A, B, C$ are $E_{a}, E_{b}, E_{c}$. Let the feet of the altitudes of $\triangle A B C$ be $M_{a}, M_{b}, M_{c}$. Prove that the lines $E_{a} M_{a}, E_{b} M_{b}, E_{c} M_{c}$ intersect at a point and this point lies on the Euler line of $\triangle A B C$.

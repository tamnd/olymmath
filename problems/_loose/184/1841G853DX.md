Point $O$ is the center of a unit circle, and $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{2 n}$ is a convex $2 n$-sided polygon inscribed in the unit circle. Prove:
$$
\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n} \overrightarrow{A_{2 i-1} A_{2 i}}\right| \leqslant 2 \sin \frac{\angle A_{1} O A_{2}+\angle A_{3} O A_{4}+\cdots+\angle A_{2 n-1} O A_{2 n}}{2} \text {. }
$$

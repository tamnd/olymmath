(Olympiade de Leningrad 1988). We place tokens in some, but not all, of the squares of an $n \times n$ chessboard, where $n>0$ is an integer. Each second, one of the tokens moves from one square to another that is empty. After a certain number of such moves, it is observed that each token has returned to its initial square after visiting all the other squares exactly once. Prove that there was a moment when no token was in its initial square.

Given an angle and a fixed point $P$ on its angle bisector. The line $e_{1}$ passing through $P$ intersects the sides of the angle at segments of lengths $a_{1}$ and $b_{1}$, and the line $e_{2}$ passing through $P$ intersects the sides of the angle at segments of lengths $a_{2}$ and $b_{2}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{b_{1}}=\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\frac{1}{b_{2}}
$$

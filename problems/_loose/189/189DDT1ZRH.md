Let $M$ be the centroid (the point of intersection of the medians) of triangle $ABC$. When point $B$ is rotated by $120^{\circ}$ around point $M$, it moves to point $P$. When point $C$ is rotated by $240^{\circ}$ around point $M$ (in the same direction), it moves to point $Q$. Prove that either triangle $APQ$ is equilateral, or points $A, P, Q$ coincide.

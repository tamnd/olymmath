For real numbers $x, y, z$, the following holds:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x+2 y+4 z \geq 3 \text { and } \\
& y-3 x+2 z \geq 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that in this case, $y-x+2 z \geq 3$.

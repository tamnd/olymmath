Let $N$ be the number of ordered pairs $(x,y)$ st $1 \leq x,y \leq p(p-1)$ and : $$ x^{y} \equiv y^{x} \equiv 1 \pmod{p} $$ where $p$ is a fixed prime number.
Show that : $$ (\phi {(p-1)}d(p-1))^2 \leq N \leq ((p-1)d(p-1))^2 $$ where $d(n)$ is the number of divisors of $n$

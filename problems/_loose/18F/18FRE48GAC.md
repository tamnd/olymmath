If $l$ and $l^{\prime}$ are the angle bisectors of angle $C$ and its adjacent angle in a triangle, then

$$
l^{\prime 2}+l^{2}=\frac{64 R^{2} S^{2}}{\left(a^{2}-b^{2}\right)^{2}}
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle of the triangle and $S$ is the area of the triangle.

For any non-empty subset $S$, let $\sigma(S)$ and $\pi(S)$ denote the sum and product of the elements of $S$, respectively. Prove: $\sum \frac{\sigma(S)}{\pi(S)}=\left(n^{2}+2 n\right)-\left(1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}\right)(n+1)$, where " $\sum$ " denotes the sum over all non-empty subsets $S$ of $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$.

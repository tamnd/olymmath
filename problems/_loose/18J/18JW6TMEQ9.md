For example, let the circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$ be $O$, and let $A'$ be the midpoint of side $BC$. $AA'$ intersects the circumcircle at point $A''$, and $A'Q_a \perp AO$, with point $Q_a$ on $AO$. The tangent to the circumcircle at point $A''$ intersects $A'Q_a$ at point $P_a$. Similarly, points $P_b$ and $P_c$ can be constructed. Prove that $P_a, P_b, P_c$ are collinear.
(2004 2005 22nd Iranian Olympiad problem)

Show that if $a, b, c$ are the sides of a triangle, and $R$ and $r$ are the radii of the circumscribed and inscribed circles, respectively, then

$$
R \cdot r=\frac{a \cdot b \cdot c}{2(a+b+c)}
$$

Problem from "Eléments de géométrie" by Legendre.

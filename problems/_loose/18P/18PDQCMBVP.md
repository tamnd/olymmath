17・109 Let the altitudes $A D, B E, C F$ of an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$ intersect at $H$. If $B C=$ $a, A C=b, A B=c$, then the value of $A H \cdot A D+B H \cdot B E+C H \cdot C F$ is
(A) $\frac{1}{2}(a b+b c+c a)$.
(B) $\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$.
(C) $\frac{2}{3}(a b+b c+c a)$.
(D) $\frac{2}{3}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1994)

Let $n$ be a natural number. In a group of $n$ people, some of them are friends, and some are not. Friendships are mutual.

The group visited a fortune-teller. Each of the $n$ people told the fortune-teller how many friends they have in the group. After that, the fortune-teller said that for every pair of people in the group, he can determine with certainty whether they are friends or not.

If the fortune-teller spoke the truth, prove that in the group either there is a person who is friends with everyone else, or there is a person who is not friends with anyone.

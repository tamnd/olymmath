Let $ n$ be a positive integer, and let $ \left( a_{1},\ a_{2} ,\ ...,\ a_{n}\right)$, $ \left( b_{1},\ b_{2},\ ...,\ b_{n}\right)$ and $ \left( c_{1},\ c_{2},\ ...,\ c_{n}\right)$ be three sequences of integers such that for any two distinct numbers $ i$ and $ j$ from the set $ \left\{ 1,2,...,n\right\}$, none of the seven integers

$ a_{i}-a_{j}$; $ \left( b_{i}+c_{i}\right) -\left( b_{j}+c_{j}\right)$;
$ b_{i}-b_{j}$; $ \left( c_{i}+a_{i}\right) -\left( c_{j}+a_{j}\right)$;
$ c_{i}-c_{j}$; $ \left( a_{i}+b_{i}\right) -\left( a_{j}+b_{j}\right)$;
$ \left( a_{i}+b_{i}+c_{i}\right) -\left( a_{j}+b_{j}+c_{j}\right)$

is divisible by $ n$.

Prove that:

**a)** The number $ n$ is odd.

**b)** The number $ n$ is not divisible by $ 3$.

Inside an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, consider the point $M$. The internal angle bisectors of angles $\angle BMC, \angle AMC, \angle AMB$ intersect the sides $BC, AC$, and $AB$ at points $D, E$, and $F$, respectively.

a) Show that the lines $AD, BE$, and $CF$ are concurrent at a point $P$.

b) Show that $\frac{FA}{FB} + \frac{EA}{EC} = \frac{PA}{PD}$.

c) Show that $\frac{PA}{PD} + \frac{PB}{PE} + \frac{PC}{PF} \geq 6$. Identify the point $M$ in the case of equality.

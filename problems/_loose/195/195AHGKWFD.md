Let $p$ be a prime number of the form $4 t+1$. Let $\left(a_{1}, b_{1}\right),\left(a_{2}, b_{2}\right), \ldots,\left(a_{k}, b_{k}\right)$ be all ordered pairs of natural numbers $(a, b)$ such that $a<b \leqslant \frac{p-1}{2}$ and $p \mid a^{2}+b^{2}$. Prove:

$$
b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{k}=2\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{k}\right)
$$

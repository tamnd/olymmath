Real numbers $x, y$ are solutions to the system of equations

$$
\begin{gathered}
2 \sqrt{2} x \cos t + 3 y \sin t = 6 \sqrt{2} \\
2 \sqrt{2} x \sin t - 3 y \cos t = 0
\end{gathered}
$$

For which values of the parameter $t \in (0, \pi)$ is the product $x y$ equal to 3?

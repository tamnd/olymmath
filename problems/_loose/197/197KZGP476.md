Let $a_{n}$ be the number of $n$-digit integers formed by 1,2 and 3 which do not contain any consecutive 1 's. Prove that $a_{n}$ is equal to $\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)(\sqrt{3}+1)^{n}$ rounded off to the nearest integer.

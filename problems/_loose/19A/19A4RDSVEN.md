The cross-section of a ball bearing consists of two concentric circles $ C $ and $ C_1 $, between which there are $ n $ small circles $ k_1, k_2, \ldots, k_n $, each of which is tangent to two adjacent ones and to both circles $ C $ and $ C_1 $. Given the radius $ r $ of the inner circle $ C $ and the natural number $ n $, calculate the radius $ x $ of the circle $ C_2 $ passing through the points of tangency of the circles $ k_1, k_2, \ldots, k_n $ and the sum $ s $ of the lengths of those arcs of the circles $ k_1, k_2, \ldots, k_n $ that lie outside the circle $ C_2 $.

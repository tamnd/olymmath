113 Let the function $f(x)$ be defined for all positive real numbers and take positive values. It is known that if $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of a triangle, then $f(a), f(b), f(c)$ are also the lengths of the sides of some triangle. Prove that there exist positive numbers $A$ and $B$ such that for all $x>0$,
$$
f(x) \leqslant A x+B .
$$

On each wall of the cube, there is exactly one integer written. In one step, we choose any two adjacent walls of the cube and increase the numbers written on them by 1. Determine the necessary and sufficient condition for the numbering of the walls of the cube at the beginning, so that after a finite number of suitable steps, the same numbers are on all walls of the cube.

Show that the system of functions $x_{1}=-\frac{1}{t^{2}}, x_{2}=-t \ln t$, defined in the interval $0<t<+\infty$, is a solution to the system of differential equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\frac{d x_{1}}{d t}=2 t x_{1}^{2} \\
\frac{d x_{2}}{d t}=\frac{x_{2}}{t}-1
\end{array}\right.
$$

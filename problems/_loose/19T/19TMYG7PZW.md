Let $\Gamma$ be a circle, $[A B]$ a chord of this circle, and $\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}$ two circles internally tangent to $\Gamma$ at $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$, and to $(A B)$ at $T_{1}^{\prime}$ and $T_{2}^{\prime}$, such that $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ are on the same side of $[A B]$. These two circles intersect at $C$ and $D$. Show that $(C D)$ passes through the midpoint of the arc $\widehat{A B}$ not containing $T_{1}$.

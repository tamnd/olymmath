Let $e$ be a line with three distinct points $E, F, P$, and among the segments $EF, FP, PE$, no two are of equal length. Furthermore, let $a$ be an arbitrary line intersecting $e$ at $P$, with two points $A$ and $C$ on $a$ distinct from each other and from $P$. Let the intersection of lines $AE$ and $CF$ be $B$, and the intersection of lines $AF$ and $CE$ be $D$. Finally, let the intersection of line $e$ and line $BD$ be $Q$. Show that if $B, D, Q$ exist, the position of $Q$ does not depend on the choice of $a, A$, and $C$.

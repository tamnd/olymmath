Let $p$ be a prime, and $N=A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{p}$ be a $p$-partition of the set of natural numbers. Prove that there exists a set $A$ among $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{p}$, such that there are infinitely many irreducible polynomials $f(x)$ of degree $p-1$, with coefficients in $A$ and all distinct.

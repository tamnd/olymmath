On 99 cards, the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 99$ are written. Then the cards are shuffled, laid out with their clean sides up, and the numbers $1,2,3,4, \ldots, 99$ are written again on the clean sides. For each card, the numbers on it are added, and the 99 resulting sums are multiplied. Prove that the result will be an even number.

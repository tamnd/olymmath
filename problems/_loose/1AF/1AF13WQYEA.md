We have $n$ natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$. For some natural number $k$, $k < n$, we define the sequence $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{n-k}$ as follows: $b_{i} = a_{i} + a_{i+1} + \ldots + a_{i+k}$.

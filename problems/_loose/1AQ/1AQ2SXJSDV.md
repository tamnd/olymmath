In one class, 5 students are engaged in swimming, 12 students in cycling, and 9 students in running, with some students participating in multiple activities and some not participating in any. Six students are engaged in both cycling and running, and four students in both cycling and swimming. Two students are engaged in all three activities, and there are no students who are engaged in only swimming and running. Two students do not participate in any of these three activities. Determine:

- How many students are engaged in at least one activity?
- How many students are engaged only in running?
- How many students are engaged in swimming and cycling but not running?
- How many students are there in total in this class?

Determine all real numbers $x, y$ and $\alpha$ for which

$$
y=-2018 x \text { and } \frac{2018 x+i}{y+i}=\frac{1+i \sin \alpha}{1-i \sin 3 \alpha}, x \neq 0, \alpha \in (0,2 \pi)
$$

$i$ denotes the imaginary unit $\left(i^{2}=-1\right)$.

\section*{Problem 3A - 251043A}

Kurt wants to construct four points $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}, P_{4}$ on a wooden sphere $K$ that satisfy the conditions

$$
P_{1} P_{2}=P_{1} P_{3}=P_{1} P_{4}=P_{2} P_{3}=P_{2} P_{4}=P_{3} P_{4}
$$

The following tools are available to him:
- A flat drawing sheet $B$, on which a line segment $D E$ is given whose length is equal to the diameter $d$ of the sphere $K$,
- a compass, with which one can draw circles both on $B$ and on the surface of the sphere $K$ (the compass has foldable, sufficiently long legs for this purpose),
- a ruler (to be used only for constructing straight lines on $B$, not for scale reading).

Describe a construction that can be carried out with these tools!

Prove that the construction you describe yields four points of the required type!

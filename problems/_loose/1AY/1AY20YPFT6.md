Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be the Fibonacci sequence, defined as follows: $a_{1}=a_{2}=1, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}, n \in \mathbf{N}$. Prove that if an $n$-degree polynomial $P(x)$ satisfies: when $k=n+2, n+3, \cdots, 2 n+2$, $P(k)=a_{k}$, then $P(2 n+3)=a_{2 n+3}-1$.

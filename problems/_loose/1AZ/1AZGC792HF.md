Let $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ intersect at $P$ and $Q$ , and let $R$ be the intersection point of $AC$ and $PQ$ . Prove that $\frac{PR}{RQ}=\frac13$ if and only if $\angle RDQ=90^o$

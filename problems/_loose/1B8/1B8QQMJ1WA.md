A graph $G$ is called balanced if for every subgraph $H \subseteq G$ we have $\varepsilon(H) \leqslant \varepsilon(G)$.
(i) Find several natural classes of balanced graphs.
(ii) Prove: The average degree is an upper bound for the arboricity of a balanced graph. Is $\varepsilon$ or $\varepsilon+1$ an upper bound for the arboricity?
(iii) Use balanced graphs or other graphs to characterize the graph family $G$: for every induced subgraph $H \subseteq G$ we have $\varepsilon(H) \geqslant \varepsilon(G)$.

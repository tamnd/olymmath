99 wise men sat at a round table. They know that fifty of them are wearing hats of one of two colors, and the other forty-nine are wearing hats of the other color (but it is not known in advance which of the two colors 50 hats are, and which 49 are). Each wise man can see the colors of all the hats except his own. All the wise men must simultaneously write down (each on their own piece of paper) the color of their hat. Can the wise men agree in advance to answer in such a way that at least 74 of them give the correct answer? (U. Feige, proposed by K. Knop)

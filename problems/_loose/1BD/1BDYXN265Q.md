In the country of $\mathrm{Oz}$, besides ordinary people (who can either lie or tell the truth), there live knights (who always tell the truth) and knaves (who always lie). A strange law stipulates that in every marriage, the spouses must either both be ordinary, or one must be a knight and the other a knave. Arriving at the duplex of the Allegri and Bianchi families, you ask if Mr. Bianchi, who is absent, is a knight, and receive an affirmative answer from both Mrs. Bianchi and both of the Allegri couple. What can you conclude about Mr. Bianchi?

(A) Mr. Bianchi is a knight

(B) Mr. Bianchi is a knave

(C) Mr. Bianchi is ordinary

(D) It is impossible for the answers given to be as reported

(E) You cannot conclude anything.

Let's determine all the number pairs $(A, B)$ for which there exist sequences $a_{n}, b_{n}$ such that $\lim \left(a_{n} b_{n}\right) = A$ and $\lim \left(a_{n} + b_{n}\right) = B$. For which $A$ and $B$ will it be true that the sequences $a_{n}$ and $b_{n}$ themselves are convergent?

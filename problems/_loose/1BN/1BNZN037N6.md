Let $P(x)=x^{2}+a x+b$ be a quadratic polynomial where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers. Suppose $\left\langle P(-1)^{2}, P(0)^{2}, P(1)^{2}\right\rangle$ is an arithmetic progression of integers. Prove that $a$ and $b$ are integers.

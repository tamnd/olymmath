Given a triangle $A B C$. Through point $P$, lines $P A, P B, P C$ are drawn, intersecting the circumcircle of this triangle at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, distinct from the vertices of the triangle. It turns out that triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is congruent to triangle $A B C$. Prove that there are no more than eight points $P$ with the given property.

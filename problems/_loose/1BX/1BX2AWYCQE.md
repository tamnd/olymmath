One of the most important characteristics of human blood is the Rh factor. The "Rh positive" gene is dominant over the "Rh negative" gene.

In one of the surveyed populations, the probability that a person has a positive Rh factor is 0.91. For the random variable $X$ - the number of Rh-negative people among 1000 people in this population, find:

a) the expected value, variance, and standard deviation;

b) the probability that the absolute value of the deviation of the number of Rh-negative people from its expected value does not exceed a given positive number $\delta$;

c) the interval, symmetric about the expected value, in which the possible values of $X$ will be contained with a probability of 0.9722.

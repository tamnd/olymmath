Let $S^{\prime}$ be the circle homothetic to the incircle $s$ of a triangle with a coefficient of $\frac{1}{2}$ relative to the Nagel point, and $S$ be the circle homothetic to the circle $s$ with a coefficient of -$\frac{1}{2}$ relative to the centroid. Prove that: a) the circles $S$ and $S^{\prime}$ coincide; b) the circle $S$ is tangent to the midlines of the triangle; c) the circle $S^{\prime}$ is tangent to the lines connecting the midpoints of the segments with endpoints at the Nagel point and the vertices of the triangle.

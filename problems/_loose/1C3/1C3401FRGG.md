Do there exist two integer-valued functions $f$ and $g$ such that for every integer $x$ we have
(a) $f(f(x)) = x, g(g(x)) = x, f(g(x)) > x, g(f(x)) > x$ ?
(b) $f(f(x)) < x, g(g(x)) < x, f(g(x)) > x, g(f(x)) > x$ ?

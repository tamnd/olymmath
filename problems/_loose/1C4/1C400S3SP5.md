Show that for all $n \geq 2$ we can find $n$ real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \neq 1$ such that the products

$$
x_{1} \cdot x_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot x_{n} \text { and } \frac{1}{1-x_{1}} \cdot \frac{1}{1-x_{2}} \cdot \ldots \cdot \frac{1}{1-x_{n}}
$$

are equal.

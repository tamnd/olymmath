Serena has written 20 copies of the number 1 on a board. In a move, she is allowed to
$ *$ erase two of the numbers and replace them with their sum, or
$ *$ erase one number and replace it with its reciprocal.
Whenever a fraction appears on the board, Serena writes it in simplest form. Prove that Serena can never write a fraction less than 1 whose numerator is over 9000, regardless of the number of moves she makes.

$a, b, c$ are three positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=1$. Which of the following conditions is equivalent to imposing that $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of a non-degenerate triangle?

(A) $0<|b-a|<\frac{1}{2}, 0<|c-b|<\frac{1}{2}, 0<|c-a|<\frac{1}{2}$

(B) $a<\frac{1}{2}, b<\frac{1}{2}, c<\frac{1}{2}$

(C) $a+b<\frac{1}{2}, b+c<\frac{1}{2}, c+a<\frac{1}{2}$

(D) $a \leq \frac{1}{3}, b \leq \frac{1}{3}, c \leq \frac{1}{3}$

(E) none of the above.

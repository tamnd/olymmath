In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2} \geqslant & 2 y z \sin \left(A-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+2 z x \sin \left(B-\frac{\pi}{6}\right) \\
& +2 x y \sin \left(C-\frac{\pi}{6}\right),
\end{aligned}
$$

where $x, y, z$ are positive real numbers.

Let $M$ be any interior point on the side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$. Let $r_{1}, r_{2}$, and $r$ denote the radii of the inscribed circles of triangles $AMC$, $BMC$, and $ABC$, respectively. Furthermore, let $\varrho_{1}$ be the radius of the excircle of triangle $AMC$ opposite to side $AM$, $\varrho_{2}$ the radius of the excircle of triangle $BMC$ opposite to side $BM$, and $\varrho$ the radius of the excircle of triangle $ABC$ opposite to side $AB$.

Prove that in this case, the equality $\frac{r_{1}}{\varrho_{1}} \cdot \frac{r_{2}}{\varrho_{2}}=\frac{r}{\varrho}$ holds.

In a telenovela about events in a conspiratorial town, $n$ citizens appear, $n \geq 4$. Each group of 3 citizens plots against one of the remaining citizens. Prove that there exists a citizen such that at least $\sqrt[3]{(n-1)(n-2)}$ citizens are involved in a plot against him.

Solve the problem independently. You have 210 minutes to solve it.

The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle with center $O$. We denote by $H_{1}, H_{2}, H_{3}, H_{4}$ the orthocenters of triangles $ABC, BCD, CDA$, and $DAB$ respectively, and by $M, N$ the midpoints of the diagonals $[AC]$ and $[BD]$. (i) Show that the segments $\left[D H_{1}\right],\left[A H_{2}\right],\left[B H_{3}\right],\left[C H_{4}\right]$ have the same midpoint $P$.

(ii) Show that the points $O, P$ and the midpoint of the segment $[M N]$ are collinear.

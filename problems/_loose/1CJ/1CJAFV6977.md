Let $P, Q$ be points on the radius $OK$ or its extension of the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$, such that $OP \cdot OQ=R^{2}$, where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle. The reflections of point $P$ over $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $U$, $V$, and $W$, respectively. The lines $QU$, $QV$, and $QW$ intersect $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$. Prove that $D$, $E$, and $F$ are collinear.

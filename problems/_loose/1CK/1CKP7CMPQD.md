Let $\xi_{n}, n \geqslant 1,$ be i.i.d. random variables, $\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1}=1\right)=\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1}=-1\right)=1 / 2$.

From general criteria for the convergence of series with independent terms, it follows that the series

$$
\sum_{m, m}^{m}
$$

converges in distribution to some random variable $X_{\alpha}$ if and only if $\alpha>1 / 2$. Establish that $X_{\alpha}$ necessarily has a density.

(See [50].) Let $\xi$ be a random variable. Also, let the function $f=f(x)$ be non-decreasing on $\mathbb{R}$, and the expression $x - f(x) + \mathrm{E} f(\xi) - \mathrm{E} \xi$ changes its sign exactly once from "-" to "+" as $x$ runs from $-\infty$ to $\infty$. Prove that for any continuous convex function $g$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the following inequalities hold:

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathrm{E} g(f(\xi) - \mathrm{E} f(\xi)) \leqslant \mathrm{E} g(\xi - \mathrm{E} \xi) \\
g(\mathrm{E} \xi) \leqslant \mathrm{E} g(f(\xi) - \mathrm{E} f(\xi) + \mathrm{E} \xi) \leqslant \mathrm{E} g(\xi)
\end{gathered}
$$

It is assumed that all the given expectations exist.

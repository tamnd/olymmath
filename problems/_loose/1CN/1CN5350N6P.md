Let $X$ be a random variable taking values $0,1, \ldots, n$, with binomial moments $b_{0}, b_{1}, \ldots, b_{n}$, defined by the formula

$$
b_{k}=\mathrm{E} C_{X}^{k}=\frac{\mathrm{E}(X)_{k}}{k!} \equiv \frac{1}{k!} \mathrm{E} X(X-1) \ldots(X-k+1)
$$

Show that the generating function has the form

$$
G(s)=\mathrm{E} s^{X}=\sum_{k=0}^{n} b_{k}(s-1)^{k}=\sum_{i=0}^{n} s^{i} \sum_{k=i}^{n}(-1)^{k-i} C_{k}^{i} b_{k}
$$

and, consequently, for each $i=0,1, \ldots, n$ the equality holds

$$
\mathrm{P}(X=i)=p_{i}=\sum_{k=i}^{n}(-1)^{k-i} C_{k}^{i} b_{k}
$$

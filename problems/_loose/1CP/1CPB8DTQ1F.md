At a round table, 99 gnomes are sitting. The hobbit Bilbo knows all the gnomes, but he cannot see how they are seated because his eyes are blindfolded. Bilbo can name any two gnomes, and all the gnomes will answer in unison how many gnomes are sitting between these two gnomes (along the shortest arc). Can Bilbo find out at least one pair of gnomes sitting next to each other by asking no more than 50 questions?

Given a circle $(O, r)$, its radius $A B$ and a point $M$ on the circle. Let $Q$ be the projection of $M$ on $A B$, $N$ be the midpoint of $M Q$, and $D$ be the other intersection point of the line $A N$ and the given circle. Determine the geometric locus of the intersection $P$ of the lines $B D$ and $Q M$, as the point $M$ moves along the circle $(O, r)$.

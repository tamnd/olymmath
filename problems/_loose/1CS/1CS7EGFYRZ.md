\section*{Problem 3B - 221043B}

Five spheres $K_{1}, \ldots, K_{5}$ with the same radius $r$ are arranged such that each sphere touches exactly two others, and their centers $M_{1}, \ldots, M_{5}$ form a regular pentagon in a plane.

A sixth sphere $K_{6}$ with radius $r$ touches each of the five spheres $K_{1}, \ldots, K_{5}$.

Determine whether $K_{6}$ intersects, touches, or does not reach the plane through $M_{1}, \ldots, M_{5}$!

Given a regular $\triangle A B C$, take $E$ on the extension of $B C$, and construct a regular $\triangle D C E$ on the same side of $B E$. $B D$ intersects $A C$ at $G$, and $C D$ intersects $A E$ at $F$. Prove:
$$
C F=F G=G C \text {. }
$$

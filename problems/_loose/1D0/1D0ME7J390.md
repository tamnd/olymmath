On successive turns, starting with Ana, each player can make the following moves: if the number of stones in the bag is even, the player can take one stone or half of the stones. If the number of stones in the bag is odd, the player must take one stone. The goal of the game is to take the last stone. Determine for which values of $n$ Ana has a winning strategy.

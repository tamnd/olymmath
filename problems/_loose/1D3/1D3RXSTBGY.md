Let $x_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$. Real numbers $\alpha, \beta$ satisfy $\alpha \cdot \beta>0$, prove that:
$$
\frac{x_{1}^{3}}{x_{2}^{a}}+\frac{x_{2}^{3}}{x_{3}^{a}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{3}}{x_{n}^{a}}+\frac{x_{n}^{3}}{x_{1}^{a}} \geqslant x_{1}^{3-\alpha}+x_{2}^{3-\alpha}+\cdots+x_{n}^{3-\alpha} .
$$

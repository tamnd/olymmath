Given the ellipse $M: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ passes through the point $P(-2,0)$, and its focal distance is $2 \sqrt{3}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $M$;
(2) Draw a line $l$ through the point $Q(-2,-1)$ intersecting the lower half of the ellipse $M$ at two distinct points $A, B$, and connect $P A, P B$ to intersect the line $y=-1$ at points $C, D$ respectively.
Prove that $|Q C|+|Q D|-|Q C| \cdot|Q D|$ is a constant.

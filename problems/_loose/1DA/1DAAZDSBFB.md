The director once noticed that among the participants of the mathematics club, exactly $1 / 6$ part also attend the physics club, and $1 / 8$ part - the informatics club; among the participants of the physics club, exactly $1 / 3$ part also attend the mathematics club, and exactly $1 / 5$ - the informatics club; finally, among the participants of the informatics club, exactly $1 / 7$ part attend the mathematics club. What part of the participants of the informatics club also attend the physics club?

The sides of a triangle are $a, b, c$, and its heights are $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}$.

Show that:

$$
m_{a} \cdot m_{b}+m_{b} \cdot m_{c}+m_{c} \cdot m_{a} \leq \frac{3}{4}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)
$$

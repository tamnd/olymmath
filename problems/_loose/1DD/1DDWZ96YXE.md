A group of 25 children from kindergarten returned from a walk. To dry, the children laid out all their 50 mittens in a row on the radiator. The group's teacher noticed that the mittens of each child were not next to each other. Moreover, it turned out that between the mittens of any child, there lay a prime number of mittens of other children. Prove that there will be a child whose mittens have exactly two other mittens between them.

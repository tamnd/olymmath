Digits and Cubes. Professor Rackbrain recently asked his young friends to find all five-digit squares for which the sum of the numbers formed by the first two and the last two digits is a perfect cube. For example, if we take the square of 141, which is 19881, and add 81 to 19, we get 100 - a number that, unfortunately, is not a perfect cube.

How many solutions exist?

On one straight line, there is a row of points $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, \ldots, A_{n}$, and on another straight line - a row of points $B_{1}, B_{2}, B_{3}, \ldots, B_{n}$, such that

$$
\frac{A_{1} A_{2}}{B_{1} B_{2}}=\frac{A_{2} A_{3}}{B_{2} B_{3}}=\frac{A_{3} A_{4}}{B_{3} B_{4}}=\ldots=\frac{A_{n-1} A_{n}}{B_{n-1} B_{n}}
$$

Prove that the geometric locus of points dividing the segments $A_{i} B$ ( $i=1,2,3, \ldots, n$ ) in equal ratios is a straight line or a point.

On the sides of an equilateral triangle $A B C$, isosceles triangles $A_{1} B C, A B_{1} C$, and $A B C_{1}$ are constructed inwardly with angles $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ at the bases, respectively, such that $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 60^{\circ}$. The lines $B C_{1}$ and $B_{1} C$ intersect at point $A_{2}$, $A C_{1}$ and $A_{1} C$ at point $B_{2}$, and $A B_{1}$ and $A_{1} B$ at point $C_{2}$. Prove that the angles of triangle $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ are $3 \alpha, 3 \beta$, and $3 \gamma$.

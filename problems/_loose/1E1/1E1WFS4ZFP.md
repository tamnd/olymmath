We will call a diagonal of a convex 101-gon principal if on one side of it lie 50, and on the other side - 49 vertices. Several principal diagonals without common endpoints are chosen. Prove that the sum of the lengths of these diagonals is less than the sum of the lengths of the remaining principal diagonals. (I. Bogdanov, S. Berlov)

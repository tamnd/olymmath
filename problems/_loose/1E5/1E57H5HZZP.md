Prove or disprove that the following relations are true for any values of the variables:
1) $(a-b)^{2}+(a b+1)^{2}=\left(a^{2}+1\right)\left(b^{2}+1\right)$
2) $(x-2)(x-3)(x+3)(x+2)+x^{2}=\left(x^{2}-6\right)^{2}$;
3) $(m-1)^{2}(m+2)>m\left(m^{2}-4\right)$;
4) $2(3 a-1)^{2}+3(2 a+1)^{2}>(5 a+1)(5 a-1)$.

[4] There are $2 n+1$ coins lying in a circle, all heads up. Moving clockwise, they make $2 n+1$ flips: they flip some coin, skip one coin and flip the next, skip two coins and flip the next, skip three coins and flip the next, and so on, finally skip $2 n$ coins and flip the next. Prove that now exactly one coin lies tails up.

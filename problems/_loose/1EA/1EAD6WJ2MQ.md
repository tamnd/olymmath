A regular $n$-sided polygon is inscribed in a circle of radius $R$. By connecting the midpoints of the sides of this polygon, we again obtain a regular $n$-sided polygon. Repeating this procedure to infinity, show that the sum of the areas of all these polygons is equal to the area of a regular $n$-sided polygon whose each side is $2 R$.

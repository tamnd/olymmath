Show that the algebra $\alpha\left(A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}\right)$ generated by the system $\left\{A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}\right\}$, where $A_{i} \subseteq \Omega, 1 \leqslant i \leqslant n$, consists of no more than $2^{2^{n}}$ elements.

For example, $\alpha(A, B), A \Delta B \neq \varnothing$, consists of 16 elements of the form

$A, B, \bar{A}, \bar{B}, A \cap B, \bar{A} \cap \bar{B}, A \backslash B, B \backslash A$,

$$
A \cup B, A \cup \bar{B}, \bar{A} \cup B, \bar{A} \cup \bar{B}, A \triangle B, \overline{A \triangle B}, \Omega, \varnothing
$$

where $A \Delta B=(A \backslash B) \cup(B \backslash A)$ is the "symmetric difference" of sets $A$ and $B$.

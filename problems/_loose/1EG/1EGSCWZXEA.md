Yura and Yasha each have a copy of the same $5 \times 5$ grid, filled with 25 different numbers. Yura selects the largest number in the grid and crosses out the row and column containing this number, then selects the largest of the remaining numbers and crosses out the row and column containing this number, and so on. Yasha performs similar actions but selects the smallest numbers. Can it happen that the sum of the numbers chosen by Yasha

a) is greater than the sum of the numbers chosen by Yura?

b) is greater than the sum of any other five numbers in the original grid that satisfy the condition: no two of them are in the same row or column?

Arbitrarily write the numbers $1,2, \cdots, n$ on a circle. Allow each time to swap the positions of two adjacent numbers, provided that the absolute difference between these two numbers is greater than 1. Prove that after several such swaps, all numbers can be arranged in natural order on the circle.

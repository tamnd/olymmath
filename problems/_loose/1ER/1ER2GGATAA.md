A triangle has perimeter $2s$ , inradius $r$ , and incenter $I$ . If $s_a, s_b$ and $s_c$ are the distances from $I$ to the three vertices, then show that $$ \frac34 +\frac{r}{s_a}+\frac{r}{s_b}+\frac{r}{s_c} \le \frac{s^2}{12r^2} $$

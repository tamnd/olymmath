On an $n \times n$ grid, there is a beetle at each node. At midnight, each beetle crawls to the center of some cell, and it turns out that the distance between any two beetles that were initially at adjacent nodes (the ends of a side of a square) has not increased. Prove: at least one beetle has crawled to a cell of which its initial position is one of the vertices.

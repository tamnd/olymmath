$k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ are two non-intersecting circles. Prove the following:

If the power of $P$ and $Q$ with respect to $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ are equal: $h\left(P, k_{1}\right)=h\left(P, k_{2}\right)$ and $h\left(Q, k_{1}\right)=h\left(Q, k_{2}\right)$, then
a) $P Q \perp O O_{2}$, where $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ are the centers of the circles.
b) Every point on $P Q$ and only the points on $P Q$ have the same power with respect to $k_{1}$ as they do with respect to $k_{2}$.

c) If the circle $k$ intersects both $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, then the power line of $k$ and $k_{1}$ intersects the power line of $k$ and $k_{2}$ on $P Q$, or is parallel to it.

How can the power line of two non-intersecting circles be constructed according to the above?

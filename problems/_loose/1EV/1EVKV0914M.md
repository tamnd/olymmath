Let $A B C$ be a triangle, and let $M$ and $N$ be the respective midpoints of $A B$ and $A C$. Suppose that

$$
\frac{C M}{A C}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

Prove that

$$
\frac{B N}{A B}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

There are $n$ boys and $n$ girls standing in a circle. We will call a pair of a boy and a girl good if there are an equal number of boys and girls on one of the arcs between them (in particular, a boy and a girl standing next to each other form a good pair). It turns out that there is a girl who participates in exactly 10 good pairs. Prove that there is also a boy who participates in exactly 10 good pairs.

(N. Vlasova)

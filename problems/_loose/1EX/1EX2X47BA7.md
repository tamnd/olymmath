The space in which we live is called 3-dimensional space. A 3-dimensional space with a fixed Cartesian coordinate system is called a 3-dimensional Euclidean space. In a 3-dimensional Euclidean space, points whose three coordinates are all integers are called lattice points. Prove that for any given 9 different lattice points, there must be two lattice points whose midpoint of the line segment connecting them is also a lattice point.

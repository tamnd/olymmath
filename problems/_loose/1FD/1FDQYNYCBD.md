If a prime number $p$ is a divisor of some number of the form $a^{2}+3$, then $p$ is either 2 or 3, or has the form $3k+1$.
1) Numbers of this form include, for example, $11=3 \cdot 3+2$, $17=3 \cdot 5+2$. On the other hand, the prime numbers 7 and 13 have the form $3k+1$.

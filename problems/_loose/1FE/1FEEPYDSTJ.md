A board of size $2005 \times 2005$ is divided into unit square cells. Some of the cells on the board are numbered in some order with the numbers $1,2, \ldots$ such that within a distance of less than 10 from any unnumbered cell, there is a numbered cell. Prove that there will be two cells within a distance of less than 150 that are numbered with numbers differing by more than 23. The distance between cells is the distance between their centers.

Show that any integer $k$ can be written in the form $k= \pm 1^{2} \pm 2^{2} \pm$ $3^{2} \cdots \pm m^{2}$ with $m$ a sufficiently large integer and well-chosen signs. Show that this representation is not unique and that each integer $k$ admits an infinite number of different representations.

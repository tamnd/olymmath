Three points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ lie on the sides $B C$, $C A$ and $A B$ of $\triangle A B C$ so that $A B_{1}=C_{1} B_{1}$ and $B A_{1}=C_{1} A_{1}$. Prove that the line $C D$ is perpendicular to the straight line through the circumcentres of $\triangle A B C$ and $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C$.

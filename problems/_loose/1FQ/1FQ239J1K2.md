We want to place 12 points on the surface of a sphere such that the distance between any two of them is at least 1 unit. Which of the following arrangements allows for a smaller sphere radius?

I. 5 points are in each of 2 parallel planes, forming the vertices of a regular pentagon, and the additional faces of the convex body they define are equilateral triangles. The remaining points form a regular pyramid with each of the pentagons.

II. The points are in 3 parallel planes, 6 in the middle one, and 3 in each of the outer ones, forming a regular hexagon and equilateral triangles, respectively.

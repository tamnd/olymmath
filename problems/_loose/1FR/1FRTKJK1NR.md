Suppose a locally finite connected graph $G$ has three distinct ends, and let $S$ be a set of vertices that pairwise separate these ends. Take an automorphism that maps $S$ to a "distant" component of $G-S$. Can it be proven that the image of $S$ separates this component such that $G$ must have more than three ends?

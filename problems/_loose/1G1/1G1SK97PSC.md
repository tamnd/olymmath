(Hungary 2004). A palace has the shape of a square divided into $2003 \times 2003$ rooms, like the squares of a large chessboard. There is a door between two rooms if and only if they share a wall. The main door allows, coming from outside the palace, to enter the palace through the room located at the northwest corner. A person enters the palace, visits some of the rooms, then leaves the palace, through the entrance door, when they return to the northwest corner room for the first time. It turns out that they visited each of the other rooms exactly 100 times, except for the room located at the southeast corner. How many times did the visitor go into the southeast corner room?

For $p \geqslant 1, q>0, a_{1} \geqslant a_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant a_{n}>0$, then
$$
\frac{a_{1}^{p}}{b_{1}^{q}}+\frac{a_{2}^{p}}{b_{2}^{q}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n}^{p}}{b_{n}^{q}} \geqslant n^{1-p+q} \frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}\right)^{p}}{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i}\right)^{q}} .
$$

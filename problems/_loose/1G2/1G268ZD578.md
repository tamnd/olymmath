Positive real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots . . ., a_{n}$, satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=1$ Prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{(n-2) a_{i}+a_{i+1}}{1-a_{i}^{2}} \geq \frac{n^{2}}{n+1}
$$

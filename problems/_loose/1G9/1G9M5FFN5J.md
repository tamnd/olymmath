Suppose $N_{1}, N_{2}, \ldots, N_{2011}$ are positive integers, Let
$$
\begin{array}{l}
X=\left(N_{1}+N_{2}+\cdots+N_{2010}\right)\left(N_{2}+N_{3}+\cdots+N_{2011}\right), \\
Y=\left(N_{1}+N_{2}+\cdots+N_{2011}\right)\left(N_{2}+N_{3}+\cdots+N_{2010}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

Which one of the following relationships always holds?
(A) $X=Y$;
(B) $X>Y$;
(C) $X<Y$;
(D) $X-N_{1}<Y-N_{2011}$;
(E) None of the above.

On a strip of paper, 80 non-zero digits are written in sequence, and the strip is cut into several pieces so that each piece has at least two digits, thus the digits on each piece form a natural number. Then, the sum of these natural numbers is calculated. Prove that there exist at least two different ways of cutting the strip such that the final sum of the numbers obtained is the same.

For real numbers $a, b, c, x, y$, it is known that

$$
\frac{1}{a+x}=6, \frac{1}{b+y}=3, \frac{1}{c+x+y}=2
$$

Prove that among the numbers $a, b, c$, one is equal to the sum of the other two.

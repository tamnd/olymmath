Let's construct the parallelograms $A B D E$ and $B C F G$ outwardly on the sides $A B$ and $B C$ of triangle $A B C$, respectively, such that the two parallelograms do not have any common interior points. The intersection point of lines $D E$ and $F G$ is $H$. Let's construct the parallelogram $C A I J$ on the side $C A$ of the triangle, such that side $A I$ is parallel and equal to segment $B H$. Show that the area of this parallelogram is equal to the sum of the areas of the two previously constructed parallelograms.

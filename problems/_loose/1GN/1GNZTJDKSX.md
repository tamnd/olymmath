Sasha chose four natural numbers $x, y, z, t$ and wrote down 12 fractions:

$$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccc}
\frac{x}{y}, & \frac{x}{z}, \frac{x}{t}, \frac{y}{x}, \frac{y}{z}, \frac{y}{t}, & \frac{z}{x}, \frac{z}{y}, \frac{z}{t}, \frac{t}{x}, & \frac{t}{y}, & \frac{t}{z}
\end{array}
$$

Prove that some two of these fractions differ by no more than 11/60.

(A. Kuznetsov)

Let the incircle of the equilateral $\triangle ABC$ touch the sides $AB, BC, CA$ at $D, E, F$, respectively. Let $P$ be any point on the arc $DF$ of the incircle, and the distances from $P$ to the sides $AC, AB, BC$ are $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$, respectively. Prove that: $\sqrt{d_{1}}+\sqrt{d_{2}}=\sqrt{d_{3}}$.

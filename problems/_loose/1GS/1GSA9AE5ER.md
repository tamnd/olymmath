Consider the triangle $ABC$ with $[\mathrm{AB}] \equiv[\mathrm{AC}]$ and $\angle \mathrm{B} \equiv \angle \mathrm{C}$. Let $M$ and $N$ be two points on the line $BC$ such that $B$ is between $M$ and $C$, and $C$ is between $B$ and $N$. Knowing that $[\mathrm{BM}] \equiv[\mathrm{CN}]$, prove that:

a) $[\mathrm{AM}] \equiv[\mathrm{AN}]$;

b) $[P N] \equiv[Q M]$, where $P$ and $Q$ are the midpoints of sides $[A B]$ and $[A C]$, respectively;

c) $[P M] \equiv[Q N]$.

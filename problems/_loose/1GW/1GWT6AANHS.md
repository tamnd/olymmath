For the distinct real numbers $a, b, c$, it holds that

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{(b-c)^{2}}+\frac{b^{2}}{(c-a)^{2}}+\frac{c^{2}}{(a-b)^{2}}=2
$$

Calculate

$$
\frac{a}{b-c}+\frac{b}{c-a}+\frac{c}{a-b}
$$

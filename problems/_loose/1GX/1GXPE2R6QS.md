Given that point $P$ moves on a circle with the orthocenter $H$ of $\triangle ABC$ as its center, and the projections of $P$ on the three sides are points $D$, $E$, and $F$. Prove:
$$
P D^{2} \sin 2 A+P E^{2} \sin 2 B+P F^{2} \sin 2 C
$$

is a constant.

Suppose there is a grasshopper at each of 3 vertices of a square, and they play a "leapfrog game," where one jumps over another. It is known that when grasshopper $A$ leaps over grasshopper $B$, the distance it lands from $B$ is the same as the distance it started from $B$, and these 3 points naturally remain in a straight line. Can it happen after several jumps that one of the grasshoppers lands on another vertex of the square?

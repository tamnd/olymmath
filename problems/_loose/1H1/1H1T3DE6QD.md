Given trapezoid $ABCD$, with sides $CD$ and $AB$ as the upper and lower bases respectively, and $\angle ADC=90^{\circ}$, diagonal $AC \perp BD$, and a perpendicular line $DE \perp BC$ is drawn from $D$ to point $E$. Prove:
(1) $AC^{2}=CD^{2}+AB \cdot CD$;
(2) $\frac{AE}{BE}=\frac{AC \cdot CD}{AC^{2}-CD^{2}}$.

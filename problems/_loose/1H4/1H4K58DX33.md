Let $n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$ and the real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n} \in(0, \infty)$ such that $a_{1} \cdot a_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot a_{n}=1$. Prove that the function

$$
f:(0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f(x)=\left(1+a_{1}^{x}\right) \cdot\left(1+a_{2}^{x}\right) \cdot \ldots \cdot\left(1+a_{n}^{x}\right)
$$

is increasing.

Two circles with centers $M$ and $N$, lying on side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, touch each other and intersect sides $AC$ and $BC$ at points $A, P$ and $B, Q$ respectively. Given that $AM = PM = 2$, $BN = QN = 5$. Find the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, if it is known that the ratio of the area of triangle $AQN$ to the area of triangle $MPB$ is $15 \sqrt{2+\sqrt{3}}) /(5 \sqrt{3})$.

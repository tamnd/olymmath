During the break, $n$ children sit in a circle to play a game.

The teacher walks around the circle in a clockwise direction and distributes candies to the children according to the following rule: $\square$

Choose a child, give him and the next child one candy each, then skip one child, give the next child one candy, then skip 2 children, give the next child one candy, then skip 3 children, and so on.

Determine the value of $n$ such that all children will eventually receive at least one candy (possibly after the teacher has made many rounds).
(31st International Mathematical Olympiad Shortlist, 1990 Asia Pacific Mathematical Olympiad, 1991)

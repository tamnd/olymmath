Let $a, b, c, d$ be given complex numbers, $a \neq 0, a d \neq b c, w=\frac{c z+d}{a z+b}$.
(1) When the point corresponding to $w$ is on a circle, find the locus of the point corresponding to $z$;
(2) When the point corresponding to $w$ is on a straight line, find the locus of the point corresponding to $z$.

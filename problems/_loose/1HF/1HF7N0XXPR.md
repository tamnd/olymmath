Let $a, b, c \in (0 ; \infty)$ with $a \cdot b \cdot c=1$.

a) Verify the equality: $\frac{1}{1+a^{2} c}+\frac{1}{1+b^{2} c}=1$;

b) Prove that: $\frac{1}{1+a^{2} b}+\frac{1}{1+b^{2} c}+\frac{1}{1+c^{2} a}<2$;

( Vasile Berghea, Mathematical Olympiads and Competitions V-VIII 2014, Bîrchi Publishing )

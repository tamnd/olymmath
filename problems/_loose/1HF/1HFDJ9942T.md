On each cell of a chessboard, there is a checker, white on one side and black on the other. In one move, you can choose any checker and flip all the checkers that are on the same vertical line and all the checkers that are on the same horizontal line as the chosen one.

a) Come up with a way to flip exactly one checker on a $6 \times 6$ board, arbitrarily filled with checkers.

b) Is it possible to make all the checkers on a $5 \times 6$ board white if exactly half of the checkers were initially black?

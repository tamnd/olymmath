Once again about the chessboard. On each square of the chessboard, we place a pawn. We collect the pawns and rearrange them, but in such a way that the pawns that were in the left corners occupy their previous positions and so that pawns which were adjacent (i.e., occupied adjacent squares) are again standing next to each other.

Is it possible for any pawn to end up in a different place than initially?

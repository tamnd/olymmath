Félicie has a chessboard of dimensions $8 \times 8$ and dominoes of size $2 \times 1$, which she can rotate if she wishes, but they cannot overlap.

1. Can Félicie cover her chessboard with dominoes, without going off the board?
2. She now removes one corner of the chessboard. Can she cover the rest of the chessboard, still without going off the board?
3. This time, suppose Félicie has removed two opposite corners of the chessboard. Can she cover the rest of the chessboard, without going off the board?

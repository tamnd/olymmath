Let $\alpha, \beta$ be any two interior angles of a triangle. If there exist $\alpha, \beta$ such that the inequality
$$
\cos \sqrt{\alpha} + \cos \sqrt{\beta} > a + \cos \sqrt{\alpha \beta}
$$

holds, find the range of real number $a$.

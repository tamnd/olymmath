2-58 Let $n$ be an odd positive integer, and $p$ a proper divisor of $n$. Try to find the smallest positive integer $m$ (expressed in binary), such that
$$
\left(1+2^{p}+2^{n-p}\right) m \equiv 1\left(\bmod 2^{n}\right) .
$$

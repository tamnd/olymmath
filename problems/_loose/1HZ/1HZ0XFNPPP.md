A quadrilateral has sides $a, b, c, d$, and diagonals $e, f$ (where $e$ starts from the common endpoint of $a$ and $d$). Given that $a > c$, $b \geq d$, and $a \| c$. Prove that

$$
e^{4}-f^{4}=\frac{a+c}{a-c}\left[d^{2}\left(2 a c+d^{2}\right)-b^{2}\left(2 a c+b^{2}\right)\right]
$$

If for the positive real numbers $a, b$ and $c$ the equality

$$
a b\left(\frac{a+b}{2}-c\right)+b c\left(\frac{b+c}{2}-a\right)+a c\left(\frac{c+a}{2}-b\right)=0
$$

holds, then $a=b=c$. Prove it!

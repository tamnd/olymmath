A circle is circumscribed around an $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} \ldots A_{n} ; l$ is an arbitrary tangent to the circle, not passing through the vertices of the $n$-sided polygon. Let $a_{i}$ be the distance from vertex $A_{i}$ to the line $l$, and $b_{i}$ be the distance from the point of tangency of side $A_{i} A_{i+1}$ with the circle to the line $l$. Prove that:

a) the value $b_{1} \ldots b_{n} /\left(a_{1} \ldots a_{n}\right)$ does not depend on the choice of line $l$;

b) the value $a_{1} a_{3} \ldots a_{2 m-1} /\left(a_{2} a_{4} \ldots a_{2 m}\right)$ does not depend on the choice of line $l$, if $n=2 m$.

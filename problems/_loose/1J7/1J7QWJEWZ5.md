Prove that the only numbers $K$ such that if $N$ is divisible by $K$, then any number obtained from $N$ by permuting its digits is also divisible by $K$, are 1, 3, and 9. (For $K=1$ this property is obvious, for $K=3$ and $K=9$ it follows from the known divisibility rules for 3 and 9.)

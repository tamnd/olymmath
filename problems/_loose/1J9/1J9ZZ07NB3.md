In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sin \left(A+\frac{B}{2}\right)+\sin \left(B+\frac{C}{2}\right)+\sin \left(C+\frac{A}{2}\right) \\
=-1+4 \cos \frac{A-B}{4} \cos \frac{B-C}{4} \cos \frac{C-A}{4} .
\end{array}
$$

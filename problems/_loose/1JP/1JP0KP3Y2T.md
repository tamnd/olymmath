(Meeting Problem). Two students agreed to meet at a certain place between 12:00 and 13:00. The one who arrives first waits for the other for $\alpha$ minutes ( $\alpha<60$ ), after which he leaves. What is the probability of their meeting (event $A$), if each of them can arrive at any time during the specified hour, and the times of their arrivals are independent? Consider the special case $\alpha=10$ minutes.

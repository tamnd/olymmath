Find all subsets $S$ of the set of integers $\mathbb{Z}$, such that:
(1) $m, n \in S$
$\Rightarrow$
$m+n \in S$
(2) $m, n \in S \Rightarrow$
$m n \in S$
(3) $(\forall m \in \mathbb{Z}) m \in S$ or $-m \in S$ or $m=0$.

In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, the curve $C_{1}:(x+1)^{2}+y^{2}=16$, the curve $C_{2}: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$, a line through $M(-1,0)$ with a non-zero slope intersects $C_{1}$ at points $A, B$, $N(1,0), A N, B N$ have perpendicular bisectors $l_{1}, l_{2}$ respectively.
Prove: (1) $l_{1}, l_{2}$ are both tangent to $C_{2}$;
(2) The intersection point $P$ of $l_{1}, l_{2}$ lies on a fixed line.

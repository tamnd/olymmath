a). At a match, a number of people enter the stadium according to the following rule: in the first minute after the stadium opens, one person enters, in the second minute, twice the number of people from the first minute plus one more person enter, and the rule continues, meaning in minute $\mathrm{k}+1$, twice the number of people from minute $\mathrm{k}$ plus one more person enter, $\mathrm{k} \geq 1$. How many people have entered after seven minutes?

b). The sequence $1,3,7,15,31,63, \ldots$ is given. Show that if $n$ is the 201st term of the sequence, then the number $n+1$ is a perfect cube.

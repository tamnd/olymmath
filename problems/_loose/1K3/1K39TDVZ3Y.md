Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer, $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ strictly positive real numbers, $b_{1}, \ldots, b_{n}$ strictly positive real numbers. We assume that $a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}=b_{1}+\cdots+b_{n}$ and we denote $S=a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}$.

- Show that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}^{2}}{b_{i}+a_{i}}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{b_{i}^{2}}{b_{i}+a_{i}}$
- Show that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}^{2}}{b_{i}+a_{i}} \geqslant \frac{S}{2}$

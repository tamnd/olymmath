Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $2n$ points be equally spaced on a circle. Prove that for any integer $0 \le k \le \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ , there exists a way to draw $ n$ line segments, each connecting two distinct points, such that exactly $k$ pairs of these line segments intersect.

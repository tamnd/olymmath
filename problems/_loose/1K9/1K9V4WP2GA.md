Prove that if for all $x, y, z$ the equality

$$
\begin{gathered}
a_{11} x^{2}+a_{22} y^{2}+a_{33} z^{2}+2 a_{12} x y+2 a_{13} x z+2 a_{23} y z= \\
=\left(b_{1} x+b_{2} y+b_{3} z\right)\left(c_{1} x+c_{2} y+c_{3} z\right)
\end{gathered}
$$

holds, then $a_{11} a_{22} a_{33}+2 a_{13} a_{12} a_{23}=a_{23}^{2} a_{11}+a_{13}^{2} a_{22}+a_{12}^{2} a_{33}$.

Inside triangle $ABC$, a point $K$ is taken, lying on the bisector of angle $BAC$. Line $CK$ intersects the circumcircle $\omega$ of triangle $ABC$ again at point $M$. Circle $\Omega$ passes through point $A$, is tangent to line $CM$ at point $K$, and intersects segment $AB$ again at point $P$, and circle $\omega$ at point $Q$. Prove that points $P, Q$, and $M$ lie on the same line.

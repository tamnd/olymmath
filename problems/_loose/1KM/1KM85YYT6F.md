For each $n=1,2,3, \ldots$ let

$$
a_{n}=\frac{1}{n^{2}} \sum_{k=1}^{n} k
$$

Furthermore, let $I_{1}, I_{2}, I_{3}$, and $I_{4}$ be the closed intervals

$$
I_{1}=[1 ; 2], I_{2}=[0.53 ; 0.531], I_{3}=[0.509 ; 0.51], I_{4}=[0.4 ; 0.5]
$$

Investigate for each of these intervals whether there are terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ lying in them.

If this is the case, determine the indices $n$ of all terms $a_{n}$ in the respective interval.

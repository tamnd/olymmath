(Arithmetic-geometric inequality) Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}>0$. Show:

$$
\frac{x_{1}+\cdots+x_{n}}{n} \geqslant\left(x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{n}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}
$$

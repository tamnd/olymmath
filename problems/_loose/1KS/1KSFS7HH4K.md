On each side of the square, 100 points are chosen, and from each chosen point, a segment perpendicular to the corresponding side of the square is drawn inside the square. It turns out that no two of the drawn segments lie on the same line. Mark all the intersection points of these segments. For what largest $k<200$ can it happen that exactly $k$ marked points lie on each drawn segment? (N. Avilov, I. Bogdanov)

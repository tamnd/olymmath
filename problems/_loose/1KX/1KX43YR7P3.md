Let the regular $\left(n^{2}+n+1\right)$-gon inscribed in a circle be $S$. Pick $(n+1)$ vertices from its vertices at random, which span a convex polygon $P$. Suppose that it was possible to pick $P$ such that its vertices span chords of all different lengths. Prove that in this case, the center of $S$ is inside $P$, provided that $n \geqq 4$.

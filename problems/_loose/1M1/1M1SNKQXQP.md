Let $A C$ be a line segment in the plane and $B$ a point between $A$ and $C$. Construct isosceles triangles $P A B$ and $Q B C$ on one side of the segment $A C$ such that $\angle A P B=\angle B Q C=120^{\circ}$ and an isosceles triangle $R A C$ on the otherside of $A C$ such that $\angle A R C=120^{\circ}$. Show that $P Q R$ is an equilateral triangle.

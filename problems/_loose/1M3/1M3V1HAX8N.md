Let $K$ be the intersection of diagonals of cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, where $|AB|=|BC|$, $|BK|=b$, and $|DK|=d$. What is $|AB|$?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ \sqrt{d^2 + bd}
\textbf{(B)}\ \sqrt{b^2+bd}
\textbf{(C)}\ \sqrt{2bd}
\textbf{(D)}\ \sqrt{2(b^2+d^2-bd)}
\textbf{(E)}\ \sqrt{bd}
$

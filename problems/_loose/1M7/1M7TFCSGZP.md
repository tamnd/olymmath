Given $C$,

In the Pythagorean multiplication table, a rectangular frame one cell thick is highlighted, with each side of the frame consisting of an odd number of cells. The cells of the frame are alternately colored in two colors - black and white. Prove that the sum of the numbers in the black cells is equal to the sum of the numbers in the white cells.

The Pythagorean multiplication table is a grid where the intersection of the $m$-th row and the $n$-th column contains the number $m n$ (for any natural numbers $m$ and $n$).

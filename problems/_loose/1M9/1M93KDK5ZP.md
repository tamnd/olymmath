$$
\begin{gathered}
a x^{2}+b x+c=0 \\
a^{\prime} x^{2}+b^{\prime} x+c^{\prime}=0 \\
\left(a+\lambda a^{\prime}\right) x^{2}+\left(b+\lambda b^{\prime}\right) x+\left(c+\lambda c^{\prime}\right)=0
\end{gathered}
$$

Let the sum of the roots of these equations be denoted by $s, s^{\prime}$, and $S$, and the product of the roots by $p, p^{\prime}$, and $P$. Determine $\lambda$ such that

$$
P=p+p^{\prime}
$$

and express $S$ as a function of $s, s^{\prime}, p$, and $p^{\prime}$.

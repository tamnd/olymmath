A positive integer, if each of its prime factors is at least double (i.e., in the factorization of this number, the exponent of each prime factor is no less than 2), is called a "beautiful number." Two consecutive positive integers that are both "beautiful numbers" are called a pair of "twin beautiful numbers," for example, 8 and 9 are a pair of "twin beautiful numbers." Prove that there are infinitely many pairs of twin beautiful numbers.

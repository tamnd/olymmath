Given the square $A B C D$. The diagonals of the square intersect at point $S$ and $P$ is the midpoint of side $A B$. Let $M$ be the intersection point of $A C$ and $P D$ and $N$ be the intersection point of $B D$ and $P C$. In the quadrilateral $P M S N$, a circle is inscribed. Prove that the radius of this circle is equal to $\overline{M P}-\overline{M S}$.

Let $m$ be any positive integer not less than 3.
1. Given a circle and a set $V_{m}$ of $m$ points equally dividing the circumference of the circle. Consider non-equilateral triangles with vertices from $V_{m}$. How many non-congruent triangles meet the above requirements?
2. Divide $m$ identical items into several piles (the order of the piles does not matter). How many ways are there to do this?

Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree 2012 with real coefficients, such that for all real numbers $a, b, c$ for which $a+b+c=0$, the following holds:

$$
P(a)^{3}+P(b)^{3}+P(c)^{3} \geqslant 3 P(a) P(b) P(c)
$$

Can the polynomial $P(x)$ have exactly 2012 distinct real roots?

(Miloš Milosavljević)

Inside a circle of radius $R$, in which an equilateral triangle $ABC$ is inscribed, a point $P$ is given. Prove that between the area $S_{1}$ of the triangle constructed on the segments $PA, PB, PC$, and the distance $d$ from the point $P$ to the center of the circle, the following relationship holds: $d^{2}+\frac{4 S_{1}}{\sqrt{3}}=R^{2}$.

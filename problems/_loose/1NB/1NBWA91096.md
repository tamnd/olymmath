Given a pile with 2010 tokens. We remove one token from the pile, and divide the remainder arbitrarily into two piles. Next, we choose one of the resulting two piles, remove one token, and divide the remainder arbitrarily into two piles, etc. Is it possible, after a finite number of repetitions of this procedure, to end up with several piles such that each of them contains exactly three tokens?

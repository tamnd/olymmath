a) Prove that the planes passing through the bisectors of the faces of a trihedral angle $SABC$ and perpendicular to the planes of these faces intersect along a single line, and that this line is defined by the vector $[\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}]+[\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c}]+[\mathbf{c}, \mathbf{a}]$, where $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$, and $\mathbf{c}$ are unit vectors directed along the edges $SA, SB$, and $SC$.

b) On the edges of a trihedral angle with vertex $O$, points $A_{1}, A_{2}$, and $A_{3}$ are taken such that $OA_{1}=OA_{2}=OA_{3}$. Prove that the bisector planes of its dihedral angles intersect along a line defined by the vector $\overrightarrow{OA}_{1} \sin \alpha_{1}+\overrightarrow{OA}_{2} \sin \alpha_{2}+\overrightarrow{OA}_{3} \sin \alpha_{3}$, where $\alpha_{i}$ is the measure of the plane angle opposite the edge $OA_{i}$.

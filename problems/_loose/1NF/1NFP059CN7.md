(Jury, France, 82). Prove that for any positive numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ the following inequality holds:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt[k]{a_{1} \ldots a_{k}} \leqslant e \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}
$$

where $e$ is the base of the natural logarithms.

Jorge, who lives in Recife, corresponds with his English friend Ralph who lives in England. The two understand each other very well in both languages, but they have a problem with dates: the date $08 / 10$ in Brazil means October 8, and in England August 10. Because of this, the two agreed not to write to each other on days when the date is ambiguous. They prefer dates like $25 / 03$ which can only mean March 25.

(a) On which of the following dates can Jorge and Ralph not write to each other?
(i) December 3
(ii) August 18
(iii) May 5

(b) When does the longest period occur during which the two friends cannot write to each other?

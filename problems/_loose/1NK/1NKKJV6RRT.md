During a boring class, Anna passes the time by writing down integers one below the other. Starting from a given number, in the next row she either writes the sum of the digits of the previous number or their product. Continuing this process, she noticed that every number she wrote down was odd. How many starting values of at most six digits are there for which it is true that every number written down is odd?

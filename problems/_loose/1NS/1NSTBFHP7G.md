Given a circle $k$, a point $O$ that does not lie on it, and a line $p$ that does not intersect it. Consider any circle $l$ that has an external tangency with circle $k$ and touches line $p$. Let the points of tangency be $A$ and $B$. If points $O, A, B$ do not lie on a straight line, we construct a circle $m$ circumscribed around triangle $OAB$. Prove that all such circles $m$ pass through a common point different from point $O$, or touch the same line.

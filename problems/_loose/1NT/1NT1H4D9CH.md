Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients such that $P(12)=20$ and $$ (x-1) \cdot P(16x)= (8x-1) \cdot P(8x) $$ holds for all real numbers $x$ . Compute the remainder when $P(2014)$ is divided by $1000$ .

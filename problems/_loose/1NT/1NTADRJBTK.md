Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n-1}, a_{0}$ be the lengths of the sides $A_{1} A_{2}$, $A_{2} A_{8}$, $A_{8} A_{4}$, $\ldots$, $A_{n} A_{1}$ of the n-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{8} A_{3} \ldots A_{n}$ inscribed in the circle $S$, and $p_{2}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{n-1}, p_{0}$ be the distances from an arbitrary point $M$ on the arc $A_{1} A_{n}$ of the circle to the corresponding sides. Prove that

$$
\frac{a_{0}}{p_{0}}=\frac{a_{1}}{p_{1}}+\frac{a_{2}}{p_{2}}+\ldots+\frac{a_{n-1}}{p_{n-1}}
$$

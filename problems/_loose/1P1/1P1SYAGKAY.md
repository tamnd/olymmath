A number is written at each vertex of a cube. In one move, each number is replaced by the arithmetic mean of the numbers written at its three neighboring vertices (the replacement happens simultaneously). On the tenth move, each vertex of the cube again has the number that was originally written there. Does it follow that all the numbers are equal?

Let triangle DABC be such that $A B=1 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $A C=2015 \mathrm{~cm}$. Let $\mathrm{M}$ be a point in the plane of triangle DABC such that:

$$
\overrightarrow{r_{M}}=\frac{1007}{2015} \overrightarrow{r_{A}}+\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{r_{B}}+\frac{1}{4030} \overrightarrow{r_{C}}
$$

Prove that $A M \perp B M$.

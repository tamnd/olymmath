Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct prime numbers, and $n$ a positive integer. Let $f(n)$ denote the number of positive integers that are less than or equal to the product $pq n$, and have a common divisor with $pq$. Show that $\frac{f(n)}{n}$ cannot be equal to 1990.

On the circumference of $\odot O$, take $n$ (integer $n \geqslant 3$) points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$. Among the $\mathrm{C}_{n}^{2}$ angles $\angle A_{i} O A_{j}$ $(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)$, there are $S(n)$ angles that are no greater than $120^{\circ}$. Prove:
(1) For $k=1,2, \cdots$, when
$$
2 \mathrm{C}_{k}^{2}<S(n) \leqslant \mathrm{C}_{k}^{2}+\mathrm{C}_{k+1}^{2}
$$

$n_{\text {min }}=2 k+1$;
(2) For $k=2,3, \cdots$, when
$\mathrm{C}_{k-1}^{2}+\mathrm{C}_{k}^{2}<S(n) \leqslant 2 \mathrm{C}_{k}^{2}$

$n_{\text {min }}=2 k$.

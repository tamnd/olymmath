Fifu and Gian play a game of constructing a sequence of numbers as follows:
1. On the first move, Fifu choose $1$ as the first term of the sequence.
2. Gian and Fifu take turns choosing the next term of the sequence.
3. On the $n$ -th turn, the player whose turn can choose any positive integer less than or equal to $n$ as the $n$ -th term of the sequence.
4. The game ends when the sum of all terms in the sequence is greater than or equal to $2025$ . The player who chooses the last term of the sequence wins the game.

Prove that Fifu has a winning strategy.

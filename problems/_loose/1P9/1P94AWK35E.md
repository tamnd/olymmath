Let the functions $C(x)$ and $S(x)$ be defined as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& C(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{lll}
1-x & \text { if } & 0 \leq x \leq 2 \\
x-3 & \text { if } & 2<x \leq 4
\end{array}\right. \\
& C(x)=C(x+4), S(x)=C(x-1)
\end{aligned}
$$

Are the following equations true?

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathrm{C}(2 \mathrm{x})=[\mathrm{C}(\mathrm{x})]^{2}-[S(x)]^{2} \\
S(2 x)=2 C(x) S(x)
\end{gathered}
$$

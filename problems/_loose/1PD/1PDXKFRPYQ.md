Four. (50 points) Each face of a convex polyhedron is a triangle, and each edge is marked with one of the letters $a$, $b$, or $c$, such that each face has exactly one $a$, one $b$, and one $c$. For each face, when the polyhedron is rotated so that the face is in front of us, if the three edges are observed in the order $a$, $b$, $c$ in a counterclockwise direction, it is called a front face; otherwise, it is called a back face. Prove: the difference between the number of front faces and back faces is divisible by 4.

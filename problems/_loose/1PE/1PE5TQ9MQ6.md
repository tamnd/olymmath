8 Let $n$ be a positive integer, and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}, c_{2}, c_{3}, \cdots, c_{2n}$ be $4n-1$ real numbers such that $c_{i-j}^{2} \geqslant a_{i} b_{j}, 1 \leqslant i, j \leqslant n$.
$$
\text { Let } \begin{aligned}
m= & \max _{\therefore} c_{i} \text {, prove that } \\
& \left(\frac{m+c_{2}+c_{3}+\cdots+c_{2 n}}{2 n}\right)^{2} \\
\geqslant & \left(\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}}{n}\right)\left(\frac{b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}}{n}\right) .
\end{aligned}
$$

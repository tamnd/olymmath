Given the ellipse $E: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+y^{2}=1$, $A$ is the right vertex of the ellipse, $BC$ is a chord of the ellipse $E$ that does not coincide with the $x$-axis and passes through the origin $O$, and the lines $AB$ and $AC$ intersect the $y$-axis at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively.
(1) Prove: The circle $\Gamma$ with diameter $PQ$ passes through at least one fixed point;
(2) Let the intersection points of circle $\Gamma$ with the $x$-axis be $M$ and $N$ (point $M$ is on the negative half-axis, and $N$ is on the positive half-axis), find the equation of the locus of the intersection point $T$ of the lines $PM$ and $QN$.

Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the bisectors $l_{A}, l_{B}, l_{C}, l_{D}$ of the external angles of this quadrilateral are drawn. The lines $l_{A}$ and $l_{B}$ intersect at point $K$, the lines $l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$ at point $L$, the lines $l_{C}$ and $l_{D}$ at point $M$, and the lines $l_{D}$ and $l_{A}$ at point $N$. Prove that if the circumcircles of triangles $ABK$ and $CDM$ touch externally, then the circumcircles of triangles $BCL$ and $DAN$ also touch externally.

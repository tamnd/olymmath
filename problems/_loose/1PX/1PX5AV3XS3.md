An infinite sequence $(a_n)$ , $n\in N$ , of positive numbers, is called *lacunary*, if there exists a number $q > 1$ such that $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\ge q$ , and is called *rare* if there exists a natural number $k$ such that for all positive $x$ the interval $(x, 2x)$ contains no more than $k$ terms of this sequence.
a) Is it true that every lacunary sequence is rare?
b) Is it true that every rare increasing sequence is lacunary?

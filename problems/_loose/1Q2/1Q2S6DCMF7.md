Given a triangle $A B C$. Through a point $X$ lying inside it, segments $c_{X}$, parallel to $A B$, with endpoints on sides $A C$ and $B C$, and segment $b_{X}$, parallel to $A C$, with endpoints on sides $A B$ and $C B$ are drawn. Prove that all points $X$ for which the lengths of segments $b_{X}$ and $c_{X}$ are equal lie on one straight line.

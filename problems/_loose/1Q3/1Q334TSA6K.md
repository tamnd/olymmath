Let $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ and $a_{n}=\frac{2 n-3}{2 n} a_{n-1}$ for all $n \geq 2$. Prove that

$$
s_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}<1
$$

for every natural number $n$.

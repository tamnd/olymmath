Let $n$ be an odd natural number greater than 3. Let $k$ be the smallest natural number such that $k n + 1$ is a perfect square, and let $l$ be the smallest natural number such that $l n$ is a perfect square. Prove that the number $n$ is prime if and only if $k > \frac{1}{4} n$ and $l > \frac{1}{4} n$.

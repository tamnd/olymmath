Find all polynomials $P_n(x)$ of the form
$$P_n(x)=n!x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\ldots+a_1x+(-1)^nn(n+1),$$with integer coefficients, such that its roots $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ satisfy $k\le x_k\le k+1$ for $k=1,2,\ldots,n$.

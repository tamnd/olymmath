Let $p$ be a prime. We say $p$ is *good* if and only if for any positive integer $a,b,$ such that $$a\equiv b (\textup{mod}p)\Leftrightarrow a^3\equiv b^3 (\textup{mod}p).$$Prove that
(1)There are infinite primes $p$ which are *good*;
(2)There are infinite primes $p$ which are not *good*.

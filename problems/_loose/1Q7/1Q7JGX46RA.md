You are interviewing three forest inhabitants A, B, C. It is known that exactly one of them is a werewolf. In conversation with you, they state:

A: C is the werewolf.

B: I am not the werewolf.

C: At least two of us are liars.

Our task consists of two parts.

a) Is the werewolf a knight or a liar?

b) If you had to choose one of the three forest inhabitants as a travel companion and the question of whether your chosen one would turn out to be a werewolf was more concerning to you than whether they would turn out to be a liar, whom among the three would you choose?

Consider all possible subsets of the set $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$ that do not contain adjacent natural numbers. Prove that the sum of the squares of the products of the numbers in these subsets is equal to $(n+1)!-1$. For example, when $n=3$, we have $1^{2}+2^{2}+3^{2}+(1 \cdot 3)^{2}=23=4!-1$.

$$
\begin{array}{l}
\cos ^{2} \theta_{1} \cos ^{2} \theta_{2}-\left(\sin \theta_{1} \sin \theta_{2}-x\right)^{2} \geqslant 0, \\
\cos ^{2} \theta_{3} \cos ^{2} \theta_{4}-\left(\sin \theta_{3} \sin \theta_{4}-x\right)^{2} \geqslant 0
\end{array}
$$

The necessary and sufficient condition for the above two inequalities to hold simultaneously is $\sum_{i=1}^{4} \sin ^{2} \theta_{i} \leqslant 2\left(1+\prod_{i=1}^{4} \sin \theta_{i}+\prod_{i=1}^{4} \cos \theta_{i}\right)$.

In a tournament, every two players play two matches of a new interesting game against each other. Before each match, it is randomly decided who plays first. A match cannot end in a draw; the winner gets 1 point, and the loser gets 0 points.

At the end of the tournament, all participants had different numbers of points. However, the organizers concluded that the rules were unfair. Therefore, they added 1 point for each win achieved by a player who did not play first.

Can all participants have the same number of points according to the new scoring system?

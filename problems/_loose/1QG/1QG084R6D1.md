Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers such that $x^2+y^2+z^2=3$. Prove that $$\frac{x+1}{z+x+1}+\frac{y+1}{x+y+1}+\frac{z+1}{y+z+1}\geq\frac{(xy+yz+zx+\sqrt{xyz})^2}{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}.$$

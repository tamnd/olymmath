a) A calculator from the country of Cincolândia has only the digits from 0 to 9 and two buttons $\square$ and $\triangle$. The button $\square$ squares the number on the display of the calculator. The button $\triangle$ subtracts 5 from the number on the display of the calculator. Mônica types the number 7 and then presses $\square$ and, subsequently, presses the button $\triangle$. What is the result shown by the calculator?

b) Show that if a natural number $x$ leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 5, then the number $x^{2}$ leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 5.

c) On the calculator of Cincolândia, is it possible to type the number 9 and then reach the result 7 by pressing the buttons $\square$ or $\triangle$ in an appropriate manner?

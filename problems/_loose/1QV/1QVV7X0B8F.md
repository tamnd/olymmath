The hour hand of a circular clock face (with the numbers $1, 2, \cdots, 12$ written around the circumference) is fixed to a blackboard at its center, allowing the clock face to rotate by any integer multiple of $30^{\circ}$. Initially, a zero is written on the blackboard at each position corresponding to a number on the clock face. The clock face is then rotated, and each time it stops, the number on the clock face is added to the number on the blackboard at the corresponding position.
Can it be possible, after several rotations, for all the numbers on the blackboard to be 1984?

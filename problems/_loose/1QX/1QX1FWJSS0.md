Let $k$ be a positive integer. Prove that:
(a) If $k=m+2mn+n$ for some positive integers $m,n$ , then $2k+1$ is composite.
(b) If $2k+1$ is composite, then there exist positive integers $m,n$ such that $k=m+2mn+n$ .

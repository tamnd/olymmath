Given is a triangle $A B C$. Circle $k_{1}$ passes through points $A$ and $B$ and is tangent to line $A C$, while circle $k_{2}$ passes through points $A$ and $C$ and is tangent to line $A B$. Circle $k_{1}$ intersects line $B C$ at point $D(D \neq B)$ and intersects circle $k_{2}$ at point $E(E \neq A)$. Prove that line $D E$ bisects segment $A C$.

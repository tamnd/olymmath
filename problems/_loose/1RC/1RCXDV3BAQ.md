The starting point for the jumps is at point $A$ on the line $L$, the length of each jump is $h$, and the direction of each jump is chosen randomly and equally likely. Find the probability that, after making from three to nine random jumps, Kuzya will be at a distance of $4h$ from $A$ at least once.

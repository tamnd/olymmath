a) Prove that if an integer is not divisible by 5, then its square has the form $5k + 1$ or $5k - 1$.

b) Prove that if an integer $n$ is not divisible by 5, then the number $n^4 - 1$ is divisible by 5.

c) Prove that the fifth power of an integer ends with the same digit as the integer itself.

d) Prove that for any integer $n$, the number $n^5 - n$ is divisible by 30.

e) Let $k$ and $n$ be integers not divisible by five. Prove that the number $k^4 - n^4$ is divisible by five.

f) Prove that if integers $k, m, n$ are related by the equation $k^2 + m^2 = n^2$, then at least one of them is divisible by 5.

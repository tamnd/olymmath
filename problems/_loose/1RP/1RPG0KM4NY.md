A cube is divided into rectangular parallelepipeds such that for any two parallelepipeds, their projections onto some face of the cube overlap (i.e., intersect in a figure of non-zero area). Prove that for any three parallelepipeds, there exists a face of the cube such that the projections of each pair of them onto this face do not overlap.

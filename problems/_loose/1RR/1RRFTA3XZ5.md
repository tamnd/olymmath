Let $k$ be an integer greater than 2, and let the integer sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots$ satisfy $a_{0}=0$, and for any non-negative integer $i$, we have $a_{i+2}=k a_{i+1}-a_{i}$. Prove: For any positive integer $m$, the number $(2 m)$! divides $a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \cdots a_{3 m}$.

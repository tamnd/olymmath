Let the set $X=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$. If the number of odd numbers in a non-empty subset $A$ of $X$ is greater than the number of even numbers, then $A$ is called an odd subset. Try to find:
(1) The number of odd subsets of $X$;
(2) The total sum of the elements of all odd subsets of $X$.

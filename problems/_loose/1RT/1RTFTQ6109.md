For $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ being real numbers. For $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$, let $\sigma_{k}=\sum_{1 \leqslant i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{k} \leqslant n} x_{i_{1}} x_{i_{2}} \cdots x_{i_{k}}$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(x_{k}^{2}+1\right) \\
\geqslant 2\left|\sum_{k=0}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]}(-1)^{k} \sigma_{2 k}\right|\left|\sum_{k=0}^{\left[\frac{n-1}{2}\right]}(-1)^{k} \sigma_{2 k+1}\right|,
\end{array}
$$

where, $\sigma_{0}=1,[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$.

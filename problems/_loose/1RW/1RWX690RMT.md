On the board, the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 17$ are written. We erase the numbers one by one, and we do this by choosing any number $k$ from the remaining numbers and erasing all numbers on the board that divide $k+17$. Prove that by repeating this procedure, we cannot erase all the numbers.

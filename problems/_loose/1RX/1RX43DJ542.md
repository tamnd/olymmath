Given the parabola $y=-2 x^{2}+x-\frac{1}{8}$ and the point $A\left(\frac{1}{4}, \frac{11}{8}\right)$. Draw any line through the point $F\left(\frac{1}{4},-\frac{1}{8}\right)$, intersecting the parabola at points $B$ and $C$.
(1) Find the equation of the centroid trajectory of $\triangle A B C$, and express it in the form $y=f(x)$;
(2) If the sequence $\left\{x_{k}\right\}, 0<x_{1}<\frac{1}{2}$, satisfies $x_{k+1}=f\left(x_{k}\right)$. Prove: $\sum_{k=1}^{n} x_{k+1}^{k}<\frac{3}{5}$.

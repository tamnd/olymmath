Let $m$ be a natural number. Calculate $\sum_{k=1}^{m} \frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \frac{k \pi}{2 m+1}}$ and $\sum_{k=1}^{m} \cot ^{2} \frac{k \pi}{2 m+1}$.

Deduce $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k^{2}}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}$. (Use $\cot \theta \leqslant \frac{1}{\theta} \leqslant \frac{1}{\sin \theta}$ when $0<\theta<\frac{\pi}{2}$.)

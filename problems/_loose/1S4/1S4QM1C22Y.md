A square $n \times n$ is divided into unit squares. It is necessary to place a certain number of isosceles right triangles with hypotenuse 2, with vertices at the vertices of the unit squares, in such a way that each side of each unit square belongs to exactly one triangle (i.e., lies in the interior or on the boundary). Determine all values of $n$ for which this is possible.

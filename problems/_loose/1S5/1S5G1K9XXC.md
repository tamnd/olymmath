Easter Problem ${ }^{3}$. (From 6th grade, 2 points) Two players are playing an "Easter egg fight." In front of them is a large basket of eggs. They randomly take an egg each and hit them against each other. One of the eggs breaks, the loser takes a new egg, while the winner keeps their egg for the next round (the outcome of each round depends only on which egg has the stronger shell; the winning egg retains its strength). It is known that the first player won the first ten rounds. What is the probability that he will win the next round as well?

A three-digit natural number is called "symmetric" if and only if its hundreds digit is equal to its units digit.

a) Form all those three-digit symmetric numbers in which only the digits $0,1,2$ appear (each possibly multiple times or not at all)! No justification is required.

b) Form all those three-digit symmetric numbers that are divisible by 6 and in which only the digits 2, 5, 8 appear! Prove that exactly the numbers you specify meet the given conditions!

c) Determine the number of all three-digit symmetric numbers!

d) Determine the sum of all three-digit symmetric numbers!

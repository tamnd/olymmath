Proof: The product of two consecutive triangular numbers cannot be a square number. However, for each triangular number $t_{n}=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, there exist infinitely many triangular numbers $t_{m}$ greater than $t_{n}$ such that $t_{n} \cdot t_{m}$ is a square number.

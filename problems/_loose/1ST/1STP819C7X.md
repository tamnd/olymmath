Let $n$ be a positive integer. Each point $(x, y)$ in the plane, where $x$ and $y$ are nonnegative integers with $x+y=n$, is colored red or blue, subject to the following condition: If a point $(x, y)$ is red, then so are all points $\left(x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}\right)$ with $x^{\prime} \leq x$ and $y^{\prime} \leq y$. Let $A$ be the number of ways to choose $n$ blue points with distinct $x$-coordinates, and let $B$ be the number of ways to choose $n$ blue points with distinct $y$-coordinates. Prowe that $A=B$.

Let $n$ be a positive integer and consider a square with dimensions $2^{n} \times 2^{n}$. We cover this square with a number of (at least 2) non-overlapping rectangles, such that each rectangle has integer dimensions and a power of two as its area. Prove that two of the rectangles in the covering have the same dimensions. (Two rectangles have the same dimensions if they have the same width and the same height, where they are not allowed to be rotated.)

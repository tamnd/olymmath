In parallelogram $A B C D$, it holds that $|A B|=|B D|$. Let $K$ be a point on line $A B$, different from $A$, such that $|K D|=|A D|$. The mirror image of point $C$ when reflected over point $K$ is denoted by $M$, and the mirror image of point $B$ when reflected over point $A$ is denoted by $N$. Prove that triangle $M D N$ is isosceles with the vertex at $D$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and denote $H_{B}$ and $H_{C}$ as the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$. Let $X$ be any point on the line $\left(C H_{C}\right)$, the line $(B X)$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at $Y$, the line $(C Y)$ intersects the line $\left(B H_{B}\right)$ at $Z$. Show that the midpoint of the segment $[X Z]$ lies on the line $\left(H_{B} H_{C}\right)$.

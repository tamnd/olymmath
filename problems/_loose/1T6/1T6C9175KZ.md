The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ of the quadrilateral $A B C D$, inscribed in a circle, intersect at point $P$. It is known that the distances from point $P$ to the sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$ are $4, \sqrt{3}, \frac{8}{\sqrt{19}}$ and $8 \sqrt{\frac{3}{19}}$ respectively (the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from point $P$ to the sides lie on these sides).
a) Find the ratio $A P: P C$.

b) Find the length of the diagonal $B D$, if it is additionally known that $A C=10$.

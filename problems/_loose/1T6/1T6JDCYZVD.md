In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $O$ is the circumcenter. Draw a perpendicular line to $AO$ through point $O$, intersecting sides $AB$ and $AC$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively. Prove:
(1) The circumcircles of $\triangle ABC$, $\triangle ODB$, and $\triangle OEC$ pass through the same point $K$;
(2) If the extension of $CO$ intersects $\odot O$ at point $F$, and $ED$ is extended to intersect $FK$ at point $G$, then $\triangle EKG$ is an isosceles triangle.

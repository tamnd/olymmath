(MMSC, Finland, 80). The sequence of numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ is defined as follows:

$$
a_{0}=1 / 2, a_{k}=a_{k-1}+(1 / n) a_{k-1}^{2} (k=1, \ldots, n)
$$

Prove that $1-1 / n<a_{n}<1$.

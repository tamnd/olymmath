One of the four gnomes, Anne, Bert, Chris, and Dirk, has stolen gold from the king. Each of the gnomes, who know each other inside and out, makes two statements. If a gnome is a liar, at least one of those two statements is a lie. If a gnome is not a liar, both statements are true.

Anne says: "Bert is a liar." and "Chris or Dirk did it."

Bert says: "Chris is a liar." and "Dirk or Anne did it."

Chris says: "Dirk is a liar." and "Anne or Bert did it."

Dirk says: "Anne is a liar." and "Bert or Chris did it."

How many of these eight statements are true?

(Tsinghua University Independent Admission Mathematics Test 2006) Given that $f(x)$ satisfies: for real numbers $a, b$ there is $f(a \cdot b) = a f(b) + b f(a)$, and $|f(x)| \leqslant 1$, prove that $f(x)$ is always zero. (You may use the following conclusion: If $\lim g(x)=0, |f(x)| \leqslant M$, where $M$ is a constant, then $\lim (f(x) \cdot g(x))=0$.)

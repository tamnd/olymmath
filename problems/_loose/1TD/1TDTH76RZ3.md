Given a positive integer $n$ , let $p(n)$ be the number of distinct primes which divide $n$ . An integer $n$ is said to be *balanced* if $p(n)$ is even, otherwise it is said to be *unbalanced*. Prove that there exist an infinite number of pairs of consecutive positive integers such that they are both balanced or both unbalanced.

Flying by, the Crow cawed, "There are more green ones among you than multicolored ones!" - "Yes!" - 50 parakeets agreed, and the rest shouted "No!" The green parakeets told the truth both times, the yellow ones lied both times, and each of the multicolored ones lied once and told the truth once.

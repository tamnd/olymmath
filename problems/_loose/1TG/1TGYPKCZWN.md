In one container, there is $a$ liter of wine. In another, there is $a$ liter of water. From the container with water, $b$ liter ($b$ is less than $a$) of water is poured into the wine and mixed thoroughly. Then, $b$ liter of the mixture is poured back into the water.

a.) In which container is there more "foreign" liquid?

b.) How does the result change if we do not wait for the complete mixing of the two liquids when pouring back, for example, if we pour back $b$ liter from the denser part of the wine-water mixture into the water?

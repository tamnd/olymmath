8・166 Let $m, n, k \in N$, and there are 4 glasses with capacities of $m, n, k$, and $m+n+k$ liters, respectively. The allowed operations are: pouring all the wine from one glass into another or pouring until the other glass is full. Initially, the large glass is full of wine while the other 3 glasses are empty. What is the necessary and sufficient condition for $m, n, k$ such that for any $s \in N, s<m+n+k$, it is possible to perform a series of operations to have exactly $s$ liters of wine in one of the glasses?

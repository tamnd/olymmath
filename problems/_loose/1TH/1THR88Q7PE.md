In a non-obtuse triangle $ABC$, prove the inequality:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{(1-\cos 2 A)(1-\cos 2 B)}{1-\cos 2 C}+\frac{(1-\cos 2 C)(1-\cos 2 A)}{1-\cos 2 B}+ \\
\left(\frac{(1-\cos 2 B)(1-\cos 2 C)}{1-\cos 2 A} \geqslant \frac{9}{2}\right.
\end{array}
$$

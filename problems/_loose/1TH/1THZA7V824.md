The sides of a square are sequentially numbered with the numbers $1,2,3,4$. For an arbitrary point $A$ and side $k$, denote by $A_{k}$ the point symmetric to the projection of $A$ onto the line $k$ relative to the point $A$. Find all points $A$ such that each of the points $A_{1}, A_{12}, A_{123}, A_{1234}, A_{12341}, \ldots$ lies inside our square.

$$
3(a+139)+b=3a^2-2016
$$

a) Determine the numbers $a$ and $b$;

b) Write the number $a^{29b}$ as a sum of three distinct, non-zero perfect squares;

c) Write the number $a^b$ as a sum of 29 consecutive natural numbers.

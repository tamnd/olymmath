Find all polynomials of the form $P(x) = (-1)^nx^n + a_1x^{n-1} + a_2x^{n-2} + ...+ a_{n-1}x + a_n$ with the following two properties:
(i) $\{a_1, a_2, . . . , a_n-1, a_n\} =\{0, 1\}$ , and
(ii) all roots of $P(x)$ are distinct real numbers

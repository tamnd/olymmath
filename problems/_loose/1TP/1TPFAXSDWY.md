Given an arithmetic sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with a common difference of $d$ satisfying $d>0$, and $a_{2}$ is the geometric mean of $a_{1}$ and $a_{4}$. Let $b_{n}=a_{2^{*}}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$, for any positive integer $n$ we have
$$
\frac{1}{b_{1}}+\frac{1}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{b_{n}}<2 \text {. }
$$

Then the range of the common difference $d$ is ( ).
(A) $\left[\frac{1}{2},+\infty\right)$
(B) $\left(\frac{1}{2},+\infty\right)$
(C) $\left[\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}\right)$
(D) $\left[\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}\right]$

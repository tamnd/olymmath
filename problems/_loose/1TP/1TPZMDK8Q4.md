On the coordinate plane, consider a figure $M$ consisting of all points with coordinates $(x ; y)$ that satisfy the system of inequalities

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
|x|+|4-x| \leqslant 4 \\
\frac{x^{2}-4 x-2 y+2}{y-x+3} \geqslant 0
\end{array}\right.
$$

Sketch the figure $M$ and find its area.

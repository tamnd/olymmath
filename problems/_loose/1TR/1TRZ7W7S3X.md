At the vertices of a regular $2 n$-gon, empty cups are placed. Petya and Vasya take turns (starting with Petya) pouring tea into the cups. In one move, a player can pour tea into either one empty cup or two cups that are symmetric with respect to the center of the $2 n$-gon, provided both are empty. The player who cannot make a move loses. For which $p$ will Petya win regardless of Vasya's actions?

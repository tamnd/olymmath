Through the vertex $A$ of the parallelogram $A B C D$, a secant is drawn, intersecting the diagonal $B D$ and the side $C D$ at points $P, Q$ respectively. Prove that if $\frac{D Q}{Q C}=\frac{1}{k}$, then $\frac{P D}{P B}=\frac{1}{k+1}$.

The vertex of angle $\alpha$ is at point $O, A$ - a fixed point inside the angle. Points $M$ and $N$ are taken on the sides of the angle such that $\angle M A N=\beta(\alpha+\beta<\pi)$. Prove that if $|A M|=|A N|$, then the area of quadrilateral $O M A N$ reaches a maximum (among all possible quadrilaterals obtained by varying $M$ and $N$).

A strip is called a set of points in the plane lying between two parallel lines, i.e., belonging to segments connecting points of one of the parallel lines with points of the other; alternatively, a strip is defined as the non-empty intersection of two half-planes with different parallel boundaries.

a) Prove that the set of centers of symmetry of the strip is a line (the axis of the strip).

b) Obtain all the main properties of parallelograms (including rectangles, rhombuses, and squares) by considering a parallelogram as the intersection of two strips.

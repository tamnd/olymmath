Given an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB = AC$ and $\widehat{BAC} < 60^{\circ}$, let $D$ be the point on $AC$ such that $\widehat{DBC} = \widehat{BAC}$, let $E$ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of $BD$ with the line parallel to $BC$ passing through $A$, and let $F$ be the point on the line $AC$, on the side of $A$ relative to $C$, such that the length of $FA$ is twice the length of $AC$.

Finally, let $r$ be the perpendicular to $AB$ from $F$, $s$ be the perpendicular to $AC$ from $E$, and $t$ be the line $BD$. Prove that:

(a) the lines $EB$ and $AC$ are parallel;

(b) the lines $r$, $s$, and $t$ concur.

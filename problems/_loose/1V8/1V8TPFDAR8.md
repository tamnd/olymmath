In the plane, points $A, P, T$ are given, not lying on a straight line. Construct triangle $A B C$ such that $P$ is the foot of its altitude $z$ from vertex $A$ and $T$ is the point of tangency of side $A B$ with the inscribed circle. Discuss the number of solutions depending on the position of the given points.

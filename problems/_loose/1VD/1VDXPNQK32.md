Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle with $A C=B C$ and let $k$ be a circle with center $C$ and radius less than the altitude $C H, H \in A B$. Lines through $A$ and $B$ are tangent to $k$ at points $P$ and $Q$ lying on the same side of the line $C H$. Prove that the points $P, Q$ and $H$ are collinear.

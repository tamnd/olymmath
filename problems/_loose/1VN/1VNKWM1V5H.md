From a point $L$ two straight roads depart, forming an acute angle $\alpha$. Along one of the two roads there are two street lamps, positioned at $P$ and $Q$, such that $L P=40 \mathrm{~m}$ and $L Q=90 \mathrm{~m}$. Eva is at $E$ on the other road, and sees the two street lamps under an angle $P \widehat{E} Q$. How far from $L$ is Eva, if $P \widehat{E} Q$ has the maximum possible amplitude?
(A) $40 \mathrm{~m}$
(B) $60 \mathrm{~m}$
(C) $65 \mathrm{~m}$
(D) $90 \mathrm{~m}$
(E) the distance depends on $\alpha$.

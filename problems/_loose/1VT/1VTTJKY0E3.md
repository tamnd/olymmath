The Paradox of Three Coins ${ }^{5}$ (from 9th grade, 4 points). Mitya, Vanya, and Dima each flipped a coin $2 n$ times. It is known that Mitya and Vanya got the same number of heads. What is more likely: event $A$ "Dima got exactly $n$ heads" or event $B$ "Mitya got exactly $n$ heads"? If the events are not equally likely, by what factor is one of these events more likely than the other?

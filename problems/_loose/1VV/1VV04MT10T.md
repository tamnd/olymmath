From place $A$ to place $B$, two groups of tourists start simultaneously. The first group starts by bus, traveling at an average speed of $20 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{s}$ and reaches place $C$, which is halfway between $A$ and $B$, and then walks. The second group starts walking, and after 1 hour, they board a bus traveling at an average speed of $30 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. At place $C$, the first group arrives 35 minutes earlier than the second group, and at place $B$, the first group arrives 1 hour and 25 minutes later. How far apart are places $A$ and $B$, and what is the average walking speed of the groups, if the average walking speed of the first group is $1 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ greater than the average walking speed of the second group?

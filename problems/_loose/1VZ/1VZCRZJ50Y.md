$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{m}\right)^{m}>\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n} \text { and } \left(1+\frac{1}{m}\right)^{m+1}<\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1}
$$

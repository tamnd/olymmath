In a certain city, they have built a network for spreading rumors, in which every rumormonger exchanges information with three rumor-mongeresses, and every rumor-mongeress exchanges information with three rumormongers. Rumors spread no other way.

a) Prove that the number of rumormongers and rumor-mongeresses is the same.

b) Assume that the rumor-spreading network is connected (rumors from any rumormonger and any rumor-mongeress can reach all others). Prove that even if one rumormonger dies, the network remains connected.

Let $X$ be a non-degenerate non-negative random variable. Show that the equality

$$
\mathrm{E} X^{a} \mathrm{E} X^{b}=\mathrm{E} X^{c} \mathrm{E} X^{d},
$$

where $a+b=c+d$ and $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R}$, can only hold if the sets $\{a, b\}$ and $\{c, d\}$ coincide. (It is assumed that the considered mathematical expectations exist.)

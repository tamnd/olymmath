Let $\mathrm{A}$ be an $n(n \geqslant 3)$ element set. When $f(x)$ is a mapping from $A$ to $A$, define: $f^{2}(x)=f(f(x))$, $f^{i+1}(x)=f\left(f^{i}(x)\right), i=2,3, \cdots$. Find the number of mappings $f$ from $A$ to $A$ that satisfy the condition that $f^{n-2}(x)$ is a constant while $f^{n-3}(x)$ is not a constant.

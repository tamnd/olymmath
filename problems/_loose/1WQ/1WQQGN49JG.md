A plane intersects the sides of a spatial polygon $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ (or their extensions) at points $B_{1}, \ldots, B_{n}$; the point $B_{i}$ lies on the line $A_{i} A_{i+1}$. Prove that $\frac{A_{1} B_{1}}{A_{2} B_{1}} \cdot \frac{A_{2} B_{2}}{A_{3} B_{2}} \cdot \ldots \cdot \frac{A_{n} B_{n}}{A_{1} B_{n}}=1$, and that an even number of points $B_{i}$ lie on the sides of the polygon (and not on their extensions).

Consider the vertices of a regular $n$-corner and connect them somehow with sides or diagonals to a closed line that passes through each corner exactly once. A parallel pair is a set of two different parallel lines in this line. Show:

(a) If $n$ is even, then there is always at least one parallel pair.

(b) If $n$ is odd, then there is never exactly one parallel pair.

The volume of a cone is $A$, the volume of a cylinder is $M$, and the volume of a sphere is $C$. They have the same radius, and the height of the cone and the cylinder is equal to the diameter of the sphere. Then
(A) $A-M+C=0$.
(B) $A+M=C$.
(C) $2 A=M+C$.
(D) $A^{2}-M^{2}+C^{2}=0$.
(E) $2 A+2 M=3 C$.
(49th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1998)

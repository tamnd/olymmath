On a board, a sequence of n consecutive natural numbers in ascending order is written in a row. Under each of these numbers, a divisor is written, which is less than the number and greater than 1. It turns out that these divisors also form a sequence of consecutive natural numbers in ascending order. Prove that each of the original numbers is greater than $\frac{n^{k}}{p_{1} p_{2} \ldots p_{k}}$, where $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{k}$ are all prime numbers less than $n$. (S. Berlov)

In a flat world, there are two rectangular islands. The coastal waters of each island are considered to be the part of the sea that is no more than 50 km away from the shore. Can it happen that the area of the first island is larger than that of the second, while the area of the coastal waters of the second island is larger than that of the first? Assume that the nearest land to each island is more than 50 km away.

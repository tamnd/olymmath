Prove: Trigonometric identities:
(1) $\sin \frac{\pi}{2 n} \sin \frac{2 \pi}{2 n} \cdots \cdot \sin \frac{(n-1) \pi}{2 n}=\frac{\sqrt{n}}{2^{n-1}} (n \geqslant 2, n \in \mathbf{N})$;
(2) $\sin \frac{\pi}{4 n} \sin \frac{3 \pi}{4 n} \cdots \sin \frac{(2 n-1) \pi}{4 n}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2^{n}}(n \in \mathbf{N})$.

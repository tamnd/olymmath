Circles $k$ and $l$ intersect at points $P$ and $Q$. Let $A$ be an arbitrary point on circle $k$, different from $P$ and $Q$, and let lines $A P$ and $A Q$ intersect circle $l$ again at points $B$ and $C$, respectively. Prove that the line defined by the altitude from vertex $A$ in triangle $A B C$ passes through a fixed point that does not depend on the point $A$.

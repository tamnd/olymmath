(11th "Hope Cup" Senior High School 2nd Test) In the function $y=f(x)$, both $x$ and $f(x)$ are positive integers, and for any positive integer $x$, it holds that $f(x)+f(x+2) \leqslant 2 f(x+1), f(x)<2000$.
Prove: There exists a positive integer $m$, such that when $x \geqslant m$, $f(x)$ is a constant.

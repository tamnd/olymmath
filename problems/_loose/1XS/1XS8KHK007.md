In a triangle, one side is 2, the median on this side is 1, and the sum of the other two sides is $1+\sqrt{3}$. Then the area of this triangle is
(A) 1.
(B) $\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{3}$.
(C) $\sqrt{3}$.
(D) Cannot be determined.
(Tianjin, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

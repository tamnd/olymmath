Let $a, b, c, d$ be real numbers such that
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a^{2}+b^{2}+2 a-4 b+4=0 \\
c^{2}+d^{2}-4 c+4 d+4=0 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Let $m$ and $M$ be the minimum and the maximum values of $(a-c)^{2}+(b-d)^{2}$, respectively. What is $m \times M$ ?

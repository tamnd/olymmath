Let $S$ be the area of a given triangle, and $R$ the radius of the circle circumscribed about it. Further, let $S_{1}$ be the area of the triangle formed by the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from a point, which is at a distance $d$ from the center of the circumscribed circle, onto the sides of the given triangle. Prove that $S_{1}=\frac{S}{4}\left|1-\frac{d^{2}}{R^{2}}\right|$ (Euler).

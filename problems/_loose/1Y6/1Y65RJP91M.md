Let the set of matrices $\mathrm{M}=\left\{\mathrm{A} \in \mathcal{M} \mathcal{M}_{3}(\mathbb{Z}) / \mathrm{A}^{3}-\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{O}_{3}\right\}$.

a) Prove that if $A \in M$ and $\operatorname{det} A \neq 0$, then $A^{*} \in M$, where $A^{*}$ is the adjugate of matrix A.

b) Prove that the set $M$ has at least four elements.

c) Prove that if $A \in M$ and $\operatorname{det}\left(A-I_{3}\right) \neq 0$, then $\operatorname{det}\left(\mathrm{A}^{2}+\mathrm{A}+2 \cdot \mathrm{I}_{3}\right)=8$.

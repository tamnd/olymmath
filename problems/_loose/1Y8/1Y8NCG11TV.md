In the plane, there are circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with different radii. It needs to be proven that if the circle $k$ changes in such a way that it always externally touches both $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, then the line connecting the points of tangency $E_{1}$ and $E_{2}$ has a fixed point. The same holds if $k$ externally touches $k_{1}$ and internally touches $k_{2}$, as well as if $k$ internally touches $k_{1}$ and externally touches $k_{2}$, and finally, if $k$ internally touches both $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$. - How does the statement change if the radii of $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ are equal?

Choose five numbers from $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9$. Then
（1）there must be two numbers that are coprime;
（2）there must be one number that is a multiple of another;
（3）there must be one number whose double is a multiple of another.
Among the above conclusions, the number of correct ones is
(A) 0.
(B) 1.
(C) 2.
(D) 3.
(Chinese Jiangsu Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

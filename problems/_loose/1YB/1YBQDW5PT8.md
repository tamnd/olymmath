Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Suppose $P,Q$ are on lines $AB, AC$ (on the same side of A) with $AP=AC$ and $AB=AQ$.

Now suppose points $X,Y$ move along the sides $AB, AC$ of $ABC$ so that $XY || PQ$. Determine the locus of the circumcenters of the variable triangle $AXY$.

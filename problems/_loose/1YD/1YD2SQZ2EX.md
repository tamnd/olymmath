Christine has a deck of cards numbered from $1$ to $25$. She asked her friend Dorothy to choose six cards from the deck. Christine wrote down the chosen numbers and put the cards back in the deck. She then asked Dorothy to choose six cards again and, again, she wrote down the chosen numbers.

a. The first six numbers that Dorothy had chosen have the property that the difference between any two of the chosen numbers is a multiple of $4$ and only one of them is not a prime. Find the six numbers.

b. In the second time around, Dorothy chose the numbers in a way that for each pair of the numbers except one, one of the numbers divide the other. Find the largest of the six numbers.

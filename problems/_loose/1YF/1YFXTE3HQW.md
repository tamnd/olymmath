In $\triangle A B C$, let $M \in(A B)$ and $N \in(A C)$ with $[B M] \equiv[C N]$ and $[B N] \equiv[C M]$. Let $\{O\}=B N \cap C M$. Then:
a) $\triangle M O N$ is isosceles;
b) $[A O$ is the bisector of $\angle B A C$.

(Mathematics Olympiads and Competitions V-VIII 2014, Editura Bîrchi)

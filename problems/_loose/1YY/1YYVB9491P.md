a) The areas of all faces of a convex polyhedron are equal. Prove that the sum of the distances from its internal point to the planes of the faces does not depend on the position of the point.

b) The heights of a tetrahedron are $ h_{1}, h_{2}, h_{3} $ and $ h_{4} $; $ d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3} $ and $ d_{4} $ are the distances from an arbitrary internal point to the corresponding faces. Prove that

$$
\sum\left(d_{i} / h_{i}\right)=1
$$

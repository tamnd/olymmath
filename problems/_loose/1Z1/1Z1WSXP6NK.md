The cards from $n$ decks of 52 playing cards are put into boxes, which can each contain at most 2022 cards. A pile of boxes is said to be regular if it contains the same number of copies of each card. Show that there exists some $N$ such that, if $n \geq N$, then the boxes can be split into two nonempty regular piles.

Let $p$ be an odd positive integer. Find all values of the natural numbers $n\ge 2$ for which holds $$ \sum_{i=1}^{n} \prod_{j\ne i} (x_i-x_j)^p\ge 0 $$ where $x_1,x_2,..,x_n$ are any real numbers.

Find all four-digit numbers $n$ such that the following conditions are satisfied:

i) the number $n$ is the product of three different prime numbers;

ii) the sum of the two smallest of these prime numbers is equal to the difference of the two largest of them;

iii) the sum of all three prime numbers is equal to the square of another prime number.

(Radek Horenský)

For two natural numbers $a$ and $b$ , denote with $\overline{ab}$ the number when we write $a$ in front of $b$ . As example, if $a=20$ and $b=25$ , then $\overline{ab}=2025$ . Prove, that the equation $(a+b)^2 = \overline{ab}$ has infinitely many solutions in natural numbers.

**a)** Prove that not all functions $ f:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ that satisfy the equality
$$ f(x-1)+f(x+1) =\sqrt 5f(x) , \forall x\in\mathbb{R} , $$
are periodic.

**b)** Prove that that all functions $ g:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ that satisfy the equality
$$ g(x-1)+g(x+1)=\sqrt 3g(x) , \forall x\in\mathbb{R} , $$
are periodic.

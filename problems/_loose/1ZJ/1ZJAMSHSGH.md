Theorem of Sines [Ratio of areas of triangles with a common base or common height] Class

Through vertex $A$ of an equilateral triangle $A B C$, a line is drawn at an angle $\alpha\left(0<\alpha<\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ to $A C$, intersecting $B C$ at point $D$. Find the ratio of the area of triangle $A D C$ to the area of triangle $A B C$.

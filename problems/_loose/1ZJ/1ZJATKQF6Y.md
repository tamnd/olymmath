Think of a three-digit number: double its first digit (the hundreds place), add 3 to the resulting product, multiply the obtained sum by 5, and add the second digit of the thought number (the tens place) to the resulting product. Finally, append the last digit of the thought number to the right of the obtained sum. Prove that to get the thought number, it is sufficient to subtract 150 from the obtained result.

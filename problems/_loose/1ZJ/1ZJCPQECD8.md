Take a point $X$ inside or on the circumference of a circle. Draw two perpendicular chords $A C$ and $B D$ through point $X$ (when $X$ is on the circumference, a degenerate case is allowed, i.e., one chord is a diameter and the other chord shrinks to a point). For all possible choices of $X$, find the maximum and minimum values of $S=|A C|+|B D|$.

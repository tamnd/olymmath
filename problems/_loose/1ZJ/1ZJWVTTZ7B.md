Given positive numbers $a, b, c$ not less than 1. Prove:
$$
\frac{a+b+c}{4} \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{a b-1}}{b+c}+\frac{\sqrt{b c-1}}{c+a}+\frac{\sqrt{c a-1}}{a+b} .
$$

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$. On the circumscribed circle of triangle $AHB$ and the circumscribed circle of triangle $AHC$ respectively, points $P$ and $Q$, different from $H$, are defined , such that the three points $P, H$, and $Q$ lie on the same straight line in this order. Prove that there exists a certain circle $\omega$, and that the midpoint of the line segment $PQ$ is on $\omega$, regardless of how $P$ and $Q$ are taken.

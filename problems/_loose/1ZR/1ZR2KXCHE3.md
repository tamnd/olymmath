The incircle of $\triangle ABC$ touches $AB, BC, CA$ at $D, E, F$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $r=1 \Leftrightarrow AD + BE + CF = AD \cdot BE \cdot CF$;
(2) The area of the pedal triangle $\triangle D' E' F'$ of $\triangle DEF$ is $\frac{r^2 \Delta}{4 R^2}$;
(3) $C=\frac{\pi}{2} \Rightarrow AD \cdot DB = \Delta$.

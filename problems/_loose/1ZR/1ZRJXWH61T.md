a) Calculate: $S=\frac{1}{1 \sqrt{2}+2 \sqrt{1}}+\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{3}+3 \sqrt{2}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n \sqrt{n+1}+(n+1) \sqrt{n}}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$

b) Show that $\sqrt{1 \cdot 2}+\sqrt{2 \cdot 3}+\ldots+\sqrt{n(n+1)}<n(n+1), \forall n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

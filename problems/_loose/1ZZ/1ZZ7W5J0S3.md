A flower shop purchases a certain number of roses from a farm at a price of 5 yuan per stem every day, and then sells them at a price of 10 yuan per stem. If they are not sold on the same day, the remaining roses are treated as garbage.
(1) If the flower shop purchases 16 stems of roses one day, find the function expression of the profit $y$ (unit: yuan) of that day in terms of the demand $n$ (unit: stems, $n \in \mathbf{N}$).
(2) The flower shop recorded the daily demand for roses (unit: stems) for 100 days, and organized it into Table 1.

Using the frequency of each demand recorded over 100 days as the probability of each demand occurring.
(i) If the flower shop purchases 16 stems of roses one day, let $X$ represent the profit of that day (unit: yuan). Find the distribution of $X$, its expected value, and variance.
(ii) If the flower shop plans to purchase 16 or 17 stems of roses one day, do you think it should purchase 16 or 17 stems? Please explain your reasoning.

Given one hundred numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{100}$, the sum of which is 1. Each of the absolute values of the differences $x_{k+1}-x_{k}$ is less than $1 / 50$.

Prove that it is possible to select 50 of these numbers such that the sum of the selected numbers differs from one half by no more than one hundredth.

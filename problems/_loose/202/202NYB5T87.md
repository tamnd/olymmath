Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $a_{1}>0, a_{n+1}=a_{n}+\frac{n}{a_{n}}(n \geqslant 1)$, prove:
(1) The sequence $\left\{a_{n}-n\right\}(n \geqslant 2)$ is monotonically decreasing;
(2) There exists a constant $c$ such that
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{a_{k}-k}{k+1} \leqslant c, n \geqslant 2 .
$$

Given intersecting lines $a$ and $b$ and a plane $\alpha$, perpendicular to line $a$ and intersecting it at point $A$. Prove that the distance between lines $a$ and $b$ is equal to the distance from point $A$ to the orthogonal projection $b^{\prime}$ of line $b$ onto plane $\alpha$, and that the angle between lines $b$ and $b^{\prime}$ complements to $90^{\circ}$ the angle between lines $a$ and $b$.

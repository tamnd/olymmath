The cryptogram of a natural number $n$ is an ordered $n$-tuple $a=\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right)$ of numbers from $\mathbb{N}_{0}$ such that

$$
a_{1}+2 a_{2}+\cdots+n a_{n}=n
$$

Let $\mathcal{K}_{n}$ be the set of all cryptograms of the number $n$. For $a \in \mathcal{K}_{n}$, let $J(a)$ denote the number of occurrences of the number 1 in the cryptogram $a$. Prove that

$$
\sum_{a \in \mathcal{K}_{n}} J(a)=\sum_{a \in \mathcal{K}_{n+1}} a_{2}
$$

C24 (25-5, Mongolia) Let the sum of the lengths of all diagonals of a plane convex $n$-gon be $d$, and the perimeter be $p$. Prove:
$$
n-3<\frac{2 d}{p}<\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]\left[\frac{n+1}{2}\right]-2,
$$

where $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

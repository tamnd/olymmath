Let $\ell$ be a real number satisfying the equation $\tfrac{(1+\ell)^2}{1+\ell^2}=\tfrac{13}{37}$ . Then
$$\frac{(1+\ell)^3}{1+\ell^3}=\frac mn,$$ where $m$ and $n$ are positive coprime integers. Find $m+n$ .

Given 12 numbers, $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots a_{12}$, and the following inequalities hold:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{2}\left(a_{1}-a_{2}+a_{3}\right)<0 \\
& a_{3}\left(a_{2}-a_{3}+a_{4}\right)<0
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
a_{11}\left(a_{10}-a_{11}+a_{12}\right)<0
$$

Prove that among these numbers, there will be at least 3 positive and 3 negative numbers.

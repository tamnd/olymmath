Find all functions $f:(0,+\infty) \rightarrow(0,+\infty)$, such that for any positive real numbers $x, y$, we have
$$
\begin{array}{l}
f(f(x)+y) \\
=f(f(x))+2 y f(x)-f(y)+2 y^{2}+1 .
\end{array}
$$

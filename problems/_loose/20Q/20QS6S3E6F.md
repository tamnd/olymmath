Let $f(x)$ be a continuous function on the interval $[a, b]$ and $F(x)=\int_{a}^{x} f(t) d t$. Prove that $F^{\prime}(x)=f(x)$, i.e., $F(x)$ is an antiderivative of $f(x)$.

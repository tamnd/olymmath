Given the number table
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc}
-1 & 2 & -3 & 4 \\
-1.2 & 0.5 & -3.9 & 9 \\
\pi & -12 & 4 & -2.5 \\
63 & 1.4 & 7 & -9
\end{array}\right)
$$

Changing the sign of all numbers in any row or any column is called a "transformation". Can all the numbers in the table be made positive after several transformations?

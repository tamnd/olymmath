\section*{Task 1 - 211011}

A traveler covered the first part of a business trip by car and the rest by train.

When he had driven the car part of the journey and exactly one fifth of the train part, he realized that at that point he had covered exactly one third of the total distance. Later, when he had covered exactly half of the total distance, he had already traveled $20 \mathrm{~km}$ more by train than he had previously by car.

How long was the total distance of this trip?

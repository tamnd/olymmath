Pentagon $A B C D E$ is inscribed in a circle. Points $M, Q, N$ and $P$ are the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from vertex $E$ to sides $A B, B C, C D$ (or their extensions) and diagonal $A D$, respectively. It is known that $|E P|=d$, and the ratio of the area of triangle $M Q E$ to the area of triangle $P N E$ is $k$. Find $|E M|$.

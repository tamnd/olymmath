Let $A, B$ and $C$ be three points on the circle $\Gamma$ with center O. Suppose $\widehat{A B C}>90$. Let $D$ be the intersection point of the line $A B$ with the perpendicular at $C$ to $A C$. Let $\ell$ be the line passing through $D$ perpendicular to $A O$. Let $E$ be the intersection point of $\ell$ with the line $A C$, and let $F$ be the intersection point of $\Gamma$ with $\ell$, located between $D$ and $E$. Show that the circumcircles of $B F E$ and $C F D$ are tangent at $F$.

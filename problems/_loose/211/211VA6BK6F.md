$p$ is a prime number and $n$ is a natural number, prove that the following propositions (I) and (II) are equivalent:

Proposition (I): There is a binomial coefficient $C_{n}^{k}, k=0,1, \cdots, n$, which is divisible by $p$.

Proposition (II): $n$ can be expressed as $n=p^{s} q-1$, where $s, q$ are integers, and $s \geqslant 0$, $0<q<p$.
(Luxembourg and four other countries' International Mathematical Competition, 1980)
Note Luxembourg and the four other countries refer to Belgium, the United Kingdom, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, and former Yugoslavia.

We are looking for a pair of natural numbers $m$ and $n$ with $m \neq n, m ; n \neq 0$, that satisfies the equation

$$
\left(\frac{m}{4}\right)^{m}=\left(\frac{n}{4}\right)^{n}
$$

Furthermore, it is to be shown that there is exactly one such pair (up to the order).

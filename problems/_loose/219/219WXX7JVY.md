On the unequal sides $AB$ and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, isosceles triangles $AC_1B$ and $AB_1C$ are constructed externally with an angle $\varphi$ at the vertex.
a) $M$ is a point on the median $AA_1$ (or its extension), equidistant from points $B_1$ and $C_1$. Prove that $\angle B_1MC_1 = \varphi$.

b) $O$ is a point on the perpendicular bisector of segment $BC$, equidistant from points $B_1$ and $C_1$. Prove that $\angle B_1OC_1 = 180^\circ - \varphi$.

In the coordinate plane, 20 points with integer coordinates are marked, with no three points lying on the same line. Prove that there exists a triangle whose vertices are the marked points and whose centroid is also a point with integer coordinates.

(We say that $T(x, y)$ is a point with integer coordinates if $x$ and $y$ are integers.)

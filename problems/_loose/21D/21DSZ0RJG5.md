In tetrahedron $ABCD$, the medians from vertex $D$ to the three lateral faces $ABD$, $ACD$, and $BCD$ form equal angles with the corresponding sides of the base $\triangle ABC$. Prove: The area of each lateral face is less than the sum of the areas of the other two lateral faces.
(1997 Polish Competition Problem)

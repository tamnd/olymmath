On one leg of angle $\angle A < 180^o$ lie the three points $P_1$ , $P_2$ , and $P_3$ . On the other leg lie the three points $Q_1$ , $Q_2,$ and $Q_3$ . Suppose that $$ |AP_1| + |AQ_1| = |AP_2| + |AQ_2| = |AP_3| + |AQ_3|. $$ Prove that the perpendicular bisectors of the line segments $[P_1Q_1]$ , $ [P_2Q_2]$ , and $ [P_3Q_3]$ are concurrent.

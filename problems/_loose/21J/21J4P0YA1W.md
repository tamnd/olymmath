In $\triangle A B C$, $a, b, c$ are the lengths of its three sides, $R, r, p$ are the circumradius, inradius, and semiperimeter respectively. Prove:
$$
\frac{a}{p-a}+\frac{b}{p-b}+\frac{c}{p-c} \geqslant \frac{3 R}{r}
$$

Equality holds if and only if $\triangle A B C$ is an equilateral triangle.

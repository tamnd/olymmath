Given a circle $O$ with radius $r$, the angles of view (i.e., the angle between the tangents drawn from the point to the circle) from the given points $A$ and $B$ are $\alpha$ and $\beta$, respectively. Construct the point on the line $AB$ from which the angle of view of circle $O$ is $\gamma=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}$.

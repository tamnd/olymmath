Let $A B C$ be a triangle, and $D, E, F$ be points on the segments $[B C]$, $[C A]$, and $[A B]$ respectively such that the corresponding cevians are concurrent. Let $M, N, P$ be points on the segments $[A D]$, $[B E]$, and $[C F]$ respectively. Show that the lines $(A M)$, $(B N)$, and $(C P)$ are concurrent if and only if the lines $(D M)$, $(E N)$, and $(F P)$ are concurrent.

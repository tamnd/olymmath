There are $100$ people in a room. Some are *wise* and some are *optimists*.

$ \bullet~$ A *wise* person can look at someone and know if they are wise or if they are an optimist.
$ \bullet~$ An *optimist* thinks everyone is wise (including themselves).

Everyone in the room writes down what they think is the number of wise people in the room. What is the smallest possible value for the average?
$$
\mathrm a. ~ 10 \mathrm b.~25 \mathrm c. ~50 \mathrm d. ~75 \mathrm e. ~100
$$

Given a triangle $A B C$. On its side $A B$, a point $P$ is chosen and lines $P M$ and $P N$ are drawn parallel to $A C$ and $B C$ respectively (points $M$ and $N$ lie on sides $B C$ and $A C$); $Q$ is the point of intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $A P N$ and $B P M$. Prove that all lines $P Q$ pass through a fixed point.

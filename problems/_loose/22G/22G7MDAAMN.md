$\odot O$ is separated from line $l$, construct $O P \perp l, P$ being the foot of the perpendicular. Let point $Q$ be any point on $l$ (not coinciding with point $P$), and draw two tangents $Q A$ and $Q B$ to $\odot O$, with $A$ and $B$ being the points of tangency. $A B$ intersects $O P$ at point $K$. Draw $P M \perp Q B$ and $P N \perp Q A$, with $M$ and $N$ being the feet of the perpendiculars. Prove that line $M N$ bisects segment $K P$.

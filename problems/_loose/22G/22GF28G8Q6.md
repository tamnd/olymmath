Let $ \{D_1, D_2, ..., D_n \}$ be a set of disks in the Euclidean plane. Let $ a_ {i, j} = S (D_i \cap D_j) $ be the area of $ D_i \cap D_j $. Prove that $$ \sum_ {i = 1} ^ n \sum_ {j = 1} ^ n a_ {i, j} x_ix_j \geq 0 $$ for any real numbers $ x_1, x_2, ..., x_n $.

Denote by $I$ the incenter of $ABC$ , by $M$ the midpoint of $BC$ and by $G$ the foot of the perpendicular from $M$ to line $EF$ . Prove that the line $ID$ is tangent to the circumcircle of the triangle $MGI$ .

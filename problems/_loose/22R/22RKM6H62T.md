$23 \star \star$ Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers, prove that:
$$
\frac{\sqrt{b+c}}{a}+\frac{\sqrt{c+a}}{b}+\frac{\sqrt{a+b}}{c} \geqslant \frac{4(a+b+c)}{\sqrt{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)}} .
$$

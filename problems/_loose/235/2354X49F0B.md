Initially, 100 cards were placed on the table, each with a natural number written on it; there were exactly 43 cards with odd numbers. Then, every minute, the following procedure was carried out. For every three cards lying on the table, the product of the numbers written on them was calculated, all these products were summed, and the resulting number was written on a new card, which was added to those on the table. After a year from the start of the process, it was found that there was a card on the table with a number divisible by $2^{10000}$. Prove that a number divisible by $2^{10000}$ was on one of the cards already a day after the start. (I. Bogdanov)

The game of mathinal is a game played by $n$ players (with $n \geqslant 2$), $n$ green cards, and $n$ red cards. Initially, each player takes one green card and one red card, and writes an integer on each of these two cards (they have the right to write the same integer on both cards). Then each player calculates the sum of the numbers on their two cards, and we denote $M$ as the largest sum among the sums of the $n$ players.

Next, the players redistribute the red cards as follows: the player with the green card with the smallest number receives the red card with the largest number, then the player with the green card with the second smallest number receives the red card with the second largest number, and so on. Each player then recalculates the sum of the numbers on their two cards, and we denote $M^{\prime}$ as the largest sum among the sums of the $n$ players.

1) Is it possible to have $M^{\prime} < M$?

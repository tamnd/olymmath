There is a $98 \times 98$ grid on the TV screen, and each small square is arbitrarily colored black or white. Take any sub-rectangle $n \times m (n>1, m>1)$ of the $98 \times 98$ grid, and then click the mouse to change the color of all small squares in the $n \times m$ rectangle. Prove that it is always possible to make all the small squares white after a finite number of such operations.

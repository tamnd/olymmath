The function $f(x)=a \ln x+1(a>0)$.
(1) When $x>0$, prove that: $f(x)-1 \geqslant a\left(1-\frac{1}{x}\right)$;
(2) On the interval $(1, \mathrm{e})$, $f(x)>x$ always holds, find the range of the real number $a$;
(3) When $a=\frac{1}{2}$, prove that: $f(2)+f(3)+\cdots+f(n+1)>2(n+1-\sqrt{n+1})\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$.

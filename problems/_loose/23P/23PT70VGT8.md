Let the radius of the inscribed sphere of the tetrahedron be denoted by $\varrho$, the radii of the spheres that touch the faces opposite to the vertices $A, B, C, D$ from the outside be denoted by $\varrho_{1}, \varrho_{2}, \varrho_{3}, \varrho_{4}$, respectively, and the lengths of the altitudes from the vertices $A, B, C, D$ to the opposite faces be denoted by $m_{1}, m_{2}, m_{3}, m_{4}$, respectively. Prove that the following relations hold:

а) $\frac{2}{\varrho}=\frac{1}{\varrho_{1}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{2}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{3}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{4}}$,
b) $\frac{1}{\varrho}=\frac{1}{m_{1}}+\frac{1}{m_{2}}+\frac{1}{m_{3}}+\frac{1}{m_{4}}$,
c) $\frac{1}{\varrho_{1}}=-\frac{1}{m_{1}}+\frac{1}{m_{2}}+\frac{1}{m_{3}}+\frac{1}{m_{4}}$.

(Similar relations can be found for $\frac{1}{\varrho_{2}}, \frac{1}{\varrho_{3}}, \frac{1}{\varrho_{4}}$.)

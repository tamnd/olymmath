Given two quadratic trinomials $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, it is known that the trinomials $f(x)$, $g(x)$, and $f(x)+g(x)$ each have two roots. It turns out that the difference between the roots of the trinomial $f(x)$ is equal to the difference between the roots of the trinomial $g(x)$. Prove that the difference between the roots of the trinomial $f(x)+g(x)$ is not greater than these differences. (In each difference, the larger root is subtracted from the smaller one.) (I. Bogdanov)

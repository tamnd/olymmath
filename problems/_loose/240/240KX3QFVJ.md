Let $0<p \leqslant a_{i} \leqslant q(i=1,2, \cdots, n), b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be any permutation of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, prove that: $n \leqslant \frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}+\frac{a_{2}}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}} \leqslant n+\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]\left(\sqrt{\frac{p}{q}}-\sqrt{\frac{q}{p}}\right)^{2}$.

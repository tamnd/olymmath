Place $n$ chess pieces on an $n \times n$ chessboard, with at most one piece per square, such that no two pieces are in the same row or column. Draw a broken line of length $2n$ from the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner along the grid, ensuring that the $n$ pieces are below the line, resulting in a "chess-line state" of the chessboard. If the positions of the pieces or the drawn line differ, they are considered different chess-line states. Find the number of all different chess-line states.

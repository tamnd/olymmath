Let $q > 1$ be a power of $2$ . Let $f: \mathbb{F}_{q^2} \to \mathbb{F}_{q^2}$ be an affine map over $\mathbb{F}_2$ . Prove that the equation
$$
f(x) = x^{q+1}
$$
has at most $2q - 1$ solutions.

Given that $a$ and $b$ are two distinct positive numbers. Prove: The equation in terms of $x$
$$
\frac{(a+b) x+a b}{16 x+4(a+b)}=\left(a^{-\frac{1}{3}}+b^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)^{-3}
$$

has only one positive solution.

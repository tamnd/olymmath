Six players compete in a chess tournament. Each player plays exactly two games against every other player. In each game, the winning player earns 1 point and the losing player earns 0 points; if the game results in a draw (tie), each player earns $\frac{1}{2}$ point. What is the minimum possible number of points that a player needs to earn in order to guarantee that he has more points than every other player?
(A) 8
(B) $8 \frac{1}{2}$
(C) 9
(D) $9 \frac{1}{2}$
(E) 10

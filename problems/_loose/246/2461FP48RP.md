In the triangle $ABC$, we measure up the perpendicular from the midpoint of side $a$ to half the length of side $BC=a$ outward, thus obtaining point $A_{2}$. On the perpendicular from the midpoint of side $b$, we measure inward half the length of side $AC=b$, thus creating point $B_{2}$. Prove that the line $A_{2}B_{2}$ passes through the foot of the altitude from $C$ to side $c$, denoted as $C_{1}$.

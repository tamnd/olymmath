For three natural numbers $a, b, c$, the following properties (1) and (2) are required:

(1) It holds that $a<b<c$.

(2) If $a, b, c$ are the measurements in centimeters of the edge lengths of a cuboid, then the cuboid has a volume of $270 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$, and the sum of the lengths of all twelve edges of the cuboid is $80 \mathrm{~cm}$.

Investigate whether there are natural numbers that satisfy these requirements, and whether these numbers are uniquely determined by the requirements (1) and (2)! If this is the case, name these numbers!

Given that circle $\Gamma$ with diameter $AB$ intersects circle $\odot A$ at points $C$ and $D$, and point $E$ is on $\odot A$ such that $E$ is outside circle $\Gamma$ and $E, C$ are on the same side of line $AB$. Line $BE$ intersects $\odot A$ again at point $F$, and $K$ is a point on circle $\Gamma$ such that $K, A$ are on the same side of the diameter of circle $\Gamma$ passing through point $C$, and satisfies $2 CK \cdot AC = CE \cdot AB$. Line $KF$ intersects circle $\Gamma$ again at point $L$. Prove: The reflection of point $D$ over line $BE$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle LFC$.

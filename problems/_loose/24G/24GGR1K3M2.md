$$

& \left|x_{2}-x_{3}\right|+\left|x_{2}-x_{4}\right|+\cdots+\left|x_{2}-x_{n}\right|+ \\
& \cdots+\left|x_{n-2}-x_{n-1}\right|+\left|x_{n-2}-x_{n}\right|+\mid x_{n-1}- \\
& x_{n} \mid,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $0 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant 1, i=1,2, \cdots, n$. Let $S(n)$ denote the maximum possible value of the sum. Find $S(n)$.

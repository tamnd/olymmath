We took three numbers $x, y, z$. We calculated the absolute values of the pairwise differences $x_{1}=|x-y|$, $y_{1}=|y-z|, z_{1}=|z-x|$. In the same way, from the numbers $x_{1}, y_{1}, z_{1}$ we constructed the numbers $x_{2}, y_{2}, z_{2}$ and so on. It turned out that for some $n$, $x_{n}=x$, $y_{n}=y$, $z_{n}=z$. Knowing that $x=1$, find $y$ and $z$.

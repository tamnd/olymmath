Given $\overrightarrow{O A}=(2,0), \overrightarrow{O C}=\overrightarrow{A B}=(0, 1)$, a moving point $M$ is at a distance $d$ from the fixed line $y=1$, and $\overrightarrow{O M} \cdot \overrightarrow{A M}=k\left(\overrightarrow{C M} \cdot \overrightarrow{B M}-d^{2}\right)$, where $O$ is the origin, and $k$ is a parameter.
(1) Find the trajectory of the moving point $M$;
(2) When $k=\frac{1}{2}$, find the maximum and minimum values of $|\overrightarrow{O M}+2 \overrightarrow{A M}|$; $\square$
(3) If the trajectory of the moving point $M$ is a conic section, find the eccentricity.

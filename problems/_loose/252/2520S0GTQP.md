In $\triangle A B C$, $\angle A B C$ and $\angle A C B$ are both acute angles. $D$ is an interior point on side $B C$, and $A D$ bisects $\angle B A C$. A perpendicular line $D P \perp A B$ is drawn from point $D$ to $P$, and $D Q \perp A C$ to $Q$. $C P$ and $B Q$ intersect at $K$. Prove: $A K \perp B C$.

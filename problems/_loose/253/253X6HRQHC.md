Consider a sequence $ x_1, x_2, x_3, ...$ , defined by
\begin{align*}x_1 &= \sqrt [3]{3}
x_2 &= \sqrt [3]{3} ^ {\sqrt [3]{3}},\end{align*}and in general
$$ x_n = (x_{n - 1}) ^ {\sqrt [3]{3}}\,\,\text{ for }\, n > 1.
$$What is the smallest value of $ n$ for which $ x_n$ is an integer? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 2 \textbf{(B)}\ 3 \textbf{(C)}\ 4 \textbf{(D)}\ 9 \textbf{(E)}\ 27$

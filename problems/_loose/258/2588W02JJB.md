In a $50 \times 50$ checkered square, each cell is painted in one of $100$ given colors so that all colors are present and it is impossible to cut a single-color domino from the square (i.e. a $1 \times 2$ rectangle). Galia wants to recolor all the cells of one of the colors into another color (out of the given $100$ colors) so that this condition is preserved (i.e., it is still impossible to cut out a domino of the same color). Is it true that Galia will definitely be able to do this?

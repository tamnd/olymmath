Let $f(x)=\left[\frac{x}{1!}\right]+\left[\frac{x}{2!}\right]+\cdots+\left[\frac{x}{2013!}\right]$ (where $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$). For an integer $n$, if the equation $f(x)=n$ has a real solution, then $n$ is called a "good number". Find the number of good numbers in the set $\{1,3, \cdots, 2013\}$. ${ }^{\text {[4] }}$
(2013, China Southeast Mathematical Olympiad)

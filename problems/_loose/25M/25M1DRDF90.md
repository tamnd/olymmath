The first $n$ positive integers are written on the board. Markéta and Tereza take turns in the following game. First, Markéta erases one of the numbers on the board. Then, on each turn, the player whose turn it is erases one of the numbers that differs from the previously erased number by neither 1 nor is coprime with it. The player who is on turn and cannot erase any more numbers loses. Decide which of the players can play in such a way that they win regardless of the moves of the other player for $n=6$ and for $n=12$.

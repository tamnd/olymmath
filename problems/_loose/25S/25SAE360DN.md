To manufacture similar glasses, it is necessary that the number representing the year contains two or more consecutive zeros (for example, 3500 is fine, 2010 is not). How many years between the year 999 and the year 9999 contain two or more consecutive zeros in their representation?
(A) 171
(B) 180
(C) 190
(D) 191
(E) 200.

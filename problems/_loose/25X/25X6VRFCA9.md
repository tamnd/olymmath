In a box, there are exactly 100 balls, including 30 red, 30 blue, 30 green, and 10 balls of which it is only known that they are either black or white and that at least one of them is black.

By touch, it is not possible to distinguish between balls of different colors.

Investigate whether it is still possible to pull out a suitable number of balls, but not all, with your eyes closed, so that you can predict with certainty:

a) Among the balls pulled out, there are at least 12 balls of the same color.

b) Among the balls pulled out, there is at least one black ball.

c) Among the balls pulled out, there are at least 5 white balls.

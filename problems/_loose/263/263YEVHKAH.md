$$
\begin{aligned}
& 1^{3}=1 \\
& a^{3}=1+7 \\
& 3^{3}=1+7+b \\
& 4^{3}=1+7+c
\end{aligned}
$$

The numerical value of $a+b+c$ is
(A) 58
(B) 110
(C) 75
(D) 77
(E) 79

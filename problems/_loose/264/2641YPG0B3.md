12 players participated in a chess tournament. After the tournament, each of them made 12 lists. On the 1st list, they only wrote their own name; on the 2nd list, they wrote their own name and the names of all the opponents they defeated; on the 3rd list, they wrote all the names on the 2nd list and the names of all the opponents these people defeated; and so on, until on the 12th list, they wrote all the names on the 11th list and the names of all the opponents these people defeated. It is known that for each player, on their 12th list, there are names that did not appear on their 11th list. How many draws were there in the tournament?

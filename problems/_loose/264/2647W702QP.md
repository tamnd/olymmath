Let $v_{0}$ be a vertex in a graph $G$, and let $D_{0}:=\left\{v_{0}\right\}$. For $n=1,2, \ldots$, inductively define $D_{n}:=N_{G}\left(D_{0} \cup \cdots \cup D_{n-1}\right)$. Prove: $D_{n}=\left\{v \mid d\left(v_{0}, v\right)=n\right\}$, and for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $D_{n+1} \subseteq N\left(D_{n}\right) \subseteq D_{n-1} \cup D_{n+1}$.

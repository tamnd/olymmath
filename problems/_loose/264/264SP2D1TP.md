How many triangles are determined by a (convex) regular heptagon: a) its side lines, b) the lines of its diagonals, c) the lines of its sides and diagonals? How many isosceles triangles are there among the triangles in each of these cases? How many different intersection points do the lines determine in each of the cases a), b), c)? How many regions do the diagonal segments divide the interior of the heptagon into?

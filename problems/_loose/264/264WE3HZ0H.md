Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral whose diagonals intersect at point $E$. The lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $P$, and the lines $AD$ and $BC$ intersect at point $Q$. Let $X$ be the intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $EBC$ and $EDA$ different from $E$, and $Y$ be the intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $EAB$ and $ECD$ different from $E$. Finally, let $W$ be the intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $PBC$ and $PDA$ different from $P$. Prove that triangles $WQY$ and $WXP$ are similar.

$\ln 1.2 \mathrm{~V} \frac{1}{6} \Leftrightarrow\left(1.2 \mathrm{Ve}^{\frac{1}{6}}\right) \Leftrightarrow\left((1.2)^{6} \mathrm{Ve}\right) \Leftrightarrow\left((1.44)^{2} * 1.44 \mathrm{Ve}\right) \Leftrightarrow 2.0736 * 1.44 \mathrm{Ve}$. Since $2.07 * 1.4=2.898>\mathrm{e}=2.718$, the first number is greater than the second.

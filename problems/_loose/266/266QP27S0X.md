Given quadrilateral $A B C D$, where $A D / / B C, \angle A=90^{\circ}, C D \perp$ $B D, D E \perp B C$, with the foot of the perpendicular at $E$, and $E$ is the midpoint of $B C$, then the incorrect statement among the following is
(A) $C D$ is the tangent to the circle passing through $A, B, D$.
(B) $B D$ is the tangent to the circle passing through $C, D, E$.
(C) $A D$ is the tangent to the circle passing through $B, D, C$.
(D) $C B$ is the tangent to the circle passing through $B, D, E$.

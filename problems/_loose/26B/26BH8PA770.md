Real numbers $a, b$, and $c$ are different from zero and satisfy the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
a^{2}+a & =b^{2} \\
b^{2}+b & =c^{2} \\
c^{2}+c & =a^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that $(a-b)(b-c)(c-a)=1$.

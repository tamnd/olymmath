Let $I$ and $G$ be the incentre and the centre of $\triangle A B C$ with sides $A B=c, B C=a, C A=b$.

(a) Prove that (if $a>b$ ) the area of $C I G$ equals $\frac{(a-b) r}{6}$ where $r$ is the inradius of $A B C$.

(b) If $a=c+1$ and $b=c-1$, prove that the segment $I G$ is parallel to $A B$ and find its length.

If $\{a\} \subset(A \cup B) \subset\{a, b, c, d\}$, and $a \in B, A \cap B=\varnothing$, then the number of sets $A$ that satisfy the above conditions is
(A) 5.
(B) 6.
(C) 7.
(D) 8.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

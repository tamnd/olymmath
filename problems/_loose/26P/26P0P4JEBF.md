The ninety-fourth problem: Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a given positive integer, and $\mathrm{p}(\mathrm{x})$ be a monic $\mathrm{n}$-degree polynomial with real coefficients. Find the minimum possible value of $\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}+1} \frac{\mathrm{i}^{2}(\mathrm{p}(\mathrm{i} 2))^{2}}{(\mathrm{i}+1)^{2}(\mathrm{i}+2)^{2} \ldots(\mathrm{i}+\mathrm{n}+1)^{2}}$.

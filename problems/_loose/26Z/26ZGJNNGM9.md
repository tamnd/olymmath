Let $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}_0$ be a function that satisfies
$$ f(mn) = mf(n) + nf(m)$$
for all positive integers $m,n$ and $f(2024)=10120$ .

Prove that there are two integers $m,n$ with $m\ne n$ such that $f(m)=f(n)$ .

If packed in groups of 9, there will be 8 left over. The third, fourth, fifth, and sixth specifications are, respectively, 8, 7, 6, and 5 per pack, and there will be 7, 6, 5, and 4 left over, respectively. Originally, there were $ $ toothpicks.

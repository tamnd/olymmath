Suppose that $n>1$ and $P_n(x)$ is a polynomial of degree $n$ . For $k =1,2, . . . ,n$ we have $P_n(k)=k(k+1)$ . Also $P_n(0) = 1$ . For all $n$ there exists an integer $m > n$ such that $P_n(m) = P_{n+2}(m)$ . Find the value of $m$ for $n = 10$ .

Let $ABCD$ be a convex cyclic quadrilateral inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$. Denote by $P$ the intersection point of $(AB)$ and $(CD)$. The perpendicular to $(BC)$ passing through $P$ intersects $(DA)$ and $(BC)$ at $E$ and $F$ respectively. The perpendicular to $(AD)$ passing through $P$ intersects $(AD)$ and $(BC)$ at $H$ and $G$ respectively. Show that $P$, the center of $\Gamma$, and the midpoint of $[EG]$ are collinear.

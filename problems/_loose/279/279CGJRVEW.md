Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $|A B|>|B C|$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of $A C$. Let $E$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle A B C$ with the line $A C$. Let $F$ be on $B E$ such that $C F$ is perpendicular to $B E$. Let further $G$ be the intersection of $C F$ and $B D$.

Prove that $D F$ bisects the line segment $E G$.

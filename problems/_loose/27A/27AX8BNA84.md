At the trial, 14 coins were presented as physical evidence. The expert found that coins from 1st to 7th are counterfeit, and from 8th to 14th are genuine. The court, however, only knows that counterfeit coins weigh the same, genuine coins weigh the same, and that counterfeit coins are lighter than genuine ones. The expert has at his disposal a balance scale without weights.

a) The expert wants to prove to the court that the coins from 1st to 7th are counterfeit. How can he do this using only three weighings?

b) Show that with three weighings, he can prove even more: that the coins from 1st to 7th are counterfeit, and from 8th to 14th are genuine.

Find all positive integers $n$ for which the equality

$$
\frac{\sin (n \alpha)}{\sin \alpha}-\frac{\cos (n \alpha)}{\cos \alpha}=n-1
$$

holds true for all $\alpha \neq \frac{k \pi}{2}, k \in Z$.

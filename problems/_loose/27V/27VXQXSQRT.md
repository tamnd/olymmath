In a certain city district, for any 3 intersections $A, B, C$, there is a road from $A$ to $B$ that does not pass through $C$. Prove that from any intersection to any other intersection there are at least two disjoint roads (an intersection is a place where at least two roads meet; the number of intersections in the city district is no less than 3).

Until 2013, in the penal colony of Zoranel, the population was $60 \%$ androids, of which $5 \%$ were assigned to surveillance; let's say $q$ is the percentage of surveillance androids out of the total population in that year. In 2014, the population increased by $10 \%$ due to the arrival of $N$ exiled humans. By how much did the percentage of surveillance androids on the total population decrease? $\begin{array}{lll}\text { (A) did not change } & \text { (B) by less than a tenth of } q & \text { (C) by more than a tenth of } q\end{array}$ (D) depends on $N $ (E) depends on how large the initial population was.

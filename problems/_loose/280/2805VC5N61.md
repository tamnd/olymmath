One of the circles (with center $O_{1}$) touches the other two (with centers $O_{2}$ and $O_{3}$, respectively), and $\angle \mathrm{O}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{1} O_{3}=90^{\circ}$. Determine which is greater: the area of the circle bounded by the circle with center $O_{1}$, or one-fifth of the area of triangle $ABC$?

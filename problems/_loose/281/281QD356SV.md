Let $S$ be the set of all 7-term sequences $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{7}\right\}$, where all $a_{i}$ are 0 or 1. The distance between two elements $\left\{a_{i}\right\},\left\{b_{i}\right\}$ in $S$ is defined as $\sum_{i=1}^{7}\left|a_{i}-b_{i}\right|$. $T$ is a subset of $S$ where the distance between any two elements is at least 3. Prove that $T$ can contain at most 16 elements.

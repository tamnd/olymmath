We call a Mersenne number any number of the form $M_{n}=2^{n}-1$. Show that

1. if $n$ is even and different from $2, M_{n}$ is composite (i.e., not prime);
2. if $n$ is composite, $M_{n}$ is composite;
3. if $p$ is prime, $p$ does not divide $M_{p}$.

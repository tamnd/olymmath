Prove that if $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n} ; y_{1}, y_{2}, \ldots, y_{n}$ are positive numbers, then

$$
\begin{gathered}
\sqrt{x_{1} y_{1}}+\sqrt{x_{2} y_{2}}+\ldots+\sqrt{x_{n} y_{n}} \leqslant \\
\leqslant \sqrt{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{n}} \cdot \sqrt{y_{1}+y_{2}+\ldots+y_{n}}
\end{gathered}
$$

$20$ players participated in a chess tournament. Each of them played one game with each other. ( $1$ point was awarded for a win, $0.5$ points for a draw, and $0$ points for a loss.) A tournament game is called unfair if the player who won it scored fewer points than his opponent in the tournament. Could such a tournament have played:
a) at least $70$ unfair games?
b) at least $140$ unfair games?

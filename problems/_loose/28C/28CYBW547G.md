Find all triples of real numbers $a, b, c$ with the property: Each of the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{3}+(a+1) x^{2}+(b+3) x+(c+2)=0, \\
& x^{3}+(a+2) x^{2}+(b+1) x+(c+3)=0, \\
& x^{3}+(a+3) x^{2}+(b+2) x+(c+1)=0
\end{aligned}
$$

has three distinct real roots, but altogether there are only five different numbers.

Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle with $\hat{A}=45^{\circ}$, and let $P$ be the foot of the altitude from $B$. We draw the circle centered at $P$ that passes through $C$ and intersects $A C$ again at point $X$ and the altitude $P B$ at point $Y$. Let $r$ and $s$ be the lines perpendicular to line $A Y$ through $P$ and $X$, respectively, and $L, K$ the intersections of $r, s$ with $A B$. Prove that $L$ is the midpoint of $K B$.

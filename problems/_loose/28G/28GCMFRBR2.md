Let triangle $ABC$ and points $M \in (BC)$, $D \in (AB)$, $E \in (AC)$ such that $BM = MC$, $AB = m \cdot AD$, and $AC = n \cdot AE$. Denote $\{F\} = AM \cap DE$. Prove that:

a) $m \cdot \overrightarrow{DF} = n \cdot \overrightarrow{FE}$.

b) $(m+n) \cdot \overrightarrow{AF} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}$.

Let $\alpha$ be a root of the polynomial

$$
f(x)=x^{n}+\beta_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\ldots+\beta_{0}
$$

where $\beta_{0}, \ldots, \beta_{n-1}$ are algebraic integers. Prove that then $\alpha$ is an algebraic integer.

An algebraic number $\alpha$ is called totally real if all its conjugates are real. In other words, all roots of the irreducible polynomial with root $\alpha$ are real.

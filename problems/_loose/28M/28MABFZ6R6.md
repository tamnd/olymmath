Randomly select a permutation $\sigma=\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}\right)$ of $1, 2, \ldots, \mathrm{n}$. If $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}=\mathrm{i}$, then $\mathrm{i}$ is called a fixed point of $\sigma$. The number of fixed points in the permutation is denoted as the random variable $\mathrm{X}_{\mathrm{n}}$. Try to find the expectation $\mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{X}_{\mathrm{n}}\right)$ of $\mathrm{X}_{\mathrm{n}}$.

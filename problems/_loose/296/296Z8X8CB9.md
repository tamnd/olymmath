The center of the square $A B C D$ is $O$, and the circle circumscribed around it is $k$. The tangent to $k$ passing through $C$ is $e$. Let $P$ be a point on the extension of the diagonal $A C$ beyond $A$, and let the line $P D$ intersect $e$ at point $Q$. The line perpendicular to $P B$ passing through $P$ intersects at point $R$. Is it true that if $Q R=O A$, then the perpendicular bisector of the segment $O P$ intersects $k$ at the vertices adjacent to $A$ in a regular heptagon inscribed in $k$?

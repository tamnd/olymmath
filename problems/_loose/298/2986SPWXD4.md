In a right-angled triangle let $r$ be the inradius and $s_a,s_b$ be the lengths of the medians of the legs $a,b$.
Prove the inequality $$ \frac{r^2}{s_a^2+s_b^2} \leq \frac{3-2 \sqrt2}{5}. $$

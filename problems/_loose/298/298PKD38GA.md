In a certain state, there were 2002 cities connected by roads in such a way that if travel through any of the cities was prohibited, it would still be possible to travel from any of the remaining cities to any other. Each year, the king selects a non-self-intersecting cyclic route and orders the construction of a new city, connecting it by roads to all the cities on the selected route, and closing all the roads of this route as unnecessary. After several years, there were no non-self-intersecting cyclic routes left in the country. Prove that at this point, the number of cities from which exactly one road leads out is no less than 2002.

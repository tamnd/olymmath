Given $n \in \mathbf{N}, n \geqslant 2, 0<\theta<\pi$. Prove:
$$
\sin \frac{\theta}{2} \cdot \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{\sin k \theta}{k}<1 \text {. }
$$

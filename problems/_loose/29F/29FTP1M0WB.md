We want to determine the position of point $A$ in a mountainous area relative to peaks $B$ and $C$ based on the following data. The horizontal projection of the distance $BC$ is $2400 \text{m}$, and peak $B$ is $800 \text{m}$ higher than $C$. The angle of elevation of the line of sight $AB$ is $20^\circ$, and that of $AC$ is $2^\circ$. The angle between the directions from $A$ to the horizontal projections $B'$ and $C'$ of $B$ and $C$ on the horizontal plane passing through $A$ is $60^\circ$.

What are the horizontal projections of the distances $AB$ and $AC$, and the height difference between $B$ and $A$?

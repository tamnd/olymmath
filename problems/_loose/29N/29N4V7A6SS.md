To be proven, that the number of positive integer solutions of the equation

$$
1 \cdot x_{1}+2 \cdot x_{2}+\ldots+n \cdot x_{n}=a
$$

is the same as the number of non-negative integer solutions of the equation

$$
1 \cdot y_{1}+2 \cdot y_{2}+\ldots+n \cdot y_{n}=a-\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

where $ a $ is a positive integer.

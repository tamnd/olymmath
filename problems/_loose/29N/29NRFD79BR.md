Prove that any integer $r>2$ is composite if and only if at least one of the following two statements is true:
a) $r=2^{s}$ for some $s \in\{2,3, \ldots\}$,
b) $r=\frac{u}{2}(2 v-u+1)$ for some $u, v \in\{3,4, \ldots\},(u \leq v)$.

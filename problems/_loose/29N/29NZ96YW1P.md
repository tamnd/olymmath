A blackboard of size $n \times n$ is divided into $n^{2}$ small squares by a grid. The intersection of $k$ rows and $l$ columns of squares on this blackboard is called a $k \times l$ submatrix of the blackboard, and $k+l$ is called the semi-perimeter of the submatrix. Given that several submatrices with semi-perimeters no less than $n$ cover the main diagonal of the blackboard, prove that they cover at least half of the squares on the blackboard.

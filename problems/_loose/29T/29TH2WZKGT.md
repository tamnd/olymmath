(1989 Australian Mathematics Competition) Let the number of distinct divisors of the positive integer $n$ be $N(n)$. For example, 24 has divisors $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$, so $N_{(24)}=8$. Determine whether the sum
$$
N_{(1)}+N_{(2)}+\cdots+N_{(1989)}
$$
is odd or even.

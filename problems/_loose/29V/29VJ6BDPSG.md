The 69th problem: Given a positive integer $\mathrm{n} \geq 3$, prove: the set $x=\left\{1,2,3, \ldots, \mathrm{n}^{2}-\mathrm{n}\right\}$ can be written as the union of two non-empty, disjoint subsets, such that neither subset contains $n$ elements $a_{1} 、 a_{2} 、 \ldots 、 a_{n}, a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{n}$, satisfying $a_{k} \leq \frac{a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}}{2}, 2 \leq k \leq n-1$.

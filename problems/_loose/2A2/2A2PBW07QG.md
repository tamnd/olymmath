Determine all positive integers $n$ for which $$ n - \big\lfloor n\big\{\sqrt{n}\big\}\big\rfloor = 2, $$ where $\lfloor x \rfloor$ and $\{x\}$ denote, respectively, the integer part and the fractional part of the real number $x$ they enclose.**Note:** For example, $\lfloor 5.7 \rfloor = 5$ and $\{5.7\} = 0.7$ .

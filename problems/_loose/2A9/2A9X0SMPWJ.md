For the angles $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \ldots, \alpha_{k}$, we have $\cos \alpha_{1}=\frac{1}{p_{1}}, \cos \alpha_{2}=\frac{1}{p_{2}}, \cos \alpha_{3}=\frac{1}{p_{3}}, \ldots, \cos \alpha_{k}=\frac{1}{p_{k}}$, where $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{k}$ are distinct prime numbers greater than 2. For which positive integers $n_{i}$ does the following equation hold:

$$
\cos \left(n_{1} \alpha_{1}\right)+\cos \left(n_{2} \alpha_{2}\right)+\cos \left(n_{3} \alpha_{3}\right)+\cdots+\cos \left(n_{k-1} \alpha_{k-1}\right)=\cos \left(n_{k} \alpha_{k}\right)
$$

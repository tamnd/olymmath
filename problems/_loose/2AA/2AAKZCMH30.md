A map of a rectangular area was placed on top of another map of the same area but at a smaller scale. Prove that it is possible to pierce both maps with a needle so that the puncture point represents the same point on both maps.

19.37*. Rotational homotheties $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ with centers $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ have the same angle of rotation, and the product of their coefficients is 1. Prove that the composition $P_{2} \circ P_{1}$ is a rotation, and its center coincides with the center of another rotation that translates $A_{1}$ to $A_{2}$ and has an angle of rotation $2 \angle\left(\overrightarrow{M A_{1}}, \overrightarrow{M N}\right)$, where $M$ is an arbitrary point and $N=P_{1}(M)$.

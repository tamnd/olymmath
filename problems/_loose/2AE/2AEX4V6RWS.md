Consider the following application:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f: \mathbb{R}^{2} & \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2} \\
(x, y) & \longmapsto(x+y, 2 x+2 y)
\end{aligned}
$$

1) Show that $f$ is an endomorphism of $\mathbb{R}^{2}$.
2) Determine $\operatorname{Im} f$ and $\operatorname{Ker} f$.
3) Is the linear application $f$ injective? Surjective? Bijective?

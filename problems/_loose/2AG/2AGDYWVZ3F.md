In the isosceles triangle $ABC$, $AC = BC$ and the angle at $C$ is $80^{\circ}$. Inside the triangle, we mark the points $D$ and $E$ such that $\angle DAB = \angle EAB = 10^{\circ}$, $\angle DBA = 30^{\circ}$, and $\angle EBA = 20^{\circ}$. Prove that $\angle DCB = 10^{\circ}$ and $\angle ECB = 20^{\circ}$. (We do not accept the trigonometric calculation of the angle as a proof.)

In front of Sharik lies an infinite number of cutlets, each with a fly sitting on it. On each move, Sharik sequentially performs two operations:

1) eats one cutlet along with all the flies sitting on it;
2) moves one fly from one cutlet to another (there can be any number of flies on a cutlet).

Sharik wants to eat no more than a million flies. Prove that he cannot act in such a way that each cutlet is eaten at some move.

If in a triangle $\operatorname{ctg} \gamma \neq 0$, let $\lambda$ denote the quantity

$$
\frac{\operatorname{ctg} \alpha+\operatorname{ctg} \beta}{\operatorname{ctg} \gamma}
$$

Prove that in this case
a)

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}=\left(1+\frac{2}{\lambda}\right) c^{2}
$$

b) if $\lambda=2$, then $\gamma$ is not greater than $60^{\circ}$.

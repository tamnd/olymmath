On the plane, there is a square $A B C D$. On the sides $B C$ and $C D$, points $P$ and $Q$ are taken such that $|C P|+|C Q|=|A B|$. Let $M$ be a point in space such that in the tetrahedron $A P Q M$, all faces are equal triangles. Determine the geometric locus of the projections of points $M$ onto the plane perpendicular to the plane of the square and passing through the diagonal $A C$.

The orthocenter of the tetrahedron.

Three spheres with radii $\sqrt{6}$, 1, and 1 touch each other pairwise. A plane $\gamma$ is drawn through the line containing the centers $A$ and $B$ of the second and third spheres, such that the center $O$ of the first sphere is at a distance of 1 from this plane. Find the angle between the projections of the lines $OA$ and $OB$ onto the plane $\gamma$ and compare it with $\arccos \frac{4}{5}$.

Count the Matches. A friend writes that he bought a small box of short matches, each an inch long. He found that he could arrange them in the form of a triangle, the area of which contained as many square inches as there were matches. Then he used 6 matches, and it turned out that from the remaining ones, he could form a new triangle, the area of which contained as many square inches as there were matches left. And after using another 6 matches, he managed to do the same again.

How many matches did he have in the box originally? This number is less than 40.

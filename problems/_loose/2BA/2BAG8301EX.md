The chessboard is marked with stations 0, 1, 2, ..., 100. The chess piece starts at station 0. The player tosses a fair coin to play the game. If heads come up, the piece moves forward one station; if tails come up, the piece moves forward two stations. The game continues until the piece reaches station 99 (Victory Camp) or station 100 (Failure Camp), at which point the game ends. Let the probability of the piece landing on station $n$ be $P_{n}$.
(1) Find the value of $P_{3}$;
(2) Prove: $P_{n+1}-P_{n}=-\frac{1}{2}\left(P_{n}-P_{n-1}\right)(2 \leq n \leq 99)$;
(3) Find the values of $P_{99}, P_{100}$.

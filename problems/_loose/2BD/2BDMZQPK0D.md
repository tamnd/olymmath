For any natural number K, we determine its remainders when divided by each of the ten natural numbers $2,3,4, \ldots, 11$ and sum these ten remainders (some of which may be zero). Determine all such numbers less than 25000 that have the smallest possible sum of these remainders. (We do not consider zero as a natural number.)

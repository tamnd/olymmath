Given a $4 \times 4$ table, some of its cells are to be filled with stars. Prove that it is possible to place seven stars in such a way that after erasing any two rows and any two columns of this table, there will always be at least one star in the remaining cells. Prove that if there are fewer than seven stars, it is always possible to erase two rows and two columns in such a way that all remaining cells will be empty.

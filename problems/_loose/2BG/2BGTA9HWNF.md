In the parliament of Anchuria, some deputies are at odds with each other (and each has at least one enemy), some are friends, and some are indifferent to each other. Friendship, enmity, and indifference are mutual, and for each deputy, the principle applies: "A friend of my enemy is also my enemy." Prove that some deputy has more enemies than friends.

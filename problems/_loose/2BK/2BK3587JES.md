7 students decide to visit 7 cinemas on Sunday, with each cinema's showtimes being $9:00, 10:40, 12:20, 14:00, 15:40, 17:20, 19:00$, and $20:40$ (a total of 8 shows). During each show, 6 students are together, while another student (not necessarily the same person for different shows) goes to a different cinema alone. By evening, it is found that each student has visited every cinema. Prove that each cinema has at least one showtime that was not visited by these students.

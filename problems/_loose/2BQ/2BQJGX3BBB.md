Find all numbers $a$ such that

(1) all numbers $[a], [2a], . . . , [Na]$ are distinct and

(2) all numbers $\left[ \frac{1}{a}\right], \left[ \frac{2}{a}\right], ..., \left[ \frac{M}{a}\right]$ are distinct.

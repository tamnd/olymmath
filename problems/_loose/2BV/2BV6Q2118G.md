The 100th problem: Given $n(n \geq 2)$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots,$ $A_{n}$ on the sphere $\left\{(x, y, z) \mid x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1\right\}$, find the maximum possible value of $\sum_{1 \leq \mathrm{i}<\mathrm{j} \leq \mathrm{n}}\left|A_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{A}_{\mathrm{j}}\right|^{2}$.

Each vertex of a regular polygon is assigned one of the numbers 0 or 1. Using diagonals that do not intersect each other except at the vertices, Rudi divides the polygon into triangles, and then writes the sum of the numbers assigned to the vertices of each triangle inside it. Prove that Rudi can choose the diagonals to divide the polygon in such a way that the largest and smallest of the numbers written in the resulting triangles differ by at most 1.

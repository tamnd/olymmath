In triangle $ABC$ with sides $a, b, c$, the bisector $l_{a}$ is at distances from the vertices whose sum is $h_{A}$; the bisector $l_{b}$ is at distances whose sum is $-h_{B}$; the bisector $l_{c}$ is at distances whose sum is $h_{C}$.

Prove that

$$
h_{A} h_{B} h_{c} \geqslant \frac{1}{8}(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)
$$

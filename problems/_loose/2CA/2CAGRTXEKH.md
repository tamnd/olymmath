On the Island of Logic, there live 40 knights (who always tell the truth), 25 liars (who always lie), and several sophists. A sophist can only utter phrases that a knight or a liar could not say in their place. For example, standing next to a liar, a sophist can say the phrase "We are both liars" (because if he were a knight, such a phrase would be a lie, and if he were a liar, it would be true). One day, a sophist made two statements about the island's inhabitants:
1. "There are exactly ... knights on the island."
2. "There are exactly ... liars on the island."
Reconstruct both statements of the sophist.

Let's define the "d-metric" in the set of random variables by setting

$$
d(\xi, \eta)=\mathrm{E} f(|\xi-\eta|)
$$

for a bounded, increasing, and concave function $ f $ such that $ f(0)=0 $, and identifying random variables that coincide almost surely (in particular, one can take $ f(x)=x /(1+x) $). Show that $ d=d(\xi, \eta) $ indeed defines a metric and that convergence in probability is equivalent to convergence in this metric.

Consider the triangle $ ABC$ with side-lenghts equal to $ a,b,c$. Let $ p=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$, $ R$-the radius of circumcircle of the triangle $ ABC$, $ r$-the radius of the incircle of the triangle $ ABC$ and let $ l_a,l_b,l_c$ be the lenghts of bisectors drawn from $ A,B$ and $ C$, respectively, in the triangle $ ABC$. Prove that:
$ l_al_b+l_bl_c+l_cl_a\leq p\sqrt{3r^2+12Rr}$

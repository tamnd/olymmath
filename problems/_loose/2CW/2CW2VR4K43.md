Let $k>1$ be an integer, set $n=2^{k+1}$. Prove that for any positive integers
$a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_n$, the number $\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq n}(a_i+a_j)$ has at least $k+1$ different prime divisors.

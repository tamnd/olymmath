A bend in a road is formed from two concentric arcs with inside radius $r$ and outside radius $R$, each of a third of a circle with the same centre. The road is then formed of tangents to the arcs.
A cyclist cuts the corner by following an arc of radius $x$ which is tangent to the outside of the road at its ends and tangent to the inside of the road in the middle.
Prove that $r+x=k R$ for some number $k$ to be found.

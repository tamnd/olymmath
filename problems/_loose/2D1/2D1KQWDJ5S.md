Let $z_{1}, \ldots, z_{n}$ be non-zero complex numbers lying in the half-plane $\alpha<\arg z<\alpha+\pi$. Prove that

a) $z_{1}+\ldots+z_{n} \neq 0$

b) $1 / z_{1}+\ldots+1 / z_{n} \neq 0$.

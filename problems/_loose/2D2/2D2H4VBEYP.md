(Inequality of Schur). 1) Prove that, for all real numbers $a, b, c > 0$, we have

$$
a(a-b)(a-c)+b(b-c)(b-a)+c(c-a)(c-b) \geqslant 0
$$

2) Prove that, for any real number $t$ and all real numbers $a, b, c > 0$, we have

$$
a^{t}(a-b)(a-c)+b^{t}(b-c)(b-a)+c^{t}(c-a)(c-b) \geqslant 0
$$

Point $G$ is the center of the inscribed sphere of a regular tetrahedron $ABCD$, and point $O$ is a point inside the tetrahedron. The line $OG$ intersects the planes of the tetrahedron's faces at points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}$. Prove: $\frac{A^{\prime} O}{A^{\prime} G}+\frac{B^{\prime} O}{B^{\prime} G}+\frac{C^{\prime} O}{C^{\prime} G}+\frac{D^{\prime} O}{D^{\prime} G}=4$.

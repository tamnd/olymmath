In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, point $P(2,1)$ lies on the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{6}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$. A line $l$ that does not pass through the origin $O$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A$ and $B$, and the midpoint of segment $A B$ is $D$. The slope of line $O D$ is 1. Let the slopes of lines $P A$ and $P B$ be $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively. Prove: $k_{1} k_{2}$ is a constant.

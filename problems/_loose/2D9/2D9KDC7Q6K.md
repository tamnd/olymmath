Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and the composition law "o" defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by the relation $x \circ y = x y - a x - a y + a^2 + a$, for any $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$.

a) Prove that $(\mathbb{R}, \circ)$ is a commutative monoid;

b) Determine the real numbers $x$ with the property $\underbrace{x \circ x \circ x}_{\text{2013 times } x \circ \text{ each other}} = x$.

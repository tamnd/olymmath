If a $2n$-digit number is a perfect square, and the numbers formed by its first $n$ digits and the last $n$ digits are also perfect squares (here the second $n$-digit number can start with 0, but cannot be 0, and the first $n$-digit number formed by the first $n$ digits cannot start with 0), such a $2n$-digit number is called a "strange number".
(1) Find all two-digit and four-digit strange numbers.
(2) Are there any six-digit strange numbers?
(3) Prove that there exists a 20-digit strange number.
(4) Prove that there are at most 10 strange numbers with 100 digits.
(5) Prove that there exists a 30-digit strange number.
(11th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1977)

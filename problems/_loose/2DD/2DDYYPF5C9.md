After buying a new car, one must expect a depreciation in value over time. For this depreciation, 20% in the first year, 15% in the second year, and another 15% in the third year are assumed, where all these percentages are based on the original purchase price. The resulting reduced value is referred to as the time value.

a) Mrs. Gruebler paid 23800 DM for her new car. Calculate the time value after two years!

b) Mr. Bauer wants to sell his car after three years. At this point, the time value of the car would be 16200 DM. However, due to an accident, the time value decreases by 10% of this amount.

What percentage of the original purchase price does the resulting reduced time value now represent?

c) Mr. Neumann buys a new car for 43000 DM. He wants to sell the car after four years at the time value, which he assumes to be 18275 DM.

What percentage (of the original purchase price) did he assume for the depreciation in the fourth year?

In a country with $N$ cities, some pairs of cities are connected by two-way air routes, with no more than one route between any pair. Each air route belongs to one of $k$ companies. It turned out that from any city, one can reach any other city (possibly with layovers), but if all the air routes of any company are closed, this property is violated. What is the maximum number of air routes (for arbitrary $N$ and $k$) that could exist in this country?

(S. Berlov, N. Vlasova)

Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $2n+1$ with complex coefficients. If the equation $f(\bar{x})=\overline{f(x)}$ has $n+1$ distinct imaginary roots in modulus, then $f(x)$ is a polynomial with real coefficients.

On the board, the numbers $1, 1, -1$ are written. From time to time, Mikhail approaches the board, erases two numbers $a$ and $b$, and replaces them with $2a + c$ and $2^{b - c}$, where $c$ is the third number written on the board at that moment. Prove that there will always be at least one negative number on the board.

(M. Antipov)

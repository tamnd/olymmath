Let $I$ be the incenter of $\triangle A B C$, prove that for any point $P$ on the plane: $a \cdot|P A|^{2}+b \cdot|P B|^{2}+c \cdot$ $|P C|^{2}=a \cdot|I A|^{2}+b \cdot|I B|^{2}+c \cdot|I C|^{2}+(a+b+c) \cdot|I P|^{2}$. (Here $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the three sides of $\triangle A B C$)

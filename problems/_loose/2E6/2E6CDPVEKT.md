Quadrilateral $P Q R S$ is inscribed in the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$ and is a rhombus.
(1) Draw a perpendicular from the origin $O$ to side $P Q$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $H$. Find the locus of $H$;
(2) Find the maximum value of $\frac{1}{|O P|}+\frac{1}{|O Q|}$;
(3) Find the maximum and minimum values of the area of $\triangle P O Q$;
(4) Find the maximum and minimum values of $|P Q|$.

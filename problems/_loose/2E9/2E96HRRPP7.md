Given a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$ with the incircle $\odot I$ touching sides $CA$ and $AB$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively. The circumcircle of $\triangle AEF$ has tangents at points $E$ and $F$ intersecting at point $S$. Line $EF$ intersects $CB$ at point $T$. Prove: The circle with diameter $ST$ is orthogonal to the nine-point circle of $\triangle BIC$. ${ }^{[3]}$
(2015, Taiwan Mathematical Olympiad Training Camp)

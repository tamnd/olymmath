Bend a rigid wire into the shape of an equilateral triangle and weld its endpoints. Allow the wire segment between any two points on the wire to be folded along the line connecting these two points, such that the position of the folded segment is symmetric to its original position with respect to the line through these two points (if the two points coincide, then any line through that point can be chosen as the axis of symmetry). Can a regular hexagon with the same perimeter be obtained after several such folds?

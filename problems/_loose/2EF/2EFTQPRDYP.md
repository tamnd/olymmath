Given a triangle $ABC$ and a point $X$ outside the triangle, show that

$$
\overline{AX}^{2}+\overline{BX}^{2}+\overline{CX}^{2}=\overline{SA}^{2}+\overline{SB}^{2}+\overline{SC}^{2}+3 \overline{SX}^{2}
$$

where $S$ is the centroid of the triangle.

In the country of Nashii, there are military bases connected by roads. A set of roads is called important if after closing these roads, there will be two bases that are not connected by a path. An important set is called strategic if it does not contain a smaller important set. Prove that the set of roads, each of which belongs to exactly one of two different strategic sets, forms an important set.

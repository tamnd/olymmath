In a certain office, six people $A, B, C, D, E, F$ take turns working the night shift. The order of the shifts is $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow D \rightarrow E \rightarrow F \rightarrow A \rightarrow B \cdots$. In the first week, Sunday is covered by $A$, Monday by $B$, $\cdots$, Friday by $F$, and Saturday again by $A$, and so on. Therefore, in the 50th week, $A$ will be on duty on
(A) Saturday.
(B) Friday.
(C) Thursday.
(D) Wednesday.
(China Guangzhou, Wuhan, Fuzhou, and other five cities junior high school mathematics league, 1992)

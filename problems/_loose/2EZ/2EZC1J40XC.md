Given a circle $k$ with center $O$ and radius $r$, and a line $p$ that has no common points with $k$. Let $E$ be the foot of the perpendicular dropped from $O$ to $p$. A point $M$ is chosen arbitrarily on $p$, different from $E$, and two tangents are drawn from $M$ to $k$, touching the circle at $A$ and $B$. If $H$ is the intersection of $AB$ and $OE$, prove that $\overline{O H}=\frac{r^{2}}{\overline{O E}}$.

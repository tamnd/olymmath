Bob starts with an empty whiteboard. He then repeatedly chooses one of the digits $1, 2, \ldots, 9$ (uniformly at random) and appends it to the end of the currently written number. Bob stops when the number on the board is a multiple of $25$ . Let $E$ be the expected number of digits that Bob writes. If $E = \frac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ , find $100m+n$ .

If the function $f(x)$ satisfies $f\left(\log _{a} x\right)=\frac{a}{a^{2}-1}\left(x-x^{-1}\right)$, where $a>0, a \neq 1$.
(1) Determine the odd/even nature and monotonicity of the function $f(x)$;
(2) When $x \in(-1,1)$, $f(1-m)+f\left(1-m^{2}\right)<0$, find the range of real numbers $m$;
(3) When $x \in(-\infty, 2)$, $f(x)-4$ is always negative, find the range of $a$.

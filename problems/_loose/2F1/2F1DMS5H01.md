On the board, $n$ non-negative integers are written. The GCD of these $n$ integers is 1. An operation consists of replacing two integers $x$ and $y$ if $x \leqslant y$ by $2x$ and $y-x$. For which initial configurations is it possible to end up, after a certain number of operations, with $n-1$ zeros written on the board?

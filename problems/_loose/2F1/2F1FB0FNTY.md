A square table of $n^{2}$ cells is filled with integers from 1 to $n$ such that each row and each column of the table contains all numbers from 1 to $n$. Prove that if the table is symmetric with respect to the diagonal leading from its upper left corner to the lower right corner, and $n$ is odd, then all numbers from 1 to $n$ will appear on this diagonal. Will this statement also be true when $n$ is even?

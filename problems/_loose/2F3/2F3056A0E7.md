Find all functions $ f:\mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{ N }_0 $ satisfy the following conditions:

i) $ f(ab)=f(a)+f(b)-f(\gcd(a,b)), \forall a,b \in \mathbb{N} $

ii) For all primes $ p $ and natural numbers $ a $, $ f(a)\ge f(ap) \Rightarrow f(a)+f(p) \ge f(a)f(p)+1 $

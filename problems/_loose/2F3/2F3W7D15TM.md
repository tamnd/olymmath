If $n \in \mathbb{N}$, prove the following equality

$\log \left(1+\frac{1}{1}\right)+2 \log \left(1+\frac{1}{2}\right)+3 \log \left(1+\frac{1}{3}\right)+\ldots+n \log \left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)=(n+1) \log (n+1)-\log ((n+1)!)$

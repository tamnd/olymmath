Show that there are infinitely many positive integers $m$ such that the number of distinct odd prime factors of $m(m+3)$ is a multiple of 3.

Note: For example, the number $60=2^{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ has two distinct odd prime factors and the number $1050=2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^{2} \cdot 7$ has three.

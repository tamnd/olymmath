The Smeshariki live on the shores of a pond in the shape of an equilateral triangle with a side length of 600 meters. Krosh and Barash live on one shore, 300 meters apart from each other. In the summer, it is 900 meters for Losharik to walk to Krosh, and 900 meters for Barash to walk to Nyusha. Prove that in the winter, when the pond freezes and it is possible to walk directly across the ice, the distance for Losharik to walk to Krosh will again be the same as the distance for Barash to walk to Nyusha.

[6 points] (E.V. Bakayev, A.V. Khachatryan)

In a certain state, there are citizens of three types: a) a fool considers everyone to be fools, and himself to be smart; b) a modest smart person knows the truth about everyone, but considers himself a fool; c) a confident smart person knows the truth about everyone, and considers himself smart. In the parliament, there are 200 deputies. The prime minister conducted an anonymous survey of the deputies: how many smart people are in this hall right now? Based on the survey data, he was unable to determine the number of smart people. But then the only deputy who had not participated in the survey returned from a trip. He filled out the survey about the entire parliament, including himself, and after reading it, the prime minister understood everything. How many smart people could there have been in the parliament (including the traveler)?

At the ends of a grid strip measuring $1 \times 101$ cells, two chips are placed: the first player's chip on the left and the second player's chip on the right. On a turn, it is allowed to move one's chip towards the opposite end of the strip by $1, 2, 3,$ or $4$ cells. It is allowed to jump over the opponent's chip, but it is forbidden to place one's chip on the same cell as the opponent's. The player who first reaches the opposite end of the strip wins. Who will win with correct play: the one who moves first, or their opponent?

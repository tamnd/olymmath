Let $\xi_{n} \xrightarrow{L^{p}} \xi$ for some $p \geqslant 1$. Show that then

$$
\mathrm{E}\left(\xi_{n} \mid \mathscr{G}\right) \xrightarrow{L^{p}} \mathrm{E}(\xi \mid \mathscr{G})
$$

for any $\sigma$-subalgebra $\mathscr{G} \subseteq \mathscr{F}$.

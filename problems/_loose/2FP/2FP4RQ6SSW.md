The vertices of a regular 12-sided polygon are placed clockwise with $0,1, \cdots, 11$. The following operation is performed: change the number on one vertex to 0, while adding half of it to the numbers on its two adjacent vertices. Question: After a finite number of operations, can 11 of the 12 vertices have the same number while the other vertex is 0?

The 39th question: Set $\mathrm{S}$ is a set of $\mathrm{n}$ points in the plane, and the distance between any two points in $\mathrm{S}$ is at least 1. Prove: there exists a subset $\mathrm{T}$ of $\mathrm{S}$, such that $|\mathrm{T}| \geq \frac{\mathrm{n}}{7}$, and the distance between any two points in $\mathrm{T}$ is at least $\sqrt{3}$.

Let $a$ and $b$ be real, positive and distinct numbers. We consider the set:
$$M=\{ ax+by\mid x,y\in\mathbb{R},\ x>0,\ y>0,\ x+y=1\} $$
Prove that:

(i) $\frac{2ab}{a+b}\in M;$ (ii) $\sqrt{ab}\in M.$

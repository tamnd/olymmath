\begin{align*}
&\{1,2,3,\cdots,10\}, \\
&\{11,12,13,\cdots,20\},\\
&\{21,22,23,\cdots,30\},\\
&\vdots\\
&\{991,992,993,\cdots,1000\}.
\end{align*}
How many of these sets contain exactly two multiples of $7$?

$\textbf{(A)} 40 \textbf{(B)} 42 \textbf{(C)} 42 \textbf{(D)} 49 \textbf{(E)} 50$

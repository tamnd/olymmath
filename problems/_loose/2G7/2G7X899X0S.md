Given a convex pentagon $A B C D E$. The sides opposite to the vertices $A, B, C, D$, $E$ are the segments $C D, D E, E A, A B, B C$, respectively. Prove that if an arbitrary point $M$, lying inside the pentagon, is connected by lines to all its vertices, then of these lines, either exactly one, or exactly three, or exactly five intersect the sides of the pentagon opposite to the vertices through which they pass.

The 25th question: In country A with 2011 cities, there is a unique bidirectional flight route operated by a certain airline between any city in A and any city in country B, which also has 2011 cities. There are no flight routes between cities within the same country, and at most 19 different airlines operate these routes. Find the maximum integer $\mathrm{k}$, such that no matter how the routes are arranged, there are always $\mathrm{k}$ cities that are connected (one can travel from one city to another, not necessarily directly), and all the routes are operated by a fixed airline.

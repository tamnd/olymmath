Red and Black (from 8th grade, 3 points). The dealer ${ }^{2}$ deals one card at a time to the player from a well-shuffled standard deck of cards. At any moment, while the dealer still has cards left, the player can say "Stop". After this, the dealer reveals one more card. If it is of a red suit, the player wins; if it is black, the player loses.

Does there exist a strategy for the player that makes the probability of winning greater than $0.5 ?$[^6]

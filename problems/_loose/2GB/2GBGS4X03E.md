If $a, b, c$ are the measures of the angles of the acute-angled triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, then show that:

$$
\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
\operatorname{tg} a & \operatorname{tg} c-1 & \operatorname{tg} b+1 \\
\operatorname{tg} c+\operatorname{tg} b & \operatorname{tg} a-1 & 1 \\
\operatorname{tg} a \cdot \operatorname{tg} b \cdot \operatorname{tg} c & -1 & 1
\end{array}\right| \leq 0
$$

In the right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at vertex $C$, the altitude $CD$, the angle bisector $CL$ of angle $ACB$, and the angle bisectors $DK$ and $DN$ of angles $ADC$ and $BDC$ are constructed. Here, $D, L \in AB$, $K \in AC$, and $N \in BC$. Prove that the points $C, K, L, D$, and $N$ lie on the same circle. Prove that $\overline{KN} = \overline{CL}$.

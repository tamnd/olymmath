Prove that for any triangle $ABC$ the following dependence is valid:

$$
O^{\prime} A \cdot O^{\prime} B \cdot O^{\prime} C=4 R r^{2}
$$

where $O^{\prime}$ is the incenter, and $r$ and $R$ are the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of this triangle.

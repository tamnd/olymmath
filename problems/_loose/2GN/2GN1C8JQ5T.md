Given a non-isosceles acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with altitudes $AD, BE, CF$, where $D, E, F$ are the feet of the perpendiculars. The circumcircles of $\triangle AEF, \triangle BFD, \triangle CDE$ intersect the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle ABC$ at the second points $A_1, B_1, C_1$, respectively. The lines $A_1D, B_1E, C_1F$ intersect $\odot O$ at the second points $A_2, B_2, C_2$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $AA_2, BB_2, CC_2$ are concurrent;
(2) $AA_2^2 + BB_2^2 + CC_2^2 \geq \frac{4(ab + bc + ca)^2}{3(a^2 + b^2 + c^2)}$, where $a = BC, b = CA, c = AB$.

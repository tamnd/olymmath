Given is an acute-angled triangle $A B C$. Let $k$ be the circle with diameter $\overline{A B}$. The circle that touches the angle bisector of $\angle B A C$ at point $A$ and passes through point $C$ intersects circle $k$ at point $P$ different from $A$. Similarly, the circle that touches the angle bisector of $\angle C B A$ at point $B$ and passes through point $C$ intersects circle $k$ at point $Q$ different from $B$. Prove that the lines $A Q$ and $B P$ intersect on the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$.

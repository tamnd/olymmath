Real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy $$xy+\frac{x}{y} = 3$$ $$\frac{1}{x^2y^2}+\frac{y^2}{x^2} = 4$$

If $x^2$ can be expressed in the form $\frac{a+\sqrt{b}}{c}$ for integers $a$, $b$, and $c$. Find $a+b+c$.

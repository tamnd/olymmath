The unit-sided equilateral triangle plate $KLM=H$ is placed such that its side $KL$ coincides with the side $AB$ of the square plate $ABCD=N$ with a side length of 2 units, with the midpoints of these two sides coinciding, and $H$ and $N$ are on opposite sides of the line $AB$. Starting from this position, $H$ rolls along the perimeter of $N$ until each of its vertices returns to its original position. (By rolling, we mean that $H$ rotates around the vertex $K$ until the side $KM$ aligns with the line $AB$, and then continues to rotate around an appropriate point on its perimeter, circling $N$ in the direction it started.)

- How far do the vertices, midpoints of the sides, and the centroid of $H$ travel until they return to their original positions, and by what angle does $H$ itself rotate to reach these positions?
- Also provide a brief answer for the case where $N$ rolls similarly around $H$.

(BXMO 2010) Let $A, B$, and $P$ be three collinear points in that order on a line $l$. Let $a$ be the line perpendicular to line $l$ passing through point $A$. Let $b$ be the line perpendicular to line $l$ passing through point $B$. A line passing through point $P$ and distinct from line $l$ intersects line $a$ at $Q$ and line $b$ at $R$. The perpendicular to line $(B Q)$ passing through $A$ intersects line $(B Q)$ at point $L$ and line $(B R)$ at point $T$. The perpendicular to line $(A R)$ passing through point $B$ intersects line $(A R)$ at $K$ and line $(A Q)$ at $S$.

1) Show that the points $P, T$, and $S$ are collinear.
2) Show that the points $P, K$, and $L$ are collinear.

Alice and Bob play the following game in turns. The number 2 is written on the board. If the integer $n$ is written on the board, the player whose turn it is erases it and writes an integer of the form $n+p$, where $p$ is a prime divisor of $n$. The first player to write a number greater than $2023^{2023}$ loses. If Alice starts, determine which player has a winning strategy.

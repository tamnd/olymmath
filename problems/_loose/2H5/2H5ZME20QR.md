Let $a, b$ be real numbers, and the function $f(x)=x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x$. If there exist three real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$ satisfying $x_{1}+1 \leq x_{2} \leq x_{3}-1$, and $f\left(x_{1}\right)=f\left(x_{2}\right)=f\left(x_{3}\right)$, and the minimum value of $|a|+2|b|$.

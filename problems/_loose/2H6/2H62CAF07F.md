Petar had 40 cows, the first of which gave $1 l$ of milk, the second $2 l$ of milk, the third $3 l$ of milk, and so on, with the fortieth cow giving 40 l of milk. Petar wanted to divide the cows among his 4 sons so that each would get ten cows and the sons would receive the same number of liters of milk from the cows they received. Can Petar divide the cows? If so, find one such division.

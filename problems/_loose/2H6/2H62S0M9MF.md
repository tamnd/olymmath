Let $n, p, k \in \mathbb{N}$. Let $f$ be a monic polynomial of degree $n$ with integer coefficients. Suppose that none of the integers $f(k), f(k+1), \ldots, f(k+p)$ is divisible by $p+1$. Show that $f$ has no rational root.

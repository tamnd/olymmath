Let $(G, *)$ be a group and $H \subseteq G$ a subset of $G$. Show that $H$ is a subgroup of $G$ if and only if the following two properties are simultaneously satisfied:

1. $H$ is non-empty;
2. for all elements $x$ and $y$ of $H, x * y^{-1}$ belongs to $H$.

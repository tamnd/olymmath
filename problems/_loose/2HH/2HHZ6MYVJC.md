Three pairwise non-intersecting circles $\omega_{x}, \omega_{y}, \omega_{z}$ with radii $r_{x}, r_{y}, r_{z}$ respectively lie on the same side of a line $t$ and touch it at points $X, Y, Z$ respectively. It is known that $Y$ is the midpoint of segment $X Z$, $r_{x}=r_{z}=r$, and $r_{y}>r$. Let $p$ be one of the common internal tangents to circles $\omega_{x}$ and $\omega_{y}$, and $q$ be one of the common internal tangents to circles $\omega_{y}$ and $\omega_{z}$. The intersection of lines $p, q, t$ forms an isosceles triangle. Prove that the radius of the inscribed circle in this triangle is $r$.

(P. Kozhevnikov)

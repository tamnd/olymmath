Given that $\triangle ABC$ is a non-isosceles acute triangle, its circumcircle is $\odot O$, the orthocenter is $H$, and the midpoint of $BC$ is $M$. The circle with $BC$ as its diameter intersects the line $AH$ at two points $D$ and $D'$, where point $D'$ is inside $\triangle ABC$. The line $AO$ intersects $MD$ at point $X$.
(1) Prove that line $AM$ bisects segment $D'X$.
(2) Define points $E$ and $F$ similarly to point $D$, and define points $Y$ and $Z$ similarly to point $X$. The tangents to $\odot O$ at points $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $S$, and the midpoint of segment $AS$ projects onto line $AO$ at point $G$. Prove that there exists a point that has equal power with respect to the four circles $\odot O$, the circumcircle of $\triangle SGO$, the circumcircle of $\triangle BYE$, and the circumcircle of $\triangle CZF$.

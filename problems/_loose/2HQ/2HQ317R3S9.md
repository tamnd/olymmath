For the quadrilateral $A B C D$, prove that

$$
\overline{A B}^{2}+\overline{B C}^{2}+\overline{C D}^{2}+\overline{D A}^{2}=\overline{A C}^{2}+\overline{B D}^{2}+4 \overline{M N}^{2}
$$

if the midpoints of the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ are denoted by $M$ and $N$, respectively.

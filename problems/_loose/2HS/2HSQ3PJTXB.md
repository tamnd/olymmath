Inside an equilateral triangle with an area of 1, 5 points are placed arbitrarily. Prove that within this equilateral triangle, it is always possible to draw 3 equilateral triangles to cover these 5 points, such that the sides of these 3 equilateral triangles are parallel to the sides of the original triangle, and the sum of their areas does not exceed 0.64.

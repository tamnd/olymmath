Given a parallelepiped $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$. Prove that the sum of the squares of the areas of three of its pairwise non-parallel faces is equal to the sum of the squares of the areas of the faces of the tetrahedron $A_{1} B C_{1} D$.

The number ( $[\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}]$, c) is called the mixed product of vectors $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$, and c. It is easy to verify that the absolute value of this number is equal to the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by vectors a, b, and c, and this number is positive if a, b, and c form a right-handed set of vectors.

Given that the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ touches sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively, and the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$, $\odot O$, intersects the circumcircles of $\triangle AEF$, $\Gamma_{A}$, $\triangle BFD$, $\Gamma_{B}$, and $\triangle CDE$, $\Gamma_{C}$, at the second points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ respectively. Prove:
(1) Circles $\Gamma_{A}$, $\Gamma_{B}$, and $\Gamma_{C}$ intersect at one point;
(2) $PD$, $QE$, and $RF$ are concurrent.

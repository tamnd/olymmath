In this problem, we will talk about two married couples from the island of Bahava: Mr. and Mrs. A, and Mr. and Mrs. B. During the questioning, three of them gave the following statements.

Mr. A: Mr. B is a knight.

Mrs. A: My husband is right: Mr. B is a knight.

Mrs. B: What is true is true. My husband is indeed a knight.

Who are these four people - knights, liars, or normal people, and which of the three statements are true?

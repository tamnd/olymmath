Determine if there exists a set $S \subset \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, the number of ways to write $n$ as a sum of strictly positive integers where each number appears at most 2 times is equal to the number of ways to write $n$ as a sum of elements of $S$ (without considering the order of the terms).

$$S_1={(x,y)\in \mathbb{R}^2:|x+|x||+|y+|y||\le 2}$$
$$S_2={(x,y)\in \mathbb{R}^2:|x-|x||+|y-|y||\le 2}$$
Find the area of the intersection of $S_1$ and $S_2$

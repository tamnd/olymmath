If the lateral surface of a right cylinder is developed, a rectangle is obtained whose diagonals are of length $l$ and form an angle of $30^{\circ}$ with the base of the rectangle. The volume of the cylinder is
(A) $\frac{1}{16} \frac{l^{3}}{\pi}$
(B) $\frac{3}{8} l^{3}$
(C) $\frac{3}{4} l^{3}$
(D) $\frac{3}{32} \frac{l^{3}}{\pi}$

(E) the above answers are all incorrect.

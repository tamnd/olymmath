For a positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ denote the number obtained by reversing the digits of $n$ in decimal notation. (So $f(2500)=52, f(1456)=6541$.) Find all positive integers $k$ such that for any multiple $n$ of $k$, $k$ is also a divisor of $f(n)$.

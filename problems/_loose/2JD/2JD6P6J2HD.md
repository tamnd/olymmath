At the factory named after Sailor Zheleznyak, they manufacture rectangles with a length of 2 m and a width of 1 m. The length is measured by worker Ivanov, and the width, independently of Ivanov, is measured by worker Petrov. Both have an average error of zero, but Ivanov has a standard measurement error (standard deviation of length) of 3 mm, and Petrov has a standard error of 2 mm.

a) Find the expected value of the area of the resulting rectangle.

b) Find the standard deviation of the area of the resulting rectangle in square centimeters.

In any triangle $A B C$, let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $B C$ and $A C$, respectively. Prove that the centroid of triangle $A B C$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $C M N$ if and only if the following equality holds:

$$
4 \cdot|A M| \cdot|B N|=3 \cdot|A C| \cdot|B C| .
$$

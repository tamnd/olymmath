Let $k, k \geqslant 14, P_{k}$ be the largest prime number less than $k$. If $P_{k} \geqslant \frac{3}{4} k, n$ is a composite number. Prove: If $n >$ $2 P_{k}$ then $n$ divides $(n-k)!$.

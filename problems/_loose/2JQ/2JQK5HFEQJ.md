The city has the shape of a square $n \times n$, divided into blocks $1 \times 1$. Streets run from north to south and from west to east. Each morning, a person walks from the southwest corner to the northeast corner, moving only north or east, and each evening, they return, moving only south or west. Each morning, he chooses his path so that the total length of familiar segments of the path (those he has already walked in any direction) is minimal, and each evening as well. Prove that in $n$ days, he will have walked all the streets completely.

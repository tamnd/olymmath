Let $A B C$ be a triangle such that $A B \neq A C$. Let $E$ be such that $A E=B E$ and $(B E)$ is perpendicular to $(B C)$ and let $F$ be such that $A F=C F$ and $(C F)$ is perpendicular to (BC). Let $\mathrm{D}$ be the point on $(\mathrm{BC})$ such that $(A D)$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $A B C$ at A.

Show that the points $D, E, F$ are collinear.

Let $T$ be the centroid of $\triangle ABC$, and let $t$ be an arbitrary line through $T$ such that vertices $A$ and $B$ are on one side of it, and vertex $C$ is on the other. Let points $A_{0}, B_{0}$, and $C_{0}$ be the orthogonal projections of points $A, B$, and $C$, respectively, onto line $t$. Prove:

$$
A A_{0}+B B_{0}=C C_{0} \text {. }
$$

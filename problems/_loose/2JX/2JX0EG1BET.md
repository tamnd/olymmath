How to convince her?

Suppose you are one of the inhabitants of the island of knights and liars. You have fallen in love with a girl and want to marry her. But your beloved has strange tastes: for some inexplicable reason, she does not want to marry a knight and will only consent to be the wife of a liar. Moreover, she insists on a rich liar, not a poor one (for convenience, we will assume that all liars on the island are either rich or poor). Suppose you are a rich liar. You are allowed to say only one sentence to your beloved. How can you convince her with just one sentence that you are a rich liar?

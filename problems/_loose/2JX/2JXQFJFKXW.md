Hardy's Inequality Let $a_{k} \geqslant 0(k$ $=0,1, \cdots, n)$. Prove: For any integer $p$ greater than 1, we have
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i}}{k}\right)^{p} \leqslant\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p} \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{p} .
$$

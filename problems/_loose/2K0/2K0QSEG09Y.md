In an $n \times n$ table of cells, there are "+" and "-". It is allowed to take a cell and change the signs in the column and row passing through this cell (at the same time, the sign of the cell itself also changes). Find all $n$ for which, from any initial arrangement of signs in the table, one can transition to a table of all plus signs.

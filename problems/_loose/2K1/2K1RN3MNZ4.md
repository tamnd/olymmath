11-4 (G30, Netherlands) Let $Y$ be the semicircular arc with diameter $AB$, and let $C$ be a point on arc $Y$ different from $A$ and $B$. Let $D$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $AB$. Let $Y_{1}, Y_{2}, Y_{3}$ be three circles that are tangent to the common tangent $AB$, where $Y_{1}$ is the incircle of $\triangle ABC$, and $Y_{2}$ and $Y_{3}$ are both tangent to $CD$ and the semicircle $Y$. Prove that the circles $Y_{1}, Y_{2}, Y_{3}$ have a second common tangent.

Let $a \geqslant 3, g(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients, $\operatorname{deg} g(x)=n$. Prove:
$$
\max _{0 \leq i \leq n+1}\left|a^{i}-g(i)\right| \geqslant 1 .
$$

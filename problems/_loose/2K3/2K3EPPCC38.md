Prove that for all positive $a_1. a_2, ..., a_n$ the inequality $$ \left( 1+\frac{a_1^2}{a_2}\right) \left( 1+\frac{a_2^2}{a_3}\right) ...\left( 1+\frac{a_n^2}{a_1}\right) \ge (1+a_1)(1+a_2)...(1+a_n) $$ holds.

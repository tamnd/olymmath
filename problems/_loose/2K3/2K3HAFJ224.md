Let $A, B, C, D$ be four distinct points placed in this order on a line. The circles with diameters $[A C]$ and $[B D]$ intersect at $X$ and $Y$. The line $(X Y)$ intersects $(B C)$ at $Z$. Let $P$ be a point distinct from $Z$ on the line $(X Y)$. The line $(C P)$ intersects the circle with diameter $A C$ at $C$ and $M$, and the line $(B P)$ intersects the circle with diameter $[B D]$ at $B$ and $N$.

1) Show that the points $M, B, C, N$ are concyclic.
2) Show that $A, M, N$ and $D$ are concyclic. Prove that the lines $(A M), (D N), (X Y)$ are concurrent.

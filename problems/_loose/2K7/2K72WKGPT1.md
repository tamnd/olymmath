In Dwarf Village, an epidemic raised its head after a nasty disease infected a few dwarfs. Fortunately, every dwarf recovers from the illness in one day, and then becomes immune for one day, but unfortunately, they can be reinfected after that. The change in status always occurs at night, during sleep. Unfortunately, the dwarfs do not give up their ingrained habit of visiting every friend every day, even when sick. Moreover, if a sick dwarf meets a healthy, non-immune dwarf, the latter will certainly become infected. Show that if 100 dwarfs live in Dwarf Village, then by the 101st day following the outbreak of the epidemic, it is certainly over.

On the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ (or on their extensions), points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken respectively. Prove that points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ lie on one straight line if and only if

$$
\frac{\overline{B A_{1}}}{\overline{C A_{1}}} \cdot \frac{\overline{C B_{1}}}{A B_{1}} \cdot \frac{\overline{A C_{1}}}{\overline{B C_{1}}}=1 (\text{Menelaus' theorem})
$$

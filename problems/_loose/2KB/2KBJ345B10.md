Two parallel classes of a school are in academic competition. One class has 2 more students than the other. By mid-term, there were no failing students in either class. In class $A$, $15\%$ of the students are excellent and have outstanding results (5s), a quarter of the class has average results (3s), a fifth of the class has satisfactory results (2s), and the number of good students (4s) is 4 more than twice the number of 5s. In class $B$, there is 1 fewer 5 than in class $A$, the number of 4s is 4 less than half the class, and $5/6$ of the remaining students have average results. Which class won the competition with a better average grade?

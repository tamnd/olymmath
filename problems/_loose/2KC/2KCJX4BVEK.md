Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with the general term $a_{n}=15 n+2+(15 n-32) \cdot 16^{n-1}, n=0,1,2, \cdots$
(1) Prove that for each non-negative integer $n, 15^{3} \mid a_{n}$.
(2) Find all $n$ such that $1991\left|a_{n}, 1991\right| a_{n+1}, 1991 \mid a_{n+2}$.
(1991 Chinese National Team Training Problem)

Let $b_{1}<b_{2}<\ldots$ be the sequence of all natural numbers that can be written as the sum of the squares of two natural numbers. Prove: There exist infinitely many positive integers $\mathrm{n}$, such that $\mathrm{b}_{\mathrm{n}+1}-\mathrm{b}_{\mathrm{n}}=2015$.

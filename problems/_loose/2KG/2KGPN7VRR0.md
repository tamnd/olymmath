Given the triangle $ABC$ and the point $P$ in its plane. Move $C$ to $E$ by the spiral similarity (i.e., rotation followed by dilation or contraction) centered at $A$ that takes $B$ to $P$. Move $C$ to $F$ by the spiral similarity centered at $B$ that takes $A$ to $P$. It is to be proven that $\overrightarrow{P E}+\overrightarrow{P F}=\overrightarrow{P C}$.

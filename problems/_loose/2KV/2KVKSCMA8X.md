The following half-completed equation was written on the board:

$$
x^{3}+\square x^{2}+\square x+\square=0
$$

Two players play a game. The first player can write a suitable integer in one of the three empty rectangles. Then the second player writes any integer in one of the remaining two rectangles, and finally, the first player writes a suitable integer in the last remaining empty rectangle.

Prove that the first player can always achieve that the resulting cubic equation has three - not necessarily distinct - integer roots.

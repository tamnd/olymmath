Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, $I$ be the center of the incircle, and $I_{a}$ be the center of the excircle opposite to vertex $A$, which touches side $BC$. Prove that:

a) $d^{2}=R^{2}-2 R r$, where $d=O I$

b) $d_{a}^{2}=R^{2}+2 R r_{a}$, where $d_{a}=O I_{a}$.

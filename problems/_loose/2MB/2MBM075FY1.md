Let $G$ be a connected graph with $n+1$ vertices whose edges are colored with three different colors: red, green, and blue. Let $r, v, b$ be three non-negative integers such that $r+v+b=n$. Suppose that $G$ has a spanning tree with exactly $r$ red edges, one with exactly $v$ green edges, and one with exactly $b$ blue edges. Show that it has a spanning tree with exactly $r$ red edges, $v$ green edges, and $b$ blue edges.

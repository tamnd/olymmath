They sit in a circle in order to play a game, starting with the first child giving 2 candies to the second child, the second child giving 4 candies to the third child, $\cdots$, that is, each child always passes on the candies received from the previous child plus 2 more of their own candies to the next child. When the game reaches a point where a child receives candies from the previous child but cannot pass on the required number of candies, the ratio of candies between two adjacent children is $13 ： 1$.

Let's determine the geometric place of points $P(u, v)$ in the coordinate plane for which the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin ^{2 n} x+\cos ^{2 n} y & =u \\
\sin ^{n} x+\cos ^{n} y & =v
\end{aligned}
$$

has a solution (where $n$ is a natural number).

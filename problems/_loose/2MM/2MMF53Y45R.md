(2003 Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad) (1) If a positive integer can be expressed as the arithmetic mean of some positive integers (these positive integers are non-negative integer powers of 2, and can be the same), then it is called a "good number". Prove that all positive integers are "good numbers".
(2) If a positive integer cannot be expressed as the arithmetic mean of some pairwise distinct positive integers (these positive integers are non-negative integer powers of 2), then it is called a "bad number". Prove that there are infinitely many "bad numbers".

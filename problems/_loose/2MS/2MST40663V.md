Let $n \geq 2$ be an integer. Determine the smallest positive integer $m$ such that: given $n$ points in the plane, no three on a line, there are $m$ lines to be found, such that no line passes through any of the given points and such that for each pair of given points $X \neq Y$, there is a line such that $X$ and $Y$ lie on opposite sides.

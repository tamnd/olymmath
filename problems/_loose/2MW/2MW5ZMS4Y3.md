Ward and Gabriëlle are playing a game on a large sheet of paper. At the beginning, there are 999 ones written on the paper. Ward and Gabriëlle take turns, with Ward starting. A player whose turn it is may choose two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the paper for which $\operatorname{gcd}(a, b)=1$, erase these numbers, and write the number $a+b$ on the paper. The first one who cannot make a move loses. Determine who of Ward and Gabriëlle can certainly win this game.

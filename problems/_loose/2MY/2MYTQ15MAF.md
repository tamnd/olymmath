For any given $n \geqslant 3(n \in$ $\left.\mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$, take $n$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots$, $A_{n}$ on the circumference of $\odot O$. Among the $\mathrm{C}_{n}^{2}$ angles $\angle A_{i} O A_{j}(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)$, there are $S(n)$ angles that are not greater than $120^{\circ}$. Find the minimum value of $S(n)$.

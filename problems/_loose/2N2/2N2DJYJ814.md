Let $p>2$ be a prime number. Let the set
$$
M=\{a \mid k!\equiv a(\bmod p), 1 \leqslant k \leqslant p-1\} \text {. }
$$

Prove: The set $M$ contains at least $\sqrt{p}$ distinct elements.

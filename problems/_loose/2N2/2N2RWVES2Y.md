The delegates at the conference are to elect a committee, and each delegate should propose 10 candidates. If a committee has at least one member who is a candidate nominated by a delegate, then the committee is called "appropriate" for that delegate. It is known that for any 6 delegates, there is a committee of only two people that is appropriate for them. Prove that it is possible to select a committee of 10 people that is appropriate for all delegates.

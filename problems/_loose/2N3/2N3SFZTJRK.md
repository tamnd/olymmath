Let $\xi=\left(\xi_{0}, \xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}\right)$ be a Markov chain with values in $X$ and $f=f(x)(x \in X)$ be some function. Will the sequence $\left(f\left(\xi_{0}\right), f\left(\xi_{1}\right), \ldots, f\left(\xi_{n}\right)\right)$ form a Markov chain? Will the "reverse" sequence $\left(\xi_{n}, \xi_{n-1}, \ldots, \xi_{0}\right)$ be a Markov chain?

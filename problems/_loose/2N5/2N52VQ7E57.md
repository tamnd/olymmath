A country contains $n \geqslant 1$ cities and $k \geqslant 1$ airlines. Some of the cities are connected by air routes, which belong to one of the $\mathrm{k}$ airlines. We assume that if an airline owns the route from city $A$ to city $\mathrm{B}$, then it also owns the route from city $\mathrm{B}$ to city $\mathrm{A}$. Furthermore, we assume that if an airline owns two routes, then these two routes have a city in common. Show that the $n$ cities can be partitioned into at most $k+2$ groups such that in each group, no city is connected by a route to another.

We inscribe a sphere in an equilateral cone, to which we lay an tangent plane parallel to the base of the cone. We then inscribe another sphere in the resulting cone, and so on. Determine the sum of the volumes of the spheres if this procedure is continued to infinity. The slant height of the cone is $l=2$.

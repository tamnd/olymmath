Prove: For any 8 real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{7}, a_{8}$, among the following 6 numbers
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{1} a_{3}+a_{2} a_{4}, a_{1} a_{5}+a_{2} a_{6}, a_{1} a_{7}+a_{2} a_{8}, \\
a_{3} a_{5}+a_{1} a_{6}, a_{3} a_{7}+a_{4} a_{8}, a_{5} a_{7}+a_{6} a_{8}
\end{array}
$$

at least one of them is non-negative, and provide an 8-tuple of numbers where exactly one of them is non-negative.

Lo Jac the wizard has an infinite number of rubies and sapphires. He receives goblins and wants to give each one a natural number of rubies and sapphires. He knows that the goblins are very jealous: if one goblin sees that another has as many or more rubies and as many or more sapphires than him, they will fight. Show that Lo Jac can satisfy all the goblins if and only if they are in finite number.

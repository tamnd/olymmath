A prime number $p$ has the property that:
For all integers $a$ and $b$ , the sequence defined by the conditions $x_1 = a, x_2 = b$ , and $x_{n+2} = x_{n+1} + x_n$ for $n \ge 1$ , contains a term that is a multiple of $p$ .

The sequence of Fibonacci numbers is defined by the relations $F_1 = F_2 = 1$ and $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_n$ for $n \ge 1$ . Prove that $F_{p+1}$ is divisible by $p$ .

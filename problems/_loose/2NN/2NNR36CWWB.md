(Mantel's Theorem) Suppose in space there are $n$ points, no four of which are coplanar, and there are $q$ line segments connecting them. Then these line segments form at least $\frac{4 q}{3 n}\left(q-\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right)$ different triangles.

One, (20 points) Let real numbers $a, b$ satisfy
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a^{2}\left(b^{2}+1\right)+b(b+2 a)=40, \\
a(b+1)+b=8 .
\end{array}
$$

Find the value of $\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}$.

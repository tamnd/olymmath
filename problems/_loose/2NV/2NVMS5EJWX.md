Let

$$
\begin{gathered}
1+2+3 \ldots+2 n=S_{1} 1^{3}+2^{3}+3^{3} \ldots+(2 n)^{3}=S_{3} ; \\
-1^{2}+2^{2}-3^{2}+4^{2}-\ldots+(2 n)^{2}=V_{2} \\
-1^{4}+2^{4}-3^{4}+4^{4}-\ldots+(2 n)^{4}=V_{4} .
\end{gathered}
$$

It is to be proven that

$$
V_{4}+2 V_{2}=S_{1}+2 S_{3} .
$$

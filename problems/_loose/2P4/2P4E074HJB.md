Given that $\angle A P B$ has an incircle tangent to the sides at points $A, B$, and $P C D$ is any secant line, intersecting the circle at points $C, D$. Point $Q$ is on $C D$ such that $\angle Q A D = \angle P B C$. Prove:
$$
\angle P A C = \angle Q B D \text{. }
$$
(2003, National High School Mathematics League Competition)

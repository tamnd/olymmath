It is known that in an $n \times n$ square table of numbers, all numbers are integers and the absolute difference between any two adjacent (meaning they share a side) numbers does not exceed 1. Prove that there is a number that appears in the table at least (1) $\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]$ times; (2) $n$ times.

The graph of the function $y=f(x)$ is $c$. The graph $c_{1}$ is the reflection of $c$ about the line $x=1$, and the graph $c_{2}$ is obtained by shifting $c_{1}$ one unit to the left. Then the function corresponding to $c_{2}$ is
(A) $y=f(-x)$.
(B) $y=f(1-x)$.
(C) $y=f(2-x)$.
(D) $y=f(3-x)$.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1992)

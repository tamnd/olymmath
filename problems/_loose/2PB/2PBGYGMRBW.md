Let $ABC$ be a non-equilateral triangle. Suppose there exists a point $P$ inside the triangle such that the three cevians ${ }^{1}$ from $P$ have the same length $\lambda$, with $\lambda$ being less than the three sides $AB, BC, CA$.

Prove that there exists another point $P^{\prime} \neq P$, inside the triangle, for which the three cevians are of the same length.

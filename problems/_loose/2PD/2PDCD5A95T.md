Show that there exists a positive integer $N$ such that for all positive integers $n>N$ , you can place $n$ points in the plane such that: $\bullet$ No three points are collinear. $\bullet$ There are at most $0.01 n^3$ ways of picking three of the $n$ points such that the triangle formed by them contains at most $n^{0.99}$ points.

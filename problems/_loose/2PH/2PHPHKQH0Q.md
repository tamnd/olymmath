Ali-Baba has 40 bags of coins. The genie can, at Ali-Baba's request, determine the number of coins in each of two specified bags, but in doing so, will take one coin from one of these bags (and Ali-Baba will not see from which one). Can Ali-Baba act in such a way that after no more than 100 such procedures, he can accurately state how many coins are currently in each of the bags, except for the two that the genie counted last? Each bag contains no fewer than 1000 coins.

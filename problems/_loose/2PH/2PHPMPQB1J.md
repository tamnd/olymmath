We will prove that if $\alpha=\frac{2 \pi}{n}$ and $r$ is any positive integer, then
a) $\sin r \alpha+\sin 2 r \alpha+\cdots+\sin n r \alpha=0$
b) $\cos r \alpha+\cos 2 r \alpha+\cdots+\cos n r \alpha=n$, or 0,

depending on whether $n$ is a divisor of $r$ or not.

The numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ are pairwise distinct, and the number $a$ is positive. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the intervals on which the inequality $\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x-a_{k}}>a$ holds is $\frac{n}{a}$.

Let $a, b$ and $c$ be three strictly positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{a^{2}+b c}+\frac{b^{2}}{b^{2}+c a}+\frac{c^{2}}{c^{2}+a b} \geqslant \frac{a+b+c}{2}
$$

The equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ , with non-zero integers $a, b, c$ , has a positive real solution smaller than $\frac{1}{2025}$ .

(a) Give an example of an equation that satisfies the statement, in which one of the numbers $a, b, c$ is equal to 2025 and the other two numbers have an absolute value less than or equal to 2025.

(b) Prove that at least one of the numbers $a, b,$ or $c$ has an absolute value greater than or equal to 2025.

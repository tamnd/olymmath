\section*{Task 4 - V11034}

Draw a circle and outside this circle, place the point $A$. Connect the point $A$ with the center $M$ of the circle.

The point $X$ on the line $A M$ is sought, such that for the tangents drawn from this point to the circle, the tangent segments $X T_{1}$ and $X T_{2}$ are equal to the distance of the point $X$ from the point $A$. ( $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ are the points of tangency.)

Justify your construction!

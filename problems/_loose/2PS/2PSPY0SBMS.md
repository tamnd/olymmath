A bumblebee flew to an apple tree at a speed of $v_{1}$ m/min. At the same time, a bee flew to another apple tree at a speed of $v_{2}$ m/min. The bumblebee had to cover a distance of $2 a$ m, while the bee had to cover a distance of $2 b$ m. Suppose the trajectories of their flights are mutually perpendicular straight lines intersecting at a point that divides the path of the bumblebee and the path of the bee in half. Find the formula expressing the dependence of the distance $y$ between the bumblebee and the bee on the time $x$ of their flight. Determine the moment when the distance between the bumblebee and the bee reaches its minimum value during their flight.

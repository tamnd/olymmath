Continuous functions $ f, g: [0; 1] \rightarrow [0; 1] $ have the property that $ f(g(x)) = g(f(x)) $ for any $ x $ from $[0; 1]$. Prove that if $ f $ is increasing, then there exists $ a \in [0; 1] $ such that $ f(a) = g(a) = a $.

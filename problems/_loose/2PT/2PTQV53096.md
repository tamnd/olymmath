Let $p,q,r$ be distinct prime numbers and let
$$A=\{p^aq^br^c\mid 0\le a,b,c\le 5\} $$
Find the least $n\in\mathbb{N}$ such that for any $B\subset A$ where $|B|=n$ , has elements $x$ and $y$ such that $x$ divides $y$ .

*Ioan Tomescu*

Given a rectangle $A B C D$. On the smaller arc $A B$ of the circle circumscribed around $A B C D$, a point $M$ is chosen arbitrarily, and two lines parallel to the sides of the rectangle are drawn through it. One of these lines intersects segments $A B$ and $C D$ at points $P$ and $R$, respectively, and the other intersects lines $B C$ and $D A$ at points $Q$ and $S$, respectively. Prove that the lines $P Q$ and $R S$ are mutually perpendicular and intersect on the diagonal of the rectangle $A B C D$.

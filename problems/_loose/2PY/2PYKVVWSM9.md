The 23rd question, find the largest $\lambda$, such that for any positive integer $n$ and distinct positive integers $k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{n}$, we have $\left(\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \frac{1}{\mathrm{k}_{\mathrm{i}}}\right)\left(\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \sqrt{\mathrm{k}_{\mathrm{i}}{ }^{6}+\mathrm{k}_{\mathrm{i}}{ }^{3}}\right)-\left(\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{k}_{\mathrm{i}}\right)^{2} \geq \lambda \mathrm{n}^{2}\left(\mathrm{n}^{2}-1\right)$.

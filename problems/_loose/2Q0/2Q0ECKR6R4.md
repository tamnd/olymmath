Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ be the plane angles of the trihedral angle $SABC$ with vertex $S$, opposite to the edges $SA, SB, SC$ respectively; $A, B, C$ be the dihedral angles at these edges. Prove that

$$
\cos \alpha=\frac{\cos A+\cos \theta \cdot \cos C}{\sin B \sin C}, \cos \beta=\frac{\cos B+\cos A \cos C}{\sin A \sin C}, \cos \gamma=\frac{\cos C+\cos A \cos B}{\sin A \sin B} .
$$

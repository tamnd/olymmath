Let the circumradius and inradius of $\triangle A B C$ be $R$ and $r$, respectively. Prove that:
$$
\frac{R-\sqrt{R^{2}-2 R r}}{2 R} \leqslant \sin \frac{A}{2} \leqslant \frac{R+\sqrt{R^{2}-2 R r}}{2 R} .
$$

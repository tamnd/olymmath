Given a sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with all terms no less than 1, it satisfies:
$$
a_{1}=1, a_{2}=1+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \left(\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}-1}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{a_{n}-1}{a_{n-1}}\right)^{2}=2 .
$$

Try to find:
(1) The general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) The value of $\lim _{n \rightarrow+\infty} \frac{a_{n}}{n}$.

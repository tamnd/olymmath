We call a positive integer $n$ whose all digits are distinct *bright*, if either $n$ is a one-digit number or there exists a divisor of $n$ which can be obtained by omitting one digit of $n$ and which is bright itself. Find the largest bright positive integer. (We assume that numbers do not start with zero.)

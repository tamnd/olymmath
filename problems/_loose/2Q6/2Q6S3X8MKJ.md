Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles that are externally tangent to each other at a point $T$. A common tangent to these two circles, not passing through $T$, touches $\Gamma_{1}$ at a point $P$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ at a point $Q$. Prove that the triangle $P T Q$ is a right triangle with the right angle at $T$.

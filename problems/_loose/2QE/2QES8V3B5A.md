Let $A B C D$ be a rectangular billiard table, equipped with elastic edges and holes at the vertices $A, B, C, D$ of the rectangle, with dimensions such that: $A B = C D = 200 \text{ cm}, B C = D A = 150 \text{ cm}$. Let $P$ be the point on side $C D$ such that $C P = 80 \text{ cm}, P D = 120 \text{ cm}$. A player rolls a ball from $A$ towards $P$. Prove that after six reflections off the sides of the rectangle, the ball falls into the hole at $C$.

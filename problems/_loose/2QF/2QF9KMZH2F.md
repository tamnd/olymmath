Let's prove that in any triangle with sides $a, b, c$ (where $c > b$),

$$
c^{2}-b^{2}=2 a \cdot M_{1} F_{1}
$$

where $M_{1}$ is the foot of the altitude from the side $a$, and $F_{1}$ is the midpoint of the side $a$.

In an isosceles $\triangle ABC$, $AD$ is the altitude on the base $BC$, and $BE$ is the altitude on $AC$. Also, $AD$ and $BE$ intersect at $H$. Meanwhile, $EF \perp BC$ intersects $BC$ at $F$, and $M$ is a point on the extension of $AD$ such that $DM = EF$. Also, $N$ is the midpoint of $AH$. Let $MN^2 = b$, $BN^2 = m$, and $BM^2 = n$. The relationship between $b$, $m$, and $n$ is
(A) $bm + n$.
(D) The size of $b$ and $m + n$ is uncertain.
(Anhui Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1996)

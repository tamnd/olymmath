Let four points $A, B, C, D$ be collinear in this order, the circles $\Gamma_{1}$, with diameter $[A C]$, and $\Gamma_{2}$ with diameter $[B D]$ intersect at $X$ and $Y$. Let $O \in [X Y]$. The lines $(O C)$ and $(O B)$ intersect $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ at $M$ and $N$ respectively.

Show that the lines $(A M)$, $(X Y)$, and $(N D)$ are concurrent.

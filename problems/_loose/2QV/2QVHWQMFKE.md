Prove that in the coordinate plane of $\triangle ABC$, there is a unique point $U$ with the property: there exist real numbers $\lambda, \mu, v, \kappa$, not all zero, such that for all points $P$ on this plane, $\lambda|PL|^2 + \mu|PM|^2 + v|PN|^2 - \kappa|PU|^2$ is a constant, where $L, M, N$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $BC, CA, AB$, respectively. Determine $U$.

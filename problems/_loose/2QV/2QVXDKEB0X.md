For a parallelogram, the following properties (1) and (2) are required:

(1) The perimeter of the parallelogram is $36 \mathrm{~cm}$.

(2) The bisector of the angle $\angle B A D$ intersects the extension of side $B C$ beyond $C$ at a point $E$, such that $\overline{C E}=3 \mathrm{~cm}$.

Prove that the side lengths $a=\overline{A B}, b=\overline{B C}$ of the parallelogram are uniquely determined by the requirements (1), (2)! Determine these side lengths!

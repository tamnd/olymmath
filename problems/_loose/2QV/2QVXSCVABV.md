In the plane, there are $n$ points ($n \geq 3$) such that no three points are collinear and the area of every triangle determined by any three of the given points is at most 1. Prove that there exists a triangle $ABC$ in the plane (the vertices do not have to be among the given points) with an area of at most 4 such that all the given points are covered by the triangle $ABC$.

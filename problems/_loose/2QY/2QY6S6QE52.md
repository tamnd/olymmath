Given three finite sets $A, B, C$ satisfying $A \cap B \cap C=\varnothing$.
(1) Prove: $|A \cup B \cup C| \geq \frac{1}{2}(|A|+|B|+|C|)$ (here, $|X|$ denotes the number of elements in the finite set $X$);
(2) Provide an example to show that the equality in (1) can hold.

Let $A, B, C, D$ be four arbitrary points in non-Euclidean Lobachevsky geometry, not lying on the same cycle; $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$ - cycles circumscribed around triangles $A B C, A B D, A C D, B C D$. Prove that:

a) if any two of the cycles $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ and $S_{4}$ intersect at a point $P$, different from the given four points, then all four cycles pass through this point;

b) the angle between any two of the cycles $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ and $S_{4}$ is equal to the angle between the other two cycles.

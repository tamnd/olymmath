Given triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ and points $E \in(A B), F \in(A C)$ such that $\frac{A E}{E B}=\frac{F C}{A F}=\frac{1}{4}$. If $\mathrm{M}$ is the midpoint of side $\mathrm{AB}$, $\mathrm{N}$ is the midpoint of side $\mathrm{AC}$, and $\mathrm{R}$ is the midpoint of segment $\mathrm{EF}$, show that points M, R, N are collinear.

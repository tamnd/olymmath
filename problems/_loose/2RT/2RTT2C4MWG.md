On a circular road, 25 posts are located at equal intervals, with a police officer standing at each post. The police officers are numbered in some order from 1 to 25. It is required that they move along the road so that there is again a police officer at each post, but in a clockwise direction, number 1 is followed by number 2, number 2 is followed by number 3, ..., and number 25 is followed by number 1. Prove that if the transition is organized so that the total distance traveled is minimal, then someone among the police officers will remain at their post.

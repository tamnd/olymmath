From a point $P$ outside the circle $\odot O$, two tangents $PE$ and $PF$ are drawn to the circle, with $E$ and $F$ being the points of tangency. An arbitrary secant line through point $P$ intersects $\odot O$ at points $A$ and $B$, and intersects $EF$ at point $C$. Prove:
$$
\frac{2}{PC}=\frac{1}{PA}+\frac{1}{PB} .
$$

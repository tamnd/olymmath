In the plane, the "distance" between two points is defined as follows:

If $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ are any two points with coordinates $\left(x_{1} ; y_{1}\right)$ and $\left(x_{2} ; y_{2}\right)$ respectively $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, y_{1}, y_{2}\right.$ being real numbers), then their "distance" is

$$
d\left(P_{1} ; P_{2}\right)=\max \left\{\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|,\left|y_{1}-y_{2}\right|\right\}
$$

Determine the set $M$ of all points in the plane that are equidistant from the points $A(0 ; 2)$ and $B(1 ; 4)$ with respect to the defined distance.

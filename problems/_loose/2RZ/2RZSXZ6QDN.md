Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{11} ; \alpha, \beta$ be positive numbers, and $\gamma=\alpha+\beta$. Prove:
$$
\frac{a_{1}^{\gamma} \gamma+a_{2} \gamma+\cdots+a_{n}^{\gamma}}{n} \geq \frac{a_{1}^{\alpha}+a_{2} a^{2}+\cdots+a_{n} a^{\alpha}}{n} \cdot \frac{a_{1}^{\beta}+a_{2}^{\beta}+\cdots+a_{n}^{\beta}}{n} .
$$

Let $p$ be a prime number, $f\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients, the degree of which in each variable is less than $p$. Prove that if for all integers $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ the congruence $f\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right) \equiv$ $\equiv 0(\bmod p)$ holds, then all coefficients of the polynomial $f\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ are divisible by $p$.

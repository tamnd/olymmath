Let's rotate all sides of triangle $A B C$ around the midpoints of the sides by the same angle $\alpha$ (and in the same direction); let $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ be the triangle formed by the rotated lines. Find the geometric locus of the points of intersection of the altitudes, angle bisectors, and medians of triangles $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, corresponding to different values of the angle $\alpha$. Prove that the centers of the circumcircles of all these triangles coincide.

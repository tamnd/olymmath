On side $A B$ of the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, points $K$ and $L$ are taken (point $K$ lies between $A$ and $L$), and on side $C D$, points $M$ and $N$ are taken (point $M$ lies between $C$ and $N$). It is known that $A K=K N=D N$ and $B L=B C=C M$. Prove that if $B C N K$ is a cyclic quadrilateral, then $A D M L$ is also cyclic.

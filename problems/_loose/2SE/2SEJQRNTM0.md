Positive numbers $a, b, c$ and $d$ are such that $a+b+c+d=1$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a^{3}}{(1+b)(1-c)}+\frac{b^{3}}{(1+c)(1-d)}+\frac{c^{3}}{(1+d)(1-a)}+\frac{d^{3}}{(1+a)(1-b)} \geqslant \frac{1}{15}
$$

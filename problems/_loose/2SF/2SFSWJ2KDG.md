Let positive real numbers $a_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ satisfy
$$
a_{1}+2 a_{2}+\cdots+n a_{n}=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
$$

Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{1+(n-1) a_{i}^{i}} \geqslant 1$.

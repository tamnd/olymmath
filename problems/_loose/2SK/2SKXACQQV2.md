Three grasshoppers sit in three vertices of a square. Every minute, one of them jumps over one of the remaining two and lands in the point symmetric to the one it jumped from with respect to the grasshopper it jumped over. Can at least one of them reach the fourth vertex of the square after a finite number of such jumps?

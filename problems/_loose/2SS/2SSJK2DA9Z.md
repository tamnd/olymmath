The country of Farr is located on 1000000000 islands. Some islands are connected by steamship routes every day. The steamship routes are arranged in such a way that from any island, one can reach any other island (possibly over several days). The spy and Major Pronin can make no more than one trip per day by steamship and have no other way to travel between islands. The spy does not travel by steamship on the 13th of each month, while Major Pronin is not superstitious and always knows where the spy is. Prove that Major Pronin can catch the spy (i.e., end up on the same island as the spy).

1.a) Show that for any natural number $n \geq 10$ the following inequality holds:

$$
9 \cdot n^{3}>5 \cdot(n+1) \cdot(n+2) \cdot(n+3)
$$

b) Show that:

$$
\frac{a^{3}}{b}+\frac{b^{3}}{c}+\frac{c^{3}}{a} \geq \frac{1}{3}(a+b+c)^{2}, \forall a, b, c \in(0 ; \infty)
$$

Giants and dwarfs. During a physical education class in a class where all students were of different heights, the teacher, having lined up the class in a rectangular formation, said: "Now we will see who among you is the tallest dwarf." He found the shortest in each row, and when these "dwarfs" stepped out of the formation and formed a front row, he chose the tallest among them: "Here is the tallest dwarf."

The boys returned to their old places, and then the teacher said: "Now I will show you the shortest giant." He pointed out the tallest in each column, and when the "giants" stepped forward, he found the shortest among them: "Here is the shortest giant."

Could it happen that the same boy could be the shortest "giant" and the tallest "dwarf"? Are there such classes where the shortest "giant" is shorter than the tallest "dwarf"? And how would things be if the teacher, when identifying "giants," looked for them in the rows instead of the columns, i.e., exactly the same way he looked for "dwarfs"?

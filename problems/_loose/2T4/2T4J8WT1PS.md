In triangle $ABC$, let $N$ and $M$ be the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Point $P$ is chosen on the arc $BC$ not containing $A$ of the circumcircle of $ABC$ such that $BNMP$ is cyclic. Given $BC=28$, $AC=30$ and $AB = 26$, the value of $AP$ may be expressed as $m/\sqrt{n}$ for positive integers $m$ and $n$, where $n$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Compute $m+n$.

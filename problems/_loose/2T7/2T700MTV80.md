For a positive number $x$, let $f(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=1}^n \left|\cos \left(\frac{2k+1}{2n}x\right)-\cos \left(\frac{2k-1}{2n}x\right)\right|$

Evaluate

$$\lim_{x\rightarrow\infty}\frac{f(x)}{x}$$

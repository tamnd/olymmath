Let n > 1 be an integer and $a_1, a_2, . . . , a_n$ be positive reals with sum 1.
a) Show that there exists a constant c ≥ 1/2 so that $\sum \frac{a_k}{1+(a_0+a_1+...+a_{k-1})^2}\geq c$ ,
where $a_0 = 0$ .
b) Show that 'the best' value of c is at least $\frac{\pi}{4}$ .

In triangle $ABC$, the projection of vertex $C$ onto side $AB$ is $C_{1}$. We know that the area of the segment of the circle with radius $CC_{1}=m_{c}$, centered at $C$, that lies inside the triangle is half the area of the triangle. Determine the angles of the triangle to the nearest minute, given that in the triangle: I. there is a right angle, II. two angles are equal. What is the length of the area-bisecting arc? Is the area-bisecting line shorter if, for this purpose, a line segment parallel to $AB$ is taken instead of the arc? $(\pi \approx 3.1416)$.

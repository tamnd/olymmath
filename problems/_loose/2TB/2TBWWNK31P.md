In a plane $\epsilon$, let two distinct points $P$ and $Q$ and a line $g$ passing through $Q$ be given arbitrarily.

a) Prove that the reflection point $P^{\prime}$ of $P$ with respect to $g$ always lies on the circle around $Q$ with radius $\overline{P Q}$!

b) Prove that for every point $P^{\prime}$ on the circle around $Q$ with radius $\overline{P Q}$, there is a line $g$ passing through $Q$ such that $P^{\prime}$ is the reflection point of $P$ with respect to $g$!

c) Prove: If $P^{*}$ is a point that does not lie on the circle around $Q$ with radius $\overline{P Q}$, then there is no line passing through $Q$ such that $P^{*}$ would be the reflection point of $P$!

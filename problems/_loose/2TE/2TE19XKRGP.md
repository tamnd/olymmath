Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, which is not a kite (a kite is a quadrilateral symmetric with respect to one of its diagonals). It is known that the bisectors of angles $A$ and $C$ intersect at a point on diagonal $B D$. Prove that the bisectors of angles $B$ and $D$ intersect on diagonal $A C$.

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers such that $a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots a_{n}=\frac{n \cdot(n+1) \cdot(2 n+1)}{3}, n \in N^{*}$. Show that $\sqrt{a_{1}+2}+\sqrt{a_{2}+6}+\ldots \sqrt{a_{n}+n \cdot(n+1)}<n \cdot(n+1)$.

Prof. Camelia Magdaş, Andrei Mureşanu Dej National College

Define the function $ f(x)$ as : $ f(x)=\left\{
\begin{array}{ll}
\frac{\ln (1+x)}{1+x}\ (x\geq 0) & \\
\frac{\ln (1-x)}{1-x}\ (-1\leq x<0) & \end{array}
\right.$

1. Examine the variation of $ f(x)$ and find the maximum and minimum value of $ f(x)$.

2. Find the value of $ a$ for which $ \int_{-1}^{e-1} |f(x)-a|\ dx$ is minimized for $ 0\leq a\leq \frac{1}{e}$.

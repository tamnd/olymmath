Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ be the vertices of a regular $n$-sided polygon inscribed in a circle with center at point $O$ and radius 1, and let point $M$ be a point on the ray $O A_{1}$ and outside the circle. Prove that: $\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\left|M A_{k}\right|} \geqslant \frac{n}{|O M|}$.

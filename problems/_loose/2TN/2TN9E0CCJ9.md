Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}\right)$ be a positive geometric sequence, with the first term no greater than the common ratio. Prove:
$$
\sqrt{a_{2}} \sqrt[3]{a_{3}} \cdots \sqrt[n+1]{a_{n+1}} \geqslant\left(\sqrt{\sqrt{a_{2}} \sqrt[n+1]{a_{n+1}}}\right)^{n} .
$$

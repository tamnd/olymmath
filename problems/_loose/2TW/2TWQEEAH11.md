Let the points of the lines $a$ and $b$ (lying in a plane) be $A, A_{1}, A_{2}$ and $B, B_{1}, B_{2}$, respectively, such that the paths $A B_{1} A_{1} B=U_{1}$ and $B A_{2} B_{2} A=U_{2}$, consisting of line segments from $A$ to $B$ and back, have their first and third segments parallel, i.e., $A B_{1} \| B A_{2}$ and $A_{1} B \| B_{2} A$. Prove that the second segments of these paths are also parallel, i.e., $B_{1} A_{1} \| A_{2} B_{2}$.

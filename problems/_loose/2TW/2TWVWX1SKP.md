Given a sequence ..., $a_{- \text{p }}, \ldots, a_{-1}, a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{\text{p }}, \ldots$ infinite in both directions, and each of its terms is equal to $\frac{1}{4}$ of the sum of its two neighbors. Prove that if any two of its terms are equal, then there are an infinite number of pairs of equal terms in it. (Note: the two terms known to be equal do not necessarily have to be adjacent).

Let $a$ be a positive integer. The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{1}=a, a_{n+1}=a_{n}^{2}+20, n=1,2, \cdots$.
(1) Prove: There exists a positive integer $a$ that is not a perfect cube, such that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ contains a term that is a perfect cube;
(2) Prove: The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ contains at most one term that is a perfect cube.

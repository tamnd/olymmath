Two sequences of positive reals, $ \left(x_n\right)$ and $ \left(y_n\right)$ , satisfy the relations $ x_{n + 2} = x_n + x_{n + 1}^2$ and $ y_{n + 2} = y_n^2 + y_{n + 1}$ for all natural numbers $ n$ . Prove that, if the numbers $ x_1$ , $ x_2$ , $ y_1$ , $ y_2$ are all greater than $ 1$ , then there exists a natural number $ k$ such that $ x_k > y_k$ .

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, satisfying $AC < BC$. A circle passing through $A, B$ intersects the line segments $AC, BC$ at another point $A_1, B_1$, respectively. The circle $\odot A_1 B_1 C$ intersects $\odot ABC$ at points $C, P$. The line segments $A_1 B, A B_1$ intersect at point $S$. Let $Q, R$ be the reflections of $S$ over $CA, CB$, respectively. Prove that $P, Q, R, C$ are concyclic.

$13.8 R$ is a rectangle, which is the union of several rectangles $\left\{R_{i} \mid 1 \leqslant i \leqslant n\right\}$, satisfying:
(1) The sides of $R_{i}$ are parallel to the sides of $R$;
(2) $R_{i}$ do not overlap;
(3) Each $R_{i}$ has at least one side of integer length.
Prove that $R$ has at least one side of integer length.
(30th International Mathematical Olympiad Preliminary Question, 1989)

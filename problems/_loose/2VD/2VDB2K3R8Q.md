Prove that if $\sqrt{5}$ is extracted in $p$-arithmetic, then in the Fibonacci sequence of ordinary integers

$$
0,1,1,2,3,5,8, \ldots
$$

the term with the number $p^{k-1}(p-1)$ (i.e., $a_{p^{k-1}(p-1)}$) is divisible by $p^{k}$.

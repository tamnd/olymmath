Consider the set $M$ of all sequences of integers $s=(s_1,\ldots,s_k)$ such that $0\leqslant s_i\leqslant n, i=1,2,\ldots,k$ and let $M(s)=\max\{s_1,\ldots,s_k\}$ . Show that
$$\sum_{s\in A} M(s)=(n+1)^{k+1}-(1^k+2^k+\ldots +(n+1)^k).$$

*Ioan Tomescu*

Given that $O, A, B, C$ are four non-collinear points, if there exists a set of real numbers $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \lambda_{3}$, such that $\lambda_{1} \overrightarrow{O A}+\lambda_{2} \overrightarrow{O B}+$ $\lambda_{3} \overrightarrow{O C}=0$, then among the three angles $\angle A O B, \angle B O C, \angle O O A$, at least one is obtuse.

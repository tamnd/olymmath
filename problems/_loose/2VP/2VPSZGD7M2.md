Consider the circular segment $A M B$, in which we inscribe triangles; the side $A B$ is fixed, the other two sides are variable and their intersection point moves along the arc.

Determine:

1) The geometric locus of the intersection point of the angle bisectors.
2) The geometric locus of the intersection point of the midlines.
3) The geometric locus of the intersection point of the altitudes.

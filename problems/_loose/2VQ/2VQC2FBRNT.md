a) Prove that any number that is not a power of two can be represented as the sum of at least two consecutive positive integers, while such a representation is impossible for powers of two.

b) Prove that any composite odd number can be represented as the sum of at least two consecutive odd numbers, while no prime number can be represented in such a form. Which even numbers can be represented as the sum of several consecutive odd numbers?

c) Prove that each power of a positive integer $ n $ (of course, with an exponent greater than 1) can be represented as the sum of $ n $ consecutive odd numbers.

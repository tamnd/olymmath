(2003 National High School Competition Problem) Let $A, B, C$ be the three non-collinear points corresponding to the complex numbers $z_{0}=a \mathrm{i}, z_{1}=\frac{1}{2}+b \mathrm{i}, z_{2}=1+c \mathrm{i}$ respectively $\left(a, b, c\right.$ are all real numbers). Prove: The curve $z=z_{0} \cos ^{4} t+2 z_{1} \cos ^{2} t \cdot \sin ^{2} t+z_{2} \sin ^{4} t(t \in \mathbf{R})$ intersects the midline of $\triangle A B C$ parallel to $A C$ at only one point, and find this point.

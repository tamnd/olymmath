The images of points $A$ and $C$ under reflection across the line $BD$ are points $A$ and $C$, respectively, and the images of points $B$ and $D$ under reflection across the line $AC$ are points $B$ and $D$, respectively. Prove that points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ lie on a circle.

Consider the polynomial $P(x) = x^2 - 1$. There is a real number $a$ that satisfies
$$P(P(P(a))) = 2024.$$
Write $a^2$ as $m + \sqrt{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are integers and $n$ is square-free. The value of $m+n$ is $\underline{\underline{6}} \underline{\underline{7}}$.

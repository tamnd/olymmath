Consider the following four statements:

Manuela has a dog and a cat.

Manuela has neither a dog nor a cat.

If Manuela has a dog, then she also has a cat.

Manuela does not have a dog, but she has a cat.

How many of them, at most, can be false simultaneously?
(A) 0
(B) 1
(C) 2
(D) 3
(E) 4.

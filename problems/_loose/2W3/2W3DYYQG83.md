A $4 \times 4$ chessboard has one square black, and the rest are white. We want to achieve that all squares are white. When is this possible, if in one step we change the color of any square to its opposite, but only in such a way that at the same time we also change the color of all squares intersected by any axis of symmetry of the board to their opposite colors.

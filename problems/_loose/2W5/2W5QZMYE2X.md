In the plane, there are $n(\geqslant 2)$ points $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n}$. Suppose there are $k$ different values among $P_{i} P_{j}(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)$. Prove: $k \geqslant \sqrt{n-\frac{3}{4}}-\frac{1}{2}$.

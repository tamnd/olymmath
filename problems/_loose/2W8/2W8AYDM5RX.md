Let
$$
\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{1+\ddots \frac{1}{1}}}}}=\frac{m}{n}
$$

where the left side of the equation has 1990 fraction bars, and the right side $\frac{m}{n}$ is a reduced fraction. Prove that $\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{m}{n}\right)^{2}=\frac{5}{4}-\frac{1}{n^{2}}$.

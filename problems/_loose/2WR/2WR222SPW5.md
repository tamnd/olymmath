This method allows solving any equation of the 4th degree by reducing it to solving an auxiliary cubic equation and two quadratic equations.

a) Prove that any 4th-degree equation can be reduced to the form $x^{4}=A x^{2}+B x+C$.

b) Introduce a real parameter $\alpha$ and rewrite equation (*) as $x^{4}+2 \alpha x^{2}+\alpha^{2}=(A+2 \alpha) x^{2}+B x+(C+$ $\left.\alpha^{2}\right) . (* *)$

Prove that for some $\alpha>-A / 2$, the right-hand side of equation (**) becomes a perfect square.

c) Using equation ( $* *$ ), describe the method for finding the roots of the equation.

A user has three computer floppy disks made by companies K, L, and M, one disk from each of these companies, and the company stamps on the disks are missing. Two of the three disks turned out to be defective. What is the probability that the defective disks are from companies L and M, if the defect rate in the products of company K is 10%, and for companies L and M - 20% and 15%, respectively?

Chords $X_1X_2$ and $Y_1Y_2$ pass through $L$ such that points $X_1,Y_1$ and $A$ lie in the same half-plane with respect to $BC$ . Lines $X_1Y_2$ and $Y_1X_2$ intersect side $BC$ in points $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ respectively.
Prove that $\angle BAZ_1=\angle CAZ_2$ .

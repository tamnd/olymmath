Let $g$ be an even positive integer and $f(n) = g^n + 1$ , $(n \in N^* )$ .
Prove that for every positive integer $n$ we have:
a) $f(n)$ divides each of the numbers $f(3n), f(5n), f(7n)$ b) $f(n)$ is relative prime to each of the numbers $f(2n), f(4n),f(6n),...$

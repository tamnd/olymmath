From a point chosen arbitrarily on one side of an equilateral triangle, we drop perpendiculars to the other two side lines. We intersect the third side line with a line passing through the foot of the perpendicular on the second side line and parallel to the first side. Show that the distance between the foot of the perpendicular on the third side line and the intersection point is equal to the distance from the starting point to the midpoint of the first side.

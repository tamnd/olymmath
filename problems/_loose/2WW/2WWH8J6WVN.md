Let $P_{1} P_{2} P_{3}$ be a given arbitrary triangle; its area be $F$, and the incircle have the center $M$ and the radius $r$.

a) Prove that a pyramid $P_{1} P_{2} P_{3} S$ has the smallest possible surface area among all pyramids $P_{1} P_{2} P_{3} S$ with this base $P_{1} P_{2} P_{3}$ and a given volume $V$ if and only if the perpendicular from $S$ to the plane through $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ has its foot at $M$.

b) Prove that this smallest possible surface area is

$$
F+\sqrt{F^{2}+\left(\frac{3 V}{r}\right)^{2}}
$$

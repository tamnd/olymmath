On the line $A B$, points $P$ and $P_{1}$ are taken, and on the line $A C$, points $Q$ and $Q_{1}$ are taken. The line connecting point $A$ with the intersection point of lines $P Q$ and $P_{1} Q_{1}$ intersects line $B C$ at point $D$. Prove that

$$
\frac{\overline{B D}}{\overline{C D}}=\frac{(\overline{B P} / \overline{P A})-\left(\overline{B P_{1}} / \overline{P_{1} A}\right)}{(\overline{C Q} / \overline{Q A})-\left(\overline{C Q_{1}} / \overline{Q_{1} A}\right)}
$$

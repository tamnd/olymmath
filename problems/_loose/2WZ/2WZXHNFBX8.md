Construct a circle with center $S$ and radius $3 \mathrm{~cm}$. Construct two mutually perpendicular diameters $A C$ and $B D$ of this circle. Construct isosceles triangles $A B K, B C L, C D M$, $D A N$ such that:

- the base of each triangle is a side of the quadrilateral $A B C D$,
- the base of each triangle is equal to the height to this base,
- no triangle overlaps the quadrilateral $A B C D$.

From the given data, calculate the area of the polygon $A K B L C M D N$. (M. Krejčová)

9,10 Authors: Blinkov A.d:, Zaslavsky A.A., Antropov A.V In a football tournament, $2 n$ teams participate ( $n>1$ ). In each round, the teams are divided into $n$ pairs, and the teams in each pair play against each other. They conducted $2 n-1$ rounds, after which each team played against each other exactly once. For a win, 3 points were awarded, for a draw - 1, for a loss - 0 points. It turned out that for each team, the ratio of the points scored to the number of games played after the last round did not change. Prove that all teams played all their matches to a draw.

Let $e$ and $f$ be two skew lines, and let $E$ and $F$ be points on $e$ and $f$ respectively, such that the segment $EF$ is perpendicular to both lines. Measure a distance $x$ from $E$ on $e$ and a distance $y$ from $F$ on $f$ such that $x \cdot y = k^2$, where $k$ is a given length.

a) Prove that the volume of the tetrahedron determined by the four endpoints of segments $x$ and $y$ is constant.
b) For which values of $x$ and $y$ will the diameter of the sphere circumscribed around the tetrahedron be the smallest?

On an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, 8 white and 8 black tokens are located on the first and eighth rows, respectively. The white player starts the game. The players alternately move one token one or more squares along its column in any direction, but no further than to the edge of the board or to an opponent's token. The player who cannot make a move loses the game. Prove that the black player has a winning strategy.

Each cell in an infinite grid is colored gray, pink, or green. Call a sub-rectangle of grid cells *indecisive* if, within the rectangle, no color appears more frequently than the other two colors. Could there exist an integer $n$ and a coloring of the grid so that every subrectangle with both side lengths at least $n$ is indecisive?

*Benny Wang*

Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Four circles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and $\delta$ touch the circumcircle of quadrilateral $A B C D$ at points $A, B, C$, and $D$, respectively. Denote by $t_{\alpha \beta}$ the segment of the tangent to circles $\alpha$ and $\beta$, where $t_{\alpha \beta}$ is the segment of the common external tangent if $\alpha$ and $\beta$ touch the given circle in the same way (internally or externally), and the segment of the common internal tangent if $\alpha$ and $\beta$ touch the given circle in different ways (similarly, the quantities $t_{\beta \gamma}, t_{\alpha \delta}$, etc., are defined). Prove that

$$
t_{\alpha \beta} t_{\gamma \delta}+t_{\beta \gamma} t_{\delta \alpha}=t_{\alpha \gamma} t_{\beta \delta}
$$

(generalized Ptolemy's theorem).

Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of positive integers such that $a_{n}<a_{n+1}$ for all $n \geqslant 1$. Suppose that for all quadruples of indices $(i, j, k, l)$ such that $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant k<l$ and $i+l=j+k$, the inequality $a_{i}+a_{l}>a_{j}+a_{k}$ is satisfied. Determine the least possible value of $a_{2008}$.

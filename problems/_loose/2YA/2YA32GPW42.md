We spread a deck of cards on the table and shuffle them according to a certain rule. Then we shuffle again by applying the same rule (i.e., if at the first shuffling the card in position $i$ moved to position $j$, then at the next shuffling the card in position $i$ will again move to position $j$). Show that if we shuffle by applying the same rule a certain number of times, at a certain number of steps, we obtain the initial order.

Consider the cube $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$. Point $K$ is an interior point of the edge $B B_{1}$ such that $B K / K B_{1}=m$. A plane $\alpha$ is drawn through points $K$ and $C_{1}$, parallel to the line $B D_{1}$.

a) Let $P$ be the point of intersection of the plane $\alpha$ with the line $A_{1} B_{1}$. Find the value of the ratio $A_{1} P / P B_{1}$.

b) The plane $\alpha$ divides the cube into 2 parts. Find the ratio of the volumes of these parts.

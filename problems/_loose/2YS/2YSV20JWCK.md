Given the side length $a$ of an equilateral triangle $A B C$. On this triangle as the base, a pyramid $A B C D$ with the following properties (1), (2) is to be constructed:

(1) The edges $A D, B D$ and $C D$ have equal length $s$.

(2) The height $h$ of the pyramid is the arithmetic mean of $a$ and $s$.

Investigate whether such a pyramid $A B C D$ exists and whether $h$ and $s$ are uniquely determined by $a$. If this is the case, determine $h$ and $s$.

A motorbike and a bicycle travel a large square track, starting at the same time from one of the vertices and both proceeding clockwise. The side of the track measures $90 \mathrm{~km}$. The motorbike travels at a constant speed of $65 \mathrm{~km}$ per hour, the bicycle at $30 \mathrm{~km}$ per hour. After how many hours will the two meet again at one of the four vertices of the track?
(A) 7
(B) $72 / 7$
(C) $30 / 7$
(D) 72
(E) it will never happen again

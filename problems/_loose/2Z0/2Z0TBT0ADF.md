For a sequence of positive terms $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with the sum of the first $n$ terms denoted as $S_{n}$, it satisfies $a_{n}^{2}+2 a_{n}=4 S_{n}+3\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$.
(1) Find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Prove:
$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{1}}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{2}}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{3}}\right) \cdots\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{n-1}}\right)>\frac{\sqrt{a_{n}}}{2} .
$$

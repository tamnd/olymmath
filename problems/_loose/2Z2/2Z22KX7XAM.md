Let $k$ be a given positive integer, and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}$ be pairwise coprime positive integers. Prove: there must exist $k$ consecutive integers such that the $j$-th number is divisible by $a_{j}$ $(1 \leqslant j \leqslant k)$.

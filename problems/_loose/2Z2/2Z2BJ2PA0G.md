Fill an empty live water truck with water, using three water pipes $A$, $B$, and $C$. If pipes $A$ and $C$ are used simultaneously, the truck is full when pipe $A$ has injected 4 tons of water; if pipes $B$ and $C$ are used simultaneously, the truck is full when pipe $B$ has injected 6 tons of water. It is known that the water injection rate of pipe $B$ is twice that of pipe $A$ per minute. How much water can the truck hold at most?

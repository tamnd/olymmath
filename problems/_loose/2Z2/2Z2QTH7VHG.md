It is well known that there is at least one point on the Earth's surface (namely the South Pole) with the following property:

If you go a distance $a$ north from it, then the same distance $a$ east (or west), and finally the distance $a$ south, you return to the starting point.

It is to be investigated which other points on the Earth's surface have the same property (here $a=0.5 \pi R$ should apply; $R$ is the radius of the Earth, assumed to be a sphere).

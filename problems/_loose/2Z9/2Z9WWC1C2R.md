Given a square $N$ and a point $P$ in its plane, which does not lie on any side line of $N$. Consider the distances from $P$ to the vertices of $N$ and show that a convex quadrilateral can always be constructed from these four segments (in the order of the vertices of $N$) in which the diagonals are perpendicular to each other.

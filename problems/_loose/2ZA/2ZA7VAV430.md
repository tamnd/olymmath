In an $n$-sided pyramid that can inscribe a sphere, each of the lateral edges of the pyramid is rotated around the corresponding side of the base until it coincides with the plane of the base, such that the image of the lateral edge has common interior points with the base. In this way, we obtain $n$ images of the vertex of the pyramid. Prove that these $n$ images lie on a single circle.

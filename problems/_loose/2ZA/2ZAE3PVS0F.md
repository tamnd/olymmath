Let $k$ be a positive integer. Prove that there exists a positive integer $\ell$ with the following property: if $m$ and $n$ are positive integers relatively prime to $\ell$ such that $m^n \equiv n^m \pmod{\ell}$, then $m \equiv n \pmod{k}$.

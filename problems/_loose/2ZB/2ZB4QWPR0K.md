Consider the set of matrices $M=\left\{\left.\left(\begin{array}{cc}2-x & x-1 \\ 2-2 x & 2 x-1\end{array}\right) \right\rvert x \in \mathbb{R}^{*}\right\}$.

a) Show that if $A, B \in M$, then $A^{2} \cdot B^{2} \in M$;

b) Does there exist a matrix $A \in M$ such that $A^{2015}=-I_{2}$? Justify your answer.

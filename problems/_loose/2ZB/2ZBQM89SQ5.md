a) Find three distinct integers $a, b$ and $c$ in the interval $(25,36)$ such that $a^2 +b^2$ is divisible by $c$ .

b) Are there always three distinct integers $a$ , $b$ and $c$ in the interval $(n^2, (n + 1)^2)$ , where $n$ is a natural number greater than $1$ , such that $a^2 +b^2$ is divisible by $c$ ?

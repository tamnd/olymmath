The 80th question: Given a prime number $\mathrm{p}$, let $A=\left(a_{i j}\right)$ be a $p \times p$ matrix, satisfying
$$
\left\{\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{ij}} \mid 1 \leq \mathrm{i}, \mathrm{j} \leq \mathrm{p}\right\}=\left\{1,2, \ldots, \mathrm{p}^{2}\right\}
$$

Permitted operations on a matrix include: selecting a row or a column, and simultaneously adding 1 or subtracting 1 from each number in that row or column. If it is possible to transform all elements of $\mathrm{A}$ to 0 through a finite number of the above operations, then $\mathrm{A}$ is called a "good matrix". Determine the number of good matrices $\mathrm{A}$.

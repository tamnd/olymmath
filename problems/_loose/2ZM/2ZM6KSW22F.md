Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with its circumcircle $\odot O$, let $P$ be any point on $\odot O$ different from $A, B, C$ and their antipodal points. Denote the circumcenters of $\triangle AOP, \triangle BOP, \triangle COP$ as $O_A, O_B, O_C$ respectively. Let the lines through $O_A, O_B, O_C$ perpendicular to $BC, CA, AB$ be $l_A, l_B, l_C$ respectively. Prove that the circumcircle of the triangle formed by the intersections of line $OP$ with $l_A, l_B, l_C$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$.

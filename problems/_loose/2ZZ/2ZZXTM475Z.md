Given a regular triangular pyramid $S A B C$, the edge of the base of which is equal to 1. Medians of the lateral faces are drawn from vertices $A$ and $B$ of the base $A B C$, which do not have common points. It is known that the edges of a certain cube lie on the lines containing these medians. Find the length of the lateral edge of the pyramid.

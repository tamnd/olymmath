Find all solutions to the equation $4[x]=25\{x\}-4.5$. Here $[x]$ denotes the integer part of the number $x$, that is, the greatest integer not exceeding the number $x$, and $\{x\}$ is the fractional part of the number $x$, which by definition is equal to $x-[x]$.

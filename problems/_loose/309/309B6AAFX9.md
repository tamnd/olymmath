Given a positive integer $m(m \geqslant 3)$, let the positive term arithmetic sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and the positive term geometric sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfy: the first term of $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ equals the common ratio of $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$, the first term of $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ equals the common difference of $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, and $a_{m}=b_{m}$. Find the minimum value of $a_{m}$, and determine the ratio of $a_{1}$ to $b_{1}$ when $a_{m}$ takes the minimum value.

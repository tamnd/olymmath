Solve the equation in the set of real numbers

$$
x\lfloor x\rfloor-5 x+7=0 \text {, }
$$

where $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the floor function of $x$, i.e., the greatest integer $k$ such that $k \leqq x$. (For example, $\lfloor\sqrt{2}\rfloor=1 \text{and} \lfloor-3.1\rfloor=-4$).

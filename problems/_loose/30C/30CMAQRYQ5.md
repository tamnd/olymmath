On each of 2013 cards, a number is written, and all these 2013 numbers are distinct. The cards are face down. In one move, it is allowed to point to ten cards, and in response, one of the numbers written on them will be reported (it is unknown which one). For what largest $t$ can it be guaranteed to find $t$ cards, for which it is known what number is written on each of them?

(I. Bogdanov)

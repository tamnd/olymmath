In quadrilateral $ABCD$ , $AC = BD$ and $\measuredangle B = 60^\circ$ . Denote by $M$ and $N$ the midpoints of $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{CD}$ , respectively. If $MN = 12$ and the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$ is 420, then compute $AC$ .

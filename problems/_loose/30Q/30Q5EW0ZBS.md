A square is partitioned into $n>1$ rectangles with sides parallel to those of the square. It is assumed that any line parallel to the sides of the square, which cuts through the interior of the square, also cuts through the interior of at least one of the rectangles in the partition. Show that there is a rectangle that does not touch the sides of the square.

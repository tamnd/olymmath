Let $P,Q$ be the polynomials:
$$x^3-4x^2+39x-46, x^3+3x^2+4x-3,$$
respectively.
1. Prove that each of $P, Q$ has an unique real root. Let them be $\alpha,\beta$, respectively.
2. Prove that $\{ \alpha\}>\{ \beta\} ^2$, where $\{ x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the fractional part of $x$.

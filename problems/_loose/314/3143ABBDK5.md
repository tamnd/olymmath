Let $a_0, a_1, \ldots , a_n$ and $b_0, b_1, \ldots , b_n$ be sequences of real numbers such that $a_0 = b_0 \geqslant 0$ , $a_n = b_n > 0$ and $$a_i=\sqrt{\frac{a_{i+1}+a_{i-1}}{2}}, b_i=\sqrt{\frac{b_{i+1}+b_{i-1}}{2}},$$for all $i=1,\ldots,n-1$ . Prove that $a_1 = b_1$ .

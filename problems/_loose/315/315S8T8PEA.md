Each rational number is painted either white or red. Call such a coloring of the rationals *sanferminera* if for any distinct rationals numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfying one of the following three conditions:

- $xy=1$ ,

- $x+y=0$ ,

- $x+y=1$ ,
we have $x$ and $y$ painted different colors. How many sanferminera colorings are there?

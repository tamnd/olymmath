On the heights (but not on their extensions) of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, points $A 1, B 1, C 1$ are taken, distinct from the orthocenter $H$, such that the sum of the areas of triangles $A B C 1, B C A 1, C A B 1$ is equal to the area of triangle $A B C$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $A 1 B 1 C 1$ passes through the point $H$.

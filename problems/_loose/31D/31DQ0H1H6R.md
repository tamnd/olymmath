Determine whether there exist bodies for which the following applies!

| | Edge length or Diameter in $\mathrm{cm}$ | Surface area in $\mathrm{cm}^{2}$ | Volume in $\mathrm{cm}^{3}$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Cube | $a$ | $b$ | $b$ |
| Sphere | $c$ | $d$ | $d$ |
| Regular Tetrahedron | $e$ | $f$ | $f$ |

Determine all real numbers $a, c, e$ that satisfy these conditions! Here, the same letters denote the same real numbers, while different letters do not necessarily denote different numbers.

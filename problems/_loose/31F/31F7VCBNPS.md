Let $a>b>c>d>0$, and $X=\sqrt{a b}+\sqrt{c d}, Y=\sqrt{a c}+$ $\sqrt{b d}, Z=\sqrt{a d}+\sqrt{b c}$. Then the size relationship of $X, Y, Z$ is
(A) $X<Z<Y$.
(B) $Y<Z<X$.
(C) $X<Y<Z$.
(D) $Z<Y<X$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

Twelve friends have decided to go out for dinner together for $n$ consecutive evenings. In each of those $n$ outings, they arrange themselves so that they sit at $m$ tables, with the same number of friends at each table. Is there such a seating arrangement that each of them has the opportunity to sit at the same table with each of the 11 remaining friends at least once, if:
a) $n=5$ and $m=4$?
b) $n=4$ and $m=3$?
c) $n=3$ and $m=2$?

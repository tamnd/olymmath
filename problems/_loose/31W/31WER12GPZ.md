In the plane, there are two intersecting lines, $a$ and $b$, and a point, $P$. The angle bisector of the two lines is denoted by $f$. ($P$ is not on $a$, $b$, or the angle bisectors of the lines.) The line through $P$ perpendicular to $t$ intersects $a$ at point $A$. Construct a line perpendicular to $a$ at $A$, and let this line intersect $f$ at point $F$. Finally, construct a perpendicular to $FP$ at $P$. - Prove that $P$ bisects the segment of the line perpendicular to $FP$ that lies between $a$ and $b$.

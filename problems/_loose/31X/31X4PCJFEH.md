Let the functions $\alpha(x)$ and $\beta(x)$ satisfy the following conditions:

1) $\alpha(x)$ and $\beta(x)$ are $n$-times differentiable;

2) $2 \alpha^{(m)}\left(x_{0}\right)=\beta^{(m)}\left(x_{0}\right)$, where $m=0,1, \ldots, n-1$;
3) $\alpha^{(n)}(x)>\beta^{(n)}(x)$ for $x>x_{0}$.

Then the inequality $\alpha(x)>\beta(x)$ holds for $x>x_{0}$.

$X$ and $Y$ are two convex polygons, with polygon $X$ contained inside $Y$. Let $S(X)$ and $S(Y)$ be the areas of these polygons, and $P(X)$ and $P(Y)$ be their perimeters. Prove that $\frac{S(X)}{P(X)} < 2 \cdot \frac{S(Y)}{P(Y)}$.

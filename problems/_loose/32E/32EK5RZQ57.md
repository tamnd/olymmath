Given a semicircle above diameter $A B$ and points $C$ and $D$ such that:

a) point $C$ lies on the arc $A D$,

b) $\measuredangle C S D$ is a right angle, where $S$ is the midpoint of segment $A B$.

Let $E$ be the intersection of lines $A C$ and $B D$, and $F$ be the intersection of lines $A D$ and $B C$. Prove that the vector $\overrightarrow{E F}$ does not depend on the choice of points $C$ and $D$.

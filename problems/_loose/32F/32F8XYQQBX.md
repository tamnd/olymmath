Initially, the numbers $1-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2}$, and $1+\sqrt{2}$ were written on the board. Every minute, all three numbers $x, y$, and $z$ on the board are erased, and instead, the numbers $x^{2}+x y+y^{2}$, $y^{2}+y z+z^{2}$, and $z^{2}+x z+x^{2}$ are written on the board. Can all three numbers on the board be rational at some point?

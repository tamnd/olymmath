Given five functions:
(1) $f(x)=0, x \in R$;
(2) $f(x)=x^{2},-3<x \leqslant 3$;
(3) $f(x)=\log _{2}|x|, x \neq 0, x \in R$;
(4) $f(x)=(1+x)^{2 n}-(1-x)^{2 n}, x \in R, n \in N$;
(5) $f(x)=\sin (\sin x), x \in R$.

Among them, the number of odd functions is
(A) 1.
(B) 2.
(C) 3.
(D) 4.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

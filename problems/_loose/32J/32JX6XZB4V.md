(Wilson's theorem) Let $p$ be a prime.

1. Show that in $\mathbb{Z} / p \mathbb{Z}$ all non-zero elements have an inverse, i.e., for any integer $k$ not divisible by $p$, there exists an integer $l$ such that

$$
k \cdot l \equiv 1[p]
$$

2. Show that

$$
n \text { prime } \Leftrightarrow(n-1)!\equiv-1[n]
$$

Let $x, y, z$ be non-negative real numbers, prove:
$$
\left(\frac{x y+y z+z x}{3}\right)^{3} \leqslant\left(x^{2}-x y+y^{2}\right)\left(y^{2}-y z+z^{2}\right)\left(z^{2}-z x+x^{2}\right) \leqslant\left(\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}}{2}\right)^{3} .
$$

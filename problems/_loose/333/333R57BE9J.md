Given a square $A B C D$ with side $a$ and center at point $O$. Through the opposite vertices $A$ and $C$ of the square, rays $A X$ and $C Y$ are drawn perpendicular to the plane of the square and on the same side of that plane. On $A X$, a point $M$ is taken such that $\overline{O M}=a$, and on $C Y$, a point $N$ is taken such that $\overline{M N}=2 a$. Prove that $M N$ is perpendicular to the plane $D M B$.

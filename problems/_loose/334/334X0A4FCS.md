A sheet of paper is folded. Through two points, A and B, on the fold, two straight cuts $\mathrm{AC}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$ are made with scissors. The piece of paper with the triangular contour $\mathrm{ABC}$ is removed. Unfolding the sheet of paper reveals a free space, delimited by a polygon. Determine conditions on the triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ such that the polygon is:
a. a triangle
b. a rhombus
c. a square
d. a rectangle that is not a square
e. a trapezoid

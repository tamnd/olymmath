In a scalene triangle $ABC$ inscribed in a circle with center $O$ and circumscribed around a circle with center $I$, the point $B'$, symmetric to point $B$ with respect to the line $OI$, lies inside the angle $ABI$. Prove that the tangents to the circle circumscribed around triangle $B B' I$, drawn at points $B'$ and $I$, intersect on the line $AC$.

(A. Kuznetsov)

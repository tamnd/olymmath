Let's consider a convex polyhedron where the number of vertices is not less than the number of faces. Cut off the vertices of the polyhedron with a plane in such a way that among the newly created faces, no two have any vertex in common. Prove that a sphere cannot be inscribed in the remaining polyhedron.

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$ and let $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ be the feet of the altitudes dropped from $A, B, C$ respectively. Let $\frac{\overline{A H}}{\overline{H A_{1}}}+\frac{\overline{B H}}{\overline{H B_{1}}}+\frac{\overline{C H}}{\overline{H C_{1}}}=2008$. Calculate the product $\frac{\overline{A H}}{\overline{H A_{1}}} \cdot \frac{\overline{B H}}{\overline{H B_{1}}} \cdot \frac{\overline{C H}}{\overline{H C_{1}}}$.

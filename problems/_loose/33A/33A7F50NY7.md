For the treatment of a patient, we have two types of medication available. The first medication contains 3, 1, 1, 0 units of vitamins A, B, C, and D, respectively, per tablet, while the second contains 0, 1, 3, 2 units, respectively. The patient's daily vitamin requirement is 3 units of A, 9 units of B, 15 units of C, and 2 units of D. Determine:

I. the minimum number of tablets the patient needs to take daily, and how many of each type of medication?

II. When is the treatment the cheapest, if one tablet of the first medication costs 20 fillers, and one tablet of the second costs 60 fillers?

III. If one tablet of the first medication dissolves twice as fast as one tablet of the second, which choice will result in the fastest dissolution?

IV. Is there a choice that is optimal in all three aspects?

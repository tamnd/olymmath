If a is a natural number, show that the number of positive integral solutions of the indeterminate equation $$ x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_3 + ... + nx_n = a \ \ (1) $$
is equal to the number of non-negative integral solutions of $$ y_1 + 2y_2 + 3y_3 + ... + ny_n = a - \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} \ \ (2) $$ [By a solution of equation (1), we mean a set of numbers $\{x_1, x_2,..., x_n\}$ which satisfies equation (1)].

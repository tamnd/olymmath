Let point $P$ be a point on the right branch of the hyperbola $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>0, b>0)$, different from the vertex, and $F_{1}, F_{2}$ be its left and right foci, respectively. Try to prove: The equation of the trajectory of the excenter on the internal angle bisector of $\angle F_{1}$ in $\triangle P F_{1} F_{2}$ is:
$$
(c-a) x^{2}-(c+a) y^{2}=(c-a) c^{2}(x>c) \text {. }
$$

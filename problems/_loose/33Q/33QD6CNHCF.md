There are 1987 glass pieces, each coated with one of the three colors: red, yellow, or blue. The following operation is performed: two pieces of different colors are wiped clean, and then coated with the third color (for example, a blue piece and a red piece have their blue and red wiped off, and then both are coated with yellow). Prove:
(1) Regardless of the initial number of red, yellow, and blue glass pieces, it is always possible to make all the glass pieces the same color after a finite number of operations;
(2) The final color is independent of the order of operations.
(2nd Northeast China Mathematical Invitational Competition, 1987)

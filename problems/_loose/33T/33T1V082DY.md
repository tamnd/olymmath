In the isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB \| CD$, the intersection point of the diagonals is $M$, and $AB > CD = AD$. Consider the incircles of triangles $CDM$ and $CDA$. Prove that the centers of these two circles are equidistant from the center of the circumcircle of the trapezoid.

Express this distance in terms of the circumcircle's radius and the angle $\angle BAD$.

Let $n \geqslant 1$ be an integer. Find all $n$-tuples of strictly positive real numbers $\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ such that

$$
\left(x_{1}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}\right)\left(x_{2}+\frac{1}{x_{3}}\right) \ldots\left(x_{n}+\frac{1}{x_{1}}\right)=\left(x_{1}^{2}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}\right)\left(x_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{x_{3}^{2}}\right) \ldots\left(x_{n}^{2}+\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}\right)
$$

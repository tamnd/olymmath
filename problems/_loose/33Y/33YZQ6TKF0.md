Given a circle passing through the vertices $A$ and $C$ of $\triangle ABC$ intersects the segments $AB$ and $BC$ at the second points $M$ and $K$, respectively. Suppose the circle passing through points $B, K, M$ intersects the segment $AK$ at point $D$. The line $BD$ intersects the segments $MK$ and $AC$ at points $S$ and $L$, respectively. Take a point $T$ on side $AB$ such that $\angle ALT = \angle CBL$. Prove: $TS \parallel AK$.

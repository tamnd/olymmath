Given non-negative real numbers $a_{1} \geq a_{2} \geq \ldots \geq a_{n}, b_{1} \leq b_{2} \leq \ldots \leq b_{n}$, satisfying
$$
a_{1} a_{n}+a_{2} a_{n-1}+\ldots+a_{n} a_{1}=b_{1} b_{n}+b_{2} b_{n-1}+\ldots+b_{n} b_{1}=1 \text {, }
$$

Find
$$
S=\sum_{1 \leq i<j \leq n} a_{i} b_{j}
$$

the minimum value.

Simplify $\frac{a^{4}-a^{2} b^{2}}{(a-b)^{2}} \div \frac{a(a+b)}{b^{2}} \cdot \frac{b^{2}}{a}$, the result is
(A) $\frac{a^{2}}{a-b}$.
(B) $\frac{a^{2}}{a+b}$.
(C) $\frac{b^{4}}{a-b}$.
(D) $\frac{b^{4}}{a+b}$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1982)

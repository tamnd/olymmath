Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and let $M$ and $N$ be midpoints of the sides $AC$ and $BC$ , respectively. The altitudes of the triangle $ABC$ from the vertices $A$ and $B$ , intersect the circumcircle of $ABC$ at the points $X$ and $Y$ , respectively. Show that the line which passes through the circumcenters of the triangles $CNX$ and $CMY$ , is parallel with the line $AB$ .

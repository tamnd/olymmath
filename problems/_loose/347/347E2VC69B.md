Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{k}$ be non-zero natural numbers such that $a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{k}$ divides the product

$$
\left(2^{a_{1}-1}+1\right)\left(2^{a_{2}-1}+1\right) \cdots\left(2^{a_{k}-1}+1\right)
$$

Prove that one of the integers $a_{i}$ is equal to 1.

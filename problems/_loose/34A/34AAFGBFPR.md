3.84 MO brand football is made of several polygonal leather pieces sewn together with 3 different colored threads, and it has the following characteristics:
(1) Any side of a polygonal leather piece is precisely sewn to an equally long side of another polygonal leather piece using a thread of one color;
(2) At each node on the football, exactly 3 polygons share a common vertex, and the 3 threads at each node are of different colors!

Try to prove that a non-1 complex number can be placed at each node of the MO brand football, such that the product of the complex numbers placed at all vertices of each polygon equals 1.

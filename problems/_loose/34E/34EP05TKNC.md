In an equilateral triangle $ABC$, a line passing through the center $O$ intersects the extensions of the sides at points $X, Y, Z$. It is to be proven that the sum

$$
\frac{1}{O X^{2}}+\frac{1}{O Y^{2}}+\frac{1}{O Z^{2}}
$$

is independent of the choice of the line.

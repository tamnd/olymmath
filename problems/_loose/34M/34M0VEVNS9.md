Let $Z=X Y$, where $X$ and $Y$ are independent random variables, $X \sim \mathscr{N}(0,1)$ and $\mathrm{P}(Y=1)=\mathrm{P}(Y=-1)=1 / 2$. Show that $Z \sim \mathscr{N}(0,1)$, and find the distributions of the vectors $(X, Z)$, $(Y, Z)$, as well as the distribution of the random variable $X+Z$. Verify that $X$ and $Z$ are uncorrelated but dependent.

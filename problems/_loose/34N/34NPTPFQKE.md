Let $\left\{\left(X_{i}, Y_{i}\right)\right\}_{i=1}^{n}$ be independent random vectors (in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ ). Prove that conditionally on $\mathscr{G}_{n}=\sigma\left(Y_{1}, \ldots, Y_{n}\right)$ the values $X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}$ are also independent, and $\operatorname{Law}\left(X_{i} \mid \mathscr{G}_{n}\right)=\operatorname{Law}\left(X_{i} \mid Y_{i}\right)$ a.s. for all $1 \leqslant i \leqslant n$.

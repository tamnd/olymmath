Let $B$ be an interior point of segment $A C$. The Thales circles of segments $A B$, $B C$, and $A C$ are $k_{1}$, $k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$, respectively, with radii $r_{1}$, $r_{2}$, and $r_{3}$. The smaller circular segment cut off from circle $k_{3}$ by one of the common external tangents of circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ contains an inscribed tangent circle with radius $r_{4}$. Show that $r_{1} \cdot r_{2}=r_{3} \cdot r_{4}$.

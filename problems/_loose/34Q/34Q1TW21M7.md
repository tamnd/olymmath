Let $A$ be a $3 \times 9$ grid, and fill each small cell with a positive integer. A sub-grid of $A$ of size $m \times n (1 \leqslant m \leqslant 3, 1 \leqslant n \leqslant 9)$ is called a "good rectangle" if the sum of all its numbers is a multiple of 10; a $1 \times 1$ cell in $A$ is called a "bad cell" if it is not contained in any "good rectangle". Find the maximum number of "bad cells" in $A$.

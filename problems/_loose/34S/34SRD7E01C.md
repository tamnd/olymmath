In an intellectual prize guessing game, each participant can answer two questions (Question 1 and Question 2), and they can choose the order in which to answer the questions. However, they can only answer the second question after correctly answering the first one. Assume: answering question $i$ (where $i=1,2$) correctly earns a prize of $a_{i}$ yuan, and the probability of answering question $i$ correctly is $p_{i}$ (where $i=1,2$), and the two answers do not affect each other. If $a_{1}=2 a_{2}, p_{1}+p_{2}=1$, which question should be answered first to potentially win more prize money?

Given a positive real $C \geq 1$ and a sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \cdots$ satisfying for any positive integer $n,$ $a_n \geq 0$
and for any real $x \geq 1$ , $$ \left|x\lg x-\sum_{k=1}^{[x]}\left[\frac{x}{k}\right]a_k \right| \leq Cx, $$ where $[x]$ is defined as the largest integer that does not exceed $x$ . Prove that for any real $y \geq 1$ , $$ \sum_{k=1}^{[y]}a_k < 3Cy. $$

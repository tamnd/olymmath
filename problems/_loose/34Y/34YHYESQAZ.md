Triangle $A B C$ satisfies the condition $a \leqq b \leqq c$ for the lengths of its sides in the usual notation. The inscribed circle touches the sides $A B, B C$, and $A C$ at points $K, L$, and $M$, respectively. Prove that a triangle can be constructed from the segments $A K, B L$, and $C M$ if and only if $b+c<3 a$.

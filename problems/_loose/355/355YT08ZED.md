Let $G$ be a graph with $n$ vertices. An edge $e$ of $G$ is a dead-end if it is possible to partition $G$ into two sets $A$ and $B$ such that:

- There are at most 2023 edges of $\mathrm{G}$ with one endpoint in $A$ and one endpoint in $B$.
- The edge $e$ has one endpoint in $A$ and the other in $B$.

Show that there are at most 2023($n-1$) dead-ends.

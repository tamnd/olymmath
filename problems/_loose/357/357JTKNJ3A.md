Let the circle with center at a point $\left(a, a^{2}\right)(a>0)$ on the parabola $y=x^{2}$, and tangent to the $x$-axis, be denoted by $C(a)$. Suppose $C\left(a_{1}\right), C\left(a_{2}\right), \cdots, C\left(a_{n}\right)$ are a sequence of circles that are pairwise externally tangent, with $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ and $a_{1}>a_{2}>\cdots>a_{n}$. The sum of the circumferences of these circles is denoted by $S_{n}$. Prove: $S_{n}=\frac{\pi}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k^{2}}$.

Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle with the vertex at $C$, where $\angle A C B < 90^{\circ}$. Let $X$ be a point on side $A C$ different from $C$, and $Y$ be a point on side $B C$ different from $C$. Let $D$ be a point such that line $D X$ is parallel to line $A B$, and line $A C$ is the internal angle bisector of $\angle B A D$. Similarly, let $E$ be a point such that line $E Y$ is parallel to line $A B$, and line $B C$ is the internal angle bisector of $\angle E B A$. Suppose there exists a point $T$ on side $A B$ such that $|X T| = |X D|$ and $|Y T| = |Y E|$. Express the value of the expression $|A X| + |B Y|$ in terms of the lengths of the sides of triangle $A B C$.

$l_{1}, l_{3}$ and $l_{2}, l_{4}$ are two sets of mutually perpendicular lines (where $l_{1} / / l_{2}, l_{3} / / l_{4}$ and $l_{1} \perp l_{3}$), PQRS is a rhombus inscribed in the two sets of lines. With the intersection point $M$ of the diagonals of rhombus $P Q R S$ as the center, rotate the diagonals of the rhombus in the positive direction by an angle $\alpha$, intersecting the two sets of lines at $P_{1}, Q_{1}, R_{1}, S_{1}$, and connect them to form a new rhombus $P_{1} Q_{1} R_{1} S_{1}$ inscribed in the two sets of parallel lines. Prove that for any rotation angle $\alpha$, the rhombuses obtained are all similar to each other.

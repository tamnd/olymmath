The function $f(x)$ on the interval $[a, b]$ is equal to the maximum of several functions of the form $y=C \cdot 10^{-|x-d|}$ (with different $d$ and $C$, and all $C$ are positive). It is given that

$f(a)=f(b)$. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the intervals where the function is increasing is equal to the sum of the lengths of the intervals where the function is decreasing.

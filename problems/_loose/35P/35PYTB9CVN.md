Let the family of subsets $A=\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{t}\right\}$ of an $n$-element set $X$ be an $I$-family, and for each $i(1 \leqslant i \leqslant t),\left|A_{i}\right| \leqslant r \leqslant \frac{n}{2}$. If $A$ is also an $S$-family, prove:
$$
t \leqslant C_{n}^{r-1} .
$$

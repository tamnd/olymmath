A positive integer $a$ consists of four digits, three of which are the same. The square of $a$ consists of seven digits, all of which are different. Number $b$ is obtained by reading number $a$ from back to front. It turns out that number $b$ is greater than $a$. In addition, it turns out that $b^{2}$ is exactly equal to $a^{2}$ read from back to front.

Find all possibilities for $a$.

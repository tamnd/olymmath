In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, the edges are $A B=c, B C=a, C A=b, D A=a_{1}, D B=b_{1}$, and finally $D C=c_{1}$. Let $h$ be the length of the median from vertex $D$ of the tetrahedron. Prove that

$$
h^{2}=\frac{1}{3}\left(a_{1}^{2}+b_{1}^{2}+c_{1}^{2}\right)-\frac{1}{9}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)
$$

Fritz claims: Two two-digit numbers that arise from each other by swapping the digits (e.g., 72 and 27) can be multiplied according to the following rule, which is illustrated using the two mentioned numbers:

(1) Calculate the product of the two digits

$$
7 \cdot 2=14
$$

(2) Write the obtained number twice in a row (Note: If the number obtained in (1) is a single digit, write a zero between the two numbers.)

1414

(3) Add the squares of the two digits

(4) Append a zero to the result

(5) Add the results of steps (2) and (4) to get the desired product

$$
1414 + 530 = 1944
$$

Prove the correctness of this claim!

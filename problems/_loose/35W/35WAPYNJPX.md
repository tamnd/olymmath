Tangent ** to the ellipse $b^{2} x^{2}+a^{2} y^{2}=a^{2} b^{2}(a>b>0)$ at one of its foci $F$, draw two mutually perpendicular chords $A B$ and $C D$.
(1) Prove: $\frac{1}{|F A|}+\frac{1}{|F B|}$ is a constant and $\frac{1}{|A B|}+\frac{1}{|C D|}$ is a constant;
(2) Find the minimum values of $|A B|+|C D|$ and $|A B| \cdot|C D|$;
(3) If $M$ is the endpoint of the major axis closer to $F$, find the maximum area of $\triangle A B M$.

Mach plays the following game. At the beginning, there are $k$ piles on the table, with $1,2,3, \ldots, k$ tokens on them, respectively. In each move, he selects any two piles and removes the same number of tokens from both. His goal is to have only one token left on the table. Can he achieve this a) for $k=10$, b) for $k=11$?

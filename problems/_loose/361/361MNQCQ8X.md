Let $A$ and $B$ be arbitrary finite sets and let $f: A\longrightarrow B$ and $g: B\longrightarrow A$
be functions such that $g$ is not onto. Prove that there is a subset $S$ of $A$ such that
$\frac{A}{S}=g(\frac{B}{f(S)})$ .

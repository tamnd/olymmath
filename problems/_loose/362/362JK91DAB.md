$A B C D$ is a convex quadrilateral with area $S$. The angle between the lines $A B$ and $C D$ is $\alpha$, and the angle between $A D$ and $B C$ is $\beta$. Prove that

$$
A B \cdot C D \sin \alpha + A D \cdot B C \sin \beta \leqslant 2 S \leqslant A B \cdot C D + A D \cdot B C
$$

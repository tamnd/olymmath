Let $P$ be a non-constant polynomial with integer coefficients. Suppose that for every integer $n$, the prime factors of $P(n)$ are also prime factors of $n$. Prove that the converse is true: for every integer $n$, the prime factors of $n$ are also prime factors of $P(n)$.

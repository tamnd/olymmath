a) Points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ are isogonally conjugate with respect to triangle $ABC$. Prove that their pedal triangles have a common circumcircle, and that its center is the midpoint of segment $P_{1} P_{2}$.

b) Prove that this statement remains true if, instead of perpendiculars from points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ to the sides, lines are drawn at a given (oriented) angle.

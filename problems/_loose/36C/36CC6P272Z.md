Given n fractions $\frac{a_{i}}{b_{i}}(i=1 ; 2 ; 3 ; \ldots ; n)$, all of which lie in an arbitrary interval $I=[a ; b]$ and whose denominators $b_{i}$ are all positive. Show that the following fraction also lies in $I$:

$$
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i}}
$$

In triangle $ABC$, the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$ are $C_{1}$ and $B_{1}$, respectively, and the foot of the altitude from $A$ is $A_{0}$. Circles are drawn around triangles $AB_{1}C_{1}$, $BC_{1}A_{0}$, and $CB_{1}A_{0}$. It is to be proven that these three circles intersect at a point $M$, and furthermore, that the line $A_{0}M$ bisects the segment $B_{1}C_{1}$.

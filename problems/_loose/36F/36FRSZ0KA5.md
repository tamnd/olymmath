Let $x,y{}$ and $z{}$ be positive real numbers satisfying $x+y+z=1$. Prove that

- $$1-\frac{x^2-yz}{x^2+x}=\frac{(1-y)(1-z)}{x^2+x};$$

- $$\frac{x^2-yz}{x^2+x}+\frac{y^2-zx}{y^2+y}+\frac{z^2-xy}{z^2+z}\leqslant 0.$$

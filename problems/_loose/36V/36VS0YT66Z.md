A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. The tangents at the vertices of the quadrilateral form a circumscribed quadrilateral. Prove that the product of the distances from any point on the circle to the sides of the inscribed quadrilateral is equal to the product of the distances to the sides of the circumscribed quadrilateral. Verify the correctness of this theorem for an inscribed $n$-sided polygon and the corresponding circumscribed $n$-sided polygon.

In the trapezoid $A B C D$ the sides $A B$ and $C D$ are parallel, the angles $\widehat{A B C}$ and $\widehat{B A D}$ are acute. Prove that it is possible to divide the triangle $A B C$ into 4 disjoint triangles $X_{1}, \ldots, X_{4}$ and the triangle $A B D$ into 4 disjoint triangles $Y_{1}, \ldots, Y_{4}$ such that the triangles $X_{i}$ and $Y_{i}$ are congruent for each $i$.

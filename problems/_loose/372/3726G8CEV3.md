Let $\mathbb{Z}^+$ denote the set of positive integers. Find all functions $f: \mathbb{Z}^+ \times \mathbb{Z}^+ \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}^+$ such that
[list=i]

- $f(m,m)=m$

- $f(m,k) = f(k,m)$

- $f(m, m+k) = f(m,k)$
, for each $m,k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.

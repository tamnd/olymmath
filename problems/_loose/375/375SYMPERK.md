We have drawn four lines in the plane, no two of which are parallel, and we observe that, since time immemorial, there has been an ant on each line advancing at a constant speed (not necessarily the same speed for different ants). After an eternity, we notice that, among the six possible pairs of ants, five have crossed paths. Prove that the sixth pair of ants has also crossed paths.

a) Determine $r \in \mathbf{R}$ such that $\overrightarrow{D M}+\overrightarrow{E N}=r \overrightarrow{Q A}$.

b) If $G_{1}, G_{2}$ are the centroids of triangles $M N D$ and $M N E$, show that $G_{1} G_{2} \| B C$.

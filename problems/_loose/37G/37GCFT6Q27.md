Given an ellipse $b^{2} x^{2}+a^{2} y^{2}-a^{2} b^{2}=0$, a line $p$ parallel to the Oy axis intersects it at points $M$ and $N$, where $M$ has a positive, and $N$ a negative ordinate. Determine the geometric locus of the intersection point $P$ of the lines $A M$ and $B N$ and the intersection point $Q$ of the lines $A N$ and $B M$, where $A(-a, 0)$ and $B(a, 0)$ are the vertices of the ellipse, as the line $p$ moves while remaining parallel to the $O y$ axis.

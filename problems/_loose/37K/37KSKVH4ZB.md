Let the roots of the equation $x^{2}-2 b x+b^{2}-c^{2}=0$ be denoted by $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$. Show that the roots of the equation

$$
x^{2}-2 b\left(b^{2}+3 c^{2}\right) x+\left(b^{2}-c^{2}\right)^{3}=0
$$

are $x_{1}^{3}$ and $x_{2}^{3}$.

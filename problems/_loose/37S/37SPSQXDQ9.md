For any convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ with diagonals intersecting at $O$, let the areas of $\triangle ABO$, $\triangle BCO$, $\triangle CDO$, $\triangle DAO$ be $S_{1}$, $S_{2}$, $S_{3}$, $S_{4}$, respectively. Which of the following conclusions must be true?
(A) $S_{1}+S_{3}=S_{2}+S_{4}$.
(B) $S_{1}-S_{3}=S_{2}-S_{4}$.
(C) $S_{1} \cdot S_{3}=S_{2} \cdot S_{4}$.
(D) $S_{1}: S_{3}=S_{2}: S_{4}$.

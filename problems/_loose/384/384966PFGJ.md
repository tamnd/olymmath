Given two points $O$ and $M$. Determine: a) the geometric locus of points on the plane that can serve as one of the vertices of a triangle with the circumcenter at point $O$ and the centroid at point $M$; b) the geometric locus of points on the plane that can serve as one of the vertices of an obtuse triangle with the circumcenter at point $O$ and the centroid at point $M$.

We connect a point $C_{1}$ on the circle with the midpoint $D$ of the radius $O C_{1}$ that is perpendicular to it. The angle bisector of $\angle O D C_{1}$ intersects $O C_{1}$ at $E$. The perpendicular to $O C_{1}$ at $E$ intersects the circle at $C_{2}$ and $C_{5}$. The reflection of $C_{1}$ over $O C_{2}$ is $C_{3}$, and over $O C_{5}$ is $C_{4}$. Prove that $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}, C_{4}, C_{5}$ form a regular pentagon.

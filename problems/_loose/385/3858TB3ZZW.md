Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with $A B>A C$. The point $F$, located on side $(BC)$, is the foot of the altitude drawn from vertex $A$, and $H$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$. On the ray $(BC$ a point $D$ is taken such that $C \in(B D)$. The circumcircle of triangle $DFH$ intersects segment $(A D)$ at point $N$ such that point $N$ also belongs to the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$. Prove that the line $N H$ passes through the midpoint of side $(B C)$.

(See [77].) Let $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be i.i.d. random variables, $\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1}=0\right)=\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1}=2\right)=1 / 2$. Show that
(a) the series $\sum_{n \geqslant 1} \frac{\xi_{n}}{3^{n}}$ converges a.s. to some random variable $\xi$;

(b) the distribution function of the random variable $\xi$ is the Cantor function (see B1.II.3);

(c) find $\mathrm{E} \xi^{n}$ for all $n \geqslant 1$.

Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,\ldots$ be a sequence of all composite positive integers in increasing order. A sequence $b_1,b_2,b_3,\ldots$ is given for all positive integers $i$ by equation $$ b_i=ia_1^2+(i-1)a_2^2+\ldots+2a_{i-1}^2+a_i^2 $$ What is the maximum amount of consecutive elements of sequence $b_1,b_2,b_3,\ldots$ which can be divisible by $3$ ?
*M. Shutro*

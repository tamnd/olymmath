Let the sum of the digits of a positive integer $m$ be denoted as $S(m)$, for example, $S(2017)=2+0+1+7=10$. Now, from the 2017 positive integers $1,2, \cdots$, 2017, any $n$ different numbers are taken out, and among these $n$ numbers, eight different numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{8}$ can always be found such that $S\left(a_{1}\right)=S\left(a_{2}\right)=\cdots=S\left(a_{8}\right)$. Then the minimum value of the positive integer $n$ is ( ).
(A) 185
(B) 187
(C) 189
(D) 191

Given a scalene triangle $ABC$. Choose an arbitrary circle $\omega$ that touches the circumcircle $\Omega$ of triangle $ABC$ internally at point $B$ and does not intersect line $AC$. Mark points $P$ and $Q$ on $\omega$ such that lines $AP$ and $CQ$ are tangent to $\omega$, and segments $AP$ and $CQ$ intersect inside triangle $ABC$. Prove that all such lines $PQ$ pass through a fixed point, independent of the choice of circle $\omega$.

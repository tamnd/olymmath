Given a triangle $A B C$ and a line $l$. Denote by $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ the midpoints of the segments cut off on the line $l$ by the angles $A, B, C$, and by $A_{2}$, $B_{2}, C_{2}$ the points of intersection of the lines $A A_{1}$ and $B C, B B_{1}$ and $A C, C C_{1}$ and $A B$. Prove that the points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ lie on one line.

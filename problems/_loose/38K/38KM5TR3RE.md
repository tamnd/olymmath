If $\cos \alpha=\frac{1}{2}$. Then $\alpha= \pm \frac{\pi}{3}+2 \pi k, 2 \alpha= \pm \frac{2 \pi}{3}+4 \pi k$. But then $\cos \alpha \neq$ $\cos 2 \alpha$. Therefore, in the second case, there are no solutions.

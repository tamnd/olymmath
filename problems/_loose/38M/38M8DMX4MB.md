Given is an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with orthocenter $H$. Let $D$ be a point such that the quadrilateral $AHCD$ is a parallelogram. Let $p$ be the perpendicular to the line $AB$ through the midpoint $A_1$ of the segment $\overline{BC}$. Denote the intersection of the lines $p$ and $AB$ by $E$, and the midpoint of the segment $\overline{A_1 E}$ by $F$. The point where the line parallel to $BD$ through point $A$ intersects $p$ is denoted by $G$. Prove that the quadrilateral $AFA_1C$ is cyclic if and only if the line $BF$ passes through the midpoint of the segment $\overline{CG}$.

(Associative Law of Union and Intersection) For any sets $A, B, C$, we have
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A \cup(B \cup C)=(A \cup B) \cup C, \\
A \cap(B \cap C)=(A \cap B) \cap C .
\end{array}
$$

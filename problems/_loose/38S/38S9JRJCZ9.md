Given that $A, B$ are the left and right vertices of the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{25}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$, and the line $l$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $M, N$. Let the slope of $A M$ be $k_{1}$, and the slope of $B N$ be $k_{2}$, with $k_{1}: k_{2}=1: 9$.
(1) Prove that the line $l$ passes through a fixed point;
(2) Let the area of $\triangle A M N$ be $S_{1}$, and the area of $\triangle B M N$ be $S_{2}$. Find the maximum value of $S_{1}-S_{2}$.

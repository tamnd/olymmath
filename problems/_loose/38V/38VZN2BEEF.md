(SL IMO C1 2009)

2023 cards, each having one red side and one white side, are laid out in a line. Initially, all cards have their red side visible. Alice and Bob take turns making a move. The move consists of choosing a block of 50 consecutive cards where the leftmost card is red, and flipping all of them. Alice starts, and the last player to be able to make a move wins.

(a) Does the game necessarily end?

(b) Does Alice have a winning strategy?

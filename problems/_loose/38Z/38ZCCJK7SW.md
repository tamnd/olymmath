The numbers $0,1, \ldots, \mathrm{n}$ are written on a board. At any time, Thanima can erase a number if it is the arithmetic mean of two numbers still present on the board. Thanima's goal is to erase as many numbers as possible, and she plays optimally. Depending on $\mathrm{n}$, how many numbers are left on the board at the end?

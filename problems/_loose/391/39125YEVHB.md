Prove that the triangle determined by the centers of the excircles of a triangle is always acute-angled. Calculate the sides and area of this triangle given the original triangle. Show that the circumcircle of the original triangle is the Feuerbach circle of the new triangle and thus $2r=R$, where $r$ is the radius of the original and $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle of the new triangle.

(Maksay Zsigmond).

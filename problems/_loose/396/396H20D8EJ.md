Prove: If $x$ is in the first or fourth quadrant, then
$$
\left|\begin{array}{lll}
\sin 2 x & \cos x & \cos x \\
\cos x & \sin 2 x & \cos x \\
\cos x & \cos x & \sin 2 x
\end{array}\right| \geqslant 0
$$

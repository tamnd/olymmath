Suppose that $x$ and $y$ are positive numbers with

$$
\begin{aligned}
x y & =\frac{1}{9} \\
x(y+1) & =\frac{7}{9} \\
y(x+1) & =\frac{5}{18}
\end{aligned}
$$

What is the value of $(x+1)(y+1)$ ?
(A) $\frac{11}{6}$
(B) $\frac{8}{9}$
(C) $\frac{16}{9}$
(D) $\frac{10}{9}$
(E) $\frac{35}{18}$

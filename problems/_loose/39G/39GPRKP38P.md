The set $\mathrm{M}$ consists of $2 n$ different positive real numbers, where $n \geqq 2$. Consider $n$ rectangles, whose dimensions are numbers from $\mathrm{M}$, with each element from $\mathrm{M}$ being used exactly once. Determine the dimensions of these rectangles, if the sum of their areas is

a) the largest possible; b) the smallest possible.

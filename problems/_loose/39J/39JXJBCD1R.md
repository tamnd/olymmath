On the plane, there is a circle with center $O$ and a point $P$ outside the circle. Inscribing a regular $n$-sided polygon in the circle, let the vertices be denoted by $A_{1}$, $A_{2}$, $\ldots, A_{n}$. Show that

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \sqrt[n]{\overline{P A}_{1} \cdot P A_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot \overline{P A}_{n}}=\overline{P O}
$$

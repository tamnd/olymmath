An infinite sequence of positive real numbers $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ is called *territorial* if for all positive integers $i,j$ with $i<j$, we have $|a_i-a_j|\ge\tfrac1j$. Can we find a territorial sequence $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ for which there exists a real number $c$ with $a_i<c$ for all $i$?

Investigate the absolute convergence of improper multiple integrals:

a) $\iint_{\Omega} \frac{\varphi(x, y)}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{p}} d x d y$, where $\Omega: x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1$ and $\Omega: x^{2}+y^{2} \geqslant 1$; $\varphi(x, y)$ - a bounded function on $\Omega$;

b) $\iiint_{\Omega} \frac{\varphi(x, y, z)}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right)^{p}} d x d y d z$, where $\Omega: x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2} \leqslant 1$ and $\Omega:$ $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2} \geqslant 1 ; \varphi(x, y, z)$ - a bounded function on $\Omega$.

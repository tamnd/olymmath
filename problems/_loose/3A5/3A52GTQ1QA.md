Let us say that a subset $M$ of the plane contains a hole if there exists a disc not contained in $M$ , but contained inside some polygon with the boundary lying in $M$ . Can the plane be presented as a union of $n$ convex sets such that the union of any $n-1$ from them contains a hole?

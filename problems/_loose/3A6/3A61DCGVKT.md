Jingoistic James wants to teach his kindergarten class how to add in Chimese. The Chimese also use base-10 number system, but have replaced the digits 0-9 with ten of its own characters. For example, the two-digit number 十六 represents $16$ . What is the sum of the two-digit numbers 十六 and 六十?

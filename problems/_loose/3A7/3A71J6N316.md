Let $S = \{(x, y) : x, y \in \{1, 2, 3, \dots, 2012\}\}$ . For all points $(a, b)$ , let $N(a, b) = \{(a - 1, b), (a + 1, b), (a, b - 1), (a, b + 1)\}$ . Kathy constructs a set $T$ by adding $n$ distinct points from $S$ to $T$ at random. If the expected value of $ \sum_{(a, b) \in T} | N(a, b) \cap T |$ is 4, then compute $n$ .

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

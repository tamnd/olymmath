A novice is shooting baskets. For each hit, she gets 1 point, and for each miss, $k$ points are deducted, where $k$ is a fixed natural number. (At the beginning, she has 0 points, and the number of points earned at any point during the training can be negative.) At the end of the training, the novice had $k$ points. If it is known that the novice hit more than $75 \%$ but less than $80 \%$ of the total number of shots, determine $k$.

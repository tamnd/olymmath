A bank pays an annual 100% interest, non-compounding (so 200% for two years, 50% for half a year). András noticed that if he deposits 100 forints in the bank, after half a year he withdraws the entire amount in his account and immediately deposits it again, then at the end of the year he will have not 200, but 225 forints. If he does this more frequently during the year, he will have even more money in his bank account.

Prove that the account will always have less than 272 forints, but András can ensure that he has at least 271 forints at the end of the year.

a) Prove that the polynomial $P(x)=x^{4 n}+x^{3 n}+x^{2 n}+x^{n}+1$ is divisible by the polynomial $Q(x)=x^{4}+x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1$ if and only if $n$ is not divisible by 5.

b) Prove that if the numbers $m$ and $n$ are coprime, then $x^{(m-1) n}+\ldots+x^{2 n}+x^{n}+1$ is divisible by $x^{m-1}+\ldots+x^{2}+x+1$.

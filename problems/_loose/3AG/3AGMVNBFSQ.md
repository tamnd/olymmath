A billiard table has the shape of a polygon (not necessarily convex), in which adjacent sides are perpendicular to each other. The vertices of this polygon are pockets, into which the ball remains if it falls. A ball is shot from a vertex with an (internal) angle of $90^{\circ}$, which reflects off the sides (edges of the polygon) according to the law "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection." Prove that the ball will never return to this vertex.

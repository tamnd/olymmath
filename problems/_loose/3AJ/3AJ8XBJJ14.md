Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$ with center $O$ and orthocenter $H$. We denote $H_{A}$ as the foot of the altitude from $A$ and $M$ as the midpoint of $[BC]$. Show that

1. The symmetric point $H^{\prime}$ of $H$ with respect to $M$ lies on $\Gamma$.
2. The symmetric point $H^{*}$ of $H$ with respect to $(BC)$.
3. Show that $\left(H^{\prime} H^{*}\right) / /(BC)$.

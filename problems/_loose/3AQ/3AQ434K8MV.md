Given that all terms of the sequence $\{f(n)\}$ are positive, and satisfy the relation $f(n+1) \leqslant f^{*}(n)-f^{*+1}(n)(k \in$ $\left.\mathbf{N}^{+}\right)$. Prove that for all positive integers $n(n \geqslant 2)$, we have
$$
f(n) \leqslant \frac{k^{k}}{(k+1)^{k+1}+(n-2) k^{k}} .
$$

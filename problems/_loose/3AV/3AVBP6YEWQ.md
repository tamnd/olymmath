Consider the tangents drawn from a point $P$ lying in the plane of a parabola to the parabola, and their points of tangency $T_{1}, T_{2}$. Show that:

$1^{0}$. the distance of the point $P$ from the axis of the parabola is the arithmetic mean of the distances of the points $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ from the same axis;

$2^{0}$. the distance of the point $P$ from the vertex tangent of the parabola is the geometric mean of the distances of the points $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ from the same line;

$3^{0}$. the distance of the point $P$ from the focus of the parabola is the geometric mean of the distances of the points $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ from the focus.

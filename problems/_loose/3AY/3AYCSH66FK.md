(1996 Japan Kenrin Mathematical Olympiad Problem) Let $x>1$, and $x$ is not an integer. Prove that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with the general term $a_{n}=\left[x^{n+1}\right]-x\left[x^{n}\right](n=1,2,3, \cdots)$ is not a periodic sequence, i.e., there does not exist a positive integer $p$ such that for any positive integer $n$, $a_{p+n}=a_{p}$. (Here $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$).

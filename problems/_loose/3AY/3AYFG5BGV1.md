Find all $f:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}$ such that
$$f\left(\left\lfloor\frac{f(x)+f(y)}{2}\right\rfloor\right)+f(x)=f(f(y))+\left\lfloor\frac{f(x)+f(y)}{2}\right\rfloor$$
holds for all $x,y\in\mathbb{Z}$ .

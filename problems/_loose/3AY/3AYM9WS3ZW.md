Two circles with radii $x$ and $y$, which touch each other externally, touch the circle $k_{1}\left(O_{1}, R_{1}\right)$ internally at two diametrically opposite points. The circle $k(O, R)$ touches all three circles. Prove that:
a) $x y=\frac{R R_{1}^{2}}{R+R_{1}}$

b) $R \leq \frac{R_{1}}{3}$. In which case is equality achieved?

A parallelepiped has three edge vectors starting from one of its vertices, let this vertex be $O$, and denote these vectors by $a$, $b$, and $c$. Find the midpoint of the three faces passing through the opposite vertex, then find the centroid of the triangle formed by these midpoints. Express the vector from $O$ to the centroid in terms of $a$, $b$, and $c$.

Let $p / q$ be an irreducible fraction, $p$ and $q$ be natural numbers. Set $f\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)=\frac{p^{2} q^{2}}{q_{1} \ldots q_{n}}$, where $q_{1}, \ldots, q_{n}$ are the distinct prime divisors of $q$. Prove that $f$ is a bijective mapping from the set of positive rational numbers to the set of all natural numbers.

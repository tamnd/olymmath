Consider the system of equations $\left\{\begin{array}{l}a_{11} x_{1}+a_{12} x_{2}+\cdots+a_{1 n} x_{n}=0, \\ a_{21} x_{1}+a_{22} x_{2}+\cdots+a_{2 n} x_{n}=0, \\ \cdots \cdots \cdots \cdots \\ a_{m 1} x_{1}+a_{m 2} x_{2}+\cdots+a_{m n} x_{n}=0 .\end{array}\right.$ where the coefficients $a_{i j}$ are integers not all zero, prove: when $n \geqslant 2 \mathrm{~m}$, there is a set of integer solutions $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}\right)$ satisfying $0<\max \left|x_{i}\right| \leqslant n\left(\max \left|a_{i j}\right|\right)$.

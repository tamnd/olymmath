Assume that $\{a_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ is an infinite arithmetic sequence and $ \{b_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ is an infinite geometric sequence. If it is given that $a_1 < a_2$ , $b_i = a^2_i$ for $i = 1, 2, 3$ , and $$ \lim_{n\to \infty} (b_1 + b_2 +...+ b_n) = \sqrt2 + 1, $$ determine, with proof, all possible sequences $\{a_n\}$ .

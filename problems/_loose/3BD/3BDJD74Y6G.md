Given the sequence (Fibonacci) $f_{1}=f_{2}=1$ and $f_{n+2}=f_{n+1}+f_{n}$.

a) Prove the equality:

$f_{1}^{2}+f_{2}^{2}+\cdots+f_{n}^{2}=f_{n} \cdot f_{n+1}$.

b) Calculate $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \sqrt[n]{\frac{f_{n+1}^{2}}{f_{1}^{2}+f_{2}^{2}+\cdots f_{n}^{2}}}$.

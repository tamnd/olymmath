$2+$ Let $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, try to find the positive root $x$ of the following equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \sqrt{a b x(a+b+x)}+\sqrt{b c x(b+c+x)}+\sqrt{c a x(c+a+x)} \\
= & \sqrt{a b c(a+b+c)} .
\end{aligned}
$$

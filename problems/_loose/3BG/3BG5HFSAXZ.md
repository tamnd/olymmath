Prove that the squares of the distances from the center of the circle inscribed in a quadrilateral to two of its opposite vertices are in the ratio of the products of the sides of the quadrilateral meeting at the corresponding vertices.

Verify the validity of the theorem for quadrilaterals circumscribed about a circle. Consider three cases: the quadrilateral is convex, concave, and self-intersecting.

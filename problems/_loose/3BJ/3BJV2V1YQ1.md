a) $\triangle P_{1} P_{2} P_{3} \cong \triangle Q_{1} Q_{2} Q_{3} \sim \triangle A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$;

b) point $K$, the centre of $k$ and the circumcentre $\triangle A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$ lie on a line.

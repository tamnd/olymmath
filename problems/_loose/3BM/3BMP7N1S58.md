For the sides $a, b, c$ of a triangle, on the one hand,

$$
a>b>c
$$

on the other hand,

$$
a=2(b-c)
$$

Let the projection of side $c$ onto side $a$ be denoted by $q$, and prove that $c+2q=3a/4$.

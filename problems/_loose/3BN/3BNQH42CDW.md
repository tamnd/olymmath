3-53 Given that $a$ is a positive integer, $r=\sqrt{a+1}+\sqrt{a}$, prove that for each positive integer $n$, there exists a positive integer $a_{n}$, such that
(1) $r^{2 n}+r^{-2 n}=4 a_{n}+2$;
(2) $r^{n}=\sqrt{a_{n}+1}+\sqrt{a_{n}}$.

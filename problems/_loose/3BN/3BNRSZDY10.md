The worm eats through the cheese in such a way that it always advances $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ parallel to one of the edges at a time, then changes direction, being careful that when it starts in a new direction, there is more than $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ of untouched cheese in front of it. Assuming that at both the start and when changing direction, the worm chooses one of the possible directions with equal probability, what is the probability that after traveling $5 \mathrm{~cm}$, it will be at most $0.8 \mathrm{~cm}$ away from one of the edges?

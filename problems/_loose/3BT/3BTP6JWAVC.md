On a white sheet of paper, several points are marked which are at a mutual distance greater than 2. An inattentive student spilled a drop of glue on the sheet, and the glue spread over the sheet covering an area with an area smaller than $\pi$. Prove that there exists a vector $\vec{v}$ such that $|\vec{v}|<1$ and that after translating the marked points by this vector, no point remains in the splattered area of the paper.

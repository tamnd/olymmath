Find eight strictly positive integers $n_{1}, n_{2}, \ldots, n_{8}$ having the following property:

for every integer $k, -2007 \leq k \leq 2007$, there exist eight integers $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \ldots, \alpha_{8}$ all belonging to $\{-1,0,1\}$, such that

$$
k=\alpha_{1} n_{1}+\alpha_{2} n_{2}+\ldots+\alpha_{8} n_{8}
$$

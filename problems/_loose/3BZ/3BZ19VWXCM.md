Given a linear function $f(x)$. It is known that the distance between the points of intersection of the graphs $y=$ $x^{2}-1$ and $y=f(x)$ is $\sqrt{30}$, and the distance between the points of intersection of the graphs $y=x^{2}$ and $y=f(x)+3$ is $\sqrt{46}$. Find the distance between the points of intersection of the graphs of the functions $y=x^{2}-1$ and $y=f(x)+1$.

800 numbers are written in the form of a rectangular table with 100 rows and 80 columns; in this case, the product of the sum of all numbers in any column and the sum of all numbers in any row is equal to the number located at the intersection of this column and this row. It is known that the number in the top right corner of the table is positive. What is the sum of all the numbers that make up the table?

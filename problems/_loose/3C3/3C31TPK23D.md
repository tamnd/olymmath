At the vertices of a convex polyhedron with $n$ vertices, two positive numbers are written: a blue one and a red one, such that the sum of the blue numbers equals the sum of the red numbers. In one move, you can change the two blue numbers at the ends of any one edge so that they remain positive and their sum is preserved. Prove that in no more than $n-1$ moves, you can achieve that in each vertex the blue number becomes equal to the red one.

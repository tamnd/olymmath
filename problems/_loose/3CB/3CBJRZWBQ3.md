Given that the area of $\triangle ABC$ is $S_{\triangle}$, and the circumradius is $R$. Draw perpendiculars from $A, B, C$ to the plane $ABC$ and take points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ on the same side of the plane $ABC$ such that $A A_{1}=h_{a}, B B_{1}=h_{b}, C C_{1}=h_{c}$, where $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$ are the altitudes from vertices $A, B, C$ to the opposite sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. Find the volume of the tetrahedron formed by the four planes $A_{1} B_{1} C, B_{1} C_{1} A, C_{1} A_{1} B$, and $ABC$.

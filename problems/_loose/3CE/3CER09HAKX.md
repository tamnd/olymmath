(La Vallée Poussin's Theorem.) Prove that $\left\{\xi_{n}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ is uniformly integrable if and only if

$$
\sup _{n} E G\left(\left|\xi_{n}\right|\right)<\infty
$$

for some non-negative increasing function $G$, such that

$$
\frac{G(x)}{x} \rightarrow \infty, x \rightarrow \infty
$$

Also show that $G$ can be taken as a convex function.

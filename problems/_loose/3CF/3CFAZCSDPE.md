Determine for which positive integers $n$ there exists a positive integer $A$ such that
• $A$ is divisible by $2022$,
• the decimal expression of $A$ contains only digits $0$ and $7$,
• the decimal expression of $A$ contains *exactly* $n$ times the digit $7$.

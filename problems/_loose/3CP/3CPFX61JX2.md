Among the students of a class, walnuts were distributed. The first student received $a$ walnuts and one-thirtieth of the remaining walnuts; the second student received $2a$ walnuts and one-thirtieth of the remaining walnuts; the third student received $3a$ walnuts and one-thirtieth of the remaining walnuts, and so on. Prove that if the first two students received the same number of walnuts, then everyone received the same number of walnuts. What is the class size in this case?

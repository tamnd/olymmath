In $\triangle A B C$, points $O$ and $I$ represent the centers of the circumcircle and the incircle, respectively. Let $O_{1}$ be the central symmetric image of point $O$ with respect to point $I$, and let $I_{1}$ be the central symmetric image of point $I$ with respect to point $O$. If $O_{1}$ lies on the altitude from vertex $A$ and $I_{1}$ lies on the altitude from vertex $B$, prove that $\triangle A B C$ is equilateral.

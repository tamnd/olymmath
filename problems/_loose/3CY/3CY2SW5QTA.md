Four men are named Baker, Fisher, Forester, and Miller. They practice the professions of baker, fisher, forester, and miller, each exactly one of these professions. One of the four men is the brother of a fifth man, called Mr. X. (He naturally has the same name as his brother.) The following statements are made about these five men:

(1) Mr. X also practices exactly one profession, the same as Mr. Baker.

(2) Mr. X practices a different profession than his brother.

(3) For each of the five men, the initial letter of his name is different from the initial letter of his profession.

a) Prove that Mr. X cannot be named Baker according to these statements!

b) Prove that it can be uniquely determined from the statements how Mr. X is named and which two professions Mr. X and his brother have!

c) Prove that it cannot be uniquely determined from the statements which profession Mr. X has and which of the four other men is the baker!

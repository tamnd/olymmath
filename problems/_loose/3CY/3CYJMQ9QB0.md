On each of the $n+1$ faces of an $n$-sided pyramid, the number 0 is written. In each step, we choose some vertex and increase or decrease by 1 the numbers on all faces containing this vertex. Prove that it is impossible for the situation to arise where the number 1 is written on all faces of the pyramid.

a) Draw a parallelogram $A B C D$ where the side lengths $A B=5 \mathrm{~cm}, B C=3 \mathrm{~cm}$, and the angle $\angle B A D$ is $\alpha=50^{\circ}$!

Construct the squares $A D P Q$ and $D C R S$ on the sides $A D$ and $D C$ such that these square areas are completely outside the parallelogram area!

b) Prove that for any parallelogram $A B C D$, where $\angle B A D$ is less than $90^{\circ}$, after constructing such squares, the segments $B Q$ and $B R$ are of equal length and perpendicular to each other!

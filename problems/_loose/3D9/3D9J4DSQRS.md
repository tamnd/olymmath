There is a set of natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$, among which there may be identical ones. Denote by $f_{k}$ the number of numbers in this set that are not less than $k$. Prove that $f_{1}+f_{2}+\ldots=a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}$.

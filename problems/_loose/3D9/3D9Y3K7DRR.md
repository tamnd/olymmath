Inside the circle $\alpha$ there is a circle $\beta$. On the circle $\alpha$ there are two sequences of points: $A_{1}, A_{2}$, $A_{3} \ldots$ and $B_{1}, B_{2}, B_{3} \ldots$, following in the same direction, such that the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, A_{3} A_{4} \ldots$ and $B_{1} B_{2}, B_{2} B_{3}, B_{3} B_{4} \ldots$ are tangent to the circle $\beta$. Prove that the lines $A_{1} B_{1}, A_{2} B_{2}$, $A_{3} B_{3} \ldots$ are tangent to one circle, the center of which lies on the line passing through the centers of the circles $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

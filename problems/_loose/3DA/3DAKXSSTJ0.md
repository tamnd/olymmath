(18 (15 points) On a circle, there are $k$ numbers, $k \geqslant 3$. If any three consecutive numbers, in clockwise order, are denoted as $a$, $b$, and $c$, there is always $b=\alpha a+\beta c$, where $\alpha \geqslant 0$, $\beta \geqslant 0, \alpha+\beta=1$. Prove that these $k$ numbers must be equal.

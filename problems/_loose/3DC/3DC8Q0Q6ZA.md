Some positive integers are written on the blackboard, some of which are red numbers, and the others are blue numbers. Person A tries to erase some of the numbers (not all), so that the remaining numbers have a count of red numbers that is a multiple of 3, and the sum of all remaining numbers is a multiple of 2013. Despite A's wish, it is impossible to achieve. How many numbers can there be at most on the blackboard?

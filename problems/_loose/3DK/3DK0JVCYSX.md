On a strip of paper, 60 characters: "x" and "0" are written in a row. This strip is cut into pieces with symmetrical arrangements of characters. For example:

$0, \times \times, 0 \times \times \times 0, \times 0 \times, \ldots$.

a) Prove that there exists a way to cut the strip such that there are no more than 24 pieces.

b) Provide an example of such an arrangement of characters where it is impossible to get fewer than 15 pieces.

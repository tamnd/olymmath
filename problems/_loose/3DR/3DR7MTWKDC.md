Show that

$$
\left(x_{1}+\ldots+x_{r}\right)^{N}=\sum C_{N}\left(n_{1}, \ldots, n_{r}\right) x_{1}^{n_{1}} \ldots x_{r}^{n_{r}}
$$

where the summation extends over all $n_{i} \geqslant 0$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{r} n_{i}=N$.

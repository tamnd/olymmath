Let $x, y, z$ be distinct non-zero digits.

a) Determine the rational values of the number $a=\sqrt{\overline{0, x(y)}+\overline{0, y(z)}+\overline{0, z(x)}}$

b) Find the rational values of the number $a=\sqrt{0,00 \ldots . .0 x(y)}+\overline{0,00 \ldots .0 y(z)}+\overline{0,00 \ldots . .0 z(x)}$, where after the decimal point in each number there are $n$ zeros, $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

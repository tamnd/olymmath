Positive real numbers $a,b,c$ satisfy $a+b+c=1$. Prove that
$$(a+1)\sqrt{2a(1-a)} + (b+1)\sqrt{2b(1-b)} + (c+1)\sqrt{2c(1-c)} \geq 8(ab+bc+ca).$$
Also, find the values of $a,b,c$ for which the equality happens.

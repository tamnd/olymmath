Let $a, b$, and $c$ be positive real numbers, $a, b, c \neq 1$. If

$$
\log _{a} 2018+\log _{c} 2018=2 \log _{b} 2018
$$

prove:
a) $\log _{a} 2019+\log _{c} 2019=2 \log _{b} 2019$;
b) $a^{2}=(a c)^{\log _{c} b}$.

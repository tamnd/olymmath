Let $f_1,f_2$ be nonnegative and concave functions. Then prove that
$$(f_1f_2)^{\frac{2^n-1}{n\cdot2^n}}\left(\frac{ \prod_{k=1}^n\left(\sqrt[2^k]{f_1}+\sqrt[2^k]{f_2}\right)}{f_1+f_2}\right)^{\frac1n}$$
is concave.

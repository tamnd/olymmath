Let $F_{1}, F_{2}$ be the left and right foci of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, and let point $P$ be a point on the ellipse. Then the excircle of $\triangle P F_{1} F_{2}$ that contains $\angle P F_{1} F_{2}$ must touch the right vertex $A_{2}$ of the ellipse, and the excircle of $\triangle P F_{1} F_{2}$ that contains $\angle P F_{2} F_{1}$ must touch the left vertex $A_{1}$ of the ellipse.

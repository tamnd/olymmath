In the $xy$-plane, consider the curve $C: y=x^3+x^2+1$, let $P_0(1,\ 3).$ For $k=1,\ 2,\ 3,\ \cdots$, let $P_k(x_k,\ y_k)$ is the point of intersection other than the point $P_{k-1}$ between the tangent line of $C$ at the point $P_{k-1}(x_{k-1},\ y_{k-1})$ and $C$. Denote by $S_k$ the area of the part enclosed by the segment $P_{k-1}P_k$ and $C$.

(1) Find $S_1$.

(2) Express $x_k$ in terms of $k$.

(3) Find $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{S_k}.$

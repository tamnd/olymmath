Prove that no convex polygon can be composed of two or more non-overlapping pairwise distinct equilateral triangles.

One could pose the problem: which convex figures can be composed of regular pentagons, or hexagons, etc. However, it is easy to see that this problem is not substantial: for $n \geqslant 5$, from any number (greater than or equal to two) of regular $n$-gons, even not necessarily pairwise distinct, no convex polygon can be composed.

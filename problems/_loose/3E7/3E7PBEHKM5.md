a) Prove that there exists a natural number $N$ for which the following statement (1) holds!

(1) For every natural number $n$ for which $n \geq N$, a square area $F$ can be divided into exactly $n$ sub-squares $T_{1}, \ldots, T_{n}$.

(The areas $T_{1}, \ldots, T_{n}$ should completely fill the area $F$; they do not need to be congruent with each other.)

b) Determine the smallest natural number $N$ for which statement (1) holds!

Let $a$ and $b$ be two real numbers.
Find these numbers given that the graphs of $f:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} , f(x)=2x^4-a^2x^2+b-1$ and $g:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} ,g(x)=2ax^3-1$ have exactly two points of intersection.

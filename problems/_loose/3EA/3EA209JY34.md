On a plane, an acute triangle $A B C$ was drawn, in which angle $A \neq 60^{\circ}$. In it, the point of intersection of the altitudes $H$ and the center of the circumscribed circle $O$ were marked, and then the lines $m=B H$ and $n=C H$ were drawn. After that, everything was erased from the drawing except for the lines $m, n$ and the point $O$ (that is, two lines and one point remained). Restore the triangle $A B C$ using a compass and a ruler.

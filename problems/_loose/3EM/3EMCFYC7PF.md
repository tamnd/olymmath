Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$, and let $D$ be a point on the segment $[B C]$ such that $B D \neq C D$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the orthogonal projections of $D$ onto $(A B)$ and $(A C)$. Finally, let $E$ be the point of intersection, other than $A$, between the circumcircles of $A B C$ and $A P Q$.

Prove that if the lines $(E P)$, $(A C)$, and the perpendicular bisector of $[P Q]$ are concurrent, then the triangle $A B C$ is right-angled at $A$.

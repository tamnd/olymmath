A sequence of natural numbers $a_1, a_2, a_3,..$ is called *periodic modulo* $n$ if there exists a positive integer $k$ such that, for any positive integer $i$ , the terms $a_i$ and $a_{i+k}$ are equal modulo $n$ . Does there exist a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers that
a) is not periodic modulo finitely many positive integers and is periodic modulo all the other positive integers?
b) is not periodic modulo infinitely many positive integers and is periodic modulo infinitely many positive integers?

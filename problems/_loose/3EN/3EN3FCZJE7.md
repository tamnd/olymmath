Consider a network of points formed by integer points on a plane, and prove the following conclusions for a line with a rational slope:
(1) Such a line either does not pass through any network points, or it passes through infinitely many network points.
(2) For every such line, there exists a positive number $ d $, such that apart from the network points that may lie on the line, there are no network points whose distance to the line is less than $ d $.

The other is allowed to ask what the sum $a_{1} x_{1}+\ldots+a_{\mathrm{n}} x_{\mathrm{n}}$ is, where $\left(a_{1} \ldots a_{\mathrm{n}}\right)--$ any set. What is the smallest number of questions with which the guesser can find out the thought set?

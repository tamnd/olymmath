On a distant planet, there are $n$ men, $n$ women, and $n$ mathematicians. A happy family is a family consisting of a man, a woman, and a mathematician. However, to form a family, the three members must like each other (liking is always mutual). Is it always possible to form $n$ disjoint families knowing that each member of each of the three groups likes at least $k$ people in each of the other two groups (so $2 k$ people in total), and if:

(i) $2 k=n$ ?

(ii) $4 k=3 n$ ?

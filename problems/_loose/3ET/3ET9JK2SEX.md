It is known that the difference between the variable quantities $y$ and $z$ is proportional to the quantity $x$, and the difference between the quantities $z$ and $x$ is proportional to the quantity $\boldsymbol{y}$. The coefficients of these proportions are $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively, and $k_{1} \neq k_{2}$. Some value of the quantity $z$ is three times the difference of the corresponding values of $x$ and $y$. Prove that if each of the coefficients $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ is increased by 3, then the product of the resulting numbers will be equal to the number 8 (it is assumed that the quantities $x$ and $y$ do not take zero values).

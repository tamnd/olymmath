Let the natural numbers a and b which satisfy the equalities:

$$
a-4=3 \cdot\left(4+4^{2}+\ldots+4^{2014}\right) \text { and } b-8=5 \cdot\left(6+6^{2}+\ldots+6^{2015}\right)
$$

Show that: i) a is a perfect square;

ii) b is not a perfect square.

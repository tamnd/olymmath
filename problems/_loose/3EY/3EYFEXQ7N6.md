Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers such that $a > b$. Professor Fernando told student Raul that if he calculated the number $A = a^2 + 4b + 1$, the result would be a perfect square. Raul, by mistake, swapped the numbers $a$ and $b$ and calculated the number $B = b^2 + 4a + 1$, which, by chance, is also a perfect square.

a) Show that $A = (a+1)^2$.

b) Find the numbers $a, b, A$, and $B$.

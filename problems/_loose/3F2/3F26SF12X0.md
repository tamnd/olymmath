(utilisation in arithmetic: generalized Bézout's theorem)

Let $n \geqslant 2$ be a natural number. The GCD of $\left\{a_{1}, a_{1} \ldots a_{n}\right\}$ is the greatest common divisor of all these numbers. We recover the known definition for $n=2$. Denoting $d$ as this GCD, show that there exist natural numbers $b_{1}, \ldots b_{n}$ such that $a_{1} * b_{1}+a_{2} * b_{2}+\ldots .+a_{n} * b_{n}=d$

Three points $A, B$, and $C$ on a sphere of radius $R$ are connected pairwise (by the shorter) arcs of great circles. Another great circle is drawn through the midpoints of the arcs $A B$ and $A C$, intersecting the extension of the arc $B C$ at point $K$. Find the length of the arc $C K$, if the length of the arc $B C$ is $l(l<\pi R)$. (A great circle is the section of a sphere by a plane passing through its center.)

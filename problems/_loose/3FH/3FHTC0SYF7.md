Three frogs have learned to jump up and down a ladder. Each can jump both up and down, but only by certain numbers of rungs. The frogs start on the ground and each would like to reach their favorite rung:

- the small frog can jump 2 or 3 rungs and wants to reach the seventh rung,
- the medium frog can jump 2 or 4 rungs and wants to reach the first rung,
- the large frog can jump 6 or 9 rungs and wants to reach the third rung.

Decide for each frog whether their wish is achievable. If yes, describe how. If not, explain why.

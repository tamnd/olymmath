If a positive integer can be expressed as the difference of cubes of two consecutive odd numbers, then the positive integer is called a "harmonious number" (for example, $2=1^{3}-(-1)^{3}, 26=3^{3}-1^{3}, 2, 26$ are both harmonious numbers). Among the positive integers not exceeding 2016, the sum of all harmonious numbers is ( ).
(A) 6858
(B) 6860
(C) 9260
(D) 9262

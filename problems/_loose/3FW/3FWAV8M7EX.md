Determine the values that the expression

$$
\frac{a+b c}{a+b}+\frac{b+c a}{b+c}+\frac{c+a b}{c+a}
$$

can take if $ a $, $ b $, and $ c $ are positive real numbers with a sum of 1. (Michal Rolínek, Pavel Calábek)

Let $a, b$, and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=3$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a^{4}+3 a b^{3}}{a^{3}+2 b^{3}}+\frac{b^{4}+3 b c^{3}}{b^{3}+2 c^{3}}+\frac{c^{4}+3 c a^{3}}{c^{3}+2 a^{3}} \leqslant 4
$$

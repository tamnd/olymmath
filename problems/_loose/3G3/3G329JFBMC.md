a) Let $x_1,x_2,x_3,y_1,y_2,y_3\in \mathbb{R}$ and $a_{ij}=\sin(x_i-y_j),\ i,j=\overline{1,3}$ and $A=(a_{ij})\in \mathcal{M}_3$ Prove that $\det A=0$.

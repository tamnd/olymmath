Consider an $8 \times 8$ chessboard filled with lamps (either off or on), and we allow operations that consist of inverting all the squares:

- of the same row
- of the same column
- of the same diagonal or parallel to a diagonal (including the parallels to a single square, i.e., the corners)

In the initial position, all the squares are lit, except the fourth square of the first row.

Can we end up in the position where all the lamps are off?

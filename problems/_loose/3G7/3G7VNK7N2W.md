Let $G=\left(\frac{1}{k}, k\right), k>1$ and the composition law:

$$
x * y=\frac{\frac{k^{2}+1}{k} x y-2(x+y)+\frac{k^{2}+1}{k}}{2 x y-\frac{k^{2}+1}{k}(x+y)+\frac{k^{4}+1}{k^{2}}}, \forall x, y \in G
$$

Show that:
a) $(G, *)$ is a group;

b) Calculate $x^{(n)}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*} \backslash\{1\}$ and determine the finite stable subsets of $(G, *)$;

c) Determine a strictly decreasing automorphism of $(G, *)$.

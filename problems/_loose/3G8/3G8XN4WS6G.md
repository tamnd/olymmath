Let $ABC$ be a right triangle with the right angle at $B$ and with $AB=5 \text{ cm}, BC=3$ $\text{cm}$. The perpendicular bisector of $AC$ intersects $AC$ at $M$ and $AB$ at $E$.

a) Prove that under these conditions, the angles $\angle MEA$ and $\angle MCB$ are equal in size!

b) A point $D$ lies on the line through $E$ and $M$ such that $AC$ bisects the angle $\angle DAB$. Prove that the quadrilateral $ABCD$ must then be a trapezoid!

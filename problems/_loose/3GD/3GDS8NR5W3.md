Show that the roots of the equation

$$
3 x^{2}(a+b+c)+4 x(a b+b c+c a)+4 a b c=0
$$

are real for all values of $a, b, c$. Furthermore, show that the roots are equal if $b=c$, or if $b c=a(b+c-a)$.

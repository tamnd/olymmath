A summer camp in Valbonne has 91 participants, some of whom are friends. Each day, if possible, three participants $A, B, C$ are chosen such that $A$ is friends with $B$ and $C$, but $B$ and $C$ are not friends, and the friendship links $A B$ and $A C$ are removed, before adding a friendship link between $B$ and $C$. Initially, 45 participants have 46 friends and 46 participants have 45 friends. The organizers of the camp want that at the end, each participant has at most one friend. Is this possible?

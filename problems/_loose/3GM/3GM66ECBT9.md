The rhombuses $ABCD$ and $DEFG$ have only one common point, $(AB) \equiv(DE), \measuredangle ABC \equiv \measuredangle DEF$, and the points $B, D$, and $F$ are collinear. Prove that:
a) $m(\measuredangle ADG)=90^{\circ}$;

b) the midpoint of segment $[BF]$ is the point $L$, where $\{L\}=AE \cap CG$;
c) $BF \leq 2 \sqrt{2} \cdot AB$.

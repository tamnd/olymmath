Prove:

For every prime number $p$, there exists a real number $c$ such that the sequence $\left(a_{k}\right)_{k=1,2,3, \ldots}$, defined by

$$
a_{1}=c, a_{k+1}=a_{k}^{2}+c (k=1,2,3, \ldots)
$$

is periodic and has $p$ as its smallest period length.

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ and $a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be distinct integers. Show that the polynomial

$$
P(X)=1+\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(X-a_{i}\right)
$$

is irreducible over $\mathbb{Z}$.

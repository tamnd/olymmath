Prove that among all lines lying in the plane of one of the faces of an acute dihedral angle, the lines perpendicular to the edge of the dihedral angle form the greatest angle with the plane of the second face.

[If the second face of the given dihedral angle is horizontal, then the line drawn in the plane of the first face perpendicular to the edge is called the line of greatest slope or the line of fall: a heavy ball rolls down along this line.]

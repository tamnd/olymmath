Let $\{a_n\}$ be a non-negative arithmetic sequence with common difference $d \neq 0$, and let $S_n$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\{a_n\}$. Prove:
(1) If $m, n, p \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, and $m+n=2p$, then $\frac{1}{S_m} + \frac{1}{S_n} \geq \frac{2}{S_p}$;
(2) If $a_{503} \leq \frac{1}{1005}$, then $\sum_{n=1}^{2007} \frac{1}{S_n} > 2008$.

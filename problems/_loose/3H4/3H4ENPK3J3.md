Construct a triangle $A B C$ from $\alpha=60^{\circ}, \beta=35^{\circ}$ and $w_{\alpha}=5.5 \mathrm{~cm}$!

Here, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the measures of the angles $\angle B A C$ and $\angle A B C$, respectively, and $w_{\alpha}$ is the length of the angle bisector of the angle $\angle B A C$.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the given pieces uniquely determine a triangle!

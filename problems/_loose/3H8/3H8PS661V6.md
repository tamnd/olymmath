We denote $\mathrm{Fib}_{k}$ as the $k$-th term of the Fibonacci sequence, defined with $\mathrm{Fib}_{0}=0, \mathrm{Fib}_{1}=$ 1. Show that if $a$ and $b$ are two integers, $a \leqslant b$ and $n$ is such that $F_{n} \leqslant a<F_{n+1}$, then the implementation of the Euclidean algorithm for $a$ and $b$ requires at most $n$ Euclidean divisions.

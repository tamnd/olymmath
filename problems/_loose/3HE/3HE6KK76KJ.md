Let $A, B \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ be two matrices such that $(A-B)^{2}=0_{2}$.

a) Show that: $\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2}-B^{2}\right)=(\operatorname{det} A-\operatorname{det} B)^{2}$.

b) Prove the equivalence: $\operatorname{det}(A B-B A)=0 \Leftrightarrow \operatorname{det} A=\operatorname{det} B$.

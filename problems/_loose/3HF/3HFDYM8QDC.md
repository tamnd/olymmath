Let's prove the following identity:

$$
\frac{A C+B \sqrt{A^{2}+B^{2}-C^{2}}}{A^{2}+B^{2}}=\frac{(B+C)\left(A+\sqrt{A^{2}+B^{2}-C^{2}}\right)}{A^{2}+B C+B^{2}+A \sqrt{A^{2}+B^{2}-C^{2}}}
$$

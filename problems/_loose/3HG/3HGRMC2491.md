Let the set $A=[-2,3] \backslash \mathbb{Z}$ and $\left\{x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}, x_{5}, x_{6}\right\} \subset A$ be any subset of it.

a) Prove that $-12 \leq\left[x_{1}\right]+\left[x_{2}\right]+\left[x_{3}\right]+\left[x_{4}\right]+\left[x_{5}\right]+\left[x_{6}\right] \leq 12$.

b) Show that there exist $i \neq j, i, j \in\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ such that $\left[\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|\right]=0$. We denote $|a|$ as the absolute value of $a$ and $[a]$ as the integer part of $a$.

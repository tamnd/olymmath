In a circle $k$ with center $M$, a trapezoid $A B C D$ with $A B \| C D$ is positioned such that the vertices $A, B, C, D$ lie on the circumference of the circle $k$ and $A B$ is the diameter of $k$. Additionally, $\angle M A C=36^{\circ}$.

Prove that then $\angle C M D=36^{\circ}$!

Let $\Gamma$ be a circle and $[B C]$ a chord of this circle. We denote by $A$ the midpoint of the arc $B C$. We consider two chords of $\Gamma$ passing through $A$, which we denote by $A D$ and $A E$, and $F$ and $G$ the points of intersection of these chords with $B C$. Show that $D, E, F, G$ are concyclic.

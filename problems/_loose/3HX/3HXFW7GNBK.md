In how many ways can the power set of an $n$-element set be divided into two disjoint subsets: the sets $I$ and $H$, such that the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied?

a) for any $a, b \in I$, $a \cup b \in I$ and $a \cap b \in I$

b) for any $a, b \in H$, $a \cup b \in H$ and $a \cap b \in H$;

c) for any $a \in I, b \in H$, $a \cup b \in I$ and $a \cap b \in H$.

(The power set of a set is the set of all its subsets.)

Let the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n+1}$ have a sum of $2 n$ and each term is a positive integer. Prove that there must be positive integers $k$ and $l(1 \leqslant k<l \leqslant n+1)$, such that $\sum_{i=k}^{l} a_{i}=n$.

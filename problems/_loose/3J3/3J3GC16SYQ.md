Inside a square, several points were marked and connected by segments to each other and to the vertices of the square in such a way that the segments did not intersect each other (anywhere except at the ends). As a result, the square was divided into triangles, so that all the marked points ended up at the vertices of the triangles, and none of them fell on the sides of the triangles. For each marked point and for each vertex of the square, the number of segments drawn from it was counted. Could it happen that all these numbers turned out to be even?

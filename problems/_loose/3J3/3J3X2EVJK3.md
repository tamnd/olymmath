Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ be a sequence defined by $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2$ and

$$
a_{n}=a_{n-1}+(n-1) a_{n-2} \text { for } n \geqslant 3
$$

Prove that $a_{2024} \geqslant \sqrt{2024!}$.

Given is an arbitrary convex quadrilateral.

Construct a parallelogram that meets the following conditions!

(1) Each pair of opposite sides of the parallelogram is parallel to a diagonal of the quadrilateral, and each of them is half as long as this.

(2) The vertices of the parallelogram lie on the sides of the quadrilateral.

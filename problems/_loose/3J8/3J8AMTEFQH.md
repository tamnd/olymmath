Theorem B.2 For each surface $S$, there exists an integer $\chi(S)$ such that if a graph $G$ with $n$ vertices and $m$ edges can be embedded into $S$ with $\ell$ faces, each of which is a disk, then
$$
n-m+\ell=\chi(S) .
$$

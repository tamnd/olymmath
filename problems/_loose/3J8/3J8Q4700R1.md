Let the function $f: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ be defined by the formula $f(m)=m+[\sqrt{m}]$. Prove that for every $m \in \mathbb{N}$ there exists $k \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

$$
f^{k}(m)=\underbrace{f(f(\ldots(f}_{k}(m)) \ldots))
$$

is a perfect square.

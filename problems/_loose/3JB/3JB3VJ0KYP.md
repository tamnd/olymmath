Prove that for all positive real numbers $a, b, c$, we have
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a}{b+c}+\frac{b}{c+a}+\frac{c}{a+b}+\sqrt{\frac{a b+b c+c a}{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}} \\
\geqslant \frac{5}{2} .
\end{array}
$$
(2017, Croatian National Team Selection Exam)

Several settlements are connected to the city by roads, but there are no roads between them. A car departs from the city with cargos for all the settlements at once. The cost of each trip is equal to the product of the weight of all cargos in the truck and the distance. Prove that if the weight of each cargo is numerically equal to the distance from the city to the destination, then the total cost of transportation does not depend on the order in which the settlements are visited.

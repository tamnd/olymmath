Adam had a piece of paper that was so large that it could be torn into several tens of thousands of pieces. First, he tore the paper into four pieces. Then he took each of these pieces and tore them either into four or ten pieces. He continued in the same way: each newly created piece he tore either into four or ten smaller pieces.

Decide and explain whether Adam can tear the paper in this way to end up with exactly 20000 pieces.

Let $n \geq 2$ be a positive integer. Suppose there exist non-negative integers ${n_1},{n_2},\ldots,{n_k}$ such that $2^n - 1 \mid \sum_{i = 1}^k {{2^{{n_i}}}}$. Prove that $k \ge n$.

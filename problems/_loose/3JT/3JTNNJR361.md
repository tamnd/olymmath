Vasya, a bibliophile, has 1300 books arranged on $k$ bookshelves in his cabinets. One day, Vasya moved the cabinets to a new place, taking all the books out beforehand. In the process, he completely forgot how the books were arranged before and placed them in the cabinets in a different order. For what maximum $k$ will there definitely be 5 books that were on the same shelf both before and after the rearrangement?

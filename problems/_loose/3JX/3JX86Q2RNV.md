It is assumed that two circles touch each other externally at a point $P$. The line that touches both circles at $P$ is $t$. Furthermore, let $s$ be another common tangent of both circles; it touches them at points $Q$ and $R$. The intersection of $s$ with $t$ is $S$.

Prove that under these assumptions, $S$ is the midpoint of the segment $Q R$!

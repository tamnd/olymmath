Prove that any natural number $n$ can be uniquely represented in the form $n=a_{1} \cdot 1!+a_{2} \cdot 2!+\ldots$ $\ldots+a_{k} \cdot k!$, where $a_{i}$ are integers satisfying the inequalities $0 \leqslant a_{i} \leqslant i$, and $a_{k} \neq 0$.

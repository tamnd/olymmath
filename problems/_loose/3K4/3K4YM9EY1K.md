On the sides $AB, BC, CA$ of triangle $ABC$, points $A_{1}$ and $B_{2}, B_{1}$ and $C_{2}, C_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ are taken such that segments $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}$, and $C_{1} C_{2}$ are parallel to the sides of the triangle and intersect at point $P$. Prove that $P A_{1} \cdot P A_{2} + P B_{1} \cdot P B_{2} + P C_{1} \cdot P C_{2} = R^{2} - O P^{2}$, where $O$ is the center of the circumscribed circle.

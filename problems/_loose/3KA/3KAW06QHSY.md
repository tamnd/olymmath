Consider a hexagon. How many diagonals, i.e., straight lines connecting two non-adjacent vertices, do you think it has?

If this question seems too easy to you, then extend the six sides of the original hexagon and determine how many points, different from the vertices of the hexagon, all these lines (extended sides and diagonals) will intersect?

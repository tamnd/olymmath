Prove that for any 2 positive integers $m$ and $n$ with $m > n$ holds the following inequality
$$
\operatorname{lcm}(m, n) + \operatorname{lcm}(m + 1, n + 1) > \frac{2mn}{\sqrt{m-n}}.
$$

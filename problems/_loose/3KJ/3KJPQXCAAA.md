We call a natural number $p{}$ *simple* if for any natural number $k{}$ such that $2\leqslant k\leqslant \sqrt{p}$ the inequality $\{p/k\}\geqslant 0,01$ holds. Is the set of simple prime numbers finite?

*Proposed by M. Didin*

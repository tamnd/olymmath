At a group party in the pioneer camp, 17 children agree on the following game:

They will count around the circle from 1 to 7, and every seventh child will leave the circle and will no longer be considered in the further counting. The last child remaining loses and has to give a forfeit.

Frank Pfiffig is allowed to suggest at which child the counting should start. He wants to annoy his friend Norbert Nörgel and starts the counting so that Norbert loses.

How can he achieve this?

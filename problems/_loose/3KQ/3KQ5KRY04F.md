Let $A \in \mathcal{M}_2(\mathbb{C})$ and $C(A)=\{B \in \mathcal{M}_2(\mathbb{C}) : AB=BA \}.$ Prove that $$ |\det(A+B)| \ge |\det B|, $$ for any $B \in C(A),$ if and only if $A^2=O_2.$

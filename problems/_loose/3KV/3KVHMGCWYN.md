Let $ C $ denote the complex unit circle centered at the origin.

**a)** Prove that $ \left( |z+1|-\sqrt 2 \right)\cdot \left( |z-1|-\sqrt 2 \right)\le 0, \forall z\in C. $
**b)** Prove that for any twelve numbers from $ C, $ namely $ z_1,\ldots ,z_{12} , $ there exist another twelve numbers $ \varepsilon_1,\ldots ,\varepsilon_{12}\in\{-1,1\} $ such that
$$ \sum_{k=1}^{12} \left| z_k+\varepsilon_k \right| <17. $$

(Rearrangement Inequality) Let $a_{1} \geqslant a_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant a_{n}, b_{1} \geqslant b_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant b_{n}$, and $b_{i_{1}}, b_{i_{2}}, \cdots, b_{i_{n}}$ be any permutation of $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$, then $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k} b_{n+1-k} \leqslant \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k} b_{i_{k}} \leqslant \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k} b_{k}$.

If in $\triangle A B C$, the circumradius of the pedal triangle $\triangle D E F$ is $R^{\prime}$, prove that $R=2 R^{\prime}$.

---

Note: The term "pedal triangle" is used for the triangle formed by the feet of the perpendiculars from a point to the sides of a given triangle. In this context, $\triangle D E F$ is the pedal triangle of $\triangle A B C$.

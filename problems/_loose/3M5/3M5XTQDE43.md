Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2007}$ be a sequence that contains each of the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 2007$ exactly once. The following operation is now performed repeatedly: If the first sequence member is equal to $n$, then the order of the first $n$ sequence members is reversed. Show that the sequence begins with the number 1 after a finite number of such operations.

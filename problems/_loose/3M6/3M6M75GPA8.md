Replacing Lamps. Along the road, there are 9 lamps. If one of them burns out and the adjacent ones are still working, the road service is not concerned. However, if two lamps burn out in a row, the road service immediately replaces all the burnt-out lamps. Each lamp burns out independently of the others.

a) Find the probability that exactly 4 lamps will need to be replaced during the next replacement.

b) Find the expected number of lamps that will need to be replaced during the next replacement.

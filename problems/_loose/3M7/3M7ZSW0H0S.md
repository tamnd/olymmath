We define an addition and a multiplication on the points of the plane as follows: the sum of points $A$ and $B$, denoted $A+B$, is the point that forms an equilateral triangle with $A$ and $B$, oriented in the positive direction. The product $A \times B$ represents the midpoint of the segment connecting points $A$ and $B$. Prove that then

$$
A \times(B+C)=(B+A) \times(A+C) .
$$

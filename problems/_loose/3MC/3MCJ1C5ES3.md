The solution to the inequality

$$
(x-1)^{[\sqrt{1}]}(x-2)^{[\sqrt{2}]} \ldots(x-k)^{[\sqrt{k}]} \ldots(x-150)^{[\sqrt{150}]}<0
$$

is the union of several non-overlapping intervals. Find the sum of their lengths. If necessary, round the answer to 0.01.

Recall that $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

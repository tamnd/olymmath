a) Prove that the product of two consecutive integers can end only in one of the digits $2, 6, 0$.

b) Prove that no permutation of the digits in the number 13579148 can result in a number that is the product of two consecutive integers.

e) Prove that the equation $x^5 - 5x^3 - y^2 + 4x - y + 8 = 0$ has no solutions in integers.

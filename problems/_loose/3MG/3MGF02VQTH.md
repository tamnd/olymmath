There are numbers $1,2, \ldots, 33$ written on the blackboard. In one step we choose a group of numbers on the blackboard (at least two) such that their product is a square, we erase them and write the square root of their product instead. We proceed until no group can be chosen. What is the least amount of numbers left on the blackboard?

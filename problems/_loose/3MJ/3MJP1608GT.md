On the board, several zeros, ones, and twos are written. It is allowed to erase two different digits and write in their place a digit different from the erased ones (for 0 and 1, write 2; for 1 and 2, write 0; for 0 and 2, write 1). Prove that if, as a result of such operations, one number remains on the board, then it does not depend on the order in which the erasures were made.

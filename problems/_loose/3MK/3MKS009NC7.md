Let point $A$ outside plane $M$ draw oblique lines $AB$ and $AC$, such that $\angle BAC = \alpha$. Also, the angles between $AB$ and $AC$ with plane $M$ are $\beta$ and $\gamma$, respectively. Find the angle formed by the projections of $AB$ and $AC$ on plane $M$.
(1962 Fujian Province Competition Problem)

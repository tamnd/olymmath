Construct a triangle $\triangle A B C$ from $c=7.5 \mathrm{~cm} ; a=6.5 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $\alpha+\beta=120^{\circ}$!

Here, $c$ is the length of side $A B, a$ is the length of side $B C, \alpha$ is the measure of angle $\angle B A C$ and $\beta$ is the measure of angle $\angle A B C$.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the given pieces uniquely determine a triangle up to congruence!

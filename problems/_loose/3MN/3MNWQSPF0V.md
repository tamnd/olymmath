Consider the points $A, O, D$, such that $O \in (A D)$ and the adjacent angles $\angle A O B, \angle B O C$, such that points $B, C$ are located on the same side of the line $A D$. If the measure of $\angle A O B$ is half the measure of $\angle C O D$, and the measure of $\angle C O D$ is the arithmetic mean of the measures of the angles $\angle A O B$ and $\angle B O C$, and (OM is the bisector of $\angle A O C$ and $N \in \operatorname{Int}(\angle C O D)$ such that $\angle M O N$ is a right angle, show that:

a) $\angle B O C$ is a right angle;

b) $\angle B O M$ and $\angle N O D$ are congruent.

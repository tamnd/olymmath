Given the vector spaces $V,W$ with coefficients over a field $K$ and function $ \phi :V\to W$ satisfying the relation : $$ \varphi(\lambda x+y)= \lambda \varphi(x)+\phi (y) $$ for all $x,y \in V, \lambda \in K$ . Such a function is called linear.

Let $L\varphi=\{x\in V/\varphi(x)=0\}$ , and $M=\varphi(V)$ , prove that :

(i) $L\varphi$ is subspace of $V$ and $M$ is subspace of $W$ (ii) $L\varphi={O}$ iff $\varphi$ is $1-1$ (iii) Dimension of $V$ equals to dimension of $L\varphi$ plus dimension of $M$ (iv) If $\theta : \mathbb{R}^3\to\mathbb{R}^3$ with $\theta(x,y,z)=(2x-z,x-y,x-3y+z)$ , prove that $\theta$ is linear function . Find $L\theta=\{x\in {R}^3/\theta(x)=0\}$ and dimension of $M=\theta({R}^3)$ .

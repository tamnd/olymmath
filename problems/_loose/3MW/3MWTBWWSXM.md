Given a circle $\odot O$ with radius $r$, and a point $A$ outside the circle. Draw a line $l$ (different from $A O$) through point $A$, intersecting $\odot O$ at points $B$ and $C$, with $B$ between $A$ and $C$. Draw the line $l$'s reflection about $A O$, intersecting $\odot O$ at points $D$ and $E$, with $E$ between $A$ and $D$. Prove that the intersection point of the diagonals of quadrilateral $B C D E$ is a fixed point, i.e., the intersection point does not depend on the position of line $l$.

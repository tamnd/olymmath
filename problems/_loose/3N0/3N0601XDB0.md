It is known that angle PAD is 120 degrees, and angle APD is 40 degrees. Find the degree measure of angle $\mathrm{ABC}$, if it turns out that triangles PAD and BDQ are equal. If there are multiple correct answers, write them separated by a comma or semicolon.

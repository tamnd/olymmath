In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, the ellipse $\Gamma: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$. Let $A$ be one of the endpoints of the major axis of $\Gamma$, $B$ be one of the endpoints of the minor axis of $\Gamma$, and $F$ be one of the foci of $\Gamma$. It is known that there exist two points $P, Q$ on $\Gamma$ that are symmetric with respect to $O$, such that $\overrightarrow{F P} \cdot \overrightarrow{F Q}+\overrightarrow{F A} \cdot \overrightarrow{F B}=|A B|^{2}$.
(1) Prove: The focus $F$ lies on the extension of $A O$;
(2) Find the range of the eccentricity of $\Gamma$.

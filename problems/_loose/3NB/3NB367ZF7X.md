In the chess tournament for the cup of the city of Krasnoyarsk, boys and girls from schools in the city participated. Each player played one game with each other, 1 point was awarded for a win, 0.5 for a draw, and 0 for a loss. At the end of the tournament, it turned out that each participant scored exactly half of their points in games against boys. Prove that the number of participants in the tournament is a square of an integer.

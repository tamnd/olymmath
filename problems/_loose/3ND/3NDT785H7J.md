$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathbf{e}_{1}^{\prime}=\mathbf{e}_{1}+2 \mathbf{e}_{3} \\
& \mathbf{e}_{2}^{\prime}=\mathbf{e}_{2}+\mathbf{e}_{3} \\
& \mathbf{e}_{3}^{\prime}=-\mathbf{e}_{1}-\mathbf{e}_{2}-2 \mathbf{e}_{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

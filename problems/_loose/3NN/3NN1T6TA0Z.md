Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle and let $M,N$ be points lying on the sides $AB$ and $BC$ respectively. Assume that $MC = CD$ and $MD = MN$ , and the points $C,D,M,N$ lie on a circle. if $(AB/BC)^2 = m/n$ where $M$ and $N$ are positive integers with $\gcd(m,n)=1$ , what is the value of $m+n$ ?

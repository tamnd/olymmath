Two identical right-angled triangles were placed together with their equal legs touching. As a result, a larger triangle was formed, in which one angle is the arithmetic mean of the other two angles.

a) Can the length of one side of the larger triangle be the arithmetic mean of the lengths of the other two sides?

b) Can the length of any side of the larger triangle NOT be equal to the arithmetic mean of the lengths of the other two sides?

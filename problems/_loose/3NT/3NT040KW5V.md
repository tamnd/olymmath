Let $x \geqslant y \geqslant 1$, prove:
$$
\frac{x}{\sqrt{x+y}}+\frac{y}{\sqrt{y+1}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}} \geqslant \frac{y}{\sqrt{x+y}}+\frac{x}{\sqrt{x+1}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{y+1}},
$$

and determine the condition for equality.

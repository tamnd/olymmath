Two circles of equal radius $\varrho$ are inscribed in triangle $A B C$ such that they touch each other, and one of them touches sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{A C}$, while the other touches sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{B C}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{2}{|A B|}=\frac{1}{\varrho}-\frac{1}{r}
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$.

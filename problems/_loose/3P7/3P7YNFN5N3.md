Scheherazade tells the Shah mathematical tales. A mathematical tale is an equation of the form $a x+b y=c$, where $a, b, c$ are natural numbers ranging from 1 to 17 (inclusive). A tale is sad if the corresponding equation has no solutions in integers (for example, the tale $2 x+4 y=7$ is sad, while the tale $3 x+5 y=2$ is not: this equation has a solution $x=-1, y=1$). Can Scheherazade invent 1001 different sad tales?

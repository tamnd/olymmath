The lengths of all sides of a right-angled triangle are integers, and the greatest common divisor of these numbers is 1. Prove that its legs are $2 m n$ and $m^{2}-n^{2}$, and the hypotenuse is $m^{2}+n^{2}$, where $m$ and $n$ are natural numbers.

A right-angled triangle, the lengths of whose sides are integers, is called a Pythagorean triangle.

There are 12 teams participating in a single round-robin invitational tournament, where each team plays exactly one match against each of the other 11 teams. In each match, the winning team gets 2 points, the losing team gets 0 points, and in the case of a draw, both teams get 1 point. Which of the following statements about the points scored by the 12 teams is incorrect?
(A) The number of teams with an odd number of points must be even.
(B) The number of teams with an even number of points must be even.
(C) It is impossible for two teams to score 0 points.
(D) The total points scored by all teams is no less than 100 points.
(E) The highest score of any team is no less than 12 points.

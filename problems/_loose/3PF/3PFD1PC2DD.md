Let $A_{n+2}$ be a convex $(n+2)$-gon with $n \geqslant 2$, and suppose that any three diagonals of $A_{n+2}$ do not intersect at a common point inside the polygon. Let $f(n)$ denote the number of non-overlapping regions into which all the diagonals of $A_{n+2}$ divide the polygon. Prove that: $f(n)=\binom{n+2}{4}+\binom{n+1}{2}$.

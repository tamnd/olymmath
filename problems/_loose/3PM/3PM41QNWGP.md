In the convex cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, the intersection point of the diagonals is $M$, and the center of the circumscribed circle $k$ is $O$. The circle with diameter $AC$ intersects the chord $A_1C_1$ at the perpendicular to $AC$ through $M$. The circle with diameter $BD$ intersects the chord $B_1D_1$ at the perpendicular to $BD$ through $M$.

a) Show that the quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1=N_1$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.

b) Let the circle circumscribed around $N_1$ intersect circle $k$ at points $E$ and $F$. Show that $EF$ is perpendicular to $OM$.

c) Provide a spatial interpretation of the statements in a) and b).

Given a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, perpendiculars are dropped from its vertices to the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$, with the feet of these perpendiculars being $A_1, B_1, C_1$, and $D_1$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A_1 B_1 C_1 D_1$ is similar to the original quadrilateral, and furthermore, that the diagonals of the rectangle formed by the perpendiculars, taken as sides, pass through the intersection points of the opposite sides of the original quadrilateral.

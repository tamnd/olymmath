Let $\left(\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}\right)$ and $\left(\alpha_{2}, \beta_{2}, \gamma_{2}\right)$ be the absolute barycentric coordinates of points $M$ and $N$. Prove that

$$
M N^{2}=S_{\mathrm{A}}\left(\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2}\right)^{2}+S_{\mathrm{B}}\left(\beta_{1}-\beta_{2}\right)^{2}+S_{\mathrm{C}}\left(\gamma_{1}-\gamma_{2}\right)^{2}
$$

where $S_{\omega}=2 S \cot \omega$ for any angle $\omega, A, B, C$ - angles of the given triangle, and $S$ - its area.

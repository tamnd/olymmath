Let $n$ be a composite number. For each proper divisor $d$ of $n$ (a proper divisor of a positive integer $n$ is a divisor $d$ such that $d \mid n$ and $1 < d < n$), write the number $d+1$ on the blackboard. Find all positive integers $n$ such that the numbers written on the blackboard are exactly the proper divisors of some positive integer $m$.

$n$ complex numbers $z_{1}\left(\left|z_{1}\right| \neq 1\right), z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}$ form a geometric sequence with a common ratio $q$ $(q \neq \pm 1)$, and the complex numbers $w_{k}(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$ satisfy: $w_{k}=z_{k}+\frac{1}{z_{k}}+h$, where $h$ is a known real number. Try to determine: What kind of figure do the points representing $w_{1}, w_{2}, \cdots, w_{n}$ form on the complex plane? Prove your answer.

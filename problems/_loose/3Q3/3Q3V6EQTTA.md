On the edges $A B, A C$ and $A D$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$, points $K, L$ and $M$ are taken such that $A B=\alpha A K, A C=\beta A L$ and $A D=\gamma A M$.

a) Prove that if $\gamma=\alpha+\beta+1$, then all planes $K L M$ contain a fixed point.

b) Prove that if $\beta=\alpha+1$ and $\gamma=\beta+1$, then all planes $K L M$ contain a fixed line.

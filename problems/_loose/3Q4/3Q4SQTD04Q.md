Given two intersecting circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ on a plane, point $A$ is one of the intersection points. There are two moving points $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$ starting from point $A$, moving at a uniform speed along the circumferences of circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ in the same direction, respectively. After each completes one full revolution, they return to point $A$ simultaneously. Prove that there exists a fixed point $P$ on the plane such that the distances from point $P$ to $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$ are equal at every moment during the entire motion.

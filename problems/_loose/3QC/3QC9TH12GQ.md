One working day, $40\%$ of the students from the first shift in a primary school bought a bagel for lunch, $36\%$ of the students bought a sandwich, and the rest bought a croissant.

In the second shift at the same school:

- the number of students who bought a croissant for lunch is $37.5\%$ higher than the number of students from the same school in the first shift who bought a croissant for lunch;
- the number of students who bought a sandwich for lunch is $75\%$ higher than the number of students from the same school in the first shift who bought a sandwich for lunch; and
- the number of students who bought a bagel for lunch is $75\%$ lower than the number of students from the same school in the first shift who bought a bagel for lunch.

In which shift is the number of students greater than the number of students in the other shift? By what percentage is it greater?

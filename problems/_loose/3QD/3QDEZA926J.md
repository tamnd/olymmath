Let be a natural number $ n\ge 3 $ with the property that $ 1+3n $ is a perfect square. Show that there are three natural numbers $ a,b,c, $ such that the number
$$ 1+\frac{3n+3}{a^2+b^2+c^2} $$
is a perfect square.

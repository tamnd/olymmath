186 In the exercise book, several numbers are written. If the arithmetic mean of two or more of these numbers does not equal any of the numbers, then this mean can be written next to the already written numbers. Prove: Starting from the number 0.1, using this method of writing, you can obtain the numbers:
(1) $\frac{1}{5}$;
(2) any rational number between 0 and 1.

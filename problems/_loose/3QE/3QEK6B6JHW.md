Let $k, n$ and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{k}$ be natural numbers for which

$$
a_{1}\left[\frac{n+1}{2}\right]
$$

Prove that there exist numbers $i$ and $r$ such that $a_{i}+a_{1}=a_{r}$.

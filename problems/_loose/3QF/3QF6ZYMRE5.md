Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers such that the equation

$$
x^{2}+4\left(p-\frac{6 q}{p}\right) x+4 q=0
$$

has no real solutions. Prove that the equation

$$
x^{2}+p x+q=0
$$

has two distinct real solutions.

Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $n (\geqslant 1)$ with real coefficients. For any point on the real number line, if $f(x)$ is a rational number, then color $x$ red; if $f(x)$ is an irrational number, then color $x$ blue. Prove: For any positive real number $\alpha$, there must be two points $P$ and $Q$ of the same color on the $x$-axis such that $|P Q|=\alpha$.

To be proven, that if $n=p^{\alpha}$, where $p$ is a prime number greater than 2 and $\alpha$ is a positive integer, then the difference $x^{2}-1$ is divisible by $n$ only if $x=t n \pm 1$, for any positive or negative integer or zero $t$.

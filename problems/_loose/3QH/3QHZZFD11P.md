Given 30 cards, on ten of which the number a is written, on another ten - the number b, and on the remaining ten - the number c (the numbers $a, b$, c are pairwise distinct). It is known that for any five cards, another five can be chosen such that the sum of the numbers written on the ten cards is 0. Prove that one of the numbers $a, b$, c is equal to 0.

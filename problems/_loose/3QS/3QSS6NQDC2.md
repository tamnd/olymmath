Let $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be arbitrary random variables. Show that the family $\left\{n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \xi_{i}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ is uniformly integrable if at least one of the families $\left\{\xi_{n}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ or $\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \xi_{i} / i\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ is uniformly integrable.

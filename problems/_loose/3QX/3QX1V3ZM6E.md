Let $R=p \cap BC, L=p \cap AC$, $P=q \cap AB, T=q \cap AC, Q=r \cap AB, N=r \cap BC$. If the areas $P_{1}=P_{P Q M}, P_{2}=P_{M R N}$, and $P_{3}=P_{L M T}$ are known, find the area $P$ of triangle $ABC$.

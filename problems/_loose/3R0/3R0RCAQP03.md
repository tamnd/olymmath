Question 172, Given the hyperbola $ C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a, b>0), F_{1}, F_{2} $ are the left and right foci of $ C $, respectively, and $ P $ is a point on the right branch of $ C $ such that $ \angle F_{1} \mathrm{PF}_{2}=\frac{\pi}{3} $, and the area of $ \triangle \mathrm{F}_{1} \mathrm{PF}_{2} $ is $ 3 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{a}^{2} $.
(1) Find the eccentricity $ e $ of $ C $;
(2) Let $ A $ be the left vertex of $ C $, and $ Q $ be any point on $ C $ in the first quadrant. Does there exist a constant $ \lambda (\lambda>0) $ such that $ \angle \mathrm{QF}_{2} \mathrm{~A}=\lambda \angle \mathrm{QAF}_{2} $ always holds? If it exists, find the value of $ \lambda $; if not, explain the reason.

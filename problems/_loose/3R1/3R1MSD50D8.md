Let $A, B, C$ and $D$ be four points lying on the same circle $\Gamma$, such that the chords $(A B)$ and $(C D)$ intersect at a point $E$. Let $P$ be a point on the line $(B E)$. Finally, assume that the tangent at $E$ to the circumcircle of $D E P$ intersects the line $(A C)$ at a point $F$. Prove that $(F E, F C) = (D B, D P)$.

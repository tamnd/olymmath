A herd of 38 elephants will drink all the water from the reservoir in one day, while a herd of 8 elephants will drink all the water from the reservoir in 5 days. If each elephant drinks the same amount of water on average, how many days, starting from today, can one elephant drink all the water from the reservoir? (The elephants start drinking water from the reservoir after it has been replenished with water.)

Let $k$ be the incircle of the isosceles triangle $\triangle ABC$, with center $S$. The circle $k$ touches the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $P$, $Q$, and $R$, respectively. The line $QR$ intersects the line $BC$ at point $M$. Let the circle that contains points $B$ and $C$ be tangent to $k$ at point $N$. The circumcircle of $\triangle MNP$ intersects the line $AP$ at point $L$, different from $P$. Prove that the points $S$, $L$, and $M$ are collinear.

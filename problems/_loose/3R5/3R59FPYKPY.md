For an equilateral triangle $A B C$, a regular hexagon $K L M N O P$ is inscribed such that points $K, L$ lie on side $A B$, points $M, N$ lie on side $B C$, and points $O, P$ lie on side $A C$. Calculate the area of the hexagon $K L M N O P$ if the area of triangle $A B C$ is $60 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$.

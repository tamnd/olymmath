Let $A$ be a set of natural numbers such that all numbers less than 256, as well as all powers of two, belong to the set $A$ (the remaining natural numbers may or may not belong to the set $A$). Now consider the infinite sequence obtained by converting all numbers in the set $A$ to binary notation and writing them one after another in ascending order. Prove that in this infinite sequence there exist 2019 consecutive digits of which exactly 1000 are ones.

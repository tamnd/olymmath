Lines $l$ and $m$ intersect at point $O$. An arbitrary point $A$ is reflected symmetrically with respect to line $l$, resulting in point $A^{\prime}$. Then point $A^{\prime}$ is reflected symmetrically with respect to line $m$, resulting in point $A^{\prime \prime}$. Prove that the angle $A O A^{\prime \prime}$ does not depend on the choice of point $A$ and is equal to twice the angle between lines $l$ and $m$.

Let $n>1$ be a natural number. An equilateral triangle with side length $n$ is divided by lines parallel to its sides into congruent equilateral smaller triangles with side length 1. The number of smaller triangles that have at least one side on the side of the original triangle is 1 less than the number of all other smaller triangles. Determine all such $n$.

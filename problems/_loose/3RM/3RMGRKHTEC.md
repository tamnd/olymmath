In one of the vertices of a hexagon, there is a gold coin, and there is nothing in the others. Koschei the Deathless is pining over the gold and every morning takes an arbitrary number of coins from one vertex, after which he immediately places six times more coins in the adjacent vertex. If by the end of some day all vertices have an equal number of coins, Koschei will become the Master of the World. Prove that even though he has as much gold as he wants, he will never become the Master of the World.

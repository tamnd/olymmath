The incircle of an isosceles $\triangle A B C$ touches the legs $A C$ and $B C$ at points $M$ and $N$ respectively. A tangent $t$ is drawn to the smaller of the arcs $\widehat{M N}$ and let $t$ intersects $N C$ and $M C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Let $T$ be the intersecting point of the lines $A P$ and $B Q$.

a) Prove that $T$ lies on the segment $M N$;

b) Prove that the sum of the areas of triangles $A T Q$ and $B T P$ is the smallest possible when $t$ is parallel to $A B$.

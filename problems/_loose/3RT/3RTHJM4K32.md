Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \cdots$ be a sequence of positive numbers. Prove that there exist infinitely many $n \in \mathbf{N}$ such that
$$
\frac{a_{1}+a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}>1+\frac{1}{n} \text {. }
$$

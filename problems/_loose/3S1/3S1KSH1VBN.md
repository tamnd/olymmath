Two chips, a black one and a white one, are placed on two squares of a chessboard. In one move, you can move either of them to an adjacent square vertically or horizontally (the two chips cannot occupy the same square). Can all possible arrangements of these two chips be encountered as a result of such moves, and exactly once each?

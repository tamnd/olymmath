Let $1,2,3, \cdots, 4 n(n \in \mathrm{N})$. If they are arranged arbitrarily on the circumference of a circle, then there exist $2 n$ non-intersecting chords of the circle, each with endpoints corresponding to two of these $4 n$ numbers, such that the absolute difference between the two numbers at the endpoints of each chord does not exceed $3 n-1$.

Let $\triangle A B C$ be a right triangle; $C$ be the vertex of the right angle. The angle bisector of this angle intersects the side $A B$ at $D$. The foot of the perpendicular from $D$ to $A C$ is $E$.

Prove the following statement:

If $\angle C A B=22.5^{\circ}$, then $\angle A D E=\angle C D B$!

How many 9-term sequences $a_1, \ldots, a_9$ of nonnegative integers are there such that
1. $0 \leq a_i < i$ for all $i = 1, \ldots, 9$ ; and
2. there are no ordered triples $(i, j, k)$ with $1 \leq i < j < k \leq 9$ , such that $a_i, a_j, a_k$ are all distinct?

Let $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ be two circles tangent at a point $A$ with $\omega_{2}$ inside $\omega_{1}$. Let $B \in \omega_{2}$ different from $A$. The tangent to $\omega_{2}$ at $B$ intersects $\omega_{1}$ at $X$ and $Y$.

Show that $\widehat{B A X}=\widehat{B A Y}$.

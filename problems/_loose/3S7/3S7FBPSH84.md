In the right-angled triangle $ABC$, the right angle is at vertex $C$. Let $D$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to the hypotenuse $AB$. The feet of the perpendiculars from $D$ to the legs are denoted by $P$ and $Q$. Prove that the sum of the segments $DP$ and $DQ$ is at most the harmonic mean of the legs $a$ and $b$. The harmonic mean of two numbers $a$ and $b$ is the reciprocal of the average of their reciprocals, i.e., $\frac{2ab}{a+b}$.

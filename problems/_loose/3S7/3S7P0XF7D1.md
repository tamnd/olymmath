In an equilateral $\triangle A B E$, vertex $E$ is inside the square $A B C D$, and $F$ is the intersection of diagonal $B D$ and segment $A E$. If the length of $A B$ is $\sqrt{1+\sqrt{3}}$, then the area of $\triangle A B F$ is
(A) 1 .
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
(D) $4-2 \sqrt{3}$.
(E) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$.
(29th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1978)

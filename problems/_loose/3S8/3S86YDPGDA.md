Let $p=4 n+1$ be a prime, $p=x^{2}+y^{2}(x, y \in$ $\left.\mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$. Then there exists a geometric sequence of $2 n$ positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}$, $\cdots, a_{2 n}$, such that the $4 n$ positive integers
$$
x^{2} a_{1}, x^{2} a_{2}, \cdots, x^{2} a_{2 n}, y^{2} a_{1}, y^{2} a_{2}, \cdots, y^{2} a_{2 n}
$$

form a reduced residue system modulo $p$.

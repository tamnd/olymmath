Let's prove that the volume of a right-angled triangular pyramid is

$$
K=\frac{1}{6} \sqrt{\left(S^{2}-a^{2}\right)\left(S^{2}-b^{2}\right)\left(S^{2}-c^{2}\right)}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the base edges, and $S^{2}=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{2}$.

(This is a spatial generalization of Heron's formula. - The side edges of a right-angled triangular pyramid are mutually perpendicular to each other.)

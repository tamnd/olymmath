A positive integer $n$ is *brgorable* if it is possible to arrange the numbers $1, 1, 2, 2, ..., n, n$ such that between any two $k$ 's there are exactly $k$ numbers (for example, $n=2$ is not brgorable, but $n = 3$ is as demonstrated by $3, 1, 2, 1, 3, 2$ ). How many brgorable numbers are less than 2019?

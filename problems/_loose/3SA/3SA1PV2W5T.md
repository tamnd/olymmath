$$
x^{2} + p x + q = 0
$$

then

$$
x_{1}^{2} + x_{2}^{2} = p^{2} - 2 q, x_{1}^{2} - x_{2}^{2} = \pm p \sqrt{p^{2} - 4 q}
$$

In triangle $ABC$, two angles are $\angle BAC = 15^\circ$ and $\angle ABC = 30^\circ$. The perpendicular from point $C$ to side $AC$ intersects side $AB$ at point $D$. The perpendicular bisector of segment $AB$ intersects line $CD$ at point $E$. Extend segment $AB$ beyond point $B$ by the length of segment $BC$, and let the resulting point be $G$. Prove that points $B, G, E, C$ lie on a circle with diameter $\sqrt{2} \cdot AB$.

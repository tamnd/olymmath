In trapezoid $A B C D$, $A D / / B C$. If $\angle B=40^{\circ}, \angle C=50^{\circ}$, and $E, M, F, N$ are the midpoints of $A B, B C, C D, D A$ respectively, and $E F=a, M N=b$, then $B C$ is
(A) $a+b$.
(B) $a+2 b$.
(C) $2 a+b$.
(D) $2 a-b$.
(2nd "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1985)

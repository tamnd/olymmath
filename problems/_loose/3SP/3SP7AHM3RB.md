The numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{100}$ are such that:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{1}-2 a_{2}+a_{3} \leqslant 0 \\
& a_{2}-2 a_{3}+a_{4} \leqslant 0 \\
& \cdots \cdots \\
& a_{98}-2 a_{99}+a_{100} \leqslant 0
\end{aligned}
$$

and at the same time $a_{1}=a_{100} \geqslant 0$. Prove that all these numbers are non-negative.

All edges of the regular quadrilateral pyramid $S A B C D$ are equal to $b$. The height of the regular quadrilateral prism $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ is equal to the height of the specified pyramid. The base $A B C D$ is common to both. (1) A plane $\alpha$ is drawn through points $A$ and $C_{1}$ parallel to the line $B D$. Find the area of the polygon formed by the intersection of plane $\alpha$ and the pyramid $S A B C D$.

(2) A plane $\beta$ is drawn through points $B$ and $D_{1}$. Find the minimum possible area of the polygon formed by the intersection of plane $\beta$ and the pyramid $S A B C D$.

- $b^{2} \sqrt{5} / 6$. This value is also equal to the smallest area of the polygon specified in the problem conditions.

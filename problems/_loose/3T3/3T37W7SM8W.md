(This sub-question is worth 16 points) Let the function $f(x)=e^{x}-1-x$.
(1) Find the maximum value $b_{n}$ of $f(x)$ in the interval $\left[0, \frac{1}{n}\right]$ (where $n$ is a positive integer);
(2) Let $a_{n}=e^{\frac{1}{n}}-1-b_{n}, p_{k}=\frac{a_{2} a_{4} \cdots a_{2 k}}{a_{1} a_{3} \cdots a_{2 k-1}} $ (where $n, k$ are positive integers), prove: $p_{1}+p_{2}+\cdots p_{n}<\sqrt{\frac{2}{a_{n}}+1}-1$.

Let $a_{i} \in \mathrm{R}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, if the sum of any two of them is non-negative, then for any non-negative real numbers $x_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$, the inequality $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} x_{i} \geqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} x_{i}^{2}$ holds.

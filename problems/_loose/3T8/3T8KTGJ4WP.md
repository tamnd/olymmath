Let $\left(A_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ and $\left(B_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ be two sequences of events, such that

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(\overline{\lim } A_{n}\right)=1, \mathrm{P}\left(\overline{\lim } \bar{B}_{n}\right)=0
$$

Show that $\mathrm{P}\left(\overline{\lim } A_{n} B_{n}\right)=1$.

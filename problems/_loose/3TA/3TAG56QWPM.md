Given that the two roots of the equation $x^{2}+p x+1=0$ are the lengths of two sides of a triangle, and the third side is 2. Then the range of the real number $p$ is
(A) $p<-2$.
(B) $p \leqslant-2$ or $p \geqslant 2$.
(C) $-2 \sqrt{2}<p<2 \sqrt{2}$.
(D) $-2 \sqrt{2}<p<-2$.
(China Sichuan Province High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

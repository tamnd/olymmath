The sequence of real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ is such that for any $m$ and $n$, the inequality

$$
\left|a_{m}+a_{n}-a_{m+n}\right| \leqslant \frac{1}{m+n}
$$

holds. Prove that this sequence is an arithmetic progression.

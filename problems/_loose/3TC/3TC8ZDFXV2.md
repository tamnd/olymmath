We can multiply twelve numbers by first multiplying two numbers, then multiplying the product with the rest one by one; but we can also multiply them by first grouping the numbers into smaller groups, multiplying the numbers within each group, and then multiplying these products together until we get the final result.

Can the factors be grouped in such a way that fewer than 11 multiplications are required? If it is possible, how do we achieve the goal with the least number of multiplications; if not, why not?

A circle with center at point $I$ is inscribed in quadrilateral $A B C D$. Rays $B A$ and $C D$ intersect at point $P$, and rays $A D$ and $B C$ intersect at point $Q$. It is known that point $P$ lies on the circumcircle $\omega$ of triangle $A I C$. Prove that point $Q$ also lies on the circumcircle $\omega$.

(A. Kuznetsov)

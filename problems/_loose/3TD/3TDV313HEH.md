The foci of an ellipse are $F$ and $G$. A line $h$ intersects the ellipse at points $P_{1}, P_{2}$. We draw a circle around $F$ with a radius equal to the length of the ellipse's major axis. We draw the tangents to the circle at the intersection points of the $F P_{1}$ and $F P_{2}$ half-lines. The intersection point of the two tangents is $M$. Prove that $M G$ is perpendicular to $h$.

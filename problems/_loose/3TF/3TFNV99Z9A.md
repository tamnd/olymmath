Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer, and let $p$ be a prime number that is congruent to $7$ modulo $8$. Prove that
$$\sum_{k=1}^{p} \left\{ \frac{k^{2n}}{p} - \frac{1}{2} \right\} = \frac{p-1}{2}$$

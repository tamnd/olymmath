Let $AB$ be a chord of $\odot O$, and $CD$ be a diameter of $\odot O$ that intersects the chord $AB$. Let $M=|S_{\triangle CAB}-S_{\triangle DAB}|, N=2S_{\triangle OAB}$, then
(A) $M>N$.
(B) $M=N$.
(C) $M<N$.
(D) The relationship between $M$ and $N$ is uncertain.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1995)

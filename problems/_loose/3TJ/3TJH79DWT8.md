We will prove that the value of the function

$$
S_{n}(x)=\frac{1}{x^{2}-2 x+3}+\frac{1}{\left(x^{2}-2 x+3\right)^{2}}+\frac{x^{2}}{\left(x^{2}-2 x+3\right)^{3}}+\ldots+\frac{x^{n-1}}{\left(x^{2}-2 x+3\right)^{n}}
$$

is less than $4 / 3$ for all real $x$. ( $n$ is a natural number.)

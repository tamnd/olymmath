Let $P$ be any point inside $\triangle ABC$, and let the distances from $P$ to the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ be $d_{1}$, $d_{2}$, and $d_{3}$, respectively. Denote $BC=a$, $CA=b$, and $AB=c$. Prove that:
$$
\frac{a}{d_{1}}+\frac{b}{d_{2}}+\frac{c}{d_{3}} \geqslant \frac{(a+b+c)^{2}}{2 S_{\triangle ABC}} \text {. }
$$

(26th USAMO Problem) Let nonnegative integers $f(1), f(2), \cdots, f(1997)$ satisfy $f(i) +$ $f(j) \leqslant f(i+j) \leqslant f(i) + f(j) + 1 (1 \leqslant i, j, i+j \leqslant 1997)$. Prove that there exists a real number $x$, such that for all $1 \leqslant$ $n \leqslant 1997$, $f(n) = [n x]$.

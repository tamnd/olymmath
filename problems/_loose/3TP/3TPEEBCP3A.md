In a tetrahedron, assuming the same as in Example 7, let $\omega=\frac{1}{2}(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)$, then the volume $V$ of the tetrahedron is $V=\frac{a b c}{3} \sqrt{\sin \omega \cdot \sin (\omega-\alpha) \cdot \sin (\omega-\beta) \cdot \sin (\omega-\gamma)}$.

In the plane, a circle $k$ is given. For each $i, 1 \leqslant i \leqslant 121$, the line $p_{i}$ intersects $k$ at two points, and a point $A_{i}$ is given on the chord determined by these two points.

Prove that there exists a point $X$ on $k$ such that for at least 29 indices $i, 1 \leqslant i \leqslant 121$, the angle between the lines $A_{i} X$ and $p_{i}$ is less than or equal to $21^{\circ}$.

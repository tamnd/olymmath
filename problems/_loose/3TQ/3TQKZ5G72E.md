Given that $\odot O$ is the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, and a circle $\Gamma$ with center $A$ intersects the line segment $B C$ at points $D$ and $E$, such that points $B, D, E, C$ are distinct and arranged in this order on the line $B C$. Let $F$ and $G$ be the two intersection points of $\odot O$ and circle $\Gamma$, such that points $A, F, B, C, G$ are arranged in this order on $\odot O$. Let $K$ be the other intersection point of the circumcircle of $\triangle B D F$ with the line segment $A B$, and $L$ be the other intersection point of the circumcircle of $\triangle C G E$ with the line segment $C A$. If lines $F K$ and $G L$ are distinct and intersect at point $X$, prove: point $X$ lies on the line $A O$.

Given concentric circles $\omega$ and $\gamma$, with $\gamma$ lying inside $\omega$. On the circle $\omega$, an arbitrary point $O$ is chosen, from which tangents $OA$ and $OB$ are drawn to the circle $\gamma$. The circle with center at point $O$ and radius $OA$ intersects the circle $\omega$ at points $C$ and $D$. Prove that the line $CD$ contains the midline of triangle $OAB$.

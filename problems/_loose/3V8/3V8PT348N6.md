Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{1}=2 t-2(t \in \mathbf{R}$ and $t \neq \pm 1)$,
$$
a_{n+1}=\frac{2\left(t^{n+1}-1\right) a_{n}}{a_{n}+2 t^{n}-2}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right) \text {. }
$$
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) If $t>0$, compare the size of $a_{n+1}$ and $a_{n}$.

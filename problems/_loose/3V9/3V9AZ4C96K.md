Let's prove that if $a, b, x, y$ are integers and $a > b^{2}$, and

$$
a^{2} x^{2} + 2 a b x y + \left(b^{2} + 1\right) y^{2} < b^{2} + 1
$$

holds, then $x = y = 0$.

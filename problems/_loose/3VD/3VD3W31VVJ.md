Let $\mathcal{F}$ be the set of elements of $(\mathbb{Z} / p \mathbb{Z})[X]$, considered modulo $H(X)$. Show that $\mathcal{F}$, equipped with the usual laws + and $\times$, is a commutative field.

Since the elements of $\mathcal{F}$ are congruence classes of polynomials in the variable $X$, we will restrict ourselves below, whenever we consider polynomials with coefficients in $\mathcal{F}$, to taking polynomials in the variable $T$.

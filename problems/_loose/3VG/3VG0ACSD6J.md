Let $ a,\ b$ be positive real numbers.

Prove that

$ \int_{a - 2b}^{2a - b} \left|\sqrt {3b(2a - b) + 2(a - 2b)x - x^2} - \sqrt {3a(2b - a) + 2(2a - b)x - x^2}\right|dx$

$ \leq \frac {\pi}3 (a^2 + b^2).$

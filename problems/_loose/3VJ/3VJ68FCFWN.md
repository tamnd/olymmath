A positive integer $a$ whose decimal representation has 2006 digits is called "bad" if 3 does not divide any integer formed by three consecutive digits of $a$.

a) Find the number of all bad integers whose digits are equal to 1,2 or 3 .

b) Let $a$ and $b$ be different bad integers such that $a+b$ is also a bad integer. Denote by $k$ the number of positions, where the digits of $a$ and $b$ coincide. Find all possible values of $k$.

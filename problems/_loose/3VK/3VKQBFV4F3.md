Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $\gamma=\angle A C B$ such that $\gamma / 2<\angle B A C$ and $\gamma / 2<\angle C B A$ holds. Let $D$ be the point on the line $B C$ so that $\angle B A D=\gamma / 2$. Let $E$ be the point on the line $C A$ such that $\angle E B A=\gamma / 2$. Furthermore, let $F$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\angle A C B$ and the line $A B$. Show that $E F+F D=A B$ holds.

Random walk of a point. A point exits from the origin on a line and makes $a$ steps one unit to the right, $b$ steps one unit to the left in some order, where $a>b$. The range of the point's walk is defined as the difference between the greatest and the smallest coordinates of the point over the entire walk.

a) Find the greatest possible range of the walk.

b) Find the smallest possible range of the walk.

c) How many different sequences of movements of the point exist such that the range of the walk is the greatest possible?

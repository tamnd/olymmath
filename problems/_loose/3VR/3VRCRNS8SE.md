In the concentric circles $O_{1}, O$, the radius of the larger circle is twice that of the smaller circle. The sides of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ inscribed in the smaller circle $O$ are extended to intersect the larger circle $O_{1}$ at $B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}, A_{1}$, respectively. Then $A_{1} B_{1}+B_{1} C_{1}+C_{1} D_{1}+D_{1} A_{1} \geqslant 2(A B+C B+C D+D A)$.

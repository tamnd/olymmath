Inside a triangle, several points were marked. The points were connected by colored segments such that three segments meet at each marked point, and each vertex of the triangle is connected by one colored segment to one of the marked points. It turned out that the drawn segments are colored in three colors in such a way that three segments of different colors meet at each marked point. Prove that the segments leading to the vertices of the triangle are colored in different colors.

At this year's JMMO, some of the students know each other (acquaintance is a symmetric relation), but there are also students who do not know each other. For any arrangement of students in two rooms, there are always acquaintances placed in different rooms. Let $A$ be an arbitrary competitor. Prove that there exist competitors $B$ and $C$ such that in the triplet $\{A, B, C\}$ there are exactly two acquaintances.

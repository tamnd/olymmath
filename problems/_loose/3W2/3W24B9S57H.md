A sphere $G$ has a center $O$ through which three lines pass, each perpendicular to the other two, intersecting $G$ to form the diameters $X X^{\prime}, Y Y^{\prime}$, and $Z Z^{\prime}$. We divide a half-great circle of $G$, whose diameter is $X X^{\prime}$, into 15 equal arcs, and take the planes passing through the division points, perpendicular to $X X^{\prime}$, and consider the circles cut out by these planes from the surface of $G$. We proceed similarly starting from the diameters $Y Y^{\prime}$ and $Z Z^{\prime}$.

How many points are there on the surface of $G$ where 3 of the considered circles intersect? How many of the considered circles do not have such a triple intersection point?

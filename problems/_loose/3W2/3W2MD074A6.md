Let $G$ be a graph with $n$ vertices such that $G$ does not contain a triangle (cycle of length 3). Prove that $G$ has at most $\left\lfloor n^{2} / 4\right\rfloor$ edges. Show that this result is optimal.

Bonus: Show that in a graph with $k$ edges, there are at most $\left\lfloor\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} k^{3 / 2}\right\rfloor$ triangles.

Find all values of the angle $\alpha$ from the interval $\left[0^{\circ} ; 360^{\circ}\right]$ such that the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
y^{3} \geqslant 3 x^{2} y \\
(x-\cos \alpha)^{2}+(y-\sin \alpha)^{2}=\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{4}
\end{array}\right.
$$

has a unique solution.

(R. Alishev)

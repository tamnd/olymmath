A snail was crawling at a non-constant speed. Over 6 minutes, several people observed it such that at no moment was it left unobserved. Each person observed for exactly one minute and noted that during that minute, the snail crawled exactly 1 m. Prove that over the entire 6 minutes, the snail could have crawled at most $10 \mathrm{~m}$.

Gabriele, the lover of cubes, has bought a magnificent collector's item: a cube entirely made of chocolate, with edges 10 cm long. Unfortunately, having lost a bet with two of his friends, he will have to give away two-thirds of the volume of the chocolate block. Gabriele has decided to take as his portion of chocolate a smaller cube, one of whose vertices coincides with one of the vertices of the chocolate cube he bought and whose faces are parallel to those of the cube he bought. In the end, he gives the remaining chocolate to his two friends. Let $S$ be the total surface area of the chocolate block given away, expressed in $\mathrm{cm}^{2}$. Then we have...
(A) $300 \leq S \leq 350$
(B) $350<S \leq 450$
(C) $450<S \leq 550$
(D) $550<S \leq 650$
(E) $650<S \leq 750$.

In the tetrahedron $ABCD$ (a pyramid with a triangular base), the edge $DA$ is perpendicular to the base $ABC$. We also know the angles between the edges meeting at vertex $D$: $\angle ADB = 30^\circ$, $\angle BDC = 45^\circ$, $\angle CDA = 60^\circ$. Construct (using the usual steps on the plane) the angle $\angle BAC$.

For students in the second grade, also calculate the angle $\angle BAC$.

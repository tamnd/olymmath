At the base of the heptagonal pyramid $S A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{7}$ lies a convex heptagon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{7}$. It is known that the projections of the vertices $A_{2}, A_{4}, A_{6}$ onto the line $S A_{1}$ fall into one point. Prove that the projections of the vertices $A_{3}, A_{5}, A_{7}$ also fall into one point.

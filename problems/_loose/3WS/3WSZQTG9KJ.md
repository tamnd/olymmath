(Academic Olympiads 2005). We have 100 cards. On each card, two consecutive integers are written, such that each of the integers $1, 2, \ldots, 200$ is written on one of the cards.
a) Alice randomly chose 21 cards. She adds up all the integers written on her cards and tells Bob that the sum is 2004. Prove that Alice made a mistake in her calculation.

b) Alice recounts and this time announces 2005. Prove that she made a mistake in her calculation again. c) In fact, Alice's true total is 2003. Meanwhile, Bob randomly chose 20 cards from the remaining ones. He adds up the numbers written on his cards and tells Alice that the sum is 1396. Prove that Bob made a mistake in his calculation.

Let's prove that

$$
\frac{r_{a} r_{b} r_{c}}{r}=p^{2}
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$, $r_{a}, r_{b}$, and $r_{c}$ are the radii of the excircles opposite to vertices $A$, $B$, and $C$ respectively, and $p$ is the semiperimeter of the triangle.

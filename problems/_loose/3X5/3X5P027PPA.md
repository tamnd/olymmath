Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2$ and $\frac{a_{n+1}^{4}}{a_{n}^{3}}=2 a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}$. Prove that for every natural number $N>1$ we have

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{N} \frac{a_{k}^{2}}{a_{k+1}}<3
$$

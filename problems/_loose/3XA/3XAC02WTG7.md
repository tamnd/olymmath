In a math club, 24 schoolchildren are participating. Each team of 6 schoolchildren is considered either played or not played by the leader. For a math battle tournament, the leader plans to divide the children into 4 teams of 6. Can it happen that in any division of the schoolchildren into 4 teams, either exactly three teams or exactly one team are played, and both scenarios are present?

Move everything to the right side

$(x+c)(x+d)-(2 x+c+d)=0$,

$x^{2}+c x+d x+c d-2 x-c-d=0$,

$x^{2}+(c+d-2) x+c d-c-d=0$.

Find the discriminant of the quadratic equation

$D=(c+d-2)^{2}-4(cd-c-d)=c^{2}+d^{2}+4+2cd-4c-4d-4cd+4c+4d=c^{2}+d^{2}-2cd+4=(c-d)^{2}+4>0$ Therefore, the equation has two distinct roots.

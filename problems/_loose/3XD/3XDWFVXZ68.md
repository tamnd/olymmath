(i) Given $\epsilon>0$ and $m \geqslant 1$, assume the regularity lemma holds for all graphs of order at least some integer $n=n(\epsilon, m)$, prove that the regularity lemma holds for all graphs.
(ii) Prove that the regularity lemma holds for sparse graphs, where a sparse graph is a graph $G_{n}$ of order $n$ such that for every sequence $\left(G_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$, as $n \rightarrow \infty$, we have $\left\|G_{n}\right\| / n^{2} \rightarrow 0$.

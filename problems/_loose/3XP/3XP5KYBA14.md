Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Pedestrian Petya starts from vertex $A$, walks along side $A B$ and continues along the perimeter of the quadrilateral. Pedestrian Vasya starts from vertex $A$ simultaneously with Petya, walks along diagonal $A C$ and arrives at $C$ simultaneously with Petya. Pedestrian Tolya starts from vertex $B$ at the moment Petya passes it, walks along diagonal $B D$ and arrives at $D$ simultaneously with Petya. The speeds of the pedestrians are constant. Could Vasya and Tolya have arrived at the intersection point of the diagonals $O$ simultaneously?

Determine whether the operation *, which associates with any two natural numbers $X$ and $Y$ a natural number $X * Y$, can simultaneously possess the following three properties:

a) $A * B=|A-B| *(A+B)$ when $A \neq B$;

b) $(A C) *(B C)=(A * B)(C * C)$;

c) $(2 k+1) *(2 k+1)=2 k+1$.

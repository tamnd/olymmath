Let $k$ integers
$$
1<a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{k} \leqslant n
$$

be such that one of them divides the product of the others. Prove: $k \leqslant \pi(n)$, where $\pi(n)$ denotes the number of primes not exceeding $n$.

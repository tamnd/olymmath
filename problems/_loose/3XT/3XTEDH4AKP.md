If on each side of a triangle we take two points which are at equal distances from the midpoint of the respective side, and if three of the six points thus obtained lie on a straight line, then the other three will also lie on a straight line. If one of these lines is tangent to one of the circles inscribed in the triangle, whose center is $M$, then the other line will pass through a point $K$. The centroid $S$ of the triangle lies on the line $M K$ and $M S: S K=1: 2$.

Given an acute triangle $ABC$ such that $|AC| \neq|BC|$. Inside its sides $BC$ and $AC$, consider points $D$ and $E$, for which $ABDE$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. The intersection of its diagonals $AD$ and $BE$ is denoted by $P$. If the lines $CP$ and $AB$ are perpendicular to each other, then $P$ is the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$. Prove it.

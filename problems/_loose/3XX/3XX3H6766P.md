Let $p$ be a fixed prime number. Find all integers $n \geqslant 1$ satisfying the following property: The positive divisors of $n$ can be paired in such a way that for each pair $\left(d, d^{\prime}\right)$ thus formed, the two conditions below are satisfied:

- $d<d^{\prime} ;$
- $p$ divides $\left\lfloor\frac{d^{\prime}}{d}\right\rfloor$.

Note: Recall that for any real number $x$, the notation $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. For example, $\lfloor 3\rfloor=\lfloor\pi\rfloor=\lfloor 3.99\rfloor=3$.

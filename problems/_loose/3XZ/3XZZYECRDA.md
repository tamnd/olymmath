Let $\Omega$ be a circle with center $O$ and $P$ and $Q$ be two points outside this circle. We denote $t_{1}, t_{2}$, $t_{3}$, and $t_{4}$ as the tangents from $P$ and then $Q$ to the circle $\Omega$. We denote $S$ as the intersection of $t_{1}$ and $t_{3}$, and $T$ as the intersection of $t_{2}$ and $t_{4}$. Furthermore, we denote $X$ as the orthogonal projection of $O$ onto the line $(P Q)$. Show that the line $(P Q)$ bisects the angle $\angle S X T$.

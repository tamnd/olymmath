Prove that for $n, p$ integers, $n \geq 4$ and $p \geq 4$ , the proposition $\mathcal{P}(n, p)$
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{{x_{i}}^{p}}\geq \sum_{i=1}^{n}{x_{i}}^{p} \textrm{for} x_{i}\in \mathbb{R}, x_{i}> 0 , i=1,\ldots,n \ , \sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}= n,$$ is false.

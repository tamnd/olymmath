Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively, and the sum of their radii is less than the distance $O_{1} O_{2}$. Let $K$ and $L$ be the points of tangency of an external common tangent to $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$, respectively. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of tangency to $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$, respectively, of an internal common tangent. Show that the lines $(K M),(N L)$ and $\left(O_{1} O_{2}\right)$ are concurrent.

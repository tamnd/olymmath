The vertices of the convex hexagon $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4} A_{5} A_{6}$ are the endpoints of the diameters $A_{1} A_{4}, A_{2} A_{5}, A_{3} A_{6}$ of the circle $k$. The point $P$ lies on $k$ and does not coincide with any vertex of the hexagon. Let $Q_{1}, Q_{2}, Q_{3}, Q_{4}, Q_{5}, Q_{6}$ be the orthogonal projections of the point $P$ onto the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, A_{4} A_{4}, A_{4} A_{5}$, $A_{5} A_{6}, A_{6} A_{1}$, respectively.

a) Prove that the lines determined by any two adjacent sides of the hexagon $Q_{1} Q_{2} Q_{3} Q_{4} Q_{5} Q_{6}$ form a right angle.

b) Prove that the point $P$, the center $O$ of $k$, and the midpoints $R_{1}, R_{2}, R_{3}$ of the segments $Q_{1} Q_{4}, Q_{2} Q_{5}, Q_{3} Q_{6}$ are concyclic.

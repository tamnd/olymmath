Let $\xi \sim \mathscr{N}(0,1)$. Show that

$$
\mathrm{E}|\xi|^{\alpha}=\frac{2^{\alpha / 2}}{\sqrt{\pi}} \Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha+1}{2}\right), \alpha>-1
$$

where $\Gamma(s)=\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}} x^{s-1} e^{-t} d t$ is the Euler gamma function. In particular,

$$
\mathrm{E} \xi^{2 n}=(2 n-1)!!=1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot \ldots \cdot(2 n-1)
$$

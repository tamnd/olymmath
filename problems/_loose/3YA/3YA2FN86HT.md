Given a circle and a tangent line $l$ to it. Let $N$ be the point of tangency, $NM$ be a diameter. A fixed point $A$ is taken on the line $NM$. Consider an arbitrary circle passing through $A$, with its center on $l$. Let $C$ and $D$ be the points of intersection of this circle with $l$, and $P$ and $Q$ be the points of intersection of the lines $MC$ and $MD$ with the given circle. Prove that the chord $PQ$ passes through a fixed point on the plane.

In Koschei's prison, there are five cells, numbered from 1 to 5. Each cell contains one prisoner. Vasilisa has persuaded Koschei to conduct an experiment: she will write a number on the wall of each cell once, and at midnight, each prisoner will move to the cell with the written number (if the number on the wall matches the cell number, the prisoner will not move). The next midnight, the prisoners will again move from cell to cell according to the numbers on the walls, and this will continue for five nights. If the arrangement of prisoners in the cells does not repeat at any point over the six days (including the first), Vasilisa will be given the title of Wise, and the prisoners will be released. Help Vasilisa write the numbers on the walls of the cells.

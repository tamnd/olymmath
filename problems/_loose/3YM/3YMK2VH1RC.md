In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle A \geqslant \angle B \geqslant \angle C$, $AD$ is the altitude on side $BC$, $BE$ is the median on side $AC$, and $CF$ is the angle bisector of $\angle C$. The three lines intersect each other at $P$, $Q$, and $R$. $D$ is on side $BC$, and can coincide with $B$ or $C$. Let $x=\frac{S_{\triangle PQR}}{S_{\triangle ABC}}$, prove that $x<1/6$, and $x$ can be infinitely close to $1/6$.

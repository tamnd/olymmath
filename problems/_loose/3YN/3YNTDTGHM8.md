Consider the functions $f, g: R \rightarrow R, f(x)=a x+b, g(x)=b x+a$, with $a, b \in N, a>b \geq 1$ In the coordinate system $x O y$ let point $A$ be the intersection of the graphs of the two functions. If the graph of the function $f$ intersects the $O y$ axis at point $B$, and the graph of the function $g$ at point $C$, show that triangle $A B C$ is not a right triangle and calculate the area of the triangle.

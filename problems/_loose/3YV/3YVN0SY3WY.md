Let $\triangle A B C$ have internal angles $A, B, C$ with opposite sides $a, b, c$ respectively. Let vectors $\boldsymbol{m}=(\sin A, b+c)$ and $\boldsymbol{n}=(\sin C-\sin B, a-b)$, and there exists a real number $\lambda$ such that $\boldsymbol{m}=\lambda \boldsymbol{n}$.
(1) Find the size of angle $C$;
(2) If $a+b=k c$, find the range of the real number $k$.

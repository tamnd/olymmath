Given $f(x)=\frac{a x^{2}+1}{b x+c}(a, b, c \in \mathbf{Z})$ is an odd function, and $f(1)=2, f(2)<3$.
(1) Find the values of $a, b, c$; (2) Determine the monotonicity of the function $y=f^{2}(x)$ in the interval $(0,1)$, and prove your conclusion.

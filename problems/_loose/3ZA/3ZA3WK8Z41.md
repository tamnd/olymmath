Let $\mathbb{R}_+$ be the set of positive real numbers. Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}_+ \to \mathbb{R}_+$ such that
$$f(xy + x + y) + f \left( \frac1x \right) f\left( \frac1y \right) = 1$$
for every $x$ , $y\in \mathbb{R}_+$ .

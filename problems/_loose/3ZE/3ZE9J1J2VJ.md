Investigate the behaviour as $t\to+\infty$ of solutions of the systems

$$\begin{cases}
\dot{x}=y\\
\dot{y}=2\sin y-y-x\end{cases}$$

$$\begin{cases}
\dot{x}=y\\
\dot{y}=2x-x^{3}-x^{2}-\epsilon y\end{cases}$$

where $\epsilon\ll 1$.

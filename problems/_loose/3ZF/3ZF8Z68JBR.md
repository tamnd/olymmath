Positive real numbers $a_1,a_2,\ldots, a_n$ satisfy the equation $$ 2a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_{n-1}=a_n+\frac{n^2-3n+2}{2} $$
For every positive integer $n \geq 3$ find the smallest possible value of the sum $$ \frac{(a_1+1)^2}{a_2}+\ldots+\frac{(a_{n-1}+1)^2}{a_n} $$ *M. Zorka*

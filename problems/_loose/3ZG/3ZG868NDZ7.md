Find the integral $\int f(x) d x$. Let the function $f(x)$ be continuous. Assuming $x=\psi(t), d x=\psi^{\prime}(t) d t$, where the derivative $\varphi^{\prime}(t)$ is a continuous function, we get

$$
\int f(x) d x=\int f[\varphi(t)]_{`} \tau^{\prime}(t) d t .
$$

To obtain the desired result in the integral found with the new variable $t$, we switch to the variable $x$ using the original formula $x=\varphi(t)$.

Let's provide some examples.

1) Find $\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2}+1}} d x$.

Let $p$ be an odd prime and $S_{q}=\frac{1}{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}+\frac{1}{5 \cdot 6 \cdot 7}+\ldots+\frac{1}{q(q+1)(q+2)}$, where $q=\frac{3 p-5}{2}$. We write $\frac{1}{p}-2 S_{q}$ in the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are integers. Prove that $m \equiv n(\bmod p)$; that is, $m$ and $n$ give the same remainder when divided by $p$.

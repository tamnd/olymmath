Prove that the sum

$\frac{\cos 3^{\circ}}{\cos 6^{\circ}-\cos 2^{\circ}}+\frac{\cos 5^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}-\cos 2^{\circ}}+\cdots+\frac{\cos (2 n+1)^{\circ}}{\cos (4 n+2)^{\circ}-\cos 2^{\circ}}+\cdots+\frac{\cos 89^{\circ}}{\cos 178^{\circ}-\cos 2^{\circ}}$ is equal to

$$
\frac{\sin 2^{\circ}-1}{4 \sin 1^{\circ} \cdot \sin 2^{\circ}}
$$

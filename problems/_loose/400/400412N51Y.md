Let real numbers $a, b, c, d$ satisfy $a b c d > 0$. Prove: there exists a permutation $x, y, z, w$ of $a, b, c, d$ such that
$$
2(x z + y w)^{2} > \left(x^{2} + y^{2}\right)\left(z^{2} + w^{2}\right).
$$

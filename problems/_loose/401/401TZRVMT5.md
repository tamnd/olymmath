Take three different points $A$, $B$, and $C$ on the parabola $y=x^{2}$, and let the circumradius of $\triangle ABC$ be $R$.
(1) Prove: $R>\frac{1}{2}$;
(2) Does there exist a constant $c>\frac{1}{2}$ such that for any three different points $A$, $B$, and $C$ taken on the parabola $y=x^{2}$, we have $R \geqslant c$?

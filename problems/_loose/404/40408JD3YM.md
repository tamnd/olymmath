After returning home, he counted the RMB he got back, which included 4 different denominations of banknotes and 4 different denominations of coins. The banknotes were all worth more than 1 yuan, and the coins were all worth less than 1 yuan. (Note: The store has banknotes of 100 yuan, 50 yuan, 20 yuan, 10 yuan, 5 yuan, and 1 yuan, and coins of 5 jiao, 1 jiao, 5 fen, 2 fen, and 1 fen for change)

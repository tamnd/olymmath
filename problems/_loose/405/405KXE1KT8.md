Let $a_{1}=1$,
$$
a_{n}=a_{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]}+a_{\left[\frac{n}{3}\right]}+\cdots+a_{\left[\frac{n}{n}\right]}+1(n \geqslant 2) \text {. }
$$

Prove: there exist infinitely many $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, such that $a_{n} \equiv n\left(\bmod 2^{2020}\right)$.

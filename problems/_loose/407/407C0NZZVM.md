Given the expression $a(t)=t^{2}-5 t+6$, by substituting $t$ with $0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots$ in sequence, we obtain an infinite sequence of numbers in both directions. Show that for any integer $t$, there exists an integer pair $(u, v)$ such that the numbers $a(t), a(u), a(v)$ form an arithmetic sequence in this order.

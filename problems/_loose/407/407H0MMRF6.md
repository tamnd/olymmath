$\odot O$ is inscribed in $\triangle A B C$, which is an equilateral triangle. $A D$ is a diameter of $\odot O$. Take any point $P$ on the arc $B C$, and let $E$ and $F$ be the incenters of $\triangle P A B$ and $\triangle P A C$, respectively. Prove: $P D=|P E-P F|$.

Experiments have shown that when 10 pieces of $1 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick flat glass are stacked, the intensity of the ray after passing through these 10 pieces of glass is the same as the intensity after passing through a single piece of $11 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick flat glass, indicating that the gaps between each pair of glass plates also cause attenuation. To ensure that the intensity does not exceed that after passing through a single piece of $20 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick flat glass, what is the minimum number of $1 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick flat glass plates that need to be stacked (Note: Assume the gaps between each pair of flat glass plates are the same)?

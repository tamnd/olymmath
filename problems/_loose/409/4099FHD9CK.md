We call the *tribi * of a positive integer $k$ (denoted $T(k)$) the number of all pairs $11$ in the binary representation of $k$. e.g $$T(1)=T(2)=0, T(3)=1, T(4)=T(5)=0, T(6)=1, T(7)=2.$$
Calculate $S_n=\sum_{k=1}^{2^n}T(K)$.

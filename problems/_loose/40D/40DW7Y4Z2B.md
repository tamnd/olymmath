Three friends are preparing for the "Kleine Friedensfahrt." They train on a circular track. Their start occurs at the same time and in the same direction at the starting line $S$. Manfred completed the first lap in exactly 3 minutes, Klaus in exactly $3 \frac{3}{4}$ minutes, and Helmut in exactly 5 minutes.

a) After how many minutes would the three friends first reach the starting line $S$ simultaneously again, assuming that Manfred takes exactly 3 minutes per lap, Klaus exactly $3 \frac{3}{4}$ minutes, and Helmut exactly 5 minutes for all subsequent laps?

b) How many laps would each of them have completed by then?

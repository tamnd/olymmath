Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the points of intersection of the rays $B A$ and $C D$, $B C$ and $A D$ respectively, and let $H$ be the projection of $D$ onto $P Q$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is tangential if and only if the incircles of triangles $A D P$ and $C D Q$ are seen from point $H$ at equal angles.

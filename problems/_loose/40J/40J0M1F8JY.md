$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
g_{1}=-x_{1}+0 \cdot x_{2}+0 \cdot x_{3} \\
g_{2}=0 \cdot x_{1}+1 \cdot x_{2}+0 \cdot x_{3} \\
g_{3}=0 \cdot x_{1}+0 \cdot x_{2}+1 \cdot x_{3}
\end{array}\right.
$$

Find the transformation matrix, will it be singular?

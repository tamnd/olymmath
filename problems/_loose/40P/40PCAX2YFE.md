(2004 National College Entrance Examination Hubei Paper) A certain emergency event, if no preventive measures are taken, has a probability of 0.3 of occurring; if it occurs, it will cause a loss of 4 million yuan. There are two independent preventive measures, A and B, available for adoption. The costs of adopting preventive measures A and B alone are 450,000 yuan and 300,000 yuan, respectively, and the probabilities of the emergency event not occurring after adopting the corresponding preventive measures are 0.9 and 0.85, respectively. If the preventive plan allows for the adoption of preventive measures A and B alone, in combination, or not at all, please determine the preventive plan that minimizes the total cost.
(Total cost $=$ cost of adopting preventive measures + expected value of losses from the emergency event occurring.)

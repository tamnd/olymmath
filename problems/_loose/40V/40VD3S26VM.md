Let $C$ be a linear code of length $n$. In $C$, append 0 to the end of codewords with even weight, and append 1 to the end of codewords with odd weight, thus forming a new code $C^{\prime}$,
(1) If $\boldsymbol{H}$ is the parity-check matrix of $C$, then the parity-check matrix of $C^{\prime}$ is
$$
\left[\begin{array}{cccc}
1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\
& & & 0 \\
& H & & \vdots \\
& & & 0
\end{array}\right]
$$
(2) Prove that the distance between any two codewords in $C^{\prime}$ is even.
(3) Prove that if the minimum distance $d$ between any two codewords in $C$ is odd, then the minimum distance between corresponding codewords in $C^{\prime}$ is $d+1$.

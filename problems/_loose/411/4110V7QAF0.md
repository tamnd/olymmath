On the outside of the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, construct equilateral triangles $\triangle B A E, \triangle C G D$, and on the inside, construct equilateral triangles $\triangle B C F$, $\triangle A H D$. Then (1) $E F G H$ is a parallelogram; (2) $H G=A C$.

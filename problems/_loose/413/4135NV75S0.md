Let $n \geqslant 2$. For any $n$ vectors $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}$ in the plane, let $M$ denote the number of real pairs $(i, j)$ satisfying $i<j$ and $\alpha_{i} \cdot \alpha_{j}<0$. Prove: $M \leqslant \frac{n^{2}}{3}$.

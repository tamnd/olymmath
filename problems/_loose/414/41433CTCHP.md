Given positive numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ and $y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}$ satisfying
(1) $x_{1}>x_{2}>\cdots>x_{n}, y_{1}>y_{2}>\cdots>y_{n}$;
(2) $x_{1}>y_{1}, x_{1}+x_{2}>y_{1}+y_{2}, \cdots, x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}>y_{1}+y_{2}+\cdots+y_{n}$.
Prove that for any natural number $k$, we have $x_{1}^{k}+x_{2}^{k}+\cdots+x_{n}^{k}>y_{1}^{k}+y_{2}^{k}+\cdots+y_{n}^{k}$.
(35th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad Problem)

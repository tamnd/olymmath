Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>$ $b>0)$ with left and right foci $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$, suppose that the points $F_{1}$, $F_{2}$, and one endpoint of the minor axis of the ellipse form an equilateral triangle with side length 4.
(1) Find the standard equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Through any point $P$ on the ellipse $C$, draw the tangent line to the ellipse $C$ and the perpendicular line $F_{1} M$ to the line $F_{1} P$ intersecting at point $M$. Find the equation of the trajectory of point $M$;
(3) If the tangent line $M P$ intersects the line $x=-2$ at point $N$, prove that $\frac{\left|N F_{1}\right|}{\left|M F_{1}\right|}$ is a constant.

a) In the complex plane with origin $O$, consider the points $A$ and $B$, with non-zero affixes $a$ and $b$ respectively. Show that $S_{[O A B]}=\frac{1}{4}|\bar{a} b-a \bar{b}|$, where $S_{[O A B]}$ represents the area of triangle $O A B$.

b) Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle $\mathcal{C}$ with center $O$. For a point $P$ inside the circle $\mathcal{C}$, let $S(P)$ be the area of the triangle with side lengths equal to the distances from $P$ to the vertices of the triangle. Let $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ be two distinct points inside the circle $\mathcal{C}$. Show that $S\left(P_{1}\right)=S\left(P_{2}\right)$ if and only if $O P_{1}=O P_{2}$.

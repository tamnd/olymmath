Let $P_1 \cdots P_{27}$ be a regular $27$ -gon. How many triples of positive integers $(a, b, c)$ with $1 \le a < b < c \le 27$ are there such that $P_a P_b P_c$ is acute? $\textit{Remark:}$ $P_a P_b P_c$ refers to a triangle, not an angle.

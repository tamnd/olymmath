Two equal circles of radius $R$ intersect at points $A$ and $B$ at an angle $\varphi\left(\varphi=\angle O_{1} A O_{2}\right)$. In one of the circles, a chord $A M$ is drawn, and in the other, a chord $A N$ is drawn such that the angle $M A N$ is equal to $\frac{\varphi}{2}$ (or the supplementary angle). Prove that the segment $M N$ has a constant length.

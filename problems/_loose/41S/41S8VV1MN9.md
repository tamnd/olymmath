Bryce has $7$ blue socks and $7$ red socks mixed in a drawer. He plays a game with Sean. Blindfolded, Bryce takes two socks from the drawer. Sean looks at the socks, and if they have the same color, Sean gives Bryce one point. Bryce keeps drawing socks until the drawer is empty, at which the game ends. The probability that Bryce's score is at most $2$ can be written in the form $p/q$ , where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $p+q$ .

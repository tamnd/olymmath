Let $C(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} C_{n} x^{n}$ be the generating function of the Catalan numbers. Prove that it satisfies the equation

$$
C(x)=x C^{2}(x)+1,
$$

and find the explicit form of the function $C(x)$.

The definition of Catalan numbers can be found in the reference manual.

Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers such that the quadratic equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ has two distinct real solutions $x_1$ and $x_2$ . Suppose $|x_1-x_2|=1$ , $|p-q|=1$ . Prove that $p, q, x_1, x_2$ are all integers.

Find three numbers such that if each gives a certain specified part of itself to the next, they will be equal after the mutual gift-giving. For example, the first number gives one-third of its value to the second, the second gives one-fourth of its value to the third, and finally, the third gives one-fifth of its value to the first. Determine the sequence of positive integer solutions.

There are $n+1$ weights with a total weight of $2 n$ and a balance with two pans, which is in equilibrium. The weight of each weight is expressed by a natural number. The weights are placed on the pans of the balance in turn: first the heaviest (or one of the heaviest), then the heaviest of the remaining ones, and so on. Each subsequent weight is placed on the pan that is currently lighter, and if the balance is in equilibrium, then on any of the pans. Prove that after all the weights have been placed on the balance, the balance will be in equilibrium.

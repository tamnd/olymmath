Find the elements of sets $A$ and $B$ knowing that the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied:
a) $A \cup B=\{x \in \mathbb{N} \mid x$ is a digit in the decimal system $\}$;
b) $A \cap B=\left\{x \in \mathbb{N} \mid x\right.$ is the last digit of $7^{n}$, with $n$ a natural number $\}$;
c) $A$ and $B$ have the same number of elements;

d) The sum of the elements of set $A$ is greater than the sum of the elements of set $B$;
e) $\mathrm{B} \backslash \mathrm{A}=\{5\}$.

Given the function $y=x+\frac{a}{x}$ has the following properties: if the constant $a>0$, then the function is decreasing on $(0, \sqrt{a}]$ and increasing on $[\sqrt{a},+\infty)$.
(1) Investigate the monotonicity of the function $y=x^{2}+\frac{a}{x^{2}}$ (constant $\left.a>0\right)$ within its domain, and provide a reason;
(2) Generalize the functions $y=x+\frac{a}{x}$ and $y=x^{2}+\frac{a}{x^{2}}$ (constant $a>0$) so that they are special cases of the generalized function. Investigate the monotonicity of the generalized function (only the conclusion is required, no proof needed).

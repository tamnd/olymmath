The Absent-Minded Scientist on the Bus (4 p. 6-11). In the bus, there are $n$ seats, and all tickets have been sold to $n$ passengers. The first to enter the bus is the Absent-Minded Scientist, who, without looking at the ticket, takes the first available seat. Subsequently, passengers enter one by one. If an entering passenger sees that their seat is free, they take their seat. If the seat is occupied, the entering passenger takes the first available free seat. Find the probability that the last passenger to enter will take the seat according to their ticket?

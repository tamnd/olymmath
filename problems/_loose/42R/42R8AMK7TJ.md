The set of rigid body rotation transformations $T=\left\{T_{a}\right\}$ in two-dimensional Euclidean space forms a group, where
$$
T_{a}:\binom{x_{1}}{y_{1}}=\left(\begin{array}{rr}
\cos \alpha & \sin \alpha \\
-\sin \alpha & \cos \alpha
\end{array}\right)\binom{x}{y}
$$

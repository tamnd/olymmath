Let $A, B, C, D$ be four non-coplanar points and $G_{1}, G_{2}, G_{3}$ the centroids of $\triangle B C D, \triangle A C D$, and $\triangle A B D$ respectively.

a) Show that the plane $\left(G_{1} G_{2} G_{3}\right)$ is parallel to the plane $(A B C)$.

b) Prove that the lines $\mathrm{A} G_{1}, \mathrm{~B} G_{2}, \mathrm{C} G_{3}$ are concurrent.

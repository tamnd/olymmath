In the English club, in the evening, $n$ of its members ($n \geq 3$) gathered. According to the club's traditions, each brought with them the type of juice they prefer, in the amount they plan to drink throughout the evening. According to the club's rules, at any moment, any three of its members can sit down at a table and drink the juice (each their own) in any amount, but all three must drink equally. Prove that for all members to be able to completely drink the juice they brought during the evening, it is necessary and sufficient that the share of juice brought by each club member does not exceed one third of the total amount.

A four-digit number, if a comma is placed between the hundreds and tens place, can be written as two two-digit numbers $(3126 \rightarrow 31,62)$. If the two two-digit numbers have an integer multiple relationship, we call such a four-digit number a "clever number". If you select 4 numbers from $1, 2, 4, 6, 8$ to form a four-digit number, how many "clever numbers" are there? $ $

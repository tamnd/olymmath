On the board, the equation $x^{3}+x^{2}+*_{x}+*=0$ is written. Petya and Vasya take turns replacing the asterisks with rational numbers: first, Petya replaces any of the asterisks, then Vasya replaces any of the two remaining, and then Petya replaces the remaining asterisk. Is it true that regardless of Vasya's actions, Petya can obtain an equation for which the difference between some two roots is 2014?

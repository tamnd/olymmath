A circle intersects each side of a rhombus at two points, dividing it into three segments. Let's traverse the contour of the rhombus, starting from any vertex, clockwise, and paint the three segments of each side sequentially in red, white, and blue. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the red segments is equal to the sum of the lengths of the blue segments.

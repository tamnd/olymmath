Let $a, b$, and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=3$. Prove:

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^{2}+a b+b c}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{b^{2}+b c+c a}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{c^{2}+c a+a b}} \geqslant \sqrt{3}
$$

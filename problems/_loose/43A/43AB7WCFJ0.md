Given a triangle $O B C(\angle B O C=\alpha)$. For each point $A$ on the side $B C$, we define points $M$ and $N$ on $O B$ and $O C$ such that $\angle M A N=\beta(\alpha+\beta<\pi)$ and the area of quadrilateral OMAN is maximized. Prove that this maximum area reaches a minimum for such points $A, M$, and $N$ for which $|M A|=|A N|$, and the line $M N$ is parallel to $B C$. (Such points exist if the angles $B$ and $C$ of triangle $A B C$ do not exceed $\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}$.)

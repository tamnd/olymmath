We are given the following definition: let $I$ be an interval included in $\mathbb{R}$, $f$ a function from $I$ to $\mathbb{R}$, and $a \in I$. We say that the function $f$ is continuous at $a$ if $: \forall \varepsilon>0, \exists \eta>0, \forall x \in I,|x-a| \leq \eta \Rightarrow|f(x)-f(a)| \leq \varepsilon$.

Prove the following theorem: let $\left(u_{n}\right)$ be a sequence of elements of $I$ that converges to a real number $\ell$ and $f$ a function (defined on an interval $I \subset \mathbb{R}$ and taking real values) continuous at $\ell$. Then the sequence $\left(f\left(u_{n}\right)\right)$ converges to $f(\ell)$.

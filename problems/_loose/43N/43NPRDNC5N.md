The portion of the cube that remains when these eight tetrahedra are cut away is called a *cuboctahedron*. The ratio of the volume of the cuboctahedron to the volume of the original cube is closest to which of these? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 75\% \textbf{(B)}\ 78\% \textbf{(C)}\ 81\% \textbf{(D)}\ 84\% \textbf{(E)}\ 87\% $

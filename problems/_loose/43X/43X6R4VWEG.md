We divide the sides of a regular hexagon in the ratio $p: q$. The points of division determine a new hexagon, and we again divide its sides in the same ratio, imagining this process to continue indefinitely. What is the sum of the areas of the hexagons? What must $p: q$ be for the sum of the areas of the inscribed hexagons to be four times the area of the original hexagon?

Aleš, Brane, and Cvetka prepared a large number of cards, on which they wrote one of the numbers $2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7,$ and $8$. Maja, who joined them later, took three cards and attached one to each of their foreheads. Aleš, Brane, and Cvetka could not see the number on their own foreheads, but each could see the numbers on the foreheads of the other two. Maja then told them: "You do not have different numbers on your foreheads, and the product of all three numbers is a perfect square." Aleš, Brane, and Cvetka then each tried to figure out which number they had on their forehead. Could all three of them determine this?

The competition lasts 210 minutes. The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

49th Mathematical Competition for High School Students in Slovenia

Velenje, April 16, 2005

On one of the cells of a $10 \times 10$ chessboard, a rook is placed. By moving to an adjacent cell each turn, it has traversed all the cells of the board, visiting each exactly once. Prove that for each main diagonal of the board, the following statement is true: in the rook's route, there are two consecutive moves, the first of which takes it off this diagonal, and the next one brings it back onto it. (The main diagonal runs from one corner of the board to the opposite corner.)

The Pharaoh Am-En-Matek gave the following task to every suitor of his daughter: Divide this square wall section into 10 squares using line segments parallel to its sides. Once you are done with this, come back every day for a week and suggest a new division, but in these, there should not be any square that has already appeared, that is, no square whose four vertices are identical to the four vertices of any square from a previous division.

Can this requirement be met?

Sixty-seventh problem: On an $\mathrm{m} \times \mathrm{n}(\mathrm{m} \geq 2, \mathrm{n} \geq 2)$ chessboard, each cell contains a non-negative integer. If two numbers are in cells that share a common edge, they are called "adjacent." If a filling method satisfies:
(a) The difference between any two adjacent numbers is 0 or 1;
(b) If a number is less than or equal to all of its adjacent numbers, then this number is 0.
Then this filling method is called a "garden." Try to find the number of all different "gardens" (expressed in terms of $\mathrm{m}$ and $\mathrm{n}$).

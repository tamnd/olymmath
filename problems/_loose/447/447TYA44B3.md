Let the following be given:

$$
z^{2}-y z+\frac{1}{4}\left(5 y^{2}+x^{2}-4\right)=0
$$

a quadratic equation in $z$, where $x$ and $y$ represent the coordinates of a variable point in a Cartesian coordinate system. The geometric loci of points are to be determined for which:

a) the roots of the equation are real and distinct,

b) the roots of the equation are equal and real,

c) the roots of the equation are complex.

In the region a), the following places are to be selected for which:

$\left.a_{1}\right)$ both roots are positive,

$\left.a_{2}\right)$ both roots are negative,

$\left.a_{3}\right)$ the two roots have opposite signs.

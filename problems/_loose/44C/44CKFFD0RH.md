8-60 Given a two-ended sequence $\cdots, a_{-n}, \cdots, a_{-1}, a_{0}, a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$, $\cdots$ that satisfies
$$
a_{k}=\frac{1}{4}\left(a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}\right) \text {, for any } k \in Z .
$$

If there are two equal terms in this sequence, prove that there must be infinitely many pairs of equal terms in the sequence.

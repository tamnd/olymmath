Let $ E(x,y)=\frac{x}{y} +\frac{x+1}{y+1} +\frac{x+2}{y+2} . $

**a)** Solve in $ \mathbb{N}^2 $ the equation $ E(x,y)=3. $
**b)** Show that there are infinitely many natural numbers $ n $ such that the equation $ E(x,y)=n $ has at least one solution in $ \mathbb{N}^2. $

For natural numbers $n$ and $b$ , let $V(n, b)$ denote the number of decompositions of $n$ into the product of integers each of which is greater than $b$ : for example $$ 36 = 6\times 6 = 4\times 9 = 3\times 3\times 4 = 3\times 12, $$ i.e. $V(36,2) = 5$ . Prove that $V(n, b) < n/b$ for all $n$ and $b$ .

(N.B. Vasiliev, Moscow)

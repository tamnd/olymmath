The ninety-sixth problem: Given a positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, label $4 \mathrm{n}$ equally spaced points on a circle with the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 4 n$ in any manner. It is always possible to connect these $4 n$ points with $2 \mathrm{n}$ non-intersecting line segments such that the difference between the numbers at the endpoints of each segment does not exceed $M$. Determine the minimum possible value of $M$.

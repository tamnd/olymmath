In a certain state, there are 101 cities.

a) Each city is connected to each of the others by a one-way road, and 50 roads enter each city and 50 roads leave each city. Prove that from each city, you can reach any other city by traveling no more than two roads.

b) Some cities are connected by one-way roads, and 40 roads enter each city and 40 roads leave each city. Prove that from each city, you can reach any other city by traveling no more than three roads.

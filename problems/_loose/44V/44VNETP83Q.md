$n$ clowns in a circle are juggling, each with a certain number of balls. Every minute, the circus director gives a signal, and each clown sends half of their balls to their right neighbor. If a clown has an odd number of balls, they take one out of their pocket just before the signal.

Prove that the clowns will eventually all have the same number of balls.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be random matrices of the same size, and suppose that the matrix $\mathrm{E}^{\top}{ }^{\top}$ is invertible. Prove the matrix Cauchy-Bunyakovsky inequality:

$$
\mathrm{E} X Y^{\top} \cdot\left[\mathrm{E} Y Y^{\top}\right]^{+} \cdot \mathrm{E} Y X^{\top} \leqslant \mathrm{E} X X^{\top}
$$

where "s" denotes the positive semi-definiteness of the matrix formed by the difference of the matrices on the right and left sides of the given matrix inequality.

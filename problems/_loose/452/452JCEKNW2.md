Let $P$ and $Q$ be two fixed points in the plane. The given parallelogram $ABCD$ moves in the plane such that the line of the side $AB$ always passes through the point $P$, and the line of the side $AD$ always passes through the point $Q$.

Show that there is a point $R$ which lies on the line of the diagonal $AC$ in every position of the parallelogram.

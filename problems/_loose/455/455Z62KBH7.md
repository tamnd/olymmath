Prove that for an integer $n$ the expression

$$
\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)}{1.2 .3 .4}
$$

is an integer. Check for several larger values of $n$ and $k$ that, generally, the expression

$$
\frac{n(n+1)(n+2) \ldots(n+k-2)(n+k-1)}{1.2 .3 \ldots(k-1) k}
$$

is an integer.

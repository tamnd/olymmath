Given two intersecting circles at points $P$ and $Q$. An arbitrary line $1$, passing through $Q$, intersects the circles again at points $A$ and $B$. The tangents to the circles at points $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $C$, and the bisector of angle $CPQ$ intersects line $AB$ at point $D$. Prove that all points $D$, which can be obtained by choosing different lines $1$, lie on one circle.

On each of two intersecting circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ at points $A$ and $B$, points $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ are given, respectively. A variable line $g_{1}$ passing through point $S_{1}$ intersects circle $O_{1}$ at point $P_{1}$, and line $A P$ intersects circle $O_{2}$ at point $P_{2}$. Prove that the geometric locus of the points of intersection of line $g_{1}$ and line $g_{2}$, passing through points $P_{2}$ and $S_{2}$, is a circle determined by points $S_{1}, S_{2}, B$.

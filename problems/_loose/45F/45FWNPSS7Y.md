Divide a $1 \times n$ rectangle into $n$ squares and number them $1,2, \cdots, n$. Place one chess piece in each of the squares numbered $n-2, n-1, n$. Two players, A and B, play the following game: A and B take turns moving the pieces, with A going first. On each turn, one of the three pieces can be moved to a smaller-numbered empty square. The player who cannot make a move loses. Prove that A has a winning strategy.

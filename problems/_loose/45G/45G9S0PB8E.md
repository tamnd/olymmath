Tetrahedron $A B C D$ has all edge lengths equal to $1, P$ is the midpoint of edge $A B$, and $Q$ is the midpoint of edge $C D$. Then the length of line segment $P Q$ is
(A) $\frac{1}{2}$.
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{3}{4}$.
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
(Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1987)

Taking a rattle with her, Alice set off to the Forest of Forgetfulness, hoping she would manage to find at least one of the brothers. To her delight, she suddenly saw both brothers sitting under a tree and grinning. Alice approached the one who was closer and sternly demanded:

- Tell me the truth! Whose rattle is this?

He replied:

- It's Trulala's rattle.

Alice thought for a moment and asked the second brother:

- Who are you?
- Trulala, - he answered.

Alice didn't remember exactly what day of the week this conversation took place, but she was sure it wasn't Sunday.

To whom should Alice have given the rattle?

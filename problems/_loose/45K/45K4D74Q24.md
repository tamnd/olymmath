A and B play the following game: A first proposes a 1000-digit number $A_{1}$, and after B knows this number, B provides a number $B_{1}$. Then A, according to his own will, decides whether to subtract the smaller number from the larger number or to add the two numbers, and tells B the result $A_{2}$. Then B provides another number $B_{2}$, and A performs the operation on $A_{2}, B_{2}$ according to the above procedure to get $A_{3}$, and so on. If a number of the form $10^{k}(k=0,1,2, \cdots)$ appears in A's results, B will terminate the game. Prove that B only needs to provide no more than 20 numbers to definitely terminate the game.

Given a sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with all terms non-zero, the sum of the first $k$ terms is $S_{k}$, and $S_{k}=\frac{1}{2} a_{k} a_{k+1}\left(k \in \mathbf{N}_{+}\right)$, where $a_{1}=1$.
(1) Find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) For any given positive integer $n(n \geqslant 2)$, the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $\frac{b_{k+1}}{b_{k}}=\frac{k-n}{a_{k+1}}(k=1,2,3, \cdots, n), b_{1}=1$. Find $b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}$.

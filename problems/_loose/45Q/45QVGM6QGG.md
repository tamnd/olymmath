Suppose 3 distinct numbers are chosen from $1,2, \ldots, 3 n$ with their sum equal to $3 n$. What is the largest possible product of those 3 numbers?
(a) $n^{3}-n$;
(b) $n^{3}$;
(c) $n^{3}+n$;
(d) $n^{3}-7 n+6$;
(e) $n^{3}-7 n-6$.

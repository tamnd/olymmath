There is an acute triangle ABC such that $AB \neq AC$. Let $D$ and $E$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$. The point $P$ on the line segment $BE$ satisfies $\angle APE = \angle BAC$, the point $Q$ on the line segment $CD$ satisfies $\angle AQD = \angle BAC$. Let $X$ be the point that is not $C$ at the intersection of a circle passing through $3$ points $A$, $C$, $P$ and a straight line $BC$, let $Y$ be the point that is not $B$ at the intersection of a circle passing through $3$ points $A$, $B$, $Q$ and a straight line $BC$. Show that $BC = XY$.

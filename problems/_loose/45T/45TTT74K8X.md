In triangle $ABC$ , $AL$ bisects angle $A$ and $CM$ bisects angle $C$ . Points $L$ and $M$ are on $BC$ and $AB$ , respectively. The sides of triangle $ABC$ are $a,b,$ and $c$ . Then $\frac{\overline{AM}}{\overline{MB}}=k\frac{\overline{CL}}{\overline{LB}}$ where $k$ is: $ \textbf{(A)}\ 1 \textbf{(B)}\ \frac{bc}{a^2} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac{a^2}{bc} \textbf{(D)}\ \frac{c}{b} \textbf{(E)}\ \frac{c}{a} $

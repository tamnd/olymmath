Let there be a rectangle $A B C D$, where $A B: A D=\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})$, construct $C_{1} B_{1}$ such that $A B_{1} C_{1} D$ is a square. Prove that $B_{1} C_{1} C B$ and $A B C D$ are similar, and prove that continuing this process yields a sequence of rectangles similar to the original rectangle.

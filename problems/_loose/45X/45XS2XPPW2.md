Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{25}$ be integers, and $b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}, \ldots, b_{25}$ be the same numbers taken in a different order. Prove that the number

$$
\left(a_{1}-b_{1}\right)\left(a_{2}-b_{2}\right)\left(a_{3}-b_{3}\right) \ldots\left(a_{25}-b_{25}\right)
$$

is even.

$\mathbf{9 3}^{\circ}$. Prove that the sum and difference of any two integers have the same parity.

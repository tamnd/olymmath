Given a quadrilateral $A_{0} A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$, draw perpendicular lines $l_{i}$ from vertex $A_{0}$ to $A_{0} A_{i}(i=1,2,3)$. Let $l_{1}$ intersect $A_{2} A_{3}$ at point $P_{1}$, $l_{2}$ intersect $A_{3} A_{1}$ at point $P_{2}$, and $l_{3}$ intersect $A_{1} A_{2}$ at point $P_{3}$. Prove: $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are collinear.
(2000, Nanchang City High School Mathematics Competition)

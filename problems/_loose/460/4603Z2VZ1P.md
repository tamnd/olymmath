A matrix $A=\left(\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right) \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ has the properties

$$
A^{2}=\left(\begin{array}{ll}
a^{2} & b^{2} \\
c^{2} & d^{2}
\end{array}\right) \text { and } A^{3}=\left(\begin{array}{ll}
a^{3} & b^{3} \\
c^{3} & d^{3}
\end{array}\right)
$$

Show that $A^{n}=\left(\begin{array}{ll}a^{n} & b^{n} \\ c^{n} & d^{n}\end{array}\right)$, for any non-zero natural number $n$.

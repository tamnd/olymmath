In triangle $A B C$, the feet of the altitudes are $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, and the midpoints of the sides are $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$. Let the areas of triangles $A B_{1} C_{2}$, $B C_{1} A_{2}$, $C A_{1} B_{2}$, and $A C_{1} B_{2}$, $B A_{1} C_{2}$, $C B_{1} A_{2}$ be $t_{a 1}, t_{b 1}, t_{c 1}$, and $t_{a 2}, t_{b 2}, t_{c 2}$, respectively. It is to be proven that

$$
t_{a 1} \cdot t_{b 1} \cdot t_{c 1}=t_{a 2} \cdot t_{b 2} \cdot t_{c 2}
$$

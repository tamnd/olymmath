Determine the smallest natural number $n$ for which there exists a set

$$
M \subset\{1,2, \ldots, 100\}
$$

of $n$ elements that satisfies the conditions:
a) 1 and 100 belong to the set $M$,

b) for every $a \in M \backslash\{1\}$, there exist $x, y \in M$ such that $a=x+y$.

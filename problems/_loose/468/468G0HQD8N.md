Show that in a right-angled triangle:

$$
c^{2}=\frac{4}{5}\left(k_{a}^{2}+k_{b}^{2}\right)
$$

if $c$ is the hypotenuse, $k_{a}$ and $k_{b}$ are the medians to the sides $a$ and $b$, respectively.

In the dihedral angle $M-l-N$, on one face $M$, there are two perpendicular lines $AB$ and $CD$, their intersection point is $O, A, C$ are on $l$, and $AB, CD$ form angles $\alpha, \beta$ with the other face $N$. If the size of the dihedral angle is an acute angle $\theta$, prove: $\sin ^{2} \theta=\sin ^{2} \alpha+\sin ^{2} \beta$.

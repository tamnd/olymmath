Several people (at least two) are sitting at a table and playing a game: in each round, each player secretly votes for one player (they can also vote for themselves). Then the round is evaluated: any player who received exactly one vote is eliminated from the game.

a) How many people could have been sitting at the table at the beginning if exactly one player was eliminated in the first round?

b) Could the game have had a single winner, that is, a person who remained in the game alone after a certain number of rounds?

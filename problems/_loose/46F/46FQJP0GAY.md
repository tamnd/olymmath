Esmeralda and Sara play a game by choosing numbers. First, Esmeralda chooses a prime number $p$ . Then, Sara chooses a positive integer $k$ . Esmeralda's goal is to find an infinite set $S$ of positive integers that are multiples of $p^k$ such that **exactly** the last $k$ digits of all elements of $S$ coincide and are **nonzero**. For example, exactly the last three digits of $9012\mathbf{345}$ , $5\mathbf{345}$ , $ \mathbf{345}$ , $15\mathbf{345}$ coincide and are nonzero.

Prove that Esmeralda can choose a prime $p$ to achieve her goal, regardless of Sara's choice.

11 Suppose there are $n(n \geqslant 2)$ points on a straight line, consider the distances between every two points, prove that the ratio $\lambda_{n}$ of the maximum distance to the minimum distance is $\lambda_{n} \geqslant \sqrt{\frac{n(n+1)}{6}}$.

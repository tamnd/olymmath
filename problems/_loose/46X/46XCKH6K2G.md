In each of ten identical bowls, milk has been poured, which is no more than $10 \%$ of the bowl's capacity. With one action, it is allowed to choose a bowl and pour its contents evenly into each of the remaining ones. Prove that the amount of milk in each of the bowls can be equalized with no more than 10 such actions.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be subsets of the real numbers. The notation $X \cdot Y$ represents the set of all real numbers that can be obtained as the product of a number from $X$ and a number from $Y$; $X+Y$ represents the set of all real numbers that can be expressed as the sum of a number from $X$ and a number from $Y$. Which real numbers $c$ have the property that there exists a proper, non-empty subset $X$ of the real numbers such that $X \cdot X+\{c\}=X$?

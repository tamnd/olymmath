[9] On the number line, infinitely many points with natural coordinates are marked. When a wheel rolls along the line, each marked point that the wheel passes over leaves a point trace on it. Prove that one can choose such a real $R$ that if the wheel of radius $R$ is rolled along the line, starting from zero, then on each arc of the wheel of $1^{\circ}$ there will be a trace of at least one marked point.

(I. Mitrofanov)

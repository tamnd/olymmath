A sequence is called an *arithmetic progression of the first order* if the differences of the successive terms are constant. It is called an *arithmetic progression of the second order* if the differences of the successive terms form an arithmetic progression of the first order. In general, for $k\geq 2$, a sequence is called an *arithmetic progression of the $k$-th order* if the differences of the successive terms form an arithmetic progression of the $(k-1)$-th order.
The numbers
$$4,6,13,27,50,84$$
are the first six terms of an arithmetic progression of some order. What is its least possible order? Find a formula for the $n$-th term of this progression.

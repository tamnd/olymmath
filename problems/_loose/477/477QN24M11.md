2(У). Indicate which of the following statements can be refuted by providing a counterexample:

1) none of the guys in our class have textbooks with scribbles and notes
2) if a number is divisible by 5, then it ends in a five;
3) the set of numbers of the form $n(n+1)$ is even for $n \in N$;
4) $x^{2}>x$ for any $x$;
5) in a triangle, the length of the height is always less than the length of any of its sides;
6) if $a>b$, then $a^{2}>b^{2}$.

In the Kingdom of Magic Energy, an enchanted transformer booth stands on a flat plain: to an observer looking parallel to the ground, it is visible only at an angle of $90^{\circ}$. In cross-section, the booth is square with a side length of $L$ cubits. Describe the geometric locus of points on the plain from which the booth is visible, and determine the minimum and maximum distances from which the enchanted booth is visible. The angle at which a figure $F$ is visible from a point $P$ is defined as the smallest angle with vertex $P$ that contains the figure $F$. In this case, this angle is located in the plane of the cross-section of the booth.

In an isosceles $\triangle A B C$, the length of the altitude from one of the equal sides is 1. This altitude forms a $45^{\circ}$ angle with the base. Then the area of $\triangle A B C$ is
(A) 1 .
(B) 0.5 .
(C) 0.25 .
(D) $\sqrt{3}$.
(E) None of the above answers is correct.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

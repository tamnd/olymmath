Let $n$ be an integer such that $1 \leqslant n \leqslant 30$. Ariane and Bérénice play the following game. First, Ariane chooses $n$ distinct numbers from the integers $1, 2, \ldots, 30$. Then, and in view of Ariane's choices, Bérénice chooses an integer $d \geqslant 2$. Bérénice wins if $d$ divides at least two of the numbers chosen by Ariane. Otherwise, Ariane wins.

For which values of $n$ can Ariane ensure her victory?

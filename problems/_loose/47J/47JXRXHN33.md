Let $M=\left\{\left.\left(\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ b & a\end{array}\right) \right\rvert a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_{6}\right\}$.

a) Determine the number of elements in $M$.

b) Calculate the sum of the elements in $M$.

c) If $G=\{A \in M \mid \operatorname{det} A=\hat{1}\}$, show that $(G, \cdot)$ is a group.

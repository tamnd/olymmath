Let $p_{n}$ denote the $n$-th prime number $\left(p_{1}=2, p_{2}=3, \ldots\right), \pi(n)$ denote the number of primes not exceeding $n$. Consider the following sets $A, B$

$$
A=\left\{n+p_{n}-1 \mid n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}\right\} ; B=\left\{n+\pi(n) \mid n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}\right\}
$$

Prove that every positive integer is an element of exactly one of the two sets!

Let $n$ be any positive integer, and let $S(n)$ denote the number of permutations $\tau$ of $\{1,\dots,n\}$ such that $k^4+(\tau(k))^4$ is prime for all $k=1,\dots,n$ . Show that $S(n)$ is always a square.

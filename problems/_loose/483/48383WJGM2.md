On the side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is taken. Three lines $m, n, p$, parallel to line $CM$, intersect line $AB$ at points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ respectively, such that $AA_1 = BB_1 = MC_1$. Prove that: 1) the sum of the segments of the parallel lines $m$ and $n$, contained within the triangle, is equal to the segment of line $p$, contained within the angle adjacent to angle $C, 2$) these segments are proportional to the parallel projections of the corresponding sides onto an arbitrary axis (the direction of projection is parallel to $MC$); the segments $m, n, p$ correspond to the projections of the sides $BC, CA, AB$.

Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$ and $M$ any point on $[BC]$. Let $E$ be the intersection of the parallel to $(AC)$ passing through $M$ and $(AB)$, and $F$ the intersection of the parallel to $(AB)$ passing through $M$ and $(AC)$.

Show that $E$ and $F$ are at the same distance from the center of the circumcircle of $ABC$.

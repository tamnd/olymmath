A magician with blindfolded eyes gives the audience 29 cards numbered from 1 to 29. The audience hides two cards and gives the rest to the magician's assistant. The assistant points to two of them, and the audience tells the magician the numbers on these cards (in any order they wish). After this, the magician guesses the numbers of the cards hidden by the audience. How can the magician and the assistant agree to ensure the trick always succeeds?

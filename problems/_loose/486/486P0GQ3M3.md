You have $n$ objects to arrange in $n$ slots. But some of the objects are identical. There are $k$ different types of objects and there are $n_{i}$ objects of the $i^{\text {th }}$ type. Therefore, $\sum_{i=1}^{k} n_{i}=n$.

How many arrangements of these objects are there?

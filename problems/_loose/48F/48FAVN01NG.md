The natural numbers from 1 to $N$ are written on a piece of paper. Players take turns circling one number, adhering to the rule: any two already circled numbers must be coprime. A number cannot be circled twice. The player who cannot make a move loses.

a) Who - the first player or the second player - will win when $N=10$?

b) And when $N=12$?

c) And when $N=15$?

d) And when $N=30$?

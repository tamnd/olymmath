A Fox and two Bear Cubs are dividing 100 candies. The Fox lays out the candies into three piles; who gets which pile is determined by a draw. The Fox knows that if the Bear Cubs get different numbers of candies, they will ask her to equalize their piles, and then she will take the excess for herself. After that, everyone eats the candies they have received.

a) Come up with a way for the Fox to distribute the candies into piles so that she can eat exactly 80 candies (no more, no less).

b) Can the Fox arrange it so that she ends up eating exactly 65 candies?

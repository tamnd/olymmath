Let positive integers $a, b$ both have exactly $m(m \geqslant 3)$ positive divisors, where $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \cdots, a_{m}$ is a permutation of all positive divisors of $a$. Is it possible that $a_{1}, a_{1}+a_{2}, a_{2}+a_{3}, \cdots, a_{m-1}+a_{m}$ is exactly a permutation of all positive divisors of $b$? Prove your conclusion.

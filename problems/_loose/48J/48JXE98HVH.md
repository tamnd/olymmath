In an arithmetic progression, the sum of the first $m$ terms is equal to the sum of the first $n$ terms ( $m \neq n$ ).

Prove that the sum of the first $m+n$ terms does not depend on $a_{1}$ (the first term) and $d$ (the common difference of the progression).

On a piece of paper, three positive numbers $x, y$ and 1 are written. In one move, it is allowed to write on the paper the sum or difference of any two already written numbers or to write the reciprocal of any of the already written numbers. Can we obtain on the paper after several moves:

a) the number $x^{2}$? b) the number $x y$?

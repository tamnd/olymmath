Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3} \in \mathbb{C}^{*}$ such that:

$\left|z_{1}\right|=\left|z_{2}\right|=\left|\frac{3 z_{3}-2 z_{1}}{2}\right|=\left|z_{3}-\frac{z_{2}}{3}\right|$

Show that: $ \operatorname{Re} \frac{z_{3}}{z_{1}}=\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Re} \frac{z_{3}}{z_{2}}$ where $\operatorname{Re}(a+b i)=a ; a, b \in \mathbb{R} ; i^{2}=-1$

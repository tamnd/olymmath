Given a sequence $a_0, a_1, a_2, ... , a_n$ , let its *arithmetic approximant* be the arithmetic sequence $b_0, b_1, ... , b_n$ that minimizes the quantity $\sum_{i=0}^{n}(b_i -a_i)^2$ , and denote this quantity the sequence's anti-arithmeticity. Denote the number of integer sequences whose arithmetic approximant is the sequence $4$ , $8$ , $12$ , $16$ and whose anti-arithmeticity is at most $20$ .

Given a square $A B C D$. With $\overline{A B}$ as radius, a circle is drawn around $A$. This circle intersects the diagonal $A C$ at $E$. The tangent to the circle at $E$ intersects the side $B C$ at $F$.

Prove that the segments $C E, E F$ and $F B$ are of equal length!

Let $m, n$ be positive integers, prove that there exists a constant $a>1$, independent of $m, n$, such that when $\frac{m}{n}<\sqrt{7}$, we have $7-\frac{m^{2}}{n^{2}} \geqslant \frac{a}{n^{2}}$, and find the maximum value of $a$.

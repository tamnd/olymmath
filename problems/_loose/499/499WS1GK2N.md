$T$ is the set of all integer points in the coordinate plane (points with both coordinates as integers are called integer points). If two integer points $(x, y),(u, v)$ satisfy $|x-u|+|y-v|=1$, then these two points are called adjacent points. Prove: there exists a set $S \leftrightarrows T$, such that for each point $P \in T$, exactly one of $P$ and its adjacent points belongs to $S$.

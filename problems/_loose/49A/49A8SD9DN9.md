For the set $A=\{1,2,3, \cdots, n\}$ and each of its non-empty subsets, define a unique "alternating sum" as follows: rearrange the subset in decreasing order, then starting from the largest number, alternately subtract or add the subsequent numbers to get the result. For example, the "alternating sum" of the set $\{1,2,4,7,10\}$ is $10-7+4-2+1=6$, the "alternating sum" of the set $\{7,10\}$ is $10-7=3$, and the "alternating sum" of the set $\{5\}$ is 5, and so on. Find the total of the "alternating sums" of all subsets of $A$.

Let $\varphi(k)$ denote the numbers of positive integers less than or equal to $k$ and relatively prime to $k$ . Prove that for some positive integer $n$ , $$ \varphi(2n-1) + \varphi(2n+1) < \frac{1}{1000} \varphi(2n). $$*Proposed by Evan Chen*

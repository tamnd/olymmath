Prove that $E, F, G, P, Q, N, K, L, M$ are concyclic, and its center $W$ lies on the Euler line passing through the orthocenter $X$, centroid $Y$, and circumcenter $Z$, and $X W: W Y: Y Z=3: 1: 2$.

Let L be any interior point of the shorter arc $C D$ of the circle circumscribed around the square $A B C D$. Denote K as the intersection of lines $A L$ and $C D$, M as the intersection of lines $A D$ and $C L$, and $N$ as the intersection of lines $M K$ and $B C$. Prove that points $B, L, M, N$ lie on the same circle. (J. Švrček)

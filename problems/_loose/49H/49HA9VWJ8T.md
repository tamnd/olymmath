There are two piles of 2002 balls each. Two players take turns making moves as follows: in each move, a player removes one pile and divides the other pile into two non-empty piles of their choice. A legal move is to remove one of the two piles of balls left by the previous player and to split the remaining pile into two non-empty piles of balls. The player who cannot make a legal move loses the game. Determine which player can definitely win and how.

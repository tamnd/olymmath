Let $P$ be a point inside triangle $ABC$ and let $R$ denote the circumradius of triangle $ABC$ . Prove that
$$ \frac{PA}{AB\cdot AC}+\frac{PB}{BC\cdot BA}+\frac{PC}{CA\cdot CB}\ge\frac{1}{R}.$$

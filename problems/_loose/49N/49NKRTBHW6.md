Given a polygon on a plane, non-convex and non-self-intersecting. D is the set of points belonging to those diagonals of the polygon that do not extend beyond its boundaries (i.e., lie entirely inside or partially inside and partially on the contour). The endpoints of these diagonals are also included in D. Prove that any two points in D can be connected by a broken line entirely belonging to D.

Let $A, B, C$ and $D$ be four points on a conic $\mathcal{E}$. We denote $P, l'$ the intersection of the tangents at $A$ and $B$ to $\mathcal{E}, Q$ the intersection of the tangents at $C$ and $D$ to $\mathcal{E}$. We also denote $T$ the intersection of the lines $(A C)$ and $(B D)$ as well as $S$ the intersection of the lines $(A D)$ and $(B C)$. Show that the points $P, Q, S$ and $T$ are collinear.

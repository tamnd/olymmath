Someone claims that the sum of squares of the following expressions

$$
1, p q+2, p q+p-2 q, 2 p q+p-2 q+1
$$

is equal to the sum of squares of the following four expressions:

$$
2, p q+p+1, p q-2 q+1, 2 p q+p-2 q
$$

Another person examined numerical examples and conjectures that the equality also holds for the sum of cubes and higher powers of these four expressions. What is the truth in this matter?

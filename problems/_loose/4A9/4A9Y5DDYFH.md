a) Consider the collinear points $A_{0}, A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{2015}$ (in this order), such that $A_{0} A_{1}=1 \text{ cm}, A_{1} A_{2}=2 \text{ cm}, \ldots, A_{2014} A_{2015}=2015 \text{ cm}$. Calculate the distance between the points $A_{1000}$ and $A_{2015}$.

b) Let $A, B, C, D, E$ and $F$ be six points, such that any three of the given points are non-collinear. We color each of the segments determined by them either orange or violet, at random. Prove that there exists a triangle with vertices in three of the six points, which has all sides of the same color.

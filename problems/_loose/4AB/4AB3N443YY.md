Place a chess piece in one of the squares of an $m \times n$ chessboard, two people take turns to move, each step can move the piece from one square to an adjacent square that shares a common edge, but a square that has been visited cannot be entered again, the one who has nowhere to go loses.
(1) If the chess piece starts in the bottom-left corner square, who has a winning strategy?
(2) If the chess piece starts in a square adjacent to the bottom-left corner square, who has a winning strategy?

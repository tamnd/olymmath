In the plane, there are two intersecting circles: $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$. Let $A$ be one of the intersection points. Two point masses: $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ move on $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively, at constant speeds in the same rotational direction. They start their motion from point $A$ at the same time, and after one complete revolution, they simultaneously return to point $A$.

Prove that there is a fixed point $P$ in the plane such that at any time during the motion, the following equality holds:

$$
P P_{1}=P P_{2}
$$

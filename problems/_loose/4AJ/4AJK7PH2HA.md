Determine the angle $\alpha$ if

$$
\operatorname{ctg} \alpha=2+\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c}, \operatorname{ctg} 2 \alpha=2+\sqrt{a}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are natural numbers not divisible by 4, and $\sqrt{a}, \sqrt{b c}$ are irrational numbers.

Given a quadratic trinomial $f(x)=a x^{2}+b x+c$ with all coefficients being positive, and $a+b+c=1$, prove that for any set of positive numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfying $x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{n}=1$, we have $f\left(x_{1}\right) f\left(x_{2}\right) \cdots f\left(x_{n}\right) \geqslant 1$.

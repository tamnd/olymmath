Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $\Gamma$ be its circumcircle. Let $P$ be the intersection point of the line $(BC)$ and the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $A$. Let $D$ and $E$ be the symmetrics of points $B$ and $A$ with respect to $P$.

Let $\omega_{1}$ be the circumcircle of triangle $DAC$ and let $\omega_{2}$ be the circumcircle of triangle $APB$. Denote by $F$ the intersection point of circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ other than $A$, and then denote by $G$ the intersection point, other than $F$, of circle $\omega_{1}$ with the line $(BF)$.

Prove that the lines $(BC)$ and $(EG)$ are parallel.

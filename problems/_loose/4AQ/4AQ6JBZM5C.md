A sports hall in the shape of a square with a side length of 100 meters is paved with square tiles 1 m $\times 1$ m. Two players take turns covering the floor of the sports hall with mats. Each mat covers two adjacent tiles on the floor. The player who starts the game places one mat on their turn, while the second player places three mats. The tiles on which the next mat is placed may have been covered by mats on previous turns (possibly, different tiles are covered by a different number of mats), but at no point in the game should any tile be covered by more than five mats. The player who cannot make a move loses. Prove that with correct play, the second player will win.

(S. Berlov)

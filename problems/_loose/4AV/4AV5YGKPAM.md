Piglet and Winnie-the-Pooh decided to eat a $7 \times 7$ square chocolate bar. They take turns eating pieces from it: Piglet eats $1 \times 1$ pieces, Winnie-the-Pooh eats $2 \times 1$ or $1 \times 2$ pieces (pieces can be eaten not necessarily from the edge). Piglet makes the first move. If before Winnie-the-Pooh's turn, there are no $2 \times 1$ or $1 \times 2$ pieces left in the chocolate bar, the entire remaining chocolate goes to Piglet. Who among the friends can eat more than half of the entire chocolate bar regardless of the actions of the second?

Ali-Baba and the 40 thieves decided to divide a treasure of 1987 gold coins in the following way: the first thief divides the entire treasure into two parts, then the second thief divides one of the parts into two parts, and so on. After the 40th division, the first thief chooses the largest of the parts, then the second thief chooses the largest of the remaining parts, and so on. The last, 41st part goes to Ali-Baba. For each of the 40 thieves, determine the maximum number of coins he can secure for himself regardless of the actions of the other thieves.

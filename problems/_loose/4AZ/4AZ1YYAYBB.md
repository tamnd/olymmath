$a, b, c$ - integers, and $(a, b)=1$. Let $\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ - some integer solution of the equation $a x+b y$ $=c$.

Prove that all solutions of this equation in integers are obtained by the formulas $x=x_{0}+k b, y=y_{0}-k a$, where $k$ - any integer.

For a point $P$ and a given triangle $A_{n} B_{n} C_{n}=H_{n}$, the pedal triangle of $P$ with respect to $H_{n}$ is the triangle $A_{n+1} B_{n+1} C_{n+1}=H_{n+1}$, whose consecutive vertices are the orthogonal projections of $P$ onto the lines $B_{n} C_{n}$, $C_{n} A_{n}$, and $A_{n} B_{n}$. Prove that starting from point $P$ and the triangle $A_{0} B_{0} C_{0}=H_{0}$, the pedal triangle $H_{3}$, formed through $H_{1}$ and $H_{2}$, is similar to $H_{0}$. (Of course, we assume that none of $H_{1}$, $H_{2}$, and $H_{3}$ are degenerate).

Can we state any further relationship between $H_{3}$ and $H_{0}$?

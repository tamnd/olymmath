Given $n$ distinct real numbers, the set of all their permutations is $A_{n}$. For $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right) \in A_{n}$, if there are exactly two distinct integers $i, j \in\{1,2, \cdots, n-1\}$ such that $a_{i}>a_{i+1}, a_{j}>a_{j+1}$ hold, then the permutation is called a "good permutation". Find the number of "good permutations" in $A_{n}$.

For the tetrahedron $P Q R S$, it is known that $P Q=4, S R=6, \angle Q R S=\angle P S R=50^{\circ}, \angle Q S R=$ $\angle P R S=40^{\circ}$. A sphere is circumscribed around the tetrahedron. Consider the set of all points on this sphere for which the sum of the spherical distances from these points to $P, Q, R, S$ is not less than $6 \pi$. What is the area of this set? The spherical distance between two points on a sphere is the length of the shortest arc of a great circle connecting these points.

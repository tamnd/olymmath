Try the substitution
$$
x=\tan \frac{B}{2} \tan \frac{C}{2}, y=\tan \frac{C}{2} \tan \frac{B}{2}, z=\tan \frac{A}{2} \tan \frac{B}{2},
$$

and appropriate trigonometric inequalities to generate the following algebraic inequalities $(x, y, z>0, x+y+z=1)$:
(1) $\sqrt{\frac{y z}{x}}+\sqrt{\frac{z x}{y}}+\sqrt{\frac{x y}{z}} \geqslant \sqrt{3}$;
(2) $\sqrt{\frac{x y}{z+x y}}+\sqrt{\frac{y z}{x+y z}}+\sqrt{\frac{z x}{y+z x}} \leqslant \frac{3}{2}$;
(3) $\frac{x}{x+y z}+\frac{y}{y+z x}+\frac{z}{z+x y} \leqslant \frac{9}{4}$;
(4) $\frac{x-y z}{x+y z}+\frac{y-z x}{y+z x}+\frac{z-x y}{z+x y} \leqslant \frac{3}{2}$;
(5) $\frac{x-y z}{x+y z} \cdot \frac{y-z x}{y+z x} \cdot \frac{z-x y}{z+x y} \leqslant \frac{1}{8}$.

A regular tetrahedron is to be divided into three tetrahedra of equal volume (which are not regular).

a) Give two possibilities for such a division!

b) Prove that the two divisions you have given are different! A division into three tetrahedra $T_{1}, T_{2}, T_{3}$ is considered different from a division into three other tetrahedra if they cannot be labeled as $T_{1}^{\prime}, T_{2}^{\prime}, T_{3}^{\prime}$ such that $T_{1} \cong T_{1}^{\prime}$, $T_{2} \cong T_{2}^{\prime}$, and $T_{3} \cong T_{3}^{\prime}$.

The vertices of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ are orthogonally projected onto two planes. Points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, $D_{1}$ and $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}, D_{2}$ are the projections of the corresponding vertices. 359 *. A schoolboy is practicing solving quadratic equations. Prove that this exercise cannot continue indefinitely. What is the maximum number of equations he might have to solve?

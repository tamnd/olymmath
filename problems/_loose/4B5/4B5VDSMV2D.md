In practice 762 (1963/2 62.p.), we examined the condition for the terms of the sequence defined by the formula

$$
a_{k}=\frac{c a_{k-1}+d}{e a_{k-1}+f}
$$

with the initial term $a_{1}$ and for $k=2,3,4 \ldots$ to repeat periodically in threes. Show that this repetition also occurs when $c=f=1, d=-e=\sqrt{3}$. Provide a trigonometric explanation for this case.

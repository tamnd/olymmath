100 prisoners were placed in jail. The guard told them:

"I will give you the evening to talk to each other, and then I will place each of you in separate cells, and you will not be able to communicate anymore. Sometimes I will take one of you to a room that has a lamp (which is initially off). When leaving the room, you can leave the lamp either on or off.

If at any point someone says to me that everyone has already been in the room, and is correct, then I will release all of you. If they are wrong, I will feed all of you to the crocodiles. Don't worry about anyone being forgotten 一 if you remain silent, everyone will eventually visit the room, and no one's visit will be the last."

Come up with a strategy that guarantees the prisoners' freedom.

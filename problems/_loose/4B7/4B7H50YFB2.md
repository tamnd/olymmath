Prove that if $b \neq c$, and

$$
\frac{a c-b^{2}}{a-2 b+c}=\frac{b d-c^{2}}{b-2 c+d}
$$

then each fraction is separately equal to the following fraction:

$$
\frac{a d-b c}{a-b-c+d}
$$

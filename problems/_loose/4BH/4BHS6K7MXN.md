Ivan the Tsarevich is battling the Serpent Gorynych on the Kalinov Bridge. The Serpent has 198 heads. With one swing of his sword, Ivan the Tsarevich can cut off five heads, but after that, the Serpent instantly grows new heads in a number equal to the remainder when the number of remaining heads is divided by 9. If the number of remaining heads is divisible by 9, no new heads grow. If the number of heads before the swing is five or fewer, Ivan the Tsarevich can kill the evil Serpent with one swing. How many sword swings must Ivan the Tsarevich make to defeat the Serpent Gorynych?

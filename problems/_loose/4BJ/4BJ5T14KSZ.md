Carnot's Theorem. Prove that for the perpendiculars dropped from points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ to the sides $B C$, $C A$ and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$ to intersect at one point, it is necessary and sufficient that $\left|A_{1} B\right|^{2}-\left|B C_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|C_{1} A\right|^{2}-\left|A B_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|B_{1} C\right|^{2}-\left|C A_{1}\right|^{2}=0$.

On the first day, $2^{n}$ schoolchildren played ping-pong in a "knockout" format: first, two played, then the winner played with the third, the winner of this pair played with the fourth, and so on until the last schoolchild (there are no draws in ping-pong). On the second day, the same schoolchildren played for the cup: first, they arbitrarily split into pairs and played, the losers were eliminated, and the winners again arbitrarily split into pairs and played, and so on. It turned out that the sets of pairs that played on the first and second days were the same (possibly with different winners). Find the largest possible value of $n$.

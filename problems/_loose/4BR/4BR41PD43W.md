Let $d$ be a line passing through $A$. The isogonal conjugate of $d$ is the reflection of $d$ with respect to the bisector of $\widehat{A}$. Show that three lines passing through $A$, $B$, and $C$ respectively are concurrent if and only if their isogonal conjugates (with respect to $A$, $B$, and $C$ respectively) are concurrent. If the intersection point of the isogonals has barycentric coordinates $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma)$, what are the barycentric coordinates of the intersection point of the three initial lines?

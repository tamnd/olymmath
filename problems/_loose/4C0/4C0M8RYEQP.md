116101 [L'Huilier's Problem [ Area of a Triangle (through semiperimeter and radius of the inscribed circle or

Let $r$ be the radius of the inscribed circle, and $r_{a}, r_{b}$, and $r_{c}$ be the radii of the excircles of triangle $A B C$, touching the sides $B C=a, A C=b, A B=c$ respectively; $p$ be the semiperimeter of triangle $A B C$, and $S$ be its area. Prove that

$$
\text { a) } \frac{1}{r}=\frac{1}{r_{a}}+\frac{1}{r_{b}}+\frac{1}{r_{c}} ; \text { b) } S=\sqrt{r r_{a} r_{b} r_{c}}
$$

Let's prove the following.

a) If a natural number can be written as the product of two consecutive natural numbers, then it cannot be written as a product of two factors that are second neighbors (i.e., their difference is 2).

b) The sum of the fourth powers of two consecutive natural numbers cannot be equal to the sum of the squares of two other consecutive natural numbers.

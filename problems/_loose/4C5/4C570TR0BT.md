Two mages are fighting each other. Initially, they both hover over the sea at a height of 100 meters. The mages take turns casting spells of the form "decrease the height of hovering over the sea by $a$ meters for oneself and by $b$ meters for the opponent," where $a, b$ are real numbers, $0<a<b$. The set of spells is the same for both mages, they can be used in any order and multiple times. A mage wins the duel if, after someone's move, their height above the sea is positive, while the opponent's is not. Does there exist such a set of spells that the second mage can guarantee a win (regardless of the first mage's actions), if the number of spells in the set is a) finite; b) infinite?

Es $\star \star$ Let point $P$ be a point inside $\triangle A B C$, $\alpha=\angle A P C-\angle A B C, \beta=\angle A P B-$ $\angle A C B$, prove:
(1) $P A \cdot B C=A B \cdot P C \cos \alpha+A C \cdot P B \cos \beta$;
(2) $P A \cdot B C \cos (\alpha-\beta)=A C \cdot P B \cos \alpha+A B \cdot P C \cos \beta$.

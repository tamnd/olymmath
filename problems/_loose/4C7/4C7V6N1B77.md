$\int 8 x^{3} d x$.

The integral of $8x^3$ with respect to $x$ is calculated as follows:

$$
\int 8 x^{3} d x = 8 \int x^{3} d x = 8 \left( \frac{x^{4}}{4} \right) + C = 2x^{4} + C
$$

Denote by $g_n$ the number of connected graphs of degree $n$ whose vertices are labeled with numbers $1,2,...,n$ . Prove that $g_n \ge (\frac{1}{2}).2^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}$ .**Note**:If you prove that for $c < \frac{1}{2}$ , $g_n \ge c.2^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}$ , you will earn some point!

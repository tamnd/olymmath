Let three real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy the system of equations $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=2, \\ \frac{1}{x^{2}}+\frac{1}{y^{2}}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}=1 .\end{array}\right.$ Then the value of $\frac{1}{x y}+\frac{1}{y z}+\frac{1}{z x}$ is
(A) 1 .
(B) $\sqrt{2}$.
(C) $\frac{3}{2}$.
(D) $\sqrt{3}$.
(Nanchang City, Jiangxi Province, Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

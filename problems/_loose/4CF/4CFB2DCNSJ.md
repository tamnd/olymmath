Let $v$ and $w$ be real numbers such that, for all real numbers $a$ and $b$ , the inequality $$(2^{a+b}+8)(3^a+3^b) \leq v(12^{a-1}+12^{b-1}-2^{a+b-1})+w$$ holds. Compute the smallest possible value of $128v^2+w^2$ .

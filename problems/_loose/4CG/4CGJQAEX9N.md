Let points $A^{*}, B^{*}, C^{*}, D^{*}$ be the images of points $A, B, C$, $D$ under inversion. Prove that:

a) $\frac{A C}{A D}: \frac{B C}{B D}=\frac{A^{*} C^{*}}{A^{*} D^{*}}: \frac{B^{*} C^{*}}{B^{*} D^{*}}$

b) $\angle(D A, A C)-\angle(D B, B C)=\angle\left(D^{*} B^{*}, B^{*} C^{*}\right)-\angle\left(D^{*} A^{*}, A^{*} C^{*}\right)$.

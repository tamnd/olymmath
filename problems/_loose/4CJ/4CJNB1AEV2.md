The 72nd problem, Given $2=p_{1}<p_{2}<p_{3}<\ldots$. is the sequence of prime numbers, for positive integers $k, n$, let $f(k, n)=$ $\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\left[n \sqrt{\frac{p_{k}}{p_{j}}}\right]$. Prove: For each positive integer $M$, there exist $k, n$ such that $f(k, n)=M$.

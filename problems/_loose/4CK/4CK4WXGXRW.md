Let $n$ be an integer greater than or equal to 2. There are $n$ people standing in a line, each of whom is either a knave (and always lies) or a knight (and always tells the truth). Each person, except the first, points to one of the people in front of them and states "This person is a knave" or "This person is a knight." Knowing that there are strictly more knaves than knights, prove that by observing the statements, it is possible to determine for each person whether they are a knave or a knight.

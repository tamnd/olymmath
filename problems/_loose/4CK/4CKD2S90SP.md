CCA's B building has 6 rooms on the second floor, labeled B201 to B206, as well as 8 rooms on the first floor, labeled B101 to B108. Annie is currently in room B205. Each minute, she chooses to stay or change floors with equal probability, and chooses a classroom on that floor to go to at random (she can stay in the classroom that she's already in). B104, B108, and B203 are the only rooms that have teachers who will scold her for randomly walking around during class time. The probability that she is first scolded in room B203 can be expressed as $\frac{p}{q}$. Compute $p+q$.

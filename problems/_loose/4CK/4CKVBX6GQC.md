Given positive numbers $a, b, c$ satisfying $a+b+c=3$. Prove:
$$
\sum \frac{1}{2 a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}} \leqslant \frac{3}{4},
$$

where, "$\sum$" denotes the cyclic sum.

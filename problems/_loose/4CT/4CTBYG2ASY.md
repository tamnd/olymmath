(1981 American High School Mathematics Exam Question) Alice, Bob, and Carl take turns rolling a die. Alice rolls first, Bob always rolls after Alice, and Carl always rolls after Bob. Find the probability that Carl is the first to roll a 6. (In each roll of the die, the probability of getting a 6 is $\frac{1}{6}$, and the rolls are independent of each other. )
A. $\frac{1}{3}$
B. $\frac{2}{9}$
C. $\frac{5}{18}$
D. $\frac{25}{91}$
E. $\frac{36}{91}$

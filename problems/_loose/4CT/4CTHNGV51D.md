A lucky number is one whose sum of digits is divisible by 7. For example, 7, 25, and 849 are lucky numbers. The smallest pair of lucky numbers is 7 and 16.

(a) Find eight consecutive numbers, of which two are lucky numbers.

(b) Find 12 consecutive numbers, such that none of them are lucky.

(c) Show that any sequence of 13 consecutive numbers contains at least one lucky number.

Let $S$ be a given set of real numbers such that:
i) $1 \in S$,
ii) for any $a, b \in S$ (not necessarily different), then $a-b \in S$,
iii) for $a \in S$, $a \neq 0$ then $\frac{1}{a} \in S$.
Prove that for any $a, b \in S$ then $a b \in S$.

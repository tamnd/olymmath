Given that $x, y, z$ are all positive numbers, prove that: $x(y+z-x)^{2}+y(z+x-y)^{2}+z(x+y-z)^{2} \geqslant$ $2 x y z\left(\frac{x}{y+z}+\frac{y}{z+x}+\frac{z}{x+y}\right)$, equality holds if and only if $x=y=z$.

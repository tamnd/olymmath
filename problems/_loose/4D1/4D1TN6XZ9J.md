In an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, we draw the altitudes, which intersect at point $M$. By bisecting the distances $M A, M B$, and $M C$, we obtain points $D, E, F$. Show that the triangles $A B C$ and $D E F$ are similar. Furthermore, draw lines through points $D, E$, and $F$ parallel to the opposite sides and show that 1) the resulting triangle $L P N$ is congruent to the original triangle; 2) point $M$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $L P N$; 3) $L, P$, and $N$ are the centers of the circles that pass through two vertices of the original triangle and point $M$.

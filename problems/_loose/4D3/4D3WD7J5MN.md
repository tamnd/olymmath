Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ inscribed in $\odot O, \angle ACB = 45^{\circ}, AD$ is the altitude, point $X$ lies on segment $AD$ internally, and satisfies $\angle XBC = 90^{\circ} - \angle ABC, AD, CX$ intersect $\odot O$ at points $M, N$ respectively, the tangent line through $M$ to $\odot O$ intersects $AN$ at point $P$. Prove: $P, B, O$ are collinear.
(2010, Iran Mathematical Olympiad)

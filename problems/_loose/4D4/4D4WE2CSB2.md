Consider two circles with the same center on the same plane, with radii $R$ and $r$ ($R>r$), respectively. Let $P$ be a fixed point on the circumference of the smaller circle. $B$ is a moving point on the circumference of the larger circle, and the line $B P$ intersects the larger circle at another point $C$. The line $l$ through point $P$ and perpendicular to $B P$ intersects the smaller circle at another point $A$ (if $l$ is tangent to the smaller circle at $P$, then $A=P$). Prove that the midpoint of segment $A B$ lies on a circle.

Show that, if $n \neq 2$ is a positive integer, that there are $n$ triangular numbers $a_1$ , $a_2$ , $\ldots$ , $a_n$ such that $ {\sum_{i=1}^n \frac1{a_i} = 1}$ (Recall that the $k^{th}$ triangular number is $\frac{k(k+1)}2$ ).

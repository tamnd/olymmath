There are three piles of stones. It is allowed to add to any of them as many stones as there are in the other two piles, or to throw out of any pile as many stones as there are in the other two piles. For example: $(12,3,5) \rightarrow(12$, 20, 5) (or (4, 3, 5)). Is it possible, starting with piles of 1993, 199, and 19, to make one of the piles empty?

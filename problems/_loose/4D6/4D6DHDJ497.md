Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB<AC$ , and let $\omega$ be an arbitrary circle passing through $B$ and $C$ . Denote by $E$ and $F$ the intersections of $\omega$ with $AB$ and $AC$ , respectively. Let $M$ and $N$ be the intersections of the perpendicular bisectors of segments $BF$ and $CE$ with side $BC$ , respectively. Let $P$ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of $MN$ and $EF$ . Prove that as $\omega$ varies, the point $P$ lies on a fixed line.

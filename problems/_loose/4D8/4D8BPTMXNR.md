Let $A$ be a positive constant, and let the area of the finite part enclosed by line $l$ and the hyperbola $C: x^{2}-y^{2}=2 (x>0)$ be $A$. Prove:
(1) The locus of the midpoint of the segment cut by the hyperbola $C$ on line $l$ is a hyperbola;
(2) Line $l$ is always tangent to the locus curve in (1).

In the field of real numbers, solve the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \operatorname{tg}^{2} x+2 \operatorname{cotg}^{2} 2 y=1, \\
& \operatorname{tg}^{2} y+2 \operatorname{cotg}^{2} 2 z=1, \\
& \operatorname{tg}^{2} z+2 \operatorname{cotg}^{2} 2 x=1
\end{aligned}
$$

(J. Švrček, P. Calábek)

In a competition, there are $a$ students and $b$ judges, where $b \geqslant 3$ is an odd integer. Each judge gives their judgment, pass or fail, on each student. Suppose that $k$ is an integer such that for every pair of judges, their verdicts coincide for at most $k$ students. Prove that:

$$
\frac{k}{a} \geqslant \frac{b-1}{2 b}
$$

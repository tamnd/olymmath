Let $ C$ be the circumference of a circle with radius 1. Take a point $ A$ on $ C$. Three points $ P,\ Q,\ R$ start from $ A$ at the time $ t = 0$ on $ C$ at fixed speed respectively, $ P,\ Q$ move in counterclockwise, $ R$ moves in clockwise during $ 0\leq t\leq 2\pi$ with speed of $ P,\ Q$ and $ R$, $ m,\ 1$ and $ 2$ respectively,or $ Q$ moves around $ C$ one time. Note that $ m$ is integer such that $ 1\leq m\leq 10$.
Determine all possible pairs of $ (m,\ t)$ such that $ \triangle{PQR}$ is a Rectangular Isoceles Triangle with hypotenuse $ PR$.

Let $P$ and $Q$ be points on the segments $[AA_1]$ and $[BB_1]$, respectively, such that the lines $(PQ)$ and $(AB)$ are parallel. Let $P_1$ be a point on the line $(PB_1)$ such that $B_1$ lies between the points $P$ and $P_1$ and such that $\widehat{PP_1C} = \widehat{BAC}$. Let $Q_1$ be a point on the line $(QC_1)$ such that $C_1$ lies between the points $Q$ and $Q_1$ and such that $\widehat{QQ_1C} = \widehat{ABC}$. Show that the points $P, Q, P_1$, and $Q_1$ are concyclic.

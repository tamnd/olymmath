Let $n$ be a positive integer, and positive real numbers $a_{i}, b_{i}$ $(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i}=1$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|a_{i}-b_{i}\right| \leqslant 2-\min _{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}\left\{\frac{a_{i}}{b_{i}}\right\}-\min _{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}\left\{\frac{b_{i}}{a_{i}}\right\}$.

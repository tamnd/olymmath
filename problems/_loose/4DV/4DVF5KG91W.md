Suppose $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ is a permutation of the numbers $1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove that if $n$ is odd, then the product $\left(a_{1}-1\right)\left(a_{2}-2\right) \cdots\left(a_{n}-n\right)$ is even.
(Hungarian Mathematical Olympiad, 1906)

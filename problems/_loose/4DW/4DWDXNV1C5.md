Prove that for an acute triangle $ABC$ the following equality holds:

$$
A H \cdot A H_{1} + B H \cdot B H_{2} + C H \cdot C H_{3} = \frac{1}{2} \left( A B^{2} + B C^{2} + C A^{2} \right)
$$

where $H, H_{1}, H_{2}, H_{3}$ are the orthocenter and the feet of the altitudes of the triangle. How does the formula change for an obtuse triangle?

Let $S$ be the set of all ordered triples $\left(a,b,c\right)$ of positive integers such that $\left(b-c\right)^2+\left(c-a\right)^2+\left(a-b\right)^2=2018$ and $a+b+c\leq M$ for some positive integer $M$. Given that $ \sum_{\left(a,b,c\right)\in S}a=k$, what is $$ \sum_{\left(a,b,c\right)\in S}a\left(a^2-bc\right)$$ in terms of $k$?

Write $N$ natural numbers $(n \geqslant 3)$ on a circle, such that the ratio of the sum of the two adjacent numbers to the number itself is a natural number for each number, and let the sum of all these ratios be $S_{n}$. Prove: $2 n \leqslant S_{n}<3 n$.

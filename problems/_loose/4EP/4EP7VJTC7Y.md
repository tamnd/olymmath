Given is a right-angled triangle $\triangle A B C$ with $A$ as the vertex of the right angle and with $\overline{A C}<\overline{A B}$ (1). The circle around $A$ with $\overline{A C}$ intersects $B C$ at another point $E$ besides $C$, and $E$ lies between $C$ and $B$ due to (1). The tangent to the mentioned circle at point $E$ intersects $A B$ at a point $D$, which lies between $A$ and $B$.

Prove that $\overline{E D}=\overline{D B}$ holds!

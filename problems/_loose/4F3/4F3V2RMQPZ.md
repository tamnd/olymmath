Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers such that $a + b + c = 1$ . If $n$ is a positive integer then prove that
$$ \frac{(3a)^n}{(b + 1)(c + 1)} + \frac{(3b)^n}{(c + 1)(a + 1)} + \frac{(3c)^n}{(a + 1)(b + 1)} \ge \frac{27}{16} \,. $$

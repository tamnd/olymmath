In a rectangular parallelepiped $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$, the lengths of the edges are known: $A B=a$, $A D=b, A A_{1}=c$.
a) Find the angle between the planes $B B_{1} D$ and $A B C_{1}$.

b) Find the angle between the planes $A B_{1} D_{1}$ and $A_{1} C_{1} D$.

c) Find the angle between the line $B D_{1}$ and the plane $A_{1} B D$.

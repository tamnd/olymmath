Inside a circle, there is a regular $N$-sided polygon. Each of its sides is extended in both directions until it intersects with the circle, obtaining two new segments located outside the polygon. Then, some of the $2N$ obtained segments are painted red, and the rest are painted blue. Prove that it is possible to color these segments such that the sum of the lengths of the red segments equals the sum of the lengths of the blue ones.

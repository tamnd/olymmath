Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ be a sequence of strictly positive integers such that for all $n \geqslant 1$,

$$
\left(a_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}}\right) \leqslant n^{2}+2021
$$

Show that the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ is constant.

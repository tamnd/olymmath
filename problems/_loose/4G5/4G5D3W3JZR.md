In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, $AB=a, BC=b, CD=c$, $DA=d, AC=e, BD=f, p=\frac{1}{2}(a+b+c+d)$, the area is $S$, and $AC$ intersects $BD$ at $P$ (Figure 4.5). Prove:
(1) $S \leqslant \frac{1}{2} e f \leqslant \frac{1}{2}(a c+b d)$;
(2) $S \leqslant \frac{1}{4}(a+c)(b+d) \leqslant \frac{p^{2}}{4}$.

The Non-existent Triangle Umbugio. With his characteristic persistence, Professor Euclid Paracelsus Bombast Umbugio is trying to prove the following theorem: if in triangle $ABC$ the orthocenter ${ }^{*} H$, the incenter $I$ of the inscribed circle, and the circumcenter $O$ of the circumscribed circle form an equilateral triangle, then

$$
\cos A+\cos B+\cos C=\frac{3}{2}
$$

Put an end to the professor's suffering by showing, first, that triangle $HIO$ cannot be equilateral and, second, that if the relation $\cos A+\cos B+\cos C=\frac{3}{2}$ holds, then triangle $HIO$ does not exist at all.

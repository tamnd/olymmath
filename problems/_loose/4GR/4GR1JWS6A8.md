In recent years, the volume of cash transactions using bank cards has significantly increased. Criminals take advantage of this by creating a copy of the magnetic strip when paying for goods and services in stores, installing skimming devices (overlaying card readers and PIN entry panels on ATMs) and shimming devices (installing flexible circuit boards inside ATMs through the card reader slot) on ATMs and payment terminals. Suppose your friend planned to withdraw some cash from a bank card. The nearest ATM was in the subway, it was rush hour, and there were many people around. After double-checking the 4-digit PIN, which he had written on the back of the card, the friend inserted it into the ATM. The card went in with difficulty, and he had to push it into the card reader slot, after which the ATM inexplicably turned off. A man standing nearby introduced himself as a bank employee, informed that the ATM was under technical maintenance, and asked to return in 15 minutes when the issue would be resolved. To pass the time, the friend went to the nearest store to grab a quick bite, and while standing in line, received an SMS that all the money had been withdrawn from his bank card.

a) Identify the mistakes your friend made.

b) Describe additional security measures that should be followed when paying with bank cards (e.g., in a cafe). (15 points)

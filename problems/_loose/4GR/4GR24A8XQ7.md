In the vertices of a regular pentagon-shaped area, we place columns of different heights, then connect the top of each column to the top of its two second-neighbors. In this case, from a top view, each connecting wire crosses two others. Can the heights of the columns be chosen in such a way that each wire is below one of the two crossings and above the other?

The figure of the rook threatens any square on the diagonal on which the rook stands on the chessboard. However, if there is a tower on some square of the diagonal, the rook no longer threatens the squares behind it. Determine the maximum number of rooks that can be placed together with four towers on an $8 \times 8$ chessboard so that the rooks do not threaten each other.

(Ján Mazák)

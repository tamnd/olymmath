Let the incenter of tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ be $I$, and let the area of $\triangle A_{k} I A_{j}$ be $S^{\prime}{ }_{k j}$, and the area of the face opposite vertex $A_{i}$ be $S_{i}$. Try to prove: $\sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} S_{k j}^{\prime} \leqslant \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4} \sum_{i=1}^{4} S_{i}$.

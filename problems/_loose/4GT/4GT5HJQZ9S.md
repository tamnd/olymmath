Two players take turns writing a digit from $0 \sim 9$ on a blackboard, arranging them from left to right. If, after a player writes a digit, the sequence of digits on the blackboard contains a single digit or a consecutive sequence of digits that can form a number divisible by 11, that player loses the game. Which player has a winning strategy?

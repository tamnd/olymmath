Two spheres $\alpha$ and $\beta$ touch the sphere $\omega$ at points $A$ and $B$. On the sphere $\alpha$, a point $M$ is taken, the line $M A$ intersects the sphere $\omega$ again at point $N$, the line $N B$ intersects the sphere $\beta$ again at point $K$. Find the geometric locus of such points $M$, for which the line $M K$ is tangent to the sphere $\beta$.

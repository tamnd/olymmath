Prove that for any positive real numbers $x$ and $y$, the inequality always holds:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{x}}{y^{6} \cdot \sqrt{y}}+\frac{\sqrt{y}}{x^{6} \cdot \sqrt{x}} \geq \frac{1}{x^{6}}+\frac{1}{y^{6}}
$$

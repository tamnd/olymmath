Let $n \geqslant 1$ be a natural number. Consider a parliament composed of $n$ deputies. We assume that each deputy has exactly three enemies. We assume that the enmity relation is symmetric (if a is an enemy of b, then b is an enemy of a). Show that it is possible to divide the parliament into two committees such that each deputy has at most one enemy in their committee.

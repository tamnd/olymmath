On a grid paper, a rectangle $A B C D$ is drawn, with its sides lying on the grid lines, and $A D$ is $k$ times greater than $A B$ ($k$ is an integer). Consider all possible paths that follow the grid lines and lead from $A$ to $C$ in the shortest way. Prove that among these paths, those in which the first segment lies on $A D$ are $k$ times more numerous than those in which the first segment lies on $A B$.

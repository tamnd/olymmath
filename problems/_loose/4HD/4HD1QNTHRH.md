Given the function
$$
f_{n}(x)=\frac{n x^{2}-x}{x^{2}+1}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) \text {. }
$$

Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be positive real numbers, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_{n}\left(x_{i}\right) \geqslant 0$.

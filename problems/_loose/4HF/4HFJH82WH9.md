Given the expression $A=x y+y z+z x$, where $x, y, z$ are integers. If the number $x$ is increased by 1, and the numbers $y$ and $z$ are decreased by 2, then the value of the expression $A$ does not change. Prove that the number $(-1) \cdot A-$ is the square of an integer.

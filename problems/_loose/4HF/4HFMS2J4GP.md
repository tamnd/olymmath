Let $a, b, c \in \mathbb{C}^{*}$ such that $|b|^{2}=3|a| \cdot|c|$ and arg $a$, arg $b$, arg $c$ are in arithmetic progression. If $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$ are the roots of the equation $a z^{2}+b z+c=0$, prove that the points $M_{1}\left(z_{1}\right), M_{2}\left(z_{2}\right)$ and $\mathrm{O}$ (the origin of the coordinate axes), are the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

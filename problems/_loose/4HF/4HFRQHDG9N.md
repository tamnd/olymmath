Let $n$ be a natural number greater than 1. Kostya has a device that, if given $2 n+1$ coins of different weights, will indicate which coin is the median among those placed. Baron Munchausen gave Kostya $4 n+1$ coins of different weights and said that one of them is the median by weight. How can Kostya, using the device no more than $n+2$ times, determine whether the baron is right? (K. Knop)

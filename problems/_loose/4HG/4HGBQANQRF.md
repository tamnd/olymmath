Let $ABCD$ be an circumscribed quadrilateral such that $m(\widehat{A})=m(\widehat{B})=120^\circ$, $m(\widehat{C})=30^\circ$, and $|BC|=2$. What is $|AD|$?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ \sqrt 3 - 1
\textbf{(B)}\ \sqrt 2 - 3
\textbf{(C)}\ \sqrt 6 - \sqrt 2
\textbf{(D)}\ 2 - \sqrt 2
\textbf{(E)}\ 3 - \sqrt 3
$

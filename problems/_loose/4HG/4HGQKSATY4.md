By the river, there lives a tribe of Mumbo-Jumbo. One day, with urgent news, a young warrior Mumbo and a wise shaman Yumbo set out simultaneously for the neighboring tribe. Mumbo ran at a speed of 11 km/h to the nearest raft storage and then sailed on a raft to the neighboring tribe. Meanwhile, Yumbo, without hurrying, walked at a speed of 6 km/h to another raft storage and then sailed to the neighboring tribe from there. In the end, Yumbo arrived before Mumbo. The river is straight, and the rafts float at the speed of the current. This speed is the same everywhere and is expressed as an integer km/h, not less than 6. What is the greatest possible value of this speed?

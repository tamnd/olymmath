Suppose a positive number $x$ exactly satisfies three of the following four conditions:
(甲) $|x-2.5|<0.5$.
(乙) $|x-3|<1$.
(丙) $|x-4|<1.5$.
(丁) $|x-2.5|>2$.
Then the condition that $x$ does not satisfy is
(A) 甲.
(B) 乙.
(C) 丙.
(D) 丁.
(E) Cannot be determined.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

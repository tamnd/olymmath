(This sub-question is worth 15 points) Given $x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}-3 x y z-3\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}-x y-y z-z x\right)=0$, where $x$, $y$, and $z$ are positive real numbers not all equal.

Prove: $\square$
(1) $x+y+z=3$;
(2) $x^{2}(1+y)+y^{2}(1+z)+z^{2}(1+x)>6$.

Let for non-zero numbers $a, b, c, x, y$ and $z$ the equalities $\mathrm{x}=\mathrm{bc}+\frac{1}{\mathrm{a}}, \mathrm{y}=\mathrm{ca}+\frac{1}{\mathrm{~b}}, \mathrm{z}=\mathrm{ab}+\frac{1}{\mathrm{c}}$ and $\mathrm{ax}+\mathrm{by}+\mathrm{cz}=1$ hold.

a) Calculate the product $abc$;

b) Prove that $a+b+c=\frac{1}{3 x}+\frac{1}{3 y}+\frac{1}{3 z}$.

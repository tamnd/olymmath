Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle with center $O$ . Let $q$ be the circle passing through $A$ , $O$ and $B$ . The lines $CA$ and $CB$ intersect $q$ at the points $D$ and $E$ (different from $A$ and $B$ ). Prove that the lines $CO$ and $DE$ are perpendicular to each other.

(S Markelov)

Circles $S_{1}, \ldots, S_{n}$ pass through the point $O$. A grasshopper jumps from a point $X_{i}$ on circle $S_{i}$ to a point $X_{i+1}$ on circle $S_{i+1}$ such that the line $X_{i} X_{i+1}$ passes through the point of intersection of circles $S_{i}$ and $S_{i+1}$, different from point $O$. Prove that after $n$ jumps (from $S_{1}$ to $S_{2}$, from $S_{2}$ to $S_{3}, \ldots$, from $S_{n}$ to $S_{1}$), the grasshopper will return to the initial point.

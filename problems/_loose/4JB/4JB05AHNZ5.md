A country has 2020 cities, and there are several one-way air routes between these cities. If a route starts from city $A$ and arrives at city $B$, then $A$ is called the "starting point", and $B$ is called the "destination". If for any 1009 cities, they have a unique common destination, prove: there must exist 1010 cities, such that by closing all routes of any one of these cities, the remaining 1009 cities still have a common destination.

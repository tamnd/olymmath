Let $f(x)=3 x-a x^{3}$,
$$
g(x)=b+3 x+c x^{2}+d x^{3},
$$

where $a, b, c, d \in \mathbf{R}$.
(1) Find the value of $a$ such that when $x \in[-1,1]$,
$$
|f(x)| \leqslant 1 \text{; }
$$
(2) Find the values of $b, c, d$ such that when $x \in[-1,1]$, $|g(x)| \leqslant 1$.

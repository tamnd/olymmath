Two different real numbers $a, b$ greater than 1 are given. Write down all possible orders of the values of the expressions

$$
1+a, 1+b, 1+\frac{a+b}{2}, \frac{a^{2}+b^{2}-2}{a+b-2}
$$

from the smallest to the largest.

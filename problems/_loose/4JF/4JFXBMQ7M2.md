Let $m$ be a real number, such that the roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ of the equation

$$
f(x)=x^{2}+(m-4) x+m^{2}-3 m+3=0
$$

are real numbers.

a) Find all values of $m$ for which $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}=6$.

b) Prove that

$$
1<\frac{m x_{1}^{2}}{1-x_{1}}+\frac{m x_{2}^{2}}{1-x_{2}}+8 \leq \frac{121}{9}
$$

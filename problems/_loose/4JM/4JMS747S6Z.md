Let $A, B, C$ be the centers of three mutually exclusive circles of equal radius. In the $\Delta ABC$, let the intersection points of the sides starting from $A$ with the first circle be $B_{1}, C_{1}$, those starting from $B$ with the second circle be $C_{2}, A_{2}$; and those starting from $C$ with the third circle be $A_{3}, B_{3}$. It is to be proven that the common points of $B_{1} C_{1}$ and $B C$, $C_{2} A_{2}$ and $C A$, $A_{3} B_{3}$ and $A B$ form a collinear set of points.

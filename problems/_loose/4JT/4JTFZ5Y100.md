The 84th problem: Define the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ as follows: $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2, a_{3}=3$, when $n \geq 4$, $a_{n}$ is the smallest positive integer not in $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n-1}\right\}$ such that $\left(a_{n-1}, a_{n}\right) \geq 3$ (the first few terms of this sequence are $1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12, 4, 8, \ldots$). Prove: Every positive integer appears exactly once in the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$.

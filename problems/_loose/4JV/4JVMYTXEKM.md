Expand the periodic functions into Fourier series:
1) $f(x)=x^{2}$ for $-\pi \leqslant x<\pi ; f(x)=f(x+2 \pi)$.

Using the obtained expansion, find the sum of the series:
a) $1-\frac{1}{2^{2}}+\frac{1}{3^{2}}-\frac{1}{4^{2}}+\ldots ;$ b) $1+\frac{1}{2^{2}}+\frac{1}{3^{2}}+\frac{1}{4^{2}}+\ldots$
2) $\varphi(x)=x^{2}$ for $0<x<\pi ; \varphi(x)=\varphi(x+\pi)$.
3) $u=\cos \frac{x}{2}$ for $0<x \leqslant 2 \pi ; u(x)=u(x+2 \pi)$.

On one side of a straight line $l$, draw a semicircle $r$. $C$ and $D$ are two points on $r$. The tangents to $r$ at $C$ and $D$ intersect $l$ at $B$ and $A$ respectively, and the center of the semicircle lies on the segment $B A$. $E$ is the intersection of segments $A C$ and $B D$, and $F$ is a point on $l$ such that $E F \perp l$. Prove that $E F$ bisects $\angle C F D$.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral in which the sides $(A D)$ and $(B C)$ are parallel, $A B = C D$ and $A D < B C$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$ and let $E$ be the point of intersection of the lines $(M D)$ and $(A C)$. Prove that the perimeter of the triangle $A M C$ is greater than or equal to the perimeter of the quadrilateral $A B M E$.

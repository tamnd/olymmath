Vanya then writes down on her notebook all the pairwise greatest common divisors among the numbers Sarji wrote on the blackboard. Shortly after, they realize that the numbers on the blackboard are all different from the numbers on the notebook. At most how many numbers could Sarji have written on the blackboard?

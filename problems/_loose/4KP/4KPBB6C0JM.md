**a)** Prove that there exist two functions $ f,g:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ having the properties:
$ \text{(i)} f\circ g=g\circ f $
$\text{(ii)} f\circ f=g\circ g $
$ \text{(iii)} f(x)\neq g(x), \forall x\in\mathbb{R} $

**b)** Show that if there are two functions $ f_1,g_1:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ with the properties $ \text{(i)} $ and $ \text{(iii)} $ from above, then $ \left( f_1\circ f_1\right)(x) \neq \left( g_1\circ g_1 \right)(x) , $ for all real numbers $ x. $

In a certain middle school, the number of students in each grade is odd, and each student has at least one best friend (best friends can be from different grades) and the friendship is mutual. During a study trip, each student chooses to go either to Paris or Rome. Prove: the students can be divided into two groups in such a way that:
（1）each student goes to the same city as their best friend;
(2) in each grade, the number of students going to Rome and the number of students going to Paris differ by 1.

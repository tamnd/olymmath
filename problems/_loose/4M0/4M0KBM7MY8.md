At the 3rd stage of the 15th Mathematics Olympiad, six Thälmann Pioneers, Anita, Bernd, Christine, Doris, Erich, and Fritz, each received a prize. Exactly two of them received full marks. In response to the question of which two Pioneers received full marks, the following five answers were given:

(1) Anita and Christine;

(2) Anita and Fritz;

(3) Bernd and Fritz;

(4) Anita and Doris;

(5) Bernd and Erich.

It was subsequently determined that in exactly one of these five answers, both statements are false, while in the other four, one statement is true and one is false.

Based on this finding, what are the names of the two prize winners who received full marks? Check whether this question can be answered uniquely from the given answers!

Let a cyclic pentagon $A B C D E$ be given. Let points $F, G, H$ and $I$ be the midpoints of segments $B C, C D, D E$ and $E A$, respectively. Let lines $F G$ and $H I$ intersect at point $J$, and lines $A C$ and $H I$ at point $K$. Prove that point $K$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle F C J$.

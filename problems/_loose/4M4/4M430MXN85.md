(2003 Anhui Province Competition Question) Let $a \in \mathbf{R}$, and the function $f(x)=a x^{2}+x-a(|x| \leqslant 1$ ).
(1) If $|a| \leqslant 1$, prove that: $|f(x)| \leqslant \frac{5}{4}$;
(2) Find the value of $a$ that makes the function $f(x)$ have a maximum value of $\frac{17}{8}$.

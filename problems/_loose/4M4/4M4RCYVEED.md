Ana, Beto, and Carolina are going to participate in the television show "Discover the Color of Your Hat". In the show, they stand in a circle and a blue or green hat will be placed on each of their heads. Each can see the hats of the others, but not the color of their own hat. Then, each of them writes down one of three options: "blue", "green", or "pass". If all those who wrote "blue" or "green" correctly guess the color of their hat, they win a brand new car. If any of them guess the color of their hat as "blue" or "green" and get it wrong, all three lose. If all of them write "pass", then all three also lose. It is worth noting that they cannot use signals and cannot see the papers of the other participants. The three meet to try to come up with a strategy. Carolina starts, "none of us should write 'pass', we should guess between 'blue' and 'green', because if we all pass, we will lose." Beto reacts by saying, "I disagree, it's better if only Ana guesses the color of her hat, while I and Carolina write 'pass'. In this case, the chance of winning will be higher." Ana speaks up, "I had an idea, if we use my strategy, the probability of winning the car will be $\frac{3}{4}$."

(a) Following Carolina's idea, what is the probability of winning the car?

(b) Changing to Beto's idea, what is the probability of winning the car?

(c) Give an example of a possible strategy from Ana that makes the probability of winning the car be $\frac{3}{4}$.

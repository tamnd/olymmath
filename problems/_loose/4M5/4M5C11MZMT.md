If $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{m}; B_{1}, B_{2}, \cdots, B_{m}$ are all subsets of $X=$ $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, and $A_{i} \cap B_{j}=\varnothing$ if and only if $i=j$. $\left|A_{i}\right|=a_{i},\left|B_{i}\right|=b_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, m)$, then
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{1}{C_{a_{i}+b_{t}}^{a_{i}}} \leqslant 1 .
$$

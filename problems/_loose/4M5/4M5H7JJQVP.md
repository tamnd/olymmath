In a bag, there are 8 balls, labeled from 1 to 8. After stirring these 8 balls sufficiently, two balls are drawn from the bag at a time. Let denote $ S$ the sum of the two balls.

(1) Find the probability such that $ S$ doesn't exceed in eight.

(2) Let $ f(n)$ be the probability such that $ S$ takes value $ n$. Find the value of $ n$ for which $ f(n)$ is maximal.

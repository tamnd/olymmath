Let $\{a_n\}$ be an arithmetic sequence with a non-zero common difference, satisfying $a_3 + a_6 = a_9$ and $a_5 + a_7^2 = 6a_9$. Let the sequence $\{b_n\}$ have the sum of its first $n$ terms denoted by $S_n$, and $4S_n + 2b_n = 3$. For any $i \in \mathbf{Z}_+$, insert $i$ numbers $x_{i1}, x_{i2}, \cdots, x_{ii}$ between $b_i$ and $b_{i+1}$, such that $b_i, x_{i1}, x_{i2}, \cdots, x_{ii}, b_{i+1}$ form an arithmetic sequence. Let $T_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{i} x_{ij}$. Does there exist positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that $T_n = \frac{a_{m+1}}{2a_m}$? If so, find all such pairs of positive integers $(m, n)$; if not, explain why.

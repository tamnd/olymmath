Consider a $2019 \times 2019$ grid. We denote by $C_{i, j}$ the cell in the i-th column and the j-th row for $1 \leqslant i, j \leqslant 2019$. A coloring of the grid is wonderful if there do not exist $1 \leqslant i<\ell \leqslant 2019$ and $1 \leqslant \mathrm{j}<\mathrm{k} \leqslant 2019$ such that the cells $\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{j}}, \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{k}}$ and $\mathrm{C}_{\ell, j}$ are of the same color. What is the smallest integer $\mathrm{N}$ such that there exists a wonderful coloring with $\mathrm{N}$ colors?

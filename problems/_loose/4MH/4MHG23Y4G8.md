The Emperor invited 2015 wizards to a festival, some of whom are good, and the rest are evil. A good wizard always tells the truth, while an evil one can say anything. The wizards know who is good and who is evil, but the Emperor does not. At the festival, the Emperor asks each wizard (in any order he chooses) a question that can be answered with "yes" or "no." After questioning all the wizards, the Emperor banishes one. The banished wizard exits through an enchanted door, and the Emperor learns whether he was good or evil. Then the Emperor asks each of the remaining wizards another question, banishes another one, and so on, until the Emperor decides to stop (he can do this after any question). Prove that the Emperor can banish all the evil wizards, removing no more than one good one in the process.

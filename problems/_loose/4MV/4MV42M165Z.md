The bisecting plane of a dihedral angle is a plane that divides this angle in half. Prove that

a) The three bisecting planes of the dihedral angles of a trihedral angle intersect along a single line. This line is called the bisector of the trihedral angle.

b) The four bisectors of the trihedral angles of a tetrahedron intersect at a single point (the center of the inscribed sphere).

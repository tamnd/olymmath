Let $A, B, C, D$ be points not lying in the same plane. Points $P, R, S, T$ are on the lines $AB, BC, CD, DA$ respectively, and point $O$ is such that

$$
O P^{2}-P A \cdot P B=O R^{2}-R B \cdot R C=O S^{2}-S C \cdot S D=O T^{2}-T D \cdot T A
$$

is a common non-negative value. It is to be proven that $P, R, S, T$ lie in the same plane.

\{re $\star \star$ Let $m$ be a positive integer. If there exists a positive integer $n$ such that $m$ can be expressed as $\frac{n}{d(n)}$, where $d(n)$ is the number of positive divisors of $n$ (including 1 and $n$), then $m$ is called a "good number". Prove:
(1) $1,2, \cdots, 17$ are all good numbers.
(2) 18 is not a good number.

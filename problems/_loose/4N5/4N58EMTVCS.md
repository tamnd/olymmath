A circle of radius $u_{a}$ is inscribed in angle $A$ of triangle $A B C$, and a circle of radius $u_{b}$ is inscribed in angle $B$; these circles touch each other externally. Prove that the radius of the circumscribed circle of the triangle with sides $a_{1}=\sqrt{u_{a} \operatorname{ctg}(\alpha / 2)}, b_{1}=\sqrt{u_{b} \operatorname{ctg}(\beta / 2)}$ and $c_{1}=\sqrt{c}$ is $\sqrt{p} / 2$, where $p$ is the semiperimeter of triangle $A B C$.

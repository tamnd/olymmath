(Topological sort, easy)

You are given a simple, directed, and acyclic graph $G(V, E)$. Show that there exists a numbering of the vertices such that if there is an arc from vertex $u$ to vertex $v$, the number of $u$ is greater than the number of $v$. Such a numbering is called a topological sort of $G$.

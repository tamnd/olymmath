Does there exist $m+n\left(m 、 n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right.$, $m \neq n)$ positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$, such that for any positive integer $k$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{1}^{k}+a_{2}^{k}+\cdots+a_{m}^{k}-b_{1}^{k}-b_{2}^{k}-\cdots-b_{n}^{k}}{k} \in \mathbf{Z} ?
$$

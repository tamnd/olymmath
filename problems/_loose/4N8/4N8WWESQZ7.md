Two painters are painting 100 sections of a fence surrounding a villa, alternating work every other day. Each person paints one section of the fence per working day, and can paint it either red or green. The first painter is colorblind and cannot distinguish between colors, but he can remember the places and colors he has painted, and can also see the places the second painter has painted, though not the colors. The first painter wants to maximize the number of red-green alternations on the fence, regardless of how the second painter works. How many red-green alternations can he achieve at most?

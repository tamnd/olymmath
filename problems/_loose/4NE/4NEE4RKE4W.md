(1) Find the analytical expression of $f(x)$ and the equation of the curve $C$;
(2) Let $S_{n}=\frac{a_{1}}{3!}+\frac{a_{2}}{4!}+\frac{a_{3}}{5!}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n}}{(n+2)!}$, for all $n \in \mathbf{N}^{\cdot}$, if $S_{n}>m$ always holds, find the maximum value of the natural number $m$.

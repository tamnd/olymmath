The fiftieth question, given a positive integer $n \geq 3$, let $S$ be a finite set of points in the plane, $|S|=n$, with no two points collinear and no four points concyclic. If each point can be colored either red or white, such that there exists a circle with all points inside it being white and all points outside it being red, then this coloring is called "proper." How many proper coloring schemes are there?

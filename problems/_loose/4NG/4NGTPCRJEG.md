Mom brought 56 strawberries and 39 raspberries to the bowl and took them to Ema, who was reading. Ema clarified her reading by snacking, and she did so by taking two random pieces of fruit at a time:

- If she drew two raspberries, she traded them with Mom for one strawberry and returned it to the bowl.
- If she drew two strawberries, she ate one and returned the other to the bowl.
- If she drew one strawberry and one raspberry, she ate the strawberry and returned the raspberry to the bowl. She continued snacking this way for a while until only one piece of fruit was left in the bowl. Decide (and explain) whether it was a strawberry or a raspberry.

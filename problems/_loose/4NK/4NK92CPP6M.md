Given a rectangle $A B C D$, let $P$ be a point on the side $C D$. The line $A P$ intersects the line $B C$ at point $T$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the side $B C$, and it is known that $\widehat{A P M}=2 \widehat{A T C}$. Knowing that the area of triangle $C P T$ is $10 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, what is the area of rectangle $A B C D$?
(A) $90 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$
(B) $120 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$
(C) $60 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$
(D) $80 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$
(E) $160 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$

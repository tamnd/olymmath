You can name the two dice numbers rolled by someone without having seen them if you perform the following calculation steps (1) to (4) and only let the final result after step (4) be told to you:

(1) The number rolled with one die is to be doubled.

(2) Add 5 to this.

(3) Multiply the resulting sum by 5.

(4) Add the number rolled with the other die to the product.

If you then subtract 25 from the result of step (4), you get the number whose one digit represents the number of the one die and whose other digit represents the number of the other die.

a) Verify this with a self-chosen example!

b) Show that this is true for any possible roll with two dice!

Given a triangle with side lengths being rational numbers. Prove that the following are rational numbers: a) the ratio $R / r$, where $R$ and $r$ are the radii of the circumscribed and inscribed circles; b) the value $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} \sin \frac{\beta}{2} \sin \frac{\chi}{2}$, where $\alpha, \beta, \chi$ are the angles of the triangle.

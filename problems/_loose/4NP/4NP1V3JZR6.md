Does there exist an integer $n>1$ satisfying the inequality

$$
[\sqrt{n-2}+2 \sqrt{n+2}]<[\sqrt{9 n+6}] ?
$$

(Here $[x]$ denotes the integer part of the number $x$, that is, the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.)

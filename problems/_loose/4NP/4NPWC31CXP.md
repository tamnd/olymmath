Given two squares with respectively parallel sides. Determine the geometric locus of points $M$ such that for any point $P$ from the first square, there exists a point $Q$ from the second such that the triangle $M P Q$ is equilateral. Let the sides of the first square be $a$, and the second be $b$. For what ratio between $a$ and $b$ is the sought geometric locus of points non-empty?

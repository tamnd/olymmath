If positive integers $a, b, c$ satisfy $a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$, and $a$ is a prime number, then the two numbers $b, c$
(A) are both odd.
(B) are both even.
(C) one is odd and one is even.
(D) are both composite.
(1st "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

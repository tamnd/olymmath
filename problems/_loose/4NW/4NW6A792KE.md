C12 (15-1, Czechoslovakia) $O$ is a point on the line $g$, $\overrightarrow{O P_{1}}, \overrightarrow{O P_{2}}, \cdots$, $\overrightarrow{O P}_{n}$ are all unit length vectors, where all points $P_{i}$ are in the same plane through $g$ and on the same side of $g$. Prove that if $n$ is odd, then
$$
\left|\overrightarrow{O P_{1}}+\overrightarrow{O P_{2}}+\cdots+\overrightarrow{O P_{n}}\right| \geqslant 1,
$$

where $|\overrightarrow{O M}|$ denotes the length of the vector $\overrightarrow{O M}$.

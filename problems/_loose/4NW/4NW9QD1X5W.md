A billiard is given in the form of a regular 1998-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{1998}$. A ball is released from the midpoint of the side $A_{1} A_{2}$, which, after reflecting sequentially from the sides $A_{2} A_{3}, A_{3} A_{4}, \ldots, A_{1998} A_{1}$ (according to the law "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection"), returns to the initial point. Prove that the trajectory of the ball is a regular 1998-gon.

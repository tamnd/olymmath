Brothers Janko and Peter sold a herd of 25 cows at the fair. For each cow, they received as many gold coins as there were cows in the herd. With the proceeds, they bought a herd of sheep, paying 20 gold coins for each sheep. They had just enough gold coins left to buy one lamb. They then divided the livestock so that each received an equal number of animals. The younger Peter was disadvantaged in this, as he received the lamb, which cost less than a sheep.

A How many gold coins did they receive for the herd of cows?

B How many gold coins did the lamb cost?

C How many gold coins should Janko pay Peter so that the wealth they earned from the herd of cows would be fairly divided?

A finite number of points in the plane are colored in four colors, with points of each color present. No three points lie on the same line. We call a triangle interesting if it has the following properties:

(1) its vertices are points colored in three different colors;

(2) there are no colored points inside the triangle.

Prove that there exist three interesting triangles (which may intersect).

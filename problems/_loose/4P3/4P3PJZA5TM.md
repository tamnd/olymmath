Let $M=\left\{a \mid a=x^{2}-y^{2}, x, y \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$. Then for any integer $n$, among the numbers of the form $4 n, 4 n+1, 4 n+2, 4 n+3$, the number that is not an element of the set $M$ is ( ).
(A) $4 n$
(B) $4 n+1$
(C) $4 n+2$
(D) $4 n+3$

Given a sphere $G$ and a circle $k$ inside it such that the orthogonal projection of $G$'s center onto the plane of $k$ lies on $k$. Furthermore, we know that there are two spheres that fit on $k$ and are tangent to $G$. Show that the sum of the radii of these two latter spheres is equal to the radius of $G$.

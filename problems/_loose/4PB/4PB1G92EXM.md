Let $\mathrm{n} \geqslant 3$ be an integer. We place $\mathrm{n}$ points on a circle. Taking turns, Noémie and Paul must draw a triangle whose vertices are among the points that are not the vertices of a triangle already drawn and whose sides do not intersect the sides of the triangles already drawn. The first player who can no longer play loses. If Noémie starts, which of the two players has a winning strategy?

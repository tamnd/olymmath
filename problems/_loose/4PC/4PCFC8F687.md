Given real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy $y>2 z>4 x$ and
$$
\begin{array}{l}
2\left(x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}\right)+15\left(x y^{2}+y z^{2}+z x^{2}\right) \\
>16\left(x^{2} y+y^{2} z+z^{2} x\right)+2 x y z .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: $4 x+y>4 z$.

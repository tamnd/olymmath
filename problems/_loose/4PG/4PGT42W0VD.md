On the shorter arc CD of the circle circumscribed around the rectangle ABCD, choose a point $P$. The feet of the perpendiculars from point $P$ to the lines $A B, A C$, and $B D$ are denoted as $K, L$, and $M$ respectively. Show that the angle $L K M$ has a size of $45^{\circ}$ if and only if $A B C D$ is a square.

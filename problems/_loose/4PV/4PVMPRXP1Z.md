Let $k_{0}$ be a unit semicircle over the diameter $A B$, and $k_{1}$ be a circle with radius $r_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ that touches both $k_{0}$ and $A B$. The circle $k_{n+1}$ with radius $r_{n+1}$ touches $k_{n}$, $k_{0}$, and $A B$. Prove:

a) For every $n \in\{2,3, \ldots\}$, $\frac{1}{r_{n+1}}+\frac{1}{r_{n-1}}=\frac{6}{r_{n}}-4$.

b) $\frac{1}{r_{n}}$ is either a square of an even natural number or twice the square of an odd natural number.

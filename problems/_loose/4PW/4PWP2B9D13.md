In a football tournament, 10 teams participated, each playing against every other team exactly once. For a win, a team gets 3 points, for a loss 0 points, and in the case of a draw, each team gets 1 point. At the end, the total number of points for each team was calculated. Let $M$ be the highest of these numbers, and $m$ be the lowest of these numbers.

a) If the total number of points for all teams is 98, what is the maximum value that $M$ can have?

b) If the total number of points for all teams is 98 and $M=19$, what can $m$ be?

Certain squares of an $\mathrm{n} \times \mathrm{n}$ board are colored black and the rest white. Every white square shares a side with a black square. Every pair of black squares can be joined by chain of black squares, so that consecutive members of the chain share a side. Show that there are at least $\left(n^{2}-2\right) / 3$ black squares.

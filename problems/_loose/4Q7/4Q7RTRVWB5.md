Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ with diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersecting at point $P$, the midpoints of sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$ are $E$, $F$, $G$, and $H$ respectively. The circumcenters of $\triangle PHE$, $\triangle PEF$, $\triangle PFG$, and $\triangle PGH$ are $O_1$, $O_2$, $O_3$, and $O_4$ respectively. Prove that the necessary and sufficient condition for $O_1$, $O_2$, $O_3$, and $O_4$ to be concyclic is that $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ are concyclic.

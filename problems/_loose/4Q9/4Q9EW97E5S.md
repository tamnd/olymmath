Let $a$ , $b$ , $c$ be the distinct roots of the polynomial $P(x) = x^3 - 10x^2 + x - 2015$ .
The cubic polynomial $Q(x)$ is monic and has distinct roots $bc-a^2$ , $ca-b^2$ , $ab-c^2$ .
What is the sum of the coefficients of $Q$ ?

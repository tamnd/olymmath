[7] Two non-intersecting wooden circles of the same size, one gray and one black, are glued to a plane. A wooden triangle is given, one side of which is gray and the other is black. It is moved so that the circles are outside the triangle, with the gray side touching the gray circle and the black side touching the black circle (the contact does not occur at the vertices). Prove that the line containing the bisector of the angle between the gray and black sides always passes through the same point on the plane.

E. Bakayev, P. Kozhevnikov, V. Rastorguyev

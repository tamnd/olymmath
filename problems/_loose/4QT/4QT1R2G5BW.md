\section*{Problem $3-340943=341042$}

On the side $AB$ of the square $ABCD$, a point $X \neq A$ is chosen. The square is then divided into four regions by the segments $AC$ and $X D$.

Determine all possibilities for choosing $X$ such that there exist natural numbers $p, q$, and $r$ for which the areas of these regions are in the ratio $1: p: q: r$ in some order!

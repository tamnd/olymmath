Musin O. Given a polygon, a line $l$ and a point $P$ on the line $l$ in general position (that is, all lines containing the sides of the polygon intersect $l$ at different points, distinct from $P$). Mark those vertices of the polygon for each of which the lines on which the sides emanating from it lie intersect $l$ on different sides of the point $P$. Prove that the point $P$ lies inside the polygon if and only if an odd number of vertices are marked on each side of $l$.

The smallest positive odd integer $ n $ that makes the product $ 2^{\frac{1}{7}} \cdot 2^{\frac{3}{7}} \cdots \cdots \cdot 2^{\frac{2 n+1}{7}} $ greater than 1000 is (in the product, the denominators of the exponents are all 7, and the numerators are consecutive odd integers from 1 to $ 2 n+1 $).
(A) 7.
(B) 9.
(C) 11.
(D) 17.
(E) 19.
(27th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1976)

Do there exist six points $A_1 \dots A_6$ in general position in the space, such that the triangles $A_1A_2A_3$ and $A_4A_5A_6$ are linked, and two triangles corresponding to any other dividing of these points into two triplets are not linked. *Two triangles on the space are linked if the outline of one triangle meets the inside of the second one at a unique point.*

*Proposed by E. Alkin and A. Skopenkov*

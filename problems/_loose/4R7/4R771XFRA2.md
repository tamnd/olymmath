Prove that for every acute triangle $ABC$ the following statement holds:

If $a, b, c$ are the side lengths, $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angle measures, $F$ is the area, $D$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $AB$, and $H$ is the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$, then for the segment lengths $\overline{CH}$ and $\overline{HD}$ the equation

$$
\overline{C H} \cdot \overline{H D}=\frac{a^{2} \cdot b^{2} \cdot c^{2} \cdot \cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta \cdot \cos \gamma}{4 F^{2}}
$$

holds.

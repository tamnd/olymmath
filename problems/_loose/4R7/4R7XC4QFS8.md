From the first $k$ prime numbers $2,3,5, \ldots, p_{k}(k>5)$, all possible products are formed, in which each number appears no more than once (for example, $3 \cdot 5,3 \cdot 7 \cdot \ldots \cdot p_{k}, 11$, etc.). Denote the sum of all such numbers by $S$. Prove that $S+1$ can be factored into a product of more than $2 k$ prime factors.

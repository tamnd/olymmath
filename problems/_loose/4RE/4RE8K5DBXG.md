We placed dominoes on an infinite square grid (possibly infinitely many) such that each of them covers two adjacent squares, and the dominoes do not touch each other, even at the corners. Prove that in this case, the uncovered part of the grid can be completely filled with $2 \times 1$ dominoes without gaps.

Let $n \geqslant 2$ be a natural number and let $\left(p_{1}, \ldots, p_{n}\right)$ be some permutation of the set $\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$. Show that

$$
\frac{1}{p_{1}+p_{2}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}+p_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{p_{k}+p_{k+1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{p_{n-1}+p_{n}}>\frac{n-1}{n+2}
$$

A complex number whose real and imaginary parts are both integers is called a "Gaussian integer". If the polynomial with integer coefficients
$$
f(z)=z^{n}+c_{n-1} z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_{2} z^{2}+3 c_{1} z+50
$$

has all distinct complex roots that are Gaussian integers, then $f(z)$ is called a "good polynomial". Find the number of such good polynomials.

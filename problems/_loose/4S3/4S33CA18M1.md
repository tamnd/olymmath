A company during its vacation managed to organize a table tennis match so that they played one match each day. They also played one game each day on each of the chess sets found in the resort. On the last day of the vacation, they realized that there were no two days when the pairing of chess players was the same, but the next day they could only write down a pairing that had already occurred during the vacation. (In this, they did not consider who played with which color pieces.) How many members were in the company and how long did the vacation last?

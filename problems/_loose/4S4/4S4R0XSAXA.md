People are asked "Do you think that the new president will be better than the most recent one?" Suppose $a$ people say "better", $ b$ say "the same" and $c$ "worse". Sociologists then calculate two measures of "social optimism": $m =a + \frac{b}{2}$ and $n = a - c$ . Suppose exactly $100$ people respond to this survey and it turns out that $m = 40$ . Find $n$ .

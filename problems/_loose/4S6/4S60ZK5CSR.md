A boat's speed in still water is $15 \mathrm{mph}$ (miles per hour), and the speed of a stream's current is $5 \mathrm{mph}$. If the boat makes a round trip over a certain distance, the ratio of the boat's average speed for the round trip to its speed in still water is
(A) $\frac{5}{4}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{1}$.
(C) $\frac{8}{9}$.
(D) $\frac{7}{8}$.
(E) $\frac{9}{8}$.
(5th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1954)

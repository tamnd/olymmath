The number $\frac{20}{21}$ is to be decomposed into two summands such that

a) the two summands are fractions with the same denominator, different from 21, and with different numerators,

b) the two summands are fractions with the same numerator and with different denominators.

In each fraction that appears as a summand, the numerator and the denominator should be natural numbers that are coprime to each other.

For a) and b), provide an example of such a decomposition, and show that it has all the required properties!

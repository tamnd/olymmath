Prove that the following two hypotheses are equivalent:

(i) there are infinitely many pairs of prime numbers with a difference of 2;

(ii) there are infinitely many natural numbers that cannot be represented in the form $6 u v \pm u \pm v$ for any natural numbers $u$, $v$ and any choice of signs.

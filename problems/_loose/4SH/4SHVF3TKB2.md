Show that there are infinitely many functions $f : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ such that for all $x, y, z \in \mathbb{Z}$ satisfying $xy +yz +zx = -1$ we have that
$$ f(x)^2 +f(y)^2 +f(z)^2 -f(x)f(y)f(z) = 4. $$

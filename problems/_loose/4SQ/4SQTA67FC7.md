Trigonometric inequality. Prove that for any set of real numbers $\left\{T_{i}\right\}$, where $i=1,2, \ldots, n$,

$$
\sum_{k, j=1}^{n} \cos \left(T_{k}-T_{j}\right) \geqslant 0
$$

Let $f: \mathbb R \to \mathbb R,g: \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$ and $\varphi: \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$ be three ascendant functions such that
$$f(x) \leq g(x) \leq \varphi(x) \forall x \in \mathbb R.$$
Prove that
$$f(f(x)) \leq g(g(x)) \leq \varphi(\varphi(x)) \forall x \in \mathbb R.$$

*Note. The function is $k(x)$ ascendant if for every $ x,y \in D_k, x \leq {y}$ we have $g(x)\leq{g(y)}$ .*

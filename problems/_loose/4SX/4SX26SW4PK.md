For a given number $\alpha{}$ let $f_\alpha$ be a function defined as $$f_\alpha(x)=\left\lfloor\alpha x+\frac{1}{2}\right\rfloor.$$Let $\alpha>1$ and $\beta=1/\alpha$ . Prove that for any natural $n{}$ the relation $f_\beta(f_\alpha(n))=n$ holds.

On December 25, 1988, January 1, 1989, and then on July 14 of the same year ${ }^{1}$, three assassination attempts were made on the President of France, which by a miracle did not lead to a tragic end. One of them was carried out by Corsican separatists, another by Bretons, and finally, the last one by Basques. However, the police did not know exactly which terrorist organization committed each of the three attempts. The International Center for Crime Investigation, to which a request was made, provided the following information: "The Christmas attempt, but not the one that occurred on New Year's Day, was carried out by the Basques. As for the July 14 attempt, it was not carried out by the Bretons."

Assuming that one of these statements is true and two are false, would you be willing to help the police determine the organizers of each of the three attempts?[^4]

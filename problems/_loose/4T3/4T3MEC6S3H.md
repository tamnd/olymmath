A schoolboy during the school year must solve exactly 25 problems every 7 consecutive days. The time required to solve one problem (any problem) does not change throughout the day, but changes throughout the school year according to a known law to the schoolboy and is always less than 45 minutes. The schoolboy wants to spend the least amount of time in total solving problems. Prove that for this, he can choose a certain day of the week and solve 25 problems on that day (every week).

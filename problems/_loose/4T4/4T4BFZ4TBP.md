To any quadrilateral $ABCD$, we assign the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $BCD$, $CDA$, $DAB$, $ABC$. Prove that if the vertices of a convex quadrilateral $Q$ do not lie on a circle, then
a) the four points assigned to quadrilateral $Q$ in the above manner are the vertices of a convex quadrilateral. Denote this quadrilateral by $t(Q)$,
b) the vertices of quadrilateral $t(Q)$ do not lie on a circle,
c) quadrilaterals $Q$ and $t(t(Q))$ are similar.

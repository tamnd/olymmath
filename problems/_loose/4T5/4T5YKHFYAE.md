A merchant has 6 barrels with capacities of 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, and 31 liters. Five of them are full of wine and only one is full of beer. The merchant keeps the beer barrel for himself and sells all the wine barrels to two different people, without dividing their contents. If one of the two buyers has bought exactly twice the amount of wine that the other has bought, how many liters does the beer barrel contain?
(A) 16
(B) 18
(C) 19
(D) 20
(E) 31.

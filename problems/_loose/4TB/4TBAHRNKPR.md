If a positive integer has eight positive divisors, and the sum of these eight positive divisors is 3240, then this positive integer is called a "good number". For example, 2006 is a good number, because the sum of its positive divisors $1, 2, 17, 34, 59, 118, 1003, 2006$ is 3240. Find the smallest good number.

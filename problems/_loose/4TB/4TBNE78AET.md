Find the point $P$ inside the triangle $A B C$ for which
$$
\frac{B C}{P D}+\frac{C A}{P E}+\frac{A B}{P F}
$$
is minimal, where $P D, P E, P F$ are the perpendiculars from $P$ to $B C$, $C A, A B$ respectively.

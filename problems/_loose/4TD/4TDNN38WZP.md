If $A, B, C, D$ represent four points in space, $AB$ represents the distance between points $A$ and $B$, $AC$ represents the distance between points $A$ and $C$, $\cdots$. Prove: $AC^{2}+BD^{2}+AD^{2}+BC^{2} \geqslant AB^{2}+CD^{2}$.
(4th American High School Mathematics Examination)

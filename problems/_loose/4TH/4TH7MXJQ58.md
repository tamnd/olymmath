Let $n$ and $q$ be two natural numbers, $n \geq 2, q \geq 2$ and $q \not \equiv 1(\bmod 4)$, and let $K$ be a finite field with exactly $q$ elements. Show that for any element $a$ in $K$, there exist $x$ and $y$ in $K$ such that $a=x^{2^{n}}+y^{2^{n}}$. (Any finite field is commutative.)

Let $(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots)$ be an arithmetic sequence. Denote by $s_{i}$ the sum of the first $i$ terms of this sequence, by $s_{j}$ the sum of the first $j$ terms, and by $s_{k}$ the sum of the first $k$ terms. Prove that the value of the expression

$$
\frac{s_{i}}{i}(j-k)+\frac{s_{j}}{j}(k-i)+\frac{s_{k}}{k}(i-j)
$$

is independent of the choice of numbers $i, j$, and $k$, as well as of the sequence.

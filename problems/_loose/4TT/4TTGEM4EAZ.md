We are placing red and blue balls on the circle. In the beginning there are exactly two red balls on the circle. We are allowed to perform the following operations:
i) add one red ball and change the colour of its neighbouring two balls (red to blue and vice versa);
ii) remove one red ball and change the colour of its neighbouring balls.
Is it possible that after a finite number of operations we obtain

a) $2013$ red and $2013$ blue balls;
b) exactly two blue balls? (Kvant 8/1981)

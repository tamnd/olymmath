a) Guess that the nine-point circles of triangles $A B C$ and $D B C$ belong to the same sphere if and only if $B C \perp A D$.

b) Prove that for an orthocentric tetrahedron, the nine-point circles of all faces belong to the same sphere (the 24-point sphere).

c) Prove that if $A D \perp B C$, then the sphere containing the nine-point circles of triangles $A B C$ and $D B C$, and the sphere containing the nine-point circles of triangles $A B D$ and $C B D$, intersect along a circle lying in a plane that bisects the common perpendicular to $B C$ and $A D$ and is perpendicular to it.

Note. The definition of the nine-point circle can be found in Prasolov, 10.2 or Sharigin, 11.160.

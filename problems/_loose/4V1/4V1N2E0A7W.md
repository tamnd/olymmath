From a chessboard of size $13 \times 13$, two opposite corner cells have been cut out. On the remaining part of the board, several cells have been marked. Prove that it is possible to place chess kings on the marked cells such that the total number of kings does not exceed 47, and they attack all the empty marked cells. Recall that a chess king attacks all cells adjacent to it vertically, horizontally, and diagonally. (S. Berlov)

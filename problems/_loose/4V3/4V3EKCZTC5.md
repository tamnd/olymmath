Quadratic function $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ satisfies that: $(1)\forall x\in\mathbb{R},f(x-4)=f(2-x),f(x)\geq x$ ; $(2)\forall x\in(0,2),f(x)\leq\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right)^2$ ; $(3)\min\limits_{x\in\mathbb{R}}f(x)=0$ Find the maximum of $m(m>1)$ , satisfying:
There exists $t\in\mathbb{R}$ , as long as $x\in[1,m]$ , then $f(x+t)\leq x$ .

(Stefan Leopoldseder). Let $k$ be a circle with radius $r$ and $AB$ a chord of $k$ with $\overline{A B}>r$. Furthermore, let $S$ be the point on the chord $AB$ such that $\overline{A S}=r$. The perpendicular bisector of $BS$ intersects the circle $k$ at points $C$ and $D$. The line through the points $D$ and $S$ intersects $k$ at another point $E$.

Prove that the triangle $CSE$ is equilateral.

These circles touch side $BC$ at points $K_{1}, K_{2}$, and $K$ respectively, with $B K_{1}=4, C K_{2}=8$, and $B C=18$.

a) Find the length of the segment $C K$.

b) Let the circle with center $O_{1}$ touch side $AB$ at point $K_{3}$. Find the angle $ABC$, if it is known that point $O_{1}$ is the center of the circle circumscribed around triangle $O K_{1} K_{3}$.

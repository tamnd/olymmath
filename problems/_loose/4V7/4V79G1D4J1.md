a) Find the area of trapezoid $A B C D$.

b) Calculate the distance from point $A$ to line $B C$.

c) If $A D \cap B C=\{P\}$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $(A B)$, and $P M \cap A C=\{S\}$, determine the ratio of the areas of triangles $A S M$ and ASP.

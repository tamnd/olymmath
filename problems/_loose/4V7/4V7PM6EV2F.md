In a graph with 400 vertices, for any edge $A B$, let's call the set of all edges emanating from vertices $A$ and $B$ (including the edge $A B$ itself) a "catshark". Each edge in the graph is labeled with either 1 or -1. It is known that the sum of the numbers on the edges of any catshark is greater than or equal to 1. Prove that the sum of the numbers on all the edges of the graph is not less than -10000.

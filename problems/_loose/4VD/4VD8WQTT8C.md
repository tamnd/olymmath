The "cryptogram" represents the task of replacing the letters with digits so that a correctly calculated addition problem results.

It should also be noted that the rule must be observed that the digit zero must not appear as the leading digit (for $M, R$, and $K$).

a) Provide a solution!

b) Prove that there are at least 15 solutions, none of which are the same!

\section*{Hints:}

1. Two solutions are called exactly the same if and only if in one of these solutions each letter is replaced by the same digit as in the other of these solutions.

2. The similarity of the letter $O$ to the digit 0 (zero) should have no significance; i.e., the letter $O$ may also be replaced by a digit other than zero.

We need to create a convex test object under the following requirements:

a) the object is bounded by 6 plane faces;

b) it has two planes of symmetry;

c) two adjacent sides of one face are 10 and 20 units;

d) the two opposite sides of the faces connected to this face are 9 units;

e) two adjacent sides of another face are 8 and 4 units.

What is the largest volume that such an object can occupy?

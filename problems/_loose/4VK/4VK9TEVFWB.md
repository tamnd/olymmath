Determine the minimum of the expression

$$
\frac{2}{|a-b|}+\frac{2}{|b-c|}+\frac{2}{|c-a|}+\frac{5}{\sqrt{a b+b c+c a}}
$$

under the conditions that $a b+b c+c a>0, a+b+c=1$ and $a, b, c$ are distinct.

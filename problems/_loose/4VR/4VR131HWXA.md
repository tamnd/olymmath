Prove that for any natural number $n$ there exist $n$ consecutive composite numbers.

This is also one of the theorems of number theory. If you think about it, we are faced with an amazing statement: after all, $n$ can be very large, for example, equal to $10^{20}$, and yet there will be $10^{20}$ composite numbers immediately following each other, i.e., there exist intervals of arbitrarily large length that do not contain a single prime number.

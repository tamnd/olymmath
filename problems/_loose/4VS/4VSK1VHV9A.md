Let $O$ be the center of a regular tetrahedron. From an arbitrary point $M$, located on one of the faces of the tetrahedron, perpendiculars are dropped to the other three faces. $K, L, N$ are the bases of these perpendiculars. Prove that the line $O M$ passes through the centroid of the triangle $K L N$.

Caroline and Daphné are playing the following game. They have 2022 cards, each with one white side and one black side. Initially, their cards are lined up from left to right, each showing its white side. Then, taking turns starting with Caroline, they perform an operation of the following type: the player whose turn it is chooses 100 consecutive cards, the leftmost of which shows its white side, and flips these 100 cards. The first player who cannot make a move, if this ever happens, loses the game, and their opponent wins.

1. Prove that the game will necessarily end after a finite number of moves.
2. Determine which of the two players has a winning strategy.

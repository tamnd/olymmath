Find all sequences of natural numbers ( $a_{n}$ ) such that the following two conditions are satisfied:

a) for any $n a_{n} \leqslant n \sqrt{n}$;

b) for any different $m$ and $n$ the difference $a_{m}-a_{n}$ is divisible by $m-n$.

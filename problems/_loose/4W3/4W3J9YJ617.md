In the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $B C$, and $N$ is the midpoint of side $C D$. The lines $A M$ and $B N$ intersect at point $P$, and we know that

$$
\frac{P M}{A M}=\frac{1}{5} \text { and } \frac{B P}{B N}=\frac{2}{5}
$$

What can we say about the quadrilateral $A B C D$?

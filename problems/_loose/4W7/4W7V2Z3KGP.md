Given $a_{k} \geqslant 0, k=1,2, \cdots, n$, and it is agreed that $a_{n+1}=a_{1}$, prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{k}^{2}-a_{k} a_{k+1}+a_{k+1}^{2}} \geqslant \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k} .
$$

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with all angles acute and $A B \neq A C$. The circle with diameter $[B C]$ intersects the sides $[A B]$ and $[A C]$ at $M$ and $N$, respectively. Let $\mathrm{O}$ be the midpoint of side $[B C]$. The angle bisectors of $\overline{B A C}$ and $\overline{M O N}$ intersect at $R$. Show that the circumcircles of triangles $B M R$ and $C N R$ intersect at a point on the side $[B C]$.

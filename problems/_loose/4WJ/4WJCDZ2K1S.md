A circle is written with 299 zeros and one one. The following moves are allowed:

- simultaneously subtract from each number its two neighbors;
- choose two numbers between which there are exactly two numbers and increase or decrease both by 1.

Can it be achieved by a finite sequence of allowed moves that in the circle there are

(a) two consecutive ones and 298 zeros?

(b) three consecutive ones and 297 zeros?

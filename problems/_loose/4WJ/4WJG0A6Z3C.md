For example, $33 n(>2)$ players participate in a round-robin table tennis tournament. Let $W_{r}$ and $L_{r}$ represent the number of wins and losses of the $r$-th player $P_{r}$, respectively. Prove:
$$
W_{1}^{2}+W_{2}^{2}+\cdots+W_{n}^{2}=L_{1}^{2}+L_{2}^{2}+\cdots+L_{n}^{2} .
$$

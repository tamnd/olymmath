Every time a knight speaks, the statement they make is true; every time a knave speaks, the statement they make is false. One of them makes the following statement: "to my right sits a knight and to my left sits a knave." The person to the right of this person states: "to my left sits a knight and to my right sits a knave." The person to the right of this one claims: "to my right sits a knight and to my left sits a knave." And so on, the statements alternate, until the eighth person, who states: "to my left sits a knight and to my right sits a knave." It can be concluded that, among the 8 people present, the total number of knights...

(A) could be 2 or 4, but not 0, 6, or 8

(B) could be 2, 4, or 6, but not 0 or 8

(C) could be 0, 2, or 4, but not 6 or 8

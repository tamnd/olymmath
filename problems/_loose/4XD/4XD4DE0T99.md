If in a convex $n$-sided polygon no three diagonals are concurrent, find the number of regions into which the interior of the $n$-sided polygon is divided by these diagonals.

The method of successive elimination, also known as the Bonsa method, is commonly used by graph theorist Bonsa. This approach considers the problem from the opposite angle, i.e., gradually removing all the diagonals, one at a time, while analyzing the reduction in regions during this process.

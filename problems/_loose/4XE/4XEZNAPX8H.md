The plane is given by the equation $A x+B y+C z+D=0$, and the numbers $A, B, C$ and $D$ are non-zero. Prove that then the equation of the plane can be written in the form $\frac{\underline{x}}{\boldsymbol{p}}+\frac{\underline{y}}{\underline{q}}+\underline{\underline{x}}=1$, where $P(0 ; 0 ; p), Q(0 ; q ; 0)$ and $R(0 ; 0 ; r)$ are the points of intersection of the plane with the coordinate axes.

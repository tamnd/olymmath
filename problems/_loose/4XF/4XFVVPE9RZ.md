Let circle $O_{1}$ and circle $O_{2}$ intersect at points $A, B$, and the radii of the two circles are $R$ and $r$, respectively. The external common tangent of the two circles is $M N$ ($M, N$ are the points of tangency). Let the circumradii of $\triangle A M N$ and $\triangle B M N$ be $a$ and $b$, respectively. Prove that $R+r \geqslant a+b$, and determine the necessary and sufficient conditions for equality.

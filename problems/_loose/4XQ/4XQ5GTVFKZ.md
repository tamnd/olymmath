One thousand numbers are written in a circle. Petya calculated the absolute differences of adjacent numbers, Vasya - the absolute differences of numbers standing one apart, and Tolya - the absolute differences of numbers standing two apart. It is known that any of Petya's numbers is at least twice as large as any of Vasya's. Prove that any of Tolya's numbers is not less than any of Vasya's. (I. Bogdanov)

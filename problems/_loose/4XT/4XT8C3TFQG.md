If all vertices of a polygon are colored with the same color, then the polygon is called "monochromatic". Given that every point in a plane is colored either red or blue. Prove: either there exists a monochromatic equilateral triangle with side length 2, or there exists a monochromatic equilateral triangle with side length $\sqrt{3}$, or there exists a monochromatic rhombus with side length 1. ${ }^{[1]}$
(2014, China Hong Kong Mathematical Olympiad)

Points $D, E$, and $F$ are given in order on the sides $BC, AC$, and $AB$ such that quadrilateral $AEDF$ is a parallelogram. The angle bisector of $\angle EDF$ and line $MD$ intersect line $EF$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that points $B, C, P$, and $Q$ lie on the same circle.

Let $\Gamma$ and $\Gamma^{\prime}$ be two circles tangent at $A$, with $\Gamma^{\prime}$ inside $\Gamma$. Let $D$ be a point on the circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ other than $A$, and let $M$ and $N$ be the points of intersection of the tangent to the circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ at $D$ with the circle $\Gamma$. Show that: $\widehat{N A D}=\widehat{M A D}$.

Prove the inequality

$$
\begin{gathered}
\left(a_{1} b_{1} c_{1} + a_{2} b_{2} c_{2} + \ldots + a_{n} b_{n} c_{n}\right) \leqslant \left(a_{1}^{3} + a_{2}^{3} + \right. \\
\left. + \ldots + a_{n}^{3}\right)\left(b_{1}^{3} + b_{2}^{3} + \ldots + b_{n}^{3}\right)\left(c_{1}^{3} + c_{2}^{3} + \ldots + c_{n}^{3}\right)
\end{gathered}
$$

where $a_{k}, b_{k}, c_{k}$ are positive numbers.

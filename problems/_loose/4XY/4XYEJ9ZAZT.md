A $2 a$-sided square, from each of its vertices as the center, we draw quarter-circle arcs within the square with radius $a$. In the quadrilateral thus formed by the curved lines, we construct a square whose sides are tangents to the aforementioned arcs. The area of this square depends on the direction of its sides. Investigate this accordingly and also illustrate the variation in the area of the square!

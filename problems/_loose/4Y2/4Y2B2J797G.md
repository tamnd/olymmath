In trapezoid $ABCD$, the ratio of the two bases $DC: AB=1: 3$. Extend the non-parallel sides $AD, BC$ to intersect at $P$. Draw $n$ line segments $X_{i} Y_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ such that $P$ is the midpoint of $X_{i} Y_{i}$. Take the midpoints $M_{i}$ of $AX_{i}$ and $N_{i}$ of $BY_{i}$. Prove that the $n$ lines $M_{i} N_{i}$ are concurrent.

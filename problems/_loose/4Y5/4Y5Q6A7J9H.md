Given the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the length of edge $AB$ is $a$, and the length of edge $CD$ is $b$. The distance between the lines containing edges $AB$ and $CD$ is $d$, and the (one of the) angles between them is $\omega$. The tetrahedron is divided into two parts by a plane $\varepsilon$ that is parallel to edges $AB$ and $CD$. What is the ratio of the volumes of these two parts, given that the distance from line $AB$ to plane $\varepsilon$ is $k$ times the distance from line $CD$ to plane $\varepsilon$?

(International Mathematical Olympiad problem, 1965. CMO6513)

In an isosceles trapezoid, the lengths of the parallel sides are $a$ and $b$, and its height is $m$.

a) Construct the point $P$ on the axis of symmetry of the trapezoid from which the legs are seen at a right angle.

b) Calculate the distance of point $P$ from one of the parallel sides.

c) What is the condition for the existence of point $P$?

The 663rd exercise and the 1063-1064th problems are from the tasks of the 2nd International Mathematical Olympiad for Secondary School Students held in Sinaia (Román N. K.).

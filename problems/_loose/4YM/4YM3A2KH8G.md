The sequence $a_0,a_1,a_2,...$ is defined such that $a_0 = 2+ \sqrt3$, $a_1 =\sqrt{5-2\sqrt5}$, and
$$a_n a_{n-1}a_{n-2} - a_n + a_{n-1} + a_{n-2} = 0.$$
Find the least positive integer $n$ such that $a_n = 1$.

In a boxing tournament with $m$ participants, each pair of participants can compete in at most 1 match. After several matches, the organizers decide to group the participants. Is it true that for any natural number $n, 1<n \leqslant m$, all participants can be divided into $n$ groups such that for each participant, the number of opponents he has already competed against within his group does not exceed $\frac{1}{n}$ of the total number of opponents he has competed against?

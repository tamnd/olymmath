Let $O$ be the center of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$. Draw $n$ radii $r_{1}, r_{2}, \cdots, r_{n}$ from $O$, with each adjacent pair of radii forming an angle of $\frac{2 \pi}{n}$. Prove that: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{r^{2}}=\frac{n}{2}\left(\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}\right)$.

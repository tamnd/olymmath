Seven skiers with numbers $1,2, \ldots, 7$ left the start one after another and completed the distance - each at their own constant speed. It turned out that each skier was involved in overtakes exactly twice. (In each overtake, exactly two skiers participate - the one who overtakes and the one who is overtaken.) At the end of the race, a protocol must be compiled, consisting of the numbers of the skiers in the order of their finishing. Prove that in a race with the described properties, no more than two different protocols can result.

(C. Volchonkov)

In triangle $ABC$, the third vertex of the equilateral triangle constructed on side $BC$ is $D$. On the rays $DB$ and $DC$ (from $D$), we measure the sum of side $AB$ and the corresponding altitude $CC_1$, with the endpoints being $B'$ and $C'$. On the ray $B'C'$ (from $B'$), we measure $AB$, with the endpoint being $D'$. Let the intersection of $DD'$ and $BC$ be $K$, the intersection of the line through $K$ parallel to $AB$ with $AC$ be $L$, and the projection of $K$ onto $AB$ be $M$. Prove that $KL = KM$.

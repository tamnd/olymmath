A cycle of three conferences had constant success, that is, in each session there was the same number of participants. However, half of those who attended the first one did not return; one-third of those who attended the second conference attended only that one, and one-quarter of those who attended the third did not attend either the first or the second. Knowing that there were 300 registered and that each one attended at least one conference, determine:

a) How many people attended each conference?

b) How many people attended all three conferences?

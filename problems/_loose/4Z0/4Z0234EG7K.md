Let $a, b, c$ be real numbers such that $1 < b \leq c^{2} \leq a^{10}$, and

$$
\log _{a} b+2 \log _{b} c+5 \log _{c} a=12
$$

Show that

$$
2 \log _{a} c+5 \log _{c} b+10 \log _{b} a \geq 21
$$

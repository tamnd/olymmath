A trapezoid is divided into four triangles by its two diagonals. If $A, B$ represent the areas of the triangles formed by the two bases and the diagonals, then the area of the trapezoid is
(A) $2(A+B)$.
(B) $(\sqrt{A}+\sqrt{B})^{2}$.
(C) $A+B+\sqrt{A B}$.
(D) $A+B+\frac{A B}{A+B}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics Correspondence Competition, 1984)

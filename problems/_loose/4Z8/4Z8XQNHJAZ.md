(Balkaniad, 84). Prove that for any positive numbers $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}(n \geqslant 2)$, the sum of which is 1, the following inequality holds:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{\alpha_{i}}{2-\alpha_{i}} \geqslant \frac{n}{2 n-1}
$$

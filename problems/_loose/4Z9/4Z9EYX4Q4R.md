Consider $2011$ nonzero integer numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2011}$. It appears that the sum of each of them with the product of all the others is negative. Let us partition these $2011$ numbers into two nonempty groups and find the product of the numbers in each group. Prove that the sum of these two products is also negative.

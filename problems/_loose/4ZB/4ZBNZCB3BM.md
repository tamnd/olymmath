Given a triangle $ABC$ and a point $M$ inside it, such that the rays $BM$ and $CM$ divide $\measuredangle ABC$ and $\measuredangle BCA$, respectively, into two equal parts. Through the point $M$, a line $p$ is drawn parallel to the side $BC$. The line $p$ intersects the sides $AB$ and $AC$ at points $P$ and $T$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\overline{BP} + \overline{CT} = \overline{PT}
$$

Let's call a trapezoid with bases 1 and 3 a "boat," which is obtained by gluing two triangular half-cells to the opposite sides of a unit square. In a $100 \times 100$ square, an invisible boat is placed (it can be rotated, it does not go beyond the boundaries of the square, its middle cell is entirely on one of the cells of the square). With one shot, you can cover any triangular half-cell. If the shot intersects the interior of the boat (i.e., the intersection of the triangular shot with the boat has a non-zero area), the boat is considered sunk. What is the minimum number of shots sufficient to definitely sink the boat?

(S. Berlov, N. Vlasova)

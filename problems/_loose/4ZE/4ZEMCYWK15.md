Let $n(n \geqslant 4)$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ be on a plane, and no three points are collinear. Each point is connected by line segments to at least 3 other points. Prove: Among these $n$ points, there exist $2 k$ distinct points $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{2 k}(k>1)$, such that $x_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant 2 k-1)$ is connected by a line segment to $x_{t+1}$, and $x_{2 k}$ is connected by a line segment to $x_{1}$.

Let the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+y^{2}=1(a>0)$.
(1) Given $a>0$, let $P$ be a moving point on the ellipse $C$ in the first quadrant. Draw the tangent line to the ellipse $C$ through point $P$, intersecting the two coordinate axes at points $A$ and $B$. When $|A B|$ is minimized, find the equation of the tangent line.
(2) Let the tangent line obtained in (1) be $l$, and the ellipse $C$ is symmetric about $l$ to get the ellipse $C^{\prime}$. If the ellipse $C^{\prime}$ intersects the $x$-axis, find the range of values for $a$.

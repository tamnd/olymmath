On the plane, there are $n$ vectors, each of length 1. The sum of all $n$ vectors is the zero vector. Prove that the vectors can be numbered in such a way that for all $k=1,2, \ldots$ ..., n the following condition is satisfied: the sum of the first $k$ vectors has a length of no more than 2.

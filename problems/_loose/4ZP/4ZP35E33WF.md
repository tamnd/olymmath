Prove that for every positive numbers $x, y, z$ the following inequality holds:
$$\sqrt{4x^2 + 4x(y + z) + (y - z)^2} <\sqrt{4y^2 + 4y(z + x) + (z - x)^2}+\sqrt{4z^2 + 4z(x + y) + (x - y)^2}.$$

On an island where knights and liars live, several (more than two) people gathered around a round table. It is known that there were both knights and liars at the table, and each said such a phrase: "Only one of my two neighbors is a knight." Who is more at the table: knights or liars, and by how many times? (Knights always tell the truth, liars always lie; and everyone knows who is who).

Prove that if in the sequence $\varnothing^{0}$ any three consecutive terms $a_{n-1}, a_{n}, a_{n+1}$ are chosen, the product of the outer terms is taken and the square of the middle term is subtracted from it, the result will be 1 or -1. Prove that for any Fibonacci sequence (not necessarily starting with the numbers 0,1) the expression $u_{n-1} u_{n+1}-u_{n}^{2}$ has the same absolute value for any $n$.

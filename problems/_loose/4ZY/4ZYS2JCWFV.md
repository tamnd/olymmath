a) Prove that for any real numbers $x, y, z, t$ the following equality holds:

$$
(x z+y t)^{2}+(x t-y z)^{2}=\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)\left(z^{2}+t^{2}\right)
$$

b) The positive rational numbers $a, b, c, d$ satisfy simultaneously the equalities:

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}=9, c^{2}+d^{2}=16, a c+b d=12
$$

Show that the number $\frac{a \sqrt{3}+b}{c \sqrt{3}+d}$ is rational.

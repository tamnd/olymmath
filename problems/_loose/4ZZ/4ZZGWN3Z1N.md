A circle inscribed in quadrilateral $ABCD$ touches its sides $DA, AB, BC$ and $CD$ at points $K, L, M$ and $N$ respectively. Let $S1, S2, S3$ and $S4$ be the circles inscribed in triangles $AKL, BLM, CMN$ and $DNK$ respectively. Common tangents, different from the sides of quadrilateral $ABCD$, are drawn to circles $S1$ and $S2, S2$ and $S3, S3$ and $S4, S4$ and $S1$. Prove that the quadrilateral formed by these four tangents is a rhombus.

Given are spheres with radii $R$ and $r$ ( $R<r$ ). The distance between the centers of these spheres is $c$, where $R-r<c<R+r$. Express the volume of the cone circumscribed around these spheres as a function of $R, d=R-r+c$. Prove that the ratio of the volume of this cone to the larger sphere is greater than or equal to 2. Determine the surface area of the cone for which this ratio is equal to 2.

For $k, n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, let the finite sequence $\left\{a_{k}\right\}$ have $n$ terms, where $a_{k} \leqslant m, a_{k}, m \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$. Determine the maximum value of the number of terms $n$ such that the sequence $\left\{a_{k}\right\}$ satisfies:
(1) For any term $a_{k}$, if $a_{k-1}$ and $a_{k+1}$ exist, then $a_{k-1} \neq a_{k+1}$;
(2) There do not exist positive integers $i_{1}<i_{2}<i_{3}<i_{4}$ such that $a_{i_{1}}=a_{i_{3}} \neq a_{i_{2}}=a_{i_{4}}$.

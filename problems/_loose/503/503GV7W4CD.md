Let $A$ be a square matrix of order 2 with real elements and $A^{t}$ the transpose matrix

(i) Show that there exists $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $\operatorname{det}\left(A+x \cdot A^{t}\right)=\left(x^{2}+1\right) \cdot \operatorname{det} A+\alpha \cdot x, \forall x \in \mathbb{R}$.

(ii) Given that $\operatorname{det}\left(A+A^{t}\right)=8$ and $\operatorname{det}\left(A+2 \cdot A^{t}\right)=27$, calculate $\operatorname{det}(A)$.

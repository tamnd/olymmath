Given an equilateral triangle $A B C$. Point $K$ divides side $A C$ in the ratio $2: 1$, and point $M$ divides side $A B$ in the ratio $1: 2$ (in both cases, counting from vertex $A$): Prove that the length of segment $K M$ is equal to the radius of the circumscribed circle around triangle $\cdot A B C$.

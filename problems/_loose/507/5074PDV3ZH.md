(Austria-Poland, 79). For any values of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}$, solve the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{n}=a \\
x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\ldots+x_{n}^{2}=a^{2} \\
\cdot \cdot \cdot \cdot \cdot \cdot \cdot \\
x_{1}^{n}+x_{2}^{n}+\ldots+x_{n}^{n}=a^{n}
\end{array}\right.
$$

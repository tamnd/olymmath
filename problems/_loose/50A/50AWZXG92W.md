A regular triangle ($n$-gon) is rotated about its center by an angle less than $120^{\circ}\left(\frac{1}{n} \cdot 360^{\circ}\right)$. Prove that the sides of the given triangle ($n$-gon) and the sides of the rotated triangle ($n$-gon) are the sides of a regular hexagon ($2n$-gon), into which a circle can be inscribed. (Such $2n$-gons are called semiregular.)

In a circle with center $O$ and radius $a$, $AB$ is a diameter, the extended chord $AD$ intersects the tangent at $B$ at $C, E$ is a point on $AC$ such that $AE=DC$, let $x$ and $y$ be the distances from $E$ to the tangent at $A$ and to $AB$, respectively. The relationship that can be derived is
(A) $y^{2}=\frac{x^{3}}{2 a-x}$.
(B) $y^{2}=\frac{x^{3}}{2 a+x}$.
(C) $y^{4}=\frac{x^{2}}{2 a-x}$.
(D) $x^{2}=\frac{y^{2}}{2 a-x}$.
(E) $x^{2}=\frac{y^{2}}{2 a+x}$.
(17th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1966)

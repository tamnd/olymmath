Maximum 15 points. In a chess tournament, 29 people are playing. One point is awarded for a win, half a point for a draw, and zero for a loss. If a player has no opponent, they receive 1 point, but according to the rules, a point without a game can be awarded to a player no more than once during the entire tournament. In the next round, players with the same number of points play against each other (if this is not possible, opponents are chosen randomly). A total of 9 rounds were played. No draws were recorded in any round.

(a) Could two players, each with 8 points, have played against each other in the final round?

(b) Starting from which round, regardless of the results of previous rounds, could a pair of players who won all previous rounds not meet?

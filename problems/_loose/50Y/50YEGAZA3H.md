Prove that the equation

$$
\left\{x^{3}\right\}+\left\{y^{3}\right\}=\left\{z^{3}\right\}
$$

has infinitely many solutions where $x, y$, and $z$ are rational numbers, but none of them are integers. (\{r\} denotes the fractional part of $r$.)

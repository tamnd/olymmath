Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle such that $A B > B C$ and $A C > B C$. Let $X$ be the point on the ray $[B C)$ such that $A B = B X$. Let $Y$ be the point on the ray $[C B)$ such that $A C = C Y$. Let $P$ be the point distinct from $A$ on the ray $[A X)$ such that $C P = C A$. Let $Q$ be the point distinct from $A$ on the ray $[A Y)$ such that $B Q = B A$. Suppose that the lines $(B Q)$ and $(C P)$ intersect at the point $Z$. Show that the point $Z$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of the segment $[B C]$.

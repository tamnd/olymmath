a) A circle $k$ touches each side of the quadrilateral $ABCD=N$ at an internal point of that side. From vertices $A$ and $C$, we measure the segments $AA_1=AA_2=\sqrt{AB \cdot AD}$, and $CC_1=CC_2=\sqrt{CB \cdot CD}$, respectively, in the direction of $B$ and $D$. It is to be proven that $A_1A_2=C_1C_2$.

b) Is the statement valid if $k$ is not inside $N$, but touches the line of each side?

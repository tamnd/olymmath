We fill a $3 \times 3$ table with distinct natural numbers from 1 to 9. Then we calculate the sum of the numbers $v$ in each of the four $2 \times 2$ squares and write these four sums in ascending order. Decide whether we can obtain the sequence
a) $24,24,25,25$,

b) 20, 23, 26, 29.

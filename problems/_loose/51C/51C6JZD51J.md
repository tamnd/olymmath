Solve the system:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{ccccccc}
& x_{2}+ & x_{3}+ & \ldots & x_{n-1}+ & x_{n}= & 1 \\
x_{1}+ & & x_{3}+ & \ldots & x_{n-1}+ & x_{n}= & 2 \\
x_{1}+ & x_{2}+ & & \ldots & x_{n-1}+ & x_{n}= & 3 \\
\ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots
\end{array}\right.
$$

where $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

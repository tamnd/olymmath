Let $F_{1}, F_{2}, \ldots, F_{n}$ be one-pound coins affixed to the plane such that each $F_{i}$ touches $F_{i+1}$ $(i=1,2, \ldots, n$, and $F_{n+1}$ means $F_{1}$), and a similar one-pound coin $G$ can roll around the outside of this "chain" of coins, touching each $F_{i}$ in sequence. How many rotations does $G$ make about its own center as it returns to its starting position?

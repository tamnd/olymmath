a) Prove that the product of two consecutive integers is either divisible by 6 or leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 18.

b) Prove that there does not exist an integer $ n $ such that the number $ 3n + 1 $ is the product of two consecutive integers.

c) Prove that for no integer $ n $ can the number $ n^3 + 5n + 4 $ be the product of two consecutive integers.

d) Prove that none of the numbers obtained by permuting the digits of the number 23456780 can be the product of two consecutive integers.

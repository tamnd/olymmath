Let $r$ be 2 or an odd number, $p_{1}, \ldots, p_{r}$ be distinct prime numbers of the form $4k+1$. Suppose that $\left(\frac{p_{i}}{p_{j}}\right)=-1$ for all $i \neq j$. Prove that the equation $x^{2}-d y^{2} = -1$, where $d=p_{1} \ldots p_{r}$, has a solution in integers.

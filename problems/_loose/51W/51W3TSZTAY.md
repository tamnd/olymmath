For any plane passing through the incenter $I$ of tetrahedron $ABCD$ and intersecting edges $AB$, $AC$, and $AD$ at points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ respectively, let the area of the face opposite vertex $X$ be denoted as $S_{X}$. Then, $S_{B} \cdot \frac{AB}{AP} + S_{C} \cdot \frac{AC}{AQ} + S_{D} \cdot \frac{AD}{AR} = S_{A} + S_{B} + S_{C} + S_{D}$.

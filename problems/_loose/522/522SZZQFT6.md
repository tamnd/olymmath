Let's say a positive real number is *stumpy* if it is not a natural number and in its decimal representation, it contains only a finite number of non-zero digits after the decimal point.

a) Find an example of two stumpy numbers $a, b$ such that $a \cdot b=2015$.

b) Decide whether there exist three stumpy numbers $a, b, c$ such that the numbers $a \cdot b, b \cdot c$ and $c \cdot a$ are all natural.

If the length of one leg is increased by $7 \mathrm{~cm}$, while the other leg remains the same, the hypotenuse of the new triangle would be $5 \mathrm{~cm}$ longer than the original hypotenuse. If the length of the same leg in the original triangle is decreased by $4 \mathrm{~cm}$, while the other leg remains the same, the new hypotenuse would be $2 \mathrm{~cm}$ shorter than the original hypotenuse. Calculate the area of the original triangle $A B C$.

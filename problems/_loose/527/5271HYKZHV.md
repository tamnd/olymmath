Grandma distributes all the cherries to the grandchildren according to this criterion: she gives each grandchild a number of cherries obtained by multiplying the grandchild's age by a certain factor, and this factor is the same for all three grandchildren. How many cherries are given to Jacopo?
(A) 20,
(B) 21, (C) 22,
(D) 23,
(E) 24.

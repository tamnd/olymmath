$$
T=0, a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \ldots
$$

infinite decimal fraction, whose formation rule is

$$
a_{n}=E\left(\frac{3 n+5}{2 n+1}\right)
$$

is a rational number. Express this number as the quotient of two integers.

Given a regular quadrilateral pyramid $T A B C D$ with base $A B C D$, where $A B=9 / 2$. On its height $T O$, a point $T_{1}$ is chosen such that $T T_{1}=T O / 3$. Points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, and $D_{1}$ divide the segments $O A, O B, O C$, and $O D$, respectively, in the ratio $1: 2$, counting from point $O$. Find the area of the section of the pyramid $T_{1} A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ by a plane parallel to the median $A K$ of the lateral face $T A B$, passing through the midpoint of edge $T C$ and point $F$ on segment $T A$ such that $A F: F T=1: 2$, given that the distance from point $C$ to this section plane is $4 \sqrt{2 / 13}$. Round the result to the hundredths place according to rounding rules.

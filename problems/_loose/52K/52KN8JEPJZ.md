(Weyl's Uncertainty Principle.) Let the random variable $\xi$ have a smooth density $f=f(x)$, and

$$
x f(x) \rightarrow 0, |x| \rightarrow \infty
$$

Prove that

$$
\mathrm{E} \xi^{2} \cdot \mathrm{E}\left|\frac{f^{\prime}(\xi)}{f(\xi)}\right|^{2} \geqslant 1
$$

Petya cut a rectangular sheet of paper along a straight line. Then he cut one of the resulting pieces along a straight line. Then he did the same with one of the three resulting pieces, and so on. Prove that after a sufficient number of cuts, it will be possible to select among the resulting pieces 100 polygons with the same number of vertices (for example, 100 triangles or 100 quadrilaterals, etc.).

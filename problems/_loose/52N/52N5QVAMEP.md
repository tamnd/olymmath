Let $ \{k_n\}_{n = 1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of real numbers having the properties $ k_1 > 1$ and $ k_1 + k_2 + \cdots + k_n < 2k_n$ for $ n = 1,2,...$ . Prove that there exists a number $ q > 1$ such that $ k_n > q^n$ for every positive integer $ n$ .

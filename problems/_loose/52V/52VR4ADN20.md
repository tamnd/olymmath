(England, 83). Prove that for the sequence of numbers (Fibonacci) $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, defined by the relations

$$
a_{1}=a_{2}=1, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n} \text { for } n \in \mathbf{N} \text {, }
$$

there exists a unique triplet of numbers $a, b, c \in \mathbf{N}$, satisfying the conditions: $b<a, c<a$ and for any value of $n \in \mathrm{N}$ the number $a_{n}-n b c^{n}$ is divisible by $a$.

Given a quarter circle with center $O$ and a smaller arc $A B$ on it, we select the internal points $C, D$ on the arc $A B$ such that the arcs $A C$ and $B D$ are equal. We draw the tangents at points $B$, $C$, and $D$, and extend their segments to the ray $O A$, denoting them as $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}$ respectively. We need to prove that the value of the following expression is independent of the choice of points $C$ and $D$:

$$
\frac{e_{1} e_{2} e_{3}}{e_{1}-e_{2}-e_{3}}
$$

a) Does there exist a sequence of natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$, possessing the following property: no term of the sequence is equal to the sum of several others and $a_{n} \leqslant n^{10}$ for any $n$?

b) The same question, if $a_{n} \leqslant n \sqrt{n}$ for any $n$.

Find all functions $f: \mathbb N \to \mathbb N$ such that for all positive integers $m,n$ ,**(i)** $mf(f(m))=\left( f(m) \right)^2$ ,**(ii)** If $\gcd(m,n)=d$ , then $f(mn) \cdot f(d)=d \cdot f(m) \cdot f(n)$ ,**(iii)** $f(m)=m$ if and only if $m=1$ .

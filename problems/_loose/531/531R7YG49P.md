In $\triangle A B C$, $D$ and $E$ are points on sides $B C$ and $A C$ respectively, and $\frac{B D}{D C}=\frac{2}{3}, \frac{A E}{E C}=\frac{3}{4}$. Then the value of $\frac{A F}{F D} \cdot \frac{B F}{F E}$ is
(A) $\frac{7}{3}$.
(B) $\frac{14}{9}$.
(C) $\frac{35}{12}$.
(D) $\frac{56}{15}$.
(Chinese Junior High School Mathematics Competition, Taiyuan City, Shanxi Province, 1997)

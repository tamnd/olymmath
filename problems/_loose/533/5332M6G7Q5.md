Prove that if

$$
\sqrt{x+a}+\sqrt{y+b}+\sqrt{z+c}=\sqrt{y+a}+\sqrt{z+b}+\sqrt{x+c}==\sqrt{z+a}+\sqrt{x+b}+\sqrt{y+c}
$$

for some $a, b, c, x, y, z$, then $x=y=z$ or $a=b=c$.

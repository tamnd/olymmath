Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral and let $P$ and $Q$ be points inside the quadrilateral $A B C D$ so that $P Q D A$ and $Q P B C$ are chordal quadrilaterals. Assume that a point $E$ exists on the line $P Q$ such that $\angle P A E=\angle Q D E$ and $\angle P B E=\angle Q C E$. Show that $A B C D$ is a chordal quadrilateral.

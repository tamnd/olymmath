For a quadrilateral $A B C D$, the following conditions (1), (2), (3) are required:

(1) $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

(2) The angle bisectors of $\angle B A D$ and $\angle A B C$ intersect at a point $E$, which lies on the line through $C$ and $D$.

(3) It is given that $A E=6.0 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $B E=4.0 \mathrm{~cm}$.

a) Prove that any quadrilateral $A B C D$ that satisfies these conditions can be constructed from the given lengths of 6.0 $\mathrm{cm}$ and $4.0 \mathrm{~cm}$!

b) Describe such a construction!

c) Prove that any quadrilateral $A B C D$ constructed according to your description satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3)!

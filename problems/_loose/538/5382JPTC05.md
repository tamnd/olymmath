Prove that the sum:

$$ S_n=\binom{n}{1}+\binom{n}{3}\cdot 2005+\binom{n}{5}\cdot 2005^2+...=\sum_{k=0}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\right\rfloor}\binom{n}{2k+1}\cdot 2005^k $$

is divisible by $2^{n-1}$ for any positive integer $n$ .

An *$ n-m$ society* is a group of $ n$ girls and $ m$ boys. Prove that there exists numbers $ n_0$ and $ m_0$ such that every *$ n_0-m_0$ society* contains a subgroup of five boys and five girls with the following property: either all of the boys know all of the girls or none of the boys knows none of the girls.

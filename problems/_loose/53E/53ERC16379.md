Given a triangle $ABC$ and a point $D$. The lines $AD$, $BD$, and $CD$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$ respectively. Consider two circles: the first one passes through $A$ and $A_1$, and the second one through $B$ and $B_1$. Prove that the endpoints of the common chord of these two circles and the points $C$ and $C_1$ lie on the same circle.

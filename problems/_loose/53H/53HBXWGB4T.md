+ Find all functions $f: \mathbf{Q}^{+} \rightarrow \mathbf{Q}^{+}$, such that
$$
f(x)+f(y)+2 x y f(x y)=\frac{f(x y)}{f(x+y)},
$$

where $\mathbf{Q}^{+}$ denotes the set of positive rational numbers. (Li Shenghong)

Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ be positive real numbers. Denote $$ s=\sum_{k=1}^na_k\text{ and }s'=\sum_{k=1}^na_k^{1-\frac1k}. $$ (a) Let $\lambda>1$ be a real number. Show that $s'<\lambda s+\frac\lambda{\lambda-1}$ .
(b) Deduce that $\sqrt{s'}<\sqrt s+1$ .

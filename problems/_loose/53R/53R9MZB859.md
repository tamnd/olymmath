There is a family of curves: $P_0,P_1,P_2,\cdots$ . $P_0$ is a regular triangle, whose area is $1$ . For all $k\in\mathbb{Z}_+$ , $P_k$ is defined in this way: trisect all sides of $P_{k-1}$ , and draw outward a regular triangle with side of the segment in the middle, then cut off the segment in the middle. $S_n$ is the area of $P_n$ .**(a)** Find $S_n$ .**(b)** Find $\lim_{n\to\infty}S_n$ .

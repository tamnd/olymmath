(26th Russian Olympiad, 2000) Along the circumference of a circle, there are 100 pairwise coprime natural numbers. It is allowed to add to any of these numbers the greatest common divisor of its two neighboring numbers. Prove that it is possible to use such operations to make all the numbers pairwise coprime.

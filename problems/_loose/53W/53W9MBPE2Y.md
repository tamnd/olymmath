In the country, there are several cities, some pairs of which are connected by non-stop flights of one of $N$ airlines, and from each city there is exactly one flight of each of the airlines. It is known that from each city you can fly to any other (possibly with layovers). Due to a financial crisis, $N-1$ flights were closed, but in none of the airlines more than one flight was closed. Prove that it is still possible to fly from any city to any other.

An Anderson number is a positive integer $k$ less than 10000 with the property that $k^{2}$ ends with the digit or digits of $k$. For example, 25 is an Anderson number because 625 ends with 25 , but 75 is not an Anderson number because 5625 does not end with 75. If $S$ is the sum of all even Anderson numbers, what is the sum of the digits of $S$ ?
(A) 17
(B) 18
(C) 11
(D) 33
(E) 24

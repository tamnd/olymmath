(41st IMO Shortlist) Given two circles intersecting at $X$ and $Y$. Prove: there exist four points such that for any circle tangent to the two given circles at $A$ and $B$ respectively, and intersecting the line $X Y$ at $C$ and $D$, then $A C$, $A D$, $B C$, and $B D$ pass through one of these four points.

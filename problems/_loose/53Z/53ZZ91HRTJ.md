Inside triangle $A B C$, a point $M$ is taken, such that

$$
\angle A M C=60^{\circ}+\angle A B C, \angle C M B=60^{\circ}+\angle C A B, \angle B M A=60^{\circ}+\angle B C A .
$$

Prove that the projections of point $M$ onto the sides of the triangle are the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

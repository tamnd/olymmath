Let's construct an equilateral triangle $ABC$ such that its side $AC$ touches a given circle at point $A$, and its side $BC$ touches another given circle at point $B$. How many such triangles exist? Prove that the third vertex $C$ of each such triangle lies on a straight line. What is the relationship between this line and the two given circles?

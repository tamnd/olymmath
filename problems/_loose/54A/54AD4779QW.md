Positive numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{r}
x^{2}+x y+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=25 \\
\frac{y^{2}}{3}+z^{2}=9 \\
z^{2}+z x+x^{2}=16
\end{array}\right.
$$

Calculate the value of $x y+2 y z+3 z x$.

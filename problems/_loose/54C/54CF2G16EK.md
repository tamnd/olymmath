Given a set of line segments on a straight line, the sum of their lengths is less than 1. Prove that for any $n$-point set on this line, it is always possible to translate the $n$ points along the line by a vector of length not exceeding $\frac{n}{2}$, such that none of the $n$ points after translation belongs to any of the given line segments.

If in the polynomial $a x^{3}+b x^{2}+c x+d$ the numbers $-7, 4, -3$ and 6 are substituted for $a, b, c$ and $d$ in any order, polynomials with one variable will be obtained, for example: $-7 x^{3}+4 x^{2}-3 x+6, 4 x^{3}-7 x^{2}+6 x-3$, etc. Prove that all such polynomials have a common root.

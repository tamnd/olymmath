Let $e, f, g$ be lines passing through a point but not lying in the same plane. The projection of $e$ onto the plane of $f$ and $g$, the projection of $f$ onto the plane of $g$ and $e$, and the projection of $g$ onto the plane of $e$ and $f$ are $e_{1}, f_{1}, g_{1}$, respectively. Draw a perpendicular from a point on $e$ to $f_{1}$ in the plane of $e$ and $g$, and to $g_{1}$ in the plane of $e$ and $f$; let these lines intersect $g$ at $G$ and $f$ at $F$, respectively. Is $F G$ perpendicular to $e_{1}$?

Two players play a game. From a pile containing 25 matches, each player takes one, two, or three matches in turn. The player who ends up with an even number of matches after all matches are taken wins.

a) Who wins with correct play - the first player or their partner? How should they play to win?

b) How would the answer change if the player who ends up with an odd number of matches wins?

c) Investigate this game in the general case, where there are $2n+1$ matches and it is allowed to take any number of matches from 1 to $m$.

(SFRY, 72). Prove that if the number $M$ and the set of numbers

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{11}, a_{12}, \ldots, a_{1 n} \\
& a_{21}, a_{22}, \ldots, a_{2 n}, \\
& a_{n 1}, a_{n 2}, \ldots, a_{n n}
\end{aligned}
$$

satisfy the inequalities

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|a_{j 1} x_{1}+a_{j 2} x_{2}+\ldots+a_{i n} x_{n}\right| \leqslant M
$$

for any values of $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n} \in\{-1 ; 1\}$, then the estimate

$$
\left|a_{11}\right|+\left|a_{22}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{n n}\right| \leqslant M
$$

holds.

On the plane, there is a non-self-intersecting closed broken line. We know about the polygon bounded by the broken line that no matter how we take points $A, B, C$ inside it, there is a point $P$ such that the segments $P A, P B, P C$ are all inside the polygon. Show that then there is a point $Q$ such that for any point $D$ taken inside the polygon, the segment $Q D$ is also inside the polygon.

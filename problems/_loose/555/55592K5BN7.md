Consider a triangle $ABC$ and $M$ an interior point. We denote $G_{A}, G_{B}$, and $G_{C}$ as the centroids of triangles $\triangle MBC, \triangle MAC$, and, respectively, $\triangle MAB$.

a) Prove that triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle G_{A} G_{B} G_{C}$ are similar;

b) Prove that triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\Delta G_{A} G_{B} G_{C}$ have the same centroid if and only if $M$ is the centroid of triangle $\triangle ABC$.

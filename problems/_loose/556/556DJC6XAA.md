Show that, if $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}$ are strictly positive real numbers such that $\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}=3$, then

$$
\frac{a b c+a^{2} c}{a^{6}+b^{2}+4 a c+2}+\frac{a b c+b^{2} a}{b^{6}+c^{2}+4 a b+2}+\frac{a b c+c^{2} b}{c^{6}+a^{2}+4 b c+2} \leqslant \frac{3}{4}
$$

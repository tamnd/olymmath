We have assembled a large cube from 64 small cubes. Among the small cubes, one is black, and the rest are white. We want to achieve that all of them become white. When is this possible, if in one step we can change the color of any small cube to its opposite, but only if this simultaneously changes the color of all small cubes intersected by a symmetry plane of the selected cube? What is the situation in the case of a $5 \times 5 \times 5$ cube?

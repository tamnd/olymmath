Let $n \geqslant 2$ be circles of radius 1 in the plane, such that no two of them are tangent, and such that the subset of the plane formed by the union of all the circles is connected.

Let $S$ be the set of points that belong to at least two circles. Show that $|S| \geqslant n$.

We are given a disk $\mathcal D$ of radius $1$ in the plane.

(a) Prove that $\mathcal D$ cannot be covered with two disks of radii $r<1$ .
(b) Prove that, for some $r<1$ , $\mathcal D$ can be covered with three disks of radius $r$ . What is the smallest such $r$ ?

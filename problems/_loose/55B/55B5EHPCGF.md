Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, the odd terms form an arithmetic sequence with the first term 1, and the even terms form a geometric sequence with the first term 2. The sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is $S_{n}$, and it satisfies $S_{5}=2 a_{4}+a_{5}, a_{9}=a_{3}+a_{4}$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) If $a_{m} a_{m+1}=a_{m+2}$, find the value of the positive integer $m$;
(3) Does there exist a positive integer $m$ such that $\frac{S_{2 m}}{S_{2 m-1}}$ is exactly a term in the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$? If it exists, find all values of $m$ that satisfy the condition; if not, explain the reason.

If the polynomial $f(x, y)$ in $x, y$ can be written in the form $g(x) h(y)+u(x) v(y)$, then $f(x, y)$ is called a $k$-class polynomial. Here, $g(x), u(x)$ are non-zero degree polynomials in $x$, and $h(y), v(y)$ are non-zero degree polynomials in $y$. Determine whether the following two polynomials are $k$-class polynomials. Provide reasons.
(1) $f_{1}(x, y)=1+x y$;
(2) $f_{2}(x, y)=1+x y+x^{2} y^{2}$.

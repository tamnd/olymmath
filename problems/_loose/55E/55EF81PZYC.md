A tangent line to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ is drawn through point $A$, intersecting line $BC$ at point $P$; a line parallel to $BC$ is drawn through point $A$, intersecting line $EF$ at point $Q$. Prove: $AM \perp PQ$.

Given that $f(x)$ is an even function defined on $\mathbf{R}$ with a period of 2, and when $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1$, $f(x)=x^{2}$. If the line $y=\frac{1}{2} x+a$ intersects the graph of $y=f(x)$ at exactly two points, then ( ).
(A) $a=\frac{1}{2}$
(B) $a=k (k \in \mathbf{Z})$
(C) $a=\frac{1}{2}+k$ or $a=k-\frac{1}{16}(k \in \mathbf{Z})$
(D) $a=2 k+\frac{1}{2}$ or $a=2 k-\frac{1}{16}(k \in \mathbf{Z})$

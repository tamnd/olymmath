Inside a convex polygon $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$, a point $O$ is taken. Let $\alpha_{k}$ be the angle at vertex $A_{k}$, $x_{k} = O A_{k}$, and $d_{k}$ be the distance from point $O$ to the line $A_{k} A_{k+1}$. Prove that $\sum x_{k} \sin \left(\alpha_{k} / 2\right) \geqslant \sum d_{k}$ and $\sum x_{k} \cos \left(\alpha_{k} / 2\right) \geqslant p$, where $p$ is the semiperimeter of the polygon.

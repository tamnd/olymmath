Given a circle $k$ with center $O$, a point $E$ on this circle, and a line $e$ passing through $O$ but not through $E$. For any point $P$ on the line $e$, let the secant $PE$ intersect the circle $k$ again at point $Q$.

Prove that there exists a point $R$ in the plane of $k$ such that the points $O, P, Q, R$ lie on a circle for any position of $P$.

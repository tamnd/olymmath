The 17th question: Let $\mathrm{F}$ be a finite set composed of integers, satisfying:
(1) For any $x \in F$, there exist $y, z \in F$ (which can be the same), such that $x=y+z$;
(2) There exists $n \in \mathbb{N}^{+}$, such that for any positive integer $k(1 \leq k \leq n)$ and any $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{k} \in F$ (which can be the same), we have $\sum_{i=1}^{k} x_{i} \neq 0$.
Prove: $\mathrm{F}$ contains at least $2n+2$ elements.

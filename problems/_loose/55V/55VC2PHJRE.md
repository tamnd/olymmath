In a game of filling symbols on an infinitely large grid paper, A and B take turns, with A going first, to fill a symbol in an empty cell. A always fills $\times$, and B always fills ○. If A manages to fill $\times$ in 11 consecutive cells in a row, a column, or a diagonal, A wins. Prove that B always has a way to prevent A from winning.

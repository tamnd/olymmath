The hour hand of the clock is overtaken several times by the minute hand during one full rotation.

1. The angles formed by both hands at the moment of overtaking with the hand position at $0^{h}$ are to be calculated.
2. The equation is to be specified, from which the time (in min) can be calculated that the minute hand needs from any given hour until it reaches the hour hand.

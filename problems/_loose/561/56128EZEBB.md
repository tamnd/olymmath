Let ABCD be a parallelogram with $A B=n \cdot A D$, where $n \in \mathbb{R}, n>2$. The angle bisectors of the parallelogram intersect as follows: the bisector of angle $A$ intersects the bisector of angle $B$ at point $R$, the bisector of angle $B$ intersects the bisector of angle $C$ at $S$, the bisector of angle $C$ intersects the bisector of angle $D$ at $T$, and the bisector of angle $D$ intersects the bisector of angle $A$ at $Q$.

a) Prove that $R S T Q$ is a rectangle;

b) If $A B \cap D T=\{P\}$, prove that $\frac{A_{A D P}}{A_{A R B}}=\frac{2}{n^{2}}$;

c) Prove that the lines $R T, A C$ and $D B$ are concurrent.

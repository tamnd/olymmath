The sequence $\left\{u_{n}\right\}, u_{1}=a$ (where $a$ is any positive number), $u_{n+1}=-\frac{1}{u_{n}+1}$, $n=1,2,3, \cdots$, which of the following values of $n$ can make $u_{n}=a$?
(A) 14.
(B) 15.
(C) 16.
(D) 17.
(E) 18.
(40th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1989)

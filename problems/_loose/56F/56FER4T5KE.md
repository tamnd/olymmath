Let $\{a_{n}\}$ be a sequence of positive integers, satisfying: $a_{1}=a, a_{n+1}=a_{n}+(n+1, a_{n}), n=1,2,3, \cdots$.
(1) Prove: When $a=3$, there exists a unique positive integer $k$ such that $a_{k}=2k$;
(2) If $a>3$, investigate the existence and number of positive integers $k$ that satisfy $a_{k}=2k$, and prove your conclusion. Note: Here $(x, y)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of the positive integers $x, y$.

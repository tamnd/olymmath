Three lines are drawn parallel to the sides of a triangle. Each line is at a distance from the side it is parallel to, equal to the length of that side. Moreover, for each side of the triangle, the line parallel to it and the vertex opposite that side are located on opposite sides of it. Prove that the points of intersection of the extensions of the sides of the triangle with the three drawn lines lie on a single circle.

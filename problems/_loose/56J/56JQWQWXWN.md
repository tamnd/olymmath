Let $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}, \mathrm{C}$ and $\mathrm{D}$ be four distinct points in the plane. It turns out that any circle passing through $A$ and $B$ intersects any circle passing through $C$ and $D$.

Prove that $A, B, C$ and $D$ are collinear or that they all lie on the same circle.

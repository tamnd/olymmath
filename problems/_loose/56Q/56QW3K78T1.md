In a non-equilateral $\triangle ABC$, the midpoints of $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $M_{a}$, $M_{b}$, and $M_{c}$, respectively. Let $S$ be a point on the Euler line of $\triangle ABC$. The lines $M_{a}S$, $M_{b}S$, and $M_{c}S$ intersect the nine-point circle of $\triangle ABC$ again at points $X$, $Y$, and $Z$, respectively. Prove that $AX$, $BY$, and $CZ$ are concurrent.

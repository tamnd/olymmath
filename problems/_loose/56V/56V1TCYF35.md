Given a sequence $a_{1}, b_{1}, a_{2}, b_{2}, \ldots, a_{k}, b_{k}$, consisting of 0 and 1. Let $N$ be the number of indices $i$ from 1 to $k$ such that $a_{i}=0$ and $b_{i}=1$. Prove that the number of sequences of the given form for which $N$ is odd is given by the formula $2^{2 k-1}-2^{k-1}$.

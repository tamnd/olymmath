On the coordinate plane, several identical square sheets of paper are placed, with each square's sides parallel to the coordinate axes, and any point on the plane is covered by at most two square sheets of paper. Prove that these square sheets of paper can be divided into 3 groups such that any two squares in the same group do not intersect.

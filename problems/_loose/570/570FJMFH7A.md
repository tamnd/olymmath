Let $R=R(s, t)$ be a symmetric non-negative definite function on $\mathbb{R}^{2}$, and $X$ be a random variable such that $\mathrm{E} \sqrt{R(X, X)}<\infty$. Prove that $0 \leqslant \mathrm{E} R(X, Y)<\infty$, when $Y$ is an independent copy of the variable $X$.

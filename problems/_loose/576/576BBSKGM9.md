A circle inscribed in an angle with vertex $O$ touches its sides at points $A$ and $B, K$ - an arbitrary point on the smaller of the two arcs $A B$ of this circle. On the line $O B$, a point $L$ is taken such that the lines $O A$ and $K L$ are parallel. Let $M$ be the point of intersection of the circumcircle of triangle $K L B$ with the line $A K$, different from $K$. Prove that the line $O M$ is tangent to the circle.

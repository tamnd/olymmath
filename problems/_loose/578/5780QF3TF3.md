(Jury, Belgium, 83). Prove that there are infinitely many numbers $n \in \mathbf{N}$, satisfying for all values $k=1,2, \ldots, n-1$ the inequalities

$$
\frac{\sigma(n)}{n}>\frac{\sigma(k)}{k}
$$

where $\sigma(n)$ denotes the sum of all divisors of the number $n$.

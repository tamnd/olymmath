The numbers $1, 2, 3, ..., 16$ are randomly placed in the unit squares of a $4 \times 4$ board, one number in each unit square. A unit square is colored red if the number in that square is greater than all the numbers in the neighboring unit squares. What is the largest number of unit squares that can be colored red?
(Note: neighboring unit squares are unit squares that have a common side.)

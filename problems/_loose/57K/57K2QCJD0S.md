Acute-angled triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle with center at $O$ ; $\stackrel \frown {AB} = 120$ and $\stackrel \frown {BC} = 72$ . A point $E$ is taken in minor arc $AC$ such that $OE$ is perpendicular to $AC$ . Then the ratio of the magnitudes of angles $OBE$ and $BAC$ is: $\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{5}{18} \textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{2}{9} \textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{1}{4} \textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{1}{3} \textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{4}{9}$

Determine all complex numbers $z$ such that:

$$
\operatorname{Re} z=5 \cdot \operatorname{Im} z \text{and} |z-(a+i b)|=5
$$

where $a$ and $b (a>b)$ are the solutions of the quadratic equation

$$
(x-1)^{2}+3(x-1)-4=0
$$

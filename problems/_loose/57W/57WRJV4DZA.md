49 The base of the pyramid $S-ABCD$ is a parallelogram $ABCD$. A plane through vertex $A$ and the midpoint $K$ of edge $SC$ intersects edges $SB$ and $SD$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Prove: $\frac{1}{3} \leqslant \frac{V_{1}}{V} \leqslant \frac{3}{8}$. Here, $V$ is the volume of the pyramid $S-ABCD$, and $V_{1}$ is the volume of the pyramid $S-AMKN$.

A non-extensible sphere and a plane $\alpha$ are given, with the plane and the sphere having no common points. At point $M$ on the plane $\alpha$, there is a light source that illuminates the sphere. Prove that the plane which separates the illuminated part from the non-illuminated part of the sphere passes through a point $P$ which does not depend on the position of point $M$.

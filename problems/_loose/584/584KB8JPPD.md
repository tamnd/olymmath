The tangent $t$ to the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$ intersects its sides $AC$ and $AB$ at points $B_1$ and $C_1$, respectively. Prove that the segments $x$ and $y \left(AC_1 = x, AB_1 = y\right)$ satisfy the equation:

$$
p x y - bc(x + y) + bc(p - a) = 0
$$

Prove that if the segments $x$ and $y$ cut off by the line $t$ on the sides $AC$ and $AB$, measured from vertex $A$, satisfy the above equation, then this line is a tangent to the inscribed circle. How does the equation change if the tangent $t$ intersects the extension of one of the sides $AB$ or $AC$?

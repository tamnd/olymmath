Let $A$ be a finite set of points in the coordinate plane. Suppose that $A$ has $n\geq3$ points. Given any $a$ in $A$, the horizontal and vertical lines through $a$ define four *closed* quadrants centered at $a$. For any real number $\alpha$, call a point $a$ in $A$ $\alpha$-good if there are two diagonally opposite closed quadrants centered at $a$ that each contain at least $\alpha n$ points from $A$. Show that there is some $a$ in $A$ that is $\frac{1}{8}$-good.

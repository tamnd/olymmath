$6.2 .9 \star \star$ Let $n(\geqslant 2)$ be a positive integer, find all real-coefficient polynomials
$$
P(x)=a_{n} x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{0},
$$

such that $P(x)$ has exactly $n$ real roots no greater than -1, and $a_{0}^{2}+a_{1} a_{n}=a_{n}^{2}+a_{0} a_{n-1}$.

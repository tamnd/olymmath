In a right-angled tetrahedron $ S A B C $, the side edges $ S A, S B, S C $ are mutually perpendicular. $ M $ is the centroid of triangle $ A B C $, and $ D $ is the midpoint of $ A B $. A line $ D P $ is drawn through $ D $ parallel to $ S C $. Prove:
(1) $ D P $ intersects $ S M $;
(2) Let the intersection point of $ D P $ and $ S M $ be $ D^{\prime} $, then $ D^{\prime} $ is the center of the circumscribed sphere of the right-angled tetrahedron $ S A B C $.

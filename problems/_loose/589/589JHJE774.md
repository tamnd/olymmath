Let $(\xi, \zeta)$ be a two-dimensional Gaussian vector, such that $\xi, \zeta \sim$ $\sim \mathscr{N}(0,1)$, and $\mathrm{E} \xi \zeta=\rho$. Show that the density of the ratio $\xi / \zeta$ is given by

$$
f_{\xi / \zeta}(z)=\frac{\sqrt{1-\rho^{2}}}{\pi\left(z^{2}-2 \rho z+1\right)}
$$

When $a<-1$, the roots of the equation
$$
\left(a^{3}+1\right) x^{2}+\left(a^{2}+1\right) x-(a+1)=0
$$

are
(A) two negative roots.
(B) one positive root, one negative root and the absolute value of the negative root is larger.
(C) one positive root, one negative root and the absolute value of the negative root is smaller.
(D) no real roots.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

Let $ m, n $ natural numbers with $ m\ge 2,n\ge 3. $ Prove that there exist $ m $ distinct multiples of $ n-1, $ namely, $ a_1,a_2,a_3,...,a_m, $ such that:
$$ \frac{1}{n} =\sum_{i=1}^m \frac{(-1)^{i-1}}{a_i} . $$

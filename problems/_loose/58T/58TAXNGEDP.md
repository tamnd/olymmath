Let point $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$. The rays $AI$ and $BI$ intersect the circumcircle $k$ of triangle $ABC$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively. The segments $\overline{DE}$ and $\overline{CA}$ intersect at point $F$, the line through point $E$ parallel to line $FI$ intersects the circle $k$ again at point $G$, and the lines $FI$ and $DG$ intersect at point $H$.

Prove that the lines $CA$ and $BH$ are tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $DFH$ at points $F$ and $H$ respectively.

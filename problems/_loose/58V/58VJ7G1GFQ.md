Investigate the systems of equations:

a) $\left\{\begin{aligned} 2 x+3 y & =5 \\ x-y & =2, \\ x+4 y & =a ;\end{aligned}\right.$

b) $\left\{\begin{aligned} x+a y & =1, \\ 2 x+4 y & =2, \\ b x+4 y & =2\end{aligned}\right.$

c) $\left\{\begin{aligned} a x+b y & =a \\ (a-2) x+y & =3 \\ x+y & =1\end{aligned}\right.$

d) $\left\{\begin{array}{l}x+a y+a^{2} z=a^{3} \\ x+b y+b^{2} z=b^{3} \\ x+c y+c^{2} z=c^{3}\end{array}\right.$

e) $\left\{\begin{aligned} x+y+z & =1 \\ a x+b y+c z & =d, \\ a^{2} x+b^{2} y+c^{2} z & =d^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$

f) $\left\{\begin{array}{l}a x+b y+c z=a+b+c \\ b x+c y+a z=a+b+c \\ c x+a y+b z=a+b+c .\end{array}\right.$

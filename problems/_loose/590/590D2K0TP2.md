Construct a triangle $ABC$ from $b-c=3 \text{~cm}, \alpha=55^{\circ}$ and $\beta=85^{\circ}$!

Here, $b$ and $c$ are the lengths of the sides $AC$ and $AB$, respectively, $\alpha$ is the measure of angle $\angle BAC$, and $\beta$ is the measure of angle $\angle ABC$.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the given pieces uniquely determine a triangle up to congruence!

At a round table sit ten people, each with several nuts. In total, there are a hundred nuts. On a common signal, each person passes part of their nuts to the person on their right: half if they (the one giving) had an even number, or one nut plus half of the remainder if they had an odd number. This operation is repeated a second time, then a third, and so on, indefinitely. Prove that after some time, everyone will have ten nuts.

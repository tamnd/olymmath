Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{n} \geqslant 0, a_{1}=0, \\
a_{n+1}^{2}+a_{n+1}-1=a_{n}^{2}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) . \\
\text {Let } S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}, T_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\prod_{j=1}^{k}\left(1+a_{j}\right)} .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: When $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$,
(1) $a_{n}n-2$;
(3) $T_{n}<3$.

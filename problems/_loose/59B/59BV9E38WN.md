Let $ABCD$ be a rhombus with center $O$ and point $N \in (AB)$. Through $N$, a line parallel to $BC$ is constructed, intersecting line $DC$ at $M$. Through $N$, a line parallel to $AC$ is constructed, intersecting $MB$ at $E$, and a line parallel to $DB$ is constructed, intersecting $MA$ at $F$. Let $S$ be the midpoint of segment $[AB]$ and $MS \cap EF = \{G\}$. Prove that:
a) $\frac{EB}{EM} + \frac{FA}{FM} = 1$;
b) $G$ is the centroid of triangle $ABM$;
c) $2 \overrightarrow{OG} = \overrightarrow{GN}$.

It is known that the hourly fuel consumption of a certain aircraft in flight is directly proportional to the cube of its speed. When the aircraft flies at a speed of $a$ km/h, its fuel cost is $m$ yuan (the price of fuel is a constant). In addition, the other costs for the aircraft to fly for 1 hour, apart from the fuel cost, are $n$ yuan. Try to find the most economical speed and the total cost for the aircraft to fly $p$ kilometers.

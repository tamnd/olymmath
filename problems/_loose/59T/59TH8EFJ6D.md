73 Given the equation of a circle as $x^{2}+y^{2}=4$. Try to find two points $A(s, t), B(m, n)$ on the coordinate plane, such that the following two conditions are satisfied:
(1) The ratio of the distance from any point on the circle to point $A$ and to point $B$ is a constant $k$;
(2) $s>m, t>n$, and $m, n$ are both natural numbers.

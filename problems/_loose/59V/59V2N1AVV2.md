Let $f$ and $g$ be two functions such that
$$f(x)=\frac{1}{\lfloor | x | \rfloor}, g(x)=\frac{1}{|\lfloor x \rfloor |}.$$
Find the domains of $f$ and $g$ and then prove that
$$\lim_{x \to -1^+} f(x)= \lim_{x \to 1^- } g(x).$$

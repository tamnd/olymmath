Given are the parallel segments $P Q, R S$. Construct a right-angled triangle such that the angle bisector of the right angle divides the hypotenuse into segments of lengths $P Q$ and $R S$. For the construction, only a straightedge and a $45^{\circ}$ set square may be used (that is, a line parallel to a given line, perpendicular to it, or forming a $45^{\circ}$ angle with it can be drawn through a given point).

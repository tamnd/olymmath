The father's step is $20 \%$ longer than Vasya's. They both walk at a constant speed and do not change the length of their steps. There is a period of time during which both the father and Vasya make a whole number of steps. Each time it turns out that the father has made $t \%$ fewer steps than Vasya. For what largest integer value of $\mathrm{t}$ will the father start to catch up with Vasya?

The sequence $ \{x_{n}\}_{n \ge 1}$ is defined by
$$ x_{1} = 2, x_{n + 1} = \frac {2 + x_{n}}{1 - 2x_{n}}\; (n \in \mathbb{N}).
$$ Prove that

a) $ x_{n}\not = 0$ for all $ n \in \mathbb{N}$,

b) $ \{x_{n}\}_{n \ge 1}$ is not periodic.

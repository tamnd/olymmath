A value of $ x$ satisfying the equation $ x^2 + b^2 = (a - x)^2$ is:

$ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac{b^2 + a^2}{2a}
\textbf{(B)}\ \frac{b^2 - a^2}{2a}
\textbf{(C)}\ \frac{a^2 - b^2}{2a}
\textbf{(D)}\ \frac{a - b}{2}
\textbf{(E)}\ \frac{a^2 - b^2}{2}$

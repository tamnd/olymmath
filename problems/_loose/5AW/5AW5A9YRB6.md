Let the set
$A=\left\{x \in \mathbf{Z}_{+} \mid x\right.$'s decimal representation does not
contain the digits $2, 0, 1, 6\}$.

Let $\sum_{x \in A} \frac{1}{x}$ denote the sum of the reciprocals of all elements in set $A$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{x \in A} \frac{1}{x}<3 .
$$

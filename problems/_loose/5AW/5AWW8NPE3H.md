Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $D, E$, and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A, B$, and $C$ respectively. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$. Let $E^{\prime}$ and $F^{\prime}$ be the reflections of points $E$ and $F$ with respect to the line $(A D)$. Let $X$ be the point of intersection of the lines $\left(\mathrm{BF}^{\prime}\right)$ and $\left(C E^{\prime}\right)$. Let $\mathrm{Y}$ be the point of intersection of the lines $(\mathrm{BE}^{\prime})$ and $\left(\mathrm{CF}^{\prime}\right)$. Show that the lines $(A X)$ and $(\mathrm{HY})$ intersect at the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$.

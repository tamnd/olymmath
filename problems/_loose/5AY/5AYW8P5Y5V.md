In a right-angled triangle $A B C$, a circle is inscribed, touching the hypotenuse $A B$ at point $K$. Segment $A K$ is rotated by $90^{\circ}$ to position $A P$ and segment $B K$ is rotated by $90^{\circ}$ to position $B Q$ such that points $P, Q$ lie in the half-plane opposite to the half-plane $A B C$.

a) Prove that the areas of triangles $A B C$ and $P Q K$ are the same.

b) Prove that the perimeter of triangle $A B C$ does not exceed the perimeter of triangle $P Q K$. When does equality occur?

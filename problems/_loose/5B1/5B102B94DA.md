On the median $C M_{3}$ of triangle $A B C$, a point $M$ is given such that $\frac{C M}{M M_{3}}=\alpha$. Through the point $M$, an arbitrary secant is drawn, intersecting the sides $A B, B C, C A$ of the triangle or their extensions at points $P, Q, R$, respectively, with points $Q$ and $R$ lying on the same side of point $M$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{M Q}+\frac{1}{M R}=\frac{2}{a} \cdot \frac{1}{M P}
$$

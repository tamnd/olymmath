Show that if the prime factorization of $n$ is $\mathfrak{n}=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}} \ldots p_{k}^{\alpha_{k}}$, then the number of positive divisors of $n$ is:

$$
\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{n})=\left(\alpha_{1}+1\right)\left(\alpha_{2}+1\right) \ldots\left(\alpha_{k}+1\right)
$$

and the product of the positive divisors of $n$ is:

$$
p(\mathfrak{n})=\mathfrak{n}^{\frac{d(n)}{2}}
$$

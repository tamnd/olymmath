Let $f:(0, \infty) \rightarrow(0, \infty)$ be a non-constant function that has the property

$$
f\left(x^{y}\right)=(f(x))^{f(y)}
$$

for all $x, y>0$. Show that

$$
f(x y)=f(x) f(y) \text { and } f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)
$$

for all $x, y>0$.

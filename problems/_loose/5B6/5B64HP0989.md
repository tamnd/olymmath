Graphs are collectively referred to as Gou shapes.
Try to determine all $m \times n$ rectangles that can be covered by Gou shapes, with the requirements:
(1) When covering the rectangle, there should be no gaps, and Gou shapes should not overlap;
(2) Gou shapes cannot cover outside the rectangle.

Positive real numbers $a, b, c$ are such that the values

$$
x_{1}=a, x_{2}=b, x_{3}=c, x_{4}=\frac{2 a^{2}}{b+c}, x_{5}=\frac{2 b^{2}}{c+a}, x_{6}=\frac{2 c^{2}}{a+b}
$$

are all distinct. Write them in increasing order:

$$
x_{i_{1}}<x_{i_{2}}<x_{i_{3}}<x_{i_{4}}<x_{i_{5}}<x_{i_{6}} .
$$

Determine how many different orderings $\left(i_{1}, i_{2}, \ldots, i_{6}\right)$ of the indices 1 to 6 we can obtain by choosing different values for $a, b, c$.

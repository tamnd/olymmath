Let $a, b, c, d, e$ be positive real numbers, and
$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}+e^{2}=2 \text {. }
$$

If the areas of five equilateral triangles are $a^{2}, b^{2},$ $c^{2}, d^{2}, e^{2}$, respectively, prove: among these five triangles, there exist four that can cover an equilateral $\triangle ABC$ with area 1.

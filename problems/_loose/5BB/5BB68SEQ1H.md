Let $p$ be a prime number, and $n$ and $a$ be natural numbers not divisible by $p$. Prove that if the congruence $x^{n} \equiv a(\bmod p)$ has a solution, then the congruence $x^{n} \equiv a\left(\bmod p^{r}\right)$ has a solution for any natural number $r$.

(1979 Anhui Province Competition Question) At the two endpoints of line segment $AB$, one is marked in red, and the other is marked in blue. Insert several points in the middle of the line segment, and randomly mark these points with red or blue. This divides the original line segment into $n+1$ non-overlapping smaller segments. Those smaller segments whose endpoints have different colors are called standard segments. Prove: the number of standard segments is odd.

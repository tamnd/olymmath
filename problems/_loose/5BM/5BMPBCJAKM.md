The ages of the five people Antje, Dirk, Manuela, Susanne, and Thomas are described as follows:

(1) Antje is older than Manuela but younger than Susanne.

(2) Thomas is exactly as old as Antje and Manuela together.

(3) Dirk is older than Antje and Susanne together.

a) Show that it follows unambiguously from these statements who the youngest and who the oldest of these five people is!

b) Determine whether it also follows unambiguously from the statements what the order of the age statements for the remaining three people is! Justify your determination!

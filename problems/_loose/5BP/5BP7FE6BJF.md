An interval $I$ is the union of not necessarily disjoint intervals $I_{1}, I_{2}, \ldots, I_{n}$. Prove that

a) the "left halves" of the intervals $I_{1}, I_{2}, \ldots, I_{n}$ - the intervals bounded by their midpoints and lower endpoints - together cover at least half of $I$.

b) if we arbitrarily discard either the left or the right halves of the intervals $I_{1}, I_{2}, \ldots, I_{n}$, then the remaining intervals cover at least a third of $I$.

(Power of a point with respect to a circle). Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a circle with radius $R$ and center $O$. Let $A, B$ be two points on $\mathcal{C}$, and $P$ a point on the line $(A B)$. Show that, in algebraic lengths, we have $\overline{P A} \cdot \overline{P B}=O P^{2}-R^{2}$.

Given a right-angled triangle $A B C$ with the hypotenuse $A B$, where the interior angle $\angle C A B$ is $60^{\circ}$.

Drop a perpendicular $C D$ from $C$ to $A B$, then drop perpendiculars $D E$ and $D F$ from $D$ to $A C$ and $B C$ respectively, and from $F$ drop a perpendicular $F H$ to $A B$!

Prove that $\overline{H B}=\overline{H A}+\overline{A E}$!

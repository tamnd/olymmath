A negligibly small beetle starts from the origin of a plane rectangular coordinate system. First, it travels 1 unit to the right to the point $(1 ; 0)$. There, it makes a $90^{\circ}$ turn counterclockwise and travels $1 / 2$ unit, thus reaching the point ( $1 ; 1 / 2$ ). If it continues its journey in the same manner, that is, after each straight segment of its path, it turns $90^{\circ}$ counterclockwise and then travels half the distance it just covered, which point will the beetle approach more and more?

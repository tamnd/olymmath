On an infinitely large chessboard, there is a $(p, q)$ knight. With each jump, it can move $q$ squares horizontally and $p$ squares vertically, or $p$ squares horizontally and $q$ squares vertically. Find all pairs of positive integers $(p, q)$ such that this $(p, q)$ knight can, from any square, reach any other square on the chessboard in a finite number of jumps.

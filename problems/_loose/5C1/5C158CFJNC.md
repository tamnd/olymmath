A hiking trail leaves from point $P$. The trail consists of a flat section from point $P$ to point $Q$, followed by an uphill section from $Q$ to a viewing platform at point $R$. A hiker walked from $P$ to $Q$ to $R$ and back from $R$ to $Q$ to $P$. The hiker's speed was $4 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ on the flat section in both directions, $3 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ while walking uphill, and $6 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ while walking downhill. If the hiker left $P$ at 1:00 p.m., spent exactly 1 hour at $R$, and returned to $P$ at 8:00 p.m. on the same day, what was the total distance walked by the hiker?

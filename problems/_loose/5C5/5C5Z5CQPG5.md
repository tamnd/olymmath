Prove the following conclusions.
(1) The centroid of a triangle divides each median (from the vertex) in the ratio of two to one;
(2) The incenter of a triangle divides each angle bisector (from the vertex) in the ratio of the sum of the lengths of the two sides forming the angle to the length of the opposite side;
(3) The excenter of a triangle divides the corresponding angle bisector (from the vertex) in the ratio of the sum of the lengths of the two sides forming the angle to the length of the opposite side;
(4) The circumcenter of a triangle divides the three segments $ A D, B E, C F $ passing through this point in the ratio of the double-angle sine values of the corresponding two angles to the double-angle sine value of the other angle;
(5) The orthocenter of a triangle internally (or externally) divides each altitude (from the vertex) in the ratio of the cosine value of the corresponding angle to the product of the cosine values of the other two angles, in absolute value.

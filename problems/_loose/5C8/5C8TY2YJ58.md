Given that $A, B$ are the intersection points of two circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ with unequal radii, the external common tangents $P_{1}$, $P_{2}$ touch the circles at $P_{1}, P_{2}$, and another external common tangent $Q_{1} Q_{2}$ touches the circles at $Q_{1}, Q_{2}$. The line $O_{1} O_{2}$ intersects circle $O_{1}$ at $C$, $N_{1}$, and intersects circle $O_{2}$ at $D, N_{2}$. The line $A B$ intersects $P_{1} P_{2}$ at $T$, and intersects $Q_{1} Q_{2}$ at $S$. Prove: (1) $T S^{2}=P_{1} P_{2}^{2}+A B^{2}$; (2) $C P_{1} \perp D P_{2}$ at $H$; (3) $P_{1} N_{1} \perp P_{2} N_{2}$ at $K$; (4) $K, H, A, B$ are collinear; (5) $P_{1}, P_{2}$, $N_{1}, N_{2}$ are concyclic.

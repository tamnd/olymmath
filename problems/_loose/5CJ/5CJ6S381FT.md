In $\triangle A B C$, construct 3 arbitrary triangles $\triangle D B C$, $\triangle E C A$, $\triangle F A B$, whose vertices form $\triangle D E F$. Then, construct 3 triangles around $\triangle D E F$: $\triangle A^{\prime} F E$, $\triangle B^{\prime} D F$, $\triangle C^{\prime} E D$, such that they are respectively directly similar to $\triangle D B C$, $\triangle E C A$, $\triangle F A B$.
Prove: $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} \backsim \triangle A B C$.

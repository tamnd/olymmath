The frog jumps from vertex to vertex of a hexagon $A B C D E F$, each time moving to one of the adjacent vertices.

a) In how many ways can it get from $A$ to $C$ in $n$ jumps?

b) The same question, but with the condition that it cannot jump to $D$?

Frog-sapper.

c) Suppose the frog's path starts at vertex $A$, and there is a mine at vertex $D$. Each second, it makes another jump. What is the probability that it will still be alive after $n$ seconds?

d)* What is the average lifespan of such frogs?

The function $y$ of $x$ is given by the formula $y=\frac{a x+b}{c x+d}$, where $a d-b c \neq 0$. Let the values of the argument $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$, and $x_{4}$ correspond to the function values $y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}$, and $y_{4}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{y_{3}-y_{1}}{y_{3}-y_{2}}: \frac{y_{4}-y_{1}}{y_{4}-y_{2}}=\frac{x_{3}-x_{1}}{x_{3}-x_{2}}: \frac{x_{4}-x_{1}}{x_{4}-x_{2}}
$$

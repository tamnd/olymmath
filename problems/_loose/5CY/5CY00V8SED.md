Let $M$ and $N$ be any two points on a given ellipse. The major axis of the ellipse is $A B$, and the minor axis is $C D$. Through point $M$, we draw a line parallel to $C D$, and through point $N$, we draw a line parallel to $A B$. These lines intersect at point $P$. The normal to the ellipse at point $M$ intersects the major axis at $Q$, and the normal to the ellipse at point $N$ intersects the minor axis at $R$. Prove that the points $P, Q, R$ lie on a straight line. (The normal to the curve at a given point is the perpendicular to the tangent at that point.)

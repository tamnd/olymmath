Let $\mathrm{P}$ be a point inside a triangle $A B C$. We denote $\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{A}}$ as the intersection of the line (AP) with $[B C]$, $P_{B}$ as the intersection of the line $(B P)$ with $[A C]$, and $P_{C}$ as the intersection of the line $(C P)$ with $[A B]$. Prove that:

$$
\frac{P_{B} P}{P_{B} B}+\frac{P_{A} P}{P_{A} A}+\frac{P_{C} P}{P_{C} C}=1 .
$$

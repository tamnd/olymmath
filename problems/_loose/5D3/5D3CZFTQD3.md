It is to be proven: If in a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$

on the side $A B$ points $E$ and $F$ lie between $A$ and $B$ such that $\overline{A E}=\overline{E F}=\overline{F B}$ holds, and on the side $B C$ points $G$ and $H$ lie between $B$ and $C$ such that $\overline{B G}=\overline{G H}=\overline{H C}$ holds, and on the side $C D$ points $I$ and $K$ lie between $C$ and $D$ such that $\overline{C I}=\overline{I K}=\overline{K D}$ holds, and on the side $D A$ points $L$ and $M$ lie between $D$ and $A$ such that $\overline{D L}=\overline{L M}=\overline{M A}$ holds, then the lines through $M, E$ and $I, H$ as well as the lines through $F, G$ and $K, L$ are each parallel to each other.

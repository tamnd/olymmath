In a quadrilateral $ABCD$, it is known that $\angle ABC = \angle ADC = 90^{\circ}$. On the ray $AB$ beyond point $B$, a point $K$ is chosen such that $\angle AKD = \angle ADB$. Point $L$ is the projection of point $K$ onto the line $AD$, and point $N$ is the projection of point $D$ onto the line $CL$. Find the degree measure of $\angle ANK$.

Had the accident happened $ 90$ miles farther along the line, it would have arrived only $ 3$ hours late. The length of the trip in miles was:

$ \textbf{(A)}\ 400 \textbf{(B)}\ 465 \textbf{(C)}\ 600 \textbf{(D)}\ 640 \textbf{(E)}\ 550$

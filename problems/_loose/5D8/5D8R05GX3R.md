Two runners $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are racing on a circular track. The distance is one lap, and the start and finish are at point $P$. When $\alpha$ reaches the midpoint of the track, denoted by point $Q$, $\beta$ is $16 \mathrm{~m}$ behind. At a later time, the positions of the two runners are symmetrical with respect to the diameter $P Q$. $1 \frac{2}{15}$ seconds after this time, $\beta$ reaches point $Q$ and after an additional $13 \frac{13}{15}$ seconds, $\alpha$ reaches the finish line. What are the speeds of the runners and the length of the track? (Assume that both runners maintain a constant speed throughout the race.)

A circle with its center at the midpoint of the base $B C$ of an isosceles triangle $A B C$ touches the lateral sides of the triangle. A tangent to this circle intersects the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that

$$
B P \cdot C Q=\frac{B C^{2}}{4}
$$

A mapping of the plane onto itself is a function $f$ that assigns to each point $P$ of the plane some point $f(P)$ of the plane. It is to be proved that if the mapping $f$ has the property that for every unit length segment $P_{1} P_{2}$, the points $f\left(P_{1}\right), f\left(P_{2}\right)$ are at a unit distance from each other, then for any pair of points $P, Q$, the distance between the images $f(P), f(Q)$ is equal to the length of the segment $P Q$.

In the plane, there is a circle $k_{2}$ and inside it, a circle $k_{1}$, as well as points $P$ and $Q$ such that point $P$ is inside $k_{1}$, and point $Q$ is outside $k_{2}$. Draw an arbitrary line $e$ through $P$ that does not pass through $Q$; let the intersection points of $k_{1}$ and $e$ be $A$ and $B$. The circumcircle of triangle $ABQ$ intersects circle $k_{2}$ at points $C$ and $D$. Show that the segments $CD$ obtained in this way pass through a single point.

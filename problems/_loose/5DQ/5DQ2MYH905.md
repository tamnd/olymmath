Consider a $4 \times 4$ square grid of 16 cells. On the cell at the top right, I place a - sign, and on all the others, a + sign. I repeat indefinitely the operation of changing all the signs in the same row, the same column, or the same diagonal. Can we, after a certain number of operations, have only + signs in the grid?

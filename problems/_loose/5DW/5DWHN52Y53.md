The numbers that are positive integers squared are called perfect squares, for example, 16 is a perfect square because it is equal to $4^{2}$. An interesting fact is that numbers that are perfect squares leave only remainders of 0 or 1 when divided by 4. With this, we can prove, for example, that 2014 is not a perfect square because 2014 leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 4.

a) Knowing that every odd integer is of the form $2k+1$, show that odd perfect squares leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 8.

b) Is it possible to place 45 consecutive odd integers in a sequence such that the sum of any 5 consecutive and any 9 consecutive numbers are perfect squares?

Suppose $x$ and $y$ are real numbers which satisfy the system of equations $$x^2-3y^2=\frac{17}x \text{and} 3x^2-y^2=\frac{23}y.$$ Then $x^2+y^2$ can be written in the form $\sqrt[m]{n}$ , where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers and $m$ is as small as possible. Find $m+n$ .

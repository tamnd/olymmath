Let the side lengths of $\triangle A B C$ be $a, b, c$, and its area be $\Delta$, then $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}$ $\geqslant 4 \sqrt{3} \Delta+(a-b)^{2}+(b-c)^{2}+(c-a)^{2}$,
with equality if and only if the triangle is equilateral.

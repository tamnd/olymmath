Prove that for every triangle the inequality

$$
r^{2}+r_{a}^{2}+r_{b}^{2}+r_{c}^{2} \geq 4 P
$$

holds, where $r$ is the radius of the inscribed circle of the triangle, and $r_{a}, r_{b}, r_{c}$ are the radii of the excircles of the triangle.

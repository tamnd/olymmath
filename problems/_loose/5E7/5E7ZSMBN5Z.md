The circumcircles $c_1$ and $c_2$, of the triangles $AEB$ and $DEC$, respectively, meet at point $F \neq E$. If the parallel from $E$ to the line $HG$ meets the circle $c_2$ at $S$, prove that: $FS \parallel CD$.

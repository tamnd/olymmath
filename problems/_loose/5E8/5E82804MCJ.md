In space, points $A(-1 ; 2 ; 0)$, $B(5 ; 2 ;-1)$, $C(2 ;-1 ; 4)$, and $D(-2 ; 2 ;-1)$ are given. Find:

a) the distance from vertex $D$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ to the point of intersection of the medians of the base $A B C$;

b) the equation of the plane $A B C$

c) the height of the tetrahedron drawn from vertex $D$;

d) the angle between the lines $B D$ and $A C$

e) the angle between the faces $A B C$ and $A C D$

f) the distance between the lines $B D$ and $A C$.

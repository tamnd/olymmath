Let $AB$ be a diameter of a circle $W$, $l$ be the tangent line of $W$ passing through point $A$, and $C$, $M$, $D$ be three points on line $l$ arranged in sequence, with $CM=MD$. Lines $BC$ and $BD$ intersect $W$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove that there exists a point $R$ on line $BM$ such that $RP$ and $RQ$ are both tangent to $W$.

On each turn, each player breaks the chocolate bar into two along one of the lines separating the squares, and then eats the part that does not contain the bad square. The player who leaves the opponent with only the bad square wins. Knowing that Roberto is the first to play, what should he eat to be sure of winning?

(A) the two squares on the far right (B) the four squares on the far right

(C) the six squares on the far right (D) the four squares at the bottom

(E) no matter what move he makes, Roberto loses!

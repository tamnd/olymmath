Given $f(x)=\sqrt{3 x^{2}+4}$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(f^{(k)}(x)\right)^{2} \geqslant 3^{n+1}-2 n-3\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)
$$

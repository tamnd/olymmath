Let $a, b$ be positive real numbers, and $|\alpha|>a$, then for any value of $\theta$, we always have
$$
\frac{\alpha \beta-\sqrt{a^{2} \beta^{2}+b^{2} \alpha^{2}-a^{2} b^{2}}}{a^{2}-a^{2}} \leqslant \frac{\beta+b \sin \theta}{\alpha+a \cos \theta} \leqslant \frac{\alpha \beta+\sqrt{a^{2} \beta^{2}+b^{2} \alpha^{2}-a^{2} b^{2}}}{\alpha^{2}-a^{2}} .
$$

Let $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ be a fixed convex polygon. Consider $X$ inside the polygon. For all $i$, let $B_{i}$ be the second intersection of $\left(A_{i} X\right)$ with the boundary of the polygon. Show that it is possible to choose $X$ in such a way that for all $i$:

$$
\frac{X A_{i}}{X B_{i}} \leq 2
$$

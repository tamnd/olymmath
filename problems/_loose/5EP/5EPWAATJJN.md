Prove that the segment $d$ of a line parallel to the bases of a trapezoid and dividing its area in the ratio $p: q$, can be determined by the formula:

$$
d=\sqrt{\frac{q a^{2}+p c^{2}}{p+q}}
$$

where $a$ and $c$ are the bases of the trapezoid.

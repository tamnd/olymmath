The white rook is on the b2 square of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, and the black rook is on the c4 square. The players take turns moving their rooks, with the whites starting. It is forbidden to place one's rook under attack by the other rook, or on a square that has already been visited by any rook. The player who cannot make a move loses. Which player can ensure a win, regardless of how the other plays? (On a move, a rook can slide horizontally or vertically by any number of squares, and it is considered to have visited only the starting and ending squares of this move.)

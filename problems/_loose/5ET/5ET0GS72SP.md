The class received the following task. Determine the smallest natural number $H$ such that its half is equal to a perfect cube, its third is equal to a perfect fifth power, and its fifth is equal to a perfect square.

Pista and Sanyi were absent that day, and one of their friends told them the problem from memory, so Pista determined the smallest natural number $P$ with the (1) property, and Sanyi determined the smallest natural number $S$ with the (2) property, where $A, B, C, D, E, F$ are natural numbers.

$$
\begin{array}{lll}
P / 2=A^{2}, & P / 3=B^{3}, & P / 5=C^{5} \\
S / 2=D^{5}, & S / 3=E^{2}, & S / 5=F^{3}
\end{array}
$$

Which is the smallest among the numbers $H, P, S$?

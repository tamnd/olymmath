The ninety-seventh problem: Given a positive integer $n$, let $f(x)$ be an $n$-degree polynomial with integer coefficients, and $f(x)$ has no integer roots. Prove: There exists a positive integer $m \leq 3n$ such that $f(m) \nmid f(m+1)$.

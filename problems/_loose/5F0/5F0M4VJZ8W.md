Let $A$ be a finite set of real numbers, and $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ be non-empty subsets of $A$, satisfying:
(1) The sum of all elements in $A$ is 0; (2) For any $x_{i} \in A_{i} (i=1, 2, \ldots, n)$, we have $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}>0$. Prove: There exist $1 \leq \mathrm{i}_{1}<\mathrm{i}_{2}<\ldots<\mathrm{i}_{\mathrm{k}} \leq \mathrm{n}$, such that $\left|A_{\mathrm{i}_{1}} \cup A_{\mathrm{i}_{2}} \cup \ldots \cup A_{\mathrm{i}_{\mathrm{k}}}\right|<\frac{\mathrm{k}}{\mathrm{n}}|\mathrm{A}|$.

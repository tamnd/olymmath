Consider the convex hexagon $A B C D E F$ and denote by $G_{A}, G_{B}, G_{C}, G_{D}, G_{E}, G_{F}$ the centroids of the pentagons $B C D E F, A C D E F, A B D E F, A B C E F, A B C D F, A B C D E$.

a) Prove that it is possible to construct a hexagon with the vectors:

$$
\overrightarrow{A G_{A}}, \overrightarrow{B G_{B}}, \overrightarrow{C G_{C}}, \overrightarrow{D G_{D}}, \overrightarrow{E G_{E}}, \overrightarrow{F G_{F}}
$$

b) Determine the ratio of the areas of the hexagons $G_{A} G_{B} G_{C} G_{D} G_{E} G_{F}$ and $ABCDEF$.

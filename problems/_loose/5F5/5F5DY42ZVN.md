Let $S=\{0,1,2, \ldots, 1994\}$. Let $a$ and $b$ be two positive numbers in $S$ which are relatively prime. Prove that the elements of $S$ can be arranged into a sequence $s_{1}, s_{2}, s_{3}, \ldots, s_{1995}$ such that $s_{i+1}-s_{i} \equiv \pm a$ or $\pm b(\bmod 1995)$ for $i=1,2, \ldots, 1994$.

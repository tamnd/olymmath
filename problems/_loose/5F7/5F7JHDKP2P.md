Let $a_{j} \in \mathbf{R}, z_{j} \in \mathbf{C}, j=1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove:
$$
\left|\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j} z_{j}\right|^{2} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}^{2}\right)\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|z_{j}\right|^{2}+\left|\sum_{j=1}^{n} z_{j}^{2}\right|\right),
$$

and state the necessary and sufficient conditions for equality in the inequality.

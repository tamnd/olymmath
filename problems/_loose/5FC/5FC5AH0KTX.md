Let $P(x)=a_{n} x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0}$ be a polynomial with real coefficients and $n$ distinct real roots. Show that for all real $x, P(x) P^{\prime \prime}(x) \leq P^{\prime}(x)^{2}$. Deduce that for $1 \leq k \leq n-1$, the inequality holds: $a_{k-1} a_{k+1} \leq a_{k}^{2}$.

16 Convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ has an incircle, which touches sides $D A, A B, B C,$ and $C D$ at points $K, L, M, N$ respectively. Let $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$ be the incircles of $\triangle A K L, \triangle B L M, \triangle C M N,$ and $\triangle D N K$ respectively. The other external common tangents of circles $S_{1}, S_{2}$, the other external common tangents of circles $S_{2}, S_{3}$, the other external common tangents of circles $S_{3}, S_{4}$, and the other external common tangents of circles $S_{4}, S_{1}$ are not sides of $A B C D$. Prove that the quadrilateral formed by these external common tangents is a rhombus.

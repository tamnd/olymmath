Prove that there exist two infinite sequences $\left\langle a_{n}\right\rangle_{n \geq 1}$ and $\left\langle b_{n}\right\rangle_{n \geq 1}$ of positive integers such that the following conditions hold simultaneously:

(i) $1<a_{1}<a_{2}<a_{3}<\cdots$;

(ii) $a_{n}<b_{n}<a_{n}^{2}$, for all $n \geq 1$;

(iii) $a_{n}-1$ divides $b_{n}-1$, for all $n \geq 1$;

(iv) $a_{n}^{2}-1$ divides $b_{n}^{2}-1$, for all $n \geq 1$.

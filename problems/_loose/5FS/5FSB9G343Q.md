Given positive integers $m, n>1$. Prove: There exist infinitely many positive integer pairs $(a, b)$, satisfying
$$
\operatorname{gcd}(a, b)=1 \text {, and }(a+b) \mid\left(a m^{a}+b n^{b}\right) \text {. }
$$

3 (1) Find all pairs of positive integers $(a, b)$ such that $a$ and $b$ are not equal, $b^{2}+a$ is a power of a prime, and $b^{2}+a \mid a^{2}+b$.
(2) Let $a$ and $b$ be two different positive integers greater than 1, and $b^{2}+a-1 \mid a^{2}+b-1$.
Prove: The number $b^{2}+a-1$ has at least two distinct prime factors.

Find the derivative $y_{x}^{\prime}$.

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=\ln \sqrt{\frac{1-\sin t}{1+\sin t}} \\
y=\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{tg}^{2} t+\ln \cos t
\end{array}\right.
$$

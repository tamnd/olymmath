As $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is a rectangular parallelepiped, the edges $A D, A B, A A^{\prime}$ meeting at vertex $A$ are equal to the side lengths of a regular hexagon, square, and triangle inscribed in a circle of radius $R$.

1. Calculate the volume $V$ of the parallelepiped and the length of the diagonal $A C^{\prime}$.
2. Calculate the volume and surface area of the solid that is formed when the quadrilateral $A A^{\prime} C^{\prime} C$ rotates around a straight line passing through point $A^{\prime}$ and parallel to the diagonal $A C^{\prime}$.
3. Calculate the value of $R$ if $V=0.034786 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$.

Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}(n>2)$ be two sets of arbitrary real numbers satisfying the conditions:
(1) $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}=0$;
(2) $\left|x_{1}\right|+\left|x_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|x_{n}\right|=1$;
(3) $a_{1} \geqslant a_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant a_{n}$. To make the inequality $\mid a_{1} x_{1}+a_{2} x_{2}+\cdots+ a_{n} x_{n} \mid \leqslant A\left(a_{1}-a_{n}\right)$ hold, find the minimum value of the number $A$.

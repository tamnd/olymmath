Find all positive integers $d$ that can be written in the form $$ d = \gcd(|x^2 - y| , |y^2 - z| , |z^2 - x|), $$
where $x, y, z$ are pairwise coprime positive integers such that $x^2 \neq y$ , $y^2 \neq z$ , and $z^2 \neq x$ .

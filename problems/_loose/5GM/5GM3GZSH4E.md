Let $O$ be the circumcenter of acute $\triangle A B C$. Draw the altitude from $A$ to $B C$, the median from $B$ to $C A$, and the angle bisector of $\angle C$, and extend them to intersect $\odot O$ at $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$. Prove that: $S_{\triangle A B C} \leqslant S_{\triangle A^{\prime} B C} + S_{\triangle A B C} + S_{\triangle A B C}$.
(1991 China Mathematical Olympiad Training Test)

Andriy and Olesia play such game. Firstly, Andriy chooses a chessman and place it on the chessboard. Then they moves in turn by the rules of the chosen chessman. However, it is not allowed to put the chessman on the field that Andriy began from or was already been used. Looser is the one who can not move. Who wins if both are trying to win and Andriy choose:
a) a knight; b) a bishop?

Remind that when a knight moves, it can move to a square that is two squares horizontally and one square vertically, or two squares vertically and one square horizontally. The bishop has no restrictions in distance for each move, but is limited to diagonal movement.

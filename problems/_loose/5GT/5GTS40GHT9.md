In rectangle $A B C D$, points $M, N, P, Q$ are on sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$ respectively, such that
$$
S_{\triangle A Q M}=S_{\triangle B M N}=S_{\triangle C N P}=S_{\triangle D P Q} .
$$

Prove: Quadrilateral $M N P Q$ is a parallelogram.

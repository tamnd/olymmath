In the acute-angled triangle $A B C$, the perpendiculars erected at vertices $B$ and $C$ to the sides $A B$ and $A C$, respectively, intersect at point $P$. The perpendicular projection of $P$ onto the segment $B C$ is $Q$. It is to be proven that the segment of any line passing through $Q$ and different from $B C$, lying between the rays of the angle $B A C$, is larger than $B C$.

A certain cinema has $m$ rows of seats, with $n$ seats in each row. An irresponsible ticket seller sold $m n$ tickets, but some of these tickets have the same seat number. As a result, the audience can be arranged in such a way that each person's seat number or row number matches at least one of the numbers on their ticket.
(1) Prove that the audience can be arranged in such a way that at least one person's seat number and row number match the numbers on their ticket, while the seats of the other audience members still meet the original conditions.
(2) Under the original conditions, in the arrangement that maximizes the number of people whose seat numbers and row numbers match the numbers on their tickets, what is the minimum number of such people?

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $\angle B A C=60^{\circ}$. The points $D$ and $E$ lie on the sides $A C$ and $A B$ respectively. The straight lines $B D$ and $C E$ intersect the circumcircle of $A B C$ at the further points $X$ and $Y$ respectively. Let the point of intersection of $B D$ and $C E$ be $S$. Prove that the lines $B Y$ and $C X$ are parallel if and only if $A E S D$ is a chordal quadrilateral.

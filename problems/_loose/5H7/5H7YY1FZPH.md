Four boys want to call their respective girlfriends at the same time. Each phone can operate on four distinct frequencies. If two phones activate on the same frequency, the communication drops. If each boy does not know which frequency the other three choose, what is the probability that all four can talk to their girlfriends?
(A) $\frac{3}{32}$
(B) $\frac{3}{64}$
(C) $\frac{1}{256}$
(D) $\frac{1}{16}$
(E) $\frac{9}{128}$.

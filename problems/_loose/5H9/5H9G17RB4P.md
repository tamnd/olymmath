From $z^{2}+z+1=0$ we get $z^{3}=1$

$A=I_{3}+B$, where $B=\left(\begin{array}{ccc}0 & 0 & 0 \\ z^{2} & 0 & 0 \\ z & z^{2} & 0\end{array}\right)$

$I_{3} B=B I_{3} \Rightarrow A^{n}=\left(I_{3}+B\right)^{n}=I_{3}+C_{n}^{1} B+C_{n}^{2} B^{2}+\cdots+B^{n}$

$B^{2}=\left(\begin{array}{lll}0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ z & 0 & 0\end{array}\right)$ and $B^{k}=O_{3}$, for any $k \geq 3$.

$A^{n}=I_{3}+n B+\frac{n(n-1)}{2} B^{2}=\left(\begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0 \\ n z^{2} & 1 & 0 \\ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} z & n z^{2} & 1\end{array}\right)$.

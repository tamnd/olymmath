The nineteenth question: A country has several cities, and any two cities form a city pair. Some city pairs are connected by roads. Each road connects exactly two cities (roads can cross, i.e., via overpasses). Some roads have a traffic flow of 1 unit, while others have a traffic flow of 2 units, and each road allows travel in only one direction. It is known that for each city, the sum of the traffic flows of all roads connected to it is odd. Prove: The Minister of Transport can arrange the direction of each road to meet the following requirements: for each city, the absolute difference between the sum of the traffic flows of all roads entering the city and the sum of the traffic flows of all roads leaving the city is 1.

In the complex plane, the vertices $A, B, C$ of $\triangle ABC$ correspond to the complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$. The ellipse $\omega$ is inscribed in the midpoints $N, K, M$ of the sides of $\triangle ABC$. Let $F$ be a focus of the ellipse, corresponding to the complex number $z$. Prove that: $\left(z-z_{1}\right)\left(z-z_{2}\right)+\left(z-z_{2}\right)\left(z-z_{3}\right)+\left(z-z_{3}\right)\left(z-z_{1}\right)=0$.

Let $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \ldots, \alpha_{\mathrm{n}}$ be $\mathrm{n}$ complex numbers, and the polynomial $\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{z})=\left(\mathrm{z}-\alpha_{1}\right)\left(\mathrm{z}-\alpha_{2}\right) \ldots\left(\mathrm{z}-\alpha_{\mathrm{n}}\right)$. Prove: There exists a complex number $z_{0}$ such that $\left|z_{0}\right|=1$, and $\left|f\left(z_{0}\right)\right| \geq \frac{\prod_{j=1}^{n}\left(1+\left|a_{i}\right|\right)}{2^{n-1}}$.

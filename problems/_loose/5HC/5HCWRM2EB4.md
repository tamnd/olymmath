Let $\ell$ be a positive integer. We say that a positive integer $k$ is nice if $k!+\ell$ is a square of an integer. Prove that for every positive integer $n \geqslant \ell$, the set $\left\{1,2, \ldots, n^{2}\right\}$ contains at most $n^{2}-n+\ell$ nice integers.

(Theo Lenoir)

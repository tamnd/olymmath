Let $D$ be the midpoint of the shorter arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of an acute triangle $ABC$. Points $E$ and $F$ are the reflections of point $D$ with respect to the line $BC$ and the circumcenter of the triangle, respectively. Let $K$ be the midpoint of the segment $EA$.

(a) Prove that the circle passing through the midpoints of the sides of triangle $ABC$ also passes through $K$.

(b) Prove that the line through $K$ and the midpoint of side $BC$ is perpendicular to $AF$.

In a room, there are several children and a pile of 2021 candies. Each of them, in turn, approaches the pile, divides the number of candies in it by the number of children in the room (including themselves), rounds the result (if it is not an integer), takes the obtained number of candies, and leaves the room. Boys round up, while girls round down. Prove that the total number of candies taken by the boys, when everyone has left the room, does not depend on the order of the children in the queue.

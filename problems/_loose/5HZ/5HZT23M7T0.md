The inhabitants of a druid village do not get along very well. It turns out that it is even impossible to place 4 or more druids in a circle so that each druid is willing to shake hands with their two neighbors. Considering that such a state of affairs tarnishes the reputation of the tribe, the chief tries to undertake a pacifist action to ease the situation. He collects 3 gold coins from each druid, then to every pair of people who agree to shake hands, he pays one gold coin to each. Show that the chief can put at least 3 gold coins in his pocket at the end of the action.

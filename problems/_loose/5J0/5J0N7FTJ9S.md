(Gottfried Perz). Let $a$ and $b$ be nonnegative real numbers with $a+b<2$. Prove the following inequality:

$$
\frac{1}{1+a^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+b^{2}} \leq \frac{2}{1+a b}
$$

For which $a, b$ does equality hold?

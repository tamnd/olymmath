In the final step of the proof of Kruskal's theorem, we consider a "topological" embedding of $T_{m}$ into $T_{n}$ such that the root of $T_{m}$ maps to the root of $T_{n}$. Assuming we can inductively assume that the trees of $A_{m}$ have been embedded into the trees of $A_{n}$ in the same way, such that the root maps to the root. Then, it seems we have a proof that "finite rooted trees are well-quasi-ordered by the subgraph relation," even with the root mapping to the root. Where is the flaw in this argument?

Let the vertices of a triangle be $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$. Let the point $A_{4}$ be the point that divides the segment $A_{1} A_{2}$ into a ratio of 1:2, closer to $A_{1}$. Similarly, for $n=4,5,6, \ldots$, define the point $A_{n+1}$ as the point that divides the segment $A_{n-1} A_{n-2}$ into a ratio of 1:2, closer to $A_{n-2}$. Prove that there is a point which, for every integer $n \geqq 1$, lies inside the triangle $A_{n} A_{n+1} A_{n+2}$.

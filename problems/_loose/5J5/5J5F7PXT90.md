A teacher in a kindergarten is overseeing a game of $n$ children sitting in a circle. Each child has a certain number of candies. The game consists of a series of steps:

Each child who has an odd number of candies is given one more candy by the teacher, and then each child divides their candies into two equal piles. Then, at the same time, each child gives half of their candies to the child sitting immediately to their right.

Prove that after a finite number of steps, all children will have the same number of candies.

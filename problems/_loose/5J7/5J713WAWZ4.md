A clothing store has a promotional sale for two weeks. In the first week, a customer buying three items gets the cheapest one for free. Janez bought a jacket, trousers, and a pullover in the first week. He paid 115.01 euros for the jacket and trousers and saved $17.85 \%$ of the regular price of the clothing due to this promotion. In the second week, they offer a $20 \%$ discount on all jackets. For the same purchase as in the first week, Janez would have paid 125 euros in the second week. Calculate the price of the pullover, trousers, and jacket in regular sale.

Let $A D, B E, C F$ be the medians, and $T$ be the centroid of triangle $A B C$. If $\rho_{1}, \rho_{2}, \rho_{3}, \rho_{4}, \rho_{5}, \rho_{6}$ and $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}, r_{4}, r_{5}, r_{6}$ are the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of triangles $B D T, D C T, C E T, E A T, A F T$, $A B T$ respectively, prove that the following equalities hold:

$$
\frac{1}{\rho_{1}}+\frac{1}{\rho_{3}}+\frac{1}{\rho_{5}}=\frac{1}{\rho_{2}}+\frac{1}{\rho_{4}}+\frac{1}{\rho_{6}} \text { and } r_{1} r_{3} r_{5}=r_{2} r_{4} r_{6}
$$

On the board, there is a table containing 100 columns and 2 rows. Sasha and Zhenya take turns filling in one cell of the table, writing either -1 or 1. Sasha makes the first move. After the table is completely filled, the products of all 100 numbers in each row are calculated. Similarly, for each column, the products of the two numbers in that column are calculated. Can Zhenya make her moves in such a way that in the filled table, the sum of the products of the numbers in the rows equals -2, and the sum of the products of the numbers in the columns equals 0?

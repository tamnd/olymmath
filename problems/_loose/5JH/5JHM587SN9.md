Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the angles at vertices $A$ and $B$, respectively, of an isosceles triangle $ABC$. Let the angle bisectors of these angles intersect the opposite sides of the triangle at $D$ and $E$, respectively. Prove that the acute angle between the lines $DE$ and $AB$ is not greater than $\frac{|\alpha-\beta|}{3}$.

(Dušan Đukić)

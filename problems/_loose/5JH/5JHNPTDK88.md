Given that $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \cdots, x_{1989}, x_{1990}$ are 1990 positive numbers, and
$$
\frac{1}{1+x_{1}}+\frac{1}{1+x_{2}}+\frac{1}{1+x_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1+x_{1989}}+\frac{1}{1+x_{1990}}=1 \text {. }
$$

Then among the 1990 positive numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \cdots, x_{1989}, x_{1990}$,
(A) There is at most 1 number less than 1.
(B) There must be a number greater than 1989.
(C) There must be a number less than 1989.
(D) There must be one that is 1990.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

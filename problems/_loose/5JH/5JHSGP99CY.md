For a right-angled triangle $\left(A=90^{\circ}\right)$, we construct squares on its legs, and denote the farthest points of these squares as $E$ and $F$. If the projections of these points onto the extension of the hypotenuse $B C$ are $E^{\prime}$ and $F^{\prime}$, then

$$
E^{\prime} B=C F^{\prime} \text { and } E E^{\prime}+F F^{\prime}=B C
$$

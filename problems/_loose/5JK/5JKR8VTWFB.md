Rhombus $ABCD$ is the base of a pyramid with vertex $S$. All its lateral faces are inclined to the base plane at the same angle of $60^{\circ}$. Points $M, N, K$, and $L$ are the midpoints of the sides of rhombus $ABCD$. A rectangular parallelepiped is constructed on rectangle $MNKL$ as its base. The edges of the upper face of the parallelepiped (opposite to face $MNKL$) intersect the lateral edges of the pyramid $SABCD$ at points $F, P, R$, and $Q$, respectively. The volume of the polyhedron with vertices at points $M, N, K, L, F, P, R, Q$ is $12 \sqrt{3}$, and the radius of the circle inscribed in rhombus $ABCD$ is 2.4. Find the side of rhombus $ABCD$.

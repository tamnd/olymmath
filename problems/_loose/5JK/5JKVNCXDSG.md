In the plane, a finite set of points $ Z $ is given with the property that no two distances between points in $ Z $ are equal. Points $ A, B $ belonging to $ Z $ are connected if and only if $ A $ is the nearest point to $ B $ or $ B $ is the nearest point to $ A $. Prove that no point in the set $ Z $ will be connected to more than five other points.

In the convex hexagon $ABCYXD$ we have $$ \angle{ACY}=\angle{BDX}=90^\circ $$ $$ \angle{BAC}=2\angle{CAY}, \angle{ABD}=2\angle{DBX} $$ $$ XY=DX+CY $$ Prove that $$ \sqrt{(CD-DX)(CD-CY)} \le \frac{AC+BD-AB}{2} $$ *Proposed by Iman Maghsoudi - Iran*

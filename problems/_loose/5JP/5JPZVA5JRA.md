Twins. The probability of twins being born in Shvambria is $p$, and triplets are not born in Shvambria.

a) Estimate the probability that a Shvambrian met on the street is one of a pair of twins?

b) In a certain Shvambrian family, there are three children. What is the probability that among them there is a pair of twins?

c) In Shvambrian schools, twins are always enrolled in the same class. There are a total of $N$ first-graders in Shvambria. What is the expected number of pairs of twins among them?

Given quadrilateral $A B C D$ with the extensions of its two pairs of opposite sides intersecting at $K, L$. Draw a line through $K, L$, and let the extensions of the diagonals $A C, B D$ intersect $K L$ at $G, F$ respectively. Prove that $\frac{1}{K F}, \frac{1}{K L}, \frac{1}{K G}$ form an arithmetic sequence.

Prove that if $S$ is the sum of the terms of a decreasing infinite geometric progression: $1, a^{p}, a^{2 p}, \ldots ; S_{1}$ is the sum of the terms of a decreasing infinite geometric progression $1, a^{q}, a^{2 q}, \ldots$, then

$$
S^{q}\left(S_{1}-1\right)^{p}=\left(S_{1}\right)^{p}(S-1)^{q}
$$

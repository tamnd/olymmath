(Mathematical Bulletin, Issue 2, 2007) Given $x, y, z \in \mathbf{R}_{+}, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, prove: $\frac{x}{n x+y+z}+\frac{y}{x+n y+z}+\frac{z}{x+y+n z} \leqslant \frac{3}{n+2}$.

Let $A, B, C, D$ be the vertices of a rhombus; furthermore, let

$$
K_{1} \text { be the circle passing through the points } B, C, D
$$

$K_{2}$ be the circle passing through the points $B, C, D$

$K_{3}$ be the circle passing through the points $B, C, D$

$K_{4}$ be the circle passing through the points $B, C, D$

It is to be proven that the tangents to $K_{1}$ and $K_{3}$ at $B$ form the same angle as the tangents to $K_{2}$ and $K_{4}$ at $A$.

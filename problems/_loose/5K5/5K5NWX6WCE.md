Let the set of numbers $M=\left\{x \left\lvert \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}<x<1\right.\right\}$, then
(A) $\log _{\frac{1}{2}} 3 \in M$.
(B) $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{\pi} \in M$.
(C) $\left(\sin 1+\cos \frac{1}{10}\right) \in M$.
(D) $10^{18^{\frac{\pi}{4}}} \in M$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

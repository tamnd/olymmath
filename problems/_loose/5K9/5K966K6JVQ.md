Let $B=\{2^1,2^2,2^3,\dots,2^{21}\}.$ Find the remainder when
$$ \sum_{m, n \in B: \ m<n}\gcd(m,n) $$
is divided by $1000,$ where the sum is taken over all pairs of elements $(m,n)$ of $B$ such that $m<n.$

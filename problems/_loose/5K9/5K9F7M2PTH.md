(PAMO 2021 P2) Let $\Gamma$ be a circle and $P$ a point outside $\Gamma$. The tangents to $\Gamma$ from $P$ touch $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $B$. Let $K$ be a point distinct from $A$ and $B$ on the segment $[A B]$. The circumcircle of triangle $P B K$ intersects the circle $\Gamma$ at point $T$. Let $P^{\prime}$ be the symmetric point of $P$ with respect to the point $K$. Show that $\widehat{P B T}=\widehat{P^{\prime} K A}$.

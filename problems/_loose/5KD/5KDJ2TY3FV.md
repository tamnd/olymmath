Consider two circles that are internally tangent at $A$. Let $C$ be a point on the smaller circle. The line $(A C)$ intersects the larger circle again at $P$. The tangent at $C$ to the smaller circle intersects the other circle at $D$ and $E$. Show that $(P E)$ is tangent to the circle $(A C E)$.

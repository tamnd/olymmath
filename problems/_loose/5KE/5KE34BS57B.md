Let $ABC$ be an acute scalene triangle, and let $M, N$, and $P$ be the midpoints of $[BC], [CA]$, and $[AB]$ respectively. The perpendicular bisectors of $[AB]$ and $[AC]$ intersect the line $(AM)$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively. Let $F$ be the intersection point of $(BD)$ and $(CE)$. Show that $A, N, F$, and $P$ are concyclic.

One hundred students, each with a number on their T-shirt, stood in a circle in the following sequence of their numbers: $1,2,3, \ldots, 100$. On command, they started to leave: the one with number 1 on the T-shirt stayed, the one with number 2 left, the one with number 3 stayed, the one with number 4 left, and so on, every other one around the circle until only one student remained. What number is on the T-shirt of this student?

The thirty-third question: Given that country K consists of 1024 cities (represented by $0, 1, \ldots, 1023$), and cities $m$ and $n$ are connected by exactly one road if and only if the binary representations of $m$ and $n$ differ by exactly one bit. Now, eight roads are closed. Prove that there exists a tour route such that each city in the country is visited exactly once, and the route ends at the starting point.

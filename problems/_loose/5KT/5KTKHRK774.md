On the legs $C A$ and $C B$ of the isosceles right $\triangle A B C$, points $D$ and $E$ are chosen, respectively, such that $\overline{C D}=\overline{C E}$. From points $D$ and $E$, normals to the line $A E$ are drawn, intersecting the hypotenuse $A B$ at points $K$ and $L$, respectively. Prove that $\overline{K L}=\overline{L B}$.

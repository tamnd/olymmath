Let $ r$ and $ n$ be nonnegative integers such that $ r \le n$ .

$ (a)$ Prove that: $ \frac{n+1-2r}{n+1-r} \binom{n}{r}$ is an integer.

$ (b)$ Prove that: $ \sum_{r=0}^{[n/2]}\frac{n+1-2r}{n+1-r} \binom{n}{r}<2^{n-2}$ for all $ n \ge 9$ .

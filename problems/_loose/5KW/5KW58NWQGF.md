Let point $O$ be a point inside quadrilateral $ABCD$. Take point $E$ outside $AB$ such that $\angle EAB = \angle OAD$ and $\angle ABE = \angle CBO$. Point $F$ is taken outside $CD$ such that $\angle CDF = \angle ADO$ and $\angle FCD = \angle OCB$. Prove:
(1) $\angle EOF = 180^{\circ} - |\angle BOC - \angle DOA|$;
(2) $OE \sin \angle AEB = OF \sin \angle DFC$.

Let $n$ be a positive integer and let

$$
f(x)=x^{n}+(k+1) x^{n-1}+(2 k+1) x^{n-2}+\cdots+((n-1) k+1) x+n k+1
$$

a) Prove that $f(1-k)=n+1$.

b) Prove that if $n \geq 3$ and $k$ is an integer $(k \neq 0)$, then the equation $f(x)=0$ has no integer solution.

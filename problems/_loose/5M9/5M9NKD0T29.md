[3+5] Before Alex, there are 100 closed boxes, each containing a red or blue ball. Alex has one pound in his account. He chooses one of the closed boxes and one of the two colors and bets some amount not exceeding the amount in his account (there is no requirement to bet whole pennies). The box is opened, and Alex's account increases or decreases by the bet amount depending on whether his prediction is correct or not. The game continues until all the boxes are opened. What is the maximum amount Alex can guarantee himself if he knows that

a) there is only one blue ball;

b) the number of blue balls is equal to $n$.

(Alex can bet 0, thereby seeing the color of the ball in the chosen box for free.)

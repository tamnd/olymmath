A sports event has a total of $2 n(n \geqslant 2)$ events, and each participant is registered for exactly two of them, with any two participants having at most one event in common. Assume that for each $k \in\{1,2, \cdots, n-1\}$, the number of events with no more than $k$ participants is less than $k$. Prove: There exist $2 n$ participants such that each event is attended by exactly two of them.

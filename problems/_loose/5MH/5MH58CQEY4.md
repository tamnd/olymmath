Show that points $A, B$ and $C$ are collinear if and only if for any point $M$ in the plane, there exist real numbers $a, b, c$, not all zero, with a sum of zero, such that

$$
a \cdot \overrightarrow{M A}+b \cdot \overrightarrow{M B}+c \cdot \overrightarrow{M C}=\overrightarrow{0}
$$

If $ a$ , $ b$ , and $ c$ are positive integers, the radicals $ \sqrt{a+\frac{b}{c}}$ and $ a\sqrt{\frac{b}{c}}$ are equal when and only when:

$ \textbf{(A)}\ a=b=c=1 \textbf{(B)}\ a=b\text{ and }c=a=1 \textbf{(C)}\ c=\frac{b(a^2-1)}{2}
\textbf{(D)}\ a=b \text{ and }c\text{ is any value} \textbf{(E)}\ a=b \text{ and }c=a-1$

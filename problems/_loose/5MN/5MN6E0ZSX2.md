Grinding Wheel. Three people bought a grinding wheel with a diameter of $20 \mathrm{~cm}$. How much should each of the companions grind off so that the wheel is divided equally, if 4 cm of the diameter is excluded for the hole? The practical value of each share is not considered, only the equal division of the total mass of the wheel.

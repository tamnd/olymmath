Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid such that $A B \| C D$ and let $P$ be the point on the extension of the diagonal $A C$ such that $C$ is between $A$ and $P$. If $X$ and $Y$ are midpoints of the segments $A B$ and $C D$, and $M, N$ intersection points of the lines $P X, P Y$ with $B C, D A$ (respectively) prove that $M N$ is parallel to $A B$.

Given the function
$$
f(x)=\ln (x+1)+\frac{a x}{x+1}(a \in \mathbf{R}) \text {. }
$$
(1) When $a=2$, find the equation of the tangent line to the graph of $y=f(x)$ at $x=0$;
(2) Determine the monotonicity of the function $f(x)$;
(3) Prove: $\ln \left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)>\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n^{2}}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$.

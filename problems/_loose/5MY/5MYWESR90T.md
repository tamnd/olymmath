Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$, and let $D$ be a point on $(AC)$ such that $A$ is located between $C$ and $D$, but is not the midpoint of $[CD]$.

We denote $\mathrm{d}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{d}_{2}$ as the internal and external bisectors of the angle $\widehat{\mathrm{BAC}}$, and $\Delta$ as the perpendicular bisector of $[B D]$. Finally, let $E$ and $F$ be the points of intersection of $\Delta$ with the lines $d_{1}$ and $d_{2}$, respectively.

Prove that the points $A, D, E$, and $F$ are concyclic.

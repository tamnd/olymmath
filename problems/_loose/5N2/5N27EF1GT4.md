A line consists of $N$ boys of pairwise distinct heights. It was divided into the smallest possible number of groups of consecutive boys, in each of which the boys stand in increasing order of height from left to right (groups of one person are possible). Then, in each group, the boys were rearranged in decreasing order of height from left to right. Prove that after $N-1$ such operations, the boys will stand in decreasing order of height from left to right.

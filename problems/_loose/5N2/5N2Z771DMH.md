Consider $n$ natural numbers $a_{1} \leqslant \ldots \leqslant a_{n} \leqslant 2 n$ such that for all $i, j \in\{1, \ldots, n\}$ with $i \neq j$, the LCM $a_{i} \vee a_{j}$ is strictly greater than $2 n$. Show that $a_{2}>\lfloor 2 n / 3\rfloor$.

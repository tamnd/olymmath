A set $M$ of real numbers will be called *special* if it has the properties:

(i) for each $x, y \in M, x\ne y$ , the numbers $x + y$ and $xy$ are not zero and exactly one of them is rational;
(ii) for each $x \in M, x^2$ is irrational.

Find the maximum number of elements of a *special* set.

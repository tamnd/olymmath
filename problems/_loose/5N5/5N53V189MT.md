$A B C D$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ are quadrilaterals in the plane such that the intersection points of the pairs of lines

$$
A B, C^{\prime} D^{\prime} ; B C, D^{\prime} A^{\prime} ; C D, A^{\prime} B^{\prime} ; D A, B^{\prime} C^{\prime} ; A C, B^{\prime} D^{\prime}
$$

lie on a line $e$. It is to be proven that the pair of lines $B D, A^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ also intersects on $e$, or both are parallel to $e$

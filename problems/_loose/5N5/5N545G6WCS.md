$P$ is inside $\triangle ABC$, $AB=AC$, the projections of $P$ on $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively, the altitude on $BC$ is $h$, the circumcenter of $\triangle BPC$ is $O$, and the midpoint of $BC$ is $M$ (Figure 4.12). Given $PD^2 = PE \cdot PF$, $PD + PF = PE$, prove:
(1) $\angle BPC = \angle A + \angle B$;
(2) $OP = h \frac{\sin \frac{A}{2}}{\cos^2 \frac{A}{2}}$;
(3) $\angle OBA = \frac{\pi}{2}$;
(4) $OA = \frac{h}{\cos^2 \frac{A}{2}}$;
(5) $PD = \frac{2h \sin \frac{A}{2}}{\sqrt{5} + 2 \sin \frac{A}{2}}$;
(6) $DM = \frac{h \tan \frac{A}{2}}{\sqrt{5} + 2 \sin \frac{A}{2}}$.

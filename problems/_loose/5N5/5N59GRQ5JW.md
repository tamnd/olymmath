Prove that the centers of the circles externally tangent to each side of any convex quadrilateral lie on the same circle.

(The circle externally tangent to side $AB$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is the circle that touches side $AB$ and the rays opposite to rays $AD$ and $BC$.)

(Jaroslav Švrček, Pavel Calábek)

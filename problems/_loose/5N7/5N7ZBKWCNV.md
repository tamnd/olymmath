Let $a, b>0$ and the functions $f, g:(0,+\infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that $\lim _{x \rightarrow a} f(x)=b^{3}$ and

$$
f\left(\frac{a x^{2}+b}{\sqrt{1+x^{4}}}\right) \cdot g(x)=x \text { for all } x>0
$$

Show that the function $g$ is not bounded.

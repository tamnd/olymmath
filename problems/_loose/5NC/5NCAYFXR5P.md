A three-digit number written with different digits has its middle digit equal to the arithmetic mean of the outer digits. Write the last digit before the first two and form the geometric mean of the thus obtained number and the original number. This way, we get the number that results from the original number by moving the first digit to the end of the other two. Determine the digits. - Are there digit triples with such properties in other number systems as well?

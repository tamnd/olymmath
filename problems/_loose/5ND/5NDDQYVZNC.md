Six islands are connected by the lines of one ferry and one hydrofoil company. Each pair of islands is connected (in both directions) by a line of exactly one of these two companies. Prove that it is possible to cyclically visit four islands using lines of only one company (i.e., there exist four islands $A, B, C$ and $D$ and a company whose boats travel on the lines $A \leftrightarrow B, B \leftrightarrow C$, $C \leftrightarrow D, D \leftrightarrow A$).

When the triangle $ABC$ is reflected over the center of its inscribed circle, we obtain the triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. Prove that if the side lengths of $ABC$ are $a, b, c$, then the perimeter of the hexagon formed by the intersection of $ABC$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ cannot be greater than

$$
\frac{2(a b+b c+c a)}{a+b+c} \text {. }
$$

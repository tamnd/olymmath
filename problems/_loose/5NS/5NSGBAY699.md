Given an arbitrary triangle $A B C$. On the line passing through vertex $A$ and perpendicular to side $B C$, two points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ are taken such that $\left|A A_{1}\right|=\left|A A_{2}\right|=|B C|$ ( $A_{1}$ is closer to line $B C$ than $A_{2}$).

Similarly, on the line perpendicular to $A C$ and passing through $B$, points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ are taken such that $\left|B B_{1}\right|=\left|B B_{1}\right|=|A C|$. Prove that segments $A_{1} B_{2}$ and $A_{2} B_{1}$ are equal in length and perpendicular to each other.

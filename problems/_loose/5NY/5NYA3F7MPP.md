Suppose on a square grid paper with $100 \times 100$ cells, several non-intersecting broken lines are drawn along the grid lines. These broken lines do not intersect each other, their endpoints are on the sides of the square, but the broken lines are strictly contained within the square. Prove that, apart from the vertices of the square paper, there exist nodes (inside the square or on its sides) that do not belong to any broken line.

In a sport competition, $m$ teams have participated. We know that each two teams have competed exactly one time and the result is winning a team and losing the other team (i.e. there is no equal result). Prove that there exists a team $x$ such that for each team $y,$ either $x$ wins $y$ or there exists a team $z$ for which $x$ wins $z$ and $z$ wins $y.$ *[i.e. prove that in every tournament there exists a king.]*

Given that obtuse $\triangle A B C$ is inscribed in $\odot O, \angle C>90^{\circ}, C^{\prime}$ is the reflection of point $C$ over $A B, A C^{\prime}$ intersects $\odot O$ at point $E, B C^{\prime}$ intersects $\odot O$ at point $F, M$ is the midpoint of $A B, M C^{\prime}$ intersects $\odot O$ at point $N$ (point $C^{\prime}$ is between $M$ and $N$), $K$ is the midpoint of $E F$. Prove: $A B, C N, K C^{\prime}$ are concurrent.
(2011, Kazakhstan Mathematical Olympiad)

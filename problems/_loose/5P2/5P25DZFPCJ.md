A ring $(A,+, \cdot)$ has property (P) if $A$ is finite and the multiplicative group of its invertible elements is isomorphic to a subgroup different from $\{0\}$ of the additive group $(A,+)$. Show that:

(a) If a ring has property (P), then the number of its elements is even.

(b) For infinitely many natural numbers $n$, there exist rings with exactly $n$ elements that have property (P).

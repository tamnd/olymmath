Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral with $A D=B C$ and $P$ the intersection of its diagonals. Let $F$ and $E$ be variable points on the segments $[A D]$ and $[B C]$ respectively, such that $B E=D F$. Let $R$ and $Q$ be the points of intersection of $(EF)$ with $(AC)$ and $(BD)$ respectively.

Show that the circumcircle of $P Q R$ has a second fixed point as $E$ and $F$ vary.

Let $\triangle A B C$ have side $B C$ touch its incircle and corresponding excircle at $K$ and $L$, respectively. Let $P$ be the projection of the incenter of $\triangle A B C$ onto the perpendicular bisector of $B C$. It is known that the circumcircle of $\triangle A K L$ intersects the tangents to the circumcircle at $K$ and $L$ at a point on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$. Prove that the lines $A B$ and $A C$ are both tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle P K L$.

Let $a$ and $n$ be positive integers such that:
1. $a^{2^n}-a$ is divisible by $n$ ,
2. $\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n} k^{2024}a^{2^k}$ is *not* divisible by $n$ .

Prove that $n$ has a prime factor *smaller* than $2024$ .

Let $(X, \leqslant)$ be a totally ordered set, and let $G=(V, E)$ be a graph on $V:=[X]^{2}$, and $E:=$ $\left\{(x, y)\left(x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}\right) \mid x<y=x^{\prime}<y^{\prime}\right\}$.
(i) Prove: $G$ does not contain a triangle.
(ii) Prove: When choosing $|X|$ sufficiently large, $\chi(G)$ can be arbitrarily large.

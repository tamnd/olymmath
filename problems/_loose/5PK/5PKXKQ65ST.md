If $w = a + bi$ , where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers, then $\Re(w) = a$ and $\Im(w) = b$ . Let $z=c+di$ , where $c, d \ge 0$ . If
\begin{align*}
\Re(z) + \Im (z) & = 7,
\Re(z^2) + \Im(z^2) & = 17,
\end{align*}
then compute $\left | \Re\left (z^3 \right ) + \Im \left (z^3 \right ) \right |$ .

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

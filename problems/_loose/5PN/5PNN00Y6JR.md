Given a triangle $ ABC$ with angle $ C \geq 60^{\circ}$ . Prove that:

$ \left(a + b\right) \cdot \left(\frac {1}{a} + \frac {1}{b} + \frac {1}{c} \right) \geq 4 + \frac {1}{\sin\left(\frac {C}{2}\right)}.$

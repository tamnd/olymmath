Given the complex sequence $\left\{z_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $z_{n+1}=z_{n}^{2}+1(n=1,2, \ldots)$, and it is known that $z_{2021}=1$.
(1) Prove: $\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{1}\right)<1$.
(2) Let the product of all possible values of $z_{1}$ be $P$, prove: $P=z_{4039}$.

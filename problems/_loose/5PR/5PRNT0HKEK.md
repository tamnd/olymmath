In $\triangle A B C$ and $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, prove:
(1) $\frac{\cos A}{\sin A}+\frac{\cos B}{\sin B}+\frac{\cos C}{\sin C} \geqslant \frac{\cos A^{\prime}}{\sin A}+\frac{\cos B^{\prime}}{\sin B}+\frac{\cos C^{\prime}}{\sin C}$;
(2) $\cot A^{\prime}(\cot B+\cot C)+\cot B^{\prime}(\cot C+\cot A)+\cot C^{\prime}$
- $(\cot A+\cot B) \geqslant 2$.

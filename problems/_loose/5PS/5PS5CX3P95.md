Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $S_n = \{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ . For a permutation $\sigma$ of $S_n$ and an integer $a \in S_n$ , let $d(a)$ be the least positive integer $d$ for which $$\underbrace{\sigma(\sigma(\ldots \sigma(a) \ldots))}_{d \text{ applications of } \sigma} = a$$(or $-1$ if no such integer exists). Compute the value of $n$ for which there exists a permutation $\sigma$ of $S_n$ satisfying the equations $$\begin{aligned} d(1) + d(2) + \ldots + d(n) &= 2017, \frac{1}{d(1)} + \frac{1}{d(2)} + \ldots + \frac{1}{d(n)} &= 2. \end{aligned}$$

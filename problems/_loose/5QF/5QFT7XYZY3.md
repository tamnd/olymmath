There are several villages, some of which are connected by roads in such a way that from any village to any other, there is a unique (if not going back and forth) way. There are exactly 20 villages from which a single road leads out. Prove that it is possible to introduce 10 bus routes (each route connects two villages by the shortest path), using which (in both directions), one can travel from any village to any other.

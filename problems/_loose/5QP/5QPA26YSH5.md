Let $a,b$ be real numbers such that the equation $$ x^3+\sqrt{3}(a-1)x^2-6ax+b=0 $$ has three real roots. Prove that $|b|\leq |a+1|^3$ .

>Clarification: $|x|$ indicates the absolute value of $x$ . For example, $|5|=5$ ; $|-1.23|=1.23$ ; etc

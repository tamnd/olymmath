From five elements, fourteen sets are formed, and three conditions are met:

1) each set contains at least one element;
2) any two sets have at least one common element;
3) no two sets are identical.

Prove that from the original five elements, it is possible to form one more (fifteenth) set that, together with the original fourteen sets, also satisfies the above three conditions.

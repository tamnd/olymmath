Prove the following theorem:

If $D, E, F$ are the feet of the altitudes of an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$, then the altitudes of the triangle $\triangle A B C$ bisect the interior angles of the triangle $\angle D E F$!

(Since the proof for all three angles is analogous, it suffices to prove it for the angle $\angle E F D$.)

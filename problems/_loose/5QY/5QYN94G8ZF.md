Six bags contain $18, 19, 21, 23, 25$ and 34 marbles, respectively, one of which has all its marbles cracked, while the other five bags do not have such cracked marbles. Jenny took three of the bags, and George took two of the bags, leaving only the bag with the cracked marbles. If Jenny got exactly twice as many marbles as George, the number of marbles with cracks is
(A) 18.
(B) 19.
(C) 21.
(D) 23.
(E) 25.
(36th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1985)

\section*{Problem 6B - 151246B}

In mathematical statistics, sums of the following form are often needed:

$$
M=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{2 n-1}{2 k} ; N=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{2 n}{2 k} ; m=\frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{2 n}{2 k}
$$

where $n$ is a natural number with $n \geq 1$.

a) Calculate the sums $M, N$ and $m$.

b) Let $f$ be the function defined for all real $x$ by

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{n}(k-x)^{2}\binom{2 n}{2 k}
$$

Calculate $f(x)$ and show that $f$ has a minimum value and that this value is attained exactly for $x=m$.

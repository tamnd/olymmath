In a kindergarten group, there are 26 children. When the children went out for a walk, each had two mittens of the same color, and the mittens of different children were of different colors. During the walk, the children formed pairs three times (not necessarily in the same way). During the first formation, the children in each pair exchanged left mittens, and during the second formation, they exchanged right mittens. When they formed pairs for the third time, it turned out that each pair of children had two mittens of one color and two of another. Prove that at this moment, there is a child with matching mittens.

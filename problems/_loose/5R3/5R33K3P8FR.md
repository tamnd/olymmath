The nonnegative real numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $a + b = 1$ . Prove that:

$$ \frac{1}{2} \leq \frac{a^3+b^3}{a^2+b^2} \leq 1 $$ When do we have equality in the right inequality and when in the left inequality?

*Proposed by Walther Janous*

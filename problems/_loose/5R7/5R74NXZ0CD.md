Consider the rhombus $ABCD$ where $AB=6 \text{cm}$ and $m(\angle BAD)=60^\circ$. From the same side of the plane $(ABC)$, perpendiculars $AM$ and $CQ$ are raised to the plane $(ABC)$ such that $AM=9 \text{cm}$ and $CQ=3 \text{cm}$.

a) Prove that the planes $(MBD)$ and $(QBD)$ are perpendicular.

b) Calculate the distance between the lines $BD$ and $MQ$.

c) Determine the cosine of the angle formed by the planes $(MBQ)$ and $(ABC)$.

Given: In $\triangle A B C$, the excircle opposite to $\angle B$ is tangent to $C A$ at point $D$, and the excircle opposite to $\angle C$ is tangent to $A B$ at point $E$. A line is drawn through the midpoints $M$ and $N$ of $D E$ and $B C$ respectively. Prove: The line $M N$ is parallel to the angle bisector $A F$ of $\angle A$, and bisects the perimeter of $\triangle A B C$.

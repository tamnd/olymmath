In triangle $ABC$, $BC > AC$. We draw the internal angle bisector $f$ at $C$ and the line $g$ perpendicular to $f$ through $C$. We also draw lines parallel to $AC$ and $BC$ through the midpoint $C_1$ of side $AB$. These parallel lines intersect $f$ at points $F_1$ and $F_2$, respectively, and $g$ at points $G_1$ and $G_2$, respectively. Prove that the triangles $C_1 F_1 F_2$ and $C_1 G_1 G_2$ are isosceles. Express their bases in terms of the sides of triangle $ABC$.

Given two triangles $A B C$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. It is known that the lines $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ intersect at one point $O$, the lines $A A_{1}, B C_{1}$ and $C B_{1}$ intersect at one point $O_{1}$, and the lines $A C_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C A_{1}$ intersect at one point $O_{2}$. Prove that the lines $A B_{1}, B A_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ also intersect at one point $O_{3}$ (the theorem of triply perspective triangles).

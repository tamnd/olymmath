Find any polynomial of the third degree of the form $P_{3}(x)=a x^{3}+b x^{2}+c x+d$, for which the inequality

$$
\left|P_{3}(x)-\frac{x}{1-\frac{x}{2}}\right|<\frac{1}{75}
$$

is satisfied for $0 \leq x \leq \frac{1}{2}$.

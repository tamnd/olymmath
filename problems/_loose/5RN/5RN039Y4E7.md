Determine all functions $f$ and $g$ that are defined for all nonnegative real numbers $x$, have real function values, and satisfy the following conditions:

(1) For all $x \geq 0$, $f(x) \geq 1$ and $g(x) \geq 0$.

(2) For all $x \geq 0$, $(f(x))^{2}-(g(x))^{2}=1$.

(3) For all $x \geq 0$ and all $y \geq 0$,

$$
f\left(\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right)=f(x) \cdot f(y)+g(x) \cdot g(y)
$$

(4) For all $x \geq 0$ and all $y \geq 0$,

$$
g\left(\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right)=f(x) \cdot g(y)+g(x) \cdot f(y)
$$

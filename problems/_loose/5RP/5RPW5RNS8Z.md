All sailors in these teams want to serve on a submarine and have passed a strict selection process. The last selection is based on height. In team A, the average height of the sailors is 166 cm. In team B, the median height of the sailors is 167 cm. In team C, the tallest sailor is 169 cm. In team D, the mode of the sailors' heights is 167 cm. In which team can at least half of the sailors definitely serve on a submarine?

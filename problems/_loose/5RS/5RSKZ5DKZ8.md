For the approximate construction of the side of a regular $n$-gon inscribed in a circle, the following procedure is known. Over a diameter $AB$ of the circle, we construct an equilateral triangle $ABC$. We divide the diameter $AB$ into $n$ equal parts, and let the 2nd division point from $A$ be $D$. Denoting the intersection point of line $CD$ with the circle, farther from $C$, as $E$, the approximate length of the side is $AE$. Determine the percentage error of this construction for $n=3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10$ and 36.

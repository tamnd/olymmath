Prove that these polynomials are irreducible in $\mathbb{Q}[x]:$
a) $\frac{{{x^p}}}{{p!}} + \frac{{{x^{p - 1}}}}{{(p - 1)!}} + ... + \frac{{{x^2}}}{2} + x + 1,$ with $p$ is a prime number.
b) $x^{2^n}+1,$ with $n$ is a positive integer.

Consider the cube $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ and $N \in(C D)$ such that $D N=\frac{1}{3} D C$.
a) Determine the edge of the cube knowing that the area of triangle $A^{\prime} A N$ is $\frac{8 \sqrt{10}}{3} \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$.

b) Determine the cosine of the angle between the planes ( $\left.A^{\prime} A N\right)$ and ( $\left.D^{\prime} D A\right)$.

c) Calculate the distance from $D$ to the plane $\left(A^{\prime} A N\right)$.

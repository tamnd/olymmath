If for all $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}, a^{n+2}+3 a^{n}+1$ is not a prime, then the positive integer $a$ is called "beautiful". Prove: in the set $\{1,2, \cdots, 2018\}$ there are at least 500 beautiful positive integers.

$$\frac{(\sqrt{s_1x_1} +\sqrt{s_2x_2}+\sqrt{s_3x_3}+\sqrt{s_4x_4}+\sqrt{s_5x_5})^2}{a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + a_3x_3 + a_4x_4 + a_5x_5}$$
($s_k = a_1 + a_2 +... + a_k$)

Denote by $ \mathcal{C}$ the family of all configurations $ C$ of $ N > 1$ distinct
points on the sphere $ S^2,$ and by $ \mathcal{H}$ the family of all closed hemispheres $ H$
of $ S^2.$ Compute:

$ \max_{H\in\mathcal{H}} \min_{C\in\mathcal{C}}|H\cap C|$, $ \min_{H\in\mathcal{H}} \max_{C\in\mathcal{C}}|H\cap C|$

$ \max_{C\in\mathcal{C}} \min_{H\in\mathcal{H}}|H\cap C|$ and $ \min_{C\in\mathcal{C}} \max_{H\in\mathcal{H}}|H\cap C|.$

In a Cartesian coordinate system, for every lattice point $(x, y)$ with integer coordinates and different from the origin, we place the center of a short needle such that the angle $\varphi$ between the needle and the $x$-axis is given by

$$
\operatorname{tg} \varphi=\frac{y^{2}-2 x y-x^{2}}{y^{2}+2 x y-x^{2}}
$$

The overall image formed by the needles resembles something similar to what we see when visualizing the magnetic field lines of a plane with iron filings. What curves do we suspect to form here from the needles, and how can we verify our hypothesis?

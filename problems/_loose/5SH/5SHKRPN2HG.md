The sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n=1}^{\infty}$ of natural numbers has the property that for every $n \geqq 1$ it holds that $a_{n+1}=a_{n}+b_{n}$, where $b_{n}$ is the number with the digits in reverse order compared to the number $a_{n}$ (the representation of $b_{n}$ may start with one or more zeros compared to the representation of $a_{n}$). For example, for $a_{1}=170$ we have $a_{2}=241, a_{3}=383, a_{4}=766, \ldots$ Decide whether $a_{7}$ can be a prime number.

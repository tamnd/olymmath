Let $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ be the roots of the quadratic equation

$$
x^{2} + p x + q = 0
$$

with rational coefficients. Prove that the expressions:

$$
x_{1}^{4} + x_{1}^{3} x_{2} + x_{1}^{2} x_{2}^{2} + x_{1} x_{2}^{3} + x_{2}^{4} \text { and } x_{1}^{4} x_{2} + x_{1}^{3} x_{2}^{2} + x_{1}^{2} x_{2}^{3} + x_{1} x_{2}^{4}
$$

are rational.

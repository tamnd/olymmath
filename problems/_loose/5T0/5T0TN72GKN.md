Find a true fraction greater than $\frac{1}{3}$ with the following properties:

a) there exists a natural number $x$ such that the value of the fraction does not change if the numerator is increased by $x$ and the denominator is multiplied by $x$,
b) the numerator and the denominator are coprime numbers.

Determine all such fractions.

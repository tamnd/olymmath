In a convex pentagon $P$, all diagonals were drawn, as a result of which it was divided into ten triangles and one pentagon $P^{\prime}$. From the sum of the areas of the triangles adjacent to the sides of $P$, the area of $P^{\prime}$ was subtracted; the result was the number $N$. Performing the same operations with the pentagon $P^{\prime}$, the number $N^{\prime}$ was obtained. Prove that $N > N^{\prime}$.

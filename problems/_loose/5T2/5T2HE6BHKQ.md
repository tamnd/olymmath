Given three non-negative numbers $a, b, c$. It is known that $a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4} \leq 2\left(a^{2} b^{2}+b^{2} c^{2}+c^{2} a^{2}\right)$.

a) Prove that each of them is not greater than the sum of the other two.

b) Prove that $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \leq 2(a b+b c+c a)$.

c) Does the inequality from part b) imply the original inequality?

The following inequality needs to be proven:

$$
\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(i+\frac{1}{n \cdot n!}\right)<n!+1
$$

The notation $\prod_{i=1}^{n} f(i)$ represents the product $f(1) \cdot f(2) \cdot \ldots \cdot f(n)$. $n!=\prod_{i=1}^{n} i$.

A hockey tournament is played in a "everyone against everyone" system. During the tournament, each pair of teams will meet exactly once. The tournament is played over several rounds. When the number of teams is even, each team plays one match in a round; when the number of teams is odd, one team has a bye in each round. For a draw, each opponent receives one point. If the match does not end in a draw, the winner gets two points, and the loser gets no points. The ranking in the table is primarily determined by the number of points, and in case of a tie in points, by the goal difference. After several rounds, no pair of teams had the same number of points. Prove that in this case, the team at the bottom of the table has lost all hope of overall victory. Solve the problem for a tournament with

a) ten teams,

b) eleven teams.

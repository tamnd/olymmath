1973 positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{1973}$ are such that

$$
a_{1}^{a_{1}}=a_{2}^{a_{3}}=a_{3}^{a_{4}}=\ldots=\left(a_{1972}\right)^{a_{1973}}=\left(a_{1973}\right)^{a_{1}}
$$

Prove that $a_{1}=a_{1973}$.

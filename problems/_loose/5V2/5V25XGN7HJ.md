In the plane of quadrilateral $A B C D$, a point $O$ is given. Prove that

$$
[(A O B)+(C O D)]-[(B O C)-(D O A)]=4(K O L)
$$

where $K$ is the midpoint of diagonal $A C$, and $L$ is the midpoint of diagonal $B D$ (the areas of the triangles given in the formula are to be considered positive or negative depending on the orientations of these triangles).

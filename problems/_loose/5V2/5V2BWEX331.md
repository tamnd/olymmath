Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with circumcenter $O$ . Rays $ \overrightarrow{OB}$ and $ \overrightarrow{DC}$ intersect at $E$ , and rays $ \overrightarrow{OC}$ and $ \overrightarrow{AB}$ intersect at $F$ . Suppose that $AE = EC = CF = 4$ , and the circumcircle of $ODE$ bisects $\overline{BF}$ . Find the area of triangle $ADF$ .

Define a function $y=f(x)$ on $\mathbf{R}$, which satisfies $f(0) \neq 0$, when $x>0$, $f(x)>1$, and for any $a, b \in \mathbf{R}$, $f(a+b)=f(a) \cdot f(b)$.
(1) Prove: $f(0)=1$;
(2) Prove: For any $x \in \mathbf{R}$, $f(x)>0$ always holds;
(3) Prove: $f(x)$ is an increasing function on $\mathbf{R}$;
(4) If $f(x) \cdot f\left(2 x-x^{2}\right)>1$, find the range of $x$.

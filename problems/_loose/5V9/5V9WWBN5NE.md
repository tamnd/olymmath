Let ABCD be a trapezoid in which sides $A B$ and $C D$ are parallel. Consider a point $P$ on the line (AC) such that $C$ is interior to the segment $[A P]$, and let $X$ and $\mathrm{Y}$ be the midpoints of sides $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{CD}$, respectively. The line (PX) intersects the line (BC) at $\mathrm{N}$ and the line (PY) intersects the line $(A D)$ at $M$. Show that the line $(M N)$ is parallel to the line (AB).

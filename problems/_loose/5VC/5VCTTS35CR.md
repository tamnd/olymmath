On the boundary line of an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with side length $1 \text{m}$, three points $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are moving, with $P_{1}$ moving at a speed of $1 \text{m/s}$, $P_{2}$ at a speed of $\sqrt{2} \text{m/s}$, and $P_{3}$ at a speed of $\sqrt{3} \text{m/s}$.

At the beginning (time $t=0$), $P_{1}$ is at $A$, $P_{2}$ is at $B$, and $P_{3}$ is at $C$.

The direction of movement for all three points is uniformly in the direction of circulation from $A$ to $B$, from $B$ to $C$, and from $C$ to $A$.

Investigate whether there is a time $t>0$ at which $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ again form the vertices of an equilateral triangle (where the case $P_{1}=P_{2}=P_{3}$ is also considered as a special case of an equilateral triangle).

At point $O$ on the shore of the Dongjiang Lake (the lake shore can be considered a straight line), a rescue boat is parked. Due to the sudden breakage of the rope, the boat is blown away, with its direction forming a $15^{\circ}$ angle with the shore, at a speed of $2.5 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. At the same time, a person starts chasing the boat from the same location on the shore. It is known that his speed on the shore is $4 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$, and his swimming speed in the water is $2 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. Can this person catch up with the boat? If the boat's speed changes, what is the maximum speed of the boat that can still be caught by this person?

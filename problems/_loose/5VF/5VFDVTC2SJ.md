In $\triangle A B C$, the sides opposite to $\angle A, \angle B, \angle C$ are $a, b, c$ respectively. There is an inequality $\left|\frac{\sin A}{\sin A+\sin B}+\frac{\sin B}{\sin B+\sin C}+\frac{\sin C}{\sin C+\sin A}-\frac{3}{2}\right|<m$, then $m=\frac{8 \sqrt{2}-5 \sqrt{5}}{6}$ is the smallest upper bound.

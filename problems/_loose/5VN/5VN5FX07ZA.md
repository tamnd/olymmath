In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the incircle of $\triangle ABD$ touches $BD$, $AD$, and $AB$ at points $A_{1}$, $A_{1}^{\prime}$, and $A_{1}^{\prime \prime}$, respectively. The incircle of $\triangle BCD$ touches $BC$, $CD$, and $DB$ at points $C_{1}^{\prime}$, $C_{1}^{\prime \prime}$, and $C_{1}$, respectively. Similarly, define (the other two incircles are not shown) $B_{1}$, $B_{1}^{\prime}$, $B_{1}^{\prime \prime}$; $D_{1}$, $D_{1}^{\prime}$, $D_{1}^{\prime \prime}$, where $B_{1}$ and $D_{1}$ are on $AC$, $B_{1}^{\prime \prime}$, $B_{1}^{\prime}$, $D_{1}^{\prime \prime}$, and $D_{1}^{\prime}$ are on $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$, respectively. Prove that $A_{1}C_{1}=B_{1}D_{1}$, $A_{1}^{\prime}C_{1}^{\prime}=B_{1}^{\prime}D_{1}^{\prime}$, $A_{1}^{\prime \prime}C_{1}^{\prime \prime}=B_{1}^{\prime \prime}D_{1}^{\prime \prime}$.

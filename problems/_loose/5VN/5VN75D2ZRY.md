Quadrilateral $ABCD$ has side lengths $AB = 24$, $BC = 40$, $CD = 56$, and $AD = 56$ with $\angle ADC = 60^o$. Point $E$ is chosen in the plane such that $\overline{DE}$ is perpendicular to $\overline{DB}$, and points $A$, $C$, and $E$ are collinear. Extend $\overline{BC}$ to intersect $\overline{DE}$ at point $F$, and $\overline{DC}$ is extended to point $G$ such that line $\overline{BG}$ is perpendicular to $\overline{FG}$. Given that the area of quadrilateral $CGEF$ can be expressed as $a\sqrt{b}$, and $a$ and $b$ are positive integers and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime, find the remainder when $a + b$ is divided by $1000$.

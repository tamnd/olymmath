Let $A B C D$ be a chordal quadrilateral with circumcircle $k$. Let $S$ be the intersection of $A B$ and $C D$ and let $T$ be the intersection of the tangents to $k$ in $A$ and $C$. Show that $A D T S$ is a chordal quadrilateral if and only if $B D$ bisects the line $A C$.

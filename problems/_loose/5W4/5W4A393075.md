There are 200 weights with masses of $1, 2, \ldots, 200$ grams. They were distributed on two scales, with 100 weights on each, and the scales balanced. On each weight, the number of weights on the opposite scale that are lighter than it was recorded.

Prove that the sum of the numbers recorded on the weights on the left scale is equal to the sum of the numbers recorded on the weights on the right scale.

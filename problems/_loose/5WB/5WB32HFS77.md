Let $n$ be a natural number. Inside a circle, there are points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$, and on the circle, there are points $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots, B_{n}$ such that the segments $\overline{A_{1} B_{1}}, \overline{A_{2} B_{2}}, \ldots, \overline{A_{n} B_{n}}$ are pairwise disjoint. A grasshopper can jump from point $A_{i}$ to point $A_{j}$ (for $i, j \in\{1, \ldots, n\}, i \neq j$) if and only if the segment $\overline{A_{i} A_{j}}$ does not pass through any internal point of the segments $\overline{A_{1} B_{1}}, \overline{A_{2} B_{2}}, \ldots, \overline{A_{n} B_{n}}$.

Show that the grasshopper can reach any point $A_{j}$ from any point $A_{i}$ through a series of jumps.

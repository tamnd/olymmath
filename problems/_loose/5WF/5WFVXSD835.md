On an infinite sheet of graph paper, $N$ cells are colored black. Prove that from this sheet, a finite number of squares can be cut out such that two conditions are met: 1) all black cells lie within the cut-out squares; 2) in any cut-out square $K$, the area of black cells will be at least 0.2 and at most 0.8 of the area of $K$.

For a triangle, the following is required:

The lengths of the sides $a, b, c$ measured in cm should be natural numbers, the side length $a$ should be exactly $36\%$ of the perimeter $u$, and the side length $b$ should be exactly $48\%$ of the perimeter.

a) Investigate whether there is a triangle under these conditions with a perimeter $u=25 \mathrm{~cm}$! If this is the case, give its side lengths!

b) Investigate whether there is a triangle under the given conditions with a perimeter $u>25$ $\mathrm{cm}$! If this is the case, give its side lengths!

c) Determine all lengths $u$ that are less than $100 \mathrm{~cm}$ and can occur as the perimeter of a triangle whose side lengths meet the stated requirements! Determine the side lengths of such a triangle for each of these values $u$!

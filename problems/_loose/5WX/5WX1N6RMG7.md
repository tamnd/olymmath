Given a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$ with circumcircle $\odot O$ and incircle $\odot I$, the line $BI$ intersects $AC$ at point $E$, and the line $CI$ intersects $AB$ at point $F$. The circle passing through point $E$ and tangent to $OB$ at point $B$ intersects $\odot O$ at a second point $M$. The circle passing through point $F$ and tangent to $OC$ at point $C$ intersects $\odot O$ at a second point $N$.

The lines $ME$ and $NF$ intersect $\odot O$ at second points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. The line $EF$ intersects $BC$ at point $K$, and the line $PQ$ intersects $BC$ and $EF$ at points $G$ and $H$, respectively. Prove that the median from vertex $G$ in $\triangle GKH$ is perpendicular to the line $OI$.

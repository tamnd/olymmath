Upon arrival at the final point, each of them, without delay, turns around and heads back in the opposite direction. Each driver counted how many times they met other buses throughout the day, and the total for all drivers was 300. Determine how many buses there were and how many trips they made. In your answer, provide the product of the number of buses and the number of trips.

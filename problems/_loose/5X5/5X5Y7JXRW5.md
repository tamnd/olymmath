Let $M_1, M_2, M_3$ be the reflections of the orthocenter $M$ of a given acute-angled triangle over its sides. Prove that the diagonals connecting opposite vertices of the convex hexagon formed by the intersections of the sides of the triangle $M_1 M_2 M_3$ and the given triangle pass through the point $M$.

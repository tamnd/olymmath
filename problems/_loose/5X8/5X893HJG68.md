Show that if

$$
a x^{2}+b y^{2}+c z^{2}+2 d y z+2 e z x+2 f x y=0
$$

then

$$
\begin{gathered}
(d y z+e z x+f x y)^{2}-b c y^{2} z^{2}-c a z^{2} x^{2}-a b x^{2} y^{2} \equiv \\
\equiv \frac{1}{4}(x \sqrt{a}+y \sqrt{b}+z \sqrt{c})(x \sqrt{a}-y \sqrt{b}+z \sqrt{c})(x \sqrt{a}+y \sqrt{b}-z \sqrt{c})(x \sqrt{a}-y \sqrt{b}-z \sqrt{c})
\end{gathered}
$$

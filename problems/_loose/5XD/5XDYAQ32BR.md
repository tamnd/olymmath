Let $X=\{1,2, \cdots, 10\}, f: X \rightarrow X$ be a mapping satisfying
(1) $f \circ f=I_{X}$, where $f \circ f$ is the composite mapping, and $I_{X}$ is the identity mapping on $X$;
(2) $|f(i)-i| \leqslant 2$, for any $i \in X$.
Find the number of mappings $f$.

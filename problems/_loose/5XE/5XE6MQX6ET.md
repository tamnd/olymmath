Given quadratic trinomials $P(x)=x^2+ax+b$ and $Q(x)=x^2+cx+d$ , where $a>c$ . It is known that for every real $t$ and $s$ with $t+s=1$ the polynomial $B(x)=tP(x)+sQ(x)$ has at least one real root.
Prove that $bc \geq ad$ .

Define the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{0}=1, a_{1}=2.1, \\
a_{n+1} a_{n-1}^{2}+2 a_{n-1}^{2} a_{n}=a_{n}^{3}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: For any non-negative integer $k$, we have
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{k} \frac{1}{a_{i}}<2.016 .
$$

a game, but to win the series, team $A$ must win 2 games, and team $B$ must win 3 games. Then the probability that team $A$ wins is
(A) 11 to 5.
(B) 5 to 2.
(C) 8 to 3.
(D) 3 to 2.
(E) 13 to 6.
(22nd American High School Mathematics Examination, 1971)

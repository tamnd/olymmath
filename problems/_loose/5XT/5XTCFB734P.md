* Find the positive integer solutions of the equation: $n!+1=(-1)^{2}$.

The equation simplifies to $n!+1=1$, since $(-1)^2=1$. Therefore, $n! = 0$. The only value of $n$ that satisfies this equation is $n=0$, but since we are looking for positive integer solutions, there are no solutions.

Let $M$ be the set of all positive integers that satisfy the following conditions: in their decimal representation, there are $n$ ones and $n$ twos, and no other digits. Let $N$ be the set of all positive integers that satisfy the following conditions: in their decimal representation, they are composed only of the digits 1, 2, 3, and 4, and the number of 1s is equal to the number of 2s, and they are $n$-digit numbers. Prove that $|M|=|N|$.

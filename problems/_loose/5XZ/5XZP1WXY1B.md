Show that $J_{A}$ and $H_{A}$ belong to the circle $\mathcal{E}$.

We have thus shown that:

- the points $A, B, C, K_{A}, K_{B}, K_{C}, L_{A}, L_{B}, L_{C}$ all belong to the circumcircle $\mathcal{C}$;
- the points $I_{A}, I_{B}, I_{C}, H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}, J_{A}, J_{B}, J_{C}$ all belong to the nine-point circle $\mathcal{E}$;
- the points $O, G, E$ and $H$ all lie on the line $(O H)$, which we will henceforth call the Euler line of the triangle $A B C$.

Let $g(m, n)$ denote the number of surjective functions from an $m$-element set $A$ to an $n$-element set $B$. Prove that:
$$
g(m, n)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(-1)^{n-k}\binom{n}{k} k^{m} .
$$

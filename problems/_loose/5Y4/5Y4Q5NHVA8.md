Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $A'$ be the reflection of point $A$ over the segment $[BC]$. Let $D$ be a point on the segment $[BC]$ different from points $B$ and $C$. The circumcircle of triangle $ABD$ intersects the segment $[AC]$ at a point $E$. The circumcircle of triangle $ACD$ intersects the segment $[AB]$ at a point $F$. The lines $(A'C)$ and $(DE)$ intersect at a point $P$, and the lines $(A'B)$ and $(DF)$ intersect at a point $Q$. Show that the lines $(AD)$, $(BP)$, and $(CQ)$ are concurrent or parallel.

The 68th question: Let $\mathrm{p}$ be a prime number, and A be a set of positive integers, satisfying the following conditions:
(1) The set of prime factors of the elements in $\mathrm{A}$ contains $\mathrm{p}-1$ elements;
(2) For any non-empty subset of $A$, the product of its elements is not a $p$-th power of an integer. Find the maximum number of elements in $\mathrm{A}$.

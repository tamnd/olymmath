Let $g_{n}$ denote the number of different permutations of $2 n(n \geqslant 2)$ distinct elements $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}$, $b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ such that $a_{k}$ and $b_{k}(k=1,2, \cdots, n)$ are not adjacent. Find the counting formula for $g_{n}$.

In space, two intersecting planes $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are given. On the line of their intersection, a point A is given. Prove that among all lines lying in the plane $\alpha$ and passing through point $A$, the one that forms the greatest angle with the plane $\beta$ is the one that is perpendicular to the line of intersection of the planes $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

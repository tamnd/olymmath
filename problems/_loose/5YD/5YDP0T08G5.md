Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$. Let $E$ be the intersection of the diagonals, $\Delta$ a line passing through $E$ intersecting the segments $[AB]$ and $[CD]$ at $U$ and $V$ and $\Gamma$ at $X$ and $Y$. Show that $E$ is the midpoint of the segment $[XY]$ if and only if it is the midpoint of the segment $[UV]$.

The incircle touches the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ of the non-isosceles triangle $ABC$ at points $C_1$, $A_1$, and $B_1$ respectively. Let $m$ be the midline of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ parallel to side $B_1C_1$. The angle bisector of $\angle B_1A_1C_1$ intersects $m$ at point $K$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $BCK$ is tangent to $m$.

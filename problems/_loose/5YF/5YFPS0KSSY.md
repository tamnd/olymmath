Let $k$ be a non-zero natural number and $(G, \cdot)$ a group with $n$ elements. Show that the following statements are equivalent:

i) For any subgroup $H$ of the group $G$ we have $H=\left\{a^{k} \mid a \in H\right\}$.

ii) $(k, n)=1$.

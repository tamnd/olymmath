Let a regular n-gon be denoted as $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$. The point $A_{3}$ is reflected over the axis $A_{2} A_{4}$, resulting in the point $A_{3}^{\prime}$. Then, the point $A_{3}^{\prime}$ is reflected over the axis $A_{1} A_{3}$, resulting in the point $A_{3}^{\prime \prime}$. For which $n \geqq 4$ is the point $A_{3}^{\prime \prime}$ identical to the intersection of the lines $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $A_{3} A_{4}$?

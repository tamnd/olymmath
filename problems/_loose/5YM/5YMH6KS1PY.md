Let $A\in M_4(C)$ be a non-zero matrix. $a)$ If $\text{rank}(A)=r<4$ , prove the existence of two invertible matrices $U,V\in M_4(C)$ , such that:
$$UAV=\begin{pmatrix}I_r&00&0\end{pmatrix}$$
where $I_r$ is the $r$ -unit matrix. $b)$ Show that if $A$ and $A^2$ have the same rank $k$ , then the matrix $A^n$ has rank $k$ , for any $n\ge 3$ .

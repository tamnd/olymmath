Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers, prove that:
$$
\frac{x y z\left(x+y+z+\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}}\right)}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right)(y z+z x+x y)} \leqslant \frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{9} \text {. }
$$

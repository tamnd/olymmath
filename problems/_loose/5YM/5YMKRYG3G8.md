a) Let $S_{a}$ be the circle of radius $R$ centered at the orthocenter of triangle $BCD$; circles $S_{b}, S_{c}$, and $S_{d}$ are defined similarly. Prove that these four circles intersect at one point.

b) Prove that the nine-point circles of triangles $ABC, BCD$, $CDA$, and $DAB$ intersect at one point.

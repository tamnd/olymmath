Given the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$, with focus $F$, and a point $P$ outside the parabola (not on the $x$-axis), two tangents are drawn from $P$ to the parabola, intersecting the parabola at points $A$ and $B$, and the $y$-axis at points $C$ and $D$. Let $M$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle P A B$. Prove:
(1) $P F$ bisects $\angle A F B$;
(2) $F M$ is a tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle F C D$.

Let $\sqrt{5}$ be extracted in $p$-arithmetic. Prove that the last $k$ digits of the members of the $p$-adic Fibonacci series repeat periodically; the length of the period is a divisor of the number $p^{k-1}(p-1)$, so that the members with indices $n$ and $n+p^{k-1}(p-1)$ (members $u_{n}$ and $u_{n+p^{k-1}(p-1)}$) have the same last $k$ digits.

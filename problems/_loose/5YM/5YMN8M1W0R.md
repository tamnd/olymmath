10.1.29 * Let the incircle $\odot I$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ touch $DA$, $AB$, $BC$, and $CD$ at $K$, $L$, $M$, and $N$, respectively. Let $\odot I_{A}$, $\odot I_{B}$, $\odot I_{C}$, and $\odot I_{D}$ be the incircles of $\triangle AKL$, $\triangle BLM$, $\triangle CMN$, and $\triangle DNK$, respectively. Find the external common tangent $I_{AB}$ of $\odot I_{A}$ and $\odot I_{B}$ other than $AB$, and similarly find $I_{BC}$, $I_{CD}$, and $I_{AD}$. These four lines form a convex quadrilateral with area $S$. Prove that $S \leqslant (12 - 8 \sqrt{2}) r^{2}$, where $r$ is the radius of $\odot I$.

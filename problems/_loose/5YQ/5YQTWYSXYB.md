Let triangle $A B C$ have altitudes $\overline{B E}$ and $\overline{C F}$ which meet at $H$. The reflection of $A$ over $B C$ is $A^{\prime}$. The circumcircles of $\triangle A A^{\prime} E$ and $\triangle A A^{\prime} F$ meet the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ at $P \neq A$ and $Q \neq A$ respectively. Lines $B C$ and $P Q$ meet at $R$. Prove that $\overline{E F} \| \overline{H R}$

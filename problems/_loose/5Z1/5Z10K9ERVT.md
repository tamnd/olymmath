A property of the sphere. Suppose it is known that all plane sections of a certain surface are circles (a point is considered a circle of zero radius). Prove that this surface is a sphere.

47a. Packing spheres (I). We have an unlimited supply of identical balls. Let's place three of them so that they all touch each other, and then add a fourth ball so that it touches the first three. We will get four niches, and we can place one ball in each of them. Now we have eight balls. How many niches do they form? How many more balls can be placed in a new layer?

Can this process be continued?

47b. Packing spheres (II). We have an unlimited supply of identical balls. Take one of them and surround it with twelve balls that touch it. How many niches for subsequent balls will there be now? Can a ball be placed in each niche? How many balls will the third layer consist of (the first layer consists of one ball, the second of 12 balls)? Will we always be able to fill all niches in subsequent layers?

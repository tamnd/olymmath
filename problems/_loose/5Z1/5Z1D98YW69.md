Tracy has been baking a rectangular cake whose surface is dissected by grid lines in square fields. The number of rows is $ 2^n$ and the number of columns is $ 2^{n + 1}$ where $ n \geq 1, n \in \mathbb{N}.$ Now she covers the fields with strawberries such that each row has at least $ 2n + 2$ of them. Show that there four pairwise distinct strawberries $ A,B,C$ and $ D$ which satisfy those three conditions:

(a) Strawberries $ A$ and $ B$ lie in the same row and $ A$ further left than $ B.$ Similarly $ D$ lies in the same row as $ C$ but further left.

(b) Strawberries $ B$ and $ C$ lie in the same column.

(c) Strawberries $ A$ lies further up and further left than $ D.$

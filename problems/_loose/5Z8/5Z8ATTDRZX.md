Given that $\alpha, \beta$ are acute angles, then the necessary and sufficient condition for $\alpha+\beta=\frac{\pi}{2}$ is: $\frac{\sin ^{4} \alpha}{\cos ^{2} \beta}+\frac{\cos ^{4} \alpha}{\sin ^{2} \beta}=1$.

Given two equations:

$$
\frac{f_{1}(x)}{f_{2}(x)}=\frac{f_{3}(x)}{f_{4}(x)} \text { and } \frac{f_{1}(x)}{f_{2}(x)}=\frac{f_{1}(x)+f_{3}(x)}{f_{2}(x)+f_{4}(x)}
$$

a) Under what condition is the second equation a consequence of the first?

b) Under what condition is the first equation a consequence of the second?

c) Under what condition are these equations equivalent?

When two two-digit numbers are written one after the other, a four-digit number is formed.

Give two two-digit numbers such that the sum of these two numbers and the four-digit number formed from them is exactly 1478!

(A proof that there is only one possibility for two such numbers is not required. However, you can try to find such a proof.)

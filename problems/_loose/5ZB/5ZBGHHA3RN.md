(52nd Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad (Final B Category) problem) Given positive real numbers $a, b, c, d$. Prove:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{(a+c)^{2}+(b+d)^{2}} & \leqslant \sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}+\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}} \\
& \leqslant \sqrt{(a+c)^{2}+(b+d)^{2}}+\frac{2|a d-b c|}{\sqrt{(a+c)^{2}+(b+d)^{2}}} .
\end{aligned}
$$

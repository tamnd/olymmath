It is New Year's Eve, a few minutes before midnight. On a clock, the following observations can be made:

1. The hour hand and the line connecting the tips of the hands are the legs of a right triangle, whose hypotenuse is the minute hand.
2. The side lengths of this triangle (measured in cm) are integers and pairwise coprime.
3. For the perimeter $U$ and the area $A$ of this triangle, it holds that $A: U = A: U = 1 \mathrm{~cm}$.
4. The shortest side of the triangle is the line connecting the tips of the hands.

How much longer will the old year last?

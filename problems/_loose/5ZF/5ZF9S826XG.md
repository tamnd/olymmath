Let $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be circles with diameters $\overline{A P}$ and $\overline{A Q}$. Let $T$ be the second intersection point of circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$. Let $Q^{\prime}$ be the second intersection point of circle $k_{1}$ and line $A Q$, and $P^{\prime}$ be the second intersection point of circle $k_{2}$ and line $A P$. Circle $k_{3}$ passes through points $T, P$, and $P^{\prime}$, and circle $k_{4}$ passes through points $T, Q$, and $Q^{\prime}$.

Prove that the line on which the common chord of circles $k_{3}$ and $k_{4}$ lies passes through point $A$.

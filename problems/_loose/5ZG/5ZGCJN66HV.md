A square with side $a$ is divided into 6 and 10 equal parts along two adjacent sides, respectively. Then, we connect the first division point, which is $\frac{a}{6}$ away from the common vertex, to the fourth division point of the adjacent side, which is also from the common vertex. Prove that the line segment thus obtained is tangent to the circle inscribed in the square.

For $39 n \geqslant 2$, prove that if $n$ has $k$ distinct odd and even factors, then
(1) $\phi(n) \geqslant n \cdot 2^{-k}$
(2) $2^{k} \mid \phi(n)$
(3) $\phi(2 n)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}\phi(n), & n \text { is odd } \\ 2 \phi(n), & n \text { is even }\end{array}\right.$

A Chinese general has an army of no more than 10000 soldiers. His soldiers can count up to 20 and can thus form rows of up to 20 soldiers (which forms a rectangle except for the last row which may not be full). The general is a good mathematician but he can only see the last row. How can he count the number of his soldiers?

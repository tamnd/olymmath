The sphere $O_{2}$ is tangent to the upper base and the lateral surface of the frustum. The frustum can also contain another sphere $O_{2}$ with radius 3, which is tangent to the sphere $O_{1}$, the lower base, and the lateral surface of the frustum. Apart from the sphere $O_{2}$, the maximum number of additional spheres with radius 3 that can be placed in the frustum is
(A) 1 .
(B) 2 .
(C) 3 .
(D) 4 .

Let $f(x)=x^2+x$ for all real $x$ . There exist positive integers $m$ and $n$ , and distinct nonzero real numbers $y$ and $z$ , such that $f(y)=f(z)=m+\sqrt{n}$ and $f(\frac{1}{y})+f(\frac{1}{z})=\frac{1}{10}$ . Compute $100m+n$ .

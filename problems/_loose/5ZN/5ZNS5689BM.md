Let $a$ be the reciprocal of an integer: $a=\frac{1}{n}$, where $n$ is a positive integer. Prove that every continuous curve connecting points $A$ and $B$, the distance between which is 1, must have a chord parallel to $AB$ of length $a$. If the number $a$ is not the reciprocal of an integer, then there probably exists a continuous curve connecting points $A$ and $B$ that does not have a chord parallel to $AB$ of length $a$.

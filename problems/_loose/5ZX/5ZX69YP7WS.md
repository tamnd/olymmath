István exchanged two car lottery tickets worth $5000 \mathrm{Ft}$ each. On the 20th day of the lottery quarter, he said to Kálmán: "Take over one of my books. If we win, we will share the prize as interest, in proportion to our stakes and the time elapsed." Kálmán gave him 4000 Ft the next day. - A month later, István borrowed 2500 Ft from László under the same conditions. - At the same time, Kálmán said to Miklós: "Give me 2000 Ft. I have 4000 Ft in a joint car lottery ticket. If it wins, we will split my share as if you had joined me at the same time." Miklós accepted the offer.

In the quarterly draw, both tickets won. One car was appraised at $60000 \mathrm{Ft}$, the other at $48000 \mathrm{Ft}$. During the distribution, István wanted to keep the first car. The others acknowledged that they would share the proceeds from the second car. It was only then that Miklós learned that Kálmán's stake had not been in the bet for the entire quarter, which would make their prize smaller. As compensation, Kálmán agreed to calculate Miklós's share as if his money had been in the bet from the beginning of the quarter.

However, after the second car was sold, only $42000 \mathrm{Ft}$ remained for distribution. How much did each of the four participants receive from this?

Simplify the following sum:

$$
\begin{gathered}
S=1 \cdot 2 \cdot \ldots \cdot k+2 \cdot 3 \cdot \ldots(k+1)+3 \cdot 4 \cdot \ldots \cdot(k+2)+\ldots \\
+n \cdot(n+1) \cdot \ldots \cdot(n+k-1)
\end{gathered}
$$

(The sum consists of $n$ terms, each term being the product of $k$ factors.)

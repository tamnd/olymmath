In the Olympiad, 55 schoolchildren participated. All of them submitted their work. During the checking of each problem, one of three grades was given: "+" - the problem was solved, "-" - the problem was attempted, "0" - the problem was not attempted. After checking all the works, it turned out that in no two works did the number of "+" grades and the number of "-" grades coincide simultaneously. What is the smallest number of problems that could have been offered at the Olympiad?

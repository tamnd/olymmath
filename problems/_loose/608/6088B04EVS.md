In triangle $ABC$, which is not equilateral, let $K$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ with side $BC$ and $L$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ with side $AC$. Further, let $S$ be the center of the inscribed circle, $O$ be the center of the circumscribed circle, and $V$ be the intersection of the altitudes of triangle $ABC$. Prove that the following two statements are equivalent:

a) The line $KL$ is tangent to the circumcircles of triangles $ALS$, $BVS$, and $BKS$.

b) Points $A$, $B$, $K$, $L$, and $O$ lie on the same circle.

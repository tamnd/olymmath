A merry-go-round has a total of $n$ seats, and boy $A$ rides for $n$ rounds. After each round, he gets up from his seat and moves to another seat in a clockwise direction, but he never completes a full circle in any single move. The number of seats (including the one he was originally sitting on) that boy $A$ passes through during a move is called the length of that move. Assuming the lengths of his $n-1$ moves are all different, find all possible values of $n$ that allow $A$ to sit in all $n$ seats over the $n$ rounds.

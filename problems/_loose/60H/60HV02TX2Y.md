Let $a, b, c, d$ be positive real numbers such that

$$
a b + c d = (a + b)(c + d)
$$

Prove that among the four triples $(a, b, c), (a, b, d), (a, c, d), (b, c, d)$, there are at least two that cannot be the lengths of the sides of a triangle.

Let $f(x)=x^4-4x^2+2.$ Find the smallest natural $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that there exists $k,c \in \mathbb{N}$ with $$ \left|f^k\left(\frac{n^2+1}{n}\right)-c^{144}\right| < \frac{1}{100}. $$

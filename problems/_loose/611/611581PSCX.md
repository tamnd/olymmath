The ninety-seventh question: Given that $a$ is any positive integer greater than 1, prove: for any positive integer $n$, there always exists an $n$-degree polynomial with integer coefficients $p(x)$, such that $p(0)、p(1)、\ldots、p(n)$ are all distinct and are positive integers of the form $2a^k + 3$, where $k \in \mathbb{N}$.

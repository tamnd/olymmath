Given that a squirrel jumps in the first quadrant $(x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0)$ of the coordinate plane in the following manner: it can jump from point $(x, y)$ to point $(x-5, y+7)$ or to point $(x+1, y-1)$, but it cannot jump out of the first quadrant. Ask from which initial points $(x, y)$ the squirrel cannot jump to points that are more than 1000 units away from the origin? Draw the set of all such points and find its area.

A shuffle of a deck of $n$ cards is defined as the following operation: the deck is divided into some (arbitrary) number of parts, which are then laid out in reverse order without changing the order of the cards within each part. Prove that a deck of 1000 cards can be moved from any position to any other position in no more than 56 shuffles.

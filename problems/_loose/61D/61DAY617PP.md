On the plane, four points $A, B, C$, and $D$ are given. Prove that:

a) if points $B$ and $C$ belong to the segment $A D$ and are symmetric with respect to the midpoint $S$ of this segment, then the sum of the distances from any point $M$ on the plane to points $A$ and $D$ is not less than the sum of the distances from the same point to points $B$ and $C$;

b) conversely, if the sum of the distances from any point $M$ on the plane to points $A$ and $D$ is not less than the sum of the distances from the same point to points $B$ and $C$, then points $B$ and $C$ belong to the segment $A D$ and are symmetric with respect to the midpoint $S$ of this segment.

At a chess tournament, 5 players participated, each playing one game with every other player, as is customary. For a win, 1 point was awarded, for a draw $\frac{1}{2}$ points, and for a loss 0 points. At the end of the tournament:

- no players had the same number of points;
- the winner did not draw any games;
- the second-place player did not lose any games;
- the player who finished in fourth place did not win any games.

Determine the results of all the games.

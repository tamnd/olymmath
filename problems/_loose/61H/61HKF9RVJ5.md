Three Forecasts (9th grade. 4 points). In Anchuria, days can be either clear or rainy, with the probability of a clear day being 0.74 regardless of the time of year. In the Committee on Climate and Forecasts of the Anchurian Parliament, there are two senators and one invited meteorologist. It is known that the proportion of correct forecasts of clear days for each of the three is the same as the proportion of correct forecasts of rainy days. However, the meteorologist's proportion is 50% higher than that of each senator.

One day, all three independently made a forecast for the approaching Day of Great Popular Celebration. Both senators stated that the day would be clear, while the meteorologist predicted rain. Which forecast should be trusted more?

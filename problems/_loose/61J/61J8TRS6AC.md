13.6.14 ** Given a point $ O $ and a line $ e $ in the plane. Find the locus of points $ P $ such that the sum of the distance $ r $ from $ P $ to the fixed point $ O $ and the distance $ \rho $ from $ P $ to the fixed line $ e $ is equal to a given number $ a $. Both $ r $ and $ \rho $ are positive numbers.

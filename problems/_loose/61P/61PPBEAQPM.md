Alice and Bob play the following game: 2012 points are arranged regularly around a circle. Alice starts. The two players take turns, and each has the right to remove one of the points, or to remove 2 consecutive points (more precisely, two points that were consecutive in the initial position with 2012 points). The winner is the one who erases the last point. Who wins? Same question if we modify the definition of consecutive points to say that they did not necessarily have to be consecutive in the initial position.

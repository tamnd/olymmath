Let $A$ be an integer and $A>1$. Let $a_{1}=A^{A}$, $a_{n+1}=A^{a_{n}}$ and $b_{1}=A^{A+1}$, $b_{n+1}=2^{b_{n}}$, $n=1, 2, 3, ...$. Prove that $a_{n}<b_{n}$ for each $n$.

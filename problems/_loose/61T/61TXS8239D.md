Let $A$ denote the set of all real numbers $x$ satisfying $$ \{x\} =\frac{x+\lfloor x \rfloor +\lfloor x + (1/2) \rfloor }{20} $$ If $S$ is the sume of all numbers in $A$ , find $\lfloor S \rfloor$

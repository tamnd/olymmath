Find all functions $f:\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ such that the numbers
$$n, f(n),f(f(n)),\dots,f^{m-1}(n)$$
are distinct modulo $m$ for all integers $n,m$ with $m>1$ .
(Here $f^k$ is defined by $f^0(n)=n$ and $f^{k+1}(n)=f(f^{k}(n))$ for $k \ge 0$ .)

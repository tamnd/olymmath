A group of people is lined up in *almost-order* if, whenever person $A$ is to the left of person $B$ in the line, $A$ is not more than $8$ centimeters taller than $B$. For example, five people with heights $160, 165, 170, 175$, and $180$ centimeters could line up in almost-order with heights (from left-to-right) of $160, 170, 165, 180, 175$ centimeters.
(a) How many different ways are there to line up $10$ people in *almost-order* if their heights are $140, 145, 150, 155,$ $160,$ $165,$ $170,$ $175,$ $180$, and $185$ centimeters?

Suppose in the cyclic hexagon $A B C D E F$, $A B=B C, C D=D E$, $E F=F A$.
Prove: (1) The three diagonals $A D, B E, C F$ intersect at one point;
(2) The perimeter of the hexagon $A B C D E F$ $A B+B C+C D+D E+E F+$ $F A \geqslant A D+B E+C F$.
(1991 Mathematics Science Class Exam)

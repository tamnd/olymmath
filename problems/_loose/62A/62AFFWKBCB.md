Let $A, B, C, D$ and $E$ be five points in this order on a circle, such that $A B=B C$ and $C D=D E$. We call $P, Q$ and $T$ the intersections of the lines $(A D)$ and $(B E)$, $(A C)$ and $(B D)$, $(B D)$ and $(C E)$, respectively.

Show that the triangle $P Q T$ is isosceles.

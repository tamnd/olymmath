In triangle $A B C$, side $B C$ is equal to half the sum of the other two sides. Through point $A$ and the midpoints $B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ of sides $A B$ and $A C$, a circle $\Omega$ is drawn, and tangents are drawn from the centroid of the triangle to this circle. Prove that one of the points of tangency is the center $I$ of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$.

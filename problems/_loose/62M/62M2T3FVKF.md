At the enterprise, 50,000 people are employed. For each of them, the sum of the number of their immediate supervisors and their immediate subordinates is 7. On Monday, each employee of the enterprise issues an order and gives a copy of this order to each of their immediate subordinates (if any). Then, every day, the employee takes all the orders received by them the previous day and either distributes copies of these orders to all their immediate subordinates, or, if they have no subordinates, executes the orders themselves. It turned out that on Friday, no documents are being passed around the institution. Prove that there are at least 97 top-level supervisors in the enterprise who have no supervisors above them.

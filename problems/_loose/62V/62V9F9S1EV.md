Let $m, n$ be positive integers, and $m>n$. For $k=1,2, \cdots, k+1$, set $x_{k}=\frac{m+k}{n+k}$. Prove: If $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n+1}$ are all integers, then $x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{n+1}-1$ has an odd prime factor.

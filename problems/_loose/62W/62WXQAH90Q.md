Question 4 In $\square A B C D$, $\angle A<90^{\circ}$, point $T$ is on side $B C$ such that $\triangle A T D$ is an acute triangle. Let the circumcenters of $\triangle A B T$, $\triangle A D T$, and $\triangle C D T$ be $O_{1}$, $O_{2}$, and $O_{3}$, respectively. Prove that the orthocenter of $\triangle O_{1} O_{2} O_{3}$ lies on the line $A D$. ${ }^{[4]}$
(37th Russian Mathematical Olympiad)

There is a (finite) sequence of zeros and ones such that all quintets of consecutive digits that can be extracted from it are distinct (and these quintets can, of course, overlap like the quintets 01011 and 01101, arranged as follows: ...0101101...). Prove that if the sequence cannot be continued while preserving the stated property, then the first four digits of the sequence coincide with its last four digits.

Aunt Amelia visited Paris as a guest of her three little nephews and walked a lot with them around the city. After this, each of them told the following.

First nephew. We went up the Eiffel Tower, but we did not visit Montparnasse, however, we visited the Arc de Triomphe.

Second nephew. We went up the Eiffel Tower and visited Montparnasse. But we did not visit either the Arc de Triomphe or the ball game hall.

Third nephew. We did not go up the Eiffel Tower, but we visited the Arc de Triomphe.

If each child lied once (and only once), then which sights of Paris did they actually visit with Aunt Amelia?

In each cell of a $24 \times 24$ table, a number is written, either +1 or -1. In one move, you can change the sign of one of these numbers, as well as simultaneously change the signs of all numbers that are in the same row or the same column with it. Prove that using several such moves, you can transform any initial arrangement of numbers into any other.

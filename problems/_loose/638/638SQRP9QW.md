Number $2016$ is written on the board. Olesya and Andriy are playing game: they take turns (Olesya is the first to start) to reduce the number on the board to an integer, not exceeding the number of the move (on the first move Olesya must reduce the number to $1$, Andriy, in his turn, to $1$ or $2$, then Olesya to $1$, $2$ or $3$ etc.). The winner is the first one to be able to write number $0$ on the board. Who wins with the right game of both opponents?

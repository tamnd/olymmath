\section*{Problem 6A - 171236A}

Let $n$ be a natural number with $n>1$.

a) Determine all functions $f$ defined on the set $\mathbb{R}$ of real numbers that are continuous on $\mathbb{R}$ and have the property that for every real number $x$, the equation $f\left(x^{n}\right)=f(x)$ holds.

b) Give an example of a function $f$ defined on $\mathbb{R}$ that is discontinuous and has the property (1).

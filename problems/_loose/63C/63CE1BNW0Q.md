Prove that if the binomial $1+x$ is raised to the $n$-th power and written in ascending powers of $x$, the result is the equality

$$
(1+x)^{n}=C_{n}^{0}+C_{n}^{1} x+C_{n}^{2} x^{2}+\ldots+C_{n}^{n} x^{n}
$$

or in $m$-arithmetic:

$$
(1+x)^{n}=P_{n}^{0}+P_{n}^{1} x+P_{n}^{2} x^{2}+\cdots+P_{n}^{n} x^{n}
$$

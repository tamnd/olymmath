The Egyptian Cheops pyramid is a regular quadrilateral pyramid $A B C D S$, with a base edge of $a=231 \mathrm{~m}$ and a height of $m=140 \mathrm{~m}$. A tourist travels in such a way that starting from $A$, they proceed in a direction perpendicular to the side edge $S B$. Upon reaching the side edge $S B$ at point $B_{1}$, they continue their journey perpendicularly to the side edge $D C$ to point $C_{1}$, and so on. At what height will the tourist be when they reach the side edge $S A$ at point $A_{1}$ again? What is the angle of elevation of the tourist's path? What is the distance traveled by the tourist to point $A_{1}$?

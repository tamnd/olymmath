Given a triangle $A B C$, let $D$ be the point of the ray $B A$ such that $B D=B A+A C$. If $K$ and $M$ are points on the sides $B A$ and $B C$, respectively, such that the triangles $B D M$ and $B C K$ have the same areas, prove that $\angle B K M=\frac{1}{2} \angle B A C$.

Let $D_0, D_1, \dots, D_{2018}$ be points on the segment $\overline{AB}$ such that $D_0 = A$, $D_{2018} = B$ and
$$
|D_0 D_1| = |D_1 D_2| = \dots = |D_{2017} D_{2018}|.
$$
If $C$ is a point such that $\angle BCA = 90^\circ$, prove that
$$
|CD_0|^2 + |CD_1|^2 + \dots + |CD_{2018}|^2 = |AD_1|^2 + |AD_2|^2 + \dots + |AD_{2018}|^2.
$$
(Ivan Krijan)

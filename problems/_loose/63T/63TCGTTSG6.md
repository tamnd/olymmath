Let's call a non-empty (finite or infinite) set $A$, consisting of natural numbers, complete if for any natural numbers $a$ and $b$ (not necessarily distinct and not necessarily in $A$) such that $a+b$ is in $A$, the number $ab$ is also in $A$. Find all complete sets of natural numbers.

(N. Agakhanov)

In the City of Flowers, a flu epidemic is spreading among the dwarves. One day, several dwarves caught a cold and fell ill. No one else caught a cold after that, but the healthy dwarves still visited their sick friends and relatives, thus getting infected. It is known that each dwarf is sick for exactly one day, and after recovery, they have immunity for at least one day (i.e., they cannot get sick again on that day). Each healthy dwarf visits their sick friends every day regardless of the epidemic. After the epidemic starts, the dwarves do not get vaccinated anymore.
(1) If some dwarves were vaccinated the day before the flu outbreak and thus had immunity on the first day of the outbreak, how long can the flu last?
(2) If no dwarf has immunity on the first day of the outbreak, will the flu eventually end?

Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle with $|AB| = |AC|$. If $D$ is a point on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ on the same side of $BC$ as $A$, prove
$$
|AB| + |AC| \ge |DB| + |DC|
$$
with equality if and only if $D = A$. Hence prove, of all triangles described in a circle, the equilateral triangle has the greatest perimeter.

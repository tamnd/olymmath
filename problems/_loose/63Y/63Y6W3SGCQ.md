Prove that in every triangle,

$$
\frac{1}{(s-a)(s-b)}+\frac{1}{(s-b)(s-c)}+\frac{1}{(s-c)(s-a)}=\frac{1}{r^{2}}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of the triangle, $s$ is its semiperimeter, and $r$ is the radius of the inscribed circle.

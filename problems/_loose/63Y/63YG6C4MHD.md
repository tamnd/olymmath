In the general triangle $ABC$, we take points $M$ and $N$ on the sides $BC$ and $AC$, or on their extensions. The line parallel to $BC$ through $N$ intersects $AB$ at $D$, and the line parallel to $AM$ through $N$ intersects $BC$ at $E$.

The intersection of the lines $AM$ and $DE$ is $L$. Let $\frac{BM}{MC}=m$ and $\frac{AN}{NC}=n$.

Prove that

$$
\frac{AL}{LM}=m+n+mn
$$

($m$ and $n$ are positive or negative according to whether the segments in the numerator and denominator of the ratio are in the same or opposite directions.)

An acute-angled triangle $ABC$ is inscribed into circle $\omega$. The tangents to $\omega$ passing through $B$ and $C$ intersect the tangent to $\omega$ passing through $A$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. The line $k$ passing through $K$ is parallel to $AB$; the line $\ell$ passing through $L$ is parallel to $AC$. Let $P$ be the meeting point of $k$ and $\ell$. Prove that $BP = CP$. (P. Kozhevnikov)

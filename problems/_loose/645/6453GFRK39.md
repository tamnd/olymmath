b) ** Determine the number of different arrangements of the maximum possible number of knights, none of which threaten each other.

Other combinatorial problems related to the placement of chess pieces can be found in a special book by L. Ya. Okunev "Combinatorial Problems on a Chessboard", M.-L., ONTI, 1935.

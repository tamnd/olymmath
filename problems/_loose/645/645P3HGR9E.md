On a plane, a network is drawn, where every cell of the net is a regular hexagon with unit side length. A beetle crawled along the lines of the network from a point $A$ to a point $B$ on a 20 unit long path. Show that if there is no shorter than 20 unit path along the lines of the network between $A$ and $B$, then the beetle moved in the same direction at some point during the halfway of its journey.

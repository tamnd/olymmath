In an acute triangle $ABC$ , $AB>AC$ , let $D, E, F$ be the projections of $A, B, C$ on the opposite sides, respectively. The perpendicular bisector of $BF$ intersects $DE$ at $P$ , and the perpendicular bisector of $CE$ intersects $DF$ at $Q$ . Let $K$ be a point on the line $PQ$ such that $\angle PKE=\angle PKF$ , and $DK$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle KEF$ at $T \neq K$ . Show that $\angle ATE=\angle ATF$ .

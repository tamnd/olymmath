On the plane, $m$ vectors $\overrightarrow{u_{1}}, \overrightarrow{u_{2}}, \cdots, \overrightarrow{u_{m}}$ satisfy
$$
\left|\vec{u}_{i}\right| \leqslant 1, i=1,2, \cdots, m, \vec{u}_{1}+\vec{u}_{2}+\cdots+\vec{u}_{m}=\overrightarrow{0} \text {. }
$$

Prove: The $m$ vectors can be rearranged as $\vec{v}_{1}, \vec{v}_{2}, \cdots, \vec{v}_{m}$, such that
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left|\vec{v}_{1}\right| \leqslant \sqrt{5},\left|\vec{v}_{1}+\vec{v}_{2}\right| \leqslant \sqrt{5},\left|\vec{v}_{1}+\vec{v}_{2}+\vec{v}_{3}\right| \leqslant \sqrt{5}, \cdots, \\
\left|\vec{v}_{1}+\vec{v}_{2}+\cdots+\vec{v}_{m}\right| \leqslant \sqrt{5} .
\end{array}
$$

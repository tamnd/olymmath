Given for any $n \in N, a_{n}>0$ and
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{3}=\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}\right)^{2} \text {. }
$$

Prove: $a_{n}=n, n=1,2,3, \cdots$

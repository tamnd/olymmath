(The Chinese Remainder Theorem) Let $n_{1}, n_{2}, \ldots, n_{k}$ be pairwise coprime numbers. Then for all integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{k}$ there exists a unique $m$ such that for $N=n_{1} \cdot n_{2} \ldots . . n_{k}$ we have:

$$
\begin{gathered}
m=a_{1} (\bmod N) \\
m=a_{2} (\bmod N) \\
\ldots \\
m=a_{k} (\bmod N)
\end{gathered}
$$

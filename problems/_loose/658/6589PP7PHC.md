A pile of 25 stones is arbitrarily divided into two piles, any of the existing piles is again divided into two, and so on, until each pile consists of one stone. Each time a pile is divided into two, the product of the number of stones in the resulting two piles is recorded. Prove that the sum of all recorded numbers is 300.

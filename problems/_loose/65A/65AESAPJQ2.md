Three grasshoppers are located at three vertices of a square, playing leapfrog. If grasshopper $A$ jumps over grasshopper $B$, it ends up at the same distance from $B$ but, naturally, on the other side and on the same line. Can one of the grasshoppers end up at the fourth vertex of the square after several jumps?

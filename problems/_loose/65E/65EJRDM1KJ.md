Let $G$ be a graph with $n$ vertices, of degrees $d_{1}, \cdots, d_{n}$ respectively. Show that there exists an independent set containing at least:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{d_{i}+1}
$$

vertices.

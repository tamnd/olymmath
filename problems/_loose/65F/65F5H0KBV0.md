Let $X Y Z$ be an acute-angled triangle. Let $s$ be the side-length of the square which has two adjacent vertices on side $Y Z$, one vertex on side $X Y$ and one vertex on side $X Z$. Let $h$ be the distance from $X$ to the side $Y Z$ and let $b$ be the distance from $Y$ to $Z$.
(a) If the vertices have coordinates $X=(2,4), Y=(0,0)$ and $Z=(4,0)$, find $b, h$ and $s$.
(b) Given the height $h=3$ and $s=2$, find the base $b$.
(c) If the area of the square is 2017 , determine the minimum area of triangle $X Y Z$.

Let $\left(d_{1}\right),\left(d_{2}\right)$ be two parallel lines. Furthermore, construct two circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ such that $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ are tangent to each other, and such that for $i \in\{1,2\}$, $\omega_{i}$ is tangent to $\left(d_{i}\right)$. Show that the three points of tangency are collinear.

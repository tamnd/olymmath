In the plane, circles $m, n$ intersect at points $K, L$. The tangent at point $K$ to circle $m$ intersects circle $n$ at point $A \neq K$, and the tangent at point $L$ to circle $n$ intersects circle $m$ at point $C \neq L$. Point $B \neq L$ is the intersection of line $A L$ with circle $m$, and point $D \neq K$ is the intersection of line $C K$ with circle $n$. Prove that quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

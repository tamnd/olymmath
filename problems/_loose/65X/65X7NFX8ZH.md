Let $n$ be a non-zero natural number and let $G$ be a finite group of order $n$. A function $f: G \rightarrow G$ has property $(\mathrm{P})$, if $f(x y z)=f(x) f(y) f(z)$, for any $x, y, z \operatorname{in} G$.

(a) If $n$ is odd, show that any function that has property (P) is an endomorphism of $G$;

(b) If $n$ is even, does the conclusion from part (a) remain true?

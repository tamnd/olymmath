Let $p$ be a prime number of the form $4k+3$. Prove that if for positive integers $x, y$, $x^{2}-x y+\frac{p+1}{4} y^{2}$ is divisible by $p$, then there exist integers $u, v$ such that

$$
x^{2}-x y+\frac{p+1}{4} y^{2}=p\left(u^{2}-u v+\frac{p+1}{4} v^{2}\right)
$$

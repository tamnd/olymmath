Given a quadrilateral $A B C D ; O$ - the intersection point of diagonals $A C$ and $B D, M$ - a point on $A C$ such that $|A M|=|O C|, N$ - a point on $B D$ such that $|B N|=|O D|, K$ and $L-$ midpoints of $A C$ and $B D$. Prove that the lines $M L, N K$, and the line connecting the points of intersection of the medians of triangles $A B C$ and $A C D$, intersect at one point.

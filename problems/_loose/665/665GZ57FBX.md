Let the hyperbola $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>0, b>0)$ have its right focus at $F$. Draw a line $l$ through point $F$ perpendicular to the $x$-axis, intersecting the two asymptotes at points $A$ and $B$. Let $P$ be one of the intersections of $l$ with the hyperbola. Let $O$ be the origin. If there exist real numbers $m$ and $n$ such that $\overrightarrow{O P}=m \overrightarrow{O A}+ n \overrightarrow{O B}$, and $m n=\frac{2}{9}$, then the eccentricity of the hyperbola is ( ).
(A) $\frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{4}$
(B) $\frac{9}{8}$
(C) $\frac{3 \sqrt{5}}{5}$
(D) $\frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $\Gamma$ its circumcircle, and $H$ its orthocenter. Let $A'$ be the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to the side $(BC)$; $B'$ the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to $(CA)$; $C'$ the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to $(AB)$. Prove that the three points $A', B'$, and $C'$ belong to $\Gamma$.

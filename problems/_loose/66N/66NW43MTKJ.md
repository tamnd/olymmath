The ranking was conducted among the top 100 leaders from the latest RBC 500 (excluding banks and insurers). The ranking also includes 21 Russian subsidiaries of foreign companies without assigning a place.

The top places in terms of average monthly salary for 2020 were taken by the NOVATEK and Sovcomflot groups. According to Rosstat, the average monthly accrued wage in 2020 across the economy was 51,300 rubles. Thus, the average pay of the leaders is about five times higher than the national average. Average salaries at 23 participants in the ranking are below the national average. PAO "Gazprom" took 27th place in this ranking.

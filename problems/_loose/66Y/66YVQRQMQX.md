On a $5 \times 5$ chessboard, 25 checkers were placed - one on each square. Then all the checkers were removed from the board, but it was remembered which square each checker was on. Is it possible to place the checkers on the board again in such a way that each checker stands on a square adjacent to the one it stood on last time (adjacent horizontally or vertically, but not diagonally)?

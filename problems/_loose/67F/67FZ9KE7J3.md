There are two perpendicular lines in space, $A x$ and $B y$; their common normal: $A B=2 a$. Let $P$ be a variable point on $A x$, and $Q$ a variable point on $B y$, such that if $A P=x$ and $B Q=y$, then $x y=k^{2}$, where $k$ is a given length.

$1^{0}$. Show that under the above conditions, the volume of the tetrahedron $P A B Q$ is constant!

$2^{0}$. Calculate the distance of vertex $A$ from the base $P B Q$ as a function of $y$!

$3^{0}$. Determine the center of the sphere circumscribed around the tetrahedron $P A B Q$, and express its diameter as a function of $x-y$! For which values of $x$ and $y$ does this diameter become minimal?

In a tennis tournament (where there are no draws), more than 4 athletes participated. Each game day, each tennis player participated in exactly one game. By the end of the tournament, everyone played against each other exactly once. We will call a player persistent if they won at least one match and never lost after their first win. All other players will be called non-persistent. Is it true that the number of game days when a match between non-persistent players took place is more than half?

For a circle with diameter $AB$, we choose points $C$ and $D$ such that they are equidistant from the center of the circle. Show that if these two points are connected to any point $P$ on the circumference of the circle, then the sum

$$
\overline{C P}^{2}+\overline{D P}^{2}
$$

is constant.

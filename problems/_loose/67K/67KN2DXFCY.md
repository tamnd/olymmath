Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be positive real numbers such that $abcd=1$. Prove that
$1/[(1/2 +a+ab+abc)^{1/2}]+ 1/[(1/2+b+bc+bcd)^{1/2}] + 1/[(1/2+c+cd+cda)^{1/2}] + 1/[1(1/2+d+da+dab)^{1/2}]$ is greater than or equal to $2^{1/2}$.

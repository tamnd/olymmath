13 Two circles on a plane intersect at points $A, B$. Let $PQ$ be one of their common tangents, with $P, Q$ being the points of tangency. $S$ is the intersection of the tangents to the circumcircle of $\triangle APQ$ at points $P, Q$. $H$ is the reflection of $B$ over $PQ$. Prove that $A, S, H$ are collinear.

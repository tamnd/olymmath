For the execution of a field game, it was necessary that each participant had a writing instrument with them. Only the following types of writing instruments were brought by the participants: ballpoint pens, red pencils, and green pencils; none of these three types were duplicated by any participant. Specifically, the following was observed:

(1) There were a total of 100 participants in the field game.

(2) Exactly 20 of the participants had a ballpoint pen but no red pencil.

(3) Exactly 15 of the participants had a ballpoint pen but no green pencil.

(4) Exactly 5 of the participants had a ballpoint pen but neither a red pencil nor a green pencil.

(5) Exactly 65 of the participants had no ballpoint pen.

(6) Exactly 55 of the participants had no red pencil.

(7) Exactly 40 of the participants had no green pencil.

(8) Exactly 15 of the participants had neither a red pencil nor a green pencil.

a) Determine from these data the number of participants who brought at least one writing instrument!

b) Were the writing instruments brought sufficient to provide each of the 100 participants with a writing instrument, given appropriate distribution?

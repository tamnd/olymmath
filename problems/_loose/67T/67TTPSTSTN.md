In a classroom, there are ten students. Aline writes ten consecutive integers on the board. Each student chooses one of the ten integers written on the board, such that any two students always choose two different integers. Each student then calculates the sum of the nine integers chosen by the other nine students. Each student whose result is a perfect square then receives a gift.

What is the maximum number of students who will receive a gift?

A perfect square is an integer of the form $n^{2}$, where $n$ is a natural number.

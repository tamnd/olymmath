Let $m$ be a natural number. The sequence $a_{n}$ is defined by the conditions $a_{1}=m$ and $a_{n+1}=\left[\frac{a_{n}^{2}+10 a_{n}}{2 a_{n}+4}\right]$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Prove that the sequence $a_{n}$ is constant from some term onwards.

Note. For any real number $x$, the notation $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not greater than $x$.

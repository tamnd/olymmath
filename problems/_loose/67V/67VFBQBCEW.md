Let $a, b$, and $c$ be the sides of triangle $ABC$, $a+b+c=2p$; $G$ - the point of intersection of its medians, $O, I$, and $I_{a}$ - the centers of the circumscribed, inscribed, and exscribed circles (the exscribed circle touches side $BC$ and the extensions of sides $AB$ and $AC$), $R, r, r_{a}$ - their radii. Prove the validity of the following relations:
a) $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=2 p^{2}-2 r^{2}-8 R r$

b) $|O G|^{2}=R^{2}-\frac{1}{9}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$

c) $|I G|^{2}=\frac{1}{9}\left(p^{2}+5 r^{2}-16 R r\right)$
d) $|O I|^{2}=R^{2}-2 R r$ (Euler);

e) $\left|O I_{a}\right|^{2}=R^{2}+2 R r_{a}$;
f) $\left|I I_{a}\right|^{2}=4 R\left(r_{a}-r\right)$.

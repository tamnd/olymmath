There are $n(\geqslant 2)$ piles of coins, and the only allowed operation is as follows: each time, choose two piles, move some coins from one pile to another, so that the number of coins in the other pile doubles.
(1) When $n \geqslant 3$, prove that it is possible to merge the coins into two piles or one pile after a finite number of moves.
(2) When $n=2$, use $r, s$ to represent the number of coins in the two piles, find the necessary and sufficient conditions for $r, s$ such that the two piles of coins can be merged into one pile after a finite number of steps.

In $\triangle A B C$, $A B=A C, O$ is the circumcenter. Draw $D E / / B C$, intersecting $A B$ and $A C$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively. $P$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle A D E$, and the circumcircle of $\triangle A P D$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle A O C$ at the second point $F$. Let $A F$ intersect $B E$ at point $M$, and points $K$ and $T$ lie on segments $A D$ and $C A$ respectively, such that $\frac{A K}{K D}=\frac{C T}{T A}$. Prove:
(1) $B M=M E$;
(2) $A, K, F, T$ are concyclic.

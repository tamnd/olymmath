Prove that an astroid is the trajectory of a marked point on a circle of radius $1 / 4$, which rolls inside a fixed circle of radius 1, with the smaller circle located inside the larger one.

The trajectory of a marked point on a circle of radius $r$, which rolls inside a fixed circle of radius $R$, with the circle of radius $r$ located inside the circle of radius $R$, is called a hypocycloid.

The trajectory of a marked point on a circle of radius $r$, which rolls outside a fixed circle of radius $R$, with the circle of radius $r$ located outside the circle of radius $R$, is called an epicycloid.

If the ratio $r / R$ is rational, then the corresponding hypocycloids and epicycloids are closed curves with a finite number of singular points (points of return). Some of them have special names. For example, an epicycloid with one point of return is called a cardioid (it resembles a heart in shape), and an epicycloid with two points of return is called a nephroid (it resembles a kidney in shape). A hypocycloid with four points of return is an astroid.

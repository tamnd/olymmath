There are five honest security guards $A, B, C, D, E$ who each worked three days on December 1, 2016. Every day, 3 security guards were on duty, and each guard's schedule repeated every 5 days. Today (January 1, 2017, Sunday), regarding their duty schedule last month, the five had the following conversation:
$A$: $B$ and I worked more weekend (Saturday, Sunday) shifts than the other three.
$B$: I worked with each of the other four at least once this month.
$C$: On December 3, I was supposed to be off, but I came to help with the Math Garden Exploration Preliminary Contest, which unfortunately doesn't count as a duty shift.
$D$: $E$ is always scheduled with me.
$E$: On Christmas (December 25), both $A$ and I were on duty.
So, the five-digit number formed by the dates of the 2nd, 6th, and 10th times $A$ was on duty in December is $ $.
(If the 2nd, 6th, and 10th times on duty were on December 3 and December 17, respectively, the answer would be 31217)

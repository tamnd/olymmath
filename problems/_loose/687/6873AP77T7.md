(31st IMO Preliminary Question) Let $a, b, c, d \in \mathbf{R}_{+}, a b+b c+c d+d a=1$, prove that:
$$
\frac{a^{3}}{b+c+d}+\frac{b^{3}}{c+d+a}+\frac{c^{3}}{d+a+b}+\frac{d^{3}}{a+b+c} \geqslant \frac{1}{3} .
$$

A king is in one of the corners of an $m \times n$ board. Two players take turns moving the king to any unvisited square. The first player who cannot move the king anymore loses. Determine, in terms of the board dimensions, who has the winning strategy.

Note: In chess, the king moves one square in any direction, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

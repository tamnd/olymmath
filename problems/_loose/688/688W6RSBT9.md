Transformation $f$ has the following property: if $A'$ and $B'$ are the images of points $A$ and $B$, then $\overrightarrow{A' B'} = k \overrightarrow{A B}$, where $k$ is a constant number. Prove that:

a) if $k=1$, then transformation $f$ is a parallel translation;

b) if $k \neq 1$, then transformation $f$ is a homothety.

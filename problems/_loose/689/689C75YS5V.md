A natural number $x$ in a base $r$ system ( $r \leqslant 36$ ) has the form $\overline{p p q q}$, and $2 q=5 p$. It turns out that the $r$-ary representation of the number $x^{2}$ is a seven-digit palindrome with a zero middle digit. (A palindrome is a number that reads the same from left to right and from right to left). Find the sum of the $r$-ary digits of the number $x^{2}$.

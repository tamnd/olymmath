Let $i_{1}, i_{2}, \ldots, i_{n}$ be the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$, but arranged in some, generally new, order. Prove that when $n$ is even, the product $\left(1-i_{1}\right)\left(2-i_{2}\right)\left(3-i_{3}\right) \ldots\left(n-i_{n}\right)$ can be either even or odd; however, when $n$ is odd, it is necessarily even.

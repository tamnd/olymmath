In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, if the angle bisectors $AE, CF$ of a pair of opposite angles $\angle A, \angle C$ intersect at $F, E$ on $BC$, then the angle formed by their intersection is equal to the half-difference of the other pair of opposite angles (interior angles).

Let $n$ be a natural number. There are $6n + 4$ mathematicians at the conference. $2n+1$ meetings are held. On every meeting mathematicians are sitting at one table with $4$ chairs and $n$ tables with $6$ chairs.
Distances between each two adjacent chairs are equal. Two mathematicians sit in $special$ position if they sit at the same table and they are adjacent or diametrically opposite. For which natural numbers $n$ is possible that after end of all meetings every 2 mathematicians are sitting at the $special$ position less than 2 times.

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be an infinite sequence of positive integers such that $a_{k}<a_{k+1}$ for $1 \leq k$. - Prove that this sequence has infinitely many elements that can be written as

$$
a_{m}=x \cdot a_{p}+y \cdot a_{q}
$$

where $x$ and $y$ are suitable positive integers, and $p \neq q$.

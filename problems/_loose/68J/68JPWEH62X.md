a) Prove that for any triangle $ABC$

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \geqslant 4 \sqrt{3} S
$$

where equality holds only for an equilateral triangle $ABC$.

b)* Prove that, moreover,

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-(a-b)^{2}-(b-c)^{2}-(c-a)^{2} \geqslant 4 \sqrt{3} S
$$

where equality also holds only when $a=b=c$.

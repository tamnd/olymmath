a) $\frac{1}{x_{1}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}$
b) $\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}$
c) $x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}$
d) $\frac{1}{\left(x_{1}+p\right)^{2}}+\frac{1}{\left(x_{2}+p\right)^{2}}$

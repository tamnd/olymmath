In a certain country, there are three cities $A, B$, and $C$. Between each pair of cities, there are several roads (at least one), and all roads are bidirectional. In addition to direct road connections between two cities, there are also indirect connections. An indirect road connection between cities $X$ and $Y$ consists of a road connecting city $X$ with a third city $Z$ and a road connecting cities $Z$ and $Y$.

It is known that there are a total of 43 road connections between cities $A$ and $B$, and a total of 29 road connections between cities $B$ and $C$. How many road connections can there be in total between cities $A$ and $C$?

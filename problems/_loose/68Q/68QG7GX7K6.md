We will denote $\bigcirc P Q R$ as the circumcircle of triangle $P Q R$.

Let three circles intersecting at $O$ and intersecting each other at $A, B$, and $C$. Choose a point $X$ on $\bigcirc A B O$, the line $(X B)$ intersects $\bigcirc B C O$ at $Y$. The line $(Y C)$ intersects $\bigcirc C A O$ at $Z$. Show that $Z, A$, and $X$ are collinear.

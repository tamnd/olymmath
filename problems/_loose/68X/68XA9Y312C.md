$ \textbf{(A)}\ \text{If the picnic is held, Sunday's weather is undoubtedly fair.}$

$ \textbf{(D)}\ \text{If it is fair Sunday, the picnic may be held.}$
$ \textbf{(E)}\ \text{If it is fair Sunday, the picnic must be held.}$

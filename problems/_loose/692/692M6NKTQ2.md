Given a cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $E$ be the intersection of lines $B C$ and $A D$. If the intersection of diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ lies on the angle bisector of $\angle A E B$, then triangle $A B E$ is isosceles. Prove this.

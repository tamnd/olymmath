For the convex pentagon $A B C D E$, we know that $E A=A B, B C=C D$, and $\angle E A B=60^{\circ}, \angle B C D=120^{\circ}$. Show that the plane can be completely and uniformly covered with congruent copies of this pentagon! Does the statement remain valid if the pentagon has a concave angle at one of the vertices $B, D, E$?

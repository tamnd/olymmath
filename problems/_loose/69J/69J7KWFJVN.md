Let $f: \mathbf{R}^{+} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}^{+}$ be a continuous function, $f(1)=1$, for $\forall x, \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, i=1,2, \cdots, n, n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$, solve the functional inequality: $f\left(\prod_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}\right) \leqslant \prod_{i=1}^{n} f\left(x_{i}\right)$.

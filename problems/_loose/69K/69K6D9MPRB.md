Inside the circle $O$, there is a point $M$, through which a chord $A B$ is drawn. Prove that the product

$$
\operatorname{tg} \frac{\angle A O M}{2} \cdot \operatorname{tg} \frac{\angle B O M}{2}
$$

does not depend on the direction of the chord. Express this product in terms of the radius of the circle and the distance from point $M$ to the center. Verify the validity of the theorem for a point $M$ lying outside the circle.

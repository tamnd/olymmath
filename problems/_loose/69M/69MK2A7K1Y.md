Sam and Jonathan play a game where they take turns flipping a weighted coin, and the game ends when one of them wins. The coin has a $\frac89$ chance of landing heads and a $\frac19$ chance of landing tails. Sam wins when he flips heads, and Jonathan wins when he flips tails. Find the probability that Sam wins, given that he takes the first turn.

On an analog clock (a clock with minute and hour hands), the angle between the hands can be measured by indicating the degree number by which the minute hand would have to be rotated clockwise to reach the (here considered immobile) hour hand.

In addition to such a clock, which we assume always runs exactly, we consider a digital clock that also runs exactly, i.e.: We assume that its hour, minute, and second display always adjust to the correct numerical value at the beginning of each second.

a) What numbers does the digital clock show at all those times between 9:30 AM and 12:30 PM when the two hands of the analog clock exactly overlap?

b) How many minutes after 9:30 AM do the two hands of the analog clock first form the same angle with each other as at 9:30 AM?

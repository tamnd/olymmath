When manufacturing a batch of $N \geq 5$ coins, a worker mistakenly made two coins from a different material (all coins look the same). The supervisor knows that there are exactly two such coins, that they weigh the same, but differ in weight from the others. The worker knows which coins are fake and that they are lighter than the others. Can he, by conducting two weighings on a balance scale without weights, convince the supervisor that the fake coins are lighter than the real ones, and which coins exactly are the fake ones?

(Steiner Theorem) In $\triangle ABC$, $BE$ and $CF$ are the angle bisectors of $\angle ABC$ and $\angle ACB$ respectively. Prove: 1) If $BE = CF$, then $AB = AC$; 2) If $BE > CF$, then $AB > AC$; 3) If $BE < CF$, then $AB < AC$.

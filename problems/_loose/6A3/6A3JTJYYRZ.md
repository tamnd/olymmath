Given the number $2^{k}$, where $k$ is a natural number greater than 3. Prove that no matter how the digits of $2^{k}$ are rearranged, it is impossible to obtain a number equal to $2^{n}$, where $n$ is any natural number greater than $k$.
(34th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1971)

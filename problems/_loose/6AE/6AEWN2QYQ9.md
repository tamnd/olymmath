Let's call a company $k$-unbreakable if in any partition of it into $k$ groups, there will be two acquaintances in one of the groups. Given a 3-unbreakable company in which there are no four people who are all mutually acquainted, prove that it can be divided into two companies, one of which is 2-unbreakable, and the other is 1-unbreakable.

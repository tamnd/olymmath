Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral. Each side of this quadrilateral is divided into three equal segments by two points. Through each pair of such points, which are closest to the same vertex of the quadrilateral $A B C D$, a line is drawn.

In this way, exactly four lines can be drawn, whose points of intersection form another quadrilateral STUV.

Which of the two quadrilaterals $A B C D$ or STUV has the larger area?

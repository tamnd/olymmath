Let the decreasing non-negative real sequence $a_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, p)$ satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{p} a_{i}=n(n>0), a_{i} \leqslant M$, where $M$ is a positive constant, and $n=q M+r\left(q \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, 0 \leqslant r<M\right)$, prove that: $\sum_{i=1}^{p} a_{i}^{2} \leqslant p M^{2}+r^{2}$.

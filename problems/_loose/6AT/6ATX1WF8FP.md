If the larger root of the equation $(1992 x)^{2}-1991 \cdot 1993 x-1=0$ is $m$, and the smaller root of the equation $x^{2}+1991 x-1992=0$ is $n$, then $m-n$ equals
(A) 1993.
(B) 1992.
(C) $\frac{1992}{1993}$.
(D) $\frac{1991}{1992}$.
(China Sichuan Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1992)

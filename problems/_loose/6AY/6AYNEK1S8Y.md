Given natural numbers $m$ and $n (m < n)$ and a large chocolate bar, the sides of which are divisible by $n^5$. (All chocolate bars in this problem are grid rectangles, with the side of each cell equal to 1.) Lesha ate several cells five times such that each time a smaller chocolate bar was left, the area of which was $\frac{m}{n}$ of the area of the previous chocolate bar. Prove that he can eat several more cells so that a very small chocolate bar is left, the area of which is $\left(\frac{m}{n}\right)^{10}$ of the area of the original large chocolate bar.

(O. Ivanova)

Shuffling Cards. An elementary method of shuffling cards consists of taking a deck face down in the left hand and transferring the cards one by one to the right hand; each successive card is placed on top of the previous one: the second on top of the first, the fourth on top of the third, and so on until all the cards have been transferred. If you repeat this procedure several times with any even number of cards, you will find that after a certain number of repeated shuffles, the cards will return to their original order. If you have $2,4,8,16,32,64$ cards, it will take $2,3,4,5$, 6,7 shuffles respectively for the cards to return to their original order.
\footnotetext{
* The correct order is: Ace, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six, Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten, Jack, Queen, King.
}

How many times do you need to shuffle a deck of 14 cards?

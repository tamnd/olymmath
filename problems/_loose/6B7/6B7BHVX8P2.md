From a deck of bridge cards, two players removed the spade, heart, and club cards numbered 1 to 6 and play with these 18 cards according to the following rules. The player first draws two cards from the deck held by the banker. If the sum of the numbers on the drawn cards is 7, they win; if it is greater than 7, they lose; if it is less than 7, they draw a third card, and only win if the new number completes the sum of the first two to 7. What is the probability that the player wins?

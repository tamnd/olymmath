$\rightarrow$ In $\triangle A B C$, circles $\omega_{B}$ and $\omega_{C}$ are the excircles that are tangent to sides $A C$ and $A B$, respectively, and to the extensions of the other two sides. Circle $\omega_{B}^{\prime}$ is symmetric to $\omega_{B}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $A C$; circle $\omega_{C}^{\prime}$ is symmetric to $\omega_{C}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $A B$. Prove: The line passing through the intersection points of circles $\omega_{B}^{\prime}$ and $\omega_{C}^{\prime}$ bisects the perimeter of $\triangle A B C$.

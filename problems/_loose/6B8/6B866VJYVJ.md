Through point $A$, a line $l$ is drawn, intersecting circle $S$ with center $O$ at points $M$ and $N$ and not passing through $O$. Let $M^{\prime}$ and $N^{\prime}$ be the points symmetric to $M$ and $N$ with respect to $OA$, and let $A^{\prime}$ be the point of intersection of the lines $MN^{\prime}$ and $M^{\prime}N$. Prove that $A^{\prime}$ coincides with the image of point $A$ under inversion with respect to $S$ (and, therefore, does not depend on the choice of line $l$).

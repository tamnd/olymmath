$A B C$ is a right-angled isosceles triangle with the hypotenuse $A B$ extended outward. A semicircle is drawn inward over the leg $A C$, and a quarter-circle arc with radius $C A$ is drawn around $C$ over $A B$. A line passing through $C$ intersects the arcs at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. Prove that the area of triangle $B C E$ is equal to the area of the figure bounded by segment $D F$ and the arcs $B D$, $B F$.

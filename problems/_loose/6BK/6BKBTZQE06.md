For any given positive integer $n \geqslant 3$. Let the mapping $f$ : $\mathbf{C} \rightarrow \mathbf{C}$ satisfy the condition: for any vertices $z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}$ of any regular $n$-gon in the complex plane, we have
$$
f\left(z_{1}\right)+f\left(z_{2}\right)+\cdots+f\left(z_{n}\right)=0 .
$$

Prove: $f(z)=0(z \in \mathbf{C})$.

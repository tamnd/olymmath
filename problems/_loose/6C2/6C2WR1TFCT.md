Three points $A, B$, and $C$, located on the surface of a sphere with radius $R$, are connected by arcs of great circles, each less than a semicircle. Another great circle is drawn through the midpoints of the arcs $\overrightarrow{A B}$ and $\widehat{A C}$, intersecting the extension of $\widetilde{B} C$ at point $K$. Find the length of the arc $\widetilde{C K}$, if $|\overrightarrow{B C}|=l(l<\pi R)$.

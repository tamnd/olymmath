Prove that it is impossible to form a spatial polygonal path $A B C D E A$ from five straight rods that satisfies the following properties (1) and (2):

(1) No four of the five points $A, B, C, D, E$ lie in a common plane.

(2) When viewed from a suitable direction, the following holds: No two of the five points $A, B, C, D, E$ are seen exactly behind each other (i.e., appear to coincide);

an interior point of the segment $C D$ obscures an interior point of $A E$, an interior point of $B C$ obscures an interior point of $D E$, an interior point of $A E$ obscures an interior point of $B C$.

To prove that the circles

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+y^{2}-2 a x+b^{2} & =0 \text { and } \\
x^{2}+y^{2}-2 c y-b^{2} & =0
\end{aligned}
$$

intersect orthogonally, i.e., the tangents at their points of intersection form a right angle (assuming, of course, that the circles exist and are distinct).

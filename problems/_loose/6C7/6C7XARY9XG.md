19.5.11 ** Find all functions with the following properties:
$$
f:\{1,2, \cdots, n, \cdots\} \rightarrow \mathrm{Z} \text {. }
$$
(1) If $a$ and $b$ are positive integers, and $a \mid b$, then $f(a) \geqslant f(b)$.
(2) If $a$ and $b$ are positive integers, then
$$
f(a b)+f\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)=f(a)+f(b) .
$$

Given an isosceles triangle $ABC (AB = AC)$. On the smaller arc $AB$ of the circumcircle of this triangle, a point $D$ is taken. On the extension of segment $AD$ beyond point $D$, a point $E$ is chosen such that points $A$ and $E$ lie in the same half-plane relative to $BC$. The circumcircle of triangle $BDE$ intersects side $AB$ at point $F$. Prove that lines $EF$ and $BC$ are parallel.

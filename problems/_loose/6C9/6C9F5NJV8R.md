$V$ each of the four rooms there are several items. Let $n \geqq 2$ be a natural number. We move one $n$-th of the items from the first room to the second. Then, one $n$-th of the (new) number of items is moved from the second room to the third. Similarly, from the third room to the fourth, and from the fourth back to the first. (Each time, we move whole items.) If you know that at the end there was the same number of items in each room, determine the least number of items that could have been in the second room at the beginning. For which $n$ can this happen?

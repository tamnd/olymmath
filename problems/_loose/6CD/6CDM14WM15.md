It is known that the distance between $A$ and $B$ is 300 meters. Two people, Jia and Yi, start from $A$ and $B$ respectively at the same time, walking towards each other, and meet at a point 140 meters from $A$; if Yi walks 1 meter more per second, then the meeting point is 180 meters from $B$. What was Yi's original speed in meters per second?
A. $2 \frac{3}{5}$
B. $2 \frac{4}{5}$
C. 3
D. $3 \frac{1}{5}$

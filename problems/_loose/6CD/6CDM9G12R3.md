(BXMO 2022) A subset $A$ of $\mathbb{N}$ is said to be suitable if every integer $n>0$ has at most one prime factor $p$ such that $n-p \in A$.

1. Show that the set of perfect squares is suitable.
2. Give an infinite suitable set that contains no perfect squares.

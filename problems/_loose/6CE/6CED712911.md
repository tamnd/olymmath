For a sequence $ a_i \in \mathbb{R}, i \in \{0, 1, 2, \ldots\}$ we have $ a_0 = 1$ and $$ a_{n+1} = a_n + \sqrt{a_{n+1} + a_n} \forall n \in \mathbb{N}.$$ Prove that this sequence is unique and find an explicit formula for this recursively defined sequence.

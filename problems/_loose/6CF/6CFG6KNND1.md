Let the inradius of the bicentric quadrilateral (KLMN) be $r$, the circumradius be $\rho$, and the distance between the circumcenter and the incenter be $h$, then $\frac{1}{(\rho+h)^{2}}+\frac{1}{(\rho-h)^{2}}=\frac{1}{r^{2}}$.

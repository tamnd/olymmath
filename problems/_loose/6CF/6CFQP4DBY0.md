On the side $BC$ of the obtuse triangle $\triangle ABC$ (where $\angle C$ is the obtuse angle), select a point $D$ (different from $B$ and $C$). Through an internal point $M$ of the segment $BC$ (different from $D$), draw the line $AM$, intersecting the circumcircle $S$ of $\triangle ABC$ at point $N$. Construct a circle passing through points $M$, $D$, and $N$, intersecting the circle $S$ at $N$ and another point $P$. For what position of point $M$ is the length of segment $MP$ the shortest?

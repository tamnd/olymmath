Irish writer Lewis once wrote an ironic fable, in which he described a barbarous country called Niatopo. In this country, they use Sibabab numbers. The shape of Sibabab numbers is the same as the commonly used Arabic numerals, but their meanings are reversed. For example, "0" represents "9", "1" represents "8", and so on. They write numbers from left to right, and the operation symbols they use are the same as ours. For example, they use 62 to represent what we write as 37. According to the custom of the Niatopo people, how should the sum of $837+742$ be written? $ $

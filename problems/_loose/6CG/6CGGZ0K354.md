In a certain archipelago, there are $n$ islands connected by two-way ship and air routes. Between every two islands, there is exactly one direct route - either a ship route or an air route. We say that the archipelago is orderly connected if every circular tourist journey that starts and ends at the same island uses an even number of air routes.

For which natural numbers $n$ does every orderly connected archipelago with $n$ islands have an even number of air routes?

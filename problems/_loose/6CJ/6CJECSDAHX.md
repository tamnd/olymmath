\section*{Problem 6B - 121246B}

In the plane, let two non-intersecting circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ and a point $A$ outside both circles be given.

We are looking for an equilateral triangle $\triangle A B C$ such that $B$ lies on $k_{1}$ and $C$ lies on $k_{2}$.

a) Justify and describe a construction of such triangles.

b) Determine the largest number that can occur as the number of such triangles $\triangle A B C$ in cases where there are not infinitely many such triangles.

In metric spaces $(E, \rho)$ with metric $\rho$, the Borel $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{E}$ is defined as the system generated by open sets.

Show that in separable spaces $\mathscr{E}$ is generated by open balls. Verify that this is not the case in non-separable spaces.

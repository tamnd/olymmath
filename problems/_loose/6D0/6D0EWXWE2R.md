Let $a, b, c$ be positive numbers, and let $d$ be the minimum of $(a-b)^{2}$, $(b-c)^{2}$, and $(c-a)^{2}$.
(1) Prove that there exists $\lambda(0<\lambda<1)$, such that $d \leqslant \lambda\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$;
(2) Find the smallest positive number $\lambda$ for which the inequality (1) holds, and provide a proof.
(2006 Jiangsu Province Competition Problem)

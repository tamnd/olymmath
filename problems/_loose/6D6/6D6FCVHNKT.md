Given that $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}, \cdots, P_{35}$ are the 35 vertices of a convex 35-sided polygon in a plane, and the distance between any two points among $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}, \cdots, P_{35}$ is no less than $\sqrt{3}$. Prove that: from these 35 points, 5 points can be selected such that the distance between any two of these 5 points is no less than 3.

[7] A traveler arrived on an island where 50 natives live, each of whom is either a knight or a liar. All the natives stood in a circle, and each named the age of their left neighbor first, and then the age of their right neighbor. It is known that each knight named both numbers correctly, while each liar increased one of the ages (at their choice) by 1 and decreased the other by 1. Can the traveler always determine who is a knight and who is a liar based on the natives' statements?

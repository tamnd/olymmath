(Continued) Let $\mathcal{V}_{H}=\mathcal{V}_{H}(G)$ be the group of all $V \rightarrow H$ mappings, and $\mathcal{E}_{H}=\mathcal{E}_{H}(G)$ be the group of all $\vec{E} \rightarrow H$ mappings satisfying (F1), both groups using pointwise addition. Each $\varphi \in \mathcal{V}_{H}$ defines $\psi \in \mathcal{E}_{H}$ by $\psi(e, x, y):=\varphi(y)-\varphi(x)$.
(i) Prove: These $\psi$ form a subgroup $\mathcal{D}_{H}=\mathcal{D}_{H}(G)$ of $\mathcal{E}_{H}$ and satisfy $\mathcal{D}_{H}=\{\psi \in \mathcal{E}_{H} \mid$ for each oriented cycle $C \subseteq G$, $\left.\psi(\vec{C})=0\right\}$.
(ii) Prove: Each $\vec{E}(T) \rightarrow H$ mapping satisfying (F1) can be uniquely extended to a mapping in $\mathcal{D}_{H}$.

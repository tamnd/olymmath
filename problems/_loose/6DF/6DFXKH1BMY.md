Given an arithmetic progression $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{100}$. It is known that $a_{3}=9.5$, and the common difference of the progression $d=0.6$. Find the sum $\left\{a_{1}\right\}+\left\{a_{2}\right\}+\ldots+\left\{a_{100}\right\}$. The notation $\{x\}$ represents the fractional part of the number $x$, i.e., the difference between $x$ and the greatest integer not exceeding $x$, for example: $\{4.7\}=0.7,\left\{-5 \frac{1}{3}\right\}=\frac{2}{3}$, etc.

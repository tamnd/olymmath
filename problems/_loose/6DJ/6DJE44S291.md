Let the function $f$ be an increasing function from $\mathbf{R}$ to $\mathbf{R}$, and for any $x \in \mathbf{R}$, we have $f(x)=$ $f^{-1}(x)$ (here $f^{-1}(x)$ is the inverse function of $f(x)$). Prove: For any $x \in \mathbf{R}$, we have $f(x)=x$.

Let $x_{i} \in \mathrm{R},(i=1,2, \cdots, n)(n \geqslant 3), p=\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}, q=$ $\sum_{1 \leqslant i<k \leqslant n} x_{i} x_{k}$, prove: (1) $\frac{n-1}{n} p^{2}-2 q \geqslant 0$;
(2) $\left|x_{i}-\frac{p}{n}\right| \leqslant \frac{n-1}{n}$ $\sqrt{p^{2}-\frac{2 n}{n-1}} q(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$.

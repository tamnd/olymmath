(2000 Hebei Province Competition Question) Given that the graph of the function $f(x)=a x^{2}+b x+c$ passes through the point $(-1,0)$. Does there exist constants $a, b, c$ such that the inequality $x \leqslant f(x) \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\left(1+x^{2}\right)$ holds for all real numbers $x$? If they exist, find the values of $a, b, c$; if not, explain the reason.

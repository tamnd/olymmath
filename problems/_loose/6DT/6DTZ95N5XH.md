A polynomial $p$ can be written as
\begin{align*}
p(x) = x^6+3x^5-3x^4+ax^3+bx^2+cx+d.
\end{align*}
Given that all roots of $p(x)$ are equal to either $m$ or $n$ where $m$ and $n$ are integers, compute $p(2)$ .

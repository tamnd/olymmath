Let the ordered array $A=\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{10}\right)$ satisfy the following 4 conditions:
(1) $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{10}$ is a permutation of $1,2, \cdots, 10$;
(2) $a_{1}a_{3}, a_{4}>a_{5}, a_{6}>a_{7}, a_{8}>a_{9}$;
(4) There does not exist $1 \leqslant i<j<k \leqslant 10$, such that $a_{i}<a_{k}<a_{j}$. Find the number of such ordered arrays $A$.

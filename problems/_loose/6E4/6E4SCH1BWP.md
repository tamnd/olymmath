We have $31$ pieces where $1$ is written on two of them, $2$ is written on eight of them, $3$ is written on twelve of them, $4$ is written on four of them, and $5$ is written on five of them. We place $30$ of them into a $5\times 6$ chessboard such that the sum of numbers on any row is equal to a fixed number and the sum of numbers on any column is equal to a fixed number. What is the number written on the piece which is not placed?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ 1
\textbf{(B)}\ 2
\textbf{(C)}\ 3
\textbf{(D)}\ 4
\textbf{(E)}\ 5
$

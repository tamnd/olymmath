Let $x_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, n), \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$, prove:
$$
\frac{x_{1}}{\sqrt{1-x_{1}}}+\frac{x_{2}}{\sqrt{1-x_{2}}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n}}{\sqrt{1-x_{n}}} \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{x_{1}}+\sqrt{x_{2}}+\cdots+\sqrt{x_{n}}}{\sqrt{n-1}} .
$$

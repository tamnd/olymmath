Determine the smallest natural number $n$ for which there exist positive real numbers $x, y, z$ such that

$$
n=\lfloor x y\rfloor \cdot\lfloor y z\rfloor=(x+y+z)^{2}+2
$$

Note. For a real number $t$, we denote by $\lfloor t\rfloor$ the greatest integer less than or equal to $t$.

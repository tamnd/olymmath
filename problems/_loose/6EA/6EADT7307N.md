The participants of the Animath workshop are called $C_{1}, \ldots, C_{n}$ and form a line in front of the restaurant according to the following rules:

- The animators choose the initial order of the participants.
- At each step, the animators choose an integer $i$ with $1 \leqslant i \leqslant n$. If participant $C_{i}$ has at least $i$ people in front of him in the queue, he pays 1 euro to the animators and moves forward by $i$ places. Otherwise, the restaurant opens and the process ends.

Show that the process necessarily terminates and determine the maximum amount of money the animators can extort from the participants.

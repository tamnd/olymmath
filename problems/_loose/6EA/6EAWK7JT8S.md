In an isosceles triangle, the base is $a$, the vertex angle is less than $120^{\circ}$, and the height corresponding to the base is $m$. Each vertex of the triangle is reflected over the opposite side. The three resulting points form another isosceles triangle, whose base is $a^{\prime}$, and the height corresponding to the base is $m^{\prime}$. Show that

$$
\frac{a^{\prime}}{a}+\frac{m^{\prime}}{m}=4
$$

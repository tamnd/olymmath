Let $K, L, M$ be the intersections of the angle bisectors of $\alpha, \beta$, $\gamma$ at the vertices $A, B$, $C$ of a given triangle $ABC$ with the opposite sides $BC, CA, AB$. Prove that the inequality

$$
\frac{|BC|}{|AK|} \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{|CA|}{|BL|} \cos \frac{\beta}{2} + \frac{|AB|}{|CM|} \cos \frac{\gamma}{2} \geq 3
$$

holds.

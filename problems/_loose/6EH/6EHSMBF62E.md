On a line, consider in order the points $A, B, C, D$ such that $A B = B C$. The perpendicular at point $B$ to the line $A D$ intersects the circle with diameter $[A D]$ at points $P$ and $Q$, and the perpendicular at point $C$ to the line $A D$ intersects the circle with diameter $[B D]$ at points $K$ and $L$. Prove that the points $P, K, L$ and $Q$ lie on a circle whose center is point $B$.

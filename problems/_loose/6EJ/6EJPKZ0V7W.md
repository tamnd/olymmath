At the edge of a billiard table, which has the shape of a regular $2 n$-sided polygon, a ball is placed. How should the ball be launched from the edge so that, after reflecting sequentially from all the edges, it returns to the same point? (During reflection, the angles of incidence and reflection are equal.) Prove that in this case, the length of the ball's path does not depend on the choice of the initial point.

For each natural number $n>0$, let $f_{n}$ be the function defined by

$$
\begin{aligned}
& f_{1}(x)=(x-1)^{2} \\
& f_{2}(x)=(x-1)^{2}+(2 x-1)^{2} \\
& \text { in general } f_{n}(x)=(x-1)^{2}+(2 x-1)^{2}+\ldots+(n x-1)^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

for all real $x$. The graph of this function, a parabola in each case, has the vertex $S_{n}$.

a) Calculate the coordinates of $S_{1}, S_{2}$, and $S_{3}$.

b) If $S_{n}$ has the coordinates $\left(x_{n}, y_{n}\right)$, prove that the sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)$ is strictly decreasing and the sequence $\left(y_{n}\right)$ is strictly increasing.

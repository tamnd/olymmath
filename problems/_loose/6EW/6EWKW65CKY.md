Prove that every convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, in which the side lengths $AB=a, BC=b, CD=c$ and the interior angles $\angle ABC, \angle BCD$ are of size $\beta$ and $\gamma$ respectively, has the area:

$$
F=\frac{1}{2}(a b \cdot \sin \beta + b c \cdot \sin \gamma - a c \cdot \sin (\beta+\gamma))
$$

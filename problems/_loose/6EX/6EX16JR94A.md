Suppose that the lines $BI$ and $CI$ intersect the line $EF$ at $Y$ and $Z$, respectively. Let $M$ denote the midpoint of the segment $BC$, and $N$ denote the midpoint of the segment $YZ$. Prove that $AI \parallel MN$.

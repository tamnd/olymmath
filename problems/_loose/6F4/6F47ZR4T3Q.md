Let $p$ and $q$ be two real numbers such that the quadratic equation $x^{2} + p x + q = 0$ has two distinct real roots $u$ and $v (u > v)$. We slightly modify the coefficients $p$ and $q$ by less than 0.01, and assume that the modified equation $x^{2} + p^{\prime} x + q^{\prime} = 0$ (where $\left|p^{\prime} - p\right| < 0.01$ and $\left|q^{\prime} - q\right| < 0.01$) has two distinct real roots $u^{\prime}$ and $v^{\prime} (u^{\prime} > v^{\prime})$. Do such values of $p, q, p^{\prime}, q^{\prime}$ exist for which $\left|u^{\prime} - u\right| > 10000$?

Prove that for all strictly positive real numbers $a, b, c$ such that $a b c=1$, we have

$$
\frac{1}{1+a^{2}+(b+1)^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+b^{2}+(c+1)^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+c^{2}+(a+1)^{2}} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}
$$

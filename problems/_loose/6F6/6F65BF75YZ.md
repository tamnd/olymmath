a) In a plane, let $P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{n}$ be an arbitrary convex $n$-gon $E$.

Prove the following statement:

If points $Q_{1}, \ldots, Q_{n}$ are located inside or on the boundary of $E$ such that $Q_{1}, \ldots, Q_{n}$ form a $n$-gon congruent to $E$, then each point $Q_{i} (i=1,2, \ldots, n)$ is a vertex of $E$. (1)

b) Are there non-convex $n$-gons $E$ for which statement (1) is false?

c) Is statement (1) false for every non-convex $n$-gon $E$?

In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $BC < CA < AB$, $AD \perp BC$ at point $D$, $BE \perp AC$ at point $E$, $CF \perp AB$ at point $F$. Through point $F$, draw a line parallel to $DE$, intersecting line $BC$ at point $M$; the angle bisector of $\angle MFE$ intersects line $DE$ at point $N$. Prove: $F$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle DMN$ if and only if $B$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle FMN$.

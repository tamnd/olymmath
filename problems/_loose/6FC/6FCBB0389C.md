23-6 (C21, Vietnam) Let $S$ be a square with side length 100, and $L$ be a non-self-intersecting broken line segment $A_{11} A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n-1} A_{n}\left(A_{n} \neq A_{n}\right)$ inside $S$. For any point $P$ on the boundary of $S$, there exists a point on $L$ such that the distance between it and $P$ is no more than $\frac{1}{2}$. Prove that there must exist two points $X, Y$ on $L$ such that the distance between them is no more than 1, but the distance between them along the broken line $L$ is at least 198.

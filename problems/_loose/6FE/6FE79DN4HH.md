Let $A$ be a finite set of integers and $n \geqslant 1$. We denote by $N$ the number of $n$-tuples $\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ in $A^{n}$ satisfying $x_{1}+\cdots+x_{n}=-1$. And $M$ is the number of $n$-tuples in $A^{n}$ satisfying $x_{1}+\cdots+x_{n}=-1$ and for all integers $i$ such that $1 \leqslant i \leqslant n-1, x_{1}+\cdots+x_{i} \geqslant 0$.

Show that $M=\frac{N}{n}$.

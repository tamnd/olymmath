In each cell of a square table $n\times n$ , $1$ or $-1$ is entered. Let $a_i$ be the product of the numbers in the $i$ -th row, and $b_j$ be the product of the numbers in the $j$ -th column. Is it possible to fill in the table in such a way that the sum $$ a_1 +a_2 +...+a_i+ ...+a_n+b_1+b_2+...+b_j+...+b_n $$ is equal to zero when
a) $n =10$ ?
b) $n =9$ ?

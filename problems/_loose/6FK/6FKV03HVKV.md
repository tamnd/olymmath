Let the area of the convex pentagon $A B C D E$ be $1, F$ be a point inside $A B C D E$, and $F$ does not lie on any diagonal of the convex pentagon. Let $M=\{A, B, C, D, E, F\}$. If the three vertices of triangle $\beta$ are all in $M$, denote it as $[\beta] \subset M$, and use $S(\beta)$ to represent the area of triangle $\beta$. Define $a(M)=\min \left\{S\left(\beta_{1}\right)+S\left(\beta_{2}\right) \mid\left[\beta_{i}\right] \subset M(i=1,2)\right.$ and $\beta_{1}$ and $\beta_{2}$ have no common points $\}$.
Prove: $a(M) \leqslant \frac{1}{2}$.

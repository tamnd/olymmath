A cylindrical glass stands on a horizontal table, the water level inside is at a height of $m_{1}$ above the table. By tilting the glass, the plane of its base forms an angle with the table, the water does not spill out, and the new water level height is $m_{2}$. How much water is in the glass? How does the result change if we take into account that the thickness of the glass at the bottom is $d_{1}$ and on the side wall is $d_{2}$? Numerical data: $m_{1}=40 \mathrm{~mm}, a=25^{\circ}, m_{2}=48 \mathrm{~mm}, d_{1}=7 \mathrm{~mm}, d_{2}=3 \mathrm{~mm}$.

On a rectangular table, there are many equal and non-overlapping square pieces of paper, whose sides are parallel to the edges of the table and are each dyed one of $k(\geqslant 2)$ colors. If we consider any $k$ squares of different colors, then there are two among them that can be pinned to the table with a single pin. Prove: It is possible to pin all the squares of one color to the table with $2 k-2$ pins. (2000 26th Russian Olympiad Grade 10 Final Problem)

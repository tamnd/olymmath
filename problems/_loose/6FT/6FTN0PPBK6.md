Triangle $ABC (AB > BC)$ is inscribed in the circle $\Omega$. Points $M$ and $N$ are chosen on sides $AB$ and $BC$ respectively such that $AM = CN$. Lines $MN$ and $AC$ intersect at point $K$. Let $P$ be the incenter of triangle $AMK$, and $Q$ be the excenter of triangle $CNK$ that touches side $CN$. Prove that the midpoint of arc $ABC$ of circle $\Omega$ is equidistant from points $P$ and $Q$.

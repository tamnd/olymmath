Let $x, y, z, \lambda \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, k \geqslant 1$ and $x+y+z=3$, prove:
$$
\frac{x^{k}}{(\lambda+y)(\lambda+z)}+\frac{y^{k}}{(\lambda+z)(\lambda+x)}+\frac{z^{k}}{(\lambda+x)(\lambda+y)} \geqslant \frac{3}{(\lambda+1)^{2}} .
$$

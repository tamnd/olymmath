If in the interior of an equilateral triangle $ABC$, we arbitrarily choose a point $O$ and draw perpendiculars from this point to the sides, then the sum of these perpendiculars is equal to the height of the triangle. If, however, from $O$ we draw lines $OD$, $OE$, $OF$ that form equal angles $(\alpha)$ with the sides, then

$$
OD + OE + OF = CG
$$

if the line $CG$ also forms the same angle $\alpha$ with the side $AB$ of the triangle.

Given that the coordinates of point $P$ $(x, y)$ satisfy
$$
\begin{array}{c}
(x-a) \cos \theta_{1}+y \sin \theta_{1}=a, \\
(x-a) \cos \theta_{2}+y \sin \theta_{2}=a, \\
\tan \frac{\theta_{1}}{2}-\tan \frac{\theta_{2}}{2}=2 c(c>1),
\end{array}
$$

where $\theta_{1}, \theta_{2}$ are parameters, then the trajectory of point $P$ is
A. Circle
B. Ellipse
C. Hyperbola
D. Parabola

Prove that: a) a rotation around point $O$ by an angle $\alpha$ is equivalent to the sequential application of two axial symmetries, the axes of which pass through point $O$, and the angle between the axes is $\alpha / 2$; a parallel translation is equivalent to two axial symmetries with parallel axes; b) two consecutive rotations around point $O_{1}$ by an angle $\alpha$ and around point $O_{2}$ by an angle $\beta$ ( $0 \leqslant \alpha<2 \pi, 0 \leqslant \beta<2 \pi$, the rotations are made in the same direction) are equivalent to a single rotation by an angle $\alpha+\beta$ around some point $O$, if $\alpha+\beta \neq 2 \pi$. Find the angles of triangle $O_{1} O_{2} O$.

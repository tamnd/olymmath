Let $AB$ and $CD$ be two perpendicular diameters of the circle $k$, and $P$ be any point on the circle. Connect $P$ to $A$, and let this line intersect the diameter $CD$ at $E$: Draw a line through $E$ parallel to $AB$, and denote the intersection of this line with $CP$ as $F$. What is the geometric locus of the points $F$ as $P$ moves along the circle $k$?

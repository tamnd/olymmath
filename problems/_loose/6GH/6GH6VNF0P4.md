Let the sequence $\{a_n\}$ , $n \ge 0$ satisfy the recurrence relation $$ a_{n + 2} =4a_{n + 1}-3a_n, \ \ (1) $$ Let us define the sequence $\{b_n\}$ , $n \ge 1$ by the relation $$ b_n= \left[ \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n-1}} \right] $$ where we put $b_n =1$ for $a_{n-1}=0$ . Prove that, starting from a certain term, the sequence also satisfies the recurrence relation (1).
Note: $[x]$ indicates the whole part of the number $x$ .

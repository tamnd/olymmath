Suppose that a sequence $t_{0}, t_{1}, t_{2}, ...$ is defined by a formula $t_{n} = An^{2} +Bn +c$ for all integers $n \geq 0$. Here $A, B$ and $C$ are real constants with $A \neq 0$. Determine values of $A, B$ and $C$ which give the greatest possible number of successive terms of the Fibonacci sequence.* The Fibonacci sequence is defined by* $F_{0} = 0, F_{1} = 1$ *and* $F_{m} = F_{m-1} + F_{m-2}$ *for* $m \geq 2$.

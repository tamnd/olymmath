Consider the natural number $A=\overline{4 a}+\overline{a 4}$, where $a$ is a digit different from 0. The following is required:

a) Determine the digit $a$ for which the number $A$ is a perfect square.

b) Show that there is no $a$ such that the number $A$ is a perfect cube.

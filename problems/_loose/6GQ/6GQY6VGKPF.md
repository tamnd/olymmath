If $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n} ; b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}, \ldots, b_{n}$ are any real numbers, then

$$
\left(a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+\ldots+a_{n} b_{n}\right)^{2} \leqslant\left(a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\ldots+a_{n}^{2}\right)\left(b_{1}^{2}+b_{2}^{2}+\ldots+b_{n}^{2}\right)
$$

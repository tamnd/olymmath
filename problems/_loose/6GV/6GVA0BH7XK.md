Given that $M$ is a point on the median $A D$ of $\triangle A B C$, the line $B M$ intersects side $A C$ at point $N$, and $A B$ is a tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle N B C$. Let $B C=\lambda B N, B M=\varphi M N$. Then the relationship between $\lambda$ and $\varphi$ is ( ).
(A) $\varphi=\lambda^{2}$
(B) $\varphi=\lambda^{2}-1$
(C) $\varphi=\frac{\lambda^{3}}{\lambda+1}$
(D) $\varphi=\lambda^{2}+1$

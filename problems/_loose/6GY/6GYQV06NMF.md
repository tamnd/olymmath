Noble maidens. Noble maidens went to the theater. There are $n$ maidens in total, and all have tickets for one row, which has exactly $n$ seats. If a maiden needs to pass by an already seated maiden to take her seat, the latter must politely stand up to let her friend through.

a) (8th grade. 2 points). Find the expected number of stand-ups.

b) (9th grade. 5 points). Find the expected number of those who will not have to stand up at all.

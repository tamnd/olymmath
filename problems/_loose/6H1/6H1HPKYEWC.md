Two circles $O$ and $O_{1}$ of equal radii intersect at points $A$ and $B$. Through point $A$, their common secant $C A D$ is drawn, and from point $B$, a perpendicular is dropped to this secant, intersecting the circles at points $E$ and $F$. Prove that: a) points $C, E, D, F$ are the vertices of a rhombus with a side equal to $O O_{1}$, b) the opposite sides of the rhombus are parallel to the line of centers.

$A B C D$ is a tetrahedron with the following property: denoted by $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}$, respectively, the incenters of the faces $B C D, A C D, A B D$ and $A B C$, it is given that the lines $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}$ and $D D^{\prime}$ have a common point. Prove that the product of the lengths of two opposite edges of the tetrahedron is constant, that is, $A B \cdot C D = A C \cdot B D = A D \cdot B C$.

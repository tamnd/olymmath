Given that $p$ is a prime number greater than 5. Prove that there exist at least two distinct positive integers $q_{1}, q_{2}$ satisfying:
(1) $1 \leqslant q_{1}, q_{2} \leqslant p-1$;
(2) $q_{i}^{p-1} \equiv 1\left(\bmod p^{2}\right)(i=1,2)$.

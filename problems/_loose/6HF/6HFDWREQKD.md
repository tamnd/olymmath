Prove that if $\alpha<0<\beta$, then $S_{\alpha}(\boldsymbol{x}) \leq S_{0}(\boldsymbol{x}) \leq S_{\beta}(\boldsymbol{x})$, and $\lim _{\alpha \rightarrow-0} S_{\alpha}(\boldsymbol{x})=\lim _{\beta \rightarrow+0} S_{\beta}(\boldsymbol{x})=S_{0}(\boldsymbol{x})$.

The definition of power means $S_{\alpha}(x)$ can be found in the reference manual.

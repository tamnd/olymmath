Let the letters $a, b, c, x, y, z$ denote segments, and the letters $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ denote angles; what kind of geometric quantities do the following expressions represent: 1) $a^{2}+b c$; 2) $\frac{a b^{2}}{x y}$; 3) $\frac{(a+b)^{3}+c^{3}}{x}$; 4) $a b \sqrt{x y}$;
5) $\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}} \cdot(\sin \alpha+\sin \beta+\sin \gamma)$;
6) $\frac{a^{3}+b c x}{y} \operatorname{tg} \alpha \cdot \operatorname{tg} \beta \cdot \operatorname{tg} \gamma$.

The number $2^{n}$ is written on the board. Below it, two natural numbers are written, which sum up to the initial number. Then, under each of the sequentially obtained numbers, two natural numbers are written, which sum up to it, until a one is encountered. Prove that the sum of all the written numbers is not less than $n 2^{n}$.

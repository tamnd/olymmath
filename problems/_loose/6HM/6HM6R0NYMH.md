A circle is inscribed in a hexagon $A B C D E F$, in which $A B \perp B D,|B C|=|E F|$. Assume that the lines $B C, E F$ intersect the ray $A D$ at points $P, Q$ respectively. Let $S$ be the midpoint of the diagonal $A D$ and $K, L$ be the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles $B P S, E Q S$. Prove that triangle $K L D$ is a right triangle.

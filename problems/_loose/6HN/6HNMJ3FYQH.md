Let $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \ldots A_{2016} A_{2017}$ be a convex 2017-gon, $B_{k}$ the midpoints of the edges $A_{k} A_{k+1}$, $k=1, \ldots, 2016$, and $B_{2017}$ the midpoint of the edge $A_{1} A_{2017}$. Prove that the area of the polygon $B_{1} B_{2} B_{3} \ldots B_{2016} B_{2017}$ is not less than half the area of $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \ldots A_{2016} A_{2017}$, i.e., $S_{A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \ldots A_{2017}} < 2 S_{B_{1} B_{2} B_{3} \ldots B_{2017}}$.

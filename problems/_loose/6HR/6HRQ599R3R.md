Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots$ be pairwise independent events and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mathrm{P}\left(A_{n}\right)=\infty$. Prove that for $S_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n} I\left(A_{k}\right)$ the following second Borel-Cantelli lemma holds:

$$
\lim _{n} \frac{S_{n}}{E S_{n}}=1 \text { a.s. }
$$

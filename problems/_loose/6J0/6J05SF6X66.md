To test Peter's puzzle-solving skills, he is given seven statements about five students on a circle afternoon, and he is told that exactly one of these statements is false. He is to find the false statement and also order the students' names by age.

The statements are:

(1) Anton is older than Elvira.

(2) Berta is younger than Christine.

(3) Dieter is younger than Anton.

(4) Elvira is older than Christine.

(5) Anton is younger than Christine.

(6) Elvira is older than Dieter.

(7) Christine is younger than Dieter.

Determine the false statement, and order the students' names by age (starting with the youngest)!

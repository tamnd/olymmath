Prove that if a squaring scheme is constructed in $p$-arithmetic ${ }^{1}$, then to each point (except for the point 0) either no arrow applies or two arrows apply. In other words: prove that for $a \neq 0$, the equation $x^{2}=a$ has in $p$-arithmetic either two distinct solutions or no solutions at all.

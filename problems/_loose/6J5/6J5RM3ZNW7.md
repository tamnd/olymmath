In a plane, there are $n(\geqslant 4)$ points, no three of which are collinear, and there are $l\left(\geqslant\left[\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right]+1\right)$ line segments connecting them. If two triangles share a common edge, they are called an (unordered) pair of triangles. Let the number of triangle pairs formed by these $l$ line segments be $T$. Prove that: $T \geqslant \frac{l\left(4 l-n^{2}\right)\left(4 l-n^{2}-n\right)}{2 n^{2}}$.

Suggestion: Study the quantity of even and odd numbers in one of the subsets with three elements.

Facts that Help: The sum of two even or odd numbers results in an even number. The sum of an even number and an odd number results in an odd number.
A set of numbers is called trilegal if it can be divided into subsets with three elements in such a way that one of the elements is the sum of the other two. For example, the set $\{1,2,3, \ldots, 11,12\}$ is trilegal because it can be divided into $\{1,5,6\}$, $\{2,9,11\},\{3,7,10\}$ and $\{4,8,12\}$.

(a) Show that $\{1,2, \ldots, 14,15\}$ is trilegal.

(b) Show that $\{1,2, \ldots, 2010\}$ is not trilegal.

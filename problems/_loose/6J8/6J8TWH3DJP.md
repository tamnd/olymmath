Given a curve $C: x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x-2 y+1=0$ and a tangent line $l$ that intersects the $x$-axis and $y$-axis at points $A$ and $B$ respectively. $O$ is the origin, $|O A|=a,|O B|=b, a>2$, $b>2$.
(1) Prove: $(a-2)(b-2)=2$;
(2) Find the equation of the locus of the midpoint of segment $A B$;
(3) Find the minimum value of the area of $\triangle A O B$.

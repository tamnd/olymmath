Let $a$ , $b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=1$ . Prove the inequality $$ \left ( \frac{1+a}{b}+2 \right ) \left ( \frac{1+b}{c}+2 \right ) \left ( \frac{1+c}{a}+2 \right )\geq 216. $$ When does equality hold?

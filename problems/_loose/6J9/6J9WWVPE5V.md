In the tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$, the area of the face opposite vertex $A_{i}$ is denoted as $S_{i}(i=1,2,3,4)$, and the dihedral angle with edge $A_{i} A_{j}$ is $\theta_{i j}$, then
(I) $\sum_{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant 4} \cos ^{2} \theta_{i j} \geqslant \frac{2}{3}$; (II) $\Pi_{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant 4} \cos \theta_{i j} \leqslant \frac{1}{3^{6}}$.

If
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a \cos \alpha + b \sin \alpha = c, \\
a \cos \beta + b \sin \beta = c,
\end{array}
$$

Prove:
$$
a : b : c = \cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} : \sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} : \cos \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} .
$$

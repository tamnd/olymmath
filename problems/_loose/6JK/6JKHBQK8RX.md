Suppose there are two types of balls (weighing $p$ and $q$ respectively, with $p>q$) totaling 5 balls, each painted in one of three colors: red, yellow, or blue. The weights of the two red balls and the two yellow balls are all different; the weight of the one blue ball is unknown, being either $p$ or $q$, and cannot be determined by appearance. There is a balance scale without weights (it can only compare weights but not measure specific weights), and you are allowed to use it twice to distinguish the weights of all 5 balls. How should you proceed?

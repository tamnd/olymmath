Find the sum of the first $55$ terms of the sequence $$\binom{0}{0}, \binom{1}{0}, \binom{1}{1}, \binom{2}{0}, \binom{2}{1}, \binom{2}{2}, \binom{3}{0}, \ldots.$$ Note: For nonnegative integers $n$ and $k$ where $0\leq k\leq n$, $$\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{k!\left(n-k\right)!}.$$

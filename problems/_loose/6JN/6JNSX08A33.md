Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2008}$ be given positive integers, and $a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{2008}<10^{100}$. Prove: The set $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2008}\right\}$ contains two non-empty and disjoint subsets $A, B$, such that: (1) $A$ and $B$ have the same number of elements; (2) the sum of the elements in $A$ equals the sum of the elements in $B$; (3) the sum of the squares of the elements in $A$ equals the sum of the squares of the elements in $B$.

In a $100 \times 100$ grid, a red dot or a blue dot is placed in each cell, such that each row contains exactly 50 red dots and 50 blue dots, and each column also contains exactly 50 red dots and 50 blue dots. Blue segments are used to connect every two adjacent (sharing a common edge) cells containing blue dots, and red segments are used to connect every two adjacent cells containing red dots. Prove that the number of red and blue segments in the grid is equal.

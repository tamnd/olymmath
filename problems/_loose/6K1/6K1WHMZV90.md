Lattice points are points on a rectangular coordinate plane having both $x$ - and $y$-coordinates being integers. A moving point $P$ is initially located at $(0,0)$. It moves 1 unit along the coordinate lines (in either directions) in a single step.
G10.1 If $P$ moves 1 step then $P$ can reach $a$ different lattice points, find $a$.

G10.2 If $P$ moves not more than 2 steps then $P$ can reach $b$ different lattice points, find $b$.

G10.3 If $P$ moves 3 steps then $P$ can reach $c$ different lattice points, find $c$.

G10.4If $d$ is the probability that $P$ lies on the straight line $x+y=9$ when $P$ advances 9 steps, find $d$.

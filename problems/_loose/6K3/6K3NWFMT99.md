Let $n \geq 2$ be a natural number. Determine the complex numbers $z$ that satisfy the relations
a) $z^{n}+z^{n-1}+\ldots+z^{2}+|z|=n$;
b) $|z|^{n-1}+|z|^{n-2}+\ldots+|z|^{2}+z=n z^{n}$.

If $n$ is an even positive integer, the *double factorial* notation $n!!$ represents the product of all the even integers from $2$ to $n$ . For example, $8!! = 2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 \cdot 8$ . What is the units digit of the following sum? $$ 2!! + 4!! + 6!! + \cdots + 2018!! + 2020!! + 2022!! $$ $\textbf{(A)} ~0 \textbf{(B)} ~2 \textbf{(C)} ~4 \textbf{(D)} ~6 \textbf{(E)} ~8 $

Prove for positive numbers $a, b, c$ the inequality

$$
\frac{a^{2} b+b^{2} c+c^{2} a}{3\left(a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3}\right)}+\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{a b+b c+a c} \geqslant \frac{4}{3}
$$

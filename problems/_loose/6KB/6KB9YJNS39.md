$$
\frac{2 a \sqrt{1+x^{2}}}{x+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}=a+b
$$

$$
x=\frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}-\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}\right) \text { and } a>0, b>0 .
$$

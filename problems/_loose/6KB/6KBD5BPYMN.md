Divide the sides of the equilateral $\triangle A B C$ into $n$ equal parts, and draw lines parallel to the sides through the division points, dividing $\triangle A B C$ into $n^{2}$ congruent small equilateral triangles. Color the vertices of each small triangle with one of the three colors: red, blue, or yellow, with the condition that the points on side $B C$ are not colored red, the points on side $C A$ are not colored blue, and the points on side $A B$ are not colored yellow. Prove: There must exist a small equilateral triangle whose 3 vertices are of different colors.

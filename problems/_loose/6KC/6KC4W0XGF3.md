There are 18 teams participating in the opening ceremony of the competition. When entering, the 1st team has 27 people, the 2nd team has 26 people, ..., the 18th team has 10 people. If they all enter in a single-file line, and all representatives of the 18 teams are numbered $1,2, \cdots, 333$ in the order of their entry. Then the number of teams whose last representative is assigned an odd number is
(A) 8.
(B) 9.
(C) 10.
(D) 11.
（2nd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990）

a) Prove that $\frac{1}{2(k+1)}<\sqrt{2 k(2 k+1)}-\sqrt{(2 k-1)(2 k+2)}<1 ; \forall k \in N^{*}$

b) Calculate $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$.

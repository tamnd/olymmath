Rotate the ray $l: y=(4+\sqrt{15}) x(x>0)$ counterclockwise around the origin by a positive angle $\theta$. Prove that no matter how small the angle $\theta$ is, there are always infinitely many lattice points $P(m, n)$ in the region swept by the ray $l$, satisfying:
(i) $1+6 m n$ and $1+10 mn$ are both perfect squares;
(ii) $m\left|n^{2}-1, n\right| m^{2}-1$.

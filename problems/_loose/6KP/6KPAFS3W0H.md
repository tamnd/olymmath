Three. (50 points)
For $n$ given points on a plane $(n \geq 3)$, the following operation can be performed: for two points $A$ and $B$, they can be rotated by a certain angle around their midpoint to become two new points. Prove: If it is possible to adjust these $n$ points into a regular $n$-sided polygon through a finite number of operations, then the center and size of this regular $n$-sided polygon are unique.

Use barycentric coordinates to show Ménélaüs' theorem: Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $P, Q$ and $R$ be points on $BC, AC$ and $AB$ respectively. The points $P, Q, R$ are collinear if and only if

$$
\frac{BP}{PC} \cdot \frac{CQ}{QA} \cdot \frac{AR}{RB} = -1
$$

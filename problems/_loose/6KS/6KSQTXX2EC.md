Let $f(x)$ be a function defined on $[-1,1]$, and the slope of the line connecting any two points on its graph is less than zero.
(I) Determine the monotonicity of the function $f(x)$;
(II) If the intersection of the domains of the functions $f(x-c)$ and $f\left(x-c^{2}\right)$ is an empty set, find the range of real numbers $c$;
(III) When $-1 \leqslant c \leqslant 2$, find the domain of the function $f(x-c)+f\left(x-c^{2}\right)$.

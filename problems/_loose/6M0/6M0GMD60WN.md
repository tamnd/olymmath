A natural number was multiplied by 5, the result was again multiplied by 5, and so on, a total of $k$ multiplications were made.

It turned out that in the decimal representation of the original number and the $k$ resulting numbers, there is no

digit 7. Prove that there exists a natural number that can be multiplied by 2 $k$ times, and again there will be no digit 7 in the decimal representation of any of the numbers.

(A) $\frac{3 a}{5}$.
(B) $\frac{5 a}{8}$.
(C) $\frac{3 a}{8}$.
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{2} a}{2}$.
(E) $\frac{a}{2}$.
(14th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1963)

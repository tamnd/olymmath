A point is chosen uniformly at random from the interior of a unit square. Let $p$ be the probability that any circle centered at the point that intersects a diagonal of the square must also intersect a side of the square. Given that $p^2$ can be written as $m-\sqrt{n}$ for positive integers $m$ and $n$, what is $m+n$?

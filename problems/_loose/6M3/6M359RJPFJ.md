The (Fibonacci) sequence $f_n$ is defined by $f_1=f_2=1$ and $f_{n+2}=f_{n+1}+f_n$ for
$n\ge1$. Prove that the area of the triangle with the sides $\sqrt{f_{2n+1}},\sqrt{f_{2n+2}},$ and $\sqrt{f_{2n+3}}$ is equal to $\frac12$.

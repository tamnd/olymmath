Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, and let circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ be tangent to line $A B$ at point $U$, and also tangent to circle $\Gamma$ at point $V$, with points $V$ and $C$ on the same side of line $A B$. The angle bisector of $\angle B C A$ intersects circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ at points $P$ and $Q$, and intersects circle $\Gamma$ at point $M$. Prove:
$$
\angle A B P=\angle Q B C .
$$

From the numerical examples of practice 970 (K. M. L. 32 (February 1966) p. 68), we conjectured that if $|x|<0.1$, then the following expression provides an approximate value for the square root of $1+x$:

$$
1+\frac{x}{2}-\frac{x^{2}}{2^{3}}+\frac{x^{3}}{2^{4}}-\frac{5 x^{4}}{2^{7}}
$$

Show that this expression can be obtained by a modification of the procedure seen in practice 1029 (K. M. L. 33 (November 1966) p. 143). - Extend (1) by two additional terms using the modified procedure. - What do we get if we apply this expression to the two expressions for $\sqrt{10}$ from practice 1100 and calculate to 7 decimal places? What do we get, however, with the usual calculator for $\sqrt{10}$?

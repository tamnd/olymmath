Let $P(x)=a_{n} x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\ldots+a_{0}$ be a polynomial with real coefficients, and $a_{n} \geqslant 1$. Prove that if the number $m$ is greater than any of the numbers $\left|a_{n-1}\right|+1, \ldots$ $\ldots,\left|a_{0}\right|+1$, then $Q(x)=P(x+m)$ is a polynomial with positive coefficients.

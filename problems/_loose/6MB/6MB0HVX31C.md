Let $a b c \neq 0$, and the remainders when the polynomial $f(x)$ is divided by $(x-a)(x-b)$, $(x-b)(x-c)$, and $(x-c)(x-a)$ are $p x+l$, $q x+m$, and $r x+n$, respectively. Prove:
$$
l\left(\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}\right)+m\left(\frac{1}{b}-\frac{1}{c}\right)+n\left(\frac{1}{c}-\frac{1}{a}\right)=0 .
$$

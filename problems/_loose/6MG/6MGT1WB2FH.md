Let $n\ge 3$ be an integer such that for every prime factor $q$ of $n-1$ exists an integer $a > 1$ such that $a^{n-1} \equiv 1 \,(\mod n )$ and $a^{\frac{n-1} {q}}\not\equiv 1 \,(\mod n )$. Prove that $n$ is not prime.

Let $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \to \mathbb{R}^{+}$such that for every rectangle $A B C D$ one has

$$
f(A)+f(C)=f(B)+f(D).
$$

Let $K L M N$ be a quadrangle in the plane such that $f(K)+f(M)=f(L)+f(N)$, for each such function. Prove that $K L M N$ is a rectangle.

*Proposed by Navid.*

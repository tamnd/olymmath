Among them, there are 4 triangles, 4 squares, 4 pentagons, and 4 hexagons, and also 4 red, 4 yellow, 4 green, and 4 blue discs, such that no two discs are the same in both shape and color. Place our discs on the 16 squares of a 4x4 grid, one per square, so that in every row, every column, and both diagonals, there are 4 discs of different shapes and different colors.

A *simple polygon* is a polygon whose perimeter does not self-intersect. Suppose a simple polygon $\mathcal P$ can be tiled with a finite number of parallelograms. Prove that regardless of the tiling, the sum of the areas of all rectangles in the tiling is fixed.\\

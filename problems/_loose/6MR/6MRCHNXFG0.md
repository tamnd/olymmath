Let's prove that if in a triangle

$$
\sin C-\cos B=\cos A,
$$

or

$$
a+\cot \left(45^{\circ}-B\right)=\frac{2}{1-\cot C}
$$

then the triangle is a right triangle. Conversely: in every right triangle

$$
\cos (2 C-B)=\frac{c}{a^{3}}\left(3 a^{2}-4 c^{2}\right)
$$

Let $a, b, c$ be the lengths of the sides of a triangle, where $a$ is the longest. Prove that the triangle is a right triangle if and only if

$$
(\sqrt{a+b}+\sqrt{a-b}) \cdot(\sqrt{a+c}+\sqrt{a-c})=\sqrt{2}(a+b+c)
$$

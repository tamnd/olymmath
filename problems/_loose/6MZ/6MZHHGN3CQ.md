Prove that for any natural $n \geqslant 2$ the following inequalities hold:

$$
n(\sqrt[n]{n+1}-1)<1+\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n}<n\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt[n]{n}}\right)+1
$$

Let $S$ be a set of natural numbers less than a given prime number $P$ such that if the numbers $A$ and $B$ are in the set, then the remainders of $A B, A$, and $B$ when divided by $P$ are also in the set. Prove that if $S$ contains at least two numbers, then the sum of the numbers in $S$ is divisible by $P$.

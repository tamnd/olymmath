If, however, it is used during charging, $40 \%$ of the introduced energy is immediately consumed, and only the remaining part accumulates in the battery. Knowing that it took 2 and a half hours to recharge the battery from zero, determine for how many minutes the phone was used during the charging (assuming that, whether or not the phone is used, the energy stored in a time interval is proportional to its duration).
(A) 72
(B) 90
(C) 60
(D) 87
(E) 75

Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers such that $a+b=7$ and $a b=5$. What is the value of $a^{3}+b^{3}$? We will not seek to explicitly determine $a$ and $b$.

---

The problem can be solved using the identity for the sum of cubes:
$$ a^3 + b^3 = (a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) $$

First, we need to find $a^2 + b^2$. We can use the square of the sum of $a$ and $b$:
$$ (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 $$

Given $a + b = 7$ and $ab = 5$, we substitute these values into the equation:
$$ 7^2 = a^2 + 2 \cdot 5 + b^2 $$
$$ 49 = a^2 + 10 + b^2 $$
$$ a^2 + b^2 = 49 - 10 $$
$$ a^2 + b^2 = 39 $$

Next, we substitute $a^2 + b^2$ and $ab$ into the identity for the sum of cubes:
$$ a^3 + b^3 = (a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) $$
$$ a^3 + b^3 = 7(a^2 + b^2 - ab) $$
$$ a^3 + b^3 = 7(39 - 5) $$
$$ a^3 + b^3 = 7 \cdot 34 $$
$$ a^3 + b^3 = 238 $$

Thus, the value of $a^3 + b^3$ is $\boxed{238}$.

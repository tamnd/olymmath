In space, there are spherical surfaces $g_{1}, g_{2}, g_{3}$ with different radii. It needs to be proven that if the sphere $g$ changes in such a way that it always touches $g_{1}$, $g_{2}$, and $g_{3}$ in the same sense (i.e., the contact with each sphere is either always external or always internal), then the lines connecting the contact points $E_{1}, E_{2}, E_{3}$ in pairs have a constant point each, and these three constant points lie on a single line. How does the statement change if two, or all three, of the radii of the spheres are equal?

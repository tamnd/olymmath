Then the second traveler woke up, took $\frac{1}{3}$ of the remaining potatoes, and went back to sleep. Finally, the third traveler woke up and took $\frac{1}{3}$ of the remaining potatoes, and went back to sleep. The innkeeper had carefully observed all of this and when he counted the remaining potatoes, he saw that there were 8 left.

M In a country, there are several castles, from each of which 3 roads extend. It is known that a knight comes out from one of the castles and walks along these roads. Whenever he passes through a castle, he turns onto the road to the left or right relative to the direction he came from. However, if he turns left this time, he will definitely turn right the next time, and he never turns in the same direction twice in a row. Prove that during his journey, he must return to the original castle at some point.

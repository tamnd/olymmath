Triangle $A B C$ has side $A C$ measuring $24 \text{cm}$ and the height from vertex $B$ is $25 \text{cm}$. Side $A B$ is divided into five equal parts, with the division points sequentially from $A$ to $B$ labeled as $K, L, M, N$. Through each of these points, a line parallel to side $A C$ passes. The intersections of these parallel lines with side $B C$ are sequentially from $B$ to $C$ labeled as $O, P, Q, R$.

Calculate the sum of the areas of trapezoids $K L Q R$ and $M N O P$.

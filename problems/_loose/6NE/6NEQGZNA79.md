The proposition equivalent to the following statement: "If the pink elephants on planet $\alpha$ have purple eyes, then the wild boars on planet $\beta$ do not have long noses" is
I. "If the wild boars on planet $\beta$ have long noses, then the pink elephants on planet $\alpha$ have purple eyes."
II. "If the pink elephants on planet $\alpha$ do not have purple eyes, then the wild boars on planet $\beta$ do not have long noses."
III. "If the wild boars on planet $\beta$ have long noses, then the pink elephants on planet $\alpha$ do not have purple eyes."
IV. "The pink elephants on planet $\alpha$ do not have purple eyes, or the wild boars on planet $\beta$ do not have long noses."
(A) Only I and III.
(B) Only III and N.
(C) Only II and IV.
(D) Only II and III.
(E) Only III.
(27th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1976)

By breaking an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, we mean the operation of placing a point $O$ inside it such that $OA=OB=OC$, and cutting it into triangles $OAB$, $OAC$, and $OBC$. Petya took a triangle with angles $3^{\circ}$, $88^{\circ}$, and $89^{\circ}$ and broke it into three triangles. Then he chose one of the pieces (also acute-angled) and broke it. He continued this until all the triangles were obtuse. How many triangles did he end up with?

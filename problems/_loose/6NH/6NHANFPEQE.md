Let $M$ be a non-empty set of positive integers and let $S_M$ be the sum of all the elements of $M$ . We define the *tlacoyo* of $M$ as the sum of the digits of $S_M$ . For example, if $M=\{2,7,34\}$ , then $S_M=2+7+34=43$ and the tlacoyo of the set $M$ is $4+3=7$ .
Prove that for every positive integer $n$ , there exists a set $M$ of $n$ distinct positive integers, such that all its non-empty subsets have the same tlacoyo.

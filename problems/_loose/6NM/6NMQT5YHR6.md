Let $a, b, c$ represent the lengths of the sides of triangle $ABC$. Draw the tangents to the incircle of triangle $ABC$ that are parallel to the sides. These tangents cut off three new triangles from triangle $ABC$; in each of these new triangles, we again draw the incircle. Calculate the sum of the areas of the four incircles.

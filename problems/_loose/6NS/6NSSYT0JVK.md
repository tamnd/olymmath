Point $P$ is taken interior to a square with side-length $a$ and such that is it equally distant from two consecutive vertices and from the side opposite these vertices. If $d$ represents the common distance, then $d$ equals: $\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{3a}{5} \textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{5a}{8} \textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{3a}{8} \textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{a\sqrt{2}}{2} \textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{a}{2}$

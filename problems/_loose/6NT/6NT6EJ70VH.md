In the circle $\omega$, a diameter $A B$ is drawn, and a point $X$ is fixed on this diameter. Point $P$ lies on the circle $\omega$ and does not coincide with points $A$ and $B$. Prove that the ratio

$$
\frac{\operatorname{tg} \angle A P X}{\operatorname{tg} \angle P A X}
$$

does not depend on the choice of point $P$.

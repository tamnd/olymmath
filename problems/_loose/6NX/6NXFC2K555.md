For each polynomial
$$
f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+\cdots+a_{n} x^{n},
$$

let $\Gamma(f(x))=a_{0}^{2}+a_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}$. Given the polynomial
$$
P(x)=(x+1)(x+2) \cdots(x+2020) \text {. }
$$

Prove: There exist at least $2^{2019}$ positive coefficient polynomials $Q_{k}(x)\left(1 \leqslant k \leqslant 2^{2019}\right)$ satisfying the following two conditions:
(1) $\operatorname{deg} Q_{k}(x)=2020\left(1 \leqslant k \leqslant 2^{2019}\right)$;
(2) For each positive integer $n$, we have
$$
\Gamma\left(Q_{k}^{n}(x)\right)=\Gamma\left(P^{n}(x)\right) .
$$

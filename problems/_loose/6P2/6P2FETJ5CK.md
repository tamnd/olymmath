Let $f(x)=\log _{3}\left(x^{2}-4 m x+4 m^{2}+m+\frac{1}{m-1}\right)$.
(1) Find all real numbers $m$ such that the domain of $f(x)$ is all real numbers.
(2) Let the set of real numbers $m$ that satisfy (1) be denoted by $M$. Prove that for any $m \in M$, the following property holds: for any $x \in \mathbf{R}$, the function value $f(x) \geqslant 1$.

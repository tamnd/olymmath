Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ be the sequence for which $a_{1}=2$ and

$$
a_{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}^{2}+1}{a_{n}+1} (n=1,2,3, \ldots)
$$

holds.

a) Calculate $a_{2}$ and $a_{3}$, and prove that $a_{n}>1$ for all $n=1,2,3, \ldots$!

b) Prove that the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)$ is strictly monotonically decreasing!

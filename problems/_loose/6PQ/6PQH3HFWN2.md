In the courtroom, witnesses $A$, $B$, and $C$ answered the same questions, each with a "yes" or "no." For the questions where both $B$ and $C$ answered "yes," $A$ also answered "yes"; for the questions where $A$ answered "yes," $B$ also did, and for the questions where $B$ answered "yes," at least one of $A$ and $C$ also did.

Prove that $A$ and $B$ answered the same to every question.

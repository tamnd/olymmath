The circles $k_{a}$ with radius $a$ and $k_{b}$ with radius $(a>b)$ have a common center $O$. From point $A$ on $k_{a}$, one of the tangents drawn to $k_{b}$ touches it at point $E$. On the ray $O E$, a perpendicular is erected at any point $P$, intersecting $k_{b}$ at points $Q$ and $R$, and the line $A O$ at point $T$. By measuring the distance $P Q$ on the perpendicular to $A O$ at $T$, we obtain points $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$. What is the geometric locus of the points $S_{i}$ as $P$ runs along the ray $O E$?

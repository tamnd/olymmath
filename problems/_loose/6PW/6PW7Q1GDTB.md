Two small villages in Landa, $A$ and $B$, are located along the same straight section of a railway track, 10 km apart. Although the terrain here is flat, the road connecting the villages is quite winding. One cyclist noticed that no matter where he is on this road at point $P$, the sum of the length $B H$, multiplied by 20, where $H$ is the projection of the cyclist's position $P$ onto the railway track, the cube of this length, multiplied by 13, and the length $PH$, multiplied by 300, is always equal to the sum of the fourth power of the length $PH$ and the square of this length, multiplied by 32 (all lengths are expressed in kilometers).

How many railway crossings does the cyclist encounter on the way from $A$ to $B$?

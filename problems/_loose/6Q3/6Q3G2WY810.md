For $\triangle A B C$, let the projections of points $B, C$ on the bisector of $\angle C A B$ be $B_{A}, C_{A}$, respectively. Let the projections of points $C, A$ on the bisector of $\angle A B C$ be $C_{B}, A_{B}$, respectively. Let the projections of points $A, B$ on the bisector of $\angle A C B$ be $A_{C}, B_{C}$, respectively. Let the midpoints of sides $B C, C A, A B$ be $M_{A}, M_{B}, M_{C}$, respectively. The circle passing through points $M_{A}, B_{C}, C_{B}$ is denoted as $\odot A^{\prime}$, the circle passing through points $M_{B}, C_{A}, A_{C}$ is denoted as $\odot B^{\prime}$, and the circle passing through points $M_{C}, A_{B}, B_{A}$ is denoted as $\odot C^{\prime}$. Prove: The nine-point circle center of $\triangle A B C$ coincides with the circumcenter of $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$.
(2015, Romanian National Team Selection Test)

In the configuration below, $\theta$ is measured in radians, $C$ is the center of the circle,
$BCD$ and $ACE$ are line segments and $AB$ is tangent to the circle at $A$.

A necessary and sufficient condition for the equality of the two shaded areas, given $0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}$, is
$\textbf{(A)}\ \tan \theta = \theta \textbf{(B)}\ \tan \theta = 2\theta \textbf{(C)}\ \tan\theta = 4\theta \textbf{(D)}\ \tan 2\theta =\theta \\ \textbf{(E)}\ \tan\frac{\theta}{2}=\theta$

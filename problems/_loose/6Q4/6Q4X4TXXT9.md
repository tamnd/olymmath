Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $A', B', C'$ the midpoints of sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, and $P, Q, R$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$ respectively. Let $S_{1}$ be the point of intersection of $(PQ)$ and $\left(A' C'\right)$, and $S_{2}$ the point of intersection of $(PR)$ and $\left(A' B'\right)$. Show that the points $A, S_{1}, S_{2}$ are collinear.

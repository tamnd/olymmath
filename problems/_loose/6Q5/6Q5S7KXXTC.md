The bisector of angle $A$ of triangle $A B C$ intersects the circumscribed circle at point $D$. Let $P$ be the point symmetric to the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$ with respect to the midpoint of side $B C$, $M$ the second intersection point of line $D P$ with the circumscribed circle. Prove that the distance from point $M$ to one of the vertices $A, B, C$ is equal to the sum of the distances from $M$ to the other two vertices.

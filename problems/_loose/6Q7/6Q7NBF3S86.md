Let $f:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a monotonic function and $a,b,c,d$ be real numbers with $a$ and $c$ nonzero. Prove that if the equalities $\int\limits_x^{x+\sqrt{3}} f(t) \mathrm{d}t=ax+b$ and $\int\limits_x^{x+\sqrt{2}} f(t) \mathrm{d}t=cx+d$ hold for every real number $x,$ then $f$ is a polynomial function of degree one.

Prove: The space of a graph is respectively supported by the following sets
(i) all its induced subgraphs;
(ii) all its geodetic cycles.
(A cycle $C$ is called geodetic in $G$ if for any two vertices of $C$, their distance in $G$ is always equal to their distance in $C$.)

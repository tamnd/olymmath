(13 (16 points) Given $p>0$, a line passing through the focus of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$ has a slope of $k$ and intersects the parabola at points $A$ and $B$. Let $f(k)=|A B|$.
(1) Try to find the analytical expression for $f(k)$;
(2) Does there exist a point $C$ on the parabola such that $\triangle A B C$ is an isosceles right triangle with $C$ as the right-angle vertex? If it exists, find the coordinates of point $C$; if not, explain the reason.

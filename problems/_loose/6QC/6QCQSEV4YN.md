An arithmetic sequence is a sequence in which each term after the first is obtained from the previous term by adding a constant. For example, $5,7,9$ is an arithmetic sequence with three terms.

(a) Three of the five numbers $2,5,10,13,15$ can be chosen to form an arithmetic sequence with three terms. What are the three numbers?

(b) The numbers $p, 7, q, 13$, in that order, form an arithmetic sequence with four terms. Determine the value of $p$ and the value of $q$.

(c) The numbers $a, b, c,(a+21)$, in that order, form an arithmetic sequence with four terms. Determine the value of $c-a$.

(d) The numbers $(y-6),(2 y+3),\left(y^{2}+2\right)$, in that order, form an arithmetic sequence with three terms. Determine all possible values of $y$.

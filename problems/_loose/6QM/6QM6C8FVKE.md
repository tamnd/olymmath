Assuming the following three statements are true:
I. All freshmen are human.
II. All graduate students are human.
III. Some graduate students are thinking.

Given the following four statements:
(1) All freshmen are graduate students.
(2) Some people are thinking.
(3) No freshmen are thinking.
(4) Some people who are thinking are not graduate students.

Which of the following statements are logical consequences of I, II, and III?
(A) (2).
(B) (4).
(D) (2), (4).
(E) (1), (2).
(C) (2), (3).
(10th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1959)

Suppose necklace $A$ has 14 beads; $B$ has 19 beads. Prove: For every odd number $n \geqslant 1$, it is possible to label the beads with the numbers in the sequence
$$
\{n, n+1, n+2, \cdots, n+32\}
$$

such that each number is used exactly once and the numbers on adjacent beads are coprime.

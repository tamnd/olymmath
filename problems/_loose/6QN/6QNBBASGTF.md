Prove that the function

$$
f(x)=x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\ldots+a_{1} x-1
$$

with $a_{i} \in G$ for $i=1 ; 2 ; \ldots ; n-1$ has no rational root if $f( \pm 1) \neq 0$ (where $G$ is the set of integers).

Compute the number of functions $f\colon\{1, \dots, 15\} \to \{1, \dots, 15\}$ such that, for all $x \in \{1, \dots, 15\}$ , $$
\frac{f(f(x)) - 2f(x) + x}{15}
$$is an integer.

Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, and $A$ one of their points of intersection. A common tangent to $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ touches them respectively at $B$ and $C$. Let $O_{3}$ be the center of the circumcircle of $A B C$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $\left[O_{1} O_{2}\right]$ and $D$ the symmetric point of $O_{3}$ with respect to $A$. Show that $\widehat{O_{1} D A}=\widehat{O_{2} D M}$.

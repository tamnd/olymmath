In the nodes of a grid of a rectangular cell, lamps are placed, initially all of them are turned off. In one move, it is allowed to draw any straight line that does not touch the lamps (the sizes of the lamps should be neglected, considering them as points), such that on one side of it no lamp is lit, and turn on all the lamps on this side of the line. Each move must light at least one lamp. Is it possible to light all the lamps in exactly four moves?

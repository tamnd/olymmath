Prove the following theorems:

I) If $A B C$ is a triangle with $\overline{A C}=\overline{B C}$, then the median from $A B$ is also the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$.

II) If in a triangle $A B C$ the median from $A B$ is also the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$, then $\overline{A C}=\overline{B C}$.

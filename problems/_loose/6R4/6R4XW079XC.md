On each cell of a $5 \times 5$ board, there is one coin, all coins look the same. Among them, exactly 2 coins are counterfeit, they weigh the same and are lighter than the genuine ones, which also weigh the same. The counterfeit coins lie in cells that share exactly one common vertex. Can one guarantee to find a) 13 genuine coins; b) 15 genuine coins; c) 17 genuine coins in one weighing on a balance scale without weights?

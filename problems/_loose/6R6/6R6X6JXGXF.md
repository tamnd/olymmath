(2005 IMO) (China National Training Team Selection Problem) Let $a$ be a given positive real number. Find all functions $f: \mathbf{N}$
$\rightarrow \mathbf{R}$, such that for any positive integers $k, m$ satisfying $a m \leqslant k<(a+1) m$, we have $f(k+m)=f(k)+f(m)$.

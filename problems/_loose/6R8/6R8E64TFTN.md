In $n$ beakers, $n$ different liquids are poured, and there is also one empty beaker. Is it possible to achieve uniform mixtures in each beaker in a finite number of operations, that is, to make it so that each beaker contains $1 / n$ of the initial amount of each liquid, and at the same time one beaker is empty.

(The beakers are identical, but the amounts of liquids in them can be different; it is assumed that any volume of liquid can be measured.)

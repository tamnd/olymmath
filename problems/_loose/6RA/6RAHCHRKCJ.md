Given triangle $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$, with sides $a_{1}=\overline{A_{2} A_{3}}, a_{2}=\overline{A_{3} A_{1}}$ and $a_{3}=\overline{A_{1} A_{2}}$. Let $s_{1}, s_{2}, s_{3}$ be the lengths of the tangent segments from the incircle of the triangle, starting from $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$, respectively. Prove that $\frac{s_{1}}{a_{1}}+\frac{s_{2}}{a_{2}}+\frac{s_{3}}{a_{3}} \geq \frac{3}{2}$. When does equality hold?

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, $k$ its circumcirle and $m$ a line such that $m\cap k=\emptyset, m\parallel BC.$ Denote $D$ the intersection of $m$ and ray $AB.$ a) Let $X$ be an inner point of the arc $BC$ not containing $A$ and denote $Y$ the intersection of lines $m,CX.$ Show that $A,D,X,Y$ are concyclic and name this circle $\kappa$ .
b) Determine relative position of $\kappa$ and $m$ in case when $C,D,X$ are collinear.

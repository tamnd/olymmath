Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid in which the lines $(A D)$ and $(B C)$ are parallel. Let $E$ be a point on the segment $[A B]$ and $F$ be a point on the segment $[D C]$. Let $A_{1}$ be the point of intersection, other than $A$, of the line (AD) with the circumcircle of triangle $A E F$. Let $C_{1}$ be the point of intersection, other than C, of the line (BC) with the circumcircle of triangle CEF. Show that the lines (BD), (EF) and $\left(A_{1} \mathrm{C}_{1}\right)$ are concurrent.

Josefina the Quail dances by the marsh, using steps of double length - short steps measure $45 \mathrm{~cm}$, long steps $60 \mathrm{~cm}$. Over time, she has worn an oval path, which she dances around repeatedly during long nights. If she repeats three long steps forward and one short step back, then the ninetieth step will bring her exactly back to where she started. If she repeats three short steps forward and one long step back, she also ends up exactly where she started.

Which step will bring Josefina back to the original spot in the second case?

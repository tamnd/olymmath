One hundred gnomes, whose weights are $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 100$ pounds, have gathered on the left bank of a river. They cannot swim, but on the same bank, there is a rowboat with a capacity of 100 pounds. Due to the current, it is difficult to row back, so each gnome has enough strength to row from the right bank to the left bank only once (any gnome is sufficient to row the boat; the rower does not change during one trip). Can all the gnomes cross to the right bank?

(A. Shapovalov, S. Usov)

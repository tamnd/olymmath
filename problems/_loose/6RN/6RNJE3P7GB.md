A frog is at the center of a circular shaped island with radius $r$. The frog jumps $1/2$ meters at first. After the first jump, it turns right or left at exactly $90^\circ$, and it always jumps one half of its previous jump. After a finite number of jumps, what is the least $r$ that yields the frog can never fall into the water?

$ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac{\sqrt 5}{3}
\textbf{(B)}\ \frac{\sqrt {13}}{5}
\textbf{(C)}\ \frac{\sqrt {19}}{6}
\textbf{(D)}\ \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}
\textbf{(E)}\ \frac34
$

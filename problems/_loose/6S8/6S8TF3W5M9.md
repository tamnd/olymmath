Between the legs $s_{1}$ and $s_{2}$ of an acute angle lies the point $P$. The vertex of the angle is $S$.

Construct on $s_{1}$ and $s_{2}$ the points $X$ for which the length of the segment $X S$ is equal to the length of the segment $X P$, that is, for which $X S = X P$ holds.

a) There are three piles of stones; a boy playing with them is allowed to take one stone simultaneously from all three piles or to double the number of stones in one (any) pile. Will the boy be able to remove all the stones from the piles after several such "moves"?

b) In all cells of a rectangular table with 8 rows and 5 columns, natural numbers are arranged. It is allowed to double the number in one column (any) or to subtract one from all the numbers in one row. Prove that by several "permissible" transformations of the table of numbers, one can achieve that all its cells contain only zeros.

Let $a_{n} x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{2} x^{2}-x+1=0$ have $n$ positive real roots. Prove that: $0<2^{2} a_{2}+\cdots+$ $2^{n} a_{n} \leqslant\left(\frac{n-2}{n}\right)^{2}+1$.
(Mathematical Olympiad Problems, Intermediate Mathematics, Issue 6, 1997)

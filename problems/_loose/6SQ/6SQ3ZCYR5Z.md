For given $n$ different numbers $a_{2}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ $\in \mathbf{N}^{+}, n>1$. Let
$p_{i}=\prod_{1 \leqslant j \leqslant n, j \neq i}\left(a_{i}-a_{j}\right)(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$. Prove: For any $k \in \mathbf{N}^{+}, \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{p_{i}^{k}}{p_{i}}$ is an integer.

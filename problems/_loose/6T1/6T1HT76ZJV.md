a) The square ABCD and the rectangle ABEF are located in perpendicular planes. If $A B=40 \text{ cm}, B E=30 \text{ cm}$, find $d(D, E F)$ and $d(E, A C)$.

b) The cube OLEMPICS has the edge length $O P=6 \text{ cm}$.

Calculate: i) the length of the diagonal of the rectangular parallelepiped formed by joining two such cubes;

ii) $d(M,(P L C))$;

iii) $m(<(O C,(O L E)))$.

The section of the rectangular parallelepiped $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ by a plane passing through its vertex $D$ intersects the lateral edges $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$, and $C C_{1}$ at points $K, M$, and $N$, respectively. Find the ratio of the volume of the pyramid with vertex at point $P$ and base $D K M N$ to the volume of the given parallelepiped, if it is known that point $P$ divides the edge $D D_{1}$ in the ratio $D P: P D_{1}=m: n$.

If an A4 sheet is precisely cut in half along a straight line parallel to the shorter side, two sheets are created that have the same shape as the original, i.e., they are obtained from it through a rotation and a reduction in scale. This year, we will say that a rectangular sheet is "contemporary" if, by cutting it into 2020 equal parts obtained with cuts parallel to its shorter side, 2020 rectangles are obtained that have the same shape as the initial one. If the shorter side of a contemporary sheet measures 1, how long is the other side?
(A) $\sqrt{2020}$
(B) $2020^{3 / 2}$
(C) $\frac{2021}{2}$
(D) 2020
(E) None of the above

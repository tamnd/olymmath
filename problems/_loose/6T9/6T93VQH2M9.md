Let $ S$ be the set of odd integers greater than $ 1$ . For each $ x \in S$ , denote by $ \delta (x)$ the unique integer satisfying the inequality $ 2^{\delta (x)}<x<2^{\delta (x) +1}$ . For $ a,b \in S$ , define:

$ a \ast b=2^{\delta (a)-1} (b-3)+a.$

Prove that if $ a,b,c \in S$ , then:
$ (a)$ $ a \ast b \in S$ and
$ (b)$ $ (a \ast b)\ast c=a \ast (b \ast c)$ .

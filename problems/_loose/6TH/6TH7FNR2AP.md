In Rt $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C=90^{\circ}$, let $B C=a, C A=b, A B=c, p=\frac{1}{2}(a+b+c)$, $r, r_{A}, r_{B}, r_{c}$ represent the radii of the incircle and the excircles opposite to sides $a, b, c$ respectively. Prove: (1) $p(p-c)=(p-a)(p-b)$; (2) $r+r_{A}+r_{B}+r_{c}=2 p$.

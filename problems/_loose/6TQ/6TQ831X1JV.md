Let $a=-\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{7}, b=\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{7}, c=\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{7}$ . Evaluate $$\frac{a^4}{(a-b)(a-c)}+\frac{b^4}{(b-c)(b-a)}+\frac{c^4}{(c-a)(c-b)}.$$

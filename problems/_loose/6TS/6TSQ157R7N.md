Submit your answer as a positive integer $E$ . If the correct answer is $A$ , your score for this question will be $\max \left( 0, 25 \min \left( \frac{A}{E} , \frac{E}{A}\right)^{\frac32}\right)$ , rounded to the nearest integer.

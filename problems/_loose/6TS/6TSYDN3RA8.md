A math lottery is now being issued, where 10 numbers are filled in on 1 lottery ticket from the first 100 natural numbers. During the draw, 10 numbers are removed from $1,2, \cdots, 100$. If the 10 numbers on the lottery ticket are all among the remaining 90 numbers, then the ticket wins. Prove:
（1）If 13 lottery tickets are purchased, then by filling them out appropriately, it can be guaranteed that at least 1 ticket will win;
（2）If 12 lottery tickets are purchased, then no matter how they are filled out, it is possible that none of the tickets will win.

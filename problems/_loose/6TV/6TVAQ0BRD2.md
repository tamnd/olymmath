Let $\delta(x)$ be the greatest odd divisor of the positive integer $x$. For any positive integer $x$, prove that:
$$
\left|\sum_{n=1}^{x} \frac{\delta(n)}{n}-\frac{2}{3} x\right|<1 .
$$
(32nd Putnam Competition Problem)

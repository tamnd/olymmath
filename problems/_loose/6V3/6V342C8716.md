If $a$ and $b$ are positive integers, then $\overline\{a.b\}$ is a decimal number obtained by writing the number $a$, then the decimal point and then the number $b$. For example, if $a = 20$ and $b = 17$, then $\overline\{a.b\} = 20.17$ and $\overline\{b.a\} = 17.2$.
Determine all pairs $(a, b)$ of positive integers such that $\overline\{a.b\} \cdot \overline\{b.a\} = 13$.

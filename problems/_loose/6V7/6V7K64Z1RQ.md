On the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ of the equilateral $\triangle ABC$, there are internal division points $D$, $E$, $F$ dividing the sides in the ratio $3:(n-3)$ (where $n>6$). The segments $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ intersect to form $\triangle PQR$ (where $BE$ intersects $AD$ at $P$ and $FC$ at $Q$). When the area of $\triangle PQR$ is $\frac{4}{49}$ of the area of $\triangle ABC$, find the value of $n$.
(1992 Japan Mathematical Olympiad Preliminary)

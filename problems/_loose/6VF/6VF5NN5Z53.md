Consider the matrix $A=\left(\begin{array}{ll}1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1\end{array}\right)$ and the set:

$G=\left\{X_{a} \in M_{2}(\mathbf{R}) \mid X_{a}=I_{2}+a A, a>-\frac{1}{2}\right\}$.

a) Show that $G$ is a stable subset of $M_{2}(\mathbf{R})$ with respect to multiplication. Then, establish that $(G, \cdot)$ is an abelian group.

b) Prove that the function $f: G \rightarrow \mathbf{R}, f\left(X_{a}\right)=\ln (2 a+1)$ is an isomorphism between the groups $(G, \cdot)$ and $(\mathbf{R},+)$.

c) Calculate $X_{\frac{1}{2}} \cdot X_{\frac{3}{2}} \cdot \ldots \cdot X_{\frac{2 n-1}{2}}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$.

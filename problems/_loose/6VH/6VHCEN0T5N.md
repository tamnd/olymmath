Let the non-constant real polynomial $f(x)$ satisfy: for any $n, k \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, we have
$$
\frac{f(n+1) f(n+2) \cdots f(n+k)}{f(1) f(2) \cdots f(k)} \in \mathbf{Z} \text {. }
$$

Prove: $x \mid f(x)$.

Study the intersection points of the graphs of the functions $y=a^{x}$ and $y=\log _{a} x$ when $0<a<1$, and solve it to the best of your ability. This might be a problem that requires your persistent thinking to solve.
(You can use the theorem: The function $f(x)=x \ln x$ has the following properties: it is monotonically decreasing on the interval $\left(0, \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}}\right]$ and monotonically increasing on the interval $\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}}, 1\right)$. )

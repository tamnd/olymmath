Four coins, namely 2 euros, 1 euro, 2 cents, and 1 cent, are on a table without overlapping. The 2 euro coin touches the 1 euro coin at $A$, the 1 euro coin touches the 2 cent coin at $B$, the 2 cent coin touches the 1 cent coin at $C$, the 1 cent coin touches the 2 euro coin at $D$. Show that: $\widehat{A B C}+$ $\widehat{C D A}=180^{\circ}$.

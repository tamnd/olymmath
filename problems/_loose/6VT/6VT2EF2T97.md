Suppose several piles of balls are placed on a circle, with the number of balls in each pile being a multiple of 3, but the number of balls in each pile does not have to be equal. Now adjust the number of balls in each pile according to the following rules: divide the balls in each pile into three equal parts, leave one part in the original pile, and place the other two parts into the left and right piles, respectively. If the number of balls in a pile is not a multiple of 3, you can take one or two balls from a bag and add them to make the number of balls in that pile a multiple of 3, then continue adjusting according to the above method. Can the number of balls in each pile be made equal after a finite number of adjustments?

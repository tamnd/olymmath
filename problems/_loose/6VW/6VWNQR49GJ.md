Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of real numbers with the property that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(a_{2 n+2}+a_{2 n+1}-a_{2 n}\right)=0$.

a) If the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ is monotonic, prove that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n}=0$.

b) Does the conclusion from part a) remain true if the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ is not monotonic? Justify your answer!

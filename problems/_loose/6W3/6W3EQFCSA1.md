In the Metal Kingdom, there are three types of square tiles: gold, silver, and copper, with side length ratios of $2: 3: 5$. The quantities of the three types of tiles are the same. The king wants to use the tiles to cover the palace. If only gold tiles are used, there would be a shortage of 75 tiles. If only silver tiles are used, there would be 50 tiles left over. How many tiles would be left over if only copper tiles are used? (Tiles cannot be divided)

Points $X$ and $Y$ are chosen inside an acute triangle $ABC$ so that: $$ \angle AXB = \angle CYB = 180^\circ - \angle ABC, \text{ } \angle ABX = \angle CBY $$ Show that the points $X$ and $Y$ are equidistant from the center of the circumscribed circle of $\triangle ABC$ .

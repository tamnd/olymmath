Let $ m$ and $ n$ be two distinct positive integers. Prove that there exists a real number $ x$ such that $ \frac {1}{3}\le\{xn\}\le\frac {2}{3}$ and $ \frac {1}{3}\le\{xm\}\le\frac {2}{3}$. Here, for any real number $ y$, $ \{y\}$ denotes the fractional part of $ y$. For example $ \{3.1415\} = 0.1415$.

The table on the right was created from the one on the left by first swapping the first two rows, then the first two columns, and finally the last two columns. On the 4x4 cells where the same letters appear in the left table, the same letters also appear in the right table.

| $A$ | $B$ | $C$ | $D$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $D$ | $C$ | $B$ | $A$ |
| $C$ | $A$ | $C$ | $A$ |
| $B$ | $D$ | $B$ | $D$ |

| $C$ | $D$ | $A$ | $B$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $B$ | $A$ | $D$ | $C$ |
| $A$ | $C$ | $A$ | $C$ |
| $D$ | $B$ | $D$ | $B$ |

Determine all the tables that can be obtained from the left table by first changing the order of the rows and then the order of the columns, and which have the above property.

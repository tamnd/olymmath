In 1907, mathematician Wythoff invented a game for two players. There are two piles of objects with any number, and the two players take turns according to the following rules: (1) one can take any number of objects from one pile (taking all at once is allowed, but taking none is not); (2) one can take the same number of objects from both piles (the number is arbitrary, but no less than 1). Thus, who takes the last object wins.

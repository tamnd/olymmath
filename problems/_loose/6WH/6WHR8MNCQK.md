Call a positive integer $x$ $n$-*cube-invariant* if the last $n$ digits of $x$ are equal to the last $n$ digits of $x^3$. For example, $1$ is $n$-cube invariant for any integer $n$. How many $2015$-cube-invariant numbers $x$ are there such that $x < 10^{2015}$?

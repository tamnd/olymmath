Under which condition does the following equality sequence hold:

$$
\frac{a+\frac{a b c}{a-b c+b}}{b+\frac{a b c}{a-a c+b}}=\frac{a-\frac{a b}{a+2 b}}{b-\frac{a b}{2 a+b}}=\frac{\frac{2 a b}{a-b}+a}{\frac{2 a b}{a-b}-b}=\frac{a}{b}
$$

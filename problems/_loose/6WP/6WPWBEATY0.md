Let $k \in \mathbf{N}$, if there exists a function $f: N \rightarrow N$ that is strictly increasing, and for each $n \in \mathbf{N}$, $f[f(n)]=k n$, prove that for each $n \in \mathbf{N}$, $\frac{2 k n}{k+1} \leqslant f(n) \leqslant \frac{(k+1) n}{2}$.

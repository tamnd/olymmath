Given $H(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}1, x \geqslant 0 \\ 0, x<0\end{array}\right.$ is called the sign function.
(1) Sketch the graph of $y=H(x-2)+1$;
(2) Sketch the graph of $y=H\left(\operatorname{sgn}\left(x^{2}-x-2\right)\right)$;
(3) Prove: $\operatorname{sgn}(x)=H(x)-H(-x)$.

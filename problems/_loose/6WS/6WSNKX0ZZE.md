We wrote an integer in every cell of an infinitely large squared paper in all directions. We know that every number is equal to the arithmetic mean of the four numbers in the adjacent cells. Does it follow that all numbers are equal? (Two cells are adjacent if they share a side.)

Solve the problem also by replacing the arithmetic mean with the geometric mean in the statement.

At a round table, 100 people are sitting. Each of them is either a knight, a liar, or a fool. A knight always tells the truth, a liar always lies. A fool tells the truth if a liar is sitting to their left; lies if a knight is sitting to their left; and can say anything if a fool is sitting to their left. Each of them said: "A liar is sitting to my right." How many liars are at the table? List all possible answers and prove that there are no others. (V. Migrin)

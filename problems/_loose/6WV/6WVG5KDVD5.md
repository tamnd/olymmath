A two-digit number $n$ is divided by the sum of its digits, obtaining a remainder $\mathrm{r}$.

(a) Find a number $n$ such that $\mathrm{r}=0$.

(b) Show that $\mathrm{r}$ cannot be greater than 15.

(c) Show that for any $\mathrm{r}$ less than or equal to 12, there exists an $n$ that leaves a remainder $\mathrm{r}$ when divided by the sum of its digits.

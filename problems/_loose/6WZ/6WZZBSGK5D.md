On the surface of a cubic block, 100 different points are arbitrarily marked with chalk. Prove: It is possible to place the block on a blackboard in two different ways (and make the positions of the two placements completely coincide), so that the chalk marks left on the blackboard are different (if the chalk marks are on the edges or vertices of the cubic block, they also produce marks).

Let $m, n$ be arbitrary integers. Find a pair of integers $p, q$ such that for

$$
x=m^{3}+p m n^{2} \text { and } y=3 m^{2} n+q n^{3}
$$

the numbers $x^{2}-y^{2}$ are cubes, and then find a pair of $p, q$ such that $x^{2}-3 y^{2}$ are cubes.

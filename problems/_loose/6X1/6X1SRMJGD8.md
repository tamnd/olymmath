Prove that for all integers $n \geq 2$, we have: $\sum_{k=2}^{n} \frac{1}{\sqrt[k]{(2 k)!}} \geq \frac{n-1}{2 n+2}$.

N.B. If $a>0$, we denote $\sqrt[k]{a}$ the unique positive real number $b$ such that $b^{k}=a$.

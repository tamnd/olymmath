Let $(u_n)$ be a sequence of real numbers which satisfies $$ u_{n+2}=|u_{n+1}|-u_n \text{for all }n\in\mathbb N. $$ Prove that there exists a positive integer $p$ such that $u_n=u_{n+p}$ holds for all $n\in\mathbb N$ .

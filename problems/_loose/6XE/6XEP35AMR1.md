Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Now, four relations are given:
(1) $\sin A=\sin C$;
(2) $\sin A+\sin C=0$;
(3) $\cos B+\cos D=0$;
(4) $\cos B=\cos D$.

The number of relations that always hold is
(A) one.
(B) two.
(C) three.
(D) four.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1985)

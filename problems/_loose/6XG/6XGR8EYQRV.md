Indiana Jones reached an abandoned temple in the jungle and entered the treasury. There were 5 chests, and it is known that only one of them contains the treasure, while the others trigger a stone slab to fall on the head of anyone who tries to open them. The chests are numbered from left to right. The first, fourth, and fifth are made of cedar, while the second and third are made of sandalwood. On the first chest, it says "The treasure is in me or in the 4th chest"; on the second chest, it says "The treasure is in the chest to my left"; on the third chest, it says "The treasure is in me or in the far right chest"; on the fourth chest, it says "There is no treasure in the chests to my left"; on the fifth chest, it says "All other chests have false inscriptions." The last guardian of the temple, dying, revealed to Indiana the secret: the number of false statements on the cedar and sandalwood chests is the same. In which chest is the treasure?

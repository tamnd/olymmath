Let $\omega$ be a circle passing through $B$, externally tangent to $\mathcal{C}_{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{A}$, and intersecting $(AC)$ at two points $E$ and $F$. Show that $\widehat{A B E}=\widehat{F B C}$.

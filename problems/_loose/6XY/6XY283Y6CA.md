A very superstitious boy received a bicycle as a gift and wanted to learn how to ride it. When he heard about a bicycle malfunction that we usually refer to as "eight," he feared that it would bring bad luck if the six-digit number stamped on the frame contained one or even several digits 8. Before he looked at the bicycle number, he made the following consideration: When writing any number, 10 digits can be involved, namely $0,1, \ldots, 9$.

Among them, only the digit 8 appears as "unlucky." Therefore, there is only one case out of ten that can be called "unlucky."

Is our young cyclist right?

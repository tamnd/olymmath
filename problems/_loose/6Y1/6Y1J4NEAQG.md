10 Given an acute-angled triangle $\triangle A B C$ with altitudes $\overline{A U}, \overline{B V}, \overline{C W}$ and altitude intersection $H$. Let $X$ lie on $\overline{A U}$, Y$ on $\overline{B V}$ and $Z$ on $\overline{C W}$ . X, Y$ and $Z$ are all different from $H$. Show

(a) If $X, Y, Z$ and $H$ lie on a circle, then

$$
[A B C]=[A B Z]+[A Y C]+[X B C]
$$

where $[P Q R]$ denotes the area of the triangle $\triangle P Q R$.

(b) The reverse of (a) also applies.

It is known that the arithmetic average of the numbers *a*, *b* is equal to the number *c*, so $c = \frac{1}{2}(a+b)$, and that the harmonious average number of *a*, *c* is equal to the number *b*, so $b = \frac{2}{\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{c}}$. Is it necessary that numbers *a*, *b*, *c* are equal?

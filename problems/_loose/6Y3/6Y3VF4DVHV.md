Given a real number $a$, the *floor* of $a$, written $\lfloor a \rfloor$, is the greatest integer less than or equal to $a$. For how many real numbers $x$ such that $1 \le x \le 20$ is
$$
x^2 + \lfloor 2x \rfloor = \lfloor x^2 \rfloor + 2x ?
$$

Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles that intersect at two distinct points $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ and $D$ be two distinct points on $\Gamma_{1}$ and different from $A$ and $B$ such that $C^{\prime}$, the intersection of the line $(B C)$ with the circle $\Gamma_{2}$, and $D^{\prime}$, the intersection of the line $(A D)$ with $\Gamma_{2}$, are distinct from $A$ and $B$. Show that the lines $(C D)$ and $\left(C^{\prime} D^{\prime}\right)$ are parallel.

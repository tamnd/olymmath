On the board, the numbers are written
a) $1,2,3, \ldots, 1997,1998$

b) $1,2,3, \ldots, 1998,1999$

c) $1,2,3, \ldots, 1999,2000$.

It is allowed to erase any two numbers from the board and replace them with the difference of the larger and the smaller. Can one, by performing this operation many times, end up with a single number - 0? If yes, how can this be done?

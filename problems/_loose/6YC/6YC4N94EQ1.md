$\mathrm{P}(n): 5^{2 \mathrm{n}+1} 2^{\mathrm{n}+2}+3^{\mathrm{n}+2} 2^{2 \mathrm{n}+1}: 19 ; \mathrm{P}(0): 38 \vdots 19$, true.

For $n \geq 0$ we assume $\mathrm{P}(n)$ is true, so $5^{2 n+1} 2^{n+2}+3^{n+2} 2^{2 n+1}=19 k, k \in \mathbb{N}$ and.

$5^{2 n+1} 2^{n+2}=-3^{n+2} 2^{2 n+1}+19 k, k \in \mathbb{N}$

$5^{2 n+3} 2^{n+3}+3^{n+3} 2^{2 n+3}=50 \cdot 5^{2 n+1} 2^{n+2}+3^{n+3} 2^{2 n+3}=50\left(19 k-3^{n+2} \cdot 2^{2 n+1}\right)+3^{n+3} \cdot 2^{2 n+3}=19 \cdot 50 k-50 \cdot 3^{n+2} \cdot 2^{2 n+1}+3^{n+3} \cdot 2^{2 n+3}=19 \cdot 50 k-3^{n+2} \cdot 2^{2 n+1}(50-12) \vdots 19 \Rightarrow \mathrm{P}(n+1)$ true

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{P}(n)$ is true for any natural $n$

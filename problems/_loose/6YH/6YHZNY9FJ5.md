On a rectangular sheet of paper, there are

a) several points on one straight line;

b) three points.

It is allowed to fold the sheet several times along a straight line so that the marked points do not fall on the fold lines, and then pierce the folded sheet once with an awl. Prove that this can be done in such a way that the holes are exactly at the marked points and no extra holes are created.

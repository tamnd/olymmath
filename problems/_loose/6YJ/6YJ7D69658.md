A non-empty set of natural numbers is called small if it has fewer elements than its smallest element. Determine the number of all small sets $\mathrm{M}$ that are subsets of the set $\{1,2,3, \ldots, 100\}$ and have the following property: if two different numbers $x$ and $y$ belong to $\mathrm{M}$, then the number $|x-y|$ also belongs to $\mathrm{M}$.

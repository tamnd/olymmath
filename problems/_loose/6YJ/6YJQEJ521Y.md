Three, (50 points) Let $p=4 n+1\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$ be a prime number, and the set $\{1,3,5, \cdots, p-2\}$ consists of numbers that are quadratic residues modulo $p$ forming set $A$, and numbers that are not quadratic residues modulo $p$ forming set $B$. Find $\left(\sum_{a \in A} \cos \frac{a \pi}{p}\right)^{2}+\left(\sum_{b \in B} \cos \frac{b \pi}{p}\right)^{2}$.

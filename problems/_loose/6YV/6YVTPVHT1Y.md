A circle of radius $r_{1}$ touches the sides $D A, A B$ and $B C$ of a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, a circle of radius $r_{2}$ touches the sides $A B, B C$ and $C D$; similarly, $r_{3}$ and $r_{4}$ are defined. Prove that $\frac{A B}{r_{1}}+\frac{C D}{r_{3}}=\frac{B C}{r_{2}}+\frac{A D}{r_{4}}$.

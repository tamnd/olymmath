We will prove that in any acute isosceles triangle,

$$
A M=\frac{a}{\operatorname{tg} \alpha}
$$

where, in the usual notation, $a$ is the base, $A$ and $\alpha$ are the vertex opposite the base, and $M$ is the foot of the altitude.

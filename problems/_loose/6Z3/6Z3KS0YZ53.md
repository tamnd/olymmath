At the theater ticket office, $2n$ tickets are sold, each ticket costing 50 cents, and each person is limited to buying one ticket. Initially, the ticket office has no money, and among the first $2n$ people in the queue, half have 50-cent coins, and the other half only have one-dollar bills. To ensure the ticket office does not encounter any difficulty in giving change, how many different ways can these $2n$ ticket buyers be arranged in the queue?

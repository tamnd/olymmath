Given a triangle $ABC$ and points $D$ and $E$ on sides $AB$ and $AC$ such that $DE \parallel BC$. Let $P$ be an arbitrary point inside $\triangle ADE$. Lines $PB$ and $PC$ intersect $DE$ at points $F$ and $G$ respectively. Let the circumcircles of triangles $PDG$ and $PFE$ intersect again at point $Q$. Prove that points $A, P, Q$ lie on the same line.

Let $a$ , $b$ , $c$ , $d$ be complex numbers satisfying
\begin{align*}
5 &= a+b+c+d
125 &= (5-a)^4 + (5-b)^4 + (5-c)^4 + (5-d)^4
1205 &= (a+b)^4 + (b+c)^4 + (c+d)^4 + (d+a)^4 + (a+c)^4 + (b+d)^4
25 &= a^4+b^4+c^4+d^4
\end{align*}
Compute $abcd$ .

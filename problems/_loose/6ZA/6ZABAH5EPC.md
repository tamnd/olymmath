Show that a random variable $\xi$ and a $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{G}$ are independent, i.e., for any $A \in \mathscr{G}$, the variables $\xi$ and $I_{A}$ are independent if and only if $\mathrm{E}[g(\xi) \mid \mathscr{G}]=E g(\xi)$ for every Borel function $g=g(x)$ such that $\mathrm{E}|g(\xi)|<\infty$.

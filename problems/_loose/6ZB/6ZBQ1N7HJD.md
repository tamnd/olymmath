At the vertices of a regular 2017-gon, the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 2017$ are placed. Any axis of symmetry divides the numbers not lying on it into two sets. We call a placement good relative to a given axis of symmetry if each number in one of the sets is greater than its symmetric counterpart. Does there exist a placement that is good relative to every axis of symmetry?

(A. V. Begun: essentially MMO 1983.8.4)

Given a triangle $A B C ; A A_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ are its altitudes. Prove that the Euler lines of triangles $A B_{1} C_{1}, A_{1} B C_{1}$, $A_{1} B_{1} C$ intersect at a point $P$ on the nine-point circle, such that one of the segments $P A, P B, P C$ is equal to the sum of the other two segments (Victor Tebo).

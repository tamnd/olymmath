10 chips are placed on the table in a circle. The top side of the chips is red, and the bottom side is blue. Two operations are allowed:

a) flip four chips in a row;

b) flip four chips arranged as follows: ××0×× (× - a chip included in the quartet, 0 - not included). Is it possible, using the allowed operations several times, to flip all the chips to the blue side up?

There are colored pencils in three boxes: in one - only red, in another - only green, in the third - red and green. On each box, pencils are drawn: on one - red, on another - green, on the third - red and green. It is known that the color of the pencils in each box does not match the drawing on it. How, by taking only one pencil from one box, can you change the drawings on them to match the color of the pencils inside?

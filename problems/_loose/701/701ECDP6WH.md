Let's prove that

$$
\frac{1+a+a^{2}+\ldots+a^{n-1}+a^{n}}{a+a^{2}+\ldots+a^{n-1}} \geq \frac{n+1}{n-1}
$$

if $a>0$, and $n$ is a natural number greater than 1.

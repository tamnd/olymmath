Given the positive integer $n$. Let $X = \{1, 2,..., n\}$. For each nonempty subset $A$ of $X$, set $r(A) = max_A - min_A$, where $max_A, min_A$ are the greatest and smallest elements of $A$, respectively.

Find the mean value of $r(A)$ when $A$ runs on subsets of $X$.

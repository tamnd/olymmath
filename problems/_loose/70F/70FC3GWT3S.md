Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic and tangential quadrilateral (that is, a quadrilateral whose all sides are tangent to the same inscribed circle within the quadrilateral). We denote by $E, F, G$, and $H$ the points of tangency of its inscribed circle with the sides $[AB], [BC], [CA]$, and $[AD]$ respectively.

Prove that $(EG) \perp (HF)$.

A point $ P$ in the interior of triangle $ ABC$ satisfies

$$ \angle BPC - \angle BAC = \angle CPA - \angle CBA = \angle APB - \angle ACB.$$

Prove that $$ \bar{PA} \cdot \bar{BC} = \bar{PB} \cdot \bar{AC} = \bar{PC} \cdot \bar{AB}.$$

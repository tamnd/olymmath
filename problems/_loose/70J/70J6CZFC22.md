$\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$'s internal common tangent lines intersect at $A$, the external common tangent lines intersect at $B$, and any point $P$ on the circle with $A B$ as its diameter. Through $P$, tangents are drawn to $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$, with the points of tangency being $C$ and $D$, respectively, and the two tangents are on the same side of $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ (or on the same inner side). Prove: (the length of the tangent from $C$ to $\odot O_{2}$) = (the length of the tangent from $D$ to $\odot O_{1}$), i.e., $O C^{\prime}=D D^{\prime} .\left(C^{\prime} 、 D^{\prime}\right.$ are the points of tangency).

Prove that the following sets do not belong to $\mathscr{B}\left(\mathbb{R}^{[0,1]}\right)$:

$$
\left\{x: x_{t}=0 \text { for at least one } t \in[0,1]\right\} \text {, }
$$

$\left\{x\right.$ : the function $x_{t}$ is continuous at a fixed point $\left.t_{0} \in[0,1]\right\}$, where $x=\left(x_{t}\right)_{0 \leqslant t \leqslant 1} \in \mathbb{R}^{[0,1]}$.

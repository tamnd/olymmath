Let
$S=\left\{n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right.$ such that there exists $n^{2}+1 \leqslant d \leqslant n^{2}+2 n$ such that $\left.d \mid n^{4}\right\}$.

Prove: For $m \in \mathbf{Z}$, the set $S$ contains infinitely many elements of the form $7 m$, $7 m+1$, $7 m+2$, $7 m+5$, and $7 m+6$, and $S$ contains no elements of the form $7 m+3$ or $7 m+4$.

On the incircle $O$ of $\triangle A B C$, draw tangents parallel to each side of the triangle. These tangents cut off three smaller triangles $A E D, B G F, C I H$ (with $E, F$ on $A B$, $G, H$ on $B C$, and $I, D$ on $A C$) from $\triangle A B C$. The inradii of these smaller triangles are $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$, respectively, and the inradius of $\triangle A B C$ is $r$. Prove that $r=r_{1}+r_{2}+r_{3}$.

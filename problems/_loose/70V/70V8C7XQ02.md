Given on a plane are 3 consecutive points $A, O, B$. Let $A O=a, O B=b$ and $a>b$. Draw a circle of arbitrary radius $x$ around $O$ as the center $(x<b)$ and draw tangents to this circle from points $A$ and $B$. Let the intersection point of these two tangents be $M$. What is the geometric locus of point $M$ as $x$ varies from 0 to $b$?

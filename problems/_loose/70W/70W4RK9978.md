Let $C$ be a graph of the function $y=x(x-1)(x-3)$ and $l$ be a line passing through the origin $O$ with the slope $t$. Suppose $C$ and $l$ intersect other than $O$ . Denote by $O,\ P,\ Q$ the points of intersection, let $g(t)$ be the product of $|\overrightarrow {OP}|$ and $|\overrightarrow{OQ}|$. Note that : if one of the points of intersection is a point of tangency, then it is interpreted as : two points of the points of intersection coincide, which means the point of tangency. Examine of the variation of the function $g(t)$, then the local extrema.

Given $f(x)=\mathrm{e}^{x}-x^{a}, x>0, a \in \mathbf{R}$.
(1) If $f(x) \geqslant 0$ always holds, find the maximum value of $a$;
(2) If $x_{1}, x_{2}$ are two zeros of $f(x)$, and $x_{1}>x_{2}$, prove that $x_{1}+x_{2}>2 a$.

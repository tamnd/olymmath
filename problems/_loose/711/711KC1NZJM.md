Let's prove that among the numbers

$$
n+1, n+2, \ldots, n+k
$$

(where $n$ and $k$ are natural numbers), there is always one that is divisible by a higher power of 2 than any of the others. (See the article about József Kürschák by R. Obláth in our previous issue.)

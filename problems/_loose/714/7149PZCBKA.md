When solving a certain quadratic equation, student A made a mistake in the constant term of the equation, and thus obtained the two roots of the equation as 8 and 2. Student B made a mistake in the coefficient of the linear term, and thus obtained the two roots of the equation as -9 and -1. Then the correct equation is
(A) $x^{2}-10 x+9=0$.
(B) $x^{2}+10 x+9=0$.
(C) $x^{2}-10 x+16=0$.
(D) $x^{2}-8 x-9=0$.
(E) None of the above.
(4th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1953)

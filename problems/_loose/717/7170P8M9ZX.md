Prove that if there exist $n$ positive integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfying the $n$ equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
2 x_{1}-x_{2}=1, \\
-x_{k-1}+2 x_{k}-x_{k+1}=1, k=2,3, \cdots, n-1 \\
-x_{n-1}+2 x_{n}=1 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

then $n$ is even.
(Swedish Mathematical Competition, 1983)

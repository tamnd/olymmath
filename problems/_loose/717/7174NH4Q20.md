In space, two triangles $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ are given. Prove that the greatest distance between points of triangle $A B C$ and points of triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ will be the greatest of the nine segments $A A^{\prime}, A B^{\prime}, A C^{\prime}, B A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}, B C^{\prime}$, $C A^{\prime}, C B^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}$.

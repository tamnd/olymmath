Let $G_{n}$ denote the geometric mean of the binomial coefficients $\binom{n}{0},\binom{n}{1}, \ldots,\binom{n}{n}$. Prove that

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \sqrt[n]{G_{n}}=\sqrt{e}
$$

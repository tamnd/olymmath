Let $\{a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i\}$ be a permutation of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\}$ such that $\gcd(c,d)=\gcd(f,g)=1$ and
$$(10a+b)^{c/d}=e^{f/g}.$$
Given that $h>i$ , evaluate $10h+i$ .

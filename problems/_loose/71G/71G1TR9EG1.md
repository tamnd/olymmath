Given on the plane are two circles, $e$ and $f$, and a point $P$ outside them. From $P$, we draw a tangent to each of the two circles, and the points of tangency are $E$ and $F$. Prove that the ratio of the segments cut by the two circles on the line $EF$ does not depend on which two tangents we drew.

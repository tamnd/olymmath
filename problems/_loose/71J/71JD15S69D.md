Let $\lambda \neq 0, n, m$ be positive integers, and $m>n, m \neq 2 n$. Then the function $f(x)$ given by the equation
$$
f(x+\lambda)+f(x-\lambda)=2 \cos \frac{2 n \pi}{m} f(x)
$$

is a periodic function with period $m \lambda$.

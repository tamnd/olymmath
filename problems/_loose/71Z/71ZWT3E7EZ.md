In parallelogram $A B C D$, points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$ lie on sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$ respectively. On the sides $A_{1} B_{1}, B_{1} C_{1}, C_{1} D_{1}$, $D_{1} A_{1}$ of quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$, points $A_{2}, B_{2}$, $C_{2}, D_{2}$ are taken respectively. It is known that

$$
\frac{A A_{1}}{B A_{1}}=\frac{B B_{1}}{C B_{1}}=\frac{C C_{1}}{D C_{1}}=\frac{D D_{1}}{A D_{1}}=\frac{A_{1} D_{2}}{D_{1} D_{2}}=\frac{D_{1} C_{2}}{C_{1} C_{2}}=\frac{C_{1} B_{2}}{B_{1} B_{2}}=\frac{B_{1} A_{2}}{A_{1} A_{2}}
$$

Prove that $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2} D_{2}$ is a parallelogram with sides parallel to the sides of $A B C D$.

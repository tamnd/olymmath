On an $n \times n$ chessboard, $n$ grams of flour are scattered such that the total weight in each row and each column is 1 gram. Prove that it is possible to arrange $k$ people, with the $i$-th person taking $c_{i}$ grams of flour from $n$ different small squares $(i=1,2, \cdots, k)$, and in the end, all the flour originally on the chessboard is taken away.

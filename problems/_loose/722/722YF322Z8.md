a) Guess that any motion of space is a composition of no more than four symmetries with respect to planes.

b) Prove that any motion of space having a fixed point $ O $ is a composition of no more than three symmetries with respect to planes.

Definition. A motion that is a composition of an even number of symmetries with respect to planes is called a motion of the first kind or a motion that preserves the orientation of space. A motion that is a composition of an odd number of symmetries with respect to planes is called a motion of the second kind or a motion that changes the orientation of space.

We will not prove that a composition of an even number of symmetries with respect to planes cannot be represented as a composition of an odd number of symmetries with respect to planes.

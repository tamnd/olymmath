They agree: each time, the number of candies taken cannot exceed one-third of the number of candies in the box, and they cannot skip their turn; when one party cannot take any more candies, the other party is declared the winner. Assuming Eddie takes the first turn, then ( ).

B. Vee has a winning strategy, leaving 26, 16, 10, 7, 4, 2 candies after each of her turns

D. Eddie has a winning strategy, leaving 28, 16, 10, 7, 4, 2 candies after each of his turns

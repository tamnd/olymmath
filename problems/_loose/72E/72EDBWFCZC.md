Area of a Triangle. Prove without the use of trigonometry that if in a triangle $\angle A=60^{\circ}$, then the area $S$ of the triangle is determined by the formula

$$
S=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\left[a^{2}-(b-c)^{2}\right]
$$

and if $\angle A=120^{\circ}$, then

$$
S=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{12}\left[a^{2}-(b-c)^{2}\right]
$$

It is proposed to construct $N$ points on a plane such that all distances between them equal predetermined numbers: for any two points $M_{i}$ and $M_{j}$, where $i$ and $j$ are any numbers from 1 to $N$.

Can the construction be carried out if the distances $r_{ij}$ are given such that any 5 of the $N$ points can be constructed?

b) Is it sufficient to require that any 4 of the $N$ points can be constructed?

c) What changes if the points are constructed not on a plane but in space? What is the smallest $k$ then, for which the possibility of constructing any $k$ of the given $N$ points ensures the possibility of constructing all $N$ points?

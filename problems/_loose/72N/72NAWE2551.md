8.2 .5 ** Let convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ be inscribed in a circle, the extensions of $A D$ and $B C$ meet at $E$, and the extensions of $A B$ and $D C$ meet at $F$. Let the internal angle bisectors of $\angle E$ and $\angle F$ intersect at $R$, and the external angle bisectors of $\angle E$ and $\angle F$ intersect at $P$. Prove that $R E \perp R F, P E \perp P F$, and that the midpoints $M$ and $N$ of $A C$ and $B D$ respectively, along with $P$ and $R$, form a harmonic range.

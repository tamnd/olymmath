a.) Find $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty} f(x)$.

b.) Prove that $f(x)$ is an increasing function.

c.) Find the number of real roots of the equation $f(x)=a \sqrt{\frac{x-3}{x}}$, where $a$ is a real parameter.

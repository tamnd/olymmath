On each edge of the tetrahedron, a point is marked. Consider four tetrahedra, one of the vertices of each of which is a vertex of the original tetrahedron, and the other vertices are the marked points lying on the edges emanating from this vertex. Prove that the volume of one of them does not exceed one eighth of the volume of the original tetrahedron.

Inside an acute triangle $ABC$, whose interior angles have the sizes $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, there is a point $P$ such that $\overline{P A}=\overline{P B}=\overline{P C}$ holds. The sizes of the angles $\angle P A B, \angle P A B$ respectively $\angle P A C$ are denoted by $\delta, \epsilon$ respectively $\eta$.

a) Calculate $\delta, \epsilon$ and $\eta$ for the case where $\alpha=70^{\circ}$ and $\beta=80^{\circ}$!

b) Determine a formula for $\delta$ in terms of $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$, as well as a formula for $\epsilon$ and a formula for $\eta$!

(1) Find all positive integers $k$ such that the equation $a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2} = k a b c$ (1) has positive integer solutions $(a, b, c)$.
(2) Prove that for each $k \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, equation (1) has infinitely many positive integer solutions $\left(a_{n}, b_{n}, c_{n}\right)$, such that the product of any two of $a_{n}, b_{n}, c_{n}$ can be expressed as the sum of two positive integers squared.

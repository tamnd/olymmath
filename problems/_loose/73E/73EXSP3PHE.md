If four distinct positive integers $m, n, p$ and $q$ satisfy the equation

$$
(7-m)(7-n)(7-p)(7-q)=4
$$

then the sum $m+n+p+q$ is equal to:
(a) 10
(b) 21
(c) 24
(d) 26
(e) 28

For a semicircle with diameter $AB = 2r$ and center $O$, the tangent at point $M$ intersects the tangents at points $A$ and $B$ at points $A'$ and $B'$, respectively. The perpendicular projection of $M$ onto the diameter $AB$ is $P$. Let $x = \frac{AP}{PB} (>0)$.

Rotate the trapezoid $AA'BB'$ and the triangle $A'OB'$ around $AB$, and denote the volumes of the resulting solids of revolution by $V_1$ and $V_2$, respectively. Express these volumes as functions of $r$ and $x$. Determine $x$ such that $\frac{V_2}{V_1} = m$, where $m$ is a given positive number. Discussion.

What is the maximum value that $m$ can take, and what position of $M$ corresponds to this value?

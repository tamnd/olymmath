For Kelvin the Frog's birthday, Alex the Kat gives him one brick weighing $x$ pounds, two bricks weighing $y$ pounds, and three bricks weighing $z$ pounds, where $x,y,z$ are positive integers of Kelvin the Frog's choice.

Kelvin the Frog has a balance scale. By placing some combination of bricks on the scale (possibly on both sides), he wants to be able to balance any item of weight $1,2,\ldots,N$ pounds. What is the largest $N$ for which Kelvin the Frog can succeed?

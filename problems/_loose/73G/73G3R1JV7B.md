On the table are pan scales and $n$ weights of different masses. The weights are placed on the pans of the scales one by one (at each step, any weight is taken from the table and added to one of the pans of the scales).

a) Prove that the weights can be placed in such an order that the left pan is heavier first, then the right pan, then the left pan again, then the right pan again, and so on.

This sequence of weighing results can be associated with a word made of letters $L$ and $R: L R L R L R \ldots$ Here, the letter $L$ indicates that the left pan is heavier, and the letter $R$ indicates that the right pan is heavier.

b)* Prove that for any word of length $n$ made of letters $L$ and $R$, the weights can be placed on the pans of the scales in such an order that this word corresponds to the sequence of weighing results.

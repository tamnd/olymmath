Given the triangle $O A B$, the angle $A O B$ is less than $90^{\circ}$. From any point $M$ on the perimeter or inside the triangle $A O B$ (different from $O$), we draw perpendiculars to $\overline{O A}$ and $\overline{O B}$. The feet of these perpendiculars are denoted by $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of the triangle $O P Q$.

What is the geometric locus of the point $H$ if $M$ traverses

a) the side $A B$;

b) the interior of the triangle $O A B$?

Let $\lambda$ and $\mu$ be two $\sigma$-finite measures on $(\Omega, \mathscr{F})$ and $f=\frac{d \lambda}{d \mu}$. Show that if $\mu(f=0)=0$, then the density $\frac{d \mu}{d \lambda}$ exists and can be taken as the function

$$
\varphi= \begin{cases}1 / f & \text { on the set }\{f \neq 0\} \\ c & \text { on the set }\{f=0\}\end{cases}
$$

where $c-$ is an arbitrarily chosen constant.

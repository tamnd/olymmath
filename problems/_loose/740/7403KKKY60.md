Variant for 10th grade. On the side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, an arbitrary point $M_{1}$ is taken. From the center $A$ with radius $AM_{1}$, an arc $M_{1} M_{2}$ is drawn inside triangle $ABC$ to side $AC (M_{2} \in AC)$, then from the center $C$ with radius $CM_{2}$, an arc $M_{2} M_{3}$ is drawn inside $ABC$ to side $BC (M_{3} \in BC)$, from the center $B$ with radius $BM_{3}$, an arc $M_{3} M_{4}$ is drawn to side $AB$, and so on, until the line of arcs closes. Find the length of the resulting closed line, given the lengths of the sides of the triangle and the measures of its angles.

Variant for 11th grade. Prove the inequality $(A+B)^{n} \leqslant 2^{n-1} \times\left(A^{n}+B^{n}\right)$, where $A$ and $B$ are non-negative numbers.

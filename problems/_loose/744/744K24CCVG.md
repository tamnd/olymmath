A decimal natural number $k$ has $n$ digits. This number is rounded to the nearest ten, and then the resulting number is rounded to the nearest hundred, and so on, until after the $(n-1)$-th rounding, the number $\bar{k}$ is obtained. Prove that $\bar{k}<\frac{18}{13} k$.
(17th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1983)

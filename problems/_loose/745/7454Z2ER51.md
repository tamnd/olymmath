Given that the point $(a, b)$ moves on the curve $\arcsin x=\arccos y$, and the ellipse $a x^{2}+b y^{2}=1$ is outside the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$ (including the case where they are tangent). Then, the range of $\theta=\arccos a$ is ( ).
A. $\frac{\pi}{6} \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{4}$
B. $\frac{\pi}{6} \leqslant \theta<\frac{\pi}{2}$
C. $\frac{\pi}{6} \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{3}$
D. $\frac{\pi}{4} \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{3}$

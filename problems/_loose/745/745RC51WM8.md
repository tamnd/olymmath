Let $A, B, C, D$ be four distinct points arranged in order on a circle. The tangent to the circle at $A$ meets the ray $C B$ at $K$ and the tangent to the circle at $B$ meets the ray $D A$ at $H$. Suppose $B K=B C$ and $A H=A D$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a trapezium.

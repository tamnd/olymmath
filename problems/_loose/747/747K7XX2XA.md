Given that the incircle $\odot I$ of $\triangle ABC$ touches the sides $BC, CA, AB$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively, and $AI, BI, CI$ intersect the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle ABC$ at points $L, M, N$ respectively. $LD, ME, NF$ intersect $\odot O$ at points $P, Q, R$ respectively. Draw the line $l_{A}$ perpendicular to $PA$ through $P$, the line $l_{B}$ perpendicular to $QB$ through $Q$, and the line $l_{C}$ perpendicular to $RC$ through $R$. Prove that $l_{A}, l_{B}, l_{C}$ are concurrent.

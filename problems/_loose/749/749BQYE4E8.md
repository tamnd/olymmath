Let $\mathbb{Q}_{0}^{+}$ be the set of all non-negative rational numbers.

Determine all functions $f: \mathbb{Q}_{0}^{+} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}_{0}^{+}$ such that for all non-negative rational numbers $x, y$ the following holds:

$$
y f(x+y) + (y-1) f(x y) = f\left(y^{2}\right) f(x+1)
$$

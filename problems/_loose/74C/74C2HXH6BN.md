Find all positive integers $n$ such that there exist two permutations $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1}$ and $b_0,b_1,\ldots,b_{n-1}$ of the set $\lbrace0,1,\ldots,n-1\rbrace$ , satisfying the condition $$ ia_i\equiv b_i\pmod{n} $$ for all $0\le i\le n-1$ .

In a square with $4 \times 4$ fields, the numbers from 1 to 16 are to be entered such that each of the numbers appears exactly once and that the sum of the numbers in each of the four rows, the four columns, and the two diagonals is always the same value $s$ ("Magic Square").

a) Prove that in all magic squares (with the numbers from 1 to 16 in $4 \times 4$ fields), the same value for $s$ must occur!

b) Prove that in every magic square of $4 \times 4$ fields, the sum of the numbers in the four corner fields must also be $s$!

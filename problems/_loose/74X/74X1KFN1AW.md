Let $a, b, c$ and $d$ be the lengths of the sides $AB, BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ of a spatial quadrilateral $ABCD$.

a) Prove that if none of the three relations $a+b=c+d, a+c=b+d$ and $a+d=b+c$ are satisfied, then there exist exactly 8 distinct spheres that are tangent to the lines $AB, BC, CD$ and $DA$.

b) Prove that if at least one of the specified relations is satisfied, then there exist infinitely many distinct spheres that are tangent to the lines $AB, BC$, $CD$ and $DA$.

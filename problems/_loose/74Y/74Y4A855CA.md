Suppose there are $n$ gas stations on a circular road, each station storing a certain number of barrels of gasoline (some stations may store none), the total amount of gasoline stored at the $n$ stations is sufficient for a car to travel around the road once. Now, let a car that originally has no gasoline travel counterclockwise along the road, and at each station, it takes all the gasoline stored there (including the starting station). Try to prove that among the $n$ stations, there is at least one station from which the car can start and travel around the road without stopping due to a lack of gasoline.

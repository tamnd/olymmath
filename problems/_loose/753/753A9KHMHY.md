Given that in the right triangle $\triangle ABC$, the hypotenuse $BC=2\pi$. A circle with center $A$ and radius $r$ is tangent to $BC$. Let the area of the remaining part of the right triangle $\triangle ABC$ after being covered by circle $A$ be $S$. Then the maximum value of $S$ is
(A) $\pi^{2}$.
(B) $\pi$.
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2}$.
(D) $\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}$.
(Anhui Province, Anqing County Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

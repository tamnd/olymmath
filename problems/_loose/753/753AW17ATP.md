For a set $S \subset \mathbb Z$ , write $\pi (S)$ to be the product of all the elements of $S$ . Compute the sum
$$ \sum_{\emptyset \neq S \subseteq \{1,2,\ldots,2024\}} \frac{1}{\pi (S)}$$
where the sum is taken over all nonempty subsets of $\{1,2,\ldots,2024\}$ . (Note that for sets with one element the product will be the sole element). $\text{(a) }1 \text{(b) }2023 \text{(c) }2024 \text{(d) }2025$

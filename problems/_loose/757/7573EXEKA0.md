Let the strictly positive real numbers $x, y$ and $z$ be such that $2x + 3y + 4z = 6$. Show that:
a) $\sqrt{2x} + \sqrt{3y} + \sqrt{4z} < \frac{9}{2}$;
b) $\frac{2}{x} + \frac{3}{y} + \frac{4}{z} \geq \frac{27}{2}$.

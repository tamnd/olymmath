If $S_{p}, S_{n}, S_{k}$ are the sums of $p, n, k$ terms of an arithmetic progression, then prove that the following equality holds:

$$
\frac{S_{p}}{p}(n-k)+\frac{S_{n}}{n}(k-p)+\frac{S_{k}}{k}(p-n)=0
$$

Let $S_{0}=0, S_{n}=\xi_{1}+\ldots+\xi_{n}, n \leqslant N$, where $\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}$ are independent Bernoulli random variables, $\mathrm{P}\left\{\xi_{n}=1\right\}=p>0$, $\mathrm{P}\left\{\xi_{n}=0\right\}=q, n \leqslant N$. Denote $P_{n}(k)=\mathrm{P}\left\{S_{n}=k\right\}$. Show that for $n<N$ and $k \geqslant 1$ the following equality holds:

$$
P_{n+1}(k)=p P_{n}(k-1)+q P_{n}(k) .
$$

On an infinitely large grid paper, each small square is filled with a real number. Given two shapes $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$, each consisting of a finite number of small squares. These two shapes can be moved along the grid lines by an integer number of squares. It is known that for any position of shape $G_{1}$, the sum of the real numbers in all the squares it covers is positive. Prove that there exists a position of $G_{2}$ such that the sum of the real numbers in all the squares it covers is also positive.

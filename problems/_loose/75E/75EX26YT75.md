Question 1 Let $H$ be the orthocenter of acute $\triangle ABC$, $O^{\prime}$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle BHC$, $N$ be the midpoint of segment $AO^{\prime}$, and $D$ be the point symmetric to $N$ with respect to side $BC$. Prove: The necessary and sufficient condition for $A, B, D, C$ to be concyclic is $b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}=3 R^{2}$, where $a=BC, b=CA, c=AB, R$ is the circumradius of $\triangle ABC$. ${ }^{[1]}$
（Eighth Chen Shengshen Cup National High School Mathematics Olympiad)

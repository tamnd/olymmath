Given an angle and its bisector. An arbitrary circle passing through the vertex of the angle cuts off segments $a, b, l$ on the sides of the angle and its bisector, respectively. Prove that the ratio

$$
\frac{a+b}{l}
$$

does not depend on the position of the circle.

2) Given an angle and its external bisector. An arbitrary circle passing through the vertex of the angle cuts off segments $a, b, l$ on the sides of the angle and its bisector, respectively. Prove that the ratio

$$
\frac{|a-b|}{l}
$$

does not depend on the position of the circle.

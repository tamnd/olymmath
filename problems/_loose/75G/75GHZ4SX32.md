Prove that if planes $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ intersect, then the central projection of $\alpha_{1}$ onto $\alpha_{2}$ with center $O$ defines a one-to-one mapping of the plane $\alpha_{1}$ with the removed line $l_{1}$ onto the plane $\alpha_{2}$ with the removed line $l_{2}$, where $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ are the lines of intersection of the planes $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$, respectively, with the planes passing through $O$ and parallel to $\alpha_{2}$ and $\alpha_{1}$. In this case, the mapping is not defined on $l_{1}$.

Definition. The line on which the central projection is not defined is called the exceptional line of the given projection.

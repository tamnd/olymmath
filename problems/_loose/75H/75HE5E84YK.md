Three. (50 points) Answer the following two questions and provide examples or proofs: (1) For any positive integer $n$, does there exist $n$ points in the plane that are not all on the same line, such that the distance between any two points is a positive integer; (2) Does there exist an infinite set of points $M$ in the plane, all distinct, such that no three points in $M$ are collinear, and the distance between any two points in $M$ is a positive integer?

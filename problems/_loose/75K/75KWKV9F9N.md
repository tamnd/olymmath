In a square $A B C D$ with side length 2, $E$ is the midpoint of $A B$. Now fold along $E C, E D$ so that $A E$ and $B E$ coincide, and points $A$ and $B$ coincide, named as point $O$. Then the volume of the tetrahedron $O-C D E$ is
(A) $\sqrt{3}$.
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$.

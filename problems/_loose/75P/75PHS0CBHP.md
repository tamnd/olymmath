3$\cdot$34 A person is sailing a sailboat downstream along a river and encounters a speedboat coming upstream. He asks the speedboat driver: "Is there a ship coming behind you?" The speedboat driver replies: "I passed a ship half an hour ago." The sailboat continues to sail for another half hour and meets a ship coming upstream. It is known that the ship's speed in still water is twice the sailboat's speed in still water. Therefore, the speedboat's speed in still water is
(A) 4 times.
(B) 5 times.
(C) 6 times.
(D) Cannot be determined due to the unknown current speed.
("Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

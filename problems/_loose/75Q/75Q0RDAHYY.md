Let the sequence $\left\{F_{n}\right\}$ satisfy the recursive relation $F_{1}=F_{2}=4, F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_{n}, n=1,2, \cdots$ Prove: For any natural number $n$, a right-angled triangle can be formed with side lengths $F_{n} \cdot F_{n+4}, F_{n+1} \cdot F_{n+3}, 2 F_{n+2}$.

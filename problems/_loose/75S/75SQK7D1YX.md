Let the quadratic function $f(x)=a x^{2}+b x+c(a>0)$, and the two roots $x_{1}, x_{2}$ of the equation $f(x)-x=0$ satisfy $0<x_{1}<x_{2}<\frac{1}{a}$
(1) When $x \in\left(0, x_{1}\right)$, prove that $x<f(x)<x_{1}$
(2) Suppose the graph of the function $f(x)$ is symmetric about the line $x=x_{0}$, prove that $x_{0}<\frac{x_{1}}{2}$

Let $a$ be an integer, $k$ a positive integer, $p$ a prime number, and $p > k+1$. Prove that if

$$
a^{k}+a^{k-1}+\ldots+a+1
$$

is divisible by $p$, then $a^{k p}+a^{(k-1) p}+\ldots+a^{p}+1$ is divisible by $p^{2}$.

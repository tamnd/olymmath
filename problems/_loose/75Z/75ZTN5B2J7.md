What? Where? When? In the game "What? Where? When?", 13 envelopes with questions from the audience are played. The envelopes are chosen in a random order using a spinner. If the Experts answer correctly, they earn a point; if incorrectly, one point goes to the audience. The game ends as soon as one of the teams scores 6 points.

Assume that the teams of Experts and Audience are equally matched.
a) (8th - 11th grades, 2 points) Find the expected value of the number of points scored by the Experts team in 100 games.

b) (8th - 11th grades, 1 point) Find the probability that in the next game, envelope number 5 will be played.

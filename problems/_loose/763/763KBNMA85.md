a) A positive integer is called *nice* if it can be represented as an arithmetic mean of some (not necessarily distinct) positive integers each being a nonnegative power of $2$ .
Prove that all positive integers are nice.
b) A positive integer is called *ugly* if it can not be represented as an arithmetic mean of some pairwise distinct positive integers each being a nonnegative power of $2$ .
Prove that there exist infinitely many ugly positive integers.

(A. Romanenko, D. Zmeikov)

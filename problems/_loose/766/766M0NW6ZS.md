At a certain moment, Karel noticed on his precisely running watch that the end of the minute hand, the end of the hour hand, and a suitable point on the circumference of the dial form the vertices of an equilateral triangle. Before this phenomenon occurred again, a time $t$ had elapsed. Find the greatest possible $t$ in terms of the ratio $k$ of the lengths of the two hands $(k>1)$, given that the radius of the dial's circumference is equal to the length of the minute hand.

(Jaromír Šimša)

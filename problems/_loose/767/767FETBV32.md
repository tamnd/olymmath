Place a white knight on the square a1 of a chessboard. Two players, A and B, take turns to apply glue to the squares of the chessboard, one square at a time. After applying glue, they must ensure that the knight can jump to any unglued square (the knight moves according to the usual rules of chess) without getting stuck. A starts first. The player who cannot continue according to the rules loses. Who will win under the correct strategy?

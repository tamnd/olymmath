Let $\ast$ be the symbol denoting the binary operation on the set $S$ of all non-zero real numbers as follows: For any two numbers $a$ and $b$ of $S$ , $a\ast b=2ab$ . Then the one of the following statements which is not true, is $\textbf{(A) }\ast\text{ is commutative over }S \textbf{(B) }\ast\text{ is associative over }S $ $\textbf{(C) }\frac{1}{2}\text{ is an identity element for }\ast\text{ in }S \textbf{(D) }\text{Every element of }S\text{ has an inverse for }\ast $ $\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{1}{2a}\text{ is an inverse for }\ast\text{ of the element }a\text{ of }S$

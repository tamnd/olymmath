a) Consider the quadratic functions $f_1,f_2: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R},$ $f_1(x)=a_1x^2+b_1x+c_1$ and $f_2(x)=a_2x^2+b_2x+c_2,$ with $a_1a_2<0.$ Prove that if the graphs of the two functions do not intersect, then there is a line that separates them.

b) If $f_1(x)=x^2+ \frac{25}{4}$ and $f_2(x)=-x^2+x,$ find all the lines with the aforementioned property.

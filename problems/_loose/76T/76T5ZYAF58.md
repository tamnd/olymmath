Let the n-th degree complex coefficient polynomial $P(z)$ have all its roots on the unit circle in the complex plane. Prove: For each real number $c \geq 0$, the polynomial $2 z(z-1) P^{\prime}(z)+[(c-n) z+(c+n)] P(z)$ has all its roots on the unit circle.

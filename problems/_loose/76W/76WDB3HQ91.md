Given the function $f(x)=\frac{16 x+7}{4 x+4}$, the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $a_{1}>0, b_{1}>0, a_{n}=f\left(a_{n-1}\right), b_{n}=f\left(b_{n-1}\right), n=$ $2,3, \cdots$.
(1) Discuss the monotonicity of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Prove: $\left|b_{n}-a_{n}\right| \leqslant \frac{1}{8^{n-3}}\left|b_{1}-a_{1}\right|, n=1,2,3, \cdots$.

In a cycling race on a circular track of $1 \mathrm{~km}$ in length, at a certain point in time, cyclist $A$ had exactly $500 \mathrm{~m}$ lead over cyclist $B$. $B$ was riding at a speed of $50 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$, and $A$ at a speed of $45 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$.

a) After how many minutes from the given time would $B$ catch up to cyclist $A$ for the first time, assuming both rode at a constant speed?

b) After how many more minutes would $B$ catch up to cyclist $A$ for the second time (i.e., "overtake" him), if both cyclists continued to ride at a constant speed? How many laps would $A$ and $B$ have completed between the first and second overtaking?

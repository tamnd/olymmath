Let $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be two circles that touch each other externally at point $P$. Let a third circle $k$ touch $k_{1}$ in $B$ and $k_{2}$ in $C$ so that $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ lie inside $k$. Let $A$ be one of the intersection points of $k$ with the common tangent of $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ through $P$. The lines $A B$ and $A C$ intersect $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ again in $R$ and $S$ respectively. Show that $R S$ is a common tangent of $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$.

Given are two concentric circles $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$ with radii $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$, and a regular $n$-gon whose circumcircle is $K_{1}$. It is to be proven that the sum of the squares of the distances from any point $P$ on $K_{2}$ to the vertices of the $n$-gon is independent of the specific position of the point $P$.

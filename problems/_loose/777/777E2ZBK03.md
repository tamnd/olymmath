Two disks of different diameters are each divided into $2 n$ equal sectors, and each sector is painted one of two colors, black or white, such that each disk has exactly $n$ white sectors and $n$ black sectors. The disks are aligned with their centers coinciding, and an axis passes through the center. At this point, the boundary (circumference) of the smaller disk has two colors on either side: the inside is the color of the smaller disk, and the outside is the color of the larger disk. Thus, the entire circumference is divided into two parts: one part of the circumference has different colors on the inside and outside, and the other part has the same color on both sides. Prove that it is possible to rotate the smaller disk so that the total length of the first part of the circumference is at least half the circumference.

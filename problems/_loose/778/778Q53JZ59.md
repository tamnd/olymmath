Prove that for all non-negative real numbers $a, b$ and $c$ the inequality holds

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a \sqrt{(a+b)(a+c)}+b \sqrt{(b+c)(b+a)}+c \sqrt{(c+a)(c+b)} \geqslant \\
& \frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+3 a b+3 b c+3 c a}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

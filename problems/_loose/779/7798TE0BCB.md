Given a triangle $ABC$ and a point $P$ inside it. Let the line $AP$ intersect $BC$ at $A_1$, $BP$ intersect $AC$ at $B_1$, and $CP$ intersect $AB$ at $C_1$. Let the midpoints of segments $AP$, $BP$, $CP$, $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ be $A_2$, $B_2$, $C_2$, $C_3$, $A_3$, and $B_3$ respectively. It is to be proven that the points $A_1$, $A_2$, $A_3$, $B_1$, $B_2$, $B_3$, $C_1$, $C_2$, and $C_3$ lie on an ellipse.

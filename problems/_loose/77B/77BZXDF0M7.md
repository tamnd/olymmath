We draw a line through the intersection point $M$ of the diagonals of a convex quadrilateral, which bisects the segment of the line that lies inside the quadrilateral. Prove that if the line intersects one of the two other sides of the quadrilateral, then it also intersects the other, and these two intersection points are symmetric with respect to $M$.

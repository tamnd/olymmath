At a birthday party, four toys are drawn by lot among the three boys present. The draws are independent, i.e., all the boys participate in all the draws. What is the probability $p$ that at least one boy ends up without a toy?
(A) $p=\frac{4}{9}$
(B) $\frac{4}{9}<p<\frac{1}{2}$
(C) $p=\frac{1}{2}$
(D) $\frac{1}{2}<p<\frac{5}{9}$
(E) $p=\frac{5}{9}$

In an indoor swimming pool, there is also a paddling pool for children. It can be filled up to a marked height by a hot water pipe and a cold water pipe. If only the hot water pipe were used, it would take $12 \frac{1}{2}$ minutes for the water level to reach this height. With only the cold water pipe, it would take 10 minutes.

To achieve a specified water temperature, both pipes were initially used for $2 \frac{1}{2}$ minutes; then the hot water pipe was closed.

Calculate the time that was still needed afterward to fill the rest of the pool to the marked height using only the cold water pipe!

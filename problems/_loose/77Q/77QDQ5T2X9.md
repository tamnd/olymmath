Given a circle $S$, points $A$ and $B$ on it, and a point $C$ on the chord $AB$. For each circle $S'$ that is tangent to the chord $AB$ at point $C$ and intersects the circle $S$ at points $P$ and $Q$, consider the point $M$ of intersection of the lines $AB$ and $PQ$. Prove that the position of point $M$ does not depend on the choice of the circle $S'$.

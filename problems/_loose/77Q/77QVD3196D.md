Let $k$ be a semicircle with diameter $P Q$. Consider a chord $B C$ of fixed length $d$ whose endpoints are distinct from $P, Q$. A ray of light emanating from $B$ reaches point $C$ after reflecting from $P Q$ at such a point $A$ that $\angle P A B=\angle Q A C$. Prove that $\angle B A C$ doesn't depend on the position of the chord $B C$ on $k$.

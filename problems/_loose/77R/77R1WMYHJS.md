A deck of 36 cards is arranged so that the suit repeats every four cards. Several cards from the top were removed, not flipped, and inserted in some arbitrary order (not necessarily consecutively) among the remaining cards. After this, the deck was divided into 9 stacks of 4 consecutive cards each. Prove that each of these stacks will contain one card of each suit.

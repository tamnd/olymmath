For example, 22016 players participate in a chess tournament. After the tournament, it is found that if player $A$ and player $B$ draw, then all other players either lose to player $A$ or player $B$. Prove: if there are at least two draws, then all players can be arranged in a line such that each player defeats the player behind them.

Let the record of the number $N$ in the decimal system of notation have the form $\bar{a}_{n}^{-} \bar{a}_{n-1} \ldots \bar{a}_{1}^{-} \bar{a}_{0}^{-}, r_{i}-$ the remainder from the division of the number $10^{i}$ by $m(i=0, \ldots, n)$.

Prove that the number $N$ is divisible by $m$ if and only if the number $M=a_{n} r_{n}+a_{n-1} r_{n-1}+\ldots+a_{1} r_{1}+a_{0}$ is divisible by $m$.

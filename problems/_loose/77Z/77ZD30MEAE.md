A traveler wishes to visit five cities $A, B, C, D$ and $E$ in sequence. All segments $A B, B C, C D$ and $D E$ have different lengths and each is served by four different types of aircraft $K, L, M$ and $N$ with varying speeds. Prove that if the traveler wants to fly on each of these aircraft and spend the least amount of time on the journey, he should use a faster aircraft the longer the segment of the route.

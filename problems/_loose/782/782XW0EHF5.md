4- 226 Several residential areas are each connected to the city center by roads, but there are no roads between the residential areas. A truck loaded with all the goods that need to be delivered to each residential area is to start from the city center and deliver to each point. The cost of transportation is calculated as the weight of the goods on the truck multiplied by the distance traveled. Assume that the weight of the goods required by each residential area is exactly equal to its distance from the city center. Prove that regardless of the order in which the truck delivers the goods to each residential area, the total transportation cost is the same.

For positive integer $n \geq 3, a_{i} \in[2,3], i=1, 2, \ldots, n$, let $S=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}$, prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}^{2}+a_{i+1}^{2}-a_{i+2}^{2}}{a_{i}+a_{i+1}-a_{i+2}} \leq 2 S-2 n$, here $a_{n+1}=a_{1}, a_{n+2}=a_{2}$.

A magician and an assistant are going to perform the following trick. They have $n \geqslant 3$ cards numbered $1,2, \ldots, n$, and a row of $n$ cells the size of a card. The back sides of all cards are indistinguishable. The audience places cards 1 and 2 on any two spots; the assistant, seeing this, places the remaining cards on the free spots. Then all cards are flipped numbers down, and the magician enters. He flips one of the cards, and then the audience flips another. After this, the magician must correctly identify card 1 and correctly identify card 2. For which $n$ can the magician and the assistant agree in advance so that the trick is guaranteed to succeed

(I. Bogdanov, K. Knop)

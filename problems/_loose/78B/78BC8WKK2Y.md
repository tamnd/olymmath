14.1.90 ** Let $p>3$ be a prime number, and let
$$
1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{p-1}+\frac{1}{p}=\frac{r}{p \cdot s},(r, s)=1,
$$

then $p^{3} \mid r-s$.

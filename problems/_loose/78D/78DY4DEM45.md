For a square $A B C D$ and four points $P, Q, R, S$, the following is assumed:

(1) $P$ lies on the segment $A B$ between $A$ and $B$,

(2) $Q$ lies on the segment $B C$ between $B$ and $C$,

(3) $R$ lies on the segment $C D$ between $C$ and $D$,

(4) $S$ lies on the segment $D A$ between $D$ and $A$,

(5) it holds that $P R \perp Q S$.

Investigate whether for every position of the points for which conditions (1) to (5) are satisfied, one of the three statements $P RQ S$ always holds!

If this is the case, name this statement!

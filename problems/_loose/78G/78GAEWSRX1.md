Let a, b, c, and d be unit vectors directed from the center of a regular tetrahedron to its vertices, and u be an arbitrary vector. Prove that $(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{u}) \mathbf{a}+(\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{u}) \mathbf{b}+(\mathbf{c}, \mathbf{u}) \mathbf{c}+(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{u}) \mathbf{d}=4 \mathbf{u} / 3$.

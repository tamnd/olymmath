We inscribed a square in a semicircle such that two vertices of the square are on the diameter of the semicircle, and the other two vertices are on the semicircular arc. In the same semicircle, we inscribed a right triangle with the same area as the square, such that the hypotenuse of the triangle is the diameter of the semicircle. Prove that the center of the inscribed circle of the triangle lies on one side of the square.

Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle. Let $D$ and $E$ be the midpoints of sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{A C}$ respectively. Let $F$ be the point such that $D$ is the midpoint of $\overline{E F}$. Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of triangle $F D B$. Let $G$ be a point on the segment $\overline{C D}$ such that the midpoint of $\overline{B G}$ lies on $\Gamma$. Let $H$ be the second intersection of $\Gamma$ and $F C$. Show that the quadrilateral $B H G C$ is cyclic.

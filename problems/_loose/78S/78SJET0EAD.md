Given two conditions:
A: Two triangles have the same area and two corresponding edge equal.
B: Two triangles are congruent.
Then, which one of the followings are true? $(\text{A})$ A is sufficient and necessary condition of B. $(\text{B})$ A is necessary but insufficient condition of B. $(\text{C})$ A is sufficient but unnecessary condition of B. $(\text{D})$ A is insufficient and unnecessary condition of B.

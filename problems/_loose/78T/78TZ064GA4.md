Assume $d$ is the greatest common divisor of the positive integers $a$ and $b$, and $d^{\prime}$ is the greatest common divisor of the positive integers $a^{\prime}$ and $b^{\prime}$. Prove that the greatest common divisor of the numbers $a a^{\prime}, a b^{\prime}, b a^{\prime}, b b^{\prime}$ is equal to $d d^{\prime}$.
(Hungarian Mathematical Olympiad, 1913)

Starting from an invertible matrix $A \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})$ with rows $L_{1}, L_{2}, \cdots, L_{n}$, we construct the matrices $B \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})$ with rows $O, L_{2}, \cdots, L_{n}$ and $C \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})$ with rows $L_{2}, \cdots, L_{n}, O$, where $O$ denotes a row with all elements zero. Let the matrices $D=A^{-1} \cdot B$ and $E=A^{-1} \cdot C$. Show that:
a) $\operatorname{rank}(D)=\operatorname{rank}\left(D^{2}\right)=\cdots=\operatorname{rank}\left(D^{n}\right)$;
b) $\operatorname{rank}(E)>\operatorname{rank}\left(E^{2}\right)>\cdots>\operatorname{rank}\left(E^{n}\right)$.

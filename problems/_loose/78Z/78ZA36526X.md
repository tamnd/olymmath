A square table is divided into $16 \times 16$ cells. A knight moves on it in two directions: to the right or down, alternating jumps of two and three cells (that is, no two consecutive jumps are of the same length). It starts with a jump of length two from the top-left cell. In how many different ways can the knight reach the bottom-right cell? (A path is understood as a sequence of cells the knight jumps to.)

Consider the points $A, B, C, D$, non-coplanar, such that

$(A C) \equiv(A D) \equiv(B C) \equiv(B D)$ and points $E$ and $F$ are the midpoints of $(A B)$ and $(C D)$, respectively.

a) Calculate the measure of the angle between the lines $A B$ and $CD$.

b) If $A A^{\prime} \perp(B C D)$, prove that the points $B, A^{\prime}$, and $F$ are collinear.

c) If $\mathrm{G}$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to the bisector of angle $" A C D$, show that $E G \|(B C D)$.

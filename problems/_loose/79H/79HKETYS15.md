(2006 Turkey National Team Selection Test) Given positive numbers $x, y, z$ satisfying $x y+y z+z x=1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{27}{4}(x+y)(y+z)(z+x) \geqslant(\sqrt{x+y}+\sqrt{y+z}+\sqrt{z+x})^{2} \geqslant 6 \sqrt{3} .
$$

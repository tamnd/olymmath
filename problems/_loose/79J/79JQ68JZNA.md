In the triangular pyramid $S A B C$, it is known that $|A C|=|A B|$, and the edge $S A$ is inclined to the planes of the faces $A B C$ and $S B C$ at an angle of $45^{\circ}$. It is also known that the vertex $A$ and the midpoints of all edges of the pyramid, except $S A$, lie on a sphere of radius 1. Prove that the center of the sphere is located on the edge $S A$, and find the area of the face $A S C$.

The trapezoid $ABCD$ ( $AD\parallel BC$ ) is inscribed in the circle $\omega$ . The circle $\omega_1$ inscribed in the triangle $ABC$ touches the segment $BC$ at point $P$ , and the segment $AD$ of the circle $\omega_2$ inscribed in the triangle $ABD$ is at point $Q$ . Let $X$ and Y be the midpoints of the arcs BC, not containing point $A$ , and $AD$ , not containing point $B$ , respectively. Prove that the point of intersection of the lines $XP$ and $Y Q$ lies in $\omega$ .

Let the set $A=\{1,2, \cdots, 10\},$ and the mapping $f$ from $A$ to $A$ satisfies the following two conditions:
(1) For any $x \in A, f_{30}(x)=x$.
(2) For each $k \in \mathbf{N}_{+}, 1 \leqslant k \leqslant 29$, there exists at least one $a \in A$ such that $f_{k}(a) \neq a$.

Find the number of such mappings $f$ (it is agreed that $f_{1}(x)=f(x), f_{k+1}(x)=f\left(f_{k}(x)\right), k=1,2, \cdots$).

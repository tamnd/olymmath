Determine five natural numbers $a, b, c, d, e$ (greater than 0), such that

$$
a+b+c+d+e=100, \operatorname{LCM}(a, b, c, d, e)=1992
$$

Without considering solutions that are obtained from another solution by permuting the numbers.

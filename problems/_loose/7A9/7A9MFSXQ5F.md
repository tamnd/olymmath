Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle at $B$. Let $D$ be the point of intersection of the bisector of angle $\widehat{B A C}$ with the segment $[B C]$. Let $E$ be the point on the segment $[A C]$, distinct from $C$, such that $D E = D C$. Let $F$ be the point of intersection of the bisector of angle $\widehat{A E D}$ with the side $[A B]$. Show that $\widehat{A F E} = \widehat{E F D}$.

From a square sheet of grid paper $100 \times 100$, 1950 two-cell rectangles were cut out along the grid lines. Prove that from the remaining part, a four-cell figure of the form $\square$ - possibly rotated - can be cut out along the grid lines. (If such a figure is already among the remaining parts, it is considered to have been cut out.)

(S. Berlov)

Let $n \geqslant 2$, real number $k \geqslant 1, a_{i} \geqslant 0, i=1,2, \cdots, n, \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=n$. Prove $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{\left(a_{i}+1\right)^{2 k}}{\left(a_{i+1}+1\right)^{k}} \geqslant n \cdot 2^{k}\left(a_{n+1}=a_{1}\right)$, and determine the condition for equality.

Imagine the numbers $1,2,3,4, \ldots$ and so on up to 100 written consecutively, forming a number $z$ of the form

$$
z=12345678910111213 \ldots 9899100
$$

a) How many digits does $z$ have?

b) 100 digits of the number $z$ should be deleted so that the number $z^{\prime}$ represented by the remaining digits is as large as possible. The order of the remaining digits in $z^{\prime}$ should not be changed.

Determine which digits to delete, and give the first ten digits of the new number $z^{\prime}$!

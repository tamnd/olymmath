Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, \ldots, z_{n}$ be complex numbers. Prove that there exist natural numbers $i_{1}, i_{2}, \ldots, i_{k}$ such that $1 \leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\ldots<i_{k} \leq n$ and

$$
\left|z_{i_{1}}+z_{i_{2}}+\ldots+z_{i_{k}}\right| \geq \frac{1}{4 \sqrt{2}}\left(\left|z_{1}\right|+\left|z_{2}\right|+\ldots+\left|z_{n}\right|\right)
$$

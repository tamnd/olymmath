Natural numbers $p$ and $q$ are coprime. The segment $[0 ; 1]$ is divided into $p+q$ equal segments. Prove that in each of these segments, except for the two extreme ones, there lies exactly one of the $p+q-2$ numbers

$$
\frac{1}{p}, \frac{2}{p}, \ldots, \frac{p-1}{p}, \frac{1}{q}, \frac{2}{q}, \ldots, \frac{q-1}{q}
$$

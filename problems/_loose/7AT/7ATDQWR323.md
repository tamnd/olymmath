On segments $A B$ and $B C$ (point $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$) of the same straight line, equilateral triangles $A M B$ and $B N C$ are constructed on the same side of the line. Prove that the midpoints of segments $M C$, $A N$, and point $B$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle. Determine the validity of the theorem for the case when point $C$ lies between points $A$ and $B$, and the constructed equilateral triangles are located on opposite sides of the line.

Let $n$ be an odd natural number and consider the vertices and midpoints of the sides of a regular $n$-gon. We want to assign the integers from $1$ to $2n$ to these $2n$ points such that the $3$ numbers on each side of the $n$-gon add up to the same sum. Show that there exists a method that provides such an assignment for any $n$ in one go.

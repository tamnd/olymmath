It is known that the sum $S=s_{a}+s_{b}+s_{c}$ of the medians of a triangle is less than the perimeter of the triangle but greater than three-quarters of the perimeter:

$$
3(a+b+c) / 4 < s_{a} + s_{b} + s_{c} < a + b + c
$$

By what fraction does $S$ remain below the stated upper limit in the isosceles triangle where the angle at the vertex is $8^{\circ}$?

- By what fraction does $S$ exceed the stated lower limit in the isosceles triangle where the angles at the base are $8^{\circ}$?
- Find an isosceles triangle shape in which $S$ deviates from the stated upper and lower limits by at most $1 / 30$ of the limit.

On a straight line, there are $k$ points. For any two points $A, B$, a circle is drawn with $A B$ as its diameter, and each circle is painted one of $n$ colors. If any two mutually externally tangent circles are painted different colors, and the given $k$ points are not painted, prove: $k \leqslant 2^{n}$.

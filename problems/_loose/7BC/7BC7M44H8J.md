$7 m(m \geqslant 3)$ people $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{m}$ pass the ball to each other. Starting with $A_{1}$, this is called the 1st pass. After $n(n \geqslant 2)$ passes, the ball returns to $A_{1}$. Try to find the number of all different ways of passing the ball $N$.

This problem differs in form from the coloring problem, planting problem, etc., mentioned above, but its solution reflects structural similarities (see reference [4]).

For real numbers $x$ and $y$, where $x \neq y$, simplify the expression

$$
\left(\left(\frac{2 x+y}{x-y}\right)^{3}+1\right) \cdot(x-y)^{3}:\left(9 x^{3}-9 y^{3}\right)
$$

and write the result as a simplified fraction.

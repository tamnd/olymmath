Calculate the internal dimensions of a cylindrical cast iron pan with a capacity of 20 t, which should exhibit the lowest possible heat losses through appropriate shaping!

Based on experience, it is assumed that the heat losses from the surface of the liquid cast iron (per unit area) are twice the heat losses through the wall and bottom surface. (Weight of liquid cast iron: $7.2 \mathrm{t} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ )

- David cycles, but not in Biograd, Šibenik, and Poreč.
- Juraj is not spending the summer in Šibenik.
- Miha is not spending the summer in Poreč and does not like speed walking.
- Matija has been diving all summer.

- Petar is not spending the summer in Biograd, Primošten, and Poreč. He does not like speed walking and swimming.

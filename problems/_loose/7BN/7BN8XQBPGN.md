Let $p$ be a sufficiently large prime. Show that the number of distinct residues taken by the set $$\{1 + \frac12 + ... + \frac{1}{n}: n = 1, 2,..., p - 1\}$$ modulo $p$ has at least $\sqrt[4]{p}$ elements.

(Carlo Sanna)

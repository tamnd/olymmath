We consider rectangular boards consisting of $m \times n$ squares arranged in $m$ (horizontal) rows and $n$ (vertical) columns. We want to color each square of such a board black or white in such a way that the following three conditions are met:

- In each row, there are exactly as many black squares as white squares.
- If a row and a column intersect at a black square, then that row and that column contain exactly the same number of black squares.
- If a row and a column intersect at a white square, then that row and that column contain exactly the same number of white squares.

Determine all pairs $(m, n)$ for which such a coloring of the board is possible.

Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy the conditions: $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2$, and $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}(n=1,2,3, \cdots)$. Prove that for any positive integer $n$, we have $\sqrt[n]{a_{n+1}} \geqslant 1+\frac{1}{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}}$.

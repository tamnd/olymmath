b) Prove that there exist sequences of arbitrary length, composed of the digits 1, 2, and 3, in which no digit or group of digits repeats twice in a row.

Note. The theorem of part a) is a consequence of the theorem of part b), since any sequence composed of the digits 1, 2, and 3 can be considered as a sequence composed of the digits 0, 1, 2, and 3, in which the digit 0 does not appear at all.

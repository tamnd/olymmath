Find all functions $f : \mathbb Z \to \mathbb Z$ such that
$$f (n |m|) + f (n(|m| +2)) = 2f (n(|m| +1)) \forall m,n \in \mathbb Z.$$**Note.** $|x|$ denotes the absolute value of the integer $x.$

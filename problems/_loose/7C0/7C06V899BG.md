Consider a triangle with unequal sides. Draw the internal and external angle bisectors from one of its vertices to the line of the opposite side, and draw a circle with the segment between the endpoints as the diameter. Repeat the construction from another vertex and prove that the acute angle between the tangents drawn to the two circles at their common point is $60^{\circ}$.

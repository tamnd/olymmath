Let $M=\left\{1,2, \cdots, 2^{m} n\right\}\left(m, n\right.$ be positive integers) be a set of $2^{m} n$ consecutive positive integers. Find the minimum value of the positive integer $k$ such that any $k$-element subset of $M$ contains $m+1$ numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m+1}$ satisfying $a_{i}$ divides $a_{i+1}(i=1,2, \cdots, m)$.

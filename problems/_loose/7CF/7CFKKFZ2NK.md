Consider the ellipse $b^{2} x^{2}+a^{2} y^{2}=a^{2} b^{2}$ rotated about its minor axis: inscribe a cylinder of maximum volume in the resulting ellipsoid. (The axis of the cylinder is the axis of rotation of the ellipsoid, the circumferences of the bases of the cylinder lie on the ellipsoid.) In this cylinder, inscribe a rotational ellipsoid so that the ellipsoid touches the lateral surface, the base, and the top of the cylinder. In the thus obtained ellipsoid, inscribe a cylinder of maximum volume s. i. t. What is the sum of the volumes of the infinitely many such right circular cylinders, and the sum of the volumes of the infinitely many such ellipsoids? What is the ratio of these two sums?

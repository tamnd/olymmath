In order to reduce urban traffic congestion, a city plans to pilot a new traffic restriction rule starting from January 1, 2014. The rule states that vehicles with license plate numbers ending in $1$ and $6$ are restricted on Mondays and Tuesdays, those ending in $2$ and $7$ on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, those ending in $3$ and $8$ on Wednesdays and Thursdays, those ending in $4$ and $9$ on Thursdays and Fridays, and those ending in $5$ and $0$ on Fridays and Mondays. Saturdays and Sundays are unrestricted. Due to the Spring Festival on January 31, January 30 and January 31 are also unrestricted. Given that January 1, 2014, is a Wednesday and restricted, which group of license plate numbers has the fewest days they can travel in January 2014?
A. 1,6
B. $2, 7$
C. 4,9
D. 5,0

Let $S$ be a set of $n$ positive real numbers. For each non-empty subset $A$ of $S$, denote the sum of the elements in $A$ as $f(A)$. Prove: The set $\{f(A) \mid A \subseteq S, A \neq \varnothing\}$ can be partitioned into $n$ disjoint non-empty subsets, and in each subset, the ratio of the largest number to the smallest number is less than 2.

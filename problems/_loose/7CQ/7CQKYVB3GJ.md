Three ants move on the Cartesian plane in the following manner. Each minute, two of the ants remain stationary, and the third ant moves along a line parallel to the line formed by its two companions; it can of course remain stationary as well if it so chooses. Originally, the three ants are located at the points with coordinates $(0,0)$, $(0,1)$, and $(1,0)$. Is it possible that, after some time, the three ants end up at the points with coordinates $(-1,0)$, $(0,1)$, and $(1,0)$?

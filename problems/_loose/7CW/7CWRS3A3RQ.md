Inside the side $BC$ of an acute triangle $ABC$, choose a point $D$ and on the segment $AD$ choose a point $P$ so that it does not lie on the median from vertex $C$. The line of this median intersects the circumcircle of triangle $CPD$ at a point we denote as $K (K \neq C)$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $AKP$ passes through another fixed point besides point $A$, which is independent of the choice of points $D$ and $P$.

We divide the sides of a quadrilateral and a triangle in the same arbitrary ratio. We continue this procedure infinitely on the segments determined by the division points. How does the sum of the areas of the infinite number of quadrilaterals compare to the sum of the areas of the infinite number of triangles, if the area of the original triangle is $1 \frac{1}{2}$ times larger than the area of the original quadrilateral?

Let $\mathrm{S}=\{1,2, \ldots, 2014\}$, for each non-empty subset $\mathrm{T}$ of $\mathrm{S}$, choose an element of $\mathrm{T}$ as the representative element of $\mathrm{T}$. Find the number of different ways to assign a representative element to each subset of $\mathrm{S}$, such that for each non-empty subset $\mathrm{D}$ of $\mathrm{S}$, if $\mathrm{D}$ is the disjoint union of non-empty subsets $\mathrm{A}$, $\mathrm{B}$, and $\mathrm{C}$ of $\mathrm{S}$ (i.e., subsets $\mathrm{A}$, $\mathrm{B}$, and $\mathrm{C}$ are pairwise disjoint and their union is $\mathrm{D}$), then the representative element of $\mathrm{D}$ is also the representative element of one of $\mathrm{A}$, $\mathrm{B}$, or $\mathrm{C}$.

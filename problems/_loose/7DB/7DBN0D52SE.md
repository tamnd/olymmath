A square $ ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. Let $ \alpha = \angle DAB, \beta = \angle BDA,$ and $ \gamma = \angle CDB$ . Then $ \angle DBC$ equals

A. $ \alpha - \beta$
B. $ \alpha - \gamma$
C. $ 90^\circ - \alpha + \beta$
D. $ 90^\circ - \alpha + \gamma$
E. $ 180^\circ - \alpha - \gamma$

Pevzner I. A set of cells on a grid plane is called rook-connected if from any of its cells you can get to any other, moving along the cells of this set by the moves of a rook (the rook is allowed to fly over fields not belonging to our set). Prove that a rook-connected set of 100 cells can be divided into pairs of cells lying in the same row or in the same column.

Let the sequence of real numbers $\left\{x_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 1)$ satisfy $x_{1}=1$. Prove: For any integer $n \geqslant 2$, we have
$$
\sum_{i \mid n} \sum_{j \mid n} \frac{x_{i} x_{j}}{[i, j]} \geqslant \prod_{\text {prime } p \mid n}\left(1-\frac{1}{p}\right),
$$

where, $\sum_{i \mid n}$ denotes the sum over all positive divisors $i$ of $n$, and the product is over all prime factors $p$ of $n$.

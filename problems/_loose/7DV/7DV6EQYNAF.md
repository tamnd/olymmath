Points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ are chosen on the sides $B C, C A$ and $A B$ of a triangle $A B C$. Point $G$ is the centroid of $\triangle A B C$, and $G_{a}, G_{b}$ and $G_{c}$ are centroids of $\triangle A B_{1} C_{1}, \triangle B A_{1} C_{1}$ and $\triangle C A_{1} B_{1}$ respectively. The centroids of $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $\triangle G_{a} G_{b} G_{c}$ are denoted by $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ respectively. Prove that:

a) the points $G, G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ lie on a straight line;

b) lines $A G_{a}, B G_{b}$ and $C G_{c}$ intersect in a point if and only if $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ intersect in a point.

The edges of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ are bisected by the points $P, Q, R, P^{\prime}, Q^{\prime}, R^{\prime}$. How many times larger is the volume of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ compared to the volume of the tetrahedron $O P Q R$?

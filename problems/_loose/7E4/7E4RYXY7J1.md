The number of positive integer pairs $(a,b)$ that have $a$ dividing $b$ and $b$ dividing $2013^{2014}$ can be written as $2013n+k$ , where $n$ and $k$ are integers and $0\leq k<2013$ . What is $k$ ? Recall $2013=3\cdot 11\cdot 61$ .

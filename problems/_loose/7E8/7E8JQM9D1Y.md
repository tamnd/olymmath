Prove Ceva's theorem¹: if points $X, Y, Z$ are given on the sides $(B C),(C A)$, $(A B)$ of triangle $A B C$ respectively, then for the lines $(A X),(B Y)$ and $(C Z)$ to intersect at one point or be parallel, it is necessary and sufficient that the condition $(B C X)(C A Y)(A B Z)=-1$ is satisfied.

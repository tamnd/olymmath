Take a point $K$ on the major axis of an ellipse (where $K$ is not the center), and draw two chords $A C$ and $B D$ through $K$. Extend the opposite sides $A B$ and $D C$ of quadrilateral $A B C D$ to intersect at point $M$, and extend $A D$ and $B C$ to intersect at point $N$. When the chords $A C$ and $B D$ vary arbitrarily (while still intersecting at point $K$), prove:
(1) Points $M$ and $N$ lie on the same fixed line;
(2) The orthocenter of $\triangle K M N$ is a fixed point.

For real numbers $a, b, c, d$ from the interval $[1,2]$, it holds that $(a+c)(b+d)=8$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{a^{2}+b^{2}-1}+\frac{1}{b^{2}+c^{2}-1}+\frac{1}{c^{2}+d^{2}-1}+\frac{1}{d^{2}+a^{2}-1} \geq 1
$$

and determine when equality occurs.

(Zdeněk Pezlar)

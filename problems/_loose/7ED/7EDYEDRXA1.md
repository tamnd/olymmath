On a circle with a circumference of $2 n$, there are $2 n$ points, dividing it into $2 n$ equal parts. There are $n+1$ intervals of lengths $1, 2, \ldots, n+1$ placed on the circle, with the endpoints of these intervals being the aforementioned $2 n$ equal points. Prove that there must be one interval that is completely inside another interval.

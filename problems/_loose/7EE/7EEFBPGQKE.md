Matko created an unusual logo for his website using circles. He constructed the medians of a triangle, dividing it into 6 smaller triangles, and then constructed the circumcircles of each of these smaller triangles. He measured the areas of the circumcircles and found an interesting equality:

$$
\frac{P_{1}}{P_{2}} \cdot \frac{P_{3}}{P_{4}} \cdot \frac{P_{5}}{P_{6}}=1
$$

Prove Matko's equality.

(The areas $P_{1}, \ldots, P_{6}$ are the areas of the circumcircles of the adjacent smaller triangles, respectively.)

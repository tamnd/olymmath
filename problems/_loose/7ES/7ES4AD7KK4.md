Inside a square of side length 1 , four quarter-circle arcs are traced with the edges of the square serving as the radii. It is known that these arcs intersect pairwise at four distinct points, which in fact are the vertices of a smaller square. Suppose this process is repeated for the smaller square, and so on and so forth. What is the sum of the areas of all squares formed in this manner?

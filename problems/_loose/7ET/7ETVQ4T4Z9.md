A mad constructor created a clock with 150 hands. The first hand rotates at a speed of one revolution per hour, the second hand makes 2 revolutions per hour, ..., the 150th hand makes 150 revolutions per hour. The clock was started from a position where all hands were pointing straight up. When two or more hands meet during the operation of the clock, these hands immediately fall off. After how much time from the start will the hand rotating at a speed of 74 revolutions per hour fall off?

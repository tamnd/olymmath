Let $p=2 r k+1$ be a prime number, and $r$ be an odd prime. If $p=\sum_{i=1}^{r} a_{i}$ (positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{r}$ form a geometric sequence), then there exists a geometric sequence of $2 k$ positive integers $b_{1}, b_{2}$, $\cdots, b_{2 k}$, such that the product set
$$
A=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{r}\right\} \text { and } B=\left\{b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{2 k}\right\}
$$

of all elements of $A B$ exactly forms a reduced residue system modulo $p$.

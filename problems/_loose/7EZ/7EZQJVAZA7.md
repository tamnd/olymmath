Given that the base of the pyramid $S-ABCD$ is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, where the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular to each other, and the foot of the perpendicular from the vertex $S$ to the base is exactly the intersection point $O$ of the diagonals of the base. Prove: The feet of the perpendiculars from point $O$ to each side face are concyclic.

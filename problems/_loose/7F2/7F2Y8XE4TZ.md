The mathematical committee consists of $2 n$ members, $n \geqslant 3$. It is known that each member of the committee is in a dispute with exactly one other member of the committee (this relationship is symmetric). In how many ways can the committee be divided into three subcommittees: one for creating problems, one for evaluating problems, and one for organizing the competition, such that each subcommittee has at least two members and no two members of the committee who are in a dispute are in the same subcommittee?

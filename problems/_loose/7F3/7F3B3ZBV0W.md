Let $a$ and $b$ be two integers. If $a - b$ is divisible by a positive integer $n$, then $a$ and $b$ are said to be congruent modulo $n$, denoted as $a \equiv b(\bmod n)$. Given that the positive integer $a$ satisfies $a \equiv 3(\bmod 4)$. Prove: $x^{2} + y^{2} = a$ has no integer solutions.

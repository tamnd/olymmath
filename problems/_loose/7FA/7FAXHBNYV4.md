For a social gathering where members are meeting, we know that it is not possible to select 4 people such that the first knows the second, the second knows the third, and the third knows the fourth. Prove that it is possible to divide the group into three rooms such that in each room, no one knows anyone else. (Every acquaintance is considered mutual, meaning if $A$ knows $B$, then $B$ also knows $A$.)

On the playground, there are three equally large circles, none of which are identical. Place 16 girls so that 9 girls stand in each circle.

Find at least eight substantially different arrangements, i.e., arrangements where the girls and the circles are not distinguished. (Swapping individual girls, or entire circles with girls, gives an arrangement that is not substantially different from the original.) (L. Hozová)

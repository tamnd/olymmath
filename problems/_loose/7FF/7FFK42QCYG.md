(2007 China Mathematical Olympiad) Let the sequence $\{f(n)\}$ satisfy
$$
f(n)<\sum_{k=n}^{2 n n, 20 a k i} \frac{f(k)}{k+1}+\frac{1}{2 n+2007}, n=1,2 \cdot 3, \cdots,
$$

Prove: $f(n)<\frac{1}{n}, n=1,2,3, \cdots$.

It is known that if there are three houses and three wells on the surface of a spherical planet, we cannot build nine non-intersecting paths connecting the wells and the houses. 1. István Reimann: Geometry and Its Boundary, pp. 324-325. (Theorem 16.5).

Is a similar statement true if the houses and wells are on a torus-shaped planet?

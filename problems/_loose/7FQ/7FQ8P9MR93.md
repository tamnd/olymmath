We know about the numbers $p, q, a, b$ that $p+q=1, p q \neq 0$, and furthermore,

$$
\frac{p}{a}+\frac{q}{b}=\frac{1}{p \cdot a+q \cdot b}
$$

Prove that $a=b$.

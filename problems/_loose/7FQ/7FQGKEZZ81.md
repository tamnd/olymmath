Given a dihedral angle $\alpha-P Q-\beta . A, B$ are two points on the half-plane $\alpha$, and point $C$ is on the half-plane $\beta$ ($A, B, C$ are not on $P Q$, and the distances from $A, B$ to $P Q$ are not equal). Using $A, B, C$ as three vertices to construct a trapezoid, such that the fourth vertex of the trapezoid is also on the half-plane of the dihedral angle, then the number of trapezoids that meet the above conditions is
(A) does not exist.
(B) there is only one.
(C) there are two.
(D) there are infinitely many.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1986)

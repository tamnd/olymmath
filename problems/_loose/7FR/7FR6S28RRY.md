A cube $n \times n \times n$ is composed of unit cubes. A closed non-self-intersecting broken line is given, each segment of which connects the centers of two adjacent (having a common face) cubes. Let's call marked the faces of the cubes intersected by the given broken line. Prove that the edges of the cubes can be colored in two colors so that each marked face has an odd number, and every unmarked face has an even number of sides of each.

In the coordinate system of the plane, an invisible flea is jumping on the grid points. The flea starts from the origin and with each jump, it moves to an adjacent grid point to the right or upwards. After each jump of the flea, we place a flea-killing poison on a grid point of our arbitrary choice. If the flea is already on that point, or later steps on a poisoned square, it will perish. Is there a method by which we can ensure the destruction of the flea in a limited number of steps?

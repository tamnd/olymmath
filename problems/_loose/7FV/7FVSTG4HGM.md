On a circle $k$, there are points $A$ and $B$, and point $P$ is on the smaller arc $\widehat{A B}$. Let $Q$ and $R$ be points on $k$, different from $P$, such that $|A P|=|A Q|$ and $|B P|=|B R|$. Let $T$ be the intersection of the lines $A R$ and $B Q$. Prove that the lines $P T$ and $A B$ are perpendicular to each other.

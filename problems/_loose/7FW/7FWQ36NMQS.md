(Austria, 83). In triangle $A B C$, points $C^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $A^{\prime}$ are taken on sides $A B, A C$, and $B C$ respectively, such that segments $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$, and $C C^{\prime}$ intersect at one point. Points $A^{\prime \prime}$, $B^{\prime \prime}$, and $C^{\prime \prime}$ are symmetric to points $A, B$, and $C$ respectively with respect to points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$. Prove that

$$
S_{A^{\prime \prime} B^{\prime \prime} C^{\prime \prime}}=3 S_{A B C}+4 S_{A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}}
$$

For the storage of crude oil, roll tanks from the Soviet Union were set up at the Rostock Oil Harbor. One such tank has the shape of a cylinder with a diameter of $d=23 \mathrm{~m}$ and a height of $h=21 \mathrm{~m}$.

a) Calculate the volume of one tank, neglecting the wall thickness!

b) How many tons of crude oil can one roll tank hold (density of crude oil approximately $0.85 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$)?

c) The tanker "Leuna I" built in Leningrad for the GDR has a total capacity of 10200 t of crude oil. Its four pumps each have a capacity of 250 t/h. In what time will the tanker be emptied by them?

d) How much time is required to fill a roll tank using these pumps?
a) $V=\frac{\pi}{4} d^{2} \cdot h \approx 8725 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$

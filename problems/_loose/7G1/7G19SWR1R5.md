A flea is on the number 12 of a clock face. It chooses a natural number $n$ between 1 and 12, inclusive, and starts jumping $n$ numbers on the clock face, in a clockwise direction (for example, if $n=3$, after the first jump it is on 3, after the second it is on 6, and so on). After 12 jumps, for the first time, it finds itself back on the number 12 of the clock face. In how many distinct ways could it have chosen $n$?
(B) 2,
(C) 4,
(D) 6,
(E) 12.

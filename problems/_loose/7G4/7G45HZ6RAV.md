Let integers $x, y, z$ satisfy
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
|x+y|+|y+z|+|z+x|=4, \\
|x-y|+|y-z|+|z-x|=2 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Find the value of $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}$.

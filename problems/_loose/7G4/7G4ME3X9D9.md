On a board with 28 rows and 37 columns, a number is written in each square with a red pen: in the top row from left to right the numbers 1 to 37, in the row below from left to right the numbers 38 to 74, and so on.

Then, with a green pen, a number is written again in each square, but now the numbers 1 to 28 go from top to bottom in the leftmost column, in the next column from top to bottom the numbers 29 to 56, and so on.

In the top-left square, the number 1 is now written in both red and green. Add up the red numbers, from all the squares where the red and green numbers are the same. What is the result?

Given two parallel lines $a, b$ and a point $O$. Then for each point $M$ the following construction can be performed. Draw through $M$ an arbitrary line $l$, not passing through $O$ and intersecting lines $a$ and $b$. Denote the points of intersection by $A$ and $B$, respectively, and let $M^{\prime}$ be the point of intersection of the line $O M$ with the line parallel to $O B$ and passing through $A$.

a) Prove that the point $M^{\prime}$ does not depend on the choice of the line $l$.

b) Prove that the transformation of the plane that maps point $M$ to point $M^{\prime}$ is a projective transformation.

Let $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=s$.
If $a_{i}>\sqrt{2+\sqrt{5}}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, then $\left(a_{1}+\frac{1}{a_{1}}\right)\left(a_{2}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(a_{n}+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right) \leqslant\left(\frac{s}{n}+\frac{n}{s}\right)^{n}$

If $0<a_{i}<\sqrt{2+\sqrt{5}}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, then the reverse inequality holds.

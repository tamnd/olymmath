Construct a large cube with a side length of $n$ using $n^{3}$ unit cubes. Arbitrarily select more than $\frac{3}{2} n^{2}$ unit cubes and mark them. Prove that a right triangle can be found, with its 3 vertices located at the centers of the marked unit cubes, and the two legs of the right angle parallel to the edges of the cube.

Andrey, Borya, Vitya, and Gena are playing on a $100 \times 2019$ board (100 rows, 2019 columns). They take turns - first Andrey, then Borya, followed by Vitya, and finally Gena, then again Andrey, and so on. Each turn, a player must color two uncolored cells forming a rectangle of two cells, with Andrey and Borya coloring vertical $2 \times 1$ rectangles, and Vitya and Gena coloring horizontal $1 \times 2$ rectangles. The player who cannot make a move loses. Which three of the boys can agree to play in such a way that the remaining one will definitely lose? (It is sufficient to provide one such trio of boys.)

(S. Berlov, N. Vlasova)

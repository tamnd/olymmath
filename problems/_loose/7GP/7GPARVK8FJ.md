Let $A B C$ be a triangle. We denote $|A B C|$ as its area. Let $P$ be an interior point of the triangle such that $\frac{|A B C|}{|P A B|}=\frac{|A B C|}{|P A C|}=10$. Let $M$ be the point on $[A B]$ such that $(P M)$ is parallel to $(A C)$, and $N$ the point on $[A C]$ such that $(P N)$ is parallel to $(A B)$. Determine $\frac{|A B C|}{|P M A N|}$.

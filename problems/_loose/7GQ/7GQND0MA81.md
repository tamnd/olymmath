Let $\omega$ and $\Omega$ be two disjoint circles with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively. Choose $t$ as an external common tangent to $\omega$ and $\Omega$, touching them at $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$, respectively, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $\left[T_{1} T_{2}\right]$. The segment $\left[O_{1} O_{2}\right]$ intersects $\omega$ and $\Omega$ at $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Let $Z$ be the intersection of $\left(T_{1} X\right)$ and $\left(T_{2} Y\right)$. Show that the line $(Z M)$ is perpendicular to the line $\left(O_{1} O_{2}\right)$.

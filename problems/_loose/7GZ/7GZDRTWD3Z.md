If $ AD$ is the altitude, $ BE$ the angle bisector, and $ CF$ the median of a triangle $ ABC$ , prove that $ AD,BE,$ and $ CF$ are concurrent if and only if:

$ a^2(a-c)=(b^2-c^2)(a+c),$

where $ a,b,c$ are the lengths of the sides $ BC,CA,AB$ , respectively.

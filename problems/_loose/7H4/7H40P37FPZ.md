Given 2014 real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{2014}$ satisfy the system of equations
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{2014} \frac{x_{k}}{n+k}=\frac{1}{2 n+1}(n=1,2, \cdots, 2014) \text {. }
$$

Try to calculate the value of $\sum_{k=1}^{2014} \frac{x_{k}}{2 k+1}$.

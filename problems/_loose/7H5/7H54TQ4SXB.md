A cylindrical cheese wheel with a height of $10 \mathrm{~cm}$ and a diameter of $30 \mathrm{~cm}$ is cut into 8 equal pieces, as we would cut a cake. Each piece is then wrapped in foil. For each piece, we use $20 \%$ more foil than the surface area of the piece of cheese. How many $\mathrm{dm}^{2}$ of foil will we use to wrap the cheese? Round the result to the nearest hundredth. Write down the answer.

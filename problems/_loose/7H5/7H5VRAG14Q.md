$$
\frac{x-y \sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}+y^{2}}}=a, \frac{y-x \sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}+y^{2}}}=b
$$

($a$ and $b$ are given numbers).

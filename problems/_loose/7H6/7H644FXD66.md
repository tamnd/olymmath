It is known that two of them are counterfeit, they lie next to each other, the left one weighs 9 grams, the right one 11 grams, and all the remaining ones are genuine and each weighs 10 grams. The coins are weighed on balance scales, which either show which of the two pans is heavier, or are in equilibrium, indicating that the weights on both pans are the same. For what maximum $n$ can the coin weighing 11 grams be found in three weighings?

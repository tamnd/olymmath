Let $A, B, C$ and $D$ be four distinct collinear points in this order. Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be the circles with diameters $[A C]$ and $[B D]$, respectively, which intersect at $X$ and $Y$. Consider $O$ an arbitrary point on $(X Y)$ that is not on the original line. $(C O)$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ again at $M$, and $(B O)$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ again at $N$. Show that $(A M)$, $(D N)$, and $(X Y)$ are concurrent.

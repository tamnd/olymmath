A square table $n \times n$ is filled with natural numbers from 1 to $n^{2}$, where the first row contains the numbers from 1 to $n$, the second row from $n+1$ to $2 n$, the third row from $2 n+1$ to $3 n$, etc. From this table, a square $m \times m(m<n)$ is cut out. Prove that twice the sum of the numbers in this cut-out square is divisible by $m^{2}$.

(2002 Romanian IMO and Balkan Olympiad Selection Exam (4th Round)) Let the sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy that for all $n \geqslant 2, a_{n+1}$ is the smallest prime factor of $a_{n-1}+a_{n}$, and the fractional part of a real number $x$ is written in the order of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, \cdots$. Prove: $x$ is a rational number.

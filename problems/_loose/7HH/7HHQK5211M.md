A convex $n$-gon $(n \in \mathbb{N}, n>2)$ is given in the plane. Its area is less than 1 . For each point $X$ of this plane, we shall denote with $F(X)$ the area of the convex hull of point $X$ and a given $n$-gon (the area of the minimal convex polygon which includes both the point $X$ and a given $n$-gon). Prove that the set of points for which $F(X)=1$ is a convex polygon with $2 n$ sides or less.

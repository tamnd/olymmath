(1) Given any quadrilateral $Z_{1} Z_{2} Z_{3} Z_{4}$ and a point $Z_{0}$, construct a quadrilateral $E F G H$ such that $\triangle I I E G \backsim \triangle Z_{0} Z_{2} Z_{3}$ and $\triangle F G E \backsim \triangle Z_{0} Z_{4} Z_{1}$. Prove that there exists a point $Z_{0}^{\prime}$ such that $\triangle Z_{0}^{\prime} Z_{3} Z_{4} \backsim \triangle E F H$ and $\triangle Z_{0}^{\prime} Z_{1} Z_{2} \backsim \triangle G H F$.
(2) For the quadrilaterals $Z_{1} Z_{2} Z_{3} Z_{4}$ and $E F G H$ in (1), prove that there exist points $R$ and $S$ such that $\triangle R H E \sim \triangle Z_{3} Z_{4} Z_{2}$, $\triangle R F G \backsim \triangle Z_{1} Z_{2} Z_{4}$, $\triangle S G H \backsim \triangle Z_{2} Z_{3} Z_{1}$, and $\triangle S E F \sim \triangle Z_{4} Z_{1} Z_{3}$.

4 For $n \geq 1$, let $a_{n}$ be the largest odd divisor of $n$, and let $b_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}$. Prove that $b_{n} \geq \frac{n^{2}+2}{3}$, and determine for which $n$ equality holds. For example,
$$
a_{1}=1, a_{2}=1, a_{3}=3, a_{4}=1, a_{5}=5, a_{6}=3 \text {, }
$$
thus
$$
b_{6}=1+1+3+1+5+3=14 \geq \frac{6^{2}+2}{3}=12 \frac{2}{3} \text {. }
$$

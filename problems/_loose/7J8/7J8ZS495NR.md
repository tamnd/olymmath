(British MO 2011 round 2) Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $X$ a point inside the triangle $ABC$. The lines $(AX), (BX)$, and $(CX)$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at points $P, Q$, and $R$. Let $U$ be a point on the segment $[PX]$. The parallels to the lines $(AB), (AC)$ through $U$ intersect the lines $(XQ)$ and $(XR)$ at points $V$ and $W$. Show that the points $R, W, V$, and $Q$ are concyclic.

Prove that if $n=p+q-1$, then

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{p}\right)+\left(a_{2}+\ldots+a_{p+1}\right)+\ldots+\left(a_{n-p+1}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)= \\
& =\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{q}\right)+\left(a_{2}+\ldots+a_{q+1}\right)+\ldots+\left(a_{n-q+1}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

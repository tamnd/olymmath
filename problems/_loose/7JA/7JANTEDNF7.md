Once in the autumn, the Absent-Minded Scholar glanced at his old wall clock and saw that three flies had fallen asleep on the dial. The first was sleeping precisely at the 12 o'clock mark, while the other two were neatly positioned at the 2 o'clock and 5 o'clock marks. The Scholar made some measurements and determined that the hour hand posed no threat to the flies, but the minute hand would sweep them all away one by one. Find the probability that exactly 40 minutes after the Scholar noticed the flies, exactly two out of the three flies were swept away by the minute hand.

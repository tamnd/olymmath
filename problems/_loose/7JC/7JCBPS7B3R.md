Find the sum of the following expressions:
(1) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{3^{k+1}}{k+1}\binom{n}{k}$.
(2) $\sum_{k=2}^{n}\binom{k}{k-2} (n \geqslant 2)$.
(3) $\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(k^{2}+3\right)\binom{n}{k}$.
(4) $\sum_{k=0}^{n}(k+1) 2^{k}\binom{n}{k}$.
(5) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{(-1)^{k}}{k+1}\binom{n}{k} (n \geqslant 1)$.
(6) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{k+3}{k+1}\binom{n}{k}$.

In the sports area, stands $A, B, C, D, E$ formed the vertices of a regular pentagon. These stands were connected by straight paths. Additionally, on the path from $A$ to $B$ there was a fountain $F$, which was connected to stand $C$ by a path perpendicular to the path from $B$ to $E$. Pat and Mat met at stand $E$ and decided to sweep some of the paths. Pat swept the path from $E$ to $B$. Mat swept the path from $E$ to $A$ and also from $A$ to $F$.

Determine the difference in the lengths of the paths swept by Pat and Mat.

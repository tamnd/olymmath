Let's prove that if $a, b, c$ are positive numbers, then the following inequality holds:

$$
\begin{gathered}
(2 a+b)(2 a-b)(a-c)+(2 b+c)(2 b-c)(b-a)+ \\
+(2 c+a)(2 c-a)(c-b) \geq 0
\end{gathered}
$$

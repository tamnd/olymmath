Each cell of a $50 \times 50$ grid contains a number. It is known that each number is three times smaller than the sum of all numbers in the cells adjacent to it by a side, and two times smaller than the sum of all numbers in the cells adjacent to it by a diagonal. Prove that each cell of the board can be painted red or blue such that the sum of all numbers in the red cells is equal to the sum of all numbers in the blue cells.

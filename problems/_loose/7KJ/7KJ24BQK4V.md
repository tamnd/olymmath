An integer $m$ is a real power if there are positive integers $a$ and $n$ such that $n>1$ and $m=a^{n}$.

(a) Show that there are 2012 distinct positive integers such that no non-empty subset of them adds up to a real power.

(b) Show that there are 2012 distinct positive integers such that each non-empty subset of them sums to a real power.

At the end of the tournament, a reporter summed up the scores of all the players and obtained 190 points. In this type of tournament, the winner is the one who scores the most points.

a) How many players participated in the tournament?

b) André participated in the tournament and scored 9 points. Show that, even without knowing the other scores, André did not win the tournament.

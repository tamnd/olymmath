Let $A\in\mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ be an antisymmetric matrix, i.e. $A=-A^t.$

- Prove that if $A\in\mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$ and $A^2=O_n$ then $A=O_n.$

- Assume that $n{}$ is odd. Prove that if $A{}$ is the adjoint of another matrix $B\in\mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ then $A^2=O_n.$

The players are seated at equal distances from each other, so when the table stops rotating, exactly one player is opposite each sector and receives the number of coins written on that sector. It is known that after 11 rotations of the table, player ㄱo4 received 90 coins, and player № 8 received 35 coins. How many coins did player № 1 receive?

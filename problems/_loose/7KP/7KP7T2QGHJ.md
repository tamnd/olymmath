On the margins of a $10 \times 10$ board, there are 91 white checkers. The painter takes one of them, repaints it black, and places it on any free cell of the board. Then he takes another white checker, repaints it black, and so on until all the checkers are black. Prove that at some point, there will be checkers of different colors on some two adjacent (by side) cells.

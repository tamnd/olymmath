The angle $x O y$ is a right angle. A circle $C$, with radius $R$, touches the $O x$ at $A$; another circle $C^{\prime}$, with radius $R^{\prime}$, touches the $O y$ at $B$ and, in addition, the two circles also touch each other. Furthermore, $O A + O B = m$. Calculate $O A$ and $O B$; construct the two circles.

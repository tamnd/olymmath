Starting a new job, Ana is always interested in which of her colleagues know each other. To remember this information more easily, she draws a circle and represents each colleague as a chord. If two chords intersect, the corresponding people know each other, and if they do not intersect, the corresponding colleagues do not know each other. Ana is sure that this is always possible. Is she right? (If two chords have a common vertex, we consider that they intersect.)

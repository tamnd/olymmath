Let $n$ be a natural number. We seek to express the sum $S_{n}=0+1+2+\ldots+n$ in a simpler form (a formula that allows for faster calculations). Some already know the formula $S_{n}=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$. This formula does not come out of nowhere; it can be found in a clever way, but for now, let's prove it by induction, with the cleanest possible writing.

Show that the least common multiple of the integers $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ is

$$
\frac{A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}}{D}
$$

where $D$ is the greatest common divisor of the products $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, A_{3} A_{1}$! Prove this for the three numbers: $120,252,350$.

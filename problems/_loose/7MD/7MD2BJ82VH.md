Suppose that the number $\alpha$ is given by the infinite continued fraction $\alpha=\left[a_{0} ; a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots\right]$. Prove that $\alpha=a_{0}+\frac{1}{Q_{0} Q_{1}}-\frac{1}{Q_{1} Q_{2}}+\frac{1}{Q_{2} Q_{3}}-\ldots+(-1)^{n} \frac{1}{Q_{n} Q_{n+1}}$, where $Q_{k}$ are the denominators of the convergents.

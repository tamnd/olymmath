Consider three points in the plane, namely: $O(0,0), M(10,0)$ and $N(6,8)$. If these points form the vertices of a triangle, determine the centroid of the triangle, the intersection point of the three altitudes, and the center of the circumscribed circle. Furthermore, prove that the mentioned three points lie on a straight line and that the distance from the center of the circle to the intersection of the altitudes is exactly three times the distance from the center of the circle to the centroid. The coordinate system is rectangular in all cases.

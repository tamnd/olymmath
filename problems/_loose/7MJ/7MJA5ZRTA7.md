Consider the rational numbers:

$\mathrm{a}=\frac{1}{1+2}+\frac{1}{1+2+3}+\frac{1}{1+2+3+4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1+2+3+\cdots+2013}$ and

$\mathrm{b}=\frac{3}{1 \cdot 4}+\frac{5}{2^{2} \cdot 3^{2}}+\frac{7}{12^{2}}+\frac{9}{20^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{89}{1980^{2}}$

Show that the number $\mathrm{c}=\sqrt{2 \cdot \frac{1-\mathrm{b}}{1-a}}$ is irrational.

We draw a circle tangent to segment $B C$ at $D$ and to minor $\operatorname{arc} \widehat{B C}$ of $\Gamma$ at the point $A_{1}$. Define $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ in a similar way. Prove that lines $A_{1} D, B_{1} E$, $C_{1} F$ are concurrent.

Given a segment $AB$ and a point $P$ on it, we associate two circles with $P$: one passes through $A$ and $P$, and the other through $B$ and $P$. Furthermore, the radii of these circles are $\lambda$ times the lengths of segments $AP$ and $BP$, respectively, where $\lambda$ is a constant greater than 1/2. What is the geometric locus of the second common point of these circles as $P$ runs through all interior points of segment $AB$?

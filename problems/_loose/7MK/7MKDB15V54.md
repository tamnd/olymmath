(See [92].) Let $\xi$ be a random variable with distribution function $F=F(x)$ such that $F(b)-F(a)>0$ for all $a, b, -\infty<a<b<\infty$. Express $\mathrm{E}(\xi \mid a<\xi \leqslant b)$ in terms of $F$.

Establish that $f(a, b)=\mathrm{E}(\xi \mid a<\xi \leqslant b)$ is an increasing function of $a, b$ (on the set $\{(a, b): a<b\}$ ).

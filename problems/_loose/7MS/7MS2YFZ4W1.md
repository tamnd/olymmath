Let $x,y,z$ be positive real numbers, less than $\pi$ , such that: $$ \cos x + \cos y + \cos z = 0 $$ $$ \cos 2x + \cos 2 y + \cos 2z = 0 $$ $$ \cos 3x + \cos 3y + \cos 3z = 0 $$ Find all the values that $\sin x + \sin y + \sin z$ can take.

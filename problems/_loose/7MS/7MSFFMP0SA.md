Let $n$ be a positive integer, and $n \geqslant 3, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n} \in \mathrm{R}$, where $a_{i} \in \subset 2$, 3], $i=1,2, \cdots, n$, if we take $S=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}$, prove: $\frac{a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}-a_{3}^{2}}{a_{1}+a_{2}-a_{3}}+\frac{a_{2}^{2}+a_{3}^{2}-a_{4}^{2}}{a_{2}+a_{3}-a_{4}}+$ $\cdots+\frac{a_{n}^{2}+a_{1}^{2}-a_{2}^{2}}{a_{n}+a_{1}-a_{2}} \leqslant 2 S-2 n$.

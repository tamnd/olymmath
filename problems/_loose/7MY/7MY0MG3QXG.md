Given positive numbers $a, b, c, d, e, f$, and $|\sqrt{a b}-\sqrt{c d}| \leqslant 2$. Prove the inequality

$$
\left(\frac{e}{a}+\frac{b}{e}\right)\left(\frac{e}{c}+\frac{d}{e}\right) \geqslant\left(\frac{f}{a}-b\right)\left(d-\frac{f}{c}\right)
$$

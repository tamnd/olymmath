Let $A B C$ be an acute angled triangle. Let $B^{\prime}, A^{\prime}$ be points on the perpendicular bisectors of $A C, B C$ respectively such that $B^{\prime} A \perp A B$ and $A^{\prime} B \perp A B$. Let $P$ be a point on the segment $A B$ and $O$ the circumcenter of the triangle $A B C$. Let $D, E$ be points on $B C, A C$ respectively such that $D P \perp B O$ and $E P \perp A O$. Let $O^{\prime}$ be the circumcenter of the triangle $C D E$. Prove that $B^{\prime}, A^{\prime}$ and $O^{\prime}$ are collinear.

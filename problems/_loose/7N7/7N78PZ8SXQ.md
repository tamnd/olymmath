a) Given on the plane is the point pair $F_{1}, F_{2}$ and a segment $s$ longer than the segment $F_{1} F_{2}$. The point $M$ moves on the plane such that the sum of its distances from $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ is equal to $s$. Show that if we draw the line $e$ through any position of $M$, which is perpendicular to the bisector of the angle $F_{1} M F_{2}$, then any other position of $M$ will lie on the same side of $e$ as $F_{1}$.

b) Given on the plane is the point pair $G_{1}, G_{2}$ and a segment $d$ shorter than the segment $G_{1} G_{2}$. The point $N$ moves on the plane such that its distance from $G_{2}$ is $d$ greater than its distance from $G_{1}$. Show that if we draw the line $g$ through any position of $N$, which bisects the angle between the rays $N G_{1}$ and $N G_{2}$, then any other position of $N$ will lie on the same side of $g$ as $G_{1}$.

Elsa wrote the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 8$ on the board. Then she allows herself operations of the following form: she chooses two numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $a+2 \leqslant b$, if such numbers exist, then she erases them, and writes the numbers $a+1$ and $b-1$ in their place.

(a) Prove that Elsa must stop after a finite number of operations.

(b) How many, at most, can Elsa perform operations?

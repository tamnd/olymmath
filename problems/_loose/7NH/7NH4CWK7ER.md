Let $c$ be a natural number. Suppose that $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$ (infinite) is a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers such that for every natural number $n$,

$$
x_{n} \mid n^{2}+c
$$

Prove that there exists a natural number $M$ such that $x_{n}=n^{2}+c$ for every $n \geqslant M$.

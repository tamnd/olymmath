For each positive integer $n\geq 2$ we denote with $p(n)$ the largest prime number less than or equal to $n$, and with $q(n)$ the smallest prime number larger than $n$. Prove that $$ \sum^n_{k=2} \frac 1{p(k)q(k)} < \frac 12. $$

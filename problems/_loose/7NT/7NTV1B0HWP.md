Show that if $m$ and $n$ are two strictly positive integers that are coprime, then there exists $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that any integer $k \geqslant N$ can be written in the form $k=a m+b n$ with $a$ and $b$ being positive integers. Determine the minimum value of $N$.

On the legs $a, b$ of a right triangle, the centers of two circles $k_{a}, k_{b}$ lie in sequence. Both circles touch the hypotenuse and pass through the vertex opposite the hypotenuse. Let the radii of the given circles be $\varrho_{a}, \varrho_{b}$. Determine the greatest positive real number $p$ such that the inequality

$$
\frac{1}{\varrho_{a}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{b}} \geqq p\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}\right)
$$

holds for all right triangles.

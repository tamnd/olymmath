For a triangle $A B C$, the following is assumed:

The interior angle $\angle A B C$ is greater than $90^{\circ}$.

If $D$ is the foot of the perpendicular dropped from $C$ to the line through $A$ and $B$, then $2 \cdot B D=A B=$ $B C$.

Prove that these assumptions uniquely determine the sizes of the interior angles of triangle $A B C$! Determine these angle sizes!

Consider the polynomial $P(X)=X^{p-1}+X^{p-2}+\ldots+X+1$ , where $p>2$ is a prime number. Show that if $n$ is an even number, then the polynomial $$-1+\prod_{k=0}^{n-1} P\left(X^{p^k}\right)$$ is divisible by $X^2+1$ .

*Mircea Becheanu*

Koschei the Deathless kidnapped three daughters of the Tsar. Ivan the Tsarevich set out to rescue them. He came to Koschei, and Koschei said to him: "Tomorrow morning you will see five enchanted maidens. Three of them are the Tsar's daughters, and the other two are mine. To you, they will be indistinguishable, but they can recognize each other. I will approach one of them and ask her about each of the five: 'Is this a Tsarevna?' She can answer both truthfully and falsely, but she is allowed to call exactly two of them Tsarevnas (including herself). Then I will question each of the other maidens in the same way, and they also must name exactly two as Tsarevnas. If you can guess who the real Tsarevnas are, I will let you go unharmed. And if you can also figure out which one is the eldest, which one is the middle, and which one is the youngest, you can take them with you." Ivan can pass a note to the Tsarevnas to instruct them whom to call Tsarevnas. Can he, regardless of the answers of Koschei's daughters,

a) return alive?

b) take the Tsarevnas with him?

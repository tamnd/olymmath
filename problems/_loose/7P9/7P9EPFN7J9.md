On the sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$ of the parallelogram $A B C D$, points $M, N, P, Q$ are given respectively such that $\frac{A M}{M B}=k_{1}, \frac{B N}{N C}=k_{2}$, $\frac{C P}{P D}=k_{1}, \frac{D Q}{Q A}=k_{2}$. Prove that the lines $A P, B Q, C M, D N$, intersecting, form a second parallelogram and that the ratio of the area of this parallelogram to the area of the given parallelogram is

$$
\frac{k_{1} \cdot k_{2}}{\left(1+k_{1}\right)\left(1+k_{2}\right)+1}
$$

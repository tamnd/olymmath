Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, the line $l$ does not pass through the origin $O$ and is not parallel to the coordinate axes, $l$ intersects $C$ at two points $A, B$, and the midpoint of segment $A B$ is $M$. Prove:
(1) The product of the slope of line $O M$ and the slope of line $l$ is a constant $-\frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}}$;
(2) If $l$ passes through the point $(b, a)$, extending segment $O M$ intersects $C$ at point $P$, when quadrilateral $O A P B$ is a parallelogram, the slope of line $l$ $k_{l}=\frac{4 \pm \sqrt{7}}{3} \cdot \frac{a}{b}$.

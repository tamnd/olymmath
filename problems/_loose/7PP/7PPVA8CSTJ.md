Let $S=\{1,2,\dots,2023\}$ . Suppose that for every two-element subset of $S$ , we get the positive difference between the two elements. The average of all of these differences can be expressed as a fraction $a/b$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime integers. Find the sum of the digits of $a+b$ .

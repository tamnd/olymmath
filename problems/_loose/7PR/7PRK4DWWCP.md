In a triangle $A B C$, let $H$ be the orthocenter, and let $D, E, F$ be the feet of altitudes from $A, B, C$ to the opposite sides, respectively. Let $L, M, N$ be midpoints of segments $A H, E F, B C$, respectively. Let $X, Y$ be feet of altitudes from $L, N$ on to the line $D F$. Prove that $X M$ is perpendicular to $M Y$.

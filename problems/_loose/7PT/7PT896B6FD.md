For 2 by 2 matrix $A=\left(
\begin{array}{cc}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{array}
\right)$, define $\Delta (A)=ad-bc,\ t(A)=a+d.$

(1) For 2 by 2 matrices $A,\ B$, show that $\Delta (AB)=\Delta (A)\Delta (B).$

(2) Suppose all elements of $A$ are real and $A^5=E$. Find the values of $x=\Delta (A)$ and $y=t(A).$

Note : $E$ is a 2 by 2 identity matrix.

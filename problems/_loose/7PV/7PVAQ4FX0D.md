Seven people are sitting around a circular table, not necessarily equally spaced from each other. Several vases are standing on the table. We say that two people can see each other if there are no vases on the line segment connecting them. (Treat both people and vases as points.)

(a) If there are 12 vases, prove that there are two people who can see each other.

(b) If there are 13 vases, prove that one can rearrange the people and the vases in such a way that no two people can see each other.

Prove that for any natural number $n$ and any real $\alpha$, the following double inequality holds:

$$
\frac{2}{3}<\frac{1}{n+\sin ^{2} \alpha}+\frac{1}{n+2 \sin ^{2} \alpha}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n+(2 n+1) \sin ^{2} \alpha} \leqslant 3
$$

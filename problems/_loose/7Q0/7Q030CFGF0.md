In $\triangle A B C$, $P, Q$ are points on sides $A B, A C$ respectively, such that $\angle A P C=\angle A Q B=45^{\circ}$. A perpendicular line from point $P$ to side $A B$ intersects $B Q$ at point $S$, and a perpendicular line from point $Q$ to side $A C$ intersects $C P$ at point $R$. Let $D$ be a point on $B C$ such that $A D \perp B C$. Prove: $P S, A D, Q R$ are concurrent, and $S R \parallel B C$.

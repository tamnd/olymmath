Consider the curve family characterized by the equation

$$
y=(p-1) x^{2}+2 p x+4
$$

1. Determine the parameter $p$ so that the parabola $g_{p}$ is tangent to the $x$-axis. (Denote the point of tangency by $A$.)
2. Determine $p$ so that the vertex of the parabola $g_{p}$ (denoted by $B$) lies on the $y$-axis.
3. Show that the two curves mentioned above are centrally symmetric with respect to the midpoint of the segment $AB$.
4. Does there exist a point through which every curve in the family passes?

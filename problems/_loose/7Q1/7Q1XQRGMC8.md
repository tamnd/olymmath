In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
(1) $h_{a}+h_{b}+h_{c}=r(a+b+c)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right)$;
(2) $\frac{1}{h_{a}}+\frac{1}{h_{b}}+\frac{1}{h_{c}}=\frac{1}{r}=\frac{1}{r_{a}}+\frac{1}{r_{b}}+\frac{1}{r_{c}}$;
(3) $\left(h_{a}+h_{b}+h_{c}\right)\left(\frac{1}{h_{a}}+\frac{1}{h_{b}}+\frac{1}{h_{c}}\right)$
$=(a+b+c)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right)$;
(4) $\frac{h_{a}+h_{c}}{r_{a}}+\frac{h_{c}+h_{a}}{r_{b}}+\frac{h_{a}+h_{b}}{r_{c}}=6$.

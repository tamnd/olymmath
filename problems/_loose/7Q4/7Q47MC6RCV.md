Does there exist a solution in real numbers of the system of equations
$$\left\{
\begin{array}{rcl}
(x - y)(z - t)(z - x)(z - t)^2 = A,
(y - z)(t - x)(t - y)(x - z)^2 = B,
(x - z)(y - t)(z - t)(y - z)^2 = C,
\end{array}
\right.$$
when
a) $A=2, B=8, C=6;$ b) $A=2, B=6, C=8.$ ?

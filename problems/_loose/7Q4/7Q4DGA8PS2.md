Extra Crocodiles (from 9th grade, 6 points). A chocolate egg manufacturer with a toy inside announced that a new collection is being released, featuring ten different crocodiles. The crocodiles are uniformly and randomly distributed among the chocolate eggs, meaning that in a randomly chosen egg, each crocodile can appear with a probability of 0.1. Leshа wants to collect the entire collection. Every day, his mother buys him one chocolate egg with a crocodile.

At first, a crocodile with glasses appeared in Leshа's collection, then a crocodile with a newspaper. The third unique specimen was a crocodile with a cane. Find the mathematical expectation of the random variable "the number of crocodiles with a cane that Leshа had by the time he completed his collection."

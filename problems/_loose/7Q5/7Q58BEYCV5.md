In triangle $ABC$, we choose point $P$ on side $BC$ and point $Q$ on side $AC$ such that

$$
\frac{CP}{CB} = \frac{CQ}{QA} = x (\neq 0)
$$

Show that there is a point in the plane through which the line $PQ$ passes, regardless of the value of $x$.

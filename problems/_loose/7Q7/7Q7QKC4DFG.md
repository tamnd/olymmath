Dan has a fair $6$-sided die with faces labeled $1,2,3,4,+,$ and $-.$ In order to complete the equation
$$ \underline{ } \ \underline{ } \ \underline{ }=\underline{ }, $$
Dan repeatedly rolls his die and fills in a blank with the character he obtained, starting with the leftmost blank and progressing rightward. The probability that, when all blanks are filled, Dan forms a true equation, is $\frac{p}{q},$ where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime integers. Find $p+q.$

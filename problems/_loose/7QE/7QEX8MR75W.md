Let $D^{\prime}, E^{\prime}, F^{\prime}$ be the points symmetric to the projections $D, E, F$ of the orthocenter $H$ of $\triangle A B C$ on the sides $BC, CA, AB$ with respect to the midpoints of $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. If $A D^{\prime}, B E^{\prime}, C F^{\prime}$ intersect at $H^{\prime}$. Prove:
$$
\frac{H^{\prime} D^{\prime}}{A H^{\prime}} \cdot \frac{H^{\prime} E^{\prime}}{B H^{\prime}} \cdot \frac{H^{\prime} F^{\prime}}{C H^{\prime}}=\frac{H D}{A H} \cdot \frac{H E}{B H} \cdot \frac{H F}{C H} .
$$

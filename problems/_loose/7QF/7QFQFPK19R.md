Let $G$ be a directed graph, $A$ and $B$ two sets of its vertices (not necessarily disjoint). Show that the minimum number of vertices that need to be deleted so that there are no more paths from $A$ to $B$ is equal to the maximum number of vertex-disjoint paths between $A$ and $B$ (a path may contain only one vertex).

A *simple hyperplane* in $\mathbb{R}^4$ has the form $$k_1x_1+k_2x_2+k_3x_3+k_4x_4=0$$ for some integers $k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4\in \{-1,0,1\}$ that are not all zero. Find the number of regions that the set of all simple hyperplanes divide the unit ball $x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2+x_4^2\leq 1$ into.

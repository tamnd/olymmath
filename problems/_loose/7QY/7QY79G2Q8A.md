Given several white and several black points. From each white point, there is an arrow to each black point, and each arrow has a natural number written on it. It is known that if you follow any closed path, the product of the numbers on the arrows going in the direction of movement is equal to the product of the numbers on the arrows going against the direction of movement. Is it necessarily possible to place a natural number in each point so that the number on each arrow is equal to the product of the numbers at its ends?

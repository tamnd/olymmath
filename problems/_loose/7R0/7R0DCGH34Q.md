Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $H$ be the intersection of its three altitudes.

Show that the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to the line $(BC)$, which we will denote as $H^{\prime}$, lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

Show that the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to the midpoint of $[BC]$, which we will denote as $H^{\prime \prime}$, also lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

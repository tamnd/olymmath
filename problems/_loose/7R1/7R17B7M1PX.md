In a group $G$ , we have two elements $x,y$ such that $x^{n}=e,y^2=e,yxy=x^{-1}$ , $n\ge 1$ . Prove that
for any $k\in\mathbb{N}$ holds $(x^ky)^2=e$ .

Note : e=group's identity .

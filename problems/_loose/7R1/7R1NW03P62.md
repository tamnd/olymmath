Use Ramsey's Theorem to prove that for any $k, \ell \in \mathbb{N}$, there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that any sequence of $n$ distinct integers contains either an increasing subsequence of length $k+1$ or a decreasing subsequence of length $\ell+1$. Find an example with $n > k \ell$ and prove the theorem of Erdős and Szekeres, i.e., $n = k \ell + 1$ is sufficient.

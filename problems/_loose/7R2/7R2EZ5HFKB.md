For a quadrilateral $A B C D$, it is required that it is a trapezoid with $A B \| D C, e=7 \mathrm{~cm}, f=6 \mathrm{~cm}$, $\alpha=48^{\circ}, \epsilon=114^{\circ}$, where $e$ is the length of the diagonal $A C$, $f$ is the length of the diagonal $B D$, $\alpha$ is the measure of the angle $\angle D A B$, and, if $S$ is the intersection point of $A C$ with $B D$, $\epsilon$ is the measure of the angle $\angle A S B$.

a) Prove: If a quadrilateral meets these requirements, then it can be constructed from the given lengths and angle measures!

b) Describe such a construction and create a construction drawing!

c) Prove: If a quadrilateral is constructed according to the description, then it meets the stated requirements!

d) Prove that the requirements uniquely determine a quadrilateral $A B C D$ up to congruence!

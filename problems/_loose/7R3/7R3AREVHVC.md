Let the convex quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$ and the points $\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}, \mathrm{Q}$ on the sides $[A B],[B C],[C D]$ and $[D A]$ respectively, such that $\frac{A M}{A B}=\frac{B N}{B C}=\frac{C P}{C D}=\frac{D Q}{D A}=k, k>0, k \neq \frac{1}{2}$. Then MNPQ is a parallelogram $\Leftrightarrow$ ABCD is a parallelogram.

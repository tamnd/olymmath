Given that $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ are two functions defined on $\mathbf{R}$, and $g(x)$ is an odd function, satisfying $f(0)=1$; for any $x, y \in \mathbf{R}$, we have $f(x-y)=f(x) f(y)+g(x) g(y)$. Prove: (1) For any $x \in \mathbf{R}$, $f^{2}(x)+g^{2}(x)=1$; (2) $f(x)$ is an even function; (3) If there exists a non-zero real number $a$ such that $f(a)=1$, then $f(x)$ is a periodic function.

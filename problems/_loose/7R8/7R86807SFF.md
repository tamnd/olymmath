Determine the greatest real number $p$ and the smallest real number $q$, for which the inequalities

$$
p<\frac{a+t_{b}}{b+t_{a}}<q
$$

hold in any triangle $ABC$ with sides $a, b$ and medians $t_{a}, t_{b}$.

If the integer part of $5-\sqrt{3}$ is $x$, and the decimal part is $y$, then the value of $2 x^{3}-$ $\left(y^{3}+\frac{1}{y^{3}}\right)$ is
(A) 2.
(B) -2.
(C) $\sqrt{3}+2$.
(D) $2+3 \sqrt{3}$.
(E) $2-6 \sqrt{3}$.
(Chongqing, Sichuan Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

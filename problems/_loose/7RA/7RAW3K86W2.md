a) Prove that in trilinear coordinates, the circumscribed conic (i.e., a conic passing through all vertices of the triangle) is given by the equation of the form

$$
p x y + q x z + r z y = 0
$$

b) Prove that in trilinear coordinates, the conic tangent to all sides of the triangle or their extensions is given by the equation of the form

$$
p x^{2} + q y^{2} + r z^{2} = 2( \pm \sqrt{p q} x y \pm \sqrt{p r} x z \pm \sqrt{q r} y z)
$$

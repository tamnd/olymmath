Find the largest positive integer $n$ for which there exists a set $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\}$ of composite positive integers with the following properties:

(i) any two of them are coprime;

(ii) $1<a_{i} \leq(3 n+1)^{2}$ for $i=1, \ldots, n$.

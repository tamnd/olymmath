Denis has cards with numbers from 1 to 50. How many ways are there to choose two cards such that the difference between the numbers on the cards is 11, and the product is divisible by 5?

The order of the selected cards does not matter: for example, the way to choose cards with numbers 5 and 16, and the way to choose cards with numbers 16 and 5 - this is the same way.

Task: Calculate $\sqrt[3]{\frac{x}{2015+2016}}$, where $x$ is the harmonic mean of the numbers

$a=\frac{2016+2015}{2016^{2}+2016 \cdot 2015+2015^{2}}$ and $b=\frac{2016-2015}{2016^{2}-2016 \cdot 2015+2015^{2}}$.

The harmonic mean of two positive numbers $a$ and $b$ is the number $c$ such that $\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}\right)$

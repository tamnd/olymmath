For real numbers $a, b, c, d$, the following equations hold:

$$
4 a^{2}+b^{2}-8 b+12=0
$$

and

$$
c^{2}-8 c+4 d^{2}+12=0
$$

What is the minimum value of the expression $(a-c)^{2}+(b-d)^{2}$?

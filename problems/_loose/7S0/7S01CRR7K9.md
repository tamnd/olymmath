In the angle $A O B$, at an arbitrary point $C$ on the side $A O$, we draw perpendiculars to the two sides, and we draw the angle bisector of the angle enclosed by these perpendiculars. If this angle bisector intersects $B O$ at $E$, then it must be proven that $O C=O E$.

In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $O$. Let $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$, and $P_{4}$ be the perimeters of triangles $A O B, B O C, C O D$, and $D O A$, respectively, and let $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$, and $r_{4}$ be the radii of the inscribed circles in these triangles. Prove the inequality

$$
O A \cdot O B \cdot O C \cdot O D \geqslant \sqrt{P_{1} r_{1} \cdot P_{2} r_{2} \cdot P_{3} r_{3} \cdot P_{4} r_{4}} .
$$

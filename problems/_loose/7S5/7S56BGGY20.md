Find all functions $f: \mathbf{N}_{+} \rightarrow \mathbf{N}_{+}$, for which there exists $k \in \mathbf{N}_{+}$ and a prime $p$, such that for any $n \geqslant k$, we have $f(n+p)=f(n)$. Additionally, it must satisfy: if $m \mid n$, then $f(m+1) \mid(f(n)+1)$.

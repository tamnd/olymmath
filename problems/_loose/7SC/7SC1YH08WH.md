The centers of two circles with equal radii are $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, intersecting at points $P$ and $Q$. $M$ is the midpoint of the common chord $PQ$. Through $P$, any two secants $AB$ and $CD$ are drawn $\left(A B, CD\right.$ do not coincide with $PQ$), with points $A, C$ on circle $O_{1}$, and points $B, D$ on circle $O_{2}$. Connect $AD$ and $BC$. Points $M, N$ are the midpoints of $AD$ and $BC$, respectively, and $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ are not in the common part of the two circles. Points $M, N$ do not coincide with point $M$. Prove: $M, N, O$ are collinear.

Show that $(X, Y) \stackrel{d}{=}(Z, Y)$ if and only if

$$
\mathrm{P}(X \in A \mid Y)=\mathrm{P}(Z \in A \mid Y)
$$

a.s. for every $A \in \mathscr{B}(\mathbb{R})$.

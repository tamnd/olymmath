Find all functions $ f : \mathbb N^\ast \to M$ such that
$$ 1 + f(n) f(n+1) = 2 n^2 \left( f(n+1) - f(n) \right), \forall n \in \mathbb N^\ast , $$
in each of the following situations:

(a) $ M = \mathbb N$;

(b) $ M = \mathbb Q$.

The solution set of the inequality $\sin 2 \pi x|\sin 2 \pi x|>\cos 2 \pi x|\cos 2 \pi x|$ is $ $
\left\{\begin{array}{l}-x^{2}, x<0 \\ x^{2}, x \geq 0\end{array}\right.$, we have $\sin 2 \pi x|\sin 2 \pi x|>\cos 2 \pi x|\cos 2 \pi x| \Leftrightarrow \sin^2 2 \pi x > \cos^2 2 \pi x \Leftrightarrow \cos 4 \pi x < 0 \Leftrightarrow x \in\left[k+\frac{1}{8}, k+\frac{5}{8}\right](k \in \mathbf{Z}) .

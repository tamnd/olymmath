Given a rhombus $ABCD$ with $\measuredangle BAD=60^{\circ}$. The angle bisectors of the angles between the diagonals intersect the sides of the rhombus at points $M, N, P$ and $Q$.

a) What type of quadrilateral is quadrilateral $MNPQ$?

b) If point $M$ lies on side $AB$, calculate the ratio $AM: MB$.

c) Determine the ratio of those segments of the larger and smaller diagonals of the rhombus that lie outside quadrilateral $MNPQ$.

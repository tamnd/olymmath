Given a trihedral angle with vertex $S$ and edges $a, b$, and $c$. Rays $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ are located in the planes of the faces opposite to the edges $a, b$, and $c$, and rays $\alpha^{\prime}, \beta^{\prime}$, and $\gamma^{\prime}$ are symmetric to these rays with respect to the bisectors of the corresponding faces.

a) Prove that the rays $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ lie in the same plane if and only if the rays $\alpha^{\prime}, \beta^{\prime}$, and $\gamma^{\prime}$ lie in the same plane.

b) Prove that the planes passing through the pairs of rays $a$ and $\alpha, b$ and $\beta, c$ and $\gamma$, intersect along a single line if and only if the planes passing through the pairs of rays $a$ and $\alpha^{\prime}, b$ and $\beta^{\prime}, c$ and $\gamma^{\prime}$, intersect along a single line.

Let $n$ be a natural number. In a village, $2 n$ people live. Some of them are friends, and friendships are mutual. A perfect matching is a division of the village residents into $n$ pairs such that each pair consists of two people who are friends.

Assume that in the village there is exactly one perfect matching. What is the maximum possible number of friendships in the village?

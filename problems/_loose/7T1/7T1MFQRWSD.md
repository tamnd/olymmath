There is a pile of more than $n^{2}$ stones on the table. Petya and Vasya take turns taking stones from the pile, with Petya going first. In one move, one can take any prime number of stones less than $n$, or any multiple of $n$ stones, or one stone. Prove that Petya can act in such a way as to take the last stone regardless of Vasya's actions.

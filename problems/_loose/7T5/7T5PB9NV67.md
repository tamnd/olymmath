There is an $n \times n$ chessboard $(n>100)$, in which $n-1$ cells are filled with 1, and the rest are filled with 0. Each time, you can select a cell, subtract 1 from the number in it, and add 1 to the numbers in the other cells in the same row and column. Is it possible to make all the numbers equal eventually?

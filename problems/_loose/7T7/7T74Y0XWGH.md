Determine the smallest real number $p$ such that the inequality

$$
\sqrt{1^{2}+1}+\sqrt{2^{2}+1}+\sqrt{3^{2}+1}+\ldots+\sqrt{n^{2}+1} \leqq \frac{1}{2} n(n+p)
$$

holds for every natural number $n$.

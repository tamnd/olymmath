Consider three isometric circles and pairwise tangent, $\mathscr{C}_{1}$, $\mathscr{C}_{2}, \mathscr{C}_{3}$, all three internally tangent to a circle $\Gamma$. Let $M$ be any point on $\Gamma$. We draw a tangent from $M$ to $\mathscr{C}_{1}$, a tangent from $M$ to $\mathscr{C}_{2}$, and a tangent from $M$ to $\mathscr{C}_{3}$, and we call $A, B$, C the three points of contact. Show that one of the lengths $M A, M B, M C$ is the sum of the other two.

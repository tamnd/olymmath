Four players, $A, B, C$, and $D$ are playing dominoes. Their set consists of 28 dominoes. The players take turns in the above order, and they can start with any tile. After two rounds, the sum of the numbers on the tiles placed by each player is: $A: 23, B: 20, C: 18, D$: 16. Then $A$ placed the 6-2 tile. What were the first 8 dominoes placed and in what order? (The domino set includes all possible combinations of numbers from 0 to 6: The tiles are placed in the usual manner, with any matching end being placed next to it.)

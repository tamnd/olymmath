In the game of Color-Swap, a board with two rows and as many columns as we want is used, where the cells can change from white to gray and vice versa. The cells in the 1st row are numbered with odd numbers, and those in the 2nd row with even numbers. In each move, a cell is pressed, and then that cell and its neighboring cells change color. A complete game starts with all cells white and ends when all cells are gray. See two examples of complete games (the numbers above the arrows indicate the cell pressed in each move):

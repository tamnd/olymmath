Let $x, y$ be positive integers such that $1 \leqslant y < x$. We say that the pair $(x, y)$ is pretty if $x$ and $y$ have exactly $x - y$ positive divisors in common. For example, 30 and 42 have exactly 4 divisors in common: $1, 2, 3$, and 6. Since $42 - 30 = 12 \neq 4$, the pair $(42, 30)$ is not pretty. On the other hand, the pair $(8, 6)$ is pretty, because 6 and 8 have $8 - 6 = 2$ positive divisors in common: 1 and 2.

For any integer $n \geqslant 2$, we denote by $s(n)$ the number of pairs of integers $(x, y)$ such that $1 \leqslant y < x \leqslant n$ and that $(x, y)$ is pretty.

a) Does there exist an integer $n \geqslant 2$ such that $s(n) = 2022$?

b) Does there exist an integer $n \geqslant 2$ such that $s(n) = 2023$?

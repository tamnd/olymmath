In the vertices of a regular 300-gon, numbers from 1 to 300 are placed once each in some order. It turns out that for each number a, among the 15 nearest numbers to it in the clockwise direction, there are as many numbers less than a as there are among the 15 nearest numbers to it in the counterclockwise direction. A number that is greater than all 30 nearest numbers to it is called huge. What is the smallest possible number of huge numbers? (S. Berlov)

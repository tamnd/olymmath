Given that $x, y, z$ are non-negative real numbers, and $x+y+z=1$. Prove:
$$
x(1-2 x)(1-3 x)+y(1-2 y)(1-3 y)+z(1-2 z)(1-3 z) \geqslant 0 \text {, }
$$

and determine the necessary and sufficient conditions for equality.

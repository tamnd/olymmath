Let $p=p_1p_2\dots p_6$ be a permutation of the integers from $1$ to $6$ . For any such permutation $p$ , we count how many integers there are which have nothing bigger on its left. We let $f(p)$ be the number of these integers for the permutation $p$ . For example, $f(612345)=1$ because only $6$ has nothing to its left which is bigger. On the other hand, $f(135462)=4$ because only $1$ , $3$ , $5$ , and $6$ satisfy the condition.

Let $S$ be the sum of $f(p)$ over all the $6!$ different permutations. Find the sum of the digits of $S$ .

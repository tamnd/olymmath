We have a $4 \times 4$ square of pieces. We can flip all the pieces in a row, a column, or a diagonal (e.g., $(0,1),(1,0)$ is a diagonal, the corners as well). We start with the first two pieces of the first row on heads, the others on tails. Prove that it is impossible to have all pieces on tails.

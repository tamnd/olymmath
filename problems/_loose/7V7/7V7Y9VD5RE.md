Let $ABC$ be a triangle with all three angles acute. We denote $D$ as the foot of the altitude from $A$. We construct four points $H, E, F, G$ such that:

1. $DCGF$ and $BDEH$ are squares,
2. $E$ and $F$ lie on $(AD)$,
3. $H$ and $A$ are not on the same side of $(BC)$,
4. $G$ and $A$ are not on the same side of $(BC)$.

Show that the lines $(AD)$, $(BG)$, and $(HC)$ intersect at a single point.

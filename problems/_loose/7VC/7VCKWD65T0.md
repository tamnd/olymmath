Let $A B C$ be a right triangle with $C$ as the vertex of the right angle. In this triangle, let $C S$ be the median of $A B, C W$ the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$, and $C H$ the altitude to $A B$.

Prove that under these conditions, $\overline{\angle S C W}=\overline{\angle W C H}$ always holds!

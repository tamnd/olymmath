We have selected some proper subsets of a finite set $H$. We know that

a) any two different elements of the set are in exactly one selected subset;

b) for any selected subset $R$ and any element $p$ not in $R$, there are exactly $m$ selected subsets that contain $p$ and intersect $R$, and exactly $n$ selected subsets that contain $p$ and do not intersect $R$.

Express the number of elements of $H$ and the number of selected subsets in terms of $m$ and $n$. (H)

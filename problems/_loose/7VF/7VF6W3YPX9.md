Five students need to submit their homework to the teacher on a certain day. Each student places their homework on top of the pile when they submit it, and the teacher takes the topmost homework to grade whenever they have time. The five homework submissions are numbered $1, 2, 3, 4, 5$ in the order they are submitted. By noon, it is known that the 4th homework has already been graded. Therefore, the possible order in which the teacher may grade the remaining homework in the afternoon is $ $
(1) $5, 2, 3, 1$
(2) $5, 3, 2, 1$
(3) $5, 3, 1$
(4) $3, 5, 1$

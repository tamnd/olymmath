The people in an infinitely long line are numbered $1,2,3,\dots$ . Then, each person says either ``Karl" or ``Lark" independently and at random. Let $S$ be the set of all positive integers $i$ such that people $i$ , $i+1$ , and $i+2$ all say ``Karl," and define $X = \sum_{i \in S} 2^{-i}$ . Then the expected value of $X^2$ is $\frac{m}{n}$ , where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers with $\gcd(m,n)=1$ . Compute $100m+n$ .

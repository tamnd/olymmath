Color each vertex of a convex 2003-gon with one color, and ensure that any two adjacent vertices are of different colors. Prove that for any such coloring method, it is possible to completely dissect the polygon into several triangles using diagonals that do not intersect at interior points, such that each diagonal used in the dissection has endpoints of different colors.

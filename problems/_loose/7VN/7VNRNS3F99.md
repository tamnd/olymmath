A natural number $k, 4 \leqq k \leqq 900$, is given. Adam and Bára are playing a game: Adam writes $k$ different three-digit numbers on the board, and Bára selects four of them. If the difference between the two smallest and the difference between the two largest selected numbers is at most 22, Bára wins; otherwise, Adam wins. Determine, depending on the value of $k$, who has the winning strategy.

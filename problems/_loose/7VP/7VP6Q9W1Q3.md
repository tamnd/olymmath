Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be a sequence of independent random variables distributed exponentially with mean $1$. Suppose $\mathbb N$ is a random variable independent of
$X_i$'s that has a Poisson distribution with mean $\lambda>0$. What is the expected value of $X_1+X_2+\ldots+X_N$?
$\textbf{(A)}~N^2$
$\textbf{(B)}~\lambda+\lambda^2$
$\textbf{(C)}~\lambda^2$
$\textbf{(D)}~\lambda$

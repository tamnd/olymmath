3 In a regular $2 n$-sided polygon $(n \geqslant 2)$ inscribed in a circle, there is a frog on each vertex. At a certain moment, they all jump simultaneously from their original positions to one of the adjacent vertices (at this point, multiple frogs can land on the same vertex), which is called a jump. Try to find all possible values of $n$ such that for this $2 n$-sided polygon, if there exists a jump, after the jump, the line connecting any two different vertices with frogs does not pass through the center of the circle.

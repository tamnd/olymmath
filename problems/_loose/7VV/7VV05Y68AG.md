At a freestyle cycling competition, there were 100 judges. Each judge ranked all participants from the best to the worst according to their opinion. It turned out that for no three participants $A, B, C$ did there exist three judges, one of whom considered $A$ the best of the three and $B$ the worst, another considered $B$ the best and $C$ the worst, and the third considered $C$ the best and $A$ the worst. Prove that it is possible to create an overall ranking of participants such that for any two participants $A$ and $B$, the one who is higher in the ranking is better than the other according to at least half of the judges.

(I. Bogdanov)

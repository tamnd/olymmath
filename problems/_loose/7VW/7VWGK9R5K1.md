Lonely Cars. (From 9th grade, 4 points.) On a very long narrow highway, where overtaking is impossible, $n$ cars are driving in a random order, each with its own preferred speed. If a fast car catches up to a slower one, the fast car has to slow down and drive at the same speed as the slower one. Thus, the cars form groups. Find the expected number of "lonely" cars, that is, groups consisting of a single car.

Given a right triangular prism $A B C A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. A sphere with diameter $A_{1} B_{1}$ intersects the edges $A_{1} C_{1}$ and $B_{1} C_{1}$ at points $T_{1}$ and $L_{1}$, respectively, different from the vertices of the prism. Segments $B T_{1}$ and $A L_{1}$ intersect at point $S$, and $A L_{1}=7, S T_{1}=2$.

a) Find the angle $S T_{1} A_{1}$.

b) Find the ratio $A_{1} T_{1}: T_{1} C_{1}$.

c) Suppose it is additionally known that $A C=5$. Find the volume of the prism.

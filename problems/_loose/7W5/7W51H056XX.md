Try to find the smallest positive number $a$, such that there exists a positive number $b$, for which when $x \in [0,1]$, the inequality $\sqrt{1-x}+\sqrt{1+x} \leqslant 2 - b x^{a}$ always holds; for the obtained $a$, determine the largest positive number $b$ that satisfies the above inequality.

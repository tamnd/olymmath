Given a circle, a line passing through its center, and a point $P$ in the plane of the circle.

1. Construct the point $Q$ on the given circle such that $Q Q^{\prime}=P Q^{\prime}$, where $Q^{\prime}$ is the orthogonal projection of the point $Q$ onto the given line.
2. What is the geometric locus of those points $P$ for which the problem is solvable?

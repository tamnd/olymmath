For real numbers $ a,\ b$ such that $ |a|\neq |b|$, let $ I_n = \int \frac {1}{(a + b\cos \theta)^n}\ (n\geq 2)$.

Prove that : $ \boxed{\boxed{I_n = \frac {a}{a^2 - b^2}\cdot \frac {2n - 3}{n - 1}I_{n - 1} - \frac {1}{a^2 - b^2}\cdot\frac {n - 2}{n - 1}I_{n - 2} - \frac {b}{a^2 - b^2}\cdot\frac {1}{n - 1}\cdot \frac {\sin \theta}{(a + b\cos \theta)^{n - 1}}}}$

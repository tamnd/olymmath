$A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ are finite sets, satisfying
$$
\left|A_{i} \cap A_{i+1}\right|>\frac{n-2}{n-1}\left|A_{i+1}\right|, i=1,2, \cdots, n\left(A_{n+1}=A_{1}\right) \text {. }
$$

Prove: $\cap_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} \neq \varnothing$.

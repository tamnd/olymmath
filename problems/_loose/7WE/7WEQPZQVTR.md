Through the points $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$, lines are drawn forming angles $2 d-\alpha_{1}, 2 d-\alpha_{2}, 2 d-\alpha_{3}$ with $l$ respectively. Prove that these lines intersect at one point. All angles are measured from the line $l$ in the same direction. (d denotes the angle $90^{\circ}$ ed. note.)

In the acute triangle $A B C$, there are two points $E$ and $F$ on side $B C$ such that $\angle B A E = \angle C A F$. Draw $F M \perp A B$ and $F N \perp A C$ ($M$ and $N$ are the feet of the perpendiculars). Extend $A E$ to intersect the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ at point $D$. Prove: The area of quadrilateral $A M D N$ is equal to the area of triangle $A B C$.

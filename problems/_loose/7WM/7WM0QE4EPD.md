A Magician performs the following trick. He lays out 36 cards in a $6 \times 6$ square (6 columns of 6 cards) and asks the Spectator to select (mentally) a card and remember the column containing it. Then the Magician collects the cards, and again lays out the cards in a $6 \times 6$ square. After that the Magician asks the Spectator to tell which columns contained the selected card in the first and in the second case, and then guesses the selected card. Find a way for the Magician to perform this trick. (L. Emelyanov)

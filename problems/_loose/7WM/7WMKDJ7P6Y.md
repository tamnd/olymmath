Suppose that $a$ is a positive integer with $a>1$. Determine a closed form expression, in terms of $a$, equal to

$$
1+\frac{3}{a}+\frac{5}{a^{2}}+\frac{7}{a^{3}}+\cdots
$$

(The infinite sum includes exactly the fractions of the form $\frac{2 k-1}{a^{k-1}}$ for each positive integer $k$.)

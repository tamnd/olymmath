Given $n$ non-negative numbers. We can choose any two of them, say $a$ and $b$, $a \leqq b$, and replace them with the numbers 0 and $b-a$. Prove that by repeating this operation, all the given numbers can be changed to zeros if and only if the original numbers can be divided into two groups such that the sums of the numbers in both groups are the same.

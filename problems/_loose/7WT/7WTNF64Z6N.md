In the school garden, a group of students is playing a game called molecules. The teacher first instructed them to divide into trios. One student was left over and thus was out of the next game. The remaining students were then supposed to divide into quartets. Again, one student was left over and was out. Then the remaining students were supposed to divide into quintets, and once again, one student was left over and was out. The teacher now orders the remaining students to divide into sextets. Prove that one student will again be left over.

Let real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy $|xyz|=1$. Prove:
$$
\sum\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}+x+1}+\frac{1}{x^{2}-x+1}\right) \leqslant 4 \text {. }
$$
(2016, China Mathematical Olympiad Hope Alliance Summer Camp)

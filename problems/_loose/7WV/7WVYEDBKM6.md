Friends Vasya, Petya, and Kolya live in the same house. One day, Vasya and Petya went fishing at the lake on foot. Kolya stayed home, promising to meet his friends on a bicycle on their way back. Vasya was the first to head home, and at the same time, Kolya set out on a bicycle to meet him. Petya, moving at the same speed as Vasya, left the lake for home at the moment Kolya and Vasya met. Kolya, upon meeting Vasya, immediately turned around and took him home, and then immediately set off again on the bicycle towards the lake. Meeting Petya, Kolya turned around again and took his friend home. As a result, the time Petya spent on the journey from the lake to home was $4 / 3$ of the time Vasya spent on the same journey. How many times slower would Vasya have reached home if he had walked the entire way? (8 points)

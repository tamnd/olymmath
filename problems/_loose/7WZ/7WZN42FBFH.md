A "mathemagician" performs magic with green, yellow, blue, and red cards, numbered from 1 to 13 for each color. He shuffles the cards and says to a child: "Without me seeing, choose a card, calculate double the number on the card, add 3, and multiply the result by 5. Then

add 1, if the card is green;

add 2, if the card is yellow;

add 3, if the card is blue;

add 4, if the card is red.

Tell me the final result, and I will tell you the color and the number of the card you chose."

a) Joãozinho chose the red card with the number 3. What number should he tell the mathemagician?
b) Mariazinha said "seventy-six" to the mathemagician. What is the number and the color of the card she chose?

c) After choosing a card, Pedrinho said "sixty-one" and the mathemagician replied "You made a mistake in your calculation." Explain how the mathemagician could have known this.

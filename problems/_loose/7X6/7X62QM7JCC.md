Let $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ be a rectangular parallelepiped and $M, N, P$ the projections of points $A, C$ and $B^{\prime}$ respectively onto the diagonal $B D^{\prime}$.

a) Show that $B M+B N+B P=B D^{\prime}$.

b) Prove that $3\left(A M^{2}+B^{\prime} P^{2}+C N^{2}\right) \geq 2 D^{\prime} B^{2}$ if and only if the rectangular parallelepiped $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is a cube.

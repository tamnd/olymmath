In the plane, there are $n$ points $(n \geq 3)$. The maximum length of the segments determined by all possible pairs of these points is $d$. We call those segments whose length is exactly $d$ the diameters of the given point system. Prove that there are at most $n$ such diameters.

(International Mathematical Olympiad problem, 1965. CMO6523)

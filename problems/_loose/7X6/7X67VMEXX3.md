Let $a, b, c, d$ be integers such that the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a x+b y=m \\
& c x+d y=n
\end{aligned}
$$

has integer solutions for all integer values of $m$ and $n$. Prove that $|a d-b c|=1$.

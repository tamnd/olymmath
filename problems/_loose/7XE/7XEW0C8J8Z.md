Consider a triangle $ABC$ with circumcenter $O$ and let $A_1,B_1,C_1$ be the midpoints of the sides $BC,AC,AB,$ respectively.
Points $A_2,B_2,C_2$ are defined as $\overrightarrow{OA_2}=\lambda \cdot \overrightarrow{OA_1}, \ \overrightarrow{OB_2}=\lambda \cdot \overrightarrow{OB_1}, \ \overrightarrow{OC_2}=\lambda \cdot \overrightarrow{OC_1},$ where $\lambda >0.$ Prove that lines $AA_2,BB_2,CC_2$ are concurrent.

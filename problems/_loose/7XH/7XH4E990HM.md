Proposition For two circles, allowing a quadrilateral to be inscribed in the first circle and circumscribed around the second circle, $r_{1} 、 r_{2}$ are the radii of the two circles, and $d$ is the distance between the centers, then
$$
\frac{1}{\left(r_{1}-d\right)^{2}}+\frac{1}{\left(r_{1}+d\right)^{2}}=\frac{1}{r_{2}^{2}} \text {. }
$$

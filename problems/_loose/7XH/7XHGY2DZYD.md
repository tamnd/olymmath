On the table, there are $n$ cardboard and $n$ plastic squares, and no two cardboard squares and no two plastic squares share any points, including boundary points. It turns out that the set of vertices of the cardboard squares coincides with the set of vertices of the plastic squares. Is it necessarily true that each cardboard square coincides with some plastic square?

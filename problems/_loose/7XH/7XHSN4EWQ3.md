Given a $(2 n-1)$-gon $A_{1} \ldots A_{2 n-1}$ and a point $O$. The lines $A_{k} O$ and $A_{n+k-1} A_{n+k}$ intersect at point $B_{k}$. Prove that the product of the ratios $A_{n+k-1} B_{k} / A_{n+k} B_{k}(k=1, \ldots, n)$ equals 1.

Let's color the vertices of a 2018-gon in two colors such that any two adjacent vertices are of different colors. If the sum of the angles at the vertices of one color is equal to the sum of the angles at the vertices of the other color, we will call such a 2018-gon interesting. In a convex 2019-gon, one vertex is marked. It turns out that removing any unmarked vertex leaves an interesting 2018-gon. Prove that removing the marked vertex also leaves an interesting 2018-gon.

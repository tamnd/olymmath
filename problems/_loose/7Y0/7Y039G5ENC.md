In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, the plane angles at vertex $D$ are right angles. Let $\angle C A D=\alpha, \angle C B D=\beta$, and $\angle A C B=\varphi$. Prove that $\cos \varphi=\sin \alpha \sin \beta$.

Given a $101 \times 101$ grid, all cells of which are painted white. It is allowed to choose several rows and repaint all cells in these rows to black. Then, choose exactly the same number of columns and repaint all cells in these columns to the opposite color (i.e., white to black, and black to white). What is the maximum number of black cells that the grid can contain after this operation?

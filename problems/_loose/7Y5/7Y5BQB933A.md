C3 Let $A B C D$ be a parallelogram. We draw in the diagonal $A C$. A circle is drawn inside $\triangle A B C$ tangent to all three sides and touches side $A C$ at a point $P$.
(a) (2 marks) Prove that $D A+A P=D C+C P$.
(b) (4 marks) Draw in the line $D P$. A circle of radius $r_{1}$ is drawn inside $\triangle D A P$ tangent to all three sides. A circle of radius $r_{2}$ is drawn inside $\triangle D C P$ tangent to all three sides. Prove that
$$
\frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}=\frac{A P}{P C} .
$$
(c) (4 marks) Suppose $D A+D C=3 A C$ and $D A=D P$. Let $r_{1}, r_{2}$ be the two radii defined in (b). Determine the ratio $r_{1} / r_{2}$.

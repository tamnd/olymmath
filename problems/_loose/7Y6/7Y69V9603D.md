Let $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be i.i.d. non-negative random variables and $\tau$ be a random variable independent of $\left(\xi_{n}\right)$ and taking values in $\mathbb{N}$.

(a) Establish that $\mathrm{E} S_{\tau}=\mathrm{E} \tau \mathrm{E} \xi_{1}$, where $S_{\tau}=\xi_{1}+\ldots+\xi_{\tau}$ and infinite values of the expectations are allowed.

(b) Assuming that $\mathrm{E} \xi_{1}=\infty$, show that

$$
\varliminf_{x \rightarrow \infty} \frac{\mathrm{P}\left(S_{\tau}>x\right)}{\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1}>x\right)}=\mathrm{E} \tau
$$

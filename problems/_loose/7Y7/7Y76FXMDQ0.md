Perhaps you have heard of Giovanni Ceva's theorem, an Italian mathematician of the 17th century. Here is what Ceva proved in 1678: if $P$ is any interior point of triangle $A B C$ and the lines $A P$, $B P$, and $C P$ intersect the sides of the triangle at points $X, Y$, and $Z$ respectively, then

$$
\frac{B X}{C X} \cdot \frac{C Y}{A Y} \cdot \frac{A Z}{B Z}=1
$$

Try to prove this theorem on your own.

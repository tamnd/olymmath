We have just once again recalled that a line passing through the vertex of the base and the midpoint of the lateral side of a triangle cuts off one third of the median of the base. This statement remains valid if the expressions "median of the base" and "lateral side" are swapped, that is:

A line passing through the vertex of the base and the midpoint of the median of the base cuts off one third of the lateral side (of the triangle).

Prove this theorem. (U. 119, P. 129.)

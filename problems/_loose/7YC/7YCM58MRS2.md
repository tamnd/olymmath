Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $P$ and $Q$ be two distinct real polynomials of degree $n$.

Show that

$$
\operatorname{deg}\left(P^{3}-Q^{3}\right) \geqslant 2 n
$$

and provide an example where equality is achieved.

Does the result remain true if we consider complex polynomials?

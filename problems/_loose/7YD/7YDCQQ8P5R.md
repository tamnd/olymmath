Given $H$ is the orthocenter of acute $\triangle A B C$, $A B \neq A C$, a point $F$ different from point $A$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, $\angle A F H=90^{\circ}$, point $K$ is symmetric to $H$ with respect to $B$, and point $P$ satisfies $\angle P H B=\angle P B C=90^{\circ}$. Draw a perpendicular from point $B$ to $C P$, and let the foot of the perpendicular be $Q$. Prove: $H Q$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle F H K$. ${ }^{[1]}$
(2018, Serbia National Team Selection Exam)

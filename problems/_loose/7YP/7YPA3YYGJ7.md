On the diameter $A B$ of the base circle, we mark the point $M$, and from the point $M$, we draw a line parallel to $S A$, which intersects $S B$ at point $N$. We draw a plane through the line $M N$, which is perpendicular to the plane $A S B$ and intersects the base circle of the cone in $P M Q$. $A B=2 R$, $S A=a$.

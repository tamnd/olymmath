Suppose the function $f(x)$ is differentiable in $(-\infty,+\infty)$, $f(0)=1$, and for any $x_{1}, x_{2}$, we have
$$
f\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right) \geqslant f\left(x_{1}\right) f\left(x_{2}\right),
$$

Try to find $f(x)$.

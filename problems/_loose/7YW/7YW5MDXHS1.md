(2008 National Training Team Test Question) Let the integer $n>1, n$ divides $2^{\alpha(n)}+3^{\kappa(n)}+\cdots+$
Olympic
$n^{\varphi(n)}$, where $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{k}$ are all distinct prime factors of $n$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{p_{k}}+\frac{1}{p_{1} p_{2} \cdots p_{k}}
$$

is an integer [here $\varphi(n)$ denotes the number of positive integers up to $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$].

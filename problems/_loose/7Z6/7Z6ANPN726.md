Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle. The circle centered at $A$ that touches side $B C$ intersects side $A B$ at point $B_{1}$ and side $C A$ at point $C_{2}$. The circle centered at $B$ that touches side $C A$ intersects side $B C$ at point $C_{1}$ and side $A B$ at point $A_{2}$. The circle centered at $C$ that touches side $A B$ intersects side $C A$ at point $A_{1}$ and side $B C$ at point $B_{2}$. Prove that the triangle determined by the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}$, and $C_{1} C_{2}$ is similar to triangle $A B C$.

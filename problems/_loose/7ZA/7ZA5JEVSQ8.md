Two secants are drawn through a point $A$ outside a circle. One secant intersects the circle at points $B$ and $C$ such that $AB = 6$ , $AC = 10$ , the other secant intersects at points $D$ and $E$ ( $D$ is between points $A$ and $E$ ). A line drawn through point $D$ parallel to line $AC$ intersects the circle at point $F$ (here $F \ne D$ ). Line $FA$ intersects the circle at point $G$ (here $G\ne F$ ), and lines $EG$ and $AC$ intersect at point $M$ . Find the length of segment $AM$ .

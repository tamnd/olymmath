Let $\mathcal{G}=(A \cup B, E)$ be a bipartite graph where the two independent components, $A$ and $B$, are countably infinite. Suppose that every vertex has a finite non-zero degree and that, for every edge $(a, b) \in E$ with $a \in A$ and $b \in B, \operatorname{deg}(a)=\operatorname{deg}(b)$. Show that there exists a bijective saturating matching $f: A \rightarrow B$.

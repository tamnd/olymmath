a) Let $H$ be the point of intersection of the altitudes of an orthocentric tetrahedron, $M'$ be the centroid of any face, and $N$ be the point of intersection of the ray $H M'$ with the circumscribed sphere of the tetrahedron. Prove that $H M' : M' N = 1 : 2$.

b) Let $M$ be the centroid of the orthocentric tetrahedron, $H'$ be the point of intersection of the altitudes of any face, and $N$ be the point of intersection of the ray $H' M$ with the circumscribed sphere of the tetrahedron. Prove that $H' M : M N = 1 : 3$.

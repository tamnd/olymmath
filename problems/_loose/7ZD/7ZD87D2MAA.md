Given that $A$ is a point outside $\odot O$, a secant line through $A$ intersects $\odot O$ at points $B, C$, with point $B$ lying on the segment $A C$ internally. Two tangents are drawn from point $A$ to $\odot O$, touching the circle at points $S, T$. Let $A C$ intersect $S T$ at point $P$. Prove: $\frac{A P}{P C}=2 \cdot \frac{A B}{B C}$.
(21st Nordic Competition Problem)

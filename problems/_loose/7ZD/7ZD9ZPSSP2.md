We will call two numbers almost equal to each other if they are equal to each other or differ from each other by no more than one. A rectangular grid with sides of natural numbers a and b is such that it is impossible to cut out a rectangle along the grid lines, the area of which is almost equal to half the area of the original rectangle. What is the smallest value that the number $|a-b|$ can take? (E. Molchanov, S. Berlov)

Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be a convex cyclic polygon, and let $A$ be a point on its circumcircle different from the vertices. Let $a_{i}=A A_{i}$ $(i=1,2, \ldots, n)$, and let $b_{i}$ be the distance from the point $A$ to the line $A_{i} A_{i+1}$ $\left(A_{n+1}=A_{1}\right)$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a_{1}^{2}}{b_{1}}+\frac{a_{2}^{2}}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{b_{n}} \geq 2 n R
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle.

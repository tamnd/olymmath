Given $\triangle A B C, A B=c, B C=a, A C=b, b \geqslant a$. Point $O$ is the center of the incircle $\Gamma$ of $\triangle A B C$, the excircle of $\triangle A B C$ opposite to angle $C$ touches $A B$ at point $P$, point $M$ is the midpoint of $A B$, and point $Q$ is the intersection of line $M O$ with $B C$.
(1) Prove that $P M=\frac{1}{2}(b-a)$; (2) Find the length of $P Q$.
(1994 Spring Bulgarian 9th Grade Competition Problem)

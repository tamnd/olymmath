On a board, a convex $n$-gon ($n \geqslant 4$) is drawn. Each of its vertices must be painted either black or white. A diagonal is called bicolored if its ends are painted in different colors. A coloring of the vertices is called good if the $n$-gon can be divided into triangles by bicolored diagonals that do not have common points (except vertices). Find the number of good colorings.

(S. Berlov)

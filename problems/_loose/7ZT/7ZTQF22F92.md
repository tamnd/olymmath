In an election, there are 2042 candidates and voters. Each voter ranks the candidates in order of preference.

A directed graph of 2042 vertices is formed, with an arrow from $U$ to $V$ when candidate $U$ is placed above $V$ by more than half of the votes. In case of a tie, there are no edges.

Is it possible to obtain all complete connected directed graphs?

Let $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ be two triangles with sides $a_{1}, b_{1}, c_{1}$, and $a_{2}, b_{2}, c_{2}$, respectively, and let $a=\sqrt{\frac{a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}}{2}}$, $b=\sqrt{\frac{b_{1}^{2}+b_{2}^{2}}{2}}, c=\sqrt{\frac{c_{1}^{2}+c_{2}^{2}}{2}} . $ Prove that there exists a triangle $T$ with sides $a, b, c$ and that, if $S_{1}, S_{2}$ and $S$ are the areas of triangles $T_{1}, T_{2}$ and $T$, then

a) $S \geqslant \sqrt{S_{1} S_{2}}$

b) $S \geqslant \frac{1}{2}\left(S_{1}+S_{2}\right)$.

In what cases do the inequalities in parts a) and b) become equalities?

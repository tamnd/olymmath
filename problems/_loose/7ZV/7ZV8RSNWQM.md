$A B C$ is a triangle with coordinates $A=(2,6), B=(0,0)$, and $C=(14,0)$.
(a) Let $P$ be the midpoint of $A B$. Determine the equation of the line perpendicular to $A B$ passing through $P$.
(b) Let $Q$ be the point on line $B C$ for which $P Q$ is perpendicular to $A B$. Determine the length of $A Q$.
(c) There is a (unique) circle passing through the points $A, B$, and $C$. Determine the radius of this circle.

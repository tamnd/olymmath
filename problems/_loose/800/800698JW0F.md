Let $a, b, x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers, and suppose $\frac{a}{b}<\frac{x}{y}$.

Prove that under these assumptions, it always follows that

$$
\frac{a}{b}<\frac{a+x}{b+y}<\frac{x}{y}
$$

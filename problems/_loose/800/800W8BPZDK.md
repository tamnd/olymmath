Let $a$ be the larger leg of a right triangle, $b$ the smaller one, and $c$ the hypotenuse. It is to be proven that

$$
a+\frac{b}{2}>c>\frac{8}{9}\left(a+\frac{b}{2}\right)
$$

(From the examination problems of József Kürschák, a university teacher in Budapest.)

Let $O x y z$ be a Cartesian coordinate system in space, $S$ be a finite set of points in space, and $S_{x}, S_{y}, S_{z}$ be the sets of orthogonal projections of all points in $S$ onto the $O y z$ plane, $O z x$ plane, and $O x y$ plane, respectively. Prove that:
$$
|S|^{2} \leqslant\left|S_{x}\right| \cdot\left|S_{y}\right| \cdot\left|S_{z}\right|,
$$
where $|A|$ denotes the number of elements in the finite set $A$. (Note: The orthogonal projection of a point onto a plane is the foot of the perpendicular from the point to the plane.)

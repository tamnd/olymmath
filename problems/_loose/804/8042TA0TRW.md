Petya has 10000 weights, and no two weights are of equal mass. He also has a magic device: if you place 10 weights in it, it will report the sum of the masses of two of them (but it is unknown which two exactly). Prove that Petya can use the magic device in such a way that after some time, he can point to one of the weights and accurately name its mass. (You cannot place a different number of weights in the magic device.) (S. Berlov, T. Korotchenko)

Given a cubic equation
$$
x^{3}+A x^{2}+B x+C=0(A, B, C \in \mathbf{R})
$$

with three roots $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$. Find the minimum value of $\frac{1+|A|+|B|+|C|}{|\alpha|+|\beta|+|\gamma|}$.

Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Prove that $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f(x^n) \ dx=f(0).$$Furthermore, if $f(0)=0$ and $f$ is right-differentiable in $0{}$, prove that the limits $$\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\int_\varepsilon^1\frac{f(x)}{x} \ dx \text{and} \lim_{n\to\infty}\left(n\int_0^1f(x^n) \ dx\right)$$exist, are finite and are equal.

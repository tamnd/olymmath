Consider the real numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x=\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{2^{2}}+\sqrt{2^{3}}+\ldots .+\sqrt{2^{2014}} \\
& y=2+2^{2}+2^{3}+\ldots .+2^{2014}
\end{aligned}
$$

a) Show that $ x=(2+\sqrt{2})\left(2^{1007}-1\right)$

b) Show that the number $n=2\left(\frac{y}{x} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{\sqrt{2}}-1\right)$ is a perfect square.

Let's prove the inequalities (5) and (6) using the general theorem mentioned above and without using complete induction.

$$
\sqrt{\frac{x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\ldots+x_{k}^{2}}{k}}>\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k}}{k}
$$

if the $x$'s are not all equal. If the $x$'s are also all positive, then

$$
\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k}}{k}>\frac{k}{\frac{1}{x_{1}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{k}}}
$$

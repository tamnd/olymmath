(Vietnam MO 2019) Let $ABC$ be a non-isosceles triangle and let $O$ be the center of its circumcircle. Let $M, N,$ and $P$ be the midpoints of the sides $[BC], [CA],$ and $[AB]$. Let $D, E,$ and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from vertices $A, B,$ and $C$. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$. Let $K$ be the reflection of point $H$ over the line $(BC)$. The lines $(DE)$ and $(MP)$ intersect at point $X$. The lines $(DF)$ and $(MN)$ intersect at point $Y$.

1) The line $(XY)$ intersects the minor arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ at point $Z$. Show that the points $K, Z, E,$ and $F$ are concyclic.
2) The lines $(KE)$ and $(KF)$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at points $S$ and $T$ respectively. Show that the lines $(BS), (CT),$ and $(XY)$ are concurrent.

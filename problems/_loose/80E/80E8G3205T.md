Let $d$ be a real number. For each integer $m \geq 0,$ define a sequence $\left\{a_{m}(j)\right\}, j=0,1,2, \ldots$ by the condition
\begin{align*}
a_{m}(0)&=d / 2^{m},\\
a_{m}(j+1)&=\left(a_{m}(j)\right)^{2}+2 a_{m}(j), j \geq 0.
\end{align*}
Evaluate $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n}(n).$

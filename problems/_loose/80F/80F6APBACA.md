On a small island $O$ (we will neglect the size of the island in the following), there is a searchlight illuminating a 1 km segment of the sea surface; this searchlight rotates, making one revolution per minute. A boat must sneak up to the island $O$ without being detected (i.e., without ever entering the searchlight beam):

a) Prove that if the speed of the boat is less than $\boldsymbol{800 \mathrm{m} / \mathrm{min}}$, it will not be able to approach the island undetected.

b) ** Determine for which values of the speed $v$ the boat can accomplish its task and approach the island undetected.

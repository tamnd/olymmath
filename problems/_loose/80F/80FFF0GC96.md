Let $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$ be an acute, non-isosceles triangle, and let $T_{i}$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $A_{i}$. Let $B_{i}$ be the intersection of the lines $A_{j} A_{k}$ and $T_{j} T_{k}$ (where $i, j$, and $k$ are distinct). Show that the points $B_{1}$, $B_{2}$, and $B_{3}$ lie on a straight line.

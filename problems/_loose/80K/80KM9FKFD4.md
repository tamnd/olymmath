At Zé's office, there are six pieces of chains with the following number of links: $10, 10, 8, 8, 5$ and 2. He needs to join these pieces to form a circular chain. He spends 1 minute to cut a link and 2 minutes to join it, totaling 3 minutes per link. If he cuts a link at the end of each separate piece, joining the pieces one at a time, he would take $6 \cdot 3 = 18$ minutes. However, since he is in a hurry, he intends to perform this operation in a faster way.

a) Explain how he can form the circular chain in just 15 minutes.

b) Is it possible for him to complete this operation in less than 15 minutes?

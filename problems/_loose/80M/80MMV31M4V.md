Let $n$ be a positive integer. A hotel has $n$ rooms numbered $1, 2, \dots, n$ . For each $i = 1, 2, \dots, n$ , room $i$ can accommodate exactly $i$ people. There are $n$ families arriving at the hotel, and each family has informed the hotel in advance of its size. The hotel must assign a room to each family so that every family can be accommodated. It is known that there exists at least one such assignment. Let $A$ denote the number of possible assignment methods. Prove that $A$ can take at most $2^{n-1}$ distinct values.

Let $n \geqslant 2$. For the sets of $n$ complex numbers
$$
\begin{array}{r}
A=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}, B=\left\{b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}\right\}, \\
\text { prove: } \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(a_{k}+b_{i}\right)}{\prod_{\substack{i=1 \\
i \neq k}}^{n}\left(a_{k}-a_{i}\right)}=\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{\prod_{\substack{i=1 \\
i=1 \\
i \neq k}}^{n}\left(b_{k}+a_{i}\right)}{n} .
\end{array}
$$

This problem was created by the author in 2005 as a training question for the national team.

At first glance, the structure of this problem seems daunting, but in fact, this problem was derived from the construction and solution of a special class of $n$-variable linear equations using Cramer's rule, utilizing the equality of an $n$-order determinant and its transpose.

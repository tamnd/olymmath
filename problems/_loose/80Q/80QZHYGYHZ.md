There are 40 knights in a kingdom. Every morning they fight in pairs (everyone has exactly one enemy to fight with) and every evening they sit around a table (during the evening they do not change their sits). Find the least number of days such that:

a) the fights can be arranged in a way that every two knights have fought at least once;

b) round the table arrangements can be done in a way that every two knights have been neighbors around the table.

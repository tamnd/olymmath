Four, (20 points) Given the function $f(x)=\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{x}-\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^{x}$. For any $x_{1} 、 x_{2} \in[1,+\infty)$, and $x_{1} < x_{2}$, prove that:
(1) $f\left(x_{1}\right) > f\left(x_{2}\right)$;
(2) $f\left(\sqrt{x_{1} x_{2}}\right) > \sqrt{f\left(x_{1}\right) f\left(x_{2}\right)}$.

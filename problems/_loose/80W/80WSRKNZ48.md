During a physical education class, $n$ children stand in a line facing the teacher. On a given signal, each of them turns $90^{\circ}$, some to the left, others to the right. Then, on a command, all those who are facing any of their neighbors turn around. Prove that by the $(n+1)$-th command, no one will move anymore.

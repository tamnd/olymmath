Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots$ be i.i.d. random variables with the Cauchy distribution with density

$$
\frac{1}{\pi\left(1+x^{2}\right)}, x \in \mathbb{R}
$$

Show that

$$
\frac{M_{n}}{n} \xrightarrow{d} \frac{1}{T}
$$

where $M_{n}=\max \left\{X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}\right\}$, and the random variable $T$ has an exponential distribution with parameter $1 / \pi$.

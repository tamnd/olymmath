$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + w^2 = 4$$
$$\frac{1}{x^2} +\frac{1}{y^2} +\frac{1}{z^2 }+\frac{1}{w^2} = 5 -\frac{1}{(xyzw)^2}$$

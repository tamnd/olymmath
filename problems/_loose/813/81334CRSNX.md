Let $A F B D C E$ be a convex inscribed hexagon (i.e., whose vertices lie on the same circle) such that

$$
\widehat{B A D}=\widehat{D A C}, \widehat{C B E}=\widehat{E B A} \text { and } \widehat{A C F}=\widehat{F C B}
$$

Show that the lines $(A D)$ and $(E F)$ are perpendicular to each other.

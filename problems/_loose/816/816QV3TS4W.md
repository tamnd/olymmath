Let $A B C D$ be any quadrilateral. Its side lengths are $a, b, c, d$; its area is $F$. Prove that the following inequality (1) always holds:

$$
F \leq \frac{1}{4}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}\right)
$$

Furthermore, determine all quadrilaterals for which the equality sign holds in (1).

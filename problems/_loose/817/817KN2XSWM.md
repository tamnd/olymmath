A rectangle has sides $e, f, g, h (e \| g)$. For every point in the plane, we take the smaller of the distances measured from $e$ and $g$, and the smaller of the distances measured from $f$ and $h$. What is the geometric locus of points (in the plane of the rectangle) for which the sum of these two smaller distances is equal to a given segment?

Given that $c$ is a positive integer, define the sequence of positive integers
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{1}=c, \\
a_{n+1}=a_{n}^{3}-4 c a_{n}^{2}+5 c^{2} a_{n}+c (n=1,2, \cdots) .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: For each integer $n \geqslant 2$, there exists a prime $p$ that divides $a_{n}$, but does not divide $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n-1}$.

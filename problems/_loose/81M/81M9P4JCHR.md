What is the value of the sum $A+B$, if

$$
\frac{1}{A}=1-\frac{1-x}{y}, \frac{1}{B}=1-\frac{y}{1-x}, x=\frac{1-a}{1-\frac{1}{a}}, y=1-\frac{1}{x}
$$

and $a \neq \pm 1$.

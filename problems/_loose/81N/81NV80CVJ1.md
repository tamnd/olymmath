Let $M=\{1,2, \cdots, 20\}$. For any 9-element subset $s$ of $M$, $f(s)$ takes an integer from 1 to 20 $(1 \leqslant$ $f(s) \leqslant 20)$. Prove: There exists a 10-element subset $T$ of $M$, such that for all $k \in T$, $f(T \backslash\{k\}) \neq k$.

From three given lengths $c=8 \mathrm{~cm}, s_{a}=6 \mathrm{~cm}, s_{b}=7 \mathrm{~cm}$, a triangle $A B C$ is to be constructed. The requirements are:

(1) The side $A B$ has the length $\overline{A B}=c$.

(2) The median $A D$ of side $B C$ has the length $\overline{A D}=s_{a}$.

(3) The median $B E$ of side $A C$ has the length $\overline{B E}=s_{b}$.

a) Construct a triangle and describe your construction!

b) Prove: If a triangle is constructed according to your description, then it satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3).

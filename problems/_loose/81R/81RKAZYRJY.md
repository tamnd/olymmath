The diameter of $\odot O$ is $A B$, $\triangle A B C$ is an equilateral triangle, $D$ is on $A B$, $A D = \frac{2}{n} A B$ ($n$ is a constant), the line $C D$ intersects $\odot O$ at $E$, $\angle A O E = \alpha$ (Figure 4.8), prove:
$$
\tan \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{n^{2} + 16 n - 32} - n}{n - 4} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} .
$$

Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$.

Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of an acute triangle $ABC$. The line passing through $O$ and parallel to $BC$ intersects $AB$ and $AC$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. It is known that the sum of the distances from point $O$ to sides $AB$ and $AC$ is equal to $OA$. Prove that the sum of segments $PB$ and $QC$ is equal to $PQ$.

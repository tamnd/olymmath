Let $x_{i}$ and $y_{i}$ be positive numbers, and $p$ and $q$ be rational numbers related by the equation $1 / p + 1 / q = 1$. Prove that if $p > 1$, then

$$
x_{1} y_{1} + \ldots + x_{n} y_{n} \leqslant \left(x_{1}^{p} + \ldots + x_{n}^{p}\right)^{1 / p} \left(y_{1}^{q} + \ldots + y_{n}^{q}\right)^{1 / q}
$$

and if $p < 1$, then

$$
x_{1} y_{1} + \ldots + x_{n} y_{n} \geqslant \left(x_{1}^{p} + \ldots + x_{n}^{p}\right)^{1 / p} \left(y_{1}^{q} + \ldots + y_{n}^{q}\right)^{1 / q}
$$

(Hölder's inequality).

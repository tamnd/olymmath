Koschei imprisoned a crowd of captives and told them: "Tomorrow you will face a trial. I will choose several of you (whomever I wish, but at least three), seat you around a round table in some order (whichever I desire), and stick a piece of paper with a figure on each of your foreheads. The figures may repeat, but no two different figures will be stuck on the same number of people. Each of you will see the figures on others, but not your own. Exchanging any signals is forbidden. After that, I will remove the stickers and order you to be taken to separate cells. There, each of you must draw the figure on a piece of paper. If at least one draws the figure that was on their forehead, all of you will be released. Otherwise, you will remain here forever."

How should the captives agree to act to save themselves?

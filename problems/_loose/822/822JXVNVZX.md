In the plane, an isosceles triangle $K L M$ is given with base $K L$. Consider any two circles $k$ and $l$ that are externally tangent to each other and which touch the lines $K M$ and $L M$ at points $K$ and $L$, respectively. Determine the set of tangency points $T$ of all such circles $k$ and $l$.

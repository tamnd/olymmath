Given integers $b, c$ and a natural number $N \geqslant 2$. Prove: there exist integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{N+1}$, such that
$$
f\left(x_{N+1}\right)=\prod_{i=1}^{N} f\left(x_{i}\right) \text {, }
$$

where, $f(x)=x^{2}+b x+c$.

This time, you find yourself on the island of Bahava, where knights always tell the truth, liars always lie, and normal people sometimes tell the truth and sometimes lie. Let's recall that on the island of Bahava, women enjoy equal rights with men. Among the women, as among the men, there are knights, liars, and normal people. As a foreigner, you are not subject to the laws of the island, and in particular, the royal decree that knights must marry only knights and liars must marry only liars does not apply to you: you are free to choose any islander as your wife, regardless of who she is.

Suppose you are to choose one of three sisters, A, B, and C, as your bride. It is known that one of them is a knight, one is a liar, and one is a normal person. It is also known that the normal sister (what a position you are in!) is a werewolf, while the other two sisters are not werewolves. Suppose you would not refuse to marry a liar (or a knight), but marrying a werewolf is too much even for such a flexible person as you! To determine who is who among the sisters, you are allowed to ask them one single question, to which they can answer either "yes" or "no."

What question would you ask?

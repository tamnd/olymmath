Let the set $I=\{1,2,3, \cdots, n\}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$, choose two non-empty subsets $A$ and $B$ of $I$, such that the smallest number in $B$ is greater than the largest number in $A$, denote the number of different selection methods as $a_{n}$, obviously $a_{1}=0, a_{2}=\mathrm{C}_{2}^{2}=1$.
(1) Find $a_{n}$;
(2) Let the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be $S_{n}$, find $S_{n}$.

A game consists of 10 rolls of a normal cubic die with faces numbered from 1 to 6. At the end, the scores obtained are summed, with the rule that if a 6 is rolled, the points of the next roll are counted doubled, and if a 6 is rolled on the last roll, the player gets one (only one) additional roll to add to the score (not doubled). Which of these final scores cannot be obtained?
(A) 64
(B) 78
(C) 92
(D) 114
(E) 120.

Number 7. (2006 Czech-Polish-Slovak Mathematical Olympiad) Prove that for every integer $k(k \geqslant 1)$, there exists a positive integer $n$ such that:
When $2^{n}$ is expressed in decimal notation, it can contain exactly $k$ consecutive 0's, i.e., $2^{n}=\cdots a \underbrace{00 \cdots 0 b \cdots}_{k \uparrow}$, where $a$ and $b$ are non-zero digits.

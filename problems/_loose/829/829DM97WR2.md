A crazy bishop is a piece that can face one of the four diagonally adjacent squares and attacks all squares directly in front of it as well as directly to the left and right (like a chess bishop that cannot see behind itself). Two squares of the game board are said to be diagonally adjacent if they share exactly one common vertex.

Determine the largest natural number $N$ for which it is possible to place $N$ crazy bishops on an $8 \times 8$ game board such that none of them attack each other.

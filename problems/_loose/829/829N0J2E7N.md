All members of an infinite arithmetic progression are natural numbers. In each term, it was possible to underline one or several consecutive digits so that in the first term, the digit 1 was underlined, in the second term, the digit 2, ..., in the 23rd term, the digits 2 and 3 consecutively, and so on (for any natural number $n$, in the $n$-th term, the underlined digits formed the number $n$). Prove that the common difference of the progression is a power of 10.

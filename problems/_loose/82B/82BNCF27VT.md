We say that a positive integer $k$ is tricubic if there are three positive integers $a, b, c,$ not necessarily different, such that $k=a^3+b^3+c^3.$ a) Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ that satisfy the following condition: exactly one of the three numbers $n, n+2$ and $n+28$ is tricubic.
b) Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ that satisfy the following condition: exactly two of the three numbers $n, n+2$ and $n+28$ are tricubic.
c) Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ that satisfy the following condition: the three numbers $n, n+2$ and $n+28$ are tricubic.

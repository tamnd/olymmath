In a group of six people, there are exactly 11 pairs of acquaintances. The relationship "to know each other" is mutual, meaning if person $A$ knows person $B$, then $B$ also knows $A$. If anyone in the group learns some news, they will tell it to all their acquaintances. Prove that in this way, everyone will eventually learn the news.

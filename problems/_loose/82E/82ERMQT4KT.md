Let triangle $ABC$ be given. Let $L$ and $M$ be the points where the internal and external angle bisectors of angle $C$ intersect the line $AB$. If $|CL|=|CM|$, prove that

$$
|AC|^{2}+|BC|^{2}=4 R^{2}
$$

where $R$ is the length of the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

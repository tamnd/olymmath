Determine all pairs $(p, q)$ of two prime numbers that satisfy the following conditions!

(1) It holds that $q > p + 1$.

(2) The number $s = p + q$ is also a prime number.

(3) The number $p \cdot q \cdot s$ is divisible by 10.

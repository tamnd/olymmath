In a chess tournament, 8 masters participate over 7 days. Each day, 4 games are played in which all masters participate, and at the end of the tournament, everyone has played against everyone else exactly once. Prove that by the end of the fifth day of the tournament, there exists a set of at least 4 masters who have already played all their games against each other.

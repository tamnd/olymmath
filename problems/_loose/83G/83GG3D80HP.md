Some of the 7 pieces of paper are each divided into exactly 7 parts, then some of the now available pieces of paper are each divided into exactly 7 parts, and so on.

Is it possible that by continuing this process for a sufficient amount of time, one can end up with exactly 1976 pieces of paper?

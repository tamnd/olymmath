For every integer $n\ge 3$ find all sequences of real numbers $(x_1,x_2,\ldots ,x_n)$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i=n$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_{i-1}-x_i+x_{i+1})^2=n$, where $x_0=x_n$ and $x_{n+1}=x_1$.

(22nd USA Mathematical Olympiad Problem) Let $a_{0}, a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers, satisfying $a_{i-1} a_{i+1} \leqslant a_{i}^{2}(i=1,2,3, \cdots, n-1)$. Prove: For all $n>1$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{0}+a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}}{n+1} \cdot \frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n-1}}{n-1} \geqslant \frac{a_{0}+a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n-1}}{n} \cdot \frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}}{n} .
$$

Mr. and Mrs. Kotnik have a shared luggage, so they would not be charged extra if the weight of their shared luggage did not exceed twice the allowed weight. Their combined luggage weighs $60 \mathrm{~kg}$, and they had to pay an additional 11 euros. Mr. Novak, who travels alone, also has a luggage weighing $60 \mathrm{~kg}$. However, he had to pay 33 euros for his luggage. What is the maximum weight of luggage a single passenger can have without having to pay extra?

(B) 18

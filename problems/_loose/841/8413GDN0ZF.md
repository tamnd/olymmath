The first block contains two elements: $\boldsymbol{A}, \boldsymbol{B}$ (they are connected in parallel) and fails when both elements fail simultaneously. $P(B)=0.85, P(C)=0.6 ;$ b) find the absolute error $\left|P-P^{*}\right|$, where $P$ is the reliability of the system, calculated analytically. Conduct 50 trials.

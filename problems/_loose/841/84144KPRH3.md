Show that there are infinitely many triples of positive integers $(a_i,b_i,c_i)$ , $i=1,2,3,\ldots$ , satisfying the equation $a^2+b^2=c^4$ , such that $c_n$ and $c_{n+1}$ are coprime for any positive integer $n$ .

Four teams, including Quixajuba, compete in a volleyball tournament where:

- each team plays against each of the others only once;
- any match ends with the victory of one of the teams;
- in any match, the teams have the same probability of winning;
- at the end of the tournament, the teams are ranked in order by the number of wins.

a) Is it possible that, at the end of the tournament, all teams have the same number of wins? Why?

b) What is the probability that the tournament will end with Quixajuba isolated in first place?

c) What is the probability that the tournament will end with three teams tied for first place?

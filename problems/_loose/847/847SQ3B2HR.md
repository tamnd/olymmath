At a chess tournament, where every two participants played exactly one game, each chess player earned an integer number of points. At the tournament, all players except the winner earned the same number of points. Prove that the number of participants in the tournament is even.

Note. In chess, a win earns 1 point, a draw earns half a point, and a loss earns 0 points.

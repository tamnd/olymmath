(2005 Romanian Mathematical Olympiad) Given that $n$ is an integer, let $p(n)$ denote the product of its digits (in decimal representation).
（1）Prove that: $p(n) \leqslant n$;
（2）Find all $n$ such that $10 p(n)=n^{2}+$ $4 n-2005$ holds.

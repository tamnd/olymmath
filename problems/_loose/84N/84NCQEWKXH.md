For any pair of coprime positive integers $a$ and $b$ , define $f(a, b)$ to be the smallest nonnegative integer $k$ such that $b \mid ak+1$ . Prove that if a and b are coprime positive integers satisfying $$ f(a, b) - f(b, a) = 2, $$ then there exists a prime number $p$ such that $p^2\mid a + b$ .

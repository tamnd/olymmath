Let $D$ be one of the vertices of a tetrahedron, $M$ its centroid, and $O$ the center of the circumscribed sphere. It is known that the points $D$, $M$, and the points of intersection of the medians of the faces containing $D$ lie on the surface of one sphere. Prove that the lines $D M$ and $O M$ are perpendicular.

We are given $2 n$ points in the plane such that no three are collinear. Exactly $n$ of these points are houses $M=\left\{M_{1}, \ldots, M_{n}\right\}$ and the remaining $n$ points are wells $P=\left\{P_{1}, \ldots, P_{n}\right\}$. Show that it is possible to bijectively associate the wells with the houses so that when each well is connected in a straight line to its house, none of the roads thus constructed intersect.

Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer. A group of $2 n$ people meet. Each of these people has at least $n$ friends in this group (in particular, if $A$ is a friend of $B$ then $B$ is a friend of $A$, and one is not a friend with oneself). Prove that these $2 n$ people can be seated around a round table so that each person is between two of their friends.

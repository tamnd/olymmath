Let $\mathscr{A}$ and $\mathscr{B}$ be two $\sigma$-algebras of subsets of $\Omega$. Define

$$
d(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B})=4 \text { sup }\{|\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)-\mathrm{P}(A) \mathrm{P}(B)|: A \in \mathscr{A}, B \in \mathscr{B}\} .
$$

Show that this quantity, characterizing the degree of dependence between $\mathscr{A}$ and $\mathscr{B}$, has the following properties:

(a) $0 \leqslant d(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B}) \leqslant 1$;

(b) $d(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B})=0$, if and only if $\mathscr{A}$ and $\mathscr{B}$ are independent;

(c) $d(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B})=1$, if and only if for some $A_{n} \in \mathscr{A}$ and $B_{n} \in \mathscr{B}$, $n \geqslant 1$, the following conditions hold

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(A_{n}\right) \rightarrow \frac{1}{2}, \mathrm{P}\left(A_{n} \triangle B_{n}\right) \rightarrow 0, n \rightarrow \infty
$$

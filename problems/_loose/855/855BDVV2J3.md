a $n \times n$ board is given for a natural number $n$. We now color $k$ of the cells black so that for every three columns there is at most one row in which all intersection cells with the three columns are colored black. Show that holds:

$$
\frac{2 k}{n} \leq \sqrt{8 n-7}+1
$$

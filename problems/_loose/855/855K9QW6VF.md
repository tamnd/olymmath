Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ and $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots, B_{n}$ be two permutations of the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, n$. Prove that for even $n$, some two numbers in the set $A_{1}+B_{1}, A_{2}+B_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}+B_{n}$ give the same remainder when divided by $n$.

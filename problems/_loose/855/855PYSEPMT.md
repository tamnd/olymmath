Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is an arithmetic sequence, and $a_{2}=5, a_{8}=23$. The sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is a geometric sequence with all terms being positive numbers, $b_{1}=2$, and for any positive integers $s, t$ there is $b_{s+t}=b_{s} \cdot b_{t}$.
(1) Find the general terms of the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Prove that there are infinitely many terms of the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ in the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$.

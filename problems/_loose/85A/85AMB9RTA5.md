Let $n$ be an even number greater than 2. Use $\frac{n^{2}}{2}$ colors to paint the squares of an $n \times n$ chessboard, with each square painted one color, and each color used exactly twice. Prove that it is possible to place $n$ rooks on the chessboard such that the $n$ squares they occupy have different colors, and the $n$ rooks cannot attack each other.

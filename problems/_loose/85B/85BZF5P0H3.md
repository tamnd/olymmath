(Nested Circles Theorem) Two circles are internally tangent at point $P$, with diameters $P A$ and $P B$, which we call the original circles. The circle with diameter $A B$ is called the initial circle. Then construct a circle that is tangent to both original circles and the initial circle, called the first circle; then construct a circle that is tangent to both original circles and the first circle, called the second circle; $\cdots \cdots$, and so on. The distance from the center of the $n$-th circle to the line $A B$ is equal to $n$ times the diameter of the $n$-th circle.

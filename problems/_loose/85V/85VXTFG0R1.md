Let $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}$ and $\mathrm{c}$ be strictly positive real numbers. Show that

$$
\frac{a^{3}+5 b^{3}}{3 a+b}+\frac{b^{3}+5 c^{3}}{3 b+c}+\frac{c^{3}+5 a^{3}}{3 c+a} \geqslant \frac{3}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)
$$

Two equal disks are mounted on the same axis. On the circumference of each of them, the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 20$ are placed in a circle at equal distances in arbitrary order. Is it always possible to rotate one disk relative to the other so that no two identical numbers stand opposite each other?

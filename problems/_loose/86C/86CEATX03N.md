Santa Claus had $n$ types of candies, with $k$ pieces of each type. He distributed all the candies randomly into $k$ gifts, each containing $n$ candies, and gave them to $k$ children. The children decided to restore fairness. Two children are willing to exchange one candy each if each receives a candy of a type they do not have. Is it always possible to organize a series of exchanges so that each child ends up with candies of all types?

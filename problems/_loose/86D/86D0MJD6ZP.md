For every positive integer $n$ let $$f(n) = \frac{n^4+n^3+n^2-n+1}{n^6-1}$$ Given $$\sum_{n = 2}^{20} f(n) = \frac{a}{b}$$ for relatively prime positive integers $a$ and $b$, find the sum of the prime factors of $b$.

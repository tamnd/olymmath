In the isosceles triangle $ABC$, the vertex angle $\angle BAC = \alpha$. The base $BC$ is divided into $n$ equal parts from $B$ to $C$, with the points of division being $D_{1}, D_{2}, \ldots, D_{n-1}$. The point $E$ divides the side $AB$ in the ratio $1:(n-1)$. Prove that

$$
\angle AD_{1}E + \angle AD_{2}E + \cdots + \angle AD_{n-1}E = \frac{\alpha}{2} .
$$

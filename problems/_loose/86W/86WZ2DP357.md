Prove that if $F(n)=\sum_{d \mid n} f(d)$, then

$$
f(n)=\sum_{d \mid n} \mu(d) F(n / d)=\sum_{d \mid n} \mu(n / d) F(d)
$$

where the sum is taken over all divisors $d$ of $n$ (Möbius).

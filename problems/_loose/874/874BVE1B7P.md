Let $m, n$ be positive integers, and $n$ be even. In an $n$-row, $m$-column grid, each cell is filled with 1 or -1. It is known that the sum of the products of any two numbers in the same row but different columns is no more than 0. Prove: the number of cells filled with 1 in this grid does not exceed $\frac{1}{2} n(m+\sqrt{m})$.

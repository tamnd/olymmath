Consider the trapezoid $ABCD$ where $AB \| DC$ and $AB > DC$. From point $D$, draw a line parallel to $CB$, and from point $C$, draw a line parallel to $DA$, intersecting $AB$ at points $M$ and $N$ respectively. What is the length of $MN$ if $AB = a$ and $DC = b$? (Three cases.) What are the conditions for the lines $DM$ and $CN$ to intersect outside the trapezoid, on the side $AB$ of the trapezoid, or inside the trapezoid?

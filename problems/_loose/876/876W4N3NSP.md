Let $A$, $B$, and $P$ be three points on the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$ such that
$$
\overrightarrow{O P}=\frac{m^{2}-n^{2}}{m^{2}+n^{2}} \overrightarrow{O A}+\frac{2 m n}{m^{2}+n^{2}} \overrightarrow{O B},
$$

where $m>n>0$.
(1) Find the equation of the locus $C$ of the midpoint of segment $A B$;
(2) Draw a tangent line to curve $C$ at any point $Q$ on the curve, and let it intersect the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$ at points $E$ and $F$. Prove that:
$$
Q E=Q F .
$$

Given a parallelogram $A B C D$. A circle $\Omega$ with a diameter of 13 is circumscribed around triangle $A B M$, where $M$ is the point of intersection of the diagonals of the given parallelogram. $\Omega$ intersects the ray $C B$ and the segment $A D$ at points $E$ and $K$ respectively. The length of arc $A E$ is twice the length of arc $B M$ (arcs $A E$ and $B M$ do not have common points). The length of segment $E M$ is 12. Find the lengths of segments $B C, B K$ and the perimeter of triangle $A K M$.

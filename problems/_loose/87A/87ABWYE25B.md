Let $A'$ , $B'$ and $C'$ be points on the sides $BC$ , $CA$ and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ respectively, such that $$ \frac{BA'}{A'C}=\frac{CB'}{B'A}=\frac{AC'}{C'B}=2. $$ The circle that passes through points $A'$ , $B'$ and $C'$ cuts again to the sides $BC$ , $CA$ and $AB$ at $A''$ , $B''$ and $C''$ respectively. Let $A_1$ , $B_1$ and $C_1$ be points on $B''C''$ , $C''A''$ and $A''B''$ such that $$ \frac{B''A_1}{A_1C''}=\frac{C''B_1}{B_1A''}=\frac{A''C_1}{C_1B''}=2. $$
Prove that lines $AA_1$ , $BB_1$ and $CC_1$ are concurrent.

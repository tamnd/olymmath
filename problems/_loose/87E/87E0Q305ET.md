The plane $\alpha$ intersects the edges $A B, B C, C D$ and $D A$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ at points $K, L, M$ and $N$ respectively. It turns out that the dihedral angles $\angle(K L A, K L M)$, $\angle(L M B, L M N)$, $\angle(M N C, M N K)$ and $\angle(N K D, N K L)$ are equal. (Here, $\angle(P Q R, P Q S)$ denotes the dihedral angle along the edge $P Q$ in the tetrahedron $P Q R S$.) Prove that the projections of the vertices $A, B, C$ and $D$ onto the plane $\alpha$ lie on a single circle.

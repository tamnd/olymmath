Let $f$ be a trigonometric polynomial of degree $n$ with leading coefficient $a_{n}$.

a) Prove that $\frac{1}{2 n} \sum_{m=0}^{2 n-1}(-1)^{m} f\left(\frac{m \pi}{n}\right)=a_{n}$.

b) Prove that $\left|f\left(\frac{m \pi}{n}\right)\right| \geqslant\left|a_{n}\right|$ for some integer $m$.

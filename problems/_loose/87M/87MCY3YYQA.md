Find the smallest positive constant $\mathrm{c}$, satisfying: for any positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, and any finite number of real numbers $\mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}, x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}=1$, we have $\sum_{k m \leq n} x_{k} x_{m}<c \sqrt{n}$.

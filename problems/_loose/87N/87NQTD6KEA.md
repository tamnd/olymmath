Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle. Denote by $H$ and $M$ the orthocenter of $A B C$ and the midpoint of side $B C$, respectively. Let $Y$ be a point on $A C$ such that $Y H$ is perpendicular to $M H$ and let $Q$ be a point on $B H$ such that $Q A$ is perpendicular to $A M$. Let $J$ be the second point of intersection of $M Q$ and the circle with diameter $M Y$. Prove that $H J$ is perpendicular to $A M$.

Prove that if a positive function $f(x)$ on the interval $[a, b]$ has a continuous derivative, then the surface area of the surface of revolution generated by the curve $y=f(x)$ is

$$
2 \pi \int_{a}^{b} f(x) \sqrt{1+\left(f^{\prime}(x)\right)^{2}} d x
$$

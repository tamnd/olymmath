(2005 Northern Mathematical Olympiad Problem) In an arithmetic sequence of positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{3 n}$ $(n \geqslant 2)$ with a common difference of $d(d>0)$, any $n+2$ numbers are taken. Prove: there must exist two numbers $a_{i} 、 a_{j}(i \neq j)$, satisfying the inequality $1<\frac{\left|a_{i}-a_{j}\right|}{n d}<2$.

Let $A,B\in\mathcal{M}_4(\mathbb{R})$ such that $AB=BA$ and $\det (A^2+AB+B^2)=0$ . Prove that:

$$\det (A+B)+3\det (A-B)=6\det (A)+6\det (B)\ .$$

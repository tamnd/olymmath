Divide two paper disks of different sizes into 1965 sectors each. On each disk, arbitrarily select 200 sectors and color them red. Pin the centers of the two disks together so that the sectors on the top disk align with those on the bottom disk. Rotate the smaller disk by an integer multiple of the sector's central angle, while keeping the larger disk stationary. Prove that there are at least 60 cases where the number of overlapping red sector pairs on the top and bottom disks is no more than 20.

An integer $n$ is called "Silesian" if there exist positive integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ such that $n = \frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{a b+b c+c a}$

(1) Prove that there are infinitely many integers that are "Silesian";

(2) Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers that are not "Silesian".

Let $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$ be the altitudes of $\triangle A B C$ on sides $a, b, c$ respectively. Prove:
$$
\frac{h_{b}^{2}+h_{c}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{h_{c}^{2}+h_{s}^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{h_{a}^{2}+h_{b}^{2}}{c^{2}} \leqslant \frac{9}{2}
$$

Note: There seems to be a typo in the original problem where $h_{\dot{c}}$ and $h_{\dot{s}}$ are used. It should likely be $h_{c}$ and $h_{b}$ respectively. The corrected version is:
$$
\frac{h_{b}^{2}+h_{c}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{h_{c}^{2}+h_{b}^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{h_{a}^{2}+h_{b}^{2}}{c^{2}} \leqslant \frac{9}{2}
$$

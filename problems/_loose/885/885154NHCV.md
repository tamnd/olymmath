Let $S$ be a finite set of points in the plane, where no three points are collinear. Let the set
$$
D(S, r)=\{\{X, Y\} \mid X, Y \in S, d(X, Y)=r\} \text {. }
$$

Here, $d(X, Y)$ denotes the distance between points $X$ and $Y$, and $r$ is a positive real number. Prove:
$$
\sum_{r>0}|D(S, r)|^{2} \leqslant \frac{3|S|^{2}(|S|-1)}{4} .
$$

Here, $|A|$ denotes the number of elements in the finite set $A$.

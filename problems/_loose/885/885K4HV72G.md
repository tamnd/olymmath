The ninety-fifth problem: Given a real-coefficient polynomial $\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{x})$ of degree not less than 1, prove: For each real number $\mathrm{c}>0$, there exists a positive integer $\mathrm{n}_{0}$ satisfying the following condition: For each real-coefficient polynomial $\mathrm{p}(\mathrm{x})$ of degree not less than $\mathrm{n}_{0}$ and with leading coefficient 1, the number of integers $\mathrm{x}$ satisfying the inequality $|\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{p}(\mathrm{x}))| \leq \mathrm{c}$ does not exceed $\operatorname{deg} \mathrm{p}(\mathrm{x})$.

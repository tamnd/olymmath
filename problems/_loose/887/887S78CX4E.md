Given $2 n$ non-negative integers $r_{1}, r_{2}, \cdots, r_{n}$ and $c_{1}, c_{2}, \cdots, c_{n}$, satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^{n} r_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} c_{i}$. Fill in each small square of an $n \times n$ grid with a non-negative integer arbitrarily, so that the sum of the numbers in the $i$-th row equals $r_{i}(i=1$, $2, \cdots, n)$, and the sum of the numbers in the $j$-th column equals $c_{j}(j=1,2, \cdots, n)$. Let $S$ be the sum of the numbers in the $n$ small squares on the main diagonal (from the top left corner to the bottom right corner). Find the maximum and minimum values of $S$.

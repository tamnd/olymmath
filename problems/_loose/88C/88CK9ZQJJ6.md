Elliptical Billiards. On an elliptical billiard table, ball $A$ lies at the edge, and ball $B$ is on the segment $s$ connecting the foci of the ellipse. It is necessary to hit ball $\boldsymbol{A}$ so that, after bouncing off the edge, it collides with ball $B$; however, it is forbidden for ball $A$ to cross segment $\boldsymbol{s}$ before hitting the edge.

Prove that the problem is unsolvable.

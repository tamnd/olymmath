On the sides of an affinely regular polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ with center $O$, squares $A_{j+1} A_{j} B_{j} C_{j+1}$ are constructed externally ($j=1, \ldots, n$). Prove that the segments $B_{j} C_{j}$ and $O A_{j}$ are perpendicular, and their ratio is $2(1-\cos (2 \pi / n))$.

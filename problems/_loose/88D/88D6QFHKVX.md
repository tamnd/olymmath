In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
(1)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{2}{b} \\
\Leftrightarrow(2) \cot ^{2} \frac{A}{2}+\cot ^{2} \frac{C}{2}=2 \cot ^{2} \frac{B}{2}
\end{array}
$$
$\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{2}{b}$
$\Leftrightarrow$ (2) $\cot ^{2} \frac{A}{2}+\cot ^{2} \frac{C}{2}=2 \cot ^{2} \frac{B}{2}$
$\Leftrightarrow$ (3) $\csc ^{2} \frac{A}{2}+\csc ^{2} \frac{C}{2}=2 \csc ^{2} \frac{B}{2}$
$\Leftrightarrow$ (4) $(2 \sin A-\sin B)(2 \sin C-\sin B)=\sin ^{2} B$.

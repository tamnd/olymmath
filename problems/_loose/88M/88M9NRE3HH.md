Let $\left\{a_{i j}\right\}_{i, j \geqslant 1}$ be a family of real numbers such that $\sum_{i, j}\left|a_{i j}\right|<\infty$. Derive from Fubini's theorem that

$$
\sum_{i, j} a_{i j}=\sum_{i} \sum_{j} a_{i j}=\sum_{j} \sum_{i} a_{i j}
$$

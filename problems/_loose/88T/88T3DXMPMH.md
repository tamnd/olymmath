Let's determine the digits $a, b, c, d$ if the number written in the decimal system with these digits satisfies:

$$
\overline{a b c d}=16\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}\right)+a+b
$$

and additionally, $b^{2}-a^{2}=2\left(c^{2}+d^{2}\right)$.

If the equation with respect to $x$
$$
\frac{1}{x^{2}-x}+\frac{k-5}{x^{2}+x}=\frac{k-1}{x^{2}-1}
$$

has an extraneous root $x=1$, then the value of $k$ is
(A) 1 .
(B) 2 .
(C) 3 .
(D) 6
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

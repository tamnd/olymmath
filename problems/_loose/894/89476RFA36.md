Two magicians show the following trick to the audience. The audience has 48 cards numbered from 1 to 48. They choose 25 cards and hand them to the first magician. The first magician returns two of them to the audience. The audience adds one of the remaining 23 cards to these two and, after shuffling, hands these three cards to the second magician. How can the magicians agree so that the second one can always determine with certainty which of the three cards the audience added?

Lemma. If 25 cards are chosen from 48 cards numbered $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 48$, there will always be a pair with a sum of 49.

The positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ sum to 1. Prove that

$$
\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}}-1\right)\left(\frac{1}{a_{2}^{2}}-1\right) \ldots\left(\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}}-1\right) \geq\left(n^{2}-1\right)^{n}
$$

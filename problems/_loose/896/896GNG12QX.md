7.36 3 automata print pairs of natural numbers on cards. The automata work as follows: the first automaton, after reading a card $(a, b)$, outputs a new card $(a+1, b+1)$; the second automaton, after reading a card $(a, b)$, outputs a new card $\left(\frac{a}{2}, \frac{b}{2}\right)$ (it only works when $a, b$ are both even); the third automaton reads two cards $(a, b)$ and $(b, c)$, and outputs a new card $(a, c)$, and it can return all read cards.

Assume there is an initial card $(5,19)$. Can we use any type of automaton to get the card:
(1) $(1,50)$?
(2) $(1,100)$?
(3) Suppose there is an initial card $(a, b), a<b$, and we want to get the card $(1, n)$. For what values of $n$ can this be achieved?
(12th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1978)

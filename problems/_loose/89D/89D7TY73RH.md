In a parallelogram $A B C D$, let $\angle D A B$ be an acute angle. From point $C$, a perpendicular is dropped to the line $g_{A B}$, and its foot is $E$. Connect $E$ with the midpoint $F$ of side $A D$.

Prove: The angle $\angle E F D$ is twice as large as the angle $\angle B E F$!

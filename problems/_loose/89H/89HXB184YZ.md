At different times of the day, the manager hands documents to the secretary for printing, each time placing the document on top of the stack of documents the secretary needs to print. The secretary takes one document from the top to print whenever there is time. If there are $n$ documents, and the manager hands them over in the order $1,2, \cdots, n$, how many possible orders are there for the secretary to finish printing these $n$ documents?

In the cells of an infinite sheet of graph paper, real numbers are written. Two figures are considered, each consisting of a finite number of cells. It is allowed to move the figures parallel to the grid lines by an integer number of cells. It is known that for any position of the first figure, the sum of the numbers written in the cells it covers is positive. Prove that there exists a position of the second figure such that the sum of the numbers in the cells it covers is positive.

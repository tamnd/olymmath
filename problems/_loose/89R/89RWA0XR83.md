Draw tangents to the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$ at the points $(0,1),(-1,0),(0,-1)$ and $(\xi, \eta)$, where $\xi$ is any positive number less than 1. Prove that the diagonals of the resulting trapezoid intersect on the $y$-axis. Does the line connecting the points of tangency of the non-parallel sides pass through the intersection point?

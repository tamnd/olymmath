$$
\begin{aligned}
E & =\sqrt{1830}\left(A C-\sqrt{A B^{2}+B C^{2}}\right) \\
& +1789-\frac{\frac{1}{A B}+\frac{1}{B C}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{B M}}{(1848)^{3}} ?
\end{aligned}
$$

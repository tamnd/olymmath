a) Prove that if $0 \leqslant t \leqslant a$, then

$1+t+\frac{t^{2}}{2!}+\frac{t^{3}}{3!}+\ldots+\frac{t^{n}}{n!} \leqslant e^{t} \leqslant 1+t+\frac{t^{2}}{2!}+\frac{t^{3}}{3!}+\ldots+\frac{t^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}+e^{a} \frac{t^{n}}{n!}$.

b) Prove that if $0 \geqslant t \geqslant a$, then for odd $n$ the same inequalities hold, and for even $n$ the inequality signs are reversed.

The 70th question: Let $\mathrm{p}$ be a prime number, and integer $\mathrm{s}$ satisfy $0<\mathrm{s}<\mathrm{p}$. Prove: There exist integers $\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{n}$ satisfying $0<\mathrm{m}<\mathrm{n}<\mathrm{p}$ and $\left\{\frac{\mathrm{sm}}{\mathrm{p}}\right\}<\left\{\frac{\mathrm{sn}}{\mathrm{p}}\right\}<\frac{\mathrm{s}}{\mathrm{p}}$ if and only if $\mathrm{s} \nmid(\mathrm{p}-1)$.

Two players, A and B, play a game where 99 chips, each labeled with a number from 1 to 99, are placed on 99 equally spaced points on a circle. The players take turns placing the chips, with A starting first. The rule is: the player who first places chips such that the numbers on three adjacent points form an arithmetic sequence wins. Who has a winning strategy, A or B? Explain your reasoning.

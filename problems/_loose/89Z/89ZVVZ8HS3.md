Let $(t_n)_{n\geqslant 1}$ be the sequence defined by $t_1=1, t_{2k}=-t_k$ and $t_{2k+1}=t_{k+1}$ for all $k\geqslant 1.$ Consider the series $$\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{t_n}{n^{1/2024}}.$$Porve that this series converges to a positive real number.

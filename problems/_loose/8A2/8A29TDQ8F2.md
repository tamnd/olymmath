A $2 \times 10$ rectangular grid is filled with the numbers from 1 to 20 in the following way. The first row contains the numbers from 1 to 10, arranged arbitrarily. The second row contains the numbers from 11 to 20, and if the number in a cell in the first row is odd, then the number in the cell directly below it must be even. Prove that for any arrangement of the numbers 1 to 20, there will always be two columns with the same sum.

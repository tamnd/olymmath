Luggage at the airport. 200 suitcases were loaded onto the plane. After the flight, passengers meet their suitcases on the conveyor belt. As soon as the conveyor belt started, the loader began placing suitcases on it one by one every two seconds in a random order (as they were brought). Among the passengers is a group of businessmen who are waiting for their 10 suitcases.

a) (from 8th grade, 3 points). Find the probability that the businessmen will have to wait exactly two minutes for their last suitcase after the conveyor belt starts.

b) (from 9th grade, 3 points). Find the expected value of the random variable "the time the businessmen will have to wait for their last suitcase."

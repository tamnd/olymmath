Four lines belonging to the same pencil are intersected by two lines at points $A, B, C, D$ and $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$ respectively. Prove that the segments formed on the intersecting lines satisfy the equation:

$$
\frac{\overline{A C}}{\overline{C B}}: \frac{\overline{A D}}{\overline{D B}}=\frac{\overline{A_{1} C_{1}}}{\overline{C_{1} B_{1}}}: \frac{\overline{A_{1} D_{1}}}{\overline{D_{1} B_{1}}}
$$

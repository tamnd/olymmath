Given the function defined on $\mathbf{R}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)= & x^{2} \log _{2} \frac{4(a+1)}{a}+2 x \log _{2} \frac{2 a}{a+1}+ \\
& \log _{2} \frac{(a+1)^{2}}{4 a^{2}} \\
> & 0
\end{aligned}
$$

is always true. Determine whether the equation $f(f(x))=9 x$ has real roots for $x \in[1,+\infty)$. Prove your conclusion.

Let the positive random variable $R$ have a Rayleigh distribution, i.e., for some $\sigma^{2}>0$ the density of $R$ is given by

$$
f_{R}(r)=\frac{r}{\sigma^{2}} e^{-\frac{r^{2}}{2 \sigma^{2}}}, r>0
$$

Let also $\theta$ be a random variable independent of $R$ with a uniform distribution on ( $\alpha, \alpha+2 \pi k$ ), where $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\alpha \in[0,2 \pi)$. Show that the variables $X=R \cos \theta$ and $Y=R \sin \theta$ are independent and have a normal distribution $\mathscr{N}\left(0, \sigma^{2}\right)$.

Verify that the densities of the distributions of $\cos \theta$ and $\sin \theta$ coincide and are equal to

$$
\frac{1}{\pi} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}, x \in[-1,1]
$$

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}$ be 4 rational numbers such that
$$
\left\{a_{i} a_{j} \mid 1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant 4\right\}=\left\{-24,-2,-\frac{3}{2},-\frac{1}{8}, 1,3\right\},
$$

Find the value of $a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+a_{4}$.

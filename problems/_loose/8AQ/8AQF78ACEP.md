Let $A B C$ be a right triangle with the right angle at $C$. The angle $\angle B A C$ has the size $\alpha=30^{\circ}$. The midpoint of side $A B$ is $D$, and the intersection of the three angle bisectors of triangle $A B C$ is $S$.

Prove that from these assumptions $\overline{C S}=\overline{D S}$ follows!

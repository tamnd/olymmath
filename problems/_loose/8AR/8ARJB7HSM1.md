The ninety-eighth problem: Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a positive integer, and the polynomial $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})=\left(\mathrm{x}^{2}-7 \mathrm{x}+6\right)^{2 \mathrm{n}}+13$. Prove that: $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})$ cannot be written as the product of $\mathrm{n}+1$ non-constant polynomials with integer coefficients.

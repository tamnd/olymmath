Mr. Anders drove past a gas station (A) on the highway at a speed of $100 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$. After driving an additional distance of $175 \mathrm{~km}$, Mr. Anders had to switch the fuel valve to reserve.

Since the next gas station (B) is still $45 \mathrm{~km}$ away from this point on the highway, Mr. Anders reduced his speed to $60 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$ to consume less fuel.

At what average speed did Mr. Anders cover the distance between A and B? (The short braking distance, during which the speed was reduced from $100 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$ to $60 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$, should not be considered in the calculation, as it only slightly affects the average speed.)

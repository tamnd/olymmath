A four-digit number $(x y z t)_{B}$ is called a stable number in base $B$ if $(x y z t)_{B}=$ $(d c b a)_{B}-(a b c d)_{B}$, where $a \leqslant b \leqslant c \leqslant d$ are obtained by arranging $x, y, z, t$ in non-decreasing order. Determine all stable numbers in base $B$.
(26th International Mathematical Olympiad Shortlist, 1985)

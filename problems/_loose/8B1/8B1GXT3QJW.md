Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle. We denote by $X$ the intersection of the diagonals $(A C)$ and $(B D)$, and by $P$ and $Q$ the orthogonal projections of $X$ onto the lines $(A D)$ and $(B C)$. Furthermore, we denote by $M$ and $N$ the midpoints of the segments $[A B]$ and $[C D]$. Show that the lines $(M N)$ and $(P Q)$ are perpendicular.

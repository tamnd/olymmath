Do there exist three different prime numbers $p$ , $q$ and $r$ such that $p^2 + d$ is divisible by $qr$ , $q^2 + d$ is divisible by $rp$ and $r^2 + d$ is divisible by $pq$ , if

(a) $d = 10$ ;
(b) $d = 11$ ?

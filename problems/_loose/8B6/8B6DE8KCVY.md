Another inequality in a triangle. Prove that in any triangle $ABC$, the inequality

$$
\frac{\sin A + \sin B + \sin C}{\sin A \cdot \sin B \cdot \sin C} \geq 4
$$

holds, and equality is achieved only in the case of an equilateral triangle.

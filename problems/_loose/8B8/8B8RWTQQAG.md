In a regular quadrilateral pyramid $S A B C D$, the height is equal to the side of the base. Points $M$ and $N$ are marked on the lateral edges $SD$ and $SB$ of the pyramid, respectively, such that the lines $AM$ and $CN$ are perpendicular to each other. Prove that

$$
2 \mathrm{SA}(\mathrm{SM}+\mathrm{SN})=\mathrm{SA}^{2}+\mathrm{SM} \cdot \mathrm{SN} .
$$

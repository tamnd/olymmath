In an isosceles triangle, the sum of the altitudes from the two equal sides equals the altitude from the base, then the sine value of the base angle is
(A) $\frac{1}{2}$.
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}$.
(D) $\frac{1}{4}$.
(Shenyang City, Liaoning Province, "Yucai Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

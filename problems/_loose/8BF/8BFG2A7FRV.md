Let be a finite field $ K. $ Say that two polynoms $ f,g $ from $ K[X] $ are *neighbours,* if the have the same degree and they differ by exactly one coefficient.

**a)** Show that all the neighbours of $ 1+X^2 $ from $ \mathbb{Z}_3[X] $ are reducible in $ \mathbb{Z}_3[X] . $

**b)** If $ |K|\ge 4, $ show that any polynomial of degree $ |K|-1 $ from $ K[X] $ has a neighbour from $ K[X] $ that is reducible in $ K[X] , $ and also has a neighbour that doesn´t have any root in $ K. $

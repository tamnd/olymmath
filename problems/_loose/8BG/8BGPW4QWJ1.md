(World City) $\triangle A B C$ is an acute triangle, $B^{\prime}$ is the midpoint of $C A$, $A^{\prime} 、 C^{\prime}$ are arbitrary points on $B C 、 A B$ respectively. Prove: (1) the area of $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ is not greater than half the area of $\triangle A B C$;
(2) the area of $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ is equal to one quarter of the area of $\triangle A B C$ if and only if: $C^{\prime} 、 A^{\prime}$ is at least one of the midpoints of $A B 、 B C$.

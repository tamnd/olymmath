Prove that for any natural number $n$ there exists a natural number $N$ such that the product $9 \cdot 5^{n} \cdot N$ is a palindrome, that is, a number whose decimal representation reads the same from right to left as from left to right. For example, for $n=1$ we can take $N=13$, since $9 \cdot 5^{1} \cdot 13=585$.

Let a line intersect one circle at points $A$ and $B$, and another circle at points $C$ and $D$. Prove that the points of intersection of the tangents to the first circle drawn at points $A$ and $B$ with the tangents drawn to the second circle at points $C$ and $D$ (considering the points where the tangents to different circles intersect) lie on one circle, the center of which lies on the line passing through the centers of the given circles.

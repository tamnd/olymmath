Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in circle $\gamma$. It turns out that the circles constructed on segments $AB$ and $CD$ as diameters touch each other externally at point $S$. Let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of segments $AB$ and $CD$ respectively. Prove that the perpendicular $\ell$ to line $MN$, erected at point $M$, intersects line $CS$ at a point lying on $\gamma$.

(I. Pochepiov, D. Brodsky)

Dopnnikov V.L. Part of the subsets of some finite set are distinguished. Each distinguished subset consists of exactly $2 k$ elements ($k-$ a fixed natural number). It is known that in each subset consisting of no more than $(k+1)^{2}$ elements, either there is no distinguished subset, or all distinguished subsets contained in it have a common element. Prove that all distinguished subsets have a common element.

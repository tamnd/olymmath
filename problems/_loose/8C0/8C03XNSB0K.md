$10.3 .27 \star$ In $\triangle A B C$, the angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects the perpendicular bisector of side $A B$ at $A^{\prime}$, the angle bisector of $\angle B$ intersects the perpendicular bisector of side $B C$ at $B^{\prime}$, and the angle bisector of $\angle C$ intersects the perpendicular bisector of side $C A$ at $C^{\prime}$. Prove that if $A^{\prime} 、 B^{\prime} 、 C^{\prime}$ are distinct, then $\angle B^{\prime} A^{\prime} C^{\prime}=\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{2} \angle B A C$.

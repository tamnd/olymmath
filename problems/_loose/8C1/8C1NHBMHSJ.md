At the vertices of a hexagon, numbers are written, and on each side, the sum of the numbers at its ends. Let's call rounding the replacement of a non-integer number with one of the two nearest integers (the nearest larger or the nearest smaller), and let an integer remain unchanged when rounded. Prove that all 12 numbers can be rounded in such a way that the sum of the numbers at the ends of each side remains the same.

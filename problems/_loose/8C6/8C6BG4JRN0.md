Each city in a certain country is assigned a unique number. There is a list in which for each pair of numbers it is indicated whether the cities with these numbers are connected by a railway or not. It turned out that for any two numbers $M$ and $N$ from the list, the cities can be renumbered so that the city with number $M$ gets number $N$, but the list remains correct. Is it true that for any two numbers $M$ and $N$ from the list, the cities can be renumbered so that the city with number $M$ gets number $N$, and the city with number $N$ gets number $M$, but the list remains correct?

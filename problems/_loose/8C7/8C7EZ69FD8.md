Let $x, y$ be integers such that $xy-1$ is divisible by the prime 1979. For the points with coordinates $(x, y)$, if there are three points on a straight line, prove that among these three points, there are at least two points whose differences in both the x-coordinates and y-coordinates are divisible by 1979.
(China Sichuan Mathematical Competition, 1979)

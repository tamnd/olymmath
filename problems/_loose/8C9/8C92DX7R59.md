Draw a $9 \times 9$ grid on a plane, and arbitrarily fill each small square with +1 or -1. The following method of changing the numbers is called making one move: For any small square, take the product of the numbers in all adjacent small squares (excluding the square itself). Thus, for each chosen square, a number is calculated. After all small squares have been chosen, place these calculated numbers back into the corresponding small squares. Can it always be done in a finite number of moves to make all numbers in the small squares become 1?

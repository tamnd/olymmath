Isosceles triangle $ABC$ with base $BC$ is rotated around point $C$ such that its vertex $A$ moves to point $A_{1}$ on line $BC$. In this process, vertex $B$ moves to some point $B_{1}$, which lies on the same side of line $BC$ as point $A$. The resulting isosceles triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C$ is then rotated around point $A_{1}$ such that vertex $B_{1}$ moves to point $B_{2}$ on line $BC$. In this process, vertex $C$ moves to some point $C_{2}$, which also lies on the same side of line $BC$ as point $A$. Prove that $C_{2} B_{2} \| A C$.

In the town, there are 100 residents, each of whom has exactly 3 acquaintances. On January 1, one resident learned a piece of news and informed his 3 acquaintances that day; on January 2, these informed residents then told the news to all their acquaintances, and so on. Is it possible that by March 5, the news has not yet spread to all residents, but by March 19, it has spread to everyone?

Consider $E(m ; n)=\sqrt{3 \cdot 5^{m}+25^{n}}$, where $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$.

a) Show that $E(2013 ; 1006) \in \mathbb{Q}$ and $E(1006 ; 2013) \in \mathbb{R} \backslash \mathbb{Q}$.

b) Show that there are infinitely many pairs $(m ; n) \in \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$ such that $E(m ; n) \in \mathbb{Q}$.

c) Show that there are infinitely many pairs $(m ; n) \in \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$ such that $E(m ; n) \in \mathbb{R} \backslash \mathbb{Q}$.

Let $p$ be a polynomial with integer coeﬃcients and let $a_1<a_2<\cdots <a_k$ be integers. Given that $p(a_i)\ne 0\forall i=1,2,\cdots, k$.

(a) Prove $\exists a\in \mathbb{Z}$ such that
$$ p(a_i)\mid p(a)\;\;\forall i=1,2,\dots ,k $$
(b) Does there exist $a\in \mathbb{Z}$ such that
$$ \prod_{i=1}^{k}p(a_i)\mid p(a) $$

In the trapezoid $A B C D(A B \| C D)$, let $E$ be the midpoint of the side $B C$. If both quadrilaterals $A B E D$ and $A E C D$ are tangential, and the side lengths of the trapezoid $A B C D$ are denoted in the usual way, then the side lengths satisfy the equalities

$$
a+c=\frac{b}{3}+d \text { and } \frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{3}{b} .
$$

Prove.

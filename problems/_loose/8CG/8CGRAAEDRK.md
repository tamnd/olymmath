A guard proposes the following game to the prisoners. All prisoners are to be taken to the prison yard, where each of them will be put a hat in one of 5 possible colors onto his head, and aligned so that each of them can see all hats but his own. The guard will then ask the first prisoner to say aloud whether he knows the color of his hat. If he answers "no", he will be publicly executed. Otherwise, he will be asked to say the color of his hat in such a way that others do not hear his answer. If the answer is correct, he will be freed, otherwise he will be publicly executed. The guard will then go on to the next prisoner in line and repeat the procedure, and so on. The prisoners may devise a strategy before the game starts, but no communication between them during the game is allowed. If there are 2015 prisoners, what is the maximal number of them that can have guaranteed freedom using an optimal strategy?

Let $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{Q}$ be two probability measures on ( $\Omega, \mathscr{F}$ ). Establish the equivalence of the following properties:

(1) the measure $Q$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $P$, and for some constant $L \geqslant 1$ the inequality

$$
\frac{d \mathbf{Q}}{d \mathrm{P}} \leqslant L \text { P-a.s.; }
$$

(2) for some $\alpha \in(0,1]$ and probability measure $\mathrm{R}$, the representation

$$
\mathbf{P}=\alpha \mathbf{Q}+(1-\alpha) \mathbf{R}
$$

holds.

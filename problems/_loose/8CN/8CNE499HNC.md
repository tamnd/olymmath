On the board are written the three numbers: $2, 3, 4$. An operation is repeated indefinitely: choose any two of the numbers written on the board, $a$ and $b$, erase the third, and replace it with $a+b-1$.

a) Can we, after a certain number of operations, have on the board: $6, 9, 14$?

b) Can we have: $101, 102, 103$?

c) Can we have: $101, 103, 105$?

$\mathbf{N 4 9}$ (42-4, Canada) Let $n$ be an odd number, and greater than $1, k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{n}$ be given integers. For each of the $n!$ permutations of $1,2, \cdots, n$, denoted by $a=\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right)$, let
$$
S(a)=\sum_{i=1}^{n} k_{i} a_{i} .
$$

Prove that there are two permutations $b$ and $c, b \neq c$, such that $S(b)-S(c)$ is divisible by $n!$.

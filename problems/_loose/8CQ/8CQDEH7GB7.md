$$
\begin{aligned}
& a=2 \cdot(1+2+3+\ldots+2016)-2016 \\
& b=1+1 \cdot 2+1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3+1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4+\ldots+1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \ldots \cdot 2016
\end{aligned}
$$

Show that:

b) a is a perfect square;

b) b is not a perfect square.

Let $K$ be any interior point on the side $AB$ of a given triangle $ABC$. The line $CK$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ at point $L (L \neq C)$. Let $k_{1}$ be the circumcircle of triangle $AKL$ and $k_{2}$ the circumcircle of triangle $BKL$.

a) Prove that the line $AC$ is tangent to the circle $k_{1}$ if and only if the line $BC$ is tangent to the circle $k_{2}$.

b) Assume that the line $AC$ is a secant of the circle $k_{1}$. Let $P (P \neq A)$ be the intersection of the line $AC$ with the circle $k_{1}$ and $Q (Q \neq B)$ the intersection of the line $BC$ with the circle $k_{2}$. Prove that the point $K$ lies on the segment $PQ$.

A triangle has side lengths $a$ , $b$ , $c$ , and the altitudes have lengths $h_a$ , $h_b$ , $h_c$ . Prove that
$$ \left( \frac{a}{h_a} \right)^2
+ \left( \frac{b}{h_b} \right)^2
+ \left( \frac{c}{h_c} \right)^2 \ge 4. $$

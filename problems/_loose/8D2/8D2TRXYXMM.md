Given a point $P$ inside a sector with an angle less than $180^{\circ}$, and points $E$ and $F$ on the sides of the angle. Let the vertex of the angle be $O$. Construct points $C$ and $D$ on the sides of the angle such that the areas of triangles $OEF$ and $OCD$ are equal, and the segment $CD$ passes through the point $P$.

Let $\alpha$ be a root of the irreducible polynomial $f(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \ldots + a_{0}$, where $n \geqslant 2$. Prove that there exists a number $c > 0$ (depending only on $\alpha$) such that $\left|\alpha - \frac{p}{q}\right| > \frac{c}{q^{n}}$ for any integer $p$ and natural number $q$ (Liouville's theorem). 32.38. Prove that the number $\alpha = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} 2^{-k!}$ is transcendental (Liouville).

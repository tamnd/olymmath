For an integer $30 \le k \le 70$ , let $M$ be the maximum possible value of $$ \frac{A}{\gcd(A,B)} \text{where } A = \dbinom{100}{k} \text{ and } B = \dbinom{100}{k+3}. $$ Find the remainder when $M$ is divided by $1000$ .

A sequence of numbers is first written on the blackboard: $1,2,3, \cdots, 100$. If each time the first 6 numbers are erased, and the sum of these 6 erased numbers is written at the end of the sequence, a new sequence of numbers is obtained. The same operation is repeated until fewer than 6 numbers remain on the blackboard. Ask:
(1) What is the sum of the numbers left on the blackboard at the end?
(2) What is the last number written?

Place 2004 bookmarks around a circle, with one side of each bookmark being white and the other side being black. Define a move as selecting a bookmark with the black side up and flipping this bookmark as well as the 2 adjacent bookmarks. Suppose at the beginning, only 1 bookmark has the black side up. Can all bookmarks be turned to the white side up after several moves? What about the case with 2003 bookmarks?

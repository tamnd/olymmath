Given the quadratic equation $a x^{2}+b x+c=0$, with roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$. Prove that the equation whose roots are $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}$ and $2 x_{1} x_{2}$, will have the form:

$$
a^{3} x^{2}-a b^{2} x+2 c\left(b^{2}-2 a c\right)=0
$$

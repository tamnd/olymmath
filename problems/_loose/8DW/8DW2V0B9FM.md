Given two triangles: $A B C$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. It is known that $A B=A_{1} B_{1}, A C=A_{1} C_{1}, \angle A=\angle A_{1}$. On the sides $A C$ and $B C$ of triangle $A B C$, points $K$ and $L$ are taken, and on the sides $A_{1} C_{1}$ and $B_{1} C_{1}$ of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, points $K_{1}$ and $L_{1}$ are taken such that $A K=A_{1} K_{1}, L C=L_{1} C_{1}$. Prove that $K L=K_{1} L_{1}$ and $A L=A_{1} L_{1}$.

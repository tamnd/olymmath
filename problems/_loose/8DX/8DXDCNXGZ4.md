Peter has 111 red and 111 blue marbles at home, which are made by his uncle. Peter can exchange 11 red marbles for 7 blue ones or 20 blue marbles for 28 red ones at his uncle's place every day.

(a) Can Peter increase the total number of marbles by 20 after a few days?

(b) Can Peter increase the total number of marbles by 33 after a few days?

(c) Can Peter achieve, after a few days, that he has three times as many blue marbles as red ones?

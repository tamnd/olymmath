A sphere and a cone have the same volume. The area of the lateral surface of the cone is $80 \pi$ and the total surface area of the cone is $144 \pi$. Determine the radius of the sphere.

(The volume of a sphere with radius $r$ is $\frac{4}{3} \pi r^{3}$. The volume of a cone with radius $r$ and height $h$ is $\frac{1}{3} \pi r^{2} h$. The surface area of a cone consists of two parts: its base and its lateral surface. The lateral surface area of a cone with radius $r$ and slant height $s$ is $\pi r s$.)

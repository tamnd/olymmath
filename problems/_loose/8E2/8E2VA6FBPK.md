Given a right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at vertex $C$. Let $D$ be any interior point on the leg $AC$ and $p$ the perpendicular from point $D$ to the hypotenuse $AB$. Let $E \neq D$ be a point on line $p$ such that points $A, B, D$, and $E$ lie on a circle. Let $F$ be the intersection of line $p$ and $BC$. Prove that $|AE|=|AF|$.

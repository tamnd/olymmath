Consider three quadratic equations

$$
x^{2}+b_{i} x+c_{i}=0
$$

( $i=1,2,3$ ). Suppose that the discriminants $\Delta_{i}=b_{i}^{2}-4 c_{i}$ are respectively 1, 4, and 9.

Show that one can choose a root from each equation such that the sum of these roots is equal to the sum of the other roots.

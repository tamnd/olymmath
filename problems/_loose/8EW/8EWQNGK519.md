For a given chord $MN$ of a circle, triangles $ABC$ are considered, the bases of which are diameters $AB$ of this circle, not intersecting $MN$, and sides $AC$ and $BC$ pass through the ends $M$ and $N$ of the chord $MN$. Prove that the altitudes of all such triangles $ABC$, dropped from vertex $C$ to side $AB$, intersect at one point.

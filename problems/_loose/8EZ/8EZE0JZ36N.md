Let the sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be defined by $x_{n}=\left\{(2+\sqrt{3})^{n}\right\}^{\frac{a n}{(2-\sqrt{3})^{n}}}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, where $a \in \mathbb{R}^{*}$, and $\{t\}$ denotes the fractional part of the real number $t$.

a) Show that $(2+\sqrt{3})^{n}+(2-\sqrt{3})^{n} \in \mathbb{N}, \forall n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

b) Determine the values of the parameter $a$ for which the sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ is convergent.

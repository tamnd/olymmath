In the cave under Krim, a terrible beast sleeps. When it gets hungry, it wakes up and eats as many sheep as the sum of the digits of that year. Then it goes back to sleep for as many years as the sheep it has eaten. We know that it woke up on April 12, 666. Could the beast be back? Could it possibly wake up in the year 3003?

The task should be solved independently. You have 210 minutes available for solving.

The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

47th Mathematical Competition for High School Students in Slovenia

Želimlje, April 12, 2003

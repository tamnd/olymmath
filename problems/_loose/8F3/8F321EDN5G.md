Define an "operation": cutting a rope of positive integer length into three ropes of positive integer length, with the longest rope being exactly one. Given a rope of length $a^{b}$ (where $a$ and $b$ are integers greater than 1), two players, A and B, take turns, with A starting first by performing one operation on the rope, then B performing one operation on the longest rope resulting from A's operation, and so on. Each player performs an operation on the longest rope from the three ropes resulting from the previous operation by the other player. If a player cannot perform an operation when it is their turn, the other player wins. Question: Who has a winning strategy?

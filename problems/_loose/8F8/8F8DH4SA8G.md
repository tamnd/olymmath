Let's prove the following identity:

$$
\left(\frac{\frac{a-c}{a+c}+\frac{b-c}{b+c}}{\frac{a+c}{a-c}+\frac{b+c}{b-c}}\right)^{2}=\frac{\left(\frac{a-c}{a+c}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{b-c}{b+c}\right)^{2}}{\left(\frac{a+c}{a-c}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{b+c}{b-c}\right)^{2}}
$$

Can the identity be written more simply with different notations? Is the identity valid if we replace the exponents 2 with 3?

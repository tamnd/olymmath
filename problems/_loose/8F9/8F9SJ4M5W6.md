In $\triangle A B C$, $I$ is the incenter, $M_{A} 、 M_{B} 、 M_{C}$ are the midpoints of $B C 、 C A 、 A B$ respectively, and $H_{A} 、 H_{B} 、 H_{C}$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $A 、 B 、 C$ to the opposite sides. Let $l_{b}$ denote the tangent line to the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ at point $B$, and $l_{b}^{\prime}$ be the reflection of $l_{b}$ over $B I$. Let $M_{A} M_{C}$ intersect $l_{b}$ at point $P_{B}$, and $H_{A} H_{C}$ intersect $l_{b}^{\prime}$ at point $Q_{B}$. Similarly define $l_{c} 、 l_{c}^{\prime} 、 P_{C} 、 Q_{C}$. If $P_{B} Q_{B}$ intersects $P_{C} Q_{c}$ at point $R$, prove: $R B=R C$.

Let $(E, \mathscr{E})$ be a Borel space, i.e., a measurable space for which there exists an injective mapping $\varphi: E \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that $\varphi(E) \in \mathscr{B}(\mathbb{R})$ and $\{\varphi(E) \cap B: B \in \mathscr{B}(\mathbb{R})\}=\{\varphi(C): C \in \mathscr{E}\}$. Show that there exists a countably-generated algebra $\mathscr{A}$ such that $\sigma(\mathscr{A})=\mathscr{E}$.

It is known that both КРУГ and КУБ are cubes of some numbers. Find all possible values of the numbers КРУГ and КУБ. List them all and explain why there are no others. (Identical digits are replaced by the same letters, different digits - by different letters.)

Note: 8 and 1000 are cubes, since $8=2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2=2^{3}$, and $1000=10 \cdot 10 \cdot 10=10^{3}$.

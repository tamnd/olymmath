Two circles intersect on a plane, and $A$ is one of the intersection points. There are two points that start from $A$ simultaneously, moving at constant speeds along their respective circumferences in the same direction, and both points return to their starting point after one complete revolution. Prove that there is a fixed point $P$ on this plane such that the distances from $P$ to the two moving points are equal at any time.

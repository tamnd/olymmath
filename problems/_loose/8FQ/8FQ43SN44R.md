Let $k$ be an integer and define a sequence $a_0 , a_1 ,a_2 ,\ldots$ by $$ a_0 =0 , \; a_1 =k \;\;\text{and} \; a_{n+2} =k^{2}a_{n+1}-a_n \text{for} n\geq 0. $$ Prove that $a_{n+1} a_n +1$ divides $a_{n+1}^{2} +a_{n}^{2}$ for all $n$ .

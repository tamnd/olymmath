Given two fair dice, each with at least six faces, each face has a different number, and is labeled with 1, $2, \cdots, n$ (where $n$ is the number of faces on the die). When the two dice are rolled simultaneously, the probability that their sum is 7 is $\frac{3}{4}$ of the probability that their sum is 10, and is $\frac{1}{12}$ of the probability that their sum is 12. The minimum sum of the number of faces on the two dice is ( ).
(A) 16
(B) 17
(C) 18
(D) 19
(E) 20

If the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ of a convex quadrilateral are perpendicular to each other and intersect at $E$, and perpendiculars are drawn from $E$ to the sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $P, Q, R, S$ respectively, and these perpendiculars intersect the sides $C D, D A, A B, B C$ at $P^{\prime}, Q^{\prime}, R^{\prime}, S^{\prime}$ respectively, and then $P^{\prime} Q^{\prime}, Q^{\prime} R^{\prime}, R^{\prime} S^{\prime}, S^{\prime} P^{\prime}$ are connected in sequence, then $R^{\prime} S^{\prime} / / P^{\prime} Q^{\prime} / / A C ; R^{\prime} Q^{\prime} / / P^{\prime} S^{\prime} / / B D$.

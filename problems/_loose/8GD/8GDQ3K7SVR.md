Given an acute triangle $ABC$ with orthocenter $H$. The angle bisector of $\angle BHC$ intersects side $BC$ at point $D$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the reflections of point $D$ across the lines $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $AEF$ passes through the midpoint $G$ of the arc $BAC$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

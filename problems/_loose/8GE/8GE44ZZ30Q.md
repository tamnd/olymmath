$$
\frac{\left(x^{5}-13 x^{3}+36 x\right) \cdot\left(x^{4}-17 x^{2}+16\right)}{\left(y^{5}-13 y^{3}+36 y\right) \cdot\left(y^{4}-17 y^{2}+16\right)} \geq 0
$$

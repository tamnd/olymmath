We define the star graph $K_{r}$ as the graph with vertices $x, u_{1}, \ldots, u_{r}$ and edges $\left\{x, u_{1}\right\}, \ldots,\left\{x, u_{r}\right\}$. Show that every graph with $\mathrm{n}$ vertices and $\mathrm{m}$ edges contains at least $n\left(\frac{2 m}{r}\right)$ subgraphs isomorphic to $K_{r}$. (we set $\left.\binom{x}{r}=\frac{1}{r!} x(x-1) \cdots(x-r+1)\right)$

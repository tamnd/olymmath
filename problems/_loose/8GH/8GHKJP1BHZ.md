A regular quadrilateral pyramid has its base as the square $ABCD$, and the vertex opposite to the base is $M$. A point $P$ lies either inside or on the perimeter of the base square. Show that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the faces $MAB$ and $MCD$ is equal to the sum of the distances from $P$ to the faces $MBC$ and $MDA$.

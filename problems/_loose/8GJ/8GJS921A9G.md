Given a function $f(x)$ defined on $\mathbf{R}$ that satisfies $f(-x)=-f(x+4)$, and when $x>2$, $f(x)$ is monotonically increasing. If $x_{1}+x_{2}<4$ and $\left(x_{1}-2\right)\left(x_{2}-2\right)<0$, determine the sign of $f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)$ and provide a proof.

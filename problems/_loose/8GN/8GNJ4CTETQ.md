In a chess tournament, every player scored at most $k$ points. (Half a point for a draw, one point for a win.) Prove that then

a) there is a player who played at most $2 k$ games;

b) the players can be placed in at most $2 k+1$ rooms, so that players in the same room did not play against each other.

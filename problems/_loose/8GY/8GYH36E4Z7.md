Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with side lengths $a, b, c$, and $R, r$ representing the circumradius and inradius of $\triangle ABC$ respectively. Prove:
$$
\sum \sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}} \geqslant 3 \sqrt{\frac{R}{2 r}} .
$$

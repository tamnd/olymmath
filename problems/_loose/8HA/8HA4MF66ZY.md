Let $a, b$ be integers such that $1 < b < a$ . Suppose that
$$
(a^{2} - 1)(b^{2} - 1)
$$
is a perfect square.

a) Prove that $a + b$ is composite.
b) If $a - b$ is prime, prove that $6ab - 3$ is a perfect square.

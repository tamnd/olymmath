There are $n$ people standing in a row, reporting numbers in the order of $1,2, \cdots, n$ from left to right. Those whose numbers are perfect squares leave the row; the remaining people report numbers again from left to right in the order of $1,2, \cdots$, and those whose numbers are perfect squares leave the row; this continues. In this process, everyone will eventually leave the row. Let $f(n)$ denote the initial number of the last person to leave the row. Find the expression for $f(n)$ (in terms of $n$); and give the value of $f(2019)$.

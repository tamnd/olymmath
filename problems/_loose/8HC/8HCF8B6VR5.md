Let there be given two inscribed polygons. Let their sides be $a_{1} \geqq a_{2} \geqq \ldots \geqq a_{k}$ and $b_{1} \geqq b_{2} \geqq \ldots \geqq b_{l}$. Suppose that $a_{1}<b_{1}, a_{2}<b_{2}, \ldots, a_{l}<b_{l}$. Prove that the perimeter and area of the first polygon are also greater than those of the second.

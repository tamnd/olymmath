Let ABC be an acute triangle with $A B < A C < B C$ and let $\Omega$ be its circumcircle. Let $D$ and $E$ be the points diametrically opposite to points $B$ and $C$ respectively in the circle $\Omega$. The circle centered at $A$ with radius $A E$ intersects $[A C]$ at $K$. The circle centered at $A$ with radius $A D$ intersects $(BA)$ at $L$ (such that $A$ lies between B and $L$). Show that the lines $(EK)$ and $(DL)$ intersect on the circle $\Omega$.

Distribute the four cards $2, 4, 7, K$ to four people, each person scores according to the number on the card ($K$ is counted as 13), then the cards are collected, reshuffled, and distributed and scored again, $\cdots$, after several rounds, it is found that the four people have accumulated scores of $16, 17, 21$ and 24. It is known that the person who scored 16 points last scored 2 points. Then his score in the first round is
(A) 2 .
(B) 4 .
(C) 7
(D) 13 .
(2nd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be strictly positive real numbers such that

$$
\frac{1}{x_{1}+1998}+\ldots+\frac{1}{x_{n}+1998}=\frac{1}{1998}
$$

prove that

$$
\frac{\sqrt[n]{x_{1} \ldots x_{n}}}{n-1} \geq 1998
$$

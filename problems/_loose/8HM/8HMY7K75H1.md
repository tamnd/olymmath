Given a triangle $A B C$ with angle $C=120^{\circ}$. Point $D$ is the foot of the perpendicular dropped from point $C$ to side $A B$; points $E$ and $F$ are the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $D$ to sides $A C$ and $B C$ respectively. Find the perimeter of triangle $A B C$, if it is known that triangle $E F C$ is isosceles and its area is $\sqrt{3}$.

(power of a point with respect to a circle) Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a circle and $P$ a point outside $\mathcal{C}$. Two lines starting from $P$ intersect $\mathcal{C}$ at $A, B$ and at $C, D$ respectively. Show that $P A \cdot P B = P C \cdot P D$. What happens to this result if $P$ is inside the circle?

A hexagon $A B C C^{\prime} B^{\prime} A^{\prime}$ is inscribed in a circle. Let $\left(B C^{\prime}\right) \cap\left(B^{\prime} C\right)=A_{1},\left(C A^{\prime}\right) \cap\left(C^{\prime} A\right)=B_{1}, \left(A B^{\prime}\right) \cap\left(A^{\prime} B\right)=C_{1}$. Prove that the points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ lie on the same line, after first proving that the three circles circumscribed around triangles $B C A_{1}, C A B_{1}$ and $A B C_{1}$ intersect at one point ${ }^{1}$.

(Olympiad Training Problem from "Intermediate Mathematics" Issue 3, 2004) For the complex numbers $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$, the following three conditions are given: (1) $\frac{z_{1}-\overline{z_{1}}}{z_{2}-\overline{z_{2}}}=0 ;(2) \overline{z_{2}}+6=\frac{2}{z_{2}+6} ;(3) z_{1} \cdot z_{2}^{2}+z_{2}+2=0$.
Does there exist complex numbers $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$ that simultaneously satisfy these three conditions? If they exist, find $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$; if not, explain the reason.

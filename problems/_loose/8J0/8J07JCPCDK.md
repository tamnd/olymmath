Find all values of the parameter $a$ for which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a^{2} x+2 a}{a x-2+a^{2}} \geqslant 0 \\
a x+a>\frac{5}{4}
\end{array}\right.
$$

has no solutions.

We drew three internally tangent circles inside a circle, each passing through the center of the original circle, and their centers are the vertices of a triangle with two angles of $30^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}$. The arcs of the smaller circles divide the area of the original circle into segments. Arrange the segments in order of their area, and also in order of the measure of their perimeter. Are there any two segments among them that have both the same area and the same perimeter?

Compute surface integrals of the first kind:
a) $\iint_{\sigma}|x| d S, \sigma: x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1, z \geqslant 0$

b) $\iint_{\sigma}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right) d S, \sigma: x^{2}+y^{2}=2 z, z=1$;

c) $\iint_{\sigma}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right) d S$, where $\sigma$ is the part of the cone $z^{2}-x^{2}-y^{2}=0$, $z \geqslant 0$, cut off by the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x=0$.

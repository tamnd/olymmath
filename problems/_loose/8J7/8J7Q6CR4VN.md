There are two piles of candies, and two people take turns to perform the following operation: at each step, one can take away one pile of candies, and if the other pile has more than one, then it can be arbitrarily divided into two piles; if the other pile has only one, then this one is also taken away to win. Suppose at the beginning, the number of candies in the two piles are $p$ and $q$ respectively, what necessary and sufficient condition should $(p, q)$ satisfy so that the one who starts the operation is sure to win?

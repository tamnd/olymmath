From the origin $O$ of a spatial, rectangular coordinate system, the segments $a, b$, and $c$ are laid off on the axes to the points $A, B$, and $C$ respectively. Then a perpendicular is dropped from $O$ to the plane $A B C$. Its foot is $M$. It is to be proven that the perpendicular has the length:

$$
h=\sqrt{\frac{1}{\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}+\frac{1}{c^{2}}}}
$$

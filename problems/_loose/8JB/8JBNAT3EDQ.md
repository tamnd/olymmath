The product of positive numbers $x, y$, and $z$ is 1.

Prove that if $1 / x + 1 / y + 1 / z \geq x + y + z$, then for any natural number $k$ the inequality $x^{-k} + y^{-k} + z^{-k} \geq x^{k} + y^{k} + z^{k}$ holds.

Let the integer $n \geqslant 3, A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ be a circumscribed convex $n$-gon of the unit circle, and for $i=1,2, \cdots, n$, $A_{i} A_{i+1}$ is tangent to the unit circle at point $B_{i}\left(A_{n+1}=A_{1}\right)$. Denote the perimeters of the $n$-gons $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} B_{2} \cdots B_{n}$ as $p_{A} \backslash p_{B}$, respectively. Prove: $p_{A} p_{B}^{2}>8 \pi^{3}$.

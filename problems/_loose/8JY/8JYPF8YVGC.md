In an Olympic training camp, there are $a$ trainees and $b$ examiners, where $b$ is odd and at least 3. Each examiner decides for each trainee whether they have passed or failed the training. Let $k$ be an integer such that no two examiners can have the same opinion on more than $k$ trainees. Show the inequality

$$
\frac{k}{a} \geqslant \frac{b-1}{2 b}
$$

For integers $n \geq 1$ and $n \geq 3$, determine the two-variable polynomials $P$ that have the following properties:

a) for all $x, y, t$, $P(t x, t y)=t^{n} P(x, y)$,

b) for all $u_{1}, u_{2}, \ldots, u_{k}$,

$$
P\left(u_{1}+\ldots+u_{k} ; u_{1}\right)+P\left(u_{1}+\ldots+u_{k} ; u_{2}\right)+\ldots+P\left(u_{1}+\ldots+u_{k} ; u_{k}\right)=0
$$

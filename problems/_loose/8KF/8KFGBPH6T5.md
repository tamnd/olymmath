Given a circle with center $O$. On the extension of chord $A B$ beyond point $B$, a segment $B C$ equal to the radius is laid out. A secant $C D$ (where $D$ is the point of intersection with the circle, lying outside the segment $C O$) is drawn through points $C$ and $O$. Prove that $\angle A O D = 3 \angle A C D$.

Let the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ be $\Gamma$, and the incenter be $I$. Take a point $D$ on the arc $\gg C$ not containing $B$. Draw the tangent to circle $\Gamma$ at $D$ and let it intersect the extension of $B C$ at $P$. Draw the angle bisector of $\angle D P C$ and let it intersect lines $B I$ and $C I$ at $E$ and $F$ respectively. Prove that points $D$, $E$, $I$, and $F$ are concyclic.

Let $A$ be a natural number. Denote by $b$ the number formed by the last two digits of $A$, and by $a$ the number formed by the remaining digits of $A$. Prove that the number $A$ is divisible by 7 if and only if:
a) $2a + b$ is divisible by 7; b) $5a - b$ is divisible by 7.

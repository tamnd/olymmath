Jutta, Günter, and Klaus are participating in the second stage of the Mathematics Olympiad.

(1) They work (not necessarily in this order) in rooms 48, 49, 50.

(2) Jutta and Günter are the same age, Klaus is one year older than Jutta.

(3) Their three mathematics teachers, Mr. Adler, Mr. Bär, and Mr. Drossel, supervise in these three rooms during the work, but none of them supervises in the room where their own student works.

(4) Mr. Bär has the same first name as his student.

(5) The number of the room where Mr. Drossel supervises corresponds to one and a half times his age.

(6) Günter's room has a higher number than Klaus's room.

(7) The three students together are exactly as old as the number of the room where Jutta works.

(8) Jutta does not know Mr. Drossel.

What is Mr. Bär's first name? In which room does he supervise? (In the age specification, only full years are considered.)

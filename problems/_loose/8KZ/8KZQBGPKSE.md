In triangle $ABC$, the first trisector of the angle at vertex $A$ is the ray that forms an angle with side $AC$ that is twice as large as the angle it forms with side $AB$. Similarly, the first trisector of angle $CBA$ forms an angle with side $BA$ that is twice as large as the angle it forms with the other side, and the first trisector of angle $ACB$ forms an angle with side $CB$ that is twice as large as the angle it forms with the other side. Show that if the angles of the triangle determined by these three trisectors are respectively equal to one of the angles of triangle $ABC$, then both triangles are equilateral.

Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
a_{1}=1, a_{n+1}=3 a_{n}+2 \sqrt{2 a_{n}^{2}-1}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) \text {. }
$$
(1) Prove: $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a sequence of positive integers;
(2) Does there exist a positive integer $m$, such that $2015 \mid a_{m}$? $^{[4]}$
(2015, Zhejiang Province High School Mathematics Competition Additional Question)

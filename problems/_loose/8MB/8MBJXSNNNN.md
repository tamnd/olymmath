A coloring of $\mathbb{Q}$ consists of coloring every rational number either red or blue. A coloring of $\mathbb{Q}$ is said to be harmonious if, for all rational numbers $x$ and $y$ of the same color, the rational number $x+y$ is also of the same color.

Find all harmonious colorings of $\mathbb{Q}$.

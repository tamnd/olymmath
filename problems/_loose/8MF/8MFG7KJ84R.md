Show that if $n$ is a natural number, then the following inequality holds:

$$
\sqrt[n]{(n!)^{3}} \leq \frac{n(n+1)^{2}}{4}
$$

(The notation $n!$ represents the product of natural numbers from 1 to $n$.)

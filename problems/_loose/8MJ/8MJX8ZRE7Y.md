In a certain state, there were 2004 cities connected by roads such that from any city, one could reach any other city. It is known that even if any road was closed, it was still possible to travel from any city to any other city. The Minister of Transport and the Minister of Internal Affairs take turns introducing one-way traffic on the roads, as long as it is possible, with one road per turn. The minister who, after their move, makes it impossible to travel from some city to some other city, immediately resigns. The Minister of Transport moves first.

Can either minister force the other to resign, regardless of their play?

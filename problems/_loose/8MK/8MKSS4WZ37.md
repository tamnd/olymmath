On the plane, a line $l$ and a point $M$ are given. Let $M_{1}$ be the point symmetric to point $M$ with respect to line $l$. When line $l$ is translated in a direction perpendicular to it by a distance $h$, line $l$ becomes line $l_{1}$. Prove that the image $M_{2}$ of point $M$ under symmetry with respect to line $l_{1}$ is obtained from point $M_{1}$ by a parallel translation in the same direction by a distance $2 h$.

Let $p, q \in N, p$ be an odd number, and there are $p q$ beads, in $q$ colors, each color in $p$ beads. Assume that beads of the same color are indistinguishable. Try to find the number of arrangements of the beads that satisfy the following conditions respectively.
(1) Beads of the same color are together;
(2) Beads of the same color are in different blocks;
(3) Beads of the same color are in at most two blocks.

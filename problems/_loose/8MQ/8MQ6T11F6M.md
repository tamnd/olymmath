Let $f:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function . Suppose
$$f(x)=\frac{1}{t} \int^t_0 (f(x+y)-f(y)) dy$$
$\forall x\in \mathbb{R}$ and all $t>0$ . Then show that there exists a constant $c$ such that $f(x)=cx\ \forall x$

Lucie has a rectangular grid of dimensions $m \times n$. She has placed a token on the bottom left square of this grid. Then she moves her token from square to square, each time moving to another square that shares a side with the current one. She wants her token to pass through each square exactly once (it will therefore never return to its starting square) until it reaches the square located at the top right. For which values of $m$ and $n$ can she achieve this?

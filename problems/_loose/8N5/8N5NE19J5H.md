Let $a$ be an integer. Show that 5 divides $a^{2}$ if and only if 5 divides $a$.

We can slightly generalize Euclid's lemma:

Theorem 5 (Generalized Euclid's Lemma).

Let $p$ be a prime and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be integers. If $p$ divides $a_{1} \cdot a_{2} \cdots a_{n}$, then it divides one of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$.

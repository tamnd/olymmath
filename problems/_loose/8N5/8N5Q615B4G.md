For $\geqslant 1$, let $S_{n}$ be the set of polynomials of the form $p(z)=z^{n}+a_{n-1} z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1} z+1$, where $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n-1}$ vary over all complex numbers. Find the value of $M_{n}=$ $\min _{p \in S_{n}}\left(\max _{|z|=1}|p(z)|\right)$.

Given the family of curves

$$
y=(m-1) x^{2}+2 m x+4
$$

a) Prove that all the given curves pass through two fixed points $A$ and $B$. Determine these points.

b) Determine the curve from the given family that touches the $O x$ axis, and then determine the curve whose vertex is point B ( $B$ is the common point of the given curves, which does not lie on the $O x$ axis ).

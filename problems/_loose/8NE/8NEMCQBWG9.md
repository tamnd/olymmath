Let $\triangle A B C$ be equilateral. On side $A B$, points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ are chosen, on $A C$, points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ are chosen, and on side $B C$, points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ are chosen, such that

$$
\overline{A_{1} A_{2}}=\overline{B_{1} B_{2}}=\overline{C_{1} C_{2}}
$$

Let the intersection points of lines $A_{2} B_{1}$ and $B_{2} C_{1}$, $B_{2} C_{1}$ and $C_{2} A_{1}$, $C_{2} A_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{1}$ be $E, F$ and $G$, respectively. Prove that the triangle formed by segments $B_{1} A_{2}$, $A_{1} C_{2}$, and $C_{1} B_{2}$ is similar to triangle $\triangle E F G$.

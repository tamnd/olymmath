Calculate the angle $\gamma$ in triangle $ABC$, if

$$
\frac{1}{a+c}+\frac{1}{b+c}=\frac{3}{a+b+c}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of triangle $ABC$ opposite to vertices $A, B, C$ respectively, and $\gamma$ is the angle at vertex $C$.

Given a parallelogram $A B C D$. The inscribed circles of triangles $A B C$ and $A D C$ touch the diagonal $A C$ at points $X$ and $Y$. The inscribed circles of triangles $B C D$ and $B A D$ touch the diagonal $B D$ at points $Z$ and $T$. Prove that if all points $X, Y, Z, T$ are distinct, then they are the vertices of a rectangle.

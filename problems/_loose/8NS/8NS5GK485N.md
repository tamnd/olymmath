$M_{a}=[111(a+b+c)]: 3=37(a+b+c)$

Writing in base $10, \overline{a a b}+\overline{b b a}=111(a+b)$

$M_{a}=111(a+b): 2$

$[111(a+b)]: 2=37 \cdot(a+b+c) \Leftrightarrow 2 \cdot 37 \cdot(a+b+c)=111(a+b) \Leftrightarrow a+b=2 c$

On the board, there were 10 numbers. In one operation, it is allowed to erase any two numbers $a, b$ from the board and write $a+2b$ and $b+2a$ instead. Can it happen that after several operations all the numbers on the board become the same, if initially there were a) the numbers 1, $2, \ldots, 10;$ b) any 10 different numbers (not necessarily integers)?

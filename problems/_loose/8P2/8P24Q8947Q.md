Let $d$ be the sum of the lengths of all diagonals of a convex polygon of $n$ $(n>3)$ vertices, and let $p$ be its perimeter. Prove that
$$
\frac{n-3}{2}<\frac{d}{p}<\frac{1}{2}\left(\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]\left[\frac{n+1}{2}\right]-2\right) .
$$

(EGMO 2017, P3) Let 2017 lines be given in the plane such that no three lines intersect at the same point. Turbo the snail is located at a point that lies on only one of these lines. He moves along the lines in the following manner. He moves along a given line until he reaches an intersection. Each time he encounters an intersection, he continues his journey on the other line, turning left or right, alternating his choice at each intersection he encounters. No U-turns are allowed. Is it possible for him to traverse the same segment of a line in opposite directions during his journey?

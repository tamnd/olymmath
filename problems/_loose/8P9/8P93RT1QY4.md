26.1.3 ** There are $n$ cards, each with a number from 1 to $n$, and each card has a different number. The $n$ cards are lined up, and the following operation can be performed: swapping two adjacent cards. Prove: regardless of the initial arrangement of the cards, they can be sorted in descending order with at most $\frac{1}{2} n(n-1)$ operations.

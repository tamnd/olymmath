The 72nd question: Let $S=\{1,2, \ldots, n\}\left(n \in Z^{+}\right)$, color the $2^{\mathrm{n}}$ subsets of $S$ in red or blue (here, a subset is colored as a whole, not each element individually). For any subset $T \subseteq S$, let the number of blue subsets of $T$ be denoted as $\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{T})$. Find the number of coloring schemes that satisfy the following condition: For any subsets $\mathrm{T}_{1} 、 \mathrm{~T}_{2}$ of $S$, it holds that $\mathrm{f}\left(\mathrm{T}_{1}\right) \cdot \mathrm{f}\left(\mathrm{T}_{2}\right)=\mathrm{f}\left(\mathrm{T}_{1} \cup \mathrm{T}_{2}\right) \cdot \mathrm{f}\left(\mathrm{T}_{1} \cap \mathrm{T}_{2}\right)$.

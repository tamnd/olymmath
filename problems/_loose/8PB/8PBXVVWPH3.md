Let $a, b, c$ be three natural numbers such that $a<b<c$ and $\operatorname{gcd}(c-a, c-b)=1$. Suppose there exists an integer $d$ such that $a+d, b+d, c+d$ form the sides of a right-angled triangle. Prove that there exist integers $l$, $m$ such that $c+d=l^{2}+m^{2}$.

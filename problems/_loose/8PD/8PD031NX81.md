Find all functions $f:\{1,2,3, \ldots\} \rightarrow\{1,2,3, \ldots\}$ such that for every prime number $p$ and every positive integer $n$ we have:

$$
f(n)^{p} \equiv n \text { modulo } f(p)
$$

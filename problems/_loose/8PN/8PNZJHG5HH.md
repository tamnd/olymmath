Two companies take turns hiring programmers, among whom there are 4 geniuses. The first programmer each company chooses is arbitrary, but each subsequent one must be acquainted with someone previously hired by that company. If a company cannot hire a programmer according to these rules, it stops hiring, and the other company can continue. The list of programmers and their acquaintances is known in advance. Can the acquaintances be arranged in such a way that the company entering the game second can hire at least 3 geniuses, no matter how the first company acts?

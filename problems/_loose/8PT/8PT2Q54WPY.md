In a conference, there are $n$ professors $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, \cdots, A_{n}$ attending. Prove: It is possible to divide these $n$ people into two groups, such that for each person $A_{i}$, the number of people $d_{i}$ he knows in the other group is greater than or equal to the number of people $d^{\prime}_{i}$ he knows in the same group, where $i=1,2,3, \cdots, n$.

Given the triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, and the acute angle $\varphi$. Construct the triangle $A B C$ such that the base angles of the isosceles triangles constructed outward on the sides $A B, B C, C A$ are $\varphi$, and the third vertices of these isosceles triangles are $C_{1}, A_{1}, B_{1}$, respectively. What is the solvability condition if $\varphi=45$?

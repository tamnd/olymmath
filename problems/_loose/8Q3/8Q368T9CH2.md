Using the factorization of the difference of squares, $x^{2}-y^{2}=(x-y)(x+y)$, we can write

$$
x^{2^{n}}-y^{2^{n}}=\left(x^{2^{n-1}}+y^{2^{n-1}}\right)\left(x^{2^{n-1}}-y^{2^{n-1}}\right) \text {. }
$$

a) Explain why $3^{2^{2018}}-2^{2^{2018}}$ can be written as a product of 2018 distinct integers greater than 1.

b) Verify that $3^{2^{n}}-1=\left(3^{2^{n-1}}+1\right)\left(3^{2^{n-2}}+1\right)\left(3^{2^{n-3}}+1\right) \ldots\left(3^{2}+1\right)\left(3^{1}+1\right)\left(3^{1}-1\right)$.

c) Using the previous item, verify that $2^{n+1}$ is a divisor of $3^{2^{n}}-1$.

d) Knowing the factorization

$$
x^{m}-y^{m}=(x-y)\left(x^{m-1}+x^{m-2} y+x^{m-3} y^{2}+\ldots+x y^{m-2}+y^{m-2}\right)
$$

find a positive integer $n$ with more than 2018 positive divisors such that $3^{n-1}-2^{n-1}$ is a multiple of $n$.

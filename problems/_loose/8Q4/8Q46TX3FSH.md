(Wiener.) Let $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be i.i.d. random variables,

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{1} \geqslant 0\right)=1 \text { and } \mathrm{E} \xi_{1} < \infty
$$

Define

$$
\tau=\inf \left\{n \geqslant 1: \sum_{i=1}^{n} \xi_{i} \geqslant a n\right\}
$$

Show that

$$
\mathrm{E} \tau < \infty
$$

Remark. The result of the problem remains valid if we consider a strictly stationary random sequence $\left\{\xi_{n}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1} \cdot$

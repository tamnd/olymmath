In a football tournament with $n \geqslant 4$ teams, each pair of teams played against each other exactly once. In the final table, the scores of the teams are $n$ consecutive numbers. Find the maximum possible score of the winner of the tournament. **Note:** A victory gives $3$ points, a draw gives $1$ point and a loss gives $0$ points.

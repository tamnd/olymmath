Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $D \in [AB]$ and $E \in [AC]$.

- Show that the lines $(BE)$ and $(CD)$ necessarily intersect at a point, which we will denote as $F$.
- Show that the circles $\mathcal{C}_{1}, \mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathcal{C}_{3}$, and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ circumscribed around $ABE, ACD, BDF$, and $CEF$ are concurrent, at a point which we will denote as $M$.
- Let $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$, and $O_{4}$ be the centers of $\mathcal{C}_{1}, \mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathcal{C}_{3}$, and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ respectively: show that $M, O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$, and $O_{4}$ are concyclic.

In quadrilateral $ABCD$, let
$$
AB=a, BC=b, CD=c, DA=d,
$$

the area be $S$, and
$$
\begin{array}{c}
a+c=b+d, \\
A+C=B+D, \\
S^{2}=a b c d,
\end{array}
$$

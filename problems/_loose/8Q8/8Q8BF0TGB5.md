Prove: For the altitudes $h_{i}$ and the radii of the exspheres $r_{i}(i=1,2,3,4)$ of any tetrahedron, we have $2 \sum_{i=1}^{4} \frac{1}{h_{i}}=\sum_{i=1}^{4} \frac{1}{r_{i}}$.
(1973 Czech Competition Problem)

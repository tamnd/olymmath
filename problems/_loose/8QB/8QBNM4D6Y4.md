The sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{1}=a_{2}=a_{3}=1$, and any four consecutive terms $a_{n}, a_{n+1}, a_{n+2}, a_{n+3}$ satisfy: $a_{n} a_{n+3}-a_{n+1} a_{n+2}=1$. Prove: For any three consecutive terms $a_{n}, a_{n+1}, a_{n+2}$, the sum of the first and last terms is necessarily a multiple of the middle term $a_{n+1}$.

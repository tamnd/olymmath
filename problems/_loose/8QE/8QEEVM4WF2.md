In $\triangle ABC$ , $\angle B = \angle C = 30^{\circ}$ and $BC=2\sqrt 3$ . $P, Q$ lie on segments $\overline{AB}, \overline{AC}$ such that $AP=1$ and $AQ=\sqrt 2$ . Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to $BC$ . We fold $\triangle ABC$ along line $AD$ in three dimensions such that the dihedral angle between planes $ADB$ and $ADC$ equals $60$ degrees. Under this transformation, compute the length $PQ$ .

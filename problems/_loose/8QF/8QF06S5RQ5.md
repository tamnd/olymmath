a) On a plane, a point $ M $ and two lines $ l_{1}, l_{2} $ are given. Construct a triangle $ ABC $ such that the point $ M $ divides the side $ AB $ in a given ratio $ \frac{BM}{MA} = k $, and $ l_{1} $ and $ l_{2} $ coincide with the perpendiculars dropped from the midpoints of sides $ BC $ and $ CA $ to these sides.

b) On a plane, two points $ M, N $ and a line $ l $ are given. Construct a triangle $ ABC $ such that the points $ M $ and $ N $ divide the sides $ AB $ and $ BC $ in given ratios $ \frac{BM}{MA} = k_{1} $ and $ \frac{CN}{NB} = k_{2} $, and $ l $ coincides with the perpendicular dropped from the midpoint of side $ AC $ to this side.

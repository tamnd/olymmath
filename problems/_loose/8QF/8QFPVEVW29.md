Given a right triangle $A B C$; angle $C$ is right, $O$ is the center of the inscribed circle, $M$ is the point of tangency of the inscribed circle with the hypotenuse, the circle with center at $M$, passing through $O$, intersects the angle bisectors of angles $A$ and $B$ at points $K$ and $L$, different from $O$. Prove that $K$ and $L$ are the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles $A C D$ and $B C D$, where $C D$ is the altitude of triangle $A B C$.

A substitute teacher leads a group of students on an outing. The homeroom teacher informs the substitute that there are two mischievous students who sometimes lie (but the substitute does not know which two students they are), while the other students always tell the truth. They get lost in the forest and eventually gather at a crossroads. The substitute teacher knows: their campsite is on one of the four paths, and it is 20 minutes away from this intersection, but they are unsure which path it is on. The students must return to the campsite before dark.
(1) If there are 8 students in the group, and it is 60 minutes before dark, please provide a plan to ensure that all students return to the campsite before dark, and explain your reasoning.
(2) If there are 4 students in the group, and it is 100 minutes before dark, is there a plan to ensure that all students return to the campsite before dark? Prove your conclusion.

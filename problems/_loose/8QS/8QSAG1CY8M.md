Let $T$ be the centroid of a scalene triangle $ABC$. Denote by $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ the midpoints of the sides $\overline{BC}, \overline{CA}$, and $\overline{AB}$, and by $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ the midpoints of the segments $\overline{AT}, \overline{BT}$, and $\overline{CT}$, respectively. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A_{1} B_{2} C_{2}, A_{2} B_{1} C_{2}$, and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{1}$ intersect at a single point.

In Italy, license plates consist of 2 letters, followed by 3 digits, and then by 2 more letters. In the country of Ailati, things are reversed, and the plates consist of 2 digits, followed by 3 letters, and then by 2 more digits. Assuming that in both countries 10 digits and 22 letters are used (I, O, U, Q are not used), determine the difference between the number of all possible plates in the two countries.
(A) 0
(B) $12 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 22^{3}$
(C) $\left(22^{2}-10^{2}\right)^{2}-\left(22^{3}-10^{3}\right)$
(D) $12 \cdot 3 \cdot 22 \cdot 3 \cdot 10$
(E) $4^{10} \cdot 3^{10} \cdot\left(4^{12}-3^{12}\right)$.

$$
93+93^{2}+93^{3}+\ldots+93^{12 n}
$$

is divisible by 100 for any natural number $n$. - Is it true that for every two-digit natural number, there exists a $k$ such that the sum of its first $k n$ (natural number exponents) powers is divisible by 100?

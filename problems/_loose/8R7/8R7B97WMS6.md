A point inside a convex quadrilateral is called a "good point" if the lines connecting it to the four vertices divide the quadrilateral into four triangles of equal area.
Proposition A: There exists a "good point" inside the quadrilateral.
Proposition B: One diagonal of the convex quadrilateral bisects the other diagonal. Then the correct statement is
(A) A is a sufficient but not necessary condition for B.
(B) B is a sufficient but not necessary condition for A.
(C) A is a necessary and sufficient condition for B.
(D) B is neither a sufficient nor a necessary condition for A.
(Anhui Province Wuhu City High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

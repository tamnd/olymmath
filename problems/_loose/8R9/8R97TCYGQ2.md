Let the ellipse be $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, with foci $F_{1}(-c, 0), F_{2}(c, 0)$, and point $Q$ be a point on the ellipse different from the vertices of the major axis. Draw the perpendicular from focus $F_{1}$ (or $F_{2}$) to the external angle bisector of $\angle F_{1} Q F_{2}$, and let the foot of the perpendicular be $P$. Then the locus of point $P$ is a circle centered at the origin with radius $a$ (excluding points $(-a, 0),(a, 0))$.

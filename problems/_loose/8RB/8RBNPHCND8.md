A rectangular parallelepiped $T$ is divided into eight smaller rectangular parallelepipeds by three planes. The smaller parallelepipeds are colored black and white such that adjacent pieces are of different colors. We know that the sum of the volumes of the black parallelepipeds is equal to the sum of the volumes of the white parallelepipeds. Prove that one of the planes bisects $T$.

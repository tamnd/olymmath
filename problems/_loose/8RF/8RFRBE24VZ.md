Let $M$ be the set of all integer points in the plane, and a polyline formed by points $\left\{P_{0}, P_{1}, \cdots, P_{n}\right\}$ in $M$ satisfies $P_{i-1} P_{i}=1, i=1,2, \cdots, n$, then this polyline is said to have a length of $n . F(n)$ represents the number of different polylines of length $n$ starting from the origin $P_{0}$ and ending at a point $P_{n}$ on the $x$-axis. Prove that $F(n)=C{ }_{2 n}^{n}$.

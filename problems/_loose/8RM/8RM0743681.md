Elementary arithmetic. Here is one question similar to those that were so popular in Venice (and not only there) in the mid-16th century. Their appearance was largely due to Niccolò Fontana, better known as Tartaglia (the stammerer).

If a quarter of twenty were four, then what would a third of ten be?

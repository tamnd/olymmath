a) Given a convex polygon. It is known that for any three of its sides, a point $O$ inside the polygon can be chosen such that the perpendiculars dropped from point $O$ to these three sides fall on the sides themselves, not on their extensions. Prove that then such a point $O$ can be chosen for all sides simultaneously.

b) Prove that in the case of a convex quadrilateral, such a point $O$ can be chosen if it can be chosen for any two sides.

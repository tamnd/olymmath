Given the function $f(x)=|\sin x|, x \in \mathbf{R}$.
(1) Prove: $\sin 1 \leqslant f(x)+f(x+1) \leqslant 2 \cos \frac{1}{2}$;
(2) Prove: For any positive integer $n$, $\frac{f(n)}{n}+\frac{f(n+1)}{n+1}+\cdots+\frac{f(3 n-1)}{3 n-1}>\frac{\sin 1}{2}$.

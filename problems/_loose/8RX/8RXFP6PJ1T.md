In $\triangle ABC$, if the altitudes are $AD, BE, CF$, and the orthocenter is $H$, prove:
(1) $A H \cdot H D=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{2}-4 R^{2}$; ($H$ is inside the triangle)
(2) $S_{\triangle A E F}=\triangle \cos ^{2} A$;
(3) $D E+E F+F D=\frac{a b c}{2 R^{2}}$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with orthocenter $H$ and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. We denote $H_{B}$ and $H_{C}$ as the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$, $M$ as the midpoint of $[BC]$, and $K$ as the second point of intersection of $\Gamma$ with the circumcircle of $A H_{B} H_{C}$. Show that $M, H$, and $K$ are collinear.

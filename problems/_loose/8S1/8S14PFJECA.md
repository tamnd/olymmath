Let $A B C$ be a right triangle at $B$. Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $B$ and let $D$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle A$ with side $B C$. Suppose that $H D$ is perpendicular to $B C$. What is $\left(\frac{A B}{B C}\right)^{2}$?
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$
(C) $\sqrt{5}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{2}$
(E) Such a configuration cannot be realized.

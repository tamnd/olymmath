Change the order of integration in the following integrals:
a) $\int_{-1}^{0} d x \int_{x+1}^{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} f(x, y) d y$
b) $\int_{0}^{1} d x \int_{x}^{2-x^{2}} f(x, y) d y$
c) $\int_{1}^{2} d y \int_{1 / y}^{y} f(x, y) d x$;
d) $\int_{-2}^{1} d y \int_{y^{2}}^{4} f(x, y) d x$
e) $\int_{0}^{2} d x \int_{\sqrt{2 x-x^{2}}}^{2} f(x, y) d y$.

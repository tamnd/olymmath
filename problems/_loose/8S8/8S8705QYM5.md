The integers $1,2, \ldots, 20$ have been arranged around a circle, in some order. For each of these integers $k$, Matthieu counts how many integers are less than $k$ among the 9 integers that follow $k$ when traversing the circle clockwise; he counts $A(k)$. He also counts how many integers are less than $k$ among the 9 integers that follow $k$ when traversing the circle counterclockwise; he counts $B(k)$. Matthieu then notes that $A(k)=B(k)$ for all $k$. What is the number diametrically opposite to 11 on the circle?

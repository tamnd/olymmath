Given the points $A_{0}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{2}$. Construct a triangle $ABC$ such that the foot of the altitude from $A$ is $A_{0}$, the midpoint of side $AC$ is $B_{1}$, and the midpoint of the segment of the altitude from $C$ is $C_{2}$. (Of course, we will also examine how many solutions the problem has depending on the relative positions of the given points.)

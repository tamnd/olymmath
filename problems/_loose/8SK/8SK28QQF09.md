In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, $n$ points $M_{1}\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right), \cdots, M_{n}\left(x_{n}, y_{n}\right)$ satisfy $y_{1}>0, \cdots, y_{k}>0$, $y_{k+1}<0, \cdots, y_{n}<0(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$. On the x-axis, there are $n+1$ points $A_{1}, \cdots, A_{n+1}$, and for each point $A_{j}(1 \leqslant$ $j \leqslant n+1)$, we have $\sum_{i=1}^{k} \angle M_{i} A_{j} X=\sum_{i=k+1}^{n} \angle M_{i} A_{j} X$.
Here $\angle M_{i} A_{j} X$ is the angle between $\vec{A}_{j} M_{i}$ and the positive direction of the x-axis (the size of the angle is between 0 and $\pi$).
Try to prove: The set of points $\left\{M_{1}, \cdots, M_{n}\right\}$ is symmetric with respect to the x-axis.

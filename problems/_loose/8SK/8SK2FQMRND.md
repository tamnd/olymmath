If the distinct numbers $a, b$, and $c$ satisfy the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a^{3}+p a+q=0 \\
& b^{3}+p b+q=0
\end{aligned}
$$

and

$$
c^{3}+p c+q=0
$$

then

$$
a+b+c=0
$$

Let $A_{0}$ be the midpoint of side $BC$ of triangle $ABC$, and let $A'$ be the point of tangency of the incircle with this side. Construct the circle $\Omega$ with center at $A_{0}$ and passing through $A'$. Construct similar circles on the other sides. Prove that if $\Omega$ touches the circumcircle on the arc $BC$ not containing $A$, then another one of the constructed circles touches the circumcircle.

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
\sin \frac{\pi x}{2022}-\sin \frac{\pi y}{2022} & =1 \\
x-y & =2022
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

if $|x| \leqslant 2022$ and $|y| \leqslant 2022$.

Let $P$ be any point on the circumcircle of the square $A B C D$ with side length $a$, then
(1) $P A^{2}+P B^{2}+P C^{2}+P D^{2}=4 a^{2}=2 d^{2}(d$ is the diameter of the circle $)$;
(2) Let $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$ be the midpoints of $A B, B C, C D, D A$ respectively, then
$$
P A_{1}^{2}+P B_{1}^{2}+P C_{1}^{2}+P D_{1}^{2}=3 a^{2}=\frac{3}{2} d^{2}
$$

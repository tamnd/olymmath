A circle of radius $r$ touches the sides of a polygon at points $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$, and the length of the side containing point $A_{i}$ is $a_{i}$. Point $X$ is at a distance $d$ from the center of the circle. Prove that $a_{1} X A_{1}^{2}+\ldots+a_{n} X A_{n}^{2}=P\left(r^{2}+d^{2}\right)$, where $P-$ is the perimeter of the polygon.

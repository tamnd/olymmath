Five points $O,A,B,C,D$ are taken in order on a straight line with distances $OA=a$ , $OB=b$ , $OC=c$ , and $OD=d$ . $P$ is a point on the line between $B$ and $C$ and such that $AP:PD=BP:PC$ . Then $OP$ equals: $\text{(A)}\ \dfrac{b^2-bc}{a-b+c-d} \text{(B)}\ \dfrac{ac-b}{a-b+c-d} \text{(C)}\ -\dfrac{bd+c}{a-b+c-d} \text{(D)}\ \dfrac{bc+ad}{a+b+c+d} \text{(E)}\ \dfrac{ac-bd}{a+b+c+d} $

Prove that in any triangle $ABC$ for the side lengths $a=BC, b=AC, c=AB$, the angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ of the interior angles $\angle CAB ; \angle ABC ; \angle BCA$ and for the inradius $\rho$ and the area $F$, the inequality holds:

$$
\frac{1}{a} \cos ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{b} \cos ^{2} \frac{\beta}{2}+\frac{1}{c} \cos ^{2} \frac{\gamma}{2} \geq \frac{27 \rho}{8 F}
$$

Identify all triangles for which the equality sign holds in (1).

To prove that from any finite set $H$ of lattice points in the plane, a subset $K$ can be selected such that the following properties hold:

(a) any axis-parallel (i.e., vertical or horizontal) line in the plane intersects $K$ in at most 2 points,

(b) any point in $H \backslash K$ lies on an axis-parallel segment with endpoints in $K$.

Let $a$ , $b$ and $c$ be three complex numbers such that $a+b+c=0$ and $|a|=|b|=|c|=1$ . Prove that:

$$3\le |z-a|+|z-b|+|z-c|\le 4,$$
for any $z\in\mathbb{C}$ , $|z|\le 1 .$

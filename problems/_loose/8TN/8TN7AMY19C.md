Let $r$ be the inradius of the tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$, and let $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}, r_{4}$ be the inradii of the triangular faces opposite the vertices $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, A_{4}$, respectively. Prove: $\frac{1}{r_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{r_{2}^{2}}+\frac{1}{r_{3}^{2}}+\frac{1}{r_{4}^{2}} \leqslant \frac{2}{r^{2}}$.

Place numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{25}$ on the vertices of a regular 25-gon in sequence, where $a_{1}=a_{2}=\cdots=a_{13}=1, a_{14}=a_{15}=\cdots=a_{25}=-1$. Add the numbers on each pair of adjacent vertices, and let $b_{1}=a_{1}+a_{2}, b_{2}=a_{2}+a_{3}, \cdots, b_{25}=$ $a_{25}+a_{1}$, then replace the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{25}$ with $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{25}$, and perform the same operation on this set of numbers, repeating this process 100 times. Prove that in the final set of 25 numbers, at least one is greater than $10^{20}$.

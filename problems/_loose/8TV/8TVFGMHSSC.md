In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, it is known that the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{27}+\frac{y^{2}}{18}=1$ has its right focus at $F$. A line $l$ passing through point $F$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A$ and $B$. Is there a fixed point $P$ on the $x$-axis such that when the line $l$ rotates around point $F$, $\overrightarrow{P A} \cdot \overrightarrow{P B}$ is always a constant?

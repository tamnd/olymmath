Let $A B$ be a diameter of a circle $\gamma . A$ point $C$ different from $A$ and $B$ is on the circle $\gamma$. Let $D$ be the projection of the point $C$ onto the line $A B$. Consider three other circles $\gamma_{1}, 72$, and $\gamma_{3}$ with the common tangent $A B: \gamma_{1}$ inscribed in the triangle $A B C$, and $\gamma_{2}$ and $\gamma_{3}$ tangent to both (the segment) $C D$ and $\gamma$. Prove that $\gamma_{1}, \gamma_{2}$, and $\gamma_{3}$ have two common tangents.

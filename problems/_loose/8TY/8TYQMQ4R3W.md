The following three statements are given about the natural number $n$:

- if $n$ is divisible by 3, then it is even;
- $n$ is divisible by 5 and it is odd;
- $n$ is not divisible by 3 or $n$ is not divisible by 5.

Determine which remainders upon division by 30 can be had by those natural numbers $n$ for which exactly two of the three given statements are true, and one is false.

Suppose $n (\geqslant 2)$ players each play a match against every other player, with no ties. The $i$-th player wins $w_{i}$ games and loses $l_{i}$ games. Prove that:
$$
w_{1}^{2}+w_{2}^{2}+\cdots+w_{n}^{2}=l_{1}^{2}+l_{2}^{2}+\cdots+l_{n}^{2} .
$$

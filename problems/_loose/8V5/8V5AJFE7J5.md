There are three piles of stones with the numbers being $19, 8, 9$. Now, the following operation is performed: each time, 1 stone is taken from any two of the three piles, and then these 2 stones are added to the other pile. The question is: can the following be achieved after several such operations?
(1) The number of stones in the three piles are $2, 12, 22$;
(2) The number of stones in each of the three piles is 12.
If the requirements can be met, complete it with the minimum number of operations. If not, explain the reason.
(China Guangzhou, Chongqing, Luoyang, Fuzhou, Wuhan Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

For certain real constants $ p, q, r$ , we are given a system of equations $$ \begin{cases} a^2 + b + c = p
a + b^2 + c = q
a + b + c^2 = r \end{cases} $$ What is the maximum number of solutions of real triplets $(a, b, c)$ across all possible $p, q, r$ ? Give an example of the $p$ , $q$ , $r$ that achieves this maximum.

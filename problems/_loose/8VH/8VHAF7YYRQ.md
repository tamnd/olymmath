Let's determine the coefficient values of $A, B, C, D$ (non-zero real numbers) for the following two polynomials such that their product consists of only 3 terms. - Is there a value system where both polynomials are perfect squares?

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{6}+4 x^{5}+A x^{4}+B x^{3}+C x^{2}+D x+1 \\
& x^{6}-4 x^{5}+A x^{4}-B x^{3}+C x^{2}-D x+1
\end{aligned}
$$

Let $z, w$ be non-zero complex numbers. Prove:
$$
|z-w| \geqslant \frac{1}{2}(|z|+|w|)\left|\frac{z}{|z|}-\frac{w}{|w|}\right|,
$$

and identify the condition under which equality holds.

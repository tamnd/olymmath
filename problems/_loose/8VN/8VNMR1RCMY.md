Let $A B C$ be a given triangle. Let $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ be the feet of the altitudes drawn from vertices $A$ and $B$ respectively. Let points $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ be symmetric to points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ respectively, with respect to the segment $A B$. Prove that the quadrilaterals $A B A_{1} B_{1}$, $A B_{2} A_{2} B$, and $A_{1} B_{1} A_{2} B_{2}$ are cyclic.

In the book "Combinatorial Foundations" edited by Mei Xiangming ${ }^{[1]}$, there is a combinatorial counting problem: A TV station broadcasts $r$ commercial advertisements over $n$ days. The questions are:
(1) If at least $p$ advertisements are broadcast each day $(n p \leqslant r)$, how many broadcasting methods are there?
(2) If at most $q$ advertisements are broadcast each day $(n q \geqslant r)$, how many broadcasting methods are there?
(3) If the number of advertisements broadcast each day is between $[p, q]$, how many broadcasting methods are there?

(1994 Romanian Mathematical Olympiad) $a, b, c, A, B, C$ are 6 positive real numbers, such that the equations $a x^{2}-b x+c=0$ and $A x^{2}-B x+C=0$ have real roots. Prove that for any real number $u$ between the two real roots of the equation $a x^{2}-b x+c=0$ and any real number $v$ between the two real roots of the equation $A x^{2}-B x+C=0$, the following inequality holds:
$$
(a u+A v)\left(\frac{c}{u}+\frac{C}{v}\right) \leqslant\left(\frac{b+B}{2}\right)^{2} .
$$

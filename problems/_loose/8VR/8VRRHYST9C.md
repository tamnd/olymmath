This game is played on a horizontal line consisting of 14 empty spaces. Taking turns, the two players place a letter $A$ or $B$ on one of the spaces (both players have the right to use both letters). A player wins if they manage to write $A B A$, and if neither of the two players achieves this, the game is a draw.

You will start this game, and your goal is to force a draw.

There is a labyrinth consisting of $n$ circles, each touching the line $A B$ at point $M$. All circles are located on one side of the line, and their lengths form a geometric progression with a common ratio of 2. Two people start walking through this labyrinth at different times. Their speeds are the same, but their directions of movement are different. Each of them goes through all the circles in order, and after passing the largest one, they go back to the smaller one. Prove that they will meet.

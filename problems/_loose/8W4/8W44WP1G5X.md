For finite sets $A, B, C$ and $D$, it holds that $D \subseteq A \cup B$, $D \subseteq C$, and

$$
|A \triangle B|+|B \backslash C|+|C \backslash D|+|B \cap D|=|A| .
$$

a) Prove that $B \cup C \subseteq A$.

b) Does one of the inclusions $B \subseteq C$ or $C \subseteq B$ necessarily hold?

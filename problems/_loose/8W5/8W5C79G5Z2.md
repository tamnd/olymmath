In the 7th grade, there are 100 students, some of whom are friends with each other. On September 1, they organized several clubs, each founded by three students (each club had its own). Every day, students who were friends with at least three members of a club joined that club. By February 19, all students in the grade were members of the club "Cheetahs." Could it have been possible that on the same day, exactly 50 students were members of the club "Turtles"?

A circle of radius $r$ is inscribed in a triangle. Three circles are constructed, each touching two sides of the triangle and the inscribed circle. Prove that the radii $R_{1}, R_{2}, R_{3}$ of these three circles satisfy the relation:

$$
\sqrt{R_{1} R_{2}}+\sqrt{R_{2} R_{3}}+\sqrt{R_{3} R_{1}}=r
$$

Given that $A D$ is the altitude of acute $\triangle A B C$, $D E \perp A B$ at point $E$, and $F$ is a point on the extension of $E D$ such that $A F \perp C F$.
(1) Prove: $\frac{A F^{2}}{C F^{2}}=\frac{A E}{B E}$;
(2) If $A B=13, B C=14, A C=15$, find the area of quadrilateral $A C F D$.

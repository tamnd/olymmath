In the tetrahedron $ABCD$, there are no right angles among its dihedral angles. Let the projection of $A$ onto the line segment $BD$ be $A_1$, onto $DC$ be $A_2$, the projection of $B$ onto the lines $CD$, $AD$ be $B_1$, and $B_2$ respectively, and the projection of $C$ onto $AD$, $BD$ be $C_1$ and $C_2$ respectively. Prove that the lines $A_1A_2$, $B_1B_2$, $C_1C_2$ are parallel to a certain plane.

The base area of a truncated triangular pyramid is $A_{1}$, the top area is $A_{2}\left(\leq A_{1}\right)$, and the sum of the areas of the lateral faces is $P$. Prove that if the solid can be cut by a plane parallel to the base such that a sphere can be inscribed in each of the resulting parts, then

$$
P=\left(\sqrt{A_{1}}+\sqrt{A_{2}}\right)\left(\sqrt[4]{A_{1}}+\sqrt[4]{A_{2}}\right)^{2}
$$

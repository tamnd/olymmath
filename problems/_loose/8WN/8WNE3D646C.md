Prove that $e^{\pi i}=-1$ and $e^{2 \pi i}=1$.

Note. In the real domain, the function $f(x)=e^{x}$ takes different values for different $x$. In the complex domain, this property no longer holds. For example, $f(0)=1=f(2 \pi i)$.

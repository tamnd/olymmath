A sphere is inscribed in an $n$-sided pyramid. The lateral faces of the pyramid are rotated around the edges of the base and are laid in the plane of the base so that they lie on the same side of the corresponding edges together with the base. Prove that the vertices of these faces, different from the vertices of the base, lie on the same circle.

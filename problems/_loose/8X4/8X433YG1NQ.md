For each positive integer $n,$ let $\sigma(n)$ denote the sum of the positive integer divisors of $n.$ How many positive integers $n \leq 2021$ satisfy $$ \sigma(3n) \geq \sigma(n)+\sigma(2n)? $$ *Proposed by Kyle Lee*

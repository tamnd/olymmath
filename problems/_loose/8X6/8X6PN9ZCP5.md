Ali-Baba stands in the corner of an empty rectangular cave of size $m \times n$ cells, which are colored in a checkerboard pattern. From any cell, he can take a step to any of the four adjacent cells (up, down, right, or left). At the same time, he must either place one coin in this cell or take one coin from it, provided, of course, that it is not empty. Can it be that after Ali-Baba's walk through the cave, there is exactly one coin on each black cell, and no coins on the white cells?

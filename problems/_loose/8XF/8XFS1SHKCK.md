a) Prove that a bounded figure cannot have more than one center of symmetry.

b) Prove that no figure can have exactly two centers of symmetry.

c) Let $ M $ be a finite set of points on a plane. A point $ O $ is called an "almost center of symmetry" of the set $ M $ if one point can be removed from $ M $ such that $ O $ becomes the center of symmetry of the remaining set. How many "almost centers of symmetry" can $ M $ have?

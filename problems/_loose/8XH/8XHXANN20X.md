Lian Example 18 (2005 National Training Team Test Question) Find all positive integers $m, n$, such that the inequality
$\left[(m+n)_{\alpha}\right]+[(m+n) \beta] \geqslant[m \alpha]+[m \beta]+[n(\alpha+\beta)]$
holds for any real numbers $\alpha, \beta$. Here $[x]$ denotes the integer part of the real number $x$.

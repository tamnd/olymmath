IDY 4 * Prove Napoleon's Theorem: From $\triangle ABC$, construct equilateral triangles $BCA_{1}$, $CAB_{1}$, and $ABC_{1}$ outwardly, with their centers at $P_{1}$, $Q_{1}$, and $R_{1}$, respectively. Then $\triangle P_{1} Q_{1} R_{1}$ is an equilateral triangle (known as the outer Napoleon triangle). Similarly, construct equilateral triangles $BCA_{2}$, $CAB_{2}$, and $ABC_{2}$ inwardly on the sides of $\triangle ABC$, with their centers at $P_{2}$, $Q_{2}$, and $R_{2}$, respectively. Then $\triangle P_{2} Q_{2} R_{2}$ is also an equilateral triangle (known as the inner Napoleon triangle).

Furthermore, prove that $S_{\triangle P_{1} Q_{1} R_{1}} - S_{\triangle P_{2} Q_{2} R_{2}} = S_{\triangle ABC}$, and express $S_{\triangle P_{1} Q_{1} R_{1}} + S_{\triangle P_{2} Q_{2} R_{2}}$ in terms of the sides of $\triangle ABC$; finally, prove that the midpoints of $AP_{1}$, $BQ_{1}$, and $CR_{1}$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle, and the same conclusion holds for $AP_{2}$, $BQ_{2}$, and $CR_{2}$.

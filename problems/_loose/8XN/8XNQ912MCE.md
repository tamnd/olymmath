One hundred pirates played cards in pairs, betting gold sand. After that, each calculated their total win or loss. Every loser has enough gold sand to pay. With one action, a pirate is allowed to distribute the same amount of sand to all the others or a pirate can collect the same amount of sand from all the others. Prove that with several such actions, every winner can receive (in total) their win and every loser can pay (in total) their loss. (It is clear that the total amount of losses is equal to the total amount of wins.)

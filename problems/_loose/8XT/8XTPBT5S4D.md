A set of natural numbers $(a,b,c,d)$ is called sunny if the numbers $a, b, c, d$ are mutually prime and satisfy the equality $$ ab +cd+210bd = ac. $$ a) Name at least one sunny set of numbers $(a,b,c,d)$ .
b) Prove that there are infinitely many sunny sets of numbers.
c) Find all sunny sets of numbers $(a,b,c,d)$ for which the sum $a+b+c+d$ takes on its smallest value.
d) What is the smallest value, greater than $2020$ , that the sum $a +b+c+d$ can take on when the set $(a,b,c,d)$ is sunny?

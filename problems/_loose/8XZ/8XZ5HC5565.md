If the circle meets $ AB$ and $ AC$ at $ D$ and $ E$, at the outer of $\triangle ABC$ , find the ratio $ \frac {Area \left(ABC\right)}{Area \left(ADE\right)}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 2\left(5 + \sqrt {3} \right) \textbf{(B)}\ 7\sqrt {2} \textbf{(C)}\ 5\sqrt {3} \\ \textbf{(D)}\ 2\left(3 + \sqrt {5} \right) \textbf{(E)}\ 2\left(\sqrt {3} + \sqrt {5} \right)$

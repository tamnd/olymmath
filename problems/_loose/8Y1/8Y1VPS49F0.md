A tangent line $l$ is drawn through a moving point $M$ on the ellipse (where $M$ does not coincide with $A$ or $B$). Vertical lines are drawn from points $A$ and $B$ to the $x$-axis, intersecting the tangent line $l$ at points $C$ and $D$, respectively. The lines $CB$ and $AD$ intersect at point $Q$, and the symmetric point of $Q$ with respect to $M$ is $P$. Find the equation of the trajectory of point $P$.

The normal to the leg $CA$ at point $E$ and the median of triangle $ABC$ drawn from vertex $C$ intersect at point $G$. Let $H$ be a point on line $CA$ such that point $A$ is the midpoint of segment $CH$. Prove that lines $GF$ and $BH$ are parallel.

(Austria-Poland, 80). Prove that for any value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$, the following equality holds:

$$
\sum_{1 \leqslant i_{1}<i_{2}<\ldots<i_{k} \leqslant n} \frac{1}{i_{1} i_{2} \ldots i_{k}}=n
$$

where the summation is taken over all possible sets of numbers

$$
i_{1}<i_{2}<\ldots<i_{k}, k=1,2, \ldots, n
$$

from the set $\{1 ; 2 ; \ldots ; n\}$.

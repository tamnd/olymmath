Starting today, the big gambler Carlo Pietro decided to visit casinos daily. On the first day, he will bet on a game where the probability of winning is equal to $\frac{1}{2}$. On the second, third, and fourth days, he will bet on different games whose probabilities of winning are, respectively, equal to $\frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{5}$, and so on for the following days.

a) What is the probability that he will not have won by the third day?

b) What is the probability that he will not have won by the fifth day?

c) What is the probability that he will not have won by the $2013^{\underline{0}}$ day?

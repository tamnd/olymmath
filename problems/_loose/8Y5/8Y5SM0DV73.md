Find the smallest positive integer $n$, for which there exist $n$ different positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ satisfying the conditions:

a) the smallest common multiple of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ is 1995 ;

b) for each $i, j \in\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$ the numbers $a_{i}$ and $a_{j}$ have a common divisor $\neq 1$;

c) the product $a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{n}$ is a perfect square and is divisible by 243 .

Find all $n$-ples $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right)$, satisfying a), b) and $\mathrm{c}$.

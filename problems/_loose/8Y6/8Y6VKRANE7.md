In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, it is known that $\angle DAB = \angle BCD = 90^{\circ}$, and $\angle CDA : \angle ABC = 2 : 1$, $AD : CB = 1 : \sqrt{3}$. Then the degree measure of $\angle BDA$ is
(A) $30^{\circ}$.
(B) $45^{\circ}$.
(C) $60^{\circ}$.
(D) cannot be determined.
(1st "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1990)

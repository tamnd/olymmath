Point $P$ lies inside triangle $ABC$, and $\angle ABP = \angle ACP$. Points $C_1$ and $B_1$ are taken on lines $AB$ and $AC$ respectively, such that $BC_1 : CB_1 = CP : BP$. Prove that one of the diagonals of the parallelogram, two sides of which lie on lines $BP$ and $CP$, and the other two sides (or their extensions) pass through $B_1$ and $C_1$, is parallel to $BC$.

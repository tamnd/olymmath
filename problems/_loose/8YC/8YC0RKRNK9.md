Let the collinear points $A, O$ and $B$, with $O \in (AB)$. On the same side of the line $AB$, consider the points $C$ and $D$ such that the angles $\angle AOC$ and $\angle COD$ are adjacent. Let $[OM$ be the bisector of the angle $\angle AOC$ and $[ON$ be the bisector of the angle $\angle BOD$. It is known that $m(\angle MOD) = 115^\circ$ and $m(\angle NOC) = 110^\circ$.

a) Show that the angle $\angle COD$ is a right angle.

b) Find $m(\angle AOC)$ and $m(\angle DOB)$.
c) Inside the angle $\angle COD$, 12 distinct rays with origin at $O$ are constructed, such that the 13 angles formed, with disjoint interiors, have measures expressed by non-zero natural numbers. Prove that at least two of these angles are congruent.

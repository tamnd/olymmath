Anniversary of the Meeting (from 6th grade, 2 points). As is known, Robinson Crusoe's friend and companion was named Friday because they met on a Friday. This happened in January, but it is impossible to determine the exact year. Robinson is certain only that it could have happened in any year with equal probability, starting from 1668 and ending in 1671. Robinson and Friday revered the day of their meeting and celebrated it solemnly every year. What day of the week could the day of their eleventh anniversary have been? With what probability?

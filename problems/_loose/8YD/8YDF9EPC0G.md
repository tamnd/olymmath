If $x$ is a positive number, and $\lg x \geqslant \lg 2+\frac{1}{2} \lg x$, then
(A) $x$ has no maximum or minimum value.
(B) The maximum value of $x$ is 1.
(C) The minimum value of $x$ is 1.
(D) The maximum value of $x$ is 4.
(E) The minimum value of $x$ is 4.
(9th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1958)

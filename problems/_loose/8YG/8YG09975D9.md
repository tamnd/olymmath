Let $f(x)=x^{n}+a_{1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1} x+a_{n}$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients, $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be the $n$ roots of $f(x)$, and $p^{i} \mid a_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, where $p$ is an integer. Prove that for any $k \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, p^{k} \mid s_{k}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{k}$.

Let $A$ and $B$ be any two points, $h_{A}, g_{A}$ (resp. $h_{B}, g_{B}$) two lines passing through $A$ (resp. $B$). We denote $C$ (resp. $D$) the intersection of $h_{A}, g_{B}$ (resp. $h_{B}, g_{A}$), and $E$ (resp. $F$) the intersection of $g_{A}, g_{B}$ (resp. $h_{A}, h_{B}$). Let $P$ be any point on the line $h_{B}$ and $H$ a point on the line $g_{A}$. We denote $X$ (resp. $Y$) the intersection of $(C P)$ and $(E F)$ (resp. $(E P)$ and $(H F)$. Prove that $X, Y$ and $B$ are collinear if and only if $A, H, E, D$ are harmonic points.

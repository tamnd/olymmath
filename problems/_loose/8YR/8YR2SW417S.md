We have a cuboid, one of whose edges is five times longer than another of its edges. If we increase the height of the cuboid by $2 \mathrm{~cm}$, its volume would increase by $90 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$. If we further change the height of the thus increased cuboid to half, the volume of the new cuboid would be equal to three fifths of the volume of the original cuboid.

What could be the dimensions of the original cuboid? Determine all possibilities.

Given complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ satisfy $\left\{\left|z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}\right|,\left|-z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}\right|,\left|z_{1}-z_{2}+z_{3}\right|,\left|z_{1}+z_{2}-z_{3}\right|\right\}=$ $\{98,84,42,28\}$.
Prove: $\max \left\{\left|z_{1}^{2}\left(2-z_{2}^{2}\right)-z_{3}^{2}\right|,\left|z_{2}^{2}\left(2-z_{3}^{2}\right)-z_{1}^{2}\right|,\left|2 z_{3}^{2}\left(z_{1}^{2}+1\right)-z_{2}^{2}\right|\right\} \geqslant 2016$.

Given $a,b,c,d>0$ , prove that:
$$\sum_{cyc}\frac{c}{a+2b}+\sum_{cyc}\frac{a+2b}{c}\geq 8(\frac{(a+b+c+d)^2}{ab+ac+ad+bc+bd+cd}-1),$$
where $\sum_{cyc}f(a,b,c,d)=f(a,b,c,d)+f(d,a,b,c)+f(c,d,a,b)+f(b,c,d,a)$ .

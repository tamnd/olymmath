Nine small balls numbered $1, 2, \cdots, 9$ are placed in a bag. These balls are identical except for their numbers. Person A draws a ball from the bag, with the number being $a$, and then returns it. Person B then draws a ball from the bag, with the number being $b$. The probability that the inequality $a-2b+10>0$ holds is
A. $\frac{52}{81}$
B. $\frac{59}{81}$
C. $\frac{60}{81}$
D. $\frac{61}{81}$

Given that $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a sequence composed of non-negative integers, $a_{1}=0, a_{2}=3$, and $a_{n+1} a_{n}=\left(a_{n-1}+2\right)\left(a_{n-2}+2\right)(n \geqslant 3)$.

If $S_{n}$ is the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$. Prove that: $S_{n} \leqslant \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, and specify the condition under which equality holds.

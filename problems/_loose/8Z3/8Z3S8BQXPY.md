Equilateral $\triangle A B C$ is divided into 4 smaller triangles $\triangle A D E$, $\triangle B D F$, $\triangle D E F$ and $\triangle C E F$ by its 3 midlines. On each of their 9 sides, take the midpoints $K, L, M, N$, $O, P, Q, R, S$. Each of these 15 points is painted one of two colors, red or blue. Prove that there must be 3 points of the same color that form the 3 vertices of an equilateral triangle.

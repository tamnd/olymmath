Given a triangle *ABC*, its *A*-excircle is the point where the external bisectors of angles $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ meet. Let $I_A$, $I_B$ and $I_C$ be the *A*, *B*, *C*-excenters of scalene triangle *ABC*, respectively, and *X*, *Y* and *Z* be the midpoints of $I_B I_C$, $I_C I_A$ and $I_A I_B$, respectively. The incircle of triangle *ABC* touches sides *BC*, *CA* and *AB* at *D*, *E* and *F*, respectively. Prove that lines *DX*, *EY* and *FZ* meet at a single point on line *IO* that connects the incenter *I* and the circumcenter *O* of triangle *ABC*.

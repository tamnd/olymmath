Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $D, E, F$ the feet of the altitudes and $H$ the orthocenter.

1) Determine all the angles of triangles $AEF$ and $DEF$.
2) What do the points $A, B, C, H$ represent for the triangle $DEF$?
3) Let $P$ and $Q$ be the projections of $D$ onto $(AB)$ and $(AC)$. Show that $(PQ)$ is parallel to $(EF)$.

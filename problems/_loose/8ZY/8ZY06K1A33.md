Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle, in which the interior angle at vertex $A$ has a size of $45^{\circ}$. Denote $D$ as the foot of the altitude from vertex $C$. Consider further any internal point $P$ on the altitude $C D$. Prove the statement: The lines $A P$ and $B C$ are perpendicular to each other if and only if the segments $A P$ and $B C$ are congruent.

At a chess tournament, every two participants played against each other at most once. Moreover, for any two of them, $A$ and $B$, who did not play against each other during the tournament, exactly two other participants, $C$ and $D$, played against both $A$ and $B$ during the tournament. No four participants played exactly five games among themselves. Prove that each participant played an equal number of games at the tournament.

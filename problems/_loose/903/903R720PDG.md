In a chess club, a tournament for beginners and advanced players was organized. Each beginner played exactly two games against every other beginner; each advanced player played exactly two games against every other advanced player.

These games were scheduled so that on each of exactly 28 game days, exactly 3 games were played. More beginners than advanced players participated in the tournament.

Show that these conditions uniquely determine how many beginners and how many advanced players participated in the tournament! State these two numbers!

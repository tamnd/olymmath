Given a regular 12-sided polygon. By drawing non-intersecting diagonals, decompose it into triangles in every possible way such that each vertex is the vertex of an odd number of the resulting triangles. (Two decompositions are not considered different if they can be transformed into each other by a rotation around the center or a reflection across a line passing through the center.)

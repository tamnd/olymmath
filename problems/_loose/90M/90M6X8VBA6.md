We draw lines $l'$ and $l$ from $A'$ parallel with Internal and external bisector of the vertex $A$ . $l'$ Cut out $AB , BC$ at $B_1$ and $B_2$ . $l$ Cut out $AC , BC$ at $C_1$ and $C_2$ .
Prove that the circumcircles of $\triangle ABC$ $\triangle CC_1C_2$ and $\triangle BB_1B_2$ have a common point.

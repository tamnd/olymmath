Let $A B C D E F$ be a straight triangular prism with $A D\|B E\| C F$. The top face $D E F$ is a right-angled triangle with $E$ as the vertex of the right angle. Furthermore, let $S$ be the intersection of the angle bisectors of triangle $D E F$.

Prove that from these assumptions, it always follows that $\overline{S B}<\overline{S A}$!

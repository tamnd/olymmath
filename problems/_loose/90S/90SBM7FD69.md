On the sides of the unit-sided equilateral triangle $ABC$, we inscribe the squares $ABRS$, $BCPQ$, and $CAMN$, and then we place pyramids with equal side edges on these squares. We rotate these pyramids around the base edges $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ so that the four-edged apexes coincide. - What is the side edge of the pyramids if, in this new position, the points $M, N, P$, $Q, R, S$ form the vertices of a regular hexagon?

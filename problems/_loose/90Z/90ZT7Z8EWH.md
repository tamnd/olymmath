Let $p$ be a prime number, and let the number $a$ not be divisible by $p$. Prove that all remainders of the division by $p$ of the numbers $a$, $2a$, $3a, \ldots, (p-1)a$ are pairwise distinct, i.e., each number from 1 to $p-1$ appears among these remainders exactly once.

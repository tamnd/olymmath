Consider all parabolas of the form $ y=x^2+2px+q$ for $ p,q \in \mathbb{R}$ which intersect the coordinate axes in three distinct points. For such $ p,q$ , denote by $ C_{p,q}$ the circle through these three intersection points. Prove that all circles $ C_{p,q}$ have a point in common.

Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with $A B \neq A C$ and vertex $H$. Let the midpoint of the side $B C$ be $M$. Let the points $D$ on $A B$ and $E$ on $A C$ be such that $A E=A D$ and $D, H, E$ lie on a straight line. Show that $H M$ and the common chord of the circumcircles of the two triangles $A B C$ and $A D E$ are perpendicular to each other.

Given points $P_1, P_2,\cdots,P_7$ on a straight line, in the order stated (not necessarily evenly spaced). Let $P$ be an arbitrarily selected point on the line and let $s$ be the sum of the undirected lengths
$$PP_1, PP_2, \cdots , PP_7.$$ Then $s$ is smallest if and only if the point $P$ is: $\textbf{(A)}\ \text{midway between }P_1\text{ and }P_7 \textbf{(B)}\ \text{midway between }P_2\text{ and }P_6 \textbf{(C)}\ \text{midway between }P_3\text{ and }P_5 $ $
\textbf{(D)}\ \text{at }P_4 \textbf{(E)}\ \text{at }P_1$

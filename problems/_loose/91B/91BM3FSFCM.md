Given the segment $A B$. We define a set $H$ as follows:
a) $A$ and $B$ belong to $H$.

b) If $X$ and $Y$ are points in the set, then the point $Z$ on the segment $X Y$ also belongs to $H$ if it satisfies $Y Z=3 \cdot X Z$.

Prove that every segment lying on $A B$ contains at least one point of $H$.

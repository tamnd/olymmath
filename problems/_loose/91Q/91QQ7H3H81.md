At a banquet of a university, there are 2017 mathematicians, each with two different main courses on their order menu, and no two people have the same pair of main courses on their menu. The price of each main course is equal to the number of mathematicians who ordered that main course. The university pays for the cheaper of the two main courses for each mathematician (if the prices are the same, either can be chosen). For all possible sets of order menus, find the maximum total payment of the university.

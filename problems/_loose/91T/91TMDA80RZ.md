Let $p, q \in \mathbf{Z}^{+}$, and $q \leqslant p^{2}$. Prove that for $n \in \mathbf{Z}^{+}$, there exists $N \in \mathbf{Z}^{+}$, such that
$$
\left(p-\sqrt{p^{2}-q}\right)^{n}=N-\sqrt{N^{2}-q^{n}},
$$

and
$$
\left(p+\sqrt{p^{2}-q}\right)^{n}=N+\sqrt{N^{2}-q^{n}}.
$$

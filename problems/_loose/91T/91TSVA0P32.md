Let $A, B$ and $C$ be non-zero digits, so that $B C$ is a two-digit positive integer and $A B C$ is a three-digit positive integer made up of the digits $A, B$ and $C$. Suppose that

$$
\begin{array}{r}
B C \\
A B C \\
+ A B C \\
\hline 876
\end{array}
$$

What is the value of $A+B+C$ ?

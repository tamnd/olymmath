**a)** prove that the function $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n^s}$ that is defined on the area $Re(s)>1$, is an analytic function.

**b)** prove that the function $\zeta(s)-\frac{1}{s-1}$ can be spanned to an analytic function over $\mathbb C$.

**c)** using the span of part **b** show that $\zeta(1-n)=-\frac{B_n}{n}$ that $B_n$ is the $n$th bernoli number that is defined by generating function $\frac{t}{e^t-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_n\frac{t^n}{n!}$.

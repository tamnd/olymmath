On the sides $AB, BC$, and $CA$ of triangle $ABC$, points $P, Q$, and $R$ are given such that

$$
AP = \lambda AB, BQ = \lambda BC, CR = \lambda CA, \left(\frac{1}{2} \leq \lambda \leq 1\right)
$$

Prove that the perimeter of triangle $PQR$ is not greater than the perimeter of triangle $ABC$ multiplied by the number $\lambda$.

Let $p\geq 3$ be an odd positive integer. Show that $p$ is prime if and only if however we choose $(p+1)/2$ pairwise distinct positive integers, we can find two of them, $a$ and $b$ , such that $(a+b)/\gcd(a,b)\geq p.$

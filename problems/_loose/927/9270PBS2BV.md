Prove that for any distinct positive numbers $a, b$ the following holds

$$
\frac{a+b}{2}<\frac{2\left(a^{2}+a b+b^{2}\right)}{3(a+b)}<\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}}{2}} .
$$

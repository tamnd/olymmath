In a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, the circumcenter is $O$. The lines $AB$ and $AC$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle OBC$ at points $B_{1}$ (different from point $B$) and $C_{1}$ (different from point $C$), respectively. The lines $BA$ and $BC$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle OAC$ at points $A_{2}$ (different from point $A$) and $C_{2}$ (different from point $C$), respectively. The lines $CA$ and $CB$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle OAB$ at points $A_{3}$ (different from point $A$) and $B_{3}$ (different from point $B$), respectively. Prove that $A_{2}A_{3}$, $B_{1}B_{3}$, and $C_{1}C_{2}$ are concurrent.
(2009, Brazilian Mathematical Olympiad)

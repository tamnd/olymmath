A country has $n(n \geqslant 2)$ cities, and there is a direct two-way flight route between every two cities. The aviation authority authorizes some airlines to use these routes, and the following conditions are met:
(1) Each route is used by exactly one airline;
(2) For each airline, passengers can use the routes of this airline to travel from one city to all other cities.

Find the maximum number of airlines that the aviation authority can authorize.

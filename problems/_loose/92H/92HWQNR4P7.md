Let the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ be $\odot O, \odot O_{1}$ be internally tangent to $\odot O$, and tangent to sides $AB$ and $AC$, $\odot O_{2}$ be externally tangent to $\odot O$, and tangent to the extensions of sides $AB$ and $AC$, and let the length of the common internal tangent of $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ be $l$. Prove:
$$
l=\frac{4 b c|b-c|}{(a+b+c)(b+c-a)},
$$

where, $BC=a, CA=b, AB=c$.

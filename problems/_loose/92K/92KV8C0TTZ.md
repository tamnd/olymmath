Prove that if $n$ is a natural number, then the following inequality holds. Prove this by using the fact that for any number $A$, there can be a certain number of different natural numbers such that the sum of their reciprocals is greater than $A$. Provide such natural numbers for example for $A=4.5$.

$$
\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\frac{1}{n+3}+\ldots+\frac{1}{3 n+1}>1
$$

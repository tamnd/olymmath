Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral with $AB$ and $CD$ not parallel. Let $X$ be a point inside $ABCD$ such that $\widehat{ADX}=\widehat{BCX}<90^{\circ}$ and $\widehat{DAX}=\widehat{CBX}<90^{\circ}$. Let $Y$ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of $[AB]$ and $[CD]$. Show that $\widehat{AYB}=2 \widehat{ADX}$.

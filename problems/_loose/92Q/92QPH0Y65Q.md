Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $AB<AC$, circumcenter $O$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the smaller arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Point $D$ lies on the extension of side $AB$ towards $B$ such that $BD=BM$. Point $E$ lies on side $AC$ (excluding the endpoints) such that $CE=CM$. Let $X(\neq A)$ be the intersection of circumcircles of triangles $ABE$ and $ACD$. Prove that the perpendicular bisector of segment $DE$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $AOX$.

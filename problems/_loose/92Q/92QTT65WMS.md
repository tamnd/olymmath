A circle has 1956 digits. It is known that starting from a certain digit and recording these numbers in a clockwise direction, a 1956-digit number is obtained that is divisible by 27. Prove that if starting from any digit and recording these digits in a clockwise direction, the resulting 1956-digit number is also divisible by 27.
(Kyiv Mathematical Olympiad, 1956)

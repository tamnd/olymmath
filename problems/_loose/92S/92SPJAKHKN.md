In space, there are 2019 points, where no four points are coplanar. Divide them into 30 groups with different numbers of points. For any three different groups, take one point from each as the vertex to form a triangle. How should the points be divided to maximize the number of such triangles?
(Adapted from the 1989 China Mathematical Olympiad)

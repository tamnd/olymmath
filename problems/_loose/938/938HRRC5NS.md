Let $\alpha$ be the largest root of the equation $x^{3}-3 x^{2}+1=0$. Prove that

$$
\left[\alpha^{1993}\right] \equiv 4(\bmod 17),
$$

that is, the integer part of $\alpha^{1993}$, when divided by 17, leaves a remainder of 4.

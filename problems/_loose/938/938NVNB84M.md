Let the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ be such that $A C+C D \geqslant A B+B D$. Prove that in this case, vertex $A$ of the quadrilateral is closer to vertex $B$ than to vertex $C$.

Does the statement of this problem remain valid if the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is not convex?

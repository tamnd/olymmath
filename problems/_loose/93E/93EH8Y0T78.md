Given the parabola $C: y^{2}=2 p x(p>$ $0)$ with focus $F$, denote $\chi M N x$ as the angle between vector $\overrightarrow{N M}$ and the positive direction of the $x$-axis. If for two points $P, Q$ on $C$ in different quadrants (point $P$ is above point $Q$), there exists a point $R$ such that
$$
2 \not P R x=\chi P F x, 2\lfloor Q R x=\lfloor Q F x \text {, }
$$

Prove: $\triangle P F R \backsim \triangle R F Q$.

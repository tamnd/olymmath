22-2 (C20, Federal Republic of Germany) Let $1 \leqslant r \leqslant n$, consider all $r$-element subsets of the set $\{1,2$, $\cdots, n\}$ and the smallest element in each such subset. Let $F(n, r)$ denote the arithmetic mean of all such smallest numbers. Prove that:
$$
F(n, r)=\frac{n+1}{r+1} .
$$

Let $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ be a convex quadrilateral that has both a circumcircle and an incircle, and let the incircle touch the sides $A_{1} A_{2} 、 A_{2} A_{3} 、 A_{3} A_{4}$ and $A_{4} A_{1}$ at points $B_{1} 、 B_{2} 、 B_{3} 、 B_{4}$. Prove:
$$
\left(\frac{A_{1} A_{2}}{B_{1} B_{2}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{A_{2} A_{3}}{B_{2} B_{3}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{A_{3} A_{4}}{B_{3} B_{4}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{A_{4} A_{1}}{B_{4} B_{1}}\right)^{2} \geqslant 8 .
$$

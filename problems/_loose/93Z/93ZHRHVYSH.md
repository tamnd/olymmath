Let the polynomial $f(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{1} x + p \in \mathbf{Z}[x]$, where $a_{n} \neq 0$, $p$ is a prime number, and $\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|a_{j}\right| < p$. Prove: $f(x)$ is irreducible in $\mathbf{Z}[x]$.

A set of 20 volumes of a literary collection is placed on a bookshelf. The librarian wants to arrange them from left to right in the order of volumes 1 to 20. Each time, he swaps the volume that is not in the correct position with the volume that occupies its position. Prove that the number of such swaps the librarian needs to make to sort the volumes is independent of the order in which he performs these actions.

In a flat terrain, exactly those points are accessible that lie on a rectangle $A B C D$ including its interior. Within this rectangular area, there is a circular arc from $A$ to $B$, whose corresponding center is not accessible. On this circular arc lies the point $P$ (with $P \neq A$ and $P \neq B$).

Construct the tangent at $P$ to the circular arc without leaving the rectangle $A B C D$ during the construction!

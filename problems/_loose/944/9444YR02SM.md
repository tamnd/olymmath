Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $D$ and $E$ be the feet of the angle bisectors from $B$ and $C$. Let $P$ be a point on the line $(DE)$ such that $P$ lies within the cone formed by the rays $[BA)$ and $[BC)$ and on the other side of $B$ from the line $(AC)$. Show that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the sides $(AC)$ and $(BC)$ equals the distance from $P$ to $(AB)$.

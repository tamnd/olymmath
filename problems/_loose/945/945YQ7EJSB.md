Consider the tetrahedron $A B C D$ (a three-sided pyramid). Let the reflection of an arbitrary point $P$ in the space across the planes of the faces $A B C, A B D, A C D, B C D$ be $P_{D}, P_{C}, P_{B}, P_{A}$, and the center of the sphere passing through these four reflections be $Q$. Let the reflections of $Q$ similarly across the planes of the faces be $Q_{D}, Q_{C}, Q_{B}, Q_{A}$. Prove that the center of the sphere passing through these points is $P$, and its radius is equal to the radius of the previously mentioned sphere.

If the value of one of the roots of the equation

$$
x^{2}+p x+q=0
$$

is the reciprocal of the value of one of the roots of the equation

$$
x^{2}+m x+n=0
$$

then there exists the dependence

$$
(p n-m)(q m-p)=(q n-1)^{2}
$$

Prove this.

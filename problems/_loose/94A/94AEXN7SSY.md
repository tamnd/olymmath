In the interior angles of a convex octagon, the number of obtuse angles is $m$, and the number of acute angles is $n$, then
(A) $mn$.
(C) $m=n$.
(D) Both $mn$ and $m=n$ are possible.
("Jianghan Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

A computer outputs a number 1 or 2 with equal probability every second. Let the probability that the sum of the first $n$ numbers output is divisible by 3 be denoted as $P_{n}$. Prove: (1) $P_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2}\left(1-P_{n}\right)$; (2) $P_{2012}>\frac{1}{3}$.

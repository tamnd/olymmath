Show that the function

$$
\psi\left(t, x_{1}, x_{2}\right)=\operatorname{arctg} \frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}-t
$$

is an integral of the system of equations

$$
\frac{d x_{1}}{d t}=\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}, \frac{d x_{2}}{d t}=-\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{x_{1}}
$$[^5]

Ivan the Tsarevich went to look for Vasilisa the Beautiful, who had been kidnapped by Koschei. On his way, he met the Forest Spirit.

- I know, - he said, - the way to Koschei's Kingdom. I have been there before. I walked for four days and four nights. In the first day and night, I covered a third of the way - a straight path to the north. Then I turned west, and for a day and night, I struggled through the forest, covering half as much. On the third day and night, I walked through the forest to the south and came out onto a straight road leading to the east. I walked 100 versts along it in a day and night and arrived in Koschei's Kingdom. You are as quick a walker as I am. Go, Ivan the Tsarevich, and you might be a guest of Koschei by the fifth day.
- No, - replied Ivan the Tsarevich, - if everything is as you say, then I will see my Vasilisa the Beautiful already tomorrow.

Is he right? How many versts did the Forest Spirit walk, and how many does Ivan the Tsarevich think he will walk?

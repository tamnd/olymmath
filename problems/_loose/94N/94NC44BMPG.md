Let the integer $N>1, 1=d_{1}<d_{2}<\cdots<d_{s}=N$ be all the positive divisors of $N$. It is known that
$$
\left(d_{1}, d_{2}\right)+\left(d_{2}, d_{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(d_{s-1}, d_{s}\right)=N-2 \text {. }
$$

Find all possible values of $N$.

$$
a+2 b=1, c+2 d=1 \text {. }
$$

Then the minimum value of $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b c d}$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{1}{16}$
(B) 8
(C) $\frac{1}{5}$
(D) 25

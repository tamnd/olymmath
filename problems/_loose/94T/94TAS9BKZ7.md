Let real numbers $x, y$ satisfy $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$. Prove: For any positive numbers $a, b$, we have
$$
\sqrt{a^{2} x^{2}+b^{2} y^{2}}+\sqrt{a^{2} y^{2}+b^{2} x^{2}} \geqslant a+b .
$$

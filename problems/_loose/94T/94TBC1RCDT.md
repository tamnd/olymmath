At the Valbonne camp, there are $n$ students. Some pairs of students know each other at the beginning of the camp. A team is a set of three students who all know each other at the beginning of the camp. We assume that there are no teams in the Valbonne camp. Furthermore, if we divide the students into two parts, one of the two parts necessarily contains a pair of students who know each other at the beginning of the camp.

Show that there exists a student who knows at most $\frac{2 n}{5}$ other students at the beginning of the camp.

Find all positive prime numbers $p$ and $q$ such that $p^{2}-q^{3}=1$.

Remark. $p$ is prime if it has only two divisors: 1 and itself. The numbers $2,3,5,7,11,13$ are prime, but $1,4,6,8,9$ are not.

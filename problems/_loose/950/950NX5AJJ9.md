$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=e^{t}(\cos t+\sin t) \\
y=e^{t}(\cos t-\sin t)
\end{array}\right. \\
& \frac{\pi}{6} \leq t \leq \frac{\pi}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

Determine all prime numbers $p$, such that the number

$$
\left(\begin{array}{l}
p \\
1
\end{array}\right)^{2}+\left(\begin{array}{l}
p \\
2
\end{array}\right)^{2}+\ldots+\left(\begin{array}{c}
p \\
p-1
\end{array}\right)^{2}
$$

is divisible by $p^{3}$.

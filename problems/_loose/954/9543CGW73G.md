We will call a triple of natural numbers $(a, b, c)$ square if they form an arithmetic progression (in that order), the number $b$ is coprime with each of the numbers $a$ and $c$, and the number $a b c$ is a perfect square. Prove that for any square triple, there exists another square triple that shares at least one common number with it.

(V. Senderov)

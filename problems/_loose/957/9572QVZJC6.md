Given a regular pentagon; $M$ is an arbitrary point inside it (or on its boundary). Number the distances from point $M$ to the sides of the pentagon (or their extensions) in ascending order: $r_{1} \leqslant r_{2} \leqslant r_{3} \leqslant r_{4} \leqslant r_{5}$. Find all positions of point $M$ for which the value of $r_{3}$ is the smallest, and all positions of point $M$ for which the value of $r_{3}$ is the largest.

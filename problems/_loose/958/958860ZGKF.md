If $a, b, c, \sin A, \sin B, \sin C > 0$ (here $a, b, c$, $A, B, C$ are not necessarily elements of $\triangle ABC$), then
(1) $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}(=k) \\ A+B+C=\pi\end{array}\right.$
$\Leftrightarrow(2)\left\{\begin{array}{l}a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-2 b c \cos A \\ b^{2}=c^{2}+a^{2}-2 c a \cos B \\ c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2 a b \cos C\end{array}\right.$
$\Leftrightarrow(3)\left\{\begin{array}{l}a=b \cos C+c \cos B \\ b=c \cos A+a \cos C . \\ c=a \cos B+b \cos A\end{array}\right.$

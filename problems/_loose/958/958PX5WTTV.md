$n$ students participate in a math exam, the test consists of $m$ questions. Consider the following statistical results: Let $\alpha(0<\alpha<1)$ be a real number, at least $\alpha m$ questions are difficult (a question is difficult if at least $\alpha n$ students fail to solve it), and at least $\alpha n$ students pass (a student passes if they solve at least $\alpha m$ questions). Try to determine whether the above scenario is possible for $\alpha=\frac{2}{3}, \frac{7}{10}$, and explain your reasoning.

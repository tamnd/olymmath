(2007 Vietnam Mathematical Olympiad) Let $b$ be a positive real number. Find all functions $f: \mathrm{R} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}$ such that $f(x+y)=f(x) \cdot 3^{b^{\prime}+f(y)-1}+b^{x}\left(3^{b^{3}+f(y)-1}\right)-$ $b^{x+y}$ for all real numbers $x, y$.

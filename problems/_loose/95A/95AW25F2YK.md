A paper has a circle $O$ with radius $R$ and a fixed point $A$ inside the circle, and $O A=a$. Fold the paper so that a point $A^{\prime}$ on the circumference coincides exactly with $A$, thus each fold leaves a straight line crease. When $A^{\prime}$ covers all points on the circumference, find the set of points on all the crease lines.

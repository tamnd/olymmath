In the field of real numbers, solve the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}-1=p(y+z), \\
& y^{2}-1=p(z+x), \\
& z^{2}-1=p(x+y)
\end{aligned}
$$

with unknowns $x, y, z$ and parameter $p$. Discuss the number of solutions.

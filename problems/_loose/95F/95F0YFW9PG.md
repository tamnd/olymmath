(Qing Gong Theorem) Let $P, Q$ be any two points on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ other than $A, B, C$. The reflections of point $P$ over $B C, C A$, and $A B$ are $U, V, W$ respectively, and $Q U, Q V, Q W$ intersect $B C, C A, A B$ at $D, E, F$ respectively. Prove that $D, E, F$ are collinear.

(Permutation Problem). In how many ways can 13 different math books be arranged on a bookshelf?

1) in any order;
2) so that 4 books on arithmetic are together (in any order);
3) so that 4 books on arithmetic are together (in any order) and 6 books on algebra are together (in any order);
4) so that 4 books on arithmetic are together (in any order), 6 books on algebra are together (in any order), and 3 books on geometry are together (in any order)?

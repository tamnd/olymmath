Given the quadratic polynomial $(k+1) x^{2}-2 k x+k-1$, where $k$ is a real parameter.

a) Determine the geometric locus of the vertices of all parabolas defined by the equation

$$
y=(k+1) x^{2}-2 k x+k-1
$$

b) Do all these parabolas have a common point?

c) Prove that only one solution of the equation

$$
(k+1) x^{2}-2 k x+k-1=0
$$

is variable and illustrate it graphically.

d) Determine for which natural number $k$ the variable solution of equation (1) is a periodic decimal number with one digit in the period.

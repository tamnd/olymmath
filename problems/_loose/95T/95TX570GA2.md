Prove that in any triangle, the following equality holds, where $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of the triangle, $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the magnitudes of the angles opposite to them; $f_{\alpha}, f_{\beta}, f_{\gamma}$ are the lengths of the corresponding internal angle bisectors.

$$
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{\cos \frac{\alpha}{2}}{f_{\alpha}}+\frac{\cos \frac{\beta}{2}}{f_{\beta}}+\frac{\cos \frac{\gamma}{2}}{f_{\gamma}} .
$$

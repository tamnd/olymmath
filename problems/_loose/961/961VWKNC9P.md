Let $ k $ be the circumcircle of a given acute triangle $ ABC $. Consider an internal point $ P $ of the arc $ BC $ of circle $ k $ that does not contain point $ A $. Let $ Q $ be the intersection of segments $ AP $ and $ BC $, and let $ O_{1} $ and $ O_{2} $ be the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $ BPQ $ and $ CPQ $, respectively. Prove that if the line $ O_{1} O_{2} $ passes through one of the vertices of triangle $ ABC $, then one of the points $ O_{1}, O_{2} $ lies on circle $ k $.

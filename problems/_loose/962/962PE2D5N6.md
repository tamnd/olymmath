Let $a, b, c$ be real numbers and let $a \neq 0$. Prove that at least one of the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a \sin x + b \cos x + c = 0 \\
& a \tan y + b \cot y + 2c = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

has real solutions.

In this and the next two problems, we encounter three forest inhabitants $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}, \mathrm{C}$, each of whom is either a knight or a liar. Only two of them, A and B, make statements. In their statements, the word "us" refers to all three of them (A, B, and C), not just A and B.

Suppose A and B declared the following:

A: At least one of us is a knight.

B: At least one of us is a liar.

It is known that at least one of them is a werewolf and none of them is both a knight and a werewolf. Who is the werewolf?

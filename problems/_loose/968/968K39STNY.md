In a plane, there are 18 points, no three of which are collinear. Each pair of points is connected by a line segment, and these line segments are colored either red or blue, with each segment being exactly one color. From a certain point $A$, the number of red line segments is odd, and from the other 17 points, the number of red line segments is distinct. Find the number of triangles with vertices among the given points, where all sides are red, and the number of triangles with two red sides and one blue side.
(1994 Hunan Provincial Training Team Problem)

From the river port $A$, a motorboat and a raft (the raft had no propulsion) started simultaneously downstream. The speed of the motorboat in still water is $u$, and the speed of the river is $v(u>v>0)$. After $t_{1}$ hours, the motorboat arrived at $B$ and immediately started to return to $A$. After $t_{2}$ hours from leaving $B$, it met the raft at $C$ and without stopping, arrived at $A$. The distance from $A$ to $B$ is 192, and from $A$ to $C$ is 48. The motorboat traveled a total of 28 hours.

a) Prove that $t_{1}=t_{2}$.

b) Prove that $u=7 v$.

c) Determine the speed of the motorboat in still water.

Consider the function $f:\left[\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{4 \pi}{3}\right] \rightarrow[-2,2]$ given by the formula $f(x)=\cos x+\sqrt{3} \sin x$.

a) Determine the constants $a$ and $b$ such that $f(x)=a \cos (x+b)$.

b) Represent the function $f$ graphically.

c) Show that $f$ is invertible.

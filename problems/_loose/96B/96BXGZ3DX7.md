In an isosceles triangle with a base length of $14 \mathrm{~cm}$ and legs of length $25 \mathrm{~cm}$, a circle is inscribed. Above it, another circle is inscribed which touches both legs and the first circle, above this one a third circle is inscribed, and so on, continuing the process of inscribing circles. If all the inscribed circles are painted with a certain color, what part of the triangle's area will remain uncolored?

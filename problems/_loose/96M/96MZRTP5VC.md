Let $a$ be a constant. For a function $f(x)=\frac{1-a\cos x}{1+\sin x}\ (0\leq x\leq \pi)$, answer the following questions.

(1) Let $t=\frac{-\cos x}{1+\sin x}\ (0<x<\pi).$ Express $\frac{dx}{dt}$ in terms of $t$.

(2) Determine the range of $a$ such that $f(x)$ has extreme value in the range of $0<x<\pi$, then express the extreme value in terms of $a$.

(3) When $a$ lies in the range determined in (2), find the volume of the solid generated by a rotation of the figure bounded by the line passing through the points $(0,\ f(0)),\ (\pi,\ f(\pi))$ and the graph of $y=f(x)$ in terms of $a$.

If $x_{1}<x_{2}<x_{3}<\ldots<x_{n}$ are natural numbers, then the sum of $n-1$ fractions, the $k$-th of which, where $k<n$, is equal to the ratio of the square root of the difference $x_{k+1}-x_{k}$ to the number $x_{k+1}$, is less than the sum of the numbers $1, 1 / 2, 1 / 3, \ldots, 1 / n^{2}$. Prove this.

During math class, each of the 25 students in the 5th grade receives a card on which a non-zero natural number is written. Each student divides the number on the card by 24 and communicates the remainder obtained to the teacher. The sum of the remainders obtained is 288. Student Daniel notices that the remainders obtained by his classmates are all different, and the quotient and remainder obtained by him are equal.

a) What number is written on Daniel's card?

b) Find the sum of the numbers written on the 25 cards, knowing that each student, except for Daniel, obtained a quotient that is 1 greater than the remainder.

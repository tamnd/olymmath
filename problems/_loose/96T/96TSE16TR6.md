(5 points) On the number line, points with coordinates $a_{k}=k \cdot \sqrt{2}, k=1,2,3, \ldots$ are marked. To the right of point 0, a segment is laid out, the length of which changes, and each subsequent time the segment is laid out from the end of the previous segment. The initial length of the segment is 1. If the segment laid out in the current step covers fewer than 5 points $a_{k}$, the length of the segment increases by 1; if it covers more than 5 points, the length decreases by 1; if the segment covers exactly 5 points $a_{k}$, its length remains the same. Is it true that, starting from some point in time, the length of the laid-out segment will be constant? Justify your answer.

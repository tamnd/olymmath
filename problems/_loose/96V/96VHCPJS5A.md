In a non-isosceles triangle $A B C$, point $M$ is the midpoint of $B C$, $P$ is the point closest to $A$ of the intersection of ray $\mathrm{AM}$ and the incircle of the triangle, $Q$ is the point farthest from $\mathrm{~A}$ of the intersection of ray $AM$ and the excircle. The tangent to the incircle at point $P$ intersects $BC$ at point $\mathrm{X}$, and the tangent to the excircle at point $\mathrm{Q}$ intersects $B C$ at point $\mathrm{Y}$. Prove that $MX=MY$.

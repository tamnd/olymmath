Let $n>3$ be a positive integer. Define an integer $k$ to be snug if $1 \leq k<n$ and

$$
\operatorname{gcd}(k, n)=\operatorname{gcd}(k+1, n) .
$$

Prove that the product of all snug integers is congruent to 1 modulo $n$.

Remark. If there are no snug integers, their product is vacuously declared to equal 1.

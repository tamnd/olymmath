Prove that every thetraedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ can be situated between two parallel planes whose distance is not greater than $\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{P / 3}$, where

$$
P=\left(A_{1} A_{2}\right)^{2}+\left(A_{1} A_{3}\right)^{2}+\left(A_{1} A_{4}\right)^{2}+\left(A_{2} A_{3}\right)^{2}+\left(A_{2} A_{4}\right)^{2}+\left(A_{3} A_{4}\right)^{2}
$$

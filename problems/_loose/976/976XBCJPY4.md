Pat and Mat went on a trip. They set out in the morning after eight o'clock, when the large and small hands of Pat's clock lay on opposite rays. The hands of Pat's clock were on opposite rays again when both friends returned before noon. Mat measured the duration of the trip with a stopwatch. Determine with precision to the second how long the trip lasted. Assume that Pat's clock and Mat's stopwatch were both accurate.

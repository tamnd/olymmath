24 Prove: For any real numbers $a, b$, there exist $x$ and $y$ in $[0,1]$, such that
$$
|x y-a x-b y| \geqslant \frac{1}{3} \text {, }
$$

and ask whether the above proposition still holds if $\frac{1}{3}$ is replaced by $\frac{1}{2}$, or 0.33334.

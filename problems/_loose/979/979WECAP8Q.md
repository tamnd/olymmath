ABC is a triangle where all angles are acute. Outside this triangle, we construct three points $X, Y$, and $Z$ such that:

- The triangle $A X B$ is isosceles with base $A B$ and the angle $\widehat{A X B}$ is $120^{\circ}$,
- The triangle $\mathrm{BYC}$ is isosceles with base $\mathrm{BC}$ and the angle $\widehat{\mathrm{BYC}}$ is $120^{\circ}$,
- The triangle CZA is isosceles with base $\mathrm{CA}$ and the angle $\widehat{\mathrm{CZA}}$ is $60^{\circ}$.

Show that the lines (XY) and (BZ) are perpendicular.

Students in an academic year are required to do 25 problems in every consecutive 7 days. The time a student spends on each problem is constant on the same day but varies according to a rule known to the student throughout the academic year, and the time spent on any problem is less than 45 minutes. Prove that to minimize the time spent on doing problems, the student can choose a certain day of the week and complete 25 problems on this day every week.

Prove that in an orthocentric tetrahedron, the following relation holds:

$$
|O H|^{2}=4 R^{2}-3 l^{2}
$$

where $O$ is the center of the circumscribed sphere, $H$ is the point of intersection of the altitudes, $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed sphere, and $l$ is the distance between the midpoints of the skew edges of the tetrahedron.

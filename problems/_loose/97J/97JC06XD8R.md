The buttons $\{\times, +, \div\}$ on a calculator have their functions swapped. A button instead performs one of the other two functions; no two buttons have the same function. The calculator claims that $2 + 3 \div 4 = 10$ and $4 \times 2 \div 3 = 5$. What does $4 + 3 \times 2 \div 1$ equal on this calculator?

$\textbf{(A) }4 \textbf{(B) }5 \textbf{(C) }7 \textbf{(D) }9 \textbf{(E) }10$

(SFRY, 81). Prove that for any values of $a, b \in \mathbf{Z}$, satisfying the inequalities $5 a \geqslant 7 b \geqslant 0$, the system

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
x+2 y+3 z+7 u & =a \\
y+2 z+5 u & =b
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

has a solution in non-negative integers.

A cage contains 10 cats, 7 of which are white and 3 are black. The cage door is opened slightly, allowing only one cat to escape at a time. The cats rush to get out. If all 10 cats eventually escape, let $X$ represent the number of segments into which the 7 white cats are divided by the 3 black cats. For example, in the order of escape, $X=3$, because the 7 white cats are divided into 3 segments. Try to find the distribution of $X$.

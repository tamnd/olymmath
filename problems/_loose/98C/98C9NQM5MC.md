Principle of Extremality (miscellaneous)
Recurrence Relations (miscellaneous).

In the sequence of triples of integers $(2,3,5),(6,15,10), \ldots$ each triple is obtained from the previous one as follows: the first number is multiplied by the second, the second by the third, and the third by the first, and the resulting products form the new triple. Prove that none of the numbers obtained in this way will be a power of an integer: a square, a cube, etc.

$A B C D$ is an isosceles trapezoid, and angle $A B D$ is $90^{\circ}$. On the larger base $A D$, as a diameter, a circle $\Omega$ is constructed. An isosceles triangle KLM (KL=LM) is inscribed in the circle $\Omega$, such that $K L \| A B$ and $L M \| C D$. Prove that the areas of triangle KLM and trapezoid ABCD are equal.

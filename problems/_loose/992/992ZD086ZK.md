Let's call the arrangement of $N$ grasshoppers on a line at various points of it $k$-successful if, after making the necessary number of moves according to the rules of leapfrog, the grasshoppers can achieve that the sum of pairwise distances between them decreases by at least $k$ times. For which $N \geqslant 2$ does there exist an arrangement that is $k$-successful for all natural $k$? (In leapfrog, on a move, one of the grasshoppers jumps to the point symmetric to it relative to another grasshopper.)

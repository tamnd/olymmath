A piece of cheese is given. It is allowed to choose any positive real number $a$, different from 1, and to cut the piece in the ratio 1 : a by weight, then to cut one of the resulting pieces in the same ratio, and so on. Is it possible with a finite number of cuts to obtain pieces of cheese that can be distributed into two portions of the same weight?

Find the largest constant $C$ such that for any positive integer $n$, if $\left\{a_{i}, b_{i}\right\}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$ are $n$ distinct pairs of positive integers, then
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \max \left\{a_{i}, b_{i}\right\} \geqslant C n \sqrt{n},
$$

where $\max \{x, y\}$ denotes the larger of $x$ and $y$.

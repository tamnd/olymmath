Let $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ be the angles of triangle ABC. Prove that

a) $\cos 2 \alpha+\cos 2 \beta+\cos 2 \gamma+4 \cos \alpha \cos \beta \cos \gamma+1=0$

b) $\cos ^{2} \alpha+\cos ^{2} \beta+\cos ^{2} \gamma+2 \cos \alpha \cos \beta \cos \gamma=1$.

c) $\cos 2 \alpha+\cos 2 \beta+\cos 2 \gamma=\frac{O H^{2}}{2 R^{2}}-\frac{3}{2}$, where $O$ is the center of the circumscribed circle, $H$ is the point of intersection of the altitudes.

Given the function
$$
f(x)=a x-1-\ln x(a \in \mathbf{R}) \text {. }
$$
(1) Discuss the number of extremum points of the function $f(x)$ within its domain;
(2) If the function $f(x)$ has an extremum at $x=1$, and for any $x \in(0,+\infty), f(x) \geqslant b x-2$ always holds, find the range of the real number $b$;
(3) When $x>y>\mathrm{e}-1$, prove:
$$
\mathrm{e}^{x-y}>\frac{\ln (x+1)}{\ln (y+1)} .
$$

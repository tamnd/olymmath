Given odd primes $x, y, z$ satisfying
$$
x \mid \left(y^{5}+1\right), y \mid \left(z^{5}+1\right), z \mid \left(x^{5}+1\right) \text {. }
$$

Find the minimum value of the product $x y z$. (Cheng Chuanping)

For any integers $1\leq n\leq m\leq5$ , how many hyperbolas does the equation $\rho=\frac{1}{1-\text{C}_m^n \cos\theta}$ represent?
Note: $\text{C}_m^n=\frac{m!}{n!(m-n)!}$ . $\text{(A)}15 \text{(B)}10 \text{(C)}7 \text{(D)}6$

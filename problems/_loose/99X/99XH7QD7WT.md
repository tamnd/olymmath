In $\triangle A B C$, $D$ is the midpoint of side $B C$, and $E, F$ are points on sides $A B, A C$ respectively, such that $D E=D F$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A E+A F=B E+C F \\
\Leftrightarrow \angle E D F=\angle B A C .
\end{array}
$$

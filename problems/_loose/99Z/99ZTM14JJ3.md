In a lottery box, there are 10 indistinguishable small balls, including 1 red ball, 2 black balls, and 7 white balls. Each red ball has a prize of 5 yuan, each black ball has a prize of 2 yuan, and each white ball has a prize of 0 yuan. Guangtou Qiang draws 2 balls from the lottery box at the same time. The probability that the prize is greater than 2 yuan is ( ).
A. $\frac{1}{10}$
B. $\frac{1}{5}$
C. $\frac{17}{90}$
D. $\frac{2}{9}$
E. $\frac{2}{5}$

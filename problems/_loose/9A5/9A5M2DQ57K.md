Let the side length of the equilateral $\triangle A B C$ be 1, and there are $n$ equal division points on side $B C$, sequentially from point $B$ to point $C$, denoted as $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n-1}$. If $S_{n}=\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A P_{1}}+\overrightarrow{A P_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{A P}_{2}+\cdots+\overrightarrow{A P}_{n-1} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}$. Prove that: $S_{n}=\frac{5 n^{2}-2}{6 n}$.

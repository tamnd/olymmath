On the plane, there are lines $l_{1}, l_{2}, \ldots, l_{2 n}$, intersecting at one point. A flea is sitting at some point $M$ on the plane and jumps over the line $l_{1}$, landing at point $M_{1}$, such that $M$ and $M_{1}$ are symmetric with respect to the line $l_{1}$, then - over the line $l_{2}$, and so on. Prove that if after $2 n$ jumps the flea ends up at point $M$, then starting from any point on the plane, after $2 n$ jumps the flea will return to its original position.

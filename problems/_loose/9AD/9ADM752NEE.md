Given a $X Y Z \triangle$ and a rational number $\lambda$. Construct a $A B C \triangle$ such that its sides $a, b, c$ (or their extensions) pass through the points $X, Y$, and $Z$ respectively, and the ratios established by these points on the sides of $A B C \triangle$ are equal to $\lambda$ $\left(\frac{X B}{X C}=\frac{Y C}{Y A}=\frac{Z A}{Z B}=\lambda\right)$. Perform the construction for $\left.a\right) \lambda=-\frac{1}{4}$ and $b$ ) $\lambda=\frac{2}{5}$. (The ratio is positive or negative depending on whether the distances in the numerator and denominator are in the same direction or opposite directions.)

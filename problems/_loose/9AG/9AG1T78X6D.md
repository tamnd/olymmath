Given a sequence $13,25,43, \cdots$, its $n$-th term is
$$
a_{n}=3\left(n^{2}+n\right)+7 .
$$

Prove that this sequence has the following properties:
(1) Among any five consecutive terms, only one is divisible by 5.
(2) No term in the sequence is a perfect cube.
(Polish Mathematical Competition, 1958)

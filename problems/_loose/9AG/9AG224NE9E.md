(England, 68). Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{7}$ be integers, and $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{7} \ldots$ the same numbers taken in a different order. Prove that the number

$$
\left(a_{1}-b_{1}\right)\left(a_{2}-b_{2}\right) \ldots\left(a_{7}-b_{7}\right)
$$

is even.

Let $n{}$ be a natural number and $f{}$ be polynomial with integer coefficients. It is known that for any integer $m{}$ there is an integer $k{}$ such that $f(k)-m$ is divisible by $n{}$ . Prove that there exists a polynomial $g{}$ with integer coefficients such that $f(g(m))-m$ is divisible by $n{}$ for any integer $m{}$ .

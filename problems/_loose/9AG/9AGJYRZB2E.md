A school is to offer 100 subjects, each subject is taught in a regular class and an advanced class. If two students have at least 51 subjects in different classes, then these two students are said to be unfamiliar with each other. It is known that there are $n$ students, any two of whom are unfamiliar with each other. Prove that $n \leqslant 34$.

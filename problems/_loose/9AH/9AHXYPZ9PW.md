Through any internal point $P$ of the diagonal $A C$ of a given rectangle $A B C D$, lines parallel to its sides are drawn, which intersect segments $A B, B C, C D$ and $D A$ in points $K, L, M$ and $N$ respectively. Prove that

a) lines $L M$ and $K N$ are parallel,

b) the distance between the parallels $L M$ and $K N$ is constant (independent of the choice of point $P$),

c) for the perimeter $o$ of the quadrilateral $K L M N$, the inequality $o \geqq 2|A C|$ holds.

On the table are written three natural numbers $x, y$, and $z$. Determine which, if you know that simultaneously:

- $x$ is the largest of them,
- the least common multiple of numbers $x$ and $y$ is 200,
- the least common multiple of numbers $y$ and $z$ is 300,
- the least common multiple of numbers $x$ and $z$ is 120.

Through a point $M$, lying inside a given trihedral angle with right dihedral angles, all possible planes are drawn. Prove that the volume of the tetrahedron cut off by such a plane from the trihedral angle will be the smallest when $M$ is the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle that is the section of the trihedral angle by this plane.

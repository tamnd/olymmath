Given various non-zero real numbers $a, b$. Prove that any root of the equation

$$
(x+a+b)\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}\right)=1
$$

is also a root of the equation

$$
\left(x^{7}+a^{7}+b^{7}\right)\left(\frac{1}{x^{7}}+\frac{1}{a^{7}}+\frac{1}{b^{7}}\right)=1
$$

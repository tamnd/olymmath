Natural numbers $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ are such that for any natural $k<n$ the sum of any $k$ numbers from this set is not less than $k(k-1)$, and the sum of all numbers in the set is equal to $n(n-1)$. Prove that a football round-robin tournament with $n$ teams could end in such a way that the points scored by the teams coincide with the numbers $A_{i}$.

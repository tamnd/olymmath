The distinct numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3} \in \mathbb{C}^{*}$ have equal magnitudes. Consider the numbers $a=\frac{z_{1}+z_{2}}{z_{1}-z_{2}}, b=\frac{z_{2}+z_{3}}{z_{2}-z_{3}}$, $c=\frac{z_{3}+z_{1}}{z_{3}-z_{1}}$. Show that if $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=-1$ then $a=b=c$.

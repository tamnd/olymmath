Let $a=2+2 \sqrt{3}, b=2-2 \sqrt{3}$ and $S_{n}=a^{n}+b^{n}$, where $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$. Show that:
a) $S_{n+2}=4 S_{n+1}+8 S_{n}$, for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$;
b) $S_{n} \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, 2^{3 n-1}$ divides $S_{2 n-1}$ and $2^{3 n+1}$ divides $S_{2 n}$, for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

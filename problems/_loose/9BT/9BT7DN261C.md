The seventy-ninth problem: In $\triangle ABC$, $D, E, F$ are points on $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, such that $BD + BF = AC$, $CD + CE = AB$. The circumcircle of $\triangle BDF$, $\odot Q$, intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle CDE$, $\odot P$, at point $K$. $O$ and $I$ are the circumcenter and incenter of $\triangle ABC$ respectively. Prove: $OI = OK$.

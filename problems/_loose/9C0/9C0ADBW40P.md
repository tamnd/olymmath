$m=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} \ldots p_{s}^{\alpha_{s}}-$ the prime factorization of the natural number $m$. Denote

$\lambda(m)=\left[\varphi\left(p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}\right), \ldots, \varphi\left(p_{s}^{\alpha_{s}}\right)\right]$.

Prove that $a^{\lambda(m)} \equiv 1(\bmod m)$ for any integer $a$ that is coprime with $m$.

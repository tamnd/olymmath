Circle $\omega$ touches the sides of angle $B A C$ at points $B$ and $C$. Line $\ell$ intersects segments $A B$ and $A C$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. Circle $\omega$ intersects $\ell$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Points $S$ and $T$ are chosen on segment $B C$ such that $K S \| A C$ and $L T \| A B$. Prove that points $P, Q, S$ and $T$ lie on the same circle.

(I. Bogdanov, P. Kozhevnikov)

Two half-lines have their common origin at point $O$. A circle $C_{1}$ is tangent to both half-lines, whose center is located at a distance $d_{1}$ from $O$, and whose radius is $r_{1}$. Subsequently, circles $C_{n}$ are constructed such that $C_{n}$ is tangent to the half-lines, externally tangent to $C_{n-1}$, and the distance from its center to $O, d_{n}$, is less than $d_{n-1}$, for $n>1$. Find the sum of the areas of the circles bounded by the circumferences $C_{n}$, for all $n$, in terms of $r_{1}$ and $d_{1}$.

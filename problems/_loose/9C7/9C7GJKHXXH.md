Given is a polygon with sides of rational length, in which all internal angles are equal to $ 90^{\circ} $ or $ 270^{\circ} $. From a fixed vertex, we emit a light ray into the interior of the polygon in the direction of the angle bisector of the internal angle at that vertex. The ray reflects according to the principle: the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection. Prove that the ray will hit one of the vertices of the polygon.

In triangle $ABC$, let $BC < AC < AB$. The tangent to the circumcircle $k$ of the triangle at $A$ intersects the line $BC$ at $E$. Furthermore, the tangent at $C$ intersects the line $AB$ at $F$, and the intersection of the two tangents is $D$. Show that if the circle passing through points $D, E, F$ also passes through vertex $B$, then

$$
\frac{AE}{AF} \cdot \frac{CE}{CF} = \frac{BA}{BC}
$$

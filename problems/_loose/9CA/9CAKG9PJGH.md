Let $\Omega_{1}$ be a circle internally tangent to another circle $\Omega_{2}$ at $A$. Let $P$ be a point on $\Omega_{2}$. The first tangent to $\Omega_{1}$ passing through $P$ intersects $\Omega_{1}$ at $X$ and $\Omega_{2}$ at $Q$. The second intersects $\Omega_{1}$ at $Y$ and $\Omega_{2}$ at $R$. Show that $\widehat{Q A R}=2 \widehat{X A Y}$.

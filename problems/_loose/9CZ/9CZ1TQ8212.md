In trapezoid $A B C D$, $A D / / B C, \angle B=30^{\circ}, \angle C=60^{\circ}, E$, $M$, $F$, $N$ are the midpoints of $A B$, $B C$, $C D$, $D A$ respectively. Given that $B C=7, M N=3$. Then the value of $E F$ is
(A) 4.
(B) $4 \frac{1}{2}$.
(C) 5.
(D) 6.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1997)

In triangle $\triangle A B C$, the angle bisector $[A E, \mathrm{E} \in(B C)$ intersects the median $[B F], \mathrm{F} \in(A C)$, at point G.

a) Determine $a \in \mathrm{N}$, such that $\frac{B G}{G F} \cdot \frac{C E}{B E}=2^{a}$.

b) If triangles $\triangle A G F$ and $\triangle B G E$ are equivalent, then G is the centroid of $\triangle A B C$.

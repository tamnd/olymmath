In the coordinate axes of velocity versus time, a trapezoid is formed with bases $V$ and $U$. Since the distance traveled is the area under the velocity graph, the problem reduces to finding a segment parallel to the bases of the trapezoid that divides the area in the ratio $1: 2(1: k)$. The desired velocity is given by

$$
W=\frac{V^{2}+k U^{2}}{1+k}
$$

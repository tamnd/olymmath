There is a hall measuring $13 \times 13$ m, divided into meter squares. It is allowed to place rectangular carpets of any size such that their sides follow the grid lines (including the hall's boundary). Carpets can be placed so that they partially overlap and even some can be completely covered by others, but it is not allowed for one carpet to lie entirely on top of or underneath another (even if there are other carpets between them). What is the maximum number of carpets you can place while these conditions are met?

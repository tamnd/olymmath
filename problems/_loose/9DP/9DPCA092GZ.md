Prove that

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
f(x)>0, \\
g(x)>0
\end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{\begin{array}{l}
f(x)>0 \\
f(x)+g(x)>0
\end{array}\right.\right.
$$

Provide an example when these systems are not equivalent.

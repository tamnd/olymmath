$m, n, p$ and $q$ are positive rational numbers, $m>n$ and $p>q$. Prove that if the lengths of the sides of a triangle are

$$
a=m n\left(p^{2}+q^{2}\right), b=p q\left(m^{2}+n^{2}\right), c=(m q+n p)(m p-n q)
$$

then the area of the triangle is a rational number.

In a volleyball tournament, $n$ teams played a round-robin (each played against each other once, there are no ties in volleyball). Let $P$ be the sum of the squares of the numbers representing the number of wins for each team, and $Q$ the sum of the squares of the numbers representing the number of losses. Prove that $P=Q$.

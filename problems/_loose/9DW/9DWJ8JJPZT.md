In the showcase of a jewelry store, there are 15 diamonds. Next to them are signs indicating their weights, which are $1, 2, \ldots, 15$ carats. The seller has a balance scale and four weights with masses of $1, 2, 4,$ and 8 carats. The customer is allowed only one type of weighing: place one of the diamonds on one pan of the scale, and the weights on the other pan to verify that the weight indicated on the corresponding sign is correct. However, for each weight taken, the customer must pay the seller 100 coins. If a weight is removed from the scale and does not participate in the next weighing, the seller keeps it. What is the smallest amount that will have to be paid to check the weights of all the diamonds?

$[8$ points]

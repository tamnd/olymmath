In convex quadrilateral $AEBC$ , $\angle BEA = \angle CAE = 90^{\circ}$ and $AB = 15$ , $BC = 14$ and $CA = 13$ . Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $\overline{AB}$ . If ray $CD$ meets $\overline{AE}$ at $F$ , compute $AE \cdot AF$ .

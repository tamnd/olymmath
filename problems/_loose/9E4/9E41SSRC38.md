In all triangles $ABC$ does it hold:
$$(b^n+c^p)\tan^{n+p}\frac A2+(c^n+a^p)\tan^{n+p}\frac B2+(a^n+b^p)\tan^{n+p}\frac C2\ge6\sqrt{\left(\frac{4r^2}{R\sqrt3}\right)^{n+p}}$$
where $n,p\in(0,\infty)$.

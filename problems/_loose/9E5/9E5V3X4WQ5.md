The 90th problem: Let $\mathrm{q}_{0}, \mathrm{q}_{1}, \mathrm{q}_{2}, \ldots$ be an infinite sequence of integers satisfying the following two conditions:
(1) For all integers $m>n \geq 0, m-n$ divides $q_{m}-q_{n}$;
(2) There exists a polynomial $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})$ such that for all non-negative integers $\mathrm{n}$, we have $\left|\mathrm{q}_{\mathrm{n}}\right|<\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{n})$.
Prove: There exists a polynomial $Q$ such that for all non-negative integers $n$, we have $q_{n}=Q(n)$.

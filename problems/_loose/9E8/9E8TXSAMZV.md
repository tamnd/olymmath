In the Emerald City, there is a social network called "Magic Mirror," which is used only by the residents of this city. The total number of friends in the "Magic Mirror" for any two residents of the Emerald City is no less than the total population of the city. The number of residents in the city is greater than 2.

a) Prove that each resident of the Emerald City has at least two friends in the "Magic Mirror."

b) The ruler of the Emerald City, the Scarecrow, will send a message to all his friends in the "Magic Mirror," they will forward this message to all their friends, and those, in turn, will forward the message to all their friends, and so on. Prove that in the end, every resident of the Emerald City will receive the message.

c) Is it true that all residents of the Emerald City will still receive the message if two of them delete each other from their friends before the Scarecrow sends it?

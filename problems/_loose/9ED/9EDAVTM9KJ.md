Let $k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{100}$ be circles that touch each other at point $A$, and the radius of each is twice that of the previous one. A particle starts from $A$ and travels along the circles in the given order at a constant speed, traversing each circle in a positive direction. After a certain time, another particle starts from $A$ and travels at the same speed in the same order, but traverses each circle in a negative direction. Whenever the particles complete their path on $k_{100}$, they start over from $k_{1}$ and repeat the process. Prove that the particles will definitely meet.

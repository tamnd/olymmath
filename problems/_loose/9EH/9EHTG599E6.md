The ninety-eighth question, given a positive integer $n>1, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n+1}$ are distinct positive integers, ask: Does there exist an integer-coefficient polynomial $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})$ of degree at most $\mathrm{n}$, satisfying the conditions:
(1) For any $1 \leq \mathrm{i}1$;
(2) For any $1 \leq \mathrm{i}<\mathrm{j}<\mathrm{k} \leq \mathrm{n}+1$, we have $\left(\mathrm{P}\left(\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{i}}\right), \mathrm{P}\left(\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{j}}\right), \mathrm{P}\left(\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{k}}\right)\right)=1$.

Call a natural number $N$ *good*if its base $3$ expansion has no consecutive digits that are the same. For example, $289$ is good since its base $3$ representation is $1012013$ . Find the $2024$ th smallest good number ( $0$ is not considered to be a natural number). Your answer should be in base $10$ .

(This question is worth 20 points) Let the sequence of complex numbers $\left\{z_{n}\right\}$ satisfy: $\left|z_{1}\right|=1$, and for any positive integer $n$, we have $4 z_{n+1}^{2}+2 z_{n} z_{n+1}+z_{n}^{2}=0$. Prove: For any positive integer $m$, we have
$$
\left|z_{1}+z_{2}+\cdots+z_{m}\right|<\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3} .
$$

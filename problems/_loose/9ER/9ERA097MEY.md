Let $k$ be a circle with center at point $O$ and point $T$ in its exterior. The tangents from $T$ to $k$ touch $k$ at points $A$ and $B$. Let $k^{\prime}$ be a circle with center at point $T$ that passes through points $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ be an arbitrary point on $k^{\prime}$ that is also in the exterior of circle $k$, such that lines $C A$ and $C B$ intersect circle $k$ again at points $D$ and $E$, respectively, and the order is $C-A-D$ and $C-E-B$. Prove that $D E$ is a diameter of circle $k$.

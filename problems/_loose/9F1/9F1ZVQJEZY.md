(Austria, 72; NRB, 78). Prove that the sequence of non-zero numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, satisfying for some number $a$ the conditions

$$
a_{1}, a_{2} \in \mathbf{Z},\left(a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+a\right) /\left(a_{1} a_{2}\right) \in \mathbf{Z}, a_{n+3}=\left(a_{n+1}^{2}+a\right) / a_{n}
$$

for $n \in \mathbf{N}$, consists of integers.

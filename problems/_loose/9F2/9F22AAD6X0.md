A team of $n$ schoolchildren participates in a game: each of them is given a hat of one of $k$ predetermined colors, and then, at a whistle, all schoolchildren simultaneously choose a scarf for themselves. The team scores as many points as the number of its members for whom the color of the hat matches the color of the scarf (there is a sufficient number of scarves and hats of any color; during the game, each participant cannot see their own hat, but can see the hats of all others, and is not allowed to convey any information until the whistle). What is the maximum number of points the team, having pre-planned the actions of each of its members, can guarantee to get:

a) when $n=k=2$

b) for arbitrary fixed $n$ and $k$?

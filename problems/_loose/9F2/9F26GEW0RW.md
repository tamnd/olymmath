(a) Determine the point of intersection of the lines with equations $y=4 x-32$ and $y=-6 x+8$.

(b) Suppose that $a$ is an integer. Determine the point of intersection of the lines with equations $y=-x+3$ and $y=2 x-3 a^{2}$. (The coordinates of this point will be in terms of $a$.)

(d) Determine the four integers $d$ for which the lines with equations $y=d x+4$ and $y=2 d x+2$ intersect at a point with integer coordinates.

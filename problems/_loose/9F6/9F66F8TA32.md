For each subset $C$ of $\mathbb N$, Suppose $C\oplus C=\{x+y|x,y\in C, x\neq y\}$. Prove that there exist a unique partition of $\mathbb N$ to sets $A$, $B$ that $A\oplus A$ and $B\oplus B$ do not have any prime numbers.

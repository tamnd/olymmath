In quadrilateral $A B C D$, two non-intersecting circles are placed such that one of them touches sides $A B, B C$ and $C D$, while the other touches sides $A B, A D$ and $C D$. Line $M N$ intersects sides $A B$ and $C D$ at points $M$ and $N$ respectively and is tangent to both circles. Find the distance between the centers of the circles if the perimeter of quadrilateral $M B C N$ is $2 p, B C=a$ and the difference in the radii of the circles is $r$.

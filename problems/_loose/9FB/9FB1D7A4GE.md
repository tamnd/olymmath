In the Alpes Maritimes, $n$ cities are connected by roads, such that between any two distinct cities, there is always exactly one one-way road. We denote $N_{A V}$ (resp. $N_{V A}$) as the maximum size of a family of paths from Antibes to Valbonne (resp. from Valbonne to Antibes) such that two distinct paths in the family never pass through the same road. Show that $N_{A V}=N_{V A}$ if and only if there are as many roads leaving Antibes as there are leaving Valbonne.

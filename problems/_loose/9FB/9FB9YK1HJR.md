Given the function $f(x)=A \cos x+B \sin x$ (where $A$ and $B$ are constants). If there exist real numbers $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ such that the difference $x_{1}-x_{2}$ is not an integer multiple of $\pi$ and $f\left(x_{1}\right)=f\left(x_{2}\right)=0$, prove that for every real number $x$, $f(x)=0$.

Let $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ be points on the circumcircle of an acute triangle $A B C$ such that $\overline{A A_{1}}, \overline{B B_{1}}$, and $\overline{C C_{1}}$ are diameters of this circle. Prove that

$$
\left|A_{1} B\right| \cdot\left|B_{1} B\right|+\left|B_{1} C\right| \cdot\left|C_{1} A\right|=|A C| \cdot|B C| \text {. }
$$

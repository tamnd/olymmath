In the plane of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is given from which its sides are seen at angles of $120^{\circ}$ (it is assumed that such a point exists). Prove that: 1) the square of the sum of the distances
from this point to the vertices of the triangle is equal to $\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+4(A B C)\right) \sqrt{3}$; 2) the sum of the squares of the distances from this point to the vertices of the triangle is equal to

$$
\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-\frac{4(A B C)}{\sqrt{3}}\right)
$$

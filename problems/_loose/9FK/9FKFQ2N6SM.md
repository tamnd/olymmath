In the domain of positive numbers, solve the system

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \sqrt{x y}+\sqrt{x z}-x=a, \\
& \sqrt{y z}+\sqrt{y x}-y=b, \\
& \sqrt{z x}+\sqrt{z y}-z=c
\end{aligned}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are given positive numbers.

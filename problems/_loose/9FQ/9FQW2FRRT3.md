Let $A$ be any point on the circumference of the circumscribed circle of a regular $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, if the radius of the circle is $r$, then
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} A A_{k}^{2 m}=n C_{2 m}^{m} r^{2 m}(m \in \mathbf{N})
$$

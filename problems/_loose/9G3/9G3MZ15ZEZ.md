Pista suggested the following game of chance to Sanyi: "We both put 10 fillers into a bowl, and the distribution of the 20 fillers will be determined as follows. Here are two bags, each containing 9 slips of paper, numbered 1, 2, ..., 9. You draw one slip from each bag and multiply the numbers. If the first digit of the product is 7, 8, or 9, like in $8 \cdot 9$ or $3 \cdot 3$, the 20 fillers are yours; if the first digit is 1 or 2, they are mine; otherwise, we split them." - Sanyi replied: "You might almost trick me with this, but we can try it."

The next day, Sanyi suggested: "Today I will be the banker. You put in 10 fillers. If the first digit of the product obtained in the same way as yesterday is at least 5, I will double your money; if it is 3 or less, I win; otherwise, you get your money back."

Let's consider the possibilities and determine which game is more advantageous for whom, and which one is fairer.

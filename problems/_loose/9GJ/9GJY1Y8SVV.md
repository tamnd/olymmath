In the security company "OOO", 2015 employees work. Several rapid response teams (several people in each) have been formed from them, and any two teams have at least one common employee. Prove that all employees of the company "OOO" can be arranged around a Very Secure Object along a circle of length 1 km in such a way that any rapid response team is stretched along this circle by at least 1/3 km (i.e., so that no rapid response team can be completely covered by an arc of length less than 1/3 km).

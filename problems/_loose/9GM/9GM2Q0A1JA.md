Given two points: $A$ and $O$. From $O$, we draw circles with varying radius $r$, and from $A$, we draw tangents to these circles.

(a) Find the maximum area of the isosceles triangle formed by the tangents and the chord of contact.

(b) Find the maximum area of the quadrilateral formed by the tangents and the radii to the points of tangency.

For each natural $n$, let $S_{n}$ denote the sum of the first $n$ prime numbers: $S_{1}=2, S_{2}=2+3=5, S_{3}=2+3+5=10, \ldots$. Can two consecutive terms of the sequence $\left(S_{n}\right)$ be squares of natural numbers?

In the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the centroids of the faces $BCD$ and $ACD$ are $A_1$ and $B_1$, respectively. Let $M$ be a point on the edge $AB$. Let $P$ be the intersection point of the plane $BCD$ with the line through $M$ parallel to $AA_1$, and let $Q$ be the intersection point of the plane $ACD$ with the line through $M$ parallel to $BB_1$. It is to be proven that the sum of the vectors $\overrightarrow{MP}$ and $\overrightarrow{MQ}$ is $4/3 \overrightarrow{MS}$, where $S$ is the centroid of the tetrahedron.

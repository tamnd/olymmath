Given that there are $m$ points inside $\triangle A B C$, together with $A, B, C:$ points, making a total of $(m+3)$ points. Using these points as vertices, $\triangle A B C$ is divided into several non-overlapping small triangles. Points $A$, $B$, $C$ are colored red, yellow, and blue, respectively. The $m$ points inside the triangle are each arbitrarily colored red, yellow, or blue. Question: Is the number of triangles whose vertices are all different colors odd or even?

Let $n$ be an integer greater than 2, and $a_{n}$ be the largest $n$-digit number that is neither the sum of two perfect squares nor the difference of two perfect squares.
(1) Find $a_{n}$ (expressed as a function of $n$);
(2) Find the smallest value of $n$ such that the sum of the squares of the digits of $n$ is a perfect square.

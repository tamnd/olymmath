Let $n \geqslant 2$ be a fixed integer. A number $S$ is said to be special if for every strictly positive integer $k$ and for any decomposition of $n$ into a sum of $k$ strictly positive integers

$$
n=n_{1}+n_{2}+\cdots+n_{k}
$$

with $n_{1} \leqslant n_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant n_{k}$, one can find integers $0 \leqslant a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{k}$ such that

$$
a_{1} n_{1}+a_{2} n_{2}+\cdots+a_{k} n_{k}=S
$$

a) Show that $n^{2}-2 n$ is not special.

b) Find all the special numbers.

On an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, a cube (the bottom face coincides with one of the cells of the board) is placed. It was rolled across the board, flipping over its edges, such that the cube visited all the cells (some cells, possibly, more than once). Could it happen that one of its faces never lay on the board?

Around a circle are written $ 2009$ integers, not necessarily distinct, so that if two numbers are neighbors their difference is $1$ or $2$ . We will say that a number is *huge* if it is greater than its two neighbors, and that it is *tiny* if it is less than its two neighbors. The sum of all the huge numbers is equal to the sum of all the tiny numbers plus $1810$ . . Determine how many odd numbers there can be around the circumference.

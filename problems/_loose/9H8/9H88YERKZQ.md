Consider the sequence $(a^n + 1)_{n\geq 1}$, with $a>1$ a fixed integer.

i) Prove there exist infinitely many primes, each dividing some term of the sequence.
ii) Prove there exist infinitely many primes, none dividing any term of the sequence.

*(Dan Schwarz)*

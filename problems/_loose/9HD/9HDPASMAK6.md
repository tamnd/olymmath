Circles $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ intersect at points $A_{1}, A_{4}$, circles $S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$ intersect at points $A_{2}, A_{5}$, and circles $S_{3}$ and $S_{1}$ intersect at points $A_{3}, A_{6}$. The broken line $M_{1} M_{2} M_{3} M_{4} M_{5} M_{6} M_{7}$ is such that each line segment $M_{k} M_{k+1}$ contains point $A_{k}$. Moreover, $M_{k}$ and $M_{k+1}$ lie on the circumferences of the two circles intersecting at point $A_{k}$, and point $M_{k+1}$ is different from point $A_{k+1}$. Prove that point $M_{1}$ coincides with point $M_{7}$.
(17th All-Russian Olympiad Problem)

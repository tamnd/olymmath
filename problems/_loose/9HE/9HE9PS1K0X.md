The convex pentagon $A B C D E$ is inscribed in a circle with radius $R$. The inradii of the triangles $A B C, A B D, A E C$ and $A E D$ are denoted by $r_{A B C}, r_{A B D}, r_{A E C}$ and $r_{A E D}$. Prove that

a.) $\cos \angle C A B+\cos \angle A B C+\cos \angle B C A=1+\frac{r_{A B C}}{R}$;

b.) If $r_{A B C}=r_{A E D}$ and $r_{A B D}=r_{A E C}$, then $\triangle A B C \cong \triangle A E D$.

Given $O$ as the origin, $F$ as the right focus of the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, a line $l$ passing through point $F$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A$ and $B$. Points $P$ and $Q$ on the ellipse satisfy
$$
\overrightarrow{O P}+\overrightarrow{O A}+\overrightarrow{O B}=\overrightarrow{O P}+\overrightarrow{O Q}=0 \text {, }
$$

and points $P, A, Q, B$ are concyclic. Find the eccentricity of the ellipse $C$.

The eighty-fourth question: In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $AD$ and $BC$ respectively, such that $\frac{AE}{ED} = \frac{BF}{FC}$. Extend $CD$ and $EF$ to intersect at point $T$, and extend $BA$ and $EF$ to intersect at point $S$. Construct the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle AES$, the circumcircle $\odot P$ of $\triangle BFS$, the circumcircle $\odot Q$ of $\triangle CFT$, and the circumcircle $\odot R$ of $\triangle DET$. Prove that $\odot O$, $\odot P$, $\odot Q$, and $\odot R$ are concurrent, and that quadrilateral $OPQR \sim$ quadrilateral $ABCD$.

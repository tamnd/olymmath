You are the owner of a company selling Tesla Model S electric vehicles. The purchase price of one car is 2.5 million rubles. To transport one car across the border, you need to pay customs duties and fees totaling 2 million rubles per car. Monthly office rent is 50,000 rubles, and the total monthly salary of employees is 370,000 rubles. Other monthly expenses, including the import of vehicles, rental of space for storage of imported vehicles, do not depend on the number of vehicles and amount to 180,000 rubles. Your store has received an order for 30 Tesla Model S cars.

a) Determine the minimum possible price of one car (in million rubles) at which you would be willing to accept this order. Assume that all operations related to fulfilling this order can be carried out within one month and that you will not receive any other orders for purchasing cars in this month.
b) A company has appeared in the city selling these cars for 5.3 million rubles each, providing a free charging station worth 400,000 rubles with each purchased car. At what minimum order volume could you compete with this company if charging stations can be freely purchased on a competitive market? (17 points).

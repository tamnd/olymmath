Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers such that
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a^{2}+a b+b^{2}=25, \\
b^{2}+b c+c^{2}=49, \\
c^{2}+a+a^{2}=64 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Find $(a+b+c)^{2}$.

Given that $P, Q, R$ are points on the sides $AB, BC, CA$ of $\triangle ABC$ respectively, and points $A', B', C'$ are on the segments $RP, PQ, QR$ respectively, and $AB // A'B', BC // B'C', CA // C'A'$. Prove: $\frac{AB}{A'B'}=\frac{S_{\triangle PQR}}{S_{\triangle A'B'C'}}$.

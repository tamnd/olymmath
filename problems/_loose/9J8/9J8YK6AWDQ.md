**a)** Show that there are infinitely many rational numbers $ x>0 $ such that $ \left\{ x^2 \right\} +\{ x \} =0.99. $
**b)** Show that there are no rational numbers $ x>0 $ such that $ \left\{ x^2 \right\} +\{ x \} =1. $

$ \{\} $ denotes the usual fractional part.

In a Cartesian coordinate system, there are 10 different points $P_{1}\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right), P_{2}\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right), \cdots, P_{10}\left(x_{10}, y_{10}\right)$. If $x_{i}=x_{j}$ or $y_{i}=y_{j}$, then $P_{i}$ and $P_{j}$ are called a "coordinate pair" (the order of $P_{i}$ and $P_{j}$ does not matter). If the 10 different points satisfy: the number of points that form a "coordinate pair" with each point does not exceed $m$; and in any case, they can be exactly divided into 5 pairs, none of which are "coordinate pairs". What is the maximum value of $m$?

(2004 Hunan Province High School Mathematics Competition) Given a tetrahedron $O-ABC$ with three edges $OA$, $OB$, and $OC$ mutually perpendicular, $P$ is any point within the base $\triangle ABC$, and $OP$ forms angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ with the three side faces. Prove that: $\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha+\beta+\gamma \leqslant 3 \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

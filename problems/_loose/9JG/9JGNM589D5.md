Denote by $X$ the intersection point (different from $F$) of the angle bisector of $\angle E F G$ with the circle $\mathcal{C}$, and by $Y$ the midpoint of the arc $\widehat{E G}$ containing $F$. Knowing that $\overline{F X}=12$ and $\overline{F Y}=5$, what is the radius of the circle $\mathcal{C}$?
(A) 9
(B) $13 / 2$
(C) 7

Let $m, n$ be distinct positive integers. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
(m, n)+(m+1, n+1)+(m+2, n+2) \\
\leqslant 2|m-n|+1,
\end{array}
$$

and determine the conditions under which equality holds.

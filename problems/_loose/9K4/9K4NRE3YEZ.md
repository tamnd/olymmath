The ninety-ninth problem: Let $\mathrm{f}$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients, and for all sufficiently large positive integers $\mathrm{n}$, it always holds that $\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{n}) \mid\left(\mathrm{n}^{\mathrm{n}-1}-1\right)$. Find all possible expressions for $\mathrm{f}$.

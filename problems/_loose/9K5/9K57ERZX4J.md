Given a square $M N P Q$ with a side length of $1 m$. On the sides of this square, points $A, B, C$ are marked:

- point $A$ is at $\frac{1}{3}$ of the side $N P$ from point $N$,
- point $B$ is at $\frac{2}{3}$ of the side $M Q$ from point $M$,
- point $C$ is the midpoint of the side $M N$.

Triangle $A B C$ is not a right triangle. On which side and by how much should point $C$ be moved so that triangle $A B C$ becomes a right triangle?

It is to be proven: If $p$ and $q$ are the projections of two sides of a triangle onto the third side, and $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the two angles adjacent to the third side $\left(\alpha ; \beta \neq 90^{\circ}\right)$, then the area $A$ of the triangle is given by $A=\frac{1}{2} p q(\tan \alpha+\tan \beta)$.

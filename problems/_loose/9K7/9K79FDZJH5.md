Let $a, b \in(0, \infty)$ and define the sequences $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}}$, respectively $\left(y_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$, by the relations $x_{1}=a, y_{1}=b$ and $x_{n+1}=\frac{2 x_{n} y_{n}}{x_{n}+y_{n}}$, $y_{n+1}=\frac{x_{n}+y_{n}}{2}$, for $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

a) Prove that these sequences are convergent and have the same limit;

b) Determine the limit of these sequences.

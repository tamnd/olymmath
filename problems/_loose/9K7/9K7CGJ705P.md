\section*{Task 1 - 171021}

For four circles $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}, k_{4}$, it is required that they have the following two properties (1), (2):

(1) The diameter of $k_{4}$ is $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ larger than the diameter of $k_{3}$, whose diameter is $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ larger than that of $k_{2}$, and whose diameter is $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ larger than that of $k_{1}$.

(2) The area of $k_{4}$ is as large as the sum of the areas of the other three circles. Investigate for which length of the diameter of $k_{1}$ these two requirements (1), (2) are satisfied!

Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \varepsilon, \varphi, \psi, \omega$ denote the internal angles at vertices $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$, $F$, $G$, $H$ of a convex octagon $A B C D E F G H$, where

$$
\alpha+\beta=\gamma+\delta=\varepsilon+\varphi=\psi+\omega \text {. }
$$

Let $K, L, M, N$ denote the midpoints of the diagonals $A D, C F, E H, G B$, respectively. Prove that the lines $K M$ and $L N$ are perpendicular to each other.

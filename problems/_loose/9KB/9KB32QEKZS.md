Given a positive integer $n$, it is known that using $k$ weights with masses that are positive integers and a balance scale, one can measure the mass of all items with masses $1, 2, 3, \cdots, n$ grams.
(1) Find the minimum value of $k$, denoted as $f(n)$.
(2) For which values of $n$ is the composition of the $f(n)$ weights uniquely determined? Prove your conclusion.

Consider the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel CD$ and a point $M$ inside it through which parallel lines $MP \parallel AB$ and $BP \parallel AM$ are constructed. It is known that the area of quadrilateral BMCP is half the area of quadrilateral ABCD.

a) Show that $[MD] \equiv [PC]$.

b) If points B, M, and D are collinear, determine the position of point M on diagonal BD if the area of triangle MAB is $\frac{1}{5}$ of the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$.

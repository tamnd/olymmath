A straight line meets the sides $A B, B C, C D, D A$ of a quadrilateral $A B C D$ or their extensions at points $P, Q$, $R, S$ respectively. Prove that the segments formed satisfy the equation:

$$
\frac{\overline{A P}}{\overline{P B}} \cdot \frac{\overline{B Q}}{\overline{Q C}} \cdot \frac{\overline{C R}}{\overline{R D}} \cdot \frac{\overline{D S}}{\overline{S A}}=1
$$

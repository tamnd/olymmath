Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}\left(k \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$ all be integers greater than 1, and satisfy
$$
\left(a_{1}!\right)\left(a_{2}!\right) \cdots\left(a_{k}!\right) \mid 2017!\text {. }
$$

When $k$ varies, find the maximum value of $\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i}$.

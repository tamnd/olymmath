On the segment $[0,1]$, $n$ numbers $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ are chosen. Prove that on the same segment, a number $x$ can be chosen such that $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|x-x_{i}\right|=\frac{1}{2}$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB < AC$ and let $I$ be its incenter. The incircle is tangent to side $BC$ at point $D$. Let $E$ be the unique point such that $D$ is the midpoint of segment $BE$. The line perpendicular to $BC$ passing through $E$ intersects $CI$ at point $P$. Prove that $BP$ is perpendicular to $AD$.

Note. The incircle of $ABC$ is the circle that is tangent to all three sides of the triangle. The incenter is the center of this circle.

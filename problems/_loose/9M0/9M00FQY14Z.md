(Jury, USA, 82). Prove that for any set of numbers $a_{i}, \ldots, a_{n} \in \mathbb{R}$, one can choose a value $k \in\{1 ; \ldots ; n\}$ such that any non-negative numbers $b_{1} \geqslant b_{2} \geqslant \ldots \geqslant b_{n}$, not exceeding 1, satisfy the inequality

$$
\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i} a_{i}\right| \leqslant\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}\right|
$$

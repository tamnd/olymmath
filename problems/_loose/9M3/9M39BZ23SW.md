Let $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ be a regular polygon inscribed in a circle of radius $r$ and center $O$, and let $P$ be a point on the extension of $O A_{i}$. Prove:
( I ) $\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left|P A_{i}\right|=|O P|^{n}-r^{n}$;
(II) $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|P A_{i}\right|^{2}=n\left(r^{2}+O P^{2}\right)$.
(15th Putnam Competition Problem)

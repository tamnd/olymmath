On each face of a cube, a regular 4-sided pyramid is placed, with the base of the pyramid being this face of the cube. All pyramids are equal.

- 4A. Can the lateral edges of three pyramids, emanating from one vertex of the cube, lie in the same plane? If this is possible, find the heights of such pyramids, expressing them in terms of the length $a$ of the cube's edge. If this is impossible, provide a proof.
- 4B. Can the sets of three edges mentioned in 4A lie in planes (each set in its own plane) simultaneously for all vertices of the cube?

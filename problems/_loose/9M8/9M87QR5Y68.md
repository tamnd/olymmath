Given $m \geqslant 0, f(x)=x^{2}+\sqrt{m} x+m+1$. Prove: For all $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, we have $f\left(\sqrt[n]{x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{n}}\right) \leqslant \sqrt[n]{f\left(x_{1}\right) \cdot f\left(x_{2}\right) \cdots f\left(x_{n}\right)}$, where equality holds if and only if $x_{1}=x_{2}=\cdots=x_{n}$.
(Mathematics in Middle School, Issue 1, 1998 Olympiad Training Problem)

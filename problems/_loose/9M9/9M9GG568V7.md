Given the matrix $A \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R}), A=\left(\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right)$. We denote $\operatorname{tr}(A)=a+d$.

a) Show that $A^{2}-\operatorname{tr}(A) \cdot A+\operatorname{det}(A) \cdot I_{2}=O_{2}$.

b) If there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $A^{n}=O_{2}$, show that $A^{2}=O_{2}$.

c) If there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $A^{n}=O_{2}$, show that $\operatorname{det}\left(I_{2}+A X A\right)=1$, for any matrix $X \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R})$.

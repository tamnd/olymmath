Let's prove that in every triangle

$$
f_{a} \cdot f_{b} \cdot f_{c} \leqq s \cdot t \leqq s_{a} \cdot s_{b} \cdot s_{c}
$$

where, using the usual notations, $f_{a}, f_{b}, f_{c}$ are the angle bisectors, $s_{a}, s_{b}, s_{c}$ are the segments of the medians inside the triangle, $s$ is the semiperimeter, and $t$ is the area of the triangle.

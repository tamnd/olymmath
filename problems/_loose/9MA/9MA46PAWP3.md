In a company, a certain amount of money, rounded to the nearest hundred, is to be distributed among four employees as a bonus. They are informed that they can choose between two principles of distribution. According to the first principle, the amount is divided in proportion to their monthly salaries, and according to the second principle, it is divided in proportion to the years they have worked. - Each employee voted for the principle that was more favorable to them, so the vote did not result in a decision. Finally, they agreed that each employee would receive the average of the bonus according to the two principles, rounded to the nearest $10 \mathrm{Ft}$. This way, the youngest received $980 \mathrm{Ft}$. How much did the others receive, if they have been working for 14, 17, 21, and 31 years, respectively, and their current salaries are $1500 \mathrm{Ft}, 1600 \mathrm{Ft}, 1800 \mathrm{Ft}$, and $2300 \mathrm{Ft}$, respectively?

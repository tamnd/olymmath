(2002 Croatian National Mathematical Olympiad) On an island, there live $n$ natives, and any two of them are either friends or enemies. One day, the chief asked each resident (including himself) to make a stone necklace according to the following principle: for every two friends, their necklaces must have at least one stone in common; for every two enemies, their necklaces must have no stones in common (a necklace can have "no stones"). Prove: To fulfill the chief's order, $\left[\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right]$ different types of stones are required, and when the number of stone types is less than $\left[\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right]$, the order may not be possible. (Here, $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.)

In Shvambania, there are 100 cities; if two cities $A$ and $B$ are not connected by a direct telephone line, then there are regular flights from $A$ to $B$ and vice versa, and if there is a direct telephone line between $A$ and $B$, then there are no such flights. It is known that in Shvambania, any two cities can be connected by telephone (possibly through several intermediate points) and that from any city to any other city, one can fly by plane, possibly with several stops along the way. Prove that in Shvambania, there are such 4 cities that from each of them, one can both call and fly to any other, using, if necessary, only two other cities of this quartet as intermediate points.

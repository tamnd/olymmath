In a fish shop, there are three water containers available for keeping live carp.

At the start of sales, there are exactly 20 carp in each of these three containers. At the end of sales, there are a total of 3 carp left. The sold carp were removed one by one.

No exchange of carp between containers took place; no new carp were added during the sales.

Calculate, rounded to 3 decimal places, the probability that at the end of sales, there is exactly one carp in each of the three containers!

Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a triangle, with $[\mathrm{BC}]$ being the smallest side. Show that there exists a point $P$ such that, given any point $X$ on the segment $[B C]$, if we construct the points $Y, Z$ on the sides $[A C]$ and $[A B]$ respectively so that $C Y=C X$ and $B Z=B X$, the circumcircle of triangle $A Y Z$ passes through the point $P$.

Let $R, r$ be the circumradius and inradius of the tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$, respectively, and $h_{i}$ be the distance from vertex $A_{i}$ to the opposite face. The incircle touches the face opposite to vertex $A_{i}$ at $A^{\prime}{ }_{i}(i=1,2,3,4)$. Prove:
( I ) $\sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} A_{k} A_{j} \leqslant 16 R^{2}$;
( II ) $\sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} A_{k} A_{j} \geqslant \frac{9}{4} \sum_{i=1}^{4} h_{i}^{2}$;
( III ) $\sum_{i=1}^{4} h_{i}^{2} \geqslant 64 r^{2}$;
(IV) $\sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} A_{k} A_{j} \geqslant 9 \sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} A^{\prime}{ }_{k} A^{\prime}{ }_{j}$.

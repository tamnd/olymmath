Prove that if
$$
0<x, y<\frac{\pi}{2}, \tan x=3 \tan y,
$$

then
$$
x-y \leqslant \frac{\pi}{6},
$$

with equality if and only if
$$
x=\frac{\pi}{3}, y=\frac{\pi}{6}.
$$

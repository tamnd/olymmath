You drop a 7 cm long piece of mechanical pencil lead on the ﬂoor. A bully takes the lead and breaks it at a random point into two pieces. A piece of lead is unusable if it is 2 cm or shorter. If the expected value of the number of usable pieces afterwards is $\frac{m}n$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ , compute $100m + n$ .

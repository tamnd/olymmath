Given a positive integer $n$. Let the set
$$
I=\left\{0,1, \cdots, 10^{n}-1\right\} \text {. }
$$

Prove: There exist sets $A, B \subseteq I$, such that
$$
A \cup B=I, A \cap B=\varnothing,
$$

and for any natural number $k(0 \leqslant k \leqslant n-1)$, we have
$$
\sum_{x \in A} x^{k}=\sum_{y \in B} y^{k} .
$$

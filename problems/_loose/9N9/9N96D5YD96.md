Show that if $\varphi(t)$ is the characteristic function of a random variable $\xi$, then the Laplace transform of the magnitude $|\xi|$ is given by the formula

$$
\mathrm{E} e^{-\lambda|\xi|}=\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{\mathbb{R}} \frac{\lambda \varphi(t)}{\lambda^{2}+t^{2}} d t, \lambda>0
$$

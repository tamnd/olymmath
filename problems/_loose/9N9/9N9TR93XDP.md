Solve the following system of equations (where $a$ and $b$ are given):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a x+z}{y-z} & =\frac{1+b}{1-b} \\
\frac{2 a x-3 b}{2 y-3 z} & =1 \\
\frac{5 z-4 b}{3 a^{2} x-2 a y} & =\frac{b}{a} .
\end{aligned}
$$

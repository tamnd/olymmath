Given an isosceles $\triangle A B C$ where $A B \cong A C$ and $\measuredangle B A C=80^{\circ}$. Let $A D$ be the altitude of this triangle. Point $E$ is chosen in its plane such that $A D \cong E B$, $\measuredangle A E D=50^{\circ}$, and points $E$ and $B$ are on the same side of line $A D$. Prove that quadrilateral $A C D E$ is a parallelogram.

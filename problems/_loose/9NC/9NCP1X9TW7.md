Two lines on a plane intersect at an angle $\alpha$. A flea is sitting on one of them. Every second, it jumps from one line to the other (the point of intersection is considered to belong to both lines). It is known that the length of each of its jumps is 1 and that it never returns to the place where it was a second ago. After some time, the flea returns to its initial point. Prove that the angle $\alpha$ is measured by a rational number of degrees.

Denote the set of all positive integers by $\mathbb{N}$ , and the set of all ordered positive integers by $\mathbb{N}^2$ . For all non-negative integers $k$ , define *good functions of order k* recursively for all non-negative integers $k$ , among all functions from $\mathbb{N}^2$ to $\mathbb{N}$ as follows:

(i) The functions $f(a,b)=a$ and $f(a,b)=b$ are both good functions of order $0$ .

(ii) If $f(a,b)$ and $g(a,b)$ are good functions of orders $p$ and $q$ , respectively, then $\gcd(f(a,b),g(a,b))$ is a good function of order $p+q$ , while $f(a,b)g(a,b)$ is a good function of order $p+q+1$ .

Prove that, if $f(a,b)$ is a good function of order $k\leq \binom{n}{3}$ for some positive integer $n\geq 3$ , then there exist a positive integer $t\leq \binom{n}{2}$ and $t$ pairs of non-negative integers $(x_1,y_1),\ldots,(x_n,y_n)$ such that $$ f(a,b)=\gcd(a^{x_1}b^{y_1},\ldots,a^{x_t}b^{y_t}) $$ holds for all positive integers $a$ and $b$ .

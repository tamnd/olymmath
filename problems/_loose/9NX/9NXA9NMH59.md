Given is a right-angled triangle $A B C$ with $A B=8, A C=15$ and $B C=17$.

On the side $A B$ lies point $D$ and on the side $A C$ lies point $E$ such that

- the area of $A D E$ is equal to $\frac{3}{4}$ of the area of $A B C$ and
- the perimeter of $A D E$ is equal to the perimeter of $B C E D$.

Calculate the length of $D E$.

The sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ is defined by the conditions $a_{0}=0, a_{n+1}=P\left(a_{n}\right)(n \geq 0)$, where $P(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, $P(x)>0$ for $x \geq 0$.

Prove that for any natural numbers $m$ and $k\left(a_{m}, a_{k}\right)=a_{(m, k)}$.

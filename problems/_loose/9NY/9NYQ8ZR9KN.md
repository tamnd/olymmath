A quadrilateral $AB(C)I$ has an inscribed circle. The circle is tangent to sides $AB, BC, (C)D, DA$ at points $A_1, B_1, (1, I)_1$ respectively. Connecting $A_1B_1, B_1(C_1), (1I)_1, I_1A_1$. Points $E, F, (H)$ are the midpoints of $A_1B_1, B_1(C_1), (1I)_1, I_1A_1$ respectively. Prove: Quadrilateral $EFGH$ is a rectangle if and only if points $A, B, (C), I$ are concyclic.

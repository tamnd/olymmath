Let $T=T(a, b, c)$ denote the triangle $\triangle A B C$ with side lengths $B C=a, C A=b, A B=c$. Define the reciprocal of $T=T(a, b, c)$ as $T^{-1}=T\left(\frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{b}, \frac{1}{c}\right)$. If $T^{-1}$ itself is a triangle, then $T$ is called "reversible". Prove: If $T$ is reversible, then $a>\frac{3-\sqrt{5}}{2} c$.

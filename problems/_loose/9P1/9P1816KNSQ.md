Let $x_{i}$ and $y_{i}$ be positive numbers, and $p>1$ a rational number. Prove that

$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_{i}+y_{i}\right)^{p}\right)^{1 / p} \leqslant\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{p}\right)^{1 / p}+\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} y_{i}^{p}\right)^{1 / p}
$$

If $p<1$, the inequality is reversed (Minkowski's inequality).

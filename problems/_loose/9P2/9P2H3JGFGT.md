(Art gallery problem) Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a non-crossing polygon (not necessarily convex) with $n \geqslant 3$ vertices. Show that there exists a set $\mathcal{A}$ of $\left\lfloor\frac{n}{3}\right\rfloor$ vertices of $\mathcal{P}$ such that for any $X$ inside $\mathcal{P}$, there exists a point $C \in \mathcal{A}$ such that the segment $[C X]$ is entirely inside $\mathcal{P}$.

At a sports competition, a multi-member committee evaluates the performances of three students: Ana, Ema, and Sara. Each member of the committee will rank the students from first to third place and assign $a$ points for first place, $b$ points for second place, and $c$ points for third place. Here, $a, b$, and $c$ are predetermined natural numbers such that $a > b > c$. After the points from all members of the committee are summed, Ana scored 17 points, Sara scored 10 points, and Ema scored 8 points.

Determine how the individual members of the committee ranked the students.

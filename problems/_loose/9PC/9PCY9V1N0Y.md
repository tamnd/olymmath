Ronald throws with three dice. They look just like ordinary dice, only different numbers are on the six faces.

On the first die, the numbers are: $1,1,2,2,3$ and 3.

On the second die, the numbers are: $2,2,4,4,6$ and 6.

And on the third die, the numbers are: 1, 1, 3, 3, 5 and 5.

He adds up the three numbers he throws.

What is the probability that the result is an odd number?
(A) $\frac{1}{4}$
(B) $\frac{1}{3}$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$
(D) $\frac{2}{3}$
(E) $\frac{3}{4}$

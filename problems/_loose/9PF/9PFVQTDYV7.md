Two KVN teams are participating in a game consisting of four contests. For each contest, each of the six judges gives a score - an integer from 1 to 5; the computer calculates the average of the scores for the contest and rounds it to the nearest tenth. The winner is determined by the sum of the four values obtained by the computer. Can it happen that the total of all scores given by the judges to the losing team is greater than that of the winning team?

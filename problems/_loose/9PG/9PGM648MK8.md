Given a plane with a line $v$ and a point $F$ not lying on $v$. Let $E_{1}, E_{2}$, and $E_{3}$ be points in the plane that are equidistant from $F$ and $v$. Let the angle bisector of the angle formed by the perpendicular segment from $E_{i}$ to $v$ and the segment $F E_{i}$ be denoted by $e_{i}$. Show that the circle circumscribed around the triangle determined by the lines $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}$ passes through $F$.

In a quadrilateral $ABCD$, where the sides are $AB=a, BC=b, CD=c$ and $DA=d$, and the angles are $A, B, C$ and $D$, if simultaneously $A=\pi-C, B=\pi-D$ and $a+c=b+d$, then the area of the quadrilateral $T=\sqrt{a b c d}$.

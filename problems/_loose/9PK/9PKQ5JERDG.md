Suppose that $x,y,z$ are positive real numbers that satisfy
\begin{align*}
x+y+z&=xyz\\
\frac{x^2}{16(1+x^2)}=\frac{y^2}{25(1+y^2)}&=\frac{z^2}{36(1+z^2)}.
\end{align*}
Compute
$$\frac{x^2(1+x^2)^2}{x^2(1+z^2)^2}.$$

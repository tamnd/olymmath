In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the circumcenter is $O$, and $M$ and $N$ are two points on line $AC$ such that $MN = AC$. Let $D$ be the projection of $M$ on line $BC$, and $E$ be the projection of $N$ on line $AB$. Prove:
(1) The orthocenter $H$ of $\triangle ABC$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle BED$ with center $O'$;
(2) The midpoint of segment $AN$ is symmetric to point $B$ with respect to the midpoint of segment $OO'$.

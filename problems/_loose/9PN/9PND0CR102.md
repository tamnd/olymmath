Let $A \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ be a matrix that satisfies the following conditions: $\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2014}-I_{2}\right)=\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2014}+I_{2}\right)$ and $\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2016}-I_{2}\right)=\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2016}+I_{2}\right)$. Prove that $\operatorname{det}\left(A^{n}-I_{2}\right)=\operatorname{det}\left(A^{n}+I_{2}\right)$, for any positive natural number $n$.

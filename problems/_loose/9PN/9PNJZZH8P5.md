In trapezoid $ABCD$, the length of the longer base $\overline{AB}$ to the length of the shorter base $\overline{CD}$ is in the ratio $3:1$. On the extension of base $\overline{CD}$ beyond vertex $C$, a point $M$ is chosen such that line $AM$ divides the trapezoid into two parts of equal area. Prove that the distance from point $M$ to vertex $C$ is equal to half the length of base $\overline{AB}$.

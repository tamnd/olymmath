a) Do there exist positive integers $a$ and $d$ such that $[a, a+d] = [a, a+2d]$?

b) Do there exist positive integers $a$ and $d$ such that $[a, a+d] = [a, a+4d]$?

Here $[a, b]$ denotes the least common multiple of integers $a, b$.

Find the smallest positive integer $n$, such that when the positive integer $k \geqslant n$, in the set $M=$ $\{1,2, \cdots, k\}$ of the first $k$ positive integers, for any $x \in M$, there always exists another number $y \in M(y \neq x)$, such that $x+y$ is a perfect square.

The circumcircle of triangle $AEF$ is denoted as $k'$. An arbitrary line through point $E$ intersects circles $k$ and $k'$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove that the midpoint of segment $PQ$ lies on the Feuerbach circle of the triangle.

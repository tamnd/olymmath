Suppose a country has 100 cities, connected by a road network. It is known that even if all roads to and from one of the cities are closed, it is still possible to travel from any city to any other city via the road network. Prove: The country can be divided into two sovereign states, each with 50 cities, such that within each state, it is possible to travel from any city to any other city via the road network.
(1992 St. Petersburg City Representative Team Selection Test)

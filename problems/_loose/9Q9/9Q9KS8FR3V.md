## Task 4

Maria Ivanovna, a highly skilled English teacher, tutors schoolchildren and students. The cost of one hour (60 minutes) of Maria Ivanovna's lesson is 2 thousand rubles (after all taxes). In addition to her tutoring income, she has a steady income from investment activities, which amounts to 30 thousand rubles per month. Maria Ivanovna dedicates her weekends entirely to her family and does not work. Maria Ivanovna is very disciplined and strictly monitors the allocation of her time and income. On working days, which are exactly 20 in each month, she sleeps for nine hours a day, works for some time, dedicates part of her time to her hobby - knitting, and spends the rest of the time on rest and household chores. She considers her hobby an important part of her life, so if she works $L$ hours in a day, she will definitely spend $2L$ hours knitting. She does not sell the knitted items but gives them to friends, acquaintances, and those in need. In addition, Maria Ivanovna is an active philanthropist and donates money to a fund for helping sick children. Every working day, she sets aside exactly $k / 3$ thousand rubles for charity if she spends a total of $k$ hours on rest and household chores that day. Maria Ivanovna spends 60 thousand rubles per month on all household expenses, hobbies, and household management (excluding charitable donations), and she has no other expenses. Of course, Maria Ivanovna loves to rest and do household chores, but she has to work, and every working day she decides for herself how many lessons with students she will conduct that day.

(a) What is the maximum amount of money Maria Ivanovna can donate to charity each month?

(b) Since Maria Ivanovna is a highly skilled English teacher, the parents of the students she teaches are willing to offer her increased pay for additional lessons (beyond those she already conducts daily). Maria Ivanovna has long taken care of the days when she will no longer be able to work, so she confidently spends all her monthly income. Will Maria Ivanovna agree to their offer? If yes, what should the pay for the extra lessons be for her to agree to conduct them? If no, explain why she would refuse such an offer.

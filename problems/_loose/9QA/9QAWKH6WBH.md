Let's prove that for any positive integer $n$, there exists a set of $n$ real numbers such that for any three distinct elements $x, y$, and $z$ of the set, either $|x-y|>\left(1+\frac{1}{n^{1.6}}\right)|x-z|$, or $|x-z|>\left(1+\frac{1}{n^{1.6}}\right)|x-y|$ holds.

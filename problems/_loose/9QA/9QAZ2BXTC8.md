The function $f(x)$ is a quadratic trinomial, $g(x)$ is a linear function, for which the inequality $\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right| \cdot g^{2}(x) \geq|f(x)|+|g(x)|$ holds for all $x$. Prove that $\lim _{x \rightarrow a} \frac{f(x)}{g^{2}(x)}$ exists for any $a$ and its value does not depend on $a$.

Prove that there do not exist integers $x, y, z$ such that the equation $x^{k} + y^{k} = z^{k}$ holds under the conditions $z > 0, 0 < x < k, 0 < y < k$, where $k$ is a natural number.
(22nd Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1959)

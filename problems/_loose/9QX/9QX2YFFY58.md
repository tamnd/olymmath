Suppose that $a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{k}\in\mathbb C$ that for each $1\leq i\leq k$ we know that $|a_{k}|=1$. Suppose that $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{k}a_{i}^{n}=c.$$ Prove that $c=k$ and $a_{i}=1$ for each $i$.

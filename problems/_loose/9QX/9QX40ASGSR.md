33 Given a fixed circle $O$ and a fixed line $l$ outside the circle, draw $OE$ perpendicular to $l$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $E$. Take any moving point $M$ on $l$ (not coinciding with $E$), and draw a tangent from this point to the circle, touching the circle at $A$ and $B$. Now draw $EC$ perpendicular to $MA$, and $ED$ perpendicular to $MB$, and connect the feet of the perpendiculars $C$ and $D$, extending them to intersect $OE$ at $F$. Prove that $F$ is a fixed point.

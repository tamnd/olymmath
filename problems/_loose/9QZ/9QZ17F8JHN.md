(Erdos-Ko-Rado Theorem) Let $2 \leqslant r \leqslant \frac{n}{2}, \mathscr{A}$ be some $r$-element subsets of $Z=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, $\{A \mid A$ is an $r$-subset of $Z$ and contains a fixed element $x$ of $Z\}$, then equality holds.

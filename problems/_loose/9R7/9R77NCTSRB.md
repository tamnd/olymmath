Prove that for any prime $p$ and natural number $n, n \geqslant p$, the following number is divisible by $p$.
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\left\{\frac{n}{p}\right\}}(-1)^{k} C_{n}^{p k}
$$
(All-Soviet Union Mathematical Winter Camp, 1991)

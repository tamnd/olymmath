$$
f(x)=\frac{a}{3} x^{3}+\frac{b}{2} x^{2}+c x+d (a<0)
$$

has two critical points $x_{1}, x_{2} \left(x_{1}<x_{2}\right)$, satisfying $f\left(x_{2}\right)=x_{1}$. Then the equation
$$
a f^{2}(x)+b f(x)+c=0
$$

has ( ) real roots.
(A) 0
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4

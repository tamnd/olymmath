$\operatorname{Az} a_{1}=1, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ is an infinite sequence of natural numbers

$$
a_{k} \leq 1+a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{k-1}
$$

for all $k>1$. It is to be proven that every natural number can be written as the sum of numbers selected from this sequence (or occurs in the sequence itself).

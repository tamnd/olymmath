Find the necessary and sufficient conditions for positive integers $a, b, n$ (where $n \geqslant 2$) such that there exists a one-to-one mapping $f: S \rightarrow S$ from the set $S=$ $\{a+b t \mid t=0,1, \cdots, n-1\}$ to itself, satisfying: for any $x \in S$, $x$ and $f(x)$ are coprime.

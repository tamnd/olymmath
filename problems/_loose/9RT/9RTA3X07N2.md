The 51st question: Find all positive integers $n>1$, for which there exists a permutation $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\}$ of $0, 1, 2, \ldots, n-1$ such that $\left\{a_{1}, a_{1} a_{2}, a_{1} a_{2} a_{3}, \ldots, a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{n}\right\}$ forms a complete residue system modulo $n$.

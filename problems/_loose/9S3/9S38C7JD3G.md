Given a natural number $k<12$. On the vertices of a regular dodecagon, the numbers 1, 2, ..., 12 are written (as on a clock face). In one step, we can either swap two opposite numbers or choose any $k$ adjacent vertices and increase the numbers written in them by 1. Let $T(k)$ denote the statement that after a finite number of steps, it is possible to make all 12 numbers the same. Prove that $T(2)$ is false, $T(5)$ is true, and determine the validity of $T(3)$.

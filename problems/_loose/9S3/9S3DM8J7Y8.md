Show that, for any positive integer $n$,
$$
\sum_{r=0}^{[(n-1)/2]}\left\{\frac{n-2r}n\binom nr\right\}^2 = \frac 1n\binom{2n-2}{n-1},
$$
where $[x]$ means the greatest integer not exceeding $x$, and $ \binom nr$ is the binomial coefficient "$n$ choose $r$", with the convention $ \binom n0 = 1$.

Let $A B$ be a focal chord of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$, and $O$ be the origin. Then (I) the minimum value of the area of triangle $O A B$ is $\frac{p^{2}}{2}$; (II) the tangents at $A, B$ intersect at point $M$, the minimum value of the area of triangle $M A B$ is $p^{2}$.

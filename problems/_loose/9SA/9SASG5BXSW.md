An increasing sequence of natural numbers $\left(a_{n}\right)$ is such that any natural number not equal to a term of the sequence can be represented as $a_{k}+2 k$ for some natural $k$. Prove that for all $k$ the inequality $a_{k}<\sqrt{2 k}$ holds.

Let $a,b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a^5+b^5+c^5=ab^2+bc^2+ca^2$ .
Prove the inequality: $$ \frac{a^2+b^2}{b}+\frac{b^2+c^2}{c}+\frac{c^2+a^2}{a}\geq 2(ab+bc+ca). $$

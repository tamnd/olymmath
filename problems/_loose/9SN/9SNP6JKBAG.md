A square $K_{1}$ with an area of 1 is circumscribed by a circle, and to this circle, a square $K_{2}$ is circumscribed, whose sides are parallel to the sides of the initial square $K_{1}$. The area between the squares $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$ is colored gray. Then, a circle is circumscribed around the square $K_{2}$, and to this circle, another square $K_{3}$ is circumscribed in the same manner. The area between the squares $K_{2}$ and $K_{3}$ is left uncolored. This process is repeated until there are 2024 gray-colored areas and 2025 uncolored areas ( $K_{1}$ is counted among the uncolored areas). What is the area of all the gray-colored areas, and what is the area of all the uncolored areas?

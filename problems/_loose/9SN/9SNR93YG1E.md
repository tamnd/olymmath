In the triangle $A B C$, from vertex $B$ we draw a secant that is antiparallel to $B C$, which intersects side $b$ at point $B_{1}$. From $B_{1}$, we draw another such secant, which is now parallel to $B C$ and intersects side $c$ at point $C_{1}$. This procedure can be repeated indefinitely.

What is the value of $\overline{B C}+\overline{B B_{1}}+B_{1} C_{1}+\ldots$ in the limit, if $b>c . ?$[^0]

[^0]: ${ }^{1}$ Editor's note: A secant antiparallel to $B C$ is a line $B B_{1}$ such that $A C \cdot A B_{1}=A B^{2}$.

(application of strong induction) Show that for all $\mathfrak{n} \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, one can find $k$ prime numbers $p_{0}, \ldots, p_{k-1}$ such that $\prod_{i \in k} p_{i}=n$.

NB. We assume that the product of zero prime numbers is 1.

Let $M=\{1,2, \cdots, 20\}$, for any 9-element subset $S$ of $M$, the function $f(S)$ takes an integer value between 1 and 20, prove that: regardless of what $f$ is, there always exists a 10-element subset $T$ of $M$, such that for all $k \in T$, $f(T-\{k\}) \neq k$ ( $T-\{k\}$ is the difference set of $T$ and $\{k\}$).

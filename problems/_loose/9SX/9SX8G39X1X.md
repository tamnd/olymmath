(A little bit of graphs)

A finite graph is a finite set of vertices connected by edges: we agree that two vertices are connected by at most one edge, but an edge can connect a vertex to itself. The degree of a vertex is the number of edges of which it is an endpoint (an edge that connects a vertex to itself counts twice).

Graphs are widely used in combinatorics, and many results are proven by induction or double counting (on edges, faces, vertices, ...), such as the following:

Show that the number of vertices of odd degree is even.

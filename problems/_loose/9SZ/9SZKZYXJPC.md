Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $H$ its orthocenter, and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle.

Show that $\mathcal{P}_{\Gamma}(H)=8 R^{2} \cos (\alpha) \cos (\beta) \cos (\gamma)$, where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle and $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles of the triangle.

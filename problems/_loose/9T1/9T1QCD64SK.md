Let $x$ be a primitive root modulo a prime $p$. Suppose that $x^{p^{\alpha-2}(p-1)} \not \equiv 1\left(\bmod p^{\alpha}\right)$, where $\alpha \geqslant 2$.

Prove that then $x$ is a primitive root modulo $p^{\alpha}$.

Let $\xi$ and $\zeta$ be independent random variables, and $\mathrm{E} \xi=0$. Show that

$$
\mathrm{E}|\zeta| \leqslant \mathrm{E}|\xi+\zeta|
$$

Generalize this property by establishing that

$$
\mathrm{E} \max _{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}\left|\zeta_{i}\right| \leqslant \mathrm{E} \max _{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}\left|\xi_{i}+\zeta_{i}\right|,
$$

if $\mathrm{E} \xi_{i}=0,1 \leqslant i \leqslant n$, and the families $\left\{\xi_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{n}$ and $\left\{\zeta_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{n}$ are independent of each other.

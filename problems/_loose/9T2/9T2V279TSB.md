We are looking for numbers ending with the digit $5$ such that in their decimal expansion each digit beginning with the second digit is no less than the previous one. Moreover the squares of these numbers must also possess the same property.
(a) Find four such numbers.
(b) Prove that there are infinitely many.

(A. Andjans, Riga)

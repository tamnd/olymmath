In a bag, there are 21 regular dice, each with numbers from 1 to 6 on their faces. All the dice are numbered. The audience member takes 3 dice from the bag, shows them to the magician, and puts one die in their pocket. The magician then places the two remaining dice on the table. The audience member writes down the numbers on the top faces of the dice (in whatever order they choose) and tells them to the second magician. The second magician then names the number of the die in the audience member's pocket. How can the magicians agree in advance to make the trick work?

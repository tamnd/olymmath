(a) Prove that for any positive numbers $x_1,x_2,...,x_k$ ( $k > 3$ ), $$ \frac{x_1}{x_k+x_2}+ \frac{x_2}{x_1+x_3}+...+\frac{x_k}{x_{k-1}+x_1}\ge 2 $$ (b) Prove that for every $k$ this inequality cannot be sharpened, i.e. prove that for every given $k$ it is not possible to change the number $2$ in the right hand side to a greater number in such a way that the inequality remains true for every choice of positive numbers $x_1,x_2,...,x_k$ .

(A Prokopiev)

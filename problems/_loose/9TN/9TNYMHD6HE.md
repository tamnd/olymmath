On a plane, there are $n(n \geqslant 5)$ points, no three of which are collinear. Using these points as vertices, connect them with line segments to form $m$ triangles $\left(m \leqslant \mathrm{C}_{n}^{3}\right)$. Prove: The number of complete graphs $K_{4}$ is $\geqslant \frac{m}{4}\left(\frac{9 m}{n}-\right.$ $\left.\frac{3}{2} n^{2}+\frac{11}{2} n-6\right)$.

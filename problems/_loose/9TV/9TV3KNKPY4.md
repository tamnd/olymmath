Through the vertex of a triangle, a line is drawn perpendicular to the line connecting the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles. Prove that this line, together with the sides of the given triangle, forms two triangles for which the difference in the radii of the circumscribed circles is equal to the distance between the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of the original triangle.

Let $(G, \bullet)$ be a group. For any $x \in G$ and any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, we denote $x^{n}=\underbrace{x \bullet x \bullet \ldots \bullet x}_{\text {n times }}$.

a) Prove that for any $m, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ and $x \in G$, the equality $x^{m} \bullet x^{n}=x^{n} \bullet x^{m}$ holds;

b) Let $x, y \in G$ with the property that there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $x^{n}=y$. Prove that $x \bullet y=y \bullet x$.

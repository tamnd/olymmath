Given a prime $p \equiv 3(\bmod 4)$. For an integer sequence of length no more than $p-1$ composed of $\pm 1, \pm 2, \cdots, \pm \frac{p-1}{2}$, if it contains an equal number of positive and negative terms, it is called a "balanced" sequence. Let $M_{p}$ denote the number of balanced sequences. Prove: $M_{p}$ is not a perfect square.

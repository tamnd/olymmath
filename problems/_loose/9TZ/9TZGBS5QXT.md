Let $p_{k}(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $k$ that takes integer values at $x=n, n+1, \ldots, n+k$ for some integer $n$. Then

$$
p_{k}(x)=c_{0} C_{x}^{k}+c_{1} C_{x}^{k-1}+c_{2} C_{x}^{k-2}+\ldots+c_{k}
$$

where $c_{0}, c_{1}, \ldots, c_{k}$ are integers.

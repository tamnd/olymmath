Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer. A sequence of $n$ strictly positive integers is said to be very friendly if it satisfies the following condition: for any positive integer $k \geq 2$, if the number $k$ appears in the sequence, then $k-1$ also appears, and furthermore, the first appearance of $k-1$ occurs before the last appearance of $k$. How many very friendly sequences of length 8 are there? Go to the cell corresponding to the integer part of the square root of this number to find a puzzle.

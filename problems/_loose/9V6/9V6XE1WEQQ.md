Let $S$ be the sum of the digits of a certain five-digit number, all of whose digits are distinct and non-zero. We listed all five-digit numbers obtained by permuting the digits of the original number, and then added all the listed numbers, including the original number. Prove that the resulting sum is divisible by $S$.

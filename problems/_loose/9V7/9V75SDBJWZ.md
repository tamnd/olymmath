The ratio of the number of red, orange, and yellow cards is $1: 3: 4$, and the ratio of the number of green, blue, and white cards is 3:1:6. It is known that there are 50 more yellow cards than blue cards. How many cards must be drawn to ensure that at least 60 cards of the same color are drawn?

Let $O_{1}$ be the center and $r_{1}$ the radius of circle $k_{1}$, and let $O_{2}$ be the center and $r_{2}$ the radius of circle $k_{2}$. The circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ intersect at point $M$. The segment $O_{1} O_{2}$ intersects the circles at points $A$ and $B$. Prove that $M A$ and $M B$ bisect the angle $O_{1} M O_{2}$ if and only if the tangent segment from $O_{1}$ to circle $k_{2}$ is the geometric mean of $r_{1}$ and $O_{1} O_{2}$, and the tangent segment from $O_{2}$ to circle $k_{1}$ is the geometric mean of $r_{2}$ and $O_{1} O_{2}$.

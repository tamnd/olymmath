Let's prove that the value of the following product does not change if we omit the last term from both factors.

$$
\left(a^{2}+2 a+4+\frac{8}{a}+\frac{16}{a^{2}}+\frac{64}{(a-2) a^{2}}\right) \cdot\left(a^{2}-2 a+4-\frac{8}{a}+\frac{16}{a^{2}}-\frac{64}{(a+2) a^{2}}\right)
$$

Prove that for the functions $f(x)=\sin x$ and $g(x)=\cos x$, all conditions of Cauchy's theorem are satisfied on the interval $x \in [0 ; \pi / 2]$. Find the point $c$ where the equality

$$
\frac{f^{\prime}(c)}{g^{\prime}(c)}=\frac{f(\pi / 2)-f(0)}{g(\pi / 2)-g(0)}
$$

holds.

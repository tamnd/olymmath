On a $10 \times 10$ chess board whose colors of square are green and blue in an arbitrary way and we are simultaneously allowed to switch all the colors of all squares in any $(2 \times 2)$ and $(5\times 5)$ region. Can we transform any coloring of the board into one where all squares are blue ? Give a proper explanation of your answer.

Note. that if a unit square is part of both the $2\times 2$ and $5\times 5$ region,then its color switched is twice(i.e switching is additive)

Let the function $f=f(x), x \in \mathbb{R}$, be such that $\int_{0}^{\infty}|f(x)|^{p} d x < \infty$. Prove that the following continuity property in $L^{p}$ holds:

$$
\int_{\mathbb{R}}|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{p} d x \rightarrow 0, h \rightarrow 0
$$

A group of tourists wants to travel from $A$ to $B$ by bus, but only one bus is available, which can only carry one-fourth of the group at a time, and there is not enough time for it to transport them from $A$ to $B$ one after another.

Therefore, the group sets out at the same time, with one-fourth of them on the bus and the rest on foot. The bus drops off the first group at some point along the route, then turns back to pick up the second quarter of the group, but does not take them all the way either, and returns for the third group, and similarly for the fourth, which it finally takes to $B$. The transportation is organized in such a way that all four groups arrive in $B$ at the same time.

Assuming that both the bus and the pedestrians have a constant speed, and the bus's speed is 7 times that of the pedestrians, what fraction of their journey do the tourists travel by bus, and what fraction on foot? How many times longer would it have taken to complete the journey if the bus had taken all four groups from $A$ to $B$?

In a group of six people, people can be friends or enemies while respecting the following conditions:

$\Delta$ Two people are always either friends or enemies.
$\triangleright$ If $A$ is a friend (respectively an enemy) of $B$, then $B$ is a friend (respectively an enemy) of $A$. (We say that the relationship is symmetric.)

Show that there exists a group of people who are all friends or all enemies.

Let $A B C$ be a triangle and $Q$ a point on the internal angle bisector of $\angle B A C$. Circle $\omega_{1}$ is circumscribed to triangle $B A Q$ and intersects the segment $A C$ in point $P \neq C$. Circle $\omega_{2}$ is circumscribed to the triangle $C Q P$. Radius of the cirlce $\omega_{1}$ is larger than the radius of $\omega_{2}$. Circle centered at $Q$ with radius $Q A$ intersects the circle $\omega_{1}$ in points $A$ and $A_{1}$. Circle centered at $Q$ with radius $Q C$ intersects $\omega_{1}$ in points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$. Prove $\angle A_{1} B C_{1}=\angle C_{2} P A$.

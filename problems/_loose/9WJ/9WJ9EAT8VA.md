Ivan the Tsarevich has 10 gold coins. He knows that among them there are 5 genuine and 5 counterfeit coins, but he cannot distinguish them. As is known, Baba Yaga can distinguish counterfeit coins from genuine ones. Ivan agreed with Baba Yaga that he would show her any three coins, and she would choose two of them (Ivan knows which coins Baba Yaga chooses) and tell him how many of them are counterfeit. Can Baba Yaga, speaking only the truth, ensure that Ivan cannot identify all 5 counterfeit coins in 2020 questions?

Given $\odot O_{1} 、 \odot O_{2}$ intersect at points $A 、 B$, a line through point $B$ intersects $\odot O_{1}$ at another point $C$, and intersects $\odot O_{2}$ at another point $D$. The tangents to $\odot O_{1} 、 \odot O_{2}$ at points $C 、 D$ intersect at point $E$, and line $A E$ intersects the circumcircle $\Gamma$ of $\triangle A O_{1} O_{2}$ at another point $F$. Prove: The length of segment $E F$ is equal to the diameter of circle $\Gamma$.

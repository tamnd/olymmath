There is a convex body bounded by 12 regular triangular faces. Let's denote the common length of its edges by $e$ and the distance between two non-adjacent vertices of a pentagonal vertex by $(e+t)$. Show that

$$
t^{3}-7 e t^{2}+2 e^{3}=0
$$

Determine the length of the diagonals of the body to two decimal places if the edge length is one unit. (The image of the body can be found in Tamás Bérczi's article "Convex Triangular Bodies" in the November issue of our journal.)

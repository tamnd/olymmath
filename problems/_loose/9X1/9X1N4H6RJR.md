Let $f(x)$ be defined on the interval $I$, for any $x_{1}, x_{2} \in I, 0 \leqslant t \leqslant 1$, the inequality $f\left((1-t) x_{1} + t x_{2}\right) \leqslant (1-t) f\left(x_{1}\right) + t f\left(x_{2}\right)$ always holds. Let $n \geqslant 2, 1 \leqslant i \leqslant n, x_{i} \in I, p_{i} \geqslant 0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i}=1$, prove: $f\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i} x_{i}\right) \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i} f\left(x_{i}\right)$.

On the plane, $4 n+2$ points are marked, no three of which lie on the same line. Half of the points are painted red, and half are painted green. Prove that there exists a line passing through one red and one green point, on one side of which there are exactly $n$ red and exactly $n$ green points.

(a discrete version of a classical theorem from topology)

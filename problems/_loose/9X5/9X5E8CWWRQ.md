Minibus. A ride in a minibus costs 75 rubles. Fifteen passengers enter one by one and do not exchange money among themselves. With a probability of 0.5, a passenger gives the driver a 100-ruble bill and receives 25 rubles in change in 5 or 10-ruble coins, and with a probability of 0.5, a passenger gives the driver 50 rubles in one bill and 25 rubles in coins.

a) (from 9th grade, 5 points) Before the trip, the driver had no coins at all. What is the probability that the driver will be able to immediately give change to everyone who pays with a 100-ruble bill?

b) (from 9th grade, 7 points) What amount in coins should the driver have before departure to have a probability of at least 0.95 of being able to immediately give change to all passengers who pay with a 100-ruble bill?

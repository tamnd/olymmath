Let $ ABC$ be an isosceles triangle with $ \left|AB\right| = \left|AC\right| = 10$ and $ \left|BC\right| = 12$. $ P$ and $ R$ are points on $ \left[BC\right]$ such that $ \left|BP\right| = \left|RC\right| = 3$. $ S$ and $ T$ are midpoints of $ \left[AB\right]$ and $ \left[AC\right]$, respectively. If $ M$ and $ N$ are the foot of perpendiculars from $ S$ and $ R$ to $ PT$, then find $ \left|MN\right|$.

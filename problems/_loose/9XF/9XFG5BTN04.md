Divide 1996 people into disjoint subsets, satisfying the following conditions:
(1) In each subset, there is no one who knows everyone in this subset;
(2) In each subset, among any three people, at least two do not know each other;
(3) In each subset, for any two people who do not know each other, there is exactly one person in this subset who knows both of them.

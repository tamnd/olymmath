A cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$ is given. The lines $A B$ and $C D$ on which the sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{C D}$ lie intersect at point $T(A \in \overline{B T}$, and $D \in \overline{C T})$. The measure of angle $A T C$ is equal to half the difference of the measures of the central angles corresponding to the chords $\overline{B C}$ and $\overline{D A}$. Prove it!

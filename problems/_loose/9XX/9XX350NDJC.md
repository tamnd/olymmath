In the country, there are two capitals and several cities, some of which are connected by roads. Among the roads, some are toll roads. It is known that on any path from the southern capital to the northern capital, there are no fewer than 10 toll roads. Prove that all toll roads can be distributed among 10 companies in such a way that on any path from the southern capital to the northern capital, there are roads belonging to each of the companies.

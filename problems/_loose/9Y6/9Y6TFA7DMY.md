Jirka made a paper model of a regular quadrilateral pyramid ABCDV with base $ABCD$. When he then cut the model along four edges, it could be unfolded (without overlapping) into a plane. How many different nets of the given pyramid could Jirka get? It turned out that the net Jirka got had the shape of a (non-convex) heptagon. Calculate the angle AVB in the side face of the pyramid.

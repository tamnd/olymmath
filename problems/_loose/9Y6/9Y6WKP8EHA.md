Let $M$ be the midpoint of side $AB$ in $\triangle ABC$, and let $P$ be a point on $AB$ between $A$ and $M$. Draw $MD$ parallel to $PC$ and intersecting $BC$ at $D$. If $r$ represents the ratio of the area of $\triangle BPD$ to the area of $\triangle ABC$, then
(A) $\frac{1}{2}<r<1$, depending on the position of $P$.
(B) $r=\frac{1}{2}$, regardless of the position of $P$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2} \leqslant r<1$, depending on the position of $P$.
(D) $\frac{1}{3}<r<\frac{2}{3}$, depending on the position of $P$.
(E) $r=\frac{1}{3}$, regardless of the position of $P$.
(17th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1966)

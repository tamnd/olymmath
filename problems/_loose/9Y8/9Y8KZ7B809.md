Ivana decided to buy some products in an online store. In the cart, she already has 4 products whose total price is $30 €$. If she adds a jacket to these products, the average price of all selected products will be $20 €$. If instead of the jacket, she chooses boots and pants, the average price would also be $20 €$. It is known that the price of the boots is $54 €$ higher than the price of the pants. Determine the price of the jacket, the price of the boots, and the price of the pants.

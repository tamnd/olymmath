In the plane, given a set $E$ of 1991 points, each point in $E$ is connected by line segments to at least 1593 other points in $E$. Prove that there must exist 6 points in $E$ such that each pair of them is connected by a line.

Also, ask whether there exist 1991 points, each of which is connected by line segments to at least 1592 other points, but in any set of 6 points, at least two points are not connected.

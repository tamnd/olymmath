On the side $\overline{A B}$ of triangle $A B C$, there are points $P_{1}, P_{2}$ and $P_{3}$ such that

$$
\left|A P_{1}\right|=\left|P_{1} P_{2}\right|=\left|P_{2} P_{3}\right|=\left|P_{3} B\right|=\frac{1}{4}|A B|
$$

Parallel lines to side $\overline{B C}$ are drawn through these points, dividing the triangle into four parts. The area of the part located between the parallels through $P_{2}$ and $P_{3}$ is 5.

What is the area of triangle $A B C$?

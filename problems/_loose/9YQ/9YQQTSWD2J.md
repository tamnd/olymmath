In $\triangle A B C$, $A B=10, A C=8, B C=6$. Circle $P$ is the smallest circle passing through $C$ and tangent to $A B$. Let $Q, R$ be the points where circle $P$ intersects sides $A C, B C$ (not coinciding with $C$). The length of segment $Q R$ is
(A) 4.75 .
(B) 4.8 .
(C) 5 .
(D) $4 \sqrt{2}$.
(E) $3 \sqrt{3}$.
(29th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1978)

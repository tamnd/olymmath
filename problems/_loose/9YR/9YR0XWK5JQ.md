A $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{2 n}$, we know that their sum is 0, and $c_{j}=c_{n+j}(j=1,2, \ldots, n)$. Prove that there is a natural number $k$ $(1 \leq k \leq n)$, for which the sum $c_{k}+c_{k+1}+\ldots+c_{k+j}$ is non-negative for every $0 \leq j<n$.

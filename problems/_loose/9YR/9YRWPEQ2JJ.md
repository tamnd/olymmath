Let event $A$ be such that it is independent of itself, i.e., $A$ and $A$ are independent. Show that then $\mathrm{P}(A)$ is equal to 0 or 1.

Show also that if events $A$ and $B$ are independent and $A \subseteq B$, then either $\mathrm{P}(A)=0$, or $\mathrm{P}(B)=1$.

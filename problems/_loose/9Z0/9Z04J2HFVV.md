Given a convex polygon $M$ such that the lengths of all its sides and diagonals are integers. $K$ is a given square. Prove that there is a finite set of polygons congruent to $M$ such that their union contains $K$ and any point of the square not lying on the side of one of the polygons is covered by the same number of polygons from this set.

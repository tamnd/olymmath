In a two-dimensional coordinate system, a flea, too small to be seen, is jumping from lattice points. Starting from the origin, every twentieth of a minute, it jumps by one of the vectors $u_{1}, u_{2}, u_{3}$, or it may not move at all. The vectors $u_{1}, u_{2}, u_{3}$, which we know, do not lie in the same half-plane. We can poison two lattice points every forty seconds. If the flea is on one of these points at that moment, or later jumps to a poisoned lattice point, it will shout "NYEKK!" in a loud voice and fall silent forever. Can we eliminate the flea?

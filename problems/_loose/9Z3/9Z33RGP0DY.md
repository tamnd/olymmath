(i) $g(1,0,0,1)=1$
(ii) $g(ea,b,ec,d)=eg(a,b,c,d)$
(iii) $g(a+e, b, c+f, d)= g(a,b,c,d)+g(e,b,f,d)$
(iv) $g(a,b,c,d)+g(b,a,d,c)=0$

is :

(A)$1$
(B)$0$
(C)$\text{infinitely many}$
(D)$\text{None of these}$

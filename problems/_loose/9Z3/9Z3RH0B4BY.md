Let $a$, $b$, and $c$ be positive real numbers. Prove that:
$$
\frac{a\left(a^{2}+b c\right)}{b+c}+\frac{b\left(b^{2}+c a\right)}{c+a}+\frac{c\left(c^{2}+a b\right)}{a+b} \geq a b+b c+c a.
$$

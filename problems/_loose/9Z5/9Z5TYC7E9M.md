In triangle $ABC$, rectangles are inscribed such that two vertices of each are on side $AB$, and the other two are on sides $AC$ and $BC$. Prove that if the base $AB$ is equal to the corresponding height of the triangle, then the perimeters of these rectangles are equal. Prove the converse theorem.

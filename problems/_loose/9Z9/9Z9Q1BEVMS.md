The vertices of a convex hexagon $A B C A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ are the endpoints of the diameters $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}$ of a circle, and $P$ is a point on the circle different from the vertices of the hexagon. Let the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to the sides $A B, B C, C A^{\prime}, A^{\prime} B^{\prime}, B^{\prime} C^{\prime}, C^{\prime} A$ be $Q_{1}, Q_{2}, Q_{3}, Q_{4}, Q_{5}, Q_{6}$, respectively. It is to be proven that any two consecutive sides of the hexagon $Q_{1} Q_{2} Q_{3} Q_{4} Q_{5} Q_{6}$ form a right angle, and that the midpoints of the segments $Q_{1} Q_{4}, Q_{2} Q_{5}, Q_{3} Q_{6}$ and $P$ lie on a circle.

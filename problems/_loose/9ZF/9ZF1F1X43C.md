Let $\left(p_{n}\right)$ be the sequence of prime numbers ordered by their size, i.e., let $p_{1}=2, p_{2}=3, p_{3}=5$, $p_{4}=7, \ldots$

Investigate whether there exists a natural number $N$ such that for all natural numbers $n$ with $n>N$ the inequality $p_{n}>4 n$ holds.

Construct a trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB \| CD$ from $a-c=3 \text{ cm}, b=4 \text{ cm}, d=6 \text{ cm}, e=9 \text{ cm}$!

Here, $a, b, c$, and $d$ represent the lengths of the sides $AB, BC, CD, DA$ in that order, and $e$ represents the length of the diagonal $AC$.

Describe and justify your construction!

Determine whether the given segments uniquely determine a trapezoid up to congruence!

Pedrinho chose 8 distinct numbers between 1 and 11 and wrote them in a certain order. Joãozinho, seeing the numbers that Pedrinho wrote, noticed the following curious fact: if we take the average of the first $n$ numbers written by Pedrinho, for $n=1, \ldots, 8$, the result will always be an integer. That is, if we take the average of the first two numbers, the first three numbers, the first four numbers, and so on, all these averages will be integers.

What are the possible sequences of numbers that Pedrinho wrote? (Hint: first figure out the possible sums for the 8 numbers, and then try to discover the chosen numbers from back to front.)

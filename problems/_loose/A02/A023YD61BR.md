In a country, transportation between cities is possible by train and bus. Both the railway company and the bus company operate routes between certain pairs of cities, but between two cities, not necessarily every direction is served by a route. We know that no matter how we choose two cities, one can travel from one to the other using one type of transportation (possibly with transfers), but from the other to the first, this is not necessarily possible. Prove that there is a city from which any other city can be reached using one type of transportation, and the means of transportation to different cities can be different.

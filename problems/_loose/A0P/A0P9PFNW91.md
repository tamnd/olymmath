A. Given that $a, b$ are non-zero real numbers, on the parabola $y=a x^{2}+b$ there are three different points $A, B, C$, and it is known that $B C \parallel x$-axis, $\angle B A C=90^{\circ}$. Then the distance from point $A$ to the line $B C$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{1}{|a|}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2|a|}$
(C) $|a|$
(D) $2|a|$

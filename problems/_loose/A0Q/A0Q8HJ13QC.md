On the board, there are the first $n$ natural numbers $(n \geqslant 3)$. Ante repeats the following procedure: first, he arbitrarily selects two numbers on the board, and then increases them by the same arbitrary amount.

Determine all natural numbers $n$ for which Ante, by repeating this procedure, can achieve that all numbers on the board are equal.

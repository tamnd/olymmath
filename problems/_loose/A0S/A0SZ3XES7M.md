Two pirates are dividing a treasure consisting of two bags of coins and a diamond, acting according to the following rules. First, the first pirate takes some coins from any bag and moves the same number of coins from this bag to the other. Then the second pirate does the same (choosing the bag from which he takes the coins at his discretion) and so on until it is possible to take coins according to these rules. The pirate who takes the coins last gets the diamond. Who will get the diamond, if each pirate is trying to get it? Give the answer depending on the initial number of coins in the bags.

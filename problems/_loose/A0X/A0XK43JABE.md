Consider a parabola on the plane. Let's call "new lines" on the plane this parabola and all its translations, as well as lines parallel to the axis of the parabola. Prove that the following statements are true for the new lines:

(a) For any two distinct points in the plane, there exists a new line that passes through both points;

(b) Two distinct new lines have at most one common point;

(c) Given a new line and a point not lying on it, there exists one and only one new line that passes through the point and has no common points with the given new line.

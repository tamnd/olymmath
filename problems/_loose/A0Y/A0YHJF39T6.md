Fish or chicken? On board an airliner, there are $2 \mathrm{n}$ passengers, and the airline has loaded $\mathrm{n}$ portions of meals with chicken and $\mathrm{n}$ portions with fish. It is known that a passenger prefers chicken with a probability of 0.5 and fish with a probability of 0.5. We will call a passenger dissatisfied if they are left with the meal they do not prefer.

a) (8-11 grades, 2 points) Find the most probable number of dissatisfied passengers.

b) (9-11 grades, 3 points) Find the expected value of the number of dissatisfied passengers.

c) (9-11 grades, 4 points) Find the variance of the number of dissatisfied passengers.

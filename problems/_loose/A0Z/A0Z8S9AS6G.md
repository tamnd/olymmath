Positive numbers $a_1,a_2,...,a_n, b_1, b_2,...,b_n$ satisfy the condition $a_1+a_2+...+a_n=b_1+ b_2+...+b_n=1$.
Find the smallest possible value of the sum $$\frac{a_1^2}{a_1+b_1}+\frac{a_2^2}{a_2+b_2}+...+\frac{a_n^2}{a_n+b_n}$$

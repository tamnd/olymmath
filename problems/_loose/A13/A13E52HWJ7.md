Given the function
$$
f(x)=-x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c (a, b, c \in \mathbf{R})
$$

is monotonically decreasing on the interval $(-\infty, 0)$, monotonically increasing on the interval $(0,1)$, and $f(x)$ has three zeros on $\mathbf{R}$, with 1 being one of them.
(1) Find the range of $f(2)$;
(2) Discuss the number of common points between the line $l: y=x-1$ and the curve $C$: $y=f(x)$.

Given an acute triangle with unequal sides $A C, B C$ and the orthocenter $V$. On the line $A B$, construct points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}\left(A^{\prime} \neq A, B^{\prime} \neq B\right)$ such that $\left|C A^{\prime}\right|=|C A|$ and $\left|C B^{\prime}\right|=|C B|$. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A C B^{\prime}$ and $V C A^{\prime}$ are congruent.

(Jaroslav Švrček)

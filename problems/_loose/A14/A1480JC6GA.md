A volleyball tournament involved six teams, each playing against each other exactly once. In each of the five rounds, three matches took place simultaneously on three courts 1, 2, and 3. How many possible schedules were there for such a tournament? By a schedule, we mean a $5 \times 3$ table, where at position $(i, j)$, where $i \in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and $j \in\{1,2,3\}$, is listed a pair of teams (without specifying the order) that played against each other in the $i$-th round on court number $j$. Instead of the decimal representation of the resulting number, you can provide its prime factorization.

In a classroom, there are $p$ rows and $q$ columns of seats, totaling $p q$ seats $(p>1, q>1)$. Each seat is occupied by a student, all of different heights. The teacher selects the shortest student from each row, and the tallest among these is of height $a$. Then, the teacher selects the tallest student from each column, and the shortest among these is of height $b$. It needs to be determined which of the $ab$ cases are possible, and whether every possible case can be guaranteed by changing the seating arrangement.

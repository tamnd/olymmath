$ (a)$ The rectangle $ PQRS$ with $ PQ=l$ and $ QR=m$ $ (l,m \in \mathbb{N})$ is divided into $ lm$ unit squares. Prove that the diagonal $ PR$ intersects exactly $ l+m-d$ of these squares, where $ d=(l,m)$ .
$ (b)$ A box with edge lengths $ l,m,n \in \mathbb{N}$ is divided into $ lmn$ unit cubes. How many of the cubes does a main diagonal of the box intersect?

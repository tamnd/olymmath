Let $P$ be a point (different from $A$ and $B$) on a circle with diameter $AB$ and center $O$. Let the circumcenter of triangles $POA$ and $POB$ be $K_{a}$ and $K_{b}$, respectively. Prove that $O, K_{a}, P$, and $K_{b}$ lie on a circle, and that the intersection of $AK_{a}$ and $BK_{b}$ lies on the original circle.

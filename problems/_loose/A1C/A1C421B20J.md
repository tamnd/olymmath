We colored four points with four colors: red, blue, green, and yellow. Then we colored the segments determined by these points such that the color of each segment matched one of its endpoints, and in the process, we used all four colors again. Show that there is always a point such that

a) either among the segments emanating from it,

b) or among the segments connecting the other three points, one is red, another is blue, and the third is green.

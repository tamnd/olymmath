For a $1 \times n$ rectangular grid, label the small squares from left to right as $1, 2, \cdots, n$. Now, color the small squares using three colors 1, 2, 3 such that the even-numbered squares can be colored with any color, and the odd-numbered squares can only be colored with color $1$ or $3$, and adjacent squares must be of different colors. How many ways are there to color this rectangle?
(43rd Austrian Mathematical Competition)

Let the length, width, and height of a rectangular prism be $a, b, c(a>b>c)$, $p$ be the sum of the lengths of all edges, $S$ be the surface area, and $d$ be the length of a diagonal. Prove: $a>\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{p}{4}+\sqrt{d^{2}-\frac{1}{2} S}\right), c<\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{p}{4}-\sqrt{d^{2}-\frac{1}{2} S}\right)$.

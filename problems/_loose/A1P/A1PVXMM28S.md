Prove that for any positive integer $n$, if $\alpha=\frac{180^{\circ}}{1+2^{n}}$, then

$$
(\cos \alpha) \cdot(\cos 2 \alpha) \cdot(\cos 4 \alpha) \cdots \cdots\left(\cos 2^{n-1} \alpha\right)=\frac{1}{2^{n}}
$$

On a circular railway, there are $n$ stations. Sometimes the station masters communicate with each other by radio. At any given time, only two people are in a communication session. Over the course of a day, exactly one radio session occurred between every two stations. For each station (considering only its sessions), it turned out that it communicated with other stations in turn according to their order on the railway (clockwise or counterclockwise, these directions can be different for different stations), starting from one of the neighboring stations and ending with the other. What can $n$ be?

Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ in the plane. Prove: there exist four distinct points $P 、 Q 、 R 、 S$ on a line and a square $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$, such that points $P 、 Q 、 R 、 S$ lie on the lines $A B$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} 、 B C$ and $B^{\prime} C^{\prime} 、 C D$ and $C^{\prime} D^{\prime} 、 D A$ and $D^{\prime} A^{\prime}$, respectively.

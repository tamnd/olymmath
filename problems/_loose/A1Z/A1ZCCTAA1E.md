A square with side $2a$ is cut into four isosceles triangles, whose bases are the sides of the given square and whose heights are equal to $x$. The remaining deck of the square represents the net of a regular quadrilateral pyramid.

a) Express the volume $V$ of this pyramid as a function of the parameter $x$.

b) Determine $x$ so that the volume $V$ is maximized.

c) Sketch the graph of the function $V(x)$.

Let $a$ and $b$ be the lengths of the legs, and $c$ the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle. Prove that the inequality

$$
a^{4}+a^{2} b^{2}+b^{4} \geq \frac{3}{4} c^{4}
$$

holds.

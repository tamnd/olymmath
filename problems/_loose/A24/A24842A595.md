With the number 123456789101112... 9989991000, the following operation is performed: two adjacent digits $a$ and $b$ ($a$ stands before $b$) are crossed out and the number $a+2b$ is inserted in their place (you can take zero, "standing" before the number, as $a$, and the first digit of the number as $b$). The same operation is performed on the resulting number, and so on. (For example, from the number 118307, on the first step, you can get the numbers 218307, 38307, 117307, 111407, 11837, 118314.) Prove that in this way it is possible to obtain the number 1.

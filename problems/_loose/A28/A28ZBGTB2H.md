For every triangle $ABC$, the following notations are introduced:

The line through $A$ and $B$ is $u$, the line through $B$ and $C$ is $v$, the line through $C$ and $A$ is $w$. When $u$ is reflected across $v$, it becomes the line $p$. When $u$ is reflected across $w$, it becomes the line $q$.

As usual, the measures of the interior angles $\angle BAC$ and $\angle ABC$ are denoted by $\alpha$ and $\beta$, respectively. For the following tasks, it is always assumed that $\alpha = 55^\circ$.

a) At what angle do the lines $p$ and $q$ intersect if $\beta = 75^\circ$?

b) How large must $\beta$ be for $p$ and $q$ to be perpendicular to each other?

c) Give a value of $\beta$ such that $p$ and $q$ can be shown to be parallel to each other!

d), e) Create a drawing in which $p$ and $q$ intersect at a point on the same side of the line $u$ as $C$; choose the triangle $ABC$

d) with an acute interior angle at $B$.

e) with an obtuse interior angle at $B$.

(For d) and e), no justification is required.)

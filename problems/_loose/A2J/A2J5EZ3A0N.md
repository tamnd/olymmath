In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, the edges $B D$ and $C D$ are mutually perpendicular, and the projection of the vertex $D$ to the plane $A B C$ is the intersection of the altitudes of the triangle $A B C$. Prove that
$$
(A B+B C+C A)^{2} \leq 6\left(D A^{2}+D B^{2}+D C^{2}\right) .
$$

For which tetrahedra does equality hold?

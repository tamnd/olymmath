A certain country has $2 k+1$ airlines, where the $i$-th company owns exactly $i$ routes, $i=1,2, \cdots, 2 k+1$, and each route connects two cities. There is a law in the country stipulating that each company can only own 1 route in each city, and the route settings of all companies comply with this law. Now these companies decide to readjust the ownership of the routes so that each company owns the same number of routes. Prove that their decision can be realized and still comply with the law.

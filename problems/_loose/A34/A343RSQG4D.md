Three. (50 points) Consider the following game: Initially, there are $k$ piles of stones $\left(k \in \mathbf{N}^{`}\right)$. The number of stones in the $i$-th pile is $n_{1}$. Two players take turns to perform the operation of taking stones: each time, they are allowed to select a pile of stones, take several stones from it (at least 1, up to all the stones in that pile), and then, distribute the remaining stones in that pile to the other piles of stones at will (if a pile of stones is completely taken, no stones can be added back to that pile). The one who takes the last stone wins. Try to determine: For which initial states $\left(n_{2}, n_{2}, \cdots, n_{k}\right)$, does the first player have a winning strategy?

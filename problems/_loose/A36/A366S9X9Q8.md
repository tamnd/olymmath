Given is an $n \times n$ board, colored like a chessboard, with the top left square being black. In each step, Azra selects six squares of the board that form a $2 \times 3$ or $3 \times 2$ rectangle and contain exactly three white squares, and then she colors those three white squares black. For which $n$ can Azra achieve that all squares are black?

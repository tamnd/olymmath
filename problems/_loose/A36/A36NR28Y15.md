Let

$$
f(r)=\frac{a^{\frac{1}{2}+r}+b^{\frac{1}{2}+r}}{a^{\frac{1}{2}-r}+b^{\frac{1}{2}-r}}
$$

Prove that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \text { a) } f(r) \leq(a b)^{r}, \text { if } r>0
\end{aligned}
$$

(For the latter problem, see the article "On Weighted Arithmetic Means" in the previous issue of our journal.)

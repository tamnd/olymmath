Let $n$ be an even integer $>3$. Mona and Alexandre are playing a game. Each in turn, they write a 0 or a 1 on a vertex of an initially blank $n$-gon. Mona starts and wins if, after one of her moves, there are three consecutive vertices of the $n$-gon such that the sum of the numbers written on them is a multiple of 3. Alexandre wins if he prevents Mona from
winning. Determine who has a winning strategy.

Two identical gears $A$ and $B$, $B$ is placed flat on a horizontal surface, and $A$ is placed on top of $B$ so that they are perfectly aligned, thus their projections on the horizontal surface are completely overlapping. Then, any four pairs of overlapping teeth are removed. If $A$ and $B$ each originally had 14 teeth, can gear $A$ be rotated around the common axis of the two gears to a suitable position so that the projections of the two gears on the horizontal surface form a complete gear projection? What if $A$ and $B$ each originally had 13 teeth? How would it be then? Please prove your argument.
(1990, National Mathematical Winter Camp Selection Exam)

In a mathematics competition, 300 participants take part. After the competition, some participants play chess. Each pair of participants plays against each other at most once. There are no three participants in this competition who all play against each other. Determine the maximum $n$ for which it is possible to satisfy the following conditions simultaneously: each participant plays at most $n$ chess games, and for every $m$ with $1 \leq m \leq n$ there is a participant who plays exactly $m$ chess games.

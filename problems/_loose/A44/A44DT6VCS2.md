Let's place a plane through the $G$ vertex of the $A B C D E F G H$ parallelepiped such that, apart from $G$, it has no common points with the parallelepiped. Let the intersection points of the plane with the lines of the edges $A B, A D, A E$ starting from the vertex $A$ opposite to $G$ be $U, V, W$, respectively, and let $A B=a, A D=b, A E=c, A U=u, A V=v, A W=w$. Prove that

$$
u v w=a v w+b u w+c u v
$$

How does the statement change if the plane goes through $G$, but also considers situations that were not allowed above?

(SRP, 80). Prove that for any vectors $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$ the following equality holds:

$$
\sum\left(\varepsilon_{1} a_{1}+\varepsilon_{2} a_{2}+\varepsilon_{3} a_{3}\right)^{2}=8 \sum_{k=1}^{3} a_{k}^{2}
$$

where the sum on the left is taken over all 8 different sets of numbers $\varepsilon_{1}, \varepsilon_{2}, \varepsilon_{3} \in\{-1 ; 1\}$. Generalize this statement for any number $n \in \mathbf{N}$ of vectors $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$.

We will prove that the

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x_{1}=2, y_{1}=3 \\
& x_{k+1}=3 x_{k}+2 y_{k}, y_{k+1}=4 x_{k}+3 y_{k}, \text { if } k=1,2,3, \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

sequences of pairs $(x_{n}, y_{n})$ are solutions to the equation $2 x^{2}+1=y^{2}$. (See the note attached to the solution of the 2nd problem of the final round of the 1958 National Mathematical Competition in the 3-4 issue of Volume XVII, November 1958.)

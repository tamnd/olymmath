In the elections to the City Duma, each voter, if they come to the polls, votes for themselves (if they are a candidate) and for the candidates who are their friends. A forecast by the municipal sociological service is considered good if it correctly predicts the number of votes cast for at least one of the candidates, and bad otherwise. Prove that for any forecast, the voters can come to the polls in such a way that this forecast will be bad.

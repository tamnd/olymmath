Prove that if the diameter of a semicircle is divided into two arbitrary parts and on each of them a semicircle is constructed (inside the given semicircle), then the area enclosed between the three semicircles is equal to the area of a circle whose diameter is the length of the perpendicular from the point of division to the circumference of the semicircle.

The eighty-fourth question: In quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $AD$ and $BC$ respectively, such that $\frac{AB}{BD}=\frac{BF}{FC}$. Extend $\mathrm{CD}$ and $\mathrm{EF}$ to intersect at point $\mathrm{T}$, and extend $\mathrm{BA}$ and $\mathrm{EF}$ to intersect at point $\mathrm{S}$. Construct the circumcircle $\odot 0$ of $\triangle \mathrm{AES}$, the circumcircle $\odot \mathrm{P}$ of $\triangle \mathrm{BFS}$, the circumcircle $\odot \mathrm{Q}$ of $\triangle \mathrm{CFT}$, and the circumcircle $\odot \mathrm{R}$ of $\triangle \mathrm{DET}$. Prove that $\odot \mathrm{O}, \odot \mathrm{P}, \odot \mathrm{Q}, \odot \mathrm{R}$ are concurrent, and that quadrilateral $\mathrm{OPQR} \sim$ quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$.

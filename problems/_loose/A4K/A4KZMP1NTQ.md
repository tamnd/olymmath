Let $x_1 \dots, x_{42}$ , be real numbers such that $5x_{i+1}-x_i-3x_ix_{i+1}=1$ for each $1 \le i \le 42$ , with $x_1=x_{43}$ . Find all the product of all possible values for $x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_{42}$ .

A certain ruler invited 28 knights to celebrate his birthday. Each knight had exactly three enemies among the others.

a) Show that the ruler can seat the knights at two tables so that each knight sits at the same table with at most one enemy.

b) Show that in the case of any such seating arrangement, no more than 16 knights sit at each table.

(Enmity is a mutual relationship: If A is an enemy of B, then B is also an enemy of A.)

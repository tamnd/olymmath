Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle $\omega$ . Distinct points $X,Y$ lie on the rays $DB,CA$ such that $DA=DX, CB=CY$ . Lines $AX,BY$ intersect the side $CD$ at points $P,Q$ respectively. Prove that the radical axis of the two circumcircles of triangles $APC,BQD$ and the perpendicular bisector of side $AB$ intersect at a point on $\omega$ .

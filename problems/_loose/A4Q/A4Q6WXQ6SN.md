For $0<s \leqslant t \leqslant n$, consider bipartite graphs with two parts of order $n$ that do not contain $K_{s, t}$, and let $z(n, s, t)$ denote the maximum number of edges in such graphs. Prove: $2 \operatorname{ex}\left(n, K_{s, t}\right) \leqslant z(n, s, t) \leqslant \operatorname{ex}\left(2 n, K_{s, t}\right)$.

Through a point $P$ located inside the triangle $ABC$, three rays are drawn: ray $p_{1}$, parallel to $AB$, intersecting $BC$ at point $A_{1}$, ray $p_{2}$, parallel to $BC$, intersecting $CA$ at point $B_{1}$, ray $p_{3}$, parallel to $CA$, intersecting $AB$ at point $C_{1}$. Prove that the following relationship holds between the segments formed:

$$
\frac{P A_{1}}{A B}+\frac{P B_{1}}{B C}+\frac{P C_{1}}{C A}=1
$$

Under what additional conditions does the specified relationship remain valid for all points on the plane?

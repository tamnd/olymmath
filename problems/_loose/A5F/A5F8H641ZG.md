Let $d$ and $d^{\prime}$ be two parallel lines. A circle $\Gamma$ is tangent to $d$ at $A$ and intersects $d^{\prime}$ at $C$ and $D$. The tangent to $\Gamma$ at $D$ intersects $d$ at $B$. Finally, let $E$ be the intersection of $(B C)$ with $\Gamma$ and $I$ the intersection of $(D E)$ with $d$. Show that $I$ is the midpoint of $[A B]$.

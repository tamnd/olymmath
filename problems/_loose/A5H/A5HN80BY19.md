Inside a triangle $ABC$ three circles with radius $1$ are drawn. (Circles can be tangent to each other and to the sides of the triangle, but can not have any common internal points.)
Find the biggest value of $r$ for which one can state that he can always draw a fourth circle inside the triangle of radius $r$ , which does not intersect three already drawn circles.

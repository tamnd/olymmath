On the border of a circular glade, points $A, B, C, D$ are marked clockwise. At point $A$ is a squirrel named An, at point $B$ is a squirrel named Bim, at point $C$ stands a pine tree, and at point $D$ stands an oak tree. The squirrels started running simultaneously, An towards the pine tree, and Bim towards the oak tree. They collided at point $M$, which is closer to the pine tree than to the oak tree. Is it true that if An had run from point $A$ to the oak tree, and Bim from point $B$ to the pine tree, An would have arrived first? Each squirrel runs in a straight line and at a constant speed.

Let's say a natural number is speckled if each digit in its decimal representation is different and all sums of three consecutive digits of the given number take on exactly two different values. (For example, the number 162735 is not speckled because the sums of the considered triplets $1+6+2=9, 6+2+7=15, 2+7+3=12$, and $7+3+5=15$ take on three different values.)

a) Provide an example of a six-digit speckled number.

b) Does a seven-digit speckled number exist?

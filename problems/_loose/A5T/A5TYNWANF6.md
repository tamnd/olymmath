In tetrahedron $ABCD$, $AB=m, CD=n, AD=p, BC=q, AC=u, BD=v$. If the angle between $AB$ and $CD$ is $\theta$, then $\left.\cos \theta=1 \frac{\left(p^{2}+q^{2}\right)-\left(u^{2}+v^{2}\right)}{2 m n} \right\rvert\,$.

Example 2 If the six edge lengths of a tetrahedron are $a, b, c, d, e, f$, and the volume is $V$, then $a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3}+d^{3}+e^{3}+f^{3} \geqslant 36 \sqrt{2} V$ (a three-dimensional extension of Weisenbock's inequality).

Example 4 In tetrahedron $ABCD$, if $AB \perp CD, AC \perp BD$, then $AD \perp BC$.
(1957 Tianjin, 1979 Shanghai High School Competition Problem).

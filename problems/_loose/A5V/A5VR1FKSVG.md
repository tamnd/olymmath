Two circles with centers $P$ and $Q$ intersect at points $M$ and $N$, the distance between which is 4. Lines $P M$ and $Q N$ intersect the first circle at points $A$ and $B$, and the second circle at points $D$ and $C$ respectively. Point $M$ lies between $A$ and $D$, and point $N$ lies between $B$ and $C$. It is known that $A B = C D$, and the area of quadrilateral $A B C D$ is $24 \sqrt{3}$. Find the distance between the centers of the circles.

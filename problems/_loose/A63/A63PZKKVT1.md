Let $G=(V, E)$ be a graph with $|V| \geqslant 3$.

Show that $G$ is connected if and only if there exist two distinct vertices $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ such that the graphs $G_{1}^{\prime}$ and $G_{2}^{\prime}$ obtained by removing $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ respectively are connected.

Let $R(x)=P(x) / Q(x)$, where $P$ and $Q$ are coprime polynomials. Prove that $R(x)$ can be represented as

$$
R(x)=A(x)+\sum_{i, k} \frac{c_{i k}}{\left(x-a_{i}\right)^{k}}
$$

where $c_{i k}$ are some numbers, and $A(x)$ is some polynomial.

An army of mice attacked a grain warehouse. To fight the mice, one cat was released into the warehouse on the first day, a second cat on the next day, and each subsequent day, one more cat was released. A mouse eats 100 grams of grain per day, and a cat eats 10 mice per day. After several days, there were no mice left. After calculating the losses, it was found that the mice had eaten 55 kg of grain over these few days. How many mice were there? (A mouse manages to eat 100 grams of grain even on the day it is caught by a cat.)

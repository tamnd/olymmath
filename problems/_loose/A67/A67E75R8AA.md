Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $P, Q$ be the points of intersection of the extensions of opposite sides $A B$ and $C D, A D$ and $B C$ respectively, $R$ be an arbitrary point inside the quadrilateral. Let $K$ be the point of intersection of lines $B C$ and $P R, L$ be the point of intersection of lines $A B$ and $Q R, M$ be the point of intersection of lines $A K$ and $D R$. Prove that points $L, M$ and $C$ lie on the same line.

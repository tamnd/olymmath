$$f(x) =\begin{cases} 1 & \text{if} \ x=1 \\ e^{(x^{10}-1)}+(x-1)^2\sin\frac1{x-1} & \text{if} \ x\neq 1\end{cases}$$

(a) Find $f'(1)$

(b) Evaluate $ \lim_{u\to\infty} \left[100u-u\sum_{k=1}^{100} f\left(1+\frac{k}{u}\right)\right]$.

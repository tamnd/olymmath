Given a non-equilateral triangle $A B C$. Points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are chosen such that triangles $B A_{1} C$, $C B_{1} A$, and $A C_{1} B$ are similar. Prove that triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is equilateral if and only if the specified similar triangles are isosceles triangles with an angle of $120^{\circ}$ at vertices $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$.

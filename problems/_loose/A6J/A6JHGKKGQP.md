Two unknown numbers $a$ and $b$ are given such that the number $a$ is three times smaller than the number $b$. If we subtract 3 from the number $a$, multiply the resulting difference by 5, add 15 to the product, and divide the sum by 20, we get the number $x$. If we divide the number $b$ by 8, add 6 to the quotient, multiply the sum by 2, and subtract 22 from the product, we get the number $y$. What are the numbers $a$ and $b$ if $x$ is two times smaller than $y$?

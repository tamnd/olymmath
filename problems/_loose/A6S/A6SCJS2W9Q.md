Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ all be positive numbers, and for any $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$, we have $a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{k} \geqslant 1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{1+a_{1}}+\frac{2}{\left(1+a_{1}\right)\left(1+a_{2}\right)}+\cdots+\frac{n}{\left(1+a_{1}\right) \cdots\left(1+a_{n}\right)}<2 .
$$

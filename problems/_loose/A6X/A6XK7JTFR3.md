Given $n$ distinct positive numbers $a_{1}$, $a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$. From them, all possible sums with any number of addends (from 1 to $n$) are formed. Prove that among these sums, there will be at least $n(n+1) / 2$ pairwise distinct sums.

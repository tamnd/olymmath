Let $S=\{1,2,3, \ldots, n\}$ and let $T_{k}=\left\{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k} \mid x_{i} \in S, x_{i} \neq x_{j}\right.$ for $\left.i \neq j\right\}$.

If $t_{k}$ is the number of elements in the set $T_{k}$, prove that

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} t_{k}=\frac{n}{6}\left(n^{2}+5\right)
$$

A country has the shape of a square and is divided into 25 identical square counties. In each county, either a knight-count, who always tells the truth, or a liar-count, who always lies, rules. One day, each count said: "Among my neighbors, there are an equal number of knights and liars." What is the maximum number of knights that could have been? (Counts are neighbors if their counties share a common side.)

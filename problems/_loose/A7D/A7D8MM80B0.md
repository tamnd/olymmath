Given a positive integer $n$, fill each cell of an $n \times n$ grid with an integer. Perform the following operation on this grid: select a cell, and add 1 to each of the $2n-1$ cells in the selected cell's row and column. Find the maximum integer $N=N(n)$, such that no matter what the initial numbers in the grid are, it is always possible to perform a series of these operations to ensure that the grid contains at least $N$ even numbers.

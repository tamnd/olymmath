Among the $n$-digit positive integers formed by $0,1,2,3,4,5$, the number of those in which the digits 1 and 2 are not adjacent is $a_{n}$.
(1) Prove: For all $n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}, 41 a_{n}^{2}+(-4)^{n+2}$ is a perfect square;
(2) Prove: There exist two sequences of positive integers $u_{n}$ and $v_{n}$ such that $x_{n}=\frac{4\left[u_{n} v_{n}-(-4)^{n}\right]}{5\left(u_{n}+v_{n}\right)}(n \in$ $\mathbf{N}_{+}$).

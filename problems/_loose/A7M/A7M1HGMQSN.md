Let $A, B, C$ and $D$ be four points. We denote $\left(\ell_{1}\right)=(A B),\left(\ell_{1}^{\prime}\right)=(C D),\left(\ell_{2}\right)=(B C),\left(\ell_{2}^{\prime}\right)=(D A)$, $\left(\ell_{3}\right)=(A C)$ and $\left(\ell_{3}^{\prime}\right)=(B D)$. We denote $M_{3}$ as the Miquel point of $\left(\ell_{1}\right),\left(\ell_{1}^{\prime}\right),\left(\ell_{2}\right)$ and $\left(\ell_{2}^{\prime}\right)$, and define $M_{2}$ and $M_{1}$ in the same manner. We then denote $X_{3}$ as the intersection of the lines $\left(\ell_{3}\right)$ and $\left(\ell_{3}^{\prime}\right)$, and define $X_{2}$ and $X_{1}$ in the same manner. For all $i$, we denote $\left(d_{i}\right)$ as the line $\left(M_{i} X_{i}\right)$.

Show that $\left(d_{1}\right),\left(d_{2}\right)$ and $\left(d_{3}\right)$ are concurrent.

Let there be a sequence of $m+n$ balls, where $m$ and $n$ are coprime numbers. The first $m$ balls in this sequence are moved in the same order after the remaining $n$ balls, and then the process is repeated with the resulting sequence. Prove that after several steps, the first ball can be brought to any (previously determined) position in the sequence.

In a certain country, between every two cities, there is either a direct bus connection or a direct railway connection (all connections are bidirectional and do not pass through any other city).

Prove that the cities in this country can be divided into two disjoint sets such that all cities in one set can be visited by traveling only by railway without visiting any city twice, and all cities in the other set can be visited by traveling only by bus without visiting any city twice.

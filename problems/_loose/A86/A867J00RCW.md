For a tetrahedron $ABCD$, it is assumed that the center $M$ of the circumsphere of the tetrahedron lies inside the tetrahedron.

The line connecting $M$ to each of the tetrahedron's vertices $A, B, C$ and $D$ intersects the opposite face of the tetrahedron at $A', B', C'$ and $D'$, respectively.

The radius of the circumsphere is $r$.

Prove that under these assumptions, it always follows that:

$$
A A^{\prime}+B B^{\prime}+C C^{\prime}+D D^{\prime} \geq \frac{16}{3} r
$$

Given
$$
f(x)=x \ln x-a x, g(x)=-x^{2}-2 \text {. }
$$
(1) For all $x \in(0,+\infty)$, $f(x) \geqslant g(x)$, find the range of the real number $a$;
(2) When $a=-1$, find the maximum and minimum values of the function $f(x)$ on the interval $[m, m+3](m>0)$;
(3) Prove that for all $x \in(0,+\infty)$,
$$
\ln x+1>\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^{x}}-\frac{2}{\mathrm{e} x} .
$$

Given are the four periodic decimal fractions

$$
p=0, \overline{3456} \ldots, q=0,3 \overline{456} \ldots, r=0,34 \overline{56} \ldots, s=0,345 \overline{6}
$$

a) Determine all natural numbers $n$ for which the following statement holds: In the $n$-th decimal place, all four decimal fractions $p, q, r, s$ have the same digit.

b) Determine all natural numbers $m$ for which the following statement holds: In the $m$-th decimal place, no two of the four decimal fractions $p, q, r, s$ have the same digit.

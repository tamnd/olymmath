Let $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ be on the sides $A C$ and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$, and let $P$ be the intersection point of lines $B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$. Prove that if

$$
\frac{A C_{1}}{A B}=\frac{C B_{1}}{B_{1} A}
$$

then

$$
\frac{P B_{1}}{B B_{1}}=\left(\frac{P C}{C C_{1}}\right)^{2}
$$

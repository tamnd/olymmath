The $1004$ numbers written on the board: $2,4,6, ..., 2008$ .
Two players alternately choose any number written on the board, erase it, and write in its place a number one less than it. Moreover, if a zero appears on the board, it is erased immediately, and if two identical numbers appear, it is erased by one of them. The player whose opponent erases the last number wins.
Prove that the player who starts the game can always win, no matter how the second player plays. How must the starting player play?

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}$ be natural numbers and let for every natural $n \geqq 2$ the number $a_{n+1}$ be 1 greater than the greatest odd divisor of the sum $a_{n}+a_{n-1}$. Prove that the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ is periodic from a certain term onwards. Find all such sequences that are periodic from the first term.

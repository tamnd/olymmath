Anne drove her car for an integer (and non-zero) number of hours, and traveled an integer number of kilometers, at a speed of $55 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. At the beginning of the trip, the odometer read abc kilometers, where abc is a 3-digit number such that $a \geqslant 1$ and $a+b+c \leqslant 7$. At the end of the trip, the odometer read cba kilometers. Determine all possible values of the number abc.

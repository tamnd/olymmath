In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the necessary and sufficient condition for the line connecting a vertex to the centroid to be perpendicular to the line connecting the incenter to the circumcenter is that the reciprocals of the three sides of the triangle form an arithmetic sequence (the reciprocal of the side opposite the vertex is the arithmetic mean).

Let $A, B, C, D$ be four points on a straight line in that order. Construct semicircles over the segments $AB, AD, CB$, and $CD$ as diameters, with the semicircle over $CB$ on the opposite side of the other three. Let the midpoints of these semicircular arcs be $E, F, G, H$. Show that the ratio of the area of the quadrilateral $EFHG$ to the area of the region enclosed by the four semicircular arcs is independent of the lengths of the segments $AB$, $BC$, and $CD$.

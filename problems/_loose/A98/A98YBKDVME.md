(Ticket Selling Problem) Each football ticket costs 50 yuan, and the ticket office has no change. There are $m$ people each holding a 50-yuan note, and another $n$ people each holding a 100-yuan note $(m \geqslant n)$ queuing to buy tickets, with each person buying only one ticket. If no one has to wait for change, it is called a smooth ticket purchase. Find the probability of a smooth ticket purchase.

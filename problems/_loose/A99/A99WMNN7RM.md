Two circles are given with a perimeter of 1979. On the first circle, 1979 points are marked, and on the second circle, several arcs are marked such that the sum of the lengths of all these arcs is less than 1. Prove that the first circle can be placed on the second circle so that none of the marked points fall within a marked arc.

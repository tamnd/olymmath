Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer. Find the largest integer $k$, depending on $n$, such that there exists a subset $S$ of $\{1, \ldots, 2 n-1\}$ having $k$ elements and such that if $a, b$ and $c$ are three elements of $S$ for which $\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}=\mathrm{c}$, the equality $\mathrm{a}=\mathrm{b}$ is necessarily satisfied.

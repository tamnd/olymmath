Set $M=\left\{(x, y) \mid x^{3}+8 y^{3}+6 x y \geqslant 1, x, y \in R\right\}$, $D=\left\{(x, y) \mid x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant t^{2}, t \in R, t \neq 0\right\}, D \cap M=\varnothing$, then
(A) $-1<t<1$.
(B) $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}<t<\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$.
(C) $-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}<t<\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$.
(D) $-\frac{1}{2}<t<\frac{1}{2}$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

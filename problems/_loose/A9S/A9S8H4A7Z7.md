Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral, $O$ the intersection point of the diagonals, $M$ a point on segment $A B$, and $N$ a point on segment $C D$. Show that $O, M, N$ are collinear if and only if

$$
A M \cdot D N \cdot O B \cdot O C = B M \cdot C N \cdot O A \cdot O D
$$

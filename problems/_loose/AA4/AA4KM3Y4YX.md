If the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$ is $M$, the circumcenter of the triangle is $O$, and the feet of the perpendiculars from $O$ to the sides $A B, A C$, and $C B$ of the triangle are $D, E$, and $F$, then $O D=\frac{1}{2} M C, O E=M B$ and $O F=$ $\frac{1}{2} M A$.

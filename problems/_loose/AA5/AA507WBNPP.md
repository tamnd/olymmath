We will prove that the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
x-y+2 z & =0 \\
-2 x+y-2 z & =-2 \\
2 x+c y+3 z & =1
\end{aligned}
$$

has a solution that does not depend on the value of the parameter $c$.

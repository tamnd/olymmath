In the interval $[1,1000]$, take $\mathrm{n}$ different numbers $\mathrm{a}_{1}, \mathrm{a}_{2}, \ldots, \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$. There always exist two numbers $\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{i}},$ $a_{j}$, such that $0<\left|a_{i}-a_{j}\right|<1+3 \sqrt[3]{a_{i} a_{j}}$. Find the minimum possible value of $n$.

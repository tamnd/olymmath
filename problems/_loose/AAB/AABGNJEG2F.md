If $k$ is an integer, we denote $p(k)$ the number of its even divisors, and $i(k)$ the number of its odd divisors. Show that for any integer $n$, the sum of the $p(k)$, where $k$ is less than $n$, differs by at most $n$ from the sum of the $i(k)$.

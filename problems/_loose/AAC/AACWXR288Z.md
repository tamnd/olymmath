Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}, x_{5}$ be real numbers whose sum of squares is 1. In the expression $\pm x_{1} \pm x_{2} \pm x_{3} \pm x_{4} \pm x_{5}$, we choose the signs in all possible ways and perform the indicated operations. Prove that the sum of the absolute values of the 32 numbers thus obtained is at most 32.

Prove that in an integer triangle the following statements are equivalent:

a) the sine of one of the angles is a rational number;

b) the sines of all angles are rational numbers;

c) the area of the triangle is a rational number;

d) the radius of the inscribed circle is a rational number;

e) the radius of the circumscribed circle is a rational number;

f) one of the altitudes is a rational number;

g) all altitudes are rational numbers;

h) the tangent of one of the half-angles (half of one of the angles in the triangle) is a rational number;

i) the tangents of all half-angles are rational numbers.

We know that the terms of the infinite sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$ are positive, $a_{0}=1$, and for any natural number $n$, $a_{n+1}=a_{n-1}-a_{n}$ holds.

Prove that the terms of this sequence are uniquely determined.

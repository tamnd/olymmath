(Jury, GDR, 82). Let $M$ be the set of functions $f: \mathbf{Z} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$, satisfying the condition $f(0) \neq 0$ and the identity

$$
f(n) f(m) \equiv f(n+m) + f(n-m), n, m \in \mathbf{Z}
$$

Find: a) all functions $f(n) \in M$, for which $f(1)=5 / 2$; b) all functions $f(n) \in M$, for which $f(1)=\sqrt{\overline{3}}$.

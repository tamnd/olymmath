The denominator of the geometric progression $b_{n}$ is $q$, and for some natural $n \geq 2$

$$
\log _{4} b_{2}+\log _{4} b_{3}+\ldots+\log _{4} b_{n}=4 \cdot \log _{4} b_{1}
$$

Find the smallest possible value of $\log _{q} b_{1}^{2}$, given that it is an integer. For which $n$ is this value achieved?

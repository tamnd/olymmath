Given the non-isosceles acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with incenter $I$, the midpoints of sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $A'$, $B'$, and $C'$, respectively. The excircles opposite to $\angle A$, $\angle B$, and $\angle C$ are $\odot I_{1}$, $\odot I_{2}$, and $\odot I_{3}$, respectively. The circles symmetric to $\odot I_{1}$, $\odot I_{2}$, and $\odot I_{3}$ with respect to points $A'$, $B'$, and $C'$ are $\odot I_{1}'$, $\odot I_{2}'$, and $\odot I_{3}'$, respectively. The radical center of $\odot I_{1}$, $\odot I_{2}$, and $\odot I_{3}$ is $P$, and the radical center of $\odot I_{1}'$, $\odot I_{2}'$, and $\odot I_{3}'$ is $Q$. Prove that $P$ is the midpoint of segment $IQ$.

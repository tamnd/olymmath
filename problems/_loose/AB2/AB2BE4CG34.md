In the sequence $f_{1}, f_{2}, \ldots$ the first two terms are 1, and each of the subsequent terms is the sum of the two preceding ones. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{f_{1} f_{3}}+\frac{1}{f_{2} f_{4}}+\frac{1}{f_{3} f_{5}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{f_{98} f_{100}}<1
$$

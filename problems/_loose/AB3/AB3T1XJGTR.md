Suppose that $ x_1, x_2, x_3, \ldots$ are positive real numbers for which $$ x^n_n = \sum^{n-1}_{j=0} x^j_n$$ for $ n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$ Prove that $ \forall n,$ $$ 2 - \frac{1}{2^{n-1}} \leq x_n < 2 - \frac{1}{2^n}.$$

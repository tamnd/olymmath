Birgit has ten candles. Each candle has a burning duration of three hours. At a certain moment, she lights one of those candles. From that moment on, she wants to burn the ten candles under the following conditions:
1) she only lights the next candle after a whole number of hours;
2) there must always be at least one candle burning;
3) no candle is blown out;
4) if a certain candle or combination of candles is burning during a period between two whole hours, then in the next period between two whole hours, not exactly the same candle or combination of candles may burn.

How many hours will it take under these conditions for all ten candles to be completely burned out?
A) 12
B) 15
C) 16
D) 17
E) 18

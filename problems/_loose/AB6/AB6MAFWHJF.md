The cells of an $N \times N$ board are painted in red, blue, and green, such that next to any red cell there is a blue one, next to any blue one there is a green one, and next to any green one there is a red cell (i.e., the corresponding cells share a side). Prove that for the number of red cells $k$ the following inequalities hold:

a) $k \leqslant 2 N^{2} / 3$

b) $k \geqslant N^{2} / 11$.

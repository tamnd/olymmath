A square is inscribed in an equilateral triangle, with each vertex of the square on the perimeter of the triangle. One side of the square cuts off a smaller equilateral triangle; into this, we again inscribe a square in the same manner, and so on. What fraction of the triangle is covered by the infinite sequence of squares?

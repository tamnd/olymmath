We take some points inside a triangle such that no three of them (including the vertices) lie on the same straight line. We connect the points to each other and to the vertices of the triangle in such a way that the resulting segments do not intersect each other, and the triangle is divided into the maximum number of small triangles. Prove that the number of small triangles formed is odd.

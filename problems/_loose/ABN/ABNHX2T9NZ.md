Given that the graph of the quadratic function $y=f_{1}(x)$ has the origin as its vertex and passes through the point $(1,1)$, and the graph of the inverse proportion function $y=f_{2}(x)$ intersects the line $y=x$ at two points with a distance of $8$, $f(x)=f_{1}(x)+f_{2}(x)$
(1) Find the expression for the function $f(x)$;
(2) Prove that when $a>3$, the equation $f(x)=f(a)$ has three real solutions.

There is a cube with a side of 1 m and a set of five paints. First, Petya cuts the cube into equal smaller cubes of size no more than 1 cm (he chooses the size himself). Then Vasya paints the cubes as he wishes (not necessarily all the same), but so that each face is monochromatic. Finally, Petya assembles the cube using all the smaller cubes. Prove that regardless of Vasya's actions, Petya can achieve that each face of the large cube is monochromatic.

Given a triangle $ABC$, in which $D, E$ are the midpoints of its sides $BC, AB$, respectively. Let $F$ be the midpoint of segment $BE$ and $G$ an interior point of side $AC$, such that $|AG|=3|CG|$. Prove that the intersection of lines $DF$ and $GE$ lies on the parallel to line $BC$ passing through point $A$.

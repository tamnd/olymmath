In a house, there are 300 apartments. In apartments whose numbers are divisible by 5, cats live, and in the rest of the apartments, there are no cats. If the sum of the digits of the apartment number is divisible by 5, then a dog definitely lives in such an apartment, and in the rest of the apartments, there are no dogs. How many apartments have both a cat and a dog living in them?

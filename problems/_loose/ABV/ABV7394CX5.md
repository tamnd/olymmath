Given the parabola $y^{2}=4 x$ with focus $F$, draw two perpendicular chords $A B, C D$ through $F$. Let the midpoints of $A B, C D$ be $M, N$ respectively.
(1) Prove that the line $M N$ must pass through a fixed point;
(2) Construct circles with diameters $A B$ and $C D$ respectively, find the equation of the locus of the midpoint $H$ of the common chord of the two circles.

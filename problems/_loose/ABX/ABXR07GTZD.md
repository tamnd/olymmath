Let $A B, A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ be chords of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ and the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=a^{2}$, respectively, with the endpoints $A$ and $A^{\prime}$, $B$ and $B^{\prime}$ having the same x-coordinates and the same sign for their y-coordinates. Prove: When $A B$ passes through a fixed point $M(p, q)$ inside the ellipse, $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ must pass through the fixed point $M^{\prime}\left(p, \frac{a}{b} q\right)$.

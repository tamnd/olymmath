The sequence of positive integers $a_1$ , $a_2$ , $...$ is such that for each $n = 1$ , $2$ , $...$ the quadratic equation $$ a_{n+2}x^2 + a_{n+1}x+ a_n = 0 $$ has a real root. Can the sequence consist of
(a) $10 $ terms,
(b) an infinite number of terms?

(A Shapovalov)

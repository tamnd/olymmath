(Beijing, 64). In triangle $A B C$, where the angle at vertex $A$ is not acute, a square $B_{1} C_{1} D E$ is inscribed (side $D E$ lies on segment $B C$, and vertices $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ lie on segments $A B$ and $A C$ respectively). Then, in triangle $A B_{1} C_{1}$, a square $B_{2} C_{2} D_{1} E_{1}$ is inscribed in the same manner, and so on. This construction is performed several times. Prove that the sum of the areas of all inscribed squares is less than half the area of triangle $A B C$.

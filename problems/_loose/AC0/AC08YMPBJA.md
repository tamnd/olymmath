(1979 Tianjin City High School Mathematics Competition, Question 2) Let the function $f(x)$ defined for all real numbers satisfy the following conditions:
(1) For any real number $x$, we have $f(x) \geqslant 2$;
(2) For any real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}$, we have
$$
f\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right) \leqslant f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right) .
$$

Prove: For any real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}$, we have
$$
\lg f\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right) \leqslant \lg f\left(x_{1}\right)+\lg f\left(x_{2}\right) .
$$

A cuboid has edge lengths $a, 2a$, and $\frac{a}{2}$, where $a$ is given. From this cuboid, a straight prism is separated. The height of this prism is $\frac{a}{2}$, and its base is an isosceles right triangle with leg length $a$. The remaining body is a prism with a trapezoidal base.

a) Draw the remaining body in cavalier perspective and choose $a=6 \text{ cm}$ for this!

b) Determine the volume of the remaining body in dependence on $a$!

c) Give the ratio of the volumes of the remaining body and the cuboid!

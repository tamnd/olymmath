I live on the ground floor of a ten-story building. On each floor, a friend of mine lives. One day, I put the numbers $1,2, \ldots$, 9 into a hat and drew them out in a random order. I visited them in the order in which I drew their floor numbers. On average, how many meters did I travel by elevator, if the distance between two floors is 4 meters, and I took the elevator from every floor to the next drawn floor number?

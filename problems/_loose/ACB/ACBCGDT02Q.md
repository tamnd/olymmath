Let $k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{6}$ be circles that touch the circle $k$ at points $P_{1}, \ldots, P_{6}$, respectively. (The points of tangency follow each other in this order on the circumference of $k$.) Prove that if $k_{1}$ touches $k_{2}$, $k_{2}$ touches $k_{3}$, $\ldots$ and $k_{6}$ touches $k_{1}$, then the segments $P_{1} P_{4}, P_{2} P_{5}$, and $P_{3} P_{6}$ intersect at a single point.

Juliana wants to assign each of the 26 letters $A, B, C, D, \ldots, W, X, Y, Z$ of the alphabet a different non-zero numerical value, such that $A \times C=B, B \times D=C, C \times E=D$, and so on, up to $X \times Z=Y$.

a) If Juliana assigns the values 5 and 7 to $A$ and $B$, respectively, what will be the values of $C, D$, and $E$?

b) Show that $G=A$, regardless of the values Juliana assigns to $A$ and $B$.

c) If Juliana assigns values to $A$ and $B$ such that $A \times B=2010$, what will be the value of the product $A \times B \times C \times D \times \cdots \times W \times X \times Y \times Z$?

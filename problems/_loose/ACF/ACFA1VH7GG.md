The seventy-seventh problem: Let $\mathrm{m}$ be a positive integer, $\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{m})$ denote the number of all distinct positive divisors of $\mathrm{m}$, and $\mathrm{s}(\mathrm{m})$ denote the sum of the prime factors of $\mathrm{m}$ (including multiplicity). Prove that for each positive integer $n$, we have $1+\frac{s(n)}{2 n} \leq \frac{d(n!)}{d(n-1)!)} \leq 1+\frac{2 s(n)}{n}$.

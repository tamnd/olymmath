For $a \geqslant 2$, an integer $n$ is said to be $a-$ Martinian if there exist integers $t_{1}, \ldots, t_{k}$ such that the base 10 representation of $n$ is the concatenation of the base 10 representations of the $t_{i}$, and satisfying $n=$ $t_{1}^{a}+\ldots+t_{k}^{a}$. Given $\ell+1$ integers $r, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{\ell}$, show that there exists $n$ which is $a_{j}-$ Martinian for $j=1, \ldots, \ell$ and is congruent to $r$ modulo 2021.

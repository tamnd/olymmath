Let $a$ be the positive root of equation $x + \frac{1}{x} = 675$. Prove that
$$
\varphi(2023(a^{2n} + a^{-2n})) > 2 \cdot \varphi(2022(a^{2n} + a^{-2n}))
$$
for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.

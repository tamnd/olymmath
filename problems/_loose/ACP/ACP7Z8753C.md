From point $S$ in space, three non-coplanar rays originate: $a, b$, and $c$. The plane determined by $c$ and $b$ is perpendicular to the plane determined by $c$ and $a$. Given the acute angles $\alpha$ between $b$ and $c$, and $\beta$ between $a$ and $c$, calculate the angle $\gamma$ formed by $a$ and $b$.

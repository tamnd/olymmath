Given a triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ with angles $\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}$. Using the external angle bisectors, a new triangle $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ with angles $\alpha_{2}, \beta_{2}, \gamma_{2}$ is constructed. In the same way, from triangle $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$, a triangle $A_{3} B_{3} C_{3}$ with angles $\alpha_{3}, \beta_{3}, \gamma_{3}$ is constructed, and so on. Prove that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \alpha_{n}=\frac{\pi}{3}, \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \beta_{n}=\frac{\pi}{3}$ and $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \gamma_{n}=\frac{\pi}{3}$.

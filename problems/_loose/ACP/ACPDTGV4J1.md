Prove that for any point $T$ outside the tetrahedron DABC, the following formula holds:

$$
T A^{2}+T B^{2}+T C^{2}+T D^{2}=M A^{2}+M B^{2}+M C^{2}+M D^{2}+4 T M^{2}
$$

(where $M$ is the center of mass of the tetrahedron).

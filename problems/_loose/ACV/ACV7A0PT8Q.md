A figure formed by a finite number of lattice points and some horizontal or vertical edges of length 1 (the endpoints of the edges belong to the set of lattice points) is called a "lattice graph." Let a lattice graph $\mathrm{A}$ have $\mathrm{e}(\mathrm{A})$ edges, $\mathrm{v}(\mathrm{A})$ vertices, and $\varepsilon(\mathrm{A})$ connected components. Prove: For any positive integer $\mathrm{e}$, $\frac{e}{2} \leq \min _{e(A) \geq e}(v(A)-\varepsilon(A)) \leq \frac{e}{2}+\sqrt{\frac{e}{2}}+1$.

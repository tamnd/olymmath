Consider a regular quadrilateral pyramid $S M N P Q$, with vertex $S$ and the center of the base $O$, having all edges of length $l$. Let $T$ be the symmetric point of $M$ with respect to the line $N P$.

a) Calculate the distance between the lines $S O$ and $P T$.

b) Calculate the distance from the point $M$ to the plane (SNP).

c) Find the tangent of the angle formed by the planes $(S M T)$ and (MPS).

d) An ant starts from $S$, moves on the face $S M N$, touches the edge $[M N]$ at a point $A$, and then moves on the base $M N P Q$ until it reaches the point $F$, the midpoint of the edge $[N P]$. Calculate $N A$ knowing that the path traveled by the ant from $S$ to $F$ is the shortest possible.

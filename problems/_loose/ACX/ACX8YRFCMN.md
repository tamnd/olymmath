The points $X, Y, Z$ lie in this order on a straight line with $|X Y| \neq|Y Z|$. Let $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be the circle with diameter $X Y$ and $Y Z$ respectively. The points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ or $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ lie on $k_{1}$ or $k_{2}$ so that

$$
\angle A_{1} Y A_{2}=\angle B_{1} Y B_{2}=90^{\circ}
$$

applies. Show that the two straight lines $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $B_{1} B_{2}$ intersect on $X Y$.

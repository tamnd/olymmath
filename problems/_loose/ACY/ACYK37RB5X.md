$\frac{3(2 x+y)}{y^{2}}-\frac{2(2 x-y)}{y^{2}}-\frac{\left.4 x^{2}-y^{2}\right)}{y^{2}(2 x-5 y)}=\frac{2 x+5 y)}{y^{2}}-\frac{\left.4 x^{2}-y^{2}\right)}{y^{2}(2 x-5 y)}=-\frac{24}{2 x-5 y}$. When $x=\frac{4}{3}, y=\frac{7}{3}$ we get $\frac{24}{9}=\frac{8}{3}$.

For positive numbers $a, b, c, d$, it is known that $a b c d=1$. Prove that among the numbers $\frac{a^{2}+1}{b^{2}}, \frac{b^{2}+1}{c^{2}}, \frac{c^{2}+1}{d^{2}}, \frac{d^{2}+1}{a^{2}}$ there is a number not less than 2.

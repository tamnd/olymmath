In the triangles $A B C$, let the measures of the interior angles at $A, B$, and $C$ be denoted by $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ respectively. Furthermore, assume that the perpendicular bisector of side $A B$ and the angle bisector passing through $A$ intersect at a point $D$ that lies on side $B C$.

a) Derive a formula that, in every triangle that meets this condition, expresses $\gamma$ in terms of $\beta$! For which values of $\beta$ does there exist a triangle that meets the given conditions; for which values of $\beta$ does no such triangle exist?

b) Determine all values of $\beta$ for which a triangle that meets the given conditions is a right triangle!

c) Determine all values of $\beta$ for which a triangle that meets the given conditions is isosceles!

(USA, 79). Three circles lying on a sphere and having a common center $O$ with it pass through the point

A. On these circles, points $B, C, D$ are chosen respectively such that $\angle A O B=90^{\circ}$, and the line $O B$ is the bisector of angle $C O D$. Prove that if the rays $A B^{\prime}$, $A C^{\prime}, A D^{\prime}$ are tangent to the arcs $A B, A C, A D$ of the corresponding circles, then the ray $A B^{\prime}$ is the bisector of angle $C^{\prime} A D^{\prime}$.

Test $\mathbf{G}$ Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients and degree $\geqslant 1$. Prove that for each $c>0$, there exists a positive integer $n_{0}$, such that for each polynomial $p(x)$ with real coefficients, degree $\geqslant n_{0}$, and leading coefficient 1, the number of integers $x$ satisfying $|f[p(x)]| \leqslant c$ does not exceed the degree of $p(x)$.
(1992 Vietnam National Team Selection Test)

Let $M$ be a point on the segment $[C D]$. The parallel to $(B D)$ passing through $M$ intersects the segment $[B C]$ at $P$. The parallel to $(A C)$ passing through $M$ intersects the segment $[A D]$ at $Q$. Show that the points $P, O$ and $Q$ are collinear.

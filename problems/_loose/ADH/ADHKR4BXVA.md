Let $A, B, C, a, b, c$ be positive integers, $a \cdot b \cdot c>1$. Prove that there exists a positive integer $n$ such that

$$
A \cdot a^{n}+B \cdot b^{n}+C \cdot c^{n}
$$

is a composite number.

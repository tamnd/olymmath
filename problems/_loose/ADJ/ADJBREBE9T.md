Given a triangle $ABC$ and a point $M$ not lying on its sides or their extensions. Tangents are drawn from point $M$ to the circles passing through the triplets of points $(A, B, M)$, $(B, C, M)$, and $(C, A, M)$, intersecting the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ at points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ respectively. Prove that these three points lie on a single straight line.

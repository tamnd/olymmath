The computer network of the Pentagon consists of 1000 computers, some pairs of which are connected by wires. Hacker Vasya has written a virus that infects all computers directly connected by a wire to already infected ones every minute. It is known that the network is structured in such a way that if Vasya loads his virus onto any one of the computers, the entire network will eventually become infected. Prove that hacker Vasya can choose 90 computers in the Pentagon network such that if he loads the virus onto them simultaneously, the entire network will be infected within 10 minutes.

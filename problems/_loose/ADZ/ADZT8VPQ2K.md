On Ivan's birthday, $3 n$ guests came. Ivan has $3 n$ cylinders with the top faces labeled with the letters $A, B, C$, with $n$ cylinders for each letter. Ivan hopes to organize a dance: distribute one cylinder to each guest and divide the guests into several (one or more) circles, such that the length of each circle is a multiple of 3, and when viewed from above, each circle can be read clockwise as $\mathrm{ABCABC} \cdots \mathrm{ABC}$. Prove: Ivan has exactly $(3 n)$ ! different ways to organize this dance (cylinders with the same letter are indistinguishable, and all guests are distinct).

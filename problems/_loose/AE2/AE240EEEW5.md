In the cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$, the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ are perpendicular, and the projections of their intersection point $F$ onto the sides are $P, Q$, $R, S$. Prove that the quadrilateral $P Q R S$ is a cyclic quadrilateral and also a tangential quadrilateral (in other words, a bicentric quadrilateral, a quadrilateral with two centers).

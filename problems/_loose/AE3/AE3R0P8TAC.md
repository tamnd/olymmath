In triangle $A B C$ with orthocenter $H$ one has that

$$
A H \cdot B H \cdot C H=3 \text { and } A H^{2}+B H^{2}+C H^{2}=7
$$

Find:

a) the circumradius of $\triangle A B C$;

b) the sides of $\triangle A B C$ with maximum possible area.

Given a parallelogram $A B C D$. On sides $A B$ and $B C$ and the extension of side $C D$ beyond point $D$, points $K$, $L$, and $M$ are chosen respectively such that triangles $K L M$ and $B C A$ are equal (with this correspondence of vertices). Segment $K M$ intersects segment $A D$ at point $N$. Prove that $L N \| A B$. (B. Obukhov)

Show that if $p, q$ are positive numbers such that: $\sqrt{7}=\frac{p}{q}$, then

$$
\sqrt{7}=\frac{7 q-2 p}{p-2 q}
$$

and here $p-2 q$ is also positive and smaller than $q$.

Let $q$ be an arbitrary line through the focus $F$ of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$, and let $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ be the intersection points of the line $q$ and the parabola. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{\overline{F S_{1}}}+\frac{1}{\overline{F S_{2}}}=\frac{2}{p}
$$

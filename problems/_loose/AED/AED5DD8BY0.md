Let $A$ be a point inside a regular $n$-gon such that the projections of $A$ onto all sides fall on the interior points of the sides. These projections divide the sides into $2n$ segments. Number these segments consecutively: $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 2n$. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments with even numbers is equal to the sum of the lengths of the segments with odd numbers.

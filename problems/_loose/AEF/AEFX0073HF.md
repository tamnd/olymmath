Given that the circumcircle $\odot O$ of the acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ has a radius of $R, AB < AC < BC, \triangle ABC$'s incircle $\odot I$ touches sides $BC, CA, AB$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively, and the circumcircles $\Gamma_{1}, \Gamma_{2}, \Gamma_{3}$ of $\triangle AEF, \triangle BDF, \triangle CDE$ intersect $\odot O$ at the second points $A', B', C'$ respectively. Prove:
(1) $D, E, A', B'$ are concyclic;
(2) $DA', EB', FC'$ are concurrent. ${ }^{[5]}$
(2017, Greek National Team Selection Exam)

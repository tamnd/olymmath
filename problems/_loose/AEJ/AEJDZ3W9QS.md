On the table lie 6 apples (not necessarily of the same weight). Tanya divided them into 3 on each of the two pans of the scales, and the scales remained in balance. And Sasha divided the same apples differently: 2 apples on one pan and 4 on the other, and the scales were again in balance. Prove that it is possible to place one apple on one pan and two on the other so that the scales remain in balance. [6 points]

(A. V. Shipovalov)

$S$ is a set composed of $m$ pairs of positive integers $(a, b)(1 \leqslant a, b \leqslant n, a \neq b)$ (unordered pairs). Prove: there are at least $\frac{4 m}{3 n}\left(m-\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right)$ triples $(a, b, c)$ such that $(a, b),(b, c),(c, a) \in S$.

Let $x, y, z \in \mathbf{R}$, and $A, B, C$ be the three interior angles of $\triangle A B C$. Prove that:
$$
(x+y+z)^{2} \geqslant 4\left(y z \sin ^{2} A+z x \sin ^{2} B+x y \sin ^{2} C\right) .
$$

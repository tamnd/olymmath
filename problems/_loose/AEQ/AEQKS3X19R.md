Given a conical frustum-shaped glass cup filled with water, the inner diameters of the bottom and top bases are $4$ and $14$, respectively, and the inner height is 12. First, place a solid sphere A at the bottom of the cup, which touches the inner side of the cup (i.e., is tangent to it) all around. Then, place another solid sphere B, which touches sphere A and also touches the inner side of the cup all around. Find the volume of the water in the cup.

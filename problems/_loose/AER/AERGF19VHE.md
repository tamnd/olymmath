(First China Southeast Mathematical Olympiad Problem)(1) Does there exist an infinite sequence of positive integers $\{f(n)\}$, such that for any positive integer $n$, we have $f^{2}(n+1) \geqslant 2 f(n) f(n+2)$.
(2) Does there exist an infinite sequence of positive irrational numbers $\{f(n)\}$, such that for any positive integer $n$, we have $f^{2}(n+1) \geqslant 2 f(n) f(n+2)$.

The height of the rhombus is 4, and the point of intersection of its diagonals is the orthogonal projection of vertex $S$ onto the base plane. A sphere with a radius of 2 touches all the faces of the pyramid. Find the volume of the pyramid if the distance from the center of the sphere to the line $A C$ is $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3} A B$.

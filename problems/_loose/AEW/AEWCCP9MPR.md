In any triangle $ABC$, let $O$ be the center of the inscribed circle, $P$ the center of the excircle opposite to side $BC$, and $D$ the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle CAB$ with side $BC$. Prove that

$$
\frac{2}{|AD|}=\frac{1}{|AO|}+\frac{1}{|AP|}
$$

(The excircle opposite to side $BC$ is a circle that touches side $BC$ and the extensions of the sides $BA$ and $CA$.)

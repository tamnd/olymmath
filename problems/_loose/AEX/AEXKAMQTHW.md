The line $DE$ intersects the circumcircle at $P$ and $Q$ , with $P$ in the small arc $AB$ and $Q$ in the small arc $AC$ . If $P$ is the midpoint of the arc $AB$ , find the angle A and the ratio $\frac{PQ}{BC}$ .

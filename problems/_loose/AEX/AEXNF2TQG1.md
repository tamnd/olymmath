The function $f(x)$ is defined for positive numbers, takes on positive values, and satisfies the equation $$ f(x)f(y)=f(xy)+f\left(\frac{x}{y}\right). $$ a) Name at least three such functions.
b) Prove that $f(x) \ge 2$ , $f(1)=2$ .
c) Prove that if $f(x)$ satisfies the condition, then the function $f^2(x)-2$ also satisfies it.

In the Cartesian plane consider the hyperbola
$$\Gamma=\{M(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^2 \vert \frac{x^2}{4}-y^2=1\} $$
and a conic $\Gamma '$ , disjoint from $\Gamma$ . Let $n(\Gamma ,\Gamma ')$ be the maximal number of pairs of points $(A,A')\in\Gamma\times\Gamma '$ such that $AA'\le BB'$ , for any $(B,B')$ For each $p\in\{0,1,2,4\}$ , find the equation of $\Gamma'$ for which $n(\Gamma ,\Gamma ')=p$ . Justify the answer.

Consider a chess tournament involving humans and aliens where each player plays against all others exactly once. At the end of the tournament, each player obtained half of the points they scored playing against humans and half playing against aliens. Let $ t $ and $ a $ be the number of humans and aliens, respectively. Answer the following:

a) What is the total number of games in this tournament, in terms of $ t $ and $ a $?

b) In this tournament, each win is worth 1 point, each draw is worth 0.5 points, and each loss is worth zero points. What is the total number of points for the humans in terms of $ t $ and $ a $?

c) Verify that the total number of players in the tournament is a perfect square.

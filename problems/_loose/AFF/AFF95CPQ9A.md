Suppose there are 30 people sitting around a round table, each of whom is either a fool or a wise person. Each person is asked: "Is your right-hand neighbor a wise person or a fool?" Wise people always give the correct answer, while fools may answer either correctly or incorrectly. It is known that the number of fools does not exceed $F$. Find the largest $F$ such that it is always possible to identify at least one wise person.

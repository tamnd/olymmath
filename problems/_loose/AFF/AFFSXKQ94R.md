Given fixed lines $O X, O Y$ and a fixed point $C$, draw any line through $C$ intersecting $O X, O Y$ at $A, B$. Draw $B P \perp O X$, with the foot of the perpendicular at $P$. Draw $A Q \perp O Y$, with the foot of the perpendicular at $Q$. Prove that $P Q$ passes through a fixed point.

Real numbers of the form $a+b \sqrt{2}(a, b \in \mathbf{Z})$ are called "good numbers". Let $A(x), B(x)$ be polynomials with all coefficients being good numbers, and the constant term of $B(x)$ is 1. Let $Q(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients, satisfying $A(x)=B(x) Q(x)$. Prove: The coefficients of $Q(x)$ are all good numbers.

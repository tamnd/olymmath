Prove that for $\xi \in L^{2}(\Omega, \mathscr{F}, \mathrm{P})$ and any $\sigma$-subalgebra $\mathscr{G} \subseteq \mathscr{F}$, the inequality

$$
\|\xi\| \geqslant\|\mathrm{E}(\xi \mid \mathscr{G})\|
$$

holds, and equality holds if and only if $\xi=\mathrm{E}(\xi \mid \mathscr{G})$ a.s.

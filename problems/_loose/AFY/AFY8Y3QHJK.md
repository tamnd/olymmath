At the moment when the first spacecraft has completed five orbits around the Earth, the second has completed 4 orbits (its speed is 4/5 of the first), the third - 3 orbits, the fourth - 2 orbits, the fifth - 1 orbit. At this moment, the spacecrafts are again in the same point. By this time, the first spacecraft has overtaken the second 1 time, the third - 2 times, the fourth - 3 times, the fifth - 4 times, i.e., the instrument on board the first spacecraft has recorded 10 overtakes of the first spacecraft. After the first spacecraft completes another 5 orbits, the instrument will record another 10 overtakes. At this moment, all spacecrafts are again in the same point. Then the first spacecraft will overtake and the instrument on board will record $21-\mathrm{m}$ the spacecraft with the smallest speed - the fifth.

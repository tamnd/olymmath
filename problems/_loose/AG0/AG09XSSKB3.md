Let $S_{1}, S_{2}, \ldots, S_{2011}$ be nonempty sets of consecutive integers such that any 2 of them have a common element. Prove that there is an integer that belongs to every $S_{\mathrm{i}}, i=1, \ldots, 2011$. (For example, $\{2,3,4,5\}$ is a set of consecutive integers while $\{2,3,5\}$ is not.)

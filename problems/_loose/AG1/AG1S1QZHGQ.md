Veronika has a classic chessboard with $8 \times 8$ squares. The rows are marked with the numbers 1 to 8, and the columns with the letters a to h. Veronika placed a knight on the square b1, which can only move as in chess.

1. Is it possible to move the knight to the square h1 in four moves?
2. Is it possible to move the knight to the square e6 in five moves?

If yes, describe all possible sequences of moves. If not, explain why it is not possible.

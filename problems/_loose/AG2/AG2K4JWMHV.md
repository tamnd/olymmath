Given a prime number $p>5$ . It is known that the length of the smallest period of the fraction $1/p$ is a multiple of three. This period (including possible leading zeros) was written on a strip of paper and cut into three equal-length parts $a$ , $b$ , $c$ (they may also have leading zeros). What could be the sum of the three periodic fractions: $0.(a)$ , $0.(b)$ , and $0.(c)$ ?

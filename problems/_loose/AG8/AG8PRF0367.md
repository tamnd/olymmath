On the stage there is an $8$ × $8$ board, the magician blindfolds himself, and then the assistant goes inviting people from the public to write down the numbers $1, 2, 3, 4, . . . , 64$ in the boxes they want (one number per box) until completing the $64$ numbers. After the assistant covers two adyacent boxes, at her choice. Finally, the magician removes his blindfold and has to $"guess"$ what number is in each square that the assistant. Explain how they put together this trick. $Clarification:$ Two squares are adjacent if they have a common side

Use lines parallel to the sides of an equilateral triangle to divide an equilateral triangle with side length $n$ into $n^{2}$ equilateral triangles with side length 1. Draw a non-closed broken line along the sides of the resulting triangles, such that it passes through each of the vertices of these triangles exactly once. Prove that there are at least $n$ pairs of adjacent segments in the broken line, such that the angle between each pair of segments is acute.

The extensions of sides $A D$ and $B C$ of a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ intersect at point $O ; M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $C D, P$ and $Q$ are the midpoints of diagonals $A C$ and $B D$. Prove that:

a) $S_{P M Q N}=\left|S_{A B D}-S_{A C D}\right| / 2$

b) $S_{O P Q}=S_{A B C D} / 4$.

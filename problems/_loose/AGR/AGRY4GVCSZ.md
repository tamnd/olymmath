Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be a geometric sequence composed of positive numbers, and $S_{n}$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms.
(1) Prove: $\frac{\lg S_{n}+\lg S_{n+2}}{2}<\lg S_{n+1}$;
(2) Does there exist a positive constant $c$, such that $\frac{\lg \left(S_{n}-c\right)+\lg \left(S_{n+2}-c\right)}{2}=\lg \left(S_{n+1}-c\right)$ holds? Provide a proof.

(2) Fritz, Hans, and the student in grade 6 found many fly agarics.

(3) Three students, namely the student in grade 8, the student in grade 7, and Hans, laughed at the fourth student, namely Werner, who had brought a fly agaric.

Which of the four students is in grade 5, which is in grade 6, which is in grade 7, and which is in grade 8?

A *lattice point* is a point in the plane whose two coordinates are both integers. A *lattice line* is a line in the plane that contains at least two lattice points. Is it possible to color every lattice point red or blue such that every lattice line contains exactly 2017 red lattice points? Prove that your answer is correct.

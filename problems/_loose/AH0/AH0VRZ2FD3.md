Each of two houses $A$ and $B$ is divided into two flats. Several cats and dogs live there. It is known that the fraction of cats in the first flat of $A$ (the ratio between the number of cats and the total number of animals in the flat) is greater than the fraction of cats in the first flat of $B$ , and the fraction of cats in the second flat of $A$ is greater than the fraction of cats in the second flat of $B$ . Is it true that the fraction of cats in house $A$ is greater than the fraction of cats in house $B$ ?

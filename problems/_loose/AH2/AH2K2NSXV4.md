Let $n=34000$. Among the vertices of a regular $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$, the vertices $A_{i}$ are painted red for which the number $i$ is a power of two, i.e., $i=1,2,4,8,16, \ldots$ In how many ways can 400 vertices of this $n$-gon be chosen so that they are the vertices of a regular 400-gon and none of them are red?

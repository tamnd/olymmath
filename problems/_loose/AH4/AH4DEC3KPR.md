The ninety-third problem: Let $k, n$ be positive integers, $\sigma_{k}(n)$ denote the sum of the $k$-th powers of all positive divisors of $n$. Prove: For any $k \geq 2$, there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $n \mid \sigma_{k}(n)$.

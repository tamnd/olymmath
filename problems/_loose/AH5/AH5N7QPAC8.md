Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $H$ its orthocenter. We denote $H_{A}$ the foot of the altitude from $A$ in $ABC$, $M_{A}$ the midpoint of the side $[BC]$, and $N_{A}$ the midpoint of the segment $[HA]$. We define the points $H_{B}, H_{C}, M_{B}, M_{C}, N_{B}$ and $N_{C}$ analogously. Prove that the nine points thus defined all lie on the same circle.

Note: This circle is known as the nine-point circle and the Euler circle.

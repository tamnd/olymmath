In the inscribed $\triangle A B C$, $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$. Construct the symmetric points of $H$ with respect to the sides $B C, C A, A B$ as $H_{1}, H_{2}, H_{3}$. If $P$ is any point on the circumference, connect $P H_{1}, P H_{2}, P H_{3}$ intersecting the sides $B C, C A, A B$ or their extensions at $D, E, F$. Try to prove: $D, E, F$ are collinear.

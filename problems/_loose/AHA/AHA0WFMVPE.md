On the rays $(O A), (O B$ and $(O C)$, perpendicular to each other in pairs, consider the points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$, such that $A^{\prime} \in(O A), B^{\prime} \in(O B)$ and $C^{\prime} \in(O C)$. Knowing that the quadrilaterals $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} B A$ and $B^{\prime} C^{\prime} C B$ are cyclic, show that:
a) $A^{\prime} C^{\prime} C A$ is a cyclic quadrilateral;

b) If $O H \perp(A B C), H \in(A B C)$, then $H$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$;

c) If $G$ is the centroid of triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, then $O G \perp(A B C)$.

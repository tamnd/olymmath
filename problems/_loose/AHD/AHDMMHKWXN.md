In triangle $ABC$ , $AB = 1$ and $AC = 2$ . Suppose there exists a point $P$ in the interior of triangle $ABC$ such that $\angle PBC = 70^{\circ}$ , and that there are points $E$ and $D$ on segments $AB$ and $AC$ , such that $\angle BPE = \angle EPA = 75^{\circ}$ and $\angle APD = \angle DPC = 60^{\circ}$ . Let $BD$ meet $CE$ at $Q,$ and let $AQ$ meet $BC$ at $F.$ If $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$ , compute the degree measure of $\angle MPF.$ *Authors: Alex Zhu and Ray Li*

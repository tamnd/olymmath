In parallelogram $ABCD$, a point $K$ is marked on the diagonal $AC$. Circle $s1$ passes through point $K$ and is tangent to lines $AB$ and $AD$, and the second intersection point of $s1$ with diagonal $AC$ lies on segment $AK$. Circle $s2$ passes through point $K$ and is tangent to lines $CB$ and $CD$, and the second intersection point of $s2$ with diagonal $AC$ lies on segment $KC$. Prove that for all positions of point $K$ on diagonal $AC$, the lines connecting the centers of circles $s1$ and $s2$ will be parallel to each other.

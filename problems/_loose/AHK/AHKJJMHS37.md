Write down the first $n$ natural numbers in decimal form on a (fairly long) strip of paper, then cut the strip so that each piece contains only one digit. Put these pieces in a box, mix them up, and draw one at random. Let $p_{n}$ denote the probability that the digit 0 is on the piece of paper drawn. Determine the limit of the sequence $p_{n}(n=1,2, \ldots)$.

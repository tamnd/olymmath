Consider a lake with two islands in it and seven cities on the shore. In the following, we will call the islands and cities places for short. There is a ship connection between exactly the following pairs of places:

(i) between the two islands,

(ii) between each city and each island,

(iii) between two cities if they are not adjacent.

Each of these connections is offered by exactly one of two competing shipping companies. Prove that there are always three places such that there are ship connections of the same company between two of these places.

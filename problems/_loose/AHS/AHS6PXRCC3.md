Given that $\alpha, \beta$ are the two roots of the quadratic equation $2 x^{2}-t x-2=0$ with respect to $x$, and $\alpha<\beta$, if the function $f(x)=$ $\frac{4 x-t}{x^{2}+1}$.
(1) Find the value of $\frac{f(\alpha)-f(\beta)}{\alpha-\beta}$;
(2) For any positive numbers $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}$, prove that $\left|f\left(\frac{\lambda_{1} \alpha+\lambda_{2} \beta}{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}\right)-f\left(\frac{\lambda_{1} \beta+\lambda_{2} \alpha}{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}\right)\right|<2|\alpha-\beta|$.

The unit radius circle $k$ with center $O$ has inscribed isosceles triangles $ABC$ and $ADE$ whose bases $BC$ and $DE$ are perpendicular to the radius $AO$. The heights of these triangles are $1/2$ and $5/4$, respectively. Points $B$ and $D$ are on the same side of the line $AO$. The intersection of segments $OB$ and $AD$ is point $P$, and the intersection of segments $OC$ and $AE$ is point $Q$. Show that the line $PQ$ intersects the points adjacent to $A$ in a regular pentagon inscribed in $k$ with vertex $A$.

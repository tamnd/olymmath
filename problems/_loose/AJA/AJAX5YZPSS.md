Let $\left(a_{0}, \ldots, a_{m n}\right)$ be a sequence of $m n+1$ real numbers. Show that one can extract:

either an increasing subsequence of length $m+1$

or a decreasing subsequence of length $n+1$.

Note: To obtain an extracted subsequence, one can remove terms from the original sequence but not reorder them. For example, $\left(a_{2}, a_{5}, a_{6}, a_{8}\right)$ is extracted from $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{10}\right)$ but $\left(a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{7}, a_{5}\right)$ is not.)

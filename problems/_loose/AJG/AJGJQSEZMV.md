Let $\Omega$ be a unit circle with diameter $AB$ and center $O$ . Let $C$ , $D$ be on $\Omega$ and lie on the same side of $AB$ such that $\angle CAB = 50^\circ$ and $\angle DBA = 70^\circ$ . Suppose $AD$ intersects $BC$ at $E$ . Let the perpendicular from $O$ to $CD$ intersect the perpendicular from $E$ to $AB$ at $F$ . Find the length of $OF$ .

At each vertex of a regular nonagon $A_{0} A_{1} \cdots A_{8}$, there is a car. When a signal is given, the nine cars start moving simultaneously along the perimeter of the nonagon in the same direction (for example, $A_{0} \rightarrow A_{1} \rightarrow A_{2} \rightarrow \cdots \rightarrow A_{8} \rightarrow A_{0}$ is the positive direction) at a uniform speed (the speeds of different cars are not necessarily the same). It is known that at some moment, the nine cars meet at the same vertex. Is it possible for the nine cars to meet at another vertex entirely?

Will the nine cars possibly meet at another vertex entirely?

In a class, there are both boys and girls, and all of them have either red or brown hair, with both hair colors present. Prove that there is a boy-girl pair in the class whose hair colors are different.

Suppose in the same class, we also distinguish between two cases based on height, and there are both short and tall students in the class. Is it always true that there are two students whose three attributes (gender, hair color, and height) are all different?

Is it always true that there are three students among whom any two have at most one attribute in common?

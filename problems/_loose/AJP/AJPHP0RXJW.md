Given a point $M$ and a circle $k$ with $M$ as its center. We are looking for a square $A B C D$ that has the following properties:

(1) The vertices $A$ and $D$ lie on the circumference of the circle $k$.

(2) The side $B C$ of the square touches the circle $k$ at a point $P$, which lies between $B$ and $C$.

Justify and describe a construction that (starting from the given circle $k$) leads to a square with these properties! Investigate whether there is (for a given $k$) exactly one such square up to congruence!

Label the endpoints of a diameter of a circle with the number 1, then equally divide each of the resulting semicircles, and write the sum of the numbers at the endpoints of the two semicircles at their division points (first step). Then equally divide the 4 arcs obtained, and write the sum of the numbers at the endpoints of these arcs at their division points (second step). Find the sum of all numbers marked on the circle after performing this process $n$ times.

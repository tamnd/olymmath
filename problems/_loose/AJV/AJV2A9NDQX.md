The digits from 0 to 9 are encrypted by the letters A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J in some order. In one question, you can find out the encrypted record of the sum of several different letters. For example, if you ask "A $+\mathrm{B}=$ ?", then in the case where $\mathrm{A}=9, \mathrm{~B}=1, \mathrm{C}=0$, the answer will be " $\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}=$ = $\mathrm{BC}$ ". How can you determine which letters correspond to which digits in five such questions?

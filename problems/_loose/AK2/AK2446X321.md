In a triangle $ABC$ , let $D$ be the midpoint of the side $BC$ , and let $E$ be a point on the side $AC$ . The lines $BE$ and $AD$ meet at a point $F$ .

Prove: If $\frac{BF}{FE}=\frac{BC}{AB}+1$ , then the line $BE$ bisects the angle $ABC$ .

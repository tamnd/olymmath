Through a moving point $A$ (A is not on the $y$-axis) on the line $x-2 y+13=0$, two tangent lines are drawn to the parabola $y^{2}=8 x$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of tangency, and let the lines $A M$ and $A N$ intersect the $y$-axis at points $B$ and $C$. Prove:
(1) The line $M N$ always passes through a fixed point;
(2) The circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ always passes through a fixed point, and find the minimum value of the radius of this circle.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral, point $P$ the midpoint of segment $[A B]$, points $M \in[B C]$ and $N \in[A D]$ such that $\frac{B M}{B C}=\frac{A N}{A D}=\frac{1}{3}$.

a) Express the vectors $\overrightarrow{P M}$ and $\overrightarrow{P N}$ in terms of the vectors $\overrightarrow{P A}, \overrightarrow{P C}, \overrightarrow{P D}$.

b) Prove that the midpoints of segments $[A B],[M N]$ and $[C D]$ are collinear.

G. Letac proposed the following problem:
Does there exist, for all positive integers $n$, a set $F_{n}$ composed of distinct integers, such that each element of $F_{n}$ is the sum of two elements (not necessarily distinct) in $F_{n}$, but the sum of any $k(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$ elements in $F_{n}$ is never zero?

Wang Zhixiong ${ }^{[4]}$ provided constructions for $F_{n}$ when $n=1,2,3,4$, partially answering Letac's question, and further asked: For general $n$, does the set $F_{n}$ exist? In particular, does $F_{5}$ exist?

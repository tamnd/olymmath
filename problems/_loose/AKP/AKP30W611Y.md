Let $a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$ and $b_{1}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be sequences of real numbers. Show:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{a_{i} b_{i}} \geqslant n^{3}
$$

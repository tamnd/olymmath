32021 equal circles $\odot O_{k}(k=1,2, \cdots, 2021)$ are sequentially externally tangent (i.e., $\odot O_{k}$ is tangent to $\odot O_{k+1}$, with the convention that $\odot O_{2021+1}=\odot O_{1}$), and all are internally tangent to $\odot O$. From any point $P$ on $\odot O$, draw the tangents $P M_{k}$ to these 2021 circles $\odot O_{k}$. Prove: The lengths of these 2021 tangents can be divided into two groups with equal sums.

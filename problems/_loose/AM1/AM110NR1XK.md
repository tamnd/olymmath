$\textbf{N6.}$ Let $a,b$ be positive integers. If $a,b$ satisfy that
\begin{align*} \frac{a+1}{b} + \frac{b+1}{a} \end{align*}
is also a positive integer, show that
\begin{align*} \frac{a+b}{gcd(a,b)^2} \end{align*}
is a Fibonacci number.

Given the ellipse $E: x^{2}+4 y^{2}=4$ with left and right vertices $M, N$ respectively, a line through point $P(-2,2)$ intersects the ellipse $E$ at points $A, B$, and $A, B$ are in the first quadrant, with $A$ on segment $B P$. The line $O P$ intersects the line $N A$ at point $C$. Connecting $M B, M C, A M$, the slopes of lines $A M, A C, M B, M C$ are denoted as $k_{A M}, k_{A C}, k_{M B}, k_{M C}$ respectively.
Prove: $\frac{k_{M B}}{k_{A M}}=\frac{k_{A C}}{k_{M C}}$.

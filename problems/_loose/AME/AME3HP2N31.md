After Don Diego's death, there were three chests of treasures and the following will: "The chests should be transferred to the ownership of Don Alvaro, Don Benito, and Don Vicente according to their choice, which is determined by the following conditions: 1) The one who saw me in a green cloak should not choose before Alvaro; 2) The one who gave me a snuffbox should not choose first; 3) If Vicente does not choose second, then Benito should choose before the one who first started carrying a sword.

These conditions are sufficient to determine the order of selection, and none of them are redundant. Determine the order in which the heirs should choose the chests, as well as who saw Diego in a green cloak, who gave him the snuffbox, and who first started carrying a sword.

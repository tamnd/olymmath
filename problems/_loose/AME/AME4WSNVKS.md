Bernd has to settle a total of 21 marks in contributions with Monika. He has 8 two-mark coins and 6 five-mark coins and no other money.

In Monika's cash register, there are exactly 20 marks, in the form of 10 two-mark coins.

She claims that under these circumstances, there are 3 different ways to settle the specified amount.

This does not include scenarios where a coin is exchanged between Bernd and Monika and an equivalent one is later returned. Also, possibilities that differ only in the order in which the coins are given should not be counted as different.

It should also not matter which five-mark or which two-mark coin is given.

Determine whether Monika's claim is correct.

Note: It is not necessary to investigate whether these 3 possibilities, if they exist, are the only ones.

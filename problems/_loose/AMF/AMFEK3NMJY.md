In a certain magical country, there are banknotes in denominations of $2^0, 2^1, 2^2, \ldots$ UAH. Businessman Victor has to make cash payments to $44$ different companies totaling $44000$ UAH, but he does not remember how much he has to pay to each company. What is the smallest number of banknotes Victor should withdraw from an ATM (totaling exactly $44000$ UAH) to guarantee that he would be able to pay all the companies without leaving any change?

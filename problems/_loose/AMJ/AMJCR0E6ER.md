$A$ is the foot of the mountain, $B$ is the peak, and $C$ is a point on the slope, with $A C=\frac{1}{3} A B$. Person A and Person B start from the foot of the mountain at the same time, reach the peak, and return to the foot, repeating this back-and-forth movement. The speed ratio of A to B is $6: 5$, and the downhill speed of both A and B is 1.5 times their respective uphill speeds. After some time, A first sees B climbing on the $A C$ segment from the peak; after some more time, A sees B climbing on the $A C$ segment from the peak for the second time. How many times has A reached the peak by the time A sees B climbing on the $A C$ segment for the second time (including this time)?

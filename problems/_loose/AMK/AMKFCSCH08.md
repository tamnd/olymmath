Let $p>3$ be a prime number. Show that $p^{2}-1$ is a multiple of 12.

Theorem 46 (Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic). Every natural number $n>1$ can be uniquely decomposed into a product of prime numbers.

Theorem 47. The set of prime numbers is infinite.

A math group consists of 24 students. For each team of 6 students, the supervisor gives either a "can cooperate" or "cannot cooperate" evaluation. To prepare for the math relay competition, the supervisor plans to divide the group into 4 teams, each with 6 students. Question: Is it possible that for any method of dividing into 4 teams, either exactly three teams can cooperate, or exactly one team can cooperate, and both situations will occur?

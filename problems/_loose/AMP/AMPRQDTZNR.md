From point $A$ at an angle $\alpha$, two rays emerge. On one ray, two points $B$ and $B_{1}$ are taken, and on the other $-C$ and $C_{1}$. Find the length of the common chord of the circumcircles of triangles $A B C$ and $A B_{1} C_{1}$, if $|A B|-|A C|=\left|A B_{1}\right|-\left|A C_{1}\right|=a$.

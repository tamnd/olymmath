Circle $k$ is inscribed in the obtuse angle $\measuredangle X O Y$, such that $k$ touches the sides $O X$ and $O Y$ at points $M$ and $N$ respectively. On the larger arc of circle $k$, a point $P$ is chosen, and perpendiculars are dropped from $P$ to the sides of the angle and the segment $M N, P B \perp O X, P C \perp O Y$, $P A \perp M N$, where $A \in M N, B \in O X, C \in O Y$. Prove that

$$
\overline{P A}^{2}=\overline{P B} \cdot \overline{P C}
$$

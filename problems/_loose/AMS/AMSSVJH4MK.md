Suppose $a_1$, $a_2$, $\ldots$, $a_{10}$ are nonnegative integers such that $$\sum_{k=1}^{10}a_k=15 \text{and} \sum_{k=1}^{10}ka_k = 80.$$ Let $M$ and $m$ denote the maximum and minimum respectively of $\sum_{k=1}^{10}k^2a_k$. Compute $M-m$.

Let the medians corresponding to the sides $a, b, c$ of a triangle be denoted by $s_{a}, s_{b}, s_{c}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{s_{a}^{2}}{b c}+\frac{s_{a}^{2}}{c a}+\frac{s_{a}^{2}}{a b} \geq \frac{9}{4}
$$

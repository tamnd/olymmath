Boxes originally contained 7 small balls. A magician took out several balls from it, turned each ball into 7 balls, and put them back into the box. Then, he took out some balls again, turned each ball into 7 balls, and put them back into the box. This process continued, and at a certain moment, when the magician stopped taking balls and performing magic, the total number of balls in the box could be one of the following:
(A) 1990.
(B) 1991.
(C) 1992.
(D) 1993.
("Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

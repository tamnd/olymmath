Consider a rational number $r$ and natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{6}$, $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{6}$ such that $1 \leq b_{1}<b_{2}<\ldots<b_{6} \leq 11$ and

$$
r=\frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}=\frac{a_{2}}{b_{2}}=\frac{a_{3}}{b_{3}}=\frac{a_{4}}{b_{4}}=\frac{a_{5}}{b_{5}}=\frac{a_{6}}{b_{6}}
$$

Show that $r$ is an integer.

$\approx 、(50$ points $)$
Let $n$ be an integer greater than 1. Prove that if $\frac{1}{n}$ is a repeating decimal, and the repeating cycle is exactly $n-1$ digits long, then $n$ is a prime number (Note, for example, the repeating cycle of $\frac{1}{7}=0.142857142857 \cdots$ is considered to be 6 digits long, not 12, 18, 24, ... digits).

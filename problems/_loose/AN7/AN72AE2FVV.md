In a math club, there are $m$ boys and $n$ girls. The teacher has $m n$ cards, on which the numbers $1, 2, 3, \ldots, m n$ are written, each appearing once. He distributes the cards to the club members (until the cards or the members run out). In the end, each club member will have one card or none, if someone did not get a card. For each pair of boy-girl, the sum of the numbers on their cards is calculated and written on the board. For which $m$ and $n$ can the numbers on the board give different remainders when divided by $m n$?

If $P=\sqrt{\underbrace{111 \cdots 1}_{2 n \uparrow}-\underbrace{222 \cdots 2}_{n \uparrow}}(n$ is a natural number). Then
(A) $P$ is an irrational number.
(B) $P=\underbrace{111 \cdots 1}_{n \uparrow}$.
(C) $P=\underbrace{222 \cdots 2}_{n \uparrow}$.
(D) $P=\underbrace{333 \cdots 3}_{n \uparrow}$.
(E) $P=\underbrace{777 \cdots 7}_{n \uparrow}$.
(Chongqing City, Sichuan Province, Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

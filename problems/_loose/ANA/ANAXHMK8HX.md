Let $x_1,x_2,\ldots, x_{2007}$ be real numbers such that $-1\leq x_i\leq 1$ for $1\leq i\leq 2007$, and $$\sum_{i=1}^{2007}x_i^3 = 0.$$ Find the maximum possible value of $\Big\lfloor\sum_{i=1}^{2007}x_i\Big\rfloor$.

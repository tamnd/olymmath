Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be algebraic numbers related by the equation $\varphi(\alpha, \beta)=0$, where $\varphi$ is a polynomial with rational coefficients. Prove that then for any number $\alpha_{i}$ conjugate to $\alpha$, there exists a number $\beta_{j}$ conjugate to $\beta$ such that $\varphi\left(\alpha_{i}, \beta_{j}\right)=0$.

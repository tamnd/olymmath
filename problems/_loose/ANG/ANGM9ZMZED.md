On the plane, a line is given. Using a compass, find two points on a line perpendicular to the given one. It is allowed to draw the compass so that the resulting circle passes through a given point or through two given points (the distance between which is less than the diameter of the compass). It is assumed that it is not possible to draw the compass so that the circle touches the given line.

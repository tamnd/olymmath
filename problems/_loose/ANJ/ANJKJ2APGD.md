For $0<x<1,$ let $f(x)=\int_0^x \frac{dt}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}\ dt$

(1) Find $\frac{d}{dx} f(\sqrt{1-x^2})$

(2) Find $f\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)$

(3) Prove that $f(x)+f(\sqrt{1-x^2})=\frac{\pi}{2}$

Carla has forgotten the login password for her brand new computer! However, she remembers that it is a sequence of 4 vowels, not necessarily distinct, of which two are uppercase and two are lowercase. How many different passwords, at most, does Carla need to try to turn on the computer?
(A) $3 \cdot 5^{4}$,
(B) $5^{5}$,
(C) $6 \cdot 5^{4}$,
(D) $5^{6}$,
(E) $3 \cdot 5^{6}$.

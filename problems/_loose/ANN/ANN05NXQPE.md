In the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, $A C$ bisects $\angle B A D$. A line $C E \perp A B$ is drawn through $C$ at $E$, and $A E=\frac{1}{2}(A B+A D)$. Then, the relationship between $\angle A B C$ and $\angle A D C$ is
(A) $\angle A B C=\angle A D C$.
(B) $\angle A B C+\angle A D C=180^{\circ}$.
(C) $\angle A B C+\angle A D C=150^{\circ}$.
(D) $\angle A B C \neq \angle A D C$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

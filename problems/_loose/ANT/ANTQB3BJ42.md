Given a sequence of random variables $X_{1}, X_{2}$, $\ldots, X_{n}$, where the discrete random variable $X_{n}$ can take three values: $-n \alpha, 0$ and $n \alpha$ ( $\alpha$ - a constant) with probabilities respectively $\frac{1}{2 n^{2}}, 1-\frac{1}{n^{2}}$, $\frac{1}{2 n^{2}}$. Is the law of large numbers (Chebyshev's theorem) applicable to this sequence?

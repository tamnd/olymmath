Feri made a triangle from thin iron rods of the same thickness. He wants to mark its center of gravity, so he lays the triangle on a piece of paper and draws the lines connecting the vertices with the midpoints of the opposite sides.

Then Laci arrives, who says that this way he won't get the center of gravity. He suggests that they connect the midpoints of the sides, and the center of the circle that can be inscribed in the resulting triangle will be the center of gravity.

Who is right?

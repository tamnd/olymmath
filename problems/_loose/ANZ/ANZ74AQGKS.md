There are 12 teacups, numbered $1 \sim 12$, all with their mouths facing down. Now, they are flipped according to the following rules: the first time, all the cups are flipped; the second time, only the cups with even numbers are flipped; the third time, only the cups that are multiples of 3 are flipped; ...; the twelfth time, only the cups that are multiples of 12 are flipped. Prove that after these twelve flips, the mouths of all cups with numbers that are perfect squares are facing up.

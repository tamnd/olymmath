A work collective wants to participate in the Tele-Lotto 5 out of 35 together. Colleagues $A, B, C$ are tasked with selecting the numbers on the betting slips to be submitted. During their discussion on which tips they want to compile, each of the three colleagues has certain requirements.

So, $A$ demands that each tip contains three prime numbers whose sum is 42. $B$ requires that each tip contains three numbers whose product is 33 times their sum. $C$ expects that each tip contains two numbers that are not prime numbers.

Determine all the tips that meet the requirements of all three colleagues.

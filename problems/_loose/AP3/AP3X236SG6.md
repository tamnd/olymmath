Let $p$ be an odd prime. Show that $\frac{1}{\pi}\cdot\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{p}\right)$ is irrational. (Note: $\cos^{-1}\left(x\right)$ is defined to be the unique $y$ with $0\leq y\leq\pi$ such that $\cos\left(y\right)=x$.)

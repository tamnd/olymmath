Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, meaning all its angles are acute, which implies that its orthocenter $H$ is inside the triangle. Let $a = BC, b = CA, c = AB$ be the lengths of the sides, $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$ respectively, $m_{a} = A H_{A}$, $m_{b} = B H_{B}$, $m_{c} = C H_{C}$ the lengths of the altitudes, and $d_{a} = A H$, $d_{b} = B H$, $d_{c} = C H$ the distances from the vertices to the orthocenter.

Show that: $m_{a} d_{a} + m_{b} d_{b} + m_{c} d_{c} = \frac{a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2}}{2}$

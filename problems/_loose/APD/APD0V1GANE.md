András fills in the faces with the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 18$ (each number is written on exactly one face and each face gets exactly one number). Then Béla chooses one of the dice, and András selects one from the remaining two. Both roll their chosen dice once. The one who rolls the higher number wins. Can András number the dice in such a way that the game favors him?

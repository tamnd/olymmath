Given an arbitrary triangle $A B C$. The bisector of the interior angle $B A C$ intersects the side $B C$ at a point we will denote as $U$. Prove the equality $|A U|^{2}=|A B| \cdot|A C|-|B U| \cdot|C U|$. Does this equality hold if we replace the point $U$ with another internal point on the side $B C$?

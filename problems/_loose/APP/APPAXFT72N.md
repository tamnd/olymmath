On the vectors $\overrightarrow{A_{i} B_{i}}$, where $i=1, \ldots, k$, regular $n$-gons $A_{i} B_{i} C_{i} D_{i} \ldots(n \geqslant 4)$ are constructed, all with the same orientation. Prove that the $k$-gons $C_{1} \ldots C_{k}$ and $D_{1} \ldots D_{k}$ are regular and have the same orientation if and only if the $k$-gons $A_{1} \ldots A_{k}$ and $B_{1} \ldots B_{k}$ are regular and have the same orientation.

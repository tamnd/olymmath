Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=2$, for any $n \in \mathbf{N}$, there is $a_{n}>0$, and $(n+1) a_{n}^{2}+a_{n} a_{n-1}-$ $n a_{n-1}^{2}=0$. It is also known that the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $b_{n}=2^{n-1}+1$.
（1）Find the general term $a_{n}$ of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and its sum of the first $n$ terms $S_{n}$;
（2）Find the sum of the first $n$ terms $T_{n}$ of the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$;
（3）Conjecture the relationship between $S_{n}$ and $T_{n}$, and explain the reason.

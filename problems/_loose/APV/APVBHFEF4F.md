Let $n$ be a positive integer. The real numbers $a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n$ and $r_1,r_2,\cdots,r_n$ are such that $a_1\leq a_2\leq \cdots \leq a_n$ and $0\leq r_1\leq r_2\leq \cdots \leq r_n$ .
Prove that $\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n a_i a_j \min (r_i,r_j)\geq 0$

Consider $2 n$ points in the plane, no three of which are collinear. Suppose that $n$ are colored red, and the other $n$ are colored blue. Show that it is possible to draw $n$ segments, pairwise without common points, such that each red point is connected to a blue point and each blue point is connected to a red point.

Given a circle with radius $r$ and one of its diameters $A B$. A tangent to the circle is drawn at point $B$, and a line through point $A$ intersects the circle at point $C$ and the tangent at point $D$. A line parallel to the tangent $B D$ is drawn through $C$, and a line parallel to the diameter $A B$ is drawn through $D$. These two lines intersect at point $E$. Determine the geometric locus of point $E$ if the chord $A C$ rotates around $A$.

An infinite sequence of real numbers $a_n$ ( $n\in N$ ) satisfies the condition $$ a_{k^2+m^2} = a^2_k + a^2_m \,\, (k\in N, m \in N) $$ (so, for example, $a_{10} = a_{1^2+3^2} = a^2_1 + a^2 _3$ ). Can or cannot the first term $a_1$ of such a sequence be equal to the number:
a) $0$ ?
b) $1$ ?
c) $ -1$ ?
d) $1/3$ ?
e) $-1/3$ ?

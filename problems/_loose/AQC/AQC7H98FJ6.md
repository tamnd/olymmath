Given $n$ lines in a plane, $n>2$, such that no two are parallel and no three intersect at the same point. These lines divide the plane into disjoint regions (some of which are finite and some are infinite). The order of a region is defined as the number of lines that contain some part of the boundary of that region. Prove that the number of regions of order 3 is at least 4 more than the number of regions of order strictly greater than 4.

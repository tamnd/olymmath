Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $H$ as its orthocenter, and let $D, E$ and $F$ be the midpoints of the segments $[A B],[A C]$ and $[A H]$, respectively. The symmetrics of $B$ and $C$ with respect to $F$ are $P$ and $Q$, respectively.

(alph*) Show that the lines $(P E)$ and $(Q D)$ intersect on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$.

(alph*) Show that the lines $(P D)$ and $(Q E)$ intersect on the segment $[A H]$.

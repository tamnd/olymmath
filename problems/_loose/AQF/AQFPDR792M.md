For real numbers $ p,\ q,\ r$, define a polynomial with degree three : $ f(x)=x^3+px^2+qx+r$. For real numbers $ a,\ c$ and real numbers $ b\neq 0$, suppose $ a+bi$ and $ c$ are the roots of the equation $ f(x)=0$.

(1) For the graph of $ y=f(x)$, denote $ s(a)$ the slope of the tangent line at the point $ (a,\ f(a))$ and denote $ s(c)$ the slope of the tangent line at the point $ (c,\ f(c))$. If $ a\neq c$, compare the size of $ s(a)$ and $ s(c)$.

(2) In addition to, suppose $ a,\ c$ are integers and $ b\neq 0$ is integer. Prove the following contexts.

(i) $ p,\ q,\ r$ are all integers.

(ii) If $ p$ is a multiple of $ 2$ and $ q$ is a multiple of $ 4$, then $ a,\ b,\ c$ are all a multiple of $ 2$.

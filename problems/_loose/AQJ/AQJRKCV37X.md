Find all natural $n$ and $k$ such that for all $a, b \geqslant 0$ the inequality

$$
a^{k} b^{k}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)^{n} \leqslant \frac{(a+b)^{2 k+2 n}}{2^{2 k+n}}
$$

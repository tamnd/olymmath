Let $S=\left\{a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\}$, where $a_{i}$ are distinct natural numbers. The sum of the numbers from no proper subset of the set $S$ is divisible by $n$. Prove that the sum of all numbers in the set $S$ is divisible by $n$.

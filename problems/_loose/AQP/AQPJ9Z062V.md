In the popular game "Minefield," some of the fields on an $a \times b(a, b \in \mathbb{N})$ board contain mines, and on each of the remaining fields, a number is written indicating the number of adjacent fields that contain mines (adjacent fields are those that share at least one corner). Investigate whether there exist numbers $a$ and $b$ and an arrangement of mines on the $a \times b$ board such that exactly 2019 fields do not have a mine and that on all of them, the number written is:
a) 3;
b) 4;
c) 7.

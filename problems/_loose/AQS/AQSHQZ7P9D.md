Given that $T$ is a point inside $\triangle A B C$, the reflections of $T$ over $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ are $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$, respectively. The circumcircle of $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is $\Gamma$. The second intersections of lines $A_{1} T$, $B_{1} T$, and $C_{1} T$ with circle $\Gamma$ are $A_{2}$, $B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$, respectively. Prove that $A A_{2}$, $B B_{2}$, and $C C_{2}$ are concurrent at a point on circle $\Gamma$.

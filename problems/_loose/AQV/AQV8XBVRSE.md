On a straight line, points $A, B, C$, and $D$ are sequentially arranged, such that $|A C|=\alpha|A B|, |A D|=\beta|A B|$. A circle is drawn through $A$ and $B$, and $C M$ and $D N$ are two tangents to this circle ( $M$ and $N$ are points on the circle, lying on opposite sides of the line $A B$ ). In what ratio does the line $M N$ divide the segment $A B$?

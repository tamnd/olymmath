Let us call a natural number $n$ good if its decimal expression has more than digits 5 than digits 2, and bad if its decimal expression has more than digits 5 than digits 2. For example, the number $2025$ is good, the number $543$ is bad, and the numbers $25852$ and $67$ are neither good nor bad. There are $a$ good natural numbers and $b$ bad natural numbers less than $202500$ . Determine the value of the difference $ a - b$ .

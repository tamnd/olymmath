The following equations are given:

$$
x^{2}+p x+q=0
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
y^{2}+\left(k+\frac{1}{k}\right) p y+p^{2}+q\left(k-\frac{1}{k}\right)^{2}=0 \\
z^{2}-y_{1} z+q=0, z^{2}-y_{2} z+q=0
\end{gathered}
$$

where $y_{1}$ and $y_{2}$ are the roots of the second equation. Show that if the roots of the first equation are real, then the roots of the other equations are also real. Denoting the roots of the first equation by $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$, express the roots of the other equations in terms of $x_{1}, x_{2}$, and $k$.

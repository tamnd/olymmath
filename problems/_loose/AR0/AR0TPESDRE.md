Given the inscribed acute $\triangle A B C$ in $\odot O$, the distances from point $O$ to the three sides $a, b, c$ of $\triangle A B C$ are $H_{a}, H_{b}, H_{c}$ respectively. Try to prove: the radius $R$ of $\odot O$ is a root of the equation $x^{3}-\left(H_{a}^{2}+H_{b}^{2}+H_{c}^{2}\right) x-2 H_{a} H_{b} H_{c}=0$.
(Mathematics Bulletin, Issue 11, 1991 Problem Solving Question)

$A B$ is a chord of a circle, dividing it into two segments. $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of the arcs into which points $A$ and $B$ divide the circle. When the circle is rotated around point $A$ by a certain angle, point $B$ moves to point $B^{\prime}$, and point $M$ moves to point $M^{\prime}$. Prove that the segments connecting the midpoint of segment $B B^{\prime}$ with points $M^{\prime}$ and $N$ are perpendicular.

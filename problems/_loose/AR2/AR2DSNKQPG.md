Question 1 Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be complex numbers. Is there necessarily a $k \in\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, such that
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|a_{i}-a_{k}\right| \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|b_{i}-a_{k}\right| ?
$$

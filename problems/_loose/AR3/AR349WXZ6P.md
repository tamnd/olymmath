The side of the square lying at the base of a regular quadrilateral pyramid is equal to $a$. A regular quadrilateral prism is inscribed in the pyramid; the vertices of the upper base lie on the lateral edges, and the vertices of the lower base lie in the plane of the pyramid's base. The diagonal of the prism forms an angle $\varphi$ with the base plane. Find the volume of the prism if the lateral edge of the pyramid forms an angle $\alpha$ with the base plane.

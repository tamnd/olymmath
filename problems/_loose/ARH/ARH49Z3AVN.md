Let $n\ge3$ be integer. Assume that inside a big circle, exactly $n$ small circles of radius $r$ can be drawn so that each small circle touches the big circle and also touches both its adjacent small circles. Then, the radius of big circle is
$\textbf{(A)}~r\csc\frac{\pi}n$
$\textbf{(B)}~r\csc\left(1+\frac{2\pi}n\right)$
$\textbf{(C)}~r\csc\left(1+\frac{\pi}{2n}\right)$
$\textbf{(D)}~r\csc\left(1+\frac{\pi}n\right)$

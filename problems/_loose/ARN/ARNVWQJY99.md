In a convex $(2n+1)$-gon, each vertex is colored with one of three colors such that no two adjacent vertices have the same color. Prove that the convex $(2n+1)$-gon can be completely divided into triangles by non-intersecting diagonals inside the polygon, such that the 3 vertices of each triangle are all different colors.

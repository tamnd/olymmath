In the right-angled triangle $ABC$, the hypotenuse is $AB$, and the projection of vertex $C$ onto the hypotenuse is $D$. On the line $AB$, there are points $E$ and $F$ such that the magnitude and direction of $ED$ and $DF$ are equal to $AB$. The line through $E$ parallel to $AC$ and the line through $F$ parallel to $BC$ intersect at point $G$. Express the distance $CG$ in terms of the sides of the triangle.

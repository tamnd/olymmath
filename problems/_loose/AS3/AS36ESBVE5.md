(b) Conrad began the race at 8:00 a.m. and completed the swimming portion in 30 minutes. Conrad biked 12 times as fast as he swam, and ran 3 times as fast as he swam. At what time did he finish the race?
(c) Alistair and Salma also began the race at 8:00 a.m. Alistair finished the swimming portion in 36 minutes, and then biked at $28 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. Salma finished the swimming portion in 30 minutes, and then biked at $24 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. Alistair passed Salma during the bike portion. At what time did Alistair pass Salma?

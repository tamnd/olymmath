Let $x,y,z$ be nonzero numbers, not necessarily real, such that
$$(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2=24yz$$
and
$$\tfrac{x^2}{yz}+\tfrac{y^2}{zx}+\tfrac{z^2}{xy}=3.$$
Compute $\tfrac{x^2}{yz}$ .

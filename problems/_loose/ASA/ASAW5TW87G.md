Let $r$ polynomials $p_{1}(x), p_{2}(x), \cdots, p_{r}(x)$ have degrees $n_{1}, n_{2}, \cdots, n_{r}$, respectively, and $n_{1}+n_{2}+\cdots+n_{r}<\frac{1}{2}(r-1)$. Prove that there exist not all zero $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{r}$, such that $\alpha_{1} p_{1}(x)+\alpha_{2} p_{2}(x)+\cdots+\alpha_{r} p_{r}(x) \equiv 0$.

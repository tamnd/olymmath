Determine the truth value of the following statements:

a) In the arithmetic progression $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}, a_{n}=2 n+1$, the terms that are perfect squares have even ranks.

b) In the arithmetic progression $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}, a_{n}=6 n-5$, the terms that are perfect squares have odd ranks.

c) There exist arithmetic progressions $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$, of non-zero natural numbers, with $a_{1}=2015$ and the even perfect square terms have odd ranks.

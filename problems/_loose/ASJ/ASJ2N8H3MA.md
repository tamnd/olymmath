Around a circle, 12 fields are arranged. Among these, four adjacent fields are occupied by 4 different figures: a red, a yellow, a green, and a blue one in sequence. Each figure can be moved by jumping over four adjacent fields to land on the fifth, provided that this field is not already occupied by another figure.

After a certain number of moves, the figures return to the four fields they originally occupied, but in a different order. In how many different orders can they return to their original positions?

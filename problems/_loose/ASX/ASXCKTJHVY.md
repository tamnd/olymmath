A rectangular tablecloth is made up of equally sized squares of white, gray, and black colors, arranged such that

- squares sharing a side have different colors,
- white squares do not share a vertex,
- black squares do not share a vertex,
- there are six black squares,
- on each side of the tablecloth, there are at least three squares.

What could the tablecloth look like? Find and draw at least three possibilities.

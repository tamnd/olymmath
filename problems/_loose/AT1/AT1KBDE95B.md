In a club, every pair of people are either mutually friendly or mutually hostile, the club has $n$ people and $q$ friendly pairs, and among any three people, there must be a pair who are hostile. Prove: there is at least one member in the club whose set of enemies contains no more than $q\left(1-\frac{4 q}{n^{2}}\right)$ friendly pairs.

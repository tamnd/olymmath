Happy Sums (from 8th grade, 4 points). In the "Happy Sum" lottery, there are $N$ balls numbered from 1 to $N$. During the main draw, 10 balls are randomly drawn. During the additional draw, 8 balls are randomly selected from the same set of balls. The sum of the numbers on the drawn balls in each draw is announced as the happy sum, and players who predicted this sum win a prize.

Can it be that events $A$ "the happy sum in the main draw is 63" and $B$ "the happy sum in the additional draw is 44" are equally likely? If yes, under what condition?

Let $p>5$ be a prime natural number. From the decimal representation of the number $\frac{1}{p}$, 2012 digits after the decimal point are removed at random. Show that the remaining number can be represented in the form $\frac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are natural numbers that are coprime, and $b$ is a multiple of $p$.

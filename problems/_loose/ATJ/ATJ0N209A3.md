Let's construct a 6-vertex 5-faced polyhedron with the following conditions:

1. one face of the polyhedron is a square with side length $a$;
2. all other edges of the polyhedron are equal to each other;
3. the angle between the square face and the two faces adjacent to two adjacent sides of the square adds up to $90^{\circ}$.

Prove that the diagonals of two faces of the polyhedron are of length $a$.

We will prove that if $n>m>0, a_{i}>0, i=1,2, \ldots, n$, then

$\left(a_{1}^{m}+a_{2}^{m}+\ldots+a_{n}^{m}\right)\left(a_{1}^{n-m}+a_{2}^{n-m}+\ldots+a_{n}^{n-m}\right) \geqslant n^{2} a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{n}$.

Let point $M$ be any point inside the convex polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, and draw perpendiculars from $M$ to the lines containing the sides of the polygon. Prove that among the feet of these perpendiculars, at least one point lies on the side of the polygon and not on the extension of the side.

A fishing cooperative wanted to get an estimate of how many specimens of a certain fish species, which have already reached a certain size, live in one of their ponds. (Hereafter, these will be referred to as suitable fish.) For this purpose, they marked 50 suitable fish from their daily catch and released them back into the water. The next day, in their catch from 3 different locations in the pond, they counted a total of 67, 94, and 43 suitable fish, respectively, and among these, 6, 10, and 3 were marked. How could they estimate the number of suitable fish after the second day's catch?

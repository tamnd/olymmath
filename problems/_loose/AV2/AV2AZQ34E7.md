We place three points $A, B$ and $P$ on a circle $\Gamma$. We denote $Q$ as the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto $(A B)$, $S$ as the projection of $P$ onto the tangent to the circle passing through $A$, and $R$ as the projection of $P$ onto the tangent to the circle passing through $B$. Show that $P Q^{2}=P R \times P S$.

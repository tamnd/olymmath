Let $x, y, z$ be three positive numbers such that $(x + y-z) \left( \frac{1}{x}+ \frac{1}{y}- \frac{1}{z} \right)=4$.
Find the minimal value of the expression $$E(x, y, z) = (x^4 + y^4 + z^4) \left( \frac{1}{x^4}+ \frac{1}{y^4}+ \frac{1}{z^4} \right) .$$

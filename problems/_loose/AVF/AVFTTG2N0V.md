A line $p$ is drawn through the midpoint $M$ of its height $O S$, which makes an angle $\beta$ with $O S$. Determine the length of the segment $A B$, of the line $p$, which passes through the cone, if the segment $C D$ of the line $p$, enclosed between the planes $\pi$ and $\Sigma$, has length $d$.

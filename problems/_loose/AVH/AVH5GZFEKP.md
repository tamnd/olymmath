Denote by $C$ the orthogonal projection of point $P$ onto line $A X$ and by $D$ the orthogonal projection of point $P$ onto line $B X$. Prove that line $C D$ passes through a point $Y$ that is independent of the choice of point $X$ on arc $\widehat{A B}$.

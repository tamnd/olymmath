According to the traditions of the Number Diviners tribe, the classification of years as lucky or unlucky is given. For example, 2013 is a lucky year because the first 2013 positive integers can be divided into at least two groups such that the sum and the number of elements in any two groups are equal. If this is not possible, then the year is marked as unlucky. Which years are the unlucky ones?

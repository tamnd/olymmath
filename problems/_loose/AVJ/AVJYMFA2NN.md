There is a stack of $n(n>1)$ cards. On each card, a positive integer is written. This stack of cards has the following property: the arithmetic mean of the numbers on any two cards is also equal to the geometric mean of the numbers on one or more cards in the stack. Find all $n$ such that it can be concluded that all the numbers on the cards are equal.

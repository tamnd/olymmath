Let $p$ be a prime number. The natural numbers $m$ and $n$ are written in the system with the base $p$ as $n = a_0 + a_1p +...+ a_kp^k$ and $m = b_0 + b_1p +..+ b_kp^k$ . Prove that $$ {n \choose m} \equiv \prod_{i=0}^{k}{a_i \choose b_i} (mod p) $$

Let $n\in\mathbb N$ and define $P(x,y)=x^n+xy+y^n.$
Show that we cannot obtain two non-constant polynomials $G(x,y)$ and $H(x,y)$ with real coefficients such that $P(x,y)=G(x,y)\cdot H(x,y).$

A rhombus $\mathcal R$ has short diagonal of length $1$ and long diagonal of length $2023$ . Let $\mathcal R'$ be the rotation of $\mathcal R$ by $90^\circ$ about its center. If $\mathcal U$ is the set of all points contained in either $\mathcal R$ or $\mathcal R'$ (or both; this is known as the *union* of $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal R'$ ) and $\mathcal I$ is the set of all points contained in both $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal R'$ (this is known as the *intersection* of $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal R'$ , compute the ratio of the area of $\mathcal I$ to the area of $\mathcal U$ .

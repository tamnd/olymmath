$\underline{\text { Baharev } \Phi .}$
A circle with center $I$, inscribed in the face $A B C$ of the triangular pyramid $S A B C$, touches the segments $A B, B C, C A$ at
points $D, E, F$ respectively. On the segments $S A, S B, S C$, points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are marked such that
$A A^{\prime}=A D, B B^{\prime}=B E, C C^{\prime}=C F ; S^{\prime}$ is a point on the circumscribed sphere of the pyramid, diametrically opposite to point $S$.
It is known that $S I$ is the height of the pyramid. Prove that the point $S^{\prime}$ is equidistant from the points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$.

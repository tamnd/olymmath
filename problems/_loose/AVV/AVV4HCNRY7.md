Thereafter, every July 1, he followed the same method to deposit money in the bank. Assuming the annual interest rate $r$ for the one-year fixed-term savings at the bank remains unchanged, by July 1, 2005, when he withdraws all the deposits and interest, the total amount of money he will withdraw is ( ) yuan.
A. $a(1+r)^{5}$
B. $\frac{a}{r}\left[(1+r)^{5}-(1+r)\right]$
C. $a(1+r)^{6}$
D. $\frac{a}{r}\left[(1+r)^{6}-(1+r)\right]$

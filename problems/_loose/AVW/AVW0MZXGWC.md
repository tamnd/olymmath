(Theorem of Schur). Let ( $\mathrm{P}_{1}, \ldots, \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{n}}$ ) be a partition of the set $\mathbb{N}^{*}$ of non-zero natural numbers. Show that there exists a set $P_{i}$ and two elements $x, y \in P_{i}$ such that $x+y \in P_{i}$.

One can then wonder about the possible generalization of this theorem to infinite graphs: if we color the edges of an infinite graph blue and red, do we get an infinite blue or red component? The answer is yes!

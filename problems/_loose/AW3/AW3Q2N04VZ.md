Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $\Omega$ be its circumcircle. Let $E$ and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from vertices $B$ and $C$, respectively. The altitude from vertex $A$ intersects the circle $\Omega$ again at point $D$. The tangents to the circle $\Omega$ at points $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $T$. The lines $(EF)$ and $(BC)$ intersect at point $P$. Show that the lines $(DT)$ and $(AP)$ intersect on the circumcircle of triangle $AEF$.

Let $A 1, A 2, A 3, A 4$ be the four vertices of a triangular pyramid (four faces, tetrahedron), and if the edges $A 1 A 2$ and $A 3 A 4$ of the pyramid are of equal length, then the plane sections of the pyramid by planes parallel to the (opposite) edges $A 1 A 2$ and $A 3 A 4$ are parallelograms of equal perimeter.

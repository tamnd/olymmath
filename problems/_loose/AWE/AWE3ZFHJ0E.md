Let the sequence of real numbers $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$, given by $x_{1}=\alpha$, with $\alpha \in \mathrm{R}^{*}, \alpha \neq 2$ and $x_{n+1}=\frac{x_{n}^{2}+2 x_{n}+2}{x_{n}^{2}+1},(\forall) n \geq 1$. Calculate:
a) $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}$,
b) $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(x_{n}-1\right)^{n}$.

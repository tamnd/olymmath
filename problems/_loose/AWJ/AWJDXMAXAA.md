Let $ABCD$ be a circumscribed quadrilateral and $\omega$ its incircle, with center $O$. We denote $X$ as the intersection of $(AD)$ and $(BC)$. The circle $\omega_{1}$ is tangent to the extensions of $[AD]$ and $[BC]$ and to the side $[AB]$ at $K$. The circle $\omega_{2}$ is tangent to the extensions of $[AD]$ and $[BC]$ and to the side $[CD]$ at $L$. Suppose that $X, K$, and $L$ are collinear.

Show that $O$, the midpoint of $[AB]$, and the midpoint of $[CD]$ are collinear.

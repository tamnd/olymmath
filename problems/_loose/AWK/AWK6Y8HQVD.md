Let $P$ be a point inside the triangle $ABC$, and let the distances from $P$ to the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ be $p_{a}$, $p_{b}$, and $p_{c}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
P A \cdot P B + P B \cdot P C + P C \cdot P A \geq \left(p_{b} + p_{c}\right)\left(p_{a} + p_{c}\right) + \left(p_{b} + p_{c}\right)\left(p_{a} + p_{b}\right) + \left(p_{a} + p_{c}\right)\left(p_{a} + p_{b}\right)
$$

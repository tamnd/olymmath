Write down $k$ consecutive positive integers in ascending order to form a positive integer, which is called a $k$-consecutive number. For example, writing down 99, 100, 101 in sequence results in 99100101, which is a 3-consecutive number. Prove: For any positive integers $N, k$, there exists a $k$-consecutive number that is divisible by $N$.

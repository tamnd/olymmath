Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersecting at point $K$. Inside $\triangle A K D$, there exists a point $P$ such that
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle A P C=90^{\circ}+\angle A D C, \\
\angle B P D=90^{\circ}+\angle B A D .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: The diagonals of the quadrilateral formed by the projections of point $P$ onto the four sides of quadrilateral $A B C D$ are perpendicular.

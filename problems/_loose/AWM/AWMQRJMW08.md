Determine, with proof, whether or not there exist positive integers $a, b$, and $c$ such that

$$
a b+b c=a c \text { and } a b c=10 ! .
$$

Remark. 10! denotes the factorial $1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdots 10$.

Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{gathered}
\binom{n}{0} x+\binom{n}{1} y+\binom{n}{2} z=\binom{n}{3} \\
\binom{n+1}{0} x+\binom{n+1}{1} y+\binom{n+1}{2} z=\binom{n+1}{3} \\
\binom{n+2}{0} x+\binom{n+2}{1} y+\binom{n+2}{2} z=\binom{n+2}{3} .
\end{gathered}
$$

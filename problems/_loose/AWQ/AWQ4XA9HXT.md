The sequence $\{a_n\}$ satisfies $a_0=1, a_1=2011,$ and $a_n=2a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$ for all $n \geq 2$ . Let
$$ S = \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_{i-1}}{a_i^2-a_{i-1}^2} $$
What is $\frac{1}{S}$ ?

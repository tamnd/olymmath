Given a sequence of natural numbers $ (a_i) $, such that for every natural number $ n $, the sum of the terms of the sequence that are not greater than $ n $ is not less than $ n $. Prove that for every natural number $ k $, one can choose a finite subsequence from $ (a_i) $ whose sum of terms equals $ k $.

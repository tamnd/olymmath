Circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with radii $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}\left(r_{1}<r_{2}\right)$ touch each other internally at point $P$. Let $q$ be a tangent to $k_{1}$, which touches it at point $R$, and is parallel to the common diameter of the given circles. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of intersection of the tangent $q$ with $k_{2}$. Prove that $P R$ is the angle bisector of $\angle M P N$.

In a certain country, there are 100 cities and 146 airlines. Any two cities are connected by bidirectional flights of one or several airlines. The cost of a flight between cities connected by flights of $k$ airlines is the same for all companies and is $\frac{1}{k}$. It turned out that there are no cities between which it is cheaper to travel with a layover than by a direct flight. Prove that you can find a route with one layover, both parts of which cost the same.

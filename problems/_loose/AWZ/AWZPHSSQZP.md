Let $P, Q$ be points taken on the side $B C$ of a triangle $A B C$, in the order $B, P, Q, C$. Let the circumcircles of $\triangle P A B, \triangle Q A C$ intersect at $M(\neq A)$ and those of $\triangle P A C$, $\triangle Q A B$ at $N$. Prove that $A, M, N$ are collinear if and only if $P$ and $Q$ are symmetric in the midpoint $A^{\prime}$ of $B C$.

Prove the following properties of the integer part of a number (see p. 37):
1) $[x+y] \geqslant[x]+[y]$
2) $\left[\frac{[x]}{n}\right]=\left[\frac{x}{n}\right], n$-integer
3) $\left[x+\frac{1}{2}\right]=[2 x]-[x]$
4) $[x]+\left[x+\frac{1}{n}\right]+\left[x+\frac{2}{n}\right]+\ldots+\left[x+\frac{n-1}{n}\right]=[n x]$.

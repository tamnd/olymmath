We will prove that if $k \geq 2$ is a natural number, and the natural numbers $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{k}$ have no common divisor greater than 1 with the natural number $q$, then the equation

$$
x_{1}^{p 1}+x_{2}^{p 2} \ldots+x_{k}^{p k}=y^{q}
$$

has infinitely many solutions in the natural numbers.

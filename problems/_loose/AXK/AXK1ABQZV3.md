Inside it, there are two metal spheres that: touch the side of the cylinder at two diametrically opposite points; touch each other and one of them touches the bottom of the cylinder. The spheres have radii of $5 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $7 \mathrm{~cm}$. The cylinder is filled with $4.5 \mathrm{~l}$ of water. Does the water cover the spheres? Justify your answer.

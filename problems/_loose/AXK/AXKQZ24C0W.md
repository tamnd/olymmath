Honza has a box of pastels in the attic of his cottage, containing 5 blue, 9 red, 6 green, and 4 yellow pastels. It is dark, the light in the attic is not working, and Honza does not have any source of light with him. Therefore, he cannot distinguish the colors of the pastels.

a) How many pastels does he need to take at minimum to be sure that he brings at least one pastel of each color?

b) How many pastels can he take at maximum to be sure that at least one pastel of each color remains in the box?

c) How many pastels can he take at maximum to be sure that at least five red pastels remain in the box?

Let $\triangle A B C$ be a non-equilateral acute triangle, and let $M, N, P$ be the midpoints of sides $B C, C A, A B$ respectively. The perpendicular bisectors of sides $A B, A C$ intersect $A M$ at points $D, E$ respectively, and the lines $B D$ and $C E$ intersect at point $F$ (inside $\triangle A B C$). Prove that $A, N, F, P$ are concyclic.

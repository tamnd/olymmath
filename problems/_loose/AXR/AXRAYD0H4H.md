Let $E$ be a $\mathbb{K}$-vector space of dimension $n$ (where $n$ is an integer greater than or equal to 1) and $f$ an endomorphism of $E$.

1) Show that if $\operatorname{Ker} f=\{0\}$ then $f$ is injective, and then show that $f$ is bijective.
2) Deduce that $f$ is bijective if and only if $\operatorname{Ker} f=\{0\}$.
3) Show that if $\operatorname{Im} f=E$ then $f$ is surjective, and then show that $f$ is bijective.
4) Deduce that $f$ is bijective if and only if $\operatorname{Im} f=E$.
5) Give then two sufficient conditions to show that an endomorphism of $E$ (where $E$ is a $\mathbb{K}$-vector space of dimension $n \geq 1$) is bijective.

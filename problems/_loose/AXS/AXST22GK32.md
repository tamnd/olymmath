From a point $P$ on the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$, draw two tangents to the circle $\odot O$ with the minor axis as its diameter and the origin as its center. The points of tangency are $A, B$. The line $A B$ intersects the $x$-axis and $y$-axis at points $M, N$, respectively, then $\frac{a^{2}}{|O N|^{2}}+\frac{b^{2}}{|O M|^{2}}=\frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}}$.

In the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, the intersection point of the lines $AB$ and $CD$ is $P$, and the intersection point of the lines $BC$ and $DA$ is $Q$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the points on the sides $AB$ and $CD$ respectively, such that $PE$ and $PF$ are the harmonic means of $PA$ and $PB$, and $PC$ and $PD$ respectively. Prove that the points $Q, E, F$ lie on a straight line.

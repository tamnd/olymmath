Consider the following procedure for constructing a regular 17-sided polygon $S_{17}$ inscribed in a given circle $k$.

"Let the center of $k$ be $O$, and one vertex of $S_{17}$ be $C_{1}$. Let $D$ be the point that divides the radius perpendicular to $O C_{1}$ into quarters, closer to $O$. Let the line that divides the angle $O D C_{1}$ into quarters, closer to $D O$, intersect $O C_{1}$ at $E$;

let $F$ be the point on the extension of the radius $C_{1} O$ beyond $O$, such that $\angle E D F = 45^{\circ}$;

let the circle with diameter $F C_{1}$ intersect the line $O D$ at $G$, and the circle centered at $E$ with radius $E G$ intersect the line $O C_{1}$ extended at $H$ and $J$;

then the perpendiculars to $O C_{1}$ at $H$ and $J$ intersect the circle $k$ at the points $C_{4}$ and $C_{15}$, and $C_{6}$ and $C_{13}$ of $S_{17}$. The midpoint of the smaller arc $C_{4} C_{6}$ is $C_{5}$, and the side of $S_{17}$ is the chord $C_{4} C_{5}$."

Is this construction exact, or is it an approximation?

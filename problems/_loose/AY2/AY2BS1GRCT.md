Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be a sequence of positive integers, each not greater than $M$, and satisfying
$$
x_{k}=\left|x_{k-1}-x_{k-2}\right|, k=3,4, \cdots, n \text {. }
$$

How many terms can such a sequence have at most?

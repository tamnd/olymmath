Find the number of ordered triples of sets $(T_1, T_2, T_3)$ such that
1. each of $T_1, T_2$, and $T_3$ is a subset of $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$,
2. $T_1 \subseteq T_2 \cup T_3$,
3. $T_2 \subseteq T_1 \cup T_3$, and
4. $T_3\subseteq T_1 \cup T_2$.

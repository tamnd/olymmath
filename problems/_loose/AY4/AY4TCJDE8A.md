If the sides of a triangle $a, b, c$ are opposite to angles $A, B, C$ respectively, and satisfy $a \cos A + b \cos B = c \cos C$, then this triangle must be
(A) An equilateral triangle.
(B) A right triangle with $a$ as the hypotenuse.
(C) A right triangle with $b$ as the hypotenuse.
(D) None of the above.
(Shanghai Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

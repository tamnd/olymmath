On the table stands a cylindrical can with water. The radius of the can's base is $R$. If a ball of radius $r$ is placed into the can, it will lie on the bottom of the can, and the water surface will rise so much that it will be tangent to the ball. Prove that the same will happen if, instead of the given ball, a ball of a different radius is placed into the can with the same amount of water. Find the radius of the new ball and determine the conditions under which it will be larger (or smaller) than the radius of the given ball.

Let $p > 3$ be a prime number, and let $F_p$ denote the (finite) set of residue classes modulo $p$ .
Let $S_d$ denote the set of $2$ -variable polynomials $P(x, y)$ with coefficients in $F_p$ , total degree $\le d$ , and satisfying $P(x, y) = P(y,- x -y)$ . Show that $$ |S_d| = p^{\lceil (d+1)(d+2)/6 \rceil} $$ .
*The total degree of a $2$ -variable polynomial $P(x, y)$ is the largest value of $i + j$ among monomials $x^iy^j$* appearing in $P$ .

Consider quadrilateral pyramids $MABCD$ with the following properties: the base of the pyramid is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, where $AB = BC = 1$, $CD = DA = 2$, and each of the planes of the lateral faces $MAB$, $MBC$, $MCD$, $MDA$ forms an angle of $45^{\circ}$ with the plane of the base.

a) Find the volume of such a pyramid if its height, dropped from vertex $M$, is $\frac{9}{5}$.

b) For what height is the volume of the considered pyramids maximized, and what is this maximum volume?

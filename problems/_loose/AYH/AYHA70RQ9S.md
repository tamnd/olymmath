A childcare worker at a kindergarten brings $n(n>1)$ identical rectangular cards and distributes them to $n$ children, one card per child. Each child divides the rectangular card they receive into several identical squares (the squares divided by different children can be different). It is known that the total number of squares is a prime number. Prove: The original rectangular cards were square-shaped.

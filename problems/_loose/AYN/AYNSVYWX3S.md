In an ellipse, (I) the necessary and sufficient condition for the line connecting one endpoint of a focal chord and a point on the corresponding directrix to pass through a vertex on the focal axis is that the line connecting the other endpoint of the chord and the point on the directrix passes through the other vertex on the focal axis; (II) the necessary and sufficient condition for the intersection point of the lines connecting the endpoints of a chord (which does not pass through the center or the vertices on the focal axis) with the vertices on the other axis to lie on the directrix is that the chord passes through the focus corresponding to that directrix.

We are given an acute triangle $ABC.$ Point $D$ lies in the halfplane $AB$ containing $C$ and satisfies $DB\perp AB$ and $\angle ADB = 45^\circ+\frac12\angle ACB.$ Similarly, $E$ lies in the halfplane $AC$ containing $B$ and satisfies $AC\perp EC$ and $\angle AEC=45^\circ+\frac12\angle ABC.$ Let $F$ be the reflection of $A$ in the midpoint of arc $BAC$ (containing point $A$ ). Prove that points $A,D,E,F$ are concyclic.

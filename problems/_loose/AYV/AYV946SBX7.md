Given the set $D=\left\{\left(x_{1}, x_{2}\right)\left|x_{1}\right\rangle\right.$ $\left.0, x_{2}>0, x_{1}+x_{2}=k\right\}$, where $k$ is a positive constant. Find the range of $k$ such that the inequality $\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}}-x_{1}\right)\left(\frac{1}{x_{2}}-x_{2}\right) \geqslant$ $\left(\frac{k}{2}-\frac{2}{k}\right)^{2}$ holds for any $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}\right) \in D$.

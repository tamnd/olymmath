Line $l$ is not perpendicular to plane $\Pi$, $l^{\prime}$ - its projection on plane $\Pi$. Let $l_{1}$ be some line in plane $\Pi$. Prove that $l \perp l_{1}$ if and only if $l^{\prime} \perp l_{1}$ (Theorem of Three Perpendiculars).

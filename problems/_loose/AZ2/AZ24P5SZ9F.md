Prove that for any positive numbers $a_{k}, b_{k}$ $(k=1,2, \ldots, n)$ the inequality

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{a_{k} b_{k}}{a_{k}+b_{k}} \leqslant \frac{A B}{A+B}
$$

holds, where $A=\sum a_{k}$ and $B=\sum b_{k}$.

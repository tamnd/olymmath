A circle $k$ has points $A$ and $B$ on it, from which we draw circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with radii equal to the length of the chord $AB$. Circle $k_{1}$ intersects circle $k$ at point $C$ (different from $B$), and circle $k_{2}$ intersects at point $D$; the second point of intersection of line $DC$ with circle $k$ is $E$. Is it always true that $DE$ is equal to the radius of $k$?

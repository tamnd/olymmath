For any positive integer $n$ , let $f(n)=\tau(n)+\sigma(n)$ , where $\tau(n)$ and $\sigma(n)$ denote the number of positive divisors of $n$ and the sum of positive divisors of $n$ , respectively.
(a) Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ , such that $f(n) \not\equiv f(n+1) \pmod 4$ .
(b) Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ , such that $f(n) \equiv f(n+1) \pmod 4$ .

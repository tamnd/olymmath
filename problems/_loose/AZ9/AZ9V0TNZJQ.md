A group of students from a class has collected chestnuts. When a classmate asks how many students are in the class and how many participated in the collection, the following answers are given:

(1) If 12 more students had participated, we could have collected $75\%$ more.

(2) If $75\%$ of the students in our class had participated, we could have collected one and a half times as much.

(3) It is assumed that each student collects the same number of chestnuts.

a) How many students participated?

b) How many students are in the class?

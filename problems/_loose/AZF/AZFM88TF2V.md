In the theory of concave mirrors, the formula is encountered:

$$
x=r \frac{\sin ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos x}
$$

For small $\alpha$, this formula is approximated with high accuracy by the formula $x=\frac{r \alpha^{2}}{4}$. Justify this. In what units of measure is the angle $\alpha$ expressed?

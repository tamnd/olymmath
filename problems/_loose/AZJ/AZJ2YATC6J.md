The intersection of lines $AE$ and $BG$ is point $F$. The line $GD$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle GCB$ and the circumcircle of $\triangle BDE$ again at points $L$ and $M$, respectively. Prove that points $A, B, F, L, M$ lie on a circle.

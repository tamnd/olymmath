Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a collection of subsets of $\mathbf{N}$, no one containing another. Let $C(\mathcal{F})$ consist of all subsets $M$ of $\mathbf{N}$ with the following properties:

(i) $M$ intersects every member of $\mathcal{F}$;

(ii) There is no proper subset $M^{\prime}$ of $M$ with the property (i).

Give an example of a collection $\mathcal{F}$ such that $C(\mathcal{F})$ is empty and 1989 belongs to exactly 1989 members of collection $\mathcal{F}$.

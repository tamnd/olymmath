The range of a list of numbers is the difference between the largest number and the smallest number in the list. For example, the range of the list $1,5,1,6,3$ is $6-1=5$.
(a) Determine the range of the list $7,13,4,9,6$.
(b) The list $11,5, a, 13,10$ has a range of 12 . Determine the two possible values of $a$.
(c) The list $6+2 x^{2}, 6+4 x^{2}, 6,6+5 x^{2}$ has a range of 80 . Determine the two possible values of $x$.
(d) The list $5 x+3 y, 0, x+y, 3 x+y$ has a range of 19 . If $x$ and $y$ are integers with $x>0$ and $y>0$, determine the values of $x$ and $y$.

A magician lays out 36 cards in a $6 \times 6$ square (6 columns of 6 cards each) and asks the Spectator to mentally choose a card and remember the column it is in. After this, the Magician collects the cards in a certain way, lays them out again in a $6 \times 6$ square, and asks the Spectator to name the numbers of the columns containing the chosen card in the first and second layouts. After the Spectator's answer, the Magician infallibly guesses the card. How should the Magician act to ensure the trick always succeeds?

(L. Emelyanov)

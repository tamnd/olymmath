Inside a circle of radius 1, there are $n$ distinct points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}(n \geq 2)$. For $i=$ $1,2, \ldots, n$, let $d_{i}$ denote the distance from $A_{i}$ to the nearest of the other points. Prove that

$$
d_{1}^{2}+d_{2}^{2}+\ldots+d_{n}^{2} \leq 16
$$

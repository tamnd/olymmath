Let $A$ be a complex $n \times n$ Matrix for $n >1$ . Let $A^{H}$ be the conjugate transpose of $A$ .
Prove that $A\cdot A^{H} =I_{n}$ if and only if $A=S\cdot (S^{H})^{-1}$ for some complex Matrix $S$ .

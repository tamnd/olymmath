The foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ is $G$, and the foot of the perpendicular from $B$ to the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ is $H$. Prove that the points $E, F, G, H$ lie on a straight line.

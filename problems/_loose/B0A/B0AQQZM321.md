In our city, there are three cinemas named after the cardinal directions. It is known about their opening hours that:

1. at least one cinema is open every day,
2. if the southern cinema is open, then the northern cinema is not open,
3. the northern and eastern cinemas are never open at the same time,
4. if the eastern cinema is open, then the southern or northern cinema is also open.

We went to the southern cinema and found out that it was closed. Which of the remaining cinemas is certainly open?

Find all pairs of positive numbers $x, y$ that are solutions to the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x \cdot y_{10}=195.6 \\
& y \cdot x_{10}=241.7
\end{aligned}
$$

The notation $z_{10}$ represents the number obtained by rounding the number $z$ to the nearest ten.

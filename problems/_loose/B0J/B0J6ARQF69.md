Prove: For any integer $k>2$, there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$, such that
$$
[n, n+1, n+2, \cdots, n+k-1]>[n+1, n+2, n+3, \cdots, n+k], \text { (*) }
$$

where $\left[a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{l}\right]$ denotes the least common multiple of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{l}$.

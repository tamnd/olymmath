The sphere $\omega$ passes through the vertex $S$ of the pyramid $SABC$ and intersects the edges $SA, SB$, and $SC$ again at points $A_1, B_1$, and $C_1$ respectively. The sphere $\Omega$, circumscribed around the pyramid $SABC$, intersects $\omega$ along a circle lying in a plane parallel to the plane $(ABC)$. Points $A_2, B_2$, and $C_2$ are symmetric to points $A_1, B_1$, and $C_1$ with respect to the midpoints of the edges $SA, SB$, and $SC$ respectively. Prove that the points $A, B, C, A_2, B_2$, and $C_2$ lie on the same sphere.

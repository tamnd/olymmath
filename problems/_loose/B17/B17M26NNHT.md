Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a finite commutative group and denote by $e$ its identity element. Let $M=\left\{x \in G \mid x^{2}=e\right\}$.

a) Prove that $M$ is a subgroup of the group $(G, \cdot)$;

b) Show that $\prod_{x \in G} x=\prod_{x \in M} x$;

c) We say that an element $a \in G$ has property "A" if there exists a subgroup $H$ of $(G, \cdot)$ such that $\prod_{x \in H} x=a$. Prove that the set $\{a \in G \mid a$ has property "A" $\}$ is a subgroup of the group $(G, \cdot)$.

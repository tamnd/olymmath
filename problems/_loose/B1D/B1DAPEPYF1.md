Let $ABC$ be a scalene acute triangle and $E$ an interior point of the median $AD (D \in BC)$. Let point $F$ be the orthogonal projection of point $E$ onto line $BC$, $M$ an interior point of segment $EF$, and $N$ and $P$ the orthogonal projections of point $M$ onto lines $AC$ and $AB$, respectively. Prove that the lines containing the angle bisectors of $\angle PMN$ and $\angle PEN$ do not have any common points.

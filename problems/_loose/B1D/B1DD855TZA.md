Solve the following system of equations in two cases, in one case considering $p$ and $q$ as unknowns, and in the other case considering $u$ and $v$ as unknowns,

$$
p u+q v=2\left(p^{2}-q^{2}\right),
$$

$$
\frac{v}{p-q}-\frac{u}{p+q}=\frac{p^{2}+q^{2}}{p q}
$$

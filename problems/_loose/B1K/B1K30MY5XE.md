A square is to be inscribed in a circular sector such that the following conditions are met:

(1) The boundary line of the circular sector - consisting of two radii and a circular arc - contains the four vertices of the square.

(2) The circular arc is divided into three equal segments by two of these vertices.

Investigate whether these conditions uniquely determine the size $\alpha$ of the central angle of the circular sector! If this is the case, specify this size!

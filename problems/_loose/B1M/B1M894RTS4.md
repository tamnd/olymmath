The acute angle of the rhombus at the base of a quadrilateral pyramid is $\alpha$. The ratio of the total surface area of the pyramid to the square of the side of the base is $k$. Find the sine of the angle between the apothem and the height of the pyramid, given that all its lateral faces are equally inclined to the base plane. What are the permissible values of $k$?

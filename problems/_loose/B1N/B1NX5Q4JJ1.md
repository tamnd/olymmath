Let $A$ be an arithmetic sequence and $G$ a geometric sequence with terms $a_{1}, a_{2} \ldots$, and $g_{1}, g_{2}, \ldots$ respectively. We know that $a_{1}=g_{1}=1, a_{2}=g_{2} \neq 1$ and $a_{m}=g_{3}$, where $m>3$ is an integer. - Express the difference of $A$ and the quotient of $G$ in terms of $m$. - Show that $g_{4}$ is also equal to one of the terms of $A$. Show that every term of $G$ appears in $A$.

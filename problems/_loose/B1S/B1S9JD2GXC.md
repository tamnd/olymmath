Let's prove that in a triangle with sides $a, b, c$ inscribed in a unit circle,

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \begin{cases}>8, & \text { if the triangle is acute, } \\ =8, & \text { if the triangle is right, } \\ <8, & \text { if the triangle is obtuse. }\end{cases}
$$

Let $a$ , $b$ , $c$ be positive real numbers for which $$
\frac{5}{a} = b+c, \frac{10}{b} = c+a, \text{and} \frac{13}{c} = a+b. $$ If $a+b+c = \frac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ , compute $m+n$ .

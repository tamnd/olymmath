Prove the following properties of suitable fractions:

a) $P_{k} Q_{k-2}-P_{k-2} Q_{k}=(-1)^{k} a_{k}(k \geq 2)$

b) $\frac{P_{k}}{Q_{k}}-\frac{P_{k-1}}{Q_{k-1}}=\frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{Q_{k} Q_{k-1}}(k \geq 1)$

c) $Q_{1}<Q_{2}<\ldots<Q_{n}$;

d) $\frac{P_{0}}{Q_{0}}<\frac{P_{2}}{Q_{2}}<\frac{P_{4}}{Q_{4}}<\ldots \leq \frac{P_{n}}{Q_{n}} \leq \ldots<\frac{P_{5}}{Q_{5}}<\frac{P_{3}}{Q_{3}}<\frac{P_{1}}{Q_{1}}$

e) $\frac{P_{2 k}}{Q_{2 k}}<\frac{P_{2 l+1}}{Q_{2 l+1}}(k, l \geq 0)$.

Let $f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+a_{2} x^{2}+\ldots+a_{n} x^{n}$ be a polynomial with real coefficients $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$, where $n \geq 1$ and $a_{n} \neq 0$.

Set

$$
r=\frac{\left|a_{0}\right|+\left|a_{0}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{n-1}\right|}{\left|a_{n}\right|}
$$

and prove:

a) If $r \geq 1$, then every real root of $f(x)$ (if such a root exists) lies in the interval $-r \leq x \leq r$.

b) If $r \leq 1$, then every real root of $f(x)$ (if such a root exists) lies in the interval $-1 \leq x \leq 1$.

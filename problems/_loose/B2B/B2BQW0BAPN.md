Assume that at the end of a year, a unit of money has depreciated by $10 \%$ compared to the beginning of the year. Find the smallest integer $n$ such that after $n$ years, a unit of money has depreciated by at least $90 \%$. $n$ is (the value of $\lg 3$ to three decimal places is 0.477)
(A) 14.
(B) 16.
(C) 18.
(D) 20.
(E) 22.
(32nd American High School Mathematics Examination, 1981)

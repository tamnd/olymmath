Let $S$ be the unit circle in the complex plane (i.e., the set of complex numbers with modulus 1), and let $f$ be a mapping from $S$ to $S$. For any $z \in S$, define $f^{(1)}(z)=f(z), f^{(2)}(z)=f(f(z)), \cdots, f^{(k)}(z)=$ $\underbrace{f(f(\cdots f}_{k \uparrow f}(z)) \cdots))$.
(4th China Mathematical Olympiad)

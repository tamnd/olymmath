(B. Frenkin) A rook has traversed an $n \times n$ board, visiting each cell exactly once, with each move being exactly one cell. The cells are numbered from 1 to $n^{2}$ in the order of the rook's traversal. Let $M$ be the maximum difference between the numbers of adjacent (by side) cells. What is the smallest possible value of $M$?

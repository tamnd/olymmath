Show that if the real numbers $a, b, c \in [0,1]$, then

$$
\frac{3a^2 - 2a}{1 + b^3 + c^3} + \frac{3b^2 - 2b}{1 + a^3 + c^3} + \frac{3c^2 - 2c}{1 + a^3 + b^3} \leqslant 1
$$

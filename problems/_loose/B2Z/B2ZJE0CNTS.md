In the vertices of a regular pentagon, vectors $v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{5}$ point from the center. Suppose that for integers $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}$, $k_{4}, k_{5}$, it holds that $k_{1} v_{1}+k_{2} v_{2}+\cdots+k_{5} v_{5}=0$. Prove that in this case $k_{1}=k_{2}=\cdots=k_{5}$.

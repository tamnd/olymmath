How many different words $\left(a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \ldots a_{n-1} a_{n}\right)$ can be formed in total from the letters $a_{i} \in$ $\{1,2,3,4,5\}, i=1, \ldots, n$ such that

$$
\left|a_{j}-a_{j+1}\right|=1
$$

for $j=1, \ldots, n-1$?

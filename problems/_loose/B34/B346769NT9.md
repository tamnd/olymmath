At a school, there are three teachers for the subjects Biology, Mathematics, Geography, History, German, and English. Their last names are Schröter, Berger, and Müller. Each of these three teachers teaches exactly two of the mentioned subjects, and each of these subjects is taught by exactly one teacher. Furthermore, the following is said about these teachers:

(1) Two of the teachers, namely Mr. Berger and the History teacher, are related.

(2) Three of the teachers, namely Mr. Schröter, the German teacher, and the English teacher, often meet on their way to school.

(3) Mr. Schröter recently substituted for the sick History teacher.

(4) Mr. Schröter and the Mathematics teacher are neighbors in their gardens.

(5) Mr. Berger is older than the German teacher.

Investigate whether there is a distribution of subjects to the teachers that can satisfy all these statements, and whether the distribution of subjects is uniquely determined by the statements! If this is the case, provide this distribution!

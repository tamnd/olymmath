In the tetrahedron $PABC$, if the projection $O$ of point $P$ on the plane $ABC$ is the incenter of $\triangle ABC$, and $T_{1}, T_{2}, T_{3}$ are the projections of point $O$ on $AB, BC, CA$ respectively, the three dihedral angles of the trihedral angle $P-ABC$ are $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and the angle between $PO$ and $PT_{1}$ is $\theta$, then
(I) $\sin \theta=\left(\frac{\tan \frac{\alpha+\beta-\gamma}{2} \cdot \tan \frac{\alpha+\gamma-\beta}{2} \cdot \tan \frac{\beta+\gamma-\alpha}{2}}{\tan \frac{\alpha+\beta-\gamma}{2}+\tan \frac{\alpha+\gamma-\beta}{2}+\tan \frac{\beta+\gamma-\alpha}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$;
(II) $\tan \frac{\angle BAC}{2}=\frac{\sin \theta}{\tan \frac{\beta+\gamma-\alpha}{2}}$ ( $\alpha$ is the dihedral angle opposite to edge $PA$).

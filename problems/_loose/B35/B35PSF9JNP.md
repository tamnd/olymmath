Four friends, Anna, Bea, Caio, and Dino, are playing poker with 20 cards from the same deck: the four kings, the four queens, the four jacks, the four aces, and the four tens. Five cards are dealt to each. Anna says: "I have a four of a kind!" (four cards of the same value). Bea says: "I have all five hearts." Caio says: "I have five red cards." Finally, Dino says: "I have three cards of the same value and the other two also have the same value." We know that one and only one of these statements is false; who is lying?
(A) Anna,
(B) Bea,
(C) Caio,
(D) Dino,
(E) it is not possible to determine.

In triangle $ABC$ , $AB > AC$ . Its inscribed circle touches the segments $AB$ and $AC$ at points $D$ and $E$ in this order. The segment $BE$ meets the incircle for second time at point $K$ . Let $L$ be a point on the extension of segment $BE$ such that $AL \perp BE$ . Point $H$ is the intersection of the altitudes of triangle $KML$ , when $M$ is the midpoint of segment $DE$ . Prove that $\angle AHK = 90^o$ and that $\angle LKA = \angle MKD$ .

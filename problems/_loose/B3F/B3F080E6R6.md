Inside a square $ABCD$, an equilateral triangle $ABE$ is constructed. Let $P$ be the intersection point of the lines $AC$ and $BE$. Let $F$ be the point symmetric to $P$ with respect to the line $DC$. It is required to prove that:

a) the triangle $CEF$ is equilateral.

b) the triangle $DEF$ is right-angled and isosceles.

c) the triangle $BDF$ is isosceles.

d) the triangle $PDF$ is equilateral.

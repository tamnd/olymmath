In the plane, points $C, V, U$ are given such that $|C V|=3 \text{ cm}, |V U|=3.5 \text{ cm}$ and $|C U|=4.5 \text{ cm}$. Construct an acute triangle $A B C$ such that $V$ is the intersection of its altitudes and point $U$ is symmetrically associated with point $A$ with respect to the center of the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$.

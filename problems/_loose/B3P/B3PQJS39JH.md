a) One day, a guest came to the hotel owner, K, without money but with a silver chain consisting of seven links. The owner agreed to keep the guest for a week on the condition that the guest would give him one of the chain links as payment each day. What is the minimum number of links that need to be cut so that the chain owner can pay the hotel owner daily for seven days (possibly taking back previously given links and giving other ones in exchange)?

b) The chain consists of 2000 links. What is the minimum number of links that need to be cut so that any number of links from 1 to 2000 can be collected by taking some number of the resulting parts?

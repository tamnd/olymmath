a) Consider $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ to be a strictly concave function. Show that there do not exist four points on the graph of the function $f$ that form a parallelogram.

b) Show that:

$$
\left(\frac{\lambda_{1} \cdot x_{1}+\lambda_{2} \cdot x_{2}}{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}\right)^{3} \leq \frac{\lambda_{1} \cdot x_{1}^{3}+\lambda_{2} \cdot x_{2}^{3}}{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}, \forall x_{1}, x_{2} \in[0, \infty), \forall \lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2} \in[0, \infty)
$$

Generalize the inequality.

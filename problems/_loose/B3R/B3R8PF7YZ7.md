Prove that if in triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC = 60^{\circ}$, then the line connecting the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$ bisects the segment $AA'$, where $A'$ is the point where the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ intersects $BC$. However, if $\angle BAC = 120^{\circ}$ and $AB \neq AC$, then it bisects the segment $AA''$, where $A''$ is the point where the external angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ intersects the line $BC$.

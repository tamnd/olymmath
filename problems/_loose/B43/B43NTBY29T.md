Let $f:\mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{N}$ be a bijection satisfying $f(ab)=f(a)f(b)$ for all $a,b\in \mathbb{N}$ . Determine the minimum possible value of $f(n)/n$ , taken over all possible $f$ and all $n\leq 2019$ .

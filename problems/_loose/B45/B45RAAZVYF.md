Another inequality in a triangle. Let the bisectors of the internal angles of triangle $T$ be $\beta_{a}, \beta_{b}, \beta_{c}$, its medians - $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}$, and the radii of its inscribed and circumscribed circles be $r$ and $R$. Prove that

$$
\beta_{a}^{6}+\beta_{b}^{6}+\beta_{c}^{6} \leqslant p^{4}\left(p^{2}-12 r R\right) \leqslant m_{a}^{6}+m_{b}^{6}+m_{c}^{6}
$$

where $p$ is the semiperimeter of the given triangle $T$, and that equality is achieved if and only if triangle $T$ is equilateral.

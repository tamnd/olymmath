Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots-$ be a sequence of non-zero numbers such that $\left(a_{m}, a_{n}\right)=a_{(m, n)}(m, n \geq 1)$.

Prove that all generalized binomial coefficients $A_{n}^{k}=\frac{a_{n} a_{n-1} \ldots a_{n-k+1}}{a_{k} a_{k-1} \ldots a_{1}}$ are integers.

There are 2021 blue tits nesting in 120 trees. Trying to drive them away, a clumsy hunter shoots at them. Each time, he misses his target but startles a blue tit, which then leaves its tree and takes refuge on a tree with at least as many blue tits as its starting tree (it counts itself on the latter). Show that after a certain finite number of shots, all the blue tits will be on the same tree.

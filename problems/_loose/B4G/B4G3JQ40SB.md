We will use that $(a+b)^{2}=a^{2}+2 a b+b^{2}$. Remember that the area of a square with side $l$ is $l^{2}$. Note that the area of the gray region is the difference between the areas of the larger and the smaller square. The side of the larger one is $a+b$, so its area is $(a+b)^{2}$. The side of the smaller one is $a$, so its area is $a^{2}$. We conclude that the area of the gray region is: $(a+b)^{2}-a^{2}=a^{2}+2 a b+b^{2}-a^{2}=2 a b+b^{2}$.

Let $f(n)$ denote the number of ways a positive integer $n$ can be expressed as the sum of numbers of the form $2^{a} 5^{b}$ (the order of the terms does not matter) such that none of them divides the other ($a$ and $b$ are non-negative integers). Prove that $f(n)$ is unbounded.

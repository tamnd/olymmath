For a constant $\alpha>0$, take the points $O(0,\ 0),\ A(\alpha,\ \ln\ (\alpha +1)))$ on the curve $C: y=\ln\ (x+1)$.
Consider a moving point $P(t,\ \ln\ (t+1))$ with $0<t<\alpha$ on the curve $C$. Denote by $S_1$ the area of the part
bounded by the curve $C$ and the line $OP$, and by $S_2$ that of the part bounded by the curve $C$ and the line $PA$.

(1) Show that for $x>0,\ \frac{x}{x+1}<\ln\ (x+1)<x$ holds.

(2) Express $S_1,\ S_2$ in terms of $\alpha,\ t$.

(3) Find $t$ such that $S_1+S_2$ is minimized.

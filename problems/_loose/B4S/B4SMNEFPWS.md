Given a triangle $A B C$ and through its vertices $A, B$ and $C$ are drawn respectively the lines $x, y, z$, intersecting at point $S$. Denote $(B C)=a,(C A)=b,(A B)=c$. Draw the lines $a^{\prime}\left\|x, b^{\prime}\right\| y, c^{\prime} \| z ;$ these lines bound the triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}: A^{\prime}=b^{\prime} \cap c^{\prime}, B^{\prime}=c^{\prime} \cap a^{\prime}, C^{\prime}=a^{\prime} \cap b^{\prime}$. Through the points $A^{\prime}$, $B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ draw respectively the lines $x^{\prime}\left\|a, y^{\prime}\right\| b, z^{\prime} \| c$. Prove that these lines $x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}, z^{\prime}$ intersect at one point.

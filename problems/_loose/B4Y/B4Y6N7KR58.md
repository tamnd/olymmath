Let $B C E D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Rays $C B$ and $E D$ meet at $A$. The line through $D$ parallel to $B C$ meets $\omega$ at $F \neq D$, and segment $A F$ meets $\omega$ at $T \neq F$. Lines $E T$ and $B C$ meet at $M$. Let $K$ be the midpoint of $B C$, and let $N$ be the reflection of $A$ about $M$. Prove that points $D, N, K, E$ lie on a circle.

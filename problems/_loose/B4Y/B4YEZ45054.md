Let $Y$ and $Z$ be two points on the lines ( $A B$ ) and ( $A C$ ) such that the lines (BZ) and (CY) are parallel to the line $(A X)$. The line $(X Y)$ intersects the line $(A C)$ at $M$ and the line $(X Z)$ intersects the line $(A B)$ at $N$. Show that the line (MN) is tangent to the incircle of $A B C$.

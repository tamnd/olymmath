Let be given a triangle $ABC$ . Equilateral triangles $BCA_1$ and $BCA_2$ are drawn so that $A$ and $A_1$ are on one side of $BC$ , whereas $A_2$ is on the other side. Points $B_1,B_2,C_1,C_2$ are analogously defined. Prove that
$$S_{ABC} + S_{A_1B_1C_1} = S_{A_2B_2C_2}.$$

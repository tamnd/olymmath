Prove that the system of equations:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
X \cap(A \cup B)=X, \\
A \cap(B \cup X)=A, \\
B \cap(A \cup X)=B, \\
X \cap A \cap B=\varnothing
\end{array}\right.
$$

has a unique solution, and find this unique $X$.

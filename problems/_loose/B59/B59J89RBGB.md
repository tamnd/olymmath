Let $A B C D$ be a non-crossing quadrilateral. Let $I$ and $K$ be points in the plane such that $A B I$ and $C D K$ are equilateral triangles outward with respect to $A B C D$. Similarly, let $J$ and $L$ be points in the plane such that $B C J$ and $D A L$ are equilateral triangles inward with respect to $A B C D$. Show that $I J K L$ is a parallelogram.

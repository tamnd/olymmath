Let us call a natural number $m$ a floating point number if it can be written as the sum of several (not necessarily distinct) natural numbers such that the sum of all reciprocals is equal to $1$ . For example, $11$ is a floating point number because $$ 11 = 2+3+6 \,\, and \,\, 1=\frac12 +\frac13+\frac16 $$ a) Prove that $28$ is a floating point number.
b) Is $58$ a floating point number?
c) Is $65$ a floating point number?
d) Is $2009$ a floating point number?

Let $k$ be an integer number and $P(X)$ be the polynomial $$ P(X) = X^{1997}-X^{1995} +X^2-3kX+3k+1 $$ Prove that:
a) the polynomial has no integer root;
β) the numbers $P(n)$ and $P(n) + 3$ are relatively prime, for every integer $n$ .

A segment of length $3^{\mathrm{n}}$ is divided into three equal parts. The first and third of these are called marked. Each of the marked segments is then divided into three parts, of which the first and third are again called marked, and so on until segments of length 1 are obtained. The ends of all marked segments are called marked points. Prove that for any integer $k\left(1 \leq k \leq 3^{\mathrm{n}}\right)$, one can find two marked points, the distance between which is $k$.

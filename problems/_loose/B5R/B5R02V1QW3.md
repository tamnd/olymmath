Given a grid of squares on a plane (a "graph paper"). Is it possible to construct an equilateral triangle whose vertices would be at the nodes of this grid (at the points of intersection of the lines)?

$4^{**}$. Prove that the distance $d$ between the centers of the circumcircle and the incircle of a triangle is expressed by Euler's formula ${ }^{1}$ :

$$
d^{2}=R^{2}-2 R r
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle, and $r$ is the radius of the incircle.

$5^{**}$. Derive a similar formula for the distance $d_{a}$ between the centers of the circumcircle of radius $R$ and the excircle of radius $r_{a}$, which is tangent to the side $[B C \mid$ :

$$
d_{a}^{2}=R^{2}+2 R r_{a}
$$

Given a right-angled triangle $ABC$ with the right angle at vertex $B$. Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle, $M$ the midpoint of the hypotenuse $AC$, and $X$ the intersection of the line $IM$ with the line $BC$. Prove that if the points $B, I, M, C$ lie on the same circle, then triangle $ABX$ is isosceles.

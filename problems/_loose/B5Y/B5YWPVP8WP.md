A chess tournament was held in "rounds". These rounds - unlike the common practice - were set up so that in each round, each participant in the tournament played exactly one game (so an even number of players participated) and that in the entire tournament, each participant was scheduled to play exactly one game against every other participant.

Michael and Robert participated in the tournament for 5 rounds, after which they had to drop out. To not further disrupt the remaining tournament, the games they were supposed to play according to the tournament schedule were simply canceled.

Michael told his friends Herbert and Gerd that, as a result, a total of 38 games were played in the entire tournament (with no other withdrawals). Herbert said, "This number is not possible." Gerd countered, "Yes, it is, and if this number is correct, then Michael's statements uniquely determine whether Michael and Robert played against each other in the tournament."

Investigate whether Herbert's or Gerd's opinion is correct! If Gerd's opinion is correct, did Michael and Robert play against each other in the tournament?

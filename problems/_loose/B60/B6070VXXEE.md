In the garden, there are sunflowers, lilies, and peonies.
(1) In a week, there is only one day when all three flowers bloom simultaneously;
(2) No flower blooms for three consecutive days;
(3) In a week, the number of days when any two flowers do not bloom simultaneously does not exceed one day;
(4) Sunflowers do not bloom on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday;
(5) Lilies do not bloom on Thursday and Saturday;
(6) Peonies do not bloom on Sunday;
So, the three flowers bloom simultaneously on Wednesday $ $. (Monday to Sunday are represented by numbers 1 to 7).

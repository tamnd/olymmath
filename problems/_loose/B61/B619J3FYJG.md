Let $A$ be a corner cell of a chessboard, and $B$ be the cell adjacent to it diagonally. Prove that the number of ways to traverse the entire board with a crippled rook (moves one cell vertically or horizontally), starting from cell $A$, is greater than the number of ways to traverse the entire board with a crippled rook, starting from cell $B$. (The rook must visit each cell exactly once.)

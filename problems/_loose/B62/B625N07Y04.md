(German Mathematical Competition 2003) In parallelogram $A B C D$, $M$ and $N$ are on $A B$ and $B C$ respectively, and $M$ and $N$ do not coincide with the endpoints, $A M=N C$. Let $A N$ and $C M$ intersect at point $Q$. Prove: $D Q$ bisects $\angle A D C$.

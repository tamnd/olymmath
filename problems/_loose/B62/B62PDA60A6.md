An isosceles triangle $ABC$ with base $AB$ of length $10 \text{cm}$ and legs of length $20 \text{cm}$ is given. Point $S$ is the midpoint of the base $AB$. Divide the triangle $ABC$ into five parts of equal area using four lines passing through point $S$. Determine the lengths of the segments cut by these lines on the legs of the triangle $ABC$.

(E. Trojáková)

Idea. The given construction is axially symmetric.

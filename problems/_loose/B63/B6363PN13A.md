Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral. We denote $P$ as the intersection of $(A D)$ with $(B C)$ and $Q$ as the intersection of $(A B)$ with $(C D)$. Show that the circumcircles of triangles $C B Q, A P B$, $D C P$, and $A D Q$ are concurrent. Their point of concurrency (which we will denote as $M$) is called the Miquel point, and satisfies other properties.

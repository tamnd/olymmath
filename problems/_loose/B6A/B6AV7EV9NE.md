Let $ S $ be a set of $ n $ ($ n \geq 3 $) points in space. For each plane $ \pi $, consider the number $ R(\pi) $ equal to the sum of the distances from the points of set $ S $ to $ \pi $. Let $ \pi_0 $ be a plane such that the number $ R(\pi_0) $ is minimal. Prove that some three points of set $ S $ lie on $ \pi_0 $.

Given are $p$ and $q$ ellipses that do not intersect each other, and they do not even share a boundary point. It is to be proven that we can select a point $P$ on $p$ and a point $Q$ on $q$ such that the line $PQ$ is perpendicular to the tangent of $p$ at $P$ and the tangent of $q$ at $Q$.

A grasshopper moves on the plane and from the point with coordinates $(x, y)$ it can jump to either $(x+y, y)$ or $(x, x+y)$. It started from a point with coordinates $(n, 9)$ where $n$ is a positive integer, but it doesn't remember the value of $n$. It only knows that after a certain number of moves it arrived at $(2021,2050)$. How many possible values of $n$ are there?

Let $A B C D$ be a rectangle and let $E$ be an arbitrary point, different from $C$, on the side $D C$. Let $H$ be the projection of $E$ onto the diagonal $A C$ and let $K$ be the projection of $C$ onto the ray $A E$.

(a) Prove that $K$ lies on the circumcircle of $A B C D$ and that the quadrilateral $C K E H$ is cyclic, i.e., inscribable in a circle.

(b) Prove that $\widehat{C K B}+\widehat{C K H}=90^{\circ}$.

(c) Prove that $K, H, B$ are collinear if and only if $A B C D$ is a square.

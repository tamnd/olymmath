Behind three of the 25 squares of a scratch card with $5 \times 5$ squares a prize is hidden. It is only
known that those three squares are all next to each other and together form a $3 \times 1$ -rectangle
(horizontally or vertically). You pay for the scratch card an amount of euros that is equal to the
number of squares you are allowed to scratch open (so for 6 euros you are allowed to scratch
open 6 squares).
What is the least amount of euros you have to pay for the scratch card to make sure you win a
prize?

For a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, the extensions of $AB$ and $DC$ meet at $P$, and the extensions of $AD$ and $BC$ meet at $Q$. The circumcircles of $\triangle PBC$ and $\triangle QDC$ intersect at $R$. Then, $P, Q, R$ are collinear.

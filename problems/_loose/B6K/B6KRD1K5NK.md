With two cannons, which we always fire simultaneously, we shoot at a stationary target. The probability of a short shot, hit, and long shot for one cannon is $0.3, 0.2, 0.5$; for the other, it is $0.6, 0.1, 0.3$. What is the probability that
a) out of 20 pairs of shots, we hit the target with both cannons at least once?
b) out of 3 pairs of shots, we hit the target with at least one cannon every time?
c) out of 2 pairs of shots, there are at most 3 short shots?

d) How many pairs of shots must be fired so that the probability of "at least one double hit" is at least 0.9?

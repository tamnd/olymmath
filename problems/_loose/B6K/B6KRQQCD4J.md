In the plane $\alpha$, passing through the center of a sphere of radius $R$, a circle with center $O 1$ and radius $r 1$ is given, located inside the sphere. All points of this circle are connected by lines to a point $A$, which belongs to the sphere and is at a distance $R$ from the plane $\alpha$. The set of points, different from $A$, where these lines intersect the surface of the sphere, forms a circle with center $O 2$ and radius $r 2$. Find the distance from the point $O 2$ to the plane $\alpha$, if the distance between the points $A$ and $O 1$ is $a$.

Given a tetrahedron with four congruent faces such that $ OA = 3,\ OB = \sqrt {7},\ AB = 2$. Denote by $ L$ a plane which contains three points $ O,\ A,\ B$.

(1) Let $ H$ be the foot of the perpendicular drawn fron the point $ C$ to the plane $ L$. Express $ \overrightarrow{OH}$ in terms of $ \overrightarrow{OA},\ \overrightarrow{OB}$.

(2) For a real number $ t$ with $ 0 < t < 1$, let $ P_t,\ Q_t$ be the points which divide internally the line segments $ OA,\ OB$ respectively.
Denote by $ M$ a plane which is perpendicular to the plane $ L$. Find the sectional area $ S(t)$ of the tetrahedron $ OABC$ cut by the plane $ M$.

(3) When $ t$ moves in the range of $ 0 < t < 1$, find the maximum value of $ S(t)$.

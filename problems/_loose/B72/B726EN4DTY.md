(USA, 76). Let polynomials $P(x), Q(x), R(x)$, and $S(x)$ satisfy the identity

$$
P\left(x^{5}\right)+x Q\left(x^{5}\right)+x^{2} R\left(x^{6}\right) \equiv\left(x^{4}+x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1\right) S(x) .
$$

Prove that the polynomial $P(x)$ is divisible by the polynomial $x-1$.

A positive integer hides another number when, by erasing some of its digits, the other number appears. For example, the number 123 hides the numbers 1, 2, 3, 12, 13, and 23, but does not hide 32, 123, and 213.

a) What is the largest three-digit number hidden by 47239?
b) What is the smallest number that hides both 2009 and 9002 simultaneously?

c) Find a multiple of 2009 that hides 2009 and whose units digit is 3.

Tourist $B$, who was in city $N$, upon receiving a signal that $A$ had already traveled 7 km, immediately set off towards him and traveled 0.05 of the total distance between $M$ and $N$ each hour. From the moment $B$ set off until his meeting with $A$, the number of hours that passed is equal to the number of kilometers $B$ traveled per hour. Find the distance between the cities $M$ and $N$, given that it is not less than 100 km.

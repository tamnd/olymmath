a) Let $a, b \in \mathbf{R}, a < b$ and $x, y, z \in [a, b]$. Prove that the arithmetic mean of the numbers $x, y$, and $z$ is also in the interval $[a, b]$.

b) Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ be three non-collinear points. We denote $\overrightarrow{A_{1} A_{2}} = \vec{u}$ and $\overrightarrow{A_{2} A_{3}} = \vec{v}$. For any $n \in \mathbf{N}$, $n \geq 4$, we denote by $A_{n}$ the centroid of the triangle $A_{n-1} A_{n-2} A_{n-3}$, and for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, we consider $x_{n}, y_{n} \in \mathbf{R}$ such that $\overrightarrow{A_{1} A_{n}} = x_{n} \vec{u} + y_{n} \vec{v}$.

i. Calculate $x_{6}$ and $y_{6}$.

ii. Show that for any $n \in \mathbf{N}, n \geq 5$, we have $x_{n} \in \left[\frac{7}{9}, \frac{9}{9}\right]$ and $y_{n} \in \left[\frac{2}{5}, \frac{3}{5}\right]$.

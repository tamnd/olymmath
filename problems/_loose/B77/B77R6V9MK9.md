When flipped, coin A shows heads $ \frac{1}{3}$ of the time, coin B shows heads $ \frac{1}{2}$ of the time, and coin C shows heads $ \frac{2}{3}$ of the time. Anna selects one of the coins at random and flips it four times, yielding three heads and one tail. The probability that Anna flipped coin A can be expressed as $ \frac{p}{q}$ for relatively prime positive integers $p$ and $q$ . Compute $p + q$ .

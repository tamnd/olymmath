In school, all students are either excellent, good, or poor performers. 99 students stood in a circle. Each of them has at least one poor performer among their three left neighbors, at least one excellent performer among their five right neighbors, and at least one good performer among their four neighbors-two to the left and two to the right. Can there be an equal number of excellent and poor performers in this circle?

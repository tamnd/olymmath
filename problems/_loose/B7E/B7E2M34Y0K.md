On two identical circular pieces of paper, the artist drew two identical dragons. The eye of the dragon on the 1st piece of paper coincides exactly with the center of the circle, while the eye of the dragon on the 2nd piece does not. Try to prove that the 2nd piece of paper can be divided into two parts, such that when the two parts are reassembled into a circle with the same radius as the original, the eye of the dragon will also coincide with the center of the circle.

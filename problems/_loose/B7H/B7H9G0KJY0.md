Gaspard has $m$ balls in one bag and $n$ in another bag, where $m \geq 1$ and $n \geq 1$. He can then perform two types of operations:

1. remove an equal number of balls from each bag, and
2. triple the number of balls in one bag.

Under what condition on $m$ and $n$ can he chain operations to empty both bags?

Let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $C D$, and points $K$ and $L$ be the midpoints of diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ respectively.

Then $M K N L$ is a rectangle (segments $M L$ and $K N$ are both equal to half the side $A D$ and are parallel to it as the midlines of triangles $A D B$ and $A D C$; an analogous statement is true for segments $M K$ and $L N$ and side $B C$). In particular, its diagonals are equal.

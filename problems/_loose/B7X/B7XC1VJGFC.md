Consider the equation
$$ 3 \lfloor x \rfloor^2 = x \lfloor x \rfloor + 21 \{x\}^2, $$
where $\lfloor x \rfloor$ and $\{ x \}$ are the greatest integer part of $x$ and the fractional part of $x$ , respectively. The sum of all solutions to the equation can be written in the form $p/q$ , where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is the value of $p + q$ ?

Suppose it is known that for any positive integer $n$, there always exists a prime $p$ such that $n \leqslant p \leqslant 2 n$. Prove the following proposition: for example, if there exists a smallest even number $2 m_{0}$ greater than 2 that cannot be expressed as the sum of two primes, then $4 m_{0}$ must be expressible as the sum of three or four primes.
(Tianjin, China Mathematics Competition, 1979)

(Erdős-Mordell Inequality)
Let $P$ be a point inside or on the boundary of $\triangle A B C$, and the distances from $P$ to the three sides are $P D, P E, P F$,

Prove: $P A+P B+P C \geqslant 2(P D+P E+$ $P F)$.

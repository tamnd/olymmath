Let's prove that the perpendiculars erected on the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ at points $U$, $V$, and $W$ on these sides, respectively, intersect at a single point if and only if

$$
A W^{2} + B U^{2} + C V^{2} = A V^{2} + C U^{2} + B W^{2}
$$

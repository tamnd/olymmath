[8] Several weights have been weighed and their masses are recorded on labels. These labels are attached to the weights in a random manner. We have a balance in the form of a segment fixed at its midpoint. The weights are attached at arbitrary points on this segment, noting whether the balance is in equilibrium or not. Can it be determined with one weighing whether all the labels are attached correctly? (The balance is in equilibrium when the sum of the moments of the weights to the left of the midpoint and to the right of the midpoint is the same; otherwise, it tilts in the direction of the larger sum. The moment of a weight is the product of its mass $m$ and the distance from the center $s$.)

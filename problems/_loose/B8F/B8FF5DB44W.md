\section*{Task 4 - 011034}

A wristwatch has, in addition to the second hand attached to the lower part of the dial, a stopwatch mechanism with a second hand whose axis runs through the center of the dial.

When both hands are in motion, they rotate at the same speed. Since the stopwatch can be started at any time, the two second hands will usually not show the same second at the same time. We now imagine both hands extended indefinitely in both directions.

a) What is the geometric locus of all intersection points of the two rotating second hands or their extensions?

b) Construct this curve for the following case:

The distance between the pivot points of the hands $a=5 \mathrm{~cm}$ (deliberately chosen so large for better constructability)!

When the stopwatch is started, the small second hand points to the 10 on the second dial.

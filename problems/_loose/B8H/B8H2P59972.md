Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $A B \neq A C$ and center of inscribed circle $I$. The inscribed circle touches the sides $B C, C A$ or $A B$ at $D, E$ or $F$. Let $M$ be the center of $E F$. The straight line $A D$ intersects the inscribed circle at $P \neq D$. Prove that $P M I D$ is a chordal quadrilateral.

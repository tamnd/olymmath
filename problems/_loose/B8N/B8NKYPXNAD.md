Xavier and Yaël are playing a game. Each in turn, they write a 0 or a 1 on a vertex of an initially blank n-gon. Xavier starts and wins if, after one of his moves, there are three consecutive vertices of the n-gon such that the sum of the numbers written on them is a multiple of 3. Yaël wins if he prevents Xavier from winning. Determine who has a winning strategy for:

1. $n=2019$
2. $n=2020$
3. $n=2021$

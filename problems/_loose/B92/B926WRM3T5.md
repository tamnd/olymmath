For each natural $n \leqslant p-2$, the pair $(n, n+1)$ is of one of four types: $(R, R), (N, N), (N, R), (R, N)$, where $R$ stands for a residue and $N$ for a non-residue. Let $RR, NN, NR, RN$ be the number of all pairs of the corresponding type.

a) Prove that $RR + NN - RN - NR = 1$.

b) Let $\varepsilon = (-1)^{(p-1) / 2}$. Prove that

$$
\begin{aligned}
RR + RN & = \frac{1}{2}(p-2-\varepsilon), & RR + NR = \frac{1}{2}(p-1) - 1 \\
NR + NN & = \frac{1}{2}(p-2+\varepsilon), & RN + NN = \frac{1}{2}(p-1)
\end{aligned}
$$

c) Prove that

$$
RR = \frac{p}{4} - \frac{\varepsilon + 4}{4}, RN = \frac{p}{4} - \frac{\varepsilon}{4}, NN = NR = \frac{p}{4} + \frac{\varepsilon - 2}{4}.
$$

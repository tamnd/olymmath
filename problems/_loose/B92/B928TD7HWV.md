The distance from Maikop to Belorechensk is 24 kilometers. Three friends need to travel between the two places: two of them need to go from Maikop to Belorechensk, and the third one needs to go from Belorechensk to Maikop. They have only one bicycle, which starts in Maikop. Each of them can walk (with a walking speed of no less than 6 kilometers per hour) and can ride the bicycle (with a cycling speed of no less than 18 kilometers per hour). The bicycle cannot be left unattended (as it might be stolen), and two people cannot ride the bicycle at the same time. Prove: All three friends can reach their respective destinations in just 2 hours and 40 minutes.

Let $(\Omega, \mathscr{F})=((0,1], \mathscr{B}((0,1]))$, and $\mathrm{P}$ be the Lebesgue measure on $(0,1]$. Show that $(\Omega, \mathscr{F}, \mathrm{P})$ is a universal probability space in the sense that for any distribution function $F=F(x)$, one can define a random variable $\xi=\xi(\omega), \omega \in \Omega$, with distribution function $F$.

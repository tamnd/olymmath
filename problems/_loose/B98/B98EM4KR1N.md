On the plane, two circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ are given, touching each other externally. On circle $\omega_{1}$, a diameter $A B$ is chosen, and on circle $\omega_{2}$, a diameter $C D$ is chosen. Consider all possible positions of points $A, B, C$, and $D$ such that $A B C D$ is a convex circumscribed quadrilateral, and let $I$ be the center of its inscribed circle. Find the geometric locus of points $I$.

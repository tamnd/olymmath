Given $\triangle A B C$ where $\angle A=60^{\circ}$, the angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects side $B C$ at point $D$. Let the inradii of $\triangle A B D, \triangle A D C$, and $\triangle A B C$ be $r_{B}, r_{C}, r$ respectively, and $A C=b$, $A B=c$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{r_{B}}+\frac{1}{r_{C}}=2\left(\frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right) .
$$

Let $A$ be a set of $2n$ points in the plane, where no three points are collinear. Now, $n$ points are painted red, and $n$ points are painted blue. Try to prove or disprove that it is possible to find $n$ line segments that do not share any endpoints, where each segment has two endpoints of different colors from $A$.

(1) For any positive integer $n$, we have
$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{2 n}\right)^{n}<e
$$
(2) For any positive integer $n>1$, we have
$$
\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(\frac{k}{n}\right)^{n}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(\frac{k}{n}\right)^{n}}>e
$$

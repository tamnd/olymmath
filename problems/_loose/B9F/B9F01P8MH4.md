Let $P_{1}(x)=a x^{2}-b x-c, P_{2}(x)=b x^{2}-c x-a, P_{3}(x)=c x^{2}-a x-b$ be three quadratic polynomials where $a, b, c$ are non-zero real numbers. Suppose there exists a real number $\alpha$ such that $P_{1}(\alpha)=P_{2}(\alpha)=P_{3}(\alpha)$. Prove that $a=b=c$.

In the acute $\triangle ABC$, $AD$ is the internal angle bisector of $\angle A$, point $D$ is on side $BC$. Through point $D$, draw $DE \perp AC$, $DF \perp AB$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $E$ and $F$ respectively. Connect $BE$ and $CF$, they intersect at point $H$. The circumcircle of $\triangle AFH$ intersects $BE$ at point $G$. Prove that the triangle formed by segments $BG$, $GE$, and $BF$ is a right triangle.

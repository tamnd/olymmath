Two postal codes $M$ and $N$ on an envelope are both composed of the 6 different digits $0,1,2,3,5,6$. There are 4 postal codes as follows:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A: 320651, B: 105263, \\
C: 612305, D: 316250 .
\end{array}
$$

It is known that codes $A, B, C$ each have exactly two digits in the same position as $M, N$, and $D$ has exactly 3 digits in the same position as $M, N$. Try to find the codes $M$ and $N$.

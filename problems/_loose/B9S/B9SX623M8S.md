$n\in \mathbb{N}$ is called *"good"*, if $n$ can be presented as a sum of the fourth powers of five of its divisors (different).
a) Prove that each *good* number is divisible by 5;
b) Find a *good* number;
c) Does there exist infinitely many *good* numbers?

The circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ intersect at two points $A$ and $B$. Let $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$ be the tangents to $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$, respectively, at point $A$. Let the second intersection of $\omega_{1}$ and $t_{2}$ be $C$, and the second intersection of $\omega_{2}$ and $t_{1}$ be $D$. On the ray $A B$, beyond $B$, points $P$ and $E$ are given such that $\overline{A E}=2 \cdot \overline{A P}$. The circumcircle of $\triangle B C E$ intersects $t_{2}$ again at point $Q$, and the circumcircle of $\triangle B D E$ intersects $t_{1}$ again at point $R$. Prove that the points $P, Q$, and $R$ are collinear.

This problem was invalid on the contest. Correct formulation as of December 9. Let $\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}$, and $\omega_{3}$ be three circles passing through the origin $O$ of the coordinate plane but not tangent to each other or to either axis. Denote by $\left(x_{i}, 0\right)$ and $\left(0, y_{i}\right)$, $1 \leq i \leq 3$, the respective intersections (besides $O$ ) of circle $\omega_{i}$ with the $x$ and $y$ axes. Prove that $\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}$, and $\omega_{3}$ have a common point $P \neq O$ if and only if the points $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right),\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right)$, and $\left(x_{3}, y_{3}\right)$ are collinear.

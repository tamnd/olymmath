We call two people a friendly couple if they know each other, and we call them a non-friendly couple if they do not know each other (being friends or not being friends is always mutual). Let $m, n$ be natural numbers. Find the smallest natural number $k$ such that the following holds: In every group of $k$ people there are always $2 m$ people who form $m$ disjoint friendly pairs, or there are $2 n$ people who form $n$ disjoint nonfriendly pairs.

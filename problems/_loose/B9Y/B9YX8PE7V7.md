$F$ is a family of some subsets of the set $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, satisfying: (1) If $A \in F$, then $|A|=3 ;$ (2) If $A \in F, B \in F, A \neq B$, then $|A \cap B| \leqslant 1$. Let $f(n)=\max |F|$, prove: When $n \geqslant 3$, $\frac{1}{6}\left(n^{2}-4 n\right) \leqslant f(n) \leqslant \frac{1}{6}\left(n^{2}-n\right)$.

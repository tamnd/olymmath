Let $A \subseteq \mathbf{N}^*, A$ be an infinite set, and for any $a \in A, a$ is the product of at most 2000 different prime numbers. Prove: there exists an infinite subset $B$ of $A$, such that the greatest common divisor of any two different numbers in $B$ is the same.

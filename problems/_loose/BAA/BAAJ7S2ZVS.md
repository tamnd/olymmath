Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Prove that the distances from points $A$ and $B$ to the intersection point of the diagonals of the quadrilateral are equal, if it is known that the perimeters of triangles $A B C$ and $A B D$ are equal, as well as the perimeters of triangles $A C D$ and $B C D$ are equal.

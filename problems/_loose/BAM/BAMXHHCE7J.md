An ancient castle was surrounded by a triangular wall. Each side of the wall was divided into three equal parts, and towers were built at these points, as well as at the vertices. In total, there were 9 towers along the wall: A, E, F, B, K, L, C, $M, N$. Over time, all the walls and towers, except for towers $E, K, M$, were destroyed. How can the locations of towers $A, B, C$ be determined from the remaining towers, given that towers $A, B, C$ stood at the vertices?

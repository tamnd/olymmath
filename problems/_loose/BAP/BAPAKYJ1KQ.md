Given that $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ are externally tangent at point $T, \cdots$ a line is tangent to $\odot O_{2}$ at point $X$, and intersects $\odot O_{1}$ at points $A$ and $B$, with point $B$ lying on the segment $A X$ internally. Line $X T$ intersects $\odot O_{1}$ at another point $S$. $C$ is a point on $T S$ that does not include points $A$ and $B$, and a tangent line is drawn from point $C$ to $\odot O_{2}$, with the tangent point being $Y$, and segment $C Y$ does not intersect segment $S T$. Line $S C$ intersects $X Y$ at point $I$.
Prove: (1) $C, T, I, Y$ are concyclic;
(2) $I$ is the excenter of $\triangle A B C$ with respect to $\angle A$.

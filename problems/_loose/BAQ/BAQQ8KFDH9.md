(1) $\left[\begin{array}{lll}1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & 2\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{lll}1 & 2 & 3 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 & 1\end{array}\right]$.

$\left[\begin{array}{llll}1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 & 1 \\ 3 & 4 & 1 & 2 \\ 4 & 1 & 2 & 3\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{llll}1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 3 & 4 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 & 1 \\ 4 & 1 & 2 & 3\end{array}\right]$.

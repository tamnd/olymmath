Let $x, y$ be positive integers, where $y$ is not a perfect square, and $y < x$. Now, let $a, b$ represent the fractional parts of $x+\sqrt{y}$ and $x-\sqrt{y}$, respectively. Then the value of $a+b$ is
(A) an irrational number.
(B) a proper fraction.
(C) 1.
(D) indeterminate.
(Anhui Province Hefei City Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

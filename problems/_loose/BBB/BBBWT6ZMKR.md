Show that $x \mapsto|x|$ is differentiable at every point except 0.

Furthermore, as mentioned above, derivatives are an extremely powerful tool for finding extrema.

Theorem 49. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \mapsto \mathbb{R}$ and $x$ be a real number at which $f$ is extremal and differentiable. Then $f^{\prime}(x)=0$.

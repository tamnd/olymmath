At the malomgátak, sloping channels are commonly used for the drainage of excess water, the cross-section of which is a right-angled parallelogram. Water arrives at the upper end of such a channel at a speed of $1.4 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\text { sec }}$ and flows over it at a thickness of $0.4 \mathrm{~m}$. The channel is $10 \mathrm{~m}$ long, has a uniform width of $3.5 \mathrm{~m}$, and an inclination angle of $12^{\circ}$. What is the thickness of the water flow at the lower part of the channel? How wide would this channel need to be built so that the water flows at the same thickness at the lower part as at the upper part?

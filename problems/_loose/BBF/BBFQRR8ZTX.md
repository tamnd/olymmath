Circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ intersect in points $A$ and $B$, such that $k_{1}$ passes through the center $O$ of the circle $k_{2}$. The line $p$ intersects $k_{1}$ in points $K$ and $O$ and $k_{2}$ in points $L$ and $M$, such that the point $L$ is between $K$ and $O$. The point $P$ is orthogonal projection of the point $L$ to the line $A B$. Prove that the line $K P$ is parallel to the $M$-median of the triangle $A B M$.

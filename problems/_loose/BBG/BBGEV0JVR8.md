Let $A, B, C, D$ be four distinct points, arranged in this order on a line $\Delta$. Let $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ be the circles with diameters $[A C]$ and $[B D]$, respectively. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the points of intersection of $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$, and $Z$ the point of intersection of $(X Y)$ and $\Delta$, and $P$ a point on $(X Y)$ distinct from $Z$.

Let $M$ be the point of $\mathcal{C} \cap(C P)$ distinct from $C$, and $N$ the point of $\mathcal{C}^{\prime} \cap(B P)$ distinct from $B$. Show that $(A M),(D N)$ and $(X Y)$ are concurrent.

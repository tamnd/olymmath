- Once I needed to get from Boguly to Bollifoyne, where a friend was waiting for me. The only available transport was the old cart of Pat Doyle, which was pulled by a mare whose working life had clearly been extended too long.

Unbearably slowly, but steadily, we moved forward.

- Listen, Pat, - I asked a few minutes after the start of our journey, - does your vehicle have another speed?
- How can it not, - the driver replied, - only it's slower than this one.
- Then we'll have to make do with this one, - I said.

Pat assured me that the horse would go at an even pace, neither slowing down nor speeding up, until the end of our journey.

- We've been driving for twenty minutes, - I noted, looking at my watch, - how many miles have we traveled from Boguly?
- We've just covered half the distance left to Pigtown, - Pat replied.

After a quick meal in Pigtown, we traveled another five miles. I asked Pat:

- How many miles are left to Bollifoyne?

To this question, I received the same answer (Pat, apparently, could only measure distances from Pigtown):

- Exactly half the distance from here to Pigtown.

Another hour passed, and our journey ended.

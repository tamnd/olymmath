Suppose there are $n$ couples attending a party, and each person can either rest alone or join a chat group, referred to as a team. No couple is in the same team, and every two people, except for the couples, are in the same team exactly once. Prove that when $n \geqslant 4$, the total number of teams $k \geqslant 2 n$.

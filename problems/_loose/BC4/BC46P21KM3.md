Let $N$ be the number of tuples $(a_1, a_2,..., a_{150})$ satisfying: $\bullet$ $a_i \in \{2, 3, 5, 7, 11\}$ for all $1 \le i \le 99$ . $\bullet$ $a_i \in \{2, 4, 6, 8\}$ for all $100 \le i \le 150$ . $\bullet$ $\sum^{150}_{i=1}a_i$ is divisible by $8$ .
Compute the last three digits of $N$ .

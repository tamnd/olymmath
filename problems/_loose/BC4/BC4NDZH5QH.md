Prove that if the diameter of the circle circumscribed around a simple triangle is an integer, then

a) it is odd, and has the form $2 R=4 n+1$;

b) the triangle has two odd sides, and one even side, which is divisible by $4$;

c) the semiperimeter is also an integer;

d) the area is an even integer;

e) the segments of the sides from the vertices to the nearest points of tangency of the inscribed circle with the sides are integers;

f) the radius of the inscribed circle is an integer.

An integer triangle will be called an integer if the diameter of the circumscribed circle is an integer.

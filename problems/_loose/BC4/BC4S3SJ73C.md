On a clean board, we wrote a three-digit natural number with yellow chalk, composed of mutually different non-zero digits. Then we wrote on the board with white chalk all other three-digit numbers that can be obtained by changing the order of the digits of the yellow number. The arithmetic mean of all the numbers on the board was 370. We underlined each number smaller than the yellow one. The underlined numbers were exactly three and their arithmetic mean was 205. Determine the yellow number.

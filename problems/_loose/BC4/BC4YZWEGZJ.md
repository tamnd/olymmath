Show that the following relation holds:
a) $\frac{1}{2^{k}+1}-\frac{1}{2^{k+1}+1}=\frac{2^{k}}{\left(2^{k}+1\right) \cdot\left(2^{k+1}+1\right)}, \forall k \in N$

b) Find $n \in N$, such that:

$\frac{2}{3 \cdot 5}+\frac{4}{5 \cdot 9}+\frac{8}{9 \cdot 17}+\ldots .+\frac{2^{n}}{\left(2^{n}+1\right) \cdot\left(2^{n+1}+1\right)}=\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{2^{2014}-1}{2^{2015}+1}$

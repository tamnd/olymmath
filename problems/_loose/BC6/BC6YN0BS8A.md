Let $A B C D E$ be a fixed convex and cyclic pentagon. A triangulation is a subdivision into three triangles using two non-intersecting diagonals of $ABCDE$ (there are 5 different triangulations). We associate with each triangulation the sum of the radii of the incircles of the three triangles. Show that this sum does not depend on the choice of the triangulation.

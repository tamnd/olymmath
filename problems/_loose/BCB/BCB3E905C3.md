In an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB=AC=5$, a point $D$ on side $AB$ satisfies $AD=3$. A point $E$ is on side $BC$ (excluding endpoints). Let $\omega$ be a circle passing through $E$ and tangent to line $AB$ at $B$. $\omega$ meet $AE$ again at $F$ and then $CF=10$. If $\omega$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $ADE$, find the length of side $BC$.

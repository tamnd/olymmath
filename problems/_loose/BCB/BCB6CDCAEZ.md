I. (1) $y<3$,
(2) $x-y<3$
(3) $x+y<4$
II. (4) $(y-3) \cdot(x-y-3) \geqq 0$,
(5) $(y-3) \cdot(x+y-4) \leqq 0$,
(6) $(x-y-3) \cdot(x+y-4) \leqq 0$.

Give some example points that satisfy each system. Is there a point whose coordinates satisfy both systems?

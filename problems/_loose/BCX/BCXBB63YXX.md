In a right-angled triangle, $a, b$ are the legs, and $c$ is the hypotenuse. The larger of the legs lies opposite the angle whose tangent is less than 2. Prove the inequality

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}+c^{2}}+\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}+c^{2}}>\frac{4}{9}
$$

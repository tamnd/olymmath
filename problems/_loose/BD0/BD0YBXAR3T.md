Show that

$$
\operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha \operatorname{tg}^{2} \beta \operatorname{tg}^{2} \gamma-\left(\operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha+\operatorname{tg}^{2} \beta+\operatorname{tg}^{2} \gamma\right)=2+2 \sec \alpha \sec \beta \sec \gamma
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{tg}^{3} \alpha \operatorname{tg}^{3} \beta \operatorname{tg}^{3} \gamma-\left(\operatorname{tg}^{3} \alpha+\operatorname{tg}^{3} \beta+\operatorname{tg}^{3} \gamma\right)=\frac{3 \operatorname{tg} \alpha \operatorname{tg} \beta \operatorname{tg} \gamma}{\cos \alpha \cos \beta \cos \gamma}
$$

if

$$
\alpha+\beta+\gamma=180^{\circ}
$$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $AB < AC$. Point $D$ is chosen on segment $BC$ so that $BD < CD$. The angle bisectors of $\angle ADB$ and $\angle ADC$ intersect segments $AB$ and $AC$ at $E$ and $F$, respectively. Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle AEF$ and let $M$ be the midpoint of $EF$. The ray $AD$ intersects the circle $\omega$ at $X$, while the line through $X$ parallel to $EF$ intersects $\omega$ again at $Y$. If $YM$ intersects $\omega$ at $T$, prove that $AT$, $EF$, and $BC$ are concurrent.

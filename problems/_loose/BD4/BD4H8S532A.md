Given a positive integer $n>1$. In each cell of an $n \times n$ grid, there is an integer, and the following conditions are satisfied:
(1) Each number in the grid is congruent to 1 modulo $n$;
(2) The sum of the numbers in each row and each column is congruent to $n$ modulo $n^2$.

Let the product of the numbers in the $i$-th row be $R_i$, and the product of the numbers in the $j$-th column be $C_j$, where $i, j=1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} R_{i} \equiv \sum_{j=1}^{n} C_{j}\left(\bmod n^{4}\right) .
$$

The line connecting point $A$ and the vertex $O$ of the parabola intersects the directrix at point $B^{\prime}$, and the line connecting point $B$ and $O$ intersects the directrix at point $A^{\prime}$. Find the area of quadrilateral $A B B^{\prime} A^{\prime}$.

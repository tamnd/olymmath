Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral such that $AB\cdot BC=AD\cdot DA$ . Let $E,F,G,H$ be the midpoints of the sides $AB,BC,CD,DA$ , respectively. Let rays $BC,AD$ intersect in $X$ and rays $AB,DC$ intersect in $Y$ . The angle bisector of $\angle AXB$ meets line $EG$ in $S$ , the angle bisector of $\angle AYD$ meets line $FH$ in $T$ . Prove that the lines $ST$ and $BD$ are parallel.

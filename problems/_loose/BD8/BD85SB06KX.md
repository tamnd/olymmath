A calculator has two special keys:

- the key A, which doubles the number displayed; and
- the key $\mathrm{B}$, which adds one unit to the number displayed.

For example, if the number 45 is on the display and the key B is pressed, the display will show the number 46. If we then press the key $\mathrm{A}$, the display will show the number 92. In this example, we pressed a total of two times the keys A and B: once the key B and then once the key A, to go from 45 to 92. Suppose the number 1 is on the display. Indicate a way to obtain the number:

(a) 10 by pressing a total of four times the keys $\mathrm{A}$ and $\mathrm{B}$;

(b) 15 by pressing a total of six times the keys $\mathrm{A}$ and B;

(c) 100 by pressing a total of eight times the keys A and B.

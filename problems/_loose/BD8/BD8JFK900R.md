Let the lengths of three sides of a triangle be $l, m, n(l>m>n)$. If $\left\{\frac{3^l}{10^4}\right\}=\left\{\frac{3^m}{10^4}\right\}=\left\{\frac{3^n}{10^4}\right\}$, find the minimum value of the perimeter of the triangle.
Note: $\{x\}=x-[x]$ and $[x]$ denotes the integral part of number $x$.

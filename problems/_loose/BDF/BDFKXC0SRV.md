(1) $f(x)=\frac{x^{2}\left(x^{3}-1\right)}{x-1}$;
(2) $f(x)=x^{3}+x^{2}-1$;
(3) $f(x)=\frac{x+1+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}{x-1+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}$;
(4) $f(x)=\frac{\sqrt{9-x^{2}}}{|x+4|+|x-3|}$.

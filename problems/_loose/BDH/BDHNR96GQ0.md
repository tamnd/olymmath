B. 4 * Let $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ have radii $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$, respectively, and intersect at $A$ and $B$. Let $MN$ be a common tangent. Prove that:
the circumradii of $\triangle A M N$ and $\triangle B M N$ are both $\sqrt{r_{1} r_{2}}$,
$$
\frac{M A}{N A}=\frac{M B}{N B}=\sqrt{\frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}} .
$$

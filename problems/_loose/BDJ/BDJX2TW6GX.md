The 49th question: Given positive integers $a, b$, where $a < b$, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}: a_{1}=a, a_{2}=b, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}$ $\left(n \in \mathbb{Z}^{+}\right)$. If there do not exist infinitely many positive integers $k$ such that $1 + m a_{k} a_{k+2}$ is a perfect square, then $m$ is said to have property $P$. Prove: There exist infinitely many positive integers $m$ such that $m$ has property $P$.

Let $a$ be a positive number, and consider the set of points $S$. The points of set $S$ have rectangular coordinates $(x, y)$ that satisfy all of the following five conditions:
(i) $\frac{a}{2} \leqslant x \leqslant 2 a$.
(ii) $\frac{a}{2} \leqslant y \leqslant 2 a$.
(iii) $x+y \geqslant a$.
(iv) $x+a \geqslant y$.
(v) $y+a \geqslant x$.
The number of sides of the boundary polygon of set $S$ is
(A) 3 .
(B) 4 .
(C) 5 .
(D) 6.
(E) 7 .
(29th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1978)

In the Cartesian coordinate system, the graph of the function $y=\frac{1}{|x|}$ is denoted as $\Gamma$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be two points on $\Gamma$ such that: $P$ is in the first quadrant, $Q$ is in the second quadrant, and the line $PQ$ is tangent to $\Gamma$ at point $Q$ in the second quadrant. Find the minimum value of $|PQ|$.

If $n$ is a natural number, then the last digit of $n^{9999}-n^{5555}$
(A) is always 0.
(B) is sometimes 0, sometimes not 0.
(C) is the same as the last digit of $n$.
(D) cannot be determined.
(2nd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

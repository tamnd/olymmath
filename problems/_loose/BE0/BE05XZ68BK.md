In the plane of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is taken, through which rays are drawn parallel to the oriented sides $AB, BC, CA$ of the triangle. On the rays from point $M$, segments equal to the sides are laid off: $MP = CA, MQ = AB, MR = BC$. Prove that: 1) point $M$ is the centroid of triangle $PQR$ and 2) the ratio of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$ is $1:3$.

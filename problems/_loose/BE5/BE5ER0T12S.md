Given that one root of the equation $x^{2}+p x+q=0$ is $\alpha$, one root of the equation $x^{2}+r x+s=0$ is $\beta$, and $\alpha / \beta=k$. It is to be proven that the following equality holds:

$$
\left(q-k^{2} s\right)^{2}+k(p-k r)(k p s-q r)=0
$$

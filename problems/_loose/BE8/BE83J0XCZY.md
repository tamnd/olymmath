Considering the remainder of the division by 3, we get that $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}$ gives a remainder of 1. Since the square of a natural number can only give a remainder of 0 or 1, we get that two of the three numbers are divisible by 3, that is, they are equal to 3. If these are the first two, then $z=2$. If, however, $z=3$, there is no solution.

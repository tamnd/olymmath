(26th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) In a $100 \times 100$ grid, the cells are colored with 4 different colors such that each row and each column contains exactly 25 cells of each color. Prove that it is possible to find two rows and two columns in the grid such that the 4 cells at their intersections are colored with 4 different colors.

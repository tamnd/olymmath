A token is placed on the square $C 1$ of a standard chessboard. Two players move it in turns, following this rule: on each turn, one can move the token as many squares as they want, but at least one, either to the right, upwards, or diagonally to the right and upwards. The player who moves the token to $H 8$ wins.

Determine which of the two players has a winning strategy.

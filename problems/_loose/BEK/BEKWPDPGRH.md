We cut out two congruent regular octagons from paper. We number the vertices of one of them (in a counterclockwise direction) with the numbers ranging from 1 to 8. Is it possible to number the vertices of the other octagon in such a way that no matter how we place the two octagons on top of each other, a vertex of the first octagon will coincide with a vertex of the same number on the second octagon?

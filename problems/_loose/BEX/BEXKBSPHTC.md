Two players play the following game: there are two piles of candies. The players take turns making a move. A move consists of eating one of the piles and dividing the other into two (equal or unequal) parts. If a player cannot divide a pile because it contains only one candy, they eat it and win. Initially, there were 33 and 35 candies in the piles. Who will win, the first player or their partner, and how should they play to win?

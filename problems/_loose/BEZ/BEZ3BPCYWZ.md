Outside the circle, there is a point $L$ such that $\angle L E F = \angle F E G, \angle L G F = \angle F G E$. Find the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $E L G$, if the area of triangle $E G O$ is $81 \sqrt{3}$ and $\angle O E G = 60^{\circ}$.

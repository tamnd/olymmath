Given are two mutually perpendicular diameters $AB$ and $CD$ of a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$. On the segment $OA$, a point $M$ is chosen such that $OM=\frac{r}{\sqrt{3}}$. A line passing through $M$ intersects the line $CD$ at point $R$ and the given circle at points $P$ and $Q$ such that points $P$ and $R$ are on the same side of point $M$. Prove that $\frac{1}{MQ}=\frac{1}{MP}+\frac{1}{MR}$.

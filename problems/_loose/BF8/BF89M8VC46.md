At a school, the three teachers Schulze, Ufer, and Krause taught in the subjects of German, Russian, History, Mathematics, Physics, and Biology. The following is known:

(1) Each of these three teachers teaches exactly two of these six subjects, and each of these six subjects is taught by exactly one of these three teachers.

(2) Both the teacher for Biology and the teacher for Physics are older than Mr. Schulze.

(3) In their free time, the teacher for Russian, the teacher for Mathematics, and Mr. Schulze enjoy playing Skat. Mr. Krause wins more often than the teacher for Biology and the teacher for Russian.

Prove that the distribution of the three teachers to the subjects can be uniquely determined from these statements, and provide this distribution!

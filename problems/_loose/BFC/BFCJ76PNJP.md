In a scalene triangle $ABC$, let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle and $k$ the circumscribed circle. The rays $BI$ and $CI$ intersect the circle $k$ again at points $S_{b} \neq B$ and $S_{c} \neq C$. Prove that the tangent to the circle $k$ at point $A$, the line through point $I$ parallel to side $BC$, and the line $S_{b} S_{c}$ intersect at one point.

(Patrik Bak)

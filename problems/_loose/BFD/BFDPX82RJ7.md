$$
\begin{array}{r}
2 x^{3}-2 a y^{3}=(a+1)^{2} \\
x^{3}+a x^{2} y+x y^{2}=1
\end{array}
$$

have exactly one real solution $(x ; y)$ with $x=-y$?

\section*{Task 2 - 310932}

In a collection of curiosities, there was supposed to be a vessel with the following inscription:

Five Stroke Two Zero fits as a measure in me, after the first digit read "through" for the stroke! Or turn around, Zero Two Five you will find, after the first digit add a decimal point!

Indeed, $\frac{5}{20}=0.25$.

Are there any other combinations of three digits where the rule, in the same or reversed order, of writing the division bar or the decimal point after the first digit, results in two identical numerical values?

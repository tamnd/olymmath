Divide an equilateral triangle into $n^{2}$ smaller equilateral triangles of the same size, and label some of these smaller triangles with numbers $1,2, \cdots, m$, such that triangles with adjacent numbers have an adjacent side. Prove: $m \leqslant n^{2}-n+1$.

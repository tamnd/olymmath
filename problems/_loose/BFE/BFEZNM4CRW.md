$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{3}-a y^{3}=\frac{1}{2}(a+1)^{2} \\
& x^{3}+a x^{2} y+x y^{2}=1
\end{aligned}
$$

have at least one solution $(x, y)$ that satisfies the condition $x+y=0$?

A natural number $n$ is called polite if it can be expressed as $$ n =s+(s+1)+...+(t-1)+t $$ where $s < t$ , natural numbers. For example, the number $18$ is polite because $18 = 5+6+7$ .
a) Is the number $2012$ polite?
b) Is the number $2048$ polite?
c) Find all polite numbers.

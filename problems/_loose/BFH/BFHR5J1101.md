The number of tourists received by a certain tourist area each month approximately follows a periodic pattern. Therefore, the number of people working in tourism services in the $n$-th month, $f(n)$, can be approximately described by the function
$$
f(n)=100[A \cos (\omega n+\alpha)+k]
$$

where $n$ is a positive integer representing the month, $A$ and $k$ are positive integers, $\omega>0$, and $\alpha \in\left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$.

Statistical findings show that the number of people working in tourism services in this region each month has the following characteristics:
(i) The number of people working in tourism services in the same month each year is basically the same;
(ii) The number of people working in tourism services in August, the busiest month, and February, the least busy month, differs by about 400 people;
(iii) In February, the number of people working in tourism services in this region is approximately 100, and it increases month by month until it reaches the maximum in August.
(1) Based on the known information, try to determine an expression for $f(n)$ that meets the conditions.
(2) Generally, when the number of people working in tourism services in this region reaches 400 or more, the region enters its peak tourism season. Determine which months of the year are the peak tourism season for this region. Please provide reasons.

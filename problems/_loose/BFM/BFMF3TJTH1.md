Prove that touching circles (a circle and a line) transform under inversion into touching circles or into a circle and a line, or into a pair of parallel lines.

Definition. Let two circles intersect at point A. The angle between the circles is defined as the angle between the tangents to the circles at point A. (It is clear that if the circles intersect at points A and B, then the angle between the tangents at point A is equal to the angle between the tangents at point B.) Similarly, the angle between a line and a circle is defined.

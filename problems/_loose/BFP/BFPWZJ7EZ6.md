(SFRY, 75). Prove that for any point $O$ lying inside triangle $ABC$ with semiperimeter $p$, the following inequality holds:

$$
O A \cdot \cos \frac{\angle B A C}{2} + O B \cdot \cos \frac{\angle A B C}{2} + O C \cdot \cos \frac{\angle A C B}{2} \geqslant p
$$

Determine when equality is achieved.

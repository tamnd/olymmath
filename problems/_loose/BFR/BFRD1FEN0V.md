12.6 (CMO 2001) Given $a \sqrt{2}<a<2$. A convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a unit circle 2 and satisfies the following conditions:
(1) The center of the circle is inside the convex quadrilateral;
(2) The longest side is $a$, and the shortest side is $\sqrt{4-a^{2}}$.
Tangents $L_{A}, L_{B}, L_{C}, L_{D}$ are drawn to circle 2 through points $A, B, C, D$ respectively. It is known that $L_{A}$ intersects $L_{B}$, $L_{B}$ intersects $L_{C}$, $L_{C}$ intersects $L_{D}$, and $L_{D}$ intersects $L_{A}$ at points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}$ respectively. Find the maximum and minimum values of the ratio $\frac{S_{\text {quadrilateral } A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}}}{S_{\text {quadrilateral } A B C D}}$.

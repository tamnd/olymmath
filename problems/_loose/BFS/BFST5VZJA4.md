Given the ellipse $\mathrm{C}: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(\mathrm{a}>\mathrm{b}>0)$ has an eccentricity of $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and it passes through the point $P(2,-1)$
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Suppose point $\mathrm{Q}$ is on the ellipse $\mathrm{C}$, and $\mathrm{PQ}$ is parallel to the $x$-axis. Draw two lines through point $\mathrm{P}$ that intersect the ellipse $\mathrm{C}$ at points $\mathrm{A}\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right), \mathrm{B}\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right)$, if line $\mathrm{PQ}$ bisects $\angle \mathrm{APB}$, prove that the slope of line $\mathrm{AB}$ is a constant, and find this constant.

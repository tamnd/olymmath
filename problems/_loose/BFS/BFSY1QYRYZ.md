Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $\ell$ be a straight line intersecting the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at the points $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively, so that the vertex $A$ and the barycentre $G$ of $A B C$ lie on the same half-plane defined by the line $\ell$. Show that

$$
\operatorname{Area}\left(B B_{1} G C_{1}\right)+\operatorname{Area}\left(C C_{1} G B_{1}\right) \geq \frac{4}{9} \operatorname{Area}(A B C)
$$

When does the equality hold?

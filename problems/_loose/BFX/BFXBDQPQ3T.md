In the right triangle $A B C$, $\angle B=90^{\circ}$, its incircle is tangent to sides $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. Connect $A D$, intersecting the incircle at another point $P$, and connect $P C$, $P E$, and $P F$. Given that $P C \perp P F$, prove that $P E \parallel B C$.

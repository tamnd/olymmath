(NBR, 82). Prove that for any numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n} \in[0 ; 2](n \geqslant 2)$ the inequality

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|a_{i}-a_{j}\right| \leqslant n^{2}
$$

holds. Determine for which values of $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ equality is achieved.

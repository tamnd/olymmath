In a mixed class of boys and girls, a student is selected to represent the class, with each student having an equal chance of being selected, and the probability of a boy being selected is $\frac{2}{3}$ of the probability of a girl being selected. Then the ratio of the number of boys to the total number of students in the class is
(A) $\frac{1}{3}$.
(B) $\frac{2}{5}$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $\frac{3}{5}$.
(E) $\frac{2}{3}$.
(36th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1985)

Given positive numbers $a, b, c$, we construct the numbers

$$
a^{\prime}=\frac{a}{a+1}, b^{\prime}=\frac{b}{b+1} \text { and } c^{\prime}=\frac{c}{c+1}
$$

Prove that if it is possible to construct a triangle from segments of lengths $a, b, c$, then it is also possible to construct a triangle from segments of lengths $a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime}$.

Is the converse of this statement true?

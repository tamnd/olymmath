17.2.3 $\star \star$ For each natural number $k \geqslant 2$, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies:
$$
a_{0}=k, a_{n}=d\left(a_{n-1}\right), n=1,2,3, \cdots
$$

where $d(a)$ is the number of positive divisors of $a$. Find all $k$ such that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ contains no perfect squares.

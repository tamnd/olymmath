Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$. The points $A_{1} \neq A, B_{1} \neq B$ and $C_{1} \neq C$ lie respectively on the circumcircles of $\triangle B C H, \triangle C A H$ and $\triangle A B H$, and $A_{1} H=B_{1} H=C_{1} H$. Denote by $H_{1}, H_{2}$ and $H_{3}$ the orthocenters of $\triangle A_{1} B C, \triangle B_{1} C A$ and $\triangle C_{1} A B$, respectively. Prove that $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $\triangle H_{1} H_{2} H_{3}$ have the same orthocenter.

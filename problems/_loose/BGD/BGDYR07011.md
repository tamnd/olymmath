(PRC, 79; Jury, USA, 79). Prove that if the function $ f : \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R} $ satisfies for all $ x, y \in \mathbf{R} $ the inequalities

$$
f(x) \leqslant x, f(x+y) \leqslant f(x)+f(y)
$$

then the identity

$$
f(x) \equiv x, x \in \mathbb{R}
$$

holds.

The zoo offered discounted admission for school groups: every fifth student gets a free ticket. Mr. Teacher of 6.A calculated that if he bought tickets for the children in his class, he would save on four tickets and pay 1995 CZK. Mrs. Teacher of 6.B suggested that he buy tickets for the children of both classes at once, and thus they would pay 4410 CZK.

How many children from 6.A and how many children from 6.B went to the zoo? (The price of a ticket in CZK is an integer.)

(Hint. How many tickets should be requested so that exactly four of them are free?)

(German Mathematics Competition) Given that $p, q$ are coprime positive integers, and $p \neq q$. The set of positive integers is divided into three subsets $A, B, C$, such that for each positive integer $z$, each of these three subsets contains exactly one of the integers $z, z+p, z+q$. Prove: Such a partition exists if and only if $p+q$ is divisible by 3.

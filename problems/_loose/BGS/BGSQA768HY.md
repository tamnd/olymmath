Let $A B C$ be a triangle with centroid $G$. Let $D \neq A$ be a point on the line $A G$ such that $A G=G D$, and let $E \neq B$ be a point on the line $G B$ such that $G B=G E$. Finally, let $M$ be the midpoint of $A B$. Prove that the quadrilateral $B M C D$ is cyclic if and only if $B A=B E$.

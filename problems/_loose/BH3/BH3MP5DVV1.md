Show that the Poisson formula, which defines the probability of $k$ events occurring over a time duration $t$

$$
P_{t}(k)=\frac{(\lambda t)^{k} \cdot \mathrm{e}^{-\lambda t}}{k!}
$$

can be considered a mathematical model of the simplest event flow; in other words, show that the Poisson formula reflects all the properties of the simplest flow.

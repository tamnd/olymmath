Construct a triangle $ABC$ from $b=7 \text{~cm}, \beta=40^{\circ}$, and $h_{b}=5 \text{~cm}$!

Here, $b$ is the length of side $AC$, $\beta$ is the size of angle $\angle CBA$, and $h_{b}$ is the length of the height from $B$.

Describe and justify your construction!

Determine whether there is exactly one triangle $ABC$, up to congruence, that has the required sizes $b, \beta$, and $h_{b}$!

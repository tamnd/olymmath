A car is called flex if it can be fueled with gasoline or ethanol. Consider that the prices of ethanol and gasoline are, respectively, R$ 1.59 and R$ 2.49 per liter.

(a) Suppose a flex car can travel $12.3 \mathrm{~km}$ per liter of gasoline, which we denote as $12.3 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{l}$. What must be the $\mathrm{km} / \mathrm{l}$ ratio of this car for ethanol to make the use of ethanol financially more advantageous than gasoline?

(b) If the performance of a flex car is $x \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{l}$ with gasoline and $\left(\frac{x}{2}+1\right) \mathrm{km} / \mathrm{l}$ with ethanol, write the expression for the function $g(x)$ that gives the cost of this car traveling $100 \mathrm{~km}$ using gasoline and the expression for the function $a(x)$ that gives the cost of this car traveling $100 \mathrm{~km}$ using ethanol.

(c) For the cost to be the same, whether using ethanol or gasoline, what must be the $\mathrm{km} / \mathrm{l}$ ratio for gasoline and for ethanol?

(d) Under what condition is the use of ethanol more financially advantageous than gasoline? Provide a numerical example that satisfies the condition.

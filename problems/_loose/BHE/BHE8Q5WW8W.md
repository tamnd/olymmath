Sports Problem (I). In a class, there are 25 students. Out of them, 17 can ride a bicycle, 13 can swim, and 8 can ski. No student masters all three sports, but both cyclists, swimmers, and skiers have good or satisfactory grades in mathematics, which is particularly noteworthy, as six students have unsatisfactory grades in this subject.

How many students have excellent grades in mathematics? How many swimmers can ski?

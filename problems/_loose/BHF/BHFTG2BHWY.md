A circle inscribed in a triangle touches one of its sides at $E$, the midpoint of this side is $F$, the angle bisector and the altitude from the opposite vertex intersect the line of this side at $G$ and $H$, respectively. Prove that $E G \cdot F H = E F \cdot E H$.

Is there a similar relationship if we replace $E$ with the point of tangency of an excircle, and $G$ with the intersection point of the external angle bisector?

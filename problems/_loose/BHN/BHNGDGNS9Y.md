Given three positive integers $a, b, c$, their greatest common divisor is $D$, and their least common multiple is $M$. Among the following statements, which are true:
(1) The product $M D$ is not less than $a b c$.
(2) The product $M D$ is not greater than $a b c$.
(3) $M D = a b c$ if and only if $a, b, c$ are prime numbers.
(4) $M D = a b c$ if and only if $a, b, c$ are pairwise coprime.
(A) 1,2 .
(B) 1,3 .
(C) 1,4 .
(D) 2,3 .
(E) 2,4 .
(10th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1959)

A lattice point in the plane is a point with integer coordinates. Let $T$ be a triangle in the plane whose vertices are lattice points, but with no other lattice points on its sides. Furthermore, suppose $T$ contains exactly four lattice points in its interior. Prove that these four points lie on a straight line.

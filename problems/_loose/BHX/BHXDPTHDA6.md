(Orthogonality of the radical axis)

We denote $P$ as the orthocenter of the triangle formed by the lines $(AB),(CD),(AD)$ and $Q$ as the orthocenter of the triangle formed by the lines $(AB),(CD),(BC)$. Show that $(MN)$ is perpendicular to $(PQ)$.

(2004 Taiwan Mathematical Olympiad) Let $O$ and $H$ be the circumcenter and orthocenter of an acute $\triangle ABC$, respectively. The angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at point $D$. The point $D$ is reflected over line $BC$ to point $E$, and over point $O$ to point $F$. If $AE$ intersects $FH$ at point $G$, and the midpoint of $BC$ is $M$. Prove that $GM \perp AF$.

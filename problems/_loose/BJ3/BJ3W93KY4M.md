For a rectangle $A B C D$ with side lengths $\overline{A B}=a$ and $\overline{A D}=b(b<a)$, exactly one parallel to one side is to cut off a rectangle similar to the original one. Solve the problem by construction!

Note: Two non-square rectangles are called similar if the ratio of the longer side to the shorter side is the same for both.

In the circle on the right, $M$ is the midpoint of arc $CAB$, $MP$ is perpendicular to chord $AB$ at
$P$. If the length of chord $AC$ is $x$, and the length of segment $AP$ is $(x+1)$, then the length of segment $PB$ is
(A) $3x+2$.
(B) $3x+1$
(C) $2x+3$.
(D) $2x+2$.
(E) $2x+1$.
(23rd American High School Mathematics Examination, 1972)

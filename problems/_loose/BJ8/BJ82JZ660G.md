Let $S(n)$ denote the number of different paths from point $A_{0}(0,0)$ to $A_{n}(n, n)$, where a path from an integer point $(a, b)$ can only move to $(a+1, b)$, $(a, b+1)$, or $(a+1, b+1)$, and does not pass through any point above the line $y=x$. For example, $S(2)=$ $6, S(3)=22$. Prove: $3 \mid S(2 n)\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$.

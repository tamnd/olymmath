Find the standard $\mathrm{PQR}$ representation of the following symmetric polynomials in three variables:
(1) $(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)$;
(2) $\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)\left(y^{2}+z^{2}\right)\left(z^{2}+x^{2}\right)$;
(3) $\sum x(x+y)(x+z)$;
(4) $x^{n}+y^{n}+z^{n}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$;
(5) $\sum\left(x^{4} y+x y^{4}\right)$;
(6) $\prod\left(x^{2}+x y+y^{2}\right)$.

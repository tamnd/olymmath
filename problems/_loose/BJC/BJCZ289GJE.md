The sides of an arbitrary convex polygon are painted on the outside. Several diagonals of the polygon are drawn so that no three intersect at the same point. Each of these diagonals is also painted on one side, i.e., a narrow colored strip is drawn on one side of the segment. Prove that at least one of the polygons into which the original polygon is divided by the diagonals is entirely painted on the outside.

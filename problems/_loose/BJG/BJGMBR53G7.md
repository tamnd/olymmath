Determine the set of all real numbers $\alpha$ with the following property: For every positive number $c$ there exists a fraction $\frac{m}{n}(m \in \mathbb{Z}, n \in \mathbb{N})$ different from $\alpha$ such that

$$
\left|\alpha-\frac{m}{n}\right|<\frac{c}{n}
$$

a) Construct a circle with a radius of $3 \mathrm{~cm}$, two parallel tangents $t_{1}, t_{2}$, and a third tangent $t_{3}$ to this circle! For the intersection points $A, B$ of $t_{3}$ with $t_{1}$ and with $t_{2}$, and for the center $M$ of the circle, make a conjecture about the size of the angle $\angle A M B$!

b) Prove that this conjecture always holds if $t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}$ are three tangents to a circle and $t_{1} \| t_{2}$!

c) Prove that then also always for the intersection point $Q$, where $A B$ intersects the parallel line $p$ through $M$ to $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$, $\overline{A Q}=\overline{M Q}$ holds!

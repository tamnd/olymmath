Prove: For each positive integer $n$, we have $\frac{2 n+1}{3} \sqrt{n} \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{i} \leqslant \frac{4 n+3}{6} \sqrt{n}-\frac{1}{6}$. The equality holds on both sides if and only if $n=1$.

In an oriented plane, the vertices $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ of two similarly oriented, similar isosceles triangles $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ coincide.

Prove: If the midpoints $A^{\prime}$ and $B^{\prime}$ of the connecting segments $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $B_{1} B_{2}$ are connected to each other and to $C^{\prime}=C_{1}=C_{2}$, one obtains a triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ that is similar to the given triangles.

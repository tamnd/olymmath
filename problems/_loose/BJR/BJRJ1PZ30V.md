Elmo writes positive real numbers on a $n \times n$ board such that $x_{i,j}$ is the number on the $i$ -th row and $j$ -th column. The sum of numbers in $a$ -th row is $R_a$ , and the sum of numbers in $b$ -th column is $C_b$ . It is known that
$$ \sum_{a=1}^{n}\frac{x_{a,k}}{R_a}=1 $$ for each $k=1,2,\ldots,n$ . Prove that $R_1R _2 \cdots R_n \leq C_1 C_2 \cdots C_n$ .

*Seongjin Shim*

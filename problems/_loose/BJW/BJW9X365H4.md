Find all real numbers $x, y$ that satisfy the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x \cdot\left\lfloor\frac{y}{x}\right\rfloor=5, \\
& y \cdot\left\lfloor\frac{x}{y}\right\rfloor=-6 .
\end{aligned}
$$

(The notation $\lfloor a\rfloor$ denotes the floor function of $a$, i.e., the greatest integer less than or equal to $a$.)

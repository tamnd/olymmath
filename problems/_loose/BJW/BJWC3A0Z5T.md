(A) Grandma did not receive any chocolate sweets.

(B) Grandma received fewer coconut sweets than chocolate sweets.

(C) Grandma received the same number of sweets of each of the 3 varieties.

(D) There are 2 varieties of sweets of which Grandma received the same number.

(E) The number of blackberry sweets Grandma received is greater than the other 2 combined.

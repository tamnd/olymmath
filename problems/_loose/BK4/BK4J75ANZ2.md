In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a}{b+c-a}+\frac{b}{c+a-b}+\frac{c}{a+b-c} \\
\geqslant \frac{b+c-a}{a}+\frac{c+a-b}{b}+\frac{a+b-c}{c} \geqslant 3 .
\end{array}
$$
(Eighth Chen Shengbei National High School Mathematics Olympiad)

In $\triangle A B C$, the lengths of the sides opposite to $\angle A, \angle B, \angle C$ are $a, b, c$ respectively,
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle C=\max \{\angle A, \angle B, \angle C\}, \\
\sin C=1+\cos C \cdot \cos (A-B), \\
\frac{2}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=1 .
\end{array}
$$

Find the minimum perimeter of $\triangle A B C$.

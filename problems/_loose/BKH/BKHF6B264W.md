Let $\Gamma$ be a circle, $A, B, C, D$ four points on $\Gamma$ (in that order) and $I, J, K, L$ the midpoints of the arcs connecting $A$ to $B, B$ to $C, C$ to $D, D$ to $A$ respectively (arcs taken by always traveling the circle in the same direction). Show that $(I K)$ is perpendicular to $(J L)$.

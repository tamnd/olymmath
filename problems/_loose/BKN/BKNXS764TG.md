Let the distances from point $M$ to the vertices $A, B$, and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ be $p, q$, and $r$ respectively. Prove that there do not exist a real number $d \neq 0$ and a point $F$ in the plane of triangle $ABC$ such that the distances from point $F$ to the vertices $A, B$, and $C$ are equal to $\sqrt{p^{2}+d}, \sqrt{q^{2}+d}$, and $\sqrt{r^{2}+d}$ respectively.

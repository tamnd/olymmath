Label $4k$ points on a circle with the numbers $1, 2, \cdots, 4k$ in any manner. Prove that:
(1) It is possible to connect these $4k$ points with $2k$ non-intersecting chords such that the difference between the numbers at the ends of each chord does not exceed $3k-1$;
(2) For any natural number $k$, the number $3k-1$ in (1) cannot be reduced.

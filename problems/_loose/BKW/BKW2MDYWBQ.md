Given an acute isosceles triangle $ABC$ with base $AC$. Inside this triangle, there is a point $M$, on the side of $C$ relative to the axis of $AC$ and such that $\angle CMA = 2 \angle CBA$, and a point $N$ inside the segment $AM$ such that $\angle BNM = \angle CBA$.

- Prove that $\angle CBN = \angle BAM$.
- Prove that $CM + MN = BN$.

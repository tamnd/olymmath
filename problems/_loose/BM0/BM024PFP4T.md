Let $F(n)$ denote the smallest positive integer greater than $n$ whose sum of digits is equal to the sum of the digits of $n$ . For example, $F(2019) = 2028$ . Compute $F(1) + F(2) + \dots + F(1000).$ *Proposed by Sean Li*

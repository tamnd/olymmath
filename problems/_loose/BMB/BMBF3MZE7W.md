Let the feet of the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ be $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$; the midpoints of the sides be $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$. Show that

1. the circumcenters $M_{1}, M_{2}, M_{3}$ of triangles $AB_{1}C_{1}$, $BC_{1}A_{1}$, and $CA_{1}B_{1}$ lie on the Feuerbach circle (K.M.L.IV.44.), and
2. $\triangle M_{1} M_{2} M_{3} \cong \triangle A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$.

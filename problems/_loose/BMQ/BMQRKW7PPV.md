Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer, we define $S_{n}:=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(k^{4}+2 k^{3}+2 k^{2}+k\right)$.

Show that $5 S_{n}+n$ is a perfect square if and only if $n$ is a perfect square.

Kiro and Rampo are playing the following game: from two piles of pebbles, Kiro takes one, and the other, at his discretion, he divides into two piles. Rampo takes one of the two new piles, and the other, at his discretion, he divides into two new piles, etc. The game is lost by the one who cannot divide the remaining pile. In which case, with a true strategy, does Kiro win the game, and in which case does Rampo win? What is the strategy?

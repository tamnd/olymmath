Given a square $A B C D$, $P$ is any point on side $C D$, and $A P, B P$ intersect the circle $\odot O$ with diameter $A B$ at points $M, N$ respectively, where $M \neq A, N \neq B$. Extend $D M$ and $C N$ to intersect at point $Q$. Prove: Point $Q$ lies on the circumference of $\odot O$, and satisfies $\frac{A Q}{Q B}=\frac{D P}{P C}$.

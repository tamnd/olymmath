In an orthocentric tetrahedron $ABCD$, the angle $ADC$ is right. Prove that $\frac{1}{h^{2}}=\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}+\frac{1}{c^{2}}$, where $h$ is the length of the height of the tetrahedron drawn from vertex $D$, $a=DA$, $b=DB$, $c=DC$.

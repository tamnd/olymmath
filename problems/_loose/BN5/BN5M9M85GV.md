Given a triangle $A B C$ inscribed in a circle $\omega$. Point $M$ is the foot of the perpendicular from point $B$ to the line $A C$, and point $N$ is the foot of the perpendicular from point $A$ to the tangent to $\omega$ drawn through point $B$. Prove that $M N \| B C$.

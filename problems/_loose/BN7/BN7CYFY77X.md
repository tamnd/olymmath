Given is a convex angle with vertex $ P $ and a point $ A $ lying inside this angle. Points $ X $ and $ Y $ lie on different
sides of this angle, such that $ PX = PY $ and the value of the sum $ AX + AY $ is the smallest. Prove that
$ \measuredangle XAP = \measuredangle YAP $.

Given an ellipse with parametric equations

$$
x=a \cos t, y=b \sin t, a \neq b
$$

Prove that the points whose parameters are $t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}, t_{4}$ lie on a circle if and only if there exists an integer $k$ such that $t_{1}+t_{2}+t_{3}+t_{4}=2 k \pi$.

Place a number at each vertex of a regular $n$-gon, where $n-1$ of the numbers are 0, and one number is 1. It is allowed to add 1 to the numbers at all $k$ vertices of some regular $k$-gon in one move. Can the numbers at all $n$ vertices be made equal after several such operations?
(1993 Saint Petersburg Selection Exam)

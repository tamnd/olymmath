Let's prove that no matter what integers we write in the following expression for $x, y$, and $z$, we always get a perfect square.

$$
9\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right)^{2}-8(x+y+z)\left(x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}-3 x y z\right) .
$$

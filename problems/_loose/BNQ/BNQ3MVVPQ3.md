From point $D$ on side $\overline{A B}$, where $|A D|>|B D|$, of an equilateral triangle $A B C$, a perpendicular is drawn to side $\overline{B C}$ with its foot at $E$. Then, from $E$, a perpendicular is drawn to $\overline{C A}$ with its foot at $F$, and from $F$, a perpendicular is drawn to $\overline{A B}$ with its foot at $G$. The area of quadrilateral $D E F G$ is $21 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, and the length of the side of triangle $A B C$ is $14 \mathrm{~cm}$. Determine the length $x=|B D|$ and the perimeter of quadrilateral $D E F G$.

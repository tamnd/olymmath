On a plane, 9 points are arranged such that 4 of them form the vertices of a square, 4 bisect the sides of the square, and the ninth marks the center of this figure.

The longest closed path connecting all points without any connection being traversed twice is sought. Only segments that run parallel to the sides or diagonals of the square are allowed.

(8th grade) In each cell of a $25 \times 25$ square table, one of the numbers 1 or -1 is written (arbitrarily). Under each column, the product of all numbers in that column is written. To the right of each row, the product of all numbers in that row is written. Prove that the sum of the 50 written products is not equal to 0.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle with incenter $I$ . Suppose $P$ and $Q$ are isogonal conjugates of $ABC$ such that the excenters of $\triangle PBC$ and $\triangle QBC$ opposite $P$ and $Q$ , respectively, lie on the external angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ . Let the internal angle bisectors of $\angle BPC$ and $\angle BQC$ meet each other at $F$ and $\overline{BC}$ at $D$ and $E$ , respectively. Prove that circumcircles of $BIC$ and $DEF$ are tangent.

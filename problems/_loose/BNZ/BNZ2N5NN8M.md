An *operation* on three given non-negative integers consists in increasing two of them by $1$ and decreasing the third by $2$ , provided the new numbers are non-negative. The process begins with three non-negative integers that add up to $100$ and are less than $100$ . Find the number of distinct triplets that can be obtained by applying the operation. (Triplets that differ only in the order of their members are considered the same).

Two runners are running at equal constant speeds along the diagonals AC and BD, respectively, of a square $ABCD$. Upon reaching the end of the diagonal, the runner immediately turns back. They started simultaneously from two randomly chosen points on their respective diagonals. Prove that there will be a moment when the distance between the runners is strictly less than half the diagonal of the square. (I. Rubanov)

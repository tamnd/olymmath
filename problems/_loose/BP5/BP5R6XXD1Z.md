A square of $2n \times 2n$ cells is given, all of whose cells are white. In one move, it is allowed to change the color of three consecutive squares in a column or row to the opposite color (if a square is white, it becomes black; if it is black, it becomes white). Find all natural numbers n $ \ge 2$ for which, using the specified moves, a square with a checkerboard coloring can be obtained from the given square.

In triangle $ ABC$ , sides $ a,b$ and $ c$ are opposite angles $ A,B$ and $ C$ respectively. $ AD$ bisects angle $ A$ and meets $ BC$ at $ D$ . Then if $ x = \overline{CD}$ and $ y = \overline{BD}$ the correct proportion is:
$ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac {x}{a} = \frac {a}{b + c} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac {x}{b} = \frac {a}{a + c} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac {y}{c} = \frac {c}{b + c}
\textbf{(D)}\ \frac {y}{c} = \frac {a}{b + c} \textbf{(E)}\ \frac {x}{y} = \frac {c}{b}$

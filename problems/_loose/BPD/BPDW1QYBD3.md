10,11 Prove the following inequalities directly and using Muirhead's inequality (problem $\underline{61424}$ ):

a) $x^{4} y^{2} z+y^{4} x^{2} z+y^{4} z^{2} x+z^{4} y^{2} x+x^{4} z^{2} y+z^{4} x^{2} y \geq 2\left(x^{3} y^{2} z^{2}+x^{2} y^{3} z^{2}+x^{2} y^{2} z^{3}\right)$

b) $x^{5}+y^{5}+z^{5} \geq x^{2} y^{2} z+x^{2} y z^{2}+x y^{2} z^{2}$

c) $x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}+t^{3} \geq x y z+x y t+x z t+y x t$.

The values of the variables are considered positive.

Given that $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$, take points $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ on segments $B H$ and $C H$ respectively, such that $B_{1} C_{1} / / B C$. Let the circumcircle $\Gamma_{1}$ of $\triangle B_{1} H C_{1}$ have its center on line $B C$. Prove: the circumcircle $\Gamma$ of $\triangle A B C$ is tangent to circle $\Gamma_{1}$.

Let $m \neq -1$ be a real number. Consider the quadratic equation
$$
(m + 1)x^2 + 4mx + m - 3 =0.
$$
Which of the following must be true?

$ \rm(I)$ Both roots of this equation must be real.

$ \rm(II)$ If both roots are real, then one of the roots must be less than $-1.$

$ \rm(III)$ If both roots are real, then one of the roots must be larger than $1.$

$$
\mathrm a. ~ \text{Only} ~(\mathrm I)\rm \mathrm b. ~(I)~and~(II) \mathrm c. ~Only~(III) \mathrm d. ~Both~(I)~and~(III) \mathrm e. ~(I), (II),~and~(III)
$$

A face of a cuboid is $A B C D$, and the perpendicular edges are $A E, B F, C G, D H$. A point mass moves along the edges from $B$ to $C$, then to $D$, and finally to $H$. Construct the points on the edges $B C, C D$, and $D H$ where the moving point is closest to the body diagonal $A G$.

Determine the positions of these points, as well as their distances from $A G$, using calculations for the case where $A B=3, A D=2, A E=1$.

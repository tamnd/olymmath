Two schoolchildren were assigned to keep a weather calendar. They were to mark the day with a "+" sign if the weather was good, and a "-" sign if the weather was bad. The first schoolchild proceeded as follows: He made observations three times a day - in the morning, at noon, and in the evening. If it rained at least once during one of the observations, he marked it with a "-". In all other cases, he marked it with a "+". The second schoolchild made observations at the same times as the first. If it did not rain at least once during one of the observations, he marked it with a "+". In all other cases, he marked it with a "-". Thus, each day the weather received one of the following ratings: $++;+\cdots ;-+;-$. Can all these ratings actually occur?

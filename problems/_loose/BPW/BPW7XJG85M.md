We are given the lines $e_{1}, e_{2}, \ldots, e_{n}$ on the plane. The perpendicular projection of an arbitrary point $P_{1}$ on $e_{1}$ onto $e_{2}$ is $P_{2}$, the perpendicular projection of $P_{2}$ onto $e_{3}$ is $P_{3}$, and so on, the perpendicular projection of $P_{n}$ onto $e_{1}$ is the point $Q$. Show that the point $P_{1}$ can be chosen on $e_{1}$ such that $Q$ coincides with $P_{1}$.

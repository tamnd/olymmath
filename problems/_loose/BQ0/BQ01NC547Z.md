We have collected 1001 pebbles into a pile. We arbitrarily divide the pile into two parts and multiply the number of pebbles in each part. Then, we repeat this process on one of the parts - if it contains at least two pebbles - by dividing it into two new parts and calculating the product of the number of pebbles in the two new parts. We continue this process until only one pebble remains in each part.

Between what limits does the sum of the 1000 products obtained this way vary?

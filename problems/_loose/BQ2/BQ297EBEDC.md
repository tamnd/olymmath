Given $\triangle A B C$ is inscribed in $\odot O, P$ is any point inside $\triangle A B C$, and through point $P$ draw lines parallel to $A B, A C, B C$, intersecting $B C, A C$ at $F, E$, intersecting $A B, B C$ at $K, I$, and intersecting $A B, A C$ at $G, H, A D$ is a chord of $\odot O$ passing through point $P$. Prove: $E F^{2}+K I^{2}+G H^{2} \geqslant 4 P A \cdot P D$.
(Mathematical Bulletin, Issue 9, 1991)

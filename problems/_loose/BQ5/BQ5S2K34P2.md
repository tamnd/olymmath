(1) Examine the variation and convexity of $ f(x)$ to draw the garph of $ f(x)$.

(2) For a positive number $ C$, let $ D_1$ be the region bounded by $ y=f(x)$, the $ x$-axis and $ x=C$. Denote $ V_1(C)$ the volume obtained by rotation of $ D_1$ about the $ x$-axis. Find $ \lim_{C\rightarrow \infty} V_1(C)$.

(3) Let $ M$ be the maximum value of $ y=f(x)$ for $ x\geq 0$. Denote $ D_2$ the region bounded by $ y=f(x)$, the $ y$-axis and $ y=M$.
Find the volume $ V_2$ obtained by rotation of $ D_2$ about the $ y$-axis.

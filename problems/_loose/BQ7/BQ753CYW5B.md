Let $P$ be an interior point of a triangle $ABC$. Through $P$, draw parallels $KP, MP$, and $NP$ to the sides $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$ that divide the initial triangle into three smaller triangles and three parallelograms. Let $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ be the areas of the new triangles and $S$ the area of triangle $ABC$. Prove that

$$
S \leq 3\left(S_{1}+S_{2}+S_{3}\right)
$$

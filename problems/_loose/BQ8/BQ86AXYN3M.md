Let $f:[0,1] \rightarrow[0,1]$ be a bijective function. Show that the following statements are equivalent:
a) $(f \circ f)(x)=x,(\forall) x \in[0,1]$;
b) $(f \circ f)(x)+f(x)=x+f^{-1}(x)$, ( $\left.\forall\right) x \in[0,1]$

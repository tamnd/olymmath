Let $a_{0}, a_{1}, \cdots, a_{k}$ be positive real numbers, prove:
$$
\frac{a_{1}}{\left(a_{0}+a_{1}\right)^{n+1}}+\frac{a_{2}}{\left(a_{0}+a_{1}+a_{2}\right)^{n+1}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{k}}{\left(a_{0}+a_{1}+\cdots+a_{k}\right)^{n+1}}<\frac{1}{a_{0}^{n}}(n \in \mathbf{N}) .
$$

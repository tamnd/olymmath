Let $M$ be an arbitrary point on the side $A B=1$ of the equilateral triangle $A B C$. The points $P$ and $Q$ are orthogonal projections of $M$ on $A C$ and $B C$, while $P_{1}$ and $Q_{1}$ are orthogonal projections of $P$ and $Q$ on $A B$.

a) Prove that $P_{1} Q_{1}=\frac{3}{4}$.

b) Find the position of $M$ for which the segment $P Q$ is with the smallest length.

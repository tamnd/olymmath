For each real number $x,\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. For example $\lfloor 2.8\rfloor=2$. Let $r \geq 0$ be a real number such that for all integers $m, n, m \mid n$ implies $\lfloor m r\rfloor\lfloor\lfloor n r\rfloor$. Prove that $r$ is an integer.

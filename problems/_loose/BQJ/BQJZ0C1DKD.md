Given the rectangle $A B C D$. We construct the circle $k_{1}$ which passes through $A$ and $B$, and touches the line $C D$, as well as the circle $k_{2}$ which passes through $A$ and $D$, and touches $B C$. Let the radii of $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be denoted by $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$, respectively, and the sides of the rectangle by $a$ and $b$. It is to be proven that

$$
r_{1}+r_{2} \geqq \frac{5}{8}(a+b)
$$

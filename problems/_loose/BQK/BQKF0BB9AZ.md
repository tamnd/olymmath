An $8 \times 8 \times n$ rectangular prism is made up from $1 \times 1 \times 1$ cubes. Suppose that $A$ is the surface area of the prism and $B$ is the combined surface area of the $1 \times 1 \times 1$ cubes that make up the prism. What is the sum of the values of $n$ for which $\frac{B}{A}$ is an integer?
(A) 86
(B) 90
(C) 70
(D) 78
(E) 96

59 Let $S_{r}=x^{r}+y^{r}+z^{r}$, where $x, y, z$ are real numbers. It is known that when $S_{1}=$ 0, for
$$
(m, n)=(2,3),(3,2),(2,5) \text { or }(5,2) \text {, }
$$

we have
$$
\frac{S_{m+n}}{m+n}=\frac{S_{m}}{m} \cdot \frac{S_{n}}{n}
$$

Determine all other positive integer pairs $(m, n)$ that satisfy (1).

Let's draw a circle around the triangle $A B C$ and draw tangents to this circle at points $B$ and $C$. Let the intersection of these tangents be $P$. Draw a line from $P$ that intersects the line $A C$ at $B^{\prime}$, the line $A B$ at $C^{\prime}$, and the circle at points $Q$ and $R$. Prove that

$$
C^{\prime} Q: B^{\prime} Q=C^{\prime} R:-B^{\prime} R .
$$

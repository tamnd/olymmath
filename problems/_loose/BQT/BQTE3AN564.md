a) On an infinite sheet of graph paper, two players play the following game: the first player colors an arbitrary cell red; the second player colors an arbitrary uncolored cell blue; then the first player colors an arbitrary uncolored cell red, and the second player colors another uncolored cell blue, and so on. The first player aims to have the centers of some four red cells form a square with sides parallel to the grid lines, while the second player wants to prevent this. Can the first player win?

b) What would be the answer to this question if the second player colors two cells blue at once?

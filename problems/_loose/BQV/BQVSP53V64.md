A variable point $P$ is considered on this circle, and the chord $P P^{\prime}$, perpendicular to $A P$, is drawn. Let $C$ be the point symmetric to $B$ with respect to $P P^{\prime}$. Find the geometric locus of the point $Q$, the intersection of $P P^{\prime}$ with $A C$, as $P$ varies on the circle.

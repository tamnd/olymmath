On the plane, there are two points $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ be some point on the plane that is equidistant from points $A$ and $B$. We construct a sequence of points

$C_{1}=C, C_{2}, C_{3}, \ldots$, where $C_{n+1}$ is the circumcenter of triangle $A B C_{n}$. For which position of point $C$

a) will the point $C_{n}$ fall on the midpoint of segment $A B$ (in this case, $C_{n+1}$ and subsequent terms of the sequence are not defined)?

b) will the point $C_{n}$ coincide with $C$?

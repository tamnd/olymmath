Let $ABC$ be a triangle. We denote $H$ as the orthocenter and $A', B'$, and $C'$ as the midpoints of the sides $[BC], [AC]$, and $[AB]$ respectively. Show that the symmetrics of $H$ with respect to the sides of the triangle, as well as the symmetrics of $H$ with respect to $A', B'$, and $C'$, are all on the circumcircle of $ABC$.

Given the parabola $C: y=\frac{1}{2} x^{2}$ and the line $l: y=k x-1$ have no common points, let point $P$ be a moving point on the line $l$, and draw two tangent lines from $P$ to the parabola $C$, with $A$ and $B$ being the points of tangency.
(1) Prove: The line $A B$ always passes through a fixed point $Q$;
(2) If the line connecting point $P$ and the fixed point $Q$ from (1) intersects the parabola $C$ at points $M$ and $N$, prove: $\frac{|P M|}{|P N|}=\frac{|Q M|}{|Q N|}$.

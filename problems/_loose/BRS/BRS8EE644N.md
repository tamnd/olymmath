The probability it stops on the next largest section is 1 in 3 . The probability it stops on the smallest section is
(A) $\frac{1}{4}$
(B) $\frac{2}{5}$
(C) $\frac{1}{6}$
(D) $\frac{2}{7}$
(E) $\frac{3}{10}$

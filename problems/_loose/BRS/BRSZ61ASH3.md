Mathematician Wiener is the founder of cybernetics. At the ceremony where he was granted his Ph.D. from Harvard University, someone, curious about his youthful appearance, asked him about his age. Wiener's response was quite interesting. He said, "The cube of my age is a four-digit number, and the fourth power of my age is a six-digit number. These two numbers together use all the digits from 0 to 9 exactly once, without repetition or omission." So, how old was Wiener that year? (Note: The cube of a number $a$ is $a \times a \times a$, and the fourth power of a number $a$ is $a \times a \times a \times a$)

Let $k$ and $\ell$ be natural numbers. Determine the smallest natural number $m$ for which it is possible to divide the square $A B C D$ into $m$ rectangles with sides parallel to the sides of the square such that every line parallel to $A B$ that intersects the interior of the square intersects at least $k$ rectangles, and every line parallel to $B C$ that intersects the interior of the square intersects at least $\ell$ rectangles.

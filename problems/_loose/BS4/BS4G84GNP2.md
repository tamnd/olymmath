Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ and a point $O$ inside it. It is known that $\angle A O B=\angle C O D=120^{\circ}, A O=O B$ and $C O=O D$. Let $K, L$ and $M$ be the midpoints of segments $A B, B C$ and $C D$ respectively. Prove that a) $K L=L M$; b) triangle $K L M$ is equilateral.

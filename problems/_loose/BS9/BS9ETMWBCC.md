Let $x, y, z$ all be positive numbers, prove that
$$
\left(\frac{x+y+z}{3}\right)^{x+y+z} \leqslant x^{x} y^{y} z^{x} \leqslant\left(\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}}{x+y+z}\right)^{x+y+z}
$$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. Consider three points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ on the sides $[BC], [CA]$, and $[AB]$ respectively. We denote $A_{3}, B_{3}$, and $C_{3}$ as the symmetrics of $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ with respect to the midpoints of their respective sides. We denote $A_{2}, B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$ as the second points of intersection of $\Gamma$ with the circumcircles of $AB_{1}C_{1}, BC_{1}A_{1}$, and $CA_{1}B_{1}$ respectively.

Show that the triangles $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ and $A_{3} B_{3} C_{3}$ are similar.

We can also find a slightly different type of exercise related to the Miquel point and similarities: a figure where a particular direct similarity and its center appear clearly. Theorem 37 can then be used to find cyclic points.

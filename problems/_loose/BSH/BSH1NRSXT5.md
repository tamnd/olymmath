Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}>0$ and $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n} \geqslant 0$ not all zero. We are also given two real numbers $0<p<q$. Show that then:

$$
\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} x_{i}^{p}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}}\right)^{1 / p} \leqslant\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} x_{i}^{q}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}}\right)^{1 / q}
$$

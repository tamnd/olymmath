On a plane, there are $N$ lines ( $N>1$ ), no three of which intersect at the same point and no two are parallel. Prove that in the parts into which these lines divide the plane, it is possible to place non-zero integers, in absolute value not exceeding $N$, such that the sums of the numbers on either side of any of the given lines are equal to zero.

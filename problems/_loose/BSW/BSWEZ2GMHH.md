Ten children decide to run around a circular lake with a perimeter of $200 \mathrm{~m}$. At the start of the race, the ten children are standing in distinct positions, and each will run clockwise or counterclockwise, depending on their preference, at a speed of $\frac{200}{k} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{min}$, where $k$ is a positive integer. Show that after a certain amount of time, there will be a moment when all the children are exactly back at their initial positions.

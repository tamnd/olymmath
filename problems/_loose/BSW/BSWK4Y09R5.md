Let $ABC$ and $XYZ$ be two acute triangles such that the points $A, B, C, X, Y, Z$ are concyclic. We denote $M_{BC}, M_{CA}, M_{AB}, M_{YZ}, M_{ZX}, M_{XY}$ as the midpoints of the segments $[BC], [CA], [AB], [YZ], [ZX]$, and $[XY]$, respectively.

We assume that the points $M_{BC}, M_{CA}, M_{AB}$, and $M_{YZ}$ lie on the same circle $\mathcal{E}$. Show that $M_{ZX}$ lies on $\mathcal{E}$ if and only if $M_{XY}$ lies on $\mathcal{E}$.

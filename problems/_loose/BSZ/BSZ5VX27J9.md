If $ xy = b$ and $ \frac{1}{x^2} + \frac{1}{y^2} = a$ , then $ (x + y)^2$ equals:

$ \textbf{(A)}\ (a + 2b)^2
\textbf{(B)}\ a^2 + b^2
\textbf{(C)}\ b(ab + 2)
\textbf{(D)}\ ab(b + 2)
\textbf{(E)}\ \frac{1}{a} + 2b$

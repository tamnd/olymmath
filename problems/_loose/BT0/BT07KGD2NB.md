Given is a scalene triangle $ABC$ with the circumcircle $\omega$ and the incenter $I$. The lines $AI, BI$, and $CI$ intersect the circle $\omega$ again at points $D, E, F$ respectively, different from $A, B, C$. Lines through point $I$ parallel to $BC, CA$, and $AB$ intersect the lines $EF, FD$, and $DE$ at points $K, L$, and $M$ respectively. Prove that the points $K, L$, and $M$ are collinear.

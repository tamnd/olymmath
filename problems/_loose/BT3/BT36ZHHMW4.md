Given real numbers $x$ and $y$. Let $s_{1}=x+y, s_{2}=x^2+y^2, s_{3}=x^3+y^3, s_{4}=x^4+y^4$ and $t=xy$.
**a)** Prove, that number $t$ is rational, if $s_{2}, s_{3}$ and $s_{4}$ are rational numbers.
**b)** Prove, that number $s_{1}$ is rational, if $s_{2}, s_{3}$ and $s_{4}$ are rational numbers.
**c)** Can number $s_{1}$ be irrational, if $s_{2}$ and $s_{3}$ are rational numbers?

Let $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \Delta$ be a triangle, and let $P$ be a point on the circumcircle of this triangle. From $P$, we draw perpendiculars to the side $A_{2} A_{3}$, and to the tangents at points $A_{2}$ and $A_{3}$; the feet of these perpendiculars are $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$, respectively. Show that $P P_{1}$ is the geometric mean of the distances $P P_{2}$ and $P P_{3}$!

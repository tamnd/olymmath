The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies:
$a_{1}=0, a_{2}=-\frac{2}{3}$,
$(n-1) a_{n+1}-n a_{n}=a_{n} a_{n+1}(n \geqslant 2)$.
(1) Find the general term formula for $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Prove: $\prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(2+a_{k}\right)>\sqrt{2 n+1}$.

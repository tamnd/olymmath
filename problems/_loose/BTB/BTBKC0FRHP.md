111568 Topic: $[$ Properties of Symmetries and Axes of Symmetry $]$

Heights $A A 1$ and $C C 1$ of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ intersect at point $H$. Point $B 0$ is the midpoint of side $A C$. Prove that the point of intersection of the lines symmetric to $B B 0$ and $H B 0$ with respect to the bisectors of angles $A B C$ and $A H C$ respectively, lies on the line $A 1 C 1$.

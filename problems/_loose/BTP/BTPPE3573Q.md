The number $N$ is written in the decimal system $N=\overline{a_{n} a_{n-1} \ldots a_{1} a_{0}}$. Prove the following divisibility rules:

a) $N$ is divisible by $3 \Leftrightarrow a_{n}+a_{n-1}+\ldots+a_{1}+a_{0}$ is divisible by 3 ;

b) $N$ is divisible by $9 \Leftrightarrow a_{n}+a_{n-1}+\ldots+a_{1}+a_{0}$ is divisible by 9 ;

c) $N$ is divisible by $11 \leftrightarrow(-1)^{n} a_{n}+(-1)^{n-1} a_{n-1}+\ldots+a_{1}+a_{0}$ is divisible by 11 .

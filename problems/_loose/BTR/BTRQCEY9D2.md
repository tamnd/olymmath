Given a subset $A$ of positive integers satisfying: (1) $A$ has at least 3 elements; (2) if $m \in A$, then all divisors of $m$ belong to $A$; (3) if $b, c \in A$, and $1<b<c$, then $1+bc \in A$. Prove that $A$ contains all positive integers.

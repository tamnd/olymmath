In an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, altitudes $\overline{B B^{\prime}}$ and $\overline{C C^{\prime}}$ are drawn. A line through the orthocenter $H$ intersects sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{A C}$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Let $M^{\prime}$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $M$ to $\overline{B B^{\prime}}$ and $N^{\prime}$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $N$ to $\overline{C C^{\prime}}$. Prove that $M^{\prime} C^{\prime} \| N^{\prime} B^{\prime}$.

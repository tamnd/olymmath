From a point $P$ outside a circle $k$, we have constructed tangents to the circle, with points of tangency $A$ and $B$. On the shorter arc $A \mathrm{~B}$ of the circle centered at $P$ with radius $P A$, we have chosen an arbitrary point $T$. The second intersection point of line $T A$ with $k$ is $K$, and the second intersection point of line $T B$ with $k$ is $L$. Prove that $K L$ is a diameter of $k$.

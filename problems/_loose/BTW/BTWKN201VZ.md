A line $p$ is tangent to the circle $k$ circumscribed around the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Let $K$ denote the point of tangency between the line and the circle. It is known that the lines $AC$ and $BD$ intersect line $p$ to form a segment symmetric with respect to point $K$. Prove that the lines $AB$ and $CD$ also intersect line $p$ to form a segment symmetric with respect to point $K$.

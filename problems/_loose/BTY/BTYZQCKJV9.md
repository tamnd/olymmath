The medians $A D, B E$ and $C F$ of triangle $A B C$ intersect at point $G$. Let $P$ be a point inside the triangle, not lying on any of the medians. The line passing through $P$ and parallel to $A D$ intersects side $B C$ at point $A_{1}$. Similarly, points $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ are defined. Show that

$$
\overline{A_{1} D}+\overline{B_{1} E}+\overline{C_{1} F}=\frac{3}{2} \overline{P G}
$$

For functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, it is known that $f(0)=g(0)>0$ and $f^{\prime}(x) \sqrt{g^{\prime}(x)}=3$ for any $x \in[0 ; 1]$. Prove that if $x \in[0 ; 1]$, then $2 f(x)+3 g(x)>9 x$.

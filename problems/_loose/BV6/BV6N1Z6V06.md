Find all functions $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ satisfying $f(x)+f(y) \neq 0$ and

$$
\frac{f(x)-f(x-y)}{f(x)+f(x+y)}+\frac{f(x)-f(x+y)}{f(x)+f(x-y)}=0
$$

for all $x, y \in \mathbf{R}$.

For reals $x_1, x_2, x_3, \dots, x_{333} \in [-1, \infty)$ , let $S_k = \sum_{i = 1}^{333} x_i^k$ for each $k$ . If $S_2 = 777$ , compute the least possible value of $S_3$ .

Let $ABCDE$ be a convex pentagon such that $\angle BCD+\angle DEA = 180^{\circ}$ and $AD=BD$ . Prove that the circumcenters of the triangles $\triangle DEA$ , $\triangle DAB$ , $\triangle DBC$ and the point $D$ lie on a circle.

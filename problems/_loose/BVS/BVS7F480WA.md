The point $M$ on the side $A B$ of $\triangle A B C$ is such that the inradii of $\triangle A M C$ and $\triangle B M C$ are equal. The incircles of $\triangle A M C$ and $\triangle B M C$
have centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, and are tangent to the side $A B$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. It is known that $S_{A B C}=6 S_{\mathrm{PQO}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{1}}$.

a) Prove that $10 C M+5 A B=7(A C+B C)$.

b) Find the ratio $\frac{A C+B C}{A B}$.

On the sides $B C, C A$ and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$, points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ are taken. Prove that if triangles $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A B C$ are similar and oppositely oriented, then the circumcircles of triangles $A B_{1} C_{1}, A_{1} B C_{1}$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C$ pass through the circumcenter of triangle $A B C$.

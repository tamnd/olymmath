Lattice points enclosed within a circle. In this problem, we are dealing with a lattice located inside a circle $K$, more precisely, with those points of the integer lattice that are enclosed within this circle (but not on its circumference).

Prove that for any non-negative integer $n$ there exists a circle containing exactly $n$ lattice points.

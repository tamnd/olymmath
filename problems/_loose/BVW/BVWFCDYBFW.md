Determine the functions $f:[0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ that simultaneously satisfy the conditions:

i) $f$ is increasing;

ii) $f$ has a primitive $F$ with the property that $F(0)=0$ and $F(x+y) \leq F(x)+$ $F(y), \forall x, y \in[0, \infty)$.

Maximum 10 points. Imagine that your family has decided to renovate the apartment: completely replace the electrical wiring, change the plumbing, level and plaster the walls, and finish the renovation with finishing work. For this, you are looking for a construction company or a private renovation team that is ready to fully implement the planned renovation.

(a) To which class (type) of goods (services) can this type of service be attributed? Explain why. What are its characteristics (in contrast to ordinary consumer goods and services)? Explain.

(b) The choice between a private renovation team and a construction company proved difficult for the family. Provide two economically justified arguments "for" and "against" using a construction company or a private renovation team to carry out the planned renovation. (Note that if you provide more than two arguments "for" or "against," only the first two from each pair will be evaluated).

Let's characterize an equilateral triangle with a direct orientation. Let A, B, C be three points with respective affixes $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}$.

1) Show that: $z=\frac{c-a}{b-a}=e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}}=-j^{2}$ and $\arg \left(\frac{c-a}{b-a}\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}$

2) Show that: $a+j b+j^{2} c=0$.

3) Suppose now only that $A B C$ is equilateral (direct or indirect). Show that: $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-a b-b c-c a=0$ i.e. $(a-b)^{2}+(b-c)^{2}+(c-a)^{2}=0$.

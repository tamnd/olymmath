Suppose that $p$ and $q$ are distinct primes and $S$ is a subset of $\{1, 2, ..., p-1\}$. Let $N(S)$ denote the number of ordered $q$-tuples $(x_1,x_2,...,x_q)$ with $x_i \in S$, $1 \le i \le q$, such that $x_1+x_2+...+x_q \cong 0 (mod p)$.

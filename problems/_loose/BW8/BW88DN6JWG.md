Let $O$ be a fixed node on a square grid paper with a side length of 1. Now, let $P_{k}$ denote the number of all different broken lines on the grid paper that start from point $O$, have a length of $k$, and each segment lies on the grid lines. Prove that for all $k$, $P_{k}<2 \times 3^{k}$.

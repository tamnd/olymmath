Let $(G, \circ)$ and $\left(G,^{*}\right)$ be two groups defined on the same set $G$, which have the same neutral element and satisfy $x * y = (x \circ x) \circ (x \circ y), \forall x, y \in G$. Show that the two composition laws coincide and that the group defined is commutative.

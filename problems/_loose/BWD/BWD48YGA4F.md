Let $A, B, C, D$ be four points in that order on a circle. The lines $(A B)$ and $(C D)$ intersect at $M$, and the lines $(A D)$ and $(B C)$ intersect at $N$.

Show that the angles (non-oriented) $\widehat{B M C}$ and $\widehat{C N D}$ are equal if and only if $A$ and $C$ are diametrically opposite or $B$ and $D$ are diametrically opposite.

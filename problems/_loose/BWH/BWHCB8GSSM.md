In Dwarf Land, 12 dwarfs live. Each of them has an odd number of friends among the other 11; all these friendships are mutual. Each of the 12 dwarfs has a birthday in each month.

Each dwarf lives alone in a house, and each house is either red or green.

Each dwarf paints their house on their birthday each year in the color that is more common among the colors of their friends' houses.

Show that under these conditions, there must always be a point in time from which the color of all houses remains unchanged!

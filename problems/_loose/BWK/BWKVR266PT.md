Prove that under these conditions:

a) the fourth vertex $R$ of the square $DPQR$ lies on $AC$,

b) the segments $BD$ and $BP$ have the same length.

Determine the side length of the square $DPQR$

c) for $\overline{BC}=49 \text{ mm}, \overline{AC}=168 \text{ mm}$,

d) generally expressed through $a=\overline{BC}, b=\overline{AC}, c=\overline{AB}$.

Given a $\triangle A B C$ with circumcentre $O$ and circumradius $R$. The incircle of $\triangle A B C$ is of radius $r$ and touches the sides $A B, B C$ and $C A$ in the points $C_{1}, A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$. Let the lines determined by the midpoints of the segments $A B_{1}$ and $A C_{1}, B A_{1}$ and $B C_{1}, C A_{1}$ and $C B_{1}$ meet at points $C_{2}, A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$. Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ is of centre $O$ and radius $R+\frac{r}{2}$.

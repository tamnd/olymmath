Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles that do not intersect. Let $O_{1}, O_{2}$ be their centers. Let $O_{1} M_{1}$ and
$O_{2} M_{2}$ be radii parallel and in the same direction of $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ respectively. Let $O_{1} P_{1}$ and $O_{2} P_{2}$ be two other radii parallel and in the same direction of $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ respectively. The line $M_{1} M_{2}$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ at $N$ and the line $P_{1} P_{2}$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ at $Q$. Show that $M, N, P, Q$ are concyclic.

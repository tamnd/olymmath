If $\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}=1$, and $\sqrt{a}=m+\frac{a-b}{2}, \sqrt{b}=n-\frac{a-b}{2}$, where $m, n$ are rational numbers, then
(A) $m n=\frac{1}{2}$.
(B) $m^{2}+n^{2}=\frac{1}{2}$.
(C) $m+n=\frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $m-n=\frac{1}{2}$.
(China "Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1993)

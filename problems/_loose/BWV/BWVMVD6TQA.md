Divide a concentric circular ring uniformly into $n(n \geqslant 3)$ sections, and fix the numbers $1 \sim n$ in the inner ring. Question: Is it possible to fill the numbers $1 \sim n$ into the outer ring sections so that after rotating the outer ring by any number of sections, there is exactly one section where the numbers in the inner and outer rings are the same?

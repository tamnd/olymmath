Let $g, s$ be given positive integers greater than or equal to 1, and let $f(n)$ denote the sum of the $s$-th powers of all the digits of the positive integer $n$ in base $g$. Prove that $n, f(n), f(f(n)), \cdots$ is periodic (that is, the sequence $\left\{f^{(t)}(n), t=0,1,2, \cdots\right\}$ is a periodic sequence).
(China National Training Team Problem, 1990)

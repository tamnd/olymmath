There is an equation involving an unknown $x$
$$
m^{2} x+m(1-x)-2(1+x)=0,
$$

where $m$ is a constant. Among the following statements, the correct one is
(A) $m$ cannot be 0, otherwise the equation has no solution.
(B) Regardless of the value of $m$, the equation always has a unique solution.
(C) When $m$ is a certain positive number, the equation has infinitely many solutions.
(D) When $m$ is a certain negative number, the equation has no solution or infinitely many solutions.
(2nd "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1985)

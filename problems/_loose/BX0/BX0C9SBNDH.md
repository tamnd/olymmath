A soccer ball is usually made from a polyhedral fugure model, with two types of faces, hexagons and pentagons, and in every vertex incide three faces - two hexagons and one pentagon.

We call a polyhedron *soccer-ball* if it is similar to the traditional soccer ball, in the following sense: its faces are $m$-gons or $n$-gons, $m \not= n$, and in every vertex incide three faces, two of them being $m$-gons and the other one being an $n$-gon.

[list='i']

- Show that $m$ needs to be even.

- Find all soccer-ball polyhedra.

We have a regular pentagonal prism and a square plate $ABCD$, all of whose edges are equal and of unit length. The prism is placed on the (horizontally placed) plate, first with one of its side faces, such that the edge $AB$ coincides with a side edge of the prism. Then, it is placed again with another side face, such that the edge $AD$ coincides with a side edge of the prism.

What is the volume of the spatial region covered by the two positions of the prism?

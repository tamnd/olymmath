We say that a circle is *half-inscribed* in a triangle, if its center lies on one side of the triangle, and it is tangent to the other two sides. Show that a triangle that has two half-inscribed circles of equal radii, is isosceles. (Recall that a triangle is said to be *isosceles*, if it has two sides of equal length.)

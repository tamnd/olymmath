14 Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle, $T$ be an arc of a circle passing through points $A$ and $C$, $T_{1}$ be a circle tangent to sides $AD$, $DC$, and arc $T$, and $T_{2}$ be a circle tangent to sides $AB$, $BC$, and arc $T$. Here, $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ are both inside the rectangle $ABCD$. Let the radius of $T_{j}$ be $r_{j}, j=1,2$. $r$ is the radius of the incircle of $\triangle ABC$. Prove: (1) $r_{1}+r_{2}=2r$; (2) a common tangent of $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ is parallel to $AC$, and its length is the difference between the lengths of the two sides of the rectangle $ABCD$.

From point $A$ on a horizontal plane, we launch a body at an angle $\alpha$ with a speed of $c \mathrm{~m} /$ sec. On the orthogonal projection of the parabolic trajectory, from point $B$ which is $a \mathrm{~m}$ away from point $A$, we simultaneously launch another body vertically upwards with the same speed. After how many seconds will the distance between the two bodies be the smallest? What is this minimum distance if $\alpha=30^{\circ}, c=50 \mathrm{~m}$ sec and $a=50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$?

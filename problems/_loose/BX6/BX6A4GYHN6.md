Given that the circumcenter of $\triangle A B C$ is $O$, and the circumcircle is circle $\Gamma$, the rays $A O, B O, C O$ intersect circle $\Gamma$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively. $X$ is a point inside $\triangle A B C$, and the rays $A X, B X, C X$ intersect circle $\Gamma$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ respectively. The rays $D X, E X, F X$ intersect circle $\Gamma$ at points $D_{1}, E_{1}, F_{1}$ respectively. Prove that the three lines $A_{1} D_{1}, B_{1} E_{1}, C_{1} F_{1}$ are concurrent.

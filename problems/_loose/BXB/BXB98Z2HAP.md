In Italy, they produce clocks where the hour hand makes one revolution in a day, and the minute hand makes 24 revolutions, with the minute hand being longer than the hour hand (in regular clocks, the hour hand makes two revolutions in a day, and the minute hand makes 24). Consider all positions of the two hands and the zero mark on the Italian clocks that also occur on regular clocks. How many such positions exist on the Italian clocks in a day? (The zero mark indicates 24 hours on Italian clocks and 12 hours on regular clocks.)

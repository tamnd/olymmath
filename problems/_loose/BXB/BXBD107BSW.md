Let the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 1)$ satisfy $x_{1}=1, x_{2}=4$, and for all integers $n > 1, x_{n}=$ $\sqrt{x_{n-1} x_{n+1}+1}$.
(1) Prove: All terms of this sequence are positive integers;
(2) Prove: For all integers $n \geqslant 1, 2 x_{n} x_{n+1}+1$ is a perfect square.

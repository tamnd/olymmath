For the following two questions, find all possible values of the positive integer $n$:
(1) There exist $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, such that $a_{i}$ and $a_{i}+i$ $(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$ form a complete residue system modulo $n$;
(2) There exist $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, such that $a_{i}$, $a_{i}+i$, and $a_{i}-i$ $(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$ form a complete residue system modulo $n$.

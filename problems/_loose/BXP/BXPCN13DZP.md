Prove that for any prime $p \geqslant 7$, there exist positive integers $n$ and integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$; $y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}$, not divisible by $p$, such that
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x_{1}^{2}+y_{1}^{2} \equiv x_{2}^{2} (\bmod p), \\
x_{2}^{2}+y_{2}^{2} \equiv x_{3}^{2} (\bmod p), \\
\cdots \cdots \\
x_{n}^{2}+y_{n}^{2} \equiv x_{n+1}^{2} (\bmod p) .
\end{array}
$$

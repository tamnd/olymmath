Let $G$ be a 2-connected graph, whose cycle space is generated by a sparse set of cycles $\mathcal{B}$. According to MacLane's theorem, there exists a double cover of $G$ consisting of cycles that generate $\mathcal{C}(G)$, i.e., the cycle boundaries in any drawing of $G$. Directly prove (i.e., without using MacLane's theorem) that $\mathcal{B}$ can be extended to a double cycle cover $\mathcal{D}$ of $G$.

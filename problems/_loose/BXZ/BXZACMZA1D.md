Read the symmetry properties of the curves represented by the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2} \\
& b^{2} x^{2}+a^{2} y^{2}=b^{2} a^{2} \\
& b^{2} x^{2}-a^{2} y^{2}=b^{2} a^{2} \\
& y^{2}=2 p x \\
& y=\sin x \\
& y=\tan x \\
& y=\log x
\end{aligned}
$$

Note. Below we will use the expression " $y$ increases proportionally with $x$ " in the sense of: "the increment of $y$ is proportional to the increment of $x$ ".

Let $a_{n}$ denote the number of arrangements of length $n$ composed of 0s and 1s without two 1s being adjacent (e.g., 00101, 10100 both have a length of 5). Let $a_{0}=1$. Try to prove: $a_{4 k+2}$ is divisible by 3, $k \geqslant 0$.

Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $\left|a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right| \leq 1$, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\left(b_{n}\right)$ be a sequence defined by $b_{n}=\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}}{n}$. Prove that $\left|b_{n+1}-b_{n}\right| \leq \frac{1}{2}$, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

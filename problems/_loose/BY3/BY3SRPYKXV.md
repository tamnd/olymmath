Write each rational number in $(0,1]$ as $\frac{a}{b}, \operatorname{gcd}(a, b)=1$, and cover $\frac{a}{b}$ with the interval
$$
\left[\frac{a}{b}-\frac{1}{4 b^{2}}, \frac{a}{b}+\frac{1}{4 b^{2}}\right]
$$

Prove: The number $\sqrt{2} / 2$ is not covered.

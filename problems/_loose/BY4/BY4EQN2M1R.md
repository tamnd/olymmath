Angela has the polynomials $x-1,(x-1)(x-2),(x-1)(x-2)(x-3), \ldots$ up to $(x-1)(x-2) \cdots(x-2017)(x-2018)$, and she divides them into two groups. Let $p(x)$ be the product of the polynomials in the first group and $q(x)$ the product of the polynomials in the second group. Angela notices that the polynomial $p(x)$ divides the polynomial $q(x)$, and that the degree of the quotient $\frac{q(x)}{p(x)}$ is the smallest possible: what is this degree?

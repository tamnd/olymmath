\section*{Task 1 - 111211}

Five soldiers $A, B, C, D, E$ from five socialist countries meet at a meeting during a joint maneuver of the friendly armies. Only members of the Bulgarian, Polish, Hungarian, Soviet armed forces, and the National People's Army of the GDR participate in the maneuver. Furthermore, the following is known:

(1) Each of the soldiers $A, B, C$ and $D$ masters, in addition to the language of their country, exactly one of the following languages as a "second language": Bulgarian, Polish, Hungarian, Russian, German.

(1a) These four second languages are pairwise different.

(2) $E$ does not master any foreign language.

(3) $A$ masters a language that is also mastered by the Soviet soldier.

(4) $B$ does not master any Slavic language, i.e., neither Bulgarian nor Polish nor Russian.

(5) The NVA member can communicate with $E$ only if one of the other three soldiers, namely $C$, acts as an interpreter.

(6) The Bulgarian can communicate with the Hungarian only through two of the other soldiers, namely $D$ and $B$.

It is to be determined for each of these soldiers which country they belong to and which second language they master, if any.

Professor Carlão decided to create a math question worth a total of 10 points and consisting of three items: $a, b$, and $c$. After formulating the items, he became unsure about the best way to distribute the 10 points among the items so that each one is worth a positive integer number of points.

a) Joana, a teacher friend of Carlão, suggested that item $c$ should be worth the same number of points as the sum of items $a$ and $b$ because, according to her, item $c$ is more difficult. If Carlão follows Joana's suggestion, in how many different ways can he distribute the points?

b) Disregarding Joana's suggestion, that is, considering that Carlão will distribute the points in any way, in how many different ways can he distribute the 10 points of the question among the three items?

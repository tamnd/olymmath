Consider the rational numbers $a=\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2}+\frac{1}{3 \cdot 4}+\frac{1}{5 \cdot 6}+\ldots . .+\frac{1}{2015 \cdot 2016}$

$$
b=\frac{1}{2 \cdot 3}+\frac{1}{4 \cdot 5}+\frac{1}{6 \cdot 7}+\ldots \ldots . .+\frac{1}{2016 \cdot 2017}
$$

a) Compare the numbers a and b.

b) Solve in the set of positive rational numbers the equation $|x-b|=a$.

c) Determine whether: i) there exists $\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}^{*}$ such that nb and na are simultaneously natural numbers?

ii) there exists $\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}^{*}$ such that nb and na are consecutive natural numbers?

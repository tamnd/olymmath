(Extension of Triangle Sides or Circum-Triangle Conclusion) Let the area of $\triangle ABC$ be $S$. If the sides $AB, CB, CA$ of $\triangle ABC$ are extended to $B', C', A'$, such that $BB'=\lambda_{1} AB, CC'=\lambda_{2} BC, AA'=\lambda_{3} CA$, a new $\triangle A' B' C'$ is obtained, and let its area be $S'$. Then,
$$
\frac{S'}{S}=1+\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}+\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{1} \lambda_{2}+\lambda_{2} \lambda_{3}+\lambda_{3} \lambda_{1}
$$

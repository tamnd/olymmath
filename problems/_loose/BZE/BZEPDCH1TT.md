Given a parallelogram $A B C D$. A circle is tangent to side $A C$ of triangle $A B C$, as well as the extensions of sides $B A$ and $B C$ at points $P$ and $S$ respectively. Segment $P S$ intersects sides $D A$ and $D C$ at points $Q$ and $R$. Prove that the incircle of triangle $C D A$ touches sides $A D$ and $D C$ at points $Q$ and $R$.

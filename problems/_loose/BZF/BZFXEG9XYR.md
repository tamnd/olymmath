Prove that under inversion, a line or circle transforms into a line or circle.

The angle between two intersecting spheres (a sphere and a plane) is defined as the angle between the tangent planes to the spheres, drawn through any point of intersection.

The angle between two intersecting circles in space (a circle and a line) is defined as the angle between the tangent lines to the circles, drawn through any point of intersection of the circles.

Four bugs crawl along the edges of a triangular pyramid, with each bug remaining in only one face (each face has its own bug). Each bug traverses the boundary of its face in a certain direction, such that any two bugs on a common edge crawl in opposite directions. Prove that if the speeds (possibly non-constant) of each bug are always greater than 1 cm/s, then at some point, two bugs will inevitably meet.

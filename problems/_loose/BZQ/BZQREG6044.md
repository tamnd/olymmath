$2 a y+b^{2}=0$ is externally tangent to $(a, b \in \mathbf{N}, a \neq 0)$, and the moving circle $C_{1}$ is tangent to the $x$-axis. Find:
(1) The equation of the locus $L$ of the center of the moving circle $C_{1}$;
(2) If the line $4(\sqrt{7}-1) a b x-4 a y+b^{2}+a^{2}-6958 a=0$ intersects the curve $L$ at exactly one point, find the values of $a$ and $b$.

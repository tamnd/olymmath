Given the side $AB = c$ of triangle $ABC$, and the height $m_b$ from vertex $B$, we also know that the foot of the altitude from $A$ is twice as far from one endpoint of side $BC$ as it is from the other. Construct the triangle. (For first-year students and younger, it is sufficient to describe the construction in the following three cases: $m_b = 24, 30, 36 \text{mm}$ and in each case $c = 90 \text{mm}$.)

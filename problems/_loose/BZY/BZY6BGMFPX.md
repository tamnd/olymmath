Let the complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}$ satisfy the relation: $z_{1} z_{2}+\bar{A} z_{1}+A z_{2}=0$, where $A$ is a non-zero complex number.

Prove (1) $\left|z_{1}+A\right| \cdot\left|z_{2}+A\right|=|A|^{2}$;
(2) $\frac{z_{1}+A}{z_{2}+A}=\left|\frac{z_{1}+A}{z_{2}+A}\right|$.

Let $u, v, w$ be integers that are pairwise relatively prime. It is to be proven that

$$
b=u v+v w+w u \text { and } c=u v w
$$

are also relatively prime. Is the following converse of this statement true: if $u, v$ and $w$ are integers such that $b$ and $c$ are relatively prime, then $u, v, w$ are pairwise relatively prime?

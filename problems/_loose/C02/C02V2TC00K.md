A man asked me: "How old are you, my son?"

To this question, I replied:

"If my father were seven times as old

As I was eight years ago in the course of time,

A quarter of my father's life

Would now certainly make fourteen.

Please calculate from this, if you already know,

How many years weigh on my shoulders!"

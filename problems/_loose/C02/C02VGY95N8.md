(Yugoslavia, 83). Prove that among the members of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, defined by the relations

$$
a_{1}=2, a_{n+1}=\left[(3 / 2) a_{n}\right] \text { for } n \in \mathbf{N} \text {, }
$$

there are infinitely many even and infinitely many odd numbers.

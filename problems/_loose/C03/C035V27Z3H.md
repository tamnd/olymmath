Let $N$ be the set of square-free positive integers less than or equal to 50. (A *square-free* number is an integer that is not divisible by a perfect square bigger than 1.) How many 3-element subsets $S$ of $N$ are there such that the greatest common divisor of all 3 numbers in $S$ is 1, but no pair of numbers in $S$ is relatively prime?

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of an acute triangle $ABC$ . Choose points $D$ on segment $AB$ and $E$ on segment $AC$ such that $\overline{DE}$ bisects $\overline{AH}$ and $\angle DHE=90^\circ$ . Let $X$ be a point on line $DE$ such that $\overline{HX} \parallel \overline{BC}$ . Prove that $\angle BXC=90^\circ$ .

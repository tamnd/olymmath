In an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with angle $A$ equal to $60^{\circ}$, the altitudes intersect at point $H$.

a) Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of segments $BH$ and $CH$ with sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively. Prove that points $M, N$, and $H$ lie on the same line.

b) Prove that the center $O$ of the circumscribed circle also lies on the same line.

Pandiagonal Heterosquare. We define a pandiagonal heterosquare as an arrangement of the first $n^{2}$ positive integers in a square, such that no two sums of numbers along any row, column, or diagonal (straight or broken) are the same*. Does there exist an $n$ for which these $4 n$ sums coincide with consecutive integers?

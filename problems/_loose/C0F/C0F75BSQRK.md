Natural numbers $m_{1}, \ldots, m_{n}$ are pairwise coprime. Prove that the number $x=\left(m_{2} \ldots m_{n}\right)^{\varphi\left(m_{1}\right)}$ is a solution to the system
$x \equiv 1\left(\bmod m_{1}\right)$,

$x \equiv 0\left(\bmod m_{2}\right)$,

$x \equiv 0\left(\bmod m_{n}\right)$.

Let $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ be two circles with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ respectively, intersecting at $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ be the intersection of the tangent to $C_{2}$ at $B$ and $D$ the intersection of the tangent to $C_{1}$ at $B$. Let $X$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\widehat{B A C}$ and $C_{1}$. Let $Y$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\widehat{B A D}$ and $C_{2}$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the centers of the circumcircles of $A C D$ and $A X Y$ respectively. Show that $P Q$ and $O_{1} O_{2}$ are perpendicular.

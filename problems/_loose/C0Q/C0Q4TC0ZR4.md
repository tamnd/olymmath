Given $x=t^{\frac{1}{t-1}}$ and $y=t^{\frac{t}{t-1}}(t>0, t \neq 1)$, the relationship between $x$ and $y$ is
(A) $y^{x}=x^{\frac{1}{y}}$.
(B) $y^{\frac{1}{x}}=x^{y}$.
(C) $y^{x}=x^{y}$.
(D) $x^{x}=y^{y}$.
(E) None of the above.
(20th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1969)

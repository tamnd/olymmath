Integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}, x_{5}$, whose sum is positive, are arranged in a circle. If $x_{i}<0$, then the numbers $x_{i-1}, x_{i}, x_{i+1}$ can be replaced by $x_{i-1}+x_{i}, -x_{i}$, $x_{i+1}+x_{i}$ (it is assumed that $x_{i+5}=x_{i}$). Prove that after a finite number of such operations, all numbers will become non-negative.

A convex polygon is cut into $p$ triangles such that there are no vertices of other triangles on their sides. Let $n$ and $m$ be the number of vertices of these triangles lying on the boundary of the original polygon and inside it, respectively.

a) Prove that $p=n+2 m-2$.

b) Prove that the number of segments that are sides of the obtained triangles is $2 n+3 m-3$.

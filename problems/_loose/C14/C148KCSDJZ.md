Six students helped with the fruit harvest. They received recognition bonuses according to their performance. Each of them donated half of the received amount to the solidarity fund. Furthermore, the following is known about these students:

(1) None of them donated less than $6 \mathrm{M}$ and none more than $12 \mathrm{M}$.

(2) Konrad donated more than Peter.

(3) Helga donated more than Gisela, Gisela more than Peter, Peter more than Inge.

(4) Frank donated more than Helga and Helga more than Konrad.

(5) Helga donated $2 \mathrm{M}$ less than Frank, Peter $2 \mathrm{M}$ more than Inge.

(6) All donated full mark amounts.

How much money did each of the students receive for picking fruit?

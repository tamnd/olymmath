A woman carried water in a straight cylindrical bucket from the well to the kitchen. The side of the bucket had a hole at a distance of $\frac{1}{4}$ of the bucket's height from the bottom, and thus, half a bucket of water leaked out on the way from the well to the kitchen. The woman plugged the hole, but soon the bottom of the bucket also leaked and on this same path, $\frac{2}{3}$ of the bucket's contents leaked out. The woman plugged this hole as well, but later both plugs fell out and at this point, 1 meter before the kitchen, only $\frac{1}{40}$ of the water remained in the bucket. How far was the well from the kitchen? (For simplicity, we assume that the same amount of water leaks out at equal time intervals through each hole.)

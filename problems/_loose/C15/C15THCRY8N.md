16-6 (A21, Sweden) Let $p(x)$ be an integer-coefficient polynomial, and not a constant. It is known that there are $n(p)$ integers $k$ such that $[p(k)]^2=1$. Prove: $n(p)-\operatorname{deg}(p) \leqslant 2$, where $\operatorname{deg}(p)$ denotes the degree of the polynomial $p(x)$.

Let $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, \lambda \in(-2,2)$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(\sqrt{a^{2}+\lambda a b+b^{2}}+\sqrt{b^{2}+\lambda b c+c^{2}}+\sqrt{c^{2}+\lambda c a+a^{2}}\right)^{2} \geqslant(2+\lambda)(a+b+c)^{2}+ \\
(2-\lambda)(a-b)^{2} .
\end{array}
$$

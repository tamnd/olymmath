Let's prove that if we write 12 numbers on the 12 edges of a cube such that the sum of the numbers on the edges bordering each face of the cube is the same, then the following regularities also hold:

1. the sum of the 4 numbers on the parallel edges of the cube is the same;
2. the sum of the numbers on the 3 edges meeting at any vertex of the cube is equal to the sum of the numbers on the 3 edges meeting at the opposite vertex.

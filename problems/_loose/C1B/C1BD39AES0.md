In the convex quadrilateral $A D P E$, $\angle A D P = \angle A E P$. Extend $A D$ to point $B$, and extend $A E$ to point $C$, such that $\angle D P B = \angle E P C$. Let the circumcenters of $\triangle A D E$ and $\triangle A B C$ be $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively, and the two circumcircles do not touch. Prove: the line $O_{1} O_{2}$ bisects $A P$.

Let $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be i.i.d. random variables with distribution function $F=F(x)$. Show that the closure of the set

$$
\left\{\xi_{1}(\omega), \xi_{2}(\omega), \ldots\right\}, \omega \in \Omega
$$

with probability one coincides with some set that does not depend on $\omega$. Describe the structure of the specified closure in relation to $F$.

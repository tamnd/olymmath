Let $D$ be a point inside an acute triangle $ABC$ such that $\angle ADC = \angle A +\angle B$, $\angle BDA = \angle B + \angle C$ and $\angle CDB = \angle C + \angle A$. Prove that $\frac{AB \cdot CD}{AD} = \frac{AC \cdot CB} {AB}$.

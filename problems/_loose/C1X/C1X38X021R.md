Given $n$ points on a plane, and there are $m n\left(m \leqslant \frac{n-1}{2}\right)$ line segments connecting them. Prove: There exists a sequence of distinct points $V_{0}, V_{1}, \cdots, V_{m}$, such that for $1 \leqslant i \leqslant m$, the points $V_{i-1}$ and $V_{i}$ are connected by one of the given line segments.

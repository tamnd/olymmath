Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. The tangent to the circle $\Gamma$ at point $A$ intersects the line $(BC)$ at a point $P$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from vertices $B$ and $C$ respectively. Let $Q$ be the point of intersection of the line $(EF)$ with the line parallel to $(BC)$ passing through the point $A$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment $[BC]$.

Show that the lines $(AM)$ and $(PQ)$ are perpendicular.

Foma and Yerema are dividing a pile of 25 coins worth $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 25$ altyns. On each turn, one of them picks a coin from the pile, and the other decides who should get it. Foma goes first, and thereafter, the one with more altyns at the moment makes the choice; if they have the same amount, the one who chose last time does so again. Can Foma act in such a way as to ensure he ends up with more altyns than Yerema, or can Yerema always prevent Foma from doing so?

The sequence of natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ is defined by the conditions $a_{1}=1, a_{n+1}=a_{n}+a_{[\sqrt{n}]}$ for all natural $n \geqslant 1$. Prove that for each natural $k$, there is a term in this sequence that is divisible by $k$. (As usual, $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.)

(A.S. Golyanov)

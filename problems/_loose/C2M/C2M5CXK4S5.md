Given points $B, C$ lie on a circle with diameter $AD$, satisfying $AB=AC$. $P$ is any point on segment $BC$. Points $M, N$ are taken on segments $AB, AC$ respectively, such that quadrilateral $PMAN$ is a parallelogram. Segment $PL$ is the angle bisector of $\angle P$ in $\triangle MPN$, and $PD$ intersects $MN$ at point $Q$. Prove: $B$, $Q$, $L$, $C$ are concyclic.

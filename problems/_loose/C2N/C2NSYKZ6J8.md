Let $n$ be a natural number. On $2 n+1$ cards, non-zero integers are written; the sum of all numbers is also non-zero. It is required to replace the asterisks in the expression $* x^{2 n}+* x^{2 n-1}+\ldots+* x+*$ with these cards so that the resulting polynomial has no integer roots. Is it always possible to do this?

(I. Bogdanov)

Let $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and $\left(y_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be sequences given by

$$
x_{0}=0, y_{0}=1, x_{n+1}=\frac{3 x_{n}+y_{n}}{4}, y_{n+1}=\frac{2 x_{n}+3 y_{n}}{5}
$$

Prove that $\left(z_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ given by $z_{n}=y_{n}-x_{n}$, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ is a geometric progression.

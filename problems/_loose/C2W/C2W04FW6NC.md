3\{s: $\underset{\star}{\star}$ An infinite sequence of positive real numbers $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ has the following property:
$$
x_{0}=1, x_{i+1} \leqslant x_{i}, i=0,1,2, \cdots
$$
(1) Prove that for any sequence with the above property, there exists an $n \geqslant 1$, such that
$$
\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}} \geqslant 3.999 ;
$$
(2) Find a sequence with the above property such that for all $n \geqslant 1$, we have
$$
\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}}<4 .
$$

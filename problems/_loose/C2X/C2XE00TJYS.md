We cut out an isosceles triangle $ABC$ from paper. On the base $AB$, we marked points $D$ and $E$, with $D$ closer to $A$ and $E$ closer to $B$. By rotating the triangles $ACD$ and $BCE$ around the fold lines $CD$ and $CE$, respectively, the vertices $A$ and $B$ met at point $F$. Prove that the orthogonal projection of the vertex $C$ of the triangle onto the plane of triangle $DEF$ coincides with the center of the excircle of triangle $DEF$ that is tangent to side $DE$.

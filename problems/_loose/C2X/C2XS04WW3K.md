Let $E$ denote the vector space of real sequences $\left(u_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ satisfying $: \forall n \in \mathbb{N}, u_{n+2}=$ $u_{n+1}+u_{n}$.

1) Determine the two non-zero geometric sequences, denoted $\left(a_{n}\right)$ and $\left(b_{n}\right)$ with $a_{0}=b_{0}=1$ that belong to $\tilde{\mathrm{A}} E$.
2) Show that the sequences $\left(a_{n}\right)$ and $\left(b_{n}\right)$ form a basis of $E$ and deduce the dimension of $E$.
3) Let $\left(\mathcal{F}_{n}\right)$ be the sequence in $E$ such that : $\mathcal{F}_{0}=0$ and $\mathcal{F}_{1}=1$.

Determine, using the previous questions, the general term of the sequence $\left(\mathcal{F}_{n}\right)$. What sequence is this?

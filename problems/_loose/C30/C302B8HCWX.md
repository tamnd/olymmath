It is known that for some $x{}$ and $y{}$ the sums $\sin x+ \cos y$ and $\sin y + \cos x$ are positive rational numbers. Prove that there exist natural numbers $m{}$ and $n{}$ such that $m\sin x+n\cos x$ is a natural number.

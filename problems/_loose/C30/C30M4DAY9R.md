The positive integer $N$ is said* amiable *if the set $\{1,2,\ldots,N\}$ can be partitioned into pairs of elements, each pair having the sum of its elements a perfect square. Prove there exist infinitely many amiable numbers which are themselves perfect squares.

(*Dan Schwarz*)

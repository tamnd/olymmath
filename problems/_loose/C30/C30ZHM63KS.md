Let $\alpha, \beta$ be the distinct roots of the equation $a \cos x + b \sin x - c = 0 \left(a^{2} + b^{2} \neq 0\right)$, and $\alpha \neq \beta + 2 k \pi (k \in Z)$, prove that: $\cos ^{2} \frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}=\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}+b^{2}}$.

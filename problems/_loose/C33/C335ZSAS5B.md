Pills for Forgetfulness (from 8th grade, 3 points). In January, the doctor gave the Forgetful Scientist a pack of 10 pills for forgetfulness. The Scientist keeps the pills in a cabinet. When the Scientist is overwhelmed by a bout of forgetfulness (which happens several times a week at random moments), he opens the cabinet, takes the pack from the shelf, takes a pill, and checks how many pills for forgetfulness are left. If the Scientist sees that only one pill is left, he immediately orders a new identical pack from the pharmacy with instant delivery and places it in the same cabinet on the same shelf.

If the Scientist sees that the current pack is empty, he immediately throws it in the trash.

What is the probability that at 10 AM on December 31, the Forgetful Scientist will have exactly two packs of pills for forgetfulness in the cabinet?

Let $f(x)=(x-x_{1})(x-$ $\left.x_{2}\right) \cdots\left(x-x_{n}\right)$, where $-1 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant 1, i=1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove: It is impossible for there to exist numbers $a$ and $b$ simultaneously satisfying
(1) $|f(a)| \geqslant 1$;
(2) $|f(b)| \geqslant 1$;
(3) $-1<a<0<b<1$.

Let $\triangle A B C$ be a triangle and $D, E$ and $F$ be any three points on the sides $A B, B C$ and $C A$ respectively. Let $P$ be the midpoint of $A E, Q$ be the midpoint of $B F$ and $R$ be the midpoint of $C D$. Prove that the area of the triangle $\triangle P Q R$ is one-fourth the area of the triangle $\triangle D E F$.

(2008 China Girls Mathematical Olympiad) For a positive integer $n$, let $f_{n}=\left[2^{n} \sqrt{2008}\right]+ \left[2^{n} \sqrt{2009}\right]$. Prove that the sequence $f_{1}, f_{2}, \cdots$ has a finite number of odd numbers and an infinite number of even numbers. ($[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$).

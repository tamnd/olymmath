Filipa plays a game. She starts with a row of 15 squares and a coin on the centre square. Filipa then rolls a die. If she rolls an even number, she moves the coin that many squares to the right; if she rolls an odd number, she moves the coin that many squares to the left. If the results of six rolls were $1,2,3,4,5,6$, where would her coin be located?

(A) On the square where it started

(B) 1 square to the right of where it started

(C) 2 squares to the right of where it started

(D) 2 squares to the left of where it started

(E) 3 squares to the right of where it started

Equality or inequality? Two players play the following game. On the board, six equalities are written:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
*=* \\
*=*+* \\
*=*+*+* \\
*=*+*+*+* \\
*=*+*+*+*+* \\
*=*+*+*+*+*+*
\end{array}\right.
$$

Taking turns, they write numbers in place of the asterisks. The first player tries to make all the equalities true, while the second player tries to prevent this. Who will win with correct play?

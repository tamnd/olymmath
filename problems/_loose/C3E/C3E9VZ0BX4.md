For the tetrahedron $S A B C$, we know that there are five spheres, each of which touches all the edges of the tetrahedron, or their respective extensions. Prove that

a) the tetrahedron $S A B C$ is regular;

b) conversely: for any regular tetrahedron, there exist five spheres that have the mentioned properties.

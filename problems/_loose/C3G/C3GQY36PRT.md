- the digits of $N$ include at least one of each of the digits $3,4,5$, and 6 ,
- the digits of $N$ include no digits other than $3,4,5$, and 6 , and
- the sum of the digits of $N$ is 900 and the sum of the digits of $2 N$ is 900 .

When the largest and smallest possible values of $N$ are multiplied together, the number of digits in the resulting product is
(A) 408
(B) 400
(C) 432
(D) 416
(E) 424

In the kite $A B C D$, $A B=A D, B C=C D$. Through a point $P$ on $B D$, draw a line intersecting $A D, B C$ at $E, F$ respectively, and draw another line through point $P$ intersecting $A B, C D$ at $G, H$ respectively. Let $G F$ and $E H$ intersect $B D$ at $I, J$ respectively, prove that: $\frac{P I}{P B}=\frac{P J}{P D}$.

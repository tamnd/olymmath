a) Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ and $\left(b_{n}\right)$ be the sequences defined by $a_{n}=3 n-2, b_{n}=a_{n}^{2}(n=1,2,3, \ldots)$. Prove that the sequence of differences $b_{n+1}-b_{n}(n=1,2,3, \ldots)$ is an arithmetic sequence!

b) A generalization of the statement to be proven in a) is:

If $\left(a_{n}\right)$ is any arithmetic sequence and $\left(b_{n}\right)$ is the sequence defined by $b_{n}=a_{n}^{2}(n=1,2,3, \ldots)$, then the sequence of differences $b_{n+1}-b_{n}(n=1,2,3, \ldots)$ is also an arithmetic sequence.

Prove this generalization as well!

Let $\mathbf{a}_{1}, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$ be the vectors of the sides of an $n$-gon, $\varphi_{i j}=\angle\left(\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{i}}, \mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{j}}\right)$. Prove that $a_{1}^{2}=a_{2}^{2}+\ldots+a_{\mathrm{n}}{ }^{2}+2 \sum_{i>j>1} a_{\mathrm{i}} a_{\mathrm{j}}$ cos $\varphi_{i j}$, where $a_{\mathrm{i}}=\left|\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{i}}\right|$.

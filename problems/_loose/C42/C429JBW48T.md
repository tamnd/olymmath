The quadrilateral $A B C D$ is inscribed in a circle with center $O$. The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect each other in the point $M, M \neq O$. The line through $M$ which is perpendicular to $O M$ intersects the sides $A B$ and $C D$ of the quadrilateral $A B C D$ in the points $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Prove that $A B=C D$ iff $B X=C Y$.

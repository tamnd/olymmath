Consider the operation to be the computational procedure that gives us the number $c=a+b+a b$ from the numbers $a, b$, and let's call this the star-operation, denoted as: $a * b=c$.
- Solve the equation $a * x=c$.
- Show the following:
1. There is at most one $x$ such that $a * x=0$. If it exists, denote it by $a^{\prime}$ (read: $a$ comma), so $a * a^{\prime}=0$.
2. $2 \cdot\left(a^{\prime}\right)^{\prime}=a$ (i.e., the comma of a number's comma is generally equal to the number itself).
3. $(a * b)^{\prime}=a^{\prime} * b^{\prime}$.
4. $\left(a^{\prime} * b\right)^{\prime}=a^{\prime} * b^{\prime}$.
5. $\left(a^{\prime} * b^{\prime}\right)=a * b$.
- Plot the function $y=x^{\prime}$.

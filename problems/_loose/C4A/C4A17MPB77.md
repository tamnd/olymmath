Given are two segments; for their lengths $p$ and $q$, it holds that $p<q$. We are looking for a quadrilateral $A B C D$ that satisfies the following conditions (1), (2), (3).

(1) The quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a trapezoid with $A B \| C D$.

(2) It holds that $\overline{A B}=p$ and $\overline{C D}=q$.

(3) There exists a circle on which the points $A, B, C$ and $D$ lie, and whose radius is $p$.

I. Show that a quadrilateral, if it satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3), can be constructed from $p$ and $q$!

II. Describe such a construction!

III. Show that a quadrilateral, if it is constructed according to this description, satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3)!

IV. Investigate under which conditions for the given solutions $p$ and $q$ such a quadrilateral

a) exists,

b) is uniquely determined by $p$ and $q$ up to congruence!

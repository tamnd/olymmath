(1986 National High School League Test Question) Let real numbers $a, b, c$ satisfy
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a^{2}-b c-8 a+7=0, \\
b^{2}+c^{2}+b c-6 a+6=0 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Then the range of values for $a$ is ( ).
A. $(-\infty,+\infty)$
B. $(-\infty, 1] \cup[9,+\infty)$
C. $(0,7)$
D. $[1,9] $

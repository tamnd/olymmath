Given positive real numbers $a, b, c$ satisfying $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=3$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{4-a^{2}}+\frac{1}{4-b^{2}}+\frac{1}{4-c^{2}} \leqslant \frac{9}{(a+b+c)^{2}} .
$$

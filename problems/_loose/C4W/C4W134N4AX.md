Given $2 n$ points in the plane, no three of which are collinear, $n$ points represent farms $F=\left\{F_{1}, F_{2}, \cdots, F_{n}\right\}$, and the other $n$ points represent reservoirs $W=\left\{W_{1}, W_{2}, \cdots, W_{n}\right\}$. A farm is connected to a reservoir by a straight road. Prove that there exists an assignment such that all roads do not intersect.

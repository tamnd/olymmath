Let integer $n \geqslant 3$, real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfy $\min _{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n}\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|=1$. Find $\min _{x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \in \mathbf{R}} \sum_{k=1}^{n}\left|x_{k}\right|^{3}$.

In an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, $O$ is the center of the circumscribed circle, and $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ are the feet of the altitudes. On the lines $O A_{1}, O B_{1}, O C_{1}$, points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are found respectively, such that the quadrilaterals $A O B C^{\prime}, B O C A^{\prime}, C O A B^{\prime}$ are cyclic. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A A_{1} A^{\prime}, B B_{1} B^{\prime}, C C_{1} C^{\prime}$ have a common point.

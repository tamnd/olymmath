a) Eighth-graders are lined up in a row. In front of each of them stands a seventh-grader who is shorter than them. Prove that if the row of eighth-graders is arranged by height and the row of seventh-graders is also arranged by height, then each eighth-grader will still be taller than the seventh-grader standing in front of them.

b) A regiment of soldiers is arranged in a rectangle such that in each row, the soldiers are standing by height. Prove that if in each column the soldiers are also arranged by height, then in each row they will still be standing by height.

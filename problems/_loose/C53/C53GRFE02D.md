Let $S_{1}$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms of the arithmetic sequence $8,12, \cdots$, and $S_{2}$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms of the arithmetic sequence $17,19, \cdots$. If $n \neq 0$, then $S_{1}=S_{2}$
(A) is impossible for any $n$ value.
(B) is true for one $n$ value.
(C) is true for two $n$ values.
(D) is true for four $n$ values.
(E) is true for more than four $n$ values.
(17th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1966)

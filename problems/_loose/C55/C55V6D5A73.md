Two people, A and B, play the following game: A selects a number from the set $\{1,2,3,4\}$, and B asks A a series of questions, each of which is of the type: "Is the number you chose in a certain subset?" A can only answer "yes" or "no," and A can lie at most once. Prove that:
(1) B only needs to ask 5 questions, and after receiving A's answers, B can determine the number A chose;
(2) To always determine the number A chose, B asking 4 questions is insufficient.

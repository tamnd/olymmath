Let $S$ be a set of $n$ points in the plane $(n \geqslant 3)$, where no three points are collinear. Prove that there exists a set $M$ of $2 n-5$ points in the plane such that in any triangle formed by any 3 points of $S$, there is at least one point of $M$ inside the triangle.

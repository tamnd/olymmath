Show that a) if the number $a$ is a root of the equation

$$
f(x)=x^{20}-123 x^{10}+1=0
$$

then the numbers $-a, 1 / a, -1 / a$, which are different from $a$ and from each other, also satisfy the equation. b) if the number $\beta$ is a root of the equation

$$
g(x)=x^{4}+3 x^{3}+4 x^{2}+2 x+1=0
$$

then neither $-\beta$, nor $1 / \beta$, nor $-1 / \beta$ satisfies this equation.

In a column of squares on the right,
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|l|l|l|}
\hline$A$ & 9 & $H$ & $M$ & $O$ & $X$ & 7 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
each square, except for 9 and 7, represents a number. It is known that the sum of any 3 consecutive squares is 19. Then $A+H+M+O$ equals
(A) 21 .
(B) 23 .
(C) 25 .
(D) 26 .
(Anhui Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1997)

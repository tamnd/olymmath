Let $ABC$ be a triangle. We denote $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ its angles. We denote $E$ and $F$ the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$. A circle passing through $E$ and $F$ is tangent to $(BC)$ at $D$. Show that $\frac{DB}{DC}=\sqrt{\frac{\tan \gamma}{\tan \beta}}$.

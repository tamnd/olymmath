Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral such that $A D = B C$, $(A B)$ and $(C D)$ are parallel, and $A B > C D$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of $[A C]$ and $F$ the point of intersection of the diagonals $(A C)$ and $(B D)$. The line parallel to $(B D)$ passing through $E$ intersects the line $(C D)$ at point $G$.

1) Show that the triangle $C G A$ is a right triangle at $G$.
2) Let $C D = b$ and $A B = a$. Calculate the ratio $\frac{E G}{C F}$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.

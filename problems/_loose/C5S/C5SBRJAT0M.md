Circle $A$ is equal to and intersects with circle $B$. Construct two externally tangent circles $C$ and $D$ such that they are both internally tangent to circle $A$ and externally tangent to circle $B$. Let the internal common tangent of circles $C$ and $D$ be $l$. Clearly, there are infinitely many pairs of circles $C$ and $D$ that satisfy these conditions. Prove: infinitely many internal common tangents intersect at a single point.

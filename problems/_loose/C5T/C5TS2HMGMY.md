On the sides of a triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, equilateral triangles $\mathrm{ABM}, \mathrm{BCN}$, and $\mathrm{CAP}$ are constructed externally, with centers $\mathrm{C}_{1}, \mathrm{~A}_{1}$, and $\mathrm{B}_{1}$, respectively. We denote by $\mathrm{G}, \mathrm{G}^{\prime}, \mathrm{G}^{\prime \prime}$ the centroids of triangles $\mathrm{ABC}, \mathrm{A}_{1} \mathrm{~B}_{1} \mathrm{C}_{1}$, and MNP, respectively. Show that $\overline{\mathrm{G}^{\prime} \mathrm{G}^{\prime \prime}}=2 \overrightarrow{\mathrm{GG}^{\prime}}$

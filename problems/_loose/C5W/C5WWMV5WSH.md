Professor M. A. Luco wrote on the board the expression:

$$
\frac{n^{2}-5 n+4}{n-4}
$$

Then, he tells the students that $n$ can be any natural number, except for 4.

a) What is the value of the expression for $n=1$?

b) Marcos substituted $n$ with a natural number and found that the value of the expression is 5. Which number did Marcos substitute for $n$?

c) Which natural numbers cannot be the numerical value of the expression?

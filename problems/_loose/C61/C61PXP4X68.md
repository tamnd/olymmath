a) On a $5 \times 5$ board, 21 tokens with the white side up are placed such that each token lies on a separate $1 \times 1$ square (each token is two-sided, with one white and one black side). In each move, Martha takes one "white" token, flips it, and places it back on any free $1 \times 1$ square. Her goal is to flip all 21 tokens without ever having a "white" and a "black" token on adjacent (sharing a side) $1 \times 1$ squares at any moment. Show that regardless of the initial arrangement of the tokens, Martha cannot achieve her goal.

b) If instead of 21 tokens, 20 tokens are placed on the board with the white side up, does there exist an initial arrangement for which Martha can achieve the stated goal?

It is easy to verify the correctness of the following equalities:

$$
\begin{gathered}
\sqrt{2}=\frac{3}{2} \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{9}}=\frac{4}{3} \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{8}}=\frac{7}{5} \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{49}}=\frac{10}{7} \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{50}} \\
\sqrt{3}=\frac{7}{4} \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{49}}=\frac{12}{7} \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{48}} \\
\sqrt{5}=\frac{20}{9} \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{80}} \\
\sqrt{10}=\frac{10}{3} \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{10}}
\end{gathered}
$$

Provide similar transformations for the square roots of 5 and 10, where the expression under the square root is a difference or a sum; and for the square roots of 6, 7, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, and 26, provide one such transformation for each, where the expression under the square root is a sum or a difference.

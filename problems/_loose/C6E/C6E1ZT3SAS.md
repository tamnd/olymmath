A garden of rectangular shape is exactly $13 \mathrm{~m}$ longer than it is wide. To completely fence it, exactly $92 \mathrm{~m}$ of fencing is needed.

a) Calculate the area of the garden!

b) The garden is to be completely divided into beds and paths, with the following conditions to be met:

Each bed has the shape of a rectangle with side lengths of $3 \mathrm{~m}$ and $1 \mathrm{~m}$. Between any two adjacent beds and between the fence and the beds, there is a $25 \mathrm{~cm}$ wide path everywhere.

Investigate whether there is a division of the garden that meets these conditions! If this is the case, determine the number of beds for such a division!

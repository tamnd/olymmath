The line through $P$ parallel to $CA$ meets $BC$ in $M$ and $BA$ in $N$ . The line through $P$ parallel to $AB$ meets $CA$ in $S$ and $CB$ in $T$ . If $KL,MN,ST$ are of equal lengths, find this common length.

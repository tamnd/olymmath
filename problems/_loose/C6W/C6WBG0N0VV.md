In a plane, given $n \geqslant 4$ points, where no three points are collinear, if the distance between every two points is determined, then these $n$ points are called stable. Prove: If there are $\frac{1}{2} n(n-3)+4$ pairs of points among these $n \geqslant 4$ points whose distances are determined, then these $n$ points are stable.

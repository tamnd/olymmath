Let $ABC$ be a triangle, let $H$ be the orthocentre and $L,M,N$ the midpoints of the sides $AB, BC, CA$ respectively. Prove that
$$HL^{2} + HM^{2} + HN^{2} < AL^{2} + BM^{2} + CN^{2}$$
if and only if $ABC$ is acute-angled.

Three bicycle paths with a total length of $24 \mathrm{~km}$ form a right-angled triangle. From the vertex of the right angle, two cyclists start simultaneously at constant speeds. One rides along one path, and the other along the other path. While one cyclist covers $5.5 \mathrm{~km}$, the other covers $6.5 \mathrm{~km}$ in the same time. If the cyclists meet exactly at the midpoint of the hypotenuse, what are the lengths of the paths, i.e., the sides of the right-angled triangle?

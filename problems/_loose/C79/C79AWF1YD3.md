Let $b$ be a positive number, $n>2$, a natural number, and

$$
d=\frac{(**+1-b) \cdot**}{**+1}
$$

where $**$ denotes the integer part of $b$ (i.e., the largest integer not greater than $b$). Prove that

$$
\frac{d+n-2}{**+n-2}>\frac{**+n-1-b}{**+n-1}
$$

\section*{Problem $5-340945=341045$}

This sphere has a second regular tetrahedron $P Q R S$ inscribed in it (i.e., its vertices $P, Q, R, S$ all lie on the surface of the sphere $K$).

What is the ratio $V_{2}: V_{1}$ of the volume $V_{2}$ of such a tetrahedron $P Q R S$ to the volume $V_{1}$ of $A B C D$?

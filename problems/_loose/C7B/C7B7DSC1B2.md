Someone has seven cards, each with one of the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7.

Show that among all the seven-digit numbers that can be formed using exactly these seven cards (where, for example, transforming a 6 into a 9 by flipping the card is prohibited), there are no two numbers such that one is an integer multiple of the other!

Given two finite point sets $A$ and $B$ on a plane, both containing at least two points, and $A \cup B$ not lying on a single straight line. Prove: either there exist two points in $A$ such that the line segment connecting these two points does not contain any points from $B$ internally, or there exist two points in $B$ such that the line segment connecting these two points does not contain any points from $A$ internally.

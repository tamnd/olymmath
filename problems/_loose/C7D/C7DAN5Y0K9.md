5.93 $n$ primary school students sit around a circle. A teacher walks along the circle in a counterclockwise direction and gives out candies to the students. Starting from a certain student, the teacher skips 1 student and gives a candy to the next student; then skips 2 students and gives a candy to the next student; then skips 3 students and gives a candy to the next student; $\cdots$, each time skipping 1 more student than the previous time. For what condition on $n$ will every student eventually receive a candy? Explain your reasoning.

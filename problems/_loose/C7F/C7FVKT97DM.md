(2002 Romanian selection test for IMO and Balkan Mathematical Olympiad (3rd round)): Let $n \geqslant 4$ be an integer, and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers such that $a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}=1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}^{2}+1}+\frac{a_{2}}{a_{3}^{2}+1}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n}^{2}+1}+\frac{a_{n}}{a_{1}^{2}+1} \geqslant \frac{4}{5}\left(a_{1} \sqrt{a_{1}}+a_{2} \sqrt{a_{2}}+\cdots+a_{n} \sqrt{a_{n}}\right)^{2} .
$$

Heinz, Gerd, and Jochen met at a Thälmann Pioneers camp. The following is known about these three boys:

(1) At least two of them play table tennis, and at least two play football.

(2) One of them lives in Berlin, one in Leipzig, and one in Rostock. None of them live in two of these places at the same time.

(3) Only Heinz and the Berliner are table tennis players.

(4) Only Gerd and the Leipziger are football players.

(5) Jochen, who plays handball, is older than the Leipziger.

(6) None of the table tennis players also play handball.

(7) The handball player is not the oldest of the three boys.

State for each of the three boys where he lives and which of the three sports he practices! Who is the oldest and who is the youngest of the three boys?

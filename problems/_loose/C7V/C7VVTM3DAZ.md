Pinocchio must successfully overcome 9 obstacles to become a real flesh-and-blood child. It is a difficult task; if he fails at one obstacle, he must return to the previous one and try again. If he fails at the very first obstacle, he will remain a puppet forever. Pinocchio does not learn from his failures, so the probability of success on each obstacle is always $\frac{1}{10}, \frac{2}{10}$, $\frac{3}{10}, \ldots, \frac{9}{10}$, no matter how many times he tries. In what order should the Blue Fairy arrange the obstacles waiting for Pinocchio so that he has the highest chance of turning into a real child, and what is the probability of success in this case?

Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of an acute angled tirangle $ABC.$ The line tangent to $\omega$ at $A$ intersects the line $BC$ at the point $T.$ Let the midpoint of segment $AT$ be $N,$ and the centroid of $\triangle ABC$ be the point $G.$ The other tangent line drawn from $N$ to $\omega$ intersects $\omega$ at the point $L.$ The line $LG$ meets $\omega$ at $S\neq L.$
Prove that $AS\parallel BC.$

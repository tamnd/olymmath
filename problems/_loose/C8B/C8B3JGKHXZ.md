Let $S_n$ be number of ordered sets of natural numbers $(a_1;a_2;....;a_n)$ for which $\frac{1}{a_1}+\frac{1}{a_2}+....+\frac{1}{a_n}=1$ . Determine
1) $S_{10} mod(2)$ .
2) $S_7 mod(2)$ .

(1) is first problem in 10 grade, (2)- third in 9 grade.

Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be real numbers, but not all of them null. Show that the equation $$\sqrt{x+a_1}+\sqrt{x+a_2}+\ldots+\sqrt{x+a_n}=n\sqrt{x}$$ has at most one real solution.

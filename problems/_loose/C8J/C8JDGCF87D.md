Suppose a knight in chess can move $n$ squares horizontally and 1 square vertically (or 1 square horizontally and $n$ squares vertically) with each step. Now, there is a knight located on a square of an infinitely large chessboard. Try to determine for which natural numbers $n$, the knight can reach any specified square, and for which $n$ it cannot.

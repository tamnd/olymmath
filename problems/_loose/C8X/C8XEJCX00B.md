For a quadrilateral $ABCD$, the following assumptions are made:

(1) $ABCD$ is a trapezoid with $AB \| CD$.

(2) It holds that $AB > CD$.

(3) The sum of the measures of the interior angles $\angle BAD$ and $\angle CBA$ is $90^{\circ}$.

The midpoint of $AB$ is $M$, and the midpoint of $CD$ is $N$.

Prove that under these assumptions, $MN = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (AB - CD)$ always holds!

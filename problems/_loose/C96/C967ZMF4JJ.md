Given is the quadratic equation

$$
a^{4} x^{2}+5 a^{2} b x+4 b^{2}=0, a, b \neq 0
$$

a) Prove that this equation has real and distinct roots for any $a$ and $b$.

b) If the roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ of the equation satisfy the condition

$$
4\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right)=5\left(x_{1} x_{2}+1\right)
$$

then they do not depend on $a$ and $b$. Prove!

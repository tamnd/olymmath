Five. (15 points) In $\triangle ABC$, it is known that
$$
\angle BAC: \angle ABC: \angle ACB = 1: 2: 4,
$$

The three angle bisectors intersect the opposite sides at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, and the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ is $I$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text { (1) } BB_{1}=AB_{1}, BC_{1}=C C_{1}, IB_{1}=IC, \\
CI=CA_{1}, BI=BC_{1} ;
\end{array}
$$
(2) $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is an isosceles triangle.

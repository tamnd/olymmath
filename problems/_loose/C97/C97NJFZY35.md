(2003 New Curriculum College Entrance Examination Question) Two representative teams, A and B, are having a table tennis match. Each team has three players. Team A's players are $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$, and Team B's players are $B_{1}, B_{2}, B_{3}$. According to the statistics from many previous matches, the probability of winning and losing between the opposing players is as follows:
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline Opposing Players & Probability of A Team Player Winning & Probability of A Team Player Losing \\
\hline$A_{1}$ vs $B_{1}$ & $\frac{2}{3}$ & $\frac{1}{3}$ \\
\hline$A_{2}$ vs $B_{2}$ & $\frac{2}{5}$ & $\frac{3}{5}$ \\
\hline$A_{3}$ vs $B_{3}$ & $\frac{2}{5}$ & $\frac{3}{5}$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

Now, the players will compete according to the matchups in the table. Each winning team gets 1 point, and each losing team gets 0 points. Let $\xi$ and $\eta$ be the total points of Team A and Team B, respectively.
(1) Find the probability distribution of $\xi$ and $\eta$;
(2) Find $E(\xi), E(\eta)$.

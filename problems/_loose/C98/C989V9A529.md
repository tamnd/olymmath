Given sets $A, B, C \subseteq \mathbf{R}$, there exist infinitely many integers $k$ satisfying:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A \subseteq\{x+k y \mid x \in B, y \in C\}, \\
B \subseteq\{x+k y \mid x \in C, y \in A\}, \\
C \subseteq\{x+k y \mid x \in A, y \in B\} .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: If $A \cup B \cup C$ is a finite set, then $A=B=C$

Consider $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{2010}$, 2010 distinct integers in $\{1, \ldots, 2010\}$, and $d_{1}, \ldots, d_{2010}$ integers such that: $d_{i}=\left|a_{i}-i\right|$ ( $d_{i}$ is the distance from $a_{i}$ to $i$ ). Is it possible for the $d_{i}$ to be pairwise distinct?

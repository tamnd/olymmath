Let $n\geq k$ be positive integers. There are $n\times n$ squares, and paint each square in white or black. There are $k$ ants, and each ant repeatedly moves to an adjacent square. In the white squares, ants can only go straight ahead, while in the black squares they can choose their direction freely. By painting some $m$ squares black and others white, and deciding the initial placement and direction of movement of the ants properly, it is possible to make every square visited by at least one ant. Find the minimum possible value of such $m$.

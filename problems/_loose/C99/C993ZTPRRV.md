Given a convex quadrilateral $A B S C$. A point $P$ is chosen on the diagonal $B C$ such that $A P=C P>B$. Point $Q$ is symmetric to point $P$ with respect to the midpoint of diagonal $B C$, and point $R$ is symmetric to point $Q$ with respect to the line $A C$. It turns out that $\angle S A B=\angle Q A C$ and $\angle S B C=\angle B A C$. Prove that $S A=S R$. (S. Berlov)

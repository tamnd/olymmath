$\mathrm{Az}$

$$
n-1, n-2, \ldots 2,1
$$

is the product of numbers from which we omit the numbers divisible by 3; then we take the first two numbers with a + sign, the next two with a - sign, the following two again with a + sign, and so on. Show that the algebraic sum of the numbers obtained this way is equal to $n$, if $n$ is a number divisible by 3.

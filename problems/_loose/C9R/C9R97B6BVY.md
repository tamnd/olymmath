(16th "Hope Cup" Senior High School Competition Problem) In the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, $a_{1}=1, a_{n}-a_{n-1}=a_{n+1}+3 a_{n} a_{n+1}$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Given that $y=f(x)$ is an even function, and for any $x$ there is $f(1+x)=f(1-x)$, when $x \in[-2,-1)$, $f(x)=\log _{2}|x+1|$. Find the range of $x$ that makes $f(x)+a_{n}<0\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$ always true.

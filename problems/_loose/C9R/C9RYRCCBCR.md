Let $\triangle ABC$ be an isosceles triangle ( $AC = BC$ ). Let point $E$ be on side $AC$ such that $BE = CE$ . The point $D$ lies on the angle bisector $CF$ of the angle $\angle ACB$ such that $\angle ADE = 2\angle EDC$ (the point D is located in the segment $BE$ ). Find the measures of angles of the triangle $\triangle ABC$ .

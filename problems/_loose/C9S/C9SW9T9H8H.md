Let $M_{n}$ and $N_{n}$ be points on sides $\overline{C A}$ and $\overline{C B}$ of triangle $A B C$, respectively, such that

$$
\left|C M_{n}\right|=\frac{1}{n}|C A|, \left|C N_{n}\right|=\frac{1}{n+1}|C B|, \forall n \in \mathbb{N}
$$

Find the locus of points $M_{i} N_{i} \cap M_{j} N_{j}$, where $i$ and $j$ are different natural number.

Note. $M_{i} N_{i} \cap M_{j} N_{j}$ denotes the intersection of lines $M_{i} N_{i}$ and $M_{j} N_{j}$.

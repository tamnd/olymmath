For each $n\in\mathbb N$ , the function $f_n$ is defined on real numbers $x\ge n$ by $$ f_n(x)=\sqrt{x-n}+\sqrt{x-n+1}+\ldots+\sqrt{x+n}-(2n+1)\sqrt x. $$ (a) If $n$ is fixed, prove that $\lim_{x\to+\infty}f_n(x)=0$ .
(b) Find the limit of $f_n(n)$ as $n\to+\infty$ .

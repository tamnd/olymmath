The teacher considers some students in class $6^{a}$ to be excellent students, while the rest are poor students. During the quarter, there were 6 math tests in the class (grades from 2 to 5 were given). All students were present at each test, and they sat in pairs at desks (possibly different pairs for different tests). A poor student miraculously received a three if they sat with an excellent student, and a two if they sat with another poor student. In total, the number of fives received during these tests was three times the number of fours, and the number of threes was ten less than the number of twos. Prove that there will be an excellent student who received at least one grade not higher than a three.

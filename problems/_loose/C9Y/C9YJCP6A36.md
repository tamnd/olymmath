Construct a triangle $ABC$ of the smallest perimeter, if:

a) the vertices $A$ and $B$ are given, and the third vertex $C$ must lie on a given line $l$;

b)* the given vertex $A$ is inside a given acute angle, and the vertices $B$ and $C$ must lie on the two sides of this angle;

c)* the vertices $A, B$, and $C$ of the desired triangle must lie respectively on the sides $[NP], [PM]$, and $[MN]$ of a given acute triangle $MNP$.

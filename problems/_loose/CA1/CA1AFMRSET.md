A regular $n$-sided polygon is inscribed in a circle. By connecting the midpoint of each arc to the two nearest vertices of the $n$-sided polygon with segments, we obtain a regular $2n$-sided polygon. a) Prove that, by sequentially connecting the midpoints of the sides of this $2n$-sided polygon, we obtain a new $2n$-sided polygon, the perimeter of which is equal to the perimeter of the original $n$-sided polygon. b) Denoting the apothem and the radius of the circumscribed circle of the given $n$-sided polygon by $h_{1}$ and $r_{1}$, respectively, derive formulas for the apothem $h_{2}$ and the radius $r_{2}$ of the circumscribed circle of the obtained (second) $2n$-sided polygon.

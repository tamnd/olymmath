In parallelogram $A B C D$ we have $B D \leq A C$. Points $M, N, P, Q \notin(A B C)$ are considered on the same side of the plane $(A B C)$ such that $M A, N B, P C, Q D$ are perpendicular to the plane $(A B C)$, $M A \leq N B \leq Q D \leq P C, [A C] \equiv[C P]$ and $[B N] \equiv[B D]$. It is known that $M P \cap N Q \neq \varnothing$.

a) Prove the equality $M A+P C=N B+Q D$;

b) Prove that $A_{M A C} \leq A_{Q D B}$.

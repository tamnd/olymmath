1 If every element $a$ of a group $G$ can be expressed as a power of some element $x$, i.e., $a=x^{m}$, then this group is called a cyclic group. If the elements of the group satisfy the commutative law, i.e., for $a, b \in G$,
$$
a \cdot b=b \cdot a
$$

then this group is called an Abelian (Abel) group. Prove that all cyclic groups are Abelian groups.

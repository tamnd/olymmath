The perpendicular bisectors of sides $AB$ and $BC$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ intersect sides $CD$ and $DA$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. It turns out that $\cdot A P B=\cdot B Q C$. Inside the quadrilateral, a point $X$ is chosen such that $Q X \| A B$ and $P X \| B C$. Prove that the line $B X$ bisects the diagonal $AC$. (S. Berlov)

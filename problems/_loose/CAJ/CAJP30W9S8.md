Every night, Juku listens to exactly $14$ songs from a playlist containing exactly $100$ songs. Every time a song ends, the next song is chosen from among all $100$ songs with equal probability (the same song may also repeat). Prove that, on more than half of all nights, Juku listens some song more than once.

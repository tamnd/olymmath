In a VEB, a certain type of workpiece was first processed in department A1 and then in department A2. Initially, the same number of workpieces could be processed daily in one department as in the other.

With the help of rationalization measures in both departments, the 53 workers in department A1 increased their production to 159%, and the 62 workers in department A2 increased their production to 124%. However, because the production output in both departments had to be the same, a sufficient number of workers from one department decided to work in the other department.

How many workers from which of the two departments took up work in the other department, so that the production output in both departments was again the same?

By what percentage of the production volume before the rationalization measures did the total production output increase?

Notes: It is assumed that the production output of both departments is proportional to the number of workers.

53 Let $m$ and $n$ both be odd numbers. A rectangle with vertices at $(0,0),(0, m),(n, m),(n, 0)$ in the coordinate plane is divided into several triangles. If a side of the resulting triangle lies on a line of the form $x=j$ or $y=k\left(j, k \in N_{0}\right)$, then this side is called a "good side"; a side that is not a good side is called a "bad side". Suppose this division satisfies the following conditions:
(1) Each triangle has at least one good side and the height on this side equals 1;
(2) If a side of a triangle is a bad side, then this side must be a common side of two triangles.
Prove that there are at least two triangles produced by this division, each having two good sides.

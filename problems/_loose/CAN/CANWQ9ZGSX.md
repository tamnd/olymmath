If the average ages of students in classes A, B, and C are $14, 13, 12$ years old, respectively, and the overall average age of students in the three classes is 13 years. Then the number of students in classes A, B, and C, $a, b, c$, satisfies
(A) $a=c$.
(B) $a+c=2 b$.
(C) $a \cdot c=b^{2}$.
(D) $a>b>c$.
(3rd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

149 For real numbers $a, b, c$, and $a \neq 0$, consider the system of equations for $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a x_{1}^{2}+b x_{1}+C=x_{2}, \\
a x_{2}^{2}+b x_{2}+C=x_{3}, \\
\cdots \cdots \cdots \cdots \cdots \cdots \\
a x_{n-1}^{2}+b x_{n-1}+C=x_{n}, \\
a x_{n}^{2}+b x_{n}+C=x_{1} .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Additionally, $\Delta=(b-1)^{2}-4 a c$. Prove that the system of equations has, in the real number domain,
(1) more than one solution when $\Delta > 0$.

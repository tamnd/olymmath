In a trapezoid $A B C D$, where $A C$ and $B D$ are the non-parallel sides, the midline $E F$ is two-thirds the length of the longer parallel side, $A B$. On the extension of the midline, measure from $F$ half the length of the midline, and connect the resulting point to $D$. The line connecting $D$ to this point intersects the longer parallel side at point $G$. Prove that the line $C F$ bisects the segment $B G$.

Prove that the only solution to the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
a_{1} & + & a_{2} & + & a_{3} & + & a_{4} & = & 0 \\
a_{2} & + & a_{3} & + & a_{4} & + & a_{5} & = & 0 \\
\ldots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots & \cdots \\
a_{1987} & + & a_{1988} & + & a_{1989} & + & a_{1} & = & 0 \\
a_{1988} & + & a_{1989} & + & a_{1} & + & a_{2} & = & 0 \\
a_{1989} & + & a_{1} & + & a_{2} & + & a_{3} & = & 0
\end{array}\right.
$$

is $a_{1}=a_{2}=\ldots=a_{1989}=0$.

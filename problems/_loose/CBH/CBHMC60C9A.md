Let the ellipse $b^{2} x^{2}+a^{2} y^{2}=a^{2} b^{2}(a>b>0)$ have two directrices intersecting the $x$-axis at $E_{1}, E_{2}$, and let point $P$ be on the ellipse, $\angle E_{1} P E_{2}=\alpha, e$ be the eccentricity, and $c$ be the semi-focal distance. Then $\alpha$ is an obtuse angle, and when $e^{2}>\frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{5}-1)$, we have $\cot \alpha \leqslant-e$, with equality if and only if $\left|y_{P}\right|=\frac{a b^{2}}{c^{2}}$.

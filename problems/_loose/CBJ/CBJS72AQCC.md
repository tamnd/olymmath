Let point $C$ be the vertex of the right angle in the right triangle $A B C$. On side $\overline{A C}$, a point $D$ is chosen such that $\angle A B D=\angle B A D=\alpha$. The length of side $\overline{A B}$ is $8 \mathrm{~cm}$, and the length of segment $\overline{A D}$ is $5 \mathrm{~cm}$. Determine the lengths of segments $\overline{B C}$ and $\overline{C D}$ and prove that for the angle $\alpha$ it holds that

$$
\sin (2 \alpha)=2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha
$$

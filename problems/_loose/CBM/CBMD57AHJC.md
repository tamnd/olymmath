On the cells of a chessboard, there are diamonds such that in every pair of adjacent (by side) cells, the weights of the diamonds differ by less than 1 carat. Prove that these diamonds can be laid out, one per cell, in a $2 \times 32$ rectangle so that in every pair of adjacent cells, the weights of the diamonds still differ by less than 1 carat.

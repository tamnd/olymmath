(1991 Jiangsu Province Competition Question) Let $A A_{1} B_{1} B$ be the axial section of a cylinder, and $C$ be a point on the circumference of the base circle. Suppose the dihedral angle $A-A_{1} B-C=\alpha$, $\angle C A B=\beta$, and $\angle C A_{1} B=\gamma$. Prove:
$$
\alpha=\arcsin \left(\frac{\cos \beta}{\cos \gamma}\right) .
$$

On a plane, there is a segment $\mathrm{AB}$ of length 1 and an arbitrary point M on it. On the segments AM and MB as sides, squares AMCD and MBEF are constructed, lying on the same side of AB. Let $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{Q}$ be the points of intersection of the diagonals of these squares, respectively. Find the geometric locus of the midpoints of segments $P Q$, as point $M$ runs through the entire segment $A B$.

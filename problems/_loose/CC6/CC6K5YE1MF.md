There are $100 N$ sandwiches lined up, each with sausage and cheese. Uncle Fyodor and Cat Matroskin are playing a game. Uncle Fyodor, in one move, eats one sandwich from one of the ends. Cat Matroskin, in one move, can remove the sausage from one sandwich (or do nothing). Uncle Fyodor performs $100$ actions in each turn, while Cat Matroskin performs only $1$ action; Uncle Fyodor goes first, followed by Cat Matroskin, and the turns alternate until Uncle Fyodor eats all the sandwiches. Uncle Fyodor wins if the last sandwich he eats has sausage. Is it true that for any natural number $N$ he can always win regardless of Cat Matroskin's moves?

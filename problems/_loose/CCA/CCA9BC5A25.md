In the plane, a rectangle $A B C D$ is given, and over its sides $A B$ and $B C$ (as diameters), semicircles $k$ and $l$ are constructed outside the rectangle. Find the segment $X Y$ of the greatest length $d$ such that $X \in k$ and $Y \in l$. Express the length $d$ in terms of the lengths $a=|A B|$ and $b=|B C|$.

Three planes cut a parallelepiped into 8 hexahedra, all faces of which are quadrilaterals (each plane intersects its two pairs of opposite faces of the parallelepiped and does not intersect the two remaining faces). It is known that a sphere can be circumscribed around one of these hexahedra. Prove that a sphere can be circumscribed around each of them.

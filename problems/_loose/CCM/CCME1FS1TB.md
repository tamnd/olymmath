The center of the hyperbola $y=1 / x$ is $O$, and the points $K, L$ on the hyperbola are symmetric with respect to $O$. Another point on the hyperbola is $M$. It needs to be proven that the projection of any point on the hyperbola onto the lines $M K, M L$ is at an equal distance from the center $O$.

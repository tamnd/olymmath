Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{gathered}
x+y=a(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}) \\
x^{2}+y^{2}=a(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

where $a$ is a real number. For which values of $a$ will the roots be real?

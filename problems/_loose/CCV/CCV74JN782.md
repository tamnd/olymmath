Every minute, they all simultaneously change color according to the following rule: Each traffic light changes color depending on the color of its two neighbors (to the right and left), and 1) if the two neighboring traffic lights were the same color, the traffic light between them turns on the same color. 2) if the two neighboring traffic lights were different colors, the traffic light between them turns on the third color.

In one variant of chess, known as Fischer chess, the initial positions of the pieces are determined by a random draw, adhering to the following rules:

- the light pieces must be on the a1, b1, ..., h1 squares;

- the king must be between the two rooks;

- the two bishops must be on squares of opposite colors;

- the dark pieces must be on the a8, b8, ..., h8 squares, in the same order as the light pieces. How many possible starting positions are there?

Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle with center $O$. Denote by $\Delta_{1}$ and $\Delta_{2}$ the images of the line $(A B)$ under the symmetries whose axes are respectively the internal bisectors of $\widehat{C A D}$ and $\widehat{C B D}$. Let $P$ be the intersection of $\Delta_{1}$ and $\Delta_{2}$.

Prove that $(O P)$ and $(C D)$ are perpendicular.

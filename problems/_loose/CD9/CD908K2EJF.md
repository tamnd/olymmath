a) For a given circle $K$, consider the square $Q$ that has the same perimeter as $K$.

Is the area of $Q$ larger, equal, or smaller than the area of $K$? What percentage of the area of $K$ is the area of $Q$?
b) For a given circle $k$, consider the square $q$ that has the same area as $k$.

Is the perimeter of $q$ larger, equal, or smaller than the perimeter of $k$? What percentage of the perimeter of $k$ is the perimeter of $q$?

The approximate value of $\pi$ accurate to four decimal places, $\pi \approx 3.1416$, can be used. The required percentages should be given to one decimal place after the decimal point.

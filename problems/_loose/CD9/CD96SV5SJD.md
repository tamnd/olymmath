A merganser mates every 7th day, a scaup mates every 11th day, and a gadwall mates every 13th day. A merganser, scaup, and gadwall all mate on Day 0. On Days N, N+1, and N+2 the merganser, scaup, and gadwall mate in some order with no two birds mating on the same day. Determine the smallest possible value of N.

After a while, she got back to work and wanted to continue counting the steps. However, when she tried to recall the number of steps already cleaned, she made a mistake. She "read" the correct three-digit number backwards, which resulted in a lower number, and continued counting from this number. After cleaning all the steps, she reached the number 746. How many steps could she have actually cleaned?

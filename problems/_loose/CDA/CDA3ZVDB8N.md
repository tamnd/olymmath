Proof: For every natural number $n(n \neq 0)$ and every real number $x \neq \frac{n \pi}{2 k}(k=0,1,2, \cdots, n ; N$ is any natural number), we have
$$
\frac{1}{\sin 2 x}+\frac{1}{\sin 4 x}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sin 2^{n} x}=\cot x-\cot 2^{n} x .
$$

A wholesale trade sells tennis rackets at a price of 363 kuna for one racket if the number of ordered rackets is 40 or less. If the number of ordered rackets is greater than 40, the price of one racket decreases by 0.60 kuna for each ordered racket above 40. In one order, a maximum of 400 rackets can be ordered. Determine how many rackets need to be ordered for the trade to achieve the maximum profit. How much is the maximum profit and at what price would the customer then pay for one racket?

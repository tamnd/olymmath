For a real number $x$, we denote $\mathrm{z}[x]$ as the greatest integer not greater than $x$.

(a) Prove that for all natural numbers $a, b$, and $c$,

$$
\left[\frac{\left[\frac{c}{a}\right]}{b}\right]=\left[\frac{c}{a b}\right]
$$

(b) Provide an example to show that the above equality does not hold for all positive real numbers $a, b$, and $c$.

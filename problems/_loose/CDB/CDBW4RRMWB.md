Let $k, l, c$ be positive integers. Prove: there exist positive integers $a, b$ such that $b-a=c \cdot(a, b)$, and $\frac{\tau(a)}{\tau\left(\frac{a}{(a, b)}\right)} \cdot l=\frac{\tau(b)}{\tau\left(\frac{b}{(a, b)}\right)} \cdot k$, where $(a, b)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $a, b$, and $\tau(m)$ denotes the number of distinct positive divisors of the positive integer $m$.

A boy needs to pass through three doors to reach the desired goal. At each door, there stands a guard. The boy carries a certain number of apples with him. To the first guard, he must give half of that number and another half apple. To the second guard, he must give half of the remainder and another half apple, and to the third guard, he must give half of the remainder and another half apple. Find the smallest number of apples the boy should carry with him to reach the desired goal without cutting any apple.

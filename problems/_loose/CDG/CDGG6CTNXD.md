Let $P$ be any point in the first quadrant on the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{5}+y^{2}=1$, and $F_{1}, F_{2}$ be the left and right foci, respectively. The lines $P F_{1}, P F_{2}$ intersect $C$ at $M, N$. If $\overrightarrow{P F_{1}}=\lambda_{1} \overrightarrow{F_{1} M}, \overrightarrow{P F_{2}}=\lambda_{2} \overrightarrow{F_{2} N}$, find the coordinates of point $P$ such that the slope of segment $M N$ is $-\frac{1}{9}$.

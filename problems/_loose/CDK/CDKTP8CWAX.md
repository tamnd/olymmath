Suppose there are $m$ cups, all facing upwards at the beginning. The cups can be flipped arbitrarily, and flipping $n$ cups at a time counts as one flip. Cups that have been flipped can be flipped again. Prove: when $m(\geqslant 3)$ is odd and $n(\geqslant 2$ and $n<m)$ is even, no matter how many times you flip, it is impossible to have all the cups facing downwards.

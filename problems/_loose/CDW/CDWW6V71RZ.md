Let $(\Omega, \mathscr{F})$ be a measurable space, where the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{F}$ is countably generated by some countable partition $\mathscr{D}=\left\{D_{1}, D_{2}, \ldots\right\}$. Show that the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{F}$ coincides with the $\sigma$-algebra

$$
\sigma(X)=\left\{X^{-1}(B): B \in \mathscr{B}(\mathbb{R})\right\}
$$

generated by the random variable

$$
X=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 10^{-n}\left(2 I_{D_{n}}+3\right)
$$

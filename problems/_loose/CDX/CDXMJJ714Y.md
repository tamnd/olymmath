The store has three floors, and movement between them is only possible by elevator. A study of floor visitation showed that from the start of the working day until the store closes:
1) of the customers entering the elevator on the second floor, half go to the first floor, and half - to the third;
2) among the customers exiting the elevator, less than a third do so on the third floor.

Which floor did customers travel to more often from the first floor - the second or the third?

Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}$ be the $n(n \geqslant 1)$ complex roots of the equation $z^{n}+a_{1} z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1} z+a_{n}=0$, where $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ are complex numbers. Let $A=\max _{1 \leqslant k \leqslant n}\left|a_{k}\right|$, prove that: $\left|z_{j}\right| \leqslant 1+A, j=1,2, \cdots, n$.

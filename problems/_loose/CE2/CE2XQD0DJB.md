Let the length, width, and height of a rectangular prism be $a$, $b$, and $c$, respectively, and its body diagonal be $l$. Prove:
$$
\left(l^{4}-a^{4}\right)\left(l^{4}-b^{4}\right)\left(l^{4}-c^{4}\right) \geqslant 512 a^{4} b^{4} c^{4} .
$$

Let $ p>5 $ be a prime number and $ S $ be a subset of $ \{1, 2, \ldots, p-1\} $ with at least two elements, such that for every $ a \in S $, there is at most one $ b \in S $ (not necessarily distinct from $ a $) for which $ a + b $ is not congruent modulo $ p $ to any element of $ S $. Find the minimum possible number of elements in $ S $.

Let $a_{i}$ with $i=1 ; 2 ; 3 ; \ldots ; n$ be real numbers $(n \geq 2)$ such that $0<a_{i} \leq a_{i+1}$. Show that then

$$
\frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} a_{i} \leq \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \leq \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=2}^{n}
$$

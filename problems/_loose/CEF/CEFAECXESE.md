Given $\triangle A B C$, a circle passing through vertices $A, B$ intersects sides $A C, C B$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Take points $R$ and $S$ on side $A B$ such that $Q R / / C A, P S / / C B$. Prove: $P, Q, R, S$ are concyclic.

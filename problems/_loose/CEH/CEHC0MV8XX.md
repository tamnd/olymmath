To which his wife replied: "In the year 1974, the case will occur that you are 5 times as old as our son, if I also only consider the full years of age."

Investigate whether there is exactly one date for which - as the son's date of birth - all these statements are true! If this is the case, provide the exact date of birth of the son (day, month, year)!

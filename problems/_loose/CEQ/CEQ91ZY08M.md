8-159 Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy
$$
1=a_{0} \leqslant a_{1} \leqslant a_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant a_{n} \leqslant \cdots
$$

Let $b_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{a_{k-1}}{a_{k}}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{k}}}, n=1,2,3, \cdots$
Prove: (1) $0 \leqslant b_{n}c$.

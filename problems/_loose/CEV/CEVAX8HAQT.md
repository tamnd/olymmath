There are two inlet pipes, A and C, and two outlet pipes, B and D, in a reservoir. To fill the reservoir, it takes 3 hours for pipe A alone and 5 hours for pipe C alone. To drain the reservoir, it takes 4 hours for pipe B alone and 6 hours for pipe D alone. Currently, the reservoir is $\frac{1}{6}$ full. If the pipes are opened in the order of A, B, C, D, and each pipe is opened for one hour per day in this cycle, how long will it take for the water to start overflowing from the reservoir?

In a $5 \times 5$ table, all numbers from 1 to 25 are written, one number per cell. Consider all pairs of numbers that are in the same row or the same column. For each pair, the ratio of the larger number to the smaller number is calculated. The minimum of these ratios among all pairs is denoted by $A$. What is the maximum value that $A$ can take for different arrangements of the numbers in the table?

Through point $P$, draw a line parallel to $CD$, intersecting the extension of side $CB$ at point $K$; through point $Q$, draw a line parallel to $BD$, intersecting the extension of side $CB$ at point $L$. Prove: The circumcircle of $\triangle BKP$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle CLQ$.

Let the sum $K=K_{1}+K_{2}$ of two convex curves $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$ contain a straight line segment $A B$ of length $a$. Prove that in this case, either one of the curves $K_{1}, K_{2}$ contains a straight line segment parallel to $A B$ of length $a$, or both curves $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$ contain straight line segments parallel to $A B$, the sum of whose lengths is $a$.

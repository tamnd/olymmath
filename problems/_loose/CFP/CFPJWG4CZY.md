Let's prove the following theorems: a) if the first and second trace of a plane form equal angles with the projection axis, then the plane itself forms equal angles with the two picture planes; b) such a plane is perpendicular to the first bisecting plane; c) if the two traces of a plane coincide, then the plane forms equal angles with the two picture planes and d) this plane is perpendicular to the second bisecting plane.

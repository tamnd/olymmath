Psychological Tests. In our time, students in schools are often offered "psychological tests." Here is one of them.

An English officer, returning from China after the Boxer Rebellion, fell asleep in church during a service. He dreamed that an executioner was approaching to behead him, and at the very moment when the saber was about to strike his neck, his wife, wanting to wake him up, gently touched his neck with a fan. The shock was so great that the officer fell dead on the spot. Something is wrong in this story.

What exactly is it?

Another good question for a student familiar with mathematics goes like this.

If we were selling apples by cubic centimeters, how would we determine how many cubic centimeters are contained, say, in a dozen dozen apples?

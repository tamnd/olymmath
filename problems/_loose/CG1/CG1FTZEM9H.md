$a_{i}, b_{i}, c_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sqrt[n]{\left(a_{1}+b_{1}+c_{1}\right)\left(a_{2}+b_{2}+c_{2}\right) \cdots\left(a_{n}+b_{n}+c_{n}\right)} \geqslant \sqrt[n]{a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}}+\sqrt[n]{b_{1} b_{2} \cdots b_{n}}+ \\
\sqrt[n]{c_{1} c_{2} \cdots c_{n}} .
\end{array}
$$

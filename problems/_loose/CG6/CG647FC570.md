There are 200 cards of size $1 \times 2$, on each of which the numbers +1 and -1 are written. Can these cards be used to fill a sheet of grid paper of size $4 \times 100$ in such a way that the products of the numbers in each column and each row of the resulting table are positive? (A card occupies two adjacent cells entirely.)

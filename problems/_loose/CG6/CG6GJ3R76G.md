An equilateral pentagon $A M N P Q$ is inscribed in triangle $A B C$ such that $M \in \overline{A B}$, $Q \in \overline{A C}$, and $N, P \in \overline{B C}$. Let $S$ be the intersection of $\overline{M N}$ and $\overline{P Q}$. Denote by $\ell$ the angle bisector of $\angle M S Q$.

Prove that $\overline{O I}$ is parallel to $\ell$, where $O$ is the circumcenter of triangle $A B C$, and $I$ is the incenter of triangle $A B C$.

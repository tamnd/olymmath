In square $A B C D$, point $P$ is taken on side $A B$, point $Q$ on side $B C$, point $R$ on side $C D$, and point $S$ on side $D A$; it turns out that figure $P Q R S$ is a rectangle. Prove that then rectangle $P Q R S$ is either a square or has the property that its sides are parallel to the diagonals of the square.

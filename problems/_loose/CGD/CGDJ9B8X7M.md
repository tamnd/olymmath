There is a relationship between the function pairs $a^{x}$ and $\stackrel{a}{\log } x$, $x^{n}$ and $\sqrt[n]{x}$, $1 / x^{n}$ and $1 / \sqrt[n]{x}$. What does this relationship consist of? We call such functions inverses of each other. Why is the function $1 / x$ special from this perspective? Can we mention other such functions? (involutive functions). When is the inverse of a convex function convex, and when is it concave?

In $\triangle A B C$, if $C=\frac{\pi}{2}, C D$ is the altitude, the inradii of $\triangle B D C$ and $\triangle A D C$ are $r_{1}, r_{2}$, and the semiperimeters are $s_{1}, s_{2}$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $r_{1}+r_{2}+r=h_{c}$;
(2) $\left(s_{1} \pm r_{1}\right)^{2}+\left(s_{2} \pm r_{2}\right)^{2}=(s \pm r)^{2}$.

(Maclaurin and Newton inequalities) Let $n \geqslant 2$ be an integer and $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be strictly positive real numbers. If $p$ is an integer such that $1 \leqslant p \leqslant n$, we set:

$$
d_{p}=\frac{1}{\binom{n}{p}} \sum_{\text {sym }} x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{p}
$$

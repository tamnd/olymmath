In a regular $\triangle A B C$, point $P$ is on side $B C$. A new triangle is formed with $A P, B P, C P$ as its sides, and the largest interior angle of this new triangle is $\theta$, then
(A) $\theta \geqslant 90^{\circ}$.
(B) $\theta \leqslant 120^{\circ}$.
(C) $\theta=120^{\circ}$.
(D) $\theta=135^{\circ}$.
(Anhui Province, Anqing and other counties and cities Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

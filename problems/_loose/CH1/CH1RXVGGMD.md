It is known that there exists a positive integer $n$, such that the number $\underbrace{11 \cdots \cdots 11}_{n \uparrow}$ is divisible by 1987.
Prove: The number
$$
p=\underbrace{11 \cdots \cdots 11}_{n \uparrow} \underbrace{99 \cdots \cdots}_{n \uparrow} 9988 \cdots \cdots \underbrace{88}_{n \uparrow} \underbrace{77 \cdots \cdots 77}_{n \uparrow}
$$

and $ q=\underbrace{11 \cdots \cdots 11}_{n+1 \uparrow} \underbrace{99 \cdots \cdots 99}_{n+1 \uparrow} \underbrace{88 \cdots \cdots}_{n+1 \uparrow} 88 \underbrace{77 \cdots \cdots 77}_{n+1 \uparrow}$

are both divisible by 1987.

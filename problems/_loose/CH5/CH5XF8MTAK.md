Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=3, a_{n+2}=3 a_{n+1}-a_{n}$.
(1) Prove that $1+a_{n} a_{n+2}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$ is a perfect square;
(2) Let $b_{n}=\left[\frac{a_{n+1}^{2}}{a_{n}}\right] \cdot\left\{\frac{a_{n+1}^{2}}{a_{n}}\right\}$, prove that $\prod_{k=2}^{2020} b_{k}$ is an integer. (where $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$, $\{x\}=x-[x]$).

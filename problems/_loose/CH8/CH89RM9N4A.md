Let $M, N$, and $P$ be the midpoints of the sides $BC, CA$, and $AB$ of the acute-angled triangle $ABC$. Denote by $A', B'$, and $C'$ the points diametrically opposite to the vertices $A, B$, and $C$, respectively, in the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. On the open segments $MA', NB'$, and $PC'$, consider points $X, Y$, and $Z$ such that $\frac{MX}{XA'}=\frac{NY}{YB'}=\frac{PZ}{ZC'}$.

a) Prove that the lines $AX, BY$, and $CZ$ are concurrent at a point $S$.

b) Show that $OS < OG$, where $O$ is the circumcenter, and $G$ is the centroid of triangle $ABC$.

(Jury, SRR, 83). Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be a sequence of numbers (Fibonacci), defined by the equalities $a_{i}=a_{2}=1, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n} (n \in \mathrm{N})$. Prove that if the polynomial $P(x)$ of degree 990 satisfies the conditions $P(k)=a_{k}$ for $k=992, \ldots, 1982$, then $P(1983)=a_{1983}-1$. 23.10. (England, 78). Prove that:

a) for any $n \in \mathrm{N}$ there exists a polynomial $P_{n}(x)$ of degree $n$ with integer coefficients, satisfying the identity

$$
2 \cos n t=P_{n}(2 \cos t), t \in \mathrm{R}
$$

b) for any $\alpha \in \mathbf{Q}$ the number $\cos \boldsymbol{\alpha}$ is either one of the numbers $0, \pm 1 / 2, \pm 1$, or irrational.

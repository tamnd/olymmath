Let $n\in \mathbb{N},\ n\ge 2$. For any matrix $A\in \mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$, let $m(A)$ be the number of non-zero minors of $A$. Prove that:

a)$m(I_n)=2^n-1$;
b)If $A\in \mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ is non-singular, then $m(A)\ge 2^n-1$.

(Jury, VNR, 77). For which values of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ do there exist non-zero polynomials $P$ and $Q$ in $n$ variables with integer coefficients, satisfying the identity

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{n}\right) P\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}\right) \equiv Q\left(x_{1}^{2}, x_{2}^{2}, \ldots, x_{n}^{2}\right) \\
& x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n} \in \mathbf{R} ?
\end{aligned}
$$

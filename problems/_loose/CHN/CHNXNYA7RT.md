$$
\overrightarrow{(r}-\overrightarrow{q)^{2}}-R^{2}=0
$$

then, by substituting an arbitrary vector $\overrightarrow{r_{1}}=\overrightarrow{O M}$ into its left side, we obtain a number equal to the power of point $M$ relative to the sphere.

Let (with respect to a Cartesian $x, y$-coordinate system) $p$ be the parabola with $y=x^{2}$ as its equation.

a) Prove: Through the point $(0 ; 1)$ there is exactly one chord of $p$ with length 2.

b) Determine all real numbers $c \geq 0$ for which the following statement holds: Through the point $(0 ; c)$ there are exactly two chords of $p$ with length 2.

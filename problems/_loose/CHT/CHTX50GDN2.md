Let $A B C$ be a right-angled isosceles triangle with $C$ as the vertex of the right angle. Squares are constructed outward on the sides $A B, B C$, and $A C$. The intersection points of the diagonals of these squares are denoted in this order as $D, B$, and $F$.

Prove that the area $A_{D}$ of triangle $D E F$ is equal to the area $A_{Q}$ of one of the squares constructed over $A C$ or $B C$!

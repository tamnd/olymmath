In the triangle $A B C$ let $\angle B A C=60^{\circ}$. Let $E$ be a point on the line $A B$ such that $B$ lies between $A$ and $E$ and $B E=B C$. Similarly, let $F$ be a point on $A C$ such that $C$ lies between $A$ and $F$ and $C F=B C$. The circumcircle of $A C E$ intersects $E F$ in $K$. Show that $K$ lies on the bisector of $\angle B A C$.

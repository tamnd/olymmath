If the system of equations

$$
\begin{gathered}
x y=a \\
x+y=b
\end{gathered}
$$

is satisfied by $x_{1}=\alpha, y_{1}=\beta$, then the solution will also be $x_{2}=\beta$, $y_{2}=\alpha$, and there is no other solution.

On the same plane, draw two convex polygons $P_{1}, P_{2}$ with $n_{1}, n_{2}$ sides respectively, where $n_{1} \leqslant n_{2}$. If $P_{1}, P_{2}$ do not have any overlapping segments, then the maximum number of intersection points between $P_{1}, P_{2}$ is
(A) $2 n_{1}$.
(B) $2 n_{2}$.
(C) $n_{1} n_{2}$.
(D) $n_{1}+n_{2}$.
(E) None of the above.
(19th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1968)

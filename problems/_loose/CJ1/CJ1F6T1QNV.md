Given a square $A B C D$ with side length $a$ and a segment $K L$ of length $5 a$ such that $A \equiv K$ and side $A B$ lies on segment $K L$. The square $A B C D$ moves along segment $K L$ by "rolling" (rotating around the lower right corner of the square by $90^{\circ}$) until side $A B$ again coincides with a part of segment $K L (B \equiv L)$.

a) Sketch how the point $S$, which is the midpoint of side $A B$, will move.

b) Determine the length of the curve traced by point $S$ during its motion.

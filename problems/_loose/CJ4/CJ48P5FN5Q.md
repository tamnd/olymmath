Lin Example 29 (1990 National Training Team Training Question) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive integers, all not exceeding $2n, n \neq$ 4. Prove that $\min _{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n}\left[a_{i}, a_{j}\right] \leqslant 6\left(\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]+1\right)$. The notation $\left[a_{i}, a_{j}\right]$ represents the least common multiple of $a_{i}$ and $a_{j}$.

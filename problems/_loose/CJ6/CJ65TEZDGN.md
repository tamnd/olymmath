Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ have the sum of the first $n$ terms $S_{n}$ related to $a_{n}$ by $S_{n}=-b a_{n}+1-\frac{1}{(1+b)^{n}}$, where $b$ is a constant independent of $n$, and $b \neq-1$.
(1) Find the expression for $a_{n}$ in terms of $b$ and $n$;
(2) When $b=3$, from which term does the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $S_{n}-a_{n}>\frac{1}{2}$?

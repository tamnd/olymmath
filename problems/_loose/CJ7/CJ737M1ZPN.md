In an isosceles triangle $ABC$, we have $|AB|=|AC|$ and $\angle BAC < 60^{\circ}$. Let point $D$ be on the segment $\overline{AC}$ such that $\angle DBC = \angle BAC$, let $E$ be the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of segment $\overline{BD}$ and the line parallel to $BC$ through point $A$, and let $F$ be a point on the line $AC$ such that $A$ lies between $C$ and $F$ and $|AF| = 2|AC|$.

(a) Prove that the lines $BE$ and $AC$ are parallel.

(b) Prove that the perpendicular from $F$ to $AB$ and the perpendicular from $E$ to $AC$ intersect on the line $BD$.

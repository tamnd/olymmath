Several chips are placed on a chessboard. In one move, one of the chips is moved to a free adjacent (vertically or horizontally) square. After several moves, it turned out that each chip had visited all the squares exactly once and returned to its original square. Prove that there was a moment when none of the chips were on their original squares.

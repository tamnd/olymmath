A square table $2009 \times 2009$ is filled with the numbers 1, 2, 3,.., 2009 such that each of these numbers appears in every row and every column. If the table is symmetric with respect to one of its diagonals, then all the numbers $1, 2, 3, \ldots$, 2009 also appear on that diagonal. Prove it!

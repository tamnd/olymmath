A row of $2n$ real numbers is called *"Sozopolian"*, if for each $m$, such that $1\leq m\leq 2n$, the sum of the first $m$ members of the row is an integer or the sum of the last $m$ members of the row is an integer. What's the least number of integers that a *Sozopolian* row can have, if the number of its members is:
a) 2016;
b) 2017?

The city $X$ consists of 10 infinite parallel avenues intersecting perpendicular streets at equal intervals. Two police officers, moving along the avenues and streets, are trying to detect a gangster who can hide behind buildings. If the gangster ends up on the same avenue or the same street as any police officer, he will be detected. The gangster's speed does not exceed 10 times the speed of the police officers, and the police know that at the initial moment, he was no more than 100 blocks away from them. Prove that the police officers can detect the gangster.

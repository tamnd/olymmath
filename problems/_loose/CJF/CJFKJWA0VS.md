For constant numbers $ a,\ b$, let $ f(x)=e^{-x}(ax+b)$. Suppose that the curve $ y=f(x)$ passes through two points $ (t,\ 1),\ (t+1,\ 1).$
Find the area of the part bounded by the curve $ y=f(x)$ and the line $ y=1$.

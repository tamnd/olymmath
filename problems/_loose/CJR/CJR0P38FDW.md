During a long break in math class, Lukcu first drew a circle and then $n$ empty fields around the circumference, where $n \geq 3$, and wrote a positive number in each. Later, he erased these numbers and in each field wrote the square root of the product of the two numbers that were previously in the adjacent fields. Show that there exists a field in which the number written is less than or equal to the number that was written there before.

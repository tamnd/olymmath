It is known that the tangents at points $\mathrm{K}$ and $\mathrm{L}$ to the circumcircle of triangle $\mathrm{BKL}$ intersect on the circumcircle of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$. Prove that the lines $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$ are tangent to the circle $\mathrm{PKL}$.

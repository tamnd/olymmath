In the complex plane, there are three points: $c_{1}=m+b \mathrm{i}, c_{2}=a+b \mathrm{i}, c_{3}=a+n \mathrm{i}$, where $a>m, n>b$, $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ (here $S_{i}$ represents the point corresponding to the complex number $c_{i}(i=1,2,3)$) form a triangle. Prove: The complex number $z$ that satisfies $\frac{1}{z-c_{1}}$ $+\frac{1}{z-c_{2}}+\frac{1}{z-c_{3}}=0$ represents a point $Z$ located inside this triangle.

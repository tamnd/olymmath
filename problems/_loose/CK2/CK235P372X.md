I $\pi$ cchi are animals that live in families of 1, 2, or 3 individuals. Originally, a family of $3 \pi$ cchi was imported to Italy. A family of $n \pi$ cchi reproduces by growing $2n-2$ new individuals, forming a total of $3n-2$, and dividing into new families (not necessarily two families with the same number of individuals divide in the same way). All families reproduce simultaneously at each brood. For example, after the first brood, there are definitely seven $\pi$ cchi, which could be divided into three families of two and one of one, or one of three and two of two, or seven of one, etc. How many possibilities are there for the total number of $\pi$ cchi after the seventh brood?
(A) 129
(B) 253
(C) $2^{7}$
(D) $\frac{2^{7}+3^{7}+1}{2}$
(E) $3^{7}$

The sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ is defined by $a_{1}=a, a_{2}=b$ and $a_{n+1}=\left(a_{n}^{2}+c\right) / a_{n-1}$ for $n=2,3, \ldots$, where $a, b, c$ are real numbers, such that $a b \neq 0, c>0$.

Prove that $a_{n}(n=1,2, \ldots)$ are integers if and only if $a, b$ and $\left(a^{2}+\right.$ $\left.b^{2}+c\right) / a b$ are integers.

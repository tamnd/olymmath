At the International Mathematical Olympiad, there were 2019 participants. Among them, 1009 had 1010 friends and 1010 had 1009 friends, with the friendship relation being reciprocal. The Machiavellian Theodore, a master in the art of making and breaking friendships, decides to bring about events such as the following:

he chooses three participants $A, B$, and $C$, such that $A$ is friends with $B$ and $C$, but $B$ is not friends with $C$; then $B$ and $C$ become friends, but they end their friendship with $A$; other friendship relations remain unchanged during this event.

Prove that, regardless of the initial friendship relations, Theodore can always manage to ensure that, by the end of the olympiad, each participant has at most one friend.

An equilateral triangle $A B C$ is inscribed in a circle. On the arc $A B$, not containing point $C$, a point $M$ is chosen, different from $A$ and $B$. Let the lines $A C$ and $B M$ intersect at point $K$, and the lines $B C$ and $A M$ intersect at point $N$. Prove that the product of segments $A K$ and $B N$ does not depend on the choice of point $M$.

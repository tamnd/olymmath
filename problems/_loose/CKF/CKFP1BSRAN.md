The function $y=x^{3}+p x+q$ is given.

a) If $M$ is a local maximum, and $m$ is a local minimum of the given function, prove that

$$
M m=q^{2}+\frac{4}{27} p^{3}
$$

b) Determine $p$ and $q$ such that $M-m=4$ and -2 is a root of the function. Investigate the behavior of the obtained function.

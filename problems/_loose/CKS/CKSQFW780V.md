Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle such that $A C > A B$, and $D$ on $(B C)$ such that $A D = A B$ and $D$ is different from $B$. Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of $A B C$, $\Delta$ the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $C$, and $E$ the intersection of $(A D)$ and $\Delta$.

Show that $C D^{2} = A D \cdot D E - B D \cdot D C$.

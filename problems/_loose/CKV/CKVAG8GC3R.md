Scrooge McDuck placed a gold coin in one of the vertices of a hexagon, and there is nothing in the other vertices. Every day, he removes an arbitrary number of coins from one of the vertices of the hexagon and immediately places six times as many coins in an adjacent vertex. If on some day Scrooge McDuck can achieve that all vertices of the hexagon have the same number of coins, he will be awarded the title of "Great Duck." Can Scrooge McDuck earn this title?

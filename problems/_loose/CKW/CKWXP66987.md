$21 \cdot 10 \triangle ABC$ has three sides $a=2, b=2 \sqrt{2}, c=\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{6}$, then the area of the smallest circular paper piece that can cover this triangle is
(A) $2 \pi$.
(B) $4 \pi$.
(C) $2 \sqrt{3} \pi$.
(D) $(2+\sqrt{3}) \pi$.
(China Hubei Province Huanggang Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

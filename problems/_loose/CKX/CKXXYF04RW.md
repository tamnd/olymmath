Given $P$ and $Q$ are moving points on the $x$-axis and $y$-axis respectively, $R(0,-3)$,
$$
\overrightarrow{P R} \cdot \overrightarrow{P M}=0, \overrightarrow{P Q}=\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{Q M} \text {. }
$$
(1) Find the trajectory $C$ of the moving point $M$;
(2) Given $A(a, b)(a \neq 0, b \neq 2)$ is a moving point outside the trajectory $C$, $S(0,2)$, $A B$ and $A D$ are tangent to the curve $C$ at points $B$ and $D$ respectively, the lines $B D$ and $S A$ intersect the line $y=-2$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively, prove that the circle with diameter $E F$ passes through a fixed point.

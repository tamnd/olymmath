(GDR, 80). Find all pairs of polynomials $P(x)$, $Q(x)$ of the third degree with real coefficients, satisfying the following four conditions:

a) both polynomials at each of the points $x=1,2,3,4$ take the value 0 or 1;

b) if $P(1)=0$ or $P(2)=1$, then $Q(1)=Q(3)=1$;

c) if $P(2)=0$ or $P(4)=0$, then $Q(2)=Q(4)=0$;

d) if $P(3)=1$ or $P(4)=1$, then $Q(1)=0$.

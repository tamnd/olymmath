The regular hexagonal plate $A B C D E F=H_{6}$ rolls along the line $A B$, that is, it rotates around $B$ until $C$ reaches a suitable point $C^{\prime}$ on the extension of the side $A B$ beyond $B$, then it rotates around $C^{\prime}$ until $D$ reaches the line $A B$, and so on, until $A$ first returns to the line $A B$ at the point $A^{\prime}$. Show that the area bounded by the segment $A A^{\prime}$ and the path traced by $A$ is equal to the sum of twice the area of $H_{6}$ and the area of the circumscribed circle of $H_{6}$. (For students in the third grade, write $H_{2 n}$ instead of $H_{6}$.)

The measure of an acute angle: $A B^{\circ} C D^{\prime} E F^{\prime \prime}$, where $A, \ldots, F$ (not necessarily distinct) are digits. $A B, C D$, $E F$ are genuine two-digit numbers, and the latter two are less than 60. If we cut the sequence of digits somewhere, move the first part as a whole behind the second part, and again cut the sequence of digits into three two-digit numbers, and consider these as the degrees, minutes, and seconds of an angle, we get the complement of the original angle. Determine all such angles with this property.

. Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $D, E$, and $F$ be three points distinct from the vertices and lying respectively on $[BC], [CA]$, and $[AB]$. Show that the lines $(AD), (BE)$, and $(CF)$ are concurrent if and only if $\frac{\sin \widehat{BAD}}{\sin \widehat{CAD}} \cdot \frac{\sin \widehat{ACF}}{\sin \widehat{BCF}} \cdot \frac{\sin \widehat{CBE}}{\sin \widehat{ABE}}=1$.

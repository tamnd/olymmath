On each side of a hexagon, we draw an equilateral triangle outward, then we mark the midpoints of the sides of the hexagon formed by the new vertices of these triangles. It is to be proven that if the figure formed by the midpoints is a regular hexagon, then the original hexagon is centrally symmetric.

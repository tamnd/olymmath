Prove that there exists a triangle $A B C$, in which, with the usual notation, both Pythagorean equalities $t_{a}^{2}+t_{b}^{2}=t_{c}^{2}$ and $v_{a}^{2}+v_{b}^{2}=v_{c}^{2}$ hold. Further, show that for the interior angles of such a triangle, $|\alpha-\beta|=90^{\circ}$ and $\cos \gamma=\frac{2}{5} \sqrt{5}$.

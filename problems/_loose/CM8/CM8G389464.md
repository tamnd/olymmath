From $[1, n]$ select $r$ numbers to form a set $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{r}\right\}$, allowing numbers to repeat and disregarding the order of these numbers. This is called an $r$-combination with repetition from $[1, n]$, also known as a combination with repetition, denoted as the number of $r$-combinations with repetition from $n$ distinct elements is $F_{n}^{r}$, prove: $F_{n}^{r}=C_{n+r-1}^{r}$.

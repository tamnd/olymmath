Let $f:\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a function that satisfies $\bullet$ $f(x+f(y))=f(x)+f(y)$ for all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$ . $\bullet$ There exist a real number $x$ such that $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{2025}$ . $\bullet$ There does not exist a real number $x$ such that $0<f(x)<\dfrac{1}{2025}$ .
Determine all possible images of $f$ . $\textbf{Note.}$ the image of a function $f$ is defined as $\{ f(x) \mid x \in \mathbb{R} \}$ .

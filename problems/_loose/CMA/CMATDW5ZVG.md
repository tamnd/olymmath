Sixteen people are queuing at the bakery. The owner of the bakery offers wine to the customers. A bottle is handed to the first person in line and passed from person to person from the first in line to the last, without returning. The bottle was passed from a woman to a woman four times, from a woman to a man three times, and from a man to a man six times.

a) How many times was the bottle passed from one customer to another?

b) How many times was the bottle passed from a man in the queue to a woman in the queue?

c) Is the first person in the queue a man or a woman? And the last person in the queue?

(54th Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad) A teacher wrote $n(n>2)$ positive integers on the blackboard, with no two numbers having a divisor relationship. Students take turns erasing some numbers from the blackboard, ensuring that each erases exactly one number, which must be a number that can divide the sum of all remaining numbers on the blackboard before they leave. In the end, there are exactly two numbers left on the blackboard. Question: Is it possible for any $n>2$?

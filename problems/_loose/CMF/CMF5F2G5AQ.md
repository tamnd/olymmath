Find the maximum number $ c$ such that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ to have $$ \{n \cdot \sqrt{2}\} \geq \frac{c}{n}$$ where $ \{n \cdot \sqrt{2}\} = n \cdot \sqrt{2} - [n \cdot \sqrt{2}]$ and $ [x]$ is the integer part of $ x.$ Determine for this number $ c,$ all $ n \in \mathbb{N}$ for which $ \{n \cdot \sqrt{2}\} = \frac{c}{n}.$

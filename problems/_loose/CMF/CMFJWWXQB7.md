Let $n > k \geqslant 2$ be positive integers. Each of the $n$ points on a circle is colored either red or blue, and it is required that any $k$ consecutive points contain at least one red point. Let the number of valid coloring schemes be denoted as $f_{k}(n)$. Try to find the necessary and sufficient conditions for positive integers $n, k$ such that $f_{k}(n)$ is even.

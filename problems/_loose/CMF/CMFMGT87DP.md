There is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, with a point $P$ on $AB$. A ray of light is emitted from point $P$, reaches point $Q$ on $BC$, reflects to point $R$ on $CD$, then reflects to point $S$ on $DA$, and finally returns to point $P$. Prove that quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral, and when point $P$ moves on $AB$, the perimeter of quadrilateral $PQRS$ is a constant (i.e., it only depends on quadrilateral $ABCD$, not on the position of point $P$).

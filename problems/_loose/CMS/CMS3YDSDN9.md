Let $A B C$ be a triangle with all angles acute.

The altitudes $\left[A A_{1}\right],\left[B B_{1}\right]$, and $\left[C C_{1}\right]$ intersect at point $H$. Let $A_{2}$ be the symmetric point of $A$ with respect to $\left(B_{1} C_{1}\right)$, and let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of $A B C$.

a) Prove that the points $O, A_{2}, B_{1}, C$ are concyclic.

b) Prove that $O, H, A_{1}, A_{2}$ are concyclic.

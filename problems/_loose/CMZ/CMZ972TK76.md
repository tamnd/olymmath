Every Friday, ten gentlemen come to the club, and each hands his hat to the doorman. Each hat fits its owner perfectly, but no two hats are the same size. The gentlemen leave one by one in a random order.

As each gentleman departs, the doorman of the club tries to put on the first hat he finds.

If it fits, the gentleman leaves with that hat. If it is too small, the doorman tries the next random hat from the remaining ones. If all the remaining hats are too small, the doorman says to the poor man: "Sir, a hat does not suit you today," and the gentleman goes home with an uncovered head. Find the probability that next Friday the doorman will not have any hats left.

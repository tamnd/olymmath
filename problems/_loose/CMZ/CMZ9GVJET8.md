On a plane, there is a regular $n$-sided polygon with center $O$. The vectors $\overrightarrow{v_{1}}, \overrightarrow{v_{2}}, \ldots, \overrightarrow{v_{n}}$ lead from $O$ to the vertices of the polygon, and they are numbered in order, starting from some vertex. Prove that if $a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{n}$ are some real numbers, then the vector $a_{1} \vec{v}_{1} + a_{2} \overrightarrow{v_{2}} + \ldots + a_{n} \overrightarrow{v_{n}}$ is non-zero.

34*. In a platoon of the national guard, there are sergeants and privates, and each private is subordinate to one or two sergeants. Prove that it is possible to discharge not more than half of the platoon so that each remaining private is commanded by exactly one sergeant.

(1) If the function has a zero in the interval $[-1,1]$, find the range of real number $p$;
(2) Does there exist a constant $q(q \geq 0)$, such that when $x \in[q, 10]$, the range of $f(x)$ is an interval $D$, and the length of $D$ is $12-q$. Note: The length of the interval $[a, b](a<b)$ is $b-a$.

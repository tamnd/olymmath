Prove that for all integers $ m$ and $ n$, the inequality
$$ \dfrac{\phi(\gcd(2^m + 1,2^n + 1))}{\gcd(\phi(2^m + 1),\phi(2^n + 1))} \ge \dfrac{2\gcd(m,n)}{2^{\gcd(m,n)}}$$
holds.

$\left\{\begin{array}{l}\sqrt[3]{x}+\sqrt[3]{y}=3 \\ \sqrt[3]{x^{2}}-\sqrt[3]{x y}+\sqrt[3]{y^{2}}=3\end{array}\right.$

The system of equations is:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\sqrt[3]{x} + \sqrt[3]{y} = 3 \\
\sqrt[3]{x^{2}} - \sqrt[3]{x y} + \sqrt[3]{y^{2}} = 3
\end{array}\right.
$$

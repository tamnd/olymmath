Given a square $A B C D$. Let $P_{1}$ be an arbitrary internal point on the side $A B$, $P_{2}$ be the intersection of the lines $D P_{1}$ and $B C$, $P_{3}$ be the intersection of the lines $A P_{2}$ and $C D$, $P_{4}$ be the intersection of the lines $B P_{3}$ and $D A$, $P_{5}$ be the intersection of the lines $C P_{4}$ and $A B$, and the points $P_{6}, P_{7}, \ldots, P_{13}$ are further constructed according to the same procedure. Prove that $P_{13}=P_{1}$.

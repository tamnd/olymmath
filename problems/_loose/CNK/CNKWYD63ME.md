Let $M$ be a point on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, different from the vertices of the triangle, and let $H_{1}, H_{2}, H_{3}$ be the orthocenters of triangles $MBC$, $MAC$, and $MAB$, respectively.

a) Show that triangles $ABC$ and $H_{1} H_{2} H_{3}$ are congruent and have their sides respectively parallel.

b) Show that $M$ is the centroid of triangle $H_{1} H_{2} H_{3}$ if and only if triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

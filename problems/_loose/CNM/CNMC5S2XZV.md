Prove: the polynomial
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x, y)= & x^{2}\left(x^{2}-1\right)^{2}+y^{2}\left(y^{2}-1\right)^{2}- \\
& \left(x^{2}-1\right)\left(y^{2}-1\right)\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-1\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

is non-negative but cannot be expressed as a sum of squares of real polynomials.

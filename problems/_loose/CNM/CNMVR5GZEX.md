Anna and Péter play the following game. They put 100 slips of paper into an urn, on which they have previously written the integers from 1 to 100. Without replacement, they draw two slips, and if the sum of the drawn numbers is odd, Anna wins, if it is even, then Péter wins. Is the game fair?

What is the answer if they play according to the sum of three drawn numbers?

Draw a circle around the $A B C$ triangle, intersecting the $B C$ side at points $P, Q$, the $C A$ side at points $R, S$, and the $A B$ side at points $T, U$. Prove that the

$$
\frac{A T}{T B} \cdot \frac{A U}{U B} \cdot \frac{B P}{P C} \cdot \frac{B Q}{Q C} \cdot \frac{C R}{R A} \cdot \frac{C S}{S A}
$$

product of the division ratios is 1.

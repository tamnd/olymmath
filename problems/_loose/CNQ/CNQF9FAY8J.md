Given the equation $\sqrt{a(x-a)}+\sqrt{a(y-a)}=\sqrt{x-a}-\sqrt{a-y}$ holds in the real number range, where $a, x, y$ are distinct real numbers, then the value of $\frac{3 x^{2}+x y-y^{2}}{x^{2}-x y+y^{2}}$ is
(A) 3.
(B) $\frac{1}{3}$.
(C) 2.
(D) $\frac{5}{3}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1991)

a) When the distance from the cheese to the smaller of the trees is 30 meters, two equal right-angled triangles are formed. In other arrangements (in the plane of the trees), it is clear that equality cannot be achieved. If you move out of the plane, the distance will be greater.

b) Place the "third crow" underground at a distance of 30 meters symmetrically opposite the second crow. The required total distance for the two crows is the length of the two-segment broken line connecting the first and "third" crow with the cheese. The shortest distance is a straight line. This results in a point located 20 meters from the first tree. The length of this shortest path is: $20 \sqrt{2} + 30 \sqrt{2} = 50 \sqrt{2}$. Since $50 \sqrt{2} > 70 \Leftrightarrow 5000 > 4900$, a path of 70 meters is unattainable.

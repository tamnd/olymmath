$$
x^{2}+p x-\frac{1}{2 p^{2}}, \text { where } p \in \mathbf{R}, p \neq 0
$$

the inequality $x_{1}^{4}+x_{2}^{4} \geqslant 2+\sqrt{2}$ holds.

Let $G$ be a connected, locally finite graph. Prove: For a spanning subgraph $T$ of $G$, the following statements are equivalent:
(i) $\bar{T}$ is a topological spanning tree of $|G|$;
(ii) $T$ is an edge-maximal graph such that $\bar{T}$ contains no cycles;
(iii) $T$ is an edge-minimal graph such that $\bar{T}$ is arc-connected.

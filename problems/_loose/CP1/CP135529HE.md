Let points $A$ and $B$ be located on opposite sides of a mountain peak and have the same altitude. The mountain path connecting the two points is in the shape of a zigzag line, with each vertex being higher than points $A$ and $B$. It is known that there is a tourist at each of points $A$ and $B$. Can the two tourists walk along the path connecting points $A$ and $B$ while maintaining the same altitude at all times?

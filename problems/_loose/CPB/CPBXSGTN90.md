Let $z_{1}, \ldots, z_{n}$ be the vertices of a regular $n$-gon in the complex plane, and $z_{0}$ be its center. Prove that if $P(z)$ is a polynomial of degree not higher than $n-1$, then $P\left(z_{1}\right)+\ldots+P\left(z_{n}\right)=n P\left(z_{0}\right)$.

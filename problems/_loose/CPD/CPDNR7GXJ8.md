If a trapezoid has a circumcircle, it is called "good". Given that trapezoid $ABCD$ is good, and $AB \parallel CD$, $CD < AB$, construct the following figure:
(1) Draw a line through point $B$ parallel to $AD$ intersecting line $CD$ at point $S$;
(2) Draw two tangents from point $S$ to the circumcircle of trapezoid $ABCD$, with the points of tangency being $E$ and $F$, where points $E$ and $A$ are on the same side of line $CD$.

If $\angle BSE = \angle FSC$, describe the good trapezoid $ABCD$ (by describing the side lengths and the interior angles of the trapezoid), and try to make the conclusion as simple as possible.

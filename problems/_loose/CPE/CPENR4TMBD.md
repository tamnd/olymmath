Agatha, Isa, and Nick each have a different type of bicycle. One of them has an electric bike, one has a racing bike, and one has a mountain bike. The bikes each have a different color: green, blue, or black. Each of the three owners makes two statements, one of which is true and one is false:

- Agatha says: "I have an electric bike. Isa has a blue bike."
- Isa says: "I have a mountain bike. Nick has an electric bike."
- Nick says: "I have a blue bike. The racing bike is black."

Exactly one of the following statements is certainly true. Which one?
A) Agatha has a green bike.
B) Agatha has a mountain bike.
C) Isa has a green bike.
D) Isa has a mountain bike.
E) Nick has an electric bike.

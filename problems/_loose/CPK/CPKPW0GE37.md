Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with two points $P$ and $Q$ inside it, such that $\angle A C P=\angle B C Q, \angle C A P=$ $\angle B A Q$. Draw perpendiculars from point $P$ to $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being points $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. Prove that $\angle D E F=90^{\circ}$ if and only if point $Q$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle B D F$.

For a triangle $A B C$, it is required: The angle bisector through $A$ and the perpendicular bisector of $A B$ intersect at a point $D$, which lies on the side $B C$.

a) What size must the angle $\angle A C B$ have in a triangle that meets this requirement and in which the angle $\angle A B C$ has a size of $35^{\circ}$? Draw such a triangle!

b) Draw a triangle that also meets the above requirement and in which the angle $\angle A B C$ has a size of $50^{\circ}$!

c) Is there a triangle that also meets the above requirement and in which the angle $\angle A B C$ has a size of $60^{\circ}$?

Consider a word $X=\ell_{1} \ell_{2} \ldots \ell_{2014}$, where $\ell_{k} \in\{A, B\}$, for any $1 \leq k \leq 2014$. Prove that 806 palindromic words are sufficient to "glue" together such that we obtain the word X.

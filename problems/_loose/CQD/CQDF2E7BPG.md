(15 points) A glass is filled to the brim with saltwater. On the surface, fresh ice of mass $m=502$ is floating. What volume $\Delta V$ of water will spill out of the glass by the time the ice melts? Neglect surface tension. The density of fresh ice $\rho_{n}=0.92 / c M^{3}$, the density of salt ice $\rho_{c}=0.952 / c M^{3}$, the density of fresh water $\rho_{n s}=12 / c M^{3}$. Neglect the change in total volume when mixing two liquids.

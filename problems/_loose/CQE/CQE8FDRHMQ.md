Andrea is sorting triangles whose sides, expressed in centimeters, are whole numbers, into narrow, wide, and other categories. A narrow triangle is one where the sum of the lengths of some two sides is only 1 cm greater than the length of the third side. A wide triangle is one where the sum of the lengths of some two sides is twice the length of the third side. Find

a) all narrow triangles with two sides measuring $6 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $8 \mathrm{~cm}$,

b) all wide triangles with two sides measuring $3 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $4 \mathrm{~cm}$,

c) all triangles with one side measuring $5 \mathrm{~cm}$ that are both wide and narrow at the same time.

(SRP, 82). Let polynomials $P_{0}(x)$, $P_{i}(x), \ldots, P_{n}(x)$ with real coefficients and numbers $a_{i}, \ldots, a_{n} \in \mathbf{R}$ be given. Prove that if the function

$$
f(x)=P_{0}(x)+\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}\left|P_{k}(x)\right|
$$

does not take any value more than once for $x \in \mathbf{R}$, then the set of all its values is $\mathbf{R}$.

Given a circle $(O)$ with radius $R$, we project the point $M$ on its circumference onto two perpendicular diameters, thus forming the rectangle $M P O Q$. Under what condition of the position of $M$ will $t=a k$, where $t$ is the area of the rectangle, $k$ is half the perimeter of the rectangle, and $a$ is a given length? Discuss!

Apply the results if $a=\frac{12}{35} R$.

136 Proof: For any natural number $k>1$, there exists a power of 2 such that at least half of the last $k$ digits are 9 (for example, for $k=2$, there is $2^{12}=$ 4096; for $k=3$, there is $2^{53}=\cdots 992$; etc.).
(57th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1995)

Prove that (1) any number of the form $2^{n}$ (where $n$ is any natural number) cannot be expressed as the sum of two or more consecutive natural numbers.
(2) any natural number that is not a power of 2 can be expressed as the sum of two or more consecutive natural numbers.
(Polish Mathematics Competition, 1960)

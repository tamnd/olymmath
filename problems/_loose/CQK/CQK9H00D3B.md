Let $a$ be a real number.
1) Prove that there is a triangle with side lengths $\sqrt{a^2-a + 1}$, $\sqrt{a^2+a + 1}$, and $\sqrt{4a^2 + 3}$.
2) Prove that the area of this triangle does not depend on $a$.

Drop perpendiculars IP and IQ from point I to sides BC and AB respectively, points P and Q are the points of tangency of these sides with the inscribed circle, quadrilateral PIQB is a square. Angles KIL and PIQ are right angles, so angles PIK and QIL are equal, which implies the congruence of right triangles PIK and QIL. By Thales' theorem, the length KP=QL is half the length of BP=BQ, and the length AQ is twice the length of BQ=BP.

Therefore, the length of side AB is $ AL + LB = \frac{6}{5} AL = \frac{6}{5} AO = \frac{3}{5} AC $. From the Pythagorean theorem, $ BC = \frac{4}{5} AC $. Therefore, $ AB : BC : CA = 3 : 4 : 5 $.

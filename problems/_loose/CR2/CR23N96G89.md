Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in a fixed circle $(O)$ with $BC$ fixed and $A$ varying on $(O)$. Denote $H$ as the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$ and take $D$, $E$ on $AB$, $AC$ respectively such that $H$ is the midpoint of $DE$. Prove that when $A$ moves on $(O)$, the center of $(ADE)$ belongs to a fixed circle.

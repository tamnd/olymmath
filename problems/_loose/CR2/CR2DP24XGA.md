The points $A, B, C$ and $D$ lie on a straight line in the given order. A circle $k$ passes through the points $B$ and $C$ and $A M, A N, D K$ and $D L$ are tangents to $k$.

a) Prove that the points $P=M N \cap B C$ and $Q=$ $K L \cap B C$ don't depend on the circle $k$.

b). If $A D=a, B C=b(a>b)$ and the segment $B C$ is moving along $A D$, find the minimal length of the segment $P Q$.

a) 2007 natural numbers greater than 1 are written. Prove that it is possible to cross out one number so that the product of the remaining numbers can be represented as the difference of squares of two natural numbers.

b) 2007 natural numbers greater than 1 are written, one of which is 2006. It turns out that there is only one such number among the written ones, such that the product of the remaining numbers can be represented as the difference of squares of two natural numbers. Prove that this number is 2006.

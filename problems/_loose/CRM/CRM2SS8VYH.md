For any triangle $ABC$, let $D$ and $E$ be the intersection points of the angle bisectors from $A$ and $B$ with the opposite sides $BC$ and $AC$, respectively; furthermore, let $P$ be any point on the segment $DE$.

Prove:

Under this assumption, the sum of the distances from the point $P$ to the lines through $B, C$ and through $A, C$ is always equal to the distance from the point $P$ to the line through $A, B$.

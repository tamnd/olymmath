Let $O$ be the center of a unit circle and $d$ be a diameter of this circle. Consider a point $X \ne O$ with $XO \perp d$ . Let points $X_1$ , $X_2$ , $...$ be the points on line $XO$ such that $\overline{X_iO} = \frac{1}{2^i}$ . Let $p_i$ be the chord passing through $X_i$ with $p_i \perp XO$ and let $C_i$ and $B_i$ be the endpoints of $p_i$ . If $A_i$ is the area of the triangle $OB_iC_i$ , compute $$ \sum^{\infty}_{i=1} A^2_i. $$

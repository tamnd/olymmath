9・100 Given the sequence $\left\{r_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $r_{1}=2$,
$$
r_{n}=r_{1} r_{2} \cdots r_{n-1}+1, n=2,3, \cdots
$$

If natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ satisfy
$$
\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}<1 \text {, }
$$

Prove: $\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}} \leqslant \frac{1}{r_{1}}+\frac{1}{r_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{r_{n}}$.

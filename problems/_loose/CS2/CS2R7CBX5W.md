Given $\triangle A B C$, points $D, E$ on side $A C$ are arranged in the order $A, E, D, C$. Through point $E$, draw a line parallel to $B C$, intersecting the circumcircle of $\triangle A B D$ at a second point $F$, with $F$ and $E$ on opposite sides of line $A B$. Through point $E$, draw a line parallel to $A B$, intersecting the circumcircle of $\triangle B C D$ at a second point $G$, with $E$ and $G$ on opposite sides of line $B C$. Prove: $D, E, F, G$ are concyclic.

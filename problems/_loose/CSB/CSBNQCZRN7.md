The numbers $13,14,15, \ldots, 25$ are painted in five colors: one black number, three red, three blue, three yellow, and three green.

It is known that:

- all four sums of three same-colored numbers are equal;
- the number 13 is red, 15 is yellow, 23 is blue.

(a) (1 point) Find the black number.

(b) (3 points) Find the three green numbers.

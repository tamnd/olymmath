Let circle $\Gamma$ be the incircle of $\triangle ABC$. Given that $AB < AC$, the excircle of $\triangle ABC$ is tangent to side $BC$ at point $A'$. $X$ is a point on segment $AA'$ and outside circle $\Gamma$. Two tangents from point $X$ to circle $\Gamma$ intersect side $BC$ at points $Y$ and $Z$. Prove that $XY + XZ$ is independent of the choice of $X$.

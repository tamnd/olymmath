Pavel came up with a new way of adding numbers: he calls the "pavlosum" of numbers $a$ and $b$ the value of the expression $a \# b=(a+b) /(1-a b)$, if it is defined. Just like in ordinary arithmetic, multiplication by a natural number Pavel understands as adding the same number of addends: $a @ b=((a \# a) \# a)$...\#a (here $b$ "addends"). Are there any unequal natural numbers $x$ and $y$ in Pavel's arithmetic for which the "products" $x @ y$ and $y @ x$ are equal?

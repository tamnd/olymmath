Consider a game played by two players taking turns, initially with $k$ piles of stones, the number of stones being $n_{1}, n_{2}, \cdots n_{k}$. Each turn allows taking 1 or several stones from one pile, and the remaining stones can be distributed to other piles (piles that have been reduced to zero stones cannot have stones added to them), the player who performs the last operation wins. Question: For which arrays $\left(n_{l}, n_{2} \cdots n_{k}\right)$ does the first player have a winning strategy?

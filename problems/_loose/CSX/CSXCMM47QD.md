A natural number $n$ is such that $n+2$ is divisible by 3, and $n+3$ is divisible by 4. Which of the following statements are guaranteed to be true, i.e., hold for all $n$ that meet the condition?

a) $n+4$ is divisible by 5;

b) $n+5$ is divisible by 6;

c) $n+6$ is divisible by 7;

d) $n+7$ is divisible by 8.

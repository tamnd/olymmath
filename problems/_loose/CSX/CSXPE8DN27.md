Given that $a, b, c, d$ are all integers, and $p=a^{2}+b^{2}$ is a prime number. If $p \mid\left(c^{2}+d^{2}\right)$, prove: $\frac{c^{2}+d^{2}}{p}$ can be expressed as the sum of two squares of integers.

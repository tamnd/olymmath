(1) Prove: For any real numbers $x$, $y$, $z$, we have
$$
x^{2}+2 y^{2}+3 z^{2} \geqslant \sqrt{3}(x y+y z+z x) .
$$
(2) Does there exist a real number $k>\sqrt{3}$, such that for any real numbers $x$, $y$, $z$, we have
$$
x^{2}+2 y^{2}+3 z^{2} \geqslant k(x y+y z+z x) \text { ? }
$$

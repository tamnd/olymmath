Let $\{a_n \}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence such that $a_1=\frac 12$ and
$$a_n=\biggl( \frac{2n-3}{2n} \biggr) a_{n-1} \forall n \geq 2.$$
Prove that for every positive integer $n,$ we have $\sum_{k=1}^n a_k <1.$

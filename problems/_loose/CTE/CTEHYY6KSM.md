\section*{Problem 3B - 261043B}

a) Prove that there exist five pairwise distinct real numbers such that the following statement holds:

For every selection of three of the five numbers, there exists a triangle whose side lengths are the three selected numbers (where the same unit of measurement is used for all three side lengths).

b) Determine, if five such numbers exist, how many pairwise non-congruent triangles can be formed from these five numbers in the manner described in part a).

c) Prove that whenever five such numbers exist, at least one of the mentioned triangles is acute-angled!

About how grasshoppers jump. Suppose there is a tape divided into cells and extending to the right to infinity. On the first cell of this tape sits a grasshopper. From any cell, the grasshopper can jump either one or two cells to the right. In how many ways can the grasshopper reach the $n$-th cell from the beginning of the tape?

We draw a quarter-circle arc between the sides of a right angle around the vertex $O$ of the angle, then construct a tangent at a point $P$ of the arc. The tangent intersects the sides of the angle at points $Q_{1}, Q_{2}$. Consider the centers $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ of the incircles of triangles $O P Q_{1}$ and $O P Q_{2}$, respectively. What path does $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ trace as $P$ runs along the quarter-circle arc?

The ninety-first problem: Prove that for any positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, there exists a set $\mathrm{S}$ consisting of $\mathrm{n}$ positive integers, such that for any two distinct $a, b \in S$, $a-b$ divides $a$ and $b$, but does not divide any other elements in the set $S$.

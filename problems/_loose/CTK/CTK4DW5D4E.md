Inside an equilateral triangle $A B C$, points $P, Q, R$ are constructed such that:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \widehat{Q A B}=\widehat{P B A}=15^{\circ} \\
& \widehat{R B C}=\widehat{Q C B}=20^{\circ} \\
& \widehat{P C A}=\widehat{R A C}=25^{\circ}
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the angles of triangle $P Q R$. Go to the puzzle at the cell corresponding to the number (the angles are in degrees) $\widehat{R P Q}+\widehat{Q R P}$.

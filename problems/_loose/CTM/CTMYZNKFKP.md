In space, there are eight points, no four of which are coplanar, and 17 line segments are drawn between these points. Prove: among these 17 line segments, there must exist three line segments with lengths $a$, $b$, and $c$ such that
$$
\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{4} \geqslant \sqrt{3 p(p-a)(p-b)(p-c)},
$$
where $p=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$.

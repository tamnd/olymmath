An equilateral triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle $\Omega$ and circumscribed around a circle $\omega$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen on sides $AC$ and $AB$ respectively such that segment $PQ$ is tangent to $\omega$. The circle $\Omega_{b}$ with center $P$ passes through $B$, and the circle $\Omega_{c}$ with center $Q$ passes through $C$. Prove that the circles $\Omega$, $\Omega_{b}$, and $\Omega_{c}$ have a common point.

(A. Akopln, P. Kozhevnikov)

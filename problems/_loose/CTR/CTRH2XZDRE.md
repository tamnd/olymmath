Let the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfy: $\mathrm{e}^{a_{n}}+a_{n}-n=0, \ln b_{n}+b_{n}-n=0$.
(1) Prove that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}+b_{n}\right\}$ is an arithmetic sequence;
(2) Define $c_{n}=\left(a_{n}+b_{n}\right)\left(\mathrm{e}^{a_{n}}+\ln b_{n}\right)$,

Prove that $\frac{1}{c_{1}}+\frac{1}{c_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{c_{n}}<\frac{5}{3}$.

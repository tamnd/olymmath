Circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ intersect at points $P$ and $Q$. Segments $A C$ and $B D$ are chords of circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$, respectively, and segment $A B$ and ray $C D$ intersect at point $P$. Ray $B D$ and segment $A C$ intersect at point $X$. Point $Y$ on circle $\omega_{1}$ is such that $P Y \| B D$. Point $Z$ on circle $\omega_{2}$ is such that $P Z \| A C$. Prove that points $Q, X, Y$ and $Z$ lie on the same line.

I. 2024 girls each has her own doll. Consider all the ways to distribute a doll to each of the 2024 girls. For any $k \ge 0$, let $p(k)$ be the number of ways to distribute the dolls so that there are exactly $k$ girls received her own doll. Prove that
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{2024} k \times p(k) = 2024!.
$$

In the vertices of a cube, numbers $1,2, \ldots, 8$ are written in some order. It turned out that on three faces of the cube, the following condition is met: one of the numbers at the vertices is equal to the sum of the other three.

From the vertex with the number 6, three edges emanate. What three numbers can stand at their ends? The answers can be entered in any order. It is sufficient to provide one suitable example.

In a mathematical laboratory, there was a large plate which the staff decided to turn into an art object: they marked 20 points with a black marker, and then, using five colored markers, connected each pair of points with a line of one of these five colors. Prove that on this art object it is possible to erase all lines of some one color so that from any marked point it is still possible to get to any other by moving along the remaining lines.

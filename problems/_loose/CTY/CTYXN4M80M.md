Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be any triangle. If $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are the points of tangency of the incircle of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ with the sides, show that the following relation holds: $a \cdot \overrightarrow{A A^{\prime}}+b \cdot \overrightarrow{B B^{\prime}}+c \cdot \overrightarrow{C C^{\prime}}=\overrightarrow{0}$.

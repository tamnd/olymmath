Octagon from two squares. Let there be two concentric squares, the corresponding sides of which are parallel, and the areas are in the ratio $2: 1$. Show that if we draw segments through the vertices of the smaller square, perpendicular to the corresponding diagonals, then these segments together with the sides of the larger square form a regular octagon.

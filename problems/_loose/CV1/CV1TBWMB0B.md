Knowing that a human has fewer than 300,000 hairs and there are 3,000,000 Parisians, at least two Parisians have the same number of hairs. We can even specify that there are at least 10 Parisians having the same number of hairs. If there were 3,000,001 Parisians each having at most 300,000 hairs, we could assert that at least 11 of them have the same number of hairs.

Given a right triangle $A B C$ with the right angle at $C$. On the circumcircle $k$ of the triangle, there is a point $P$ on the arc $\widehat{A B}$ that does not contain $C$, and is different from $A$ and $B$.

Symmetric to $P$ with respect to the lines through $A$ and $C$ and through $B$ and $C$, the points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ lie, respectively.

a) Prove that $C$ lies on the line $g$ through $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$.

b) Prove that $g$ is the tangent to the circumcircle $k$ at point $C$ if and only if $C P \perp A B$.

Let $p(x)=x^{n}+a_{1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1} x+a_{n}$ and $g(x)=x^{n}+b_{1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+b_{n-1} x+b_{n}$ be two polynomials with complex coefficients. The roots of $g(x)$ are the squares of the roots of $p(x)$. If $a_{1}+a_{3}+a_{5}+\cdots$ and $a_{2}+a_{4}+a_{6}+\cdots$ are real numbers, prove that $b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}$ is also a real number.

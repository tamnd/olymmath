Anke, Bernd, and Claudia want to distribute 21 bottles of lemonade among themselves, with 7 full, 7 half-full, and 7 empty bottles. Each child should receive the same number of bottles and the same amount of lemonade, and nothing should be poured from one bottle to another. Furthermore, Anke should not receive fewer full bottles than Bernd, and Bernd should not receive fewer full bottles than Claudia.

a) Give two distributions that meet these conditions!

b) Prove that there can be no further distributions that meet these conditions!

Let the set $Q_{0}=\left\{\left.\frac{m}{n} \right\rvert m, n \in \mathbb{Z}, m\right.$ and $n$ are odd $\}$ and $G=Q_{0} \times \mathbb{Z}$. On $G$ we define the composition law $\left(q_{1}, k_{1}\right) *\left(q_{2}, k_{2}\right)=\left(q_{1} q_{2}, k_{1}+k_{2}\right), \forall q_{1}, q_{2} \in Q_{0}, \forall k_{1}, k_{2} \in \mathbb{Z}$. Show that the function $f: G \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}^{*}, f((q, k))=q \cdot 2^{k}$ is an isomorphism between the groups $\left(G,{ }^{*}\right)$ and $\left(\mathbb{Q}^{*}, \cdot\right)$, where "$\cdot$" is the multiplication operation of rational numbers.

Given a triangle $A B C$. Let $P, Q$ be the midpoints of sides $A B, A C$ respectively, and let $R$, $S$ be internal points on segment $B C$ such that $|B R|=|R S|=|S C|$. Denote by $T$ the intersection of lines $P R$ and $Q S$. Prove that $A B T C$ is a parallelogram.

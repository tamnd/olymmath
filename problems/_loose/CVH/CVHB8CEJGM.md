Given a triangle $ABC$ , let the tangent lines to the circumcircle of $\vartriangle ABC$ at points $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $T$ . Line $CT$ intersects the circumcircle for a second time at point $D$ . Let the projections of $D$ onto $AB$ , $BC$ , $AC$ be $M$ , $N$ , $P$ respectively. From $M$ draw a line perpendicular to $NP$ intersecting $BC$ at $E$ . If $EC=5$ , $EB= 11$ , and $\angle CEP = 120^o$ , compute the length of $CP$ .

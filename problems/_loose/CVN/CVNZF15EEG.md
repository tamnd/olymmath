Prove that the fixed points of three similar figures are their corresponding points.

The circle of similarity of triangle $ABC$ is called the circle of similarity of segment $AB$, segment $BC$, and segment $CA$ (or any three similar triangles constructed on these segments). The fixed points of the triangle are the fixed points of the three considered figures.

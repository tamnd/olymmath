The 54th question: Let $\mathrm{C}$ be a closed region bounded by a regular polygon. Prove that for any positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, there always exists a planar point set $S(n)$ such that any $\mathrm{n}$ points in $S(n)$ can be covered by $\mathrm{C}$, but $S(n)$ itself cannot be covered by $\mathrm{C}$.

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and $O$ the center of its circumcircle. The line $OA$ intersects the altitude $h_{b}$ at $P$ and the altitude $h_{c}$ at $Q$. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$. Prove that the center of the circumcircle of triangle $P Q H$ lies on the median of triangle $A B C$ passing through $A$.

Note: The altitude $h_{a}$ is the line perpendicular to $BC$ passing through $A$.

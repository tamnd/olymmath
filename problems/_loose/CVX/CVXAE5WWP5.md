In the plane, there are 3 parallel lines $e_{i}(i=1,2,3)$, and on each of them, there is a point $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$. Let $P$ be a given point in the plane. Determine the line $f$ passing through $P$ such that for the intersection points $B_{i}$ (where $i=1,2,3$) of $f$ with $e_{i}$, the following condition is satisfied: $\overrightarrow{A_{1} B_{1}}+\overrightarrow{A_{2} B_{2}}=\overrightarrow{A_{3} B_{3}}$.

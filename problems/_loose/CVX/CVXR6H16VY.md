Let $k$ be a positive integer, and the quadratic equation
$$
(k-1) x^{2}-p x+k=0
$$

has two positive integer roots. Find the value of $k^{k p}\left(p^{p}+k^{k}\right)$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Grade 2 Mathematics Competition, 1984)

Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, $M \in (AC)$ such that $\frac{AM}{AC} = \frac{2}{3}$, and $N$ is the reflection of $M$ over $BC$.

The line $NC$ intersects the line through $A$ parallel to $BC$ at $T$, and $TM$ intersects $BC$ at $O$ and $AB$ at $Q$.

a) Prove that $ABCT$ is a rhombus.

b) Show that $[BO]$ is a midline in triangle $ATQ$.

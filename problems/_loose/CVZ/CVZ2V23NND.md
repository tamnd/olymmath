On the board, there are $1000$ consecutive integers. In one move, you can partition the written numbers into pairs in any way and replace each pair of numbers with their sum and difference (it is not necessary to subtract the smaller from the larger; all replacements occur simultaneously). Prove that $1000$ consecutive integers will never appear on the board again.

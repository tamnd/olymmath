Consider the table filled with the following signs:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
+ & + & - & + \\
- & - & + & + \\
+ & + & + & + \\
+ & - & + & -
\end{array}
$$

We repeat several times the operation which consists of choosing a row or a column and changing all the signs to their opposites. Is it possible to reach a table consisting only of - signs?

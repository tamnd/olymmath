Given a cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $s 1$ be the circle passing through points $A$ and $B$ and tangent to the line $A C$, and $s 2$ be the circle passing through points $C$ and $D$ and tangent to $A C$. Prove that the lines $A C$, $B D$ and the second common internal tangent to the circles $s 1$ and $s 2$ pass through one point.

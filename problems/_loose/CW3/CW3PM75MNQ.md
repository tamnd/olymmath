Let $f(x)=x^3+7x^2+9x+10$. Which value of $p$ satisfies the statement
$$
f(a) \equiv f(b) \ (\text{mod } p) \Rightarrow a \equiv b \ (\text{mod } p)
$$
for every integer $a,b$?

$ \textbf{(A)}\ 5
\textbf{(B)}\ 7
\textbf{(C)}\ 11
\textbf{(D)}\ 13
\textbf{(E)}\ 17
$

$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2, \\
\frac{a_{n+2}}{a_{n}}=\frac{a_{n+1}^{2}+1}{a_{n}^{2}+1}(n=1,2, \cdots) .
\end{array}
$$

Find $\left[a_{2021}\right]$.

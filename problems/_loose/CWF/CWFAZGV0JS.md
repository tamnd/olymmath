A manager of a company has eight employees under him. One day, the manager convenes an employee meeting, with the following rules:
(1) Each employee meeting lasts 1 hour, and there are no breaks between meetings;
(2) Each employee meeting is attended by three employees called by the manager;
(3) Any two employees must have attended at least one meeting together;
(4) Each employee cannot leave the company from the time they attend their first meeting to the time they attend their last meeting.

For all meeting arrangements that meet the requirements, consider the employee who stays in the company the longest on that day. What is the minimum number of hours this employee stays in the company? Prove your conclusion.

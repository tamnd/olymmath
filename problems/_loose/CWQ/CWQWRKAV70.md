Rapunzel was born with a height of 40 cm, without any hair. Due to magic, Rapunzel grows 6 cm taller each year, and her hair grows by the same length as her height from the previous birthday. At 1 year old, her height is 46 cm, and her hair is 40 cm long; at 2 years old, her height is 52 cm, and her hair is 86 cm long; Rapunzel is unfortunately trapped in a 11-meter tall tower. On her birthday, standing at the top of the tower, her long hair just touches the grass at the bottom of the tower.

\underbrace{[x]-\left[\frac{1}{x}\right]}_{\in Z}=\{x\}-\left\{\frac{1}{x}\right\} \in(-1,1) (3 \text{p}) \Rightarrow[x]-\left[\frac{1}{x}\right]=0 (1 \text{p}) \Rightarrow\{x\}=\left\{\frac{1}{x}\right\} (1 \text{p})
$$

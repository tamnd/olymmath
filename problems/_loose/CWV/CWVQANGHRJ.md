In the given triangle $A B C$, inscribe a triangle $X Y Z$ such that the value

$$
m \cdot X Y+n \cdot Y Z+p \cdot Z X
$$

where $m, n, p$ are given positive numbers, is the smallest (i.e., so that the "weight" of the inscribed triangle $X Y Z$ in $A B C$ is the smallest possible, where the "specific weight" of the wire used to make the sides $X Y, Y Z$, and $Z X$ is respectively $m, n$, and $p$).[^6]$\mathbf{7 5 *} \cdot$ Find

a) inside the triangle $A B C$;

b) in the plane of this triangle

the point $X$, the sum of whose distances from the vertices of the triangle is the smallest.

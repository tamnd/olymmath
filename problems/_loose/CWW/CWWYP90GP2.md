Let $1 \leqslant a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{k} \leqslant n$, where $a_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant k)$ are all positive integers, and all sums of two numbers $a_{i}+a_{j}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant k)$ are distinct. Prove:
$$
k \leqslant \sqrt{2 n}+1 \text {. }
$$

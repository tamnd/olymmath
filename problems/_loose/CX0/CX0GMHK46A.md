Given a regular quadrilateral pyramid $S A B C D$ and a cone, the center of the base of which lies on the line $S O$ ($S O$ is the height of the pyramid). Point $E$ is the midpoint of edge $S D$, and point $F$ lies on edge $A D$, such that $A F=\frac{3}{2} F D$. The triangle, which is one of the axial sections of the cone, is positioned such that two of its vertices lie on the line $C D$, and the third vertex lies on the line $E F$. Find the volume of the cone if $A B=4, S O=3$.

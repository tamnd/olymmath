Let $n \geqslant 1$ be a positive integer. Prove:
$$
\mathrm{C}_{2 n}^{n} \mid[1,2, \cdots, 2 n] \text {, }
$$

where, $\left[a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right]$ denotes the least common multiple of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$.

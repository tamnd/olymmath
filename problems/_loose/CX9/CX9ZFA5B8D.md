Find all triples $(p,n,k)$ of positive integers, where $p$ is a Fermat's Prime, satisfying $$p^n + n = (n+1)^k$$.

*Observation: a Fermat's Prime is a prime number of the form $2^{\alpha} + 1$ , for $\alpha$ positive integer.*

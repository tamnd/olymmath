a) Prove that in every right-angled triangle for the side lengths $a, b, c$ and the heights $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$, the inequality

$$
3\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right) \geq 4\left(h_{a}^{2}+h_{b}^{2}+h_{c}^{2}\right)
$$

holds!

b) Investigate whether (1) also holds in every acute-angled triangle!

Are there a) right-angled, b) acute-angled triangles for which the equality sign holds in (1)?

The cells of a $2017 \times 100$ board (2017 rows, 100 columns) are painted in a checkerboard pattern. A grasshopper standing on the board attacks all cells in its row that have the same color as the cell it stands on, as well as all cells in its column that have the opposite color. (To attack a cell, the grasshopper can jump over other pieces.) What is the maximum number of non-attacking grasshoppers that can be placed on this board?

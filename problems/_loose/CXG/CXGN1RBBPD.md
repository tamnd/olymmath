In the country of Dolphinia, there are $n$ cities, each pair of which is connected by a highway, and movement along all highways is one-way. Prove that if $n \neq 2$ or 4, the directions of movement along the highways can be chosen such that from any city to any other city, one can travel with no more than one intermediate stop; if, however, $n=2$ or 4, such movement cannot be organized.

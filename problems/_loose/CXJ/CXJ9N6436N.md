Let $a, b, s$ be three given lengths. Peter is supposed to divide a segment of length $s$ in the ratio $a^{2}: b^{2}$. He gives the following construction:

(1) Construct a right triangle with $\overline{B C}=a, \overline{A C}=b$ and $\overline{\angle A C B}=90^{\circ}$

(2) Drop a perpendicular from $C$ to $A B$, and let its foot be $D$.

(3) At $B$, construct an angle whose measure is between $0^{\circ}$ and $180^{\circ}$ on $B A$. On the free leg of this angle, mark a segment of length $s$ from $B$, and let its other endpoint be $E$.

(4) The parallel to $E A$ through $D$ intersects $B E$ at a point, which is named $F$.

a) Carry out the described construction!

b) Prove:

If a segment $B E$ and a point $F$ are constructed according to Peter's description, then $F$ divides the segment $B E$ in the ratio $\overline{B F}: \overline{F E}=a^{2}: b^{2}$.

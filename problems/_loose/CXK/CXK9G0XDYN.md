Let the sides of a triangle satisfy $a \geq b \geq c$; then, as is known, the corresponding altitudes satisfy $m_{a} \leq m_{b} \leq m_{c}$. Prove that

$$
a+m_{a} \geq b+m_{b} \geq c+m_{c}
$$

Is it true that

$$
a+m_{a}^{*} \geq b+m_{b}^{*} \geq c+m_{c}^{*}
$$

where $m_{a}^{*}, m_{b}^{*}, m_{c}^{*}$ denote the lengths of the segments from the vertex to the orthocenter along the corresponding altitudes?

The sequence $a_1, a_2, \dots , a_n$ is a permutation of $1, 2,\dots, n$ . Each time, Alice swaps two neighbouring numbers in the sequence. Her goal is to make any three consecutive numbers in the sequence non-monotone. Find the smallest positive integer $k$ such that Alice can always achieve her goal in at most $k$ swaps.

* Remark. A monotonic sequence is either an increasing or decreasing sequence.

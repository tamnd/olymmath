As the endpoints $A B$ of a line segment slide along the rays of the angle $x O y=\omega$, the point $M$ on the line segment $A B$ then traces out an ellipse. Prove that the normal drawn at the point $M$ of the ellipse passes through the intersection of the perpendiculars drawn from points $A$ and $B$ to the lines $O x$ and $O y$, denoted as $I-\mathrm{n}$.

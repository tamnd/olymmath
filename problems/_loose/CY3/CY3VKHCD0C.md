Consider a convex polygon $P$ with $n$ sides and perimeter $P_0$ . Let the polygon $Q$ , whose vertices are the midpoints of the sides of $P$ , have perimeter $P_1$ . Prove that $P_1 \geq \frac{P_0}{2}$ .

A natural number is called *bright* if it is the sum of a perfect square and a perfect cube.
Prove that if $r$ and $s$ are any two positive integers, then
(a) there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that both $r+n$ and $s+n$ are *bright*,
(b) there exist infinitely many positive integers $m$ such that both rm and sm are *bright*.

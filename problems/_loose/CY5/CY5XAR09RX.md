After long-term observation, it is found that in a busy traffic period, the traffic flow $y$ (thousand vehicles/hour) of a certain road section and the average speed $v$ (km/h) of vehicles have the following functional relationship:
$$
y=\frac{920 v}{v^{2}+3 v+1600}(v>0) .
$$
(1) In this period, at what average speed $v$ is the traffic flow maximum? What is the maximum traffic flow? (Accurate to 0.1 thousand vehicles/hour)
(2) If the traffic flow needs to exceed 10 thousand vehicles/hour in this period, what should be the range of the average speed of vehicles?

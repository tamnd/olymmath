Let $a, b$ be non-negative integers, and satisfy $a b \geqslant c^{2}$, where $c$ is an integer. Prove that there exist a number $n$ and integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} ; y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}$, such that
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}=a, \sum_{i=1}^{n} y_{i}^{2}=b, \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} y_{i}=c .
$$

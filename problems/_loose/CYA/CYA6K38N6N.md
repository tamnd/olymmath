Write the first $2 n$ natural numbers in any order in a row, and number them sequentially as $1,2, \cdots, 2 n$, then find the sum of each number and its number, obtaining $2 n$ sum numbers. Divide each sum number by $2 n$, yielding $2 n$ quotients and remainders. Prove: at least two of the remainders are the same.

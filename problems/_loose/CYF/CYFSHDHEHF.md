$A$ and $B$ wrote two positive integers respectively in a black board. The product of the numbers written by $A$ was twice as the sum of the numbers written by $B$ and the product of the numbers written by $B$ was twice as the sum of the numbers written by $A$ and the sum of the numbers written by $A$ is more than or equal to that of $B$. Find all possible sum written by $B$.
Note: Four numbers written are not always distinct.

On a $2018 \times 2018$ grid, a fly and $k$ spiders are wandering. At each step, the fly moves to an adjacent cell (i.e., a cell that shares a side with its starting cell) or decides to stay in place, and similarly, each spider moves to an adjacent cell or decides to stay in place.

(a) For which values of $k$ are the spiders sure to catch the fly, regardless of the starting points of the $k+1$ creatures?

(b) Same question if we replace the $2018 \times 2018$ grid with a $2018 \times 2018 \times 2018$ block, and the cells with cubes, two cubes being adjacent when they share a face.

Let AL be the bisector of an acute-angled triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, and let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of this triangle. Denote by P the intersection point of the extension of the altitude BH of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ with the circle $\omega$. Prove that if $\angle \mathrm{BLA}=\angle \mathrm{BAC}$, then $\mathrm{BP}=\mathrm{CP}$.

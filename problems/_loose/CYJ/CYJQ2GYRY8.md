- The second from the left in the row said: "My nearest fellow tribesman in this row stands 2 meters away from me."
- The third said: "My nearest fellow tribesman in this row stands 1 meter away from me."
- The fourth said: "My nearest fellow tribesman in this row stands 3 meters away from me."

It is known that there are three knights and three liars in the row. On which positions are the liars guaranteed to stand?

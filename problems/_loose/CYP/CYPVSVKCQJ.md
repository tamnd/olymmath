Let $P$ be an arbitrary point on the base of a regular quadrilateral pyramid. The normal at point $P$ to the plane of the base intersects the planes of the lateral faces at points $M_{1}, M_{2}, M_{3}, M_{4}$. Prove that the sum

$$
P M_{1}+P M_{2}+P M_{3}+P M_{4}
$$

is four times the height of the pyramid.

In $\$ 3$ it is shown (using the relation $\left.x^{N}=\sum_{n=0}^{N} S_{N}^{n}(x)_{n}\right)$ that the exponential generating function

$$
E_{S_{\mathfrak{n}}}(x)=\sum_{N \geqslant 0} S_{N}^{n} \frac{x^{N}}{N!}
$$

of the sequence $S_{\cdot}^{n}=\left(S_{N}^{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 0}$ of Stirling numbers of the second kind is given by the formula

$$
E_{S_{0}^{n}}(x)=\frac{\left(e^{x}-1\right)^{n}}{n!}
$$

Prove this formula, based on property I.2.5(d).

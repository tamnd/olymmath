Given the system:

$$
\left\lvert \begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{x+y}+x & =a-1 \\
\frac{x}{x+y} & =a-2
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

where $a$ is a real parameter.

a) Solve the system if $a=0$.

b) Find all values of $a$, such that the system has an unique solution.

c) If $a \in(2,3)$ and $(x, y)$ is a solution of the system find all values of $a$ such that the expression $\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{x}$ takes its minimal value.

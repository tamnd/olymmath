At the national competition, students solved 4 problems. Each was rated with an integer number of points, at least 0 and at most 7 points. The competition was attended by 42 students. Exactly half of the participants achieved at least $50 \%$ of the points. To win an award, it was necessary to score at least 22 points, which was achieved by one-sixth of the participants.

The participants who did not receive an award collectively scored three times as many points as all the awarded participants combined. Prove that there are at least 6 participants who individually achieved at least $25 \%$ of the points but less than $50 \%$ of the points.

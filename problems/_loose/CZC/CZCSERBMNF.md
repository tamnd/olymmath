G67 (36-5, New Zealand) Let convex hexagon $A B C D E F$ satisfy $A B=B C=C D$, $D E=E F=F A$, $\angle B C D=\angle E F A=60^{\circ}$. Let $G$ and $H$ be two points inside the hexagon such that $\angle A G B=\angle D H E=120^{\circ}$. Prove that:
$$
A G+G B+G H+D H+H E \geqslant C F \text {. }
$$

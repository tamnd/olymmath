a) Let $a, b, c, d, \alpha \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|a|=|b| \neq 0,|c|=|d| \neq 0$. Prove that for $n \geq 1$, the roots of the equation $c(b x+a \alpha)^{n}-d(a x+b \bar{\alpha})^{n}=0$ are real numbers.

b) Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be a regular polygon inscribed in a circle with center $O$ and radius $R$. On the ray $\left[O A_{1}\right.$, a point $P$ is taken such that $A_{1} \in(O P)$. Prove that $\prod_{k=1}^{n} P A_{k}=P O^{n}-R^{n}$.

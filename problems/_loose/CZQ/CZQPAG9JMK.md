Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers, and $S_{k}$ be the sum of the products taken $k$ at a time from $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$. Prove
$$
S_{k} \cdot S_{n-k} \geqslant\left(C_{n}^{k}\right)^{2} a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}, k=1,2, \cdots, n-1 .
$$

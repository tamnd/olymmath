Two players play the following game. One calls out a digit, and the other inserts it at their discretion in place of one of the asterisks in the following difference:

Then the first one calls out another digit, the second one places it, the first one calls out a digit again, and so on until all asterisks are replaced by digits. The first one aims to make the difference as large as possible, while the second one aims to make it as small as possible. Prove that

a) the second one can place the digits in such a way that the resulting difference does not exceed 4000, regardless of which digits the first one called out;

b) the first one can call out digits in such a way that the difference is not less than 4000, regardless of where the second one places the digits.

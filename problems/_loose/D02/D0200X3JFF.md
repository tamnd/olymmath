Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. From point $A$, tangents $A M$ and $A N$ are drawn to these circles ($M$ and $N$ are points on the circles). Prove that:

a) $\angle A B N + \angle M A N = 180^{\circ}$

b) $B M / B N = (A M / A N)^{2}$.

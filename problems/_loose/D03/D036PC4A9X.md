Two sages, A and B, are appointed for a trial. In the morning, they will be brought to a room where six identical-looking pills will be laid out in a circle on the table, four of which are harmless, and two are poisonous. Then, sage A will be informed which pills are poisoned, but he will not be able to pass this information to B. The sages must take turns (starting with A) to eat a pill, until only the two poisonous ones remain. How can the sages agree in advance to successfully pass the trial?

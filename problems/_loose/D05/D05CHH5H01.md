Let $n=2^{k} h+1$ with $0 \leq h<2^{k}$ odd. Show the equivalence:

$n$ prime $\Longleftrightarrow$ there exists an integer $a$ such that $a^{\frac{n-1}{2}} \equiv-1 \bmod n$.

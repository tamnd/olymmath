In the Cartesian coordinate system, circle $C_{1}$ has center $C_{1}(-2,0)$ and radius 3, $O$ is the origin, and $A$ is (1,0). Prove that there exists a positive constant $c$ such that for any point $X$ outside $C_{1}$, $|O X| - 1 \geqslant c \cdot \min \left\{|A X|,|A X|^{2}\right\}$. Find the minimum value of $c$.

Suppose $P$ is an interior point of a triangle $A B C$ such that the ratios

$$
\frac{d(A, B C)}{d(P, B C)}, \frac{d(B, C A)}{d(P, C A)}, \frac{d(C, A B)}{d(P, A B)}
$$

are all equal. Find the common value of these ratios. [Here $d(X, Y Z)$ denotes the perpendicular distance from a point $X$ to the line $Y Z$.]

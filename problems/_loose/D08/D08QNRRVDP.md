If a spherical shell "coat" is uniformly wrapped around a sphere of radius $R$, and if the volume of the "coat" is equal to the volume of the sphere, the thickness of the spherical shell "coat" is
(A) $(\sqrt[3]{3}-1) R$.
(B) $\sqrt[3]{3} R$.
(C) $\sqrt[3]{2} R$.
(D) $(\sqrt[3]{2}-1) R$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

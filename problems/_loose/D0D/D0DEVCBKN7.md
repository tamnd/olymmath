It is assumed that three lines pass through a point $C$. It is also assumed that a fourth line does not pass through $C$ and intersects the three other lines at points $A, B, D$, where $B$ lies between $A$ and $D$. On the line through $A$ and $C$, there is a point $E$ such that $C$ lies between $A$ and $E$. Furthermore, it is assumed that the angles $\angle E C D$ and $\angle A B C$ are equal in size.

a) Draw four lines and the points $A, B, C, D, E$ such that these conditions are satisfied!

b) Prove that under these conditions, the angles $\angle B C D$ and $\angle B A C$ must always be equal in size!

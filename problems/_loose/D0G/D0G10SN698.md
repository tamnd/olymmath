Let $A$ and $B$ be natural numbers, and let $S$ be the set of all points with integer coordinates inside the rectangle with vertices $(0,0), (A, 0), (A, B)$, and $(0, B)$. The points with integer coordinates on the boundary of this rectangle are colored red, and inside the set $S$ they are colored with one of three colors: blue, green, or red.

A unit square whose all four vertices are in $S$ is called special if exactly one pair of its adjacent vertices is the same color. Prove that the number of special unit squares is even.

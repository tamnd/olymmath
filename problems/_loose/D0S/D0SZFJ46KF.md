Find all triples $(a, b, c)$ of natural numbers such that the sets $$ \{ gcd (a, b), gcd(b, c), gcd(c, a), lcm (a, b), lcm (b, c), lcm (c, a)\} $$ and $$ \{2, 3, 5, 30, 60\} $$ are the same.

Remark: For example, the sets $\{1, 2013\}$ and $\{1, 1, 2013\}$ are equal.

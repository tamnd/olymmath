If we deposit 5/6 of our savings in bank $A$ and the remainder in bank $B$, our savings will grow to 67000 Kč after one year and to 74900 Kč after two years. However, if we deposit 5/6 of our savings in bank $B$ and the remainder in bank $A$, our savings will grow to 71000 Kč after one year. To what amount would our savings increase after two years in the latter case?

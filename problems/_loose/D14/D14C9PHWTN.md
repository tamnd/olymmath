Let point $I$ be the midpoint of the base $BC$ of isosceles $\triangle ABC$. Point $F$ is the point on the arc inside $\triangle ABC$ of the circle passing through points $A$, $I$, and $D$. The circle passing through points $B$, $D$, and $F$ satisfies:
$$
(` I) \cdot E F + I F \cdot A E = I B D \cdot A F .
$$
(Leng Gangsong, Xiao Zhengang)

Let $p$ be a prime. How many different (infinite) sequences $\left(a_k\right)_{k\ge0}$ exist such that for every positive integer $n$ $$\frac{a_0}{a_1}+\frac{a_0}{a_2}+\cdots+\frac{a_0}{a_n}+\frac{p}{a_{n+1}}=1?$$

Can we wind an inextensible tape around an infinite cone to make infinitely many turns around its axis? The tape cannot be wound around the apex of the cone, nor can it be cut or twisted. If necessary, it can be assumed that the tape is infinite, and the angle between the axis and the generatrix of the cone is sufficiently small.

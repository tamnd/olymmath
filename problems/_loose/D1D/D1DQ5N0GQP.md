In the city of dwarves, an epidemic broke out, and some of them caught a cold and became infected with the flu. Then the epidemic spread through infection: if a healthy dwarf visited their sick friends, they would also become sick the next day. Dwarves are sick for one day, and then they are immune for one day (on that day, they cannot be infected). Every healthy dwarf visits their sick friends daily. Prove that if any dwarf was vaccinated (i.e., immune) on the first day of the epidemic, then the epidemic can last indefinitely; otherwise, it will definitely end.

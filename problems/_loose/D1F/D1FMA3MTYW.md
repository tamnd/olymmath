Let $k$ and $k^{\prime}$ be two concentric circles with center $O$. Let the circle $k^{\prime}$ be larger than the circle $k$. A straight line through $O$ intersects $k$ in $A$ and $k^{\prime}$ in $B$ so that $O$ lies between $A$ and $B$. Another straight line through $O$ intersects $k$ in $E$ and $k^{\prime}$ in $F$, so that $E$ lies between $O$ and $F$. Show that the circumcircle of $O A E$, the circle with diameter $A B$ and the circle with diameter $E F$ intersect at a point.

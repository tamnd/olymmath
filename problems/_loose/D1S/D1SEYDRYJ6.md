Given a square $A B C D$ with side length $a$.

A line parallel to $A B$ intersects the sides $B C$ and $A D$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. A line parallel to $B C$ intersects $A B$ and $E F$ at points $G$ and $H$, respectively, and a line parallel to $A B$ intersects the segments $B E$ and $G H$ at points $J$ and $K$, respectively.

a) Determine the perimeter of the rectangle $K J E H$ in terms of $a$ under the condition that the rectangles $A G H F, G B J K, K J E H$, and $F E C D$ are equal in area!

b) Determine the area of the rectangle $K J E H$ in terms of $a$ under the condition that the rectangles $A G H F, G B J K, K J E H$, and $F E C D$ are equal in perimeter!

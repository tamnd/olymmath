Let $A B C$ be a triangle such that $3|B C|=|A B|+|C A|$. Let $T$ be a point on the side $\overline{A C}$ such that $4|A T|=|A C|$, and let $K$ and $L$ be points on the sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{C A}$, respectively, such that $K L \| B C$ and the line $K L$ is tangent to the incircle of triangle $A B C$.

In what ratio does the segment $\overline{B T}$ divide the segment $\overline{K L}$?

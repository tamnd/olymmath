On a circular glade with radius $R$, three circular pines of the same diameter grow. The centers of their trunks are located at a distance of $\frac{R}{2}$ from the center of the glade at the vertices of an equilateral triangle. Two people, starting simultaneously from diametrically opposite points of the glade, walk around the edge of the glade at the same speed and in the same direction, and never see each other. Will three people see each other if they also walk around the glade, starting from points located at the vertices of an equilateral triangle inscribed in the glade?

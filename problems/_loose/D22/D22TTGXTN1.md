Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. The rays $A B$ and $C D$ intersect at point $P$, and the rays $B C$ and $A D$ intersect at point $Q$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is tangential if and only if one of the following conditions is satisfied: $A B+C D=B C+A D, A P+C Q=A Q+C P$ or $B P+B Q=D P+D Q$.

$a, b, c$ and $d$ are positive numbers. Prove that among the inequalities

$$
\begin{gathered}
a+b<c+d \\
(a+b)(c+d)<a b+c d \\
(a+b) c d<a b(c+d)
\end{gathered}
$$

there is at least one that is false.

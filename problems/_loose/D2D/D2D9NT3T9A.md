Given a positive integer $k>2$ and a second-order arithmetic sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}: 1, k, 3 k-3, 6 k-8, \cdots$; and the sequence formed by Fermat numbers: $3=2^{2^{2}}+1, 5=2^{2^{2}}+1, 17=2^{2^{2}}+1$, $\cdots$. Find all positive integers $k$ such that the two sequences have a common term.

Each subsequent day, each party formed on the previous day, consisting of at least two members, split into two factions. Factions consisting of at least two people immediately declared themselves parties. No other parties were formed. When the process ended, each resident of Chernomorsk paid a membership fee of 1 ruble to each party they belonged to. What is the maximum amount the total fees could have been?

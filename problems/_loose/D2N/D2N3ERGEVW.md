Choose $k$ and $i$ such that $n=(r-1) k+i$ and $0 \leqslant i<r-1$. First handle the case $i=0$, then prove that in the general case $t_{r-1}(n)=\frac{1}{2} \frac{r-2}{r-1}\left(n^{2}-i^{2}\right)+\binom{i}{2}$.

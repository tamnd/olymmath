Consider the parallelogram $A B C D$. A line that does not contain point $A$ intersects lines $A B, A C$, and $A D$ at points $B_{1}, C_{1}$, and $D_{1}$, respectively. Show that if $\overrightarrow{A B_{1}}=\lambda_{1} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}, \overrightarrow{A D_{1}}=\lambda_{2} \cdot \overrightarrow{A D}$, and $\overrightarrow{A C_{1}}=\lambda_{3} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}$, then $\frac{1}{\lambda_{1}}+\frac{1}{\lambda_{2}}=\frac{1}{\lambda_{3}}$

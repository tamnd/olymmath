To travel a straight path of 10 meters in length, a flea uses the following strategy: each day, it covers half of the remaining distance. Thus, it travels 5 meters on the first day, 2.5 meters on the second, and so on (the size of the flea can be disregarded).

(a) How many meters will it have traveled by the end of the seventh day? And by the tenth?

(b) From which day will the flea be less than $0.001 \mathrm{~m}$ from the end of the path?

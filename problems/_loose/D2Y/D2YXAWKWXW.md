Positive real numbers $a, b, c$ and non-negative real numbers $x, y$ satisfy the condition
$$
a^{6}+b^{6}+c^{6}=3 .(x+1)^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 2 .
$$

Find the minimum value of $I=\frac{1}{2 a^{3} x+b^{3} y^{2}}+\frac{1}{2 b^{3} x+c^{3} y^{2}}+\frac{1}{2 c^{3} x+a^{3} y^{2}}$.

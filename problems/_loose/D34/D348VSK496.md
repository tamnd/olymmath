Let's prove that if the natural numbers $a_{1}=b_{1}-1, b_{1}, c_{1}=b_{1}+1$ are the side lengths of a triangle, and the area of this triangle is also a natural number (with the area unit being the area of a square with a side length of one unit, as is customary), then the same is true for the triangle whose middle side is $b_{2}=b_{1}^{2}-2$, and the other two sides are $1-1$ unit shorter and longer, respectively. - It is known that the triples $3,4,5$ and $51,52,53$ are such. Starting from the first, write down two more such triples, then calculate the angles of these four triangles.

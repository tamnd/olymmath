In a certain city, each road connects two intersections and is designated as a one-way street. The municipal authorities are holding a design competition for the placement of gas stations, requiring that from each intersection, one can reach at least one gas station by following the designated direction, but from any gas station, it is not possible to reach any other gas station by following the designated direction. Prove that in all the submitted designs, the same number of gas stations are planned.

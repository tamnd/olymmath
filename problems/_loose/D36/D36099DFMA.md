Around a circle with radius $r$, a trapezoid $A B C D$ is circumscribed. If $M, N, P$ and $Q$ are the points of tangency of the sides $A B, B C, C D$ and $D A$ respectively, then

$$
\overline{A M} \cdot \overline{B N} \cdot \overline{C P} \cdot \overline{D Q}=r^{4}
$$

Prove!

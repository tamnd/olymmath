Given a line $l$, a circle, and a point $M$ lying on the circle and not on the line $l$. Let $P_{\mathrm{M}}$ be the projection of the line $l$ onto the given circle from the point $M$ (a point $X$ on the line is mapped to the other point of intersection of the line XM with the circle, different from $M$), $R$ - a motion of the plane that preserves the given circle (i.e., a rotation of the plane around the center of the circle or a reflection relative to a diameter). Prove that the composition $P_{\mathrm{M}}{ }^{-1}{ }^{\circ} R_{\circ} P_{\mathrm{M}}$ is a projective transformation.

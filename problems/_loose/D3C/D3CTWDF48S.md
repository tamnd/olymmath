Solve the riddle

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \text { } \\
& \mathrm{H} \mathrm{H} \\
& +4 \mathrm{H} 4 \mathrm{H} \\
& 4 \mathrm{HH}
\end{aligned}
$$

All digits denoted by the letter H are odd (not necessarily equal); all digits denoted by the letter Ч are even (also not necessarily equal).

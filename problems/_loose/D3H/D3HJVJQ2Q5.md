(24th All-Soviet Olympiad) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive numbers, and $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=1$. Prove that: $\frac{a_{1}^{2}}{a_{1}+a_{2}}+\frac{a_{2}^{2}}{a_{2}+a_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n-1}^{2}}{a_{n-1}+a_{n}}+\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{a_{n}+a_{1}} \geqslant \frac{1}{2}$.

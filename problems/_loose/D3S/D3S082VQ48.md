In the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, $a_{1}=-1, a_{2}=\frac{1}{2}, a_{n}=\frac{n-1}{n} a_{n-2}(n \geqslant 3)$, then $a_{n}=(-1)^{n} \frac{(n-1)!!}{n!!}(n \geqslant 3)$. Here, $n!!=n(n-2)(n-4) \cdots 3 \cdot 1$, when $n$ is odd; or $n!!=n(n-2)(n-4) \cdots 4 \cdot 2$, when $n$ is even.

Given the following definition: let $\left(u_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a real sequence. We call a subsequence or extracted sequence of the sequence $\left(u_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$, any sequence $\left(v_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ defined by: $v_{n}=u_{\varphi(n)}$ where $\varphi$ is a strictly increasing function from $\mathbb{N}$ to $\mathbb{N}$. Show that if a sequence $\left(u_{n}\right)$ converges to a real number $\ell$ then any subsequence of $\left(u_{n}\right)$ converges to $\ell$.

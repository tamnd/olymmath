A compact cube has each edge 1 dm. On each of its faces, we draw a smaller square whose center coincides with the center of the face and whose sides are parallel to the edges of the face. The side of the drawn square is $a$ on the base and top faces, $b$ on the front and back faces, and $c$ on the left and right faces, where $c<b<a<1$. We sequentially remove the square prisms defined by the pairs of squares with sides $c$, $b$, and $a$ (in the second and third cases, removing the material still present in the prisms' volumes). Determine the values of $c$, $b$, and $a$ so that the volumes of the sequentially removed parts and the remaining body are equal.

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers. Show that

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{a} \frac{1}{2^{b+i}}\binom{b+i}{i}+\sum_{i=0}^{b} \frac{1}{2^{a+i}}\binom{a+i}{i}=2
$$

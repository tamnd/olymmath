on $AB$ such that $CB = CE$ and $F$ is on $AD$ such that $CF = CD$. If the circumcenter of $CEF$ is
$O_1$ and the circumcenter of $ABD$ is $O_2$. Prove that $C,O_1,O_2$ are collinear.

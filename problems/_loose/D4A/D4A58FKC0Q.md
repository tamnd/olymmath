The operation $a \nabla b$ is defined by $a \nabla b=\frac{a+b}{a-b}$ for all integers $a$ and $b$ with $a \neq b$. For example, $2 \nabla 3=\frac{2+3}{2-3}=-5$. If $3 \nabla b=-4$, what is the value of $b$ ?
(A) 5
(B) -7
(C) 7
(D) -5
(E) 3

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with all angles greater than $45^{\circ}$ and less than $90^{\circ}$.

(a) Prove that three squares can be placed inside $ABC$ with the following properties:

- the three squares all have the same side length;
- the three squares have a common vertex $K$ inside the triangle;
- any two squares do not have any points in common other than $K$;
- each of the squares has two opposite vertices on the perimeter of $ABC$ and the rest is entirely inside the triangle $ABC$.

(b) Let $P$ be the center of the square with side $AB$ and external to $ABC$. Let $r_{C}$ be the symmetric line of the line passing through $C$ and $K$ with respect to the bisector of $A \widehat{C} B$ emanating from $C$. Prove that $P$ lies on $r_{C}$.

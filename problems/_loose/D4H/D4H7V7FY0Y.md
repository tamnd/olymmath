Let $\xi$ be a Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^{d}$ with the identity matrix as its covariance matrix. Let also the matrix $B=\left(b_{i j}\right)_{i, j=1}^{d}$ be symmetric and positive definite ($B>0$), i.e., such that $z^{\top} B z>0$ for all non-zero vectors $z \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$. For which $d \geqslant 1$ does $\mathrm{E}\left(\xi^{\top} B \xi\right)^{-1}$ exist?

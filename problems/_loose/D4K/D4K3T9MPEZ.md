Three gardeners invited a ploughman to plough their plots. The ploughman charged the same price per square meter for all of them. In total, the gardeners paid 570 Kč.

Two of the plots were square, one was rectangular. The area of the rectangular plot was 6 ares and one of its sides measured $20 \mathrm{~m}$. The side length of one of the square plots was the same as the length of the rectangular plot, and the side length of the other square plot was the same as the width of the rectangular plot.

How much did each of the gardeners pay for the ploughing?

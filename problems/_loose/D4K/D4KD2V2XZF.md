The International Mathematical Olympiad Chief Committee has $n$ countries participating, with each country being represented by a team leader and a deputy leader. Before the meeting, the participants shake hands with each other, but the team leader does not shake hands with their own deputy leader. After the meeting, the team leader of the host country asks the participants about the number of handshakes, and everyone gives a different answer. The deputy leader of the host country shakes hands $\frac{m^{2}+n^{2}}{50}-n+49$ times. Find the values of the real numbers $m$ and $n$.

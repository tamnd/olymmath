In the plane, let there be two right angles with the legs $h_{1}$ and $h_{2}$ respectively $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, such that no leg of one angle is parallel to any leg of the other angle.

Show that with a parallel ruler (an instrument that, in addition to an ordinary ruler, also allows the parallel translation of lines), for any point $P$ and any line $g$, the line perpendicular to $g$ through $P$ can be constructed!

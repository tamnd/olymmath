If you place a cube on a table so that it does not protrude over the tabletop on any side, exactly five of its six faces are visible.

Similarly, a smaller cube can be placed on a larger one so that exactly five of the smaller cube's six faces are visible, while the sixth lies completely on the larger cube, without protruding over it on any side.

In this way, three cubes with edge lengths $a_{1}=20 \mathrm{~cm}, a_{2}=10 \mathrm{~cm}, a_{3}=4 \mathrm{~cm}$ are to be stacked on top of each other in size order, with the largest cube at the bottom on the tabletop. What is the sum of the areas of all visible face parts of the three cubes?

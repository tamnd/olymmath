Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle, $\Gamma$ its circumcircle, $K$ the foot of the angle bisector from vertex $A$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the arc $B C$ that contains $A$. Let $A^{\prime}$ be the second intersection of $M K$ with $\Gamma$, and let $t, t^{\prime}$ be the tangents to $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $A^{\prime}$, respectively. Let $r$ (resp. $r^{\prime}$) be the perpendicular to $A K$ (resp. $A^{\prime} K$) passing through $A$ (resp. $A^{\prime}$). Let $T = t \cap t^{\prime}$ and $R = r \cap r^{\prime}$. Prove that $T, R$ and $K$ are collinear.

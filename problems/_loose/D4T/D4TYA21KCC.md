In a right-angled triangle $ABC$, with the right angle at vertex $B$, the angle $\alpha$ at vertex $A$ and the altitude from $B$ to the hypotenuse $BA_1 = h_1$ are given. From point $A_1$, a perpendicular $A_1 B_1$ is drawn to $BC$, from point $B_1$ a perpendicular $B_1 A_2$ is drawn to $AC$, from point $A_2$ a perpendicular $A_2 B_2$ is drawn to $BC$, and so on. Then, in each of the triangles $ABA_1$, $BA_1B_1$, $A_1B_1A_2$, $B_1A_2B_2$, etc., an inscribed circle is drawn.

a) Determine the sum of the areas of all the inscribed circles.

b) Determine the angle $\alpha$ such that this sum is maximized.

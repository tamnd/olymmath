Let $\xi, \xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be a sequence of random variables.

(a) Show that

$$
\forall \varepsilon>0: \mathrm{P}\left(\left|\xi_{n}-\xi\right| \geqslant \varepsilon \text { a.s. }\right)=0 \Leftrightarrow \xi_{n} \rightarrow \xi \text { a.s. }
$$

(b) Prove the following implication:

$$
\left(\exists\left(n_{k}\right): \xi_{n_{k}}=\xi, \max _{n_{k-1}<n \leqslant n_{k}}\left|\xi_{n}-\xi_{n_{k-1}}\right| \rightarrow 0, k \rightarrow \infty\right) \Rightarrow \xi_{n} \rightarrow \xi
$$

where the convergence of random variables is taken to be almost sure convergence.

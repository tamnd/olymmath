At the entrance and exit of a library, there is a board each. Every person entering or leaving must write on the corresponding board how many people they found or left in the library.

Prove that over the course of a full day, the same numbers will appear on both boards, possibly in a different order (assuming that only one person arrives or leaves at a time).

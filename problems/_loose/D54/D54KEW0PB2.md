Prove the following theorem!

If from a vertex of a triangle $\triangle ABC$ a perpendicular is dropped to the opposite side or its extension, and the foot of the perpendicular is connected to the midpoints of the other two sides, then the sum of the lengths of these connecting segments is equal to half the sum of the lengths of the two sides.

There are four colors and an infinite number of small squares with a side length of 1. Color the edges of the small squares so that the colors of each square's sides are all different, and stick together the edges of the same color. For what numbers $m$ and $n$ can these small squares be stuck together to form an $m \times n$ rectangular block, with each side of the rectangle being a single color, and the colors of the four sides being all different?

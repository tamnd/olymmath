In a $2^{n} \times n$ table, all possible rows of length $n$ consisting of the numbers 1 and -1 were written down. Then some of the numbers were replaced by zeros. Prove that it is possible to select several rows such that their sum is a row of zeros. (The sum of rows is a row whose elements are the sums of the corresponding elements of the addends.)

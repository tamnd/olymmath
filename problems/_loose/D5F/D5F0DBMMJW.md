Given $k \ge 1,$ let $p_k$ denote the $k$ -th smallest prime number. If $N$ is the number of ordered $4$ -tuples $(a,b,c,d)$ of positive integers satisfying $abcd=\prod_{k=1}^{2023} p_k$ with $a<b$ and $c<d,$ find $N \pmod{1000}.$

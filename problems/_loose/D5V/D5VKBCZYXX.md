Let $ABC$ be a scalene triangle and $D$ be the feet of altitude from $A$ to $BC$. Let $I_1$, $I_2$ be incenters of triangles $ABD$ and $ACD$ respectively, and let $H_1$, $H_2$ be orthocenters of triangles $ABI_1$ and $ACI_2$ respectively. The circles $(AI_1H_1)$ and $(AI_2H_2)$ meet again at $X$. The lines $AH_1$ and $XI_1$ meet at $Y$, and the lines $AH_2$ and $XI_2$ meet at $Z$.

Suppose the external common tangents of circles $(BI_1H_1)$ and $(CI_2H_2)$ meet at $U$. Prove that $UY=UZ$.

Pills. On February 28, the doctor prescribed Pills for Absentmindedness to the Absent-Minded Mathematician, which he had to take once a day after lunch. The Mathematician bought two bottles, each containing 10 pills. Starting from March 1, the Mathematician takes one of the bottles to work with him each day (choosing it randomly), and after lunch, he takes a pill from the bottle in his pocket.

a) (9th grade. 4 points). Find the probability that on March 14, the Mathematician will first discover that the bottle in his pocket is empty.

b) (9th grade. 4 points). Find the expected number of pills the Mathematician will have taken by the time he discovers that the bottle is empty.

We consider "words" written with the letters $x, y, z$ and $t$. We allow the following three transformations: $x y \mapsto y y x, x t \mapsto t t x$ and $y t \mapsto t y$. Are the following words equivalent?

(i) $x x y y$ and xyyyyx,

(ii) $x y t x$ and $t x y t$,

(iii) $x y$ and $x t$.

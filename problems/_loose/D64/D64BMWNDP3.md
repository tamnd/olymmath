Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Let $E$ be the midpoint of the diagonal $AC$. Let the normal from $D$ to the Euler line of $\triangle ABC$ and the normal from $B$ to the Euler line of $\triangle ADC$ intersect at point $F$. Let $G$ be a point on segment $EF$ such that $GF = 2GE$. Prove: $GB = GD$.

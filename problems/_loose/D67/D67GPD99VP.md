From a point inside a convex $n$-gon, rays are drawn perpendicular to its sides and intersecting the sides (or their extensions). On these rays, vectors $\mathbf{a}_{1}, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_{n}$ are laid out, the lengths of which are equal to the lengths of the corresponding sides. Prove that $\mathbf{a}_{1}+\ldots+\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{n}}=0$.

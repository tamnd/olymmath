The parabola $y=a x^{2}-b x+b$ intersects the line $y=a^{2} x$ (where $a>0$) at points $P$ and $Q$. The absolute value of the difference in the x-coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ is 1. Let $d$ be the maximum distance from a point on the parabolic arc $PQ$ to the line. (I) Express $d$ in terms of $a$; (II) For what values of $a$ and $b$ is $d$ maximized?
(1991 Japanese College Entrance Examination)

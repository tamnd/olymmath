Suppose that $a$ is real number. Sequence $a_1,a_2,a_3,....$ satisfies $$ a_1=a, a_{n+1} =
\begin{cases}
a_n - \frac{1}{a_n}, & a_n\ne 0
0, & a_n=0
\end{cases}
(n=1,2,3,..) $$ Find all possible values of $a$ such that $|a_n|<1$ for all positive integer $n$ .

Let's call the distance between numbers the absolute value of their difference. It is known that the sum of the distances from thirty-three consecutive natural numbers to some number $a$ is 3168, and the sum of the distances from these same thirty-three numbers to some number $b$ is 924. Find all possible values of $a$, given that $a+b=120$.

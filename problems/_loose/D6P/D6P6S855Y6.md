Let $k, n \geq 3$ be two odd integers. Suppose there exists an integer $a \geq 1$ such that $k$ divides $2^{a}+1$ and $n$ divides $2^{a}-1$. Show that there does not exist an integer $b \geq 1$ such that $k$ divides $2^{b}-1$ and $n$ divides $2^{b}+1$.

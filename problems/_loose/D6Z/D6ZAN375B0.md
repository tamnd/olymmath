Let $n$ be an even integer, $n \geq 4$, and let $G$ be a subgroup of order $n$ of the multiplicative group of invertible matrices $\operatorname{in} \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{C})$. Show that $G$ has a subgroup $H$, such that $\left\{I_{2}\right\} \subsetneq H \subsetneq G$ and $X Y X^{-1} \in H$, for any $X \in G$ and any $Y \in H$.

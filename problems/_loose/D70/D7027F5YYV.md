Let $B_{1}, \ldots, B_{n}$ be boxes containing respectively $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ balls. Suppose $a_{1} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant a_{n}$, and let $b_{k}$ be the number of boxes containing at least $k$ balls. Show that:

1. $a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+\ldots$
2. $a_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}=b_{1}+3 b_{2}+5 b_{3}+\ldots$

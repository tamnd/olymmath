Let's prove that for every triple of real numbers $a, b, c$, there exists a number $x$ with an absolute value not greater than 1, such that

$$
\left|x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c\right| \geqq 1 / 4
$$

Can a number larger than 1/4 be used in the inequality?

Prove by induction the Binet's formula:

$$
F_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{\varphi^{n}-\hat{\varphi}^{n}}{\sqrt{5}}
$$

where $\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ - the "golden ratio" or number of Phidias, and $\widehat{\varphi}=\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}$ ("phi with a hat") - conjugate to it.

The numerical sequence $\{x_n\}$ satisfies the condition $$ x_{n+1}=|x_n|-x_{n-1} $$ for all $n > 1$ . Prove that the sequence is periodic with period $9$ , i.e. for any $n > 1$ we have $x_n = x_{n+9}$ .

(M Kontsevich, Moscow)

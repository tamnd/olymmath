Artur and Dinah are going to play the crazy even or odd game. Dinah chooses "even" and Artur chooses "odd." Then, each of them writes a positive integer on a piece of paper without the other seeing. Emílio collects the two sheets, multiplies the numbers, and declares Dinah the winner if the result is even and Artur the winner if it is odd.

a) How should Dinah proceed to always win the game?

Emílio suggests a modification to the game. First, he asks Artur and Dinah to write only numbers that are not divisible by three. He collects the sheets, multiplies the two numbers, divides the result by three, and declares Dinah the winner if the remainder of the division is 1 and Artur the winner if the remainder is 2.

b) Show that Dinah can no longer have a winning strategy.

c) Show that Artur and Dinah have the same probability of winning.

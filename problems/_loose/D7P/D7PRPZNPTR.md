Construct the triangle $A B C$ with the condition that the point $A_{1}$ is the vertex of the equilateral triangle constructed outwardly on the side $B C$, similarly, the points $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ are the vertices of the equilateral triangles constructed outwardly on the sides $C A$ and $A B$.

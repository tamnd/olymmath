Let $a, b, c$ and $a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime}$ be the lengths of the sides of triangles $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}, S$ and $S^{\prime}$ be their areas. Prove that

$$
a^{2}\left(-a^{\prime 2}+b^{\prime 2}+c^{\prime 2}\right)+b^{2}\left(a^{\prime 2}-b^{\prime 2}+c^{\prime 2}\right)+c^{2}\left(a^{\prime 2}+b^{\prime 2}-c^{\prime 2}\right) \geq 16 S S^{\prime}
$$

with equality if and only if the triangles are similar (Pido).

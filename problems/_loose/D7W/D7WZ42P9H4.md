Let $ABC$ be a triangle with side lengths $a, b$, and $c$, and let $P$ be a point in its interior. Let the line $AP$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $BCP$ again at point $A'$, and let $B'$ and $C'$ be defined analogously. Prove that for the perimeter $O$ of the hexagon $AB'C A' B C'$, we have

$$
O \geq 2(\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{bc}+\sqrt{ca})
$$

Determine all pairs $(a, b)$ of real numbers for which the quadratic trinomials $P(x)=x^{2}+a x+b$ and $Q(x)=x^{2}+b x+a$ have the following property: each of the equations

$$
a P(x)+b Q(x)=0 \text{and} a Q(x)+b P(x)=0
$$

is a quadratic equation with a double root.

(Jaroslav Švrček)

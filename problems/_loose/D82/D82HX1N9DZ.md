Let $a$ be a positive real number different from 1. Furthermore, let $f$ be the function defined for all real numbers $x$ by

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{x}-a^{-x}\right)
$$

Prove that $f$ has an inverse function $g$ defined for all real numbers, and determine this function $g$!

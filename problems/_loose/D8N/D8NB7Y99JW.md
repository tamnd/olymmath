Consider a sequence of polynomials such that $P_0(x)=2,P_1(x)=x$ and for all $n\ge1$ $$P_{n+1}(x)+P_{n-1}(x)=xP_n(x).$$
a) Determine the polynomial $$Q_n(x)=P^2_n(x)-xP_n(x)P_{n-1}(x)+P^2_{n-1}(x)$$ for $n=1972.$ b) Express the polynomial $$\bigl(P_{n+1}(x)-P_{n-1}(x)\bigr)^2$$ in terms of $P_n(x),Q_n(x).$

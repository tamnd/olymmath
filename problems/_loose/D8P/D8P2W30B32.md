For example, the rules of a "level-passing game" stipulate: on the $n$-th level, a die must be rolled $n$ times. If the sum of the points from these $n$ rolls is greater than $2^{n}$, the player passes the level. Questions:
( I ) What is the maximum number of levels a person can pass in this game?
( II ) What is the probability that he can pass the first three levels consecutively?
(2004 National High School Mathematics Competition)
(Note: A die is a uniform cube with points numbered $1,2,3,4,5,6$ on its faces. The number of points on the face that lands up is the result of the roll.)

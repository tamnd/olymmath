Zeros and ones are placed in each square of a rectangular board. Such a board is said to be *varied* if each row contains at least one $0$ and at least two $1$ s. Given n $\geq 3,$ find all integers $k>1$ with the following property:

The columns of each varied board of $k$ rows and n columns can be permuted so that in each row of the new board the $1$ s do not form a block (that is, there are at least two $1$ s that are separated by one or more $0$ s).

If the three sides of a triangle $a, b, c$ are integers, and the height on one side is exactly the sum of the heights on the other two sides, then such a triangle is called a "Linglong Triangle". Prove:
(1) Linglong Triangles exist;
(2) In a Linglong Triangle, $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}$ is a perfect square.

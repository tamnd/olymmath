Let $x, y, z ; p, q$, all be positive numbers, and $p+q+r=1$. Try to prove: when $n \geq 1$, we have:
$$
p x^{n}+q y^{n}+r z^{n}>x^{p} y^{q} z^{r}\left(p x^{n-1}+q y^{n-1}+r z^{n-1}\right) .
$$

Let $x_{i} \geqslant 0(i=1,2, \cdots, n-1)$, and $\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \sqrt[5]{x_{k}^{6}}=\frac{2}{n(n-1)}$. Denote $\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} x_{k}=S, x_{0}=0$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(\prod_{l=1}^{k}\left(S-\sum_{i=0}^{l-1} x_{i}\right)\right)^{\frac{2}{k}}<\frac{\mathrm{e}}{\mathrm{C}_{n}^{2}} .
$$

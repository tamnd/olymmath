Frida bought herself a pair of pants that were $105 \mathrm{~cm}$ long. Since they were too long for her, she had a tailor shorten them by $5 \mathrm{~cm}$. After washing the pants and taking them out of the washing machine, she noticed that they were $2 \mathrm{~cm}$ too short. Of course, Frida had overlooked the attached sheet about shrinkage along the length.

a) What percentage of shrinkage did the manufacturer indicate?

b) To what shortest length should the tailor have shortened the pants (before washing) so that they would not be too short for Frida? Round the result to the nearest centimeter.

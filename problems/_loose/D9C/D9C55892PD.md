Let $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$ with $0<a<b$ . Prove that:

**a)** $2\sqrt {ab}\le\frac {x+y+z}3+\frac {ab}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}}\le a+b$ , for $x,y,z\in [a,b] .$ **b)** $\left\{\frac {x+y+z}3+\frac {ab}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}} | x,y,z\in [a,b]\right\}=[2\sqrt {ab},a+b] .$

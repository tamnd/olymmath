Let $f(x)$ be a periodic function defined on $(-\infty,+\infty)$ with a period of 2, and it is an even function. In the interval $[2,3]$, $f(x)=-2(x-3)^{2}+4$. The rectangle $A B C D$ has two vertices $A$ and $B$ on the $x$-axis, and $C$ and $D$ on the graph of the function $y=f(x)(0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2)$. Find the maximum value of the area of this rectangle.

Let's find the regularities shown in the following table:

$$
\begin{aligned}
1 & = 1 \\
2+3+4 & =1+8 \\
5+6+7+8+9 & =8+27 \\
10+11+12+13+14+15+16 & =27+64
\end{aligned}
$$

Write the common value of the two sides in the form of a product of two factors. Try to extend the table with additional rows. What will be the next row? Without writing the intermediate rows, give the first, middle, and last term on the left side of the twentieth row, as well as the two terms on the right side. Does the equality still hold there?

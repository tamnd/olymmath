Point $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of an acute, non-isosceles triangle $ABC$. On the bisector of angle $ABC$ inside triangle $ABC$, point $D$ is marked, and on segment $BD$, point $E$ is such that $AE = BE$ and $BD = CD$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $AOE$ and $COD$ respectively. Prove that points $A, C, P$, and $Q$ lie on the same line or on the same circle.

(A. Kuznetsov)

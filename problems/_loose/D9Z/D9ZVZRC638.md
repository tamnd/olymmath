Given two intersecting circles at points $P$ and $Q$. $C$ is an arbitrary point on one of the circles, different from $P$ and $Q$; $A, B$ are the second points of intersection of the lines $C P, C Q$ with the other circle. Find the geometric locus of the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $A B C$.

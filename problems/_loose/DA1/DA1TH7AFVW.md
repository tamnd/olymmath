Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle with center $O$ and area 1. Let $D, E, F$ be the points symmetric to $O$ with respect to the three sides of the triangle. What is the area common to triangles $A B C$ and $D E F$?
(A) $\frac{1}{3}$
(B) $\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{9}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
(E) $\frac{2}{3}$

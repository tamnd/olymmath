Given are two perpendicular lines: $a \perp b$, whose intersection point is $M$, and a circle with an arbitrary center $O$ and radius $OM$. A line passing through $O$ intersects the given lines at points $A$ and $B$, respectively. Among the two intersection points of this line with the circle, the one lying between $A$ and $B$ is denoted by $C$. Can a line $e$ passing through $O$ be constructed such that

$$
A C = C B ?
$$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $M$ the midpoint of $[AB]$. The perpendicular bisector of $[AB]$ intersects $(AC)$ at $P$ and $(BC)$ at $Q$. Let $R$ and $S$ be the orthogonal projections of $P$ onto $(BC)$ and of $Q$ onto $(AC)$, respectively.

Show that $M, R, S$ are collinear.

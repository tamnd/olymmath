Given a subset $X$ of $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$ satisfies: for any $a, b \in X$, if $\frac{a+b}{2} \in \mathbf{Z}$, then $\frac{a+b}{2} \in X$. Hence, $X$ is called a "good subset". Let $A(n)$ be the number of good subsets of $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$ (for example, $A(3)=7$, among the eight subsets of $\{1,2,3\}$, only $\{1,3\}$ is not a good subset). Prove: $A(100)+A(98) \geqslant 2 A(99)+6$.

An $n$-digit number (possibly with leading zeros) consists of several periods of the decimal representation of the fraction $1 / m$. Moreover, if the conditions $(m, 3)=1$ and $E_{n}-$ the first repunit divisible by $m$ are satisfied, then the number $9 E_{n / m}$ will coincide with the period.

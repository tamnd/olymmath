Consider the derived point $P^{\prime}\left(a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}\right)$ of a point $P(a, b)$ in a Cartesian coordinate system, where

$$
a^{\prime}=0.6 a+0.8 b, b^{\prime}=0.8 a-0.6 b
$$

Show that the line connecting any point with its derived point is parallel to a certain direction (provided the two points are distinct). Find another property of the relationship between the points $P$ and $P^{\prime}$.

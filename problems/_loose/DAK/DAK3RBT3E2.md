Ctirad programmed two cooperative drawing robots, Miky and Niki. Miky can construct squares, regular pentagons, and regular hexagons. However, in one day, he draws only congruent polygons. Niki adds all the diagonals to all of Miky's polygons.

1. On Monday, Miky constructed the same number of segments as Niki. Which polygons were they drawing?
2. On Tuesday, Miky constructed 18 segments. How many did Niki construct?
3. On Wednesday, Miky and Niki constructed a total of 70 segments. How many polygons did Ctirad give them to draw?

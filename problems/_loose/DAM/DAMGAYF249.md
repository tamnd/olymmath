Consider two circles, $O$ and $O^{\prime}$, and a point $A$ inside the first one, and a point $A^{\prime}$ inside the second one. We draw the chord $C D$ through the point $A$. Construct the chord $C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ through the point $A^{\prime}$ such that the points $C, D, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}$ lie on a circle $k$. Rotate the chord $C D$ around $A$ (and thus also $C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ around $A^{\prime}$). What will be the geometric locus of the center of the circle $k$?

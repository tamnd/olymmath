Suppose that a function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies the following conditions:

(i) $\left|f(a)-f(b)\right|\le|a-b|$ for all $a,b\in\mathbb R$ ;
(ii) $f(f(f(0)))=0$ .

Prove that $f(0)=0$ .

(Jury, Sweden, 83). Prove that if a number $n \in \mathbf{N}$ is not an integer power of a prime number, then there exists a permutation

$$
\left(i_{1} ; i_{2} ; \ldots ; i_{n}\right)
$$

of the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$, for which the equality

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k \cos \left(2 \pi i_{k} / n\right)=0
$$

holds.

(1) The triangle $\triangle A B C_{1}$ is equal in area to the triangle $\triangle A B C$,

(2) $A C = A C_{1}$.

(3) $C \neq C_{1}$.

a) Provide a construction method to obtain all points $C_{1}$ that have the properties (1), (2), (3)!

b) Investigate how the number of points $C_{1}$ with the properties (1), (2), (3) depends on the properties of the given triangle $\triangle A B C$! (Case distinction)

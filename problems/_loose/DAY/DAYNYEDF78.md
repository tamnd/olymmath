A cylinder of height $10 \mathrm{~cm}$ is given. Points $A$ and $B$ are marked on the edges of its bases such that $\overline{A B}$ is parallel to the axis of the cylinder. If we connect points $A$ and $B$ with the shortest line that goes once around the cylinder (along the lateral surface), its length will be $15 \mathrm{~cm}$. What is the length of the shortest line that goes around the cylinder twice and connects points $A$ and $B$?

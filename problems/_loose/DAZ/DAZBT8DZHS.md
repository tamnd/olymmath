In the plane, there is a quadrilateral $ABCD$. The line passing through $A$ and parallel to $BC$ intersects $BD$ at $D_1$, and the line passing through $B$ and parallel to $AD$ intersects $AC$ at $C_1$. It needs to be proven that $C_1D_1 \parallel CD$.

Let $K$ be any point on the line $AB$. The line passing through $A$ and parallel to $KC_1$ intersects the line passing through $B$ and parallel to $KD_1$ at $L$, and the line passing through $A$ and parallel to $KC$ intersects the line passing through $B$ and parallel to $KD$ at $L_1$. It needs to be proven that $L$ is on $CD$, $L_1$ is on $C_1D_1$, and the line $LL_1$ passes through $K$.

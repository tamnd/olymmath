John, Bill, and George sold apples next to each other one day. In the morning, they sold 2 apples for 5 cents - that is, 1 apple for $5 / 2$ cents, and in the afternoon, 3 apples for 5 cents - that is, 1 apple for $5 / 3$ cents. John sold the same amount in the afternoon as in the morning. Bill's revenue was the same in the morning and in the afternoon. The ratio of the number of apples George sold in the morning to the number he sold in the afternoon was the same as the ratio of $5 / 3$ to $5 / 2$. At what average price did each boy sell one apple? - What formulas yield the average unit price if the morning unit price is denoted by $A$ and the afternoon unit price by $B$?

In a plant, two types of fluids were used to make two mixtures. For the I, they used $a$ and $b$ grams of the fluids, respectively, and for the II, they used $c$ and $d$ grams. The I mixture turned out to be $e$ times as much as the II. From both mixtures, $f$ grams were taken for laboratory inspection. They immediately determined that the I mixture was usable because, although its density is lower than the required $g$ value, it only deviates by $h$ thousandths. Later, they reported that the II mixture could not be used because its density is high, deviating by $i$ thousandths from $g$, which is more than the maximum allowed deviation. - By this time, $j$ grams of the I mixture had already been used. When the remainder was mixed with the II, it turned out that the new mixture's density deviated exactly by the maximum allowed value from $g$. - Express the maximum allowed deviation in thousandths and the density of the original fluids using the given data.

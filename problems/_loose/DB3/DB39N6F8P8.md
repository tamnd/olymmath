The nonnegative real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}, x_{5}, a$ satisfy the following relations:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} i x_{i}=a, \sum_{i=1}^{5} i^{3} x_{i}=a^{2}, \sum_{i=1}^{5} i^{5} x_{i}=a^{3} .
$$

What are the possible values of $a$ ?

Let $a$ and $b$ be non-negative integers. Prove that $5 a \geq 7 b$ if and only if there exist non-negative integers $x, y, z, t$ such that

$$
\begin{array}{r}
x+2 y+3 z+7 t=a \\
y+2 z+5 t=b
\end{array}
$$

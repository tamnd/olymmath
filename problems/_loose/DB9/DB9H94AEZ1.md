A line passing through point $O$ perpendicular to the plane $ABC$ intersects the sphere at point $E$ such that $D$ and $E$ lie on opposite sides relative to the plane $ABC$. The line $DE$ intersects the plane $ABC$ at point $F$, which lies inside triangle $ABC$. It turns out that $\angle ADE = \angle BDE$, $AF \neq BF$, and $\angle AFB = 80^\circ$.

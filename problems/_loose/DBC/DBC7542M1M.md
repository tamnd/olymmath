Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Denote by $D$ the midpoint of the arc $AB$ that does not contain point $C$, and by $E$ the midpoint of the arc $AC$ that does not contain point $B$. Let $F$ and $G$ be the points of tangency of the incircle of triangle $ABC$ with sides $AB$ and $AC$. Let $X$ be the intersection of lines $EG$ and $DF$. Suppose that triangle $DEX$ is isosceles with the vertex at $X$. Prove that triangle $ABC$ is also isosceles with the vertex at $A$.

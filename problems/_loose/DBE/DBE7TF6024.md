Let $D$ be an interior point on side $BC$ of $\triangle ABC$, and let $AD$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $X$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $X$ to $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $\tau$ be the circle with diameter $XD$. Prove that $PQ$ is tangent to $\tau$ if and only if $AB = AC$.

Consider the equation $$ n!-(n-1)n=4p^2 $$ where $n$ is a positive integer and $p$ is a prime number. (Here $n!$ represents the product of the numbers $1$ to $n$ . For example, $4!=1\cdot 2\cdot 3\cdot 4=24$ .)
Find all solutions $(n, p)$

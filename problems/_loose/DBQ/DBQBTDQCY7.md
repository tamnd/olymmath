a) A worm always moves along a straight line. Every day, it advances 5 m and retreats 3 m. At the end of 15 days, how far from the starting point will the worm be?

b) At the end of these 15 days, how many meters in total will this worm have walked?

c) Another worm also moves along a straight line, but in a different way from the first one. On the first day, it moves 1 m forward and 1/2 m backward. On the second day, it moves 1/2 m forward and 1/3 m backward. On the third day, it moves 1/3 m forward and 1/4 m backward, and so on. How many meters will it have walked after 1000 days?

d) Will this second worm ever manage to be 2 m away from the starting point?

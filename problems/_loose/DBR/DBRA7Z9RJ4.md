Dima has 9 identical-looking balls numbered from 1 to 9. Dima knows that one of the balls is slightly heavier than the others, but to determine which one, he needs ultra-precise scales. Such scales are owned by his perpetually busy neighbor, a chemist, who agrees to help Dima under the following conditions:

- no more than two weighings will be made;
- Dima must write down in advance the numbers of the balls to be placed on each pan of the scales for each weighing;

Can Dima find the heavy ball?

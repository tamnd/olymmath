We would like to prove the following proposition $P$.

Proposition $P$

There exist positive integers $A$ satisfying the following condition of both (a) and (b).

(a) $A$ is the product of three consecutive integers.

(b) When $A$ is expressed by decimal notation, there are some places in which $1$ appears successively more than or equal to $99$ times.

Answer the questions as below.

(1) Let $y$ be a positive integer. Find the range of positive real numbers $x$ for which the inequality
$$x^3+3yx^2<(x+y-1)(x+y)(x+y+1)<x^3+(3y+1)x^2$$
holds.

(2) Prove the proposition $P$.

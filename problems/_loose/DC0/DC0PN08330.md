(13 Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AH \perp BC$ at $H$, and $P$ is a point on the altitude $AH$. A line through $P$ perpendicular to $AB$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABH$ at points $D$ and $D'$. A line through $P$ perpendicular to $AC$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ACH$ at points $E$ and $E'$. Prove that $D$, $D'$, $E$, and $E'$ are concyclic, and identify the center of the circle they lie on.

Divide all natural numbers from 1 to 1000000 into two disjoint classes, one class being the sum of perfect squares and perfect cubes, and the rest of the numbers forming the other class. Which class has more numbers? Prove your conclusion.
（22nd All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, 1996）

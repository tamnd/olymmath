Let $\mathbf{\Xi}$ be the set where $p$ is a prime number, $n \in \mathbf{N}^{\top}$. Prove:
$$
C_{n}^{p-1} \equiv\left\{\begin{array}{l}
0(\bmod p), n \neq q p-1\left(q \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right) \\
1(\bmod p), n=q p-1\left(q \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right) .
\end{array}\right.
$$

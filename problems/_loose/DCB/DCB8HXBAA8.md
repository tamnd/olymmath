\section*{Problem 6B - 131246B}

a) Prove the following statement:

There is no polynomial function $f$ such that for every $x$ the two inequalities hold:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) & > f''(x) \\
f'(x) & > f''(x)
\end{aligned}
$$

b) Does a correct statement arise if in the statement made in a) the inequality (2) is replaced by $f(x) > f'(x) (3)$?

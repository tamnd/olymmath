Consider a billiard table whose surface is a regular 1990-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{1990}$. A ball is hit from a point $P_{0}$ on $A_{1} A_{2}$, strikes a point $P_{1}$ on $A_{2} A_{3}$, and subsequently hits points $P_{2}, P_{3}, \cdots, P_{1989}, P_{1990}$ on $A_{3} A_{4}, A_{4} A_{5}, \cdots, A_{1990} A_{1}, A_{1} A_{2}$ respectively. Let $P_{0} A_{2} = \lambda A_{1} A_{2} (0 < \lambda < 1)$ and $\angle P_{1} P_{0} A_{2} = \theta$.
(1) Find the range of $\theta$;
(2) If $P_{1990} = P_{0}$, find the value of $\theta$.

Let the sequence of natural numbers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy:
$a_{1}=2, a_{n+1}=a_{n}^{2}-a_{n}+1, n=1,2,3, \cdots$ Prove:
(1) For any $m, n \in N, m \neq n$, the greatest common divisor of $a_{m}$ and $a_{n}$ is 1.
(2) $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{a_{k}}=1$.

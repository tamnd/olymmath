(Hölder's Inequality) Let $p, q \geqslant 0$ such that $p+q=1$ and $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}>0$ and $y_{1}, \ldots, y_{n}>0$. Show that

$$
x_{1}^{p} y_{1}^{q}+\cdots+x_{n}^{p} y_{n}^{q} \leqslant\left(x_{1}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)^{p}\left(y_{1}+\cdots+y_{n}\right)^{q}
$$

Generalize to the case of $k n$ variables with $k \geqslant 2$.

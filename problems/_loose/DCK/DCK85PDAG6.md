Let points $A, B, C$ and $D$ lie on a conic defined by the second-degree equation $f=0$. Prove that

$$
f=\lambda l_{\mathrm{AB}} l_{\mathrm{CD}}+\mu l_{\mathrm{BC}} l_{\mathrm{AD}}
$$

where $\lambda$ and $\mu-$ are some numbers.

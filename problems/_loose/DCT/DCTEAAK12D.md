Through point $B$ on their common tangent $A B$, two lines are drawn, one intersecting the first circle at points $M$ and $N$, and the other intersecting the second circle at points $P$ and $Q$. It is known that $A B=6, B M=9, B P=5$. Find the ratio of the areas of triangles $M N O$ and $P Q O$, where point $O$ is the intersection of lines $M P$ and $N Q$.

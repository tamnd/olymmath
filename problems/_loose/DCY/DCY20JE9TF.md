A line $d$ is called a "focal line" of a triangle $T$ if and only if there exists a point in this plane such that its projections on the three sides of triangle $T$ all lie on line $d$. Triangles $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ are "equivalent" if and only if the set of focal lines of $T_{1}$ is the same as the set of focal lines of $T_{2}$. For any given triangle $T$ in this plane, prove: there exists exactly one equilateral triangle that is equivalent to triangle $T$. ${ }^{[1]}$

For positive real numbers $ x,y,z$ the inequality
$$\frac1{x^2+1}+\frac1{y^2+1}+\frac1{z^2+1}=\frac12$$
holds. Prove the inequality
$$\frac1{x^3+2}+\frac1{y^3+2}+\frac1{z^3+2}<\frac13.$$

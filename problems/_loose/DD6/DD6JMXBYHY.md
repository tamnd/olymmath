Determine the first 10 digits after the decimal point of

$$
(5+\sqrt{26})^{100}
$$

Let $s$ be the sum of these 10 digits.

Go to the puzzle at the cell corresponding to:

$$
\frac{s \times a \times b \times d \times f \times e}{c \times g \times h}
$$

where:

- $a$ is Igor's shoe size,
- $b$ is the number of rooms in Emmanuel's house,
- $c$ is the number formed by the last two digits of Pierre's mobile phone number,
- $d$ is the number of ears François has,
- $e$ is the country code of Ilia's country of origin,
- $f$ is the number of brothers or sisters Antoine has,
- $g$ is the number of lectures/TD/TND given by Bodo during this training,
- $h$ is the number of the classroom where Louis was in his second year of Classes Préparatoires.

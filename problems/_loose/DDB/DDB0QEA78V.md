(Viskov.) Let $\xi, \eta \sim \mathscr{N}(0,1)$ be independent Gaussian variables. Show that for any entire function $f=f(z), z \in \mathbb{C}$, satisfying the condition $\mathrm{E}|f(x+\xi+i \eta)|<\infty$, the following "averaging" property holds:

$$
f(x)=\mathrm{E} f(x+\xi+i \eta) .
$$

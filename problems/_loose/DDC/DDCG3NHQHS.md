Arc $AB$ of a circle is divided into three equal parts by points $C$ and $D$ ($C$ is the point closest to $A$). After rotating around $A$ by an angle of $\pi / 3$, points $B, C$, and $D$ will move to $B_{1}, C_{1}$, and $D_{1}$, respectively. $F$ is the intersection point of lines $A B_{1}$ and $D C_{1}$, and $E$ is a point on the angle bisector of $\angle B_{1} B A$ such that $|B D|=|D E|$. Prove that triangle $C E F$ is equilateral (Finland).

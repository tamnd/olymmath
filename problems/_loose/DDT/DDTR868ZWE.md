In a parallelogram, let the adjacent sides be $a$ and $b$, and the longer diagonal be $e$. Let the projections of the diagonal on the extensions of sides $a$ and $b$ be $p_{a}$ and $p_{b}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
a p_{a} + b p_{b} = e^{2}
$$

Three circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$ intersect in point $O$. Let $A, B$, and $C$ be the second intersection points (other than $O$ ) of $k_{2}$ and $k_{3}, k_{1}$ and $k_{3}$, and $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively. Assume that $O$ lies inside of the triangle $A B C$. Let lines $A O$, $B O$, and $C O$ intersect circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$ for a second time at points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$, respectively. If $|X Y|$ denotes the length of segment $X Y$, prove that
$$
\frac{|A O|}{\left|A A^{\prime}\right|}+\frac{|B O|}{\left|B B^{\prime}\right|}+\frac{|C O|}{\left|C C^{\prime}\right|}=1 .
$$

Let $P$ be the intersection point of $AF$ and $DE$, $Q$ be the intersection point of $BG$ and $AF$, $R$ the intersection point of $CH$ and $BG$, and $S$ the point of intersection of $DE$ and $CH$. Prove that $PQRS$ is a square.

Construct a triangle $ABC$ from $a=9.7 \text{ cm}, b=7.6 \text{ cm}$, and $\beta+\gamma=115^{\circ}$!

Here, $a$ is the length of side $BC$, $b$ is the length of side $AC$, $\beta$ is the measure of angle $\angle ABC$, and $\gamma$ is the measure of angle $\angle ACB$.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the given pieces uniquely determine a triangle up to congruence!

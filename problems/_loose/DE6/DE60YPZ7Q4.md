Let some three-element subsets of an $n$-element set $X$ form a set $S$, and any two elements (subsets) in $S$ have at most 1 common element. Prove that there exists a set $A \subset X$ such that $|A| \geqslant [\sqrt{2 n}]$ and no element of $S$ is a subset of $A$.

Let the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 0}, a_{0}>0$ and $a_{n+1}=\frac{16 a_{n}^{3}+15 a_{n}}{16},(\forall) n \in \mathbb{N}$.

a) Show that, for $a_{0} \in\left(0 ; \frac{1}{4}\right]$, the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 0}$ is convergent and calculate its limit.

b) Show that, for $a_{0}>\frac{1}{4}$, the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 0}$ is divergent.

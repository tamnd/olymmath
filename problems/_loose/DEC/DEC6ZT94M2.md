In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $I$ is the incenter, and $AB \neq AC$. The incircle $\Gamma$ of $\triangle ABC$ touches sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. The line through $D$ perpendicular to $EF$ intersects the circle $\Gamma$ again at point $R$. The line $AR$ intersects the circle $\Gamma$ again at point $P$. The circumcircles of $\triangle PCE$ and $\triangle PBF$ intersect again at point $Q$. Prove that the intersection of line $DI$ and $PQ$ lies on the line through point $A$ and perpendicular to $AI$.

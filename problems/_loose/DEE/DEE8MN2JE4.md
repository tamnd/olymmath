Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let the complex-coefficient polynomial $P(z)=\sum_{i=0} a_{i} z^{i}$. Prove: There exists a complex number $z$, satisfying $|z| \leqslant 1$, and $|P(z)| \geqslant\left|a_{0}\right|+\max _{1 \leqslant k \leqslant n} \frac{\left|a_{k}\right|}{\left[\frac{n}{k}\right]}$.

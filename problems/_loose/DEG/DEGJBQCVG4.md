For some functions $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ and $g: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$, the identity

$$
f(x) g(y)=a x y+b x+c y+1
$$

holds, where $a, b, c$ are constants, and $x$ and $y$ are any real numbers. Prove that $a=b c$.

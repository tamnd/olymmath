Let the ray system $S(a, b, c, d)$ be given. Cut this system with two lines drawn from point $C$. Denote the intersection points of the first line with rays $a, b, c$, and $d$ by $A, B, C$, and $D$, respectively, and the intersection points of the second line with these rays by $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$. Prove that

$$
(A B C D)=\left(A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}\right)
$$

where the interpretation of the symbol $(A B C D)$ is given by the following equation:

$$
(A B C D)=\frac{A C}{B C}: \frac{A D}{B D}
$$

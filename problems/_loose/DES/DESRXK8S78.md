Let $p$ be an odd prime number. Prove that for every such $p$ , regardless of how we divide the set $$ \mathbb{A} = \{1^{p^2-p}, 2^{p^2-p},..., (p-1)^{p^2-p}, p^2-p+1\}, $$ into two disjoint sets, the arithmetic means of the elements of the two subsets cannot be equal.

On the external bisector of an angle, a point is given through which an arbitrary secant is drawn, cutting off from the sides of the angle or from the extensions of both its sides segments $a$ and $b$. Prove that $\left|\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}\right|$ does not depend on the position of the secant. Formulate and prove the converse theorem.

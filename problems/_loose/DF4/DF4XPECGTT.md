Let $n \geqslant 0$ and $a_{0}, \ldots, a_{n} \in \mathbb{R}$. Show that there exists $k \in \llbracket 0, n \rrbracket$ such that for all $x \in[0,1]$, we have $\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_{i} x^{i} \leqslant \sum_{i=0}^{k} a_{i}$.

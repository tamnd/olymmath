Let $O$ be the circumcenter of the acute triangle $ABC$ and let $M$ be an arbitrary point on the side $AB$. The circumcircle of triangle $AMO$ intersects the line $AC$ again at point $K$ and the circumcircle of triangle $BOM$ intersects the line $BC$ again at point $N$. Prove that

$$
\text { Area }(MNK) \geq \frac{1}{4} \text { Area }(ABC)
$$

and determine the case in which equality is achieved.

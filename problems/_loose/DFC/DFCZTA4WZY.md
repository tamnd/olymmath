(Saint Petersburg Olympiads 1996) Let $A B C$ be a triangle and $D$ the foot of the bisector from $B$. We denote $E$ as the second intersection point of the circumcircle of $B C D$ with $(A B)$, and $F$ as the second intersection point of the circumcircle of $A B D$ with $(B C)$. Show that $A E = C F$.

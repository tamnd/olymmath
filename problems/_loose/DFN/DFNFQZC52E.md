Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $K$ the foot of the bisector relative to $BC$, and $J$ the foot of the trisector relative to $BC$ closest to side $AC$ (i.e., $J$ is the point on $BC$ such that $3 \cdot \angle CAJ = \angle CAB$). Let $C'$ and $B'$ be two points on the line $AJ$, on the side of $J$ relative to $A$, such that $AC' = AC$ and $AB = AB'$. Prove that the quadrilateral $ABB'C$ is inscribed in a circle if and only if the lines $C'K$ and $B'B$ are parallel.

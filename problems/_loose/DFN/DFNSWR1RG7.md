The radius of the base of a cone is $R$, and the angle between the generatrix and the base plane is $\alpha$. A sphere is inscribed in this cone. Through a point $P$ lying on the circle of contact between the sphere and the conical surface, a tangent line to this circle is drawn, and through this line a plane is drawn parallel to the generatrix of the cone passing through the point diametrically opposite to point $P$. Find the area of the section of the sphere by this plane.

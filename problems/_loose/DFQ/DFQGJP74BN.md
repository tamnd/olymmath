Mr. A is driving his car on the highway at $100 \mathrm{~km} \cdot \mathrm{h}^{-1}$ past a gas station ($T_{1}$). 35 $\mathrm{km}$ behind $T_{1}$, Mr. A has to switch the fuel gauge to reserve.

Since the next gas station ($T_{2}$) on the highway is another $35 \mathrm{~km}$ away, Mr. A slows down to $60 \mathrm{~km} \cdot \mathrm{h}^{-1}$ to consume less fuel.

At what average speed will Mr. A cover the distance between $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ under these conditions?

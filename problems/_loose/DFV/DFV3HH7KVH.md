The 66th problem: Let $A$ be a 2017-element set, and its 3-element subsets $A_{i}(1 \leq i \leq k)$ satisfy $U_{i=1}^{k} A_{i}=A$, and for any $1 \leq \mathrm{i}<\mathrm{j} \leq \mathrm{k}$, we have $\left|A_{i} \cap A_{j}\right| \leq 1$. Prove: The elements of set $A$ can be colored red and blue such that at least 64 elements in $A$ are blue, and each $A_{i}$ contains at least one red element.

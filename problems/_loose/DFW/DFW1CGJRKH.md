(A) $\frac{100}{231}$.
(B) $\frac{115}{231}$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $\frac{118}{231}$.
(E) $\frac{6}{11}$.
(35th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1984)

Determine whether it is possible to partition $\mathbb{N}$ into two sets $A$ and $B$ such that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, there are as many ways to express $n$ as the sum of two distinct elements of $A$ as there are to express $n$ as the sum of two distinct elements of $B$.

On the board, two integers are written whose sum is divisible by seven. The third number is equal to the difference between the first and the second, the fourth - the difference between the second and the third, the fifth - the difference between the third and the fourth, and so on. Prove that the sum of the squares of the first seven numbers obtained in the above manner is divisible by seven.

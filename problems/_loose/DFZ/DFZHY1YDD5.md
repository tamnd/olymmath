(4th International Mathematical Olympiad for College Students) Prove: there does not exist a mapping $f$ from the set of real numbers to the set of real numbers, such that for all positive real numbers $x, y$, we have
$$
f(x+y)>f(x)(1+y f(x))
$$

holds.

Five Dice. Five dice are thrown simultaneously. We will call such a simultaneous throw of several dice a volley. The dice on which sixes have fallen are set aside. The rest are thrown again - again in a volley. Those that did not show sixes on the second throw are thrown again, and so on, until sixes have fallen on all dice.

a) (8th grade. 2 points). Find the expected total number of dice thrown.

b) (9th grade. 4 points). Find the expected total number of points that have fallen by the time all dice have shown sixes.

c) (9th grade. 6 points). Find the expected number of volleys.

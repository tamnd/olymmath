Suppose there are 6 points on a plane, where no three points are collinear. If we connect some pairs of points with line segments such that the following condition is satisfied: for any three points, at least one pair of points is not connected by a line segment (i.e., any three points do not form the vertices of a triangle). How many line segments can be drawn at most under these conditions? Prove your conclusion.

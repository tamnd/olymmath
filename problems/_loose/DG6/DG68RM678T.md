Let $A, B, D, E, F, C$ be six points on a circle in that order, satisfying $AB = AC$. Line $AD$ intersects line $BE$ at point $P$, line $AF$ intersects line $CE$ at point $R$, line $BF$ intersects line $CD$ at point $Q$, line $AD$ intersects line $BF$ at point $S$, and line $AF$ intersects line $CD$ at point $T$. Point $K$ lies on segment $ST$ such that $\angle SKQ = \angle ACE$. Prove that $\frac{SK}{KT} = \frac{PQ}{QR}$.
(30th China Mathematical Olympiad)

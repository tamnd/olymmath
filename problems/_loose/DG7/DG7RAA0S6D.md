Let the edge lengths of an arbitrary cuboid be $a, b, c$ and the length of its space diagonal be $d$. Prove that the following inequality (1) always holds:

$$
(a b)^{2}+(b c)^{2}+(a c)^{2} \geq a b c d \cdot \sqrt{3}
$$

Furthermore, determine all cuboids for which the equality sign holds in (1).

(See [62].) Let $X=\left(X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots\right)$ be a Gaussian random sequence, and $L$ be an arbitrary linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{\infty}$.

Prove that then the zero-one law for Gaussian systems holds:

$$
\mathrm{P}(X \in L)=0 \text{ or } 1.
$$

In particular, derive that $\mathrm{P}\left(\sup _{n}\left|X_{n}\right|<\infty\right)=0$ or 1.

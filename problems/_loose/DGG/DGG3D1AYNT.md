Let the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ pass through the point $P\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)$, with an eccentricity of $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, and a moving point $M(2, t)$ $(t>0)$.
(1) Find the standard equation of the ellipse.
(2) Find the equation of the circle with diameter $OM$ that is intersected by the line $3 x-4 y-5=0$ to form a chord of length 2.
(3) Let $F$ be the right focus of the ellipse. Draw a perpendicular from point $F$ to $OM$ that intersects the circle with diameter $OM$ at point $N$. Prove that the length of segment $ON$ is a constant, and find this constant.

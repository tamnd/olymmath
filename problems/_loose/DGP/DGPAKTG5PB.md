For the family of subsets $\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{1}\right\}$ of the set $F=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, if any two elements $A_{i}, A_{j}(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)$ do not contain each other, then $F$ is called a Sperner family, or simply an $S$ family. Prove that the number of elements in an $S$ family is at most $C_{n}^{\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]}$, i.e.,
$$
\max \{t\}=C_{n}^{\left[\frac{\pi}{2}\right]} .
$$

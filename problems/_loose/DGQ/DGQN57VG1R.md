Consider a natural number $n\geqslant 3$ and a graph $G{}$ with a chromatic number $\chi(G)=n$ which has more than $n{}$ vertices. Prove that there exist two vertex-disjoint subgraphs $G_1{}$ and $G_2{}$ of $G{}$ such that $\chi(G_1)+\chi(G_2)\geqslant n+1.$ *Proposed by V. Dolnikov*

Let $k$ be the inscribed circle of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with center at point $I$, and let $k_{c}$ be the excircle of the same triangle opposite to the angle $\angle BCA$. If point $D$ is the point of tangency of side $\overline{AB}$ and circle $k_{c}$, and point $S$ is the intersection of line $DI$ with circle $k_{c}$ (different from point $D$), prove that line $DI$ is the angle bisector of $\angle ASB$.

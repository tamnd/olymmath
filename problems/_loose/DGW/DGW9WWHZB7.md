In an $8 \times 8$ square grid, fill each cell with a non-negative number. It is known that the sum of the 64 numbers is 1956, the sum of the 16 numbers on the two diagonals is 112, and the numbers in positions symmetric about each diagonal are the same. Prove that the sum of the numbers in each row is less than 518.

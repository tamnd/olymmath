Anya, Borya, and Vitya are sitting in a circle at a table and eating nuts. Initially, all the nuts are with Anya. She divides them equally between Borya and Vitya, and eats the remainder (if any). Then the process repeats: each subsequent person (clockwise) divides the nuts they have equally between their neighbors, and eats the remainder. There are many nuts (more than 3). Prove that:

a) at least one nut will be eaten;

b) not all nuts will be eaten.

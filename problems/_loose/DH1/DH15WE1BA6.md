Maresin $B$. For each natural number $n>1$ there exists a number $c_{n}$ such that for any $x$ the product of the sine of the number $x$, the sine of the number $x+\pi / n$, the sine of the number $x+2 \pi / n, \ldots$, and finally, the sine of the number $x+(n-1) \pi / n$ is equal to the product of the number $c_{n}$ and the sine of the number $n x$. Prove this and find the value of $c_{n}$.

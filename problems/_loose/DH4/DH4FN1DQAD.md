(SFRY, 75). Four ones and five zeros are written in a circle in some arbitrary order. The following operation is performed on them: a zero is written between identical digits, and a one between different digits, after which the original digits are erased. Then the same operation is performed on the resulting digits, and so on. Prove that after several such operations it is impossible to obtain 9 zeros.

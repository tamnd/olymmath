Determine all functions $f:(0, \infty) \rightarrow(0, \infty)$ (i.e., $f$ maps from the set of positive real numbers to the set of positive real numbers) such that

$$
\frac{(f(w))^{2}+(f(x))^{2}}{f\left(y^{2}\right)+f\left(z^{2}\right)}=\frac{w^{2}+x^{2}}{y^{2}+z^{2}}
$$

holds for any positive real numbers $w, x, y, z$ satisfying $w x=y z$.

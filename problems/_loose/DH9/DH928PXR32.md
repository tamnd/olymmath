The distribution law of a discrete random variable $X$ is as follows:

| $x_{i}$ | -2 | -1 | 0 | $m$ | $m+n$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $P_{i}$ | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.2 | $P_{4}$ | $P_{5}$ |

Find the probabilities $P_{4}, P_{5}$ and the variance $D(x)$, if the mathematical expectation $M(x)=-0.5+0.5 \cdot m+0.1 \cdot n$.

A wolf is surrounded by dogs located at points $M, N, P$ and $Q$ on the sides of a square $A B C D$, $M \in[A ; B], N \in[B ; C], P \in[C ; D], Q \in[D ; A]$ such that $A M: M B=B N: N C=C P: P D=D Q: Q A=1: 3$. The wolf, located inside the square at the intersection of lines $M P$ and $N Q$, can run in a straight line in any direction with a speed of $v_{w}$. The dogs run only along the sides of the square with a speed not exceeding $v_{c}$. The wolf can escape if it meets no more than one dog on the boundary of the square. For what values of the ratio $v_{c} / v_{w}$ does the wolf have a chance to escape?

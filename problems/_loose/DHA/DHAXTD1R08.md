(30th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) On the table, there are 2004 small boxes, each containing 1 ball. It is known that some of the balls are white, and there is an even number of white balls. You are allowed to point to any 2 boxes and ask: "Do they contain at least 1 white ball?" How many times do you need to ask, at a minimum, to determine 2 boxes that both contain white balls?

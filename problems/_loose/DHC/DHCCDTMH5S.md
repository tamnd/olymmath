João was studying for the Math Olympics and came across the following equation

$$
\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{z}
$$

where $x, y$, and $z$ are positive integers. After trying to find all solutions without success, he asked for help from Professor Piraldo, who decided to give some hints on how he should proceed. Let's help João interpret the hints.

(a) If $x=1$, determine all pairs $(y, z)$ of positive integers that satisfy the equation.

(b) If $x=2$ and $y \geq 2$, determine all pairs $(y, z)$ of positive integers that satisfy the equation.

(c) If $x=3$ and $y \geq 3$, determine all pairs $(y, z)$ of positive integers that satisfy the equation.

(d) If $x$ and $y$ are greater than or equal to 4, verify that the equation has no solution.

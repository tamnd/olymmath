In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $E$ and $F$ are taken on sides $AB$ and $BC$ respectively, such that segments $DE$ and $DF$ trisect $AC$. It is known that the areas of $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle CDF$ are each equal to $\frac{1}{4}$ of the area of the quadrilateral. Prove that $ABCD$ is a parallelogram.
(16th All-Russian Competition Problem)

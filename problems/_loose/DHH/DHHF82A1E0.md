\section*{Task 2 - 151022}

Hubert has three boxes, each containing a number of balls.

He takes from the first box and puts into each of the other two as many balls as are already in each of them. Then he takes from the second box and puts into each of the other two as many balls as are currently in each of them. Finally, he takes from the third box and puts into each of the other two as many balls as are currently in each of them.

Afterwards, he finds that each of the boxes contains exactly 64 balls.

Determine the number of balls that each of the boxes originally contained!

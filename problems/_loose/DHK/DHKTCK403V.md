Find all functions $f: \mathbb R^{+} \to \mathbb R^{+}$ satisfying
\begin{align*}
f(f(x)+y) = f(y) + x, \text{for all } x,y \in \mathbb R^{+}.
\end{align*}
Note that $\mathbb R^{+}$ is the set of all positive real numbers.

The Fly and the Cars. The length of the road is 300 km. Car $A$ starts at one end of the road at noon and moves at a constant speed of 50 km/h. At the same time, at the other end of the road, car $B$ starts at a constant speed of 100 km/h and a fly, flying at 150 km/h. Upon meeting car $A$, the fly turns and flies towards $B$.
1) When will the fly meet $B$?
2) If, upon meeting $B$, the fly turned, flew towards $A$, met him, turned again, and continued flying back and forth between $A$ and $B$ until they collided, when would the cars crush the fly?

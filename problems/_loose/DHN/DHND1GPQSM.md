Let $K$, $L$, $M$, $N$ be the orthogonal projections of point $H$ onto the faces $ABS$, $BCS$, $CDS$, $DAS$, respectively. Prove that the lines $KL$, $MN$, and $AC$ are either parallel or intersect at a single point.

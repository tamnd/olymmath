2.32 * In parallelogram $A B C D$, $\triangle A B D$ is an acute triangle, $A B=a, A D=$ $1, \angle B A D=\alpha, k_{A} 、 k_{B} 、 k_{C} 、 k_{D}$ are unit circles centered at $A 、 B 、 C 、 D$ respectively.

Prove: The sufficient and necessary condition for the four circles $k_{A} 、 k_{B} 、 k_{C} 、 k_{D}$ to cover $\square A B C D$ is $a \leqslant \cos \alpha+$ $\sqrt{3} \sin \alpha$.

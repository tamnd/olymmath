The general solution of the first-order differential inequality $y^{\prime}+p(x) y \geqslant q(x)$ is $x=\mathrm{e}^{-\int \rho(x) d r}\left[\int q(x) \mathrm{e}^{\int(x) \mathrm{d}} \mathrm{d} x+\int g(x) \mathrm{d} x\right]$, $\forall g(x) \geqslant 0$. The equality holds if and only if $g(x)=0$ in the formula.

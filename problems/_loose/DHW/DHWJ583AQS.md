$$
a=b+2 c, a+c=b+d, b=d+c
$$

Which of the following equations is definitely correct?
(A) $d=2 c$
(B) $a=3 c$
(C) $a=6 c$
(D) $a=b+2 d$
(E) $b=2 a+2 d$

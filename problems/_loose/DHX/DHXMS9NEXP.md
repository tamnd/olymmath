(2001 World Inter-City Mathematical Competition) On a chessboard, there is one black piece and one white piece. Each step can move one piece to an adjacent empty square horizontally or vertically. The goal is to move these two pieces so that all possible positions of the two pieces appear exactly once. Can this goal be achieved?
(a) If the two pieces must take turns moving;
(b) If there is no such requirement.

Maty landed on an island inhabited by two tribes of natives: the Truth-tellers, who always tell the truth, and the Liars, who always lie. Before landing, Maty saw a port in the distance, which he intended to reach.

At the first crossroads, Maty met one native and saw another nearby. He asked the first one to ask the second one whether he is a Liar or a Truth-teller. The first native agreed, went to ask, and when he returned, he told Maty that the second native claimed to be a Liar. Then Maty asked the first native which path leads to the port. The native pointed to one path and ignored Maty further.

Should Maty trust the native or not? Does that path lead to the port or not?

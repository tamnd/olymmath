(Austria-Poland, 80). A sequence of natural numbers $a_{1}1$ and $B>1$ and a sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}(n \in \mathbf{N})$ of numbers from the interval $[1 ; A B]$. Prove that there exists a sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ of numbers from the interval $[1 ; A]$, such that for any $m, n \in \mathbf{N}$ the estimate $a_{m} / a_{n} \leqslant B b_{m} / b_{n}$ is satisfied.

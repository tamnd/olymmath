If the equations
$$
x^{2}+A x+B=0 \text { and } x^{2}+C x+D=0
$$

have roots with modulus less than $1(A, B, C, D \in \mathbf{R})$, prove: the equation $x^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(A+C) x+\frac{1}{2}(B+D)=0$ also has roots with modulus less than 1.

On a line, there are points $A, B$ and, in addition, 57 points lying outside the segment $A B$. Each of these 57 points is either red or blue. Consider the following sums:

$S_{1}$ - the sum of the distances from point $A$ to all red points plus the sum of the distances from point $B$ to all blue points;

$S_{2}$ - the sum of the distances from point $A$ to all blue points plus the sum of the distances from point $B$ to all red points.

Prove that $S_{1} \neq S_{2}$.

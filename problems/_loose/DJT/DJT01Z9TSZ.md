Let $a$ be a positive number. If the geometric sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with $a$ as the first term satisfies that $a_{1}+1, a_{2}+2, a_{3}+3$ also form a geometric sequence, then $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is called a $P$ sequence corresponding to $a$. If $a_{n}=a^{2 n-1}(n=1,2, \cdots)$ is a $P$ sequence, where, $\frac{1}{2}<a<1), x_{n}^{2}-x_{n-1} x_{n+1}$ are all constants.

For real numbers $x_0,x_1,\cdots,x_n$ , there exists real numbers $y_0,y_1,\cdots,y_n$ , satisfying that $z_0^2=z_1^2+z_2^2+\cdots+z_n^2$ , where $z_k=x_k+\text{i}y_{k}(k=0,1,\cdots,n)$ . Find all such $(x_0,x_1,\cdots,x_n)$ .

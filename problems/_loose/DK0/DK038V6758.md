$n$ players take their seats at a circular table, among whom we distribute $n-1$ cards. Then the players exchange their cards according to the following rule: if there is a player with at least two cards, then one such player gives a card to each of their two neighbors. Prove that no matter how they exchange, eventually every player will have at most one card.

Given $p, q(q \neq 0)$ are real numbers, the equation $x^{2}-p x+q=0$ has two real roots $\alpha, \beta$, and the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=p, a_{2}=p^{2}-q, a_{n}=p a_{n-1}-q a_{n-2}(n=3,4, \cdots)$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ (expressed in terms of $\alpha, \beta$);
(2) If $p=1, q=\frac{1}{4}$, find the sum of the first $n$ terms of $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$.

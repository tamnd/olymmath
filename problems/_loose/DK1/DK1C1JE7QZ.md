Let $ABC$ be a triangle in which $\overline{BC} \leq \overline{AC} \leq \overline{AB}$ and let $M$ be a point inside $\triangle ABC$ which is at distances $x, y$ and $z$ from the sides $AB, BC$ and $CA$ respectively. If $h_{a}$ and $h_{c}$ are the altitudes of $\triangle ABC$ to the sides $BC$ and $AB$ respectively, then prove that $h_{c} \leq x+y+z \leq h_{a}$.

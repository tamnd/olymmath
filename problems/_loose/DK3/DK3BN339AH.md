In $\triangle A B C$, the sides opposite to $\angle A, \angle B, \angle C$ are $a, b, c$, respectively. It is known that $a^{2}=$ $b(b+c)$, and $\angle C$ is an obtuse angle, then the size relationship of $a, 2 b, c$ is
(A) $a<2 b<c$.
(B) $a<c<2 b$.
(C) $2 b<a<c$.
(D) $a=2 b<c$.
(Huanggang Region, Hubei Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1996)

Consider a triangle where one of the medians is three times the half of the corresponding side, i.e., $ A A_{1} = \frac{3}{2} B C $. Prove that in this triangle,

$$
{\overline{A A_{1}}}^{2} = {\overline{B B_{1}}}^{2} + {\overline{C C_{1}}}^{2}
$$

if the midpoints of the sides are denoted by $ A_{1} $, $ B_{1} $, and $ C_{1} $.

$\frac{3}{4}$ times.

Of course, you know the theorem that in any triangle, the three medians intersect at one point, and each median is divided by this point in the ratio $2: 1$, counting from the vertex. Prove this theorem based on mechanical considerations. (U. 119, P. 128.)

Note that this theorem was known even to Archimedes, who arrived at it based on mechanical considerations. Without using the concept of the center of gravity, it was proved much later, about 14 centuries after Archimedes, by the Italian mathematician Leonardo Fibonacci.

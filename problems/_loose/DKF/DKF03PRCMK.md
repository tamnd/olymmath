Prove that the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{1}-x_{2} & =a, \\
x_{3}-x_{4} & =b, \\
x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4} & =1
\end{aligned}
$$

has at least one positive solution

$$
x_{1}>0, x_{2}>0, x_{3}>0, x_{4}>0
$$

if and only if $|a|+|b|<1$.

In trapezoid $ABCD$ ($AD \| BC$), draw a line through vertex $A$ parallel to diagonal $BD$, and a line through vertex $C$ parallel to side $AB$. Let their intersection point be $E$. Show that if

$$
CE: AB = AD: BC,
$$

then $DE$ is parallel to $AC$, and $BE$ is parallel to $CD$.

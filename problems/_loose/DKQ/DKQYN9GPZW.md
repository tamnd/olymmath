We have a quadrilateral $ABCD$ inscribed in a circle of radius $r$ , for which there is a point $P$ on $CD$ such that $CB=BP=PA=AB$ .

(a) Prove that there are points $A,B,C,D,P$ which fulfill the above conditions.

(b) Prove that $PD=r$ .

*Virgil Nicula*

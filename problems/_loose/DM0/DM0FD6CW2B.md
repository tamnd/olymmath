1) Borya is not a first-grader;
2) when Vasya goes to the pool on the neighboring Southern Street, Ivanov walks his dog in his courtyard on Green Street;

4) Borya and Orlov are neighbors and live on Northern Street;
5) Krylov met Petrov exactly a year ago, when he was still a first-grader;
6) Vasya gave Borya a textbook that he used last year.

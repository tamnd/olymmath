The method of dividing a line segment is to make the ratio of the smaller part to the larger part the same as the ratio of the larger part to the whole segment. If $R$ is the ratio of the smaller part to the larger part, then
$$
R^{\left[R^{\left(R^{2}+\frac{1}{R}\right)}+\frac{1}{R}\right]}+\frac{1}{R}
$$

is
(A) 2.
(B) $2 R$.
(C) $\frac{1}{R}$.
(D) $2+\frac{1}{R}$.
(E) $2+R$.
(25th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1974)

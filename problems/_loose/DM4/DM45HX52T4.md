Prove that

$$
\sin \frac{\pi}{2 m} \sin \frac{2 \pi}{2 m} \sin \frac{3 \pi}{2 m} \cdot \cdots \sin \frac{(m-1) \pi}{2 m}=\frac{\sqrt{\bar{m}}}{2^{m-1}}
$$

and

$$
\sin \frac{\pi}{4 m} \sin \frac{3 \pi}{4 m} \sin \frac{5 \pi}{4 m} \ldots \sin \frac{(2 m-1) \pi}{4 m}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2^{m}}
$$

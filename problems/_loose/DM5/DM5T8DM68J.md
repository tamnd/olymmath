A circle has two perpendicular radii $O A$ and $O B$, a point $C$ on the shorter arc $A B$, its projection on $O A$ is $D$, on $O B$ is $E$, on $D E$ is $F$, and the perpendicular bisector of $O C$ intersects $C F$ at $G$. Show that the arc between $C$ and $F$ of the circle centered at $G$ with radius $G C$ is equal in length to the arc $A C$ of the original circle, and the other arc is equal to the arc $B C$. - How does the statement change if $C$ is any point on the original circle?

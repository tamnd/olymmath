Given that $\triangle A B C$ is inscribed in the unit circle $O$, and the three altitudes $A D, B E, C F$ intersect at point $H$, and the product of the distances from the orthocenter $H$ to the three sides is $\frac{1}{4}$. Prove: the distance from the orthocenter $H$ to the vertices of $\triangle A B C$ is at least one not less than $\sqrt[6]{2}$, and at least one not greater than $\sqrt[5]{2}$.

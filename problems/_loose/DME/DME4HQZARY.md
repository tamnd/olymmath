Let $d(n)$ be the number of divisors of a natural number $n$ (for example, $d(1) = 1$ , $d(2) = 2$ , $d(4) = 3$ ). A natural (not necessarily distinct) set of numbers $a_1$ , $a_2$ , $...$ , $a_m$ is called perfect if $d(a_1+a_2+....+a_k)=a_k$ with each $k = 1, 2, ...,m$ .
a) Name at least one perfect set of five numbers.
b) Find all perfect sets of five numbers
c) Is there at least one perfect set of $2011$ natural numbers?

Given a prime number $p$. We need to determine the smallest integer $N$ that is divisible by $p$, but when divided by any integer $n$ smaller than $p$,

$1^{0}$. the remainder is $n-1, 2^{0}$. the remainder is 1.

For example, $p=11$.

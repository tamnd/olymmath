Let $n$ be any natural number. Find all $n$ -tuples of real numbers $x_1\le x_2\le ... \le x_n$ , for which holds
$$ \left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right)^2 \le n \sum_{i=1}^n x_i x_{n-i+1}. $$

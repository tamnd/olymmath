Points $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$ do not lie on the same line. Let two different points $P$ and $Q$ have the property that $A_{1} P+\ldots+A_{n} P=A_{1} Q+\ldots+A_{n} Q=s$. Prove that then $A_{1} K+\ldots+A_{n} K<s$ for some point $K$.

Given $A^{2}+B^{2}=C^{2}$, then the product $A B$ is divisible by 6.

(Here $A, B$, and $C$ are understood to be integers. First, show that the square of an integer cannot be of the form $4n+2$ or $3n+2$; then conclude from this that the sum of the squares of two numbers cannot be a perfect square if both numbers are odd, or if neither is divisible by 3.)

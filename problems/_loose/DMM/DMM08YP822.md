If $P$ is not outside $\triangle A B C$, the angle bisectors from $P$ in $\triangle B P C, \triangle C P A, \triangle A P B$ are denoted as $p_{t}, q_{t}, r_{t}$, and the distances from $P$ to the three vertices are $x, y, z$, then:
(1) $x y z \geqslant 8 p, q_{1} r_{t}$;
(2) $x+y+z \geqslant 2\left(p_{t}+q_{t}+r_{t}\right)$.

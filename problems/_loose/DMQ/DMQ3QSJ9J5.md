Let $x,y$ be real numbers such that $xy=1$ . Let $T$ and $t$ be the largest and smallest values of the expression $\hspace{2cm} \frac{(x+y)^2-(x-y)-2}{(x+y)^2+(x-y)-2}$ .

If $T+t$ can be expressed in the form $\frac{m}{n}$ where $m,n$ are nonzero integers with $GCD(m,n)=1$ , find the value of $m+n$ .

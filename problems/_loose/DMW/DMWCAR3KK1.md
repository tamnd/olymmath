If $x,y,z$ are real numbers and

\begin{align*}
2x+y+z\leq66
x+2y+z\leq60
x+y+2z\leq70
x+2y+3z\leq110
3x+y+2z\leq98
2x+3y+z\leq89
\end{align*}

What is the maximum possible value of $x+y+z$ ?

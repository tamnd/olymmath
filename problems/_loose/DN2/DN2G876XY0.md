Points $O, A, B$ and $C$ do not lie in the same plane. Prove that point $X$ lies in the plane $ABC$ if and only if $\overrightarrow{O X}=p \overrightarrow{O A}+q \overrightarrow{O B}+r \overrightarrow{O C}$, where $p+q+r=1$. Moreover, if point $X$ belongs to triangle $ABC$, then $p: q: r=S_{\text {BXC }}: S_{C \text { CXA }}$ : $: S_{\mathrm{AXB}}$.

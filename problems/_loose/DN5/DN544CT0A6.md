Two players play the following game. There are 9 cards with numbers $1,2, \ldots, 9$. The players take turns picking one card for themselves. The winner is the one who has three cards with numbers that sum to 15. Prove that this game is equivalent to a game of "tic-tac-toe" on a $3 \times 3$ board.

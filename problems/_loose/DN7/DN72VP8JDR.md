Prove that the volume of the body obtained by rotating a circular segment around a diameter not intersecting it can be calculated using the formula

$$
V=\frac{1}{6} \pi a^{2} h
$$

where $a$ is the length of the chord of this segment, and $h$ is the projection of this chord onto the diameter.

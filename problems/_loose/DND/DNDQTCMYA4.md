Let $ E$ be a system of $ n^2 + 1$ closed intervals of the real line. Show that $ E$ has either a subsystem consisting of $ n + 1$ elements which are monotonically ordered with respect to inclusion or a subsystem consisting of $ n + 1$ elements none of which contains another element of the subsystem.

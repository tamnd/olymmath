Given a large vat of water and 5 measuring cups, the capacities of which are $1, 5, 25, 125, 625$ (milliliters) respectively. Try to prove that for any integer $a$ not exceeding 1562, it is possible to pour $a$ milliliters of water into an empty bucket using these 5 cups, with each cup being used at most twice. (Note: Pouring a cup of water from the vat into the bucket, or pouring a cup of water from the bucket back into the vat, counts as using the cup once)
(China Jiangxi Province Nanchang City Mathematics Competition, 1991)

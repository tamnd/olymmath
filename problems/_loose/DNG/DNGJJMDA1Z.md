In a plane, given 381 points, where no three points are collinear, they are connected by $l$ line segments. If there is no quadrilateral in the graph (i.e., there do not exist four given points $A, B, C, D$ such that $A$ is connected to $B$, $B$ is connected to $C$, $C$ is connected to $D$, and $D$ is connected to $A$). Prove: $l \leqslant 3810$.

Given a pyramid $S A B C D$, where its base is a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ with perpendicular diagonals, and the plane of the base is perpendicular to the line $S O$, where $O$ is the point of intersection of the diagonals. Prove that the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from point $O$ to the lateral faces of the pyramid lie on one circle.

a) Each side of an equilateral triangle is divided into $m$ equal parts, and lines parallel to the sides are drawn through the division points, cutting the triangle into $m^{2}$ small triangles. Among the vertices of the resulting triangles, $N$ vertices need to be marked so that for no two marked vertices $A$ and $B$ is the segment $A B$ parallel to any of the sides. What is the largest possible value of $N$ (for a given $m$)?

b) Divide each edge of a tetrahedron into $m$ equal parts and draw planes through the division points, parallel to the faces. Among the vertices of the resulting polyhedra, $N$ vertices need to be marked so that no two marked vertices lie on a line parallel to one of the faces. What is the largest possible value of $N$?

c) Among the solutions of the equation $x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k}=m$ in non-negative integers, $N$ solutions need to be chosen so that in no two of the chosen solutions does any variable $x_{i}$ take the same value. What is the largest possible value of $N$?

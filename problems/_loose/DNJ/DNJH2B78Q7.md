There are $\mathrm{n}$ players participating in a competition, with each pair of players playing at most one match, and there are no ties. It is known that for any set $\mathrm{X}$ of no more than $\mathrm{m}$ players, there exists a player not in $\mathrm{X}$ who has defeated all players in $\mathrm{X}$. Prove: $\mathrm{m}+1 \leq\left[\log _{2}(\mathrm{n}+1)\right]$.

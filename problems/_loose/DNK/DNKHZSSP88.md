We consider the segment $AB$ and the point $\Gamma$ on it such that $AB = 3 \cdot AG$. We construct the parallelogram $A\Gamma\Delta E$ with $A\Gamma = \Delta E = \Gamma E > AE$. We consider point $Z$ on the side $A\Gamma$ such that $A\hat{E}Z = A\Gamma E = \omega$. Prove that the line perpendicular from $B$ to the line $E\Gamma$ and the line from the point $\Delta$ perpendicular to the line $AB$ meet on a point, say $K$, which lies on the line $EZ$.

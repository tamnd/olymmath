On his turn, Alberto multiplies the last number written by $x^{2}$ and writes the result. On her turn, Barbara adds $x+1$ to the last number written and writes the result.

They stop when 2020 numbers are written on the blackboard. Which of the following statements is always true?

(A) There is at least one negative number written on the blackboard (B) All the numbers written on the blackboard are positive (C) Alberto has written only positive numbers (D) Alberto has written only negative numbers (E) Barbara has written only positive numbers

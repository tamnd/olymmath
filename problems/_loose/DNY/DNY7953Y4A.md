Let $ p,\ q\ (p < q)$ be the $ x$ coordinates of curves $ x^2 + y^2 = 1,\ y\geq 0$ and $ y = \frac {1}{4x}.$

(1) Find $ \alpha ,\ \beta$ such that $ \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} = p,\ \cos \frac {\beta }{2} = q\ (0 < \alpha < \pi ,\ 0 < \beta < \pi).$

(2) Find the area bounded by the curves.

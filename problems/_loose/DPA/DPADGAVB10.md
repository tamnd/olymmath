At a dance festival, two dance groups are participating. In the first part of the festival, each dancer performed one dance with every other dancer from their own group. No dancer from one group danced with a dancer from the other group. How many dancers are in each individual group if together they have 42 dancers, and in the first part of the festival, a total of 421 dances were performed? In the second part of the festival, dance pairs are formed, each consisting of one dancer from each dance group. What is the maximum number of such pairs that can be found on the dance floor? (Note: dancers are not distinguished by gender.)

Given a line $g$ and two points $A$ and $B$ on it.

Prove: The length $C T$ of a tangent from a point $C$ on $g$ to a circle passing through $A$ and $B$ (where $T$ is the point of tangency) depends only on the position of $C$, not on the radius $r$ of the circle.

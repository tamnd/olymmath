Prove that if a polynomial $p(x)$ has a root $x=a$ of multiplicity $k (k>1)$, then its derivative has a root $x=a$ of multiplicity $k-1$.

31${}^{\circ}$. Prove that if $f(x)$ is a polynomial with rational coefficients and

$$
f(a+b \sqrt{c})=P+Q \sqrt{c},
$$

where the numbers $a, b, c, P, Q$ are rational, $c>0$, and $\sqrt{c}$ is an irrational number, then

$$
f(a-b \sqrt{c})=P-Q \sqrt{c} .
$$

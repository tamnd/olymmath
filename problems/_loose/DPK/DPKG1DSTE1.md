Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers. Assume that

$$
\frac{a^{19}}{b^{19}}+\frac{b^{19}}{c^{19}}+\frac{c^{19}}{a^{19}} \leq \frac{a^{19}}{c^{19}}+\frac{b^{19}}{a^{19}}+\frac{c^{19}}{b^{19}}
$$

Prove that

$$
\frac{a^{20}}{b^{20}}+\frac{b^{20}}{c^{20}}+\frac{c^{20}}{a^{20}} \leq \frac{a^{20}}{c^{20}}+\frac{b^{20}}{a^{20}}+\frac{c^{20}}{b^{20}}
$$

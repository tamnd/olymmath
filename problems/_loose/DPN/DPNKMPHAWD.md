Inside a convex polygon $M$, a circle of the largest possible radius $R$ is placed (this means that inside $M$ it is impossible to place a circle of a larger radius). It is known that inside it is possible to rotate a segment of length 1 by any angle (i.e., we can move a unit segment as a rigid rod on the plane in such a way that it does not go beyond the boundaries of the polygon $M$ and at the same time can be rotated by any given angle). Prove that $R \geq 1 / 3$

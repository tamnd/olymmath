Do there exist real numbers $a, b, c, d$ such that the conditions:

a) the equation $a x^{2}+b d x+c=0$ has real and distinct roots $x_{1}, x_{2}$,

b) the equation $b x^{2}+c d x+a=0$ has real and distinct roots $x_{2}, x_{3}$,

c) the equation $c x^{2}+a d x+b=0$ has real and distinct roots $x_{3}, x_{1}$, hold simultaneously.

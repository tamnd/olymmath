These lines divide the triangle into six parts, three of which are triangles with areas $S_{1}, S_{2}$, and $S_{3}$. Prove that the area of triangle $A B C$ is equal to $\left(\sqrt{S_{1}}+\sqrt{S_{2}}+\sqrt{S_{3}}\right)^{2}$

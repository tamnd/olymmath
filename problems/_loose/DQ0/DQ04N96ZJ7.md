Let $n$ be a natural number, and $n \geqslant 4$. Suppose the maximum number of acute angles in a convex $n$-gon is $M$, and the minimum number of acute angles is $m$; then the value of $M+m$ is
(A) 3.
(B) 4.
(C) A natural number greater than 4.
(D) Cannot be determined.
(Shanghai Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

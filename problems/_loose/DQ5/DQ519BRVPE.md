Let $S_{1}$ be the sequence of natural numbers $1,2,3,4,5, \ldots$. We define the sequence $S_{n+1}$ ( $n=1,2, \ldots$ ) using the sequence $S_{n}$, by increasing those terms of the sequence $S_{n}$ that are divisible by $n$ by 1. Thus, for example, $S_{2}$ is the sequence $2,3,4,5,6, \ldots ., S_{3}$ is the sequence 3 , $3,5,5,7,7, \ldots$ Prove that in the sequence $S_{n}$ the first $n-1$ terms are equal to $p$ if and only if $n$ is a prime number.

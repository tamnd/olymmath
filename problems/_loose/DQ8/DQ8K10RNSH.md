Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$. We denote $O$ as the center of its circumcircle. Let $D$ be a point on $[\mathrm{BC}]$. The line parallel to (AB) passing through $\mathrm{D}$ intersects $(\mathrm{AC})$ at $\mathrm{E}$. The line parallel to $(\mathrm{AC})$ passing through $\mathrm{D}$ intersects $(\mathrm{AB})$ at $\mathrm{F}$. Show that $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{E}, \mathrm{O}, \mathrm{F}$ are concyclic.

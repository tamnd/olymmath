Among four real numbers, if the sum of any three numbers is not less than the other number, then the following statement must be false:
(A) There cannot be only one non-zero number.
(B) All four numbers can be positive.
(C) There are two negative numbers.
(D) If there is a zero, there are no negative numbers.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

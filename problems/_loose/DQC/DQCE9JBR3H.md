Prove that for any natural number $n$ the number

$$
n-\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]-\left[\frac{n}{2^{2}}\right]-\left[\frac{n}{2^{3}}\right]-\ldots
$$

is equal to the sum of the digits in the binary representation of $n$.

See also problems $19.5,33.5$.

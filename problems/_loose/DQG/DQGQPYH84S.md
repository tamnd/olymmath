On a plane, there are $2 n(n>1, n \in \mathbf{N})$ points with no three points collinear. Line segments are drawn between any two points, and any $n^{2}+1$ of these line segments are colored red. Prove: There are at least $n$ triangles with all three sides red.
(2017, National High School Mathematics League Jiangsu Province Re-test)

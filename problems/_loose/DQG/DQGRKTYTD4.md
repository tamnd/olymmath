Let $k, n$ be natural numbers, $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n ; x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{k}$ be $k$ positive real numbers, and their sum equals their product. Prove that $x_{1}^{n-1}\left|x_{2}^{n-1}\right| \cdots+x_{k}^{n-1} \geqslant k n$, and determine the necessary and sufficient conditions for equality in the inequality.

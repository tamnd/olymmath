Prove that there are finitely many positive integers $n$ such that there exists a subset $S$ of $\{1,2,\ldots ,n\}$ satisfying the following conditions:

- $S$ has at least $\lfloor \sqrt{n}\rfloor +1$ elements

- For any $x,y\in S$, $xy$ is a perfect power, i.e. $xy$ is of the form $a^b$ for positive integers $a$ and $b\ge 2$.

In the city of the future, super trains are used for the transportation of residents. They consist of cars with a different number of compartments, and each of them has a certain number of double seats. The first car has only one compartment with one double seat. To it, a car is attached which has one more compartment in which there are two more double seats than in the first compartment, and so on. Each subsequent car is increased by one compartment with two more double seats than in the previous compartment. How many passengers can fit in the $n$-th car? How many passengers can fit in the 10th car?

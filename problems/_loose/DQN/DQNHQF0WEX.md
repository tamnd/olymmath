Draw a square $A B C D$ with $A B=4 \mathrm{~cm}$!

Then draw a translation arrow $\overrightarrow{P Q}$, which is $5 \mathrm{~cm}$ long and parallel to the line through $A$ and $C$ in the direction from $A$ to $C$!

Construct the image $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ of the square $A B C D$ under the translation $\overrightarrow{P Q}$!

A construction description is not required.

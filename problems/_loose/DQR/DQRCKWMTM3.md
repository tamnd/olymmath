Let $\varepsilon$ be a primitive $n$-th root of unity. Prove that

$$
1+\varepsilon^{k}+\varepsilon^{2 k}+\ldots+\varepsilon^{(n-1) k}= \begin{cases}0 & \text { if } 1 \leqslant k \leqslant n-1 \\ n & \text { if } k=n\end{cases}
$$

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{1993}$ and $b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}, \ldots, b_{1993}, p$ and $q$ be non-zero real numbers, and let

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\ldots+a_{1993}^{2}=p^{2} \\
& b_{1}^{2}+b_{2}^{2}+\ldots+b_{1993}^{2}=q^{2} \\
& a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+\ldots+a_{1993} b_{1993}=p q
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that

$$
\frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}=\frac{a_{2}}{b_{2}}=\ldots=\frac{a_{1993}}{b_{1993}}
$$

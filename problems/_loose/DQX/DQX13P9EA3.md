Given $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \varphi \in \mathbf{R}$, and satisfying $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\sin \alpha+7 \sin \beta=4(\sin \gamma+2 \sin \varphi) \\ \cos \alpha+7 \cos \beta=4(\cos \gamma+2 \cos \varphi)\end{array}\right.$,

Prove: $2 \cos (\alpha-\varphi)=7 \cos (\beta-\gamma)$.

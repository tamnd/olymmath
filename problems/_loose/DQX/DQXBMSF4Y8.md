Three circles have a common point $M$ and intersect each other in pairs at points $P, Q, R$. Through an arbitrary point $A$ on one of the circles, lying on the arc $P Q$ that does not contain the point $M$, and the points $P$ and $Q$ where the circle intersects the other two circles, lines are drawn intersecting these same two circles at points $B$ and $C$. Prove that the points $B, C$, and $R$ lie on the same line.

For triangles $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, it holds that

$$
|A B|=\left|A^{\prime} B^{\prime}\right|, |A C|=\left|A^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right|, |\nless B A C|+\left|\nless B^{\prime} A^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right|=180^{\circ} .
$$

Show that the size of the angle formed by side $B C$ and the median from vertex $A$ is the same as the size of the angle formed by side $B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ and the median from vertex $A^{\prime}$.

(Patrik Bak)

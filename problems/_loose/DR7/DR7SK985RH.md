Through the vertex $B$ of the parallelogram $A B C D$, a secant is drawn, intersecting the extensions of sides $D A$ and $D C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that the sum of segments $P A$ and $C Q$ will be the smallest if each of these segments is equal to $\sqrt{a b}$ (where $a$ and $b$ are the lengths of the sides of the parallelogram).

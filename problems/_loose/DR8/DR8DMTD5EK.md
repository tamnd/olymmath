Twenty knights put on twenty cloaks, and each cloak turned out to be too short. Then the knights, taking off their cloaks, lined up by height. The tallest knight took the longest cloak, the second took the longest cloak from the remaining ones, and so on. The knight of the smallest height took the shortest cloak. Prove that in this case, each knight's cloak will still be too short.

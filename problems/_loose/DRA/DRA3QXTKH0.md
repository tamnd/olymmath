Let $n$ be a positive integer and $p$ a prime number $p > n + 1$.
Prove that the following equation does not have integer solution $$1 + \frac{x}{n + 1} + \frac{x^2}{2n + 1} + ...+ \frac{x^p}{pn + 1} = 0$$

Due to the spread of the internet and rumors, the shaman had to lower the prices for his services. For the tribe living in the valley, the price "in furs" was reduced by $10 \%$ and "in fish" by $15 \%$. The ratio of the prices of furs and fish in the local market is constant. For the tribe on the hill, the price "in furs" was reduced by $20 \%$. By how many percent did the price of services "in fish" decrease for this tribe? Round the answer to one decimal place.

Let $0 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant 1, i=1,2, \cdots, n, n \geqslant 2$. Prove: there exists $i$ such that:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
1 \leqslant i \leqslant n-1 \text { and } \\
x_{i}\left(1-x_{i+1}\right) \geqslant \frac{1}{4} x_{1}\left(1-x_{n}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

Given: $\boldsymbol{A}, \boldsymbol{B}, \boldsymbol{C}$ - angles of a certain triangle. Prove:

$\sin (2 n+1) A+\sin (2 n+1) B+\sin (2 n+1) C=(-1)^{n} \cdot 4 \cos \frac{2 n+1}{2} A \times \cos \frac{2 n+1}{2} B \cdot \cos \frac{2 n+1}{2} C$, where $n-$ is an integer.

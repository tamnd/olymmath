Given $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ are positive numbers, $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \cdots, \lambda_{n}$ are real numbers, and $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=1, 0<\lambda_{1} \leqslant \lambda_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant \lambda_{n}$, prove that $\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}}{\lambda_{i}}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} \lambda_{i}\right) \leqslant \frac{\left(\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{n}\right)^{2}}{4 \lambda_{1} \lambda_{n}}$.

a) Show that $\operatorname{Imf}=(0,2]$.

b) Study the bijectivity of the function $\bar{f}:[1, \infty] \rightarrow(0,2], \bar{f}(x)=\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{x}),(\forall) \mathrm{x} \geq 1$.

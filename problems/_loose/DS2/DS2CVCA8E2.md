A correspondence mathematical competition takes place in three rounds, whose difficulty increases. Only those solvers who were successful in the first round advance to the second round, and only successful solvers of the second round advance to the third round. The winner is anyone who is a successful solver of the last, i.e., the third round. In the last year of this competition, exactly $14 \%$ of the solvers were successful in the first round, exactly $25 \%$ of the solvers of the second round advanced to the third round, and exactly $8 \%$ of the solvers of the third round won.

What is the smallest number of participants who could have taken part in the first round? How many winners would there be in such a case?

(M. Petrová)

Idea. All intermediate results must be natural numbers.

Given

the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$
has an eccentricity of $\frac{1}{2}, F$ is the right focus, $A(-a, 0),|A F|$
$=3$. Let $O$ be the origin, $P$ be a point on the ellipse $C$, $M$ be the midpoint of $A P$, the line $O M$ intersects $x=4$ at point $D$, and the line through point $O$ parallel to $A P$ intersects $x=4$ at point $E$. Prove:
$$
\angle O D F=\angle O E F .
$$

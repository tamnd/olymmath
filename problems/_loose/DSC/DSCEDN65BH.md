A country has $N$ cities. Some cities are connected by direct two-way air routes. It is known that for any $k(2 \leqslant k \leqslant N)$, the number of air routes between any $k$ cities does not exceed $2 k-2$. Prove: All air routes can be assigned to two airlines such that the routes owned by any one airline do not form a closed polygon.

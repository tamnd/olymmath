An infinite sequence of positive real numbers $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ has the following property: $x_{0}$ $=1, x_{i+1} \leqslant x_{i}(i \geqslant 0)$.
(1) Prove that for any sequence with the above property, there exists an $n \geqslant 1$ such that the following inequality holds:
$$
\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}} \geqslant 3.999 \text {; }
$$
(2) Find a sequence such that the following inequality $\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}}<4$ holds for any $n$.

In a non-isosceles triangle with side lengths $a, b, c$ being integers, the following relationship holds. What is the smallest height of the triangle?

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{c}-(a-c)^{2}=\frac{b^{2}}{c}-(b-c)^{2}
$$

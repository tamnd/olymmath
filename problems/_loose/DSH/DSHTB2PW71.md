For any integer $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, we denote $\ell(n)$ as the least common multiple of all integers $k \leqslant n$ and

$\hat{\ell}(n)$ as the product $n\binom{2 n}{n}$. Show that $\hat{\ell}(n)$ divides $\ell(2 n)$ and that, for $n \geqslant 4, \hat{\ell}(n) \geqslant 2^{2 n}$. Deduce that $\ell(n) \geqslant 2^{n}$ for all integers $n \geqslant 7$.

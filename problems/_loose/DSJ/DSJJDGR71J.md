Given any $2n+3$ points in a plane $(n \geqslant 1)$, no three of which are collinear and no four of which are concyclic, a circle can be drawn through any three of these points such that the remaining $2n$ points are exactly $n$ points inside the circle and $n$ points outside the circle. Let the number of such circles be $k$. Prove: $k>\frac{1}{\pi} C_{2 n+3}^{2}$.
(28th IMO Preliminary Problem)

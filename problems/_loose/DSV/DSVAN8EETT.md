Consider the isosceles triangle $A B C$ with $m(\angle B A C)=120^{\circ}$. Outside of it, construct the obtuse isosceles triangle $A B E$ with base $[A B]$. If $M$ is the midpoint of segment $[A B]$, $F$ is the symmetric point of $M$ with respect to $B C$, and $A F \cap B C=\{P\}$, the following are required:

a) Prove that points $P, M$, and $E$ are collinear.

b) Given that $M P=a(\mathrm{~cm})$, and $E M \cap A C=\{D\}$, prove that $[A F] \equiv[M D]$.

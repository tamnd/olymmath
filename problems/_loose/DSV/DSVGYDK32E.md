To protect valuable equipment from bad weather, a tent in the shape of a pyramid is required, with a rectangle as its base, and one of the lateral edges should be perpendicular to the base. Find the maximum possible volume of the tent, given that no edge of the pyramid should be longer than 2 meters.

$8 /(9 \sqrt{3})$.

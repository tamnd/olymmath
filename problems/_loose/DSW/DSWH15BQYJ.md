An athlete runs $5000 \mathrm{~m}$ per week on a sports field, which has a short track and a long track. In a certain week, he trained six days, each day running once on the long track and twice on the short track. In the following week, he trained seven days, each day running once on each track. We can then state that:

(a) the long track is $500 \mathrm{~m}$ longer than the short one;

(b) the long track is four times longer than the short one;

(c) the long track is five times longer than the short one;

(d) the long track is $600 \mathrm{~m}$ longer than the short one;

(e) the long track is three times longer than the short one.

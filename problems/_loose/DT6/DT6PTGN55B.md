\section*{Problem 3B - 281043B}

Assume that among 13 otherwise arbitrary points in a plane, it is always true that among any three of these 13 points, there are always two whose distance from each other is less than $1 \mathrm{~cm}$.

a) Prove that from this assumption it always follows:

There is a circle with a radius of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ whose interior contains seven of the 13 points. b) Investigate whether from this assumption it always follows:

There is a circle with a radius of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ whose interior contains eight of the 13 points.

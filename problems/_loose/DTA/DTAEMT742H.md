The story I am about to tell in this problem is truly amazing. Once I saw two caskets, a golden one and a silver one, and I wanted to find out whether at least one of them was made by Bellini. After reading the inscription on one of the caskets, I could not deduce from it that at least one of the two caskets that caught my attention was made by Bellini. Imagine my surprise when, looking at the inscription that adorned the lid of the other casket, I found that it was no different from the first inscription. But even greater amazement overcame me when it turned out that, having read the second inscription, I could assert with complete certainty: "Both caskets are the work of Bellini."

What inscriptions, in your opinion, could have adorned the lids of the caskets?

The sequence of non-negative integers $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{1997}$ satisfies $c_{1} \geq 0$ and $c_{m}+c_{n} \leq c_{m+n} \leq c_{m}+c_{n}+1$ for all $\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{n}>0$ with $\mathrm{m}+\mathrm{n}<1998$. Show that there is a real $\mathrm{k}$ such that $\mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{n}}=[\mathrm{nk}]$ for $1 \leq \mathrm{n} \leq 1997$.

At the Mathematics Olympiad, three students meet. "Have you noticed," one of them, a girl, asks, that we are called Long, Fat, and Thin, and that exactly one of us is exceptionally tall, another is notably fat, and the third is alarmingly thin?"

"Yes, indeed," replies another, the thin student, "but none of us has a name that matches the characteristic that describes the respective student." "You are absolutely right!" agrees the student named Long.

According to this, what is the name of the tall student, the name of the fat student, and the name of the thin student, given that the girl who asked the question is not fat? Which of the characteristic traits and which name does the girl have?

Prove: If $a$ and $b$ are any integers, with only $b \neq 0$ assumed, then the number

$$
z=a^{5}+3 a^{4} b-5 a^{3} b^{2}-15 a^{2} b^{3}+4 a b^{4}+12 b^{5}
$$

is the product of five integers, no two of which are equal!

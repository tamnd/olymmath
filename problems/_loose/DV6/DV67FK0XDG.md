We write the decimal expressions of $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{3}$ as $$\sqrt{2}=1.a_1a_2a_3\dots \sqrt{3}=1.b_1b_2b_3\dots$$ where each $a_i$ or $b_i$ is a digit between 0 and 9. Prove that there exist at least 1000 values of $i$ between $1$ and $10^{1000}$ such that $a_i\neq b_i$ .

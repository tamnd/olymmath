On the sides $A B, B C$ and $C A$ of triangle $A B C$, points $C_{1}, A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ are taken such that $\left|A C_{1}\right|:\left|C_{1} B\right|=\left|B A_{1}\right|:\left|A_{1} C\right|=\left|C B_{1}\right|:\left|B_{1} A\right|=k$. On the sides $A_{1} B_{1}$, $B_{1} C_{1}$ and $C_{1} A_{1}$, points $A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ are taken such that $\left|A_{1} C_{2}\right|:\left|C_{2} B_{1}\right|=\left|B_{1} A_{2}\right|:\left|A_{2} C_{1}\right|=\left|C_{1} B_{2}\right|:\left|B_{2} A_{1}\right|=1 / k$. Prove that $\triangle A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ is similar to $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, and find the similarity coefficient.

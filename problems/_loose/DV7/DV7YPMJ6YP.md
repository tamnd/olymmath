Let $A B C$ be a triangle, $M$ the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$, and $D$ the foot of the altitude from $B$ in triangle $A B C$. Let $P$ be the point of intersection, other than $A$, of the line $(AM)$ with the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$. Let $Q$ be the point such that the quadrilateral $B C A Q$ is a parallelogram. Show that the points $\mathrm{Q}, \mathrm{P}, \mathrm{D}$, and $A$ are concyclic.

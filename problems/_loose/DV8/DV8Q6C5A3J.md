In Northern Venice, mayoral elections were held in which all city residents participated. The winning candidate received more than half of the votes, with $96 \%$ of the politically illiterate population and $4 \%$ of the politically literate population voting for him. Prove that if $40 \%$ of the literate population had not come to vote, then this candidate would have collected more than $60 \%$ of the votes (from the number of those who came).

Five sailors landed on an island and by evening had gathered a pile of coconuts. They postponed the division until morning. One of them, waking up at night, gave one coconut to a monkey, and from the remaining coconuts took exactly one fifth for himself, after which he went back to sleep and quickly fell asleep. Each of the other sailors did the same during the night, one after another; each was unaware of the actions of their predecessors. In the morning, they divided the remaining coconuts equally, but this time there was no extra coconut for the monkey. What could be the smallest number of coconuts in the original pile?

In a triangle $ABC$, the angles are $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and the centroid is $S$. The angles $BSC = \alpha'$, $CSA = \beta'$, and $ASB = \gamma'$. Prove that

$$
3 \operatorname{cotg} \alpha' = \operatorname{cotg} \alpha - 2 \operatorname{cotg} \beta - 2 \operatorname{cotg} \gamma
$$

and consequently,

$$
\operatorname{cotg} \alpha' + \operatorname{cotg} \beta' + \operatorname{cotg} \gamma' = (\operatorname{cotg} \alpha + \operatorname{cotg} \beta + \operatorname{cotg} \gamma)
$$

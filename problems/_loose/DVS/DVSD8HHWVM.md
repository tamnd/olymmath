Let's call a Transylvanian reliable if they are either a sane human or an insane vampire, and unreliable if they are either an insane human or a sane vampire. Reliable Transylvanians always make true statements, while unreliable ones make false statements (either due to mental disorder or delusion).

Suppose you ask a Transylvanian: "Are you reliable?" They answer you either "yes" or "no." Can you conclude from the answer whether your interlocutor is a human or not a vampire? Can you determine whether they are sane?

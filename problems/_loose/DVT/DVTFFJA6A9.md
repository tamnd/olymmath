Construct any segment $D S$, then construct a circle $k$ with center at point $S$, which passes through point $D$.

1. Construct an equilateral triangle $D A S$, whose vertex $A$ lies on the circle $k$.
2. Construct an equilateral triangle $A B C$, whose vertices $B$ and $C$ also lie on the circle $k$.

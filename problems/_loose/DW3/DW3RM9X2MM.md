Find the least positive integer number $n(n \geq 3)$ with the following property: for any colouring of $n$ different points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ on a line and such that $A_{1} A_{2}=A_{2} A_{3}=\ldots=$ $A_{n-1} A_{n}$ in two colours, there are three points $A_{i}, A_{j}, A_{2 j-i}(1 \leq i<$ $2 j-i \leq n$ ) which have the same colour.

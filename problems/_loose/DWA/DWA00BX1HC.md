Given 2011 non-zero integers. It is known that the sum of any one of them with the product of the remaining 2010 numbers is negative. Prove that if all the given numbers are arbitrarily divided into two groups and the numbers in the groups are multiplied, then the sum of the two resulting products will also be negative.

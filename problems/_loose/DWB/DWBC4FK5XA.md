The lines $A P, B P$, and $C P$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ at points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$. Prove that triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ is equilateral.

a) Let $0<\alpha<1$ and $x \geqslant 0$. Prove that $x^{\alpha}-\alpha x \leqslant 1-\alpha$.

b) Let $a, b, p$ and $q$ be positive numbers, with $\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1$. Prove that $a b \leqslant \frac{1}{p} a^{p}+\frac{1}{q} b^{q}$.

After removing the largest positive integer, the arithmetic mean of the remaining elements is 32; after removing the smallest positive integer, the arithmetic mean of the remaining elements is 35; if the largest positive integer is added back, the arithmetic mean of the elements is 40. It is known that the largest positive integer in set $S$ is 72 more than the smallest positive integer. Then the arithmetic mean of all elements in set $S$ is ( ).
(A) 36.2
(B) 36.4
(C) 36.6
(D) 36.8
(E) 37

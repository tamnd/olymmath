For an isosceles trapezoid $A B C D$ with $A B \| C D$, it is given that $B C=C D=D A=a$ and $A B>a$.

a) Prove that the diagonal $A C$ bisects the interior angle $\angle D A B$ of the trapezoid!

b) Calculate the length of $A B$ for the case where $\angle D A B=60^{\circ}$!

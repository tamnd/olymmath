The first monkey was fed only oranges and bananas, with bananas being $40 \%$ more than oranges. The second monkey was fed only bananas and coconuts, with coconuts being $25 \%$ more than bananas. The third monkey was fed only coconuts and oranges, with oranges being twice as many as coconuts. The monkeys ate all the fruits brought.

Let the first monkey eat $a$ oranges, and the third monkey eat $b$ oranges. Find $a / b$.

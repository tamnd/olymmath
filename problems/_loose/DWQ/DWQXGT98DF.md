Suppose a regular 1968-gon is given in the plane. Two players, A and B, take turns connecting the vertices of the polygon with line segments according to the following rules: if at least one of the two vertices has already been connected to another vertex, then these two vertices cannot be connected; a line segment connecting two points must not intersect any existing line segments. The player who cannot continue to connect vertices according to these rules loses. Who has a winning strategy, A or B, and how should they connect the vertices?

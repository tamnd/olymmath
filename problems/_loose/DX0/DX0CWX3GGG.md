In Rt $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C$ is the right angle, and for any point $P$ inside the triangle, the projections of $P$ on the three sides $A B, B C, A C$ are $D, E, F$ respectively. Let $B E=a_{1}, E C=a_{2}, C F=b_{1}, F A=b_{2}, A D=c_{1}, D B=$ $c_{2}$. Prove: (1) $a a_{1}+b b_{1}+c c_{1}=c^{2}$; (2) $a\left(a_{1}-a_{2}\right)+b\left(b_{1}-b_{2}\right)+c\left(c_{1}-c_{2}\right)=$ 0.

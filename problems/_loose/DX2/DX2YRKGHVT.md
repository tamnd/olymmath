A ball falls from a height of $h$ and, after elastic bounces on a horizontal surface, comes to a stop after a time $t$ from the moment of release. We assume that with each collision, a certain fraction of the ball's mechanical energy is converted into non-mechanical energy. What is this loss? The duration of the collisions can be neglected. (Let $h=20 \mathrm{~cm}$, $t=18 \mathrm{~s}$, and $g=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$.)

A line parallel to a diagonal is drawn through a point on a side of a quadrilateral, up to its intersection with the adjacent side of the quadrilateral. Through the resulting point, a line parallel to the other diagonal is drawn, and so on. Prove that the fifth point obtained in this way will coincide with the original point.

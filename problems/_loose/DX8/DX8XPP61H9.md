There are $r$ balls numbered from 1 to $r$ and $n$ boxes numbered from 1 to $n$, where $r \geqslant n$. Now the balls are placed into the boxes, with no limit on the capacity of each box. Find the number of ways $f(r, n)$ such that no box is empty. If the $n$ boxes are unnumbered and indistinguishable, still find the number of ways $s(r, n)$ such that no box is empty.

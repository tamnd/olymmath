Jura has sticks of lengths $2 \mathrm{~cm}, 3 \mathrm{~cm}, 3 \mathrm{~cm}, 3 \mathrm{~cm}, 4 \mathrm{~cm}, 5 \mathrm{~cm}, 5 \mathrm{~cm}, 5 \mathrm{~cm}, 6 \mathrm{~cm}, 6 \mathrm{~cm}$, and $9 \mathrm{~cm}$. She assembles them into the sides of triangles such that no stick is part of the side of two or more triangles. She can use as many sticks as she wants, but she must not break them, and the used sticks must lie entirely on the boundary of the triangle. Jura claims that it is possible to use the sticks to form the sides of three triangles with the same perimeter. Is she right? What is the largest possible perimeter that these triangles could have?

For a positive integer $n$ , $\sigma(n)$ denotes the sum of the positive divisors of $n$ . Determine $$ \limsup\limits_{n\rightarrow \infty} \frac{\sigma(n^{2023})}{(\sigma(n))^{2023}} $$ **Note:** Given a sequence ( $a_n$ ) of real numbers, we say that $\limsup\limits_{n\rightarrow \infty} a_n = +\infty$ if ( $a_n$ ) is not upper bounded, and, otherwise, $\limsup\limits_{n\rightarrow \infty} a_n$ is the smallest constant $C$ such that, for every real $K > C$ , there is a positive integer $N$ with $a_n < K$ for every $n > N$ .

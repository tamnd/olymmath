A square with one side of length $2 a$ is always located in the first quadrant of the $x O y$ plane. As the square moves, two of its adjacent vertices always remain on the $x$-axis and $y$-axis, respectively. Prove that the trajectory of points inside or on the boundary of the square is usually a quadratic curve (a part of it). For which points of the square would such a trajectory degenerate?

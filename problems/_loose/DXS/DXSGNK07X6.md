For the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$, tangents are drawn from point $P$ in the plane. The direction determinants of these, $m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$, satisfy the equation

$$
m_{1} m_{2}^{2}+m_{1}^{2} m_{2}=k
$$

where $k$ is a given constant. What is the geometric locus of point $P$?

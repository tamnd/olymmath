Dante's house is located at point $D$ at the foot of a conical mountain with a base diameter of $4 \mathrm{~km}$ and the summit at point $C$. It is known that $D$ is $4 \mathrm{~km}$ away from $C$ in a straight line and that, if $P$ is the point diametrically opposite to $D$ on the base of the mountain, the gate of Hell is located at $3 / 4$ of the segment $C P$, closer to $P$. What is the minimum distance Dante must travel (walking on the slopes of the mountain) to reach the gate of Hell from his house?
(A) $\pi+1 \mathrm{~km}$
(B) $5 \mathrm{~km}$
(C) $2 \pi \mathrm{~km}$
(D) $7 \mathrm{~km}$
(E) $2 \pi+1 \mathrm{~km}$.

Let $A$ and $B$ be positive numbers, and $p$ and $q$ be rational numbers related by the equation $1 / p + 1 / q = 1$. Prove that if $p > 1$, then $A^{1 / p} B^{1 / q} \leqslant A / p + B / q$, and if $p < 1$, then $A^{1 / p} B^{1 / q} \geqslant A / p + B / q$. (If $A \neq B$, then both inequalities are strict.)

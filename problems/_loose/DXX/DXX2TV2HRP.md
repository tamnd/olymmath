In the left bottom cell of a $100 \times 100$ board, a chip is placed. Alternating between horizontal and vertical moves to an adjacent cell (the first move being horizontal), it first reaches the left top cell, and then the right top cell. Prove that there will be two such cells $\mathrm{~A}$ and $B$, such that the chip made a move from $\mathrm{~A}$ to $B$ at least twice.

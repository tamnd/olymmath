If the graph of the quadratic function $y=a x^{2}+b x+c(a \neq 0)$ has its vertex in the first quadrant, and passes through the points $(0,1)$ and $(-1,0)$, then the range of values for $S=a+b+c$ is
(A) $0<S<1$.
(B) $0<S<2$.
(C) $1<S<2$.
(D) $-1<S<1$.
(Shaanxi Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1997)

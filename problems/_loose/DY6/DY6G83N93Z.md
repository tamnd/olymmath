Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $O$ the center of the circumcircle, $I$ the center of the incircle, $I_{a}, I_{b}, I_{c}$ the centers of the excircles, $S$ the reflection of the orthocenter with respect to $O$. Let $D, E, F$ be the points of tangency of the excircles with $(BC), (CA), (AB)$. We know that $(AD), (BE), (CF)$ are concurrent at a point called the Nagel point $N$.

Show that $I_{a} I_{b} I_{c}$ and $S I N$ have the same centroid.

Prove that for every real solution $x$ of the equation $x^{2}+p x+q=0$ (where $p$ and $q$ are real numbers) the following holds:

$$
x \geq \frac{4 q-(p+\lambda)^{2}}{4 \lambda}
$$

where $\lambda$ is an arbitrary positive number.

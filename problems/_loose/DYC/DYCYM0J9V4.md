On the lateral sides $K L$ and $M N$ of an isosceles trapezoid $K L M N$, points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen respectively such that the segment $P Q$ is parallel to the bases of the trapezoid. It is known that a circle can be inscribed in each of the trapezoids $K P Q N$ and $P L M Q$, and the radii of these circles are $R$ and $r$ respectively. Determine the bases $|L M|$ and $|K N|$.

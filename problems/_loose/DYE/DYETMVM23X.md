On an infinitely large grid paper, there are $n$ cells that have been colored black. Try to prove that it is possible to cut out a finite number of squares from this grid paper, such that they satisfy the following two conditions:
(1) All black cells are within the squares that have been cut out;
(2) In any of the squares that have been cut out, the area of the black cells is not less than $\frac{1}{5}$ of the area of the square and not more than $\frac{4}{5}$ of the area of the square.

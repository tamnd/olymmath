Given $n^{2}$ points arranged in the plane in the form of a square grid $n \times n$. A broken line of length $l$ is the union of closed segments $A_{0} A_{1}$, $A_{1} A_{2}$, $A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots, A_{l-1} A_{l}$ in that plane. Determine the smallest natural number $l$ for which there exists a broken line of length $l$ on which all $n^{2}$ observed points lie.

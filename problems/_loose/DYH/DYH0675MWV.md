Given the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ intersects the hyperbola $y=-\frac{1}{x}$ at point $R$, and the common tangent line of the parabola and the hyperbola touches the parabola and the hyperbola at points $S, T$ respectively.
Prove: For any positive real number $p$, the area of $\triangle R S T$ is a constant independent of $p$, and find this constant.

Let $\triangle A B C$ be a triangle. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $B C$ and let $D$ be a point on the interior of side $A B$. The intersection of $A M$ and $C D$ we call $E$. Suppose that $|A D|=|D E|$. Prove that $|A B|=|C E|$.

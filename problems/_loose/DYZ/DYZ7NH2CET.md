The $A$ and $B$ are second-order square matrices that satisfy the following equations:

$$
A B=A^{2} B^{2}-(A B)^{2} \text { and } \operatorname{det}(B)=2
$$

a) Prove that the matrix $A$ is not invertible!

b) Calculate the difference $\operatorname{det}(A+2 B)-\operatorname{det}(B+2 A)$!

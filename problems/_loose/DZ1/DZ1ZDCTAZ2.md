Let $a$ be a fixed real number and let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function that admits primitives and does not vanish. Show that the function $g: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, given by $g(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{rl}f(x), & x \leq a \\ 2 f(x), & x>a\end{array}\right.$, does not have primitives.

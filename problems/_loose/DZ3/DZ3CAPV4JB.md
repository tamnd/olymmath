In an infinitely large grid paper, two players, A and B, play a game. Starting with A, the two players take turns marking 1 node on the grid paper, ensuring that all nodes marked by both players are vertices of some convex polygon (starting from A's second marking). If it is a player's turn and they cannot mark a node according to the rule, they lose. Who can win if both players choose their points correctly?

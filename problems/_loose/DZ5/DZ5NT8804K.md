Perpendiculars dropped from an internal point of an equilateral triangle to its sides, and segments connecting this point with the vertices, divide the triangle into six right triangles.

Prove that the sum of the areas of three of them, taken every other one, is equal to the sum of the areas of the three remaining ones.

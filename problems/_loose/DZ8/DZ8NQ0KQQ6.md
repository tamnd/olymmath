Given the three sides of an obtuse triangle are $3, 4, x$, then the range of $x$ is
(A) $1<x<7$.
(B) $5<x<7$.
(C) $1<x<\sqrt{7}$.
(D) $5<x<7$ or $1<x<\sqrt{7}$.
(6th "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1989)

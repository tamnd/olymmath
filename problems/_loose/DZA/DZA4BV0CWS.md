a) Let $A \in M_{2}(\mathbb{C})$. Show that $\operatorname{det}\left(A-x I_{2}\right)=x^{2}-(\operatorname{Tr} A) x+\operatorname{det} A$, for any $x \in \mathbb{C}$.

b) If $A \in M_{2}(\mathbb{C})$ with $\operatorname{Tr} A=2$ and $\operatorname{det} A=3$, prove that:

$$
2 \cdot \operatorname{det}\left(A^{2}+I_{2}\right)-\operatorname{det}\left(A^{2}+3 I_{2}\right)=4
$$

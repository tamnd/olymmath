Given a triangle $ABC$, with centroid $G$.

a) Prove that for any point in the plane $M$ the following holds:

$\overline{M A}^{2}+\overline{M B}^{2}+\overline{M C}^{2} \geq \overline{G A}^{2}+\overline{G B}^{2}+\overline{G C}^{2}$, with equality if and only if $M=G$

b) For a fixed number $k>\overline{G A}^{2}+\overline{G B}^{2}+\overline{G C}^{2}$, find the geometric locus of points $M$ such that

$$
\overline{M A}^{2}+\overline{M B}^{2}+\overline{M C}^{2}=k
$$

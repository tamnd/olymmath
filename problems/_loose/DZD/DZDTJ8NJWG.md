Given a quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ be the points of intersection of the altitudes of triangles $B C D, A C D$ and $A B D$. Prove that the perpendiculars dropped from $A, B$ and $C$ to the lines $B_{1} C_{1}$, $C_{1} A_{1}$ and $A_{1} B_{1}$ respectively, intersect at one point.

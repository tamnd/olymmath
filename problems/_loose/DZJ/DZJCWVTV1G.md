Let KP denote the 64 points in space whose three coordinates are 1, 2, 3, or 4. Kata and Péter play a game of spatial amoeba on the points of KP. Kata starts the game by selecting any point from KP and coloring it blue. In the second move, Péter selects a point different from the previous one and colors it red. Then they take turns coloring a previously uncolored point of KP blue or red, respectively. The one who first colors four points, lying on a straight line, in their own color wins. Show that it makes no difference to Kata whether she colors the point $(1,1,2)$ or the point $(2,2,1)$ blue in her first move.

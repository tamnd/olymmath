In a circumscribed quadrilateral $ABCD$, the points of tangency on sides $AB, BC, CD, DA$ are $P, Q, R, S$ respectively. The extensions of $AD$ and $BC$ meet at point $E$, and the extensions of $AB$ and $DC$ meet at point $F$. Prove: (I) $AC, BD, PR, QS$ are concurrent; (II) $AC, EF, PQ, RS$ are concurrent; (III) $BD, EF, PS, QR$ are concurrent (assuming $BD \neq EF$).

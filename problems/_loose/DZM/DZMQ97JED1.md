In the triangle $ABC$, the angle bisector from $C$ intersects the side $AB$ at point $D$. A circle is drawn around $D$ with radius $DC$, and the side $CB$ is extended beyond $B$ to a segment $BE = CA$. Show that in this case, the length of the tangent drawn from $E$ to the circle is equal to $AB$. - Can a similar statement be made if we start from the external angle bisector at $C$?

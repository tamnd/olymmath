$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x+2 y+4 z=12, \\
x y+4 y z+2 x z=22, \\
x y z=6 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

The number of ordered triples $(x, y, z)$ is
(A) 0.
(B) 1.
(C) 2.
(D) 4.
(E) 6.

In triangle $ABC$, $\angle B$ is trisected by $BD$ and $BE$, intersecting $AC$ at $D$ and $E$, respectively, then
(A) $\frac{AD}{EC}=\frac{AE}{DC}$.
(B) $\frac{AD}{EC}=\frac{AB}{BC}$.
(C) $\frac{AD}{EC}=\frac{BD}{BE}$.
(D) $\frac{AD}{EC}=\frac{AB \cdot BD}{BE \cdot BC}$.
(E) $\frac{AD}{EC}=\frac{AE \cdot BD}{DC \cdot BE}$.

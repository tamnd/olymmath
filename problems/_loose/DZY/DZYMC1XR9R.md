(42nd Moscow Mathematical Olympiad) Konya and Vitya are playing a game on an infinitely large grid paper. Starting with Konya, they take turns marking nodes, which are the intersections of vertical and horizontal lines on the paper. Each time a node is marked, all previously marked nodes must be vertices of some convex polygon (starting from Konya's second move). The player who cannot continue according to the rules loses. Who, under normal circumstances, can win this game?

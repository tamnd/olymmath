a) A rectangular map of a city is completely covered by a second map of the same city, the scale of which is 5 times larger, and such that their corresponding sides are parallel. Prove that it is possible, without moving the maps, to pierce them with a needle simultaneously so that the point of the puncture indicates the same point in the city on both maps.

b) Prove that this statement remains true even if it is not required that the corresponding sides of the maps be parallel.

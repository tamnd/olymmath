There are 100 points inside a circle (none at the center) and no two points lie on the same radius. (1) Prove that there exists a sector with a central angle of $\frac{2 \pi}{11}$ radians that contains exactly 10 points; (2) Can a sector with a central angle of $\frac{2 \pi}{11}$ radians be found that contains exactly 11 points? (Assume the radius of the sector belongs to the sector)
（16th All-Russian Olympiad Problem）

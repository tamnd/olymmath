For an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $P$ is a point on the extension of $CB$ such that $AB = BP$; $Q$ is a point on the extension of $BC$ such that $AC = CQ$. Let $\odot J$ be the excircle of $\triangle ABC$ opposite to vertex $A$, and it touches the lines $AB$ and $AC$ at points $D$ and $E$, respectively. Suppose the extension of $PD$ intersects the extension of $QE$ at point $F$ (different from point $J$). Prove: $AF \perp FJ$.

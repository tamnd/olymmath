There are $n$ students at a school. It is known that, for any two students $A$ and $B$, either $A$ loves $B$ or $B$ loves $A$ (but not both). (A student may thus be in love with several other students.) A *love triangle* is a configuration of three students $A$, $B$, $C$ such that $A$ loves $B$, $B$ loves $C$ and $C$ loves $A$. What is the maximal number of love triangles, given the number $n$ of students?

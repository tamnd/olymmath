All the supplies were found in the house where the March Hare, the Mad Hatter, and the Dormouse lived. All three were taken into custody and gave the following testimonies in court:

March Hare. The butter was stolen by the Mad Hatter. Mad Hatter. The eggs were stolen by the Dormouse.

Dormouse. I stole the milk.

During the trial, it was revealed that the one who stole the butter told the truth, and the one who stole the eggs lied.

If $(x, y)$ is a solution to the system of equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x y=6 \\
x^{2} y+x y^{2}+x+y=63
\end{array}\right.
$$

then $x^{2}+y^{2}$ equals
(A) 13 .
(B) $\frac{1173}{32}$.
(C) 55 .
(D) 69 .
(E) 81 .
(38th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1987)

Let $\theta$ be an obtuse angle with $\sin{\theta}=\frac{3}{5}$. If an ant starts at the origin and repeatedly moves $1$ unit and turns by an angle of $\theta$, there exists a region $R$ in the plane such that for every point $P\in R$ and every constant $c>0$, the ant is within a distance $c$ of $P$ at some point in time (so the ant gets arbitrarily close to every point in the set). What is the largest possible area of $R$?

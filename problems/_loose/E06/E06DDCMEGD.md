Let $a$ and $S$ be the length of the side and the area of regular triangle inscribed in a circle of radius $1$ . A closed broken line $A_1A_2...A_{51}A_1$ consisting of $51$ segments of the same length $a$ is placed inside the circle. Prove that the sum of areas of the $ 51$ triangles between the neighboring segments $$ A_1A_2A_3, A_2A_3A_4,..., A_{49}A_{50}A_{51}, A_{50}A_{51}A_1, A_{51}A_1A_2 $$ is not less than $3S$ .

(A. Berzinsh, Riga)

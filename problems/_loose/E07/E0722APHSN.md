Triangles $\mathrm{ABC}$ and $\mathrm{ADE}$ are in different planes and share the common median AM. Consider points $\mathrm{P}, \mathrm{Q}, \mathrm{R}, \mathrm{S}$ located respectively on segments [AB], [AC], [AD], [AE] such that $\frac{A P}{P B}=\frac{A Q}{Q D}=\frac{A R}{R C}=\frac{A S}{S E}$.

a) Show that PRQS is a parallelogram.

b) If $\mathrm{AB}^{2}+\mathrm{AC}^{2}=\mathrm{AD}^{2}+\mathrm{AE}^{2}$, then PRQS is a rectangle.

A train departed and after 1 hour it stopped for half an hour due to an incident, then proceeded at $\frac{3}{4}$ of its original speed, resulting in a delay of $3 \frac{1}{2}$ hours. If the incident had occurred 90 miles further ahead, the train would have been only 3 hours late. The total distance of the journey in miles is
(A) 400.
(B) 465.
(C) 600.
(D) 640.
(E) 550.
(5th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1954)

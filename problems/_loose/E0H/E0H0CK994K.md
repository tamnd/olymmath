For each positive integer $k$ , let $S(k)$ be the sum of the digits of $k$ in the decimal system.
Find all positive integers N for which there exist positive integers $a$ , $b$ , $c$ , coprime two by two, such that: $S(ab) = S(bc) = S(ca) = N$ .

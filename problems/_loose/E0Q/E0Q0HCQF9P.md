Four. (50 points) Define a solid angle for each vertex of a convex polyhedron. The supplement of the sum of all dihedral angles at a vertex with respect to $2 \pi$ is called the solid angle of that vertex (for example, the solid angle of a vertex of a cube is $\frac{\pi}{2}$, and the solid angle of a vertex of a regular tetrahedron is $\pi$). Prove: The sum of the solid angles of all vertices of a convex polyhedron is $4 \pi$.

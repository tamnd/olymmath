Let $S$ and $T$ be perpendicular planes whose common line is $m$, and let points $C$ and $D$ lie on $S$ and $T$ respectively. The projections of $C$ and $D$ onto $m$ are $C'$ and $D'$, and $C C' = c$, $D D' = d$, $C' D' = e$. Construct two equal-radius, tangent spheres, one of which touches $S$ at $C$ and the other touches $T$ at $D$.

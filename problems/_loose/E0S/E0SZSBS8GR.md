For the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$ (where $p$ is a prime number not equal to 2), a line $l$ passing through the focus $F$ and not perpendicular to the $x$-axis intersects the parabola at points $M$ and $N$. The perpendicular bisector of segment $M N$ intersects $M N$ at point $P$ and the $x$-axis at point $Q$.
(1) Find the equation of the locus $L$ of the midpoint $R$ of $P Q$;
(2) Prove that there are infinitely many integer points on $L$, but the distance from any integer point on $L$ to the origin is not an integer.

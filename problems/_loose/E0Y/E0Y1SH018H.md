Consider two circles which intersect at points $A$ and $B$; we draw a secant through point $B$, which intersects one circle at $C$ and the other at $D$; we connect these points to point $A$;

What will be the geometric locus of the center $M$ of the circle inscribed in the triangle $ACD$, and the centers $M_{1}, M_{2}$, and $M_{3}$ of the circles that are externally tangent to the same triangle, as the secant rotates around point $B$?

What will be the geometric locus of the centroid $G$ of triangle $ACD$ under the same condition?

Suppose that the variable secant is in the position $C_{1} B D_{1}$ when the area of triangle $ACD$ is maximum. Determine this position and connect $C$ to $C_{1}$ and $D$ to $D_{1}$. What will be the geometric locus of the intersection point $P$ of the lines $CC_{1}$ and $DD_{1}$?

The number is greater than the root.

$$
\sqrt{|8 \sqrt{3}-16|}-\sqrt{8 \sqrt{3}+16}=\sqrt{(2 \sqrt{3}-2)^{2}}-\sqrt{(2 \sqrt{3}+2)^{2}}=-4
$$

$4 x^{2}+21 x+17=0 \Leftrightarrow x=-\frac{17}{4}, x=-1$.

Therefore, $\sqrt{|8 \sqrt{3}-16|}-\sqrt{8 \sqrt{3}+16}$ is greater.

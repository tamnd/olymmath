Given $0<a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{n}$, for any permutation $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, let
$$
M=\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(a_{i}+\frac{1}{b_{i}}\right) .
$$

Find the permutation $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ that maximizes $M$.

There is a light bulb and 10 numbered buttons. It is known that exactly 5 of them are active. In one attempt, you are allowed to press any three buttons simultaneously. If at least one of them is active, the light bulb will turn on. How can you determine in no more than 9 attempts whether the button numbered 1 is active? (When the pressed buttons are released at the end of each attempt, the light bulb always turns off)

Construct a circle $k$ with the following property:

If $AB$ is a diameter of $k$, $g$ is the tangent to $k$ at $B$, and a point $Q$ lies on $g$ such that $\overline{BQ} = 6$ cm, then $k$ intersects the segment $AQ$ at a point $P$ such that $\overline{PQ} = 3$ cm.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the given segments uniquely determine such a circle $k$ up to congruence!

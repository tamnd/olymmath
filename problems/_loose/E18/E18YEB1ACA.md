There are three one-liter jars and a 100 ml measure. The first jar is empty, the second contains 700 ml of sweet tea, and the third contains 800 ml of sweet tea. In the second jar, 50 g of sugar is dissolved, and in the third jar, 60 g of sugar is dissolved. It is allowed to take a full measure of tea from any jar and pour all this tea into any other jar. Is it possible to achieve, with several such pourings, that the first jar is empty and the amount of sugar in the second jar equals the amount of sugar in the third jar? (V. Shevyakov)

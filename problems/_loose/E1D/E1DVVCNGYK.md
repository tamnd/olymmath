Prove: (1) $\cos x+\cos (x+d)+\cdots+\cos (x+n d)$
$$
=\frac{\sin \frac{n+1}{2} d \cos \left(x+\frac{n d}{2}\right)}{\sin \frac{d}{2}} ;
$$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text { (2) } \sin x+\sin (x+d)+\cdots+\sin (x+n d) \\
=\frac{\sin \frac{n+1}{2} d \sin \left(x+\frac{n d}{2}\right)}{\sin \frac{d}{2}} .
\end{array}
$$

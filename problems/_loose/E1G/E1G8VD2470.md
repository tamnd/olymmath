In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the angle bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle C$ intersect at point $E$, and the angle bisectors of $\angle B$ and $\angle D$ intersect at point $F$, with points $E$ and $F$ inside quadrilateral $ABCD$. $M$ is the midpoint of segment $EF$, and through point $M$ perpendiculars are drawn to sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $H_1$, $H_2$, $H_3$, and $H_4$ respectively. Prove:
$$
M H_1 + M H_3 = M H_2 + M H_4.
$$

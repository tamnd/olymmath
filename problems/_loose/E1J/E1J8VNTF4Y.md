Let $[A B]$ be a segment and $M$ its midpoint. In the same half-plane with respect to $A B$, consider points $C$ and $D$, and in the opposite half-plane, consider point $E$ such that triangles $A D M$, $B C M$, and $A B E$ are equilateral. Prove that:
a) $A_{A B C D}=\frac{3}{4} \cdot A_{A B E}$
b) $A N=N P=P B$ where $\{N\}=D E \cap A B$ and $\{P\}=C E \cap A B$

Show that each of the fractions $3 / 5, 3 / 10, 3 / 15, 3 / 20, 3 / 25, \ldots, 3 /(5 n), \ldots$ can be written as the sum of the reciprocals of two different natural numbers. Which of these fractions can also be written as the sum of the reciprocals of two equal integers? Which of them can be written as the difference of the reciprocals of two natural numbers?

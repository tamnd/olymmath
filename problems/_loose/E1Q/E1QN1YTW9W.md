Let $S_{n}$ denote the sum of the first $n$ prime numbers ( $S_{1}=2, S_{2}=5, S_{3}=10$ etc.). Prove that for every $n$, there exists an integer $m$ such that

$$
S_{n} \leq m^{2} \leq S_{n+1}
$$

Determine the largest positive integer $N$, such that there exists a $6 \times N$ table $T$ satisfying:
(1) Each column is a permutation of $1,2, \cdots, 6$;
(2) For any two columns $i \neq j$, there exists a row $r(r \in$ $\{1,2, \cdots, 6\}$ ) such that $t_{r i}=t_{r j}$;
(3) For any two columns $i \neq j$, there exists a row $s(s \in$ $\{1,2, \cdots, 6\})$ such that $t_{s i} \neq t_{s j}$.

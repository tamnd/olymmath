Let K, L, $M$ be the interior points of the sides $B C, C A, A B$ of a given triangle $A B C$ such that the incircles of the pairs of triangles $A B K$ and $CAK$, $BCL$ and $A B L$, $C A M$ and $BCM$ have external tangency. Then it holds that

$$
|B K| \cdot|C L| \cdot|A M|=|C K| \cdot|A L| \cdot|B M| .
$$

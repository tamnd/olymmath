Let $f:\mathbb{Z}^2\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}$ be a function satisfying
$$f(x+1,y)+f(x,y+1)+1=f(x,y)+f(x+1,y+1)$$
for all integers $x$ and $y$ . Can it happen that $|f(x,y)|\leq 2024$ for all $x,y\in\mathbb{Z}$ ?

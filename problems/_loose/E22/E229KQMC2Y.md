Write the positive integer $n$ as the sum of several 1s and several 2s, considering different orders of the summands as different ways, and denote the number of all such ways as $\alpha(n)$; write $n$ as the sum of several positive integers greater than 1, considering different orders of the summands as different ways, and denote the number of all such ways as $\beta(n)$. Prove that for each $n$, $\alpha(n)=\beta(n+2)$.

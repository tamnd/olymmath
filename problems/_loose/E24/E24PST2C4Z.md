Let the heights corresponding to the vertices of tetrahedron $ABCD$ be $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}, h_{d}$, respectively. Take points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$ on each height line such that $A A_{1}=\frac{k}{h_{a}}, B B_{1}=\frac{k}{h_{b}}, C C_{1}=\frac{k}{h_{c}}, D D_{1}=\frac{k}{h_{d}}, k$ is any real number. Prove that the centroid of tetrahedron $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ coincides with the centroid of tetrahedron $A B C D$.

Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be an inscribed polygon; the center of the circle is inside the polygon. A system of circles touches the given one from the inside at points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$, and one of the intersection points of two adjacent circles lies on the corresponding side of the polygon. Prove that if $n$ is odd, then all the circles have equal radii. The length of the outer boundary of the union of the inscribed circles is equal to the length of the given circle.

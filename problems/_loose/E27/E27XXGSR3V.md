Given $x<y<0$. Let $M=|x|, N=|y|, P=$ $\frac{|x+y|}{2}, Q=\sqrt{x y}$. Then the size relationship of $M, N, P, Q$ is
(A) $M<Q<P<N$.
(B) $M<P<Q<N$.
(C) $Q<N<P<M$.
(D) $N<Q<P<M$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics Correspondence Competition, 1990)

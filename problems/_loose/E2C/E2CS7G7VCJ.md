a) Prove the following theorem:

If a triangle $ABC$ is equilateral, then the sum of any two exterior angles belonging to different vertices is always twice as large as the sum of the corresponding interior angles.

b) Investigate whether the following converse of the theorem stated in a) also holds: If in a triangle $ABC$ the sum of any two exterior angles belonging to different vertices is always twice as large as the sum of the corresponding interior angles, then the triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

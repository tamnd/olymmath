Let $p$ be prime number and $n$ be non negative integers.

(1) Let $m$ be integer such that $0\leq m\leq n.$ How many numbers are there among integers 1 through $p^{n+1}$ which can be divided by $p^{m}$ not but by $p^{m+1}?$

(2) For two integers $x,\ y$ 1 through $p^{n+1},$ how many pairs of $(x, y)$ such that the product $xy$ can be divided by $p^{n+1}?$

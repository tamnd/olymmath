Prove the inequality

$$
|\mu-a|^{p} \leqslant 2 \mathrm{E}|\xi-a|^{p}
$$

where $a \in \mathbb{R}, p \geqslant 1$ such that $\mathrm{E}|\xi|^{p}<\infty$, and $\mu$ is the median of the random variable $\xi$.

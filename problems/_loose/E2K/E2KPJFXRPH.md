Three circles are constructed on the medians of a triangle as on diameters. It is known that they intersect in pairs. Let $C_1$ be the intersection point of the circles built on the medians $AM_1$ and $BM_2$ , which is more distant from the vertex $C$ . Points $A_1$ and $B_1$ are defined similarly. Prove that the lines $AA_1, BB_1$ and $CC_1$ intersect at one point.

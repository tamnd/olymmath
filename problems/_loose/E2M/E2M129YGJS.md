Area of a Quadrilateral Connection between the Angle Size, Arc Length, and Chord

From vertex $A$ of the square $A B C D$ with side 1, two rays are drawn intersecting the square such that vertex $C$ lies between the rays. The angle between the rays is $\beta$. Perpendiculars are drawn from vertices $B$ and $D$ to the rays. Find the area of the quadrilateral with vertices at the bases of these perpendiculars.

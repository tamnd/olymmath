(G2 2018) Let $A B C$ be a triangle such that $A B = A C$ and let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$. Let $P$ be a point satisfying $P B < P C$ and such that the lines $(A P)$ and $(B C)$ are parallel. Let $X$ and $Y$ be points lying on the lines $(P B)$ and $(P C)$, respectively, such that point $B$ is on the segment $[P X]$, point $C$ is on the segment $[P Y]$, and $\widehat{P X M} = \widehat{P Y M}$. Show that the quadrilateral $A P X Y$ is cyclic.

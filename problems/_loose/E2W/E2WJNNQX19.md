$$ \left\lfloor 2+\frac{1}{2^{2021}} \right\rfloor+\left\lfloor 2+\frac{1}{2^{2020}} \right\rfloor+\cdots+\left\lfloor 2+\frac{1}{2^1} \right\rfloor+\left\lfloor 2+\frac{1}{2^0} \right\rfloor. $$

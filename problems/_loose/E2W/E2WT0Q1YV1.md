Property of a Quadrilateral. A certain quadrilateral of area $Q$ is divided by its diagonals into 4 triangles, the areas of which are respectively $A, B, C$ and $D$. Show that

$$
A \cdot B \cdot C \cdot D=\frac{(A+B)^{2}(B+C)^{2}(C+D)^{2}(D+A)^{2}}{Q^{4}}
$$

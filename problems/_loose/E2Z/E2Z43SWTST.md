Let $A B C D$ be a square, and let $P$ be a point on the smaller arc $A D$ of the circle passing through the vertices of the square. The intersection of lines $P C$ and $A D$ is $E$, and the intersection of lines $P D$ and $A B$ is $F$.

Prove that

$$
\frac{2}{A E}=\frac{1}{A B}+\frac{1}{A F}
$$

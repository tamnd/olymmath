We are given a triangle $A B C$. Let $K$ be the midpoint of $[B C]$. The line $(A K)$ is then a median of the triangle $A B C$. We construct the line symmetric to the line $(A K)$ with respect to the bisector of the angle $\widehat{A}$. This latter line intersects the segment $[B C]$ at $D$. The line $(A D)$ is a symmedian of the triangle $A B C$. We construct the other two symmedians of the triangle $A B C$ in the same way. Show that the symmedians are concurrent.

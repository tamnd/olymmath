$[4+4]$ The magician's eyes are tied, while one of the spectators arranges $N$ identical coins in a row - some in the 'heads' position, the others 'tails'. The magician's assistant asks the spectator to write down a natural number from 1 to $N$ on a piece of paper and show it to the audience. Seeing the number, the assistant chooses one coin from the row and flips it. Then they untie the magician's eyes, he looks at the row and recognizes the number chosen by the spectator.

a) Prove that if the magician and his assistant have a method to recognize the chosen number for $N=a$ and for $N=b$, then there exists a method for $N=a b$.

b) Find all values of $N$ for which such a method exists.

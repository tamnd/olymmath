106 Prove: There exists a unique pair of real numbers $(p, q)=\left(-1, \frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{2}\right)$, such that the inequality
$$
\left|\sqrt{1-x^{2}}-p x-q\right| \leqslant \frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{2}
$$

holds for all $x \in[0,1]$.

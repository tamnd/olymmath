Prove that the distance $d$ from an arbitrary point $P$ on the plane to the center $O$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ is determined by the formula:

$$
\begin{gathered}
d^{2}=R^{2}-\left[a^{2}(C A M)(A M B)+b^{2}(A B M)(B C M)+\right. \\
\left.+c^{2}(B C M)(C A M)\right] \cdot \frac{1}{(A B C)^{2}}
\end{gathered}
$$

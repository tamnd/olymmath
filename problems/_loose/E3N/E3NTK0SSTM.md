Let set $A$ consist of finite sequences whose terms are 0 and 1. If in any infinite sequence whose terms are 0 and 1, a segment can be taken out that is exactly an element of $A$, then $A$ is called a base set. Prove that among all 8-term sequences, no more than 51 sequences can be selected to form a base set.

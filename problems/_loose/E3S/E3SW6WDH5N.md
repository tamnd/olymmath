Three different numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$ satisfy the relations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x_{1}^{3}+x_{1} p+q=0 \\
& x_{2}^{3}+x_{2} p+q=0 \\
& x_{3}^{3}+x_{3} p+q=0
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove the validity of the equality:

$$
x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+x_{3}^{3}=3 x_{1} \cdot x_{2} \cdot x_{3}
$$

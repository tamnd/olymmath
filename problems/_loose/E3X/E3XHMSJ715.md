$N$ circles, the centers of which lie on the major axis of an ellipse, touch the ellipse. At the same time, a circle of radius $r_{\mathrm{i}}(2 \leqslant i \leqslant N-1)$ touches the circles of radii $r_{\mathrm{i}-1}$ and $r_{\mathrm{i}+1}$. Prove that if $3 n-2>N$, then

$$
r_{2 n-1}\left(r_{1}+r_{2 n-1}\right)=r_{n}\left(r_{n}+r_{3 n-2}\right)
$$

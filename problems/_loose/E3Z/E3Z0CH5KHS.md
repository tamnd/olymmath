Four. (50 points) Let $M=\{1,2, \cdots, 10\}$. For a partition of $M$ into $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ (all $A_{i}$ are non-empty), if there exists a permutation $A_{i 1}, A_{i 2}, A_{i 3}$ of $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ such that
$$
\max A_{i k}>\min A_{i, k+1}\left(k=1,2,3, A_{i 4}=A_{i 1}\right) \text {, }
$$
then it is called a "good" partition. Find the number of all good partitions of $M$.

A line passing through $Q$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ at $B \neq Q$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ at $B^{\prime} \neq Q$. Show that $(A B)$ and $\left(A^{\prime} B^{\prime}\right)$ are parallel.

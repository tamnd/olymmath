Let triangle $ABC$ be acute-angled and point $H$ be the foot of the altitude dropped from vertex $A$. Points $M \in AB$ and $N \in AC$ are such that $HM \perp AB$ and $HN \perp AC$. Let $l_{A}$ be the line through $A$, perpendicular to $MN$. Analogously to the previous construction, we construct lines $l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$. Prove that the lines $l_{A}, l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$ intersect at the same point.

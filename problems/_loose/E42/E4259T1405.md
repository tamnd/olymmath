Points $P_{1}, P_{2}$ and $P_{3}$, not lying on the same line, are located inside a convex $2 n$-gon $A_{1} \ldots A_{2 n}$. Prove that if the sum of the areas of triangles $A_{1} A_{2} P_{i}, A_{3} A_{4} P_{i}, \ldots, A_{2 n-1} A_{2 n} P_{i}$ is the same number $c$ for $i=1,2,3$, then for any internal point $P$ the sum of the areas of these triangles is $c$.

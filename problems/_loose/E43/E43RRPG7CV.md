Let's imagine a large cube made up of 27 smaller cubes. A termite sits on the center of one face of one of the outer cubes and begins to gnaw a path. Once the termite has visited a cube, it does not return to it. It always moves parallel to one of the edges of the large cube. Can the termite gnaw through all 26 outer cubes and end its path in the central cube? If possible, show what the termite's path should be.

We have twelve balls, 6 white and 6 black, and the balls are labeled with consecutive numbers: $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 11, 12$.

(a) How many arrangements are possible for the twelve balls such that when placed in a row, a white ball is always followed by a black ball?

(b) How many arrangements are possible such that only white balls occupy the first six positions?

(c) How many different groups of seven can be formed such that each group contains 4 white and 3 black balls?

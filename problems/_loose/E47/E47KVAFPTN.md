The inhabitants of a city played a game called Pass It On. At the very beginning, one citizen sent a letter to seven of his fellow citizens. Then, some of the citizens who received the letter dropped out of the game, while others sent a letter to seven fellow citizens who had not yet participated in the game. This process was repeated several times, with each citizen who received a letter either dropping out of the game or sending letters to seven new people. After some time, the game stopped because no one in the city was sending letters anymore. Prove that the number of citizens who dropped out of the game cannot be equal to 2006.

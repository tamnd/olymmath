Let the sides of the equilateral triangle $ABC$ be $a$. Construct regular hexagons $BAD_{1}GH E_{2}$, $CBE_{1}JKF_{2}$, and $ACF_{1}LMD_{2}$ outwardly over the sides of the triangle. Fold the hexagonal plates along the edges $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ such that first the points $D_{1}$ and $D_{2}$, and then the points $E_{1}$ and $E_{2}$ coincide at points $D$ and $E$, respectively. Show that in this way $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ also coincide at a point $F$, and that the points $G$, $H$, $J$, $K$, $L$, and $M$ lie in a plane. Calculate the volume of the convex solid that the 12 vertices of the spatial figure formed by the folds determine. How many body diagonals does this solid have (i.e., segments connecting two vertices that lie inside the solid)?

Once a month, Uncle Chernomor gathers all his 33 bogatyrs and proposes a new salary list, which he himself has compiled. Chernomor himself does not vote. The bogatyrs whose pay is proposed to be increased vote in favor, while the others vote against. The proposal is adopted by a majority vote. Can Chernomor achieve in 3 years that his own pay is increased tenfold, while the salaries of all the bogatyrs are reduced tenfold?

In a triangle with angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ and the sides $a, b, c$ opposite to them, the following holds:

$$
\tan \alpha \cdot \tan \beta=3 ; \tan \beta \cdot \tan \gamma=6
$$

Calculate the angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ and the side ratios $\frac{a}{c}$ and $\frac{b}{c}$.

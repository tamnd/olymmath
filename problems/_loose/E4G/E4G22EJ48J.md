Given two circles $(C)$ and $\left(C^{\prime}\right)$ with centers $O$ and $O^{\prime}$ intersecting at $A$ and $B$. A variable circle $\Gamma$ passing through $A$ intersects them respectively at $M$ and $M^{\prime} .(A M)$ intersects $\left(C^{\prime}\right)$ at $N^{\prime},\left(A M^{\prime}\right)$ intersects $(C)$ at $N$. The circle $\left(A N N^{\prime}\right)$ intersects $\Gamma$ at $P$. Show that the locus of $P$ is the circle centered at the midpoint of $\left[O O^{\prime}\right]$ and passing through $A$.

In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, $AD$ and $BC$ are not parallel, and there exists a circle $\Gamma$ that is tangent to sides $BC$ and $AD$ at points $C$ and $D$, respectively. Let $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $P$, and let the circle $\Gamma$ intersect $AB$ at two distinct points $K$ and $L$. Prove that the line $KP$ bisects $CD$ if and only if $LC = LD$.

In a school (where there were more than 5 students), the results of the academic year were summed up. It turned out that in every set of five or more students, not less than $80 \%$ of the twos (grades) received by these students throughout the year were given to no more than $20 \%$ of the students in this set. Prove that at least three quarters of all the twos given in the school were received by one student.

On the sides of angle $S$, two arbitrary points $A$ and $B$ are given. From these points, on the sides of the angle and on the same side of the line $A B$, equal segments are laid off: $A D = B C$, $D D_{1} = C C_{1}$, $D_{1} D_{2} = C_{1} C_{2}$, and so on. Prove that the geometric locus of the midpoints of segments $A B$, $C D$, $C_{1} D_{1}$, $C_{2} D_{2}$, and so on, is a ray parallel to the bisector of the angle.

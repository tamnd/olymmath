Given an odd natural number $ a $, greater than 100. On the board, all natural numbers of the form $ \frac{a-n^{2}}{4} $, where $ n $ is a natural number, were written. It turned out that for $ n \leq \sqrt{a / 5} $ all of them are prime. Prove that each of the other natural numbers written on the board is also prime or equal to one. (A. Khryabrov)

On a flat ground, there are $n$ people, and the distance between any two people is different. Each person holds a water gun, and when the signal is given, each person shoots the person closest to them. Prove: when $n$ is odd, at least one person will remain dry; when $n$ is even, there exists a scenario where all $n$ people are hit.

In the plane region
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x-\sqrt{3} y+4 \geqslant 0, \\
x+\sqrt{3} y+4 \geqslant 0, \\
x \leqslant 2
\end{array}\right.
$$

draw a circle, and denote the largest circle within this region as $\odot M$.
(1) Find the equation of $\odot M$;
(2) $\odot M$ intersects the $x$-axis at points $A$ and $B$. For a moving point $P$ inside the circle such that $|P A|,|P M|,|P B|$ form a geometric sequence, find the range of $\overrightarrow{P A} \cdot \overrightarrow{P B}$.

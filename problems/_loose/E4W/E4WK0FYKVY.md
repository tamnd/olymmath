Let A be the point of intersection of two circles. From this point, points $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ start moving along each circle, clockwise, at constant speeds. After one complete revolution, both points return to $A$. Prove that there always exists a fixed point $B$ such that the equality $X_{1} B=X_{2} B$ holds throughout the motion.

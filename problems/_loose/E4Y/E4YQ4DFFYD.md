A trapezium $A B C D$, in which $A B$ is parallel to $C D$, is inscribed in a circle with centre $O$. Suppose the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ of the trapezium intersect at $M$, and $O M=2$.

(a) If $\angle A M B$ is $60^{\circ}$, determine, with proof, the difference between the lengths of the parallel sides.

(b) If $\angle A M D$ is $60^{\circ}$, find the difference between the lengths of the parallel sides.

Let $a, b, c, d, e$ be real numbers such that $a<b<c<d<e.$ The least possible value of the function $f: \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$ with $f(x) = |x-a| + |x - b|+ |x - c| + |x - d|+ |x - e|$ is
$$
\mathrm a. ~ e+d+c+b+a \mathrm b.~e+d+c-b-a \mathrm c. ~e+d+|c|-b-a \mathrm d. ~e+d+b-a \mathrm e. ~e+d-b-a
$$

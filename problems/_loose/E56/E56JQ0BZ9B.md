Provide an example of a bounded Borel function $f=f(x, y)$ on $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ such that (for $y \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$ respectively)

$$
\int_{\mathbb{R}} f(x, y) d x=0, \int_{\mathbb{R}} f(x, y) d y=0
$$

however, this function is not Lebesgue integrable on ( $\mathbb{R}^{2}, \mathscr{B}\left(\mathbb{R}^{2}\right)$ ).

Let $f$ and $g$ be polynomials and $\gamma$ be a closed non-self-intersecting curve in the complex plane. Prove that if

$$
|f(z)-g(z)|<|f(z)|+|g(z)|
$$

for all $z \in \gamma$, then inside the curve $\gamma$ there is the same number of roots of the polynomials $f$ and $g$, counting multiplicities (Rouche's theorem).

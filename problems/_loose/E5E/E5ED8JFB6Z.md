There is a cube of size $10 \times 10 \times 10$, consisting of small unit cubes. In the center $O$ of one of the corner cubes sits a grasshopper. The grasshopper can jump to the center of a cube that shares a face with the one it is currently in, and in such a way that the distance to point $O$ increases. In how many ways can the grasshopper jump to the cube opposite the initial one?

$$
\frac{S_{n}}{S_{2 n}-S_{n}}=\frac{S_{2 n}-S_{n}}{S_{3 n}-S_{2 n}}
$$

where $S_{n}, S_{2 n}, S_{3 n}$ are the sums of the terms of a geometric progression.

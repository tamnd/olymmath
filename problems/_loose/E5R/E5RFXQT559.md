In a regular tetrahedron $ABCD$, the height $AH$ is dropped; $H_1$ is the point of intersection of the heights of the face $BCD$; $h_1$ and $h_2$ are the lengths of the segments into which one of the heights of the face $BCD$ is divided by the point $H_1$.

a) Prove that the points $H$ and $H_1$ are symmetric with respect to the center of the circumscribed circle of triangle $BCD$.

b) Prove that $AH^2 = 4 h_1 h_2$.

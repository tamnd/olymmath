On a curved piece, the direction of the train changes by $30^{\circ}$ (this is the acute angle between the tangents at the endpoints). (Tracks that can be transformed into each other by axial reflection or rotation are not considered different.) Also, list the possible axes of symmetry for the tracks. Is it possible to assemble a track without a center of symmetry?

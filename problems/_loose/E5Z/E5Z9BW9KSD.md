In the country of "Numberland," only natural numbers live. Men and boys are even numbers, women and girls are odd numbers. After marriage, couples immediately have children, and these are all the numbers that divide their product without a remainder. The sum of the values of all the children of the Kvádřík family is 28. Mr. Kvádřík has a lower value than at least one of his sons. Determine the values of Mr. and Mrs. Kvádřík.

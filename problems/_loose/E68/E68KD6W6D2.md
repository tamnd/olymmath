On the real line, $22$ points are marked: $$ 0 \leqslant a_1 < a_2 < a_3 < \cdots < a_{21} < a_{22} \leqslant 1. $$ The allowed operation is to choose any two points $A$ and $B$ , erase them, and mark the midpoint of $AB$ . Prove that, regardless of the initial configuration of the $22$ points, it is possible to perform $20$ allowed operations so that, after these operations, the distance between the only two remaining points is at most $0.001$ .**Note:** Assume that in each operation, the midpoint of $AB$ does not coincide with any of the already marked points.

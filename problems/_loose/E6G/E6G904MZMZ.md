We arbitrarily select a point on each face of a tetrahedron and construct the reflection of each point with respect to the centroid of the respective triangular face. We then take the centroid of the original set of four points and the centroid of the four reflected points. Show that these two centroids are mirror images of each other with respect to the centroid of the tetrahedron.

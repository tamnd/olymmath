A table with dimensions $5 \times 5$ is divided into 25 unit squares (small squares). In each square, there is one token. A move consists of moving any two tokens, each to an adjacent square. Let's mark one square. Can it be achieved, after a certain number of moves, that all 25 tokens are in that square? (Two squares are adjacent if they share a side.)

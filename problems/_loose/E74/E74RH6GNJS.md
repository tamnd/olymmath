(not covered in class) The town hall had a beautiful work of art installed on the town square. It consists of a certain number of non-empty piles of pebbles, totaling $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ pebbles. One night, a joker takes one pebble from each pile and forms a new pile. The next night, another does the same, and so on. Will we ever reach a stable configuration, that is, with the same number of piles always containing the same number of pebbles?

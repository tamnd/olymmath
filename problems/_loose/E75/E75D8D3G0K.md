The sum of an infinite series of numbers is defined as follows. Let the series be

$$
a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\ldots+a_{n}+\ldots
$$

Denote by $S_{n}$ the sum of the first $n$ terms. If the sequence $\left\{S_{n}\right\}$ has a limit $S$, then the number $S$ is called the sum of the given series. If the sequence $\left\{S_{n}\right\}$ does not have a limit, then the given series is said to diverge and no sum is assigned to it.

Find the sums

a) $1+a+a^{2}+\ldots+a^{n}+\ldots$

b) $a+2 a^{2}+3 a^{3}+\ldots+n a^{n}+\ldots$

c) $\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2}+\frac{1}{2 \cdot 3}+\frac{1}{3 \cdot 4}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n(n+1)}+\ldots$

d) $\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}+\frac{1}{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}+\frac{1}{3 \cdot 4 \cdot 5}+\ldots+$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& +\frac{1}{n(n+1)(n+2)}+\ldots \\
& \text { e)* } 1+\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n}+\ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

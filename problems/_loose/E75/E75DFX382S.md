A chip is placed in the corner of an $n \times n$ chessboard. Each of the two players, in turn, moves it to an adjacent square (one that shares a side with the current square). It is not allowed to move to a square that the chip has already visited. The player who cannot make a move loses.

a) Prove that if $n$ is even, the player who starts the game can ensure a win, and if $n$ is odd, the second player wins.

b) Who wins if the chip is initially placed not on a corner square, but on an adjacent one?

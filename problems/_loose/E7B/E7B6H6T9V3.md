Consider the set $M=\{1,2,3,...,2020\}.$ Find the smallest positive integer $k$ such that for any subset $A$ of $M$ with $k$ elements, there exist $3$ distinct numbers $a,b,c$ from $M$ such that $a+b, b+c$ and $c+a$ are all in $A.$

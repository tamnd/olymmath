Misha came up with two composite numbers: $a$ and $b$. On the board, in the left column, he listed all the proper natural divisors of the number $a$, and in the right column, all the proper natural divisors of the number $b$. There were no identical numbers on the board. Misha wants the number $a + b$ to not be divisible by any sum of two numbers from different columns. Prove that for this, it is sufficient for him to erase no more than half of the numbers from each column. (A divisor of a number is called proper if it is different from 1 and the number itself.)

For every positive integer $n$, define $f(n)$ as the number of non-empty subsets $\mathcal N\subseteq \{1,\ldots ,n\}$ such that $\gcd(n\in\mathcal N)=1$. Show that $f(n)$ is a perfect square if and only if $n=1$.

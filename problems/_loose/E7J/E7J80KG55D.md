Given the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>$
$b>0)$ with the right focus at $F$, the right directrix $l$ intersects the $x$-axis at point $N$. A line segment $PM$ is drawn from a point $P$ on the ellipse, perpendicular to the directrix $l$, with the foot of the perpendicular at $M$. If $PN$ bisects $\angle FPM$, and the quadrilateral $OFMP$ is a parallelogram, prove: $e>\frac{2}{3}$.

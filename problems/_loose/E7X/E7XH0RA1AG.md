Given an equilateral triangle $A B C$ with side $a, B D$ is its height. On $B D$, a second equilateral triangle $B D C_{1}$ is constructed, and on the height $B D_{1}$ of this triangle, a third equilateral triangle $B D_{1} C_{2}$ is constructed. Find the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $C C_{1} C_{2}$. Prove that its center lies on the side of triangle $A B C$ (where $C_{2}$ is outside triangle $A B C$).

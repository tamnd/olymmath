Let $a$ be a positive integer. If a positive integer $b$ satisfies that for all positive integers $n$ such that $a n \geqslant b$, $\mathrm{C}_{a n}^{b}-1$ is divisible by $a n+1$, then $b$ is called "$a$-good". If a positive integer $b$ is $a$-good, and $b+2$ is not $a$-good, prove: $b+1$ is a prime number.

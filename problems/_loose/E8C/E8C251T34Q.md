Determine the parameter $\lambda$ such that the distance from the center of the circle

$$
x^{2}+y^{2}+4 x-4 y-17=0
$$

to the line $x-(\lambda+2) y-\lambda-4=0$ is equal to $\frac{5}{\sqrt{2}}$. The tangents that touch the circle at the intersection points with the given line and the line itself form a triangle. Determine the area of the intersection of the circumcircle of that triangle and the given circle. (Take the integer solution for $\lambda$.)

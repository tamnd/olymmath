In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, there exist three interior points $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$, not lying on the same line, and possessing the property that the sum of the areas of triangles $A B P_{i}$ and $C D P_{i}$ is equal to the sum of the areas of triangles $B C P_{i}$ and $A D P_{i}$ for $i=1,2,3$. Prove that $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

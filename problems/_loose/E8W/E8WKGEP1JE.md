Let $d \geqslant 1$ and $G$ be a graph with average degree at least $2 \mathrm{~d}$.

1. Show that $\mathrm{G}$ has a subgraph with minimum degree strictly greater than $\mathrm{d}$.
2. Let $T$ be a tree with a number of vertices less than or equal to $d+2$. Show that $G$ contains a copy of $T$ as a subgraph.

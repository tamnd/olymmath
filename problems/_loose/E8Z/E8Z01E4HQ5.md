Let $M$ be any point on the plane of $\triangle ABC$, and $P$ be the semiperimeter of $\triangle ABC$. Prove:
$$
|M A|+|M B|+|M C| \geqslant P \cdot \min \left\{\sec ^{2} \frac{A}{4}, \sec ^{2} \frac{B}{4}, \sec ^{2} \frac{C}{4}\right\} .
$$

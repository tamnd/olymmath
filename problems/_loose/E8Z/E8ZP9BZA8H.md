On the shores of a circular island (viewed from above), there are cities $A, B, C$, and $D$. The straight asphalt road $A C$ divides the island into two equal halves. The straight asphalt road $B D$ is shorter than road $A C$ and intersects it. The speed of a cyclist on any asphalt road is 15 km/h. The island also has straight dirt roads $A B, B C, C D$, and $A D$, on which the cyclist's speed is the same. The cyclist reaches each of the points $A, C$, and $D$ from point $B$ via a straight road in 2 hours. Find the area enclosed by the quadrilateral $A B C D$.

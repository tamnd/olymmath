A small town has $n(n \geqslant 2)$ couples, numbered from 1 to $n$. At the New Year's Eve party, each man congratulates two women whose numbers differ by 1. As a result, for any two men whose numbers differ by 1, there is one woman who receives congratulations from both of these men. Prove: At least one man congratulates his own wife.

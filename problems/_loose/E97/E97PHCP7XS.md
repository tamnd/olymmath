In the store "Everything for School," chalk is sold in packages of three grades: regular, unusual, and superior. Initially, the ratio of the quantities by grade was 3:4:6. As a result of sales and deliveries from the warehouse, this ratio changed to 2:5:8. It is known that the number of packages of superior chalk increased by $80\%$, and the number of packages of regular chalk decreased by no more than 10 packages. How many packages of chalk were there in the store initially?

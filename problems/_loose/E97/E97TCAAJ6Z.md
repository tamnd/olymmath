Different real numbers $a, b, c$ are such that among the three equations

$$
x^{2}+a x+b=0, x^{2}+b x+c=0, x^{2}+c x+a=0
$$

any two have exactly one common root. Find $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}$.

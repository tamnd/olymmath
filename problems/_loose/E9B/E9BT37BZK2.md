The Spanish king decided to rearrange the portraits of his predecessors in a round tower of the castle in his own way. However, he wants to swap only two adjacent portraits at a time, and these should not be portraits of two kings, one of whom reigned immediately after the other. Moreover, he is only concerned with the relative positions of the portraits, and considers two arrangements that differ by a rotation of the circle to be the same. Prove that no matter how the portraits are initially hung, the king can achieve any new arrangement of them according to these rules.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral inscribed in a circle and satisfying
$$
D A<A B=B C<C D .
$$

Points $E$ and $F$ are chosen on sides $C D$ and $A B$ such that $\overline{B E} \perp \overline{A C}$ and $\overline{E F} \| \overline{B C}$. Prove that $F B=F D$.

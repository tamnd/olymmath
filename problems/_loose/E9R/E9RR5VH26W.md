Given a right triangle $ABC$, right-angled at $A$ and with $AC$ as the longer leg; let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$, $N$ the reflection of $A$ with respect to $BC$, and $O$ the intersection of the perpendicular to $MN$ passing through $N$ and the line containing $BC$.

a) Prove that the angle $OMN$ is twice the angle $ACB$.

b) Prove that the ratio of the areas of $MNO$ and $ABC$ is one quarter of the ratio of the lengths of $BC$ and $HM$, where $H$ is the foot of the altitude from $A$ to $BC$.

If $m$ ! ends with exactly $n$ zeros, we say that $n$ is the tail of the factorial $m$ !. Observe the examples and answer:

- 5 ! = $5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1=120$, ends in one zero, so the tail of the factorial 5 ! is $1 ; \text{and}$
- $10!=3628800$, ends in two zeros, so the tail of the factorial 10 ! is equal to 2.

a) What are the tails of the factorials of 20! and 25!?

b) What is the tens digit of $7!+8!+9!+\ldots+2018$ !?

c) Is there any $m$ ! with a factorial tail equal to 5?

d) What is the tail of the factorial of 2018! (two thousand and eighteen factorial)?

e) How many positive integers less than 2018 are not factorial tails?

(Olympiad Training Problem from "Intermediate Mathematics" Issue 3, 2004) $f(n)$ is defined on the set of positive integers, and satisfies $f(1)=2$, $f(n+1)=(f(n))^{2}-f(n)+1, n=1,2, \cdots$. Prove that for all integers $n>1$, we have
$$
1-\frac{1}{2^{2^{n-1}}}<\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{f(i)}<1-\frac{1}{2^{2^{n}}} .
$$

Let $n$ be the smallest positive integer of 4 digits greater than or equal to 2016 that has the following property: there exists a positive integer $S$ such that

$$
S=\sqrt{a+\sqrt{b+\sqrt{c+\sqrt{d+S}}}}
$$

where $a, b, c, d$ are, in order, the thousands, hundreds, tens, and units digits of $n$. What is the value of $n$?

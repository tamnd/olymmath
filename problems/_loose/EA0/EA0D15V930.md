Let $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be such that $a_{1}+\ldots+a_{n}=n$ and $b_{1}, \ldots, b_{n}$ be such that $b_{1} \times \ldots \times b_{n}=1$. Show that $\left(1+a_{1}\right) \times \ldots \times\left(1+a_{n}\right) \leq\left(1+b_{1}\right) \times \ldots \times\left(1+b_{n}\right)$.

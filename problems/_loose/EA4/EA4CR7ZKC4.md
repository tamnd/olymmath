In $\triangle ABC$, $P$ is a point inside or on the boundary of the triangle, the distances from $P$ to the three sides are $PD=p, PE=q, PF=r; PA=x, PB=y, PC=z$.
Prove: (1) $2\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}\right) \leqslant \frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{r}$;
(2) $xy+yz+zx \geqslant 2(px+qy+rz)$.

$P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are reduced quadratic trinomials, each having two distinct roots.

It turned out that the sum of two numbers obtained by substituting the roots of the trinomial $P(x)$ into the trinomial $Q(x)$ is equal to the sum of two numbers obtained by substituting the roots of the trinomial $Q(x)$ into the trinomial $P(x)$. Prove that the discriminants of the trinomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are equal.

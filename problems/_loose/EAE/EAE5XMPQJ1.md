Let $\omega$ and $\Omega$ be two circles, with $\omega$ contained within $\Omega$, such that there exist $\omega_{i}$ for $i=1, \ldots 8$ tangent to both $\Omega$ and $\omega$, with the additional condition that $\omega_{i}$ and $\omega_{i+1}$ are tangent (we take the indices modulo 8). We denote by $T_{i}$ the point of tangency of $\omega_{i}$ with $\omega$. Show that the lines of the form $\left(T_{i} T_{i+4}\right)$ are concurrent.

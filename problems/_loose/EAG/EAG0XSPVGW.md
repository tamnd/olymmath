One of the processes in the preparation of pastries is the cooling of a special mixture of ingredients. The mixture is made at a temperature of $180^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and is immediately cooled in a room with a constant temperature of $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. It has been found that the current temperature $T$ of the mixture after $t$ hours of cooling in a room with a constant temperature $c$ can be calculated using the formula $T=a \cdot b^{t}+c$.

a) Determine the constants $a$ and $b$, given that the mixture has a temperature of $180^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ at the start $(t=0)$ and a temperature of $160^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ after 1 hour of cooling.

b) How long after preparation does the mixture cool to $150^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$? Calculate to the nearest minute. Write down your answer.

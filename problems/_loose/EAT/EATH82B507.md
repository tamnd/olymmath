Given a triangle $ABC$ and concentric circles $\omega_{b}, \omega_{c}$ centered at $A$. An arbitrary ray emanating from $A$ intersects these circles at points $B'$ and $C'$, respectively. The perpendicular bisectors of segments $BB'$ and $CC'$ intersect at point $X$. Prove that the points $X$, constructed in this manner for all rays emanating from $A$, lie on a single line.

Prove that there exist an infinite number of triples $n-1 $ , $n$ , $n + 1$ such that

(a) $n$ can be represented as the sum of two squares of natural numbers but neither of $n-1$ and $n+1$ can;

(b) each of these three numbers can be represented as the sum of two squares.

(V Senderov)

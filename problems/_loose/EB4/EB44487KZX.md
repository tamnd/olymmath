$A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{29}$ are 29 different sequences of positive integers. For $1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant 29$ and a natural number $x$, define:
$N_{i}(x)=$ the number of elements in the sequence $A_{i}$ that are $\leqslant x$,
$N_{i j}(x)=$ the number of elements in the sequence $A_{i} \cap A_{j}$ that are $\leqslant x$.
It is known that for all $1 \leqslant i \leqslant 29$ and every natural number $x, N_{i}(x)=\frac{x}{\mathrm{e}}, \mathrm{e}=2.71828 \cdots$
Prove: There exists at least one pair $i, j (1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant 29)$ such that $N_{i j}(1988)>200$.
(29th IMO Shortlist)

It is known that for any integer $x$, the trinomial $a x^{2}+b x+c$ has an integer value. Try to determine:
(1) Whether at least one of the coefficients $a, b, c$ must be an integer?
(2) Whether $a, b, c$ must all be integers.
(Ukrainian Mathematical Olympiad, 1992)

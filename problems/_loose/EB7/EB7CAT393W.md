Let the segments of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides $a, b, c$ of the triangle that fall within the triangle be $t_{a}, t_{b}, t_{c}$, respectively. Show that if $a>b>c$, then $t_{a}>t_{b}$ and $t_{c}>t_{b}$, and furthermore, that for any pair of values $b>c(>0)$, there exists an $a$ such that $a>b$ and $t_{a}=t_{c}$.

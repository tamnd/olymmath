Two circles of different radii touch at point $C$ of a straight line and are located on the same side of it. Segment $C D$ is the diameter of the larger circle. Two lines are drawn from point $D$, touching the smaller circle at points $A$ and $B$. The line passing through points $C$ and $A$ forms a $75^{\circ}$ angle with the common tangent to the circles at point $C$ and intersects the larger circle at point $M$. It is known that $A M=\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}$.

Find the area of the figure bounded by the tangent segments $D A, D B$ and the arc $A C B$ of the smaller circle.

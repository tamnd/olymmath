Let's have 70 turned-off light bulbs. For any group of light bulbs, we can prepare a switch that will change the state of each light bulb in this group (turn off the lit ones and turn on the turned-off ones) and not affect the other light bulbs. What is the smallest number of switches using which it is possible to light up any quartet of light bulbs (while the others will be turned off)?

(Martin Melicher)

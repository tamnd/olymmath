Let $a, b, c, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

a) Show that:

$\mathrm{A}=\left(a^{n}+b^{n}\right)\left(c^{n}+b^{n}\right)\left(a^{n}+c^{n}\right)$, is divisible by 2.

b) Show that: $4^{A}-1 \vdots 15$.

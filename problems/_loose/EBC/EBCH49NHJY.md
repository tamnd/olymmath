10.1.31 ※ Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the circumcenter and incenter are $O$ and $I$ respectively. Extend $BO$ to intersect the circumcircle at $K$, extend $AB$ to point $N$, and extend $CB$ to point $M$, such that $AN = CM = \text{semiperimeter of } \triangle ABC$. Prove: $MN \perp IK$.

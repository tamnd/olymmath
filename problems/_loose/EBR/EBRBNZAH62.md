Circles $k, l$ intersect at points $A, B$. Let $K, L$ be the points of tangency of their common tangent chosen such that point $B$ is an interior point of triangle $AKL$. On circles $k$ and $l$, choose points $N$ and $M$ respectively, such that point $A$ is an interior point of segment $MN$. Prove that quadrilateral $KLMN$ is cyclic if and only if line $MN$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $AKL$.

Consider a convex pentagon with all sides of equal length.

a) Prove that there exists a point inside it, lying on the longest diagonal, from which all sides are visible at angles not exceeding a right angle.

b) Prove that the circles constructed on its sides as diameters do not cover the entire pentagon.

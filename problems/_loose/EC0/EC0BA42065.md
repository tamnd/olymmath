Two non-intersecting circles have a common axis of symmetry which intersects the circles at points $A, B, C, D$ respectively. One of the common external tangents of the circles touches them at points $E$ and $F$, and one of the common internal tangents touches them at points $G$ and $H$. Show that each of the segments $EF, GH$ is the geometric mean between two segments determined by the points $A, B, C, D$.

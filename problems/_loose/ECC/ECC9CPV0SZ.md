Given a chord $AB$ on a circle with radius 1, whose length is less than $\sqrt{2}$, a line $l$ that does not intersect the circle forms a $45^{\circ}$ angle with the chord $AB$. Using a compass and a straightedge, construct a point $C$ on the line $l$ such that the segment $DE$ is perpendicular to $AB$ ($C, E$ are the intersections of $CA, CB$ with the circle).
(16th All-Russian Competition Problem)

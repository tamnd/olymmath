In a scalene triangle $ABC$ inscribed in circle $\Gamma$, the angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects segment $BC$ at point $E$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of arc $BAC$. Line $ME$ intersects $\Gamma$ again at point $D$. Prove that the center of the circumcircle of triangle $AED$ coincides with the intersection of the tangent to $\Gamma$ at point $D$ and line $BC$.

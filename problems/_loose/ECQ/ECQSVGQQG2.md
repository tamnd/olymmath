Compute the surface integral of the second kind

$$
\iint_{S}\left(x y^{2}+z^{2}\right) d y d z+\left(y z^{2}+x^{2}\right) d z d x+\left(z x^{2}+y^{2}\right) d x d y
$$

where $S-$ is the outer side of the upper ( $z \geqslant 0$ ) hemisphere $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=a^{2}$.

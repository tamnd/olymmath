The Tennis Federation has assigned qualification numbers to all its tennis players: the strongest player gets the first number, the next strongest gets the second number, and so on. It is known that in matches where the qualification numbers differ by more than 2, the player with the lower number always wins. A tournament involving the 1024 strongest tennis players is held according to the Olympic system: participants in each round are randomly paired, and the winner of each pair advances to the next round, so the number of participants is halved after each round. Thus, after the tenth round, the winner will be determined. What is the highest number he can have?

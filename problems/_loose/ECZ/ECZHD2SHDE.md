On a warehouse, $n$ containers numbered $1, 2, 3, \ldots, n$ are stacked in two piles in a random order. An automated forklift approaches one of the piles, removes several containers from the top, and places them on the other pile. Prove that in $2 n-1$ such operations, all containers can be arranged in one pile in order of their numbers (with № 1 at the bottom, then № 2, and so on).

Prove that for every natural number $n \geq 3$ there are natural numbers $a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{n}$ such that

$$
a_{k} \mid\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)
$$

applies for every $k=1,2, \ldots, n$.

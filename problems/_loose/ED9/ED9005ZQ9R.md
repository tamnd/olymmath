Anna and Elie are playing a game. They are both given the same set A, which consists of a finite number of distinct positive integers. Anna secretly chooses an integer $a \in A$. If Elie chooses an integer $b$ (not necessarily in $A$) and gives it to Anna, Anna gives him the number of positive divisors of $a b$. Show that Elie can choose $b$ in such a way as to definitely determine the integer chosen by Anna.

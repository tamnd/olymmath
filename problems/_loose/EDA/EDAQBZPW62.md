On the same side of line $l$, there are three adjacent equilateral triangles $\triangle A D E, \triangle A F G, \triangle A B C$, and $G, A, B$ are all on line $l$. Let the side lengths of these triangles be $b, c, a$ respectively. Connect $G D$ to intersect $A E$ at $N$, and connect $B N$ to intersect $A C$ at $L$. Prove that: $A L=\frac{a b c}{a b+b c+a c}$.

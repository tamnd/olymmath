Consider lattice points $(x, y)$ in a plane rectangular coordinate system, where $x, y$ are integers, satisfying $1 \leqslant x \leqslant 12,1 \leqslant y \leqslant 12$. These 144 lattice points are colored red, white, and blue, respectively. Prove that there exists a rectangle whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axes and whose vertices have the same color.

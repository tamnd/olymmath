Fermat's Little Theorem. Let $p$ be a prime number and $p$ does not divide $a$. Then $a^{p-1} \equiv 1(\bmod p)$.

Prove Fermat's theorem by expanding $(1+1+\ldots+1)^{p}$ using the multinomial theorem .

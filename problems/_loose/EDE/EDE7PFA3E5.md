We label the triangle $ABC$ in a positive direction. The angles of the triangle at vertices $A, B$, and $C$ are $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$ respectively. We rotate the vertex $B$ around point $A$ in a negative direction by an angle $\alpha$, then rotate the resulting point $B_{1}$ around point $B$ in a negative direction by an angle $\beta$, and finally rotate the resulting point $B_{2}$ around point $C$ in a negative direction by an angle $\gamma$, arriving at point $B_{3}$.

Construct the triangle if the points $B, B_{3}$, and the center $O$ of the incircle of triangle $ABC$ are given. Also, examine how the solution changes depending on the arrangement of the three given points.

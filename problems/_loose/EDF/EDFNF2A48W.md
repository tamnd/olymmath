Integers $a,b$ and $c$ satisfy the equality $a+b+c=0$ . Denote $S=ab+bc+ac$ , $A=a^2+a+1$ , $B=b^2+b+1$ and $C=c^2+c+1$ .
Prove that the number $(S+A)(S+B)(S+C)$ is a perfect square.

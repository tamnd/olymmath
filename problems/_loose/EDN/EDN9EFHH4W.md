Let's prove that a triangle is right-angled if and only if the following relationship holds between the radius $r$ of the circumscribed circle, the radius $\varrho$ of the inscribed circle, the distance $d$ between the centers of these circles, and the area $t$ of the triangle:

$$
t=\varrho^{2}+r^{2}-d^{2}
$$

Decide whether for every sequence $(a_n)$ of positive real numbers,

$\frac{3^{a_1}+3^{a_2}+\cdots+3^{a_n}}{(2^{a_1}+2^{a_2}+\cdots+2^{a_n})^2} < \frac{1}{2024}$

is true for at least one positive integer $n$ .

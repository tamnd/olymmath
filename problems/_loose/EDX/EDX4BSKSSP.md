The eccentricity $ e = \sqrt{\frac{2}{2}} $ of the ellipse $ E $ is centered at the origin $ O $, with its foci on the $ x $-axis. A line $ l $ with slope $ k $ (where $ k \in \mathbf{R} $) passing through the point $ C(-1,0) $ intersects the ellipse at points $ A $ and $ B $, and satisfies $ \overrightarrow{B A} = (\lambda + 1) \overrightarrow{B C} $ ($ \lambda \geqslant 3 $).
(1) For a fixed $ \lambda $, when the area of $ \triangle O A B $ is maximized, find the equation of the ellipse $ E $;
(2) If $ \lambda $ varies, and $ \lambda = k^2 + 1 $. Determine the values of the real number $ \lambda $ and the slope $ k $ of the line $ l $ when the length of the major axis of the ellipse $ E $ is maximized. Also, find the equation of the ellipse at this time.

On the side $A B C$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$, a point $O$ is given, through which lines $\overline{O A_{1}}, \overline{O B_{1}}$ and $\overline{O C_{1}}$ are drawn parallel to the edges $\overline{D A}, \overline{D B}$ and $\overline{D C}$, intersecting the sides of the tetrahedron at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{\left|O A_{1}\right|}{|D A|}+\frac{\left|O B_{1}\right|}{|D B|}+\frac{\left|O C_{1}\right|}{|D C|}=1
$$

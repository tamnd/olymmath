For $f(t)=t\cos t,\ g(t)=t\sin t$, find the maximum value of
$$\frac{f'(t)f''(t)+g'(t)g''(t)}{\sqrt{f'(t)^2+g'(t)^2}\sqrt{f''(t)^2+g''(t)^2}}$$

and evaluate $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(t)g'(t)\ dt.$

On 5 cards, natural numbers from 1 to 10 are written (each exactly once, one on each side). It is known that on each card, one of the numbers divides the other. The cards are laid out on the table so that the numbers on the top sides are visible, while the numbers on the bottom sides are not. Can the numbers on the reverse sides of the cards be uniquely determined?

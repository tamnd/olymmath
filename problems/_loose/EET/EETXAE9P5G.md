Given four lines $m_{1}, m_{2}, m_{3}, m_{4}$ intersecting at point $O$, through any point $A_{1}$ on line $m_{1}$, draw a line parallel to $m_{4}$ intersecting line $m_{2}$ at $A_{2}$, through $A_{2}$ draw a line parallel to $m_{1}$ intersecting $m_{3}$ at $A_{3}$, through $A_{3}$ draw a line parallel to $m_{2}$ intersecting $m_{4}$ at $A_{4}$, through $A_{4}$ draw a line parallel to $m_{3}$ intersecting $m_{1}$ at point $B$. Prove:
$$
O B \leqslant \frac{O A_{1}}{4} \text {. }
$$

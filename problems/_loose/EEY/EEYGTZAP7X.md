Given a square $ABCD$ inscribed in a circle, the midpoint of the shorter arc $AB$ is $E$. A line $e$ passing through $E$ intersects $AB$ at $P$ and $CD$ at $Q$. For which lines $e$ is the following statement true?

"The hyperbola, whose asymptotes are parallel to the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$, with its center at the midpoint of segment $PQ$, and which passes through $P$, is tangent to the circle inscribed in the square."

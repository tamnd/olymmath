In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, prove:
$$
\text{(1)} \begin{array}{l}
\cos A + \cos B + \cos C \\
\leqslant \frac{1}{3}(\sin A + \sin B + \sin C)(\cot A + \cot B + \cot C) \text{; }
\end{array}
$$
(2)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
(a + \cos A)(b + \cos B)(c + \cos C) \\
\geqslant (a + \cos B)(b + \cos C)(c + \cos A) .
\end{array}
$$

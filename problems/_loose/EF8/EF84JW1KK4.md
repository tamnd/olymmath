Let $n \ge 2$ be a positive integer and $a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_{2n}$ be real numbers. If $S = \sum_{i=1}^{2n} a_i$, $A_1 = \sum_{i,j,i<j} a_{2i}a_{2j}$ and $A_2 = \sum_{i,j,i<j} a_{2i-1}a_{2j-1}$, prove the inequality
$$
(n-1)S^2 > 4n(A_1 + A_2).
$$

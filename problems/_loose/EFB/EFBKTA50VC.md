Point $Q$ lies outside the circle $\omega_{1} \cdot Q A$ and $Q B$ are tangents to the circle ( $A$ and $B$ belong to $\boldsymbol{\omega}_{1}$ ). Through points $A$ and $B$, a second circle $\omega_{2}$ is drawn with its center at point $Q$. On the arc $A B$ of circle $\omega_{2}$, which lies inside circle $\omega_{1}$, an arbitrary point $K$ is taken. The line $A K$ intersects circle $\omega_{1}$ again at point $C$, and the line $B K$ intersects circle $\omega_{1}$ again at point $D$. Prove that $C D$ is a diameter of circle $\omega_{1}$.

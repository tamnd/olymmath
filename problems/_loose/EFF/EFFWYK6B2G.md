Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle with orthocenter $H$. Segments $A H$ and $C H$ intersect segments $B C$ and $A B$ in points $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively. The segments $B H$ and $A_{1} C_{1}$ meet at point $D$. Let $P$ be the midpoint of the segment $B H$. Let $D^{\prime}$ be the reflection of the point $D$ in $A C$. Prove that quadrilateral $A P C D^{\prime}$ is cyclic.

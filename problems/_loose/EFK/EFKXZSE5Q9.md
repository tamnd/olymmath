In a competition for composing and solving math problems, each of the three classes had to compose 5 problems and offer them for solving to the other two classes, as well as solve the problems composed by the rival classes. For each correctly solved problem, 5 points were awarded to the class that solved it, and for each unsolved problem, 4 points were awarded to the class that authored it. When the results were tallied, it turned out that 5A solved only one of the problems composed by 5B and 4 problems composed by 5C; 5B solved 3 problems composed by 5C and 2 problems composed by 5A; 5C solved 4 problems composed by 5A but did not solve the same number of problems composed by 5B. How many points did each class score, and how were the places distributed among them?

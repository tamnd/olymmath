Five friends must get off a five-seat chairlift and can do so by going in three different directions: left, straight, or right. Getting off a chairlift, it is easy to collide with your fellow riders. For example: if I decide to go straight and someone to my left decides to go right, we collide; the same happens if I decide to go right and someone to my right goes straight (or left); if, however, someone goes in the same direction as me, we do not collide; and so on. If each of the five friends chooses at random where to go, with a probability of $1 / 3$ for each direction, what is the probability that there are no collisions?
(A) $\frac{25}{27}$
(B) $\frac{3}{5}$
(C) $\frac{40}{81}$
(D) $\frac{1}{3}$
(E) $\frac{7}{81}$

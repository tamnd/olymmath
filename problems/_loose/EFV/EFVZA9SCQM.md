A deck has 900 cards numbered from 100 to 999. Cards whose sum of digits is the same have the same color, and cards with different sums have different colors. Alice, Bia, Carla, and Dani each take a certain number of cards.

a) All the cards Alice took had different colors. What is the maximum number of cards Alice could have taken?

b) Bia took all the cards that contain the digit 1. How many cards did Bia take?

c) Carla took all the cards with exactly 2 identical digits. How many cards did Carla take?

d) Dani said: - I took two cards of the same color with consecutive numbers. Is it possible for this to have happened?

Let the vertices of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ be connected to the centroid $S$ by segments that are divided in the ratio $4: 5$ by points $K, L, M, N$, and let us draw planes through these points parallel to the faces $B C D, A C D, A B D$, and $A B C$ respectively. It is to be proven that these planes divide the edges of the tetrahedron into three equal segments.

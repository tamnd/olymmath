Let $a,b$ be postive numbers. In $xy$ plane, let $C$ be the part of the ellipse $E: \ ax^2+by^2=1\ (x\geq 0,\ y\leq 0),$
and $l_t$ be the half-line $y=tx\ (x\geq 0)$ with slope $t\geq 0.$ When the segment $PP'$ joining the point on $l_t$ and the point $P'$ on $C$ moves parallel to $y$-axis, denote $P_t$ be $P$ which maximize the length of $PP'$ and denote $C'$ be the curve ploted by $P_t.$ Let $Q(\alpha,\ \beta)$ be the intersection point of $E$ and $C'.$

(1) Find the equation $y=f(x)$ of the curve $C'.$
(2) Find $\alpha$ and $\beta.$
(3) Let $D$ be the region enclosed by the line $y=\beta$ and the curve $C'.$ Find the volume $V$ formed by the revolution of $D$ about $y$-axis.

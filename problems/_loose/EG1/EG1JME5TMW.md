There are 100 pies arranged in a circle, 53 of which are with cabbage, and the rest are with rice. Alexei knows which ones are with what, and he wants to choose 67 consecutive pies such that among them there are exactly $k$ with cabbage. For which $k$ is he guaranteed to be able to do this regardless of the arrangement of the pies? Provide all possible options and prove that there are no others.

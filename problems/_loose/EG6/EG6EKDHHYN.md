Jörg writes down the following equation:

$$
\frac{1}{a+b}+\frac{1}{c+d}=\frac{1}{(a+b)(c+d)}
$$

Michael claims that it is "wrong." Jörg, who is not easily "convinced," chooses numbers for the variables $a, b, c,$ and $d$, substitutes them into equation (1), and to Michael's surprise, obtains a true statement.

Determine all possibilities for selecting $a, b, c,$ and $d$ from the numbers $-1, 0, 1$ such that equation (1) is satisfied!

Determine for which values of the real parameter $k$ the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
|x+6|+2|y| & =24 \\
|x+y|+|x-y| & =2 k
\end{aligned}
$$

has an odd number of solutions in the set of real numbers.

(Pavel Calábek)

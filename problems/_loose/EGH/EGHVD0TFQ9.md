a) Given $\frac{1}{2}\left(3^{n}-3\right)$ coins $(n \geqslant 2)$, among which there is one counterfeit coin that differs in weight from the genuine ones. Using $n$ weighings on a balance scale without weights, find the counterfeit coin and determine whether it is heavier or lighter than the genuine ones.

b) Do the same in the case when there are fewer than $\frac{1}{2}\left(3^{n}-3\right)$ coins, but more than two.

In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{c}
A+C=2 B, \tan A \tan C=2+\sqrt{3} \\
\Leftrightarrow A=\frac{\pi}{4}, B=\frac{\pi}{3}, C=\frac{5 \pi}{12}
\end{array}
$$

or
$$
A=\frac{5 \pi}{12}, B=\frac{\pi}{3}, C=\frac{\pi}{4} .
$$

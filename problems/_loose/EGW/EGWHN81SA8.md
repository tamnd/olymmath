Consider 18 points in the plane such that no three of them are collinear and such that 6 of them are colored red, 6 others are blue, and the last 6 are green. Let $A$ be the sum of the areas of all triangles formed by these points. Show that the sum of the areas of the triangles having all their vertices of the same color cannot exceed $\frac{A}{4}$.

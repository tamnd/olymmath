In city $N$, all roads are currently two-way. It is decided to renovate all roads within two years. For this purpose, some roads will become one-way in the first year; by the second year, these roads will revert to two-way, while the rest will be restricted to one-way. It is known that at any time during the renovation, it is possible to drive from any location in the city to any other location. Prove that all roads in city $N$ can be converted to one-way such that it is still possible to drive from any location in the city to any other location.

For the sequences $\left(a_{n}\right)$ and $\left(b_{n}\right)$, each term starting from the third is equal to the sum of the previous two. Find the intersection of the sets $A=\left\{a_{n} \mid n \in \mathbb{N}\right\}$ and $B=\left\{b_{n} \mid n \in \mathbb{N}\right\}$, if $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2, b_{1}=2$ and $b_{2}=1$.

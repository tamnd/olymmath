Let $y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}, \cdots \cdots$ be a sequence, where $y_{1}=1$, and for integers $k>0$, we have
$$
\begin{array}{l}
y_{2 k}=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
2 y_{k} & k \text { is even } \\
2 y_{k}+1 & k \text { is odd, }
\end{array}\right. \\
y_{2 k+1}=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
2 y_{k} & k \text { is odd } \\
2 y_{k}+1 & k \text { is even. }
\end{array}\right.
\end{array}
$$

Prove: Every natural number appears exactly once in the sequence $y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}, \cdots \cdots$.

Prove that if

$$
p+q+r=1
$$

and

$$
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{r}=0
$$

then

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=(p a+q b+r c)^{2}+(q a+r b+p c)^{2}+(r a+p b+q c)^{2}
$$

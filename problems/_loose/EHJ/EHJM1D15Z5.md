Consider $\mathrm{ABC}$ a generic triangle and $\mathrm{B}^{\prime}, \mathrm{C}^{\prime}$ such that: $A C=A C^{\prime},\left(\overrightarrow{A C}, \overrightarrow{A C^{\prime}}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}[2 \pi]$ and $A B=A B^{\prime},\left(\overrightarrow{A B}, \overrightarrow{A B^{\prime}}\right)=-\frac{\pi}{2}[2 \pi]$. Let I be the midpoint of [BC] and I' the midpoint of [ $\left.\mathrm{B}^{\prime} \mathrm{C}^{\prime}\right]$. Show that $(A I) \perp\left(B^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right), 2 A I=B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ and $\left(A I^{\prime}\right) \perp(B C), 2 A I^{\prime}=B C$.

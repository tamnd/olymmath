In the isosceles $\mathrm{Rt} \triangle A B C$, $A D$ is the median on the right-angled side $B C$, and $B E \perp A D$ intersects $A C$ at $E, E F \perp B C$. If $A B=B C=a$, then $E F$ equals
(A) $\frac{1}{3} a$.
(B) $\frac{1}{2} a$.
(C) $\frac{2}{3} a$.
(D) $\frac{2}{5} a$.

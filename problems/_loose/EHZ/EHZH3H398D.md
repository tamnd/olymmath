$$
\frac{(a+b)(c-x)}{a^{2}}-\frac{(b+c)(x-2 c)}{b c}-\frac{(c+a)(c-2 x)}{a c}=\frac{(a+b) c}{a b}+2
$$

Examine the case when $a: b: c=6: 3: 4$.

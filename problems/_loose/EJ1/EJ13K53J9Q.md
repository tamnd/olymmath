Let's consider $n$ events, each of which has a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$, and the joint probability of any two of them occurring is $\frac{1}{4}$.

(a) Prove that the probability that none of them occur is at most $\frac{1}{n+1}$.

(b) Show that there are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ for which the events can be defined in such a way that the probability that none of them occur is exactly $\frac{1}{n+1}$.

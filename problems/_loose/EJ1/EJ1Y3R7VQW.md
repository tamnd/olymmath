At the city festival of the Fairy Tale Resort, white, green, and red balloons were used. A bored schoolboy decided to determine what percentage of the total number of balloons each color of balloon represented. After counting the results, it turned out that the three groups of balloons added up to $146 \%$. The schoolboy found his mistake; it turned out that he had mistakenly calculated the percentage of white balloons not from the total number of all balloons, but only from the number of green and white ones (the other percentages he calculated correctly). It is known that there are more than 1000 green balloons. Prove that there are more than 850 white balloons. (7 points)

Let $a_{n}=1-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}+\ldots+(-1)^{n} \frac{1}{2 n+1}$.

a) Prove that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n}=\pi / 4$.

b) Prove that $\frac{1}{2(4 k+1)} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{4}-a_{2 k-1} \leqslant \frac{1}{4 k+1}$.

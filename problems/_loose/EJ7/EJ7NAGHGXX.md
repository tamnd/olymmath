Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers such that $x y z=1$. Prove that

$$
\left(x^{4}+\frac{z^{2}}{y^{2}}\right)\left(y^{4}+\frac{x^{2}}{z^{2}}\right)\left(z^{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{x^{2}}\right) \geq\left(\frac{x^{2}}{y}+1\right)\left(\frac{y^{2}}{z}+1\right)\left(\frac{z^{2}}{x}+1\right)
$$

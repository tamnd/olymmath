Let $f: I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be convex. Show that for all $a_{1}, \ldots a_{n} \in I$ and for all $\lambda_{1}, \ldots, \lambda_{n} \in[0,1]$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i}=1$, show that

$$
f\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} a_{i}\right) \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} f\left(a_{i}\right)
$$

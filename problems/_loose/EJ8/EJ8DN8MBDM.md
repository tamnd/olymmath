In a resort, three people jointly filled out 30 regular lottery tickets. For this, $A$ contributed 25 Ft, $B 36$ Ft, and $C 38$ Ft. They managed to win with more than one ticket and received 1100 Ft. From this, they bought 30 monthly (5-week) and $20 \mathrm{db} 1$ weekly tickets for the following week, and divided the remaining amount so that $C$ received twice as much as $B$, and $B $ received twice as much as $A$. They won again, receiving 73000 Ft. They could no longer play together, so they agreed to divide the winnings according to the ratio of their second-week contributions, and that each would take 10 of the remaining tickets. How much cash did each person receive?

Inside triangle $ABC$, an arbitrary point $M$ is taken. Through the vertices of the triangle and this point, three segments are drawn until they intersect the opposite sides. Prove that among these segments, two can be chosen such that point $M$ divides one of them (measured from the vertex) in the ratio $\geq 2$, and the other in the ratio $\leq 2$.

On the sides $BC, CA, AB$ of any $\triangle ABC$, there are points $M, N, L$ respectively. Let $Q$ be any point inside $\triangle ABC$, and the lines $AQ, BQ, CQ$ intersect the segments $NL, LM, MN$ at $M_{1}, N_{1}, L_{1}$ respectively. Prove that the three lines $MM_{1}, NN_{1}, LL_{1}$ are concurrent if and only if the three lines $AM, BN, CL$ are concurrent, and this is independent of the position of point $Q$.

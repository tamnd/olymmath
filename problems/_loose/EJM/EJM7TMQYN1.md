A permutation $ \pi$ is selected randomly through all $ n$-permutations.
a) if $$ C_a(\pi)=\text{the number of cycles of length }a\text{ in }\pi$$ then prove that $ E(C_a(\pi))=\frac1a$
b) Prove that if $ \{a_1,a_2,\dots,a_k\}\subset\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ the probability that $ \pi$ does not have any cycle with lengths $ a_1,\dots,a_k$ is at most $ \frac1{\sum_{i=1}^ka_i}$

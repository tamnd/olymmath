Given a regular tetrahedron $S-ABC$ with height $SO=3$, and the side length of the base is 6. Draw a perpendicular from point $A$ to the opposite face $SBC$, and let the foot of the perpendicular be $D$. On $AD$, take a point $P$ such that $\frac{AP}{PD}=8$. Find the area of the section passing through point $P$ and parallel to the base.

$A$ is an acute angle, $\lg (1+\sin A)=m, \lg \frac{1}{1-\sin A}=n$, then the value of $\lg \cos A$ is
(A) $m+\frac{1}{n}$.
(B) $m-n$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2}\left(m+\frac{1}{n}\right)$.
(D) $\frac{1}{2}(m-n)$.
(E) $2(m-n)$.
(Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

In space, a set $S$ is given, consisting of 100 points such that no three are collinear and no four are coplanar. Each pair of points in the set $S$ is connected by segments, and then the segments are colored such that exactly 2017 of them are colored red, and the remaining ones are colored blue. Prove that there exists a triangle with vertices in $S$ whose exactly one side is colored blue or a tetrahedron with vertices in $S$ whose exactly one edge is colored red.

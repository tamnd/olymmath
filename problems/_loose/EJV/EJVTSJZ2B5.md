Consider an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ with $|A B|<|A C|$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of segments $\overline{B C}$ and $\overline{A B}$, respectively. The circle with diameter $\overline{A B}$ intersects the lines $B C, A M$ and $A C$ at $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. Let $G$ be the midpoint of $\overline{F C}$. Prove that the lines $N F, D E$ and $G M$ are concurrent.

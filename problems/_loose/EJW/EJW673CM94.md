Determine all polynomials $\mathrm{P} \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{X}]$ such that:

(i) $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{n}) \geqslant 1$ for all $n \geqslant 1$

(ii) $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{mn})$ and $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{m}) \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{n})$ have the same number of prime divisors for all $m, n \geqslant 1$.

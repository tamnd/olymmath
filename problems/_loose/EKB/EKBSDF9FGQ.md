The hundredth question: For an integer $m$, let $q(m)$ denote the largest prime factor of $m$ (with the convention $q(1)=q(-1)=1, q(0)=+\infty$). Find all integer-coefficient polynomials $f(x)$ such that $\operatorname{deg} f \geq 1$, and the sequence $\left\{q\left(f\left(n^{2}\right)\right)-2 n\right\}_{n \in N}$ is bounded above.

Giovanna has arranged 9 coins in a row. Some show the head side, others the cross side, in this sequence: CCTTCTTCC. She plays this game: with each move, she chooses two consecutive coins and flips both of them. Giovanna, with several moves of this type, wants to achieve a row of coins arranged in the sequence TCTCTCTCT. What can be concluded?

(A) She cannot succeed.

(B) She can succeed with a minimum of 4 moves.

(C) She can succeed with a minimum of 6 moves.

(D) She can succeed with a minimum of 8 moves.

(E) She can succeed with an odd number of moves.

Consider the function $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$, which satisfies the relation: $(f \circ f)(x) = x^3 + x - 8 (\forall) x \in \mathbf{R}$.

Knowing that any polynomial equation $a_{n} X^{n} + a_{n-1} X^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{1} X + a_{0} = 0$ with real coefficients and odd degree $n$ has at least one real root:

a) show that the function $f$ is bijective,

b) Calculate $f(2)$.

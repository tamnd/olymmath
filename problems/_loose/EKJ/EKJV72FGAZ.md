Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be a regular $n$-gon. For all $i \in\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$, let $X_{i}$ be the intersection of the lines $\left(A_{i-2} A_{i-1}\right)$ and $\left(A_{i} A_{i+1}\right)$. Suppose that the points $X_{i}$ are all inside a circle $\Gamma$. Let $\omega_{i}$ be the circle tangent to the rays $\left[A_{i-1} X_{i}\right)$ and $\left[A_{i} X_{i}\right)$ beyond $X_{i}$ and internally tangent to $\Gamma$ at $T_{i}$. Let $\Omega_{i}$ be the circle tangent to the rays $\left[A_{i-1} X_{i}\right)$ and $\left[A_{i} X_{i}\right)$ beyond $X_{i}$ and externally tangent to $\Gamma$ at $S_{i}$.

a) Show that the $n$ lines $\left(X_{i} T_{i}\right)$ are concurrent.

b) Show that the $n$ lines $\left(X_{i} S_{i}\right)$ are concurrent.

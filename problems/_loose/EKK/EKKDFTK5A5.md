Let $A B C$ be a right triangle at $A$, with $A \widehat{B} C=15^{\circ}$. Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ and let $J, K$ be the projections of $H$ onto $A B$ and $A C$. Knowing that the area of $A J H K$ is $45 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, how many $\mathrm{cm}^{2}$ is the product $B J \cdot C K$?

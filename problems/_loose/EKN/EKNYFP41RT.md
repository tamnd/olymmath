Let's call a natural number $N=\overline{a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{k}}$ an anti-square if $a_{k} \neq 0$ and the number $\overline{a_{k} a_{k-1} \ldots a_{1}}$ is a perfect square (for example, the numbers 94 and 441 are anti-squares, while the numbers 51 and 190 are not). Prove that the number $400 \ldots 005$ (2023 zeros) cannot be represented as the sum of two anti-squares. (20 points)

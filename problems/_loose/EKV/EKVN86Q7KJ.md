Without Euler's theorem, show that the order is well-defined.

The interest of the order lies in the following proposition:

Proposition 55. Let $x \in(\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z})^{*}$, and $\mathfrak{n} \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then $x^{n}=\overline{1}$ if and only if $\omega(x)$ divides $n$.

Given is a triangular pyramid $A B C S$, whose base $A B C$ forms an equilateral triangle with a side length of $4 \mathrm{~cm}$, and whose apex $S$ is positioned such that the perpendicular from $S$ to the plane through $A, B, C$ has the centroid $F$ of the base as its foot, and that $F S$ has a length of 6 $\mathrm{cm}$.

This pyramid is to be represented in a two-plane projection. It is required that the side face $A B S$ lies in the ground plane. To be constructed is the projection using only a compass and ruler from the given lengths $4 \mathrm{~cm}, 6 \mathrm{~cm}$.

Note: A description and justification of the construction are not required. For example, the required projection can be obtained from another representation in which the equilateral triangle $A B C$ lies in the ground plane.

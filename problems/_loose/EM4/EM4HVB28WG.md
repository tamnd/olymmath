In an urn, there are only white and black balls, the number of which, rounded to the nearest hundred, is 1000. The probability of drawing two black balls is $\frac{17}{43}$ greater than the probability of drawing two white balls. How many white and how many black balls are in the urn? (The probability of drawing any ball is the same.)

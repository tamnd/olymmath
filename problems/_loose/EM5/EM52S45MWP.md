Given a circle $K$ and a line $e$ that intersects $K$ at two distinct points $P$ and $Q$. Let $F$ be the midpoint of the arc $PQ$, and consider those circles $k$ that are externally tangent to $K$ on the side of $e$ not containing $F$, and also tangent to the line $e$.

Prove that the length of the tangent segment from $F$ to any of the circles $k$ is the same!

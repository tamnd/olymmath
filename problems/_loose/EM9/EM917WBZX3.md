Arbitrarily orient the edges of a convex polyhedron such that every vertex has at least one incoming edge and one outgoing edge. Show that there exist two faces that are consistently oriented. You may introduce for each $s \in G$, $c(s)$ the number of orientation changes in the cyclic order of edges around $s$, and for each face $F$, the number $c(F)$ of orientation changes in the cyclic order of edges around $F$.

A greyhound chases a hare. Initially, the hare has an $n$-jump lead and the hare makes $a$ jumps while the greyhound runs $b$ steps, but $d$ greyhound steps equal $c$ hare jumps in distance. How many jumps can the hare make before the dog catches up, and how many steps did the latter take in the meantime? (E.g., $n=34, a=11, b=8, c=9, d=5$.) - Solve the problem if the hare has an $n(=34)$ greyhound step lead.

Four, (50 points) Find all positive integers $c$ such that there exists a positive constant $T$, for which, for any positive integer $n$, we have
$$
[1,2, \cdots, n] \leqslant T \cdot 2^{c n},
$$

where $[1,2, \cdots, n]$ denotes the least common multiple of $1,2, \cdots, n$.

Let $P=\{$ natural numbers less than 3 $\}$, and define the function $f$ on $P$ as follows:
If $n \in P, f(n)$ represents the smallest natural number that is not a divisor of $n$, for example, $f(7)=2, f(12)=5$, etc.
Let the range of $f(n)$ be the set $M$. Prove that $19 \in M, 88 \notin M$.
(China Beijing High School Grade 1 Mathematics Competition, 1988)

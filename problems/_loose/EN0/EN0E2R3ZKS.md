In ancient times, a day and night were divided into twelve hours, each hour lasting 2 hours, named by the twelve Earthly Branches "Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, Xi, Xu, Hai". Midnight 23:00～1:00 is Zi hour, 1: $00 \sim 3: 00$ is Chou hour, 3: $00 \sim 5: 00$ is Yin hour, and so on. In ancient times, the time from the Dog hour to the Yin hour was divided into five watches, each watch being one hour, or 120 minutes, with the Dog hour as the first watch, the Pig hour as the second watch, and the Rat hour as the third watch.

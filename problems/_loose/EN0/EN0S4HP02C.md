Let $p$ be a prime of the form $4k+1$, and satisfy $p-1<3 \varphi(p-1)$, where $\varphi(n)$ denotes the Euler's function. Prove: There are at least $\frac{3 \varphi(p-1)-(p-1)}{2}$ positive integers $k$, such that $k$ is a primitive root modulo $p$, and $(k, p-1)=1$.

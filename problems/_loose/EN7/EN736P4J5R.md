There is a figure of prince on a field of a $6 \times 6$ square chessboard. The prince can in one move jump either horizontally or vertically. The lengths of the jumps are alternately either one or two fields, and the jump on the next field is the first one. Decide, whether one can chose the initial field for the prince, so that the prince visits in an appropriate sequence of 35 jumps every field of the chessboard.

(Peter Novotný)

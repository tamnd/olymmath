In a class of EOS, $75 \%$ of the students subscribe to the magazine "Science and Progress," and 35 $\%$ subscribe to the magazine "Urania."

The truth value of the following statements is to be determined (true, false, not decidable).

a) All students in the class have subscribed to one of the two magazines, but not both.

b) The majority of students who subscribe to "Urania" are also subscribers to "Wissenschaft und Fortschritt."

c) The smaller part of the subscribers to "Wissenschaft und Fortschritt" also subscribe to "Urania."

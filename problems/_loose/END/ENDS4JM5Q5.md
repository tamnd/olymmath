There are $n(n \geqslant 6)$ people who greet each other by phone before the Spring Festival. It is known:
1. Each person can make phone calls to at most $\left[\frac{n-1}{2}\right]$ others;
2. Among any 3 people, there must be 2 who have talked on the phone.

Prove: These $n$ people can be divided into two disjoint groups such that any two people within the same group have talked on the phone.

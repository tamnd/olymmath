From $n^{2}$ bulbs, an $n \times n$ display was assembled. Each bulb has two states - on and off. When pressing any bulb, its state remains unchanged, while all bulbs in the same row or column change their state to the opposite. Initially, all bulbs on the display are off. Petya sequentially pressed several bulbs, as a result of which the display did not turn off completely. What is the smallest number of bulbs that can be on the display?

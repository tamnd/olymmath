Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the minimum possible value of
$$
S = 2^0 x_0^2 + 2^1 x_1^2 + \dots + 2^n x_n^2,
$$
where $x_0, x_1, \dots, x_n$ are nonnegative integers such that $x_0 + x_1 + \dots + x_n = n$ .

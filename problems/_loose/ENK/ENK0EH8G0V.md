In space, two segments $A B$ and $A_{1} B_{1}$ are given. Prove that the distance between their midpoints $M$ and $M_{1}$ does not exceed half the sum of the distances $A A_{1}$ and $B B_{1}$ between the endpoints of the segments and is not less than (the absolute value of) half the difference of the distances $A A_{1}$ and $B B_{1}$.

In which case $M M_{1}=\frac{1}{2}\left(A A_{1}+B B_{1}\right)$? And when $M M_{1}=\frac{1}{2}\left|A A_{1}-B B_{1}\right|$?

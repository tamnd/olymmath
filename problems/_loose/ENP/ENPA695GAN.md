Given triangle $A B C$. Points $K, L$ and $M$ are placed on the plane such that triangles $K A M, C L M$ and $K L B$ are equal to triangle $K L M$. What inequality sign should be placed between the perimeter of triangle $K L M$ and the semiperimeter of triangle $A B C?$

The vertices of the triangles are given in an arbitrary order: for example, it cannot be asserted that in the equality of triangles $K A M$ and $C L M$, point $K$ corresponds to point $C$.

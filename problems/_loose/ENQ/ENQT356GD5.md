Prove that there exists $f_{\mathbf{3}} \mathbf{N} \rightarrow \mathbf{N}$, satisfying
$$
f^{(k)}(n)=n+a(n \in \mathbf{N})
$$

if and only if $a$ is a non-negative integer and $k \mid a$.

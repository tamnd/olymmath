Let $x, y, n$ be natural numbers with $x \geq 3, n \geq 2$ and

$$
x^{2}+5=y^{n}
$$

Show that every prime divisor $p$ of $n$ satisfies the congruence $p \equiv 1(\bmod 4)$.

Fyodorovna's Tea Party. (7th grade. 3 points). Fyodorovna has 3 teacups. One day, she washed them and started a new life. Each time she wants to drink tea, Fyodorovna randomly picks one of the cups, and then puts it back with the others, but does not wash it again. Find the probability that during the fifth tea party since the start of her new life, Fyodorovna uses the last clean cup.

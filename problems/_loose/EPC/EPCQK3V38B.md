Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral. The orthogonal projections of $D$ on the lines $B C$ and $B A$ are denoted by $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, respectively.

The segment $A_{1} C_{1}$ meets the diagonal $A C$ at an interior point $B_{1}$ such that $D B_{1} \geq D A_{1}$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is cyclic if and only if

$$
\frac{B C}{D A_{1}}+\frac{B A}{D C_{1}}=\frac{A C}{D B_{1}}
$$

Nikolai Nikolov

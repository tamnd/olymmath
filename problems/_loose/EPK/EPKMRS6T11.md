An equilateral triangle is divided by lines parallel to its sides into equal equilateral triangles. One of the small triangles is black, and the others are white. It is allowed to repaint simultaneously all triangles intersected by a line parallel to any side of the original triangle. Is it always possible to achieve, with several such repaintings, that all small triangles become white?

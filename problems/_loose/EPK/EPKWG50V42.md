At the base of the quadrilateral prism $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ lies a rhombus $A B C D$, in which $B D=3$ and $\angle A D C=60^{\circ}$. A sphere passes through the vertices $D, C, B, B_{1}, A_{1}, D_{1}$.

a) Find the area of the circle obtained by the intersection of the sphere with the plane passing through the points $A_{1}, C_{1}$, and $D_{1}$.

b) Find the angle $B_{1} C_{1} A$.

c) Suppose it is additionally known that the radius of the sphere is 2. Find the volume of the prism.

In the isosceles triangle $ABC$, the base is $BC$, the height is $AH$, and $BC = AH = 2R$. The base $BC$ lies on the line $e$. We construct a circle with radius $R$ that touches the line $e$, and a line $e'$ parallel to $e$ at a distance $d$ from $e$. The line $e'$ intersects the circle at the chord $MN$ and the triangle at the segment $KL$.

$1^{0}$. For what value of $d$ will $MN = KL$?

$2^{0}$. Let $2R = 1$ and plot the variation of the difference $KL - MN$ as $d$ varies from 0 to 1.

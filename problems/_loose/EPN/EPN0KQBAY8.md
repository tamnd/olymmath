Given is an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ where $|A C|<|B C|$. Its altitudes $\overline{A D}$ and $\overline{B E}$ intersect at the orthocenter $H$. The segments $\overline{D E}$ and $\overline{C H}$ intersect at point $I$, and the lines $D E$ and $A B$ intersect at point $X$. Let $H_{1}$ be the orthocenter of triangle $X A C$, and $H_{2}$ be the orthocenter of triangle $X I C$.

If $\left|A H_{1}\right|=\left|I H_{2}\right|$, prove that $|A I|=\left|D H_{2}\right|$.

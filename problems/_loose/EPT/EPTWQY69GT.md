Given real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy
$$
x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}+2 x y z=1 \text {. }
$$

Prove: $8 x y z \leqslant 1$ if and only if $(x, y, z)$ is one of the following cases when the equality holds:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right),\left(-\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right), \\
\left(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\right),\left(\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

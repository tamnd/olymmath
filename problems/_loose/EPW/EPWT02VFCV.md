Let the sides of the convex, positively oriented pentagon $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4} A_{5}$ be denoted by $a_{i}$ for $(i=1,2,3,4)$, and the angle of rotation from the side line $a_{i}$ to the side line $a_{j}$ by $\alpha_{i j}$ for $(1 \leq i<j \leq 4)$. Prove that the area of the pentagon is given by half of the following sum:

$$
a_{1} a_{2} \sin \alpha_{12}+a_{1} a_{3} \sin \alpha_{13}+a_{1} a_{4} \sin \alpha_{14}+a_{2} a_{3} \sin \alpha_{23}+a_{2} a_{4} \sin \alpha_{24}+a_{3} a_{4} \sin \alpha_{34}
$$

In chess, a rook may move any number of squares only either horizontally or vertically. In how many ways can a rook from the bottom left corner of an $8\times8$ chessboard reach the top right corner in exactly $\mathbf{4}$ moves? (The rook must not be on the top right corner prior to the 4th move.)

Let $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be two intersecting circles, and let one of their common points be $A$. The one common tangent of the two circles touches $k_{1}$ at $P$ and $k_{2}$ at $Q$, while the other common tangent touches $k_{1}$ at $R$ and $k_{2}$ at $S$. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $P A Q$ and $R A S$ are tangent to each other.

Two balls, the sizes of which can be neglected in this problem, move along a circle. When moving in the same direction, they meet every 20 seconds, and when moving in opposite directions - every 4 seconds. It is known that when moving towards each other along the circle, the distance between the approaching balls decreases by 75 cm every 3 seconds (until they meet). Find the speed of the slower ball (in cm/sec).

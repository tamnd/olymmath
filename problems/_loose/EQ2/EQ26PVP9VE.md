Let $a, b, c, d$ be integers, and the roots of the following four equations in $x$
$$
\begin{array}{rr}
(a-2 b) x=1, & (b-3 c) x=1, \\
(c-4 d) x=1, & x+100=d,
\end{array}
$$

are all positive. Then the smallest possible value of $a$ is
(A) 2433 .
(B) 2425 .
(C) 2401 .
(D) 2400 .
(China Guangzhou, Wuhan, Fuzhou, etc. Five Cities Junior High School Mathematics League, 1990)

8-149 Arrange a deck of cards with four suits in a row. If two cards of the same suit are adjacent or separated by only one card, then the left one of the two is removed. Additionally, you can add as many cards as you like from other decks of the same cards to the right end. Try to prove that it is possible to add and remove cards in this way so that only 4 cards remain in the end.

Two Diggers. This curious problem is actually more difficult than it might seem at first glance. Abraham, a frail old man, contracted to dig a ditch for 2 dollars. He hired Benjamin, a strapping young man, to help him. The money was to be divided in proportion to each man's digging abilities. Abraham digs as fast as Benjamin shovels out the dirt, and Benjamin digs four times as fast as Abraham shovels out the dirt.

How should they divide the money? Of course, we assume that the strength ratio of the old man to the young man is the same for digging as it is for shoveling.

Five sisters, named Mária, Malvin, Margit, Márta, and Magda, exchanged gifts at Christmas. Each girl spent 140 forints on gifts for the other four sisters, and each received four gifts totaling 140 forints. The price of each gift, expressed in forints, was a whole number divisible by 10. Each of the five girls distributed the 140 forints differently, but in such a way that each of the four gifts they bought was of a different value.

The most expensive gift was bought by Mária for Márta. The next most expensive gift was bought by Malvin for Magda. Margit spent the most on Mária and the least on Márta. Malvin received gifts of equal value from all her sisters except Margit. Margit received a more expensive gift from Magda than from any other sister. How much did Magda spend on Mária?

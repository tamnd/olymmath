82a*. Five complex numbers $c_{1}, c_{2}, c_{3}, c_{4}$, and $c_{5}$ are related by the equation

$$
\frac{1}{c_{1}}+\frac{1}{c_{2}}+\frac{1}{c_{3}}+\frac{1}{c_{4}}+\frac{1}{c_{5}}=0
$$

Prove that if the points in the plane representing the complex numbers $c_{1}, c_{2}, c_{3}, c_{4}$, and $c_{5}$ lie at the vertices of a convex pentagon, then the point 0 is inside this pentagon.

b*. Given $n$ complex numbers $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{n}$ such that the points representing them in the plane are the vertices of a convex $n$-gon. Prove that if

$$
\frac{1}{z-c_{1}}+\frac{1}{z-c_{2}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{z-c_{n}}=0
$$

then the point corresponding to $z$ lies inside this $n$-gon.

83* Prove that if the number $\alpha$ is irrational, then the function $f(x)=\cos x+\cos \alpha x$ is non-periodic.

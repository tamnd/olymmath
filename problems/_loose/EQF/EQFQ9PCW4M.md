There are two boxes, one with 2017 candies, and the other with 2018. Two players take turns. On each turn, a player can eat any non-zero number of candies from any one box. The rules of the game do not allow the number of candies in one box to be divisible by the number of candies in the other after any move. The player who cannot make a move without violating this condition loses. Who can win: the player starting the game or the second player, regardless of how their opponent plays?

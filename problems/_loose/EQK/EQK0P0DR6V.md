The arithmetic progression $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with a non-zero difference is such that the sequence $b_{n}=a_{n} \cdot \cos a_{n}$ is also an arithmetic progression with a non-zero difference. Find the possible values of the first term and the difference of the progression $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, if for all $n$ the equality $\sin 2 a_{n}+\cos a_{n+1}=0$ holds.

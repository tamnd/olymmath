On each vertex of a cube, write a distinct natural number, and on each edge, write the greatest common divisor (GCD) of the two numbers at its ends. Is it possible for the sum of the numbers on the vertices to equal the sum of the numbers on the edges? Prove your conclusion.
（22nd All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, 1996）

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n-m} \in \mathbf{R}_{+}, m, n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, $n-m \geqslant 3$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\prod_{i=1}^{n-m}\left(a_{i}^{n}-a_{i}^{m}+n-m\right) \\
\geqslant\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n-m}\right)^{n-m} .
\end{array}
$$

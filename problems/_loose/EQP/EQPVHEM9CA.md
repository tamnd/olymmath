In each cell of an infinite sheet of graph paper, some real number is written. Prove that in some cell, the number written does not exceed the numbers written in at least four of the eight surrounding cells.

344 *. Prove that from any sequence of real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$, one can select a part of the numbers such that the following three conditions are satisfied:

a) no three consecutive numbers are selected;

b) from any three consecutive numbers, at least one is selected;

c) the absolute value of the sum of the selected numbers is not less than

$$
\frac{\left|a_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{n}\right|}{6}
$$

Let $n{}$ be a natural number. The pairwise distinct nonzero integers $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ have the property that the number $$(k+a_1)(k+a_2)\cdots(k+a_n)$$is divisible by $a_1a_2\cdots a_n$ for any integer $k{}.$ Find the largest possible value of $a_n.$ *Proposed by F. Petrov and K. Sukhov*

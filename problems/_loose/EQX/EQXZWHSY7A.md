A line $p$ intersects the sides of triangle $ABC$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, respectively, and the cevians drawn through a fixed point $P$ intersect the corresponding sides of the triangle at points $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{C P_{2}}{P_{2} A}: \frac{C B_{1}}{B_{1} A}+\frac{C P_{1}}{P_{1} B}: \frac{C A_{1}}{A_{1} B}=\frac{C P}{P P_{3}}: \frac{C Q}{Q P_{3}}
$$

where $Q$ is the intersection point of the lines $C P_{3}$ and $p$ (segments are considered oriented).

A team has exactly $n(n \geq 3)$ people, and the team organizes a meeting every day, with at least three members attending. During each meeting, every two participants shake hands exactly once. After $n$ days, it is found that each pair of the $n$ people have shaken hands exactly once. Prove: the number of people attending the meeting each day is the same.

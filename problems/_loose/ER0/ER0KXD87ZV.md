On the sides of a quadrilateral, semicircles are constructed, located outside the quadrilateral, with the sides as their diameters. Prove that the midpoints of the arcs of these semicircles are the vertices of a quadrilateral with equal and perpendicular diagonals. Prove the validity of the theorem in the case when the semicircles are replaced by their complements to full circles.

Given a plane $S$, a circle $k$ on it, and a point $A$ outside the plane. Consider all spheres that pass through $A$ and are tangent to $S$ at some point of $k$. What is the geometric locus of the centers of these spheres? Show that these spheres pass through an additional fixed point.

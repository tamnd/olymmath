On a circular road, several identical cars are parked. It is known that if all the gasoline from the cars is drained into one of them, this car will be able to travel the entire circular road and return to its original position. Prove that at least one of these cars can travel the entire circular road in the given direction, picking up gasoline from the other cars along the way.

If the area $\triangle$ of $\triangle A B C$ is constant, and it satisfies any of the following conditions: (1) the product of the three sides is minimum, (2) the sum of the three sides is minimum, (3) the sum of the squares of the three sides is minimum, (4) the circumcircle is minimum, then the triangle is an equilateral triangle.

Correspondingly, if $\triangle A B C$ has any of the following conditions: (1) the product of the three sides is constant, (2) the sum of the three sides is constant, (3) the sum of the squares of the three sides is constant, (4) the circumcircle is constant, then when it has the maximum area $\triangle$, $\triangle A B C$ is an equilateral triangle.

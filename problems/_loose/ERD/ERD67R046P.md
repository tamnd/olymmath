Let $\varphi=\varphi(t)$ and $\psi=\psi(t)$ be the characteristic functions of some random variables $X$ and $Y$.

(a) Determine what the equality

$$
f(\varphi(t))=g(\psi(t)), t \in \mathbb{R},
$$

can mean for some functions $f=f(x)$ and $g=g(x)$ that are analytic in the disk $B_{r}=\{x:|x|<r\}$ with $r>1$, in terms of the distributions of $X$ and $Y$.
(b) Suppose that $\phi=\phi(t)$ is the characteristic function of a random variable $Z, \mathrm{P}(Z \leqslant 0)=1$. Also, let $\mathrm{P}(Y>0)=1$. Determine what the equality

$$
f(\varphi(t))=g(\psi(t))+h(\phi(t)), t \in \mathbb{R}
$$

can mean for some functions $f=f(x), g=g(x)$, and $h=h(x)$ that are analytic in the disk $B_{r}=\{x:|x|<r\}$ with $r>1$, in terms of the distributions of $X$ and $Y$.

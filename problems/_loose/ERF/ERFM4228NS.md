Find the number of pairs $(m, n)$ of positive integers, both of which are $\le 1000$ , such that $\frac{m}{n+1}< \sqrt2 < \frac{m+1}{n}$

(recalling that $ \sqrt2 = 1.414213..$ .).

(D. Fomin, Leningrad)

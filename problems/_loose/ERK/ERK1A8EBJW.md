Prove that the quantity $S_{\omega}$, introduced in problem $14.41 \mathrm{~B}$, has the following properties:

a) $S_{\mathrm{A}}=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2}, S_{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{c^{2}+a^{2}-b^{2}}{2}, S_{\mathrm{C}}=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}}{2}$.

b) $S_{\mathrm{A}}+S_{\mathrm{B}}=c^{2}, S_{\mathrm{B}}+S_{\mathrm{C}}=a^{2}, S_{\mathrm{C}}+S_{\mathrm{A}}=b^{2}$

c) $S_{\mathrm{A}}+S_{\mathrm{B}}+S_{\mathrm{C}}=S_{\varphi}$, where $\varphi-$ is the Brocard angle.

d) $S_{\mathrm{A}} S_{\mathrm{B}}+S_{\mathrm{B}} S_{\mathrm{C}}+S_{\mathrm{C}} S_{\mathrm{A}}=4 S^{2}$

e) $S_{\mathrm{A}} S_{\mathrm{B}} S_{\mathrm{C}}=4 S^{2} S_{\varphi}-(a b c)^{2}$

a) Maruška will place different numbers of candies from one to eight in the vertices of a regular octagon. Petr can then choose which three piles of candies to give Maruška, and he will keep the rest. The only condition is that these three piles must lie at the vertices of an isosceles triangle. Maruška wants to place the candies in such a way that she gets as many as possible, no matter which three vertices Petr chooses. How many candies can Maruška guarantee to get?

b) Solve the same problem for a regular nonagon, where Maruška will place 1 to 9 candies in its vertices.

(Isosceles triangles include equilateral triangles.)

In triangle $ABC$, the side $BC$ is the geometric mean between the other two sides. Let $A_{0}$ be the midpoint of side $BC$, $A_{1}$ the intersection of the internal angle bisector from $A$ with $BC$, and $A_{2}$ the reflection of $A_{1}$ over $A_{0}$. It is to be proven that the line $AA_{2}$ is perpendicular to the line connecting the centroid of the triangle to the center of the circumscribed circle.

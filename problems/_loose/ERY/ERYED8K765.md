Consider a polygon with 100 vertices, of which 10 are colored red and 10 others are colored blue. Show that there exist four vertices $A, B, C, D$ such that

- $A$ and $B$ are blue.

- $C$ and $D$ are red.

- The lengths $A B$ and $C D$ are equal.

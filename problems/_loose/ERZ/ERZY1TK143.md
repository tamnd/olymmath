$A$ and $B$ are connected by a railway line; the train arriving in $B$ immediately returns to $A$. A railway also runs alongside the track. A cyclist started from $A$ at the same time as the train. Half an hour later, the cyclist was as far from $A$ as the train was from $B$. After the cyclist had traveled an additional 2 km, the positions of the two vehicles were again symmetrical with respect to the midpoint of the $A B$ segment. (The train had meanwhile turned around in $B$). A quarter of an hour later, the two vehicles met for the first time. - What is the distance between $A$ and $B$ and the speeds of the two vehicles? (We assume that the speeds are constant.)

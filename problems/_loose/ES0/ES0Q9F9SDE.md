A convex quadrilateral has consecutive side lengths $a, b, c, d$, diagonal lengths $e$ and $f$, and one of the angles between the diagonals is $\varphi$. It is to be proven that if $a c - b d = e f \cos \varphi$, then a circle can be inscribed in the quadrilateral.

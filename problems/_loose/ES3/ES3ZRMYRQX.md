Forty-four interns have been kidnapped by the diabolical François, who, in his great kindness, gives them a chance to escape his trap. He has them enter one by one into a large room containing 44 drawers, each of these drawers containing the name of an intern (the names are assumed to be distinct). If by opening fewer than 22 drawers the intern finds his name, he moves to another room where he must wait for his friends. Otherwise, François kills everyone. Find a strategy that gives the interns at least a $30\%$ chance of survival.

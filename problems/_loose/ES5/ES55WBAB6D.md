Among the employees of a company, some pairs of individuals are in an antagonistic relationship. If a group of people has an odd number of members greater than or equal to 3, and they can be seated around a table such that every two adjacent people are in an antagonistic relationship, then this group is called "isolated." If there are at most 2015 isolated groups, prove: the company's employees can be divided into 11 departments such that no two people in the same department are in an antagonistic relationship.

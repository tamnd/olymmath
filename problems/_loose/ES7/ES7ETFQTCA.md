(2002 Bulgarian Winter Mathematical Competition) Three sticks each have a length no less than $n$, where $n$ is a positive integer. If the sum of the lengths of these three sticks is $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, prove: the three sticks can be cut into $n$ segments, whose lengths are $1,2,3, \cdots$, $n$.

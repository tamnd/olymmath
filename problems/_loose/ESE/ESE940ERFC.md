On the plane of rectangle $A B C D$, let's erect a perpendicular line at point $A$. Connect the arbitrary point $O$ on this perpendicular, different from $A$, with points $B, C, D$. Let the orthogonal projection of point $A$ on $O B$ be denoted by $P$, on $O C$ by $Q$, and on $O D$ by $R$. Prove that points $A, P, Q, R$ lie in the same plane and that $O C$ is perpendicular to this plane.

$\frac{a^{4}}{(a+b)(a+c)(a+d)}+\frac{b^{4}}{(b+c)(b+d)(b+a)}+\frac{c^{4}}{(c+d)(c+a)(c+b)}+\frac{d^{4}}{(d+a)(d+b)(d+c)}$.

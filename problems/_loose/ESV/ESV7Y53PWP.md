The Bee Swarm. Here is an example of an elegant form into which the aforementioned Bhaskara clothed a small puzzle:

"The square root of half the total number of bees in the swarm flew out to a jasmine bush; $\frac{8}{9}$ of the entire swarm remained in place; one bee is flying around its beloved, humming inside a lotus, where it flew in at night, attracted by the fragrance of this flower, which has now become its prison. Tell me the number of bees in the swarm."

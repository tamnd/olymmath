The octahedron $A B C D E F$ has a square $A B C D$ as its base, while the line $E F$ is normal to the plane determined by the square $A B C D$ and passes through its center. It is known that the sphere that touches all faces of the octahedron and the sphere that touches all lateral edges (i.e., $E A, E B, E C, E D, F A, F B, F C$ and $F D$) have the same center, and that the sphere that touches the lateral edges has a surface area that is $50\%$ larger. Find the ratio $\frac{E F}{A B}$.

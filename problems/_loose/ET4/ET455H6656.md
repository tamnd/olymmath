(2007 National High School Mathematics League Hubei Province Preliminary Test) Given the sequence $\{f(n)\}$ satisfies the recurrence relation: $f(n+1)=\frac{1}{2} f^{2}(n)-f(n)+2, n \geqslant 1, n \in \mathbf{N}$.
(1) If $f(1)=4$, prove: (1) When $n \geqslant 2$, $f(n+1) \geqslant 2 f(n)$; (2) When $n \geqslant 1$, $f(n+1) \geqslant\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n} f(n)$.
(2) If $f(1)=1$, prove: When $n \geqslant 5$, $\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{f(k)}<n-1$.

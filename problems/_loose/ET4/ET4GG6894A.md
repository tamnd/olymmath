Let $H$ be a point which altitudes of the acute-angled triangle $ABC$ intersect at. Points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ are midpoints of the sides $BC, CA, \text{and } AB$ respectively. Let $A_2$

and $C_2$ be such points for which $A_2A \perp AC$ and $A_2C_1 \perp AB$, $C_2C \perp AC$ and $C_2A_1 \perp BC$. Prove the following:

a) midpoint of the sector BH is on line $A_2C_2$;

b) let line $BB_1$ intersect a circle circumscribed about triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ at points $B_1$ and $B_3$, point $B_3$ is then on line $A_2C_2$.

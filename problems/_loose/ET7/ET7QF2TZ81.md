For example, in $\triangle A B C$, $r_{a}, r_{b}, r_{c}$ are the radii of the excircles opposite to vertices $A, B, C$ respectively. Prove:
$$
\frac{a^{2}}{r_{a}\left(r_{b}+r_{c}\right)}+\frac{b^{2}}{r_{b}\left(r_{c}+r_{a}\right)}+\frac{c^{2}}{r_{c}\left(r_{a}+r_{b}\right)}=2
$$

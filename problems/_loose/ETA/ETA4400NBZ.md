Let $f(x)=a_{n} x^{n}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0} \in \mathbf{R}[x]$, satisfying: $0 \leqslant a_{i} \leqslant a_{0}, i=1,2, \cdots, n$. Also let $b_{i}(i=0,1, \cdots, 2 n)$ satisfy $[f(x)]^{2}=b_{2 n} x^{2 n}+b_{2 n-1} x^{2 n-1}+\cdots+b_{1} x+b_{0}$, prove that: $b_{n+1} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}[f(1)]^{2}$.

Given a triangle $\triangle A B C$, let $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ be points on the sides $A B$ and $A C$ such that $B B^{\prime}=$ $C C^{\prime}$. Let $O$ and $O^{\prime}$ be the circumcentres (i.e., the centre of the circumscribed circle) of $\triangle A B C$ and $\triangle A B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, respectively. Suppose $O O^{\prime}$ intersect lines $A B^{\prime}$ and $A C^{\prime}$ at $B^{\prime \prime}$ and $C^{\prime \prime}$, respectively. If $A B=\frac{1}{2} A C$, then
(A) $A B^{\prime \prime}A C^{\prime \prime}$.

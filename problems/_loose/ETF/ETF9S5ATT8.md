In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, $P$ is a moving point not on the $x$-axis, satisfying the condition: Through $P$, two tangent lines can be drawn to the parabola $y^{2}=4 x$, and the line $l_{P}$ connecting the two points of tangency is perpendicular to $P O$. Let the intersection points of the line $l_{P}$ with $P O$ and the $x$-axis be $Q$ and $R$, respectively.
(1) Prove: $R$ is a fixed point;
(2) Find the minimum value of $\frac{|P Q|}{|Q R|}$.
(2019, National High School Mathematics Joint Competition (A Volume))

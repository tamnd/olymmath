Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle with $A B<A C$. Points $D, E$ on the rays $A B, A C$, respectively, satisfy $A D=A C$ and $A E=A B$. Let $F$ be the point of intersection of the line passing through $D$ perpendicular to $A D$ and the line passing through $E$ perpendicular to $A E$. Show that $A F \perp B C$.

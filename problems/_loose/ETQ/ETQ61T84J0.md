Given non-negative real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2022}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{2022}, c_{1}, c_{2}, \cdots, c_{2022}$ satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{2022} a_{i}=1$, $\sum_{i=1}^{2022} b_{i}^{2}=2, \sum_{i=1}^{2022} c_{i}^{3}=3$. Find the maximum value of $\sum_{i=1}^{2022} a_{i} b_{i+1} c_{i+2}+\sum_{i=3}^{2022} a_{i} b_{i-1} c_{i-2}$.

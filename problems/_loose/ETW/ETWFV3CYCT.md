Let $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ satisfy $f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)<\frac{f(x)+f(y)}{2}, x, y \in(a, b), x \neq y$, prove: $f\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}}{n}\right)<\frac{f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)+\cdots+f\left(x_{n}\right)}{n}$.

where $x_{i}$ belongs to $(a, b)$, and there is at least one pair $(i, j), x_{1} \neq x_{1}$.

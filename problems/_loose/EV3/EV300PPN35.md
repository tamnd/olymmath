Let $A B C \triangle$ be given. From the variable point $P$, we draw perpendiculars to the altitudes of $A B C \triangle$; the feet of these perpendiculars are $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Prove that if $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} \triangle \stackrel{\infty}{=} A B C \triangle$, then the geometric locus of point $P$ is a circle whose center is the orthocenter of $A B C \triangle$, and whose radius is equal to the diameter of the circumcircle of $A B C \triangle$.

Given $x, y \in N$, find the largest $y$ value such that there exists a unique $x$ value satisfying the following inequality:
$$
\frac{9}{17}<\frac{x}{x+y}<\frac{8}{15} \text {. }
$$
(Wuhan, Hubei Province, China Mathematical Summer Camp, 1987)

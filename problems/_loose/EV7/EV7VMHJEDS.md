(2005 National High School Mathematics League Sichuan Preliminary Competition Question) A class has 20 students, each taking one Chinese and one Math exam. The exam results are: (1) No one scored 0; (2) No two students have the same Chinese and Math scores.
We say "Student A's performance is better than Student B's" if "Student A's Chinese and Math scores are both no less than B's".
Prove: There exist three students $A$, $B$, and $C$, such that Student A's performance is better than Student B's, and Student B's performance is better than Student C's.

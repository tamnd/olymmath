Given real numbers $x_{k}>1, k=1,2, \ldots, 2 n$. Prove that the interval [0,2] contains at most $\binom{2 n}{n}$ sums of the form $\sum_{k=1}^{2 n} a_{k} x_{k}$, where $a_{k} \in\{-1,1\}$, for every $k=1,2, \ldots, 2 n$.

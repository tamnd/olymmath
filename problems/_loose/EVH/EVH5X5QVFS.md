If any positive number is rounded to two, three, and four decimal places respectively, the resulting numbers are $a, b, c$, then
(A) $a \geqslant b \geqslant c$.
(B) $a \leqslant b \leqslant c$.
(C) $a \geqslant b \geqslant c$ or $a \leqslant b \leqslant c$.
(D) None of the above conclusions are correct.
(3rd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

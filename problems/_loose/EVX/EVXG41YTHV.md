Inside a regular $2n$-gon, an arbitrary point is chosen and connected to every vertex of the $2n$-gon. The triangles obtained are colored alternately black and white so that triangles with a common side are of different color. Prove that the sum of the areas of all white triangles equals the sum of the areas of all black triangles.

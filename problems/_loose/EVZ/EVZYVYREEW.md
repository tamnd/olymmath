In a futsal tournament, bets are accepted on four teams. Bets on the first team are accepted at a ratio of $1: 5$ (if the first team wins, the player receives the amount they bet on this team plus five times the amount, i.e., they receive six times the amount of money they bet, and if they lose, the money is not returned). Bets on the second team are accepted at a ratio of $1: 1$, on the third team $-1: 8$, on the fourth team $-1: 7$. Is it possible to place bets in such a way as to win regardless of the tournament outcome?

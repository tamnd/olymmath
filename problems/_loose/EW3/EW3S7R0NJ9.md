Let $C[0,1]$ be the space of continuous functions

$$
x=\{x(t)\}_{0 \leqslant t \leqslant 1} \text { with the metric } \rho(x, y)=\sup _{0 \leqslant t \leqslant 1}|x(t)-y(t)| \text {. }
$$

Prove that the space $C[0,1]$ is Polish, i.e., a complete separable metric space.

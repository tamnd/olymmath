The area of the equilateral triangle $A B C$ is 7 . Points $M$ and $N$ are chosen respectively on $A B$ and $A C$ so that $A N=B M$. Denote by $O$ the intersecting point of the straight lines $B N$ and $C M$. The area of $B O C$ is 2 .

(a) Prove that $M B: A B=1: 3$ or $M B: A B=2: 3$.

(b) Find $\angle A O B$.

Let $ABC$ be an arbitrary triangle and $M$ be an arbitrary point from its interior. The lengths of the normals from point $M$ to the sides $BC, CA, AB$ are denoted by $n_{a}, n_{b}, n_{c}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{n_{a}}{h_{a}}+\frac{n_{b}}{h_{b}}+\frac{n_{c}}{h_{c}}=1
$$

where $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$ are the corresponding altitudes of the triangle.

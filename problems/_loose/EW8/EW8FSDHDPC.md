Three boys agree to distribute a bag of stones as follows: the first gets half the total plus one, the second gets a third of what remains, and the third finds that what is left for him is exactly twice that of the second. Then the total number of stones is
(A) 8 or 38.
(B) Undetermined from the given data.
(C) 20 or 26.
(D) 14 or 32.
(E) None of the above.
(6th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1955)

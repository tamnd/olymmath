$$
A=\left\{x \in N^{*} \mid x=k^{2}+k, k<8\right\}, B=\left\{y \in N \mid(y+3) \cdot 2016^{2} \leq 12^{6} \cdot 7^{2}\right\} \text {. }
$$

a) Determine the elements of the sets $A$ and $B$.

b) Determine all subsets of two elements of the set $A$, which are also subsets of the set $B$.

Let $f(x)$ be a quadratic polynomial such that $f(f(1)) = f(-f(-1)) = 0$ and $f(1) \neq -f(-1)$. Suppose furthermore that the quadratic $2f(x)$ has coefficients that are nonzero integers. Find $f(0)$.

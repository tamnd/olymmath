Four, it is known that the obtuse triangle $\triangle A B C$ satisfies the following conditions:
(1) The lengths of $A B, B C, C A$ are all positive integers;
(2) The lengths of $A B, B C, C A$ are all no more than 50;
(3) The lengths of $A B, B C, C A$ form an arithmetic sequence with a positive common difference.

Find the number of obtuse triangles that satisfy conditions (1), (2), and (3), and determine the side lengths of the triangle with the largest perimeter among them.

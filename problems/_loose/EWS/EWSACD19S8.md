An ordered list of four numbers is called a quadruple.

A quadruple $(p, q, r, s)$ of integers with $p, q, r, s \geq 0$ is chosen at random such that

$$
2 p+q+r+s=4
$$

What is the probability that $p+q+r+s=3$ ?
(A) $\frac{3}{22}$
(B) $\frac{3}{11}$
(C) $\frac{3}{19}$
(D) $\frac{6}{19}$
(E) $\frac{2}{7}$

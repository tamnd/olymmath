For any permutation $ T = \left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2000}\right) $ of $ 1, 2, \cdots, 2000 $, construct the sum
$$
S_{T} = \frac{a_{1}}{1} + \frac{a_{2}}{2} + \cdots + \frac{a_{2000}}{2000}.
$$

Prove: When $ T $ runs through all such permutations, $ \left\{S_{T}\right\} $ contains at least 300 consecutive natural numbers.

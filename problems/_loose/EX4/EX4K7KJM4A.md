At a large round table, 100 people are sitting: knights, who always tell the truth, and liars, who always lie, and it is known that among those present, there is at least one knight and at least one liar. Each person can see only 10 nearest neighbors to the right and 10 nearest neighbors to the left of themselves. Each person was asked: Do you see more knights than liars? Prove that someone will answer: No!

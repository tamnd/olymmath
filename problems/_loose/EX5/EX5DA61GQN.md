Let $K$ and $K^{\prime}$ be two squares in the same plane with equal side lengths. Can $K$ be divided into a finite number of non-overlapping triangles $\triangle_{1}, \triangle_{2}, \cdots, \triangle_{n}$, and can $n$ translations $t_{1}, t_{2}, \cdots, t_{n}$ be found, such that $K^{\prime}=U_{i=1}^{U} t_{i}\left(\triangle_{i}\right)$?

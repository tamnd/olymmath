If $x=\frac{-1+\sqrt{3} i}{2}, y=\frac{-1-\sqrt{3} i}{2}$. Where $i^{2}=-1$. Then the incorrect expression among the following is
(A) $x^{5}+y^{5}=-1$.
(B) $x^{7}+y^{7}=-1$.
(C) $x^{9}+y^{9}=-1$.
(D) $x^{11}+y^{11}=-1$.
(E) $x^{13}+y^{13}=-1$.
(36th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1985)

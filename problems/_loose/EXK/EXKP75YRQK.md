Given the parabola $C: y=\frac{1}{2} x^{2}$ and the line $L: y=k x-1$ have no common points, let $P$ be a moving point on the line $L$, and let the two tangent lines from $P$ to the parabola $C$ touch the parabola at points $A$ and $B$.
(1) Prove: the moving line $A B$ always passes through a fixed point $Q$;
(2) Let the line connecting point $P$ and the fixed point $Q$ from (1) intersect the parabola $C$ at points $M$ and $N$, prove: $\frac{P M}{P N}=\frac{Q M}{Q N}$.

Points $A$ and $A^{\prime}$ are symmetric with respect to the point of tangency $E$ on a tangent of the circle. The reflection of a point $B$ on the segment $AE$ with respect to $E$, denoted as $B^{\prime}$, was constructed using a single straightedge as follows. From $B$, we drew an arbitrary secant to the circle, intersecting it at points $P$ and $Q$ (with $P$ being closer to $B$). The secant $AP$ intersects the circle again at point $R$. The second intersection point of the line through $Q$ and $A^{\prime}$ with the circle, different from $R$, is $S$. Finally, the line $RS$ intersects the tangent at point $B^{\prime}$. Prove the correctness of this construction.

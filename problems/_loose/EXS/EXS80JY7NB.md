The lateral surface of a rectangular parallelepiped with a base $a \times b$ and height $c$ ($a, b$, and $c$ are natural numbers) is covered by cells without overlaps or gaps using rectangles with sides parallel to the edges of the parallelepiped, each consisting of an even number of unit squares. It is allowed to bend the rectangles over the lateral edges of the parallelepiped. Prove that if $c$ is odd, then the number of ways to cover the surface is even.

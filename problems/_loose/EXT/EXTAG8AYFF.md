Write 2013 different real numbers on 2013 cards, with the side containing the numbers facing down on the table. Two players, A and B, play the following game: in each round, A can arbitrarily select ten cards, and B will tell A one of the ten numbers written on these cards (B does not tell A which card this number is written on). Find the maximum positive integer $t$, such that A can definitely determine the numbers on $t$ cards after a finite number of rounds. ${ }^{[1]}$
(39th Russian Mathematical Olympiad (11th Grade))

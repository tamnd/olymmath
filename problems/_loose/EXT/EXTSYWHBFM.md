Let $p$ and $q$ be any natural numbers, prove: there exists an integer-coefficient polynomial $p(x)$, such that for every point $x$ in some interval of length $\frac{1}{q}$ on the $x$-axis, we have $\left|p(x)-\frac{p}{q}\right|<\frac{1}{q^{2}}$.

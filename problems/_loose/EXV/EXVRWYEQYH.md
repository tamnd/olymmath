Consider the regular quadrilateral pyramid $V A B C D$, with $A B=12$. Let $A C \cap B D=\{O\}$, $M$ be the midpoint of segment $[B C]$, and $P$ be the midpoint of segment $[A B]$. If the cosine of the dihedral angle formed by the planes $(V B C)$ and $(A B C)$ is equal to $\frac{3}{4}$, determine:

a) the distance from point $P$ to the plane $(V B C)$;

b) the distance from point $O$ to the plane (VPM);

c) the tangent of the angle formed by the planes $(V A C)$ and $(V B C)$.

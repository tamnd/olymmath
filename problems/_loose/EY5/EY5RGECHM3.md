(2005 China National Team Training Problem) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}$ be $k$ distinct positive integers, none of which have square factors (where 1 is allowed), then for any non-zero rational numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{k}$, we have $\sum_{i=1}^{k} b_{i} \sqrt{a_{i}} \neq 0$. Note: If there does not exist a positive integer $d>1$ such that $d^{2} \mid n$, then the positive integer $n$ is said to have no square factors.

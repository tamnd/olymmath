Let $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $z \in \mathbb{C}$, such that $z^{2}-z+5=0$. Show that

$$
(z-1)^{a}-(z+4)^{b}=0
$$

if and only if there exists a number $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, such that $a=4 n$ and $b=2 n$.

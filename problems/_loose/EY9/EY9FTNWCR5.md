In the country of Oz, there are several castles, from each of which three roads lead. A wandering knight set out from his ancestral castle and embarked on a journey through the country. The knight loves variety; therefore, upon reaching each subsequent castle, he turns left if he turned right the previous time, and turns right if he previously turned left. (Passing the first castle on his
journey, the knight can turn in any direction.) Prove that the knight will eventually return to his castle.

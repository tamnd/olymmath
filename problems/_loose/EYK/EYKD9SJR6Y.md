Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be such that for some $a>0$, for all real $x$,

$$
f(x+a)=\frac{1}{2}+\sqrt{f(x)-f^{2}(x)}
$$

Show that $f$ is periodic.

(IMO 1968)

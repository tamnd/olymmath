Prove the validity of the inequality

$$
\frac{\sum a_{i}^{\sum a_{i}+1}}{\left(\sum a_{i}+1\right)^{\sum a_{i}}}>\sqrt[n]{\prod a_{i}}
$$

for $n$ positive real numbers $a_{i}$, where $n>2$.

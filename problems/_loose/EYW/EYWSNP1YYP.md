Given two progressions with positive terms: a geometric and an arithmetic one:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \because a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n} \\
& \div b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{n}
\end{aligned}
$$

(the difference of the second is positive). Prove that there always exists a system of logarithms for which:

$$
\log _{k} a_{n}-b_{n}=\log _{k} a_{1}-b_{1}
$$

9 I. V. Baranova and S. E. Lyapin

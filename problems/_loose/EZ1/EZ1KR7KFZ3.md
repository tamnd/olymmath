Consider three circles, each passing through one vertex of a triangle and the bases of the two angle bisectors - internal and external, emanating from this vertex (these circles are known as Apollonian circles). Prove that: a) these three circles intersect at two points ($M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$); b) the line $M_{1} M_{2}$ passes through the center of the circle circumscribed around the given triangle; c) the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from points $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$ to the sides of the triangle serve as the vertices of two equilateral triangles.

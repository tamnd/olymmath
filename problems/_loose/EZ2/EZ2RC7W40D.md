For a positive integer $n \geqslant 2$, color any positive integer not exceeding $\frac{n\left(n^{2}-2 n+3\right)}{2}$ in red or blue.

Prove: There must exist $n$ same-colored positive integers $a_{1}<a_{2}$ $<\cdots<a_{n}$, satisfying $a_{2}-a_{1} \leqslant a_{3}-a_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant a_{n}$ $a_{n-1}$.

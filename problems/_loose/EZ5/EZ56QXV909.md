A commercial bank offers the following interest accrual scheme (simple interest) for a term deposit with a duration of one year:

- for the first 3 months, interest is accrued at a rate of $12 \%$ per annum;
- for the next 3 months, interest is accrued at a rate of $8 \%$ per annum;
- for all the remaining months, interest is accrued at a rate of $6 \%$ per annum.

Upon closing the deposit, a one-time commission "for document processing" of $1 \%$ of the deposit amount is deducted from the depositor.

Calculate the actual annual yield that the depositor will receive on this deposit over the year.

(GDR, 67; England, 76). Prove that for any positive numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}(n \geqslant 2)$ the inequality

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}}{s-a_{i}} \geqslant \frac{n}{n-1}, \text { where } s=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} \text { is denoted. }
$$

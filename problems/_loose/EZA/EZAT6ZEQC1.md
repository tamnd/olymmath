Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. A common tangent is drawn to them, touching the first circle at point $C$ and the second at point $D$. Let $B$ be the point of intersection of the circles closest to the line $C D$. The line $C B$ intersects the second circle again at point $E$. Prove that $A D$ is the bisector of angle $C A E$.

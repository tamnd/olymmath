Let $p$ be the product of $n$ real numbers $x_1$ , $x_2$ , $...$ , $x_n$ . Prove that if $p - x_k$ is an odd integer for $k = 1, 2,..., n$ , then each of the numbers $x_1$ , $x_2$ , $...$ , $x_n$ is irrational.

(G Galperin)

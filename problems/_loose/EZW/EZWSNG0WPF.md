Let $f(x) = 4x - x^{2}$ . Give $x_{0}$ , consider the sequence defined by $x_{n} = f(x_{n-1})$ for all $n \ge 1$ . For how many real numbers $x_{0}$ will the sequence $x_{0}, x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$ take on only a finite number of different values? $ \textbf{(A)}\ \text{0} \textbf{(B)}\ \text{1 or 2} \textbf{(C)}\ \text{3, 4, 5 or 6} \textbf{(D)}\ \text{more than 6 but finitely many} \textbf{(E)}\ \text{infinitely many} $

Given $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}_{+}, 0 \leqslant k < 2$. Prove:
$$
\sum \frac{a^{2}-b c}{b^{2}+c^{2}+k a^{2}} \geqslant 0,
$$

where, $\sum$ denotes the cyclic sum. ${ }^{[1]}$

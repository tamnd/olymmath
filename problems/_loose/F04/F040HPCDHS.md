Real numbers $p, q, r, s$ satisfy the equations

$$
p^{2}+q^{2}+r^{2}+s^{2}=4 \text { and } p q+r s=1 .
$$

Prove that some two of these four numbers differ by at most 1 and some two differ by at least 1.

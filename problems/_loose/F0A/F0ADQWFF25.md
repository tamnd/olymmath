Let $A B C D E$ be a convex pentagon and $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ the centroids of triangles $A B C$ and $B C D$, respectively.

a) Show that if $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of segments $[A E]$ and $[D E]$, respectively, then segments $\left[G_{1} N\right]$ and $\left[G_{2} M\right]$ have a common point $G$ such that $\frac{G_{1} G}{G N}=\frac{G_{2} G}{G M}$;

b) Find the position vector of the point $G$ determined above, in terms of the position vectors of points $A, B, C, D$ and $E$.

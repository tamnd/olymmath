At the beginning of 2020, Alexander, due to fear of uncertainty, bought several kilograms of buckwheat at a price of 70 rub/kg. By the beginning of 2022, Alexander had 1 kg of buckwheat left, and its price had risen to 100 rub/kg. It is known that at the beginning of 2020, Alexander could open annual deposits at an interest rate of 10% per year, two-year deposits at an interest rate of 8% per year, and at the beginning of 2021, annual deposits at an interest rate of 5% per year. Interest on all deposits is paid at the end of the year.

1) Can we conclude that Alexander had the opportunity to allocate his funds more profitably at the beginning of 2020?
2) What non-monetary factors should also be considered to evaluate the optimality of Alexander's decision?
3) If the parameters of the problem were such that in the conclusion of part (a) Alexander could have allocated his funds more profitably at the beginning of 2020, nevertheless, we could not claim that his actions were not optimal. Why?

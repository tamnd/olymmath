Trees in a forest stand on the points of a regular triangular grid with side length $d$ (that is, if the trees $A, B, C$ form a triangle with no other trees inside, then $A B=B C=C A=d$). They want to demarcate a rectangular part of the forest such that the corners of the rectangle are trees, the distance between the trees on two opposite sides is $d$, and there are as many trees inside the rectangle as on its perimeter. Is this possible?

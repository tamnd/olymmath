G57 (30-2, Australia) Given that $\triangle ABC$ is an acute triangle, the angle bisectors of $\angle A, \angle B$, and $\angle C$ are extended to intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, respectively. The line $AA_{1}$ intersects the external angle bisectors of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ at $A_{0}$, and similarly, we obtain points $B_{0}$ and $C_{0}$. Prove:
(1) The area of $\triangle A_{0} B_{0} C_{0}$ is twice the area of the hexagon $A C_{1} B A_{1} C B_{1}$;
(2) The area of $\triangle A_{0} B_{0} C_{0}$ is at least four times the area of $\triangle ABC$.

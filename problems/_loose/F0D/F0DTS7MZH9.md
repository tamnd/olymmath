a) Give an example of two matrices $A, B \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R}), A \neq I_{2}, B \neq I_{2}$ such that $A B=I_{2}$

b) Give an example of a matrix $B \in \mathcal{M}_{3}(\mathbb{R})$ such that $B^{2}=A$, where $A=\left(\begin{array}{lll}5 & 5 & 5 \\ 5 & 5 & 5 \\ 5 & 5 & 5\end{array}\right)$

c) Let $A, B \in M_{2}(\mathbb{C})$ that satisfy $A^{2}=B^{2}=I_{2}$ and det $A=\operatorname{det} B=-1$.

Prove that there exists $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ such that $A B+B A=\lambda I_{2}$.

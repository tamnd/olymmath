Take any $n(n>2)$ points on a graph, and connect each pair of points with a line segment. Can all these line segments be drawn in one stroke, such that the endpoint of the 1st line segment is the starting point of the 2nd line segment, the endpoint of the 2nd line segment is the starting point of the 3rd line segment, and so on, until the endpoint of the last line segment coincides with the starting point of the 1st line segment?

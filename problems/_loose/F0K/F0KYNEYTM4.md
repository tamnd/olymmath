Let $\mathcal P$ be a simple polygon and let $j$ and $k$ be positive integers with $j > k$ . Suppose that it is possible to partition $\mathcal P$ into $j$ rectangles and shade $k$ of them gray such that no two shaded rectangles share a positive amount of perimeter. (It is permissible for any two of them to share a vertex.) In terms of $j$ and $k$ , what is the fewest number of sides that $\mathcal P$ could have?

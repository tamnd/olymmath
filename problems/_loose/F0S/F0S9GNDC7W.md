Call an integer $k$ *debatable* if the number of odd factors of $k$ is a power of two. What is the largest positive integer $n$ such that there exists $n$ consecutive debatable numbers? (Here, a power of two is defined to be any number of the form $2^m$ , where $m$ is a nonnegative integer.)

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ and $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be real numbers, and $a_{1}^{2}-a_{2}^{2}-\cdots-a_{n}^{2}>0$, then
$$
\left(a_{1}^{2}-a_{2}^{2}-\cdots-a_{n}^{2}\right)\left(b_{1}^{2}-b_{2}^{2}-\cdots-b_{n}^{2}\right) \leqslant\left(a_{1} b_{1}-a_{2} b_{2}-\cdots-a_{n} b_{n}\right)^{2}
$$

Equality holds if and only if $\frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}=\frac{a_{2}}{b_{2}}=\cdots=\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}$ (where if some $b_{i}=0$, it is understood that the corresponding $a_{i}=0$)

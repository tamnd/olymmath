(1991 Asia-Pacific Mathematical Olympiad) During the break, $n(n \geqslant 2)$ students sit in a circle around the teacher to play a game. The teacher distributes candies to the students in a counterclockwise direction according to the following rules: first, the teacher selects a student and gives them a candy, then skips one student to give the next student a candy, then skips 2 students to give the next student a candy, then skips 3 students to give the next student a candy, and so on. Determine the value of $n$ such that, after many rounds (perhaps many circles), each student has at least one candy.

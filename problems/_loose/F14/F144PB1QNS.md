Clock face (a circular disk with numbers $1,2, \cdots, 11,12$), with its center as the axis, is fixed on the blackboard in the classroom. The clock face can rotate by any integer multiple of $30^{\circ}$. Initially, near the numbers on the clock face, "0" is written on the blackboard. Then, the clock face is rotated several times, and each time it stops, the number on the clock face is added to the number written on the blackboard near it. Can the following be achieved:
(1) All the numbers written on the blackboard are 1984?
(2) All the numbers written on the blackboard, except one, are 1984?
(3) All the numbers written on the blackboard, except two, are 1984?
(10th All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, 1984)

On the swamp, there are 64 bumps, they are arranged in an $8 \times 8$ square. On each bump sits a frog or a toad. Frogs never lie, while toads only tell lies. Each of them, both frogs and toads, croaked: "At least one of the neighboring bumps has a toad." What is the maximum number of toads that could sit on these bumps?

Neighboring bumps are those located horizontally and vertically in the rows of the square, diagonal bumps are not considered neighbors.

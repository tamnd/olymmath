A polygon is cut into several polygons, and neither on the sides of the original polygon nor on the sides of the resulting polygons do the vertices of the resulting polygons lie. Let $p$ be the number of resulting polygons, $q$ be the number of segments that are their sides, and $r$ be the number of points that are their vertices. Prove that $p-q+r=1$ (Euler's formula).

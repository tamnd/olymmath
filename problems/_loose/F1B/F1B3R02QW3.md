If $n, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}$ are integers, $n \geqslant a_{1}>\cdots>a_{k}>0$, and for all $i$ and $j$, the least common multiple of $a_{i}$ and $a_{j}$ does not exceed $n$, prove that for $1 \leqslant i \leqslant k, i a_{i} \leqslant n$.

Let $O$ be the circumcenter of triangle $A B C$. A tangent $t$ to the circumcircle of triangle $B O C$ meets the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $D$ and $E$, respectively $(D, E \not \equiv A)$. Point $A^{\prime}$ is the reflection of $A$ in line $t$. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A^{\prime} D E$ and $A B C$ are tangent to each other.

(Dušan Djukić)

for high school students

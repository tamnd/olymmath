On the sides $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{B C}$ of the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, there are points $E$ and $F$ respectively such that the lines $D E$ and $D F$ divide the diagonal $\overline{A C}$ into three congruent parts. If

$$
P(A D E)=P(C D F)=\frac{1}{4} P(A B C D)
$$

prove that $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

Note. $P(. ..)$ denotes the area of the figure.

We are to investigate whether from 1982 numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{1982}$, which satisfy the condition $\left|a_{k}\right|=1 (k=$ $1,2, \ldots, 1982$ ), but are otherwise arbitrarily given, it is always possible to select numbers such that the following conditions are met:

(1) At least one of the numbers $a_{k}$ is selected.

(2) At least one of the numbers $a_{k}$ is not selected.

(3) The sum of all selected numbers is equal to the sum of all unselected numbers.

Many years ago, a traveler was on the way from Altdorf to Neudorf. When he asked for directions along the way, a local explained to him:

"You are on the right path and will soon come to a fork in the road with a signpost showing three direction signs. These point to the paths leading to Altdorf, Neudorf, and Mittendorf. However, I must inform you that exactly two of these direction signs have been incorrectly labeled."

The traveler thanked him, reached the signpost, and read it.

Determine whether the traveler could definitively determine the correct path to Neudorf with the information provided!

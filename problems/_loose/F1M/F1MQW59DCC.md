Prove the inequality

$$
h_{1}^{\alpha}+h_{2}^{\alpha}+h_{3}^{\alpha} \geqslant 3(3 r)^{\alpha}
$$

where $\alpha \geqslant 1, \alpha \in \mathbf{R}, h_{1}, h_{2}, h_{3}$ are the lengths of the altitudes of the triangle, $r-$ is the length of the radius of the inscribed circle.

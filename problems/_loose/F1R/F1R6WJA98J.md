Given a line $l$, a circle, and points $M, N$ lying on the circle and not on the line $l$. Consider the mapping $P$ of the line $l$ onto itself, which is the composition of the projection of the line $l$ onto the given circle from the point $M$ and the projection of the circle onto the line $l$ from the point $N$. (If a point $X$ lies on the line $l$, then $P(X)$ is the intersection of the line $N Y$ with the line $l$, where $Y$ is the point of intersection of the line $M X$ with the given circle, distinct from $M$.) Prove that the transformation $P$ is projective.

Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ where $AB \parallel CD$, and $AB + BC = CD + AD$. Then
(A) $AD > BC$.
(B) $AD < BC$.
(C) $AD = BC$.
(D) The size relationship between $AD$ and $BC$ cannot be determined.
(Chinese Jiangsu Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1987)

Three spheres touch the plane of a given triangle at its vertices and touch each other. Prove that, if the triangle is scalene, there exist two spheres that touch the three given spheres and the plane of the triangle, and if $r$ and $\rho(\rho>r)$ are the radii of these spheres, $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle of the triangle, then $\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\rho}=\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{R}$.

The natural numbers are written in a sequence (without spaces between them): 1234567891011... Starting from the left, this sequence is divided into parts of seven digits each. Prove that for any seven-digit natural number

a) there exists at least one part in which it is written;

b) there exist infinitely many parts in which it is written.

Let $\xi, \xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots$ be a sequence of random variables defined on a complete probability space such that $\xi_{n} \xrightarrow{p} \xi$. Show that

$$
\sigma\left(\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots\right)=\sigma\left(\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \ldots, \xi\right)
$$

where $\sigma(X, Y, \ldots)$ is the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the random elements $X, Y, \ldots$ and containing all events of zero probability.

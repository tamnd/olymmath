A circle touches the side $B C$ of triangle $A B C$ at point $M$, and the extensions of sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. The inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$ touches side $B C$ at point $K$, and side $A B$ at point $L$. Prove that:

a) the segment $A P$ is equal to the semiperimeter $p$ of triangle $A B C$;

b) $B M = C K$;

c) $B C = P L$.

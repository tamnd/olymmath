Triangles. In this problem, $n$ denotes a natural number. On the plane, there are $3 n$ points, no three of which lie on the same line. Is it possible to form $n$ triangles from these points (taking them as vertices) that do not intersect and do not contain each other?

Similar problems can be posed for quadrilaterals with $4 n$ points, for pentagons with $5 n$ points, and so on. Are all such problems solvable positively?

For the two functions $f_{1}(x)=$ $x^{2}-2 x+5, f_{2}(x)=\sqrt{|x|}$, the following relationship that holds is
A. $f_{1} \in M, f_{2} \in M$
B. $f_{1} \notin M, f_{2} \notin M$
C. $f_{1} \notin M, f_{2} \in M$
D. $f_{1} \in M, f_{2} \notin M$

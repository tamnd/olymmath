Let $D:=\{(x, y)\mid x,y\in\mathbb R_+,x \ne y,x^y=y^x\}$. (Obvious that $x\ne1$ and $y\ne1$). And let $\alpha\le\beta$ be positive real numbers. Find
$$\inf_{(x,y)\in D}x^\alpha y^\beta.$$

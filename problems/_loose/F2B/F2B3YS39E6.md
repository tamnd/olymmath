Given that $n$ is a positive integer. Find the integer solution $q>1$ for the following equation:
$$
q^{n}-1=q-1+\sum_{\substack{n(a) \backslash n \\ n(a) \neq n}} \frac{q^{n}-1}{q^{n(a)}-1} .
$$

Given that the area of $\triangle A B C$ is 4, the side lengths are $B C=a, C A=b, A B=c(c>b)$, and $A D$ is the angle bisector of $\angle A$. $C^{\prime}$ is the symmetric point of $C$ with respect to the line $A D$. If $\triangle C^{\prime} B D$ is similar to $\triangle A B C$, find the minimum perimeter of $\triangle A B C$.
$(2010$, New Knowledge Cup Shanghai Junior High School Mathematics Competition)

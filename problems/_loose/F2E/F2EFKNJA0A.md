In each cell of an $8 \times 8$ table, a non-negative integer is written such that any two numbers in cells symmetrically paired along either diagonal are the same. The sum of all 64 numbers is 1000, and the sum of the 16 numbers on the diagonals is 200. Show that the sum of the numbers in each row and each column of the table is at most 300. Does the same conclusion hold for the number 299?

Birgit and Frank receive the following information about the students in a class:

The total number of students in this class is less than 40.

Exactly $60\%$ of these students participate in the "Visual Arts" club, exactly $66\%$ of all students in the class go swimming regularly, and exactly $50\%$ of all students in the class are readers of the children's library.

Birgit names a natural number $x$ and says:

From the information, it follows that at least $x$ students in this class participate in the "Visual Arts" club and go swimming regularly; however, it does not follow that more than $x$ students in the class engage in these two leisure activities.

Frank names a natural number $y$ and says:

From the information, it follows that at least $y$ students in this class participate in all three forms of leisure activities (the "Visual Arts" club, swimming, and the children's library).

a) Show that the number of students in the class can be uniquely determined from the given information, and state this number!

b) Determine a natural number $x$ such that Birgit's statements are true!

c) Prove that Frank's statements are false for any natural number $y>0$!

Point $M$ lies inside a triangle, $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}$ are the distances from $M$ to the sides of the triangle, $h_{1}, h_{2}, h_{3}$ are the corresponding heights. Find the minimum value of the expression

$$
\left(\frac{k_{1}}{h_{1}}\right)^{\alpha}+\left(\frac{k_{2}}{h_{2}}\right)^{\alpha}+\left(\frac{k_{3}}{h_{3}}\right)^{\alpha} \text { for } \alpha \geqslant 1
$$

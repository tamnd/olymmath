For each natural number $n$ we define the sets:

$$
A_{n}=\left\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid x^{2}+[x] \leq n\right\}, B_{n}=\left\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid\left[x^{2}\right]+x \leq n\right\}
$$

Prove that $A_{n} \subset B_{n+1}$ and $B_{n} \subset A_{n+1}$ for any $n$.

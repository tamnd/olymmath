Given a sphere of radius 1 with center at point $O$. From a point $A$ outside the sphere, four rays are drawn. The first ray intersects the sphere's surface sequentially at points $B 1$ and $C 1$, the second at points $B 2$ and $C 2$, the third at points $B 3$ and $C 3$, and the fourth at points $B 4$ and $C 4$. The lines $B 1 B 2$ and $C 1 C 2$ intersect at point $E$, and the lines $B 3 B 4$ and $C 3 C 4$ intersect at point $F$. Find the volume of the pyramid $O A E F$, if $A O=2$, $E O=F O=3$, and the angle between the faces $A O E$ and $A O F$ is $30^{\circ}$.

Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, in which $A B=B C=C D=4$. Points $K$ and $L$ are chosen on sides $A B$ and $C D$ respectively such that $A K=D L=1$. On side $A D$, an external triangle $A M D$ is constructed, in which $A M=M D=2$. It turns out that $K L=2$. Prove that $B M=C M$. (C. Frantsuzov)

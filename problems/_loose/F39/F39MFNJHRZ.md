There are many two kinds of black and white cards. When you have $ k$ cards in hand, consider the following procedure $ (\bf{A})$.

$ (\bf{A})$ You choose one card from $ k$ cards in hand with equal probability $ \frac {1}{k}$ and replace the card with different color one.

Answer the following questions.

(1) When you have 4 cards with 2 white and 2 black, first, find the probabilty such that you will first have 4 cards in same color after repeating the procedure $ \bf{(A)}$
$ n$ times.

(2) When you have 6 cards with 3 white and 3 black, first, find the probabilty such that you will first have 6 cards in same color after repeating the procedure $ \bf{(A)}$
$ n$ times.

We call a number lucky if it can be expressed as the sum of positive integers whose reciprocals also sum to 1. For example, 11 = $2+3+6$ and $4=2+2$ are lucky numbers, since

$$
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}=1
$$

Is 2005 a lucky number?

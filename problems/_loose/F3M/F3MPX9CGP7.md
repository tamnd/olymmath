If $p$ is an odd prime number and $a$ is an integer not divisible by $p$, then one and only one of the numbers

$$
A=a^{1+2+\ldots+(p-1)}+1, B=a^{1+2+\ldots+(p-1)}-1
$$

is divisible by $p$. Prove it!

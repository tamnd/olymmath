An item is worth $C$ dollars, and it is sold for 100 dollars at a loss of $x \%$ of the selling price, then it is sold again at a profit of $x \%$ of the new selling price $S$. If the difference between $S$ and $C$ is $1 \frac{1}{9}$ dollars, then $x$ is
(A) undetermined.
(B) $\frac{80}{9}$.
(C) 10.
(D) $\frac{95}{9}$.
(E) $\frac{100}{9}$
(16th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1965)

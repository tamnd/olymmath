Seven friends are celebrating their birthdays on the same day and are respectively aged $a_{1}, \cdots, a_{7}$. They have a total of $a_{1}+\cdots+a_{7}-6$ candles to distribute among themselves. Each would like to have at least as many candles as their age. Show that no matter how the candles are distributed, one of the friends will have enough candles to celebrate their birthday.

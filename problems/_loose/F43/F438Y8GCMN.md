Let $a, b$ and $c$ be natural numbers such that

$$
D(a, b)=4, D(b, c)=6 \text { and } V(a, b, c)=36000
$$

What is the maximum value of $D(a, c)$?

$D(m, n)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $m$ and $n$, and $V(p, q, r)$ denotes the least common multiple of $p, q$ and $r$.

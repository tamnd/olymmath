Given that $m, n$ are positive integers and $(m, n)=1$. Let $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{t}\right\},\left\{b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{s}\right\}$ be the reduced residue systems modulo $m$ and modulo $n$, respectively. Prove: $S=\left\{m b_{i}+n a_{j} \mid 1 \leqslant i \leqslant s, 1 \leqslant j \leqslant t\right\}$ is a reduced residue system modulo $m n$.

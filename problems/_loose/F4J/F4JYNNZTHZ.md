Given a triangle $ABC$ and a line $l$ that does not pass through its vertices.

a) Prove that the curve isogonal conjugate to the line $l$ is an ellipse if $l$ does not intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$; a parabola if $l$ is tangent to the circumcircle; a hyperbola if $l$ intersects the circumcircle at two points.

b) Prove that the curve isotomic conjugate to the line $l$ is an ellipse if $l$ does not intersect the Steiner circumellipse of triangle $ABC$; a parabola if $l$ is tangent to the Steiner circumellipse; a hyperbola if $l$ intersects the Steiner circumellipse at two points.

(7-8 grades) Each inhabitant of the island is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. One day, 50 islanders sat around a round table, and each one said whether their right-hand neighbor was a knight or a liar. In this case, the residents sitting in the $1-\mathrm{m}, 3-\mathrm{m}, \ldots$ and $49-\mathrm{m}$ places said "Knight," while those sitting in the 2-nd, 4-th, ... and 48-th places said "Liar." What could the resident sitting in the 50-th place have said? (The places are numbered in a circle, starting from some point.)

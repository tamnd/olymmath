It is known that from segments of lengths $a$ , $b$ and $c$ , a triangle can be formed. Could it happen that from segments of lengths $$ \sqrt{a^2 + \frac{2}{3} bc}, \sqrt{b^2 + \frac{2}{3} ca} \text{and} \sqrt{c^2 + \frac{2}{3} ab}, $$ a right-angled triangle can be formed?

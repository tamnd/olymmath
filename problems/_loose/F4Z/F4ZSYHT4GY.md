The sides and the diagonals of a regular $n$-gon $X$ are coloured in $k$ colours so that:
(i) for each colour $a$ and any two vertices $A$ and $B$ of $X$, the segment $A B$ is coloured in colour $a$ or there is a vertex $C$ such that $A C$ and $B C$ are coloured in colour $a$;

(ii) the sides of any triangle with vertices among the vertices of $X$ are coloured in at most two colours.

Prove that $k \leq 2$.

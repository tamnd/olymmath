Let $a, b, x, y$ be non-negative numbers, $k > 0$ and for these numbers the following inequalities are true:
$$
a + kx \le 1; a + ky \le 1; b + \frac{x}{k} \le 1; b + \frac{y}{k} \le 1.
$$

Prove that the following equation is true:
$$
(a + x)^2 + (b + y)^2 \le 2.
$$

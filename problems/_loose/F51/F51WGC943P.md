Show without using a table that if the angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ satisfy the system of equations

$$
\cos \alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4 \cos 10^{\circ}}, \cos \beta=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4 \cos 10^{\circ}}, \cos \gamma=\frac{1}{2 \cos 10^{\circ}}
$$

then $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles of a triangle.

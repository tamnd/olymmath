On a plane, all vertices of a regular $n$-gon and its center were marked. Then the contour of this $n$-gon was drawn, and the center was connected to all vertices; as a result, the $n$-gon was divided into $n$ triangles. Vasya wrote a number in each marked point (some of the numbers may be equal). In each triangle of the partition, he wrote in an arbitrary order the three numbers standing at its vertices; after that, he erased the numbers in the marked points. For which $n$ can Petya always restore the number in each marked point from the triples of numbers written in the triangles?

(I. Rubanov)

On the hundredth year of his reign, the Immortal Treasurer decided to start issuing new coins. In this year, he put into circulation an unlimited supply of coins with a value of $2^{100}-1$, the following year - with a value of $2^{101}-1$, and so on. As soon as the value of the next new coin can be exactly matched using the previously issued new coins, the Treasurer will be removed from office. In which year of his reign will this happen? (I.

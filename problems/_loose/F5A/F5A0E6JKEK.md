Express in terms of proportionality the formulas for centripetal acceleration:

$$
a=\frac{v^{2}}{\gamma}=\gamma \omega^{2}=\frac{4 \pi \gamma}{T^{2}}
$$

where $a$ denotes acceleration, $v$ - the speed of the moving point, $\gamma$ - the radius of the circular trajectory, $\omega$ - angular velocity, $\boldsymbol{T}$ - the period of revolution.

On a grid sheet of size $100 \times 100$, several non-overlapping cardboard isosceles right triangles with a leg of 1 were placed; each triangle occupies exactly half of one of the cells. It turned out that each unit segment of the grid (including boundary segments) is covered by exactly one leg of a triangle. Find the maximum possible number of cells that do not contain any triangle.

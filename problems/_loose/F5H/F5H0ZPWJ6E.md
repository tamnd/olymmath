The base of the triangular pyramid $S A B C$ is an equilateral triangle $A B C$ with a side length of 4. It is known that for any point $M$ on the extension of the height of the pyramid $S H$ (point $S$ is between points $M$ and $H$), the angles $M S A, M S B, M S C, A S B, A S C$, and $B S C$ are equal to each other. A sphere with a radius of $1 \mathrm{c}$ is constructed with its center at point $S$. Find the volume of the common part of the pyramid $S A B C$ and the sphere (the volume of a sphere with radius $R$ is calculated using the formula $V=\frac{4}{3} \pi R^{3}$.)

Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{1}=3, \\
n a_{n+1}=2(n+1) a_{n}-n-2(n \geqslant 1) .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: For any odd prime $p$, there exists a positive integer $m$, such that $a_{m}$ and $a_{m+1}$ are both multiples of $p$.

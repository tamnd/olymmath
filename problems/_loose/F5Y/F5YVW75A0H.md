Given that $C, D$ are two points on the semicircle $O$ with diameter $AB$, the chords $AD, BC$ intersect at point $E, F, G$ are points on the extensions of $AC, BD$ respectively, and satisfy $AF \cdot BG = AE \cdot BE$. If the orthocenters of $\triangle AEF, \triangle BEG$ are $H_1, H_2$ respectively, prove (1) the intersection point $K$ of $AH_1, BH_2$ lies on the circle $O$; (2) $F, K, G$ are collinear.

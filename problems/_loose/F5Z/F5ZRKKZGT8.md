On some fields of a square chessboard $n \times n (n \geq 2)$, we place a piece and then move it alternately "diagonally" and "straight". "Diagonally" means to a field that shares exactly one point with the previous one. "Straight" means to an adjacent field that shares a side with the previous one. Determine all $n$ for which there exists an initial field and a sequence of moves starting "diagonally" such that the piece visits every field of the chessboard exactly once.

(Peter Novotný)

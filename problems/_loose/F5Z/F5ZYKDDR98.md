Let $ABC$ be a triangle, where $\gamma > \beta$ (with $\gamma$ denoting the angle at vertex $C$, and $\beta$ the angle at vertex $B$). Let $O$ be the center of the inscribed circle, and $M, N$, and $L$ be the points on sides $BC, AB$, and $AC$, respectively, where the inscribed circle touches the sides of the triangle. Furthermore, let $K$ be the intersection point of the lines $AO$ and $MN$. Prove that $\measuredangle AKC$ is a right angle.

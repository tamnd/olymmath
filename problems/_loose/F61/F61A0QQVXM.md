The numbers $1,2,3,\ldots,2010$ are written on the blackboard. Two players in turn erase some two numbers and replace them with one number. The first player replaces numbers $a$ and $b$ with $ab-a-b$ while the second player replaces them with $ab+a+b.$ The game ends when a single number remains on the blackboard. If this number is smaller than $1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot\ldots\cdot2010$ then the first player wins. Otherwise the second player wins. Which of the players has a winning strategy?

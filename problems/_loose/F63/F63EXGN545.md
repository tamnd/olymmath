Given $2 n$ positive real numbers $a_{1} \geqslant a_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant a_{n}, b_{1} \geqslant b_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant b_{n}$ and $0 \leqslant \lambda \leqslant 2$, let
$$
M=\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{i}{ }^{2}+b_{i}{ }^{2}-\lambda a_{i} b_{i}}, N=\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{i=1}^{2}+b_{i}^{2}-\lambda a_{i+1} b_{i}} \text {, }
$$

where, $a_{n+1}=a_{1}$. Then the relationship between $M$ and $N$ is
A. $M \geqslant N$
B. $M \leqslant N$
C. depends on $\lambda$
D. completely uncertain

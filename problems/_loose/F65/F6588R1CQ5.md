We say that a number $n$ undergoes an interesting transformation if $n$ is multiplied by 2, and then the result is increased by 4; we say that $n$ undergoes a special transformation if $n$ is multiplied by 3, and then the result is increased by 9; we say that $n$ undergoes a wonderful transformation if $n$ is multiplied by 4, and then the result is increased by 16.

a) Show that there is a unique number which, through three successive transformations, one interesting, one special, and one wonderful, applied in this order, becomes 2020.

b) Determine the numbers which, after exactly two successive different transformations, among the three types, become 2014.

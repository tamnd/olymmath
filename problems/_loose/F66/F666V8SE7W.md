We say that two natural numbers form a perfect pair when the sum and the product of these two numbers are perfect squares. For example, 5 and 20 form a perfect pair, since $5+20=25=5^{2}$ and $5 \times 20=100=10^{2}$. Does 122 form a perfect pair with another natural number?

(4th China High School Mathematics Olympiad Winter Camp Problem) Let $f$ be a function defined on $(1,+\infty)$ and taking values in $(1,+\infty)$, satisfying the condition: for any $x, y>1$ and $u, v>0$, it holds that
$$
f\left(x^{n} y^{v}\right) \leqslant f^{\frac{1}{1 .}}(x) f^{\frac{1}{1 .}}(y)
$$

Determine all such functions $f$.

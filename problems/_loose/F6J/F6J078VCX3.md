A sphere of radius 2 is inscribed in a regular triangular pyramid $S A B C$ with vertex $S$ and base $A B C$; the height of the pyramid $S K$ is 6. Prove that there exists a unique plane intersecting the edges of the base $A B$ and $B C$ at some points $M$ and $N$ such that $|M N|=7$, touching the sphere at a point equidistant from points $M$ and $N$, and intersecting the extension of the pyramid's height $S K$ beyond point $K$ at some point $D$. Find the length of the segment $S D$.

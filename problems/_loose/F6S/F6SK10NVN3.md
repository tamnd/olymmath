On a circular table with radius $R$, we placed $n$ coins with radius $r$ such that each coin lies flat on the table with its entire face. No more coins can be placed on the table. Show that $\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{R}{r}-1\right) \leq \sqrt{n} \leq \frac{R}{r}$.

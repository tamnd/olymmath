a) In a convex hexagon $A B C D E F$, all interior angles are equal. Prove that $\boldsymbol{A} B-D E=\boldsymbol{F} E-B C=D C-\boldsymbol{F} A$.

b) Conversely, prove that from six segments $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}, a_{5}$, $a_{6}$, whose lengths satisfy the relations $a_{1}-a_{4}=a_{5}-a_{2}=a_{3}-a_{6}$, a convex hexagon with equal angles can be formed.

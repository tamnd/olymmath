In the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, $a_{1}=1$, the equation about $x$
$$
x^{2}-a_{n+1} \cos x+\left(2 a_{n}+1\right)=0
$$

has a unique solution.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Let $b_{n}=\left[1+\frac{1}{\log _{2}\left(a_{n}+1\right)}\right]^{n}$, prove: $b_{n}<3$;
(3) Let $c_{n}=n^{2} a_{n}$, find the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$, $S_{n}$.

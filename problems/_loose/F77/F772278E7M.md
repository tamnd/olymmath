Let $\alpha, \beta, \delta$ be fixed numbers. Using these, we define two operations denoted by $\circ$ and $*$, such that for any numbers $a, b$,

$$
\begin{gathered}
a \circ b=\alpha a+\beta b \\
a * b=\gamma a+\delta b
\end{gathered}
$$

What conditions must the numbers $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ satisfy for the following two equalities to be identities?

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (a \circ b) * c=(a * c) \circ(b * c) \\
& (a * b) \circ c=(a \circ c) *(b \circ c)
\end{aligned}
$$

The radii of the base circles of a truncated cone are: $R$ and $r$ (where $R > r$). Each base circle, together with the opposite circle's center as the vertex, defines a rotational cone. The parts of these two cone surfaces and the lateral surface of the given truncated cone bound a solid with a volume of $k$. Calculate:

a) the volume $K$ of the truncated cone,

b) the surface area $F$ of the lateral surface of the truncated cone, given $R$, $r$, and $k$.

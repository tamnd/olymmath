(Adapted from the 2002 National High School Competition) Let the quadratic function $f(x)=a x^{2}+b x+c(a, b, c \in$ R) satisfy the following conditions:
(1) For $x \in \mathbf{R}$, $f(x-4)=f(2-x)$, and $f(x) \geqslant x$;
(2) For $x \in(0,2)$, $f(x) \leqslant\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right)^{2}$;
(3) The minimum value of $f(x)$ on $\mathbf{R}$ is 0.
Find $f(x)$.

Let $\ell$ be a line and let points $A$ , $B$ , $C$ lie on $\ell$ so that $AB = 7$ and $BC = 5$ . Let $m$ be the line through $A$ perpendicular to $\ell$ . Let $P$ lie on $m$ . Compute the smallest possible value of $PB + PC$ .

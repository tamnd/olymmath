Each median of a triangle divides the triangle's area into two triangular areas that have equal base lengths and equal heights, and are thus equal in area. The intersection point of the medians is called the centroid of the triangle.

Investigate whether every line through the centroid $S$ of a triangle $\triangle A B C$ divides its area into two equal-area sub-regions!

On the base $B C$ of trapezoid $A B C D$, a point $E$ is taken, lying on the same circle with points $A, C$, and $D$. Another circle, passing through points $A, B$, and $C$, is tangent to the line $C D$. Find $B C$, if $A B=12$ and $B E: E C=4$ : 5. Find all possible values of the ratio of the radius of the first circle to the radius of the second under these conditions.

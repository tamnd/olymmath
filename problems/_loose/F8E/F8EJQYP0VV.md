Use Chebyshev's inequality to prove the relationship between the quadratic mean and the arithmetic mean:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}^{2}}{n}} \geq \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}}{n}
$$

There are $3 n(n>1)$ points equally dividing a circle, and $n$ +2 points are chosen from these $3 n$ points.

Prove that among the chosen points, there always exist two points such that both arcs connecting these two points on the circle are greater than $\frac{1}{3}$ of the circumference.

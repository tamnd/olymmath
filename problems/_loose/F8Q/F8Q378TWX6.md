On the left bank of the river, there are 100 dwarfs who cannot swim, with weights of 1, $2, \cdots, 100$ pounds respectively, who need to get to the right bank. On the left bank, there is a small boat with a carrying capacity of 100 pounds. Due to the current, it is very difficult to go from the right bank to the left bank, and each dwarf can only row from the right bank to the left bank once (any dwarf can row from the right bank to the left bank, but cannot be replaced mid-way). Question: Can all the dwarfs be ferried to the right bank?

Circle $\omega$ is tangent to sides $A B$ and $A C$ of triangle $A B C$. Circle $\Omega$ is tangent to side $A C$ and the extension of side $A B$ beyond point $B$, and also tangent to $\omega$ at point $L$, which lies on side $B C$. Line $A L$ intersects $\omega$ and $\Omega$ again at points $K$ and $M$ respectively. It turns out that $K B \| C M$. Prove that triangle $L C M$ is isosceles.

(I. Bogdanov)

Six integers. Find six different smallest integers in such a way that the product of any five arbitrarily chosen from this six equals one or several periods in the decimal representation of the number, which is the reciprocal of the remaining sixth number. For example, if we take the decimal representation of the reciprocal of 41,

$$
\frac{1}{41}=0,243902439 \ldots
$$

then the period will be 02439.

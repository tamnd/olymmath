Given two different positive integers $a, b > 1$. Prove:
(1) There exist infinitely many positive integers $n$, such that
$$
s_{n}=a^{n}+b^{n+1}
$$

is composite.
(2) There exist infinitely many primes $p$, such that there exists $n$, $p \mid\left(a^{n}+b^{n+1}\right)$

If the difference between the roots of the equation $x^{2}+p x+1=0 (p>0)$ is 1, then $p$ equals
(A) 2.
(B) 4.
(C) $\sqrt{3}$.
(D) $\sqrt{5}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1998)

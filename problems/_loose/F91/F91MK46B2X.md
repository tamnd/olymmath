Given a finite sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ with $2 k$ terms (integer $k \geqslant 2$), the first term $a_{1}=2$. Let the sum of the first $n$ terms be $S_{n}$, and $a_{n+1}=(a-1) S_{n}+2(n=1,2, \cdots, 2 k-1)$, where the constant $a>1$.
(1) Prove that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a geometric sequence;
(2) If $a=2^{\frac{2}{2 k-1}}$, the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $b_{n}=\frac{1}{n} \log _{2}\left(a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}\right)(n=1,2, \cdots, 2 k)$, find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$;
(3) If the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ in (2) satisfies the inequality $\left|b_{1}-\frac{3}{2}\right|+\left|b_{2}-\frac{3}{2}\right|+\cdots+\mid b_{2 k-1}-$ $\left.\frac{3}{2}|+| b_{2 k}-\frac{3}{2} \right\rvert \leqslant 4$, find the value of $k$.

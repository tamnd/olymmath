a) If $x, y$ and $z$ are strictly positive rational numbers and $\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}+\sqrt{z} \in \mathbb{Q}$, show that $\sqrt{x}, \sqrt{y}$, and $\sqrt{z}$ are rational numbers.

b) Consider the non-zero natural numbers $a, b$, and $c$ such that the numbers $x=(a+1)^{2}-4 b$, $y=(b+1)^{2}-4 c$, and $z=(c+1)^{2}-4 a$ are non-zero natural numbers. If $\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}+\sqrt{z}=2013$, determine the numbers $a, b$, and $c$.

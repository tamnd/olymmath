Given a right triangular prism $A B C A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. A sphere with diameter $B C$ intersects the edges $A C$ and $A B$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively, different from the vertices of the prism. Segments $B_{1} P$ and $C_{1} Q$ intersect at point $T$, and $B_{1} P=5, T Q=2$.

a) Find the angle $T P A$.

b) Find the ratio $A P: C P$.

c) Suppose it is additionally known that $A C=3$. Find the volume of the prism.

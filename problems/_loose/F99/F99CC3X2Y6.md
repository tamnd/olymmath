Tournaments. The so-called cup system for determining the winner from 8 athletes consists of dividing the players into pairs by drawing lots. 4 matches determine four winners, who participate in the second round; the third round of the competition is the final. The winner of the final match receives the first prize, and their opponent receives the second prize. Let's assume that each player has a certain strength (similar to how each object has a certain weight) and that a stronger player always wins against a weaker one (similar to how a heavier object always outweighs a lighter one if placed on different scales). Under these assumptions, the process described above is suitable for determining the champion, as the winner will indeed be stronger than all their opponents; however, the second place is not always occupied by the second strongest player.

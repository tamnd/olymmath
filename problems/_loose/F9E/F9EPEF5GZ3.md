(USA, 82). Inside the equilateral triangle $A B C$, a point $A_{1}$ is taken, and inside the triangle $A_{1} B C$ - a point $A_{2}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{S_{1}}{P_{1}^{2}}>\frac{S_{2}}{P_{2}^{2}}
$$

where $S_{1}, S_{2}$ and $P_{1}, P_{2}$ are the areas and perimeters of triangles $A_{1} B C, A_{2} B C$ respectively.

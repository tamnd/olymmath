We will call a cascade generated by a number $r$, a set of 12 natural numbers: $r, 2r, \ldots, 12r$.

a) Can some pair of numbers $(a, b)$ be contained in six different cascades? If yes, provide an example of such numbers; if no, explain why it cannot be.

b) Is it true that the set of natural numbers can be colored in 12 colors such that in each cascade all elements will be of different colors?

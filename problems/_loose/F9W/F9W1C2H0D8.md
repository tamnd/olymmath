Prove that for any positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, the inequality
$$
\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{2}{a_{1}+a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{n}{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}}<4\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right) \text { holds. }
$$
(20th All-Soviet Union Olympiad)

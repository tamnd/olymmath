Prove that: if the sum

$$
a_{1} \cos \left(x+\alpha_{1}\right)+a_{2} \cos \left(x+\alpha_{2}\right)+\ldots+a_{n} \cos \left(x+\alpha_{n}\right)
$$

at $x=0$ and $x=x_{1} \neq k \pi, k \in \mathbb{Z}$ is equal to zero, then it is zero for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

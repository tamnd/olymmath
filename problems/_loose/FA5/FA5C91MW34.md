Given that the center of ellipse $C$ is at the origin, the foci are on the $x$-axis, the eccentricity is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and the area of the triangle formed by any three vertices of the ellipse $C$ is $\frac{1}{2}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) If a line $l$ passing through the point $P(\lambda, 0)$ intersects the ellipse at two distinct points $A$ and $B$, and $\overrightarrow{A P}=2 \overrightarrow{P B}$, find the range of the real number $\lambda$.

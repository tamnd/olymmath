Find all triangles $ABC$ for which the equality

$$
|BC| \cdot|AX|=|AC| \cdot|BY|,
$$

holds, where point $X$ is the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ with side $BC$ and point $Y$ is the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ with side $AC$. (P. Černek)

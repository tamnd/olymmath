Given points $O, A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ on a plane. The distance between any two of these points is the square root of a natural number. Prove that there exist such vectors $\boldsymbol{x}$ and $\boldsymbol{y}$, that for any point $A_{i}$ the equality $\overrightarrow{O A_{i}}=k \boldsymbol{x}+l \boldsymbol{y}$ holds, where $k$ and $l$ are some integers.

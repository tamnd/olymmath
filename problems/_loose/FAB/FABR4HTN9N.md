In the pyramid $S A B C$, a line intersecting the edges $A C$ and $B S$ and perpendicular to them passes through the midpoint of the edge $B S$. The face $A S B$ is equal in area to the face $B S C$, and the area of the face $A S C$ is twice the area of the face $B S C$. Inside the pyramid, there is a point $M$, the sum of the distances from which to the vertices $B$ and $S$ is equal to the sum of the distances to all the faces of the pyramid. Find the distance from point $M$ to vertex $B$, if $|A C|=\sqrt{6},|B S|=1$.

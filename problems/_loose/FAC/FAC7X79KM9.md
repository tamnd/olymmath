Let $D$ and $E$ be points on the sides $BC$ and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$ such that points $A, B, D$, and $E$ lie on the same circle. Let $L$ be the center of the incircle of triangle $BCE$, and let $G$ be the point of tangency of this incircle with side $EC$. Let $K$ be the center of the incircle of triangle $DCA$, and let $F$ be the point of tangency of this incircle with side $DC$. Let $N$ be the intersection of lines $EL$ and $DK$, and $M$ be the intersection of lines $KF$ and $LG$. Prove that points $A, B, D$, and $N$ lie on the same circle and that $KMLN$ is a kite.

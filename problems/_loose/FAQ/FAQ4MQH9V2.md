If the three roots of the equation $(x-1)\left(x^{2}-2 x+m\right)=0$ can be the lengths of the three sides of a triangle, then the range of the real number $m$ is
(A) $0 \leqslant m \leqslant 1$.
(B) $m \geqslant \frac{3}{4}$.
(C) $\frac{3}{4}<m \leqslant 1$.
(D) $\frac{3}{4} \leqslant m \leqslant 1$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1995)

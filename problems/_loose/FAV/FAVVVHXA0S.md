Let $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{~b}$ be two real numbers, $\mathrm{A}=\{(\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}) \mid \mathrm{x}=\mathrm{n}, \mathrm{y}=\mathrm{na}+\mathrm{b}, \mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{Z}\}, \mathrm{B}=$ $\left\{(\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}) \mid \mathrm{x}=\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{y}=3 \mathrm{~m}^{2}+15, \mathrm{~m} \in \mathrm{Z}\right\}, \mathrm{C}=\left\{(\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}) \mid \mathrm{x}^{2}+\mathrm{y}^{2} \leq 144, \mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y} \in \mathrm{R}\right\}$. Does there exist real numbers $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{~b}$ that simultaneously satisfy: (1) $\mathrm{A} \cap \mathrm{B} \neq \emptyset$; (2) $(\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}) \in \mathrm{C}$?

In a school mathematics competition, 1200 students participated. The number of points a student can achieve is an integer between 0 and 50. Due to a computer error, all students who scored 3 or fewer points had their results recorded as 0 points, and all students who scored 47 or more points had their results recorded as 50 points. Because of this error, the average score according to the computer data is 0.1 higher than the actual average.

Prove that there exist numbers $a$ and $b$ such that the number of students who scored exactly $a$ points and the number of students who scored exactly $b$ points differ by at least 20.

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive numbers such that both of the equations $(a+b-x)^{2}=a-b$ and $(a b+1-x)^{2}=a b-1$ have two distinct real roots. Prove that if the two bigger roots are equal then the two smaller roots are also equal.

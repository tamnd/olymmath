Let $S_{n}=\sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}$. Prove that for every natural number $n>1$ the inequality holds:

$$
2 \sqrt{n+1}-2<S_{n}<2 \sqrt{n}-1
$$

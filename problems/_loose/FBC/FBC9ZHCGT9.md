If $A B C$ is a triangle, let $S$ denote the intersection of its medians, and let $U, V$ and $W$ denote the feet of the perpendiculars from $S$ to the sides $B C, C A$ and $A B$ respectively. Let $J(A B C)$ and $J(U V W)$ denote the area of triangle $A B C$ and triangle $U V W$ respectively.

Prove that the ratio $r=J(U V W): J(A B C)$ is the same for all right-angled triangles $A B C$ and determine this value $r$.

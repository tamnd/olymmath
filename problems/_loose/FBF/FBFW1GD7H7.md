In an equilateral triangle $ABC$, through a random point inside it, three lines are drawn: parallel to $AB$ until intersecting with $BC$ and $CA$; parallel to $BC$ until intersecting with $AB$ and $CA$; parallel to $CA$ until intersecting with $BC$ and $AB$. Prove that the sum of the three obtained segments is equal to twice the side of the triangle $ABC$.

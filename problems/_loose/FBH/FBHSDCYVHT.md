Let $p$ be a prime number. Prove that

$1^{0} .\binom{p-1}{k}^{2}-1$ is divisible by $p$,

$2^{0} .\binom{p-1}{0}^{s}+\binom{p-1}{1}^{s}+\binom{p-1}{2}^{s}+\cdots+\binom{p-1}{p-1}^{s}$ is divisible by $p$ if $s$ is even, but leaves a remainder of 1 when $s$ is odd.

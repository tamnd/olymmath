(CSSR, 73). Prove that for the heights $ h_{i} (i=1, 2,3,4) $ of any tetrahedron and the radii $ r_{i} $ of the exinscribed spheres, the following equality holds:

$$
2\left(\frac{1}{h_{1}}+\frac{1}{h_{2}}+\frac{1}{h_{3}}+\frac{1}{h_{4}}\right)=\frac{1}{r_{1}}+\frac{1}{r_{2}}+\frac{1}{r_{3}}+\frac{1}{r_{4}}
$$

Consider the equation $x^{5}+y^{2}=z^{3}$, where $x, y$ and $z$ are non-zero natural numbers. A solution to this equation is a triplet of non-zero natural numbers $(a, b, c)$ with the property that $a^{5}+b^{2}=c^{3}$.

a) Show that the triplet $(3,10,7)$ is a solution to the given equation.

b) Determine a solution to the given equation of the form ( $2^{m}, 2^{n}, 2^{p}$ ), where $m, n$ and $p$ are natural numbers.

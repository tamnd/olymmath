Let $n \geqslant 3$ be an integer and $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ a regular polygon inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$ with center $O$ and radius $R$. Let $M$ be a point on $\Gamma$. Prove that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} M^{2}=2 n R^{2}
$$

We conclude with a very useful property:

Proposition 3.7. Let $A, B, C, D$ be points in the plane with $A \neq B$ and $C \neq D$. The lines $(AB)$ and $(CD)$ are perpendicular if and only if $A C^{2}+B D^{2}=A D^{2}+B C^{2}$.

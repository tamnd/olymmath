Let $P(\mathrm{x})=a_{n} x^{n}+\ldots+a_{1} x+a_{0}$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients.

Prove that at least one of the numbers $\left|3^{n+1}-P(n+1)\right|, \ldots,\left|3^{1}-P(1)\right|,|1-P(0)|$ is not less than 1.

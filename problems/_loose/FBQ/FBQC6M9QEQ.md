Given real numbers $a>b>c$, and satisfying:
$$
a+b+c=1, a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=3 \text {. }
$$

Then the range of $s=b+c$ is ( ).
(A) $-\frac{2}{3}<s<2$
(B) $-\frac{2}{3}<s<1$
(C) $-\frac{2}{3}<s<\frac{2}{3}$
(D) $-\frac{2}{3}<s<0$

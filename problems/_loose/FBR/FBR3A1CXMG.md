Given two tangent circles $c_{1}, c_{2}$, point $P$ is on the radical axis, i.e., on the common tangent perpendicular to the line connecting the centers of the two circles. Try to construct all circles $c$ using a compass and straightedge such that $c$ is tangent to $c_{1}, c_{2}$ and passes through point $P$.

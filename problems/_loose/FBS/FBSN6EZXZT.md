In an acute triangle $ABC$ with $AB < AC$, let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$, and let $P$ be an arbitrary point on the line perpendicular to $BC$ at $B$. The perpendicular from $P$ to $AC$ meets $AD$ at $M$. The reflection of $P$ with respect to $M$ is denoted by $Q$. The lines \AD and \CQ intersect at $K$. If $E$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $M$ to \AB, prove that:
$$
\angle KEM = \angle KQM.
$$

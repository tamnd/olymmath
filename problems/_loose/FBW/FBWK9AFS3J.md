The diagonals of trapezoid $A B C D$ with bases $\overline{A B}$ and $\overline{C D}$ intersect at point $S$. Let $P_{1}$ be the area of triangle $\triangle A B S$, $P_{2}$ be the area of triangle $\triangle C D S$, and $P$ be the area of trapezoid $A B C D$. Prove that $P=\left(\sqrt{P_{1}}+\sqrt{P_{2}}\right)^{2}$.

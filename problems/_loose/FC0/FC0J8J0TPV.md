A square with a side of $4 \mathrm{~cm}$ is divided into 16 smaller squares with a side of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$. In each of these smaller squares, we can write exactly one of the numbers $0, \sqrt{2015}$, and $-\sqrt{2015}$. All three numbers are used.

After this completion, we calculate the sums of each column, each row, and the two diagonals.

a) Prove that the smallest possible sum that can be obtained is an irrational number.

b) Can the square be completed such that all the sums considered are equal? Provide an example.

c) Can the square be completed such that all the sums considered are different? Justify.

Suppose there are 6 labeled balls, 3 of which are red balls, denoted as $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$; the remaining 3 are blue balls, denoted as $b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}$. The combinations of choosing 3 balls from the 6 balls are:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
r_{1} r_{2} r_{3}, b_{1} b_{2} b_{3}, r_{1} r_{2} b_{1}, r_{1} r_{2} b_{2}, r_{1} r_{2} b_{3}, r_{1} r_{3} b_{1}, r_{1} r_{3} b_{2}, \\
r_{1} r_{3} b_{3}, r_{2} r_{3} b_{1}, r_{2} r_{3} b_{2}, r_{2} r_{3} b_{3}, r_{1} b_{1} b_{2}, r_{1} b_{1} b_{3}, r_{1} b_{2} b_{3}, \\
r_{2} b_{3} b_{2}, r_{2} b_{1} b_{3}, r_{2} b_{2} b_{3}, r_{3} b_{1} b_{2}, r_{3} b_{3}, r_{3} b_{2} b_{3} .
\end{array}
$$

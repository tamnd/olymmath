In the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, it is known that $AD \perp CD$ at point $D$, $AB \perp CB$ at point $B$, $AE \perp BD$ at point $E$, points $H$ and $I$ are on sides $AD$ and $AB$ respectively, and satisfy
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle CEH - \angle CHD = 90^{\circ}, \\
\angle CEI - \angle CIB = 90^{\circ}.
\end{array}
$$

Prove: The circumcircle of $\triangle HIE$ is tangent to the line $BD$. [2]

In an acute triangle $ABC$, altitudes $AA'$ and $BB'$ are drawn. Let $D$ be a point on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ on the arc $ACB$. Suppose the line $AA'$ intersects $BD$ at point $P$, and the line $BB'$ intersects $AD$ at point $Q$. Prove: The line $A'B'$ passes through the midpoint of segment $PQ$.

What is the length of the road on flat ground if the tourist's speed downhill is 6 km/h, uphill is 4 km/h, and on flat ground is 5 km/h, and the total distance between $A$ and $B$ is $x$ km? Additionally, the distances uphill and on flat ground are whole numbers of kilometers.

Let $a_{0}$ be a strictly positive integer. We define the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 0}$ such that for all $n \geqslant 0, a_{n+1}$ is the smallest strictly positive integer such that $a_{n+1}+a_{n}+\cdots+a_{0}$ is a multiple of $n+1$. Show that the sequence becomes stationary.

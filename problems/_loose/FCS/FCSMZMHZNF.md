To verify whether the cross-section of a bolt is exactly circular, the bolt is rotated between the closed measuring jaws of a caliper and the change in the distance between the measuring jaws is observed. Undoubtedly, a change in the distance between the measuring jaws allows the conclusion that the cross-section is not circular.

Does the constancy of the measuring jaw distance, conversely, allow the conclusion that the cross-section is exactly circular?

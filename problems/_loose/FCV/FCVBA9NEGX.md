Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a finite group with the neutral element denoted by $e$. Suppose there exists $a \in G \backslash\{e\}$ and a prime number $p$ with the property $x^{p+1}=a^{-1} x a$, for any $x \in G$.

a) Show that there exists $k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $\operatorname{ord}(G)=p^{k}$.

b) Show that the set $\left\{x \in G \mid x^{p}=e\right\}$ is a subgroup $H$ of $G$ and $(\operatorname{ord}(H))^{2}>$ $\operatorname{ord}(G)$.

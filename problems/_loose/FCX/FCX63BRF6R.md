Let $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be distinct integers. Prove that the polynomial

$$
\left(X-a_{1}\right)^{2} \ldots\left(X-a_{n}\right)^{2}+1
$$

is irreducible over $\mathbb{Z}[X]$.

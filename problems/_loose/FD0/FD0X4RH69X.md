Petya and Vasya found two bags of caramels and one huge chocolate bar. They decided to divide what they found by playing the following game: on the first move, Petya takes several caramels from one of the bags (he chooses the bag from which he takes the caramels at his discretion) and moves the same number of caramels from this bag to the other. Then Vasya does the same (he chooses the bag from which he takes the caramels at his discretion). This continues until it is no longer possible to take caramels according to these rules. The one who takes the last caramel gets the chocolate bar and is declared the winner. Who will win with the correct play, if the bags contain:

a) 2021 and 2021 caramels?

b) 1000 and 2000 caramels?

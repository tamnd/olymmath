A nine-digit natural number $A$, written in the decimal system, is obtained from the number $B$ by moving the last digit to the first place. It is known that the number $B$ is coprime with the number 18 and $B>222222222$. Find the largest and smallest among the numbers $A$ that satisfy these conditions. (Two natural numbers are called coprime if they have no common divisors other than one). (8 points)

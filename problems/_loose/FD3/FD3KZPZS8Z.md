Let the roots of $A x^{2}+B x+C=0$ be $r$ and $s$, and the roots of $x^{2}+p x+q=0$ be $r^{2}$ and $s^{2}$, then $p$ must equal
(A) $\frac{B^{2}-4 A C}{A^{2}}$.
(B) $\frac{B^{2}-2 A C}{A^{2}}$.
(C) $\frac{2 A C-B^{2}}{A^{2}}$.
(D) $B^{2}-2 C$.
(E) $2 C-B^{2}$.
(9th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1958)

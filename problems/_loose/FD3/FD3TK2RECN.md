Participants to an olympiad worked on $n$ problems. Each problem was worth a positive integer number of points, determined by the jury. A contestant gets $0$ points for a wrong answer, and all points for a correct answer to a problem. It turned out after the olympiad that the jury could impose worths of the problems, so as to obtain any (strict) final ranking of the contestants. Find the greatest possible number of contestants.

Let $f, g: \mathbb{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{*}$ be functions. Salomé starts from $\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)=(0,0)$ and moves on the integer grid. For each $n \geqslant 1$, she jumps by $\pm f(n)$ on one axis (the one she chooses) and by $\pm g(n)$ on the other. Can she be sure to pass through $(0,0)$ infinitely many times:

1. if $f, g$ are polynomials in $\mathbb{Z}[X]$?
2. if $f, g$ are arbitrary?

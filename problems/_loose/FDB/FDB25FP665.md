50 students stand in a row facing the teacher. The teacher first asks everyone to report numbers from left to right as $1, 2, \cdots, 50$; then asks the students who report numbers that are multiples of 3 to turn around, and then asks the students who report numbers that are multiples of 7 to turn around. How many students are now facing the teacher?

Let's find all values of $a$ for which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
2^{b x}+(a+1) b y^{2}=a^{2} \\
(a-1) x^{3}+y^{3}=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

has at least one solution for any value of $b,(a, b, x, y \in \mathbf{R})$.

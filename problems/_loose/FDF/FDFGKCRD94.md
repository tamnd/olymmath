Let $\Gamma$ and $\Gamma^{\prime}$ be two intersecting circles at $A$ and $A^{\prime}$. A first common tangent to both circles touches them at $B$ and $C$, and the other common tangent touches them at $D$ and $E$. Show that the orthocenters of the triangles $A B C, A^{\prime} B C, A D E$ and $A^{\prime} D E$ form a rectangle.

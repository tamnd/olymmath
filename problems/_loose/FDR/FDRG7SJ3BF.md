Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a circle with center $O$ passing through $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ be the midpoint of the arc $A B$. Let $D$ be a point such that $A, B$, and $D$ are collinear in that order. Let $E$ be the intersection of $(B C)$ with the line parallel to $(A C)$ passing through $D$. Let $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ be the circumcircle of $O C E$ with center $I$. $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ intersect at $C$ and $F$. Show that $(O I)$ is parallel to $(D F)$.

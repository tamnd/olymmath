Given an angle $\varphi<180^{\circ}$ with vertex $O$ and a fixed point $M$ within the angle. A variable line $g$ through $M$ intersects the sides of the angle at points $A$ and $B$. Prove that then

$$
\frac{1}{F(A M O)}+\frac{1}{F(B M O)}=\text { constant }
$$

holds, where $F(X M O)$ denotes the area of triangle $X M O$.

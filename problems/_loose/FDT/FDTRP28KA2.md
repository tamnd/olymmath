Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, and $H$ its orthocenter. The distance from $H$ to rays $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ is denoted by $d_a$, $d_b$, and $d_c$, respectively. Let $R$ be the radius of circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$ and $r$ be the radius of incenter of $\triangle ABC$. Prove the following inequality:
$$d_a+d_b+d_c \le \frac{3R^2}{4r}$$.

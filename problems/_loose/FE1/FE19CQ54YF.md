Given a positive integer $k$ , let $f(k)$ be the sum of the $k$ -th powers of the primitive roots of $73$ . For how many positive integers $k < 2015$ is $f(k)$ divisible by $73?$ *Note: A primitive root of $r$ of a prime $p$ is an integer $1 \le r < p$ such that the smallest positive integer $k$ such that $r^k \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$ is $k = p-1$ .*

Žiga will prepare identical shelves from a board that is $30 \mathrm{~cm}$ wide and $30 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick. He will attach them to a wall in the shape of a rectangle that is $2.5 \mathrm{~m}$ high and $3 \mathrm{~m}$ wide, one above the other, with a vertical distance of $4 \mathrm{dm}$ between them. Each shelf will be $8 \mathrm{dm}$ from the right and left edges of the wall. The highest shelf will be $1 \mathrm{~m}$ from the ceiling, and the lowest will be at least half a meter but no more than one meter from the floor.

A. How many shelves will he attach to the wall in this way?

B. What is the minimum length of the board he will use to prepare these shelves?

C. What is the minimum amount of wood he will use for these shelves? Express the volume in cubic decimeters to one decimal place.

D. Žiga will stretch a non-elastic string from the upper left corner of the front part of the highest shelf to the lower right corner of the front part of the lowest shelf. How long will the stretched string be? Write the length of the string to the nearest millimeter.

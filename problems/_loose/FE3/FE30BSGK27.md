a) Prove that $[a, [b, c]] + [b, [c, a]] + [c, [a, b]] = 0$ (Jacobi identity).

b) Let point $O$ lie inside triangle $ABC$ and $\mathbf{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$, $\mathbf{b} = \overrightarrow{OB}$, and $\mathbf{c} = \overrightarrow{OC}$. Prove that the Jacobi identity for vectors $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$, and $\mathbf{c}$ is equivalent to the equality

$$
\mathbf{a} S_{BOC} + \mathbf{b} S_{COA} + \mathbf{c} S_{OAB} = \mathbf{0}
$$

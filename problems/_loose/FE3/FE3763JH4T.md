The capacities of three vessels $A$, B, C, each of which has the shape of a cube, are in the ratio $1: 8: 27$, and the volumes of water poured into them are in the ratio 1:2:3. After transferring part of the water from vessel A to vessel B and from vessel B to vessel C, the water level in all three vessels became the same. Then, $128 \frac{4}{7}$ liters of water were transferred from vessel C to vessel B, and after that, from vessel B to vessel A, so that the water depth in vessel A became twice that in vessel B. As a result, it turned out that there was now $10 \theta$ liters less water in vessel A than there was initially. How much water was there initially in each vessel?

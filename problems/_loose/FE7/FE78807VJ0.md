Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ with an eccentricity of $\frac{2}{3}$, $A_{1}, A_{2}$ are the left and right vertices of the ellipse $C$, $B$ is the upper vertex of the ellipse, $F_{1}$ is the left focus of the ellipse $C$, and the area of $\triangle A_{1} F_{1} B$ is $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Let the moving line $l$ passing through the point $D(1,0)$ intersect the ellipse $C$ at points $E, F$ (point $E$ is above the $x$-axis), and let $M, N$ be the points where the lines $A_{1} E, A_{2} F$ intersect the $y$-axis, respectively. Find the value of $\frac{|O M|}{|O N|}$.

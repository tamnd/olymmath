Let $n$ be a positive integer. Show that \begin{align*}& \,\,\frac{1}{\binom{n}{1}}+\frac{1}{2\binom{n}{2}}+\frac{1}{3\binom{n}{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n\binom{n}{n}}\\&=\frac{1}{2^{n-1}}+\frac{1}{2\cdot2^{n-2}}+\frac{1}{3\cdot2^{n-3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n\cdot2^0}.\end{align*}

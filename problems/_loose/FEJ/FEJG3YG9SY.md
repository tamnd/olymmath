$^{+}$Notice that, in order to apply the infinite lemma, it suffices to find a set of independent edges in each finite induced subgraph $G_{2}$ of $G$, such that this edge set covers those vertices that have no neighbors in $G-G_{n}$. To find such an edge set, apply the finite 1-factor theorem to the graph $H_{n}$ (here, $H_{n}$ is obtained by adding a large complete graph $K$ to $G_{n}$ and fully connecting $K$ to those vertices in $G_{n}$ that have neighbors in $G-G_{n}$). If you encounter difficulties, change the parity of $|K|$.

In trapezoid $A B C D$, $A B / / C D, A B=3 C D, E$ is the midpoint of diagonal $A C$, and line $B E$ intersects $A D$ at $F$. Then the value of $A F: F D$ is
(A)2.
(B) $\frac{5}{3}$.
(C) $\frac{3}{2}$.
(D) 1 .

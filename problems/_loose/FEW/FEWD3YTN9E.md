[10] Behind each of two round tables sit $n$ gnomes. Each gnome is friends only with their neighbors to the left and right at their table. A kind wizard wants to seat the gnomes at one round table so that every two neighboring gnomes are friends. He has the ability to make $2 n$ pairs of gnomes friends (the gnomes in a pair can be from the same table or different tables), but after that, an evil wizard will make $n$ pairs of these $2 n$ pairs enemies. For which $n$ can the kind wizard achieve his goal, no matter how the evil wizard acts?

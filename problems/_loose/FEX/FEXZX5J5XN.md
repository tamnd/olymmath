For every real number $m$ and every real number $n$, the function $f$ is defined by $y=f(x)=m x+n$ ( $x$ real), whose graph is a line $g$.

a) Let $m=\frac{1}{2}$ and $n$ be any real number.

Determine the coordinates of all points on $g$ whose ordinate is twice as large as their abscissa!

b) Let $m$ and $n$ be any real numbers.

Determine the coordinates of all points on $g$ whose ordinate is twice as large as their abscissa! (In particular, determine for which $m$ and $n$ such a point exists on $g$!)

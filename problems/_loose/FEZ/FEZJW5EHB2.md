Given a positive integer $n$. Consider all representations of $n$ as a sum of exactly two different positive integer summands. Representations that differ only in the order of the summands, such as $9=4+5$ and $9=5+4$, should be considered the same. Determine

a) for $n=7$,

b) for $n=10$,

c) for any (positive integer) $n$ the number of all such representations!

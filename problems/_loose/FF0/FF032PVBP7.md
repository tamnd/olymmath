A youth radio station, to attract listeners, holds prize and super-prize draws among them. Prizes are drawn over sixteen hours (one prize per hour), and super-prizes are drawn during four evening hours (also one super-prize per hour). The probability that listeners will win a prize is 0.3, and a super-prize is 0.02.

Find the probability that over 30 days

a) listeners will win three super-prizes;

b) listeners will win between 130 and 160 prizes.

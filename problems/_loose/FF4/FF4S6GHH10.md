Let $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ be nonnegative real numbers of sum equal to $1$.
Let $F_n=x_1^{2}+x_2^{2}+\cdots +x_n^{2}-2(x_1x_2+x_2x_3+\cdots +x_nx_1)$.
Find:
a) $\min F_3$;
b) $\min F_4$;
c) $\min F_5$.

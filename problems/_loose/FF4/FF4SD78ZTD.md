Prove: If in $A B C D$ of Figure 4.7, $A B=a, B C=b, C D$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
=c, D A=d, \angle B A C=\alpha_{1}\frac{\pi}{2}, x_{1}, x_{2}>0, \\
\frac{\cos \alpha_{1}}{\cos \left(\pi-\alpha_{2}\right)}=\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}, \text{ then } \\
\frac{a}{x_{1}}+\frac{d}{x_{2}}<\frac{b}{x_{1}}+\frac{c}{x_{2}} .
\end{array}
$$

A problem inspired by A.D. Sakharov's problem (7-11). Vasya, in a fit of rage, is cutting a rectangular sheet of paper with scissors. Every second, he cuts the first piece he comes across with a random straight cut into two parts.

a) (1 pt.) Find the expected number of sides of the polygon that will randomly fall into Vasya's hands after an hour of such work.

b) (2 pts.) Solve the same problem if the sheet of paper initially had the shape of an arbitrary polygon.

Note. This problem, in a different formulation and in a more general form, was conceived and solved by Academician Andrei Dmitrievich Sakharov. Sakharov wrote that the problem arose when he was chopping cabbage.

Given a triangle $K L M$ with base $K M$, equal to $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and side $K L$, equal to 1. A circle is drawn through points $K$ and $L$, with its center lying on the height $L F$, dropped to the base $K M$. It is known that $F M=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$, and point $F$ lies on $K M$. Find the area of the circle bounded by this circle.

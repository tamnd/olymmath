a.) Prove that for every natural number $n$, there exist natural numbers $A_{n}, B_{n}$ such that

$$
(3-\sqrt{7})^{n}=A_{n}-B_{n} \sqrt{7}
$$

b.) Since the value on the left side is small, the fraction $A_{n} / B_{n}$ provides a good approximation of $\sqrt{7}$. Choose the exponent $n$ such that the fraction approximates $\sqrt{7}$ with an error less than $10^{-4}$.

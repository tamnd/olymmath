The integers $1,2, \ldots, 7$ are written on the board. At each step, we are allowed to erase two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the board and, in their place, write $\frac{2 a+b}{3}$ and $\frac{a+2 b}{3}$.

1. After a finite number of steps, can we obtain a board on which the number 3 appears 7 times?
2. After a finite number of steps, can we obtain a board on which the number 4 appears 7 times?

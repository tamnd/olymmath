Let triangle $A B C$ be right-angled at $C$. Let $C D = h_{c}$ be the altitude to the hypotenuse; further, let $\rho$ be the radius of the incircle of triangle $A B C$, $\rho_{1}$ and $\rho_{2}$ the radii of the incircles of the sub-triangles $A D C$ and $B D C$.

Prove that the sum $\sigma$ of the inradii $\rho, \rho_{1}$, and $\rho_{2}$ is equal to the altitude $h_{c}$!

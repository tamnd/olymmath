We drew a circle around the origin of the coordinate system, with a radius of $r=\sqrt{1000}$ units. How many lattice points are inside and on the circumference of this circle? (A lattice point is a point whose both coordinates are integers.) How many lattice points will be on the circle and inside it if the value of $r^{2}$ is taken as the following consecutive numbers: 999, 998, 997, 996, 995; 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004, 1005?

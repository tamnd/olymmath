Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $A'$ the foot of the bisector from $A$. Consider a point $X$ on $[A' A]$, and set: $B'=(B X) \cap (A C)$, $C'=(C X) \cap (A B)$, $P=(A' B') \cap (C X)$, and $Q=(A' C') \cap (B X)$. Show that $\widehat{P A C}=\widehat{Q A B}$.

Let's prove that for every acute-angled triangle with sides $a, b, c$,

$$
\frac{1}{s_{a}^{2}-\frac{a^{2}}{4}}+\frac{1}{s_{b}^{2}-\frac{b^{2}}{4}}+\frac{1}{s_{c}^{2}-\frac{c^{2}}{4}} \geq \frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2 t}
$$

where $t$ is the area of the triangle and, for example, $s_{a}$ is the median to side $a$.

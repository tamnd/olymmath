We know that the most common dice currently are six-sided dice, which are cubes with one to six holes (or numbers) on each face, and the sum of the numbers on opposite faces is seven. Obviously, rolling a six-sided die once can only produce one of six numbers (the one on top). Now, you are asked to design a "decimal die" that can produce one of the ten numbers $0 \sim 9$ when rolled, and each number has an equal probability of being produced. Can you design such a die? If yes, please write down your design; if not, provide the reason.

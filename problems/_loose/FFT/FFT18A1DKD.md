Suppose in the Cartesian coordinate plane, $P(x, y)$ and $P^{\prime}\left(x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}\right)$ correspond to each other, satisfying $\left\{\begin{array}{l}x^{\prime}=a x-b y+p, \\ y^{\prime}=b x+a y+q,\end{array}\right.$ where $a, b, p, q$ are constants, and $b \neq 0$. Given two sets of corresponding points $P_{1}\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right), P_{1}^{\prime}\left(x_{1}^{\prime}, y_{1}^{\prime}\right)$ and $P_{2}\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right), P_{2}^{\prime}\left(x_{2}^{\prime}, y_{2}^{\prime}\right)$. Try to answer:
(1) What is the relationship between $\left|P_{1} P_{2}\right|$ and $\left|P_{1}^{\prime} P_{2}^{\prime}\right|$?
(2) If point $Q$ corresponds to itself, find the coordinates of $Q$;
(3) What is the relationship between $\triangle Q P_{1} P_{2}$ and $\triangle Q P_{1}^{\prime} P_{2}^{\prime}$? And find their area ratio.

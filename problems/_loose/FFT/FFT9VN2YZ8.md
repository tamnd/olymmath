Given that $\triangle A B C$ is a non-isosceles acute triangle, the projection of point $A$ on its Euler line (the line passing through the circumcenter and orthocenter) is $D$. The circle $\Gamma$ with center $S$ passing through points $A$ and $D$ intersects $A B$ and $A C$ at points $X$ and $Y$, respectively. If the projection of point $A$ on $B C$ is $P$, and the midpoint of $B C$ is $M$, prove that the circumcenter of $\triangle X S Y$ is equidistant from points $P$ and $M$.

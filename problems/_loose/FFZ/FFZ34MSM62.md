There are stones in boxes. In one move, a number $k$ is chosen, then the stones in the boxes are divided into groups of $k$ and the remainder of less than $k$ stones. One stone is left from each group and the entire remainder. Can it be achieved in 5 moves that there is exactly one stone left in each box, if each of them contains a) no more than 460 stones; b) no more than 461 stones?

Let $\Omega$ be a circle with a chord $A B$ which is not a diameter. Let $\Gamma_{1}$ be a circle on one side of $A B$ such that it is tangent to $A B$ at $C$ and internally tangent to $\Omega$ at $D$. Likewise, let $\Gamma_{2}$ be a circle on the other side of $A B$ such that it is tangent to $A B$ at $E$ and internally tangent to $\Omega$ at $F$. Suppose the line $D C$ intersects $\Omega$ at $X \neq D$ and the line $F E$ intersects $\Omega$ at $Y \neq F$. Prove that $X Y$ is a diameter of $\Omega$.

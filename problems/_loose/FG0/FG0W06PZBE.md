The 46th question: Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n \in Z^{+}}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers, and there exists a constant $M$ such that for any positive integer $n$, $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}^{2}<M a_{n+1}^{2}\left(n \in Z^{+}\right)$. Prove: There exists a constant $C$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}<C a_{n+1}$ always holds.

Two circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ intersect in $A$ and $B$. Let $r$ be a straight line through $B$ that intersects $k_{1}$ in $C$ and $k_{2}$ in $D$ so that B lies between $C$ and $D$. Let $s$ be the line parallel to $A D$ that touches $k_{1}$ in $E$ and has the smallest possible distance to $A D$. The straight line $A E$ intersects $k_{2}$ in $F$. Let $t$ be the tangent to $k_{2}$ through $F$. Prove that holds:

(a) The straight line $t$ is parallel to $A C$.

(b) The lines $r, s$ and $t$ intersect at a point.

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be real numbers, and let

$$
f(x)=\cos a_{1} x+\cos a_{2} x+\cdots+\cos a_{n} x
$$

Prove that there exists a positive integer $k \leq 2 n$ such that

$$
|f(k)| \geq \frac{1}{2}
$$

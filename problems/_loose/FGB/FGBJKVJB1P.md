We will prove that if we multiply any number of elements from the sequence

$$
1 \cdot 2+3, 4 \cdot 5+6, \ldots,(3 k-2)(3 k-1)+3 k, \ldots
$$

the product can always be expressed as the sum of two squares of integers.

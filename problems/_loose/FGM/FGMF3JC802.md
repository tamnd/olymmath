Let $A B C$ be a triangle. A circle is tangent to segments $B C, C A, A B$ at points $D, E, F$, respectively. Given that the measures of $\angle C A B, \angle A B C, \angle B C A$ form an arithmetic progression in some order, prove that the measures of $\angle F D E, \angle D E F, \angle E F D$ also form an arithmetic progression in some order.

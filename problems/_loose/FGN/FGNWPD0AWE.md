Give an infinite sequence
$$
a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, \cdots
$$

where each term is obtained by appending a non-9 digit to the end of the previous number. It is known that $a_{1}$ is any ten-digit number. Prove that the sequence contains at least two composite numbers.
(35th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1972)

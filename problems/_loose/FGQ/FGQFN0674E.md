Suppose that for the nonnegative numbers $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}$, the inequality $\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{j} \leq \pi$ holds if $i \neq j$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \cos \alpha_{i} \leq \cos \left(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \alpha_{i}\right)
$$

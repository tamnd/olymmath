In a plane, there are triangles $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$, $B_{1} B_{2} B_{3}$, and $C_{1} C_{2} C_{3}$. Find a point $P$ in their plane such that

$$
{\overline{P A_{1}}}^{2}+{\overline{P A_{2}}}^{2}+{\overline{P A_{3}}}^{2}={\overline{P B_{1}}}^{2}+{\overline{P B_{2}}}^{2}+{\overline{P B_{3}}}^{2}={\overline{P C_{1}}}^{2}+{\overline{P C_{2}}}^{2}+{\overline{P C_{3}}}^{2}
$$

holds.

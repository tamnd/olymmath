Let $(A,+,\cdot)$ a 9 elements ring. Prove that the following assertions are equivalent:

(a) For any $x\in A\backslash\{0\}$ there are two numbers $a\in \{-1,0,1\}$ and $b\in \{-1,1\}$ such that $x^2+ax+b=0$.

(b) $(A,+,\cdot)$ is a field.

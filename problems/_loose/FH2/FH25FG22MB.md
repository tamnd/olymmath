Let $A B C D E F G H$ be a cube with $A E\|B F\| C G \| D H$; here, $E F G H$ is a side face of the cube; the intersection point of its diagonals $E G$ and $F H$ is $S$.

a) Prove that the angle $\angle E S A$ is not a right angle!

b) Prove that the angle $\angle D S E$ is a right angle!

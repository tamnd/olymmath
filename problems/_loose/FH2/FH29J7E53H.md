(USA, 80). Prove that for any numbers $a, b, c \in [0; 1]$ the inequality

$$
\frac{a}{b+c+1}+\frac{b}{c+a+1}+\frac{c}{a+b+1}+(1-a)(1-b)(1-c) \leqslant 1
$$

holds.

Let the points $A_{0}, A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ be situated in this order on a line d such that $\mathrm{A}_{0} \mathrm{~A}_{1}=1 \mathrm{~cm}, \mathrm{~A}_{1} \mathrm{~A}_{2}=2 \mathrm{~cm}$, $\mathrm{A}_{2} \mathrm{~A}_{3}=2^{2} \mathrm{~cm}, \ldots, \mathrm{A}_{\mathrm{n}-1} \mathrm{~A}_{\mathrm{n}}=2^{\mathrm{n}-1} \mathrm{~cm}$.

a) Determine the natural number $\mathrm{p}$ such that $\mathrm{A}_{0} \mathrm{~A}_{\mathrm{p}}=2047 \mathrm{~cm}$.

b) If $\mathrm{M}$ is the midpoint of the segment $\mathrm{A}_{2} \mathrm{~A}_{12}$ and $\mathrm{N}$ is the midpoint of the segment $\mathrm{A}_{4} \mathrm{~A}_{10}$, determine the length of the segment MN.

Let $n>m$ be integers. Find all sequences of $m$ integers $1 \leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{m} \leq n$ such that
$$
i_{k+1}-i_{k} \geq 2 \text{for all } 1 \leq k < m.
$$

In a complete quadrilateral $A B C D E F$, vertices $A, B, D, F$ are concyclic with circle $O$, and the diagonals $A D$ and $B F$ intersect at point $G$.
(1) If the angle bisectors of $\angle C$ and $\angle E$ intersect at point $K$, then $C K \perp E K$.
(2) The angle bisector of $\angle B G D$ is parallel to $C K$, and the angle bisector of $\angle D G F$ is parallel to $E K$.
(3) If tangents are drawn from $C$ and $E$ to circle $O$, with the points of tangency being $P$ and $Q$ respectively, then $C E^{2}=C P^{2}+E Q^{2}$; under this condition, the Miquel point of the complete quadrilateral $A B C D E F$ lies on the diagonal $C E$; if arcs are drawn with $C$ and $E$ as centers and $C P$ and $E Q$ as radii, intersecting at point $T$, then $C T \perp E T$.
(4) If tangents are drawn from $C$ (or $E$) to circle $O$, with the points of tangency being $R$ and $Q$, then $E$ (or $C$), $R, G, Q$ are concyclic.
(5) The lines passing through any two of the points $C, E, G$ are the polars of the third point with respect to circle $O$.
(6) Point $O$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle G C E$.
(7) The intersection of the tangents to circle $O$ at the endpoints of diagonal $B F$ (or $A D$ when $B F \perp C E$) lies on the line containing diagonal $C E$.
(8) Let $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ be the circumcenters of $\triangle A C F$ and $\triangle A B E$ respectively, then $\triangle O O_{1} O_{2} \sim \triangle D C E$.
(9) Let point $M$ be the Miquel point of the complete quadrilateral $A B C D E F$, then $O M \perp C E$, and $O, G, M$ are collinear, $O M$ bisects $\angle A M D$, and $O M$ bisects $\angle B M F$.
(10) If a secant through point $E$ (or $C$) intersects circle $O$ at $R$ and $P$, and line $P C$ (or $P E$) intersects circle $O$ at point $S$, then $R, G, S$ are collinear.
(11) If the extension of diagonal $A D$ intersects diagonal $C E$ at $W$, then $W C=W E$ if and only if $W A \cdot W D=W C^{2}$.
(12) If the midpoint of diagonal $C E$ is $Z$, and $A Z$ intersects circle $O$ at $N$, then $C, D, N, E$ are concyclic.

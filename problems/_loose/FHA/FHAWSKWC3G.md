20 varieties of pastries, with 10 pastries of each variety, are packed in some way into 20 boxes (10 pastries in each box). Prove that regardless of the distribution of pastries in the boxes, it is always possible to choose 20 pastries of twenty different varieties, selecting one pastry from each box.

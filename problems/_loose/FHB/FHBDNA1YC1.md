Let $A B C D T$ be a quadrilateral pyramid with base $A B C D$ (where $A$ and $C$ are not adjacent vertices) whose diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ are mutually perpendicular and intersect at the point $O$, which is the foot of the altitude of the pyramid from the vertex $T$. Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ be the angles between the altitude of the pyramid and the altitudes dropped from the vertex $T$ to the lateral faces $A B T, B C T, C D T$ and $D A T$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\operatorname{ctg}^{2} \alpha+\operatorname{ctg}^{2} \gamma=\operatorname{ctg}^{2} \beta+\operatorname{ctg}^{2} \delta
$$

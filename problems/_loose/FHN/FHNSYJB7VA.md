For example, if $2 n$ (>3) table tennis players have played several singles matches, and the opponents of any two players are exactly not completely the same. Try to prove: it is always possible to remove 1 player so that among the remaining players, the opponents of any two players are still not completely the same.
(1987 National High School League Question)

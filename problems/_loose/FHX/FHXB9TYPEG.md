Four points on a plane, if the areas of the triangles formed by any three of these points are four distinct positive integers, and the distances between any two points are six distinct positive integers, then the convex hull of these four points is called a "Lotus Diagram".
(1) Provide a specific example of a Lotus Diagram, along with the areas of the four triangles and the distances between each pair of points;
(2) Prove that there exist infinitely many non-similar Lotus Diagrams on the plane.
(13th China Southeast Mathematical Olympiad)

In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, $A D=D C=C B=x, A C=B D$ $=A B=y$. Then the value of $\frac{x}{y}$ is
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$.
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{3}-1}{2}$.
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2}$.
(Anhui Province, Anqing and other counties and cities Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

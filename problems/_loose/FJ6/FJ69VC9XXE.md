Form new numbers from the $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{2 n+1}$ positive numbers in the following way. Take out $(n+1)$ of them in every possible way and divide their product by the product of the $n$ remaining ones. Prove that the arithmetic mean of the new numbers thus obtained cannot be less than the arithmetic mean of the original numbers.

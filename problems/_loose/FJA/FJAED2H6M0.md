In $\triangle A B C$, for a real number $\lambda$ there is $\cos ^{2} A+\lambda(\sin 2 B+\sin 2 C) \leqslant 1+\lambda^{2}, \sin ^{2} A+\lambda(\cos 2 B$ $+\cos 2 C) \leqslant 1+\lambda^{2}$; equality holds if and only if $0<\lambda \leqslant 1$ and $B=C=\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{2} \arcsin \lambda$.

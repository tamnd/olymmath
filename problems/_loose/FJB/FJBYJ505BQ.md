Given a right triangle $A B C$, where angle $A$ is 60 degrees, and the hypotenuse $A B$ is $4+4 \sqrt{3}$. Through vertex $B$, a line $p$ parallel to $A C$ is drawn. Points $D$ and $E$ are placed on line $p$ such that $A B=B D, B C=B E$. $F$ is the intersection point of lines $A D$ and $C E$. Find what the perimeter of the triangle can be

$$
\begin{gathered}
4 \sqrt{2}+2 \sqrt{6}+18+10 \sqrt{3} \\
2 \sqrt{3}+2+2 \sqrt{2} \\
18+10 \sqrt{3}+4 \sqrt{6}+6 \sqrt{2} \\
2+2 \sqrt{3}+2 \sqrt{6}
\end{gathered}
$$

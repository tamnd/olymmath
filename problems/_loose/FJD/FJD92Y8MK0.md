Let $A,B\in \mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ such that $AB=BA$ and $\det B\neq 0$ .

a) If $|\det(A+zB)|=1$ for any $z\in \mathbb{C}$ such that $|z|=1$ , then $A^n=O_n$ .
b) Is the question from a) still true if $AB\neq BA$ ?

For a right-angled triangle with legs $a, b$, and hypotenuse $c$, according to higher mathematical calculations, the ratio $3 a /(2 c+b)$ approximately gives the radian measure of the angle opposite to $a$. Determine the accuracy of this approximation if the lengths of the legs are:

$$
\text { I. } 11 \text { and } 60, \text { II. } 51 \text { and } 140, \text { III. } 1 \text { and } \sqrt{3} \text {, }
$$

IV. 88 and $105, $ V. 1 and 1.

Show that the following construction method is based on the above relationship. Let $O$ be the center of a circle with radius $r$, and let $t$ be the tangent to the circle at point $A$, one of the endpoints of the arc $AB$. On the ray $AO$, let $D$ be the point that is $3r$ away from $A$, and let the line $DB$ intersect the tangent $t$ at point $E$. Then the segment $AE$ is approximately equal in length to the arc $AB$.

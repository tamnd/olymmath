A person has 12 pints of wine in a barrel (pint - an old French unit of volume, 1 pint ≈ 0.568 liters) and wants to give away half of the wine, but does not have a 6-pint container. However, there are two empty containers with capacities of 8 pints and 5 pints. How can one use them to measure out exactly 6 pints of wine?

This is the most famous problem of its kind; it is known as Poisson's problem. The famous French mathematician, mechanician, and physicist Siméon Denis Poisson (1781-1840) solved it in his youth and later said that it was precisely this problem that inspired him to become a mathematician.

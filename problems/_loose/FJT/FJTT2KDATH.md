Given a positive real number $\lambda$. Prove: there exist infinitely many positive real numbers
$$
a \notin \underset{m, n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}}{\cup}\left[\frac{m^{2}}{n^{2}}, \frac{m^{2}+\lambda}{n^{2}}\right] .
$$

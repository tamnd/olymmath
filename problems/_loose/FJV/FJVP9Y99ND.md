There are 2025 lightbulbs on a circle, initially turned off. Lorenzo and Mateo, take turns playing a game, starting with Lorenzo. On their turn, the player chooses a lightbulb that has not already been switched on, switches it on and turns off its two neighbors. The game ends when every lightbulb has already been switched on at some point. Determine the greatest amount of lightbulbs that Lorenzo can guarantee will be on at the end of the game, regardless of how Mateo plays.

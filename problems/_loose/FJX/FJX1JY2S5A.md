The edge of a regular tetrahedron $ABCD$ is equal to $a$. The vertices of the spatial quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ lie on the corresponding faces of the tetrahedron ($A_{1}$ - on the face opposite to $A$, and so on), and its sides are perpendicular to the faces of the tetrahedron: $A_{1} B_{1} \perp BCD, B_{1} C_{1} \perp CDA, C_{1} D_{1} \perp DAB$ and $D_{1} A_{1} \perp ABC$. Calculate the lengths of the sides of the quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$.

The numbers are given:

$S_{1}=1+2+3+4+\cdots+(n-1)+n$ and

$S_{2}=1-2+3-4+\cdots-(n-1)+n$, where $n$ is an odd natural number.

a) Determine the number $n$ knowing that $S_{1}=2015 \cdot S_{2}$;

b) What is the smallest non-zero natural number by which $S_{1}+S_{2}$ must be multiplied so that a perfect square is obtained;

c) Find the natural numbers $n$ and $m$, with $n$ odd, such that $S_{1}-S_{2}=2^{m}$.

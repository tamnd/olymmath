In a regular decagon, all diagonals are drawn. The number +1 is placed at each vertex and at each point of intersection of the diagonals (only the diagonals themselves, not their extensions are considered). It is allowed to simultaneously change the signs of all numbers located on one side or one diagonal. Is it possible to change all the signs to the opposite using several such operations?

Let $S$ be a set of integers which has the following properties:
1) There exists $x,y\in S$ such that $(x,y)=(x-2,y-2)=1$;
2) For $\forall$ $x,y\in S, x^2-y\in S$.
Prove that $S\equiv \mathbb{Z}$ .

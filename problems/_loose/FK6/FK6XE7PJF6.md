Thus, we have divided the triangle $A B C$ into 4 right-angled triangles. Show that the lines determined by pairs of points among the centers of the circles inscribed in these triangles, which lie on the same side of the altitude $A A_{1}$, are the legs of an isosceles right-angled triangle whose hypotenuse lies on $B C$.

Given a natural number $k$ . In a country whose currency is tugriks, only banknotes of two integer denominations are used. Both the buyer and the seller have quite a lot of both bills, but they can use no more than $k$ bills together (including change) for each payment. It is known that this way you can make a payment for any integer amount from $1$ to $n$ tugriks. What is the largest possible value of $n$ ?
*A. Shapovalov*

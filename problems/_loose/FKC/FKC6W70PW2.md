The First Ace. In a certain card game, four players participate. To determine who starts the game, they usually draw lots "until the first ace." There are 32 cards in the deck, from sevens to aces. One of the players deals the cards face up to each player in turn, clockwise (to himself - last), until someone gets an ace. This player will start the game.

a) (8th grade. 3 points) For each player, find the probability that they will get the first ace.

b) (6th grade. 1 point). How can the rules be changed to make the draw fair?

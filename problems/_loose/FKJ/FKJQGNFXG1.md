Given the function $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}-x$. If for any $x \in(0, k)$, we have $f(x) \cdot f(k-x) \geqslant \left(\frac{k}{2}-\frac{2}{k}\right)^{2}$ always holds. Find the range of positive real number $k$.

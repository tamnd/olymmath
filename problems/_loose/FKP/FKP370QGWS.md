Several balls are divided into $n$ piles. Now they are recombined into $n+k$ piles, where $n, k$ are given positive integers, and the number of balls in each pile is at least 1.
Prove: There exist $k+1$ balls, the number of balls in the piles they originally belonged to is greater than the number of balls in the piles they are in now.

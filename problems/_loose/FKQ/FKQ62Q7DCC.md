Given that there are $2 n+3(n \geqslant 1)$ points on a plane, where no three points are collinear and no four points are concyclic, can a circle be drawn through three of these points such that the remaining $2 n$ points are equally divided, with half inside the circle and half outside? Prove your conclusion.

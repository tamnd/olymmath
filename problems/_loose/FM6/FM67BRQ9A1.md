Let $\left(u_{n}\right)$ and $\left(v_{n}\right)$ be two sequences defined by $u_{0}=6$ and $v_{0}=4$ and

$\forall n \in \mathbb{N}, u_{n+1}=\frac{3}{5} u_{n}-\frac{4}{5} v_{n}$ and $v_{n+1}=\frac{4}{5} u_{n}+\frac{3}{5} v_{n}$. Does there exist $k$ in $\mathbb{N}$ such that $u_{k}=7$ and $v_{k}=2$?

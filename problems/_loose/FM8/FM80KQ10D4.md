Given 100 numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{100}$, their sum equals 1, and the absolute value of the difference between any two adjacent numbers is less than $\frac{1}{50}$. Prove that it is possible to select 50 numbers from them, such that the sum of these 50 numbers differs from $\frac{1}{2}$ by an absolute value less than $\frac{1}{100}$.

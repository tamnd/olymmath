(1) Prove that the area $S$ of $\triangle A O B$ is a constant;
(2) Suppose the line $l: y=-2 x+4$ intersects the circle $C$ at two distinct points $M$ and $N$, and $|O M|=|O N|$, find the standard equation of the circle $C$.

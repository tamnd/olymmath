Given that circle $S_{1}$ intersects circle $S_{2}$ at points $P$ and $Q$, $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ are two points on circle $S_{1}$ different from $P$ and $Q$, lines $A_{1} P$ and $B_{1} P$ intersect circle $S_{2}$ at $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$, and lines $A_{1} B_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{2}$ intersect at point $C$. Prove that as points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ vary, the circumcenter of $\triangle A_{1} A_{2} C$ always lies on a fixed circle.
(IMO-43 Shortlist, 2003 National Team Training Test)

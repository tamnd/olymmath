Let $x, y, z \in(0, \infty)$ with the property $x y, x z, y z \leq 1$.

a) Determine $m \in(0, \infty)$ maximum such that $\frac{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+y^{2}\right)}{2+x^{2}+y^{2}} \geq m(1+x y)$, for any $x, y \in(0, \infty)$ with the property $x y \leq 1$;

b) Prove the inequality $\frac{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+y^{2}\right)}{2+x^{2}+y^{2}}+\frac{\left(1+x^{2}\right)\left(1+z^{2}\right)}{2+x^{2}+z^{2}}+\frac{\left(1+y^{2}\right)\left(1+z^{2}\right)}{2+y^{2}+z^{2}} \geq \frac{3+x y+x z+y z}{2}$.

(Gazeta Matematică - 9/2013)

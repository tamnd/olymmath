Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers such that $x y z=1$. Prove that

$$
\frac{x^{3}+y^{3}}{x^{2}+x y+y^{2}}+\frac{y^{3}+z^{3}}{y^{2}+y z+z^{2}}+\frac{z^{3}+x^{3}}{z^{2}+z x+x^{2}} \geq 2
$$

Determine all integers $n>3$ such that there are $n$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ in the plane that satisfy the following two conditions simultaneously:
(a) No three lie on the same line.
(b) There exist real numbers $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{n}$ such that the area of $\triangle A_{i} A_{j} A_{k}$ is equal to $p_{i}+p_{j}+p_{k}$, for $1 \leq i<j<k \leq n$.

The base $AD$ of parallelogram $ABCD$ is divided by points $M_{1}, M_{2}, \ldots, M_{6}$ into seven equal parts. The lines $B M_{1}, B M_{2}, \ldots B M_{6}$ intersect the diagonal $AC$ at points $N_{1}, N_{2}, \ldots, N_{6}$ respectively. Find the length of the fourth segment from vertex $A$ in the division of the diagonal by these points, if the length of the diagonal is 110.

If two people, A and B, run on a circular track at their respective constant speeds, and if they start from the same point and run in opposite directions, B takes another 8 minutes to reach the starting point after their first meeting. It is known that A takes 6 minutes to complete one lap. Therefore, how long does B take to complete one lap?
(A) 11 minutes.
(B) 12 minutes.
(C) 14 minutes.
(D) 16 minutes.
("Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1989)

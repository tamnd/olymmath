For each pair of different natural numbers a and b, not greater than 20, Petya drew on the board the line $y=a x+b$ (that is, he drew the lines $y=x+2, \ldots, y=x+20, y=2 x+1, y=2 x+3, \ldots$, $y=2 x+20, \ldots, y=3 x+1, y=3 x+2, y=3 x+4, \ldots, y=3 x+20, \ldots, y=20 x+1, \ldots, y=20 x+19)$. Vasya drew on the same board a circle of radius 1 centered at the origin. How many of Petya's lines intersect Vasya's circle? (I. Rubanov)

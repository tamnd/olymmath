Given
(1) a polynomial $P(x)$ with natural number coefficients,
(2) a polynomial $P(x)$ with integer coefficients.
Let $a_{n}$ denote the sum of the digits of $P(n)$ in decimal notation.
Prove that there exists a number that appears infinitely many times in the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}^{\prime}, \cdots$.
(9th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1975)

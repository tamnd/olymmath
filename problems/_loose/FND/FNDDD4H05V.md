In a football tournament, exactly 14 teams participate. Each team plays exactly one match against every other team. If a team wins, it receives 2 win points and its opponent receives 2 loss points. If a match ends in a draw, each of the two teams receives one win point and one loss point.

a) After all matches are completed, the sum of all points that each team has received, regardless of whether they are win or loss points, can be calculated for each team.

Prove that each of the 14 teams will receive the same sum of points, and state this sum!

b) After all matches are completed, the sum of all win points can also be calculated, regardless of which teams received them.

Prove that for every possible outcome of the individual matches in the tournament, the same sum of all win points will result, and state this sum!

c) In another tournament with the same point allocation rules, a different number of teams participated. Again, each team played exactly one match against every other team.

Can the sum of all win points, as calculated in b), be 432? Justify your answer!

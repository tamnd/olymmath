Prove the following theorems:

a) If $S$ is the intersection of the three medians of a triangle $A B C$, then the triangles $A B S, B C S$, and $C A S$ have the same area.

b) If $S$ is a point inside a triangle $A B C$ such that the triangles $A B S, B C S$, and $C A S$ have the same area, then $S$ is the intersection of the medians of the triangle $A B C$.

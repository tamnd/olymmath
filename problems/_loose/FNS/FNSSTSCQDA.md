In a certain kingdom, there are $k$ major cities and several towns, all of which are connected by roads, and it is possible to travel from any city to any other city. Prove that this kingdom can be divided into $k$ republics, such that each republic has one of the major cities as its capital; and for any city, the road to the capital of its republic is the shortest among the roads to the $k$ major cities (a road consisting of the fewest roads is called the shortest road).

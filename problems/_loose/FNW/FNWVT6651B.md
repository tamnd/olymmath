The English geometer $M$. Stewart (1717-1785) obtained an interesting theorem:

If $C_{1}$ is any point on the side $A B$ of triangle $A B C$, then

$$
C A^{2} \cdot C_{1} B + C B^{2} \cdot C_{1} A - C C_{1}^{2} \cdot A B = C_{1} A \cdot C_{1} B \cdot A B
$$

Prove Stewart's theorem. (U. 123, P. 181.)

A $2024 \times 2024$ board is given. A post office is found in the bottom left unit square of the board, whereas there is a house that is waiting for an order on every other unit square. The postman travels in this way: he chooses a house as a destination and travels from the postal office to the destination according to his wish while moving up or right on the board. On the way, he sends the order to all the houses on the unit squares on which he passes through. After arriving at the destination, he returns to the postal office through the same route. What is the smallest number of such travels that the postman needs so he could send the order to all the houses on the board?

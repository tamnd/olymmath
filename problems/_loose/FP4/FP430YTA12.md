a) Let $a>0$ and the sequences $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1},\left(y_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ with $x_{n}, y_{n} \in[0, a], \forall n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$. It is known that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n} y_{n}=a^{2}$. Show that the sequences $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ and $\left(y_{n}\right)_{n>1}$ are convergent.

b) If $x_{n}, y_{n} \in(-1,1), \forall n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ and $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n} y_{n}=1$, does it follow that the sequences $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ and $\left(y_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ are convergent?

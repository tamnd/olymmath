$$
\left\{\begin{array}{rl}
a_{1}+2 a_{2}+3 a_{3}+4 a_{4}+5 a_{5}+6 a_{6} & =26 \\
a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+a_{4}+a_{5}+a_{6} & =5
\end{array} .\right.
$$

If $c$ is the number of solutions to the system of equations, determine the value of $c$.

Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle $\Omega$ of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$. On the arc $AC$ of this circle, not containing point $B$, a point $P$ is taken. On the segment $BC$, a point $X$ is chosen such that $PX \perp AC$. Prove that the center of the circumcircle of triangle $BXP$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABO$. (I. Frolow)

Let $a, b, c$ be the sides and $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ the corresponding angles in triangle $ABC$ with area $P$. Prove that the following equality holds:

$$
a^{2}(\sin 2 \beta+\sin 2 \gamma)+b^{2}(\sin 2 \gamma+\sin 2 \alpha)+c^{2}(\sin 2 \alpha+\sin 2 \beta)=12 P
$$

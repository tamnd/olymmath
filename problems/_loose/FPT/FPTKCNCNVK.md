Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers with $a b c \geq 1$. Prove the inequality

$$
\frac{a^{4}-1}{a b^{3}+a b c+a c^{3}}+\frac{b^{4}-1}{b c^{3}+a b c+b a^{3}}+\frac{c^{4}-1}{c a^{3}+a b c+c b^{3}} \geq 0
$$

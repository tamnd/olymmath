A robot is placed at point $P$ on a number line, except for points $0$ and $1$, and the robot can only move left or right along the number line. Two players, A and B, play the following game: A gives a distance, and B gives a direction, and the robot will move the specified distance in the specified direction. A's goal is to move the robot to point 0 or 1, while B tries to prevent A from achieving this goal. Question: At which point $P$ does A have a winning strategy?

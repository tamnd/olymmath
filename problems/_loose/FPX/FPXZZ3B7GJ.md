Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A B C_{2}$, $B C A_{2}$, $C A B_{2}$, $A_{1} B_{1} C_{2}$, $B_{1} C_{1} A_{2}$, and $C_{1} A_{1} B_{2}$ intersect at one point.

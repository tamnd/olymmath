Let $F_{n}=F_{n}(t), t \in[0,1], n \geqslant 1$, be a non-decreasing function satisfying the condition $F_{n}(t) \rightarrow t$ for all rational $t \in Q \cap[0,1]$. Show that this convergence will be uniform:

$$
\sup _{t \in[0,1]}\left|F_{n}(t)-t\right| \rightarrow 0, n \rightarrow \infty
$$

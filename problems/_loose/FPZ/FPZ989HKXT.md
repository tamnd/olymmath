Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$; we draw an arbitrary secant through point $B$, which intersects one circle at $C$ and the other at $D$. Connect these points to $A$. If the centers of the circles tangent to the sides of triangle $C A D$ are denoted by $O, O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$, then the questions are:

1. What is the geometric locus of points $O, O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ as the secant $C B D$ rotates around $B$?
2. What is the geometric locus of point $G$, where the medians of triangle $C A D$ intersect?
3. Determine the position of the secant $C B D$ in the configuration $C_{1} B D_{1}$ for which the area of the triangle is maximal. Finally, find the geometric locus of point $P$, where the lines $C C_{1}$ and $D D_{1}$ intersect.

Determine all even natural numbers $z$ with the following properties:

(1) The number $z$ is five-digit, none of its five digits is a 0.

(2) The three-digit number formed by the first three digits of $z$ in this order is a square number.

(3) The three-digit number formed by the last three digits of $z$ in this order is a cube number.

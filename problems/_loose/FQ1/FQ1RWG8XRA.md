A store has 89 gold coins waiting to be sold, numbered from $1 \sim 89$, each priced at 30 yuan, and only one of them is a "lucky coin". Feifei can choose some of the numbers and ask the honest clerk: "Is the lucky coin's number among them?". If the answer is "yes", Feifei needs to pay 20 yuan for the consultation fee; if the answer is "no", Feifei needs to pay 10 yuan for the consultation fee. Of course, she can also choose not to ask any questions and directly buy some of the coins. Feifei needs to pay at least $ $ yuan to ensure she can definitely get the lucky coin.

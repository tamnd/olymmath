Prove by mathematical induction the following statements:
1) For any natural $n$

$1 \cdot 2+2 \cdot 3+3 \cdot 4+\ldots+n(n+1)=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{3}$.

2) For any natural $n$

$$
\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2}+\frac{1}{2 \cdot 3}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n(n+1)}=\frac{n}{n+1}
$$

3) For any natural $n$,

$$
|\sin n x| \leqslant n|\sin x| \text {. }
$$

4) For any natural $n$, $n^{3}+5 n$ is divisible by 6.

Given a triangle $A B C$. On the lines $A B, B C$ and $C A$, points $C_{1}, A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ are taken, such that $k$ of them lie on the sides of the triangle and $3-k$ on the extensions of the sides. Let

$$
R=\frac{B A_{1}}{C A_{1}} \cdot \frac{C B_{1}}{A B_{1}} \cdot \frac{A C_{1}}{B C_{1}}
$$

Prove that:

a) points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ lie on one straight line if and only if $R=1$ and $k$ is even (Menelaus);

b) the lines $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ intersect at one point or are parallel if and only if $R=1$ and $k$ is odd (Ceva).

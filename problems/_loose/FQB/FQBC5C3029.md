(Problem from the 49th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad in 1986): In a plane rectangular coordinate system, a circle with radius $\frac{1}{14}$ is drawn centered at each integer point. Prove that any circle with a radius of 100 will intersect at least one of these circles.
(1) From the lattice point $(a, b)$, it can move to the lattice point $(2a, b)$ or $(a, 2b)$;
(2) From the lattice point $(a, b)$, it can move to the lattice point $(a-b, b)$, where $a>b$;
(3) From the lattice point $(a, b)$, it can move to the lattice point $(a, b-a)$, where $b>a$.
Question: For which positive integers $x, y$, can it move to the corresponding grid $(x, y)$?

Let $n$ be a natural number with exactly $m$ digits in decimal notation $(m \geq 2)$, which is divisible by 11. By reversing the sequence of digits, the natural number $n^{\prime}$ (not necessarily with exactly $m$ digits) is formed. How many sums $n+n^{\prime}$ are divisible by 11?

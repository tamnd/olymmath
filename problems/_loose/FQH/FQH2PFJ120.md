Let's call a valuable digit of a number any digit that appears in its decimal representation no more times than each of the other digits (this can also be a digit that does not appear in the representation of $n$). Petya forms an infinite sequence of digits: at the $n$-th position, he places any of the valuable digits of the number $n$. Can Petya obtain a sequence that is periodic from some point?

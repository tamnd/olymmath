Given that $p$ is an odd prime, $m (m>1)$ and $n$ are positive integers, and satisfy $\frac{m^{p n}-1}{m^{n}-1}$ is a prime number. Prove:
$$
p n \mid\left((p-1)^{n}+1\right) \text {. }
$$

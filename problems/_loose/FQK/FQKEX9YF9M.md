The rules of the "Make 24" game are: from a deck of cards, remove the jokers leaving 52 cards (if you are a beginner, you can also use only the 40 cards from $1 \sim 10$), randomly draw 4 cards (called a card group), and use addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division (parentheses can be added) to make the numbers on the cards equal 24. Each card must be used once and only once, and you cannot combine several cards to form a multi-digit number. For example, if the drawn cards are $3, 8, 8, 9$, then the equation could be $(9-8) \times 8 \times 3$ or $(9-8 \div 8) \times 3$, etc. Among the following 4 options, the one that cannot make 24 is ( )
A. $1,2,3,3$
B. $1,5,5,5$
C. $2, 2, 2, 2$
D. $3, 3, 3, 3$

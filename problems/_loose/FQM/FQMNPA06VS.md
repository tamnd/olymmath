32 Suppose there are three different points on the parabola $y=x^{2}-2$: $P\left(a, a^{2}-2\right), Q\left(b, b^{2}-2\right)$, $R\left(c, c^{2}-2\right)$. Construct a circle centered at the origin $O$ with a radius of 1. The lines $P Q$ and $P R$ are tangent to $\odot O$. Try to express the coordinates of the midpoint of segment $Q R$ using $a$, and prove that the line $Q R$ is also tangent to $\odot O$.

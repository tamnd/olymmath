Let $ S$ be the set of natural numbers $ n$ satisfying the following conditions:

$ (i)$ $ n$ has $ 1000$ digits,
$ (ii)$ all the digits of $ n$ are odd, and
$ (iii)$ any two adjacent digits of $ n$ differ by $ 2$ .

Determine the number of elements of $ S$ .

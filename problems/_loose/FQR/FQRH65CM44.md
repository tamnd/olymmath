On an 8x8 chessboard, 8 rooks are arranged in such a way that none of them can attack each other. Prove that each of them can be simultaneously moved to one of the diagonally adjacent cells in such a way that none of them will be able to attack each other after the move. Recall that a chess rook attacks all cells in the row and column it occupies.

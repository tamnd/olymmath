The following sequence of numbers is given: $a_{1}=1+1^{-1}, a_{2}=1+2^{-1}, a_{3}=1+3^{-1}, \ldots, a_{2016}=1+2016^{-1}$.

a) Compare $a_{2016} \mathrm{with} a_{2011}$.

b) Show that the product $a_{1} \cdot a_{2} \cdot a_{3} \cdot \ldots \cdot a_{2016}$ is a natural number.

c) Determine the number $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, for which $a_{n} \cdot a_{n+1} \cdot a_{n+2} \in \mathbb{N}$.

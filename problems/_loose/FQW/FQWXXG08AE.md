In a game, three students, Anna, Brigitte, and Claudia, each hide an object in their handbags, namely a ball, a pencil, and a pair of scissors. Dieter is supposed to figure out who has the ball, who has the pencil, and who has the scissors.

In response to his questions, he receives the following answers, of which only one is true, while the other two are false:

(1) Anna has the ball.

(2) Brigitte does not have the ball.

(3) Claudia does not have the scissors.

Who has the ball, who has the pencil, and who has the scissors?

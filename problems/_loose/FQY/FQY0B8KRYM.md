Let $G$ be the centroid of $\triangle A B C$, and $P$ be any point on the plane, then we have $P A^{2}+P B^{2}+P C^{2}=$ $G A^{2}+G B^{2}+G C^{2}+3 P G^{2}=3 P G^{2}+\frac{1}{3}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$ (the latter formula is known as the Leibniz formula).

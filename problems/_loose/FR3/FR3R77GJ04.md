Let $x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers such that
$$
2^{x^2} = 16^y \text{and} \log_{\sqrt{2017}} x + \log_{\sqrt{2017}} y > 0.
$$
Prove that $y > \frac{1}{2}$. (Kristina Ana Škreb)

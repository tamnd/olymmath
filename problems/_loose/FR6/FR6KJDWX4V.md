Let $v_{1}, v_{2}, \cdots, v_{1989}$ be a set of coplanar vectors,
and $\left|v_{i}\right| \leqslant 1, i=1,2, \cdots, 1989$. Prove that there exist $\varepsilon_{i} \in\{-1,1\}, i=1,2$, $\cdots, 1989$, such that $\left|\sum_{i=1}^{1989} \varepsilon_{i} v_{i}\right| \leqslant \sqrt{3}$.

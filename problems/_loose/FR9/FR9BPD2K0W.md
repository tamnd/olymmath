Let $p$ and $q, p < q,$ be two primes such that $1 + p + p^2+...+p^m$ is a power of $q$ for some positive integer $m$ , and $1 + q + q^2+...+q^n$ is a power of $p$ for some positive integer $n$ . Show that $p = 2$ and $q = 2^t-1$ where $t$ is prime.

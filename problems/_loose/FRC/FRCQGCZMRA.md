Given $\triangle A B C$. On the arc $B C$ of the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, which does not contain point $A$, points $X$ and $Y$ are taken such that $\measuredangle B A X = \measuredangle C A Y$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the chord $A X$. Prove that $\overline{B M} + \overline{C M} > \overline{A Y}$.

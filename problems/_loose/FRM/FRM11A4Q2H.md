In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, $|A D|=|C D|$ and $\angle A D C=90^{\circ}$. If $|A B|=a$, $|B C|=b$, $|B D|=d$, and $\angle A B C=\beta$, prove that

$$
2 d^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}+2 a b \sin \beta
$$

Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer. Aline wishes to write $2n$ real numbers on the board such that

$\triangleright$ the $2n$ numbers written are not all equal,

$\triangleright$ if Aline circles $n$ numbers written on the board, no matter which $n$ numbers she chooses, the sum of the $n$ circled numbers is equal to the product of the $n$ numbers that are not circled.

For which values of $n$ can Aline fulfill her wish?

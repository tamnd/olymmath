Let $n$ be a given positive integer, and let $S$ be the area of the planar region: $\left\{z \left\lvert \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{|z-k|} \geqslant 1\right.\right\}$. Prove that:
$$
S \geqslant \frac{\pi\left(11 n^{2}+1\right)}{12} .
$$

On a plane, two convex polygons $P$ and $Q$ are drawn. For each side of polygon $P$, polygon $Q$ can be clamped between two lines parallel to this side. Let $h$ be the distance between these lines, and $l$ be the length of the side, and compute the product $l \cdot h$. Summing these products over all sides of $P$, we obtain a certain value $(P, Q)$. Prove that $(P, Q) = (Q, P)$.

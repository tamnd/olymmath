Given a frustum of a cone with the top base radius $r_{1}=5 \mathrm{~cm}$. The bottom base radius $r_{2}=10 \mathrm{~cm}$, and the slant height $A_{1} A_{2}=20 \mathrm{~cm}$. A rope is pulled from the midpoint of $A_{1} A_{2}$, wraps around the lateral surface of the frustum to a point $A_{2}$ on the bottom base circumference. Find:
(1) What is the minimum length of the rope?
(2) When the rope is at its minimum length, what is the minimum length of the line segment connecting a point on the rope to a point on the top base circumference?

Three flies are crawling along the sides of triangle $ABC$ in such a way that the centroid of the triangle they form remains in the same place. Prove that it coincides with the centroid of triangle $ABC$, given that one of the flies has crawled along the entire perimeter of the triangle. (The centroid of a triangle is the point of intersection of its medians.)

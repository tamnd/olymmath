Consider a triangle **ABC** and a line that does not coincide with the triangle sides and passes through the point **A**. This line meets altitudes $BH_2$ and $CH_3$ at the points $D_1$ and $E_1$, respectively. By $D_2$ and $E_2$ we denote points that are symmetrical to $D_1$ and $E_1$ with respect to the sides **AB** and **AC**, respectively. Prove that the circumcircle of the triangle $D_2AB$ is tangent to the circumcircle of the triangle $E_2AC$.

(Myhailo Plotnikov)

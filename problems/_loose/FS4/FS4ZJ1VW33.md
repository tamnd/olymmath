The river water is flowing, and at point $Q$ it flows into a still lake. A swimmer travels downstream from $P$ to $Q$, and then across the lake to $R$, taking a total of 3 hours. If he travels from $R$ to $Q$ and then to $P$, it takes a total of 6 hours. If the lake water also flows, with a speed equal to that of the river, then traveling from $P$ to $Q$ and then to $R$ would take $\frac{5}{2}$ hours. Under these conditions, how long would it take to travel from $R$ to $Q$ and then to $P$?

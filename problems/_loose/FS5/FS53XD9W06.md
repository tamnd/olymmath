At the vertices of a hexagon, the numbers $12,1,10,6,8,3$ (in that order) are written. In one move, it is allowed to choose two adjacent vertices and simultaneously add one to the numbers in these vertices or simultaneously subtract one from them. Can we end up with six numbers in the following order:

a) $14,6,13,4,5,2 ;$ b) $6,17,14,3,15,2 ?$

Let $\theta$ be an absolutely continuous random variable taking values in the interval $[0, \pi]$. Show that if

$$
\cos 2 \theta \stackrel{d}{=} \cos \theta,
$$

then $\theta$ has a uniform distribution on $[0, \pi]$.

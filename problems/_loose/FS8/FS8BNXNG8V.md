Let $p$ be an odd prime number, $a$ a number coprime with $p$, and $\pi_{a, p}: i \mapsto a i(\bmod p)$ a permutation of the residues modulo $p$. Prove that then $\operatorname{sgn} \pi_{a, p}=(a / p)$, where $\operatorname{sgn}=1$ for an even permutation and $\operatorname{sgn}=-1$ for an odd permutation (Zolotarev).

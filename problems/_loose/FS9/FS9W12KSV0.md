Let the sides of the self-intersecting quadrilaterals $K L M N$ and $K^{\prime} L^{\prime} M^{\prime} N^{\prime}$, inscribed in the same circle, intersect the chord $A B$ of this circle at points $P, Q, R, S$ and $P^{\prime}, Q^{\prime}, R^{\prime}, S^{\prime}$ respectively (side $K L$ at point $P$, side $L M$ at point $Q$, and so on). Prove that if three of the points $P, Q, R, S$ coincide with the corresponding three of the points $P^{\prime}, Q^{\prime}, R^{\prime}, S^{\prime}$, then the remaining two points also coincide. (It is assumed that the chord $A B$ does not pass through the vertices of the quadrilaterals.)

a) $\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{a} \geq 2, \forall a, b \in \mathbf{R}_{+}^{*}$
b) $\left(\frac{1}{x}-1\right) \cdot\left(\frac{1}{y}+1\right) \geq 4, $ where $ x, y \in \mathbf{R}^{*} $ and $ x=\frac{a}{a^{2}+a+1}$,

$$
y=\frac{a}{a^{2}-a+1}
$$

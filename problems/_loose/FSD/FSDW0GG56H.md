There is a circular cake with a radius of $10 \mathrm{~cm}$, and during baking, a pearl with a radius of $3 \mathrm{~mm}$ was placed inside. After baking, the goal is to find it. For this purpose, it is allowed to cut the cake into two pieces along a straight line (whether equal or not is not limited). If the knife does not cut the pearl, one of the pieces can be cut again; if the pearl is still not found, one of the three pieces obtained can be cut again; and so on. Try to prove that no matter how you cut, it is possible that after 32 cuts, the pearl has not yet been found. However, it is possible to cut 33 times appropriately so that no matter where the pearl is, it can be found.

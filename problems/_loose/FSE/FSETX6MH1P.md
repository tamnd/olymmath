If we connect the intersection point $A$ of two tangents of an ellipse with one of its foci $G$, then the line $A G$ bisects the angle $K G H$, where $K$ and $H$ are the points where the tangents touch the ellipse. Prove this theorem if the equation of the ellipse is $9 x^{2}+25 y^{2}=225$, and the coordinates of the points of tangency are: $x_{1}=3, y_{1}>0$ and $x_{2}=-4, y_{2}>0$.

For parallelogram $A B C D$, the circle passing through points $A$ and $D$ intersects $A B$, $B D$, $A C$, and $C D$ at points $B_{1}$, $B_{2}$, $C_{1}$, and $C_{2}$, respectively. The line $B_{1} B_{2}$ intersects $C_{1} C_{2}$ at point $K$. Prove: The distance from point $K$ to lines $A B$ and $C D$ is equal.

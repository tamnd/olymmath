On the solar eclipse of May 20, 1966, the so-called greatest phase for observers in Battonya will be 0.766, and for those in Sopron, it will be 0.678. (By this, we mean that at the time of the greatest obscuration, the part of the solar disk that is not visible is the given ratio of the diameter of the solar disk that lies along the common axis of symmetry of the apparent solar and lunar disks.) Calculate the ratio of the covered area of the solar disk to its total area for these situations (considering the areas as they would appear in a suitable photograph). The angular diameters of the Sun and Moon on this day are $31^{\prime} 39^{\prime \prime}$ and $31^{\prime} 6^{\prime \prime}$, respectively.

On the sides $B C, C A$, and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$, points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken, such that segments $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$, and $C C_{1}$ intersect at point $P$. Let $l_{a}, l_{b}, l_{c}$ be the lines connecting the midpoints of segments $B C$ and $B_{1} C_{1}$, $C A$ and $C_{1} A_{1}$, $A B$ and $A_{1} B_{1}$. Prove that the lines $l_{a}, l_{b}$, and $l_{c}$ intersect at one point, and that this point lies on the segment $P M$, where $M$ is the centroid of triangle $A B C$.

In the plane of the right triangle $ABC$, with $AB = AC = 4\sqrt{2} \text{cm}$, at point $A$ a perpendicular is raised, on which points $E$ and $F$ are considered, such that $AE = 4\sqrt{3} \text{cm}$ and $AF = 4\sqrt{15} \text{cm}$. Show that the area of triangle $EBC$ is the geometric mean of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $FBC$.

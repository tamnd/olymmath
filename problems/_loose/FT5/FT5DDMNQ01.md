On a circle $k$ with center at point $O$, we choose points $A$ and $B$ such that the chosen points are not endpoints of a diameter. On the circumcircle of triangle $OAB$, we choose a point $C$ that does not coincide with either $A$ or $B$. The line $AC$ intersects circle $k$ at points $A$ and $D$. Prove that triangle $DCB$ is isosceles.

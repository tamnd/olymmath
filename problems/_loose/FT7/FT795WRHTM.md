Let $s_{1}, s_{2}, \ldots, s_{2004}$ be non-negative real numbers whose sum is 2, and we know that

$$
s_{1} s_{2}+s_{2} s_{3}+\ldots+s_{2003} s_{2004}+s_{2004} s_{1}=1
$$

Determine the possible minimum and maximum value of $S=s_{1}^{2}+s_{2}^{2}+\ldots+s_{2004}^{2}$ under these conditions.

Given two perpendicular lines and a point. Draw a circle around the point with an arbitrary radius, but in such a way that it intersects both lines. At the points of intersection, raise perpendiculars to the lines and find the intersection points of these perpendiculars. What curve do the intersection points trace as the radius of the circle changes?

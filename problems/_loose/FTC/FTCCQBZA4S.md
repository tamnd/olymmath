If the coordinates of the two foci of a hyperbola are $F_{1}(-2,0)$ and $F_{2}(2,0)$, and a tangent line to the hyperbola intersects the $x$-axis at $Q\left(\frac{1}{2}, 0\right)$, with a slope of 2.
(1) Find the equation of the hyperbola;
(2) Let the point of tangency of the line with the hyperbola be $P$. Prove that $\angle F_{1} P Q=\angle F_{2} P Q$.

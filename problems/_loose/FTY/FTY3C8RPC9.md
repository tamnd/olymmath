Let $\mathrm{A}$ and $\mathrm{B}$ be two points in the plane such that $\mathrm{AB}=1 \mathrm{~m}, \mathrm{M}_{1}$ is the midpoint of the segment $[\mathrm{AB}], \mathrm{M}_{2}$ is the midpoint of the segment [AM $M_{1}$ ], $M_{3}$ is the midpoint of the segment $\left[A M_{2}\right.$ ], and so on, $M_{n}$ is the midpoint of the segment $\left[A M_{n-1}\right]$ for any natural number $n$.

a) Calculate the length of the segment $\left[\mathbf{M}_{5} \mathbf{M}_{4}\right]$.

b) Determine the smallest number $\mathbf{n}$ for which the length of the segment $\left[A M_{n}\right]$ is less than $1 \mathrm{~mm}$.

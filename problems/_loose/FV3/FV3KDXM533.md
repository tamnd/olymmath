(2008 Zhejiang Province Science College Entrance Examination Pressure Test Promotion) Define a non-negative function $f(x)$ on $\mathbf{R}$ that satisfies
$$
f^{2}(x+1)+f(x+1)-1 \leqslant f^{2}(x) .
$$

and when $x \in[0,1]$, $f(x)=\left|x-\frac{1}{2}\right|$. Prove: For any $x \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, $f(x)<1$.

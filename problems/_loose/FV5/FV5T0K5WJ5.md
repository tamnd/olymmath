Inside an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, a point $O$ is taken, and inside an acute-angled triangle $A' B' C'$, a point $O'$ is taken. Perpendiculars are dropped from point $O$: $OA_1$ to side $BC$, $OB_1$ to side $CA$, and $OC_1$ to side $AB$. Similarly, perpendiculars are dropped from point $O'$: $O'A_1'$ to $B'C'$, $O'B_1'$ to $C'A'$, and $O'C_1'$ to $A'B'$. It turns out that $OA_1 \parallel O'A'$, $OB_1 \parallel O'B'$, $OC_1 \parallel O'C'$, and $OA_1 \cdot O'A' = OB_1 \cdot O'B' = OC_1 \cdot O'C'$.

Prove that $O'A_1' \parallel OA$, $O'B_1' \parallel OB$, $O'C_1' \parallel OC$, and $O'A_1' \cdot OA = O'B_1' \cdot OB = O'C_1' \cdot OC$.

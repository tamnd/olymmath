A magician and an assistant want to perform the following trick. In the absence of the magician, the audience writes nine numbers, each 0 or 1, in the sectors of a circular drum. Then the assistant covers one of the numbers with a black card, and the audience rotates the drum. After this, the magician, who has just entered, must guess the number under the card. Can the magician and the assistant agree on a strategy to ensure that this trick always works?

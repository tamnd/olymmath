There are two countries: Ordinary and Wonderland. Each city in the Ordinary country has a "twin" in Wonderland, and vice versa. However, if two cities in the Ordinary country are connected by a railway, then in Wonderland these cities are not connected, and every two cities that are not connected in the Ordinary country are necessarily connected by a railway in Wonderland. In the Ordinary country, Alice cannot travel from city $A$ to city $B$ with fewer than two transfers. Prove that Alice in Wonderland can travel from any city to any other city with no more than two transfers.

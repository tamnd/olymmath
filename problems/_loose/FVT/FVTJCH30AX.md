Customers of the online store "Ali-Express" live in six houses located at the vertices of a convex hexagon. One order was placed by residents of the first house, two orders by residents of the second house, and so on, with five orders from the residents of the fifth house. However, the residents of the last sixth house made 15 orders. The store manager is thinking about where to deliver all the orders so that the total distance traveled by all customers to pick up their items is minimized. Help him solve this problem and justify the result.

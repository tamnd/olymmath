[8] There are $100 \mathrm{~N}$ sausages in a row. Uncle Fyodor and the cat Matroskin are playing a game. Uncle Fyodor, in one move, eats one of the end sausages. Matroskin, in one move, can remove the sausage from one of the sausages (or do nothing). Uncle Fyodor makes 100 moves in a row, while Matroskin makes only 1 move; Uncle Fyodor goes first, Matroskin second, and the moves alternate. Uncle Fyodor wins if the last sausage he eats has a sausage in it. Is it true that for every natural $N$ he can win regardless of Matroskin's moves?

Let's determine all the numbers $x$ for which

$$
\left[\frac{2 x-1}{3}\right]+\left[\frac{4 x+1}{6}\right]=\frac{5 x-4}{3}
$$

The square brackets denote the integer part of the number, i.e., $[a]$ is the integer such that $[a] \leq a<[a]+1$.

Let $n \geqslant 3$ and $a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be strictly positive real numbers such that:

$$
\left(a_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}\right)^{2}>(n-1)\left(a_{1}^{4}+\cdots+a_{n}^{4}\right)
$$

Show that for any triplet $\left(a_{i}, a_{j}, a_{k}\right)$ such that $i, j, k$ are distinct, they correspond to the lengths of the sides of a non-degenerate triangle. (ITYM 2011)

If $a>b>c>d$, and if $x=(a+b)(c+d)$, and $y=(a+c)(b+d)$, and $z=(a+d)(b+c)$. Then the order of magnitude of $x, y, z$ is
(A) $x<z<y$.
(B) $y<z<x$.
(C) $x<y<z$.
(D) $z<y<x$.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

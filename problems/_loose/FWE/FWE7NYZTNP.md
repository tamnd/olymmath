Two cars, the distance between which is initially 120 km, are moving in the same direction on a highway, the speed limit on which is 60 km/h. At a certain point on the highway, the permitted speed increases to 70 km/h. Further, every 100 km, the permitted speed increases by 10 km/h (i.e., 100 km after the first point - to 80 km/h, another 100 km - to 90 km/h, and so on), until it reaches 120 km/h. At any moment, the cars are driving at the maximum permitted speed. What distance (in km) will be between the cars when both are traveling at 120 km/h? If necessary, round the answer to 0.01.

Given arithmetic sequences $\left(x_{i}\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ and $\left(y_{i}\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ have the same first term and the following property: there exists an index $k(k>1)$, for which the equalities

$$
x_{k}^{2}-y_{k}^{2}=53, x_{k-1}^{2}-y_{k-1}^{2}=78, x_{k+1}^{2}-y_{k+1}^{2}=27 .
$$

hold. Find all such indices $k$.

A circle touches the sides of an angle with vertex $A$ at points $P$ and $Q$. The distances from points $P, Q$, and $A$ to a certain tangent to this circle are $u, v$, and $w$. Prove that $uv / w^2 = \sin^2(A / 2)$.

5.9*. a) On the side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, a point $P$ is taken. Let $r, r_1$, and $r_2$ be the radii of the inscribed circles of triangles $ABC, BCP$, and $ACP$; $h$ be the height dropped from vertex $C$. Prove that $r = r_1 + r_2 - 2r_1 r_2 / h$.

b) Points $A_1, A_2, A_3, \ldots$ lie on a straight line (in the given order). Prove that if the radii of the inscribed circles of all triangles $BA_iA_{i+1}$ are equal to the same number $r_1$, then the radii of the inscribed circles of all triangles $BA_iA_{i+k}$ are equal to the same number $r_k$.

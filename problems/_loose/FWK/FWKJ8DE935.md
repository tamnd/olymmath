A cylinder is 4 cm high, with a radius of 1 cm. From point $P$ on the lower base to point $Q$, which lies on the upper base directly above point $P$, we stretch a string along the lateral surface of the cylinder, wrapping it around the cylinder exactly once. How many centimeters long is the shortest string that can be stretched in this way?
(A) $2 \pi$
(B) $4 \pi$
(C) $\pi \sqrt{2}$
(D) $2 \sqrt{\pi^{2}+4}$
(E) $\sqrt{2 \pi^{2}+4}$

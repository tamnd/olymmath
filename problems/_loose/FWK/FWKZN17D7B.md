A $10 \times 10$ table is filled according to the rules of the game "Minesweeper": some cells are marked with a mine, and in each of the remaining cells, the number of mines in the neighboring cells (adjacent by side or corner) is recorded. Can the sum of all numbers in the table increase if all the "old" mines are removed, and mines are placed in all previously mine-free cells, after which the numbers are rewritten according to the rules?

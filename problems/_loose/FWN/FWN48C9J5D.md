In $\triangle A B C$, $D, E$ are on sides $B C, C A$ respectively, and $F$ is on the extension of $B A$ (Figure 3.39). Prove:
$$
\angle D E C=\angle A E F \Leftrightarrow \frac{A F}{A E} \cdot \frac{B D}{B F} \cdot \frac{C E}{C D}=1 .
$$

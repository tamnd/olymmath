On a plane, three lines are drawn forming an acute scalene triangle. Fedya, who has a compass and a ruler, wants to draw all the altitudes of this triangle. Vanya, with an eraser, tries to hinder him. In a move, Fedya either draws a line through two marked points or a circle centered at a marked point passing through another marked point. After this, Fedya marks any number of points (intersection points of the drawn lines, random points on the drawn lines, and random points on the plane). Vanya, in his turn, erases no more than three marked points. (Fedya cannot use erased points in his constructions until he marks them again). They take turns, with Fedya starting. Initially, no points on the plane are marked. Can Fedya draw the altitudes?

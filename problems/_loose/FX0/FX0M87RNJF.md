Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. The perpendicular to $B A$, erected at point $A$, intersects line $C D$ at point $M$, and the perpendicular to $D A$, erected at point $A$, intersects line $B C$ at point $N$. Prove that $M N$ passes through the center of the circle circumscribed around quadrilateral $A B C D$.

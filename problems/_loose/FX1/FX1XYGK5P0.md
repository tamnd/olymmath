Let's prove that as $n$ increases, the numbers of the form $\lg \left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n}$ increase, while those of the form $\lg \left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1}$ decrease; the numbers of the form $b_{n}=\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1}$ form a decreasing sequence, and any element of this sequence is greater than any element of the sequence $a_{n}=\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n}$.

Show that the function $\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^{x}$ increases as $x$ increases.

In tetrahedron $ABCD$, the lengths of $AB, AC, AD$ are $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$, and their opposite edges are $b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}$. The angles between these 3 pairs of opposite edges are $\theta_{1}, \theta_{2}, \theta_{3}$. Let $t_{i}=a_{i} h_{i} \cos \theta_{r}(i=1,2,3)$, prove that among $t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}$, there exists one number that is equal to the sum of the other two.

Let $P$ be a point, $\mathcal{C}_{1}, \mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathcal{C}_{3}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ be four circles such that $\mathcal{C}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{3}$ are externally tangent at $P$, and $\mathcal{C}_{2}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ are externally tangent at $P$. Suppose that $\mathcal{C}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathcal{C}_{2}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{3}, \mathcal{C}_{3}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{4}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{1}$ intersect at points $A, B, C$ and $D$ distinct from $P$. Prove that

$$
\frac{A B \cdot B C}{A D \cdot D C}=\frac{P B^{2}}{P^{2}}
$$

Consider a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ . Pairs of its opposite sides are continued until they intersect: $BA$ and $CD$ at the point $P$ , $BC$ and $AD$ at the point $Q$ . Let $K$ be the intersection point of the exterior bisectors of the angles $A$ and $C$ of the quadrilateral, $L$ be the intersection point of the exterior bisectors of the angles $B$ and $D$ of the quadrilateral, and $M$ be the intersection point of the exterior bisectors of the angles $P$ and $Q$ (the exterior bisector of an angle $X$ is the line passing through X and perpendicular to its ordinary bisector). Prove that the points $K$ , $L$ and $M$ lie on a straight line.

(S Markelov)

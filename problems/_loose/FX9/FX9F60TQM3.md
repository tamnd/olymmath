Two positive numbers $m, n$ have a ratio of $t(t>1)$. If $m+n=s$, then the smaller of $m, n$ can be expressed as
(A) $t s$.
(B) $s-t s$.
(C) $\frac{t s}{1+s}$.
(D) $\frac{s}{1+t}$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

Let $I \subset \mathbb{R}$ be an open interval and $f, g: I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be two functions that have the property

$$
\frac{f(x)-g(y)}{x-y}+|x-y| \geq 0, \text { for any } x, y \in I, x \neq y
$$

i) Prove that $f$ and $g$ are increasing functions.

ii) Give an example of functions $f, g: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f \neq g$ that satisfy the relation from the hypothesis.

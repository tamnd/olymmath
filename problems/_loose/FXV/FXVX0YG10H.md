A person starts a journey when the two hands of the clock coincide between 8 and 9 in the morning, and arrives when the two hands of the clock are exactly $180^{\circ}$ apart between 2 and 3 in the afternoon. Therefore, the journey took
(A) 6 hours.
(B) 6 hours $43 \frac{7}{11}$ minutes.
(C) 5 hours $16 \frac{4}{11}$ minutes.
(D) 6 hours 30 minutes.
(E) None of the above.
(6th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1955)

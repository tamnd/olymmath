Let in a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ there are no parallel sides. Denote by $E$ and $F$ the points of intersection of the lines $A B$ and $D C, B C$ and $A D$ respectively (point $A$ lies on the segment $B E$, and point $C$ lies on the segment $B F$). Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is tangential if and only if $E A + A F = E C + C F$.

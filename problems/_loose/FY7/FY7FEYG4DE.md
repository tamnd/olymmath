Let the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$ be $M$, and let the feet of the altitudes be denoted by $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$. Show that

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1^{\circ} . & \frac{AB}{CM}+\frac{BC}{AM}+\frac{CA}{BM}=\frac{AB \cdot BC \cdot CA}{AM \cdot BM \cdot CM} \\
2^{\circ} . & A^{\prime}M \cdot AM = B^{\prime}M \cdot BM = C^{\prime}M \cdot CM
\end{array}
$$

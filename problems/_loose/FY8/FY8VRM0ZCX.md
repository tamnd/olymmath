Let $S_{i}, R_{i}$ and $l_{i} (i=1,2,3,4)$ be the areas of the faces, the radii of the circles circumscribed around these faces, and the distances from the centers of these circles to the opposite vertices of the tetrahedron. Prove that $18 V^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{4} S_{i}^{2}\left(l_{i}^{2}-R_{i}^{2}\right)$, where $V-$ is the volume of the tetrahedron.

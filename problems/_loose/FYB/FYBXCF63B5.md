In a regular eighteen-sided polygon $A_{0} . . A_{17}$, the diagonals $A_{0} A_{p+3}, A_{p+1} A_{18-r}$, and $A_{1} A_{p+q+3}$ are drawn. Prove that the specified diagonals intersect at one point in any of the following cases:

a) $\{p, q, r\}=\{1,3,4\}$

b) $\{p, q, r\}=\{2,2,3\}$.

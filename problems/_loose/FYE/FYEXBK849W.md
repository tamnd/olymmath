Place $n$ distinct balls into $r(n \geqslant r)$ boxes, with no limit on the number of balls in each box. Find the number of ways to place the balls such that no box is empty in the following scenarios:
(1) The $r$ boxes are distinct (distinguishable);
(2) The $r$ boxes are identical (indistinguishable).

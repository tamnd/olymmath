A chef has ten sous-chefs under his command, some of whom are friends with each other. Each working day, the chef assigns one or several sous-chefs to be on duty, and each of the on-duty sous-chefs takes one pastry for each of their off-duty friends. At the end of the day, the chef learns the number of missing pastries. Can the chef figure out over 45 working days who among the sous-chefs are friends and who are not?

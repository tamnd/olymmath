Let $A B C$ be a non-obtuse triangle with the radius of its inscribed circle $r$, and its orthocenter $M$. The feet of the altitudes from vertices $A, B, C$ are $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
A_{0} M+B_{0} M+C_{0} M \leq 3 r
$$

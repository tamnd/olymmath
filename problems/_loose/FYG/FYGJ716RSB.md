Given that in the convex pentagon $A B C D E$, there are no parallel segments among its sides and diagonals. Extend side $A B$ and diagonal $C E$ to intersect at some point. Then, mark an arrow on side $A B$ pointing in the direction of the intersection point. Follow this method to mark arrows on the other sides of the pentagon. Prove that there must be two arrows pointing to the same vertex of the pentagon.

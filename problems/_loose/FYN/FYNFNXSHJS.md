Longfellow's Bees. When Longfellow was a professor of modern languages at Harvard College, he often entertained himself by giving his students more or less simple arithmetic puzzles. Here is one of them.

If $\frac{1}{5}$ of the bee swarm flew to the ladamba flowers, $\frac{1}{3}$ to the slendang flowers, three times the difference between these numbers flew to the tree, and one bee continued to fly between the fragrant ketaki and malati, then how many bees were there in total?

Let $A B C D$ be a parallelogram. A circle of radius $R$ passes through points $A$ and $B$. Another circle of the same radius passes through points $B$ and $C$. Let $M$ be the second point of intersection of these circles. Prove that the radii of the circumcircles of triangles $A M D$ and $C M D$ are equal to $R$.

In a volleyball tournament, any two teams play against each other once, with no ties. We say "Team $A$ is superior to Team $B$" if: Team $A$ beats Team $B$ or Team $A$ beats Team $C$ and Team $C$ beats Team $B$. After the tournament, any team that is superior to all other teams can be crowned champion. Prove: (1) A champion team must exist; (2) If there is only one champion team, then it must have beaten all other teams; (3) It is impossible to have exactly two champion teams.

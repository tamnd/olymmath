Let $\Gamma$ be a circle and $[A C]$ and $[B D]$ be two chords intersecting at $X$. Let $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}, C_{4}$ be circles passing through $X$ and respectively through $A, B, C, D$, such that $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ intersect on $\Gamma$, as do $C_{3}$ and $C_{4}$. Show that the intersections of $C_{1}$ and $C_{4}$ as well as $C_{2}$ and $C_{3}$ are aligned with $X$.

Given a natural number $a_{0}$, a sequence of numbers $a_{n}, n=1,2, \cdots$ can be constructed according to the following rules: if the last digit of $a_{n-1}$ is no more than 5, then it is removed, thereby obtaining $a_{n}$ (at this point, nothing may be left, in which case the number construction process ends), otherwise, let
$$
a_{n}=9 a_{n-1} .
$$

Can the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ constructed in this way be an infinite sequence?
(25th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1991)

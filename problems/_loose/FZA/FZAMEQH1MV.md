Given an equilateral $\triangle ABC$, take $D$ on the extension of $BC$ such that
$$
AB=BC=CA=CD=a,
$$
$DM$ intersects $AB, AC$ at $M, N$ respectively. Prove that:
$$
BM=\frac{4 a \tan D}{\sqrt{3}+\tan D}, CN=\frac{2 a \tan D}{\sqrt{3}-\tan D},
$$

where $D=\angle BDM$.

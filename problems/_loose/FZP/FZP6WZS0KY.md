Let $\mathscr{A}_{1}, \mathscr{A}_{2}, \cdots, \mathscr{A}_{m}$ be $m$ chains of $X$. No two chains are comparable, i.e., no member (subset) of any chain is a subset of a member of another chain. If the length of each chain is $k+1$, use $f(n, k)$ to denote the maximum value of $m$. Prove:
$$
f(n, k)=C_{n-k}^{\left[\frac{n-k}{2}\right]} .
$$

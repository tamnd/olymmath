A natural number $m$ is called orderly if, with any natural numbers $a$ and $b$ , the number $a^2m +b^2m +a^mb^m$ is divisible by $a^2 +b^2 +ab$ .
a) Prove that the number $2$ is orderly.
b) Is the number $100$ orderly?
c) Is the number $101$ orderly?
d) Find all orderly numbers.

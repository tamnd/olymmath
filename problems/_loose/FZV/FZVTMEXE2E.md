a) Two tangents are drawn to a given circle. Let $A$ and $B$ be the points of tangency, and $C$ be the point of intersection of the tangents. Draw an arbitrary line $l$ tangent to the given circle, not passing through $A$ and $B$. Let $u$ and $v$ be the distances from $A$ and $B$ to $l$, and $w$ be the distance from $C$ to $l$. Find $uv / w^2$ if $\angle ACB = \alpha$.

b) A polygon is circumscribed around a circle. Let $l$ be an arbitrary line tangent to the circle and not coinciding with any side of the polygon. Prove that the ratio of the product of the distances from the vertices of the polygon to $l$ to the product of the distances from the points of tangency of the sides of the polygon with the circle to $l$ does not depend on the position of the line $l$.

c) Let $A_1 A_2 \ldots A_{2n}$ be a $2n$-sided polygon circumscribed around a circle, and $l$ be an arbitrary tangent to the circle. Prove that the product of the distances from the vertices with odd indices to $l$ and the product of the distances from the vertices with even indices to $l$ are in a constant ratio, independent of $l$ (assuming that $l$ does not contain any vertices of the polygon).

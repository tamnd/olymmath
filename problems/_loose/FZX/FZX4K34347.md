On a table there are $k\ge 2$ piles having $n_1,n_2,\ldots,n_k$ pencils respectively. A *move* consists in choosing two piles having $a$ and $b$ pencils respectively, $a\ge b$ and transferring $b$ pencils from the first pile to the second one. Find the necessary and sufficient condition for $n_1,n_2,\ldots,n_k$ , such that there exists a succession of moves through which all pencils are transferred to the same pile.

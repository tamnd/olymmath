7・121 There are three medicine bottles, $A$, $B$, and $C$. Bottle $A$ contains 1997 tablets, while bottles $B$ and $C$ are empty. When full, bottle $B$ can hold 97 tablets and bottle $C$ can hold 19 tablets. Each tablet contains 100 units of active ingredient, and each time a bottle is opened, each tablet in that bottle loses 1 unit of active ingredient. A person opens a bottle once a day to take one tablet. He can use this opportunity to transfer tablets to other bottles to minimize future losses, and then close all the bottles. How many units of active ingredient will be lost in total when all the tablets are consumed?

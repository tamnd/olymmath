We placed a cube in the center of a potter's wheel, with each of its faces inscribed with a natural number. Just before we spun the wheel, we could see three faces of the cube and thus only three numbers from our position. Their sum was 42. After rotating the potter's wheel by $90^{\circ}$, we observed from the same position three faces with numbers summing to 34, and after another $90^{\circ}$ rotation, we saw three numbers summing to 53 from the same spot.

1. Determine the sum of the three numbers we will see from our position after the wheel is rotated by another $90^{\circ}$.
2. The cube was lying on a face with the number 6 throughout the entire process. Determine the maximum possible sum of all six numbers on the cube.

Let the random variable $\xi$ have a normal distribution $N(0,1)$, i.e., $\xi$ is a continuous random variable with probability density

$$
p(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2 \pi}} e^{-x^{2} / 2}, x \in(-\infty,+\infty)
$$

Find the mathematical expectation and variance of the random variable $\eta=10^{\xi}$.

Given a circle $\Gamma$ passing through point $A$, and $B, C$ are two moving points on circle $\Gamma$ different from point $A$. The angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ intersects circle $\Gamma$ at point $X$. $Y$ is the point symmetric to $A$ with respect to $X$. $CY$ intersects circle $\Gamma$ at point $D$. Prove: $D$ is a fixed point independent of points $B, C$.

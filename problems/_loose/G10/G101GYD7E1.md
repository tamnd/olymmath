The maze is represented by an $8 \times 8$ square, with each $1 \times 1$ cell containing one of four arrows (up, down, right, left). The top side of the top-right cell is the exit of the maze. In the bottom-left cell, there is a token that moves one cell in the direction indicated by the arrow on each move. After each move, the arrow in the cell where the token just was rotates $90^{\circ}$ clockwise. If the token is supposed to make a move that would take it outside the $8 \times 8$ square, it remains in place, and the arrow also rotates $90^{\circ}$ clockwise. Prove that the token will eventually exit the maze.

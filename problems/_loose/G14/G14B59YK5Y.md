Each cell of an $n \times n$ chessboard contains a lamp. At the beginning, two lamps located in two opposite corners are turned on, and the others are off. An operation consists of choosing a row (row or column) of the chessboard and changing the state of all the lamps in that row.

Before starting the operations, Alice can choose to turn on as many lamps as she wants individually. How many lamps must she turn on at a minimum so that there exists a sequence of operations after which all the lamps are off?

Find all values of the parameter $a \in R$, for which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
y=\sqrt{5+4 x-x^{2}}+2 \\
y=\sqrt{9-a^{2}+2 a x-x^{2}}+a
\end{array}\right.
$$

has exactly one solution in $R \times R$.

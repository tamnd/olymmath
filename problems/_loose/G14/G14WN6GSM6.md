In parallelogram $A B C D$, diagonal $A C$ is perpendicular to side $A B$. A certain circle is tangent to side $B C$ of parallelogram $A B C D$ at point $P$ and is tangent to the line passing through vertices $A$ and $B$ of the same parallelogram at point $A$. A perpendicular $P Q$ is drawn from point $P$ to side $A B$ (point $Q$ is the foot of this perpendicular). Find the angle $A B C$, given that the area of parallelogram $A B C D$ is $\frac{1}{2}$, and the area of pentagon $Q P C D A$ is $S$.

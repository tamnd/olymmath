In the square $ABCD$, from vertex $A$, we draw an arbitrary line that passes through the interior of the square; this line generally intersects the perimeter of the square for the second time at a point $M$ different from $C$. We then draw the angle bisector of $\angle BAM$ if $M$ lies on the side $CD$; if $M$ lies on the side $BC$, we draw the angle bisector of $\angle DAM$ (thus always bisecting the larger angle): the angle bisector intersects the perimeter of the square at point $N$. Prove that $BN + DM = AM$, or $DN + BM = AM$ depending on whether we bisected $\angle BAM$ or $\angle DAM$.

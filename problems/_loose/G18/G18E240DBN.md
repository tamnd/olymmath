In a turn of the game, Alice moves 5 points clockwise and Bob moves 9 points counterclockwise. The game ends when they stop on the same point. How many turns will this take? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 6 \textbf{(B)}\ 8 \textbf{(C)}\ 12 \textbf{(D)}\ 14 \textbf{(E)}\ 24 $

Let a and b be non-zero natural numbers.

(a) If $7 \mid (2a + 5b)$, show that $7 \mid (5a + 2b)$.

(b) If $7 \mid (5a + 2b)$, and $a = 2^0 + 2^1 + 2^2 + \ldots + 2^{2012}$, show that $b$ is divisible by 7.

(c) Find the non-zero natural numbers a and b such that $5a + 2b$ is equal to the largest two-digit number divisible by 7 and 5.

Show that

$$
\frac{1}{A_{2 n}}=\frac{1}{A_{n}}+\frac{1}{a_{n}}
$$

if for the same circle, $a_{n}$ represents the side of the inscribed $n$-sided polygon, $A_{n}$ represents the side of the circumscribed $n$-sided polygon, and $A_{2 n}$ represents the side of the circumscribed $2 n$-sided polygon.

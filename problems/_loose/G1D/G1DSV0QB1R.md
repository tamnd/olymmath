(MMС, Luxembourg, 80). Prove that for any value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and a prime number $p$ the following conditions are equivalent:

a) none of the numbers $C_{n}^{k}$ for $k=0,1, \ldots, n$ is divisible by $p$.

b) $n=p^{s} m-1$, where $s \in \mathbf{Z}^{+}, m \in \mathbf{N}, m<p$.

Triangle $ABC$ has side $[BC]$ included in a plane $\alpha$. Knowing that $AB=a$, $BC=2a$, $\mathrm{m}(\widehat{ABC})=30^{\circ}$, and the sine of the dihedral angle between the planes $(ABC)$ and $\alpha$ is equal to $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$, determine:

a) The length of the segment $\left[A A^{\prime}\right]$, where $A^{\prime}$ is the projection of $A$ onto the plane $\alpha$.

b) The distance from $A^{\prime}$ to the plane $(ABC)$.

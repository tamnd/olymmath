Alice and Bob play the following game. The positive divisors of an integer $N>0$ are written on the board. Alice starts by erasing $N$. Then, when a player has erased a number, the next one erases a multiple or a divisor of that number. The first player who can no longer play loses. Determine, depending on $N$, which player has a winning strategy.

Let $A B C D$ be a parallelogram such that $|A C|=|B C|$. Let $P$ be a point on the line $A B$ such that $B$ lies between $A$ and $P$. The circumcircle of triangle $A C D$ intersects the segment $\overline{P D}$ at point $Q, Q \neq D$. The circumcircle of triangle $A P Q$ intersects the segment $\overline{P C}$ at point $R, R \neq P$.

Prove that the lines $C D, A Q$, and $B R$ intersect at a single point.

Given that $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is an arithmetic sequence with a common difference of 2, $P_{n}\left(a_{n}, b_{n}\right)$ are points on the curve $C: y=3 \cdot 2^{x-1}$, and $P_{1}$ is the intersection point of curve $C$ with the $y$-axis.
(I) Find the general terms of the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$;
(II) Let $A_{n}=a_{2} a_{3} \cdots a_{n}, B_{n}=b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}$, find the set $M$ of $n$ that satisfies $A_{n}+B_{n}<895$.
(III) Let $u=\left|\overrightarrow{P_{i} P_{j}}\right|, i, j \in M$, if $u^{2 t}+2 u^{i} \leqslant 8\left|A_{n}+B_{n}-895\right|$ holds for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, find the range of the real number $t$.

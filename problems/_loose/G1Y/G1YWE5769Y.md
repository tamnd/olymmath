On the sides $A B, B C, C D, D E$ of the pentagon $A B C D E$, points $P, Q, R, S$ are given respectively such that $A P: P B = D R: R C = k_{1}$, $B Q: Q C = E S: S D = k_{2}$. On the segments $P R$ and $Q S$, points $M$ and $N$ are constructed respectively such that $P M: M R = k_{2}$, $S N: N Q = k_{1}$. Prove that the segment $M N$ is parallel to the side $A E$ and $M N = \frac{A E}{\left(k_{1}+1\right)\left(k_{2}+1\right)}$.

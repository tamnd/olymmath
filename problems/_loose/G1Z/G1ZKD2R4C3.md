For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ , let $P(n)$ denote the product of the digits in $n$ and $S(n)$ denote the sum of the digits in $n$ . Consider the set $A=\{n \in \mathbb{N}: P(n)$ is non-zero, square free and $S(n)$ is a proper divisor of $P(n)\}$ .
Find the maximum possible number of digits of the numbers in $A$ .

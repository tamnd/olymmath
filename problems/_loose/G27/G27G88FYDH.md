There are $n$ airports which form a regular $n$-gon. In the beginnig, there is exactly one plane at only $k$ airports. Each of the planes flies to one of the nearest airport each day. For which of the following ordered pairs $(n,k)$, it is impossible to gather all planes at a airport on one day however the planes are arranged initially?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ (10,6)
\textbf{(B)}\ (10,4)
\textbf{(C)}\ (11,3)
\textbf{(D)}\ (11,5)
\textbf{(E)}\ (13,8)
$

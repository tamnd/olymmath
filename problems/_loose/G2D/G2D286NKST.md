Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $x_{1} \leq x_{2} \leq \cdots \leq x_{n}$ be real numbers.
(a) Prove that
$$
\left(\sum_{i, j=1}^{n}\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|\right)^{2} \leq \frac{2\left(n^{2}-1\right)}{3} \sum_{i, j=1}^{n}\left(x_{i}-x_{j}\right)^{2} .
$$
(b) Show that equality holds if and only if $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ is an arithmetic progression.

In the triangle $ABC$, the center of the inscribed circle is $O$, its radius is $\varrho$, and the radius of the circumscribed circle of the triangle is $r$. It is to be proven that

$$
O A \cdot O B \cdot O C=4 \varrho^{2} r
$$

Based on this, prove that

$$
O A+O B+O C \geq 6 \varrho
$$

and equality holds if and only if the triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

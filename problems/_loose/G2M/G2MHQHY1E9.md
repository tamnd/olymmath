a) Are there irrational numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $a^{b}$ is a natural number?

b) A student writes the numbers $729, 15625, 343, 1331$ on the board. In step 1, he erases the four numbers and in place of each, he writes the geometric mean of the other three numbers. In step 2, he applies step 1 to the numbers thus obtained. He continues writing the numbers in this manner.

i) What numbers did he obtain after the first step?

ii) Is it possible that after a finite number of steps, he writes the numbers 847, 567, 297, 8019 on the board?

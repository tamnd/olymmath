On the segment $A C$, a point $B$ is taken, and on the segments $A B, B C, C A$, semicircles $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ are constructed on the same side of $A C$. $D$ is a point on $S_{3}$ such that $B D \perp A C$. The common tangent to $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ touches these semicircles at points $F$ and $E$ respectively.

a) Prove that the line $E F$ is parallel to the tangent to $S_{3}$, drawn through the point $D$.

b) Prove that $B F D E$ is a rectangle.

47-2 (G87, CS) Let $P$ be a regular 2006-gon. If an end of a diagonal of $P$ divides the boundary of $P$ into two parts, each containing an odd number of sides of $P$, then the diagonal is called a "good diagonal". It is stipulated that each side of $P$ is also a "good diagonal".
It is known that 2003 non-intersecting diagonals inside $P$ divide $P$ into several triangles.

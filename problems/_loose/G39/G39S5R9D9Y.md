Let's determine the geometric locus of points in space,

I. which are, on the one hand, equidistant from the given points $A$ and $B$, and on the other hand, equidistant from the given points $C$ and $D$;

II. from which a) a given distance, b) two given parallel lines, c) two given intersecting lines are seen at a right angle? (By the "visual angle" of a point with respect to two lines, we mean the dihedral angle formed by the point and the two lines.)

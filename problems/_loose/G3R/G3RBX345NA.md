Given the equation
$$
a^b\cdot b^c=c^a
$$
in positive integers $a$, $b$, and $c$.
*(i)* Prove that any prime divisor of $a$ divides $b$ as well.
*(ii)* Solve the equation under the assumption $b\ge a$.
*(iii)* Prove that the equation has infinitely many solutions.

*(I. Voronovich)*

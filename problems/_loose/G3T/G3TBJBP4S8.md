Suppose that Eisen can do the following as many times as he wants to a number written on a blackboard: choose two positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that the number on the blackboard is $mn$ , erase this number on the board, and replace it with $m+n$ . For example, if 12 is written on the blackboard, Eisen can replace it with 7, because $12=3\times 4$ and $7=3+4$ . If the number 2023 is initially written on a blackboard, determine, with proof, all possible numbers that Eisen can write on the blackboard.

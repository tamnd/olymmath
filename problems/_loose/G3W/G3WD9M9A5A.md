The foci of an ellipse are $F_{1}, F_{2}$, and a point on the ellipse is $P$. Consider the circle that is tangent to the side $P F_{1}$ of the triangle $P F_{1} F_{2}$ and the extensions of the sides $F_{2} F_{1}, F_{2} P$. Show that this circle passes through the endpoint of the major axis of the ellipse closer to $F_{1}$.

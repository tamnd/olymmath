Given $a, b, c > 0$. Prove:
$$
\left(a^{3}+\frac{1}{b^{3}}-1\right)\left(b^{3}+\frac{1}{c^{3}}-1\right)\left(c^{3}+\frac{1}{a^{3}}-1\right) \leqslant\left(a b c+\frac{1}{a b c}-1\right)^{3} .
$$

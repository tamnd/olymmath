In a resort, there are $2n$ guests together, and each of them has at least $n$ acquaintances. Prove that it is possible to select four of them and seat them around a round table in such a way that each of them is seated next to two acquaintances. (The acquaintance is mutual; if $A$ knows $B$, then $B$ also knows $A$.)

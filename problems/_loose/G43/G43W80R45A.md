Xiao Qian, Xiao Lu, and Xiao Dai are guessing a natural number between 1 and 99. The results:

Xiao Qian said: "It is a perfect square, and it is less than 5."
Xiao Lu said: "It is less than 7, and it is a two-digit number."
Xiao Dai said: "The first half of what Xiao Qian said is true, but the second half is false."
If among these 3 people, 1 person is telling the truth in both statements, 1 person is lying in both statements, and 1 person is telling the truth in one statement and lying in the other. Then, what is this number? (Note: A perfect square is a number that can be expressed as the square of an integer, for example, $4=2^2$, $81=9^2$, so we call 4 and 9 perfect squares).

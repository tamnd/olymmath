Let $ABC$ be a triangle with its centroid $G$. Let $D$ and $E$ be points on segments $AB$ and $AC$, respectively, such that,
$$\frac{AB}{AD}+\frac{AC}{AE}=3.$$
Prove that the points $D, G$ and $E$ are collinear.

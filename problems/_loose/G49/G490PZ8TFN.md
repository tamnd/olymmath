Prove that the centers of the faces of each regular polyhedron serve as the vertices of another regular polyhedron, and that the centers of the faces of this second polyhedron are the vertices of a third polyhedron, homothetic to the original. Two types of regular polyhedra, connected by this construction, are called duals of each other.

With a natural number (written in the decimal system), the following operations are allowed:

A) append the digit 4 at the end;

B) append the digit 0 at the end;

C) divide by 2 (if the number is even).

For example, if we perform operations C, C, A, and B sequentially on the number 4, we get the number 140.

a) Starting from the number 4, obtain the number 1972.

b)* Prove that any natural number can be obtained from the number 4.

On an infinitely large grid board with each square having a side length of $l$, there are four frogs at the four vertices of one of the squares $A B C D$. Every minute, one of the frogs jumps and lands at a position symmetrical to another frog's position. Prove that it is impossible for the four frogs to be at the four vertices of a larger square at any moment.

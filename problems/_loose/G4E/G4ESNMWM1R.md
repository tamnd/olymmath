Let $p$ be a prime number, $1 \leq k \leq p$, and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots a_{k}$ be integers not divisible by $p$. Form all sums $e_{1} a_{1}+e_{2} a_{2}+ \cdots+e_{k} a_{k}$, where $e_{1}, e_{2}, \ldots e_{k}$ independently take the values 0 or +1. Prove that these sums give at least $k$ different remainders when divided by $p$.

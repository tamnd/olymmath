The sequence of Fibonnaci's numbers if defined from the two first digits $f_1=f_2=1$ and the formula $f_{n+2}=f_{n+1}+f_n$, $\forall n \in N$.
**(a)** Prove that $f_{2010} $ is divisible by $10$.
**(b)** Is $f_{1005}$ divisible by $4$?

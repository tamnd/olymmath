Let $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at two distinct points $A$ and $B$. A common tangent $t$ to both circles touches the circle $k_{1}$ at a point $C$ and the circle $k_{2}$ at a point $D$. Let $M$ be the point of intersection of the line $(A B)$ with the tangent $t$. Show that $M$ is the midpoint of the segment $[C D]$.

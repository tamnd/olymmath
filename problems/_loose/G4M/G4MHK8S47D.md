Let $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$ be $n$ points in the plane, no three of which are collinear. Connect $m$ line segments between them such that among any 4 points, there are 3 points that form a triangle, i.e., each pair of these 3 points is connected by a line segment. Find the minimum value of $m$.

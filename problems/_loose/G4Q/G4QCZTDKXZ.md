Given a parallelogram $A B C D$ with an obtuse angle $A B C$. On its diagonal $A C$ in the half-plane $B D C$, choose a point $P$ such that $|\angle B P D|=|\angle A B C|$. Prove that the line $C D$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $B C P$ if and only if the segments $A B$ and $B D$ are congruent.

For any triangle $A B C$, let $S$ be the intersection of the angle bisectors. Furthermore, as usual, let $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ denote the sizes of the interior angles $\angle B A C, \angle C B A$, and $\angle A C B$, respectively.

a) What are the sizes of the angles $\angle A S B$ and $\angle B S C$ in a triangle $A B C$ where $\alpha=42^{\circ}$ and $\beta=98^{\circ}$?

b) Determine $\gamma$ in any triangle $A B C$ where $\angle A S B$ has a size of $140^{\circ}$!

c) Prove that every triangle $A B C$ in which $\angle A S B$ and $\angle B S C$ are equal in size is isosceles! Also specify which two sides of the triangle are equal in length in each such triangle!

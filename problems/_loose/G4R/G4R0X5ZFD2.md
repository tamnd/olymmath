A New Inequality. Show that for arbitrary real numbers $a_{i}>0$ and for any integers $M, P>0$, the following inequality holds:

$$
M \cdot\left(\sum_{i=1}^{M} a_{i}^{P}\right) \leqslant\left(\sum_{i=1}^{M} a_{i}^{P+1}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{M} a_{i}^{-1}\right)
$$

On a circular racetrack, at the sound of a signal from various points on the track, 25 cars start. The cars can overtake each other, but at most 2 cars can fit side by side (i.e., there is no "triple" overtaking). At the end of the race, every car returns to the point from which it started. It needs to be proven that an even number of overtakes occurred during the race.

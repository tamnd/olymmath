A square area with a side length of $10 \mathrm{~km}$ has two parallel roads running along two of its sides. There are 4 observation stations on this area. It is necessary to build connecting roads, which consist of segments parallel to the sides of the square, so that it is possible to cycle from each station to each of the roads. (Cycling on the roads is not allowed.)

Prove that this can be done with at most $25 \mathrm{~km}$ of road, regardless of where the observation stations are located, but not always with less.

In the pyramid $ABCD$, the faces $ABC$ and $ADC$ are isosceles triangles with a common base $AC$. A sphere of radius $R$ with its center at point $O$, which lies on the face $ABC$, is tangent to all the edges of the pyramid $ABCD$. Find the lengths of the segments into which the points of tangency of the sphere divide the edges of the pyramid, and the volume of the pyramid $ABCD$, if the angle $ABC$ is equal to $2\alpha$. Find the value of the angle $ABC$ for which the volume of the pyramid will be the smallest. Find this smallest value of the volume of the pyramid $ABCD$.

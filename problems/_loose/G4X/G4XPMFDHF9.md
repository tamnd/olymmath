Prove the following combinatorial identities:
(1) $\sum_{k=0}^{m}(-1)^{k}\binom{n-k}{n-m}\binom{n}{k}=0 (n \geqslant m>0)$.
(2) $\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n-k}{n-m}\binom{n}{k}=2^{m}\binom{n}{m} (n \geqslant m>0)$.
(3) $\sum_{k=0}^{n-m}(-1)^{k}\binom{n-k}{m}\binom{n}{k}=0 (n>m \geqslant 0)$.

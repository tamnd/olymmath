The range of $x$ that satisfies the inequality $|x+1|+|x|<2$ is
(A) $-\frac{3}{2}<x<-1$.
(B) $-\frac{3}{2}<x<0$.
(C) $-\frac{3}{2}<x<\frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $0<x<\frac{1}{2}$.
(E) $-\frac{1}{2}<x<\frac{3}{2}$.

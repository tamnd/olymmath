Points are placed at equal intervals on a circle, forming a 33-sided polygon by connecting adjacent points with segments. Each side of this 33-gon is painted in one of three colors, and it turns out that there are an equal number of segments of each color. Prove that the 33-gon can be divided into triangles by non-intersecting diagonals, with each diagonal painted in one of the same three colors, such that each triangle has one side of each color.

Given three lines: $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}$. We are looking for a triplet of circles $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}$, such that $k_{1}$ is tangent to the lines $e_{2}, e_{3}$ and the circles $k_{2}, k_{3}$, $k_{2}$ is tangent to the lines $e_{3}, e_{1}$ and the circle $k_{3}$, $k_{3}$ is tangent to the lines $e_{1}, e_{2}$. - Construct the circles if

a) the three lines are parallel;
b) $e_{3}$ is perpendicular to $e_{1}$ and $e_{2}$.

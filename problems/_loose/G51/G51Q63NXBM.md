All the diagonals of the lateral faces of an $n$-sided prism are colored in yellow and blue colors, so that single-colored diagonals do not have common points. Prove that the sum of the squares of lengths of all yellow diagonals is equal to the sum of the squares of lengths of the blue ones.
(Oleg Kryzhanovsky.)

Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D, B C=A D$, and $B C$ is not parallel to $A D$. Let points $E$ and $F$ be on the interiors of sides $B C$ and $A D$, respectively, such that $B E=D F$. Line $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $P$, line $B D$ and $E F$ intersect at point $Q$, and line $E F$ and $A C$ intersect at point $R$. Prove: As points $E$ and $F$ vary, the circumcircle of triangle $P Q R$ passes through another fixed point besides point $P$.

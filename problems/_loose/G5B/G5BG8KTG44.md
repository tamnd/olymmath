Given a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$, and a triangle $ABC$ inscribed in it, where vertex $A$ is fixed, while for the other two vertices,

$$
\overline{A B}^{2}+\overline{A C}^{2}=k^{2}
$$

where $k^{2}$ is a constant.

$1^{0}$. What is the geometric locus of the midpoint of side $BC$?

$2^{0}$. What curve envelops $BC$?

$3^{0}$. What is the geometric locus of the center of the circumcircle $\omega$ of $\triangle OBC$?

Let $M \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a set that satisfies the following properties:
i) $0 \in M$;

ii) $x \in M \Rightarrow (\cos x + i \sin x) \in M$;

iii) $(\cos 2x + i \sin 2x) \in M \Rightarrow x \in M$.

Show that:
a) $M$ contains at least 3 integers;
b) $M$ contains infinitely many irrational numbers;
c) $M$ contains infinitely many non-real complex numbers;

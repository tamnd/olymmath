Consider the isosceles trapezoid $A B C D$ where $A B \| C D$ and $A B=2 \cdot C D$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the larger base and the smaller base, respectively, and let $P$ be a point on the segment $(M N)$ such that $\measuredangle B P M \equiv \measuredangle A B C$. Show that the line $AP$ is perpendicular to $DM$.

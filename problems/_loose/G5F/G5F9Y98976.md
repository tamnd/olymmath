Let $n>14$, and let $S$ be a set of $n$ points in the plane such that the distance between any two points in $S$ is at least 1 unit. Prove: There exists a subset $T$ of $S$ consisting of at least $\left[\begin{array}{l}n \\ 7\end{array}\right]$ points, and the distance between any two points in $T$ is at least $\sqrt{3}$ units.

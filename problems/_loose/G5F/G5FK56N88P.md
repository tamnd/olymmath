I rearranged the tiles within the given frame so that one black rectangle, one black square, and one contiguous white shape were formed. The individual tiles still touched each other with their entire sides after rearrangement. The black shapes did not touch (not even at a corner), and each of them had at least one side in common with the edge of the chessboard.

Determine the maximum possible perimeter of the white shape and draw how it might look in such a case.

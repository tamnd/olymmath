(1) Let the equation
$$
x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0}=0
$$

have real coefficients, and satisfy the condition
$$
0<a_{0} \leqslant a_{1} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant a_{n-1} \leqslant 1
$$

Given that $\lambda$ is a complex root of this equation, and satisfies the condition $|\lambda| \geqslant 1$. Prove: $\lambda^{n+1}=1$.

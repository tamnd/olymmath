Let's call two numbers almost equal if they are equal or differ from each other by no more than one. Is it true that from any rectangle with natural sides, one can cut out some rectangle with natural sides, the area of which is almost equal to half the area of the original rectangle? The sides of the rectangle being cut out do not necessarily have to be parallel to the sides of the original rectangle.

(E. Molianov)

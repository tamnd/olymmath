Let $a, b, c, d$ be complex numbers, and the angles $a 0 b$ and $c 0 d$ are equal and oppositely oriented. Prove that then $\operatorname{Im} a b c d = 0$.

We will say that triangles $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ are properly similar if there exists a rotational homothety that maps $A$ to $A^{\prime}, B$ to $B^{\prime}, C$ to $C^{\prime}$.

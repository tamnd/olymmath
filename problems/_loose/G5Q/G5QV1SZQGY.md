An urn contains 5 black, 6 white, and 7 blue balls. Someone offers to pay 3 forints to anyone who, in one draw, pulls out 2 black, 3 white, and 4 blue balls, and the person offering the bet will pay 50 fillér in all other cases. Is this bet to their advantage or disadvantage? What would have been the fair bet instead of 3 forints?

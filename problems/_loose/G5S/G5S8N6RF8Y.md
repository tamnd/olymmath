Compute the line integral of the second kind

$$
\int_{L} x^{2} y d x - y^{2} x d y
$$

where $ L: \left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}=a^{2}\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)^{2}, y \geqslant 0 $ from $ A(0,0) $ to $ B(a, 0) $.

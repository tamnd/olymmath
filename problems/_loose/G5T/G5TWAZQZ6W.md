Find all polynomials $ P(x, y) $ with real coefficients such that for every two real numbers $ x, y > 1404 $, we have:
$$
P(\{x\}, \{y\}) = \{P(x, y)\},
$$
where $ \{x\} $ denotes the fractional part of $ x $.

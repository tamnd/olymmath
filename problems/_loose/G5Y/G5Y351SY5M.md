Let $A, B, C, D$ be the four vertices of a three-sided pyramid (also known as a tetrahedron or quadrilateral solid). Let the distances from a point $P$ inside the tetrahedron to the faces opposite to points $A, B, C, D$ be denoted by $p_{a}, p_{b}, p_{c}, p_{d}$, and the heights corresponding to these faces be denoted by $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}, m_{d}$.

a) Show that

$$
\frac{p_{a}}{m_{a}}+\frac{p_{b}}{m_{b}}+\frac{p_{c}}{m_{c}}+\frac{p_{d}}{m_{d}}=1
$$

b) Can the theorem be generalized to the case where the point lies outside the tetrahedron? If so, how?

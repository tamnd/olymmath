Prove that repeating such an operation many times cannot ensure that There are only zeros left on the board.**7.2** Prove that the radius of a circle is equal to the difference between the lengths of two chords, one of which subtends an arc of $1/10$ of a circle, and the other subtends an arc in $3/10$ of a circle.**7.3** Prove that for any natural number $n$ the number $ n(2n+1)(3n+1)...(1966n + 1) $ is divisible by every prime number less than $1966$ .**7.4** What number needs to be put in place * so that the next the problem had a unique solution:

Prove that all these points can be enclosed in a triangle of area $4$ .

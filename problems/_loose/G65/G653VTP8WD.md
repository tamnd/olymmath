Let the corresponding elements of two similar right triangles be denoted by $a, b, c, m_{c}$ (where $c$ is the hypotenuse), and $a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime}, m_{c}^{\prime}$. Prove that

1. $a a^{\prime}+b b^{\prime}=c c^{\prime}$,
2. $\frac{1}{a a^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{b b^{\prime}}=\frac{1}{m_{c} m_{c}^{\prime}}$.

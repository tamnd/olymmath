In square $ABCD$, $P$ is a point on side $CD$ (point $P$ does not coincide with the vertices). Extend $AP$ to intersect the extension of $BC$ at point $Q$. Let the inradii of $\triangle ABQ$, $\triangle PAD$, and $\triangle PCQ$ be $r_{1}$, $r_{2}$, and $r_{3}$, respectively.
(1) Prove: $r_{1}^{2} \geqslant 4 r_{2} r_{3}$, and determine the position of point $P$ when equality holds;
(2) If $AB=1$, prove:
$$
3-2 \sqrt{2}<r_{1}^{2}+r_{2}^{2}+r_{3}^{2}<\frac{1}{2} \text {. }
$$

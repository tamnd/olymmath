Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle; and let $P$ , $Q$ and $R$ be points on the segments $BC$ , $AC$ and $AB$ respectively, such that $\angle ABC=\angle PQR$ , $\angle ACB=\angle QRP$ and $\angle BAC= \angle RPQ$ . Prove that the circumcenter of the triangle $ABC$ is the orthocenter of the triangle $PQR$ .

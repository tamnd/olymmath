Knights and liars played "Chinese whispers" according to the following rules: the first in the chain whispers a number to the second, the second to the third, and so on. The last person in the chain announces the number out loud. At the same time, a knight repeats the same number that was told to them, while a liar adds or subtracts one.

The first person (a liar) whispered the number 7 to their neighbor. The last person announced the number 3 out loud.

After that, the participants played this game again, but this time they passed the numbers to each other in reverse order. The last person whispered the number 5 to their neighbor, and the first person announced the number 2 at the end of the game.

Who was the last person in the chain?

Prove that for any natural $n$
a) $n^{4}+2 n^{3}+11 n^{2}+10 n $ is divisible by 24,

b) $n^{7}-n$ is divisible by 42,

c) $7^{n}+3 n-1$ is divisible by 9,

d) $7+7^{2}+7^{3}+\ldots+7^{4 n}$ is divisible by 100,

e) $4^{2 n+2}-15 n-16$ is divisible by 225,

f) $n^{3}+11 n$

is divisible by 6,

g) $n^{5}-5 n^{3}+4 n$

is divisible by 120,

h) $2^{5 n}-1$

is divisible by 31,

i) $6^{n}+n^{3}+6 n^{2}+11 n+6$ is divisible by 6,

j) $n^{7}+6 n $ is divisible by 7,

k) $10^{n+1}-9 n-10 $ is divisible by 81.

a) Two triangles and one quadrilateral.

b) One triangle and two quadrilaterals.

c) One triangle and three quadrilaterals.
d) One triangle, one quadrilateral, and one pentagon.

e) Two triangles and one hexagon.

Carry out these drawings! No justifications are required.

Let $a=(\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{3})^{2}, b=(\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{3})^{-2}$. Then $(a+b)$ : $(a-b)$ is
(A) $\sqrt{5}: \sqrt{3}$.
(B) $5: 3$.
(C) $\sqrt{3}: \sqrt{5}$.
(D) $3: 5$.
(3rd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

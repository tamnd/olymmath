At the beginning of the game, Little Boy and Karlson have one piece of chocolate in the form of a $2019 \times 2019$ square grid. Each move, Little Boy divides some piece along the grid lines into three rectangular pieces, and Karlson eats one of these three pieces at his choice. The game ends when no more moves can be made. If the total number of moves made is even, Little Boy wins; if it is odd, Karlson wins. Who wins with correct play?

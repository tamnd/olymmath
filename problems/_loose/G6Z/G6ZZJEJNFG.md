Find all such numbers $k$ for which

$$
(k / 2)!(k / 4)=2016+k^{2}
$$

The symbol $n!$ denotes the factorial of the number $n$, which is the product of all integers from 1 to $n$ inclusive (defined only for non-negative integers; $0!=1$).

Let $\omega$ be an arbitrary circle that passes through $A$ and $I_a$ and intersects the extensions of sides $AB$ and $AC$ (extended from $B$ and $C$) at $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Suppose that lines $BT$ and $CS$ intersect at $K$, and lines $KI_a$ and $TS$ intersect at $Z$. Prove that $X, Y, Z$ are collinear.

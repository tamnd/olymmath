A square grid paper has a distance of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ between its parallel lines. We draw a circle with a radius of 5 cm around the intersection of two grid lines on the paper. How many regions are formed that are bounded by arcs of the circle and segments of the grid lines (and there is no segment of a grid line inside the region)?

What is the area of each of these regions?

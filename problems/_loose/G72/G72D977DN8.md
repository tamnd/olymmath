Divide the interval $[0,1]$ into three equal parts, and remove the middle open interval $\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right)$. Divide each remaining closed interval $\left[0, \frac{1}{3}\right],\left[\frac{2}{3}, 1\right]$ into three equal parts, and remove the middle open intervals $\left(\frac{1}{9}, \frac{2}{9}\right)$ and $\left(\frac{7}{9}, \frac{8}{9}\right)$. Then divide each remaining closed interval into three equal parts and remove the middle open interval. Continue this process infinitely. The set that remains is denoted by $P$. Prove:
(i) $P$ is a closed set, and it has no isolated points;
(ii) The cardinality of the point set $P$ is uncountable.

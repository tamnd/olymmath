(1991 CIS Math Competition) A cinema has a total of $m$ rows, each with $n$ seats, and the box office has sold a total of $m n$ movie tickets. Due to negligence, some of the ticket numbers are duplicates, but each audience member can sit according to either the row number or the seat number on their ticket. Prove: At least one audience member can sit in the correct row and seat, while the others can still sit according to the aforementioned conditions.

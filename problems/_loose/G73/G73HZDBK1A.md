In the plane, there is a circle and a point. Starting from point $A$, we travel along a closed broken line, each segment of which lies on a tangent of the circle, until we return to the starting point. Consider the segments of the path where we approach the circle as positive, and the segments where we move away from the circle as negative.

It is to be proven that the sum of these segments, taken with their respective signs, is 0.

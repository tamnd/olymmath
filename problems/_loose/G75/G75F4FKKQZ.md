The bisectors $B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ intersect at point $I$. Points $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ are marked on the extensions of segments $B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$, respectively, such that the quadrilateral $A B^{\prime} I C^{\prime}$ is a parallelogram. Prove that if $\angle B A C=60^{\circ}$, then the line $B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ passes through the intersection point of the circumcircles of triangles $B C_{1} B^{\prime}$ and $C B_{1} C^{\prime}$.

If in $p$-arithmetic $\sqrt{5}$ can be extracted, then the number of elements in any circular Fibonacci series without repetitions is a divisor of the number $p-1$.

$p$-arithmetic Fibonacci series. Let $v_{0}, v_{1}$, $v_{2}, \ldots, v_{n}, \ldots$ be an arbitrary $p$-arithmetic Fibonacci series. Construct the sequence

$$
t_{1}=\frac{v_{1}}{v_{0}}, t_{2}=\frac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}, \ldots, t_{n}=\frac{v_{n}}{v_{n-1}}, \ldots
$$

We will call this sequence the sequence of ratios for the series $v_{0}, v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{n}, \ldots$ If some of the numbers $v_{0}, v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{n}, \ldots$ are zero, then in the sequence of ratios, besides numbers from $p$-arithmetic, the symbol $\infty^{1}$ appears (if any two consecutive elements of the series $v_{0}, v_{1}, \ldots, v_{n}, \ldots$ are zero, then all elements of this series are zero; this case we exclude from consideration).

Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer and let $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ be positive real numbers. Prove that
$$x_1\left(1-x_1^2\right)+x_2\left(1-(x_1+x_2)^2\right)+\cdots+x_n\left(1-(x_1+...+x_n)^2\right)<\frac{2}{3}.$$

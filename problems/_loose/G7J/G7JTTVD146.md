Let $G$ be a countable connected graph with vertices $v_{0}, v_{1}, \ldots$, and for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $S_{n}:=\left\{v_{0}, \ldots, v_{n-1}\right\}$. Prove the following statements:
(i) For each end $\omega$ of $G$, there exists a unique sequence of components $C_{n}$ of $G-S_{n}$ such that $C_{0} \supseteq C_{1} \supseteq \cdots$ and for all $n$ we have $C_{n}=C\left(S_{n}, \omega\right)$.
(ii) For every infinite sequence of components $C_{n}$ of $G-S_{n}$ such that $C_{0} \supseteq C_{1} \supseteq \cdots$, there exists a unique end $\omega$ such that for all $n$ we have $C_{n}=C\left(S_{n}, \omega\right)$.

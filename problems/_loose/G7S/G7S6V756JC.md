Let $p$ be a prime number. Tristan and Abigaëlle play the following game. Tristan writes an integer $X \geq 1$ on the board and gives a sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of strictly positive integers to Abigaëlle. Abigaëlle then plays an infinite number of turns. During the $n^{\text{th}}$ turn,

Abigaëlle replaces, according to her choice, the integer $Y$ written on the board with the integer $Y+a_{n}$ or with the integer $Y \cdot a_{n}$.

Abigaëlle wins if, after a finite number of turns, she manages to write a multiple of $p$ on the board. Determine if she can succeed in winning regardless of Tristan's initial choices, in each of the following two cases:
a) $p=10^{9}+7$;
b) $p=10^{9}+9$.

Note: It is given that $10^{9}+7$ and $10^{9}+9$ are prime.

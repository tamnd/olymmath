Provide a brief proof of the following theorem by Erdös and Szekeres: For each $k \in \mathbb{N}$, there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$, such that any $n$ points in the plane, no three of which are collinear, contain $k$ points that form a convex $k$-gon, i.e., such that no point is inside the convex hull of the others.

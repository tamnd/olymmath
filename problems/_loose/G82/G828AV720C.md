Given a right-angled triangle $ABC$ at $C$ with the legs $a$ and $b$ (where $b > a$) and the hypotenuse $c$. On $AC = b$, a point $D$ is fixed such that $AD = BD$. Prove that then

$$
\cos \measuredangle ADB = \frac{a^2 - b^2}{c^2}
$$

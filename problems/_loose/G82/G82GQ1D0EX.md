Let $f(x)=\arccos x+2 \arcsin x$, then $f^{-1}(x)$ is
(A) $\sin x, x \in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$.
(B) $\cos x, x \in[0, \pi]$.
(C) $-\sin x, x \in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$.
(D) $-\cos x, x \in[0, \pi]$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

Two players, A and B, play a game using the first 100 natural numbers: A first writes these 100 numbers in some order as a sequence without showing it to B. Then B can write down any 50 of these 100 natural numbers and ask A to write them in the order they appear in the sequence. After seeing the results of the previous questions, B can then select the next 50 numbers and continue this process. How many times does B need to ask at minimum to ensure that, regardless of how A writes the sequence, B can write it out? Prove your conclusion.

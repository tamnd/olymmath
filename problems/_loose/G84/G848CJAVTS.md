Let $r$ and $s$ be two parallel lines, and $A$ a fixed point equidistant from both lines. For each point $B$ on line $r$, let $C$ be the point on line $s$ such that $\angle B A C = 90^{\circ}$, and let $P$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to the line $B C$. Prove that, regardless of which point $B$ on line $r$ we take, the point $P$ lies on a fixed circle.

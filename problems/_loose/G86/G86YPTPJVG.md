Points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ are on the sides $\overline{B C}, \overline{C A}, \overline{A B}$ of triangle $A B C$ respectively, such that:

$$
\frac{\left|B A_{1}\right|}{|B C|}=\frac{\left|C B_{1}\right|}{|C A|}=\frac{\left|A C_{1}\right|}{|A B|}=k
$$

Determine $k$ so that the area of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is minimized.

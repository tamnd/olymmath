Seven students play the game of passing the flower under the guidance of their teacher. Every time the teacher knocks, the students pass the flower to the next student in a clockwise direction, for example, student 1 passes to student 2, student 2 passes to student 3, ..., student 7 passes to student 1. So, when the teacher knocks the 50th time, and the students complete the 50th pass, the flower will be in the hands of student $ $.

Let $n \geq 3$ be a natural number. We want to write one of the numbers 1, 2, or 3 in each cell of an $n \times n$ table, such that on any three cells that can be covered by a domino of the form $\square \square$ (which can also be rotated), different numbers are written. In how many ways can we do this?

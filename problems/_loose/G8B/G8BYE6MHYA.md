Let the area of the larger base of a frustum be $T$, the area of the smaller base be $t$, and its height be $m$. The volume of the frustum is $V=k T m$, where $\frac{1}{3} \leq k \leq 1$. What is the ratio $\lambda(<1)$ of proportionality between the two bases? (The ratio of proportionality, or the proportionality factor: the ratio of corresponding distances in two similar figures.)

Application: a) For a certain frustum, the area of the larger base is $T=532.8 \mathrm{~cm}$, and its height is $m=23.2 \mathrm{~cm}$. The ratio of the corresponding sides of the bases is $3: 5$. What is its volume? - b) For a certain frustum, its volume is half that of a right prism whose base is congruent to the larger base of the frustum and whose height is equal to the height of the frustum. What is the ratio of the areas of the two bases?

Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $BC > CA$ and $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$ . The incircle of triangle $ABC$ is tangent to $BC, CA$ , and $AB$ at $D, E,$ and $F$ respectively. Let $DE$ and $AB$ intersect at $G$ . Point $O$ is the circumcentre of triangle $FCG$ and $OA$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $FOG$ at $H$ ( $H \neq O$ ). If $\angle OCB = 90^\circ$ , prove that $MH$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $FOG$ .

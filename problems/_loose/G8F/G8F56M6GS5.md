We will say that two positive integers $a$ and $b$ form a *suitable pair* if $a+b$ divides $ab$ (its sum divides its multiplication). Find $24$ positive integers that can be distribute into $12$ suitable pairs, and so that each integer number appears in only one pair and the largest of the $24$ numbers is as small as possible.

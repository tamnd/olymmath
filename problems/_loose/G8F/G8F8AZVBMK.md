If the decimal part of $\log _{a} b$ is 0, and $\log _{a} \frac{1}{b}>\log _{a} \sqrt{b}>\log _{b} a^{2}$, then among the following four conclusions:
(1) $\frac{1}{b}>\sqrt{b}>a^{2}$.
(2) $\log _{a} b+\log _{b} a=0$.
(3) $0<a<b<1$.
(4) $a b-1=0$.

the number of correct conclusions is
(A) 1 .
(B) 2 .
(C) 3 .
(D) 4 .
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1990)

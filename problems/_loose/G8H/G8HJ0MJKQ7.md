In triangle $ABC$, the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ (interior) intersects the circumcircle of the triangle at $D$, the perpendicular bisectors of sides $AB$ and $AC$ intersect at $E$ and $F$, respectively. Prove the following equality:

$$
\frac{A F}{D F} \cdot \frac{D E}{A E}=\frac{A C^{2}}{A B^{2}}
$$

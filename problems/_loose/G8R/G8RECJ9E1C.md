Draw a regular octagon with side length $c$, and cut out $4 \mathrm{db} 45^{\circ}$ and $2 \mathrm{db} 90^{\circ}$ angle, $c$ side rhombuses. Show (in a drawing) that it is possible to cover the octagon without gaps or overlaps using these 6 rhombuses, i.e., every point is covered, the rhombuses do not overlap each other, and they do not extend outside the octagon. - Show the same for a regular 12-sided polygon with side length $c$, 16-, 6-, 10-, and 14-sided polygons, using $c$ side rhombuses with $6,6,3 \mathrm{db} 30^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 90^{\circ}$ angles, respectively, and $8,8,8,4$ db $22.5^{\circ}, 45^{\circ}, 67.5^{\circ}, 90^{\circ}$ angles, $3 \mathrm{db} 60^{\circ}$ angles, $5-5 \mathrm{db} 36^{\circ}, 72^{\circ}$ angles, and finally $7-7 \mathrm{db}$ with acute angles being $1/7, 2/7, 3/7$ of $180^{\circ}$. - For each polygon, it is sufficient to show a single solution.

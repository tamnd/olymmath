Prove the following duality property of Stirling numbers of the first and second kind:

$$
\sum_{n \geqslant 0} S_{N}^{n} s_{n}^{M}=\delta_{N M}
$$

where $\delta_{a b}$ is the Kronecker delta ( $\delta_{a a}=1, \delta_{a b}=0$, if $a \neq b$ ).

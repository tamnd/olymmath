Each student in the class wrote down in his notebook a geometric progression consisting of different positive numbers. Let $A$ be the sum of the first terms of all these progressions, $B$ the sum of their second terms, and $C$ the sum of their third terms. Prove that the sequence $A$, $B$, $C$ is not an arithmetic progression.

At the vertices of a square, four grasshoppers are sitting. They jump in a random order, but not simultaneously.

Each grasshopper jumps to a point that is symmetric to the point where it was before the jump, relative to the center of mass of the other three grasshoppers. Can a grasshopper ever land on another grasshopper at some point? (The grasshoppers are point-like.)

Let $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{100}$ be one hundred distinct prime numbers. Natural numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{k}$, greater than 1, are such that each of the numbers $p_{1} p_{2}^{3}, p_{2} p_{3}^{3}, \ldots, p_{99} p_{100}^{3}, p_{100} p_{1}^{3}$ is equal to the product of some two of the numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{k}$. Prove that $k \geq 150$. (I. Rubanov)

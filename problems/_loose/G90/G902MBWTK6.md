A square piece of paper has 1965 holes pierced inside it. No three of these holes and the vertices of the square are collinear. Draw several non-intersecting line segments, whose endpoints are these holes or the vertices of the square, to divide the square into some triangles, and ensure that there are no more holes inside or on the sides of these triangles. How many line segments were drawn in total? How many triangles were obtained?

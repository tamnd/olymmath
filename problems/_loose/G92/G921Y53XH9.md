Determine all real numbers $p$ for which the system of inequalities

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}+(p-1) x+p \leqq 0 \\
& x^{2}-(p-1) x+p \leqq 0
\end{aligned}
$$

has at least one solution in the set of real numbers.

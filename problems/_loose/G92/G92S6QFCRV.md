Construct a triangle $ABC$ from $\alpha=50^{\circ}, r=4 \text{ cm}$, and $h_{a}=6 \text{ cm}$!

Here, $\alpha$ denotes the size of the angle $\angle BAC$, $r$ the circumradius, and $h_{a}$ the length of the perpendicular height from $A$ to $BC$ of the triangle $ABC$.

Describe and justify your construction! Investigate whether a triangle $ABC$ is uniquely determined up to congruence by the given pieces! Triangles $ABC, A'B'C'$ should also be considered congruent if they can be brought into coincidence with each other by any permutation of the vertices.

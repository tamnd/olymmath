Given a tetrahedron $P-ABC$ with three lateral edges $PA$, $PB$, and $PC$ that are mutually perpendicular, the dihedral angles formed by the lateral faces $PAB$, $PBC$, and $PCA$ with the base $ABC$ have plane angles of $\theta_{1}$, $\theta_{2}$, and $\theta_{3}$, respectively. The area of the base $\triangle ABC$ is $4 \sqrt{3}$.
(1) Prove: $\tan \theta_{1} \cdot \tan \theta_{2} \cdot \tan \theta_{3} \geqslant 2 \sqrt{2}$;
(2) If $\tan \theta_{1}+\tan \theta_{2}+\tan \theta_{3}=3 \sqrt{2}$, find the volume $V_{P-ABC}$ of the tetrahedron.

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2, k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, and the matrix $A \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{R})$ for which the following relation holds:

$$
A^{4 k+1}=A^{4 k-1}+A^{4 k-2}+\cdots+A+I_{n} .
$$

Prove that:

a) A is invertible.

b) $\operatorname{det}\left(A+I_{n}\right) \geq 0$.

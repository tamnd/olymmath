Let $n(n>3)$ be a given integer. Find the largest integer $d$, such that for any set $S$ of $n$ integers, there exist four distinct non-empty subsets (which may intersect), the sum of the elements in each of which is divisible by $d$.
(2015, Singapore Mathematical Olympiad)

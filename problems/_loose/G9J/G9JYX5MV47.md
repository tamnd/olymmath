Let $\lambda$ be a positive real number. For any pairwise distinct positive real numbers $a, b, c$, we have
$$
\frac{a^{3}}{(b-c)^{2}}+\frac{b^{3}}{(c-a)^{2}}+\frac{c^{3}}{(a-b)^{2}} \geqslant \lambda(a+b+c) \text {. }
$$

Find the maximum value of $\lambda$.

Given an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$ with its circumcircle $\odot O$, the tangent line to $\odot O$ at point $A$ intersects $B C$ at point $D, P$ is any point on the line $O D$, $P M \perp A B$ at point $M, P N \perp A C$ at point $N$. Prove: the orthocenter $H$ of $\triangle P M N$ lies on $B C$.

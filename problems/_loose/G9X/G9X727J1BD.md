A broken line with a finite number of segments is inscribed in a parabola. Its starting point coincides with the vertex of the parabola, and any two segments of the broken line sharing a vertex form equal angles with the tangent to the parabola at that point. Prove: such a broken line can only lie on one side of the parabola's axis of symmetry.

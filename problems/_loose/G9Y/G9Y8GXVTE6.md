Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of real numbers converging to $a$, with all terms different from $a$, and let $k: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ be a function. Prove that the following statements are equivalent:

(a) $\lim _{n \rightarrow+\infty} a_{k(n)}=a$;

(b) for any $y \in \mathbb{N}$ the set $\{x \in \mathbb{N} \mid k(x)=y\}$ is finite (the empty set is finite, having zero elements).

A circle $k$ is inscribed in a quadrilateral $A B C D$, whose diagonal $B D$ is not a diameter. Prove that the intersection of the lines tangent to the circle $k$ at points $B$ and $D$ lies on the line $A C$ if and only if $|A B| \cdot|C D|=|A D| \cdot|B C|$.

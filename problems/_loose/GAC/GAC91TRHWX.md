In $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C=90^{\circ}$, and $C D$ is its angle bisector. Given $A D=p$, $D B=q$, then the altitude $h$ from $A B$ is
(A) $\frac{p q}{p^{2}+q^{2}}$.
(B) $\frac{p+q}{p^{2}+q^{2}}$.
(C) $\frac{p q(p+q)}{p^{2}+q^{2}}$.
(D) $\frac{p^{2}+q^{2}}{p q(p+q)}$.
(Chinese Junior High School Mathematics Competition, Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, 1985)

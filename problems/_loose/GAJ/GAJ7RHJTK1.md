During a summer excursion, a German and a Hungarian class spent several days together. At the end of the trip, they exchanged addresses and have been regularly corresponding ever since: every Hungarian student maintains contact with at least half of the German class, but none of the German students asked for the addresses of more than half of the Hungarian class. Prove that this is only possible if both the German and Hungarian students are each corresponding with exactly half of the other class.

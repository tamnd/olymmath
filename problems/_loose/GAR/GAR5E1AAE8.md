Amálka observed squirrels in the garden of the nursery, where these three trees grew: spruce, beech, and fir. The squirrels were sitting quietly on the trees, so she could count them - there were 34 of them. When 7 squirrels jumped from the spruce to the beech, there were as many on the beech as on both conifers combined. Then, 5 more squirrels jumped from the fir to the beech, at which point there were as many squirrels on the fir as on the spruce. On the beech, there were then twice as many as on the fir at the beginning. How many squirrels were originally sitting on each of the trees?

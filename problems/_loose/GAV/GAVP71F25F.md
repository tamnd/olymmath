Kanel-Belov A.Y. In the cells of an $n \times n$ table, there are pluses and minuses. In one move, it is allowed to change all the signs in any row or any column to their opposites. It is known that from the initial arrangement, one can achieve an arrangement where plus signs are in all cells. Prove that this can be achieved in no more than $n^2$ moves.

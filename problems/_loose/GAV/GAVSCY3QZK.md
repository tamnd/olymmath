Consider the tetrahedron $A B C D$ with opposite edges perpendicular. Let $A E \perp B C, E \in B C, A F \perp C D$, $F \in C D$. Let $B F \cap D E=\{R\}$ and $C R \cap B D=\{J\}$.

(i) Prove that $D E \perp B C$.

(ii) Prove that $A J \perp B D$.

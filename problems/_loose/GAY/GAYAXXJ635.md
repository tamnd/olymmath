(Jury, Canada, 82). Let one of the roots of the polynomial

$$
P(x)=x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c, \text { where } a, b, c \in \mathbf{Z}
$$

be the product of the other two. Prove that the number $2 P(-1)$ is divisible by the number $P(1)+P(-1)-2(1+P(0))$.

For example, $8 n$ chess players participate in a round-robin tournament. Assume there are no draws, and no situation where $V_{1}$ beats $V_{2}$, $V_{2}$ beats $V_{3}$, ..., $V_{k}$ beats $V_{1}$ occurs. Prove: There must be one player who wins all games, and there must be one player who loses all games.

Let $\boldsymbol{a}_{1}, \ldots, \boldsymbol{a}_{n}$ be the vectors of the sides of an $n$-gon, $\varphi_{i j}=\angle\left(\boldsymbol{a}_{i}, \boldsymbol{a}_{j}\right)$. Prove that $a_{1}^{2}=a_{2}^{2}+\ldots+a_{n}^{2}+2 \sum_{i>j>1} a_{i} a_{j} \cos \varphi_{i j}$, where $a_{i}=\left|\boldsymbol{a}_{i}\right|$.

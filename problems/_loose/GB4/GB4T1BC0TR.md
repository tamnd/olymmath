Consider the matrices $A, B \in M_{n}(\mathbb{C}), n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$ with the property that there exists $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}^{*}$ such that $\alpha A B + A + B = 0_{n}$.

a) Show that $\left(\alpha A + I_{n}\right)\left(\alpha B + I_{n}\right) = I_{n}$.

b) Show that $A B = B A$.

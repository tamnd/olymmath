(40 points) The shaman of the Sunlovers tribe calculates every midnight whether the coming day will be lucky: according to the tribe's beliefs, the day with number D from the beginning of time will be lucky if the number

$$
\left(D^{2}+4\right)\left(R^{2}+4\right)-2 D\left(R^{2}+4\right)-2 R\left(D^{2}+4\right)
$$

is non-negative, where $R$ is the number of the day when the tribe's chief was born. Are there days for the Sunlovers that are not lucky?

Given a circle, we take a point $A$ on its circumference, from which we draw the chords $A B$ and $A M$. From $M$, we measure $M B$ on the line $A M$ in both directions, such that $M P = M Q = M B$. What is the geometric locus of points $P$ and $Q$ if $M$ moves along the circumference of the given circle?

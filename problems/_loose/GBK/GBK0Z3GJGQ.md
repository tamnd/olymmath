Consider the triangle $\triangle A B C$. With respect to a Cartesian reference system with the origin at the center of the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, the vertices $A, B, C$ have affixes $a, b$ respectively $c$. If the equation $a z^{2}+b z+c=0$ has a root of modulus one, prove that $\triangle A B C$ is isosceles.

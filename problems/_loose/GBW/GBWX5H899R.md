Let $M A$ be a chord of circle $\omega_{1}$ that is tangent to circle $\omega_{2}$ at point $M$, and let $M B$ be a chord of circle $\omega_{2}$ that is tangent to circle $\omega_{1}$ at point $M$. On the line $M P$, a segment $P H = M P$ is laid out. Prove that the quadrilateral $M A H B$ is cyclic.

| Arrival time | Train A | $8{:}10$ | $8{:}30$ | $8{:}50$ |
|--------------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Train B | | $9{:}10$ | $9{:}30$ | $9{:}50$ |
| Probability | | $\frac{1}{6}$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ |

Suppose that these random events are independent of each other. Now, a traveler comes into the station at $8{:}20$. Then the mathematical expectation of his waiting time is ______ (round to minute).

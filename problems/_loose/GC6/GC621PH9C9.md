On the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ of triangle $ABC$ (or on their extensions), points $C_1$, $A_1$, and $B_1$ are taken such that $\angle(C C_1, AB) = \angle(A A_1, BC) = \angle(B B_1, CA) = \alpha$. The lines $A A_1$ and $B B_1$, $B B_1$ and $C C_1$, $C C_1$ and $A A_1$ intersect at points $C'$, $A'$, $B'$ respectively. Prove that:

a) the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$ coincides with the circumcenter of triangle $A' B' C'$;
b) $\triangle A' B' C' \sim \triangle ABC$, and the similarity coefficient is $2 \cos \alpha$.

5.57*. In each of the angles of triangle $ABC$, a circle is inscribed. From one vertex, the circles inscribed in the other two angles are seen at equal angles. From another vertex, the same is true. Prove that then from the third vertex, the two circles are also seen at equal angles.

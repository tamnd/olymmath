In the 7th grade, there are 25 people, and each attends a dance club or a drama club (some attend both clubs). Everyone took a math test, and the teacher calculated the percentage of students who received a failing grade (2) separately among the "dancers" and among the "actors." It turned out that the percentage is the same and equals $30 \%$. Can the percentage of failing grades in the entire class be more than $35 \%$?

There are finitely many points on the plane such that no three of them are on the same line. For at most how many points can the segments connecting the points be colored with three colors so that the following conditions are satisfied:

a) no point can have three segments of different colors starting from it;

b) among the points, it is not possible to find three such that the three segments they determine have the same color;

c) all three colors are used.

Pasha and Igor are flipping a coin. If it lands on heads, Pasha wins; if tails, Igor wins. The first time the loser pays the winner 1 ruble, the second time - 2 rubles, then - 4, and so on (each time the loser pays twice as much as the previous time). After 12 games, Pasha is 2023 rubles richer than he was initially. How many of these games did he win?

(*****) Let $E$ be a set of $n \geq 2$ points in the plane. We denote by $D$ and $d$ the largest and smallest distances, respectively, between any two distinct points in $E$.

Prove that:

$$
D \geq \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}(\sqrt{n}-1) d
$$

Two years ago, Anke, Birgit, and Christine were discussing their travel destinations for the summer holidays of 1981 and 1982. In each year, one of them wanted to go to the Baltic Sea, another to the Saxon Switzerland, and the third to the Thuringian Forest. For both years, the following statements were made:

(1) Anke is going to the Baltic Sea.

(2) Christine is going to the Thuringian Forest or Anke is going to the Saxon Switzerland.

Later it turned out: For the year 1981, statement (1) is true and statement (2) is false; for the year 1982, statement (1) is false and statement (2) is true.

Investigate
a) for the year 1981
b) for the year 1982,

which of the three students can thus have their travel destination determined uniquely and which cannot! Name all travel destinations that can be uniquely determined!

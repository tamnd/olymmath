On the board, Maena has written 2021 integers in a row. Then, every minute, below the last row $\ell$ she has written, she writes a new row $\ell^{\prime}$ of 2021 numbers following this rule: directly below each integer in row $\ell$, she writes the number of times that integer appears in row $\ell$. Show that Maena will necessarily end up writing the same row twice in a row.

Consider the sequence of real numbers $\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}\right)_{\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}^{*}}$ defined as follows: $\mathrm{x}_{1}>\sqrt{3}$ and $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}}{\sqrt{1+\mathrm{nx}_{\mathrm{n}}^{2}}}, \forall \mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}^{*}$.

a) Calculate $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}$

b) Show that the sequence $\left(n x_{n}\right)_{n \in N^{*}}$ is convergent and determine $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} n x_{n}$.

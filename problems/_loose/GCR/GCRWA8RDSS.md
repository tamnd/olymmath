Let 4 positive integers $a, b, c, d$ be fixed such that $\frac{a}{b} \leq \frac{c}{d} \leq 1$. Which of the following inequalities is certainly true?
(A) $\frac{a+c}{b+d} \geq \frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}$
(B) $\frac{a+c}{b+d} > 1$
(C) $\frac{a+c}{b+d} \leq \frac{c}{d}$
(D) $\frac{a+c}{b+d} < 1$
(E) none of the above

When $4 p^{3}+27 q^{2}<0$, the equation $x^{3}+p x+q=0$ has three real roots (the irreducible case of the cubic equation), but to find them using Cardano's formula, the use of complex numbers is necessary. However, all three roots can be explicitly indicated in terms of trigonometric functions.

a) Prove that when $p<0$, the equation $x^{3}+p x+q=0$ can be reduced to the equation $4 t^{3}-3 t-r=0$ in the variable $t$ by the substitution $x=k t$.

b) Prove that when $4 p^{3}+27 q^{2} \leq 0$, the solutions to the equation (*) will be the numbers $t_{1}=\cos \frac{\varphi}{3}, t_{2}=\cos \frac{\varphi+2 \pi}{3}, t_{3}=\cos$ $\frac{\varphi+4 \pi}{3}$, where $\varphi=\arccos r$.

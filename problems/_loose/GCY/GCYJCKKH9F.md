Two quantities $x$ and $y$ depend on each other, such that when one increases, the other also increases. Someone, not accustomed to precise mathematical formulations, expressed this as: " $y$ increases proportionally to $x$ ". What is wrong with this formulation? Does it express more, less, or something entirely different from what is known about the mutual dependence of $y$ and $x$? Is what is known about the mutual dependence of the variables at least a necessary condition for proportionality?

Given a triangle $ABC$ with $AB=AC$ and circumcenter $O$. Let $D$ and $E$ be midpoints of $AC$ and $AB$ respectively, and let $DE$ intersect $AO$ at $F$. Denote $\omega$ to be the circle $(BOE)$. Let $BD$ intersect $\omega$ again at $X$ and let $AX$ intersect $\omega$ again at $Y$.

Suppose the line parallel to $AB$ passing through $O$ meets $CY$ at $Z$. Prove that the lines $FX$ and $BZ$ meet at $\omega$.

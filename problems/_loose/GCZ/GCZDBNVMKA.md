(IMO 1995) Let A, B, C, D be four distinct points on a line (in this order). The circles with diameters $[A C]$ and $[B D]$ intersect at $X$ and $Y$. The line $(X Y)$ intersects $(BC)$ at $Z$. Let $P$ be a point on the line $(X Y)$ other than $Z$. The line $(C P)$ intersects the circle with diameter $[A C]$ again at $M$, and the line $(B P)$ intersects the circle with diameter $[B D]$ again at $N$. Prove that the lines $(\mathrm{AM}), (\mathrm{DN})$, and $(\mathrm{XY})$ are concurrent.

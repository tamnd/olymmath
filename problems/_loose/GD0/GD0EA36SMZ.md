Given a convex polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$. Points $B_{1}$ and $D_{2}$ are taken on side $A_{1} A_{2}$, points $B_{2}$ and $D_{3}$ on side $A_{2} A_{3}$, and so on, such that if parallelograms $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}, \ldots, A_{n} B_{n} C_{n} D_{n}$ are constructed, then the lines $A_{1} C_{1}, \ldots, A_{n} C_{n}$ intersect at a single point $O$. Prove that $A_{1} B_{1} \cdot A_{2} B_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot A_{n} B_{n}=A_{1} D_{1} \cdot A_{2} D_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot A_{n} D_{n}$.

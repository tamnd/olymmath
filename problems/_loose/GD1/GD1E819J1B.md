A convex quadrilateral $ABCD = N$ has midpoints $A', B', C', D'$ on its sides $BC, CD, DA, AB$, respectively. The intersection points of the adjacent pairs of lines $AA', BB', CC', DD'$ are $A_1, B_1, C_1, D_1$. The area of quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ is $T_1$, and the area of $N$ is $T$. It is to be proven that $5 T_1 \leqq T \leqq 6 T_1$.

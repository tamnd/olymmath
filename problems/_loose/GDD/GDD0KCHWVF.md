Morgane has written the three integers 3, 4, and 12 on the board. She then makes successive changes as follows: she chooses two numbers $a$ and $b$ written on the board, erases them, and replaces them with $(3a + 4b) / 5$ and $(4a - 3b) / 5$.

Can Morgane, after a finite number of such changes,

a) end up writing the number 13 on the board?

b) end up writing the number -12 on the board?

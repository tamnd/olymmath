Let $P$ be any point inside $\triangle ABC$, and the lines connecting vertices $A, B, C$ with point $P$ intersect $BC, CA, AB$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively. $P'$ is any point on the perimeter of $\triangle DEF$. Draw lines through $P'$ parallel to $PD, PE, PF$, intersecting $BC, CA, AB$ at points $D', E', F'$ respectively. Prove that among the ratios $\frac{P'D'}{PD}, \frac{P'E'}{PE}, \frac{P'F'}{PF}$, one is equal to the sum of the other two.

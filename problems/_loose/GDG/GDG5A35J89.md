Benoît and Théo are playing the following game: at the start, the polynomial $X^{2020}$ is written on the board. Each in turn, Benoît and Théo add a monomial of the form $X^{k}$ with $0 \leqslant k \leqslant 2020$ to the previous polynomial. At the end of Théo's turn, Benoît wins if, denoting $P$ the polynomial written, there exists $x$ such that $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})<0$; otherwise, the game continues. If Benoît starts, show that Théo can ensure he does not lose.

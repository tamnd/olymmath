In a rectangular parallelepiped $A B C D A 1 B 1 C 1 D 1$, it is known that $A B = A A 1 = 12$ and $A D = 30$. Point $M$ is located in the face $A B B 1 A 1$ at a distance of 1 from the midpoint of $A B$ and at equal distances from vertices $A$ and $B$. Point $N$ lies in the face $D C C 1 D 1$ and is symmetric to point $M$ with respect to the center of the parallelepiped. Find the length of the shortest path along the surface of the parallelepiped between points $M$ and $N$.

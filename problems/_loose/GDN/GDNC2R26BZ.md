Calculate the areas of the surfaces:

a) of the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=R^{2}$, bounded by the planes $z=0(z \geqslant 0), z-y=0$

b) of the cylinder $y^{2}+z^{2}=R^{2}, z \geqslant 0$, bounded inside the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=R^{2}$;

c) of the paraboloid $x^{2}+y^{2}=6 z$, bounded inside the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=27$

d) of the sphere $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=3 a^{2}$, bounded inside the paraboloid $x^{2}+y^{2}=2 a z, a>0$.

Prove that if $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ and $\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}$ are the angles of two triangles, then

$$
\frac{\cos \alpha_{1}}{\sin \alpha}+\frac{\cos \beta_{1}}{\sin \beta}+\frac{\cos \gamma_{1}}{\sin \gamma} \leqslant \operatorname{ctg} \alpha+\operatorname{ctg} \beta+\operatorname{ctg} \gamma
$$

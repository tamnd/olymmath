Call a quadratic *invasive* if it has $2$ distinct real roots. Let $P$ be a quadratic polynomial with real coefficients. Prove that $P(x)$ is invasive **if and only if** there exists a real number $c \neq 0$ such that $P(x) + P(x - c)$ is invasive.

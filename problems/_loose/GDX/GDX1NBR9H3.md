Let $x, y$ be positive integers, and $x<y$. Also, the characteristic of $\lg x$ is $a$, and the mantissa is $\alpha$; the characteristic of $\lg y$ is $b$, and the mantissa is $\beta$, satisfying the conditions $a^{2}+b^{2}=5, \alpha+\beta=1$. Then the maximum value of $x$ is
(A) 50.
(B) 80.
(C) 100.
(D) 200.
(Anhui Province Hefei City Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

Let the consecutive vertices of a regular octagon inscribed in a unit circle $k$ be $P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, W$. In a Cartesian coordinate system, the center of $k$ is at the origin and the coordinates of $P$ are $(1,0)$. Consider the following segments as individual domains: $P Q=\mathbf{A}, Q R=\mathbf{B}, R S=\mathbf{C}, S T=\mathbf{D}$. Write down the following sum domains: $\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{C}$, $\mathbf{B}+\mathbf{D}, \mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B}+\mathbf{C}, \mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B}+\mathbf{C}+\mathbf{D}$

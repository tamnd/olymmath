Draw a circle on a plane, then draw $n$ intersecting lines one by one. When $r$ is an odd number greater than 1, the $r$-th line intersects only one of the previous $r-1$ lines inside the circle. When $r$ is even, the $r$-th line intersects all the previous $r-1$ lines inside the circle. If no three lines intersect at the same point inside the circle, into how many non-overlapping parts do these $n$ lines divide the circle?

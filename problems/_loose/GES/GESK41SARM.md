In the parallelogram $ABCD$, we draw a perpendicular from vertex $C$ to the angle bisector $f$ of $\angle BCD$. This perpendicular intersects line $AB$ at $P$ and line $AD$ at $Q$. A semicircle is drawn over $PQ$ as a diameter on the side of $PQ$ that does not contain $A$, and this semicircle intersects the extension of $f$ at $R$. Perpendiculars are drawn from $C$ and $R$ to $AB$ and $AD$, intersecting at points $S$ and $T$. Prove that $CSRT$ is a rhombus and that its area is equal to the area of parallelogram $ABCD$.

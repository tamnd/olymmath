Let $\alpha_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}; q_{1}, q_{2}, \cdots, q_{n} \leqslant, \beta$ all be positive numbers, and $\gamma=\alpha+\beta$, prove that,
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{q_{1} a_{1} \gamma+q_{2} a_{2} \gamma+\cdots+q_{n} a_{n} \gamma}{q_{1}+q_{2}+\cdots+q_{n}} \\
\geqslant \frac{q_{1} a_{1} \alpha_{1}+q_{2} a_{2} \alpha_{2}+\cdots+q_{n} a_{n} a^{\alpha}}{q_{1}+q_{2}+\cdots+q_{n}} \cdot \frac{q_{1} a_{1}^{a}+q_{2} a_{2}^{s}+\cdots+q_{n} a_{n}^{s}}{q_{1}+q_{2}+\cdots+q_{n}} .
\end{array}
$$

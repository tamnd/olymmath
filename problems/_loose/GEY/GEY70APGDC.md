Let $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$ be the angular bisectors of a triangle $A B C$ with incenter $I$. The segments $C I$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ meet at $D$ and the midpoints of the segments $A I$ and $B I$ are denoted by $M$ and $N$, respectively.

a) If $a=B C, b=A C$ and $c=A B$, find the ratio $C D: D I$.

b) If $K=A C \cap \overrightarrow{C^{\prime} M}$ and $L=B C \cap \overrightarrow{C^{\prime} N}$, prove that $D$ is the incenter of $\triangle K L C$.

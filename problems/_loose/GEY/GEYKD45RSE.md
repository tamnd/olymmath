Determine for which natural numbers $k$ the system of inequalities

$$
k(k-2) \leqq\left(k+\frac{1}{k}\right) x \leqq k^{2}(k+3)
$$

with the unknown $x$ and parameter $k$ has exactly $(k+1)^{2}$ solutions in the set of integers.

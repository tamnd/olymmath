In the complete quadrilateral $A B C D E F$, vertices $B, C, E, F$ are concyclic on circle $O$, and point $M$ is the Miquel point of the complete quadrilateral.
(1) Tangents $A P, A Q$ are drawn from point $A$ to circle $O$, with points of tangency $P, Q$, then $P, D, Q$ are collinear.
(2) The two arcs of circle $O$ harmonically divide the line containing diagonal $A D$ (refer to Property 8 of Chapter 11).
(3) Point $M$ lies on the line containing diagonal $A D$.
(4) $A M$ bisects $\angle C M E$, $A M$ bisects $\angle B M F$, and $C, O, M, E$ are concyclic, $B, O, M, F$ are concyclic.
(5) $O M \perp A D$.
(6) Points $A, P, O, M, Q$ are concyclic.
(7) Lines $Q M, B F, C E$ are concurrent or parallel.

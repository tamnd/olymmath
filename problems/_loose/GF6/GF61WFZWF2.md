In $\triangle A B C$, $A B=2 \sqrt{3}, A C=2$, and the altitude from $A$ to $B C$ is $\sqrt{3}$. Then the length of $B C$ is
(A) 4.
(B) 2.
(C) 4 or 2.
(D) None of the above.
(Chinese Zhejiang Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1993)

Given 100 natural numbers $a_{1} \leqslant a_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant a_{100}<100$. Prove: If it is not possible to select several numbers from $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{100}$ such that their sum equals 100, then $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{100} \neq 200$.

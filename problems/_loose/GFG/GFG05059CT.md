The equation $x^{2}-D y^{2}=1$, where $D$ is a natural number that is not a perfect square, is called Pell's equation. Show that if $x_{0}, y_{0}$ is an integer solution to this equation, then the numbers determined by the formula $x+y \sqrt{D}=\left(x_{0}+y_{0} \sqrt{D}\right)^{k}$, where $k=1,2,3, \ldots$, are also solutions to this equation.

Show also that the values $x_{0}, x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$, $x_{k}, x_{k+1}, x_{k+2} \ldots$ are related by the dependency $x_{k+2}=2 x_{0} \cdot x_{k+1} -x_{k}$.

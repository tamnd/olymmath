On the segment $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1$, a function $f$ is given. It is known that this function is non-negative and $f(1)=1$. Moreover, for any two numbers $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ such that $x_{1} \geqslant 0, x_{2} \geqslant 0$ and $x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 1$, the inequality

$$
f\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right) \geqslant f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)
$$

a) Prove that for any function $\mathfrak{f}$ satisfying the listed conditions, for all $x$ the inequality $f(x) \leqslant 2 x$ holds.

b) Is it true that for all $x$

$$
f(x) \leqslant 1.9 x ?
$$

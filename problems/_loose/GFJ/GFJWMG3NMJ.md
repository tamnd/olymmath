If $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ are the lower and upper foci of the hyperbola $\frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$, $O$ is the origin, $P$ is on the lower branch of the hyperbola, and point $M$ is on the directrix. It is given that: $\overrightarrow{F_{2} O}=$ $\overrightarrow{M P}, \overrightarrow{F_{1} M}=\lambda\left(\frac{\overrightarrow{F_{1} P}}{\left|\overrightarrow{F_{1} P}\right|}+\frac{\overrightarrow{F_{1} O}}{\left|\overrightarrow{F_{1} O}\right|}\right)(\lambda>0)$.
(1) Find the eccentricity $e$ of this hyperbola;
(2) If this hyperbola passes through $N(\sqrt{3}, 2)$, find the equation of this hyperbola;
(3) If the imaginary axis endpoints of the hyperbola passing through $N(\sqrt{3}, 2)$ are $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ (with $B_{2}$ on the positive $x$-axis), and points $A$ and $B$ are on the hyperbola, and $\overrightarrow{B_{2} A}=\mu \overrightarrow{B_{2} B}$, find $\overrightarrow{B_{1} A} \cdot \overrightarrow{B_{1} B}$ when the equation of line $AB$ is determined.

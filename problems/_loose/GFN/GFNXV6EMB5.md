Baron Munchausen claims that he launched a ball from the edge of a billiard table shaped as an equilateral triangle, such that the ball, reflecting off the edges, passed through a certain point three times in three different directions and returned to the starting point. Could the baron's words be true? (The reflection of the ball from the edge follows the law "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection".)

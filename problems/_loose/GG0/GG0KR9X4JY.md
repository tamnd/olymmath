a) Use the described geometric scheme to prove the relation

$$
C_{n}^{m}+C_{n-1}^{m-1}+C_{n-2}^{m-2}+\ldots+C_{n-m}^{0}=C_{n+1}^{m}
$$

$C_{n}^{m} C_{k}^{0}+C_{n-1}^{m-1} C_{k+1}^{1}+C_{n-2}^{m-2} C_{k+2}^{2}+\ldots+C_{n-m}^{0} C_{k+m}^{m}=C_{n+k+1}^{m}$

c) Find the value of the sum

$$
C_{n}^{0} C_{m}^{k}+C_{n}^{1} C_{m}^{k-1}+C_{n}^{2} C_{m}^{k-2}+\ldots+C_{n}^{k} C_{m}^{0}
$$

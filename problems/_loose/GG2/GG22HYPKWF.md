On each card, a number is written: 1 or -1. You can point to 3 cards and ask: "What is the product of the numbers on these 3 cards?" (but it does not tell what numbers are written on the cards)
(1) When there are 30 cards in total, what is the minimum number of questions needed to know the product of all the numbers on the cards?
(2) For 31 cards, answer the same question as (1).
(3) For 32 cards, answer the same question as (1).
(4) On a circle, 50 numbers are written: 1 or -1. If asking one question can reveal the product of 3 consecutive numbers, how many questions are needed to know the product of all 50 numbers?

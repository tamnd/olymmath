Let $n$ be an integer, $n>1$. We denote $d_{1}, \ldots, d_{k}$ the positive divisors of $n$ with $1=d_{1}<$ $d_{2}<\ldots<d_{k}=n$. We set $D=d_{1}d_{2}+\ldots+d_{k-1}d_{k}$. Show that $D<n^{2}$. Find all integers $n$ for which $D$ is a divisor of $n^{2}$.

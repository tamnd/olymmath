Let $A B C D$ be a square. For two different points $E$ and $F$, which lie in some order on the side $B C$ between $B$ and $C$, it holds that $B E = F C$ and $B E : E F = 41 : 11$.

The line through $A$ and $E$ is $g$, the line through $D$ and $F$ is $h$, and the intersection of $g$ and $h$ is $S$.

Investigate whether, for a configuration of points $A, B, C, D, E, F, S$ that satisfies these conditions, the triangle $E F S$ is equilateral!

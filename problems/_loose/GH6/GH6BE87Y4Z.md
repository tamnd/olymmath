Given the set of matrices

$$
M=\left\{X \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{C}) \mid \exists k \in \mathbb{N}, k \geq 2 \text {, such that } X^{k}=O_{2}\right\}
$$

a. Prove that if $A \in M$, then $\operatorname{det}\left(I_{2}+A+A^{2}+\ldots+A^{2015}\right)=1$.

b. Prove that the matrix $I_{2}$ cannot be written as a finite sum of matrices from $M$.

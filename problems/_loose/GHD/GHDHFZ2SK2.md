Let $ABC$ be a triangle. We denote $\Gamma$ its circumcircle and $H$ its orthocenter. We denote $M_{A}, M_{B}$ and $M_{C}$ the midpoints of $[BC],[CA],[AB]$ respectively.

a) Show that the reflections of $H$ over $(BC),(CA),(AB)$ lie on $\Gamma$.

b) Show that the reflections of $H$ over $M_{A}, M_{B}, M_{C}$ lie on $\Gamma$.

Find a number of the form $3 p^{2}$, where $p$ is a prime number not equal to 3, if the sum of all its divisors is 124.

A divisor of a number $N$, different from $N$, is called a proper divisor of this number. A number $N$ is called perfect if it is equal to the sum of all its proper divisors.

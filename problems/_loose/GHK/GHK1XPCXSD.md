Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$, and $R, r$ be the circumradius and inradius of the triangle, respectively. Prove:
(1) When $\triangle A B C$ is an acute triangle, $|H A|+|H B|+|H C|=2(R+r)$;
(2) When $\triangle A B C$ is an obtuse triangle $\left(C>90^{\circ}\right)$, $|H A|+|H B|-|H C|=2(R+r)$.

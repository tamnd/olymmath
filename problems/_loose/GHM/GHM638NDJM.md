Touching Circles [Pythagorean Theorem (direct and inverse).]

Two circles with radii $ r $ and $ R $ have centers at points $ O_{1} $ and $ O $ and touch externally at point $ K $. At point $ A $ on the circle with radius $ R $, a tangent is drawn that intersects the circle with radius $ r $ at points $ B $ and $ C $. It is known that $ B C: A B = p $ and the segment $ A C $ intersects the segment $ O_{1} K $. Determine:

a) under what conditions on $ r, R $, and $ p $ such a geometric configuration is possible;

b) the length of the segment $ B C $.

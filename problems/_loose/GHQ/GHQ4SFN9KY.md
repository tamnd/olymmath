Grandfather Frost has an infinite number of candies in his bag, numbered with natural numbers. One minute before the New Year, he starts giving candies to children. First, he gives the children a candy with the number 1. Half a minute before the New Year, he gives 2 candies with the numbers 2 and 3, and takes back the candy with the number 1, 15 seconds before the New Year, he gives 4 candies with the numbers 4, 5, 6, 7, and takes back 2 candies with the numbers 2 and 3, and so on, for $1 / 2^{\text {n }}$ of a minute before the New Year, Grandfather Frost gives $2^{\mathrm{n}}$ candies with numbers from $2^{\mathrm{n}}$ to $2^{\mathrm{n}+1}-1$ and takes back $2^{\mathrm{n}-1}$ candies with numbers from $2^{\mathrm{n}-1}$ to $2^{\text {n }}-1$. How many candies will Grandfather Frost and the children have at the moment of the New Year?

One, (40 points) Given positive real numbers $a, b, c, d$ satisfying $a+b+c+d=4$. Prove:
$$
\frac{b}{\sqrt{a+2 c}}+\frac{c}{\sqrt{b+2 d}}+\frac{d}{\sqrt{c+2 a}}+\frac{a}{\sqrt{d+2 b}} \geqslant \frac{4 \sqrt{3}}{3} .
$$

Three containers are each filled with an integer number of liters of water, and it is possible to pour into any container an amount of water equal to what it already contains from another container. Prove that after several such "re-pourings," one of the containers can be emptied. (Each container is large enough to hold all the water)
（5th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1971）

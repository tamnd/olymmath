If $n$ is a natural number, and $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are arbitrary angles, prove the following inequalities:

(a) $|\sin n \alpha| \leq n|\sin \alpha|$,

(b) $|\cos n \beta - \cos n \alpha| \leq n^{2}|\cos \beta - \cos \alpha|$.

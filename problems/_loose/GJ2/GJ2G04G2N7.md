On the plane of a circle with radius $R$, we erect a perpendicular at the center $O$ of the circle and measure the distance $OS = h$ on it.

$1^{\circ}$. Let two points on the circle be $A$ and $B$. Express the area of $\triangle SAB$ as a function of $\frac{1}{2} AB = x$.

$2^{\circ}$. If $A$ is a fixed point on the circle, how does the area of $\triangle SAB$ change as $B$ traces the circumference of the circle?

$3^{\circ}$. For $h \leq R$, construct the $\triangle SAB$ with maximum or minimum area. Show that the $\triangle SAB$ with maximum area is a right triangle!

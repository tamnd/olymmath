The total number of questions is $\mathrm{X}+\mathrm{Y}+\mathrm{Z}$.

Let's form a system of equations.
$\left\{\begin{array}{l}\mathrm{X}+\mathrm{Y}+\mathrm{Z}=80 \\ \mathrm{X} 1+\mathrm{Y} 1+\mathrm{Y} 3+\mathrm{Z}=55 \\ \mathrm{X} 2+\mathrm{Y} 1+\mathrm{Y} 2+\mathrm{Z}=50 \\ \mathrm{X} 3+\mathrm{Y} 2+\mathrm{Y} 3+\mathrm{Z}=45\end{array}\right.$

By adding the last three equations, we get

$\mathrm{X}+2 \mathrm{Y}+3 \mathrm{Z}=55+50+45$

$X-Z=2 * 80-55-50-45=10$

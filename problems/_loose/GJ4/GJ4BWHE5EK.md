Let $\alpha=\left(\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}\right)$ and $\beta=\left(\beta_{1}, \ldots, \beta_{n}\right)$ be two sets of exponents with equal sums.

Prove that, if $\alpha \neq \beta$, then for all non-negative $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$, the inequality $T_{\alpha}\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right) \geq$ $T_{\beta}\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ holds.

Definition of polynomials $T_{\alpha}$ see in problem $\underline{61417}$, the definition of comparison for exponents can be found in the reference manual.

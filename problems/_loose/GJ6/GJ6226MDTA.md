Given that $A_{1} 、 B_{1} 、 C_{1}$ are points on the sides $B C 、 C A 、 A B$ of the acute triangle $\triangle A B C$, and $A A_{1} 、 B B_{1} 、 C C_{1}$ are the angle bisectors of $\angle B A C 、 \angle C B A 、 \angle A C B$ respectively, $I$ is the incenter of $\triangle A B C$, and $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. Prove:
$$
A H+B H+C H \geqslant A I+B I+C I \text {. }
$$

Circle $\omega_{2}$ is tangent to the segments $CF,BF$ and it also touches the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ . Prove that the exsimilicenter of two circles $\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}$ lies on the radical axis of incircle and circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ .

Given a regular $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ inscribed in a unit circle and a point $P$ on the circumference of the circle. Prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left|P A_{k}\right|^{2}=2 n .
$$

Let $(\mathbb{K},+, \times, 0,1)$ be a commutative field and let $P(X)$ be a polynomial of degree $d \geqslant 0$, with coefficients in $\mathbb{K}$. Show that $P$ has at most $d$ roots, counted with their multiplicity, and that, if $P(X)$ is divisible by a non-constant polynomial $Q(X)^{2}$, then $Q(X)$ divides both $P(X)$ and $P^{\prime}(X)$

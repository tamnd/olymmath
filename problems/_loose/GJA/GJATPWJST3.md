Given a right triangle $ABC$ with hypotenuse $AB$ and longer leg $BC$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude $z$ from vertex $C$. The circle $k$ with center $D$ and radius $CD$ intersects the leg $BC$ at point $Q$ and the line $AB$ at points $E$ and $F (E \neq F)$, where $F$ is a point on the hypotenuse $AB$. The segment $QE$ intersects the leg $AC$ at point $P$. Prove that $|PE|=|QF|$.

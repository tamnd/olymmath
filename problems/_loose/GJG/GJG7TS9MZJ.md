Let $A$ and $B$ be two points in the plane different from the origin $O$, corresponding to the complex numbers $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$, respectively. Prove that
$$
\cos \angle A O B=\frac{t_{1} \overline{t_{2}}+\overline{t_{1}} t_{2}}{2\left|t_{1} t_{2}\right|}=\frac{\operatorname{Re}\left(\overline{t_{1}} t_{2}\right)}{\left|t_{1} t_{2}\right|}=\frac{\operatorname{Re}\left(t_{1} \overline{t_{2}}\right)}{\left|t_{1} t_{2}\right|} .
$$

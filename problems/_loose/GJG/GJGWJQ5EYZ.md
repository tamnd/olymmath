39 Color each point in the plane with one of 1993 colors, and ensure that points of each color exist. $T$ is any given triangle. Prove: There exists a triangle $T^{\prime}$ in the plane that is congruent to triangle $T$, such that on each pair of sides of $T^{\prime}$ there are points of the same color (excluding the vertices).

The vertices of a regular $(2 n+1)$-gon are marked with zeros and ones. There are $n+1$ zeros and $n$ ones in total. We will consider two such sets of markings to be the same if one can be obtained from the other by rotating the polygon.

a) Prove that the number of different sets of markings is $\frac{1}{2 n+1} C_{2 n+1}^{n}=\frac{1}{n+1} C_{2 n}^{n}$.

b) Prove that the number of different sets of markings is $c_{n}$.

There is a red card box and $k$ blue card boxes ($k>1$), and a deck of cards, totaling $2n$ cards, numbered from 1 to $2n$. Initially, these cards are stacked in the red card box in any order. From any card box, the top card can be taken out, placed in an empty box, or placed on top of a card with a number exactly one greater. For what largest $n$ can all the cards be moved to one of the blue card boxes through these operations?

Consider $\alpha \notin \mathbb{Q}$ (i.e., $\alpha$ cannot be written in the form $\frac{a}{b}$ with $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$). For example, we can take $\alpha=\sqrt{2}$.

Show that we can find $p, q \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $-\frac{1}{q^{2}} \leqslant \alpha-\frac{p}{q} \leqslant \frac{1}{q^{2}}$.

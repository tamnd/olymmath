We call a number "preferred" any three-digit natural number with distinct non-zero digits, which has the property that the product of its digits is a perfect square.

a) Write two "preferred" numbers that have the last digit 2.

b) How many "preferred" numbers exist?

c) Determine whether the sum of all "preferred" numbers is a perfect square.

(Gheorghe Radu, Olympiads and Mathematics Competitions V-VIII 2014, Bîrchi Publishing)

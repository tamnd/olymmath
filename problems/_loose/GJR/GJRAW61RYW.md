Let $f(x)$ be an irreducible polynomial of degree $n$ over a field $k$, and let $\alpha$ be one of its roots. Prove that if $\sum_{m=0}^{n-1} c_{m} \alpha^{m}=\sum_{m=0}^{n-1} d_{m} \alpha^{m}$, where $c_{0}, \ldots, c_{n-1}$ and $d_{0}, \ldots, d_{n-1}$ are numbers from the field $k$, then $c_{m}=d_{m}$ for $m=0,1, \ldots, n-1$.

(1998 Israel-Hungary Mathematical Competition Problem) Let the prime numbers be arranged in ascending order as $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$, $\cdots$, and let $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i}$. Prove that for any positive integer $n$, there exists a perfect square $a_{n}$ such that $S_{n}<a_{n}<S_{n+1}$.

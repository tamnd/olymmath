Let the two legs of a right triangle be $a, b$, the hypotenuse be $c$, and the altitude to the hypotenuse be $h$. Then, the shape of the triangle formed by $c+h, a+b, h$ as sides is
(A) a right triangle.
(B) an acute triangle.
(C) an obtuse triangle.
(D) cannot be determined, the shape depends on the sizes of $a, b, c$.
(Chinese "Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1989)

Let the vertices $A, B$ of $\triangle ABC$ be the two foci of the ellipse $\Gamma$, and point $C$ lies on the ellipse $\Gamma$. The eccentricity of the ellipse $\Gamma$ is $e$.
Prove: $\frac{1+\cos A \cos B}{\sin A \sin B}=\frac{1+e^{2}}{1-e^{2}}$.
(Li Hong)

Consider a graph $G$ with the following property: any induced subgraph $H$ of $G$ satisfies that each maximal clique of $H$ intersects with each maximal independent set of $H$.
(i) Prove: Such a graph $G$ is perfect.
(ii) Prove: Such a graph $G$ is exactly the graphs that do not contain an induced copy of $P^{3}$.

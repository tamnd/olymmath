On the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, $C_{1}$ are taken. Prove that

$$
\frac{A C_{1}}{C_{1} B} \cdot \frac{B A_{1}}{A_{1} C} \cdot \frac{C B_{1}}{B_{1} A}=\frac{\sin A C C_{1}}{\sin C_{1} C B} \cdot \frac{\sin B A A_{1}}{\sin A_{1} A C} \cdot \frac{\sin C B B_{1}}{\sin B_{1} B A}
$$

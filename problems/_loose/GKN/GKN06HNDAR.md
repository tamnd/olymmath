Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$ be such that the set $A= \{ a+b \alpha : a,b \in \mathbb{Z} \}$ is a ring with respect to the usual operations of $\mathbb{C}.$ If the ring $A$ has exactly four invertible elements, prove that $A= \mathbb{Z}*.$

In the Pythagorean multiplication table, a rectangular frame with a thickness of one square is selected. Each side of the frame consists of an odd number of squares. The squares of the frame are colored in a checkerboard pattern. Prove that the sum of the numbers in the black squares is equal to the sum of the numbers in the white squares.

(Pythagorean multiplication table is called a table in which the square of the $m$-th row and $n$-th column contains the product $m n$ for every pair of natural numbers $m, n$.)

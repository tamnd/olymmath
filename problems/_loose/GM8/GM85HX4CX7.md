For any $-1 \leq x \leq 1$, let $M(x)$ denote the maximum value at $x$ of those quadratic polynomials which are non-negative on the interval $[-1 ; 1]$ and whose integral over this interval is 1.

It is to be proven that $\min _{-1 \leq x \leq 1} M(x)=\frac{2}{3}$

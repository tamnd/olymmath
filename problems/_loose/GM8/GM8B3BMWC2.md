Let one vertex of the convex nonagon $T$ be denoted as $A$. Translate $T$ in the plane so that vertex $A$ successively coincides with the other 8 vertices of the original polygon, resulting in 8 congruent convex nonagons to $T$. Prove that among these 8 convex nonagons, there are always two that partially overlap.

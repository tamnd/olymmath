The Dobráks cultivated tulips in a square bed with a side of 6 meters. Later, they added a square terrace with a side of 7 meters to their cottage. One vertex of the terrace was exactly at the center of the tulip bed, and one side of the terrace divided the side of the tulip bed in the ratio $1: 5$.

In what ratio did the second side of the terrace divide the second side of the bed? By how many square meters did the tulip bed decrease due to the construction of the terrace?

(L. Hozová)

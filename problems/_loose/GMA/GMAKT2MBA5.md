(PNR, 79). Natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ when divided by some number $m \in \mathbf{N}$ give different remainders, and $n>m / 2$. Prove that for each number $k \in \mathbf{Z}$ there exist such indices

$$
i, j \in\{1 ; \ldots ; n\}
$$

(not necessarily different), that the number $a_{i}+a_{i}-k$ is divisible by $m$.

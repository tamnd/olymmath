On the hypotenuse $AB$ of the isosceles right triangle $ABC$, take two points $M$ and $N$ such that $\angle MCN=45^{\circ}$. Let $AM=m, MN=x, BN=n$. Then the shape of the triangle with side lengths $x, m, n$ is
(A) Acute triangle.
(B) Right triangle.
(C) Obtuse triangle.
(D) Varies with $x, m, n$.
(Anhui Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1997)

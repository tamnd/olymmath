Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{100}$ and $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_{100}$ be $200$ distinct real numbers. On a $100\times 100$ board, the number $a_i + b_j$ is written in the cell of row $i$ and column $j$ . It turns out that in each column, the product of the $100$ numbers is equal to $1$ . Prove that in each row, the product of the $100$ numbers is equal to $-1$ .

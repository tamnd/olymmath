Consider the prime numbers $p<q<r$ and the non-zero natural number $a<p$. The number $n$ is the smallest non-zero natural number which, when divided by $p, q$, and $r$, gives a remainder that is $a$ less than the divisor each time.

a) Determine, in terms of $p, q, r$, and $a$, the number $n$.

b) If $n=1000$, determine the possible values of $a$.

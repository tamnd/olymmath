Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$. The common external tangents to the circles $ABC$ and $ACD$
intersect at point $E$, to the circles $ABD$ and $BCD$ - at point $F$. Prove that if point $F$
lies on the line $AC$, then point $E$ lies on the line $BD$.

In one bag there are two cabbage-filled pies, in another two cherry-filled pies, and in the third - one with cabbage and one with cherry. The pies look and weigh the same, so it is unknown which is which. The grandson needs one pie for school. The grandmother wants to give him a cherry pie, but she herself got confused with her pies and can determine the filling only by breaking a pie. The grandson does not want a broken pie; he wants a whole one.

a) Show that the grandmother can act in such a way that the probability of giving the grandson a whole cherry pie is equal to $2 / 3$.

b) Is there a strategy that would make the probability of giving the grandson a whole cherry pie higher than $2 / 3$?

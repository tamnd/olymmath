In a certain country, there are 100 cities connected by a road network such that from any city to any other, there is only one way to travel without turning back. The network scheme is known, intersections and crossroads of the network are not necessarily cities, and every dead-end branch of the network must end at a city. A navigator can measure the distance between any two cities along this network. Can the total length of the entire road network be guaranteed to be determined with 100 such measurements?

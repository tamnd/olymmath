(GDR, 69; GDR, 71). Prove that if a polynomial $P(x)$ of degree $n$ with real coefficients has no real roots, then the polynomial

$$
Q(x)=P(x)+\alpha P^{\prime}(x)+\ldots+\alpha^{n} P^{(n)}(x)
$$

for any value of $\alpha \in \mathbf{R}$ also has no real roots.

In the acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $CA > CB$. A semicircle $\odot O$ is constructed with $AB$ as its diameter, intersecting $CA$ and $CB$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively. The angle bisector of $\angle EOF$ intersects the angle bisector of $\angle ACB$ at point $S$. Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle AES$ and the circumcircle of $\triangle BFS$ intersect at another point on $AB$.

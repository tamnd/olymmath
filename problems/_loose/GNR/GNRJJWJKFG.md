41-4 (C52, Hungary) A magician has 100 cards, each labeled with a number from 1 to 100. He places these 100 cards into three boxes, one red, one white, and one blue. Each box must contain at least one card. A spectator then selects two of the three boxes, picks one card from each of the selected boxes, and announces the sum of the numbers on the two cards. Knowing this sum, the magician can identify which box was not selected. How many ways are there to place the cards so that the trick always works? (Two methods are considered different if at least one card is placed in a different colored box.)

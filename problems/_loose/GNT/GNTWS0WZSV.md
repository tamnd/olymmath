Prove that if the numbers $ x_1 $ and $ x_2 $ are roots of the equation $ x^2 + px - 1 = 0 $ , where $ p $ is an odd number, then for every natural $n$ number $ x_1^n + x_2^n $ and $ x_1^{n+1} + x_2^{n+1} $ are integer and coprime.

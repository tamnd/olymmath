$\log _{b} N=\frac{\log _{a} N}{\log _{a} b}(N>0, a>0, b>0, a \neq 1, b \neq 1)$.

This property is usually called the change of base rule.

We recommend proving these properties.

There are quite a number of problems where the variable $x$ or some function of it is under the logarithm sign. Before solving such problems, it is necessary to impose the necessary restrictions on these variables.

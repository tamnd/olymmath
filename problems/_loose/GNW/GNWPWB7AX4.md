In a $100 \times 100$ table, ten thousand numbers are written arbitrarily. Let $a_{1}$ denote the sum of the numbers in the first column, $a_{2}$ the sum of the numbers in the second column, and so on, with $a_{100}$ the sum of the numbers in the hundredth column. Furthermore, let $b_{1}$ denote the sum of the numbers in the first row, $b_{2}$ the sum of the numbers in the second row, and so on, with $b_{100}$ the sum of the numbers in the hundredth row. Calculate the value of the expression:

$$
\left(a_{1}-b_{1}\right)+\left(a_{2}-b_{2}\right)+\ldots+\left(a_{100}-b_{100}\right)
$$

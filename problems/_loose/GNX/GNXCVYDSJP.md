Dani are the equations $(y+3)^{2}-x(x-4)=7+(y-x)(y+x)$ and $x+\frac{y-1}{2}=\frac{x}{2}-y$.

a) Solve the system of equations and write the solution as a point $A$ in the coordinate system.

b) Write the equation of the line that passes through point $A$ and has the same $x$-intercept as the line $3 x+2 y-15=0$ in explicit, implicit, and intercept form.

21st Mathematics Knowledge Competition for Students of Secondary Technical and Vocational Schools, Selection Competition, April 22, 2021, Problems for 3rd Year

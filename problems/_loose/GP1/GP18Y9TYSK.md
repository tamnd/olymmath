Every natural number $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ can be represented as a sum where each summand is either 1 or 2. For example, for the number 3, considering the order, there are exactly the representations

$$
3=1+1+1=1+2=2+1
$$

(a) Give all representations for each of the numbers 4, 5, and 6 as well!

(b) How many representations are there for each of the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, respectively?

Find a pattern that holds for these six counts!

How many representations must there be for the number 10 if the pattern you mentioned holds generally?

In $\triangle A B C$, $B D, C E$ are the altitudes on sides $A C, A B$, respectively, then $\frac{D E}{B C}$ equals
(A) $\frac{A E}{A B}$.
(B) $\sin A$.
(C) $\cos A$.
(D) $|\cos A|$.
("Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1988)

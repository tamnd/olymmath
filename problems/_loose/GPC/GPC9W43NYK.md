Let's call a partition any side of some unit square on a grid sheet of paper. Can several non-intersecting partitions be marked in a 7 by 7 square of cells so that the projections of all marked partitions onto the horizontal and vertical sides of the square completely cover them? Partitions do not intersect if they do not have common points (ends). Marked partitions can be located both inside the 7 by 7 square and on its boundary.

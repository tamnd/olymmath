Let $\triangle A B C$ be given and let $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$, and $C C^{\prime}$ be the medians of the triangle intersecting at point $T$, and it is given that $\overline{B A^{\prime}}=\overline{A^{\prime} T}$. On the extension of $C C^{\prime}$, we choose a point $C^{\prime \prime}$ such that $\overline{C^{\prime} C^{\prime \prime}}=\frac{\overline{C C^{\prime}}}{3}$, and on the extension of $B^{\prime}$, we choose a point $B^{\prime \prime}$ such that $\overline{B^{\prime} B^{\prime \prime}}=\frac{\overline{B B^{\prime}}}{3}$. Prove that quadrilateral $TB^{\prime \prime}AC^{\prime \prime}$ is a rectangle.

Maximum 15 points. Masha was given a chest with multicolored beads (each bead has a unique color, there are a total of $\mathrm{n}$ beads in the chest). Masha chose seven beads for her dress and decided to try all possible combinations of them on the dress (thus, Masha selects from a set of options to sew one, two, three, four, five, six, or seven beads, and the order of the beads does not matter to her). Then she counted how many options she got and was very surprised that the number turned out to be odd.
1) What number did Masha get?
2) Is it true that if Masha had chosen from an even number of beads, she could have gotten an even number of options?
3) Is it true that if the order of the beads sewn on the dress mattered to Masha, she could have gotten both even and odd numbers?

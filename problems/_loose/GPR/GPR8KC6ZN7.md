(EGMO 2021 P3) Let $ABC$ be a triangle with an obtuse angle at $A$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the points of intersection of the external bisector of angle $\widehat{BAC}$ with the altitudes from vertices $B$ and $C$, respectively. Let $M$ be a point on the segment $[EC]$ such that $\widehat{EMA}=\widehat{ACB}$. Let $N$ be a point on the segment $[FB]$ such that $\widehat{ANF}=\widehat{ABC}$. Show that the points $E, M, N$ and $F$ are concyclic.

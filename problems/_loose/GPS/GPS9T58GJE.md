Let $A, B, C, D$ be any four points on a plane; $\alpha=\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ be the two smallest of the 12 angles formed by these points, and $\beta=\beta_{1}$ and $\beta_{2}$ be the two largest angles (so that $\alpha=. \alpha_{1} \leqslant \alpha_{2} \leqslant \ldots \leqslant \leqslant \beta_{2} \leqslant \beta_{1}=\beta$). Prove that

a) $0 \leqslant \alpha_{2} \leqslant 45^{\circ}$

b) $72^{\circ} \leqslant \beta_{2} \leqslant 180^{\circ}$.

For which quadruples of points $A, B, C, D$ are the values $\alpha_{2}=45^{\circ}$ and $\beta_{2}=72^{\circ}$ achieved?

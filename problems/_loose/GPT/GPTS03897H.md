(ii) All pieces are identical, and pieces must be placed in a cell, with only one piece allowed per cell;

(1) If two players, A and B, play the game, with A going first, does A have a winning strategy?
(2) If three players, A, B, and C, play the game, with A going first, B second, and C third, and if B and C can discuss their strategy beforehand, does B have a winning strategy?

Let $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R}_{+} \backslash\{1\}$ and $x, y, z, t \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $a^{x}=b c d, b^{y}=c d a, c^{z}=d a b, d^{t}=a b c$. Prove that:

$$
\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{1}{1+y}+\frac{1}{1+z}+\frac{1}{1+t}=1
$$

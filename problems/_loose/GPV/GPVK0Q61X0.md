Given a triangle $ABC$. On the side $AB$, there is a point $P$ that moves. From $P$, we draw parallels to $BC$ and $AC$. The first one intersects the side $AC$ at $A'$, and the second one intersects the side $BC$ at $B'$. Find the geometric locus of the second intersection point $M$ of the circumcircles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ of triangles $AA'P$ and $BB'P$.

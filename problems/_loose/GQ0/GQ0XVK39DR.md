Given the triangle $ABC$ with $T$ is its *Fermat-Torricelli* point. Let $(N_a)$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle TBC$. Choose a point $X$ on $(N_a)$ such that $TX$ is perpendicular to $BC$. The segment $BC$ intersects $(TN_aX)$ at $D$. Similar definition of points $Y, Z, E, F$. The reflection lines of the *Euler* line of $\triangle ABC$ wrt $BC, CA, AB$ intersect $XD, YE, ZF$ at $P, Q, R$, respectively.

Prove that: $AP$ is perpendicular to $QR$ if and only if $AB = AC$ or $2BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2$.

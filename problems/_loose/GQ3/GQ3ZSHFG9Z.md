Let $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}$ be a sequence of non-negative real numbers. Prove: for any positive integer $n$, the inequality $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}$ $\leqslant 4 \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}^{2}$ holds, where $x_{i}$ is the arithmetic mean of $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{i}$, i.e., $x_{i}=\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{i}}{i}$.

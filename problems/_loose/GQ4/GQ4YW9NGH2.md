In the plane of triangle $A B C$, a point $M$ is given. This point starts moving in a straight line towards vertex $A$, but stops halfway and begins moving towards vertex $B$, again stopping halfway and moving towards vertex $C$, stopping halfway again, and continues this movement towards vertex $A$ and so on. Prove that with unlimited continuation of such movement, the point will move infinitely close to the contour of a uniquely determined limiting triangle. Construct this triangle and calculate the ratio of its area to the area of the original triangle $A B C$.

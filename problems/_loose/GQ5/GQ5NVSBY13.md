22 In the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, $AB < AD$, $\angle ABC = \angle ADC = 90^{\circ}$, points $E$ and $G$ are both on $BC$ ($E$ is between $B$ and $G$), and point $F$ is on $DC$, satisfying:
$$
\frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{BE}{DF} = \frac{EG}{FC}.
$$

Prove that $AG$, $BF$, and $DE$ are concurrent.

Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be real numbers such that

$$
(n-1)\left(x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\ldots+x_{n}^{2}\right)=\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{n}\right)^{2}
$$

Prove that the numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$ are all non-positive or all non-negative.

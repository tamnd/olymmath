The four sides of a convex quadrilateral have lengths $a, b, c, d$, and the lengths of the two diagonals are $e, f$, then the area of the quadrilateral is
$$
S=\frac{1}{4} \sqrt{4 e^{2} f^{2}-\left(a^{2}+c^{2}-b^{2}-d^{2}\right)^{2}}
$$

Let vertices $B$ and $C$ of a triangle be fixed, and vertex $A$ move such that the Brocard angle $\varphi$ of triangle $A B C$ remains constant. Then point $A$ moves along a circle of radius $(a / 2) \sqrt{\operatorname{ctg}^{2} \varphi-3}$, where $a=B C$ (Neuberg circle).

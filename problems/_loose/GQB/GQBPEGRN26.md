(Equality of Quotients) Let $(G, *)$ be a finite group, $H \subseteq G$ a subgroup of $G$, and $x, y$ two elements of $G$. Show the equivalence between the following propositions:

1. $x * H = y * H$;
2. $(x * H) \cap (y * H) \neq \emptyset$;
3. $x^{-1} * y \in H$.

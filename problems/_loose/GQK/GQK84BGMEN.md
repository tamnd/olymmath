\begin{align*}x_1^2+x_2^2+\cdots+x_{1985}^2&=y^3,\\
x_1^3+x_2^3+\cdots+x_{1985}^3&=z^2\end{align*}
with distinct integers $x_1$, $x_2$, $\ldots$, $x_{1985}$.

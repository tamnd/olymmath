Let $ABC$ be a triangle with area $S$. We denote $a=BC, b=CA, c=AB$ the lengths of its sides, and $a'=AA', b'=BB', c'=CC'$ the lengths of its medians, where $A', B', C'$ are the midpoints of $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. Let $R$ be the radius of the circumcircle of $ABC$. Show that $aa'+bb'+cc' \leq 2S + R(a+b+c)$ if $ABC$ is acute (i.e., if all angles of $ABC$ are less than $90^\circ$). Is this inequality also true for all obtuse triangles (where one angle is greater than $90^\circ$)?

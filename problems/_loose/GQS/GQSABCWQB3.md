Let $a,b,c$ be real nonzero numbers such that $a+b+c=12$ and $$\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}+\frac{1}{abc}=1.$$ Compute the largest possible value of $abc-\left(a+2b-3c\right)$ .

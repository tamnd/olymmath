Do there exist natural numbers $a, b$, and $c$ such that each of the equations

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
a x^{2}+b x+c=0, & a x^{2}+b x-c=0 \\
a x^{2}-b x+c=0, & a x^{2}-b x-c=0
\end{array}
$$

has both roots as integers?

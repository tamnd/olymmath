Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with circumcenter $O$ . Let $D$ and $E$ be points on the circumcircle of $ABC$ such that $AD$ is perpendicular to $BC$ and $BE$ is perpendicular to $CA$ . Segment $CD$ intersects the circle with diameter $DO$ at $P$ , while segment $CE$ intersects the circle with diameter $EO$ at $Q$ .
Prove that $OP=OQ$ .

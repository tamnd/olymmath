What is the geometric locus of points $P$ in space from which three tangent planes can be drawn to a given sphere of radius $r$, such that these planes are pairwise perpendicular to each other?

The three points of tangency determine a circle $k$ on the sphere. Determine:

a) the surface area of the smaller spherical cap associated with $k$,
b) the surface area of the conical frustum determined by $k$ and $P$.

How do these two surface areas compare to each other?

In a parallelogram $A B C D$, let $P$ be any point on the diagonal $A C (P \neq A, P \neq C)$. The line through $P$ parallel to $A B$ intersects $B C$ at $H$ and $A D$ at $G$; the line through $P$ parallel to $B C$ intersects $A B$ at $E$ and $C D$ at $F$.

Prove that the two parallelograms $E B H P$ and $G P F D$ have the same area!

Someone constructs a square $A B C D$ with the diagonal length $A C=10 \mathrm{~cm}$. Then he chooses an arbitrary point $E$ on the side $A B$ and constructs the intersection point $F$ of $B C$ with the line through $E$ parallel to $A C$, the intersection point $G$ of $C D$ with the line through $F$ parallel to $B D$, and the intersection point $H$ of $A D$ with the line through $G$ parallel to $A C$.

a) Prove that every such constructed quadrilateral $E F G H$ is a rectangle!

b) Determine the perimeter for every such constructed quadrilateral!

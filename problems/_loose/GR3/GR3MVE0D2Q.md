Let $O x, O y, O z, O t$ be four different rays in three-dimensional space, and the angle between any two of them is equal. (1) Find this angle;
(2) Let $O r$ be a ray different from the above four, and let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ be the angles formed between $O r$ and $O x, O y, O z, O t$, respectively, and let $p=\cos \alpha+\cos \beta+\cos \gamma+\cos \delta ; q=\cos ^{2} \alpha+\cos ^{2} \beta+\cos ^{2} \gamma+\cos ^{2} \delta$.
Prove: When $O r$ rotates around point $O$, the values of $p, q$ remain unchanged.

Let $a_{ij} (i=1,2, \cdots, m; j=1,2, \cdots, n)$ be real numbers. Prove:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(a_{11}^{n}+a_{21}^{n}+\cdots+a_{m 1}^{n}\right)\left(a_{12}^{n}+a_{22}^{n}+\cdots+a_{m 2}^{n}\right) \cdots\left(a_{1 n}^{n}+a_{2 n}^{n}+\cdots+a_{m n}^{n}\right) \\
\geqslant & \left(a_{11} a_{12} \cdots a_{1 n}+a_{21} a_{22} \cdots a_{2 n}+\cdots+a_{m 1} a_{m 2} \cdots a_{m n}\right)^{n} .
\end{aligned}
$$

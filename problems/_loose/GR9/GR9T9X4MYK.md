Let $G=(-k, k)$ where $k>0$ and the operation $x * y=\frac{k^{2}(x+y)}{k^{2}+x y}$.

a) Show that G is a stable subset with respect to the operation *.

b) Let $f: R \rightarrow G, f(x)=\frac{1}{2 k} \ln \frac{x+k}{x-k}$. Show that f is an isomorphism between the groups $(R,+)$ and $(G, *)$.

Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $A^{\prime}$ be the symmetric point of $A$ with respect to $B C$; let $D A A^{\prime}$ be similar to $A B C$ and let $D^{\prime}$ be the symmetric point of $D$ with respect to $A A^{\prime}$. Given that the product of the areas of the quadrilaterals $A B A^{\prime} C$ and $A D A^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is 16, we can say that $A A^{\prime} \ldots$
(A) is 1
(B) is $2 \sqrt[4]{2}$
(C) is 2
(D) is $2 \sqrt{2}$
(E) is not uniquely determined by the data. (Note: the similarity between $D A A^{\prime}$ and $A B C$ should be understood in an ordered manner: $D A / A B = A A^{\prime} / B C = A^{\prime} D / C A$)

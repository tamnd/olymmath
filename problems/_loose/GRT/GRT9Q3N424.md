Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle with base $BC$, and let $M$ be any point on the base $BC$. The perpendicular projection of $M$ onto $AB$ is $H$, and onto $AC$ is $K$. Prove that

a) the perpendicular bisector of $HK$ always passes through a point independent of $M$;

b) the perpendicular from $M$ to $HK$ also always passes through a fixed point.

Let $a$ , $b$ , $c$ and $d$ be real numbers such that $a+b+c+d=0$ and the sum of any two of them is nonzero. Prove that the following inequality holds: $$ \frac{ab}{(a+b)^2} + \frac{bc}{(b+c)^2} + \frac{cd}{(c+d)^2}
+ \frac{da}{(d+a)^2} + \frac{ac}{(a+c)^2} + \frac{bd}{(b+d)^2}
\leq -\frac{3}{2}. $$ When does equality occur?

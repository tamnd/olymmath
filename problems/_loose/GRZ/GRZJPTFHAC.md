Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a group with the identity element $e$ and $H$ a proper subgroup of $G$.

i) If $a \in G \backslash H$, denote $a H=\{a h \mid h \in H\}$. Prove that $H \cap a H=\emptyset$.

ii) If $G$ is commutative and $a \in G \backslash H$ satisfies $a^{2}=e$, then $K=H \cup a H$ is a subgroup of $G$.

Mathematical Gazette 1986

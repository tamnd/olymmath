To prove the following divisibility rule for 37. To determine if a number is divisible by 37, it should be divided from right to left into groups of three digits. If the sum of the resulting three-digit numbers is divisible by 37, then the given number is also divisible by 37. (The term "three-digit" is used conditionally: some of the groups may start with zeros and be actually two-digit or smaller; the leftmost group will not be three-digit if the number of digits in our number is not a multiple of 3.$)$

In isosceles $\wedge A B C$, $A B=A C$, $\odot O$ is the incircle of $\triangle A B C$, touching the sides $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ at points $K$, $L$, and $M$ respectively. Let $N$ be the intersection of line $O L$ and $K M$, $Q$ be the intersection of line $B N$ and $C A$, and $P$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to line $B Q$. If $B P=A P+2 P Q$, find all possible values of $\frac{A B}{B C}$.

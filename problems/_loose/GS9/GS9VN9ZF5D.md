Find all triples of nonnegative real numbers $(x, y, z)$ that satisfy the equality:
$$
\frac{\left(x^2 - y\right)(1 - y)}{(x - y)^2} + \frac{\left(y^2 - z\right)(1 - z)}{(y - z)^2} + \frac{\left(z^2 - x\right)(1 - x)}{(z - x)^2} = 3
$$

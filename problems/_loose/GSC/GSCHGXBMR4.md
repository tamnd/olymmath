At the base of the pyramid lies a rectangle with an acute angle between the diagonals $\alpha\left(\alpha<60^{\circ}\right.$ ), the lateral edges are equal to each other, and the height is $h$. Inside this pyramid, there is a triangular pyramid whose vertex coincides with the vertex of the first pyramid, and the vertices of its base lie one each on three sides of the rectangle. Find the volume of the quadrilateral pyramid if all the edges of the triangular pyramid are equal to each other, and the lateral faces are equal in area.

In $\triangle A B C$, we have the trigonometric identity $\sin A+\sin B+\sin C=4 \cos \frac{A}{2} \cos \frac{B}{2} \cos \frac{C}{2}$. What about the converse?

That is: If $A, B, C \in (0, \pi)$, and $\sin A+\sin B+\sin C=4 \cos \frac{A}{2} \cos \frac{B}{2} \cos \frac{C}{2}$, can $A, B, C$ be the three interior angles of some triangle?

Five, let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ all be positive numbers, prove:
$$
\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{x_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}}+\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{x_{1}} \geqslant x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n} \text {. }
$$

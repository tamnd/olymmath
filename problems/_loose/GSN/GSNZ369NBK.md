At noon, Vasya put 10 paper-cut convex decagons on the table. Then, from time to time, he took scissors, cut one of the polygons lying on the table along a straight line into two, and put both resulting pieces back on the table. By midnight, Vasya had performed this operation 51 times. Prove that by midnight, among the polygons lying on the table, there was a triangle or a quadrilateral. (I. Rubanov)

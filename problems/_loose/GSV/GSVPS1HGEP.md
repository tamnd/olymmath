Agent 007 must press two keys simultaneously to infiltrate the enemy base, and if he makes a mistake, he will trigger the alarm. However, he only knows that the two keys are not adjacent (i.e., they do not share a side or a vertex). What is the probability that he will succeed in infiltrating?
(A) $\frac{1}{64}$
(B) $\frac{1}{78}$
(C) $\frac{1}{128}$
(D) $\frac{1}{156}$
(E) $\frac{1}{160}$.

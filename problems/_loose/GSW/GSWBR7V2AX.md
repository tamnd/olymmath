Given the nonincreasing sequence of non-negative numbers in which $a_1 \ge a_2 \ge a_3 \ge ... \ge a_{2n-1}\ge 0$ .

Prove that $a^2_1 -a^2_2 + a^2_3 - ... + a^2_{2n- l} \ge (a_1 - a_2 + a_3 - ... + a_{2n- l} )^2$ .

( L . Kurlyandchik , Leningrad )

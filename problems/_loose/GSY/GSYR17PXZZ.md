a natural number $x$ is called good if $x$ is the product of an even number of (not necessarily different) prime numbers. Let $a, b$ be natural numbers and define $m(x)=(x+a)(x+b)$.

(a) Prove that natural numbers $a, b$ exist such that $m(1), m(2), \ldots, m(2010)$ are all good numbers.

(b) If $m(x)$ is good for every natural number $x$, then $a=b$.

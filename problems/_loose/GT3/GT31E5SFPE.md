For a finite group $G$ we denote by $n(G)$ the number of elements of the group and by $s(G)$ the number of subgroups of it.
Decide whether the following statements are true or false.

a) For every $a>0$ the is a finite group $G$ with $\frac{n(G)}{s(G)}<a.$ b) For every $a>0$ the is a finite group $G$ with $\frac{n(G)}{s(G)}>a.$

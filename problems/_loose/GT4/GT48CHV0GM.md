In a convex hexagon $A B C D E F$, the diagonals $A D, B E$, and $C F$ are equal. Let $P$ be the point of intersection of diagonals $A D$ and $C F$, $R$ be the point of intersection of diagonals $B E$ and $C F$, and $Q$ be the point of intersection of diagonals $A D$ and $B E$. It is known that $A P=P F$, $B R=C R$, and $D Q=E Q$. Prove that the points $A, B, C, D, E$, and $F$ lie on the same circle.

a) There is an unlimited set of cards with the words "abc", "bca", "cab". From them, a word is formed according to the following rule. Any card is chosen as the initial word, and then at each step, a card can be attached to the existing word either on the left or on the right, or the word can be cut at any point (between letters) and a card can be inserted there.

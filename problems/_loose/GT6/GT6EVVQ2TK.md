Points $A$ and $B$ lie on a hyperbola. The line $A B$ intersects the asymptotes of the hyperbola at points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$.

a) Prove that $A A_{1}=B B_{1}$ and $A B_{1}=B A_{1}$.

b) Prove that if the line $A_{1} B_{1}$ is tangent to the hyperbola at point $X$, then $X$ is the midpoint of segment $A_{1}, B_{1}$.

The circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively, are externally tangent at point $C$, while the circle $k$ with center $O$ is externally tangent to $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$. Let $\ell$ be the common tangent to $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ at point $C$ and $[A B]$ the diameter of $k$ perpendicular to $\ell$. Assume that $O_{1}$ and $A$ are on the same side of $\ell$. Show that the lines $\left(A O_{2}\right),\left(B O_{1}\right)$ and $\ell$ are concurrent.

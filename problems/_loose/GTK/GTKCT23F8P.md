The domain $D$ of the function $f(x)$ is symmetric about the origin, but does not include the number 0. For any number $x \in D$, there exist $x_{1}, x_{2} \in D$, such that $x=x_{1}-x_{2}, f(x) \neq f\left(x_{2}\right)$, and the following conditions are satisfied:
(1) If $x_{1}, x_{2} \in D, f(x) \neq f\left(x_{2}\right)$ or $00$.

Prove: (1) $f(x)$ is an odd function;
(2) $f(x)$ is a periodic function, and find its period;
(3) $f(x)$ is a decreasing function on the interval $(0,4a)$.

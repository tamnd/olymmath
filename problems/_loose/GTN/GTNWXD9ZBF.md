Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid, $A D / / B C, A D=a, B C=b$, the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at $P_{1}$, draw $P_{1} Q_{1} / / A D$ intersecting $C D$ at $Q_{1}$, connect $A Q_{1}$, intersecting $B D$ at $P_{2}$, draw $P_{2} Q_{2} / / A D$ intersecting $C D$ at $Q_{2}$, connect $A Q_{2}$ intersecting $B D$ at $P_{3}, \cdots$, if this continues, let the length of $P_{n} Q_{n}$ be $x_{n}$.
(1) Express $x_{n}$ in terms of $a, b, n$; (2) Prove that $x_{n} \leqslant \frac{a+n b}{4 n}$.

In the country, there are roads with one-way traffic. The King goes for a walk along one of the roads every day, disregarding traffic rules and not walking the same road two days in a row. The Duke, on even days of the month, drives along the road according to the rules, and on odd days, he drives against the rules (unlike the King, he can travel back and forth on the same road for two consecutive days). It is known that the Duke can travel indefinitely. Prove that the King can also do so (by correctly choosing the starting city).

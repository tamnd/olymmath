Let the base of a regular quadrilateral pyramid $P$ be a square with side length 2, and the height be $h$. A plane $\pi$ is parallel to one of the diagonals of the square, and intersects the base of $P$ at an angle $\alpha$. When $P$ is orthogonally projected onto $\pi$, for what value of $\alpha$ is the area of the resulting figure maximized? What is the maximum value?

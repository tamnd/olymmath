In each cell of a square table with $M \times M$ cells, there is either a natural number or zero. If a zero is at the intersection of a row and a column, then the sum of the numbers in that row and that column is not less than $M$. Prove that the sum of all numbers in the table is not less than $\frac{M^{2}}{2}$.

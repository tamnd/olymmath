Let $A B C$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of $B C$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the orthogonal projections of $D$ respectively on $(A B)$ and $(A C)$. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the circle centered at $D$ passing through $E$ (and therefore through $F$). Let $M \in[A B]$ and $N \in[A C]$ such that $(M N)$ is a tangent to $\mathcal{C}$. Show that $B D^{2}=B M \cdot C N$

In a classroom, 24 light fixtures were installed, each capable of holding 4 bulbs. When some of the fixtures were filled with four bulbs, it became clear that the available stock would be insufficient. Thereafter, they started to screw in the bulbs in groups of three, then in pairs, and finally individually. Unfortunately, even this way, there were still fixtures that did not receive any bulbs. How many bulbs were missing if twice as many fixtures received one bulb as those that received four, and half as many fixtures received no bulbs at all as those that received three?

Let $a_1$, $a_2$, ..., $a_{2004}$ be non-negative real numbers such that $a_1+...+ a_{2004} \le 25$. Prove that among them there exist at least two numbers $a_i$ and $a_j$ ($i \neq j$) such that
$|\sqrt{a_i}-\sqrt{a_j}| \le \frac{5}{2003}$.

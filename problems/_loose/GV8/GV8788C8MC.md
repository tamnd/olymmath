Let $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, a+2 b+c=2$, and $\lambda \geqslant \frac{1}{2}$, prove that:
$$
(a+b)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}+(b+c)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}} \geqslant 2(a+b)(b+c) .
$$

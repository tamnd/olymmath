In a plane, let $G$ be the set of all points whose Cartesian coordinates are integers.

Furthermore, let $F$ be the set of 1988 different colors.

Prove: For any distribution of colors where each point in $G$ is assigned exactly one color from $F$, there exist four points in $G$ of the same color that are the vertices of a rectangle with sides parallel to the axes.

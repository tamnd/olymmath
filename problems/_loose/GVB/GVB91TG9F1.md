Some positive integers, not necessarily distinct, are written on a board. Prove that Elmo can write additional positive integers on the board so that

- he writes no number more than twice
- the square of the sum of the resulting numbers on the board is equal to the sum of the cubes of the numbers.

*Seongjin Shim*

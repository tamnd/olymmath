The ant starts from a point $X$ on one of the shorter sides. The first time the ant reaches the opposite side, it arrives at the midpoint. What is the distance, in centimetres, from $X$ to the nearest corner of the rectangle?
(A) 3
(B) 4
(C) 6
(D) 8
(E) 9

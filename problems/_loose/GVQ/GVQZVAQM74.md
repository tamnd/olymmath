Starting from the regular pentagon $A B C D E$, the points $F, G, H, J, K$ were determined under the following conditions. Are these five points on a straight line, or is this only approximately the case?
(1) $F D \perp D C, F B \perp B D$;
(2) $G D \perp D E, G A \perp A D$;
(3) $H A \perp A C, H B \perp B E$;
(4) $J H\|A B\| H K$,
(5) $J A \perp C D, K B \perp D E$.

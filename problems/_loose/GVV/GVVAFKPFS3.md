Let two external circles $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ with centers $\mathrm{O}$ and $\mathrm{O}^{\prime}$. We draw two parallel radii of the same direction $[O M]$ and $\left[O^{\prime} M^{\prime}\right]$, and two other parallel radii of the same direction $[OP]$ and $\left[\mathrm{O}^{\prime} \mathrm{P}^{\prime}\right]$. The line $\left(M \mathrm{M}^{\prime}\right)$ intersects $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ at $\mathrm{N}$ and the line $(\mathrm{PP}^{\prime})$ intersects $\mathcal{C}^{\prime}$ at $\mathrm{Q}$. Show that $\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}, \mathrm{Q}$ are concyclic.

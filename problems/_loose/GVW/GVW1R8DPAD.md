Among the students admitted to the University of Peoples' Friendship, exactly 50 people know English, exactly 50 people know French, and exactly 50 people know Spanish. Prove that the students can be divided into 5 (not necessarily equal) groups such that in each group there are exactly 10 people who know English, exactly 10 people who know French, and exactly 10 people who know Spanish. (It is assumed that some students may either not know any of these languages or know any number of them.)

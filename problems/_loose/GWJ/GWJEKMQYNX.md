Several people gather, some of whom know each other. It is known that if two people in the gathering have the same number of acquaintances, then they do not have any common acquaintances. Prove: If there is someone in the gathering who has at least 2008 acquaintances, then there must also be someone who has exactly 2008 acquaintances.

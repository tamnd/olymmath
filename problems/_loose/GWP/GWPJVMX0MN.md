The following theorem is true: The sum of the squares of the distances from the vertices of a regular $n$-gon to any point taken on the circle circumscribed around it is a constant value, independent of the position of the point on the circle (it is equal to $2 n R^{2}$, where $R$ is the radius of the circle).

Prove this theorem for $n=6$. (U. 123, P. 178.)

The infinite sequence of positive integers $\{f(n)\}$ satisfies $\frac{f(n-1)+f(n+1)}{2} \leqslant f(n)\left(n \geqslant 2, n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}\right)$. Let the coordinates of point $P_{n}$ in the coordinate plane be $(n, f(n))$. Prove that there exists a line that passes through infinitely many points in the set $P_{n}$.

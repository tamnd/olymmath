Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $P$ be a point in the plane. The orthogonal projections of $P$ onto the sides $[AB], [BC], [AC]$ are denoted by $Q, R, S$ respectively. Show that $P$ is on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ if and only if $P, Q, R$ are collinear. It is said that $Q, R, S$ are collinear on the Simson line.

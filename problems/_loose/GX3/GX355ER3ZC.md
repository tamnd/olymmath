Let the circumcenter of $\triangle Z_{1} Z_{2} Z_{3}$ be $Z$, and the corresponding complex numbers be $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z$, respectively. Prove:
$$
z=\frac{\left|z_{1}\right|^{2}\left(z_{2}-z_{3}\right)+\left|z_{2}\right|^{2}\left(z_{3}-z_{1}\right)+\left|z_{3}\right|^{2}\left(z_{1}-z_{2}\right)}{\overline{z_{1}}\left(z_{2}-z_{3}\right)+\overline{z_{2}}\left(z_{3}-z_{1}\right)+\overline{z_{3}}\left(z_{1}-z_{2}\right)}
$$

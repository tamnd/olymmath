Let $U=\{1,2,3, \cdots, n\}(n \geqslant 3), S$ be a subset of $U$. If an element not in $S$ appears somewhere in a permutation $P$ of $U$, and in this permutation $P$ it is between two elements of $S$, then we say $S$ is split by the permutation $P$ (for example, the permutation 13542 can split the subset $\{1,2,3\}$, but cannot split the subset $\{3,4,5\}$). Try to find the maximum positive integer $m$, such that for any $m$ subsets of $U$ (each subset has at least 2 elements and at most $n-1$ elements), there exists a permutation of $U$ that splits all these $m$ subsets.

Given in the plane the square $ABCD$ , the square $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ , smaller than the first, and a quadrilateral $PQRS$ that satisfy the following conditions $\bullet$ $ABCD$ and $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ have a common center and respectively parallel sides. $\bullet$ $P$ , $Q$ , $R$ , $S$ belong one to each side of the square $ABCD$ . $\bullet$ $A_1$ , $B_1$ , $C_1$ , $D_1$ belong one to each side of the quadrilateral $PQRS$ .
Prove that $PQRS$ is a square.

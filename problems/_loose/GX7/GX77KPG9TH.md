Let $F=F(x)$ be a distribution function. Show that for any $a>0$ the functions

$$
G(x)=\frac{1}{a} \int_{x}^{x+a} F(u) d u \text { and } H(x)=\frac{1}{2 a} \int_{x-a}^{x+a} F(u) d u
$$

are also distribution functions.

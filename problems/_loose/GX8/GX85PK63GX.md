Let two circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$ be positioned such that they intersect at two different points $A, B$ and that the connecting segment $M_{1} M_{2}$ of the two circle centers is intersected by the segment $A B$ at a point lying between $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$.

Among all those lines that pass through $A$ and also intersect the circle $k_{1}$ at a point $P$ different from $A$ and $B$ and the circle $k_{2}$ at a point $Q$ different from $A$ and $B$, we are now looking for a line for which the segment $P Q$ is as long as possible.

Investigate whether such a line exists, whether it is then uniquely determined by the circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$, and, if this is the case, what position this line then has.

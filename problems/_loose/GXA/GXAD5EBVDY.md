Let $ABCD$ be a tetrahedron and $A'$ , $B'$ , $C'$ be arbitrary points on the edges $[DA]$ , $[DB]$ , $[DC]$ , respectively. One considers the points $P_c \in [AB]$ , $P_a \in [BC]$ , $P_b \in [AC]$ and $P'_c \in [A'B']$ , $P'_a \in [B'C']$ , $P'_b \in [A'C']$ such that $$ \frac{P_cA}{P_cB}= \frac{P'_cA'}{P'_cB'}=\frac{AA'}{BB'}\,\, , \,\,\,\frac{P_aB}{P_aC}= \frac{P'_aB'}{P'_aC'}=\frac{BB'}{CC'}\,\, , \,\, \frac{P_bC}{P_bA}= \frac{P'_bC'}{P'_bA'}=\frac{CC'}{AA'} $$ Prove that:

a) the lines $AP_a,$ $BP_b$ , $CP_c$ have a common point $P$ and the lines $A'P'_a$ , $B'P'_b$ , $C'P'_c$ have a common point $P'$

b) $\frac{PC}{PP_c}=\frac{P'C'}{P'P'_c} $ c) if $A', B', C'$ are variable points on the edges $[DA]$ , $[DB]$ , $[DC]$ , then the line $PP'$ is always parallel to a fixed line.

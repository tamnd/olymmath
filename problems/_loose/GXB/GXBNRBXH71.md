The period of the fraction $1 / 7$ is the number $N=142857$. It has the following property: the sum of the two halves of the period is a number consisting of all nines

$142+857=999)$. Prove in the general case that for a prime $q>5$ and a natural number $p<q$, the period of the fraction $p / q$ is a $2 n$-digit number $N=\overline{N_{1}^{1}} \overline{N_{2}}$, such that $N_{1}+N_{2}=\underbrace{99 \ldots 9}_{n}$.

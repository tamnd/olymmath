Inside a right-angled triangle $A B C$, a point $P$ is chosen arbitrarily, from which perpendiculars $P K$ and $P M$ are dropped to the legs $A C$ and $B C$ respectively. The lines $A P$ and $B P$ intersect the legs at points $A^{\prime}$ and $B^{\prime}$ respectively. It is known that $S_{A P B^{\prime}}: S_{K P B^{\prime}}=m$. Find $S_{M P A^{\prime}}: S_{B P A^{\prime}}$.

Do there exist natural numbers $m$ and $n$ such that $n \leqslant 2019$ and

$$
\left\lfloor\frac{a m}{n}\right\rfloor=\lfloor a \pi\rfloor \text { for all } a=1,2, \ldots, 2019 \text { ? }
$$

(For a real number $x$, we denote by $\lfloor x\rfloor$ the greatest integer not greater than $x$.)

Consider the lattice points in the plane whose coordinates take the values $0,1,2,3$. Color the point $(0,1)$ blue, and the rest red. Then, step by step, we change the color of one or possibly more points (lying on a horizontal, vertical, or a line with a slope of +1 or -1) from one color to the other. Show that it is not possible to color all 16 points red using such steps.

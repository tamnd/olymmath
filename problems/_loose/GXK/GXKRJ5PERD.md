Let $k\ge0$ be a given integer. Suppose there exists positive integer $n,d$ and an odd integer $m>1$ with $d\mid m^{2^k}-1$ and $m\mid n^d+1$ . Find all possible values of $\frac{m^{2^k}-1}d$ .

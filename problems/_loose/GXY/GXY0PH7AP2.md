Define $\lfloor x \rfloor$ as the largest integer less than or equal to $x$ . Define $\{x \} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor$ . For example, $\{ 3 \} = 3-3 = 0$ , $\{ \pi \} = \pi - 3$ , and $\{ - \pi \} = 4-\pi$ . If $\{n\} + \{ 3n\} = 1.4$ , then find the sum of all possible values of $100\{n\}$ .

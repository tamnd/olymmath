On a $22.5 \mathrm{~km}$ long tram line, trains are to run in both directions from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM at ten-minute intervals, starting with the departure time exactly at 8:00 AM at both terminal stops. The average speed of the trains is $18 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$. Each train, upon arrival at a terminal stop, should take a break before its departure, which is more than 10 minutes but less than 20 minutes.

a) When will the train that departed at 8:00 AM from one terminal stop leave this terminal stop for the second time?

b) How many trains are sufficient to maintain the described traffic flow?

c) How much time elapses for a train traveling from one terminal stop to the other, on average, from one encounter with an oncoming train to the next encounter with an oncoming train?

Consider a paper model of a cube and a paper model of an octahedron defined by the midpoints of the faces of the cube. We call the edge of the octahedron corresponding to a cube edge the edge whose endpoints are the midpoints of the faces forming the cube edge.

We cut 7 edges of the cube model in such a way that the faces remain connected along the other edges and can be flattened into a plane. On the octahedron model, we cut those edges that correspond to the uncut edges of the cube. It needs to be proven that this way the faces of the octahedron model remain connected and can be flattened into a plane.

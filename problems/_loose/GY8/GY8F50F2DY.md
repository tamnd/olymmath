For an infinite sequence $a_1, a_2,. . .$ denote as it's *first derivative* is the sequence $a'_n= a_{n + 1} - a_n$ (where $n = 1, 2,..$ .), and her $k$ - th derivative as the first derivative of its $(k-1)$ -th derivative ( $k = 2, 3,...$ ). We call a sequence *good* if it and all its derivatives consist of positive numbers.
Prove that if $a_1, a_2,. . .$ and $b_1, b_2,. . .$ are good sequences, then sequence $a_1\cdot b_1, a_2 \cdot b_2,..$ is also a good one.

R. Salimov

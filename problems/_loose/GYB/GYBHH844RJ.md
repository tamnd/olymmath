In triangle $A B C$, it is known that $\angle A=120^{\circ}$, sides $A C=1$ and $B C=\sqrt{7}$. On the extension of side $C A$, a point $M$ is taken such that $B M$ is the altitude of triangle $A B C$. Find the radius of the circle passing through points $A$ and $M$ and tangent at point $M$ to the circle passing through points $M, B$ and $C$.

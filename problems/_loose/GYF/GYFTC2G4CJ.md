Let $A B C$ be a triangle. On the lines $(A B)$ and $(A C)$, we construct points $A_{B}$ and $A_{C}$ such that $A$ is on the segments $\left[B A_{B}\right]$ and $\left[C A_{C}\right]$ and that $A A_{B}=A A_{C}=B C$. Similarly, we define the points $B_{A}, B_{C}$ and $C_{A}, C_{B}$ on the sides of $B$ and $C$ respectively. Show that the points $A_{B}, A_{C}, B_{A}, B_{C}, C_{A}$ and $C_{B}$ lie on the same circle.

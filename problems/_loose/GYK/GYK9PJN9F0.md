Let $k$ be a circle and $AB$ a chord of $k$ such that the center of $k$ does not lie on $AB$. Let $C$ be a point on $k$ different from $A$ and $B$. For each choice of $C$, let $P_{C}$ and $Q_{C}$ be the projections of $A$ onto $BC$ and $B$ onto $AC$, respectively. Let $O_{C}$ be the circumcenter of triangle $P_{C} Q_{C} C$. Show that there exists a circle $\omega$ such that $O_{C}$ lies on $\omega$ for every choice of $C$.

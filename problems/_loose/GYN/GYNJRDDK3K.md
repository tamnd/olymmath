In $\triangle A B C$, let $s$ be the semi-perimeter, $R$ and $r$ be the circumradius and inradius, respectively. Prove:
$$
2 \sqrt{r(r+4 R)}<2 s \leqslant \sqrt{4(r+2 R)^{2}+2 R^{2}} \text {. }
$$

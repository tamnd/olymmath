Given that $G$ is the centroid of $\triangle ABC$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $BC$, a line through point $G$ parallel to $BC$ intersects sides $AB$ and $AC$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively, $EC$ intersects $BG$ at point $Q$, and $FB$ intersects $CG$ at point $P$. Prove: $\triangle MPQ \sim \triangle ABC$.
(3rd Asia Pacific Mathematical Olympiad)

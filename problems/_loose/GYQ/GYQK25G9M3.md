(1) $a,b,c$ are complex numbers, if $a^2+b^2>c^2$ , then $a^2+b^2-c^2>0$ .
(2) $a,b,c$ are complex numbers, if $a^2+b^2-c^2>0$ , then $a^2+b^2>c^2$ .
Then, which is true? $\text{(A)}$ (1) is correct, (2) is correct as well $\text{(B)}$ (1) is correct, (2) is incorrect $\text{(C)}$ (1) is incorrect, (2) is incorrect as well $\text{(D)}$ (1) is incorrect, (2) is correct

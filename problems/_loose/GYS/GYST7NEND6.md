Let $G$ be a countable graph, and suppose that every partial matching has an augmenting path. Let $M$ be any matching. Is there a sequence of augmenting paths (possibly transfinite) such that each augmenting path (which is an augmenting path with respect to the current matching) extends $M$ to a 1-factor.

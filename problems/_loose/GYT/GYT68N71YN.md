Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{41}$ be an arithmetic sequence such that

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{1}}+\sqrt{a_{2}}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{2}}+\sqrt{a_{3}}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{40}}+\sqrt{a_{41}}}
$$

is a natural number. If $a_{1}=1$ and the difference of the sequence is a natural number, determine the difference of the sequence.

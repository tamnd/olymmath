Assume that the following three statements are true: $I$ . All freshmen are human. $II$ . All students are human. $III$ . Some students think.
Given the following four statements: $ \textbf{(1)}\ \text{All freshmen are students.} $ $\textbf{(2)}\ \text{Some humans think.} $ $\textbf{(3)}\ \text{No freshmen think.} $ $\textbf{(4)}\ \text{Some humans who think are not students.}$ Those which are logical consequences of I,II, and III are: $ \textbf{(A)}\ 2 \textbf{(B)}\ 4 \textbf{(C)}\ 2,3 \textbf{(D)}\ 2,4 \textbf{(E)}\ 1,2 $

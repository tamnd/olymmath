(1) Norbert collected more than Mike.

(2) Karsten and Lutz together collected as much as Mike and Lutz together.

(3) Karsten and Norbert together collected less than Lutz and Mike together.

Investigate whether from these statements:

a) exactly one of the four boys can be identified as the one who collected the most mushrooms,

b) exactly one of the four boys can be identified as the one who collected the fewest mushrooms!

Give the name of the respective boy, if that is the case!

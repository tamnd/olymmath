Triangles $M A B$ and $M C D$ are similar but have opposite orientations. Let $O_{1}$ be the center of rotation by the angle $2 \angle(\overrightarrow{A B}, \overrightarrow{B M})$, which maps $A$ to $C$, and $O_{2}$ be the center of rotation by the angle $2 \angle(\overrightarrow{A B}, \overrightarrow{A M})$, which maps $B$ to $D$. Prove that $O_{1}=O_{2}$.

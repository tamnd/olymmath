Let the ellipse be $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$. Given that $a b=2 \sqrt{3}$, and the eccentricity $e=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse.
(2) There is a line $l$ with slope $k$ passing through the right focus $F$ of the ellipse, and intersecting the ellipse at points $A$ and $B$. Point $P$ lies on the line $x=3$. Is there such a line $l$ that makes $\triangle A B P$ an equilateral triangle? If it exists, find the equation of the line $l$; if not, explain the reason.

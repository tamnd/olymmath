A positive integer $n$ is called $asri$ if there exist positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that
$$n = ab+20a+25b. $$
(a) Prove that there exist a **non** $asri$ positive integer that is greater than $20^{25}$ .
(b) Prove that there exist $26$ consecutive $asri$ positive integers.

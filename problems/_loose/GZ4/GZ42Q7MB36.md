Given two complex coefficient functions $f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_{i} x^{n-i}$ and $g(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{n} b_{i} x^{n-i}$ (where $a_{0}=b_{0}=1$), $\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor} b_{2 i}$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n+1}{2}\right\rfloor} b_{2 i-1}$ are both real numbers, and the negatives of the squares of all roots of $g(x)=0$ are the roots of $f(x)=0$. Prove that $\sum_{i=1}^{n}(-1)^{i} a_{i}$ is a real number.
(《High School Mathematics》2000 Issue 5 Olympiad Training Problem)

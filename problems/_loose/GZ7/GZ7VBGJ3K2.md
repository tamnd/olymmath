(JBMO SL 2021 N4) Paul and Martin are playing the following game. First, Martin chooses an infinite set $S$ of prime numbers. Paul then gives an infinite sequence of integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$ Martin then chooses an integer $M$ and a prime $p$ from $S$. Paul wins if there exists a rank from which all terms of the sequence are divisible by $p^{M}$. Otherwise, Martin wins.

Which player has a winning strategy?

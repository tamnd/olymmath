Three students were solving the same test, which consisted of several problems. For each correct answer, a student was given two points, for each incorrect answer, one point was deducted, and if the answer to a problem was not written, zero points were given. Together, these three students scored exactly 100 points. Prove that one of them did not write answers to all the problems.

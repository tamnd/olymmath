\section*{Problem 3B - 211043B}

Prove that on the surface of a sphere with radius $r$, 12 points $P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{12}$ can be distributed such that the distance between any two of these points is greater than $r$! Here, the distance between two points is defined as the length of their straight-line connecting segment (not the arc on the surface of the sphere).

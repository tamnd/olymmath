On ray $C A$, points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ are chosen, and on ray $C B$, points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ are chosen, such that the four points $A_{1}, C, B_{1}, D$ lie on one circle, and the four points $A_{2}, C, B_{2}, D$ lie on another circle. Prove that $A_{1} A_{2}=B_{1} B_{2}$.

Doctor Aibolit wants to visit a cow, a wolf, a bug, and a worm. All four live along the same straight road. In the morning, eagles are ready to deliver Aibolit to the first patient, and in the evening, they will pick him up from the last one, but the three intermediate transitions he will have to make on foot. If Aibolit starts with the cow, the length of his shortest route will be 6 km, if with the wolf - 7 km, and if with the bug - 8 km.

Draw how the houses of the cow, the wolf, the bug, and the worm could be arranged (one example of the arrangement is sufficient).

If $G$ and $G^{\prime}$ are two groups
$$
\begin{array}{c}
G \times G^{\prime} \triangleq\left\{\left(g, g^{\prime}\right) \mid g \in G, g^{\prime} \in G^{\prime}\right\}, \\
\left(g_{1}, g^{\prime}{ }_{1}\right)\left(g_{2}, g^{\prime}{ }_{2}\right) \triangleq\left(g_{1} g_{2}, g^{\prime}{ }_{1} g^{\prime}{ }_{2}\right),
\end{array}
$$
the unit element of $G \times G^{\prime}$ is $\left(e, e^{\prime}\right)$. Prove that $G \times G^{\prime}$ is a group.

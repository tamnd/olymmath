Given a square $\square O A B C$ on the coordinate plane, let $O$ be the origin, and $A, B, C$ be integer points (points with integer coordinates). Prove: For any integer point $P$ inside or on the boundary of $\triangle A B C$, there exist integer points $Q$ and $R$ inside or on the boundary of $\triangle O A C$ (which can be the same), such that $\overrightarrow{O P}=\overrightarrow{O Q}+\overrightarrow{O R}$.

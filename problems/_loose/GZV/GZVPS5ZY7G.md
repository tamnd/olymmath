Let ABC be a right-angled triangle $\left(m(\angle A)=90^{\circ}\right)$ and let $\mathrm{D}, \mathrm{E}, \mathrm{M}$ be the feet of the altitude, angle bisector, and median from A, respectively. Denote $BC=a, AC=b, AB=c$. Show that:

$$
\overrightarrow{A E}=\left(\frac{a}{b+c}\right)^{2} \overrightarrow{A D}+\left[1-\left(\frac{a}{b+c}\right)^{2}\right] \overrightarrow{A M}
$$

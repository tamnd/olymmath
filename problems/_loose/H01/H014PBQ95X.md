At a conference, delegations of the same size arrive from two countries, $A$ and $B$; some of their members had already known each other from earlier. Prove that it is possible to select some (at least one) members from the delegation of country $A$ such that one of the following possibilities holds:

a) among the selected members, each member from country $B$ has an even number of acquaintances;

b) among the selected members, each member from country $B$ has an odd number of acquaintances.

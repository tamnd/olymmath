Determine the natural numbers $a, b, c, d, e$, of which the following is known:

(1) $a$ is half of $b$.

(2) $b$ is the sum of $c$ and $d$.

(3) $c$ is the difference between $d$ and $e$.

(4) $d$ is three times $e$.

(5) $e$ is one fourth of 56.

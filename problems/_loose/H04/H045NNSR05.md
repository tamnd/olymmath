Let point $N$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $A$ of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, points $P$ and $Q$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from point $N$ to sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$, and point $O$ be the center of the circumscribed circle of the given triangle. If $|AC|=2|OP|$, prove that $|AB|=2|OQ|$.

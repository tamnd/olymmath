A set $M$ of positive integers is called *connected* if for any element $x\in M$ at least one of the numbers $x-1,x+1$ is in $M$. Let $U_n$ be the number of the connected subsets of $\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$.

a) Compute $U_7$;

b) Find the smallest number $n$ such that $U_n \geq 2006$.

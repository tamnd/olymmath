Giulio writes a polynomial $P_{1}(x)$ and another polynomial $P_{2}(x)$, a product of first-degree factors, having a degree strictly greater than the previous one. By performing the division of $P_{2}(x)$ by $P_{1}(x)$, the remainder is 0. Indicating with $Q(x)$ the quotient of this division, which of the following statements is always true?

(A) $Q(x)$ can be a constant

(B) If $P_{2}(a)=0$, then $Q(a)=0$

(C) There exists a real number $a$ such that $P_{2}(a)=Q(a)=0$

(D) $Q(x)$ certainly has a degree less than $P_{1}(x)$

(E) If $P_{1}(a)=0$, then $Q(a)=0$

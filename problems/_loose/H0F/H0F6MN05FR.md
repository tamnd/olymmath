$$
\begin{aligned}
& 4 x+7 y+z=11 \\
& 3 x+y+5 z=15
\end{aligned}
$$

Given that $x+y+z=\frac{p}{q}$ where $p$ and $q$ are positive integers and the fraction $\frac{p}{q}$ is in lowest terms, what is the value of $p-q$ ?

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with orthocenter $M$. The line $e$ is not parallel to any side of the triangle. The lines through $A, B, C$ parallel to $e$ intersect the perpendicular bisectors of the segments $A M, B M, C M$ at points $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$, respectively. Prove that the points $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$ lie on a straight line.

Let $n$ be a positive odd integer and $k$ a strictly positive integer. Suppose that the number of positive divisors of $2 n$ that are less than or equal to $k$ is odd. Show that there exists a divisor $d$ of $2 n$ for which $k<d \leqslant 2 k$.

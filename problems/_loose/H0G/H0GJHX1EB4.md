The Fibonacci sequence. Consider the Fibonacci sequence $1,1,2,3,5,13, \ldots$, whose terms $F_{n}$ (Fibonacci numbers) satisfy the relation $F_{n+2}=F_{n}+F_{n+1}$, $F_{1}=F_{2}=1$. Now consider the sequence of digits in the units place of the Fibonacci numbers. Will this sequence be cyclic, that is, can it be obtained by the unlimited repetition of the same finite set of digits, as is true, for example, in the case of the sequence $055055 \ldots$.

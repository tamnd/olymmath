The regular $ABC$ triangle's midpoint points, $X, Y, Z$ satisfy the following three conditions:

a) $X, Y, Z$ are internal points of the sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively;

b) $XC + CY = YA + AZ = ZB + BX$

c) the lines $AX, BY, CZ$ intersect at a single point.

Can such $X, Y, Z$ points be found for every $ABC$ triangle that satisfy these three conditions?

For a natural number $ n$, let $ a_n=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} (\tan x)^{2n}dx$.
Answer the following questions.

(1) Find $ a_1$.

(2) Express $ a_{n+1}$ in terms of $ a_n$.

(3) Find $ \lim_{n\to\infty} a_n$.

(4) Find $ \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=1}^n \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{2k-1}$.

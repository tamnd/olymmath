Given a triangle $A B C$. On the rays $A B$ and $C B$, segments $A K$ and $C M$ are laid off, equal to $A C$. Prove that the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $B K M$ is equal to the distance between the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of triangle $A B C$, and that the line $K M$ is perpendicular to the line connecting the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles.

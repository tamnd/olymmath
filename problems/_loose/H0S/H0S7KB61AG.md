The sequence of nonnegative integers $F_0, F_1, F_2, \dots$ is defined recursively as $F_0 = 0$ , $F_1 = 1$ , and $F_{n+2}= F_{n+1} + F_{n}$ for all integers $n \geq 0$ . Let $d$ be the largest positive integer such that, for all integers $n\geq 0$ , $d$ divides $F_{n+2020}-F_n$ . Compute the remainder when $d$ is divided by $1001$ .

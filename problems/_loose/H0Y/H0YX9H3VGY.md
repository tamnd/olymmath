Let $p$ be a prime number and $a_{k} \cdots a_{0}$ its representation in base 10. We define

$$
Q_{p}(x)=a_{k} x^{k}+a_{k-1} x^{k-1}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0}
$$

Show that $\mathrm{Q}_{p}$ has no integer roots, except for 4 values of $p$ which we will determine.

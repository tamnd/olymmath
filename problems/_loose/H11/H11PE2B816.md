Let $A, B, C$ be points in a plane such that for every point $M$ in that plane, at least one of the following two conditions is satisfied:
1) $d(A, M) < d(B, M)$, and
2) $d(A, M) < d(C, M)$.

Here, $d(X, Y)$ denotes the distance between points $X$ and $Y$. Prove that point $A$ lies on the segment $BC$.

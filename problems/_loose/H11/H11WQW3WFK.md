The maximum breeding capacity of fish in a fish farm is $m$ tons. To ensure the growth space for the fish, the actual breeding quantity cannot reach the maximum breeding capacity; an appropriate idle capacity must be left. It is known that the annual increase in fish, $y$ tons, is directly proportional to the product of the actual breeding quantity, $x$ tons, and the idle rate, with the proportionality constant being $k (k>0)$ (the idle rate is the ratio of the idle capacity to the maximum breeding capacity).
(1) Write the function relationship of $y$ with respect to $x$, and specify the domain of this function;
(2) Find the maximum annual increase in fish and the range of values for $k$ at this point.

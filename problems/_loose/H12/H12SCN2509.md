Let $h_{n}$ denote the number of non-associative (i.e., not satisfying commutative and associative laws) products (referred to as $n$-ary products) formed by $n(n \geqslant 1)$ distinct elements $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$. Prove that: $h_{n}=\frac{(2 n-2)!}{(n-1)!}=n!C_{n}$, where $C_{n}$ is the Catalan number.

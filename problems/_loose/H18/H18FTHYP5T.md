A box of chocolates in the shape of a cuboid was full of chocolates arranged in rows and columns. Misha ate some of them and rearranged the remaining ones so that they filled (without gaps) up to one place three whole rows. The remaining chocolates from the next incomplete row were eaten. Then the remaining chocolates were rearranged and filled five columns without one place. The chocolates in the incomplete column were eaten again. Thus, only a third of the original number of chocolates remained in the box. Find:

a) how many chocolates the entire box had,

b) how many chocolates Misha ate before he did the first rearrangement.

(Bednářrová)

Given the coordinates of the vertices of a pyramid $A_{1}(4,2,5)$, $A_{2}(0,7,2)$, $A_{3}(0,2,7)$, $A_{4}(1,5,0)$. Find: 1) the equations of the planes $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$ and $A_{1} A_{2} A_{4}$; 2) the angle between the edge $A_{1} A_{3}$ and the face $A_{1} A_{2} A_{4}$; 3) the distance from the vertex $A_{3}$ to the face $A_{1} A_{2} A_{4}$; 4) the shortest distance between the lines $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $A_{3} A_{4}$; 5) the equation of the height dropped from the vertex $A_{3}$ to the face $A_{1} A_{2} A_{4}$.

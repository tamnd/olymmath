Let $x, y, z$ be positive numbers, satisfying $x y + y z + z x = 1$.

Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x y z(x+y)(y+z)(z+x) \\
\geqslant\left(1-x^{2}\right)\left(1-y^{2}\right)\left(1-z^{2}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

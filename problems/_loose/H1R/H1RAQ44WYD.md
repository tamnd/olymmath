Given the function $f(x)=3x+4$, find the analytical expression of the function $y=g(x)$ according to the following conditions.
(1) The graph of $y=g(x)$ is obtained by shifting the graph of $y=f(x)$ 1 unit to the left and then 2 units downward;
(2) The graph of $y=g(x)$ is symmetrical to the graph of $y=f(x)$ about the $y$-axis;
(3) The graph of $y=g(x)$ is symmetrical to the graph of $y=f(x)$ about the line $y=1$;
(4) The graph of $y=g(x)$ is symmetrical to the graph of $y=f(x)$ about the line $y=-x$;
(5) The graph of $y=g(x)$ is centrally symmetrical to the graph of $y=f(x)$ about the point $P(a, b)$.

In a certain city, there are $M$ streets and $N$ squares, where $M$ and $N$ are natural numbers such that $M > N$. Each street connects two squares and does not pass through other squares.

The citizens want to change the appearance of the city. This year, each street will be painted red or blue for the first time. It is agreed that each year one square will be chosen, and all streets leading to that square will simultaneously change color from blue to red and vice versa.

Prove that the citizens can choose the colors of the streets in such a way that it will never happen in the future that all streets are the same color.

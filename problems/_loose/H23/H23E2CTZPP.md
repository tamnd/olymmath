$X=\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{m}\right\}$ is a family of subsets of an $n$-element set $X$, for $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant$ $m, A_{i} \cup A_{j} \neq X$, prove that $m \leqslant 2^{n-1}$. Furthermore, when $m<2^{n-1}$, it is always possible to add several subsets to $\mathscr{N}$ so that $|\mathscr{N}|=2^{n-1}$, and at that time, the union of any two subsets in $\mathscr{N}$ is not equal to $X$.

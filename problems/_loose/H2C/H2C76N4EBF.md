The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is defined by $a_{1}=0$ and $a_{n+1}=$ $a_{n}+4 n+3, n \geq 1$.

a) Express $a_{n}$ as a function of $n$.

b) Find the limit

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{\sqrt{a_{n}}+\sqrt{a_{4 n}}+\sqrt{a_{4^{2} n}}+\cdots+\sqrt{a_{4}{ }^{10} n}}{\sqrt{a_{n}}+\sqrt{a_{2 n}}+\sqrt{a_{2^{2} n}}+\cdots+\sqrt{a_{2^{10} n}}}
$$

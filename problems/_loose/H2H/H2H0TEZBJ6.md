On an island, there live liars and knights. Knights always tell the truth, and liars always lie. Each inhabitant of the island knows whether each of the others is a knight or a liar. One day, 19 islanders met. Three of them said: "Exactly three of us are liars," then six of the others said: "Exactly six of us are liars," finally, nine of the remaining said: "Exactly nine of us are liars." How many liars were among those who met? Provide all possible options and prove that there are no others.

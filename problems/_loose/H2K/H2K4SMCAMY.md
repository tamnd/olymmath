We need to connect the origin of the coordinate system with the intersection point of the lines $e_{1}$ and $e_{2}$, whose equations are $3 x-4 y+3=0$ and $48 x-55 y-55=0$, respectively. However, the intersection point lies outside our drawing paper. Show that the connecting line can be obtained using only a compass and a straightedge with the following construction. A circle $k$ of arbitrary radius intersects $e_{1}$ at points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, and $e_{2}$ at points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$; furthermore, the line $O B_{1}$ intersects $k$ at point $D$, and the line $O C_{1}$ intersects $k$ at point $E$. Then, the intersection point $M$ of the lines $C_{2} D$ and $B_{2} E$, when connected to $O$, gives the desired line.

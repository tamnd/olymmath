Following Banach [30], use Lusin's theorem to establish that if a measurable function $f=f(x)$ on $[0,1]$ satisfies

$$
f(x+h)=f(x)+f(h), 0 \leqslant x, h \leqslant 1, x+h \leqslant 1
$$

then $f$ is right-continuous at zero, and derive from this that

$$
f(x)=f(1) x
$$

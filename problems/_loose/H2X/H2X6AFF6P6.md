Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $\angle A B C=90^{\circ}$. Points $D$ and $E$ on $A C$ and $B C$ respectively satisfy $B D \perp A C$ and $D E \perp B C$. The circumcircle of $\triangle C D E$ intersects $A E$ at two points, $E$ and $F$. Prove that $B F \perp A E$.

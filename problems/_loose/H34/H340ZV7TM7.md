\section*{Problem 6A - 111246A}

Let $n$ be a natural number such that $4 \leq n \leq 8$. In the plane, there are $n$ points arranged such that on every line through any two of these points, there is at least one more of these $n$ points. Prove that there exists a line on which all these $n$ points lie.

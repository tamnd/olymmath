There are 200 tennis players participating in a points-based round-robin tournament. Each player plays exactly one match per day, and each match has a clear winner and loser. The winner gets 1 point, and the loser gets 0 points. Before each day's matches, all players are ranked according to their total points, and they are paired up in adjacent pairs (i.e., the 1st place player with the 2nd place player, the 3rd place player with the 4th place player, ..., the 199th place player with the 200th place player). Prove that if the tournament continues for a sufficiently long time, there will be a day when the difference in total points between two players is greater than 50.

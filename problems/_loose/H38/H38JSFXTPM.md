Let $n$ be a positive integer with exactly twelve positive divisors $1=d_1 < \cdots < d_{12}=n$ . We say $n$ is *trite* if $$
5 + d_6(d_6+d_4) = d_7d_4.
$$ Compute the sum of the two smallest trite positive integers.

*Proposed by Brandon Wang*

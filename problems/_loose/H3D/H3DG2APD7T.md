Chameleons can meet each other, but only two at a time. If two chameleons of the same color meet, they do not change their color. If two chameleons of different colors meet, then both simultaneously change their color to the third color. For what values of $x$ is it possible for all chameleons in the zoo to be of the same color at some point in time?

Given the infinite sequences $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$, for any positive integer $n$, we have
$$
x_{n+1}=3 x_{n}+2 y_{n}, y_{n+1}=4 x_{n}+3 y_{n},
$$

and $x_{1}=3, y_{1}=4$.
(1) Prove that $\left\{2 x_{n}^{2}-y_{n}^{2}\right\}$ is a constant sequence;
(2) Determine whether the sequences $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$ contain any integer cubes.

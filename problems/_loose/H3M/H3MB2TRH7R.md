On a circle, 2021 points are marked and these points are connected by segments to form a convex inscribed 2021-gon, which is divided into triangles by its diagonals. In this process, no two diagonals intersect at interior points (the only common points of different diagonals are the vertices). Prove that among the resulting triangles, there cannot be more than one acute triangle.

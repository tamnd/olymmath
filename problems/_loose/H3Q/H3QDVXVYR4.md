(Infinite Ramsey Theorem). Let $\mathrm{K}_{\infty}$ be an infinite complete graph whose edges are colored with $k$ colors. Then $\mathrm{K}_{\infty}$ contains an infinite complete subgraph whose edges are all of the same color.

In general, results on graph coloring are well demonstrated using compactness notions: if we have a local coloring of the vertices or edges of a graph, we can gradually extend it to a larger and larger coloring, until we color every finite part of the graph, and ultimately the graph itself.

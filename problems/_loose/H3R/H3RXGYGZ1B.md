Let $A B C D$ be a parallelogram. A line $d$ passing through $C$ intersects the lines $(A B)$ and $(A D)$ at $X$ and $Y$ respectively. The tangents at $X$ and $Y$ to the circumcircle of $A X Y$ intersect at a point $T$, then the line $(C T)$ intersects the circumcircle of $C D Y$ at a point $P$.

Prove that the points $A, B, D$ and $P$ are concyclic.

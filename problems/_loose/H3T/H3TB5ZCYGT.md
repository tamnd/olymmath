Let $A=\left\{a \mid a=x^{2}-y^{2}, x, y \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$, prove:
(1) All odd numbers belong to $A$;
(2) Even numbers $4 k-2(k \in \mathbf{Z})$ do not belong to $A$;
(3) The product of two integers belonging to $A$ still belongs to $A$.

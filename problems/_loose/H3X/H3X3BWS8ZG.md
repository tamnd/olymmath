Let $A$ be a natural number with an even number $n$ of digits, and $B$ be a number obtained by any permutation of the digits of $A$, such that $A + B = 10^n$.

(a) Find at least one pair of numbers $A$ and $B$ that satisfy the above property for $n=4$.

(b) Prove that for every even number $n$, each of the numbers $A$ and $B$ with the above property is divisible by 10.

Let the perimeter of the regular $n$-gon and $2n$-gon circumscribed around a circle of radius $r$ be $K_{n}, K_{2 n}$, and the perimeter of the regular $n$-gon and $2n$-gon inscribed in the circle be $k_{n}, k_{2 n}$. Prove that

$$
K_{2 n}=\frac{2 K_{n} k_{n}}{K_{n}+k_{n}}, k_{2 n}=\sqrt{k_{n} K_{2 n}}
$$

Using these, write down the values of $K_{8}, k_{8}$ from $K_{4}$ and $k_{4}$, and the value of $K_{24}$ from $K_{6}$ and $k_{6}$.

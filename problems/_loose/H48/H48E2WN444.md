Find the smallest real $\lambda$ , such that for any positive integers $n, a, b$ , such that $n \nmid a+b$ , there exists a positive integer $1 \leq k \leq n-1$ , satisfying $$ \{\frac{ak} {n}\}+\{\frac{bk} {n}\} \leq \lambda. $$

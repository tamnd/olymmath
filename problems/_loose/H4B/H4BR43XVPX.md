In a plane rectangular coordinate system, on each lattice point of a certain adjacent $\mathrm{n} \times \mathrm{n}$ lattice square, there is exactly one beetle. At a certain moment, all the beetles fly up and then land again on the plane lattice points, with multiple beetles possibly landing on the same lattice point. It is known that if the distance between two beetles was 1 at the start, then the distance between them after landing is no more than 1. Prove: There exists a line with a slope of 1 on which at least $\mathrm{n}$ beetles have landed.

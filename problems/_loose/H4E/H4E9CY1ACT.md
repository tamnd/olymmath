(Mono-generated subgroup) Let $(G, *)$ be a group and $g$ an element of $G$. Show that the map $\varphi^{g}: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow\langle g\rangle$ is a group homomorphism, with kernel $\operatorname{Ker}\left(\varphi^{g}\right)=n \mathbb{Z}$ if $g$ has finite order $n$, and $\operatorname{Ker}\left(\varphi^{g}\right)=\{0\}$ if $g$ has infinite order.

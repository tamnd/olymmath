Let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ be positive numbers and $A$ their arithmetic mean. Prove that $$ A^{n-1}\ge \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^na_1\dots \hat{a}_i\dots a_n, $$ where $\hat{a}_i$ denotes the term $a_i$ is omitted.

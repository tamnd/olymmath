Let's define a $\prec$ ordering relation on the positive integers such that for every triple of numbers $a, b, c$ where $a \prec b \prec c$ holds, $2 b \neq a+c$.

$\prec$ is an ordering relation if

a) for every pair of numbers $a, b$, exactly one of $a \prec b$, $b \prec a$, $a=b$ holds;

b) if $a \prec b$ and $b \prec c$, then $a \prec c$.

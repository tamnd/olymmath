Let $p$ and $q$ be two parallel lines. Circle $k$ touches line $p$ at point $A$ and intersects line $q$ at different points $B$ and $C$. Let $T$ be a point on line $p$ and let segments $\overline{T B}$ and $\overline{T C}$ intersect the shorter arc $\widetilde{A C}$ at points $K$ and $L$, respectively, different from $B$ and $C$.

Prove that line $K L$ passes through the midpoint of segment $\overline{A T}$.

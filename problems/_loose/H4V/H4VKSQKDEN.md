Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with circumcenter $O$ and $AB>BC$ , now $E$ is a point on $AC$ with $AE<EC$ . Let $l$ be the line through $E$ perpendicular to $AC$ intersecting the circumcircles of $\triangle AED, \triangle BED, \triangle CED$ at $X, Y, Z$ respectively and such that $E, Y, Z, X$ lie on $l$ in that order.
If $AX=BY=CZ$ , prove that $BD$ and $OY$ intersect at the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ .

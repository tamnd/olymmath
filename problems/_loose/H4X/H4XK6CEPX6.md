In an exam of a class, there are 3 multiple-choice questions, each with 4 options. It is known that a group of students' answers have property $P$: any two students' answers have at most 1 question with the same answer. Moreover, if this group of students is increased by 1 person, regardless of this person's answers, property $P$ no longer holds. How many students are there in this group at minimum?

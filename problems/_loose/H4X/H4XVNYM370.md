$$
\begin{array}{r}
x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{1} x_{2}=2 m+k \ldots \\
\left(x_{1}-1\right)\left(x_{2}-1\right)=m+1-k \ldots
\end{array}
$$

$3^{0}$. What are $k$ and $m$ if, in addition to the above, $k-m=1$? Calculate $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$.

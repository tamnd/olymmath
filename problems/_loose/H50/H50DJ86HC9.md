(2006 Thailand Mathematical Olympiad) 229 boys and 271 girls are evenly divided into 10 groups, and each group's students are marked with numbers from 1 to 50. Now, select 4 students (including an odd number of girls) who satisfy the property: they come from two groups, and among the 4 students, there are two pairs of students with the same numbers. Prove: the number of 4-person groups that meet the requirements is odd.

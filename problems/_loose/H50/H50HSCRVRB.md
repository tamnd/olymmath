Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be a positive geometric sequence with the sum of the first $n$ terms denoted as $S_{n}$, and
$$
2^{10} S_{30}+S_{10}=\left(2^{10}+1\right) S_{20} .
$$

Then the common ratio of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{1}{8}$
(B) $\frac{1}{4}$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$
(D) 1

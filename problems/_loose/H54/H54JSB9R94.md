A table of size $n \times n$ is filled with natural numbers from 1 to 10 such that no two identical numbers appear in the same row or column. Coincidence of numbers in different rows and columns is allowed. Let $f(n)$ be the number of such arrangements. For example, $f(1)=10, f(11)=0$.

a) Which is greater, $f(9)$ or $f(10)$?

b) Which is greater, $f(5)$ or $f(6)$?

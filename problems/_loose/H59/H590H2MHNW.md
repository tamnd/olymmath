Let's prove that if we eliminate the parameter $ t $ from the system of equations

$$
\begin{gathered}
x=1+2 t-2 t^{2} \ldots \\
y=2(1+t) \sqrt{1-t^{2}} \ldots
\end{gathered}
$$

we arrive at the equation

$$
y^{4}+2 y^{2}\left(x^{2}-12 x+9\right)+x^{4}+8 x^{3}+18 x^{2}-27=0 \ldots
$$

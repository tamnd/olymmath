If a quadrilateral has two of the three properties listed below, namely: 1) the diagonals of the quadrilateral are perpendicular, 2) the quadrilateral is cyclic, 3) the perpendicular dropped from the vertex of one side to the opposite side passes through the point of intersection of the diagonals, then it has the third property as well.

In a triangle with no obtuse angles, two squares are inscribed such that the vertices of the squares lie on the sides of the triangle. Prove that: 1) if the squares are equal, then the triangle is isosceles or right-angled, 2) if the squares are not equal, then two vertices of the larger square lie on the side of the triangle that is smaller than the side on which two vertices of the smaller square lie.

$C$ and $D$ played based on the following agreement: 1) the loser of a won game gives the winner as many seed beads as the winner currently has, 2) the value of a game won after a draw is twice the number of beads of the game played two games ago. The winners of the consecutive games were $C, C, D$, then the game was a draw: $X$, followed by $C, X, D, C$, $X, X, D$. After the last game, $C$ refused to pay, saying that the value of the second-to-last game was 0. Therefore, $D$ did not play further and determined that they had the same number of beads as they had before the game. - How many beads did each of them have, if the smallest prize that occurred was 20 seed beads?

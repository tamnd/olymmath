A river has a constant current of 3 miles per hour. A motorboat travels 4 miles downstream and then returns to the starting point at a constant speed, taking a total of 1 hour (ignoring the time to turn the boat around). The ratio of the speed downstream to the speed upstream is
(A) $4: 3$.
(B) $3: 2$.
(C) $5: 3$.
(D) $2: 1$.
(E) $5: 2$.
(22nd American High School Mathematics Examination, 1971)

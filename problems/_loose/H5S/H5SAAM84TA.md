Find the smallest constant $ k$ such that

$ \frac {x}{\sqrt {x + y}} + \frac {y}{\sqrt {y + z}} + \frac {z}{\sqrt {z + x}}\leq k\sqrt {x + y + z}$

for all positive $ x$ , $ y$ , $ z$ .

Prove that if the numbers $a, b$ and $c$ form an arithmetic progression, then the numbers

$$
a^{2}+a b+b^{2}, a^{2}+a c+c^{2}, b^{2}+b c+c^{2}
$$

also form (in the given order) an arithmetic progression.

In a working group, the following figure is being discussed: Let $ABCD$ be a square; the midpoints of the sides $AD$ and $CD$ are $M$ and $N$, respectively, and the intersection of the segments $CM$ and $BN$ is $P$.

a) Simone measures the angle $\angle BPM$ and finds that the segments $CM$ and $BN$ are perpendicular to each other!

b) Frank measures the side and height lengths of the triangles $ABM$ and $BPM$ and finds that these two triangles are not equal in area.

Investigate, without making measurements on a figure, whether each of these statements is true for every square!

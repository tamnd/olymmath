Let the positive integer $\mathrm{n} \geq 3$, and select $\mathrm{n}$ equally spaced points on a circle. Label one of these points as $\mathrm{A}$, and place a card at point $\mathrm{A}$. Each operation can move the card to the next point or the point after the next in the clockwise direction. Thus, each point has two different operations. Let $\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$ be the number of ways to move the card from point $\mathrm{A}$, clockwise around the circle twice, and return exactly to point $A$, without repeating any operation. Prove: For all $n \geq 4, a_{n-1}+a_{n}=2^{n}$.

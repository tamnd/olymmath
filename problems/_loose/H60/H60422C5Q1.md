Someone said to his friend: "In the regular pentagon and heptagon, I found that starting from any triangle determined by any three vertices and forming its pedal triangle, and then the pedal triangle of that, and so on, a triangle similar to the original one eventually appears in the sequence. Is this true for every regular polygon?"

His friend replied, "Not always true."

How much of the conjecture is true?

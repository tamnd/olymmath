Corresponding vertices of two $n$-gons $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \ldots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} B_{2} B_{3} \ldots B_{n}$ are connected by lines. Prove that if these lines are parallel and the segments $A_{i} B_{i}$ are intersected by some line in a given ratio $k$, then $\left(A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \ldots A_{n}\right):\left(B_{1} B_{2} B_{3} \ldots B_{n}\right)=k$.

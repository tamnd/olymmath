Among the four functions listed below, the function $f(x)$ that satisfies the property
$$
f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{2}[f(x)+f(y)]
$$

is
(A) $\lg x$.
(B) $\frac{1}{x}$.
(C) $3 x$.
(D) $3^{x}$.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1986)

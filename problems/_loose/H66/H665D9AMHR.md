Given two concentric circles with center $O$ and points $A$ and $B$, lying on the outer circle. From these points, tangents are drawn to the inner circle such that one tangent meets the other at point $M$, which is not on the perpendicular from point $O$ to the chord $A B$. Tangents are drawn from points $A$ and $B$ to the outer circle, intersecting at point $P$. Prove that the segment $O P$ is seen from point $M$ at a right angle.

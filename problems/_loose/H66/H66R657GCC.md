Inheritance. A man left his three sons and a hospital an inheritance of 1320 dollars. If the part of the inheritance designated for the hospital had gone to the eldest son, he would have received as much as his two brothers together. If the "hospital" part of the inheritance had been added to the inheritance of the middle son, the latter would have received twice as much as both his brothers together. Finally, if this part of the inheritance had been added to the youngest son, he would have received three times as much as both his brothers together. How many dollars did each of the sons receive, and what amount was bequeathed to the hospital?

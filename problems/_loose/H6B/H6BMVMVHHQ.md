For the positive sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, we know that for every pair of indices $(i, j)$, they satisfy the inequality

$$
(i+j) a_{i+j} \leq j a_{i}+i a_{j}
$$

Show that the sequence is bounded, has a greatest lower bound among its lower bounds, and that the sequence is convergent!

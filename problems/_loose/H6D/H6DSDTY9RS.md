After dropping off the passengers partway, the bus returns, picks up the rest, and continues to $B$. The walking group and the bus all arrive at $B$ simultaneously. The total time taken is twice the time it would take if two buses operated simultaneously. Find the ratio of the time the first group spends in the bus to the time they spend walking.

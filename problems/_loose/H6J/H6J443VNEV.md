In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, it is known that the midpoints of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$ are $E$, $F$, $G$, and $H$, respectively. The orthocenters of $\triangle AHE$, $\triangle BEF$, $\triangle CFG$, and $\triangle DGH$ are $W$, $X$, $Y$, and $Z$, respectively. Prove: The area of quadrilateral $ABCD$ is equal to the area of quadrilateral $WXYZ$.

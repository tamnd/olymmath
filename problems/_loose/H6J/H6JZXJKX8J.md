If $A B C D$ is a tetrahedron, then $R$ denotes the radius of its circumsphere (i.e., the sphere on which the points $A, B, C, D$ lie) and $r$ denotes the radius of its insphere (i.e., the sphere whose center lies inside the tetrahedron and which touches each of the faces $A B C, A B D, A C D, B C D$).

Prove that among all tetrahedra $A B C D$ with $A B \perp A C, A C \perp A D, A D \perp A B$, there are also those for which the ratio $R: r$ attains its smallest value; determine this value.

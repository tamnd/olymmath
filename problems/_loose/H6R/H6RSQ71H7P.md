A road paved with yellow bricks needs to be constructed. It will pass through an area where there is a straight section of a power line (PL) and a brick factory located at a distance $d$ from the PL $(d \neq 0)$. For rhythmic work, it is required that each point on the road being built is equally distant from the factory and from the PL. Introduce a coordinate system such that the brick factory has coordinates $(0,0)$, and the PL passes through the point $(0, d)$ parallel to one of the coordinate axes. Find the coordinates of the point on the road that is at a distance of $5 d$ from the factory.

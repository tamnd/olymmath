In $\triangle ABC$, let the circumcenter be $O$, the centroid be $G$, the orthocenter be $H$, the incenter be $I$, and the excenters opposite to angles $A, B, C$ be $P_{A}, P_{B}, P_{C}$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $O G=\frac{1}{3} \sqrt{9 R^{2}-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)}$;
(2) $\mathrm{OH}=\sqrt{9 R^{2}-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)}$;
(3) $G H=\frac{2}{3} \sqrt{9 R^{2}-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)}$;
(4) $G I^{2}=9 r^{2}+2\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)-3 s^{2}$;
(5) $H I^{2}=2 r^{2}+4 R^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$;
(6) $\angle A G H=\angle D G O$;
(7) $P_{A} O^{2}=R^{2}+2 R r_{a}$;
(8) $P_{A} I=\frac{a}{\cos \frac{A}{2}}$;
(9) $P_{B} P_{C}=\frac{a}{\sin \frac{A}{2}}$;
(10) $\frac{S_{\triangle P_{A} P_{B} P C}}{S_{\triangle A B C}}=1+\frac{a}{-a+b+c}+\frac{b}{a-b+c}+\frac{c}{a+b-c}$;
(11) $I A \cdot I B \cdot I C=4 r^{2} R$;
(12) $H A+H B+H C=2(R+r)$;
(13) $H A^{2}=4 R^{2}-a^{2}$;
(14) $H A^{2}+a^{2}=H B^{2}+b^{2}=H C^{2}+c^{2}$;
(15) $P A^{2}+P B^{2}+P C^{2}=G A^{2}+G B^{2}+G C^{2}+3 P G^{2}$.

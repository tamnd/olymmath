Let $f:[0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function with $f(1)=0.$ Prove that the limit $$ \lim_{t \nearrow 1} \left( \frac{1}{1-t} \int\limits_0^1x(f(tx)-f(x)) \mathrm{d}x\right) $$ exists and find its value.

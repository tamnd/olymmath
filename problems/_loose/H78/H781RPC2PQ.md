Given the parabola $y^{2}=4 a x(0<a<1)$ with focus $F$, a circle is drawn above the $x$-axis with center $A(a+4,0)$ and radius $|A F|$, intersecting the parabola at two different points $M$ and $N$. Let $P$ be the midpoint of $M N$.
(1) Find the value of $|M F|+|N F|$;
(2) Does there exist a value of $a$ such that $|M F|, |P F|, |N F|$ form an arithmetic sequence? If so, find the value of $a$; if not, explain why.

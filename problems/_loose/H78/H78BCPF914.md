Suppose the radius of the circumscribed circle of the lower base of a regular quadrilateral frustum is less than the radius of the circumscribed circle of its side. Try to prove: among the paths connecting the two endpoints of the spatial diagonal of the frustum along its surface, the shortest path only passes through the side faces and does not pass through the base.

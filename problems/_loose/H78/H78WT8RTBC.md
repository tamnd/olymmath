Let $A, B$ be two finite sets of points in the plane, $A \cap B=\varnothing$, and no three points in $A \cup B$ are collinear. If at least one of $A, B$ has at least 5 points, prove: there exists a triangle whose vertices are all in $A$ or all in $B$, and its interior does not contain any points from the other set. 26th IMO Shortlist

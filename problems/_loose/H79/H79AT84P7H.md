Tourists from the USA, when traveling to Europe, often use an approximate formula to convert temperatures in degrees Celsius $C$ to the familiar degrees Fahrenheit $F$: $\mathrm{F}=2 \mathrm{C}+30$. Indicate the range of temperatures (in degrees Celsius) for which the deviation of the temperature in degrees Fahrenheit, obtained using the approximate formula, from the temperature obtained using the exact formula does not exceed $5 \%$. To obtain the exact formula, it should be known that water freezes at $32 F$, and boils at $212 F$.

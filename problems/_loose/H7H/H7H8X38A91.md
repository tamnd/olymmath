Given that $A$ and $B$ are two non-empty sets of positive integers, $|A|=m, |B|=n$, and set $A$ satisfies the following condition: for any $x, y, u, v \in A$, if $x+y=u+v$, then $\{x, y\}=\{u, v\}$. Prove: $|A+B| \geq \frac{m^2 n}{m+n-1}$. Here $A+B=\{a+b \mid a \in A, b \in B\}$.

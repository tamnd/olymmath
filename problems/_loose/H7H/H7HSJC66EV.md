A $6 \times 6$ board, similar to a chessboard, is completely covered by domino tiles without any gaps or overlaps. Each domino tile covers two adjacent squares. It needs to be proven that among the 5 horizontal and 5 vertical lines separating the squares, there is at least one line that does not cut any domino tile in half.

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and $D$ an interior point of its side $AC$. We call a side of the triangle $ABD$ friendly, if the excircle of $ABD$ tangent to that side has its center on the circumcircle of $ABC$. Prove that there are exactly two friendly sides of $ABD$ if and only if $|BD| = |DC|$.

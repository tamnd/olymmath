Given $n$ circles: $O_{1}, O_{2}, \ldots O_{n}$, passing through a common point $O$. The second points of intersection of $O_{1}$ with $O_{2}, O_{2}$ with $O_{3}, \ldots, O_{3}$ with $O_{1}$ are denoted by $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$, respectively. On $O_{1}$, take an arbitrary point $B_{1}$. If $B_{1}$ does not coincide with $A_{1}$, draw a line through $B_{1}$ and $A_{1}$ until it intersects $O_{2}$ again at point $B_{2}$. If $B_{2}$ does not coincide with $A_{2}$, draw a line through $B_{2}$ and $A_{2}$ until it intersects $O_{3}$ again at point $B_{3}$. Continuing in this manner, we obtain a point $B_{n}$ on circle $O_{n}$. If $B_{n}$ does not coincide with $A_{n}$, draw a line through $B_{n}$ and $A_{n}$ until it intersects $O_{1}$ again at point $B_{n+1}$. Prove that $B_{n+1}$ coincides with $B_{1}$.

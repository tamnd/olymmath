Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid with bases $AB,CD$ such that $CD=k \cdot AB$ ($0<k<1$). Point $P$ is such that $\angle PAB=\angle CAD$ and $\angle PBA=\angle DBC$. Prove that $PA+PB \leq \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-k^2}} \cdot AB$.

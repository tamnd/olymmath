a) Let $a$ be an integer greater than or equal to 2, and $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$. Show that if $m \mid n$, then $\left(a^{m}-1\right) \mid \left(a^{n}-1\right)$.

b) We still assume that $m \mid n$. Under what condition do we have $\left(a^{m}+1\right) \mid \left(a^{n}+1\right)$? What can we say in the opposite case?

Find the domain of convergence of the series:
1) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{n}}{n!}=\frac{x}{11}+\frac{x^{2}}{2!}+\frac{x^{3}}{3!}+\ldots+\frac{x^{n}}{n!}+\ldots$;
2) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n$ $n!x^{n}=1!x+2!x^{2}+3!x^{3}+\ldots+n!x^{n}+\ldots$
3) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1} \frac{x^{n}}{n^{2}}=\frac{x}{1^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{2^{2}}+\frac{x^{3}}{3^{2}}-\frac{x^{4}}{4^{2}}+\ldots+(-1)^{n-1} \frac{x^{n}}{n^{2}}+\ldots ;$
4) $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 2^{n}(x-1)^{n}=1+2(x-1)+2^{2}(x-1)^{2}+\ldots+2^{n}(x-1)^{n}+\ldots$
5) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{n}}{n^{n}}$.

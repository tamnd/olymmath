$n$ boys and $n$ girls stand along a circle. We call a pair of a boy and a girl good if one of two arcs joining them contains equal numbers of girls and boys (in particular, neighboring boy and girl form a good pair). It appears that there exists a girl belonging to exactly 10 good pairs. Prove that there also exists a boy belonging to exactly 10 good pairs.

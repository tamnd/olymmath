Let $f(x)=x^3-6x^2+\tfrac{25}{2}x-7$ . There is an interval $[a,b]$ such that for any real number $x$ , the sequence $x,f(x),f(f(x)),\dots$ is bounded (i.e., has a lower and upper bound) if and only if $x\in[a,b]$ . Compute $(a-b)^2$ .

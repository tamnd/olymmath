Given a triangle $ABC$ with an obtuse angle at vertex $A$, where $D$ denotes the foot of the altitude from vertex $C$. On the perpendiculars to $AB$ passing through points $A$ and $B$, construct points $E$ and $F$ in the half-plane $ABC$ such that $|AE|=|AD|$ and $|BF|=|BD|$. Finally, let $P$ and $Q$ be the intersections of the lines $AF$ and $BE$ with the line $CD$. Prove that $D$ is the midpoint of segment $PQ$.

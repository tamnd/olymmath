Vladik thought of a natural number $N$. He divided it by one of its prime divisors and wrote the result on the board. Then he divided this result by one of its prime divisors and wrote the new result on the board. He continued this process until he wrote 1 on the board. This 1 turned out to be the 22nd number written on the board. It turned out that the sum of all the numbers written on the board was $N / 2$. What could $N$ be?

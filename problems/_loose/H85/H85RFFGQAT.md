On the square, a huge number of boys and girls have gathered, some of whom are acquainted with each other. Is it always possible to distribute ties of 99 colors (each person receives one tie) in such a way that if some boy is acquainted with at least 2015 girls, then among these girls there are two in ties of different colors, and conversely, if some girl is acquainted with at least 2015 boys, among them there are boys in ties of different colors?

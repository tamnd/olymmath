Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle with side $a$, and $M$ be some point on the plane at a distance $d$ from the center of the triangle $A B C$. Prove that the area of the triangle with sides equal to the segments $M A, M B$, and $M C$ is given by the formula $S=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{12}\left|a^{2}-3 d^{2}\right|$.

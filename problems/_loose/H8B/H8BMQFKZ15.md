In a box, there are 67 balls of two sizes (small and large) and two colors (white and red). It is known:

- the number of red balls is divisible by 5;
- the number of large red balls is equal to the number of white balls;
- of the four types of balls, the smallest number is small white;
- the number of balls of each type is prime.

Determine how many balls of each type are in the box.

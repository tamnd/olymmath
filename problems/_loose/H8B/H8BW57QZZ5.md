It is known that the shape of a certain city is a polygon, which is divided into some polygonal areas by roads. Squares are established at the vertices of these polygons. Each road connects two squares and does not pass through any other squares. On each road, vehicles are only allowed to travel in one direction, and
(1) vehicles can both enter and exit every square;
(2) vehicles can travel around the perimeter of the city in a loop. Prove that there exists a region in the city where vehicles can travel around the boundary of this region in a loop.

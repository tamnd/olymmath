(28th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) A city has several squares, some of which are connected by one-way roads, and from each square, there are exactly two outgoing roads. Prove that the city can be divided into 1014 districts such that each road connects two squares in different districts, and for any two districts, all roads connecting them are in the same direction (i.e., all roads are from district A to district B, or all are from district B to district A).

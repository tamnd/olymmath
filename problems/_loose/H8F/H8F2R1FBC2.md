Through the point $P$, lines $a, b$ are drawn which intersect the plane $\sigma$ at points $A, B, C$, and form angles $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$, respectively, whose sum is equal to $90^{\circ}$. The projections of segments $P A, P B$, and $P C$ onto the plane $\sigma$ have lengths equal to $p, q$, and $r$, respectively. Determine the distance from the point $P$ to the plane $\sigma$.

Prove that there exists a real $c<\frac{3}{4}$ , such that for each sequence $x_1, x_2, \ldots$ satisfying $0 \leq x_i \leq 1$ for all $i$ , there exist infinitely many $(m, n)$ with $m>n$ , such that $$ |x_m-x_n|\leq \frac{c} {m}. $$

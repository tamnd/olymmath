We will prove that for every integer $n \geqq 2$, the following inequality holds:

$$
n(\sqrt[n]{n+1}-1) \leqq 1+\frac{1}{2}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n} \leqq n-\frac{n-1}{\sqrt[n-1]{n}}
$$

Two players, A and B, take turns playing a symbol-filling game on an infinitely large grid paper. A goes first, and they take turns filling an empty cell with a symbol. A always fills with $\times$, and B always fills with $\bigcirc$. If 11 consecutive cells in a row, column, or diagonal are all filled with $x$, then A wins. Prove that B can always prevent A from winning.

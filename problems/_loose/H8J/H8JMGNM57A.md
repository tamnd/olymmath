Find the largest real number $c$, such that the following conclusion holds for all integers $n \geqslant 3$: Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ be $n$ arcs on a circle (each arc includes its endpoints). If there are at least $\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{3}$ triples $(i, j, k)$ satisfying
$$
1 \leqslant i < j < k \leqslant n \text{, and } \bigcap_{i \in I} A_{i} \neq \varnothing \text{. }
$$

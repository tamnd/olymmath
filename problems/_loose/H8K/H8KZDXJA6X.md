There are $N$ points on the plane with diameter $D$ .
Show that there exist two distinct points $X,Y$ and two not necessarily distinct points $A,B$ not equal to $X$ or $Y$ satisfying that
$$|AX-XY|+|BY-XY|\leq \frac{2D}{N-2}.$$

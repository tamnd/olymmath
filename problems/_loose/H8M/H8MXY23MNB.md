To count the number of readers coming to the library, two blackboards were hung at the library entrance. Each reader who enters must write down the number of readers in the reading room when they enter on one blackboard, and when they leave, they must write down the number of readers remaining in the reading room on the other blackboard. Prove that all the integers that appear on both blackboards during the day are the same (the order can be different).

Three magicians are casting spells with numbers, but each can only perform one spell:

- the first magician can subtract one from any number,
- the second magician can divide any number by two,
- the third magician can multiply any number by three.

The magicians can take turns casting spells, but each can use their spell no more than five times during a performance, and no intermediate result can be greater than 10. During one performance, they had to turn a set of five numbers $3,8,9,2,4$ into a set of threes, and during another performance, they had to turn the same set of numbers into a set of fives.

How could they solve the problem? Find possible solutions, or explain why it is not possible.

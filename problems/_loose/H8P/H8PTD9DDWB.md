If $a, b,c,p,q, r $ are real numbers such that, for every real number $x,$ $$ ax^2 - 2bx + c \ge 0 \ \ and \ \ px^2 + 2qx + r \ge 0; $$ prove that then $$ apx^2 + bqx + cr \ge 0 $$ for all real $x$ .

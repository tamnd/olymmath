Let $a \geq b \geq c \geq 0$ be real numbers such that $ab + bc + ca = 3$.
Prove that:
$$
3 + (2 - \sqrt{3}) \cdot \frac{(b-c)^2}{b+(\sqrt{3}-1)c} \leq a+b+c
$$
and determine all the cases when the equality occurs.

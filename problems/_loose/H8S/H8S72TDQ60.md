Let $R$ be a rectangle which is the union of $n$ rectangles $R_{1}, R_{2}, \cdots, R_{n}$ and satisfies the following conditions:
(1) The sides of $R_{i}$ are parallel to the sides of $R$, $i=1,2, \cdots, n$;
(2) The $R_{i}$ are pairwise non-overlapping;
(3) Each $R_{i}$ has at least one side of integer length. Prove that $R$ has at least one side of integer length.

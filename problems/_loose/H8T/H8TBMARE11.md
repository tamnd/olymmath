In $\triangle A B C$, prove that the following expressions are all equal to the area $\triangle$.
(1) $2 R^{2} \sin A \sin B \sin C$;
(2) $r^{2} \cot \frac{A}{2} \cot \frac{B}{2} \cot \frac{C}{2}$;
(3) $s^{2} \tan \frac{A}{2} \tan \frac{B}{2} \tan \frac{C}{2}$;
(4) $a^{2} \frac{\sin B \sin C}{2 \sin A}$;
(5) $\frac{\left(a^{2}-b^{2}\right) \sin A \sin B}{2 \sin (A-B)}$;
(6) $\frac{1}{4}\left(a^{2} \sin 2 B+b^{2} \sin 2 A\right)$;
(7) $s(s-a) \tan \frac{A}{2}$;
(8) $r r_{a} \cot \frac{A}{2}$;
(9) $r_{a}(s-a)$;
(10) $\sqrt{r r_{a} r_{b} r_{c}}$;
(11) $4 R r \cos \frac{A}{2} \cos \frac{B}{2} \cos \frac{C}{2}$;
(12) $\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{4(\cot A+\cot B+\cot C)}$;
(13) $\frac{1}{4}\left(a^{2} \cot A+b^{2} \cot B+c^{2} \cot C\right)$.

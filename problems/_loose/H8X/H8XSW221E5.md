On a circle, two points $A$ and $C$ are placed. The point $D$ is the midpoint of the arc $\widehat{A C}$. The point $B$ is a point on the arc $\widehat{D C}$ that does not contain $A$. Show that the line $(B D)$ is the external bisector of the triangle $A B C$ at $B$. Let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of $D$ onto the line $A B$. Show that $A E = B E + B C$.

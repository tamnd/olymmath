Probabilistic Voting (6-9). In the final of a play competition for March 8, two plays made it to the finals. In the first play, $n$ students from 5th grade A participated, and in the second play, $n$ students from 5th grade B participated. At the play, $2n$ mothers of all $2n$ students were present. The best play is chosen by the mothers' votes. It is known that each mother votes for the best play with a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$ and for the play in which her child participates with a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$.

a) (1 pt.) Find the probability that the best play will win by a majority of votes.

b) (1 pt.) The same question, if more than two classes made it to the final.

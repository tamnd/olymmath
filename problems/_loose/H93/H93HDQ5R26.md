a) A point $M$ moves around the circumcircle of a triangle, and for each of its positions, we consider the projections of $M$ onto the sides of the triangle. We find that the points where $M$ is such that all three projections fall on the extensions of the sides (or exactly at the vertices) cover $\frac{8}{10}$ of the circumference of the circle. What is the largest angle of the triangle?

b) A point $N$ moves around the incircle of a triangle. We find that the points where $N$ is such that one of the three projections (formed in the same way as before) falls on the extension of a side cover $\frac{8}{10}$ of the circumference of the circle. What is the largest angle of the triangle? (It is sufficient to give the angle with an accuracy of $0.1^{\circ}$.)

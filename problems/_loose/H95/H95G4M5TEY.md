Let $n\ge 2$ be an integer. Let $z_1, z_2,..., z_n$ be complex numbers in such a way that for all integers $k$ with $1\le k\le n$: $$\Pi_{i = 1,i\ne k}^{n} (z_k- z_i) = \Pi_{i = 1,i\ne k}^{n} (z_k+ z_i).$$ Show that two of them are the same.

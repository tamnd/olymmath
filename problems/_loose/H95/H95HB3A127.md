Let $P$ be a point inside $\triangle ABC$, then $P$ is the positive Brocard point if and only if
$$
\frac{S_{\triangle P B C}}{c^{2} a^{2}}=\frac{S_{\triangle P C A}}{a^{2} b^{2}}=\frac{S_{\triangle P A B}}{b^{2} c^{2}}=\frac{S_{\triangle A B C}}{a^{2} b^{2}+b^{2} c^{2}+c^{2} a^{2}}
$$

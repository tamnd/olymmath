Container $A$ contains $a$ liters of brine solution with a concentration of $p$, and container $B$ contains $b$ liters of brine solution with a concentration of $q$ $(p \neq q)$. Now, $m$ liters of solution are simultaneously taken from both $A$ and $B$ and poured into the other $(m<a, m<b)$, and this operation is repeated $n$ times. Find the concentrations $p_{n}, q_{n}$ of the brine solutions in $A$ and $B$, and determine $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} p_{n}$ and $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} q_{n}$.

In the plane, a circle $k$ and 121 of its secants $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{121}$ are given. Inside this circle, a point $A_{i}$ is given on each line $p_{i}$. Prove that there exists a point $X$ on the circle $k$ such that the segment $A_{i} X$ forms an angle less than $21^{\circ}$ with the line $p_{i}$ for at least 29 different indices $i$.

(J. Šimša)

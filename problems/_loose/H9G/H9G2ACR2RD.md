a) a square $Q$ with side 1;

b) a circle $K$ of radius $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$;

c)* a regular hexagon $P$ with side $\frac{\sqrt{-3}}{3}$;

d) a regular triangle $T$ with side $\sqrt{3}$, and that none of the figures $Q, K, P, T$ can be reduced in size while maintaining their shape, so that they continue to satisfy the condition of the problem.

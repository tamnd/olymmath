A function $y=f(x)$ defined on $R$ has the following properties:
(1) For any $x \in R$, there is $f\left(x^{3}\right)=f^{3}(x)$;
(2) For any $x_{1}, x_{2} \in R, x_{1} \neq x_{2}$, there is $f\left(x_{1}\right) \neq f\left(x_{2}\right)$. Then the value of $f(0)+f(1)+f(-1)$ is
(A) 0.
(B) 1.
(C) -1.
(D) Uncertain.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

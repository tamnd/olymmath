Let's form two number sequences starting from the numbers $x_{1}=2$ and $y_{1}=1$ based on the following rules:

$$
x_{k}=2 x_{k-1}+3 y_{k-1}, y_{k}=x_{k-1}+2 y_{k-1}
$$

where $k=2,3,4, \ldots$ Show that for the terms of the same order in the two sequences, the difference $x_{k}^{2}-3 y_{k}^{2}$ is constant.

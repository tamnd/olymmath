Let $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$ be a periodic sequence, that is, for some natural $T$, $a_{n+T}=a_{n}$ $(n \geq 0)$. Prove that

a) among all periods of this sequence, there exists a period of the smallest length $t$;

b) $T$ is divisible by $t$.

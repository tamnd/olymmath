Given $n^{2}+n$ right angles, each made by welding two iron rods of length 1. These right angles are used to form a square grid, which contains $n^{2}$ $1 \times 1$ small squares. We call the right angles with edges pointing upwards and to the right as type $A$, and the right angles with edges pointing downwards and to the left as type $B$. Prove that the number of type $A$ right angles is equal to the number of type $B$ right angles in the grid.

$n^{2}$ numbers $x_{i j}^{\prime}(i, j=1,2, \ldots, n)$ satisfy the system of $n^{3}$ equations:

$$
x_{i j}+x_{l k}+x_{k l}=0 (i, j, k=1,2, \ldots, n)
$$

Prove that there exist such numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$, that $x_{i j}=a_{i}-a_{i}$ for any $i$ and $j$.

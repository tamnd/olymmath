(Canada, 1988). Consider a set of $r$ natural numbers $S=\left\{a_{1}, \ldots, a_{r}\right\}$. If $A$ is a non-empty subset of $S$, we denote $p_{A}$ as the product of its elements, and $m(S)$ as the arithmetic mean of the $p_{A}$ for all non-empty subsets $A$ of $S$.

We assume that $m(S)=13$ and $m\left(S \cup\left\{a_{r+1}\right\}\right)=49$. Calculate $a_{r+1}$ as well as the values of the elements of $S$.

Given the ellipse $C:(x-2)^{2}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$, the line $l: x-y+1=0$, and the point $R(1,4)$.
( I ) Find a point $M$ inside the ellipse $C$ that satisfies the following condition: If a line through $M$ intersects line $l$ at point $Q$ and intersects the ellipse $C$ at two points $A, B$, then it must be true that $\frac{|A M|}{|M B|}=\frac{|A Q|}{|Q B|}$. Does such a point $M$ exist?
(II) Draw a line through point $R$ that intersects the ellipse at points $E, F$. If $|R E|=|E F|$, find the equation of the line $E F$.

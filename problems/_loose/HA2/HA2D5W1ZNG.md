Three, (50 points) Given that $p$ is an odd prime, discuss the following (1) and (2) respectively, whether there exist $x_{1}$, $x_{2}, \cdots, x_{p-1} \in\{0,1, \cdots, p-1\}$, not all zero, such that:
(1) For any positive integer $t$, and $(p-1) \nmid t$, we have
$$
p!\left(x_{1}+2^{t} x_{2}+3^{t} x_{3}+\cdots+(p-1)^{t} x_{p-1}\right) ;
$$
(2) For any positive integer $t$, we have
$$
p \mid\left(x_{1}+2^{t} x_{2}+3^{t} x_{3}+\cdots+(p-1)^{t} x_{p-1}\right) ?
$$

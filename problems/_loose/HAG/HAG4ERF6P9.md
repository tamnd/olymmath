Let the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ have its left and right foci as $F_{1}, F_{2}$, with a focal distance of $2 c$. The point $N\left(\frac{3 c}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2} c}{2}\right)$ is inside the ellipse, and point $M$ is a moving point on the ellipse $C$, such that $\left|M F_{1}\right|+|M N|<2 \sqrt{3}\left|F_{1} F_{2}\right|$ always holds. Then the range of the eccentricity of the ellipse $C$ is
A. $\left(0, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$
B. $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}, 1\right)$
C. $\left(\frac{4 \sqrt{3}}{21}, 1\right)$
D. $\left(\frac{4 \sqrt{3}}{21}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$

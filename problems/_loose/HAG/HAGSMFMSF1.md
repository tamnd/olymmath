When Annika and $3$ of her friends play Buffalo Shuffle-o, each player is dealt $15$ cards. Suppose $2$ more friends join the next game. How many cards will be dealt to each player? $\textbf{(A) } 8 \textbf{(B) } 9 \textbf{(C) } 10 \textbf{(D) } 11 \textbf{(E) } 12$

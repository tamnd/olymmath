In triangle $ABC$, let the intersection point of the angle bisectors $AA_1, BB_1, CC_1$ be $O$. Prove that $8 \leq \frac{AO}{A_1O} \cdot \frac{BO}{B_1O} \cdot \frac{CO}{C_1O} \leq \frac{4R}{r}$, where $R$ is the circumradius and $r$ is the inradius.

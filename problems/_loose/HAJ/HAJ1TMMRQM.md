Let $P$ be an internal point of triangle $A B C$. Let us denote by $p_{a}, p_{b}$ and $p_{c}$ the distances from point $P$ to the sides $B C, C A$ and $A B$ respectively, and by $h_{a}, h_{b}$ and $h_{c}$ the altitudes dropped from $A, B$ and $C$ respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{p_{a}}{h_{a}}+\frac{p_{b}}{h_{b}}+\frac{p_{c}}{h_{c}}=1
$$

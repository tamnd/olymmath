Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a circle in the plane, and $\mathcal{K}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{K}_{2}$ be disjoint circles that are internally tangent to circle $\mathcal{K}$ at points $A$ and $B$. Let $t$ be the common tangent of circles $\mathcal{K}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{K}_{2}$, touching them at points $C$ and $D$ such that $\mathcal{K}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{K}_{2}$ are on the same side of line $t$, and the center of circle $\mathcal{K}$ is on the other side. Let $E$ be the intersection of lines $A C$ and $B D$. Prove that point $E$ lies on circle $\mathcal{K}$.

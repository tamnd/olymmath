In space, two intersecting planes $\tau$ and $\sigma$ are given. On the line of their intersection, a point $A$ is taken. Prove that among all lines lying in plane $\tau$ and passing through point $A$, the one that forms the greatest angle with plane $\sigma$ is the one that is perpendicular to the line of intersection of the planes.

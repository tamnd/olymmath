(13 (40 points) Let $\theta_{i}$ be real numbers, and $x_{i}=1+3 \sin ^{2} \theta_{i}, i=1,2, \cdots, n$, prove:
$$
\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}}\right) \leqslant\left(\frac{5 n}{4}\right)^{2} \text {. }
$$

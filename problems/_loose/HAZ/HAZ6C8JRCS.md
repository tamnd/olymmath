Prove that if the numbers $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ do not exceed one in absolute value, then

$$
\sqrt{1-x_{1}^{2}}+\sqrt{1-x_{2}^{2}} \leqslant 2 \sqrt{1-\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}\right)^{2}}
$$

For which values of $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ is the left side of this inequality exactly equal to the right side?

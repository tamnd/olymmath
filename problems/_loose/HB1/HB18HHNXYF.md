Given a natural number $k$, greater than 1. A natural number $n$, greater than 1 and coprime with $k$, is called correct if for any natural divisor $d (d < n)$ of the number $n$, the number $d + k$ is not coprime with $n$. Prove that the number of correct numbers is finite. (S. Berlov)

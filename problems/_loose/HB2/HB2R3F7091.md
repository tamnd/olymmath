Consider a quarter circle bounded by the radii $O A$ and $O B$. Draw a line parallel to the chord $A B$ that intersects the quarter circle, and denote one of the intersection points of this line with the quarter circle as $C$, and the intersection points with the rays $O A$ and $O B$ as $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove that

$$
A B^{2}=P C^{2}+Q C^{2}
$$

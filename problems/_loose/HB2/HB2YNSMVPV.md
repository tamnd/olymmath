For the internal angles $\alpha=\measuredangle B A C$ and $\beta=\measuredangle A B C$ of triangle $A B C$, it holds that $\alpha-\beta=90^{\circ}$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the intersection points of line $A B$ with the internal and external angle bisectors of vertex $C$, respectively. Prove that $\overline{C M}=\overline{C N}$!

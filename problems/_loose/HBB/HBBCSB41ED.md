In isosceles $\triangle ABC$, $AC = BC$, and $I$ is its incenter. $P$ is a point on the circumcircle of $\triangle IAB$ inside $\triangle ABC$. A line through $P$ parallel to $AB$ intersects $CA$ and $CB$ at $M$ and $N$, respectively. Points $E$ and $F$ are taken on $AB$ such that $PE \parallel CA$ and $PF \parallel CB$. Prove that the intersection of lines $ME$ and $NF$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$.

Let $x$ and $y$ be distinct real numbers such that $2xy + 1 \neq 0$ and let

$$
A=\frac{6x^2y^2+xy-1}{2xy+1} \text{and} B=\frac{x(x^2-1)-y(y^2-1)}{x-y}
$$

Determine which number is greater, $A$ or $B$.

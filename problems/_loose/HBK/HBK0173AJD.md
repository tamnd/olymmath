In Bolondócia, the road network is such that any city can be reached by roads; the road segments do not cross each other (road segment: a part of a road connecting two cities, which does not touch any other city in between). Due to the excessive congestion of the roads, the Ministry of Discouraging Travel (ULM) has implemented the following measures:

A) traffic on every road segment should be one-way;

B) the direction of the road segments should be such that at any given time, there should be no route that leads back to the starting city from any city.

A portion of Bolondócia's population criticized the situation created by the measures, as, for example, there were only incoming roads to the capital. Therefore, the ULM ordered the following:

C) if there are cities from which only outgoing roads exist, then one of them should have its outgoing road segments reversed in direction at the beginning of the following week; the direction of the other road segments remains unchanged.

The Institute of Future Studies immediately joined in to investigate whether there will come a week when all roads from the capital will lead out.

Businessman Ramūnas gave $2000$ balloons to kindergarten children. The balloons were in $20$ different colors, $100$ of each color. The kindergarten director Austėja gave each of the $100$ children attending the kindergarten $20$ balloons, but did not take into account their color. The children would like to exchange balloons so that each of them has $20$ balloons of different colors. Austėja allows any two children to exchange two balloons (when they take one balloon from each other), but not just any way, but only if each of them takes from the other a balloon of a color that he did not have before this exchange between them. Can the children always (no matter how Austėja initially distributed the balloons) exchange balloons in such a way that after a finite number of such exchanges each of them would have $20$ balloons of different colors?

Consider the equation in $x$
$$
(1+a) x^{4}+x^{3}-(3 a+2) x^{2}-4 a=0
$$
(1) Does the equation always have a real solution for any real number $a$?
(2) Is there a real number $x_{0}$ such that, regardless of what real number $a$ is, $x_{0}$ is never a solution to this equation?

Ivan Tsarevich needs to obtain the apples of youth. Baba Yaga, Koschei, and Leshy gave him the following answers.

Baba Yaga: "Yes, Koschei has them. He took them and hasn't given them back for 100 years. And Leshy is a good guy: if he had them, he would have given them to me."

Koschei: "Baba Yaga is a trickster, she hid them, they are with her, and I don't have them."

Leshy: "I don't have them. What do I need them for? I am handsome without them. And Baba Yaga doesn't have them either: she has become very old."

Vasilsa the Wise warned Ivan that this trio are all liars, none of them will ever tell the truth, and the apples of youth are with at least one of them.

Who has the apples of youth? Who doesn't have them? About whom is there not enough information? (The statement "A and B" is false if and only if at least one of the statements A or B is false)?

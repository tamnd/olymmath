Several glass marbles are arranged in three piles. A boy, who has an unlimited supply of marbles, can, in one move, take one marble from each pile or add from his supply to one of the piles as many marbles as there are already in it. Prove that in several moves the boy can achieve that there will be no marbles left in any of the piles.

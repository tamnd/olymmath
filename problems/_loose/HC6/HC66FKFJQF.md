Let $f$ be a function defined on the positive integers, taking positive integral values, such that

$f(a)f(b) = f(ab)$ for all positive integers $a$ and $b$,
$f(a) < f(b)$ if $a < b$,
$f(3) \geq 7$.

Find the smallest possible value of $f(3)$.

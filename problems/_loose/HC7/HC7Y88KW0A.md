Let $u_{0}, u_{1}, u_{2}, \ldots, u_{n}, \ldots$ be some Fibonacci sequence. Prove that the $n$-th term of this sequence is expressed in terms of $u_{0}$ and $u_{1}$ by the formula

$$
u_{n}=a_{n-1} u_{0}+a_{n} u_{1}{ }^{\circ}
$$

(In order for this formula to remain valid for $n=0$, we must set $a_{-1}=1$.)

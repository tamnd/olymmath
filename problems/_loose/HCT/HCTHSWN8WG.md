Find all positive $x, y, z$ for which the expression

$$
\frac{x^{2} y z}{324}+\frac{144 y}{x z}+\frac{9}{4 x y^{2}}
$$

takes the smallest value and among all such $x, y, z$ the expression

$$
\frac{z}{16 y}+\frac{x}{9}
$$

is also minimal.

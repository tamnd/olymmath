Given a square $ABCD$ with side length 1. A point $P$ is chosen arbitrarily on the side $AB$. The line passing through vertex $C$ and perpendicular to line $CP$ intersects the extensions of sides $AB$ and $AD$ at points $S$ and $T$, respectively. If $\overline{AS}=x$ and $\overline{AT}=y$, prove that $\log (x+y)=\log x+\log y$.

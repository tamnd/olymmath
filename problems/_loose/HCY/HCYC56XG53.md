A sequence of numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ satisfies the conditions: $a_{0}=0, 0 \leq a_{k+1}-a_{k} \leq 1$ for $k=0,1, \ldots, n-1$. Prove the inequality $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{3} \leq\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}\right)^{2}$.

Let $l, m$ be two skew lines, on $l$ there are three points $A, B, C$, and $A B=B C$. Draw perpendiculars from $A, B, C$ to $m$, denoted as $A D, B E, C F$, with feet of the perpendiculars being $D, E, F$ respectively. Given $A D=\sqrt{15}, B E=\frac{7}{2}, C F=\sqrt{10}$. Find the distance between $l$ and $m$.

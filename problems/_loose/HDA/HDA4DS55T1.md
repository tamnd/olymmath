Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, and around each vertex, draw circles with equal radius $a, b, c$, then take the circle $d$ which touches all three and encloses them. From any point on the circle $d$, draw a tangent to each of the circles $a, b$, and $c$. It is to be proven that the sum of the lengths of any two of the resulting three tangent segments is equal to the length of the third.

Is a similar statement valid for the points of the circle $d^{*}$ which touches each of $a, b, c$ externally, and for the circle which touches each of them internally?

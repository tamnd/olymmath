Prove that the distances $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$ from the incenter of a triangle to its vertices satisfy the equation:

$$
\frac{d_{1}^{2}}{b c}+\frac{d_{2}^{2}}{c a}+\frac{d_{3}^{2}}{a b}=1
$$

Prove the converse theorem.

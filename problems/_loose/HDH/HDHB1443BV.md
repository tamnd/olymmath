Given is a rectangle $A B C D$ whose side lengths differ by $4 \mathrm{~cm}$, and its perimeter is 16 $\mathrm{dm}$, with $|A B|>|B C|$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of side $\overline{B C}$ of the rectangle. Point $N$ lies on side $\overline{C D}$ and it is given that $|N C|=3|D N|$. Segments $\overline{A M}$ and $\overline{B N}$ intersect at point $P$. Is the area of triangle $A B P$ greater or the area of quadrilateral $P M C N$? By how much?

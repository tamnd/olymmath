(20 points) For how many natural numbers $n$, not exceeding 600, are the triples of numbers

$$
\left[\frac{n}{2}\right],\left[\frac{n}{3}\right],\left[\frac{n}{5}\right] \text{and} \left[\frac{n+1}{2}\right],\left[\frac{n+1}{3}\right],\left[\frac{n+1}{5}\right]
$$

different? As always, $[x]$ denotes the integer part of $x$, i.e., the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

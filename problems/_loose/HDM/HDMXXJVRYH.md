For every positive integer $m > 1$ , let $p(m)$ be the largest prime number that divides $m$ . For $m = 1$ , define $p(1) = 1$ .**a)** Prove that there exists a positive integer $n$ such that the numbers $p(n - 2022)$ , $p(n)$ , and $p(n + 2022)$ are all less than $\frac{\sqrt{n}}{20}$ .
**b)** Given a positive integer $N$ , prove that there exists a positive integer $n$ such that the numbers $p(n - 2022)$ , $p(n)$ , and $p(n + 2022)$ are all less than $\frac{\sqrt{n}}{N}$ .

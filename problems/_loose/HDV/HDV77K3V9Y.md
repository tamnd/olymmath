On the circular fortification of the castle, there are several towers. Five black and five red knights will be distributed among them (there can be more than one and of different colors in each tower) and start guarding. After each hour, all black knights will move to the neighboring tower in the direction of the clock hands, and all red knights will move to the neighboring tower in the opposite direction. Prove the following statements:

a) If there are eight towers, the knights can be initially distributed in such a way that during each hour, there will be at least one knight in each tower.

b) If there are seven towers, at some hour at least one tower will remain unoccupied, regardless of how the knights are initially distributed.

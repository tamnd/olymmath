Let $n$ be a natural number. There are $n$ climbing poles in a monkey cage with $n$ monkeys. To give the monkeys some exercise, the keepers place a banana at the top of each pole for feeding. In addition, they connect the poles with a finite number of ropes so that two different rope ends are attached at different points. If a monkey climbs up a pole and finds a rope, it cannot resist and will shimmy over the rope before continuing its ascent. Each monkey starts at a different pole. Show that each monkey gets a banana.

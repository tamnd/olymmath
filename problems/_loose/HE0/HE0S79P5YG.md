Given a line $l$ and a point $P$ outside $l$, there are two moving points $Q, R$ on $l$ such that $\angle Q P R$ is a constant. Let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle P Q R$, and the altitude from vertex $P$ is $P H$, the angle bisector is $P S$, with points $S$ and $H$ both on $l$. $Q S$ intersects $P H$ at $D$. Prove that $D$ is a fixed point.

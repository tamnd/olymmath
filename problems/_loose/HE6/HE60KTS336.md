The segments $O A, O B$, and $O C$ are mutually perpendicular and their lengths are $a, b$, and $c$ respectively. Prove that the distance from point $O$ to the plane $A B C$ is

$$
\left(\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}+\frac{1}{c^{2}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}
$$

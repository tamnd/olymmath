(1988 Sichuan Province Competition Question) Given $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \in \mathbf{R}$, and $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}=a(a>0), x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\cdots+$
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{n}^{2} & =\frac{a^{2}}{n-1}(n \in \mathbf{N}, \text { and } n \geqslant 2), \text { prove: } \\
0 \leqslant x_{i} & \leqslant \frac{2 a}{n}(i=1,2, \cdots, n) .
\end{aligned}
$$

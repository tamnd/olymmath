In $\triangle A B C$, $\angle A=60^{\circ}$ (i.e., $\angle A$ is the arithmetic mean of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$), $O$, $I$, and $H$ are the circumcenter, incenter, and orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$, respectively. Then the following conclusions hold:
(1) $B$, $O$, $I$, $H$, $C$ are concyclic;
(2) $A H=A O=R, O I=I H$; if the line $O H$ intersects $A B$ and $A C$ at $P$ and $Q$, respectively, then $P O=H Q$;
(3) $O H=|A B-A C|$ (when $A B \neq A C$), $O H=$ $\frac{|H B-H C|}{\sqrt{3}}$ (when $A B \neq A C$);
(4) If the line $B I$ intersects $A C$ at $F$, then $\frac{1}{I B}+\frac{1}{I C}=\frac{1}{I F}$.

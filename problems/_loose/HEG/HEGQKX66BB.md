$A$ the time required to complete a job is $m$ times the time required for $B$ and $C$ to complete the job together; the time required for $B$ to complete the job is $n$ times the time required for $A$ and $C$ to complete the job together, and the time required for $C$ to complete the job is $x$ times the time required for $A$ and $B$ to complete the job together, then $x$ is
(A) $\frac{2 m n}{m+n}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{2(m+n)}$.
(C) $\frac{1}{m+n-m n}$.
(D) $\frac{1-m n}{m+n+2 m n}$.
(E) $\frac{m+n+2}{m n-1}$.
(16th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1965)

In Benoît's house, there are 2023 switches and $2023 \times 1011$ light bulbs. Each light bulb corresponds to a pair of switches and lights up precisely when both switches are on. However, Benoît has forgotten to label the light bulbs and does not remember which bulb corresponds to which pair of switches. Initially, all switches are off. Each minute, Benoît can change the state of one switch and then notes the state of the bulbs. What is the minimum time Benoît will need to determine all the correspondences between his bulbs and switches?

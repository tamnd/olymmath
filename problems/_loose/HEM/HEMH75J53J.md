The 18th question: Distinct real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ and distinct real numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{m}$ all belong to $[0,1)$. Write the fractional parts of their pairwise sums on the blackboard. It is known that there are at most $m+n-2$ different numbers on the blackboard. Prove: Each number on the blackboard appears at least twice.

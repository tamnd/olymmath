On the set $G=(0, \infty) \backslash\{1\}$, the operation $x * y=x^{\ln y}$ is defined. It is known that $(G, *)$ is a commutative group with the neutral element $e$, the base of the natural logarithm.

a) If $H=\left\{e^{\alpha} \mid \alpha \in Q^{*}\right\}$, show that $H$ is a subgroup of the group $(G, *)$.

b) Considering the group $\left(R^{*}, \cdot\right)$, show that $f: G \rightarrow R^{*}, f(x)=\ln x$ is a group isomorphism.

Given an angle with vertex $A$ and two points $M$ and $N$ inside it. A line through $M$ intersects the sides of the angle at points $B$ and $C$. Prove that for the area of quadrilateral $A B N C$ to be the smallest, it is necessary and sufficient that the line $B C$ intersects $A N$ at a point $P$ such that $|B P|=|M C|$. Provide a method for constructing this line.

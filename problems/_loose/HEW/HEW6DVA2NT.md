Numbers instead of letters. One morning, Professor Rackbrain proposed the following rather difficult problem to his young friends. He wrote down the letters of the alphabet in the following order:

$$
A B C D \times E F G H I=A C G E F H I B D
$$

- Each letter, he said, represents its own digit from 1 to 9 (0 is excluded). A four-digit number, multiplied by a five-digit number, gives a number containing all 9 digits in the specified order. Can you substitute digits for the letters so that the written equation is satisfied?

Let $A B C$ be an isosceles right triangle with $\angle C=90^{\circ}$. Let $X$ be a point on the line segment $A C$, and let $Y$ and $Z$ be, respectively, the feet of the perpendiculars from $A$ and $C$ to the line $B X$. Prove that

$$
B Z=Y Z+A Y
$$

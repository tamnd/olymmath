A square $A B C D$ with side length $10 \mathrm{~cm}$ is given. On side $\overline{A B}$, point $E$ is chosen, on side $\overline{B C}$, point $F$, on side $\overline{C D}$, point $G$, and on side $\overline{D A}$, point $H$ such that $E F G H$ is also a square. Calculate the length of segment $\overline{A E}$ so that the sum of the area of the circle inscribed in square $E F G H$ and the areas of the circles inscribed in triangles $E B F$, $F C G$, $G D H$, and $H A E$ is minimized.

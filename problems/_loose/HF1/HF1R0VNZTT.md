If in a right triangle $A B C$ with the right angle at $A$, a square $D E F G$ is inscribed such that the side $D E$ lies on the hypotenuse $B C$, then $D E$ is the geometric mean of $B D$ and $E C$ (on the hypotenuse, the points are in the order $B, D, E, C$)

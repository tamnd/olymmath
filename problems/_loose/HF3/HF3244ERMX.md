Petya and Kolya are playing the following game: they take turns changing one of the coefficients $a$ or $b$ of the quadratic trinomial $x^{2}+a x+b$: Petya by 1, Kolya by 1 or by 3. Kolya wins if after one of the players' moves, the trinomial has integer roots. Is it true that Kolya can win with any initial integer coefficients $a$ and $b$ regardless of Petya's play?

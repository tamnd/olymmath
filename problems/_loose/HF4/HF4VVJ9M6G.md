The circumcircle of $ODE$ meets $AD$ at second point $F$ (different to $D$). The line $EF$ meets $AB$ at $M$, the line $DM$ meet $(O)$ at the second point $K$ (different to $D$).
Prove that $K,F,C$ are collinear.

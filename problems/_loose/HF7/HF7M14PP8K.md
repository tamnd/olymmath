Prove the following statement:

If $a, b, c$ are positive real numbers for which $b^{2}-a^{2}=c^{2}-b^{2} $ (1) holds, then it also always holds that

$$
\frac{1}{a+c}-\frac{1}{b+c}=\frac{1}{a+b}-\frac{1}{a+c}
$$

In the small town $A$, the butcher is closed every Monday, the household goods store every Tuesday, and the shoemaker every Thursday. The optician is only open on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. All shops are closed on Sundays.

One day, the friends Anja, Ilka, Katrin, and Susann each went to one of these four shops. When they met on the way, they said:

(1) Anja: "Susann and I wanted to go shopping earlier this week, but there was no day when we could both have done our shopping."

(2) Ilka: "I didn't actually want to go shopping today, but the shop I want to go to is closed tomorrow."

(3) Katrin: "I could have gotten everything done yesterday or the day before."

(4) Susann: "I could have done my shopping yesterday or even tomorrow."

Investigate whether these statements are consistent with each other and whether they uniquely determine:

(a) which of the girls went to which of the specified shops.

(b) on which day of the week the conversation took place!

If this is the case, then give the corresponding answers to questions (a) and (b)!

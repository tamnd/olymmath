Let $a$ and $n$ be two integers that are coprime. Show that there exists a unique integer $b$ modulo $n$ such that $a b \equiv 1(\bmod n)$.

In the following, we denote $a^{-1}$ as the inverse of $a$ modulo $n$ (when the considered modulo is clear).

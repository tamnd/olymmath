Let $n$ be an integer, $n \geq 2$, and let $\mathbf{A}$ be a matrix in $\mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})$, such that $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{A}^{2}$ have different ranks. Show that there exists a non-zero matrix $\mathbf{B}$ in $\mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})$, such that $\mathbf{A B}=\mathbf{B A}=\mathbf{B}^{2}=\mathbf{O}_{n}$.

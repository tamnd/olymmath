If the quadratic equation $(b-c) x^{2}+(a-b) x+c-a=0$ has two equal real roots, and $b \neq c$, then the relationship between $a, b, c$ is
(A) $b=\frac{a-c}{2}$.
(B) $b=\frac{a+c}{2}$.
(C) $a=\frac{b+c}{2}$.
(D) $c=\frac{a+b}{2}$.
(China Sichuan Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

Inside the sides $AB$ and $AC$ of a given triangle $ABC$, points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen, respectively. Let $R$ be the intersection of the lines $BQ$ and $CP$, and let $p, q, r$ be the distances from points $P, Q$, $R$ to the line $BC$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}>\frac{1}{r}
$$

(Patrik Bak)

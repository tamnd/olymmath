Buses. (From 8th grade, 2 points) At the stop near the Absent-Minded Scholar's house, buses of two routes stop: No. 152 and No. 251. Both of them head to the metro station. The interval between buses No. 152 is exactly 5 minutes, and the interval between buses of route No. 251 is exactly 7 minutes. The intervals are strictly observed, but the two routes are not coordinated with each other and their schedules do not depend on each other. At a completely random moment in time, the Absent-Minded Scholar comes to the stop and gets on the first bus that arrives to get to the metro. What is the probability that the Scholar will get on bus No. 251?

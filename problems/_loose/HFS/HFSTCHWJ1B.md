For all positive integers $n(n \geqslant 3)$, try to find those for which, after removing any one square from an $n \times n$ grid paper, the remaining grid paper can definitely be entirely cut into L-shaped paper pieces. Here, an L-shaped paper piece refers to the three small squares left after cutting one small square from a $2 \times 2$ grid paper piece.

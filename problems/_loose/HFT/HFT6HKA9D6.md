Let $K, L, M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the edges $A B, B C$, $C D$ and $D A$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$.

a) Prove that $A C \perp B D$ if and only if $K M = L N$.
b) Prove that the tetrahedron is orthocentric if and only if the segments connecting the midpoints of opposite edges are equal.

Acute $\triangle A B C$ is inscribed in $\odot O$. Tangents to $\odot O$ are drawn through points $B$ and $C$, and they intersect the tangent to $\odot O$ at point $A$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. $A D$ is the altitude from $A$ to side $B C$. Prove that $A D$ bisects $\angle M D N$.
(1998 All-Russian Olympiad Problem)

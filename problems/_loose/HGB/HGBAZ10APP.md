Let $a, b$ be two positive integers. The general term of the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ is given by $x_{n}=a^{n}+b^{n}(n=1,2, \cdots)$. Prove: There exists an infinite sequence of positive integers $m_{1}<m_{2}<\cdots$, such that any two terms in the sequence $\left\{x_{m_{i}}\right\}$ are coprime if and only if $a$ and $b$ are coprime and have different parity.

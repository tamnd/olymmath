G36 (16-2, Finland) Let the three interior angles of $\triangle ABC$ be $\angle A=\alpha, \angle B=\beta$, $\angle C=\gamma$. Prove that the necessary and sufficient condition for there to be a point $D$ on $AB$ such that $CD$ is the geometric mean of $AD$ and $BD$ is:
$$
\sin \alpha \sin \beta \leqslant \sin ^{2} \frac{\gamma}{2} .
$$

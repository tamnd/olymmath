An isosceles triangle $P Q T$ with base $P Q$ is inscribed in a circle $\Omega$. Chords $A B$ and $C D$, parallel to the line $P Q$, intersect the side $Q T$ at points $L$ and $M$ respectively, and $Q L=L M=M T$. Find the radius of the circle $\Omega$ and the area of triangle $P Q T$, if $A B=2 \sqrt{14}, C D=2 \sqrt{11}$, and the center $O$ of the circle $\Omega$ is located between the lines $A B$ and $C D$.

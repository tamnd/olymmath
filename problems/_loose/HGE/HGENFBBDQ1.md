a) Calculate $3 p(-2)+2 q(3)$.

b) Write the leading term of the polynomial $2(p(x))^{2}$.

c) Calculate $p(x) \cdot(q(x))^{2}$.

d) Divide $p(x)$ by $q(x)$.

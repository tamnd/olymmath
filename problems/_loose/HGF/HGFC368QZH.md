In the sequence $1,9,7,5,2, \cdots$, starting from the 5th term, each number is equal to the unit digit of the sum of the previous 4 numbers. In this sequence, (1) will there be a continuous 4 terms of $1,2,3,4$ and 3,2,6,9? (2) Will there be another continuous 4 terms of $1,9,7,5$? (3) Will there be a continuous 4 terms of $8,1,9,7$?

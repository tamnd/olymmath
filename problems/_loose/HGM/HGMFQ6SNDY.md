The *runcible* positive integers are defined recursively as follows:

- $1$ and $2$ are runcible
- If $a$ and $b$ are runcible (where $a$ and $b$ are not necessarily distinct) then $2a + 3b$ is runcible.

Is $2024$ runcible?

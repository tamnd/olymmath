A bronze lion was carved, and on it the following inscription was written: "I can pour water from my eyes, throat, and right leg. If I open my right eye, I fill my basin in 2 days, if I open my left eye, in 3 days. The water flowing from my leg fills the basin in 4 days, and the water flowing from my throat fills it in 6 hours. Tell me how long it would take to fill the basin if water flowed from my eyes, throat, and leg all at once?"

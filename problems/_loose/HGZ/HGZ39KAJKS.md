Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2^{2016}}$ be positive integers. For any positive integer $n \in\left[1,2^{2016}\right]$, we have $a_{n} \leqslant 2016$, and $\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}+1$ is a perfect square. Prove that $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2^{2016}}$ contains at least one number that is 1.
$(2016$, Serbian Mathematical Olympiad)

Given the circle $k_{1}$ with center $O_{1}$ and the line $e$ passing through its center. Consider all circles $k_{2}$ that pass through the point $O_{1}$, have their centers on the line $e$, and intersect $k_{1}$ at two points. Draw the common tangents of these circles with $k_{1}$!

What figure do the points of tangency on $k_{2}$ form, when $k_{2}$ takes all possible positions?

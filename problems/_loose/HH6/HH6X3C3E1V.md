Given a regular tetrahedron and a segment $P Q$ in space. We project the vector $P Q$ perpendicularly onto the faces of the tetrahedron, resulting in the projections $\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{1}}, \mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{2}}, \mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{3}}$, and $\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{4}}$. Prove that $\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{1}}+\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{2}}+\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{3}}+\mathbf{v}_{\mathbf{4}}=\frac{8}{3} \overrightarrow{P Q}$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with circumcircle $\Gamma$. We denote $S$ as the tangency point of $C_{1}$, the $A$-mixtilinear circle (i.e., the circle tangent to $[AB]$, $[AC]$, and $\Gamma$) with $\Gamma$, and $T$ as the tangency point of $C_{2}$, the $A$-excircle with $[BC]$. Show that $\widehat{B A S}=\widehat{T A C}$.

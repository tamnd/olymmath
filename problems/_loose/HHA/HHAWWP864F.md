Let $a>0$ and $f:[0, a] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function with the property that $f(0)=f(a)=1$. Let $I=\int_{0}^{a} f(x) d x$. If $\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right| \leq 1, \forall x \in(0, a)$, prove that $|I-a| \leq \frac{a^{2}}{2}$.

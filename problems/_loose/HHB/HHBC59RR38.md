(The Towers of Hanoi) In the temple of Benares, three diamond needles are erected. On the left needle are threaded 64 discs of pure gold, the largest at the base and the others, increasingly smaller, stacked to the top. Day and night, the monks move the discs from one needle to another, following two rules: they can only move one disc at a time, and they cannot place a disc on a smaller one. It is said that when the 64 discs are on the right needle, the temple will collapse and it will be the end of the world. How much time do we have left?

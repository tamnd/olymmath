24.- We call a vertex $v \in G$ dominate an end $\omega$ of $G$ if any of the following three conditions hold:
(i) For every ray $R \in \omega$, there is an infinite $v-(R-v)$ path in $G$.
(ii) For every ray $R \in \omega$, there is an infinite $v-(R-v)$ walk in $G$.
(iii) There is no finite subset of $V(G-v)$ that separates $v$ from a ray in $\omega$.
Prove that the above three conditions are equivalent.

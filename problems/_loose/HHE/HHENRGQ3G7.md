Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,...$ be a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers. A number $a_n$ in the sequence is said to be *lucky* if it is the sum of several (not necessarily distinct) smaller terms of the sequence, and *unlucky*otherwise. (For example, in the sequence $4,6,14,15,25,...$ numbers $4,6,15$ are *unlucky*, while $14 = 4+4+6$ and $25 = 4+6+15$ are *lucky*.) Prove that there are only finitely many *unlucky*numbers in the sequence.

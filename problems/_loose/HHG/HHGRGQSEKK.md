Let $n$ be an odd integer and $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be distinct real numbers. Show that the numbers in the set $\left\{\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|, i<j\right\}$ can be partitioned into two disjoint subsets $A$ and $B$ such that the sum of the elements in $A$ is equal to the sum of the elements in $B$.

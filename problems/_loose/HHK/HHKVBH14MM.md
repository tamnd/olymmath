If for any two numbers $a, b$ in a number set $A$, their sum $a+b$, difference $a-b$, and product $a b$ are all within the number set $A$, then the number set $A$ is called a number ring. Among the following six number sets:
(1) $Z=\{$ all integers $\}$
(2) $N=\{$ all natural numbers $\}$
(3) $Q=\{$ all rational numbers $\}$
(4) $R=\{$ all real numbers $\}$
(5) $M=\{$ all numbers of the form $n+m \sqrt{2}$, where $n, m$ are integers $\}$
(6) $P=\left\{\right.$ all numbers of the form $\frac{m}{2 n}$, where $n, m$ are natural numbers $\}$ are number rings:
(A) 6.
(B) 5.
(C) 4.
(D) 3.
(E) 1.
(China Partial Provinces and Cities Junior High School Mathematics Correspondence Competition, 1985)

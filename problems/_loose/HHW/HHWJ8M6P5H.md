Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at $P$ and $Q$. Let $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ be two variable points on $\Gamma_{1}$, and $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ be the second points of intersection of $\Gamma_{2}$ with $\left(A_{1} P\right)$ and $\left(B_{1} P\right)$, respectively. Let $C=\left(A_{1} B_{1}\right) \cap\left(A_{2} B_{2}\right)$.

Show that the center $O$ of the circumcircle of triangle $C A_{1} A_{2}$ remains on a fixed circle as $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ vary.

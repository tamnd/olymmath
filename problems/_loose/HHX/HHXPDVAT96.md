Let $a, b, c$ be three positive numbers such that the sum of any two of them is at most 1. It is to be proven that

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \leq a+b+c-a b-b c-c a \leq \frac{1}{2}\left(1+a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)
$$

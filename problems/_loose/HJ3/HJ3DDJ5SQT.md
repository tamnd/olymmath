On a plane, there are $n$ figures. Let $S_{i_{1} \ldots i_{k}}$ be the area of the intersection of the figures with numbers $i_{1}, \ldots, i_{k}$, and $S$ be the area of the part of the plane covered by these figures; $M_{k}$ be the sum of all $S_{i_{1} \ldots i_{k}}$. Prove that:
a) $S=M_{1}-M_{2}+M_{3}-\ldots+(-1)^{n+1} M_{n}$

b) $S \geqslant M_{1}-M_{2}+M_{3}-\ldots+(-1)^{m+1} M_{m}$ for $m$ even and $S \leqslant M_{1}-M_{2}+M_{3}-\ldots+(-1)^{m+1} M_{m}$

for $m$ odd.

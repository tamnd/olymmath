a) Prove that in a regular polyhedron, all faces, all edges, all plane angles, all dihedral angles, and all polyhedral angles are equal to each other.

b) Prove that a regular polyhedron can be superimposed on itself in such a way that any face $\mu_{1}$ and any edge $m_{1}$ belonging to this face coincide respectively with any face $\mu_{2}$ and an edge $m_{2}$ belonging to it.

Conversely, any polyhedron possessing this property is regular.

c) Prove that every regular polyhedron is also topologically regular.

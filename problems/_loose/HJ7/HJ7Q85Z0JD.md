Let $M, N$, and $P$ be points located on the sides $AB, BC$, and $CA$ of triangle $ABC$ and dividing these sides in the same ratio (i.e., $\frac{AM}{MB} = \frac{BN}{NC} = \frac{CP}{PA}$). Prove that:

a) the point of intersection of the medians of triangle $MNP$ coincides with the point of intersection of the medians of triangle $ABC$;

b) the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle formed by the lines $AN, BP$, and $CM$ coincides with the point of intersection of the medians of triangle $ABC$.

Given a triangle $\triangle A B C$ and a point $P$ inside it. Let

$$
A P \cap B C=\left\{A_{1}\right\}, B P \cap A C=\left\{B_{1}\right\}, C P \cap A B=\left\{C_{1}\right\}
$$

Prove that

$$
P_{\triangle A C_{1} P} \cdot P_{\triangle B A_{1} P} \cdot P_{\triangle C B_{1} P}=P_{\triangle B C_{1} P} \cdot P_{\triangle C A_{1} P} \cdot P_{\triangle A B_{1} P}
$$

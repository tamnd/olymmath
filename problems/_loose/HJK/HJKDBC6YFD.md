In a kindergarten, a teacher took $n>1$ identical cardboard rectangles and distributed them to $n$ children, giving one rectangle to each child. Each child cut their rectangle into several identical squares (the squares could be different for different children). It turned out that the total number of squares was a prime number. Prove that the original rectangles were squares.

(S. Berlov)

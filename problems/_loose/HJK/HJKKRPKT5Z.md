Let $z\neq0$ be a complex number satisfying $z^2=z+i|z|$. ($|z|$ denotes the length between the origin and $z$ in the complex plane.) Find $z\cdot\overline{z}$, where $\overline{z}=a-bi$ is the complex conjugate of $z=a+bi$.

Given three equidistant parallel lines. Express by points of the corresponding lines the values of the resistance, voltage and current in a conductor so as to obtain the voltage $V = I \cdot R$ by connecting with a ruler the points denoting the resistance $R$ and the current $I$ . (Each point of each scale denotes only one number).

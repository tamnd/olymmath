[4] On 29 cards, the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 29$ are written. A magician, with his eyes blindfolded, gives the cards to one of the spectators. The spectator hides two cards and gives the rest to the magician's assistant. The assistant selects two of them and the spectator tells the magician the numbers chosen by the assistant. Then the magician guesses which cards the spectator has hidden. What agreement between the magician and his assistant could be behind this trick?

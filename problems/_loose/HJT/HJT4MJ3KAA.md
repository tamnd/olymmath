Businessman Boris Mikhailovich decided to have a race with tractor driver Vasya on the highway. Since his "Lexus" is ten times faster than Vasya's tractor, he gave Vasya a head start and set off an hour after Vasya. After Vasya's tractor had driven exactly half of the planned route, a spring fell off, so he covered the remaining part of the journey at half the speed of the first half. As a result, Boris Mikhailovich had to stop at a nearby service station for 4 hours after encountering Vasya's spring, and then continued his journey at half the speed he had before. Prove that as a result, he fell behind Vasya by at least an hour. (S. Berlov)

Let point $P$ be any point on the hyperbola $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>0, b>0)$, and let the line through point $P$ intersect the two asymptotes $l_{1}: y=\frac{b}{a} x, l_{2}: y=-\frac{b}{a} x$ at points $P_{1}, P_{2}$, respectively. Let $\lambda=\frac{P_{1} P}{P P_{2}}$. Prove: $S_{\triangle O P_{1} P_{2}}=\frac{(1+\lambda)^{2}}{4|\lambda|} a b$.

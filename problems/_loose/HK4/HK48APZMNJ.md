If $a, b, c$ are non-negative real numbers with $ a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 1$, prove that:
$$ \frac {a}{b^2 + 1} + \frac {b}{c^2 + 1} + \frac {c}{a^2 + 1} \geq \frac {3}{4}(a\sqrt {a} + b\sqrt {b} + c\sqrt {c})^2$$

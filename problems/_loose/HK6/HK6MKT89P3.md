Let's call a company $k$-indivisible if in any partition of it into $k$ groups, there will be two acquaintances in one of the groups. Given a 3-indivisible company in which there are no four people who are all mutually acquainted. Prove that it can be divided into two companies, one of which is 2-indivisible, and the other is 1-indivisible.

(V. Dol'nikov)

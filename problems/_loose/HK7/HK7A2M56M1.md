$\sqrt{2} \cos 2 x \geqslant \sin x-\cos x \Longleftrightarrow(\cos x-\sin x)(\sqrt{2}(\cos x+\sin x)+1) \geqslant 0 \Longleftrightarrow$

$$
\Longleftrightarrow(\cos x-\sin x)\left(2 \cos \left(x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+1\right) \geqslant 0
$$

The solutions to the equation $(\cos x-\sin x)\left(2 \cos \left(x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+1\right)=0$ are the points $x=\frac{\pi}{4}+\pi n,-\frac{5 \pi}{12}+2 \pi n, \frac{11 \pi}{12}+2 \pi n$. Marking these points on the unit circle and applying the interval method, we get the answer: $\left[-\frac{5 \pi}{12}+2 \pi n ; \frac{\pi}{4}+2 \pi n\right] \cup\left[\frac{11 \pi}{12}+2 \pi n ; \frac{5 \pi}{4}+2 \pi n\right]$.

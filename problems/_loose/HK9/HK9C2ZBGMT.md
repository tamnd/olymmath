A thousand delegates from different countries arrived at the congress. It is known that any three of them can communicate with each other without the help of others (however, it is possible that one of the three will have to serve as a translator for the other two). Prove that all the delegates of the congress can be accommodated in a hotel with double rooms in such a way that in each room there will be delegates who can communicate with each other.

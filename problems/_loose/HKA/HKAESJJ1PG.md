Let $D$ be an arbitrary point on side $BC$, and let $E$ and $F$ be points on $AC$ and $AB$ such that $DE$ is parallel to $AB$, and $DF$ is parallel to $AC$. The circle passing through $D$, $E$, and $F$ intersects $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ again at points $D_{1}$, $E_{1}$, and $F_{1}$, respectively. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of intersection of $DE$ and $F_{1}D_{1}$, and $DF$ and $D_{1}E_{1}$. Prove that $M$ and $N$ lie on the symmedian from vertex $A$. Furthermore, if $D$ coincides with the foot of the symmedian, then the circle passing through $D$, $E$, and $F$ is tangent to side $BC$. (This circle is called the Tucker circle.)

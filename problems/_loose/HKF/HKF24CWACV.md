Let $\mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{n}}$ be the nth prime. Let $0<\mathrm{a}<1$ be a real. Define the sequence $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}$ by $\mathrm{x}_{0}=\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}=$ the fractional part of $\mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{n}} / \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}-1}$ if $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}} \neq 0$, or 0 if $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}-1}=0$. Find all a for which the sequence is eventually zero.

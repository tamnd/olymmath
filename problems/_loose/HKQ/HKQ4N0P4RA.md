Three grasshoppers sit on a straight line such that the two outer ones are 1 m away from the middle one. Every second, one of the grasshoppers jumps over another to a symmetrical point (if $A$ jumps over $B$ to point $A_{1}$, then $A B=B A_{1}$).

After some time, the grasshoppers ended up in the same positions as they started, but in a different order. Prove that the outer grasshoppers have swapped places.

19.1 Suppose Jack wins 5 games and Jill gains 15 points. How many games were played altogether?
19.2 Suppose Jack does not win any games and Jill gains 6 points. How many points did they gain between them?
19.3 Suppose Jack wins exactly $a$ games, and Jill gains $b$ points. Find a formula, in terms of $a$ and $b$, for the total number of games they played. Are there any restrictions that you need to put on the values of $a$ and $b$ ?

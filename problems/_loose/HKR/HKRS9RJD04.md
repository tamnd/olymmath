Let $a$ be a positive number. If $a$ is not an integer, denote the largest integer less than $a$ by $[a]$ (read as: the integer part of $a$), if $a$ is an integer, then $[a]=a$. Show that if $a$ is a positive number and $n$ is an integer, then:

$$
\left[\frac{[a]}{n}\right]=\left[\frac{a}{n}\right]
$$

$\because$ For the constant function $f(x)$ defined on the interval $(0,1)$, it satisfies: when $x \notin \mathbf{Q}$, $f(x)=0$; when $x=\frac{p}{q}$ (where $p, q$ are integers), $(p, q)=1, 0<p<q$, $f(x)-\frac{p+1}{q}$. Then the maximum value of $f(x)$ in the interval $\left(\frac{7}{8}, \frac{8}{9}\right)$ is
(A) $\frac{8}{7}$
(B) $\frac{8}{9}$
(C) $\frac{15}{17}$
(D) $\frac{16}{17}$

**two variable ploynomial** $P(x,y)$ is a two variable polynomial with real coefficients. degree of a monomial means sum of the powers of $x$ and $y$ in it. we denote by $Q(x,y)$ sum of monomials with the most degree in $P(x,y)$ .
(for example if $P(x,y)=3x^4y-2x^2y^3+5xy^2+x-5$ then $Q(x,y)=3x^4y-2x^2y^3$ .)
suppose that there are real numbers $x_1$ , $y_1$ , $x_2$ and $y_2$ such that $Q(x_1,y_1)>0$ , $Q(x_2,y_2)<0$ prove that the set $\{(x,y)|P(x,y)=0\}$ is not bounded.
(we call a set $S$ of plane bounded if there exist positive number $M$ such that the distance of elements of $S$ from the origin is less than $M$ .)

Given that $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ are two perpendicular lines passing through the point $P(-\sqrt{2}, 0)$, and $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ each intersect the hyperbola $y^{2}-x^{2}=1$ at two points, respectively $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $A_{2}, B_{2}$.
(1) Find the range of the slope $k_{1}$ of $l_{1}$;
(2) If point $A_{1}$ is exactly one of the vertices of the hyperbola, find the value of $\left|A_{2} B_{2}\right|$;
(3) If $\left|A_{1} B_{1}\right|=\sqrt{5}\left|A_{2} B_{2}\right|$, find the equations of $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$.

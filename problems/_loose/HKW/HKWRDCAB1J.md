The warehouse received cement in bags weighing $25 \mathrm{~kg}$ and $40 \mathrm{~kg}$. There were twice as many smaller bags as larger ones. The warehouse keeper reported the total number of bags to the manager but did not specify how many of each. The manager assumed all bags weighed $25 \mathrm{~kg}$. Therefore, he multiplied the reported number of bags by 25 and documented the result as the weight of the cement delivery. Overnight, thieves stole 60 larger bags, leaving exactly the same number of kg of cement as the manager had recorded. How many kg of cement remained?

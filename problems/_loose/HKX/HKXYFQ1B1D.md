10,11 Inside a circle of radius 1, there is a closed broken line (self-intersecting) containing 51 segments, and it is known that the length of each segment is $\sqrt{3}$. For each angle of this broken line, consider a triangle whose two sides are the segments of the broken line forming this angle (there are 51 such triangles in total). Prove that the sum of the areas of these triangles is not less than three times the area of an equilateral triangle inscribed in the circle.

Let $\mathrm{S}$ be $A=\left\{z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}\right\}$ a set composed of $n(n \geqslant 2)$ distinct complex numbers.
It is known that for any $i \in\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, $\left\{z_{i} z_{1}, z_{i} z_{2}, \cdots, z_{i} z_{n}\right\}=A$
1) Prove that $\left|z_{i}\right|=1, i=1,2, \cdots, n$; 2) Prove that if $z \in A$, then $\bar{z} \in A$.

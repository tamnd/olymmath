Circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, with radii $r$ and $R$ respectively $(r<R)$, touch each other internally at point $A$. Let $p$ be a line parallel to their common tangent, let $B$ be one intersection point of line $p$ with circle $k_{1}$, and let $C$ be one intersection point of line $p$ with circle $k_{2}$, such that points $B$ and $C$ are on the same side of the line connecting the centers of the given circles.

Prove that the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ does not depend on the choice of line $p$ and express this radius in terms of $r$ and $R$.

If $p$ and $q$ are both prime numbers, the number of divisors $d(a)$ of the natural number $a=p^{\alpha} q^{\beta}$ is given by the formula
$$
d(a)=(\alpha+1)(\beta+1)
$$

For example, $12=2^{2} \times 3^{1}$, so the number of divisors of 12 is
$$
d(12)=(2+1)(1+1)=6,
$$

with the divisors being $1, 2, 3, 4, 6$, and 12.
According to the given formula, please answer: Among the divisors of $20^{30}$ that are less than $20^{15}$, how many are not divisors of $20^{15}$? $ $

Prove that for every natural number $m \geq 1$ there exists a finite set $S_{m}$ of points in the plane satisfying the following condition: If $A$ is any point in $S_{m}$, then there are exactly $m$ points in $S_{m}$ whose distance to $A$ equals 1 .

In each cell of a strip $1 \times N$ there is either a plus or a minus. Vanya can perform the following operation: choose any three cells (not necessarily consecutive), one of which is exactly in the middle between the other two cells, and change the three signs in these cells to their opposites. A number $N$ is called positive if from an arrangement of $N$ minuses Vanya can, by such operations, obtain an arrangement of $N$ pluses.

Consider the numbers $3,4,5, \ldots, 1400$. How many of them are positive?

Prove that if $h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4$ are the altitudes of a tetrahedron and $d_1,d_2,d_3$ the distances between the pairs of opposite edges of the tetrahedron, then $$ \frac{1}{h_1^2}
+\frac{1}{h_2^2}
+\frac{1}{h_3^2}
+\frac{1}{h_4^2}
=\frac{1}{d_1^2}
+\frac{1}{d_2^2}
+\frac{1}{d_3^2}. $$

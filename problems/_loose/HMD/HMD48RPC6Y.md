Given trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $\overline{AB}=a$ and $\overline{CD}=b$. Find the length of the segment that satisfies the following conditions:

- parallel to $AB$ and $CD$
- divides the trapezoid into two parts with equal areas,
- its endpoints lie on the non-parallel sides of the trapezoid.

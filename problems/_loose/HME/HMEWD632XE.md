Several balls are distributed among $2 n+1$ bags. If any one bag is removed, the remaining $2 n$ bags can always be divided into two groups, each containing $n$ bags, and the number of balls in these two groups is equal. Prove that the number of balls in each bag is the same.
(Italian Mathematical Competition, 1990)

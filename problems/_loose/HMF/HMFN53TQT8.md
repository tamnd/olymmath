On a long street, there are four houses, numbered from $1$ to $4$, where the distances between the houses are all distinct. The houses have their front door directly on the street. There are eight people living in the first house, two people each in the second and the third house, and three people in the fourth house. A new bus stop is constructed in the street, in such a way that the total distance for the $15$ inhabitants of the street to the bus stop is as short as possible.
Which house will be closest to the bus stop?
A) House $1$
B) House $2$
C) House $3$
D) House $4$
E) That depends on the distances between the houses.

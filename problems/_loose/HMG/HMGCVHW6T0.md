On a segment of length 1, there are pairwise non-intersecting segments, the sum of whose lengths is $p$. Denote this system of segments by $A$. Let $B$ be the complementary system of segments (segments of systems $A$ and $B$ do not have common interior points and completely cover the given segment). Prove that there exists a parallel translation $T$, for which the intersection of $B$ and $T(A)$ consists of segments, the sum of whose lengths is not less than $p(1-p) / 2$.

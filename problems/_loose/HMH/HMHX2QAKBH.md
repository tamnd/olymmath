Given the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$ and a point $A$ in its plane. We draw an arbitrary chord $O B$ from the vertex $O$ of the parabola; let the projection of point $B$ on the tangent at the vertex be $C$. What is the geometric locus of the intersection point $M$ of the lines $O B$ and $A C$? Determine the nature of this geometric locus depending on whether the point $A$ lies on the parabola, inside the parabola, or outside the parabola.

Solve the equation

$$
x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c=0
$$

if we know that

$$
a^{2} b=2 b^{2}=4 a c
$$

Prove that under these conditions, there is always one and only one real root, and if $a, b, c$ are integers, this root is also an integer.

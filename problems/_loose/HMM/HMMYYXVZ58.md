If all its terms are increased by 1 or all its terms are increased by 2, then in both cases the sum of the absolute values of the terms of the resulting progression will also be equal to 100. What values can the quantity $n^{2} d$ take under these conditions, where $d$ is the common difference of the progression, and $n$ is the number of its terms?

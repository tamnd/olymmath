For a homogeneous material curve consisting of one half of a cycloid arch $x=a(t-\sin t), y=a(1-\cos t)$, $t \in[0 ; \pi]$, find (assuming $\rho=1$ ):

1) the mass of the curve;
2) the static moments with respect to the coordinate axes;
3) the coordinates of the center of mass;
4) the moments of inertia with respect to the coordinate axes $O x$ and $O y$.

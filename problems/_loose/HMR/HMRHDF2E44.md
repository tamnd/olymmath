A square wall has side length $n$ metres. Guillaume paints $n$ non-overlapping circular targets on the wall, each with radius 1 metre. Mathilde is going to throw a dart at the wall. Her aim is good enough to hit the wall at a single point, but poor enough that the dart will hit a random point on the wall. What is the largest possible value of $n$ so that the probability that Mathilde's dart hits a target is at least $\frac{1}{2}$ ?

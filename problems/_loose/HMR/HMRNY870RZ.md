Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients, and there exists an integer $k>1$, such that $f(1), f(2), \cdots, f(k)$ are all not multiples of $k$. Prove that for any integer $a, f(a)$ is not a multiple of $k$, and therefore $f(x)$ has no integer roots. Furthermore, from the above conclusion, deduce that if $f(1), f(2)$ are both odd, then $f(x)$ has no integer roots.

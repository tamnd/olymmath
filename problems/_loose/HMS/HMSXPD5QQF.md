For Vasya's birthday, a cake with a square base of $4 \times 4$ dm was bought, divided into 16 equal pieces with square bases of $1 \times 1$ dm. Vasya decided to cut the cake with a knife along a straight line so that the maximum number of separate pieces, not necessarily equal, would be formed. What is the maximum number of pieces of cake that can be obtained with one straight cut? What is the minimum number of straight cuts needed to ensure that each of the 16 original pieces is cut?

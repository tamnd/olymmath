A line intersects the circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, which have no common points, at equal-length chords $A B$ and $C D$, respectively. The intersection points are in order $A, B, C$, and $D$. For a point $P$, it holds that $P A$ is tangent to $k_{1}$ and $P D$ is tangent to $k_{2}$. Determine the ratio $\frac{P A}{P D}$ using the radii of the two circles.

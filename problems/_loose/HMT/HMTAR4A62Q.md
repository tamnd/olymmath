In the plane, points $A, T, U$ are given such that angle $ATU$ is obtuse. Construct triangle $ABC$, where $T, U$ are, respectively, the points of tangency of side $BC$ with the incircle and the excircle of the triangle. (By the excircle, we mean the circle that, in addition to side $BC$, also touches the rays opposite to rays $BA$ and $CA$.)

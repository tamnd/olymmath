a) Let $n \geq 0$ be an integer. Calculate $\int_{0}^{1}(1-t)^{n} e^{t} d t$.

b) Let $k \geq 0$ be a fixed integer and let $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq k}$ be the sequence defined by

$$
x_{n}=\sum_{i=k}^{n}\binom{i}{k}\left(e-1-\frac{1}{1!}-\frac{1}{2!}-\ldots-\frac{1}{i!}\right)
$$

Prove that the sequence converges and find its limit.

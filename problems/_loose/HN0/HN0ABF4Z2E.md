Hour part of a defective digital watch displays only the numbers from $1$ to $12$. After one minute from $ n: 59$, although it must display $ \left(n + 1\right): 00$, it displays $ 2n: 00$ (Think in $ mod 12$). For example, after $ 7: 59$, it displays $ 2: 00$ instead of $ 8: 00$. If we set the watch to an arbitrary time, what is the probability that hour part displays $4$ after exactly one day?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \frac {1}{12} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac {1}{4} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac {1}{3} \textbf{(D)}\ \frac {1}{2} \textbf{(E)}\ \text{None}$

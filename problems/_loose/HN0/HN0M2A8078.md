Let $R(n)$ be the number of representations of a natural number $n$ as a sum $n=a+b$ of two prime numbers (for example, $R(3)=0, R(4)=1, R(10)=3$, since $10=3+7=5+5=7+3$). Prove that if $p<q$ are two consecutive prime numbers, then the sum

$$
2 R(q-2)+3 R(q-3)+5 R(q-5)+\ldots+p \cdot R(q-p)
$$

is divisible by $q$.

Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, A_{4}, A_{5}, A_{6}$ be 6 points on a plane such that no three points are collinear.
(1) If any 13 line segments are drawn between these points, prove that there must exist a complete graph $K_{4}$ of order 4.
(2) If any 12 line segments are drawn between these points, draw a diagram to show that the conclusion in (1) does not hold.
(3) Can the conclusion in (1) be strengthened to state that there must exist 4 complete graphs of order 4? Provide a counterexample or a proof.

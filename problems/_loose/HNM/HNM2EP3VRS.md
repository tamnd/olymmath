Four dwarfs are sitting around a round table. Each has a mug of milk in front of them. One of the dwarfs pours $1 / 4$ of his milk into the mug of the dwarf to his right. Then the dwarf to his right does the same. Next, the following dwarf to the right does the same, and finally, the fourth dwarf pours $1 / 4$ of the milk that is left in his mug into the first dwarf's mug. Together, there are 2 liters of milk in all the mugs. How much milk was there initially in the mugs if

a) in the end, all dwarfs have the same amount of milk

b) in the end, all dwarfs have the same amount of milk as they had at the beginning?

Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,\ldots,a_{250}$ be real numbers such that $a_1=2$ and $$ a_{n+1}=a_n+\frac{1}{a_n^2} $$ for every $n=1,2, \ldots, 249$ . Let $x$ be the greatest integer which is less than $$ \frac{1}{a_1}+\frac{1}{a_2}+\ldots+\frac{1}{a_{250}} $$ How many digits does $x$ have?

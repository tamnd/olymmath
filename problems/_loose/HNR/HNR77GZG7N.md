For any real $ a$ define $ f_a : \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$ by the law $ f_a(t) = \left( \sin(t), \cos(at) \right)$.
a) Prove that $ f_{\pi}$ is not periodic.
b) Determine the values of the parameter $ a$ for which $ f_a$ is periodic.
**Remark**. L. Euler proved in $ 1737$ that $ \pi$ is irrational.

Let positive integers $M$, $m$, $n$ be such that $1\leq m \leq n$, $1\leq M \leq \dfrac {m(m+1)} {2}$, and let $A \subseteq \{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ with $|A|=m$. Prove there exists a subset $B\subseteq A$ with
$$0 \leq \sum_{b\in B} b - M \leq n-m.$$

(*Dan Schwarz*)

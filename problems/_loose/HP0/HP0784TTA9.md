On the set $M$, a multiplicative operation is defined with the property

$$
x \cdot(y \cdot x)=y, \forall x, y \in M
$$

Prove that each of the equations

$$
a \cdot x=b
$$

and

$$
x \cdot a=b
$$

where $a, b \in M$, has a unique solution.

R.M.T. 1-2014

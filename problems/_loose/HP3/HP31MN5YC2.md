Show (naturally avoiding the use of the geometric series sum formula), that if

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
1+q+q^{2}+\ldots+q^{2 n-1}+q^{2 n}=S \\
1-q+q^{2}-\ldots-q^{2 n-1}+q^{2 n}=S_{1}
\end{array}\right.
$$

then

$$
1+q^{2}+q^{4}+\ldots+q^{4 n-2}+q^{4 n}=S S_{1}
$$

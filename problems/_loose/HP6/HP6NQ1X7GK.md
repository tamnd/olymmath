In the tetrahedron $A-BCD$, $\triangle BCD$ and $\triangle ACD$ are both equilateral triangles with side length 2, and $\triangle BCD$ lies in plane $\alpha$. The side length $AB=\sqrt{3}$. Now, four of the eight labels numbered 1 to 8 are randomly attached to the four vertices, and the corresponding labels are denoted as $f(\eta)$ (where $\eta$ takes values $A, B, C, D$), and $E$ is a point on the side $AB$.
(1) Find the probability $P_{1}$ of the event "$f(C)+f(D)$ is even";
(2) If $|BE|:|EA|=f(B):f(A)$, find the probability $P_{2}$ that the dihedral angle $E-CD-A$ is greater than $\frac{\pi}{4}$.

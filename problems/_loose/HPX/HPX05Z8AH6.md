The radii of the circumscribed circle and the inscribed circle of a regular $n$ -gon, $n\ge 3$ are denoted by $R_n$ and $r_n$ , respectively. Prove that
$$\frac{r_n}{R_n}\ge\left(\frac{r_{n+1}}{R_{n+1}}\right)^2.$$

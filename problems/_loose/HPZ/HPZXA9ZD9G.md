The plane angles at vertex $D$ of the pyramid $A B C D$ are equal to $90^{\circ}$. Let $S 1, S 2, S 3$ and $Q$ be the areas of the faces $A B D, B C D, C A D$ and $A B C$ respectively, and let $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ be the dihedral angles at the edges $A B, B C$ and $A C$ respectively. 1. Express $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ in terms of $S 1, S 2, S 3$ and $Q$. 2. Prove that $S 21+S 22+S 23=Q 2$. 3. Prove that $\cos 2 \alpha$ $+\cos 2 \beta+\cos 2 \gamma=1$.

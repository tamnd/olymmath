Given functions $f,g:N^*\rightarrow N^*$, $g$ is surjective and $2f(n)^2=n^2+g(n)^2$, $\forall n>0$. Prove that if $|f(n)-n|\le2005\sqrt n$, $\forall n>0$, then $f(n)=n$ for infinitely many $n$.

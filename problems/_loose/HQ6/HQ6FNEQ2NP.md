Let $P$ be an interior point of triangle $ABC$. It is known that if the line $AP$ is reflected over the angle bisector from $A$, the line $BP$ is reflected over the angle bisector from $B$, and the line $CP$ is reflected over the angle bisector from $C$, then the reflected lines intersect at a single point. Let this point be denoted by $Q$. Prove that the perpendicular projections of $P$ and $Q$ onto the sides of the triangle form a cyclic hexagon.

From point $O$, 12 rays $l_{1}, l_{2}, \ldots, l_{12}$ are drawn such that each pair of adjacent rays forms an angle of $30^{\circ}$. An ant sitting on ray $l_{1}$, at some moment, started crawling along the perpendicular to ray $l_{2}$. Upon reaching $l_{2}$, it turned and crawled along the perpendicular to ray $l_{3}$, and so on. The first complete revolution around point $O$ took 1 minute. How much time will it take for the second revolution if it continues to crawl at a constant speed? What distance will it crawl to point $O$ if the first loop is $a$ m?

The ninety-fourth problem: Let $\alpha$ be the positive root of the equation $x^{2}+x=5$, and let positive integer $n$ and non-negative integers $c_{0}, c_{1}, \ldots, c_{n}$ satisfy: $\sum_{\mathrm{k}=0}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{k}} \alpha^{\mathrm{k}}=2015$, (1) Prove: $\sum_{\mathrm{k}=0}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{k}} \equiv 2(\bmod 3)$; (2) Find the minimum value of $\sum_{\mathrm{k}=0}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{k}}$.

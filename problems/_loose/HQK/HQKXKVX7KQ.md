In a chess tournament, each of the participants played exactly once against each of the others, and there were no draws. Show that there exists a player $P$ such that, for any other player $Q$, distinct from $P$, one of the following situations occurs:
i) $Q$ lost to $P$;

ii) $Q$ lost to someone who lost to $P$.

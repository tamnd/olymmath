Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{1987}$ be nonnegative real numbers, for which the sum of the squares is equal to 10 and the sum of the third powers is greater than 1.

Investigate whether it is always possible under these conditions to select

a) 9 of these numbers

b) 10 of these numbers

such that the sum of the selected numbers is greater than 1!

(If a number appears multiple times among the $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{1987}$, it may also be included in the selected numbers at most as many times as it appears.)

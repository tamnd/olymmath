A square frame is placed on a horizontal plane, touching one of its sides to a line e, on the same side of e. The lower left corner of each frame is hinged to the lower right corner of its left neighbor. Each side of a frame is as long as the diagonal of its right neighbor. The smallest frame is fixed, considered as the first one, and the rest, as a whole, are rotated counterclockwise by $45^{\circ}$. Then we fix the 2nd frame, and step by step, we proceed similarly with the rest, until a $45^{\circ}$ angle is formed at each hinge. In this final position, which numbered frames will have a common point with the line e?

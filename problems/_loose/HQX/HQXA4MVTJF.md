Given 3 collinear points. Prove that for every positive integer $n$, it is possible to draw $n$ circles of different radii around each point such that the number of points in the plane that lie on 3 circle circumferences is at least $c n^{2}$, where $c$ is a positive constant independent of the position of the points.

Given $a b c=1, a+b+c=2, a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=3$, then the value of $\frac{1}{a b+c-1}$ $+\frac{1}{b c+a-1}+\frac{1}{c a+b-1}$ is
(A) 1.
(B) $\frac{1}{2}$
(C) 2.
(D) $-\frac{2}{3}$.
(China Shanxi Province Taiyuan City Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1997)

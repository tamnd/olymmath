Prove: If
$$
\tan ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2} \leqslant \tan ^{2} \frac{\beta}{2} (\beta \neq k \pi),
$$

then
$$
\frac{\sin ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\sin ^{2} \frac{\beta}{2}} \leqslant \frac{x^{2}-2 x \cos \alpha+1}{x^{2}-2 x \cos \beta+1} \leqslant \frac{\cos ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos ^{2} \frac{\beta}{2}} .
$$

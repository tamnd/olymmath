Given $n$ distinct points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}, n \geqslant 4$, on a straight line, and each of these points is painted one of 4 colors, with all 4 colors being used. Prove that there must be a segment on the line that contains points of all 4 colors, where points of two of the colors each appear exactly once, and points of the other two colors each appear at least once.

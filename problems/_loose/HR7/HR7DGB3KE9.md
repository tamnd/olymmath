Given a right triangular prism $A B C A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. A sphere with diameter $A_{1} C_{1}$ intersects the edges $B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{1} B_{1}$ at points $N_{1}$ and $K_{1}$, respectively, which are different from the vertices of the prism. Segments $A N_{1}$ and $C K_{1}$ intersect at point $F$, and at the same time, $A N_{1}=7, C F=4$.

a) Find the angle $F N_{1} B_{1}$.

b) Find the ratio $C_{1} N_{1}: N_{1} B_{1}$.

c) Suppose it is additionally known that $B C=8$. Find the volume of the prism.

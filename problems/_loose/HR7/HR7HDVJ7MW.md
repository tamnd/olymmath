A school's math interest group consists of $m$ students, and the school has arranged $n$ teachers as advisors. During one of the group's activities, each pair of students asks each other a question, each student asks each advisor a question, and each advisor also poses a question to the entire group, with all these questions being distinct, resulting in a total of 51 questions. Try to find the values of $m$ and $n$.

Twelve houses, blue or white, are arranged in a circle. In these twelve houses live twelve painters. During each of the twelve months of the year, a different painter goes out and starts repainting the houses in a clockwise direction, starting with their own. He changes the color of each house he encounters until he has changed a white house to blue. He stops at that point. We assume that one of the houses was blue at the start. Show that when the year is over, the houses have all returned to their original color.

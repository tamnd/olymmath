A stick of length $\mathrm{L}$ is divided into $m$ equal parts with red marks and into $n$ equal parts with black marks $(m>n)$.
(1) Let $x$ be the number of red and black marks that coincide. Please explain: $x+1$ is a common divisor of $m$ and $n$;
(2) If the stick is sawed into small segments along the marks, a total of 170 segments of unequal lengths can be obtained, with the longest segment being exactly 100 of them. Determine the values of $m$ and $n$.

Three circles pass through two given points on the plane each. Let $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ be their centers. A line passing through one of the points, common to all three circles, intersects them again at points $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ respectively. Prove that $\left|A_{1} A_{2}\right|:\left|A_{2} A_{3}\right|=\left|O_{1} O_{2}\right|:\left|O_{2} O_{3}\right|$.

On the surface of a cube, 100 different points are marked with chalk. Prove that it is possible to place the cube on a black table in two different ways (precisely in the same place) such that the chalk marks on the table from these placements are identical. (If a point is marked on an edge or at a vertex, it also leaves a mark).

If $x=\sqrt{7}+\sqrt{6}$, then $\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right):\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)$ equals
(A) $\sqrt{7}: \sqrt{6}$.
(B) $7: 6$.
(C) $x^{2}: 1$.
(D) $x$.
(1st "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

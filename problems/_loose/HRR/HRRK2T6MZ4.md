(2003 Tianjin Competition Question) There are 20 cards each written with a number $1,2, \cdots, 19,20$. They are placed in a box, and 4 people each draw one card. The two people who draw the two smaller numbers are in one group, and the two people who draw the two larger numbers are in another group. If two of them draw 5 and 14, respectively, then the probability that these two are in the same group is ( ).
A. $\frac{1}{2}$
B. $\frac{2}{51}$
C. $\frac{5}{51}$
D. $\frac{7}{51}$

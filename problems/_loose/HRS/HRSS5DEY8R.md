Let $n$ be a natural number. To prove that the value of the expression
$$\prod^n_{i=0}\frac{x^{n-1}_i}{\prod_{j \ne i}(x_i - x_j)}$$
does not depend on the choice of the different real numbers $x_0, x_1, ... , x_n$.

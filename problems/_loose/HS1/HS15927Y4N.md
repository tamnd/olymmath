If $ \theta$ is a constant such that $ 0 < \theta < \pi$ and $ x + \frac{1}{x} = 2\cos{\theta}$ . then for each positive integer $ n$ , $ x^n + \frac{1}{x^n}$ equals

$ \textbf{(A)}\ 2\cos{\theta}
\textbf{(B)}\ 2^n\cos{\theta}
\textbf{(C)}\ 2\cos^n{\theta}
\textbf{(D)}\ 2\cos{n\theta}
\textbf{(E)}\ 2^n\cos^n{\theta}$

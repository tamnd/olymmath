Let $ABC$ be a scalene triangle and $l_0$ be a line that is tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$ at point $A$. Let $l$ is a variable line which is parallel to line $l_0$. Let $l$ intersect segment $AB$ and $AC$ at the point $X$, $Y$ respectively. $BY$ and $CX$ intersects at point $T$ and the line $AT$ intersects the circumcirle of $ABC$ at $Z$. Prove that as $l$ varies, circumcircle of $XYZ$ passes through a fixed point.

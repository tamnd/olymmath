Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $P$ a point inside it. Let $D, E, F$ be the orthogonal projections of $P$ onto $[BC], [CA]$, and $[AB]$ respectively. We assume that

$$
AP^{2}+PD^{2}=BP^{2}+PE^{2}=CP^{2}+PF^{2}
$$

Furthermore, let $I_{A}, I_{B}, I_{C}$ be the centers of the excircles of triangle $ABC$. Show that $P$ is the center of the circumcircle of $I_{A} I_{B} I_{C}$.

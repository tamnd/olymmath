(1964 Hungarian Olympiad) At the graduation dance, every young man danced with at least one young woman, but no young man danced with all the young women, and every young woman danced with at least one young man, but no young woman danced with all the young men. Prove: Among all the people attending the dance, it is possible to find two young men and two young women such that each of the two young men danced only with one of the two young women, and each of the two young women danced only with one of the two young men.

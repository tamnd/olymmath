The country of ELMOpia has $n \ge 4$ cities, where some pairs of cities are connected by a road. An astute traveler notices that for any $2$ cities $A$ and $B$ , there exist (distinct) cities $C$ and $D$ such that $A-C$ , $C-B$ , $B-D$ , $D-A$ are all connected by roads. Find the minimum number of roads in ELMOpia, in terms of $n$ .

Let $\xi$ be a non-negative random variable and $\mathscr{G}$ a $\sigma$-subalgebra of the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{F}$. Show that $\mathrm{E}[\xi \mid \mathscr{G}]<\infty$ a.s. if and only if the measure $\mathrm{Q}$, defined on sets $A \in \mathscr{G}$ by $\mathrm{Q}(A)=\int_{A} \xi d \mathrm{P}$, is $\sigma$-finite.

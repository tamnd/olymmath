There is an infinite grid plane, none of the points of which are painted blue. For one ruble, you can choose a cell and paint all its sides blue, even if some side was already painted. What is the minimum amount of money you need to pay to get a grid square $1001 \times 1001$, all grid lines inside and on the boundary of which will be painted blue? It is not required that only the grid lines of the square are painted in the end.

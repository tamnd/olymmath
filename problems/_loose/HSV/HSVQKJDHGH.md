Try to construct some geometric extremum problems from
$$
\begin{array}{l}
h_{a} \leqslant r\left(1+\csc \frac{A}{2}\right), \\
r \leqslant s \tan \frac{A}{2}\left(\sec \frac{A}{2}-\tan \frac{A}{2}\right)^{2}, \\
s \leqslant 2 R \cos \frac{A}{2}\left(1+\sin \frac{A}{2}\right), \\
r \leqslant 2 R \sin \frac{A}{2}\left(1-\sin \frac{A}{2}\right),
\end{array}
$$

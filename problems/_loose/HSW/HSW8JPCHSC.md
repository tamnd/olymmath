Four spheres, $g_{1}, g_{2}, g_{3}, g_{4}$, touch each other pairwise. From the points of contact between $g_{4}$ and any two of the other spheres, project the point of contact between these two spheres onto $g_{4}$. Prove that repeating this for all three pairs of spheres touching $g_{4}$, the resulting six points on $g_{4}$ are three pairs of opposite points on a great circle of $g_{4}$.

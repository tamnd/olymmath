Given the quadratic function $y=a x^{2}+b x+c$ whose graph intersects the $x$-axis at points $A$ and $B$, and whose vertex is $C$.
(1) If $\triangle A B C$ is a right triangle, find the value of $b^{2}-4 a c$.
(2) Consider the quadratic function
$$
y=x^{2}-(2 m+2) x+m^{2}+5 m+3
$$

whose graph intersects the $x$-axis at points $E$ and $F$, and intersects the graph of the linear function $y=3 x-1$ at two points, with the point having the smaller $y$-coordinate denoted as point $G$.
(i) Express the coordinates of point $G$ in terms of $m$;
(ii) If $\triangle E F G$ is a right triangle, find the value of $m$.

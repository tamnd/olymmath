Let $p(x)=x^{n}+a_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0}$ be a polynomial with real coefficients. Prove: Among any $n+$ 1 distinct integers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}, b_{n+1}$, there must exist a $b_{j}$ $(1 \leqslant j \leqslant n+1)$, such that $\left|p\left(b_{j}\right)\right| \geqslant \frac{n!}{2^{n}}$.

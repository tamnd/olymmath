Let $C$ be an interior point of the segment $AB$. Over the segments $AC$ and $CB$, as bases, we construct equilateral triangles on one side of the line $AB$, and over the segment $AB$ we construct an equilateral triangle on the other side. It is to be proven that the centroids of these triangles are the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

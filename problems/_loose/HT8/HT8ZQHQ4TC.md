Let $n \ge 2$ be a positive integer, and $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ be non-negative real numbers. Prove that
$$ \frac{1}{3} \left( \sum_{i=1}^n x_i \right)^2 \ge \sum_{1 \le i<j \le n} \frac{x_i x_j}{2^{\nu_2(j-i)}}. $$
*Note: For a positive integer $k$ , $\nu_2(k)$ denotes the largest integer $\alpha$ such that $2^\alpha \mid k$ .*

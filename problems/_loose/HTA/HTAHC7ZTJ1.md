Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be an arithmetic progression of non-zero natural numbers and the set

$$
A=\left\{p \in \mathbf{N}^{*} \mid a_{a_{p}}=a_{p}+a_{p+1}\right\}
$$

Show that if 2014 is a term of the progression and $A \neq \varnothing$, then $A=\mathrm{N}^{*}$.

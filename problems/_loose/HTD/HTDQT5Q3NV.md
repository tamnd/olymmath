In a class of moderate size, there were 2 problems assigned for the written math test, and every student solved at least one of them.
- Those who solved only one problem were one less than twice as many as those who solved both problems.
- Those who solved both problems were given 1 new problem to solve for the next class, those who only managed to solve the harder problem were given 4 new problems, and those who only managed to solve the easier problem were given 1 harder, new problem to solve at home. Thus, the first two groups together submitted as many solutions as the second group.
How many students are in the class?

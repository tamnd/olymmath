If a sequence of numbers, except for the first and last numbers, each number is equal to the sum of the two adjacent numbers, it is called having the "oscillatory property". For example, $2,3,1,-2,-3$ works, because $3=2+1,1=3-2,-2=1-3$. It is known that in the following expression, each * represents a number, and satisfies the "oscillatory property".
$$
1 * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 1
$$

Then the sum of the 18 * represented numbers is
(A) -64 .
(B) 64 .
(C) 18 .
(D) 0 .
(3rd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

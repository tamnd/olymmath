(52nd Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad (Final B Class) Question) Given that $A$ and $B$ are finite sets composed of different positive real numbers, $n$ $(n>1)$ is a given positive integer, and both $A$ and $B$ have at least $n$ elements. If the sum of any $n$ different real numbers in $A$ belongs to $B$, and the product of any $n$ different real numbers in $B$ belongs to $A$, find the maximum number of elements in $A$ and $B$.

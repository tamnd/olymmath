If the functions $f_{1}(x), f_{2}(x), \cdots, f_{n}(x)$ are defined on the interval $D$ with endpoints $a$ and $b$, and $b>a, n \in \mathbf{N}, n \geqslant 2$. Then there exist $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \in D$, such that
$$
\left|\prod_{1=1}^{n} x_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{n} f_{1}\left(x_{1}\right)\right| \geqslant\left(\frac{b-a}{2}\right)^{n} .
$$

There are 9 sticks of different lengths from 1 cm to 9 cm. What are the side lengths of the squares that can be formed from these sticks, and in how many ways can they be formed? Ways of forming a square are considered different if different sticks are used and not necessarily all of them.

Can squares with side lengths of 1 cm, 2 cm, ... 6 cm be formed? Why? Can a square with a side length of 7 cm be formed? How can it be done? In how many different ways can it be formed? Consider all possible cases. Can a square with a side length of 12 cm or more be formed? Why?

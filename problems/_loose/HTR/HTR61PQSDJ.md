Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be real numbers such that $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n = 0$. Prove that:
$$
\frac{n}{n-1} \min\{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n\} \ge \min\{a_1, a_2\} + \min\{a_2, a_3\} + \dots + \min\{a_n, a_1\}.
$$

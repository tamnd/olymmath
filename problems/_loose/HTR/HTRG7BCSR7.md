In trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $AB=a$ and $CD=b$, a segment $A_{1} B_{1}$ is drawn connecting the midpoints of the diagonals.

In the resulting trapezoid, a segment $A_{2} B_{2}$ is also drawn connecting the midpoints of the diagonals, and so on. Can any number appear twice in the sequence of segment lengths $AB, A_{1} B_{1}, A_{2} B_{2}, \ldots$? Is this sequence monotonic (increasing or decreasing)? Does it converge to any limit?

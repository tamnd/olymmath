36 Let $D_{n}(k)=D(n, n, k)$, prove that
(1) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} k D_{n}(k)=n$!
(2) $D_{n}(0)-D_{n}(1)=(-1)^{n}$
(3) $\sum_{k=0}^{n}(k-1)^{2} D_{n}(k)=n$!
(4) $\sum_{k=r}^{n} k(k-1) \cdots(k-r+1) D_{n}(k)=n$!, where $r \leqslant n$.

Ten trees are lined up in a row, each adjacent pair separated by 10 meters. At the first tree, there is a well, and Xiao Zhang draws water from the well, carries it in a bucket, and waters the trees, with each tree requiring one bucket of water. Then, after watering all ten trees, the minimum distance he has to walk in meters is
(A) 410.
(B) 490.
(C) 500.
(D) 510.
(3rd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

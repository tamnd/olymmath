Inside a sphere of radius $R$, a point $A$ is taken at a distance $a$ from its center. Through point $A$, three pairwise perpendicular chords are drawn.

a) Find the sum of the squares of the lengths of these chords.

b) Find the sum of the squares of the lengths of the segments into which point $A$ divides these chords.

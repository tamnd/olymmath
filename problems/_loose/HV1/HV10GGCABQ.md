Given a direction $\vec{l}$ in plane $\alpha$, and $F$ is a convex set in plane $\alpha$ with area $S(F)$. Among all triangles inscribed in $F$ and having one side parallel to $\vec{l}$, the one with the largest area is denoted as $\triangle$, and its area is denoted as $S(\Delta)$. Find the maximum positive real number $c$ such that for any convex figure $F$ in plane $\alpha$, we have $S(\Delta) \geqslant c \cdot S(F)$.

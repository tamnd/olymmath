Assume that $P$ is a point inside triangle $ABC$ such that

$$
\frac{|AP|+|BP|}{|AB|}=\frac{|BP|+|CP|}{|BC|}=\frac{|CP|+|AP|}{|CA|}
$$

Let the lines $AP$, $BP$, $CP$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at points $A'$, $B'$, $C'$, respectively. Prove that triangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ have a common incircle.

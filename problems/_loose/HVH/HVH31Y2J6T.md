Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with unequal sides, and let its incircle be $\odot I$. Let line $d$ be tangent to $\odot I$ at point $K$. Draw perpendiculars from $I$ to $I A$, $I B$, and $I C$, intersecting line $d$ at points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$, respectively. Let line $d$ intersect $A B$, $B C$, and $C A$ at points $M$, $N$, and $P$, respectively. Draw lines through $P$, $M$, and $N$ parallel to $I A$, $I B$, and $I C$, respectively, to form $\triangle X Y Z$. Prove that $A A_{1}$, $B B_{1}$, and $C C_{1}$ are concurrent, and that $I K$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle X Y Z$.

Let's call the distance between numbers the absolute value of their difference. It is known that the sum of the distances from twelve consecutive natural numbers to some number $a$ is 358, and the sum of the distances from these same twelve numbers to some number $b$ is 212. Find all possible values of $a$, given that $a+b=114.5$.

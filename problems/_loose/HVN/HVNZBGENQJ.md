If $a, b, c$ and $d$ are positive real numbers such that $a + b + c + d = 2$, prove that
$$\frac{a^2}{(a^2+1)^2}+\frac{b^2}{(b^2+1)^2}+\frac{c^2}{(c^2+1)^2}+\frac{d^2}{(d^2+1)^2} \le \frac{16}{25}$$

(14th "Hope Cup" Mathematics Invitational Test) Let the function $f(x)=\sqrt[3]{1+x}-\lambda x$, where $\lambda>0$.
(1) Find the range of $\lambda$ such that $f(x)$ is a monotonic function on the interval $[0,+\infty)$;
(2) Can this monotonicity be extended to the entire domain $(-\infty,+\infty)$?
(3) Solve the inequality $2 x-\sqrt[3]{1+x}<12$.

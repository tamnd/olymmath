1) $x^{2}-\frac{x^{3}-4 x y^{2}}{x(x-2 y)+y^{2}} \cdot \frac{x^{2}-2 x y+y^{2}}{x-2 y}, x=-\frac{1}{2}, y=-5$;

4) $\frac{5 m+5 n}{m-n}\left(\frac{m}{m+n}-\frac{m}{m^{2}-n^{2}}\right), m=3 \frac{1}{2}, n=-\frac{1}{2}$.

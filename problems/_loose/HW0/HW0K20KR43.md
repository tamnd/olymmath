A bag of popping corn contains $\frac{2}{3}$ white kernels and $\frac{1}{3}$ yellow kernels. Only $\frac{1}{2}$ of the white kernels will pop, whereas $\frac{2}{3}$ of the yellow ones will pop. A kernel is selected at random from the bag, and pops when placed in the popper. What is the probability that the kernel selected was white? $ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac{1}{2} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac{5}{9} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac{4}{7} \textbf{(D)}\ \frac{3}{5} \textbf{(E)}\ \frac{2}{3} $

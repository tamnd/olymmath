In the plane, a segment $A B$ is given. For any point $X$ in this plane, which is different from $A$ and $B$, let $X_{A}$, respectively $X_{B}$, be the image of point $A$, respectively $B$, under the axial symmetry with respect to the line $X B$, respectively $X A$. Find all such points $X$ that, together with points $X_{A}, X_{B}$, form the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

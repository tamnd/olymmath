(2004 Croatian Mathematical Competition) Let point $C$ be a point on the diameter $AB$ of a semicircle with center $O$, and different from $A$, $B$, and $O$. Draw two lines through point $C$ that form equal angles with line $AB$, intersecting the semicircle at points $D$ and $E$ (different from $A$ and $B$); draw a line through point $D$ perpendicular to line $CD$ intersecting the semicircle at $K$. If $K$ is different from $E$, prove that $KE \parallel AB$.

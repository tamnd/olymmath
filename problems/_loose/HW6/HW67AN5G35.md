Let $a\geq 2$ a fixed natural number, and let $a_{n}$ be the sequence $a_{n}=a^{a^{.^{.^{a}}}}$ (e.g $a_{1}=a$ , $a_{2}=a^a$ , etc.). Prove that $(a_{n+1}-a_{n})|(a_{n+2}-a_{n+1})$ for every natural number $n$ .

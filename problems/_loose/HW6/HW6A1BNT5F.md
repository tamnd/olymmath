Let $\omega$ be circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ and let $AD$ and $BE$ be altitudes. A line $DE$ intersects the circle $\omega$ at points $P$ and $Q$ with order $P$, $E$, $D$, $Q$ in the line. Let bisectors of angle $APQ$ and $BQP$ intersect a circle $\omega$ at point $K$ and $L$, respectively. Prove that the line $KL$ is perpendicular to the bisector of angle $ACB$.

We randomly throw a 10-forint coin onto a $40 \times 40$ cm chessboard. What is the probability that the coin

a) is completely inside one square?

b) partially covers exactly one side of a square?

c) partially covers two sides of a square, but not a corner?

d) covers a corner? (The diameter of the 10-forint coin is $19 \mathrm{~mm}$).

Let $Q_{n}$ denote the number of digits required to write down the natural numbers from 1 to $n$, and $P_{n}$ the number of zeros required. Prove that

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{P_{n}}{Q_{n}}=\frac{1}{10}
$$

(We understand all of this in the decimal system.)

Let $A B C$ be a triangle, and let the center of the circumcircle of $A B C$ be $O$, with radius $r$. Show that

$$
\frac{1}{A A^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{B B^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{C C^{\prime}}=\frac{1}{r}
$$

if the extensions of $A O, B O$ and $C O$ intersect the circles passing through $B, O \text{ and } C$, $C, O \text{ and } A$, and $A, O \text{ and } B$ at points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$, respectively.

$$ P(x) = x^{10}-20x^9+135x^8+a_7x^7+a_6x^6+a_5x^5+a_4x^4+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0 $$ with real coefficients $a_0\ldots,a_7$. Determine the largest real number that occurs as a root of some Mediterranean polynomial.

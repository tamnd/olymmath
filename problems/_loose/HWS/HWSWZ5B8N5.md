Determine how to distribute 100 books into 10 piles with all distinct quantities of books in such a way that dividing any of these piles into two others results in a new distribution of 11 piles, where at least two piles have the same number of books. Justify your answer.

Note: A pile can only be divided into two others if the piles have positive integers as quantities of elements.

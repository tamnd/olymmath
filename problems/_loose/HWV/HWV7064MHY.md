The sports club "I tropici" has organized a football tournament with 3 teams, each consisting of 15 players (including reserves) with jerseys numbered from 1 to 15. The night before the matches, it snowed, and to be able to play, it is necessary to clear the snow from the field. It is then decided to appoint a group of 3 snow shovelers by choosing one player from each team in such a way that there are no two players with the same jersey number. In how many different ways can the group of shovelers be formed?
(A) 48
(B) 455 ,
(C) 1125 ,
(D) 2730 ,
(E) 3375 .

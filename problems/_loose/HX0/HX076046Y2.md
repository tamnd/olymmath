This task is a bit more challenging than the previous ones. A rumor has spread that a treasure is buried on the island. You arrive on the island and, before starting your search, you want to find out whether anyone has actually buried a treasure or not. All the natives are well-informed about whether a treasure really exists. Is it possible to determine, by asking any native just one question, whether it is worth searching for the treasure? Remember, the native can answer you either "bal" or "da," and you need to draw a conclusion from his answer regardless of what exactly the word "bal" (or "da") means in the local dialect.

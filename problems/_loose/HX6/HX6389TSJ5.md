Alberto, Barbara, and Carlo are playing cards. In each hand, the winner gains 2 points, while the other two players each lose 1 point. Initially, everyone has 0 points. What is the probability that, after 10 hands, they are all back to zero points?
(A) 0
(B) $\frac{1}{5}$
(C) $\frac{1}{3}$
(D) $\frac{\left(\begin{array}{c}10 \\ 6\end{array}\right)}{3^{10}}$
(E) $1-\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{10}$.

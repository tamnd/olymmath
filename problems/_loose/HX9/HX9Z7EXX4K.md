In the final stage of a professional bowling tournament, the top five players compete as follows: first, the fifth place player competes with the fourth place player, the loser gets the 5th prize; the winner competes with the third place player, the loser gets the 4th prize; the winner competes with the second place player, the loser gets the 3rd prize; the winner competes with the first place player, the loser gets the 2nd prize; the winner gets the 1st prize. The number of different prize-winning sequences is
(A) 10.
(B) 16.
(C) 24.
(D) 120.
(E) None of the above answers.
(39th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1988)

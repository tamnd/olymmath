A right circular cylinder with a base radius of 1 is cut by a plane, resulting in an elliptical cross-section. The eccentricity of this ellipse is $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$. The acute angle formed between this cross-section and the base of the cylinder is
(A) $\frac{\pi}{4}$.
(B) $\frac{\pi}{3}$.
(C) $\arccos \frac{2}{3}$.
(D) $\arccos \frac{1}{3}$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

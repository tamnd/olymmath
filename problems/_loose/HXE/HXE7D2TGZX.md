$a,b$ are real numbers (neither is $0$ ).Given two conditions:
A: $a>0$ .
B: $a>b$ and $a^{-1}>b^{-1}$ .
Then, which one of the followings are true? $(\text{A})$ A is sufficient but unnecessary condition of B. $(\text{B})$ A is necessary but insufficient condition of B. $(\text{C})$ A is sufficient and necessary condition of B. $(\text{D})$ A is insufficient and unnecessary condition of B.

Given a triangle $\triangle ABC$ with acute angles, construct equilateral triangles on each side of $\triangle ABC$ outside the shape, resulting in $\triangle ABD, \triangle BCE, \triangle CAF$. The conclusions are:
(1) $AE = BF = CD$.
(2) $AE, BF, CD$ intersect at one point.
(3) The angle (acute) between any two of $AE, BF, CD$ is $60^{\circ}$.

Then
(A) None of (1)(2)(3) are true.
(B) All of (1)(2)(3) are true.
(C) (1)(3) are true, (2) is false.
(D) (1) is true, (2)(3) are false.
(Shenyang City, Liaoning Province, "Yucai Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

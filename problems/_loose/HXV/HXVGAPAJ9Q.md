Given an equilateral $\triangle ABC$ with the altitude $AD$ from $A$ to $BC$, construct a regular $\triangle ADE$ on $AD$ intersecting $AC$ at $N$. Then, construct a regular $\triangle HGE$ on the altitude $HE$ of $\triangle ADE$ intersecting $AN$ at $M$, $DC$ at $F$, and $DE$ at $J$. Prove: (1) Point $C$ lies on side $GE$; (2) Points $M$ and $N$ are the centroids of $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle HCE$, respectively; (3) $S_{\triangle FGC}: S_{\triangle HEC}: S_{\triangle ADE}: S_{\triangle ABC}: S_{ABCE} \approx 1: 9: 12: 16: 24$.

a) Prove that for every triangle, the following statement holds!

If $a$ and $b$ are two of its side lengths, then its area is not greater than $\frac{a \cdot b}{2}$.

b) Prove that for every quadrilateral $A B C D$, the following statement holds!

If $a=\overline{A B}, b=\overline{B C}, c=\overline{C D}$, and $d=\overline{D A}$ are its side lengths, then its area is not greater than

$$
\frac{(a+c) \cdot(b+d)}{4}
$$

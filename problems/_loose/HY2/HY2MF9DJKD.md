Solve the following equation, where $b$ and $n$ are natural numbers, and $b>n$.

$$
\frac{x-(b-1)}{1}+\frac{x-(b-2)}{2}+\frac{x-(b-3)}{3}+\ldots+\frac{x-(b-n)}{n}=\frac{x-1}{b-1}+\frac{x-2}{b-2}+\frac{x-3}{b-3}+\ldots+\frac{x-n}{b-n}
$$

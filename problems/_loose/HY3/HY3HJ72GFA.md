In a large bag, 2007 black balls and 2007 white balls have been placed. We randomly draw two balls from the bag: if they are not of the same color, we put the white ball back in the bag and set the black one aside; if they are of the same color, we remove both from the bag. We repeat the process until there are zero or one ball left.

Show that in fact, one ball remains in the bag and determine its color.

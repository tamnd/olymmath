Initially, several (more than one) natural numbers are written on the board. Then, every minute, a number equal to the sum of the squares of all the numbers already written on the board is added (so, if the numbers 1, 2, 2 were initially written on the board, then on the first minute the number $1^2 + 2^2 + 2^2$ would be added). Prove that the hundredth number added has at least 100 different prime divisors.

(I. Bogdanov, P. Kozhevnikov)

Given a right circular cone and a point $A$ outside it, located at a distance from the plane of its base equal to the height of the cone. Let $M$ be a point on the surface of the cone such that a light ray coming from $A$ to $M$, reflecting off the surface of the cone, will be parallel to the plane of the base. Find the geometric locus of the projections of points $M$ on the plane of the base of the cone.

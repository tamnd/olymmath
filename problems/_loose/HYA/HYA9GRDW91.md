Given points $A$ and $B$ are at equal distances from the given line $e$, and $B$ is not in the plane determined by $A$ and $e$. Reflect $A$ and $B$ over $e$, and let the reflections be $C$ and $D$, respectively. It is to be proven that the faces of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ are congruent to each other.

It is known that every quadrilateral has exactly two diagonals.

a) Determine the number of diagonals in a pentagon and a hexagon!

b) Find a formula for the number of diagonals of a polygon depending on the number of vertices $n$ of the polygon! The formula should be valid for all natural numbers $n \geq 4$. Justify this formula!

c) What value does this formula give when applied for $n=3$? Can this value also be expressed in a geometrically intuitive statement?

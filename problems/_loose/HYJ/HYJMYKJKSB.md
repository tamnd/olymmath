From $M$, a perpendicular $M N$ is dropped onto $A B$ and a perpendicular to $A C$ is erected, intersecting the circle at point $L$ (segment $C L$ intersects $A B$). Find the distance between the midpoint of $A O$ and the midpoint of $C L$, if $|A N|=a$.

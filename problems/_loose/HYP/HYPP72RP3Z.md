$$
\begin{aligned}
\binom{y+1}{x+1}-\binom{y}{x+1} & =6 \\
\binom{x}{x-2} \cdot \frac{2}{x-1}+\binom{y}{y-1} \cdot \frac{1}{y} & =3
\end{aligned}
$$

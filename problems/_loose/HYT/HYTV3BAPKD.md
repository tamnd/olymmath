Eugene received a gift of a magic box containing $106,000$ euros and two buttons, yellow and green. By pressing the yellow button, the number of euros in the box doubles, and by pressing the green button, the number of euros in the box decreases by $17$ euros. (If there was no more than $17$ euros in the box when the green button was pressed, the box becomes empty.) Eugene can press the buttons in any order. Can the box become even at a certain point:
a) ten euros?
b) a thousand euros?
c) a million euros?
d) Find all natural values of m for which it is possible for there to be exactly $m$ euros in the box at a certain moment.

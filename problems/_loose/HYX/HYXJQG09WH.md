Given is an acute-angled $\triangle A B C$. Let $D$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\measuredangle A$ and side $B C$, and $E$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\measuredangle B$ and side $A C$. Let $D_{0}$ and $E_{0}$ be the orthogonal projections of points $D$ and $E$, respectively, onto side $A B$. Denote by $A_{1}$ the point symmetric to point $A$ with respect to the line $C E_{0}$, and by $B_{1}$ the point symmetric to point $B$ with respect to the line $C D_{0}$. Prove that $\triangle A A_{1} C$ and $\triangle B B_{1} C$ have no common points except for point $C$.

On a chessboard, there are several pieces. In each step, one piece can move to an adjacent (along a horizontal or vertical direction) empty square. After several steps, each piece has visited every square on the board exactly once and has returned to its original square. Prove that there must be a moment when none of the pieces are in their original squares.

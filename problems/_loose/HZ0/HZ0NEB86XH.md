Let $O$ be the center of a unit circle $k$, and let $A$ be a point on $k$. On the perpendicular to the radius $OA$ at $A$, we measure the segment $AB = (\sqrt{7} - 2)$. The segment $OB$ intersects $k$ at $C$. The midpoint of $OC$ is $D$, and the reflection of $D$ over $OA$ is $E$.

Show that there exists a square whose two vertices are $D$ and $E$, and the other two vertices are on $k$.

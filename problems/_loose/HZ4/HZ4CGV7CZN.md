Two circles intersect on a plane, one of the intersection points being $A$. Two moving points $Q_{1}, Q_{2}$ start from point $A$ at a uniform speed and move along different circumferences in the same direction. These two points return to point $A$ simultaneously after completing one full revolution. Prove: Any secant line through point $A$ intersects the two circles at points $M, N$ and the lines connecting the corresponding points $Q_{1}, Q_{2}$ are parallel, i.e., $M Q_{1} \parallel N Q_{2}$.

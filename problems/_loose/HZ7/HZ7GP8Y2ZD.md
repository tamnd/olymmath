For $\left|x_{0}\right|>\sqrt{2}$, through the point $A\left(x_{0}, x_{0}^{2}\right)$ on the parabola, there pass two normals whose feet are $B$ and $C$, different from $A$. Prove that the line $B C$ intersects the axis of the parabola at a fixed point which does not depend on $x_{0}$.

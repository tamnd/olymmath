Let $n \geq 3$ be a fixed positive integer. Evan has a convex $n$-gon in the plane and wishes to construct the centroid of its vertices. He has no standard ruler or compass, but he does have a device with which he can dissect the segment between two given points into $m$ equal parts. For which $m$ can Evan necessarily accomplish his task?

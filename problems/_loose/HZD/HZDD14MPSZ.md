Let the volume of tetrahedron $ABCD$ be $V$, and the areas of the faces opposite vertices $A, B, C, D$ be $S_{A}, S_{B}, S_{C}, S_{D}$, respectively. Let $BC=a, DA=a', CA=b, DB=b', AB=c, DC=c'$, and the angles between the three pairs of opposite edges be $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, then we have
( I ) $S_{A}^{2}+S_{B}^{2}+S_{C}^{2}+S_{D}^{2}=\frac{1}{4}\left[\left(a a' \cdot \sin \alpha\right)^{2}+\left(b b' \cdot \sin \beta\right)^{2}+\left(c c' \cdot \sin \gamma\right)^{2}\right]$;
( II ) $S_{A}^{2}+S_{B}^{2}+S_{C}^{2}+S_{D}^{2} \geqslant 9\left(3 V^{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}$.

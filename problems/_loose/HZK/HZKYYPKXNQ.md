Let $k>2$ be an integer. A positive integer $\ell$ is called $k$-capable if we can partition the numbers $1,3,5, \ldots, 2 k-1$ into two sets $A$ and $B$ such that the sum of the elements of $A$ is exactly $\ell$ times the sum of the elements of $B$. Prove that the smallest $k$-capable number is relatively prime to $k$.

The thirteenth question: Prove that for any positive integer $n>1$, the following two conditions are equivalent:
(1) For any positive integer $m$ less than $n$, $\left(n, \frac{n-m}{(n, m)}\right)=1$;
(2) There exists a prime $p$ such that for any positive integer $m$ less than $n$, $\frac{v_{n}(m p)}{m}<\frac{\left.v_{p}(n)\right)}{n}$

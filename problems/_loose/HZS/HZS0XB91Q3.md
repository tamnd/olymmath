In triangle $ABC$, with $m(\widehat{A})=60^{\circ}$ and $AB=\frac{2}{3} AC$, we construct the median $[CM]$.

a) Show that $CM=BC$.

b) Let $P \in (AB)$, such that $2AP=AC$, and $S$ is the symmetric point of $C$ with respect to point $P$. Show that quadrilateral $CBSM$ is a rhombus.

c) Let $T \in (AC)$, such that $3TC=2AC$. The parallel line through $A$ to $BC$ intersects $BT$ at $V$. Show that quadrilateral $ABCV$ is an orthodiagonal non-isosceles trapezoid.

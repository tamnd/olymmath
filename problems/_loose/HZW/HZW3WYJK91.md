A collection of $n > 2$ numbers is called *crowded* if each of them is less than their sum divided by $n - 1$ . Let $\{a, b, c, ,...\}$ be a crowded collection of $n$ numbers whose sum equals $S$ . Prove that:

(a) each of the numbers is positive,

(b) we always have $a + b > c$ ,

(c) we always have $a + b \ge \frac{S}{n-1}$ .
(Regina Schleifer)

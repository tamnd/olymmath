Given 2013 people, each holding $1, 2, \cdots, 2013$ cards respectively, sitting around a round table in any order. A single pass refers to a person passing one of their cards to one of their two adjacent neighbors. Prove: if after $m$ passes, each person holds the same number of cards, and $m$ is the smallest, then there exist two adjacent people who have not passed any cards to each other.

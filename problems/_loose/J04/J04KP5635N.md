(Test Selection China 2008) Let $m, n$ be strictly positive integers and $\mathrm{P} \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$ a polynomial of degree $n$ such that all its coefficients are odd. Suppose that $(x-1)^{m}$ divides $P$. Show that if $m \geqslant 2^{k}$ (with $k \geqslant 2$ an integer), then $n \geqslant 2^{k+1}-1$.

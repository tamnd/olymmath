Given a right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at vertex $C$. Let $F$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $C$ to the hypotenuse $AB$. On the perpendiculars to line $AB$ passing through vertices $A$ and $B$, points $D$ and $E$ are chosen in the half-plane opposite to the half-plane $ABC$, such that $|AF|=|AD|$ and $|BF|=|BE|$. Let $R$ be the midpoint of segment $DE$. Prove that the inequality $|RF| \geqq |CF|$ holds, and determine when equality occurs.

Consider a cube with an edge of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ and two vertices $A$ and $B$ diagonally opposite on one face of the cube. A path of length $n$ is defined as a sequence of $n+1$ vertices such that two consecutive vertices are $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ apart. Then: Which of the following numbers is greater: the number of paths of length $1000 \mathrm{~cm}$ that start and end at $A$, or the number of paths of length $1000 \mathrm{~cm}$ that start at $A$ and end at $B$? Justify the answer.

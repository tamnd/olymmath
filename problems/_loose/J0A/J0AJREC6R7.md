The point $O$ is circumcenter of the acute triangle $A B C$. The points $P$ and $Q$ lie on the sides $A B$ and $A C$ respectively. Prove that $O$ lies on the line $P Q$ if and only if

$$
\sin 2 \alpha=\frac{P B}{P A} \sin 2 \beta+\frac{Q C}{Q A} \sin 2 \gamma
$$

where $\alpha=\angle B A C, \beta=\angle A B C, \gamma=\angle A C B$.

Place 4 Go stones evenly on the circumference of a circle, and define the operation rules as follows: if the adjacent stones are of the same color, place a black stone between them; if they are of different colors, place a white stone between them. Then remove the original 4 stones, and this completes one operation. Prove: regardless of the initial arrangement of black and white stones on the circumference, it will take at most 4 operations for the circumference to be entirely black stones.

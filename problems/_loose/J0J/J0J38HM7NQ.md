On an island, five people and a monkey live. One day, they all gathered coconuts and put them in a common pile. They agreed to divide the coconuts among themselves the next day. During the night, one of the five islanders took their share. She divided the coconuts into 5 equal piles and had one coconut left over. She gave that one to the monkey, hid her pile, and combined the remaining four piles back into one large pile. The other four islanders did the same, one after another, each giving one coconut to the monkey so that the piles could be evenly divided. What is the smallest possible number of coconuts in the original pile?

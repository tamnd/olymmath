Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ with an eccentricity of $\frac{1}{2}$, $A_{1}, A_{2}$ are the left and right vertices of the ellipse $C$, $F_{1}, F_{2}$ are the left and right foci of the ellipse $C$, $B$ is the upper vertex of the ellipse $C$, and the circumradius of $\triangle B A_{1} F_{1}$ is $\frac{2 \sqrt{21}}{3}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Suppose a line $l$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $P, Q$ (with $P, Q$ on opposite sides of the $x$-axis), and the slopes of the lines $A_{1} P, P A_{2}$, $A_{2} Q, Q A_{1}$ are denoted as $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}, k_{4}$, respectively. Given that $k_{1}+k_{4}=\frac{5}{3}\left(k_{2}+k_{3}\right)$, find the range of the area of $\triangle F_{2} P Q$.

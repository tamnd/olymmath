Consider $M$ cells, numbered $1, \ldots, M$. Suppose that in the cell with number $n$ there is one white ball and $n$ black ones. A "random" selection of balls is made from each of the $M$ cells. Let

$$
\xi_{n}= \begin{cases}1, & \text { if a white ball is drawn from the cell with number } n, \\ 0, & \text { if a black ball is drawn, }\end{cases}
$$

and let $S_{M}=\xi_{1}+\ldots+\xi_{M}-$ be the number of white balls drawn. Show that " $S_{M}$ has the order $\ln M$ for large $M$ " in the sense that for any $\varepsilon>0$ as $M \rightarrow \infty$ the relation

$$
\mathrm{P}\left\{\left|\frac{S_{M}}{\ln M}-1\right| \geqslant \varepsilon\right\} \rightarrow 0
$$

holds.

a) The shape of a mountain is a regular quadrilateral frustum, with the lower and upper base edges being 320 and 135 meters, respectively, and one side of the base square is oriented north-south. Two paths lead from the southwest corner of the base square to the northwest corner of the top surface: one goes straight up the western slope, and the other goes around the mountain, passing through the southern, eastern, and northern slopes, with a constant incline throughout. The angle of inclination of the I. path is 16, $7^{\circ}$ greater than that of the II. path. How high can the mountain be?

b) Estimate where the III. path, planned from the midpoint of the eastern side of the base square, ascending the eastern, northern, western, and southern slopes to the top surface with the same angle of inclination as the II. path, would end.

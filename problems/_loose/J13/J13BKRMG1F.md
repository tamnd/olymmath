(40 points) On the board, the numbers $1^{2}, 2^{2}, 3^{2}, \ldots, 1000^{2}$ are written. Two players take turns erasing one of the numbers on the board until only two numbers remain. If the difference between these two numbers is divisible by 13, the second player wins; otherwise, the first player wins. How should the second player act to win regardless of the opponent's actions?

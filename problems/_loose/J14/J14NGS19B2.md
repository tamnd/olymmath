Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. The perpendicular bisectors of the diagonals $B D$ and $A C$ intersect the side $A D$ at points $X$ and $Y$ respectively, with $X$ lying between $A$ and $Y$. It turns out that the lines $B X$ and $C Y$ are parallel. Prove that the lines $B D$ and $A C$ are perpendicular.

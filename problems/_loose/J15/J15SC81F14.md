Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D ; A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ and $D_{1}$ are the centers of the circumscribed circles of triangles $B C D, C D A, D A B$ and $A B C$. Similarly, for quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$, points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ and $D_{2}$ are defined. Prove that quadrilaterals $A B C D$ and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2} D_{2}$ are similar, and the coefficient of their similarity is $\mid(\operatorname{ctg} A + \operatorname{ctg} C)(\operatorname{ctg} B + \operatorname{ctg} D) / 4 \mid$.

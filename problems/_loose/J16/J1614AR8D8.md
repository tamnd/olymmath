In a $5 \times 5$ matrix $X$, each element is 0 or 1. Let $x_{i, j} (i, j=1,2, \cdots, 5)$ represent the element in the $i$-th row and $j$-th column of $X$. Consider all the five-element ordered arrays in the rows, columns, and diagonals of $X$ (a total of 24 arrays):
$$
\left(x_{i, 1}, x_{i, 2}, \cdots, x_{i, 5}\right),\left(x_{i, 5}, x_{i, 4}, \cdots, x_{i, 1}\right),
$$

where $i=1,2, \cdots, 5$;
$$
\left(x_{1, j}, x_{2, j}, \cdots, x_{5, j}\right),\left(x_{5, j}, x_{4, j}, \cdots, x_{1, j}\right),
$$

where $j=1,2, \cdots, 5$;
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(x_{1,1}, x_{2,2}, \cdots, x_{5,5}\right),\left(x_{5,5}, x_{4,4}, \cdots, x_{1,1}\right), \\
\left(x_{1,5}, x_{2,4}, \cdots, x_{5,1}\right),\left(x_{5,1}, x_{4,2}, \cdots, x_{1,5}\right) .
\end{array}
$$

If these arrays are all distinct, find the possible values of the sum of all elements in matrix $X$.

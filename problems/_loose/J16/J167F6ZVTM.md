Calculate the line integral

$$
\int_{L} \frac{z^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}} d l
$$

where $L-$ is the first turn of the helical line

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=\cos t \\
y=\sin t, 0 \leq t \leq 2 \pi \\
z=t
\end{array}\right.
$$

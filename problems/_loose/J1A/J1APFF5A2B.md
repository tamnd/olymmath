In a $3 \cdot 3$ square grid, fill in nine different natural numbers such that the product of the three numbers in each row and the product of the three numbers in each column are equal (we use $P$ to represent this product).
(1) Prove that this method of filling in the numbers is possible.
(2) Determine which of the six numbers $1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995$ can be the value of $P$.
(3) Find the minimum value of $P$, and explain your reasoning.
(China Beijing Junior High School Grade 2 Mathematics Competition, 1991)

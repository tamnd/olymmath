If two circles touch each other externally, their common tangents form an angle $\varphi$ with each other, and the radii of the circles are $r, r^{\prime}$, then

$$
\left(r+r^{\prime}\right)^{2} \sin \varphi=4\left(r-r^{\prime}\right) \sqrt{r r^{\prime}}
$$

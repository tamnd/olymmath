There is a stack of $n$ cards numbered $1, 2, \cdots, n$ from top to bottom. Each time, a small stack of cards (which can be just one card) can be taken out from this stack and inserted as a whole without changing the order into any position of the original stack (it can be inserted between two cards, or placed on top of the first card or below the $n$-th card). Such an operation is called a single move. Question: What is the minimum number of moves required to completely reverse the order of the stack, so that the $n$ cards are numbered from top to bottom as $n$, $n-1, \cdots, 2, 1?$

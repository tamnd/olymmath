The function $f:[a, b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is differentiable, with $0 \leq f^{\prime}(x) \leq 1, \forall x \in[a, b]$ and $f(a)=0$.

Show that $3\left(\int_{a}^{b} f^{2}(x) d x\right)^{3} \geq \int_{a}^{b} f^{8}(x) d x$.

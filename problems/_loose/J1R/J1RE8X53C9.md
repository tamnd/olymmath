In a chess tournament, the participants played one game with each other, and no game ended in a draw. Show that among the participants there is a chess player $A$ with the following property: if $B$ is any other participant in the tournament, then $A$ has defeated $B$, or there is a chess player $C$ who has been defeated by $A$ and has defeated $B$.

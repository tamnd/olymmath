A measuring tape has a length of $l$ meters and a thickness of $d^{\mathrm{m}} / \mathrm{m}$. If we roll it up in the usual way, how many turns will it make, given that the circumference of the first turn is $k \mathrm{~cm}$? What is the length of the last turn?

For example, $l=1.5, d=1, k=1$.

The diagonals of the inscribed quadrilateral $A B C D$ intersect at point $O$. Prove that

$$
\frac{A B}{C D}+\frac{C D}{A B}+\frac{B C}{A D}+\frac{A D}{B C} \leqslant \frac{O A}{O C}+\frac{O C}{O A}+\frac{O B}{O D}+\frac{O D}{O B}
$$

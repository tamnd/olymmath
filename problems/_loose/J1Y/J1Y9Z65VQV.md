Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence such that $a_{1}=43, a_{2}=$ $142, a_{n+1}=3 a_{n}+a_{n-1}$ for $n \geq 2$. Prove that:
a) $a_{n}$ and $a_{n+1}$ are relatively prime for all $n$;

b) for every natural number $m$ there exist infinitely many natural numbers $n$, such that $a_{n}-1$ and $a_{n+1}-1$ both are divisible by $m$.

Let $C H$ be the altitude of $\triangle A B C$ on side $A B$, and $H$ is between $A$ and $B$. $P$ and $Q$ are the incenters of $\triangle A H C$ and $\triangle B H C$, respectively. Prove: Quadrilateral $A B Q P$ is a cyclic quadrilateral if and only if $A C=B C$ or $\angle A C B=90^{\circ}$.

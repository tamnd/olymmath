Let for every non-negative random variable $\xi$ the equalities hold

$$
\begin{aligned}
& L_{*} \xi=\sup \sum_{i} \mathrm{P}\left(A_{i}\right) \inf _{\omega \in A_{i}} \xi(\omega), \\
& L^{*} \xi=\inf \sum_{i} \mathrm{P}\left(A_{i}\right) \sup _{\omega \in A_{i}} \xi(\omega),
\end{aligned}
$$

where sup and inf are taken over all finite partitions $\left(A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}\right)$ of the space $\Omega$ into sets $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n} \in \mathscr{F}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_{i}=\Omega\right), n \geqslant 1$.

Prove that $L_{*} \xi \leqslant L^{*} \xi$ and if the random variable $\xi$ is bounded, then $L_{*} \xi=L^{*} \xi=\mathrm{E} \xi$.

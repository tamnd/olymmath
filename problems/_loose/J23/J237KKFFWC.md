Given a line $e$ and points $A, B$ on the same side of it at different distances from it. András claims that the following procedure prepares for the marking of the centers of the circles passing through $A$ and $B$ and tangent to $e$.

I. The point where line $AB$ intersects $e$ is $C$.

II. The reflection of $A$ over $e$ is point $A^{\prime}$.

III. The perpendicular bisector of segment $A^{\prime} B$ intersects the perpendicular to $e$ at $C$ at point $D$.

IV. The circle centered at $D$ with radius $D A^{\prime}$ intersects $e$ at points $T_{1}, T_{2}$.

How can the marking of the said centers be completed based on these steps?

To make three kinds of products $A,B,C$ , we have three parts $a,b,c$ . A product $A$ is made of two $a$ and two $b$ ; a product $B$ is made of one $b$ and one $c$ ; a product $C$ is made of two $a$ and one $c$ . We have a few parts.
If we make $p$ product $A$ , $q$ product $B$ , $r$ product $C$ , then $2$ part $a$ and $1$ part $b$ are remained.
Prove: no matter how we make products, we cannot use up all the parts.

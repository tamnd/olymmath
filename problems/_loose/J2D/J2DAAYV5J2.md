Initially, a flea is located at the point $(0,0)$ of the Cartesian plane. Subsequently, it makes $n$ jumps. Each jump is made in one of the four cardinal directions. The first jump is of length 1, the second of length 2, the third of length 4, and so on, up to the $n$-th jump, which is of length $2^{n-1}$. Prove that, if the final position of the flea is known, then it is possible to uniquely determine its position after each of the $n$ jumps.

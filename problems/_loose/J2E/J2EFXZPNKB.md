In the isosceles triangle $ABC$ ( $AB=AC$ ), let $l$ be a line parallel to $BC$ through $A$ . Let $D$ be an arbitrary point on $l$ . Let $E,F$ be the feet of perpendiculars through $A$ to $BD,CD$ respectively. Suppose that $P,Q$ are the projections of $E,F$ on $l$ . Prove that $AP+AQ\le AB$ *Proposed by Morteza Saghafian*

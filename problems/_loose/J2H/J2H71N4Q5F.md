Consider the acute-angled triangle $A B C$ with altitudes $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$ and orthocenter $H$.

a) If $\frac{A H}{H A^{\prime}}=x$ and $\frac{S_{\triangle H B C}}{S_{\triangle A B C}}=y$, show that $\frac{1}{y}=x+1$, where $S_{\triangle H B C}, S_{\triangle A B C}$ represent the area of triangle $H B C$ and the area of triangle $A B C$, respectively.

b) If $A A^{\prime} \leq B B^{\prime} \leq C C^{\prime}$, prove that $A H+B H+C H \geq 2 A A^{\prime}$.

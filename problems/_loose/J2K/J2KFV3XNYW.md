The ratio in which the bisector divides the side.

[Theorem on the lengths of a tangent and a secant; the product of the entire secant and its external part

From point $T$, a tangent $T A$ and a secant intersecting the circle at points $B$ and $C$ are drawn. The bisector of angle $A T C$ intersects chords $A B$ and $A C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that $P A=\sqrt{P B \cdot Q C}$.

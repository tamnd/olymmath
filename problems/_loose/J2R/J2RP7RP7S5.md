In the plane of one of the lateral faces of a regular quadrilateral pyramid, an arbitrary triangle is taken. This triangle is projected onto the base of the pyramid, and the resulting triangle is then projected onto the adjacent lateral face. Prove that the triangle obtained from the last projection is similar to the original triangle.

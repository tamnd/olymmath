On the edge $ |OA_1| $ of the tetrahedron $ |OA_1B_1C_1| $, points $ A_2 $, $ A_3 $ are chosen such that $ |OA_1| > |OA_2| > |OA_3| > O $. Let $ B_2 $, $ B_3 $ be points on the edge $ |OB_1| $, and $ C_2 $, $ C_3 $ be points on the edge $ |OC_1| $, such that the planes $ |A_1B_1C_1| $, $ |A_2B_2C_2| $, $ |A_3B_3C_3| $ are parallel. Denote by $ V_i $ ($ i=1,2,3 $) the volume of the tetrahedron $ |OA_iB_iC_i| $, and by $ V $ the volume of the tetrahedron $ |OA_1B_2C_3| $. Prove that $ V_1 + V_2 + V_3 \geq 3V $.

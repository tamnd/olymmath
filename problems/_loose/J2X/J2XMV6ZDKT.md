Let $M=\{(x, y)\mid x, y\in\mathbb N, x+y\le2025\}$ . Paint the points in $M$ with 3 colors. Prove that there exists an isosceles right triangle $\triangle$ $ABC$ such that $A, B, C$ are painted the same color and $AB\parallel\ell_1:y=0$ , $AC\parallel\ell_2:x=0$ , $BC\parallel\ell_3:x+y=0$ .

In an isosceles right triangle, inscribe all possible isosceles right triangles such that the vertices of the inscribed triangles coincide with an interior point of each of the different sides of the given triangle. Among the inscribed triangles, which one has the smallest area? What fraction of the area of the given triangle is this smallest area?

Around a circle, $m$ numbers are written in such a way that any two adjacent numbers differ by 1. We call a number strong if both of its neighbors are less than it, and weak if both of its neighbors are greater than it. Let the sum of all strong numbers be $S$, and the sum of all weak numbers be $s$. Prove that $m=2(S-s)$.

(2007 Italian National Team Selection Exam) $p$ is a prime number greater than 3, prove:
(1) $(p-1)^{p}+1$ has at least one prime factor different from $p$;
(2) Let $(p-1)^{p}+1=\prod_{i=1}^{n} p_{i}^{\alpha_{i}}$, where $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$ are distinct primes, and $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}$ are positive integers, then $\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i} \alpha_{i} \geqslant \frac{p^{2}}{2}$.

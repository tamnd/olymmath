(GDR, 83). In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, the edges $A D, B D$ and $C D$ are mutually perpendicular, and their lengths are $a, b, c$ respectively. Prove that for any point $M$, lying on one of the sides of the triangle $A B C$, the sum $S$ of the distances from the vertices $A, B$ and $C$ to the line $D M$ satisfies the inequality

$$
S \leqslant \sqrt{2\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)}
$$

Determine when equality is achieved.

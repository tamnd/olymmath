For a general triangle $ABC$, draw a point $D$ such that the area of triangle $ABD$ is equal to half the area of triangle $ABC$ and the area of triangle $BCD$ is equal to one-sixth the area of triangle $ABC$.

(The solution should be generally valid, i.e., independent of the chosen triangle, its special properties, or dimensions. The construction must not be based on measurement and calculation. Choose a triangle that is neither isosceles nor right-angled.)

Given the polynomial $P(x)=1+2 x+3 x^{2}+4 x^{3}+\ldots+101 x^{100}$. Is it possible, by rearranging the coefficients in it, to obtain a polynomial $Q(x)=q_{0}+q_{1} x+q_{2} x^{2}+q_{3} x^{3}+\ldots+q_{100} x^{100}$ such that for all natural numbers $k \geqslant 2$ the difference $P(k)-Q(k)$ is not divisible by 2020?

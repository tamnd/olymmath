The five friends Arnaldo, Bernaldo, Cernaldo, Dernaldo, and Ernaldo competed in five races among themselves. In each race, the winner received five points, the second place received four points, and so on, until the last place, which received only one point. To obtain the final score of each runner, the points obtained in the five races were summed. In the final score, Arnaldo came in first place with 19 points, followed by Bernaldo with 16 points. The third, fourth, and fifth places were occupied by Cernaldo, Dernaldo, and Ernaldo, respectively. There were no ties in any race or in the final score. But a curious fact is that neither Arnaldo nor Bernaldo won any of the five races.

a) Show that in each race, both Arnaldo and Bernaldo always finished in second and third place (in some order).

b) State how many points Cernaldo scored.

c) How many races did each of the runners win?

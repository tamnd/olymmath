Coal mined at point A is sold at q rubles per ton, while coal mined at point B is sold at $p\%$ more expensive. Points A and B are connected by a road $s$ km long. In which zone of this road AB are the consumers of coal located, for whom the purchase and delivery of coal from B is cheaper than from A, if the transportation of 1 ton of coal for a distance of 1 km costs $r$ rubles? At what point on the road AB is the enterprise located, the expenses for which the consumption of coal do not depend on the choice of point $A$ or $B$?

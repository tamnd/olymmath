A convex quadrilateral $P Q R S$ has integer coordinates for each of its vertices. Let the intersection of the diagonals of the quadrilateral be $E$. Prove that if the sum of the angles at vertices $P$ and $Q$ is less than $180^{\circ}$, then the triangle $P Q E$ contains a point in its interior or on its boundary, different from $P$ and $Q$, which also has integer coordinates.

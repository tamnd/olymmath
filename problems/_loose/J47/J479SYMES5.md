On an island where knights, liars, and normal people live, liars are considered to be of the lowest rank, normal people are of the middle rank, and knights are of the highest rank.

I really like the following problem. Two people, A and B, each of whom is either a liar or a normal person, make the following statements:

A: I am of a lower rank than B.

B: That's not true!

Can the rank of A or B be determined? Can it be established whether each of these two statements is true or false?

Let $B C X$ be an acute triangle (you weren't expecting that one, were you, sometimes you have to change your habits). The tangents to its circumcircle at $B$ and $C$ intersect at $A$. The line $(A X)$ intersects the circumcircle again at $Y$. The circumcircle of $X Y B$ intersects $(A B)$ at $P$ and the circumcircle of $X Y C$ intersects $(A C)$ at $Q$. Show that $A P Q$ is isosceles at $A$.

(Jury, 81). Let for polynomials $P(x)$, $Q(x)$ of degree greater than 0 be denoted

$$
P_{c}=\{z \in \mathbf{C} \mid P(z)=c\}, Q_{c}=\{z \in \mathbf{C} \mid Q(z)=c\}
$$

Prove that if $P_{0}=Q_{0}$ and $P_{1}=Q_{1}$, then $P(x)=Q(x)$, $x \in \mathbf{R}$.

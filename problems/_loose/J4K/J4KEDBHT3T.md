There are $n$ players $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}(n>1)$ participating in a round-robin competition, each player competes against all other players once and there are no ties, use $W_{i}$ and $L_{i}$ to respectively denote the number of wins and losses for player $p_{i}$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} W_{i}^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} L_{i}^{2}$.

On the graph of the function $y=\frac{1}{x}$, two points $A$ and $B$ with positive abscissas are selected. Points $H_{A}$ and $H_{B}$ are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from these points to the x-axis, and $O$ is the origin. Prove that the area of the figure bounded by the lines $O A, O B$ and the arc $A B$ is equal to the area of the figure bounded by the lines $A H_{A}, B H_{B}$, the x-axis, and the arc $A B$.

Let $p, q$ be two distinct prime numbers, $m, n$ natural numbers, and the sum

$$
\frac{m p-1}{q}+\frac{n q-1}{p}
$$

is an integer. Prove that the inequality

$$
\frac{m}{q}+\frac{n}{p}>1
$$

holds.

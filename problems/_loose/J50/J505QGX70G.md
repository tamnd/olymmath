Consider the set of words that can only be written using the letters $x, y$, and $t$. Additionally, we are allowed to replace groups of letters according to the following rules:

(i) $x y \longleftrightarrow y y x$

(ii) $x t \longleftrightarrow t t x$

(iii) $y t \longleftrightarrow t y$

Using the allowed operations, can we transform:

a) the word $x y$ into the word $x t$?

b) the word $x y t x$ into the word $t x y t$?

c) the word $x t x y y$ into the word ttxyyyyx?

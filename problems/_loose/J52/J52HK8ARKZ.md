If $a_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, m)$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{m} a_{i}=p$, and $q, r$ are positive constants, $n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$, prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(q a_{i}+\frac{r}{a_{i}}\right)^{n} \geqslant \frac{\left(q p^{2}+m^{2} r\right)^{n}}{m^{n-1} \cdot p^{n}} .
$$

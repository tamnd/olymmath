For positive integers $m,n,$ define $f(m,n)$ as the number of ordered triples $(x,y,z)$ of integers such that $$
\begin{cases}
xyz=x+y+z+m,
\max\{|x|,|y|,|z|\} \leq n
\end{cases} $$ Does there exist positive integers $m,n,$ such that $f(m,n)=2018?$ Please prove your conclusion.

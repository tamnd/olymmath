Let $I_{A}, I_{B}, I_{C}$ be the centers of the excircles of $\triangle A B C$ that are tangent to $B C, C A, A B$ respectively. Prove: (1) the nine-point circle of $\triangle I_{A} I_{B} I_{C}$ is the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$; (2) the perpendiculars from $I_{A}, I_{B}, I_{C}$ to $B C, C A, A B$ respectively are concurrent.

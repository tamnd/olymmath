Given quadratic trinomials $f_{1}(x)=x^{2}-x-a, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}+b x+2, f_{3}(x)=4 x^{2}+(b-3) x-3 a+2$ and $f_{4}(x)=4 x^{2}+(3 b-1) x+6-a$. Let the differences of their roots be $A, B, C$ and $D$, respectively, and given that $|A| \neq|B|$. Find the ratio $\frac{C^{2}-D^{2}}{A^{2}-B^{2}}$. The values of $A, B, C, D, a, b$ are not specified.

Given a circle $k$ with center $M$. On $k$ lie the points $A$ and $B$ such that the angle $\angle B M A$ is a right angle. Furthermore, a point $C$ is defined by the following conditions:

(1) $C$ lies on $k$.

(2) It holds that $M B = B C$.

(3) The line through $A$ and $C$ intersects the segment $M B$ at a point $D$.

Determine the size of the angle $\angle C D B$ from these conditions!

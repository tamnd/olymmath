Prove that for any $\alpha \leqslant 1$ and any numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfying $1 \geqslant x_{1} \geqslant x_{2} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant x_{n}>0$, we have
$$
\left(1+x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)^{\alpha} \leqslant 1+1^{\alpha^{-1}} \cdot x_{1}^{\alpha}+2^{\alpha-1} x_{2}^{\alpha}+\cdots+n^{\alpha-1} x_{n}^{\alpha} .
$$

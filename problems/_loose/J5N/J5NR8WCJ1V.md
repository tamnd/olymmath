$e, f, g$ are lines in a plane passing through a point $O$. For any point $P$, its reflection over $e$ is $P_{1}$, the reflection of $P_{1}$ over $f$ is $P_{2}$, and the reflection of $P_{2}$ over $g$ is $P_{3}$. The line $P P_{3}$ intersects $e$ and $g$ at points $Q_{1}$ and $Q_{2}$, respectively. Prove that the lines $Q_{1} P_{1}$ and $Q_{2} P_{2}$ intersect on $f$.

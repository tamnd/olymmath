For three strictly positive numbers $a, b$, and $c$, we associate:

$$
\begin{aligned}
u & =\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{a} \\
v & =\frac{a}{c}+\frac{b}{a}+\frac{c}{b}
\end{aligned}
$$

1 Show that for any $a, b, c, u+v \geqslant 6$.
2. Show that for any $a, b, c, u v \geqslant 9$.
3. What is the minimum value of $u^{2}+v^{2}$?

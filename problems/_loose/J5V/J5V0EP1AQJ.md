b) Through point $D$ on the angle bisector $B B_{1}$ of angle $A B C$, lines $A A_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ are drawn (points $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ lie on the sides of the triangle). Prove that if $A A_{1}=C C_{1}$, then $A B=B C$.

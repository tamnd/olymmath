Given a triangle $A B C$. From a point $P$ inside it, perpendiculars $P A^{\prime}, P B^{\prime}, P C^{\prime}$ are dropped to the sides $B C$, $C A$, $A B$ respectively. Then from the point $P$, perpendiculars $P A^{\prime \prime}, P B^{\prime \prime}$ are dropped to the sides $B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime} A^{\prime}$ respectively. Prove that

$P A \cdot P A^{\prime} \cdot P A^{\prime \prime}=P B \cdot P B^{\prime} \cdot P B^{\prime \prime}$.

Briot (an English mathematician, who lived from 1817 to 1882) and Ruffini (an Italian mathematician, who lived from 1765 to 1822) developed methods to find solutions for equations called reciprocal. In this question, you will develop, step by step, the essence of these methods. Item (a) is a preparation for the other items.

(a) If $y=x+\frac{1}{x}$, calculate the expressions $x^{2}+\frac{1}{x^{2}}$ and $x^{3}+\frac{1}{x^{3}}$ in terms of $y$.

(b) Determine all real roots of the equation $x^{2}-5 x+8-\frac{5}{x}+\frac{1}{x^{2}}=0$.

(c) Determine all real roots of $x^{4}-5 x^{3}+8 x^{2}-5 x+1=0$.

(d) Determine all real roots of $x^{6}-2 x^{5}-5 x^{4}+12 x^{3}-5 x^{2}-2 x+1=0$.

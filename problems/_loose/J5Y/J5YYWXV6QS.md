prove that there exists infinite sets $A,B\subset \mathbb{Q}$ such that:
- $A\cup B=\mathbb{Q}$ , $A\cap B=\emptyset$ - $\forall x,y \in A$ there is $xy\in B$ , $\forall x,y \in B$ there is $xy\in B$ - $\forall n\in\mathbb{Z}$ there is $(n,n+1)\cap A\neq\emptyset$ and $(n,n+1)\cap B\neq\emptyset$ note that $(a,b)$ denotes the set of all real numbers between $a$ and $b$ .

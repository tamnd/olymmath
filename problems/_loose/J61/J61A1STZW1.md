Circles $k$ and $l$ with different radii touch each other externally at point T. Let $M$ be the intersection of their common external tangents, and draw a secant $s$ through $M$ intersecting both circles. Let $X$ be the intersection of circle $k$ with secant $s$ that is farther from point $M$. Similarly, let $Y$ be the intersection of circle $l$ with secant $s$ that is farther from point $M$. Let $P$ be a point such that XTYP is a parallelogram. Determine the set of points $P$ corresponding to all such secants $s$.

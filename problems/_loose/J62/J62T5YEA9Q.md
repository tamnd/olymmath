Equilateral triangles $\triangle A B D, \triangle A C E$, and $\triangle B C F$ are drawn outside $\triangle A B C$ on each of its sides, with centers $G, H$, and $I$, respectively.

a) Prove that $\triangle G H I$ is equilateral.

b) Let $G^{\prime}, H^{\prime}$, and $I^{\prime}$ be the reflections of $G, H$, and $I$ across $A B, B C$, and $C A$, respectively. Prove that $\triangle G H I$ and $\triangle G^{\prime} H^{\prime} I^{\prime}$ have the same circumcenter.

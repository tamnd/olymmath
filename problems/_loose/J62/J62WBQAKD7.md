Given three numbers $p, p_{1}, p_{2}$ with $0<p_{1}<p<p_{2}<100$.

From a suitable amount $x$ kg of a $p_{1}$-percent solution of a substance (i.e., a solution that contains $p_{1}$ % of this substance and the rest water) and a suitable amount $y$ kg of a $p_{2}$-percent solution of the same substance, a $p$-percent solution is to be produced by mixing.

a) Determine the required mixing ratio, i.e., the number $x: y$, first specifically for the values $p_{1}=25, p_{2}=60$ and $p=35$!

b) Then derive a formula for the mixing ratio that is valid for arbitrary values of $p_{1}, p_{2}$ and $p$!

Note: The given percentages refer to the mass, and are not to be interpreted as volume percentages.

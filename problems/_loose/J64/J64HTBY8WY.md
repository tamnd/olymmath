Two non-intersecting circles are inscribed in an angle $B$. The circle with the larger radius touches the sides of this angle at points $A$ and $C$, and the smaller circle touches the sides at points $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ (points $A, A_{1}$ and $C, C_{1}$ lie on different sides of angle $B$). The line $A C_{1}$ intersects the larger and smaller circles at points $E$ and $F$ respectively. Find the ratio of the areas of triangles $A B C_{1}$ and $A_{1} B C_{1}$, if $A_{1} B=2, E F=1$, and the length of $A E$ is the arithmetic mean of the lengths of $B C_{1}$ and $E F$.

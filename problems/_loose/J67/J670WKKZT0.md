Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}, a_{n+1}$ be a sequence of distinct elements forming an arithmetic sequence. Write the sum

$$
S_{n}=x^{a_{2}^{2}-a_{1}^{2}}+x^{a_{3}^{2}-a_{2}^{2}}+\ldots+x^{a_{n+1}^{2}-a_{n}^{2}}
$$

in a simplified form, if $x \neq 1$.

Semi-circle $AB$ with diameter $AB$, and $AB=2r$. Given line $l$, satisfying that $l \perp BA, l \cap BA=T , |AT|=2a(2a<r)$. $M,N$ are two points on the semi-circle, such that
$$d(M,l)=|AM|,d(N,l)=|AN|(M\neq N).$$
Prove: $|AM|+|AN|=|AB|$.

The Calculating Carrier. Ship $A$ has dropped anchor 9 miles from the nearest point on a straight shoreline $O$. Ship $B$ is 3 miles from the shore opposite a point on the shore located 6 miles from $O$. A boat departs from ship $A$, sails to some point on the shore, picks up a passenger there, and delivers him to ship $B$. One mile of travel costs the boat owner 1 dollar regardless of whether he is carrying a passenger or traveling empty. The passenger pays the boat owner 2 dollars per mile of travel. Where should the boat owner arrange to meet the passenger so that his net income (on the journey from $A$ to the shore and then to $B$) is maximized?

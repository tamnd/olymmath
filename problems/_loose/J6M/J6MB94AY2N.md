Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{1}=\frac{3}{2}$, and $a_{n}=\frac{3 n a_{n-1}}{2 a_{n-1}+n-1}\left(n \geqslant 2, n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}\right)$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) For all positive integers $n$, the inequality $a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \cdots a_{n}<\lambda \cdot n!$ always holds, try to find the minimum value of the positive integer $\lambda$.

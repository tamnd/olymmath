Prince George has 100 coins, some of which may be counterfeit (possibly all or none). George can show the expert between 10 and 20 coins, and the expert will tell him how many of them are counterfeit. The problem is that the only expert in the area is Baron Münchhausen, and he exaggerates: the result given by the baron is always greater than the true number by some fixed (and unknown to George) natural number. The baron is not bothered by the fact that he might say "thirteen," for example, if he is given only twelve coins. Can George guarantee to find out which coins are counterfeit by consulting the expert fewer than 120 times?

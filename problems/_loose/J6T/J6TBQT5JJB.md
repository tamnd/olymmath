Circle $O$ is the incircle of the square $ABCD$ . $O$ is tangent to $AB$ and $AD$ at $E$ and $F$ , respectively. Let $K$ be a point on the minor arc $EF$ , and let the tangent of $O$ at $K$ intersect $AB$ , $AC$ , $AD$ at $X$ , $Y$ , $Z$ , respectively. Show that $ \frac{AX}{XB} + \frac{AY}{YC} + \frac{AZ}{ZD} =1$ .

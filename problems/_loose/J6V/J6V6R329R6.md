To promote the development of freshwater fish farming in a certain area, the government controls the price within an appropriate range and provides subsidies for freshwater fish farming. Let the market price of freshwater fish be $x$ yuan/kg, and the government subsidy be 1 yuan/kg. According to market research, when $8 \leqslant x \leqslant 14$, the market daily supply $P(\mathrm{~kg})$ and the market daily demand $Q(\mathrm{~kg})$ approximately satisfy the relationship: $P=1000(x+t-8)(x \geqslant 8, t \geqslant 0), Q=500 \sqrt{40-(x-8)^{2}}(8 \leqslant x \leqslant 14)$. The market price when $P=Q$ is called the market equilibrium price.
(1) Express the market equilibrium price as a function of the government subsidy, and find the domain of the function;
(2) To ensure that the market equilibrium price does not exceed 10 yuan/kg, what is the minimum subsidy per kilogram that the government should provide?

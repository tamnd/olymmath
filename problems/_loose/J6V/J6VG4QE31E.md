Let $G$ be a graph, $T$ a set, and $\mathcal{V}=\left(V_{t}\right)_{t \in T}$ a family of subsets of $V(G)$ satisfying (T1) and (T2) in the definition of a tree decomposition. Prove: There exists a tree $T$ satisfying (T3) if and only if there exists an ordering $t_{1}, \ldots, t_{n}$ of $T$ such that for each $k=2, \ldots, n$ there exists a $j<k$ satisfying $V_{t_{k}} \cap \bigcup_{i<k} V_{t_{i}} \subseteq V_{t_{j}}$.

1404 integers are arranged around a circle. For $ k \leq 1404 $, a block of length $ k $ is any $ k $ consecutive numbers around the circle. A block is called balanced if the sum of the numbers in the block is zero. It is known that there are no balanced blocks of even length. Find the maximum number of balanced blocks of odd length.

Players have a pile of equal tokens, from which they alternately take one and place it on any empty cell of a square table of size $2008 \times 2008$. The player who first places a token such that it, together with three others, forms the vertices of a rectangle with sides parallel to the sides of the table wins. Which player has a winning strategy - the one who starts the game, or his opponent?

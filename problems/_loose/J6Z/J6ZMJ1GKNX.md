Connect all diagonals of a convex $(4k+3)$-gon, where no three lines intersect at a point inside the polygon. Let $P$ be a point inside the polygon that does not lie on any diagonal. Prove that the number of quadrilaterals, whose vertices are among the $(4k+3)$ vertices of the $(4k+3)$-gon and which contain point $P$, is a multiple of 2 (where $k$ is a positive integer).

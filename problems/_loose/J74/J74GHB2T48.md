The first 50 natural numbers will cross a corridor that contains 50 doors numbered from 1 to 50, all of which are initially locked. The first to cross will be the number 1, the second will be the number 2, followed by the number 3, and so on, until the number 50, which will be the last to cross. As they cross the corridor, the number $n$ will carry the keys to the doors numbered with multiples of $n$. For example, the number 1 will carry the keys to all the doors, while the number 2 will carry only the keys to the doors with even numbers, and the number 25 will carry only the keys to the doors numbered 25 and 50. During their passage, each number uses the keys they possess to lock the doors that are open and unlock those that are closed.

a) Which doors will be unlocked by the number 15?

b) Show that, after the number 50 has crossed the corridor, the door numbered 10 will be unlocked while the door numbered 9 will be locked.

c) After the number 50 has crossed the corridor, which doors will be unlocked?

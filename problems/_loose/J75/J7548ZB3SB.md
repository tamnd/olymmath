Determine all functions $f$ that are defined for all real numbers $x$ with $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq 1$, and for all real numbers $x$ with $x \neq 0$, $x^{2}-x-1 \neq 0$, and $x^{2}+x-1 \neq 0$, and satisfy the following equation (1):

$$
2 \cdot f\left(\frac{x^{2}+x-1}{x^{2}-x-1}\right)-3 \cdot f\left(\frac{x^{2}-x-1}{x^{2}+x-1}\right)=5 \cdot\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)
$$

Let $N(x)$ be the number of sign changes in the sequence $f(x), f'(x), \ldots, f^{(n)}(x)$, where $f$ is a polynomial of degree $n$. Prove that the number of roots of the polynomial $f$ (counting multiplicities) between $a$ and $b$, where $f(a) \neq 0$, $f(b) \neq 0$ and $a < b$, does not exceed $N(a) - N(b)$, and the number of roots can differ from $N(a) - N(b)$ only by an even number (Fourier-Budan).

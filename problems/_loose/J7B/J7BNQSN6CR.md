Given an equilateral triangle $A B C$. Inside it, a point $D$ is taken such that it divides triangle $A B C$ into three triangles $A B D, B C D, A C D$ such that one of them is a right triangle, and another is an isosceles triangle with a vertex angle of 130 degrees. Find the smallest and largest possible values of angle $B C D$ in degrees.

It is known that the sum of the angles in a triangle is 180 degrees.

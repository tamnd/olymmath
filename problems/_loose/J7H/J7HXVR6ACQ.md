A circle passes through the vertices $A$ and $N$ of triangle $A C N$ and intersects its sides $A C$ and $C N$ at points $B$ and $K$, respectively, different from the vertices of the triangle. The ratio of the area of triangle $B C K$ to the area of triangle $A C N$ is $\frac{1}{4}$.

a) Find the ratio $A N: B K$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the areas of triangles $B C N$ and $A C K$ is $\frac{9}{16}$. Find the ratio $N K: A B$.

There is a time interval during which both the fox and the dog make a whole number of jumps. Each time it turns out that the dog manages to make $t \%$ fewer jumps than the fox, where $\mathrm{t}$ is an integer. Assuming that all jumps, both of the dog and the fox, are the same, find the minimum value of $t$ for which the fox can escape from the dog.

Given a trapezoid $A B C D$ with bases $A B$ and $C D$. The diagonals of the trapezoid intersect at point $T$. If $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ are the areas of triangles $A B T$ and $C D T$, and $P$ is the area of the trapezoid, prove that:

$$
P=\left(\sqrt{P_{1}}+\sqrt{P_{2}}\right)^{2}
$$

A Haiku is a Japanese poem of seventeen syllables, in three lines of five, seven, and five.

In how many ways
Can you add three integers
Summing seventeen?
Order matters here.
For example, eight, three, six
Is not eight, six, three.
All nonnegative,
Do not need to be distinct.
What is your answer?

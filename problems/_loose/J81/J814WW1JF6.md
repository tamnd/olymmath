Inside a rectangular grid with a perimeter of 50 cells, a rectangular hole with a perimeter of 32 cells is cut out along the cell boundaries (the hole does not contain any boundary cells). If the figure is cut along all horizontal grid lines, 20 strips 1 cell wide will be obtained. How many strips will be obtained if, instead, it is cut along all vertical grid lines? (A $1 \times 1$ square is also a strip!)

Let $\Gamma$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$, and $\ell$ be a line that does not intersect $\Gamma$. We denote $E$ as the point of intersection between $\ell$ and the line perpendicular to $\ell$ passing through $O$.

Let $M$ be a point on $\ell$ different from $E$. The tangents to the circle $\Gamma$ passing through $M$ touch $\Gamma$ at points $A$ and $B$. Finally, let $H$ be the point of intersection of the lines $(AB)$ and $(OE)$.

Show that $OH = r^2 / OE$.

Let $A O B$ be a given angle less than $180^{\circ}$ and let $P$ be an interior point of the angular region determined by $\angle A O B$. Show, with proof, how to construct, using only ruler and compasses, a line segment $C D$ passing through $P$ such that $C$ lies on the ray $O A$ and $D$ lies on the ray $O B$, and $C P: P D=1: 2$.

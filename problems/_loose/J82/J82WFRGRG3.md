Let $AB$ be the diameter of the circle $K$ with radius $R$ lying in the plane $\alpha$, and let $S$ be a point on the normal to the plane $\alpha$ at point $B$. A plane $\beta$ is drawn through point $S$, which is perpendicular to the plane $ABS$ and forms an angle of $\frac{\pi}{3}$ with the plane $\alpha$.

a) Determine the boundary values for $x = BS$ such that $\beta$ intersects the circle $K$ at two points $C$ and $D$.

b) Prove that the angles $SCA$ and $SDA$ are right angles.

c) Determine the sum $y$ of the squares of the edges of the tetrahedron $SDAC$ as a function of $x$ and $R$.

d) Determine the geometric locus of the extremum of the function $y(x)$ as $R$ varies.

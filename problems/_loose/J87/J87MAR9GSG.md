Let $G=\left\{\left(\begin{array}{rr}a & -b \\ b & a\end{array}\right): a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_{7}\right\}, O_{2}=\left(\begin{array}{ll}\widehat{0} & \widehat{0} \\ \widehat{0} & \widehat{0}\end{array}\right)$ and $I_{2}=\left(\begin{array}{ll}\widehat{1} & \widehat{0} \\ \widehat{0} & \widehat{1}\end{array}\right)$.

a) Prove that if $A \in G$ and $\operatorname{det}(A)=\widehat{0}$, then $A=O_{2}$;

b) Prove that $G \backslash\left\{O_{2}\right\}$ is a stable subset under matrix multiplication in $\mathcal{M}_{2}\left(\mathbb{Z}_{7}\right)$;

c) Determine whether the equation $X^{12}=\left(\begin{array}{rr}\widehat{2} & -\widehat{2} \\ \widehat{2} & \widehat{2}\end{array}\right)$ has solutions in the set $G$.

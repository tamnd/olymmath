a) Let $m$ and $n$ be natural numbers. Prove that if for some non-negative integers $k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{n}$ the number $2^{k_{1}}+2^{k_{2}}+\ldots+2^{k_{n}}$ is divisible by $2^{m}-1$, then $n \geqslant m$.

b) Does there exist a natural number that is divisible by $\underbrace{111 \ldots 1}$ and has a sum of digits less than $m$?

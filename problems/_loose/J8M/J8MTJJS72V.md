For a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ inscribed in a circle, draw any line through vertex $C$ that intersects the extensions of $AB$ and $AD$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively. Prove that: (1) $AB \cdot CE \cdot DF + AD \cdot BE \cdot CF = BC \cdot CD \cdot EF$; (2) $AE \cdot CF \cdot BC = AF \cdot EC \cdot CD$.

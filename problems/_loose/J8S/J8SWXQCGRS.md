The sequence of real numbers $a_1,a_2,...,a_{2015}$ is such that the 2015 equations:
$a_1^3=a_1^2;a_1^3+a_2^3=(a_1+a_2 )^2;...;a_1^3+a_2^3+...+a_{2015}^3=(a_1+a_2+...+a_{2015} )^2$
are true. Prove that $a_1,a_2,…,a_{2015}$ are integers.

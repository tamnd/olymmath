Find all real $\alpha$ for which the system of equations

$$
\frac{a^{3}}{b+c+\alpha}=\frac{b^{3}}{c+a+\alpha}=\frac{c^{3}}{a+b+\alpha}
$$

has a solution in distinct real $a, b, c$ from $[-1 ; 1]$.

$$

In areas where the flu is widespread, everyone visits their friends every day. Healthy people who visit sick friends will also fall ill the next day, and everyone is sick for one day and then recovers. After recovery, a person has at least one day of immunity (varies from person to person, no infection during the immune period). Prove: (1) If some people have immunity on the first day, the flu may spread indefinitely; (2) If no one has immunity on the first day, the flu will eventually end.

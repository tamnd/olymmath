Let the intersection point of the diagonals of the convex cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ inscribed in a circle with center $O$ be $M$, and the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $ABM$ and $CDM$ be $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively. It is to be proven that $O, O_{1}, M$ and $O_{2}$ are the vertices of a parallelogram.

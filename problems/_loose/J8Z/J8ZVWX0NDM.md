Given a $\triangle A B C$ and an internal point $P$, the lines $A P, B P$ and $C P$ intersect the opposite sides at $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ respectively. Prove: Among the three ratios $A P: P A^{\prime}, B P: P B^{\prime}, C P: P C^{\prime}$, at least one is not greater than 2, and at least one is not less than 2.

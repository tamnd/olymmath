- $A$ writes to $B$ every third day, and to $C$ every second day;
- $B$ writes to $A$ after receiving four letters from $A$ and $C$, and to $C$ every third day;
- $C$ writes to $A$ after receiving three letters from $A$ and $B$, and to $B$ every fourth day.

After how many days will $A$ receive 61 letters, if letters take one day to travel?

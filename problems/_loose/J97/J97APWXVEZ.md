Solve the following systems of equations:

$$
\begin{gathered}
x+r y-q z=1 \\
-r x+y+p z=r \\
q x-p y+z=-q
\end{gathered}
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
x^{\prime}+r y^{\prime}-q z^{\prime}=-r \\
-r x^{\prime}+y^{\prime}+p z^{\prime}=1 \\
q x^{\prime}-p y^{\prime}+z^{\prime}=p
\end{gathered}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{\prime \prime}+r y^{\prime \prime}-q z^{\prime \prime} & =q \\
-r x^{\prime \prime}+y^{\prime \prime}+p z^{\prime \prime} & =-p \\
q x^{\prime \prime}-p y^{\prime \prime}+z^{\prime \prime} & =1 .
\end{aligned}
$$

(1) When $f(x)$ is a convex function, $f(x) \leqslant f^{\prime}\left(x_{11}\right)\left(x-x_{0}\right)+f\left(x_{0}\right)$;
(2) When $f(x)$ is a concave function, $f(x) \leqslant f^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)\left(x-x_{11}\right)+f\left(x_{1}\right)$.

If $n$ is an integer such that $n \ge 2^k$ and $n < 2^{k+1}$ , where $k = 1000$ , compute the following: $$ n - \left( \lfloor \frac{n -2^0}{2^1} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{n -2^1}{2^2} \rfloor + ...+ \lfloor \frac{n -2^{k-1}}{2^k} \rfloor \right) $$

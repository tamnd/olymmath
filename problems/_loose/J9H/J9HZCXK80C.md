An example of division. Kolya wrote an example of multiplying two-digit numbers on the board. Then he erased all the digits and replaced them with letters: the same digits with the same letters, and different ones with different letters. The resulting equation was: $\overline{c d} \cdot \overline{a b}=\overline{e f f e}$. Prove that he made a mistake.

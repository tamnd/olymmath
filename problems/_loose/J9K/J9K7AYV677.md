Two circles touch each other at point $A$. One line passing through $A$ intersects these circles again at points $B$ and $C$, another - at points $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ ( $B$ and $B_{1}$ - on the same circle). Find the geometric locus of the points of intersection of the circles circumscribed around triangles $A B_{1} C$ and $A B C_{1}$.

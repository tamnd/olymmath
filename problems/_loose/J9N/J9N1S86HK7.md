$30$ segments of lengths $$ 1, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5}, \sqrt{7}, \sqrt{9}, \ldots , \sqrt{59} $$ have been drawn on a blackboard. In each step, two of the segments are deleted and a new segment of length equal to the hypotenuse of the right triangle with legs equal to the two deleted segments is drawn.
After $29$ steps only one segment remains. Find the possible values of its length.

Let $M$ be a point on the side $BC$ of a right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$. The midpoint of the segment $AM$ is denoted by $P$. Let $P_{B}$ and $P_{C}$ be the reflections of the point $P$ across the sides $AC$ and $AB$, and let $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ be the reflections of the points $B$ and $C$ across the point $P$. Suppose that the points $B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, P_{B}$, and $P_{C}$ are collinear. Prove that in this case, $M$ is the midpoint of the side $BC$.

Let $A, B, C$ be square matrices of order $n$ with real elements such that $A C = C(B + C)$ and $A B + C^2 = I_n$.

a) Show that $(A + \varepsilon C)(B - \varepsilon C) = I_n$, where $\varepsilon$ is a complex cubic root of unity.

b) Prove that $A B = B A$.

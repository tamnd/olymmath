Let $ABCD $ be cyclic quadrilateral with circumcircle $\omega $ and $M $ be any point on $\omega $.\\
Let $E $ and $F $ be the intersection of $AB,CD $ and $AD,BC $ respectively.\\
$ME $ intersects lines $AD,BC $ at $P,Q $ and similarly $MF$ intersects lines $AB,CD $ at $R,S $.\\
Let the lines $PS $ and $RQ $ meet at $X $.\\
Prove that as $M $ varies over $\omega $\\ $MX $ passes through fixed point.\\

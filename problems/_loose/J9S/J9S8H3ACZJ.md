At a dance evening, $n$ pairs of partners arrived, each pair consisting of a girl and a boy. The evening consists of at least $n$ dances, each of which involves all the attendees. Initially, the boys are seated around a round table. For the first dance, each girl invites one of the boys (not necessarily her partner). After the dance, the girl escorts the boy she danced with back to his seat at the table, and for the next dance, she invites the next boy counterclockwise.

For which $n$ can the boys be seated at the table and the pairs for the first dance be specified such that in each dance at least one girl dances with the partner she came with?

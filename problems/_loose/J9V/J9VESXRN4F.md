A natural number $n$ is called a "Golden Monkey Number" if it can be expressed as the sum of several positive integers, and the sum of the reciprocals of these positive integers is exactly 1 (for example, $2+4+8+8=22$, and $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{8}+\frac{1}{8}=1$, so 22 is a Golden Monkey Number).
(1) Prove that 11 and 28 are both Golden Monkey Numbers;
(2) Prove that if $n$ is a Golden Monkey Number, then $2n+2$ and $2n+9$ are also Golden Monkey Numbers;
(3) Determine whether 2016 is a Golden Monkey Number.

In $\triangle A B C$, the incenter is $I$. A line through $A$ parallel to $B C$ intersects $B I$ and $C I$ at $P$ and $Q$, respectively. $B I$ intersects $A C$ at $E$, and $C I$ intersects $A B$ at $F$. $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ are the circumcenters of $\triangle B F Q$ and $\triangle C E P$, respectively. Prove that: $\angle O_{1} A O_{2}=\angle B I C$.

Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle and let $D$ be an interior point of the side $B C$. A circle touches $B C$ in $D$ and separates the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at the inner points $M, N$ and $P, Q$. Prove that

$$
|B D|+|A M|+|A N|=|C D|+|A P|+|A Q|
$$

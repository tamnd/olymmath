Let $0<\alpha<\beta<\frac{\pi}{2}$. Determine which is greater: $\frac{\sin \alpha}{\alpha}$ or $\frac{\sin \beta}{\beta} \cdot(О .117, У .126$, P. 207.)

The Second Theorem of Guldin. If a body is formed by the rotation of a certain plane figure (plate) around a certain axis, and the plate lies in the same plane as the axis and entirely on one side of the axis, then the volume of this body is equal to the product of the area of the plate and the circumference of the circle described by the center of gravity of the plate.

Thus, if a plate with area $S$ rotates and the center of gravity of the plate is at a distance $z$ from the axis of rotation, then the volume $V$ of the resulting body of revolution is

$$
V=2 \pi z S
$$

It is assumed that the center of gravity makes a complete revolution around the axis.

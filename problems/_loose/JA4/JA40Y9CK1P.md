Let's draw a right-angled triangle and construct a semicircle outward over each side, treating each side as the diameter. Then, find the intersection point of the extension of the altitude from the opposite vertex with the semicircle. Prove that any two such intersection points are equidistant from the common endpoint of the semicircles passing through them.

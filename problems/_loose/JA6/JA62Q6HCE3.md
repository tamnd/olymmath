$$
\frac{A C_{1}}{C_{1} B}=\frac{B A_{1}}{A_{1} C}=\frac{C B_{1}}{B_{1} A}=2
$$

Find the area of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, if the area of triangle $ABC$ is 1.

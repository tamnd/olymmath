Let's say that the function $f(x)$ is periodic with period $a$ if $a \neq 0$ and $f(a+x)=f(x)$ for all real $x$. Prove that if $f$ and $g$ are periodic with periods $a$ and $b$ respectively, where $a$ is rational and $b$ is irrational, then $f+g$ can only be periodic if one of the functions $f$ or $g$ is constant.

Let the rectangle $A B C D$ with $A D=4 \text{ cm}, A B=4 \sqrt{3} \text{ cm}$ and $m(A \hat{B} D)=30^{\circ}$. We denote by $M$ and $N$, respectively by $P$ and $Q$, the feet of the perpendiculars from $A$ and $C$ to the diagonal $B D$, respectively from $B$ and $D$ to the diagonal $A C$.

a) Show that $MQNP$ is a rectangle.

b) Calculate the area of the rectangle $M Q N P$.

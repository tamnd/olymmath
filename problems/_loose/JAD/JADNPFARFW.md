For the isoperimetric ratio of a convex plane figure, we understand the number $t / p^{2}$, where $t$ is the measure of the area of the figure, and $p$ is the measure of its perimeter.

Prove that if $D$ is an interior point of the equilateral triangle $A B C$, and $E$ is an interior point of the triangle $A B D$, then the isoperimetric ratio of the triangle $A B D$ is greater than the isoperimetric ratio of the triangle $A B E$.

The real points of the form $19 a+85 b$, where $a, b \in \mathbf{N}_{0}$, are coloured red, while all the rest of the integral points of $\mathbf{R}$ are coloured green. Examine whether there is a point $A \in \mathbf{R}$ such that for every pair $(B, C) \in \mathbf{Z} \times \mathbf{Z}$ with $B, C$ symmetrical with respect to $A$, the colours of $B$ and $C$ are different.

Let $\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}$ be i.i.d. random variables with a continuous distribution function. Show that

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(\xi_{\max }=\xi_{1}\right)=n^{-1}
$$

where $\xi_{\max }=\max \left\{\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}\right\}$.

Also, establish that the random variables $\xi_{\max }$ and $I\left(\xi_{\max }=\xi_{1}\right)$ are independent.

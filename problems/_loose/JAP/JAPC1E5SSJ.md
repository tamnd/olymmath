We colored the set of integers with a hundred colors in such a way that all hundred colors were used, and the following condition is satisfied: for any two intervals $[a, b]$ and $[c, d]$ of the same length with integer endpoints, if the color of $a$ is the same as the color of $c$ and the color of $b$ is the same as the color of $d$, then the complete coloring of $[a, b]$ and $[c, d]$ is identical (i.e., for $0 \leqq x \leqq b-a, x$ integer, the color of $a+x$ is the same as the color of $c+x$). Prove that the colors of -1982 and 1982 are different.

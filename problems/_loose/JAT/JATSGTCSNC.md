Given two triangular pyramids $A B C D$ and $A^{\prime} B C D$ with a common base $B C D$, and the point $A^{\prime}$ lies inside the pyramid $A B C D$. Prove that the sum of the plane angles at vertex $A^{\prime}$ of the pyramid $A^{\prime} B C D$ is greater than the sum of the plane angles at vertex $A$ of the pyramid $A B C D$.

In Rt $\triangle ABC$, $AD$ is the altitude on the hypotenuse $BC$. Connecting the incenter of $\triangle ABD$ and the incenter of $\triangle ACD$, the line intersects side $AB$ at $K$ and side $AC$ at $L$. $KL$ intersects $AD$ at $E$. Prove: $\frac{1}{AB}+\frac{1}{AC}=\frac{1}{AE}$.

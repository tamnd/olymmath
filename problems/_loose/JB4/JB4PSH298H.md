In an acute triangle $ABC$, the median $[AM]$ from $A$ has the same length as the altitude $[BH]$ from $B$, and $\widehat{MAC} = \widehat{HBC}$. Show that $ABC$ is an equilateral triangle.

A triangle is called acute if all its angles are acute.

Natural number $N$ has exactly 12 positive divisors (including 1 and $N$), which are numbered in increasing order: $d_{1}<d_{2}<\cdots<d_{12}$. It is known that the positive divisor with index $d_{4}-1$ equals
$$
\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+d_{4}\right) \cdot d_{8} \text {. }
$$

Find the natural number $N$.
(23rd All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1989)

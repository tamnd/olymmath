Depending on the real parameter $k$, determine the number of solutions of the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}+k x y+y^{2}=z \\
& y^{2}+k y z+z^{2}=x \\
& z^{2}+k z x+x^{2}=y
\end{aligned}
$$

in the set of real numbers.

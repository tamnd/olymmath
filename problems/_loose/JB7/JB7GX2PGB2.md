We inscribe a square in a circle, then draw rays from the midpoints of two opposite sides through the midpoint of a third side. It must be proven that these two rays intersect the circle at the endpoints of a side of a regular 12-sided polygon (dodecagon) inscribed in the circle, whose vertices include the vertices of the square.

- We inscribe a cube in a sphere, then draw rays from the midpoints of two opposite faces through the midpoint of a third face. It must be proven that these two rays intersect the sphere at the endpoints of an edge of a regular dodecahedron (12-faced regular solid) inscribed in the sphere, whose vertices include the vertices of the cube.

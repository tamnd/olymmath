Let $\mathbb{R}_{+}$ denote the set of all positive real numbers. Determine all functions $f$ : $\mathbb{R}_{+} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{+}$ that for any positive numbers $x$, $y$ satisfy the equation

$$
x^{2}(f(x)+f(y))=(x+y) f(f(x) y) \text {. }
$$

(P. Kaňovský)

In the city of Nikitovka, there is two-way traffic. Over the course of two years, all roads in the city were under repair.

As a result, in the first year, one-way traffic was introduced on some roads. In the following year, two-way traffic was restored on these roads, while one-way traffic was introduced on the remaining roads. It is known that at any point during the repairs, it was possible to travel from any point in the city to any other point. Prove that in Nikitovka, one-way traffic can be introduced such that it is possible to travel from any point in the city to any other point.

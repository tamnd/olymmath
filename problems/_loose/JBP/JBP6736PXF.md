A circle passes through the vertices $P$ and $T$ of triangle $MPT$ and intersects its sides $MP$ and $MT$ at points $D$ and $E$, respectively, different from the vertices of the triangle. The ratio of the area of triangle $MDE$ to the area of triangle $MPT$ is $\frac{1}{4}$.

a) Find the ratio $DE: TP$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the areas of triangles $MDT$ and $EMP$ is $\frac{4}{9}$. Find the ratio $TE: PD$.

For any positive real numbers $x, y, z$, prove the inequality

$$
(x+y+z)\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}\right) \leqq m^{2}, \text { where } m=\min \left(\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}, \frac{y}{x}+\frac{z}{y}+\frac{x}{z}\right) .
$$

Let events $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots$ be independent and $\mathrm{P}\left(A_{n}\right)<1$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $\mathrm{P}\left(A_{n}\right.$ i.o. $)=1$, if and only if $\mathrm{P}\left(\cup A_{n}\right)=1$.

There is an unlimited number of tiles in the form of a polygon $M$. We will say that a parquet can be made from these tiles if they can cover a circle of arbitrarily large radius without any gaps or overlaps.

a) Prove that if $M$ is a convex $n$-gon, where $n \geqslant 7$, then it is impossible to make a parquet.

b) Provide an example of such a convex pentagon with pairwise non-parallel sides that can be used to make a parquet.

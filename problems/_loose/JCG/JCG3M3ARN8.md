Let $x, y$, and $z$ be three not necessarily real numbers that satisfy the following system of equations:
$x^3 -4 = (2y +1)^2$
$y^3 -4 = (2z +1)^2$
$z^3 -4 = (2x +1)^2$.
Find the greatest possible real value of $(x -1)(y -1)(z -1)$.

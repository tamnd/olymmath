In a parallelogram $A B C D$, a point $M$ is taken on the diagonal $(A C)$. From $M$, a perpendicular to $(A B)$ is drawn, intersecting $(A B)$ at a point $E$. Similarly, the perpendicular to $(A D)$ passing through $M$ intersects $(A D)$ at a point $F$. Prove that: $\frac{M E}{M F}=\frac{A D}{A B}$.

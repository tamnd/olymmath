At the base of a pyramid with volume $V$ lies a trapezoid with bases $m$ and $n$. A plane cuts off a pyramid with volume $U$ from it, and in the section, a trapezoid with bases $m_1$ and $n_1$ is obtained. Prove that $\frac{U}{V}=\frac{\left(m n_{1}+n_{1}\right) m n_{1} n_{1}}{\left(m+n_{2}\right) m n_{n}}$.

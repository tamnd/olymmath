A backpack contains 12 cards labeled $1,1,2,2, \cdots, 6,6$. A person takes one card at a time from it, without putting it back; if he draws a card with the same label as one he has already drawn, he discards both cards; if he has 3 single cards in his hand or all the cards in the backpack have been taken, the operation ends. Find the probability that all the cards in the backpack are taken.

In a triangle $A B C$, a line parallel to $A B$, whose position is otherwise not specified, intersects the side $A C$ at a point $A_{1}$ between $A$ and $C$, and it intersects the side $B C$ at $B_{1}$. Furthermore, let $P$ be a point on $A B$ between $A$ and $B$, whose position is also not otherwise specified.

The area of triangle $A B C$ is $F_{0}$, and the area of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C$ is $F_{1}$.

Determine the area $F$ of the quadrilateral $A_{1} P B_{1} C$ in terms of $F_{0}$ and $F_{1}$!

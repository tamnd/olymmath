Given $a, b, c, d \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, prove:
$$
\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}+\sqrt{b^{2}+c^{2}}+\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}+\sqrt{d^{2}+a^{2}} \geqslant \sqrt{2}(a+b+c+d) .
$$

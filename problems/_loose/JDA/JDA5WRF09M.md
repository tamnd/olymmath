Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $1<a_{1} \leqslant \frac{4}{3}, a_{n+1}=\frac{2 a_{n}^{2}+1}{a_{n}^{2}+2}$. The sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is $S_{n}$.
Prove: $n<S_{n}<n+1$.

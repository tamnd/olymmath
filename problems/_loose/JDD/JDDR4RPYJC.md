A regular hexagon in the plane is called sweet if its area is equal to 1. Is it possible to place 2000000 sweet hexagons in the plane such that the union of their interiors is a convex polygon of area at least 1900000 ?

Remark: A subset $S$ of the plane is called convex if for every pair of points in $S$, every point on the straight line segment that joins the pair of points also belongs to $S$. The hexagons may overlap.

(Josip Pupic, Borna Vukorepa)

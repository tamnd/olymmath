Due to various reasons, the TVs that are delivered to the repair shop for repair usually have all kinds of malfunctions. Therefore, the time (service time) required to eliminate the malfunctions is a random variable $T$. Find the mathematical expectation and variance of the service time of TVs, if the probability distribution function is as follows:

$$
F(t)= \begin{cases}0 & \text { for } t \leqslant 0 \\ 1-e^{-v t} & \text { for } t>0\end{cases}
$$

(exponential distribution of service time).

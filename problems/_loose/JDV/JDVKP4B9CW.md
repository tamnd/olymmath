Consider a triangle $ABC$ , and the line passing through the center $I$ of the circle inscribed in it intersects the segments $AB$ and A $C$ at points $P$ and $Q$ , respectively, and $AP = AQ$ . The circumcircles of triangles $BPI$ and $CQI$ intersect at a point $M$ different from $I$ . Let's say that lines $BI$ and $PM$ intersect at $D$ , and lines $CI$ and $MQ$ intersect at $E$ . It is known that lines $IM$ and $DE$ intersect at $R$ . Prove that $DR = RE$ .

The 27th question, given positive integers $\mathrm{n} \geq 2, \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{i}} \in \mathrm{R}^{+}(1 \leq \mathrm{i} \leq \mathrm{n})$, prove:
$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{a_{i}}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{a_{i}+n-2}\right) \geq \frac{n^{2}}{\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}+n-2} \text { . }
$$

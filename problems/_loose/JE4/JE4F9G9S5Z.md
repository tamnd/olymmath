An infinite checkered board is painted in a chessboard pattern, and a non-zero integer is written in each white cell. After that, for each black cell, the difference is calculated: the product of what is written in the horizontally adjacent cells, minus the product of what is written in the vertically adjacent cells. Can all such differences be equal to 1?

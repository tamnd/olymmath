The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{0}=0, a_{n}=\frac{1}{2}\left(3 a_{n-1}+\sqrt{5 a_{n-1}^{2}+4}\right)(n \geqslant 1)$. Prove: all terms of the sequence are natural numbers, and for any three consecutive terms $a_{n}, a_{n+1}, a_{n+2}$ in the sequence, the product of any two of them plus 1 is a perfect square of a positive integer.

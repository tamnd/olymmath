Consider a triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ and points $\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}$ on the sides $[\mathrm{BC}],[\mathrm{AC}]$, and $[\mathrm{AB}]$ respectively, such that $: \frac{B M}{B C}=\frac{C N}{C A}=\frac{A P}{A B}=\frac{1}{3}$. If E is the midpoint of [NP] and F is the midpoint of [BC], prove that $\mathrm{EF}$ is parallel to $\mathrm{AM}$ and $\mathrm{EF}=\frac{1}{2} \cdot A M$.

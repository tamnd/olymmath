Let $a_n, b_n$ be sequences of positive reals such that, $$ a_{n+1}= a_n + \frac{1}{2b_n} $$ $$ b_{n+1}= b_n + \frac{1}{2a_n} $$ for all $n\in\mathbb N$ .
Prove that, $\text{max}\left(a_{2018}, b_{2018}\right) >44$ .

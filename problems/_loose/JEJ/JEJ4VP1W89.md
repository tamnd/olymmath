Define $S(n)$ as the sum of the digits of $n$ in its decimal representation, for example, $S(2017)=2+0+1+7=10$. Prove: For any prime $p$, there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $S(n) \equiv n(\bmod p)$.

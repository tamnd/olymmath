On the given circle $\omega$, a fixed point $A$ is chosen. We select two arbitrary points $B$ and $C$ on the circle, and find the point $D$ where the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ intersects the circle $\omega$. Let $K$ be the point such that $D$ is the midpoint of segment $AK$. The line $KC$ intersects the circle a second time at point $P (P \neq C)$. Prove that the point $P$ does not depend on the choice of points $B$ and $C$.

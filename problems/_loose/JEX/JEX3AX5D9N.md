If the irreducible fraction $\frac{n}{m}$ satisfies
$$
m n \leqslant 2009\left(m, n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right),
$$

then $\frac{n}{m}$ is called a "Bull Fraction". Now, all Bull Fractions are arranged in increasing order to form a sequence $\frac{n_{1}}{m_{1}}, \frac{n_{2}}{m_{2}}, \frac{n_{3}}{m_{3}}, \cdots$, which is called the "Bull Sequence".

Prove: For any two consecutive terms $\frac{n_{k}}{m_{k}}$ and $\frac{n_{k+1}}{m_{k+1}}$ in the Bull Sequence, the following holds:
$$
m_{k} n_{k+1}-m_{k+1} n_{k}=1 \text {. }
$$
2009 is the Year of the Ox, and the ox symbolizes auspiciousness and prosperity. For example, the New York Stock Exchange uses the "Bull of the Prosperous Market" as a mascot.

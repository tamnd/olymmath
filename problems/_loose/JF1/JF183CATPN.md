On the plane, there are $n>1$ points. Two players take turns to connect an unconnected pair of points with a vector in one of two possible directions. If after a player's move, the sum of all drawn vectors is zero, the second player wins; if the next move is impossible and the sum has never been zero, the first player wins. Who wins with correct play?

We transfer one full pipette of the solution from I. to II., then add enough water to make it half full. After mixing, we transfer one full pipette of the solution to III., fill it halfway, mix it, then use the new mixture to fill I. halfway, and pour the remainder into II. Now, the dilution in I. and II. is the same. - What fraction of the container volume is the pipette volume?

Every square on a $n\times n$ chessboard is colored with red, blue, or green. Each red square has at least one green square adjacent to it, each green square has at least one blue square adjacent to it, and each blue square has at least one red square adjacent to it. Let $R$ be the number of red squares. Prove that $ \frac{n^2}{11} \le R \le \frac{2n^2}{3}$ .

Let $a, b, c$ and $d$ be the lengths of the sides of a cyclic quadrilateral (with $a$ and $c$ being opposite sides), and $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$ and $h_{d}$ be the distances from the center of the circumscribed circle to the respective sides. Prove that if the center of the circle is inside the quadrilateral, then $a h_{c} + c h_{a} = b h_{d} + d h_{b}$.

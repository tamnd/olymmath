Show that the function $\varphi(x)=\sin \frac{\pi x}{2}$ is a solution to the Fredholm integral equation

$$
\varphi(x)-\frac{\pi^{2}}{4} \int_{0}^{1} K(x, t) \varphi(t) d t=\frac{x}{2}
$$

where the kernel is given by

$$
K(x, t)= \begin{cases}\frac{x(2-t)}{2}, & 0 \leqslant x \leqslant t \\ \frac{t(2-x)}{2}, & t \leqslant x \leqslant 1\end{cases}
$$

(2005 Guangdong College Entrance Examination Question) Given a function $f(x)$ defined on $\mathbf{R}$ that satisfies: for any real number $x$, $f(2+x)=f(2-x)$, $f(7+x)=f(7-x)$, and in the interval $[0,7]$, only $f(1)=f(3)=0$.
(1) Determine the odd or even nature of $f(x)$;
(2) Find the number of roots of the equation $f(x)=0$ in the interval $[-2005,2005]$.

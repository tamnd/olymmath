(15 points) A glass is filled to the brim with saltwater. On the surface, there is a piece of fresh ice with a mass of $m=100$ g. What volume $\Delta V$ of water will spill out of the glass by the time the ice melts? Neglect surface tension. The density of fresh ice $\rho_{\mathrm{n}}=0.9 \text{ g/cm}^{3}$, the density of salt ice $\rho_{\mathrm{c}}=0.95 \text{ g/cm}^{3}$, the density of fresh water $\rho_{\text {fw }}=1 \text{ g/cm}^{3}$, the density of salt water $\rho_{\text {sw }}=1.03 \text{ g/cm}^{3}$. Neglect the change in total volume when mixing two liquids.

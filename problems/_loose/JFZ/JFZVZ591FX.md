Given a triangle $A B C$. A line intersects the lines $A B$, $B C$, and $C A$ at points $D, E$, and $F$ respectively. The lines $D C, A E$, and $B F$ form a triangle $K L M$. Prove that the circles constructed on $D C, A E$, and $B F$ as diameters intersect at two points $P$ and $N$ (assuming that these circles intersect pairwise), and that the line $P N$ passes through the center of the circumcircle of triangle $K L M$, as well as through the orthocenters of triangles $A B C, B D E, D A F$, and $C E F$.

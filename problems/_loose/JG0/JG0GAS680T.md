Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid with $A B / / C D$, and there are two circles $w_{1}$ and $w_{2}$ inside $A B C D$ such that: circle $w_{1}$ is tangent to three sides $D A$, $A B$, and $B C$, and circle $w_{2}$ is tangent to three sides $B C$, $C D$, and $D A$. Let $l_{1}$ be the other tangent line to circle $w_{2}$ passing through point $A$ different from line $A D$, and $l_{2}$ be the other tangent line to circle $w_{1}$ passing through point $C$ different from line $C B$. Prove: $l_{1} / / l_{2}$.

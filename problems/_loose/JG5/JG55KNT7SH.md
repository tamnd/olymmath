$$
\begin{aligned}
& a b+c d=38 \\
& a c+b d=34 \\
& a d+b c=43
\end{aligned}
$$

What is the value of $a+b+c+d$ ?
(A) 15
(B) 16
(C) 17
(D) 18
(E) 19

The continuations of the medians of a triangle intersect the circumscribed circle at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{A G}{G A_{1}}+\frac{B G}{G B_{1}}+\frac{C G}{G C_{1}}=3
$$

where $G$ is the centroid of triangle $A B C$.

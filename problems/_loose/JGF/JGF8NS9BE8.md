Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $AB \neq AC$ and let $O$ be the center of its circumcircle. The bisector of angle $\widehat{BAC}$ intersects $[BC]$ at $D$. Let $E$ be the symmetric point of $D$ with respect to the midpoint of $[BC]$. The perpendicular to $(BC)$ passing through $D$ intersects $[AO]$ at $X$ and the perpendicular to $(BC)$ passing through $E$ intersects $(AD)$ at $Y$. Show that the points $B, X, C$ and $Y$ are concyclic.

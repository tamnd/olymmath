Place 1 white piece in each of the 8 squares of the 1st row on an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, and place 1 black piece in each of the 8 squares of the 8th row. The game proceeds according to the following rules: White moves first, and the two players take turns moving their own pieces along the columns. In each move, one piece is moved forward or backward 1 or several squares along the column, neither removing pieces from the board nor placing a piece in a square occupied by an opponent's piece or jumping over an opponent's piece. The player who cannot make a move loses. Prove that Black has a winning strategy.

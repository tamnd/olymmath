The forty-fourth question: Find all positive integers $\mathrm{n}$ greater than 1, such that there exist $\mathrm{n}$ distinct and pairwise coprime positive integers $\mathrm{a}_{1}$, $a_{2}$, $\ldots$, $a_{n}$, satisfying that for any $m \in\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$, $\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}\right) \mid\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j} m\right)$.

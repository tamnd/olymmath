One, (50 points) Given in a scalene $\triangle ABC$, the lengths of the three sides $BC, CA, AB$ form an arithmetic sequence. $I, O$ are the incenter and circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $IO \perp BI$;
(2) If $BI$ intersects $AC$ at point $K$, and $D, E$ are the midpoints of sides $BC, AB$ respectively, then $I$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle DEK$.

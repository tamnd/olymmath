Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $\mathrm{H}$ be its orthocenter, $\Omega$ its circumcircle, and $O$ the center of $\Omega$. Let $D$ be the point on the ray $[B O)$, other than $B$, such that $\widehat{A D C}=\widehat{A B C}$. Let $E$ be the intersection point of the line parallel to (BO) passing through $\mathrm{H}$ with the arc $A C$ of $\Omega$ not containing the point $\mathrm{B}$. Show that $\mathrm{DE}=\mathrm{BH}$.

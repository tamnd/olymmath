Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ be fixed points, and $k_{1}$, $k_{2}, \ldots, k_{n}$ be given numbers. Then the geometric locus of points $M$ such that the sum $k_{1}\left|A_{1} M\right|^{2}+k_{2}\left|A_{2} M\right|^{2}+\ldots+k_{n}\left|A_{n} M\right|^{2}$ is constant, will be: a) a circle, a point, or an empty set, if $k_{1}+k_{2}+\ldots+k_{n} \neq 0$; b) a line, an empty set, or the entire plane, if $k_{1}+k_{2}+\ldots+k_{n}=0$.

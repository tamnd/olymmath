The forty-ninth question: For a convex polyhedron, color its edges in red and blue. For any vertex $\mathrm{A}$, define $\mathrm{S}(\mathrm{A})$ as the number of dihedral angles at $\mathrm{A}$ formed by edges of different colors. Prove that there exist vertices B and C such that $S(B)+S(C) \leq 4$.

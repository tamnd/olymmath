In a tennis game, each point won scores 1 point, and the first to score 4 points wins (at this point, the loser can have at most 2 points). If the score reaches $3: 3$, the game continues until one side wins by 2 points. Suppose two players, A and B, are playing, and the probability of A winning each point is $p\left(p \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\right)$, and each point is independent. Prove: The probability of A winning a game is no more than $2 p^{2}$.

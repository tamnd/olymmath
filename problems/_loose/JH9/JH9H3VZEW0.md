In-1 Prove that the number $(\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{7})^{2021}$ can be represented in the form

$$
n \sqrt{3}+m \sqrt{5}+k \sqrt{7}+l \sqrt{3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7}
$$

where $n, m, k, l$ are natural numbers, and at the same time $1-10^{-500}<\sqrt{35} \frac{l}{n}<1$.

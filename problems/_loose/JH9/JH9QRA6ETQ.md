Place the positive integers $1,2, \cdots, 10$ at the ten vertices of a regular pentagram, such that the sum of the four numbers on each straight line is equal. Question: Does such a numbering scheme exist? If it exists, please give the number of numbering schemes (schemes that can be superimposed by rotation or symmetry are considered the same scheme); if it does not exist, please explain the reason.

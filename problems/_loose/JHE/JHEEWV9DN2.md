Given points $D$ and $E$ are on the sides $AB$ and $AC$ of $\triangle ABC$ respectively, and $BE$ intersects $CD$ at point $F$. Let $S_{\text{quadrilateral } E D F D}=S_{1}$, $S_{\triangle B D F}=S_{2}$, $S_{\triangle B C F}=S_{3}$, $S_{\triangle C E F}=S_{4}$. Then the relationship between $S_{1} S_{3}$ and $S_{2} S_{4}$ is ( ).
(A) $S_{1} S_{3}<S_{2} S_{4}$
(B) $S_{1} S_{3}=S_{2} S_{4}$
(C) $S_{1} S_{3}>S_{2} S_{4}$
(D) Cannot be determined

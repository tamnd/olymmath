Let's write the integers from 1 to 6 on the six faces of a cube such that pairs of consecutive numbers - and the pair 6, 1 as well - end up on adjacent faces. How many different arrangements are possible? (We do not consider two arrangements different if one can be transformed into the other by a symmetry of the cube - rotation, reflection in a plane or about the center, nor do we consider different two arrangements if one is obtained from the other by replacing each number with the number that is one greater, and 6 with 1.)

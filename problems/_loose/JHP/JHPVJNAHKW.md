Let $ n$ be positive integer. For $ n = 1,\ 2,\ 3,\ \cdots n$, let denote $ S_k$ be the area of $ \triangle{AOB_k}$ such that $ \angle{AOB_k} = \frac {k}{2n}\pi ,\ OA = 1,\ OB_k = k$. Find the limit $ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac {1}{n^2}\sum_{k = 1}^n S_k$.

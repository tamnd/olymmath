(New York, 76). Let a continuous function

$$
f:[0 ; 1] \rightarrow[0 ; 1]
$$

be differentiable on the interval $(0 ; 1)$, and $f(0)=0$, $f(1)=1$. Prove that there exist numbers $a, b \in(0 ; 1)$ such that

$$
a \neq b \text { and } f^{\prime}(a) f^{\prime}(b)=1
$$

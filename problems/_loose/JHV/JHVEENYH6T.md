Let points $A$ and $B$ be the intersections of circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$. Let lines $a$ and $b$ through point $A$ be such that $a$ intersects $k_{1}$ at point $C$ and $k_{2}$ at point $D$, and $b$ intersects $k_{1}$ at point $E$ and $k_{2}$ at point $F$, with points $C, D, E, F$ lying in the same half-plane relative to line $A B$ and point $E$ lying inside the angle $\angle B A C$. Prove that $\triangle B E C \sim \triangle B F D$.

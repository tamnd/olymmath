Given that $A D$ and $B E$ are two altitudes of an acute $\triangle A B C$ intersecting at point $H$, a circle $\odot H$ is drawn with $H E$ as its radius. A tangent is drawn from point $C$ to $\odot H$, with the point of tangency being $P$; a circle $\odot B$ is drawn with $B E$ as its radius. A tangent is drawn from point $C$ to $\odot B$, with the point of tangency being $Q$. Prove that $D$, $P$, and $Q$ are collinear. ${ }^{[2]}$
(2015, Estonian National Team Selection Exam)

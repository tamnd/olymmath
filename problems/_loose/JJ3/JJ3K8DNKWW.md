Two players take turns writing natural numbers on a board, not exceeding $p$. The rules of the game prohibit writing divisors of already written numbers on the board. The player who cannot make the next move loses.

a) Determine which player has a winning strategy for $p=10$, and indicate it.

b) Determine which player has a winning strategy for $p=1000$.

Petar and Vasko each drew a quadrilateral without parallel sides. Each of them constructed a diagonal in their quadrilateral and measured the angles formed by this diagonal and the sides of the quadrilateral. Petar obtained angles $\alpha, \alpha, \beta$ and (in some order), and Vasko obtained the same angles, possibly in a different order. Prove that the angles between the diagonals in Petar's quadrilateral are the same as the angles between the diagonals in Vasko's quadrilateral.

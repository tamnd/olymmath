[5 points] Consider all possible tetrahedra $ABCD$ in which $AB=2$, $AC=CB=5$, and $AD=DB=6$. Each such tetrahedron is inscribed in a cylinder such that all vertices lie on its lateral surface, and the edge $CD$ is parallel to the axis of the cylinder. Choose the tetrahedron for which the radius of the cylinder is the smallest among those obtained. What values can the length of $CD$ take in such a tetrahedron?

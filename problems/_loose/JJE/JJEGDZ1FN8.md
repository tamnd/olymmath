For any non-empty numerical numbers $A$ and $B$ , denote $$ A + B = \{a + b | a \in A, b \in B\} $$ a) Determine the largest natural number not $p$ with the property:

*there exists* $A,B \subset N$ *such that* $card A = card B = p$ *and* $A+B = \{0, 1, 2,..., 2012\}$ b) Determine the smallest natural number $n$ with the property:

*there exists* $A,B \subset N$ *such that* $card A = card B $ *and* $A+B =\{0, 1, 2,..., 2012\}$

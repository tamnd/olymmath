(35 points) In country $B$, there are 4 southern cities and 5 northern cities. Transportation links connect each southern city with each northern city in both directions; there is no transportation between other pairs of cities. A one-way ticket from any city $A$ to any city $B$ costs $N$ rubles. A round-trip ticket for the route $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow A$ costs $1.6 N$ rubles. A traveler starts from a southern city, plans to visit all southern cities, and return to the starting point. Determine the minimum possible amount the traveler will have to spend on tickets.

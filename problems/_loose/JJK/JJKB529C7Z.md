For $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, n \geqslant 2$, prove the following identities:
(1) $\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}\left|\cos \frac{k \pi}{n}\right|=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}\left[1-(-1)^{n}\right]$;
(2) $\int_{k=1}^{n-1} \sin \frac{k \pi}{n}=n \cdot\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-1}$.

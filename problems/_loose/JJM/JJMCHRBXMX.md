a) Let $p$ be a prime number. Prove that no number can be represented in two different ways as $m+n \sqrt{p}$, where $m$ and $n$ are rational numbers.

b) Prove the analogous statement for representations of the form $m+n \sqrt{p}$, where $p$ is a product of distinct prime numbers.

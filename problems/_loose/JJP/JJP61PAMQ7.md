$ A B C D$ is a trapezium.

$A B$ is parallel to $C D$ and $|A B|>|C D|$.

The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $S$. The extension of $A D$ intersects the extension of $B C$ at point $T$.

Prove that the line through $S$ and $T$ passes through the midpoints of the sides $A B$ and $C D$.

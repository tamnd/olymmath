Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{gathered}
\frac{\sin x+\cos x}{\sin y+\cos y}+\frac{\sin y-\cos y}{\sin x+\cos x}=\frac{1}{\sin (x+y)+\cos (x-y)} \\
2(\sin x+\cos x)^{2}-\left(2 \cos ^{2} y+1\right)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

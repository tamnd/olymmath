We create a rectangular grid with steps of width $2c$. The grid openings are identical rectangles, with sides $a$ and $b$ (where $a > b$). We shoot a ball of radius $r$ perpendicularly to the plane of the grid. a) What is the probability that the ball will pass through the grid without touching it? b) With a constant grid opening, what step width should be chosen so that the above probability - for any ball - is always less than 1/2? Construct this step width.

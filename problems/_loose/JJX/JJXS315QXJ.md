We place 2004 bicolored chips in a circle: white on one side and black on the other. A move consists of selecting a black chip and flipping three chips: the selected one, the one to its right, and the one to its left. Suppose initially there is only one chip with the black side facing up. Is it possible, by repeating the described move, to achieve that all chips have the white side facing up? What if we had 2003 chips, among which exactly one has the black side facing up at the beginning?

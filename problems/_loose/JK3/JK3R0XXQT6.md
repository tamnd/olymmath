Let $a, b \in \mathbb{R}, a \geq-\frac{18}{15}, b \geq-\frac{7}{10}$ such that $3 a+2 b=17$ and the expression

$$
E(a, b)=3 \sqrt{15 a+8}+4 \sqrt{10 b+7}
$$

a) For $a=3$ prove that $E(a, b)<50$;

b) Determine the maximum of the expression $E(a, b)$ and the values of the numbers $a, b$ for which this maximum is achieved.

Local Olympiad, Botoșani 2014

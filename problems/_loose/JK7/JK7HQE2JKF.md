On a plane, there is a nearsighted Cockroach and the Truth. The Truth is stationary, and the Cockroach can move exactly 1 meter in any direction in one move. After each move, friends tell him whether he has gotten closer to the Truth or not. Initially, the Cockroach is at a distance of $2^{n}$ meters from the Truth. Prove that he can see the Truth, that is, get within a distance of no more than a meter from it, in no more than $2^{n}+100 n$ moves.

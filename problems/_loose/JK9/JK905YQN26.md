Let $ f:[0,\infty )\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ a bounded and periodic function with the property that
$$ |f(x)-f(y)|\le |\sin x-\sin y|, \forall x,y\in[0,\infty ) . $$
Show that the function $ [0,\infty ) \ni x\mapsto x+f(x) $ is monotone.

A transparent and flexible rectangular sheet $ABCD$ is made of a material, with $BC = 2AB$. The midpoints of sides $AD$ and $BC$ are $E$ and $F$. We have marked the segments $AF$ and $EC$ on the sheet. By bending the sheet into a cylindrical shape, we join the generators $AD$ and $BC$, thus $E$ and $F$ merge, and the marked line forms two turns of a helix. Prove that the projection of the line $AFEC$ onto a plane parallel to the axis of the cylinder is a sine curve. - Characterize those planes onto which, when projecting the line $AFEC$, the projection has a peak (not the image of an endpoint as the peak).

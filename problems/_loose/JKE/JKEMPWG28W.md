The internal bisector of angle $A$ in a triangle $A B C$ with $A C>A B$, meets the circumcircle $\Gamma$ of the triangle in $D$. Join $D$ to the centre $O$ of the circle $\Gamma$ and suppose $D O$ meets $A C$ in $E$, possibly when extended. Given that $B E$ is perpendicular to $A D$, show that $A O$ is parallel to $B D$.

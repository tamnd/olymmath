The vertex $S$ of the triangular pyramid $S A B C$ coincides with the vertex of a cone, and the points $A, B$ and $C$ lie on the circumference of its base. The dihedral angles at the edges $S A, S B$ and $S C$ are $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$. Find the angle between the plane $S B C$ and the plane tangent to the surface of the cone along the generatrix $S C$.

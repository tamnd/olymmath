Given the function
$$
f(x)=|x+1| \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x}}-a \text {. }
$$
(1) If $f(x)=0$ has exactly three roots, find the range of the real number $a$;
(2) Under the condition in (1), suppose the three roots of $f(x)=0$ are $x_{1} 、 x_{2} 、 x_{3}$, and $x_{1}<x_{2}<x_{3}$, prove: $x_{2}-x_{1}<a$.

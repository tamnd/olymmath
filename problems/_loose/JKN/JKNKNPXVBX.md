Prove that if on a plane there is a circle $S$ and its center $O$, then with the help of a single ruler one can:

a) from any point draw a line parallel to a given line, and drop a perpendicular to the given line

b) on a given line from a given point lay off a segment equal to a given segment;

c) construct a segment of length $a b / c$, where $a, b, c$ are the lengths of given segments;

d) construct the points of intersection of a given line $l$ with a circle, the center of which is a given point $A$, and the radius is equal to the length of a given segment

e) construct the points of intersection of two circles, the centers of which are given points, and the radii are given segments.

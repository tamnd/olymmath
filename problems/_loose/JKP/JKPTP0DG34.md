A person initially has 64 yuan, and bets 6 times, winning 3 times and losing 3 times, with the order of wins and losses being arbitrary. The probability of winning and losing is equal. If the betting amount is half of the remaining money each time, then the final result is
(A) Lost 27 yuan.
(B) Won 27 yuan.
(C) Lost 37 yuan.
(D) Neither won nor lost.
(E) The result depends on the order of wins and losses.
(14th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1963)

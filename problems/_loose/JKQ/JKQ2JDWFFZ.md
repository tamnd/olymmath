For a positive constant number $ a$, the function $ f(x)$ satisfies the following equation.

$ f(x)=xe^{-\frac{x}{a}}+\frac{1}{a+1}\int_0^a f(t) dt$

(1) Express the value of $ \int_0^a f(t)dt$ in terms of $ a.$

(2) Find the maximum and minimum value of $ f(x)$ for $ -1\leq x\leq 1.$

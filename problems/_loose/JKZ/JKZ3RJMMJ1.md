Given a line $g$ and two points $A$ and $B$ lying on different sides of $g$.

Construct all points $P$ on $g$ that have the property that the ray $P B$ bisects one of the angles formed by $g$ and the line $g_{1}$ through $A$ and $P$!

Describe and justify your construction! Investigate whether it is always uniquely executable!

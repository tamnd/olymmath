Find the smallest positive real $k$ such that for any positive integer $n\ge 2$ and positive reals $a_0,a_1,\ldots ,a_n$, $$\frac{1}{a_0+a_1} +\frac{1}{a_0+a_1+a_2} +\ldots +\frac{1}{a_0+a_1+\ldots +a_n} < k\left(\frac{1}{a_0}+\frac{1}{a_1}+\ldots +\frac{1}{a_n}\right).$$

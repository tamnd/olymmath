In the library, two boards were set up. On one board, a reader records the number of readers they encountered upon entering the reading room, and on the other - how many readers remained when they left the library. Early in the morning and late in the evening, there were no readers in the library. Prove that over the course of the day, the same numbers will appear on both boards (possibly in a different order).

Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid such that $(A B) / /(C D)$. Two circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ are located inside the trapezoid such that $\omega_{1}$ is tangent to $(D A),(A B),(B C)$ and $\omega_{2}$ is tangent to $(B C),(C D),(D A)$. Let $d_{1}$ be a line passing through $A$, other than $(A D)$, tangent to $\omega_{2}$. Let $d_{2}$ be a line passing through $C$, other than $(C B)$, tangent to $\omega_{1}$. Show that $d_{1} / / d_{2}$.

In an acute $\triangle ABC$, $AE$ and $AF$ are the trisectors of $\angle A$, and they intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Points $P$ and $R$ are taken on sides $AB$ and $AC$ such that $\angle PEA = \angle B$ and $\angle AER = \angle C$. Let $PR$ intersect $AE$ at point $L$, and $LN$ intersect $BC$ at point $D$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{MN} + \frac{1}{EF} = \frac{1}{ED}.
$$

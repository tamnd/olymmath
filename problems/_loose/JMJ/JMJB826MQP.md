The sixty-sixth problem: The positive sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=1,\left(n^{2}+1\right) a_{n-1}^{2}=(n-1)^{2} a_{n}^{2}(n \geq 2)$, prove:
$$
\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{2 a_{2}}+\frac{1}{3 a_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n a_{n}} \leq 1+\sqrt{1-\frac{n^{2}}{a_{n}^{2}}} \text {. }
$$

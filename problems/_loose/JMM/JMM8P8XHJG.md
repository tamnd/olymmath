Let $X$ be an $n$-element set, $n \geqslant 4, A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{100}$ be subsets of $X$, which can be identical, satisfying $|A|>,\frac{3}{4} n, i=1,2, \cdots, 100$. Prove that there exists $Y \subseteq X$, $|Y| \leqslant 4$ and $Y \cap A_{i} \neq \varnothing, i=1,2, \cdots, 100$.

Two lines $p$ and $q$ intersect at point $C$. Point $C$ divides line $p$ into two rays, which together with one of the same rays from line $q$ with initial point $C$ form two angles $p C q$ and $q C p$. On the bisector of angle $p C q$, a point $M$ is chosen, and on the bisector of angle $q C p$, a point $N$ is chosen such that $M N \| p$. Segment $M N$ intersects line $q$ at point $D$. Prove that $D$ is the midpoint of segment $M N$.

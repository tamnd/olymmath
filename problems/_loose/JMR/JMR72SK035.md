A set $M$ of rational numbers has the following properties:
1) $6 \in M$ and $12 \in M$;
2) if $x \in M$ and $y \in M$ then $\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{y}{3}\right) \in M$;
3) if $(2 x+3 y) \in M$, then $(x+y) \in M$.

Show that:

a) The set $M$ contains at least two consecutive natural numbers;

b) The set $M$ contains at least three prime numbers;

c) There exist $a, b, c, d \in M$, distinct in pairs, such that $a+b=c+d$.

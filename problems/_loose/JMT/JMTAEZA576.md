Let $a, b, c$ be the sides of a triangle, $S=$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a+b+c}{2} \text {, then } \\
2 S(\sqrt{s-a}+\sqrt{s-b}+\sqrt{s-c}) \leqslant 3 \\
{[\sqrt{b c(s-a)}+\sqrt{c a(s-b)}+\sqrt{a b(s-c)}] .}
\end{array}
$$

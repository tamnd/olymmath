Let $a$ and $b$, as well as the elements $x_{0}, x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}, \ldots$ of the sequence, be natural numbers and let

$$
A_{n}=\sum_{j=0}^{n} x_{j} a^{j}, B_{n}=\sum_{j=0}^{n} x_{j} b^{j}
$$

What is the limit of the ratio $A_{n} / B_{n}$ as $n$ approaches infinity?

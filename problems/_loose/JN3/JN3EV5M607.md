Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a circle with center $O$ and a point $A$ outside the circle. From point $A$, draw two rays: one that intersects the circle at points $B$ and $C$ (in that order) and the other at points $D$ and $E$ (also in that order). Show that

$$
\widehat{C A E}=\frac{\widehat{C O E}-\widehat{B O D}}{2}
$$

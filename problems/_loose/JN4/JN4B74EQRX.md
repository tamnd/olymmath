Given an $n \times n$ ($n$ is odd) chessboard where each unit square is colored in a checkerboard pattern, and the four corner unit squares are colored black, a $L$-shaped figure composed of 3 connected unit squares is called a "domino". For what value of $n$ can all black squares be covered by non-overlapping "dominoes"? If it can be covered, what is the minimum number of "dominoes" needed?

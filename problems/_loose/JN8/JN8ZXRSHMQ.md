On the circumference of the circle, there are 3000 chess pieces placed, numbered clockwise as $1,2,3, \cdots$, 2999,3000. First, remove the 3rd chess piece, then proceed clockwise, removing 1 chess piece every 2 chess pieces, $\cdots$, until the 1st chess piece is removed. Question: (1) How many chess pieces are left on the circumference? (2) Among the remaining chess pieces on the circumference, starting from the chess piece with the smallest number, what is the number of the 181st chess piece?

Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral, and let $E$ (resp. $F$) be the intersection of the lines $(A B)$ and $(C D)$ (resp. $(B C)$ and $(D A))$. Let $P$ be a point that is not on the line $(E F)$, the lines $(P A),(P B),(P C)$ and $(P D)$ intersect the line $(E F)$ at $X, Y, Z$ and $W$ respectively. Show that the lines $(A Z)$, $(B W)$, $(C X)$ and $(D Y)$ are concurrent.

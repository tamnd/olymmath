Let the real number sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfy:
$$
(2+\mathrm{i})^{n}=a_{n}+b_{n} \mathrm{i}(n \in \mathbf{N}) \text {. }
$$

Then the value of $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{a_{n} b_{n}}{7^{n}}$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{3}{8}$
(B) $\frac{7}{16}$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$
(D) $\frac{9}{16}$
(E) $\frac{4}{7}$

Let $ D $ be the region on the plane bounded by the half-line $ \{(x,y) : x \geq 0, y = 0\} $ and the parabola with the equation $ y = x^2 $, i.e., $ D = \{(x,y) : x > 0, 0 < y < x^2\} $. In the region $ D $, a billiard ball (treated as a material point) moves without friction, reflecting off the boundary of the region $ D $ according to the rule "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection". Prove that the ball will reflect off the boundary of the region $ D $ only a finite number of times.
Note. The ball reflects off the parabola in the same way it reflects off the tangent to the parabola at that point.

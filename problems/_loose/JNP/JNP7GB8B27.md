In the plane, there are 2017 lines, among which no three lines are concurrent. A snail starts from a point on one of the lines that is not an intersection point, and moves along the lines according to the following rules: the snail only turns at intersection points, and always alternates between left and right turns (the direction of the first turn can be chosen arbitrarily); if no intersection point is encountered, the snail continues in the same direction. Is there a line segment such that the snail can cross it from both directions in a single movement?

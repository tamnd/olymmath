A store sells $R$ items per day, and the store restocks periodically: every certain number of days, with a fixed quantity each time. It is known that the handling fee for each restocking is $b$, and the storage cost per item per day is $s$. How many days should the store restock to be most economical? What should the restocking quantity be (assuming the scale of commodity circulation is extremely large, and the cycle is long enough to ignore marginal effects, treating the number of days and restocking quantity as real numbers)?

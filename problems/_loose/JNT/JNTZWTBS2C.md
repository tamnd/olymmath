Place nine balls numbered $1,2, \cdots, 9$ randomly on nine equally spaced points on a circle, with one ball on each point. Let the sum of the absolute differences of the numbers of all adjacent balls on the circle be $S$. Find the probability of the arrangement that minimizes $S$. (Note: If one arrangement can be made to coincide with another by rotation or reflection, they are considered the same arrangement)

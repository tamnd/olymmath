Given that the vertex of the parabola $\Gamma$ is the origin $O$, and the focus is $F(0,1)$. Through any point $A$ on the line $y=-2$, two tangent lines to the parabola $\Gamma$ are drawn, with the points of tangency being $P, Q$. Prove:
(1) The line $P Q$ passes through a fixed point;
(2) $\angle P F Q=2 \angle P A Q$.

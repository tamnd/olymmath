Students Arnim, Bert, Conny, and Detlef live in different cities of the GDR, and each lives in exactly one of the cities Dresden, Magdeburg, Potsdam, Schwerin. Over this, Arnim makes the following four statements:

(1) I am neither from Potsdam nor from Dresden.

(2) Bert is either from Potsdam or from Schwerin.

(3) Conny is neither from Dresden nor from Magdeburg.

(4) Detlef is either from Potsdam or from Magdeburg.

Determine whether all of these statements by Arnim can be true at the same time! Justify your determination!

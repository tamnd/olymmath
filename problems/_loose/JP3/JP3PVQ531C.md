a) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2016} \in \mathbb{R}, a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{2016}=0$. Calculate

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(a_{1} \sqrt{n+a_{1}}+a_{2} \sqrt{n+a_{2}}+\ldots+a_{2016} \sqrt{n+a_{2016}}\right)
$$

b) Prove that there exist sequences $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 0}, \text{with} a_{n} \in\{-1,1\}$ for any $n \in \mathbb{N}$, such

$$
\text { that } \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(\sqrt{n+a_{1}}+\sqrt{n+a_{2}}+\ldots+\sqrt{n+a_{n}}-n \sqrt{n+a_{0}}\right)=\frac{1}{2} \text {. }
$$

The endpoints of a quarter-circle arc with center $O$ are $A$ and $B$. Draw a semicircle over $AB$ as the diameter in the half-plane that does not contain point $O$. Divide the diameter $AB$ into $n$ equal parts and draw perpendiculars to the diameter at the division points. These perpendiculars intersect the semicircle at $n-1$ points. Prove that the lines connecting these points to $O$ divide the crescent-shaped area between the quarter-circle and the semicircle into $n$ equal parts.

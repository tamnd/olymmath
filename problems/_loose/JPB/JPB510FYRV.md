A strictly increasing sequence $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ of positive integers is said to be *large* if, for every real number $L$ , there exists an integer $n$ such that $\frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{x_n} > L$ . Do there exist large sequences $\{a_i\}_{i=1}^\infty$ and $\{b_i\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ such that the sequence $\{a_i+b_i\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ is not large?

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

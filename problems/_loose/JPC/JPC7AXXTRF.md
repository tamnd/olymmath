Prove that if $a$ and $c (a > c)$ are the bases of a trapezoid, then

$$
\frac{e^{2}+f^{2}}{a^{2}-b^{2}}=\frac{a+c}{a-c}
$$

where $e$ and $d$ are the lengths of the diagonal and the lateral side emanating from one vertex of the larger base, and $f$ and $b$ are the lengths of the second diagonal and the second lateral side.

The ticket price for a concert is 50 yuan per ticket. Among the customers queuing to buy tickets, there are $m$ customers holding 50-yuan bills and $n$ customers holding 100-yuan bills. The ticket booth has no 50-yuan change prepared. How many ways are there to queue so that the ticket purchasing process can proceed smoothly without encountering a situation where change cannot be given? Assume each customer buys only one ticket, and $m \geqslant n$.

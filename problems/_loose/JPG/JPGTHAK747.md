Determine all convex polygons $P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{n}$, in which there exists a point $X$ such that

$$
P_{1} X^{2}+P_{2} X^{2}+\ldots+P_{n} X^{2}
$$

is equal to twice the area of $P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{n}$.

Inside the given regular $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$, determine the geometric locus of points $M$ for which

$$
\measuredangle M A_{1} A_{2}+\measuredangle M A_{2} A_{3}+\cdots+\measuredangle M A_{n-1} A_{n}+\measuredangle M A_{n} A_{1}=\frac{(n-2) \pi}{2}
$$

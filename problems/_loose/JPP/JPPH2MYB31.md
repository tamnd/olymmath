(1) Let $n$ be a prime number greater than 3. Find the value of $\left(1+2 \cos \frac{2 \pi}{n}\right)\left(1+2 \cos \frac{4 \pi}{n}\right) \cdots\left(1+2 \cos \frac{2 m \pi}{n}\right)$.

(2) Let $n$ be a natural number greater than 3. Find the value of $\left(1+2 \cos \frac{\pi}{n}\right)\left(1+2 \cos \frac{2 \pi}{n}\right)\left(1+2 \cos \frac{3 \pi}{n}\right) \cdots\left(1+2 \cos \frac{(n-1) \pi}{n}\right)$.

(1994 Shanghai Competition Problem)

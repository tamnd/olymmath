Find all integers $n \geq 3$, such that for any $n$ positive real numbers $a_{1} 、 a_{2} 、 \ldots 、 a_{n}$, if they satisfy $\operatorname{Max}\left\{a_{1} 、 a_{2} 、 \ldots 、 a_{n}\right\} \leq n \cdot \operatorname{Min}\left\{a_{1} 、 a_{2} 、 \ldots 、 a_{n}\right\}$, then among them, three numbers can be selected that can form the lengths of the three sides of an acute triangle.

Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $D, E$, and $F$ be the feet of its altitudes from vertices $A$, $B$, and $C$, respectively. Let $k_{B}$ and $k_{C}$ be the incircles of triangles $B D F$ and $C D E$, respectively. The circle $k_{B}$ touches the segment $\overline{D F}$ at point $M$, and the circle $k_{C}$ touches the segment $\overline{D E}$ at point $N$. The line $M N$ intersects the circle $k_{B}$ at points $M$ and $P$, and the circle $k_{C}$ at points $N$ and $Q$.

Prove that $|M P|=|N Q|$.

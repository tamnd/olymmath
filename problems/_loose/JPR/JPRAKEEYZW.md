On a $10 \times 10$ board, two players take turns placing crosses and noughts (each can place either a cross or a nought). The player who, after their move, has three crosses (or noughts) in a row-horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, with no gaps-wins. Can either player always ensure a win, and if so, who: the player who starts or their opponent?

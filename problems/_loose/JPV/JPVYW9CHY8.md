Let $A$ and $B$ be points on circle $\Gamma$ such that $AB=\sqrt{10}.$ Point $C$ is outside $\Gamma$ such that $\triangle ABC$ is equilateral. Let $D$ be a point on $\Gamma$ and suppose the line through $C$ and $D$ intersects $AB$ and $\Gamma$ again at points $E$ and $F \neq D.$ It is given that points $C, D, E, F$ are collinear in that order and that $CD=DE=EF.$ What is the area of $\Gamma?$ *Proposed by Kyle Lee*

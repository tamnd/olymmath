Given the function $f(x)=\frac{1}{4^{x}+2}(x \in \mathbf{R})$, points $P_{1}\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right), P_{2}\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right)$ are two points on the graph of the function $f(x)$, and the x-coordinate of the midpoint $P$ of the line segment $P_{1} P_{2}$ is $\frac{1}{2}$.
(1) If the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is $a_{n}=f\left(\frac{n}{m}\right)(m \in \mathbf{N}, n=1,2, \cdots, m)$, find the sum of the first $m$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, $S_{m}$;
(2) If the inequality $\frac{a^{m}}{S_{m}}<\frac{a^{m+1}}{S_{m+1}}$ always holds when $m \in \mathbf{N}^{\vee}$, find the range of the real number $a$.

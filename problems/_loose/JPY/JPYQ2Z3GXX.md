Let $ABC$ be a triangle with centroid $G$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Prove that if $A$, $G$, $M$, and $N$ are concyclic, then the line through $A$ perpendicular to line $AG$ and the line through $G$ perpendicular to line $BC$ intersect on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

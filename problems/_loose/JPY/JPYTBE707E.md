Polyhedron. All faces of a convex polyhedron are necessarily convex. Conversely, is a polyhedron necessarily convex if all its faces are convex polygons? In particular, do there exist two polyhedra (for example, two triacontahedra), bounded by the same number of pairwise congruent faces (faces of the same body do not necessarily have to be congruent with each other), one of which is convex and the other is not?

The 36 students of a 7th grade class participate in extracurricular sports, each in exactly one of the sections Track and Field, Table Tennis, Swimming, Judo, and Chess. It is further known about the participation of the students in these sections:

(1) More than half practice Track and Field.

(2) More belong to the Swimming section than to the Table Tennis section.

(3) The sum of the number of members of the Chess section and the Judo section is exactly one ninth of all students.

(4) The number of students in the Table Tennis section is twice the number of students in the Chess section.

(5) The number of members in the Chess section is greater than double but less than four times the number of members in the Judo section.

Determine the number of students in each of the mentioned sections!

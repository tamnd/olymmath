Let $P_{n}(x)=1+x+x^{2}+\cdots+x^{n}$. Prove that for all positive $x$ the inequality

$$
P_{20}(x) \cdot P_{21}\left(x^{2}\right) \leqslant P_{20}\left(x^{2}\right) \cdot P_{22}(x)
$$

holds.

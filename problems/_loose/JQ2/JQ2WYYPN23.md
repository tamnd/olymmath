Let the sides of $\triangle ABC$ be of lengths $a, b, c$, and let $f(\lambda)=\frac{a}{\lambda a+b+c}+\frac{b}{\lambda b+c+a}+\frac{c}{\lambda c+a+b}$. Prove: (1) When $-1<\lambda<1$, $1<f(\lambda) \leqslant \frac{3}{2}$; (2) When $\lambda>1$, $\frac{2}{\lambda+1}<f(\lambda) \leqslant \frac{3}{\lambda+2}$.

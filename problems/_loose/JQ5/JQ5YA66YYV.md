Given a regular tetrahedron with edge length 1, prove that
(1) there exist 4 points on the surface $S$ such that for any point on $S$, there is always one of the 4 points whose distance to it does not exceed $\frac{1}{2}$ (the distance between two points on $S$ refers to the distance along the surface);
(2) for any set of 3 points on $S$, it does not have a similar property.

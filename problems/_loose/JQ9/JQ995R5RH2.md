Consider the plane tessellated with equilateral triangles, and let $F_{0}$ be any one of them. A sequence of increasingly larger figures is constructed as follows: $F_{1}$ is the polygon obtained by adding to $F_{0}$ the border formed by all the triangles of the tessellation that touch $F_{0}$ (on a side or at a vertex), $F_{2}$ is the polygon obtained by adding to $F_{1}$ the border formed by the triangles that touch $F_{1}$, and similarly, the subsequent ones are constructed up to $F_{10}$. How many triangles of the tessellation is the last polygon composed of?
(A) 541
(B) 661
(C) 691
(D) 721
(E) 841.

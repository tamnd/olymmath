A Strange Agreement. At breakfast, Professor Rackbrain told his family about a conversation he had witnessed the previous evening in a train carriage.

One passenger said to another:

- Here is my wallet, Richard, give me the same amount of money as you find inside.

Richard counted the money in the wallet, added the same amount from his pocket, and remarked:

- And now, John, if you give me as much money as I have left, we will be even.

Doing as his friend asked, John found that he had 3 dollars and 50 cents left in his wallet. Richard, on the other hand, had 3 dollars. How much money did each of the friends originally have?

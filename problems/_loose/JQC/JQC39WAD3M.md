Two schools compete in a chess tournament, each sending 75 students: 75 matches are organized to have all students play one against one (one member of the first school against one of the second) under the supervision of an external referee. Each school numbers its students from 1 to 75, and the referee imposes the rule that two boys cannot face each other if the difference between their numbers is a multiple of 3. If $n$ is the number of possible pairings that satisfy this rule, how many zeros does $n$ end with?
(A) 6
(B) 12
(C) 15
(D) 18
(E) 21

The grid figure F has the following property: for any filling of the cells of the rectangle $m \times n$ with numbers, the sum of which is positive, the figure F can be placed in the rectangle in such a way that the sum of the numbers in the cells of the rectangle covered by the figure F is positive (the figure F can be rotated). Prove that the given rectangle can be covered by the figure F in several layers.

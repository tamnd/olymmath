The city has a plan view of a rectangle divided into cells: $n$ streets are parallel to each other, $m$ others intersect them at right angles. On the city streets - but not at the intersections - there are police officers. Each police officer reports the number of the car passing by, the direction of its movement, and the time it passed. What is the minimum number of police officers needed to be placed on the streets so that their reports can uniquely reconstruct the path of any car traveling on a closed route (the route does not pass through the same street segment twice)?

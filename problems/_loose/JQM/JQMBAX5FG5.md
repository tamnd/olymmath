Let $k \geqslant 6$ be an integer and $P$ a polynomial with integer coefficients such that there exist $k$ distinct integers
$x_{1}, \ldots, x_{k}$ such that for all $i \in\{1, \ldots, k\}, P\left(x_{i}\right) \in\{1, \ldots, k-1\}$. Show that $P\left(x_{1}\right)=\ldots=$ $P\left(x_{k}\right)$.

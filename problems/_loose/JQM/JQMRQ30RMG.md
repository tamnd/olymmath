Numbers are written on cards, each of which is either "+1" or "-1". It is allowed to point to three cards and ask: "What is the product of the numbers on these cards?" (the numbers themselves are not told to us). What is the smallest number of such questions that need to be asked to find out the product of the numbers on all the cards, if the number of cards is: a) 30; b) 31; c) 32? In each case, prove that it is impossible to manage with fewer questions.

d) Fifty numbers, each of which is either "+1" or "-1", are written around a circle. It is required to find the product of all these numbers. In one question, the product of three consecutive numbers can be found. What is the smallest number of questions that need to be asked?

In the convex pentagon $A B C D E$, the midpoints of the sides $A B, B C, C D, D E$ are $M, N, P$ and $Q$ respectively. The midpoints of segments $M P$ and $N Q$ are $R$ and $S$ respectively. Show that the segment $R S$ is parallel to the side $E A$ of the pentagon, and its length is one-fourth of the length of the side $E A$.

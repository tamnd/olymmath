In the cells of a $9 \times 9$ table, all natural numbers from 1 to 81 were placed. The products of the numbers in each row of the table were calculated, resulting in a set of nine numbers. Then, the products of the numbers in each column of the table were calculated, also resulting in a set of nine numbers. Could the obtained sets be the same?

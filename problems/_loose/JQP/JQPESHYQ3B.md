Let us have $n$ points in a plane, $n \geqq 3$, such that no three of them lie on a straight line. Consider the interior angles of all triangles $\mathrm{s}$ with vertices $\mathrm{v}$ at the given points, and let the size of the smallest such angle be $\varphi$. For a given $n$, find the largest possible $\varphi$.

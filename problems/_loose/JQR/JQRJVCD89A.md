In the Inner Sanctuary!

Our philosopher managed to choose the right door and found himself in the Inner Sanctuary. There, on two thrones studded with diamonds, sat the two greatest priests (there were no greater priests in the entire world!). Perhaps one of them knew the answer to the Question of Questions: "Why is there something rather than nothing?"

Needless to say, each of the two great priests was either a knight or a liar (whether the priests were human or monkeys was not important). Therefore, we cannot say in advance about each priest whether he is a knight or a liar and whether he knows the answer to the Question of Questions. Upon seeing the philosopher, the priests uttered the following incantations.

First Priest. I am a liar and I do not know why there is something rather than nothing.

Second Priest. I am a knight and I do not know why there is something rather than nothing.

Did anyone of the priests actually know why there is something rather than nothing?

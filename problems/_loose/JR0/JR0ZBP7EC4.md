In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, angles $\angle B$ and $\angle D$ are equal. It turns out that the points of intersection of the angle bisectors of adjacent angles $A B C D$ form a convex quadrilateral $E F G H$ ( $E$ lies on the bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B, F-\angle B$ and $\angle C$, and so on). Let $K$ be the point of intersection of the diagonals $E F G H$. The rays $A B$ and $D C$ intersect at point $P$, and the rays $B C$ and $A D$ intersect at point $Q$. Prove that $P$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $B K Q$.

Two circles touch each other internally at point $N$. Chords $B A$ and $B C$ of the outer circle touch the inner circle at points $K$ and $M$ respectively. Let $Q$ and $P$ be the midpoints of arcs $A B$ and $B C$, not containing point $N$. The circumcircles of triangles $B Q K$ and $B P M$ intersect at point $B_1$. Prove that $B P B_1 Q$ is a parallelogram.

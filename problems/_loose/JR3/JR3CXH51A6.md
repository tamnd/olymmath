In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, draw $CD \perp AB$ at point $D$, and let $E$ be any point on $CD$. Draw perpendiculars from $D$ to $AC$, $AE$, $BE$, and $BC$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $P$, $Q$, $R$, and $S$ respectively. Prove that $P$, $Q$, $R$, and $S$ are concyclic or collinear. ${ }^{[3]}$
(2010, German Mathematics Competition)

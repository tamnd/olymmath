In an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, altitudes $AA_1$ and $BB_1$ are drawn. On the smaller arc $AB$ of the circumcircle, a point $L$ is chosen such that $LC = CB$. It turns out that $\angle BLB_1 = 90^{\circ}$. Prove that the altitude $AA_1$ is bisected by the altitude $BB_1$.

On the bisector of angle $A$ of triangle $A B C$, a point $A_{1}$ is taken such that $A A_{1}=p-a=(b+c-a) / 2$, and through point $A_{1}$ a line $l_{a}$ is drawn perpendicular to the bisector. If similar lines $l_{b}$ and $l_{c}$ are drawn, then triangle $A B C$ will be divided into parts, among which there are four triangles. Prove that the area of one of these triangles is equal to the sum of the areas of the other three.

See also problems $3.38-3.41,13.52-13.56,16.5,24.5$.

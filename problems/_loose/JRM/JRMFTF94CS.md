Let $f:[0,1] \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ be a continuous and injective function with the property that $f(1)=1$. Prove that if there exists $x_{0} \in(0,1)$ such that $f\left(x_{0}\right) \geq 2$, then $\int_{0}^{1} \operatorname{arctg}(f(x)) d x>\frac{3}{4}$.

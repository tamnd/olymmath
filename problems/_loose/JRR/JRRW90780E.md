The 84th question: Given two sequences of positive integers $\left\{\mathrm{a}_{0}, \mathrm{a}_{1}, \ldots\right\}$ and $\left\{\mathrm{b}_{0}, \mathrm{~b}_{1}, \ldots\right\}$ satisfying: $\mathrm{a}_{0} 、 \mathrm{~b}_{0} \geq 2$, for all non-negative integers $n, a_{n+1}=\left(a_{n}, b_{n}\right)+1, b_{n+1}=\left[a_{n}, b_{n}\right]-1$, prove: there exist a non-negative integer $N$ and a positive integer $t$, such that for all integers $n \geq N$, we have $a_{n+t}=a_{n}$.

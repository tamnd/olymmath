It is known that among 100 balls, exactly 51 are radioactive. There is a device into which two balls can be placed, and if both are radioactive, a light will turn on (if at least one of the two balls is not radioactive, the light will not turn on). Can all the radioactive balls be found using the device no more than 145 times?

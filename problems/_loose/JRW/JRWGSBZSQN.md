Cards with numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 32$ are stacked in order. It is allowed to take any number of cards from the top and insert them between the remaining cards, without changing the order of the cards in each of the two parts, and otherwise arbitrarily.

a) Prove that the cards can be arranged in any order with 5 such operations.

b) Prove that the cards cannot be arranged in reverse order with 4 such operations.

Dana je družina linearnih funkcij: $f(x)=m x+r x-2 m+r+1$. Poišči vrednosti parametrov $m$ in $r$ tako, da bo graf izbrane linearne funkcije vzporeden premici $y=-x$ in bo potekal skozi točko $T(0,3)$.

The family of linear functions is given by: $f(x)=m x+r x-2 m+r+1$. Find the values of the parameters $m$ and $r$ such that the graph of the chosen linear function is parallel to the line $y=-x$ and passes through the point $T(0,3)$.

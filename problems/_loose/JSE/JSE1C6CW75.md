The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is defined by

$$
a_{1}=2, a_{2}=5 \text { and } a_{n+2}=\left(5-n^{3}\right) a_{n+1}+\left(5+n^{3}\right) a_{n} \text {, for } n \geq 1
$$

Do there exist natural numbers $m, n, t$ such that $a_{m} a_{n}=a_{t}$?

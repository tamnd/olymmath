Anja has tiles of shape $\square$, and Bojan has $\square$. They alternately place one tile on a rectangular board. If it is Bojan's turn and he cannot place a tile on the board, even though there is still an uncovered square on it, Anja wins; otherwise, Bojan wins. Prove:

(a) if they have a board of size $6 \times 9$, Bojan cannot win, regardless of who starts;

(b) if they have a board of size $8 \times 8$, Bojan can place the tiles in such a way that he will win regardless of how Anja plays, and regardless of who starts.

(Note: The tiles must lie entirely on the board, they must not overlap, and they must cover all the squares of the board.)

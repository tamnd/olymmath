A convex $n$-sided polygon can be placed inside a unit square. Prove that it is possible to find three vertices $A, B, C$ of this convex $n$-sided polygon such that $S_{\triangle A B C} \leqslant \frac{8}{n^{2}}$, where $S_{\triangle A B C}$ denotes the area of $\triangle A B C$.

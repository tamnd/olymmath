Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers with $a \neq 0,|a| \neq 1$. We are looking for all functions $f(x)$ such that for every real $x \neq 0$ the following relationship holds:

$$
a x \cdot f(x)+\frac{1}{a x} \cdot f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=b
$$

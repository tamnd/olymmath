Lottery. (From 7th grade, 3 points) It so happened that the Absent-Minded Scholar had only 20 rubles left, and he needed to buy a bus ticket to go home. The ticket costs 45 rubles. Near the bus stop, instant lottery tickets are sold for exactly 10 rubles each. With a probability of $ p = 0.1 $, a ticket contains a win of 30 rubles, and there are no other prizes. The Scholar decided to take a risk, as he had nothing to lose. Find the probability that the Absent-Minded Scholar will be able to win enough money to buy a bus ticket.

Around a round table, $n$ players are playing the following game. Each of them holds a piece of paper on which they need to write a sentence, and then pass their paper to the player who is $k_{1}$ places to the right. Each player then needs to draw something on the received paper and pass it to the player who is $k_{2}$ places to the right. The players then need to write a sentence on the received paper and pass the paper to the player who is $k_{3}$ places to the right, and so on. (In the $i$-th step, each player needs to write a sentence if $i$ is odd, or draw something if $i$ is even, and then pass the paper to the player who is $k_{i}$ places to the right.) One round of the game ends after $n$ steps, where in the last step the players do not pass the paper further (but only write or draw something). A round is considered valid if at the end of the game each player has written or drawn exactly once on each of the existing $n$ papers.

a) The players want to play one valid round of the game, in such a way that during that round each player passes the paper exactly once to each of the other players. For which values of $n$ is it possible to choose the $(n-1)$-tuple of parameters ( $k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{n-1}$ ) to satisfy this?

b) The players want to play two valid rounds of the game, in such a way that in total during these two rounds each player passes their sentence exactly once to each of the other players, and passes their drawing exactly once to each of the other players. For which values of $n$ is it possible to choose the $(n-1)$-tuples of parameters $\left(k_{1}, k_{2}, \ldots, k_{n-1}\right)$ for the first and second round (not necessarily the same for both rounds) to satisfy this?

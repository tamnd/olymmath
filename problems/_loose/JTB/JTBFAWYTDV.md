In quadrilateral $B E F D$, $B F$ intersects $D E$ at point $C$, points $U$ and $V$ are on $B E$ and $F D$ respectively, and $\frac{B U}{U E}=\frac{F V}{V D}$, $U V$ intersects $D E$ and $B F$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Prove:
(1) During the movement of points $U$ and $V$, the circumcircle of $\triangle C P Q$ passes through a fixed point;
(2) The circumcircle of $\triangle B U Q$ and the circumcircle of $\triangle P V D$ intersect at another point on $B D$.

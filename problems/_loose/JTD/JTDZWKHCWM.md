Let $f$ be a function from $\mathbb{N}^{*}$ to $\mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that if $n$ and $m$ are coprime, $f(n)$ and $f(m)$ are also coprime, and for all $n, n \leqslant f(n) \leqslant n+2023$. Show that if $p$ divides $f(n)$, then $p$ divides $n$.

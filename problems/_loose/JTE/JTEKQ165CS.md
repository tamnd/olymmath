Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be non-negative numbers whose sum is 1, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i a_{i}=b$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i^{2} a_{i}=c$. Show that if $c=b^{2}$, then $b$ is an integer.

a) Construct the triangle $ABC$ if $a+b, r$, and $\alpha$ are given!

Here, $a$ is the length of side $BC$, $b$ is the length of side $AC$, $r$ is the length of the circumradius, and $\alpha$ is the measure of angle $\angle CAB$.

b) Describe and discuss the construction!

Note: The construction description should be brief. For constructing triangles, it is sufficient to specify sides and angles from which the triangle can be constructed.

Two circles intersect at points $P$ and $Q$. Through point $P$, draw three arbitrary lines $A A^{\prime}$, $B B^{\prime}$, and $O C^{\prime}$, intersecting the two circles at $A$, $B$, $C$ and $A^{\prime}$, $B^{\prime}$, $C^{\prime}$ respectively. Let $A B \cap A^{\prime} B^{\prime}=M$, $A C \cap A^{\prime} C^{\prime}=N$, and $B C \cap B^{\prime} C^{\prime}=R$. Prove: $\angle M=\angle N=\angle R$.

a) $x, y, z \geqslant 0 ; x+y+z=1 / 2$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1-x}{1+x} \frac{1-y}{1+y} \frac{1-z}{1+z} \geqslant \frac{1}{3}
$$

b) $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n} \geqslant 0 ; x_{1}+\ldots+x_{n}=1 / 2$. Prove that $\frac{1-x_{1}}{1+x_{1}} \frac{1-x_{2}}{1+x_{2}} \times \ldots \times \frac{1-x_{n}}{1+x_{n}} \geqslant \frac{1}{3}$.

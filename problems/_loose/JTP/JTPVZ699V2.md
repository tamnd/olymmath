For a right triangle $A B C$ with the right angle at $C$, it is required that this right angle be divided into four equal angles by the median of side $A B$, the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$, and the altitude perpendicular to side $A B$.

Investigate whether there exists a triangle $A B C$ that meets these requirements, and whether all such triangles are similar to each other!

Determine, if this is the case, the measures of the angles $\angle B A C$ and $\angle A B C$!

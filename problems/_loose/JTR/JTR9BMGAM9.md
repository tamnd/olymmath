3 Exploratory Existence Problems
Exploratory existence propositions refer to knowing $M$, and investigating whether there exist or how many objects $N$ there are, such that they possess property $P$.

Example 7 In a cube box with an edge length of 1, do there exist three non-overlapping regular tetrahedra with edge lengths of 1?

Pairs of numbers are written on a board. Initially, the pair of numbers $(1,2)$ is written on the board. If the pair of numbers $(a, b)$ is written on the board, then the pair $(-a,-b)$ and the pair $(-b, a+b)$ can also be written on the board. Additionally, if the pairs of numbers $(a, b)$ and $(c, d)$ are written on the board, then the pair $(a+c, b+d)$ can be written on the board. Could the pair $(2022,2023)$ appear on the board after some time? The order of the numbers in the pair is significant, for example, the pairs of numbers $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ are considered different.

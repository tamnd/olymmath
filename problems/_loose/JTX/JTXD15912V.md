Let $P$ be an arbitrary point in the plane of triangle $ABC$, and reflect the lines $PA, PB, PC$ over the angle bisectors of $\angle BAC, \angle ABC, \angle ACB$, respectively. It is to be proven that these three lines intersect at a point $P'$.

Take $P$ as the circumcenter, centroid, and orthocenter of the triangle, and determine $P'$ in each case.

Let for real numbers $a, b, c \neq 0$ the equality
$\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{a+b+c}$ hold. Prove that for every odd number $n$ the equality $\frac{1}{a^{\prime \prime}}+\frac{1}{b^{\prime \prime}}+\frac{1}{c^{\prime \prime}}=\frac{1}{a^{n}+b^{\prime \prime}+c^{n}}$ also holds.

Two planes intersect the lateral edges of a regular $n$-sided prism at points $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$ and $B_{1}, \ldots, B_{n}$ respectively, and these planes do not have common points inside the prism. Let $M$ and $N$ be the centers of mass of the polygons $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} \ldots B_{n}$.

a) Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments $A_{1} B_{1}, \ldots, \ldots, A_{n} B_{n}$ is equal to $n M N$.

b) Prove that the volume of the part of the prism enclosed between these planes is equal to $s M N$, where $s$ is the area of the base of the prism.

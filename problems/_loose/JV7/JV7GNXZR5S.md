Let $\mathbb{Q}$ denote the set of rational numbers. Determine all functions $f:\mathbb{Q}\longrightarrow\mathbb{Q}$ such that, for all $x, y \in \mathbb{Q}$, $$f(x)f(y+1)=f(xf(y))+f(x)$$

The three sides of a triangle are positive integers $a, b, c$, and $a \leqslant b \leqslant c$. If it is known that $c=6$, then the number of triangles that can be formed with $a, b, c$ as sides and satisfying the above conditions is
(A) 9.
(B) 10.
(C) 11.
(D) 12.
(China Hubei Province Wuhan City Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

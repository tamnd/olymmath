In the right trapezoid $A B C D$, $\angle D A B=\angle C B A=90^{\circ}$, and $\triangle D B C$ is an equilateral triangle. Now, construct a regular $\triangle A B E$ outside the trapezoid with $A B$ as one side, and connect $C E$ to intersect $B D$ at $F$. Prove that $C E$ is bisected by $B D$ at $F$.

(Romania, 2000). Let $n$ and $k$ be fixed positive integers. Show that there exist integers $a_{1}>a_{2}>a_{3}>a_{4}>a_{5}>k$ such that

$$
n= \pm\binom{ a_{1}}{3} \pm\binom{ a_{2}}{3} \pm\binom{ a_{3}}{3} \pm\binom{ a_{4}}{3} \pm\binom{ a_{5}}{3}
$$

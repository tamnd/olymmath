19 If $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=\frac{1}{x+y+z}=1$. Then,
(A) at least one of $x, y, z$ is equal to 1.
(B) $x, y, z$ are all equal to 1.
(C) $x, y, z$ are all not equal to 1.
(D) none of the above conclusions is correct.
(8th "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

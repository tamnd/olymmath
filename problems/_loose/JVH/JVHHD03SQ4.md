Let $A_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ be finite non-empty sets, satisfying $\sum_{1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant n} \frac{\left|A_{i} \cap A_{j}\right|}{\left|A_{i}\right|\left|A_{j}\right|}<1$. Prove: There exist $a_{i} \in A_{i}$ $(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ such that when $i \neq j$, $a_{i} \neq a_{j}$.

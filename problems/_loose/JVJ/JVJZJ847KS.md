The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is defined as follows:
$$
a_{1}=1, a_{n+1}=d\left(a_{n}\right)+c (n=1,2, \cdots) \text {, }
$$

where $c$ is a fixed positive integer, and $d(m)$ denotes the number of positive divisors of $m$. Prove: There exists a positive integer $k$ such that the sequence $a_{k}, a_{k+1}, \cdots$ is periodic.
(2006, Polish Mathematical Olympiad)

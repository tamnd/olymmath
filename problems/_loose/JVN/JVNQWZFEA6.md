Let $S=\{1,2,3, \ldots, 20\}$ be the set of all positive integers from 1 to 20 . Suppose that $N$ is the smallest positive integer such that exactly eighteen numbers from $S$ are factors of $N$, and the only two numbers from $S$ that are not factors of $N$ are consecutive integers. Find the sum of the digits of $N$.

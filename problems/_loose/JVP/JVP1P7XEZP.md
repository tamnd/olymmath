Given a triangle $ABC$ with area $S$. Inside the triangle, whose vertices are the midpoints of the sides of triangle $ABC$, a point $U$ is arbitrarily chosen. Let $A', B', C'$ be the images of points $A, B, C$ respectively under reflection about the center $U$. Prove that the hexagon $A C' B A' C B'$ has an area of $2S$.

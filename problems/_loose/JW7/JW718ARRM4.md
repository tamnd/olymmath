For $n \ge 2,$ let $\omega(n)$ denote the number of distinct prime factors of $n.$ We set $\omega(1) = 0.$ Compute the absolute value of $$ \sum_{n=1}^{160} (-1)^{\omega(n)} \left\lfloor \frac{160}{n} \right\rfloor. $$

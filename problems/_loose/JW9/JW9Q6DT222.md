Given a positive integer $n(n \geqslant 5)$, let the rectangle $ABCD$ have side lengths $AB=2a, BC=2b(a>b)$. Points $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n}=C, Q_{1}, Q_{2}, \cdots, Q_{n}=D$, and $R_{0}=D, R_{1}, R_{2}, \cdots, R_{n}=C$ are such that: $BP_{1}=P_{1}P_{2}=\cdots=P_{n-1}P_{n}=\frac{2b}{n}$, $AQ_{1}=Q_{1}Q_{2}=\cdots=Q_{n-1}Q_{n}=\frac{2b}{n}$, and $R_{0}R_{1}=R_{1}R_{2}=\cdots=R_{n-1}R_{n}=\frac{2a}{n}$.
(1) For $i=1,2, \cdots, n$, find the equation of the conic section $F_{i}$ on which the intersection points of $AP_{i}$ and $BR_{n-i}$, and $AR_{i}$ and $BQ_{i}$ lie;
(2) If points $S_{1}, S_{2}, \cdots, S_{n}$ on the extension of $DC$ satisfy $CS_{1}=S_{1}S_{2}=\cdots=S_{n-1}S_{n}=\frac{2a}{n}$, for $i=1,2, \cdots, n$, find the equation of the conic section $F_{2}$ on which the intersection points of $AP_{i}$ and $BS_{i}$ lie;
(3) Let $M$ be the point on the conic section $F_{1}$ that is farthest from $\Lambda B$. Find the minimum distance between $M$ and the points on the conic section $F_{2}$.

Given a circle $k$ with center $O$ and a point $A$ inside it. The perpendicular to the line $OA$ at $A$ is $e$. The reflection of any point $B$ on the circle $k$ over $e$ is $B^{\prime}$, and the point where the ray $AB^{\prime}$ intersects the circle $k$ is $C$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ passes through the point $O$.

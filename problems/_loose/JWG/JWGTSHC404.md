Let $A H, B K$ and $C L$ be the altitudes of an arbitrary triangle $A B C$. Prove that

$$
\overline{A K} \cdot \overline{B L} \cdot \overline{C H}=\overline{A L} \cdot \overline{B H} \cdot \overline{C K}=\overline{H K} \cdot \overline{K L} \cdot \overline{L H}
$$

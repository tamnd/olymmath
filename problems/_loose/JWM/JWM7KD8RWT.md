On the sides of triangle $A B C$, points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken such that $A B_{1}: B_{1} C=c^{n}: a^{n}, B C_{1}: C_{1} A=a^{n}: b^{n}$, and $C A_{1}: A_{1} B=b^{n}: c^{n}$ (where $a, b$, and $c$ are the lengths of the sides of the triangle). The circumcircle of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ intersects the sides of triangle $A B C$ at segments of length $\pm x, \pm y$, and $\pm z$ (the signs are chosen according to the orientation of the triangle). Prove that $\frac{x}{a^{n-1}}+\frac{y}{b^{n-1}}+\frac{z}{c^{n-1}}=0$.

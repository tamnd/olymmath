Let $t$ be a fixed integer. Show that for every odd prime $p$, there exists a positive integer $n$ such that

$$
(3-7 t) 2^{n}+(18 t-9) 3^{n}+(6-10 t) 4^{n}
$$

is divisible by $p$.

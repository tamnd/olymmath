Let's introduce the following notations: $f$ - the number of faces of a polyhedron, $a$ - the number of its edges, $p$ - the number of its vertices. In the subsequent problems, we will be talking about convex polyhedra. Let $f_{k}$ be the number of $k$-sided faces; prove the equality: $2 a=3 f_{3}+4 f_{4}+5 f_{5}+\cdots$.

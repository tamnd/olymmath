The number $x$ of students in an 8th grade class is known to satisfy the following conditions:

(1) The number $x$ is a prime number.

(2) Exactly 9 students in this class can ice skate.

(3) Exactly 12 students in this class can ski.

(4) Exactly 4 students in this class can neither ice skate nor ski.

Determine whether the number of students $x$ can be uniquely determined from these conditions!

There are 12 positions arranged in a circle, and 4 adjacent positions are occupied by 4 pieces, whose colors are red, yellow, green, and blue, respectively. Each move allows any one of the pieces to be moved from its original position, over 4 positions to the 5th position, as long as this position is empty. Moves can be made in both directions. It is known that after several moves, the 4 pieces return to the original 4 adjacent positions. What could be the possible arrangement of the pieces at this time?

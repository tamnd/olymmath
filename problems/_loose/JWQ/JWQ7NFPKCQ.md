We have two gears. By connecting them, the ratio of the rotational speeds of the driving and driven gears (the transmission ratio) is $7: 9$ or $9: 7$. By replacing one of the gears with a gear that has 3 fewer teeth and the other with a gear that has 3 more teeth, the transmission ratio becomes $1: 3$ or $3: 1$. - Which gear was replaced with one that has fewer teeth? How many teeth does each of the 4 gears have?

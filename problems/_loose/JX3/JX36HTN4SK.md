(BPR, 82). For a given natural number $k>1$, $Q(n), n \in \mathbf{N}$, denotes the least common multiple of the numbers $n, n+1, \ldots, n+k$. Prove that there are infinitely many values of $n \in \mathbf{N}$, satisfying the inequality $Q(n)>Q(n+1)$.

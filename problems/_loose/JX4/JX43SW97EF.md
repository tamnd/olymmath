Inside triangle $ABC$, an arbitrary point $O$ is chosen. Prove that the following equality holds:

$$
S_{A} \cdot \overrightarrow{O A} + S_{B} \cdot \overrightarrow{O B} + S_{C} \cdot \overrightarrow{O C} = 0
$$

where $S_{A}, S_{B}, S_{C}$ are the areas of triangles $BCO$, $CAO$, and $ABO$ respectively.

Let the length of the median to side $c$ in a triangle with sides $a, b, c$ be denoted by $s_{c}$. It is to be proven that

$$
\frac{c^{2}-(a-b)^{2}}{2(a+b)} \leq a+b-2 s_{c}<\frac{c^{2}+(a-b)^{2}}{4 s_{c}}
$$

Let $n$ be a positive even number. Prove: In the $n \times n$ matrix
$$
A=\left(\begin{array}{ccccc}
1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n \\
2 & 3 & 4 & \cdots & 1 \\
3 & 4 & 5 & \cdots & 2 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
n & 1 & 2 & \cdots & n-1
\end{array}\right)
$$

you cannot find a set of $1,2, \cdots, n$, all in different rows and different columns.

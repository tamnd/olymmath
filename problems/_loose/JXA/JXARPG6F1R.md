Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle such that $AB < BC, CA$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of the segment $[AB]$, and let $P$ be a point inside the triangle $ABC$ such that $\widehat{CAP} = \widehat{CBP} = \widehat{ACB}$. We denote by $M$ and $N$ the orthogonal projections of the point $P$ onto the lines $(BC)$ and $(AC)$, respectively. Let $d_{1}$ be the line parallel to the line $(AC)$ and passing through the point $M$, and let $d_{2}$ be the line parallel to the line $(BC)$ and passing through the point $N$. Suppose that the lines $d_{1}$ and $d_{2}$ intersect at a point $K$. Show that $D$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $MNK$.

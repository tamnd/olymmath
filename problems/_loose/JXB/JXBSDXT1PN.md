If facilities for division are not available, it is sometimes convenient in determining the decimal expansion of $1/a$ , $a>0$ , to use the iteration $$ x_{k+1}=x_k(2-ax_k), k=0,1,2,\dots , $$ where $x_0$ is a selected "starting" value. Find the limitations, if any, on the starting values $x_0$ , in order that the above iteration converges to the desired value $1/a$ .

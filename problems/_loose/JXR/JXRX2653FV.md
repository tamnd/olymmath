Consider the rectangle $ABCD$. Draw two lines parallel to the diagonal $AC$ at an equal distance from it. Show that these two parallel lines intersect the sides of the rectangle at points that form an isosceles trapezoid and that the perimeter of this isosceles trapezoid is equal to the sum of the lengths of the two diagonals of the rectangle.

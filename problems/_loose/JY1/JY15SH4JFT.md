Given $P$ is a point inside $\triangle A B C$, lines $A P$, $B P$, and $C P$ intersect sides $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. $Q$ is a point on the extension of $B E$ such that $\angle E D Q = \angle F D B$. If $B E \perp A D$ and $D Q = 2 B D$, prove: $\angle F D E = 60^{\circ}$.

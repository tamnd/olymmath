Let $A, B$ be two points on the circle $\Gamma$ with center $O$, and let $D$ be the intersection point of the angle bisector of $\widehat{O A B}$ with $\Gamma$, and $E$ the second intersection of $(A B)$ and the circumcircle of $O B D$.

Show that the triangle $O A E$ is isosceles at $A$.

A regular 100-gon is placed on a table, with the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 100$ written at its vertices. Then these numbers are rewritten in the order of their distance from the front edge of the table. If two vertices are at the same distance from the edge, the left number is written first, followed by the right one. All possible sets of numbers corresponding to different positions of the 100-gon are written out. Calculate the sum of the numbers that stand in the 13th position from the left in these sets.

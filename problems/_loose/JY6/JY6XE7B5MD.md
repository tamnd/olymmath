We will call *smalls* the positive integers not larger than $2025$.

a) Find the number of smalls which are perfect squares.

b) Find the number of smalls which are perfect squares and leave remainder $0$ when divided by $45$.

c) Find the number of smalls which neither are perfect squares, nor leave remainder $0$ when divided by $45$.

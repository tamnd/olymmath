During a mathematical afternoon game, all players were asked by the game leader to take an even number of sticks in one hand and an odd number of sticks in the other hand.

Subsequently, each player was given the task to multiply the number of sticks in their right hand by 2 and add the resulting product to the number of sticks in their hand.

Each time a player informed the game leader of the sum thus formed, the leader was able to correctly state whether the player had an even number of sticks in their right or left hand.

How was this possible?

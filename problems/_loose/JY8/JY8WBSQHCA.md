Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ be three non-zero and distinct complex numbers, and satisfy the relations $z_{1} z_{2}=z_{3}^{2}$, $z_{2} z_{3}=z_{1}^{2}$.
(1) Write the complex trigonometric form of $\alpha$ that satisfies $z_{2}=z_{1} \alpha$;
(2) Prove: The three points $A, B, C$ corresponding to $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ on the complex plane form the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

In triangle $ABC$, $AC = 2AB$, and the midpoint of side $AC$ is $F$. The internal angle bisector from vertex $A$ intersects side $BC$ at point $D$.

a) Prove that the quadrilateral $ABDF$ is a tangential quadrilateral.

b) Let the radius of the circle inscribed in quadrilateral $ABDF$ be $r_{1}$, and the radius of the circle inscribed in triangle $FDC$ be $r_{2}$. Prove that

$$
1<\frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}<2
$$

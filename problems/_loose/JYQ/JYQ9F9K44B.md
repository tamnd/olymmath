First prove the identity

$$
(x+y+z)^{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left((x-y)^{2}+(y-z)^{2}+(z-x)^{2}\right)+3(x y+y z+z x)
$$

and then:

a) If the sum $x+y+z$ has a constant value $s$, determine under what conditions the expression $x y+y z+z x$ has its maximum possible value.

b) Using the obtained result, solve the following problem: It is known that the value of a diamond is proportional to the square of its mass. If a diamond is divided into three parts with masses $x, y, z$, prove that the total value is always less than the value of the whole diamond and that it is the smallest when the diamond is divided into three equal parts.

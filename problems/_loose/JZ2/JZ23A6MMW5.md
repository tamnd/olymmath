Given the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2}(r$ is an odd number), it intersects the $x$-axis at $A(r, 0), B(-r, 0)$, and the $y$-axis at $C(0,-r), D(0, r)$. $P(u, v)$ is a point on the circumference of the circle, where $u=p^{m}, v=q^{n}$ ($p, q$ are prime numbers, $m, n$ are natural numbers) and $u>v$. The projections of point $P$ on the $x$-axis and $y$-axis are $M, N$ respectively.
Prove: $|A M|, |B M|, |C N|, |D N|$ are $1, 9, 8, 2$ respectively.

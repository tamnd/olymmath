A game works as follows: the player pays $2$ tokens to enter the game. Then, a fair coin is flipped. If the coin lands on heads, they receive $3$ tokens; if the coin lands on tails, they receive nothing. A player starts with $2$ tokens and keeps playing this game until they do not have enough tokens to play again. What is the expected value of the number of tokens they have left at the end?

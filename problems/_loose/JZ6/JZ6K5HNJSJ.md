The point $O$ is on the edge $A B$ of the tetrahedron $A B C D$. The circumscribed sphere of the tetrahedron $A O C D$ intersects the edges $B C$ and $B D$ in the points $M$ and $N$ $(M \neq C, N \neq D)$ respectively. The circumscribed sphere of the tetrahedron $B O C D$ intersects the edges $A C$ and $A D$ in the points $P$ and $Q(P \neq C, Q \neq D)$ respectively. Prove that the triangles $O M N$ and $O Q P$ are similar.

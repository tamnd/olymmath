All natural numbers, in decimal notation of which there are no more than $n$ digits, are divided into two groups. The first group includes all numbers with an odd sum of digits, and the second group includes all numbers with an even sum of digits. Prove that if $1 \leqslant k<n$, then the sum of the $k$-th powers of all numbers in the first group is equal to the sum of the $k$-th powers of all numbers in the second group.

In $\triangle A B C$, $A B+A C=10$, and $\angle A=30^{\circ}$, then the area of this triangle
(A) has both a maximum and a minimum value.
(B) has a minimum value, but no maximum value.
(C) has a maximum value, but no minimum value.
(D) has neither a maximum nor a minimum value.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

What will happen if we try to prove an incorrect statement by contradiction?

If we attempt to prove an incorrect statement by contradiction, we will eventually arrive at a logical inconsistency or a contradiction. This method, known as proof by contradiction, assumes the opposite of what we want to prove and shows that this assumption leads to an absurd or impossible situation, thereby proving the original statement. However, if the statement itself is incorrect, the process will reveal the inconsistency, confirming that the statement is indeed false.

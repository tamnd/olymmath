The Sultan gathered 300 court wise men and proposed a test. There are 25 different colors of hats, known in advance to the wise men. The Sultan informed them that one of these hats would be placed on each of the wise men, and if the number of hats of each color is written down, all the numbers will be different. Each wise man will see the hats of the others, but not his own. Then all the wise men will simultaneously announce the supposed color of their own hat. Can the wise men agree in advance to act in such a way that at least 150 of them will definitely name the correct color?

$P T_{1}$ and $P T_{2}$ are secants of $\odot O$, intersecting $\odot O$ at $S_{1}, S_{2}$, and $P T_{1}=P T_{2}$. A line through $P$ intersects $\odot O$ at $Q, R$ ($Q$ is between $R$ and $P$), and intersects $T_{1} T_{2}, S_{1} S_{2}$ at $T, S$. Prove: $\frac{1}{P Q}+\frac{1}{P R}=\frac{1}{P S}+\frac{1}{P T}$.

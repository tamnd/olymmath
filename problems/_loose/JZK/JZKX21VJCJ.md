The 80th question: Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a positive integer, and the set of points $\mathrm{S}$ on the plane satisfies the following properties:
(1) There do not exist $\mathrm{n}$ lines on this plane such that each point in $\mathrm{S}$ lies on at least one of these $\mathrm{n}$ lines;
(2) For each point $X \in S$, there exist $n$ lines on this plane such that each point in $S \backslash\{X\}$ lies on at least one of these $\mathrm{n}$ lines.
Find the maximum number of points in $S$.

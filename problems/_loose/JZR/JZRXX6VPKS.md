It is known that liars always lie, truth-tellers always tell the truth, and tricksters can either lie or tell the truth. You can ask questions that can be answered with "yes" or "no" (for example: "Is it true that this person is a trickster?").

a) In front of you are three people - a liar, a truth-teller, and a trickster, who all know who they are. How can you find out who is who?

b) In front of you are four people - a liar, a truth-teller, and two tricksters (all four know who they are). Prove that the tricksters can agree to answer in such a way that, by asking these four people, you cannot definitively determine who any of them are.

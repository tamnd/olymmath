Construct a trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB \| DC$ and the following properties (1), (2), (3) from $b=6$ $\mathrm{cm}$. Here, $b$ is the length of side $BC$. The required properties are:

(1) It holds that $\overline{AD} = \overline{BC}$.

(2) It holds that $\overline{AB} : \overline{DC} = 2 : 1$.

(3) The circles with diameters $AD$ and $BC$ touch each other.

Describe and justify your construction! Investigate whether the given length $b$ uniquely determines a trapezoid with the specified properties up to congruence!

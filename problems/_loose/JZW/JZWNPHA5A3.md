Two counterfeit coins of equal weight are mixed with 8 identical genuine coins. The weight of each of the counterfeit coins is different from the weight of each of the genuine coins. A pair of coins is selected at random without replacement from the 10 coins. A second pair is selected at random without replacement from the remaining 8 coins. The combined weight of the first pair is equal to the combined weight of the second pair. What is the probability that all 4 selected coins are genuine? $ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac{7}{11} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac{9}{13} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac{11}{15} \textbf{(D)}\ \frac{15}{19} \textbf{(E)}\ \frac{15}{16} $

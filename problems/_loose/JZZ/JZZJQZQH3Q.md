Let $A, B$ be adjacent vertices of a regular $n$-gon in the plane and let $O$ be its center. Now let the triangle $A B O$ glide around the polygon in such a way that the points $A$ and $B$ move along the whole circumference of the polygon. Describe the figure traced by the vertex $O$.

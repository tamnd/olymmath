(Tournament of the Towns 1987). We choose a square on a standard $8 \times 8$ chessboard, whose squares are alternately black and white. We denote by $a$ (resp. $b$) the sum of the squares of the distances from the center of the chosen square to the centers of the black (resp. white) squares.

Prove that $a=b$.

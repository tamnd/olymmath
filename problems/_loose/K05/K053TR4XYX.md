Lemma B.6 Let $S$ be a surface, $\mathcal{C}$ a finite set of disjoint circles in $S$. Suppose $S \backslash \cup \mathcal{C}$ has a component $D_{0}$, whose closure in $S$ intersects every circle in $\mathcal{C}$, and no circle in $\mathcal{C}$ bounds a disk in $S$ disjoint from $D_{0}$, then $\varepsilon(S) \geqslant|\mathcal{C}|$.

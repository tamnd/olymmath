Arash is given a paper isosceles right triangle. A folding of this paper is called good if the polygon obtained after this fold has all angles less than $180^\circ$ . Arash performs a good fold. Babak takes the paper and performs two good folds, so that the paper is folded exactly three times at the end. Arash wants the final polygon to have the most possible number of sides but Babak wants the opposite. Assuming they do their best how many sides does the final polygon have?

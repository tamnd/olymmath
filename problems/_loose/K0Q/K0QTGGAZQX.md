Circle $\omega$ with radius 8 and center $O$ is inscribed in an acute-angled triangle $E N T$ and touches its sides $E N$ and $N T$ at points $C$ and $A$ respectively. Circle $\Omega$ with radius $\frac{\sqrt{145}}{2}$ and center $B$ is circumscribed around triangle $A C N$.

a) Find $O N$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the area of triangle $B T E$ to the area of triangle $E N T$ is $\frac{7}{10}$. Find the length of the angle bisector $N L$ of triangle $E N T$, as well as its area.

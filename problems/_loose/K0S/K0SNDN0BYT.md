Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, and $M$ and $N$ be the points of tangency of the inscribed circle with sides $AB$ and $BC$ respectively. A line $\ell$ is drawn through point $I$ parallel to side $AC$, and perpendiculars $AP$ and $CQ$ are dropped from $A$ and $C$ to $\ell$. Prove that points $M, N, P$, and $Q$ lie on the same circle.

(R. Zhinodarov, O. Dmitriev)

Let $a, b$ be positive real numbers satisfying $a+b=1$. Show that if $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{5}$ are positive real numbers such that $x_{1} x_{2} \ldots x_{5}=1$, then
$$
\left(a x_{1}+b\right)\left(a x_{2}+b\right) \cdots\left(a x_{5}+b\right) \geq 1 .
$$

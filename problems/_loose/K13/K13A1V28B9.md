Let $a, b, c > 1$, and satisfy $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} > 1$. Define the set $S(x) = \left\{[k x] \mid k \in \mathbb{N}^{+}\right\}$. Prove: Among $S(a)$, $S(b)$, and $S(c)$, there must be two whose intersection is an infinite set.

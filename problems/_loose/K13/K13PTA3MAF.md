On a $6 \times 6$ board, six non-attacking rooks were placed. Then, each unoccupied square was painted according to the following rule: if the rooks threatening this square are at the same distance from it, the square is painted red; if they are at different distances, the square is painted blue. Could all unoccupied squares be

a) red

b) blue?

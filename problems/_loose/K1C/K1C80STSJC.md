Let $a,\ b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a b+b c+c a=1$ . Show that $$ \frac{a^3}{a^2+3 b^2+3 a b+2 b c}+\frac{b^3}{b^2+3 c^2+3 b c+2 c a}+\frac{c^3}{c^2+3 a^2+3 c a+2 a b}>\frac{1}{6\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)^2} . $$

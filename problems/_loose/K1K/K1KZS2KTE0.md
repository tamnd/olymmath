$A B N M$ is a rectangular quadrilateral. Given in it are $A B, A M$, and $A C$ (where $C$ is a point on the line $M N$) and the angle $A B A^{\prime}=x$. $A^{\prime} B=A B$ and the point $A^{\prime}$ falls inside the quadrilateral $A B N M$. $A B$ is a horizontal line that can be rotated around point $B$, maintaining its reflection on the horizontal line $M N$ along the line $A C$. If the line $A B$ slides down to come to the position $A B$, thus forming an angle $x$ with its horizontal position, the line $A C$ comes to the position $A^{\prime} D$, moving its lower point from $C$ to $D$. How large is $C D ?$

Let $L(n)$ denote the least common multiple of $\{1,2 \ldots, n\}$.
(i) Prove that there exists a positive integer $k$ such that
$$
L(k)=L(k+1)=\cdots=L(k+2000) .
$$
(ii) Find all $m$ such that $L(m+i) \neq L(m+i+1)$ for all $i=0,1,2$.

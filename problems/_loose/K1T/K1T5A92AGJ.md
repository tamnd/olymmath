Anna and Balázs play the following game: They lay out 9 number cards on the table, and take turns placing one card at a time on a $3 \times 3$ board. (Only one card can be placed on each square.) If the sum of the numbers in the first and third rows is greater than the sum of the numbers in the first and third columns, Anna wins; if the two sums are equal, it's a draw; otherwise, Balázs wins. Prove that if Anna starts, she can always play in such a way that she does not lose, regardless of the numbers on the cards.

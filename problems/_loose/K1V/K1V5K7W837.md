Let $0!!=1!!=1$ and $n!!=n\cdot (n-2)!!$ for all integers $n\geq 2$ . Find all positive integers $n$ such that
$$\dfrac{(2^n+1)!!-1}{2^{n+1}}$$
is an integer.

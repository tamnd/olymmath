A certain number of black and an equal number of white checkers are arranged in a circle. Let $A$ be the number of those triples of consecutive checkers in which the white color predominates, and $B$ be the number of triples of consecutive checkers in which the black color predominates. Prove that $A \leqslant 3 B$.

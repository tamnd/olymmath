23.2.17 ** A city has $n$ bus routes, satisfying the following conditions:
（1）Any station is served by at most 3 routes;
（2）Any route has at least two stations;
(3) For any two specified routes, there exists a third route such that a passenger can transfer from one of the specified routes to the other via the third route.
Prove: The number of stations is at least $\frac{5}{6}(n-5)$.

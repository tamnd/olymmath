A hundred elves (female scouts) are sitting in a large circle around the campfire. Each elf has one or more chestnuts, and no two elves have the same number of chestnuts. Each elf divides her number by the number of her right neighbor and writes the remainder on a green note. Each elf also divides her number by the number of her left neighbor and writes that remainder on a red note. For example, if Anja has 23 chestnuts and her right neighbor Bregje has 5, then Anja writes 3 on her green note and Bregje writes 5 on her red note.

If the number of different remainders on the 100 green notes is equal to 2, what is the smallest possible number of different remainders on the 100 red notes?

Let $p$ be a prime number, $p \neq 2,5$. Prove that there exists a number of the form $1 \ldots 1$ that is divisible by $p$.

Come up with two solutions to the problem: one using Fermat's theorem (problem $\underline{60736}$), and the second - the Pigeonhole Principle.

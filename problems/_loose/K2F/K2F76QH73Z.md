Color the outside of each side of a convex polygon, then draw several diagonals of the polygon, and color one side of these diagonals, i.e., draw a colored narrow strip along these line segments. Try to prove that among the polygons formed by these diagonals from the original polygon, at least one polygon has all its sides' outside colored (coloring at the vertices of the polygon is allowed to start from the inside).

\section*{Task 2 - 330922}

To mill a quantity of grain, two milling machines $A$ and $B$ can be used. Each milling machine processes the same amount in the same time.

If machine $A$ is used for 8 hours first and then only machine $B$ is used, $B$ would need exactly 18 more hours to complete the entire grain quantity. If, however, machine $A$ is used for 10 hours first and then only machine $B$ is used, $B$ would need exactly 15 more hours to complete the entire quantity.

How long will it take to complete the entire quantity if $A$ and $B$ are used together from the beginning?

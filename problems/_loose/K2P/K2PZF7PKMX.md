A quadrilateral $A B C D$ has the following properties:

(1) $A B \| D C$ and $A D \nVdash B C$,

(2) $A D=B C=3 \cdot D C=a$, where $a$ is a given length,

(3) $\angle B A D=60^{\circ}$.

Determine the perimeter $u$ of this quadrilateral in terms of $a$!

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $E$ and $D$ points on the sides $[AB]$ and $[AC]$ such that $BE = CD$. Let $P$ be the intersection of the diagonals of the quadrilateral $BEDC$ and $Q$ the second intersection point of the circumcircles of $\triangle EPB$ and $\triangle DPC$. Let $K$ and $L$ be the midpoints of $[BE]$ and $[CD]$ respectively, and $R$ the intersection of the perpendicular to $(QK)$ passing through $K$ and the perpendicular to $(QL)$ passing through $L$.

Show that:
a) $Q$ lies on the bisector of angle $\widehat{BAC}$.

b) $R$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

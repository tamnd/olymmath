In the country of Timbroland ruled by the ruthless LoJac, there are two stamp companies. They issue stamps in turn, each with an integer value strictly greater than 1 jacomos (the official currency of the country). Unfortunately, this despot forbids the issuance of new stamps whose value can already be composed with existing stamps. The first company that can no longer create a stamp faces the wrath of the sovereign (you can imagine the consequences)... Which of the two companies has a winning strategy to avoid punishment?

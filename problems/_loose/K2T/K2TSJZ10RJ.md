(Olympiade de Leningrad 1989). Let $k>1$ be an integer. Prove that it is impossible to place the numbers $1, \ldots, k^{2}$, one in each cell of a $k \times k$ square table, such that the sums of all the numbers marked in a row or in a column is always a power of 2 (not necessarily always the same).

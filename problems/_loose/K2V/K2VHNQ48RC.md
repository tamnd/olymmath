Two identical letters Г lie on a plane. The ends of the short bars of these letters are denoted by $A$ and $A^{\prime}$. The long bars are divided into $n$ equal parts by points $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n-1} ; A_{1}^{\prime}, \ldots, A_{n-1}^{\prime}$ (the division points are numbered from the ends of the long bars). The lines $A A_{i}$ and $A^{\prime} A_{i}^{\prime}$ intersect at point $X_{i}$. Prove that the points $X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n-1}$ form a convex polygon.

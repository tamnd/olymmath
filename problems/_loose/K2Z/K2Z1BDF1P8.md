Given that $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ are the two foci of the hyperbola $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>0, b>0)$, and a regular triangle $M F_{1} F_{2}$ is constructed with the line segment $F_{1} F_{2}$ as one side. If the midpoint of side $M F_{1}$ lies on the hyperbola, then the eccentricity of the hyperbola is ( ).
(A) $4+2 \sqrt{3}$
(B) $\sqrt{3}-1$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2}$
(D) $\sqrt{3}+1$

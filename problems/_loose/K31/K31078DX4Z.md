Points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ are given on a circle. Consider all different convex polygons formed with these points as vertices, and divide these polygons into two groups: the first group consists of polygons that have $A_{1}$ as a vertex, and the second group consists of polygons that do not have $A_{1}$ as a vertex. Which group has more polygons?

Let $f(x)$ be a differentiable function. Find the value of $x$ for which
$$\{f(x)\}^2+(e+1)f(x)+1+e^2-2\int_0^x f(t)dt-2f(x)\int_0^x f(t)dt+2\left\{\int_0^x f(t)dt\right\}^2$$
is minimized.

*1978 Tokyo Medical College entrance exam*

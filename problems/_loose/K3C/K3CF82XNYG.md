Given an operation *, which associates with any two integers X and Y an integer X * Y. It is known that every integer is equal to X * Y for some integers X and Y. Prove that such an operation cannot simultaneously have the following two properties:
a) $A * B=-(B * A)$

b) $(A * B) * C=A *(B * C)$.

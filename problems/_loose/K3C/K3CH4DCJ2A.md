Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $O{}$ be its circumcenter, $I{}$ its incenter and $I_A,I_B,I_C$ the excenters. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ and $H_1$ and $H_2$ be the orthocenters of the triangles $MII_A$ and $MI_BI_C$. Prove that the parallel to $BC$ through $O$ passes through the midpoint of the segment $H_1H_2$.

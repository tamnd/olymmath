a) Draw all points in the plane with coordinates $(x ; y)$ such that

$$
(|3 x-y|-3)(|3 x+y|-3)=0
$$

b) Find all $x$ and $y$ for which

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
(|3 x-y|-3)(|3 x+y|-3) & = & 0 \\
y-\{4 x\} & = & 0 \\
-1 \leq x \leq 1 & &
\end{array}
$$

(For a real number $x$ we denote the unique number in the interval $[0,1)$ for which $x-\{x\}$ is an integer by $\{x\}$ ).

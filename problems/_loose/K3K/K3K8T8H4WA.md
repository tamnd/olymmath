A school has $b$ teachers and $c$ students and satisfies: (1) each teacher teaches exactly $k$ students; (2) any two different students are taught by exactly $h$ teachers. Prove: $\frac{b}{h}=\frac{c(c-1)}{k(k-1)}$.

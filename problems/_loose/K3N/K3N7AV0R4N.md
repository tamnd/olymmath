Sperner's Theorem Let $A$ be an $n$-element set, $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{m}$ be subsets of $A$ and no two of them are contained in each other, then the maximum value of $m$ is $C_{n}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]}$.

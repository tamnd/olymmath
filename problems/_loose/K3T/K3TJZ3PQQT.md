A running track represents a circle 1 km in length. Along an arc, which is 100 m long, spectator stands are built. Is it possible to launch 10 runners on this track in the same direction, moving at constant speeds of 20, 21, ..., 29 km/h, so that at any moment in time, at least one of the runners is running in front of the stands?

(The starting position of each runner can be designated at any point, independently of the other runners.)

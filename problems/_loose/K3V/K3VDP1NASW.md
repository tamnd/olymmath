(Problem from the 28th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1965) Prove the following characteristic for divisibility by 37: To determine whether an integer is divisible by 37, it can be divided from right to left into sections, each consisting of three digits. If the sum of these three-digit numbers is divisible by 37, then the original number is also divisible by 37 (the term "three-digit number" is used in a broad sense here, as some sections may start with 0, making them in fact two-digit or one-digit numbers, and when the number of digits in the original number is not divisible by 3, the leftmost section may not be a three-digit number).

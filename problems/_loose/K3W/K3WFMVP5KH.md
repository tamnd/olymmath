Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in circle $\omega_{1}$, and let $\omega_{2}$ be a circle intersecting circle $\omega_{1}$ at $X$ and $Y$. It intersects the lines $(AB)$ and $(CD)$ at $P, Q, R$ and $S$ in that order. The lines $(QR)$ and $(PS)$ intersect the lines $(AC)$ and $(BD)$ at $U, V, W$ and $Z$. Show that the points $X, Y, U, V, W$ and $Z$ lie on the same circle.

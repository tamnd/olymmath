Let the complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ be such that $\left|z_{1}\right|=\left|z_{2}\right|=\left|z_{3}\right|=1$ and $z_{1} z_{2} z_{3} \neq-1$. Show that

$$
w=\frac{z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}+z_{1} z_{2}+z_{1} z_{3}+z_{2} z_{3}}{1+z_{1} z_{2} z_{3}}
$$

is a real number.

The territory of a certain kingdom is a square with a side length of 2 kilometers. The king decided to summon all his subjects to his palace for a ball at 7 PM. For this purpose, he sent out a messenger at noon, who can convey any instructions to any subject, and these subjects can also convey any instructions to any other subjects, and so on. Each subject, before hearing the instructions, is in a certain position (at their home) and can run in any direction at a speed of 3 kilometers per hour (in a straight line). Prove that the king can manage to successfully convey his intentions so that every subject can arrive at the palace before the ball starts.

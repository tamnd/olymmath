The foundation of a tent is a square with a side length of $a(=15 \mathrm{~m})$. The boundary of the foundation surrounds this square and consists of 4 arcs and 4 straight line segments. The centers of the arcs are the vertices of the square, with radii of $a, a, a / 2, a / 2$, respectively. The straight line segments are the common external tangents of 2 adjacent arcs. At each vertex of the square, there is a column, and the height of each column is equal to the radius of the arc around its base. The tent's roof consists of 4 parts of conical surfaces, whose base edges are the arcs and whose apexes are the column tops above the arc centers, as well as 5 planar quadrilaterals, whose vertices are the column tops and the endpoints of the tangent segments of the arcs. Calculate the area of the roof, the volume of the tent, and its average height (the ratio of its volume to its base area). Draw a possible, single-piece layout of the roof.

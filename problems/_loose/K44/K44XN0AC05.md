Prove that the functions

$$
x^{n}, x^{+}=x \vee 0, x^{-}=(-x) \vee 0, |x|=x^{+}+x^{-}
$$

are Borel functions. Also prove the more general result: any continuous function $ f=f(x), x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}, n \geqslant 1 $, is a Borel function.

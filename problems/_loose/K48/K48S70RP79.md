Let $y=\frac{1}{x}$ be a hyperbola, and let $P$ be the point on this hyperbola with an abscissa $a \geq 1$. Denote by $e$ the tangent at $P$, and let $t(a)$ represent the area enclosed by this tangent $e$ and the coordinate axes; let $T(a)$ represent the area of the triangle bounded by the tangent $e$, the perpendicular line raised at $P$, and the $x$-axis. Does the ratio $\frac{t(a)}{T(a)}$ have a maximum and a minimum value?

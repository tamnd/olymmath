Let $ABCD$ be any trapezoid with $AB \| CD$. The lengths of its sides and diagonals are denoted as follows:

$$
AB=a, BC=b, CD=c, DA=d, AC=e, BD=f
$$

Prove that the following equations (1) and (2) always hold:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a f^{2}+c e^{2}=(a+c)\left(a c+b^{2}\right) \\
& a e^{2}+c f^{2}=(a+c)\left(a c+d^{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

The line $l$ passes through the right focus $F$ of the ellipse $C$ and intersects the ellipse at points $A, B$. The projections of points $A, F, B$ on the line $x=4$ are $D, K, E$ respectively.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Connect $A E$ and $B D$. Explore whether the lines $A E$ and $B D$ intersect at a fixed point when the inclination angle of line $l$ changes. If so, find the coordinates of the fixed point and provide a proof; if not, explain the reason.

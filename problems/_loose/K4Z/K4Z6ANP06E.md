Given two moving points $A$ and $B$, and a fixed point $M\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$, all on the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ ($A$ and $B$ do not coincide with $M$). Let $F$ be the focus of the parabola, and $Q$ be a point on the axis of symmetry. If $\left(\overrightarrow{Q A}+\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B}\right) \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}=0$, and $|\overrightarrow{F A}|,|\overrightarrow{F M}|, |\overrightarrow{F B}|$ form an arithmetic sequence.
(1) Find the coordinates of $\overrightarrow{O Q}$;
(2) If $|\overrightarrow{O Q}|=3,|\overrightarrow{F M}|=\frac{5}{2}$, and the projections of points $A$ and $B$ on the directrix of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$ $(p>0)$ are $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ respectively, find the range of the area of quadrilateral $A B B_{1} A_{1}$.

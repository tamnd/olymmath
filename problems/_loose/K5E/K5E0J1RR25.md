A. $\left[-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\right]$
B. $\left(-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\right)$
C. $\left(-\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3}, \frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$
D. $\left[-\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3}, \frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3}\right]$

Prove: there do not exist positive integers $m, n, k$ such that for all real numbers $x, y$ the following is satisfied:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
(x+1)^{2}+y^{2}=m^{2}, \\
(x-1)^{2}+y^{2}=n^{2}, \\
x^{2}+(y-\sqrt{3})^{2}=k^{2} .
\end{array}\right.
$$

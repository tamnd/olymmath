Five, there is a rectangular prism with length, width, and height being positive integers $m, n, r (m \leqslant n \leqslant r)$, respectively. After the surface is painted red, it is cut into cubes with an edge length of 1. It is known that the sum of the number of cubes without red and the number of cubes with two red faces, minus the number of cubes with one red face, equals 1985. Find the values of $m, n, r$.

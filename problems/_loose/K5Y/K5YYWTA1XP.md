Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $AB < AC$ . Let $D, E, F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$ , respectively, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ . Let $P$ be the intersection of lines $EF$ and $BC$ . Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $ABC$ . The line $PH$ intersects the circle with diameter $AH$ again at point $J$ . Let $R$ be the reflection of $A$ across $BC$ . Lines $JD$ and $PR$ meet at $K$ . Prove that the quadrilateral $KDMR$ is cyclic.

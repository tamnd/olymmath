Fomin S.B. A rectangular chocolate bar of size $5 \times 10$ is divided by longitudinal and transverse grooves into 50 square pieces. Two players play a game. The first player breaks the chocolate bar along some groove into two rectangular parts and places the resulting parts on the table. Then the players take turns performing similar operations: each time the current player breaks one of the parts into two parts. The player who first breaks off a square piece (without grooves), a) loses; b) wins. Who of the players can ensure their win: the starting player or their partner?

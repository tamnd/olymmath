Two equilateral triangles are inscribed in a circle. The vertices $ A $, $ B $, $ C $ of one triangle follow each other on the circle in the same direction as the vertices $ A_1 $, $ B_1 $, $ C_1 $ of the other triangle. The lines $ AB $ and $ A_1B_1 $ intersect at point $ P $, the lines $ BC $ and $ B_1C_1 $ at point $ M $, and the lines $ CA $ and $ C_1A_1 $ at point $ N $. Prove that the points $ M $, $ N $, $ P $ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

Consider two spheres $\left(O_{1}, r_{1} ; O_{2}, r_{2}\right)$ and a point $P$ on their intersection circle. Construct a line through $P$ such that its first dihedral angle is $30^{\circ}$ and which, with respect to the intersection points $(A, B)$ with the spheres, satisfies: $\overline{P A}=\overline{P B}$.

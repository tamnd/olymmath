Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence defined by: $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ and $a_{n+1}+a_{n}=\frac{2}{n^{2}+2 n}, \forall n \geq 1$.

a. Find the general term of the sequence.

b. Calculate the sum $S=\sum_{k=1}^{m}(2 k+1) a_{k}^{2}$ and show that $S<1$.

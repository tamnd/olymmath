Let $f(x)=3x^3-5x^2+2x-6$ . If the roots of $f$ are given by $\alpha$ , $\beta$ , and $\gamma$ , find
$$
\left(\frac{1}{\alpha-2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{1}{\beta-2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{1}{\gamma-2}\right)^2.
$$

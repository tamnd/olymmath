Let the roots of the equation $x^{2} + p x + q = 0$ be denoted by $x_{1}, x_{2}$. On the phase plane $O p q$, draw the sets of points $M(p, q)$ that are defined by the conditions:
a) $x_{1} = 0, x_{2} = 1$
b) $x_{1} \leq 0, x_{2} \geq 2$;
c) $x_{1} = x_{2}$;
d) $-1 \leq x_{1} \leq 0, 1 \leq x_{2} \leq 2$.

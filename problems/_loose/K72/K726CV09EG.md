Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ three points on the sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, chosen such that the lines $\left(A A_{1}\right),\left(B B_{1}\right)$, and $\left(C C_{1}\right)$ are concurrent. Let $M$ be the orthogonal projection of $A_{1}$ on $\left(B_{1} C_{1}\right)$. Show that $\left(M A_{1}\right)$ is the bisector of $\widehat{B M C}$.

Circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ intersect at points $P$ and $Q$. A line passing through point $Q$ intersects circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ at points $R$ and $S$, respectively. The line $S P$ intersects circle $k_{1}$ again at point $M$, and the line $R P$ intersects circle $k_{2}$ again at point $N$. Let $T$ be the intersection of lines $R M$ and $S N$.

Prove that triangle $T M N$ is equilateral if and only if line $M N$ is a common tangent to circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$.

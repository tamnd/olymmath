Find the number of quadratic polynomials $ax^2 + bx +c$ which satisfy the following:

(a) $a,b,c$ are distinct;

(b) $a,b,c \in \{ 1,2,3,\cdots 1999 \}$;

(c) $x+1$ divides $ax^2 + bx+c$.

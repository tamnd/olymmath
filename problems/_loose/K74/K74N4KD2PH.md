Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $P$ an interior point, and let $D, E, F$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $BC, CA$, and $AB$. Suppose that $PA^2 + PD^2 = PB^2 + PE^2 = PC^2 + PF^2$. Show that $P$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $I_{a} I_{b} I_{c}$, where $I_{a}, I_{b}$, and $I_{c}$ are the centers of the excircles of triangle $ABC$.

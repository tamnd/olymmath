Let the natural number $n \geqslant 5, n$ different natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ have the following property: for any two different non-empty subsets $A$ and $B$ of the set $S=$ $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}$, the sum of all numbers in $A$ and the sum of all numbers in $B$ will not be equal. Under the above conditions, find the maximum value of $\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}$. (1994 Shanghai Competition Problem)

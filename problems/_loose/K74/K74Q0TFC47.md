Let $a, b$ be two lines that do not intersect, and from any point $P$ on $a$, a perpendicular $p$ is dropped to line $b$, intersecting $b$ at a point $Q$; furthermore, the angle of inclination of line $b$ to the plane through $a$ and $p$ is $\alpha$, then the product: $P Q \cdot \operatorname{tg} \alpha$ is constant and independent of the arbitrary point chosen on $a$.

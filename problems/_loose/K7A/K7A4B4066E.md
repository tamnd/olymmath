Find the range of real numbers $m$ such that the equation $x^{2}-m x+2 m-2=0$:
(1) has a solution in the interval $\left[0, \frac{3}{2}\right]$;
(2) has a solution in the interval $\left(0, \frac{3}{2}\right)$;
(3) has exactly one solution in the interval $\left(0, \frac{3}{2}\right)$;
(4) has two solutions in the interval $\left[0, \frac{3}{2}\right]$.

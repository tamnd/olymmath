Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute triangle, and construct isosceles right triangles $\triangle B C D$, $\triangle A B E$, and $\triangle C A F$ outside $\triangle A B C$, where $\angle B D C$, $\angle B A E$, and $\angle C F A$ are right angles. Construct another isosceles right triangle $\triangle E F G$ outside quadrilateral $B C F E$, with $\angle E F G$ being a right angle. Prove that: (1) $G A=\sqrt{2} A D$, (2) $\angle \dot{G} A D=135^{\circ}$.

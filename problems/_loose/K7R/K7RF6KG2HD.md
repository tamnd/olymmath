Given a rectangle and a square with equal areas. Let $a$ and $b$ be two adjacent sides of the rectangle, and $c$ be the side of the square. Prove that

$$
a+b-c > R \sqrt{2}
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle around the rectangle.

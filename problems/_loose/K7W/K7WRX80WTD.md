We consider all possible tilings of an 8 by 8 chessboard with dominoes, each consisting of two adjacent squares. Determine the maximum natural number $ n $ such that for any tiling of the 8 by 8 board with dominoes, one can find some rectangle composed of $ n $ squares of the board that does not contain any domino in its entirety. The lengths of the sides of the rectangle in squares can be any natural numbers, starting from one.

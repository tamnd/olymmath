In a square with side 1, a broken line of length $L$ is located. It is known that each point of the square is at a distance less than $\varepsilon$ from some point of this broken line. Prove that then $L \geqslant \frac{1}{2 \varepsilon}-\frac{\pi \varepsilon}{2}$.

The natural numbers $1, 2, 3, \cdots, 2014, 2015, 2016$ are arranged in a circle clockwise. Starting from the number 1, the following operations are performed clockwise:
First step: Cross out 1, keep 2;
Second step: Cross out $3, 4$, keep 5;
Third step: Cross out $6, 7, 8$, keep 9;
Fourth step: Cross out $10, 11, 12, 13$, keep $14; \cdots$;
That is, in the nth step, first cross out n numbers, then keep 1 number, and continue this operation until all numbers are crossed out.
What is the last number to be crossed out? $ $

Is it possible to cut a circle into parts in such a way that a) the center of the circle is on the boundary of each part and b) from some of the parts obtained as a result of the cutting, a regular hexagon inscribed in this circle can be formed? If it is possible, describe the cutting and indicate how to form the hexagon from the resulting parts; if not, prove that it is impossible.

\section*{Task 5 - 041215}

In an IL 18 of Interflug, which flies to Berlin, five passengers are sitting next to each other in a row. Their professions are: Journalist, Precision Mechanic, Teacher, Captain, and Engineer. They belong to the following nationalities: Poland, GDR, Hungary, Cyprus, and USSR. They are of different ages (21, 24, 32, 40, and 52 years). The passengers engage in different sports (Handball, Swimming, Volleyball, Athletics, and Football). Their travel destinations are: Berlin, Leipzig, Dresden, Karl-Marx-City, and Rostock.

From conversations, we gather the following information:

(1) The Engineer is sitting all the way to the left.

(2) The Volleyball player has the middle seat.

(3) The Pole is a Journalist.

(4) The Precision Mechanic is 21 years old.

(5) The Teacher practices swimming.

(6) The Captain is traveling to Rostock.

(7) The Handball player is from the GDR.

(8) The traveler from the Soviet Union is flying to Leipzig.

(9) The traveler to Berlin is 32 years old.

(10) The Athlete has Karl-Marx-City as their travel destination.

(11) The traveler from the GDR is sitting next to the traveler from Hungary.

(12) The 52-year-old is sitting next to the traveler heading to Dresden.

(13) The 24-year-old is sitting next to the traveler heading to Leipzig.

(14) The Engineer is sitting next to the Cypriot.

a) How old is the Captain?

b) What nationality does the Football player have?

Show that the information provided is sufficient to answer both questions uniquely!

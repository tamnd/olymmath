Let the positive integer $a$ be composed entirely of the digits 1 and 2. A $k$-digit number formed by any $k (k \geqslant 2)$ consecutive digits of $a$ is called a "$k$-segment" of the number $a$. If any two $k$-segments of the number $a$ are different, prove that for the largest positive integer $a$ with this property, its initial $k-1$ segment and its final $k-1$ segment must be the same.

In a non-equilateral $\triangle ABC$, it is known that the angle bisectors of $\angle A$, $\angle B$, and $\angle C$ intersect the opposite sides at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. The perpendicular bisector of $AD$ intersects $BC$ at point $P$, the perpendicular bisector of $BE$ intersects $AC$ at point $Q$, and the perpendicular bisector of $CF$ intersects $AB$ at point $R$. Prove that points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are collinear.

A class consists of pupils who each wear either a red tie or a green tie. There are two more pupils wearing green ties than wearing red ties. Two pupils are chosen at random. The probability of selecting two pupils of different tie-colour is exactly treble the probability of selecting two pupils who both wear red ties. Given that $\mathrm{R}$ is the number of pupils wearing a red tie, and that $R>0$, determine $R^{3}+R^{2}+R$.

On one side of an angle with vertex at point $M$, equal to $30^{\circ}$, points $A$ and $B$ are marked such that $M A=1, M B=3$. A point $C$ moves along the other side of the angle. Find $M C$, if it is known that

a) the angle $B C A$ is the largest;

b) the sum of the squares of the distances from $C$ to points $B$ and $A$ is the smallest;

c) the sum of the distances from $C$ to points $B$ and $A$ is the smallest;

d) the trajectory $B C M$ is optimal for moving from point $B$ to point $M$ under the condition that movement along the ray containing point $C$ is twice as fast as along any other path outside this ray.

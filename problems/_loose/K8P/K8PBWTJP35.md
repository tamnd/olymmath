Given $u_0,u_1$ with $0<u_0,u_1<1$ , define the sequence $(u_n)$ recurrently by the formula $$ u_{n+2}=\frac12\left(\sqrt{u_{n+1}}+\sqrt{u_n}\right). $$ (a) Prove that the sequence $u_n$ is convergent and find its limit.
(b) Prove that, starting from some index $n_0$ , the sequence $u_n$ is monotonous.

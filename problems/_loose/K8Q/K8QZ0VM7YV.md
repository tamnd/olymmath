Let the sides of a triangle be $a, b, c$, and its area be $t$. Let the semi-perimeter of the triangle be $s$, and the radii of the inscribed circle and the excircles opposite to the sides $a, b, c$ be denoted by $\varrho, \varrho_{a}, \varrho_{b}, \varrho_{c}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\begin{gathered}
\varrho_{a} \varrho_{b}+\varrho_{b} \varrho_{c}+\varrho_{c} \varrho_{a}=s^{2} \\
\frac{1}{\varrho}=\frac{1}{\varrho_{a}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{b}}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{c}} \\
\varrho \cdot \varrho_{a} \cdot \varrho_{b} \cdot \varrho_{c}=t^{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

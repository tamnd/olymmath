Find the number of oredered triples $(a,b,c)$ of positive integers such that $1 \leq a,b,c \leq 50$ which satisfy the relation $$ \dfrac{\operatorname{lcm}(a,c)+\operatorname{lcm}(b,c)}{a+b} = \dfrac{26c}{27}. $$ Here, by lcm $(x,y)$ we mean the LCM, that is, least common multiple of $x$ and $y$ .

- My task is about the March Hare and the Mad Hatter,- began the Griffin. - One of them was born in 1842, but I won't tell you which one. The other was born either in 1843 or 1844, but again, I won't say which. By the way, I want to ask you, do you know that the March Hare was born in March?
- I didn't know,- answered Alice, - but I'm not surprised at all.
- Neither did I,- added Quasi the Turtle.
- But everyone should know,- continued the Griffin, - that the March Hare has a watch that shows...
- Oh yes!- interrupted Alice. - Those funny watches that show the date instead of the time! I've seen them.
- I mean a different watch,- shouted the Griffin. The March Hare has another watch that shows the time like a regular watch. The Mad Hatter also has his own watch. Neither of these watches shows the correct time. The Mad Hatter's watch gains ten seconds every hour, while the March Hare's watch loses ten seconds every hour.

One day in January, the Mad Hatter and the March Hare set their watches to the exact time at exactly twelve o'clock noon.

- You know,- said the Mad Hatter, - our watches won't show the same time until your next birthday, when you turn twenty-one.
- That's right,- confirmed the March Hare.

Who is older: the March Hare or the Mad Hatter?

- This problem is not for me,- said Quasi the Turtle, slowly moving away.
- Does it really have a solution?- asked Alice.
- Of course!- assured her the Griffin.

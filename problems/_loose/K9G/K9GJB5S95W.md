Rubanov I.S. In the country there are 2000 cities. Each city is connected by non-stop two-way air routes to some other cities, and for each city, the number of outgoing air routes is a power of two (i.e., $1, 2, 4, 8, \ldots$). For each city $A$, a statistician calculated the number of routes with no more than one transfer connecting $A$ to other cities, and then summed up the results for all 2000 cities. He got 100000. Prove that the statistician made a mistake.

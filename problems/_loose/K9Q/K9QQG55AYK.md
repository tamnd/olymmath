(Mathematics for Middle School, Issue 6, 2004, Olympiad Training Problem) Given that $A B C D-A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ is a rectangular prism, the perpendiculars from point $A$ to the lines $A_{1} B, A_{1} C, A_{1} D$ intersect the lines $A_{1} B_{1}, A_{1} C_{1}, A_{1} D_{1}$ at points $M, N, P_{1}$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $E, G, F$ respectively. Prove:
(1) $M, N, P$ are collinear;
(2) The lines $P E, M F, A N$ intersect at one point.

Let $n \geqslant 0$ be an integer, and $2 n+1$ children are located in the plane. Suppose that the distances between all pairs of children are distinct. Each child has a water gun and simultaneously, each one shoots at the child closest to them.

(a) Show that there exist two children who shoot at each other.

(b) Show that there exists a dry child, that is, a child at whom no one shoots.

The interior angles of a convex $n$-sided polygon are denoted as $A, B, C, \cdots$, and
$$
\lg \sin A+\lg \sin B+\lg \sin C+\cdots=0 .
$$

Then the polygon is
(A) an isosceles trapezoid.
(B) a rectangle.
(C) a right triangle.
(D) a regular hexagon. (Anhui Province, Anqing and other counties and cities Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

Given that $n$ is a positive integer, $2 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant 8$ $(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$. Prove:
$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x_{i}}\right) \leqslant\left(\frac{5}{4} n\right)^{2},
$$

and discuss when equality holds.

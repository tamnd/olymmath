At every integer on the number line from 0 to 2022, a person stands at the beginning.

In each move, two people with a distance of at least 2 are selected. These move 1 step towards each other.

If no such move is possible anymore, the process ends.

Show that this process always ends after a finite number of moves, and determine all possible configurations where the people can end up. (For each configuration, only the number of people standing at each number is of interest.)

(Birgit Vera Schmidt)

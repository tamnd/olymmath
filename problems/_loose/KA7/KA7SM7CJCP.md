1.2 (CMO 14) In an acute-angled $\triangle ABC$, $\angle C > \angle B$. Point $D$ is a point on side $BC$ such that $\angle ADB$ is obtuse. $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABD$, and point $F$ is inside $\triangle ABC$ and lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABD$. Prove that point $F$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$ if and only if $HD$ is parallel to $CF$, and $F$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$.

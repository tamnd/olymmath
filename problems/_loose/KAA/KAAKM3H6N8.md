Let $m,n>2$ be integers. A regular ${n}$ -sided polygon region $\mathcal T$ on a plane contains a regular ${m}$ -sided polygon region with a side length of ${}{}{}1$ . Prove that any regular ${m}$ -sided polygon region $\mathcal S$ on the plane with side length $\cos{\pi}/[m,n]$ can be translated inside $\mathcal T.$ In other words, there exists a vector $\vec\alpha,$ such that for each point in $\mathcal S,$ after translating the vector $\vec\alpha$ at that point, it fall into $\mathcal T.$ Note: The polygonal area includes both the interior and boundaries.

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of a triangle $ABC$, and $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$. For simplicity, we assume that $H$ is inside the triangle $ABC$, which means that all angles of the triangle are acute (such a triangle is called acute-angled). Determine the angles of the triangles $A H_{B} H_{C}, H_{A} B H_{C}, H_{A} H_{B} C$ and $H_{A} H_{B} H_{C}$, in terms of the angles of triangle $ABC$, which we will denote as $\widehat{A}, \widehat{B}$ and $\widehat{C}$.

Note: In triangle geometry, many points are used, and to avoid using the same name for too many different points, it can happen that one runs out of notations. The notations $H_{A}, H_{B}$ and $H_{C}$ are not common, but they can be useful in many cases.

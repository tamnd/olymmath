Given the parabola $x^{2}=4 y$ and a fixed point $P(0,8)$, let $A$ and $B$ be two moving points on the parabola, and $\overrightarrow{A P}=\lambda \overrightarrow{P B}$ $(\lambda>0)$. Tangents to the parabola are drawn at points $A$ and $B$, and their intersection point is $M$. (1) Prove that the y-coordinate of point $M$ is a constant; (2) Does there exist a fixed point $Q$ such that regardless of how $A B$ moves, $\angle A Q P=$ $\angle B Q P$? Prove your conclusion.

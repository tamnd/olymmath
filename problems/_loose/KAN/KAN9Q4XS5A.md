Let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of a triangle $ABC$ with $\angle BAC > 90^{\circ}$ and $AB > AC$ . The tangents
of $\Omega$ at $B$ and $C$ cross at $D$ and the tangent of $\Omega$ at $A$ crosses the line $BC$ at $E$ . The line
through $D$ , parallel to $AE$ , crosses the line $BC$ at $F$ . The circle with diameter $EF$ meets the
line $AB$ at $P$ and $Q$ and the line $AC$ at $X$ and $Y$ . Prove that one of the angles $\angle AEB$ , $\angle PEQ$ , $\angle XEY$ is equal to the sum of the other two.

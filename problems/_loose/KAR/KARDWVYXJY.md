How many permutations of the word ``ACADEMY'' have that there exist two vowels that are separated by an odd distance? For example, the X and Y in XAY are separated by an even distance, while the X and Y in XABY are separated by an odd distance. Note: the vowels are A, E, I, O, and U. Y is **NOT** a vowel.

Paulinho was studying the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) at school and decided to practice at home. He called the ages of three people living with him $a, b$, and $c$. Then, he performed some operations with their prime factors and obtained the greatest common divisors of the 3 pairs of numbers. A few days later, he forgot the ages $a, b$, and $c$, but found the following results noted:

$$
\begin{aligned}
a \cdot b \cdot c & =2^{4} \cdot 3^{2} \cdot 5^{3} \\
G C D(a, b) & =15 \\
G C D(a, c) & =5 \\
G C D(b, c) & =20
\end{aligned}
$$

Help Paulinho determine the values of $a, b$, and $c$.

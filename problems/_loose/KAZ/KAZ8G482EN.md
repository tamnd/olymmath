Let $ABC$ be points such that $AB=7, BC=5, AC=10$ , and $M$ be the midpoint of $AC$ . Let $\omega$ , $\omega_1$ be the circumcircles of $ABC$ and $BMC$ . $\Omega$ , $\Omega_1$ are circles through $A$ and $M$ such that $\Omega$ is tangent to $\omega_1$ and $\Omega_1$ is tangent to the line through the centers of $\omega_1$ and $\Omega$ . $D, E$ be the intersection of $\Omega$ with $\omega$ and $\Omega_1$ with $\omega_1$ . If $F$ is the intersection of the circumcircle of $DME$ with $BM$ , find $FB$ .

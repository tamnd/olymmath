Let $n$ and $m$ be two natural numbers, $m\ge n\ge 2$ . Find the number of injective functions

$$f\colon\{1,2,\ldots,n\}\to\{1,2,\ldots,m\}$$
such that there exists a unique number $i\in\{1,2,\ldots,n-1\}$ for which $f(i)>f(i+1) .$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB < AC$ and with circumcircle $\Gamma$ . Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ . The perpendicular bisector of segment $BC$ intersects line $AB$ at $Y$ and line $AC$ at $X$ . Let $\omega$ be the circumference passing through $B$ , $X$ , and $M$ . The circumferences $\omega$ and $\Gamma$ intersect at $Z$ , with $Z \neq B$ . Let $N$ be the intersection of line $AZ$ with line $XY$ . Show that $N$ is the midpoint of $XY$ .

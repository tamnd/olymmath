Show that for all integers $n \geq 2$,

$$
\frac{1}{1 \times 2}+\frac{1}{2 \times 3}+\frac{1}{3 \times 4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{(n-1) \times n}=1-\frac{1}{n}
$$

Question 20 Let $f(x)$ be a function on $[0,1]$
$$
f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
2 x+\frac{1}{3} & 0 \leqslant x<\frac{1}{3} ; \\
\frac{3}{2}(1-x) & \frac{1}{3} \leqslant x \leqslant 1 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Try to find 5 different points $x_{0}, x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}$ on $[0,1]$ such that
$$
f\left(x_{0}\right)=x_{1}, f\left(x_{1}\right)=x_{2}, f\left(x_{2}\right)=x_{3}, f\left(x_{3}\right)=x_{4}, f\left(x_{4}\right)=x_{0} .
$$

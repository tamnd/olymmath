a) At a sports competition, 20 athletes are participating; the competition is judged by 9 judges. Each judge ranks the competitors from 1st to 20th according to their assessment of the performances, and it turned out later that the ratings of all judges did not differ significantly: no athlete received places from two judges that differed by more than three. The final ranking is based on the "average place" of the athlete according to the ratings of the 9 judges, i.e., the sum of all assigned places divided by the number of judges (9). What is the highest possible value of the "average place" for the best of the 20 athletes?

b) The tennis federation has assigned qualification numbers to all tennis players in the country: the 1st number to the strongest of them, the 2nd to the next strongest, and so on; it is known that in a match between tennis players whose qualification numbers differ by more than two, the player with the lower number always wins. 1024 tennis players from the country are holding a competition according to the Olympic system, i.e., after each stage of the competition, all losers are eliminated from the game, and the remaining players are randomly paired for the next round. The question is, what is the highest number that the winner of such a competition can have?

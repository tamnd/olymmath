Let $d>1$ be a natural number. Prove that any natural number $n$ can be uniquely represented in the form $n=a_{0}+a_{1} d+a_{2} d^{2}+\ldots+a_{k} d^{k}$, where $0 \leqslant a_{i} \leqslant d-1$ are integers, and $a_{k} \neq 0$.

The expression $n=a_{0}+a_{1} d+a_{2} d^{2}+\ldots+a_{k} d^{k}$ is called the $d$-ary representation of the number $n$.

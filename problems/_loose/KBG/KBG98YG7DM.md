On the segment $[0, N]$, its ends and two more points are marked such that the lengths of the segments into which the segment $[0, N]$ is divided are integers and are pairwise coprime. If there are two such marked points $A$ and $B$ such that the distance between them is divisible by 3, then the segment $A B$ can be divided into three equal parts, one of the division points can be marked, and one of the points $A$, B can be erased. Is it true that after several such actions, any predetermined integer point on the segment $[0, N]$ can be marked?

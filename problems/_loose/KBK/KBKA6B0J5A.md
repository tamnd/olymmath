$$
\angle A P B=\frac{\pi}{2}, A B=\sqrt{2} A D,
$$
$P C, P D$ intersect $A B$ at $E, F$, respectively. Prove:
$$
A E^{2}+B F^{2}=A B^{2} \text {. }
$$

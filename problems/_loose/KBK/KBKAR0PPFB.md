Let $C=C[0, \infty)$ be the space of continuous functions $x=\left(x_{t}\right)$ defined for $t \geqslant 0$. Show that with respect to the metric

$$
\rho(x, y)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 2^{-n} \min \left\{\sup _{0 \leqslant t \leqslant n}\left|x_{t}-y_{t}\right|, 1\right\}, x, y \in C
$$

this space is Polish, i.e., complete and separable metric, and the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{B}_{0}(C)$ generated by the open sets coincides with the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{B}(C)$ generated by the cylindrical sets.

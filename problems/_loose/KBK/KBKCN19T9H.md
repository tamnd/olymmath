Let $\sigma(n)$ denote the sum of all positive divisors of the positive integer $n$, and let $D(n)$ be the set of all distinct prime factors of $n$. Prove: For any natural number $k$, there exists a positive integer $m$ such that $|D(\sigma(m)) \backslash D(m)|=k$.

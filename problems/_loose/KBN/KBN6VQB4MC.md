Let $a, b$ be two positive integers, prove: $\frac{\varphi(a b)}{\sqrt{\varphi^{2}\left(a^{2}\right)+\varphi^{2}\left(b^{2}\right)}} \leq \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ (where $\varphi(n)$ denotes the number of positive integers less than $n$ that are coprime to $n$, with the convention $\varphi(1)=1$)

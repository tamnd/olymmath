Let $S$ be the set of all pairs $(a,b)$ of real numbers satisfying $1+a+a^2+a^3 = b^2(1+3a)$ and $1+2a+3a^2 = b^2 - \frac{5}{b}$ . Find $A+B+C$ , where $$
A = \prod_{(a,b) \in S} a
, B = \prod_{(a,b) \in S} b
, \text{and} C = \sum_{(a,b) \in S} ab.
$$*Proposed by Evan Chen*

Call an *ordered* triple $(a,b,c)$ *$d$-tall* if there exists a triangle with side lengths $a,b,c$ and the height to the side with length $a$ is $d$. Suppose that for some positive integer $k$, there are exactly $210$ $k$-tall ordered triples of positive integers. How many $k$-tall ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ are there such that a triangle $ABC$ with $BC=a,CA=b,AB=c$ satisfies both $\angle{B}<90^\circ$ and $\angle{C}<90^\circ$?

On the teacher's desk, there are scales. On the scales, there are weights of not necessarily the same weight, on each of which the surnames of one or several students are written. When a student enters the classroom, they move to the other pan of the scales each weight that has their surname written on it. Prove that it is possible to let such students into the classroom so that in the end, the pan that was heavier at the beginning is no longer the heavier one.

G64 (34-4, Macedonia) For any three points $P, Q, R$ in the plane, define $m(P Q R)$ as the length of the shortest altitude of $\triangle P Q R$ (when $P, Q, R$ are collinear, $m(P Q R)=0$). Now let $A, B, C$ be three points in the plane, and for any point $X$ in this plane, prove that:
$$
m(A B C) \leqslant m(A B X)+m(A X C)+m(X B C) .
$$

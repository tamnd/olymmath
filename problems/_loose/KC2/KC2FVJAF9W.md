Prove that there exists a constant $ c>0$ such that in every nontrivial finite group $ G$ there exists a sequence of length at most $ c\ln |G|$ with the property that each element of $ G$ equals the product of some subsequence. (The elements of $ G$ in the sequence are not required to be distinct. A *subsequence* of a sequence is obtained by selecting some of the terms, not necessarily consecutive, without reordering them; for example, $ 4,4,2$ is a subesequence of $ 2,4,6,4,2,$ but $ 2,2,4$ is not.)

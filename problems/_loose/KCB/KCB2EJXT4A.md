Given that the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ is $\odot O$, with radius $R$, and points $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ are symmetric to point $O$ with respect to $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. Prove:
(1) The circles with centers at $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ and radius $R$ intersect at one point (denoted as $T$);
(2) Any line through point $T$, intersecting $\odot O_{i}$ $(i=1,2,3)$ at points $M_{i}$, and the perpendiculars from $M_{1}, M_{2}, M_{3}$ to $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, intersect at one point. ${ }^{[1]}$
(2010, Greek Mathematical Olympiad)

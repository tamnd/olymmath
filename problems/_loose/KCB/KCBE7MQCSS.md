A positive integer $ n$ is called $ good$ if it can be uniquely written simultaneously as $ a_1+a_2+...+a_k$ and as $ a_1 a_2...a_k$ , where $ a_i$ are positive integers and $ k \ge 2$ . (For example, $ 10$ is good because $ 10=5+2+1+1+1=5 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 1$ is a unique expression of this form). Find, in terms of prime numbers, all good natural numbers.

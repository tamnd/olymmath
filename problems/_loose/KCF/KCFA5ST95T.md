A king wants to build a palace with 1990 rooms on one level and such that the following conditions are met:

(i) the number of doors in each room is 0, 1, or 2,

(ii) there is at most one door between any two rooms; from each room to the street, there is at most one door,

(iii) the number of rooms with a door to the street is 19, and the number of rooms with exactly one door is 90.

Is it possible to build such a palace?

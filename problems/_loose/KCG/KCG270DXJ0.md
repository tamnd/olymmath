Let $F_{n}=a^{n} \sin n A+b^{n} \sin n B+c^{n} \sin n C$, where $a, b, c, A, B, C$ are real numbers, and $A+B+C=k \pi$, for some $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that from $F_{0}=F_{1}=0$ it follows that for every natural number $n$ we have $F_{n}=0$.

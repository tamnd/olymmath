(Adapted from the 2004 Jiangsu Province College Entrance Examination for Science) Given the function $ f(x) (x \in \mathbf{R}) $ satisfies the following conditions: for any real numbers $ x_{1}, x_{2} $, we have $\lambda\left(x_{1}-x_{2}\right)^{2} \leqslant\left(x_{1}-x_{2}\right)\left[f\left(x_{1}\right)-f\left(x_{2}\right)\right]$ and $\left|f\left(x_{1}\right)-f\left(x_{2}\right)\right| \leqslant \mid x_{1}-x_{2} \mid$, where $\lambda$ is a real number greater than 0.
Let real numbers $ a_{i}, a, b $ satisfy $ f\left(a_{0}\right)=0 $ and $ b=a-\lambda f(a) $.

Prove: $[f(b)]^{2} \leqslant\left(1-\lambda^{2}\right)[f(a)]^{2}$.

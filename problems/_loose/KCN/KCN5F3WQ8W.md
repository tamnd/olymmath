Diophantine system * ${ }^{*}$. Prove that the system

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
a+b+c & =x+y \\
a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3} & =x^{3}+y^{3}
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

has infinitely many solutions in integers, with the property that $a, b, c$ form an arithmetic progression.

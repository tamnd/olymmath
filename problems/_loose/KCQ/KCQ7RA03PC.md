Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ both satisfy the recurrence relation $x_{n}+b_{1} x_{n-1}+b_{2} x_{n-2}=0$, prove that
(1) $\left\{a_{n} b_{n}\right\}$ satisfies a third-order homogeneous linear recurrence relation with constant coefficients;
(2) $a_{0}, a_{2}, a_{1}, \cdots$ satisfies a second-order homogeneous linear recurrence relation with constant coefficients.

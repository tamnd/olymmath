Let the three excircles of $\triangle ABC$ touch the sides $BC, CA, AB$ at points $A', B'$, and $C'$, respectively. The circumcircles of $\triangle A' B' C$, $\triangle A B' C'$, and $\triangle A' B C'$ intersect the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ again at points $C_1, A_1$, and $B_1$, respectively. Prove that $\triangle A_1 B_1 C_1$ is similar to the triangle formed by the points where the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ touches its sides.

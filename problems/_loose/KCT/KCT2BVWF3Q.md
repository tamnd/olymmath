Let $a$ and $b$ be natural numbers, with $a > b$ and $\operatorname{GCD}(a, b) = d$. Prove that if the Euclidean algorithm applied to $a$ and $b$ stops after $n$ steps, then $a \geqslant d F_{n+2}$ and $b \geqslant d F_{n+1}$.

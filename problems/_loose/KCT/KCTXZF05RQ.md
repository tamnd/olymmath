In $\triangle A B C$, $A C>B C, F$ is the midpoint of $A B$, and through $F$ a diameter $D E$ of its circumcircle is drawn, such that $C$ and $E$ are on the same side of $A B$. A line parallel to $A B$ through $C$ intersects $D E$ at $L$. Prove: $(A C+B C)^{2}=4 D L \cdot E F$.

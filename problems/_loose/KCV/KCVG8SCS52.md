There were $m$ participants in a meeting $(m\ge 3)$ sitting at a round table. During the meeting, they ate sweets, and each of them ate at least one sweet. Moreover, each of them ate either eight times as many sweets as his neighbor sitting on his right, or twenty-four sweets less than that neighbor. For any value of $m$ , could they all eat
a) less than $1000$ sweets?
b) exactly $2020$ sweets?

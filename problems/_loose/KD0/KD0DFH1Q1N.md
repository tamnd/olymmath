For a $100 \times 100$ checkerboard, the top edge was glued to the bottom edge, and the right edge to the left edge, after which the board took the shape of a donut. Can 50 rooks - red, blue, and green - be placed in its cells such that each red rook attacks at least two blue rooks, each blue rook attacks at least two green rooks, and each green rook attacks at least two red rooks?

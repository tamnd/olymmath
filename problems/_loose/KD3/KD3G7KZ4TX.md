Given $\triangle A B C$ with area $S$, and the incircle, the excircle opposite to $\angle A$, the excircle opposite to $\angle B$, and the excircle opposite to $\angle C$ touch the sides of the triangle at points forming triangles $\triangle D E F$, $\triangle X Y Z$, $\triangle P Q R$, and $\triangle L M N$ respectively. Prove:
$$
S_{\triangle X Y Z}+S_{\triangle P Q R}+S_{\triangle L M N}-S_{\triangle D E F}=2 S .
$$

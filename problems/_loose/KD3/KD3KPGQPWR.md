Let an be a sequence such that $a_0 = 0$ and: $a_{3n+1} = a_{3n} + 1 = a_n + 1$ $a_{3n+2} = a_{3n} + 2 = a_n + 2$ for all natural numbers $n$ . How many $n$ less than $2012$ have the property that $a_n = 7$ ?

Find all natural numbers $n(n \geqslant 2)$, such that there exist real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, making $A=\left\{\mid a_{i}-a_{j}\right\}$ $\mid 1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n\}$ have exactly $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ elements.

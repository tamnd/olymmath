Ivan the Tsarevich has two vessels with a capacity of 1 liter each, one of which is completely filled with ordinary water, and the other contains $a$ liters of living water,

$0<a<1$. He can pour any volume of liquid from one vessel to another to any level without overflowing and wants to obtain a 40% solution of living water in one of the vessels in a finite number of such pourings. For which values of $a$ can Ivan the Tsarevich do this? Assume that the level of liquid in each vessel can be accurately measured at any time.

Let 1998 points be chosen on the plane so that out of any 17 it is possible to choose 11 that lie inside a circle of diameter 1. Find the smallest number of circles of diameter 2 sufficient to cover all 1998 points.

(We say that a circle covers a certain number of points if all points lie inside the circle or on its outline.)

On an $8 \times 8$ board, several dominoes (i.e., $2 \times 1$ rectangles) can be placed without overlapping each other. Let $N$ be the number of ways to place 32 dominoes, and $S$ be the number of ways to place 16 dominoes. Which is greater, $N$ or $S$? Ways that can be transformed into each other by rotation or reflection of the board are considered different.

Let's prove that in every convex quadrilateral, the intersection point $U$ of the diagonals, the intersection point $V$ of the lines connecting the midpoints of the opposite sides, and the centroid $W$ of the quadrilateral area lie on one line, and $W V: V U=1: 3$.

(The centroid of the quadrilateral area lies on the line connecting the centroids of the two triangles formed by one of the diagonals.)

(A quadrilateral's area centroid is on the line connecting the centroids of the two triangles formed by one of the diagonals.)

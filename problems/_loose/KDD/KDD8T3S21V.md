The coefficients of the polynomial $f(x)$ are integers, in absolute value not exceeding 5000000. Moreover, each of the equations

$$
f(x)=x, f(x)=2 x, \ldots, f(x)=20 x
$$

has an integer root. Prove that $f(0)=0$.

(M. Antipov)

The larger root of the equation $(1984 x)^{2}-1983 \cdot 1985 x-1=0$ is $r$, and the smaller root of $1983 x^{2}-1984 x+1=0$ is $s$, then $r-s$ equals
(A) $\frac{1984}{1983}$.
(B) $\frac{1985}{1984}$.
(C) $\frac{1982}{1983}$.
(D) $\frac{1983}{1984}$.
(E) 0 .

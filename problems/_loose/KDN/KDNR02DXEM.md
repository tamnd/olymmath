We place red and blue balls on a circle. Initially, the circle contains only two red balls. The following moves are allowed:

i) add one red ball and change the color of each of its two neighboring balls (red to blue and vice versa);

ii) remove one red ball and change the color of each of its two neighboring balls.

Can we achieve the following by a series of such moves:
a) 2013 red and 2013 blue balls;

b) only two blue balls?

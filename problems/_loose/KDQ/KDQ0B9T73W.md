Let the center of the circumscribed circle of the equilateral triangle $ABC$ be $O$, and the other endpoint of the diameter starting from point $A$ be $D$. Draw the (smaller than a semicircle) arcs with centers at $A$ and $D$, respectively, passing through $B$ and $C$. Let one of the points of these two arcs be $P$, and the other be $Q$. Prove that if points $P$ and $Q$ are equidistant from the side $BC$, then they are also equidistant from the point $O$.

\section*{Task 2 - 131042}

Construct a right-angled triangle $A B C$ with the right angle at $C$ from $s_{a}=6 \mathrm{~cm}, s_{b}$ $=8 \mathrm{~cm}!$

Here, $s_{a}$ is the length of the median from $B C$ and $s_{b}$ is the length of the median from $A C$.

Describe and justify your construction. Investigate whether a triangle $A B C$ with the given lengths $s_{a}, s_{b}$ exists and is uniquely determined up to congruence!

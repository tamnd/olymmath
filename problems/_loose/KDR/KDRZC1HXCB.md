A secant line through the centroid $G$ of a triangle intersects the sides of the triangle or their extensions at points $P, Q, R$ respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{G P}=\frac{1}{G Q}+\frac{1}{G R},
$$

if points $Q$ and $R$ lie on the same side of $G$.

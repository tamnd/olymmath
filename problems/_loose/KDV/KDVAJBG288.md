Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, passing through the point $P\left(3, \frac{16}{5}\right)$, with an eccentricity of $\frac{3}{5}$. A line $l$ with slope $k$ is drawn through the right focus of the ellipse $C$, intersecting the ellipse at points $A$ and $B$. The slopes of $P A$ and $P B$ are denoted as $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively.
(1) Find the standard equation of the ellipse;
(2) If $k_{1}+k_{2}=0$, find the real number $k$.

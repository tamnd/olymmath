Factorial number system. Prove that each natural number $n$ can be uniquely represented in the form

$$
n=a_{1} \cdot 1!+a_{2} \cdot 2!+a_{3} \cdot 3!+\ldots
$$

where $0 \leqslant a_{1} \leqslant 1,0 \leqslant a_{2} \leqslant 2,0 \leqslant a_{3} \leqslant 3 \ldots$

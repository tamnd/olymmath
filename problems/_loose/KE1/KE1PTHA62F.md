On two square grids of size $1982 \times 1983$, each small square is painted either red or blue, such that each row and each column contains an even number of blue squares. When these two grids are placed on top of each other, it is found that one blue square overlaps with a red square. Prove that there are at least 3 more small squares where two squares of different colors overlap.

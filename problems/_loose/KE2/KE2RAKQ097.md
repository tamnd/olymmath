Does there exist a sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, such that $a_{2013}=2013$, and for each $k \in\{2,3, \cdots, 2013\}$, we have $\left|a_{k}-a_{k-1}\right|=20$ or 13; and the terms $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2013}$ form a permutation of $1,2, \cdots, 2013$? Prove your conclusion.
(2013, National High School Mathematics League Jiangxi Province Preliminary)

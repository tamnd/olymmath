Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers that satisfy the equations
$$
\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{a}=\frac{5}{2} \text { and } a-b=\frac{3}{2} \text {. }
$$

Find all possible values of $a^{2}+2 a b+b^{2}+2 a^{2} b+2 a b^{2}+a^{2} b^{2}$.

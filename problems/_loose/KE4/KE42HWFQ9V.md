Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocentre $H$ and circumcircle $\Gamma$ . Let $D$ be the point
diametrically opposite $A$ on $\Gamma$ . The line through $H$ , parallel to $BC$ , intersects $AB$ and $AC$ at $X$ and $Y$ , respectively. Let $AD$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $DXY$ again at $S$ . Let the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $A$ intersect $XY$ at $T$ . Prove that lines $DT$ and $HS$ intersect on $\Gamma$ .

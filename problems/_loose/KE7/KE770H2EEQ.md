Show that for any integer $r \geq 2$, there exists an $r$-chromatic graph that does not have cycles of length strictly less than 6.

(A graph is called $r$-chromatic if $r$ is the minimum number of colors needed to color the vertices such that any two adjacent vertices have different colors.)

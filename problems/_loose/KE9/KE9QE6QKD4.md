In the plane, there are two parallel lines $a$ and $b$, and a point $M$ that does not lie on $a$. For every position of a line rotating around $M$, we find its intersection point $A$ with $a$. Let the perpendicular to $a$ at $A$ intersect $b$ at $B$, and draw the line through $B$ that is perpendicular to $MA$. Prove that every such line is tangent to the same parabola.

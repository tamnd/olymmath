Prove that the composition of symmetries with respect to two parallel planes is a vector (parallel translation) directed perpendicular to these planes from the first to the second, and its length is equal to twice the distance between the planes.

Prove the converse statement: any parallel translation can be represented as the composition of two symmetries with respect to planes. How to construct such planes?

In a scalene triangle $ABC$, the length of the altitude drawn from vertex $C$ to side $AB$ is equal to the sum of the lengths of the other two altitudes. Express the length of side $AB$ as a function of the lengths of the other two sides. Then prove that there are no triangles that satisfy the given condition and have the length of side $BC$ equal to 6, and the lengths of the other two sides expressed as natural numbers.

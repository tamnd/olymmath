15 seats are arranged at equal distances around a round table, and 15 guests' name cards are placed on the table. The guests do not notice the name cards until they are seated and then realize that no one is sitting in front of their own name card.
Prove: The round table can be rotated to a certain position so that at least two guests are sitting in front of their own name cards.

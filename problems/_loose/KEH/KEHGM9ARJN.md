Let $\xi_{n} \xrightarrow{p} \xi, \zeta_{n} \xrightarrow{p} \zeta$ and the random variables $\xi$ and $\zeta$ are equivalent. Show that then for any $\varepsilon>0$ the condition

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(\left|\xi_{n}-\zeta_{n}\right| \geqslant \varepsilon\right) \rightarrow 0, n \rightarrow \infty
$$

holds.

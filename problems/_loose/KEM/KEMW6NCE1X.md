A certain commodity, originally priced at $p$ yuan per piece, will sell $n$ pieces per month. If the price increases by $x$ percent (here $x$ percent means $\frac{x}{10}, 0<x \leqslant 10$), the monthly sales volume will decrease by $y$ percent, and the sales revenue will become $z$ times the original. (1) Let $y=a x$, where $a$ is a constant satisfying $\frac{1}{3} \leqslant a<1$, express the value of $x$ when the sales revenue is maximized in terms of $a$; (2) If $y=\frac{2}{3} x$, find the range of $x$ values that will increase the sales revenue compared to the original.

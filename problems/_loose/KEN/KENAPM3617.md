Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is circumscribed around circle $\omega$, and the lines containing sides $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $O$. Circle $\omega_{1}$ is tangent to side $BC$ at point $K$, and is also tangent to the lines containing sides $AB$ and $CD$. Circle $\omega_{2}$ is tangent to side $AD$ at point $L$, and is also tangent to the lines containing sides $AB$ and $CD$. It is known that points $O, K, L$ are collinear. Prove that the midpoints of sides $BC$ and $AD$, and the center of circle $\omega$ are collinear.
(26th Russian Competition Problem)

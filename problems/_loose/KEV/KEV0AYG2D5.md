a) An ant is at vertex $A$ of a rectangle $ABCD$ and moves towards $B$ along the side of the rectangle. After a second movement, it goes in the same way to $C$. After three movements, in the same manner, it reaches $D$ and on the fourth, maintaining the path along the side, it returns to $A$. After 2018 similar movements to the previous ones, at which vertex will the ant be?

b) Ants Albormiga and Clarormiga start from vertices $A$ and $C$ of a rectangle, respectively, moving with constant speeds (even during the $90^{\circ}$ turns at each vertex). The first one moves clockwise and the second one counterclockwise. From $A$ to the first meeting, Albormiga traveled 360 meters. From the first to the second meeting, Clarormiga walked 300 meters. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?

c) Three ants are located at distinct vertices of a rectangle, leaving the other vertex free. An ant can move only along a line parallel to the line determined by the points where the other two ants are located, moving one at a time. Is it possible, after several movements of the ants, for all of them to be at the midpoint of three distinct sides of the rectangle?

Let $f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+a_{2} x^{2}+\ldots+a_{n} x^{n}, a_{i}>0(i=0,1,2, \ldots n)$, and in the interval $[-1,+1]$, $|f(x)| \leqq 1$. Show that in this interval $\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right| \leqq n$; the equality holds only when $f(x)=x^{n}$.

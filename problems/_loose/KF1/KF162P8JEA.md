The first section passes through the edge $A B$ and the midpoint of the edge $C C_{1}$, while the second section passes through the edge $A_{1} B_{1}$ and the midpoint of the edge $C B$. Find the ratio of the length of the segment of the line of intersection of these sections, contained within the prism, to the length of the edge $A B$.

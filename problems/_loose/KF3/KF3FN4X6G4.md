If given a cubic equation:

$$
x^{3}-(a+b+c) x^{2}+(a b+a c+b c) x=a b c
$$

then $a, b$ and $c$ are the roots of this equation.

Check on the equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \text { 1) } x^{3}-6 x^{2}+11 x-6=0 \\
& \text { 2) } x^{3}-4 x^{2}-4 x+16=0
\end{aligned}
$$

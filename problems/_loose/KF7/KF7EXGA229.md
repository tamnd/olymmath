A father, accompanied by his sons Fritz and Heinz, bought a suit in a department store that had a sign on it reading: "reduced in price by $20 \%$."

On the way home, Heinz said: "Dad, you saved $25 \%$ of the price you paid." Fritz, who doubted this remark, asked his father: "Is that true?"

To this, the father explained: "That's correct. If the price of the suit had only been reduced by 10\%, I would have saved only $11 \frac{1}{9} \%$ of the price I paid."

Prove that these statements are true regardless of the specific value of the price before the price reduction!

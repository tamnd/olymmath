The sequence $x_1,x_2, ...$ is defined by the following equations: $$ x_1=19, \ \ x_2=97, \ \ x_{n+2} =x_n - \frac{1}{x_{n+1}} $$ for $n \ge 1$ . Prove that there exists a positive integer $k$ such that $x_k=0$ and find $k$ .

(A Berzinsh)

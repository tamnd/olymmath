(26th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) On a rectangular table, many equal squares are placed, whose sides are parallel to the edges of the table and are colored in $k$ different colors. If any $k$ squares of different colors are considered, then there are always two of them that can be caught on the table with one hook. Prove: It is possible to catch all the squares of one color on the table with $2 k-2$ hooks.

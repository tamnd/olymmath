In an $8 \times 8$ grid, each cell contains a knight or a knave. Knights always tell the truth, and knaves always lie. It is known that each resident in the grid says: "The number of knaves in my column is (strictly) greater than the number of knaves in my row." Determine the number of arrangements of residents that satisfy this condition.

Let $A, B, P, Q, R$ be points in a plane. Prove that

$$
\overline{A B}+\overline{P Q}+\overline{Q R}+\overline{R P} \leq \overline{A P}+\overline{A Q}+\overline{A R}+\overline{B P}+\overline{B Q}+\overline{B R}
$$

( $\overline{X Y}$ denotes the distance between points $X, Y$.)

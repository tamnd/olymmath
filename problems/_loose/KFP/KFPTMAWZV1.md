Let non-negative random variables $\xi, \eta$ be such that

$$
\mathrm{E} \xi^{p} \leqslant \mathrm{E} \xi^{q} \eta \text { for some } p>q>1 .
$$

Prove that

$$
\mathrm{E} \xi^{p} \leqslant \mathrm{E} \eta^{\frac{p}{p-q}}
$$

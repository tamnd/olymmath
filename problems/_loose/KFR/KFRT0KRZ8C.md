A divisor of a natural number is called proper if it is less than the number but greater than 1. For a natural number $ n $, all proper divisors (there were at least three of them) were found and all possible pairwise sums of these divisors were recorded (repeated identical sums were not recorded). Prove that the resulting set could not be the set of all proper divisors of any natural number. (A. Khryabrov)

Given that $a_{n}$ is the real root of the equation $x^{3}+\frac{x}{2^{n}}=1$ $\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$. Prove:
（1）the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is monotonically increasing;
(2) $\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k\left(2^{k}+1\right) a_{k}}<a_{n}$.

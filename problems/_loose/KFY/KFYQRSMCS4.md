In a trapezoid $A B C D$ with $A B \| C D$ and $B C=D A$, let two points $E$ and $F$ be fixed on $B C$ and $D A$ such that $E C=F A$.

Prove: $E F$ is minimal if and only if $E F$ is the midline.

In a certain country, two villages, A and B, were located close to each other. The inhabitants of village A always told the truth, while the inhabitants of B always lied. A tourist traveling through this country found himself in one of these villages. To find out which one, the tourist could ask the first person he met a question, to which the person would answer "yes" or "no." However, this person could be either a resident of A or B (they could visit each other). What is the smallest number of questions and what kind of questions should the tourist ask the first person he meets to determine which village he is in and from which village the person he met is from?

We consider the solutions of the equation $1 / x + 1 / y = 1 / p (p > 1)$, where $x, y$, and $p$ are natural numbers. Prove that if $p$ is a prime number, then the equation has exactly three solutions; if $p$ is composite, then there are more than three solutions $((a, b)$ and $(b, a)$ are different solutions if $a \neq b)$.

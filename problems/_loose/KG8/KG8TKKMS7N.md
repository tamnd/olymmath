Let $n \ge 3$ be an odd integer such that $\gcd ( p - 1, n) = 1$ for every prime divisor $p$ of $n$ . Find the number of $(a,b,c) \in \Bbb Z_n^3$ in terms of $n$ such that $\gcd (a,b,c,n) = 1$ and there is a permutation $\left(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\right)$ of $\left\{1,2,\dots,n \right\}$ satisfying that $n \mid ax_{k + 2} + bx_{k + 1} + cx_k$ for $1 \le k \le n$ , where indices are taken module $n$ .

Let $a_{k} \in \mathbf{R}, z_{k} \in \mathbf{C}, k=1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove that:
$$
\left|\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k} z_{k}\right|^{2} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{2}\right)\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left|z_{k}\right|^{2}+\left|\sum_{k=1}^{n} z_{k}^{2} .\right|\right) .
$$

(BXMO 2012) Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$. Let $P$ be a point inside the triangle $A B C$ such that $\widehat{C P M}=\widehat{P A B}$. Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of triangle $P A B$. Let $Q$ be the second point of intersection of the line $(P M)$ with the circle $\Gamma$. Let $R$ be the symmetric point of $P$ with respect to the tangent to the circle $\Gamma$ at $B$. Show that as $P$ varies, the distance $Q R$ remains constant.

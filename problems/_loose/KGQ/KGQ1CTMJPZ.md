a) Let A be a finite, non-empty set and $f: A \rightarrow A$ an injective function. Show that f is surjective.

b) Let $f:\{1,2,3, \ldots, 63\} \rightarrow\{1,2,3, \ldots, 63\}$ be a function such that $f(1)+f(2)+f(3)+\cdots+f(63)-1=2016$. Show that f is not injective.

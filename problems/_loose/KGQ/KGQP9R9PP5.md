A deltoid is a convex quadrilateral with a single axis of symmetry. Deltoid $ABCD$ is symmetric with respect to the diagonal $AC$, with side $AB$ of length $5 \text{~cm}$, side $BC$ of length $3 \text{~cm}$, and angle $BCD$ of size $60^{\circ}$. Point $E$ is the foot of the perpendicular from vertex $B$ to side $AD$, and $F$ is the foot of the perpendicular from vertex $D$ to side $BC$.

Determine the perimeter and area of quadrilateral $DEBF$.

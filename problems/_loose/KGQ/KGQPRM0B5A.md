Let $E_{1}=\frac{a(2 a+8)+8}{(a+4)(2 a+12)-16}$ and $E_{2}=\frac{a(a+4)+4}{a(a+1)-2}$.

a) Determine $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that the expressions make sense;

b) Determine $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ for which $\frac{1}{E_{1}}+\frac{1}{E_{2}} \in \mathbb{N}$;

c) Calculate $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $\frac{1}{E_{1}}+\frac{1}{E_{2}}=\frac{3 a+10}{a+2}$.

$$
\left[\frac{3 x^{2}+1}{3}\right]-\left[\frac{2 x^{2}+1}{2}\right]+\left[\frac{3 x^{2}+2}{3}\right]=0
$$

where $[x]$ represents the integer part of the real number $x$.

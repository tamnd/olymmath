Given three pairwise parallel lines $a, b$ and $c$, not lying in the same plane. On the first, $\left[A A^{\prime}\right]$ is given, on the second $\left[B B^{\prime}\right]$, and on the third $\left[C C^{\prime}\right]$. Calculate the volume of the polyhedron $A B C A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, bounded by the triangles $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ and three trapezoids with bases $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}$, if the lengths $\left|A A^{\prime}\right|=m, \left|B B^{\prime}\right|=n, \left|C C^{\prime}\right|=p$ and the area $S$ of the triangle with vertices at the points of intersection of the lines $a, b$ and $c$ with a plane perpendicular to them are given. Show that this volume will not change if the segments $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}$ are moved along the lines $a, b, c$, preserving their length.

Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots$ be a sequence of independent random variables, $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_{i}$. Show that $\left(S_{1}, \ldots, S_{n-1}\right)$ and $\left(S_{n+1}, S_{n+2}, \ldots\right)$ are conditionally independent given the $\sigma$-algebra $\sigma\left(S_{n}\right)$.

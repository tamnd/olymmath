(Kuri, Belgium, 83). In the hexagon $A B C D E F$, the angles at vertices $A, C, E$ are equal and do not exceed $180^{\circ}$, and

$$
\angle A B F=\angle C B D, \angle A F B=\angle E F D
$$

Prove that if point $A^{\prime}$ is symmetric to vertex $A$ with respect to the diagonal $B F$ and does not lie on the line $C E$, then the quadrilateral $A^{\prime} C D E$ is a parallelogram.

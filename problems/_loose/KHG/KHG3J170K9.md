Divide a circle into $n(n \geqslant 2)$ sectors, sequentially denoted as $s_{1}, s_{2}, \cdots, s_{n}$. Each sector can be painted with any of the three different colors: red, white, and blue, with the requirement that adjacent sectors must have different colors. How many ways are there to color the sectors?
(1989 Zhejiang Province Competition Question)

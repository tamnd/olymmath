a) Where and how long a rope did he tie the goat to so that it can graze the most grass without crossing the boundary? Mark it on a sketch to scale $1: 200$.

b) How many square meters of grass did the goat graze? Round the result to one decimal place.

c) What percentage of the lawn could the goat not graze? Round the result to one decimal place.

A line $l$ passes through the point $P(1,0)$ and intersects the curve $\Gamma$ at points $A$ and $B$, and intersects the imaginary axis at point $M$. If $\overrightarrow{M A}=t \overrightarrow{A P}$ and $\overrightarrow{M B}=s \overrightarrow{B P}$, find the value of $t+s$.

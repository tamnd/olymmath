Let $a, b, c$ be three positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=1$.

Show that

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{\frac{b+c}{2}+\sqrt{b c}}+\frac{b^{2}}{\frac{c+a}{2}+\sqrt{c a}}+\frac{c^{2}}{\frac{a+b}{2}+\sqrt{a b}} \geq \frac{1}{2}
$$

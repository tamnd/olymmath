Let $Q_{n}$ denote the number of permutations of $1,2, \cdots, n(n \geqslant 2)$ that do not contain consecutive pairs. Prove:
(1) $Q_{n}=(n+1) D_{n-1}+(-1)^{n}$.
(2) $Q_{n}=\frac{1}{n} D_{n+1}$.

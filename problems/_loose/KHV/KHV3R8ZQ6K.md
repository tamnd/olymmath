Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral. For a given point $O$ inside it, let $p_{O}$ be the line through $O$ parallel to $A B$, and let $P_{O}$ and $Q_{O}$ be the points of intersection of the line $p_{O}$ with the sides $A D$ and $B C$, respectively; similarly, let $q_{O}$ be the line through $O$ parallel to $C D$, and let $R_{O}$ and $S_{O}$ be the points of intersection of the line $q_{O}$ with the sides $A D$ and $B C$, respectively. Prove that the following conditions are equivalent:

- there exists a point $O \in \operatorname{int} A B C D$ such that $P_{O} O \cdot O Q_{O}=R_{O} O \cdot O S_{O}$;
- for every point $O \in \operatorname{int} A B C D$, $P_{O} O \cdot O Q_{O}=R_{O} O \cdot O S_{O}$.

(We denote the interior of the quadrilateral $A B C D$ by int $A B C D$.)

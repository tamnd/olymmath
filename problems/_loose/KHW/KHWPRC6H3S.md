A right-angled triangle $ABC\left(m \nless A=90^{\circ}\right)$ has legs $AB=3 m, AC=4 m$. At $A$, a perpendicular is raised to the plane $(ABC)$, and a segment $AD=1 m$ is taken on it. Find:
a) $d(A,(DBC))$;

b) The areas of triangles $DAB, DAC, DBC, ABC$;
c) $\sin (\angle((DBC);(ABC)))$.

On an island, there are 1111 residents, and the 11 members of the national football team distribute their autographs to these residents. It is known that each resident receives 1 or 0 autographs from each player.
(1) In how many different ways can all the players distribute their autographs to the residents?
(2) After distributing the autographs, it is found that no two residents have received exactly the same set of autographs. Prove: There are two residents whose combined set of autographs includes exactly one autograph from each player.

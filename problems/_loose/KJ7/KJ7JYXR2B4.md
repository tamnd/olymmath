There are 1000 representatives from different countries attending a conference, each of whom speaks several languages. It is known that any 3 representatives can communicate with each other without the help of others (it is possible that one of the 3 acts as a translator for the other two). Prove that all representatives can be assigned to 500 rooms, with two people in each room, such that the two people in each room can communicate with each other.

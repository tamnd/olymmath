Given 10 numbers: $10, 20, 30, \ldots, 100$. With them, you can perform the following operation: choose any three and add one to each of the chosen numbers. The same operation is then performed on the resulting 10 numbers, and so on. Can you, after several operations, obtain: a) all the same numbers? b) all numbers equal to 200?

In trapezoid $A B C D$, $A B / / C D$, and $\angle A+\angle B=90^{\circ}$. If $A B=$ $a, C D=b$, and $E, F$ are the midpoints of $A B, C D$ respectively, then $E F$ equals
(A) $\frac{1}{2}(a+b)$.
(B) $\frac{1}{2}(a-b)$.
(C) $\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}$.
(D) $\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}$.
("Zu Chongzhi Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

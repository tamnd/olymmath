The lower base of a frustum is the triangle $ABC$, with side edges $AA_{1}, BB_{1}, CC_{1}$. The plane $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ has an arbitrary point $P$. We construct a plane through the edge $B_{1}C_{1}$ parallel to the plane $BCP$, a plane through the edge $C_{1}A_{1}$ parallel to the plane $CAP$, and a plane through the edge $A_{1}B_{1}$ parallel to the plane $ABP$. The common point of these three planes is $Q$. Prove that the volumes of the pyramid $ABB_{1}A_{1}C$ and the tetrahedron $ABCQ$ are equal!

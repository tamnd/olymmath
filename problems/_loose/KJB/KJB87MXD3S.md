$O C$ and $O D$ are conjugate semi-diameters of an ellipse, not perpendicular to each other. Show that the axes of the ellipse can be constructed in the following steps.

1. Draw a perpendicular to $O D$ through $C$, and measure the length of $O D$ from $C$ in both directions, with endpoints $E$ and $F$.
2. The two angle bisectors of the pair of lines $O E, O F$ give the lines of the axes.
3. Measure $O F$ from $O$ on $O E$, with the endpoint $G$.
4. The midpoint of the segment $E G$ is $H$; then the length of the semi-major axis is $O H$, and the length of the semi-minor axis is $G H$.
5. Measure $O H$ along the angle bisectors that pass through the acute angles between $O C$ and $O D$, and $G H$ along the bisectors that pass through the other angles, to obtain the endpoints of the axes.

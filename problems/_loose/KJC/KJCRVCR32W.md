Let $M$ be an arbitrary point on the semicircle with diameter $AB = 2R$, and let $H$ be the orthogonal projection of $M$ onto $AB$. Write the sum $y = 2AM + 3HB$ as a function of $AM = x$. For which $x$ is this sum equal to a given length $l$? (Discussion). Write down the variation of $y$ and plot the function. Show that the question of the common points of the curve and the line $y = l$ is a visual equivalent to the previous discussion. Let $M_1$ be the position of $M$ for which $y$ is maximum. Calculate the trigonometric functions of the angle $AOM_1$. ($O$ is the midpoint of segment $AB$).

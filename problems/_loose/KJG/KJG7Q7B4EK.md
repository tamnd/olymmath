Let $ p, q $ be given coprime natural numbers. Prove that if the equation

$$
p x^{2}-(p+q) x+p=0
$$

has an integer root, then the equation

$$
p x^{2}+q x+p^{2}-q=0
$$

also has an integer root. (Patrik Bak)

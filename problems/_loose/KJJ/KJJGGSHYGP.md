Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be $n$ non-negative real numbers $\left(n>2, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=n, \sum_{i=1}^{n} i x_{i}=2 n-2$, find the maximum value of the expression $S=\sum_{i=1}^{n} i^{2} x_{i}$.

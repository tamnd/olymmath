On the arc $A_{1} A_{2 n+1}$ of the circumscribed circle $S$ of a regular $(2 n+1)$-gon $A_{1} \ldots A_{2 n+1}$, a point $A$ is taken. Prove that:

a) $d_{1}+d_{3}+\ldots+d_{2 n+1}=d_{2}+d_{4}+\ldots+d_{2 n}$, where $d_{i}=A A_{i}$
b) $l_{1}+\ldots+l_{2 n+1}=l_{2}+\ldots+l_{2 n}$, where $l_{i}$ is the length of the tangent drawn from point $A$ to a circle of radius $r$ that is tangent to $S$ at point $A_{i}$ (all tangencies are either internal or external simultaneously).

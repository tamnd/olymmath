Günter spent a number of days in a camp with his FDJ group during his vacation. Every day, exactly two students from his group were assigned to table duty in the morning and exactly two students in the afternoon. Over the days, all students in his group were assigned to this table duty equally often.

Furthermore, the following is known:

(1) Günter was assigned to table duty on exactly 6 days.

(2) If he had table duty in the afternoon, he did not have it in the morning.

(3) He did not have table duty in the afternoon on exactly 13 of these days.

(4) He did not have table duty in the morning on exactly 11 of these days.

How many students were in Günter's group?

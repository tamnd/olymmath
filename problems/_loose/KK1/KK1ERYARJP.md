$2 A B C D$ is a convex quadrilateral, $I_{1} 、 I_{2}$ are the incenters of $\triangle A B C 、 \triangle D B C$ respectively, the line through points $I_{1} 、 I_{2}$ intersects $A B 、 D C$ at points $E 、 F$ respectively, extend $A B$ and $D C$, they intersect at point $P$, and $P E=P F$, prove: $A 、 B 、 C 、 D$ are concyclic.

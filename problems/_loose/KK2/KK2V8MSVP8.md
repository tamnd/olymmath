A country has $N(\geqslant 3)$ cities, and between every two cities, there is either a road or a railway. A traveler hopes to visit each city exactly once and return to the starting city. Prove: the traveler can choose a city as the starting point, not only to fulfill his wish, but also to change the mode of transportation at most once during the journey.

In a triangle with side lengths $a, b, c$, circumradius $R$, and area $S_{\triangle}$, prove:
(1) $R \leqslant \frac{1}{32 S_{\triangle}}(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)$;
(2) $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c} \geq \frac{\sqrt{3}}{R}$;
(3) $3(a b+b c+c a) \leqslant(a+b+c)^{2}<4(a b+b c+c a)$;
(4) $(s-a)^{4}+(s-b)^{4}+(s-c)^{4} \geqslant S_{\triangle}^{2}$ (where, $\left.s=\frac{1}{2}(a+b+c)\right)$;
(5) $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c} \geqslant \frac{9}{p}$ (where, $p=a+b+c$, the same applies below);
(6) $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \geqslant \frac{1}{3} p^{2}$;
(7) $p^{2} \geq 12 \sqrt{3} S_{\triangle}$;
(8) $a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3} \geq \frac{1}{9} p^{3}$;
(9) $a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3} \geqslant \frac{4 \sqrt{3}}{3} S_{\triangle} \cdot p$;
(10) $a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4} \geqslant 16 S_{\triangle}^{2}$.

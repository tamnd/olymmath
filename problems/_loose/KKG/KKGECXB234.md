Suppose that in each issue of our magazine in the "Kvant" problem section, there will be five math problems. Let $f(x, y)$ denote the number of the first problem in the $x$-th issue of the $y$-th year. Write a general formula for $f(x, y)$, where $1 \leq x \leq 12$ and $1970 \leq y \leq 1989$. Solve the equation $f(x, y)=y$.

For example, $f(6,1970)=26$. Starting from 1989, the number of problems became less predictable. For example, in recent years, half of the issues have 5 problems, and the other half have 10. And the number of issues of the magazine is now 6 instead of 12.

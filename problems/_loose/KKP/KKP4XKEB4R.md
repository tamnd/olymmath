a) Given a parallelogram $A B C D$. Prove that the value $A X^{2}+C X^{2}-B X^{2}-D X^{2}$ does not depend on the choice of point $X$.

b) Quadrilateral $A B C D$ is not a parallelogram. Prove that all points $X$ satisfying the relation $A X^{2}+C X^{2}=B X^{2}+D X^{2}$ lie on a single line perpendicular to the segment connecting the midpoints of the diagonals.

See also problems $6.17,15.15$.

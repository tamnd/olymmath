In the city of Ikozapolisz, the road network graph matches the graph of the edge network of an icosahedron. Jorgosz's lodging is located at one vertex of the icosahedron, while his favorite theater is at the opposite vertex. After dark, when leaving the theater and heading home, he becomes uncertain at each vertex about which direction to take next. Suppose that at each vertex, the probability $p$ is that he encounters someone who points him in the direction that will take him to his lodging with the fewest edges. Otherwise, he continues in a random direction, with no direction being favored, meaning it is even possible for him to turn back. For what value of $p$ will there be a $50 \%$ probability that he reaches his lodging before returning to the theater?

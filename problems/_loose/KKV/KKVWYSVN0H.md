Which of the following statements are true and which are false?
1) For all numbers $x$ and $y$, $x=2 y$.
2) For every number $x$, there exists a number $y$ such that $x=2 y$.
3) For every number $y$, there exists a number $x$ such that $x=2 y$.
4) There exists a number $x$ such that for all numbers $y$, $x=2 y$.
5) There exists a number $y$ such that for all numbers $x$, $x=2 y$.
6) There exist numbers $x$ and $y$ such that $x=2 y$. (Here and in the next problem, $x$ and $y$ are real numbers.)

There are 40 red points, 30 blue points, and 20 green points on a circle. The circle is divided into 90 arcs, and each arc is labeled with a number based on the colors of its endpoints: red-blue arcs are labeled 1, red-green arcs are labeled 2, blue-green arcs are labeled 3, and arcs with endpoints of the same color are labeled 0. Find the maximum and minimum values of the sum of all the numbers.
$(2016$, National Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition)

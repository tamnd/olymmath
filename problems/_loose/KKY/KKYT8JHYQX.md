Let in the complex plane the points $A, B, C$ and $D$ correspond to the complex numbers $z_{j}, j=1,2,3,4$ such that $\left|z_{j}\right|=1, j=1,2,3,4$ and

$$
z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}+z_{4}=0
$$

Prove that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a rectangle.

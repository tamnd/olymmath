It is known that on the planet XMO there are one hundred million cities. Initially, there are no flight routes between the cities. Now, an airline plans to establish 5050 two-way routes, each connecting two different cities, and no two routes will connect the same pair of cities. The "prosperity" of each city is defined as the number of routes originating from that city, and the "revenue" of each route is the product of the prosperity of the two cities it connects.
Find the maximum possible value of the sum of the revenues of these 5050 routes.

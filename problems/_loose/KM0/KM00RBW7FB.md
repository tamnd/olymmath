In the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, there is a point $M(P, 0)$, and $0<P<a$, and a moving point $Q$ on the ellipse. The vector $\overrightarrow{M Q}$ rotates $90^{\circ}$ clockwise around point $M$ to reach $\overrightarrow{M P}$. Try to find the equation of the trajectory of point $P$.

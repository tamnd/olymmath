If the family of subsets $\triangle$ of the $n$-element set $X=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$ is an $S$ family, then the number of elements in $\mathcal{A}$ is at most $C_{n}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]}$, that is,
$$
\max _{\text {S family }}|\mathscr{A}|=C_{n}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]} \text {. }
$$

This is the theorem discovered by Sperner (1905) in 1928 (S family is the abbreviation for Sperner family).

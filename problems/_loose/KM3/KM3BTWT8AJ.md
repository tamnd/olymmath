Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be any infinite increasing sequence of positive integers. (For every integer $i>0, a_{i+1}>a_{i}$.) Prove that there are infinitely many $m$ for which positive integers $x, y, h, k$ can be found such that $0<h<k<m$ and $a_{m}=x a_{k}+y a_{k}$.

Inference 1 Let $f_{1}(x)=f_{2}(x)=1$,
$$
\begin{array}{l}
f_{n+1}(x)=f_{n}(x)+x f_{n-1}(x)(n>1) . \\
\text { Then } f_{n}(x)(\bmod 2)=1 \Leftrightarrow n=2^{\alpha}(\alpha \in \mathbf{N}) \text {. }
\end{array}
$$

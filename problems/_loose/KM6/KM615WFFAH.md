The number of students who registered to participate was $\frac{2}{13}$ more than the number of thank-you cards he prepared, but on the actual Math Night, $\frac{1}{15}$ of the registered students did not show up. How many students participated in the Math Night if the teacher had to print an additional 20 thank-you cards?

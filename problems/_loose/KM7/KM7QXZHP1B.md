In the cube $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$, let $R, S, T$ be the midpoints of the edges $[A B],\left[B^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right]$, and respectively $\left[D D^{\prime}\right]$. If $M, P, Q$ are the midpoints of the segments $\left[C^{\prime} R\right],[A S]$, and respectively $[B T]$, then:

a) Show that the line $M Q$ is parallel to the plane $\left(D C C^{\prime}\right)$;

b) Determine the area of triangle $M P Q$, if $A B=4 \mathrm{~cm}$.

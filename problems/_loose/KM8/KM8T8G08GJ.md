To construct a graph that contains arbitrarily many improved combs $T$, but not infinitely many copies of $T$, start with infinitely many disjoint copies of $T$, then partition them into disjoint sets $S_{1}, S_{2}, \ldots$ such that $S_{n}$ is the disjoint union of $n$ copies of $T$, and then identify vertices from different sets $S_{n}$ so that they disrupt the infinite "diagonal" set of disjoint copies of $T$.

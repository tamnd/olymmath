Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a triangle and $\omega$ its incircle, with points of tangency on $[\mathrm{BC}]$, $[\mathrm{CA}]$, and $[A B]$ being $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. Let $\Omega$ be the circle tangent to $\omega$ and passing through $B$ and $C$, and let $T$ be the point of tangency of these two circles. Let $\mathrm{X}$ and $\mathrm{Y}$ be the midpoints of segments [DE] and [DF]. Let $\mathrm{U}$ and $\mathrm{V}$ be the second points of intersection of the lines (TX) and (TY) with $\Omega$. Also, let $Z$ and $W$ be the second points of intersection of the lines (AB) and (AC) with $\Omega$. Show that (ZU), (WV), (XY), and the angle bisector of $\widehat{\mathrm{BAC}}$ are concurrent.

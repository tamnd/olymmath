Given that $P$ and $Q$ are two points inside the acute $\triangle ABC$, and they satisfy
$$
\angle PAB = \angle QAC, \angle PBA = \angle QBC \text{, }
$$
$D$ is a point on side $BC$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle DPC + \angle APB = 180^{\circ} \\
\Leftrightarrow \angle DQB + \angle AQC = 180^{\circ} .
\end{array}
$$
(2010, USA National Team Selection Exam)

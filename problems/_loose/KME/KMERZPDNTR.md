Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$. Let $D$ be the internal angle bisector from $A$ and $\Delta$ the perpendicular bisector of $[BC]$. Then $D$ and $\Delta$ intersect on $\Gamma$ at a point $K$.

Furthermore, $I, B$ and $C$ are equidistant from $K$ (where $I$ is the incenter of $ABC$).

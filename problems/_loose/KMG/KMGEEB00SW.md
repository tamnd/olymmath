Consider the ellipse $E: 3x^2+y^2=3$ and the hyperbola $H: xy=\frac 34.$

(1) Find all points of intersection of $E$ and $H$.

(2) Find the area of the region expressed by the system of inequality
$$\left\{
\begin{array}{ll}
3x^2+y^2\leq 3 & \\
xy\geq \frac 34 , & \end{array}
\right.$$

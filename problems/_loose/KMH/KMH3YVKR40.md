Let $ABCDE$ be any convex pentagon,
$$
S_{ABC}=S_{BOD}=S_{ODE}=S_{DEA}=S_{EAB},
$$

where $S_{ABO}$ represents the area of $\triangle ABC$, and so on.
(1) Prove that any two intersecting diagonals mutually divide each other in the golden ratio;
(2) Suppose $S_{ABC}=S_{BCD}=S_{ODE}=S_{DEA}=S_{EAB}=1$, try to find the area of pentagon $ABCDE$, the area of the star $ACEBD$, and the area of pentagon $FGHIJ$;
(3) Prove that there exists a convex pentagon such that $S_{ABC}=S_{BOD}=S_{ODE}=S_{DEA}=S_{EAB}=1$.

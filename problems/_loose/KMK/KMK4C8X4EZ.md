Investigate whether any quadrilateral area $V$ can be divided into two sub-areas by a line segment such that each of the two sub-areas has a smaller diameter than $V$!

Here, each of the mentioned areas is to be taken including its boundary. (In particular, a dividing line segment thus belongs to both sub-areas.)

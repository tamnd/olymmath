Two signals $A$ and $B$ are transmitted over a communication line with probabilities 0.72 and 0.28, respectively. Due to interference, $\frac{1}{6}$ of the $A$-signals are distorted and received as $B$-signals, and $\frac{1}{7}$ of the transmitted $B$-signals are received as $A$-signals.

a) Determine the probability that an $A$-signal will be received at the receiving point.

b) Given that an $A$-signal has been received, what is the probability that it was also transmitted?

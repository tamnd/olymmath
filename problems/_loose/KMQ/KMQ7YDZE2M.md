Let $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{25}$ be non-negative integers, and let $k$ be the smallest of them. Prove that

$$
\left[\sqrt{a_{1}}\right]+\left[\sqrt{a_{2}}\right]+\ldots+\left[\sqrt{a_{25}}\right] \geqslant\left[\sqrt{a_{1}+\ldots+a_{25}+200 k}\right]
$$

(As usual, $[x]$ denotes the integer part of $x$, that is, the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.)

(S. Berlov, A. Khokhlov)

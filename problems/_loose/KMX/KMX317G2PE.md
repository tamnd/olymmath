The fourth grade of the School for Wizards has 20 students. All students participated in the construction of a tower. Ten students were stacking blocks one on top of the other so that each placed twice as many blocks as the previous one. The first student placed one block, the second two blocks, the third four blocks, and so on. After that, the remaining students took blocks from the top, with the first taking one block, the second taking two, the third taking three, and so on, up to the last remaining student. What is the final height of the constructed tower if the height of each block is $5 \mathrm{~cm}$?

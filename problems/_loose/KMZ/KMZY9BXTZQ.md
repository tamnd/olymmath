Given two sequences $u_{1}$, $u_{2}, \ldots$ and $v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots$ with the same sequence of differences, i.e., such that $\Delta u_{n}=\Delta v_{n}$ for all $n$.

a) Can we assert that $u_{n}=v_{n}$?

b) Can we assert that $u_{n}=v_{n}$ if it is additionally known that $u_{1}=v_{1}^{n}$?

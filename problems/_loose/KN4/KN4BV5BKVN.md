a) Given a point $ X $ and a triangle $ ABC $. Prove that

$$
\frac{XB}{b} \cdot \frac{XC}{c} + \frac{XC}{c} \cdot \frac{XA}{a} + \frac{XA}{a} \cdot \frac{XB}{b} \geqslant 1
$$

where $ a, b, c $ are the lengths of the sides of the triangle.

b) Points $ A_1, B_1, C_1 $ are taken on the sides $ BC, CA, AB $ respectively. Let $ a, b, c $ be the lengths of the sides of triangle $ ABC $, $ a_1, b_1, c_1 $ be the lengths of the sides of triangle $ A_1 B_1 C_1 $, and $ S $ be the area of triangle $ ABC $. Prove that

$$
4 S^2 \leqslant a^2 b_1 c_1 + b^2 a_1 c_1 + c^2 a_1 b_1
$$

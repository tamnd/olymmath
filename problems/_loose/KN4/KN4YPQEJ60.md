On a chessboard of size 8 by 8, 8 domino pieces are arbitrarily placed, each occupying two adjacent cells. Different dominoes do not share any cells. Prove that there will always be a 2 by 2 square on the board where none of the cells are covered by a domino. Is this true if 9 dominoes are placed on the board?

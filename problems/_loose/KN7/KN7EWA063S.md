Three Punctures. On a rocky road, the Absent-Minded Scholar punctured the car's tire in three completely random and independent points. Serious repair is impossible, but fortunately, an old and rather torn inner tube was found in the trunk. The Scholar managed to cut out a large undamaged piece from it, which can cover the punctured tire from the inside for $2 / 3$ of its length or even a bit more.

a) (recommended for 6th grade, 1 point). Prove that the Scholar will be able to place the cut piece of the tube inside the tire so that all three punctures are covered.

b) (recommended for 9th grade, 5 points). What is the probability that the tire can be divided into three equal parts by length, each of which will have exactly one puncture?

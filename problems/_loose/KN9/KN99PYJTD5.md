There are 100 white stones lying in a circle. Given an integer $k$ ranging from 1 to 50. In one move, it is allowed to choose any $k$ consecutive stones, the first and last of which are white, and paint the first and last stones black. For which $k$ is it possible to paint all 100 stones black in several such moves?

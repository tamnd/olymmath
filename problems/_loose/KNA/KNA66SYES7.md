A point $C$ lies on the circumference of a circle. Two points $A$ and $B$ are chosen counterclockwise away from $C$ such that if $\angle C A B = \alpha$ and $\angle C B A = \beta$, the following equality holds:

$$
2 \cos \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} + \beta\right) = \sin \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta\right)
$$

Prove that the bisectors of $\angle C A B$ pass through a fixed point.

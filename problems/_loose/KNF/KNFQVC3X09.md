$P$ is a moving point on the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$. A tangent line is drawn through point $P$ to the ellipse, intersecting the circle $\odot O$ : $x^{2}+y^{2}=12$ at points $M$ and $N$. The tangents to $\odot O$ at points $M$ and $N$ intersect at point $Q$.
(1) Find the equation of the locus of point $Q$;
(2) If $P$ is a point in the first quadrant, find the maximum value of the area of $\triangle O P Q$.

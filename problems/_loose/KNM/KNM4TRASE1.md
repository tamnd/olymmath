Given trapezoid $A B C D$. A line parallel to its bases intersects the lateral sides $A B$ and $C D$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively, and the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ at points $L$ and $R$, respectively. The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $O$. It is known that $B C=a, A D=b$, and the areas of triangles $B O C$ and $L O R$ are equal. Find $P Q$, if point $L$ lies between points $A$ and $O$.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex cyclic quadrilateral. We assume that the angle $\widehat{A B C}$ is acute. Let $E$ be the midpoint of $[A B]$. The perpendicular to $(A B)$ passing through $E$ intersects $(B C)$ at $P$. The perpendicular to $(C D)$ passing through $E$ intersects $(A D)$ at $Q$. Show that $(P Q)$ is perpendicular to $(A D)$.

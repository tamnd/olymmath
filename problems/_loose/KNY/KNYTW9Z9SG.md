Inside a non-equilateral (not necessarily regular) heptagon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{7}$, a point $O$ is taken arbitrarily. Let $H_{1}, H_{2}, \ldots, H_{7}$ be the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $O$ to the sides $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots$, $A_{7} A_{1}$, respectively. It is known that the points $H_{1}, H_{2}, \ldots, H_{7}$ lie on the sides themselves, not on their extensions. Prove that $A_{1} H_{1}+A_{2} H_{2}+\ldots+A_{7} H_{7}=H_{1} A_{2}+H_{2} A_{3}+\ldots+H_{7} A_{1}$.

Consider the regular tetrahedron $A B C D$. Let $M$ and $N$ denote the midpoints of the edges $A D$ and $B C$, respectively. Take a point $P$ on the segment $M N$ that is different from $M$ and $N$, and draw a plane through $P$ that is perpendicular to the segment $M N$. This plane intersects the other four edges at one point each. Prove that the perimeter of the quadrilateral determined by these intersection points is independent of the choice of point $P$.

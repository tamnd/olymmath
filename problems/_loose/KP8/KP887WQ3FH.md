Given a sequence of points $A_{n}\left(a_{n}, b_{n}\right)\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$, satisfying $A_{1}(0,1)$, and $a_{n+1}=1+\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n}^{2}+b_{n}^{2}}, b_{n+1}=$ $\frac{-b_{n}}{a_{n}^{2}+b_{n}^{2}}$.
(1) Find the center and radius of the circle $C$ passing through the points $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$;
(2) Determine the position relationship between $A_{n}\left(n \geqslant 4, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$ and the circle $C$, and prove your conclusion.

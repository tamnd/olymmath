All positive integers are colored red or green, such that the following conditions are met:

- There are both red and green numbers.
- The sum of three (not necessarily distinct) red numbers is red.
- The sum of three (not necessarily distinct) green numbers is green.

Find all possible colorings that satisfy these conditions.

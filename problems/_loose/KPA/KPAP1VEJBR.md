Let $a, b, c, d$ be non-negative real numbers such that $a+b+c+d=1$. Denote

$$
M=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}-b c-c d-d b}{3}
$$

Prove the inequality

$$
\sqrt{a+M}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c}+\sqrt{d} \leqslant 2
$$

Consider the matrix $A=\left(\begin{array}{ll}6 & 2 \\ 3 & 1\end{array}\right)$ and the set $\mathrm{M}=\left\{X \in \mathbb{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R}) \mid \operatorname{det} X=\operatorname{det}(A+X)=0\right\}$.

a) Determine two sets $\mathrm{K}$ and $\mathrm{L}$, stable subsets of the monoid $\left(\mathbb{m}_{2}(\mathbb{R}), \cdot\right)$, such that $\mathrm{K} \cup \mathrm{L}=\mathrm{M}$.

b) Is M stable with respect to matrix multiplication?

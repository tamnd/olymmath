Let $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{n}, z$ be complex numbers such that

$$
\frac{1}{z-c_{1}}+\frac{1}{z-c_{2}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{z-c_{n}}=0
$$

Prove that if the numbers $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{n}$ represent the vertices of a convex $n$-gon on the complex plane, then the number $z$ represents a point lying inside this $n$-gon.

On a plane, there is a point $O$, and a polygon $M$. Let $\rho$ denote the perimeter of $M$, $d$ the sum of the distances from point $O$ to the vertices of $M$, and $h$ the sum of the distances from point $O$ to the lines passing through the sides of $M$. Prove: $d^{2}-h^{2} \geqslant \frac{\rho^{2}}{4}$.

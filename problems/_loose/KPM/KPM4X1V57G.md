Two points $C$ and $M$ are given in the plane. Let $H$ be the orthocentre of $\triangle A B C$ such that $M$ is a midpoint of $A B$.

(a) Prove that $C H \cdot C D=\left|A M^{2}-C M^{2}\right|$ where $D \in A B$ and $C D \perp A B$.

(b) Find the locus of points $H$ when $A B$ is of given length $c$.

Move the equilateral triangle so that one of the two constrained vertices completes exactly one full turn along the perimeter of the hexagon. What is the length of the path traveled by the unconstrained vertex?
(A) $\pi / 2$
(B) $\frac{\pi \sqrt{3}}{3}$
(C) $8 \sqrt{3}-12$
(D) $(4 \sqrt{3}-6) \pi$
(E) None of the above.

Let $\Omega$ be a set consisting of no more than a countable number of elements, and let $\mathscr{F}$ be some $\sigma$-algebra of its subsets. Show that there always exists a partition $D_{1}, D_{2}, \ldots$ (i.e., $\bigcup_{n} D_{n}=\Omega$ and $D_{m} \cap D_{n}=\varnothing, m \neq n$), generating $\mathscr{F}$:

$$
\mathscr{F}=\left\{\bigcup_{n \in N} D_{n}, N \subseteq \mathbb{N}\right\}
$$

The elements of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ tending to infinity are natural numbers. Prove that if the sequence

$$
b_{n}=\frac{a_{1} \cdot a_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot a_{n}}{2^{a_{n}}}
$$

is convergent, then its limit is zero!

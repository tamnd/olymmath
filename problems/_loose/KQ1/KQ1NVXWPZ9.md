What is the sum of all positive divisors of the numbers $p^{\alpha}, p^{\alpha} q^{\beta}, p^{\alpha} q^{\beta} r^{\gamma}$? ($p, q, r$ are distinct prime numbers, $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are positive integers). - Show - without listing the divisors - that the following pairs of numbers have the same sum of divisors: 605 and 637; 99 and 125; 8214000 and 18396 875. - Find all numbers whose sum of divisors is a) $72, b) 399$.

There are many cards, each with a natural number from 1 to $n$ written on it. It is known that the sum of the numbers on all the cards is $n! \cdot k$, where $k$ is an integer. Prove that the cards can be divided into $k$ groups such that the sum of the numbers written on the cards in each group is $n!$.

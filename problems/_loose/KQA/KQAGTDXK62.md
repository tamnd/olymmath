The monument to Mathenkamen is in the shape of a pyramid resting on its square base with a side of $18 \mathrm{~m}$. Its height measures $15 \mathrm{~m}$, and the foot of the height falls at the center of the square. The pyramid is oriented so that when the sun's rays come from the south at an angle of $45^{\circ}$ to the ground, the area of the ground on which it casts its shadow is as small as possible. What is this area expressed in $\mathrm{m}^{2}$?

(Note: the ground covered by the base of the pyramid is not counted as ground in shadow.)

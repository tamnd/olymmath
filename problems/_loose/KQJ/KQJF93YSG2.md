A jumping point stands at point $P$ on the number line. Points 0 and 1 are traps. Before each jump of the jumper, we randomly choose a positive number $X$, after which the jumper jumps a distance $X$ to the left or to the right (at its choice). During the entire time, we see where the jumper is. For which $P$ can we choose the numbers in such a way that we can definitely trap the jumper, no matter where it was initially located and regardless of how it chooses the directions for jumping?

For a quadrilateral $A B C D$, it is required that the sum of the lengths of the two diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ is $11 \mathrm{~cm}$, that the side $A B$ has the length $a=6 \mathrm{~cm}$, and the side $A D$ has the length $d=1 \mathrm{~cm}$.

Determine a length $x$ and a length $y$ such that for the perimeter $u$ of any quadrilateral that meets the given requirements, the inequality $x \leq u \leq y$ holds, where the equality sign holds exactly when the quadrilateral $A B C D$ degenerates into a line segment, i.e., when the points $A, B, C, D$ lie on the same straight line!

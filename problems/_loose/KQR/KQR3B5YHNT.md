For which integer values of $a$ and $b$ does the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
m^{2}+n^{2}=b \\
\frac{m^{n}-1}{m^{n}+1}=a
\end{array}\right.
$$

have a solution (for $m$ and $n$) in the set $\mathbb{Z}$ of integers.

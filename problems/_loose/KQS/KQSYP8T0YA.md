Determine for each of the following subtasks a) to e) all natural numbers $n$ that satisfy the given requirement!

a) The sum $\left(\frac{7}{12}+\frac{n}{12}\right)$ is a proper fraction.

b) The sum $\left(\frac{7}{12}+\frac{n}{12}\right)$ is a proper fraction that cannot be simplified further by canceling.

c) The problem of calculating the difference $\left(\frac{7}{12}-\frac{n}{12}\right)$ is not solvable within the domain of fractional numbers.

d) The difference $\left(\frac{7}{12}-\frac{n}{12}\right)$ is a proper fraction.

e) The sum $\left(\frac{7}{12}+\frac{n}{12}\right)$ is a natural number.

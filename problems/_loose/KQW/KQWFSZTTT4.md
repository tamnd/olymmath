Given quadratic trinomials $f_{1}(x)=x^{2}+2 x+a, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}+b x-1, f_{3}(x)=2 x^{2}+(6-b) x+3 a+1$ and $f_{4}(x)=2 x^{2}+(3 b-2) x-a-3$. Let the differences of their roots be $A, B, C$ and $D$, respectively, and given that $|A| \neq|B|$. Find the ratio $\frac{C^{2}-D^{2}}{A^{2}-B^{2}}$. The values of $A, B, C, D, a, b$ are not specified.

Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral, in which $AD \perp BD$. Let $M$ be the intersection of its diagonals, and construct the orthogonal projection $P$ of point $M$ onto line $AB$ and the orthogonal projection $Q$ of point $B$ onto line $AC$. Prove that point $M$ is the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $PQD$.

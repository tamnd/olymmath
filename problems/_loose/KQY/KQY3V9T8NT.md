(USA, 82). Find all pairs of non-zero integer values $ m \leqslant n $, satisfying the inequality $ m+n \neq 0 $ and the identity

$$
f_{m}(x, y) f_{n}(x, y)=f_{m+n}(x, y), x, y \in \mathbf{R}, x y(x+y) \neq 0,
$$
where it is denoted

$$
f_{k}(x, y)=\left(x^{k}+y^{k}+(-1)^{k}(x+y)^{k}\right) / k
$$

Let there be a parallelogram $A B C D$ and the points: $Q \in(A B \backslash[A B]$ such that $\frac{B Q}{A Q}=\frac{2}{3}, M \in\left(D A \backslash[A D]\right.$ such that $\frac{A M}{A D}=1$, and $\{P\}=D B \cap M Q$.

a. Show that $A P Q D$ is a trapezoid.

b. If the area of triangle $M D Q$ is $24 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, find the area of parallelogram $A B C D$.

Given the right-angled triangle $ABC$. $\left(\widehat{A}=90^{\circ}\right)$.

Construct the circle that passes through $A$ and is tangent to $BC$ at $B$, and the circle that passes through $A$ and is tangent to $BC$ at $C$. Let the centers of these circles be $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$. Show that these circles are tangent to each other! If $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, then $O_{1} M O_{2} \Delta$ is a right-angled triangle!

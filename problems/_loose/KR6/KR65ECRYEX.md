Three lines, parallel to the sides of triangle $ABC$ and passing through one point, cut off trapezoids from triangle $ABC$. The three diagonals of these trapezoids, which do not share endpoints, divide the triangle into seven parts, four of which are triangles. Prove that the sum of the areas of three of these triangles, adjacent to the sides of triangle $ABC$, is equal to the area of the fourth.

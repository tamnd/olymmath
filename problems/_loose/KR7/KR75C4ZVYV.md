Given a convex quadrilateral and two intersecting lines that do not intersect its sides. The sums of the distances from opposite vertices of the quadrilateral to one line are equal to each other, and the sums of the distances from opposite vertices to the other line are also equal to each other. Prove that the sums of the distances from opposite vertices of this quadrilateral to any line that does not intersect its sides are also equal to each other.

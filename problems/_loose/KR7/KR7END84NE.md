Let $A B C$ be a non-right triangle such that $A B < A C$. We denote $H$ as the projection of $A$ onto $(B C)$, and $E, F$ as the projections of $H$ onto $(A B)$ and $(A C)$, respectively. The line $(E F)$ intersects $(B C)$ at point $D$. Consider the semicircle with diameter $[C D]$ located in the same half-plane defined by $(C D)$ as $A$. Let $K$ be the point on this semicircle that projects onto $B$. Show that $(D K)$ is tangent to the circle $K E F$.

Find all integers $z \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that

$$
2^{z}+2=r^{2}
$$

where $r \in \mathbb{Q}$ is a rational number.

A rational number is a number that can be written in the form $\frac{a}{b}$ with $a, b$ integers and $b \neq 0$.

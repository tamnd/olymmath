(using the symmedian formula)

Let $A, B, C$ be three collinear points in that order, and $\Gamma$ a circle passing through $A$ and $C$. The two tangents to $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $C$ intersect at $T$, and the line $(T B)$ intersects $\Gamma$ at $S$. Finally, let $X$ be the intersection of $(A C)$ with the angle bisector of $\widehat{C S A}$. Show that $X$ is fixed when $\Gamma$ varies.

Prove that in the strings of length $n$ generated by the alphabet $\{0,1,2\}$,
(1) the number of strings in which 0 appears an even number of times is $\frac{3 n+1}{2}$;
(2) $\binom{n}{0} 2^{n}+\binom{n}{2} 2^{n-2}+\cdots+\binom{n}{q} 2^{n-q}=\frac{3^{n}+1}{2}$, where $q=2\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]$.

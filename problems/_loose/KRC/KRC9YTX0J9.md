For some integer $n>4$ a convex polygon has vertices $v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{n}$ in that cyclic order. All its edges are the same length. It also has the property that the lengths of the diagonals $v_{1} v_{4}, v_{2} v_{5}, \ldots, v_{n-3} v_{n}, v_{n-2} v_{1}, v_{n-1} v_{2}$ and $v_{n} v_{3}$ are all equal.
For which $n$ is it necessarily the case that the polygon has equal angles?

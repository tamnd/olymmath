The cells of a $10 \cdot 10$ board are colored red, blue, and white. Any two cells that share a side are colored differently. It is known that there are 20 red cells.

a) Prove that it is always possible to cut out 30 rectangles, each consisting of two white and blue cells.

b) Provide an example of a coloring where it is possible to cut out 40 such rectangles.

c) Provide an example of a coloring where it is impossible to cut out more than 30 such rectangles.

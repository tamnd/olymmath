Find all pairs (m, $\mathrm{n}$) of strictly positive integers such that

$$
\mathrm{m}!+\mathrm{n}!=\mathrm{m}^{\mathrm{n}}
$$

Remark. For any integer $\mathbf{n}$, recall that $\mathrm{n}$! denotes the integer $1 \times 2 \times 3 \times \cdots \times \mathrm{n}$.

Three friends are dividing a basket of apples equally and notice they can do so without cutting the apples. A fourth friend arrives and they decide to redistribute the apples equally again, and they manage to do so without cutting the apples. One of the four goes home and eats 2 apples on the way. Upon arriving home, he realizes he can divide the remaining apples in half with his girlfriend without cutting them. What is the minimum number of apples that could have been in the basket originally?
(A) 12
(B) 16
(C) 24
(D) 36
(E) 50.

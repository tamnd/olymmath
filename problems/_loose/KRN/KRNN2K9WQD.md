Given an acute-angled triangle $A B C$. Circles centered at $A$ and $C$ pass through point $B$, intersect again at point $F$, and intersect the circumcircle $w$ of triangle $A B C$ at points $D$ and $E$. Segment $B F$ intersects circle $w$ at point $O$. Prove that $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $D E F$.

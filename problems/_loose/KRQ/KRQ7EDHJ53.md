Given a triangle $ABC$ on the plane, a line $t$, and a point $T$ on $t$. Connect the reflections of points $A, B, C$ over $t$ to $T$, and denote the resulting lines by $p$, $q$, and $r$, respectively. Let the intersection of $p$ and $BC$ be $P$, the intersection of $q$ and $CA$ be $Q$, and the intersection of $r$ and $AB$ be $R$. Show that if the intersection points $P, Q, R$ exist, they lie on a single line.

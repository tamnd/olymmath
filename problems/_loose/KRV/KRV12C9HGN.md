At a sports festival, a race is to be held between young Pioneers and FDJ members according to the following rules:

At the midpoint of one of the shorter sides of a rectangular playing field ($50 \mathrm{~m} \times 70 \mathrm{~m}$), an FDJ member stands, and at the midpoint of the opposite side, a Pioneer stands. Both are to run on a command to the same flag placed on the playing field, taking the shortest route from their starting positions to the flag.

For this purpose, the flag, when the two runners are in their starting positions, should be placed such that it is $50 \mathrm{~m}$ from the FDJ member and $25 \mathrm{~m}$ from the Pioneer.

Give the number of all possible ways to place the flag on the playing field according to the conditions!

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}, \ldots a_{2 n-1}, a_{2 n}$ be arbitrary integers.

Show that

$$
a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{2 n}\left(a_{1}^{2}-a_{2}^{2}+a_{3}^{2}-a_{4}^{2}+\ldots+a_{2 n-1}^{2}-a_{2 n}^{2}\right)
$$

is divisible by 6.

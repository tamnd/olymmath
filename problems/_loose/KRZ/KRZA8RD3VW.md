Prove Euler's formula:
$$
O I^{2}=R^{2}-2 R r \text {, }
$$

where, $O, I$ are the circumcenter and incenter of $\triangle ABC$, and $R, r$ are the radii of $\odot O$ and $\odot I$, respectively.

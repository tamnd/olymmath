In a trapezoid, the endpoints $A, C$ of one of its diagonals are fixed, vertex $B$ moves along a straight line, and $D$ moves such that $D C \| A B$ and the ratio $C D: A B$ is constant. What path does $D$ trace?

Construct the right-angled trapezoids among those thus formed.

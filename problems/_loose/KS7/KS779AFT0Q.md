Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f(x)=a x+b, a>0$ and $f(f(x))=4 x+9$, for all real numbers $x$. Prove that the number $(f(p-1))^{n}-(2 n p+1)$ is divisible by $p^{2}$ for any prime number $p$ and natural number $n$.

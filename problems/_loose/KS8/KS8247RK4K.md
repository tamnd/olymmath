We colored each side of unit squares with one of the red, yellow, blue, and green colors so that all four sides of the squares are of different colors. Squares colored in this way can be assembled along their monochromatic sides.

Can a $99 \times 99$ or $100 \times 100$ square, whose sides are monochromatic and each of the four sides is of a different color, be assembled from such colored squares?

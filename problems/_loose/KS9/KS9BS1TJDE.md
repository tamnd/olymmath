We write $n=2$ on a board. Taking turns, Alice (the first) and Bob add a divisor $d$ of the number $n$ that is written at the moment they play, with $0<d<n$ (thus leaving $n+d$).

a) The first player to exceed $2011^{2012}$ loses. Who has a winning strategy?

b) The first player to exceed $2012^{2011}$ loses. Who has a winning strategy?

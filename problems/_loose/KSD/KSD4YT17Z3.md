Prove that if in some $p$-arithmetic $\sqrt{5}$ is extracted and if

$$
v_{0}, v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{n}, \ldots
$$

- is an arbitrary Fibonacci sequence in this arithmetic, then

$v_{p-1}=v_{0}, v_{p}=v_{1}, v_{p+1}=v_{2}, \ldots, v_{k+p-1}=v_{k}, \ldots$

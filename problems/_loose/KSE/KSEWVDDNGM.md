If $A=(a_1,\cdots,a_n)$ , $B=(b_1,\cdots,b_n)$ be $2$ $n-$ tuple that $a_i,b_i=0 \ or \ 1$ for $i=1,2,\cdots,n$ , we define $f(A,B)$ the number of $1\leq i\leq n$ that $a_i\ne b_i$ .
For instance, if $A=(0,1,1)$ , $B=(1,1,0)$ , then $f(A,B)=2$ .
Now, let $A=(a_1,\cdots,a_n)$ , $B=(b_1,\cdots,b_n)$ , $C=(c_1,\cdots,c_n)$ be 3 $n-$ tuple, such that for $i=1,2,\cdots,n$ , $a_i,b_i,c_i=0 \ or \ 1$ and $f(A,B)=f(A,C)=f(B,C)=d$ . $a)$ Prove that $d$ is even. $b)$ Prove that there exists a $n-$ tuple $D=(d_1,\cdots,d_n)$ that $d_i=0 \ or \ 1$ for $i=1,2,\cdots,n$ , such that $f(A,D)=f(B,D)=f(C,D)=\frac{d}{2}$ .

If $a, b, c$ are real numbers satisfying $a > 1, b > 1, c > 1$, then prove that:
$$\frac{a^a}{b^b}+\frac{b^b}{c^c}+\frac{c^c}{a^a}\ge\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{a}$$

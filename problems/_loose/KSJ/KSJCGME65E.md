Let two tangents $A M$ and $A N$ (where $M$ and $N$ are the points of tangency) and two secants be drawn from a point $A$ outside a circle, and let $P$ and $Q$ be the points of intersection of the circle with the first secant, and $K$ and $L$ be the points of intersection with the second. Prove that the lines $P K, Q L$, and $M N$ either intersect at one point or are parallel.

From this, derive a method for constructing a tangent to a given circle passing through a given point using only a straightedge.

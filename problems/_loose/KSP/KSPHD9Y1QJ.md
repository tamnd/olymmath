We choose a point $\mathrm{Q}$ on the ray $[\mathrm{OP})$ outside the segment $[\mathrm{OP}]$ and a point $\mathrm{R}$ on the ray $[\mathrm{PO})$ outside the segment $[\mathrm{OP}]$. Show that the angles $\widehat{\mathrm{QAP}}$ and $\widehat{\mathrm{OBR}}$ are equal if and only if the angles $\widehat{\mathrm{PDQ}}$ and $\widehat{\mathrm{RCO}}$ are equal.

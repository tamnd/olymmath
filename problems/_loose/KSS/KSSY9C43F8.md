Let $\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}$ be i.i.d. random variables, $n \geqslant 2$. Show that the distribution of the vector $\xi=\left(\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}\right)$ is spherically symmetric if and only if each of the variables $\xi_{1}, \ldots, \xi_{n}$ is normally distributed with zero mean.

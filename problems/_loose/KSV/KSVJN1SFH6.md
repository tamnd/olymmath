On the blackboard, there are 2000 numbers $1,2,3, \cdots, 2000$. Each time, you are allowed to erase two numbers $a, b(a \geqslant b)$ and write down $a-$ $b, \sqrt{a b}, \sqrt{a b}$ these three numbers (i.e., $\sqrt{a b}$ is written twice). After performing this operation 8000 times, 10000 numbers are obtained. Question: Can these 10000 numbers all be less than 500?

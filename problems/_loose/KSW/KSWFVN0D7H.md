A point $A$ moves with velocity $v$ along one arm of a right angle, and a point $B$ moves with velocity $v_{1}$ along the other arm towards the vertex of the angle; the distance of point $A$ from the vertex is $a$, and that of point $B$ is $b$.

1. Determine when the distance between the two points will be the smallest possible, and what this distance will be.
2. What will be the distances of the points from the vertex of the angle at that time?
3. What will the results be if $v=v_{1}$?

Katya invited three kittens and a puppy to her place to solve math problems. After solving the problems, Katya counted the pastries in the kitchen and noticed that two were missing. Katya has a balance scale without weights, on which she can place pastries, kittens, and the puppy. All pastries weigh the same, and all kittens weighed the same when they arrived. It is also known that the puppy is on a diet and therefore could not have eaten more than one pastry. How can Katya determine in two weighings who ate the pastries?

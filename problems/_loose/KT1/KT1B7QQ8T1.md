Let $A, B, C, D \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C}), n \geq 2$ and $k \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $A C+k B D=I_{n}$ and $A D=B C$. Prove that $C A+k D B=I_{n}$ and $D A=C B$.

Circle $\omega$ with radius 4 and center $O$ is inscribed in an acute-angled triangle $E F Q$ and touches its sides $F Q$ and $E Q$ at points $M$ and $P$ respectively. Circle $\Omega$ with radius $\frac{\sqrt{65}}{2}$ and center $T$ is circumscribed around triangle $P Q M$.

a) Find $O Q$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the area of triangle $F T E$ to the area of triangle $E F Q$ is $\frac{2}{3}$. Find the length of the angle bisector $Q A$ of triangle $E F Q$, as well as its area.

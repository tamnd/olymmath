A function $f$ is called periodic if it takes at least two different values and there exists $p>0$ such that $f(x+p)=f(x)$ for any $x$. Each of the numbers $p$ are called periods of the function $f$.

Is it possible to construct functions $g$ and $h$ with periods 1 and $\pi$ respectively such that $g+h$ is also a periodic function?

Let $N$ be a positive integer. Prove that

$$
\left[\frac{N^{2}}{1^{2}}\right]+\left[\frac{N^{2}}{2^{2}}\right]+\cdots+\left[\frac{N^{2}}{(N-1)^{2}}\right]+\left[\frac{N^{2}}{N^{2}}\right]=\left[\frac{N}{\sqrt{1}}\right]+\left[\frac{N}{\sqrt{2}}\right]+\cdots+\left[\frac{N}{\sqrt{N^{2}-1}}\right]+\left[\frac{N}{\sqrt{N^{2}}}\right]
$$

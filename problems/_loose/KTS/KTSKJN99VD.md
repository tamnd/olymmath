The sum of nine fractions is 1, all of whose numerators are 1. Five of them are: $\frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{7}, \frac{1}{9}, \frac{1}{11}, \frac{1}{33}$. The denominators of the other four fractions all end in 5. Then the denominators of these fractions are
(A) $5,15,35,105$.
(B) $5,25,35,135$.
(C) $5,15,45,385$.
(D) $5,25,105,385$.
（1st "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational, 1990)

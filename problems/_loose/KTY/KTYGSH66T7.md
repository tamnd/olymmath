The length of the major axis of an ellipse is $12 \mathrm{~cm}$, and the minor axis is $10 \mathrm{~cm}$. If we use the major axis as the x-axis and one of its vertices as the origin, we need to find the abscissa corresponding to an ordinate of $4 \mathrm{~cm}$. Furthermore, determine the equation of the tangent line to the ellipse at the endpoint of the positive ordinate, its angle of inclination, and the area of the triangle formed by the ordinate axis, the abscissa axis, and the tangent line.

a) We have depicted a polygon in the Monge projection plane system, then combined the projection planes with the usual rotation. Show that if the plane of the polygon is not perpendicular to either projection plane, then there is an affinity in which the 1st and 2nd images of the polygon correspond to each other (i.e., the lines connecting the corresponding points are parallel to each other and the intersection points of the corresponding - and intersecting - lines lie on a straight line).

b) What is the position of the polygon's plane relative to the projection plane system in the following three cases?

1. The affinity is orthogonal.
2. The two images can be transformed into each other by reflection.
3. The two images can be transformed into each other by translation (shifting).

Given a triangular pyramid $ABCD$. A point $F$ is taken on edge $AC$, such that $CF: FA=2: 9$, and a point $M$ is taken on edge $CD$, such that $AM$ is the bisector of angle $DAC$. A plane is drawn through points $F, M$, and the centroid of triangle $DAB$, intersecting edge $DB$ at point $N$. It is known that $CA: AD = DN: NB + 1$. It is also known that the ratio of the area of triangle $ADB$ to the sum of the areas of all faces of the pyramid $ABCD$ is $p$, and the perpendicular dropped from vertex $C$ to the plane $ABD$ is $h$. A plane is drawn through point $N$, parallel to the plane $ACB$, intersecting edges $CD$ and $DA$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. Find the radius of the sphere inscribed in the pyramid $DKLN$.

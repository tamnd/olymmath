Vitya's Torments (from 7th grade, 2 points). Vitya has five math lessons in a week, one each day from Monday to Friday. Vitya knows that with a probability of $1 / 2$ the teacher will not check his homework during the school week, and with a probability of $1 / 2$ the teacher will check, but only during one of the math lessons, and it's impossible to guess when - on any lesson with equal chances.

By the end of the math lesson on Thursday, Vitya realized that the teacher had not yet checked his homework this week. What is the probability that the homework will be checked on Friday?

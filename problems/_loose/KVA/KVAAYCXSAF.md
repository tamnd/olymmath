Let $G$ be a locally finite graph, $X$ a standard subspace of $|G|$ consisting of at least two edges from a set of connected components. Prove: $X$ is a bond if and only if for any two distinct edges $e, e^{\prime} \in E(G)$, the subspace $X \backslash$ is connected, but $X \backslash\left(e \cup e^{\prime}\right)$ is disconnected.

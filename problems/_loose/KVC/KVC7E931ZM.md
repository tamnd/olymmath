A circle is circumscribed around triangle $A B C$. Let $N$ be the point of intersection of the tangents to the circle passing through points $B$ and $C, M$ be the point on the circle such that $A M \| B C$, and $K$ be the point of intersection of $M N$ and the circle. Prove that $K A$ bisects $B C$.

Let the coefficients of the quadratic trinomial $a x^{2}+b x+c$ be positive integers. It is known that when $x=$ 1991, the value of the quadratic trinomial $a \cdot 1991^{2}+b \cdot 1991+c=p$ is a prime number. Prove that $a x^{2}+b x+c$ cannot be factored into the product of two linear polynomials with integer coefficients.
(4th Zu Chongzhi Cup Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

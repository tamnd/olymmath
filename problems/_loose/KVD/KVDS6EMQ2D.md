The sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are infinite arithmetic and geometric sequences, respectively. The sum of the first $n$ terms of $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is $S_{n}=\frac{3 n^{2}+5 n}{2}$. In the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$, $b_{3}=4$ and $b_{6}=32$. Let $\left\{c_{n}\right\}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}\right)$ be the new sequence formed by all common terms of $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ (in the original order). Prove that $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ is a geometric sequence.

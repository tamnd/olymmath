In the isosceles triangle $ABC$ ($AC = BC$), we draw a tangent from $A$ and $B$ to the circumcircle $k$ of the triangle around $C$, such that the intersection point $M$ of the tangents is not on the axis of the triangle. The points of tangency are $T$ and $T^{*}$. The circle centered at $C$ with radius $CM$ intersects the rays $CA$ and $CB$ at points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$, respectively. Show that $A_{1}B_{1} = MT + MT^{*}$.

Based on this, solve the following problem. Given a hyperbola $h$ with foci $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ and vertex $A$ ($AF_{1} < AF_{2}$), and a circle $k$ passing through the foci. Construct the points of intersection of $h$ and $k$.

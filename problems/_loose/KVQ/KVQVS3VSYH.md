In the center of a square is a policeman, and at one of its vertices is a gangster. The policeman can run throughout the entire square, while the gangster can only run along its sides. It is known that the maximum speed of the policeman is half the maximum speed of the gangster. Prove that the policeman can run in such a way that at some point he will be on the same side as the gangster.

In $\triangle A B C$, $C=\frac{\pi}{2}$, $C D$ is the altitude, and the projections of $D$ on $B C$ and $A C$ are $E$ and $F$ respectively. Prove:
(1) $C D^{3}=A B \cdot A F \cdot B E$;
(2) $A F \cdot F C \cos ^{2} B=B E \cdot E C \cos ^{2} A$.

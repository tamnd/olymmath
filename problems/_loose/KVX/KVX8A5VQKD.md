Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle in the usual notation, and let $AB \geq BC$. $A_1$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to the diagonal $DB$. $A_2$ is the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle DAB$ with $DB$, $C_2$ is the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle BCD$ with $DB$, and $C_1$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $DB$.

Prove that under these conditions $\angle A_1 A A_2 \cong \angle A_2 A C \cong \angle A C C_2 \cong \angle C_2 C C_1$ holds.

The following cases are to be considered: a) $AB = BC$, b) $AB > BC$.

Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ have the sum of the first $n$ terms $S_{n}$ satisfying $S_{n}=k q^{n}-k(k, q$ are non-zero constants $)$, and
$$
a_{1}=3, a_{4}=81 \text {. }
$$
(1) Find the general formula for the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Let $b_{n}=a_{n}-\frac{1}{a_{n}}$, prove:
$$
\frac{1}{b_{1}}+\frac{1}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{b_{n}}<\frac{9}{16} \text {. }
$$

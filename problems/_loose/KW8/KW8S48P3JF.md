Let $f$ denote the bisector of the angle formed by the rays " $a$ " and $b$. A circle $k_{1}$, tangent to the rays "a" and $f$, touches "a" at point $A$, and a circle $k_{2}$, tangent to the rays $a$, $b$, and $f$, touches $b$ at point $B$. Prove that the line $AB$ cuts equal chords from the circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$.

In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
a \geqslant b \Leftrightarrow \sin A \geqslant \sin B \Leftrightarrow m_{a} \leqslant m_{b} \Leftrightarrow h_{a} \leqslant h_{b},
$$

where $h_{a}, m_{a}$ are the altitude and median from $A$, etc.

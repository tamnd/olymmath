Expressed in the form $x^{2}-2 y^{2}$, where $x, y$ are positive integers, there are also infinitely many such numbers that cannot be represented as $k-u_{n} \leqslant u_{n-1}<u_{n}$, so $u_{n}$ in the representation of $k-u_{n}$. Thus, $k=\left(k-u_{n}\right)+u_{n}$ can be expressed as the sum of different terms in the Fibonacci sequence.

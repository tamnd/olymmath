(Minkowski Inequality) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be two sets of positive numbers, prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{k}^{2}+b_{k}^{2}} \geqslant \sqrt{\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}\right)^{2}+\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} b_{k}\right)^{2}} .
$$

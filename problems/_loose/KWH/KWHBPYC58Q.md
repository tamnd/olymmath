Given five different positive numbers, the sum of the squares of which is equal to the sum of all ten of their pairwise products.

a) Prove that among the five given numbers, there are three that cannot be the lengths of the sides of one triangle.

b) Prove that there will be at least six such triples (triples that differ only in the order of the numbers are considered the same).

6・28 In a certain sports competition, every two players play one match, and each match must have a winner, to determine the outstanding player. Player $A$ is determined to be an outstanding player under the condition that: for any other player $B$, either $A$ beats $B$, or there exists a player $C$, such that $A$ beats $C$, and $C$ beats $B$. If the outstanding player determined by the above rule is only 1, prove that this player must have won all other players.

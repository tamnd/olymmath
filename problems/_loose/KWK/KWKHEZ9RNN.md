Prove that the diagonals of a trapezoid are expressed through its sides by the formulas:

$$
e^{2}=a c+\frac{a d^{2}-c b^{2}}{a-c}, f^{2}=a c+\frac{a b^{2}-c d^{2}}{a-c}
$$

where $a$ and $c$ - are the lengths of the bases of the trapezoid.

A triplet of polynomials $u,v,w \in \mathbb{R}[x,y,z]$ is called *smart* if there exists polynomials $P,Q,R\in \mathbb{R}[x,y,z]$ such that the following polynomial identity holds :$$u^{2019}P +v^{2019 }Q+w^{2019} R=2019$$

a) Is the triplet of polynomials $$u=x+2y+3 , \;\;\; v=y+z+2, \;\;\;\; w=x+y+z$$ *smart*?
b) Is the triplet of polynomials $$u=x+2y+3 , \;\;\; v=y+z+2, \;\;\;\; w=x+y-z$$ *smart*?

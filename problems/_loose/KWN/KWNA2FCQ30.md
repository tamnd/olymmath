Three grasshoppers are located at the points $(0,0),(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$ on a grid paper. Each minute, one grasshopper jumps to another point on the grid in such a way that its jump is parallel to the line passing through the other two grasshoppers. Is it possible that after some time the grasshoppers end up at the points $(-1,0),(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$?

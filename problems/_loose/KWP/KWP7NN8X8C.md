Let $K$ be a subset of natural numbers. For any two numbers $a$ and $b$ in the set $K$, it holds that $a$ divides $b$ or $b$ divides $a$. Prove that then every number $c$ in the set $K$ is greater than the sum of all those numbers in the set $K$ that are smaller than $c$.

A line $l$ is tangent to the incircle of triangle $ABC$. Let $\delta_{a}, \delta_{b}, \delta_{c}$ be the signed distances from the line $l$ to the points $A, B, C$ (the distance is positive if the point and the center of the incircle lie on the same side of the line $l$; otherwise, the distance is negative). Prove that $a \delta_{a}+b \delta_{b}+c \delta_{c}=$ $2 S_{\mathrm{ABC}}$.

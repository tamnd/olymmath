Let $a>2$, and given the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$, where $x_{1}=a, x_{n+1}=\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{2\left(x_{n}-1\right)}(n=1$, $2, \cdots)$, prove:
(1) $x_{n}>2, \frac{x_{n+1}}{x_{n}}<1(n=1,2,3, \cdots)$;
(2) If $a \leqslant 3$, then $x_{n}<2+\frac{1}{2^{n-1}}(n=1,2,3, \cdots)$.

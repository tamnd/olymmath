Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle A$-acute. Let $P$ be a point inside $\triangle ABC$ such that $\angle BAP = \angle ACP$ and $\angle CAP =\angle ABP$. Let $M, N$ be the centers of the incircle of $\triangle ABP$ and $\triangle ACP$, and $R$ the radius of the circumscribed circle of $\triangle AMN$. Prove that $ \frac{1}{R}=\frac{1}{AB}+\frac{1}{AC}+\frac{1}{AP}. $

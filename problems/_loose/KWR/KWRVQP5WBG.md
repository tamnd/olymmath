7 Prove Pascal's theorem: For a hexagon $A B C D E F$ inscribed in a circle, the three pairs of opposite sides (the lines on which they lie) intersect at collinear points. (The method of coincidence can be used: Let $A B$ and $E D$ intersect at $L$, and $C D$ and $A F$ intersect at $N$. Suppose $B C$ intersects $L N$ at $M$, and $F E$ intersects $L N$ at $M'$. Prove that $M$ and $M'$ coincide; or use Menelaus' theorem to prove it.)

Given a spherical, smooth-surfaced body. The vertices of an inscribed cube need to be marked on it. (On the sphere, it is possible to draw a circle around a surface point with a radius smaller than the diameter of the sphere using a compass, and it is possible to set the compass to the distance between two surface points, i.e., the length of the chord defined by the two points. Furthermore, any Euclidean construction on a flat piece of paper is allowed.)

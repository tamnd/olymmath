Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1$, a moving line $l$ passes through the point $P\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3},-\frac{1}{3}\right)$ but does not pass through the point $Q(\sqrt{2}, 1)$, and intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A$ and $B$.
(1) Find $\angle A Q B$;
(2) Let the area of $\triangle Q A B$ be $S$, prove: $S<3$.

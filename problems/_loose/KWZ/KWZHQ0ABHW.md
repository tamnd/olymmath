Consider a function $f(x)=\frac{\sin x}{9+16\sin ^ 2 x}\ \left(0\leq x\leq \frac{\pi}{2}\right).$ Let $a$ be the value of $x$ for which $f(x)$ is maximized.

Evaluate $\int_a^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(x)\ dx.$

Peter rides his bike from school to the sports field. When he had covered 3/4 of the distance, he met his friend Hans, who was also riding at the same speed.

How fast are both of them riding if the teacher, traveling by moped ( $v=40 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ ), meets Peter and Hans exactly at their departure from the school and the sports field, respectively?

(Since the route passes through a sparsely trafficked and open area, the speeds can be considered constant.)

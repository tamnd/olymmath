(1) Given a polynomial $P(x)$ with natural number coefficients, and let $a_{n}$ denote the sum of the digits of $P(n)$ in decimal notation. Prove that there is a number that appears infinitely many times in the sequence $a_{1}$, $a_{2}$, $\cdots$, $a_{n}$, $\cdots$.
(2) For a polynomial $P(x)$ with integer coefficients, prove the same conclusion.

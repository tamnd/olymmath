The random variable $\xi$ has a negative binomial distribution $\mathrm{P}(\xi=k)=C_{m+k-1}^{k} p^{m} q^{k}$. Here $m>1$ and $p \in(0,1)$ are parameters, $q=1-p$. From the sample $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ of values of $\xi$, the empirical moments $M_{1}=\bar{x}=1.21$ and $M_{2}=\overline{x^{2}}=3.54$ are determined. Using these moments, find the parameters $m$ and $p$.

Peter decides to buy a new car. He has gathered some data on the model with a gasoline engine and the model with a diesel engine in a table.

| | car price | fuel costs | consumption |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| gasoline engine | 13300 EUR | 0.940 EUR / liter | 7.4 liters / 100 km |
| diesel engine | 14970 EUR | 0.892 EUR / liter | 5.2 liters / 100 km |

A By what percentage is the price of the model with a diesel engine higher than that of the model with a gasoline engine? Round the result to one decimal place.

B How much fuel would the car with a gasoline engine and how much would the car with a diesel engine consume if it were to travel 15000 km?

C After how many months would the savings due to fuel costs cover the price difference between the more expensive and the cheaper model, if he were to drive 15000 km annually?

Show that for all integers $n \geqslant 1$, we have the two equalities

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k(k+1)} & =\frac{n}{n+1} \\
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k \cdot k! & =(n+1)!-1
\end{aligned}
$$

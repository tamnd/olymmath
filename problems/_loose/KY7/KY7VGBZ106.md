Three points $A, B$ and $C$ are given in the plane. Prove that $C$ is a midpoint of the line-segment $A B$ if and only if

$$
P A^{1989}+P B^{1989} \geq 2 \cdot P C^{1989}
$$

for every point $P$ in the plane.

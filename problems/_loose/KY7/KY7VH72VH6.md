Let the function $f(x)=x-\frac{2}{x}-a \ln x(a \in \mathbf{R}, a>0)$.
(1) Discuss the monotonicity of $f(x)$;
(2) If $f(x)$ has two critical points $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$, let the slope of the line passing through points $A\left(x_{1}, f\left(x_{1}\right)\right), B\left(x_{2}, f\left(x_{2}\right)\right)$ be $k$. Is there an $a$ such that $k=2-a$? If it exists, find the value of $a$; if not, explain the reason.

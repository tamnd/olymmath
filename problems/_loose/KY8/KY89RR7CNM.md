(CSSR, 72). Prove that there exist numbers $A$ and $B$, satisfying for any value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ the equality

$$
a_{\mathbf{i}}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}=A \operatorname{tg} n+B n
$$

where it is denoted

$$
a_{k}=\operatorname{tg} k \operatorname{tg}(k-1)
$$

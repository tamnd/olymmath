In the methods for finding the zeros of a function, there is one called the Newton's method. The process of this method is: for the function $f(x)$, given $x_{1}$, and the intersection of the tangent line of $f(x)$ passing through the point $Q_{n}\left(x_{n}, f\left(x_{n}\right)\right)$ with the $x$-axis is denoted as $x_{n+1}(n=1,2,3, \ldots \ldots)$, when $n$ is sufficiently large, we can use $x_{n}$ as an approximate value of the zero. Let $f(x)=x^{2}-2, x_{1}=2$, find the recursive formula of the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$, and find its limit.

Fastiov $A$.

a) In an $m \times n$ table, signs "+" and "-" are placed. In one move, it is allowed to change the signs to their opposites in any row or column. Prove that if the table cannot be transformed into a table of all pluses using such moves, then there is a $2 \times 2$ square in it that also cannot be transformed.

b) In an $m \times n$ table, signs "+" and "-" are placed. In one move, it is allowed to change the signs to their opposites in any row, column, or diagonal (corner cells are also considered diagonals). Prove that if the table cannot be transformed into a table of all pluses using such moves, then there is a $4 \times 4$ square in it that also cannot be transformed.

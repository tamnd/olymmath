There is a convex polyhedron with at least 5 vertices in which exactly three edges meet at each vertex. Prove that it is possible to assign a rational number to each vertex of this polyhedron so that the following conditions are satisfied:

(i) at least one of the assigned numbers is equal to 2020;

(ii) for each face of the polyhedron, the product of the numbers at all vertices of that face is equal to 1.

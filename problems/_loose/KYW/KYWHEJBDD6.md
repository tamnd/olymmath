Let $ABC$ be a triangle On the line $(AC)$, place the two points $E$ and $E'$ such that the lengths $AE', AE, AB$ are equal and such that $E'$ is on the side of $C$. Show that then:

1. the lines $(BE)$ and $\left(BE'\right)$ are respectively parallel to the internal bisector and the external bisector of angle $\widehat{A}$,
2. the triangle $EBE'$ is a right triangle.

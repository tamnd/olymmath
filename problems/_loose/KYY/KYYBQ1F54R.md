The eyes of eleven magicians were covered, and on the head of each, a hat in one of 1000 possible colors was placed. After uncovering the eyes of the magicians, each saw the hats of the others but not their own. Then, simultaneously, each magician showed the others a red or green card. Is it possible for all of them to simultaneously recognize the color of their own hat? (The magicians can agree beforehand, and they know the 1000 possible colors.)

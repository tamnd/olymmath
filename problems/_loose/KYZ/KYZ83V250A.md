Define the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ :
$$
x_{1}=5, x_{k+1}=x_{k}^{2}-3 x_{k}+3(k \geqslant 1) \text {. }
$$

Prove: For any positive integer $k$, we have $x_{k}>3^{2^{k-1}}$.
(20th Hong Kong Mathematical Olympiad)

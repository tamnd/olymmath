[6] On an island, there live knights, liars, and yes-men; each knows who everyone else is. All 2018 residents were lined up and asked to answer "Yes" or "No" to the question: "Are there more knights than liars on the island?". The residents answered in turn, and everyone could hear their answers. Knights answered truthfully, liars lied. Each yes-man answered the same way as the majority of those who answered before him, and if the number of "Yes" and "No" answers was equal, he gave either of these answers. It turned out that there were exactly 1009 "Yes" answers. What is the maximum number of yes-men that could be among the residents?

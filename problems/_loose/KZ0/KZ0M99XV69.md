Given three circles $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$. Let $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ be the common internal tangents to circles $\alpha$ and $\beta, m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$ be the common internal tangents to circles $\beta$ and $\gamma, n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$ be the common internal tangents to circles $\gamma$ and $\alpha$. Prove that if the lines $l_{1}, m_{1}$ and $n_{1}$ intersect at one point, then the lines $l_{2}, m_{2}$ and $n_{2}$ also intersect at one point.

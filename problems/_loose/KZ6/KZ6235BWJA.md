Let the polynomial $p(x)=(x-a_{1}) \cdot(x-a_{2}) \cdots(x-a_{n})-1$, where $a_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$ are $n$ distinct integers. Prove: $p(x)$ cannot be factored into the product of two polynomials with integer coefficients and degrees greater than zero.
(1984 Shanghai Competition Problem)

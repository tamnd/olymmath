A positive-oriented $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ equilateral triangle is rotated by $+23^{\circ}$ around its centroid, and then enlarged from its centroid by a factor of $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$. This results in the $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ equilateral triangle. Show that there is a point $P$ such that both the $P A_{1} A_{2} \triangle$ and the $P B_{2} C_{1} \triangle$ are equilateral.

Given a quadrilateral $A B C D$. Prove the statement: If there exists a point $P$ such that triangles $A B P$ and $C D P$ are equally oriented isosceles right triangles with right angles at vertex $P$, then there exists a point $Q$ such that triangles $B C Q$ and $D A Q$ are isosceles right triangles with right angles at vertex $Q$.

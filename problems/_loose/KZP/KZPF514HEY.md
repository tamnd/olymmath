Let $a_{i}, b_{i}, i=1,2, \ldots, 7$ be non-negative real numbers such that $a_{i}+b_{i} \leq 2$. Prove that there exist $i, j, i \neq j$ such that

$$
\left|a_{i}-a_{j}\right|+\left|b_{i}-b_{j}\right| \leq 1
$$

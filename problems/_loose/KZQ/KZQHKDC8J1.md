Someone deposits $t$ forints in a savings bank; how much money will they have at the end of the $n$-th year, if after the deduction of tax they receive a $p_{1} \%$ interest rate, but after the annually due interest, they only receive a $p_{2} \%$ interest rate? For example, $t=5600 \mathrm{frt}$.

$$
p_{1}=4 \frac{1}{2}, p_{2}=4, n=12 \text { years. }
$$

Let $n \geq 4$. Number $n$ points on a circle with numbers from 1 to $n$. We say that two non-adjacent points, numbered with $a$ and $b$, form a correct pair if on at least one of the arcs determined by $a$ and $b$, all points are numbered with numbers less than $a$ and $b$. Prove that the number of correct pairs is equal to $n-3$.

Solve the problems independently. Time for solving: $3 \frac{1}{2}$ hours.

The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

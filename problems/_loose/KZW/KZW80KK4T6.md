Given a convex hexagon $A B C D E F$. $M_{1}$ is the midpoint of $A B$; $M_{2}$ is the midpoint of $C D$; $M_{3}$ is the midpoint of $M_{1} M_{2}$; $M_{4}$ is the midpoint of $E F$; $M_{5}$ is the midpoint of $A F$; $M_{8}$ is the midpoint of $D E$; $M_{7}$ is the midpoint of $M_{5} M_{6}$; $M_{8}$ is the midpoint of $B C$. Prove that the segments $M_{3} M_{4}$ and $M_{7} M_{8}$ necessarily intersect.

Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, and let $C_{1}$ (on side $A B$), $A_{1}$, and $B_{1}$ be the midpoints of its sides. Let $P$ be any point in the plane. Consider the three angles formed by the following pairs of lines: $A B$ and $P C_{1}$, $B C$ and $P A_{1}$, and $C A$ and $P B_{1}$ (always taking the smaller angle if they are not equal). Prove that the largest of these angles is not less than $60^{\circ}$.

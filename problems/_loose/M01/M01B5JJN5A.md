Three points lying on the same line
The inscribed angle is half of the central angle The angle between two chords and two secants The bisector divides the arc in half

Given a circle $\Omega$ and a point $P$ outside it. A line $l$ passing through point $P$ intersects the circle at points $A$ and $B$. A point $C$ is marked on the segment $A B$ such that $P A \cdot P B = P C^2$. Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of the two arcs into which the chord $A B$ divides the circle $\Omega$. Prove that the measure of angle $M C N$ does not depend on the choice of line $l$.

D and 2 Let $A B C$ be a scalene triangle with the longest side $A C$. (A scalene triangle has sides of different lengths.) Let $P$ and $Q$ be the points on the side $A C$ such that $A P=A B$ and $C Q=C B$. Thus we have a new triangle $B P Q$ inside triangle $A B C$. Let $k_{1}$ be the circle circumscribed around the triangle $B P Q$ (that is, the circle passing through the vertices $B, P$, and $Q$ of the triangle $B P Q$ ); and let $k_{2}$ be the circle inscribed in triangle $A B C$ (that is, the circle inside triangle $A B C$ that is tangent to the three sides $A B$, $B C$, and $C A)$. Prove that the two circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ are concentric, that is, they have the same center.

Two national roads intersect at a right angle. On one of them, a car travels at $v_{1}=60 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$, and on the other, a car travels at $v_{2}=40 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$, both heading towards the intersection. When the distance of the first car from the intersection is $200 \mathrm{~m}$, the distance of the second car is $500 \mathrm{~m}$. When will the two cars be closest to each other, and what is the minimum distance? Where will the first and second car be at that time?

In the plane of triangle $ABC$, two points $M$ and $N$ are given. Prove that

$$
M N^{2}=p_{1} r_{1}^{2}+p_{2} r_{2}^{2}+p_{3} r_{3}^{2}-\left(a^{2} p_{2} p_{3}+b^{2} p_{3} p_{1}+c^{2} p_{1} p_{2}\right)
$$

where $r_{1}=N A, r_{2}=N B, r_{3}=N C, p_{1}=\frac{M A_{1}}{A A_{1}}, p_{2}=\frac{M B_{1}}{B B_{1}}, p_{3}=\frac{M C_{1}}{C C_{1}}$

(the directed segments on the cevians of point $M$ are meant).

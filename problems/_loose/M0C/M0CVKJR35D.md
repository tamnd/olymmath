A team consists of $7$ players. In each round of the tournament, five of them play and two sit on the bench. Prove that, regardless of the (positive) number of rounds and the choice of who plays in what round, at the end of the tournament there are two players who have been together (either on the field or on the bench) in more than half of the rounds. (David Hruška)

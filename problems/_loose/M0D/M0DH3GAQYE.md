Bettina and Axel are both stamp collectors. Now, Axel suggests the following game involving stamps to Bettina:

Each of them independently writes down (without showing the other) exactly one of the three numbers 1, 2, or 3 on a piece of paper. Afterward, the papers are revealed. If the number written by Axel is less than or equal to the number written by Bettina, the number written by Axel is subtracted from the number written by Bettina. In the other cases, the numbers are added.

If the resulting number is less than 3, Axel is allowed to take as many stamps from Bettina as the number indicates; in the other cases, Bettina is allowed to take stamps from Axel. After carefully considering these complicated rules, Bettina tells Axel that this game is pointless. Each of them could play in such a way that they are sure not to lose. This would mean that neither of them should be able to take any stamps from the other.

Is Bettina's opinion correct?

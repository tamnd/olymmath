Let $a, b, c \in (0,1)$ and $x, y, z \in (0, \infty)$ such that $a = (bc)^x$, $b = (ca)^y$, and $c = (ab)^z$. Prove that:
a) $x, y, z > 0$;
b) $\frac{1}{x+1} + \frac{1}{y+1} + \frac{1}{z+1} = 2$;
c) $\frac{1}{x+y+2} + \frac{1}{y+z+2} + \frac{1}{z+x+2} \leq 1$.

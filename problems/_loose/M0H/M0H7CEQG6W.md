The solution to the equation $x^{2}-1 x-1=0$ is
(A) $\frac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$.
(B) $\frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$.
(C) $\frac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$ or $-\frac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$.
(D) $\pm \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1991)

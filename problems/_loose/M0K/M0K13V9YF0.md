Let $B$ be a Borel set on the real line with Lebesgue measure $\lambda$. We define the density of the set $B$ as the limit

$$
D(B)=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{\lambda(B \cap[-n, n])}{2 n}
$$

(assuming this limit exists).

(a) Provide an example of a set $B$ for which the density $D(B)$ is not defined (i.e., the limit in the formula (*) does not exist).

(b) Show that if Borel sets $A$ and $B$ do not intersect and have densities, then

$$
D(A+B)=D(A)+D(B)
$$

(c) Construct a sequence of non-intersecting Borel sets $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots$, having densities, such that

$$
D\left(B_{1}+B_{2}+\ldots\right) \neq D\left(B_{1}\right)+D\left(B_{2}\right)+\ldots
$$

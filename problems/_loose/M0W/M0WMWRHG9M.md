Given $5 n$ real numbers $a_{i}, b_{i}, c_{i}, d_{i}, e_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$ all greater than 1, and let $A=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}$, $B=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i}, C=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} c_{i}, D=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} d_{i}, E=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} e_{i}$, prove that: $\prod_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i} b_{i} c_{i} d_{i} e_{i}+1}{a_{i} b_{i} c_{i} d_{i} e_{i}-1} \geqslant$ $\left(\frac{A B C D E+1}{A B C D E-1}\right)^{n}$.
(1994 China National Training Team Selection Test Problem)

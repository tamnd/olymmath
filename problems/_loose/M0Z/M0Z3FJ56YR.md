In 10 identical jugs, milk was distributed - not necessarily evenly, but each turned out to be filled to no more than $10 \%$. In one operation, you can choose a jug and pour any part of its contents equally into the other jugs. Prove that in no more than 10 such operations, it is possible to achieve that the milk is evenly distributed in all jugs.

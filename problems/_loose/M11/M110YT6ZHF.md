In an opaque container, there are 2016 candies numbered $1, 2, \ldots, 2016$, of which 1008 candies are red and the remaining 1008 are yellow. Raja Ratak proceeds as follows: in one move, he draws one candy from the container (he cannot see the candy until he draws it), places it on a pile in front of him (before the first move, there are no candies on the pile in front of Raja), and then, if he wishes, he can choose two candies of the same color from the pile in front of him and eat them. Raja repeats this process 2016 times (i.e., until there are no more candies in the container). Whenever he eats two candies (in the described manner), say numbered $a$ and $b$, Raja earns $|a-b|$ points.

a) Prove that Raja can devise a strategy that guarantees him at least $2 \cdot 504^{2}$ points at the end of the game.

b) Can Raja devise a strategy that guarantees him more than $2 \cdot 504^{2}$ points?

Time for work: 240 minutes.

Let $\Gamma$ be a circle of radius $R$ and $A, B, C, D, E$ and $F$ in this order on $\Gamma$ such that $AB = CD = EF = R$. For all points $X$ and $Y$ in the plane, $M_{XY}$ denotes the midpoint of the segment $[XY]$.

Show that the triangle $M_{BC} M_{DE} M_{FA}$ is equilateral.

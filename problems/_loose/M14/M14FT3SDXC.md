Let $ABCD$ be a square, $O$ the intersection of the diagonals, and $M$ a point inside it. The points $P, Q, R, S$ are the projections of point $M$ onto the sides $(AB), (BC), (CD)$, and $(DA)$, respectively. Show that:
a) $\overrightarrow{MA} + \overrightarrow{MB} + \overrightarrow{MC} + \overrightarrow{MD} = 4 \overrightarrow{MO}$
b) $\overrightarrow{MP} + \overrightarrow{MQ} + \overrightarrow{MR} + \overrightarrow{MS} = 2 \overrightarrow{MO}$.

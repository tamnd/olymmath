For any given triangle $A B C$, let points $X, Y, Z$ be on $B C$, $C A$, $A B$ respectively, and $B X \leqslant X C, C Y \leqslant Y A, A Z \leqslant Z B$. Prove that the area of $\triangle X Y Z$ is not less than $\frac{1}{4}$ of the area of $\triangle A B C$.

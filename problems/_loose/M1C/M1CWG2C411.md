(2003 Hunan Province Competition Question) Let the domain of the function $y=f(x)$ be $\mathbf{R}$, and when $x>1$, and for any real numbers $x, y \in \mathbf{R}$, $f(x+y)=f(x) f(y)$ holds. The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=f(0)$, and
$$
f\left(a_{n+1}\right)=\frac{1}{f\left(-2-a_{n}\right)}(n \in \mathbf{N}) .
$$

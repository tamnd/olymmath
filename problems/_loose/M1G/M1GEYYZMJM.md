If $a x+b$ is divisible by a prime number $p$ at $x=\alpha$ and $x=\beta$, and the difference $\alpha-\beta$ is not divisible by $p$, then $a$ and $b$ are multiples of $p$ and, consequently, $a x+b$ is divisible by $p$ for any integer $x$.

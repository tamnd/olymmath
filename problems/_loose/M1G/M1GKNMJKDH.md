Let $n(\geqslant 3)$ be a positive integer, and $M$ be an $n$-element set. Find the maximum positive integer $k$ such that there exists a family $\psi$ of $k$ three-element subsets of $M$ with the property that the intersection of any two elements (note: elements of $\psi$ are three-element sets) is non-empty.

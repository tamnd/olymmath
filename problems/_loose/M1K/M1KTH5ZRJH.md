In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, $\odot A$ is a circle with center at point $A(-3,0)$ and radius 5, and point $B(2,0)$. Prove: there exists a positive constant $c$, such that for any point $X$ outside $\odot A$, we have
$$
|O X|-2 \geqslant c \min \left\{|B X|,|B X|^{2}\right\},
$$

and find the maximum value of $c$.

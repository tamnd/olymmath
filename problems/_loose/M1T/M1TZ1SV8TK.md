When taking $F(n)=\frac{1}{2} n(n+1)$, that is, $F(n)$ is a triangular number (which refers to the sum of several consecutive integers starting from 1), then the $n$-th non-triangular number is $n+\langle\sqrt{2 n}\rangle$, where $\langle x\rangle$ denotes the nearest integer to $x$.

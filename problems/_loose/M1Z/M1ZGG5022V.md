A banker learned that among identical-looking coins, one is counterfeit (lighter). He asked an expert to identify this coin using a balance scale without weights, and required that each coin participate in weighings no more than twice. What is the maximum number of coins the banker can have so that the expert can definitely identify the counterfeit one in $n$ weighings?

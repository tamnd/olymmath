Given $x_{i}, y_{i}$ are real numbers, $y_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, prove:
$$
\left(y_{1}+y_{2}+\cdots+y_{n}\right)\left(\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{y_{1}}+\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{y_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{y_{n}}\right) \geqslant\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)^{2} .
$$

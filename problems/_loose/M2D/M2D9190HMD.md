Given $n$ complex numbers $C_{1}, C_{2}, \ldots, C_{n}$, such that if they are represented as points on a plane, they form the vertices of a convex $n$-gon. Prove that if the complex number $z$ has the property that

$$
\frac{1}{z-C_{1}}+\frac{1}{z-C_{2}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{z-C_{n}}=0
$$

then the point on the plane corresponding to $z$ lies inside this $n$-gon.

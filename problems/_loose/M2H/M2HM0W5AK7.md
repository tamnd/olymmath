Let the integer-coefficient polynomial $f(x)=a_{0} x^{n}+a_{1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1} x+p \cdot a_{n}$, where $a_{0} a_{n} \neq 0, p$ is a prime number, and $p>\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\left|a_{i}\right| \cdot\left|a_{n}\right|^{-i-1}$. Prove: $f(x)$ cannot be factored into the product of two integer-coefficient polynomials.

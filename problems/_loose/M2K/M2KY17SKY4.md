Point $P$ is located inside triangle $A B C$ such that $\angle P A B=\angle P B C$ $=\angle P C A=\varphi$. If $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ are the angles of the triangle, prove that

$$
\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \varphi}=\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \alpha}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \beta}+\frac{1}{\sin ^{2} \gamma}
$$

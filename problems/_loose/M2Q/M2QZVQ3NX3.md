For any real numbers $x, y$, the function $f(x)$ satisfies the relation
$$
f(x+y)=f\left(x^{2}\right)+f(2 y) .
$$

Then the value of $f(1985)$ is
(A) 1985 .
(B) $\sqrt{1985}$.
(C) 3990 .
(D) None of the above.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

Trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $AD$ and $BC$ is inscribed in circle $\omega$. Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $P$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of segment $AB$. The perpendicular bisector of segment $AD$ intersects circle $\omega$ at points $K$ and $L$. Point $N$ is the midpoint of the arc $CD$ of the circumcircle of triangle $PCD$, not containing point $P$. Prove that points $K, L, M$, and $N$ lie on the same circle.

(A. Kuznetsov)

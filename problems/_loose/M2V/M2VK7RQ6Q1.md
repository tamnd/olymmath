Let $P$ be any interior point of triangle $A B C$, and let the orthogonal projections of $P$ onto sides $B C, C A, A B$ be $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$, respectively.

Prove that

$$
P A \cdot P B \cdot P C \geq\left(P A_{0}+P B_{0}\right)\left(P B_{0}+P C_{0}\right)\left(P C_{0}+P A_{0}\right)
$$

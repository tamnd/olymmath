Let $A D$ and $A E$ be the bisectors of the internal and external angles of triangle $A B C$, and let $S_{a}$ be the circle with diameter $D E$. Circles $S_{b}$ and $S_{c}$ are defined similarly. Prove that:

a) the circles $S_{a}, S_{b}$ and $S_{c}$ have two common points $M$ and $N$, and the line $M N$ passes through the center of the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$;

b) the projections of point $M$ (and point $N$) onto the sides of triangle $A B C$ form an equilateral triangle.

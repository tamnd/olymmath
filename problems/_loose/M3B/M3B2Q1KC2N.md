Given a circle $(O)$ with radius $R$, starting from point $A$ on the circumference, we measure on the circle the $60^{\circ}$ arc $\widehat{A B}$, the $90^{\circ}$ arc $\widehat{B C}$, and the $120^{\circ}$ arc $\widehat{C D}$.

$1^{0}$. Calculate the sides of the quadrilateral $A B C D$ as functions of $R$!

$2^{0}$. Show that the diagonals of this quadrilateral are perpendicular to each other! What are the lengths of the segments of the diagonals?

$3^{0}$. Calculate the area of the quadrilateral!

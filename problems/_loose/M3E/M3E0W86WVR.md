\section*{Problem 6B - 151236B}

Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients and $p, q, r, s$ be real numbers such that $p \neq q$.

When this polynomial is divided by $(x-p)$, the remainder is the number $r$, and when the same polynomial is divided by $(x-q)$, the remainder is the number $s$.

Under these conditions, what remainder results from the division of the polynomial $P(x)$ by $(x-p)(x-q)$?

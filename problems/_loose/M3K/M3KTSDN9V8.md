Trapezoids $A B C D$ and $A P Q D$ have a common base $A D$, and the lengths of all their bases are pairwise distinct. Prove that the following pairs of lines intersect at points lying on the same straight line:
a) $A B$ and $C D, A P$ and $D Q, B P$ and $C Q$;

b) $A B$ and $C D, A Q$ and $D P, B Q$ and $C P$.

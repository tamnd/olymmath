Let $p$ be an odd prime greater than 3, and $q=\frac{p-1}{2}$. Prove: $\sum_{i=0}^{q} \mathrm{C}_{2 i}^{i} \equiv 1(\bmod p)$ or $\sum_{i=0}^{q} \mathrm{C}_{2 i}^{i} \equiv-1(\bmod p)$.
(2016, Tsinghua University Mathematics Autumn Camp)

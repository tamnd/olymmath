Let $V$ be a finite subset of Euclidean space consisting of points $(x, y, z)$ with integer coordinates. Let $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ be the projections of $V$ onto the $y z, x z, x y$ planes, respectively. Prove that
$$
|V|^{2} \leq\left|S_{1}\right|\left|S_{2}\right|\left|S_{3}\right|
$$
$(|X|$ denotes the number of elements of $X)$.

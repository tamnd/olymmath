Let $d(n)$ be the quantity of positive divisors of $n$ , for example $d(1)=1,d(2)=2,d(10)=4$ . The **size** of $n$ is $k$ if $k$ is the least positive integer, such that $d^k(n)=2$ . Note that $d^s(n)=d(d^{s-1}(n))$ .
a) How many numbers in the interval $[3,1000]$ have size $2$ ?
b) Determine the greatest size of a number in the interval $[3,1000]$ .

When manufacturing a metal wedge, the waste is $12.5\%$ of the material used. In this way, from one piece of metal, exactly 100,000 such wedges are made. All the waste obtained is melted back into one piece of metal, and from it, more such wedges are made in the same way. This process is repeated until it is possible to make at least one such wedge from the waste.

Determine the total number of wedges obtained, including the initial 100,000 wedges.

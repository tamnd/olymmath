\section*{Task 2 - 211022}

Regarding the result of a 100 m race with six participants, none of whom achieved the same time, the following four statements were made:

(1) A did not come in second, or B came in first.

(2) A came in second, and C came in fourth.

(3) A came in second, and B came in third.

(4) C came in fourth, or B came in fifth.

Determine whether it is possible that

a) all four statements (1) to (4),

b) exactly three of these statements,

c) exactly two of these statements,

d) exactly one of these statements,

e) none of these statements are true at the same time!

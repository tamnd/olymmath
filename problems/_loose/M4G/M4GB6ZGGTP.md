If
$$\frac{1} {x} + \frac{1} {2x^2} +\frac{1} {4x^3}+\frac{1}{8x^4}+\frac{1}{16x^5}+\cdots=\frac{1} {64}, $$
and $x$ can be expressed in the form $\frac{m}{n},$ where $m,n$ are relatively prime positive integers, find $m+n$ .

Each player plays one match against each of the other $n-1$ players, and assume there are no ties. $w_{r}$ and $l_{r}$ represent the number of wins and losses for player $p_{r}$, respectively. Prove that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_{i}^{2}=$ $\sum_{i=1}^{n} l_{i}^{2}$

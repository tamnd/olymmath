Find all values of the parameter $a$, for each of which the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x^{2}+y^{2}=26(y \sin a-x \cos a) \\
x^{2}+y^{2}=26(y \cos 2 a-x \sin 2 a)
\end{array}\right.
$$

has two solutions $\left(x_{1} ; y_{1}\right)$ and $\left(x_{2} ; y_{2}\right)$ such that the distance between the points $P\left(x_{1} ; y_{1}\right)$ and $Q\left(x_{2} ; y_{2}\right)$ is 24.

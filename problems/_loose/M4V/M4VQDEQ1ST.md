A token is placed on a certain square of a chessboard. Two players take turns moving the token, and on each move, starting from the second, the distance it is moved must be strictly greater than on the previous move. The player who cannot make a move loses. Who wins with correct play? (The token is always placed exactly in the center of each square.)

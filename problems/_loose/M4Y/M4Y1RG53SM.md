Two bullets are placed in two consecutive chambers of a 6-chamber pistol. The cylinder is then spun. The pistol is fired but the first shot is a blank. Let $p$ denote the probability that the second shot is also a blank if the cylinder is spun after the first shot and let $\mathrm{q}$ denote the probability that the second shot is also a blank if the cylinder is not spun after the first shot. Find the smallest integer $N$ such that
$$
N \geq \frac{100 p}{q} .
$$

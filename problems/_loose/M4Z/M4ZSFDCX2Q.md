The Yirong ferry has a speed of 40 kilometers per hour. On odd days, it travels downstream from $A$ to $B$, and on even days, it travels upstream from $B$ to $A$. (The water speed is 24 kilometers per hour.) On an odd day, the ferry lost power while traveling to a point $C$ and could only drift to $B$. The captain noted that the time taken on that day was $\frac{43}{18}$ times the original time on odd days. On an even day, the ferry lost power while traveling to a point $C$ and, during the drift, the repair crew worked for 1 hour to fix the ferry, after which it traveled to $A$ at twice its speed. The captain found that the time taken on that day was exactly the same as the original time on even days. What is the distance between $A$ and $B$ in kilometers?

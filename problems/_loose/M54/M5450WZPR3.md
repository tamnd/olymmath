If one of two rectangles can be placed inside the other after translation and rotation, then the two rectangles are said to be "comparable in size." A square with sides parallel to the coordinate axes is divided into $ n^{2} \left(n^{2}>1\right) $ rectangles by $ n-1 $ horizontal lines and $ n-1 $ vertical lines. Prove: From these $ n^{2} $ rectangles, it is always possible to select $ 2 n $ such that any two of these $ 2 n $ are comparable in size.

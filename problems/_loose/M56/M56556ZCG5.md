Let $p$ be an odd prime number and $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{p}$ be integers. Suppose that for any integer $k \geqslant 1$, we have

$$
p \mid x_{1}^{k}+\cdots+x_{p}^{k}
$$

Show that the integers $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{p}$ are all congruent modulo $p$.

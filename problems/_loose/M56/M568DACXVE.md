Let $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ be integers such that $a_{0}=19, a_{1}=25$, and for every $n \geq 0$ we have $a_{n+2}=2 a_{n+1}-a_{n}$. What is the smallest $i>0$ for which $a_{i}$ is a multiple of 19?
(A) 19
(B) 25
(C) 38
(D) 44
(E) 50.

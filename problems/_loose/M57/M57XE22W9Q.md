Let $n \geqslant 1$ be an integer. Suppose that $n$ people are standing around a circle. Initially, person 1 has $n$ tokens. At each step, a person can give one token to each of their two neighbors. For which integers $n$ is it possible that, after a certain number of steps, each person has exactly one token?

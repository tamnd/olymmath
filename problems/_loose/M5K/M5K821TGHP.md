Let $k$ be the incircle of $\triangle A B C$ with $A C \neq B C, I$ be the center of $k$ and let $D, E$ and $F$ be the tangent points of $k$ to the sides $A B$, $B C$ and $A C$, respectively.

a) If $S=C I \cap E F$, prove that $\triangle C D I \sim \triangle D S I$.

b) Let $M$ be the second intersection point of $k$ and $C D$. The tangent line to $k$ at $M$ intersects the line $A B$ at point $G$. Prove that $G S \perp C I$.

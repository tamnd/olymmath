Let $w(x)$ denote the greatest odd divisor of the natural number $x$. If $a$ and $b$ are coprime natural numbers such that $a+w(b+1)$ and $b+w(a+1)$ are powers of two, prove that $a+1$ and $b+1$ are also powers of two.

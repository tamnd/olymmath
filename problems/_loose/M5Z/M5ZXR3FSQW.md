The base of the pyramid is a square with side $a$; two lateral faces of the pyramid are perpendicular to the base, and the larger lateral edge is inclined to the base plane at an angle $\beta$. A rectangular parallelepiped is inscribed in the pyramid; one of its bases lies in the plane of the pyramid's base, and the vertices of the other base lie on the lateral edges of the pyramid. Find the volume of the parallelepiped if its diagonal makes an angle $\alpha$ with the base plane.

Some $k{}$ vertices of a regular $n{}$ -gon are colored red. We will call a coloring *uniform* if for any $m$ the number of red vertices in any two sets of $m$ consecutive vertices of the $n{}$ -gon coincide or differ by 1. Prove that a uniform coloring exists for any $k<n$ and is unique, up to rotations of the $n{}$ -gon.

*Proposed by M. Kontsevich*

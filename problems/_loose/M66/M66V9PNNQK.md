Let $a_{i} \geqslant 0(i=1,2, \cdots, n), a=\min \left\{a_{1}\right.$, $\left.a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}$,

Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1+a_{i}}{1+a_{i+1}} \leqslant n+\frac{1}{(1+a)^{2}} \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(a_{i}-\right.$ $a)^{2}$, where $a_{n+1}=a_{1}$.

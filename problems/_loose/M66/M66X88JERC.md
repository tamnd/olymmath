Suppose in a large wooden box there are two smaller boxes, and in each of these smaller boxes there are two even smaller boxes, and so on, for a total of $n$ layers. In the $2^{n}$ smallest boxes, there is a coin in each, with some of the coins showing the national emblem side up and the rest showing the number side up. Each time, it is allowed to flip any one of the boxes (regardless of size), along with everything inside it. Prove that it is possible to make the number of coins showing the national emblem side up equal to the number of coins showing the number side up in no more than $n$ flips.

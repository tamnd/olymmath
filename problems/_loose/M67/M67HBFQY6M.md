In a right-angled triangle, the median and the angle bisector of the right angle divide the hypotenuse into three segments whose lengths, in some order, form an arithmetic sequence. Determine all possible ratios of the lengths of the legs of this triangle.

Three numbers form an arithmetic sequence if the sum of the smallest and the largest is equal to twice the middle number.

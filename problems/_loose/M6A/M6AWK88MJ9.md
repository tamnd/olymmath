For a sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n=1}^{\infty}$, if it is known that $\frac{S_{m}}{S_{k}}=\frac{m^{2}}{k^{2}}$, for any $k, m \in \mathbb{N}$ where $S_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\ldots+a_{n}$, prove that

$$
\frac{a_{m}}{a_{k}}=\frac{2 m-1}{2 k-1}
$$

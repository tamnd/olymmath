Suppose $0<m_1<...<m_n$ and $m_i \equiv i (\mod 2)$ . Prove that the following polynomial has at most $n$ real roots. ( $\forall 1\le i \le n: a_i \in \mathbb R$ ).
$$a_0+a_1x^{m_1}+a_2x^{m_2}+...+a_nx^{m_n}.$$

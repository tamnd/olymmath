In a circle with center $O$, a diameter is drawn; $A$ and $B$ are points on the circle, located on the same side of this diameter. A point $M$ is taken on the diameter such that $AM$ and $BM$ form equal angles with the diameter. Prove that $\angle AOB = \angle AMB$.

Continue $AM$ and $BM$ until they intersect the circle and apply the theorem about the angle between intersecting chords.

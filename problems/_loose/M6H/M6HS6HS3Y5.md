It is known about the triangle $ABC$ that $3 BC = CA + AB$. Let the $A$-symmedian of triangle $ABC$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ at point $D$. Prove that $\frac{1}{BD}+ \frac{1}{CD}= \frac{6}{AD}$.

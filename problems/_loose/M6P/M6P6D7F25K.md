$$
(\overrightarrow{P B}-\overrightarrow{P A})(\overrightarrow{P B}+\overrightarrow{P A}-2 \overrightarrow{P C})=0 \text {, }
$$

then $\triangle A B C$ must be ( ).
(A) a right triangle
(B) an equilateral triangle
(C) an isosceles right triangle

Determine for which natural numbers n it is possible to fill an $n \times n$ square table, whose fields are colored like a chessboard, with the numbers 2 and -1 such that simultaneously:

(i) the sum of all numbers in each row and each column of the table is equal to 0,

(ii) the sum of the numbers on all black fields of the table is equal to the sum of the numbers on all its white fields.

(Martin Melicher)

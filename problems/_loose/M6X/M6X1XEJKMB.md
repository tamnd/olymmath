Given a point $P$, lines $e, f$, and acute angles $\varepsilon, \varphi$. The circles to be constructed are those that pass through $P$ and intersect $e$ at angle $\varepsilon$ and $f$ at angle $\varphi$. (By the angle between a circle and a line, we mean the angle between the circle's tangent at the intersection point and the line.)

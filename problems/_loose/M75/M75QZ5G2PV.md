(Austria, 73). Prove that for any value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ the inequalities

$$
0<\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{g(k)}{k}-\frac{2 n}{3}<\frac{2}{3}
$$

hold, where $g(k)$ denotes the greatest odd divisor of the number $k$.

Prove: If $\odot O_{1}$ intersects $\odot O_{2}$ at $P$ and $Q$, the secant $A P B$ intersects $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ at $A$ and $B$, the radii of $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ are $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$, $\angle O_{1} P O_{2}=\alpha$, then
$$
P A \cdot P B \leqslant 4 r_{1} r_{2} \sin ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2} .
$$

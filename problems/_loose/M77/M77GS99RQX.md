Prove that the area of a triangle is equal to twice the square of the radius of the circumscribed circle around the triangle, multiplied by the product of the sines of the angles of the triangle, i.e.,

$$
S_{\Delta}=2 R^{2} \sin \alpha \sin \beta \sin \gamma
$$

where $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles of the triangle, and $R$ is the radius of its circumscribed circle.

For the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the edges satisfy $AB = BC = CA$ and $DA = DB = DC$. The center of the circumscribed sphere is $O$. It needs to be proven that

$$
\cos \angle AOB + \cos \angle AOD \geqq -\frac{2}{3}.
$$

Given two triangles $ABC,\ A'B'C'$ such that $\angle{A }$ and $\angle{A'}$ are supplementary angles and $\angle{B}=\angle{B'}.$
Prove that we have $aa'=bb'+cc'$, where $a,\ b,\ c$ denote the side lengths of $BC,\ CA,\ AB$ respectively
and $a',\ b',\ c'$ denote the side lengths of $B'C',\ C'A',\ A'B'$ respectively.

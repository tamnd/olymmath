Prove Theorem 8.1.3 for countable graphs. Note that, in this case, the proof is based on the fact: the topological space $X$ defined in the third proof is sequentially compact. (So, every infinite sequence of points in $X$ has a convergent subsequence: there exists $x \in X$ such that every neighborhood of $x$ contains a tail of this subsequence.)

Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$, and let
$$
S_{n}=\left[1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}\right](n=1,2, \cdots) .
$$

Prove: There exist infinitely many $n$ such that $\mathrm{C}_{n}^{S_{n}}$ is even.

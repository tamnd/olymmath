At an international mathematics conference, lectures are held in Russian, English, German, French, and Hungarian. Furthermore, we know:

(1) Those participants who understand both Russian and French also understand English.

(2) Those participants who understand Hungarian also understand French and German.

Investigate whether the participants who understand both Hungarian and Russian need an interpreter to understand any of the lecture languages!

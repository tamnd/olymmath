Let $A, B, C, D$ be four collinear points in this order. Consider $\Gamma_{1}$ the circle with diameter $[A C]$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ the circle with diameter $[B D]$. They intersect at $X, Y$. Let $P$ be any point on $(X Y)$, $M=(C P) \cap \Gamma_{2}$ and $N=(B P) \cap \Gamma_{1}$.

Show that the lines $(A M),(B N),(X Y)$ are concurrent.

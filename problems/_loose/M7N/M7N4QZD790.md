Consider a $10 \times 10$ grid, composed of 100 cells. The rows of the grid are numbered from top to bottom in the order of 1 to 10: the topmost row has the number 1, the bottommost row has the number 10. The columns of the grid are numbered from left to right in the order of 1 to 10: the leftmost column has the number 1, the rightmost column has the number 10. Martin colors some cells of the grid such that for any colored cell $c$, there is at most one different colored cell whose column number is greater than or equal to that of the column of $c$ and whose row number is greater than or equal to that of the row of $c$.

What is the maximum number of cells that Martin can color?

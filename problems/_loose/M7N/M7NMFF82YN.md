In the trapezoid $ABCD$, one of the bases is $AB$, and the projection of point $B$ onto $CD$ is $E$. A circle $k$ is drawn around $D$ with radius $CE$. A line $a$ is drawn through $A$ in any direction, and through $D$, a line $d_{1}$ is drawn parallel to $a$, and a line $d_{2}$ perpendicular to $a$. $d_{1}$ intersects the circle $k$ at $F$, and $d_{2}$ intersects line $a$ at $G$. Finally, the intersection of the circle drawn around $G$ with radius $GF$ and line $d_{2}$ is $H$. What is the geometric locus of $H$ as the line $a$ rotates around $A$?

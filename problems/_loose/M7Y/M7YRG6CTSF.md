Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid with $A B \| C D$ and $(A C) \cap(B D)=\{O\}$. If $M \in(A D)$ and $N \in(B C)$ such that points $M, O$ and $N$ are collinear, then:

a) express the vectors $\overrightarrow{O M}$ and $\overrightarrow{O N}$ in terms of the vectors $\overrightarrow{O C}$ and $\overrightarrow{O D}$

b) prove that $\frac{A B}{C D} \leq \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{N B}{C N}+\frac{M A}{D M}\right)$.

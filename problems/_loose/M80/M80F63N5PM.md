Let $\left(A_{n}\right)_{n \geqslant 1}$ be a sequence of events from $\mathscr{F}$ such that

$$
\sum_{n} \mathrm{P}\left(A_{n} \triangle A_{n+1}\right)<\infty
$$

Show that then

$$
\mathrm{P}\left(\overline{\lim } A_{n} \backslash \underline{\lim } A_{n}\right)=0
$$

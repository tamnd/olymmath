On the segment $A B$, a point $M$ is chosen, and isosceles triangles $A M C$ and $M B N$ are constructed with bases $A M$ and $M B$ respectively. It turns out that points $B, N$, and $C$ lie on the same line, and $A B = B C$. The perpendicular from $B$ to segment $A C$ intersects segment $C M$ at point $H$. Prove that $N H$ is the bisector of angle $M N C$.

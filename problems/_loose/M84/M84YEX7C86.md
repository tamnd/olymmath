G42 (21-3, USSR) On a plane, two circles intersect, and $A$ is one of the intersection points. Two moving points start from $A$ at the same time, moving at a uniform speed along their respective circles in the same direction. After one complete revolution, they return to the starting point simultaneously. Prove that there exists a point $P$ on the plane such that at any time, the distances from $P$ to the two moving points are equal.

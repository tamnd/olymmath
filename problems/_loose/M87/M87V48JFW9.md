Let the circumcenter of triangle $ABC = H$ be denoted by $T$ with respect to the reflection over the line $AB$. Calculate the angles of $H$ in the following three cases:
I. $T$ coincides with the incenter of $H$;

II. $T$ coincides with the excenter of $H$ opposite to the side $AB$;

III. $T$ coincides with the centroid of $H$.

(The excenter is the center of the circle that is externally tangent to the side $AB$ and the extensions of the other two sides. - For first-year students, a complete answer to the third question will also be accepted if it is based on construction and measurement.)

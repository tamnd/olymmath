Let $a_{i}>11, x_{i}>0, a_{i}>0, \beta_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, N)$, and
$$
a_{1} x_{1}^{\beta_{1}}+a_{2} x_{2}^{\beta_{2}}+\cdots+a_{n} x_{n}^{\beta_{n}}=c,
$$

prove that the function
$$
x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} x_{2}^{\alpha_{2}} \ldots x_{n}^{\alpha_{n}}
$$

is maximized when
$$
\frac{\beta_{1} a_{1} x_{1} x_{1}^{\beta_{1}}}{a_{1}}=\frac{\beta_{2} a_{2} x_{2} x_{2}^{\beta_{2}}}{\alpha_{2}}=\cdots=\frac{\beta_{n} a_{n} x_{n} x_{n}^{\beta_{n}}}{\alpha_{n}}
$$

On a plane, there is a circle and a point $M$ at a distance from its center, less than $1 / 3$ of its radius. Let $A B C$ be an arbitrary triangle inscribed in the given circle, with its centroid at point $M$. Prove that there exist two fixed points in space - $D$ and $D_{i}^{\prime}$ symmetric with respect to the given plane, such that the tetrahedra $A B C D$ and $A B C D^{\prime}$ are equifacial.

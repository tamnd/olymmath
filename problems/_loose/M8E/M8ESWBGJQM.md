Touching Circles [Tangent and Secant Theorem; product of the entire secant and its external part

Two circles with diameters $d$ and $D (d < D)$ are placed inside a semicircle such that each circle touches the arc and the diameter of the semicircle, as well as the other circle. A line is drawn through the centers of the circles, intersecting the extension of the semicircle's diameter at point $M$. A tangent is drawn from point $M$ to the arc of the semicircle ($N$ is the point of tangency). Find $MN$.

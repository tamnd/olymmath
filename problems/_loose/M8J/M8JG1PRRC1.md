The midpoints of sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$ are $L$, $M$, $N$, and $K$, respectively. If $EG$ intersects $MK$ at point $Q$, and $FH$ intersects $LN$ at point $R$, prove that points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are collinear.

Polynomials $u_i(x) = a_ix+b_i$ ( $a_i,b_i \in R$ , $ i = 1,2,3$ ) satisfy $$ u_1(x)^n +u_2(x)^n = u_3(x)^n $$ for some integer $n \ge 2.$ Prove that there exist real numbers $A$ , $B$ , $c_1$ , $c_2$ , $c_3$ such that $u_i(x) = c_i(Ax+B)$ for $i = 1,2,3$ .

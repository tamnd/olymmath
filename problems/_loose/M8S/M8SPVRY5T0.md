Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be positive numbers, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$. Prove: $\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{x_{i}}-1\right) \geqslant(n-1)^{n}$.

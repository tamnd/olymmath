Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding $x$. If $M=\sqrt{[\sqrt{x}]}$ and $N=[\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}]$ (where $x \geqslant 1$). Then it must be true that
(A) $M>N$.
(B) $M=N$.
(C) $M<N$.
(D) None of the above. (Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

From the digits $0,1, \ldots, 9$, exactly seven are to be selected, none of which are the same.

Determine the number of (in the decimal system) seven-digit numbers that contain each of the selected digits in their (decimal) digit representation!

Assume that

a) the 0 does not occur among the selected digits,

b) the 0 does occur among the selected digits.

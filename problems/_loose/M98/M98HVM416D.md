In a unit-square grid, points $A, B, C$, and $D$ are the vertices of a $10 \times 10$ square. At points $A, B$, and $C$, there is a doll each. We moved these dolls to $B$, $A$, and $D$ respectively through some sequence of steps; in each step, we moved one arbitrarily chosen doll to an adjacent grid point, without stepping out of the square. In every position, the area of the triangle formed by the dolls was at least $t$. What is the largest possible value of $t$?

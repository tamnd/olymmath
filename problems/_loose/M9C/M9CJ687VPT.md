In two consecutive natural numbers, the smaller number is the product of two different prime numbers, and the larger number is the product of three different prime numbers. We call these two consecutive natural numbers a "brother pair." For example, $2021=43 \times 47$, $2022=2 \times 3 \times 337$, so 2021 and 2022 form a "brother pair." What is the minimum sum of the two numbers that form a "brother pair"? $ $

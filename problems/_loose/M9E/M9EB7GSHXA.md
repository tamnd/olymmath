In a platoon, there are three sergeants and several soldiers. The sergeants take turns on duty in the platoon. The commander issued the following order:

1. For each duty, at least one extra duty must be assigned.
2. No soldier should have more than two extra duties and should not receive more than one extra duty per duty.
3. The lists of soldiers receiving extra duties should not coincide for any two duties.
4. The sergeant who first violates any of the above rules will be punished with detention.

Can at least one of the sergeants, without coordinating with the others, assign extra duties in such a way as to avoid being sent to detention?

Given 4 congruent right-angled triangular paper pieces and allowing the following operation: each time, one can choose a right-angled triangle and cut it along the altitude to the hypotenuse into two right-angled triangles. Prove that no matter how many times the operation is performed, there will always be two congruent triangles among the resulting triangles.

The 92nd question: Given $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{m} (m \geq 1)$ are all the positive divisors of a positive integer $n$. If there exist $m$ integers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{m}$, such that $n=\sum_{i=1}^{m}(-1)^{b_{i}} a_{i}$, then $n$ is called a "good number". Prove: there exists a "good number" that has exactly 2013 distinct prime factors.

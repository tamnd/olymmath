Let $a_{i}, b_{i}, \ldots, z_{i} \geq 0, \lambda_{a}, \lambda_{b}, \ldots, \lambda_{z}>0$ such that $\lambda_{a}+\lambda_{b}+\ldots+\lambda_{z}=1$. Show that

$$
a_{1}^{\lambda_{a}} b_{1}^{\lambda_{b}} \ldots z_{1}^{\lambda_{z}}+\ldots+a_{n}^{\lambda_{a}} b_{n}^{\lambda_{b}} \ldots z_{n}^{\lambda_{z}} \leq\left(a_{1}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)^{\lambda_{a}}\left(b_{1}+\ldots+b_{n}\right)^{\lambda_{b}} \ldots\left(z_{1}+\ldots+z_{n}\right)^{\lambda_{z}}
$$

Determine the cases of equality.

Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle with circumcircle $\gamma$ and incenter $I$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of minor $\operatorname{arc} \widehat{B C}$ of $\gamma$. Let $P$ be the reflection of the incenter of $A B C$ over side $B C$. Suppose line $D P$ meets $\gamma$ again at a point $Q$ on minor $\operatorname{arc} \widehat{A B}$. Show that $A I=I Q$.

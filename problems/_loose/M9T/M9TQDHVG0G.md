a) In a symmetric trapezoid, the parallel sides are $a$ and $b$, the leg is $c$, and the diagonal is $e$. Prove that $a b=\left(e^{2}-c^{2}\right)$.

b) In the triangle $P Q R$, the sides are $Q R=p, R P=q, P Q=r$ and $p<q$. We draw a line through vertex $R$ parallel to $P Q$ and mark point $S$ on this line such that $R S=\left(q^{2}-p^{2}\right) / r$ and the direction of $R S$ coincides with the direction of $Q P$. Prove that the quadrilateral $P Q R S$ is a symmetric trapezoid.

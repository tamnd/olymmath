Vera tells her friend Ute that she measured the edge lengths of a cuboid and noticed the following:

(1) One of the edges is twice as long as another of the edges.

(2) The sum of the lengths of all twelve edges of the cuboid is $320 \mathrm{~cm}$.

(3) Exactly two of the six faces of the cuboid are squares.

Ute thinks that these statements uniquely determine the edge lengths of the cuboid.

Investigate whether Ute's opinion is correct!

If it is correct, give the edge lengths; if it is not correct, give all possibilities for the edge lengths of a cuboid that meet Vera's statements!

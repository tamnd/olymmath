Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of integers taking an infinite number of positive values and an infinite number of negative values. Suppose that for all $n \geqslant 1, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ all have distinct values modulo $n$. Show that every integer appears exactly once in the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$.

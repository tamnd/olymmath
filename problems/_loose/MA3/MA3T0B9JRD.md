(1) Find all prime sequences $p_{1}<p_{2}<\cdots<p_{n}$, such that
$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{p_{1}}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{p_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1+\frac{1}{p_{n}}\right)
$$

is a positive integer.
(2) Does there exist a positive integer $n$ and integers $a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{n}$ greater than 1, such that
$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{2}^{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}}\right)
$$

is a positive integer?

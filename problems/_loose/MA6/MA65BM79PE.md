Through the vertices of the trapezoid $A B C D$, parallel lines are drawn which intersect an arbitrary line at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$. The segments $A A_{1}, B B_{1}, C C_{1}$, $D D_{1}$ are divided by points $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}, D_{0}$ in equal ratios, counting from the vertices of the trapezoid. Prove that: 1) the quadrilateral $A_{0} B_{0} C_{0} D_{0}$ is a trapezoid and 2) the ratios of the corresponding bases of the trapezoids are equal.

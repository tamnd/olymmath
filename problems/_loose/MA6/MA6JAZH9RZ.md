$A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots A_{n}$ are the vertices of a convex polygon and $M$ is the point for which the sum of the distances from these vertices is minimum. The circle of radius $k$ centered at $M$ intersects the ray $M A_{i}$ at point $S_{i}$. Prove that the center of mass of equal masses placed at the points $S_{1}, S_{2}, \ldots S_{n}$ is $M$!

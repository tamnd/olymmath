In a group of 9 people, arrange for each person to shake hands with exactly two other people in the group. Two different handshake arrangements are considered distinct if and only if there are at least two people who shake hands in one arrangement but do not shake hands in the other. How many different handshake arrangements are there? (2012 AIME Problem)

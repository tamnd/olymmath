In $\triangle A B C$, the lengths of the sides opposite to $\angle A$, $\angle B$, and $\angle C$ are $a$, $b$, and $c$ respectively. If
$$
\frac{a}{\cos A}=\frac{b}{2 \cos B}=\frac{c}{3 \cos C},
$$

then the size of $\angle A$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{\pi}{6}$
(B) $\frac{\pi}{4}$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{3}$
(D) $\frac{5 \pi}{12}$

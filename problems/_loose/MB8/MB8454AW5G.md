We will call a pair of positive integers $(n, k)$ with $k>1$ a lovely couple if there exists a table $n \times n$ consisting of ones and zeros with following properties:

- In every row there are exactly $k$ ones.
- For each two rows there is exactly one column such that on both intersections of that column with the mentioned rows, number one is written.

Solve the following subproblems:

a) Let $d \neq 1$ be a divisor of $n$. Determine all remainders that $d$ can give when divided by 6 .

b) Prove that there exist infinitely many lovely couples.

(Miroslav Marinov, Daniel Atanasov)

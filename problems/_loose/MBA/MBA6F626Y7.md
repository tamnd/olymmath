Divide a circle $K$ with radius $R$ into three circular sectors such that the sum of the areas of the two smaller sectors is equal to the area of the largest sector, and the difference in the areas of the two smaller sectors is equal to one-third of the area of the largest sector. In each of these sectors, inscribe the largest possible circle and denote these circles by $K_{1}, K_{2}$, and $K_{3}$. Express the area of the region $K \backslash\left(K_{1} \cup K_{2} \cup K_{3}\right)$ in terms of the parameter $R$.

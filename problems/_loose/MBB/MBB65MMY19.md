Let $\Gamma$ and $\omega$ be two circles that are internally tangent at $T$, with $\omega$ inside $\Gamma$. Let $A$ and $B$ be points on $\Gamma$ such that $[A B]$ is tangent to the circle $\omega$ at a point $K$. Show that $\widehat{A T K}=\widehat{B T K}$.

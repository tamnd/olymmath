73 Let there be an infinite sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, \cdots$. For any natural numbers $m$ and $n$, the inequality
$$
\left|a_{m+n}-a_{m}-a_{n}\right|<\frac{1}{m+n},
$$

is satisfied.

Prove: This sequence is an arithmetic sequence.

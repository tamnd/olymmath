A tournament is a complete graph where each edge has an orientation. A proper edge coloring is an edge coloring such that if $a, b, c$ are three distinct vertices of the graph, and if the edge between $a$ and $b$ points to $b$, and the edge between $b$ and $c$ points to $c$, then these two edges are of different colors.

The edge chromatic number of a complete oriented graph is the smallest number of colors needed to obtain a proper edge coloring. What is the smallest edge chromatic number possible for a tournament?

Prove that in Euclidean space the surfaces

$$((A-\lambda E)^{-1}x,x)=1$$

passing through the point $x$ and corresponding to different values of $\lambda$ are pairwise orthogonal ($A$ is a symmetric operator without multiple eigenvalues).

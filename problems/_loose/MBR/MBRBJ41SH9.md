In the country, there are 100 cities connected by roads in such a way that even if any city $A$ closes all roads leading out of it, it is still possible to travel from any city to any other city (excluding, of course, city $A$ itself). Prove that the country can be divided into two sovereign states, each with 50 cities, such that in both states it is possible to travel from any city to any other city.

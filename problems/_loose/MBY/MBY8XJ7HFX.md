(2003 Hunan Province Mathematics Competition Question) Given that $a, b, c$ are all positive numbers, and $a+b+c=1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{a(3 a-1)}{1+a^{2}}+\frac{b(3 b-1)}{1+b^{2}}+\frac{c(3 c-1)}{1+c^{2}} \geqslant 0 .
$$

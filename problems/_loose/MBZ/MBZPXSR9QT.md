Three. (50 points) Try to determine whether there exist two non-intersecting infinite point sets $X$ and $Y$ in the plane that satisfy the following conditions:
(1) In $X \cup Y$, no three points are collinear, and the distance between any two points is at least 1;
(2) For any triangle with vertices in $Y$, there exists a point in $X$ inside the triangle, and for any triangle with vertices in $X$, there exists a point in $Y$ inside the triangle.

Let $a, b$ be two perpendicular unit vectors in the plane. Let
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\boldsymbol{c}=c_{1} \boldsymbol{a}+c_{2} \boldsymbol{b}, \\
\boldsymbol{d}=d_{1} \boldsymbol{a}+d_{2} \boldsymbol{b},
\end{array}\right.
$$

where $c_{1}, c_{2}, d_{1}, d_{2} \in \mathbf{R}$. If $\boldsymbol{c}, \boldsymbol{d}$ are also perpendicular unit vectors, prove:
(1) $c_{1}^{2}+d_{1}^{2}=c_{2}^{2}+d_{2}^{2}=1$, and $c_{1} c_{2}+d_{1} d_{2}=0$;
(2) $\left\{\begin{array}{l}a=c_{1} c+d_{1} d, \\ b=c_{2} c+d_{2} d .\end{array}\right.$

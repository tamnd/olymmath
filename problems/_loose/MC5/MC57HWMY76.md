From the medians of a triangle with angles $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$, a triangle with angles $\alpha_{m}, \beta_{m}$, and $\gamma_{m}$ is formed (the angle $\alpha_{m}$ is opposite the median $A A_{1}$, etc.). Prove that if $\alpha>\beta>\gamma$, then $\alpha>\alpha_{m}, \alpha>\beta_{m}, \gamma_{m}>\beta>\alpha_{m}, \beta_{m}>\gamma$ and $\gamma_{m}>\gamma$.

See also problems $10.91,10.92,10.94$.

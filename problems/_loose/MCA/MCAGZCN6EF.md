Given are the sets $H_{1}, H_{2}, \ldots, H_{n}$. The set $H_{k}$ (for $k=1,2, \ldots, n$) consists of $k$ intervals on the real number line, which have no common points pairwise. Prove that among the intervals forming the above $H_{k}$ sets, it is possible to select $[(n+1) / 2]$ intervals such that each of these intervals belongs to a different $H_{k}$ set, and no two of them have a common point. ([x] denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$.)

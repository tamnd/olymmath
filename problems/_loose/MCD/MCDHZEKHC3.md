Let $p, q, r$ be the volumes of raspberries in one liter (percentage content) of the solutions in the first, second, and third barrels, respectively. Note that $0 \leqslant p, q, r \leqslant 1$. Nезнayka's plans imply

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
p+2 q+3 r=6 \cdot 0.1 \\
5 p+4 q+3 r=12 \cdot 0.25 .
\end{array} \Rightarrow p+q+r=0.6=3 \cdot 0.2\right.
$$

Further, multiplying the first equation by 5, and subtracting the second from the result, we get $6 q+12 r=0 \Rightarrow q=r=0$.

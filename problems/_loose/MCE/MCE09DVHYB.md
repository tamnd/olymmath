In City A, the streetlights are on day and night, all powered by batteries. If the battery is new, the streetlight can illuminate the ground within a radius of 200 meters. As the battery works, this radius decreases linearly at a rate of 10 meters per hour. Sometimes, the city's residents replace some of the used batteries with new ones. It is known that 18,000 batteries are exhausted in one day and night, and the city's area is a circle with a radius of 10 kilometers. Prove that at some point in the city, there is at least one point that is illuminated by more than 1 streetlight at a certain moment.

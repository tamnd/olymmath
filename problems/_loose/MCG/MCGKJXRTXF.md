Let's prove that the internal dihedral angle bisector plane through the edge $AB$ of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ intersects the edge $CD$ at a point $M$ such that

$$
CM: MD = t_{ABC}: t_{ABD}
$$

(i.e., the ratio of the segments is equal to the ratio of the areas of the two triangular faces). What is the condition for the external dihedral angle bisector plane through the edge $AB$ to intersect the edge $CD$? If this intersection point $M'$ exists, what can we say about the ratio $CM': M'D$?

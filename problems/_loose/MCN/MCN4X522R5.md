Werner still has to walk $2.4 \mathrm{~km}$ to the school; this is twice the length of Fred's distance.

a) What distances do the individual students still have to cover before they reach the school? b) How many minutes will pass until all students have arrived at the school, if each student takes exactly $90 \mathrm{sec}$ for every $100 \mathrm{~m}$?

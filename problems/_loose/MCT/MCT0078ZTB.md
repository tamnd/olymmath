Let $\xi$ and $\eta$ be random variables. Show that $\inf _{f} \mathrm{E}(\eta-f(\xi))^{2}$ is achieved at the function $f^{*}(\xi)=\mathrm{E}(\eta \mid \xi)$. (Thus, the optimal mean-square estimate of $\eta$ based on $\xi$ is the conditional expectation $\mathbf{E}(\eta \mid \xi)$.)

Given a triangle $A B C$ let $D, E, F$ be orthogonal projections from $A, B, C$ to the opposite sides respectively. Let $X, Y, Z$ denote midpoints of $A D, B E, C F$ respectively. Prove that perpendiculars from $D$ to $Y Z$, from $E$ to $X Z$ and from $F$ to $X Y$ are concurrent.

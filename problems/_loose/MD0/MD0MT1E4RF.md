Water is poured into $n$ cups of sufficiently large capacity, with each cup receiving an equal amount of water. Water can be poured from any cup to any other cup, but the amount of water poured must be equal to the amount of water already in the receiving cup. It is known that after a finite number of pourings, all the water can be collected into one cup. Find all possible natural numbers $n$.

Cells of the board $m \times n$ are painted in two colors. It is known that no matter which cell a rook is placed on, it will attack more cells of the opposite color from the one it stands on (the cell under the rook is also considered attacked). Prove that in each column and each row, there are an equal number of cells of both colors.

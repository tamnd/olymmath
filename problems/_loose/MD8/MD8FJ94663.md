Let $ f:[0,1]\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ be a continuous and nondecreasing function.

**a)** Show that the sequence $ \left( \frac{1}{2^n}\sum_{i=1}^{2^n} f\left(\frac{i}{2^n}\right) \right)_{n\ge 1} $ is nonincreasing.

**b)** Prove that, if there exists some natural index at which the sequence above is equal to $ \int_0^1 f(x)dx, $ then $ f $ is constant.

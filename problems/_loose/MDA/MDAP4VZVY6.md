In a mathematics study group, the following problem from a Soviet textbook was posed:

Wassja bought two stamp albums. Kolja asked him how much he paid for them.

"I used only one type of coin for the payment," Wassja replied, "and for one album exactly 7, for the other exactly 5. I paid a total of 60 kopecks for both albums."

(In the Soviet Union, there are 1-, 2-, 3-, 5-, 10-, 15-, 20-, and 50-kopeck coins, and no other types of kopeck coins.)

How much did one album and how much did the other cost?

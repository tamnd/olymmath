Let $a$, $b$, $c \in (0,+\infty)$ . Then the following inequality is true:$$\sqrt{(a+b)(b+c)}+\sqrt{(b+c)(c+a)}+\sqrt{(c+a)(a+b)}+a+b+c\leq \left(ab+bc+ca\right)\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{ab}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{bc}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{ca}}\right)$$

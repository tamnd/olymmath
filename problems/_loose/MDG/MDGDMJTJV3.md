Let $A, B$ be two different points on the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$, and the line $A B$ intersects the $x$-axis and $y$-axis at $P(m, 0), Q(0, n)$ $(m \neq \pm 2, m \neq 0, n \neq \pm \sqrt{3}, n \neq 0)$. If $M$ is any point on the line $y=\frac{3}{n}$, and the slopes of the lines $M A$, $M Q$, and $M B$ exist and are not equal to 0. Can the reciprocals of the slopes of the lines $M A$, $M Q$, and $M B$ form an arithmetic sequence? If so, please provide a proof; if not, please explain the reason.

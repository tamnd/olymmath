Let $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ be a right rectangular parallelepiped, with dimensions $A B=3 a, B C=2 a$ and $A A^{\prime}=a, a>0$. Let $M \in(A B), N \in(B C)$ such that $A M=B N=a$.

a) Prove that the lines $D^{\prime} M$ and $M N$ are perpendicular.

b) Determine the measure of the angle between the planes $\left(D^{\prime} D M\right)$ and $\left(D^{\prime} D N\right)$.

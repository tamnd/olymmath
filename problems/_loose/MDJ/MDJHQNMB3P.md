Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a group and $A, B$ be two non-empty subsets of $G$.

Consider the set $A / B=\left\{a b^{-1} / a \in A, b \in B\right\}$ (we denote by $b^{-1}$ the inverse of $b$ in the group $G$).

a) If $G$ is an abelian group and $A$ and $B$ are subgroups of it, prove that $A / B$ is a subgroup of $G$.

b) In the group $\left(\mathbb{Z}_{2015},+\right)$, consider the sets $A=\left\{\hat{a} \in \mathbb{Z}_{2015} / a\right.$ is divisible by 13$\}$ and $B=\left\{\hat{b} \in \mathbb{Z}_{2015} / b\right.$ is divisible by 31$\}$. Determine the set $A / B$.

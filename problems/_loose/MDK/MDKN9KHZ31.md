In this story, we will talk about a caravan crossing the Sahara Desert. One day, the caravan stopped for the night. Let's denote the three main characters as A, B, and C. A hated C and decided to kill him by poisoning the water bag (the only water supply C had). Independently of A, another caravan member B also decided to kill C and (not knowing that the water belonging to C was already poisoned) made a tiny hole in the water bag so that the water would slowly leak out. A few days later, C died of thirst. The question is, who is the murderer? A or B?

Some consider the murderer to be caravan member B, as C did not have time to drink the poisoned water that his enemy A had laced, and would have died even if A had not poisoned the water. Others consider the murderer to be caravan member A, as they believe that the actions of caravan member B had no impact on the outcome: since A had poisoned the water, C was doomed and would have died even if the other enemy B had not made a hole in the water bag.

Whose reasoning is correct?

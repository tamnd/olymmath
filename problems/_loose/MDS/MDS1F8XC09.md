A group of $n$ chess players $(n \geqslant 3)$ is to be divided into three groups for a competition. Players in the same group do not compete, while every two players from different groups must play a match.

Prove that to maximize the total number of matches, the groups should be divided such that the number of people in any two groups differs by at most one.

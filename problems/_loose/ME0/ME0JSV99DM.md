In a square with a side of 7, there are 170 squares with a side of 1, such that the sides of the small squares are parallel to the sides of the large square. Prove that the center of at least one of the squares with a side of 1 lies strictly inside another square with a side of 1 (a point lies strictly inside a square if the point belongs to the square and does not lie on its boundary).

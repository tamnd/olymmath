$\mathbb{N}^{*}$ denotes the set of integers greater than or equal to one. Find all functions $\mathrm{f}: \mathbb{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{*}$ satisfying:

i) for any $\mathrm{n}$ in $\mathbb{N}^{*}, f(n+f(n))=f(n)$

ii) $\mathrm{f}(2013)=1$.

The number $\frac{16}{15}$ is to be represented in the form $\frac{16}{15}=\frac{a}{m}+\frac{b}{n}$. Here, $a, b, m, n$ should be natural numbers such that the fractions $\frac{a}{m}$ and $\frac{b}{n}$ are not reducible and are not whole numbers.

Give three examples of such a representation, where

in the first example $m=n$ and $a \neq b$ holds, in the second example $a=b$ and $m \neq n$ holds, in the third example $a=b$ and $m=n$ holds!

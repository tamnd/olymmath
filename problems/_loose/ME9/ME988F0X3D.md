In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, the sequence of points $A_{n}\left(x_{n}, y_{n}\right)\left(\left\{x_{n}\right\} 、\left\{y_{n}\right\}\right.$ are two sequences of positive real numbers) satisfies that for any $n \geqslant 1$,
$$
x_{n+1}=\sqrt{\frac{x_{n}^{2}+x_{n+2}^{2}}{2}}, y_{n+1}=\left(\frac{\sqrt{y_{n}}+\sqrt{y_{n+2}}}{2}\right)^{2} \text {. }
$$

It is known that points $O 、 A_{1} 、 A_{2018}$ lie on the line $d$, and $A_{1}$ and $A_{2018}$ are distinct. Prove: $A_{2}, A_{3}, \cdots, A_{2017}$ are on the same side of the line $d$.

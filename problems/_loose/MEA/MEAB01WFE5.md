Given $k, m, n \in \mathbf{Z}^{-}$, and $m+k+1$ is a prime number greater than $n+1$, let $C_{N}=s(s+1)$, prove: $\left(C_{m+1}-C_{k}\right)$ $\left(C_{m, 2}-C_{k}\right) \cdots\left(C_{m, n}-C_{k}\right)$ can be divided by the product $C_{1} \cdot C_{2} \cdot \cdots \cdot C_{n}$.

Let $x$ be such a real number for which $\cos \left(60^{\circ}-x\right) \neq 0$ and $\sin \left(120^{\circ}-x\right) \neq 0$. Without using a pocket calculator, calculate the exact value of the expression: $\frac{\sqrt{3}+4 \sin x \cos x}{\cos \left(60^{\circ}-x\right) \cdot \sin \left(120^{\circ}-x\right)}$.

On the sides $A B$ and $B C$ of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, isosceles triangles $A F B$ and $B L C$ are constructed with these sides as bases, with one of them lying inside triangle $A B C$ and the other constructed on the outside. Moreover, $\angle A F B = \angle B L C$ and $\angle C A F = \angle A C L$. Prove that the line $F L$ cuts off an isosceles triangle from angle $A B C$.

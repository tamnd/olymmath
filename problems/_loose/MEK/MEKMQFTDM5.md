Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle (Figure 3.23), $AB = a, CD = b, BC = x, DA = y, A + B \neq \pi$. Prove:
$$
x=\frac{a \sin A - b \sin B}{\sin (A+B)}, y=\frac{a \sin B - b \sin A}{\sin (A+B)}.
$$

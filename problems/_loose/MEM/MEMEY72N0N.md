Given a square with a side length of one unit and 5 equal circular disks. 4 of the disks are fixed in such a way that they internally touch two adjacent sides of the square (there is no overlap). The fifth disk is moved within the part of the square not covered by the stationary disks. What area can the moving disk sweep over in the process?

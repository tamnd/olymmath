(P3 BMO 2015)

3366 film critics participate in the Oscar voting. Each critic votes for exactly one actor and one actress. At the end of the voting, it is observed that for any integer $1 \leqslant n \leqslant 100$, there is an actor or an actress who received exactly $n$ votes. Prove that there exist two critics who voted for the same actor and the same actress.

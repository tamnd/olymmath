The sides $AB$ and $D\mathcal{C}$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ are divided into parts proportional to the other two adjacent sides of the quadrilateral. Prove that the square of the distance between the points of division is equal to

$$
\frac{bd}{(b+d)^{2}}\left(e^{2}+f^{2}+2bd-a^{2}-c^{2}\right) .
$$

Prove that the distance between the points of division can be calculated by the formula:

$$
t=\frac{2bd \cos \frac{\psi}{2}}{b+d}
$$

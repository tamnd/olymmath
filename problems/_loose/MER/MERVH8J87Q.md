Given $n \geqslant 3\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right), n$ pairwise coprime positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ satisfy: it is possible to appropriately add " + " or " - " to make their algebraic sum 0. Ask: Does there exist a set of positive integers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ (allowing repetition), such that for any positive integer $k, b_{1}+k a_{1}, b_{2}+k a_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}+k a_{n}$ are pairwise coprime?
（Zhang Limin, Contributed）

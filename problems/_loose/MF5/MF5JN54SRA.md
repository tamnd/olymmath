Consider four points $P_1,\ P_2,\ P_3$ and $P_4$ on the coordinate plane with origin $O$ which satisfy the condition $$ \overrightarrow {{OP}}_{n-1}+\overrightarrow {{OP}}_{n+1}=\frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow {{OP}}_{n},\ (n=2,3). $$

(1) If $P_1,\ P_2$ lie on the curve $xy=1,$ then prove that $P_3$ doesn't lie on this curve.

(2) If $P_1,\ P_2$ and $P_3$ lie on the circle $x^2+y^2=1,$ then prove that $P_4$ lie on this circle as well.

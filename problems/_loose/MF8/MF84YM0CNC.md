The diagonal of a quadrilateral is the bisector of only one of its angles. Prove that the square of this diagonal is equal to

$$
a b+\frac{a c^{2}-b d^{2}}{a-b}
$$

where $a, b, c, d$ are the lengths of the sides of the quadrilateral, and the diagonal meets the sides of length $a$ and $b$ at one vertex.

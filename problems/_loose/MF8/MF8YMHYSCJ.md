Fifty cards (from 9th grade, 3 points). On the table, in a row, 50 cards numbered from 1 to 50 from left to right were laid out. The bottom of the cards is blue, and the top is red. Vasya randomly selects 25 consecutive cards (a segment of the row) and flips them. Then Asey approaches the table and does the same - selects a random segment of 25 consecutive cards and flips them, independently of what Vasya did earlier. For which card (or cards) is the probability of ending up red side up after all the flips less than for the others?

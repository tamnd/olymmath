Find the generating function for the following combination problems, and solve the corresponding problems:
(1) Find the number of $k$-combinations of $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Find the number of $k$-combinations of $\left\{\infty \cdot a_{1}, \infty \cdot a_{2}, \cdots, \infty \cdot a_{n}\right\}$;
(3) Find the number of 10-combinations of $\{3 \cdot a, 4 \cdot b, 5 \cdot c\}$;

where $\{3 \cdot a, 4 \cdot b, 5 \cdot c\}$ represents a set composed of 3 $a$'s, 4 $b$'s, and 5 $c$'s, etc.

On the board, all proper divisors of some composite natural number $n$, increased by 1, were written down. Find all such numbers $n$ for which the numbers on the board turn out to be all proper divisors of some natural number $m$. (Proper divisors of a natural number $a>1$ are all its natural divisors, different from $a$ and from 1.) (A. Khryabrov)

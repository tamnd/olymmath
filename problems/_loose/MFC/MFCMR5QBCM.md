6- 155 (1) Prove that if a continuous function $f: R \rightarrow R$ satisfies
$$
f(f(f(x))) \equiv x, x \in R,
$$

then for any $x \in R$ we have
$$
f(x)=x .
$$
(2) Provide an example of a (discontinuous) function $g: R \rightarrow R$ that satisfies $g(x) \neq x$ and $g(g(g(x))), x \in R$.

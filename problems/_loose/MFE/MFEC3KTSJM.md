If the sides and angles of triangle $ABC$ are $a, b, c$ and $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ respectively, and if $s=(a+b+c) / 2, s_{1}=s-a$, $s_{2}=s-b, s_{3}=s-c$, and if $\rho, \rho_{1}, \rho_{2}, \rho_{3}$ are the radii of the triangle's incircle and excircles, then it must be proven that the area of the triangle is:

$$
t=\rho \cdot s, t=\rho_{1} \cdot s_{1}, t=\rho_{2} \cdot s_{2}, t=\rho_{3} \cdot s_{3}
$$

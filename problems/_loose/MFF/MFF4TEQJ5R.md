[5 points] A water strider and a water beetle slide counterclockwise around a float on two circles. A rectangular coordinate system is introduced on the water surface, in which the float (the common center of the circles) is at the point ( $0 ; 0$ ). The speed of the water strider is twice the speed of the beetle. At the initial moment, the water strider and the beetle are at points $M_{0}(-2 ;-2 \sqrt{7})$ and $N_{0}(5 ; 5 \sqrt{7})$ respectively. Determine the coordinates of all positions of the beetle at which the distance between the insects will be the shortest.

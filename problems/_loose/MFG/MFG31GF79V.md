5, $S=\left\{n^{k} \mid n, k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \geq 2\right\}$ is the set of perfect powers, i.e., $S=\{1,4,8,9,16, \cdots\}$. Arrange the elements of $S$ in increasing order as the sequence $\left\{a_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$, prove: there exist infinitely many $n$, such that $9999 \mid\left(a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right)$.

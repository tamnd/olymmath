Above the valley looms a formidable castle. Along one of its walls, towering like a high cliff, are five towers, each containing a prisoner. Two of them are inveterate liars, two others never lie, and the last one can either lie or tell the truth.

One fine day, as the guard was distributing the daily rations of bread and water to the prisoners in turn, each of them sadly told him about his lady love.

The first said: "My dear Anna is a blonde, and the lady of the prisoner in the neighboring cell is also a blonde."

The second remarked: "My beloved Brünhild has red hair, and the ladies of the two neighboring prisoners are brunettes."

The third said: "My charming Clotilde has red hair. The ladies of the two neighboring prisoners also have red hair."

The fourth added: "My gentle Gudrun has red hair. The ladies of my neighboring prisoners are brunettes."

The fifth said: "My beautiful Yehanna is a brunette. The lady of the neighboring prisoner is also a brunette. As for the prisoner in the opposite tower, his lady is neither a brunette nor red-haired."

The guard pondered, trying to determine the color of Anna's, Brünhild's, Clotilde's, Gudrun's, and Yehanna's hair. What color are their hair?

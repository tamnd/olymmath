Let $ABC$ be a triangle and points $M, N, P$ such that $\overrightarrow{BM}=\overrightarrow{MC} ; \overrightarrow{AN}=2 \overrightarrow{NC} ; \overrightarrow{AP}=3 \overrightarrow{PB}$ and $Q$ is the midpoint of segment $(PM)$.

a) Show that $\overrightarrow{BN}=\frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{BC}+\frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{BA}$ and $\overrightarrow{BQ}=\frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{BC}+\frac{1}{8} \overrightarrow{BA}$;

b) Prove that points $B, Q, N$ are collinear and calculate the value of the ratio $\frac{BQ}{QN}$.

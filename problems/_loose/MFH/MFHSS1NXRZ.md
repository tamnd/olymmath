Alice and Bob have a chocolate bar of dimensions $m \times n (m \geqslant 2)$. Each in turn, starting with Alice, they choose a square and eat all the squares that are both below and to the right of the chosen square. The player who eats the square in the top left corner loses. Show that the initial position is a winning position for Alice.

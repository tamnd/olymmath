Determine the first six terms $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{6}$ of all those sequences $\left(a_{n}\right)$ of real numbers that have the following properties (1) to (5):

(1) It holds that $a_{1}=-\frac{5}{2}$

(2) It holds that $a_{5}=3$.

(3) $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}$ are (in this order) terms of an arithmetic sequence.

(4) $a_{4}, a_{5}, a_{6}$ are (in this order) terms of a geometric sequence.

(5) The sum of the first six terms of the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)$ is $\frac{13}{2}$.

In $A$ and $B$, there is one factory each producing the same product. Where is each factory competitive, if:

a) they have exchanged their methods of work and generally the working conditions in both plants can be considered the same, and thus the cost of production is the same in both places, but due to different site conditions, the transportation cost is different, with the unit transportation cost from $A$ being $c_{1}$ fillers per kilometer, and from $B$ being $c_{2}$ fillers per kilometer;

b) the production cost is different in the two plants (e.g., because the capacity of the factories is different), but the transportation cost is the same in both places;

c) if both the production cost and the unit transportation cost are the same in both places.

A positive integer is called a repunit, if it is written only by ones. The repunit with $n$ digits will be denoted as $\underbrace{{11\cdots1}}_{n}$ . Prove that:
α) the repunit $\underbrace{{11\cdots1}}_{n}$ is divisible by $37$ if and only if $n$ is divisible by $3$ b) there exists a positive integer $k$ such that the repunit $\underbrace{{11\cdots1}}_{n}$ is divisible by $41$ if $n$ is divisible by $k$

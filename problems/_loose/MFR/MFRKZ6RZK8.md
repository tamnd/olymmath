Their common external tangent touches circle $a$ at point $T_{a}$ and circle $b$ at point $T_{b}$. Using $r_{a}, r_{b}$, express the ratio of the radii of the circles $k_{a}$, $k_{b}$ circumscribed around the triangles $T_{a} A T$, $T_{b} B T$ respectively.

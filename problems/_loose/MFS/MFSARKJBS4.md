Let $a, b$ and $u$ be three real numbers, $a \neq 1$, and $\left(u_{n}\right)$ a sequence defined by: $u_{0}=u$ and for all $n \geqslant 0$, $u_{n+1}=a u_{n}+b$. Prove that for all $n \geqslant 0, u_{n}=a^{n} u+b \frac{a^{n}-1}{a-1}$.

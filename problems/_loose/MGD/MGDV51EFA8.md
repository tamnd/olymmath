For real numbers $x$ let $$f(x)=\frac{4^x}{25^{x+1}}+\frac{5^x}{2^{x+1}}.$$ Then $f\left(\frac{1}{1-\log_{10}4}\right)=\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.

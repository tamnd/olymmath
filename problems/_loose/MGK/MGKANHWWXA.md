Four students, Anja, Birgit, Christoph, and Dirk, played the following game, whose rules were known to all of them:

One of them, for example, Dirk, leaves the room. Now, one of the people, Anja, Birgit, or Christoph, takes a predetermined object, such as a thimble, and Dirk is called back in. He then receives statements from the other players, with the exact one who has the thimble making a false statement.

In one execution of this game, the statements were as follows:

Anja: I do not have the thimble, and Christoph has the thimble.

Birgit: Anja has the thimble, and I do not have the thimble.

Christoph: I do not have the thimble.

Investigate whether it can be conclusively determined from these statements which player took the thimble! If this is the case, identify this player!

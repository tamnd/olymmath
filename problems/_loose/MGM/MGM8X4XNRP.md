We draw a circle through the centers of the excircles of a triangle, and the center of this circle is $L$. (An excircle is a circle that is tangent to one side of the triangle and the extensions of the other two sides.) The orthocenter of the triangle is $M$, and the center of the incircle of the medial triangle is $K$. It needs to be proven that $K$ bisects the segment $LM$.

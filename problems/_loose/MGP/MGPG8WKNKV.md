Masha, preparing to receive guests, placed 13 oranges and 3 apples in 4 vases, with 4 fruits in each. Then her sister Sasha decided to change the composition of the fruits in the vases. She took one fruit from each vase at the same time and replaced each fruit with the opposite: an apple with an orange, and an orange with an apple. Or she replaced all four fruits in one vase with their opposites. Could Sasha end up with the same fruits in all 4 vases at the same time: only apples or only oranges?

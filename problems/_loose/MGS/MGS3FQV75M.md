Given the quadratic equation $3 a^{2} x^{2}+10 a \sqrt{b} x+3 b=0, b>0, a \neq 0$. Prove that if the roots of the equation, $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$, satisfy the condition

$$
3\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right)=5\left(x_{1} x_{2}+1\right)
$$

then they do not depend on $a$ and $b$.

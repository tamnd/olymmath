Let quadrilateral $A B C D$ be inscribed in a circle, and let the extensions of $B A$ and $C D$ meet at point $R$, and the extensions of $A D$ and $B C$ meet at point $P$. $\angle A$, $\angle B$, and $\angle C$ refer to the interior angles of $\triangle A B C$. Prove that if $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $Q$, then $\frac{\cos A}{A P}+\frac{\cos C}{C R}=\frac{\cos B}{B Q}$.

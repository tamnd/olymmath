(Olympiade Francophone 2020) Let $\mathbb{N}_{\geqslant 1}$ be the set of strictly positive integers. Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be a sequence of integers in $\mathbb{N}_{\geqslant 1}$ and let $m$ be an integer. Suppose that for any non-empty finite subset $S$ of $\mathbb{N}_{\geqslant 1}$, the number

$$
-1+\prod_{k \in S} a_{k}
$$

is a prime number.

Show that among the integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, only a finite number have fewer than $m$ distinct prime factors.

Inside the sides $AB, BC, CD$ and $DA$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $K, L, M$ and $N$ are chosen respectively. Let $S$ be the intersection of lines $KM$ and $LN$. If it is possible to inscribe circles in quadrilaterals $AKSN, BLSK, CMLS$ and $DNSM$, it is also possible to inscribe a circle in quadrilateral $ABCD$. Prove this.

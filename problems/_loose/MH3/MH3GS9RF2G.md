Let $ a,b,c\in\mathbb{C}^* $ pairwise distinct, having the same absolute value, and satisfying:
$$ a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca=0. $$
Prove that $ a,b,c $ represents the affixes of the vertices of a right or equilateral triangle.

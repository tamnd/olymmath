Consider the set of points in the plane with integer coordinates, on which tokens can potentially be placed. An operation consists of removing a token located at $(x, y)$ and placing one at $(x+1, y)$ and one at $(x, y+1)$ if neither of these two points already has a token; otherwise, the operation is impossible.

Initially, tokens are placed on each of the points $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$, and $(0,1)$. Prove that it is impossible to free these three points in a finite number of operations.

Find all functions $f:(0,+\infty) \rightarrow(0,+\infty)$, such that it is
1. increasing. and for all real numbers $a, b, c$, the following relation holds:
$$
f(a b) f(b) f(c a)=f\left(a^{2} b^{2} a^{2}\right)+f\left(a^{2}\right)+f\left(b^{2}\right)+f\left(c^{2}\right) .
$$

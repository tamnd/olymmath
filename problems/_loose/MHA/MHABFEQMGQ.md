Let's drop a perpendicular from the point $P_{1}(a, b, c)$ in the spatial rectangular coordinate system to the line $e$ passing through the origin and the point $(1; 1; 1)$ in the plane determined by $P_{1}$ and $e$. Prove that the intersection point of the perpendicular and $e$ coincides with the centroid of the triangle formed by $P_{1}(a, b, c), P_{2}(b, c, a), P_{3}(c, a, b)$.

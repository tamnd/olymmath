The inscribed circle of the triangle $A B C$ touches the sides $A B, B C$ and $C A$ at the points $D, E$ and $F$. Let $P$ be a point in the interior of $A B C$ so that the inscribed circle of $A B P$ also touches the side $A B$ in $D$ and the sides $A P$ and $B P$ in the points $Q$ and $R$. Show that the four points $E, F, R$ and $Q$ lie on a circle.

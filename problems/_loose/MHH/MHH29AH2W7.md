In the $xy$-plane, the set of $(x,\ y)$ with integers is called lattice point. Consider a triangle ${ABC}$ whose vertices are lattice point.

(1) If there are odd number's lattice point on the sides $AB,\ AC$ except their endpoints respectively, then show that there is odd number's lattice point on the side $BC$ except its endpoints as well.

(2) If there are exactly three lattice points on each of the sides $AB,\ AC$ except their endpoints respectively, then the area of triangle ${ABC}$ is integer which is devisible by 8.

*1992 Tokyo University entrance exam/Humanities*

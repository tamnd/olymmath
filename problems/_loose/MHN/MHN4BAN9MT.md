Given a cube $A B C D-A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ with edge length 1, take point $M$ on the diagonal $A_{1} D$ and point $N$ on $C D_{1}$, such that the line segment $M N$ is parallel to the diagonal face $A_{1} A C C_{1}$. Then the minimum value of $M N$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
(B) 1
(C) $\sqrt{2}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

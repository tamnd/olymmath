A staircase has $100$ steps. Kolya wishes to descend the staircase by alternately jumping down some steps and then up some. The possible jumps he can do are through $6$ (i.e. over $5$ and landing on the $6$ th) , $7$ or $8$ steps . He also does not wish to land twice on the same step . Can he descend the staircase in this way?

( S . Fomin, Leningrad)

Given a square $A B C D$. Prove that for all points $P$ on the arc $A B$ of the circle circumscribed about the square, which does not contain the points $C$ and $D$, the expression

$$
\frac{|A P|+|B P|}{|C P|+|D P|}
$$

has the same value. Determine it.

We have drawn the angle trisectors of the $A B C$ triangle. Let us call the first trisector of the angle at vertex $A$ the ray that forms half the angle with $A B$ as it does with $A C$. Similarly, the first trisector of angle $B$ forms half the angle with $B C$ as it does with $B A$, and the first trisector of angle $C$ forms half the angle with $C A$ as it does with $C B$. Calculate the angles of the triangle if the two angles of the triangle formed by the first trisectors are $45^{\circ}$ and $55^{\circ}$. What are the angles of the triangle formed by the second trisectors?

In the plane, there are two lines; on one of them, there are four points: $A, B, C$, and $D$; on the other, there are also four points: $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$. Construct a point $X$ on the first line and a point $X^{\prime}$ on the second line such that:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A X: B X=A^{\prime} X^{\prime}: B^{\prime} X^{\prime} \\
& C X: D X=C^{\prime} X^{\prime}: D^{\prime} X^{\prime}
\end{aligned}
$$

(From Dr. Lipót Klug, a university private lecturer in Budapest.)

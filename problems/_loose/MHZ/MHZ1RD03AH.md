Find the number of 16 -tuples $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{16}\right)$ such that
(i) $x_{i}= \pm 1$ for $i=1, \ldots, 16$,
(ii) $0 \leq x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{r}<4$, for $r=1,2, \ldots, 15$,
(iii) $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{10}=4$.

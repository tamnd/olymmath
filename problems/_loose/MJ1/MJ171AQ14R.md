Let $n \ge 3$ be an integer. Prove that there exist positive integers $\ge 2$ , $a_1,a_2,..,a_n$ , such that $a_1 a_2 ... \widehat{a_i}... a_n \equiv 1$ (mod $a_i$ ), for $i = 1,..., n$ . Here $\widehat{a_i}$ means the term $a_i$ is omitted.

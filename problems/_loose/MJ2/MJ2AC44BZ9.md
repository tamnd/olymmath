Establish that, for any sets $ A_{n} \subseteq \Omega, n \geqslant 1 $, the following equality holds:

$$
\overline{\lim } A_{n}=\bigcup_{n_{\infty}} \underline{\lim } A_{n_{k}},
$$

where the union is taken over all increasing sequences $ n_{\infty}=\left(n_{1}, n_{2}, \ldots\right) $ of natural numbers.

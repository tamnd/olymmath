Consider ten arbitrary integers, $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots a_{10}$. Show that one can choose, from $\{-1,0,1\}$, ten numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots b_{10}$ not all zero such that: $b_{1} a_{1}+b_{2} a_{2}+\ldots+b_{10} a_{10}$ is divisible by 1000.

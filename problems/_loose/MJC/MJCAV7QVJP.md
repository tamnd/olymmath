On a desk in a classroom stands a scale, with weights in its pans. Initially, the right pan is heavier. Among the weights, no two are the same. Each weight has the name of one or more (at least one) students. When someone enters the room, the weight(s) with their name (if any) are moved to the other pan of the scale. Prove that it is possible to send some students in one after another so that eventually the left pan will be the heavier.

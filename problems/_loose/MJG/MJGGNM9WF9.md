In the plane of quadrilateral $A B C D$, a point $M$ is given. Points $M_{1}$, $M_{2}$, and $M_{3}$ are constructed as follows: $M_{1}$ is symmetric to $M$ with respect to the midpoint of side $A B$; $M_{2}$ is symmetric to $M_{1}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $B C$; $M_{3}$ is symmetric to $M_{2}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $C D$. Prove that points $M_{3}$ and $M$ are symmetric with respect to the midpoint of side $D A$.

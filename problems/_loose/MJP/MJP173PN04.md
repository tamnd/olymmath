Show that for the Poisson distribution, the following local theorem holds: for each $k=0,1,2, \ldots$ and $\lambda \rightarrow \infty$ the condition

$$
\sqrt{\lambda}\left|\frac{\lambda^{k}}{k!} e^{-\lambda}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2 \pi \lambda}} \exp \left\{-\frac{1}{2 \lambda}(k-\lambda)^{2}\right\}\right| \rightarrow 0
$$

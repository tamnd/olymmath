If $\sqrt{2} x>\sqrt{3} x+1$, then
$$
\sqrt[3]{(x+2)^{3}}-\sqrt{(x+3)^{2}}
$$

equals
(A) $2 x+5$.
(B) $-2 x-5$.
(C) 1 .
(D) -1 .
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

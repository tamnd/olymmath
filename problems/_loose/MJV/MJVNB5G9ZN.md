Prove that if two equations with integer coefficients:

$$
X^{2}+p_{1} X+q_{1}=0 \text { and } X^{2}+p_{2} X+q_{2}=0
$$

have a common non-integer root, then $p_{1}=p_{2}, q_{1}=q_{2}$.

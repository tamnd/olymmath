For two functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ defined on the interval $[p, q]$, if for any $x \in [p, q]$, we have $|f(x) - g(x)| \leqslant 1$, then $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ are said to be close functions on the interval $[p, q]$. Otherwise, they are said to be non-close functions on $[p, q]$. Now, consider two functions $f_{1}(x) = \log _{a}(x - 3a)$ and $f_{2}(x) = \log _{a} \frac{1}{x - a}$ (where $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$), and a given interval $[a+2, a+3]$.
(1) If $f_{1}(x)$ and $f_{2}(x)$ are both defined on $[a+2, a+3]$, find the range of values for $a$;
(2) Discuss whether $f_{1}(x)$ and $f_{2}(x)$ are close functions on the interval $[a+2, a+3]$.

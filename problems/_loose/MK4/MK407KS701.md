On a field, $2013 \times 2013$ chairs are placed at the vertices of a grid. Each chair is occupied by a person. Some people decide to change places: some move one step to the right, others move two steps forward, others move three steps to the left, and others move six steps backward. In the end, each chair is still occupied by one person.

Prove that at least one person did not change their place.

Let $\varphi_{k}=\varphi_{k}(t), t \in \mathbb{R}, k \geqslant 1,$ be characteristic functions. Show that for any non-negative numbers $\lambda_{k}, \sum \lambda_{k}=1$, the function $\varphi=\sum \lambda_{k} \varphi_{k}$ is a characteristic function.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be equilateral with side length $6$ . Suppose $P$ is a point on the same plane as $\triangle ABC$ satisfying $PB=2PC$ . The smallest possible length of segment $PA$ can be expressed in the form $a+b\sqrt{c}$ , where $a,b,c$ are integers, and $c$ is not divisible by any square greater than $1$ . What is the value of $a+b+c$ ?

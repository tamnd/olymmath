In a circle with center $O$, we draw the diameter $A O B$; from $O$ we draw a random radius, which intersects the circumference at $C$; from $C$ we drop a perpendicular to $O A$, the foot of which is $D$. Determine the point $M$ on $O C$ such that $O M=C D$. What is the geometric locus of the point $M$?

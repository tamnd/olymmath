For each non-zero complex number $ z,$ let $\arg(z)$ be the unique real number $ t$ such that $ -\pi < t \leq \pi$ and $ z = |z|(\cos(t) + \textrm{i} sin(t)).$ Given a real number $ c > 0$ and a complex number $ z \neq 0$ with $\arg z \neq \pi,$ define $$ B(c, z) = \{b \in \mathbb{R} \ ; \ |w - z| < b \Rightarrow |\arg(w) - \arg(z)| < c\}.$$ Determine necessary and sufficient conditions, in terms of $ c$ and $ z,$ such that $ B(c, z)$ has a maximum element, and determine what this maximum element is in this case.

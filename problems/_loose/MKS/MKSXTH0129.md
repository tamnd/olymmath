Let $x_{0}=a, x_{1}=b$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers and

$$
x_{n+1}=2 x_{n}-9 x_{n-1}, \text { for } n \geq 1
$$

Determine a necessary and sufficient condition for $a$ and $b$ such that there exists a term in the sequence that is divisible by 7.

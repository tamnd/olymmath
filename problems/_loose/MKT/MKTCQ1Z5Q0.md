A fraction has both its numerator and denominator as positive integers, and the numerator is 1 less than the denominator. If both the numerator and the denominator are decreased by 1, the resulting fraction is a positive number less than $\frac{6}{7}$. Then the number of fractions that satisfy the above conditions is
(A) 5.
(B) 6.
(C) 7.
(D) 8.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational, 1991)

Inside a square with side 1, there are $n$ points. Prove that:

a) the area of one of the triangles with vertices at these points or at the vertices of the square does not exceed $1 /(2(n+1))$;

b) the area of one of the triangles with vertices at these points does not exceed $1 /(n-2)$.

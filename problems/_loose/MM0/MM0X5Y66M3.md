Let's prove that

$$
\binom{n+t}{n}-\binom{n}{1}\binom{n+t-1}{n}+\binom{n}{2}\binom{n+t-2}{n}+\cdots+(-1)^{n}\binom{n}{n}\binom{t}{n}=1
$$

if $t$ is any integer and $n$ is a positive integer, where

$$
\binom{t}{n}=\frac{t(t-1)(t-2) \ldots(t-n+1)}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdots \cdots n}
$$

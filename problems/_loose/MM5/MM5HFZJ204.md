The Mysterious Inheritance. Someone bequeathed a sum of money, which was slightly less than 1500 dollars, to be distributed as follows. Five of his children and a notary received such amounts that the square root of the eldest son's share, half of the second son's share, the third son's share minus 2 dollars, the fourth son's share plus 2 dollars, twice the daughter's share, and the square of the notary's fee were equal to each other. All heirs and the notary received whole dollar amounts, and the entire sum of money was used for the distribution of the inheritance and the notary's fee. What was the amount left in the inheritance?

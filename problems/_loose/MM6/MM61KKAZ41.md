Let there be a pentagon $A B C D E$ in which $C D=A B+A E$. Let the bisector of $\measuredangle B A E$ intersect $B E$ at point $P$, let $Q$ be a point on $C D$ such that $C Q=A B$, and let $X$ and $Y$ be the midpoints of segments $B C$ and $D E$, respectively. Let $T$ be the centroid of $\triangle A P Q$ and let $Y T$ intersect $A X$ at point $Z$. Find $\frac{A Z}{Z X}$ as a function of $A B$ and $A E$.

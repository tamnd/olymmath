$\mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{n}}$, and $\mathrm{y}_{1}, \mathrm{y}_{2}, \ldots, \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{n}}$ are non-negative reals. Show that $\sum \min \left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{j}}, \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{j}}\right) \leq \sum \min \left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{j}}, \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{j}} \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{i}}\right)$, where each sum is taken over all $\mathrm{n}^{2}$ pairs $(\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{j})$.

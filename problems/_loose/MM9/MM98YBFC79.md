Given are two real numbers $m \neq 0$ and $n$. Furthermore, let $f$ be the function defined by $f(x)=m x+n$ for all real numbers.

a) Determine all numbers $x_{0}$ for which $2 \cdot f\left(x_{0}\right)=f\left(x_{0}+2\right)$ holds (i.e., for which the function value at $x_{0}+2$ is twice as large as the one at $x_{0}$) when $m=1$ and $n=0$!

b) Determine all numbers $x_{0}$ for which $2 \cdot f\left(x_{0}\right)=f\left(x_{0}+2\right)$ holds for any given real numbers $m \neq 0$ and $n$!

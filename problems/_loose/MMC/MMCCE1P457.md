A hundred mages and a hundred alchemists gathered together to turn iron into gold. Each mage took three magic wands. For the experiment to be successful, a hundred people from those gathered must sit at a round table, and each mage sitting at the table must point their magic wands at two neighbors and the person sitting directly opposite. The iron will turn into gold only if the following two conditions are met:
1) among any four people sitting in a row, there should be an equal number of mages and alchemists;
2) each mage sitting at the table must point their magic wands at least at two mages.

Will the mages and alchemists be able to turn iron into gold?

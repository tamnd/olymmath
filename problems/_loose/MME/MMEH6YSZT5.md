Let $a, b, c$ and $d$ be positive real numbers such that $a+b+c+d=4$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a}{a^{3}+5}+\frac{b}{b^{3}+5}+\frac{c}{c^{3}+5}+\frac{d}{d^{3}+5} \leqslant \frac{2}{3}
$$

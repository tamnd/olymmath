Two circles of equal radius intersect, the distance between their centers is less than their radii. From one of the centers, we draw a ray in an arbitrary direction to the intersection point with the other circle, then from the other center, we draw a parallel and co-directional ray to the intersection point with the first circle. Prove that the distance between the two intersection points is independent of the direction chosen.

Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$, and one of their common tangents touches them at points $T$ and $U$. A secant line through $A$ parallel to $T U$ intersects the circles again at points $C$ and $D$. On this line, $E$ is a point (different from $A$) such that $E C: E D = A C: A D$. The circles passing through points $A, B, E$ and $A, T, U$ intersect at $F$. It is to be proven that $A F$ is perpendicular to $T U$.

Two candidates participated in an election with $p+q$ voters. Candidate $A$ received $p$ votes and candidate $B$ received $q$ votes, with $p>q$. During the counting, only one vote is recorded at a time on a board. Let $r$ be the probability that the number associated with candidate $A$ on the board is always greater than the number associated with candidate $B$ throughout the entire counting process.

a) Determine the value of $r$ if $p=3$ and $q=2$.

b) Determine the value of $r$ if $p=1010$ and $q=1009$.

Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers such that $x y z=1$. Prove that

$$
\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}+z}{x^{2}+2}+\frac{y^{2}+z^{2}+x}{y^{2}+2}+\frac{z^{2}+x^{2}+y}{z^{2}+2} \geq 3
$$

When does equality hold?

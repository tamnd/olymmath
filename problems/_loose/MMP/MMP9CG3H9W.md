Let the ratios $a: b$ and $c: d$ be equal, i.e., $a: b=c: d$. If the dividend $a$ is greater than the divisor $b$, then the dividend $c$ is greater than $d$. Take an arbitrary positive number $a$. Since $a:(-a)=-1$ and $(-a): a=-1$, we have $a:(-a)=(-a): a$. But $a>-a$, so based on the previous conclusion, from the right part of the equality we get $-a>a$. We have reached a contradiction. Find the error.

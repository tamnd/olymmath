Let's construct the pentagon $ABCDE$, given the consecutive sides $AB=a, BC=b, CD=c, DE=d, EA=e$, the angle $\angle ABC=\alpha$, and the angle $\angle CAD=\varphi$.

- (For 1st year students, it is enough to construct the case where $a=6, b=9/2, c=7.2, d=8.5, e=12, \alpha=120^{\circ}, \varphi=30^{\circ}$.)

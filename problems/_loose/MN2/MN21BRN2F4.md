Given that when $x \in[0, \sqrt{3}]$, $\left|x^{3}+\frac{3}{2}(1-a) x^{2}-3 a x+b\right| \leq 1$ always holds,
(1) Prove: The equation $x^{3}+\frac{3}{2}(1-a) x^{2}-3 a x+b=0$ has three real roots $x_{1}<x_{2}<x_{3}$,
(2) Find $\left(x_{1}^{2}-2-x_{2}\right)\left(x_{2}^{2}-2-x_{3}\right)\left(x_{3}^{2}-2-x_{1}\right)$.

Given a triangle $A B C$ and a point $M$. A line passing through $M$ intersects the lines $A B, B C$, and $C A$ at points $C_{1}, A_{1}$, and $B_{1}$, respectively. The lines $A M, B M$, and $C M$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ at points $A_{2}, B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$, respectively. Prove that the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}$, and $C_{1} C_{2}$ intersect at a single point located on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$.

Given a quadrilateral prism, whose base $(A B C D)$ is in the first picture plane. Furthermore, a point $O$ is given inside the prism. A rhombus $P Q R S$ needs to be constructed, whose center is $O$. The four vertices of the rhombus are on the four faces of the prism, and the first picture plane angles of the two diagonals of the rhombus are equal.

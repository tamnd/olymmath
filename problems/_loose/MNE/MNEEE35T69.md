Prove: there exist infinitely many pairs of positive integers $(a, b)$, satisfying the following three conditions:
(1) $(a, b)=1$;
(2) $a \mid\left(b^{2}-5\right)$;
(3) $b \mid\left(a^{2}-5\right)$.

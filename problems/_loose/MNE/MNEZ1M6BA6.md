Show the de Moivre's formula:

$$
(\cos (\theta)+i \sin (\theta))^{n}=\cos (n \theta)+i \sin (n \theta)
$$

Deduce that if $\cos (\theta)$ and $\sin (\theta)$ are rational, then $\cos (n \theta)$ and $\sin (n \theta)$ are also rational.

Let $n\ge 2$ be an integer and let $P(X)=X^n+a_{n-1}X^{n-1}+\ldots +a_1X+1$ be a polynomial with positive integer coefficients. Suppose that $a_k=a_{n-k}$ for all $k\in 1,2,\ldots,n-1$ . Prove that there exist infinitely many pairs of positive integers $x,y$ such that $x|P(y)$ and $y|P(x)$ .

*Remus Nicoara*

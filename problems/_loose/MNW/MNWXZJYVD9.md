Sírva találta. Erest egykor Kypris. Szeretettel

When asked what caused her distress... she sadly replied:

'Apples, those I brought from Helicon to my bosom.'

After tearing at me, - here these Muses stole them bit by bit:

One fifth was taken by Clio; then a twelfth disappeared

In the hands of Euterpe; an eighth was scattered by Thalia.

A twentieth by Melpomene. And a quarter by Terpsichore

Was carried away. A seventh was claimed by Erato.

Then a thirtieth was claimed by Polyhymnia;

And four times as much was taken by noble Urania

Clio with three hundred of my beautiful apples made off.

Now I return home empty-handed. See how many are left:

They did not leave more than fifty in my bosom.

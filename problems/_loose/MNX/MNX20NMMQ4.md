Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle A = 62^\circ$, $\angle B = 64^\circ$ and $D$, $E$ the feet of the altitudes lying on $AB$, $AC$ respectively. Suppose the intersection points of the line $DE$ and the circumcircle at $\widehat{AB}$, $\widehat{AC}$ are $P$, $Q$ respectively, then $\angle QAE - \angle PAD = \underline{\underline{\stackrel{\bigcirc}{9}\ \stackrel{\bigcirc}{8}}}^\circ$.

On a piece of paper, the positive integers $1,2, \cdots, n$ are written. In the first step, the first 4 numbers are crossed out, and the sum of these 4 numbers, 10, is written at the end of the sequence; in the second step, the next 4 numbers $5,6,7,8$ are crossed out, and their sum, 26, is written at the end; and so on (i.e., in each step, the first 4 numbers are crossed out, and the sum of these 4 numbers is written at the end).
(1) If only one number remains at the end, what is the necessary and sufficient condition for $n$?
(2) For $n=2002$, what is the total sum of all the numbers written (including the original $1,2, \cdots, 2002$) until only one number remains?
(2002 Shanghai High School Competition Problem)

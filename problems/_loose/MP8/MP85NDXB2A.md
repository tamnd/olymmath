Given three points $A, B, C$, not lying on the same straight line. Prove that any straight line not passing through points $A, B$ and $C$ either does not intersect any of the segments $B C, C A$ and $A B$, or intersects exactly two of them.

Note that in some geometry courses, the statement of this problem is accepted as an axiom, known as Pasch's axiom ${ }^{1}$.

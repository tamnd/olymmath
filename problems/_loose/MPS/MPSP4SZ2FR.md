There is a system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
* x+* y+* z=0 \\
* x+* y+* z=0 \\
* x+* y+* z=0
\end{array}\right.
$$

Two people take turns filling in the asterisks with numbers. Prove that the one who starts can always ensure that the system has a non-zero solution.

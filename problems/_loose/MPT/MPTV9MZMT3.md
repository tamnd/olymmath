In a plane, if there is a point that is equidistant from the four vertices of a convex quadrilateral, then this point
(A) is inside the quadrilateral.
(B) is outside the quadrilateral.
(C) is on one side.
(D) any of the above is possible.
(2nd China Tianjin "Chinese Youth Cup" Mathematics Invitational, 1988)

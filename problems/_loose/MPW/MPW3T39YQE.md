The function $f(x)=\sqrt{1-x}(x \leq 1)$ is given. Let $F(x)=f(f(x))$.

a) Solve the equations $f(x)=x$ and $F(x)=x$.

b) Solve the inequality $F(x)>x$.

c) If $a_{0} \in(0,1)$, prove that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$, determined by $a_{n}=f\left(a_{n-1}\right)$ for $n=$ $1,2, \ldots$ is convergent and find its limit.

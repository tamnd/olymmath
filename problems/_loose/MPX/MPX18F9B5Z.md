a circle $k_{1}$ lies inside a second circle $k_{2}$ and touches it at point $A$. A straight line through $A$ intersects $k_{1}$ again in $B$ and $k_{2}$ in $C$. The tangent to $k_{1}$ through $B$ intersects $k_{2}$ at the points $D$ and $E$. The tangents to $k_{1}$ through $C$ touch $k_{1}$ at the points $F$ and $G$. Prove that $D, E, F$ and $G$ lie on a circle.

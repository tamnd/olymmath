Three students are discussing the relationship between the numbers 1 and $\frac{2}{x-10}$ for real numbers $x \neq 10$. They observe:

For $x=11$, $\frac{2}{x-10}=2$, so $1<\frac{2}{x-10}$.

Then Marion claims: The equation $1=\frac{2}{x-10}$ holds exactly for $x=12$.

Norbert claims: The inequality $1<\frac{2}{x-10}$ holds exactly for all $x>12$.

Investigate whether each of these three statements is true or false!

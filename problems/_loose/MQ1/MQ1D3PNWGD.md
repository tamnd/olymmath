A closed, possibly self-intersecting broken line is symmetric with respect to a point $O$ not lying on it. Prove that the number of turns of the broken line around $O$ is odd. (The number of turns around $O$ is the sum of the oriented angles $\angle A_1OA_2 + \angle A_2OA_3 + \ldots + \angle A_{n-1}OA_n + \angle A_nOA_1$, divided by $2\pi$.)

Two sequences of numbers $\left(x_{n}\right)$ and $\left(y_{n}\right)$ are constructed such that $x_{1}=x_{2}=10, y_{1}=y_{2}=-10$ and

$$
x_{n+2}=\left(x_{n}+1\right) x_{n+1}+1, y_{n+2}=\left(y_{n+1}+1\right) y_{n}+1
$$

for all natural $n$. Prove that any natural number appears in at most one of these sequences.

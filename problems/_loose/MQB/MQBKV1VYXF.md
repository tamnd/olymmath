In the acute-angled triangle $ABC$, tangents are drawn to its circumcircle at points $A$, $B$, and $C$. Consider the triangle $PQR$ whose sides are these tangents, with the labeling chosen such that $C$ lies on the side $PQ$, $B$ on the side $PR$, and $A$ on the side $QR$.

Let $C_{1}$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ of triangle $ABC$ on side $AB$. Prove that $CC_{1}$ bisects the angle $QC_{1}P$.

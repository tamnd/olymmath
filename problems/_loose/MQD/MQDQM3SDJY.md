Let $ABC$ be a non-isosceles triangle. Let $\omega$ be the incircle and $I$ its center. We denote by $M, N, P$ the points of tangency of $\omega$ with the sides $[BC], [CA], [AB]$. Let $J$ be the intersection point between $(MN)$ and $(IC)$. The line $(PJ)$ intersects $\omega$ again at $K$. Show that
a) $CKIP$ is cyclic;
b) $(CI)$ is the bisector of $\widehat{PC K}$.

There is a new type of problem-solving robot that can solve problems, but it has an IQ balance limit. Each time it solves a problem, it subtracts the score of the problem from its IQ balance, consuming an amount of IQ balance equal to the score. When it solves a problem correctly, its IQ balance increases by 1. If its IQ balance is less than the score of the problem it is solving, it will fail to solve the problem. If Xiaopeng uses a problem-solving robot with an initial IQ limit of 25 to solve a set of problems with scores ranging from $1-10$, what is the maximum score he can achieve? $ $

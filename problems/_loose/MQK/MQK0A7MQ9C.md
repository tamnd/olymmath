(1) Let $M\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ be a fixed point on the parabola $y^{2}=2 x$. Draw two perpendicular chords $M P$ and $M Q$ through $M$. Prove that the line $P Q$ always passes through the fixed point $M^{\prime}\left(x_{0}+2,-y_{0}\right)$.

(2) The line $x+m y+1=0$ intersects the parabola $y=2 x$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Is there any point $M$ on the parabola such that $\triangle M P Q$ is a right-angled triangle with $P Q$ as the hypotenuse?

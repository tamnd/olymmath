Tanks $A$ and $B$, which have the same cylindrical shape, have a volume of $1000 \ell$. Tank $A$ contains $500 \ell$, and tank $B$ contains $200 \ell$ of water. Water is transferred from tank $A$ to tank $B$ through a pipe. Every minute, $5.5 \ell$ of water flows through.

(a) How much water is in tank $A$ after 10 minutes?

(b) After how long will tank $B$ contain $530 \ell$ of water?

(c) Calculate after how long the amount of water in both tanks will be the same. Round the result to the nearest minute.

(d) To what height is tank $A$ filled at the start of the transfer if the diameter of the tank is $60 \mathrm{~cm}$? Round the result to two decimal places.

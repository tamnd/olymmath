Let the numbers $a, b, c > 0$ and the non-zero numbers $x, y, z$ be such that $a x + b y + c z = 0$. Show that the expression

$$
E(x, y, z) = \frac{b c(y-z)^{2} + c a(z-x)^{2} + a b(x-y)^{2}}{a x^{2} + b y^{2} + c z^{2}}
$$

does not depend on $x, y, z$.

Alternative formulation. Two identical packs of $n$ different cards are shuffled together; all arrangements are equiprobable. The cards are then laid face up, one at a time. For every natural number $n$, find out which is more probable, that at least one pair of identical cards will appear in immediate succession or that there will be no such pair.

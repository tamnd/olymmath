Each morning in the parliament of Tabulistan begins with the following procedure. The president sticks a piece of paper with an arbitrary natural number from 1 to 2015 on the forehead of each of the 2015 deputies (the same number can be written on several pieces of paper). The deputies can see the numbers on their colleagues' foreheads but cannot see their own. Then, simultaneously, each one says out loud what number they think is written on their forehead. If at least one guesses correctly, the parliament adjourns for a break. Can the deputies agree in advance so that they never have to work at all?

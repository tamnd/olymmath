In the plane, there are two circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$ with different radii, which are externally tangent at point $T$. Consider any two points $A \in k_{1}$ and $B \in k_{2}$, both different from point $T$ and chosen such that the angle $A T B$ is a right angle.

a) Prove that all considered lines $A B$ pass through the same point.

b) Find the set of midpoints of all such segments $A B$.

Find all positive real numbers $a$, such that for any positive real numbers $t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}, t_{4}$ satisfying $t_{1} \cdot t_{2} \cdot t_{3} \cdot t_{4}=a^{4}$, we have
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t_{1}}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t_{2}}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t_{3}}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t_{4}}} \leqslant \frac{4}{\sqrt{1+a}} \text {. }
$$

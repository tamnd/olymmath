Let $e$ and $f$ be intersecting lines with points $A$ and $B$, and $C$ and $D$, respectively, such that $AB = CD$. Draw lines parallel to $AC$ through $B$ and $D$, and lines parallel to $BD$ through $A$ and $C$. Show that one of the diagonals of the resulting parallelogram lies on one of the angle bisectors of the pair of lines $e$, $f$.

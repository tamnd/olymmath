We have two groups of tennis players: one consists of 1000 players, and the other consists of 1001 players. Within each group, we know the ranking of the players. Provide a procedure that, after playing 11 matches, can determine who is the median (i.e., the 1001st) player out of the 2001 players. (We assume that all players have different strengths, and the stronger player always beats the weaker one.)

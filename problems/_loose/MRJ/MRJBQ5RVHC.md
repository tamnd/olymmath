Using the formula $\left(1-t^{2}\right)^{-n}=(1+t)^{-n}(1-t)^{-n}$, prove the following identities:
(1) $\sum_{k=0}^{2 s}(-1)^{k}\binom{n+k-1}{k}\binom{n+2 s-k-1}{2 s-k}=\binom{n+s-1}{s}$.
(2) $\sum_{k=0}^{2 s+1}(-1)^{k}\binom{n+k-1}{k}\binom{n+2 s-k}{2 s+1-k}=0$.

An archipelago consists of $n$ islands, between any two of which there is a ferry. The fare on each ferry is the same in both directions, but the cost is different on any two different ferries. A traveler wants to fly to one of the islands by helicopter and then sail on $n-1$ ferries in such a way that each time he pays less than he did before. The fact that he may end up on the same island several times does not bother him. He can fly to any island by helicopter, and all the fare costs are known to him. Prove that he can accomplish his plan.

Suppose $a_{1}, \cdots, a_{10}$ and $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{10}$ are sequences composed of distinct complex numbers. It is known that for $i=1,2, \cdots, 10$, we have $\left(a_{1}+b_{i}\right)\left(a_{2}+b_{i}\right) \cdots\left(a_{10}+b_{i}\right)=$ 100. Prove that for any $j=1,2, \cdots, 10$, the product $\left(b_{1}+a_{j}\right)\left(b_{2}+a_{j}\right) \cdots\left(b_{10}+a_{j}\right)$ is equal to the same constant, and find this constant.

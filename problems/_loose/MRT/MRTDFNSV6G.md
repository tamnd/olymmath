$A, B, C$ three people went to Teacher $D$'s house to play. Teacher $D$ gave each of them a hat and wrote a four-digit number on each hat. It is known that these three four-digit numbers are all perfect squares (for example, $4=2^{2}, 100=10^{2}, 4.100$ are all squares of some number, such numbers are called perfect squares), and the tens digit of these three four-digit numbers is 0, and the units digit is not 0. Each child can only see the numbers on others' hats. These three children are very smart and honest, and the following conversation took place:
$A$ said: "The units digit of the numbers on $B$ and $C$'s hats are the same."
$B$ and $C$ said at the same time: "After hearing what $A$ said, I know my number."
$A$ said: "After hearing what $B$ and $C$ said, I also know my number, and the units digit of my number is an even number."
Find: The sum of the numbers on $A, B, C$'s hats.

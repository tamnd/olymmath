On a $23 \times 23$ grid, there are four chips: white chips are placed in the bottom-left and top-right corners, and black chips are placed in the top-left and bottom-right corners. White and black chips take turns moving, with the white chips starting. Each move involves sliding one chip to any adjacent (by side) free cell. The white chips aim to end up in two adjacent (by side) cells. Can the black chips prevent them from doing so?

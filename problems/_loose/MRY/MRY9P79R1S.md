If positive numbers $a, b, c$ satisfy the equation $a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3}-3 a b c=0$, then
(A) $a=b=c$.
(B) $a=b \neq c$.
(C) $b=c \neq a$.
(D) $a, b, c$ are all unequal.
(1st "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

Given a circle $k$ with diameter $EF$, the line $e$ is tangent to the circle at point $E$. Consider all pairs of points $A, B$ on line $e$ such that segment $AB$ contains point $E$ and $AE \cdot EB$ is a fixed constant. For such a pair of points, let $A'$ and $B'$ be the intersection points of the circle $k$ with segments $AF$ and $BF$, respectively. Prove that the segments $A'B'$ all pass through a single point.

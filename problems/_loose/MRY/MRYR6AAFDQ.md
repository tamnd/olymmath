For the real numbers $x$ and $y$ , $[\sqrt{x}] = 10$ and $[\sqrt{y}] =14$ .
How large is $\left[\sqrt{[ \sqrt{x+y} ]}\right]$ ?

(Note: the square roots are the positive values and $[x]$ is the largest integer less than or equal to x.)

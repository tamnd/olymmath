On the first of December last year, a student started preparing for the regional mathematics competition, which was held on the first of March this year. Every day he solved one problem, but to avoid overworking, during one week (from Monday to Sunday) he solved no more than twelve problems. Show that during his preparation for the competition, there exist several consecutive days during which the student solved exactly 21 problems.

(New York, 78). The function $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ satisfies the identity

$$
f(x y) \equiv \frac{f(x)+f(y)}{x+y}, x, y \in \mathbf{R}, x+y \neq 0
$$

Does there exist a value $x \in \mathbb{R}$ for which $f(x) \neq 0$?

A Gaussian prime is a Gaussian integer $ z=a+bi$ (where $ a$ and $ b$ are integers) with no Gaussian integer factors of smaller absolute value. Factor $ -4+7i$ into Gaussian primes with positive real parts. $ i$ is a symbol with the property that $ i^2=-1$ .

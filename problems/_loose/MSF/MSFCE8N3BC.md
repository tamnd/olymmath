a) It is known that $x+y=u+v, x^{2}+y^{2}=u^{2}+v^{2}$.

Prove that for any natural number $n$, the equality $x^{n}+y^{n}=u^{n}+v^{n}$ holds.

b) It is known that $x+y+z=u+v+t, x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=u^{2}+v^{2}+t^{2}, x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}=u^{3}+v^{3}+t^{3}$.

Prove that for any natural number $n$, the equality $x^{n}+y^{n}+z^{n}=u^{n}+v^{n}+t^{n}$ holds.

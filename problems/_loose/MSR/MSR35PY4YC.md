Given a circle $S$ and a line $l$ that do not have common points. From a point $P$, moving along the line $l$, tangents $P A$ and $P B$ are drawn to the circle $S$. Prove that all chords $A B$ have a common point.

If a point $P$ lies outside the circle $S$, and $P A$ and $P B$ are tangents to the circle, then the line $A B$ is called the polar of the point $P$ with respect to the circle $S$.

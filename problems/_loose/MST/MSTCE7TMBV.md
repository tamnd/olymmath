Determine whether there exists a number $n \geq 2$ and a positive real number $c$ and $n$ positive real numbers $a_{i} (i=1,2, \ldots, n)$ such that the sum of the $a_{i}$ is equal to $n \cdot c$, the sum of the squares of the $a_{i}$ is equal to $2 n \cdot c^{2}$, and at least one of the numbers $a_{i}$ is greater than $(1+\sqrt{n-1}) \cdot c$.

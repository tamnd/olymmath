For the sequence of real numbers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, it holds that

$$
a_{1}=1 \text { and } a_{n+1}^{2}+a_{n+1}=a_{n}, \text { for every } n \in \mathbb{N} .
$$

Prove that $a_{n} \geq \frac{1}{n}$.

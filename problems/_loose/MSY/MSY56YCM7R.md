On the sides $AB$ and $CD$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $M$ and $N$ are taken, dividing them in the same ratio (counting from vertices $A$ and $C$). These points are connected to all vertices of the quadrilateral, as a result of which $ABCD$ is divided into six triangles and one quadrilateral. Prove that the area of the resulting quadrilateral is equal to the sum of the areas of the two triangles adjacent to the sides $BC$ and $AD$.

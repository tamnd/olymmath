$\mathrm{Na}$ our planned cottage, we brought the cat Vilda. On Monday, she caught $\frac{1}{2}$ of all the mice, on Tuesday $\frac{1}{3}$ of the remaining, on Wednesday $\frac{1}{4}$ of those left after Tuesday's hunt, and on Thursday only $\frac{1}{5}$ of the remainder. On Friday, the remaining mice preferred to move away. How many mice were originally at the cottage if on Friday, two more mice moved away than Vilda caught on Tuesday? Don't forget to verify that each day's catch was an integer number of mice.

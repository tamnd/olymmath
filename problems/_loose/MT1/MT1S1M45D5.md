The game "Geographic Dice" consists of each participant rolling a die a number of times $n$ and recording the sequence, where the first roll indicates the distance, in meters, that the participant will walk to the North, the second to the East, the third to the South, the fourth to the West, the fifth to the North, and so on. After that, the distance to the origin is measured. The winner is the one who ends up closest to the starting position.

a) Márcia rolled 214365 (6 rolls). What is her distance from the origin?
b) In 4 rolls, how many possibilities exist for the participant to return to the origin after the last roll?

c) In 5 rolls, how many possibilities exist for the participant to return to the origin after the last roll?

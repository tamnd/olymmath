Let $a_1 , a_2 , ..., a_n$ be non-zero integers that satisfy the equation $$ a_1 +\dfrac{1}{a_2+\dfrac{1}{a_3+ ... \dfrac{1}{a_n+\dfrac{1}{x}} } } = x $$ for all values of $x$ for which the lefthand side of the equation makes sense.
(a) Prove that $n$ is even.
(b) What is the smallest n for which such numbers $a_1 , a_2 , ..., a_n$ exist?

(M Skopenko)

Fibonacci Sequence.
The Fibonacci sequence is another typical problem in combinatorial mathematics, proposed as follows: There is a pair of rabbits, one male and one female. Assuming that two months after birth, they can produce another pair of rabbits, one male and one female, every month thereafter, how many pairs of rabbits will there be after $n$ months?

Minimum problem. Show that for any positive numbers $p, q, r$ and $s$ the fraction

$$
\frac{\left(p^{2}+p+1\right)\left(q^{2}+q+1\right)\left(r^{2}+r+1\right)\left(s^{2}+s+1\right)}{p q r s}
$$

is not less than 81.

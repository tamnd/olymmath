1.1 * Let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ be an arithmetic sequence with all terms non-zero. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{x_{1} x_{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2} x_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n-1} x_{n}}=\frac{n-1}{x_{1} x_{n}}(n \geqslant 2) .
$$

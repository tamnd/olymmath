We have a deck of 52 cards. Shuffling will be called the execution of the following actions: an arbitrary division of the deck into an upper and a lower part, and then an arbitrary mixing of the cards while maintaining the order within each part. Formally, shuffling is any mixing of the cards where the $i$-th card from the top moves to position $p_i$, with the condition that there exists some $m \in \{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 51\}$ such that $p_i < p_{i+1}$ for $i < m$ and for $i > m$.
Determine whether, starting from a fixed initial ordering of the cards, any other ordering can be achieved by performing five shuffles.

(Let's consider Miquel's first theorem).

Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively, intersecting at two points $X$ and $Y$. Let $A$ be a point on $\Gamma_{1}$ distinct from $X$ and $Y$, and let $B$ be the intersection of $(A Y)$ and $\Gamma_{2}$. Show that the triangles $X O_{1} O_{2}$ and $X A B$ are similar.

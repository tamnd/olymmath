Inside an acute angle, there is a convex quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$. It turns out that for each of the two lines containing the sides of the angle, the following condition is met: the sum of the distances from vertices A and C to this line is equal to the sum of the distances from vertices B and D to the same line. Prove that ABCD is a parallelogram.

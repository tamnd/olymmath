19.2.21 ** Proof: For a given positive integer $g$, any rational number $w>1$ can be expressed as $w=$ $\left(1+\frac{1}{k}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{k+1}\right) \cdots\left(1+\frac{1}{k+s}\right)$, where $k$ is an integer greater than $g$, and $s$ is a non-negative integer.

(F.Nilov) Given isosceles triangle $ ABC$ with base $ AC$ and $ \angle B = \alpha$ . The arc $ AC$ constructed outside the triangle has angular measure equal to $ \beta$ . Two lines passing through $ B$ divide the segment and the arc $ AC$ into three equal parts. Find the ratio $ \alpha / \beta$ .

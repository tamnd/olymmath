A little squirrel collected 15 nuts in the forest weighing $50, 51, \ldots, 64$ grams. He knows the weight of each nut. Using a balance scale, the little squirrel tries to prove to his friends that the first nut weighs 50 g, the second 51 g, the third 52 g, and so on (initially, the friends know nothing about the weights of the nuts). What is the minimum number of weights the little squirrel will need, if both the weights and the nuts can be placed on either pan of the balance, and the number of weighings is unlimited? (The weights of the weights are known to both the little squirrel and his friends. There is an unlimited supply of weights weighing $1, 2, \ldots, 1000$ g.)

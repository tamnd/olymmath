Given $x_{i}, y_{i} \in \mathbf{R}(i=1,2, \cdots, n), \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}$ $=\sum_{i=1}^{n} y_{i}^{2}=1$,
prove: $\left(x_{1} y_{2}-x_{2} y_{1}\right)^{2} \leqslant 2\left|1-\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} y_{i}\right|$.

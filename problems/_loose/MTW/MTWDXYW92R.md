Given that the internal angles $A, B, C$ of $\triangle A B C$ have opposite sides $a, b, c$ respectively. Prove:
$$
\frac{3}{2} \leqslant \frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}+c^{2}}+\frac{b^{2}}{c^{2}+a^{2}}+\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}+b^{2}} \leqslant 2\left(\cos ^{2} A+\cos ^{2} B+\cos ^{2} C\right) .
$$

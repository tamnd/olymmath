In each cell of a square table, a real number is written. It is known that in each row of the table, the sum of the $k$ largest numbers is $a$, and in each column of the table, the sum of the $k$ largest numbers is $b$.

a) Prove that if $k=2$, then $a=b$.

b) For the case $k=3$, provide an example of such a table for which $a \neq b$.

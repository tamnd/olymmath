A chord is at a distance $h$ from the center of a circle. In each of the two segments of the circle subtended by this chord, a square is inscribed such that a pair of its adjacent vertices lies on the chord, and the other pair of adjacent vertices lies on the corresponding arc of the circle. Find the difference in the lengths of the sides of the squares.

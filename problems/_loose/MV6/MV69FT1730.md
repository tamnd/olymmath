Given natural numbers $M$ and $N$, both greater than ten, consisting of the same number of digits, and such that $M = 3N$. To obtain the number $M$, one of the digits of $N$ must be increased by 2, and each of the other digits must be increased by an odd digit. What digit could the number $N$ end with? Find all possible answers.

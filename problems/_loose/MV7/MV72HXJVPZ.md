Given triangle $A B C$. Points $K, L$, and $M$ are placed on the plane such that triangles $A B K, L B C$, and $A M C$ are equal to $A B C$. What inequality sign should be placed between the semiperimeter of triangle $K L M$ and the perimeter of triangle $A B C$?

The vertices of the triangles are given in an arbitrary order: for example, it cannot be asserted that in the equality of triangles $A B K$ and $L B C$, point $A$ corresponds to point $L$.

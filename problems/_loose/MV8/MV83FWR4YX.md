Let $k$ be a semicircle constructed over the diameter $AB$, which lies within the square $ABCD$. Consider its tangent $t_{1}$ from point $C$ (different from $BC$) and denote by $P$ its intersection with side $AD$. Let $t_{2}$ be the common external tangent of the semicircle $k$ and the incircle of triangle $CDP$ (different from $AD$). Prove that the lines $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$ are perpendicular to each other.

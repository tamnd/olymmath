Let $A$ be a set of natural numbers that simultaneously satisfies the conditions:
a) $2 \in A, 3 \in A$;

b) If $x \in A$, then $4 x \in A$;

c) If $5 x-2 \in A$, then $x \in A$.

Show that $26 \in A$ and $154 \in A$.

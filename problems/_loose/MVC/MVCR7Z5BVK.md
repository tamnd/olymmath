(***) Let $E=\left\{M_{1}, M_{2}, \ldots, M_{n}\right\}$ be a set of $n \geqslant 3$ points lying on the same circle $\Gamma$. For $i=1, \ldots n$, let $G_{i}$ denote the isobarycenter of the $n-1$ points in $E \backslash\left\{M_{i}\right\}$, and let $\Delta_{i}$ be the line passing through $G_{i}$ and perpendicular to the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $M_{i}$.

Prove that the lines $\Delta_{1}, \Delta_{2}, \ldots, \Delta_{n}$ are concurrent.

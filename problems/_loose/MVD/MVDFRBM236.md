Prove that for positive real numbers $a, b$ and $c$ such that $a+b+c \leqslant 3$, the following inequality holds:

$$
\frac{a+1}{a(a+2)}+\frac{b+1}{b(b+2)}+\frac{c+1}{c(c+2)} \geqslant 2
$$

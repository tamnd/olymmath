Let $r_{a}, r_{b}, r_{c}, r_{d}$ be the radii of the excircles of a bicircular quadrilateral $ABCD$ that are tangent to one side and the extensions of the adjacent sides, and let $r, p$ be the inradius and semiperimeter of the bicircular quadrilateral $ABCD$, respectively. Prove:
(1) $r_{a} r_{b} r_{c} r_{d}=r^{4}$;
(2) $\frac{1}{r_{a}+r}+\frac{1}{r_{c}+r}=\frac{1}{r_{b}+r}+\frac{1}{r_{d}+r}=\frac{1}{r}$;
(3) $\sqrt{r_{d} \tau_{b}}+\sqrt{r_{b} r_{c}}+\sqrt{r_{d} \tau_{d}}+\sqrt{r_{d} I_{d}}=p$.

Suppose the population of two neighboring islands consists only of knights and liars (there are no normal people on the islands). You are told that on one island there is an even number of knights, and on the other, an odd number of knights. You are also informed that on the island with an even number of knights, treasures are buried, while on the island with an odd number of knights, there are no treasures.

You randomly choose one of the islands and travel there. All the inhabitants of the island know how many knights and how many liars live among them. You converse with three inhabitants A, B, and C of the island and receive the following statements:

A: The number of liars on this island is even.

B: The number of people on our island is odd.

C: I am a knight if and only if A and B are of the same type.

Assume that you are neither a knight nor a liar and that when you were on the island, there were no other visitors. Are the treasures hidden on this island?

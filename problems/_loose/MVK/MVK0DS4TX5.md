In triangle $ABC$, the midpoints of sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$, respectively, and the feet of the perpendiculars from the vertices to the opposite sides are $A_{0}$, $B_{0}$, and $C_{0}$. Is it always true that the following equality holds:

$$
\angle B_{0} A_{1} C_{0} + \angle C_{0} B_{1} A_{0} + \angle A_{0} C_{1} B_{0} = 180^{\circ} .
$$

A rotating disk has $n\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$ balloons hanging uniformly around it. There are $n$ people holding air guns standing uniformly around the disk. When someone is directly facing a balloon (the person, the balloon, and the center of the disk are collinear), they can shoot, but no two people can shoot simultaneously.
(1) If each gun has only one bullet, and when the disk rotates one full turn, all $n$ balloons are hit (assuming everyone is a sharpshooter), find all possible values of $n$;
(2) For the positive integers $n$ that satisfy the condition in (1), prove: the number of design sequences that meet the requirements is at least $n$ !!.
Note: When $n$ is odd, $n!!=n \cdot(n-2) \cdot(n-4) \cdots \cdot 3 \cdot 1$; when $n$ is even, $n!!=n \cdot(n-2) \cdot(n-4) \cdots \cdot 4 \cdot 2$.

Find all values of the parameter $a$, for each of which the sum of the lengths of the intervals that make up the solution to the inequality

$$
\frac{x^{2}+\left(2 a^{2}+2\right) x-a^{2}+4 a-6}{x^{2}+\left(a^{2}+5 a-5\right) x-a^{2}+4 a-6}<0
$$

is not less than 1.

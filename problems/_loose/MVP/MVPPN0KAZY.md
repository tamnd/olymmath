Let $A B C D E F$ be a convex hexagon, and $A B \parallel E D, B C \parallel F E, C D \parallel A F$. Let $R_{A}, R_{C}, R_{E}$ denote the circumradii of $\triangle F A B, \triangle B C D$, and $\triangle D E F$ respectively, and let $p$ denote the perimeter of the hexagon. Prove: $R_{A}+R_{C}+R_{E} \geqslant \frac{p}{2}$.

The Tsar summoned two sages. He gave the first 100 blank cards and ordered him to write a positive number on each (the numbers do not have to be different), without showing them to the second. Then the first can tell the second several different numbers, each of which is either written on some card or equal to the sum of numbers on some cards (without specifying how each number was obtained). The second must determine which 100 numbers are written on the cards. If he cannot, both will have their heads cut off; otherwise, a number of hairs will be plucked from each beard equal to the number of numbers the first told the second. How can the sages, without communicating, stay alive and lose the minimum number of hairs?

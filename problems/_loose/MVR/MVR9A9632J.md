(Sine Law) Let $A B C$ be a triangle. We denote $B C=a, A C=b, A B=c$ and $R$ the radius of the circumcircle. Show that

$$
\frac{a}{\sin (\widehat{A})}=\frac{b}{\sin (\widehat{B})}=\frac{c}{\sin (\widehat{C})}=2 R
$$

In the plane, there are two lines $e$ and $e^{\prime}$, the first one has a fixed point $A$, and the second one has a fixed point $A^{\prime}$. For every point $M$ on line $e$, there is a point $M^{\prime}$ on line $e^{\prime}$ such that $\frac{\overline{A M}}{\overline{A^{\prime} M^{\prime}}}=k$. What is the geometric place of the midpoint of $M M^{\prime}$?

Given 1981 positive integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{1981}$ satisfy
$$
x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{1981}=x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{1981} \text {. }
$$
(1) Prove that at least 1971 of these positive integers are 1;
(2) Try to find a few sets of solutions.

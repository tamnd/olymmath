Given $f(x)=2 x-\frac{2}{x^{2}}+\frac{a}{x}$, where the constant $a \in(0,4]$. Find all real numbers $k$, such that for $x_{1}, x_{2} \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, it always holds that $\left|f\left(x_{1}\right)-f\left(x_{2}\right)\right|>k\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|$.

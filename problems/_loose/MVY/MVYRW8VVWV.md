Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be a set of positive numbers, and satisfy $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}=c$, find what values of $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ make the expression $x_{1}^{\alpha}+x_{2}^{\alpha}+\cdots+x_{n}^{\alpha}$ take the minimum value? Here $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ is a constant greater than 1.

In the plane $\alpha$, a line $p$ and a point $P$ are given, which does not lie on the line $p$. Determine the geometric locus of points $M$ in the plane $\alpha$, for which $\frac{M P}{M N}=c$, where $N$ is the foot of the perpendicular from point $P$ to the line $p$, and $c$ is a given positive number.

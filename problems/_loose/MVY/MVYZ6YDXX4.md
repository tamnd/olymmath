In the unit square $ABCD$, points $E$ and $F$ are on the side $AB$ such that $AE = \frac{1}{n}$ and $BF = \frac{1}{m}$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers. A line starting from point $B$ and forming a $30^{\circ}$ angle with $AB$ is tangent to the semicircle drawn inside the square with $EF$ as its diameter. Determine the values of $m$ and $n$. Are there any pairs of values for $m$ and $n$ such that the semicircle is also tangent to the diagonal $AC$?

For $k$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{k}$ on a circle, connect each pair of points. For the line segment $A_{i} A_{j}$, when $ij$, it is called the personal line of $A_{i}$. Now color each line segment with one of $n$ colors. If the outgoing lines from each point have colors different from the personal lines (outgoing lines from each point can be the same color, and personal lines can also be the same color), prove that $k \leqslant 2^{n}$.

Self-taught inventor Euclid Algorithm made a machine that works as follows: you can insert a paper rectangle into it, and it will cut off a square (making a cut parallel to the shorter side). Then the remaining rectangle can be inserted into the machine again, another square will be cut off, and so on. What will happen if you start with a rectangle $14 \times 36$?

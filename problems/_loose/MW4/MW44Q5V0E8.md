Four. (50 points) The grid shape $\pi$ has the following property: for any way of filling numbers in an $m \times n$ grid, as long as the total sum of the numbers filled in is positive, the shape $\pi$ can be placed somewhere on the grid (the shape can be rotated but must not exceed the boundaries) such that the sum of the numbers covered by $\pi$ is positive. Prove: the shape $\pi$ can cover several layers of the $m \times n$ grid.

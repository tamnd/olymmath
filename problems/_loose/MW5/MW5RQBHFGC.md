Determine the largest real number $a$ and the smallest real number $b$ such that for all non-negative $x, y, z$ we have

$$
a(x+y+z)^{2} \leqslant x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}+y z \leqslant b(x+y+z)^{2}
$$

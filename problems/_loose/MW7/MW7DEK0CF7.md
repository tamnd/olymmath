Say that a positive integer is *red* if it is of the form $n^{2020}$, where $n$ is a positive integer. Say that a positive integer is *blue* if it is not red and is of the form $n^{2019}$, where $n$ is a positive integer. True or false: Between every two different red positive integers greater than $10^{100{,}000{,}000}$, there are at least 2019 blue positive integers. Prove that your answer is correct.

Let $T$ be any point inside the square $A B C D$, and let $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$ be the other intersection points of the lines $T A, T B, T C$, and $T D$ with the circumcircle of the square $A B C D$. Prove that $\left|A^{\prime} B^{\prime}\right| \cdot\left|C^{\prime} D^{\prime}\right|=\left|A^{\prime} D^{\prime}\right| \cdot\left|B^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right|$.

Let the function $f(x)$ satisfy for all real numbers $x$:
$$
f(2-x)=f(2+x), f(7-x)=f(7+x) \text {, and } f(0)=0 .
$$

Prove: $f(x)$ has at least 13 zeros in the interval $[-30,30]$, and $f(x)$ is a periodic function with a period of 10.

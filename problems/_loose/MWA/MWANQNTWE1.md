Let $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, and the set $S=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$. If the number of odd numbers in a non-empty subset $X$ of $S$ is greater than the number of even numbers, then $X$ is called "good". Denote the number of all "good" subsets of $S$ as $F_{n}$. Prove:
(1) $F_{2 n+1}=2^{2 n}$;
(2) $F_{2 n}=\frac{1}{2}\left(2^{2 n}-C_{2 n}^{n}\right)$.

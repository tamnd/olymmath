Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers such that $a b+b c+a c=3$. Show that:

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{1+b c}+\frac{b^{2}}{1+c a}+\frac{c^{2}}{1+a b} \geqslant \frac{3}{2}
$$

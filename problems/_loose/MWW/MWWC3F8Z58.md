Among the following numbers, the one that definitely cannot be the square of some natural number (where $n$ is a natural number) is
(A) $3 n^{2}-3 n+3$.
(B) $4 n^{2}+4 n+4$.
(C) $5 n^{2}-5 n-5$.
(D) $7 n^{2}-7 n+7$.
(E) $11 n^{2}+11 n-11$.
(Tianjin Junior High School Mathematics Competition; China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1984)

Given an isosceles triangle $A B C$ with base $A B$ and a point $P$ inside its altitude $z$ from vertex $C$. The line $A P$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ at point $Q \neq A$. The line parallel to the base $A B$ through point $P$ intersects the side $B C$ at point $R$. Prove that the ray $Q R$ is the angle bisector of $\angle A Q B$.

(Jaroslav Švrček)

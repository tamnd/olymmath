The 22nd question, given integers $n \geq 3, a_{k}>0 （ 1 \leq k \leq n ）$, satisfying $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}=\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{a_{k}}$, prove:
$$
\sum_{\mathrm{k}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \frac{1}{\left[(\mathrm{n}-2) \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{k}}+\sum_{\mathrm{j}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{j}}\right]^{2}} \leq \frac{\mathrm{n}}{4(\mathrm{n}-1)^{2}} \text {. }
$$

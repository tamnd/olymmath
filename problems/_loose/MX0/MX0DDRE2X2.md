Consider the set $\Omega$ formed by the first twenty natural numbers, $\Omega = \{1, 2, . . . , 20\}$ . A nonempty subset $A$ of $\Omega$ is said to be *sumfree [/i ] if for all pair of elements $ x, y \in A$ , the sum $(x + y)$ is not in $A$ , ( $x$ can be equal to $y$ ). Prove that $\Omega$ has at least $2018$ sumfree subsets.

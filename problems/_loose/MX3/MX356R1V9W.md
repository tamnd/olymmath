(Taiwan, 2001). Let $S$ be the set $\{1, \ldots, n\}$ of integers between 1 and $n$. We choose $n$ distinct subsets of $S$, denoted $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$. Show that there exists an element $x$ of $S$ such that the sets $B_{1}=A_{1} \backslash\{x\}, \ldots, B_{n}=A_{n} \backslash\{x\}$ are also pairwise distinct.

Note: the notation $A \backslash\{x\}$ denotes the set $A$ from which the element $x$ has been removed (if $x$ is not in $A$, $A \backslash\{x\}=A$).

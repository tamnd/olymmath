For a positive integer $n$, denote by $S_n$ the sum of the $n$ least primes: $S_1 = 2$, $S_2 = 2+3=5$, $S_3 = 2+3+5=10$, and so on. Determine whether two consecutive terms of the sequence $S_1, S_2, S_3, \ldots$ can be perfect squares. (V. Sharich)

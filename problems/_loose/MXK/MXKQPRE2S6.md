Some students can do two of them, and no one can do all three. If 24 students in the class cannot play football, 25 students cannot play basketball, and 30 students cannot play volleyball, then the number of students who can do at least two of the sports is ( ).
(A) 20
(B) 21
(C) 25
(D) 26

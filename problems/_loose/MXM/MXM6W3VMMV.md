\section*{Task 3 - 161213}

A school has the following tents available for a camping trip:

2 tents for 3 people each,

1 tent for 8 people,

2 tents for 10 people each, and

2 tents for 16 people each.

Each of these tents is either occupied by girls to exactly 50% of its maximum capacity or by boys to a maximum of 70% and a minimum of 50% of its capacity. In total, more girls than boys are to be considered for the camping trip.

a) How many people can participate in the camping trip under these conditions at most?

b) Provide a corresponding tent allocation for such a case.

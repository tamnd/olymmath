The base plane, axis, and apex of two circular cones are common, the ratio of their aperture angles is $1: 2$. The two generators of the larger aperture angle cone form an angle $\alpha$. Project these generators perpendicularly onto the base plane, the angle of the projections is $\beta$. Determine the aperture angle of each cone, as well as the angle formed by the generators cut by the projection planes from the other cone, given $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

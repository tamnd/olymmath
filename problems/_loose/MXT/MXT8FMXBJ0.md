On an infinitely large grid paper, a real number is written in each cell, such that each number is equal to the arithmetic mean of the numbers in its four neighboring cells (up, down, left, right). A rectangular grid table is cut out from this paper. Prove that the maximum number in the table must appear in one of the edge cells.

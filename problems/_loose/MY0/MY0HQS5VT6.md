11.1.1 ** Let the altitudes of an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ be $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$, then $\min \left(h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}\right) \leqslant R+r \leqslant \max \left(h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}\right)$. Here $R, r$ are the circumradius and inradius of $\triangle ABC$, respectively.

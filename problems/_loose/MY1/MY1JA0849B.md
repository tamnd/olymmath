In the tetrahedron $P A B C$, if the projection $O$ of point $P$ on the plane $A B C$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle A B C$, and the dihedral angles opposite to the edges $P A, P B, P C$ are $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and the angle between $P O$ and $P A$ is $\varphi$, then
( I ) $\cos \angle B A C=\frac{\sin ^{2} \frac{\beta}{2}+\sin ^{2} \frac{\gamma}{2}-\sin ^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}{2 \sin \frac{\beta}{2} \cdot \sin \frac{\gamma}{2}}$ (similarly for the others);
( II ) $\frac{\sin \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\sin \angle B A C}=\frac{\sin \frac{\beta}{2}}{\sin \angle A B C}=\frac{\sin \frac{\gamma}{2}}{\sin \angle A C B}=\sin \varphi$.

For the compact tetrahedron $ABCD$, we know the following: $AB = BC = CD = e$, $AC = BD = f$, and the dihedral angle between the faces $ADB$ and $ADC$ is $60^{\circ}$.

a) Express the length of the edge $AD$ in terms of the measures $e, f$!

b) Show that the dihedral angles at the edges of length $f$ are right angles!

c) Show that the body can be cut into two parts by suitable planes, and these parts can be rearranged to form a regular triangular prism!

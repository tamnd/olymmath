In a cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$, let $L, M$ be the centers of the incircles of triangles $BCA$ and $BCD$, respectively. Further, let $R$ be the intersection of the perpendiculars drawn from points $L$ and $M$ to the lines $A C$ and $B D$, respectively. Prove that triangle $L M R$ is isosceles.

Let $T$ be a locally finite tree, construct a continuous map $\sigma:[0,1] \rightarrow|T|$, which maps 0 and 1 to the root of the tree, and passes through each edge exactly twice, once in each direction. (Strictly, define $\sigma$ so that each interior point of an edge is the image of exactly two points in $[0,1]$).

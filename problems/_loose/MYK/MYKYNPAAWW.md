The plane of the face adjacent to the cut line is perpendicular to the previous face, its shape is an isosceles triangle, the base of which is the cut line, and its height is $c$. The distance from the seventh vertex to the plane of the first face is $d$. What is the surface area and volume of the polyhedron?

Numerical data: $a=20, b=30, c=15, d=25$ units.

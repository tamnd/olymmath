Given an angle of size $\alpha$ with vertex at point $O$ and a point $A$ inside it. Consider all possible quadrilaterals $O M N N$, where vertices $M$ and $N$ are located on the sides of the angle and such that $\widehat{M A N} = \beta\left(\alpha+\beta>180^{\circ}\right)$. Prove that if among these quadrilaterals there is a convex quadrilateral such that $|M A|=|A N|$, then this quadrilateral has the smallest area among all considered quadrilaterals.

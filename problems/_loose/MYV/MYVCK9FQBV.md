In the younger group of a kindergarten, there are two small Christmas trees and five children. The teachers want to divide the children into two circles around each of the trees, with at least one child in each circle. The teachers can distinguish the children but not the trees: two such divisions into circles are considered the same if one can be obtained from the other by swapping the trees (along with their respective circles) and rotating each circle around its tree. In how many ways can the children be divided into circles? Points for the task: 8.

On the plane, a regular $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ is given. a) Prove that if $n$ is an even number, then for any point $M$ on the plane, in the expression $\pm \overrightarrow{M A}_{1} \pm \overrightarrow{M A}_{2} \pm \ldots \pm \overrightarrow{M A}_{n}$, the signs plus and minus can be chosen such that the resulting sum will be equal to 0. b) Prove that if $n$ is an odd number, then the specified expression can be turned into 0 by choosing the signs plus and minus only for a finite number of points $M$ on the plane.

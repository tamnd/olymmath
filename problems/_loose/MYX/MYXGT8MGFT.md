Given an isosceles triangle $A B C$ and a circle tangent to the equal sides $[C A]$ and $[C B]$ at points $A$ and $B$. From a point $M$ on the arc of this circle inside the triangle, perpendiculars $[M D]$ are drawn to the base of the triangle, and $[M F]$ and $[M E]$ to the equal sides.

Prove that $M D^{2}=M E \cdot M F$.

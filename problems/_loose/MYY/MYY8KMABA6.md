In the center of each cell of a rectangular grid $M$ there is a point light bulb, initially all of them are off. In one move, it is allowed to draw any straight line that does not touch the light bulbs and turn on all the light bulbs on one side of this line, if all of them are off. Each move must turn on at least one light bulb. It is required to turn on all the light bulbs, making as many moves as possible. What is the maximum number of moves that can be made if

a) $\mathrm{M}$ is a square $21 \times 21$;

b) $\mathrm{M}$ is a rectangle $20 \times 21$?

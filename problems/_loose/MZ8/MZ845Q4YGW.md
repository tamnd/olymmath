Let $(\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z})^{*}$ be the set of congruences modulo $n$ representing integers coprime with $n$, and let $a$ be an element of $(\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z})^{*}$. Show that multiplication by $a$ induces a bijection of $\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z}$ onto itself, as well as a bijection of $(\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z})^{*}$ onto itself.

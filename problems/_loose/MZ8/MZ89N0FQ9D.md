A rectangle, whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axes, is divided into lattice triangles, each with an area of $1 / 2$. It is to be proven that

among the triangles, there are at least twice as many right-angled ones as the

length of the shorter side of the rectangle. (A polygon is a lattice polygon if all of its vertices have integer coordinates.)

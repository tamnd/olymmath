Let $f(x)$ be a convex function, and $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}$ be positive numbers whose sum is 1.

a) Prove that if the numbers $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}$ are rational, then

$$
f\left(\alpha_{1} x_{1}+\ldots+\alpha_{n} x_{n}\right) \leqslant \alpha_{1} f\left(x_{1}\right)+\ldots+\alpha_{n} f\left(x_{n}\right)
$$

b) Prove that if the function $f(x)$ is continuous, then

$$
f\left(\alpha_{1} x_{1}+\ldots+\alpha_{n} x_{n}\right) \leqslant \alpha_{1} f\left(x_{1}\right)+\ldots+\alpha_{n} f\left(x_{n}\right)
$$

(Jensen's inequality).

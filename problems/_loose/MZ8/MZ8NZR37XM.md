Prove that there do not exist integers $a, b, c, d$ satisfying the following equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a b c d-a=1961, \\
a b c d-b=961, \\
a b c d-c=61, \\
a b c d-d=1 .
\end{array}\right.
$$
(24th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1961)

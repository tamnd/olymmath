Multiplication of dates. In 1928, there were four dates with a remarkable property: when written in the usual way, the product of the day and the month gives the year of the XX century. Here are these dates: $28 / 1-28, 14 / 2-28, 7 / 4-28$ and $4 / 7-28$.

How many times in the XX century (from 1901 to 2000 inclusive) does this property occur? Perhaps you will try to find the year of our century in which the number of such dates is maximal? There is only one such year.

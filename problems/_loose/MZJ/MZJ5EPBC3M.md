In the office, there are $\mathrm{N}$ slobs, and each has a non-zero amount of garbage on their desk. The slobs go out to lunch one by one (after the previous one has returned), and in the meantime, each of the remaining ones moves half of the garbage from their desk to the desk of the one who went out. Can it happen that after everyone has had lunch, the amount of garbage on the desks has not changed for anyone, if a) $\mathrm{N}=2$; b) $\mathrm{N}=10$?

A theater company performed thirty "Evenings of Improvisation" during the season as planned. Fan, an admirer of the main protagonist, calculated at the beginning of the season how much she would spend in total on admission if she attended every performance. After a few performances, however, the admission price unexpectedly increased by 60 Kč. Later, the company gained a significant sponsor and reduced the new admission price by 85 Kč. At the end of the season, Fan could say that she did not miss a single performance of the program and spent exactly as much on admission as she had calculated at the beginning of the season. How many times did Fan attend the performance at the original admission price?

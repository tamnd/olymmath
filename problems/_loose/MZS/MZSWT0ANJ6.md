Given a circle (c) with center $O$ and radius $R$. The point $O_{1}$, which lies on the circle (c), is the center of another circle with radius $\frac{R}{2}$.

If $A$ and $B$ are the intersection points of these two circles, determine the approximate value of $\measuredangle A O B$ (in radians), and then the volume of the intersection of the spheres obtained by rotating the two circles around the line $O O_{1}$.

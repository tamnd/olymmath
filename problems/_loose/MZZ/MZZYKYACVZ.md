On the edges $A C, B C, B S, A S$ of a regular triangular pyramid $S A B C$ with vertex $S$, points $K, L, M, N$ are chosen respectively. It is known that points $K, L, M, N$ lie in the same plane, and $K L = M N = 2, K N = L M = 18$. In the quadrilateral $K L M N$, there are two circles $\Omega_{1}$ and $\Omega_{2}$, where circle $\Omega_{1}$ is tangent to sides $K N, K L$ and $L M$, and circle $\Omega_{2}$ is tangent to sides $K N, L M$ and $M N$. The right circular cones $\mathcal{F}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{F}_{2}$ with bases $\Omega_{1}$ and $\Omega_{2}$ respectively are located inside the given pyramid, with the vertex $P$ of cone $\mathcal{F}_{1}$ lying on edge $A B$, and the vertex $Q$ of cone $\mathcal{F}_{2}$ lying on edge $C S$.

a) Find $\angle S A B$.

b) Find the length of segment $C Q$.

A morning passenger train departing from Budapest to Érsekújvár travels at a speed of $v_{1}$; this train is to be overtaken by a later departing express train, which travels at a speed of $v_{2}$. After the passenger train has already covered $\frac{2}{3}$ of its journey, it continues at half its original speed, as a result of which the express train overtakes it $d$ kilometers before the Érsekújvár station. What is the distance between the two stations? $\left(d=27 \frac{1}{9} \mathrm{~km}\right.$, the passenger train covers $\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{~km}$ per minute, and the express train covers $1 \mathrm{~km}$ per minute.)

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $A', B'$, and $C'$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B$, and $C$ respectively, and $H$ the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$.

1. Show that the quadrilaterals $AB A' B', BC B' C', CA C' A', AB' H C', BA' H C'$, and $CB' H A'$ are cyclic.
2. Show that the triangles $ABC, AB' C', A' BC', A' B' C$ are similar.

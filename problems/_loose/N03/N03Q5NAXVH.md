()Kyur, Australia, 83). Some of the cities $P_{1}, \ldots, P_{1988}$ are connected by some airline routes belonging to companies $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{10}$. It is known only that from any city it is possible to fly to any other city without a layover and that each airline operates in both directions. Prove that (regardless of how the cities are connected by airline routes) there exists at least one company that can provide a trip starting and ending in the same city and using an odd number of airline routes.

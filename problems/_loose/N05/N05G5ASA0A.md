For all $x\in\left(0,\frac\pi4\right)$ prove
$$\frac{(\sin^2x)^{\sin^2x}+(\tan^2x)^{\tan^2x}}{(\sin^2x)^{\tan^2x}+(\tan^2x)^{\sin^2x}}<\frac{\sin x}{4\sin x-3x}$$

In a line of $n$ people, a cake is passed from left to right. When someone receives the cake, they may either pass it on untouched, or cut off a piece and pass the rest of the cake on. If someone wants to cut off a piece and there are $k - 1$ people left on the right, then the rules of politeness dictate that the size of the piece is $\frac{1}{k}$ of the size of the piece of cake they received (i.e., the kth part of the piece of cake they received). $S_n$ is the set of all possible fractions of the original cake that can remain for the last person. The number of elements of $S_n$ is denoted as $a_n$ . Thus, $S_2 =\left\{\frac12, 1\right\}$ and $a_2 = 2$ .
a. What is the set $S_5$ equal to?
b. Show that $a_n = 2a_{n-1}$ if and only if $n$ is prime.
c. Show that $a_n$ is odd if and only if $n$ is a perfect square.

Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \unlhd 1}$ be an increasing sequence of real numbers. Show that if for any natural number $k \geq 3$, the set $A_{k}=\left\{a_{j}-a_{i} \mid 1 \leq i<j \leq k\right\}$ has $k-1$ elements, then $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ forms an arithmetic progression.

In space, three rays $D A, D B$, and $D C$ are given, having a common origin $D$, and $\angle A D B=\angle A D C=\angle B D C=$ $90^{\circ}$. A sphere intersects the ray $D A$ at points $A 1$ and $A 2$, the ray $D B$ - at points $B 1$ and $B 2$, and the ray $D C$ - at points $C 1$ and $C 2$. Find the area of triangle $A 2 B 2 C 2$, if the areas of triangles $D A 1 B 1, D A 1 C 1, D B 1 C 1$ and $D A 2 B 2$ are respectively $\frac{\frac{15}{2}}{2}, 10,6$ and 40.

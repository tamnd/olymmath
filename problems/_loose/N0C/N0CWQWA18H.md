Prove that the equality

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{1}{a(a+1)}+\frac{1}{(a+1)(a+2)}+\ldots+ \\
& +\frac{1}{(a+n-1)(a+n)}=\frac{n}{a(a+n)}
\end{aligned}
$$

is valid for all natural $n$ and all $a$ that are not zero or negative integers.

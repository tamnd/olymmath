We have an $n \times n$ square table. It is necessary to write a real number in each of its cells so that the sum of the numbers on each diagonal is 2017. (Here we consider all possible diagonals of different lengths: thus, two diagonals of length $n$, four diagonals of length $n-1$, four diagonals of length $n-2$, ..., four diagonals of length 2, and four diagonals of length 1.) Is it possible to achieve this for:
a) $n=5$;
b) $n=2017$?

A system of points connected by segments is called "connected" if you can get from any point to any other point along these segments. Can five points be connected in a connected system in such a way that erasing any segment results in exactly two connected systems of points, not connected to each other? (We assume that at the points where segments intersect, transitioning from one to another is not possible.)

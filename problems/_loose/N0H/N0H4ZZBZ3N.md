Consider the following sequence of numbers:

$3,5,17,257,65537, \ldots$

(that is, the sequence of numbers of the form $2^{m}+1$ where $m=2^{n}, n \geq 0$, is an integer). Show that the product of any number of terms taken from the beginning of the sequence is 2 less than the smallest of the remaining numbers.

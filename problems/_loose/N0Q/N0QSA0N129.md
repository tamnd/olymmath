Kolya and Vitya are playing the following game on an infinite grid paper. Starting with Kolya, they take turns marking the nodes of the grid paper - the points where vertical and horizontal lines intersect. Each of them, on their turn, must mark such a node that after this, all marked nodes lie at the vertices of a convex polygon (starting from Kolya's second move). The player who cannot make their next move is considered the loser. Who wins with correct play?

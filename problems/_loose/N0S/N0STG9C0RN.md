Given a triangle $A B C$, $M$ is an arbitrary point on the plane. The bisectors of two angles formed by the lines $A M$ and $B M$ intersect the line $A B$ at points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ ( $C_{1}$ is on the segment $A B$ ), similarly, points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$, $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ are determined on $B C$ and $C A$. Prove that $A_{1}, A_{2}, B_{1}, B_{2}, C_{1}, C_{2}$ are located three on four lines.

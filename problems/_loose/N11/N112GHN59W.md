10,11 On a rectangular table, there are equal cardboard squares of $\mathrm{k}$ different colors with sides parallel to the sides of the table. If any $\mathrm{k}$ squares of different colors are considered, then some two of them can be nailed to the table with one nail. Prove that all squares of some color can be nailed to the table with $2 \mathrm{k}-2$ nails.

On the side $\overline{A C}$ of triangle $A B C$, there are points $D$ and $E$ such that point $D$ is between $C$ and $E$. Let $F$ be the intersection of the circumcircle of triangle $A B D$ with the line passing through point $E$ and parallel to $B C$, such that points $E$ and $F$ are on opposite sides of the line $A B$. Let $G$ be the intersection of the circumcircle of triangle $B C D$ with the line passing through point $E$ and parallel to $A B$, such that points $E$ and $G$ are on opposite sides of the line $B C$.

Prove that points $D, E, F$, and $G$ lie on the same circle.

The periodic sequence

$$
1,4,5,9,3,1,4,5,9,3,1,4,5,9,3, \ldots
$$

of elements in 11-arithmetic is simultaneously a geometric progression and a Fibonacci sequence. The sequence

$b, 4 b, 5 b, 9 b, 3 b, b, 4 b, 5 b, 9 b, 3 b, b, 4 b, 5 b, 9 b, 3 b, \ldots$, obtained from sequence (1) by multiplying by an arbitrary element $b$ from 11-arithmetic, also has these properties.

a) Find all 11-arithmetic sequences that are simultaneously geometric progressions and Fibonacci sequences.

b) Prove that in 7-arithmetic, no such sequence exists.

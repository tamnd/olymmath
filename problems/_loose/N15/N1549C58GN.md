On a drawing sheet, let three points $A, B, C$ be given with $A \neq B, A \neq C$ and $B \neq C$. We are looking for two mutually equal, distinct circles, one of which passes through $A$, the other through $C$, and which touch each other at point $B$.

Describe the possible positions of the given points $A, B, C$, for which

a) no such circles,

b) more than one pair of such circles,

c) exactly one pair of such circles exist!

For (a), show why no such circles exist; for (b) and (c), describe and justify a construction method by which one can obtain several such pairs of circles or the one such pair of circles from the given points! Carry out the construction you describe! Choose $A, B, C$ in suitable positions for (b) and (c) and construct from these $A, B, C$ two pairs of circles in (b) and one pair of circles in (c)!

The device consists of $n$ elements. The probability of failure of any element during the experiment is $p$. Find the mathematical expectation of the number of such experiments, in each of which exactly $m$ elements will fail, if a total of $\boldsymbol{N}$ experiments are conducted. It is assumed that the experiments are independent of each other.

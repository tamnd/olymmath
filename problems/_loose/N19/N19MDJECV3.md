Prove that the sum of $n$ terms of a geometric progression, in which the $p$-th term is equal to $(-1)^{p} a^{4 p}$, for any value of $p$ is

$$
\frac{a^{4}}{a^{4}+1} \cdot\left[(-1)^{n} \cdot a^{4 n}-1\right]
$$

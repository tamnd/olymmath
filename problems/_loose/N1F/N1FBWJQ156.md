The base of the pyramid $TABC$ is the triangle $ABC$, all sides of which are equal to $\sqrt{3}$, and the height of the pyramid coincides with the lateral edge $TA$. Find the area of the section of the pyramid by a plane passing through the center of the sphere circumscribed around the pyramid, forming an angle of $60^{\circ}$ with the base plane, intersecting the edge $AB$ at point $M$ such that $MB=2AM$, and intersecting the edge $BC$. It is known that the distance from point $A$ to the section plane is 0.25.

Prove that if the function $f(x)$ is concave (convex upwards) on the interval $[a ; b]$, then for any distinct points $x_{1}, x_{2}$ from $[a ; b]$ and any positive $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}$ such that $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}=1$, the following inequality holds:

$$
f\left(\alpha_{1} x_{1}+\alpha_{2} x_{2}\right)>\alpha_{1} f\left(x_{1}\right)+\alpha_{2} f\left(x_{2}\right)
$$

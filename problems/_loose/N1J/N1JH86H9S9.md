$$
x^{5}-5 x^{3}+5 x-1=0
$$

has two roots

$$
x_{1}=(-1+\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{u}) / 4, x_{2}=(-1-\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{v}) / 4
$$

where

$$
u=30+6 \sqrt{5} \text { and } v=30-6 \sqrt{5} \text {. }
$$

Give the other three roots of the equation.

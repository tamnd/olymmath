$$
S_{n}=1 \cdot C_{2 n}^{1}+2 \cdot C_{2 n}^{2}+3 \cdot C_{2 n}^{3}+\ldots+n \cdot C_{2 n}^{n}
$$

is a perfect square less than 1000000.

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude corresponding to side $BC$; $M$ the midpoint of side $BC$ and $F$ the intersection point of the internal angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ with side $BC$. Determine all triangles for which $F$ is the midpoint of segment $DM$.

Note. It is understood that the determination of a triangle consists in giving the length of its sides, in terms of some parameter if necessary.

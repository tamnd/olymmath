Prove that if $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ are the roots of the equation

$$
x^{2}+p x+q=0
$$

then

$$
x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}=3 p q-p^{3}, x_{1}^{3}-x_{2}^{3}= \pm\left(p^{2}-q\right) \sqrt{p^{2}-4 q}
$$

* See the note on page 35, i.e., if $A+B \sqrt{C}=0$, where $A, B$ are rational numbers, $\sqrt{C}$ is an irrational number, then $A=0$ and $B=0$.

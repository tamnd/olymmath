For $n(n \geqslant 3)$ integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, arrange these numbers in a circle. Each operation involves taking the absolute difference of two adjacent numbers, resulting in $n$ new integers, which are then arranged in a circle without changing their order. Find all positive integers $n$ such that for any $n$ integers, it is possible to reach a state after a finite number of operations where all $n$ numbers are the same.

Let $f(n)$ be a function defined on the set of integers, and satisfy the following two conditions:
(1) $f(0)=1, f(1)=0$;
(2) For any $m, n \in \mathbf{N}$, $f(m+n)+f(m-n)=2 f(m) \cdot f(n)$, find $f(n)$.

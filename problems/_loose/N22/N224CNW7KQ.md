Circle $k_{1}$ is constructed with side $A D$ as its diameter, and circle $k_{2}$ passes through vertices $B, C$ and is tangent to line $A B$. The circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ have an external tangency at point $P$. Prove that angles $CPD$ and $ABC$ are congruent.

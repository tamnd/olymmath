A triangle will be called rational if the degree measures of its angles are rational numbers. A point inside a rational triangle will be called interesting if all three triangles obtained by connecting this point to the vertices of the given triangle are rational. Prove that inside every acute-angled rational triangle there are at least three interesting points.

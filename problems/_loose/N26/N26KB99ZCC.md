Children at the camp threw a die and, depending on the result, performed the following tasks:

| 1 | go $1 \mathrm{~km}$ west |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 | go $1 \mathrm{~km}$ east |
| 3 | go $1 \mathrm{~km}$ north |
| 4 | go $1 \mathrm{~km}$ south |
| 5 | stand still |
| 6 | go $3 \mathrm{~km}$ north |

After five throws, Mark's team was $1 \mathrm{~km}$ east of the starting point.

1. What route could Mark's team have taken? Indicate at least four possibilities.
2. What could the total sum of all the numbers that fell for this team be? Determine all possibilities.

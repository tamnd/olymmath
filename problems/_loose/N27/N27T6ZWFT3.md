In trapezoid $A B C D$, $A B / / C D, A C$ intersects $B D$ at $O$, $M O N / / A B$, and $M O N$ intersects $A D, B C$ at $M, N$. Then $\frac{1}{A B}+\frac{1}{C D}$ equals
(A) $\frac{1}{M N}$.
(B) $\frac{2}{M N}$.
(C) $\frac{3}{M N}$.
(D) $\frac{4}{M N}$.
(China Guangzhou, Wuhan, Fuzhou, and other five cities junior high school mathematics league, 1987)

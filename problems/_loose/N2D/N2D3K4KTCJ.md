Construct any segment $A S$, then construct a circle $k$ with center at point $S$, which passes through point $A$.

1. Construct points $E, F, G$ on the circle $k$ such that together with point $A$ they form a rectangle $A E F G$. Find at least two solutions.
2. Construct points $B, C, D$ on the circle $k$ such that together with point $A$ they form a square $A B C D$.

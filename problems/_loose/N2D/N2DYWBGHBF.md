There are $n(n>2)$ lines on a plane, any two of which intersect, and any three of which do not intersect at the same point. First, mark the intersection points of these lines, then remove all the lines but keep the marked points. If we do not know which two lines a marked point is the intersection of, can we reconstruct the original positions of all the lines?

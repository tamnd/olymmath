Let $(X, Y)$ be a Gaussian system, where $X$ and $Y$ are random vectors (in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ and $\mathbb{R}^{m}$ respectively). Prove the theorem of normal correlation: for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$, the following equalities hold

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{E}(Y \mid X=x)=\mathrm{E} Y+\operatorname{cov}(Y, X)(\mathrm{D} X)^{+}[x-\mathrm{E} X] \\
& \mathrm{D}(Y \mid X=x) \equiv \Delta=\mathrm{D} Y-\operatorname{cov}(Y, X)(\mathrm{D} X)^{+} \mathrm{D} X
\end{aligned}
$$

Here, $\operatorname{Law}(Y \mid X=x)=\mathscr{N}(\mathrm{E}(Y \mid X=x), \Delta)$.

To cook instant noodles, it is necessary to cook the noodles for exactly 3 minutes. Marking exactly 3 minutes is very complicated without a clock, but it is possible if you have certain hourglasses that mark exact times in minutes. For example, suppose you have two hourglasses, one that marks exactly 7 minutes and another that marks exactly 4 minutes. Just turn them over at the same time and, when the 4-minute one finishes, put the noodles in the pot. You should remove them from the pot when the 7-minute one finishes. Thus, the noodles will have cooked for exactly $7-4=3$ minutes.

a) A certain type of instant rice needs to cook for exactly 4 minutes. Show that it is possible to time the cooking of this rice using only hourglasses of $9 \mathrm{mi}$ nutes and 7 minutes. What is the minimum total time required to complete this task?

b) Would it be possible to mark 9 minutes if we only had hourglasses of 6 and $10 \mathrm{mi}$ nutes?

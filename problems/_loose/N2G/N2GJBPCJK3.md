Let $A B C D$ be a parallelogram with $A B > A D$ and $A D = 15 \text{ cm}$. The bisectors of angles $\langle A D C$ and $\langle B C D$ intersect at a point $M \in (A B)$ and $A C \cap B D = \{O\}$.

a) Calculate the perimeter of parallelogram $A B C D$;

b) If $A C \cap D M = \{E\}$, determine the ratio $\frac{O E}{A C}$;

c) Let $P$ be the foot of the perpendicular from point $A$ to line $D M$ and $Q$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $B$ to line $C M$. Prove that points $P, O, Q$ are collinear.

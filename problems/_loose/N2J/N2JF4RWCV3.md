Take the chequered board $10 \times 10$ and consider all the possible patterns of painting 10 sectors so that there is only one painted sector in every row and every column. For each pattern we find rectangle of maximal area the sides of which are on the lines of the grid. Besides, this rectangle must have no painted sectors. What can be the maximal area of such a rectangle?

Question 8 Given an integer $n \geqslant 2$, a real number $a$ satisfies $0<a<\frac{n+1}{n-1}$, and a complex number $z$ satisfies
$$
z^{n+1}-a z^{n}+a z-1=0 \text {. }
$$

Prove: $|z|=1$.

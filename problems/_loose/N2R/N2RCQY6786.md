Let $f : Z_{>0} \to Z_{>0}$ be a function which satisfies $k|f^k(x)-x$ for all $k, x \in Z_{>0}$ and $f(x)-x \le 2023$ . If $f(1) = 2000$ , what can $f$ be?
*Remark*: Here, $f^k (x)$ denotes the $k$ -fold application of $f$ to $x$ .

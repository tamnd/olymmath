In the $x O y$ plane, determine the set of points $(x, y)$ for which the coordinates satisfy $0 \leq x \leq 2 \pi$ and

$$
\frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{1+\sin x}-\sqrt{1-\sin x}) \leq y \leq 1+\frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{1+\sin 4 x}+\sqrt{1-\sin 4 x})
$$

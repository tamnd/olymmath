In a prison, $n$ prisoners are kept. The bored guards come up with the idea to put red or blue hats on each prisoner's head in the courtyard so that no one can see the color of their own hat. After the prisoners have had a good look at each other (each prisoner can see the hats of all the others except their own), each of them must write down on a piece of paper what color hat is on their head. If all of their answers are correct, they will be released into the courtyard. What strategy should the prisoners agree on to maximize their chances of getting out?

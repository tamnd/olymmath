Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $ABC$, $M$ the midpoint of $[BC]$, $A'$ the point diametrically opposite to $A$ on the circumcircle $\Gamma$, and $H'$ the symmetric point of $H$ with respect to $(BC)$. Show that $M$ is the midpoint of $[HA']$, that $H' \in \Gamma$, and that $H'$ and $A'$ are symmetric with respect to the perpendicular bisector of $[BC]$. Furthermore, $(AO)$ and $(AH)$ are symmetric with respect to the angle bisector of $\widehat{A}$.

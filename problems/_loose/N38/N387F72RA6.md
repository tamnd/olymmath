Let $p > 200$ be a prime number. We call a positive integer $n$ *good* if $p$ divides the numerator of the irreducible fraction $\frac{a_n}{b_n}= 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{n}$ . Prove that for all large enough $N$ the number of good numbers not exceeding $N$ is not greater than $CN^\frac{3}{4}$ , where $C$ is a constant possibly depends on $p$ .

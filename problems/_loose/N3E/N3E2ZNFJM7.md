We want to wrap a cube-shaped package with a ribbon such that the ribbon runs along the common perpendicular bisector planes of the three different direction edges (along the intersection lines of these planes with the cube's faces) once, and turns at every node (face center). In how many ways is this possible?

How many ways can this wrapping be done if we take a rectangular package with edge lengths $a, a, 2a$, and respectively, if the edge lengths are $b, 2b, 3b$? (We do not consider two wrappings different if they can be transformed into each other by moving the package.)

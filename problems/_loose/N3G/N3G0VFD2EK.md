In the park, there are $m n$ paths and several flower beds, each path connecting two flower beds. It is known that each path is painted in one of $m$ colors, such that no two paths connected to the same flower bed are of the same color. Prove: There exists a coloring method such that the paths painted in each color are exactly $n$ in number.

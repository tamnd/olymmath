In Rt $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C$ is a right angle, $C H_{1}\left(C K_{1}\right) \perp A B$. When $n$ is a positive odd number, $H_{n} H_{n+1} \perp A C, K_{n} K_{n+1} \perp B C$; when $n$ is a positive even number, $H_{n} H_{n+1} \perp A B, K_{n} K_{n+1} \perp A B$. Let $B C=a$, $A C=b, A B=c, C H_{1}=C K_{1}=h, A H_{n}=q_{n}, B K_{n}=p_{n}, H_{n} H_{n+1}=r_{n}, K_{n} K_{n+1}=$ $s_{n}$. Prove: (1) $P_{n}: q_{n}=a^{n+1}: b^{n+1} ;$ (2) $s_{n}: r_{n}=a^{n}: b^{n} ;$ (3) $\frac{2}{\frac{2}{n+1}}+q_{n}^{\frac{2}{n+1}}=c^{\frac{2}{n+1}}$.

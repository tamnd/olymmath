For a regular 20-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} \cdots A_{20}$, 10 of its vertices are colored black, and the other 10 vertices are colored white. Consider the set of diagonals formed by $A_{1} A_{4}$ and all other diagonals of the same length. Prove that the number of diagonals with both ends black is equal to the number of diagonals with both ends white.

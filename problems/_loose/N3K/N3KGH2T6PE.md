Clamping piece 2 length $119.7 \text{ mm}$,

Clamping piece 3 length $120.2 \text{ mm}$,

Clamping piece 4 length $120.1 \text{ mm}$,

Clamping piece 5 length $120.6 \text{ mm}$.

b) What is the deviation of the measurements from the target length (120.0 mm) for each individual clamping piece? (absolute error).

c) What percentage of the target length do the deviations represent? (percentage error).

d) Which clamping pieces are usable if the percentage error must not exceed 1/2 percent?

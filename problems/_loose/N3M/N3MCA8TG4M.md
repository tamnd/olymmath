Let's find such an $n$ and such numbers $a_{i}, b_{i}, c_{i},(i=1,2, \ldots, n)$, for which the points $(x, y)$ satisfying the condition

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|a_{i} x+b_{i} y+c_{i}\right|=10
$$

when plotted on the coordinate system, form the vertices of a regular octagon!

On a chessboard $8 \times 8$, a cube is placed, the bottom face of which coincides with one of the squares on the board. By flipping over one of its edges, the cube traverses all the squares on the board (possibly more than once). Is it possible that during this movement, one of the faces of the cube never touches the board at all?

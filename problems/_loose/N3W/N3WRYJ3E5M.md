A certain truck can only carry $L$ liters of gasoline, with which it can travel $a$ kilometers. Now it needs to travel $d=\frac{4}{3} a$ kilometers to a certain place, with no refueling stations along the way, but it can transport gasoline to any location along the road and store it for later use. Assuming there is only this one truck, how should it travel to reach the destination and use the least amount of gasoline? If the distance to the destination is $d=\frac{23}{15} a$ kilometers, how should it proceed?

It is known that the difference between the largest and smallest of the numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \ldots, x_{9}, x_{10}$ is 1. What is the a) largest; b) smallest possible difference between the largest and smallest of the 10 numbers $x_{1}, 1 / 2\left(x_{1}+\right.$ $\left.x_{2}\right), 1 / 3\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}\right), \ldots, 1 / 10\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{10}\right)$?

c) What would the answer be if there are not 10, but $n$ numbers?

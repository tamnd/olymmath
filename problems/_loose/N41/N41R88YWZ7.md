Find the maximum possible value of $n$, such that the integers $1,2,\ldots n$ can be filled once each in distinct cells of a $2015\times 2015$ grid and satisfies the following conditions:

- For all $1\le i \le n-1$, the cells with $i$ and $i+1$ share an edge. Cells with $1$ and $n$ also share an edge. In addition, no other pair of numbers share an edge.

- If two cells with $i<j$ in them share a vertex, then $\min\{j-i,n+i-j\}=2$.

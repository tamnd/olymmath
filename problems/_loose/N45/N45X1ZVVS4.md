A $7 \times 7$ board is given. Some of the unit squares are colored in black such that inside every $2 \times 2$ dimension square, there is exactly one square unit colored black.
(a) What is the smallest possible number of black colored unit squares that could be on the board?
(b) What is the largest possible number of black colored unit squares that could be on the board?

A bad wizard has imprisoned $\mathrm{n}$ mathematicians. He has $\mathrm{n}$ colors at his disposal. The wizard places a hat of one of the $n$ colors on the head of each mathematician; two hats can have the same color. Each mathematician can see the color of the hat of each of their colleagues but not their own. The mathematicians must then all at the same time announce the color of their hat. If at least one of the mathematicians guesses correctly, all the mathematicians are free. They can, of course, discuss a strategy before knowing the color of the hats of the other mathematicians.

Propose a strategy that ensures the mathematicians will be freed.

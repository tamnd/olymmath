Let $n \geqslant 2$ be a positive integer. Find the maximum value of the constant $C(n)$ such that for all real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfying $x_{i} \in(0,1)(i=1,2$, $\cdots, n)$, and
$$
\left(1-x_{i}\right)\left(1-x_{j}\right) \geqslant \frac{1}{4}(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)
$$

we have
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} \geqslant C(n) \sum_{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n}\left(2 x_{i} x_{j}+\sqrt{x_{i} x_{j}}\right) .
$$

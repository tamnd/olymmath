You can specify any size square (it can be $1 \times 1, 2 \times 2, \cdots, 100 \times 100$) in the first table, and then pay one shilling to Merlin to find out the sum of the numbers in the corresponding sub-table in the second table. How many shillings do you need to pay at least to get the sum of the numbers on the diagonal of the second table?

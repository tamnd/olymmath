Theory of sports tournaments. Dr. Silvester Sharadek's chess club has 10 members. Every year, tournaments are held to classify the players. Each plays against each other until the first decisive game (draws are not taken into account). We will say that "A wins against B" if A has won against B in the classification tournament of the current year. After the tournament, there will be 45 such results, and the players will be divided into classes, for example: those who have won against eight, those who have won against seven, and so on. (Note that the system

45 matches allow for the possibility that A wins against B, B wins against C, and C wins against A.)

The question concerns possible classification results. In particular, is it possible for the club to be divided into three classes?

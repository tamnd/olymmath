Draw a square $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ with $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}=5.0 \text{ cm}$! Then draw a displacement arrow $\overrightarrow{P Q}$ that is $6.5 \text{ cm}$ long and parallel to the line through $B^{\prime}$ and $D^{\prime}$, in the direction from $B^{\prime}$ to $D^{\prime}$!

The original $A B C D$ is to be determined from the image $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ for this displacement. For solving this task, the mm scale of the ruler may no longer be used.

a) Solve the given task using a compass, ruler, and also the set square to draw parallels through the points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$!

b) Solve (in a new drawing) the task using only a compass and ruler, and such that neither the line through $A$ and $A^{\prime}$ nor the line through $C$ and $C^{\prime}$ is drawn!

No justification or construction descriptions are required.

A triple of positive integers $(a, b, c)$ is called *quadratic* if $a, b, c$ is a nondecreasing arithmetic progression, $\gcd(b, a) = \gcd(b, c) = 1$, and $abc$ is a perfect square. Prove that for any quadratic triple, there exists some other quadratic triple containing at least one number from the first triple.

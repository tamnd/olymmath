Given a real number $x$. Using the operations of multiplication and division of two obtained numbers, we can change the power of the number $x$. For example: $x \cdot x=x^{2}, x^{2} \cdot x^{2}=x^{4}, \frac{x^{4}}{x}=x^{3}$. Prove that in 14 steps we can obtain the number $x^{2013}$!

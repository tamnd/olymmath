Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be an arithmetic sequence. For any natural number $k$, we denote $S_{k}=$ $a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{k}$. Prove that for any natural numbers $m$ and $n$,

$$
S_{n}-S_{m}=\frac{n-m}{n+m} S_{n+m}
$$

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}$ be a set of positive integers, let $N=\max \left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right\}, b_{k}$ be the number of $a_{i}(i=1$, $2, \cdots, n)$ that do not exceed $k$, prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}+\sum_{k=1}^{N} b_{k}=n(N+1)$.

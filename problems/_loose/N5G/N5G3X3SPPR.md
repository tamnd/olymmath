Let $n \geqslant 3$ be a natural number. An $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ is inscribed in a circle. Prove that there exist three vertices $A, B, C \in\left\{A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}\right\}$ such that

$$
|A B|^{2}+|B C|^{2}+|C A|^{2} \geqslant\left|A_{1} A_{2}\right|^{2}+\left|A_{2} A_{3}\right|^{2}+\ldots+\left|A_{i} A_{i+1}\right|^{2}+\ldots+\left|A_{n} A_{1}\right|^{2}
$$

(1) The converse of the proposition "If $x+y=0$, then $x, y$ are opposite numbers";
(2) The negation of the proposition "Congruent triangles have equal areas";
(3) The contrapositive of the proposition "If $q \leqslant 1$, then $x^{2}+2 x+q=0$ has real roots";
(4) The converse of the proposition "In a scalene triangle, the three interior angles are equal".

Among these, the true propositions are
A. (1)(2)
B. (2) (3)
C. (1)(3)

Let $n \geqslant 1$ be a natural number and $a>0$. Show the following inequalities:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^{n-1} & \leqslant n! \\
n! & \leqslant n^{n} \\
(n+3)^{2} & \leqslant 2^{n+3} \\
1+n \cdot a & \leqslant(1+a)^{n}
\end{aligned}
$$

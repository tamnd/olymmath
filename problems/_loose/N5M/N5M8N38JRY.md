(England, 71). On the circle inscribed in a regular $2 n$-gon, points $A$ and $B$ are taken. Prove that if the diagonals connecting opposite vertices of the $2 n$-gon are seen from point $A$ at angles $\alpha_{i}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}$, and from point $B$ at angles $\beta_{i}, \ldots, \beta_{n}$ respectively, then

$$
\operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha_{i}+\ldots+\operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha_{n}=\operatorname{tg}^{2} \beta_{i}+\ldots+\operatorname{tg}^{2} \beta_{n}
$$

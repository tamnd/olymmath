Let $A B C$ be a triangle. The external and internal angle bisectors of $\angle C A B$ intersect side $B C$ at $D$ and $E$, respectively. Let $F$ be a point on the segment $B C$. The circumcircle of triangle $A D F$ intersects $A B$ and $A C$ at $I$ and $J$, respectively. Let $N$ be the mid-point of $I J$ and $H$ the foot of $E$ on $D N$. Prove that $E$ is the incenter of triangle $A H F$.

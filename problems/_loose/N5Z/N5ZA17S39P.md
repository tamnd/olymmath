A cube is inscribed in a regular quadrilateral pyramid $S A B C D$. All four vertices of one face of the cube lie on the base $A B C D$ of the pyramid. The vertices of the opposite face of the cube lie on the lateral edges of the pyramid. It is known that $S A=A B=a$, i.e., the lateral edge of the pyramid is equal to $a$ and is equal to the side of its base. What is the volume of the cube?

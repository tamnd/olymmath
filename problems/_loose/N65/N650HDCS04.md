Every year, at the time of paying taxes, the user makes a declaration regarding the current year. If the declaration is true, they must pay the taxes; if it is false, they do not pay. A young mathematician, who considers the system unfair, finds a way to block it with one of the following statements: which one?
(A) "Fish live in water"
(B) "I live in water"
(C) "Fish do not pay taxes"
(D) "I do not pay taxes"
(E) "I pay taxes".

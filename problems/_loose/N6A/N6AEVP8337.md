There are 12 positive real numbers. It is known that the ratio of any two numbers from this set does not exceed the number 2. Prove that they can be divided into six pairs such that if the sums of the numbers in each pair are calculated, the ratio of any two of the resulting six sums will not exceed $\frac{3}{2}$.

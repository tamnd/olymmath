For any natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{m}$, none of which are equal to each other and none of which are divisible by the square of a natural number greater than one, and for any integers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{m}$ that are not equal to zero, the sum $\sqrt{a_{1}} b_{1}+\sqrt{a_{2}} b_{2}+\ldots+\sqrt{a_{m}} b_{m}$ is not equal to zero. Prove this.

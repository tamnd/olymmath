Given two identical circles. On each of them, $k$ arcs are marked, the angular measure of each of which is less than $\frac{1}{k^{2}-k+1} \cdot 180^{\circ}$, and the circles can be superimposed so that the marked arcs of one circle coincide with the marked arcs of the other. Prove that these circles can be superimposed so that all the marked arcs end up on unmarked areas.

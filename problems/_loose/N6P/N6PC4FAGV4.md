Klara has 6 identical cubes. On the side faces of each cube, she wrote the letters $B, A, B, I, C$ and $A$, one letter on each side face. Then she rolled all 6 cubes at once. What is the probability that she can form the word $B A B I C A$ from the letters that land on the top faces of the cubes?
(A) $\frac{6!}{(2!)^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4}\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2}$
(B) $\frac{6!}{(2!)^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{6}$
(C) $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4}\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2}$
(D) $\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{6}$
(E) 1

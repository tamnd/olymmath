Each operation allows you to select a chip to jump over one, two, or three chips to an empty position, but not to move it directly. The chips that are jumped over must be flipped, but the jumping chip remains the same. Now, it is desired to transform the situation on the left into the situation on the right in six operations. If the positions of the chips before they jump are recorded in sequence, a six-digit number can be formed. Please provide a possible six-digit number that can accomplish the task. $ $ (Provide one possible answer only).

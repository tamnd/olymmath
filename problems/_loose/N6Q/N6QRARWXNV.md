King Arthur's fifty knights sit by the Round Table. In front of each of them is a goblet, filled either with red or white wine. At midnight, everyone passes their goblet to someone else: those who have red wine pass it to their right neighbor, while the others pass it to their left second neighbor. Prove that this way there will be a knight who does not receive a goblet. (There was both red and white wine in the goblets.)

Construct a convex pentagon $A B C D E$ that has the following properties:

(1) $A B=C D=5 \text{ cm}$,

(2) $\angle E A B=\angle A B C=95^{\circ}$,

(3) $B C=C E=B E$,

(4) $A E=E D$.

Describe and justify your construction! Determine whether the conditions (1) to (4) uniquely determine a convex pentagon $A B C D E$ up to congruence!

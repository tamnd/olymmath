Find all functions $f$ from the set of positive integers to itself such that the following conditions both hold:

(i) $f(n!)=f(n)$ ! for all positive integers $n$;

(ii) $m-n$ divides $f(m)-f(n)$ whenever $m$ and $n$ are different positive integers.

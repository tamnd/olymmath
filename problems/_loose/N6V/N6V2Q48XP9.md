Once, three boys met in the library. One of them said: "Now I will go to the library every other day." The second stated that he would go to the library every three days, and the third said he would go to the library every four days. The librarian, who overheard their conversation, noted that the library is closed on Wednesdays. The boys replied that if any of them were to have a visit date fall on the library's day off, they would come the next day and the subsequent count of visits would start from that day. This is what the boys did. One Monday, they all met in the library again. On which day of the week did the conversation described above take place?

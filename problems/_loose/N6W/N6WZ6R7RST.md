In the corners of a 3x3 chessboard, knights are placed: white knights in the top corners, black knights in the bottom corners. Prove that to swap their positions, it will take no fewer than 16 moves. (The knights do not necessarily move in the order of white, then black. A move is defined as one knight moving.)

During its flight around the Moon, the Soviet space station Luna 3 approached the Earth's satellite to within about $7000 \mathrm{~km}$. For the following calculations, assume the Moon's radius $r$ to be $r \approx 1750 \mathrm{~km}$, and the surface area $F$ of a spherical cap with the sphere's radius $r$ and cap height $h$ is $F=2 \pi r h$, where $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$ is used.

a) How large is the area of the Moon that could be seen from this distance?

b) What percentage of the Moon's surface does this represent?

c) Under what viewing angle $\varphi$ would the Moon be observable from this distance?

d) How wide must an object be to be seen under the same viewing angle from a distance of $100 \mathrm{~m}$?

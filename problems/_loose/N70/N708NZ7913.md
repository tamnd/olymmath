Given different natural numbers $a, b$. On the coordinate plane, the graphs of the functions $y=\sin a x, y=\sin b x$ are drawn, and all their intersection points are marked. Prove that there exists a natural number $c$, different from $a, b$, such that the graph of the function $y=\sin c x$ passes through all the marked points.

(I. Bogdanov)

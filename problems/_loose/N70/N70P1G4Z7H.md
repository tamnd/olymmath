Find all integers $n>3$, such that there exist $n$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots$, $A_{n}$ and real numbers $r_{1}, r_{2}, \cdots, r_{n}$ in the plane, satisfying the following two conditions:
(1) No three points among $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ are collinear;
(2) For each triplet $\left\{A_{i}, A_{j}, A_{k}\right\}, 1 \leqslant i<j<k \leqslant n, \triangle A_{i} A_{j} A_{k}$ has an area $S_{i j k}=r_{i}+r_{j}+r_{k}$.

We consider the first die to be more advantageous than the second if the probability that the number rolled on the first die is greater than the number rolled on the second die is greater than 1/2. Can the first 18 natural numbers be written on the faces of three fair dice in such a way that the first die is more advantageous than the second, the second die is more advantageous than the third, and the third die is more advantageous than the first?

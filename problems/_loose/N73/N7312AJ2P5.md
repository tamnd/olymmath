Petya named two non-zero real numbers $x$ and $y$ to Vasya and asked him to compute two new numbers: $x + \frac{1}{y^2}$ and $y^2 + \frac{1}{x}$. Vasya got confused and calculated the values of the expressions $x^2 + \frac{1}{y}$ and $y + \frac{1}{x^2}$. Nevertheless, the results turned out to be the same, only in reverse order. Prove that Petya named Vasya two identical numbers.

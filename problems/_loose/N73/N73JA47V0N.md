Show the following properties, for $p$ a prime number and $n, m$ two integers:

1. $v_{p}(n m)=v_{p}(n)+v_{p}(m)$,
2. $v_{p}(m+n) \geqslant \min \left(v_{p}(m), v_{p}(n)\right)$,
3. $v_{p}(\text{GCD}(m, n))=\min \left(v_{p}(m), v_{p}(n)\right)$,
4. $v_{p}(\text{LCM}(m, n))=\max \left(v_{p}(m), v_{p}(n)\right)$.

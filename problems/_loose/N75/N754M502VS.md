Given on the plane are two circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ that touch each other at $E$. On each circle $k_{i}$ $(i=1,2)$, we mark points $X_{i}$ and $Y_{i}$ such that the two lines $X_{i} Y_{i}$ intersect on the common internal tangent of the circles. Prove that the radical axis of the circles $X_{1} X_{2} E$ and $Y_{1} Y_{2} E$, as well as the radical axis of the circles $X_{1} Y_{2} E$ and $X_{2} Y_{1} E$, intersect on the common internal tangent.

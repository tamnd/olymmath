8-155 Let real numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, \cdots, a_{n}$ satisfy
$$
a_{0}=a_{n}=0, a_{k-1}-2 a_{k}+a_{k+1} \geqslant 0, k=1,2, \cdots, n-1 .
$$

Prove: $a_{k} \leqslant 0, k=0,1,2, \cdots, n$.

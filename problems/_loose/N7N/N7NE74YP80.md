(Jury, VNR, 79). Prove that if a polynomial $P(x)$ with real coefficients takes only non-negative values for all $x \in \mathbf{R}$, then it can be represented in the form

$$
P(x)=Q_{1}^{2}(x)+\ldots+Q_{n}^{2}(x)
$$

where $Q_{1}(x), \ldots, Q_{n}(x)$ are some polynomials with real coefficients.

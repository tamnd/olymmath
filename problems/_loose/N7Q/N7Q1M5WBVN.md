An irreducible polynomial is a not-constant polynomial that cannot be factored into product of two non-constant polynomials. Consider the following statements :-
**Statement 1 :** $p(x)$ be any monic irreducible polynomial with integer coefficients and degree $\geq 4$. Then $p(n)$ is a prime for at least one natural number $n$
**Statement 2 :** $n^2+1$ is prime for infinitely many values of natural number $n$
Show that if **Statement 1** is true then **Statement 2** is also true

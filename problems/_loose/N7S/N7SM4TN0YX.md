Three friends competed in shooting at a target.

- "I bet," said the first before the competition began, "that at least one of you two will miss the target on the first try."
- "I bet," replied the second, "that if you manage to hit the target on your first shot, you will win your bet."
- "And I," said the third, "I simply bet that each of us three will hit the target on the first try."

They shot. Could it have happened that the second and the third shooters both won or both lost their bets at the same time?

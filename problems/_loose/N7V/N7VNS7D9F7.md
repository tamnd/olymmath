Find the number of divisors (positive) of the following numbers:

a $2 \times 2023$

b $a \times b$ if $a$ and $b$ are coprime and have $n_{a}$ and $n_{b}$ divisors, respectively.

c $a_{1} \times a_{k}$ if the $a_{i}$ are pairwise coprime and have $n_{1}, \ldots, n_{k}$ divisors.

Does the formula found in (c) work without the assumption of having pairwise coprime numbers? Why?

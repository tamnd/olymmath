Consider a sector on the plane with its vertex at the origin and an angle of 30 degrees. Place a ball (a material point with zero diameter) on one of the sides of the angle (ray) and release it with a uniform speed into the sector at an angle of 110 degrees to the considered ray. The ball will fly within the sector, bouncing elastically off its sides with the speed magnitude preserved: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection. How many times will it hit the walls of the sector before flying off to infinity?

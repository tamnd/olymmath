Let $a, b, c, d$ be lines passing through a point $O$, $c$ is perpendicular to $a$, and $b$ and $d$ form a $45^{\circ}$ angle with $a$. We draw a circle around $O$ and take one of its tangents that intersects each of our lines. From each intersection point, we draw the second tangent (i.e., the one different from the intersecting line).

What can we say about the quadrilateral defined by the four new tangents?

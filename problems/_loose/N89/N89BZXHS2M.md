In a country of knights (who always tell the truth) and liars (who always lie), 10 people are sitting at the vertices of a regular decagon around a round table, and among them, there are liars. A traveler can stand somewhere and ask the seated people: "What is the distance from me to the nearest liar among you?" After this, each of them answers him. What is the minimum number of questions the traveler must ask to guarantee finding out who the liars are at the table? (There are no outsiders nearby, and the traveler cannot stand on the table. Consider people as points. Everyone, including the traveler, can accurately measure any distance.)

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive integers not less than $A$, and for any $i, j, 1 \leq i, j \leq n$, we have $\left(a_{i}, a_{j}\right) \leq B$. Prove: $\left[a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right] \geq \max _{1 \leq i \leq n} \frac{A_{B}^{i}}{i^{\frac{i(i-1)}{2}}}, A, B \in Z^{+}$.

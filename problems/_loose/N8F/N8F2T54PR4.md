A line $l$ passing through $F_{1}$ intersects the ellipse at points $P, Q$, and $\overrightarrow{A P} \cdot \overrightarrow{A Q}=\frac{1}{2}(a+c)^{2}$. Try to find the minimum value of the eccentricity of the ellipse $C$.

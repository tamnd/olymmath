In the quadrilateral $A B C D$, the intersection point of the diagonals is $E$. On the diagonal $A C$, we measure the segment $A F=C E$, and on the diagonal $B D$, we measure the segment $B G=D E$. Prove that the centroids of triangles $A C G$, $B D F$, and $E F G$ are the same, and the same as the centroid of the quadrilateral area. (The centroid of the quadrilateral area lies on the line connecting the centroids of any two triangles formed by one of its diagonals.)

Which digit is most likely to be the units digit of the **sum** of Jack's integer and Jill's integer? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 0 \textbf{(B)}\ 1 \textbf{(C)}\ 8 \textbf{(D)}\ 9 \textbf{(E)}\ \text{each digit is equally likely} $

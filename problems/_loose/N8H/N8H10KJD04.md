Given an integer $n \geqslant 2$. From an $n \times n$ grid

remove $n$ cells located in different rows and different columns, the resulting shape is called an $n \times n$ "sub-grid". $1 \times k (k \in$ $\mathbf{Z}_{+}$) or $k \times 1$ sub-grids are both called a "bar". For any $n \times n$ sub-grid $A$, partition it into several bars (i.e., these bars do not overlap and exactly cover the sub-grid $A$), in all possible partitions of $A$, the minimum number of bars is denoted as $m(A)$. When $A$ ranges over all $n \times n$ sub-grids, find the range of values for $m(A)$.

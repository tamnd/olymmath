In a utopian novel, there is talk of an author who lives infinitely long.

Every day, he writes a text, filling at least one sheet of paper, and if he starts additional sheets on that day, he also fills each of these sheets on the same day. Over the course of each year, he fills a certain number of sheets; these numbers can vary from year to year, but in no year does this number exceed 730.

Prove:

In the life of this author, for every positive integer $ n $, there is a period of consecutive days during which the author fills exactly $ n $ sheets.

Determine the values of the real parameter $a$, such that the solutions of the system of inequalities
$\begin{cases}
\log_{\frac{1}{3}}{(3^{x}-6a)}+\frac{2}{\log_{a}{3}}<x-3\\
\log_{\frac{1}{3}}{(3^{x}-18)}>x-5\\
\end{cases}$
form an interval of length $\frac{1}{3}$.

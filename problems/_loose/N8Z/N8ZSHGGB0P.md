The line $O G$ intersects the faces of the tetrahedron at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$. Then the value of $\frac{A_{1} O}{A_{1} G}+\frac{B_{1} O}{B_{1} G}+\frac{C_{1} O}{C_{1} G}+\frac{D_{1} O}{D_{1} G}$ is
A. $\frac{4}{3}$
B. 2
C. $\frac{8}{3}$
D. 4

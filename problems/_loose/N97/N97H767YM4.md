Given an arbitrary triangle $ABC$. On the sides $AB, BC$, and $CA$, arbitrary points $C_{1}, A_{1}$, and $B_{1}$ are chosen. Let $P_{1}, P_{2}$, and $P_{3}$ denote the areas of triangles $A C_{1} B_{1}, B C_{1} A_{1}$, and $C A_{1} B_{1}$ respectively, and let $P$ be the area of triangle $ABC$. Prove that

$$
\sqrt{P_{1}}+\sqrt{P_{2}}+\sqrt{P_{3}} \leq \frac{3}{2} \sqrt{P}
$$

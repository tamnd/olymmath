$ \textbf{(A)}\ \frac{\pi}{36}\sqrt 2
\textbf{(B)}\ \frac{\pi}{36}\sqrt 3
\textbf{(C)}\ \frac{\pi}{36}
\textbf{(D)}\ \frac12

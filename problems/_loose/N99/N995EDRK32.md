For positive integers $k, m$, if for any positive integer $\mathrm{a}$, there exists a positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, such that $\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{i}^{\mathrm{k}} \equiv \mathrm{a}(\bmod \mathrm{m})$, then the positive integer $\mathrm{k}$ is said to have property $\mathrm{T}(\mathrm{m})$. Find:
(1) All positive integers $k$ that have property $\mathrm{T}(20)$;
(2) The smallest positive integer $k$ that has property $\mathrm{T}\left(20^{15}\right)$.

$n$ people participate in a training competition consisting of 12 rounds. After each round, each participant receives a score $a_{k}\left(a_{k} \in \mathbf{N}_{+}, k=1,2, \cdots, n, a_{1}>a_{2}>\cdots>a_{n}\right)$ based on their rank $k$ in that round. After all the matches, the final ranking is determined by the sum of the scores each person has accumulated over the 12 rounds. Find the smallest positive integer $n$, for which it is possible to appropriately select $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ such that after the penultimate round, regardless of the rankings, at least 2 people have the possibility of becoming the ultimate champion.

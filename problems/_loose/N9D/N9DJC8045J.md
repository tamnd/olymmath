Every student in the Netherlands receives a finite number of cards. Each card has a real number in the interval $[0,1]$ written on it. (The numbers on different cards do not have to be different.) Find the smallest real number $c>0$ for which the following holds, regardless of the numbers on the cards that everyone has received.

Every student whose sum of the numbers on the cards is at most 1000 can distribute the cards over 100 boxes so that the sum of the numbers in each box is at most $c$.

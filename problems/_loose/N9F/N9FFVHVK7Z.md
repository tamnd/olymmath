For real numbers $a, b, c, x, y, z$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=25 \\
& x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=36 \\
& a x+b y+c z=30
\end{aligned}
$$

What is the value of the ratio $\frac{a+b+c}{x+y+z}$?

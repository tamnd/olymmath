IAT $\star \star$ Let $P$ be a point inside $\triangle ABC$, and $Q$ its isogonal conjugate (i.e., $\angle BAP - \angle QAC$ and so on). Prove:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& AP \cdot AQ \cdot BC + BP \cdot BQ \cdot AC + CP \cdot CQ \cdot AB \\
= & AB \cdot BC \cdot CA .
\end{aligned}
$$

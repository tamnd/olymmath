Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral. Let $P$ be the intersection of the external angle bisectors of $D \widehat{A} C$ and $D \widehat{B} C$.

Prove that $A \widehat{P} D = B \widehat{P} C$ if and only if $A D + A C = B C + B D$.

[Note: It is recalled that the external angle bisector of an angle is the line passing through the vertex of the angle and perpendicular to the internal bisector (i.e., the usual bisector) of the angle itself.]

Any positive integer $n$ can be written in the form $n = 2^aq$ , where $a \ge 0$ and $q$ is odd. We call $q$ the *odd part* of $n$ . Define the sequence $a_0,a_1,...$ as follows: $a_0 = 2^{2011}-1$ and for $m > 0, a_{m+i}$ is the odd part of $3a_m + 1$ . Find $a_{2011}$ .

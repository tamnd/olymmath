Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{x}{b+c}+\frac{y}{c-a}=a+b \\
& \frac{y}{c+a}+\frac{z}{a-b}=b+c \\
& \frac{z}{a+b}+\frac{x}{b-c}=c+a
\end{aligned}
$$

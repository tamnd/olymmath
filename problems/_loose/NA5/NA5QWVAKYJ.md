Let $Z$ be a set of $n(\geqslant 4)$ points in space, where no four points are coplanar, and there are $\left[\frac{n^{2}}{4}\right]+1$ line segments connecting these points. Prove: These line segments must form two triangles with a common edge (in particular, when $n$ is even, this problem was from the 1987 National Training Team Selection Examination).

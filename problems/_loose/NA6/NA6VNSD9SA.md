The numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 100$ are written in ascending order. It is allowed to erase several consecutive numbers and write down the number equal to their count (for example, erase the numbers $13,14,15$ and write the number 3 instead). Is it possible to achieve that after several such operations, the numbers 50,51 remain on the board?

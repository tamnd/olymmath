Find all real numbers $a$ and $b$

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\left|\frac{x^{y}-1}{x^{y}+1}\right|=a \\
x^{2}+y^{2}=b
\end{array}\right.
$$

given that $x>0$, there is a unique solution.

Given an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, in which $A B < A C$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $A C$ respectively, and $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$. On the segment $M N$, there is a point $K$ such that $B K = C K$. The ray $K D$ intersects the circumcircle $\Omega$ of triangle $A B C$ at point $Q$. Prove that points $C, N, K$, and $Q$ lie on the same circle.

(K. Ivanov)

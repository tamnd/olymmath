In the plane, there is a point $O$ and three lines passing through it: $e_{1}, e_{2}$, and $f$. The line $f$ has two points $F_{1}, F_{2}$, which are different from $O$ and from each other. The perpendicular line to $f$ at $F_{i}$ intersects $e_{i}$ at $E_{i}$ $(i=1,2)$. The lines $E_{1} F_{2}$ and $E_{2} F_{1}$ intersect at point $G$.

Prove that the line $O G$ is independent of the choice of points $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$.

Little Boole has drawn a strip of 8 cells, in each of which a 0 or a 1 can be written. Initially, each cell contains a 0. With each move, Boole performs one of the following operations:

(a) replaces every 0 with a 1 and every 1 with a 0;

(b) chooses three consecutive cells and only in these replaces every 0 with a 1 and vice versa.

How many different combinations of 0s and 1s can Boole obtain?
(A) $2^{5}$
(B) $2^{6}$
(C) $2^{7}$
(D) $2^{8}-2$
(E) $2^{8}$

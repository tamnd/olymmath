Consider the points $O, A, B, C$ such that $[O A] \equiv [O B] \equiv [O C]$, $\angle A O B \equiv \angle B O C$ and $m(\angle A O B) > 90^{\circ}$. On the semiline opposite to the semiline $(O B)$, consider the point $D$.

a. Show that $(O D)$ is the bisector of the angle $\angle A O C$.

b. Show that $\triangle B C D \equiv \triangle B A D$

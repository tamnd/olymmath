Find all natural numbers $m$ and $n$ such that there exist natural numbers $x$ and $y$ for which

$$
x^{2}+y^{2}+3 x y=11 \cdot 5^{m} \cdot 3^{n}
$$

For such natural numbers $m$ and $n$, determine all $x$ and $y$ that satisfy the above equation in terms of $m$ and $n$.

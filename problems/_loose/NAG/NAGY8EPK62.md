Mientka Publishing Company prices its bestseller *Where's Walter?* as follows: $$C(n) =
\begin{cases}
12n, &\text{if } 1 \le n \le 24
11n, &\text{if } 25 \le n \le 48
10n, &\text{if } 49 \le n
\end{cases}$$ where $ n$ is the number of books ordered, and $ C(n)$ is the cost in dollars of $ n$ books. Notice that $ 25$ books cost less than $ 24$ books. For how many values of $ n$ is it cheaper to buy more than $ n$ books than to buy exactly $ n$ books? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 3
\textbf{(B)}\ 4
\textbf{(C)}\ 5
\textbf{(D)}\ 6
\textbf{(E)}\ 8$

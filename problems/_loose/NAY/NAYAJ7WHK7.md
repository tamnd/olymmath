Consider the real numbers $a, b$ and the continuous function $f: R \rightarrow R$ with the property that $\int_{a t}^{b t} f(x) d x \leq \int_{a}^{b} f(x) d x$, for any $t \in(0,2)$. Prove that $a f(a)=b f(b)$.

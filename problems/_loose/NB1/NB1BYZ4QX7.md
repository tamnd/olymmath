a) Write the formula that gives Joško's daily wage depending on the number of people who come to training.

b) What is Joško's daily wage if no one comes to training?

c) What is Joško's daily wage if 20 people come to training?

d) How many people need to come to training for Joško to receive a daily wage of 300 euros?

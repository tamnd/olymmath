The technical control department inspected $n=100$ batches of $N=10$ items each, and as a result, obtained a distribution table of the random variable $X$ - the number of non-standard items in one batch:

| $x_{\imath}$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $n_{\imath}$ | 2 | 3 | 10 | 22 | 26 | 20 | 12 | 5 |.

It is required to test the hypothesis at a significance level $\alpha=0.01$ that $X$ is distributed according to the binomial law.

In convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ , only $D$ is an obtuse angle. Use some line segments to divide it into $n$ obtuse triangles. But on its sides (except $A,B,C,D$ ), there is no vertex of triangles we divided into.
Prove that if and only if $n\geq4$ , we can divide the convex quadrilateral into such $n$ triangles.

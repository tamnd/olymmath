Then, the area of the region where the point corresponding to the complex number $z_{1}+z_{2}$ is located is ( ).
(A) $4+\pi$
(B) $2 \sqrt{2}+\pi$
(C) $\pi$
(D) $\frac{3 \pi}{2}+1$

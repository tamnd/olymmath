Lemma B.4 Let $C$ be any non-separating circle on a surface $S$, and let $S^{\prime}$ be the surface obtained by cutting $S$ along $C$ and covering the one or two holes thus formed.
(i) If $C$ is one-sided in $S$, then $\varepsilon\left(S^{\prime}\right)=\varepsilon(S)-1$.
(ii) If $C$ is two-sided in $S$, then $\varepsilon\left(S^{\prime}\right)=\varepsilon(S)-2$.

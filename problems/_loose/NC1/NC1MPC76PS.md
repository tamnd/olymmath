On the $O x$ axis, different points $X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}, n \geq 3$ are arbitrarily placed. All parabolas defined by reduced quadratic trinomials and intersecting the $O x$ axis at the given points (and not intersecting it at any other points) are constructed. Let $y=f_{1}(x), \ldots, y=f_{m}(x)$ be the corresponding parabolas. Prove that the parabola $y=f_{1}(x)+$ $\ldots+f_{m}(x)$ intersects the $O x$ axis at two points.

On one square of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, a "-" is written, and in all other squares, a "+" is written. In one step, we can simultaneously change all four signs in any $2 \times 2$ square on the chessboard to their opposites. Determine whether, after a certain number of steps, the number of each sign on the chessboard can be the same.

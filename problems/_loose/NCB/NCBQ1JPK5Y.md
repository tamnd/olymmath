a) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be an increasing sequence of natural numbers, in the decimal representation of which the digit 1 does not appear. Prove that the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 1 / a_{n}$
converges.

b) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ be an increasing sequence of natural numbers, in the decimal representation of which one hundred consecutive ones do not appear. Prove that the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 1 / a_{n}$
converges.

For the set of positive integers 99 yuan
$$
M=\{1,2, \cdots, 99\}
$$

color the elements with red, blue, and green such that each color has 33 numbers, which is called a "tricolor partition" of set $M$. For the following three propositions, please provide a proof or a counterexample:
(1) There exists a tricolor partition of $M$ such that any Pythagorean triple in $M$ has all three colors;
(2) There exists a tricolor partition of $M$ such that any Pythagorean triple in $M$ is monochromatic;
(3) For any tricolor partition of $M$, there must exist a Pythagorean triple where the three numbers are either all the same color or all different colors.

Different parallelograms $A B C D$ and $A K L D$ are arranged so that their sides $B C$ and $K L$ lie on the same line, and the lines $A C$ and $K D$ are not parallel. Prove that the point of intersection of the lines $A K$ and $D C$, the point of intersection of the lines $A B$ and $D L$, and the point of intersection of the lines $A C$ and $K D$ lie on one line.

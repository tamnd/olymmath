Let the function $f_{1}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}0 (x=0) \\ -\frac{1}{2} x \left(4^{k-1} \leqslant|x|<2 \cdot 4^{k-1}, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right) \\ 2 x \left(2 \cdot 4^{k-1} \leqslant|x| \leqslant 4^{k}, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right) .\end{array}\right.$
(1) Find the domain of $f_{1}(x)$;
(2) The graph of $y=f_{1}(x)$ is rotated by $\frac{\pi}{2}$ around the origin to obtain the graph of $y=f_{2}(x)$. Find the analytical expression for $y=f_{2}(x)$;
(3) For a function $f(x)$ defined on $\mathbf{R}$, if the graph of $y=f(x)$ remains unchanged after being rotated by $\frac{\pi}{2}$ around the origin, prove that the equation $f(x)=x$ has exactly one real solution.

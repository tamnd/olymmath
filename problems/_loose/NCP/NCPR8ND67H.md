Find the smallest possible sum $a + b + c + d + e$ where $a, b, c, d,$ and $e$ are positive integers satisfying the conditions

$\star$ each of the pairs of integers $(a, b), (b, c), (c, d),$ and $(d, e)$ are **not** relatively prime

$\star$ all other pairs of the five integers **are** relatively prime.

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a family of sets. If the graph $G$ on $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies $X Y \in E(G) \Leftrightarrow X \cap Y \neq \emptyset$, then we say $G$ is the intersection graph of $\mathcal{F}$. Prove: A graph is a chordal graph if and only if it is isomorphic to the intersection graph of a family of subtrees (of the vertex set) of a tree.

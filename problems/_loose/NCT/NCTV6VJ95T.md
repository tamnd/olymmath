In a cell, there are 10 viruses of type $A$. At a certain moment, a virus of type $B$ enters this cell. After that, every hour, each virus $B$ kills one virus $A$, and immediately after this event, each virus divides into 2, both of the same type as that virus.

a) After 3 hours from the arrival of the virus $B$ in the cell, what is the quantity of each type of virus, including the duplications from the battles after $3 h$?

b) After how long will the quantity of viruses of type $B$ be equal to the quantity of viruses of type $A$?

c) Will the viruses of type $A$ be extinct from the cell?

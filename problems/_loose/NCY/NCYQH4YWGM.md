Consider four points $A, B, C$, and $D$ aligned in this order. The circles $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ with diameters $A C$ and $B D$ respectively intersect at $K$ and $L$. Let $P$ be a point on $(K L)$ that does not belong to $(A D)$. The line $(C P)$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ at $C$ and $M$, and the line $(B P)$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ at $B$ and $N$. Show that the lines $(A M),(D N)$, and $(K L)$ are concurrent.

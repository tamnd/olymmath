In the pyramid $A B C D$, the dihedral angles with edges $A B, B C$ and $C A$ are equal to $\alpha 1, \alpha 2$ and $\alpha 3$ respectively, and the areas of triangles $A B D, B C D$ and $C A D$ are $S 1, S 2$ and $S 3$ respectively. The area of triangle $A B C$ is $S$. Prove that $S=S 1 \cos \alpha 1+S 2 \cos \alpha 2+S 3 \cos \alpha 3$ (some of the angles $\alpha 1, \alpha 2$ and $\alpha 3$ may be obtuse).

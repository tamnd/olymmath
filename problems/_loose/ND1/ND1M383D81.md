Prove that the function $f(x)$ is convex on the interval $[a, b]$ if and only if for any $n$ and any points $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ from this interval, the inequality

$$
f\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{n}}{n}\right) \leqslant \frac{f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)+\ldots+f\left(x_{n}\right)}{n}
$$

holds.

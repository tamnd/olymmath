Let $n>1$ be a natural number. We write down the fractions $1 / n, 2 / n, \ldots, (n-1) / n$ and reduce each to its simplest form; the sum of the numerators of the resulting fractions is denoted by $f(n)$. For which natural numbers $n>1$ do the numbers $f(n)$ and $f(2015 n)$ have different parity?

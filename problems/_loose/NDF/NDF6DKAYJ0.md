Let $n$ be an integer greater than 1, and its all positive divisors are $d_{1}, d_{2}, \cdots, d_{k}$, where $1=d_{1}<d_{2}<\cdots<d_{k}=n$. It is known that $d_{2}-d_{1}, d_{3}-d_{2}, \cdots, d_{k}-d_{k-1}$ are all the positive divisors of another positive integer (all distinct but not necessarily in increasing order). Find all possible values of $n$.

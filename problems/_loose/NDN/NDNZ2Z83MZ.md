To complete the same work, the time required for $A$ to work alone is $m$ times the time required for $B$ and $C$ to work together; the time required for $B$ to work alone is $n$ times the time required for $A$ and $C$ to work together; and the time required for $C$ to work alone is $k$ times the time required for $A$ and $B$ to work together; then $k$ expressed in terms of $m, n$ is
(A) $\frac{2 m n}{m+n}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{m+n-m n}$.
(C) $\frac{1-m n}{m+n+2 m n}$.
(D) $\frac{m+n+2}{m n-1}$.
(2nd "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1985)

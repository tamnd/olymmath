Consider the following equation:

$$
x^{4}+4 x^{2}+1-a x\left(x^{2}-1\right)=0
$$

1. Show that if we substitute $-\frac{1}{x}$ for $x$, we arrive at the same equation!
2. By substituting $y=x-\frac{1}{x}$, we obtain a quadratic equation in $y$. When are the roots of this equation real?
3. Calculate the values of $x$ as well, when the values of $y$ are equal!

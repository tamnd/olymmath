Prove that for any triangle, the following equality holds:

$$
r=\frac{a \sin \frac{\beta}{2} \sin \frac{\gamma}{2}}{\cos \frac{\alpha}{2}}
$$

where $ r $ is the radius of the inscribed circle, $ \alpha, \beta $ and $ \gamma $ are the angles of triangle $ ABC $, and $ a = BC $.

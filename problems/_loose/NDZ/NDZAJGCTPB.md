Given an acute scalene triangle $A B C$, point $O$ is the center of its circumscribed circle. The extension of the altitude $B H$ of triangle $A B C$ intersects its circumscribed circle at point $N$. Points $X$ and $Y$ are marked on sides $A B$ and $B C$ respectively such that $O X \| A N$ and $O Y \| C N$. The circumscribed circle of triangle $X B Y$ intersects segment $B H$ at point $Z$. Prove that $X Y \| O Z$.

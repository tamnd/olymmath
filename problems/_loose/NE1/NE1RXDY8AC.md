In the triangle $ABC$, let the intersection point of the angle bisector $AA_1$ with side $BC$ be $A_1$. Draw lines through $A_1$ parallel to $AC$ and $AB$, and name their intersection points with $AB$ and $AC$ as $A_{2b}$ and $A_{2c}$, respectively, which are called second-step or second-type points. Draw lines through the second-type points parallel to $BC$, and name their intersection points with $AC$ and $AB$ as $A_{3b}$ and $A_{3c}$, respectively, which are called third-step or third-type points. Similarly, construct the second- and third-type points using the angle bisectors $BB_1$ and $CC_1$. Prove that the distance between any two second-type points on any side of the triangle is the same as the distance between the two third-type points. What other regularities can be observed in the placement of these points?

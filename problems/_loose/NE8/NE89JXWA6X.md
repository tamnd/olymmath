Three cars $A, B, C$ each travel at a certain speed from location A to location B. $B$ starts 5 minutes later than $C$, and catches up with $C$ 20 minutes after starting. If $A$ starts 10 minutes later than $B$, and catches up with $C$ 50 minutes after starting. Then the time it takes for $A$ to catch up with $B$ after starting is
(A) 100 min.
(B) 200 min.
(C) 250 min.
(D) 50 min.
(2nd "Jinyun Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1985)

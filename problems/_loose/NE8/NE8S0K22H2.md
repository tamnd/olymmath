In space, there are five points, $A, B, C, D$, and $E$. We know that there is a congruence transformation of the space that swaps $A$ and $B$, and leaves $C$, $D$, and $E$ in place. Furthermore, there is another congruence transformation that maps $A$ to $B$, $B$ to $C$, $C$ to $D$, $D$ to $E$, and $E$ to $A$. What is the ratio $AB: CD$?

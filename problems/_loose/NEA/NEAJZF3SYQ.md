Consider a semicircle with diameter $AB = 2R$, and center $O$. Draw two parallel lines through points $A$ and $O$, which intersect the semicircle at points $C$ and $D$ and form an angle $x$ with the diameter $AB$. Draw the chords $BC$, $BD$, and $CD$, which form the trapezoid $AODC$ and the triangle $BCD$. Determine the angle $x$ such that:

1. the sum of the areas of the trapezoid and the triangle is $S^2$. What is the maximum value of $S^2$?
2. the area of the trapezoid is twice the area of the triangle;
3. the area of the triangle is twice the area of the trapezoid.

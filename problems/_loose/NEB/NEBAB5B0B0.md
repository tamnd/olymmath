On the side $\overline{A B}$ of the right-angled triangle $A B C$, there is a point $D$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $A D C$ and $B C D$, respectively. Prove that

$$
P(X D Y) \geqslant \frac{1}{4} P(A B C)
$$

where $P(K L M)$ is the area of triangle $K L M$. When does equality hold?

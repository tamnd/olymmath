Prove: If
$$
-\frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2} (i=1,2, \cdots, n),
$$

then
$$
\frac{\cos x_{1}+\cos x_{2}+\cdots+\cos x_{n}}{n} \leqslant \cos \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}}{n} ;
$$

If
$$
0 \leqslant x_{i}<\frac{\pi}{2} (i=1,2, \cdots, n),
$$

then
$$
\frac{\tan x_{1}+\tan x_{2}+\cdots+\tan x_{n}}{n} \geqslant \tan \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}}{n} .
$$

Given a triangle $A B C$. An inscribed circle is tangent to the sides $A B, A C, B C$ at points $C_{1}$, $B_{1}, A_{1}$ respectively. Find the radius of the excircle $w$, which is tangent to side $A B$ at point $D$, the extension of side $B C$ at point $E$, and the extension of side $A C$ at point $G$. It is known that $C E=6$, the radius of the inscribed circle is $1$, and $C B_{1}=1$.

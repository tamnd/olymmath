Given the ellipse $\Gamma: \frac{x^{2}}{16}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$, $A(-4,0), B(4,0), C$ is a point outside the ellipse $\Gamma$, $C A$ and $C B$ intersect the ellipse $\Gamma$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively, the tangents to the ellipse $\Gamma$ at points $A$ and $Q$ intersect at point $R$, and the tangents to the ellipse $\Gamma$ at points $B$ and $P$ intersect at point $S$. Prove: $R$, $C$, and $S$ are collinear.

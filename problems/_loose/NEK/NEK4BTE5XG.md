A group of pirates has seized 128 gold pieces. If one of them has at least half of the gold pieces, each of the other pirates steals as many gold pieces from him as they already have in their possession. If two pirates each have 64 gold pieces, one of these two individuals steals all the pieces from the other. It is assumed that seven rounds of theft take place. Show that in the end, one pirate ends up with the entire loot.

Let $ A$ and $ B$ be set in the plane and define the sum of $A$ and $B$ as follows: $A \oplus B = \{a + b | a \in A$ and $B \in B \}$ .
Let $T_1, T_2, .., T_n$ be equilateral triangles with side $\ell$ in the plane and let $S$ equal the area of $T_1 \oplus T_2 \oplus ... \oplus T_n$ . Prove that $$ \sqrt3 \cdot 2 ^ {2n-2} \cdot \ell ^ 2 \le S \le \frac {3 \cdot 2 ^ {n-2} \cdot \ell ^ 2} {\tan (\pi / (2 ^ n \cdot 3))} $$

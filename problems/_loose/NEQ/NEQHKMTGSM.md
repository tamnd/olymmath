Find the side $B C$ of the quadrilateral $A B C D$, if $\angle B A C=\alpha, \angle A C D=\beta, \angle B C A+\angle C A D=\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $A D=a$. In the answer, write the result rounded to two decimal places.

$$
\alpha=\arcsin \frac{5}{13}, \beta=\arcsin \frac{12}{13}, a=24
$$

4.6.43 $\stackrel{\star \star}{\star}$ Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be non-negative real numbers, and satisfy
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}+\sum_{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n}\left(x_{i} x_{j}\right)^{2}=\frac{n(n+1)}{2} \text {. }
$$
(1) Find the maximum value of $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}$;
(2) Find all positive integers $n$, such that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} \geqslant \sqrt{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}$.

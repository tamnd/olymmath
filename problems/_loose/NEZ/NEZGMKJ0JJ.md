On a plane, grasshopper Kolya and 2020 of his friends are sitting. Kolya is going to jump over each of the other grasshoppers (in any order) such that the starting and ending points of each jump are symmetric relative to the grasshopper being jumped over. We will call a point a finish point if Kolya can end up in it after the 2020th jump. For what largest number $N$ can there be an initial arrangement of grasshoppers such that there are exactly $N$ different possible finish points?

Let $f(x), g(x)$ be continuous on $\mathbf{R}$, $g(0)=0, a=g^{\prime}(0)$, and for $\forall x, y \in \mathbf{R}$, the inequality
$$
f(x+y) \geqslant f(x)+g(y)
$$

holds, then
$$
f(x)=a x+b, g(y) \leqslant a y,
$$

where the equality in (1) holds if and only if the equality in (2) $g(y) \leqslant a y$ holds.

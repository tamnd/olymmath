Trapezoid $A B C D$ with bases $A B$ and $C D$ is inscribed in circle $\Omega$. Circle $\omega$ passes through points $C, D$ and intersects segments $C A, C B$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ respectively. Points $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ are symmetric to points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ with respect to the midpoints of segments $C A$ and $C B$ respectively. Prove that points $A, B, A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ lie on the same circle.

(I. Bogdanov)

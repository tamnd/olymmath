In the foggy city of London, there are exactly $10^{10}$ clubs, and in each club, there are exactly 10 gentlemen. An enemy spy wants to kidnap several gentlemen so that among the kidnapped, there is at least one member of each club. It turns out that for any two clubs, there is a gentleman who is a member of both. Prove that the spy needs to kidnap only 9 gentlemen.

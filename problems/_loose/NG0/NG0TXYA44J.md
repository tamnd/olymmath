The Lost Cent. This is an old problem that can still stump many people today. Two traders were selling apples, one at three for a cent, and the other at two for a cent. They had to leave for a while. Each still had 30 unsold apples, which they entrusted to a friend to sell at 2 cents for five apples. If the traders had managed to sell the remaining apples themselves, they would have earned 25 cents for them, but as it turned out, they only earned 24 cents. "Where," you might ask, "did the 1 cent go? Selling three apples for a cent and two apples for a cent is the same as selling five apples for 2 cents."

Could you explain this simple puzzle?

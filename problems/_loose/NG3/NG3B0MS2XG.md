Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle with $AC < BC$; let $M$ be the midpoint of segment $AB$. In the circumcircle $\Omega$ of triangle $ABC$, a diameter $CC'$ is drawn. The line $CM$ intersects the lines $AC'$ and $BC'$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. Let the perpendicular to the line $AC'$ through point $K$, the perpendicular to the line $BC'$ through point $L$, and the line $AB$ form triangle $\Delta$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $\Delta$ is tangent to the circle $\Omega$.

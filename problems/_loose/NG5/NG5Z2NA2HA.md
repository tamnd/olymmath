In an attempt to colonize Mars, humanity flooded the solar system with 50 satellites, which established 225 communication lines between them (each line exists between one pair of satellites, and no two satellites have more than one line between them). We say that a trio of satellites is connected if one of them has communication lines established with both of the other two satellites. Determine the smallest and largest possible number of connected trios of satellites. (The order of satellites in pairs or trios does not matter.)

(Ján Mazák, Josef Tkadlec)

A circle passes through the vertices $L$ and $M$ of triangle $F L M$ and intersects its sides $F L$ and $F M$ at points $A$ and $H$, respectively, different from the vertices of the triangle. The ratio of the area of triangle $F L M$ to the area of triangle $A F H$ is $\frac{49}{9}$.

a) Find the ratio $L M: A H$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the areas of triangles $A F M$ and $F H L$ is $\frac{1}{4}$. Find the ratio $A L: M H$.

A square $A B C D$ and an equilateral triangle $P Q R$ are inscribed in a circle centred at $O$ in such a way that $A B \| Q R$. The sides $P Q$ and $P R$ of the triangle meet the side $A B$ of the square at $X$ and $Y$ respectively.

The value of $\tan \angle X O Y$ is
(A) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$;
(B) 1 ;
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2}}$;
(D) $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}-2}{\sqrt{3}}$;
(E) $\sqrt{3}$.

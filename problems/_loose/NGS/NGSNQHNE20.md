In $\triangle A B C$, the angle bisector of $\angle A$ intersects side $B C$ at point $D$. Through $D$, draw the tangents to the circumcircles of $\triangle A B D$ and $\triangle A C D$, which intersect sides $A C$ and $A B$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. The line $B E$ intersects $C F$ at point $G$. Prove: $\angle E D G = \angle A D F$.

It is known that the first basket contains 20 apples, the second - 30, the third - 40, the fourth - 60, and the fifth - 90. After the contents of one of the baskets were completely sold, the number of apples of the first variety became twice the number of apples of the second variety. How many apples could have been in the sold basket? If there are multiple answers, list them all.

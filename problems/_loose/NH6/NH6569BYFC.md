In triangle $ABC$, on the rays $AB$ and $CB$, segments $|AM|=|CN|=p$ are laid out, where $p$ is the semiperimeter of the triangle (with $B$ lying between $A$ and $M$, and between $C$ and $N$). Let $K$ be the point on the circumcircle of $ABC$ that is diametrically opposite to $B$. Prove that the perpendicular dropped from $K$ to $MN$ passes through the incenter of the inscribed circle.

At the vertices of a regular 100-gon, 100 chips numbered $1, 2, \ldots, 100$ were placed, in exactly that order clockwise. In one move, it is allowed to swap two adjacent chips if their numbers differ by no more than $k$. For what smallest $k$ can a series of such moves result in a configuration where each chip is shifted one position clockwise relative to its initial position? (S. Berlov)

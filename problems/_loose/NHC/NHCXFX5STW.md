Numbers $a_{1}, b_{1}, a_{2}, b_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}, b_{n}$ satisfy the conditions

$$
\begin{gathered}
a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}=2018^{2}, b_{1}^{2}+b_{2}^{2}+\cdots+b_{n}^{2}=2017^{2} \\
a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+\cdots+a_{n} b_{n}=2017 \cdot 2018
\end{gathered}
$$

Find the ratios $a_{1} / b_{1}, a_{2} / b_{2}, \ldots, a_{n} / b_{n}$.

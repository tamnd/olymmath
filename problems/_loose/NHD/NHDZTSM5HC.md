+ To prove the first inequality, calculate the number of ordered pairs $(x, Y)$ where $x \in A$, $|Y|=r$, $Y \subseteq B$, and $x$ is connected to all vertices in $Y$. For $\operatorname{ex}\left(n, K_{r, r}\right)$, use the inequality $(s / t)^{t} \leqslant \binom{s}{t} \leqslant s^{t}$ and the fact that the function $z \mapsto z^{r}$ is convex.

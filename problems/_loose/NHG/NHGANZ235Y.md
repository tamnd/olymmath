A rectangular plate is measured in two ways by equal segments on its sides. In the first case, starting from each corner and moving towards the next corner (in the direction of one perimeter of the rectangle), and in the second case, starting from the endpoints of one diagonal towards the two adjacent corners. Consider the area of the quadrilateral defined by the endpoints of the segments in both cases. For what segment measurement does the area reach an extreme value in each case? For what segment measurement will the areas of the quadrilaterals obtained in the two ways be equal?

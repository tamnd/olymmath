In a $10 \times 10$ grid, place 1 white stone in each of any 91 cells. Each time, remove one white stone and place one black stone, and the black stone can be placed in any empty cell. Then remove another white stone and place another black stone. Continue this process until there are no more white stones. Prove that at some point, there must be two adjacent (sharing a common edge) cells with stones of different colors.

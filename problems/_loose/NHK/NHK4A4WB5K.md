Quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$ is inscribed in a circle. The rays $\mathrm{BA}$ and $\mathrm{CD}$ intersect at point $\mathrm{P}$. A line passing through $\mathrm{P}$ and parallel to the tangent to the circle at point $\mathrm{D}$ intersects the tangents drawn to the circle at points $\mathrm{C}$, $\mathrm{U}$, and $\mathrm{V}$ at points $\mathrm{U}$ and $\mathrm{V}$. The tangents to the circle at points $\mathrm{C}$, $\mathrm{U}$, and $\mathrm{V}$ form triangle $\mathrm{CUV}$, and the quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$ is tangent to these tangents.

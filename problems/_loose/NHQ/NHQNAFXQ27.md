Let $k_{i} \in \mathbf{Z}, \alpha_{i} \in \mathbf{R},\left|\alpha_{i}\right|=1, x_{i}=x_{0}+k_{i}, i=1,2, \cdots, m, x_{0}$ be a constant. Prove that:
$$
\sum_{i \neq j} \alpha_{i} \alpha_{j} \cos \left[\left(x_{i}-x_{j}\right) \frac{\pi}{2}\right] \leqslant s^{2}+(m-s)^{2}-m \text {. }
$$

where $s$ is the number of even numbers among $k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{m}$.

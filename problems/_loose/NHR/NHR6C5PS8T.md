Given a regular triangular pyramid $S A B C$ (with $S$ as its vertex). The edge $S C$ of this pyramid coincides with the lateral edge of a regular triangular prism $A 1 B 1 C A 2 B 2 S$ (where $A 1 A 2$, $B 1 B 2$, and $C S$ are the lateral edges, and $A 1 B 1 C$ is one of the bases). The vertices of the prism $A 1$ and $B 1$ lie in the plane of the face $S A B$ of the pyramid. What fraction of the volume of the entire pyramid is the volume of the part of the pyramid that lies inside the prism, if the ratio of the length of the lateral edge of the prism to the length of the side of its base is $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$.

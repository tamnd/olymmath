The sides of a dodecagon are numbered $C_{1}, C_{2}, \ldots, C_{12}$. In how many ways can the sides of a dodecagon be colored with four colors so that two adjacent sides are always of different colors? (We consider two colorings to be different as soon as one of the sides $C_{i}$ is colored with two different colors).

Fermat's Little Theorem can be generalized as follows: if the number $a$ is coprime with $k$, then the difference $a^{\varphi(k)}-1$ is divisible by $k$, where $\varphi(k)$ is the number of numbers less than $k$ and coprime with it (Euler's theorem). Prove this theorem for the cases:

a) $k=p$ (prime number);

b) $k=p q$ ( $p$ and $q$ - prime numbers);

c) $k=p^{2}$ ( $p$ - prime number);

d) $k=p^{\lambda}$ ( $p$ - prime number).

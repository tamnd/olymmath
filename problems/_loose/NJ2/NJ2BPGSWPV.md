In a certain store, the unit prices of three types of goods, A, B, and C, are 2 yuan, 3 yuan, and 5 yuan, respectively. A person buys several pieces of these three types of goods, paying a total of 20 yuan. This person then realizes that they bought too much of one type of good, and returns two pieces of this good, but the cashier only has 10 yuan bills and no change. As a result, this person adjusts the quantities of the other two types of goods (and each type of good is still bought at least one piece), keeping the total price unchanged. At this point, the number of items of type B that this person has bought is
(A) 1.
(B) 2.
(C) 3.
(D) 4.
(China Jiangxi Nanchang Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

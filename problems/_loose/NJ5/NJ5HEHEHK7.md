Given a circle $S$, a point $P$ located outside $S$, and a line $l$ passing through $P$ and intersecting the circle at points $A$ and $B$. The point of intersection of the tangents to the circle at points $A$ and $B$ is denoted by $K$.

a) Consider all possible lines passing through $P$ and intersecting $A K$ and $B K$ at points $M$ and $N$. Prove that the geometric locus of the points of intersection of the tangents to $S$, different from $A K$ and $B K$, drawn from points $M$ and $N$, is a certain line passing through $K$, excluding its intersection with the interior of $S$.

b) We will choose a point $R$ on the circle in different ways and draw a line connecting the points of intersection of the lines $R K$ and $R P$ with $S$, different from $R$. Prove that all the obtained lines pass through one point, and this point lies on $l$.

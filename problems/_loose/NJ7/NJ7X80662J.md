We draw circles $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}$ with equal radii around the vertices of a regular triangle. The inverse image of a point $P$ with respect to $k_{1}$ is $P_{1}$, the inverse of $P_{1}$ with respect to $k_{2}$ is $P_{2}$, and the inverse of $P_{2}$ with respect to $k_{3}$ is $P_{3}$. Construct a point $P$ such that $P_{3}$ coincides with $P$.

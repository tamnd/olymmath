During a football championship, the four teams $A, B, C$, and $D$ played in the semi-finals. Experts of the four teams made the following predictions about the outcome of the matches and thus the determination of the two teams for the final:

(1) The final will be: $B$ against $C$.

(2) The final will not be: $A$ against $B$.

(3) The final will be: $C$ against $D$.

(4) If $A$ reaches the final, then $B$ will not reach the final.

(5) The final will be played by two of the teams $B, C, D$.

After the semi-finals, it turned out that exactly two of the predictions (1) to (5) were false.

Show that it is possible to determine the two teams that reached the final based on this information!

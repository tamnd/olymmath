Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a group and $f, g$ be two endomorphisms of the group $G$, with $g$ being injective. Show that the group $G$ is commutative in each of the following situations:
a) $f(x) = g(x^2), \forall x \in G$.
b) $f(x) = g(x^{-1}), \forall x \in G$.

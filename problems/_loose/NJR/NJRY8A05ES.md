In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\angle BAD=90^{\circ}$, diagonal $AC=BD$, the perpendicular bisectors of $AB$ and $CD$, and the perpendicular bisectors of $AD$ and $BC$ intersect at $Q$ and $P$, respectively, then $Q, P, A$ are collinear. (Refer to Example 18 in the first chapter of the first volume of Xian Yi)

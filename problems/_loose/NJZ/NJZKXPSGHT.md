Let's now move on to the second raised question. Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive numbers, for the sake of clarity, let's arrange them in ascending order: $a_{1} \leqq a_{2} \leqq \ldots \leqq a_{n}$; let $q_{1}, q_{2}, \ldots, q_{n}$ be positive numbers, $q_{1}+q_{2}+\ldots+q_{n}=1$.

Prove that the weighted $r$-th power mean of the given numbers with these weights, if $r$ is a positive rational number, lies between $q_{n}^{1 / r} a_{n}$ and $a_{n}$; if, however, $s$ is negative, then the $s$-th power mean lies between $a_{1}$ and $q_{1}^{1 / s} a_{1}$.

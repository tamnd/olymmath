In the Wild West, two straight rivers flow parallel to each other at a distance of $10 \mathrm{~km}$. The medicine man of an Indian village, who is in his hut, must collect a vial of water from each of the two rivers and bring the vials to the village for the prayer ceremony to Manitou. What is the minimum distance of the path he must travel, knowing that the hut and the village are equidistant from the two rivers and are $48 \mathrm{~km}$ apart?
(A) 48
(B) 52
(C) 53
(D) 58
(E) $\sqrt{48^{2}+10^{2}}$

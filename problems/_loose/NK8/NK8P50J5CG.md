Dima went to school in the morning, but after walking exactly half the distance, he realized he had forgotten his mobile phone at home. Dima estimated (he had an A in mental arithmetic) that if he continued walking at the same speed, he would arrive at school 3 minutes before the first bell, but if he ran home for the phone and then ran to school, he would arrive 3 minutes after the bell. Dima decided to run home, but he got out of breath while running (he had a C in physical education), and walked from home to school at his usual speed. As a result, he was 15 minutes late for the first class! How many times faster is the speed at which he runs compared to the speed at which he walks?

19100 athletes participate in a long-distance running competition. It is known that among any 12 of them, 2 can be found who are acquainted. Prove that no matter how the athletes are assigned numbers (not necessarily from 1 to 100), it is always possible to find 2 acquainted athletes whose numbers start with the same digit.

Let $A, B$ be subsets of the set of integers, and $B$ is a finite set. If every integer can be uniquely represented as the sum of an element from $A$ and an element from $B$. Prove: there exists a non-zero integer $a$, such that $A+a=A$. (Where $A+a=\{x+a: x \in A\}$).

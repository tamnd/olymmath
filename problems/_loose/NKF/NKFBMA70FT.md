In the triangles $ABC=H$ and $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}=H_{1}$ on the plane, the sides $AB$ and $A_{1}B_{1}$, as well as the sides $AC$ and $A_{1}C_{1}$, lie on parallel lines and are equal in pairs. The two triangles have opposite orientations with the vertices listed in the above order. Reflect $A_{1}$ onto $A$, $B_{1}$ onto $B$, and $C_{1}$ onto $C$, and show that the area of the triangle determined by the three new points is 3 times larger than that of $H$ and $H_{1}$.

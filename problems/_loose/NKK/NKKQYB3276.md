Consider the surjective function $f: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ and the strictly increasing function $g: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$, such that $f(x) \geq g(x)$, for every $x \in \mathbb{N}$.

a) Show that $f(x)=g(x)$, $(\forall) x \in \mathbb{N}$;

b) Calculate $f(1)-g(2)+f(3)-g(4)+\ldots+f(2015)-g(2016)$.

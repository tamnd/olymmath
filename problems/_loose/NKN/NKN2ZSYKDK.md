A player on a computer controls a fox hunting two rabbits. At vertex $A$ of the square $A B C D$ there is a burrow: if at least one rabbit gets into it in the absence of the fox, the game is lost. The fox catches a rabbit as soon as it is in the same point (possibly at point $A$). Initially, the fox is at point $C$, and the rabbits are at points

$B$ and $D$. The fox runs everywhere with a speed not exceeding $v$, while the rabbits run along the rays $A B$ and $A D$ with a speed not exceeding 1. For what values of $v$ can the fox catch both rabbits?

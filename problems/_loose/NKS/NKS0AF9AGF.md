$S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ are non-empty sets of integers. For any permutation $i, j, k$ of $1,2,3$, if $x \in S_{i}, y \in S_{j}$, then $x-y \in S_{k}$.
(1) Prove: At least two of the three sets are equal.
(2) Is it possible for two of the three sets to have no common elements?

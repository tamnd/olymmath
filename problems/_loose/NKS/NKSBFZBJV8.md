Prove: (1) When the incircle is used as the base circle for inversion, the inverted images of the three sides are three circles with diameters $ID$, $IE$, and $IF$ respectively; (2) The other intersection points of these three circles lie on $AI$, $BI$, and $CI$ respectively.

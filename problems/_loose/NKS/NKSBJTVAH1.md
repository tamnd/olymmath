$$
\begin{gathered}
x^{2}+3 x y+y^{2}=909 \\
3 x^{2}+x y+3 y^{2}=1287
\end{gathered}
$$

What is a possible value for $x+y$ ?
(A) 27
(B) 39
(C) 29
(D) 92
(E) 41

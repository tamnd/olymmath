Sequences $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ , $(y_n)_{n\ge1}$ satisfy the relations $x_n=4x_{n-1}+3y_{n-1}$ and $y_n=2x_{n-1}+3y_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$ . If $x_1=y_1=5$ find $x_n$ and $y_n$ .

Calculate $\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{x_n}{y_n}$ .

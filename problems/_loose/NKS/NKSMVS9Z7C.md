Determine all pairs $(m ; n)$ of natural numbers $m$ and $n$ for which the following conditions (1) and (2) are satisfied:

(1) $m+n$ and $m \cdot n$ are two-digit numbers.

(2) If the digits of the number $m+n$ are reversed, the result is the (digit representation of the)

number $m \cdot n$.

João wrote some non-zero real numbers, all distinct, on the board in such a way that if we take any one of them and square it, the result is greater than the product of any other two numbers written on the board.

(a) Explain why there cannot be three positive real numbers written on the board.

(b) Explain why there cannot be three negative real numbers written on the board.

(c) Explain why there cannot be two positive numbers and two negative numbers written on the board.

(d) Determine the maximum number of numbers that can be written on the board with an example of a set of numbers that satisfies the required condition.

Let $p$ is a prime and $p\equiv 2\pmod 3$. For $\forall a\in\mathbb Z$, if
$$p\mid \prod\limits_{i=1}^p(i^3-ai-1),$$then $a$ is called a "GuGu" number. How many "GuGu" numbers are there in the set $\{1,2,\cdots ,p\}?$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4}=y_{1}+y_{2}+y_{3}+y_{4} \\
& x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+x_{3}^{2}+x_{4}^{2}=y_{1}^{2}+y_{2}^{2}+y_{3}^{2}+y_{4}^{2} \\
& x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+x_{3}^{3}+x_{4}^{3}=y_{1}^{3}+y_{2}^{3}+y_{3}^{3}+y_{4}^{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that

$$
\left(x_{1}-y_{2}\right)\left(x_{1}-y_{3}\right)\left(x_{1}-y_{4}\right)=\left(y_{1}-x_{2}\right)\left(y_{1}-x_{3}\right)\left(y_{1}-x_{4}\right)
$$

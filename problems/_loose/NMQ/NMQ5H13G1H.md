Gábor noticed that the sum of $12^{3}+14^{3}$ has digits that are the reverse of the digits of $14^{3}$. Based on this, he gave the following problem to his mathematician friend, Feri. "Determine the four-digit (integer) number from which, when subtracting the number formed by writing its digits in reverse order, the difference is equal to the cube of an integer." - The next day, Feri said: There are more than 200 numbers that meet your conditions. - Gábor: Where is the 200 subtraction, since there is no calculation on your side? - F.: I managed to determine the number of such numbers without writing down even one of them. - G.: I don't believe in that calculation! - Was Feri right?

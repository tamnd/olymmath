Positive integer $n \geqslant 5, P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n}$ are integer points in a Cartesian coordinate system with $O$ as the origin, $\triangle O P_{1} P_{2}, \triangle O P_{2} P_{3}, \cdots, \triangle O P_{n} P_{1}$ all have an area of $\frac{1}{2}$. Prove that there exists a pair of integers $i, j$ such that $2 \leqslant|i-j| \leqslant n-2$, and $\triangle O P_{i} P_{j}$ has an area of $\frac{1}{2}$. (31st International Mathematical Olympiad Candidate Question, 1990)

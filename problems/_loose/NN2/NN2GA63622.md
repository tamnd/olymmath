For a regular octagon, a square is constructed such that the center of the octagon is a vertex of the square and that the side length $a$ of the octagon and the side length $b$ of the square satisfy the inequality $b \geq \frac{4}{3} a$.

Let $f$ denote the area of the region that is common to both the octagon and the square.

Prove that for a given octagon, $f$ has the same value for all squares that meet this description.

In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the incenter is $I$, and the circumcenter is $O$.
1. If $\angle B=60^{\circ}$, $|OI|=|AB-AC|$.

Prove: $\triangle ABC$ is an equilateral triangle.
2. If $AB<AC$, $IO=\frac{1}{2}(AC-AB)$.

Prove: $S_{\triangle LAO}=\frac{1}{2}\left(S_{\triangle BAO}-S_{\triangle CAO}\right)$.

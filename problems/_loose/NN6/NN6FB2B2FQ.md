Let $P$ be a point, and draw 4 circles $\Gamma_{i}$ for $i \in\{1,2,3,4\}$ passing through $P$ such that no pair of these circles are tangent. Denote $P_{i, j}$ with $i \neq j$ as the second intersection point of $\Gamma_{i}$ and $\Gamma_{j}$ (other than $P$). Denote $\omega_{i}$ as the circle passing through the 3 points $P_{j, k}$ where $j$ and $k$ take all values other than $i$. Show that the four circles $\omega_{i}$ have a common point.

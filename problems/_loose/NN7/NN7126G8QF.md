Isosceles Triangle. Prove that if for the angles $A, B, C$ of some triangle the following relation holds

$$
\operatorname{tg}(A-B)+\operatorname{tg}(B-C)+\operatorname{tg}(C-A)=0
$$

then this triangle is isosceles.

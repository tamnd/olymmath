In a | mile race, the first person is 2 miles ahead of the second person, and 4 miles ahead of the third person. If the runners maintain a constant speed throughout the race, the number of miles by which the second person will be ahead of the third person when the second person reaches the finish line is
(A) 2.
(B) $2 \frac{1}{4}$.
(C) $2 \frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $2 \frac{3}{4}$.
(E) 3.
(15th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1964)

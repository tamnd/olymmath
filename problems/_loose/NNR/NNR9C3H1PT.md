Let $x$ be a positive real number. Define
$$
A = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{3k}}{(3k)!}, B = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{3k+1}}{(3k+1)!}, \text{and} C = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{3k+2}}{(3k+2)!}.
$$ Given that $A^3+B^3+C^3 + 8ABC = 2014$ , compute $ABC$ .

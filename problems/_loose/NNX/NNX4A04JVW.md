(2002-2003 Hungarian Mathematical Olympiad Final Problem) Let $t$ be a fixed positive integer, and $f_{1}(n)$ denote the number of integers $k$ such that $C_{k}^{t}$ is odd, where $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$ and $k$ is an integer. If $1 \leqslant k < A$, then $C_{k}^{t}=0$ is defined. Prove: If $n$ is a sufficiently large power of 2, then $\frac{f_{t}(n)}{n}=\frac{1}{2^{r}}$, where $r$ is an integer that depends on $t$ but not on $n$.

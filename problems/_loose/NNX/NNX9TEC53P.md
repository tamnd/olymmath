In the plane (in a fixed position), there is a triangle $ABC$ and a triangle $X'Y'Z'$. Write a triangle $XYZ$ into $\triangle ABC$ such that the sides of $\triangle XYZ$ are parallel to the sides of $\triangle X'Y'Z'$. (The points $X, Y, Z$ are on the sides of $\triangle ABC$ and not on their extensions.) What are the conditions for solvability and how many solutions are there? How many solutions are there if the vertices of the sought triangle can also be on the extensions of the sides of the given $\triangle ABC$?

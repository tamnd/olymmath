Suppose on the coordinate plane there are infinitely many rectangles, whose vertices have coordinates of the form
$$
(0,0),(0, m),(n, 0),(n, m) \text {, }
$$

where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers. Prove that it is always possible to select two rectangles from these such that one rectangle is contained within the other.

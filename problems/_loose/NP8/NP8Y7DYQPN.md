Let's inscribe an equilateral triangle in a square, with one of its vertices coinciding with one of the square's vertices. In this triangle, let's draw a square such that two of its vertices lie on one side of the triangle, and the third and fourth vertices lie on the other sides of the triangle. In this square, we inscribe another triangle, and so on, to infinity. What is the sum of the areas of the squares, if one side of the original square is $a$?

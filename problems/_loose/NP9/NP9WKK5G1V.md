(1988 14th All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad) On a grid paper with small squares of size $1 \times 1$, a circle of radius $R$ is drawn with one of the nodes (the intersection points of the grid lines) as the center. Prove that if there are exactly 1988 nodes on the circumference, then either $R$ or $\sqrt{2} R$ is an integer.

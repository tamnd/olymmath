An isosceles triangle $ A B C (A B = B C) $ is inscribed in a circle. The line $ C D $, perpendicular to $ A B $, intersects the circle at point $ P $. The tangent to the circle passing through point $ P $ intersects the line $ A B $ at point $ Q $. Find the segments $ P A $ and $ P Q $, if $ A C = 5 $ and $ \angle A B C = 2 \arccos \sqrt{\frac{5}{6}} $.

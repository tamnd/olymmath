In $\triangle A B C$, let $E$ and $F$ be arbitrary points on sides $A C$ and $A B$, respectively. Let $O$ and $O^{\prime}$ be the circumcenters of $\triangle A B C$ and $\triangle A E F$, respectively. Let $P$ and $Q$ be points on $B E$ and $C F$ such that $\frac{B P}{P E}=\frac{F Q}{Q C}=\frac{B F^{2}}{C E^{2}}$. Prove that $O O^{\prime} \perp P Q$.

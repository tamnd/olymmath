In an $n \times n$ chessboard, the common part of any $k$ rows and any $l$ columns is called its "sub-region," and $k+l$ is referred to as its semi-perimeter. If several sub-regions with semi-perimeters not less than $n$ together cover the entire main diagonal of the chessboard, prove: the number of squares covered by these sub-regions is no less than half the total number of squares on the chessboard.

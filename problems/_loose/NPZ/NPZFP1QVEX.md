After three hours, the motorcyclist arrived at point $B$, passing through $C$ along the way. After that, he turned around and, still uniformly increasing his speed, headed back. Two hours later, he passed point $C$ again at a speed of 110 km/h and continued on to $A$. Find the distance between points $A$ and $C$.

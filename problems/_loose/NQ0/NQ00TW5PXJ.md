Someone claims that the equation

$$
x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}=n
$$

where $n$ is a natural number, has

$$
0<x_{1}<x_{2}<x_{3}
$$

natural number solutions

$$
n=21, 57 \text { and } 165
$$

in the cases of

$$
27\left(=3^{3}\right), 243\left(=3^{5}\right), 2187\left(=3^{7}\right)
$$

Is this a coincidence, or are we dealing with special cases of a generally valid statement?

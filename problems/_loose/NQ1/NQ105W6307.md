On the sides of a convex hexagon $ ABCDEF$ , equilateral triangles are constructd in its exterior. Prove that the third vertices of these six triangles are vertices of a regular hexagon if and only if the initial hexagon is *affine regular*. (A hexagon is called affine regular if it is centrally symmetric and any two opposite sides are parallel to the diagonal determine by the remaining two vertices.)

Determine all odd integers $n \ge 3$ with the following property: Let $S$ denote the set of all positive integers less than $n$ which are relatively prime to $n$ , and let $k = \tfrac{1}{2}|S|$ . Then one can label the integers in $S$ by $a_1, \ldots, a_{2k}$ in some order such that $$ \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_i^2 = \tfrac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_i a_{i+k}. $$ *Xu Zi Jie*

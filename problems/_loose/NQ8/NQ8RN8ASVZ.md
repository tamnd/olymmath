Given an angle whose size is less than $180^{\circ}$ and a point $P$ inside this angle. The vertex of the angle is $A$.

Construct a line $g$ that passes through the point $P$ and intersects the sides of the angle at points $B \neq A$ and $D \neq A$ respectively, such that $P$ is the midpoint of $B D$!

Describe and justify your construction!

Determine whether there is exactly one line that satisfies the conditions of the task!

Let $\mathrm{f}: \mathbb{N}^{\star} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{\star}$ be a function such that for any integer $n \geqslant 1, f(f(n))$ is equal to the number of positive divisors of $n$. Show that if $p$ is a prime number, then $f(p)$ is also a prime number.

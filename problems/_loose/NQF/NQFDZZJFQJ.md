For a circle that touches the sides of a right-angled triangle, if the distances from the center of the circle to the vertices are $l, m, n$, then:

$$
\frac{1}{l^{2}}=\frac{1}{m^{2}}+\frac{1}{n^{2}}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{m n}
$$

( $l$ is the distance from the center of the circle to the right-angle vertex).

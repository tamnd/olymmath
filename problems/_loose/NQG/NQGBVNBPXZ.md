Ani, Bobi, and Viki are sitting at a round table and eating nuts. Initially, all the nuts are with Ani. She distributes them evenly to Bobi and Viki, and eats the remainder (if any). Each subsequent girl (in a clockwise direction) does the same: she distributes her nuts evenly to her neighbors, and eats the remainder (if any). The nuts are many (more than 3). Prove that:
a) at least one nut will be eaten;
b) not all nuts will be eaten.

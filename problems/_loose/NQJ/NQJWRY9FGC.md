The sides of a regular pentagon are $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}, a_{5}$. The orthogonal projections of points $M$ and $N$ in the plane of the pentagon onto $a_{i}$ are $M$ and $N_{i}(i=1,2,3,4,5)$. Show that

$$
2\left(\overrightarrow{M_{1} N_{1}}+\overrightarrow{M_{2} N_{2}}+\overrightarrow{M_{3} N_{3}}+\overrightarrow{M_{4} N_{4}}+\overrightarrow{M_{5} N_{5}}\right)=5 \overrightarrow{M N}
$$

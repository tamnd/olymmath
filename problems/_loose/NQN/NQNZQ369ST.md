Let $n$ be a positive integer. The integers from $1$ to $n$ are written in the cells of an $n \times n$ table (one integer per cell) so that each of them appears exactly once in each row and exactly once in each column. Denote by $r_i$ the number of pairs $(a, b)$ of numbers in the $i^\text{th}$ row ( $1 \le i \le n$ ), such that $a > b$ , but $a$ is written to the left of $b$ (not necessarily next to it). Denote by $c_j$ the number of pairs $(a, b)$ of numbers in the $j^\text{th}$ column ( $1 \le j \le n$ ), such that $a > b$ , but $a$ is written above $b$ (not necessarily next to it). Determine the largest possible value of the sum

$$
r_1 + r_2 + \cdots + r_n + c_1 + c_2 + \cdots + c_n.
$$ $\textbf{Note:}$ In the $n \times n$ table we label the rows $1$ to $n$ from top to bottom, and we label the columns $1$ to $n$ from left to right.

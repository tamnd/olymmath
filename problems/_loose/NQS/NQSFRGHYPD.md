$AB>AC$ hold in a given triangle $ABC$ . It's incircle $\odot I$ is tangent to side $AB$ at $F$ , $BC$ at $D$ and $CA$ at $E$ . $M,N$ is the midpoint of $DE,DF$ in order, and $FE$ intersects $BC$ at $P$ .
A moving line that pass through $P$ intersects $\odot I$ at different points $G$ and $H$ , such that $IMG$ and $INH$ are both $\text{Not}$ collinear. prove that the intersection of $\odot(IMG)$ and $\odot(INH)$ lies on a static circle.

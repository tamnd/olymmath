Let $n$ be a positive integer. $2n$ persons are around a round table. Let $a \ge n$ be an integer. We call two persons *friends* if and only if there are exactly $a$ persons between one of the arcs passing between them. For each positive integer $n$ find all possible values of $a$ for which, we can choose $n$ persons, none of them are antipodal, and there would be $n$ total friendly relations between them.

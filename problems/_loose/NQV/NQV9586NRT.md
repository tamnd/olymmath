If the roots of the equation $p x^{2}+n x+n=0$ are in the ratio $a: b$, then prove that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}+\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}+\sqrt{\frac{n}{p}}=0 \\
& a \neq 0, b \neq 0, n \neq 0, p \neq 0
\end{aligned}
$$

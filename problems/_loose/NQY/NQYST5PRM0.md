Write three integers on the blackboard, then erase one of them and replace it with the difference of 1 and the sum of the other two numbers. After repeating this process several times, the numbers $(17,1967, 1983)$ are obtained. Could the three numbers initially written on the blackboard have been:
(1) $(2,2,2)$;
(2) $(3,3,3)$.
(17th All-Soviet Union Mathematical Olympiad, 1983)

Let the coefficients of the $n$-degree monadic equation $b_{n} x^{n}+b_{n-1} x^{n-1}+\cdots+b_{1} x+b_{0}=0$ all be imaginary numbers, $b_{k}=a_{k}+a_{n-k} \mathrm{i}(k=0,1, \cdots, n)$, and satisfy $0<a_{0}<a_{1}<a_{2}$ $<\cdots<a_{n-1}<a_{n}$. If $z$ is a complex root of the equation, try to find $|z|$.

Let's prove that if $x$ and $y$ are different positive numbers, and $p, q$ are natural numbers, then

$$
\frac{x^{-p / q}-y^{p / q} \cdot x^{2 p / q}}{x^{(1-2 p) / q}-y^{1 / q} \cdot x^{-2 p / q}}>p(x y)^{(p-1) / 2 q}
$$

It is known that the red figure lies between the blue and green ones; to the right of the yellow figure lies the rhombus; the circle lies to the right of both the triangle and the rhombus; the triangle does not lie at the edge; the blue and yellow figures do not lie next to each other. Determine the order in which the figures lie and what colors they are.

Let $A B C D$ and $C D E F$ be two quadrilaterals inscribed in two circles $\Gamma_{1}, \Gamma_{2}$. Suppose that among the lines $(A B),(C D)$ and $(E F)$, no two are parallel. Then the lines $(A B),(C D)$ and $(E F)$ are concurrent if, and only if, the points $A, B, E$ and $F$ are concyclic.

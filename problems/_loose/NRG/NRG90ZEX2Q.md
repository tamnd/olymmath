At the Olympiad, 2018 participants arrived, some of whom are acquainted with each other. We will say that several pairwise acquainted participants form a "circle" if any other participant of the Olympiad is not acquainted with someone among them. Prove that it is possible to seat all the participants of the Olympiad in 90 auditoriums such that no auditorium contains all representatives of any "circle".

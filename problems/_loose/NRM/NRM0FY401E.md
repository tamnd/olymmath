$n$ numbers ( $n>1$ ) are called close if each of them is less than the sum of all numbers divided by $n-1$.

Let $a, b, c, \ldots-n$ be close numbers, $S-$ their sum. Prove that

a) all of them are positive

b) $a+b>c$

c) $a+b>S / n-1$.

Let $ m $ and $ n $ be natural numbers, satisfying: for any natural number $ n, 11k-1 $ and $ m $ and $ 11k-1 $ and $ n $ have the same greatest common divisor. Prove that there exists an integer $ l $, such that $ m = 11^l n $.
(Romanian Mathematical Olympiad, 1978)

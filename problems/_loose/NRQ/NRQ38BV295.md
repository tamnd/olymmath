Let $n \geqslant 1$ be an integer. We have $n$ cubes with sides $1, 2, \cdots, n$. We want to stack them in a certain order, such that a cube of side $k$ can only be placed on a cube of side $\ell$ with $\ell \geqslant k-2$. How many such different stackings exist?

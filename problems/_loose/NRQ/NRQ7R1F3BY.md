From point $A$, tangents $A B$ and $A C$ to a circle and a secant intersecting the circle at points $D$ and $E$ are drawn; $M$ is the midpoint of segment $B C$. Prove that $B M^{2}=D M \cdot M E$ and angle $D M E$ is twice the angle $D B E$ or angle $D C E$; furthermore, $\angle B E M=\angle D E C$.

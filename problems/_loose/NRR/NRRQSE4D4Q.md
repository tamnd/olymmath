Three lines form a triangle. On each of them, we draw a perpendicular line such that the perpendiculars also form a triangle (that is, the perpendicular drawn to the third line does not pass through the intersection point of the first two). Show that the angles of the new triangle are always equal to the respective angles of the first triangle.

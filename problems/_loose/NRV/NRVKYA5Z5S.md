The Law of Sines
[The ratio in which the bisector divides the side.]

Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. A line passing through point $A$ intersects these circles again at points $C$ and $D$, with point $A$ lying between $C$ and $D$, and the chords $A C$ and $A D$ are proportional to the radii of their respective circles. Prove that the bisectors of angles $A D B$ and $A C B$ intersect on the segment $A B$.

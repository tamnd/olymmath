In a right triangle, given $B M+M A=B C+C A$, if $M B=x$, $C B=h, C A=d$, then $x$ equals
(A) $\frac{h d}{2 h+d}$.
(B) $d-h$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2} d$.
(D) $h+d=\sqrt{2} d$.
(E) $\sqrt{h^{2}+d^{2}}=h$.
(5th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1954)

＊* An $n \times n$ square chessboard is enclosed by $n(n+1)$ "L"-shaped angle irons. Prove: The number of "L"-shaped angle irons placed in an "L" shape is the same as those placed in a "入" shape.

Note: The "入" shape in Chinese refers to an inverted "L" shape, which should be understood as an "L" shape placed in a different orientation.

A space probe, moving in a straight line at a constant speed, flies past Mars and measures the distance to this planet every day at exactly 12:00. It is known that on February 1st, the distance was 5 million km, on February 10th - 2 million km, and on February 13th - 3 million km.

Determine when the probe will pass at the minimum distance from Mars.

In this problem, Mars can be considered a point.

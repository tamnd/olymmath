In each $1 \times 1$ cell of a rectangular board, a natural number is written. In each step, it is allowed to double the numbers written in each cell of any arbitrarily chosen row, or to decrease the numbers written in the cells of any arbitrarily chosen column by 1. Can it happen that after a finite number of steps, all the numbers on the board are zeros.

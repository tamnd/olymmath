Variation Let $n$ be an integer greater than 1, and positive real numbers $x_{1}$, $x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x_{i}-x_{i}^{3}} \geqslant \frac{n^{4}}{n^{2}-1} .
$$

Let $H$ be a finite set of integers. Let $H_{+}$ and $H_{-}$ be the sets of sums and differences of pairs of numbers in $H$, respectively. (We allow pairs of equal numbers and negative differences.) Prove that

$$
|H| \cdot\left|H_{-}\right| \leq\left|H_{+}\right|^{2}
$$

$|X|$ denotes the number of elements in the set $X$.

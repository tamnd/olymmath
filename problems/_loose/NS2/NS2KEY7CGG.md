Height of a circular truncated cone is $8$ . Center of sphere $O_1$ with a radius of $2$ is on the axis of the circular truncated cone. Sphere $O_1$ is tangent to the top surface and the flank. We can put another sphere $O_2$ , satisfying that sphere $O_2$ with a radius of $3$ have only one common point with sphere $O_1$ , bottom surface and the flank. Besides $O_2$ , how many spheres can we put inside the circular truncated cone? $\text{(A)}1 \text{(B)}2 \text{(C)}3 \text{(D)}4$

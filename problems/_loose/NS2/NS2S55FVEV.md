Maximum dimensions & \begin{tabular}{l}
Length and twice the diameter together $100 \mathrm{~cm}$, \\
Length, however, not more than $80 \mathrm{~cm}$;
\end{tabular} \\
Minimum dimensions & \begin{tabular}{l}
Length and twice the diameter together $17 \mathrm{~cm}$, \\
greatest dimension not less than $10 \mathrm{~cm}$.
\end{tabular}
\end{tabular}

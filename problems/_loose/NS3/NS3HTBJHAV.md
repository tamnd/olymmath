We want to build a cement storage tank in the shape of a prism. The base is formed from a square with a side length of $2 \mathrm{~m}$, such that two of the base's additional vertices fall on one midline of the square, and the other two fall on the other midline. The height of the prism is 2 $\mathrm{m}$. The foundation, floor, and walls cost $1000 \mathrm{Ft}$ per $\mathrm{m}^{2}$, and the completed storage tank costs $2500 \mathrm{Ft}$ per $\mathrm{m}^{3}$. How should we shape the base to maximize the benefit of the construction?

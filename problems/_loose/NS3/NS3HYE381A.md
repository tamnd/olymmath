Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral, $E$ an arbitrary point on the line $A B$, and $F$ an arbitrary point on the line $D C$. The line $A F$ intersects the circle at point $M$, and the line $D E$ intersects the circle at point $N$. Prove that the lines $B C$, $E F$, and $M N$ intersect at one point or are parallel.

4-224 A unit square is divided into 9 equal parts by lines parallel to the sides, and the central part is removed. The remaining 8 smaller squares are each divided into 9 equal parts by lines parallel to the sides, and the central part is removed. Then, a similar process is applied to each of the remaining squares. If this process is repeated $n$ times, try to find:
（1）How many squares with side length $\frac{1}{3^{n}}$ are there?
（2）What is the limit of the sum of the areas of the squares removed when $n$ is infinitely increased?

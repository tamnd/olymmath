Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with its incircle $\odot I$ touching side $BC$ at point $K$, $AD$ is the altitude of $\triangle ABC$, and $M$ is the midpoint of $AD$. If $N$ is the intersection of $\odot I$ and $KM$, prove:
$\odot I$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle BCN$ at point $N$.

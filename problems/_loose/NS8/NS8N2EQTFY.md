It is easy to see that there is no triplet of numbers $x, y, z$ that satisfies the following system of equations. Determine values of $x, y, z$ such that if we form the square of the difference between the two sides of each equation, the sum of the four squares is as small as possible:

$$
\begin{gathered}
x+y+z=10 \\
x+y-z=7 \\
x-y+z=6 \\
-x+y+z=5
\end{gathered}
$$

If equilateral triangles are drawn outwardly on the sides of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ and the resulting vertices are connected to the opposite vertices of the triangle, the following statements hold for the connecting segments:

1. The three connecting segments are of equal length.
2. The three connecting segments intersect each other at angles of $60^{\circ}$ each.
3. The three connecting segments intersect at a single point inside the triangle.

These statements are to be proven.

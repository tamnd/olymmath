A 100×100 board is divided into 10,000 unit squares. One of them is cut out, creating a hole. Is it possible to cover the remaining part of the board with isosceles right triangles with a hypotenuse of length 2, such that their hypotenuses lie along the sides of the squares, and the legs lie along the diagonals, without the triangles overlapping or hanging off the board?

Inside an equilateral triangle $ABC$, a point $P$ is chosen arbitrarily.

The feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to the sides $BC, CA, AB$ are denoted in this order by $X, Y, Z$.

Prove that the sum of the lengths $x, y, z$ of the segments $BX, CY, AZ$ does not depend on the choice of the point $P$.

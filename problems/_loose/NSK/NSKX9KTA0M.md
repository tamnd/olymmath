If $A$ is a $k$-element subset of $S=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, and $m$ is a positive integer, satisfying the condition $n>$ $(m-1)\left(C_{k}^{2}+1\right)$, then there exist elements $t_{1}, t_{2}, \cdots, t_{m}$ in $S$, such that $A_{j}=\{x+t, \mid x \in A\}(j=1,2, \cdots, m)$

are pairwise disjoint.

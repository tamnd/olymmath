Let $a$ and $b$ be two parallel lines. $A$ and $P$ are points on $a$, and $B$ and $Q$ are points on $b$. It is given that $P Q \perp a$. The midpoint of $P Q$ is $M$, and $c$ is the line parallel to $a$ through $M$.

Prove the following theorem: If $S$ is the intersection of $c$ with $A B$, then $A S = B S$.

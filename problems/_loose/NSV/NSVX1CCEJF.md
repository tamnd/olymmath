Find the integers $n \geqslant 1$ such that, when the positive divisors of $n$ are ordered to form a list $d_{1}, d_{2}, \ldots, d_{k}$ such that $1=d_{1}<d_{2}<\cdots<d_{k}$, each integer $d_{i}$ obtained when $1 \leqslant i \leqslant k-2$ divides the sum $d_{i+1}+d_{i+2}$.

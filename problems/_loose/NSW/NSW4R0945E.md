Let us mark a point on each edge of a regular octahedron that divides the edge in the ratio $1:(1+\sqrt{5}) / 2$ such that, starting from any vertex of the octahedron and moving along the 4 edges emanating from it, we alternately select the closer and the farther of these dividing points. Show that

a) the dividing points can be chosen according to the stated rule;

b) the 12 selected dividing points determine a regular icosahedron.

Right-angled triangle $ABC$ has legs $|AB|=5 \text{ cm}$ and $|BC|=12 \text{ cm}$. The vertices of triangle $ABC$ are mapped such that

- point $A$ is mapped to point $A^{\prime}$ symmetrically with respect to the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$,
- point $B$ is mapped to point $B^{\prime}$ symmetrically with respect to the midpoint of segment $BC$,
- point $C$ is mapped to point $C^{\prime}$ symmetrically with respect to the perpendicular bisector of segment $AB$.

Determine the area of triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$.

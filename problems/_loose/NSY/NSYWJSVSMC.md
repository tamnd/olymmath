(Hungary, 1982). Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$. Show that if the equation $x^{3}-3 x y^{2}+y^{3}=n$ has a solution $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^{2}$, it has at least three. Show that for $n=2009$, this equation has no solution.

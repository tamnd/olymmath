(A) $40^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 80^{\circ}$.
(B) $60^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}$.
(C) $30^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 90^{\circ}$.
(D) $45^{\circ}, 45^{\circ}, 90^{\circ}$.

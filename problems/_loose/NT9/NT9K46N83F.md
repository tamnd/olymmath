Let the circumcenter of $\triangle A B C$ be $O$, and the perpendicular bisector of side $B C$ intersects sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $X$ and $Y$, respectively. The line $A O$ intersects $B C$ at point $D$, and the midpoint of side $B C$ is $M$. The circumcircle of $\triangle A D M$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ at a second point $E$. Prove that the line $O E$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle A X Y$.

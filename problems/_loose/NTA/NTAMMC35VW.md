Consider the tetrahedron $ABCD$ where $AB \perp CD$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of edge $BC$ and $N$ be the midpoint of edge $BD$. On the ray $(DM$ choose point $E$ such that $DE = 2DM$, and on the ray $(CN$ choose point $F$ such that $CF = 2CN$.

a) Prove that points $F, B, E$ are collinear;

b) Prove that triangle $\triangle AEF$ is isosceles.

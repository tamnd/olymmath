Consider the domain $D$ expressed by the following system inequality:
$$x^2+(y-1)^2\leq 1,\ x\geq \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}.$$

Suppose that the line $l$ passes through the origin and the common part of $l$ and $D$ is line segment.
Find the maximum length of the line segment $L$, then when $L$ is maximized, find the value of $\cos \theta$ for the angle $\theta\ \left(0<\theta <\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ between the $x$-axis and $l$.

Once, someone studying the results of a recently completed two-legged cup match between teams in an NB I league concluded the following. Let's denote the (signed) goal difference that team $A$ achieved against team $B$ at home by $d(A, B)$. Then,

a) for any teams $A, B$, $d(A, B) + d(B, A) = 0$

b) for any teams $A, B, C$, $d(A, B) + d(B, C) + d(C, A) = 0$.

Show that in this case, we can assign numbers to the teams such that for any teams $A, B$, $d(A, B)$ is equal to the difference between the numbers assigned to $A$ and $B$.

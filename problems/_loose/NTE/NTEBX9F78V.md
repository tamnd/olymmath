The positive integers from $1$ to $n^2$ are placed arbitrarily on the squares of a chess board with dimensions $n\times n$ . Prove that there are two adjacent squares (having a common vertex or a common side) such that the difference between the numbers placed on them is not less than $n + 1$ .

(N Sedrakyan, Yerevan)

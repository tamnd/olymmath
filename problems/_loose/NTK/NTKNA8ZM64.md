Let the domain of the function $f(x)$ be $\mathbf{R}$, and for any $x, y$, we have $f(x+y)+f(x-y)=$ $2 f(x) \cdot f(y)$. There exists a non-zero real number $c$ such that $f\left(\frac{c}{2}\right)=0$. Also, $f(0) \neq 0$. Prove that the graph of the function $f(x)$ is symmetric about the line $x=2 k c(k \in \mathbf{Z})$.

Let $O$ be the intersection of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, and let $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}, P_{4}$ be the areas of triangles $ABO, BCO, CDO$, and $DAO$. If $P_{1}^{2}+P_{3}^{2}=P_{2}^{2}+P_{4}^{2}$, prove that point $O$ is the midpoint of at least one of the diagonals.

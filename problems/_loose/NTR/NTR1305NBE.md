Solve the system of equations (1) - (3) for the right-hand side values (4) and (5).

$$
x+a y+a^{2} z=A
$$

$$
x+b y+b^{2} z=B
$$

$$
x+c y+c^{2} z=C
$$

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
A=b+c \\
B=c+a \\
C=a+b
\end{array}\right.
$$

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
A=b c \\
B=c a \\
C=a b
\end{array}\right.
$$

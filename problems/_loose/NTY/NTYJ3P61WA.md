Given the hyperbolas $ C_{1}: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1, C_{2}: \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} - \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}=1, $ and $ P $ is a moving point on $ C_{2} $. From $ P $, draw tangents $ P A $ and $ P B $ to $ C_{1} $, where $ A $ and $ B $ are the points of tangency. Prove that the area of the triangle formed by the line $ A B $ and the two asymptotes is a constant.

Suppose that $a,b,c$ are complex numbers with $a+b+c = 0$, $|abc| = 1$, $|b| = |c|$, and $$\frac{9-\sqrt{33}}{48}
\le \cos^2 \left( arg \left( \frac{b}{a} \right) \right)\le \frac{9+\sqrt{33}}{48} .$$
Find the maximum possible value of $|-a^6+b^6+c^6|$.

A game for two players has the following rule:

One of the two names an arbitrary single-digit number. The other names a larger natural number, which, however, may be at most 10 greater than the previously named number. They take turns. The one who can name the number 100 while adhering to the game rules wins.

a) Is there a way for the starting player to always win the game?

b) Is there also a game start that subsequently gives the second player a way to always win the game?

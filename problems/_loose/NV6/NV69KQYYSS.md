Seven scientific workers are engaged in a confidential technical research. Their laboratory is equipped with an "electronic lock", and each person involved in the project has a key to open the "electronic lock". For safety reasons, at least 4 people must be present to open the door of the laboratory. How many features must the "electronic lock" have? How many of these features should each scientific worker's "key" possess?

This is a quantitative study, not a specific design of such a "lock" and "key". To make it easier to understand, you can consider this problem as how many locks should be hung on the door? And how many keys should each scientific worker carry?

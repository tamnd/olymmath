If $A B C$ is an isosceles triangle with $A B=A C$ and $g$ is any line through $A$ that intersects $B C$ or its extension at $D$ and the circumcircle of the triangle at $E$, then it is always true that $A D \cdot A E=A B^{2}=A C^{2}$.

This theorem is to be proven.

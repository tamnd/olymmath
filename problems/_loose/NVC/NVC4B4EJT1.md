Let the random variable $C$ be distributed according to the Cauchy distribution. Show that

$$
C \stackrel{d}{=} \frac{X}{Y} \stackrel{d}{=} \frac{X}{|Y|}
$$

where $X$ and $Y$ are i.i.d. random variables, distributed according to $\mathscr{N}(0,1)$.

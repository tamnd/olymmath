Let $B_{1}, B_{2}, \cdots, B_{m}$ be a family of subsets of $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, each with $k(k \geqslant 2)$ elements, where $n, k$ are given positive integers, and for any $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant m$, we have $\left|B_{i} \cap B_{j}\right| \leqslant 1$. Prove:
$$
m \leqslant\left[\frac{n}{k}\left[\frac{n-1}{k-1}\right]\right] .
$$

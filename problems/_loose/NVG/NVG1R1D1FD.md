Determine the pairs $(X, Y)$ of sets which have as elements non-zero natural numbers and which simultaneously satisfy the following properties:

(1) each of the sets $X$ and $Y$ has three elements;

(2) $3 \in X$ and $5 \in Y$;

(3) the set $X \cap Y$ has exactly one element;

(4) if $a$ and $b$ are different elements of the set $X$, then $(a+b) \in Y$.

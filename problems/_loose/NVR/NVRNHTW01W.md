$A B C$ is an isosceles triangle; the angle at vertex $A$ is $120^{\circ}$; $A B=A C=a$. Raise two perpendiculars to the plane of the triangle at points $B$ and $C$ and take two points, $P$ and $Q$, on these such that the triangle $P A Q$ is right-angled at $A$ and that the volume of the pyramid $A B C P Q$ is a given value $\left(m^{3}\right)$.

Riding in windy weather. A cyclist takes 3 minutes to travel 1 km if the wind is at his back, and 4 minutes if he has to ride against a headwind. How long will it take him to travel 1 km if the wind dies down? Someone might say that since the arithmetic mean of 3 and 4 is $3 \frac{1}{2}$, the cyclist will need $3 \frac{1}{2}$ minutes, but this solution is incorrect.

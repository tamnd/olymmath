Adam has a triangular field $A B C$ with $A B=5, B C=8$ and $C A=11$. He intends to separate the field into two parts by building a straight fence from $A$ to a point $D$ on side $B C$ such that $A D$ bisects $\angle B A C$. Find the area of the part of the field $A B D$.
(A) $\frac{4 \sqrt{21}}{11}$;
(B) $\frac{4 \sqrt{21}}{5}$;
(C) $\frac{5 \sqrt{21}}{11}$;
(D) $\frac{5 \sqrt{21}}{4}$;
(E) None of the above.

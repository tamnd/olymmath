Initially, natural numbers are placed at integer points on the number line. On the first step, the arithmetic mean of each pair of adjacent numbers is written between them, and the original numbers are erased. On the second step, the same operation is performed with the written numbers, and so on. It turns out that all numbers we obtain at each step are natural. Can we assert that at some step all numbers will be equal to each other?

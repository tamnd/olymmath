Let $M, N$ and $P$ be the midpoints of sides $B C, A C$ and $A B$ respectively, and $O$ be the circumcenter of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$. The circumcircles of triangles $B O C$ and $M N P$ intersect at distinct points $X$ and $Y$ inside the triangle $A B C$. Prove that

$$
\measuredangle B A X=\measuredangle C A Y
$$

Novi Sad, 06.04.2013.

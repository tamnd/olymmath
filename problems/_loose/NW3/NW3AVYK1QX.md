Construct a non-crossing quadrilateral $ABCD$ that satisfies the following conditions (I) to (V)!

(I) The side $AB$ has a length of $a=7.0 \mathrm{~cm}$.

(II) $C$ lies on the perpendicular bisector $p$ of the segment $AB$.

(III) $D$ lies on the perpendicular bisector $q$ of the segment $AC$.

(IV) $A$ lies on the perpendicular bisector $r$ of the segment $BD$.

(V) The lines $p$ and $q$ intersect at a point $S$ that lies on the segment $AB$.

Describe your construction! Prove that any quadrilateral that has the required properties can be constructed according to your description! Prove that any quadrilateral constructed according to your description has the required properties!

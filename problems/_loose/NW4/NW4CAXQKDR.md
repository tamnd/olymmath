A student council must select a two-person welcoming committee and a three-person planning committee from among its members. There are exactly $10$ ways to select a two-person team for the welcoming committee. It is possible for students to serve on both committees. In how many different ways can a three-person planning committee be selected? $ \textbf{(A)}\ 10 \textbf{(B)}\ 12 \textbf{(C)}\ 15 \textbf{(D)}\ 18 \textbf{(E)}\ 25$

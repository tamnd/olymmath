For real numbers $x$ and $y$ , define $$\nabla(x,y)=x-\dfrac1y.$$ If $$\underbrace{\nabla(2, \nabla(2, \nabla(2, \ldots \nabla(2,\nabla(2, 2)) \ldots)))}_{2016 \,\nabla\text{s}} = \dfrac{m}{n}$$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ , $n$ , compute $100m + n$ .

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k}$ be $k$ distinct elements, and let $R_{m}\left(n_{1}, n_{2}, \cdots, n_{k}\right)$ represent the number of ways to place $n_{1}$ $a_{1}$'s, $n_{2}$ $a_{2}$'s, $\cdots$, $n_{k}$ $a_{k}$'s into $m$ distinct boxes such that no box is empty. Find the counting formula for $R_{m}\left(n_{1}, n_{2}, \cdots, n_{k}\right)$.

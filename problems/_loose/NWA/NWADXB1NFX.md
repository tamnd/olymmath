Let the sequence of positive numbers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 5)$ satisfy: $\frac{1}{a_{1} a_{2} a_{3}}+\frac{1}{a_{2} a_{3} a_{4}}+\cdots+$ $\frac{1}{a_{k-2} a_{k-1} a_{k}}=\frac{(k+1) a_{k-2}}{4 a_{k-1} a_{k}}(k=3,4, \cdots, n)$, and $a_{4}=4, a_{5}=5$, prove: the general term of the sequence is $a_{n}=n$.

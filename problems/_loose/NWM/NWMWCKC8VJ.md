Giovanni has drunk too much and starts to walk in a strange way:

- he takes 1 step forward;
- then he turns $90^{\circ}$ to the right and takes 2 steps forward;
- then he turns $90^{\circ}$ to the right and takes 1 step forward;
- then he turns $90^{\circ}$ to the left and takes 1 step backward; - after that, he starts over.

Each step is 1 meter. After 186 steps, he falls to the ground unconscious. How many meters from where he started does Giovanni end his walk?
(A) $0 $ (B) 1
(C) 2
(D) $\sqrt{5}$
(E) 4 .

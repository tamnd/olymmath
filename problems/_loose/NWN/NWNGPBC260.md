Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ have the sum of the first $n$ terms as $S_{n}$, and $S_{n}=2 n-a_{n}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) If the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $b_{n}=2^{n-1} a_{n}$, prove that: $\frac{1}{b_{1}}+\frac{1}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{b_{n}}<\frac{5}{3}$. (Yang Zhiming provided the problem)

Given a positive integer $n$ and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}>1$.
Prove: the set
$X=\left\{p|p|\left(a_{1}^{k}+a_{2}^{k}+\cdots+a_{n}^{k}\right), p\right.$ is a prime, $\left.k \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right\}$

is an infinite set.

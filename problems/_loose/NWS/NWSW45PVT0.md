Given $N \geqslant 3$ points in a plane such that no three lie on the same line. Each line segment connecting two of the given points is colored either red or blue. Prove that there exist $N-1$ segments of the same color that do not divide the plane into more than one part and that no two of them intersect except at the vertices.

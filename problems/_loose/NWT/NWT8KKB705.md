The altitude $C D$ of the rectangle triangle $A B C\left(\angle A C B=90^{\circ}\right)$ is a diameter of the circle $k$, which meets the sides $A C$ and $B C$ in $E$ and $F$, respectively. The intersection point of the line $B E$ and the circle $k$, which is different from $E$, is denoted by $M$. Let the intersection point of the lines $A C$ and $M F$ be $K$, and the intersection point of the lines $E F$ and $B K$ be $P$.

a) Prove that the points $B, F, M$ and $P$ are concyclic;

b) If the points $D, M$ and $P$ are colinear, find the angles $A$ and $B$ of the triangle $A B C$.

- one of the products ("A") is better than the others in certain parameters, but its price is clearly overpriced (which makes the probability of its purchase quite low);
- another product ("B"), slightly inferior to the first in consumer characteristics, has a significantly lower price (although it exceeds the average market price)

Sometimes a product "C", which is in the "economy class" in terms of price and quality, is also placed on the shelf.

What is the purpose of using such a technique? (10 points)

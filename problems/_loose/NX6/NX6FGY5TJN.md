\section*{Problem $5-330945=331045$}

When using a Cartesian coordinate system, a point in the plane is called "rational" if both of its coordinates are rational numbers; it is called "irrational" if both of its coordinates are irrational numbers; it is called "mixed" if one of its coordinates is rational and the other is irrational.

a) Are there lines in the plane that contain only points of one type?

Determine the answer to this question for each of the three types "rational", "irrational", "mixed"!

b) Are there lines in the plane that contain points from exactly two types (at least one point from each type)?

Determine the answer to this question for each combination of two of the three types!

c) Are there lines in the plane that contain points from each of the three types (at least one point from each type)?

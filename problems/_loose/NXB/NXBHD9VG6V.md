In a circle $\omega$ with center at point $O$, non-intersecting chords $AB$ and $CD$ are drawn such that $\angle AOB = \angle COD = 120^{\circ}$. The tangent to $\omega$ at point $A$ intersects the ray $CD$ at point $X$, and the tangent to $\omega$ at point $B$ intersects the ray $DC$ at point $Y$. The line $\ell$ passes through the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $DOX$ and $COY$. Prove that $\ell$ is tangent to $\omega$.

(A. Kuznetsov)

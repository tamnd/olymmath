Baron Munchausen told a story that he had a map of the country of Oz with five cities. Every two cities are connected by a road that does not pass through other cities. Each road on the map intersects with no more than one other road (and no more than once). The roads are marked in yellow or red (according to the color of the bricks they are paved with), and when walking around each city (along the perimeter), the colors of the roads leading out of it alternate. Could the words of the baron be true?

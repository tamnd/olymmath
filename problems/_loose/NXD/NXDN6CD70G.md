The triangle $ABC$ is given. Consider the point $C'{}$ on the side $AB$ such that the segment $CC'$ divides the triangle into two triangles with equal radii of inscribed circles. Denote by $t_c$ the length of the segment $CC'$ . Similarly, we define $t_a$ and $t_b$ . Express the area of triangle $ABC$ in terms of $t_a,t_b$ and $t_c$ .

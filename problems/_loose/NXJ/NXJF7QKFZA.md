Given a circle with diameter $AB$ and a point $X$ on the circle, let $t_{A}, t_{B}$, and $t_{X}$ be the tangents to the circle at $A, B, X$ respectively. Let $Z$ be the intersection of line $AX$ with $t_{B}$, and $Y$ be the intersection of line $BX$ with $t_{A}$. Prove that the lines $YZ, t_{X}, AB$ are concurrent.
(6th Canadian Competition Problem)

Let $\left( A,+, \cdot \right)$ be a ring that verifies the following properties:
(i) it has a unit, $1$, and its order is $p$, a prime number;
(ii) there is $B \subset A, |B| = p$, such that: for all $x,y \in A$, there is $b \in B$ such that $xy = byx$.

Prove that $A$ is commutative.

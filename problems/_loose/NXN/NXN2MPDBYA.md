Prove using complex numbers that the composition of two homotheties is a homothety or a translation: $H_{A_{2}}^{k_{2}} \circ H_{A_{1}}^{k_{1}}=\left\{\begin{array}{l}T_{a}, k_{1} k_{2}=1, \\ H_{A}^{k}, k_{1} k_{2}=1,\end{array}\right.$ where in the first case the vector $a$ is parallel to the line $A_{1} A_{2}$, and in the second case the center of the resulting homothety $A$ lies on the line $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $k=k_{1} k_{2}$. Here $H_{A}^{k}$ denotes a homothety with center at $A$ and coefficient $k$.

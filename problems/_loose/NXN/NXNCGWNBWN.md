Today, there are $2n$ men and $2n$ women, forming two concentric circles for an invitation dance, with $2n$ people in each circle, including both men and women. The dance rules are as follows: whenever the music starts, if a man and a woman face each other, the man invites the woman to dance; if both are men or both are women, they clap to encourage the others. When the music ends, the people in the outer circle move one step forward, and this continues. Try to prove: during the entire dance process, there is at least one time when the number of dancing couples is no less than $n$ pairs.

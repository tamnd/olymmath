Four friends participate in a mathematics contest, where they have to solve 4 problems. For a problem solved correctly and completely, 7 points are awarded. For a problem solved almost correctly, 6 or 4 points are awarded, depending on how close the student was to the correct solution. For obtaining only a correct result, without further elaboration, 2 points are awarded, and for an incorrect solution or irrelevant considerations, no points are awarded.

a) If Alin scored exactly 23 points, show that he solved at least one problem correctly.

b) If Bogdan and Daniel together scored 48 points, and Cristi and Emil together also scored 48 points, each having a different score, show that at least one of them solved at least 3 problems correctly.

In the center of a circular flower bed, there is an irrigation device, and its irrigation area is a sector with a central angle of $\frac{2 \pi}{11}$. The irrigation device rotates uniformly around the center of the flower bed. There are 100 rose bushes planted in the flower bed, and no two rose bushes lie on the same radius. Prove that there must be a moment when exactly 10 rose bushes are being watered simultaneously, and determine whether it can be asserted that there must be a moment when exactly 11 rose bushes are being watered simultaneously.

For the circumscribed quadrilateral $ABCD$ of $\odot I$, extend $BA$ and $CD$ to intersect at $E$, and extend $AD$ and $BC$ to intersect at $F$. Let $\angle BEC = \alpha$ and $\angle AFB = \beta$. Prove that: $\frac{AD \cdot BC}{AB \cdot CD} = \frac{\cos^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos^2 \frac{\beta}{2}}$.

a) Prove that for any $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ and $t \in [0,1]$, the inequality $|t x + (1-t) y| + |(1-t) x + t y| \leq |x| + |y|$ holds;

b) Let $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $a \leq b \leq c \leq d$ and $b - a = d - c$. Then $|b| + |c| \leq |a| + |d|$.

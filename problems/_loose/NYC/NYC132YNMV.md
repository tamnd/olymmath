In quadrilateral $A B C D$, $A B=A D, C B=C D$. Through the intersection point $O$ of $A C$ and $B D$, draw any two lines, one intersecting $A D$ at $E$ and $B C$ at $F$; the other intersecting $A B$ at $G$ and $C D$ at $H$. $G F$ and $E H$ intersect $B D$ at $I$ and $J$ respectively. Prove: $O I=O J$. (Butterfly theorem in a quadrilateral).

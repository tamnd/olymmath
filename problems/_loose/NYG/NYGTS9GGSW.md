$$ (\cos x+i\sin x)^n=\cos n x +i\sin nx, \forall x\in\left( 0,\frac{\pi }{2} \right) , \forall n\in\mathbb{N} $$
by supposing it is true for two consecutive $ n'\text{s} . $

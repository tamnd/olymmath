A grasshopper is sitting on the number line. Initially, it is sitting at the number 0 . Each second it jumps one unit to the left or to the right, with equal probability. The directions of the jumps are chosen independently of each other.

Let $p$ denote the probability that, after 2022 jumps, the grasshopper is sitting at a number divisible by 5 . Determine whether $p\frac{1}{5}$.

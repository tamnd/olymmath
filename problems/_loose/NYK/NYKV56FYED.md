Let $M=\left\{A(x) \left\lvert A(x)=\left(\begin{array}{ccc}1-x & 0 & x \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ x & 0 & 1-x\end{array}\right)\right., x \in \mathbf{R} \backslash\left\{\frac{1}{2}\right\}\right\}$.

a) Show that $(M, \cdot)$ is an abelian group.

b) Calculate the inverse of the element $A(2016)$.

c) Calculate $A^{n}(x), n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$.

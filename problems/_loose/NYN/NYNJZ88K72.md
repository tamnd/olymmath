Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in a circle $\Gamma, P$ a variable point on the arc $AB$ that does not contain $C$. Let $I$ and $J$ be the centers of the inscribed circles of triangles $ACP$ and $BCP$ respectively. Consider $Q$ the intersection point of $\Gamma$ and the circumcircle of triangle $PIJ$.

Show that $Q$ remains fixed as $P$ varies.

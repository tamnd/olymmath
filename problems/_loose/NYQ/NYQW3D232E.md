In an acute triangle $ABC$, $D$ and $E$ are interior points on side $BC$, with $D$ lying between $B$ and $E$, $|AD|=|CD|$, and $|AE|=|BE|$. Assume that the angle bisector of $\angle DAE$ intersects the perpendicular bisector of segment $BC$ at a single point, denoted by $F$. Prove that $|\angle BAC| + |\angle DFE| = 180^{\circ}$.

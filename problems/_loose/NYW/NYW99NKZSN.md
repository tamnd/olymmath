In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, let $AB=a$, $BC=b$, $CD=c$, $DA=d$, $AC=e$, $BD=f$. Find all non-negative real solutions to the equation
$$
a \sqrt[3]{x+c^{3}}+b \sqrt[3]{x+d^{3}}=e \sqrt[3]{x+f^{3}}
$$

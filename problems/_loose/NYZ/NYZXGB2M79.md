The plane is divided into regular triangles with side length 1. Consider the set $M$ of all possible distances between the vertices of these triangles, i.e., $x \in M$ if and only if there exist two vertices at a distance $x$ from each other. Prove that if $x \in M$ and $y \in M$, then $x \cdot y \in M$.

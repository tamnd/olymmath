For real numbers $x, y$ if

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+x y+y^{2} & =4 \\
x^{4}+x^{2} y^{2}+y^{4} & =8
\end{aligned}
$$

prove that

$$
x^{6}+x^{3} y^{3}+y^{6}
$$

is a natural number and determine it.

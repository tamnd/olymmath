(Olympiades 1995). Let $A B C D E F$ be a convex hexagon such that:

$$
A B=B C=C D, D E=E F=F A, \widehat{B C D}=\widehat{E F A}=60^{\circ}
$$

Let $G$ and $H$ be two points inside the hexagon such that $\widehat{A G B}=\widehat{D H E}=120^{\circ}$. Show the inequality:

$$
A G+G B+G H+D H+H E \geqslant C F
$$

Let $I\subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be an open interval and $f:I\to\mathbb{R}$ a strictly monotonous function. Prove that for all $c\in I$ there exist $a,b\in I$ such that $c\in (a,b)$ and $$\int_a^bf(x) \ dx=f(c)\cdot (b-a).$$

99 points are marked at equal intervals on a circle. Betya and Vasya take turns coloring these points, with Betya starting first. Betya can arbitrarily color one point red or blue, and subsequently, each player must color an uncolored point adjacent to an already colored point red or blue. If, after all points are colored, a monochromatic equilateral triangle can be found with its three vertices being the same color, Vasya wins. The question is: Can Betya interfere with Vasya's victory?

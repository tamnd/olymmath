Prove that the three lines passing through the vertices of a triangle and dividing its perimeter in half intersect at one point $N$ (the Nagel point). Let $M$ be the centroid of the triangle, $I$ the incenter, and $S$ the center of the circle inscribed in the triangle with vertices at the midpoints of the sides of the given triangle. Prove that the points $N, M, I$, and $S$ lie on one line, and that $|M N|=2|I M|,|I S|=|S N|$.

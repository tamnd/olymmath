Let the sets be: $ \mathrm{A}=\left\{p^{2} \mid p \in \mathbb{N}\right\}, B=\{5 n+2 \mid n \in \mathbb{N}\}, C=\{7 m+3 \mid m \in \mathbb{N}\}$, $D=\left\{9^{k} \mid k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, k \leq 2012\right\}$. Show that:
a) $A \cap B=\emptyset$
b) $2012 \in B \cap C$
c) $D \subset A$
d) $9^{2011}$ can be written as the sum of two perfect cubes, and $9^{2012}$ as the sum of three perfect squares.

(Dorina Bocu, Mathematics Olympiads and Competitions V-VIII 2014, Bîrchi Publishing)

In a class of 35 students, the learners can be divided into two groups: the blockheads and the sky-scrapers. The sky-scrapers claim that they are taller than the blockheads, who, in turn, consider themselves better at math. One day, one of them asked a sky-scraper: "What do you mean by saying that you are taller than us? Do you mean that

1. Every sky-scraper is taller than every blockhead?
2. There are more short blockheads than the shortest sky-scraper, compared to the number of short sky-scrapers than the tallest blockhead?
3. There are more sky-scrapers who have shorter blockheads than there are blockheads who have shorter sky-scrapers?
4. The average height of blockheads is lower than the average height of sky-scrapers?"

Upon hearing the questions, the sky-scraper visibly shrank... However, the task is to determine the relationship between the above statements, that is, for any two statements, decide whether one follows from the other.

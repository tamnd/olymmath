Let $A B C$ be a triangle that is not isosceles at $A$, and let $\Gamma$ be its circumcircle. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the arc of $\Gamma$ between $B$ and $C$ that contains $A$. Let $\mathrm{I}$ be the center of the incircle of $A B C$, $\mathrm{J}$ be the center of the $A$-excircle of $A B C$, and $K$ be the point of intersection of $(BC)$ with the external bisector of $\angle B A C$.

Show that $(\mathrm{JN}) \perp(\mathrm{IK})$.

The number 144 is very special in that it enjoys the mirror property: not only is it true that $144=12^{2}$ (i.e., it is a perfect square), but it is also true that $441=21^{2}$, meaning that reversing the order of its digits results in precisely the square of the number obtained by reversing the digits of its square root. The number 100 also has the mirror property, as does the number 1, while the number 49 does not. How many positive integers with at most 3 digits enjoy the mirror property?

Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle. Suppose that $(AB)$ and $(CD)$ intersect at $E$ and that $(AD)$ and $(BC)$ intersect at $F$. The bisector of $\widehat{AFB}$ intersects the segments $[AB]$ and $[CD]$ at points $P$ and $R$, and that of $\widehat{BEC}$ intersects the segments $[BC]$ and $[AD]$ at points $Q$ and $S$.

1) Show that $(PR)$ and $(SQ)$ are perpendicular.
2) Show that $PQRS$ is a rhombus.

Let's perform the following operation on a natural number: add up its digits, then add up the digits of the resulting number, and so on until the result is a single-digit number. (For example, the digit sum of 1987 is 25, and the digit sum of 25 is 7). Prove that the sequence of single-digit numbers obtained from consecutive powers of 2 in this way is periodic.

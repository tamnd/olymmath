Prove that the solutions of the indeterminate equation $x^{2}+(x+1)^{2}=y^{2}$ are
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x=\frac{1}{4}\left[(1+\sqrt{2})^{2 n+1}+(1-\sqrt{2})^{2 n+1}-2\right], \\
y=\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{2}}\left[(1+\sqrt{2})^{2 n+1}-(1-\sqrt{2})^{2 n+1}\right],
\end{array}
$$

and there are no other solutions.

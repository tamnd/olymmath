For each of the following statements I-XII, we need to decide whether they are true or false.

I. If the sides of a circumscribed polygon are equal, then its angles are also equal.

II. If the sides of a polygon inscribed in a circle (and containing the circle within it) are equal, then its angles are also equal.

The further statements have a similar structure to I and II. To describe them, we introduce the following abbreviations. Each letter represents a condition or consequence.
"the sides of a circumscribed $n$-gon are equal"
is abbreviated as: $A_{n}$
"the angles of a circumscribed $n$-gon are equal"
is abbreviated as: $B_{n}$
"the sides of an inscribed $n$-gon are equal"
is abbreviated as: $C_{n}$;
"the angles of an inscribed $n$-gon are equal"
is abbreviated as: $D_{n}$
"the angles of the polygon are also equal"
is abbreviated as: $P$
"the sides of the polygon are also equal"
is abbreviated as: $Q$.

(With these, the abbreviation for I is: If $A_{n}$, then $P$; for II: If $C_{n}$, then $P$.) The further statements to be decided are: III. If $B_{n}$, then $Q$. -IV. If $D_{n}$, then $Q$. -V-VIII. If $A_{5}$, or $A_{6}$ or $C_{5}$ or $C_{6}$, then $P$. -IX-XII. If $B_{5}$, or $B_{6}$, or $D_{5}$, or $D_{6}$, then $Q$.

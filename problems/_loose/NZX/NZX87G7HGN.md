Consider a rotation about an axis $l$ by an angle equal to $\frac{2 \pi}{n}$, where $n$ is a natural number, $n \geqslant 3$. From a point $S$ on the axis $l$, draw a ray forming an angle $\varphi < 90^{\circ}$ with $l$. Then $n$ rays, obtained from this ray by successive applications of the considered rotation, can be taken as the edges of an $n$-sided angle. Prove that: a) all plane angles of this $n$-sided angle are congruent to each other; b) all dihedral angles of this $n$-sided angle are also congruent to each other.

A polyhedral angle possessing the properties a) and b) is called a regular polyhedral angle.

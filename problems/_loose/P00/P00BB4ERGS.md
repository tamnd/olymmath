For any $k\in\mathbb{Z}^+$ , prove that:-

$2(\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt{k})<\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}<2(\sqrt{k}-\sqrt{k-1})$

Also compute integral part of $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}+...+\frac{1}{\sqrt{10000}}$.

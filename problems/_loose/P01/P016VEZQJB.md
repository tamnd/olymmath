In a group of n students, some of them are friends. We know that each of them has at least four friends among the others. The teacher wants to divide the students into two groups, each with a maximum of four members, so that each student has at least one friend in their group.

a) Show that in the case of $n=7$, the students can be divided in the required way.

b) Determine whether the students can be divided in this way in the case of $n=8$.

A square board is divided into $4 \times 4$ squares. Initially, all the squares are white. We want to color as many squares blue as possible, in such a way that each blue square has exactly one white neighbor (two squares are neighbors if they share a side). What is the maximum number of squares we can color blue?
A) 6
B) 8
C) 10
D) 12
E) 14

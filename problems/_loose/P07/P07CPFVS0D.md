G3 (1-6, Czechoslovakia) Two planes $P$ and $Q$ intersect at line $p$. It is known that point $A$ is in plane $P$ but not in plane $Q$, and point $C$ is in plane $Q$ but not in plane $P$, and neither is on $p$. Construct a quadrilateral $A B C D$ such that $A B / / C D, A D = B C$, and point $B$ is in plane $P$, point $D$ is in plane $Q$, and the quadrilateral has an inscribed circle.

An atoll has the shape of a circular crown delimited by two concentric circumferences with radii of $1 \mathrm{~km}$ and $6 \mathrm{~km}$, respectively. Giovanni and Marco are the only inhabitants of the atoll; after a storm that destroyed their huts, they decide to rebuild them as far apart as possible, but in such a way that there exists a straight path connecting them and lying entirely on the atoll. How far apart will Giovanni's and Marco's huts be? (Assume that the two circumferences delimiting the atoll are part of it)
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{35}}{2} \mathrm{~km}$,
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{37}}{2} \mathrm{~km}$,
(C) $\sqrt{37} \mathrm{~km}$,
(D) $2 \sqrt{35} \mathrm{~km}$,
(E) $2 \sqrt{37} \mathrm{~km}$.

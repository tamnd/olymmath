Around a circle, 2022 ones are written. Two players take turns: in one move, a player erases two adjacent numbers and writes their sum in place (once). The player who gets the number 4 wins. If at the end of the game only one number remains that is not 4, the game ends in a draw. Can any of the players ensure their victory, and if so, how?

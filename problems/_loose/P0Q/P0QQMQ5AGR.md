Consider the binomial equation $z^{4}+1=0$. Let $M_{k}\left(z_{k}\right), k \in\{1,2,3,4\}$ be the geometric images of the roots of the equation. Show that for any points $P_{q}\left(z_{q}\right), q \in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, taken inside the quadrilateral $M_{1} M_{2} M_{3} M_{4}$, there exist two points $P_{i}, P_{j}, i, j \in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, such that $\left|z_{i}-z_{j}\right| < 1$ (OLM 2015-local stage)

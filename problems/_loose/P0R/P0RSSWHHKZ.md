Show that there exists a real constant $C>1$ with the following property:
For any positive integer $n$ , there are at least $C^n$ positive integers with exactly $n$ decimal digits, which are divisible by the product of their digits. (In particular, these $n$ digits are all non-zero.)
*Proposed by Jean-Marie De Koninck and Florian Luca*

$$
8abc \le \left(\sqrt{bc} + \frac{1}{2a + \sqrt{bc}}\right) + \left(\sqrt{ca} + \frac{1}{2b + \sqrt{ca}}\right) + \left(\sqrt{ab} + \frac{1}{2c + \sqrt{ab}}\right).
$$
where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive real numbers such that $ab + bc + ca = 1$.

Let $\mathbb{N}$ denote the set of positive integers. For how many positive integers $k\le 2018$ do there exist a function $f: \mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{N}$ such that $f(f(n))=2n$ for all $n\in \mathbb{N}$ and $f(k)=2018$ ?

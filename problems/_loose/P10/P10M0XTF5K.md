Let $\Gamma$ be a circle. Let $\omega$ be a circle internally tangent to the circle $\Gamma$, and let $T$ be their point of tangency. Let $P$ be a point on $\omega$ different from $T$. Let $O$ be the center of $\omega$. We denote $M$ as the midpoint of the segment $[\mathrm{PT}]$. Let $A$ and $B$ be the points of intersection of the tangent to $\omega$ at point $P$ with the circle $\Gamma$. Show that $\mathrm{O}, \mathrm{M}, \mathrm{A}$, and $\mathrm{B}$ are concyclic.

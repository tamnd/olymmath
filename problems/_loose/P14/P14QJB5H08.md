Let $\varphi=\varphi(t)$ be a characteristic function such that for some $b>0$ the condition

$$
|\varphi(t)| \leqslant a \text { for } |t| \geqslant b
$$

holds, where $0<a<1$. Show that then for $|t|<b$ the Cramér inequality

$$
|\varphi(t)| \leqslant 1-\left(1-a^{2}\right) \frac{t^{2}}{8 b^{2}}
$$

is valid.

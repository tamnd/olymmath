(IMO 40 Preliminary) Let $M$ be a point inside a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ such that $MA = MC$,
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle AMB = \angle MAD + \angle MCD, \angle CMD = \angle MCB + \angle MAB. \text{ Prove: } AB \cdot CM = BC \cdot MD, \\
BM \cdot AD = MA \cdot CD.
\end{array}
$$

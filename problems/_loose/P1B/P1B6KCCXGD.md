Let the volume of a regular tetrahedron be $V_{T}$, and the volume of its circumsphere be $V_{K}$.

Calculate the ratio $V_{K}: V_{T}$ and round it to the nearest integer (i.e., determine the integer closest to $V_{K}: V_{T}$)!

You may use the approximate values $\sqrt{3} \approx$ 1.7 and $\pi \approx 3.14$ (accurate to one or two decimal places).

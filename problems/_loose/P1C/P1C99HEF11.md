Determine whether there exist real numbers $b, c, d$ such that the sequence defined by $a_{n}=\frac{n+b}{c n+d}(n=1,2,3, \ldots)$ satisfies $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}, a_{2}=\frac{3}{8}$, and $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n}=\frac{1}{2}$.

If such $b, c, d$ exist, determine whether they are uniquely determined by these conditions, and give them in this case.

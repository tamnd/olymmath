On the extreme horizontal rows of a $15 \times 15$ board, two rows of chips are placed: on the bottom horizontal row, there is a white chip in each cell, and on the top horizontal row, there is a black chip in each cell. Each move, the players slide one of their chips (the first player a white one, the second a black one) any number of cells respectively upwards or downwards, but they cannot move to a cell occupied by an opponent's chip or "jump over" it. The player who cannot make a move loses. Can either player guarantee a win? Justify your answer.

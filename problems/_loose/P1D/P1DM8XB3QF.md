We draw a circle on the main chord $AB$ of a parabola, which intersects the parabola at points $C$ and $D$. Prove:

$1^{0}$, that the chords $CD$ and $AB$ are parallel and their mutual distance is $2p$;

$2^{0}$, that the tangents drawn from the vertex of the parabola to the mentioned circle pass through the points $C$ and $D$.

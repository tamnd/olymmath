Let $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be $n$ positive real numbers, and the system of equations $x_{k-1}-2 x_{k}+x_{k+1}+b_{k} x_{k}=0, k=1,2, \cdots, n$, has a non-zero real solution $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$. Here $x_{0}=x_{n+1}=0$. Prove:
$b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n} \geqslant \frac{4}{n+1}$.
(2003 China National Team Training Problem)

Yang the Spinning Square Sheep is a square in the plane such that his four legs are his four vertices. Yang can do two different types of *tricks*:

(a) Yang can choose one of his sides, then reflect himself over the side.
(b) Yang can choose one of his legs, then rotate $90^\circ$ counterclockwise around the leg.

Yang notices that after $2016$ tricks, each leg ends up in exactly the same place the leg started out in! Let there be $N$ ways for Yang to perform his $2016$ tricks. What is the remainder when $N$ is divided by $100000$ ?

*Proposed by James Lin*

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $\angle A B C \neq \angle B C A$. The inscribed circle $k$ of the triangle $A B C$ touches the sides $B C, C A$ and $A B$ at the points $D, E$ and $F$ respectively. The line $A D$ intersects $k$ once more at $P$. Let $Q$ be the intersection of $E F$ with the line perpendicular to $A D$ through $P$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the intersection of $A Q$ with $D E$ and $D F$ respectively. Show that $A$ is the center of $X Y$.

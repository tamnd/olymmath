Let $k$ be a circle with diameter $A B$ and let $C \in k$ be an arbitrary point. The excircles of $\triangle A B C$ tangent to the sides $A C$ and $B C$ are tangent to the line $A B$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Denote by $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ the circumcenters of $\triangle A M C$ and $\triangle B N C$. Prove that the area of $\triangle O_{1} C O_{2}$ does not depend on $C$.

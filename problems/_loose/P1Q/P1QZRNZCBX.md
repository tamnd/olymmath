In a square $ABCD$ with side $2$, a segment $MN$ of length $1$ is constrained to have endpoint $M$ on side $AB$ and endpoint $N$ on side $BC$. This segment divides the square into a triangle $T$ and a pentagon $P$. What is the maximum value that the ratio of the area of $T$ to that of $P$ can take?

(A) $\frac{1}{4}$
(B) $\frac{2}{5}$
(C) $\frac{1}{7}$
(D) $\frac{2}{15}$
(E) $\frac{1}{15}$.

A grasshopper begins at the vertex $(1,0)$ of an infinite lattice with origin $O(0,0)$. It can jump from a point $A$ to any lattice point $B$ such that $\triangle O A B$ has area $1 / 2$.

(a) Find all points $(x, y)$ of the lattice which the grasshopper can reach.

(b) Prove that if the grasshopper can reach a point $(x, y)$, then it can reach it in at most $|y|+2$ jumps, starting from $(1,0)$.

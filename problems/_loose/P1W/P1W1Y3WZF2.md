$$
\begin{aligned}
& x=\frac{1}{2}\left(y+\frac{1}{y}\right) \\
& y=\frac{1}{2}\left(z+\frac{1}{z}\right) \\
& z=\frac{1}{2}\left(t+\frac{1}{t}\right) \\
& t=\frac{1}{2}\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

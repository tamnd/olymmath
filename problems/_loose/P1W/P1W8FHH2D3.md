We say that a positive integer is *quad-divi* if it is divisible by the sum of the squares of its digits, and also none of its digits is equal to zero.
a) Find a quad-divi number such that the sum of its digits is $24$.
b) Find a quad-divi number such that the sum of its digits is $1001$.

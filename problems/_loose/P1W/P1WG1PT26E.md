Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral such that $\widehat{D A B}=\widehat{B C D}=90$ and $\widehat{A B C}>\widehat{C D A}$. Let $Q$ and $R$ be points on segments $[B C]$ and $[C D]$ respectively, such that the line $Q R$ intersects lines $A B$ and $A D$ at points $P$ and $S$ respectively, with $P Q=R S$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $[B D]$ and $N$ the midpoint of $[Q R]$. Show that the points $M, N, A$ and $C$ are concyclic.

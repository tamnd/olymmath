Let $p$ be a prime and $1 \leqq k \leqq p-1$. Choose $k$ different numbers from $1,2, \ldots, p-1$ in every possible way, and multiply these $k$ numbers together. Prove that the sum of the $\binom{p-1}{k}$ products obtained this way is divisible by $p$.

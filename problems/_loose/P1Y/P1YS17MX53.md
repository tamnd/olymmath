Let $ABC$ be a triangle, let $\Gamma$ be its circumcircle, and let $S$ be the midpoint of the arc of the circle with endpoints $B$ and $C$ that does not contain $A$. Prove that the line $(AS)$ is the angle bisector of $\widehat{BAC}$.

Note: The point $S$ is called the South Pole of $ABC$ from $A$. The point $N$, defined as the midpoint of the arc of the circle with endpoints $B$ and $C$ that contains $A$, is called the North Pole of $ABC$ from $A$.

It is known that $A$ has the form $A=\left(\frac{n+\sqrt{n^{2}-4}}{2}\right)^{m}$ where $m, n \geqslant 2$ are natural numbers. Prove that $A$ can also be represented as $A=\frac{k+\sqrt{k^{2}-4}}{2}$, where $k$ is a natural number.

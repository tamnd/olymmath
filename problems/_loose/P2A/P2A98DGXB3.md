The extensions of the medians $A E$ and $B F$ of triangle $A B C$ intersect the circumcircle of the triangle at points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$. It is known that $A A_{1}=B B_{1}$. Prove that either triangle $A B C$ is isosceles, or its sides $a, b$, and $c$ are related by the equation $c^{4}=a^{4}-a^{2} b^{2}+b^{4}$.

The figure on the coordinate plane consists of points $(x, y)$, satisfying for any $t \in \mathbb{R}$ the two inequalities

$$
x^{2}+y^{2}<\pi^{2}+t^{2}, \cos y<2+\cos 2 x+\cos x(4 \sin t-1)-\cos 2 t
$$

Find the area of this figure.

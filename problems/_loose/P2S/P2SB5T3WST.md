TikTok-House represents a $3 \times 3$ square of nine rooms, each inhabited by a blogger. On Monday, the bloggers randomly swapped rooms, after which every two people who ended up in adjacent rooms recorded a collaborative TikTok. On Tuesday, the bloggers again swapped rooms and recorded TikToks according to the same rule as the previous day. The same thing happened on Wednesday. Prove that some two bloggers did not record a collaborative TikTok.

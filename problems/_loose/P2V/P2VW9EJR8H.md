The edges of a cube are numbered from $1$ to $12$ , then is calculated for each vertex the sum of the numbers of the edges going out from it.
a) Prove that these sums cannot all be the same.
b) Can eight equal sums result after one of the edge numbers is replaced by the number $13$ ?

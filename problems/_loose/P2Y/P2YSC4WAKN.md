On a plane, there are 3 consecutive points on a line: $A, C, B$. $AB = a$, $AC = 2x$, and the midpoint of $AC$ is denoted as $\mathrm{O}$. Draw a semicircle around $\mathrm{O}$ with radius $OA = OC = x$. The point of tangency from $B$ to the semicircle is denoted as $D$, and the intersection of this tangent and the perpendicular line from $A$ to $AB$ is denoted as $E$. The perpendicular projection of $D$ onto $AB$ is denoted as $H$.

a) Calculate the lengths of the segments $BO, BD, AE, BE, OH$, and $AH$ as functions of $a$ and $x$.

b) Rotate the entire diagram around the $AB$ axis. Calculate the surface areas $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ of the surfaces of revolution formed by the segment $BE$ and the arc $AD$, respectively.

c) Determine $x$ such that $\frac{F_{2}}{F_{1}} = m$, where $m$ is a given positive number. Discuss. What is $x$ if $m = \frac{1}{2}$?

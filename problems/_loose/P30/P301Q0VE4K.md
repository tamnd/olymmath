a) 100 weights of $1, 2, \ldots, 100$ grams were placed on two scales such that there is equilibrium.

Prove that it is possible to remove two weights from each scale without disrupting the equilibrium.

b) Consider such $n$ that the set of weights $1, 2, \ldots, n$ grams can be divided into two parts of equal weight.

Is it true that for any such $n$, greater than 3, it is possible to remove two weights from each part without disrupting the equality of the weights?

Let $m$ and $n$ be natural numbers and $p$ a prime number such that $m<n<p$ holds. Further apply:

$$
p \mid m^{2}+1 \text { and } p \mid n^{2}+1
$$

Show that applies:

$$
p \mid m n-1
$$

Let $c$ be a real number. For all $x$ and $y$ real numbers we have,
$$f(x-f(y))=f(x-y)+c(f(x)-f(y))$$
and $f(x)$ is not constant.
$a)$ Find all possible values of $c$.
$b)$ Can $f$ be periodic?

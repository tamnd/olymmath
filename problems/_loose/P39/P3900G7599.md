In acute triangle $ ABC$ , show that:

$ \sin^3{A}\cos^2{(B - C)} + \sin^3{B}\cos^2{(C - A)} + \sin^3{C}\cos^2{(A - B)} \leq 3\sin{A} \sin{B} \sin{C}$

and find out when the equality holds.

Let $T_{n}$ be the product of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, satisfying
$$
T_{n}=1-a_{n}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) \text {. }
$$
(1) Find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Let $S_{n}=T_{1}^{2}+T_{2}^{2}+\cdots+T_{n}^{2}$, prove:
$$
a_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2}<S_{n}<a_{n+1}-\frac{1}{3} \text {. }
$$

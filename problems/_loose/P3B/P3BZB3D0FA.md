A mathematician managed to chain a countably infinite number of Lo Jac' the pirate on a galley. He put a hat on each of their heads. Each hat is of a certain color among 10 different colors. The chain is such that the Lo Jac' are aligned but cannot turn their heads. Thus, the first Lo Jac' sees all the hats except his own, the second sees all except the first's and his own, etc. Each Lo Jac' in turn will whisper a color name into the mathematician's ear. If there is only a finite number of Lo Jac' who did not say the color of their own hat, they are all freed. Otherwise, it's perpetual galley. Can Lo Jac' get out of it?

a) Convex polygons $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} \ldots B_{n}$ are such that all their corresponding sides, except $A_{1} A_{n}$ and $B_{1} B_{n}$, are equal, and $\angle A_{2} \geqslant \angle B_{2}, \ldots, \angle A_{n-1} \geqslant \angle B_{n-1}$, with at least one of these inequalities being strict. Prove that $A_{1} A_{n} > B_{1} B_{n}$.

b) The corresponding sides of unequal polygons $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} \ldots B_{n}$ are equal. We write the sign of the difference $\angle A_{i} - \angle B_{i}$ next to each vertex of the polygon $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$. Prove that for $n \geqslant 4$, there will be at least four pairs of adjacent vertices with different signs. (Vertices with zero difference are excluded from consideration: two vertices, between which only vertices with zero difference stand, are considered adjacent.)

See also problems $4.37, 4.53, 13.42$.

A function $f: \mathbb{Z}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is called discretely harmonic if its value at a point is equal to the arithmetic mean of its neighbors along the vertical and horizontal directions. Suppose that the values of such a function $f$ are known at all non-zero points with even coordinates. Can the value of $f$ at the origin be recovered from this information?

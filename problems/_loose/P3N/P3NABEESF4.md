On the planet Zilot, the units of measurement are quite different from what we know on Earth. The standard unit of length is the Zimeter, and one of its submultiples is the Zimimimeter, which is equivalent to $10^{-7}$ Zimeters. A calculator can perform only two operations: multiplying a number by $10^{8}$ or dividing it by $10^{5}$. For example, using the operations of the calculator, we can perform the following conversions:

$$
3 \rightarrow 3 \cdot 10^{-5} \rightarrow 3 \cdot 10^{-10} \rightarrow 3 \cdot 10^{-2}
$$

a) Explain how we can combine the two operations of the calculator to make the number representing the conversion of $7 \cdot 10^{2}$ Zimeters to Zimimimeters appear on the screen.

b) How can we obtain the conversion of $10^{10} \cdot 10^{-4}$ Zimeters to Zimimimeters starting with the number 1000 on the calculator screen?

c) Using the calculator, is it possible to transform $10^{2017}$ to $10^{11}$ using the two mentioned keys?

Let $M$ be a set of natural numbers from $1$ to $2015$ which are not perfect squares.
a) Prove that for any $n\in M$ $\{\sqrt{n}\}\geq 0.011$
b) Prove that there exists number $n\in M$ such that $\{\sqrt{n}\}<0.0115$ Here $\{y\}$ means the fractional part of number $y$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $AB < AC$, $H$ its orthocenter, $\Gamma$ its circumcircle, $d$ the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $A$. Consider the circle centered at $B$ passing through $A$. It intersects $d$ at $D$ and $(AC)$ at $E$.

Show that $D$, $E$, and $H$ are collinear.

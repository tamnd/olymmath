Given two non-intersecting circles. A third circle touches both given circles externally and has its center on the line passing through the centers of the given circles. Prove that the third circle intersects the common internal tangents to the given circles at four points, forming a quadrilateral, two sides of which are parallel to the common external tangents to the given circles.

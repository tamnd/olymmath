Given four circles $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$, let $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ intersect at points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$, $S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$ intersect at points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, $S_{3}$ and $S_{4}$ intersect at points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$, $S_{4}$ and $S_{1}$ intersect at points $D_{1}$ and $D_{2}$. Prove: If points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}$ lie on the same circle, then $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}, D_{2}$ are also concyclic (or collinear).

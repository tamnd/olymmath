$\mathbf{N 4}$. Let $n$ be a positive integer. Show that the numbers
$$
\binom{2^{n}-1}{0}, \binom{2^{n}-1}{1}, \binom{2^{n}-1}{2}, \ldots, \binom{2^{n}-1}{2^{n-1}-1}
$$
are congruent modulo $2^{n}$ to $1,3,5, \ldots, 2^{n}-1$ in some order.

For $t \ge 2$ , define $S(t)$ as the number of times $t$ divides into $t!$ . We say that a positive integer $t$ is a *peak* if $S(t) > S(u)$ for all values of $u < t$ .
Prove or disprove the following statement:
For every prime $p$ , there is an integer $k$ for which $p$ divides $k$ and $k$ is a peak.

In the plane, given a set $S$ of 27 distinct points, where no three points are collinear. $S$ contains 4 points that are the vertices of a unit square, and the remaining 23 points are inside this square. Prove: There exist three points $X, Y, Z$ in $S$ such that $[X Y Z] \leqslant \frac{1}{48}$, where $[X Y Z]$ denotes the area of $\triangle X Y Z$.

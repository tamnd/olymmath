Given a line $p$ and points $A$ and $B$ which do not lie on $p$ and are on the same side of $p$. Let point $C$ be the symmetric point of point $A$ with respect to line $p$. The lines $p$ and $B C$ intersect at point $D$. Let $E$ be any point on $p$ different from $D$. Prove that the perimeter of triangle $A B D$ is less than the perimeter of triangle $A B E$.

Suppose there are $n$ cards, and on each side of every card, there is a natural number chosen from $1,2, \cdots, n$, and each number appears exactly twice on these $n$ cards. Try to prove that these $n$ cards can be placed on the table so that the numbers $1,2, \cdots, n$ all appear on the top side.

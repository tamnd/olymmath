The $26$ students in fifth grade have a fixed seat in the classroom with $26$ chairs. Adam enters first, but he's having a messy day and sits down at a random chair. The other students enter one by one. If their own seat is still free, they sit there. If their own seat is occupied, they sit down at a random unoccupied chair, just like Adam. Zoe is the last student to enter. What is the probability that she can sit at her own seat?

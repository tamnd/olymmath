Let $\mathcal A = A_0A_1A_2A_3 \cdots A_{2013}A_{2014}$ be a *regular 2014-simplex*, meaning the $2015$ vertices of $\mathcal A$ lie in $2014$ -dimensional Euclidean space and there exists a constant $c > 0$ such that $A_iA_j = c$ for any $0 \le i < j \le 2014$ . Let $O = (0,0,0,\dots,0)$ , $A_0 = (1,0,0,\dots,0)$ , and suppose $A_iO$ has length $1$ for $i=0,1,\dots,2014$ . Set $P=(20,14,20,14,\dots,20,14)$ . Find the remainder when $$PA_0^2 + PA_1^2 + \dots + PA_{2014}^2 $$ is divided by $10^6$ .

Suppose there are $n$ ordered pairs of positive integers $(a_i,b_i)$ such that $a_i+b_i=2020$ and $a_ib_i$ is a multiple of $2020$, where $1\le i \le n$. Compute the sum $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i+b_i.$$

Given that the ex-circle $\odot A^{\prime}$ of $\triangle A B C$ opposite vertex $A$ is tangent to $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively, the circumcircle $\Gamma$ of $\triangle A E F$ intersects line $B C$ at points $P$ and $Q$, and the midpoint of segment $A D$ is $M$. Prove: The circumcircle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ of $\triangle M P Q$ is tangent to $\odot A^{\prime}$.

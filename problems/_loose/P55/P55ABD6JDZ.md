An equilateral triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle $\Omega$ and circumscribed around a circle $\omega$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen on sides $AC$ and $AB$ respectively such that the segment $PQ$ passes through the center of triangle $ABC$. Circles $\Gamma_{b}$ and $\Gamma_{c}$ are constructed on segments $BP$ and $CQ$ as diameters. Prove that circles $\Gamma_{b}$ and $\Gamma_{c}$ intersect at two points, one of which lies on $\Omega$, and the other on $\omega$.

(A. Akopyan)

In the plane, two distinct points $O$ and $P$ are given. Let $ABCD$ be a parallelogram whose diagonals intersect at point $O$, such that point $P$ does not lie on the reflection of line $AB$ over line $CD$. The midpoints of segments $AP$ and $BP$ are denoted by $M$ and $N$, respectively, and the intersection of lines $MC$ and $ND$ is denoted by $Q$. Prove that points $P$, $Q$, and $O$ are collinear and that the position of point $Q$ is independent of the choice of parallelogram $ABCD$.

Solve the problem independently. You have 150 minutes for solving. The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

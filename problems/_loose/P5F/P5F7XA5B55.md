In each cell of a $100 \times 100$ square, a certain natural number is written. A rectangle, whose sides lie along the grid lines, is called good if the sum of the numbers in all its cells is divisible by 17. It is allowed to simultaneously color all the cells in some good rectangle. It is forbidden to color a cell more than once. What is the largest $d$ such that at least $d$ cells can be colored for any arrangement of the numbers?

(P. Zusmanovich, F. Petrov)

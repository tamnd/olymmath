Let the area of convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ be $S$, $AB=a$, $BC=b$, $CD=c$, $DA=d$. Prove: For any permutation $(x, y, z, w)$ of $a, b, c, d$, we have $S \leqslant \frac{1}{2}(xy + zw)$.

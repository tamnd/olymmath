Let $f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+\ldots+a_{n} x^{n}$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients, and let $p$ be a prime number such that the coefficient $a_{n}$ is not divisible by $p$, the coefficients $a_{0}, \ldots, a_{n-1}$ are divisible by $p$, but the coefficient $a_{0}$ is not divisible by $p^{2}$. Prove that then $f$ is an irreducible polynomial (Eisenstein's criterion).

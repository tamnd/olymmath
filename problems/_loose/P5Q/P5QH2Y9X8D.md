Given a cube with six different positive integers written on its six faces, the sum of these six positive integers is 60. Now perform the following operation on this cube: each time, select a vertex of the cube, and add 1 to the numbers on the three faces that include this vertex. After multiple operations, all the numbers on the faces of the cube become the same. How many different cubes satisfy this condition? (Once the six numbers are chosen, it counts as one type, regardless of how these six numbers are placed on the cube.)

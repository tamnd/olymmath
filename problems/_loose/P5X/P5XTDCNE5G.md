Given a line $g$ and a point $A$ on it.

Construct four more points $B, C, D, E$ on this line $g$ such that they lie in this order on the same ray starting from $A$ and the following conditions are met:

(1) The segment $A B$ is $2.5 \mathrm{~cm}$ long.

(2) The segment $B C$ is $0.3 \mathrm{dm}$ longer than the segment $A B$.

(3) The segment $C E$ is as long as the sum of the segments $A B$ and $B C$.

(4) The segment $D E$ is $50 \mathrm{~mm}$ shorter than the segment $C E$.

Describe the construction, and determine the length of the segment $A D$ (in $\mathrm{cm}$)!

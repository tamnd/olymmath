Given that $A$, $B$, and $C$ are any three non-collinear points on a plane, and point $O$ is inside $\triangle ABC$, satisfying
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\angle A O B=\angle B O C=\angle C O A=120^{\circ} . \\
\text { Find the maximum value of } \frac{O A+O B+O C}{A B+B C+C A} \text { . }
\end{array}
$$

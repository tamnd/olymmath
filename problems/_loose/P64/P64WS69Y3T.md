Four, (50 points) Let $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$ be $n$ distinct prime numbers. Use these primes as terms (repetition allowed) to form any sequence such that no product of some adjacent terms is a perfect square. Prove: The number of terms in such a sequence has a maximum value (denoted as $L(n)$), and find the expression for $L(n)$.

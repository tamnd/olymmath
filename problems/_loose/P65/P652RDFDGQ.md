Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. The diagonal $A C$ is equal to $a$ and forms angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively with the sides $A B$ and $A D$. Prove that the area of the quadrilateral is between $\frac{a^{2} \sin (\alpha+\beta) \sin \beta}{2 \sin \alpha}$ and $\frac{a^{2} \sin (\alpha+\beta) \sin \alpha}{2 \sin \beta}$.

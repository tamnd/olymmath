A certain meeting has 30 participants, each of whom knows at most five others; among any five people, at least two are not acquaintances. Find the largest positive integer $k$, such that in a group of 30 people satisfying the above conditions, there always exists a subgroup of $k$ people, none of whom are acquaintances.

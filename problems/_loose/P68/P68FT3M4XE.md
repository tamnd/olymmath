$\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{a_{k}}{1-a_{k}} \geqslant \frac{n S}{n-S}$.

The sum from k=1 to n of $\frac{a_{k}}{1-a_{k}}$ is greater than or equal to $\frac{n S}{n-S}$.

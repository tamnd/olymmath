Prove that if
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a=b \cos C+c \cos B, \\
b=c \cos A+a \cos C, \\
c=a \cos B+b \cos A,
\end{array}
$$

and $a, b, c$ are not all 0, then
$$
\cos ^{2} A+\cos ^{2} B+\cos ^{2} C+2 \cos A \cos B \cos C=1 \text {. }
$$

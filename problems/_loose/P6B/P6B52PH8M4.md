Given a right triangular prism $A B C A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. A sphere with diameter $A C$ intersects the edges $A B$ and $B C$ at points $F$ and $N$, respectively, different from the vertices of the prism. Segments $C_{1} F$ and $A_{1} N$ intersect at point $P$, and at the same time $A_{1} N=7, C_{1} P=6$.

a) Find the angle $P F A$.

b) Find the ratio $A F: F B$.

c) Suppose it is additionally known that $A B=6$. Find the volume of the prism.

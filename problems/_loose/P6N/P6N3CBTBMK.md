In the regular pyramid $S M N P Q$ ( $S$ - vertex), points $H$ and $F$ are the midpoints of edges $M N$ and $N P$ respectively, and point $E$ lies on segment $S H$, such that $S H=3, S E=\frac{9}{1}$. The distance from point $S$ to line $E F$ is $\sqrt{5}$. Find the volume of the pyramid. A sphere of radius 1 is given with its center at point $S$. Consider all possible regular tetrahedra $A B C D$ such that points $C$ and $D$ lie on line $E F$, and line $A B$ is tangent to the sphere at one of the points on segment $A B$. Find the minimum value of the edge length of the considered tetrahedra.

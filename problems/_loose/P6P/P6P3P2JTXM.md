For the points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}, B$ and $C$ in the plane, we know that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} B=\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} C=d
$$

furthermore, for every point $P$ in the plane, it is true that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} P \geq d
$$

Show that the points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ lie on a straight line.

2.3.9 $\star \star$ In a non-right $\triangle ABC$, the side lengths $a, b, c$ satisfy $a+c=\lambda b, (\lambda>1)$.
(1) Prove: $\tan \frac{A}{2} \cdot \tan \frac{C}{2}=\frac{\lambda-1}{\lambda+1}$;
(2) Does there exist a function $f(\lambda)$ such that for all $\lambda$ satisfying the condition, the expression $\frac{\cos A+\cos C+f(\lambda)}{f(\lambda) \cos A \cdot \cos C}$ is always a constant? If it exists, provide a function $f(\lambda)$ that satisfies the condition and prove it; if not, give a reason.

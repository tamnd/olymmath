Given a rectangle ABCD. Let lines p and q, which pass through vertex A, intersect the semicircles externally tangent to sides $BC$ and $CD$ of the given rectangle at points $K$ and $L (B \neq K \neq C \neq L \neq D)$, and also intersect sides $BC$ and $CD$ at points $P$ and $Q$ such that triangle $ABP$ has the same area as triangle $KCP$ and triangle $AQD$ has the same area as triangle $CLQ$. Prove that points K, L, and C lie on the same line.

Let $m$ , $n$ and $p$ are real numbers such that $\left(m+n+p\right)\left(\frac 1m + \frac 1n + \frac1p\right) =1$ .
Find all possible values of $$ \frac 1{(m+n+p)^{2023}} -\frac 1{m^{2023}} -\frac 1{n^{2023}} -\frac 1{p^{2023}}. $$

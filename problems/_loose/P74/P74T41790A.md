Let $a=\sqrt[2017]{2017}$ and let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ be the sequence such that

$$
a_{1}=a \text { and } a_{n+1}=a^{a_{n}} \text {, for } n \geq 1 \text {. }
$$

Does there exist a natural number $n$, such that $a_{n} \geq 2017$? Justify your answer!

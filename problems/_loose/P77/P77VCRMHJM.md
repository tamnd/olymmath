$A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}, E^{\prime}$ - midpoints of the sides of a convex pentagon $A B C D E$. Prove that the areas of the pentagons $A B C D E$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime} E^{\prime}$ are related by the inequality:

$$
S_{A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime} E^{\prime}} \geqslant \frac{1}{2} S_{A B C D E}
$$

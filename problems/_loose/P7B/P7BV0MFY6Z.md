**4** Let $A$ and $B$ be positive integers. The set $S$ has the following properties:

For each nonnegative integer $k$ , $A^k \in S$ ;

If $n \in S$ , then all positive divisors of $n$ belong to $S$ as well;

If $m$ , $n \in S$ , and $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime, then $mn \in S$ ;

If $n \in S$ , then $An+b \in S$ .

Prove that for any $p$ that is relatively prime with $B$ , $p \in S$ .

Given is an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ such that $|BC|<|CA|<|AB|$. Let $D, E$, and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from vertices $A, B$, and $C$, respectively. The line through $F$ parallel to $DE$ intersects the line $BC$ at point $M$, and the angle bisector of $\angle MFE$ intersects the line $DE$ at point $N$.

Prove that point $F$ is the circumcenter of triangle $DMN$ if and only if point $B$ is the circumcenter of triangle $FMN$.

A rectangular parallelepiped has three edges of different lengths $A B, B C$, and $C D$. A plane perpendicular to $A D$ intersects all six edges of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ at different points. Show that it is possible to choose three points from the set $H$ of these intersection points such that the orthocenter of the triangle determined by them is also an element of $H$.

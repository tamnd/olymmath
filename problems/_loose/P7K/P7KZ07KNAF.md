Given $n-1$ points $P_{k}(k=1,2, \cdots, n-1)$ in the complex plane. Prove: there must exist a point $A$ on the unit circle $O$ such that $\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}\left|A P_{k}\right| \geqslant 1$.

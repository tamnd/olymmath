Thirty students participate in a math competition with sixteen questions. For each question, they must give a number as an answer. For each question a student answers correctly within a minute, they receive 10 points. For each question they answer correctly but not within a minute, they receive 5 points. For each question they answer incorrectly, they receive 0 points.

$\mathrm{After}$ the competition, it turns out that more than half of all 480 answers are correct and given within a minute. The number of answers that are correct but not given within a minute is equal to the number of incorrect answers.

Show that there are two students who have scored exactly the same total score.

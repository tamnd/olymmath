Let $R$ be the set of real numbers and $f : R \to R$ be a function that satisfies: $$ f(xy) + y + f(x + f(y)) = (y + 1)f(x), $$ for all real numbers $x, y$ .
a) Determine the value of $f(0)$ .
b) Prove that $f(x) = 2-x$ for every real number $x$ .

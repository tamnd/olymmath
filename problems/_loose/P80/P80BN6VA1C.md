Consider the acute-angled triangle $ABC$, and $D \in (BC)$. If $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ are the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $ABD$ and $ACD$, show that:
a) $\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}$ where $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ are the radii of the two circles.
b) $\frac{S_{AQB}}{S_{AQ_{2}C}} = \left(\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\right)^2$.
c) $S_{ABC} \leq 2 \cdot S_{AO_{1}DO_{2}}$. When do we have equality?

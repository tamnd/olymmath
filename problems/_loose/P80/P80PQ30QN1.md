Let $a>0, b>0$ and $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=1$. Prove that for all natural numbers $n$, we have $(a+b)^{n}-a^{n}-b^{n} \geqslant 2^{2 n}-2^{n+1}$.
(1988 National High School League Question)

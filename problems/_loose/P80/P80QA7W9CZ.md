Let $A, B, C, D$ be points in space not lying in the same plane, and let $E, F, G, H$ be the projections of a point $P$ onto the lines $AB, BC, CD, DA$, respectively. Show that

$$
A E^{2}+B F^{2}+C G^{2}+D H^{2}=E B^{2}+F C^{2}+G D^{2}+H A^{2}
$$

Is it true that if the lines $AB, BC, CD, DA$ at points $E, F, G, H$ satisfy (1), then the planes perpendicular to the lines passing through $E, F, G, H$ intersect at a single point?

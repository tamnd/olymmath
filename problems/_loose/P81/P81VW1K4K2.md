Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$. We denote by $P$ the intersection of the lines $(A B)$ and $(C D)$. We denote by $(d)$ the line passing through $P$ and parallel to the tangent to $\Gamma$ passing through $D$. We also denote by $U$ and $V$ the intersections with $(d)$ of the tangents at $A$ and $B$. Show that $\Gamma$ and the circumcircle of $U V C$ are tangent.

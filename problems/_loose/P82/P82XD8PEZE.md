$41 .^{+}$ Let $S$ be a surface to which $H$ can be embedded. We can use the fact that the number of disjoint copies of $H$ that can be embedded on $S$ is bounded by some number $n \in \mathbb{N}$. To prove that $f$ cannot be defined for $k>n$, consider a candidate value $\ell \in \mathbb{N}$ for $f(k)$, and extend a fixed embedding of $H$ on $S$ to an embedding of a graph $H^{\prime}$ on $S$ such that the remaining graph still contains an $H$ minor after the deletion of any $\ell$ vertices.

Let $A > 0$ and $B = (3 + 2\sqrt{2})A$ . Prove that in the finite sequence $a_k = \lfloor k / \sqrt{2} \rfloor$ for $k \in (A, B) \cap \mathbb{Z}$ , the number of even and odd terms differs by at most $2$ .

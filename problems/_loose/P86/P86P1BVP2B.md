Prove that if in a group of six people there are at least ten pairs of acquaintances, then there are three people who know each other. The relationship of "knowing" is mutual, meaning if person $A$ knows person $B$, then $B$ also knows $A$. Show that such a trio does not necessarily exist if in a group of six people there are fewer than ten pairs of acquaintances.

The number 1, raised to any power, always gives 1; a number slightly greater than 1, when multiplied by itself, gives successively larger and larger numbers, its power increases without limit as the exponent increases; conversely, the power of a number slightly less than 1 decreases without limit and approaches zero as the exponent increases. Prove these statements.

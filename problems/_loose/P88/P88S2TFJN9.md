Through each of the intersection points of the extensions of the sides of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, two lines are drawn. These lines divide the quadrilateral into nine quadrilaterals.

a) Prove that if three of the quadrilaterals adjacent to the vertices $A, B, C, D$ are circumscribed, then the fourth quadrilateral is also circumscribed.

b) Prove that if $r_{\mathrm{a}}, r_{\mathrm{b}}, r_{\mathrm{c}}, r_{\mathrm{d}}$ are the radii of the circles inscribed in the quadrilaterals adjacent to the vertices $A, B, C, D$, then

$$
\frac{1}{r_{a}}+\frac{1}{r_{c}}=\frac{1}{r_{b}}+\frac{1}{r_{d}}
$$

We say that two positive integers $a$ and $b$ , with $a$ greater than $b$ , are *friends* if $a$ can be written as the sum of some positive and distinct divisors of $b$ . For example, $19$ and $12$ are friends, since $19 = 3 + 4 + 12$ . Determine whether there exist $25$ distinct positive integers such that every pair of them is mutually friends.

The Student Union of Taperoá is going to have a party, selling tickets for R$6.00 each. To encourage the early purchase of tickets, the directors of the Union decided that:

- the tickets will be numbered starting from number 1 and sold in ascending order of their numbering;
- at the end of the party, each participant will receive R$0.01 for each ticket sold that has a number greater than the number of their ticket.

a) If 100 tickets are sold, how much will the person who bought the ticket with number 1 receive at the end of the party? And the one who bought the ticket with number 70?

b) What will be the profit of the Union if 100 tickets are sold?

c) How many tickets should the Union sell to achieve the maximum possible profit?

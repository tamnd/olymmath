Find all values of the parameter $a$, for each of which the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
(|y-4|+|x+12|-3)\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-12\right)=0 \\
(x+5)^{2}+(y-4)^{2}=a
\end{array}\right.
$$

has exactly three solutions.

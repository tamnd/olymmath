A point of inflection of a cubic parabola is the center of a circle; this circle is internally tangent to the parabola at points $A(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$ and $B(-\sqrt{2},-\sqrt{2})$ and intersects it at two other points. The tangents drawn to the parabola at points $A$ and $B$ are parallel to each other. If the abscissa of one of the intersection points is $x_{1}<0$, determine the magnitude of the area bounded by the two curves between $x_{2}=\sqrt{2}$ and these $x_{1}$ limits!

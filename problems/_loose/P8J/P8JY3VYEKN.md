Prove that the area of a triangle is equal to the product of its three sides divided by four times the radius of the circumscribed circle around the triangle, i.e.,

$$
S_{\Delta}=\frac{a b c}{4 R}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the sides of the triangle, and $R$ is the radius of its circumscribed circle.

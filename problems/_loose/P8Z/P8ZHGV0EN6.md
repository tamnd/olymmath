Let the lines passing through the Lemoine point of triangle $ABC$ and parallel to the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ intersect the sides $AB$, $AC$ at points $P$, $Q$, the sides $BC$, $BA$ at points $R$, $S$, and the sides $CA$, $CB$ at points $T$, $U$. Prove that

$$
UR: QT: SP = BC^3: CA^3: AB^3
$$

(See the 13th figure in László Surányi's article in the November 1984 issue of the Magazine, on page 350.)

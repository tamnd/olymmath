In the square $A B C D$, an arc through vertices $A$ and $C$ is drawn inside the square with $D$ as the center. A semicircle is constructed inside the square with $A D$ as the diameter. A line segment connecting an arbitrary point $P$ on the arc $A C$ with point $D$ intersects the semicircle $A D$ at point $K$. Prove that the length of the segment $P K$ is equal to the distance from point $P$ to side $A B$.

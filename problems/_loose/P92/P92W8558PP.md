Given a circle with center $M$. A diameter of this circle is $A B$. Two points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ start moving uniformly from the same time on each of the two semicircular arcs from $A$ to $B$, with the movement of point $P_{1}$ being four times as fast as that of point $P_{2}$.

Is there a time between the start and the arrival of $P_{1}$ (at $B$) when the triangles $\triangle A B P_{1}$ and $\triangle A B P_{2}$ have the same area?

If yes, determine for each such time the size of the angle $\angle A M P_{2}$!

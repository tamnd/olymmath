Write a rational number in the grid, requiring that the number is different from those already written in the table, Alice writes first, then the two take turns. When each cell is filled with a number, the cell containing the largest number in the row is colored black. Alice wins if she can draw a straight line from the top to the bottom of the table, such that the line is always within black cells (if two black cells share a common point, a straight line can be drawn to connect these two black cells). Which player has a winning strategy?

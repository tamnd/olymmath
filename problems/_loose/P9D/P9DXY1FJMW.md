Let $f$ and $g$ be two polynomials such that $f(g(x))=g(f(x))$ . If $g(x)$ is linear but not identically equal to $x$ , and $f(x)=x^3+60x^2+1000x+c$ for some $c$ , find the value of $c$ .

*Clarification*: $g$ is not constant.

An element $x$ is chosen from the set $A=\left\{1,2, \ldots, 2^{n}\right\}, n \geq 3$. Questions of the type: Does $x$ belong to $B \subset A$ where the sum of the elements of $B$ equals $2^{n-2}\left(2^{n}+1\right)$ are allowed? Prove that one can find $x$ with exactly $n$ questions stated in advance.

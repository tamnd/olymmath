On a plane, a convex $n$-gon is given. Let $a_{k}$ be the length of its $k$-th side, and $d_{k}$ be the length of its projection onto the line containing this side $(k=1,2,3, \ldots, n)$. Prove that

$$
2<\frac{a_{1}}{d_{1}}+\frac{a_{2}}{d_{2}}+\ldots+\frac{a_{n}}{d_{n}} \leqslant 4
$$

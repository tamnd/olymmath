20-2 (G40, USA) In a sphere, there is a fixed point $P$, and there are three moving points $A, B, C$ on the sphere's surface, $\angle B P A=\angle C P A=\angle C P B=90^{\circ}$. A parallelepiped is formed with $P A, P B$, and $P C$ as edges, and point $Q$ is the vertex of the parallelepiped that is diagonally opposite to $P$. When $A, B, C$ move on the sphere's surface, find the trajectory of point $Q$.

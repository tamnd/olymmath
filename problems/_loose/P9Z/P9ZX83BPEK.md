In the cells of a $12 \times 12$ table, natural numbers are arranged such that the following condition is met: for any number in a non-corner cell, there is an adjacent cell (by side) that contains a smaller number. What is the smallest number of different numbers that can be in the table?

(Non-corner cells are those that are not in the corner of the table. There are exactly 140 of them.)

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Let $P$ and $Q$ be two points on the segment $[BC]$. We denote by $\mathrm{O}_{1}, \mathrm{O}_{2}, \mathrm{O}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{O}_{4}$ the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $ABP$, $ABQ$, $ACP$ and $\mathrm{ACQ}$. Show that the points $\mathrm{O}_{1}, \mathrm{O}_{2}, \mathrm{O}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{O}_{4}$ are concyclic if and only if $\widehat{\mathrm{BAP}}=\widehat{\mathrm{QAC}}$.

Let $p_1,p_2,...,p_n$ be $n\ge 2$ fixed positive real numbers. Let $x_1,x_2,...,x_n$ be nonnegative real numbers such that $$x_1p_1+x_2p_2+...+x_np_n=1.$$
Determine the *(a)* maximal; *(b)* minimal possible value of $x_1^2+x_2^2+...+x_n^2$.

Davide and Golia live in a building whose floor plan is a regular pentagon with a side of 10 meters. The entrance of the building is located at one of the vertices of the pentagon, and the building is surrounded, for several kilometers, by flat ground. Golia steals David's slingshot, exits through the entrance, travels no more than 20 meters (without re-entering the building) and leaves the slingshot on the ground. What is the maximum area in which David will have to search before finding his slingshot?
(A) $320 \pi \mathrm{m}^{2}$,
(B) $160 \pi \mathrm{m}^{2}$,
(C) $100\left(4 \pi-\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\right) \mathrm{m}^{2}$,

(D) $100\left(2 \pi+\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}\right) \mathrm{m}^{2}$,

(E) $280 \pi \mathrm{m}^{2}$

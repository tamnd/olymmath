On a circle, 1993 points are marked, one of which is colored red, and all the others are blue. The given points determine various polygons inscribed in the circle, such that if one of the vertices of the polygon is the red point, the polygon is red, and if all the vertices of the polygon are blue, the polygon is blue. Are there more red or blue polygons, and why? (In the solution, do not consider polygons where non-adjacent sides intersect.)

Question 5 Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ satisfying $AB > AC$, the incenter is $I$, the excenter opposite to $\angle A$ is $I_{1}$, and the midpoint of $BC$ is $L$. The line $LI$ intersects side $AC$ at point $M$, and the line $I_{1}L$ intersects side $AB$ at point $N$. Let $BC = a$, $CA = b$, and $AB = c$. Prove:
$$
\frac{S_{\triangle M N}}{S_{\triangle A B C}}=\frac{a(c-b)}{(a+c-b)^{2}} .
$$

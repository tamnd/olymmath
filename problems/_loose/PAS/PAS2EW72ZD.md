A regular triangle $ABC$, each side is divided into $n$ equal parts, and lines parallel to the other two sides are drawn through each division point, dividing the original triangle into smaller regular triangles. Ask:
(1) How many regular triangles (including the original regular triangle) are produced in total?
(2) How many rhombuses are produced in total?
(1990 Chinese National Training Team Problem)

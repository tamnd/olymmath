An inscribed angle that subtends a diameter. Pythagorean Theorem (direct and inverse).

A circle passes through the adjacent vertices $M$ and $N$ of the rectangle $M N P Q$. The length of the tangent drawn from point $Q$ to the circle is $1, P Q=2$. Find all possible values that the area of the rectangle $M N P Q$ can take if the diameter of the circle is $\sqrt{5}$.

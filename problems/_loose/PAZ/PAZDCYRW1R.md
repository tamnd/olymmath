A polygon is inscribed in a circle. If a vertex $A$ divides the arc between two other vertices into two unequal parts, then such a vertex $A$ is called unstable. Every second, some unstable vertex jumps to the midpoint of its arc. As a result, a new polygon is formed every second. Prove that no matter how many seconds pass, the polygon will never be the same as the original one.

$ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral with $AC \perp BD$; $AC$ meets $BD$ at $E$. Prove that $$ EA^2 + EB^2 + EC^2 + ED^2 = 4 R^2 $$
where $R$ is the radius of the circumscribing circle.

Triangle $A B C$ is right-angled with the right angle at vertex $C$. Points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are the images of points $A, B, C$ respectively under central symmetries with centers $C, A, B$. Prove that

$$
\left|A^{\prime} B^{\prime}\right|^{2}+\left|B^{\prime} C^{\prime}\right|^{2}+\left|C^{\prime} A^{\prime}\right|^{2}=14 \cdot|A B|^{2} .
$$

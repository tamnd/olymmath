Let real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy $xy \neq 0, x+y \neq 0$. Find
$$
\left(\frac{y+z}{x}+2\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{z}{y}+2\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{z}{x+y}-1\right)^{2}
$$

the minimum value.

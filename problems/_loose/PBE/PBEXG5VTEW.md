In the Umnovka River, there live Premudrye (Wise) minnows, and they have a law: as soon as one of them changes the color of their eyes from gray to green, they must swim to the Mozgovka River on the same night they learn about this transformation. There are no mirrors under the water, and the minnows understand everything, but they cannot speak or gesture. Only the old carp, who sometimes swims to them, can speak. But he only says two phrases to them: "All of you have gray eyes" - and then the life of the minnows goes on as usual, and "At least one of you has green eyes" - and then all the green-eyed fish must leave. How do the minnows understand that they need to leave - if there is only one green-eyed minnow? If there are two? If there are three or more?

Let $a_1,...,a_n$ be $n$ real numbers. If for each odd positive integer $k\leqslant n$ we have $a_1^k+a_2^k+\ldots+a_n^k=0$ , then for each odd positive integer $k$ we have $a_1^k+a_2^k+\ldots+a_n^k=0$ .

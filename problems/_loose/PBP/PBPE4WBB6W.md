There are two diverging, perpendicular lines $m$ and $n$ in space. The line that intersects both lines perpendicularly (the so-called normal-transversal or binormal) intersects the given lines at points $M$ and $N$ respectively, with $MN = 2d$. On line $m$, we take a point $A$ at a distance $x$ from $M$, and on line $n$, we take a point $B$ at a distance $y$ from $N$. Determine the center $O$ and the radius of the sphere $G$ circumscribed around the tetrahedron $MABN$.

What is the geometric locus of the points $O$ if the points $A$ and $B$ move on the lines $m$ and $n$ respectively, such that

a) the radius of the sphere $G$ is constantly $2d$;

b) $x = y$ is always true.

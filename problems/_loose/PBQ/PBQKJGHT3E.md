Given trapezoid $ABCD$ with the larger base $AD$. Point $O$ is the midpoint of the midline of the trapezoid. Points $K, L, M, N$ are the images of vertices $A, B, C, D$ under central symmetry with respect to point $O$. Lines $KM$ and $NL$ intersect sides $CD$ and $AB$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that each of the segments $CM, BL, AP, DQ$ divides the area of the trapezoid in half.

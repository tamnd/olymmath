A cube is to be divided into two parts by a planar cut in different ways. Can the following cutting figures be created:
a) equilateral triangle
b) isosceles triangle (not equilateral)
c) right-angled triangle
d) scalene triangle
e) square
f) rectangle (not square)
g) pentagon
h) octagon?

Which possible cutting figures are not included in the list?

Make a sketch for each cutting figure showing how the planar cut must be made to obtain the respective cutting figure!

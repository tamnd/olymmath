In a tennis tournament, 1152 schoolchildren are participating. 1 point is awarded for a win, and 0 points for a loss. Before each round, pairs are formed by drawing lots among participants with the same number of points (those who do not find a pair are awarded a point without playing). A player is eliminated after the second loss. The tournament continues as long as at least one pair of opponents can be formed. How many rounds will have to be played?

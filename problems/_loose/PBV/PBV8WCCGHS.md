It is known that for any four lines in general position on a plane (i.e., such that no two are parallel and no three intersect at the same point), the circles circumscribed around the four triangles formed by their intersections pass through one point. Does there exist a set of 45 lines in general position such that all circles circumscribed around the triangles formed by their intersections pass through one point?

In commerce, the difference between the acquisition and selling price of goods (cost and profit) is often expressed as a ratio to the acquisition price and the selling price. When expressed as a percentage, this ratio is called the profit key from bottom to top or from top to bottom, depending on the reference point. Prove that:

1) given a profit key calculated from the bottom up, converting it to the other method always results in the same profit key from top to bottom, regardless of the acquisition price, and vice versa;
2) the difference between the profit key calculated from the bottom up and the corresponding profit key calculated from the top down is equal to one hundredth of the product of the two keys.

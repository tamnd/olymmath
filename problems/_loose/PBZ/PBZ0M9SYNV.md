Raphaël has inscribed, on each vertex of a pentagon, an integer, such that the sum of these five integers is strictly positive. Then he allows himself operations of the following form: he chooses three consecutive vertices on which are found integers $x, y$ and $z$, such that $y<0$, and replaces them respectively with $x+y, -y$ and $y+z$. Is any sequence of such operations necessarily finite? And if Raphaël repeats this process on a 2019-gon instead of a pentagon?

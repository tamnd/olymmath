Let $AB$ be a given line segment of length $a$ in the plane $\epsilon$. In $\epsilon$, let $g$ be the line through $A$ that is perpendicular to $AB$.

At $B$, let $s$ be the perpendicular line to the plane $\epsilon$. Finally, let $C$ be a point on $g$ different from $A$ and $D$ be a point on $s$ different from $B$.

a) Prove that there exists a sphere that passes through the points $A, B, C$, and $D$.

b) Calculate the radius of such a sphere in the case where $CA = a \sqrt{2}$ and $BD = a \sqrt{3}$.

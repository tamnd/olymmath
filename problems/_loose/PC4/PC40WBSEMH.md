In all the streets of the city of Zurbagan, there was two-way traffic. However, when the city authorities needed to carry out repairs on all roads, they were forced to temporarily introduce one-way traffic on some streets, leaving traffic two-way on the remaining streets; after this, on the contrary, one-way traffic was established on the streets that had not been repaired previously, while on the streets where traffic had previously been one-way, two-way traffic was again allowed. At the same time, during the road repair period, in both cases, it was possible to travel from any point in Zurbagan to any other. Prove that in Zurbagan, one-way traffic can be introduced on all streets in such a way that travel from any point in the city to any other remains possible.

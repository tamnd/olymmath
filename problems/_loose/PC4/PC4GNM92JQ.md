Given that $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{\mathscr{M}}, a_{100}$ are all real numbers. In the set $\left\{a_{1}, \frac{a_{1}+a_{2}}{2}, \frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}}{3}, \cdots\right.$, $\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{10}}{100}$, at least 51 elements have the same value. Prove: $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{10}$ contain two equal numbers.

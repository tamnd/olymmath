Theorem of Leibniz. Let $M$ be an arbitrary point on the plane, and $G$ be the centroid of triangle $A B C$. Then the following equality holds:
$3|M G|^{2}=|M A|^{2}+|M B|^{2}+|M C|^{2}-\frac{1}{3}\left(|A B|^{2}+|B C|^{2}+|C A|^{2}\right)$.

Let's prove that in any triangle $ABC$, the following three lines intersect at a single point: the angle bisector from $A$,

the line parallel to side $AB$ through the midpoint of the opposite side,

the line connecting the points where the incircle touches the sides starting from $B$.

Formulate the corresponding statement for the excircle based on geometric intuition.

In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular, and the opposite sides $AB$ and $CD$ are not parallel. Point $P$ is the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of segments $AB$ and $CD$, and $P$ is inside the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Prove that $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral if and only if the areas of $\triangle ABP$ and $\triangle CDP$ are equal.

(New York, 76). Let $a, a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be arbitrary integers. Is it true that the integer

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(a^{2}+1\right)^{3 k} a_{k}
$$

is divisible by $a^{2}+a+1$ (or by $a^{2}-a+1$) if and only if the number

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^{k} a_{k}
$$

is divisible by $a^{2}+a+1$ (or respectively by $a^{2}-a+1$)?

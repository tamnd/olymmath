Let the distance of a (closed) set $K$ and a point $P$ be the minimum of the distances $PQ$, where $Q \in K$. Given a square and a circle outside it on the plane, the center of which lies on one of the square's side lines. Determine the set of points in the plane that are equidistant from the circle and the square.

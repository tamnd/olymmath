The ninety-seventh question: Let $\mathrm{m}$ be a positive integer. In a $4 \mathrm{~m} \times 4 \mathrm{~m}$ grid, if two cells are in the same row or the same column, they are called "related". Each cell is not related to itself. Now, some cells in the grid are colored blue, such that each cell is related to at least two blue cells. Find the minimum number of cells that are colored blue.

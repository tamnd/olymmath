In the gnome clan, some are acquainted with each other. Each gnome owns a certain number of coins. During the day, each gnome finds out how many coins each of his acquaintances has. In the evening, he gives one coin to each of his acquaintances who were richer than him during the day. A gnome cannot give more than he has (for example, a poor gnome gives nothing). If a gnome had fewer coins during the day than the number of richer acquaintances, he decides for himself whom to give the coins to. Prove that, starting from some day, the gnomes will stop transferring coins to each other.

Holiday Ham. Three neighbors pooled 15 rubles each and bought a ham (without skin, fat, and bones). One of them divided it into three parts, insisting that the parts were equal in weight. Another claimed she only trusted the scales at the corner store; there it turned out that the supposedly equal parts, when recalculated for cost, were worth 14, 15, and 16 rubles. The third participant checked the weight on her home scales, which also gave a different result. An argument ensued, with the first insisting she had divided the ham into equal parts, the second recognizing only the store scales, and the third her own. How can the argument be settled and the pieces (without further cutting) be divided so that each woman acknowledges that she has received a piece of ham worth at least 15 rubles according to the scales she trusts?

At each vertex of a convex $k$-gon, there is a hunter armed with a laser gun. All hunters simultaneously shot at a rabbit sitting at point $O$ inside this $k$-gon. At the moment of the shot, the rabbit crouched, and all the hunters died. Prove that there is no other point, except for $O$, with the specified property.

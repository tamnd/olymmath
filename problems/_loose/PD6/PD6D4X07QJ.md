The plane is divided into regions by a finite number of lines, no three of which pass through the same point. Two regions are called "adjacent" if their common boundary is a segment, ray, or line. It is necessary to write an integer in each region such that the following two conditions are satisfied:

$\left(1^{\circ}\right)$ the product of the numbers in adjacent regions is less than their sum;

$\left(2^{\circ}\right)$ the sum of all numbers on each side of any line is equal to zero.

Prove that this is possible if and only if not all lines are parallel.

(Serbia and Montenegro)

Let $n>1$ be a natural number and $A_0A_1\ldots A_{2^n-2}$ be a regular polygon. Prove that $$\frac{1}{A_0A_1}=\frac{1}{A_0A_2}+\frac{1}{A_0A_4}+\frac{1}{A_0A_8}+\cdots+\frac{1}{A_0A_{2^{n-1}}}.$$*Proposed by Le Hoang and Ngoc Thai (Vietnam)*

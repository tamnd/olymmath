In a regular triangular pyramid $A B C D$, the angle $A D C$ is equal to $2 \arcsin \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{}$, and the side of the base $A B C$ is 2. Points $K, M$, and $N$ are the midpoints of edges $A B, C D, A C$ respectively. Point $E$ lies on segment $K M$ and $3 M E=K E$. A plane П passes through point $E$ and is perpendicular to segment $K M$. In what ratio does the plane П divide the edges of the pyramid? Find the area of the cross-section of the pyramid by the plane П and the distance from point $N$ to the plane П.

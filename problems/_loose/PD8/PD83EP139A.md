We will prove that if $n$ is a positive integer and the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ are each at least 1, then

$$
\left(a_{1}+1\right)\left(a_{2}+1\right) \cdot \ldots \cdot\left(a_{n}+1\right) \geq 2^{n-1}\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}-n+2\right)
$$

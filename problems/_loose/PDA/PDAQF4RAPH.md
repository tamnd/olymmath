Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $M$ the midpoint of the segment $[BC]$. The tangent to the circle $(ABC)$ at $A$ intersects $(BC)$ at $D$. The circle $\Gamma$ with center $D$ and radius $AD$ intersects the circle $ABC$ at $E$. The line $(BE)$ intersects $\Gamma$ at $X$. The line $(CX)$ intersects $\Gamma$ at $Y$. Show that $A, Y$ and $M$ are collinear.

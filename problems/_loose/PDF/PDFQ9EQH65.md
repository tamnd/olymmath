Chebyshev Polynomials. Prove that for $x$ between -1 and +1, the expression

$$
T_{n}(x)=\cos (n \operatorname{arc} \cos x)
$$

represents a polynomial in $x$ of degree $n$ with the leading coefficient $2^{n-1}$. Find all roots of the equation $T_{n}(x)=0$ and all values of $x$ between -1 and +1 for which the polynomial $T_{n}(x)$ takes its maximum and minimum values.

1) Both "Nim" and "jian shi zi" are Chinese folk games ("jian shi zi" translates to "picking stones" in Russian; naturally, replacing matches with stones in the game does not change it at all).

Given a regular pentagon paper piece $A$. Prove: there exists a region $B$ in the plane, satisfying:
(1) No matter how the paper piece $A$ is moved, it cannot cover the region $B$;
(2) For any 2015 red points in the region $B$, the paper piece $A$ can be moved appropriately to cover all these points.

A certain public figure and all of his descendants, regardless of gender, are included in the family tree, but their spouses are not included in the family tree. The family tree only indicates whether a member is male or female and the parent-child relationships between them. It is known that the family tree of this public figure includes a total of $n$ male and female members, each member has at most two children, and if they have two children, they must be one male and one female. How many possible family trees are there for this public figure?

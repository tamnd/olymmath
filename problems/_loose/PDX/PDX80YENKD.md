Show that if for a certain triangle the following relationships hold simultaneously:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3}}{a+b+c} & =c^{2} \ldots \text { and } \\
\sin \alpha \sin \beta & =\sin ^{2} \gamma \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

then the triangle is equilateral.

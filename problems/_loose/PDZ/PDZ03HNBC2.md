Hula Hoop. Imagine a slender girl who has momentarily stopped while a hula hoop is rotating (without slipping) around her waist. The girl's waist is in the shape of a circle, with a diameter that is half the diameter of the hula hoop. Show that after one complete revolution of the hoop, a point on it that was initially in contact with the girl's waist travels a distance equal to the perimeter of a square circumscribed around the waist.

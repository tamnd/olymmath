On a line, points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{20}$ are taken in this order, such that $A_{1} A_{2}=6 \text{~cm}$, $A_{2} A_{3}=12 \text{~cm}$, $A_{3} A_{4}=18 \text{~cm}$, and so on.

a) What is the length of the segment $\left[A_{1} A_{20}\right]$? What about the segment $\left[A_{15} A_{20}\right]$?

b) Determine $i \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ for which $M \in\left[A_{i} A_{i+1}\right]$, where $M$ is the midpoint of $\left[A_{1} A_{20}\right]$.

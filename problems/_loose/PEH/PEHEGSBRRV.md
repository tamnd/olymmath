Suppose $x,y,z$ , and $w$ are positive reals such that
$$ x^2 + y^2 - \frac{xy}{2} = w^2 + z^2 + \frac{wz}{2} = 36 $$ $$ xz + yw = 30. $$ Find the largest possible value of $(xy + wz)^2$ .

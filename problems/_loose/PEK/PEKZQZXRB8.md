For real $\theta_i$ , $i = 1, 2, \dots, 2011$ , where $\theta_1 = \theta_{2012}$ , find the maximum value of the expression

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{2011} \sin^{2012} \theta_i \cos^{2012} \theta_{i+1}.
$$

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

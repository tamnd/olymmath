Find the range of the following functions:
(1) $y=x+\sqrt{1-2 x}$;
(2) $y=\frac{10^{x}+10^{-x}}{10^{x}-10^{-x}}$;
(3) $y=\log _{0.5}\left(2 x-x^{2}+3\right)$;
(4) $y=\sqrt{x^{2}+2 x+2}+\sqrt{x^{2}-2 x+2}$;
(5) $y=|x+1|+|x-1|+|x+2|$;
(6) $y=\frac{x^{2}+4 x+3}{x^{2}+x-6}$;
(7) $y=x^{2}+\frac{1}{x} \left(x \in \mathrm{R}^{+}\right)$.

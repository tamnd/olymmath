A sheet in the form of a regular hexagon with an area of $2010 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ is given. Alexander and Elena are playing a game. The game consists of placing circular coins with an area of $1 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ on the sheet in such a way that the coins must be entirely within the sheet and must not overlap. Alexander starts by placing the first coin. The winner of the game is the one who places the last coin on the sheet. Prove that if Alexander plays well, he can win the game regardless of how Elena plays.

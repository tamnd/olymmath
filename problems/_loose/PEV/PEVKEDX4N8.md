A sequence of independent random variables $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}, \ldots$ is given by the distribution law

$$
\begin{array}{lcc}
X_{n} & n+1 & -n \\
p & n /(2 n+1) & (n+1) /(2 n+1)
\end{array}
$$

a) Verify that the requirement of the Chebyshev theorem for uniform boundedness of variances is not satisfied; b) Can we conclude from this that the Chebyshev theorem is not applicable to the given sequence?

\end{aligned}

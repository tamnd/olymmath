Let $F$ be the left focus of the hyperbola $\Gamma: x^{2}-y^{2}=1$, and let a line passing through $F$ intersect $\Gamma$ at points $M$ and $N$.
(1) If $M$ and $N$ are both on the left branch of the hyperbola, find the minimum value of the area of $\triangle O M N$;
(2) Does there exist a point $P$ on the $x$-axis such that $\overrightarrow{P M} \cdot \overrightarrow{P N}$ is a constant? If so, find the coordinates of point $P$; if not, explain why.

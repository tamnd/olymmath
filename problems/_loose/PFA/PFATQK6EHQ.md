Find the coordinates of the vector

$$
X=\left(\begin{array}{l}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{array}\right)
$$

if the linear transformation that maps it to the vector

$$
Y=\left(\begin{array}{l}
1 \\
2 \\
3
\end{array}\right)
$$

is given by the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x_{1}+2 x_{2}+3 x_{3}=1 \\
-x_{1}+3 x_{2}+4 x_{3}=2 \\
2 x_{1}-3 x_{2}+5 x_{3}=3
\end{array}\right.
$$

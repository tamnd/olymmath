Let $\boldsymbol{i}, \boldsymbol{j}$ be the unit vectors in the positive directions of the $x, y$ axes in a Cartesian coordinate system. If the vectors $\boldsymbol{a}=(x+2) \boldsymbol{i}+y \boldsymbol{j}$, $\boldsymbol{b}=(x-2) \boldsymbol{i}+y \boldsymbol{j}$, and $|\boldsymbol{a}|-|\boldsymbol{b}|=2$.
(1) Find the equation of the locus of point $P(x, y)$ that satisfies the above conditions;
(2) Let $A(-1,0), F(2,0)$. Does there exist a constant $\lambda(\lambda>0)$ such that $\angle P F A=\lambda \angle P A F$ always holds? Prove your conclusion.

Two travel-loving friends $A$ and $B$ decide to walk towards each other on a hiking trail of $30 \mathrm{~km}$. At the beginning, $A$ is at one endpoint of the trail, and $B$ is at the other endpoint. They coordinate their plan over the phone and assume that each of them will maintain their personal walking speed throughout the entire journey. This leads to the following statements:

(1) If $A$ starts 2 hours earlier than $B$, they will meet $2 \frac{1}{2}$ hours after $B$ starts.

(2) If, however, $B$ starts 2 hours earlier than $A$, they will meet 3 hours after $A$ starts.

Show that under these assumptions, if statements (1) and (2) are true, the walking speeds of $A$ and $B$ are uniquely determined; determine these speeds!

Verify that the reverse is also true: If $A$ and $B$ have the determined speeds, then statements (1) and (2) are true.

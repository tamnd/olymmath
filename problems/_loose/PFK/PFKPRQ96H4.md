C14 (16-1, USA) $A, B, C$ are playing a game: On 3 cards, integers $p, q, r (0<p<q<r)$ are written. The 3 cards are shuffled and distributed to $A, B, C$, each getting one. Then, according to the number on each person's card, they are given marbles. After that, the cards are collected, but the marbles are kept by each person. This process is repeated for more than two rounds (each round includes shuffling the cards, distributing the cards, giving marbles, and collecting the cards). After the final round, $A, B, C$ have a total of 20, 10, 9 marbles, respectively. It is known that $B$ received $r$ marbles in the last round. Who received $q$ marbles in the first round?

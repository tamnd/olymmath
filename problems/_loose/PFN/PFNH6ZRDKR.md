In the time it takes for the grandmother to decorate five gingerbreads, Mařenka decorates three and Jeníček decorates two. During their last visit, all three of them decorated five full trays together. Mařenka and the grandmother decorated the whole time, while Jeníček, in addition to decorating, arranged the gingerbreads in groups of twelve on one tray and carried them to the pantry. All three started and finished at the same time.

1. How many gingerbreads did Jeníček decorate?
2. How long did the entire work take if the grandmother decorates one gingerbread in 4 minutes?
3. How long did Jeníček help with decorating?

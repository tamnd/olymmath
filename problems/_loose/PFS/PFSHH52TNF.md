Let $P$ be a point outside the circle $\Gamma$. Draw tangents from $P$ to the circle $\Gamma$, and let the points of tangency be $A$ and $B$. Point $C$ lies on the segment $AB$. The circle passing through points $P$, $B$, and $C$ intersects the circle $\Gamma$ again at point $D$. Extend $PA$ to point $Q$ such that $A$ is the midpoint of $PQ$. Prove: $AD \parallel QC$.

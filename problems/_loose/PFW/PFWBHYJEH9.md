*Reduced name* of a natural number $A$ with $n$ digits ( $n \ge 2$ ) a number of $n-1$ digits obtained by deleting one of the digits of $A$ : For example, the *reduced names* of $1024$ is $124$ , $104$ and $120$ .
Determine how many seven-digit numbers cannot be written as the sum of one natural numbers $A$ and a *reduced name* of $A$ .

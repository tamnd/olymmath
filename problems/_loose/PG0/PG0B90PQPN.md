Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $P$ a point inside the triangle. We call $D, E, F$ the orthogonal projections of $P$ onto $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. Show that:

$$
\frac{EF \times PD}{AP}+\frac{FD \times PE}{BP}+\frac{DE \times PF}{CP}
$$

is independent of the position of $P$.

There are two very strict laws in the country of Militaria.

(i) Anyone who is shorter than $80\%$ (or more) of his "neighbours" (i.e. men living at distance less then $r$ from him) is freed from the military service.

(ii) Anyone who is taller than $80\%$ (or more) of his "neighbours" (i.e. men living at distance less then $R$ from him) is allowed to serve in the police.

A nice thing is that each man $X$ may choose his own (possibly different) positive numbers $r = r(X)$ and $R = R(X)$ . Can it happen that $90\%$ (or more) of the men in Militaria are free from the army and, at the same time, $90\%$ (or more) of the men in Militaria are allowed to serve in the police? (The places of living of the men are fixed points in the plane.)

(N Konstantinov)

Let $n$ be a positive integer. Each vertex of a regular $2n+1$ polygon is assigned a real number, and the sum of the numbers assigned to any $2$ adjacent vertices is greater than or equal to $0$. Let $S$ be the sum of the positive numbers and $T$ be the sum of the negative numbers among the $2n+1$ numbers. Show that
$$nS+(n+1)T \ge 0.$$ If there are no positive numbers assigned to the vertices, then $S = 0,$ and if there are no negative numbers, then let $T = 0$

It is known that $a<b<c$. Prove that the equation

$$
3 x^{2}-2(a+b+c) x+a b+b c+a c=0
$$

has two distinct roots $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$, such that $a<x_{1}<b, b<x_{2}<c$.

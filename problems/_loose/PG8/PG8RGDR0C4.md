B-1 Two circular tracks $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of the same radius touch each other. Car $A$ is driving clockwise on track $\alpha$, and car $B$ is driving counterclockwise on track $\beta$. At the start, cars $A$ and $B$ are on the same straight line with the center of track $\alpha$, and this line is tangent to track $\beta$. After the start, the cars begin to approach the point where the tracks touch. Each car completes a full circle on its track in one hour (and never switches to the other track). How much time out of this hour will the distance between the cars be no less than the diameter of each track?

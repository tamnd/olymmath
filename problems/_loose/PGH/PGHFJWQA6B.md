An apprentice is supposed to count 1000 balls with a diameter of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ in a ball bearing factory. To speed up this task, he takes a container with inner dimensions of $10 \mathrm{~cm} \times 10 \mathrm{~cm} \times 10$ $\mathrm{cm}$; he lays the first layer neatly and then continues to fill it.

In the end, he finds that, contrary to his expectations, the inner space of the container is not completely filled. He therefore counts the balls. Surprisingly, there are more than a thousand.

How many were there, and how many centimeters were missing from the top layer of balls to the edge of the container?

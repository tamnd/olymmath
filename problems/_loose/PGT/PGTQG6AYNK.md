\section*{Task 2 - 101022}

Four people A, B, C, and D make three statements each about the same object, a monochrome ball. The statements are as follows:

A (1) The ball is neither red nor yellow.

(2) The ball is either red or green.

(3) The ball is black.

B (1) If the ball is not yellow, it is white.

(2) A makes a false statement when he says the ball is black.

(3) The ball is green.

C (1) The ball is either black or green.

(2) The ball is red.

(3) The ball is either green or black or yellow.

D (1) The ball has the same color as my sweater.

(2) If the ball is yellow, it is not black.

(3) The ball is black and green.

Determine the color of the ball for the following two cases and investigate whether the color of D's sweater can be determined solely from the given information!

If so, provide this color!

Case a) Of the three statements made by each of the four people, exactly two are true.

Case b) Of the three statements made by each of the four people, exactly two are false.

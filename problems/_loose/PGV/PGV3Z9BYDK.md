Given an acute-angled triangle $K L M$. Circles centered at $K$ and $M$ pass through point $L$, intersect again at point $P$, and intersect the circumcircle $\omega$ of triangle $K L M$ at points $S$ and $T$. Segment $L P$ intersects circle $\omega$ at point $O$. Prove that $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $S T P$.

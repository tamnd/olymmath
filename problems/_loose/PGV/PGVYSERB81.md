On a certain field of a chessboard, there is a chip. Two players take turns moving the chip, and on each move, starting from the second, the distance it is moved must be strictly greater than on the previous move. The player who cannot make the next move loses. Who wins with correct play? (The chip is always placed exactly in the center of each field.)

Prove that if a polynomial of degree $n$ has $n$ roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$, then

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{0} x^{n}+a_{1} x^{n-1}+\ldots+a_{n-1} x+a_{n}= \\
& =a_{0}\left(x-x_{1}\right)\left(x-x_{2}\right) \ldots\left(x-x_{n}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

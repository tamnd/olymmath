Given the inequality about $x$: $(2 a-b) x+a-5 b>0$, the solution is $x>-\frac{3}{5}$. The solution for $a-5b>0$ is
(A) $x>-\frac{3}{5}$.
(B) $x<\frac{3}{5}$.
(C) $x>\frac{3}{5}$.
(D) $x<\frac{3}{5}$.

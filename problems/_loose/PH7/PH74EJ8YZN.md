Let $p > 2$ be a fixed prime number. Find all functions $f: \mathbb Z \to \mathbb Z_p$ , where the $\mathbb Z_p$ denotes the set $\{0, 1, \ldots , p-1\}$ , such that $p$ divides $f(f(n))- f(n+1) + 1$ and $f(n+p) = f(n)$ for all integers $n$ .

Prove the following:

(1) $ \left[\left(a_{1}+b_{1}\right)^{r}+\left(a_{2}+b_{2}\right)^{r}+\ldots+\left(a_{k}+b_{k}\right)^{r}\right]^{1 / r} \leqq\left(a_{1}^{r}+a_{2}^{r}+\ldots+a_{k}^{r}\right)^{1 / r}+\left(b_{1}^{r}+b_{2}^{r}+\ldots+b_{k}^{r}\right)^{1 / r}$

inequality, where $r>1$. (Minkowski inequality)

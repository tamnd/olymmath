In an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, a piece is placed in one of the squares. Two players, A and B, take turns moving it to another square that has never been visited, and each move must be longer than the previous move. The player who cannot make a move loses. Who will win if both players play optimally?
(1990 Chinese National Training Team Problem)

\section*{Task 4 - 300924}

For each natural number $m \geq 2$, consider the following task:

Someone wants to draw $m$ different rays originating from a point $P$.

Then, they want to determine all the angle measures between $0^{\circ}$ and $360^{\circ}$ that can occur when measuring an angle from one of these rays to another in a mathematically positive direction. They want to draw the $m$ rays such that

a) as few as possible,

b) as many as possible
different angle measures can be determined.

Determine, in dependence on $m$, the smallest and largest possible number of different angle measures that can be achieved!

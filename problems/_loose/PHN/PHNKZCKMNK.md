Given three lines passing through a single point $A$. Let $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ be two points on one line, $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ on another, and $D_{1}$ and $D_{2}$ on the third. Prove that

$$
\frac{V_{A B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}}}{V_{A B_{2} C_{2} D_{2}}}=\frac{\left|A B_{1}\right| \cdot\left|A C_{1}\right| \cdot\left|A D_{1}\right|}{\left|A B_{2}\right| \cdot\left|A C_{2}\right| \cdot\left|A D_{2}\right|} .
$$

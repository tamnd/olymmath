Let $O$ be the orthocenter of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$. Through the vertices $A, B$, and $C$ of the triangle, lines $p, q$, and $r$ are drawn parallel to the sides $BC, AC$, and $AB$, respectively. Let $A_{1}$ be the intersection of $q$ and $r$, $B_{1}$ the intersection of $p$ and $r$, and $C_{1}$ the intersection of $p$ and $q$.

a) Prove that $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$.

b) If $\overline{AB} = \overline{OC}$, determine the angle $\angle ACB$.

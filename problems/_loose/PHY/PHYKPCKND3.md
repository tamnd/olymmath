Let $S_{a}, S_{b}, S_{c}$ and $S_{d}$ be the areas of the faces $BCD, ACD, ABD$ and $ABC$ of the tetrahedron $ABCD$. Prove that in the system of barycentric coordinates associated with the tetrahedron $ABCD$:

a) the center of the inscribed sphere has coordinates $(S_{a}, S_{b}, S_{c}, S_{d})$

b) the center of the exsphere tangent to the face $ABC$ has coordinates $(S_{a}, S_{b}, S_{c}, -S_{d})$.

The eightieth question: In the complete quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCDEF}$, $\mathrm{L}$, $\mathrm{M}$, and $\mathbb{N}$ are the midpoints of $\mathrm{AC}$, $\mathrm{BD}$, and $\mathrm{EF}$, respectively. Then $L$, $M$, and $\mathbb{N}$ are collinear, which is known as the Newton line of the complete quadrilateral (Newton's theorem).

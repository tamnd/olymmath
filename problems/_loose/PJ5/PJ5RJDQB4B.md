In Cartesian space, let $\Omega = \{(a, b, c) : a, b, c$ are integers between $1$ and $30\}$ .
A point of $\Omega$ is said to be *visible* from the origin if the segment that joins said point with the origin does not contain any other elements of $\Omega$ . Find the number of points of $\Omega$ that are *visible* from the origin.

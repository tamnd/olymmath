In Rt $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C=90^{\circ}$, draw $C D \perp$ $A B$ at point $D$, $O$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle B C D$, and there is a circle $\Gamma$ inside $\triangle A C D$, which is tangent to segments $A D$, $A C$ at points $M$, $N$, respectively, and is tangent to $\odot O$. Prove:
(1) $B D \cdot C N + B C \cdot D M = C D \cdot B M$;
(2) $B M = B C$.
(2009, Hong Kong Mathematical Olympiad)

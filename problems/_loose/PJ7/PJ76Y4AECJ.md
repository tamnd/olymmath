We want to place $n$ points on the surface of a sphere such that they do not "interfere" with each other. By this, we mean, following the Dutch mathematician L. B. Van den Waerden, that the distance between any two of the placed points is at least 1 unit. - What diameter sphere is sufficient for $n=2,3,4,5,6$ and 8 cases?

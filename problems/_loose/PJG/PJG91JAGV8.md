Let the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ be $\Omega$, and the incircle be $\omega$. $\omega$ is tangent to $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. Extend $EF$ to intersect $\Omega$ at point $P$, and let line $PD$ intersect $\omega$ and $\Omega$ at points $G$ and $Q$, respectively. Let the tangents to $\Omega$ at $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $T$. Prove:
(1) $TG$ is a tangent to $\omega$; (2) $PQ/DG = (R+r) / r$. Where $R$ and $r$ are the radii of $\omega$ and $\Omega$, respectively.

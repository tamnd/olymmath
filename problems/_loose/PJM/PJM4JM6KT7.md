Marlies explains to Claus-Peter a method, according to which, she believes, the squares of natural numbers from 26 to 50 can be easily determined if one knows the squares of natural numbers up to 25 by heart.

"If you want to calculate the square of 42, for example, you form the complement of this number to 50 and square it. In this case, it would be 64.

You place the difference between your number and 25 in front of it, in your case, 17.

The number thus formed, 1764 in this case, is already the square of 42."

Check the correctness of this method for all numbers in the specified range!

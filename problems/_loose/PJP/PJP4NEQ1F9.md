Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ inscribed in $\odot O$, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $Q$. Through $Q$, perpendiculars are drawn to the lines $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $E$, $F$, $G$, and $H$ respectively. Prove that the lines $EH$, $BD$, and $FG$ are concurrent or parallel.

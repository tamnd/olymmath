Given a regular triangle $ OAB$ with side length $ 1$. Let $ M,\ N$ be the point which divides equally $ AB$ into three parts, $ M$ is the closest to $ A$.
Denote $ \overrightarrow{OA} = \overrightarrow{a},\ \overrightarrow{OM} = \overrightarrow{m},\ \overrightarrow{ON} = \overrightarrow{n},\ \overrightarrow{OB} = \overrightarrow{b}$.

(1) Find the real numbers $ x,\ y$ such that $ \overrightarrow{m} = x \overrightarrow{a} + y \overrightarrow{b}$.

(2) Find the dot product of $ \overrightarrow{m},\ \overrightarrow{n}$ denoted by $ (\overrightarrow{m},\ \overrightarrow{n})$.

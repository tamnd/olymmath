Peter has a $5 \times 5$ table filled with 25 different numbers. Peter selects the largest number in the table and crosses out the row and column in which this number is located. Then he selects the largest of the remaining numbers, crosses out the row and column in which it is located, and so on. Ivan has the same table and proceeds similarly, but each time he selects the smallest number. Is it possible for the sum of the numbers chosen by Ivan to be:

a) greater than the sum of the numbers chosen by Peter;

b) greater than the sum of any other five numbers from the given table, each two of which are in different rows and different columns?

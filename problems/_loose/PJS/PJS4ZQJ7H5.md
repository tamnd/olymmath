Given $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, and $n \geqslant 2$. Prove:
$$
\frac{4}{7}<\sum_{i=1}^{2 n} \frac{(-1)^{i+1}}{i}<\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$

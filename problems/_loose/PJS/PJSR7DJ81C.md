Let the sequence of positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, \cdots$ satisfy
$$
a_{n \leqslant}^{2} a_{n}-a_{n+1}, n=1,2,3, \cdots
$$

Prove: For any $n \in N$, we have $a_{n}<\frac{1}{n}$.

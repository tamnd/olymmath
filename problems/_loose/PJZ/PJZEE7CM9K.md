Show that for an equilateral triangle,

$$
\rho^{\prime}\left(\rho+\rho^{\prime}\right)=a^{2}
$$

where $a$ is the side of the triangle, $\rho$ is the radius of the inscribed circle, and $\rho^{\prime}$ is the radius of the excircle.

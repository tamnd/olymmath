In the vertices of a heptadecagon (17-sided polygon), different integers were written (one in each vertex). Then all the numbers were simultaneously replaced with new ones: each was replaced by the difference of the two following it in a clockwise direction (subtracting the next from the adjacent one). Could the product of the resulting numbers be odd?

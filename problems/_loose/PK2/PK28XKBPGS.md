Let $\Gamma_{1}, \Gamma_{2}$ and $\Gamma_{3}$ be three circles with $A ; B=\Gamma_{1} \cap \Gamma_{2}, C ; D=\Gamma_{2} \cap \Gamma_{3}$ and $E ; F=\Gamma_{3} \cap \Gamma_{1}$. Consider $P_{1}$ on $\Gamma_{1}$ and denote $P_{2}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{1} A\right)$ and $\Gamma_{2}, P_{3}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{3} C\right)$ and $\Gamma_{3}, P_{4}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{3} E\right)$ and $\Gamma_{1}, P_{5}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{4} B\right)$ and $\Gamma_{2}, P_{6}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{5} D\right)$ and $\Gamma_{3}$, and finally $P_{7}$ as the second intersection point of $\left(P_{6} F\right)$ and $\Gamma_{1}$.

Show that $P_{7}=P_{1}$.

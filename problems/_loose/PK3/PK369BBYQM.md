On the cover of a 1958 issue of the German mathematical journal titled "Archimedes," the following numerical curiosity is visible: $1958-19 \cdot 58=8591-85 \cdot 91$. Is there another $\overline{a b c d}$ number with this property, that is, for which the following holds: $\overline{a b c d}-\overline{a b} \cdot \overline{c d}=\overline{d c b a}-\overline{d c} \cdot \overline{b a} ?$

A sequence of integers $ f_0, f_1, f_2, \cdots $ is defined
by the conditions: $ f_0 = 0, f_1 = 1 $,

Find all polynomials $ W $ with integer coefficients,
having the following property: For every $ n =0, 1, 2, \cdots $
there exists an integer $ k $ such that $ W(k)= f_n $.

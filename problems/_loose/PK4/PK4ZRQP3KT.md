In the cells of a $10 \times 10$ table, the natural numbers 1, 2, ..., 99, 100 are arranged. Let's call a corner the figure that results from removing one cell from a 2x2 square. We will call a corner good if the number in its cell, which borders by sides with two other cells, is greater than the numbers in these two other cells. What is the maximum possible number of good corners? (Each corner is counted independently of how it is positioned relative to others, different corners can partially overlap). (A. Gолованов)

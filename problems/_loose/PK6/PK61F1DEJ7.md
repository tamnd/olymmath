Let $p$ be an odd prime number. Divide each side of a triangle into $p$ equal parts, and then draw the segments inside the triangle that connect the vertices of the triangle with the division points on the opposite sides. Prove that the segments have $3(p-1)^{2}$ intersection points inside the triangle.

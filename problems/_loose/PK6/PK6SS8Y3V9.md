Flatbread division. Any flatbread, regardless of its shape, can be divided into four equal parts by two mutually perpendicular cuts. In other words, for any flat region with area $ P $, one can find two mutually perpendicular lines such that each of the four quadrants they form contains a part of that region with area $ P / 4 $.

(Proving this theorem is much easier than actually dividing, say, a triangle with sides $3, 4, 5$ into four equal parts.)

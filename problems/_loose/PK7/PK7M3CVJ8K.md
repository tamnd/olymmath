6.- A convex polygon with $n$ sides is decomposed into $m$ triangles, with disjoint interiors, such that each side of these $m$ triangles is also a side of another adjacent triangle or of the given polygon. Prove that $\mathbf{m}+\mathbf{n}$ is even. Given $\mathbf{n}$ and $\mathbf{m}$, find the number of distinct sides that remain in the interior of the polygon and the number of distinct vertices that remain in that interior.

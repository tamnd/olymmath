Let $a, b, c$ be the sides of a triangle, $p$ its semi-perimeter, and $r$ and $R$ the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles, respectively. Formulate an equation with coefficients depending on $p, r, R$, whose roots are the numbers $a, b, c$. Prove the equality

$$
\frac{1}{a b}+\frac{1}{b c}+\frac{1}{a c}=\frac{1}{2 r R}
$$

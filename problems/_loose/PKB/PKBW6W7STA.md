Let $A B$ be a moving chord of length $m$ on the parabola $y=a x^{2}(a>0)$, then (I) when $m \geqslant \frac{1}{a}$ (length of the latus rectum), the minimum distance from the midpoint $M$ of $A B$ to the $x$-axis is $\frac{2 m a-1}{4 a}$; (II) when $m<\frac{1}{a}$ (length of the latus rectum), the minimum distance from the midpoint $M$ of $A B$ to the $x$-axis is $\frac{a m^{2}}{4}$.

A classic problem: On a circular city wall, 12 guards are on duty. At noon, each of them starts from their post and moves in some direction along the wall at a speed that would allow them to circle the city in one hour. If two guards meet head-on, they turn the corner and continue at the same speed in the opposite direction. Prove that exactly at one o'clock, each guard will be back at their own post.

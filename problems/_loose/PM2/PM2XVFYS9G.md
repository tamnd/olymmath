At the ends of the diameter of a circle, units are placed. Each of the resulting semicircles is divided in half, and in its middle, the sum of the numbers at the ends is written (first step). Then each of the four resulting arcs is divided in half, and in its middle, the number equal to the sum of the numbers at the ends of the arc is written (second step). This operation is performed $n$ times. Find the sum of all the written numbers.

Let $A$ be an invertible matrix in $\mathcal{M}_{4}(\mathbb{R})$, such that $\operatorname{tr} A=\operatorname{tr} A^{*} \neq 0$, where $A^{*}$ is the adjugate of $A$. Show that the matrix $A^{2}+I_{4}$ is singular if and only if there exists a non-zero matrix $B$ in $\mathcal{M}_{4}(\mathbb{R})$, such that $A B=-B A$.

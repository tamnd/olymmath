Let $n\geq 2$ be integer. Call a number $x$ as $n$-*powered number* such that $x=p^n$ for a positive integer $p$.
Answer the following questions:

(1) Prove that the product of two consecutive positive integers is not $n$-*powered number*.

(2) Prove that the product of $n$ consecutive positive integers is not $n$-*powered number*.

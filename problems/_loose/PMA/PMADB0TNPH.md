In a row, there are 10 boxes. These boxes contain balls of two colors: red and blue. In some boxes, all the balls may be of the same color; there are no empty boxes. It is known that in each subsequent box (from left to right), there are no fewer balls than in the previous one. It is also known that there are no two boxes with the same set of red and blue balls. How many blue and how many red balls are in the rightmost box, if the total number of red balls in all the boxes is 11 and the total number of blue balls is 13?

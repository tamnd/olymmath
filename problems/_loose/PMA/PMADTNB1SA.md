(PRC, 81). The cells of an $n \times n$ chessboard, where $n$ is an even number greater than 2, are colored with $n^{2} / 2$ colors such that each color is used to color exactly two cells. Prove that it is possible to place $n$ rooks on the board such that they stand on cells of different colors and do not attack each other.

In triangle $ABC$, a point $D$ is taken on side $AB$ and a point $E$ on $AC$. It turns out that $DE \parallel BC$, $AD = DE = AC$, and $BD = AE$.

Prove that the length of $BD$ is equal to the length of the side of a regular decagon inscribed in a circle of radius $R = AC$.

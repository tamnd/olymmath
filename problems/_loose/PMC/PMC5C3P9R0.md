Let $S$ be the sum of all possible values of $a \cdot c$ such that $$a^3+3ab^2-72ab+432a=4c^3$$ if $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers, $a+b > 11$, $a > b-13$, and $c \le 1000$. Find the sum of all distinct prime factors of $S$.

On the side $BC$ of triangle $ABC$, a point $D$ is taken. Circle $S_{1}$ touches segments $BE$ and $EA$ and the circumcircle, circle $S_{2}$ touches segments $CE$ and $EA$ and the circumcircle. Let $I, I_{1}, I_{2}$ and $r, r_{1}, r_{2}$ be the centers and radii of the incircle and circles $S_{1}, S_{2}$; $\varphi = \angle ADB$. Prove that the point $I$ lies on the segment $I_{1}I_{2}$, and that $I_{1}I : II_{2} = \tan^2 \frac{\varphi}{2}$. Also prove that $r = r_{1} \cos^2 \frac{\varphi}{2} + r_{2} \sin^2 \frac{\varphi}{2}$ (Thebault).

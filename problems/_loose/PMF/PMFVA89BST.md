Let the midpoints of segments $P_{1}^{\prime} P_{2}^{\prime}$, $P_{2}^{\prime} P_{3}^{\prime}$, $P_{3}^{\prime} P_{4}^{\prime}$, $P_{4}^{\prime} P_{1}^{\prime}$ be $o_{1}$, $O_{2}$, $O_{3}$, $O_{4}$. Prove that $O_{1} O_{2} O_{3} O_{4}$ is a square.

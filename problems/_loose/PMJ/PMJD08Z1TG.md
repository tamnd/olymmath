Given is a trapezoid $A B C D$. The perpendicular bisector of side $\overline{B C}$ intersects side $\overline{A D}$ at point $M$, and the perpendicular bisector of side $\overline{A D}$ intersects side $\overline{B C}$ at point $N$.

Let $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ be the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $A B N$ and $C D M$, respectively. Prove that the line $O_{1} O_{2}$ passes through the midpoint of segment $\overline{M N}$.

Two circles, one with center $O$ and radius $R$, the other with center $O^{\prime}$ and radius $R^{\prime}$ with $R < R^{\prime}$, are externally tangent at $B$. Let $(T)$ be the common tangent passing through $B$.

We draw another common tangent ($M M^{\prime}$) (where $M$ belongs to the first circle and $M^{\prime}$ to the second). This tangent intersects $(T)$ at $A$. The lines $(O A)$ and $(O^{\prime} A)$ intersect $B M$ and $B M^{\prime}$ at $D$ and $D^{\prime}$. The lines $(O O^{\prime})$ and $(M M^{\prime})$ meet at $E$.

(1) Prove that $(D D^{\prime})$ is parallel to $(M M^{\prime})$.

(2) Prove that $O A O^{\prime}$ is a right triangle at $A$.

(3) Calculate the length $E O$ in terms of $R$ and $R^{\prime}$.

(4) Calculate the length $D D^{\prime}$ in terms of $R$ and $R^{\prime}$.

Consider the set of isosceles triangles whose bases lie on a given line, one of the vertices is at a given point $A$ on this line, and the radii of the inscribed circles are equal to a given value $r$. Prove that all the lateral sides of these triangles, not passing through vertex $A$, touch one circle.

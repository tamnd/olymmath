Let $O$ be the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$. The circumcircle of $ABC$ is intersected by the angle bisectors from vertices $A, B$, and $C$ at points $F_{a}, F_{b}, F_{c}$, respectively. It is to be proven that

$$
\left.\frac{A O}{F_{a} O}+\frac{B O}{F_{b} O}+\frac{C O}{F_{c} O} \geqq 3\right]
$$

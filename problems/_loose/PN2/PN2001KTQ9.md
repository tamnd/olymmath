There are $n$ footballers at the training, some of whom are forwards and the rest are goalkeepers, practicing penalty kicks. A total of $k$ goals were scored. Prove that their coach can assign numbers from 1 to $n$ to them such that for each goal scored, the numbers assigned to the forward and the goalkeeper differ by at least $n-k$.

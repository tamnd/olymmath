Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle A = 60^\circ$ . Points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen on sides $AB$ and $AC$ , respectively, such that $BP = PQ = QC$ . Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle APQ$ passes through the projection of the orthocenter of $ABC$ onto its $A$ -median.

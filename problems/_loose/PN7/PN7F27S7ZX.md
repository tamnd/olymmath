A sequence of letters, where each letter is either $A$ , $B$ , $C$ , or $D$ , is called *alternating* if:

- between every two $A$ s there is at least one $B$ ;
- between every two $B$ s there is at least one $A$ ;
- between every two $C$ s there is at least one $D$ ;
- between every two $D$ s there is at least one $C$ .

Calculate the number of alternating sequences of length $6$ .

If the equation $\sqrt{x-p}=x$ has two distinct real roots, then the range of the real number $p$ is
(A) $p \leqslant 0$.
(B) $p<\frac{1}{4}$.
(C) $0 \leqslant p<\frac{1}{4}$.
(D) $p \geqslant \frac{1}{4}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1994)

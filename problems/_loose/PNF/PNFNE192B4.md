Solve the following problems using graphical methods.
(1) $\max z=2 x_{1}+x_{2}$
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x_{1}+4 x_{2} \leqslant 32 \\
x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 11 \\
5 x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 35 \\
x_{1}, x_{2} \geqslant 0
\end{array}\right.
$$
(2) $\max z=x_{1}+x_{2}$
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
4 x_{1}+5 x_{2} \leqslant 10 \\
5 x_{1}+2 x_{2} \leqslant 10 \\
3 x_{1}+8 x_{2} \leqslant 12 \\
x_{1}, x_{2} \geqslant 0
\end{array}\right.
$$
(3) $\max z=2 x_{1}+2 x_{2}$
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
-x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 1 \\
x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 3 \\
x_{1}, x_{2} \geqslant 0
\end{array}\right.
$$
(4) $\max z=x_{1}+x_{2}$
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
-x_{1}+2 x_{2} \leqslant 2 \\
x_{1}-x_{2} \geqslant 2 \\
x_{1}, x_{2} \geqslant 0
\end{array}\right.
$$

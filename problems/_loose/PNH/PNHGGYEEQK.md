Let $a_{n, m}$ denote the number obtained by writing down all numbers from $n$ to $m$ consecutively, where $n$ and $m$ are natural numbers, and $n > m \geq 1$. For example, the number $a_{4,2}=432$, and the number $a_{11,7}=1110987$. Prove that among such numbers, there is a number divisible by 2021.

Prove that if $F(x)$ and $G(x)$ are polynomials with coefficients $0$ and $1$ such that $$ F(x)G(x) = 1 +x + x^2 +...+ x^{n-1} $$ holds for some $n > 1$ , then one of them can be represented in the form $$ (1 +x + x^2 +...+ x^{k-1}) T(x) $$ for some $k > 1$ where $T(x)$ is a polynomial with coefficients $0$ and $1$ .

(V Senderov, M Vialiy)

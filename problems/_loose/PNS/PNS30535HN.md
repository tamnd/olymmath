The triangular pyramid $O A B C$ has side edges $O A=a, O B=b, O C=c$, and the angles $A O B, B O C, C O A$ are right. If $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles at the vertices $A, B, C$ of the base $A B C$, prove that

$$
\operatorname{ctg} \alpha: \operatorname{ctg} \beta: \operatorname{ctg} \gamma=a^{2}: b^{2}: c^{2}
$$

In $\triangle A B C$, $a, b, c$ are the sides opposite to the internal angles $A, B, C$ respectively, and satisfy $\sin A+\sqrt{3} \cos A=2$.
(1) Find the size of $A$;
(2) Now, three conditions are given: (1) $a=2$; (2) $B=45^{\circ}$; (3) $c=\sqrt{3} b$.
Select two conditions that can determine $\triangle A B C$, write down your choice and use it to find the area of $\triangle A B C$. (Only one selected scheme is required, and multiple schemes will be scored based on the first one.)

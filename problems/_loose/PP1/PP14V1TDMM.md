The carpet merchant Alibaba has a rectangular carpet of unknown dimensions. Unfortunately, his measuring tape is broken, and he has no other measuring tools. However, he discovers that if he lays the carpet flat in either of his two store rooms, each corner of the carpet meets a different wall of the room. He knows that the length and width of the carpet are both integers in feet. The two rooms have one side of the same length (unknown), and the other sides are 38 feet and 50 feet, respectively. Find the dimensions of the carpet. (1 foot = 0.3048 meters)
(30th International Mathematical Olympiad Preliminary Problem, 1989)

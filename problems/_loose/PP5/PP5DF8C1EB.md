We placed $n$ one-forint coins in a row on the table so that some of them have the head side up, and some have the tail side up. András and Béla play the following game. They take turns, and the one who moves selects a coin with the head side up, and flips this coin, as well as all the coins to the right of it. The one who cannot make a move loses. Prove that the game ends in a finite number of moves. Who has a winning strategy?

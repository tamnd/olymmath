Given positive rational numbers $a, b$. One of the roots of the quadratic polynomial $x^{2}-a x+b$ is a rational number, in its irreducible form having the form $m / n$. Prove that the denominator of at least one of the numbers $a$ and $b$ (in their irreducible form) is not less than $n^{2 / 3}$.

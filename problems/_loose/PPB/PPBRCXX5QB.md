(England, 73). A teacher and students of a certain class are asked questions. The probability that the teacher's answer will be correct is $\alpha$, and the probability of a correct answer from a student is $\beta$ or $\gamma$ depending on whether a boy or a girl is answering, respectively. The probability that the answer of a randomly selected student will coincide with the teacher's answer is $1 / 2$. Find the ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls in the class.

2xTNlo If every vertex of a polygon $V$ lies on the boundary of a polygon $W$, then $V$ is called an inscribed polygon of $W$. Prove: For each convex polygon $W$ with unit area, there exists a non-degenerate centrally symmetric inscribed convex hexagon $V$, and the area of $V$ is no less than $\frac{2}{3}$.

17-126 $D$ is a point on the base $BC$ of an isosceles acute $\triangle ABC$, then $AD, BD$, $CD$ satisfy the relation
(A) $A D^{2}=B D^{2}+C D^{2}$.
(B) $A D^{2}>B D^{2}+C D^{2}$.
(C) $2 A D^{2}=B D^{2}+C D^{2}$.
(D) $2 A D^{2}>B D^{2}+C D^{2}$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

Let $A \in M_{n}(\mathbf{R})$, where $n$ is odd. Show that if $A^{2}=O_{n}$, then for any matrix $X \in M_{n}(\mathbf{R})$ with the property that $A X=X A$, the following inequalities hold:

$$
\operatorname{det}\left(X A+X^{2}\right) \geq 0 \leq \operatorname{det}\left(X A-X^{2}\right)
$$

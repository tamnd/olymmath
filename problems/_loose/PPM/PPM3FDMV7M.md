Prove that for each natural number $N$ there exists an integer $k \geqslant 0$ such that $N$ can be written as a sum of the numbers $2^{0}, 2^{1}, 2^{2}, \ldots, 2^{k}$, each of which appears in this sum 1 or 2 times. (For example, $\left.12=2^{0}+2^{0}+2^{1}+2^{2}+2^{2}.\right)$

Does there exist constants $a, b, c$, such that the equation
$$
1 \cdot 2^{2}+2 \cdot 3^{2}+\cdots+n(n+1)^{2}=\frac{n(n+1)}{12}\left(a n^{2}+b n+c\right)
$$

holds for all natural numbers $n$? Prove your conclusion.

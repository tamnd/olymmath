Prove that there exist integers $a$ , $b$ , $c$ with $1 \le a < b < c \le 25$ and $$ S(a^6+2014) = S(b^6+2014) = S(c^6+2014) $$ where $S(n)$ denotes the sum of the decimal digits of $n$ .

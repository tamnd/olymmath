Three men, Alpha, Beta, and Gamma, working together, do a job in $6$ hours less time than Alpha alone, in $1$ hour less time than Beta alone, and in one-half the time needed by Gamma when working alone. Let $h$ be the number of hours needed by Alpha and Beta, working together to do the job. Then $h$ equals: $\text{(A)}\ \dfrac{5}{2} \text{(B)}\ \frac{3}{2} \text{(C)}\ \dfrac{4}{3} \text{(D)}\ \dfrac{5}{4} \text{(E)}\ \dfrac{3}{4}$

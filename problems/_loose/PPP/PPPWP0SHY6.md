A motorist and a cyclist set off from $A$ to $B$ simultaneously. After covering one-third of the distance, the cyclist stopped and only resumed when the motorist had one-third of the distance left to $B$. The motorist, having reached $B$ without stopping, turned back towards $A$. Who will arrive earlier: the motorist in $A$ or the cyclist in $B$?

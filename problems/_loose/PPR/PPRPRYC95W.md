Find the smallest real number $\lambda$, such that for any positive integer $n$, we have $(n,[n \sqrt{5}])<\lambda \sqrt{n}$, where $(a, b)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of the positive integers $a$ and $b$, and $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$.

Given the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ with eccentricity $e=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, the line $y=2 x-1$ intersects $C$ at points $A, B$, and $|A B|=\frac{8}{9} \sqrt{5}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) A line $l$ (with non-zero slope) passing through the point $M(2,0)$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at two different points $E, F$ (with $E$ between $F, M$). Let $\lambda=\frac{S_{\triangle O M E}}{S_{\triangle O M F}}$, find the range of $\lambda$.

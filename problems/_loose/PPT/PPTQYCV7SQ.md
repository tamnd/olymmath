The numbers $1,2,3,...,23$ are written on a board. Ana and Ben play the following game: in every move the player who has to play picks one of the remaining numbers such that at least one number consecutive to the picked number is still on the board. A player loses the game if they can no longer choose a number. If Ana starts the game, which player has a winning strategy?

Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with $AD \nparallel BC$ and let $E$ be the intersection of its diagonals.
Let $F$ be a point on segment $AB$ . Let $G$ and $H$ be points on lines $AC$ and $BD$ respectively, such that $FG \parallel BC$ and $FH \parallel AD$ . Show that as $F$ moves along segment $AB$ , the circumcircle of triangle $\triangle EGH$ passes through a fixed point different from $E$ .

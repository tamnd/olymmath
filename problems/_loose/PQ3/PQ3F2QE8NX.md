Let the function $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f(x)=a x+b, a, b \in \mathbb{R}^{*}$. If $m, n$ are distinct natural numbers such that

$$
\begin{gathered}
n>2, m>2, f(1)+f(2)+\cdots+f(m)=-n \text { and } f(1)+f(2)+\cdots+f(n)=-m \text {, calculate } \\
\frac{f(1)+f(2)+\cdots+f(m+n)}{m+n}
\end{gathered}
$$

Let $a, b$ be natural numbers such that $\operatorname{GCD}(a, b)=1$. Prove that the number of natural numbers $n$ for which the equation $a x+b y=n$ has no solution $(x, y) \in \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$ is equal to $\frac{(a+1)(b+1)}{2}-1$.

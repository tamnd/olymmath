Wrap a $5 \mathrm{~cm}$ wide strip of paper around a cardboard cylinder with a diameter of $2 \mathrm{~cm}$, 600 times to form a cylinder with a diameter of $10 \mathrm{~cm}$ (assuming the paper strip is wound into 600 concentric cylinders, whose diameters uniformly change from $2 \mathrm{~cm}$ to $10 \mathrm{~cm}$. In meters $(\mathrm{m})$, the length of the paper strip is
(A) $36 \pi$.
(B) $45 \pi$.
(C) $60 \pi$.
(D) $72 \pi$.
(E) $90 \pi$.
(38th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1987)

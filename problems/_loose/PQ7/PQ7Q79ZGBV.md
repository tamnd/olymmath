Three pulleys with parallel axes and identical radii $r=2$ cm must be connected by a flat belt drive. The distance between the axes of rotation of pulleys $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ is 12 cm, and the distance between the axes of rotation of pulleys $O_{1}$ and $O_{3}$ is 10 cm. The distance from the axis $O_{3}$ to the plane in which the axes $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ are located is 8 cm. Determine the length of the belt for the drive, which is made by sewing the ends of an inextensible rubberized cord (assume that the length of the belt is equal to the length of this cord). Will a cord 54 cm long always be sufficient for its manufacture?

A square $100 \times 100$ is divided into three vertical strips. The width of the first (left) strip is 19 cells, and the second is 70 cells. In the first row, the numbers from 1 to 100 are written in sequence. In the second row, the numbers are written starting from the left with the numbers from the third strip of the first row without changing the order, then from the second strip, and finally from the first strip, also without changing the order. The same process is repeated to fill the third row from the second row, and so on, until the entire square is filled. Prove that in each column, all numbers from 1 to 100 appear.

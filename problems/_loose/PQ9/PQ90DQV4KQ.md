Given the function $f(x), x \in \mathbf{R}$ satisfies $f(x)+f(y)=2 f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right) f\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)$, and $f(0) \neq 0$, $f(1)=0$.
(1) Prove that $f(x)$ is a periodic function;
(2) When $n \in \mathbf{N}(n=0,1,2, \cdots)$, find the analytical expression for $f(n)$.

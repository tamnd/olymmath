Prove that for any positive integer $a_{1}>1$, there exists a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3} \cdots$, such that for any $k \geqslant 1$, the sum $a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{k}^{2}$ is divisible by the sum $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{k}$.
(21st All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, 1995)

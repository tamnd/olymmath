A pioneer group collected waste paper; the entire proceeds were transferred to the solidarity account. The pioneers formed two brigades, with each pioneer in the group belonging to exactly one of these brigades. The following is known about the collection results:

(1) Each pioneer in Brigade $A$ collected exactly $13 \mathrm{~kg}$, except for one who only brought $6 \mathrm{~kg}$.

(2) Each pioneer in Brigade $B$ collected exactly $10 \mathrm{~kg}$, except for one who only brought exactly $5 \mathrm{~kg}$.

(3) Brigade $A$ collected the same amount in total as Brigade $B$.

(4) The entire pioneer group collected more than $100 \mathrm{~kg}$ but less than $600 \mathrm{~kg}$ of waste paper.

a) How many pioneers belonged to each brigade in total?

b) How many Marks could the pioneer group transfer to the solidarity account if the scrap dealer paid 0.15 Marks per kg of waste paper?

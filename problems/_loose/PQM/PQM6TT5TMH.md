Two congruent heptagonal pyramids are placed with their bases overlapping to form a top-shaped polyhedron $P$, the common face being $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{7}$, and the other two vertices being $B_{1} 、 B_{2}$. Color any line segment between any two points of the polyhedron $P$, except the sides of heptagon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{7}$, either red or blue. Prove: There exist three vertices of $P$ such that the three line segments connecting them are of the same color.

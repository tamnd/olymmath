In an acute $\triangle A B C$, point $D$ is on side $B C$, and $A D$ bisects $\angle B A C, D F \perp A C$ at point $F, D E \perp A B$ at point $E, B F$ and $C E$ intersect at point $K$. Prove: $A K \perp B C .$
(2015, Singapore Mathematical Olympiad)

(1)For any $1\geq i<j\geq100$ , a_i+a_j\neq201.
(2) $\sum_{i=1}^{100}a_i=10080$ .
Prove that the number of odd numbers in $G$ is a multiple of $4$ , and the sum the square of all numbers in $G$ is fixed.

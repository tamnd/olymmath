Suppose that the numbers $m_{1}, \ldots, m_{n}$ are pairwise coprime. Prove that any proper fraction of the form $\frac{c}{m_{1} \ldots m_{n}}$ can be represented as an algebraic sum of proper fractions of the form $\frac{n_{i}}{m_{i}}(i=1, \ldots, n)$.

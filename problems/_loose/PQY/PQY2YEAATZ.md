$m < n$ are positive integers. Let $p=\frac{n^2+m^2}{\sqrt{n^2-m^2}}$.

**(a)** Find three pairs of positive integers $(m,n)$ that make $p$ prime.

**(b)** If $p$ is prime, then show that $p \equiv 1 \pmod 8$.

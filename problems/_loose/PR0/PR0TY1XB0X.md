In the city, there are 10 parallel streets running from north to south, and another 10 streets running from west to east, all forming a $9 \times 9$ square. At each intersection, there is a square (a total of 100 squares). The distance from each square to the next along any street is 1 km. A pharmacy within walking distance is defined as a pharmacy that can be reached by walking no more than 3 km along the streets. The Ministry of Health wants to install pharmacies on some squares so that any person walking along any street, regardless of the direction, would always be getting closer to some pharmacy within walking distance (i.e., the distance between them is decreasing). Would 12 pharmacies be enough for this?

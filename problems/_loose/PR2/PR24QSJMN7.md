Once, Karlson and Winnie the Pooh were competing in the speed of eating honey and jam. Karlson, a great specialist in jam, eats a jar of jam in 2 minutes, while Winnie the Pooh manages to finish the same jar in a whole 7 minutes. However, Winnie the Pooh deals with a pot of honey in 3 minutes, while it takes Karlson 5 minutes to do so. In total, they had 10 jars of jam and 10 pots of honey. How many minutes did they take to eat everything, given that they started and finished eating at the same time? (Each eats a pot of honey or a jar of jam in its entirety).

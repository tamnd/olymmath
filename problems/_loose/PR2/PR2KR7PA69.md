Several numbers are written on the blackboard. If the arithmetic mean of two or more of these numbers does not equal any of these numbers, this mean can be added to the blackboard. Prove that starting from the numbers 0 and 1, the following can be written according to the above rule: (1) $\frac{1}{5} ;$ (2) any rational number between 0 and 1.

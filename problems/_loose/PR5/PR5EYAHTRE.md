$$
x_{n}=1+\rho \cos \alpha+\rho^{2} \cos 2 \alpha+\ldots+\rho^{n} \cos n \alpha
$$

where $0<\rho<1, n=1,2, \ldots$. Find $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}$.

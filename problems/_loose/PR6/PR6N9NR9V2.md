A short-sighted wise man who can only see objects at a distance of less than $1 \mathrm{~m}$, made the following bet: If at any point at a distance $d$ from him an object is placed, then he (under the condition that after each step of $1 \mathrm{~m}$ he is told whether he has moved closer or farther from the object) in a finite number of steps will find the object, and the number of steps taken will certainly be less than $\frac{3 d}{2}+7$. It is considered that the object is found when the wise man can see it. Prove that the wise man will win the bet.

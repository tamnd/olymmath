Let $p$ be a natural number greater than or equal to 2 and let $(M, \cdot)$ be a finite monoid such that $a^{p} \neq a$, for any $a \in M \backslash\{e\}$, where $e$ is the neutral element of $M$. Show that $(M, \cdot)$ is a group.

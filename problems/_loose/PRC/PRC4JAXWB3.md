We have placed $n$ ants on the edges of a cube with edge length 1. For any positive real number $d$, we say that two ants are at a distance $d$ if the first ant must travel a distance of at least $d$ to reach the second ant by moving only along the edges of the cube.

1. Suppose that $n=13$. Prove that among the 13 ants, there exist two ants at a distance of at most 1.
2. Suppose that $n=9$. Prove that among the 9 ants, there exist two ants at a distance of at most 1.

The forty-fifth question: Let $S(n)$ denote the sum of the digits of the positive integer $n$ in decimal notation. Find all positive integers $k$ such that there exists a positive constant $c_{k}$, depending only on $k$, for which any positive integer $n$ satisfies $S(k n) \geq c_{k} S(n)$.

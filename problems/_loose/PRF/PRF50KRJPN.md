a) Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be pairwise distinct positive numbers, $n \geqslant 3$. Prove that among them, one can choose two numbers $x_{i}$ and $x_{j}$, for which

$$
0<\frac{x_{i}-x_{j}}{1+x_{i} x_{j}}<\tan \frac{\pi}{2(n-1)}
$$

b) Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be pairwise distinct numbers, $n \geqslant 3$. Prove that among them, one can choose two numbers $x_{i}$ and $x_{j}$, for which

$$
0<\frac{x_{i}-x_{j}}{1+x_{i} x_{j}} \leqslant \tan \frac{\pi}{n}
$$

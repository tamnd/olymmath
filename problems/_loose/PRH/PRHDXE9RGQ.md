In a quadrilateral $ABCD$ of arbitrary shape, the midpoint of diagonal $AC$ is $M$, and the midpoint of diagonal $BD$ is $N$. A line through $M$ parallel to $BD$ and a line through $N$ parallel to $AC$ intersect at point $O$. Show that if point $O$ is connected to the midpoints of the sides of the quadrilateral, the quadrilateral is divided into four regions of equal area.

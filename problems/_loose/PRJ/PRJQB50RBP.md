Let $a, b, c$ be integers, each between 1 and 2021 (inclusive), that satisfy the equation

$$
\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}=\sqrt{a+c \sqrt{b}} .
$$

How many distinct values of $c$ are possible?
(A) 130
(B) 132
(C) 133
(D) 1936
(E) 2025

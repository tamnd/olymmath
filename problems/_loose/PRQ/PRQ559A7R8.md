This game is played on an $8 \times 8$ chessboard with two kings. The columns are numbered from $A$ to $H$ from left to right, and the rows are numbered from 1 to 8 from bottom to top. The white king plays first and starts at the bottom left (at $A 1$), the black king starts at $A8$. The goal of the white king is to reach one of the squares $F 8, G 8$ or $H 8$, the goal of the black king is to prevent this. (The kings follow the movement rules of chess: on their turn, a king moves one square in any of the eight directions, and is not allowed to move into the range of the enemy king).

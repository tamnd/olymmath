Let $a,b, c$ be real numbers with $a+b+c = 2018$.
Suppose $x, y$, and $z$ are the distinct positive real numbers which are satisfied $a = x^2 - yz - 2018, b = y^2 - zx - 2018$ , and $c = z^2 - xy - 2018$.
Compute the value of the following expression $P = \frac{\sqrt{a^3 + b^3 + c^3 - 3abc}}{x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz}$

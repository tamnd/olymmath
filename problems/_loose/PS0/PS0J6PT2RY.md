A convex $n$-hedral angle cuts out a spherical $n$-gon on a sphere of radius $R$ with the center at the vertex of the angle. Prove that its area is equal to $R^{2}(\sigma - (n-2) \pi)$, where $\sigma$ is the sum of the dihedral angles.

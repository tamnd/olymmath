Prove that

a) for $p \geq 0$ the graph of the polynomial $x^{3}+p x+q$ intersects each horizontal line at exactly one point;

b) for $p<0$ the graph intersects some horizontal lines at three points;
c) for $p<0$ the graph has one minimum and one maximum;

d) the abscissas of the points of minimum and maximum are opposite.

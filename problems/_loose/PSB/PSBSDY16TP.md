In the vertices of a regular 1983-gon, the numbers $1,2, \ldots, 1983$ are arranged. Any axis of symmetry divides the numbers not lying on it into two sets. We call an arrangement "good" relative to a given axis of symmetry if each number in one set is greater than its symmetric counterpart. Does there exist an arrangement that is "good" relative to any axis of symmetry?

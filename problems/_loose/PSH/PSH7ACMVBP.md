Let's consider the question of which triangles $A B C$ with acute angles at vertices $A$ and $B$ have the following property: If we draw three lines through the midpoint of the altitude $z$ from vertex $C$ parallel to the sides of triangle $A B C$, these lines will intersect the sides at six points lying on a single circle.

a) Show that every right-angled triangle $A B C$ with the right angle at vertex $C$ satisfies this property.

b) Explain why no other triangle $A B C$ satisfies this property.

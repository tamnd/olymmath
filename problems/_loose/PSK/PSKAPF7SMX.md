The vertices of a square sheet of paper are $ A$, $ B$, $ C$, $ D$. The sheet is folded in a way that the point $ D$ is mapped to the point $ D'$ on the side $ BC$. Let $ A'$ be the image of $ A$ after the folding, and let $ E$ be the intersection point of $ AB$ and $ A'D'$. Let $ r$ be the inradius of the triangle $ EBD'$. Prove that $ r=A'E$.

Three points $A, B$, and $C$ are located on the same line ($B$ is between $A$ and $C$). Take an arbitrary circle centered at $B$ and denote by $M$ the point of intersection of the tangents drawn from $A$ and $C$ to this circle. Find the geometric locus of points $M$ such that the points of tangency of $A M$ and $C M$ with the circle lie inside the segments $A M$ and $C M$.

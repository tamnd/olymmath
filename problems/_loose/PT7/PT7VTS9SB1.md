In the isosceles triangle $ABC$, $AC = BC$. A point $P$ is given on the side $AB$ such that $\angle ACP = 30^\circ$, and a point $Q$ is given outside the triangle such that

$$
\angle CPQ = \angle CPA + \angle APQ = 78^\circ.
$$

The angles of triangles $ABC$ and $QPB$ are integers when expressed in degrees. What are the angles of these two triangles?

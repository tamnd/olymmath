Let $m, n \geqslant 2$ be two integers. Alice and Bob are playing the following dangerous game: they have in front of them a chocolate bar consisting of $m \times n$ squares, the one at the bottom left being poisoned. Alice starts to play and the two players then alternate turns. On each turn, the player whose turn it is chooses one of the remaining squares and eats that square as well as all the squares above and to its right. The loser is the player who eats the poisoned square. Who has a winning strategy?

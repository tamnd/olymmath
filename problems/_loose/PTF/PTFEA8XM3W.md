In a VEB, it is necessary to plan long-term qualification measures for each of the workers Arnold, Bauer, Donath, Funke, Große, Hansen, Krause, and Lehmann. Within four weeks, from 1.11.1976 (Monday) to 27.11.1976 (Saturday), each worker can be delegated to a three-day training course (either from Monday to Wednesday or from Thursday to Saturday).

To ensure that ongoing production is not jeopardized, a release from work can only occur at certain times:

(1) Arnold cannot participate in the third week.

(2) Bauer is indispensable in the first half of each week, but also not from 11. to 13.11. and not in the second half of the fourth week.

(3) Donath can only go in the same week as Lehmann.

(4) Funke can only be released in the first or second week.

(5) Große can only be considered from 4. to 6.11. or from 18. to 20.11.76 or in the second or fourth week, each in the second half.

(6) Hansen can only be deployed in the second or third week, each in the second half, but not in the week when Funke goes to the training.

(7) Krause can only be sent to the training in the first week or from 22. to 24.11.76.

(8) Lehmann can only participate in the first half of each week.

Determine all possibilities to implement the planned qualification measures under these conditions!

For each of the workers, specify the time during which they are delegated to the training!

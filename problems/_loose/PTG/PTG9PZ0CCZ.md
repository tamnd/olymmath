The symbol * denotes an operation defined for natural numbers $x ; y$ as follows: $x * y=(x+y)^{2}$.

It is to be checked whether

a) the operation is commutative, i.e., whether $x * y=y * x$ holds,

b) the operation is associative, i.e., whether $(x * y) * z=x *(y * z)$ holds,

c) the operation is distributive with respect to addition, i.e., whether $x *(y+z)=(x * y)+(x * z)$ holds,

d) there exists a neutral element for the operation, i.e., whether there is an element $e$ such that $x * e=$ $e * x=x$ for every $x$ and $e$ is a natural number.

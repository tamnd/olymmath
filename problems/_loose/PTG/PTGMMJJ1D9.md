Let $A$ and $B$ be fixed points, $\lambda$ and $\mu$ be fixed numbers. Choose an arbitrary point $X$ and define the point $P$ by the equation $\overrightarrow{X P}=\lambda \overrightarrow{X A}+\mu \overrightarrow{X B}$. Prove that the position of point $P$ does not depend on the choice of point $X$ if and only if $\lambda+\mu=1$. Also prove that in this case, point $P$ lies on the line $A B$.

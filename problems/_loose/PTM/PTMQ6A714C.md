Consider the isosceles trapezoid $A B C D$ with $A B \parallel C D, A C \cap B D=\{O\}, A B=40 \mathrm{~cm}$, $C D=\frac{1}{4} A B$ and the diagonals are perpendicular. On the plane of the trapezoid, the perpendicular $O M$ is raised such that $O M=5 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~cm}$.

a) Calculate the area of triangle $M C D$.

b) Determine $\sin (\angle(B M, A D))$.

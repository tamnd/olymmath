Let the convex polyhedron $P_{1}$ have 9 vertices $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{9}, P_{\text {, }}$ be the polyhedron obtained by translating $P_{1}$ such that $A_{1} \rightarrow A_{i}$ $(i=2,3, \cdots, 9)$. Prove: Among $P_{1}, P_{2}$, $\cdots, P_{9}$, there are at least two polyhedra that have at least one common interior point.

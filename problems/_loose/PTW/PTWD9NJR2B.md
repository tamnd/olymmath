A pin is stuck vertically into each node of an infinite grid paper. A needle of length $l$ lies on the paper parallel to the grid lines. For which $l$ can the needle be rotated by $90^{\circ}$ without removing it from the plane of the paper? The needle can be moved in any way on the plane, but it must pass between the pins; the thickness of the pins and the needle can be neglected.

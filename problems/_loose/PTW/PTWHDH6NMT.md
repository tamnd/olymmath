In Anchuria, a day can be either clear, when the sun shines all day, or rainy, when it rains all day. And if today is not like yesterday, the Anchurians say that the weather has changed today. Once, Anchurian scientists established that January 1 is always clear, and each subsequent day in January will be clear only if the weather changed exactly a year ago on that day. In 2015, January in Anchuria was quite varied: sunny one day, rainy the next. In which year will the weather in January first change in exactly the same way as it did in January 2015?

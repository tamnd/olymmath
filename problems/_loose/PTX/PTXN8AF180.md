Two players, A and B, take turns moving a piece on a $1994 \times 1994$ chessboard. A can only move the piece horizontally to an adjacent square, while B can only move it vertically to an adjacent square. However, neither player can move the piece into a square it has already visited. The player who cannot make a move when it is their turn loses. A starts by placing the piece in any square and makes the first move. Prove that A has a winning strategy.

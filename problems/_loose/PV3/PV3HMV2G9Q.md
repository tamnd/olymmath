From one bacterium, $n$ bacteria were obtained as follows. First, the bacterium divided into two, then one of the two resulting bacteria divided into two, then one of the three resulting bacteria divided into two, and so on. Prove that for any natural number $a \leqslant n / 2$ there was a moment when there existed a bacterium, the number of descendants of which among the final $n$ bacteria was not less than $a$ and not more than $2 a-1$.

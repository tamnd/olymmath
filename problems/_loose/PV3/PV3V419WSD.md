Test $\mathbf{E}$ places $2^{n}$ chess pieces on a circle and makes adjustments: if two adjacent pieces are of the same color, a piece is placed between them; if they are of different colors, a white piece is placed between them. After all pieces are placed, the original pieces are removed, which is called one adjustment.
Prove: After a finite number of adjustments, all the pieces on the circle will be black.
(1st All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad)

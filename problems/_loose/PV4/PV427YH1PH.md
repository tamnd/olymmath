Let $\gamma$ and $\gamma^{\prime}$ be two circles that are externally tangent at a point $A$. Let $t$ be a common tangent to both circles, touching $\gamma$ (resp. $\gamma^{\prime}$) at $T$ (resp. $T^{\prime}$). We call $M$ the midpoint of $\left[T T^{\prime}\right]$.

Show that $M T=M A=M T^{\prime}$.

A triangle $A B C$ and a point $T$ lie in a plane, so that the triangles $T A B$, $T B C, T C A$ have the same circumference and the same area. Prove that

a) if $T$ is in the interior of the triangle $A B C$, then $A B C$ is equilateral,

b) if $T$ is not in the interior of the triangle $A B C$, then $A B C$ is rectangular.

A line is drawn through the midpoints of segments $A D$ and $B C$, intersecting the sides of the angle at points $M$ and $N$ ($M, A$ and $B$ lie on one side of the angle; $N, C$ and $D$ - on the other). Prove that

$O M: O N=A B: C D$.

(Iran MO 2018 Round 3 ) Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $D, E,$ and $F$ be the points of tangency of the incircle with the sides $[BC], [CA],$ and $[AB]$, respectively. Let $P$ be a point such that $PF = FB$, the lines $(FP)$ and $(AC)$ are parallel, and the points $P$ and $C$ are in the same half-plane delimited by the line $(AB)$. Let $Q$ be a point such that $QE = EC$, the lines $(EQ)$ and $(AB)$ are parallel, and the points $Q$ and $B$ are in the same half-plane delimited by the line $(AC)$. Show that the points $P, D,$ and $Q$ are collinear.

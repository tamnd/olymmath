Given sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ satisfy: for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*},\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, the number of terms not greater than $n$ is exactly $b_{n}$, and the number of terms in $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ not greater than $n$ is exactly $a_{n}$.
(1) If $a_{1}=b_{1}$, find $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$;
(2) If $a_{1}=b_{1}+2014$, find $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$.

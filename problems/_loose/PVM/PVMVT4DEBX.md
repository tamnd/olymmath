Given an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB = BC$. In the circumcircle $\Omega$ of triangle $ABC$, a diameter $CC'$ is drawn. A line passing through point $C'$ parallel to $BC$ intersects segments $AB$ and $AC$ at points $M$ and $P$ respectively. Prove that $M$ is the midpoint of segment $C'P$.

(B. Obukhov)

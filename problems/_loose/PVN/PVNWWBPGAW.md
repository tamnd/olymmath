Snow White and the seven dwarfs live in the forest. In any consecutive 16 days, some of the dwarfs work in the diamond mine, while the remaining dwarfs pick strawberries in the forest. No dwarf does both jobs on the same day, and in any two days (not necessarily consecutive) at least three dwarfs do these two jobs. Additionally, on the first day, all seven dwarfs work in the mine. Prove: In these 16 days, there is one day when all seven dwarfs pick strawberries in the forest. ${ }^{[2]}$
(2013, European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad)

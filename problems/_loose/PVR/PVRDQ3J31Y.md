Which of the following statements do not depend on the parallel postulate?
1) The sum of two angles of a triangle is always less than $180^{\circ}$.
2) Two lines cut by a third cannot intersect on the side of the transversal where the sum of the interior angles on the same side is greater than or equal to $180^{\circ}$.
3) A line passing in the plane of two parallel lines and intersecting one of them also intersects the other.
4) Through an external point $A$, a line parallel to a given line $a$ can always be drawn.

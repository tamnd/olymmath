Two circles of radii $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$ touch a circle $O$ of radius $r$ externally (internally) at points, the distance between which is $m$. Prove that the length $t_{12}$ of the common external tangent of the first two circles is given by the formula:

$$
t_{12}=\frac{m}{r} \sqrt{\left(r \pm r_{1}\right)\left(r \pm r_{2}\right)}
$$

(the minus sign corresponds to external tangency).

In triangle $A B C$, it holds that $|A C| \neq|B C|$. Let $S$ be the midpoint of side $A B$, $D$ the intersection of side $A B$ and the angle bisector of $\angle A C B$, and $E$ the reflection of point $D$ over point $S$. The circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ intersects line $C D$ at points $C$ and $F$, and line $C S$ at points $C$ and $G$. Prove that points $E, G, F$, and $S$ lie on the same circle.

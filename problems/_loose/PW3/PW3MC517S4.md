The sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)$ is defined by the conditions:

$$
x_{1}=\frac{1}{2}, x_{n+1}=\sqrt{\frac{1-\sqrt{1-x_{n}^{2}}}{2}}
$$

Find a formula for $x_{n}$.

A wheel with a diameter of $5 \text{ cm}$ is connected to another wheel via a transmission belt. The first wheel rotates at 1000 revolutions per minute. What diameter should the second wheel have to rotate at 200 revolutions per minute?

$\begin{array}{llll}\text { (A) } 20 \text{ cm} & \text { (B) } 25 \text{ cm} & \text { (C) } 27 \text{ cm} & \text { (D) } 50 \text{ cm}\end{array}$

(E) it depends on the distance between the axes of the wheels.

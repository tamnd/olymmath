Let's derive the Pythagorean theorem from the following two starting points (separately).

I. Rotate the right-angled triangle $90^{\circ}$ around the right-angle vertex, and consider the area of the (concave) quadrilateral whose vertices are the original and new positions of the hypotenuse endpoints.

II. Use the fact that the area of any triangle is equal to the product of the semiperimeter and the radius of the inscribed circle, and that the radius of the inscribed circle in a right-angled triangle is equal to the difference between the semiperimeter and the hypotenuse.

In each derivation, also write down which previous theorems our derivation relies on (to avoid using a theorem that might rely on the Pythagorean theorem).

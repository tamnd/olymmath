\section*{Task 2 - 011042}

On a river with a constant flow velocity $v$, a motorboat travels downstream to a target that is a distance $s$ away from the starting point, and then back again.

Another motorboat travels with the same own speed to a target that is also at a distance $s$, but exactly perpendicular to the direction of the current, and then back again.

a) How much pure driving time do the two boats require?

b) What result is obtained for $s=250 \mathrm{~m}, v=150 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{min}}$ and $c=250 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{min}}$ ?

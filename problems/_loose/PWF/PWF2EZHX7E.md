Find the largest $C \in \mathbb{R}$ such that

$$\frac{x+z}{(x-z)^2} +\frac{x+w}{(x-w)^2} +\frac{y+z}{(y-z)^2}+\frac{y+w}{(y-w)^2} + \sum_{cyc} \frac{1}{x} \ge \frac{C}{x+y+z+w}$$

where $x,y,z,w \in \mathbb{R^+}$.

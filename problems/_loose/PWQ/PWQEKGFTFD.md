(Kürschák 1980). Let $n$ be a positive odd integer. Show that there exist positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that

$$
\frac{4}{n}=\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}
$$

if and only if $n$ has a prime divisor congruent to 1 modulo 4.

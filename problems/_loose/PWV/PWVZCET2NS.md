Let $A B C D$ be a circumscribed quadrilateral. Take $(g)$ a line passing through $A$, it intersects the interior of the segment $[B C]$ at $M$ and $(D C)$ at $N$. We denote $I_{1}, I_{2}$ and $I_{3}$ the centers of the inscribed circles of the triangles $A B M, M C N$ and $A D N$. Show that the orthocenter of the triangle $I_{1} I_{2} I_{3}$ is on the line $(g)$.

In one operation, you can either add 9 to a number or erase the digit 1 in it at any position.
Can you get the number $A+1$ from any natural number $A$ using such operations?
(If a 1 is erased at the beginning of the number, and it is immediately followed by zeros, these zeros are also erased.)

If we combine the two amounts of water, we get 8.5 liters. We also know that $\frac{4}{5}$ of the capacity of the first vessel is 6.2 liters greater than $\frac{3}{4}$ of the capacity of the second vessel.

Calculate the capacities of the two vessels!

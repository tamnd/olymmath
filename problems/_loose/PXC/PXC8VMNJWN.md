In $\triangle ABC$, $AE$ and $AF$ are the internal and external angle bisectors, respectively, intersecting the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $E'$ and $F'$. Prove:
(1) $AB \cdot AC = AE \cdot AE' = AF \cdot AF'$;
(2) $AE^2 = AB \cdot AC - EB \cdot EC$;
(3) $AF^2 = FB \cdot FC - AB \cdot AC$.

In a school, $n$ students are selected to join the school's math discussion group. In one of the discussion sessions, they discuss three problems: $A$, $B$, and $C$. It is known that each student discusses one of the problems with every other student in the group, and each pair of students discusses only one problem. If at least three students discuss the same problem with each other, find the minimum value of $n$, and provide a proof.

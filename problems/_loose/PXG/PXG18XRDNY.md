Consider the trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB \| CD, AD \neq CD$ and $AB = AD + DC$. Let $M$ be the point of intersection of the angle bisectors of angles $A$ and $D$. Prove that:

a) points $A, M, C$ are not collinear;

b) the area $[ADCM] = \text{area}[AMB]$.

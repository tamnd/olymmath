1000 different positive numbers are written in a row in ascending order. Vasya divided these numbers into 500 pairs of adjacent numbers and found the sums of the numbers in all pairs. Petya divided these same numbers into 500 pairs such that there are exactly three other numbers between the numbers in each pair, and also found the sums of the numbers in all pairs. Prove that the product of the sums found by Petya is greater than the product of the sums found by Vasya. (S. Berlov)

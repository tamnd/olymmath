Consider a triangle $A B C$, where $|\angle B A C|<60^{\circ}$. The image of point $B$ under reflection across the line $A C$ is denoted as $D$, the image of $C$ under reflection across $A B$ is denoted as $E$, and the image of $B$ under reflection across $A D$ is denoted as $F$. Prove that $|C F|=|D E|$.

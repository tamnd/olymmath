G66 $(36-1$, Bulgaria) Let $A, B, C$, $D$ be four distinct points on a line in that order, and let the two circles with diameters $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at $X$ and $Y$. The line $X Y$ intersects $B C$ at $Z$. If $P$ is a point on the line $X Y$ different from $Z$, the line $C P$ intersects the circle with diameter $A C$ at $C$ and $M$, and the line $B P$ intersects the circle with diameter $B D$ at $B$ and $N$. Prove that $A M$, $D N$, and $X Y$ are concurrent.

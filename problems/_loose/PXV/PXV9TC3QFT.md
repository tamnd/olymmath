A circle $k$ with center $O$ and diameter $A B$ is given. The points $C$ and $D$ are moving along the arc $\widehat{A B}$ so that $C$ is between $B$ and $D$ and if $\angle B O C=2 \beta$ and $\angle A O D=2 \alpha$, then $\tan \alpha=\tan \beta+\frac{3}{2}$. Prove that the lines, which are perpendicular to $C D$ and divide $C D$ in ratio 1:4 measured from $C$, pass through a fixed point of the given circle.

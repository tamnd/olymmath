Consider a finite set $M$ of unit squares on the plane. Their sides are parallel to the coordinate axes (it is allowed for the squares to intersect). It is known that for any pair of squares, the distance between their centers does not exceed 2. Prove that there exists a unit square (not necessarily from the set $M$) with sides parallel to the axes, intersecting at least one point with each square in the set $M$.

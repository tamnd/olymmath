Peti, the young prince, took on the care of a vegetable garden. They measured the width and length of the rectangular garden, from which they got a rounded area, and the result had two zeros in it. Peti, who is a good calculator, while writing in his diary at home, could not find the slip of paper with the calculation. However, he remembered that the three-digit length of the garden (measured in rounded meters) multiplied by the two-digit width (and the digits of the multiplier starting from the tens) resulted in the first partial product being a three-digit number ending in 1, and the second being a four-digit number ending in 7. After a bit of a brain teaser, he was able to finish his diary. Calculate for yourselves as well, how wide and how long the vegetable garden is.

Given a real number $k \in\left(0, \frac{1}{2}\right), a_{0}, b_{0}$ are any real numbers in the interval $(0,1)$. The sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are defined as follows for $n \geqslant 0$:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}+1}{2}, \\
b_{n+1}=b_{n}^{k},
\end{array}
$$

Prove: There exists a positive integer $N$, such that for any positive integer $n>N$, we have $a_{n}<b_{n}$.

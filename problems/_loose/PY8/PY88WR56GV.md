In a bag, there are chips of two colors, blue and green. The probability of drawing two chips of the same color is equal to the probability of drawing two chips of different colors (drawing one chip after another without replacement). It is known that there are more blue chips than green chips, and the total number of chips is more than 2 but less than 50. How many blue chips can there be in the bag? Find all answers.

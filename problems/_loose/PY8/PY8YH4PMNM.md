In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, it is known that points $A_{1}(-2,0), A_{2}(2,0)$, and a moving point $P(x, y)$ satisfies the product of the slopes of lines $A_{1} P$ and $A_{2} P$ is $-\frac{3}{4}$. Let the trajectory of point $P$ be curve $C$.
(1) Find the equation of $C$;
(2) Suppose point $M$ is on the line $x=4$, and two lines passing through $M$ intersect $C$ at points $A, B$ and $P, Q$ respectively, and $|M A| \cdot|M B| = |M P| \cdot|M Q|$. Find the sum of the slopes of lines $A B$ and $P Q$.

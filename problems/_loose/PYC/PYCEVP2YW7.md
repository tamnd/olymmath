In triangle $ABC$, we draw the altitude from vertex $A$, which intersects side $BC$ at point $D$. Draw a circle that is tangent to side $BC$ at $D$, and intersects side $AB$ at points $M, N$, and side $AC$ at points $P, Q$. Prove that

$$
\frac{\overline{A M}+\overline{A N}}{\overline{A C}}=\frac{\overline{A P}+\overline{A Q}}{\overline{A B}}
$$

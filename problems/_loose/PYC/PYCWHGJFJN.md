Let $A B C$ be a semicircle. From point $B$, a perpendicular $B D$ is dropped onto the diameter $A C$, and on segments $A D$ and $D C$ as diameters, two semicircles $A F D$ and $D H C$ are described. The area of the obtained arbelos (shoemaker's knife) $A F D H C B$ is equal to the area of a circle with diameter $D B$.

A provincial TV station has $n$ ad insertion moments on a certain day, broadcasting a total of $m$ ads. The first time, one ad and $\frac{1}{8}$ of the remaining $(m-1)$ ads were broadcast, the second time, two ads and $\frac{1}{8}$ of the remaining ads were broadcast, and so on, following this pattern for ad insertion. In the last moment, the $n$-th time, the remaining $n$ ads were broadcast $(n>1)$. How many ad insertion moments were there that day? And find the number of ads $m$.

From a point $S$ outside a circle $\gamma$ of radius 1, draw a tangent line to $\gamma$ and let $T$ be the point of tangency. As a point $P$ varies on $\gamma$, the centroid of the triangle $P S T$ describes a curve $\gamma^{\prime}$. What is the ratio between the length of $\gamma^{\prime}$ and that of $\gamma$?
(A) $\frac{1}{\pi}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}$
(C) $\frac{1}{3}$
(D) $\frac{4}{3} \pi$
(E) depends on the position of $S$.

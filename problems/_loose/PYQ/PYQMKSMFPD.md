(18th Asia Pacific Mathematical Olympiad) Given that $p(p \geqslant 5)$ is a prime number. From a $p \times p$ chessboard, select $p$ squares such that the selected squares are not in the same row (they can be in the same column), and let the number of such selections be $r$. Prove: $p^{5} \mid r$.

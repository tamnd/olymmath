On the medians of an equilateral triangle $ABC$, points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ are taken respectively. Prove that: 1) these points can be chosen such that the line $C_1 A_1$ passes through point $A$; the line $A_1 B_1$ - through point $B$, the line $B_1 C_1$ - through point $C$; 2) the triangle $A_1 B_1 C_1$ is equilateral; 3) the point $C_1$ divides the median $M_1 M_2$ in the ratio $(1+\sqrt{5}): 2$. Investigate the solution under the condition that the points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ are on the extensions of the medians.

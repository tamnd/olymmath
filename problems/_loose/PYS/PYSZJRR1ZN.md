Given that the function $g(x)=\cos x$ is transformed by stretching all points on its graph vertically by a factor of 2 (keeping the horizontal coordinates unchanged), and then shifting the resulting graph to the right by $\frac{\pi}{2}$ units to obtain the graph of the function $y=f(x)$, and that the equation $f(x)+g(x)=m$ has two distinct solutions $\alpha, \beta$ in the interval $[0,2 \pi)$.
(1) Find the range of real numbers $m$ that satisfy the condition;
(2) Find $\cos (\alpha-\beta)$ (expressed in terms of $m$).

Let $A B C$ be a triangle. We denote $P$ as the symmetric point of $B$ with respect to $(A C)$ and $Q$ as the symmetric point of $C$ with respect to $(A B)$.

Let $T$ be the intersection between $(P Q)$ and the tangent at $A$ to the circumcircle of $(A P Q)$.

Show that the symmetric point of $T$ with respect to $A$ lies on $(B C)$.

A circle has four points $A, B, C, D$, and the lines $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at point $M$ inside the circle. Perpendiculars are dropped from $M$ to the lines $A B$, $B C$, $C D$, and $D A$, and the feet of these perpendiculars are denoted by $P, Q, R, S$ respectively. Prove that the distances from $M$ to the lines $P Q$, $Q R$, $R S$, and $S P$ are equal.

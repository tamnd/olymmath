Given a set of $k n+1$ numbers $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k n+1}\right\}$, it is known that by removing any one of them, the remaining $k n$ numbers can be divided into $k$ groups, each containing $n$ numbers, such that the sum of the numbers in each group is equal. Prove: $a_{1}=a_{2}=\cdots=a_{k n+1}$.

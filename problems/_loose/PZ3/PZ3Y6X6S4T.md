Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at $A$ and $B$. A line $d$ passing through $A$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ at $P$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ at $Q$. Similarly, a line $d^{\prime}$ passing through $B$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ at $P^{\prime}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ at $Q^{\prime}$.

Show that the lines $\left(P P^{\prime}\right)$ and $\left(Q Q^{\prime}\right)$ are parallel.

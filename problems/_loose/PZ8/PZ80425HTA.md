Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, and let its circumcenter be $M$. On the extension of $A B$ beyond $B$, there is a point $P$ such that $B P=2 \text{ cm}$. On the extension of $B C$ beyond $C$, there is a point $Q$ with $C Q=2 \text{ cm}$, and on the extension of $C A$ beyond $A$, there is a point $R$ with $A R=2 \text{ cm}$.

Prove that under these conditions, regardless of the side length of the triangle $A B C$, the following two statements a) and b) always hold!

a) The triangle $P Q R$ is equilateral.

b) It is $M P=M Q=M R$.

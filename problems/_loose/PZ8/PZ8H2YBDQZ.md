Two bandits are dividing a loot consisting of coins with odd values from 1 to 2017 tugriks. It is known that the total amount of tugriks they have is even, and for any odd $k$ not exceeding 2017, there is a coin worth $k$ tugriks in their loot. Prove that the bandits can divide the loot equally.

(A. Khryabrov based on a problem by V. Frank)

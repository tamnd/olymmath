A parallelogram and a square are arranged such that the vertices of the square lie on the sides of the parallelogram (one vertex on each side). From each vertex of the parallelogram, a line is drawn perpendicular to the nearest side of the square. Prove that the points of pairwise intersection of these lines are also the vertices of a square.

Given a parallelogram $A B C D$ with an acute angle $A$. On the perpendicular bisector of $A D$, points $E$ and $F$ are chosen such that $\angle A B E=\angle D C F=90^{\circ}$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of $B C$. Prove that $\angle A M D=\angle E M F$.

Demonstrate that $\frac{3}{1 \cdot 2}+\frac{4}{2 \cdot 3}+\frac{5}{3 \cdot 4}+\ldots+\frac{n+2}{n \cdot(n+1)}-\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n}\right)<2, \forall n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$.

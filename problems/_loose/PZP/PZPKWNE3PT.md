A trapezoid $ABCD$ is given with a right angle at vertex $A$ and base $AB$, where $|AB| > |CD| \geq |DA|$. Let $S$ be the intersection of the angle bisectors of the internal angles at vertices $A$ and $B$, and $T$ be the intersection of the angle bisectors of the internal angles at vertices $C$ and $D$. Similarly, let $U$ and $V$ be the intersections of the angle bisectors of the internal angles at vertices $A$ and $D$, and $B$ and $C$, respectively.

a) Show that the lines $UV$ and $AB$ are parallel.

b) Prove that the intersection $E$ of the ray $DT$ with the line $AB$ and the points $S, T, B$ lie on the same circle.

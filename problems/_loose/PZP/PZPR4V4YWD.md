Inside a sphere of radius $R$ there is a convex polyhedron. The length of its $i$-th edge is $l_{i}$, and the dihedral angle at this edge is $\varphi_{i}$. Prove that

$$
\sum l_{i}\left(\pi-\varphi_{i}\right) \leqslant 8 \pi R .
$$

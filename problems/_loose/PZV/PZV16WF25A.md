$13.3 .21 \stackrel{\text { * }}{\text { Tai }}$ A line $l$ does not intersect with a circle whose center is $O$, $E$ is a point on $l$, $O E \perp l, M$ is any point on $l$ different from $E$. From $M$ draw two tangents to circle $O$ touching the circle at points $A$ and $B, E C \perp M A$, and intersect $M A$ at $C, E D \perp M B$, and intersect $M B$ at $D$, line $C D$ intersects $O E$ at $F$. Prove: The position of $F$ does not depend on the position of $M$.

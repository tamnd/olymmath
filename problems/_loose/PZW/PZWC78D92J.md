On the sides $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $A_{2} A_{3}$ of the regular $2 n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{2 n}$, take points $K$ and $N$ respectively, such that $\angle K A_{n+2} N=\frac{\pi}{2 n}$. Prove: $N A_{n+2}$ is the angle bisector of $\angle K N A_{3}$.

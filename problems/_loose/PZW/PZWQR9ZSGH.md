$A B C$ is a right-angled triangle, and $K L M N$ is a rectangle such that $N$ lies on the segment $A C$, and $K$ and $L$ lie on the segment $A B$. Translate the rectangle $K L M N$ parallel to $A C$ so that $M^{\prime}$ lies on the segment $B C$. Thus, we obtain the rectangle $K^{\prime} L^{\prime} M^{\prime} N^{\prime}$. Show that the line connecting the intersection point of $C L^{\prime}$ and $A B$ with the intersection point of $A M$ and $C B$ is perpendicular to the line $A B$.

Given a segment $AB$ and a value $\lambda > 1$. It is known that the geometric locus of points whose distances from $A$ and $B$ are in the ratio $\lambda$ is a (Apollonian) circle. This circle intersects the segment $AB$ at $X$ and its extension at $Y$. Prove that the radius of the circle inscribed in the right triangle formed by the segments $AB$ and $XY$ as legs is $XB$.

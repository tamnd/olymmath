$\mathrm{Az}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a & =\frac{a x+c y}{b z+1} \\
b & =\frac{b x+y}{b z+1} \\
c & =\frac{a z+c}{b z+1}
\end{aligned}
$$

is a system of equations to be solved for $x, y$, and $z$, given that $a \lessgtr b c$.

(From Dr. József Kürschák, private lecturer at the Technical University, Budapest.)

On an infinite cone, the angle of which is equal to $\alpha$, a point is taken. From this point, a line is drawn perpendicular to the generatrix of the cone in both directions, such that after the cone is unrolled, this line turns into segments of straight lines. Determine the number of self-intersections of this line on the surface of the cone.

Note. This problem arose from an important question for many practical problems about geodesic lines on curved surfaces, i.e., lines that represent the shortest path on the surface from one point to another. Since a straight line is the shortest distance between two points, the line depicted on the unrolled cone as segments of straight lines will obviously be a geodesic of the cone. See the book by L. A. Lopatnik "Geodesic Lines", M.-L., Gostekhizdat, 1940.

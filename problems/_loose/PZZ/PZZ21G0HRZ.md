A semicircle $k$ is given above the diameter $P Q$. On it, a chord $B C$ of fixed length $d$ is constructed, whose endpoints are different from the points $P, Q$. A ray emitted from point $B$ is reflected off the diameter $P Q$ to point $C$ at a point $A$ such that $|\nless P A B|=|\nless Q A C|$. Prove that the measure of angle $B A C$ does not depend on the position of the chord $B C$ on the semicircle $k$.

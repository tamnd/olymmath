In an intelligence competition show, there are three doors, one of which hides a prize. The contestant can ask the host if the prize is behind the left (or right) door. The contestant can ask the host multiple times in any order of the chosen doors, and the host will answer "yes" or "no" each time. It is known that before the official competition, the contestant needs to inform the host in advance of the number of times they will choose a door, and the host can lie at most 10 times. How many times does the contestant need to ask at least to definitely determine the door hiding the prize?

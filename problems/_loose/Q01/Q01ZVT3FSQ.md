Test $\mathbf{B}$ Given for any $n \in \mathbf{N}$, we have $a_{n}>0$ and
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}=\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}\right)^{2} .
$$

Prove: $a_{n}=n$.
(1989 National High School League First Test Question 5)

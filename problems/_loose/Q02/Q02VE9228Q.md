In Tabulistan, there are several cities, some of which are connected by roads, and it is possible to travel from any city to any other city via these roads. From the capital, $d$ roads lead out, including one to Petushki. The frugal President wants to close several roads so that it is still possible to travel from any city to any other city, but closing any of the remaining roads would violate this property. Prove that the number of ways to do this without closing the road from the Capital to Petushki is at least $\frac{1}{d}$ of the total number of ways to do this.

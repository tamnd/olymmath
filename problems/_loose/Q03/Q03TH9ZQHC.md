Given a parallelogram $A B C D$, where $K, L$ denote the midpoints of its sides $B C$, $A D$ respectively. Let the foot $M$ of the perpendicular from point $D$ to line $A B$ lie inside the side $A B$ of the given parallelogram, and let $N$ be the midpoint of segment $M B$. Prove that $|N K|=|N L|$.

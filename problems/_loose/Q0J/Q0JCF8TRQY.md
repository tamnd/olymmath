Let $\mathscr{I}=\left\{A \in \mathscr{F}: I_{A} \in \mathscr{H}\right\}$, where $\mathscr{H}$ is some system of $\mathscr{F}$-measurable functions satisfying the following properties:

(a) if $f, g \in \mathscr{H}$, then $f+g \in \mathscr{H}$ and $c f \in \mathscr{H}$ for any $c \in \mathbb{R}$;

(b) if $h_{n} \in \mathscr{H}, 0 \leqslant h_{n} \uparrow h$, then $h \in \mathscr{H}$;

(c) $1=I_{\Omega} \in \mathscr{H}$.

Prove that $\mathscr{I}$ is a $\lambda$-system of subsets of $\mathscr{F}$.

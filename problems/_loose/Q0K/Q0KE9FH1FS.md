Which are the quadrilateral pyramids where among the side faces only regular triangles and isosceles right triangles appear? The triangles of the type that appear more than once are congruent.

Calculate the volume of the pyramid among these, in which the number of right triangles is the largest. Choose the shortest side as the unit!

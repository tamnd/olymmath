In two urns, there are $2 p+1$ balls. Every second, half of the balls from the urn where an even number of balls lie are moved to the other urn. Let $k<2 p+1$ be some natural number and it is known that the numbers $p$ and $2 p+1$ are prime. Prove that sooner or later, there will be exactly $k$ balls in one of the urns.

Let $A, B, C, A', B', C'$ be six pairs of different points. Prove that the Circles $BCA'$, $CAB'$ and $ABC'$ have a common point, then the Circles $B'C'A, C'A'B$ and $A'B'C$ also share a common point.

Note: For three pairs of different points $X, Y$ and $Z$ we define the *Circle * $XYZ$ as the circumcircle of the triangle $XYZ$, or - in the case when the points $X, Y$ and $Z$ lie on a straight line - this straight line.

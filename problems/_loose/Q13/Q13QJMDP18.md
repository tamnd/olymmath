197 Prove: If $a, b, c$ are the side lengths of a triangle, and $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles opposite them, and
$a(1-2 \cos \alpha)+b(1-2 \cos \beta)+c(1-2 \cos \gamma)=0$, then the triangle is equilateral.

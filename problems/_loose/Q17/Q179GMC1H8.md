A35 (37-6, France) Let $n, p, q$ be positive integers, and $n > p + q$. If $x_{0}, x_{1}, \cdots, x_{n}$ are integers satisfying the following conditions:
(1) $x_{0} = x_{n} = 0$;
(2) For each integer $i (1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$, either $x_{i} - x_{i-1} = p$ or $x_{i} - x_{i-1} = -q$

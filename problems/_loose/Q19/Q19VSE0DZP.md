10 distinct real numbers are given. For each pair $a, b$ of given numbers, Basil writes down into his exercise book the number $(a-b)^2$, while Pete writes down into his exercise book the number $|a^2 - b^2|$. Determine whether it may appear that Basil and Pete obtain the same collections of 45 numbers in their exercise books.

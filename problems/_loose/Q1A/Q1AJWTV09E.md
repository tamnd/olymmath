A triplet of positive integers $(x, y, z)$ satisfying $x, y, z > 1$ and $x^3 - yz^3 = 2021$ is called *primary * if at least two of the integers $x, y, z$ are prime numbers.
a) Find at least one primary triplet.
b) Show that there are infinitely many primary triplets.

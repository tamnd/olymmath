Let $m$ and $n$ be two natural numbers. A school has $n$ students eating in the cafeteria. Over the course of the year, the cafeteria has offered 100 different dishes. Each student has tasted 10 different dishes. Furthermore, if we take any two dishes out of the 100, we know that exactly $m$ students have tasted each of the two dishes. Calculate the ratio: $\frac{n}{m}$.

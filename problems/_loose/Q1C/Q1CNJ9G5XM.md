Given a line $l$ passing through the focus $F$ of the parabola $y^{2}=2 x$ intersects the parabola at points $A$ and $B$, and the tangents to the parabola at points $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $E$.
(1) Prove: $E F \perp A B$;
(2) Let $\overrightarrow{A F}=\lambda \overrightarrow{F B}$, when $\lambda \in\left[\frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{2}\right]$, find the minimum value of the area $S$ of $\triangle A B E$.

The rowers' puzzle. A team of rowers can cover a given distance against the current in $8 \frac{4}{7}$ minutes. In the absence of the current, the same distance is covered in 7 minutes less than it would take to cover it with the current. How many minutes does it take the team to cover the given distance with the current?

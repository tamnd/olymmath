Let $r>1, 1/r + 1/s = 1$. Prove the

$$
\left(a_{1}^{r}+a_{2}^{r}+\ldots+a_{n}^{r}\right)^{1 / r}\left(b_{1}^{s}+b_{2}^{s}+\ldots+b_{n}^{s}\right)^{1 / s} \geqq a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+\ldots+a_{n} b_{n}
$$

i.e., the Hölder inequality. What do we get if we write $r=s$? (Cauchy-Schwarz inequality.)

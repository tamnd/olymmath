At the beginning, a figure is placed on the field numbered 2, and then a regular die is rolled. If a number divisible by three is rolled, the figure moves to the field with a number one less, otherwise to the field with a number one greater. The game ends with a victory for player $A$ or $B$ if the figure reaches the field numbered 1 or 6, respectively. With what probability will player $A$ win?

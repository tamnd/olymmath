Let the altitudes of $\triangle ABC$ be $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}$, and the orthocenter of the triangle be $M$. Prove that

$$
m_{a}^{2}+m_{b}^{2}+m_{c}^{2} \leq \frac{3}{2}\left(m_{a} \cdot A M+m_{b} \cdot B M+m_{c} \cdot C M\right)
$$

Inside a right-angled triangle, two equal circles are constructed such that the first circle touches one of the
legs and the hypotenuse, the second circle touches the other leg and the hypotenuse, and these circles also touch each other.
Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of tangency of the circles with the hypotenuse. Prove that the midpoint of segment $M N$ lies on
the bisector of the right angle of the triangle.

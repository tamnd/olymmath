12 (14 points) Given the function $f(x)=-2 x+4$, let
$$
S_{n}=f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)+f\left(\frac{2}{n}\right)+\cdots+f\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)+f(1)\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right) \text {, }
$$

If the inequality $\frac{a^{n}}{S_{n}}<\frac{a^{n+1}}{S_{n+1}}$ always holds, find the range of real number $a$.

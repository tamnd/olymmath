Two players, A and B, play a game on an $n \times n$ chessboard. At the start, all squares are white except for one corner square which is black and contains a "rook". Player A starts, and the two players take turns moving the rook horizontally or vertically by any number of squares. The squares the rook passes over and lands on immediately turn black, and the rook cannot pass through or land on black squares. The player who cannot make a legal move loses, and the game ends.

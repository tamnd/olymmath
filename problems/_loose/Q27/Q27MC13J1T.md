\section*{Task 1 - 171041}

In a plane $\epsilon$, a line $g$ and two circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ are given.

Construct a square $A B C D$ such that its vertices $A$ and $C$ lie on $g$, vertex $B$ lies on $k_{1}$, and vertex $D$ lies on $k_{2}$! Describe and justify the construction! Determine for which positional possibilities of the given $g, k_{1}, k_{2}$ such a square exists and for which of these positional possibilities it is uniquely determined!

Let $P$ be the intersection of the directrix $l$ and the axis of symmetry of an ellipse, $F$ the corresponding focus, and $AB$ a chord passing through $F$. Then the maximum value of $\angle APB$ is 2arctan(e), where $e$ is the eccentricity.

Let four points $A, B, C, D$ be given in the plane, no three of which lie on a straight line. Draw the circles $k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}$ passing through the points $B C D$, $C D A$, $D A B$, respectively. Let $P$ be a point on $k_{1}$, and let the line $P C$ intersect $k_{2}$ again at $Q$, and the line $P B$ intersect $k_{3}$ again at $R$. It is to be proven that $A, Q, R$ lie on a straight line.

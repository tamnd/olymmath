Wine or water? In one glass is wine, in the other is water. A drop of wine is transferred from the first glass to the second, then thoroughly mixed, and a drop of the mixture is transferred back. Which is greater: the amount of wine in the water glass or the amount of water in the wine glass? How would the answer change if this transfer was done 10 times? If the mixture was not mixed very thoroughly?

Given $f(x)=\log _{a} \frac{1-m x}{x-1}$ is an odd function $(a>1, a \neq 1)$.
(1) Find the value of $m$;
(2) Based on the result of (1), determine the monotonicity of $f(x)$ on $(1,+\infty)$ (no proof required);
(3) If when $x \in(r, a-2)$, the range of $f(x)$ is exactly $(1,+\infty)$, find the values of $a$ and $r$.

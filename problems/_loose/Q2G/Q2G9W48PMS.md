In $\triangle A B C$, $a, b, c$ are the three sides opposite to angles $A, B, C$ respectively, and $a \geqslant b \geqslant c$, then $\frac{a^{2}-b^{2}}{c^{2}}+\frac{b^{2}-c^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{c^{2}-a^{2}}{b^{2}} \geqslant 0$.

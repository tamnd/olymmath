A race on an unpredictable distance is held as follows. On a circular running track 1 kilometer long, two points $A$ and $B$ are chosen randomly (using a spinning arrow), after which the athletes run from $A$ to $B$ along the shorter arc. Find the median value of the length of this arc, that is, such an $m$ that the length of the arc will exceed $m$ with a probability of exactly $50 \%$.

(A. A. Tesler)

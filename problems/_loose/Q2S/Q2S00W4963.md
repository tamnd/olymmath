Given a real number $\alpha \geqslant \frac{1}{2}$. Prove that for positive real numbers $x, y, z$ the following inequality holds:

$$
x(x-y)(\alpha x-y)+y(y-z)(\alpha y-z)+z(z-x)(\alpha z-x) \geqslant 0
$$

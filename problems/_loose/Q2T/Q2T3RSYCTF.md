a) Prove that for any natural $n$

$\ln (n+1)=\ln n+2\left(\frac{1}{2 n+1}+\frac{1}{3(2 n+1)^{3}}+\frac{1}{5(2 n+1)^{5}}+\ldots\right)$.

b) Prove that

$$
\ln 2=\frac{2}{3}\left(1+\frac{1}{3 \cdot 9}+\frac{1}{5 \cdot 9^{2}}+\frac{1}{7 \cdot 9^{3}}+\ldots\right)
$$

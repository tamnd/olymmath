For the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the edges satisfy $AB = CD = a$ and $AC = BD = b$. Prove that

1. the dihedral angles at the equal edge pairs are equal;
2. the ratio of the sines of the dihedral angles at edges $AB$ and $AC$ is equal to $a / b$.

For $n$ consecutive positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}\right) \\
\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right)<\frac{n(n+1) \ln (\mathrm{e} n)}{2} .
\end{array}
$$

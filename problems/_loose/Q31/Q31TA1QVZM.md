a) Prove that in trilinear coordinates any circle is given by the equation of the form

$$
(p x+q y+r z)(x \sin \alpha+y \sin \beta+z \sin \gamma)=y z \sin \alpha+x z \sin \beta+x y \sin \gamma
$$

b) Prove that the radical axis of two circles, given by equations of this form, is given by the equation

$$
p_{1} x+q_{1} y+r_{1} z=p_{2} x+q_{2} y+r_{2} z
$$

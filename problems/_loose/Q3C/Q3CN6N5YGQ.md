Let the volume of tetrahedron $ABCD$ be $V$, the distances between the three pairs of opposite edges $AB$ and $CD$, $BC$ and $AD$, $AC$ and $BD$ be $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$, and the lengths of the lines connecting the midpoints of these three pairs of opposite edges be $m_{1}, m_{2}, m_{3}$, then $d_{1} d_{2} d_{3} \leqslant 3 V \leqslant$ $m_{1} m_{2} m_{3}$.

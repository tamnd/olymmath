Let $f:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be a differentiable function such that $|f'(x)|\ne1$ for all $x\in[0,1]$. Prove that there exist unique $\alpha,\beta\in[0,1]$ such that $f(\alpha)=\alpha$ and $f(\beta)=1-\beta$.

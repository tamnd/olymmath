A company produces a certain product in the amount of $x_{1}$ in January, $x_{2}$ in February, $\ldots, x_{12}$ in December. The average production of the product from the beginning of the year is calculated as follows:

$x_{1}=x_{1}, \bar{x}_{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right), \bar{x}_{3}=\frac{1}{3}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}\right), \ldots, \bar{x}_{12}=\frac{1}{12}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{12}\right)$.

It is known that $\bar{x}_{k}x_{k}$ for $k$ from 7 to 12. In which month was the average production of the product from the beginning of the year the highest?

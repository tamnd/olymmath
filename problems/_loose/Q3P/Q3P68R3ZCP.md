Let the roots of the equation

$$
x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c=0
$$

be $ x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3} $. What relationship must hold among the coefficients of the equation so that

$$
x_{2}=\frac{x_{1}+x_{3}}{2}
$$

$$
x_{2}=\sqrt{x_{1} x_{3}}
$$

$$
x_{2}=\frac{2 x_{1} x_{3}}{x_{1}+x_{3}}
$$

is true? Determine the roots of the equation in each of the three cases.

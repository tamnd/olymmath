It is known that the motorcycle is 15 km/h faster than the bicycle, and the electric scooter is 10 km/h faster than the bicycle. They all start from point A to point B at the same time. As a result, B arrives $a$ hours earlier than A, and C arrives $b$ hours earlier than A. Then the range of $\frac{b}{a}$ is ( ).

Let $G^{*}$ be the abstract dual of $G$, and $e=e^{*}$ be an edge. Prove the following two results:
(i) $G^{*} / e^{*}$ is the abstract dual of $G-e$;
(ii) $G^{*}-e^{*}$ is the abstract dual of $G / e$.

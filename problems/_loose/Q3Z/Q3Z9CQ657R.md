Let $p_{1}, \ldots, p_{k}$ be distinct prime numbers. Prove that the number $\sqrt{p_{k}}$ cannot be represented as the sum of a rational number and numbers $\sqrt{p_{i_{1}} \ldots p_{i_{s}}}$, where $1 \leqslant i_{1}<\ldots<i_{s} \leqslant k-1$, with rational coefficients.

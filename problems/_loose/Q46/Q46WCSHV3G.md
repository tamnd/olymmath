Given a circular ring and a point inside it. Construct the chord of the circular ring (the segment connecting one point of the smaller circle and one point of the larger circle) that passes through the point and divides it in the given $m: n$ ratio. (For 1st graders, it is enough to construct the chord that is bisected by the given point.)

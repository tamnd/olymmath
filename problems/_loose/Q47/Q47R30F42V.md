Let $u_{1}=1 ; u_{n+1}=u_{n}+8 n$, $n$ - a natural number. Prove that $u_{n}=(2 n-1)^{2}$.

Let a sequence $u_{1}$, $u_{2}, \ldots$ be given. We will form a new sequence $v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots$ as follows: $v_{n}=u_{n+1}-u_{n}$ for all n. This sequence is called the sequence of differences of the sequence $u_{1}, u_{2}, \ldots$ We will denote our new sequence as: $\Delta u_{1}, \Delta u_{2}, \ldots$ ( $\triangle$ - capital Greek letter, read as "delta").

Color each of the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, n$ either red or blue. A step means selecting three different numbers that form an arithmetic sequence and changing the color of each of these three numbers to the other color. For which $n$ can we achieve, starting from any coloring of $1, 2, \ldots, n$, that all numbers are red?

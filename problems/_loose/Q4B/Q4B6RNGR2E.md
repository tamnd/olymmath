Suppose $x, y, z$ and $\lambda$ are positive real numbers such that
$$
\begin{aligned}
y z & =6 \lambda x \\
x z & =6 \lambda y \\
x y & =6 \lambda z \\
x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2} & =1
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the value of $(x y z \lambda)^{-1}$.

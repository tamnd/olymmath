A box contains 9 cards labeled from 1 to 9. Note that distinct number are written on distinct card. Choose 2 cards from the box at a time, let $X$ be smaller number among them. Take a back these cards to the box, choose 2 cards again at a time, let $Y$ be smaller number among them. Find the probability such that $X=Y$.

*2011 Kyoto University enrance exam/Science, Problem 1A*

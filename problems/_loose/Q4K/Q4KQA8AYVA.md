Eight teams participate in a tournament. Each team plays one and only one match against each of the other teams, and there are no ties.

a) Prove that there must necessarily be four teams $A, B, C, D$ such that $A$ has won against $B$, $C$, and $D$, $B$ has won against $C$ and $D$, and $C$ has won against $D$.
b) Can the same be said if there are only seven teams participating in the tournament?

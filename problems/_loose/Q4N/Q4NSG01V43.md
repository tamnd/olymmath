We will prove that for any positive integers $m$ and $n$,

$$
\sqrt{x^{2}+1}=\frac{1-x}{\sqrt{\frac{1}{x}}}
$$

( $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$, i.e., the largest integer not greater than $x$.)

Given a segment $A B$, its midpoint $C$, and a point $D$ inside the segment $A B$. Circles $k(C,|B C|)$ and $m(B,|B D|)$ intersect at points $E$ and $F$, and the ray $F D$ intersects circle $k$ at point $K, K \neq F$. A line parallel to $A B$ passing through point $K$ intersects circle $k$ at point $L, L \neq K$. Prove that $|K L|=|B D|$.

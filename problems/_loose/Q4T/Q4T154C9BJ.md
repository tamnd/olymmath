Let $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at two points $B$ and $B^{\prime}$. A line $d$ passing through the point $B$ intersects the circle $\omega_{1}$ at point $A$ and the circle $\omega_{2}$ at point $C$. A line $d^{\prime}$ passing through the point $B^{\prime}$ intersects the circle $\omega_{1}$ at point $A^{\prime}$ and the circle $\omega_{2}$ at point $C^{\prime}$. Let $\omega_{3}$ be a circle passing through the points $C$ and $C^{\prime}$. $\omega_{3}$ intersects the line $d$ at point $D$ and the line $d^{\prime}$ at point $D^{\prime}$. Show that the points $A, A^{\prime}, D$ and $D^{\prime}$ are concyclic.

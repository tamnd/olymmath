In an acute-angled $\triangle A B C$, the angles at vertices $A, B$, and $C$ are denoted by $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$, respectively. If

$$
4 \sin \alpha+5 \cos \beta=6
$$

and

$$
5 \sin \beta+4 \cos \alpha=5
$$

determine $\gamma$.

The bases of the prism are two identical convex $n$-gons. The number $v$ of vertices of this body, the number $s$ of its wall diagonals, and the number $t$ of its body diagonals form the first three terms of an arithmetic sequence in a certain order. For which $n$ does this hold?

(Note: By the walls of the prism, we mean the lateral faces as well as the bases. A body diagonal is a segment that connects two vertices of the prism that do not lie in the same face.)

The city square is in the shape of a regular octagon. The city council has decided to renovate the square and place a fountain inside it. They did not tell Master Matko where to place the center of the fountain, but instead told him: "If the center of the fountain is connected to the vertices of the octagon, the eight triangles obtained should be paved alternately with black and white tiles, so that the total area paved with black tiles is equal to the total area paved with white tiles." Master Matko, however, asked them to tell him the exact location, as he claims that their requirement is met wherever he places the fountain inside the square. Prove that Master Matko is right.

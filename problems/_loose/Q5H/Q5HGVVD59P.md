Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be $n$ natural numbers, where the sum of any $k(k \leqslant n)$ natural numbers is not less than $k(k-1)$ and $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=n(n-1)$. After a round-robin football tournament with $n$ teams, the total points of all teams is $n(n-1)$ (in football matches, each team gets 2 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss). Prove that there can be such an outcome where the points of each team are exactly $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$.

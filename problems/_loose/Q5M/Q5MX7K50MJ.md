Two woodcutters, Ivan and Prokhor, were working together in the forest and sat down to have a snack. Ivan had 4 flatbreads, and Prokhor had 8. Suddenly, a hunter approached them.

- Well, brothers, I got lost in the forest, the village is far away, and I am very hungry. Please, share your bread and salt with me!
- Well, sit down, we'll share what we have, - said the woodcutters.

The twelve flatbreads were divided equally among the three of them. After eating, the hunter searched his pockets, found a hryvnia and a half-ruble, and said:

- Don't be offended, brothers, I have nothing more. Share the money as you see fit!

The hunter left, and the woodcutters started arguing. Prokhor said: - I think the money should be divided equally! But Ivan objected: - For 12 flatbreads - 60 k., so for each flatbread - 5 k. Since you had 8 flatbreads - you get 40 k., and I had 4 flatbreads - I get 20 k.! And how would you divide the money between the woodcutters?

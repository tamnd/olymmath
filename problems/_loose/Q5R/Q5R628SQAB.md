There is a circle and a point $P$ in its plane. Draw two perpendicular lines through $P$. The lines intersect the circle at points $A$ and $C$, and $B$ and $D$. What are the distances from the center of the circle $O$ to the lines $OH=x$ and $OK=y$, if the area of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ is $=\frac{a^{2}}{2}$. Calculate the sides of the quadrilateral $ABCD$, given the radius of the circle $R$, the distance of point $P$ from the center of the circle $d=\frac{R}{2} \sqrt{4-\sqrt{5}}$, and twice the area of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ $a^{2}=R^{2} \sqrt{5+2 \sqrt{5}}$.

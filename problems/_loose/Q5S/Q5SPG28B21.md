Let each sequence of capital Bulgarian letters be a word (Note that the Bulgarian alphabet consists of 30 letters). We say that a word is *calm*, if there is no sequence of the letter $P$ in it (two letters $P$ next to each other). Find a clear expression for $f(n)$ (closed formula), which represents the number of calm words with length $n$.
Example: *РНТ* and *ТТРВ* are calm, but *ЖГРР* and *ЖРРРП* aren't.

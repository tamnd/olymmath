A piece of paper is stained with ink. For each point in the ink stain, determine the maximum and minimum distances to the boundary points of the stain. Among all the minimum distances, take the maximum value; among all the maximum distances, take the minimum value, and compare the two values. If these two values are equal, what shape should the ink stain have?

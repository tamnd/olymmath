Let $0<\theta<2 \pi$, the complex number $z=1-\cos \theta+\mathrm{i} \sin \theta, u=a^{2}+a \mathrm{i}$, and $z u$ is a pure imaginary number, $a \in \mathbf{R}$.
(1) Find the principal value of the argument of the complex number $u$ (expressed in terms of $\theta$);
(2) Let $w=z^{2}+u^{2}+2 z u$, is it possible for $w$ to be a positive real number? Why?

A semicircle is inscribed in triangle $ABC$ such that its diameter lies on side $BC$, and the arc touches sides $AB$ and $AC$ at points $C_{1}$ and $B_{1}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{A C_{1}}{C_{1} B} \cdot \frac{B H}{H C} \cdot \frac{C B_{1}}{B_{1} A}=1
$$

where $H$ is the foot of the altitude dropped from point $A$ to side $BC$.

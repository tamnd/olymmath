Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers, $a < b$, and let $f$ be a polynomial of degree 3, with real coefficients, so that the polynomials $f - a$ and $f - b$ have only real roots.
Prove that the set of the real numbers $x$ such that $a < f(x) < b$ is the disjoint union of three open intervals, and the length of one of these intervals is the sum of the lengths of the other two.

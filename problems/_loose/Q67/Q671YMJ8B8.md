Given is $\triangle A B C$. On the sides $A C$ and $B C$, points $M$ and $N$ are chosen, respectively, such that $A M=B N$. The circumcircles of $\triangle A N C$ and $\triangle B M C$ intersect at another point $P$ (besides point $C$). Prove that the line $C P$ is the angle bisector of the angle at vertex $C$.

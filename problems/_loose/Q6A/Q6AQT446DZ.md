Let non-zero complex numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{5}$ satisfy:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}}=\frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}}=\frac{a_{4}}{a_{3}}=\frac{a_{5}}{a_{4}}, \\
\sum_{k=1}^{5} a_{k}=4 \sum_{k=1}^{5} \frac{1}{a_{k}}=s,
\end{array}\right.
$$

where $s \in \mathbf{R},|s| \leqslant 2$. Prove: The complex numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{5}$ correspond to points on the same circle in the complex plane.

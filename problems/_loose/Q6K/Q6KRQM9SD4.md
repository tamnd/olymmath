Points $A, D, C$ and $B$ are collinear, in this order, such that $[A D] \equiv[D C] \equiv[C B]$. Point $E$ is outside the line $A B$, $O$ is the midpoint of segment $[A B]$, and $F$ is the symmetric point of $E$ with respect to $O$. If $F C \cap E B=\{M\}, M D \cap A F=\{N\}$ and $N C \cap E B=\{P\}$, show that:

a) $E B=8 P B$.

b) $A_{A F B E}=48 A_{C P B}$.

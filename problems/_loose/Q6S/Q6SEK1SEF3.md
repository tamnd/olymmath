To determine the durability of a motorcycle tire type, two tires were tested. It was found that the tire on the rear wheel was no longer within the required profile depth and thus worn out after 15000 kilometers, and the tire on the front wheel after 25000 kilometers.

a) It is now desired to achieve that two such tires are worn out simultaneously by swapping them after a certain number of kilometers driven. Determine this number of kilometers!

b) After how many kilometers will both tires be worn out under the conditions of part a)?

It is assumed that the wear on both the front and rear wheels is proportional to the distance driven.

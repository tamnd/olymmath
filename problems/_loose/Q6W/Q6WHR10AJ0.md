Circle $\omega$ with radius 6 and center $O$ is inscribed in an acute triangle $ABC$ and touches its sides $AB$ and $BC$ at points $F$ and $T$ respectively. Circle $\Omega$ with radius $\frac{\sqrt{85}}{2}$ and center $M$ is circumscribed around triangle $BFT$.

a) Find $BO$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the area of triangle $ACM$ to the area of triangle $ABC$ is $\frac{7}{10}$. Find the length of the angle bisector $BQ$ of triangle $ABC$, as well as its area.

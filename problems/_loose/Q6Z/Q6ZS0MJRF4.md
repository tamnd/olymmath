Determine the smallest positive integer $n$ such that there exists a polynomial of degree $n$ with real coefficients $P(x)$ whose graph contains 2025 distinct points that are the vertices of a 2025-sided regular polygon.

*Note:* The points on the graph of $P(x)$ are the points on the cartesian plane of the form $(x,P(x))$ .

Let $p$ be a prime number and $a, b, c$ and $n$ be positive integers with $a, b, c<p$ so that the following three statements hold:

$$
p^{2}\left|a+(n-1) \cdot b, p^{2}\right| b+(n-1) \cdot c, p^{2} \mid c+(n-1) \cdot a
$$

Show that $n$ is not a prime number.

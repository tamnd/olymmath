A game about passing barriers rules that in the $n$ th barrier, you need to throw a dice for $n$ times. If the sum of points you get is larger than $2^n$ , then you can pass this barrier.**(a)** How many barriers can you pass at most?**(b)** Find the probablity of passing the first three barriers.

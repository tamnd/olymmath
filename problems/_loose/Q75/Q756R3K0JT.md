The normal cross-section of a triangular prism $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ is an equilateral triangle $A B C$ with side $a$. Two arbitrary points $P$ and $Q$ are taken on the lateral edges $B_{1} B_{2}$ and $C_{1} C_{2}$. Let $\overline{B P}=x$ and $\overline{C Q}=y$.

a) Find the relationship between $x$ and $y$ for triangle $A P Q$ to be a right triangle with a right angle at vertex $P$.

b) Determine $x$ and $y$ such that triangle $A P Q$ is an isosceles right triangle with a right angle at vertex $P$.

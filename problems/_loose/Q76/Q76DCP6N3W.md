Let $\left(u_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and $\left(v_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}}$ be the sequences defined by: $\forall n \in \mathbb{N}, u_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{1}{k!}$ and $\forall n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, v_{n}=u_{n}+\frac{1}{n \times n!}$. Show that the sequences $\left(u_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and $\left(v_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}}$ are adjacent. (Their common limit is none other than the number $e=\exp (1)$, and it can also be shown using our two sequences that it is an irrational number!)

Let $C_{1}, C_{2}$ be concentric circles, with the radius of $C_{2}$ being $\lambda(\lambda>1)$ times the radius of $C_{1}$. An $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ is inscribed in $C_{1}$, and the extensions of $A_{n} A_{1}, A_{1} A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n-1} A_{n}$ intersect circle $C_{2}$ at $B_{1}, B_{2}, \cdots B_{n}$, respectively. If the perimeters of the $n$-sided polygons $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ and $B_{1} B_{2} \cdots B_{n}$ are $p_{1}$ and $p_{2}$, respectively, prove that: $p_{2} \geqslant \lambda p_{1}$, with equality if and only if the $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ is a regular $n$-sided polygon.

Let $p_{1}^{2}, p_{2}^{2}, \cdots, p_{n}^{2}$ be $n$ distinct perfect squares greater than 1. Prove:
$$
\left(1-\frac{1}{p_{1}^{2}}\right)\left(1-\frac{1}{p_{2}^{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1-\frac{1}{p_{n}^{2}}\right)>\frac{1}{2} .
$$

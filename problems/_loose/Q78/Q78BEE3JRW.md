Let $a, b, c, d$ be positive integers, and $x_0, x_1, x_2, \cdots$ be a sequence of positive integers and $x_{n+3} = a x_{n+2} + b x_{n+1} + c x_{n} + d$ for all $n\geqslant 0$ . If there are $2025$ consecutive terms in the sequence which are all primes, then prove that $x_3 > 16$ .

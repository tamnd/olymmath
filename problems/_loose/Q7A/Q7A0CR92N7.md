Given the set $M=\{f(x) \mid f(x)+f(x+2)=f(x+1), x \in \mathbf{R}\}, g(x)=\sin \frac{\pi x}{3}$.
(1) Prove that the function $g(x) \in M$;
(2) Are all elements in the set $M$ periodic functions? Prove your conclusion;
(3) Are all elements in the set $M$ odd functions? Prove your conclusion.

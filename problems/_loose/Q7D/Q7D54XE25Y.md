Four trucks $A, B, C$, and $D$ travel the same route. If $A$ travels at an average speed of $56 \frac{km}{h}$ and $B$ at $40 \frac{km}{h}$, then $A$ takes exactly 2 hours less than $B$ to complete this route.

At what average speed would $C$ need to travel if $D$ were to start 4 hours earlier than $C$, travel at an average speed of $35 \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}$, and arrive at the common destination at the same time as $C$?

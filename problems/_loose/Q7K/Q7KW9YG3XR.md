The radius $O M$ of the circle rotates uniformly, turning through an angle of $\left.360^{\circ}\right)_{N}$ per second (where $N$ is a natural number greater than 3). Initially, it was in the position $O M_{0}$, after one second - $O M_{1}$, after another two seconds (i.e., three seconds after the start) - $O M_{2}$, after another three seconds - $O M_{3}$, and so on, after another $N-1$ seconds after $O M_{N-2}-O M_{N-1}$.

For which $N$ do these positions of the radius divide the circle into $N$ equal sectors?

a) Is it true that all powers of two belong to the set of such $N$?

b) Do any numbers that are not powers of two belong to the set of such $N$?

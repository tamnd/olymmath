Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer, consider an $n \times n$ square board, which is divided into $n^{2}$ unit squares, two squares on the board are called adjacent if they share a common edge.

Mark $N$ unit squares on the board, such that every square on the board (marked or unmarked) is adjacent to at least one marked square, determine the minimum value of $N$.

(2002 National Competition Question) Real numbers $a, b, c$ and a positive number $\lambda$ make $f(x)=x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c$ have three real roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$, and satisfy
(1) $x_{2}-x_{1}=\lambda$; (2) $x_{3}>\frac{1}{2}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right)$.
Find the maximum value of $\frac{2 a^{3}+27 c-9 a b}{\lambda^{3}}$.

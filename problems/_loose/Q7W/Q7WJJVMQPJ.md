Each voter in the election enters the surnames of 10 candidates into the ballot. There are 11 ballot boxes at the polling station. After the election, it turned out that each ballot box contains at least one ballot, and for any selection of 11 ballots, one from each box, there is a candidate whose surname appears in each of the selected ballots. Prove that in at least one of the ballot boxes, all ballots contain the surname of the same candidate.

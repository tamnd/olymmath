Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{\log _{a} N}-\frac{2}{\log _{b} N}+\frac{1}{\log _{c} N}=0
$$

for any positive number $N$ different from 1, if the positive numbers $a, b, c$ are in geometric progression with $b$ being the geometric mean of $a$ and $c$.

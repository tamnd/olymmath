Let $\mathbb{P}$ be the set of all prime numbers and $M$ a subset of $\mathbb{P}$ having at least three elements. Suppose that for any integer $k \geq 1$ and for any subset $A=\left\{p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{k}\right\}$ of $M$ such that $A \neq M$, all prime factors of the number $p_{1} \cdot p_{2} \cdot \ldots \cdot p_{k}-1$ are in $M$. Show that $M=\mathbb{P}$.

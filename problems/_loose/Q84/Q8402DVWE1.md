Three. (50 points) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers.
Prove:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{\frac{k}{k+1}} \leqslant \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}+\sqrt{\frac{2\left(\pi^{2}-3\right)}{9} \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}} .
$$

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive real numbers with a product different from 1. The operation star, represented by "*", is defined by the equation

$$
a * b=\frac{a+b-2 a b}{1-a b} \text {. }
$$

On a blackboard, there are 2015 numbers all equal to $\frac{1}{2}$. In each step, two numbers $x$ and $y$ written on the blackboard are erased and the number $x * y$ is written. This step is repeated 2014 times until only one number remains on the blackboard.

(a) Prove that the equation

$$
\frac{x * y}{1-x * y}=\frac{x}{1-x}+\frac{y}{1-y}
$$

is true for any real $x$ and $y$ with $x \neq 1, y \neq 1$, and $x y \neq 1$.

(b) If for each number $x$ written on the blackboard, we calculate $\frac{x}{1-x}$ and sum all these results, we will get a certain result. Show that this result is always the same regardless of how many steps have been performed up to that moment.
(c) What number will be written on the blackboard after the 2014 steps?

(d) If, in addition to the 2015 numbers equal to $\frac{1}{2}$ in the initial situation, we also write the number 1, what will be the final number after the execution of 2015 steps?

Who is richer? (9th grade, 4 points). Cat Basilio and Fox Alisa have no solido left. And then they got hired to set up and remove the scenery in Carabas-Barabas's theater. Every evening after the performance, Carabas gives them a one-soldo coin, and a fight immediately breaks out between the cat and the fox.

With a probability of 0.25, the cat wins and takes the coin, with a probability of 0.25, the fox wins and takes the coin, and with a probability of 0.5, both are taken to the police and all their money is confiscated (not just today's, but all of it). Question: By how many soldos will one of the companions have more than the other at the end of the 10th day of part-time work (the absolute difference in their wealth)? Find the expected value of this quantity.

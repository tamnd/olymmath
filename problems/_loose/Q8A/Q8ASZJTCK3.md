There are $4 n(n \in N)$ points on the circumference of a circle, which are alternately painted blue and red (one red and one blue alternately). All blue points are divided into several pairs, and a blue line segment is drawn between each pair of blue points. The same is done for all red points. Prove: At least $n$ pairs of red and blue line segments intersect (i.e., one red line segment intersects with one blue line segment, another red line segment intersects with another blue line segment, and so on, for a total of $n$ pairs).

There are $a$ white balls and $b$ black balls $(a \neq b)$ in a jar, from which all the balls are drawn one by one without replacement. Try to determine which of the following two events has a higher probability:
Event $A$: At some point, the number of white balls drawn equals the number of black balls drawn,
Event $B$: At some point, the number of white balls left in the jar equals the number of black balls left in the jar.
Try to find the probabilities of these events.

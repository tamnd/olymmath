For a school sports festival, class $8 c$ wants to select a team of four students from the seven best students in the 100-m sprint to participate in the $4 \times 100$-m relay race.

a) How many different teams could be selected from the seven students?

b) How many different teams could be selected from the students if two specific students out of the seven must be included?

c) How many different teams could be selected from the students if three specific students out of the seven must be included?

d) In how many different orders of their starts can the four students of a team always be arranged for the relay race?

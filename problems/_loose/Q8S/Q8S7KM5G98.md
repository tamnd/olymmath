Let the orthocenter of triangle $ABC$ be $M$, and let the circumcenters of triangles $BCM$, $CAM$, and $ABM$ be $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$ respectively. Prove that

a) triangles $ABC$ and $A_1B_1C_1$ are congruent,
b) $M$ is the circumcenter of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ and conversely, the circumcenter of triangle $ABC$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$.

(See the article by Richárd Obláth about Gyula Vályi in the previous issue of our journal, especially page 6.)

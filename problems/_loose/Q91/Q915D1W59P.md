Consider a rectangular parallelepiped $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$, where $A B C D$ is the bottom face with the letters assigned in a clockwise direction, and $A, B, C$, and $D$ are directly below $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$ respectively. The parallelepiped is divided into eight pieces by three planes that are orthogonal to each other and parallel to the faces of the parallelepiped. For each vertex $P$ of the parallelepiped, let $V_{P}$ denote the volume of the piece of the parallelepiped that contains $P$. Given that $V_{A}=40, V_{C}=300, V_{B^{\prime}}=360$ and $V_{C^{\prime}}=90$, what is the volume of the parallelepiped $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$?

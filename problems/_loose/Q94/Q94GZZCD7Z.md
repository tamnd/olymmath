Let $A_{n}=\left\{a \in \mathbb{Z} \mid a= \pm p_{1} \pm p_{2} \pm p_{3} \pm \ldots \pm p_{n}\right\}$ where $p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{n}$ are the first $n$ natural prime numbers and the signs + and - are chosen in all possible ways.

a) Show that $0 \in A_{5}$

b) Show that $A_{2 p}, p \geq 1, p \in \mathbb{N}$, has an even number of elements.

c) Show that if $k, p \geq 1, k, p \in \mathbb{N}$, then: $A_{2 p} \cap A_{2 k+1}=\varnothing$.

Given a square table of size $2023 \times 2023$. Each of its cells is colored in one of $n$ colors. It is known that for any six cells of the same color located in the same row, there are no cells of the same color above the leftmost of them and below the rightmost of them. What is the smallest $n$ for which this is possible?

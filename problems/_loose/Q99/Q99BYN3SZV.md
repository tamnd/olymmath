The bisector $A D(D \in B C)$ of the acute isosceles triangle $A B C$ divides it into two isosceles triangles. Let $O$ and $I$ be the incenter and the circumcenter of $\triangle A B C$, respectively. $A O$ meets $B C$ in point $E$, while $F$ is the intersection point of the lines $B I$ and $D O$. Prove that:

a) the quadrilaterals $A B E F$ and $A D C F$ are rhombi with equal side lengths;

b) If $H$ is the altitude center of $\triangle A B E$, then the points $A, D, E, F, H$ are concyclic.

Definition. A sequence of numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$, which satisfies the relation with given $p$ and $q$

$$
a_{\mathrm{n}+2}=p a_{\mathrm{n}+1}+q a_{\mathrm{n}} (n=0,1,2, \ldots)
$$

is called a linear recurrence (or recursive) sequence of the second order. The equation

$$
x^{2}-p x-q=0
$$

is called the characteristic equation of the sequence ( $a_{\text {n }}$ ).

Prove that if the numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}$ are fixed, then all other terms of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ are uniquely determined.

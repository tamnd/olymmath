In a $3 \times 3$ grid of an international chessboard, a knight is placed in each of the four corner squares, with white knights in the top two corners and black knights in the bottom two corners. The goal is to move the white knights to the bottom two corners and the black knights to the top two corners. Each step can move any knight, but must follow the rules of international chess, and can only move to an empty square. Prove that the minimum number of moves required for this is 16.

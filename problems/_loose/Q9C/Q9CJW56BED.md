Consider a cube $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$, with side length $4 \text{ cm}$, and $P$ a point inside the cube.

a) Prove that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the faces of the cube is constant.

b) Prove that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the vertices of the cube is greater than or equal to $16 \sqrt{3}$.

c) If $P \in [B D^{\prime}]$ such that $\mathrm{BP}=3 P D^{\prime}$, calculate the sum of the distances to the lateral edges $\mathrm{AA}^{\prime}, \mathrm{BB}^{\prime}, C C^{\prime}, \mathrm{DD}^{\prime}$.

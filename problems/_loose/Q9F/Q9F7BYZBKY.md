On the plane, there are two fixed points $A, B$, and 4 points $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}, C_{4}$ are placed arbitrarily, and do not coincide with points $A 、 B$. If there exist points $C_{i} 、 C_{j}(i \neq j)$, such that the inequality $\mid \sin \angle A C_{i} B$ $-\sin \angle A C_{j} B \left\lvert \leqslant \frac{1}{3}\right.$ holds, then $(C_{i} 、 C_{j})$ is called a point pair. Then such point pairs
(A) do not exist
(B) there is at least 1
(C) there is at most 1
(D) there is exactly 1

Prove the following statement!

If $x, y$ and $z$ are non-zero natural numbers, then

$$
\begin{array}{r}
a=\frac{(x+y \sqrt{z})^{2}+(x-y \sqrt{z})^{2}}{2} \\
b=\frac{(x+y \sqrt{z})^{2}-(x-y \sqrt{z})^{2}}{2 \sqrt{z}} \\
c=a^{2}-\left(x^{2}-y^{2} z\right)^{2}
\end{array}
$$

are natural numbers, and $b$ is a divisor of $c$.

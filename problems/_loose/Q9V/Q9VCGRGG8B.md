(1) Find the maximum positive integer $n$ such that the equation $x_{1}+2 x_{2}+3 x_{3}+\cdots+n x_{n}=2017$ (1) has a positive integer solution $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \cdots, x_{n}\right)$;
(2) Let $A_{n}$ denote the set of all positive integer solutions $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \cdots, x_{n}\right)$ of equation (1). When $n$ is odd, we call each element in $A_{n}$ an odd solution of equation (1); when $n$ is even, we call each element in $A_{n}$ an even solution of equation (1). Prove that the number of all odd solutions of equation (1) is equal to the number of all even solutions.

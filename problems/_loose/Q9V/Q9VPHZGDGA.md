Random variables $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ have distribution functions $F_{1}(x)$ and $F_{2}(x)$, respectively. Prove that the function

$$
F(x)=C_{1} F_{1}(x)+C_{2} F_{2}(x)
$$

is the distribution function of some random variable $X$, where $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ are non-negative numbers whose sum is one.

Let circle $w$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$, and let point $P$ be an interior point of $\triangle ABC$. The rays $AP$, $BP$, and $CP$ intersect circle $w$ at points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$, respectively. Let the points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$ be symmetric to points $A_2$, $B_2$, and $C_2$ with respect to the midpoints of sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$, respectively. Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle A_2B_2C_2$ passes through the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$.

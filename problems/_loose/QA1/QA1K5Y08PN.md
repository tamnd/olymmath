A hundred boys and a hundred girls stand in lines facing each other. Each boy selects a girl (multiple boys can choose the same girl) and goes straight to her. During this process, the paths of the boys do not cross. Then the boys return to their places, and now the girls choose, and again, while going straight to the selected boy, their paths do not cross. Prove that there is a boy and a girl who chose each other.

(51st Czech and Slovak Mathematical Olympiad (Open Book) Question) For a natural number $n(n \geqslant 2)$, for what value of the parameter $p$ does the system of equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x_{1}^{4}+\frac{2}{x_{1}^{2}}=p x_{2}, \\
x_{2}^{4}+\frac{2}{x_{2}^{2}}=p x_{3}, \\
\cdots \cdots \\
x_{n-1}^{4}+\frac{2}{x_{n-1}^{2}}=p x_{n}, \\
x_{n}^{4}+\frac{2}{x_{n}^{2}}=p x_{1}
\end{array}\right.
$$

have at least two real solutions?

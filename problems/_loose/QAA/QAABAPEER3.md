Given an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, in which $A B < B C$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $A C$ respectively, and $H$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $B$. The incircle touches side $A C$ at point $K$. The line passing through $K$ and parallel to $M H$ intersects segment $M N$ at point $P$. Prove that a circle can be inscribed in quadrilateral $A M P K$.

(I. Bibikov)

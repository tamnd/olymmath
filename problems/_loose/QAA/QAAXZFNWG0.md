Let $H$ be the orthocenter of an acute triangle $A B C$. The circumcircle $\omega$ of triangle $H A B$ intersects line $B C$ at the point $D \neq B$. Let $P$ be the intersection of the line $D H$ and the line segment $A C$, and let $Q$ be the circumcenter of triangle $A D P$. Show that the center of $\omega$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $B D Q$.

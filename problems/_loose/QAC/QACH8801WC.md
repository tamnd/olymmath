Given three positive integers $a, b, c$. Let $x=ab, y=ac, z=bc$, which of the following statements is true?

(A) If $x, y, z$ are squares, then $a, b, c$ are squares

(B) if $x, y, z$ are even, then $a, b, c$ are even

(C) if $x, y, z$ are cubes, then $a, b, c$ are cubes

(D) if $x, y, z$ are multiples of 10, then $a, b, c$ are multiples of 10

(E) none of the above statements is correct.

Electoral Districts. In Anchuria, presidential elections are being prepared, in which President Miraflores wants to win. Exactly half of the numerous voters support Miraflores, and the other half support Dick Maloney. Miraflores is also a voter. By law, he has the right to divide all voters into two electoral districts at his discretion. Voting in each district is conducted as follows: each voter marks the name of their candidate on the ballot; all ballots are placed in an urn. Then one random ballot is drawn from the urn, and the candidate whose name is marked on it wins in that district. A candidate wins the election only if they win in both districts. If no winner is determined, a second round of voting is held under the same rules. How should Miraflores divide the voters to maximize the probability of his victory in the first round?

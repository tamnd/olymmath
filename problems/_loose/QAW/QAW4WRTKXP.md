In a certain class, there are thirty-three students. Each student wrote two numbers on the board: how many other students in the class, besides themselves, have the same first name as they do, and how many other students in the class, besides themselves, have the same last name as they do.

If each of the numbers $0,1,2, \ldots, 10$ appears on the board at least once, prove that there is at least one pair of students in the class with the same first name and last name.

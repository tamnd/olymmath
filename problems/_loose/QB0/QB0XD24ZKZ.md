Consider the real numbers $a, b, x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n} \in(0,1)$ such that $b^{n} \leq x_{1} x_{2} \ldots x_{n}$. Show that $\left(\log _{a} b\right)^{n} \geq \log _{a} x_{1} \log _{a} x_{2} \ldots \log _{a} x_{n}, \forall n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$.

Two players are playing. At the beginning of the game, there is one stick. The first player breaks this stick into two parts. And so the players take turns breaking any of the available sticks into two parts at that moment. If, by breaking a stick, a player can form one or several separate triangles (each - exactly from three sticks) from all the available sticks, then he wins. Which of the players (the first or the second) can ensure their victory regardless of the other player's actions?

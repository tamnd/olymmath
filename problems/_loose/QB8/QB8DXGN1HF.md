Let $O$ be an interior point of triangle $A B C$. Let $K, L, M$ be the points where the lines passing through $O$ and parallel to the sides $C A, A B, B C$ intersect the sides $A B, B C, C A$ respectively. Furthermore, let $P, Q, R$ be the points where the lines $C K$ and $A L, A L$ and $B M, B M$ and $C K$ intersect respectively. Prove that the sum of the areas of triangles $A K P, B L Q$ and $C M R$

is equal to the area of triangle $P Q R$.

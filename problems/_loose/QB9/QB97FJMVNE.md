A grasshopper moves by making jumps of exactly $10 \mathrm{~cm}$. Its motion follows this pattern: it makes a certain number of jumps in a given direction, then turns $120^{\circ}$ to its left and makes, in the new direction, twice the number of jumps it had made in the previous direction. At this point, it turns again by $120^{\circ}$ to the left and doubles the number of jumps once more. Knowing that it starts by making a single jump in a given direction, how far from the starting point will it be after 17 jumps?
(A) $20 \mathrm{~cm}$
(B) $20 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~cm}$
(C) $40 \mathrm{~cm}$
(D) $40 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~cm}$
(E) $50 \mathrm{~cm}$

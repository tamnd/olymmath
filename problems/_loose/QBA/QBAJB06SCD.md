Given that $a, b, c$ are real numbers, and $b+c=6-4a+3a^{2}, c-b=4-4a+a^{2}$. Then the size relationship between $a, b, c$ is
(A) $a<b \leqslant c$.
(B) $b<a \leqslant c$.
(C) $b<c \leqslant a$.
(D) $c<a \leqslant b$.
(China Sichuan Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1992)

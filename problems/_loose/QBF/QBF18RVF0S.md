It is required to prepare a mixture containing three chemical substances $A, B, C$. It is known that the mixture to be prepared must contain substance $\boldsymbol{A}$ no less than 6 units, substance $B$ no less than 8 units, and substance $C$ no less than 12 units.

Substances $A, B, C$ are contained in three types of products - I, II, and III - in the concentrations indicated in Table 19.

Table 19

| Products | Chemical substances | | | |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| | $A$ | $B$ | $C$ | |
| I | 2 | 1 | 3 | |
| II | 1 | 2 | 4 | |
| III | 3 | 15 | 2 | |

The cost of a unit of products I, II, and III is different: a unit of product I costs 2 rubles, a unit of product II - 3 rubles, a unit of product III - 2.5 rubles. The mixture must be prepared so that the total cost of the products used is the lowest.

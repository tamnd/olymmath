Consider a right triangle $A B C$ with a right angle at vertex $A$. Describe a semicircle around it with diameter $B C$; then construct two semicircles with diameters $A B$ and $A C$, external to the triangle $A B C$. The sum of the areas of the two resulting lunes is equal to the area of triangle $A B C^{1}$. Why?

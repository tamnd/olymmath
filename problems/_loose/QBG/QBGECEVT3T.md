Given the relations

$$
5\left[(x+y)^{2}+\log _{2}^{2} y\right]=\left[x+\log _{2}\left(2^{y} \cdot y^{2}\right)\right]^{2}
$$

and

$$
4^{y}=4 y(3 x+4)
$$

a) Prove that there do not exist $x, y \in(0,+\infty)$ that satisfy relation (1).

b) Determine $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ that satisfy both relations (1) and (2) simultaneously.

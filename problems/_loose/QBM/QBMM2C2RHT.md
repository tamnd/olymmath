Let the three excircles of $\triangle ABC$ be $I_{A}, I_{B}, I_{C}$, which touch the sides of $\triangle ABC$ at points $M$, $N$, $E$, $G$, $F$, $D$, respectively, and the lines $DF$, $EG$, $MN$ intersect pairwise at $P_{1}$, $P_{2}$, $P_{3}$. Let $R$ and $r$ be the circumradius and inradius of $\triangle ABC$, respectively, with the circumcenter $O$ and the incenter $I$. Prove:
(1) The lines $P_{1}A$, $P_{2}B$, $P_{3}C$ are concurrent, and the Gergonne point of $\triangle ABC$ is the excenter of $\triangle P_{1}P_{2}P_{3}$; $\frac{1}{P_{1}A} + \frac{1}{P_{2}B} + \frac{1}{P_{3}C} = \frac{1}{r}$
(2) $\frac{1}{OI_{A}^{2} - R^{2}} + \frac{1}{OI_{B}^{2} - R^{2}} + \frac{1}{OI_{C}^{2} - R^{2}} = \frac{1}{2RT}$; $OI_{A} + OI_{B} + OI_{C} \leq 6R$; $I_{A} \cdot I_{B} \cdot I_{C} \leq 6R$;
(3) $\triangle I_{A}I_{B}I_{C} \sim \triangle P_{1}P_{2}P_{3}$, and the similarity ratio is $2R : (R + r)$;
(4) $\frac{\text{Product of the side lengths of } \triangle I_{A}I_{B}I_{C}}{\text{Product of the side lengths of } \triangle ABC} = \frac{4R}{r}$, $\frac{S_{\triangle I_{A}I_{B}I_{C}}}{S_{\triangle ABC}} = \frac{2R}{r}$.

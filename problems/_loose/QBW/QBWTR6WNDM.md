Let's prove the following identity:

$$
\frac{n^{3}-3 n+\left(n^{2}-1\right) \sqrt{n^{2}-4}-2}{n^{3}-3 n+\left(n^{2}-1\right) \sqrt{n^{2}-4}+2}=\frac{(n+1) \sqrt{n-2}}{(n-1) \sqrt{n+2}}
$$

Three ants are standing on three of the four vertices of a rectangle in the plane. The ants move in the plane one at a time. Each time, the ant that moves does so along the line parallel to the one determined by the positions of the other two ants. Is it possible that, after some moves, the ants are situated at the midpoints of three of the four sides of the original rectangle?

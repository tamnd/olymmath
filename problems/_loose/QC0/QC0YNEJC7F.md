Let $p_{1}=2, p_{2}=3, \ldots$ be the sequence of prime numbers.

a) Prove that

$$
\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-1}\left(1-\frac{1}{3}\right)^{-1} \ldots\left(1-\frac{1}{p_{m}}\right)^{-1}>\sum_{n=1}^{p_{m}} \frac{1}{n}
$$

b) Prove that $1+\sum_{n=1}^{m} \frac{1}{p_{n}}>\ln \ln p_{m}$.

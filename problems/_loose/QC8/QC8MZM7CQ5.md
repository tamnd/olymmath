Let $\theta$ be a random variable uniformly distributed on $[0,2 \pi)$, and $C$ be a random variable with a Cauchy distribution. Show that

$$
\operatorname{ctg} \theta \stackrel{d}{=} \operatorname{ctg} \frac{\theta}{2} \stackrel{d}{=} C \text { and } \cos ^{2} \theta \stackrel{d}{=} \cos ^{2} \frac{\theta}{2} \stackrel{d}{=} \frac{C^{2}}{1+C^{2}}
$$

Find the number of ways to partition a set of $10$ elements, $S = \{1, 2, 3, . . . , 10\}$ into two parts; that is, the number of unordered pairs $\{P, Q\}$ such that $P \cup Q = S$ and $P \cap Q = \emptyset$ .

The medians $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$, and $C C_{1}$ of triangle $A B C$ intersect at point $M$; $P$ is an arbitrary point. Line $l_{a}$ passes through point $A$ parallel to line $P A_{1}$; lines $l_{b}$ and $l_{c}$ are defined similarly. Prove that:

a) lines $l_{a}, l_{b}$, and $l_{c}$ intersect at a single point $Q$;

b) point $M$ lies on segment $P Q$, and $P M: M Q=1: 2$.

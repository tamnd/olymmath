Consider the distinct real numbers $a, b, c$ and the following system of equations in the set of real numbers:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x+y+z=1 \\
a x+b y+c z=1 \\
a^{3} x+b^{3} y+c^{3} z=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

a) Show that the system has a unique solution if and only if $a+b+c \neq 0$.

b) Solve the system in the case $a+b+c=0$. Discussion.

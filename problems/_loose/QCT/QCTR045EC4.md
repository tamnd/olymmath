On Sunday, Aunt Ernestina is supposed to come for lunch. My wife went out in the car. Whether she just wanted to take a drive or went to pick up Aunt Ernestina, who cannot get to us without help, I know absolutely nothing about. Of course, I could go and pick up our old aunt, who lives 10 km away, in the second car, but I don't want to drive around unnecessarily. Of course, I could call Aunt Ernestina on the phone and only go to pick her up if she hasn't left with my wife and is at home. But unfortunately, Aunt Ernestina has very poor hearing, and I have noticed that on average she fails to pick up the phone twice out of five times, even when she is at home. However, it is three times worse to not pick up Aunt Ernestina at all than to go for her in vain... So, I have three options:

1) not to go and pick up Aunt Ernestina;
2) to go and pick her up;
3) to call her on the phone and only go to pick her up if she answers.

What would you do in my place?

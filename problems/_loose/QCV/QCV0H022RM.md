Prove the inequality

$\frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{a\left(b+c\right)}+\frac{c^2+a^2-b^2}{b\left(c+a\right)}+\frac{a^2+b^2-c^2}{c\left(a+b\right)}\geq\frac32$

for any three positive reals $a$, $b$, $c$.

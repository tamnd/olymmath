In a gym, there is a student standing at each of $n$ positions. A ball can be safely passed from position $P_{1}$ to position $P_{2}$ if no other position lies within the circle with diameter $P_{1} P_{2}$. Let Ivo and Marica be two students in the gym, and let the ball initially be with Ivo. Prove that, regardless of which positions they are in, there exists a sequence of safe passes by which the ball can be passed from Ivo to Marica.

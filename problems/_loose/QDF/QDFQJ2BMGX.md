Participants of a chess tournament played one game against each other. For each participant $A$, the number of points scored (1 point for a win, $-1 / 2$ point for a draw, 0 points for a loss) and the strength coefficient were calculated using the formula: the sum of the points of the participants whom $A$ defeated, minus the sum of the points of the participants whom $A$ lost to.

a) Can the strength coefficients of all participants be greater than 0?

b) Can the strength coefficients of all participants be less than 0?

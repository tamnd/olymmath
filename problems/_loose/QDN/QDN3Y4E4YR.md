7・16 A deck of cards has $N$ cards, among which there are three $A$s. Now, the deck is shuffled randomly (assuming all possible distributions of the cards have an equal chance), and then the cards are flipped one by one from the top until the second $A$ appears. Prove that the expected (average) number of cards flipped is $\frac{N+1}{2}$.

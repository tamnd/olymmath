One, (50 points)
If there exists a point $I$ inside an acute triangle $\triangle XYZ$ such that the reflections of $I$ across the three sides of $\triangle XYZ$ form an equilateral triangle, then $I$ is called a regular point of $\triangle XYZ$. Now, construct acute triangles $\triangle ABN, \triangle ACM, \triangle BCL$ outward on the three sides of $\triangle ABC$ such that each of these triangles contains a regular point, and $\triangle ABN \sim \triangle AMC \sim \triangle LBC$. Prove that the regular points of $\triangle ABN, \triangle ACM, \triangle BCL$ form an equilateral triangle.

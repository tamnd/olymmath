We have 6 different colors and a large number of cubes. We paint each cube with all 6 colors, with each face painted one color. What is the maximum number of cubes we can paint so that each of them is painted differently? (If we can rotate one of the painted cubes so that the colors of the faces are arranged in the same way as on another cube, the cubes are considered to be painted the same.)

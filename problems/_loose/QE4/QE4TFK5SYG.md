In a country where residents' homes are represented as points on a plane, two laws are in effect:
1) A person can play basketball only if they are taller than the majority of their neighbors.
2) A person is entitled to free public transportation only if they are shorter than the majority of their neighbors.

In each law, a person's neighbors are all people living within a circle of a certain radius centered at the person's home. Each person can choose their own radius for the first law and a radius (not necessarily the same) for the second law. Can at least **90%** of the people in this country play basketball and at least **90%** of the people have the right to free public transportation?

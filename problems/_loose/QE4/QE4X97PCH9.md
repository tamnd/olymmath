Perpendiculars are drawn from the vertices $A, B, C, D$ to the plane of the parallelogram $A B C D$, on the same side of it, and points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, and $D^{\prime}$ are taken coplanar. It is known that $m(\widehat{B A D})=60^{\circ}, A B=A A^{\prime}=2 a, A D=B B^{\prime}=a$ and $C C^{\prime}=\frac{a}{2}$.

a) Show that $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is a parallelogram.

b) Determine the distance from $D^{\prime}$ to $B C$.

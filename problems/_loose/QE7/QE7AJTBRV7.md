Given is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ with $\angle BAD = \angle BCD$ and $\angle ABC < \angle ADC$ . Point $M$ is the midpoint of segment $AC$ . Prove that there exist points $X$ and $Y$ on the segments $AB$ and $BC$ , respectively, such that $XY \perp BD, MX = MY$ and $\angle XMY = \angle ADC - \angle ABC$ .

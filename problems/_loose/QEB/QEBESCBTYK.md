Manheim Theorem: If a fixed point $A$ is outside a fixed circle $\odot O$, and points $B$ and $C$ are on the tangents $A J$ and $A K$ to $\odot O$, respectively, and $B C$ is tangent to $\odot O$ at point $D$, then the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ is tangent to a certain fixed circle. ${ }^{[1]}$

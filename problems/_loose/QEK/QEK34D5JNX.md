The lateral edges of the tetrahedron $S A B C$ are mutually perpendicular. Prove that $P_{1}+P_{2}+P_{3} \geq \frac{9}{2} H^{2}$, where $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are the numerical values of the areas of the lateral faces of the tetrahedron, and $H$ is the numerical value of its height.

Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer, $p$ a prime number, $a$ an integer. Prove that if the equation $P(y) \equiv a(\bmod p)$ has a solution such that $P^{\prime}(y) \not \equiv 0(\bmod p)$, then the equation $P(x) \equiv a$ $\left(\bmod p^{n}\right)$ has a solution.

Let $f:[0,1) \to \mathbb{R}$ be a monotonic function. Prove that the limits $\lim_{x \nearrow 1} \int_0^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t$ and $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \left[ f(0) + f \left(\frac{1}{n}\right) + \ldots + f \left( \frac{n-1}{n} \right) \right]$ exist and are equal.

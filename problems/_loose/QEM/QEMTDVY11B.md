In the acute triangle $A B C$, the points $E, F, G$ are the feet of the altitudes drawn from the vertices $A, B, C$, respectively, and $H$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$. If $\overline{A B}=\overline{C H}$, prove that

$$
P_{A G F} \cdot \overline{B C}^{2}+P_{B E G} \cdot \overline{A C}^{2}=P_{C F E} \cdot \overline{A B}^{2}
$$

where $P_{A G F}, P_{B E G}, P_{C F E}$ are the areas of triangles $A G F, B E G, C F E$, respectively.

Let $f(X)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. Suppose that $p \mid f(n)$ implies $p^2 \mid f(n)$ for any integer $n$ and any prime number $p$. Show that $(X-k) \mid f(X)$ implies $(X-k)^2 \mid f(X)$ for any integer $k$.

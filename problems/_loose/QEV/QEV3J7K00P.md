In $\triangle A B C$, draw the perpendicular $C D$ from $C$ to $A B$ (with $D$ as the foot of the perpendicular), and then take a point $E$ on $A B$ such that $A E = B D$. Then the line segment through $E$ that intersects the two lines $A C$ and $B C$ is the smallest with respect to $A B$.

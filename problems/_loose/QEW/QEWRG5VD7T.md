In the plane, a line $p$ and a circle $k$ are given. Construct a triangle $ABC$ such that $k$ is its inscribed circle, its center lies on the quarter of the median to side $AB$, and vertex $C$ lies on the line $p$. Discuss the number of solutions $v$ depending on the relative position of the line $p$ and the circle $k$.

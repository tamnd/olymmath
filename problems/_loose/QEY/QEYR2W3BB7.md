(Constructions in $\mathbb{R}$) Let $f:[0,1] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{+}$ such that $f(1)=1$ and for all $x_{1}, x_{2}$ such that $x_{1}+x_{2} \leqslant 1, f\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right) \geqslant f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)$. Show that for any real $x$,

$$
f(x) \leqslant 2 x
$$

and that this constant cannot be improved.

(USSR 1974)

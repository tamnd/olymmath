In $\triangle A B C$, $B C=a, A C=b, A B=c, \angle C=90^{\circ} . C D$ and $B E$ are two medians of $\triangle A B C$, and $C D \perp B E$. Then $a: b: c=$
(A) $1: 2: 3$.
(B) $3: 2: 1$.
(C) $\sqrt{3}: \sqrt{2}: 1$.
(D) $1: \sqrt{2}: \sqrt{3}$.
(Anhui Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1997)

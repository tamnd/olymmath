a) In the decimal expression of a positive number, $a$ , all decimals beginning with the third after the decimal point, are deleted (i.e., we take an approximation of $a$ with accuracy to $0.01$ with deficiency). The number obtained is divided by $a$ and the quotient is similarly approximated with the same accuracy by a number $b$ . What numbers $b$ can be thus obtained? Write all their possible values.

b) same as (a) but with accuracy to $0.001$

c) same as (a) but with accuracy to $0.0001$

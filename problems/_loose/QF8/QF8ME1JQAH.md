Let ABC be a triangle and $\mathrm{H}$ its orthocenter. Let $\ell_{1}$ and $\ell_{2}$ be two lines passing through $H$ and perpendicular to each other. Let $X_{1}, Y_{1}$ and $Z_{1}$ be the points of intersection of $\ell_{1}$ with the lines (BC), (CA) and (AB) respectively. Also, let $X_{2}, Y_{2}$ and $Z_{2}$ be the points of intersection of $\ell_{2}$ with the lines (BC), (CA) and (AB) respectively. Show that the midpoints of the segments $\left[X_{1} X_{2}\right],\left[Y_{1} Y_{2}\right]$ and $\left[Z_{1} Z_{2}\right]$ are collinear.

The circles $ K' $ and $ K'' $ are externally tangent, and their external common tangent touches them at points $ A_1 $ and $ A_2 $. Let $ C_1 $ and $ C_2 $ be the centers of $ K' $ and $ K'' $ respectively, and let $ E $ be the intersection of the external and internal common tangents of these circles.

a) Prove that triangle $ C_1 E C_2 $ is a right triangle.

b) If the circles $ K' $ and $ K'' $ and the segment $ A_1 A_2 $ rotate around the line $ C_1 C_2 $, then the segment $ A_1 A_2 $ describes the lateral surface of a frustum of a cone, and the circles $ K' $ and $ K'' $ describe spheres. Determine the area $ M $ of the frustum of the cone.

c) If the radii $ r_1 $ and $ r_2 $ of the resulting spheres are variable, but their sum $ r_1 + r_2 = a $ is constant, determine the maximum possible value of the area $ M $.

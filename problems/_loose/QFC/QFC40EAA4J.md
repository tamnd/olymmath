29-1 (G55, Luxembourg) Consider two concentric circles on the same plane with radii $R$ and $r$ ($R > r$), respectively. Let $P$ be a fixed point on the smaller circle, $B$ be a moving point on the larger circle, and the line $BP$ intersects the larger circle again at $C$. The line $l$ through point $P$ and perpendicular to $BP$ intersects the smaller circle again at $A$ (if $l$ is tangent to the smaller circle at $P$, then $A = P$). Try to find:
(1) The set of values taken by the expression $A B^{2} + B C^{2} + C A^{2}$;
(2) The locus of the midpoint of segment $AB$.

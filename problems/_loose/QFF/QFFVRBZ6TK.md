Let be two matrices $ A,B\in M_2\left(\mathbb{R}\right) $ that satisfy the equality $ \left( A-B\right)^2 =O_2. $

**a)** Show that $ \det\left( A^2-B^2\right) =\left( \det A -\det B\right)^2. $
**b)** Demonstrate that $ \det\left( AB-BA\right) =0\iff \det A=\det B. $

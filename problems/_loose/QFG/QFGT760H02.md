It is to be proven: If $\left|x_{i}\right| \leq 1$ and $n$ is a natural number with $n \geq 2$, then

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2} \geq n \cdot \prod_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}
$$

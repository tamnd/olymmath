$$
r_{4}-r_{3}=r_{3}-r_{2}=r_{2}-r_{1}=r_{1} \text { holds. }
$$

Determine the ratio of the area of $K_{1}$ to the areas of the three annuli formed by $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$, $K_{2}$ and $K_{3}$, and $K_{3}$ and $K_{4}$!

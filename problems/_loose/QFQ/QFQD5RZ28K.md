Given two coprime numbers $ p > 0 $ and $ q > 0 $. An integer $ n $ is called good if it can be represented as $ n = px + qy $, where $ x $ and $ y $ are non-negative integers, and bad otherwise. a) Prove that there exists an integer $ c $ such that out of two integers $ n $ and $ c - n $, one is always good, and the other is bad. b) How many bad non-negative numbers are there?

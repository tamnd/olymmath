Let $\left\{X_{n}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ be a uniformly integrable family of non-negative random variables, and $M_{n}=\max \left\{X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}\right\}$. Prove that

$$
\frac{\mathrm{E} M_{n}}{n} \rightarrow 0, n \rightarrow \infty
$$

Establish that if in addition $X_{n} \preccurlyeq X$ for some non-negative random variable $X, \mathrm{E} X<\infty$, then

$$
\mathrm{P}(\xi>x) \leqslant \mathrm{P}(\eta>x)
$$

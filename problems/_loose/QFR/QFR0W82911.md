Find the largest real number $c$ , such that for any integer $s>1$ , and positive integers $m, n$ coprime to $s$ , we have $$ \sum_{j=1}^{s-1} \{ \frac{jm}{s} \}(1 - \{ \frac{jm}{s} \})\{ \frac{jn}{s} \}(1 - \{ \frac{jn}{s} \}) \ge cs $$ where $\{ x \} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor $ .

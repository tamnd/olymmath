Let $g$ be a straight line in the plane. The circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ lie on the same side of $g$ and touch $g$ at the points $A$ and $B$ respectively. Another circle $k_{3}$ touches $k_{1}$ in $D$ and $k_{2}$ in $C$. Prove that it holds:

(a) The quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a chordal quadrilateral.

(b) The straight lines $B C$ and $A D$ intersect at $k_{3}$.

a) Solve the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
a x+y=a^{2} \\
x+a y=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

For which values of $a$ does this system have no solutions and for which values does it have infinitely many solutions?

b) The same question for the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a x+y=a^{3} \\
x+a y=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

c) The same question for the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a x+y+z=1 \\
x+a y+z=a \\
x+y+a z=a^{2}
\end{array}\right.
$$

In the specified plane, a rectangular coordinate system is introduced, in which the flower (the common center of the circles) is at the point $O(0 ; 0)$. The bee moves clockwise, while the bumblebee moves counterclockwise. At the initial moment, the bee and the bumblebee are at points $M_{0}\left(-\frac{1}{2} ; \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ and $N_{0}(2 ; 0)$, respectively. Determine the coordinates of all positions of the bumblebee where the distance between it and the bee will be the shortest.

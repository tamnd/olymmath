Given a convex hexagon $A B C D E F$ that has both an incircle and a circumcircle, the incircles of $\triangle A B C, \triangle B C D, \triangle C D E, \triangle D E F, \triangle E F A, \triangle F A B$ are denoted as $\Gamma_{B}, \Gamma_{C}, \Gamma_{D}, \Gamma_{E}, \Gamma_{F}, \Gamma_{A}$, respectively. Let $l_{A B}$ be the external common tangent of $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{A}$ other than the tangent $A B$, and similarly define $l_{B C}, l_{C D}, l_{D E}, l_{E F}, l_{F A}$. Let the intersection of $l_{F A}$ and $l_{A B}$ be $A_{1}$, and similarly define $B_{1}, C_{1}, D_{1}, E_{1}, F_{1}$. If the hexagon $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1} E_{1} F_{1}$ is convex, prove: the diagonals of this hexagon are concurrent.

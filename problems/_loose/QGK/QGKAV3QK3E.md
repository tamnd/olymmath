In a triangle $A B C$ the following relation holds:

$$
\sin ^{23} \frac{\alpha}{2} \cdot \cos ^{48} \frac{\beta}{2}=\sin ^{23} \frac{\beta}{2} \cdot \cos ^{48} \frac{\alpha}{2}
$$

where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the corresponding angles at $A$ and $B$. Find the ratio $A C / B C$.

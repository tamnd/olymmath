Five patricians each have five statues, each costing a natural number of sesterces. First, the patricians compared who had the greater total cost of their statues, then each of them smashed their cheapest statue. Then they compared again, smashed their cheapest remaining statues, and so on, three more times, until all the statues were destroyed. Could it have been that each time a new sole winner emerged?

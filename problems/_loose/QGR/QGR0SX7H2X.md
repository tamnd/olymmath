An integer $n$ is friendly when there are positive integers $a, b$, and $c$ such that $a<b<c$ and $n=a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}$. For example, the numbers 1 and 2 are friendly, since $1=4^{2}+7^{2}-8^{2}$ and $2=5^{2}+11^{2}-12^{2}$.

a) Verify that $(3 x+1)^{2}+(4 x+2)^{2}-(5 x+2)^{2}$ is equal to $2 x+1$, for any $x$.

b) Find integers $m$ and $n$ such that $(3 x-m)^{2}+(4 x-n)^{2}-(5 x-5)^{2}=2 x$, for any $x$.

c) Show that the number 4 is friendly.

d) Show that all positive integers are friendly.

Given two circles lying outside each other. Let $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ be the farthest points from each other where these circles intersect their line of centers, such that $A_{1}$ lies on the first circle, and $A_{2}$ lies on the second. From point $A_{1}$, two rays are drawn tangent to the second circle, and a circle $K_{1}$ is constructed that is tangent to these rays and the first circle from the inside. From point $A_{2}$, two rays are drawn tangent to the first circle, and a circle $K_{2}$ is constructed that is tangent to these rays and the second circle from the inside. Prove that the circles $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$ are equal.

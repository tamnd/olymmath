Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle at $A$ and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. Let $\gamma$ be the circle tangent to the sides $[AB]$ and $[AC]$ and to $\Gamma$ internally. We denote $P, Q$ and $R$ the points of tangency of $\gamma$ with $[AB], [AC]$ and $\Gamma$ respectively. We denote $O$ the center of $\gamma$, $J$ the midpoint of $[PQ]$, and $K$ the midpoint of $[BC]$. Show that:

$$
\frac{AJ}{AK} = \frac{AO}{AR}
$$

Deduce that $J$ is the center of the inscribed circle of $ABC$.

Marie-Luise wants to specify a two-digit natural number $z$ that simultaneously meets the following conditions (1), (2), (3):

(1) The number $z$ is not divisible by 10.

(2) If the units digit of the number $z$ is increased by 4, the result is the tens digit of $z$.

(3) If the digits of $z$ are swapped, the resulting number is such that its triple is less than 100.

Determine all numbers $z$ that satisfy the given conditions!

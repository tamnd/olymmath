A school is hosting a Mathematics Culture Festival. According to statistics, there were more than 980 (no less than 980, less than 990) students visiting the school that day. Each student visits for a period of time and then leaves (and does not return). If, regardless of how these students arrange their visit times, we can always find $k$ students such that either all $k$ students are in the school at the same time, or at no time are any two of them in the school at the same time. What is the maximum value of $k$?

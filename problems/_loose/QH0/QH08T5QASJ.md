Prove that there are exactly eight ways for a knight to tour a $3 \times 4$ chessboard, visiting each square exactly once, and that there is no way for the knight to return to the starting square on its last move. (Two tours of the board that are mirror images of each other, obtained by making the same moves in reverse order, are considered the same.)

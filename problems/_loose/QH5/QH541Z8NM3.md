Stirling's Problem (from 9th grade, 5 points). An incredible legend says that one day Stirling ${ }^{4}$ was contemplating Stirling numbers of the second kind and, in a pensive mood, was tossing 10 fair dice onto the table. After one of the tosses, he suddenly noticed that the combination of points that came up included all the numbers from 1 to 6. Stirling immediately wondered what the probability of such an event would be. What is the probability that when 10 dice are rolled, each number of points from 1 to 6 will appear at least once?

Let $S$ be the vertex of an acute angle, whose sides are denoted by $s_{1}$ and $s_{2}$. It is assumed that on the ray $s_{1}$ there are two points $A, B$ different from each other and from $S$, and that on the ray $s_{2}$ there are three points $C, D, E$ different from each other and from $S$, such that the following holds:

The points $S, A, B$ are arranged on $s_{1}$ in this order; the points $S, C, D, E$ are arranged on $s_{2}$ in this order; it is $S C=C A=A D=D B=B E$ and $S B=S E$.

Determine the size $\alpha$ of the angle $\angle B S E$ from these assumptions!

(GDR, 74). a) Prove that there does not exist a polynomial $P(x)$ for which for any $x \in \mathbf{R}$ the inequalities are satisfied: 1) $P^{\prime}(x)>P^{\prime \prime}(x)$ and 2) $P(x)>P^{\prime \prime}(x)$.

b) Will the statement in a) be true if inequality 1) is replaced by the inequality $\left.1^{\prime}\right) P(x)>P^{\prime}(x)$?

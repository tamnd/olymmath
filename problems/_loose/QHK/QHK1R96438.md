On a circle, twelve points are marked, similar to the numbers on a clock face. Exactly one game piece is placed on each of these points. Two players $A$ and $B$ take turns to either remove exactly one piece or exactly two pieces that lie on adjacent points.

Player $A$ starts. The player who takes the last game piece wins.

How can player $B$ proceed to win in every case?

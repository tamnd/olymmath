Board $P$ is obtained by removing three corner squares from a $7 \times 7$ board. Each of the 46 squares on board $P$ is filled with a natural number. The difference between the numbers in any two adjacent squares (sharing a side) is at most 4. Prove that the same number is written in at least two squares.

A tetrahedron of volume $V$ has planes parallel to its faces drawn through an internal point. The volumes of the 4 smaller tetrahedrons formed are $V_{1}, V_{2}, V_{3}, V_{4}$.

Show that

$$
\sqrt[3]{V}=\sqrt[3]{V_{1}}+\sqrt[3]{V_{2}}+\sqrt[3]{V_{3}}+\sqrt[3]{V_{4}}
$$

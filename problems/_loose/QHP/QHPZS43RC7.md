Given an integer $\mathrm{n} \geq 4$, let $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B} \subseteq \{1,2, \ldots, \mathrm{n}\}$, for any $\mathrm{a} \in \mathrm{A}, \mathrm{b} \in \mathrm{B}$, it holds that $\mathrm{ab}+1$ is a perfect square. Prove: $\min \{|\mathrm{A}|,|\mathrm{B}|\} \leq \log _{2} \mathrm{n}$.

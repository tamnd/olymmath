Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, where $a_{n} \in \mathbf{R}_{+}$, and
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a_{1}=1, a_{2}=10, \\
a_{n}^{2} a_{n-2}=10 a_{n-1}^{3}(n=3,4, \ldots) .
\end{array}
$$

Find $a_{n}$.

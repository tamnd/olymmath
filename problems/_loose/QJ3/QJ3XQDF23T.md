It turns out that triangles $A B P$ and $D E Q$ are isosceles triangles with the angle at the vertex (exactly at the vertex, not at the base) equal to 80 degrees. Find the value of angle $A C E$, given that triangles $A P O$ and $E Q O$ are also isosceles.

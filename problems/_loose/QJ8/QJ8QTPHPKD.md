In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$. We draw circles with radius $BC$ first around $B$ - this circle intersects the line $AC$ at a point $D$ different from $C$ -, and then around $D$, one of the intersection points of this circle with the line $AC$ is $E$. We find that the lines $BD$ and $BE$ are reflections of each other over the angle bisector of the triangle from $B$. Prove that in this case $EA = EB$.

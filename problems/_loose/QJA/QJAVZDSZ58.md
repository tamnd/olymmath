Let $\mu$ be a finitely additive measure on the algebra $\mathscr{A}$, the sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots$ belong to $\mathscr{A}$, are pairwise disjoint, and $A=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_{n} \in \mathscr{A}$. Show that $\mu(A) \geqslant \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mu\left(A_{n}\right)$.

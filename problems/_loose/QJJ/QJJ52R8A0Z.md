Prove that for any positive real numbers $a, b, c$,

$$
\frac{1}{a^{2}-a b+b^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}-b c+c^{2}}+\frac{1}{c^{2}-c a+a^{2}} \leqq \frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}+\frac{1}{c^{2}}
$$

Determine when equality holds.

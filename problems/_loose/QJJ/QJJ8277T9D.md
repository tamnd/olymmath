Let $ D$ be a point on $ \left[AC\right]$ and $ E$ be a point on $ \left[BC\right]$ such that $ \left|AD\right| = 4$ and $ Area\left(ABC\right) = 2Area\left(CDE\right)$. Find $ Area\left(ABE\right)$.

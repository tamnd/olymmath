Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $H$ be its orthocenter. We denote $B'$ and $C'$ as points on the lines $(HB)$ and $(HC)$ such that $(B'C')$ is parallel to $(BC)$. Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of triangle $B'H C'$. Suppose that the center of circle $\omega$ lies on the segment $[BC]$. Show that the circle $\omega$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

Let $A B C D$ be a tetrahedron, and let points $K, L, M, N$ be the midpoints of segments $[B C],[C D],[A K]$ and respectively $[A L]$, and let point $P$ be the intersection of lines $B L$ and $D K$. Prove that the line of intersection of planes $(B M P)$ and $(D N P)$ is parallel to plane $(A B D)$.

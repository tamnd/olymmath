We have a complete set of dominoes. Two players play, each in turn, according to the following rule: The player chooses a domino from those not yet used, and places it at one of the ends of the already formed chain, so that two adjacent dominos always have the same numbers marked on their adjacent squares.

The first player who cannot play loses.

Determine which of the two players has a winning strategy.

Let complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}$ satisfy $\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{1}\right)>0$, $\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{2}\right)>0$, and $\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{1}^{2}\right)=\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{2}^{2}\right)=2$ (where $\operatorname{Re}(z)$ denotes the real part of the complex number $z$).
(1) Find the minimum value of $\operatorname{Re}\left(z_{1} z_{2}\right)$;
(2) Find the minimum value of $\left|z_{1}+2\right|+\left|\overline{z_{2}}+2\right|-\left|\overline{z_{1}}-z_{2}\right|$.

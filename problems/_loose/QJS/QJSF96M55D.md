The legs of a compass are located at the nodes of an infinite sheet of graph paper, the cells of which are squares with a side length of 1. It is allowed, without changing the setting of the compass, to rotate it around one of the legs to move the second leg to another node on the sheet. Is it possible to swap the legs of the compass in several such steps?

Let $n$ be a natural number. Determine (in terms of $n$) the smallest natural number $m$ for which it is possible to color exactly $n$ cells of an infinite square grid such that the following condition holds:

- Every rectangle with sides along the grid lines, whose two opposite corners are colored, contains at most $m$ colored cells.

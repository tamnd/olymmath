On the sides of a convex quadrilateral with perpendicular diagonals, similar isosceles triangles are constructed outside it, the axes of symmetry of which are the perpendicular bisectors of the sides of the given quadrilateral. Prove that the vertices of these isosceles triangles, from which the sides of the given quadrilateral are seen at equal angles, are the vertices of a quadrilateral with equal diagonals.

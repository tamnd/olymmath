$\mathbf{a} 2 \mathbf{2 a}$ ** Let $P$ be a point outside (or inside) $\odot O$. Then any moving chord through $P$ intersects the circle at two points. On this line, find another point $Q$ inside (or outside) the circle, and the four points form a harmonic range, then the locus of $Q$ is a straight line, which is called the polar line of $P$; conversely, if there is a straight line, there must be a corresponding point $P$ (pole) existing.

Given that quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in circle $\Gamma$, $AB > CD$, $AB$ and $CD$ are not parallel, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $E$, the projection of $E$ on $AB$ is $F$, and $EF$ bisects $\angle CFD$. Prove: $AB$ is the diameter of circle $\Gamma$.

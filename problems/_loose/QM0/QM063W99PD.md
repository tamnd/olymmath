Given a pyramid $M A B C D$ with a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ as its base. A plane intersects the edges $M A, M B, M C$, and $M D$ at points $K, L, P$, and $N$ respectively. Prove that the following relation holds:

$$
S_{B C D} \frac{|M A|}{|M K|}+S_{A D B} \frac{|M C|}{|M P|}=S_{A B C} \frac{|M D|}{|M N|}+S_{A C D} \frac{|M B|}{|M L|}
$$

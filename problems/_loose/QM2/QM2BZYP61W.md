(Image) Let $(G, *)$ and $(H, \circledast)$ be two groups and $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ a group homomorphism. Show that $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is a subgroup of $H$.

Definition 73. (Kernel of a group homomorphism) Let $(G, *)$ and $(H, \circledast)$ be two groups and $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ a group homomorphism. The set $\operatorname{Ker}(\varphi)=\left\{x \in G \mid \varphi(x)=e_{H}\right\}$ is called the kernel of $\varphi$.

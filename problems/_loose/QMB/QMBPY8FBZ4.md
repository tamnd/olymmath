Uli has four different types of steel balls, labeled $A, B, C$, and $D$. Balls of the same type always have the same weight. Using a balance scale, he found that two balls of type $B$ weigh exactly as much as one ball of type $A$. He also found that three balls of type $C$ weigh as much as one ball of type $B$, and that five balls of type $D$ weigh the same as one ball of type $C$.

a) How many balls of type $D$ must Uli place in one (empty) scale pan if they are to balance one ball of type $A$ in the other scale pan?

b) In one scale pan, there are 20 balls of type $D$ and 5 balls of type $C$. How many balls of type $B$ must Uli place in the other (empty) scale pan to achieve balance?

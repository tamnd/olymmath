In triangle $A B C$, points $M$ and $L$ on side $B C$ are the feet of the median and the angle bisector, respectively, drawn from vertex $A$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $L$ to sides $A B$ and $A C$, respectively. Point $X$ on median $A M$ is such that $X L \perp B C$. Prove that points $P, X$, and $Q$ lie on the same line.

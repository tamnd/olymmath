If $x, y, z$ and $w$ are positive real numbers such that

$$
\frac{x}{y+z+w}+\frac{y}{z+w+x}+\frac{z}{w+x+y}+\frac{w}{x+y+z}=1
$$

determine

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{y+z+w}+\frac{y^{2}}{z+w+x}+\frac{z^{2}}{w+x+y}+\frac{w^{2}}{x+y+z}
$$

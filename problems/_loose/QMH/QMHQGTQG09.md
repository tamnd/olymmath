Let the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ be $\odot O, \angle BAC = 90^{\circ}, \angle ABC < \angle ACB$. Draw the tangent to $\odot O$ at point $A$, intersecting $BC$ at point $D$. The reflection of point $A$ over $BC$ is point $E$, and $AH \perp BE$ at point $H$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of segment $AH$, and let $BN$ intersect $\odot O$ again at point $P$. Prove:
(1) $A, N, M, P$ are concyclic;
(2) $BD$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle ADP$.

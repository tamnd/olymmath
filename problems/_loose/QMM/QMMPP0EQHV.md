Show that every natural number $n\leq2^{1 000 000}$ can be obtained first with 1 doing less than $1 100 000$ sums; more precisely, there is a finite sequence of natural numbers $x_0,\ x_1,\dots,\ x_k\text{ with }k\leq1 100 000,\ x_0=1,\ x_k=n$ such that for all $i=1,\ 2,\dots,\ k$ there exist $r,\ s$ with $0\leq{r}\leq{s}<i$ such that $x_i=x_r+x_s$ .

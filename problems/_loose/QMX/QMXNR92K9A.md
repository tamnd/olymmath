Fill in one of the three letters A, B and C in each grid of a $2\times 2$ table. How many ways are there to fill in, such that the letters in any two grids with a common side are different?

Remark: It is legal that some character may not be written even once in the square. If two writing methods can be the same only after being rotated or flipped, we still treat them as two different methods.

If $U, V, W, X, Y, Z$ are six points on a circle, the points of intersection of the pairs of lines

$$
(U V, X Y),(V W, Y Z),(W X, Z U)
$$

lie on a line, called the Pascal line of the hexagon $(U, V, W, X, Y, Z)$. Show that if $A, a, B, b, C, c$ are six points on a circle, then the Pascal lines of the hexagons

$$
(A, a, B, b, C, c),(A, b, B, c, C, a),(A, c, B, a, C, b)
$$

are concurrent.

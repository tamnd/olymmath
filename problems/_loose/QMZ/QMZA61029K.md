Let $x_{1}<x_{2}<\ldots<x_{n}$ be an arithmetic progression consisting of natural numbers, and $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ are coprime. Suppose the product of the terms of this progression has $m<n$ prime divisors. Prove that $x_{1}^{n-m} \leqslant(n-1)!$

Find all ordered triples of positive integers $(a, b, p)$ such that $p$ is a prime, $(a-1, b+1)=1$, and there exist $p-1$ odd integers $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{p-1}$ greater than 1, satisfying $a^{p}-p=\sum_{i=1}^{p-1} b^{c_{i}}$.

34-5 (F14, Germany) Let $\mathbf{N}^{*}=\{1,2,3, \cdots\}$. Argue whether there exists a function $f: \mathbf{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbf{N}^{*}$, such that
(1) $f(1)=2$;
(2) $f(f(n))=f(n)+n$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$;
(3) $f(n)<f(n+1)$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$.

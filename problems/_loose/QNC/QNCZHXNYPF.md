4 variables $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \ldots, x_{2012}$ belong to the interval $[0 ; 1]$. Prove the inequality

$$
\mathrm{x}_{1} \mathrm{x}_{2} \mathrm{x}_{3} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{2012}+\left(1-\mathrm{x}_{1}\right)\left(1-\mathrm{x}_{2}\right)\left(1-\mathrm{x}_{3}\right) \ldots\left(1-\mathrm{x}_{2012}\right) \leq 1
$$

For what values of $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \ldots, x_{2012}$ can equality be achieved?

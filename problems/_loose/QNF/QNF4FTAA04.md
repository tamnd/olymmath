B4 In a cube with edge 6, there is a sphere with diameter 6. The sphere thus touches the six faces of the cube internally. We imagine the cube divided into 216 unit cubes of $1 \times 1 \times 1$. How many of these unit cubes lie entirely within the sphere? A unit cube with a vertex on the sphere's surface and otherwise inside the sphere is counted as lying entirely within the sphere.

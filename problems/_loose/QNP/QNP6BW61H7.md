Two sequences $\{a_i\}$ and $\{b_i\}$ are defined as follows: $\{ a_i \} = 0, 3, 8, \dots, n^2 - 1, \dots$ and $\{ b_i \} = 2, 5, 10, \dots, n^2 + 1, \dots $ . If both sequences are defined with $i$ ranging across the natural numbers, how many numbers belong to both sequences?

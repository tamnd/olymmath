A $k$-coloring graph is critically $k$-chromatic, or simply critical, if for any $v \in V(G)$, we have $\chi(G-v)<k$. Prove: Any $k$-coloring graph has a critical $k$-chromatic induced subgraph, and the minimum degree of any such subgraph is at least $k-1$.

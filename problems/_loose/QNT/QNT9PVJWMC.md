$A B$ and $C D$ are two segments, both 4 units long, sharing the midpoint $M$ and such that $B \widehat{M} D=60^{\circ}$. Let $X$ be the set of all and only the points that are at most 1 unit away from at least one of the two segments. What is the area of $X$?
(A) $8-\frac{4}{3} \sqrt{3}$
(B) $16-\frac{8}{3} \sqrt{3}$
(C) $16-\frac{4}{3} \sqrt{3}+\pi$
(D) $16-\frac{8}{3} \sqrt{3}+2 \pi$
(E) $8+2 \pi$.

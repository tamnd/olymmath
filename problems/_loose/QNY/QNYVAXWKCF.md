The lengths of the sides of a rectangle are integers $x$ and $y$ greater than 1. In the rectangle, we mark a division into $x \cdot y$ unit squares and then form the lateral surface of a cylinder by rolling and gluing two opposite sides. Each pair of vertices of the unit squares on the lateral surface is connected by a line segment. How many of these line segments pass through the interior points of the cylinder? In the case $x>y$, determine when this number will be greater - when the circumference of the base of the cylinder is equal to $x$, or $y$?

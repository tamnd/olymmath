In a country, there are $n \geqslant 5$ cities served by two airlines. Every pair of cities is connected, possibly by one of these airlines. However, each airline is prohibited from offering a cycle of length strictly less than 6. Show that the two airlines together have fewer than $\left[\frac{n^{2}}{3}\right]$ flights.

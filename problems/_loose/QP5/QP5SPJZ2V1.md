Given that $A B$ is the diameter of $\odot O$, $C$ and $D$ are two points on the circumference different from points $A$ and $B$ and on the same side of $A B$. The tangents to the circle at points $C$ and $D$ intersect at point $E$. The line segment $A D$ intersects $B C$ at point $F$, and the line $E F$ intersects $A B$ at point $M$. Prove: $E$, $C$, $M$, and $D$ are concyclic.

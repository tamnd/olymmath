Joe, who already has a son Tom and a daughter Anna, wants to have twins. If the newborns are both boys, then Tom's number of brothers will be exactly $k$ times his number of sisters; if the newborns are both girls, then Anna's number of brothers will be exactly $t$ times her number of sisters. If the newborns are one boy and one girl, find:
(1) How many times the number of brothers is the number of sisters for the boy?
(2) How many times the number of brothers is the number of sisters for the girl?

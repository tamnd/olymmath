Two people, A and B, start from point $M$ to point $N$ at the same time. A walks at a speed of $a$ kilometers per hour for half the time and at a speed of $b$ kilometers per hour for the other half. B walks at a speed of $a$ kilometers per hour for half the distance and at a speed of $b$ kilometers per hour for the other half. If $a \neq b$, then the order of their arrival at point $N$ is
(A) Both arrive at the same time.
(B) A arrives first.
(C) B arrives first.
(D) If $a > b$, A arrives first; if $a < b$, B arrives first.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

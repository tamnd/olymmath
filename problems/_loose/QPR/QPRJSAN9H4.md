Assume $n$ pairwise distinct real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ (positive integer $\left.n \geqslant 4\right)$ satisfy the conditions
$$
x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}=0
$$

and
$$
x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}=1 \text {. }
$$

Prove: There must exist 4 different real numbers $a, b, c, d$ among $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$, such that $a+b+c+n a b c \leqslant x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+\cdots+x_{n}^{3} \leqslant a+b+d+n a b d$.
(45th Polish Mathematical Olympiad, 1994)

12 tennis players participated in a tournament. It is known that every two players played against each other exactly once and there was no player who lost all their matches. Prove that there will be players $A, B, C$ such that $A$ won against $B$, $B$ won against $C$, and $C$ won against $A$. (In tennis, there are no ties.)

(Liste courte 2009) In 2009, 2009 cards, each having a blue side and a yellow side, are lined up on a (large) table, all blue side up. Two people situated on the same side of the table play a game where they take turns. A move consists of choosing a block of 50 cards whose first card is blue and flipping all these cards. When a person can no longer play, they have lost.

(a) Does the game necessarily end?

(b) Does someone have a winning strategy?

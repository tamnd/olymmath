Five points $A, B, C, D$ and $E$ lie on a circle $\tau$ clockwise in that order such that $A B$ is parallel to $C E$ and $\measuredangle A B C>90^{\circ}$. Let $k$ be a circle tangent to $A D, C E$ and $\tau$ such that the circles $k$ and $\tau$ touch on the $\operatorname{arc} E D$ which doesn't contain $A, B$ and $C$. Let $F \neq A$ be the intersection of $\tau$ and the line tangent to $k$ passing through $A$ different from $A D$.

Prove that there exists a circle tangent to $B D, B F, C E$ and $\tau$.

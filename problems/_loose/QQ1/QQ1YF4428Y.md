Given points $A$ and $B$ on parallel lines $a$ and $b$ respectively, such that $AB$ is perpendicular to $a$. For any point $M$ on line $a$, we draw the circle $k$ with diameter $AM$. The line passing through the intersection points of the Thales circle of segment $AB$ and circle $k$ intersects line $b$ at point $P$. The perpendicular to $b$ at $P$ intersects circle $k$ at points $Q$ and $R$.

What is the geometric locus of points $Q$ and $R$ as $M$ runs along line $a$?

Let $S_k$ be the set of all triplets of real numbers $(a, b, c)$ satisfying $a <k (b + c)$, $b <k (c + a)$, and $c <k (a + b)$. For what value of $k$ then $S_k$ is a subset of $\{(a, b, c) | ab + bc + ca> 0\}$ ?

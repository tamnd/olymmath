Let the sequence of positive integers $\left\{g_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $g_{1}=1$, and for any $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, we have $g_{n+1}=g_{n}^{2}+g_{n}+1$. Prove: For any $n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, we have
$$
\left(g_{n}^{2}+1\right) \mid\left(g_{n+1}^{2}+1\right) .
$$

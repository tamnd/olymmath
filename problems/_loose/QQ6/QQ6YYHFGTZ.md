28 A regular octahedron, using red and blue to color 6 vertices; using yellow and green to color 8 faces, find the number of schemes where 4 vertices are red, 2 vertices are blue, and the faces are 4 yellow and 4 green.
Note: A regular octahedron can be viewed as the dual of a cube, with each face represented by its center, and 3 faces intersecting at a vertex corresponding to a triangle formed by 3 centers, thus forming a geometric figure with 6 vertices and 8 faces.

$n$ points are placed on a circle with diameter $n / \pi$. It is assumed that the length of any arc between two of these $n$ points is strictly greater than the number of points on that arc (excluding the points at the endpoints). Show that the circle can be divided into $n$ arcs of the same length, each containing exactly one point.

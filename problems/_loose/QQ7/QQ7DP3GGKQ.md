In a given circle, let $AB$ be a side of an inscribed square. Extend $AB$ to $C$ so that $BC = AB$. If we connect $C$ with the center $O$ of the circle and $OC$ intersects the circle at $D$, then $CD$ is equal to twice the side of a regular decagon inscribed in the circle.

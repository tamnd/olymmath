Let the distances from some point $M$ to the vertices $A$, $B$, and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ be expressed by the numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$. Prove that for no $d \neq 0$ and for no point on the plane can the distances to the vertices in the same order be expressed by the numbers $\sqrt{a^{2}+d}, \sqrt{b^{2}+d}, \sqrt{c^{2}+d}$.

Let $\triangle OAB$ and $\triangle OA^{\prime}B^{\prime}$ be equilateral triangles such that $\angle AOA^{\prime}=90^{\circ},$ $\angle BOB^{\prime}=90^{\circ},$ and $\angle AOB^{\prime}$ is obtuse. Given that the side length of $\triangle OA^{\prime}B^{\prime}$ is $1$ and the circumradius of $\triangle OAB^{\prime}$ is $\sqrt{61},$ compute the side length of $\triangle AOB.$

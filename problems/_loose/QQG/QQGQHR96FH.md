A snail crawls forward at a non-uniform speed (without moving backward). Several people observe its crawling in sequence within 6 minutes. Each person starts observing before the previous one finishes, and each observes for exactly 1 minute. It is known that each person observes the snail crawling 1 meter in their 1-minute observation period. Prove that the distance the snail crawls in these 6 minutes does not exceed 10 meters.

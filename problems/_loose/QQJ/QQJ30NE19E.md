Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle. Let $k_{a}, k_{b}$, and $k_{c}$ be its excircles opposite to vertices $A, B$, and $C$, respectively. Let $O$ and $I$ be the centers of the circumcircle and the incircle. Denote by $t_{a}, t_{b}$, and $t_{c}$ the external common tangents of the pairs of circles $k_{b}$ and $k_{c}, k_{c}$ and $k_{a}, k_{a}$ and $k_{b}$, respectively, different from the lines $B C, C A$, and $A B$. Prove that the center of the incircle of the triangle determined by the lines $t_{a}$, $t_{b}$, and $t_{c}$ lies on the line $O I$.

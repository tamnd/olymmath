Let $f(x)$ be a convex function, and $p$ and $q$ be positive numbers whose sum is 1.

a) Prove that if the numbers $p$ and $q$ are rational, then $f\left(p x_{1}+q x_{2}\right) \leqslant p f\left(x_{1}\right)+q f\left(x_{2}\right)$.

b) Prove that if the function $f(x)$ is continuous, then $f\left(p x_{1}+q x_{2}\right) \leqslant p f\left(x_{1}\right)+q f\left(x_{2}\right)$.

Two young men and three girls went on a hiking trip. They had backpacks, the total weight of which was 44 kg. The backpacks of the young men weighed the same, and the backpacks of the girls also weighed the same. To ensure that one of the girls always walked without a load and rested on the way, the young men carried the girls' backpacks, one on each shoulder and one together. The young men's backpacks were carried alternately by two girls. Thus, the load of each, except for the resting girl, increased by the same weight. Determine the weight of a girl's backpack and the weight of a young man's backpack.

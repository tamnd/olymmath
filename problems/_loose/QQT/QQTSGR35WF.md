Aksinya, Darina, Milana, Ratibor, and Yaropolk are circus trainers. Their ages are 18, 19, 20, 22, and 25 years. Their wards are a fox, a parrot, a tiger, a walrus, and a goat. The trainers' acts are called "Eastern Tale," "Around the World," "Leap over the Abyss," "Spring Melody," and "Riddle of the Sphinx." They rehearse with their wards at different times: 9:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 14:00. Determine the age, ward, act name, and rehearsal time of each trainer, if:

- Ratibor's fox rehearses earlier than Yaropolk's ward, but later than the goat, which does not work with Darina.
- The 19-year-old trainer has a walrus in "Spring Melody" that rehearses later than Aksinya's ward.
- The rehearsal of the act "Around the World," which starts later than 10:00, does not involve the fox. The circus artist in this act is younger than the trainer in "Eastern Tale," but older than Yaropolk.
- Aksinya's ward performs later than the tiger, but earlier than the animal from the act "Riddle of the Sphinx."
- Milana, whose ward is not a goat and who does not participate in the act "Leap over the Abyss," is younger than Ratibor.
- The tiger, which is not trained by Darina, starts rehearsing at an even hour.

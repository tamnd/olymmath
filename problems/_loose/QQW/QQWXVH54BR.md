For every positive integer $n$ , define $f(n)$ as the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(x, y)$ where $x,y \le n$ such that the greatest common divisor of $x, y,$ and $n$ is equal to $1$ . Prove that for any composite number $n$ ,
$$ f(n) \le n^2 - n $$ and determine all positive integers $n$ such that equality holds.

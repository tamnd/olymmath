Given collinear points $A, O$, and $B$, with $O \in (AB)$. On the same side of line $AB$, consider points $C$ and $D$, such that angles $\widehat{AOC}$ and $\widehat{COD}$ are adjacent. Let $[OM$ be the bisector of angle $\widehat{AOC}$ and $[ON$ be the bisector of angle $\widehat{BOD}$. It is known that $\mathrm{m}(\widehat{MOD})=105^{\circ}$ and $\mathrm{m}(\widehat{NOC})=120^{\circ}$.

a) Show that angle $\widehat{COD}$ is a right angle.

b) If 12 distinct rays with origin $O$ are constructed inside angle $\widehat{COD}$, such that the 13 angles formed, with disjoint interiors, have measures expressed by non-zero natural numbers, prove that at least two of these angles are congruent.

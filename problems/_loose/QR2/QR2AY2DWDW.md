Property of medians. Let a triangle $A B C$ be given. Further, let $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ lie strictly inside the segments $B C, C A$ and $A B$, respectively, and $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$ intersect at point $G$, with $\frac{A G}{G A^{\prime}}=\frac{B G}{G B^{\prime}}=$ $\frac{C G}{G C^{\prime}}$. Prove that $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$ are the medians of triangle $A B C$. How will this statement change if the words "strictly inside the segments" are replaced by the words "on the lines"?

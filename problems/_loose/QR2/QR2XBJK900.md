Let $\ell_1$ intersect the $ x$ -axis at $B$ , and $\ell_2$ intersects the $ x$ and $y$ axes at $C$ and $D$ , respectively. Suppose that line $BD$ has slope $-m$ and $BD = 2$ . Compute the length of $CD$ .

For a positive integer $n \geqslant 2$, consider a set of distinct integers $F$, where $0$ does not belong to $F$, and each integer in $F$ is the sum of two integers in $F$. The sum of any $S$ (allowing repetition, $2 \leqslant S \leqslant n$) integers in $F$ is not zero. Prove that: $|F| \geqslant 2 n+2$.

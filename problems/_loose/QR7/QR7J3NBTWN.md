(53rd Romanian Mathematical Olympiad (2nd Round) Problem) For a positive integer $n(n \geq 2)$, define $f(n)$ as the minimum number of elements in a set $S$ that satisfies the following conditions: the smallest element $1 \in S$, the largest element $n \in S$, and every element other than 1 is the sum of two elements in $S$ (which can be the same). Prove:
(1) $f(n) \geqslant\left[\log _{2} n\right]+1$;
(2) There are infinitely many $n$ such that $f(n)=f(n+1)$.

Martina came up with a procedure for generating a numerical sequence. She started with the number 128. From it, she derived the next member of the sequence as follows: $8^{2}+5=64+5=69$. Then she continued in the same way, and from the number 69, she got $9^{2}+5=81+5=86$. Each time, she took the digit in the units place of the previous member of the sequence, squared it, and added the constant 5.

1. What is the 2011th number in this sequence?
2. Martina started again with the number 128, but instead of the number 5, she chose a different natural number as the constant. This time, she got the number 16 on the 2011th place. What constant did she choose in this case?

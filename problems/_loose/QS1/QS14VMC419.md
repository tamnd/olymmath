Prove that $\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-a b-b c-a c\right)\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}-x y-y z-x z\right)=X^{2}+Y^{2}+Z^{2}-X Y-Y Z-X Z$,

if $X=a x+c y+b z, Y=c x+b y+a z, Z=b x+a y+c z$.

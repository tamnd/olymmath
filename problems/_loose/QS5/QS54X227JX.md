2.11 Let $f(x)$ be a complex-coefficient polynomial with a degree less than 3. Prove: There exist three complex-coefficient polynomials $f_{j}(x), j=1,2,3$, satisfying:
(1) $f_{1}(x)+f_{2}(x)+f_{3}(x)=f(x)$;
(2) $\operatorname{deg} f_{j}(x) \leqslant \operatorname{deg} f(x), j=1,2,3$;
(3) Each root of $f_{j}(x)$ is real, $j=1,2,3$.

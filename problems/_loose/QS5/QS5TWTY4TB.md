An $m \times n$ rectangle can be
repeatedly covered, and each cell has the same
overlap degree if and only if it is not one of the following cases: $1 \times n, m \times 1,(2 m+1) \times 3,3 \times(2 n+1), 5$ $\times 5,5 \times 7,7 \times 5$.

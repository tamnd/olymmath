In a school, no student has read all the books in the school library, and it is known that any two books in the library have been read by at least one student. Can we find two students, Jia and Yi, and three books $A$, $B$, $C$, such that Jia has read $A$ and $B$ but not $C$, and Yi has read $B$ and $C$ but not $A$? Explain the reasoning process.

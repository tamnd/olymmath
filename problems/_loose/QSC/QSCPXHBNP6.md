(21st Russian Mathematical Olympiad) Prove: For any natural number $a_{1}>1$, there exists a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \cdots$ such that for any $k \geqslant 1, \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i}^{2}$ is divisible by $\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i}$.

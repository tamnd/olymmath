Josef and Marie discovered a regular pyramid on their vacation, whose base was a square with a side of $230 \mathrm{~m}$ and whose height was equal to the radius of a circle with the same circumference as the base square. Marie marked the vertices of the square as $A B C D$. Josef marked a point $E$ on the line connecting point $B$ with the apex of the pyramid such that the length of the broken line $A E C$ was the shortest possible.

Determine the length of the broken line $A E C$ rounded to the nearest centimeter.

(M. Krejčová, F. Steinhauser)

For any real number $ x$ prove that:

$$ x\in \mathbb{Z}\Leftrightarrow \lfloor x\rfloor +\lfloor 2x\rfloor+\lfloor 3x\rfloor+...+\lfloor nx\rfloor=\frac{n(\lfloor x\rfloor+\lfloor nx\rfloor)}{2}\ ,\ (\forall)n\in \mathbb{N}^*$$

Let $a_{0}, b_{0}$ be natural numbers, and define the sequences $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2} \cdots$ and $b_{0}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots$ as follows: $a_{k+1}=\left[\frac{a_{k}}{2}\right], b_{k+1}=2 b_{k}, k=0,1,2, \cdots$. If $p$ is the number such that $a_{p}=1$, prove: for all $j$ such that $a_{j}$ is odd, the sum of the corresponding $b_{j}$ equals $a_{0} b_{0}$.

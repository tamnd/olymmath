a) Prove that the projections of point $P$ on the Simson lines of triangles $BCD$, $CDA$, $DAB$, and $BAC$ lie on one line (the Simson line of the inscribed quadrilateral).

b) Prove that analogously, by induction, the Simson line of an inscribed $n$-gon can be defined as the line containing the projections of point $P$ onto the Simson lines of all $(n-1)$-gons obtained by removing one of the vertices of the $n$-gon.

See also problems $5.11, 5.65$.

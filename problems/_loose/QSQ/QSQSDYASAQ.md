In this triangle, let's draw a median to the larger side. This median divides the triangle into two. In each of the resulting triangles, we also draw a median to the larger side, and so on. Prove that all the resulting triangles can be divided into a finite number of classes in such a way that all triangles belonging to the same class are similar to each other. Also prove that any angle of any resulting triangle is not less than half the smallest angle of the original triangle.

The thirtieth question: Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers, $n>1$, and let $g_{n}$ denote their geometric mean. Let $A_{k}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i}}{k}(1 \leq k \leq n)$, and let $G_{n}$ denote the geometric mean of $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$. Prove: $n \cdot \sqrt[n]{\frac{G_{n}}{A_{n}}}+\frac{g_{n}}{G_{n}} \leq n+1$, and determine the conditions under which equality holds.

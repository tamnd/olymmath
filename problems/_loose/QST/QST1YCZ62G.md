Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function that admits primitives and $F$ be one of its primitives.

Determine the function $f$ such that $\left(e^{2 x}+1\right)(f(x)-F(x))=e^{2 x}, \forall x \in \mathbb{R}$.

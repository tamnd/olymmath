In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $H$ is the orthocenter, $O$ is the circumcenter (points $A$, $H$, and $O$ are not collinear), point $D$ is the projection of $A$ on side $BC$, and the perpendicular bisector of segment $AO$ intersects line $BC$ at point $E$. Prove: The midpoint of segment $OH$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ADE$.
(2012, China Western Mathematical Invitational)

Given is $\triangle A B C$ with sides $a, b$ and $c, a \leqslant b \leqslant c$, and the medians corresponding to these sides are $t_{a}, t_{b}$ and $t_{c}$, respectively. Prove:
a) the equality $a^{2}+c^{2}=2 b^{2}$ holds if and only if $t_{a}^{2}+t_{c}^{2}=2 t_{b}^{2}$ holds;

b) the equality $a^{2}+c^{2}=2 b^{2}$ holds if and only if $\triangle A B C$ is similar to a triangle whose sides are of length $t_{a}, t_{b}$ and $t_{c}$.

In one American city, a rumor spread that some residents had allegedly observed "flying saucers" in the sky. A reporter from the city newspaper conducted a survey of four of these residents. A stated: 1) These were not false suns; 2) It's true, I observed this phenomenon for no more than a minute; 3) Resident D has the right thoughts on this matter. B: 1) These were not weather balloons; 2) I discussed this phenomenon with D; 3) These were "flying saucers." C: 1) These were not flying saucers; 2) These were ordinary airplanes under unusual lighting conditions; 3) B is wrong when he says these were "saucers." D: 1) I thoroughly studied this phenomenon. 2) These were false suns. 3) I never discussed this phenomenon with B. Each of these residents admitted that they might have made a mistake, but two of their three statements are definitely true and one is false. What phenomenon was observed?

It is given that $a, b$ and $c$ are three positive integers such that
$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=2011 .
$$

Let the highest common factor (HCF) and the least common multiple (LCM) of the three numbers $a, b, c$ be denoted by $x$ and $y$ respectively. Suppose that $x+y=388$. Find the value of $a+b+c$. (Remark: The highest common factor is also known as the greatest common divisor.)

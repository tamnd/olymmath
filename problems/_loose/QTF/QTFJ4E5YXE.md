Of all functions $h : Z_{>0} \to Z_{\ge 0}$ , choose one satisfying $h(ab) = ah(b) + bh(a)$ for all $a, b \in Z_{>0}$ and $h(p) = p$ for all prime numbers $p$ . Find the sum of all positive integers $n\le 100$ such that $h(n) = 4n$ .

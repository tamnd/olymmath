Prove that for any $2 n$ real numbers $a, b, \ldots k, A, B, \ldots, K$ the following inequality holds:

$$
\begin{gathered}
(a A+b B+\ldots+k K)^{2} \leqslant\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+\ldots+k^{2}\right) \times \\
\times\left(A^{2}+B^{2}+\ldots+K^{2}\right)
\end{gathered}
$$

The equality sign holds when the numbers of these sequences are proportional, i.e.,

$a=p A, b=p B, \ldots, k=p K$ (Bunyakovsky-Cauchy inequality).

Let $(R,+,\cdot)$ be a ring and let $f$ be a surjective endomorphism of $R$ such that $[x,f(x)]=0$ for any $x\in R$ , where $[a,b]=ab-ba$ , $a,b\in R$ . Prove that:

**a)** $[x,f(y)]=[f(x),y]$ and $x[x,y]=f(x)[x,y]$ , for any $x,y\in R\ ;$ **b)** If $R$ is a division ring and $f$ is different from the identity function, then $R$ is commutative.

Given a real number $a \neq 0$ with $|a| \neq 1$.

Determine all real solutions $x$ of the equation

$$
\frac{\left(x^{4}+1\right)\left(x^{4}+6 x^{2}+1\right)}{x^{2}\left(x^{2}-1\right)^{2}}=\frac{\left(a^{4}+1\right)\left(a^{4}+6 a^{2}+1\right)}{a^{2}\left(a^{2}-1\right)^{2}}
$$

In a mathematics competition organized by the student magazine "alpha," three students from a school won prizes. When asked about their first names, the following seven answers were given:
(1) Christian, Uwe, Iris
(2) Eva, Elke, Uwe
(3) Roland, Marion, Bernd
(4) Iris, Heike, Uwe
(5) Roland, Heike, Bernd
(6) Eva, Marion, Christian
(7) Christian, Eva, Elke.

It turned out that in exactly one of the answers, all three first names were correct, in exactly two answers exactly two first names were wrong, and in exactly three answers all three first names were wrong.

Determine the first names of the three students who won the prizes!

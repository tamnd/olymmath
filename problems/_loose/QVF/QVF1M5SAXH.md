Given $N$ blue and $N$ red sticks, where the sum of the lengths of the blue sticks equals the sum of the lengths of the red sticks. It is known that an $N$-gon can be formed from the blue sticks, and another $N$-gon can be formed from the red sticks. Is it always possible to choose one blue and one red stick and repaint them (the blue one to red, and the red one to blue) such that it is still possible to form an $N$-gon from the blue sticks and another $N$-gon from the red sticks?

a) for $N=3$

b) for any natural number $N>3$.

Let $a$ be a real number such that $$5 \sin^4 \left( \frac{a}{2} \right)+ 12 \cos a = 5 cos^4 \left( \frac{a}{2} \right)+ 12 \sin a.$$ There are relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that $\tan a = \frac{m}{n}$ . Find $10m + n$.

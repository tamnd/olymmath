Brigade Schulz plays "Tele-Lotto (5 out of 35)" according to a so-called "fully mathematical system with $n$ numbers". This means that if $n$ is a natural number with $5 < n \leq 35$, the system is as follows:

Exactly $n$ numbers from 1, 2, ..., 35 are selected, and then all different subsets of five elements are determined in the set of these $n$ numbers. Each of these subsets is submitted as a tip.

a) The brigade plays according to a "fully mathematical system with 6 numbers". In the subsequent draw in Tele-Lotto, it turns out that exactly four of the five winning numbers are among the six numbers used by the brigade in their system.

Furthermore, the following prize amounts are announced after this draw: For each tip with exactly three correctly predicted numbers, there is $20 \mathrm{M}$, for each tip with exactly four correctly predicted numbers, there is $400 \mathrm{M}$, and for each tip with five correctly predicted numbers, there is $3000 \mathrm{M}$.

Determine the winnings of the brigade in this case!

b) The brigade plays according to a "fully mathematical system with 7 numbers". In a draw, it is observed that exactly four of the five winning numbers are among the seven numbers used by the brigade. The prize amounts are the same as in a).

Determine the winnings of the brigade in this case as well!

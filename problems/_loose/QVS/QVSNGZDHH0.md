Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a triangle. We denote by $\mathrm{P}$ the intersection point of the line (AC) with the tangent at $B$ to the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$. We denote $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ as the respective symmetrics of points $B$ and $C$ with respect to point $P$. Show that the points $B, A, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$ are concyclic.

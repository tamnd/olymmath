Let $k \geqslant 2, n$ in $\mathbb{N}^{*}$ and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{k}$ be pairwise distinct in $\llbracket 1, n \rrbracket$ such that $n$ divides $a_{1}\left(a_{2}-1\right), a_{2}\left(a_{3}-1\right), \ldots, a_{k-1}\left(a_{k}-1\right)$.

Show that $n$ does not divide $a_{k}\left(a_{1}-1\right)$.

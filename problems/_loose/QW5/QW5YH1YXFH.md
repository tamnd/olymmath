Let $A M$ be the median of $\triangle A B C$ on side $B C$, and draw any line intersecting $A B$, $A C$, and $A M$ at $P$, $Q$, and $N$ respectively. Prove that $\frac{A B}{A P}$, $\frac{A M}{A N}$, and $\frac{A C}{A Q}$ form an arithmetic sequence.
(1978 Liaoning Province Competition Problem)

Lottery players often complain that they didn't win again, especially | which is particularly frustrating | the numbers next to the ones they played were drawn. Let's examine the question in detail: were they really that close to the jackpot?

Assume that in the 5-number lottery, the winning numbers are: $7, 13, 28, 46, 75$. How many different ways can a lottery ticket be filled out such that at least four of the marked numbers are adjacent to the listed numbers? (Two numbers are adjacent if the absolute value of their difference is 1.)

Add matrices $A$ and $B$, if:

a) $A=\left(\begin{array}{rr}2 & 4 \\ -1 & 3\end{array}\right), B=\left(\begin{array}{rr}-1 & 3 \\ 1 & -4\end{array}\right)$;

b) $A=\left(\begin{array}{rrr}1 & 2 & -3 \\ 2 & -4 & 5\end{array}\right), B=\left(\begin{array}{rrr}2 & -4 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 & 2\end{array}\right)$;

c) $A=\left(\begin{array}{rrr}2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 3 & 8\end{array}\right), B=\left(\begin{array}{rrr}-5 & 3 & 16 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 7 & 10 & 0\end{array}\right)$,

d) $A=\left(\begin{array}{rr}2 & -1 \\ 3 & 5 \\ 0 & -8\end{array}\right), B=\left(\begin{array}{rrr}1 & 0 & -3 \\ 2 & 4 & 8\end{array}\right)$.

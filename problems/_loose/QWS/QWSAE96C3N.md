For example, $2 M$ is any point on the side $A B$ of $\triangle A B C$, $r_{1}, r_{2}, r$ are the radii of the incircles of $\triangle A M C, \triangle B M C, \triangle A B C$ respectively, and $q_{1}, q_{2}, q$ are the radii of the excircles of the above triangles opposite to $\angle A C B$. Prove: $\frac{r_{1}}{q_{1}} \cdot \frac{r_{2}}{q_{2}}=\frac{r}{q}$.

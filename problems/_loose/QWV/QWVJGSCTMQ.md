Alina and Bogdan are playing the following game. They have a pile consisting of 330 stones. The two players take turns. On a move, one takes $1, n$ or $m$ stones from the pile. The player who takes the last stone wins the game. Before starting, Alina chooses the number $n, (1<n<10)$, after which Bogdan chooses the number $m, (m \neq n, 1<m<10)$. Alina moves first. Which of the two players has a winning strategy? What if there are 2018 stones in the pile at the beginning?

Belarus Olympiad, adapted

Isosceles trapezoid $ ABCD$ , with $ AB \parallel CD$ , is such that there exists a circle $ \Gamma$ tangent to its four sides. Let $ T = \Gamma \cap BC$ , and $ P = \Gamma \cap AT$ ( $ P \neq T$ ).
If $ \frac{AP}{AT} = \frac{2}{5}$ , compute $ \frac{AB}{CD}$ .

In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the feet of the perpendiculars from points $A$ and $B$ to the opposite sides are points $H_{a}$ and $H_{b}$, respectively. The angle bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ intersect the opposite sides at points $T_{a}$ and $T_{b}$, respectively. Prove that the following four statements are equivalent:
(1) The incenter $I$ of $\triangle ABC$ lies on $H_{a} H_{b}$; (2) The circumcenter $O$ of $\triangle ABC$ lies on $T_{a} T_{b}$; (3) $\cos C = \frac{a+b}{a+b+c}$; (4) $\cos C = \cos A + \cos B$. Here, $a$, $b$, and $c$ are the lengths of the corresponding sides of $\triangle ABC$.

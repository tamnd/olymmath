Let $a_{1}$ and $a_{2}$ be any two positive numbers such that $a_{1}>a_{2}$, and let $b_{1}, b_{2}$ be numbers such that

$$
b_{1} \geq a_{1}, b_{1} b_{2} \geq a_{1} a_{2}
$$

It is to be proven that

$$
b_{1}+b_{2} \geq a_{1}+a_{2}
$$

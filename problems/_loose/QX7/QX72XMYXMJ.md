Given a right triangle $\triangle A B C$ with a right angle at vertex $C$. Let $D$ and $E$ be points on the hypotenuse $A B$ such that $\overline{B C}=\overline{B D}$ and $\overline{A C}=\overline{A E}$. Let $F$ be the orthogonal projection of point $D$ onto side $A C$, and $G$ be the orthogonal projection of point $E$ onto side $B C$. Prove that $\overline{D E}=\overline{D F}+\overline{E G}$.

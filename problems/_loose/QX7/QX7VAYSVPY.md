Given a triangle $A B C$. Four circles of equal radius $\rho$ are constructed such that one of them touches the other three, and each of these three touches two sides of the triangle. Find $\rho$, if the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of the triangle are $r$ and $R$ respectively.

In a mathematics working group, Monika presents the participants with the following problem:

Each of the letters $A, L, P, H$ represents a single-digit natural number. The following conditions apply:

(1) The number $H$ is twice as large as the number $P$.

(2) The number $A$ is equal to the sum of the number $P$ and twice the number $H$.

(3) The number $L$ is equal to the sum of the numbers $A, P$, and $H$.

If the numbers $A L P H A$ are written in this order, one obtains the (five-digit) reader number of the mathematical student magazine "alpha".

What is this reader number?

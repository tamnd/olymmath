Somewhere in the ocean, there is an island of Misfortune, on which several cities are located, connected by roads in such a way that a random tourist can get from any city to any other. It turned out that if any two cities are placed under quarantine and all roads leading to them are blocked, it is still possible to travel from any of the remaining cities to any other.

A tourist randomly selected three roads, none of which lead to the same city, and wants to travel along them, starting and ending his route in the same city, without visiting any city more than once along the way. Can he always do this?

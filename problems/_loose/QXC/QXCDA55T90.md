Schur's Inequality (Theorem 1) is an extremely important result in proving ternary higher-order homogeneous inequalities.

Theorem 1 Let $r \in \mathbb{R}$, for any non-negative real numbers $x, y, z$, we have
$$
\sum_{\text {cyc }} x^{r}(x-y)(x-z) \geqslant 0 \text {. }
$$

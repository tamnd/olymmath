For a regular $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ with circumradius $R$, let $P$ be a point on its plane, $O$ be the center, and $P O=a$ (Figure 3.44). Prove that:
$$
P A_{1}^{2}+P A_{2}^{2}+\cdots+P A_{n}^{2}=n\left(R^{2}+a^{2}\right) .
$$

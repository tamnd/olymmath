a) In some rural areas of Rossin, there once existed the following divination. A girl holds six blades of grass in her hand so that the ends of the blades stick out at the top and bottom; a friend ties these blades of grass in pairs at the top and bottom separately. If, as a result, all six blades of grass are tied into one ring, it was supposed to mean that the girl would get married in the current year. What is the probability that the blades of grass will form a ring when tied at random?

b) Solve the same question for the case of $2 n$ blades of grass.

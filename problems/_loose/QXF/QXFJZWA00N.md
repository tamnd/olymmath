Lara and Sara are taking turns drawing $n$ straight lines on a rectangular piece of paper, each drawing one line at a time. Each line will be parallel to one of the edges of the paper and will run from edge to edge. No line can coincide with an edge or an already drawn line. In the end, the piece of paper will be divided into several rectangles. If the number of these rectangles is odd, Lara wins; if it is even, Sara wins. Depending on $n$ and who starts, determine who has a winning strategy.

Peter and Heinz tell that they have drawn triangles whose side lengths, measured in centimeters, had the values

$$
a=3 x+9, b=5 x+8, c=4 x+1
$$

where $x$ was a previously chosen non-zero natural number.

Anke claims: For every non-zero natural number $x$, there is a triangle with the side lengths $a, b, c$ formed in this way.

Birgit claims: There is a non-zero number $x$ for which a triangle with these side lengths is a right triangle.

Investigate whether each of these two claims is true!

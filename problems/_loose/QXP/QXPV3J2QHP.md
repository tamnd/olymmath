Three girlfriends met at a cottage and went mushroom picking. They found a total of 55 mushrooms. Upon returning, they made a fried mushroom dish, divided it into four equal portions, and invited their friend Pepa to join them. Líba contributed six of her mushrooms to the dish, Maruška eight, and Sárka five. Each then had the same number of mushrooms left. Pepa gave them a box of chocolates, containing 38 chocolates, and said they should fairly divide them according to how much they contributed to his meal.

1. How many mushrooms did each find?
2. How should they divide the chocolates according to Pepa?

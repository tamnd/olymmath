If real numbers $a>b>c>0$. Also, let $l_{1}=\sqrt{(a+c)^{2}+b^{2}}$, $l_{2}=\sqrt{a^{2}+(b+c)^{2}}, l_{3}=\sqrt{(a+b)^{2}+c^{2}}$. Then the smallest of the products $l_{1} l_{2}, l_{1} l_{3}$, $l_{2} l_{3}, l_{2}^{2}, l_{3}^{2}$ is
(A) $l_{1} l_{2}$.
(B) $l_{1} l_{3}$.
(C) $l_{2} l_{3}$.
(D) $l_{2}^{2}$.
(E) $l_{3}^{2}$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

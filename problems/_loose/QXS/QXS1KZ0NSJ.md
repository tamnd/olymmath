Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{8} \geq 0$ are real numbers such that for $i=1, \ldots, 8$ $x_{i}+x_{i+1}+x_{i+2} \leq 1$, where $x_{9}=x_{1}$ and $x_{10}=x_{2}$. Prove the inequality

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{8} x_{i} x_{i+2} \leq 1
$$

and find all cases in which equality prevails.

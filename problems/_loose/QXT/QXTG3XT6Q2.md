After every full lap, he recorded the distance between the tips of the two hands on his watch. In the order they were recorded, these lengths were $11$ mm, $16$ mm, and $17$ mm. The minute hand is longer than the hour hand by $\sqrt{c}$ mm, where $c$ is a positive integer.
What is the value of $c$ ?

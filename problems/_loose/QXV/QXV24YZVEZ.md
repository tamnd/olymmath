Let $0<t_{1} \leqslant t_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant t_{n}<1$. Prove:
$$
\left(1-t_{n}\right)^{2}\left[\frac{t_{1}}{\left(1-t_{1}^{2}\right)^{2}}+\frac{t_{2}^{2}}{\left(1-t_{2}^{3}\right)^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{t_{n}^{n}}{\left(1-t_{n}^{n+1}\right)^{2}}\right]<1 .
$$

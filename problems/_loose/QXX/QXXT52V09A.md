There are $m$ white balls and $n$ black balls $(m>n)$ in a jar. The balls are taken out one by one, without replacement. If at some point during the process, the number of white balls taken out equals the number of black balls taken out, it is said that a coincidence has occurred. Find the probability that at least one coincidence occurs.

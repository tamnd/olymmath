Let ABC with orthocenter $H$ and circumcenter $O$ be an acute scalene triangle satisfying
$AB = AM$ where $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$. Suppose $Q$ and $K$ are points on $(ABC)$ distinct from
A satisfying $\angle AQH = 90$
and $\angle BAK = \angle CAM$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of $AH$.
• Let $I$ be the intersection of $B\text{-midline}$ and $A\text{-altitude}$ Prove that $IN = IO$.
• Prove that there is point $P$ on the symmedian lying on circle with center $B$ and radius $BM$
such that $(APN)$ is tangent to $AB$.

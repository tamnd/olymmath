On a flat ground, there are $n$ people, and for each person, the distances to the others are all different. Each person has a water gun, and when a fire alarm is sounded, each person shoots the person closest to them. When $n$ is odd, prove that at least one person will remain dry. When $n$ is even, is this conclusion always true?

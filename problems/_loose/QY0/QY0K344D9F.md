(50 points) Let the sequence of numbers $A$ be such that $A_{1}=3, A_{2}=8, A_{3}=13, \ldots$. Prove that there exists an infinite number of numerical sequences $B$ with the following properties:
1) $B_{1}=7$
2) $B_{k}=B_{k-1}+d$, where $d-$ is some number $(k=2,3,4, \ldots)$;
3) have infinitely many matching terms with the sequence $A$.

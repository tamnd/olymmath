Given the line $l: A x+B y+C=0$ intersects the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ at points $P, Q$, and $O$ is the center of the ellipse. Prove: $\triangle O P Q$ has a maximum area of $\frac{1}{2} a b$ if and only if $a^{2} A^{2}+b^{2} B^{2}=2 C^{2}$.

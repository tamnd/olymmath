On the board, $2 \cdot n$ consecutive integers are written. In one move, you can split the written numbers into pairs in any way and replace each pair of numbers with their sum and difference (it is not necessary to subtract the smaller number from the larger one, all replacements occur simultaneously). Prove that $2 \cdot n$ consecutive integers will never appear on the board again.

9-5 (A14, USSR) Consider the sequence $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$:
$$
\begin{array}{c}
c_{1}=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{8}, \\
c_{2}=a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{8}^{2}, \\
\cdots \cdots \\
c_{n}=a_{1}^{n}+a_{2}^{n}+\cdots+a_{8}^{n}, \\
\cdots \cdots
\end{array}
$$
$\bullet \bullet \bullet \bullet$
where $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{8}$ are real numbers, not all zero, and it is known that the sequence $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ has infinitely many terms equal to zero. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $c_{n}=0$.

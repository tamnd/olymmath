From the point $K$ of intersection of the ray $B A$ and the circle, a line perpendicular to the bisector of the angle is drawn, intersecting the circle at points $K$ and $P$, and the ray $B C$ at point $M$. Prove that the segment $P M$ is twice as long as the perpendicular dropped from the center of the circle to the bisector of angle $A B C$.

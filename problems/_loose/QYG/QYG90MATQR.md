$3$ builders are scheduled to build a house in $60$ days. However, they suffer from a bout of procrastination and thus do nothing for the first $50$ days. Panicked, they realize in order to build the house on time, they must hire more workers *and* work twice as fast as they would have originally. If the new workers they hire also will work at the doubled rate, how many new workers will they need to hire? Assume each builder works at the same rate as the others and they do not get in each other's way.

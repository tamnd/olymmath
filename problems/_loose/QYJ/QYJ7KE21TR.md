Given a circle with diameter $AB$, touching the line $d$ at point $B$. Through point $A$, two rays are drawn, intersecting the line $d$ at points $M$ and $N$, and the circle at points $P$ and $Q$ (points $P$, $M$ lie on the same ray, and $Q$, $N$ lie on the other). a) Prove that the lengths of the segments satisfy the inequality $PQ < MN$. b) Prove that $I_{PQ} < MN$, where $I_{PQ}$ is the length of the arc with endpoints $P$ and $Q$, not containing point $A$.

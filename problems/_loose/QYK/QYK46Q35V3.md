In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $E$ is a point on $CD$ such that $\angle AED = \angle BEC$. Points $M, N, P, Q$ are on $AD, BC, BD, AC$ respectively, and satisfy
$$
\frac{AM}{DM} \cdot \frac{CN}{BN} = \frac{BP}{DP} \cdot \frac{CQ}{AQ} = \frac{CE}{DE}.
$$

Connecting $MQ$ and $PN$ intersect $AE$ and $BE$ at points $U$ and $V$ respectively. If $UE = VE$, determine the shape of quadrilateral $ABCD$.

Let

$$
f(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(a_{k} \cos k x+b_{k} \sin k x\right)
$$

where $a_{k}, b_{k}(k=1,2, \ldots, n)$ are given numbers.

Prove that the equation $f(x)=0$ always has a solution.

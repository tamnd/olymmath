(Mathematics for Middle School, 1994, Issue 4, Olympiad Training Problem) Find the real-coefficient polynomial $f(x)$ that satisfies the following conditions: (1) For any real number $a$, $f(a+1)=f(a)+f(1)$; (2) There exists a real number $k_{1} \neq 0$ such that $f\left(k_{1}\right)=k_{2}, f\left(k_{2}\right)=k_{3}, \cdots, f\left(k_{n-1}\right)=k_{n}, f\left(k_{n}\right)=k_{1}$, where $n$ is the degree of $f(x)$.

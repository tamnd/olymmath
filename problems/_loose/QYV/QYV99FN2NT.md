Four apparently identical coins are given, of which three are genuine and one is counterfeit. The three genuine coins have the same weight, and the counterfeit coin has a different weight from the genuine ones, but it is not known whether the counterfeit coin is lighter or heavier than the genuine ones.

Show that it is possible to determine the counterfeit coin using only two weighings on a two-pan balance.

Note: On a two-pan balance, we can only compare the weights placed on the two pans: the balance can only be balanced or, alternatively, tip to the heavier side.

Let $a_1,a_2,\dots$ be a sequence of real numbers satisfying
$$\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}-\frac{a_{n+2}}{a_n}-\frac{a_{n+1}a_{n+2}}{a_n^2}=\frac{na_{n+2}a_{n+1}}{a_n}.$$
Given that $a_1=-1$ and $a_2=-\tfrac{1}{2}$, find the value of $\tfrac{a_9}{a_{20}}$.

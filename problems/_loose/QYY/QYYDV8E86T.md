Once, a skater moved straight ahead for six minutes. His path was observed by several people under the following conditions:

(a) During the six minutes, someone was always watching.

(b) Everyone watched continuously for one minute.

(c) Everyone found that while they were watching, the skater moved one meter forward.

How many meters did the skater travel at least?

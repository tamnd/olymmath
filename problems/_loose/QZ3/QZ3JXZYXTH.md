$E, F$ are the midpoints of sides $BC, AD$ of quadrilateral $ABCD$, respectively. The extension of $EF$ intersects the extensions of $BA, CD$ at $G, H$.
(1) If $AB=CD$, then $\angle B GH=\angle E HC$;
(2) If $\angle B GE=\angle E HC$, then $AB=CD$.

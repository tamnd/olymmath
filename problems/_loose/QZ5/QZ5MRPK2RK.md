Sergio asked Ivan to think of a positive integer. Then, he asked Ivan to calculate the sum of its digits and, finally, square the result. Without revealing the initial number, Ivan told Sergio that he obtained a final result of $x$. Show Sergio how to reach the following conclusions:

a) If Ivan had thought of a number with 3 or fewer digits, then $x$ would be less than 730.

b) If Ivan had thought of a number with 4 digits, then $x$ would be less than the number Ivan thought of.

c) If Ivan had thought of a number with 5 or more digits, then $x$ would be less than the number Ivan thought of.

Sergio then did the following: He considered the number $x$ that Ivan said, calculated the sum of its digits, and squared the result. When Sergio told Ivan the number he obtained, Ivan said with surprise that this was the number he had thought of.

d) Determine all possible values for the number Ivan thought of.

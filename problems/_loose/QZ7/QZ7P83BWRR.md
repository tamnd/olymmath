Let $p$ be an odd prime number.

Show that for any integer $n$ not divisible by $p$, $n$ is a square modulo $p$ (i.e., there exists $a$ such that $\left.n \equiv a^{2}[p]\right)$ if and only if $n^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \equiv 1[p]$.

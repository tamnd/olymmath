Prove that for any numbers $\alpha, \beta \in\left\langle 0, \frac{1}{2} \pi\right)$ the inequality holds:

$$
\frac{1}{\cos \alpha}+\frac{1}{\cos \beta} \geqq 2 \sqrt{\operatorname{tg} \alpha+\operatorname{tg} \beta}
$$

Determine also when equality occurs.

Let $X$ be a finite set of cardinality $n$, and let $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{m}$ be subsets of cardinality 3 whose pairwise intersections have cardinality at most 1. Show that there exists a subset $A$ of $X$ of cardinality at least $[\sqrt{2 n}]$ (where $[x]$ denotes the integer part of $x$) and containing none of the $A_{i}$.

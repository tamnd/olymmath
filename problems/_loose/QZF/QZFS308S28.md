Consider a continuous, decreasing function $f:[0,2 \pi] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(\pi)=0$ and let $F$ be one of its primitives. Prove that

$$
\int_{0}^{2 \pi} F(x) \cos x \mathrm{~d} x \leq 0
$$

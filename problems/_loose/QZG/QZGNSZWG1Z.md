Compute the sum $$\frac{\varphi(50!)}{\varphi(49!)}+ \frac{\varphi(51!)}{\varphi(50!)} + \dots + \frac{\varphi(100!)}{\varphi(99!)}$$ where $\varphi(n)$ returns the number of positive integers less than $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$.

a) Show that, for any complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}$, the following equality holds:

$$
4\left|z_{1}+z_{2}\right|^{2}+\left|z_{1}-2 z_{2}\right|^{2}+\left|-2 z_{1}+z_{2}\right|^{2}=9\left|z_{1}\right|^{2}+9\left|z_{2}\right|^{2}
$$

b) Calculate, in terms of the complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, \ldots, z_{n}, n \in N^{*}$, the sum $\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left|S-t z_{k}\right|^{2}$, where $S=z_{1}+z_{2}+\cdots z_{n}, t \in N^{*}$.

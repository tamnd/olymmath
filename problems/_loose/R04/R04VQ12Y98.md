A student needs to copy a convex polygon that fits inside a circle of radius 1. First, the student laid out the first side, then drew the second from its end, the third from the end of the second, and so on. Upon completing the construction, the student found that the polygon did not close, and the first and last vertices drawn were separated by a distance $d$. It is known that the angles were laid out accurately, and the relative error in laying out the length of each side did not exceed the number $p$. Prove that $d \leqslant 4 p$.

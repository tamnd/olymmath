Arbitrarily choose a natural number as $a_{0}$, and then arbitrarily select $a_{1} \in \{a_{0}+54, a_{0}+77\}$, and so on, that is, after selecting $a_{k}$, select $a_{k+1} \in \{a_{k}+54, a_{k}+77\}$. Prove: In the sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots$ obtained, there must be a term whose last two digits are the same.
(1992 St. Petersburg Competition Problem)

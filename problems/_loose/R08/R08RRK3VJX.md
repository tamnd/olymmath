On an escalator which is not moving, a person descends faster than he ascends. Is it faster for this person to descend and ascend once on an upward-moving escalator, or to descend and ascend once on a downward-moving escalator? (It is assumed that all the speeds mentioned here are constant, that the speed of the escalator is the same no matter if it is moving up or down and that the speed of the person relative to the escalator is always greater than the speed of the escalator.)

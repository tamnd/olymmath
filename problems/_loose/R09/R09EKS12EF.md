From a point $M$ inside the quadrilateral $A B C D$, perpendiculars are dropped to the sides. The bases of these perpendiculars lie within the sides. Let's denote these bases: the one lying on side $A B$ - by $X$, lying on side $B C$ - by $Y$, lying on side $C D$ - by $Z$, lying on side $D A$ - by $T$. It is known that $A X \geq X B, B Y \geq Y C, C Z \geq Z D, D T \geq T A$. Prove that a circle can be circumscribed around the quadrilateral $A B C D$.

A city has $n$ high schools, the $i$-th high school sends $C_{i}$ students to the stadium to watch a ball game. It is known that $0 \leqslant C_{i} \leqslant 39, i=1,2, \cdots, n, C_{1}+C_{2}+\cdots+C_{n}=1990$, each row of seats in the stand has 199 seats. It is required that students from the same school must sit in the same row. How many rows at least should the stadium arrange to ensure that all students can be seated according to the requirements?

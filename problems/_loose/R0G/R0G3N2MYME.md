For two chords $A B$ and $B C(A \neq C)$ of a circle $k$, it is given that $\overline{A B}=\overline{B C}$. $D$ is any point on $k$ that lies on the other side of the line through $A$ and $C$ compared to $B$.

It is to be proven that the line through $D$ and $B$ bisects the angle $\angle A D C$!

On a board, all natural numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 15$ are written. Determine the minimum number of numbers that can be erased from the board so that with the remaining numbers we can form two non-empty sets of numbers that simultaneously satisfy the following properties:

a) they have no common elements;

b) they have the same number of elements;

c) the product of the elements of the two sets is the same.

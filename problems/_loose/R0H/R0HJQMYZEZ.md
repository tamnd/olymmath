126 Question: When and only when do real numbers $x_{0}, x_{1}, \cdots, x_{n}(n \geqslant 2)$ satisfy what conditions so that there exist real numbers $y_{0}, y_{1}, \cdots, y_{n}$, such that $z_{0}^{2}=z_{1}^{2}+z_{2}^{2}+\cdots+z_{n}^{2}$ holds. Here $z_{k}=x_{k}+i y_{k}, i$ is the imaginary unit, $k=0,1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove your conclusion.
(China High School Mathematics League, 1997)

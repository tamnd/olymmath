In the trade, there are three aquariums in the shape of cubes, all with the same volume. Some of the internal dimensions of the aquariums are shown in the table.

| | length | width | height |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1st aquarium | $4 d m$ | $6 d m$ | $0.5 \mathrm{~m}$ |
| 2nd aquarium | $2 d m$ | $10 d m$ | |
| 3rd aquarium | | | |

A What is the maximum number of liters of water that can be poured into each aquarium?

B What is the internal height of the 2nd aquarium?

C The 3rd aquarium has the shape of a cube. Determine the external length of the base of the aquarium to the nearest millimeter, if the glass is $6 \mathrm{~mm}$ thick!

D In the first aquarium, $75 \%$ of the volume is filled with water. To what height does the water reach in the aquarium?

E In how many different ways can the three aquariums be arranged in a row on a shelf, if the aquarium in the shape of a cube must be at the first or last position from the left or right?

F If an empty aquarium is filled with five identical sources, it fills in 1.2 minutes. In what time will an empty aquarium be filled if it is filled with only two sources?

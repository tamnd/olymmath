(Butterfly Theorem) Given that $M$ is the midpoint of the chord $AB$ of $\odot O$, and any two chords $CD, EF$ are drawn through $M$. Connecting $CF$ and $DE$ intersect $AB$ at $G, H$ respectively, then $MH = MG$.

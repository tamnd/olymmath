The 58th problem: The sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}}$ and $\left\{\mathrm{~b}_{\mathrm{n}}\right\}_{\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}}$ satisfy $\mathrm{a}_{0}=0, \mathrm{~b}_{0}=1$, and
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
(n+1) a_{n}=n a_{n-1}+2 n b_{n-1} \\
b_{n}=2 a_{n-1}+b_{n-1}
\end{array}\left(n \in Z^{+}\right)\right.
$$
Prove: For any positive integer $n, a_{n} \in Z$, and $n \mid a_{n}$.

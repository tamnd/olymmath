Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, and $D$ a point on line segment $AB$ .
Let $E$ be the point such that $ADE$ is an equilateral triangle and $E$ and $C$ are on opposite sides of $AB$ .
Let $F$ be the intersection of lines $CD$ and $BE$ .

Prove that $F$ lies on the circle through $A$ , $B$ , and $C$ as well as on the circle through $A$ , $D$ , and $E$ .

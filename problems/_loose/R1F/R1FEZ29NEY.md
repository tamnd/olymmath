Let $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{k}$ be positive numbers with the following property: each natural number appears exactly once among the numbers $\left[\alpha_{1}\right], \ldots,\left[\alpha_{k}\right],\left[2 \alpha_{1}\right], \ldots$ $\ldots,\left[2 \alpha_{k}\right],\left[3 \alpha_{1}\right], \ldots,\left[3 \alpha_{k}\right], \ldots$ Prove that $k \leqslant 2$.

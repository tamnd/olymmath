Let the function $f(x)=x^{2}+b x+c$, and the two real roots of the equation $f(x)-x=0$ are $x_{1}, x_{2}$, and $x_{2}-x_{1}>2$.
(1) Prove that $x_{1}, x_{2}$ are the roots of the equation $f[f(x)]=x$;
(2) If the other two roots of the quartic equation $f[f(x)]=x$ are $x_{3}, x_{4}$, and $x_{3}>x_{4}$, try to determine the order of $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$, $x_{4}$.

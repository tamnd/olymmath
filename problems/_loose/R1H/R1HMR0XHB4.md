Given the ellipse $\varepsilon: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, and the moving circle $\Gamma: x^{2}+y^{2}=R^{2}$, where $b<R<a$. If $A$ is a point on the ellipse $\varepsilon$, and $B$ is a point on the moving circle $\Gamma$, and the line $A B$ is tangent to both the ellipse $\varepsilon$ and the moving circle $\Gamma$, find the maximum value of the distance $|A B|$ between points $A$ and $B$.

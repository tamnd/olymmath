Let $ f(x) $ and $ g(x) $ be polynomials with integer coefficients. Prove that if for every integer value $ n $ the number $ g(n) $ is divisible by the number $ f(n) $, then $ g(x) = f(x) \cdot h(x) $, where $ h(x) $ is a polynomial. Show by example that the coefficients of the polynomial $ h(x) $ do not have to be integers.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, admitting $\mathrm{O}$ as the center of the circumscribed circle. We construct a point $P$ on the ray $[A C)$ and a point $Q$ on the ray $[B A)$ such that the triangles $BPQ$ and $ABC$ are similar, that is, we have the equalities of angles (non-oriented): $\widehat{BPQ} = \widehat{ABC}$ and $\widehat{PQB} = \widehat{BCA}$. Show that $O$ is the orthocenter (intersection of the altitudes) of the triangle $BPQ$.

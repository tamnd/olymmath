Prove that the following equations have no solutions in integers:
a) $x^{2}+y^{2}=2003$

b) $12 x+5=y^{2}$

c) $-x^{2}+7 y^{3}+6=0$

d) $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1999$

e) $15 x^{2}-7 y^{2}=9$
f) $x^{2}-5 y+3=0$

g) $x_{1}^{4}+\ldots+x_{14}^{4}=1999$;

h) $8 x^{3}-13 y^{3}=17$.

Given two convex polygons $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4} \ldots A_{\mathrm{n}}$ and $B_{1} B_{2} B_{3} B_{4} \ldots B_{\mathrm{n}}$. It is known that $A_{1} A_{2}=B_{1} B_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}=B_{2} B_{3}, \ldots$, $A_{\mathrm{n}} A_{1}=B_{\mathrm{n}} B_{1}$ and $n-3$ angles of one polygon are equal to the corresponding angles of the other. Will the polygons be equal?

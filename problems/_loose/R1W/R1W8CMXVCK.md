Let $a_1,\ldots,a_n\ (n>2)$ be real numbers with at most one zero. Solve the system
\begin{align*}
x_1x_2 &= a_1,
x_2x_3 &= a_2,
&\ \vdots
x_{n-1}x_n &= a_{n-1},
x_nx_1 &\ge a_n.
\end{align*}

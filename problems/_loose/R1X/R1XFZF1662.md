The sum of 1959 positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{1959}$ is 1; prove that the sum of all possible products of 1000 different factors from our numbers is less than 1. [Among the considered products, all those that differ from each other by at least one factor are included, but not products that differ only in the order of factors-such products are considered the same and only one of them is counted.]

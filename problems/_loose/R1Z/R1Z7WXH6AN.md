(2004 National College Entrance Examination Chongqing Paper) Suppose a car has to pass through 4 intersections on its way forward. The probability of the car encountering a green light (allowing passage) at each intersection is $\frac{3}{4}$, and the probability of encountering a red light (prohibiting passage) is $\frac{1}{4}$. Assume the car stops only when it encounters a red light or reaches its destination, and let $\xi$ represent the number of intersections passed when the car stops. Find: (I) the probability distribution of $\xi$ and the expectation $E \xi$;
(II) the probability that the car has passed at most 3 intersections when it stops.

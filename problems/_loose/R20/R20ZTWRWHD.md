Let $ABC$ be a triangle with acute angles, and such that $AB \neq AC$. We denote by $D$ the foot of the angle bisector of $\widehat{BAC}$. The point $E$ (resp. $F$) is the foot of the altitude from $B$ (resp. from $C$). The circumcircle of triangle $DBF$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $DCE$ at a point $M$ other than $D$.

Prove that $ME = MF$.

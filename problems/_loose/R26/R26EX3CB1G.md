\section*{Task 1 - 041111}

A company delivers annually 600 tons and 400 tons of a certain product to companies (1) and (2). For transportation, trucks 1 and 2 with payloads of 1 ton and 4 tons are available. The smaller truck is available for a maximum of 300 trips per year, and the larger one for 200 trips. The transportation costs in thousand euros per trip are as follows for

\begin{tabular}{l|c|c}
& Truck 1 & Truck 2 \\
\hline to the trip to Company 1 & 10 & 20 \\
to the trip to Company 2 & 30 & 60
\end{tabular}

How many trips must each truck make to each of the two companies in a year to minimize the total transportation costs?

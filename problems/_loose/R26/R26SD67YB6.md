Let $f_n(x,\ y)=\frac{n}{r\cos \pi r+n^2r^3}\ (r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2})$,

$I_n=\int\int_{r\leq 1} f_n(x,\ y)\ dxdy\ (n\geq 2).$

Find $\lim_{n\to\infty} I_n.$

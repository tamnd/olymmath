Of the seven students Annette, Beate, Christine, Dieter, Frank, Gerd, and Hans, each had at least one of the two subjects Mathematics and Russian with a grade of 1.

When asked who had a grade of 1 in exactly one of these two subjects, only Annette, Christine, Frank, Gerd, and Hans of these students responded. In Mathematics, only Beate, Christine, Dieter, and Frank had a grade of 1.

Determine from these statements all of the seven students who

a) had a grade of 1 in both Mathematics and Russian,

b) had a grade of 1 in Mathematics but not in Russian,

c) had a grade of 1 in Russian but not in Mathematics!

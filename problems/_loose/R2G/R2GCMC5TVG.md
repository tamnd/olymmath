In an isosceles trapezoid $A B C D$, a perpendicular $C E$ is dropped from the vertex $C$ of the smaller base $B C$ to $A D$, and the midpoint $M$ of side $A B$ is connected to vertex $C$. Prove that if the segment $B E$ is equal to the midline of the trapezoid, then angle $M C D$ is a right angle.

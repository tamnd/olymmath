The ninetieth problem: Given a positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, let $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x})$ and $Q(\mathrm{x})$ be real-coefficient polynomials with degrees not exceeding $\mathrm{n}$, satisfying $x^{n+1} P(x)+(x+1)^{n+1} Q(x)=1$. Try to find $Q(x)$, and determine the value of $Q\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$.

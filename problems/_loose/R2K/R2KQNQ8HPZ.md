In the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, let $A_{1}$ be the point diametrically opposite to vertex $A$. Let $A^{\prime}$ be the point where the line $A A_{1}$ intersects the side $BC$. The perpendicular to the line $A A^{\prime}$ drawn through $A^{\prime}$ intersects the sides $AB$ and $AC$ (or their extensions) at $M$ and $N$, respectively. Prove that the points $A, M, A_{1}$, and $N$ lie on a circle whose center is on the altitude from $A$ in triangle $ABC$.

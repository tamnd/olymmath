On the coordinate plane, a parallelogram $O A B C$ is drawn, with its center located at the point $\left(\frac{19}{2}, \frac{15}{2}\right)$, and points $A, B$, and $C$ have natural coordinates. Find the number of such parallelograms. (Here, $O$ denotes the origin - the point $(0,0)$; two parallelograms with the same set of vertices are considered the same, i.e., $O A B C$ and $O C B A$ are considered the same parallelogram.)

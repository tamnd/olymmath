If several lines are distributed on a plane, where no two lines are parallel and no three lines are concurrent, then this set of lines is called a normal distribution.

It is known that for any 4 normally distributed lines, the circumcircles of the 4 triangles formed by each set of 3 lines are concurrent. Can there exist 45 normally distributed lines such that the circumcircles of all triangles formed by each set of 3 lines are concurrent?

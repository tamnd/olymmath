Given the differential equation

$$
\left(x-x^{3}\right) y^{\prime}+\left(2 x^{2}-1\right) y-x^{3}=0
$$

Check whether the function $y=x \sqrt{1-x^{2}}+x$ is a solution to this equation. Will it be its general solution?

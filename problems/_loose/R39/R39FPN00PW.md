Suppose the function $\psi$ satisfies $\psi(1)=\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}}$ and $\psi(3x)+3\psi(x)=\psi(x)^3$ for all real $x$ . Determine the greatest integer less than $ \prod_{n=1}^{100}\psi(3^n)$ .

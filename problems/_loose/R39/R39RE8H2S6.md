Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle with unit side length, and let $P$ be a point on the side opposite the line $A B$ with respect to point $C$, such that the angle $\widehat{A P B}$ measures $60^{\circ}$. Suppose that the angle bisector of $\overline{A P B}$ intersects the segments $A B$ and $A C$ at points $X$ and $Y$, respectively. What is the minimum possible value for the area of triangle $A X Y$?
(A) $\frac{1}{3 \sqrt{3}}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{3}}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{12}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{8}$
(E) None of the above.

Find the characteristic function of a discrete random variable $\xi$ having a geometric distribution (this means that $\xi$ takes values $n=1,2, \ldots$ and

$$
\mathrm{P}(\xi=n)=p q^{n-1}
$$

where $p>0, q>0, p+q=1)$. Using the characteristic function, find the mathematical expectation $\mathrm{M} \xi$ and the variance $\mathrm{D} \xi$.

Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ inscribed in $\odot O$, $H$ is the orthocenter, $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, and point $U$ is on $BC$ such that $\angle BAM = \angle CAU$. $K$ is the projection of point $H$ onto the tangent line of $\odot O$ passing through point $A$, and $L$ is the projection of point $H$ onto $AU$. Prove that $K$, $L$, and $M$ are collinear.

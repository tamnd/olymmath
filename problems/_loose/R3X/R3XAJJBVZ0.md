Given a right triangle $ABC$ and two mutually perpendicular lines $x$ and $y$ passing through the right angle vertex $A$. For a point $X$ moving along line $x$, define $y_{b}$ as the image of line $y$ under reflection about $XB$, and $y_{c}$ as the image of line $y$ under reflection about $XC$. Let $y_{b}$ and $y_{c}$ intersect at point $Y$. Find the geometric locus of points $Y$ (for non-coincident $y_{b}$ and $y_{c}$).

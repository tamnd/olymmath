For any non-negative integer $n$, let $S(n)=n-m^{2}$, where $m$ is the largest integer satisfying $m^{2} \leqslant n$. Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $a_{0}=A$, and
$$
a_{n+1}=a_{n}+S\left(a_{n}\right), n \geqslant 0 .
$$

For which non-negative integers $A$ does this sequence become constant from some term onwards?

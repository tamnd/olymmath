The 71st question: The sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}(n \geq 2)$ consists of non-negative terms, with $a_{0}=a_{n}=0$. Find the smallest $k$ such that for all sequences $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ satisfying the above conditions, there always exists an $i \in\{1,2,3, \ldots, n-1\}$ for which $a_{i-1}+a_{i+1} \leq k a_{i}$.

b) Prove that the area of a triangle with vertices at points $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right),\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right)$, and ( $x_{3}, y_{3}$ ) is

$$
\frac{1}{2}\left|x_{1} y_{2}+x_{2} y_{3}+x_{3} y_{1}-x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{3}-x_{3} y_{2}\right| \text {. }
$$

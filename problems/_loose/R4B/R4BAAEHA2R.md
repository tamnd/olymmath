A horizontal line $m$ passes through the center of circle $O$, line $l \perp m, l$ intersects $m$ at $M$, point $M$ is to the right of the center, and three different points $A, B, C$ on line $l$ are outside the circle and above line $m$, with point $A$ being the farthest from $M$ and point $C$ being the closest to $M$. $A P, B Q, C R$ are three tangents to circle $O$, with $P, Q, R$ being the points of tangency. Prove:
(1) When $l$ is tangent to circle $O$, $A B \times C R + B C \times A P = A C \times B Q$;
(2) When $l$ intersects circle $O$, $A B \times C R + B C \times A P = A C \times B Q$.

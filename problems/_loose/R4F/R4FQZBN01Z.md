Sasha has a very long strip of paper and a 2019-digit number $n$. He writes down consecutive natural numbers starting from $n$ on the strip in a row without spaces: $n, n+1, n+2, \ldots$ Prove that sooner or later, after writing down the next number, there will be a number on the strip that is divisible by 101.

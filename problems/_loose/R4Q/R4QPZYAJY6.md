Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

1. Let $d \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $d \mid 2 n^{2}$. Show that $n^{2}+d$ is not a perfect square.
2. Find all $d \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that $d \mid 3 n^{2}$ and $n^{2}+d$ is a perfect square.

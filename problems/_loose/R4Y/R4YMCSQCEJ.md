On the bisectors of the angles $A, B, C, D$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $A', B', C', D'$ are taken respectively such that the line $A'B'$ is parallel to $AB$, the line $B'C'$ is parallel to $BC$, and the line $C'D'$ is parallel to $CD$. Prove that a) (3 p.) the line $D'A'$ is parallel to $DA$; b) (4 p.) if it is additionally known that $A'C' \| BD$, then $B'D' \| AC$.

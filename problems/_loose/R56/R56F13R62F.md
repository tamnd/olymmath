Consider the function $f(x)$ on $[0,1]$ satisfying the conditions:
(1) $f(x) \geqslant 0$, for any $x \in[0,1]$;
(2) $f(1)=1$;
(3) $f(x)+f(y) \leqslant f(x+y), x, y, x+y \in[0,1]$

Try to find the smallest constant $c$, such that for any function $f(x)$ satisfying (1) - (3), we have $f(x) \leqslant c x, x \in[0,1]$.

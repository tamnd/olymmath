A regular n-gon inscribed in the unit circle has the property that it maps onto itself when rotated by $180^{\circ}$ about the center of the unit circle. On the periphery of the unit circle, any point $P$ is given.

Determine, under these conditions, from the given number $n$ the sum $s$ of the squares of the distances from the point $P$ to all points of the n-gon!

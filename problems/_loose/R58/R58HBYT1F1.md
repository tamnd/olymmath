There are $2016$ candies in a pile. Hermina divides the pile into two non-empty piles, then divides any of them (of course, if there is more than one candie in it) into two non-empty piles again, and so on, until finally there are $2016$ piles of one candie each. Will there always be a moment (no matter how Hermina divides the candies) when, from all the piles formed at that time, it will be possible to:
a) choose $300$ piles containing exactly $1000$ candies?
b) choose $500$ piles containing exactly $1000$ candies?

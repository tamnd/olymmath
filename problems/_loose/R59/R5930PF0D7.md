Prove the theorem of parallel lines dividing segments proportionally: If $A B, A C$ intersect at $A$, and points $B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are on $A B, A C$ respectively, then
$$
B^{\prime} C^{\prime} / / B C \Leftrightarrow \frac{A B^{\prime}}{B^{\prime} B}=\frac{A C^{\prime}}{C^{\prime} C} .
$$

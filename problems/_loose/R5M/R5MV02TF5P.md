The 87th question: Let for all $i=1, 2, \ldots, r, P_{i}(x)$ be non-zero polynomials, $\operatorname{deg} P_{i}(x)=n_{i}$, and $n_{1}+n_{2}+\cdots+n_{r}<\frac{r(r-1)}{2}$, prove: There exist not all zero numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{r}$, such that $a_{1} P_{1}(x)+a_{2} P_{2}(x)+\cdots+a_{r} P_{r}(x) \equiv 0$.

(2005 Balkan Mathematical Olympiad) Let $m, n$ be coprime positive integers, with $m$ even and $n$ odd. Prove: the sum $\frac{1}{2 n}+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)\left[\frac{\pi}{n}\right]\left\{\frac{m k}{n}\right\}$ does not depend on $m, n$, where $[x]$ is the greatest integer not exceeding $x$, $\{x\}=x-\lceil x\rceil$, and the sum of an empty sequence is 0.

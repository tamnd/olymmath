$\mathrm{Az} A B C D$ quadrilateral has all four of its side lines tangent to a circle, the center of which is $O$. Express the sum of the angles $A O B$ and $C O D$ in terms of the angles of the quadrilateral, as well as the sum of the angles $B O C$ and $D O A$, in the following situations:

I. Each of the four side lines touches the circle at a point on the side segment.

II. Two side lines touch the circle at a point outside the side segment.

III. All four side lines touch the circle at a point outside the side segment.

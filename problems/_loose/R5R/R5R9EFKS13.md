Let $f$ be a twice differentiable function on the open interval $(-1,1)$ such that $f(0)=1$. Suppose $f$ also satisfies $f(x) \ge 0, f'(x) \le 0$ and $f''(x) \le f(x)$, for all $x\ge 0$. Show that $f'(0) \ge -\sqrt2$.

Let $\xi$ and $\zeta$ be random variables, $\mathrm{E}|\xi|, \mathrm{E}|\zeta|<\infty, \mathrm{E}[\xi \mid \mathscr{E}] \geqslant \zeta$ and $\mathrm{E}[\zeta \mid \mathscr{G}] \geqslant \xi$ a.s. for some (completed) $\sigma$-algebras $\mathscr{E}$ and $\mathscr{G}$. Show that $\xi$ is $\mathscr{E} \cap \mathscr{G}$-measurable and $\mathrm{P}(\xi=\zeta)=1$.

In every triangle, the sum of the interior angles is $180^{\circ}$, and in every quadrilateral, it is $360^{\circ}$.

a) Draw a pentagon, a hexagon, and a heptagon! Measure the interior angles and calculate the sum of the interior angles for each! What do you conjecture?

b) Prove your conjecture for every pentagon, hexagon, and heptagon!

c) Try to find and prove a formula that gives the sum of the interior angles in every n-gon for every natural number $n>3$!

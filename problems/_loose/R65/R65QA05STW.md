Let $ f:[0,1]\longrightarrow [0,1] $ a function with the property that, for all $ y\in [0,1] $ and $ \varepsilon >0, $ there exists a $ x\in [0,1] $ such that $ |f(x)-y|<\varepsilon . $

**a)** Prove that if $ \left. f\right|_{[0,1]} $ is continuos, then $ f $ is surjective.
**b)** Give an example of a function with the given property, but which isn´t surjective.

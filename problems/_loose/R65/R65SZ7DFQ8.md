We need boys! A ruler of a certain country, for purely military reasons, would like there to be more boys than girls among his subjects. Under threat of severe punishment, he decreed that there should be no more than one girl in each family. As a result, in this country, among the children of each woman, the last - and only the last - was a girl, because no woman, having given birth to a girl, dared to have more children. What proportion of boys were there in the total number of children in this country, if the chances of giving birth to a girl or a boy are equal?

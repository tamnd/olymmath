Given a convex polygon $M$ and a point $P$ inside it. Let $N$ be the number of vertices of $M$ such that the segment connecting $P$ with this vertex divides the angle at it into two acute angles, and $n$ be the number of sides such that the foot of the perpendicular dropped from point $P$ to this side falls on the segment of the side, not on its extension. Prove that $n=N$.

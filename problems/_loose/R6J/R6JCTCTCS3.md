For real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{10}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots$, $b_{10}$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{10}^{2}\right)\left(b_{1}^{2}+b_{2}^{2}+\cdots+b_{10}^{2}\right) \\
\geqslant\left(a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+\cdots+a_{10} b_{10}\right)^{2}+ \\
\left(a_{1} b_{2}-a_{2} b_{1}+a_{3} b_{4}-a_{4} b_{3}+\cdots+\right. \\
\left. a_{9} b_{10}-a_{10} b_{9}\right)^{2} .
\end{array}
$$

Show that if $a, b, c$ are non-zero rational numbers such that $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=1$, then the number $\mathrm{N}=\left(\frac{a b}{c}+1\right)\left(\frac{b c}{a}+1\right)\left(\frac{a c}{b}+1\right)$ is non-negative and $\sqrt{N}$ is rational.

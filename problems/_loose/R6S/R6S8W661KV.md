Two persons $A,\ B$ will finance to undertake a joint enterprise. Denote by $s$ their total sum of investment. Suppose that the profit $f(s)$ by the enterprise is given by

$$f(s) =\left\{
\begin{array}{ll}
\frac 14s(s-3)^2\ \ \ \ \ \ (0\leq s\leq 2) \\
-\frac 34s+2\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (s>2) & \end{array}
\right.$$

and the profit is made proportional allotment among each person according to their investment.

If $A's$ investment $a\ (0\leq a\leq 2)$ is constant, then find the value of $s$ for which $B's$ sum of profit-allotment is maximized.

*1992 Tokyo University entrance exam/Humanities*

A "Moskvich", a "Zaporozhets", and a "Niva" driving towards them passed an observer on the highway. It is known that when the "Moskvich" was level with the observer, it was equidistant from the "Zaporozhets" and the "Niva", and when the "Niva" was level with the observer, it was equidistant from the "Moskvich" and the "Zaporozhets". Prove that the "Zaporozhets" was equidistant from the "Niva" and the "Moskvich" at the moment it passed the observer. (Assume the speeds of the cars are constant. In the considered moments, the equidistant cars were on opposite sides of the observer.)

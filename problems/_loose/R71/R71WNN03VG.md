Six players participated in a chess tournament where each player played exactly one game against every other player. As usual, in the event of a draw, each player received half a point, and otherwise, the winner received 1 point and the loser 0 points. After the tournament, an observer noted that no two of the six players had the same score.

The goal is to find the highest possible score that the last-placed player (i.e., the player with the lowest score) could achieve.

a) State this number and prove that the last-placed player cannot achieve a higher score, assuming only that the observer's statement is true!

b) Furthermore, show - for example, with a possible table of the results of the individual games - that there can be results where (the observer's statement is true and) the last-placed player achieves the score you have stated!

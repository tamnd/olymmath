Let $A A^{\prime}$ be the major axis of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{9}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$, and $P\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right)$ be a point on the ellipse. Draw a line $l$ through $P$ with a slope of $-\frac{4 x_{1}}{9 y_{1}}$. Draw lines perpendicular to the major axis through $A$ and $A^{\prime}$, intersecting $l$ at $M$ and $M^{\prime}$, respectively. Prove that $|A M| \cdot\left|A^{\prime} M^{\prime}\right|=4$, and find the minimum value of the area of quadrilateral $M A A^{\prime} M^{\prime}$.

Prove that the radius $R$ of the circumcircle of an arbitrary triangle, the radius $r$ of the inscribed circle, and its perimeter $P$ satisfy the inequality:

$$
\text { a) } R>\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \sqrt{Pr} \text {, }
$$

if the triangle is acute or obtuse;

$$
\text { b) } R \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \sqrt{Pr}
$$

if the triangle is right.

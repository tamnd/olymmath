Given 100 integers. From the first number, the sum of the digits of the second number is subtracted, from the second number, the sum of the digits of the third number is subtracted, and so on, finally, from the 100th number, the sum of the digits of the first number is subtracted. Can these differences be equal to $1, 2, \ldots, 100$ in some order?

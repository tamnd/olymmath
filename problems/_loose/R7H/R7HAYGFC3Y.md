Twenty students go abseiling during a school trip. In each round, one student gets a turn to abseil, so after twenty rounds, everyone has safely descended. To determine who goes first in round 1, cards with numbers 1 to 20 are distributed to the students. The one who gets the 1 starts. In round 2, cards with numbers 1 to 19 are distributed to the remaining students; the student who gets the 1 is next. This continues for each round, until in round 20, only one student is left, who automatically gets a card with 1. By a bizarre coincidence, no student gets a card with the same number twice. Sara gets the card with 11 in the first round.

What result does Sara get if she adds up all the numbers on her cards?

Let $k$ be a circle, its center be $M$. This circle is inscribed with an acute triangle $ABC$, for which the sizes $\alpha, \beta$ of the angles $\angle BAC, \angle ABC$ are assumed to satisfy $\alpha > \beta$. In triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $AB$. The ray from $C$ through $M$ intersects $k$ at $E$.

Prove that under these assumptions, the angle $\angle DCE$ always has the size $\alpha - \beta$!

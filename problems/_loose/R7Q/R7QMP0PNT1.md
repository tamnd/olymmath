Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and $\gamma$ its circumcircle. Let $B^{\prime}$ be the midpoint of $AC$ and $C^{\prime}$ the midpoint of $AB$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$, and let $G$ be the centroid. Let $\omega$ be the circle passing through $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ and tangent to $\gamma$ at a point $X \neq A$. Show that $D, G$, and $X$ are collinear.

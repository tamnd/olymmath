Show something trigonometric: Let $A B C$ be a triangle and $P, Q$, and $R$ be points on $B C, A C$ and $A B$ respectively. The lines $A P, B Q$ and $C R$ are concurrent if and only if

$$
\frac{\sin (\widehat{B A P})}{\sin (\widehat{P A C})} \cdot \frac{\sin (\widehat{C B Q})}{\sin (\widehat{Q B A})} \cdot \frac{\sin (\widehat{A C R})}{\sin (\widehat{R C B})}=1
$$

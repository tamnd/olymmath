(Jury, SFRY, 79). Prove that any function $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$, satisfying one of the identities

$$
\begin{gathered}
f(x+y) \equiv f(x)+f(y), x, y \in \mathbf{R} \\
f(x y+x+y) \equiv f(x y)+f(x)+f(y), x, y \in \mathbf{R}
\end{gathered}
$$

satisfies the other.

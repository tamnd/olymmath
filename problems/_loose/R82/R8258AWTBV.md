The sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is defined by the conditions $a_{1}=2$ and $a_{n+1}=2^{a_{n}}+2$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. If $m$ and $n$ are natural numbers and $m<n$, prove that $a_{m} \mid a_{n}$.

Find all natural numbers $n\ge 3$ for which there exist $n$ non-zero real numbers $x_1$ , $x_2$ , $...$ , $x_n$ which themselves form an increasing arithmetic progression, i.e. $$ x_2-x_1=x_3-x_2=...=x_n-x_{n-1}>0 $$ and their inverses $\frac{1}{x_1}$ , $\frac{1}{x_2}$ , $...$ , $\frac{1}{x_n}$ , written perhaps in a different order, also form an increasing arithmetic progression.

Given an ellipse $C$ centered at the origin with foci on the $x$-axis, the eccentricity of the ellipse is $\frac{1}{2}$, and it passes through the point $M\left(1, \frac{3}{2}\right)$. A line $l$ passing through the point $P(2,1)$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at two distinct points $A, B$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) Does there exist a line $l$ such that $\overrightarrow{P A} \cdot \overrightarrow{P B}=\overrightarrow{P M}^{2}$? If it exists, find the equation of the line $l$; if not, explain the reason.

A tortoise is given an $80$-second head start in a race. When Achilles catches up to where the tortoise was when he (Achilles) began running, he finds that while he is now $40$ meters ahead of the starting line, the tortoise is now $5$ meters ahead of him. At this point, how long will it be, in seconds, before Achilles passes the tortoise?

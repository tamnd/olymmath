In the inn U Temného hvozdu, giant twins Pravdoslav and Křivomil serve the guests. Pravdoslav is honest and always charges accurately, while Křivomil is dishonest and always adds two krejcars to the price of each pie and each jug of mead. One day, seven dwarfs visited this inn and sat at two tables. The dwarfs paid the same amount for four pies from one giant as they did for three jugs of mead from the other. Another time, the dwarfs paid 14 krejcars more for four jugs of mead from Křivomil than for three pies from Pravdoslav.

Determine how much one pie and one jug of mead cost at Pravdoslav. All prices are in whole krejcars and did not change between the two visits.

(M. Petrová, M. Dillingerová)

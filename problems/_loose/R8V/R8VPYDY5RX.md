A highway has 10 bus stops $A_{0}, A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{9}$. The distance between adjacent stops is $a$ kilometers. A car departs from station $A_{0}$, travels to all stations to deliver goods. The car only stops once at each station and finally returns to the starting station $A_{0}$. Due to the need for cargo transportation, the car does not necessarily stop at stations $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, \cdots$ in sequence (for example, it can go from $A_{3} \rightarrow A_{5} \rightarrow A_{7} \rightarrow \cdots$). What is the maximum possible distance the car can travel in kilometers? What is the minimum distance? Explain your reasoning.

Let the side lengths of an $n$-sided polygon inscribed in a circle of radius $R$ be $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, and the area be $F$. Prove or disprove
$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}\right)^{3} \geqslant 8 n^{2} R F \sin \frac{\pi}{n} \tan \frac{\pi}{n} ;
$$

equality holds if and only if the $n$-sided polygon is a regular $n$-sided polygon.

The slant height of a cone is $l$, and the apex angle of the axial section is $120^{\circ}$. Then the range of the area of the cone's section passing through the apex is
(A) $\left(0, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} l^{2}\right]$.
(B) $\left[\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} l^{2}, \frac{l^{2}}{2}\right]$.
(C) $\left(0, \frac{l^{2}}{2}\right]$.
(D) $\left[0, \frac{l^{2}}{2}\right]$.
（2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991）

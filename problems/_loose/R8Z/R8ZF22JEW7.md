Find all functions $f: \mathbb{Q}[x] \to \mathbb{R}$ such that:
(a) for all $P, Q \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ , $f(P \circ Q) = f(Q \circ P);$ (b) for all $P, Q \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ with $PQ \neq 0$ , $f(P\cdot Q) = f(P) + f(Q).$

( $P \circ Q$ indicates $P(Q(x))$ .)

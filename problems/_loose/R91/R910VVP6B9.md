Let $a, b$ and $c$ be natural numbers. Determine the smallest value that the following expression can take: $$ \frac{a}{gcd\,\,(a + b, a - c)}
+
\frac{b}{gcd\,\,(b + c, b - a)}
+
\frac{c}{gcd\,\,(c + a, c - b)}. $$ .
Remark: $gcd \, (6, 0) = 6$ and $gcd\,\,(3, -6) = 3$ .

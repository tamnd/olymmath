a) A miserly knight keeps his gold coins in six chests. One day, while counting them, he noticed that if he opens any two chests, he can redistribute the coins in them equally between these two chests. He also noticed that if he opens any 3, 4, or 5 chests, he can also redistribute the coins in them so that all the opened chests have an equal number of coins. At this moment, he thought he heard a knock at the door, and the old miser never found out whether he could redistribute all the coins equally among all six chests. Can we, without looking into the cherished chests, give a precise answer to this question? $[2$ points $]$

b) And if there were eight chests, and the Miserly Knight could redistribute the coins equally in any $2,3,4,5,6$ or 7 chests?

$[5$ points $]$ (I. V. Raskina)

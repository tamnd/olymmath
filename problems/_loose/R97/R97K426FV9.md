Let $n(n>3)$ complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}$ satisfy the following conditions:
(1) $\sum_{i=1}^{n} z_{i}=0$;
(2) $\left|z_{j}\right|<1(j=1,2, \cdots, n)$.
Prove: $\min _{1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n}\left|z_{i}+z_{j}\right|<1$.

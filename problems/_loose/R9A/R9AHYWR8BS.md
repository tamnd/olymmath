In a ski jumping competition, 65 participants are involved. They jump one by one according to a predetermined order. Each participant performs one jump. We assume that the scores obtained are all non-zero and that every final ranking is equally likely. At any point during the competition, the leader is the participant who has achieved the best score up to that moment. Let $ p $ be the probability that the leader changes exactly once during the entire competition. Prove that $ p > \frac{1}{16} $.

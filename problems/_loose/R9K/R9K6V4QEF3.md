7.2 can be represented as $2=2=1+1, 3$ can be represented as $3=3=1+2=2+1=1+1+1, 4$ can be represented as $4=4=1+3=3+1=2+2=2+1+1=1+2+1=1+1+2=1+1+1+1$ and so on. The above method of representing positive integers is called the ordered partition of positive integers. Find the number of ordered partitions of a natural number $n$.

The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ of a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ are perpendicular and intersect at point $\boldsymbol{O}$. Circles $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$ with centers at points $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}, O_{4}$ and radii $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}, r_{4}$ are inscribed in triangles $A O B, B O C, C O D, D O A$, respectively. Prove the following statements:

a) $2\left(r_{1}+r_{2}+r_{3}+r_{4}\right) \leq(2-\sqrt{2})(A C+B D)$;

b) $O_{1} O_{2}+O_{2} O_{3}+O_{3} O_{4}+O_{4} O_{1} \leq 2(\sqrt{2}-1)(A C+B D)$.

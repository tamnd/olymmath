In an acute triangle $A B C$, points $D, E, F$ are located on the sides $B C, C A, A B$ respectively such that

$$
\frac{C D}{C E}=\frac{C A}{C B}, \frac{A E}{A F}=\frac{A B}{A C}, \frac{B F}{B D}=\frac{B C}{B A}
$$

Prove that $A D, B E, C F$ are the altitudes of $A B C$.

Let $A B C D E$ be a pentagon inscribed in a circle with center $O$. Segments $\overline{A C}$ and $\overline{E B}$ intersect at point $P$, and segments $\overline{B D}$ and $\overline{E C}$ intersect at point $Q$. If lines $P Q$ and $A D$ are parallel to each other, prove that line $E O$ is perpendicular to both of these lines.

The function $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ has the following property: If $A, B, C \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$ are vertices of an equilateral triangle with side length 1, then

$$
f(A)+f(B)+f(C)=0 \text {. }
$$

Prove that $f(x)=0$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$.

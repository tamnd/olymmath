Let $f(x)=\mathrm{e}^{x}-\cos x(x>0)$. The sequence of positive numbers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies $a_{1}=1$, and for $n \geqslant 2$, $f\left(a_{n}\right)=a_{n-1}$. Prove:
(1) For $n \geqslant 2$, $a_{n-1}>a_{n}+a_{n}^{2}$;
(2) $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}<2 \sqrt{n}$.

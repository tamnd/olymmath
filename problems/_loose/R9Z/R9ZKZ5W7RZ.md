(Mathematics for Middle School, Issue 1, 2004 Olympiad Training Problem) Let $n$ be a given positive integer, and $n \geqslant 3$. For $n$ real numbers $x_{1}$, $x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$, denote the minimum value of $\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n)$ as $m$. If $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}=1$, try to find the maximum value of $m$.

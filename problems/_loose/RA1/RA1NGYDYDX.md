Given that the center of a hyperbola is at the origin, and its foci are on the $x$-axis. A line passing through the right focus of the hyperbola with a slope of $\sqrt{\frac{3}{5}}$ intersects the hyperbola at points $P$ and $Q$. If $O P \perp O Q$, and $|P Q|=4$, find the equation of the hyperbola.
(1991 National College Entrance Examination for Science)

Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a right triangle at $\mathrm{C}$. We denote $\mathrm{N}$ as the foot of the altitude from vertex $\mathrm{C}$ in triangle $A B C$. The bisectors of angles $\widehat{N C A}$ and $\widehat{\mathrm{BCN}}$ intersect the line (AB) at $\mathrm{K}$ and $\mathrm{L}$, respectively. Let $\mathrm{S}$ and $\mathrm{T}$ be the centers of the inscribed circles of triangles $B C N$ and $\mathrm{NCA}$. Show that the points $\mathrm{S}, \mathrm{K}, \mathrm{T}$, and $\mathrm{L}$ are concyclic.

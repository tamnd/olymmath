Let $n$ be a strictly positive integer, $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n+1}$ be strictly positive real numbers, and $px_{1}^{p}+\ldots+x_{n}^{p}$.

1) Show that $x_{n+1}^{q}>x_{1}^{q}+\ldots+x_{n}^{q}$.
2) Show that $\left(x_{n+1}^{p}-\left(x_{1}^{p}+\ldots+x_{n}^{p}\right)\right)^{q}<\left(x_{n+1}^{q}-\left(x_{1}^{q}+\ldots+x_{n}^{q}\right)\right)^{p}$.

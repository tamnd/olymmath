Let's draw the isosceles right triangle $A B C$. We draw a random line through the right angle vertex $A$ and drop perpendiculars $B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ to this line. Show that

$$
\overline{A B}_{1}^{2}+\overline{A C}_{1}^{2}=\overline{B B}_{1}^{2}+\overline{C C}_{1}^{2}
$$

and that each of these sums is a constant value.

A finite number of rectangular tablecloths are placed on a square desk so that they do not protrude from the desk.
Each side of the tablecloth is parallel to the side of the desk. Show that if the area of the overlapping parts of any two tablecloths is not $0$, then the area of the overlapping parts of all tablecloths is not $0$.

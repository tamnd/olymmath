Let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be angles of a triangle. Determine all real triplets $x,y,z$ satisfying the system
\begin{align*}
x\cos\beta+\frac1z\cos\alpha &=1,
y\cos\gamma+\frac1x\cos\beta &=1,
z\cos\alpha+\frac1y\cos\gamma &=1.
\end{align*}

Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be positive numbers, $m \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$. Prove: $\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(m+x_{i}\right) \geqslant\left(m+\sqrt[n]{\prod_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}}\right)^{n}$.

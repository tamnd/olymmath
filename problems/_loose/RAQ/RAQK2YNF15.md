Let two circles $\Gamma 1$ and $\Gamma 2$, intersecting at two distinct points $A$ and $B$, and let $P \in \Gamma 1$ and $Q \in \Gamma 2$ such that $P, B, Q$ are collinear in this order. Let $T$ be the intersection of the tangents to $\Gamma 2$ at $P$ and to $\Gamma 2$ at $Q$.

Show that $A Q T P$ is cyclic.

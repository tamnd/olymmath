a) Construct a pentagon $A B C D E$ in which the side lengths $A B=50 \mathrm{~mm}, B C=45 \mathrm{~mm}, A E=54$ mm and the interior angles $\angle B A E, \angle A B C, \angle B C D, \angle A E D$ are, in this order, $\alpha=100^{\circ}, \beta=110^{\circ}, \gamma=106^{\circ}, \rho=114^{\circ}$!

b) Now construct two points $F$ and $G$ that lie on the line through $A$ and $B$ such that the triangle $F G D$ has the same area as the pentagon $A B C D E$!

Describe your construction of the points $F$ and $G$! Prove that the conditions required are satisfied by the points constructed according to your description!

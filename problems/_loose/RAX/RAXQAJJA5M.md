In the trihedral angle $V-A B C$, $V D$ is the bisector of the dihedral angle $B V C$. If $\angle A V D \gtreqless \frac{\pi}{2}$, then $\frac{1}{2}(\angle A V B+\angle A V C) \leqslant \angle A V D$.
(1957 Shanghai Competition Problem)

The circle $k_{1}$ with radius $R$ touches the circle $k_{2}$ with radius $2R$ externally at point $E_{3}$. The circle $k_{3}$ with radius $3R$ also touches the circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ externally. The point of tangency between the circles $k_{2}$ and $k_{3}$ is $E_{1}$, and the point of tangency between the circles $k_{3}$ and $k_{1}$ is $E_{2}$. Prove that the circumcircle of the triangle $E_{1} E_{2} E_{3}$ is congruent to the circle $k_{1}$.

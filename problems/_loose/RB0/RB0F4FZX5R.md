Given a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ such that the tangents at points $B$ and $D$ to its circumscribed circle $k$ intersect on the line $AC$. Points $E$ and $F$ lie on the circle $k$ such that the lines $AC$, $DE$, and $BF$ are parallel. Let $M$ be the intersection of the lines $BE$ and $DF$. If $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are the feet of the altitudes of triangle $ABC$, prove that the points $P$, $Q$, $R$, and $M$ lie on the same circle.

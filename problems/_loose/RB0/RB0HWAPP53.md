Prove: There exist infinitely many numbers $B$ such that the equation
$$
\left[x^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]+\left[y^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]=B
$$

has at least 1980 natural number solutions $x, y$ (where $[z]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $z$).

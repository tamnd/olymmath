Given $n^{3}$ small cubes to form an $n \times n \times n(n \geqslant 3)$ cube, prove that it is possible to write distinct integers in these $n^{3}$ small cubes such that the sum of the integers in any string of $n$ small cubes parallel to an edge of the cube is zero.

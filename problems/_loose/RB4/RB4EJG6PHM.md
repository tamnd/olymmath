On the vectors $\overrightarrow{A_{i} B_{i}}$, where $i=1, \ldots, k$, regular and identically oriented $n$-gons $A_{\mathrm{i}} B_{\mathrm{i}} C_{\mathrm{i}} D_{\mathrm{i}} \ldots$ (n $\geq 4$ ) are constructed. Prove that the $k$-gons $C_{1} \ldots C_{\mathrm{k}}$ and $D_{1} \ldots D_{\mathrm{k}}$ are regular and identically oriented if and only if the $k$-gons $A_{1} \ldots A_{\mathrm{k}}$ and $B_{1} \ldots B_{\mathrm{k}}$ are regular and identically oriented.

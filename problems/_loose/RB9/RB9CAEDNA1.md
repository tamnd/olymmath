The subset $T$ of positive rational numbers is called complete if from $\frac{p}{q} \in T$ it follows that $\frac{p}{p+q}$ and $\frac{q}{p+q}$ are also in $T$.

Which positive rational numbers $r$ have the following property: if $r$ is in a complete set, then this set contains every rational number between 0 and 1.

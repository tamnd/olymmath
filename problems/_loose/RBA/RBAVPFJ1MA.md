Let $\triangle ABC$ have $G$ and $I$ as the centroid and the incenter of $\triangle ABC$, respectively, and let $M$ be a point in the plane of the triangle. Prove:
a) $\overrightarrow{M A} + \overrightarrow{M B} + \overrightarrow{M C} = 3 \cdot \overrightarrow{M G}$
b) $a \overrightarrow{M A} + b \overrightarrow{M B} + c \overrightarrow{M C} = (a + b + c) \cdot \overrightarrow{M I}$, with the notation $a = BC, b = AC, c = AB$.
c) If $\vec{v} = a \overrightarrow{G A} + b \overrightarrow{G B} + c \overrightarrow{G C}$ and $\vec{u} = \overrightarrow{I A} + \overrightarrow{I B} + \overrightarrow{I C}$, show that the vectors $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{u}$ are collinear and

$$
3 \cdot |\vec{v}| = (a + b + c) \cdot |\vec{u}|
$$

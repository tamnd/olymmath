Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, let the quadrilateral formed by the centroids of $\triangle A B C, \triangle B C D, \triangle D B A$, and $\triangle C D A$ be denoted as $K L M N$. Prove: The intersection point of the line segments joining the midpoints of the opposite sides of $A B C D$ coincides with the intersection point of the line segments joining the midpoints of the opposite sides of $K L M N$.

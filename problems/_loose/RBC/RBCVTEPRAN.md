Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. The midpoints of sides $A B$ and $C D$ are denoted as $K$ and $M$, respectively. The intersection point of $A M$ and $D K$ is denoted as $O$, and the intersection point of $B M$ and $C K$ is denoted as $P$. Prove that the area of quadrilateral $M O K P$ is equal to the sum of the areas of triangles $B P C$ and $A O D$.

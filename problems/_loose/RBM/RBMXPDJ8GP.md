Question 1 In a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, $D$, $E$, $F$ are the midpoints of sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively. Draw another tangent from point $D$ to the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ (different from line $BC$), intersecting $EF$ at point $X$. Similarly, construct points $Y$, $Z$. Prove that $X$, $Y$, $Z$ are collinear.

Question 2 In a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, $D$, $E$, $F$ are points on lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ (different from the vertices of $\triangle ABC$) respectively. Lines $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ intersect at point $P$. Draw another tangent from point $D$ to the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ (different from line $BC$), intersecting $EF$ at point $X$. Similarly, construct points $Y$, $Z$. Prove that $X$, $Y$, $Z$ are collinear.

Question 3 In a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, the incircle $\Gamma$ touches sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at points $R$, $S$, $T$ respectively. Points $D$, $E$, $F$ are on lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ (different from the vertices of $\triangle ABC$) respectively, and lines $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ intersect at point $P$. Draw another tangent from point $D$ to circle $\Gamma$ (different from line $BC$), with the tangency point being $X$. Similarly, construct points $Y$, $Z$. Prove that $AX$, $BY$, $CZ$ are concurrent.

Given that quadrilateral $A B C D$ is inscribed in circle $\Gamma$, point $P$ lies on the extension of line segment $A C$, and $P B, P D$ are tangent to circle $\Gamma$. A tangent to circle $\Gamma$ through point $C$ intersects lines $P D, A D$ at points $Q, R$ respectively, and the second intersection point of line $A Q$ with circle $\Gamma$ is $E$. Prove that points $B, E, R$ are collinear. ${ }^{[4]}$
(25th Asia Pacific Mathematical Olympiad)

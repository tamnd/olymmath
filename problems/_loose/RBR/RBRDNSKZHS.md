Given points $P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{k}$ on a straight line. For each pair of points, we draw a circle with the segment as its diameter and color each circle with one of $n$ different colors. Show that if $k>2^{n}$, then there will be two circles of the same color that touch each other externally.

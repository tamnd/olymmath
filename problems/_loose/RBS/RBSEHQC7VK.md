A permutation $(a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_{100})$ of $(1, 2, 3, \dots, 100)$ is chosen at random. Denote by $p$ the probability that $a_{2i} > a_{2i - 1}$ for all $i \in \{1, 2, 3, \dots, 50\}$ . Compute the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(a, b)$ satisfying $ \frac{1}{a^b} = p$ .

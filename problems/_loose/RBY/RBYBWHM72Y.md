The seventy-third problem: In $\triangle A B E$, $O$ is the circumcenter, $H$ is the orthocenter, $\mathrm{BD} \perp A C$ at $\mathrm{D}$, $\mathrm{CE} \perp \mathrm{AB}$ at $E$, $A G \perp$ $\mathrm{OH}$, intersects $\mathrm{DE}$ at $\mathrm{F}$, and intersects $\mathrm{BC}$ at $\mathrm{G}$. Prove that $\mathrm{F}$ is the midpoint of $\mathrm{GG}$. (Adapted from the 2005 China Training Team Problem by Tian Kai Bin)

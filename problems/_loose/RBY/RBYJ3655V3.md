Let $A, B$ and $C$ be points in space that do not lie on the same line. Denote by $S$ the area of triangle $ABC$. Prove that

$$
S^{2}=S_{x y}^{2}+S_{y z}^{2}+S_{x z}^{2}
$$

where $S_{x y}, S_{y z}$ and $S_{x z}$ are the areas of the projections of triangle $ABC$ onto the coordinate planes $O x y, O y z$ and $O x z$, respectively.

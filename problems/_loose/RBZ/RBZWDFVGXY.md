Given a semicircular plate of radius $R$ and a point $P$ on its diameter $AB$. We divide the plate into three parts with lines $PC$ and $PD$ such that $\angle APD = \angle DPC = \angle CPB = 60^{\circ}$. Show that the sum of the radii of the circles inscribed in each of the three parts is not greater than $R$!

In the right-angled triangle $ABC$ ($\angle A = 90^{\circ}$); from point $F$, we draw a circle passing through point $A$, which touches $BC$ at $B$; from point $G$, we similarly draw a circle passing through point $A$, which touches $BC$ at $C$. Tasks:

$1^{\circ}$. Show that the two circles touch each other.

$2^{\circ}$. If the sides of the right-angled triangle $ABC$ are given, calculate the radii of the circles, as well as the chords $AD$ and $AE$, which are the extensions of the sides $AC$ and $AB$.

$3^{\circ}$. If only the side $BC = a$ is given, determine the sides $AB$ and $AC$ such that $CD + BE = m$.

9・148 Given non-negative real numbers $a, b, c$ satisfying $a+b+c \leqslant 3$, prove:
$$
\frac{a}{1+a^{2}}+\frac{b}{1+b^{2}}+\frac{c}{1+c^{2}} \leqslant \frac{3}{2} \leqslant \frac{1}{1+a}+\frac{1}{1+b}+\frac{1}{1+c} .
$$

Given $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ are positive numbers, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=1$, prove:
$$
\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{2}^{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1+\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}}\right) \geqslant\left(1+n^{2}\right)^{n} .
$$

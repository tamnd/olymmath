(Problem from the 54th Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad) In an $n \times n (n \geqslant 3)$ grid, each cell is filled with a certain integer. It is known that the sum of all numbers in any $3 \times 3$ subgrid is even, and the sum of all numbers in any $5 \times 5$ subgrid is also even. Find all $n$ such that the sum of all numbers in the grid is even.

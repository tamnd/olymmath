In any acute scalene triangle $A B C$, let $O, V$ and $S$ denote the center of the circumscribed circle, the intersection of the altitudes, and the center of the inscribed circle, respectively. Prove that the perpendicular bisector of segment $O V$ passes through the point $S$ if and only if one of the interior angles of triangle $A B C$ has a measure of $60^{\circ}$.

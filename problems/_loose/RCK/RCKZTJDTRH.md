We will prove the following equalities:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1^{3}+2^{3}+3^{3}+4^{3}+5^{3}+6^{3}}{5^{2}} & =\frac{\frac{13}{8}+\frac{8}{13}+2}{\frac{13}{8}+\frac{8}{13}-2} \\
\frac{1^{3}+2^{3}+3^{3}+4^{3}+5^{3}+6^{3}+7^{3}}{6^{2}} & =\frac{\frac{17}{11}+\frac{11}{17}+2}{\frac{17}{11}+\frac{11}{17}-2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Write in a closed form similar to the right-hand sides the quotient formed from the sum of the cubes of the first $n$ natural numbers and $(n-1)^{2}$.

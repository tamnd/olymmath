We say that a set $A \subset \mathbb{R}$ with at least three elements is free of arithmetic progressions if for any distinct $a, b, c \in A$, we have $a+b \neq 2 c$.

Show that the set $\left\{0,1,2, \ldots, 3^{8}-1\right\}$ contains a subset $A$ with at least 256 elements, free of arithmetic progressions.

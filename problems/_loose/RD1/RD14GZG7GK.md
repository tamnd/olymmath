Each video begins with a score of 0, and the score increases by 1 for each like vote and decreases by 1 for each dislike vote. At one point Sangho saw that his video had a score of 90, and that $65\%$ of the votes cast on his video were like votes. How many votes had been cast on Sangho's video at that point? $\textbf{(A) } 200 \textbf{(B) } 300 \textbf{(C) } 400 \textbf{(D) } 500 \textbf{(E) } 600 $

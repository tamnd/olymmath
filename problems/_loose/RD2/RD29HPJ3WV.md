In the lower left corner square $a 1$ of the chessboard, there is a rook. With each move, the rook can move 1 square horizontally or vertically. Can the rook visit all 64 squares of the chessboard, and visit the 64 squares exactly $1, 2, \cdots, 64$ times respectively, and finally
(1) return to the original starting square $a 1$?
(2) end the journey in square a2?
(The initial position in square $a 1$ also counts as visiting the square 1 time.)

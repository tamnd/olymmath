a) Helena and Gabriel jump simultaneously from a raft into a river and swim in opposite directions. Gabriel swims downstream with the current, and Helena swims upstream against the current, possibly at a different speed. After 5 minutes, they turn around and swim back to the raft, each maintaining a constant speed throughout the time. Who arrives first? (During this time, the raft was anchored, i.e., it remained stationary despite the current.)

b) At his usual speed, a man descends a river that is $15 \mathrm{~km}$ long in 5 hours less than the time he spends swimming the same river in the opposite direction. If he doubles his usual speed, he spends only 1 hour less descending the river than the time spent on the return trip. Assuming the speed of the current remains constant during the trips, what is its value in $\mathrm{km} / \mathrm{h}$?

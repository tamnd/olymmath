In rectangular coordinate system, define that if and only if both $x$ -axis and $y$ -axis of a point are integers, we call it integral point. Prove that there exists a series of concentric circles, satisfying:
(1)Exery itengral point is on the concentric circles.
(2)On each circle, there is exactly one itengral point.

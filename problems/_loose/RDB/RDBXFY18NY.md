Consider a matrix $A \in M_{3}(\mathbb{R})$ that has the following properties:

(1) $A$ and $A^{3}$ have the elements $-1,0,1$ on the main diagonal in this order;

(2) $A^{2}$ has the elements $1,0,1$ on the main diagonal in this order.

Prove that $A$ is singular, but has at least one non-zero minor of order 2.

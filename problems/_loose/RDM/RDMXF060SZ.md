A circle has a regular 2018-gon inscribed in it. The numbers 1, 2, ..., 2018 are arranged at the vertices of the 2018-gon, with one number per vertex, such that the sum of any two adjacent numbers (from this arrangement) is equal to the sum of the two numbers diametrically opposite to them. Determine the number of different arrangements of the numbers.

(Arrangements obtained by rotation around the center of the circle are considered the same).

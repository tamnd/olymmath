Mutually perpendicular lines $l$ and $m$ intersect at point $P$ of a circle, dividing the circle into three arcs. On each arc, mark a point such that the tangent line through this point intersects lines $l$ and $m$ at points equidistant from the point of tangency. Prove that the three marked points are the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

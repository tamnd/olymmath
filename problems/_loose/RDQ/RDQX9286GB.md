Let $p$ a prime number, $p\geq 5$ . Find the number of polynomials of the form
$$ x^p + px^k + p x^l + 1, k > l, k, l \in \left\{1,2,\dots,p-1\right\}, $$ which are irreducible in $\mathbb{Z}[X]$ .

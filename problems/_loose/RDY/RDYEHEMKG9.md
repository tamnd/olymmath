In the isosceles triangle $ABC$ ($AB=AC$), the perpendicular to the side $AB$ at its endpoint $B$ intersects the altitude $AE$ at point $D$. Prove that the difference $BD-ED$ gives the radius of the inscribed circle of the triangle, and the sum $BD+ED$ gives the radius of the circle that is tangent to the base $BC$ from the outside.

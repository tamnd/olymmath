In a regular hexagon with an area of $144 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, we have marked all its diagonals. The hexagon thus "broke down" into several triangles and quadrilaterals.

a) Determine into how many parts the diagonals divided the hexagon.

b) Determine the area of the regular hexagon that will be formed by combining all the quadrilateral parts of the given hexagon.

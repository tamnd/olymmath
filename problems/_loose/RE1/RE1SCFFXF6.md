Given the triangle $A B C$ and an arbitrary point $P$ in its plane. Draw a circle around the triangle $A B P$ and reflect this circle over the side $A B$, similarly reflect the circle around the triangle $A C P$ over the side $A C$, and the circle around the triangle $B C P$ over the side $B C$. Prove that the reflected circles also have a common point!

Let $A B C D$ be a square and $k$ a circle with center at point $B$ passing through points $A$ and $C$, and let $T$ be a point on circle $k$ inside the given square. The tangent to circle $k$ at point $T$ intersects segments $\overline{C D}$ and $\overline{D A}$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. Let $G$ and $H$ be the intersections of lines $B E$ and $B F$ with segment $\overline{A C}$, respectively.

Prove that the lines $B T, E H$, and $F G$ pass through the same point.

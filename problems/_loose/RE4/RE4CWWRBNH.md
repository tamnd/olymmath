Which area is larger? For two triangles, the sides are respectively equal to $\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}, \sqrt{b^{2}+c^{2}}, \sqrt{c^{2}+a^{2}}$ and $\sqrt{p^{2}+q^{2}}$, $\sqrt{q^{2}+r^{2}}, \sqrt{r^{2}+p^{2}}$. Which one has a larger area, given that, in addition, $a^{2} b^{2}+b^{2} c^{2}+c^{2} a^{2}=p^{2} q^{2}+q^{2} r^{2}+r^{2} p^{2}$ and $a>p$, $b>q$?

In the rectangle $ABCD$, points $E$ and $F$ are marked on the side $AB$ such that $AE = BF < BE$, and on the side $CD$, points $G$ and $H$ are marked such that $CG = DH = EF$.

a) What can be the value of the ratio $AE: AB$ so that $H$ is an interior point of the segment $DG$?

b) Let $J$ be the common point of the segments $EG$ and $FH$ - assuming the condition found is satisfied. Express the area of the V-shaped figure $DEFCGJHD$ using the data $AB = a$, $AD = b$, and the segment $EF = x$.

c) For which value of $x$ will the area of the V-shaped figure be equal to half the area of the rectangle? Construct the V-shaped figure in this situation.

d) For which $x$ will the area of the V-shaped figure be maximal, and for which will it be minimal?

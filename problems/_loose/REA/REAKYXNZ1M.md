Let $\mathbb{N}$ be the set of positive integers. Find all functions $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$ , $$m^2f(m) + n^2f(n) + 3mn(m + n)$$ is a perfect cube.

C31 (29-2, Czechoslovakia) Let $n$ be a positive integer, and $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{2 n+1}$ be a family of subsets of a set $B$, satisfying the conditions:
(1) Each $A_{i}$ contains exactly $2 n$ elements;
(2) $A_{i} \cap A_{j}(1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant 2 n+1)$ contains exactly one element;
(3) Each element of $B$ belongs to at least two subsets $A_{i_{1}}$ and $A_{i_{2}}, 1 \leqslant i_{1}<i_{2} \leqslant 2 n$ +1.

For which positive integers $n$ is it possible to label each element of $B$ with a tag that has either 0 or 1 written on it, such that each $A_{i}$ contains exactly $n$ elements labeled with 0? Explain your reasoning.

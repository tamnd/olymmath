In Cartesian coordinates, two areas $M,N$ are defined below: $M:y\geq0,y\leq x,y\leq 2-x$ ; $N:t\leq x\leq t+1$ . $t$ is a real number that $t\in[0,1]$ .
Then the area of $M\cap N$ is $\text{(A)}-t^2+t+\frac{1}{2} \text{(B)}-2t^2+2t \text{(C)}1-2t^2 \text{(D)}\frac{1}{2}(t-2)^2$

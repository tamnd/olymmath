- Allow me to introduce myself: Tra-la-la,- the second brother suddenly spoke up - I have a card of the black suit in my pocket.

Alice easily determined who he really was. Who?

- Congratulations!- said both brothers at the same time, shaking Alice's right hand with one and her left hand with the other. - You won the first round!
- Before each of the next four rounds,- said the first brother,- we will go to our cottage, where we keep a deck of cards. We will shuffle them thoroughly, and then one of us will draw a card, hide it in our pocket, and, coming out of the cottage, make some statement, and you must determine who is in front of you.
- And does the one who speaks have a card of the same suit in their pocket as in the previous round? - Alice asked.
- Not necessarily,- came the reply. - After all, every time we go into the cottage, the game starts anew, and we are free to choose a card of any suit.
- I see,- nodded Alice.

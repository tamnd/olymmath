Let $a, b, c$ be non-negative real numbers, not all zero. Show that:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{b+c}{2 a+b+c}}+\sqrt{\frac{c+a}{2 b+c+a}}+\sqrt{\frac{a+b}{2 c+a+b}} \leq 1+\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

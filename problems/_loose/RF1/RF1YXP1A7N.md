Each edge and each diagonal of a convex $n$-gon $(n \geqslant 3)$ is colored either red or blue. Prove that the vertices of the $n$-gon can be labeled as $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ such that one of the following two conditions is satisfied:

$1^{\circ}$ the segments $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots, A_{n-1} A_{n}, A_{n} A_{1}$ are of the same color;

$2^{\circ}$ for some $k, 1<k<n$, the segments $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots, A_{k-1} A_{k}$ are blue, while the segments $A_{k} A_{k+1}, \ldots, A_{n-1} A_{n}, A_{n} A_{1}$ are red.

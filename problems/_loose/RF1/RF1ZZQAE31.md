One hundred square envelopes of different sizes are arranged so that for any two different envelopes, the smaller envelope is inside the larger one or the envelopes are separate from each other. At the same time, both the smaller and the larger envelopes can contain other envelopes. Two arrangements are considered different if there are two envelopes that are one inside the other in one arrangement, but not in the other. How many different arrangements are there in which all other envelopes are inside the largest envelope?

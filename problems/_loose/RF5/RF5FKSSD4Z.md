Let $P$ and $Q$ be two distinct points on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ such that $|PA|^2 = |PB| \cdot |PC|$, $|QA|^2 = |QB| \cdot |QC|$ (one of the points is on the arc $\breve{AB}$, the other is on the arc $\widehat{AC}$). Find the difference $\widehat{PAB} - \widehat{QAC}$, if the difference of the angles $\hat{B}$ and $\hat{C}$ of triangle $ABC$ is $\alpha$.

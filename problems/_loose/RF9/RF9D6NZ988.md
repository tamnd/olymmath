Three friends enter Giorgio's pizzeria and each sit at one side of a rectangular table; the first is sitting at a side of length $70 \mathrm{~cm}$, the second and third sit opposite each other, on sides of length $l$. The pizzas have a diameter of $30 \mathrm{~cm}$; Giorgio serves the first customer's pizza so that it is tangent to his side of the table at the midpoint, and the pizzas of the other two so that they are tangent to their respective sides of the table and to the first pizza. What is the minimum value of $l$ (in centimeters) for which the three pizzas can fit entirely on the table?
(A) $10 \sqrt{5}$
(B) $20+5 \sqrt{5}$
(C) $15+10 \sqrt{5}$
(D) $30+10 \sqrt{5}$
(E) 60

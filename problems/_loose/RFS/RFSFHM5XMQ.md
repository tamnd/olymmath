Two circles are outside each other, with centers $O_{1}, O_{2}$. The radii perpendicular to the line connecting the centers and pointing in the same direction are $O_{1} A_{1}, O_{2} A_{2}$. The perpendicular bisector of segment $A_{1} A_{2}$ intersects $O_{1} O_{2}$ at $K$, and the reflection of $K$ over the midpoint of $O_{1} O_{2}$ is $L$. Prove that the tangent segments from $L$ to the two circles are of equal length.

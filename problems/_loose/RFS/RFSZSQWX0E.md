The 34th question: Given positive integers $\mathrm{n} k$, there is a password lock with $\mathrm{n}$ buttons, numbered from $1 \sim \mathrm{n}$. The password to open the lock is a sequence of $\mathrm{k}$ buttons. The lock will only open when these $\mathrm{k}$ buttons are pressed in the correct sequence consecutively (for example, if $\mathrm{n}=3, \mathrm{k}=2$, and the password is 13, pressing the buttons in the sequence $1, 2, 3, 2, 1, 1, 3$ will open the lock; pressing $2, 2, 3, 1, 3$ will also open the lock). To ensure that the lock is opened, what is the minimum number of button presses required?

The members of the arithmetic progression $1, 1+2n, 1+4n, \ldots (n \in \mathbb{N})$ are grouped in order into groups, such that the first group contains the first term, the second group contains the next $1+n$ terms, the third group contains the next $1+2n$ terms, and so on. Prove that the sum of all terms in each group is equal to the cube of the number of terms in that group.

Let $\triangle A B C$ have internal angles $\angle A, \angle B$, and $\angle C$ with opposite sides $a, b$, and $c$, respectively, and satisfy
$$
\sin A+\sin B=(\cos A+\cos B) \sin C \text {. }
$$
(1) Prove: $\triangle A B C$ is a right triangle;
(2) If $a+b+c=1+\sqrt{2}$, find the maximum area of $\triangle A B C$.

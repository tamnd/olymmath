Consider $k$ positive integers $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k$ satisfying $1\le a_1<a_2<\ldots<a_k\le n$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a_i,a_j)\le n$ for any $i,j$. Prove that
$$k\le2\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor.$$

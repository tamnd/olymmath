$\mathrm{Az} \mathrm{ABC}$ triangle has a circle inscribed touching its sides. Let the points of tangency be $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, and the center of the circle be O. What are the volumes of the solids that are formed when the segment (bounded by two lines and an arc) $A O$ rotates around, the segment $B C_{1} A_{1}$ rotates around $B O$, and the segment $C A_{1} B_{1}$ rotates around $C O$? How do the volumes of the three rotational solids compare to each other?

Given the angles $A, B, C$ of the triangle and the radius $r$ of the inscribed circle.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $M$ be a point on the side $AB$ and let $N$ be a point on the side $AC$. Suppose that $CM$ and $BN$ intersect at point $D$ and suppose that the circumcircles of $BDM$ and $BNC$ intersect again at point $E$. Show that $M, N, E$ are collinear if $\angle BDM = \angle ACB$.

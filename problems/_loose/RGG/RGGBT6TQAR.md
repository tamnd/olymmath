Circle $\omega$ is circumscribed around an acute-angled triangle $ABC$. A point $D$ is chosen on side $AB$, and a point $E$ is chosen on side $BC$ such that $AC \parallel DE$. Points $P$ and $Q$ on the smaller arc $AC$ of circle $\omega$ are such that $DP \parallel EQ$. The rays $QA$ and $PC$ intersect line $DE$ at points $X$ and $Y$ respectively. Prove that $\angle XBY + \angle PBQ = 180^{\circ}$.

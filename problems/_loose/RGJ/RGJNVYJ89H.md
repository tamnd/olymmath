Let $m$ and $n$ be two integers bigger than one $1$ . $m+n$ positive integers not exceeding $mn-1$ are chosen. Prove that among them one can find $x \neq y$ , that satisfy
$\lfloor \frac{x}{n} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{y}{n} \rfloor$ and $\lfloor \frac{x}{m} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{y}{m} \rfloor$ *A. Voidelevich*

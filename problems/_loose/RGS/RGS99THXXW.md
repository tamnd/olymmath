Let $a, b, c$ be any three real numbers, prove that the following system of equations has no real solutions:
$$
\begin{array}{c}
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+3\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right)=6, \\
a x+b y+c z=2 .
\end{array}
$$

Show that from $n$ different natural numbers, one can always select at least $\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{60}$ different triples $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}\right)$ such that $a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}$ is divisible by 3. (We do not consider triples different if they only differ in the order of their elements.)

In the $ x - y$ plane, let $ l$ be the tangent line at the point $ A\left(\frac {a}{2},\ \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}b\right)$ on the ellipse $ \frac {x^2}{a^2} + \frac {y^2}{b^2}=1\ (0 < b < 1 < a)$. Let denote $ S$ be the area of the figure bounded by $ l,$ the $ x$ axis and the ellipse.

(1) Find the equation of $ l$.

(2) Express $ S$ in terms of $ a,\ b$.

(3) Find the maximum value of $ S$ with the constraint $ a^2 + 3b^2 = 4$.

Consider the system $\left\lvert \begin{aligned} & x^{2}+y^{2}=a^{2}+2 \\ & \frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}=a\end{aligned}\right.$, where $a$ is a real number.

a) Solve the system for $a=0$.

b) Find all $a$, for which the system has exactly two solutions.

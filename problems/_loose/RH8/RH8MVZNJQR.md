Consider an infinite sequence of positive integers in which each positive integer occurs exactly once. Let $\{a_n\}, n\ge 1$ be such a sequence. We call it *consistent * if, for an arbitrary natural $k$ and every natural $n ,m$ such that $a_n <a_m$, the inequality $a_{kn} <a _{km}$ also holds. For example, the sequence $a_n = n$ is consistent .
a) Prove that there are consistent sequences other than $a_n = n$.
b) Are there consistent sequences for which $a_n \ne n, n\ge 2$ ?
c) Are there consistent sequences for which $a n \ne n, n\ge 1$ ?

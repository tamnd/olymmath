Let $\Omega=\{r: r \in[0,1] \cap \mathbb{Q}\}, \mathscr{A}$ be an algebra of sets, each of which is a finite sum of non-intersecting sets $A$ of the form $\{r: a<r<b\},\{r: a \leqslant r<b\},\{r: a<r \leqslant b\},\{r: a \leqslant r \leqslant b\}$, and $\mathrm{P}(A)=b-a$. Show that $\mathrm{P}(A), A \in \mathscr{A}$, is a finitely additive but not countably additive set function.

$P:$ I don't know what $n$ is.
$S$ : I don't know either, but I know whether $n$ is even.
$P$ : Now I know what $n$ is.
$S$ : Now I also know.
The teacher confirmed that $S$ and $P$ are honest and trustworthy, and every word they said is based on evidence.
What is the value of $n$? Why?

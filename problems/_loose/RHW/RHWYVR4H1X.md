The side of the base of a regular triangular prism $A B C A_1 B_1 C_1$ is 4, and the lateral edge is 3. On the edge $B B_1$, a point $F$ is taken, and on the edge $C C_1$, a point $G$ is taken such that $B_1 F=1, C G=\frac{2}{3}$. Points $E$ and $D$ are the midpoints of edges $A C$ and $B_1 C_1$ respectively. Find the minimum possible value of the sum $E P+P Q$, where point $P$ belongs to the segment $A_1 D$, and point $Q$ belongs to the segment $F G$.

In the trapezoid $A B C D$, the line passing through the intersection of the diagonals and parallel to the base $A B$ intersects the leg $A D$ at $E$. The lines through $D$ and $E$ parallel to the leg $B C$ intersect $A B$ at points $G$ and $F$ respectively, and the intersection of lines $A C$ and $D G$ is $H$. It is to be proven that the line $C F$ bisects the segment $G H$.

Given two coprime natural numbers $a$ and $b$. Consider the set $M$ of integers that can be represented in the form $a x + b y$, where $x$ and $y$ are non-negative integers.

a) What is the largest integer $c$ that does not belong to the set $M$?

b) Prove that of the two numbers $n$ and $c - n$ (where $n$ is any integer), one belongs to $M$ and the other does not.

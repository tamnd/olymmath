(Special case of Poncelet's theorem)

Let $ABC$ be a triangle. We denote $\Gamma$ its circumcircle and $\omega$ its incircle. Suppose that $P, Q$, and $R$ are on $\Gamma$ such that $(PQ)$ and $(QR)$ are tangent to the circle $\omega$. Show that in this case the line $(RP)$ is tangent to the incircle.

Write out and prove the "Three Perpendiculars Theorem" in solid geometry.

---

The "Three Perpendiculars Theorem" in solid geometry states that if a line is perpendicular to a plane, then any line in that plane that is perpendicular to the projection of the first line onto the plane is also perpendicular to the first line.

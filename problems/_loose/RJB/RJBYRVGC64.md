Among the following four propositions:
(1) If $a, b$ are real numbers, then $(a+b)^{0}=1$;
(2) If $a a b^{4}>a b^{2}$;
(3) If a quadrilateral is divided into four triangles of equal area by its two diagonals, then this quadrilateral is a parallelogram;
(4) If $P$ is any point inside rectangle $A B C D$, and points $E, F, G$, $H$ are the reflections of point $P$ about the four sides of the rectangle, then $S_{E F Y H}$ $=2 S_{A B C D}$.
The number of correct propositions is
(A) 1 .
(B) 2 .
(C) 3 .
(D) 4 .
(China Sichuan Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1992)

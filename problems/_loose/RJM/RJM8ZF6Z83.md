$$
x=1+0.1^{n} ; y=-0.1^{-n}, z=(-0.1)^{n}, u=(-1)^{n}+0.1^{n}
$$

and determine the nature of their change as $n$ increases without bound, i.e., as $n \rightarrow+\infty$.

Given a regular pentagon $A B C D E$. On side $A E$ there is a point $K$, and on side $C D$ there is a point $L$. It is known that $\angle L A E + \angle K C D = 108^{\circ}$, and $A K: K E = 3: 7$. Find $C L: A B$.

A regular pentagon is a pentagon in which all sides are equal and all angles are equal.

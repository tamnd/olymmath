a) Let $f:[0, \infty) \rightarrow[0, \infty)$ be a differentiable and convex function. Show that if $f(x) \leq x$ for all $x \geq 0$, then $f^{\prime}(x) \leq 1$ for all $x \geq 0$.

b) Determine the functions $f:[0, \infty) \rightarrow[0, \infty)$ that are differentiable and convex and have the property that $f(0)=0$ and $f^{\prime}(x) \cdot f(f(x))=x$ for all $x \geq 0$.

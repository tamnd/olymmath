(CPR, 78). Prove that if polynomials $P(x), Q(x), R(x)$ of the third degree with real coefficients satisfy the inequalities $P(x) \leqslant Q(x) \leqslant$ $\leqslant R(x)$ for all values of $x \in \mathbf{R}$ and at least at one point $x_{0} \in \mathbf{R}$ the equality $P\left(x_{0}\right)=R\left(x_{0}\right)$ holds, then for some number $k \in[0 ; 1]$ the identity

$$
Q(x) \equiv k P(x)+(1-k) R(x), x \in \mathbf{R}
$$

holds. Can the same be asserted for polynomials of the fourth degree?

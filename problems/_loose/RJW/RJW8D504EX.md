Hókuszpók enchanted the dwarves, who from now on can only move according to the following rules:

1. They can only move in straight lines.

2. In the morning, when they leave their hut, they can only head north.

3. During their movement, they can change direction as many times as they want, but every second direction change must be towards the north.

4. During the day, they cannot step on a place they have already been, except for their hut.

The dwarves would like to sleep in their own hut in the evening. Can they start their journey from home in the morning?

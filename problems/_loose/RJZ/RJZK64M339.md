Let $P(x)=a_{k} x^{k}+a_{k-1} x^{k-1}+\cdots+a_{1} x+a_{0}$, where each coefficient $a_{i}(i=0,1, \cdots, k)$ is an integer. Suppose there are four distinct integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}$ such that $P\left(x_{i}\right)(i=1,2,3,4)$ all equal 2. Prove that for any integer $x, P(x)$ cannot equal any of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.

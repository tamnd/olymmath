73 In an $n \times n$ square grid, fill in 1 or $-1$ arbitrarily, where $n$ is an odd number. Below each column, write the product of all numbers in that column, and to the right of each row, write the product of all numbers in that row. Prove: The sum of the $2n$ products written is not equal to zero.

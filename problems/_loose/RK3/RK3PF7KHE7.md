Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies: $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=2, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}(n \geqslant 1)$.
(1) Prove that the infinite points $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}\right),\left(a_{3}, a_{4}\right), \cdots,\left(a_{2 k-1}, a_{2 k}\right), \cdots$ formed by adjacent terms of the sequence all lie on the curve $x^{2}+x y-y^{2}+1=0$;
(2) If $f(x)=x^{n}+x^{n-1}-a_{n} x-a_{n-1}, g(x)=x^{2}-x-1$, prove that $g(x)$ divides $f(x)$.

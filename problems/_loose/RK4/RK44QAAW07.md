Through a convex $n$-gon $P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{n}$, which has an incircle $c$, a line $g$ is drawn that intersects the side $P_{n} P_{1}$ at a point $M$ and a side $P_{k} P_{k+1}(1 \leq k<n)$ at a point $N$.

The line $g$ is drawn such that it bisects both the perimeter and the area of the $n$-gon, i.e., the following conditions (1) and (2) are satisfied:

(1) The lengths of the polygonal chains $M P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{k} N$ and $N P_{k+1} P_{k+2} \ldots P_{n} M$ are equal.

(2) The areas of the polygons $M P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{k} N$ and $N P_{k+1} P_{k+2} \ldots P_{n} M$ are equal. Prove that from these assumptions it always follows:

The line $g$ passes through the center of the circle $c$.

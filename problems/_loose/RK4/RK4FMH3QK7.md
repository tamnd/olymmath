Given that $A B$ is the hypotenuse of the right $\triangle A B C$, take points $B^{\prime}, A^{\prime}$ on the rays $A C, B C$ respectively, such that $A B^{\prime}=B A^{\prime}=A B$. $P, Q$ are two points inside $\triangle A B C$, if the sum of the distances from $P, Q$ to the sides of $\triangle A B C$ are equal, then $P Q / / A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$.

A box $3\times5\times7$ is divided into unit cube cells. In each of the cells, there is a c**ockchafer. At a signal, every c**ockchafer moves through a face of its cell to a neighboring cell.

(a) What is the minimum number of empty cells after the signal?
(b) The same question, assuming that the c**ockchafers move to diagonally adjacent cells (sharing exactly one vertex).

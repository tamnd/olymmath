Day 1: $\underset{\star}{* *}$ Let $AB$ be the diameter of $\odot O$, $M$ a point on this diameter, and chord $PQ \perp AB$. The circle with $AM$ as its diameter is $\odot O_{1}$, and the circle with $BM$ as its diameter is $\odot O_{2}$. $\odot O_{3}$ and $\odot O_{4}$ are both tangent to $\odot O$ and line $PQ$, and also tangent to $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$, respectively. Prove: The radii of $\odot O_{3}$ and $\odot O_{4}$ are equal, and the radii of $\odot O$, $\odot O_{1}$, $\odot O_{2}$, and $\odot O_{3}$ are in proportion.

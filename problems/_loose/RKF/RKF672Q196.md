A convex hexagon $A B C D E F$ inscribed in a circle satisfies $A B=C D=E F$, and the diagonals $A D 、 B E 、 C F$ are concurrent. The intersection of $A D$ and $C E$ is $P$. Prove: $\frac{C P}{P E}=\left(\frac{A C}{C E}\right)^{2}$.

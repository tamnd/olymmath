10 Let $S=\left\{P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{2000}\right\}$ be a set of 2000 points inside a circle of radius 1 such that one of the points is the center of the circle. For $i=1,2, \ldots, 2000$, let $x_{i}$ denote the distance from $P_{i}$ to the nearest point $P_{j} \neq P_{i}$ from $S$. Show that the following applies

$$
x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\ldots+x_{2000}^{2}<9
$$

A group of children, after the municipal stage of the mathematics competition, formed a circle, discussing the solved problems. It turned out that there are exactly 20 future prizewinners and exactly 25 future winners of the municipal stage such that each of them has at least one neighbor who is a participant who did not become either a prizewinner or a winner. Prove that there are two participants standing next to each other who did not become either prizewinners or winners.

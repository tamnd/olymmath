A cylindrical hole $6 \mathrm{~cm}$ long is drilled through a sphere, the axis of the cylinder passing through the center of the sphere. What is the remaining volume?

(It is recalled that the volume of a spherical cap is $\pi h^{2}(R-h / 3)$, where $R$ is the radius of the sphere and $h$ is the height of the cap.)

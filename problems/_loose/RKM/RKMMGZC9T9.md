(Tournoi des villes 1985)

Let $n$ be a natural number.

Consider an $n \times n$ table in which each number from 1 to $n$ is written $n$ times.

Show that there exists a row or a column containing at least $\lceil\sqrt{n}\rceil$ different numbers.

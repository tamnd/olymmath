Let sets $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{N} \subset\{1, \ldots, n\}$ be such that none of them is a subset of any other. Prove Sperner's lemma, which states that

$$
N \leqslant C_{n}^{[n / 2]}
$$

where $[x]$ is the integer part of $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

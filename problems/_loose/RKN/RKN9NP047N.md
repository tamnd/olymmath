Teacher Lehmann asks the 26 students in his class which of the three working groups they are attending. Truthfully, it turns out:

- When asked who is in the Photography working group, exactly 10 students respond.
- When asked who is in the Technology working group, exactly 8 students respond.
- When asked who is in the Informatics working group, exactly 7 students respond.
- Exactly 6 students do not respond to any of these three questions.

On the way home, Uwe says, "Exactly 3 students are in all three working groups."

Michael says, "Exactly 2 students are in exactly two of the working groups."

Jörg says, "Exactly 14 students are in exactly one working group."

Show that all three statements are false!

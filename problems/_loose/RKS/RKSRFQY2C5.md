In the plane, there are two circles with different radii, and they have no common interior points. Their radii are $R, r$, and the distance between their centers is $d$. We draw a secant through their external similarity point, then rotate the figure around the line connecting the centers of the circles. Consider the two frustums of cones described by the chords cut by the secant from the circles. What angle does the secant make with the axis of rotation when the difference in the areas of the lateral surfaces is the greatest?

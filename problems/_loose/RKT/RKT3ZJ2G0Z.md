Define the *inverse* of triangle $ABC$ with respect to a point $O$ in the following way: construct the circumcircle of $ABC$ and construct lines $AO$ , $BO$ , and $CO$ . Let $A'$ be the other intersection of $AO$ and the circumcircle (if $AO$ is tangent, then let $A' = A$ ). Similarly define $B'$ and $C'$ . Then $A'B'C'$ is the inverse of $ABC$ with respect to $O$ . Compute the area of the inverse of the triangle given in the plane by $A(-6, -21)$ , $B(-23, 10)$ , $C(16, 23)$ with respect to $O(1, 3)$ .

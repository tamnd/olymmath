51 If a triangle has each side length greater than 1, it is called a "large" triangle. Given an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with a side length of 5, prove:
(1) From $\triangle ABC$, 100 large triangles can be cut out.
(2) $\triangle ABC$ can be divided into no fewer than 100 large triangles.
(3) $\triangle ABC$ can be dissected into no fewer than 100 large triangles, meaning any two of the triangles either do not intersect, share a common vertex, or share a common side.
(4) For an equilateral triangle with a side length of 3, prove (2) and (3).

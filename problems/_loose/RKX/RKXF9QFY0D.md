Let $A B C D$ be a regular tetrahedron with edge length $a$. The midpoint of edge $A B$ is $M$, and the midpoint of edge $C D$ is $N$.

a) Prove that the line through $M$ and $N$ is perpendicular to both the line $g$ through $A$ and $B$ and the line $h$ through $C$ and $D$!

b) Determine the distance $M N$ between $M$ and $N$!

c) Prove that for any point $X$ on $g$ and any point $Y$ on $h$, the distance $X Y$ between $X$ and $Y$ satisfies the inequality $X Y \geq M N$!

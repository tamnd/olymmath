Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with unequal sides with $\angle BAD < \angle ABC = 90^{\circ}$ and let $X$ be the intersecting point of his diagonals. Let $M$ be the median of the segment $BD$ . The line $AM$ and the line which passes through $X$ and is perpendicular to $BD$ , intersect on point $Y$ . Let Z be a point on the line $BD$ . Show that $ZY = AX$ if and only if $\angle XYZ = \angle BAD$ .

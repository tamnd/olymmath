In a given circle, draw a peripheral angle $CAD$ at will and the angle bisector of this angle $AB$. From an arbitrary point $E$ on the circumference of the circle, draw chords $GE$, $FE$, and $HE$ parallel to the chords $CA$, $BA$, and $DA$, respectively. Show that the sum of the sides of the first peripheral angle $(CA + DA)$ is in the same ratio to the sum of the sides of the second peripheral angle $(GE + HE)$ as the angle bisector of the first $(BA)$ is to the angle bisector of the second $(FE)$.

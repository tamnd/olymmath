An integer is called *autodivi * if it is divisible by the two-digit number formed by its last two digits (tens and units). For example, $78013$ is autodivi as it is divisible by $13$, $8517$ is autodivi since it is divisible by $17$. Find $6$ consecutive integers that are autodivi and that have the digits of the units, tens and hundreds other than $0$.

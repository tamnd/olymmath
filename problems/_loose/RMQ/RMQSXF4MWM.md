In a certain language, there are only two characters $A$ and $B$. Only words in which no more than two identical characters stand next to each other are permissible. Prove that the number $p_{n}$ of all permissible words of length $n$ can be determined using the equations $p_{1}=2, p_{2}=4$ and $p_{k+2}=p_{k+1}+p_{k}$ for every natural number $k$. (J. Zhouf)

Given a family of subgraphs of a graph $G$ (not necessarily distinct), if each edge of $G$ is contained in exactly two of these subgraphs, then this family of subgraphs is called a double cover (double cover) of $G$. The cycle double cover conjecture states: every bridgeless multigraph has a double cover. Prove that the conjecture holds for graphs that contain a 4-flow.

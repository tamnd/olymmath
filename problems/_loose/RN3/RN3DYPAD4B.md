3.9 ** Let point $I$ be the incenter of $\triangle A B C$, and $\odot I$ be tangent to $A B, B C$ at points $X, Y$ respectively. $X I$ intersects $\odot I$ again at point $T$. Extend $C T$ to intersect $A B$ at point $X^{\prime}$. Point $L$ lies on segment $X^{\prime} C$ such that $X^{\prime} L = C T$. Prove that $A B = A C$ if and only if points $A, L, Y$ are collinear.

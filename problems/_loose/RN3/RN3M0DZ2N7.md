If four circles are internally tangent to a fifth circle, the length of the common external tangent between the first and second circle is denoted as $l_{12}$, and the lengths of the common external tangents between other pairs of the first four circles are denoted similarly. The first four circles are arranged in a clockwise order. Try to prove that the four vertices of the convex quadrilateral formed with $l_{12}, l_{23}, l_{34}, l_{41}$ as side lengths and $l_{13}, l_{24}$ as diagonals are concyclic.
(《High School Mathematical Olympiad Problems》1999, Issue 5)

$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text { 10. (20 points) Let } a>b>0, \\
f(x)=2(a+b) \mathrm{e}^{2 x}+2 a b, \\
g(x)=4 \mathrm{e}^{2 x}+a+b .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: The equation $f(x)=\left(\frac{a^{\frac{1}{3}}+b^{\frac{1}{3}}}{2}\right)^{3} g(x)$ with respect to $x$ has a unique solution.

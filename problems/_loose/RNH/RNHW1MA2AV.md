Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, and $S_{A}, S_{B}, S_{C}$ be circles centered at $O$ that are tangent to the sides $BC, CA$, and $AB$ respectively. Prove that the sum of the three angles: between the tangents to $S_{A}$ from point $A$, to $S_{B}$ from point $B$, and to $S_{C}$ from point $C$, is $180^{\circ}$.

Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid that is not a rectangle, with $(A B)$ parallel to $(C D)$ and $A B < C D$. Let $P$ be a point on $[B C]$. The line parallel to $(A P)$ passing through $C$ intersects $[A D]$ at $Q$ and the line parallel to $(D P)$ passing through $B$ intersects $[A D]$ at $Q'$. Show that $Q=Q'$, that is, the points $Q$ and $Q'$ are coincident.

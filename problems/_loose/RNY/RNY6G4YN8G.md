Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive numbers, and $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=1$. Prove that: $\left(a_{1}+\frac{1}{a_{1}}\right)^{2}+\left(a_{2}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}\right)^{2}+\cdots+\left(a_{n}+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right)^{2} \geqslant \frac{\left(n^{2}+1\right)^{2}}{n}$.

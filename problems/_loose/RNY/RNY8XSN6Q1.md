Let $a, b, c$ be a Pythagorean triple, with $c$ being the hypotenuse. Show that the numbers

$$
2(c-a)(c-b), 2(c-a)(c+b), 2(c+a)(c-b), 2(c+a)(c+b)
$$

are all perfect squares. - Prove that the equations

$$
x+y+\sqrt{2 x y}=c, x+y-\sqrt{2 x y}=c
$$

are solvable in integers. Write down the solutions for $c=13$ and $c=50$.

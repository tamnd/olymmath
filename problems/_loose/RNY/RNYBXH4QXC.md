Salvo-control. The artillery system of a missile cruiser fires at a target. If the target is not hit, the system automatically makes a second shot at the same target. A third shot at this target is not made. The cruiser has 10 missiles on board. During exercises, it conducts firings at several targets under identical conditions. The probability of hitting with each single shot is $p$.

a) (from 8th grade, 2 points). If there are only five targets, what is the probability that exactly three unused missiles will remain after firing?

b) (from 9th grade, 3 points). Find the expected number of targets hit if there are nine targets in total.

When Alíz returned home, April Fool and the Hatman were just fiercely quarreling; they couldn't divide the odd number of pastries left from the hospitality in any way. Alíz immediately suggested three ways to divide them. The first one sounded like this: first, the Hatman divides the whole into two parts, each consisting of at least two pastries, then April Fool further divides these into two non-empty parts each. Then, April Fool gets the smallest and the largest of the four resulting parts. The second differed from this in that at the end, April Fool gets the two middle parts, while in the third case, April Fool can choose either of the previous two options, but must give one pastry to the Hatman. After Alíz described the suggestions, she waited in vain for the tempers to calm down, as the argument then continued over which distribution method to choose. Which one did the Hatman recommend, and which one did April Fool?

On the blackboard, there are several $0$s, $1$s, and $2$s. Each step of the operation involves erasing two different numbers on the blackboard and writing down the third kind of number (replacing 0 and 1 with a 2, replacing 1 and 2 with a 0, replacing 0 and 2 with a 1). Try to prove that if after several steps of operation only one number remains on the blackboard, then this number is independent of the order of operations.

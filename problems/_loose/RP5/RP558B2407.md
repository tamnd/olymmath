Prove that

$$
\frac{x}{1-x^{2}}+\frac{x^{2}}{1-x^{4}}+\frac{x^{4}}{1-x^{8}}+\ldots+\frac{x^{2^{n-1}}}{1-x^{2^{n}}}=\frac{1}{1-x} \cdot \frac{x-x^{2 n}}{1-x^{2 n}}
$$

for $|x| \neq 1$.

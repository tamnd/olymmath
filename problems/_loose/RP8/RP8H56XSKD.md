Let $a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h$ be pairwise distinct single-digit natural numbers not equal to zero, and let it be given that:

$$
a+b=10, c+d+e=16, f+g+h=14
$$

Which single-digit natural number (not equal to zero) was not used in these three equations?

Provide a possible solution for $a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h$!

Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of acute $\triangle A B C$. Given $A C<B C, M$ is the midpoint of $A B$, $C C^{\prime}$ is the diameter of circle $\Gamma$, line $C M$ intersects $A C^{\prime}$ and $B C^{\prime}$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. The line through $K$ perpendicular to $A C^{\prime}$ is $l_{1}$, and the line through $L$ perpendicular to $B C^{\prime}$ is $l_{2}$. Prove: The circumcircle of the triangle formed by $l_{1}$, $l_{2}$, and line $A B$ is tangent to circle $\Gamma$.

In the plane, there are two parallel lines, $e_{1}$ and $e_{2}$, and a line $f$ perpendicular to them, which intersects $e_{1}$ at $O_{1}$ and $e_{2}$ at $O_{2}$. Determine the geometric place of those points through which a line can be drawn such that its intersection points with $e_{1}$ and $e_{2}$, denoted by $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ respectively, satisfy:
a) $O_{1} P_{1} \cdot O_{2} P_{2}=1$
b) $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ are on the same side of $f$.

Solve the problem also if in b) "same" is replaced by "opposite".

Let's assume that

a) there is a person who loves the banjo and is not a pearl fisher;

b) every zither enthusiast is a pearl fisher, or at least does not like the banjo;

c) either it is true that whoever is not a pearl fisher is a zither enthusiast, or that whoever is a pearl fisher is not a zither enthusiast.

Does it follow from the above that whoever loves the banjo is not a zither enthusiast?

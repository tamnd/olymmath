Let triangle $ABC$ have an obtuse angle at vertex $B$, let $D$ and $E$ be the midpoints of sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ respectively, $F$ be a point on side $\overline{BC}$ such that $\angle BFE$ is a right angle, and $G$ be a point on segment $\overline{DE}$ such that $\angle BGE$ is a right angle.

Prove that points $A, F$, and $G$ lie on the same line if and only if $2|BF|=|CF|$.

Let $k$ be any natural number. Consider pairs $(a, b)$ of integers for which the quadratic equations

$$
x^{2}-2 a x+b=0, y^{2}+2 a y+b=0
$$

have real roots (not necessarily distinct), which can be denoted as $x_{1,2}$, respectively $y_{1,2}$, in such an order that the equality $x_{1} y_{1}-x_{2} y_{2}=4 k$ holds.

a) For a given $k$, determine the greatest possible value of $b$ among all such pairs $(a, b)$.

b) For $k=2004$, determine the number of all such pairs $(a, b)$.

c) For a given $k$, calculate the sum of the numbers $b$ from all such pairs $(a, b)$, where each number $b$ is counted as many times as it appears in the pairs $(a, b)$.

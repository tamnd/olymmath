A chip is placed in the central cell of a $7 \times 7$ square. Two players take turns moving the chip to an adjacent cell by side. The first player can move the chip in the same direction as the second player's previous move or turn left, while the second player can move the chip in the same direction as the first player's previous move or turn right. The first player starts by moving in any direction. The player who cannot make a move loses. Can the first player ensure a win?

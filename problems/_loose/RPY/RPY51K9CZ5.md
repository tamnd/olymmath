$$
\begin{gathered}
1^{2}+2^{2}+(1 \times 2)^{2}=3^{2} \\
2^{2}+3^{2}+(2 \times 3)^{2}=7^{2} \\
3^{2}+4^{2}+(3 \times 4)^{2}=13^{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

Prove that if $a$ and $b$ are consecutive integers, then the number

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+(a b)^{2}
$$

is a perfect square.

In square $A B C D$, a point $E$ is taken on side $B C$, points $F$ and $G$ are taken on side $C D$ (point $F$ is between $C$ and $G$), and a point $H$ is taken on side $A D$. Given that $C E=C F$ and $D G=D H$, prove that a circle can be circumscribed around the quadrilateral formed by the intersection of angles $H B G$ and $E A F$.

On a plane, a point $M$ and $n-1$ lines $l_{2}$, $l_{3}, \ldots, l_{n}$ are given. Construct an $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$,

a) the midpoint of side $A_{1} A_{2}$ of which coincides with point $M$, and the perpendiculars erected to the other sides at their midpoints coincide with the lines $l_{2}, l_{3}, \ldots, l_{n}$;

b) the angle $A_{1}$ of which has a known value $\alpha$, the bisector of angle $A_{1}$ passes through $M$, and the bisectors of angles $A_{2}, A_{3}, \ldots, A_{n}$ coincide with $l_{2}, l_{3}, \ldots, l_{n}$.

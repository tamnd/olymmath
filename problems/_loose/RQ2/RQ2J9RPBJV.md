For a prime number $p > 3$, define the following irreducible fraction:

$$\frac{m}{n} = \frac{p-1}{2} + \frac{p-2}{3} + \ldots + \frac{2}{p-1} - 1$$

Prove that $m$ is divisible by $p$.

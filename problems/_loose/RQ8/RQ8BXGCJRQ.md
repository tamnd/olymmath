Does there exist a triangle with sides $a, b$, and $c$ such that

$$
a, b, c>2019 \text { and } \lfloor a\rfloor+\lfloor b\rfloor<\lfloor c\rfloor \text { ? }
$$

(For a real number $t$, $\lfloor t\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer not greater than $t$.)

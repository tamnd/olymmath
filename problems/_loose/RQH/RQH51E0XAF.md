Determine all four-digit natural numbers $n$ in the decimal system with the following properties:

1. All digits $a_{i}$ and the cross sum $Q$ of $n$ are prime numbers.
2. It holds that $n=Q \cdot P+2$, where $P$ is the cross product of $n$.

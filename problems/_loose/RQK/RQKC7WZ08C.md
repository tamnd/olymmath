Harald wants to report in the wall newspaper about the active leisure activities of the pioneers Marion, Petra, and Ruth. He knows:

(1) Each of the three girls practices exactly one of the sports: swimming, table tennis, volleyball. Each of these three sports is practiced by one of the three girls.

(2) In her free time, Marion also likes to read adventure books, but the volleyball player does not.

(3) The volleyball player, on the other hand, enjoys mathematics and solved more problems correctly at the last Mathematics Olympiad than Petra.

(4) In the Russian Olympiad, Marion performed better than the table tennis player.

Prove that the distribution of the three sports among the three girls is uniquely determined by the statements (1), (2), (3), (4)! Which girl practices which sport?

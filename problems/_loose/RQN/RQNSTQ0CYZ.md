Boy Seryozha saw two two-headed dragonets, whose heads were tangled. Dragons are either truthful, i.e., both heads always tell the truth, or lying, i.e., both heads always lie. Seryozha decided to help the dragonets untangle their heads. But for this, he needed to know which head belonged to which dragon. He asked the dragonets, and the heads replied:

first: "I am the truthful head";

second: "The third head is my own head";

third: "The second head is not my own head";

fourth: "The third head is lying."

Which heads belong to which dragonets?

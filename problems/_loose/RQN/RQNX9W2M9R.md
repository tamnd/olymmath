Let the sequence of positive integers $m_{1}<\cdots<m_{s}(s \geqslant 2)$ contain no element that can be expressed as the sum of two or more distinct numbers. For any integer $r(1 \leqslant r<s)$, prove:
$$
r m_{r}+m_{s} \geqslant(r+1)(s-1) .
$$

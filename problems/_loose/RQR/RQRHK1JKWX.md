Given a triangle $A B C$. On the rays $B A, C B$ and $A C$ are defined points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively, such that

$$
\overline{B A_{1}}=(1+n) \overline{A B}, \overline{C B_{1}}=(1+n) \overline{C B}, \overline{A C_{1}}=(1+n) \overline{A C}
$$

Determine the ratio of the areas of triangles $A B C$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$.

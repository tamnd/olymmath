Vsevolod placed the white square on the table and is going to cover it with 19 black squares such that the sides of all black squares are parallel to the sides of the white square (black squares can overlap). Yaroslav claims that no matter how Vsevolod completes this task, it will always be possible to remove one black square so that the white square is still fully covered by the black squares. Is Yaroslav right? Justify your answer.

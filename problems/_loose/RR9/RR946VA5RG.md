Suppose that $a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ are integers such that $a_1 +2^ia_2 +3^ia_3 +...+n^ia_n = 0$ for $i = 1,2,...,k -1$, where $k \ge 2$ is a given integer. Prove that $a_1+2^ka_2+3^ka_3+...+n^ka_n$ is divisible by $k!$.

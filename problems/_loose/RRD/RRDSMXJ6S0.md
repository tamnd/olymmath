In a mathematics team competition, 5 teams participate. Each team consists of 3 students, including the team leader. All 5 teams sit at the same round table, so that students from the same team sit together, one next to the other, and the team leader is always between the members of their team. How many different seating arrangements are there around the round table? (Arrangements are considered the same if one can be obtained from the other by rotation. Arrangements are different if there is a pair of people who sit one to the left of the other in one arrangement, but not in the other.)

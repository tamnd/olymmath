$$\sum_{k=1}^{2^{16}}\binom{2k}{k}(3\cdot 2^{14}+1)^k (k-1)^{2^{16}-1}$$is divided by $2^{16}+1$ . (*Note:* It is well-known that $2^{16}+1=65537$ is prime.)

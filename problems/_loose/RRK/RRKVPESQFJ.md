We need to construct a given segment's $\frac{1}{k}$ part, where $k=n^{2}+1$ or $k=n^{2}-1$, and $n$ is an integer greater than 1. For this construction, based on the proportional theorems of right triangles, a simpler (i.e., fewer steps) procedure can be provided, rather than measuring $k$ equal segments on one ray of an angle and then using parallel lines. Let's find such a construction procedure with fewer steps.

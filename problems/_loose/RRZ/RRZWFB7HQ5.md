(Gergonne Point) Let P, Q, R be the points of contact of the incircle with the three sides. Show that AP, BQ, and CR are concurrent. Find the barycentric coordinates of the intersection point.

Let $ \mathrm{P}^{\prime} $ be the point of contact of the excircle opposite to $ A $ with $ BC $, $ Q^{\prime} $ the point of contact of the excircle opposite to $ B $ with $ AC $, and $ R^{\prime} $ the point of contact of the excircle opposite to $ C $ with $ AB $ etc. Show that $ A P^{\prime}, B Q^{\prime} $, and $ C R^{\prime} $ are concurrent. Find the barycentric coordinates of the intersection point.

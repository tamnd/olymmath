Anchurian Checkers ${ }^{1}$ (from 7th grade, 1 point). A tournament of Anchurian Checkers is held in several rounds. If an even number of players participate in a round, they are divided into random pairs. If the number of players is odd, random pairs are selected by lot, and one player remains without a pair and does not participate in the round. A draw cannot occur; the loser in each pair is eliminated from the tournament, while the winners and the player without a pair, if there is one, advance to the next round, which is conducted according to the same rules. This continues until only two players remain, who play the final round, that is, the last game, which determines the winner of the tournament.[^5]

26 participants arrived at the Anchurian Checkers tournament, and all of them play equally well, meaning that in any game played by any two of them, the chances of the opponents are equal. Among the players are Denis and Oleg. Find the probability that they will play against each other.

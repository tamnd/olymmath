The positive integer number $S$ is called a "*line number*". if there is a positive integer $n$ and $2n$ positive integers $a_1$ , $a_2$ ,..., $a_n$ , $b_1$ , $b_2$ ,..., $b_n$ , such that $S = \sum^n_{i=1} a_ib_i$ , $\sum^n_{i=1} (a_i^2-b_1^2)=1$ , and $\sum^n_{i=1} (a_i+b_i)=2023$ , find:
(1) The minimum value of *line numbers*.
(2)The maximum value of *line numbers*.

Vintik and Shpuntik were designing a nanorover. Vintik drew a rectangle $9 \times 11$ cm and marked two hundred points in it - places for rivets. Shpuntik divided the rectangle into square compartments with a side of 1 cm. In doing so, none of Vintik's marks fell on Shpuntik's lines. Prove that there must be a compartment that contains three or more rivets.

Given $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is an increasing geometric sequence, and
$$
a_{1}+a_{2}=6, a_{3}+a_{4}=24 \text {. }
$$

Let $b_{n}=\frac{a_{n}}{\left(a_{n}-1\right)^{2}}$, and the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is $T_{n}$. Prove: For all positive integers $n$, $T_{n}<3$.

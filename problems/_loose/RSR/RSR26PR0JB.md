Consider the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, points $M$ and $N$ being the midpoints of sides $[A B]$ and $[A D]$, respectively, and points $P, Q$ being the symmetric points of $C$ with respect to points $M$ and $N$.

a). Show that $\overrightarrow{B P}=\overrightarrow{D Q}$.

b). If $\overrightarrow{C P}+\overrightarrow{C Q}=3 \overrightarrow{C A}$, show that quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

c). If $E, F$ are the midpoints of segments $[B D]$ and $[P Q]$, respectively, show that points $C, A, E, F$ are collinear or are the vertices of a parallelogram.

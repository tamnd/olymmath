Given that $M$ is the midpoint of the base $BC$ of isosceles $\triangle ABC$, a semicircle is drawn with $M$ as the center and is tangent to the two legs. A tangent line is drawn through any point $D$ on the semicircle, intersecting the two legs at $E$ and $F$. Perpendiculars are then drawn from $E$ and $F$ to the legs, intersecting at $P$. A line $PQ \perp BC$ is drawn from $P$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $Q$. Prove that $AP: PQ = 2AB: BC$.

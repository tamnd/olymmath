Positive numbers $a, b$, and $c$ satisfy the condition $a^{3}+b^{3}+c^{3}=3$. Prove the inequality

$$
\frac{1}{a^{4}+3}+\frac{1}{b^{4}+3}+\frac{1}{c^{4}+3} \geqslant \frac{3}{4}
$$

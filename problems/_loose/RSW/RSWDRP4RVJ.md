Let $P(x)=a_{3} x^{3}+a_{2} x^{2}+a_{1} x+a_{0}$ be a polynomial with rational coefficients $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$. Prove:

If $P(x)$ has a root of the form $x_{0}=b+\sqrt{c}$ with rational numbers $b, c$ such that $\sqrt{c}$ is irrational, then $x_{1}=b-\sqrt{c}$ is also a root of $P(x)$.

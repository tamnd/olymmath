5, 2. Two circles $C$ and $C^{\prime}$ intersect at two points $P$ and $Q$. The line joining the centers intersects $C$ at $R$ and $C^{\prime}$ at $R^{\prime}$. The line joining $P$ and $R^{\prime}$ intersects $C$ at $X \neq P$ and the line joining $P$ and $R$ intersects $C^{\prime}$ at $X^{\prime} \neq P$. If the three points $X, Q, X^{\prime}$ are collinear, we are asked to:

(i) find the angle $\angle X P X^{\prime}$.
(ii) prove that $\left(d+r-r^{\prime}\right)\left(d-r+r^{\prime}\right)=r r^{\prime}$, where $d$ is the distance between the centers of the circles and $r$ and $r^{\prime}$ their radii.

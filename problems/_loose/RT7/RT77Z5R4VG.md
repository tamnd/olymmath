Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral such that $\widehat{C B A}=90^{\circ}$, the lines (AC) and (BD) are perpendicular, and $B C=C D$ and $A B=A D$. Let $E$ and $F$ be two points on the lines (AB) and $(A D)$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be two points on the segment $[E F]$ such that $\frac{A E}{E P}=\frac{A F}{F Q}$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the orthogonal projections of points $B$ and $D$ on the lines (CP) and (CQ), respectively. Show that the points $P, Q, Y$ and $X$ are concyclic.

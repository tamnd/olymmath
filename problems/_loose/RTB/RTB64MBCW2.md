Let be given a fixed circle $(O, R)$ and a fixed point $A$ on this circle. Suppose that $B, C$ are two arbitrary points on $(O)$ such that $\angle BAC = \alpha$ with $\alpha$ is a constant.
On rays $BA, CA$ respectively the points $E, F$ be taken in such that $BE = CF = BC$.
a) Let $r$ be the radius of $(AEF)$. Prove that $r \ge R \frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi-3\alpha}{4}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi+\alpha}{4}\right)}$.
b) Let $P$ is a midpoint of the arc $AC$ which not contains $B$ and $Q$ is the midpoint of the arc $AB$ which not contains $C$. The line pass through $O$ and
perpendiculars to *EF* cut two lines *AB*,*AC* in order at *M*,*N*. Prove that the intersection of *PM*,*QN* is always belongs to the fixed line.

Consider the following operation. Given a positive integer $n$ , if $n$ is a multiple of $3$ , then you replace $n$ by $\dfrac{n}3$ . If $n$ is not a multiple of $3$ , then you replace $n$ by $n + 10$ . Then continue this process. For example, beginning with $n = 4$ , this procedure gives $4 \to 14 \to 24 \to 8 \to 18 \to 6 \to 2 \to 12 \to \cdots$ . Suppose you start with $n = 100$ . What value results if you perform this operation exactly $100$ times? $\textbf{(A) }10 \textbf{(B) }20 \textbf{(C) }30 \textbf{(D) }40 \textbf{(E) }50$

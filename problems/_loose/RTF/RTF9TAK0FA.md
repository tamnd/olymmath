Let $O, I$ and $H$ be respectively the circumcenter, incenter and orthocenter for an acute nonequilateral $\triangle A B C$. Prove
that if the circumcircle of $\triangle O I H$ passes through one of the vertices of $\triangle A B C$ then it passes through another vertex of $\triangle A B C$.

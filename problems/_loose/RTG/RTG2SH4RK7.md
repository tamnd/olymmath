Let $S$ be the vertex of a regular pyramid whose base is a regular $2 n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{2 n}(n \geqslant 2), \Omega$ is a sphere passing through point $S$. Suppose $\Omega$ intersects the edges $S A_{i}$ at points $B_{i}, i=1,2, \cdots, 2 n$. Prove that: $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|S B_{2 i-1}\right|=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|S B_{2 i}\right|$.

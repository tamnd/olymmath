Given a trapezoid $A B C D$, in which the lateral side $C D$ is perpendicular to the bases $A D$ and $B C$. A circle with diameter $A B$ intersects $A D$ at point $P$ ( $P$ is different from $A$ ). The tangent to the circle at point $P$ intersects $C D$ at point $M$. From $M$, a second tangent to the circle is drawn, touching it at point $Q$. Prove that the line $B Q$ bisects $C D$.

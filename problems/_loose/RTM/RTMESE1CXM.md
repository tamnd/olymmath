Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $D, E$, and $F$ the points of tangency of the incircle of triangle $ABC$ with the sides $BC, CA$, and $AB$, respectively. Let $P$ be a point such that $PF = BF$ and $\widehat{BFP} = \widehat{BAC}$, and assume that points $P$ and $C$ are in the same half-plane delimited by the line $(AB)$. Let $Q$ be a point such that $QE = CE$ and $\widehat{BFP} = \widehat{QEC} = \widehat{BAC}$, and assume that points $B$ and $Q$ are in the same half-plane delimited by the line $(AC)$. Show that the points $P, D$, and $Q$ are collinear.

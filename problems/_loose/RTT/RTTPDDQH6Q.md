Let ABC be a triangle and $\Omega$ its circumcircle. Let $X$ be the point of intersection of the tangents to $\Omega$ at $B$ and $C$. Let $\varphi$ be the angle $\widehat{\mathrm{BAX}}$ and $\mu$ be the angle $\widehat{\mathrm{XAC}}$. Let $Y$ be the point on the line $(A X)$ such that $\widehat{A C Y}=\varphi$. Show that $\widehat{A B Y}=\mu$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that, denoting $H$ as the foot of the altitude from $C$, we have $AH=3 \cdot HB$. Furthermore:

- $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$;

- $N$ is the midpoint of $AC$;

- $P$ is the point on the side opposite to $B$ with respect to the line $AC$ such that $NP=NC$ and $PC=CB$.

Prove that $\widehat{APM}=\widehat{PBA}$.

Solve the following system of equations.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}=z \\
2 x+2 y+p=0 \\
z^{4}+p z^{2}+q=0
\end{gathered}
$$

Under what conditions do the real values of $x, y$, and $z$ satisfy the system of equations?

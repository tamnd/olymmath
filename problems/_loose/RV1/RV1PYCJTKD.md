On a billiard table shaped as a convex polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, there are two billiard balls $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$. In what direction should ball $M_{1}$ be hit so that it hits ball $M_{2}$ after sequentially colliding with the sides $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \cdots, A_{n} A_{1}$? (The reflection rule for billiards is that the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection.)

A triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle. Let $A_1$ be the point diametrically opposed to $A$ , $A_0$ be the midpoint of the side $BC$ and $A_2$ be the point symmetric to $A_1$ with respect to $A_0$ ; the points $B_2$ and $C_2$ are defined in a similar way starting from $B$ and $C$ . Prove that the three points $A_2$ , $B_2$ and $C_2$ coincide.

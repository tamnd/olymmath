Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with $B C>A C$. The central perpendicular of the side $A B$ intersects the line $B C$ in $X$ and the line $A C$ in $Y$. Let $P$ be the projection of $X$ onto $A C$ and let $Q$ be the projection of $Y$ onto $B C$. Prove that the line $P Q$ intersects the line $A B$ at its center.

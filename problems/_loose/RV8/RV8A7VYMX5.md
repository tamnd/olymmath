Inside triangle $ABC$, a point $P$ is taken. Let $d_{a}, d_{b}$, and $d_{c}$ be the distances from point $P$ to the sides of the triangle, and $R_{a}, R_{b}$, and $R_{c}$ be the distances from it to the vertices. Prove that

$$
3\left(d_{a}^{2}+d_{b}^{2}+d_{c}^{2}\right) \geqslant\left(R_{a} \sin A\right)^{2}+\left(R_{b} \sin B\right)^{2}+\left(R_{c} \sin C\right)^{2}
$$

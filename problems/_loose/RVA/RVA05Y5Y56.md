Let $a_1,a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be real numbers such that $a_1<a_2< \ldots < a_n.$ a) Prove that for any positive integer $n,$ there exist real numbers $A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_n$ such that $$ \prod\limits_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{x+a_k}=\sum\limits_{k=1}^n \frac{A_k}{x+a_k}, ~\forall~ x \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-a_1,-a_2,\ldots,-a_n\}. $$ b) Prove that for any integer $n \ge 2,$ the signs of the terms in sequence $A_1,~ A_1+A_2,~ \ldots,~ A_1+A_2+\ldots+A_n$ alternate.

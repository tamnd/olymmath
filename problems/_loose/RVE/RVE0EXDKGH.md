An ordered pair $(k,n)$ of positive integers is *good* if there exists an ordered quadruple $(a,b,c,d)$ of positive integers such that $a^3+b^k=c^3+d^k$ and $abcd=n$. Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $(2022,n)$ is not good but $(2023,n)$ is good.

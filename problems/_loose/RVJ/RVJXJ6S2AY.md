Let $x_1,x_2,... ,x_{2014}$ be real numbers different from $1$ , such that $x_1 + x_2 +...+x_{2014} = 1$

and $\frac{x_1}{1-x_1}+\frac{x_2}{1-x_2}+...+\frac{x_{2014}}{1-x_{2014}}=1$ What is the value of $\frac{x^2_1}{1-x_1}+\frac{x^2_2}{1-x_2}+...+\frac{x^2_{2014}}{1-x_{2014}}$ ?

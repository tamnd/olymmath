Three brave warriors fight against a snake demon. Warrior A can cut off half of the snake demon's heads plus one with each attack, Warrior B can cut off a third of the snake demon's heads plus two with each attack, and Warrior C can cut off a quarter of the snake demon's heads plus three with each attack. The three warriors take turns attacking in the order they deem necessary. If any of the warriors cannot cut off an integer number of the snake demon's heads, the snake demon will devour all three warriors. Question: Can the three warriors cut off all the heads of a snake demon with $20^{20}$ heads?

What is the radius of the ball (unit: $\mathrm{m}$, assuming the sunlight is parallel, and the meter stick is a line segment)?
A. $\frac{5}{2}$
B. $9-4 \sqrt{5}$
C. $8 \sqrt{10}-23$
D. $10 \sqrt{5}-20$

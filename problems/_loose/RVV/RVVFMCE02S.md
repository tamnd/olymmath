Given $a_{0}=\sin x, a_{n}=(-1)^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]} \sqrt{1-a_{n-1}^{2}}$, where $n \in$ $N$, and $\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]$ is the integer part of $\frac{n}{2}$. Then $a_{1989}$ equals
(A) $\sin x$.
(B) $-\cos x$.
(C) $-\sin x$.
(D) $\cos x$.
(China Sichuan Province High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

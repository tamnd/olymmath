suppose that $0\le p \le 1$ and we have a wooden square with side length $1$. in the first step we cut this square into $4$ smaller squares with side length $\frac{1}{2}$ and leave each square with probability $p$ or take it with probability $1-p$. in the next step we cut every remaining square from the previous step to $4$ smaller squares (as above) and take them with probability $1-p$. it's obvios that at the end what remains is a subset of the first square.
**a)** show that there exists a number $0<p_0<1$ such that for $p>p_0$ the probability that the remainig set is not empty is positive and for $p<p_0$ this probability is zero.
**b)** show that for every $p\neq 1$ with probability $1$, the remainig set has size zero.
**c)** for this statement that the right side of the square is connected to the left side of the square with a path, write anything that you can.

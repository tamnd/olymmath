Let $x$ and $y$ be two real numbers. We denote by $x_{n}$ and $y_{n}$ the $n^{\text{th}}$ digits after the decimal point of $x$ and $y$, respectively. Suppose that $y_{n}=x_{3^{n}}$ for all $n \geqslant 1$ and that $x$ is a rational number. Prove that $y$ is a rational number.

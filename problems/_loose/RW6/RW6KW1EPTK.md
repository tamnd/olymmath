$P, A, B, C, D$ are 5 distinct points in space, such that
$$
\angle A P B=\angle B P C=\angle C P D=\angle D P A=\theta,
$$

where $\theta$ is a given acute angle. Find the maximum and minimum values of $\angle A P C+\angle B P D$.

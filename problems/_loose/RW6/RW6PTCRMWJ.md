Prove that if the number of sides of a convex polygon $ M $ is not less than seven, then it is impossible to tile the plane with polygons of this type.

Does this statement remain true if we do not require the convexity of the polygon $ M $?

Besides triangles, convex quadrilaterals, and convex centrally symmetric hexagons, there are several types of convex pentagons and hexagons that can also tile the plane. However, listing all possible types and proving that there are no others is quite complex.

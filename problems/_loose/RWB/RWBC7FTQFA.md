Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $t$ be the tangent at $A$ to the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. We denote by $A'$ the point on $t$ such that the line $(BC)$ intersects the segment $\left[A A^{\prime}\right]$ at its midpoint. We denote by $C'$ the reflection of $C$ with respect to $t$. Show that the points $B, A, C'$ and $A'$ are concyclic.

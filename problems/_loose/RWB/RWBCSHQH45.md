This time you find yourself on an island of knights and liars. Every inhabitant of this island is either a knight or a liar. You fall in love with one of the beautiful islanders, a girl named Elizabeth, and want to marry her. But you would like to know who she is (naturally, you wouldn't want to marry a liar). If you were allowed to ask her one question, it would be very simple. But there is an ancient taboo on the island that forbids a man from speaking to any islander until she becomes his wife. Fortunately, Elizabeth has a brother. He, like all islanders, is either a knight or a liar (the brother and sister are not necessarily of the same type: one can be a knight and the other a liar). You are allowed to ask the brother one question, to which he can answer either "yes" or "no."

Come up with a question such that, upon hearing the answer, you can be sure who Elizabeth is: a knight or a liar. What question would you ask?

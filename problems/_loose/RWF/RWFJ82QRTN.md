(China 2017 TSTST 3 Day 2 P3) Let $n$ be an odd integer. We color the cells of an $n \times n$ grid in red or blue. Suppose that the cells of a given color form a path, that is, we can number the cells of color $C_{1}, \ldots, C_{m}$ such that $C_{i}$ and $C_{j}$ share a side if and only if $|i-j|=1$. Show that the center of the grid is an endpoint of one of the two paths.

In triangle $P Q R$, the midpoint of side $P Q$ is $A$, the midpoint of $Q R$ is $B$, and the midpoint of $R P$ is $C$. We enlarge triangle $A B C$ from its centroid $S$ by a factor of $k$, where $1<k<4$. The sides of the enlarged triangle intersect the sides of triangle $P Q R$ at points $D_{1} D_{2}, E_{1} E_{2}$, and $F_{1} F_{2}$, respectively. For what value of $k$ will the area of the hexagon $D_{1} D_{2} \ldots F_{1} F_{2}$ be twice the area of triangle $A B C$?

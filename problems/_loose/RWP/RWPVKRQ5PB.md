Let $a, b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a+b+c=1$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1+9 a^{2}}{1+2 a+2 b^{2}+2 c^{2}}+\frac{1+9 b^{2}}{1+2 b+2 c^{2}+2 a^{2}}+\frac{1+9 c^{2}}{1+2 c+2 a^{2}+2 b^{2}}<4
$$

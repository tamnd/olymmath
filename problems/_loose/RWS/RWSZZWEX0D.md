With the right-angle side $AB$ (with $B$ as the right-angle vertex) as the diameter, draw a circle intersecting the hypotenuse at $D$. Draw a tangent line through $D$ intersecting the other right-angle side at $E$. Then the line connecting $E$ and the center of the circle must be parallel to the hypotenuse. Try to prove this.

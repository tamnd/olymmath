Let $M_{1}, M_{2}, M_{3}, \ldots, M_{n}$ be collinear points in this order, such that:

$$
M_{1} M_{2}=9 ; M_{2} M_{3}=17, M_{3} M_{4}=33, M_{4} M_{5}=65, \ldots, M_{n-1} M_{n}
$$

a) Find the length of the segment $\left[M_{8} M_{9}\right]$.

b) Find $n \in \mathbb{N}$ if $M_{1} M_{n}=8194$.

c) Find the length of the segment $M_{2} A$ where $A$ is the midpoint of the segment $\left[M_{7} M_{9}\right]$.

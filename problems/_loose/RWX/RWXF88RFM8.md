On a straight road running from north to south, there stands a cart driven by a Swan. Exactly at midnight, a Crab and a Pike chose natural numbers $m>n$. Every $n$ minutes (i.e., after $n, 2 n, 3 n \ldots$ minutes past midnight), the Pike commands "South!", and every $m$ minutes (after $m, 2 m, 3 m \ldots$ minutes past midnight), the Crab commands "North!". Upon hearing any command, the Swan immediately starts (or continues) pulling the cart in the indicated direction at a speed of 1 m/min. Before the first command, the cart was stationary. After $m n$ minutes past midnight, the Crab and the Pike gave the Swan two different commands for the first time, and the exhausted Swan stopped the cart. At what distance from the starting point did it end up at this moment?

Positive numbers $a, b, c$ are such that the product of any two of them is greater than 1. Prove the inequality:

$$
\frac{a+b+1}{a^{2}+b^{2}+1}+\frac{b+c+1}{b^{2}+c^{2}+1}+\frac{a+c+1}{a^{2}+c^{2}+1}<\frac{2}{a+1}+\frac{2}{b+1}+\frac{2}{c+1}
$$

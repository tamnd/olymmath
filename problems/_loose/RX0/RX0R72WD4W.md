$17 \cdot 116 P$ is a point inside an equilateral triangle of height $h$. Let the distances from $P$ to each side be $x, y, z$. If the three segments of lengths $x, y, z$ can form a triangle, then the conditions that $x, y, z$ must satisfy are
(A) $x<h, y<h, z<h$.
(B) $x<\frac{h}{2}, y<\frac{h}{2}, z<\frac{h}{2}$.
(C) $x \leqslant \frac{h}{2}, y \leqslant \frac{h}{2}, z \leqslant \frac{h}{2}$.
(D) $x<\frac{h}{3}, y<\frac{h}{3}, z<\frac{h}{3}$.
(E) $x \leqslant \frac{h}{3}, y \leqslant \frac{h}{3}, z \leqslant \frac{h}{3}$.
(Jilin Province, China, Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1986)

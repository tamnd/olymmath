Given $\triangle A B C$ and circle $\Gamma$ satisfy the following conditions:
(1) Circle $\Gamma$ passes through points $A, B$ and is tangent to $A C$;
(2) The tangent line to circle $\Gamma$ at point $B$ intersects $A C$ at point $X$ (not coinciding with point $C$);
(3) The circumcircle $\Gamma_{1}$ of $\triangle B X C$ intersects circle $\Gamma$ at point $Q$ (not coinciding with point $B$);
(4) The tangent line to circle $\Gamma_{1}$ at point $X$ intersects $A B$ at point $Y$.
Prove: The circumcircle of $\triangle B Q Y$ is tangent to $X Y$.

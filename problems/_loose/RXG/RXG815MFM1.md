On a circle $k$, let $A_{1}, A_{2}$, and $P$ be three pairwise distinct points; the segment $A_{1} A_{2}$ is not a diameter of $k$.

For $i=1 ; 2$, let $k_{i}$ be a circle that touches $k$ externally at $A_{i}$, and let $B_{i}$ be the point of intersection of the circle $k_{i}$ with the line through $P$ and $A_{i}$, different from $A_{i}$. The center of $k_{i}$ is $M_{i}$.

Prove that under these conditions, the lines $a$, $b$, and $m$ through $A_{1}, A_{2}$, $B_{1}, B_{2}$, and $M_{1}, M_{2}$, respectively, either all intersect at exactly one point or are all parallel to each other.

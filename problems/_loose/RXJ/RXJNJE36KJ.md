The teams $A, B, C, D$, and $E$ competed in a football tournament with the following rules:

- the winner of a match earns 3 points and the loser earns nothing;
- in case of a draw, each team earns 1 point;
- each team plays exactly one match against each of the others.

The champion of the tournament was team $A$, followed in the standings by $B, C, D$, and $E$, in that order. Additionally:

- team $A$ did not draw any matches;
- team $B$ did not lose any matches;
- all teams finished the tournament with different point totals.

a) Did team $A$ win, lose, or draw its match against team $B$? Why?

b) How many points did team $A$ finish the tournament with? Why?

c) Explain why team $B$ obtained an even number of points in this tournament.

d) In the table, each column represents a match. Knowing that there were exactly 5 draws in this tournament, draw a circle around the name of the winning team or around the $\times$, in case of a draw, in each column of the table.

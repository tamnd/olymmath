On the table lay a pile of silver coins. With each action, either one gold coin was added and the number of silver coins was recorded on the first sheet, or one silver coin was removed and
the number of gold coins was recorded on the second sheet. In the end, only gold coins were left on the table. Prove that at this moment the sum of all the numbers on the first sheet was equal to the sum of all the numbers on the second.

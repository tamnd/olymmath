Around the edge of a round table, there are $2 n$ empty glasses ( $n \geqslant 2$ ). Petya and Vasya take turns (starting with Petya) filling the empty glasses with orange and apple juice. In one move, each player chooses two empty glasses and fills them with the same type of juice (of their choice). The game ends when all glasses are filled. Petya wants to achieve a situation where, at the end of the game, there is a glass such that the neighboring glasses contain juice of the opposite type. For which $n$ can Petya achieve his goal regardless of Vasya's actions?

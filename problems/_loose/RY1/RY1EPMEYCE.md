Let $E$ be a set of prime numbers with two elements such that if $p_{1}, \cdots, p_{n} \in$ $E$ (the $p_{i}$ being distinct), then all prime factors of $p_{1} \times \cdots \times p_{n}-1$ are in $E$. Show that $E$ is the set of all prime numbers.

For triangle MBN, the second circle is inscribed in it, therefore, the center of the third (inscribed in $\mathrm{ABC}$) circle coincides with point K, and the distance sought in the problem is

$K O=B O-B K=B O\left(1-\cos ^{2} \angle O B E\right)=B O \sin ^{2} \angle O B E=4 \cdot \frac{1-\cos 45^{\circ}}{2}=2-\sqrt{2}$.

$1^{\circ}$

$$
r^{2}=\frac{s_{1} s_{2} s_{3}}{s}, r_{1}^{2}=\frac{s s_{2} s_{3}}{s_{1}}, \text { etc. }
$$

$2^{\circ}$

$$
\frac{1}{r_{1}}+\frac{1}{r_{2}}+\frac{1}{r_{3}}=\frac{1}{r}
$$

$3^{\circ}$

$$
r r_{1} r_{2} r_{3}=s s_{1} s_{2} s_{3}
$$

${ }^{1}$ For these problems, we use the following notations: $s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}, s_{1}=s-a, s_{2}=s-b, s_{3}=s-c$. $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle of the triangle, $O$ is its center; $r$ is the radius of the incircle of the triangle, $O^{\prime}$ is the center of this circle; $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ are the radii of the circles that are tangent to the sides of the triangle from the outside; $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ are the centers of these circles. $O O^{\prime}=d, O O_{1}=d_{1}, O O_{2}=d_{2}, O O_{3}=d_{3}$. The incircle touches the sides of the triangle at points $K_{1}, K_{2}, K_{3}$; the circles with radii $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ touch the sides of the triangle at points $K_{1}^{\prime}, K_{1}^{\prime \prime}, K_{1}^{\prime \prime \prime}, K_{2}^{\prime}, K_{2}^{\prime \prime}, K_{2}^{\prime \prime \prime}, K_{3}^{\prime}, K_{3}^{\prime \prime}, K_{3}^{\prime \prime \prime}$.

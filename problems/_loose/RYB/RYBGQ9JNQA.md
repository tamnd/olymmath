On a piece of paper with a Cartesian coordinate system, draw a circle with center at point $A(-1,0)$ and radius $2 \sqrt{2}$, and a fixed point $B(1,0)$. Fold the paper so that a point $P$ on the circumference of the circle exactly coincides with point $B$, and connect $A P$ to intersect the fold line at point $C$. Repeatedly folding in this manner yields the set of points $C$.
(1) Find the equation of the curve $E$ corresponding to the set of points $C$;
(2) If points $S, T, M, N$ are all on the curve $E$, and $\overrightarrow{S B}$ is collinear with $\overrightarrow{B T}$, $\overrightarrow{M B}$ is collinear with $\overrightarrow{B N}$, and $\overrightarrow{S B} \cdot \overrightarrow{M B}=0$, find the minimum and maximum values of the area of quadrilateral $S M T N$.

a) Show that if $a, b \in [1, \infty)$, then $\frac{1}{1+a^{2}}+\frac{1}{1+b^{2}} \geqslant \frac{2}{1+a b}$.

b) Let $x, y, z \in [0, \infty)$ with the property that $2 x+2 y+2 z+x y+y z+z x=9$. Show that:

$$
\frac{1}{x^{2}+2 x+2}+\frac{1}{y^{2}+2 y+2}+\frac{1}{z^{2}+2 z+2} \geqslant \frac{3}{5}
$$

(PRC, 76; Jury, Sweden, 76). Let the polynomial $P(x)$ with real coefficients satisfy the inequality $P(x)>0$ for all values of $x>0$. Prove that there exist polynomials $Q(x)$ and $R(x)$ with non-negative coefficients, for which the identity

$$
P(x) \equiv Q(x) / R(x)
$$

holds.

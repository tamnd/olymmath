At the beginning of a game, André had 7 pieces of paper. In the first round, he took some of these pieces and cut each of them into 7 pieces, which were mixed with the pieces of paper that were not cut in this round. In the second round, he again took some pieces and cut each of them into 7 pieces that were mixed with the other papers. Continuing in this way, at the end of some round, could André have exactly 2009 pieces of paper?

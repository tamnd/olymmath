Given that there are $2 n\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$ points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{2 n}$ on the upper half of the unit circle in the upper half-plane, and $O$ is the origin. The vector $v=\sum_{k=1}^{2 n} \overrightarrow{O A_{k}}$ has a projection length on the $x$-axis that is an odd number. Prove: $\boldsymbol{v}$ has a projection length on the $y$-axis of at least 1.

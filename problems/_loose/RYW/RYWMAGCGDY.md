Is the following game known: two players take turns drawing symbols in the squares of a large, grid-lined piece of paper. One player always draws an $\times$, the other always draws a circle. The player who first gets 4 of their symbols in a row, either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, wins the game.

Prove that the first player can win the game by the 6th move if they play well.

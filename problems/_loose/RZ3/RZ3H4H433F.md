Let $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $n \geqslant 2$, $n$ distinct sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ satisfy $\left|A_{i}\right|=n(i=1$, $2, \cdots, n)$, and for any $k(2 \leqslant k \leqslant n-1)$ of the sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$, we have
$$
\left|A_{i_{1}} \cap A_{i_{2}} \cap \cdots \cap A_{i_{k}}\right| \geqslant n+1-k .
$$

Determine the maximum and minimum values of $S=\left|A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{n}\right|$.

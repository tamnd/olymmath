An integer sequence $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ has $a_1=0$ , $a_n\leq 10$ and $a_{i+1}-a_i\geq 2$ for $1\leq i<n$ . How many possibilities are there for this sequence? The sequence may be of any length.

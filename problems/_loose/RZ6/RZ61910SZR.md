Given three circles $S_{1}, S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$. Construct a circle $S$,

a) perpendicular to $\dot{S_{1}}, S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$;

b) intersecting $S_{1}, S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$ at diametrically opposite points

c) such that $S_{1}, S_{2}$ and $S_{3}$ intersect $S$ at diametrically opposite points.

Let's draw Thales' circles over the sides $BC$ and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$, and a circle of arbitrary radius around the midpoint $F$ of side $AC$, such that this circle intersects both Thales' circles at two points each.

Prove that from these four intersection points, we can select two pairs of points such that the lines determined by them pass through vertex $B$ of the triangle.

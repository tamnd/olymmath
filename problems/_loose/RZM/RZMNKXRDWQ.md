Let $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=0, a_{3}=1$ and

$$
a_{n+3}=\frac{\left(n^{2}+n+1\right)(n+1)}{n} a_{n+2}+\left(n^{2}+n+1\right) a_{n+1}-\frac{n+1}{n} a_{n}
$$

for $n \geqslant 1$. Prove that $a_{n}$ is a square of an integer for any $n \geqslant 1$.

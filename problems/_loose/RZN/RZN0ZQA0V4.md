In $\triangle A B C$, $B D=D C$. There is a piece of cheese on side $A C$, located at the point closest to point $C$ among the four equal segments. On $A D$, there are three mirrors $W_{1}, W_{2}, W_{3}$, which divide $A D$ into four equal segments. A very suspicious mouse is crawling on $A B$ (from $A$ to $B$), with $A B=400$ meters. The mouse can observe the cheese when the mouse, a certain mirror, and the cheese are aligned. Due to its suspicion, the mouse hopes to see the cheese multiple times to ensure that the cheese has not been eaten by other mice before it reaches it. Therefore, in the first minute, it crawls forward 80 meters, in the second minute, it retreats 20 meters, in the third minute, it crawls forward 80 meters, in the fourth minute, it retreats 20 meters, and so on. ... When the mouse reaches point $B$, it directly rushes along $B C$ to eat the cheese. Question: How many times can the mouse see the cheese on segment $A B$?

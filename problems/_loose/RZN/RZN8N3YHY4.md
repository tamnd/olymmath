(1) If the $k$-order constant coefficient linear recursive sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ has $k$ distinct characteristic roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{k}$, and there exist $T_{j} \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}(j=1,2, \cdots, k)$, such that $x_{j}^{T_{j}}=1$. Prove: $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a purely periodic sequence.
(2) Let $m$ be a given positive integer, and the sequence $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
y_{n}+y_{n+2 m}=2 y_{n+m} \cos \frac{2 \pi}{7}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) \text {. }
$$

Prove: $7 m$ is a period of the sequence $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$.

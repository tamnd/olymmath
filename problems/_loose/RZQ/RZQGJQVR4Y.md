Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree at most $2018$ such that $P(i)=\binom{2018}i$ for all integer $i$ such that $0\le i\le 2018$ . Find the largest nonnegative integer $n$ such that $2^n\mid P(2020)$ .

Prove that if $\alpha$ and $\beta$ satisfy the inequalities $0 \leqslant \alpha \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}, 0 \leqslant \beta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\operatorname{tg} \alpha=5, \operatorname{ctg} \beta=\frac{2}{3}$, then $\alpha+\beta=\frac{3 \pi}{4}$.

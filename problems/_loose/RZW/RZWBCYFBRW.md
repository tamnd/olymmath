(Finland, 80). Perpendiculars drawn through the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $A C$ of triangle $A B C$ intersect the line $B C$ at points $X$ and $Y$ respectively. Prove that the equality $B C = X Y$ : a) is satisfied if

$$
\operatorname{tg} \angle B \cdot \operatorname{tg} \angle C = 3
$$

b) can also be satisfied under the condition

$$
\operatorname{tg} \angle B \cdot \operatorname{tg} \angle C \neq 3
$$

find the set $M \subset \mathbf{R}$, for which the specified equality is equivalent to the condition

$$
\operatorname{tg} \angle B \cdot \operatorname{tg} \angle C \in M
$$

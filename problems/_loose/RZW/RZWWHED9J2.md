6・165 Let $n$ be a given natural number, find all continuous functions $f(x)$ such that
$$
\begin{array}{l}
(0) f(x)+(1) f\left(x^{2}\right)+(2) f\left(x^{2^{2}}\right)+\cdots+(n-1) f\left(x^{2^{n-1}}\right)+ \\
(n) f\left(x^{2^{n}}\right)=0 \text {. }
\end{array}
$$

Given a circle $\mathrm{c}$ and two fixed points $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}$ on it. $\mathrm{M}$ is another point on $\mathrm{c}$, and $\mathrm{K}$ is the midpoint of BM. P is the foot of the perpendicular from $\mathrm{K}$ to AM.

(a) prove that $\mathrm{KP}$ passes through a fixed point (as $\mathrm{M}$ varies);

(b) find the locus of $\mathrm{P}$.

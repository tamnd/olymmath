There are 5 balls in the box that are identical except for their color, including 1 red ball, 1 black ball, and 3 yellow balls. Huanhuan takes one ball from the box at a time without seeing the color. If a red or black ball is drawn, the game ends; if a yellow ball is drawn, the game continues (the drawn balls are not returned). The probability that Huanhuan draws a red ball when the game ends is ( ).
A. $\frac{1}{5}$
B. $\frac{1}{4}$
C. $\frac{1}{3}$
D. $\frac{1}{2}$
E. $\frac{2}{3}$

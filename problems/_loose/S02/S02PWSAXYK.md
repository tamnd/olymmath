In the Cartesian coordinate plane, points with both integer coordinates are called "lattice points," and polygons with all vertices as lattice points are called "lattice polygons." Let $\Gamma$ be a convex lattice polygon. Prove: $\Gamma$ can be placed inside another convex lattice polygon $\Gamma^{\prime}$, such that all vertices of $\Gamma$ lie on the boundary of $\Gamma^{\prime}$, and $\Gamma^{\prime}$ has exactly one vertex that is not a vertex of $\Gamma$.

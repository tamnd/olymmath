9 - 156 Let real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$ be such that the sum of any two is greater than the third. Prove:
$$
\frac{2}{3}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i}^{2}\right)>\sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i}^{3}+x_{1} x_{2} x_{3} .
$$

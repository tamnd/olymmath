For delivering the mail, the postman uses a bicycle. From the post office to location $A$ is $48 \mathrm{~km}$. The first $10 \mathrm{~km}$ of the route is flat, the next $10 \mathrm{~km}$ is uphill, then $6 \mathrm{~km}$ is flat again, and the remaining part of the route is downhill, inclined at the same angle as the uphill section. On the flat sections, the postman travels at $8 \mathrm{~km}$ per hour, and on the uphill section, he travels at $3 \mathrm{~km}$ per hour. At what speed does the postman ride downhill if it takes him $\frac{14}{5}$ hours longer to return from location $A$ to the post office than it takes to travel from the post office to location $A$?

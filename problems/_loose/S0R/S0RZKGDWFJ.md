Find all values of the parameter $a$, for each of which there exists a number $b$ such that the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=|y+a|+\frac{4}{a} \\
x^{2}+y^{2}+24+b(2 y+b)=10 x
\end{array}\right.
$$

has at least one solution $(x ; y)$.

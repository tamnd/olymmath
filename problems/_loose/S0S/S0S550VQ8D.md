If n is a non-zero natural number, then the number:

$\mathrm{A}=\sqrt{1}+\sqrt{1+3}+\sqrt{1+3+5}+\sqrt{1+3+5+7}+\ldots .+\sqrt{1+3+5+\ldots+2 n-1}$

a) Show that the number $\sqrt{8 A+1}$ is rational, for any $\mathrm{n} \in \mathbb{N}-\{0\}$.

b) Show that the number $\sqrt{2 A+1}$ is irrational, for any $\mathrm{n} \in \mathbb{N}-\{0\}$.

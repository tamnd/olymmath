A rectangle of size $7 \times 9$ is divided into squares of size $1 \times 1$. The central square is shaded. Prove that it is impossible to draw a broken line through the centers of all unshaded squares such that each segment connects the centers of adjacent squares by side without passing through any of them more than once.

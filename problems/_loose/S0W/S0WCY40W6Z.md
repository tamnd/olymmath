Draw a tangent line from the origin $O(0,0)$ to the hyperbola $C: f(x)=$ $\frac{1}{x+1}$, the point of tangency is $P_{1}$, the projection of $P_{1}$ on the $x$-axis is $Q_{1}$, draw a tangent line from $Q_{1}$ to the curve $C$, the point of tangency is $P_{2}$, the projection of $P_{2}$ on the $x$-axis is $Q_{2}$, and so on, continuing infinitely. This results in the sequence of points $Q_{n}\left(x_{n}, 0\right)(n=1,2,3 \cdots)$.
(1) Find the relationship between $x_{n+1}$ and $x_{n}$ $\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$;
(2) Find the general term formula for $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$;
(3) Let $a_{n}=\frac{2}{x_{n}+1}+(-1)^{n}$, prove that: $\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{3}}$ $+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}<\frac{1}{2}$

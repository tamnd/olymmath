One is a uniform water supply pipe, and the other is a uniform water discharge pipe, with the first pipe filling the pool 2 hours longer than the second pipe empties it. When the pool was filled to $1 / 3$ of its capacity, both pipes were opened, and the pool was found to be empty after 8 hours. How many hours, acting separately, does the first pipe take to fill, and the second pipe to empty the pool?

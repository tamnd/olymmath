Let $ABC$ be a triangle with orthocenter $H$. Let $P$ be a point different from $A, B$, and $C$. The intersections of the lines $(PA), (PB)$, and $(PC)$ with the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ are respectively $A', B'$, and $C'$. The symmetric point of $A'$ (resp. $B'$ and $C'$) with respect to $(BC)$ (resp. $(CA)$ and $(AB)$) is denoted as $A^*$ (resp. $B^*$ and $C^*$). Show that the points $A^*, B^*, C^*$, and $H$ lie on the same circle.

13 Given an integer $n \geqslant 2$, let $M_{0}\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ be an intersection point of the parabola $y^{2}=n x-1$ and the line $y=x$. Try to prove that for any positive integer $m$, there must exist an integer $k \geqslant 2$, such that $\left(x_{0}^{m}, y_{0}^{m}\right)$ is an intersection point of the parabola $y^{2}=k x-1$ and the line $y=x$.

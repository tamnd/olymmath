On the height $A H$ of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, a point $K$ is marked, and on the side $B C$, a point $L$ is marked. It turns out that $\frac{A K}{H K}=\frac{B L}{C L}$. Point $P$ is the foot of the perpendicular dropped from point $B$ to the line $A L$. Prove that the line $K L$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $C L P$.

(M. Stynyan)

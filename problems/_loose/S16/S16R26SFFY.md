In the tetrahedron (four-sided pyramid) $ABCD$, the centroid of the face $ABC$ is $S$. Prove that the length of the median $DS$ from vertex $D$ can be expressed using the edges of the tetrahedron by the following formula:

$$
D S^{2}=\frac{1}{3}\left(D A^{2}+D B^{2}+D C^{2}\right)-\frac{1}{9}\left(A B^{2}+B C^{2}+C A^{2}\right)
$$

Let $k \geq 3$ and $n>2^{k / 2}$. Let $K_{n}$ be the complete graph on $n$ vertices. Show that it is possible to color the edges of $K_{n}$ in yellow and purple in such a way that there do not exist $k$ vertices in $K_{n}$ such that all the edges connecting them are of the same color.

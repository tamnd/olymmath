The numbers from 1 to 1000 are written in a circle. Starting from the first, every 15th number is crossed out (i.e., the numbers $1, 16, 31$, etc.), and during subsequent rounds, already crossed-out numbers are also taken into account. The crossing out continues until it turns out that all the numbers to be crossed out have already been crossed out previously. How many numbers will remain uncrossed out?

On a straight line, there are three equal segments $A B, B C, C D$. Through the point $S$, not lying on the given line, the lines $S A$, $S B$, $S C$, $S D$ are drawn. An arbitrary secant, not passing through the point $S$, intersects these lines at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, $D_{1}$ respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{A A_{1}}{A_{1} S}+\frac{D D_{1}}{D_{1} S}=\frac{B B_{1}}{B_{1} S}+\frac{C C_{1}}{C_{1} S}
$$

(segments are considered oriented).

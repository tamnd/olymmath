In the monotonically increasing sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, $a_{1}=2, a_{2}=$ 4, and $a_{2 n-1}, a_{2 n}, a_{2 n+1} (n=1,2, \cdots)$ form an arithmetic sequence, $a_{2 n}, a_{2 n+1}, a_{2 n+2}$ form a geometric sequence.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(2) Let the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left\{\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right\}$ be $S_{n}$, prove:
$$
S_{n}>\frac{4 n}{3(n+3)}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) .
$$

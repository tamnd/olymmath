The Fibonacci numbers are defined as the sequence $F_n$ with $F_0=1$, $F_1=1$ and $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} +F_n$. How many ways can $10$ be written as an ordered sum of numbers found in the Fibonacci sequence? For example, $3$ can be written as $1 + 1 + 1$, $2 + 1$, $1 + 2$, and $3$, for a total of $4$ ways.

Let $P$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. We say $P$ is *good* if there exist infinitely many prime numbers $q$ such that the set $$ X=\left\{P(n) \mod q : n\in \mathbb N\right\} $$ has at least $\frac{q+1}{2}$ members.
Prove that the polynomial $x^3+x$ is good.

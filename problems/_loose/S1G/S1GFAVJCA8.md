(2006 Italian National Team Selection Exam) For each positive integer $n, A_{n}$ represents the set of positive integers, whose elements $a$ satisfy $a \leqslant n, n \mid\left(a^{n}+1\right)$.
(1) Find all positive integers $n$, such that $A_{n}$ is non-empty;
(2) Find all positive integers $n$, such that $\left|A_{n}\right|$ is non-zero and even;
(3) Does there exist a positive integer $n$, such that $\left|A_{n}\right|=130$?

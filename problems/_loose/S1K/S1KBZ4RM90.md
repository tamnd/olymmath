Tangents to a parabola are drawn from points $A, B$, and $C$ on the parabola. They intersect in pairs at points $K, L$, and $M$ such that $K L$ is the tangent at point $A$, $K M$ is the tangent at point $B$, and $L M$ is the tangent at point $C$. The intersection of the line $A C$ and the parallel to the axis of the parabola through point $B$ is point $N$. Prove that the quadrilateral $K L M N$ is a parallelogram.

(Problem from the 54th Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad) In decimal notation, if a $k$-digit number $a$ satisfies: if the product of two positive integers both ending in $a$ also ends in $a$, we call $a$ "stable" (for example, 0 and 25 are stable). Prove: For any positive integer $k$, there are exactly four stable $k$-digit numbers.

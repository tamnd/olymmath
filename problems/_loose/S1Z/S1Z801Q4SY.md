Find all pairs $a, b$ of positive integers, for which $$ (a, b) + 3[a, b] = a^3 - b^3 $$ Here $(a, b)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $a, b$ , and $[a, b]$ denotes the least common multiple of $a, b$ .

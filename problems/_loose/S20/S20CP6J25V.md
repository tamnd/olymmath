A cubic polynomial $P(x)$ with real coefficients is divisible by two quadratic trinomials

$$
Q(x)=x^{2}+(k-a) x-k \text { and } R(x)=2 x^{2}+(2 k-(2 a-15)) x+k
$$

without a remainder. Find the largest value of $k$ for which this is possible.

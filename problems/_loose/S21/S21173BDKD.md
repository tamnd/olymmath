For any finite non empty set $X$ of integers, let $\max (X)$ denote the largest element of $X$ and $|X|$ denote the number of elements in $X$ . If $N$ is the number of ordered pairs $(A, B)$ of finite non-empty sets of positive integers, such that $$ \begin{aligned}
& \max (A) \times|B|=12 ; \text { and }
& |A| \times \max (B)=11
\end{aligned} $$ and $N$ can be written as $100 a+b$ where $a, b$ are positive integers less than 100 , find $a+b$ .

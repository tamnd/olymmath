Let $a_{n}=4^{2 n+1}+3^{n+2}(n=1,2,3, \cdots), p$ is the smallest prime that can divide infinitely many terms of $a_{1}, a_{2}$, $a_{3}, \cdots$, $q$ is the smallest prime that can divide every term of $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \cdots$, then $p \cdot q$ is
(A) $5 \cdot 7$.
(B) $7 \cdot 13$.
(C) $13 \cdot 13$.
(D) None of the above answers.
(High School Mathematics Competition in Taiyuan City, Shanxi Province, China, 1993)

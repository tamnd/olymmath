Let $a, b, c$ be distinct non-zero real numbers, satisfying: the equations
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a x^{3}+b x+c=0, \\
b x^{3}+c x+a=0, \\
c x^{3}+a x+b=0
\end{array}
$$

have a common root, and among these three equations, there exist two equations without imaginary roots. Find the minimum value of $\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{a}$.

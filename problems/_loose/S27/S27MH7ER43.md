(Mathematics for Middle School, 2004, Issue 4, Olympiad Training Problem) Let $z=m+n \mathrm{i}$ be a complex root of the equation $a z^{4}+\mathrm{i} b z^{2}+$ $c z^{2}+\mathrm{i} d z+e=0$, where $a, b, c, d, e, m, n \in \mathbf{R}$. Then which of the following numbers must also be a root of the equation?
A. $m-n \mathrm{i}$
B. $-m+n \mathrm{i}$
C. $n+m \mathrm{i}$
D. $1-m+n \mathrm{i}$

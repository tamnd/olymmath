Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at two points $A$ and $D$. The tangent to $\Gamma_{1}$ at $A$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ again at $B$, and the tangent to $\Gamma_{2}$ at $A$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ again at $C$. Let $E \in [AB)$ such that $BE = AB$, and $F$ be the second intersection of $[AC)$ with the circumcircle $\Omega$ of $ADE$. Show that $AC = AF$.

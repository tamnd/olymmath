19-3 (N18, Netherlands) Let $n$ be a given integer greater than 2, and let $V_{n}$ be the set of numbers of the form $k n+1$ (where $k=1,2, \cdots$). A number $m \in V_{n}$ is called an irreducible number in $V_{n}$ if there do not exist two numbers $p, q \in V_{n}$ such that $p q=m$. Prove that there exists a number $r \in V_{n}$ that can be expressed as the product of several irreducible numbers in $V_{n}$ in more than one way.

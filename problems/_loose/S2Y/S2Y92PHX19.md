Triangle $ ABC$ obeys $ AB = 2AC$ and $ \angle{BAC} = 120^{\circ}.$ Points $ P$ and $ Q$ lie on segment $ BC$ such that
\begin{eqnarray*}
AB^2 + BC \cdot CP = BC^2 \\
3AC^2 + 2BC \cdot CQ = BC^2
\end{eqnarray*}
Find $ \angle{PAQ}$ in degrees.

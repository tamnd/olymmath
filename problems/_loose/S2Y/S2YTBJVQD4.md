The island of Tolpygo has the shape of a polygon. Several countries are located on it, each of which has the shape of a triangle, and any two adjacent countries have a common whole side (i.e., a vertex of one triangle does not lie on the side of another). Prove that the map of this island can be colored with three colors such that each country is colored with one color and any two adjacent countries are colored differently.

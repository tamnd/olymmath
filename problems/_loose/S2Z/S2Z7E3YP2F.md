In a triangle $\triangle A B C$, let the sizes of the interior angles be denoted as $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ as usual, where $\alpha=60^{\circ}$. $B B^{\prime}$ is the bisector of the angle $\angle A B C$ and $C C^{\prime}$ is the bisector of the angle $\angle A C B$; each of them intersects the opposite side of the triangle at an interior point ($B^{\prime}$ or $C^{\prime}$).

Furthermore, let the sizes of the angles $\angle A B^{\prime} B$ and $\angle A C^{\prime} C$ be denoted by $\epsilon$ and $\delta$, respectively.

Prove that for any such triangle $\epsilon+\delta=180^{\circ}$ holds!

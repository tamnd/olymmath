(inspired by IMO 06, 1) Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $I$ the center of its inscribed circle. Let $P$ be a point inside the triangle such that

$$
\widehat{P B A}+\widehat{P C A} \geqslant \widehat{P B C}+\widehat{P C B}
$$

Show that $A P \geqslant A I$, and that equality holds if and only if $P=I$.

Let $A B C$ be any triangle and $D, E, F$ be the midpoints of $B C, C A, A B$. The lines of gravity $A D, B E$ and $C F$ intersect at the center of gravity $S$. At least two of the quadrilaterals

$$
A F S E, B D S F, C E S D
$$

are chordal quadrilaterals. Show that the triangle $A B C$ is equilateral.

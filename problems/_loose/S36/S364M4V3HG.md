44 Let $a, b$ be positive integers, not both 1, and
$$
(a, b)=1 .
$$

If the set $S$ has the following properties:
(i) $a, b \in S$.
(ii) $x, y, z \in S \Rightarrow x+y+z \in S$.

Prove: every integer greater than $2 a b$ belongs to $S$.

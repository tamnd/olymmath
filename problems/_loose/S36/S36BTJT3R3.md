(12th Ya Guo Competition Problem) Let the function $f(x)$ satisfy for all rational numbers $m, n$, $\mid f(m+n)-f(m) - 1 \leqslant \frac{n}{m}$. Prove: For all positive integers $k$, $\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left|f\left(2^{k}\right)-f\left(2^{i}\right)\right| \leqslant \frac{k(k-1)}{2}$.

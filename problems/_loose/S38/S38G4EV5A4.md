In a round-robin tournament, there were no draws; 1 point was awarded for a win, and 0 for a loss. Then, a coefficient for each participant was determined. It was equal to the sum of the points scored by those whom the athlete defeated. It turned out that all participants had equal coefficients. The number of participants in the tournament is greater than two. Prove that all athletes scored the same number of points.

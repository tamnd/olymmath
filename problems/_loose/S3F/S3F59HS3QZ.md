In a tetrahedron $S-ABC$, the lateral edges $SA, SB, SC$ are mutually perpendicular. $M$ is a moving point inside the base triangle $ABC$, and the angles formed by the line $MS$ with $SA, SB, SC$ are $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ respectively.
(1) Prove that $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ cannot be the three interior angles of an acute triangle;
(2) Let $S=\frac{1}{\cos ^{2} \alpha}+\frac{1}{\cos ^{2} \beta}+\frac{1}{\cos ^{2} \gamma}-\frac{2\left(\cos ^{3} \alpha+\cos ^{3} \beta+\cos ^{3} \gamma\right)}{\cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta \cdot \cos \gamma}$, prove that $S \geq 3$.

In trapezoid $A B C D$ with bases $A B$ and $C D$, it holds that $|A B|>|C D|$ and $\angle A D C = 90^{\circ}$. Let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of vertex $A$ onto line $B C$. Prove that lines $A C$ and $D E$ intersect perpendicularly if and only if triangle $A B C$ is isosceles with vertex $B$.

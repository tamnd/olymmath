Let $A B C$ be a right-angled triangle with $C$ as the vertex of the right angle and with $C A=4 \mathrm{~cm}$, $C B=20 \mathrm{~cm}$.

A natural number $x$ is required to satisfy the following conditions (1) and (2):

a) The segment $C B$ can be shortened by $x \mathrm{~cm}$; i.e., there is a point $B^{\prime}$ between $C$ and $B$ with $C B^{\prime}=C B-x \mathrm{~cm}$.

b) If at the same time the segment $C A$ is extended beyond $A$ by $x \mathrm{~cm}$ to a point $A^{\prime}$, then the area of the triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C$ is exactly $55 \%$ of the area of the triangle $A B C$.

Investigate whether there is exactly one natural number $x$ that satisfies conditions (1) and (2)! If this is the case, determine this number!

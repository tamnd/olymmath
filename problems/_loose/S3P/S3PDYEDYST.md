Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. A line through point $A$ intersects the first circle again at point $C$ and the second circle at point $D$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the arcs $BC$ and $BD$, not containing point $A$, and $K$ be the midpoint of segment $CD$. Prove that angle $M K N$ is a right angle. (You can assume that points $C$ and $D$ lie on opposite sides of point $A$.)

Let the function $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f(x)=x+\log _{3}\left(1+3^{x}\right)$. Show that:

a) The function $f$ is invertible and $f^{-1}(x)<f(x), \forall x \in \mathbb{R}$.

b) $f(n) \in \mathbb{R} \backslash \mathbb{Q}, \forall n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

$$
r^{100}=2 r^{98}+14 r^{96}+11 r^{94}-r^{50}+a r^{46}+b r^{44}+c r^{40}
$$

What is the value of $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}$ ?
(A) 11421
(B) 20229
(C) 16291
(D) 15339
(E) 17115

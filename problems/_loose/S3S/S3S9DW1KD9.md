A car can only carry $L$ liters of gasoline, with which it can travel $a$ kilometers. Now, it is required to travel $d(>a)$ kilometers to a certain place, with no refueling points along the way. However, gasoline can be transported to any point along the route and stored for later use. Assuming there are $k$ cars, all of the same model, how should they travel to ensure that all cars reach the destination and use the least amount of gasoline?

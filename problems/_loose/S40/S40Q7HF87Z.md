Along the shore of a circular lake, there are 20 villages, each with 20 warriors. In the martial arts competition, every two warriors from different villages will have a match. If in all the matches between villages $\mathrm{A}$ and $\mathrm{B}$, at least $\mathrm{k}$ results are $\mathrm{A}$ winning, then village $\mathrm{A}$ is said to be stronger than village $\mathrm{B}$. It is known that each village is stronger than the village adjacent to it in a clockwise direction. Find the maximum possible value of $\mathrm{k}$ (assuming all warriors have different levels, and the match results are always the stronger one wins).

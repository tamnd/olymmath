Let positive numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}(n \geqslant 3)$ satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=n$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{i}{1+x_{i}+x_{i}^{2}+\cdots+x_{i}^{i-1}} \\
\leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{i+1}{1+x_{i}+x_{i}^{2}+\cdots+x_{i}^{i}} .
\end{array}
$$

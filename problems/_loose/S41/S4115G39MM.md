Lines $\ell$ and $m$ going through $R$ are parallel to $CA$ and $AB$ respectively. Line $\ell$ meets $AB$ and $BC$ at $P$ and $P^\prime$ respectively, and $m$ meets $CA$ and $BC$ at $Q$ and $Q^\prime$ respectively.

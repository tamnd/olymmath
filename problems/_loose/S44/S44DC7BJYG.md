Somewhere in the ocean, there is an island of Misfortune, on which there are $2 n$ cities, connected by roads in such a way that more than $n$ roads lead out of each city. A tourist heard in the news that two cities had to be closed for quarantine, so all roads leading to these cities were blocked. Unfortunately, he could not make out the names of the cities. Prove that the tourist, despite the blockades, can still travel from any open city to any other.

There are two circles given in a plane, then we can inscribe zero or infinitely many triangles in one circle, which are simultaneously inscribed in the other circle. In the latter case, the orthocenters of the triangles lie on a circle whose center is symmetric to the center of the first circle with respect to the center of the second circle, and whose radius is $\pm 2 r_{b}+r_{k}$; here $r_{b}$ represents the radius of the first circle, and $r_{k}$ the radius of the second circle.

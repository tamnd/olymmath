$$
a x^{2}+b x+c=0
$$

THEN

$$
x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}=\frac{b^{2}-2 a c}{a^{2}}, x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}=\frac{3 a b c-b^{3}}{a^{3}}
$$

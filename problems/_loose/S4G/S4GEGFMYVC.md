On the table, there are 32 chess pieces, and two people, A and B, take turns to take some of the pieces. The rule is: A takes first, and each person must take at least 1 piece and at most 6 pieces each time, and the one who takes the last piece wins. To ensure victory, at the beginning, the number of pieces A should take first is
(A) 6 pieces.
(B) 5 pieces.
(C) 4 pieces.
(D) 3 pieces.
(Chinese Jiangsu Province Suzhou City High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

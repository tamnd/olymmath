Let $n$ be a "Good Number" if sum of all divisors of $n$ is less than $2n$ for $n\in \mathbb{Z}.$
Does there exist an infinite set $M$ that satisfies the following?

For all $a,b\in M,$ $a+b$ is good number. ($a=b$ is allowed.)

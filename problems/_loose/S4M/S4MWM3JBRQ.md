For $a, b > 0$ , denote by $t(a,b)$ the positive root of the equation $$ (a+b)x^2-2(ab-1)x-(a+b) = 0. $$ Let $M = \{ (a.b) | a \ne b \,\, and \,\, t(a,b) \le \sqrt{ab} \}$ Determine, for $(a, b)\in M$ , the mmimum value of $t(a,b)$ .

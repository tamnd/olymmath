Given the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
x_{n+2}=2 x_{n+1}+x_{n}, x_{1}=2, x_{2}=6 \text {; }
$$

The sequence $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
y_{n+2}=y_{n+1}+2 y_{n}, y_{1}=3, y_{2}=9 \text {. }
$$

Prove: There exists a positive integer $n_{0}$, such that for any $n>n_{0}$, we have $x_{n}>y_{n}$.

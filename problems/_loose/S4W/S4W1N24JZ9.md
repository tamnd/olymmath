The numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, n$ are written in some order: $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n}$. The sum $S=a_{1 / 1}+a_{2 / 2}+\ldots+$ $a_{n / n}$ is taken. Find such an $n$ that among these sums (for all possible permutations of $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n}$), all integers from $n$ to $n+100$ are encountered.

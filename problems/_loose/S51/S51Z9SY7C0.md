If the sides of an acute-angled triangle are $a, b, c$, and the perpendicular dropped from the vertex of the angle opposite side $a$ divides this side into segments, of which the smaller is $p$, then

$$
\frac{a}{c+b}=\frac{c-b}{a-2 p}
$$

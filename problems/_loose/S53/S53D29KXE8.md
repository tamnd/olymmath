Given an integer $n \geqslant 2$ and $n$ positive numbers $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$ whose sum is 1. Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be any $n$ positive numbers that are not all equal. For $k=2,3$, define
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{k}= & p_{1} x_{1}^{k}+p_{2} x_{2}^{k}+\cdots+p_{n} x_{n}^{k}- \\
& \left(p_{1} x_{1}+p_{2} x_{2}+\cdots+p_{n} x_{n}\right)^{k} .
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove: If a constant $C$ satisfies
$$
\frac{d_{3}}{d_{2}} \geqslant C \min \left\{x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}\right\}
$$

always holds, then the maximum value of $C$ is 3.

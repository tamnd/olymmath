On the side $AB$ of the parallelogram $ABCD$, where $|AB|=1$, points $K$ and $L$ are chosen such that $|BK|=\frac{1}{2}$ and $|BL|=\frac{1}{3}$. On the side $CD$, points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen such that $|CP|=\frac{1}{2}$ and $|CQ|=\frac{1}{3}$. Let the intersection of lines $LD$ and $KP$ be $X$, and the intersection of lines $BD$ and $LQ$ be $Y$. Prove that the line $XY$ bisects the side $BC$. (Jaroslav Zhouf)

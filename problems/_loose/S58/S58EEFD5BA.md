In the role-playing game "World of MSU," there are three classes: warrior, mage, and healer. Each player can control a character of a certain class (single class) or a character combining the abilities of two classes (dual class), for example, a mage-healer. A party of 32 players is storming the "Citadel of Evil." It is known that the number of healers (i.e., all those with healing abilities) is twice the number of mages and $k$ times fewer than the number of warriors ($k$ is an integer greater than two). How many players have a single class, given that the number of players with a dual class is 2 more than the number of healers?

From 100 cards numbered 1 to 100, a card is randomly drawn each time and then put back. This process is repeated 20 times. Let the probability that the 20 numbers drawn are all different be $p$. Prove:
$$
p<\left(\frac{9}{10}\right)^{19}<\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^{2}} \text {. }
$$

Among all convex curves of length 1, having an angular point with angle a (see above p. 24), find the curve bounding a figure of maximum area.

In three-dimensional space, the "isoperimetric problem" is as follows: among all bodies bounded by surfaces of area 1, find the body of maximum volume. It turns out that this body is a sphere (radius $\left.\frac{1}{2 \gamma \frac{\pi}{\pi}}\right)$; a proof of this statement is provided, for example, in the book by D. A. Kryzhanovsky cited above.

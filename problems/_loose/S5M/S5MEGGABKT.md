Show that there exists a positive constant $C$ such that, for all positive reals $a$ and $b$ with $a + b$ being an integer, we have $$ \left\{a^3\right\} + \left\{b^3\right\} + \frac{C}{(a+b)^6} \le 2. $$ Here $\{x\} = x - \lfloor x\rfloor$ is the fractional part of $x$ .

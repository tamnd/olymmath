János and Gábor play the following game: János draws a card from a 32-card Hungarian deck. Gábor has to guess the color of the drawn card. If he guesses correctly, he wins 2 Ft from János, if not, he gives him 1 Ft. Before guessing, Gábor can name four cards, and János must tell him whether the drawn card is among them. How should Gábor choose the four cards to maximize his expected winnings?

Is the game fair? (A game is considered fair if the expected winnings of both players are $0 \mathrm{Ft}$.)

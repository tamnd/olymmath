Let $k$ be a positive integer. A positive integer $n$ is said to be a $k$-flip if the digits of $n$ are reversed in order when it is multiplied by $k$. For example, 1089 is a 9 -flip because $1089 \times 9=9801$, and 21978 is a 4 -flip because $21978 \times 4=87912$. Explain why there is no 7-flip integer.

Points $M$ and $N$ belong to the lateral sides $A B$ and $A C$ of an isosceles triangle $A B C$, respectively, such that $M N \| B C$, and a circle can be inscribed in the trapezoid $B M N C$. Its radius is $R$, and the radius of the inscribed circle of triangle $A M N$ is $r$. Find

a) the base $B C$

b) the distance from point $A$ to the nearest point of tangency;

c) the distance between the chords of the circles connecting the points of tangency with the lateral sides of the trapezoid $B M N C$.

B. Given that $m$, $n$, and $t$ are positive integers, and satisfy $t\left(m^{2}-n^{2}\right)+m-n^{2}-n=0$.
Prove: (1) $m-n$ is a perfect square;
(2) For any positive integer $t$, there exists a set of positive integers $m$, $n$ that satisfy the above equation.

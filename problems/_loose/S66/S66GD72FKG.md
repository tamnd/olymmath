In a class of seven students, a poll is conducted. The poll asks

$~$(a) Were you born after $2021$?

$~$(b) Were you born after $2019$?

$~$(c) Were you born in $2020$?

Five students respond "yes" to question (a), and four students respond "no" to question (b). If everyone truthfully answers all questions, how many students responded "yes" to (c)?

$\textbf{(A) }1 \textbf{(B) }2 \textbf{(C) }3 \textbf{(D) }4 \textbf{(E) }5$

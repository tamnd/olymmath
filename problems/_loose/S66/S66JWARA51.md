To solve the equation $\sqrt{5-x}+2=7$, we first isolate the square root term:

1. Subtract 2 from both sides:
$$
\sqrt{5-x} = 5
$$

2. Square both sides to eliminate the square root:
$$
5-x = 25
$$

3. Solve for $ x $:
$$
-x = 25 - 5
$$
$$
-x = 20
$$
$$
x = -20
$$

In a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ and its exterior angle bisector intersect $AC$ at points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, respectively. From points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, draw the tangents to the incircle $\odot I$ of $\triangle ABC$ that are different from $AC$. These tangents touch $\odot I$ at points $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$, respectively. Prove that $B$, $K_{1}$, and $K_{2}$ are collinear. ${ }^{[s]}$
(41st Russian Mathematical Olympiad)

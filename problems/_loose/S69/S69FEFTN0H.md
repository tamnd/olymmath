(Exploring divisibility a bit)

Let $A$ and $B$ be two polynomials in two variables such that $\frac{A(x, y)}{B(x, y)}$ is a polynomial in $x$ for an infinite number of values of $y$ and a polynomial in $y$ for an infinite number of values of $x$. Show that $B$ divides $A$.

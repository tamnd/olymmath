When solving the problem: to divide the area of a trapezoid in half by a line parallel to its bases, the formula obtained is

$$
\frac{x}{h}=\frac{\sqrt{2\left(\overline{a^{2}}+c^{2}\right)}-c}{2(a-c)}
$$

where $ x $ represents the distance of the section from the smaller of the parallel sides, $ a $ and $ c $ are these sides, and $ h $ is the height of the trapezoid. Check this formula for $ c=0 $. Can we conclude from this that the formula itself is correct?

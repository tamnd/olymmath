Let $f$ be a function on the interval $(0,1)$ (taking real values), if
(1) $f(x)>0, x \in(0,1)$;
(2) $\frac{f(x)}{f(y)}+\frac{f(1-x)}{f(1-y)} \leqslant 2$, for any $x, y \in(0,1)$.
Prove: $f$ must be a constant function.

Substance $A$ decomposes into two substances $X$ and $Y$ with the rate of formation of each being proportional to the amount of undecomposed substance. Find the law of change of the quantities $x$ and $y$ of substances $X$ and $Y$ as functions of time $t$, if at $t=0$ we have $x=y=0$, and after one hour $x=\frac{a}{8}, y=\frac{3 a}{8}$, where $a$ is the initial amount of substance $A$.

Starting from an arbitrary triangle $\left(H_{0}\right)$, we form the sequence of triangles $H_{0}, H_{1}, H_{2}, \ldots$ such that $H_{k+1}$ is the triangle whose sides are the external angle bisectors of $H_{k}$. Prove that the triangles become "increasingly regular," that is, each angle of $H_{k}$ approaches $60^{\circ}$ as $k$ grows beyond any bound.

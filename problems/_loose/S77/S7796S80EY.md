Positive numbers $b_1, b_2,..., b_n$ are given so that $b_1 + b_2 + ...+ b_n \le 10$ .
Further, $a_1 = b_1$ and $a_m = sa_{m-1} + b_m$ for $m > 1$ , where $0 \le s < 1$ .
Show that $a^2_1 + a^2_2 + ... + a^2_n \le \frac{100}{1 - s^2} $

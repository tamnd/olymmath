A positive integer will be called *typical* if the sum of its decimal digits is a multiple of $2011$ .
a) Show that there are infinitely many *typical* numbers, each having at least $2011$ multiples which are also typical numbers.
b) Does there exist a positive integer such that each of its multiples is typical?

a) Calculate $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty} \frac{[x]+[2 x]+\ldots[n x]}{x}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, where $[a]$ represents the integer part of the real number $a$.

b) Let the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 0}$ for which $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{a_{n}}{\ln n}=l$ and $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} n\left(a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right)=L$ exist and are finite. Determine the relationship between $l$ and $L$.

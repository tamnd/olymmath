Let $S$ be a subset of the real numbers for which the following conditions hold:

(i) $Z \subseteq S$

(ii) $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3} \in S$

(iii) $x, y \in S \Rightarrow x+y \in S, x y \in S$

Prove that $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} \in S$.

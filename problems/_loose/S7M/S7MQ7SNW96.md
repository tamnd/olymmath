In a class, one outstanding student cadre is to be selected. The table below shows the scores of the class leader, study committee member, and youth league branch secretary:
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline & Class Leader & Study Committee Member & Youth League Branch Secretary \\
\hline Ideological Performance & 24 & 28 & 26 \\
\hline Academic Performance & 26 & 26 & 24 \\
\hline Work Ability & 28 & 24 & 26 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

Assuming that in the selection of outstanding cadres, the weight ratio of ideological performance, academic performance, and work ability is $3: 3: 4$, calculate and explain who should be selected as the outstanding student cadre.

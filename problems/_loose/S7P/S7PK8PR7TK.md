Rectangle has one side of length 5, and the other side is less than 4. The rectangle is folded so that two opposite vertices coincide. If the length of the fold is $\sqrt{6}$, then the length of the other side is
(A) $\sqrt{2}$.
(B) $\sqrt{3}$.
(C) 2 .
(D) $\sqrt{5}$.
(E) $\sqrt{\frac{11}{2}}$.
(16th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1965)

$\vec{a}_{1}, \vec{a}_{2}, \ldots, \vec{a}_{n}$ - vectors on a plane, no two of which are collinear. It is known that for any two different indices $i$ and $j$ among these vectors, there is a vector of the form $x \vec{a}_{i}+y \vec{a}_{j}$, where $x$ and $y$ are some negative numbers. Prove that $n-$ is odd.

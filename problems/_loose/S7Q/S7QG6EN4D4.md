(Median Length Formula) In $\triangle A B C$, $A O$ is the median on side $B C$, prove: $m_{a}^{2}=\frac{1}{2} b^{2}+\frac{1}{2} c^{2}-\frac{1}{4} a^{2}$.
(here $m_{a}=|A O|,|A C|=b,|A B|=c$ )

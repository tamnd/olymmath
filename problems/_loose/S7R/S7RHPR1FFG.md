Given is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ whose diagonals intersect at point $P$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be points chosen such that the quadrilaterals $ABPX$, $CDXP$, $BCPY$, and $DAYP$ are cyclic. The lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $Q$, the lines $BC$ and $DA$ at point $R$, and the lines $XR$ and $YQ$ at point $Z$. Prove that the points $X, Y, Z$, and $P$ lie on the same circle.

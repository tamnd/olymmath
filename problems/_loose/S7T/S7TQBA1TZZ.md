For each positive integer $n$ , define $a_n = n(n + 1)$ . Prove that $$ n^{1/a_1} + n^{1/a_3} + n^{1/a_5} + ...+ n^{1/a_{2n-1}} \ge n^{a_{3n+2}/a_{3n+1}} $$ .

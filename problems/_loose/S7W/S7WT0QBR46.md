A circle with center at point $O$ lies on the hypotenuse $A C$ of a right triangle $A B C$, touching its legs $A B$ and $B C$. Find $A C$, if it is known that $A M=\frac{20}{9}, A N: M N=6: 1$, where $M$ is the point of tangency of $A B$ with the circle, and $N$ is the point of intersection of the circle with $A C$, located between points $A$ and $O$.

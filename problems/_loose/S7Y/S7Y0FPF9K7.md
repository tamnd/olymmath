Consider $\left(\triangle_n=A_nB_nC_n\right)_{n\ge 1}$. We define points $A_n',B_n',C_n'$ in sides $C_nB_n,A_nC_n,B_nA_n$ such that
$$(n+1)B_nA_n'=C_nA_n',~(n+1)C_nB_n'=A_nB_n',~(n+1)A_nC_n'=B_nC_n'$$$\triangle_{n+1}$ is defined by the intersections of $A_nA_n',B_nB_n',C_nC_n'$. If $S_n$ denotes the area of $\triangle_n$. Find $\frac{S_1}{S_{2020}}$.

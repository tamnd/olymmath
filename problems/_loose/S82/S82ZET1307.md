Given that $X$ is a finite set, and $|X|>10$. $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{2016}$ are subsets of set $X$, $2\left|X_{i}\right|>|X|$ $(i=1,2, \cdots, 2016)$. Prove: There exists a ten-element subset $B$ of $X$, such that
$$
A_{i} \cap B \neq \varnothing(i=1,2, \cdots, 2016) .
$$

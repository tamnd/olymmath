Determine all pairs $(a, b)$ for which the following condition holds: there exists index $n_0$ such that the sequence $(a_n + a_{n+1}), n \ge n_0$ is non-decreasing.

Consider a sequence $(a_n)$: $a_1 = a, a_2 = b$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers, and for all $n \ge 2$ $a_{n+1}$ equals the number of indexes $i, 1 \le i \le n$, such that $a_i = a_n$.

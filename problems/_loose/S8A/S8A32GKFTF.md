In the field of real numbers, solve the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
2 x+\lfloor y\rfloor & =2022, \\
3 y+\lfloor 2 x\rfloor & =2023 .
\end{aligned}
$$

(The symbol $\lfloor a\rfloor$ denotes the floor of the real number $a$, i.e., the greatest integer not greater than $a$. For example, $\lfloor 1.9\rfloor=1$ and $\lfloor-1.1\rfloor=-2$.)

Two circles intersect each other perpendicularly. Let the points lying on the centers of the two circles be $A, B, C, D$ and one of the intersection points of the two circles be $P$. Show that the square of the distance between the centers is the arithmetic mean of the squares of the distances $P A, P B, P C, P D$.

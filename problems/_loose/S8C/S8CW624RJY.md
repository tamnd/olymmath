Let $n \ge 2023$ be an integer. Prove that there exists a permutation $(p_1, p_2, \dots, p_n)$ of $(1, 2, \dots, n)$ such that
$$
p_1 + 2p_2 + 3p_3 + \dots + np_n
$$
is divisible by $n$.

A pentagon $ABCDE$ is inscribed in a circle $\omega$. The line $CD$ intersects the rays $AB$ and $AE$ at points $X$ and $Y$ respectively. Segments $EX$ and $BY$ intersect at point $P$ and intersect the circle $\omega$ again at points $Q$ and $R$. The point $A'$ is symmetric to point $A$ with respect to the line $CD$. The circle $\gamma$, circumscribed around triangle $PQR$, intersects the circle circumscribed around triangle $A'XY$ at two points. Prove that these points can be called $M$ and $N$ such that the lines $CM$ and $DN$ intersect on the circle $\gamma$.

(M. Diden, A. Kuznecov)

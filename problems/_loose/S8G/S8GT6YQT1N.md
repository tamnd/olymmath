Triangles $A B C_{1}$ and $A B C_{2}$ have a common base $A B$ and $\angle A C_{1} B=\angle A C_{2} B$. Prove that if $\left|A C_{1}-C_{1} B\right|<\left|A C_{2}-C_{2} B\right|$, then:

a) the area of triangle $A B C_{1}$ is greater than the area of triangle $A B C_{2}$;

b) the perimeter of triangle $A B C_{1}$ is greater than the perimeter of triangle $A B C_{2}$.

11.48*. a) Prove that among all $n$-sided polygons inscribed in a given circle, the regular $n$-sided polygon has the largest area.

b) Prove that among all $n$-sided polygons inscribed in a given circle, the regular $n$-sided polygon has the largest perimeter.

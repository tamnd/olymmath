$$
\frac{O A \cdot O A^{\prime}}{O B \cdot O B^{\prime}}=\left(\frac{O A+O A^{\prime}}{O B+O B^{\prime}}\right)^{2}
$$

Is it true that the triangles $O A B$ and $O A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ are similar?

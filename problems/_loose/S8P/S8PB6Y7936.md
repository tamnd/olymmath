Find all primes $ p$ for that there is an integer $ n$ such that there are no integers $ x,y$ with $ x^3 + y^3 \equiv n \mod p$ (so not all residues are the sum of two cubes).

E.g. for $ p = 7$, one could set $ n = \pm 3$ since $ x^3,y^3 \equiv 0 , \pm 1 \mod 7$, thus $ x^3 + y^3 \equiv 0 , \pm 1 , \pm 2 \mod 7$ only.

(2002, Bulgaria National Mathematical Olympiad (Regional Level) Problem) Let $E, F$ be points on the sides $AD, CD$ of $\square ABCD$, such that $\angle AEB = \angle AFB = 90^{\circ}$, and $EG \parallel AB$, where $EG$ intersects $BF$ at point $G$. If $AF$ intersects $BE$ at point $H$, and $DH$ intersects $BC$ at point $I$, prove that $FI \perp GH$.

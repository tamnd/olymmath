The youngest son says: "Regardless of what the first of the four numbers is, one of these numbers must have a digit sum that is divisible by 4."

The older son, on the other hand, claims that among four consecutive six-digit numbers, it is not necessarily the case that one of the numbers has a digit sum that is divisible by 4.

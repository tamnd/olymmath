Let's prove that if $a>b>0$, and

$$
x=\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}+\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}
$$

then

$$
\frac{2 b \sqrt{x^{2}-1}}{x-\sqrt{x^{2}-1}}=a-b
$$

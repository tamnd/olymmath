Prove that the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from the intersection point of the diagonals of an inscribed quadrilateral to its sides are the vertices of a quadrilateral into which a circle can be inscribed. Find the radius of this circle if the diagonals of the inscribed quadrilateral are perpendicular, the radius of the given circle is $R$, and the distance from its center to the intersection point of the diagonals is $d$.

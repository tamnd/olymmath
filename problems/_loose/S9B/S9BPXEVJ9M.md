$$
\frac{1}{x-a}+\frac{1}{x-2 a}=1, a \in \mathbb{R}
$$

a) Prove that for every $a$ the solutions of the equation are real.

b) Determine $a$ such that $\frac{x_{1}}{x_{2}}+\frac{x_{2}}{x_{1}}=3$.

Let $a>1$ be a natural number and let $f$ and $g$ be polynomials with integer coefficients. Suppose there is a natural number $n_{0}$ such that $g(n)>0$ for all $n \geq n_{0}$ and

$$
f(n) \mid a^{g(n)}-1 \text { for all } n \geq n_{0} \text {. }
$$

Show that $f$ must then be constant.

For any given $h=2^{r}$ (where $r$ is a non-negative integer), find all natural numbers $k$ that satisfy the following conditions: for each such $k$, there exist an odd natural number $m>1$ and a natural number $n$, such that
$$
k\left|m^{k}-1, m\right| n^{\frac{m^{k}-1}{k}}+1
$$
(China National Training Team Selection Exam, 1998)

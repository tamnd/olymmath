Hong and Song each have a shuffled deck of eight cards, four red and four black. Every turn, each player places down the two topmost cards of their decks. A player can thus play one of three pairs: two black cards, two red cards, or one of each color. The probability that Hong and Song play exactly the same pairs as each other for all four turns is $\frac{m}{n}$ , where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers with $\gcd(m,n)=1$ . Compute $100m+n$ .

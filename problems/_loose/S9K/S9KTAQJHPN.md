Given that the cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$ is similar to the cyclic quadrilateral $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$.
Prove: $A A^{\prime 2} \cdot S_{\triangle B C D}+C C^{\prime 2} \cdot S_{\triangle A B D}=B B^{\prime 2} \cdot S_{\triangle A C D}+D D^{\prime 2} \cdot S_{\triangle A B C}$.

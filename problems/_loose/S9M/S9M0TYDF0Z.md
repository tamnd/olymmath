Let $p=A B C$ be a three-digit number represented in decimal notation, where $0 \leq A, B, C \leq 9$, and $A, B, C$ are natural numbers, meaning $A, B, C$ represent the digits of the respective places. It is to be proven: If $p=A B C$ is divisible by 37, then the numbers $q=B C A$ and $r=C A B$ are also divisible by 37.

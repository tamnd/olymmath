a) Show that for two of the seven students, the first and last name can be uniquely determined from these details! Provide the first and last names of these two students!

b) Investigate whether the first and last names of any additional students can be uniquely determined from the given information or if for each additional student, there are multiple possibilities to combine first and last names to satisfy the above details!

In the acute triangle $\triangle A B C$ with $\angle B A C=45 \mathrm{deg}$, $B E(E \in A C)$ and $C F(F \in A B)$ are altitudes. Let $H, M$ and $K$ be the orthocentre of $A B C$ and the midpoints of $B C$ and $A H$, respectively.

a.) Prove that the quadrangle $M E K F$ is a square.

b.) Prove that the diagonals of the quadrangle $M E K F$ intersect at the midpoint of $O H$, where $O$ is the circumcentre of $\triangle A B C$.

c.) Find the length of $E F$ when the circumradius of $\triangle A B C$ is 1 .

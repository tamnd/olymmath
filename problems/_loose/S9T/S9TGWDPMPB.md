Let $(\Omega, \mathscr{F}, \mathrm{P})$ be a probability space, $\mathscr{A}$ and $\mathscr{B}$ be two $\sigma$-subalgebras in $\mathscr{F}$. Let also $L^{2}(\Omega, \mathscr{G}, \mathrm{P})$ for any $\sigma$-algebra $\mathscr{G}$ be the space of $\mathscr{G}$-measurable random variables with finite second moments.

We define

$$
\rho^{*}(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B})=\sup \left|\rho^{*}(\xi, \zeta)\right|
$$

where $\rho^{*}(\xi, \zeta)$ is the correlation coefficient of the random variables $\xi$ and $\zeta$, and the supremum is taken over all random variables $\xi \in L^{2}(\Omega, \mathscr{A}, \mathrm{P})$ and $\zeta \in L^{2}(\Omega, \mathscr{B}, \mathrm{P})$.

(a) Show that if $\mathscr{A}=\sigma(X)$ and $\mathscr{B}=\sigma(Y)$, where $X$ and $Y$ are some random variables on $(\Omega, \mathscr{F}, \mathrm{P})$, then

$$
\rho^{*}(\sigma(X), \sigma(Y))=\rho^{*}(X, Y)
$$

(b) Let $\mathscr{A}=\sigma\left(\mathscr{A}_{i}, i=1,2\right), \mathscr{B}=\sigma\left(\mathscr{B}_{i}, i=1,2\right)$, and the $\sigma$-algebras $\sigma\left(\mathscr{A}_{i}, \mathscr{B}_{i}\right), i=1,2$, are independent (here $\sigma(\mathscr{E}, \mathscr{G})$ denotes the smallest $\sigma$-algebra generated by sets $E \in \mathscr{E}$ and $G \in \mathscr{G}$). Show that

$$
\rho^{*}(\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B})=\max \left\{\rho^{*}\left(\mathscr{A}_{1}, \mathscr{B}_{1}\right), \rho^{*}\left(\mathscr{A}_{2}, \mathscr{B}_{2}\right)\right\}
$$

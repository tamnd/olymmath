Let $P$ be an arbitrary interior point of triangle $ABC$, and let $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ be arbitrary interior points on the sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. Show that the product of the radii of the circumcircles of triangles $A C_{1} P, B A_{1} P$ and $C B_{1} P$ is equal to the product of the radii of the circumcircles of triangles $C_{1} B P, A_{1} C P$ and $B_{1} A P$.

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m}$ be given positive integers, $p(x) \in \mathbf{Z}[x]$, and for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$, there exists $a_{i} \in$ $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m}\right\}$, such that $a_{i} \mid p(n)$. Prove: there exists $a_{j} \in$ $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m}\right\}$, such that for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$, we have $a_{j} \mid p(n)$.

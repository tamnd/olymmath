For each strip region in the Cartesian coordinate system
$$
S_{n}=\{(x, y) \mid n \leqslant x < n+1, y \in \mathbb{R}\} (n \in \mathbb{Z}),
$$
if the points in the region are colored with a finite number of colors, then for any positive real numbers $a$ and $b$ $(a < b)$, there always exist four points of the same color $A, B, C, D$, such that the quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a rectangle with length $a$ and width $b$.

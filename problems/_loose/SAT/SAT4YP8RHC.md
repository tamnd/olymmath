Let polynomials $f(x), g(x), h(x), k(x)$ satisfy
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\left(x^{2}+1\right) h(x)+(x-1) f(x)+(x-2) g(x)=0 \\
\left(x^{2}+1\right) k(x)+(x+1) f(x)+(x+2) g(x)=0
\end{array}\right.
$$

Prove: $\left(x^{2}+1\right)\left|f(x),\left(x^{2}+1\right)\right| g(x)$.

In the decimal representation of a six-digit number $a$, there are no zeros and the sum of its digits is 18. Find the sum of all different numbers obtained from the number $a$ by cyclic permutations of its digits. In a cyclic permutation, all digits of the number, except the last one, are shifted one place to the right, and the last one is moved to the first place.

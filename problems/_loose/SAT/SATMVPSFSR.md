From an arbitrary point $M$ on the circle circumscribed around the rectangle $A B C D$, perpendiculars $M Q$ and $M P$ are dropped to its two opposite sides, and perpendiculars $M R$ and $M T$ are dropped to the extensions of the other two sides. Prove that the lines $P R$ and $Q T$ are perpendicular, and the point of their intersection lies on the diagonal of the rectangle $A B C D$.

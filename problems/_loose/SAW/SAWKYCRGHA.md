Prove that the sides of any rectangular integer triangle are proportional multiples of the sides of a primitive triangle with legs $a$ and $b$ and hypotenuse $c$, which are expressed by the formulas (up to a permutation of the legs)

$$
a=2 m n, b=m^{2}-n^{2}, c=m^{2}+n^{2}
$$

where $m$ and $n$ are coprime natural numbers of different parity, satisfying the inequality $0<n<m$.

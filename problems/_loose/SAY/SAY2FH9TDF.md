Let the centroids of the equilateral triangles constructed outwardly on the sides of triangle $ABC$ be $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$; and those of the inwardly constructed ones be $S_{a}, S_{b}, S_{c}$. Show that the triangles $S_{1} S_{2} S_{3}$ and $S_{a} S_{b} S_{c}$ are equilateral and that their centroids coincide with the centroid of triangle $ABC$.

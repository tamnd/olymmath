Let $m, n$ be positive integers, and $m>1$. Person A divides the integers $1,2,3, \cdots, 2m$ into $m$ pairs. Person B selects one number from each pair and calculates the sum of these numbers. Prove: A can appropriately pair the numbers such that no matter how B selects, the sum never equals $n$.

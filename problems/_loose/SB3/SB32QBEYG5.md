The currency units of Kiria and Daria are called Kiner and Diner, respectively. In Kiria, 1 Kiner can be exchanged for 10 Dinars, and in Daria, 1 Dinar can be exchanged for 10 Kiners. A magician originally has 1 Dinar. He can freely travel between the two countries and can exchange currencies at will.

Prove that at any time, the amounts of the two types of money he holds are never equal.
(Leningrad Mathematical Olympiad, 1987)

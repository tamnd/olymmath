On a certain island, there are 2022 people, each of whom is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. One day, all the inhabitants of this island stood in a circle, and each was asked in turn, "Is the person to your left a liar?" The total number of "Yes" answers was 2, and the number of "No" answers was 2020. After that, everyone was asked, "Is the person to your right, skipping one person, a liar?" Again, there were 2 "Yes" answers and 2020 "No" answers. How many "Yes" answers will be received if everyone is asked, "Is the person standing opposite you in the circle a liar?" (Find all possible answers and prove that there are no others.)

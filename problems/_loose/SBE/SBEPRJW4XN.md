Given a parallelogram $A B C D$ satisfying $\angle B A D>90^{\circ}$, construct $\triangle D C E$ and $\triangle B C F$ outside the quadrilateral such that $\angle E D C=\angle C B F$, $\angle D C E=$ $\angle B F C$, connect $E F$, and construct $\triangle E F G$ outside $\triangle C E F$ such that $\angle E F G=\angle C F B$, $\angle F E G=\angle C E D$. Prove: $\triangle A E F \cong \triangle G E F$.

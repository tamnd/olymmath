Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be given real numbers. Prove that there exists a real number $x$ such that
$$
\left\{x-x_{1}\right\}+\left\{x-x_{2}\right\}+\cdots+\left\{x-x_{n}\right\} \leqslant \frac{n-1}{2},
$$

where $\{y\}$ denotes the fractional part of $y$ (i.e., $\{y\}=y-[y]$).

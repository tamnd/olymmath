On a plane, several points are given. It is allowed to replace a pair of given points $A, B$ with a pair of new points $C, D$, if segments $A C$ and $B D$ have the same length, are perpendicular to $A B$, and points $C$ and $D$ lie on opposite sides of the line $A B$. Prove that after a series of such operations, we cannot return to the original set of points.

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an arithmetic progression with first term $m$ and difference 2 is equal to the sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric progression with first term $n$ and ratio 2 .

a) Prove that $m+n=2^{m}$;

b) Find $m$ and $n$, if the third term of the geometric progression is equal to the 23 -rd term of the arithmetic progression.

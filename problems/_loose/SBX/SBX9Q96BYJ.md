(26th Russian Olympiad, 2000) In a $2 n \times 2 n$ grid, some cells contain black or white go stones, with at most one stone per cell. First, remove all black stones that are in the same column as any white stone; then, remove all white stones that are in the same row as any remaining black stone. Prove that in the grid, the remaining stones are such that either the number of black stones is no more than $n^{2}$, or the number of white stones is no more than $n^{2}$.

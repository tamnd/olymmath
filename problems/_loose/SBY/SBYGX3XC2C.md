Aladdin traversed every point on the equator, moving westward or eastward along the equator at times, and at other times teleporting himself to the antipodal point on Earth. During his journey, he moved westward for a maximum of $19000 \mathrm{~km}$. Prove that there was a time when the difference in the lengths of the paths he traveled eastward and westward was at least half the length of the equator.

You are at one vertex of a equilateral triangle with side length $ 1 $ . All of the edges of the equilateral triangle will reﬂect the laser beam perfectly (angle of incidence is equal to angle of reﬂection). Given that the laser beam bounces oﬀ exactly $ 137 $ edges and returns to the original vertex without touching any other vertices, let $ M $ be the maximum possible distance the beam could have traveled, and $ m $ be the minimum possible distance the beam could have traveled. Find $ M^2 - m^2 $ .

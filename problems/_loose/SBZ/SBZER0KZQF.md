A racing turtle moves in a straight line as follows. In the first segment of the path, which measures $\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{~m}$, she runs at a speed of $3 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. In the second segment, which measures $\frac{1}{3} \mathrm{~m}$, she runs at a speed of $4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. In the third segment, which measures $\frac{1}{4} \mathrm{~m}$, she runs at a speed of $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$, and so on.

a) How long does it take the turtle to cover the first segment? Write the result as the difference of two unit fractions, that is, fractions with a numerator equal to 1.

b) Do the same with respect to the second segment.

c) Calculate the time it takes the turtle to cover the first 2013 segments.

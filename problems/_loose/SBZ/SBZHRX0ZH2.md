Let $a_{k}=\sqrt{4 n^{4}+k}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ and $a=\sum_{k=1}^{8 n^{2}}\left[a_{k}\right]$, where $[x]$ represents the integer part of the real number $x$.

a) Show that $a$ cannot be a perfect square for all $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$;

b) If $4 n^{2}+1<2013<8 n^{2}$ and $a=\sum_{k=1}^{2013}\left[a_{k}\right]$, find $n$ such that

$$
a-2013=2011 \cdot 800
$$

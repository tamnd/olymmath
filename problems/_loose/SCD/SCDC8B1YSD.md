On a magic apple tree, 15 bananas and 20 oranges have grown. It is allowed to pick one or two fruits at a time. If you pick one fruit, the same one will grow back. If you pick two identical fruits, an orange will grow back. If you pick two different fruits, a banana will grow back.

a) In what order should the fruits be picked so that only one fruit remains on the tree?

b) Can you determine what this fruit will be?

c) Is it possible to pick the fruits in such a way that nothing remains on the tree?

Given
$$
\begin{array}{l}
M=\left\{(x, y) \mid y \geqslant x^{2}\right\}, \\
N=\left\{(x, y) \mid x^{2}+(y-a)^{2} \leqslant 1\right\} .
\end{array}
$$

Then, the necessary and sufficient condition for $M \cap N=N$ to hold is:
(A) $a \geqslant 1 \frac{1}{4}$;
(B) $a=1 \frac{1}{4}$;
(C) $a \geqslant 1$;
(D) $0<a<1$.

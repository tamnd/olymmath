A convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is given. Let $O_a$ be the circumcenter of the triangle $DBC$ , and define $O_b,O_c$ and $O_d$ similarly. The points $O_a, O_b, O_c, O_d$ are the vertices of a convex quadrilateral. Prove that its area is equal to half of the absolute value of the difference between the areas of $AO_bCO_d$ and $BO_cDO_a$ .

*Proposed by V. Dubrovsky*

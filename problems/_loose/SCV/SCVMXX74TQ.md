(10 points) In a $3 \times 5$ table, all numbers from 1 to 15 were arranged such that the sum of the numbers in any column of three cells is divisible by 3. Then some of them were erased and replaced with numbers $a, b, c$:

| 11 | $a$ | 3 | 8 | 10 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 7 | 9 | 13 | 15 | 12 |
| $c$ | 2 | 5 | 4 | $b$ |

Which of the following statements is true? a) $a=1, b=14$; b) $b=14, c=12$; c) $a=4$, $c=6$; d) $a=14, b=6$; e) none of the options are correct.

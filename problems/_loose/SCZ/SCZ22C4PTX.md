The midpoint of each side of the base of a quadrilateral pyramid is connected by a segment to the point of intersection of the medians of the opposite lateral face. Prove that: a) these segments intersect and are divided by the point of their intersection in the ratio $3: 2$, counting from the side of the base; b) the midpoints of these segments are the vertices of a parallelogram. c) Also find the ratio of the area of this parallelogram to the area of the base of the pyramid.

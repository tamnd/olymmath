Show that we get equal remainders when dividing $a$ and $1000a$ by 111.

Based on this, prove that the numbers

$$
A=10^{6 n+2}+10^{3 n+1}+1 \text { and } B=10^{6 n+4}+10^{3 n+2}+1
$$

are divisible by 111, and that

$$
C=10^{6 n}+10^{3 n}+1
$$

is three times a multiple of 111; $n$ is any integer.

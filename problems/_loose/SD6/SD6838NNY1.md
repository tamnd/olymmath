$18$ teams participated in a football tournament. Each team played two matches against each other, one at home and one away. $3$ points were awarded for a win, $1$ point for a draw, and $0$ points for a loss. At the end of the tournament, all teams had the same number of points. Prove that at least two teams, playing away from home, achieved the same number of victories (or perhaps none).

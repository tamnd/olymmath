Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be a differentiable function, while $f'$ is continuous on $[0,1]$ and $|f'(x)|\le1$, $(\forall)x\in[0,1]$. If
$$2\left|\int^1_0f(x)dx\right|\le1$$
Show that:
$$(n+2)\left|\int^1_0x^nf(x)dx\right|\le1,~(\forall)x\ge1$$

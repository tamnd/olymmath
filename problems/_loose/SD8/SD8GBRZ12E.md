The exponential reliability law refers to the reliability function defined by the equation $R(t)=\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda t}$, where the positive number $\lambda$ is the failure intensity. Prove the characteristic property of the exponential reliability law: the probability of fault-free operation of an element over a time interval of duration $t$ does not depend on the time of previous operation up to the beginning of the considered interval, but depends only on the duration of the interval $t$ (given the failure intensity $\lambda$).

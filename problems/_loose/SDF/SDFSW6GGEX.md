If $x$ is a real number, let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer not greater than $\mathrm{x}$. (For example, $[3.7]=3,[-3.7]=-4,[4]=4$.)

Determine all real numbers $\mathrm{x}$ for which:

$$
\left[\frac{10+3 x}{6}\right]=\frac{5 x+3}{7}
$$

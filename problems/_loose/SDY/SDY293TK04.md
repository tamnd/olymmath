Let the circumcircle $\odot O$ and the incircle $\odot I$ of $\triangle ABC$ have radii $R, r$ respectively, and $AB \neq BC$. Prove:
(1) $IB=2 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{R \cdot r \cdot \cot \frac{B}{2}}{\cot \frac{A}{2}+\cot \frac{C}{2}}}$;
(2) $\angle BOI \neq \frac{\pi}{2}$ if and only if: $\left(\angle BOI-\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \cdot\left(\cot \frac{A}{2} \cdot \cot \frac{C}{2}-\frac{R+r}{R-r}\right)<0$.

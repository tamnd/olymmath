Given numbers $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ and $k$. Let $x, y, z$ be the distances from a point $M$ inside a triangle to its sides. Prove that the geometric locus of points $M$ such that $\alpha x + \beta y + \gamma z = k$ is either empty, a segment, or coincides with the set of all points of the triangle.

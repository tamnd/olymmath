During the World Cup there are $n$ different Panini stickers to collect. Marco's friends are all trying to complete their collections, but no one has all the stickers yet! We'll call a pair of two of his friends complete if their combined collection contains every sticker at least once. Marco knows who has which stickers and wants to invite all his friends to a restaurant for his birthday. However, he wants to prevent a complete pair from sitting at the same table.

(i) Show that Marco may need to reserve at least $n$ tables.

(ii) Show that $n$ tables are always sufficient to achieve Marco's goal.

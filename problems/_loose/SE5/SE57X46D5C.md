Let $A$ and $B$ be the two intersection points of circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$. Draw a line through $A$ that intersects circle $O_{1}$ and circle $O_{2}$ at points $C$ and $D$, respectively. Draw the tangents to circles $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ at points $C$ and $D$, respectively, and draw perpendiculars from point $B$ to these tangents, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $P$ and $Q$. Prove that $PQ$ is a tangent to the circle with diameter $AB$.

Let $p \geqslant 5$ be a prime and $S = \left\{ 1, 2, \ldots, p \right\}$ . Define $r(x,y)$ as follows: $$ r(x,y) = \begin{cases} y - x & y \geqslant x y - x + p & y < x \end{cases}.$$
For a nonempty proper subset $A$ of $S$ , let $$ f(A) = \sum_{x \in A} \sum_{y \in A} \left( r(x,y) \right)^2. $$ A *good* subset of $S$ is a nonempty proper subset $A$ satisfying that for all subsets $B \subseteq S$ of the same size as $A$ , $f(B) \geqslant f(A)$ . Find the largest integer $L$ such that there exists distinct good subsets $A_1 \subseteq A_2 \subseteq \ldots \subseteq A_L$ .

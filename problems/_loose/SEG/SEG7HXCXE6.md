Let $S=\{1, 2, 3, \dots 2021\}$ and $f:S \to S$ be a function such that $f^{(n)}(n)=n$ for each $n \in S$.
Find all possible values for $f(2021)$.
(Here, $f^{(n)}(n) = \underbrace{f(f(f\dots f(}_{n \text{ times} }n)))\dots))$.)

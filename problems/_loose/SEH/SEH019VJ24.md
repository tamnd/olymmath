Given $A>1, B>1$ and a sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ consisting of numbers from the interval $[1, A B]$. Prove: There exists a sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ consisting of numbers from the interval $[1, A]$, such that for any $m, n \in N$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{m}}{a_{n}} \leqslant B \frac{b_{m}}{b_{n}} .
$$

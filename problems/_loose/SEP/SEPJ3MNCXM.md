Let $p$ be a prime number. Suppose we are given $r$ natural numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{r}$, all less than $p$. Prove that if $r<p$, then from these numbers, one can form at least $r+1$ sums that give different remainders when divided by $p$ (the sum of the "empty set" of addends, which is considered to be zero, is allowed).

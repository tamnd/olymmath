Let $O, H$ be the circumcenter and orthocenter of an acute $\triangle ABC$, respectively. $CD \perp AB$ at point $D$. The line through $D$ and perpendicular to $OD$ intersects $BC$ at point $E$. The circumcircle of $\triangle B C H$ intersects $AB$ at a second point $F$. Prove that $E, F, H$ are collinear. ${ }^{[6]}$
(2011, Slovenia National Team Selection Exam)

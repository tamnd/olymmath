Let the two foci of the ellipse $C$ be $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$, and the two directrices be $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$. Draw a line through a point $P$ on the ellipse parallel to $F_{1} F_{2}$, intersecting $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ at points $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$, respectively. The line $M_{1} F_{1}$ intersects $M_{2} F_{2}$ at point $Q$. Prove: $P$, $F_{1}$, $Q$, and $F_{2}$ are concyclic.

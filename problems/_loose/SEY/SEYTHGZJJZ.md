Let $\overline{A D}$ be the altitude of the acute-angled triangle $A B C$. On the line $A D$ there are distinct points $E$ and $F$ such that $|D E|=|D F|$ and point $E$ is inside the triangle $A B C$. The circumcircle of triangle $B E F$ intersects segments $\overline{B C}$ and $\overline{A B}$ at points $K$ and $M$, respectively. The circumcircle of triangle $C E F$ intersects segments $\overline{B C}$ and $\overline{C A}$ at points $L$ and $N$, respectively.

Prove that the lines $A D, K M$ and $L N$ intersect at one point.

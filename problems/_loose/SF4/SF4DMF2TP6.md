Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be positive real numbers such that $a + b + c = 1$. Prove that $$\sqrt[3]{\left (\frac{1+a}{b+c}\right )^{\frac{1-a}{bc}}\left (\frac{1+b}{c+a}\right )^{\frac{1-b}{ca}}\left (\frac{1+c}{a+b}\right )^{\frac{1-c}{ab}}} \geq 64 $$

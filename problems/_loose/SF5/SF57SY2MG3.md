In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, it is known that the ellipse $\Gamma: \frac{x^{2}}{6}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$. Through the point $P(2,2)$, draw lines $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$, which intersect the ellipse $\Gamma$ at points $A$ and $B$, $C$ and $D$, respectively, and the slopes of lines $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ are opposite numbers.
(1) Prove: $P A \cdot P B = P C \cdot P D$;
(2) Let the slopes of lines $A C$ and $B D$ be $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$, respectively. Prove: $k_{1} + k_{2}$ is a constant.

Let the parabola $y=x^{2}+k x+4$ intersect the $x$-axis at two different points $\left(x_{1}\right.$, $0),\left(x_{2}, 0\right)$. Then which of the following conclusions must be true?
(A) $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}=17$.
(B) $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}=8$.
(C) $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}>8$.
(D) $x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}<17$.
(Chinese Jiangsu Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

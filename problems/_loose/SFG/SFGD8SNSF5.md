Given a positive integer $N$, let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be $n$ distinct positive integers less than $N$. Let the least common multiple of these $n$ numbers be $M$, and their greatest common divisor be $m$. It is known that $M \mid m^{2}$. Prove: $n^{3}<4 N$.

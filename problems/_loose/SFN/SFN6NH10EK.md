For a positive integer $n$, does there exist a permutation of all its positive integer divisors $(d_1 , d_2 , \ldots, d_k)$ such that the equation $d_kx^{k-1} + \ldots + d_2x + d_1 = 0$ has a rational root, if:

a) $n = 2024$;
b) $n = 2025$?

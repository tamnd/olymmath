Let $A=\left(\begin{array}{cc}m & m^{2} \\ -1 & -m\end{array}\right), m \in \mathbb{R}^{*}$.

a) Show that the matrix $I_{2}+A$ is invertible and its inverse is $I_{2}-A$.

b) Show that there does not exist a matrix $X \in \mathcal{M}_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ such that $X^{3}=A$.

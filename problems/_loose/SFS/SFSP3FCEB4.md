Consider the trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB \| CD, AB > CD$ and $AC \perp BD$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of diagonal [AC]. The line parallel to $BD$ through $E$ intersects $AB$ at $M$. Prove that:
a) $\triangle AMC$ is isosceles;
b) $ME = \frac{BD}{2}$ and $CM = \frac{AB + CD}{2}$.

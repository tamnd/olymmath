In graph $G$ there are $e$ edges, and the degrees of $n$ vertices are $d_{1}, d_{2}, \cdots, d_{n}$, respectively. An integer $k$ is less than $\min \left\{d_{1}, d_{2}, \cdots, d_{n}\right\}$. Prove: Graph $G$ contains an induced subgraph $H$ (i.e., if two vertices in $H$ are connected by an edge in graph $G$, then they are also connected by an edge in $H$), such that graph $H$ does not contain $K_{k+1}$ (i.e., a complete graph with $k+1$ vertices where every pair of vertices is connected by an edge), and graph $H$ has at least $\frac{k n^{2}}{2 e+n}$ vertices.

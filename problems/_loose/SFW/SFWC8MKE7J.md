1-118 Does there exist real numbers $a, b$ such that:
(1) $a+b$ is a rational number, and for every natural number $n \geqslant 2, a^{n}+b^{n}$ is irrational?
(2) $a+b$ is irrational, and for every natural number $n \geqslant 2, a^{n}+b^{n}$ is rational?

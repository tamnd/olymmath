The following example is from the time of the Bolyais; it is taken from Ferencz Pethe's work "Mathesis" (Béts 1812) and we publish it in its original wording and spelling:

Three Bombardiers threw the bomb from three mortars onto a city for a whole day. The first two together threw 26 more than the third; the last two 38 more than the first two; the first and last 24 more than the middle one: how many did each throw then?

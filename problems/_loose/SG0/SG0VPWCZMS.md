This task is, in a sense, "dual" to the previous one. It is known that the criminal is not a normal person, you are not guilty, but not a knight. Suppose that for some reason you are not averse to acquiring the reputation of a liar or a normal person, but you hold knights in contempt. Could you convince the jury of your innocence and that you are not a knight with a single statement?

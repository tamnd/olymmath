Given a circle centered at the origin with radius $a$. Let the intersection points of the circle with the $x$-axis be $A, A^{\prime}$, and the moving chord perpendicular to the diameter $A A^{\prime}$ be $Q Q^{\prime}$. Let the intersection point of $Q Q^{\prime}$ with the $x$-axis be $R$, and the midpoints of $R Q, R Q^{\prime}$ be $M, M^{\prime}$, respectively. Try to find the locus of the intersection point $P$ of the two lines $A^{\prime} M$ and $A M^{\prime}$.

If the blocks are known as
$$
\begin{array}{l}
B_{1}: 2,4,6,7 ; B_{2}: 1,3,6,7 ; B_{2: 3,1,5,7 ;} B_{4}: 1,2,5,7: \\
B_{:}: 2,3,5,6 ; B_{6}: 1,4,5,6 ; B_{7}: 1,2,3,4 \text {. } \\
\end{array}
$$

Try to find $A A^{\top}$, where $A$ is the block matrix of the given blocks.

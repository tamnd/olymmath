A point moves along the x-axis according to the law $x=2 t^{3}-4 t^{2}+2 t+3$, where $t$ is time in seconds measured from $t=0$, and $x$ is the distance of the moving point from the origin in meters. It is required: a) to determine the law of variation of velocity and acceleration with time $t$; b) to find the initial velocity and the velocity at the moment $t=3$ s; c) to determine whether there are moments of time when the velocity is zero, and if so, what positions of the moving point correspond to these moments.

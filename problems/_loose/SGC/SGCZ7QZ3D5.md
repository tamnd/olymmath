Given that $\odot O$ is tangent to line segment $AB$ at point $M$, and is tangent to the semicircle with diameter $AB$ at point $E$, $CD \perp AB$ at point $D$, $CD$ intersects the semicircle with diameter $AB$ at point $C$, and is tangent to $\odot O$ at point $F$. Connect $AC, CM$. Prove:
(1) $A, F, E$ are collinear;
(2) $AC = AM$;
(3) $MC^2 = 2MD \cdot MA$.

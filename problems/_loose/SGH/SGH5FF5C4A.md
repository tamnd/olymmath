Given an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ and let $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ be its angles respectively to $A, B$ and $C$. For an arbitrary interior point $M$ denote by $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively the feet of the perpendiculars from $M$ to $B C, C A$ and $A B$. Find the locus of $M$ for which the triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is a right angle triangle.

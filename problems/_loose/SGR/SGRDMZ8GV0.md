Let $p$ be an odd prime number, and $q$ be a natural number not divisible by $p$. For each natural number $l$ from 1 to $\frac{p-1}{2}$, write $l q \equiv \pm r_{l}(\bmod p)$, where $1 \leqslant r_{l} \leqslant \frac{p-1}{2}$. Let $\mu$ be the number of all minus signs encountered here. Prove that $\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)=(-1)^{\mu}$ (Gauss).

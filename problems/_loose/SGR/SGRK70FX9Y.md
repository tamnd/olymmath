In the same half-plane bounded by a line d, isosceles congruent triangles $A B M$ and $C D N$ are constructed with bases $[A B]$, respectively [CD], where $A, B, C, D$ belong to the line d in this order. If $A N \cap M D=\{E\}, M B \cap N C=\{F\}, A N \cap M B=\{P\}, M D \cap N C=\{Q\}$, prove that $A P=D Q$ and $\mathrm{EF} \perp$ d. (The theorem can be used: A triangle is isosceles if and only if the angles at the base are congruent).

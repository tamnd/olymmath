We draw two lines through a point on the angle bisector. One line intersects the sides of the angle at segments of lengths $a$ and $b$ from the vertex, while the other intersects at segments of lengths $a^{\prime}$ and $b^{\prime}$. It is to be proven that

$$
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=\frac{1}{a^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{b^{\prime}}
$$

For the real numbers $a_{\circ}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$, it holds that $a_{\circ}=a_{n}=0$ and

$$
a_{k-1}-2 a_{k}+a_{k+1} \geq 0, \text { for } k=1,2,3, \ldots, n-1
$$

Prove that none of the numbers $a_{\circ}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ is positive.

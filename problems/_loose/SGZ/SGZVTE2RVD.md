Let $S$ be a sequence $n_{1}, n_{2}, \ldots, n_{1995}$ of positive integers such that $n_{1}+\cdots+n_{1995}=$ $m<3990$. Prove that for each integer $q$ with $1 \leq q \leq m$, there is a sequence $n_{i_{1}}, n_{i_{2}}, \ldots, n_{i_{k}}$, where $1 \leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{k} \leq 1995, n_{i_{1}}+\cdots+n_{i_{k}}=q$ and $k$ depends on $q$.

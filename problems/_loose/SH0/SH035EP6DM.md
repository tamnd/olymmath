Prove the inequality

$$
\sqrt{\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)^{2}+\left(b_{1}+b_{2}+\ldots+b_{n}\right)^{2}} \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{i}^{2}+b_{i}^{2}}
$$

where $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{n} \in \mathbb{R}$.

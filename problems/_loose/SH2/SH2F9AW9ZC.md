(ELMO 2014 SL G4) Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle $\omega$. The tangent to the circle $\omega$ at point $A$ intersects the line $(C D)$ at point $E$. The tangent to the circle $\omega$ at point $A$ intersects the line $(B C)$ at point $F$. The line $(B E)$ intersects the circle $\omega$ again at point $G$. The lines $(B E)$ and $(A D)$ intersect at point $H$. The line $(D F)$ intersects the circle $\omega$ again at point $I$ and the lines $(D F)$ and $(A B)$ intersect at point $J$. Show that the lines $(G I),(H J)$ and the symmedian from vertex $B$ in the triangle $A B C$ are concurrent.

Prove that for all positive $x$ the inequality

$$
\left(1+x+x^{2}\right) \cdot\left(1+x+x^{2}+x^{3}+x^{4}\right) \leqslant\left(1+x+x^{2}+x^{3}\right)^{2}
$$

holds.

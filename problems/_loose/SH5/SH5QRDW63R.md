Initially, there are three cards in the box, each with the numbers $1, 2, 3$ written on them. Each time, two cards are taken out of the box, the sum of the numbers on them is written on another blank card, and one card is put back into the box. After 5 such operations, except for the last card with the written number, all other cards have been taken out at least once and no more than twice. What is the largest number on the card in the box at this point?

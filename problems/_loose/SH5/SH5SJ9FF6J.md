Think about the following questions using the convexity of the function $f(x)=\lg x$ to prove:
(1) For $a_{i}>0$, we have $\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}}{n} \geqslant \sqrt[n]{a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}}$.
(2) For $a_{i}>0, x_{i}>0$. We have
$$
\frac{\alpha_{1} a_{1}+\alpha_{2} a_{2}+\cdots+\alpha_{n} a_{n}}{\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\cdots+\alpha_{n}} \geqslant\left(a_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} a_{2}^{\alpha_{2}} \cdots a_{n}^{\alpha_{n}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\cdots+\alpha_{n}}}.
$$

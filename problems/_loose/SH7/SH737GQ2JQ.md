In the right-angled triangle $A B_{0} C_{0}$, which is right-angled at $B_{0}$. From $B_{0}$, we draw a perpendicular to $A C_{0}$; from the foot of this perpendicular, $C_{1}$, we draw a perpendicular to $A B_{0}$, and from the foot of this perpendicular, $B_{1}$, we again draw a perpendicular to $A C_{0}$, and so on, indefinitely. The feet of the perpendiculars drawn to $A C_{0}$ and $A B_{0}$ are $C_{1}, C_{2}, \ldots C_{i}, \ldots$, and $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots B_{i}, \ldots$ respectively. The following sums are to be calculated (from $i=0$ to $i=\infty$):

$1^{0} . \sum B_{i} C_{i} ; 2^{0} \cdot \sum A B_{i} ; 3^{0} \cdot \sum A C_{i} ; 4^{0} \cdot \sum C_{i+1} B_{i}$.

In the Cartesian coordinate plane, take the $3 \times 7$ region $D=\{(x, y) 1 \leqslant x \leqslant 3,1 \leqslant y \leqslant 7\}$, and color the integer points on $D$ (including the boundary) with red and blue, each point being colored one and only one color. Prove: regardless of how the coloring is done, there always exists a rectangle on region $D$ whose vertices are integer points of the same color, and whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axes.

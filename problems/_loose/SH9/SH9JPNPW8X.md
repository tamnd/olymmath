A cube-shaped cake is completely covered with chocolate, including the bottom. We want to share the cake among $K$ people so that everyone gets the same amount of dough and the same amount of chocolate coating. We want to achieve this by dividing the cake into $N \times N \times N$ identical small cubes, and giving each person the same number of pieces, ensuring that the total area of the chocolate-coated sides of the pieces received by each person is the same. Is this possible for any $K$? What is the minimum number of pieces the cake must be divided into if $K=1997$?

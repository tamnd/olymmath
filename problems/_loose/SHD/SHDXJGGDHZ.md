Find all functions $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that for all real numbers $a, b, c$ , we have
$$
f(a+b+c)f(ab+bc+ca) - f(a)f(b)f(c) = f(a+b)f(b+c)f(c+a).
$$

Let $\mathbf{B}$ be $\mathbf{N}=\{1,2,3, \cdots\}$, argue whether there exists a function $h: \mathbf{N} \rightarrow \mathbf{N}$, such that
(1) $h(1)=2$;
(2) $h(h(n))=h(n)+n$, for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$;
(3) $h(n)<h(n+1)$, for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$.

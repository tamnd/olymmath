Find a combinatorial interpretation of the terms of the sequence $\left(u_{n}\right)_{\mathfrak{n} \in \mathbb{Z}}$ satisfying $u_{n}=0$ if $n<0, u_{0}=1$ and, for $n \geqslant 1$,

$$
u_{n}=c_{1} u_{n-1}+c_{2} u_{n-2}+\ldots+c_{k} u_{n-k}
$$

where the $c_{i}$ are positive integers.

For a finish, a small example of an olympic-type exercise involving double counting.

Three equal circles $S 1, S 2, S 3$ touch each other pairwise, and a circle $S$ is described around them, touching all three. Prove that for any point $M$ on the circle $S$, the tangent drawn from point $M$ to one of the three circles $S 1, S 2, S 3$ is equal to the sum of the tangents drawn from point $M$ to the other two circles.

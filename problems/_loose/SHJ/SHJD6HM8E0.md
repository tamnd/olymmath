Let $\triangle A B C$ be such that $A B=A C$, and let $C D \perp A B$ at $D$. Suppose the midpoint of $C D$ is $M$, and let $A E \perp B M$ at point $E$. Also, let $A F \perp C E$ at point $F$. Prove that $A F \leqslant \frac{1}{3} A B$.

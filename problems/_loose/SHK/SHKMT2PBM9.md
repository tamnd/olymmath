Find all values of $a$, for each of which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
|y|+|y-x| \leqslant a-|x-1| \\
(y-4)(y+3) \geqslant(4-x)(3+x)
\end{array}\right.
$$

has exactly two solutions.

From an arbitrary point $P$, not lying on the circumcircle, perpendiculars $P A_{1}, P B_{1}, P C_{1}$ are dropped to the sides of triangle $A B C$ or their extensions. It is known that $A B=c, B C=a, A C=b, P A=x, P B=y, P C=z$. Find the sides of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, if the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ is $R$.

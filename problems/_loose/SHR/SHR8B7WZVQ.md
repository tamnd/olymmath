Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral. The bisector of the angle $ACD$ intersects $BD$ at point $E$. It is known that $\angle CAD = \angle BCE= 90^o$. Prove that the $AC$ is the bisector of the angle $BAE$ .

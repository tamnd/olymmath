Let $e$ and $f$ be lines, and $S$ and $T$ be planes, about which we know the following: $S$ is perpendicular to $T$,

taking the orthogonal projections of lines $e$ and $f$ onto plane $S$, these projections are perpendicular to each other, and similarly, their projections onto plane $T$ are also perpendicular to each other.

Show that the angle between lines $e$ and $f$ deviates from a right angle by less than $20^{\circ}$.

(von Aubel's Theorem) Construct four squares $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$ outwardly on the sides of a closed broken line $A B C D$, with their centers being $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}, O_{4}$ respectively. Prove:
$$
O_{1} O_{3} \perp \mathrm{O}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{4}, \mathrm{O}_{1} \mathrm{O}_{3}=\mathrm{O}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{4} \text {. }
$$

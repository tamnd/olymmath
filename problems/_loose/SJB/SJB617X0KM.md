For a set $T$ of numbers, we define $S(T)$ to be the sum of the elements of $T$ . We take S(∅)=0. As usual, |T| denotes the number of elements of $T$ . Let $A$ be the set of positive integers less than 1000 whose digits consist of only 1s or 3s (or both). We choose a random subset $B$ of $A$ . The probability that $S(B)$ and $|B|$ end in the same digit can be expressed in the form $a/b$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $a+b$ ?

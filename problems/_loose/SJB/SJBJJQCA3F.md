9 Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be non-negative real numbers, $c_{k}=$ $\prod_{i=1}^{k} b_{i}^{1 / k}, 1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$, prove:
$$
n c_{n}+\sum_{k=1}^{n} k\left(a_{k}-1\right) c_{k} \leqslant \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}^{k} b_{k} .
$$

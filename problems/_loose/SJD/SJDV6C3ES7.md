(16th Irish Mathematical Olympiad) Let $T$ be a triangle with perimeter 2, and let $a, b, c$ be the lengths of the sides of $T$. Prove:
(1) $a b c + \frac{28}{27} \geqslant a b + b c + c a$;
(2) $a b + b c + c a \geqslant a b c + 1$.

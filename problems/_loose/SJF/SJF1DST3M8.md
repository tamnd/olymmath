Let's assume that humanity does not die out indefinitely, but the age of people always remains finite and everyone has a finite number of children. Prove that under these conditions, there will be an infinite sequence of people $E_{0}, E_{1}, E_{2}$, ..., where $E_{0}$ is some person living today, and $E_{n+1}$ is the child of $E_{n}$ for every $n \geqq 0$.

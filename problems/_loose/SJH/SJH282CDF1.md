A wise cockroach, which can see no further than 1 cm, decided to find the Truth. The Truth is located at a point that is $D$ cm away. The cockroach can take steps, each no longer than 1 cm, and after each step, it is told whether it has gotten closer to the Truth or not. The cockroach can remember everything, including the direction of its steps. Prove that it can find the Truth in no more than $\frac{3}{2} D+7$ steps.

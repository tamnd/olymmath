Prove that for no vectors $a, b, c$ can the three inequalities

$$
\sqrt{3}^{|a|<|b-c|,} \sqrt{3}^{|b|<|c-a|,} \sqrt{3}|c|<|a-b|
$$

hold simultaneously.

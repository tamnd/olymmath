Given any $\triangle A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$ with centroid $G$, and $M$ is any point inside $\triangle A_{1} A_{2} A_{3}$. The line $M G$ intersects the opposite sides (or their extensions) of $\angle A_{1}, \angle A_{2}, \angle A_{3}$ at points $B_{1}, B_{2}, B_{3}$, and $M B_{i}=m_{i}$, $G B_{i}=g_{i}(i=1,2,3)$. Prove: Among the three ratios $\frac{m_{1}}{g_{1}}, \frac{m_{2}}{g_{2}}, \frac{m_{3}}{g_{3}}$, at least one is not less than 1, and at least one is not greater than 1.

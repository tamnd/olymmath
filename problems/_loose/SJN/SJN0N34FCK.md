Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, and let $C'$ be the image point of $C$ under reflection across $AB$.

For any point $P$ on $AB$ between $A$ and $B$, let $Q$ be on $BC$ and $R$ be on $CA$ such that $PQCR$ is a parallelogram.

Then let $X$ be the point of intersection of the circumcircles of the triangles $APR$ and $BPQ$ different from $P$. Prove: The set of all such points $X$ coincides with the arc inside the triangle $ABC$ of the circumcircle of the triangle $ABC'$.

Let the complex coefficient polynomial $f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n} a_{k} x^{k}\left(a_{0} a_{n} \neq 0\right)$ have all roots with modulus less than 1. Prove that the polynomial $g(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(a_{k}+\lambda \overline{a_{n-k}}\right) x^{k}$ has all roots with modulus equal to 1, where $|\lambda|=1$.

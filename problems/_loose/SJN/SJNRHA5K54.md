16 (20 points) Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be a positive, increasing arithmetic sequence. Prove:
(1) For any $k, l \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, when $l>k \geqslant 2$, $\frac{a_{l+1}}{a_{k+1}}<\frac{a_{l}}{a_{k}}<\frac{a_{l-1}}{a_{k-1}}$;
(2) For any $k \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, when $k \geqslant 2$,
$$
\sqrt[k]{\frac{a_{2013 k+1}}{a_{k+1}}}<\frac{a_{k+2}}{a_{k+1}} \cdot \frac{a_{2 k+2}}{a_{2 k+1}} \cdot \frac{a_{3 k+2}}{a_{3 k+1}} \cdots \cdots \cdot \frac{a_{2012 k+2}}{a_{2012 k+1}}<\sqrt[k]{\frac{a_{2012 k+2}}{a_{2}}} .
$$

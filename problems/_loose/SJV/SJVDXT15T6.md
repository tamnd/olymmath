Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$ be real numbers and

$$
4\left(b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}\right)=a_{1} a_{2}+a_{2} a_{3}+\cdots+a_{n} a_{1}
$$

Prove that among the equations $x^{2}+a_{k} x+b_{k}=0$ $(k=1,2, \ldots, n)$, at least one has a real root.

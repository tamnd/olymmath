Let $n \geqslant 3, \omega=\cos \frac{2 \pi}{n}+\mathrm{i} \sin \frac{2 \pi}{n}$ be an $n$-th root of unity, and let $x_{i}(i=0,1, \cdots, n-1)$ be real numbers, with $x_{0} \geqslant x_{1} \geqslant \cdots \geqslant$ $x_{n-1}$. Find the necessary and sufficient conditions that $x_{0}, x_{1}, \cdots, x_{n-1}$ must satisfy for the following equation to hold: $x_{0}+x_{1} \omega+x_{2} \omega^{2}+\cdots+x_{n-1} \omega^{n-1}=0$.

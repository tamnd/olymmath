$\triangle B C D$'s incircle is tangent to its sides $A B, B C$, (I), AI) at points $K, L, M, N$. The external angle bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ intersect at point $K^{\prime}$. The external angle bisectors of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ intersect at point $L^{\prime}$, the external angle bisectors of $\angle C$ and $\left.\angle I\right)$ intersect at point $M^{\prime}$, and the external angle bisectors of $\angle D$ and $\angle A$ intersect at point $N^{\prime}$. Prove: The lines $K K^{\prime}, L L^{\prime}, M M^{\prime}, \mathrm{VN}^{\prime}$ pass through the same point.

A circle is circumscribed around an acute-angled triangle $A B C$. Points $A 1, B 1$, and $C 1$ are taken on its smaller arcs $B C, A C$, and $A B$ respectively. Points $A 2, B 2$, and $C 2$ are the orthocenters of triangles $B A 1 C, A B 1 C$, and $A C 1 B$ respectively. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $B A 2 C, A B 2 C$, and $A C 2 B$ intersect at one point.

Let's draw a chessboard on the plane. Let the centers of the white squares be $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{32}$, the centers of the black squares be $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots, B_{32}$, and $P$ be any point on the chessboard. Prove that

$$
A_{1} P^{2}+A_{2} P^{2}+\cdots+A_{32} P^{2}=B_{1} P^{2}+B_{2} P^{2}+\cdots+B_{32} P^{2} .(\mathbf{H})
$$

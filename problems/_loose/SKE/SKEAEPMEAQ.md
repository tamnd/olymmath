Given quadratic trinomials $f_{1}(x)=x^{2}-a x-3, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}+2 x-b, f_{3}(x)=3 x^{2}+(2-2 a) x-6-b$ and $f_{4}(x)=3 x^{2}+(4-a) x-3-2 b$. Let the differences of their roots be $A, B, C$ and $D$ respectively. It is known that $|C| \neq|D|$. Find the ratio $\frac{A^{2}-B^{2}}{C^{2}-D^{2}}$. The values of $A, B, C, D, a, b$ are not given.

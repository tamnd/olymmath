(MS, 66). On the edges $A B, A C, A D$ of a given tetrahedron $A B C D$, for each value $n \in \mathbf{N}$, points $K_{n}, L_{n}, M_{n}$ are chosen respectively such that

$$
A B=n A K_{n}, A C=(n+1) A L_{n}, A D=(n+2) A M_{n}
$$

Prove that all planes $K_{n} L_{n} M_{n}$ pass through the same line.

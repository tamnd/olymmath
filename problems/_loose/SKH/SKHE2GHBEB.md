In a plane, there exist $n(n \geq 3)$ points with no three points being collinear. The line segments formed by connecting these points in pairs are colored either red or blue. Prove: There exist $\mathrm{n}-1$ line segments of the same color, such that they cannot divide the plane into more than one region, and they do not intersect each other except at their endpoints.

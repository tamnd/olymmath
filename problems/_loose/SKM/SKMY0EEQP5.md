The system of inequalities $a_{1} x+b_{1} \geq 0, a_{1}>0 ; a_{2} x+b_{2} \geq 0, a_{2}<0$, has no solution. Prove that there exist numbers $c_{1}$ and $c_{2}$, such that

$$
b_{1} c_{1}+b_{2} c_{2}<0 \text { and } a_{1} c_{1}+a_{2} c_{2}=0
$$

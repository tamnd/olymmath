The 59th question: Find the largest real number $a$ such that for all positive integers $n$ and all real numbers $x_{0}, x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ $\left(0=x_{0}<x_{1}<\ldots<x_{n}\right)$, we have $\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \frac{1}{\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}-\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}-1}} \geq \mathrm{a} \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \frac{\mathrm{i}+1}{\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}}$.

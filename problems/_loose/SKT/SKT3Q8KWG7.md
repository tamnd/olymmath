In a plane, we take a point $C$ and two intersecting lines $a$ and $b$ at point $D$. If a line passing through point $C$ intersects $a$ and $b$ at points $A$ and $B$, and the feet of the perpendiculars from these points to the line $DC$ are $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$, then

$$
\frac{1}{A A_{1}}-\frac{1}{B B_{1}}
$$

is a constant, regardless of how we draw the line $CAB$ through point $C$. The segments $A A_{1}$ and $B B_{1}$ have opposite signs if they are separated by the line $DC$, and the same sign if they are not separated.

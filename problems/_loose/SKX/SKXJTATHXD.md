a) Calculate $\int\left[\frac{x}{(x+1)^{2}} \sin x+\frac{x+2}{(x+1)^{2}} \cos x\right] \mathrm{d} x, x \in(-1, \infty)$.

b) Calculate $\int\left[\frac{x}{(x+1)^{2}} f(x)+\frac{x+2}{(x+1)^{2}} g(x)\right] \mathrm{d} x, x \in(-1, \infty)$, where $f, g:(-1, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ are two differentiable functions with $f^{\prime}(x)=g(x)$ and $g^{\prime}(x)=-f(x), \forall x \in(-1, \infty)$.

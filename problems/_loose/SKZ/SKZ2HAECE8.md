In the plane, a set $ M $ of points is given with the following properties:
1. The points of set $ M $ do not lie on a single straight line,
2. If points $ A, B, C, D $ are vertices of a parallelogram and $ A, B, C \in M $, then $ D \in M $,
3. If $ A, B \in M $, then $ AB \geq 1 $.
Prove that there exist two families of parallel lines such that $ M $ is the set of all intersection points of the lines from the first family with the lines from the second family.

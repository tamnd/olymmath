(2002 National Girls' Mathematical Olympiad) A summer camp has $3n$ (where $n$ is a positive integer) female participants, and every day 3 of them are on duty. By the end of the summer camp, it is found that any two of these $3n$ participants have been on duty together exactly once.
（1）Question: When $n=3$, does there exist an arrangement that satisfies the conditions? Prove your conclusion.
（2）Prove: $n$ is an odd number.

Let $r, s, n$ be positive integers, and $r+s=n$. Prove that $A=\left\{\left[\frac{n}{r}\right],\left[\frac{2 n}{r}\right], \cdots,\left[\frac{(r-1) n}{r}\right]\right\}$ and $B$ $=\left\{\left[\frac{n}{s}\right],\left[\frac{2 n}{s}\right], \cdots,\left[\frac{(s-1) n}{s}\right]\right\}$ form a partition of $M=\{1,2, \cdots, n-2\}$ if and only if $r$ and $s$ are both coprime with $n$.

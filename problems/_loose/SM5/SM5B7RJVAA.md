a) Prove that inside triangle $ABC$ there exists a point $P$ such that $\angle ABP = \angle CAP = \angle BCP$.

b) On the sides of triangle $ABC$, similar triangles $CA_1B$, $CAB_1$, and $C_1AB$ are constructed externally (the angles at the first vertices of all four triangles are equal, etc.). Prove that the lines $AA_1$, $BB_1$, and $CC_1$ intersect at one point, and that this point coincides with the point from part a).

The point $P$ is called the Brocard point of triangle $ABC$. Similarly, it can be proven that there is another Brocard point $Q$, for which $\angle BAQ = \angle ACQ = \angle CBQ$.

Let $ABCD$ be a non-crossing quadrilateral inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$. The tangents to $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $B$ intersect at $P$. The line parallel to $(AC)$ passing through $P$ intersects $(AD)$ at $X$. The line parallel to $(BD)$ passing through $P$ intersects $(BC)$ at $Y$.

Show that the points $X, Y, C, D$ are concyclic.

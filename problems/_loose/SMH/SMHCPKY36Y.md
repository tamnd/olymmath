A line perpendicular to $EF$ through point $F$ intersects $ED$ at point $P$; a line perpendicular to $ED$ through point $D$ intersects $EF$ at point $Q$. Prove that $B$ is the midpoint of $PQ$. ${ }^{[5]}$

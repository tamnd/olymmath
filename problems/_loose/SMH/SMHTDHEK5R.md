Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a group with $2 n+1$ elements and a function $f: G \rightarrow G$ with the property:

$$
f(x f(x y))=y f\left(x^{2}\right), \forall x, y \in G
$$

Prove that $G$ is a commutative group.

Let the lengths of the sides of a quadrilateral be denoted by $a, b, c, d$, and the lengths of its diagonals by $e$ and $f$. Prove that

$$
(a+b+c+d)(e+f)>2\left(e^{2}+f^{2}\right)
$$

Let the lengths of the sides $P Q, Q R, R S, S T, T P$ of the pentagon $P Q R S T$ be denoted by $p, q, r, s, t$, and the lengths of its diagonals $P R, Q S, R T, S P, T Q$ by $p_{1}, q_{1}, r_{1}, s_{1}, t_{1}$. Prove that the value of the expression

$$
p\left(t_{1}+p_{1}\right)+q\left(p_{1}+q_{1}\right)+r\left(q_{1}+r_{1}\right)+s\left(r_{1}+s_{1}\right)+t\left(s_{1}+t_{1}\right)
$$

is greater than the sum of the squares of the diagonals.

Through the vertex $C$ of the smaller base $B C$ of the trapezoid $A B C D$, a line parallel to the side $A B$ is drawn and intersects the diagonal $B D$ at point $P$. Prove that if $D P=B O$ (where $O$ is the point of intersection of the diagonals), then a line parallel to $C D$ and passing through the vertex $B$ cuts off a segment $A Q$ on the other diagonal equal to $O C$.

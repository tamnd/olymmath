Let $ABC$ be a right triangle with $AB=3$, $BC=4$, and $\angle B = 90^\circ$. Points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are chosen on segments $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$, respectively, such that $PQR$ is an equilateral triangle, and $BP=BQ$. Given that $BP$ can be written as $\frac{\sqrt{a}-b}{c}$, where $a,b,c$ are positive integers and $\gcd(b,c)=1$, what is $a+b+c$?

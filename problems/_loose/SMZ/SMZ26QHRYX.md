Given quadratic trinomials $f_{1}(x)=x^{2}+a x+3, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}+2 x-b, f_{3}(x)=x^{2}+2(a-1) x+b+6$ and $f_{4}(x)=x^{2}+(4-a) x-2 b-3$. Let the differences of their roots be $A, B, C$ and $D$, respectively, and given that $|A| \neq|B|$. Find the ratio $\frac{C^{2}-D^{2}}{A^{2}-B^{2}}$. The values of $A, B, C, D, a, b$ are not specified.

Note that $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{6}=1$ and $\frac{1}{6}-\frac{1}{7}=\frac{1}{6 \times 7}$ and $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{7}+\frac{1}{42}=1$.
Determine one triple $(x, y, z)$ of positive integers with $1000<x<y<z<2000$ and
$$
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{7}+\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}+\frac{1}{45}=1
$$

In tetrahedron $ABCD$, the 4 altitudes $AA_1, BB_1, CC_1, DD_1$ intersect at point $H$ ($A_1, B_1, C_1, D_1$ are the feet of the perpendiculars). Points $A_2, B_2, C_2$ on the three altitudes satisfy $AA_2 : A_2A_1 = BB_2 : B_2B_1 = CC_2 : C_2C_1 = 2 : 1$. Prove that $H, A_2, B_2, C_2, D_1$ lie on the same sphere.
(21st All-Russian Competition Problem)

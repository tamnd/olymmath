Dad was driving his son to a math club lesson. On the way, they were overtaken by 4 cars. The son admitted to his father that he could only remember the numbers of the two smallest cars - 119 and 179, and that the others definitely had the digit 3. The father, however, remembered all of them and gave his son the following problem. If the numbers are paired and each pair is multiplied, and then the two resulting numbers are added, the result is 105080. Help the son remember the two remaining numbers. (All car numbers are three-digit numbers).

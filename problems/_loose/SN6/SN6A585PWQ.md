A water pool contains one-eighteenth of its capacity. Two water pipes start filling the pool simultaneously. When the water level reaches two-ninths of the pool's capacity, the first pipe continues to fill the pool alone for 81 minutes, during which it injects an amount of water equal to what the second pipe has already injected. Then, the second pipe fills the pool alone for 49 minutes, at which point the total amount of water injected by both pipes is the same. After that, both pipes continue to fill the pool. How many more minutes do the two pipes need to fill the pool together?

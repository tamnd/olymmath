Given is a square $A B C D$ with circumscribed circle $\Gamma_{1}$. Let $P$ be a point on arc $A C$ where $B$ also lies. A circle $\Gamma_{2}$ is internally tangent to $\Gamma_{1}$ at $P$ and also tangent to diagonal $A C$ at $Q$. Let $R$ be a point on $\Gamma_{2}$ such that line $D R$ is tangent to $\Gamma_{2}$. Prove that $|D R|=|D A|$.

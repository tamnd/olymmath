Let $P$ be a point on the arc of the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ that does not contain $C$. Prove that

$$
\frac{a^{2}}{t_{a}}+\frac{b^{2}}{t_{b}}=\frac{c^{2}}{t_{c}}
$$

where $t_{a}, t_{b}, t_{c}$ are the areas of $\triangle BCP, \triangle ACP, \triangle ABP$ respectively.

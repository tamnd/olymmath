Given a trihedral angle, all plane angles of which are right angles, $O$ - the vertex of this angle. Consider all possible broken lines of length $a$, starting at point $O$ and such that any plane parallel to one of the faces of the angle intersects this broken line in no more than one point. Find the geometric locus of the ends of this broken line.

15 Given a non-isosceles $\triangle ABC$, let the incircle $k$ (center $O$) touch the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ at points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$ respectively. Furthermore, let $AA_1 \cap k = A_2$, $BB_1 \cap k = B_2$, and $A_1A_3$, $B_1B_3$ be the angle bisectors of $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$ ($A_3 \in B_1C_1$, $B_3 \in A_1C_1$). Prove: (1) $A_2A_3$ is the angle bisector of $\angle B_1A_2C_1$; (2) If $P$, $Q$ are the intersection points of the circumcircles of $\triangle A_1A_2A_3$ and $\triangle B_1B_2B_3$, then $O$ lies on the line $PQ$.

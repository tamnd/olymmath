Let $M$ be an arbitrary point inside triangle $ABC$; $x, y$, and $z$ be the distances from $M$ to $A, B$, and $C$ respectively; $u, v$, and $w$ be the distances from $M$ to the sides $BC, CA$, and $AB$ respectively; $a, b, c$ be the corresponding sides of triangle $ABC$; $S$ be its area; $R$ and $r$ be the radii of the circumcircle and incircle. Prove the inequalities:

a) $a x + b y + c z \geqslant 4 S$

b) $x + y + z \geqslant 2(u + v + w) (\text{Erdös-Mordell inequality})$

c) $x u + y v + z w \geqslant 2(u v + v w + w u)$

d) $2\left(\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{z}\right) \leqslant \frac{1}{u} + \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{w}$

e) $x y z \geqslant \frac{R}{2 r}(u + v)(v + w)(w + u)$

f) $x y z \geqslant \frac{4 R}{r} u v w$

g) $x y + y z + z x \geqslant \frac{2 R}{r}(u v + v w + w u)$.

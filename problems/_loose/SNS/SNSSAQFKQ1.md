Consider a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let rays $B A$ and $C D$ intersect at $E$, rays $D A$ and $C B$ intersect at $F$, and the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ intersect at $G$. It is given that the triangles $D B F$ and $D B E$ have the same area.
(a) Prove that $E F$ and $B D$ are parallel.
(b) Prove that $G$ is the midpoint of $B D$.
(c) Given that the area of triangle $A B D$ is 4 and the area of triangle $C B D$ is 6 , compute the area of triangle $E F G$.

Given positive real numbers $a, b, c$ satisfying $a b + b c + c a = 1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{3}{\sqrt{a^{2}+1}}+\frac{4}{\sqrt{b^{2}+1}}+\frac{12}{\sqrt{c^{2}+1}}<\frac{39}{2} .
$$

An integer $n\ge 2$ is written on a piece of paper. Two players make alternate moves in a game where each move involves choosing a prime divisor of the written number and subtracting it from the number itself. The result replaces the written number. The game ends when the number obtained after the subtraction is $0$ . The player who makes the last move loses. For what values of $n$ does the starting player have a winning strategy?

The midpoints of the non-parallel sides of a trapezoid and the midpoints of the diagonals lie on a straight line parallel to the base. The distance between the midpoints of the non-parallel sides is equal to half the sum of the parallel sides, while the distance between the midpoints of the diagonals is equal to half the difference of the parallel sides.

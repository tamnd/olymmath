Given $4$ lines in Euclidean $3-$space:

$L_1: x = 1, y = 0;$
$L_2: y = 1, z = 0;$
$L_3: x = 0, z = 1;$
$L_4: x = y, y = -6z.$

Find the equations of the two lines which both meet all of the $L_i.$

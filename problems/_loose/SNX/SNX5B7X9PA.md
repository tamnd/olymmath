For a certain isosceles triangle with base $a$ and side $b$, let's construct 1 - 1 isosceles triangle on the height corresponding to the base $a$ and the height corresponding to the side $b$, such that the sides of the first triangle are equal to the base of the second triangle, and the sides of the second triangle are equal to the base $a$ of the original triangle.

a) When will the two derived triangles be similar to each other?

b) When will the first and when will the second triangle be similar to the original triangle?

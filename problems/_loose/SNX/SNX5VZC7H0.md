The sequence of real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ is such that for any natural number $k$ the equality

$$
a_{k+1}=\frac{k a_{k}+1}{k-a_{k}}
$$

holds. Prove that this sequence contains infinitely many positive and negative numbers.

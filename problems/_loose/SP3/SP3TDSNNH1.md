Let $n$ be a positive integer greater than 2, and the function $f: \mathbf{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ satisfies: for any regular $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, we have $\sum_{i=1}^{n} f\left(A_{i}\right)=0$. Prove that $f \equiv 0$.

A train ticket costs 50 rubles, and the fine for traveling without a ticket is 450 rubles. If a fare dodger (referred to as a "hare") is caught by a conductor, they have to pay both the fine and the cost of the ticket. It is known that a conductor is encountered on average once every 10 trips. The hare has familiarized himself with the basics of probability theory and decided to follow a strategy that minimizes the expected expenditure. What should he do: buy a ticket every time, never buy a ticket, or flip a coin to decide whether to buy a ticket or not?

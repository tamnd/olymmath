A square-stick number is a number you get by placing two or more squares of each two digits next to each other. (A two-digit number cannot start with a 0.) Because, for example, 16 and 25 are squares, 1625 is a square-stick number.

(a) Determine all four-digit square-stick numbers where the first and fourth digits are the same.

(b) Determine all six-digit square-stick numbers that are themselves squares.

Through the midpoint of each side of a quadrilateral, a line is drawn parallel to the segment connecting an arbitrary point $M$ on the plane with the midpoint of the opposite side. Prove that: 1) these lines intersect at one point $N$; 2) the point $N$ lies on the same line with the given point $M$ and the point of intersection $S$ of the midlines of the quadrilateral; 3) the point $S$ bisects the segment $M N$.

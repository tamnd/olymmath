$$
\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}+\frac{1}{4 \cdot 5 \cdot 6}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1984 \cdot 1985 \cdot 1986}

is expressed in its simplest fractional form, then the numerator of this fraction will be divisible by 1987.

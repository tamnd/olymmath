(1) Prove: If and only if $\frac{1}{a+b}+\frac{1}{b+c}=\frac{2}{c+a}$,
$$
2 b^{2}=a^{2}+c^{2} \text {. }
$$
(2) Simplify $\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{1}+\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{3 \sqrt{2}+2 \sqrt{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{100 \sqrt{99}+99 \sqrt{100}}$.
(3) Let $a, b, p, q, r, s$ be positive integers, satisfying $q r-p s=1, \frac{p}{q}<\frac{a}{b}<\frac{r}{s}$. Prove: $b \geqslant q+s$.

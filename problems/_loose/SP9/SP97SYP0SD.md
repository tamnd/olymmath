Let $A, B, C$ and $O$ be four distinct points such that the triangles $O A B$ and $O A C$ are equilateral with side length 1. Let $D$ and $E$ be two distinct points different from $A$ such that the triangles $O B D$ and $O C E$ are equilateral. Let $P$ be the point on the segment $[A D]$ such that $D P=1$. Let $Q$ be the point on the segment $[A E]$ such that $A Q=1$. Show that the points $B, P$ and $Q$ are collinear.

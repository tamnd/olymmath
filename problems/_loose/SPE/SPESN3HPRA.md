Three mutually tangent spheres touch a plane at three points lying on a circle of radius $R$. Prove that there exist two spheres tangent to the three given spheres and the plane, and if $r$ and $\rho(\rho>r)$ are the radii of these spheres, then $\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{\rho}=\frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{R}$.

Four numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}$ form an arithmetic progression. The product of the first and fourth terms is equal to the larger root of the equation

$$
x^{1+\log x}=0.001^{-\frac{2}{3}}
$$

and the sum of the squares of the second and third terms is five times the number of the term in the expansion of

$$
\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{y}}+\sqrt[6]{y}\right)^{20}
$$

which does not depend on $y$. Find these numbers.

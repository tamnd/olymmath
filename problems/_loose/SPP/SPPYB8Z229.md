Given $I, H$ are the incenter and orthocenter of acute $\triangle ABC$, respectively, and $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $A, B, C$ to the opposite sides. Points $U, V, W$ lie on $A H_{A}$, $B H_{B}$, $C H_{C}$, respectively, such that $A U = B V = C W = 2r$, where $r$ is the inradius of $\triangle ABC$. Prove that $H, U, W, V$ are concyclic.

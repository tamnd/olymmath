Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute triangle, $H$ be the intersection of its altitudes, and $D, E, F$ be their feet, where $D$ lies on $B C$, $E$ on $C A$, and $F$ on $A B$.

Prove that then $A H \cdot H D = B H \cdot H E = C H \cdot H F$ holds.

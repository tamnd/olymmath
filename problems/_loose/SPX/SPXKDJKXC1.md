Given an arbitrary triangle. On its sides, equilateral triangles are constructed outward, the centers of which serve as the vertices of triangle $\Delta$. The centers of the equilateral triangles constructed on the sides of the original triangle inward serve as the vertices of another triangle $\delta$. Prove that: a) triangles $\Delta$ and $\delta$ are equilateral; b) the centers of triangles $\Delta$ and $\delta$ coincide with the centroid of the original; c) the difference in the areas of triangles $\Delta$ and $\delta$ is equal to the area of the original.

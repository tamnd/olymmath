In the right-angled triangle $ABC$, the angle bisectors of the acute angles at $A$ and $B$ intersect at $O$. The angle bisector from $A$ intersects the leg $CB$ at $A'$, and the angle bisector from $B$ intersects the leg $CA$ at $B'$. Prove that $A A' \cdot B B' = 2 A O \cdot B O$.

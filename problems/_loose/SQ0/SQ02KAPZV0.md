We drew a semicircle $k$ over the segment $AB$ as its diameter. Let $C$ be any point on $k$ different from $A$ and $B$, and let $D$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $AB$. Consider the following three circles ($k_{1}$, $k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$), each of which has $AB$ as a common tangent: $k_{1}$ is the incircle of triangle $ABC$, while $k_{2}$ and $k_{3}$ each touch the segment $CD$ and the semicircle $k$. Prove that $k_{1}$, $k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$ have another common tangent.

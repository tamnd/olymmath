Given the sequence $\left\{S_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n} i!\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right) \text {. }
$$

Prove: For any given prime $p$, there exist infinitely many positive integers $a$ such that $v_{p}\left(S_{a+1}\right) \geqslant v_{p}\left(S_{a}\right)$, where $v_{p}(d)$ denotes the power of $p$ in $d$.

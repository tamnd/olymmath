A sequence of integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ satisfies
$$a_{n+2}^2 + a_{n+1} a_{n} \le a_{n+2} \left(a_{n+1} + a_{n}\right). (m = 1, 2, \ldots)$$
Show that there exists a positive integer $N$ such that $a_{n+2} = a_n$ when $n \ge N$.

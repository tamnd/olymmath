Let $p \geq 3$ be a prime number and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{p-2}$ be a sequence of natural numbers such that $p$ does not divide both $a_{k}$ and $a_{k}^{k}-1$ for all $k=1,2, \ldots, p-2$. Prove that the product of some elements of the sequence is congruent to 2 modulo $p$.

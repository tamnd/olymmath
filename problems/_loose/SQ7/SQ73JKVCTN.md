Pooh tied a napkin around Piglet's mouth and single-handedly ate 10 pots of honey and 22 cans of condensed milk, with each pot of honey taking him 2 minutes and each can of milk taking 1 minute. Upon learning that there was no more sweet food in the house, Pooh said goodbye and took Piglet away. Rabbit sadly thought that he wouldn't have been late for the meeting with the donkey if Pooh had shared with Piglet. Knowing that Piglet eats a pot of honey in 5 minutes and a can of milk in 3 minutes, Rabbit calculated the minimum time it would take for the guests to finish his supplies. What is this time? (A can of milk and a pot of honey can be divided into any parts.)

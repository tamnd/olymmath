(This question is worth 30 points) Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers, and satisfy $a b c=1$. For any integer $n \geq 2$, prove:
$$
\frac{a}{\sqrt[n]{b+c}}+\frac{b}{\sqrt[n]{c+a}}+\frac{c}{\sqrt[n]{a+b}} \geq \frac{3}{\sqrt[n]{2}} .
$$

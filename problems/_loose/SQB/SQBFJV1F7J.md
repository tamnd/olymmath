Given an isosceles triangle $A B C$ with vertex $A$. The length of the grasshopper's jump is equal to the base $B C$. It is known that starting from point $C$, the grasshopper reached point $A$ in 22 jumps, landing on the lateral sides of the triangle $A B C$ after each jump and alternating sides with each jump, except for the last one. Find the angles of triangle $A B C$, given that with each jump the grasshopper got closer to point $A$.

Five concentric circles $\Gamma_{0}, \Gamma_{1}, \Gamma_{2}, \Gamma_{3}, \Gamma_{4}$ have radii that form a geometric sequence with common ratio $q$ in the given order. Find the maximum value of $q$ such that a closed broken line $A_{0} A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ can be drawn, with each segment of equal length, and points $A_{i} (i=0,1, \cdots, 4)$ lying on circle $\Gamma_{i}$.

a) Demonstrate the identity $1^{3}+2^{3}+\ldots+n^{3}=\frac{n^{2}(n+1)^{2}}{4}$, for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$;

b) Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ be fixed. Determine the numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, . ., x_{n} \in(0, \infty)$, which satisfy the relation $\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k}\right)^{2}=x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+\ldots+x_{k}^{3}$, for any $k \in\{1,2,3, \ldots, n\}$.

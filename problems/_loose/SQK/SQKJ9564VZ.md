Let $\triangle A B C$ be a triangle such that $\angle A=90^{\circ}$. Determine whether it is possible to partition $\triangle A B C$ into 2006 smaller triangles in such a way that

$1^{\circ}$ Each triangle in the partition is similar to $\triangle A B C$;

$2^{\circ}$ No two triangles in the partition have the same area.

Explain your answer!

Above each side of a regular dodecagon, we construct an outward equilateral triangle. Prove that by connecting the newly formed vertices of these triangles in sequence, we again obtain a regular dodecagon. Prove, for example, by dividing the appropriate segments, that the area of the annular region between the two dodecagons is equal to the area of the inner dodecagon.

Given a right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at vertex $C$ and the lengths of the legs in the ratio $1:3$. Points $K$, respectively $L$, are the centers of squares that have one side in common with the legs $AC$, respectively $BC$, and do not overlap with triangle $ABC$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of the hypotenuse $AB$.

a) Justify that point $C$ lies on the segment $KL$.

b) Calculate the ratio of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $KLM$.

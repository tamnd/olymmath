Humpty buys a box of 15 eggs, with 3 rows and 5 columns. Each meal he removes one egg to cook and eat. If necessary, he moves one or more eggs in the box so that between meals there are always two lines of reflective symmetry. What is the smallest total number of extra egg moves he can make while he empties the box?
Note: You must carefully justify that your answer is minimal; that it is impossible to make fewer extra egg moves while emptying the box.

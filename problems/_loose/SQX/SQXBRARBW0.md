Consider the trapezoid $A B C D, A B \| C D, A B > C D, A D = D C = a \sqrt{2}$ and $B C = A C = 2 a$. On the plane of the trapezoid, a perpendicular is raised at $D$ on which the point $M$ is taken such that $M D = a \sqrt{3}$. Calculate:

a) the distance from point $M$ to $A B$;

b) the area of triangle $M A B$;

c) the distance from point $M$ to $A C$;

d) the measure of the angle between the planes (MAC) and (ABC).

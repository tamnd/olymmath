1) Let $a$ and $b$ be two real numbers such that $a^{n}+b^{n}$ is an integer for $n=1,2,3,4$. Show that $a^{n}+b^{n}$ is an integer for all $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

2) Is it true that if $a$ and $b$ are two real numbers such that $a^{n}+b^{n}$ is an integer for $\mathfrak{n}=1,2,3$ then $\mathrm{a}^{\mathrm{n}}+\mathrm{b}^{\mathfrak{n}}$ is an integer for all $\mathrm{n} \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$?

a) Andrei and Bob are playing a game in which they take turns coloring the vertices of a regular 2023-gon. Andrei starts, and at the beginning, no vertex is colored. Additionally, both players start with 0 points. On their turn, the player colors a vertex that has never been colored and earns $k$ points, where $k$ is the number of adjacent colored vertices (so $k$ can be 0, 1, or 2). The game ends when all vertices have been colored, and the player with the most points wins (in case of a tie, no one wins). Determine who has a winning strategy.

b) Same question but Andrei and Bob play this time on a regular 2022-gon.

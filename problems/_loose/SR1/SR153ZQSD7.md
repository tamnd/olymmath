Given that $F$ is the right focus of the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$, and the ratio of the distance from any point $P$ on the ellipse $C$ to the point $F$ to the distance from point $P$ to the line $l: x=m$ is $\frac{1}{2}$.
(1) Find the equation of the line $l$.
(2) Let $A$ be the left vertex of the ellipse $C$. A line passing through the point $F$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $D$ and $E$. The lines $A D$ and $A E$ intersect the line $l$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Does the circle with diameter $M N$ always pass through a fixed point? If so, find the coordinates of the fixed point; if not, explain the reason.

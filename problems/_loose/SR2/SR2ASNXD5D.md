Let $\mathbb{Q}^+$ be the set of all positive rational numbers. Find all functions $f:\mathbb{Q}^+\rightarrow \mathbb{Q}^+$ satisfying $f(1)=1$ and
$$ f(x+n)=f(x)+nf(\frac{1}{x}) \forall n\in\mathbb{N},x\in\mathbb{Q}^+$$

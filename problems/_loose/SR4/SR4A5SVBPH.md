a) From point $P$ on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$, lines $P A_{1}, P B_{1}$, and $P C_{1}$ are drawn at a given (directed) angle $\alpha$ to the lines $B C, C A$, and $A B$ respectively (points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ lie on the lines $B C, C A$, and $A B$). Prove that points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ lie on a single straight line.

b) Prove that when the angle $90^{\circ}$ in the definition of the Simson line is replaced by the angle $\alpha$, the line will rotate by an angle $90^{\circ}-\alpha$.

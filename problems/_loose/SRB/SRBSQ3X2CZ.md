A water pipe is set up vertically and water jets out to a height of $h=15 \mathrm{~m}$.

a) What will be the path of the water jet if a wind blows at $c=16 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{sec}}$?

b) What is the greatest distance that can be reached horizontally by this water jet in the absence of wind?

c) What is this distance if the aforementioned wind blows in the direction of the pipe's inclination?

d) What is it if the wind blows in the opposite direction of the pipe's inclination?

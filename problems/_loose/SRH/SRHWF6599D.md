If the sequence $a_{n}=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n}\left[\frac{n}{k}\right]$, prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $a_{n+1}>a_{n}$, and determine whether there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $a_{n+1}<a_{n}$? (Here $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$)

Points on a segment. Let $0=x_{0}<x_{1}<\cdots<x_{n}=1-$ be points that divide the unit circle into $n$ parts (not necessarily equal). Prove or disprove the following statement: there exists a composite integer $m$ such that in each of the segments $\left[x_{i}, x_{i+1}\right], i=0, \ldots, n-1$ there is at least one irreducible fraction of the form $\frac{l}{m}$.

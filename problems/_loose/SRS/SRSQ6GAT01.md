Two parallel lines intersect the graph of the function $y=a x^{2}$. The first intersects it at points with abscissas $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}\left(x_{1}<x_{2}\right)$, the second - at points with abscissas $x_{3}$ and $x_{4}\left(x_{3}<x_{4}\right)$. Prove that ( $x_{3}-x_{1}$ ) $=$ $\left(\mathrm{x}_{2}-\mathrm{x}_{4}\right)$.

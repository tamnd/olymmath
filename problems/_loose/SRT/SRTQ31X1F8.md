For a point $P$ on the ellipse, let $d$ be the distance from the center of the ellipse to the tangent line through $P$. Prove: When $P$ varies on the ellipse, $\left|P F_{1}\right| \cdot\left|P F_{2}\right| \cdot d^{2}$ is a constant. Here $\left|P F_{1}\right|$ and $\left|P F_{2}\right|$ are the distances from $P$ to the foci $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ of the ellipse.

Let $ABC$ and $DEF$ be two arbitrary given triangles, and $O$ a given point in the plane. Let $X$ and $Y$ represent an arbitrary point of $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$, respectively. Find the point $Z$ for every such pair of points such that $OXZY$ forms a parallelogram.

a) It is to be proven that all such $Z$ points fill a polygon.

b) How many sides can this polygon have?

c) It is to be proven that the perimeter of the resulting polygon is equal to the sum of the perimeters of the two triangles.

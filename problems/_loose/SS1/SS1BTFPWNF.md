There are $2 n$ people queuing up to buy tickets at the theater box office, among whom $n$ people each hold a 50-cent bill, and $n$ people each hold a one-dollar bill. Each person wants to buy a 50-cent ticket, and the box office has no change before selling tickets. How many ways are there to queue so that the ticket purchasing process goes smoothly (i.e., there is no trouble due to a lack of change at any point)?

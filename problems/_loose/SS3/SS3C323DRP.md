Consider triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ with sides of lengths $\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}, \mathrm{z}$, which simultaneously satisfy the relations:

$x^{2}+y^{2} \leq \frac{2}{x}, y^{2}+z^{2} \leq \frac{2}{y}, z^{2}+x^{2} \leq \frac{2}{z}, x y z=1$. Perpendiculars $\mathrm{AM}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ and $\mathrm{BN}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ are raised from the plane of the triangle.

a) Show that $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$ is equilateral.

b) If $x=1$, calculate: i) the measure of the angle between MB and NC;

ii) the value of the tangent of the angle between $\mathrm{CN}$ and the plane (ABM).

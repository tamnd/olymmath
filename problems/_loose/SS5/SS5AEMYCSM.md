For example, a $42 \times 2$ grid paper piece with one small square cut out, leaving 3 small squares, is called an $L$-shaped paper piece. Prove: for any positive integer $n$, a $2^{n} \times 2^{n}$ grid paper piece, after cutting out one small square, the remaining part can be completely cut along the grid lines into $L$-shaped paper pieces.
(1982 Shanghai Competition Problem)

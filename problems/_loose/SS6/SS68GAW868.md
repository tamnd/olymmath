If the roots of the equation $x^{2}+(m-1) x+4-m=0$ are both greater than 2, then the range of values for $m$ is
(A) $m \leqslant-6$.
(B) $-6<m \leqslant-5$.
(C) $m<-3$.
(D) $m<-6$ or $-6<m \leqslant-5$.
(China Hubei Province Huanggang Region Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1989)

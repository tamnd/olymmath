The centroid of tetrahedron $ABCD$ is the point $T$ whose radius vector is given by

$$
\vec{r}_{T}=\frac{1}{4}\left(\vec{r}_{A}+\vec{r}_{B}+\vec{r}_{C}+\vec{r}_{D}\right)
$$

If the centroid is equidistant from vertices $A$ and $B$, prove that

$$
|AC|^{2}+|AD|^{2}=|BC|^{2}+|BD|^{2}
$$

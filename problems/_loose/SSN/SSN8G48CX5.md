The lines $P F_{1}$ and $P F_{2}$ intersect the hyperbola at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. If $P F_{1}=\lambda_{1} F_{1} M$ and $P F_{2}=\lambda_{2} F_{2} N$, find the value of $\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}$ and the range of the slope $k_{M N}$ of the line $M N$.

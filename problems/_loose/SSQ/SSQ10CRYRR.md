On the circle $k$, point $A$ moves with a uniform speed $v_{1}$ and point $B$ moves with a uniform speed $v_{2}$, where $v 1 \neq v_{2}$.

If both points move in the same direction (e.g., clockwise), point $A$ overtakes point $B$ every 56 minutes. If both points move in opposite directions, they meet each other every 8 minutes. In this case, their distance measured along the circumference increases or decreases by $14 \mathrm{~m}$ every $24 \mathrm{~s}$.

a) How long is the circumference of the circle?

b) What are the speeds $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ (in $\mathrm{m} / \mathrm{min}$)?

In a round-robin tennis tournament with $2 n+1$ players, each pair of players competes in one match, with a clear winner and loser. The $2 n+1$ players have distinct levels of skill. It is known that exactly $k$ matches were won by the weaker player over the stronger player. Prove: there is a player whose number of wins lies in the interval $[n-\sqrt{2 k}, n+\sqrt{2 k}]$.

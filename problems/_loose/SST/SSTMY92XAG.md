Exactly $p-1$ distinct positive integers are written on the blackboard for some prime number $p$. The number $p$ is among these $p-1$ numbers. For any pair of the numbers the absolute value of their difference is also on the board. Prove that all the numbers on the blackboard are divisible by $p$.

Given an arbitrary convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Points $K$ and $M$ are the midpoints of sides $A B$ and $C D$ respectively. Point $P$ is the intersection of lines $K C$ and $B M$, point $N$ is the intersection of $A M$ and $K D$. Find the area of quadrilateral $K P N M$, if angles $C B P$ and $N D A$ are 30 degrees, $B P C-105^{0}$, $D A N-15^{0} . B P=2 \sqrt{2}, N D=\sqrt{3}$.

(2002 First Women's Mathematical Olympiad) Let $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n}(n \geqslant 2)$ be any permutation of $1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove that:
$$
\frac{1}{P_{1}+P_{2}}+\frac{1}{P_{2}+P_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{P_{n-2}+P_{n-1}}+\frac{1}{P_{n-1}+P_{n}}>\frac{n-1}{n+2} .
$$

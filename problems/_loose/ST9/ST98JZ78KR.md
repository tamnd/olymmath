Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of a scalene triangle $ABC$. The tangents to $\omega$ at $A$ and $C$ meet in $P$, and the line $BP$ intersects $\omega$ in $D$. Let $BB'$ be a diameter of $\omega$. The exterior angle bisector of $\angle ABC$ and the lines $B'A$ and $B'C$ intersect in $A'$ and $C'$, respectively. Prove that $A', B', C', D$ are cyclic.

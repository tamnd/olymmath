In the country, some pairs of cities are connected by one-way direct flights (there is no more than one flight between any two cities). We say that city $A$ is accessible from city $B$ if it is possible to fly from $B$ to $A$, possibly with layovers. It is known that for any two cities $P$ and $Q$, there exists a city $R$ such that both $P$ and $Q$ are accessible from $R$. Prove that there exists a city from which all cities in the country are accessible. (It is assumed that it is possible to fly from a city to itself.) (V. Dolnikov)

Let's draw the diagonals of the parallelogram $\mathrm{AC}$ and $\mathrm{BD}$. Suppose they intersect at point $\mathrm{O}$. Then CO, BN, DM are the medians of triangle BCD. Let them intersect at point K. By the property of medians $\mathrm{BK}: \mathrm{KN}=2: 1$. Since $D M \perp A C$, triangle CKD is a right triangle. KN is its median to the hypotenuse. Therefore, $K N=1 / 2 C D$. Then $B N: C D=3: 2$. Therefore, $\mathrm{BN}=1.5 \mathrm{CD}=9$.

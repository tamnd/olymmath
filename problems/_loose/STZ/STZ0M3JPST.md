Let $\omega=\cos \frac{\pi}{5}+i\sin \frac{\pi}{5}, f(x)=(x-\omega)\left(x-\omega^{3}\right)\left(x-\omega^{7}\right)\left(x-\omega^{9}\right)$, prove that: $f(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, and $f(x)$ cannot be factored into the product of two polynomials with integer coefficients, each of at least degree one.
(《High School Mathematics》2001 Issue 1 Olympiad Training Problem)

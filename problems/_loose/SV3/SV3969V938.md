Let the function $f(x)=a x^{2}+b x+c,(a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}, a \neq 0)$ satisfy that when $|x| \leqslant 1$, we have $|f(x)| \leqslant 1$. Let the maximum value of $\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right|$ for $|x| \leqslant 1$ be $K$. Find all functions $f(x)$ that satisfy the condition that there exists $x_{0} \in[-1,1]$, such that $\left|f^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)\right|=K$.

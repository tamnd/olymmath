With a non-convex, non-self-intersecting polygon, the following operations are performed. If it lies on one side of the line $A B$, where $A$ and $B$ are non-adjacent vertices, then one of the parts into which the contour of the polygon is divided by points $A$ and $B$ is reflected relative to the midpoint of segment $A B$. Prove that after several such operations, the polygon will become convex.

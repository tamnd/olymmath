a) Prove that for any positive integer $m > 2$, the equation $$y^3 = x^3_1 + x^3_2 + ... + x^3_m$$ always has a positive integer solution.
b) Given a positive integer $n > 1$ and suppose $n \ne 3$. Prove that every rational number $x > 0$ can be expressed as
$$x =\frac{a^3_1 + a^3_2 + ... + a^3_n}{b^3_1 + b^3_2 + ... + b^3_n}$$ where $a_i, b_i$ $(i = 1, . . . , n)$ are positive integers.

Let $A B C$ be a triangle in which the interior angle $\angle A C B$ has a size of $32^{\circ}$. On the extension of $B A$ beyond $A$, there is a point $D$ such that $A D = A C$; on the extension of $A B$ beyond $B$, there is a point $E$ such that $B E = B C$.

Prove that by these conditions, independent of the other properties of the triangle $A B C$, the size of the angle $\angle D C E$ is uniquely determined; determine this angle size!

The extensions of sides $A B$ and $D C$ of a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ intersect at point $K$, and the extensions of sides $A D$ and $B C$ intersect at point $L$, such that segments $B L$ and $D K$ intersect. Prove that if one of the three relations holds: $|A B|+|C D|=|B C|+|A D|,|B K|+|B L|=|D K|+|D L|,|A K|+|C L|=|A L|+|C K|$, then the other two also hold.

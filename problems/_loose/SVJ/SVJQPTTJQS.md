A right circular cone has a regular triangular pyramid inscribed in it, the apothem of which is equal to $k$, and the lateral face forms an angle of $\alpha$ with the base plane. A plane is drawn through one of the lateral edges of the pyramid, intersecting the conical surface. Find the area of the section of the cone by this plane, given that this area has the largest of all possible values.

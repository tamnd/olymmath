Determine real numbers $a$ and $b$ such that for any real numbers $u$ and $v$ the following holds:

$$
\max _{0 \leq x \leq 1}\left|x^{2}-u x-v\right| \geq \max _{0 \leq x \leq 1}\left|x^{2}-a x-b\right| .
$$

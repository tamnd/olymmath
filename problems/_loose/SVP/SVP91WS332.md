Prove that the center of the circumscribed sphere of a regular polyhedron is its center of mass (i.e., the center of mass of a system of points with unit masses located at its vertices).

The center of the circumscribed sphere of a regular polyhedron, coinciding with the center of the inscribed sphere and the center of mass, is called the center of the regular polyhedron.

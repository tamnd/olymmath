Let $X$ be a non-negative random variable with a continuous distribution function $F=F(x)$. Provide an example of some non-negative function $G=G(x)$, depending on $F$, such that $\mathrm{E} G(X)<\infty$ and at the same time, the finiteness of $\mathrm{E} G(\alpha X)^{2}$ for some $0<\alpha<1$ ensures the existence of all moments of $X$.

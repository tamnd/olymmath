Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers such that $a^{2}, b^{2}, c^{2}$ are consecutive terms of an arithmetic progression. Prove that the numbers $\frac{1}{b+c}, \frac{1}{a+c}, \frac{1}{a+b}$ are also consecutive terms of an arithmetic progression.

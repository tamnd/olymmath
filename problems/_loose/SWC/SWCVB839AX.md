A certain project, the number of days required for Team A to complete it alone is $a$ times the number of days required for Teams B and C to complete it together; the number of days required for Team B to complete it alone is $b$ times the number of days required for Teams A and C to complete it together; the number of days required for Team C to complete it alone is $c$ times the number of days required for Teams A and B to complete it together. Then the value of $\frac{1}{a+1}+\frac{1}{b+1}+\frac{1}{c+1}$ is
(A) 1 .
(B) 2 .
(C) 3 .
(D) 4 .
(Chinese Jiangsu Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

Let $a,b,c\in\mathbb R_+$ satisfy that
$$\sqrt{(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)}=\sqrt{(ab-a-b+1)(1+c)}+\sqrt{(bc-b-c+1)(1+a)}+\sqrt{(ca-c-a+1)(1+b)}.$$
Find the value range of $a+b+c.$

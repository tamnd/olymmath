Given an acute triangle $ABC$ such that the angle at vertex $C$ is the largest. Let $E$ and $G$ be the intersection points of the altitude from vertex $A$ with the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ and side $BC$, respectively, and let the center $O$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ lie on the normal from vertex $A$ to the line $BE$. Let points $M$ and $F$ be the feet of the normals from point $E$ to lines $AC$ and $AB$, respectively. Prove that

$$
P_{MFE} < P_{FBEG}
$$

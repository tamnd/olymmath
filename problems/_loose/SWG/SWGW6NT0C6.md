In triangle $ABC$, the radius of the inscribed circle is $r$, the center of the inscribed circle is $O$, and the radius of the circumscribed circle is $R$. The points where the angle bisectors from vertices $A, B, C$ intersect the opposite sides are $A_{0}, B_{0}$, and $C_{0}$. Prove that

$$
O A_{0} \cdot O B_{0} \cdot O C_{0} \leqq \frac{r^{2} \cdot R}{2}
$$

Let $a_{1} \geqslant 1, a_{n}=\left[\sqrt{n a_{n-1}}\right](n \geqslant 2)$, where $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$. Prove: regardless of the positive integer value of $a_{1}$, the prime numbers not in the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ are only finitely many.

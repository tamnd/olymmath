A vertex of a cube is $A$, the adjacent vertices are $B, C$, and $D$, and the vertex opposite to $A$ is $E$. Let us draw a plane through the point $E$ that intersects the rays $A B, A C$, and $A D$. Denote the intersection points as $P$, $Q$, and $R$, respectively.

Prove that the volume of the tetrahedron $A P Q R$ is the smallest when the chosen plane is perpendicular to $A E$.

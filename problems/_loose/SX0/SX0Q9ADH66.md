a) Prove that
$$ \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3} + ... + \dfrac{1}{2^{2n}} > n,
$$
for all positive integers $ n$ .

b) Prove that for every positive integer $ n$ we have $ \min\left\{ k \in \mathbb{Z}, k\geq 2 \mid \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3} + \cdots + \dfrac{1}{k}>n \right\} > 2^n$ .

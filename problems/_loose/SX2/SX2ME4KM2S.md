The surface area of a rectangular solid is $22 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, and the total length of all its edges is $24 \mathrm{~cm}$. Then the length of its diagonal (in $\mathrm{cm}$) is
(A) $\sqrt{11}$.
(B) $\sqrt{12}$.
(C) $\sqrt{13}$.
(D) $\sqrt{14}$.
(E) cannot be uniquely determined.
(35th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1984)

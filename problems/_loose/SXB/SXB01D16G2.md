On a plane, $n$ lines ($n>2$) are drawn, dividing the plane into several regions. Some of these regions are colored, with the condition that no two colored regions can touch along a boundary. Prove that the number of colored regions does not exceed $\frac{1}{3}\left(n^{2}+n\right)$.

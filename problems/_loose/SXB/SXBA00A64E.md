Pavel and Michal are playing the following game: At the vertices of a tetrahedron (triangular pyramid), there are initially zeros. Pavel first selects one of the vertices of the tetrahedron and increases the number written there by 2. Then Michal selects one of the edges of this tetrahedron and increases the numbers at both of its endpoints by 1. Their "moves" alternate regularly. The player who, after their move, has different numbers in all the vertices of the tetrahedron wins. Which of the players can ensure a win?

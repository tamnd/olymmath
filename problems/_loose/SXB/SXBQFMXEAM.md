(SRP, 62). Prove that for any values of $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $\alpha \in \mathbf{R}$, satisfying the conditions $n \neq 1$ and $\sin \alpha \neq$ $\neq 0$, the polynomial

$$
P(x)=x^{n} \sin \alpha-x \sin n \alpha+\sin (n-1) \alpha
$$

is divisible by the polynomial

$$
Q(x)=x^{2}-2 x \cos \alpha+1
$$

Provide an estimate for the accuracy of the following procedure for trisecting acute angles. Let $k$ be any circle with radius $r$, center $O$, and diameter $D E$, and let $C$ be the point on $k$ such that $\angle C O D$ is equal to the angle to be trisected. Measure on the ray $O E$ the segments $O A_{1}=2 r, O B_{1}=\frac{5}{3} r$. Denote the intersection of $k$ and $C A_{1}$, as well as $C B_{1}$, by $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$, respectively, and measure on the extensions of $C A_{2}$ and $C B_{2}$ the segments $A_{2} A = B_{2} B = r$. Finally, let the intersection of $A B$ and $D E$ be $T$, then $\angle C T D \approx \frac{1}{3} \angle C O D$.

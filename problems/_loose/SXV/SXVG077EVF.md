Let $n>1, n \in \mathbf{N}$, and let $M_{n}$ denote the set of all natural number pairs $(p, q)$ such that $1 \leqslant p \leqslant n$, $(p, q)=1$. For example, for $n=5$, we have $M_{5}=$ $\{(1,5),(2,5),(3,5),(4,5),(3,4)\}$. Prove that $\sum_{(p, q) \in M_{n}} \frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{2}$.
(Soviet Union Mathematical Competition)

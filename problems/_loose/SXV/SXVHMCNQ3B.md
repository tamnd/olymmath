A busy bee buzzes between the cells of a large honeycomb made up of a plane of tessellated hexagons. A flight of length $n$ consists of picking any of the six neighbouring cells and flying to the $n^{\text {th }}$ cell in that direction. After consecutive flights of lengths $n=N, N-1, \ldots, 2,1$, the bee finds that it has returned to its starting location. For which values of $N$ is this possible?

Inside the sides $AB, AC$ of a given triangle $ABC$, points $E, F$ are chosen in sequence, such that $EF \parallel BC$. The segment $EF$ is then divided by point $D$ so that

$$
p=|ED|:|DF|=|BE|:|EA|.
$$

a) Show that the ratio of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$ is the same for $p=2:3$ as it is for $p=3:2$.

b) Explain why the ratio of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$ is at least 4.

Let $a, b, c$ be positive numbers, and $a+b+c=1$, then:
$$
\left(a+\frac{1}{a}\right)^{2}+\left(b+\frac{1}{b}\right)^{2}+\left(c+\frac{1}{c}\right)^{2} \geqslant \frac{100}{3} .
$$

Let the area of $\triangle ABC$ be $\Delta$, the circumcenter be $O$, and the radius of the circumcircle be $R$. For any point $P$, the projections on the three sides are $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Let the area of $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ be $\Delta_{1}, OP=d$. Prove Euler's theorem $\Delta_{1}=$ $\frac{\Delta}{4}\left|1-\frac{d^{2}}{R^{2}}\right|$.

Let $k$ be a non-zero natural integer. Let $G$ be a non-empty directed graph such that no vertex is connected to itself and if $u$ and $v$ are two distinct vertices, one cannot have both an edge from $u$ to $v$ and an edge from $v$ to $u$. Suppose that for every $k$-tuple of vertices $\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{k}\right)$ of $G$, one can find a vertex $u$ of $G$ such that for all $i=1, \ldots, k$, there exists an edge from $u$ to $x_{i}$ in $G$. Show that $|\mathrm{G}| \geqslant 2^{\mathrm{k}+1}-1$.

In the country of Euleria, there are 101 cities. Every two cities are connected by a two-way non-stop flight of one of 99 airlines. It is known that from each city, flights of all 99 companies depart. We will call a triangle three cities that are pairwise connected by flights of the same company. Prove that in Euleria there is no more than one triangle. (I. Bogdanov, D. Karpov)

Determine whether there exist natural numbers $a, b, c, d$ that satisfy the following two conditions (1) and (2):

$$
\begin{aligned}
a \cdot b \cdot c \cdot d & =111111111111 \\
a+b+c+d & <11111
\end{aligned}
$$

If this is the case, provide such numbers.

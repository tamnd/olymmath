A collector of tin soldiers has an almost uncountable number of figures. When arranging them in different formations, he noticed something peculiar.

When the figures are arranged in rows of 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10, there is always exactly one figure left over. Only when arranging them in rows of 11, no figure is left over.

Since he had paid attention in math class, he was able to determine the number of his figures after a short calculation. How many tin soldiers did the collector have?

A cube is cut by all planes that pass through the midpoints of each of the three edges emanating from a vertex. This results in a residual body.

a) Represent this cube with edge length $a=6 \mathrm{~cm}$ and the residual body in an oblique drawing $\left(\alpha=60^{\circ} ; q=\frac{1}{3}\right)$!

b) Determine the number of all vertices and the number of all edges of the residual body!

c) State the shape and the number of all partial surfaces of the surface of the residual body!

Let the functions $f_{0}, f_{1}, f_{2}, f_{3}, \ldots$ be defined for all real numbers $x$ by

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{0}(x) & =|x| \\
f_{1}(x) & =|| x|-2| \\
f_{2}(x) & =|||x|-2|-2|
\end{aligned}
$$

...

in general: $f_{k}(x)=\left|f_{k-1}(x)-2\right|$ for all integers $k \geq 1$.

Draw the graphs of the functions $f_{0}, f_{1}$ and $f_{2}$! Describe in general the appearance of the graph of the function $f_{k}$!

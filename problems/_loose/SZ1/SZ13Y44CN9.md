Determine all possible values of the sum $a+b+c+d$, where $a, b, c, d$ are natural numbers satisfying the equation

$$
\left(a^{2}-b^{2}\right)\left(c^{2}-d^{2}\right)+\left(b^{2}-d^{2}\right)\left(c^{2}-a^{2}\right)=2021 .
$$

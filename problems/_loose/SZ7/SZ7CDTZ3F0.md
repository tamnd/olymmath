Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $P$ a point in its interior. Let $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ be the intersections of $(AP)$, $(BP)$, and $(CP)$ with the respective sides of the triangle. Let $A_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ be on $\left(A_{1} C_{1}\right)$ such that $\left(A A_{1}\right)$ intersects $\left[B_{1} A_{2}\right]$ at its midpoint and $\left(C C_{1}\right)$ intersects $\left[B_{1} C_{2}\right]$ at its midpoint. Let $A_{3}=\left(A_{2} B_{1}\right) \cap\left(C C_{1}\right)$ and $C_{3}=\left(C_{2} B_{1}\right) \cap\left(A A_{1}\right)$.

Show that $\left(A_{3} C_{3}\right) \|(A C)$.

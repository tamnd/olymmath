Two cyclists are riding along two intersecting circles. Each is riding along their own circle at a constant speed. Starting simultaneously from one point where the circles intersect and completing one revolution, the cyclists meet again at this point. Prove that there exists a fixed point such that the distances from this point to the cyclists are always equal if they are riding: a) in the same direction (clockwise); b) in opposite directions.

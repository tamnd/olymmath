Let ABC be an acute-angled triangle and let $\omega$ be its incircle and $\mathrm{I}$ the center of $\omega$. Let $D, E$ and $F$ be the points of tangency of the circle $\omega$ with the sides $[B C],[C A]$ and $[A B]$ respectively. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment $[B C]$. Let $P$ be a point inside the triangle $A B C$ such that $M D=M P$ and $\widehat{\mathrm{PAB}}=\widehat{\mathrm{PAC}}$. Let $\mathrm{Q}$ be a point, other than $D$, on the circle $\omega$ such that $\widehat{\mathrm{AQD}}=90^{\circ}$. Show that $\widehat{\mathrm{PQE}}=90^{\circ}$ or $\widehat{\mathrm{PQF}}=90^{\circ}$.

Consider triangle $ABC$ and points $M \in (AB)$ and $N \in (AC)$. Segments $MC$ and $NB$ intersect at point $T$. Line $AT$ intersects $MN$ and $BC$ at points $P$ and $D$.

Show that the ratios

$$
\frac{AM}{MB}, \frac{AP}{PD}, \frac{AN}{NC}
$$

are in arithmetic progression.

At the same time, a fly with a speed of 50 km/h takes off from $A$, flies to meet the cyclist from $B$, then turns around and flies back to meet the cyclist from $A$, and so on. How many kilometers will the fly fly in the direction from $A$ to $B$ until the cyclists meet?

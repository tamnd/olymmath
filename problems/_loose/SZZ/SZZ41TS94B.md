Let " $\sum$ " denote the cyclic sum. Given positive real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$ satisfying $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=1$. Prove:
$$
\sum \frac{1}{a^{2}} \geqslant\left(\sum \frac{4 b c}{a^{2}+1}\right)^{2} \text {. }
$$

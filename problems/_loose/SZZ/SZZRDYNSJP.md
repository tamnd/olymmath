From the vertex $C$ of the right angle of the right triangle $ABC$, a height $CD$ is drawn, and circles are inscribed in triangles $ACD$ and $BCD$ with centers $P$ and $Q$. The common external tangent to these circles intersects the legs $AC$ and $BC$ at points $M$ and $N$, and the height $CD$ at point $K$. Prove that:

a) triangles $CMN$ and $CBA$ are similar;

b) points $C, M, N, P$, and $Q$ lie on a circle with center $K$, the radius of which is equal to the radius of the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$.

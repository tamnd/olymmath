Consider a circle with diameter $A B$. Point $C$ lies on the line segment $A B$ such that $2 \cdot|A C|=|B C|$. The points $D$ and $E$ lie on the circle so that $C D$ is perpendicular to $A B$ and $D E$ is also a diameter of the circle. Denote the areas of triangles $A B D$ and $C D E$ as $O(A B D)$ and $O(C D E)$. Determine the value of $\frac{O(A B D)}{O(C D E)}$.

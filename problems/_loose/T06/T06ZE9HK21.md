In a sports area, there is a rectangular plot $A B C D$ with the longer side $A B$. The diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ form an angle of $60^{\circ}$. Runners train on the large circuit $A C B D A$ or on the small track $A D A$. Mojmír ran ten times around the large circuit, and Vojta ran fifteen times around the small track, i.e., fifteen times in one direction and fifteen times in the opposite direction. Together, they ran $4.5 \mathrm{~km}$. How long is the diagonal $A C$?

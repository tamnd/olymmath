$\mathbf{R}_{+} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}_{+}$ that satisfies
$$
2 f\left(x^{2}\right) \geqslant x f(x)+x (x>0) \text {. }
$$
(2) Prove: For functions satisfying (1), we have
$$
f\left(x^{3}\right) \geqslant x^{2} (x>1) .
$$

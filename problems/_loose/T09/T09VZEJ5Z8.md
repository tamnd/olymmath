Let $ABC$ be a triangle, $\Gamma 1$ its circumcircle, and let $S$ be the center of the circle $\Gamma 2$ passing through $B$, $C$, and $I$, where $I$ is the incenter. Let $D$ (respectively $E$) be the second intersection of $\Gamma 2$ with $BS$ (with $CS$). Let $F$ be a point on the arc $BC$ of $\Gamma 1$ not containing $S$ such that $\widehat{BSA}=\widehat{FSC}$.

Show that $A F D E$ is cyclic.

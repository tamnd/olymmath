Let $ABC$ be an obtuse triangle with the obtuse angle at vertex $B$. Let $D, E, F$ be the feet of the altitudes from vertices $A, B, C$, respectively. Let $T$ and $S$ be the midpoints of segments $\overline{AD}$ and $\overline{CF}$, respectively. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points symmetric to point $T$ with respect to the lines $BE$ and $BD$, respectively. Prove that point $S$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $BMN$.

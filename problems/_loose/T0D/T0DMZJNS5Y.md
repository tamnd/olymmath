Charlotte's teacher announces that for every five consecutive questions on the test, the answers to exactly three of them are TRUE. Just before the test starts, the teacher whispers to Charlotte that the answers to the first and last questions are both FALSE.
(a) Determine the number of questions for which the correct answer is TRUE.
(b) What is the correct answer to the sixth question on the test?
(c) Explain how Charlotte can correctly answer all 100 questions on the test.

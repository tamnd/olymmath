a) Triangles $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ are inscribed in a circle, and the vertices of triangle $T_{1}$ are the midpoints of the arcs into which the circle is divided by the vertices of triangle $T_{2}$. Prove that in the hexagon $T_{1} \cap T_{2}$, the diagonals connecting opposite vertices are parallel to the sides of triangle $T_{1}$ and intersect at one point.

b) The segment connecting the midpoints of arcs $A B$ and $A C$ of the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ intersects sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $D$ and $K$. Prove that points $A, D, K$, and the center $O$ of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$ are the vertices of a rhombus.

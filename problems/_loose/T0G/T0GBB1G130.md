On the chord $AB$ of the circle $O$, as on a diameter, a second circle $O_{1}$ is described. Through the point $A$, a secant is drawn, intersecting the circles $O_{1}$ and $O$ at points $M_{i}$ and $N_{i}$, respectively. Prove that the geometric locus of points symmetric to $N_{i}$ with respect to points $M_{i}$, as the secant describes a pencil, is a circle.

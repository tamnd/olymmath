In an $n \times n$ grid, some cells are colored black, but the cell $L$ at the bottom-left corner and the cell $R$ at the top-right corner are not colored black. A knight moves from $L$ to $R$, and every possible path of the knight contains at least one black cell. For which $n>3$ can we assert that there exist three cells connected diagonally, at least two of which are colored black?

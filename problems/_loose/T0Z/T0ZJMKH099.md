Suppose there is an $n$-term geometric sequence, the first term is 1, the common ratio is $r$, and the sum is $s$, where $r$ and $s$ are non-zero numbers. The sum of the new geometric sequence obtained by replacing each term of the original sequence with its reciprocal is
(A) $\frac{1}{s}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{r^{n} s}$.
(C) $\frac{s}{r^{n-1}}$.
(D) $\frac{r^{n}}{s}$.
(E) $\frac{r^{n-1}}{s}$.
(27th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1976)

Given the function $f: \mathbf{Z}_{+} \rightarrow \mathbf{Z}_{+}$, which satisfies for any $x \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}$,
$$
f(x)+f(x+2) \leqslant 2 f(x+1) \text {. }
$$

Prove: There exists an infinite set of positive integers $M$, such that for any $i, j, k \in M$, we have
$$
(i-j) f(k)+(j-k) f(i)+(k-i) f(j)=0 .
$$

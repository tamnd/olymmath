For two non-intersecting circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$, three common tangents are drawn - two external, $a$ and $b$, and one internal, $c$. The lines $a, b$, and $c$ touch the circle $\omega_{1}$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ respectively, and the circle $\omega_{2}$ at points $A_{2}, B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$ respectively. Prove that the ratio of the areas of triangles $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ is equal to the ratio of the radii of circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$.

(L. Emelyanov)

Given that $f(x)$ is a monotonically decreasing function. The range is $[-1,1]$, $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=1$, and it satisfies $f(x y)=f(x)+f(y)$.
(1) Prove that $\frac{1}{4}$ is not in the domain;
(2) Solve the inequality $f^{-1}(x) \cdot f^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{1-x}\right) \leqslant \frac{1}{2}$.

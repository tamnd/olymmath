In each cell of a square table $n \times n$, we write one of the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$ such that in each row and each column, either all numbers are the same or all are different. An example for $n=5$ is the following table

| 5 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 1 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| 2 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 3 |

Let $S$ be the sum of all numbers in the table. How many different values of $S$ exist for a given $n$?

$A B C D$ is a quadrilateral in the order of vertices. Show that the necessary and sufficient condition for the angle bisectors of the opposite angles $A$ and $C$ to be parallel is that the angles $B$ and $D$ are equal.

If the two angle bisectors coincide, then the sides of the quadrilateral are common tangents of two circles.

Determine all ordered pairs $(x, y), x \in \mathbb{N}, y \in \mathbb{N}$ for which three of the following statements are true, and only one is false:
(1) $y \mid(x+1)$
(2) $x=2 y+5$
(3) $3 \mid(x+y)$
(4) $x+7 y$ is a prime number

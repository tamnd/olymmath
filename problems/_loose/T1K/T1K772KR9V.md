In the first row of a $5 \times 5$ board, 5 chips are placed, each having one white side and one black side, all showing the white side. Each chip can move from one square to any adjacent square (horizontally or vertically), flipping over with each move. Additionally, multiple chips can occupy the same square. Can it be achieved through a sequence of moves that the 5 chips end up in the last row, in different squares, and all showing the black side?

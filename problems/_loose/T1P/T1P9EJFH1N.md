In the thirtieth kingdom, on February 29th of an olden year, a merchant was selling self-playing boots for 2000 altyn at a fair. According to trading rules, the price of goods is adjusted every morning before the market opens. The price can be increased by $10 \%$, decreased by $1 \%$ or $12 \%$ relative to the previous day's price, or it can remain unchanged. The price must be a whole number of altyn, and rounding is not allowed. On April 1st of the same year, a nobleman from the trade inspection found that the same merchant was selling the same self-playing boots for 2017 altyn, and issued an act of violation of trading rules. The merchant, in response, claimed that he had not violated any rules. Who is right?

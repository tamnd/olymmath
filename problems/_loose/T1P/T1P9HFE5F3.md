Vojta wanted to add several three-digit natural numbers on a calculator. On the first attempt, he got the result 2224. To check, he added the numbers again and got 2198. He calculated once more, and this time the sum was 2204. It turned out that the last three-digit number was cursed - each time Vojta did not press one of its digits hard enough, and the calculator thus entered a two-digit number instead of the three-digit one. There were no other errors in the addition. What is the correct sum of Vojta's numbers?

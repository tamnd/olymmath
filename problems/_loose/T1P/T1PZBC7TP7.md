A reservoir is filled from two pipes: one with fresh water, the other with salt water. If the fresh water tap is opened, the reservoir will fill in 7 hours. If both taps are opened, it will fill in 4 hours and 40 minutes. Filling an empty reservoir, the salt water tap was opened first, and after some time, the fresh water tap was also opened. When the reservoir was full, the salinity of the water in it was $17 \%$. How many minutes passed between the opening of the first and second taps, if the salinity of the salt water was $35 \%$?

In the isosceles triangle $ABC$, the midpoint of the side $AB$ is $E$, and the midpoint of the base $BC$ is $F$. Two points on the base, symmetric to $F$, are $H$ and $G$. The intersection of the line $EH$ and the angle bisector of $\angle EAH$ is $K$, and the intersection of the line $EG$ and the angle bisector of $\angle EAG$ is $L$. Prove that $KL \parallel BC$. Express the measure of $\angle KAL$ in terms of $\angle HAG$.

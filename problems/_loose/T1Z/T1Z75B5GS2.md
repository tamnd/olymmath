On the sides $A B$ and $A C$ of the isosceles triangle $A B C$, points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen respectively such that $P Q \| B C$. On the bisectors of triangles $A B C$ and $A P Q$, starting from vertices $B$ and $Q$, points $X$ and $Y$ are chosen respectively such that $X Y \| B C$. Prove that $P X = C Y$. (A.

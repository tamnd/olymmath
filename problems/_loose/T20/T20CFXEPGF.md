Define the function $m$ of the three real variables $x$ , $y$ , $z$ by $m$ ( $x$ , $y$ , $z$ ) = max( $x^2$ , $y^2$ , $z^2$ ), $x$ , $y$ , $z$ ∈ $R$ .

Determine, with proof, the minimum value of $m$ if $x$ , $y$ , $z$ vary in $R$ subject to the following restrictions: $x$ + $y$ + $z$ = 0, $x^2$ + $y^2$ + $z^2$ = 1.

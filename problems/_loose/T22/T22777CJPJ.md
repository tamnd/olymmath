Prove that for a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ the following inequality holds

$$
S_{A B C D} \leqslant \frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}}{4}
$$

where $a, b, c, d$ are the lengths of its sides.

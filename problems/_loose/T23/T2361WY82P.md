Given a triangle $ABC$ where $AC + BC = 3AB$. The incircle of triangle $ABC$ with center $I$ is tangent to sides $BC$ and $CA$ at points $D$ and $E$, respectively. Let $K$ and $L$ be the points symmetric to $D$ and $E$ with respect to point $I$. Prove that points $A, B, K, L$ lie on the same circle.

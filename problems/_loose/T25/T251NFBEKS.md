Given a rectangular trapezoid $A B C D (B C \| A D$ and $A B \perp A D)$, the diagonals of which are perpendicular and intersect at point $M$. Points $K$ and $L$ are chosen on sides $A B$ and $C D$ respectively, such that $M K$ is perpendicular to $C D$, and $M L$ is perpendicular to $A B$. It is known that $A B=1$. Find the length of the segment $K L$.

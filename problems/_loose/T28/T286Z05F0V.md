Let

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A=a+b+c+d \\
& B=a+b-c-d \\
& C=a-b+c-d \\
& D=a-b-c+d
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that the equation

$$
A^{3}=A\left(B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2}\right)-2 B C D
$$

is identically true if

$$
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}+\frac{1}{d}=0
$$

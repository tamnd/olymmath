If $f(a+b)=f(a) \cdot f(b)$, and $f(1)=1$. Then
$$
\frac{f(2)}{f(1)}+\frac{f(3)}{f(2)}+\frac{f(4)}{f(3)}+\cdots+\frac{f(1988)}{f(1987)}
$$

is
(A) 1986.
(B) 1987.
(C) 1988.
(D) Cannot be determined.
(Suzhou, Jiangsu Province, China High School Mathematics Competition, 1988)

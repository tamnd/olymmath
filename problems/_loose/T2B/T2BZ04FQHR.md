In the plane of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is given, through which lines perpendicular to $MA$, $MB$, and $MC$ are drawn. Prove that the points of intersection $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, $C_{1}$ of these perpendiculars with the corresponding sides of the triangle lie on one straight line.
but at points $P$ and $Q$. Prove that if $PA=\sqrt{cd}-(a-c)(a-$ the larger base of the trapezoid, $c$ - the smaller base, vertex $A$ lies on the larger base), then the sum of the segments $PA$ and $CQ$ will be the smallest.

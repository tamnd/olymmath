Prove:
(1)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sin \left(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\cdots+\alpha_{n}\right) \\
=\cos \alpha_{1} \cos \alpha_{2} \cdots \cos \alpha_{n}\left(T_{1}-T_{3}+T_{5}-\cdots\right) ;
\end{array}
$$
(2)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\cos \left(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\cdots+\alpha_{n}\right) \\
=\cos \alpha_{1} \cos \alpha_{2} \cdots \cos \alpha_{n}\left(1-T_{2}+T_{4}-\cdots\right) .
\end{array}
$$

where $T_{k}(k=1,2, \cdots, n)$ is the sum of the products of every $k$ of $\tan \alpha_{1}, \tan \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \tan \alpha_{n}$.

Let $AC$ be the diameter of the circumcircle of the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$. Assume that on the rays opposite to the rays $AD$ and $DC$ there exist points $A' \neq A$ and $C' \neq D$ respectively, such that $|AB| = |A'B|$ and $|BC| = |BC'|$. Prove the following statements:

a) The points $A', B, C'$, and $D$ lie on the same circle $k$.

b) If $O$ is the center of the circle $k$ and $O_A, O_C$ are the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $AA'B$ and $CC'B$ respectively, then $OO_A \perp OO_C$.

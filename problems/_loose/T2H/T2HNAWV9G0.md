Given is an arbitrary triangle $A B C$.

Construct a line parallel to $B C$ such that it intersects the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively, for which $E D = D B + E C$ holds!

Describe and justify your construction!

Investigate whether there is exactly one such parallel line (to the given triangle $A B C$)!

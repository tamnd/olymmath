(2004 National High School Competition Fujian Province Preliminary Question) In the infinite sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 1)$, for each odd $n$, $x_{n}$, $x_{n+1}$, $x_{n+2}$ form a geometric sequence, and for each even $n$, $x_{n}$, $x_{n+1}$, $x_{n+2}$ form an arithmetic sequence. Given $x_{1}=$ $a, x_{2}=b$.
(1) Find the general term formula of the sequence, and what necessary and sufficient conditions must the real numbers $a$, $b$ satisfy for such an infinite sequence to exist?
(2) Find the harmonic mean of $x_{2}, x_{4}, \cdots, x_{2 n}$, i.e., $\frac{n}{\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x_{2 k}}}$.

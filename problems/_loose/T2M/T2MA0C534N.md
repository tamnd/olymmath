Independent random variables $X$ and $Y$ are given by the probability density functions

$$
\begin{aligned}
& p(x)=\frac{1}{5} e^{-x / 5} (0 \leq x<+\infty) \\
& p(y)=\frac{1}{6} e^{-y / 6} (0 \leq y<+\infty)
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the composition of these laws, i.e., the probability density function of the random variable $Z=X+Y$.

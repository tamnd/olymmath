For any strictly positive integer $n$, we define $a_{n}$ as the last digit of the sum of the digits of the number 20052005...2005, (we write "2005" $n$ times in a row). For example, $\mathrm{a}_{1}=7$ and $\mathrm{a}_{2}=4$.

a) What are the strictly positive integers $n$ such that $a_{n}=0$?

b) Calculate $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{2005}$.

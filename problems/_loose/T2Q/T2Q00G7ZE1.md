Alice draws a triangle $\triangle{ABC}$ and marks a point $S$ on side $BC$ , closer to $B$ than to $C$ . Bob must construct a line passing through $S$ that divides triangle $ABC$ into two regions of equal area, using only an unmarked straightedge and a compass. Prove that Bob can always achieve this, regardless of Alice's drawing. Describe the steps of the construction and explain why the two resulting regions have equal area.

Three bandits want to divide the loot. Each is sure that he would divide the loot into equal parts, but the others do not trust him. If there were only two bandits, it would be easy to get out of the situation: one would divide the loot into two parts, and the other would take the part that seems larger to him. Indicate how the three bandits should act so that each of them is sure that his share constitutes no less than one third of the entire loot. (The loot is so diverse that there is no objective way to compare individual parts.)

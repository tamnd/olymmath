Let $\omega$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$. Let $A$ be a point inside the circle $\omega$. First, show that $A^{*}$, the inverse of $A$ in the inversion with center $O$ and radius $r$, is outside the circle $\omega$. Let $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ be the points of tangency of $A^{*}$ to the circle $\omega$. Show that $A$ lies on the line $\left(T_{1} T_{2}\right)$.

Let $M$ be an interior point of $\triangle A B C$. The lines $A M, B M$ and $C M$ meet the lines $B C, C A$ and $A B$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, respectively, such that $S_{C B_{1} M}=2 S_{A C_{1} M}$. Prove that $A_{1}$ is the midpoint of the segment $B C$ if and only if $S_{B A_{1} M}=3 S_{A C_{1} M}$.

Given a quadrilateral $ABCD$. Find a point $P$ in its plane such that the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from $P$ to the sides of the quadrilateral lie on a straight line. In connection with this, prove the following - Steiner's theorem: The orthocenters of the four triangles formed by the four sides of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ lie on a straight line.

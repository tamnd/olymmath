(2004 National College Entrance Examination Chongqing Paper) Let the function $f(x)=x(x-1)(x-a)(a>1)$.
( I ) Find the derivative $f^{\prime}(x)$, and prove that $f(x)$ has two distinct extreme points $x_{1} 、 x_{2}$;
(II) If the inequality $f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right) \leqslant 0$ holds, find the range of values for $a$.

Consider 100 points in the plane, such that no three are collinear. We draw all the lines passing through two of these points, and we assume that if $A, B, C, D$ are four of these points, then $(A B)$ and $(C D)$ are not parallel, and their intersection point does not lie on another line. How many regions of the plane are thus formed by these lines?

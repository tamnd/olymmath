$$S=\frac{1}{1-x_1^3}+\frac{1}{1-x_2^3}+...+\frac{1}{1-x_{2004}^3}+\frac{1}{1-y_1^3}+\frac{1}{1-y_2^3}+...+\frac{1}{1-y_{2004}^3}$$

Before the Christmas concert, the students offered for sale 60 products from their art classes. Each customer could determine the price themselves, and the entire proceeds went to charitable causes. At the beginning of the concert, the students calculated how many crowns they had earned on average for each sold product, and it came out to be an exact whole number. Since they had not yet sold all 60 products, they continued to offer them after the concert. Then, people bought seven more, for which they paid a total of 2505 Kč. This increased the average revenue per sold product to exactly 130 Kč. How many products were then left unsold?

(L. Šimünek)

Idea. Determine the relationship between the number of products sold before the concert and the amount earned from them. Keep in mind that all unknowns should be whole numbers.

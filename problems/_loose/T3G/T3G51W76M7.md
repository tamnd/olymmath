For the same circle with center $O$, inscribe congruent triangles $ABC$ and $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$. The lines $AA_{1}, BB_{1}, CC_{1}$ intersect at points $\alpha \beta \gamma$, while the intersections of the sides $BC-B_{1}C_{1}, CA-C_{1}A_{1}, AB-A_{1}B_{1}$ form the triangle $\alpha_{1} \beta_{1} \gamma_{1}$.

Show that the latter is similar to triangle $ABC$, and the former is similar to the pedal triangle of $ABC$; $O$ is the incenter of triangle $\alpha \beta \gamma$ and the orthocenter of triangle $\alpha_{1} \beta_{1} \gamma_{1}$. What must be the relative position of triangles $ABC$ and $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ so that triangle $\alpha \beta \gamma$ is maximized, and triangle $\alpha_{1} \beta_{1} \gamma_{1}$ is minimized?

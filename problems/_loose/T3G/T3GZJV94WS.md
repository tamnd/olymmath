If from any point $P$ in the plane, perpendiculars are dropped to the connecting lines $A B, B C$, and $C A$ of three points $A, B$, and $C$ in this plane that do not lie on the same straight line, the feet of these perpendiculars are $X, Y$, and $Z$. Prove that the following always holds:

$$
A X^{2}+B Y^{2}+C Z^{2}=B X^{2}+C Y^{2}+A Z^{2}
$$

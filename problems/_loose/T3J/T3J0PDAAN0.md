Prove that if

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{0}^{4}+a_{1} x_{0}^{3}+a_{2} x_{0}^{2}+a_{3} x_{0}+a_{4} & =0 \\
4 x_{0}^{3}+3 a_{1} x_{0}^{2}+2 a_{2} x_{0}+a_{3} & =0
\end{aligned}
$$

then $x^{4}+a_{1} x^{3}+a_{2} x^{2}+a_{3} x+a_{4}$ is divisible by $\left(x-x_{0}\right)^{2}$.

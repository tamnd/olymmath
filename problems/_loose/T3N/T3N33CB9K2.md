In a country, some pairs of cities are connected by roads. It is known that there are no three cities that are pairwise connected by roads. Moreover, for any $n$ roads, there is a city from which at least two of them originate. Prove that the cities can be divided into $n$ districts in such a way that any road connects cities from different districts.

Let $V$ be the intersection of the altitudes of an acute triangle $ABC$. The line $CV$ is a common tangent to circles $k$ and $l$, which touch each other externally at point $V$ and each passes through one of the vertices $A$ and $B$. Their intersections with the interiors of sides $AC$ and $BC$ are denoted by $P$ and $Q$. Prove that the ray $VC$ is the angle bisector of $\angle PVQ$ and that points $A, B, P, Q$ lie on a single circle.

Two skew lines, $a$ and $b$, are perpendicular to each other. A line $c$, which is perpendicular to both $a$ and $b$, intersects them at points $A$ and $B$ respectively, with $AB = d$. Consider a point $A'$ on $a$ and a point $B'$ on $b$ such that $AA' = BB' > d$. It is to be proven that the segment $A'B'$, as a diameter, defines a sphere that passes through $A$ and $B$. What is the geometric locus of the center of this sphere as $A'$ and $B'$ take on all permissible positions?

There are several parallel rails. On these rails, there are 30 freight and 20 passenger cars, each car standing on two adjacent rails. A car is called movable if both rails it stands on are not occupied by other cars (in the provided diagram, there are no movable cars). Among the passenger cars, exactly 10 are movable, and among the freight cars, exactly 9 are movable. Prove that there is a rail on which the wheels of at least two freight cars stand.

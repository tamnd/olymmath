In a country, there are more than 7 cities. Prove that there does not exist a network of one-way roads with the following properties:

a) Between any two cities, there is exactly one direct road.

b) For any two cities $A$ and $B$, there is exactly one city that can be directly reached from both $A$ and $B$.

c) For any two cities $A$ and $B$, there is exactly one city from which both $A$ and $B$ can be directly reached.

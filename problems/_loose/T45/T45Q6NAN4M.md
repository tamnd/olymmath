If a circle can be inscribed in a cyclic quadrilateral touching its sides, then the chords connecting the opposite points of tangency are perpendicular to each other; however, if two chords of a circle are perpendicular to each other and tangents are drawn to the circle at the endpoints of the chords, then these tangents form a cyclic quadrilateral.

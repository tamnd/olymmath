(27th American High School Mathematics Examination) Let there be an $n$-term geometric sequence, with the first term being 1, the common ratio being $r$, and the sum being $s$, where $r$ and $s$ are non-zero numbers. Then the sum of the new geometric sequence obtained by replacing each term of the original sequence with its reciprocal is
A. $\frac{1}{s}$
B. $\frac{1}{r^{n} s}$
C. $\frac{s}{r^{n-1}}$
D. $\frac{r^{n}}{s}$

2 ᄂ Given an integer $c$, let the sequence $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots$ satisfy $x_{1}=c \mathrm{H}$
$$
x_{n}=x_{n-1}+\left[\frac{2 x_{n}-(n+2)}{n}+1\right], n=2,3, \cdots
$$

where $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$.

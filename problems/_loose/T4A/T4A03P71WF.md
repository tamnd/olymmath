Let $k$ be an integer in the interval $[1,99]$ . A fair coin is to be flipped $100$ times. Let $$ \varepsilon_j =\begin{cases}
1, \text{if the j-th flip is a head}
2, \text{f the j-th flip is a tail}\end{cases} $$ Let $M_k$ denote the probability that there exists a number $i$ such that $k+\varepsilon_1 +...+\varepsilon_i = 100$ . How to choose $k$ so as to maximize the probability $M_k$ ?

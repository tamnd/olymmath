In one urn lie two white balls, in another - two black balls, in the third - one white ball and one black ball. Each urn had a sign indicating its contents: $W W, B B, W B$. But some joker switched all the signs so that now each of them incorrectly indicates the contents of the urn. It is allowed to draw a ball from any urn without looking inside. How many minimum draws are required to determine the contents of all three urns? (After each draw, the ball is put back.)

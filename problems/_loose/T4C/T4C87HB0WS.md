There are 60 students in the class, and their birthdays are recorded as a certain month and day. Ask each student the same two questions: How many students in the class have the same birth month as you? How many students in the class have the same birth day as you (for example, the birth day of January 12 and December 12 is the same). It is found that the answers obtained include all integers from 0 to 14. Then, what is the minimum number of students who have the same birthday?

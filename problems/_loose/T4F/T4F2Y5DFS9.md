From a box containing $n$ white and $m$ black balls $(n \geqslant m)$, all balls are drawn one by one at random. Find the probability of the following events:

(a) there will be a moment when the number of white and black balls in the sequence of drawn balls will be equal

(b) at any moment, the number of white balls in the sequence of drawn balls will not be less than the number of black balls.

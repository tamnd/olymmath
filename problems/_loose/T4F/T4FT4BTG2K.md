Consider a parabola. The *parabolic length* of a segment is the length of the projection of this segment on a straight line perpendicular to the axis of symmetry of the parabola. In the parabola, two chords $AB$ and $CD$ are drawn, intersecting at the point $N{}$ . Prove that the product of the parabolic lengths of the segments $AN$ and $BN$ is equal to the product of the parabolic lengths of the segments $CN$ and $DN$ .

*Proposed by M. Panov*

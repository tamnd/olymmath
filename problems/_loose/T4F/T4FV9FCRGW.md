$O A, O B, O C$, they respectively form positive angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ with the positive direction of $O x$. Prove:
(1) $\tan \frac{\beta+\gamma}{2}(\cos \beta+\cos \gamma)-\tan \frac{\gamma+\alpha}{2}(\cos \gamma+\cos \alpha)=\sin \beta-\sin \alpha$;
(2) The orthocenter $H$ of $\triangle A B C$ has coordinates
$$
(R(\cos \alpha+\cos \beta+\cos \gamma), R(\sin \alpha+\sin \beta+\sin \gamma)) .
$$

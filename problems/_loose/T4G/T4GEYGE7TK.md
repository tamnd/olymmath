The arc $AB$ with center $S$ has a central angle $\alpha$, and points $C$ and $D$ divide the chord $AB$ into three equal parts.

a) If $\measuredangle C S D=x$, prove that $\cos x=\frac{4+5 \cos \alpha}{5+4 \sin \alpha}$.

b) Calculate the difference $\cos x-\cos \frac{\alpha}{3}$.

c) Determine the angle $\alpha$ for which this difference is zero.

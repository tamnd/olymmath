Let $n$ be a natural number. Each of the numbers $\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$ is colored white or black. You can now repeatedly select a number and recolor it, as well as all numbers that are not divisors of it. Initially, all numbers are white. For which $n$ can you achieve that all numbers are black at some point?

Prove that for every natural number $k$, there exist infinitely many natural numbers $n$ such that

$$
\frac{n-d\left(n^{r}\right)}{r} \in \mathbb{Z}, \text { for every } r \in\{1,2, \ldots, k\}
$$

Here, $d(x)$ denotes the number of natural divisors of a natural number $x$, including 1 and $x$ itself.

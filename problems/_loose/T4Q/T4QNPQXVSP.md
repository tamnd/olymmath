Let $ a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_n $ be positive integers such that for every distinct $1\leq{i,j}\leq{n}$ we have $ a_j-a_i $ divides $ a_i $ .
Prove that
$$ ia_j\leq{ja_i} \text{ for } 1\leq{i}<j\leq{n} $$

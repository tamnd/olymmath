If $a, b, c, d$ are the sides of a convex quadrilateral, $s$ is the semiperimeter, and $2 \phi$ is the sum of two of its opposite angles, then its area is

$$
P=\sqrt{(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)(s-d)-a b c d \cos ^{2} \phi}
$$

Prove it!

Given that the side length of square $ABCD$ is 1, and circles with radius 1 are drawn with $A, B, C, D$ as centers, then the area of the common part of the four sectors is
(A) $1-\sqrt{3}+\frac{\pi}{3}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{4}$.
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2}-1$.
(D) $2-\sqrt{3}-\frac{\pi}{8}$.
(China Zhejiang Province Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

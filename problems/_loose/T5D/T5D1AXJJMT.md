Draw the following lines on the coordinate plane:
$$
y=k, y=\sqrt{3} x+2 k, y=-\sqrt{3} x+2 k,
$$

where $k=0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \cdots, \pm 10$. These 63 lines can divide the plane into several equilateral triangles. Find the number of equilateral triangles with side length $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$.

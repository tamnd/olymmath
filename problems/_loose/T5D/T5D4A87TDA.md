Let $A B C D$ be a triangular pyramid that satisfies the condition that the four triangles $A B C, A B D$, $A C D$ and $B C D$ are all mutually perimeter-equal.

Investigate whether this condition and the lengths

$$
\overline{A D}=p, \overline{B D}=q, \overline{C D}=r
$$

uniquely determine the lengths $\overline{B C}=a, \overline{C A}=b$ and $\overline{A B}=c$!

If this is the case, give these lengths $a, b, c$ in terms of $p, q, r$!

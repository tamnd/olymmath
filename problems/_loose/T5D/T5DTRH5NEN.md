A blanket-pulling tournament was held at the school, consisting of several rounds. In each round, two teams participated, each consisting of a non-zero number of students, of course, a person could not be in both teams at the same time. After the tournament, it turned out that every possible team that could be formed from the students of this school (except for the team consisting of all people at once) participated in exactly one round of the tournament. Prove that in this case, all the students competed in each round.

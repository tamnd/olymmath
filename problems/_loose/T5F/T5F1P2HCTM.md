A sheet of grid paper of size $5 \times$ n is filled with cards of size $1 \times 2$ such that each card completely occupies two adjacent cells. Each card has the numbers 1 and -1 written on it. It is known that the products of the numbers in the rows and columns of the resulting table are positive. For which $n$ is this possible?

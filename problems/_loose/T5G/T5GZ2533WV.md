In the inscribed hexagon $A B C D E F$ of a circle, $B D$ intersects with $C F$, $A C$ intersects with $B E$, and $A D$ intersects with $C E$ at points $G$, $H$, $I$, respectively, and it is given that $B D \perp C F$ and $A I=C I$. Prove:
$$
C H=A H+D E \Leftrightarrow B C \cdot D E=B D \cdot G H .
$$

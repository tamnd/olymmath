In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, the midpoints of the edges $B C, C A, A B$ are $E, F, G$ respectively. Prove that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& D A^{2}-D B^{2}+D C^{2}+A E^{2}+B F^{2}+C G^{2}=D E^{2}+D F^{2}+D G^{2}+ \\
& +4\left(E F^{2}+F G^{2}+G E^{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

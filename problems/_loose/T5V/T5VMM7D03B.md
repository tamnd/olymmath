Given two planes $\left(A_{1}, A_{2}\right)$, two distances $\left(\varrho_{1}, r_{1}\right.$ and $\left.\varrho_{2}, r_{2}\right)$, and a plane $(T)$. Two spheres are to be constructed:

1) whose radii are $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$.
2) which cut out circles of radii $\varrho_{1}$ and $\varrho_{2}$ from the planes $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$.
3) which have a common tangent plane at a common point, parallel to $T$. One of the two spheres should touch the first picture plane.

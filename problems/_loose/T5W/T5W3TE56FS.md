Given a triangle $O B C(\widehat{B O C}=\alpha)$. For each point $A$ on the side $B C$, define points $M$ and $N$ on $O B$ and $O C$ such that $\widehat{M A N}=\beta\left(\alpha+\beta<180^{\circ}\right)$ and the area of quadrilateral $O M A N$ is maximized. Prove that this maximum area reaches a minimum for such points $A, M$, and $N$, for which $|M A|=|A N|$, and the line $M N$ is parallel to $B C$. (Such points exist if the angles $\hat{B}$ and $\hat{C} \triangle O B C$ do not exceed $90^{\circ}+\beta / 2$.)

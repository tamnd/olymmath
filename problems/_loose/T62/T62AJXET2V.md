Sine Theorem [Inscribed, circumscribed, and exscribed circles; their radii]

Prove that in any acute-angled triangle $k_{a}+k_{b}+k_{c}=R+r$, where $k_{a}, k_{b}, k_{c}$ are the perpendiculars dropped from the center of the circumscribed circle to the corresponding sides; $r$ and $R$ are the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles.

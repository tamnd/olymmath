A trihedral angle in which all plane angles are right angles is called orthogonal (for example, the trihedral angle $0 x y z$ in a rectangular coordinate system¹). Prove that for an orthogonal trihedral angle: a) three points lying on its three edges and not coinciding with the vertex $O$ are the vertices of an acute-angled triangle; b) the projection of the vertex $O$ onto the plane of this triangle coincides with the orthocenter of the triangle.

Let $a, b, c$ be distinct complex numbers of the same modulus. Show that the points with affixes $a, b, c$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle if and only if the following relation holds:

$$
a^{3}(b-c)+b^{3}(c-a)+c^{3}(a-b)=0
$$

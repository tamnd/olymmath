Emily Thorne is throwing a Memorial Day Party to kick off the Summer in the Hamptons, and she is trying to figure out the seating arrangment for all of her guests. Emily saw that if she seated $4$ guests to a table, there would be $1$ guest left over (how sad); if she seated $5$ to a table, there would be $3$ guests left over; and if she seated $6$ to a table, there would again be $1$ guest left over. If there are at least $100$ but no more than $200$ guests (because she's rich and her house is $20000$ square feet), what is the greatest possible number of guests?

On the sides $AB, BC, CA$ of triangle $ABC$ (but not at the vertices), points $D, E, F$ are chosen respectively. Let $d_{0}, d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$ be the lengths of the greatest sides of triangles $DEF, ADF, BDE, CEF$.

Prove that $d_{0} \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \min \left(d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}\right)$. In which cases does equality hold?

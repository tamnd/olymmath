A trapezoid $A B C D$ with $A B \| D C$ and $\overline{A B}=a>\overline{C D}=c$ is to be divided into two parts of equal area by a line segment $G H$ parallel to $A B$.

Prove that there is exactly one such line segment $G H$ and that its length $\overline{G H}=s$ is uniquely determined by $a$ and $c$! Determine $s$ in terms of $a$ and $c$!

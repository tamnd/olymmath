Lines $EF$ and $BC$ meet at $P$ and circles $(PDS)$ and $(AEF)$ meet again at $Q$. Prove that, as $D$ varies, isogonal conjugate of $Q$ with respect to triangle $ ABC$ lies on a fixed circle.

Let the complex numbers $z_{1}=r_{1}\left(\cos t_{1}+i \sin t_{1}\right)$ and $z_{2}=r_{2}\left(\cos t_{2}+i \sin t_{2}\right)$, with $t_{1}, t_{2} \in[0,2 \pi)$ and $r_{1}, r_{2} \geq 1$. Show that

$$
\left(t_{1}-t_{2}\right)^{2}+2 r_{1} r_{2}+z_{1} \overline{z_{2}}+\overline{z_{1}} z_{2} \geq 4
$$

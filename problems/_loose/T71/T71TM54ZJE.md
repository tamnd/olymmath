Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2008}$ be real numbers such that

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\ldots+a_{2008}=0 \\
a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+a_{3}^{2}+\ldots+a_{2008}^{2}=502
\end{array}\right.
$$

Determine the maximum value of $a_{2008}$. Find at least one sequence of values $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2007}$ in which it is achieved.

Given real numbers $a_{1} \leqslant a_{2} \leqslant a_{3}$ and $b_{1} \leqslant b_{2} \leqslant b_{3}$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3} & =b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3} \\
a_{1} a_{2}+a_{2} a_{3}+a_{1} a_{3} & =b_{1} b_{2}+b_{2} b_{3}+b_{1} b_{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that if $a_{1} \leqslant b_{1}$, then $a_{3} \leqslant b_{3}$.

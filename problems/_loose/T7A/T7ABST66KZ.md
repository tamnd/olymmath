There are $n>1$ matches in a pile, and two players, A and B, take turns to pick matches according to the following rules: (1) at least 1 match must be taken each time; (2) the first player cannot take all the matches on the first turn; (3) starting from the second turn, the number of matches taken each time cannot exceed twice the number just taken. The player who takes the last match wins. Under what circumstances does the first player A have a winning strategy?

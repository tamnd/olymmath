Two circles with the same radius intersect at $X$ and $Y$. Let $P$ be a point on an arc $X Y$ of one circle that is inside the other. Given that the segment $X Y$ is 3 units long and that the angle $X \widehat{P} Y$ measures $120^{\circ}$, what is the area of the intersection between the two circles?
(A) $2\left(\pi-\frac{1}{4} \sqrt{3}\right)$
(B) $3(\pi-\sqrt{3})$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}(3 \pi-\sqrt{3})$
(D) $2\left(\pi-\frac{2}{3} \sqrt{3}\right)$
(E) $2\left(\pi-\frac{3}{4} \sqrt{3}\right)$.

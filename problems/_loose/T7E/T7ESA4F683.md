The seventy-fourth question: Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a positive integer, and consider the undirected graph with vertices being all $\mathrm{n}$-element subsets of $\{1,2, \ldots, 2 \mathrm{n}\}$. The graph satisfies: if $\mathrm{n}$-element subsets $A$ and $B$ meet $B=\{1,2, \ldots, 2 n\} \backslash A$ or $B=\{2 n+1-i \mid i \in A\}$, then an edge is drawn between $A$ and $B$. Find the number of connected components in this undirected graph.

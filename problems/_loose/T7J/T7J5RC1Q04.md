Let $n$ be a positive integer and $M=\{1,2,\ldots, n\}.$ A subset $T\subset M$ is called *heavy* if each of its elements is greater or equal than $|T|.$ Let $f(n)$ denote the number of heavy subsets of $M.$ Describe a method for finding $f(n)$ and use it to calculate $f(32).$

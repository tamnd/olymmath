The extension of the angle bisector $AF$ of angle $A$ of this triangle intersects the circle at point $L$, and the radius $AO$ intersects the side $BC$ at point $E$. Let $AH$ be the altitude of triangle $ABC$. Find the ratio of the area of triangle $OAL$ to the area of quadrilateral $OEF L$, given that $AL=4\sqrt{2}$, $AH=\sqrt{2\sqrt{3}}$, and $\angle AEH=60^{\circ}$.

Let $A$ and $B$ be points on the circle $k$ with center $O$ such that $A B>A O$ holds. Let $C$ be the point of intersection of the bisectors of $\angle O A B$ and $k$ other than $A$. Let $D$ be the point of intersection of the line $A B$ with the circumcircle of the triangle $O B C$ other than $B$. Show that $A D=A O$ holds.

Three. (50 points) A country has several cities and $k$ different airlines. Between any 2 cities, there is either 1 bidirectional direct flight route belonging to some airline, or no route at all. It is known that any 2 routes of the same airline have a common endpoint. Prove: All cities can be divided into $k+2$ groups such that there is no flight route between any 2 cities in the same group.

$$\sqrt{(x+4)^2 + (y+2)^2} + \sqrt{(x-5)^2 + (y+4)^2} \le \sqrt{(x-2)^2 + (y-6)^2} + \sqrt{(x-5)^2 + (y-6)^2} + 20$$

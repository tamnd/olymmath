In tetrahedron $ABCD$, let $P$ be the sum of the lengths of all edges, $V$ be its volume, and $S_{(CD)}$ represent the area of the section passing through the edge $CD$ and the midpoint of the opposite edge $AB$ (i.e., the median plane), with similar notation for other median planes. Prove that: $\frac{1}{S_{(CD)}}+\frac{1}{S_{(AB)}}+$ $\frac{1}{S_{(BC)}}+\frac{1}{S_{(AD)}}+\frac{1}{S_{(BD)}}+\frac{1}{S_{(AC)}} \leqslant \frac{P}{3V}$, with equality if and only if the tetrahedron is a regular tetrahedron.

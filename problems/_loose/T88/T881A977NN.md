A proper divisor of a number is any of its natural divisors, except 1 and the number itself. With a composite natural number $ a $, the following operations are allowed: divide by its smallest proper divisor or add any natural number that is divisible by its largest proper divisor. If the number becomes prime, no further operations can be performed. Is it true that using such operations, any composite number can be transformed into the number 2011? (S. Berlov)

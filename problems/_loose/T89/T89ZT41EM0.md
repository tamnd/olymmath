There is an infinite chessboard. Denote by $(a, b)$ the square located at the intersection of the horizontal line numbered $a$ and the vertical line numbered $b$. A piece from the square $(a, b)$ can move to any of the eight squares: $(a \pm m, b \pm n),(a \pm n, b \pm m)$, where $m, n$ are fixed numbers, and "+" and "-" can be combined arbitrarily. After making $x$ moves, the piece returns to the initial square. Prove that $x$ is even.

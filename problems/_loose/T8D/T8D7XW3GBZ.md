Let the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ have left and right foci $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$, respectively. A line $l$ passing through point $F_{1}$ with an inclination angle $\alpha$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A$ and $B$. If $\cos \alpha=\frac{5}{13}$, $5 \overrightarrow{F_{1} A}=8 \overrightarrow{B F_{1}}$, and the distance from point $F_{2}$ to the line $A B$ is $\frac{72}{13}$.
(1) Find the equation of the ellipse $C$;
(2) If $P$ and $Q$ are two points on the right directrix of the ellipse $C$, and $|P Q|=3$, find the equation of the trajectory of the incenter $M$ of $\triangle F_{1} P Q$.

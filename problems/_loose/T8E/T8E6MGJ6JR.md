Let the numbers $c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{n}$ be given; introduce the following abbreviations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(c_{1}-x\right)\left(c_{2}-x\right)\left(c_{3}-x\right) \ldots\left(c_{n}-x\right)=P_{n}(x) \\
& \left(c_{1}+x\right)\left(c_{2}+x\right)\left(c_{3}+x\right) \ldots\left(c_{n}+x\right)=Q_{n}(x)
\end{aligned}
$$

Which powers of $x$ appear in the polynomial form of $P_{n}(x)+Q_{n}(x)$ and $P_{n}(x)-Q_{n}(x)$ when $n=2,3,4,5,6$? (Justification.)

[5 points] A magician has a set of $12^{2}$ different cards. Each card has one side red and the other side blue; on each card, there is a natural number from 1 to 12 written on both sides. We will call a card a duplicate if the numbers on both sides of the card are the same. The magician wants to draw two cards such that at least one of them is a duplicate, and no number appears on both drawn cards simultaneously. In how many ways can he do this?

It is known that a cube with an edge length of 100 is formed by stacking one million unit cubes, the edges of which are connected to form the framework of the large cube. A set of three mutually perpendicular edges emanating from a vertex of a unit cube is called a frame. Can the entire framework be divided into a set of frames, none of which share a common edge?

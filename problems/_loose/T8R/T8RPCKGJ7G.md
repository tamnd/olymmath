Given an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$ with the midpoints of sides $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ being $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ respectively. From $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$, perpendiculars are drawn to the other two sides of $\triangle A B C$, intersecting at points $A_{2}$, $B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$ respectively. Prove that the area of hexagon $A_{1} C_{2} B_{1} A_{2} C_{1} B_{2}$ is equal to half the area of $\triangle A B C$.

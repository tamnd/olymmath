Let $ x, y, z $ be three real numbers such that:
1. $ y, x, z $ form a harmonic sequence; and
2. $ 3xy, 5yz, 7zx $ form a geometric sequence.

The numerical value of $ \frac{y}{z} + \frac{z}{y} $ can be expressed in the form $ \frac{p}{q} $ , where $ p $ and $ q $ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $ p + q $ ?

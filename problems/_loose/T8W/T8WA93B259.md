Show that it is possible to arrange 16 different numbers, appropriately selected from the integers ranging from 1 to 30, in a rectangular grid divided into 4 rows and 4 columns, so that the product of the numbers in each row and each column is the same.

Is it possible to have such a selection and arrangement where the product of the numbers along the two diagonals is also equal to the product of the numbers along the rows and columns?

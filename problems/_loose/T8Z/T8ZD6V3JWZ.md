We call a triangle *conventional* if each of its angles is at least $30^{\circ}$.

A triangle is marked by the following procedure. We divide the circumference of a circle into 180 equal arcs and randomly select 3 different points from the division points. (Any set of three division points is chosen with equal probability.) What is the probability that the selected division points form a conventional triangle?

What is the probability of selecting a conventional triangle if the vertices can be chosen arbitrarily on the circumference of the circle?

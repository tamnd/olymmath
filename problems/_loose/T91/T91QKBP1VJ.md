In a box, there are 12 red, 15 blue, and 8 yellow balls, which differ only in color. Anke wants to take out a number of these balls with her eyes closed. She wants to choose the number in such a way that she can be sure that among the balls taken out, there will be 5 of the same color.

She says: "It is sufficient to take out 15 balls for this."

Birgit says: "Even 13 balls are sufficient."

Cornelia claims: "12 balls are sufficient for this."

Decide for each of the three statements whether it is true, and justify your decision!

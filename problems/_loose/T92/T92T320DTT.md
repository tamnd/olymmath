Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots$ be some sequence of events from $\mathscr{F}$. Show that

$$
\varlimsup A_{n} \backslash \underline{\lim } A_{n}=\varlimsup\left(A_{n} \backslash A_{n+1}\right)=\varlimsup\left(A_{n+1} \backslash A_{n}\right)=\varlimsup\left(A_{n} \triangle A_{n+1}\right)
$$

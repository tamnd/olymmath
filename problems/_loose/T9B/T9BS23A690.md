Alice and Bob play the following game: Alice chooses an integer $n>2$ and an integer $c$ such that $0<c<n$. She writes $n$ integers on the board. Bob chooses a permutation $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ of the integers. Bob wins if $\left(a_{1}-a_{2}\right)\left(a_{2}-a_{3}\right) \ldots\left(a_{n}-a_{1}\right)$ is 0 or $c$ modulo $n$. Who has a winning strategy?

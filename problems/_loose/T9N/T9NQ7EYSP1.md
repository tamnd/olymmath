Consider the sequence $a(n)$ defined by the following conditions: $$ a(1) = 1\,\,\, a(n + 1) = a(n) + [\sqrt{a(n)}] \,\, , \,\,\, n = 1,2,3,... $$ Prove that the sequence contains an infinite number of perfect squares. (Note: $[x]$ means the integer part of $x$ , that is the greatest integer not greater than $x$ .)

(A Andjans)

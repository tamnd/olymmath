We denote $E, F$ and $G$ as the symmetrics of $A$ with respect to the lines $(BC), (CD)$ and $(DB)$. Finally, we assume that the line $(BD)$ intersects the segments $[AE]$ and $[AF]$ at two points $K$ and $L$.

Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $BEK$ and $DFL$ are tangent to each other at $G$.

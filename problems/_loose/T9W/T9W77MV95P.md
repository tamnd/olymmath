In the equilateral triangle $ABC$, $D$ is on the opposite ray of the $(BC$ ray such that $[DB] \equiv [BC]$. The point $E$ is in the half-plane determined by the line $AD$ that does not contain the point $B$. We know that the distance of point $E$ from the line $AB$ is equal to the length of the segment $[EA]$, the distance of point $E$ from the line $DC$ is equal to the length of the segment $[ED]$, and $EA = ED$. Let $F$ be a point such that $D \in (BF)$ and $[FD] \equiv [BC]$.

a) Prove that $\Delta FDE \equiv \Delta BAE$.

b) Prove that $EB$ is the angle bisector of $\widehat{AED}$!

Color the vertices of a regular $n$-gon red or blue. Then color the diagonals and sides connecting vertices of the same color green, and the diagonals and sides connecting vertices of different colors brown.

Prove that if among the equal-length diagonals there are as many brown ones as green, and among the sides there are also as many brown ones as green, then $n$ can only be a perfect square.

Is there a coloring that satisfies the above conditions besides $n=16$?

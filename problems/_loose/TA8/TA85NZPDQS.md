The sequence $\{a_i\}_{i \ge 1}$ is defined by $a_1 = 1$ and $$ a_n = \lfloor a_{n-1} + \sqrt{a_{n-1}} \rfloor $$ for all $n \ge 2$ . Compute the eighth perfect square in the sequence.

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

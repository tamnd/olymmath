In the language of the mumbo-jumbo tribe, there are only 4 letters: $a, u, o$ and $e$. The letter $e$ is special: if we insert $e$ into a word - once or multiple times, at the beginning, end, or somewhere in between - the meaning of the word does not change. Additionally, the letter groups aаaaaaa, ииuиuии, ооооооo in a word can be replaced by 1 $e$ without changing the meaning of the word. The meaning of a word also does not change if the aaau group in it is replaced by ua, the oou group by $o u$, or the aaaao group by oa.

The tribe has 400 members. Could each of them have a different name?

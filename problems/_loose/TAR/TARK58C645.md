Find the smallest positive integer $n$, such that when $n$ different numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ are chosen from the interval $(1,1000)$, there always exist two numbers $a_{i}, a_{j}, 1 \leqslant i, j \leqslant n$, satisfying the inequality
$$
0<a_{i}-a_{j}<1+3 \sqrt[3]{a_{i} a_{j}} .
$$

Let $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive numbers, and $\alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{n}$ be pairwise distinct positive numbers. For $x>0$, let $f(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} x^{\alpha_{i}}$. Choose $a$ and $\alpha$ such that the graphs of the functions $y=f(x)$ and $y=a x^{\alpha}$ touch at $x=x_{0}$. Prove that $f(x) \geqslant a x^{\alpha}$, and equality is achieved only at $x=x_{0}$.

See also problems $10.13,13.10,25.21$.

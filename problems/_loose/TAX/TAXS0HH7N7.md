Let $w(x)$ be the number of sign changes in the sequence $f(x), f_{1}(x), \ldots, f_{n}(x)$. Prove that the number of roots of the polynomial $f$ (not counting multiplicities), lying between $a$ and $b$, where $f(a) \neq 0, f(b) \neq 0$ and $a<b$, is exactly $w(a)-w(b)$ (Sturm).

Place some stones on a 10-row 14-column rectangular chessboard, allowing more than 1 stone to be placed in each unit square, and then find that there is an odd number of stones in each row and each column. If the unit squares on the chessboard are alternately colored black and white, prove: the number of stones on the black squares is even.

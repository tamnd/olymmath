In triangle $ABC$ , $\angle B = 90^{\circ}, AB=1$ and $BC=2$ . On the side $BC$ there are two points $D$ and $E$ such that $E$ lies between $C$ and $D$ and $DEFG$ is a square, where $F$ lines of $AC$ and $G$ lies on the circle through $B$ with centre $A$ . If the area of $DEFG$ is $\dfrac{m}{n}$ where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers with $\gcd(m,n) = 1$ , what is the value of $m+n$ ?

Read the following equalities from left to right and from right to left and formulate the corresponding rules for operations with radicals:
1) $\sqrt[n]{a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{k}}=\sqrt[n]{a_{1}} \sqrt[n]{a_{2}} \ldots \sqrt[n]{a_{k}}$
2) $\sqrt[n]{\frac{a}{b}}=\frac{\sqrt[n]{a}}{\sqrt[n]{b}}$.

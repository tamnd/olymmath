At the beginning, a natural number $n$ is given. Jurica chooses two natural numbers $a$ and $b$ whose product is the number $n$, and then repeats the process with the number $a+b$ instead of $n$.

Determine, depending on the number $n$, the smallest possible natural number that Jurica can obtain as a result after a finite number of steps.

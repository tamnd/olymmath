King Arthur provides 3 types of sports for his bodyguards: swimming, chess playing, and tennis playing. About the bodyguards, we know the following:

a) among the tennis players, exactly those swim who also play chess;

b) there is no chess player who neither swims nor plays tennis;

c) those who do not swim but play tennis also play chess.

Which of the following statements are true:

a) every chess player swims;

b) every swimmer plays tennis;

c) every tennis player plays chess.

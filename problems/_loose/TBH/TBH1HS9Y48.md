When designing a certain technical device, there arose a need to solve equations

$$
a \circ x=b,
$$

where the operation $\circ$ over two numbers is defined by the condition

$$
y \circ z=\frac{y+z+|y-z|}{2} \text {. }
$$

Find all numerical sets $X$ such that for any $a, b$ from $X$ the specified equation has a unique solution $x$ and this solution belongs to the set $X$.

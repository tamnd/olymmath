Consider the natural numbers $a=3 n+2, b=2 n+1$ and $c=n+1, n \in \mathbb{N}$.

a) Prove that $a$ and $b$ are coprime.

b) Show that the number $[a, b]+[a, c]$ is a perfect square, for any natural number $n$ (denoted by $[x, y]$ the least common multiple of the numbers $x$ and $y$).

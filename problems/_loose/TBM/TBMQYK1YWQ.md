Adam and Bára play a game with the fraction

$$
\frac{10 a+b}{10 c+d}
$$

in four moves: The players alternately replace any of the yet undetermined letters $a, b, c, d$ with a digit from 1 to 9. Bára wins if the resulting fraction is equal to either an integer or a number with a finite number of decimal places; otherwise, Adam wins (for example, if the fraction $\frac{11}{29}$ is created). If Adam starts, how should Bára play to ensure she always wins? If Bára starts, is it possible to advise Adam so that he always wins?

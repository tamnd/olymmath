Card Puzzle. Take 13 cards of the diamond suit from the deck and, placing the five on top and the king at the bottom, stack them in the following order: five, jack, ten, ace, seven, eight, four, two, queen, six, nine, three, king. Now lay them out in a row according to the following rule. Name the cards in the correct order*. The word "ace" consists of three letters, so you will sequentially move the top three cards of our stack to the bottom, and lay the fourth card on the table.

The word "two" contains three letters, so move three cards from the top to the bottom, and place the fourth card to the right of the first one laid on the table. Proceed in the same manner.

For example, the word "jack" consists of five letters, so, after moving the top five cards to the bottom, place the sixth card on the table to the right of the others. When you reach the end, you will find that your cards are arranged in the correct order.

Can you do the same with a whole deck of cards so that the diamonds come first, followed by the hearts, then the spades, and finally the clubs?

(GDR, 73). Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, in which $A B=A D$ and $C B=C D$. Prove that:

a) a circle can be inscribed in it;

b) a circle can be circumscribed around it if and only if $A B \perp B C$;

c) if $A B \perp B C$, then the square of the distance between the center of the inscribed circle (radius $r$) and the center of the circumscribed circle (radius $R$) is

$$
R^{2}+r^{2}-r \sqrt{r^{2}+4 R^{2}}
$$

Given a circle $k(S, r)$ and points $M, N$ on it such that the angle $M S N$ is acute. Through any point $X$ on the smaller arc $M N$, draw a line parallel to the line $M S$ and denote $Y$ as its intersection with the segment $S N$. Construct such a point $X$ for which the area of triangle $S X Y$ is maximized.

Suppose there are $n$ blue points and $n$ red points on a plane, where no three points are collinear and the vertices of the boundary polygon are of the same color. Prove that a straight line can be drawn to divide the $2n$ points into two sides, such that the number of points on each side is not zero and the number of red and blue points on each side is equal.

Along the beach, parking spaces of the same length are marked. Vehicles park in a row, and all drivers observe the marked spaces. The parking lot is reserved only for buses, and each bus occupies exactly three parking spaces.

At a certain moment, only two buses were standing in the parking lot - one occupied the fifth to seventh spaces from the left, the other occupied the eighth to tenth spaces from the right. Later, four more buses parked there, and if one more had arrived, there would not have been enough space for it. Determine the maximum number of parking spaces that could be in the parking lot.

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ and $t_{1}<\cdots<t_{n}$ be strictly positive integers. $t_{n}+1$ people participate in a chess tournament. Each pair of participants plays against each other at most once. Show that the following two conditions can be satisfied simultaneously:

i The number of games played by each participant is equal to one of the $t_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$.

ii For every $1 \leqslant i \leqslant n$, there exists a participant who has played exactly $t_{i}$ games.

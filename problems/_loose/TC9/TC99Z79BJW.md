A buyer came to an antique shop. The trader laid out 2022 coins on the table, among which there are genuine and counterfeit coins, and warned the buyer that the genuine coins among them are more than half. To the buyer, all the coins look indistinguishable, while the trader knows exactly which coins are genuine and which are counterfeit.

In one move, the following happens:

- the buyer points to any two coins,
- the trader says whether they are of the same type,
- the buyer removes one of these two coins from the table.

Can the buyer ensure that after 2021 moves, a genuine coin is guaranteed to remain on the table?

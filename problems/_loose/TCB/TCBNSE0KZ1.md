The vertices of a pyramid's base lie on the circumference of a circle. It is to be proven that the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from the foot of the pyramid's height to the side edges also lie on a circle. (The height of the pyramid is the perpendicular dropped from the vertex outside the base plane to the base plane. The pyramid in question can be "oblique," meaning the foot of the height does not necessarily coincide with the center of the mentioned circle.)

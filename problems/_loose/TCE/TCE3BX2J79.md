On the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ of triangle $ABC$, isosceles triangles $AC_1B$, $BA_1C$, and $AB_1C$ are constructed with the angle at the base $\varphi$ (all three either externally or internally simultaneously). Prove that the lines $AA_1$, $BB_1$, and $CC_1$ intersect at a single point lying on the Kiepert hyperbola.

Note. The following points lie on the Kiepert hyperbola: the orthocenter ($\varphi=\pi / 2$), the centroid ($\varphi=0$), the Torricelli points ($\varphi= \pm \pi / 3$), and the vertices of the triangle ($\varphi=-\alpha,-\beta,-\gamma$).

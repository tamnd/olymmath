For a $2 m \times n$ rectangular grid paper, each cell is painted one of two colors, blue or green, such that the number of cells of each color is equal, and the cell at the bottom left corner is blue, while the cell at the top right corner is green. Connect the centers of all blue cells with line segments, and similarly connect the centers of all green cells with line segments. Prove that it is possible to label each line segment with an arrow such that the sum of all the resulting vectors is the zero vector.

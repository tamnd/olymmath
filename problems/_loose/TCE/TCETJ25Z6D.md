(10th Hope Cup - Senior High School Mathematics Test) Given $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, constants $p, q$ are both greater than 1, and neither equals 2, then $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{p^{n+1}-q^{n}}{p^{n+2}-2 q^{n+1}}$ equals
A. $\frac{1}{p}$ or $\frac{1}{2 q}$
B. $-\frac{1}{p}$ or $-\frac{1}{2 q}$
C. $\frac{1}{p}$ or $\frac{1}{2 q}$ or $\frac{p-1}{p^{2}-2 p}$
D. $-\frac{1}{p}$ or $-\frac{1}{2 q}$ or $\frac{1-p}{p^{2}-2 p}$

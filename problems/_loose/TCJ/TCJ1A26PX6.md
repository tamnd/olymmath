Through the vertex $A$ of an equilateral triangle $A B C$, a secant is drawn, intersecting the side $B C$ at point $M$, and the line through vertex $C$ parallel to side $A B$ at point $N$. Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{m^{2}}-\frac{1}{m n}+\frac{1}{n^{2}}=\frac{1}{a^{2}}
$$

where $m=A M, n=A N, a$ is the side of the triangle.

Let's examine for which natural numbers $n$ there exist exactly four natural numbers $k$ such that the number $n+k$ is a divisor of the number $n+k^2$.

a) Show that $n=58$ satisfies this condition, and find the corresponding four $k$.

b) Prove that even $n=2p$, where $p \geq 3$, satisfies this condition if and only if both $p$ and $2p+1$ are prime numbers.

(Zero is not considered a natural number.)

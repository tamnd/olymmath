In triangle $A B C$, $A A_{1}$ is the altitude, $H$ is the orthocenter. Let $P$ be an arbitrary point on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$, and $M$ be a point on the line $H P$ such that $|H P| \cdot|H M|=\left|H A_{1}\right| \cdot|H A|(H$ is on the segment $M P$ if triangle $A B C$ is acute and outside it if it is obtuse). Prove that $M$ lies on the nine-point circle of triangle $A B C$.

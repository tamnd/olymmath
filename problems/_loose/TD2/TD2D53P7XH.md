A circle passes through the vertices $Q$ and $E$ of triangle $M Q E$ and intersects its sides $M Q$ and $M E$ at points $B$ and $D$, respectively, different from the vertices of the triangle. The ratio of the area of triangle $B D M$ to the area of triangle $M Q E$ is $\frac{9}{121}$.

a) Find the ratio $Q E: B D$.

b) Suppose it is additionally known that the ratio of the areas of triangles $B M E$ and $D Q M$ is 4. Find the ratio $B Q: D E$.

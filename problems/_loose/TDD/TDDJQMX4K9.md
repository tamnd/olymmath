Given a point $A$ on a circle with center $O$ and a line $d$ passing through $O$. A secant from $A$ intersects the circle at $B$ and the line $d$ at $D$. Prove that as the secant rotates around $A$, in any position, the circle passing through $O, B, D$ intersects at another fixed point outside $O$.

It is known that 12 girls in a class were all born in the same year, but no two were born in the same month. If you multiply the number representing the day of the birth date by the number representing the month of the birth date, you get the following products for the twelve girls:

Astrid 49, Beate 3, Christina 52, Doris 130, Evelyn 187, Friederike 300, Gudrun 14, Heike 42, Ines 81, Kerstin 135, Liane 128, and Martina 153.

Determine the birthday of each of the twelve girls from these data!

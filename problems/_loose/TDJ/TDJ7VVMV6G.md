For a simple odd number $p$ let

$$
f(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{p-1} \frac{x^{k}}{k}
$$

Prove that $f(2)-f(-1)$ can be represented as an irreducible fraction, the numerator of which is divisible by $p$.

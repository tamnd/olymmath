$17^{+}$. To prove the implication (ii) $\rightarrow$ (i), for a given orientation of a graph $G$ that does not contain directed cycles, consider one of its maximal acyclic directed subgraphs $D$. To $k$-color its underlying undirected graph, use the fact that all directed paths in $D$ are short, thereby proving that this coloring is also a $k$-coloring of $G$.

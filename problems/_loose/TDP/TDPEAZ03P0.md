Positive numbers $a, b, c, d$ are such that the numbers $a^{2}, b^{2}, c^{2}, d^{2}$ in the given order form an arithmetic progression and the numbers $\frac{1}{a+b+c}, \frac{1}{a+b+d}, \frac{1}{a+c+d}, \frac{1}{b+c+d}$ in the given order form an arithmetic progression. Prove that $a=b=c=d$. (20 points)

For $\triangle ABC$, take an arbitrary point $M$ on the arc $\widehat{BC}$ of its circumcircle; the distance $AM$ intersects $BC$ at point $D$. Show that

$$
\frac{\sin \alpha}{M D}=\frac{\sin \beta}{M B}+\frac{\sin \gamma}{M C}
$$

where $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles of the triangle.

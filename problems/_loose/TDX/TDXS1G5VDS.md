Let $a, b$ be positive numbers, and $a \neq 1, b \neq 1$, prove:
$$
\frac{a^{5}-1}{a^{4}-1} \cdot \frac{b^{5}-1}{b^{4}-1}>\frac{25}{64}(a+1)(b+1) .
$$

$\mathcal{A}$ is a family of subsets of an $n$-element set $X$. If every subset $B$ of $X$ is comparable to at least one subset $A$ in $\mathcal{A}$ (i.e., $B \subseteq A$ or $A \subseteq B$), then $\mathcal{A}$ is called a transversal family. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the smallest transversal family (i.e., $\mathcal{A}$ is a transversal family but any of its proper subfamilies is not a transversal family). Prove: $|\mathcal{A}| \leqslant C_{n}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]}$.

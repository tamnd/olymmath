Let $a, b, c$ be the altitudes of triangle $A$ .
a) Let $r > 0$ be a real number. Prove that there exists a triangle $A$ with area $\frac12$ and $abc < r$ .
b) Prove that for all triangles $A$ with area $\frac12$ , it is true that $abc < 1$ .

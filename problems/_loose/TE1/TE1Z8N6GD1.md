Let $A$ and $B$ be distinct points in a plane. Furthermore, let $\omega\left(0^{\circ}<\omega<90^{\circ}\right)$ be a given oriented angle. For any point $X$, denote by $X_{A}, X_{B}$ the images of point $X$ under rotations about centers $A$ and $B$ by angle $\omega$. Find all points $X$ such that the triangle $X X_{A} X_{B}$ is equilateral.

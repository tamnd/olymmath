4・113 Try to find the necessary and sufficient condition for the system of equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x+p y=n, \\
x+y=p^{z} .
\end{array}\right.
$$
(where $n$ and $p$ are given natural numbers) to have positive integer solutions $(x, y, z)$. Also prove that the number of such solutions cannot be greater than 1.

Given an angle with vertex $S$ and size $60^{\circ}$. On one of its sides lies a point $P$. From $P$, a perpendicular is dropped to the other side. The intersection of this perpendicular with the bisector of the given angle is called $Q$.

Prove that $Q$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of the segment $S P$!

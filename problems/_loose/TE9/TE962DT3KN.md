Let $a$ and $b$ be strictly positive numbers that satisfy the relation: $a^{2}+b^{2}=1$.

a) Show that $a b \leq \frac{1}{2}$.

b) Show that $\sqrt{a^{4}+4 b^{2}}+\sqrt{b^{4}+4 a^{2}}=3$.

c) Determine the intersection and union of the intervals

$$
I_{1}=\left(-\infty, \frac{a+b}{2}\right] \text { and } I_{2}=\left[\sqrt{a b}, \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}\right]
$$

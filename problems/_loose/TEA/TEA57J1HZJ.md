In a convex 2018-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{2018}$ (not necessarily regular), the sides $A_{1} A_{2}$ and $A_{3} A_{4}$ are extended to intersect at point $B_{2}$; the same is done with the pairs of sides $A_{2} A_{3}$ and $A_{4} A_{5}$ (resulting in point $B_{3}$), $\ldots, A_{2017} A_{2018}$ and $A_{1} A_{2}$ (resulting in point $B_{2018}$), $A_{2018} A_{1}$ and $A_{2} A_{3}$ (resulting in point $B_{1}$).

In the end, a "star" $A_{1} B_{1} A_{2} B_{2} A_{3} B_{3} A_{4} \ldots B_{2018} A_{1}$ is obtained. Find the sum of the angles $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots, B_{2018}$ of this "star".

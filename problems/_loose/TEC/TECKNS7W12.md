Let $m$ and $r_{1}, r_{2}, \cdots, r_{m}$ be positive integers, and $\lambda$ be a positive real number. Prove: the number of integer solutions $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{m}\right)$ satisfying the system of inequalities
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{x_{i}}{r_{i}} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}(m-\lambda), 0 \leqslant x_{i} \leqslant r_{i} (i=1,2, \cdots, m)
$$

is at most $\frac{\sqrt{2 m}}{\lambda}\left(r_{1}+1\right)\left(r_{2}+1\right) \cdots\left(r_{m}+1\right)$.

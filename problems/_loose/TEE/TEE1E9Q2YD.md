The real numbers $x$ , $y$ , $z$ , and $t$ satisfy the following equation:
$$2x^2 + 4xy + 3y^2 - 2xz -2 yz + z^2 + 1 = t + \sqrt{y + z - t} $$
Find 100 times the maximum possible value for $t$ .

The first term of an arithmetic sequence is $a_{1}$, the sum of the first and $n$-th term is $s_{a}$, and the corresponding data for another arithmetic sequence are $b_{1}, s_{b}$. A third sequence is formed such that the term of any given order is equal to the product of the terms of the same order from the first two sequences. What is the sum of the first $n$ terms of the third sequence?

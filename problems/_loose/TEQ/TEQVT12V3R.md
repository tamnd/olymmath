In the plane, 15 points are marked. Some are colored red, some blue, and the rest green. It is known that the number of red points is greater than both the number of blue points and the number of green points. The sum of the lengths of all segments where one endpoint is red and the other is green is 31. The sum of the lengths of all segments where one endpoint is green and the other is blue is 25. The sum of the lengths of all segments where one endpoint is blue and the other is red is 5.

Determine the number of points of each color.

Prove: If $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}, D^{\prime}$ are on the sides $A B$, $B C$, $C D$, $D A$ of quadrilateral $A B C D$ respectively, and
$$
\frac{A A^{\prime}}{A^{\prime} B}=\frac{B B^{\prime}}{B^{\prime} C}=\frac{C C^{\prime}}{C^{\prime} D}=\frac{D D^{\prime}}{D^{\prime} A}=\lambda,
$$

then
the area of $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is $\geqslant \frac{1}{2}$ the area of $A B C D$.

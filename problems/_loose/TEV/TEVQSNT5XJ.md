Color each vertex of a convex $n(n \geqslant 4)$-gon either black or white. If the two endpoints of a diagonal are of different colors, then the diagonal is called "bichromatic". If a coloring of the vertices allows the convex $n$-gon to be triangulated using bichromatic diagonals, then the coloring is called "good". Determine the number of good colorings.

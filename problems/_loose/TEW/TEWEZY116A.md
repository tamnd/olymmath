Suppose that $P(x)$ is a polynomial with real coefficients, such that for some positive real numbers $c$ and $d$, and for all natural numbers $n$, we have $c|n|^3\leq |P(n)|\leq d|n|^3$.

Prove that $P(x)$ has a real zero.

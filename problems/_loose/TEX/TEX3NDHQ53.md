Your shoes exchange their positions every day by phone, and they do so in such a way that any two of them talk to each other exactly once. One day, each shoe called at least one of the others. Prove that there exist three shoes such that the first called the second, the second called the third, and the third called the first.

The tasks should be solved independently. You have 210 minutes for solving. The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not allowed.

49th Mathematical Competition for High School Students in Slovenia Velenje, April 16, 2005

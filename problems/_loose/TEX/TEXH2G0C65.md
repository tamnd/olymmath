39-3 (N45, Belarus) For any positive integer $n$, let $d(n)$ denote the number of positive divisors of $n$ (including 1 and $n$ itself). Determine all possible positive integers $k$ such that there exists a positive integer $n$ satisfying:
$$
\frac{d\left(n^{2}\right)}{d(n)}=k .
$$

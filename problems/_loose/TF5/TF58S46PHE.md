Let's prove that if $a, b, c$ are the sides of a triangle, $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles, and $r$ is the radius of the circumcircle, then:

$$
\frac{a}{\cos \alpha}+\frac{b}{\cos \beta}+\frac{c}{\cos \gamma}=2 r \operatorname{tg} \alpha \cdot \operatorname{tg} \beta \cdot \operatorname{tg} \gamma
$$

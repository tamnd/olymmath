We decompose a number into its prime factors; let $N=a^{\alpha} \times b^{\beta} \times c^{\gamma}$. First, we need to list all divisors of this number; second, determine the number of all divisors; third, prove that if these divisors are arranged in increasing order, the product of two divisors equidistant from the ends is equal to $N$; finally, calculate the product of all divisors of the given number. The results obtained should be applied to the number 630.

a) Prove that for any $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}$ strictly positive we have

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{a}+\frac{y^{2}}{b} \geq \frac{(x+y)^{2}}{a+b}
$$

b) Let $t \in[0,1]$. Prove that

$$
\frac{t^{2}}{a^{2}-t^{2}} \leq \frac{t}{a^{2}-1}, a>1
$$

c) Let $x, y, z$ be positive real numbers with sum 1 and let $a$ be real, $a>1$. Show that

$$
\begin{gathered}
\frac{27}{9 a^{2}-1} \leq \frac{1}{a^{2}-x^{2}}+\frac{1}{a^{2}-y^{2}}+\frac{1}{a^{2}-z^{2}} \leq \frac{3 a^{2}-2}{a^{2}\left(a^{2}-1\right)} \\
\text { G.M. }
\end{gathered}
$$

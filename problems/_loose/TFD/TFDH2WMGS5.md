Prove: For each positive odd number $k$, there exist positive integers $\beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{t}$ (which can be the same), such that
$$
k=\left(2-\frac{1}{\beta_{1}}\right) \cdot\left(2-\frac{1}{\beta_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(2-\frac{1}{\beta_{t}}\right) .
$$

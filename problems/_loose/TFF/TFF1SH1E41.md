Two friends need to get to the neighboring city. They have one bicycle that only one person can ride at a time. What is the minimum time it will take for both to reach the city (considering the last one to arrive), if their walking speeds are $u_{1}$ and $u_{2}$, their cycling speeds are $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$, and the distance to the city is $S$ (they can return and leave the bicycle for each other)?

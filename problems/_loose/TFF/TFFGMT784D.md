The sides of the given triangle are the diagonals of three parallelograms. The sides of these parallelograms are parallel to two lines $-l$ and $p$. Prove that the three diagonals of these parallelograms, different from the sides of the triangle, intersect at one point $M$. Find the geometric locus of points $M$ if $l$ and $p$ are two arbitrary perpendicular lines.

A polygon is circumscribed around a circle. The points of tangency of its sides with the circle serve as the vertices of a second polygon inscribed in this circle. Prove that the product of the distances from any point $M$ on the circle to the sides (or their extensions) of one polygon is equal to the product of the distances from this point to the sides (or their extensions) of the second.

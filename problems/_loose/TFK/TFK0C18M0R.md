The king of a country plans to build $n$ cities and $n-1$ roads, such that each road connects two cities without passing through any other cities, and every two cities can reach each other. The shortest distances between these $n$ cities are exactly $1,2,3, \cdots, C_{n}^{2}=\frac{1}{2} n(n-1)$. Can the king's plan be realized in the following cases:
(1) $n=6$; (2) $n=2004$.

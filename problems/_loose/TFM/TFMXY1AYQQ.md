Let $\mathrm{m}$ be a positive integer, $\mathrm{n}=2^{\mathrm{m}}-1, \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{n}}=\{1,2, \ldots, \mathrm{n}\}$ be a set of $\mathrm{n}$ points on a number line. A grasshopper jumps among these points, each step moving from one point to an adjacent point. Find the maximum value of $m$ such that for any $x, y \in P_{n}$, the number of ways to jump from point $x$ to point $y$ in 2012 steps (allowing intermediate visits to points $x, y$) is even.

Given a triangle $ABC$; in its plane, a line $e$ and a point $P$ on it.

$1^{\circ}$. Find a point $X$ on the line $e$ such that

$$
\overline{X A}^{2}+\overline{X B}^{2}+\overline{X C}^{2} \text { is minimized }
$$

$2^{\circ}$. Find the geometric locus of this $X$ point when the line $e$ rotates around the fixed point $P$.

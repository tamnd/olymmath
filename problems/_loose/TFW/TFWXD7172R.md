A non-constant infinite sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies the recurrence relation
$$
a_{n+1}=2 a_{n}+1 \text { or } a_{n+1}=2 a_{n}-1(n=1,2, \cdots) \text {. }
$$

Prove: The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ contains at least one composite number.

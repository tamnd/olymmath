Given a planar quadrilateral $A B C D$. Find the geometric locus of points $M$ such that the lateral surface of the pyramid $A B C D M$ can be intersected by a plane so that the intersection is: a) a rectangle, b) a rhombus, c) a square, d) in the previous case, find the geometric locus of the centers of the squares.

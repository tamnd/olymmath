In a right-angled triangle, we construct squares on the legs, which enclose the right angle of the triangle; from the extreme vertices of these squares, we drop perpendiculars to the hypotenuse of the triangle. Show that the sum of these perpendiculars is equal to the hypotenuse of the triangle. How can we state this theorem for an oblique-angled triangle?

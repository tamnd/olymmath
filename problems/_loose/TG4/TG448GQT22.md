Two players, A and B, take turns removing stones from a pile of $n$ stones. A starts first, and on his first turn, he can take any number of stones but not all and not zero. Thereafter, each player must take a number of stones that is a divisor of the number of stones the other player took on their last turn. The player who takes the last stone wins. For what smallest $n>1992$ does B have a winning strategy?

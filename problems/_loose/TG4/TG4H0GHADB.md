Achim, Bernd, Christian, and Detlef were the four participants in the final round of a chess tournament. Each had to play against each other exactly twice. One point was awarded for each win, half a point for each draw, and zero points for each loss.

A wall newspaper article about this tournament contains the following information:

- Bernd and Christian together scored exactly one point more than Achim and Detlef together.
- Christian and Detlef together scored exactly 7 points.
- Achim and Christian together could achieve exactly 5 points less than Bernd and Detlef together.

The question is, how many points did each of the four participants receive? Determine all answers that correspond to the given information!

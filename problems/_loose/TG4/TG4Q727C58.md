The sequence $a_{n}$ is constructed as follows: $a_{1}, a_{2}$ are arbitrary real numbers, and for $n \geq 3$, the number $a_{n}$ is the smallest of the numbers $\left|a_{i}-a_{j}\right|, 1 \leq$ $i<j \leq n-1$. For example, if $a_{1}=6, a_{2}=\frac{19}{2}$, then we get the sequence $6, \frac{19}{2}, \frac{7}{2}, \frac{5}{2}, 1,1,0,0,0 \ldots$ For some choice of $a_{1}, a_{2}$, the sequence resulted in $a_{10}=1$. Find the smallest possible value of $a_{3}$ in such a sequence.

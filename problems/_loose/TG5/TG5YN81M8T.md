Given three circles $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}$, no two of which are equal, and the centers of the three circles are not collinear, then:
（1）the exsimilicenters of each pair of circles are collinear;
（2）the insimilicenters of each pair of circles, and the exsimilicenter of the third circle, are collinear;
（3）the lines joining one circle's center to the insimilicenters of the other two circles, such three lines are concurrent;
（4）the lines joining one circle's center to the insimilicenters of the other two circles, and the lines joining the centers of the other two circles to their respective exsimilicenters, such three lines are concurrent.

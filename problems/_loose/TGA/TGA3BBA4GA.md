Let $h_{1}, h_{2}, h_{3}$ be the altitudes of triangle $ABC$, and $u, v, w$ be the distances from a point $M$ inside triangle $ABC$ to the corresponding sides. Prove the inequalities:

a) $\frac{h_{1}}{u}+\frac{h_{2}}{v}+\frac{h_{3}}{w} \geqslant 9$;

b) $h_{1} h_{2} h_{3} \geqslant 27 u v w$

c) $\left(h_{1}-u\right)\left(h_{2}-v\right)\left(h_{3}-w\right) \geqslant 8 u v w$.

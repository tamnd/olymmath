Let the points of tangency of the incircle of triangle $ABC$ with the sides opposite to vertices $A, B, C$ be $A', B', C'$, and let the internal angle bisector from $A$ intersect the line $A'B'$ at $B^*$ and the line $A'C'$ at $C^*$.

- Prove that $B'C' \parallel B B^* \parallel C C^*$.
- Find a similar property related to the excircle.

(Vitali's Criterion.) Let $p>0, \mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right|^{p}<\infty, \xi_{n} \xrightarrow{p} \xi$. Prove that the following conditions are equivalent:
1) $\mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right|^{p} \rightarrow \mathrm{E}|\xi|^{p}<\infty$
2) $\lim _{n} \mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right|^{p} \leqslant \mathrm{E}|\xi|^{p}<\infty$
3) the family $\left\{\left|\xi_{n}\right|^{p}\right\}_{n \geqslant 1}$ is uniformly integrable,
4) $\mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}-\xi\right|^{p} \rightarrow 0$.

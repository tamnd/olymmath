There are $N$ sections of children's toy railway tracks, each shaped as a $\frac{1}{4}$ circle with the same radius. Try to prove that when they are connected end-to-end and each transition between two sections is smooth, it is impossible to form a closed loop where the start and end points coincide and the angle between the first and last track sections is 0 (see the right figure).

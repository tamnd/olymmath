Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and let $M$ be the midpoint of $AC$ . Let $\Gamma$ be the circumference with center $M$ that passes through $A$ . The line $BM$ intersects $\Gamma$ at $D$ and $E$ , where $D$ lies inside $ABC$ and $E$ lies outside $ABC$ . Let $r$ be the line perpendicular to $BC$ passing through $B$ , and let $F$ be the intersection of $r$ with line $AD$ . Prove that line $CF$ is tangent to the circumference $\omega$ passing through points $B$ , $C$ , and $E$ .

Determine all pairs $(x, y)$ of real numbers that satisfy the following system of inequalities:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{4}+8 x^{3} y+16 x^{2} y^{2}+16 & \leq 8 x^{2}+32 x y \\
y^{4}+64 x^{2} y^{2}+10 y^{2}+25 & \leq 16 x y^{3}+80 x y
\end{aligned}
$$

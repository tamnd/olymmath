For each $i=1,2,3$, let $x_{i}$ and $y_{i}$ be two distinct real numbers, and let $d_{i}$ denote the larger of the two numbers $x_{i}$ and $y_{i}$.

a) Prove:

If $x_{1} \leq x_{2}+x_{3}$ and $y_{1} \leq y_{2}+y_{3}$, then $d_{1} \leq d_{2}+d_{3}$.

b) Determine whether the following statement is also true.

If $d_{1} \leq d_{2}+d_{3}$, then $x_{1} \leq x_{2}+x_{3}$.

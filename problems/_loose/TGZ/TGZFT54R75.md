Given $\triangle A B C$ with its circumcircle and incircle as $\odot O$ and $\odot I$ respectively, $\odot I$ touches sides $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. Draw $D S \perp E F$ at point $S$. Prove: $S I$ intersects $\odot O$ at a point that is symmetric to point $A$ with respect to point $O$.

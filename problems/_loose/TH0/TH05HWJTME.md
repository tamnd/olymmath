Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}$ be complex numbers, and satisfy
$$
\left|z_{1}\right|+\left|z_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|z_{n}\right|=1,
$$

Prove that among these $n$ complex numbers, there must exist some complex numbers whose sum has a modulus not less than $\frac{1}{6}$.

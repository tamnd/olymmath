a) Prove that all circles and lines are described by equations of the form

$$
A z \bar{z}+c z+\bar{c} \bar{z}+D=0
$$

where $A$ and $D$ are real numbers, and $c$ is a complex number. Conversely, prove that any equation of this form describes either a circle, a line, a point, or an empty set.

b) Prove that under inversion, circles and lines transform into circles and lines.

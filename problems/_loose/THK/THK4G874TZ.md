Given that $c$ is a positive integer not exceeding 2017, and there exist positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that
$$
2^{a} \times 5^{b}=\left(a^{3}+a^{2}+a+1\right) c \text {. }
$$

Find the maximum value of $c$.

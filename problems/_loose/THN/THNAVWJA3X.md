In $\triangle A B C$, points $D$ and $E$ are on sides $A B$ and $A C$ respectively, and $D E / / B C$. Two other points $F$ and $G$ are on side $B C$, and $B F = B D$, $C G = C E$. A circle is drawn through points $G$, $A$, and $B$, and another circle is drawn through points $F$, $A$, and $C$. The two circles intersect at points $A$ and $P$. Prove: $A P$ bisects $\angle B A C$.

Show that there are no polynomials $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ such that the following equation holds for every natural number $n$:

$$
1+\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\ldots+\frac{1}{n}=\frac{p(n)}{q(n)}
$$

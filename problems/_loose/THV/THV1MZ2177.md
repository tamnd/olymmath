A natural number represented in the decimal positional system is called a mirror number if its digits are symmetrically arranged, i.e., if the first and the last, the second and the second-to-last, etc., digits are the same.

For example, the numbers 358853, 27672, 44444 are mirror numbers.

Determine whether, for every two-digit natural number $a$, whose last digit is different from 0, there exists a non-zero mirror number that is divisible by $a$.

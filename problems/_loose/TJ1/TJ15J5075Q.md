(1990 19th US Mathematical Olympiad) The sequence of functions $\left\{f_{n}(x)\right\}$ is recursively defined by:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
f_{1}(x)=\sqrt{x^{2}+48}, \\
f_{n+1}(x)=\sqrt{x^{2}+6 f_{n}(x)}
\end{array} \text { for each } n \geqslant 1 .\right.
$$

Find all real solutions to the equation $f_{n}(x)=2 x$.

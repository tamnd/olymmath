Given an arbitrary triangle $A B C$. On its sides as bases, three isosceles triangles $A K B, B L C, C M A$ are constructed with angles at the vertices $K, L$, and $M$ equal to $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$, respectively, where $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 2 \pi$. Moreover, all three triangles are either outside triangle $A B C$ or inside it. Prove that the angles of triangle $K L M$ are $\alpha / 2, \beta / 2, \gamma / 2$.

(IMO 35 Preliminary) $N$ is any point on the bisector of $\angle B A C$. $P$ and $O$ are points on the lines $A B$ and $A N$ respectively, such that $\angle A N P=90^{\circ}=\angle A P O$. $Q$ is any point on $N P$, and any line through $Q$ intersects the lines $A B$ and $A C$ at points $E$ and $F$ respectively.
Prove: $\angle O Q E=90^{\circ}$ if and only if $Q E=Q F$.

Consider a cube ABCDA'B'C'D', with side length $4 \mathrm{~cm}$, and $P$ a point inside the cube.

a) Prove that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the faces of the cube is constant.

b) Prove that the sum of the distances from $P$ to the vertices of the cube is greater than or equal to $16 \sqrt{3}$.

c) If $P \in [BD']$ such that $BP = 3PD'$, calculate the sum of the distances to the lateral edges AA', BB', CC', DD'.

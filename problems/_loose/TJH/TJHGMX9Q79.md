Given the parabolas $C_{1}: y=x^{2}+2 x$ and $C_{2}: y=-x^{2}+a$, if a line $l$ is simultaneously tangent to $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$, we call $l$ a common tangent of $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$. The line segment between the two points of tangency on the common tangent is called the common tangent segment.
(1) For what value of $a$ does $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ have exactly one common tangent? Write the equation of this common tangent;
(2) If $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ have two common tangents, prove that the corresponding two common tangent segments bisect each other.

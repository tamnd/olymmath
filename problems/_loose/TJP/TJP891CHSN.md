A quadrilateral with mutually perpendicular diagonals is inscribed in a circle $O$ of radius $R$, and the point of intersection $M$ of the diagonals is at a distance $d_{0}$ from the center $O$. Prove that the radii of the circles inscribed and circumscribed around the quadrilateral, whose vertices are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from point $M$ to the sides of the given quadrilateral, are respectively

$$
\frac{R^{2}-d_{0}^{2}}{2 R} \text { and } \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{2 R^{2}-d_{0}^{2}} .
$$

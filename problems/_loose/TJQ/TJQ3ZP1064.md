In triangle $A B C$, if $A B=A C=x+1$ and $B C=2 x-2$, where $x>1$, then the area of the triangle is always equal to
(A) $(x-1) \sqrt{2 x^{2}+2}$
(B) $2(x-1)$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}(x+1)^{2}$
(D) $(x+1)(x-1)$
(E) $2(x-1) \sqrt{x}$

In a math club, there are 6 schoolchildren, each of whom has at least 3 friends in this club (friendship is mutual). If someone gets a book, they read it and then give it to one of their friends who hasn't read the book yet. Prove that the teacher can give a new book to one of the schoolchildren so that it will eventually reach all the participants in the club.

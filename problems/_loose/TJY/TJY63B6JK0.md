(2) Prove: $\csc \alpha=\csc 2 \alpha+\csc 4 \alpha$;
(3) Find the value: $\cos \alpha-\cos 2 \alpha+\cos 3 \alpha$;
(4) Prove: $\cos \alpha$ is a real root of the equation $8 x^{3}+4 x^{2}-4 x-1=0$;
(5) Prove: $\cos \alpha$ is an irrational number;
(6) Find the value: $\tan \alpha \tan 2 \alpha \tan 3 \alpha$;
(7) Find the value: $\tan ^{2} \alpha+\tan ^{2} 2 \alpha+\tan ^{2} 3 \alpha$;
(8) Find the value: $\tan ^{2} \alpha \tan ^{2} 2 \alpha+\tan ^{2} 2 \alpha \tan ^{2} 3 \alpha+\tan ^{2} 3 \alpha \tan ^{2} \alpha$;

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be real numbers, and for a natural number $k(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$, let
$$
S_{k}=\sum_{1 \leqslant i_{1}<\cdots<i_{k} \leqslant n} a_{i_{1}} a_{i_{2}} \cdots a_{i_{k}} \text {, }
$$

Prove:
$$
S_{k} S_{n-k} \geqslant\left(C_{n}^{k}\right)^{2} a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}
$$

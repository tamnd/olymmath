Petya cut out a rectangle from paper, placed another identical rectangle on top of it, and glued them along the perimeter. In the upper rectangle, he drew a diagonal, dropped perpendiculars from the two remaining vertices to this diagonal, cut the upper rectangle along these lines, and folded the resulting triangles outward, so that together with the lower rectangle they formed a rectangle.

How can the original rectangle be restored from the resulting rectangle using a compass and a ruler?

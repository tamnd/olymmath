We place 2020 points on a circle, numbered randomly from 1 to 2020; all numbers are different. For a chord connecting a vertex numbered a to a vertex numbered b, we associate the value $|\mathrm{b}-\mathrm{a}|$. Show that it is possible to connect the vertices by 1010 chords such that

$\triangleright$ the chords do not intersect,

$\triangleright$ exactly one chord starts from each vertex,

$\triangleright$ the sum of the values of the chords is equal to $1010^{2}$.

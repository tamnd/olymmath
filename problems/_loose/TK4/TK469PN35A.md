Given 64 unit cubes, each with one face painted black and the other five faces painted white, and they are placed on an $8 \times 8$ chessboard such that one face of each cube exactly covers one square. Each cube's black face can face any direction. It is allowed to perform the following operation: rotate all the cubes in a row or a column simultaneously. Prove that it is always possible to perform a series of operations such that all the black faces of the cubes are facing up.

A pool can be filled with two pipes. If both pipes are open for 2 hours, 4200 liters of water will be missing to fill the pool to the top, and if both are kept open for 5 hours, the pool will be full to the top and 3000 liters of water will overflow from the pool. The first pipe releases as much water into the pool in two hours as the second pipe releases in three hours. How many liters of water does the pool hold, and how long does it take for each pipe to fill the pool on its own?

(The rates at which the pipes fill the pool are constant.)

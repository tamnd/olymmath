Let ABC be a triangle, $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. Let $\omega_{\mathcal{A}}$ be the circle internally tangent to $(\mathrm{AB}),(\mathrm{AC})$ and to $\Gamma$. Denote $T_{A}$ as the tangency point of $\Gamma$ with $\omega_{A}$. Similarly, define $T_{B}$ and $T_{C}$.

Show that $\left(A T_{A}\right),\left(B T_{B}\right)$ and $\left(C T_{C}\right)$ are concurrent.

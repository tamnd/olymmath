On the bisector of the angle with vertex $A$ , a point $B$ is fixed. On segment $AB$ , an arbitrary point $O$ is chosen for which the circle with center $O$ and radius $OB$ intersects the sides of the angle at four points. Connect two of these points, lying on different sides of the angle and at different distances from $A$ , with a segment. Prove that all segments constructed in this way touch one fixed circle.

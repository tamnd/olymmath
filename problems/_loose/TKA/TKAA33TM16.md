In a deck of 36 cards, the suits are arranged in a periodic sequence: spades, clubs, hearts, diamonds, spades, clubs, hearts, diamonds, and so on. A portion of the deck is removed, flipped as a whole, and reinserted into the remaining part. Cards are then drawn in groups of four. Prove that in each group of four, all suits are different.

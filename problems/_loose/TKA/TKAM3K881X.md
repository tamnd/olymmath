In the field of real numbers, solve the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}-y z=a, \\
& y^{2}-z x=a-1, \\
& z^{2}-x y=a+1
\end{aligned}
$$

with a real parameter $a$. Discuss the number of solutions.

In selecting $k$ different and non-adjacent numbers from the natural numbers from 1 to $n$, let the number of such selection schemes be $f(n, k)$.
（1）Find the recurrence relation for $f(n, k)$.
（2）Use induction to find $f(n, k)$.
（3）If 1 and $n$ are considered adjacent numbers, and if under this assumption the number of ways to select $k$ different and non-adjacent numbers from 1 to $n$ is denoted by $g(n, k)$, use $f(n, k)$ to find $g(n, k)$.

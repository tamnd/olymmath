Let the function $f(x)$ satisfy $f(x)+f(y)=f\left(\frac{x+y}{1+xy}\right)$ and has an inverse function, prove:
(1) The domain of $f(x)$ is $(-1,1)$;
(2) If in some interval $(a, b)$, $f^{-1}(x)>0, (0<a<b)$, then $f^{-1}(x)$ is a monotonically increasing function.

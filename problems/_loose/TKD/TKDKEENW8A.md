Find the geometric locus of the centers of all possible rectangles circumscribed around a given triangle. (A rectangle will be called circumscribed if one vertex of the triangle coincides with a vertex of the rectangle, and the other two lie on the two sides of the rectangle that do not contain this vertex.)

(This question is worth 20 points) In the plane rectangular coordinate system $x O y$, the equation of the ellipse is $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0), A_{1} 、 A_{2}$ are the left and right vertices of the ellipse, respectively, and $F_{1} 、 F_{2}$ are the left and right foci of the ellipse, respectively. $P$ is any point on the ellipse different from $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$. If two points $Q 、 R$ in the plane satisfy $Q A_{1} \perp P A_{1}, Q A_{2} \perp P A_{2}, R F_{1} \perp P F_{1}, R F_{2} \perp P F_{2}$, determine the relationship between the length of line segment $Q R$ and $b$, and provide a proof.

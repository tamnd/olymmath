$O A B C$ tetrahedron has its edges meeting at vertex $O$ pairwise perpendicular to each other. Let the areas of the faces $O B C, O C A, O A B$ be denoted by $t_{A}, t_{B}, t_{C}$, respectively. The spherical excess of the trihedral angles at vertices $A, B, C$ are $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and $\sigma$ is half the sum of the spherical excesses of the four trihedral angles. It is to be proven that

$$
t_{A}: t_{B}: t_{C}=\cos (\sigma-\alpha): \cos (\sigma-\beta): \cos (\sigma-\gamma)
$$[^0]

[^0]: ${ }^{1}$ The spherical excess of the spherical triangles defined by these trihedral angles.

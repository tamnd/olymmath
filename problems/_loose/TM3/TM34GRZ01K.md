In triangle $ABC$, a circle is inscribed, touching the sides $[\bar{B} C], [C A]$, and $[A B]$ at points $M, N$, and $P$ respectively. Prove that the lines $A M, B N$, and $C P$ pass through one point.

28*. On the lines $B C, C A$, and $A B$, which contain the sides of triangle $A B C$, points $X, Y, Z$ are taken, such that $(A X) \cap (B Y) \cap (C Z) = S$. A circle is drawn through points $X, Y, Z$, intersecting $(B C), (C A)$, and $(A B)$ again at points $X^{\prime}, Y^{\prime}$, and $Z^{\prime}$ respectively. Prove that $\left(A X^{\prime}\right), \left(B Y^{\prime}\right)$, and $\left(C Z^{\prime}\right)$ pass through one point.

59 Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be the three interior angles of a triangle, and $x$ satisfies
$$
\cos (x+A) \cdot \cos (x+B) \cdot \cos (x+C) + \cos^3 x = 0.
$$
(1) Prove: $\tan x = \cot A + \cot B + \cot C$.
(2) Prove: $\sec^2 x = \csc^2 A + \csc^2 B + \csc^2 C$.

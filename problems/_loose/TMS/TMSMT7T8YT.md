Show that the only polynomials $\mathrm{P} \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$ that are bounded are those of degree 0.

Proposition 49. Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$. Suppose there exists $P \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ such that $P(\alpha)=0$. Then there exists a monic polynomial $\mu_{\alpha}$ (i.e., with leading coefficient 1) that divides any other polynomial that is annihilated by $\alpha$. Furthermore, $\mu_{\alpha}$ is irreducible and is unique with this property.

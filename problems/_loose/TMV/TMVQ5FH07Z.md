Given two points $A\left(A^{\prime}, A^{\prime \prime}\right), D\left(D^{\prime}, D^{\prime \prime}\right)$ and two distances $\left(\varrho, \varrho_{a}\right)$. One vertex of the triangle $ABC$ is $A$ and the other endpoint of the angle bisector is $D$. The plane of the triangle forms equal angles with the two image planes. $\varrho$ is the radius of the incircle, and $\varrho_{a}$ is the radius of the excircle opposite to side $BC$. The triangle $ABC$ is to be constructed!

On a line, there are 2019 grasshoppers sitting. In each step, one grasshopper jumps over another to the other side, maintaining the same distance. It is known that under the condition that they can only jump to the right, they can achieve a situation where the distance between two grasshoppers is exactly 1 unit. Prove: if it is stipulated that they can only jump to the left, then they can also achieve this.

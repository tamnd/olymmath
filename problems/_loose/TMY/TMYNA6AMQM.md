$x^{2}+x y+y^{2}=a^{2}$,
$y^{2}+y z+z^{2}=b^{2}$,
$x^{2}+x z+z^{2}=c^{2}$.

Express the value of $x y+y z+x z$ in terms of $a, b$ and $c$.

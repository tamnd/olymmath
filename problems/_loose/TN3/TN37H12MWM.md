In an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $A C$. The circle $\Gamma$ passing through points $B$ and $M$ intersects sides $A B$ and $B C$ at the second points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Point $T$ satisfies that quadrilateral $B P T Q$ is a parallelogram. If $T$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$, find all possible values of $\frac{B T}{B M}$.

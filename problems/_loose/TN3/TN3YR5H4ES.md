Some of $n$ towns are connected by two-way airlines. There are $m$ airlines in total. For $i=1,2, \ldots, n$, let $d_{i}$ be the number of airlines going from town $i$. If $1 \leq d_{i} \leq$ 2010 for each $i=1,2, \ldots, 2010$, prove that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} d_{i}^{2} \leq 4022 m-2010 n
$$

Find all $n$ for which equality can be attained.

(Aleksandar Ilić)

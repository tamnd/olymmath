On the plane, there are three points. Any two of them are chosen, a perpendicular bisector to the segment connecting them is constructed, and all points are reflected with respect to this line. Then, from all the points (old and new), any two points are chosen again, and the entire procedure is repeated. This is done infinitely many times. Prove that there will be a line in the plane such that all the obtained points will lie on one side of it.

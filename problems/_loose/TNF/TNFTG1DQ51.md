Given in the plane a regular hexagon. For every point $P$ in the plane, let $l(P)$ be the sum of the six distances between $P$ and the lines on which the sides of the hexagon lie, and $v(P)$ be the sum of the six distances from $P$ to the vertices of the hexagon.

(a) For which points $P$ in the plane is $l(P)$ minimized?

(b) For which points $P$ in the plane is $v(P)$ minimized?

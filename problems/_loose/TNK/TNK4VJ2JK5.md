A positive integer $n{}$ is called *discontinuous* if for all its natural divisors $1 = d_0 < d_1 <\cdots<d_k$ , written out in ascending order, there exists $1 \leqslant i \leqslant k$ such that $d_i > d_{i-1}+\cdots+d_1+d_0+1$ . Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $n{}$ such that $n,n+1,\ldots,n+2019$ are all discontinuous.

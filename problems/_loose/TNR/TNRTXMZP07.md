On a square board with side $N$, where $N$ is some natural number, a chess bishop stands in the bottom-left corner. Alexei and Danil take turns moving this bishop, with Alexei moving first, and players are not allowed to move to cells the bishop has already visited (the bishop moves diagonally to any distance). The player who cannot make a move loses. Which of the boys can guarantee a win regardless of the opponent's actions?

Let $a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{n}$ be given real numbers. If

$$
f:\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\} \rightarrow\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\}
$$

is a bijection such that

$$
a_{1}+f\left(a_{1}\right)<a_{2}+f\left(a_{2}\right)<\ldots<a_{n}+f\left(a_{n}\right)
$$

then $f$ is the identity mapping. Prove!

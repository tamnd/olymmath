a) Prove that the product of $n$ consecutive integers is divisible by $n!$.

b) Prove that the fraction $\frac{n!}{a!b!\ldots k!}$ is an integer if and only if $a+b+\ldots+k \leqslant n$.

c) Prove that $(n!)!$ is divisible by $n!^{(n-1)!}$.

d)* Prove that the product of $n$ consecutive integers forming an arithmetic progression, the difference of which is coprime with $n!$, is divisible by $n!$.

Note. Problem 75d) is a generalization of problem 75a).

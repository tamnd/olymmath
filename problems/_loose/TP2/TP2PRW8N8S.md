If the three vertices of any inscribed equilateral triangle in a circle are all colored with different colors, then such a colored circle is called a "good circle". Let the circle with center $P$ and radius $r$ be denoted as $\odot(P, r)$.
(1) Is it possible to color each point in $\odot(O, 2)$ and its interior with one of the three colors red, yellow, and blue, such that $\odot(O, 2)$ and any circle inscribed in $\odot(O, 2)$ with a radius of at least 1 are all good circles?
(2) What if "three colors" in (1) is changed to "exactly seven colors"? ${ }^{[1]}$

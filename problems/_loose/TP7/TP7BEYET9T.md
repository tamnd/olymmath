Let $e$ and $e^{\prime}$ be skew lines. We draw planes $S$ and $S^{\prime}$ through them such that these planes are perpendicular to each other, and their line of intersection is $d$. $1^{0}$. What is the geometric locus of the intersection points of the plane $\Sigma$ and line $d$, and the plane $\Sigma^{\prime}$ and line $d$ (if $S$ rotates around $e$), where $\Sigma$ is a rigid plane perpendicular to $e$, and $\Sigma^{\prime}$ is a rigid plane perpendicular to $e^{\prime}$? $2^{0}$. What curve do the centers of these geometric loci ($C$ and $C^{\prime}$) trace if $\Sigma$ and $\Sigma^{\prime}$ move parallel to themselves? $3^{0}$. Show that the curves $C$ and $C^{\prime}$ lie on a sphere!

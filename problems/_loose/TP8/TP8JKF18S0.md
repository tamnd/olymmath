Given that quadrilateral $A B C D$ is a rectangle, and it is not a square. $O$ is a point on the perpendicular bisector of $B D$ and lies inside $\triangle B C D$. The circle centered at $O$ and passing through points $B$ and $D$ intersects $A B$ and $D A$ at the second points $E$ and $F$, respectively. The line $B F$ intersects $D E$ at point $G$. The projections of $G$ onto $A B$, $B D$, and $D A$ are $X$, $Y$, and $Z$, respectively. The projections of $O$ onto $C D$, $B D$, and $B C$ are $L$, $M$, and $N$, respectively. The lines $X Y$ and $M L$, $Y Z$ and $M N$ intersect at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove: $B P \parallel D Q$.

Let $E$ be the set of real polynomials with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$ whose all roots are real. The goal of the exercise is to describe $E$.

1. Show that it suffices to know the elements of $E$ that are monic and do not vanish at 0.
2. For $n$ strictly positive real numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$, prove the inequality $\sum_{1 \leq i, j \leq n} \frac{a_{i}}{a_{j}} \geq n^{2}$.
3. Let $P$ be a non-constant monic polynomial that does not vanish at 0 and belongs to $E$. Show that $\operatorname{deg}(P) \leq 3$. Conclude.

Around a circle with radius $r$, a trapezoid is described whose angles at the longer base are $\alpha, \beta$. Prove that the ratio of the areas of the trapezoid and the circle is $\frac{2}{\pi}\left(\frac{1}{\sin \alpha}+\frac{1}{\sin \beta}\right)$.

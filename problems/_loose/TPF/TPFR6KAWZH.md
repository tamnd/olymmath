In the parallelogram $A B C D=N_{0}$, the angle bisectors of its angles form the quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}=N_{1}$, and the angle bisectors of this quadrilateral form the quadrilateral $N_{2}$. It is to be proven that if the acute angle of $N_{0}$ is $30^{\circ}$, then the areas of $N_{1}$ and $N_{2}$ are equal.

In the nodes of a grid of a $4 \times 5$ rectangular grid, there are $30$ light bulbs, all initially turned off. In one move, it is allowed to draw any straight line that does not touch the light bulbs (the size of the light bulbs can be neglected, considering them as points), such that on one side of the line no light bulb is lit, and to turn on all the light bulbs on that side of the line. Each move must light at least one light bulb. Is it possible to light all the light bulbs in exactly four moves?

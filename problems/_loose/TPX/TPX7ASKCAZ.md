Let $A$ be a point on the axis of symmetry of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ (located inside the parabola), and $B$ be the point symmetric to $A$ with respect to the $y$-axis. (I) If a line through $A$ intersects the parabola at points $P, Q$ on either side of the axis of symmetry, then $\angle P B A=\angle Q B A$; (II) If a line through $B$ intersects the parabola at points $P, Q$ on the same side of the axis of symmetry, then $\angle P A B+\angle Q A B=180^{\circ}$.

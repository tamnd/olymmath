In the corner of a chessboard stands a piece. The first player can move it twice in a row like an ordinary knight (two squares in one direction and one in a perpendicular direction), while the second player can move it once like a knight with an extended move (three squares in one direction and one in a perpendicular direction). They take turns moving. The first player aims to place the piece in the opposite corner, while the second player tries to prevent this. Who wins (the board size is $n \times n$, where $n>3$)?

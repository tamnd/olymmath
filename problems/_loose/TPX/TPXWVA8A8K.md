In a certain kingdom, the territory of which has the shape of a square with a side of 2 km, the king decides to summon all the inhabitants to a ball at his palace by 7 PM. To do this, he sends a messenger at noon with a task, who can convey any instruction to any inhabitant, who in turn can convey any instruction to any other inhabitant, and so on. Each inhabitant, before receiving the instruction, is in a known place (at home) and can move in any direction (in a straight line) at a speed of 3 km/h. Prove that the king can organize the notification in such a way that all inhabitants will manage to arrive by the start of the ball.

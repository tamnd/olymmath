On the diagonal $[\mathrm{AC}]$ of the square $\mathrm{ABCD}$, a point $\mathrm{E}$ is taken such that $\frac{E A}{E C}=\frac{1}{2}$. If $\mathrm{AE}=\frac{10 \sqrt{2}}{3} \mathrm{~cm}$, determine the area and perimeter of the square $\mathrm{ABCD}$, as well as the area of the triangle $\mathrm{ABE}$.

( Gheorghe Achim, Mathematics Olympiads and Competitions V-VIII 2014, Bîrchi Publishing )

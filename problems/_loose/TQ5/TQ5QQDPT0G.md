Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}$ be distinct positive real numbers. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a_{1}^{3}}{\left(a_{2}-a_{3}\right)^{2}}+\frac{a_{2}^{3}}{\left(a_{3}-a_{4}\right)^{2}}+\frac{a_{3}^{3}}{\left(a_{4}-a_{1}\right)^{2}}+\frac{a_{4}^{3}}{\left(a_{1}-a_{2}\right)^{2}} \\
>a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+a_{4} .
\end{array}
$$

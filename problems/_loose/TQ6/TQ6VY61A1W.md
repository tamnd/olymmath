$D$ is a point on the extended diameter $A O B$ of the known circle $\odot O$. Draw tangents $D S$ and $D T$ from $D$ to $\odot O$, with $S$ and $T$ being the points of tangency. Draw any secant line through $D$ intersecting $\odot O$ at $E$ and $F$, and let $S T \cap E F=H$. Then $D$ and $H$ harmonically divide $E F$, i.e., $(D H, E F)=-1 ; G F^{2}=G H \cdot G D$.

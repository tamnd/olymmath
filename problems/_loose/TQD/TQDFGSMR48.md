At the beginning of the game, the number 0 is written on the board. Two players take turns. On a turn, a player adds to the written number any natural number not exceeding 10, and writes the result on the board in place of the original number. The player who first obtains a four-digit number wins. Which of the players (the starting player or their opponent) has a winning strategy, i.e., a strategy that allows them to win regardless of how the opponent plays?

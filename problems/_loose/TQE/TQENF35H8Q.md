A right-angled triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ is given, with the lengths of the legs $\mathrm{AB}=\mathrm{acm}$ and $\mathrm{AC}=\mathrm{a} \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~cm}$. This triangle is folded along the median $\mathrm{AM}, \mathrm{M} \in(\mathrm{BC})$, such that the distance between points $\mathrm{B}$ and $\mathrm{C}$ becomes equal to $\mathrm{a} \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~cm}$, and then point $\mathrm{C}$ is projected onto the plane $(\mathrm{ABM})$ at point $\mathrm{O}$.

a) Show that $O B \perp A B$;

b) Calculate the distance from point C to the plane (ABM).

On the line $l$, points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken, and from the vertices of triangle $A B C$ perpendiculars $A A_{2}, B B_{2}$, and $C C_{2}$ are dropped to this line. Prove that the perpendiculars dropped from points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ to the lines $B C, C A$, and $A B$ intersect at one point if and only if $\overline{A_{1} B_{1}}: \overline{B_{1} C_{1}}=\overline{A_{2} B_{2}}: \overline{B_{2} C_{2}}$ (the ratios of the segments are oriented).

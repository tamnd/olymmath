Let

$$
\begin{aligned}
& t_{1}=\frac{v_{1}}{v_{0}}, t_{2}=\frac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}, \ldots, t_{n}=\frac{v_{n}}{v_{n-1}}, \ldots \\
& t_{1}^{\prime}=\frac{v_{1}^{\prime}}{v_{0}^{\prime}}, t_{2}^{\prime}=\frac{v_{2}^{\prime}}{v_{1}^{\prime}}, \ldots, t_{n}^{\prime}=\frac{v_{n}^{\prime}}{v_{n-1}^{\prime}}, \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

- be the sequences of ratios corresponding to some two $p$-Fibonacci arithmetic sequences. Prove that if neither $t_{1}$ nor $t_{1}^{\prime}$ satisfy the equation $x^{2}-x-1=0$, then both sequences (4) and (5) have the same period length.

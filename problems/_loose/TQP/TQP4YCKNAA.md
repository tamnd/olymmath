Inside the convex polyhedron $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$, a point $A$ is taken, and inside the convex polyhedron $B_{1} \ldots B_{n}$, a point $B$ is taken. Prove that if $\angle A_{\imath} A A_{j} \leqslant \leqslant \angle B_{i} B B_{j}$ for all $i, j$, then in fact all these non-strict inequalities are equalities.

A unit circle is inscribed with a square, a circle in the square, an octagon in this circle, and again a circle in the octagon, and so on. Generally, in the $n$-th circle, a regular $2^{n+1}$-gon is inscribed, and in this, the $(n+1)$-th circle. Let $R_{n}$ be the radius of the $n$-th circle. What is $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} R_{n}$?

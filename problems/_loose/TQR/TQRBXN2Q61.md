Fabián has four cards, on each of which he wrote a different positive integer less than 10. He wrote the numbers in different colors, with the following conditions:

- The product of the green and yellow numbers is the green number.
- The blue number is the same as the red number.
- The product of the red and blue numbers is a two-digit number written with the green and yellow digits (in that order).

Determine these four numbers.

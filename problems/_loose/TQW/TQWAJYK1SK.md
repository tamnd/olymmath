After reaching points $B$ and $A$ respectively, they immediately return. On the return trip, the speed of Car A remains unchanged, and the speed of Car B equals the speed of Car A. On the return trip, the two cars meet at point $C$. If the midpoint of $A B$ is point $D$, and $\frac{C D}{A D}=\frac{1}{2}$, find: $k$. (Please provide the solution process)

A circle is inscribed in the curvilinear triangle $ABC$, touching the segment $AB$ at point $M$, and the arc $AC$ at point $N$. Prove that the radius of the circle inscribed in the curvilinear triangle $AMN$ is $\frac{27-12 \sqrt{2}}{98} a$.

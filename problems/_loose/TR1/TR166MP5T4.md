We say a positive integer $n$ is *$k$-tasty* for some positive integer $k$ if there exists a permutation $(a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots , a_n)$ of $(0,1,2, \ldots, n)$ such that $|a_{i+1} - a_i| \in \{k, k+1\}$ for all $0 \le i \le n-1$. Prove that for all positive integers $k$, there exists a constant $N$ such that all integers $n \geq N$ are $k$-tasty.

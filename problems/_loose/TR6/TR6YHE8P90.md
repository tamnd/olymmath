Let $n$ be a natural number. A good word is a sequence of $3 n$ letters where each of the letters $A, B$, and $C$ appears exactly $n$ times. Prove that for every good word $X$ there exists a good word $Y$ such that $Y$ cannot be obtained from $X$ in fewer than $\frac{3}{2} n^{2}$ swaps of adjacent letters.

Let's find the total amount of gases in the ampoule after the reactions are completed

$$
v=0.02+0.02=0.04 \text { mol }
$$

If we assume that the composition of the gas phase does not change upon cooling, the pressure in the ampoule will be

$$
p=\frac{v R T}{V}=\frac{0.04 \cdot 8.314 \cdot 298}{0.25}=396.4 \text { kPa. }
$$

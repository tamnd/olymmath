Given $2012$ stones divided into several groups, a *legal move* is to merge two of the groups into one, as long as the size of the new group is less than or equal to $51$ . Two players, $A$ and $B$ , take turns making legal moves, starting with $A$ . Initially, each stone is in a separate group. The player who cannot make a legal move on their turn loses.

Determine which of the two players has a winning strategy and provide that strategy.

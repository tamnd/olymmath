Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots$ be an integer sequence, where there are infinitely many positive integers and infinitely many negative integers. If for every positive integer $n$, the integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ yield $n$ distinct remainders when divided by $n$. Prove: each integer appears exactly once in the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots$.

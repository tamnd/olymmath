Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ be real numbers satisfying the following conditions:
$$
\left|x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right|=1 \text {, and }\left|x_{i}\right| \leqslant \frac{n+1}{2}, i=1,2, \cdots, n \text {. }
$$

Prove: There exists a permutation $y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}$ of $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ such that
$$
\left|y_{1}+2 y_{2}+\cdots+n y_{n}\right| \leqslant \frac{n+1}{2} \text {. }
$$

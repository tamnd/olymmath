The 5th Junior High School "Zu Chongzhi Cup" Mathematics Invitational Competition had an interesting problem:

Define that on a plane there are $n(n \geqslant 3)$ points. If the distances between all pairs of points take $z$ different values, and if $z=\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]$, then the figure formed by these $n$ points and the lines connecting any two of them is called the Zu Chongzhi figure of $n$ points. Please draw all different Zu Chongzhi figures of 4 points for 4-6 points.

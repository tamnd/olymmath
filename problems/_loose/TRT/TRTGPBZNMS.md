Is it true that in any triangle

$$
\frac{1}{f_{a}}+\frac{1}{f_{b}}+\frac{1}{f_{c}}>\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the sides of the triangle; $f_{a}, f_{b}, f_{c}$ denote the angle bisectors from vertices $A, B$, and $C$ respectively.

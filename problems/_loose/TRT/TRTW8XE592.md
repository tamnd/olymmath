Consider a regular right triangular prism $A B C A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ with $A B=2 a$ and $A A^{\prime}=a$.

a) Show that the perimeter of triangle $A C B^{\prime}$ is greater than the perimeter of quadrilateral $B C C^{\prime} B^{\prime}$.

b) Prove that the areas of $A C B^{\prime}$ and $B C C^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ are equivalent.

c) If $B^{\prime} E$ is the bisector of angle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} B, E \in A B$ and M is the midpoint of $[B C]$, prove that $A^{\prime} C^{\prime} \|\left(B^{\prime} M E\right)$.

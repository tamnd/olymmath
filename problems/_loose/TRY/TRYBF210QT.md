\section*{Task 4 - 261024}

On the worksheet, two lines $g$ and $h$, a point $A$ on $h$, and a circle $k$ are marked. Investigate whether there exists a rhombus $A B C D$ that, in addition to the given vertex $A$, has its vertex $B$ on $g$, vertex $C$ on $h$, and vertex $D$ on $k$!

Investigate whether there is more than one such rhombus!

If this is the case, are all such rhombi congruent to each other?

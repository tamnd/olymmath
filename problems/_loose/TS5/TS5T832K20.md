A reservoir is supplied by two channels. Water flows out evenly through the first channel, and flows in evenly through the second channel. How many hours will it take for $n$ liters of water to pass through the first channel, if it is known that through the second channel, twice as much water will flow in when it is open for $a$ hours less than the time it takes for $n$ liters to pass through the first channel? If both channels are opened simultaneously, then every hour $a$ liters of water are added to the reservoir.

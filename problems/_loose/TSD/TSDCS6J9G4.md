Circles $\odot O_{1}, \odot O_{2}$ with radii $R_{1}, R_{2}$ respectively are externally tangent at $C, A B$ are tangent to $\odot O_{1}, \odot O_{2}$ at $A, B, C D \perp A B$ at $D, C D=d$. Prove:
$$
\frac{1}{R_{1}}+\frac{1}{R_{2}}=\frac{2}{d} .
$$

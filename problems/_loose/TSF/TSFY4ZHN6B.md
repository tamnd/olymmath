Let $a$ and $b$ be two positive real numbers such that $2 a b=a-b$ holds. For any positive integer $k$, let $x_{k}$ and $y_{k}$ denote the integers closest to $a k$ and $b k$, respectively; if a number has two closest integers, choose the larger one. Prove that any positive integer $n$ appears in the sequence $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$ if and only if $n$ appears at least three times in the sequence $y_{1}, y_{2}, \ldots$.

The Lattice Point Jumping Frog jumps between lattice points in a coordinate plane that are exactly $1$ unit apart. The Lattice Point Jumping Frog starts at the origin and makes $8$ jumps, ending at the origin. Additionally, it never lands on a point other than the origin more than once. How many possible paths could the frog have taken?

Determine the perimeter of the scalene triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ knowing that

$$
p^{2} \cdot \overrightarrow{G I}=(4 p-b-c) \cdot \overrightarrow{A I}+(4 p-c-a) \cdot \overrightarrow{B I}+(4 p-a-b) \cdot \overrightarrow{C I}
$$

where $\mathrm{G}$ is the centroid of $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$, I is the incenter of $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$, and $p$ is the semiperimeter of $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$.

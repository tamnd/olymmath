If a moving chord within a circle always subtends a right angle at a fixed point $M$. Then the midpoint of this moving chord and the foot of the perpendicular from $M$ to this chord trace the same circle; and the intersection of the tangents drawn at the ends of the chord also traces a circle. These three circles share the same axis. (With $M$ as a limiting point.)

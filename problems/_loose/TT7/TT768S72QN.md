Given a positive integer $A$, divide it by 50 with a remainder, let the quotient be $A_{1}$ and the remainder be $r_{1}$. Repeating the procedure for $A_{1}$, we get the quotient $A_{2}$ and the remainder $r_{2}$. Continue this until we get a quotient of 0. Prove that $A$ is divisible by 7 if and only if the sum of the remainders $r_{1}$, $r_{2}, \ldots$ obtained during the procedure is divisible by 7. Find a similar rule for divisibility by 13.

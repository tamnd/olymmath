Let $M$ be any interior point of the tetrahedron $ABCD$. Let $N, P, Q$, and $A_{1}$ be the intersection points of the planes $BCM, CDM$, $BDM, BCD$ with the lines $AD, AB, AC, AM$ respectively. Finally, let the plane through $A$ parallel to the plane $BCD$ intersect the lines $A_{1}N, A_{1}P$, and $A_{1}Q$ at points $D_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ respectively. Show that the centroid of the triangle $B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ is $A$.

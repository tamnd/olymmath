$\sqrt{2} x+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2} x}+\sqrt{2} y+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2} y}+\sqrt{2} z+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2} z}=6-2 \sqrt{2 x}|y-z|-\sqrt{2 y}(x-z)^{2}-\sqrt{2 z} \sqrt{|x-y|}$

The right-hand side of the obtained equality does not exceed 6, and equality is achieved only if $\mathrm{x}=\mathrm{y}=\mathrm{z}$. In the left-hand side, we have the sum $f(x)+f(y)+f(z)$, where the function $f$ is defined by the equation $f(t)=\sqrt{2} t+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2} t}$. This function on the positive half-axis has a minimum of 2 at $t=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. Therefore, the value on the left-hand side is not less than 6. Equality can only be achieved under the condition $x=y=z=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

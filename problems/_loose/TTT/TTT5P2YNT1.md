Given a cube $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{\mathrm{i}}$. A plane is drawn through vertex $A$, tangent to the sphere inscribed in the cube. Let $M$ and $N$ be the points of intersection of this plane with the lines $A_{1} B$ and $A_{1} D$. Prove that the line $M N$ is tangent to the sphere inscribed in the cube.

Show that the exponential generating function

$$
E_{s_{\bullet}^{n}}(x)=\sum_{N \geqslant 0} s_{N}^{n} \frac{x^{N}}{N!}
$$

of the sequence $s_{\cdot}^{n}=\left(s_{N}^{n}\right)_{N \geqslant 0}$ of Stirling numbers of the first kind is given by the formula

$$
E_{s^{\prime}}(x)=\frac{(\ln (1+x))^{n}}{n!}
$$

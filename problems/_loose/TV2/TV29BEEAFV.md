Clara has a pair of nine-sided fair dice, each of whose faces are labeled $1, 2, 3, . . . , 9$ . Justin also has a pair of nine-sided fair dice, and the faces of his dice have positive integer labels, but one of Justin's dice has the number $13$ . When Clara and Justin roll their dice, it turns out that for every number $S$ , the probability that the sum of the results of Clara's dice is $S$ is equal to the probability that the sum of the results of Justin's dice is $S$ . What is the probability that when Justin's dice are rolled, the results of the dice are equal?

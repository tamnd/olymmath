The base $\overline{B C}$ is the longest side of the isosceles triangle $A B C$. Let $M$ be a point on the side $\overline{B C}$ such that $|B M|=|A B|$. The foot of the perpendicular from point $M$ to $\overline{A B}$ is point $N$. Prove that the triangle $B M N$ and the quadrilateral $A C M N$ have equal areas and equal perimeters.

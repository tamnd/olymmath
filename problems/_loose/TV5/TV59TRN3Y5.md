Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle such that $A B = B C$. Let $S$ be the point on the ray $[B C)$ such that $A S = B S$. Let $T$ be the point on the ray $[B A)$ such that $B T = C T$. Let $D$ be the point of intersection of the lines $(A S)$ and $(C T)$. Let $E$ be the point on the segment $[B A]$ such that $B E = C D$. Show that the line $(C E)$ is the bisector of the angle $\widehat{D C B}$.

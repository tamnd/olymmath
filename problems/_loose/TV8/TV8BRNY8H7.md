In $\square A B C D$, $\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{B D}=0$, fold along $B D$ to form a right dihedral angle $A-B D-C$, and $4 A B^{2}+2 B D^{2}=1$. Then the surface area of the circumscribed sphere of the triangular prism $A-B C D$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
(B) $\frac{\pi}{4}$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{48}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{2} \pi}{24}$

Consider two concentric circles and a point $P$ on the smaller one. Through point $P$, I draw the chords $P A$ and $B P C$ in the two circles, which are perpendicular to each other. If the chords rotate around the fixed point $P$, prove the following:

$1^{\circ}$ That the sum $P A^{2}+P B^{2}+P C^{2}$ is constant and always equal to the sum of the squares of the sides of triangle $A B C$.

$2^{\circ}$ That the centroid of the triangle does not change its position.

$3^{\circ}$ Find the geometric loci described by the midpoints of the sides of the triangle.

Concept. In Anchuria, there are $K$ laws and $N$ ministers. The probability that a randomly chosen minister knows a randomly chosen law is $p$. One day, the ministers gathered for a council to write the Concept. If at least one minister knows a law, then this law will be considered in the Concept; otherwise, this law will not be considered in the Concept. Find:

a) The probability that exactly $M$ laws will be considered in the Concept.

b) The expected number of laws considered in the Concept.

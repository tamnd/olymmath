Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x=\sqrt{y^{2}-a^{2}}+\sqrt{z^{2}-a^{2}} \\
& y=\sqrt{z^{2}-b^{2}}+\sqrt{z^{2}-b^{2}} \\
& z=\sqrt{x^{2}-c^{2}}+\sqrt{y^{2}-c^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

( $a, b, c$ are non-zero, given numbers).

Four friends found the following note in their textbook:

$$
\text { We know, } \check{z} e \sqrt{a \cdot b}=99 \sqrt{2} \text { and } \check{z} e \sqrt{a \cdot b \cdot c} \text { is a natural number. }
$$

Now they are thinking and arguing about what they can say about the number $c$:

Anna: "It definitely cannot be $\sqrt{2}$."

Dana: "It can be, for example, 98."

Hana: "It can be any even number."

Jana: "There is definitely only one such number."

Decide which of the girls (if any) is/are right, and explain why.

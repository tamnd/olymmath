Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral, and let $\left(b_{1}\right),\left(b_{2}\right),\left(b_{3}\right),\left(b_{4}\right)$ be the angle bisectors of $\widehat{D A B}, \widehat{A B C}, \widehat{B C D}, \widehat{C D A}$, respectively.

Let $W, X, Y, Z$ be the points of intersection of $\left(b_{1}\right),\left(b_{2}\right),\left(b_{3}\right)$, and $\left(b_{4}\right)$ with the circumcircle of $A B C D$.

Show that $W Y$ and $X Z$ intersect at their midpoints.

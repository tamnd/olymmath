Given four points $A, B, C, D$. Let $P, Q, R$ be the points of intersection of the lines $A B$ and $C D, A D$ and $B C, A C$ and $B D$ respectively; $K$ and $L$ be the points of intersection of the line $Q R$ with the lines $A B$ and $C D$ respectively. Prove that $(Q R K L)=-1$ (the theorem of the complete quadrilateral).

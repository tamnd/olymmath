For a positive integer $k$, let the sum of all its digits in decimal representation be $S(k)$.
(1) Prove: For any positive integer $n$, there exists a positive integer arithmetic sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, such that
$$
S\left(a_{1}\right)<S\left(a_{2}\right)<\cdots<S\left(a_{n}\right) ;
$$
(2) Does there exist an infinite arithmetic sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, such that
$$
S\left(a_{1}\right)<S\left(a_{2}\right)<\cdots<S\left(a_{n}\right)<\cdots ?
$$

Prove that the inequality $M_{\lambda}(x) \geqslant M_{\mu}(x)$ holds for all $x=\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$ with positive $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ if and only if $|\lambda|=|\mu|$ and $\lambda \geqslant \mu$. In this case, equality is achieved only when $\lambda=\mu$ and $x_{1}=\ldots=x_{n}$ (Muirhead).

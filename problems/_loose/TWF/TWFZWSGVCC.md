The 54th question: Let integer $n \geq 2$, if for a given set of $n$ points in the plane with no three points collinear, there exist $m$ lines such that no line passes through any of the given points; for any two distinct points $X, Y$ chosen from the $n$ points, there exists a line such that $X, Y$ are on opposite sides of the line. Find the smallest positive integer $m$.

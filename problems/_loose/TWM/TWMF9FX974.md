Every day, 12 tons of potatoes are supplied to the city by one type of transport from three collective farms: from the I farm at a price of 4 rubles per ton, from the II - at a price of 3 rubles, from the III - 1 ruble. To ensure timely delivery of potatoes to the city, it is necessary to spend no more than 40 minutes on loading the required 12 tons. It is known that the level of mechanization in the I farm allows loading 1 ton in 1 minute, in the II - in 4 minutes, in the III - in 3 minutes. The production capacities of these farms are as follows: the I farm should allocate no more than 10 tons daily for delivery to the city, the II - no more than 8 tons, the III - no more than 6 tons. How should the orders for the supply of 12 tons be distributed among the farms so that the total cost of the potatoes delivered to the city is minimized?

In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $AB$ intersects $DC$ at $P$ and $\angle ACB = \angle ADB = \angle P$. Let $PC = a$, $CD = b$, $PB = y$, $BA = x$ (Figure 4.9). Prove:
(1) $x = \left(a + \frac{b}{2}\right) \sec P - \frac{2a(a+b)}{2a+b} \cos P$;
(2) $y = \frac{2a(a+b)}{2a+b} \cos \theta$.

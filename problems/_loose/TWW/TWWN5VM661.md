(SRP, 78). On a plane, there is a set $M$ consisting of $n$ points, no three of which lie on the same line. Each segment with endpoints in $M$ is assigned either the number +1 or the number -1, and the number of segments assigned the number -1 is $m$. A triangle with vertices in $M$ is called negative if the product of the three numbers corresponding to its sides is -1. Prove that the number of negative triangles has the same parity as the product $n m$.

If $0<\theta<\frac{\pi}{4}$ is such that $\operatorname{cosec} \theta-\sec \theta=\frac{\sqrt{13}}{6}$, then $\cot \theta-\tan \theta$ equals
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{13}}{6}$;
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{12}}{6}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{6}$ :
(D) $\frac{13}{6}$;
(E) $\frac{5}{6}$.

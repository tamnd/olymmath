In the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\odot O_{1}, \odot O_{2}, \odot O_{3}, \odot O_{4}$ are the incircles of $\triangle ABD, \triangle BCA, \triangle CDB$, $\triangle DAC$ respectively. Let the points of tangency on $AB, BC, CD, DA$ be $E, F, M, N, G, H, P, Q$ respectively, and let the radii of $\odot O_{i}$ be $R_{i}(i=1,2,3,4)$. Prove that: $EF \cdot MN = R_{1} R_{3} + R_{2} R_{4}$.

Let $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ be the midpoints of the diagonals $[A C]$ and $[B D]$ of the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$.

(i) Show that $\overline{O_{1} O_{2}}=\frac{\overrightarrow{A D}-\overrightarrow{B C}}{2}$

(ii) Show that if $\overrightarrow{A D}+\overrightarrow{C B}=3 \cdot \overrightarrow{O_{1} O_{2}}$, then $A B C D$ is a parallelogram.

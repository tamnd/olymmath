Petya took some three-digit natural numbers $a_{0}, a_{1}, \ldots, a_{9}$ and wrote the following equation on the board:

$$
a_{9} x^{9}+a_{8} x^{8}+\ldots+a_{2} x^{2}+a_{1} x+a_{0}=*
$$

Prove that Vasya can write a certain 30-digit natural number instead of the asterisk so that the resulting equation has an integer root. (A. Kuznetsov, A. Antropov)

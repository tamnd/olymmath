In triangle $A B C$, a point $D$ is taken on side $A C$. Let $\dot{O}_{1}$ be the center of the circle tangent to segments $A D, B D$ and the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$, and $O_{2}$ be the center of the circle tangent to segments $C D, B D$ and the circumcircle. Prove that the line $O_{1} O_{2}$ passes through the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$ - point $O,$ and that $\left|O_{1} O\right|:\left|O O_{2}\right|=\operatorname{tg}^{2}(\varphi / 2)$, where $\varphi=\angle B D A$ (Viktor Tebo).

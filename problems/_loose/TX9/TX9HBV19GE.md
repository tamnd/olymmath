On a plane, a straight line is given. Using a coin, construct two points of some line perpendicular to the given one. The following operations are allowed: mark a point, apply the coin to it and trace around it; mark two points (at a distance less than the diameter of the coin), apply the coin to them and trace around it. It is not possible to apply the coin to the line so that it touches it.

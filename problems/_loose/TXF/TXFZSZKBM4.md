Ivan and Petr are playing the following game. From a pile containing 2018 stones, they take some number of stones in turns. If there are $N$ stones in the pile before a move, the player can take $k$ stones only if $k$ is a divisor of the number $N$. The player who takes the last stone loses. Which player has a winning strategy if Ivan takes the stones first?

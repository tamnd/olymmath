Let $a, b, c$ be distinct natural numbers and $k$ a natural number such that

$$
a b + b c + c a \geqslant 3 k^{2} - 1
$$

Prove that $\frac{1}{3}\left(a^{3} + b^{3} + c^{3}\right) \geqslant a b c + 3 k$.

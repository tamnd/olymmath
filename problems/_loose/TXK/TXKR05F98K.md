Between two 1s, there are 1991 digits of 9, i.e.,
$$
1 \underbrace{99 \cdots 991}_{1991 \uparrow 9}
$$

Try to determine:
(1) Where should the "+" signs be placed so that the sum is minimized?
(2) Where should the "$\times$" signs be placed so that the product is maximized?
(54th Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 1991)

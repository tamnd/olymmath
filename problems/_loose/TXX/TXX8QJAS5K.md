Let $\triangle ABC$ be an equilateral triangle. $G$ is the set of all points contained in the three segments $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ (including $A$, $B$, $C$). Some points in $G$ are colored black, and the rest are colored white. Prove: there is at least one right triangle inscribed in the equilateral triangle $ABC$, whose three vertices are of the same color.

a) Construct a quadrilateral $A B C D$ that can be inscribed in a circle, given the lengths of its sides $A B=a$, $B C=b, C D=c, D A=d$.

b) Construct a quadrilateral $A B C D$, knowing the sum of the opposite angles $B$ and $D$ and the lengths of all its sides $A B=a$, $B C=b, C D=c, D A=d$.

Problem a) is a special case of problem b).

Prove that for $n \geqslant 3$ among the resulting parts there are at least $(2 n-2) / 3$ triangles.

Now let's abandon the assumption that no three of the considered lines intersect at one point. If $P$ is a point of intersection of two or more lines, then the number of lines of the given system passing through point $P$ will be denoted by $\lambda(P)$.

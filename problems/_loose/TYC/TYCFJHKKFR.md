Let $m, n$ be strictly positive integers. A pawn called an $(m, n)$-condylure moves on a board with an infinite number of rows (infinite to the left and to the right) and columns (infinite upwards and downwards). At each move, the condylure moves $m$ squares in one direction (horizontal or vertical) and $n$ squares in the perpendicular direction. The knight in chess is therefore a (2,1)-condylure.

For which values of $m$ and $n$ can the squares of this board be colored blue and red so that at each move, the square on which the condylure stands changes color?

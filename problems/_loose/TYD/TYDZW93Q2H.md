Let $m,n,k$ be positive integers, $m> n> k$ . An $1 \times m$ strip of paper is divided into the $1 \times 1$ cells. A teacher asks Bill and Pit to place numbers $0$ and $1$ in the cells of the strip so that the sum of the numbers in any $n$ consecutive cells is equal to $k$ . After the task was performed it turned out that the sum $S(B)$ of all numbers on the strip of Bill was different from the sum $S(P)$ of Pit.
Find the largest possible value of $|S(B) - S(P) |$ .

(I. Voronovich)

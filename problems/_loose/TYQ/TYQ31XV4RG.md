A continuous function $f(x)$ is defined for all $x$, and $f\left(x_{0}\right) \neq 0$ for some $x_{0}$. Prove that if the relation

$$
f(x+y)=f(x) f(y)
$$

holds, then $f(x)=a^{x}$ for some $a>0$.

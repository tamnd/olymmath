In the plane of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is given, through which three rays are drawn, perpendicular to the sides of the triangle and not lying in the same half-plane. On these rays from point $M$, segments $M A_{1}, M B_{1}, M C_{1}$ are laid off, respectively equal to the sides of the triangle. Prove that: 1) point $M$ is the centroid of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$, 2) the ratio of the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ is $1: 3$.

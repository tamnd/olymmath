In $\triangle A B C$, the sides opposite to $\angle A, \angle B, \angle C$ are $B C=a, C A=b, A B=c$, respectively. Prove: $2 b \cos \frac{C}{2}+$ $2 c \cos \frac{B}{2}>a+b+c$.

Let $ a_{0}=0, a_{n}=P\left(a_{n-1}\right) $ for $ n=1,2, \ldots $, where $ P(x) $ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, and $ P(x)>0 $ for $ x \geqslant 0 $. Prove that if $ m, n>0 $, then $\gcd \left(a_{m}, a_{n}\right)=a_{d}$, where $ d=\gcd (m, n) $.

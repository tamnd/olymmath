Person A throws a standard cubic die three times, then he examines the dice and selects a subset of them (which could be empty or all three dice) to re-roll. After the re-roll, he wins if and only if the sum of the numbers on the top faces of the three dice is exactly 7. To increase his chances of winning, the probability that A chooses to re-roll two dice is ( ).
(A) $\frac{7}{36}$
(B) $\frac{5}{24}$
(C) $\frac{2}{9}$
(D) $\frac{17}{72}$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}$

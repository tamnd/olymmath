Show that every triangle can be covered by closed segments, that is, given a triangle, there exists a system of closed segments such that none of the segments extend outside the triangle, and every point of the triangular area is passed through by exactly one of them. (A set consisting of a single point is not considered a segment.)

Can the covering be done with segments of equal length?

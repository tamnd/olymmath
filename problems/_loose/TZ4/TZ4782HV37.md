(See [75].) Let $X=\left(X_{1}, \ldots, X_{2 n}\right), n \geqslant 1,$ be a Gaussian vector with zero mean and covariance matrix $C=\left(c_{i j}\right)_{i, j=1}^{2 n}$. Prove that

$$
\mathrm{E} X_{1} X_{2} \ldots X_{2 n}=\frac{1}{n!} \sum_{\sigma} c_{\sigma_{1} \sigma_{2}} \ldots c_{\sigma_{2 n-1} \sigma_{2 n}}
$$

where the summation is over all permutations $\sigma=\left(\sigma_{1}, \ldots, \sigma_{2 n}\right)$ of the numbers $1, \ldots, 2 n$ such that $\sigma_{2 k-1}<\sigma_{2 k}, 1 \leqslant k \leqslant 2 n$.

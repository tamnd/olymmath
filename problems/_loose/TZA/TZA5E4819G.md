Multiple-digit numbers are written on a piece of paper. From these, we draw those whose last digit is odd, but the second last is even, as well as those whose last digit is odd and not divisible by three, and also those whose second last digit is odd and divisible by three. Prove that this way only even numbers remain on the paper.

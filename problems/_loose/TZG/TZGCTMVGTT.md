On the hypotenuse $AB$ of a right-angled triangle $ABC$ , point $R$ is chosen, on the cathetus $BC$ a point $T$ , and on the segment $AT$ a point $S$ are chosen in such a way that the angles $\angle ART$ and $\angle ASC$ are right angles. Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of the segments $CB$ and $CR$ , respectively. Prove that points $M$ , $T$ , $S$ , and $N$ lie on the same circle.

Let * be an associative and commutative composition law on a set $S$. Suppose that for any $x, y \in S$ there exists $z \in S$ such that $x \star z=y$ ( $z$ may depend on $x$ and $y$ ). Show that if $a$, $b, c \in S$ satisfy $a \star c=b \star c$ then $a=b$.

Test Question $\mathrm{A}$ Let the positive irrational numbers $\alpha$ and $\beta$ satisfy the equation
$$
\frac{1}{\alpha}+\frac{1}{\beta}=1,
$$

Prove: The two sequences $\{[n \alpha]\},\{[n \beta]\}$ together exactly and without repetition constitute the set of natural numbers, where the notation $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

Let $p \geq 3$ be a prime number, and consider the right triangle with the larger leg $p^{2}-1$ and the smaller leg $2p$. We inscribe a semicircle in the triangle whose diameter lies on the larger leg of the triangle and is tangent to the hypotenuse and the smaller leg of the triangle. Find the values of $p$ for which the radius of the semicircle is an integer.

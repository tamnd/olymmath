On a checkered board, dominoes lie without touching even at the corners. Each domino occupies two adjacent (by side) cells of the board. The lower left and upper right cells of the board are free. Is it always possible to move from the lower left cell to the upper right cell, making moves only up and to the right to adjacent cells and not stepping on dominoes, if the board has dimensions

a) $1001 \times 101$ cells;

b) $1001 \times 100$ cells?

Given real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{5}$ satisfy
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text { that } \sum_{i=1}^{5} a_{i}^{2}=1 \text {. Find } \\
T=\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left|a_{i}-a_{i+1}\right|\left(a_{6}=a_{1}\right)
\end{array}
$$

the maximum value.

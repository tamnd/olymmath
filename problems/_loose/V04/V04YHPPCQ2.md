As Mr. Schäfer found out, with one of the dogs, he had made a profit of $20 \%$ of its original purchase price, while he sold the other dog at a loss of $20 \%$ of its original purchase price.

Determine whether, after selling both dogs, Mr. Schäfer overall made a profit or a loss compared to the total original purchase price! If this is the case, determine how much the profit or loss amounts to!

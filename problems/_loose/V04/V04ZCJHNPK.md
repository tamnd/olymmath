The teacher wrote on the board in alphabetical order all possible $2^{n}$ words consisting of $n$ letters A or B. Then he replaced each word with a product of $n$ factors, changing each letter A to $x$, and each letter B to (1 $-x)$, and added together several of the first of these polynomials in $x$. Prove that the resulting polynomial represents either a constant or an increasing function on the interval $[0,1]$.

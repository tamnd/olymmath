Find the polynomials $ f(x),\ g(x)$ such that:

$ \frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{tf'(t)-6g(t)}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}\ dt=f(x)-g(x)+x$

$ \frac{6}{\pi}\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{8f(t)-5g'(t)}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}\ dt=2f(x)-3g(x)-x^2+2x$.

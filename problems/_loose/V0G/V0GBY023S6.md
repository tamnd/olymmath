\section*{Task 1 - 151021}

Before the start of a horse race, spectators discuss the possible finish of the three favorites A, B, and C.

Spectator (1): "A or C will win."

Spectator (2): "If A finishes second, B will win."

Spectator (3): "If A finishes third, then C will not win."

Spectator (4): "A or B will finish second."

After the race, it turned out that the three favorites A, B, and C indeed took the first three places, and that all four statements were true.

What was the finish order?

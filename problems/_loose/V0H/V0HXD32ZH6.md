In the plane, there are 11 points such that no three of them are collinear. All pairs of the given points are connected by segments, and each of these segments is colored in one of three colors: red, yellow, and blue. Prove that among the given points, there exist three points that are vertices of a triangle such that all its sides are colored in the same color or all its sides are colored in different colors.

On a plane, a circle $s$ and a line $l$ passing through the center $O$ of the circle $s$ are given. Through the point $O$, an arbitrary circle $s^{\prime}$ with its center on the line $l$ is drawn. Find the set of points $M$ where the common tangent of the circles $s$ and $s^{\prime}$ touches the circle $s^{\prime}$.

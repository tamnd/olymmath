Prove that for every pair of positive integers $k$ and $n$ , there exists integer $x_1$ , $x_2$ , $...$ , $x_k$ with $0 \le x_j \le 2^{k-1}\cdot \sqrt[k]{n}$ for $j = 1$ , $2$ , $...$ , $k$ , and such that $$ x_1 + x^2_2+ x^3_3+...+ x^k_k= n. $$

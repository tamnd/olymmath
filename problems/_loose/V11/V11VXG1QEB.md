Let $p$ be a prime number. Divide the numbers $0,1, \ldots, p-1$ into two (non-empty) classes arbitrarily. It is to be proven that there is at most one number $a$ among them such that neither $a$ nor $a+p$ can be expressed as the sum of two numbers from different classes.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle. We place $P$ and $Q$ with $P \neq Q$ two points on $[BC]$. We denote $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$ and $O_{4}$ the centers of the circumcircles of $ABP, ABQ, ACP$ and $ACQ$. Show that $O_{1} O_{2} O_{3} O_{4}$ is cyclic if and only if $\widehat{B A P}=\widehat{C A Q}$.

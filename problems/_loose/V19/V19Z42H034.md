In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the three sides are $a, b, c$, and the distances from its circumcenter to the three sides are $m, n, p$. Then, $m: n: p$ equals
(A) $\frac{1}{a}: \frac{1}{b}: \frac{1}{c}$.
(B) $a: b: c$.
(C) $\cos A: \cos B: \cos C$.
(D) $\sin A: \sin B: \sin C$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1993)

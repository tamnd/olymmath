Consider the sets $A=\{a, b, c, d\} \subset \mathbb{N}$ and $S=\{s \in \mathbb{N} \mid s=x+y, x \in A, y \in A, x \neq y\}$.

It is known that $S=\{82,96,104,112,126\}$.

a) Calculate the sum of the elements in set $A$;

b) Determine the elements of set $A$.

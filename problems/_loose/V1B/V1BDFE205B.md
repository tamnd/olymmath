Cauchy-Davenport Inequality Let $p$ be a prime, integers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{k}$ be incongruent modulo $p$, and integers $y_{1}, y_{2}$, $\cdots, y_{l}$ be incongruent modulo $p$, then
$$
x_{i}+y_{j}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant k, 1 \leqslant j \leqslant l)
$$

modulo $p$ has at least $\min \{p, k+l-1\}$ distinct residues.

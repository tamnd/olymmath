Given that $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $a \neq 0$, the function $f(x)=\frac{x}{a x+3}$ satisfies $f(2)=1$, and the value of $x$ that makes $f(x)=x$ is unique. (1) Find the values of $a$ and $b$; (2) Let $x_{1}=1, x_{n}=$ $f\left(x_{n-1}\right)(n=2,3, \cdots)$, find the general term formula of the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$.

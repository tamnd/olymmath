\section*{Problem $5-011135=011234$}

Given a line segment $A B=a=6 \mathrm{~cm} . M$ is the midpoint of the segment.

Draw a semicircle over $A B$ with radius $A M$ centered at $M$! Bisect $A M$ and $M B$ and draw two semicircles with radius $\frac{A M}{2}$ over both segments, which lie within the larger semicircle! Construct the center of the circle that touches the large semicircle from the inside and the two smaller semicircles from the outside!

The construction must be justified!

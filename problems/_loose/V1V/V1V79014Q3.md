A connected graph has more than $1000$ vertices and remains connected even if any $100$ vertices are removed from it. Prove that there is a path in this graph $a_1a_2 \cdots a_{102}$ such that the graph remains connected after removing these $102$ vertices. (Some pairs of non-adjacent vertices in the path may also be adjacent.)

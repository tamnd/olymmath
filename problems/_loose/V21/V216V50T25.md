For each bag, Maria calculates the probability of randomly drawing two marbles of the same colour in two draws from that bag, without replacement. (Drawing two marbles without replacement means drawing two marbles, one after the other, without putting the first marble back into the bag.) (A) 4
(B) 5
(C) 6
(D) 7
(E) 8

Antje proposes as a continuation for discussion: "... $a$ is negative."

Bernd proposes as a continuation for discussion: "... $a$ and $b$ are negative."

Cornelia proposes as a continuation for discussion: "... $b$ is negative."

Doris proposes as a continuation for discussion: "... neither $a$ nor $b$ needs to be negative."

Each of these four statements put up for discussion should be examined to determine whether they are true!

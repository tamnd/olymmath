From a point $O$ lying inside a convex polyhedron, rays are drawn intersecting the planes of the faces and perpendicular to them. On these rays, from point $O$, vectors are laid off whose lengths are equal to the areas of the corresponding faces. Prove that the sum of these vectors is equal to zero.

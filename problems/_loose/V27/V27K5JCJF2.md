Six dice are strung on a skewer so that each can rotate independently of the others (piercing through the centers of opposite faces). The skewer is laid on a table and the number formed by the digits on the top faces of the dice is read. Prove that the dice can be rotated so that this number is divisible by 7. (The faces are numbered from 1 to 6, and the sum of the numbers on opposite faces is 7.)

Prove that the interval $[0,1]$ can be divided into several black and white subintervals such that for any quadratic polynomial $p(x)$, the sum of the increments of $p(x)$ over all black subintervals equals the sum of the increments of $p(x)$ over all white subintervals (the increment of $p(x)$ over the interval $[a, b]$ is defined as $p(a)-p(b)$).

For the case of a cubic polynomial, does the same conclusion hold?
For the case of a quintic polynomial, does the same conclusion hold?

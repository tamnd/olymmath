For positive real numbers $ a,\ b$, two graphs of the function : $ x^a$ and $ \ln bx$ have a tangency of point.

(1) Let $ (s,\ t)$ be the tangency of point, express this in terms of $ a$, then express $ b$ as the function of $ a$.

(2) For $ h$ with $ 0 < h < s$, denote the area as $ A(h)$ of the domain bounded by the line $ x = h$ and two curves $ y = x^a,\ y = \ln bx$.

Express $ \lim_{h\rightarrow 0} A(h)$ in terms of $ a$.

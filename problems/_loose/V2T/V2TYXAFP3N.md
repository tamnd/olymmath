Given that $a, b$ are unequal positive numbers, insert two sets of numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ and $y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}$ between $a$ and $b$, such that $a, x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}, b$ form an arithmetic sequence, and $a, y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots, y_{n}, b$ form a geometric sequence, then which of the following inequalities (1) $\frac{1}{n}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)>\sqrt{a b}+ \left(\frac{\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}}{2}\right)^{2}$
(2) $\frac{1}{n}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)>\frac{a+b}{2}$;
(3) $\sqrt[n]{y_{1} y_{2} \cdots y_{n}}< \sqrt{a b}$; (4) $\sqrt[n]{y_{1} y_{2} \cdots y_{n}}<\frac{a+b}{2}-\left(\frac{\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}}{2}\right)^{2}$ are true?
A. (1) (3)
B. $(1)(4)$
C. (2) $(3)$
D. $(2)(4)$

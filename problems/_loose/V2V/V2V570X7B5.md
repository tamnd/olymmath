(a) If $ ABC$ is a triangle and $ M$ is a point from its plane, then prove that
$$ AM \sin A \leq BM \sin B + CM \sin C . $$

(b) Let $ A_1,B_1,C_1$ be points on the sides $ (BC),(CA),(AB)$ of the triangle $ ABC$, such that the angles of $\triangle A_1 B_1 C_1$ are $\widehat{A_1} = \alpha, \widehat{B_1} = \beta, \widehat{C_1} = \gamma$. Prove that
$$ \sum A A_1 \sin \alpha \leq \sum BC \sin \alpha . $$

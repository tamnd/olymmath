Given the hyperbola $x^{2}-y^{2}=2$ with left and right foci $F_{1}, F_{2}$, a tangent line is drawn through the fixed point $P(2,3)$ to the hyperbola $x^{2}-y^{2}=2$. The points of tangency are $A$ and $B$, and the x-coordinate of point $A$ is less than that of point $B$.
(1) Find the equation of the line $A B$;
(2) Prove: $\angle F_{1} P A=\angle F_{2} P B$.

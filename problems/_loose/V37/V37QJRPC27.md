Find the infinite periodic decimal numbers expressing the ratios $1: 3, 1: 7, 1: 9$. Verify the results by multiplication.
1) A reader might ask why numbers with an infinite number of digits after the decimal point, i.e., numbers infinite in both directions, are not considered. The reason is that it is impossible to reasonably define arithmetic operations (addition, etc.) for such numbers; figuratively speaking, there is "no way to approach them."

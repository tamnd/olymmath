Density is the ratio of the mass of a body to the volume it occupies. Since the mass did not change as a result of tamping, and the volume after tamping $V_{2}=$ $0.8 V_{1}$, the density after tamping became $\rho_{2}=\frac{1}{0.8} \rho_{1}=1.25 \rho_{1}$, that is, it increased by $25 \%$.

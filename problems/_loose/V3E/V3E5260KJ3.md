On a square field of size $99 \times 99$, divided into cells of size $1 \times 1$, two players play. The first player places a cross in the center of the field; following this, the second player can place a zero in any of the eight cells surrounding the first player's cross. After this, the first player places a cross on any of the cells adjacent to the already occupied ones, and so on. The first player wins if they manage to place a cross in any corner cell. Prove that, regardless of the second player's moves, the first player can always win.

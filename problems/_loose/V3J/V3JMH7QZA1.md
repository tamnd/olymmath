Estimate the number of primes among the first thousand primes divide some term of the sequence
$$2^0+1,2^1+1,2^2+1,2^3+1,\ldots.$$
An estimate of $E$ earns $2^{1-0.02|A-E|}$ points, where $A$ is the actual answer.

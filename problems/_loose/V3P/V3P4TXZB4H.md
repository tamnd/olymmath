Let $f : R^+ \to R$ be a strictly increasing function such that $f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right) < \frac{f(x)+ f(y)}{2}$ for all $x,y > 0$ .
Prove that the sequence $a_n = f(n)$ ( $n \in N$ ) does not contain an infinite arithmetic progression.

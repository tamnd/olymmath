$$
x_{1} y_{1}+\ldots+x_{n} y_{n} \leq \sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\ldots+x_{n}^{2}} \sqrt{y_{1}^{2}+\ldots+y_{n}^{2}}
$$

Determine the cases of equality.

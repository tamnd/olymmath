Prove that

a) $\sin n \alpha=C_{n}^{1} \sin \alpha \cos ^{n-1} \alpha-C_{n}^{3} \sin ^{3} \alpha \cos ^{n-3} \alpha+$

$$
+C_{n}^{5} \sin ^{5} \alpha \cos ^{n-5} \alpha-\ldots
$$

for example,

$\sin 6 \alpha=6 \sin \alpha \cos ^{5} \alpha-20 \sin ^{3} \alpha \cos ^{3} \alpha+6 \sin ^{5} \alpha \cos \alpha$.

b) $\cos n \alpha=\cos ^{n} \alpha-C_{n}^{2} \sin ^{2} \alpha \cos ^{n-2} \alpha+C_{n}^{4} \sin ^{4} \alpha \cos ^{n-4} \alpha \ldots \ldots$; for example,

$\cos 6 \alpha=\cos ^{6} \alpha-15 \sin ^{2} \alpha \cos ^{4} \alpha+15 \sin ^{4} \alpha \cos ^{2} \alpha-\sin ^{6} \alpha$.

c) $\operatorname{tg} n \alpha=\frac{C_{n}^{1} \operatorname{tg} \alpha-C_{n}^{3} \operatorname{tg}^{3} \alpha+C_{n}^{5} \operatorname{tg}^{5} \alpha-\cdots}{1-C_{n}^{2} \operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha+C_{n}^{4} \operatorname{tg}^{4} \alpha-\ldots}$;

for example,

$$
\operatorname{tg} 6 \alpha=\frac{6 \operatorname{tg} \alpha-20 \operatorname{tg}^{3} \alpha+6 \operatorname{tg}^{5} \alpha}{1-15 \operatorname{tg}^{2} \alpha+15 \operatorname{tg}^{4} \alpha-\operatorname{tg}^{6} \alpha}
$$

Find all real numbers $p$ for which the system of inequalities

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 25+2 x^{2} \leqq 13 y+10 z-p \\
& 25+3 y^{2} \leqq 6 z+10 x \\
& 25+4 z^{2} \leqq 6 x+5 y+p
\end{aligned}
$$

with unknowns $x, y, z$ has a solution in the set of real numbers.

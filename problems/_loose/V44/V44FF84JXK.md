Let $ABCD$ be a tangential quadrilateral. Prove that:

a) The inscribed circles in the two triangles into which the diagonal $AC$ divides the quadrilateral touch each other.
b) The points of tangency of the two circles with the sides of the quadrilateral are vertices of a cyclic quadrilateral.

On the board, there are 10 numbers: $1,2,3,4,4,5,5,11,12,13$. You can perform two types of operations with them: either subtract 1 from any nine of them and add 9 to the remaining one, or vice versa, subtract 9 from one and add 1 to each of the others. Negative numbers cannot be obtained. Can all ten numbers be made different by applying several such operations?

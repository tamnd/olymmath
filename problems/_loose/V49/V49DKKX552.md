a) Let $P$ be the Brocard point of triangle $ABC$. The angle $\varphi = \angle ABP = \angle BCP = \angle CAP$ is called the Brocard angle of this triangle. Prove that $\operatorname{ctg} \varphi = \operatorname{ctg} \alpha + \operatorname{ctg} \beta + \operatorname{ctg} \gamma$.

b) Prove that the Brocard points of triangle $ABC$ are isogonal conjugates.

c) The tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ at point $C$ and the line passing through point $B$ parallel to $AC$ intersect at point $A_{1}$. Prove that the Brocard angle of triangle $ABC$ is equal to the angle $A_{1}AC$.

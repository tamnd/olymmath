"Countries" on a sphere. Suppose that using some triangulation we divided the sphere into "countries". Here, by triangulation we mean such a division of the sphere that each resulting "country" borders (i.e., has a common boundary segment of non-zero length) with exactly three other "countries". A vertex of the graph consisting of boundary lines will be called even or odd, depending on whether an even or odd number of such lines emanate from it. Can we construct such a triangulation of the sphere where there would be exactly two odd vertices, and these vertices would be adjacent?

| Tank A | Tank B | Tank C |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $3600 \mathrm{~L}$ | $1600 \mathrm{~L}$ | $3800 \mathrm{~L}$ |

(A) $500 \mathrm{~L}$
(B) $600 \mathrm{~L}$
(C) $700 \mathrm{~L}$
(D) $800 \mathrm{~L}$
(E) $900 \mathrm{~L}$

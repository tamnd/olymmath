Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ be an arithmetic progression with positive terms. Prove that the sum of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)$, if $x_{n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{n}}+\sqrt{a_{n+1}}}$, is equal to $\frac{n}{\sqrt{a_{1}}+\sqrt{a_{n+1}}}$.

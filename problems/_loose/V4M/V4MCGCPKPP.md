A circle $k$ with center $S$ and a chord $A B$ is given, which is not its diameter. On the half-line opposite to the half-line $B A$, an arbitrary point $K$ different from $B$ is chosen. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $A K S$ intersects circle $k$ at a point $C$ which is symmetric to point $B$ with respect to the line $S K$.

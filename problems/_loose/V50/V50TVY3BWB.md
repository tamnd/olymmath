If $a<b<c, x<y<z$, which of the following four algebraic expressions:

甲: $a x+b y+c z$. 乙: $a x+b z+c y$.
丙: $b x+a y+c z$. 丁: $b x+c y+a z$.
is the largest one?
(A) It must be 甲.
(B) It must be 乙. (C) It must be 丙.
(D) It must be 丁 (Shanghai Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983; Fujian Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1984)

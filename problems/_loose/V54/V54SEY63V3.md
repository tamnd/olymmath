Denoting the common value of the logarithms by $t$, we get

$$
(5+\sqrt{15})^{t}=x^{2}-2 x-2, (5-\sqrt{15})^{t}=-x^{2}+2 x+12
$$

Adding these equations, we get

$$
(5+\sqrt{15})^{t}+(5-\sqrt{15})^{t}=10
$$

Since the function $(5+\sqrt{15})^{t}+(5-\sqrt{15})^{t}$ is increasing, the only solution is $t=1$. From this, we have $x^{2}-2 x-2=5+\sqrt{15}$.

Let $n \geqslant 3$ be a positive integer. Given two regular $n$-gons $\mathscr{A}, \mathscr{B}$ in the plane. Prove: The vertices of $\mathscr{A}$ that lie on the interior or boundary of $\mathscr{B}$ are consecutive, i.e., there exists a line that divides the vertices of $\mathscr{A}$ into two parts, one of which lies entirely in the interior or on the boundary of $\mathscr{B}$.

$ 30$ persons are sitting at round table. $ 30 - N$ of them always speak true ("true speakers") while the other $ N$ of them sometimes speak true sometimes not ("lie speakers"). Question: "Who is your right neighbour - "true speaker" or "lie speaker" ?" is asked to all 30 persons and 30 answers are collected. What is maximal number $ N$ for which (with knowledge of these answers) we can always be sure (decide) about at least one person who is "true speaker".

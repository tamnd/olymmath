There is a $3 \times 3$ board and 9 cards of one cell size, on which some numbers are written. Two players take turns placing these cards on the board. After all the cards are placed, the first player (the one who starts) calculates the sum of the six numbers in the top and bottom rows, and the second player calculates the sum of the six numbers in the left and right columns. The player with the higher sum wins. Prove that with correct play, the second player cannot win regardless of the numbers written on the cards.

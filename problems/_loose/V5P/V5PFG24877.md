Let's answer the following April Fool's jokes (only the questions are joking, the answers can be logically and clearly given):

a) On his deathbed, an Arab sheikh reveals to his two sons that he has buried his treasures near a palm tree not far from the village. His final decree is that the one whose camel reaches the palm tree later should inherit the treasure.

After their father's death, the two sons each want to obtain the treasure, but they also want to respect their father's last wish. They don't know what to do. Finally, they go to the judge and ask for advice on how one of them can acquire the treasure while fulfilling their father's will. The judge calls them to lean closer. The sons dismount from their camels, and the judge whispers something into their ears, after which both jump back onto their camels and, driving them mercilessly, race towards the palm tree. One of them wins the treasure. What did the judge whisper?

b) In the vastness of Africa, two tribes live: the pelicans and the crocodiles. Their language and customs are identical. They themselves do not know which tribe the other belongs to. The only difference between them is that while the pelicans always tell the truth, the crocodiles always say the opposite of what is true.

The members of a European expedition are familiar with the characteristics of these two tribes, including the fact that their newly hired interpreter is from one of these two tribes. They encounter a native. The interpreter's first task is to find out which tribe the approaching native belongs to. After exchanging a few words with the native, the interpreter reports, "He says he is from the ... (and here he named one of the two tribes)!" In response, the expedition immediately dismissed the interpreter from service. Why? How can this be explained?

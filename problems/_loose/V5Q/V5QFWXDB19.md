Prove that for any three infinite sequences of natural numbers

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots \\
& b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{n}, \ldots \\
& c_{1}, c_{2}, \ldots, c_{n}, \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

there exist indices $p$ and $q$ such that $a_{p} \geqslant a_{q}, b_{p} \geqslant b_{q}$, $c_{p} \geqslant c_{q}$.

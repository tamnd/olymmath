Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the following three sums:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
S_{n}=\mathrm{C}_{n}^{0}+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{3}+\cdots+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{3 k}+\cdots, \\
T_{n}=\mathrm{C}_{n}^{1}+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{4}+\cdots+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{3 k+1}+\cdots, \\
H_{n}=\mathrm{C}_{n}^{2}+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{5}+\cdots+\mathrm{C}_{n}^{3 k+2}+\cdots .
\end{array}
$$

Here it is specified that when $m>n$, $\mathrm{C}_{n}^{m}=0$.

From $a$ cards, $b$ piles are formed, with each pile having $c$ points. The first card of each pile counts as 11 points if it is an ace. 10 points if it is a face card or a ten, and so on. Other cards count as one point. If, after forming the piles, we have $d$ cards left over, we need to determine the sum $\boldsymbol{x}$ of the points of the first cards only.

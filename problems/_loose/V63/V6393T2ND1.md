Alice and Bob play a game. The gaming field is a checked line of size $1 \times 2022$. Players take turns (Alice starts) writing any of the letters O and G in an empty cell. The player who, after their move, has the letters OGO in three adjacent cells wins. If all cells are filled and there is no OGO, the game ends in a draw. What will be the outcome if both players play optimally?

Alice and Bob play a game. The gaming field is a checked line of size $1 \times 2022$. Players one by one write any of the letters $\mathrm{W}$ or $\mathrm{O}$ (the first to write is Alice). The person after which turn there would be a work WOW on the desk wins. If all cells are filled without WOW word, the players draw. How the game would end with a optimal play of both players?

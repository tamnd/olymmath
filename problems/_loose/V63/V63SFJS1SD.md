Given $\triangle A B C, \angle C=90^{\circ}$.
Prove: For any $n$ points inside $\triangle A B C$, they can be appropriately labeled as $P_{1}, P_{2}, \cdots$, $P_{n}$, such that
$$
P_{1} P_{2}^{2}+P_{2} P_{3}^{2}+\cdots+P_{n-1} P_{n}^{2} \leqslant A B^{2} .
$$

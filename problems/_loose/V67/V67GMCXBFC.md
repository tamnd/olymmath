In a trapezoid where $A B=a, B C=b, C D=c, D A=d$, then

$1^{\circ} a=d \cos \alpha+b \cos \beta-c \cos (\beta+\gamma)=d \cos \alpha+b \cos \beta-c \cos (\alpha+\delta)$,

$2^{\circ} a \sin \alpha=c \sin \delta+b \sin (\alpha+\beta)$,

$3^{\circ} a \sin \beta=c \sin \gamma+d \sin (\alpha+\beta)$.

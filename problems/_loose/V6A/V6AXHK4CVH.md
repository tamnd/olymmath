There are 13 weights. It is known that any 12 of them can be distributed into two pans of a balance, six on each, so that equilibrium is achieved.

Prove that all the weights have the same mass, if it is known that:

a) the mass of each weight is an integer number of grams;

b) the mass of each weight is a rational number of grams;

c) the mass of each weight can be any non-negative real number.

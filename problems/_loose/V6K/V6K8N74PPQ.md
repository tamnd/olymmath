Initially, the point-like particles $A, B$ and $C{}$ are located respectively at the points $(0,0), (1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ in the coordinate plane. Every minute some two particles repel each other along the straight line connecting their current positions, moving the same (positive) distance.

- Can the particle $A{}$ be at the point $(3,3)$ ? What about the point $(2,3)$ ?

- Can the particles $B{}$ and $C{}$ be at the same time at the points $(0,100)$ and $(100,0)$ respectively?

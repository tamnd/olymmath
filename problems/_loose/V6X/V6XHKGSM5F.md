Given that $P$ is a point inside an acute $\triangle ABC$, the point symmetric to $P$ with respect to the midpoint $M_{A}$ of side $BC$ is $P_{A}$, the point symmetric to $P_{A}$ with respect to side $BC$ is $Q_{A}$, and the midpoint of $A Q_{A}$ is denoted as $R_{A}$. Similarly, define points $R_{B}$ and $R_{C}$. If the projections of point $P$ onto sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are $H_{A}$, $H_{B}$, and $H_{C}$ respectively, prove that $\triangle R_{A} R_{B} R_{C}$ and $\triangle H_{A} H_{B} H_{C}$ are centrally symmetric.

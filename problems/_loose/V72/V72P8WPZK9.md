Let the two tangents of a parabola be $t_{1}, t_{2}$, and the points of tangency be $T_{1}, T_{2}$. Denote the intersection point of the tangents by $M$, and prove that the focus of the parabola is given by the second intersection point of the circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$, where $k_{1}$ passes through $T_{1}$ and $M$ and is tangent to $t_{2}$ at $M$, and $k_{2}$ passes through $T_{2}$ and $M$ and is tangent to $t_{1}$ at $M$.

Someone bought wine of two sorts, of which a measure of the first costs 5 drachmas, and the second - 8. For all the wine, he paid a known number of drachmas, which is a square number. This number, when added to 60, also gives a square. The square root of this last number equals the number of measures of wine purchased. It is required to find out how much was paid for each sort of wine.

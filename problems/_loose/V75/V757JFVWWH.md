Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral in which no two opposite sides are parallel. The intersection of lines $A B$ and $C D$ is denoted by $E$, and the intersection of lines $A D$ and $B C$ is denoted by $F$. The angle bisector of $\angle A F B$ intersects segment $A B$ at point $P$ and segment $C D$ at point $R$. The angle bisector of $\angle B E C$ intersects segment $B C$ at point $Q$ and segment $A D$ at point $S$. Prove that quadrilateral $P Q R S$ is a rhombus.

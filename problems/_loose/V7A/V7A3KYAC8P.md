For the following calculation and construction tasks, formulate inverse problems. Are all of them solvable?
1) The sides of triangle $A B C$ are $a$ cm, $b$ cm, $c$ cm. Calculate the heights $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}$.
2) The surfaces of two spheres are in the ratio $m: n$. Find the ratio of their volumes.
3) Given the sides of a triangle, determine the radius of the inscribed circle.
4) Given the sides $a, b, c$ of triangle $A B C$, construct the medians $m_{a}, m_{b}, m_{c}$.

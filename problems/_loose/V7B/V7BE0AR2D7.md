For each pair of positive integers $(x, y)$ a nonnegative integer $x\Delta y$ is defined.
It is known that for all positive integers $a$ and $b$ the following equalities hold:
i. $(a + b)\Delta b = a\Delta b + 1$.
ii. $(a\Delta b) \cdot (b\Delta a) = 0$.
Find the values of the expressions $2016\Delta 121$ and $2016\Delta 144$.

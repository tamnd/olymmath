Given a triangle $ABC$. On the lines $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ are located points $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ respectively. Prove that the necessary and sufficient condition for points $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ to lie on one straight line is the equality $(BCX) \cdot (CAY) \cdot (ABZ) = 1$, or $\frac{\overrightarrow{XB}}{\overrightarrow{XC}} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{YC}}{\overrightarrow{YA}} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{ZA}}{\overrightarrow{ZB}} = 1$. (This statement is known as Menelaus' theorem ${ }^{2}$.)

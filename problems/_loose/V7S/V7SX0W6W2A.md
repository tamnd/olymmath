For any natural number $n$, let us form all possible "words" of length $n$ from the letters $A$ and $B$, and let $p_{n}$ denote the number of those that do not contain any triple $A A A$ of consecutive letters $A$, nor any pair $B B$ of consecutive letters $B$. Determine for which natural numbers $n$ both numbers $p_{n}$ and $p_{n+1}$ are even.

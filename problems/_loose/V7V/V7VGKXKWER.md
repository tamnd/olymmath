What shape should a homogeneous vertical column with a circular cross-section have so that the pressure from the load $P$ it supports and its own weight, per unit area of horizontal cross-section, is the same everywhere? (Column of equal pressure.) The specific weight of the column material is $\delta$, and the radius of its upper base is $r$.

Find then the radii of the upper and lower bases of a bridge pier so that the pressure in any of its horizontal sections
is $3000 \kappa \Gamma / \partial m^{2}$, if the specific weight of the pier material is 2.5, its height is $12 m$, and the load it supports is $90000 \kappa \Gamma$.

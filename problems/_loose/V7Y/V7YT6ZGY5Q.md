Which regular polyhedron has the following properties?

1. If the centers of the faces are connected to each other accordingly, another regular polyhedron is formed.
2. If the midpoints of the edges of this second polyhedron are connected to each other accordingly, a third regular polyhedron is formed.
3. If the centers of the faces of this third polyhedron are connected to each other accordingly, a fourth regular polyhedron is formed. Which polyhedron is this?

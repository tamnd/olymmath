There is a checkered board of size $22 \times 23$. Each cell of the board has one checker. In one move, you can choose any two checkers and move each of them to an adjacent cell. If at least two checkers end up on the same cell, you can remove exactly two checkers from that cell. Is it possible to remove all the checkers from the board using several such moves?

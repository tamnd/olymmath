The base of a pyramid is a convex polygon with $9$ sides. Each of the diagonals of the base and each of the edges on the lateral surface of the pyramid is coloured either black or white. Both colours are used. (Note that the sides of the base are not coloured.) Prove that there are three segments coloured the same colour which form a triangle.

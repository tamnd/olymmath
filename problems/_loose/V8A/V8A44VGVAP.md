In last year's UEFA Champions League, for the first time, four teams from the same country, England (Arsenal, Chelsea, Liverpool, and Manchester United), all reached the quarter-finals. The eight quarter-finalists were paired through a blind draw, with the winners of each pair advancing to the semi-finals.

a) Some English fans would have preferred if the English teams avoided each other, as this would have opened up the possibility for all four teams to reach the semi-finals. What is the probability of such a pairing?

b) Other English fans would have preferred if two pairs of English teams were drawn against each other, as this would have guaranteed that two of their teams would advance to the semi-finals. What is the probability of such a pairing?

c) In the end, two English teams were drawn against each other, while the other two faced non-English opponents. What is the probability of such a pairing?

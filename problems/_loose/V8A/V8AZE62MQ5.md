Let $A B C$ be a triangle that is not isosceles at $A$; $I$ and $O$ are respectively the centers of the inscribed circle and the circumscribed circle of this triangle $A B C$, and the inscribed circle touches the side $BC$ at $D$. The internal angle bisector of the angle at $A$ in triangle $A B C$ meets the circumscribed circle at point $M$, and the line $(DM)$ intersects the circumscribed circle again at $P$.

Show that the angle $\widehat{A P I}$ is a right angle.

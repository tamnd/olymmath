Let $S_{0}$ be a sphere with a fixed point $P_{0}$ as its center and radius $r$, and $\pi_{0}$ be a fixed plane. The distance from $P_{0}$ to $\pi_{0}$ is $a>r$. Let $S_{M}$ be a sphere with point $M$ as its center, which is externally tangent to $S_{0}$ and tangent to $\pi_{0}$. Let $\Lambda$ be the set of points $M$ that satisfy the above conditions. Suppose plane $\pi$ is parallel to $\pi_{0}$ and contains points from $\Lambda$. Let $d_{\Sigma}$ be the distance between plane $\pi$ and $\pi_{0}$. Find the minimum value $m$ of $d_{\Sigma}$.

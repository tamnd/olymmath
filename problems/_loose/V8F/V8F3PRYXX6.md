Let $N$ be a positive integer, and $N+1$ be a prime number, $a_{i} \in\{0,1\}, i=0,1,2, \cdots, N$, and $a_{i}$ are not all the same. The polynomial $f(x)$ satisfies $f(i)=a_{i}, i=0,1,2, \cdots, N$. Prove: The degree of $f(x)$ is at least $N$.

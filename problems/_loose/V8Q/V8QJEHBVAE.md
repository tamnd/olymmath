In an $n \times n$ square of cells on an infinite chessboard, $n^{2}$ chips are placed, one chip in each cell. A move is defined as jumping any chip over an adjacent chip along a side, provided that the next cell is free. In this process, the chip that was jumped over is removed from the board. Prove that a position in which no further moves are possible will not occur earlier than after $\left[\begin{array}{l}\frac{n^{2}}{3} \\ 3\end{array}\right.$ moves.

Let $G$ be a simple undirected graph with vertices $v_1,v_2,...,v_n$ . We denote the number of acyclic orientations of $G$ with $f(G)$ .**a)** Prove that $f(G)\le f(G-v_1)+f(G-v_2)+...+f(G-v_n)$ .**b)** Let $e$ be an edge of the graph $G$ . Denote by $G'$ the graph obtained by omiting $e$ and making it's two endpoints as one vertex. Prove that $f(G)=f(G-e)+f(G')$ .**c)** Prove that for each $\alpha >1$ , there exists a graph $G$ and an edge $e$ of it such that $\frac{f(G)}{f(G-e)}<\alpha$ .

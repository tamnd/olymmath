In the Cartesian coordinate system, the ellipse $\Gamma: \frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$, point $P$ is inside the ellipse $\Gamma$ and moves along the line $y=x$. Points $K, L$ are on $\Gamma$, such that $\overrightarrow{P K}, \overrightarrow{P L}$ are in the positive directions of the $x$-axis and $y$-axis, respectively, and point $Q$ makes $P K Q L$ a rectangle. Does there exist two points $A, B$ in the plane such that when the rectangle $P K Q L$ changes, $|Q A|-|Q B|$ is a non-zero constant? If so, find all possible lengths of segment $A B$; if not, explain the reason.

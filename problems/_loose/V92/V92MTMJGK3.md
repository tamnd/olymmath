Given is an isosceles triangle $A B C$ with base $\overline{A B}$ of length $14 \text{~cm}$ and legs of length $25 \text{~cm}$. Point $P$ is the midpoint of the base $\overline{A B}$. Point $Q$ is chosen on side $\overline{B C}$, and point $R$ is chosen on side $\overline{A C}$ such that $A B \| Q R$ and the area of triangle $P Q R$ is maximized. Determine the perimeter and area of triangle $P Q R$.

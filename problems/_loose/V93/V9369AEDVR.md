Let vectors $\vec{i}$ and $\vec{j}$ be the unit vectors in the positive directions of the $x$-axis and $y$-axis, respectively, in a rectangular coordinate plane. If $\vec{a}=(x+2) \vec{i}+y \vec{j}$, $\vec{b}=(x-2) \vec{i}+y \vec{j}$, and $|\vec{a}|-|\vec{b}|=2$.
(1) Find the equation of the locus of point $P(x, y)$ that satisfies the above conditions;
(2) Let $A(-1,0)$ and $F(2,0)$. Is there a constant $\lambda(\lambda>0)$ such that $\angle P F A=\lambda \angle P A F$ always holds? Prove your conclusion.

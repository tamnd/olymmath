Provide a threshold number $\mu_{100}$, or $\mu_{1} 000000$, such that

$$
F=m n \sin \frac{\pi}{m} \sqrt{\frac{1}{n^{2}}+\sin ^{4} \frac{\pi}{m}}
$$

is definitely greater than 100 and 1000 000, respectively, for all $m$ greater than $\mu_{100}$, or $\mu_{1} 000000$, assuming that $n=m^{3}$. (It is not necessary to provide the smallest such $\mu$ numbers. - See the article about Geő́cze Zoár in our No. VIII. 1.)

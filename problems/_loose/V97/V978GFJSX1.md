A brother and sister wanted to buy a ball. If the brother were to buy the ball alone, he would be short of $\frac{5}{19}$ of the ball's price, and if the sister were to buy it alone, she would be short of $\frac{1}{4}$ of the ball's price. Together, they had 185 denars more than the price of the ball. They bought the ball together, with the brother paying $45 \%$ of the ball's price, and the sister paying the remaining amount. How much money was left with the brother, and how much with the sister?

From point $P$ which lies on the base $B C$ of isosceles triangle $A B C$, lines are drawn parallel to the legs of the triangle. If $Q$ and $R$ are the intersection points of the lines with the legs $A B$ and $A C$, respectively, and $P_{1}$ is the symmetric point of $P$ with respect to the line $Q R$, then $P_{1}$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$. Prove!

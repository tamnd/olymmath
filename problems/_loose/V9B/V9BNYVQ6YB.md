Given a rectangular box that can be filled with unit cubes. If it is instead filled with as many volume-2 cubes as possible, and these cubes are aligned with the edges of the box, then the box's volume is exactly filled to $40 \%$. Find the volume of such a rectangular box.
$$
(\sqrt[3]{2}=1.2599 \cdots) \text {. }
$$
(18th International Mathematical Olympiad, 1976)

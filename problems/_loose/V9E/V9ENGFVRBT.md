Let $p$ denote a positive number, and consider the infinite sequence $1, p, \frac{1}{p}, p^{2}, \frac{1}{p^{2}}, \ldots, p^{n}, \frac{1}{p^{n}} \ldots$. For which numbers $p$ is it true that any positive number can be approximated to arbitrary precision by the sum of a finite number of suitable elements from the above sequence?

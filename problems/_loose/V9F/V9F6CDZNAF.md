1) to the parabola $y=x^{2}-4 x$ at the point where $x=1$;
2) to the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x+4 y-3=0$ at the points of its intersection with the $O x$ axis;
3) to the cycloid $x=t-\sin t, y=1-\cos t$ at the point where $t=\frac{\pi}{2}$;
4) * to the curve $y=\left|x^{3}-1\right|$ at its angular point.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral with diagonals intersecting at $E$, and let its area be $t$, the areas of triangles $E A B$ and $E C D$ be $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$, respectively. Prove that $A B C D$ is a trapezoid if and only if

$$
\sqrt{t}=\sqrt{t_{1}}+\sqrt{t_{2}}
$$

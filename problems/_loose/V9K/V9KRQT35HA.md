Let real numbers $a, b \in[\alpha, \beta]$, prove that: $\frac{b}{a} + \frac{a}{b} \leqslant \frac{\beta}{\alpha} + \frac{\alpha}{\beta}$. The equality holds if and only if $a=\alpha, b=\beta$ or $a=\beta, b=\alpha$, where $\alpha, \beta$ are positive real numbers.

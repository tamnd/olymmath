Given a triangle $A B C$ with median $B M$. On the median, an arbitrary point $P$ is marked, and through $P$ a line parallel to $A B$ is drawn, and through point $C$ a line parallel to $B M$ is drawn. These lines intersect at point $Q$. Prove that the segment $B P$ is bisected at the point of intersection with the line $A Q$.

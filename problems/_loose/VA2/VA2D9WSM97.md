In a completely enclosed cuboid glass case, there are exactly $600 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$ of water. When the case is placed successively on its different outer surfaces on a horizontal plane, the water height in the case is once $2 \mathrm{~cm}$, once $3 \mathrm{~cm}$, and once $4 \mathrm{~cm}$.

Determine the values for the capacity of the case that correspond to these measurements!

Note: The water level is assumed to be part of a horizontal plane, and adhesion is neglected.

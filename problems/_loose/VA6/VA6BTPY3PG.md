Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a strictly positive integer. Dominoes (of size $1 \times 2$) are placed on a chessboard of size $2 \mathrm{n} \times 2 \mathrm{n}$ in such a way that each square of the chessboard is adjacent to exactly one square covered by a domino (diagonals do not count). For all $\mathrm{n}$, determine the maximum number of dominoes that can be placed in this manner.

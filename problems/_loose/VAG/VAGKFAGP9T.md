Traveling from town $A$ to town $B$ , the speed limit changes every $5$ miles: from $25$ to $40$ to $20$ miles per hour (mph). Two cars, one at town $A$ and one at town $B$ , start moving toward each other at the same time. They drive exactly the speed limit in each portion of the road. How far from town $A$ , in miles, will the two cars meet? $\textbf{(A) }7.75 \textbf{(B) }8 \textbf{(C) }8.25 \textbf{(D) }8.5 \textbf{(E) }8.75$

Consider all tetrahedra $A X B Y$ circumscribed around a given sphere. Prove that for fixed points $A$ and $B$, the sum of the angles of the spatial quadrilateral $A X B Y$, i.e., the value

$$
\angle A X B + \angle X B Y + \angle B Y A + \angle Y A X
$$

does not depend on the choice of points $X$ and $Y$.

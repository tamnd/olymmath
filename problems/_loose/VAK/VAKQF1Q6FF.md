The 81st problem: Let $a_{0}$ be a given positive integer, and define the infinite sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{+\infty}$ recursively as follows: for any positive integer $n, a_{n}$ is the smallest positive integer such that $\left(\prod_{i=0}^{n} a_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}$ is a positive integer. Prove: The sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{+\infty}$ eventually becomes a constant sequence.

In a certain country, there are 100 cities. Some pairs of cities are connected by two-way flights, with no more than $n$ flights in each city. It is possible to buy a ticket only for a route that connects two different cities and consists of two flights and a layover in a third city (routes that differ in direction of travel or the layover city are considered different). It turns out that tickets are sold for 1000 different routes. What is the smallest $n$ for which this is possible?

These circles touch side $AB$ at points $K_{1}$, $K_{2}$, and $K$ respectively, with $AK_{1}=4$, $BK_{2}=6$, and $AB=16$.

a) Find the length of segment $AK$.

b) Let the circle with center $O_{1}$ touch side $AC$ at point $K_{3}$. Find the angle $CAB$ if it is known that point $O_{1}$ is the center of the circle circumscribed around triangle $OK_{1}K_{3}$.

Arbitrarily connecting the $n$ vertices of a regular $n$-sided polygon, we obtain a closed $n$-broken line. Try to prove that when $n$ is even, there are two parallel sides in the broken line; when $n$ is odd, it is impossible to have exactly two parallel sides in the broken line.

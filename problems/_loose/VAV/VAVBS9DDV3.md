5-114 $P(x)$ is a non-constant polynomial with integer coefficients, $n(P)$ is the number of integers $k$ for which $[P(k)]^{2} = 1$. Prove that:
$$
n(P)-\operatorname{deg}(P) \leqslant 2,
$$

where $\operatorname{deg}(P)$ is the degree of the polynomial $P(x)$.

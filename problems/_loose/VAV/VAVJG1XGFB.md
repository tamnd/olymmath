In the interior of triangle $A B C$, let's mark point $P$. Let $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ be the intersection points of the lines connecting $P$ with $A$, $B$, and $C$ and the opposite sides. For which points $P$ is it true that

$$
\frac{A A_{1}}{P A_{1}}+\frac{B B_{1}}{P B_{1}}+\frac{C C_{1}}{P C_{1}} \geq 9
$$

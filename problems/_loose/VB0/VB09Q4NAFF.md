Prove that if $p(x)$ is a 100th-degree polynomial such that $p(1), p(2), \ldots, p(102)$ are consecutive elements of the Fibonacci sequence in the same order, then the leading coefficient of $p(x)$ is $\frac{1}{100!}$. (For the elements $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$ of the Fibonacci sequence, $a_{1}=a_{2}=1, a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}.$)

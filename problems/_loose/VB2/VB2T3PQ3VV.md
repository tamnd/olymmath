a) Determine the parameters $p, q$ such that the expressions

$$
(x+3)^{2}+(7 x+p)^{2} \text { and }(3 x+5)^{2}+(p x+q)^{2}
$$

are equal to the square of a first-degree polynomial.

b) Let $a, b, c, A, B, C$ be given, non-zero numbers such that the polynomials

$$
(a x+b)^{2}+(A x+B)^{2} \text { and }(b x+c)^{2}+(B x+C)^{2}
$$

are equal to the square of a first-degree polynomial. Show that the same holds for the following polynomial:

$$
(c x+a)^{2}+(C x+A)^{2}
$$

The triangles $\mathrm{Az} A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ are similar and have the same orientation. On the lines connecting the corresponding vertices, take points $A, B, C$ such that

$$
\frac{A_{1} A}{A A_{2}}=\frac{B_{1} B}{B B_{2}}=\frac{C_{1} C}{C C_{2}} .
$$

Prove that the triangle $A B C$ is similar to the other two triangles.

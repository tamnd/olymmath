a) Prove that the circle passing through the midpoints of the sides of a triangle is tangent to its inscribed and three excircles (Feuerbach).

b) On the sides $AB$ and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, points $C_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ are taken such that $AC_{1} = B_{1}C_{1}$ and the inscribed circle $S$ of triangle $ABC$ is an excircle of triangle $AB_{1}C_{1}$. Prove that the inscribed circle of triangle $AB_{1}C_{1}$ is tangent to the circle passing through the midpoints of the sides of triangle $ABC$.

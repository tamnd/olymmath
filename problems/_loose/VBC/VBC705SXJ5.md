Is the following statement true: "For every quadrilateral inscribed in a convex polygon, there is an inscribed rhombus in the polygon such that one side of the quadrilateral is not greater than the side of the rhombus."

A quadrilateral is said to be inscribed in a polygon if all its vertices lie on the boundary of the polygon.

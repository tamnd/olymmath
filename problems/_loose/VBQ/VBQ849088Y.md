Let the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $a_{n+1}=a_{n}^{2}-n a_{n}+1, n=1,2,3, \cdots$.
(1) When $a_{1}=2$, find $a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}$, and conjecture a general formula for $a_{n}$;
(2) When $a_{1} \geqslant 3$, prove that for all $n \geqslant 1$,
( i ) $a_{n} \geqslant n+2$;
(ii) $\frac{1}{1+a_{1}}+\frac{1}{1+a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1+a_{n}} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}$.

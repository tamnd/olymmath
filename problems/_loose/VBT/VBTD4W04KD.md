Let's call the distance between numbers the absolute value of their difference. It is known that the sum of the distances from eight consecutive natural numbers to some number $a$ is 612, and the sum of the distances from these same eight numbers to some number $b$ is 240. Find all possible values of $a$, given that $a+b=100.5$.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be an acute isosceles triangle with $AB = AC$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of the shorter arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$. The line through $M$ parallel to $AC$ intersects lines $BC$ and $AB$ at points $D$ and $E$, respectively. The line through $D$ parallel to $AB$ intersects $AC$ at point $F$. Prove: $\angle MEF = 90^{\circ}$.

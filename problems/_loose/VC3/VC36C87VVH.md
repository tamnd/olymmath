If the angle (defined as the angle between the positive directions of the two lines) between two skew lines $a$ and $b$ is $\theta$ ($0<\theta<\pi$), take points $A', E, A, F$ on lines $a$ and $b$ respectively. If the positive directions of $A'E$ and $AF$ are the same as those of $a$ and $b$, and $\angle EA'A=\alpha, \angle A'AF=\beta, A'A=m$, then the distance between $a$ and $b$ is
$$
d=\frac{m}{\sin \theta} \sqrt{1-\cos ^{2} \theta-\cos ^{2} \alpha-\cos ^{2} \beta-2 \cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta \cdot \cos \theta} .
$$

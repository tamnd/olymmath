In $\triangle A B C$, $\angle A=90^{\circ}, \angle B<\angle C$. Through point $A$, draw the tangent of the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle A B C$, intersecting line $B C$ at $D$. Let the symmetric point of $A$ with respect to $B C$ be $E$, and draw $A X \perp B E$ at $X, Y$ is the midpoint of $A X$, and $B Y$ intersects $\odot O$ at $Z$. Prove: $B D$ is the tangent of the circumcircle of $\triangle A D Z$.

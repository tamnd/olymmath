On the domain of definition (O.D.Z.), the inequality is equivalent to $(\pi-3)^{\ln \left(x^{2}-2 x\right)} \leqslant(\pi-3)^{\ln (2-x)}$ or $x^{2}-2 x \geqslant 2-x$. The solution to the inequality $x^{2}-x-2 \geqslant 0$ is the set $(-\infty ;-1] \cup[2 ;+\infty)$. Taking into account the O.D.Z., we get $x \in(-\infty ;-1]$.

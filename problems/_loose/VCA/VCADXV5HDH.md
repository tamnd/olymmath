Find all functions $ f: \mathbb{R} \mapsto \mathbb{R}$ such that $ \forall x,y,z \in \mathbb{R}$ we have: If
$$ x^3 + f(y) \cdot x + f(z) = 0,$$
then
$$ f(x)^3 + y \cdot f(x) + z = 0.$$

Some Young Pioneers have come to the summer camp, each of whom knows between 50 and 100 of the others. There are some hats, with 761 different patterns, and the number of hats of each pattern is no less than the number of Young Pioneers. Prove: It is possible to give each Young Pioneer a hat so that each Young Pioneer's acquaintances have at least 20 different patterns of hats.

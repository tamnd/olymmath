Consider a network formed by 49 points, which are the vertices of 36 squares of side length 1 into which a square of side length 6 is decomposed.

We say that a square with vertices on the network points is good if its sides and diagonals do not lie on the sides of the network squares.

a) Find the number of good squares that can be formed with the network points.

b) Show that there exist two disjoint and non-congruent good squares such that the smallest distance between their points is $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$.

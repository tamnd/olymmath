In a school, it was decided to hold a table tennis tournament between the mathematical and humanities classes. The team of humanities classes consists of $n$ people, and the team of mathematics - of $m$, with $n \neq m$. Since there is only one table for the game, it was decided to play as follows. First, any two students from different teams start playing against each other, and all other participants line up in one common queue. After each game, the person standing first in the queue replaces the member of their team at the table, who then goes to the end of the queue. Prove that sooner or later every mathematician will play with every humanist.

Let the side lengths of $\triangle A B C$ and $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ be $a, b, c$ and $a_{1}, b_{1}, c_{1}$, and their areas be $\triangle$ and $\triangle_{1}$. Prove: Pedoe's inequality
$$
a^{2}\left(b_{1}^{2}+c_{1}^{2}-a_{1}^{2}\right)+b^{2}\left(c_{1}^{2}+a_{1}^{2}-b_{1}^{2}\right)+c^{2}\left(a_{1}^{2}+b_{1}^{2}-c_{1}^{2}\right) \geqslant 16 \triangle \triangle_{1} .
$$

When does equality hold?

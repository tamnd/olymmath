We say that a convex polygon $P$ can be *doubled* into a convex polygon $Q$ when a paper with the format of the polygon $P$ it can be folded so that it covers the surface of $Q$ exactly two times. The folds are all straight lines. Prove that if $P$ can be doubled into $Q$ then $Q$ cannot be a regular polygon with five or more sides.

## Task 1

Santa Claus is preparing gifts for children for New Year. In his gift cabinet, there are five shelves, and on each shelf, there are n different items. Santa Claus takes any item from the first shelf, then from the second, third, fourth, and fifth. This results in one set of items, which defines one gift. Santa Claus counted all possible gift combinations and found out that he would have to remove n combinations that children did not request in their letters. Prove that the total number of combinations he came up with can be divided by 30 for any initial n.

Given the ellipse $C: \frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ with an eccentricity of $\frac{1}{2}$, the upper and lower foci are $F_{1}, F_{2}$, and the right vertex is $D$. A line perpendicular to $D F_{2}$ through $F_{1}$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $A, B$, and $|B D|-\left|A F_{1}\right|=\frac{8 \sqrt{3}}{39}$.
(1) Find the value of $|A D|+|B D|$.
(2) Construct the tangents to the ellipse $C$ at points $A, B$ which intersect at point $E$. If $F_{1} E$ intersects the $x$-axis at $P$ and $F_{2} E$ intersects the $x$-axis at $Q$, find the value of $|P Q|$.

In an acute scalene triangle $ABC$, the altitude $AH$, the median $AM$, and the center $O$ of its circumcircle $\omega$ are drawn. The segments $OH$ and $AM$ intersect at point $D$, the lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $E$, and the lines $BD$ and $AC$ intersect at point $F$. The rays $EH$ and $FH$ intersect the circle $\omega$ at points $X$ and $Y$. Prove that the lines $BY$, $CX$, and $AH$ intersect at one point.

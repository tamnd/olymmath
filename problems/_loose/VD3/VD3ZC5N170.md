In the vertices of a convex $n$-gon, $m$ chips are placed ($m>n$). In one move, it is allowed to move two chips from the same vertex to adjacent vertices: one to the right, the other to the left. Prove that if after several moves there are as many chips in each vertex of the $n$-gon as there were initially, then the number of moves made is a multiple of $n$.

Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle $\alpha$ of triangle $ABC$. The circumcircle of triangle $AIC$ intersects $\alpha$ at points $P$ and $Q$ (such that $P$ and $A$ lie on the same side of line $BI$, and $Q$ and $C$ lie on the opposite side). Let $M$ be the midpoint of the smaller arc $AC$ of circle $\alpha$, and $N$ be the midpoint of the smaller arc $BC$. Prove that if $PQ \parallel AC$, then triangle $ABC$ is isosceles.

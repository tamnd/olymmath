Let $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ be the two distinct real roots of the equation $x^{2} + px + 4 = 0$. Determine which of the following conclusions is correct:
(A) $\left|x_{1}\right| > 2$ and $\left|x_{2}\right| > 2$.
(B) $\left|x_{1} + x_{2}\right| > 4$.
(C) $\left|x_{1} + x_{2}\right| < 4$.
(D) $\left|x_{1}\right| = 4$ and $\left|x_{2}\right| = 1$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1982)

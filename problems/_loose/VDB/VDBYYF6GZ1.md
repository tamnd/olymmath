On a plane, there are two convex polygons $F$ and $G$. Denote by $H$ the set of midpoints of segments, one end of each of which belongs to $F$, and the other to $G$. Prove that $H$ is a convex polygon.

a) How many sides can $H$ have if $F$ and $G$ have $n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$ sides respectively?

b) What can the perimeter of $H$ be if the perimeters of $F$ and $G$ are $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ respectively?

All vertices of a convex polyhedron are located in two parallel planes. Prove that its volume is equal to $h\left(S_{1}+S_{2}+4 S\right) / 6$, where $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ are the areas of the faces lying in these planes, $S$ is the area of the section of the polyhedron by a plane equidistant from these, and $h$ is the distance between the given planes.

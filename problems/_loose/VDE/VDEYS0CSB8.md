Let $a, b, c$ be nonnegative real numbers with arithmetic mean $m =\frac{a+b+c}{3}$ . Provethat $$ \sqrt{a+\sqrt{b + \sqrt{c}}} +\sqrt{b+\sqrt{c + \sqrt{a}}} +\sqrt{c +\sqrt{a + \sqrt{b}}}\le 3\sqrt{m+\sqrt{m + \sqrt{m}}}. $$

(1) Prove: The sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is also an arithmetic sequence;
(2) Suppose the common differences of the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are both $d \neq 0$, and there exist positive integers $s, t$, such that $a_{s}+b_{t}$ is an integer, find the minimum value of $\left|a_{1}\right|$.

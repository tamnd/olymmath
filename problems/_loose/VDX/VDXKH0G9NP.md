Let $ n\ge 2 $ be a natural number. Prove the following propositions:

**a)** $ a_1,a_2,\ldots ,a_n\in\mathbb{R}\wedge a_1+\cdots +a_n=a_1^2+\cdots +a_n^2\implies a_1+\cdots +a_n\le a_n. $
**b)** $ x\in [1,n]\implies\exists b_1,b_2,\ldots ,b_n\in\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} x=b_1+\cdots +b_n=b_1^2 +\cdots +b_n^2 . $

Let $ ABCD$ be a chordal/cyclic quadrilateral. Consider points $ P,Q$ on $ AB$ and $ R,S$ on $ CD$ with
$$ \overline{AP}: \overline{PB} = \overline{CS}: \overline{SD}, \overline{AQ}: \overline{QB} = \overline{CR}: \overline{RD}.$$
How to choose $ P,Q,R,S$ such that $ \overline{PR} \cdot \overline{AB} + \overline{QS} \cdot \overline{CD}$ is minimal?

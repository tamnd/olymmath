New Year's Tradition (from 9th grade. 3 points). On one island tribe, there is a tradition - during a ritual dance, the shaman throws up three thin straight rods of the same length, tied in a shape resembling the letter П. Adjacent rods are connected by a short thread and can freely rotate relative to each other. The rods fall on the sand, forming a random figure. If a self-intersection occurs (the first and third rods cross each other), the tribe will face poor harvests and various troubles in the coming year. If there is no self-intersection, the year will be successful, bountiful, and happy. Find the probability that the rods will predict good fortune for the year 2017.

Given an integer $n$ , an integer $1 \le a \le n$ is called $n$ -*well* if $$ \left\lfloor\frac{n}{\left\lfloor n/a \right\rfloor}\right\rfloor = a. $$ Let $f(n)$ be the number of $n$ -well numbers, for each integer $n \ge 1$ . Compute $f(1) + f(2) + \ldots + f(9999)$ .

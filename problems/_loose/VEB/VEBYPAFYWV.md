Can we assert that they have the same remainders when divided by 10, if it is known that the numbers $3a + b$ and $3b + a$ have the same remainders when divided by 10? b) Given natural numbers $a, b$, and $c$. It is known that the numbers $2a + b, 2b + c$, and $2c + a$ have the same remainders when divided by 10. Prove that the numbers $a, b$, and $c$ also have the same remainders when divided by 10.

(England, 70). For each value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$, denote by $a_{n} \in \mathbf{Z}^{+}$ the number of solutions to the equation

$$
n^{2}+x^{2}=y^{2}
$$

in natural numbers greater than $n$.

a) Prove that for any number $M$, the inequality $a_{n}>M$ holds for at least one value of $n \in \mathbf{N}$. b) Is it true that lim $a_{n}=\infty$?

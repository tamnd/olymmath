A tangent is drawn to a circle at the end $M$ of the diameter $M N$, and on it, equal segments $A B$ and $B C$ are laid out successively. Lines passing through point $N$ and points $A, B, C$ intersect the circle at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Prove that the tangents to the circle at points $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ intersect on the line $N B$.

Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid with parallel sides $AB > CD$. Let $K$ and $L$ be points on the segments $[AB]$ and $[CD]$ respectively, such that

$$
\frac{AK}{KB} = \frac{DL}{LC}
$$

Suppose there exist points $P$ and $Q$ on the segment $[KL]$ such that $\widehat{APB} = \widehat{BCD}$ and $\widehat{CQD} = \widehat{ABC}$. Show that the points $P, Q, B, C$ are concyclic.

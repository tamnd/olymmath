The integers $n>1$ is given . The positive integer $a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n$ satisfing condition :
(1) $a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_n$ ;
(2) $\frac{a^2_1+a^2_2}{2},\frac{a^2_2+a^2_3}{2},\cdots,\frac{a^2_{n-1}+a^2_n}{2}$ are all perfect squares .
Prove that : $a_n\ge 2n^2-1.$

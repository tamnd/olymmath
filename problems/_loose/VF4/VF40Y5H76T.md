For positive integers $a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_n$ prove that $$\frac{1}{\operatorname{lcm}(a_1, a_2)}+\frac{1}{\operatorname{lcm}(a_2, a_3)}+\dots+\frac{1}{\operatorname{lcm}(a_{n-1}, a_n)} \leq 1-\frac{1}{2^{n-1}}.$$

Ali-Baba and the bandit are dividing a treasure consisting of 100 gold coins, arranged in 10 piles of 10 coins each. Ali-Baba chooses 4 piles, places a cup next to each, and sets aside several coins from each pile (at least one, but not the entire pile). The bandit must then rearrange the cups, changing their initial positions, after which the coins are poured from the cups into the piles next to which the cups are placed. Ali-Baba then again chooses 4 piles out of 10, places cups next to them, and so on. At any point, Ali-Baba can leave, taking with him any three piles of his choice. The remaining coins go to the bandit. What is the maximum number of coins Ali-Baba can take if the bandit also tries to get as many coins as possible?

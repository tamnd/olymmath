The game takes place on an infinite plane. Two players are involved: one moves a single wolf token, while the other moves 50 sheep tokens. After the wolf's move, one of the sheep moves, then after the wolf's next move, another sheep moves, and so on. Both the wolf and the sheep can move no more than one meter in any direction in a single move. Is it true that, regardless of the initial position, the wolf will eventually catch at least one sheep?

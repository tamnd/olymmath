Angela, Bodo, Constanze, and Dietmar are discussing the outcomes of two football matches between class 7a and class 7b. For both matches, they make the same statements, namely:

Angela: The match ended in a draw.

Bodo: Class 7a won.

Constanze: Bodo's statement is false.

Dietmar: Angela's statement is true.

(a) Petra, who saw the first match, truthfully states that for the first match, exactly one of the four statements is false.

(b) Rolf, who saw the second match, truthfully states that for the second match, exactly one of the four statements is true.

Investigate whether (a) from Petra's statement, (b) from Rolf's statement, the outcome of the respective match (win for 7a, win for 7b, or draw) can be uniquely determined!

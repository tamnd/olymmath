For each integer $n$ with $n \geq 0$, let $f_{n}$ be the function defined by

$$
f_{n}(x)=x^{3}+(n+3) \cdot x^{2}+2 n \cdot x-\frac{n}{n+1}
$$

for all real $x$.

Determine all integers $n$ with $n \geq 0$ for which the following holds:

All roots of $f_{n}$ lie in an interval of length 3.

Prove that if $x-7 y+3=0$ and $x \in[-3 ; 4]$ then

$$
E(x, y)=\sqrt{(x+3)^{2}+y^{2}}+\sqrt{(x-4)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}}=5 \sqrt{2} .
$$

On a square grid of side $n$, some cells have been colored black and the others white. Each white cell is adjacent to a black cell, and it is always possible to go from one black cell to another by following a path made up only of black cells (the set of black cells is connected). Show that there are at least $\frac{n^{2}-2}{3}$ black cells.[^0]

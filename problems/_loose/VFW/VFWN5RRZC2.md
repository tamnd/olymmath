Let $(a_n)_{n\ge 1}$ be an increasing sequence of positive integers. Assume that there is a constant $M>0$ satisfying$$0<a_{n+1}-a_n<M.a_n^{5/8},\forall n\ge 1.$$
Prove that: there exists a real number $A$ such that for each $k\in \mathbb{Z}^+,[A^{3^k}]$ is an element of $(a_n)_{n\ge 1}.$

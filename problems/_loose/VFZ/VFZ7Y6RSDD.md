Given are natural numbers $N$ and $K$. Some number of students is arranged into $N$ non-empty groups, and then the same students are rearranged into $N+K$ non-empty groups. Prove that in the second arrangement, at least $K+1$ students ended up in a smaller group than the one they were in in the first arrangement.

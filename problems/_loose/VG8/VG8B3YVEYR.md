The 62 interns from the Animath internship each take one ice cream ball from 5 flavors offered. We know that

- the malabar-flavored ice cream was at least as popular as the tabasco-flavored ice cream;
- the number of students who chose cactus-flavored ice cream is a multiple of 6;
- at most 5 students tasted lemon basil ice cream;
- at most one student opted for foie gras ice cream.

When Igor places the order with the ice cream vendor, he communicates, in alphabetical order of the flavors, the number of students who chose each flavor. How many different lists of numbers could he have pronounced?

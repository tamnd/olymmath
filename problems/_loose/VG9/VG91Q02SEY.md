Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers, and $t$ be a real number, then $a b c\left(a^{t}+b^{t}+c^{t}\right) \geqslant a^{t+2}(-a+b+c)+b^{t+2}(a-b+c)+c^{t+2}(a+b-c)$, equality holds if and only if $a=b=c$.

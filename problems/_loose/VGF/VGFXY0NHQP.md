A farmer had a large field in the shape of a parallelogram $A B C D$, on which there was a well at some point $O$. Feeling his death approaching, the farmer bequeathed to his son Pierre two triangular plots $A O B$ and $O C D$, and all the remaining land to his son Jean. The well, however, remained their joint property. Suppose that $A B$ is greater than $B C$.

Which of the two brothers, in your opinion, inherited more land?[^7]

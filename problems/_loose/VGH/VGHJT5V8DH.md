(Simson Line) Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $\Gamma$ its circumcircle. Let $D$ be a point in the plane, and let $A', B'$ and $C'$ be the orthogonal projections of $D$ onto $(BC), (AC)$ and $(AB)$, respectively. Show that $A', B'$ and $C'$ are collinear if and only if $D \in \Gamma$.

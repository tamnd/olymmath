In the triangle $A B C$, the inscribed circle touches the sides at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Show that

$$
\frac{a_{1}^{2}}{a(s-a)}=\frac{b_{1}^{2}}{b(s-b)}=\frac{c_{1}^{2}}{c(s-c)}
$$

if $a, b, c$ are the sides of the original triangle, and $a_{1}, b_{1}, c_{1}$ are the sides of the triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$.

During the draw, a random winning combination of 6 numbers is determined. A "jackpot" is the event where a participant, by making a bet, guesses all 6 numbers correctly.

A player bought 100 tickets and in each, selected 6 numbers such that there were no repeating combinations in two different tickets. Estimate the probability p of the "jackpot."

Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $f:[0,+\infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{cc}x^{a} \sin ^{32} \frac{1}{x} \cos ^{23} \frac{1}{x}, & x>0 \\ a, & x=0 .\end{array}\right.$

a) Determine $a \in[0,+\infty)$ such that $f$ admits primitives.

b) Determine $a \in(-\infty, 0)$ such that $f$ is integrable on $[0,2 \pi]$.

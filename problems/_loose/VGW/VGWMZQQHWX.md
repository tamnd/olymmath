Let $ x $ be a 1978-digit number, and $ y $ be a number obtained by rearranging the digits of $ x $. Is it possible for the equation
$$
x + y = \underset{1978 \uparrow}{99 \cdots 9}
$$
to hold? If 1978 is changed to 1977, does the answer change?

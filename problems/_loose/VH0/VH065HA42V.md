$$
1-\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{a^{2}}-\frac{1}{a^{3}}+\frac{1}{a^{4}}-\frac{1}{a^{5}}+\ldots
$$

if $a$ is a positive number greater than one. What will the sum be as the value of $a$ approaches 1?

Two circles of radii $R$ and $r$ touch each other internally (externally) ($R > r$). Prove that the radius $r_{0}$ of the circle that touches the given two circles and their line of centers is given by the formula:

$$
r_{0}=\frac{4 R r(R \mp r)}{(R \pm r)^{2}}
$$

where the upper signs correspond to internal tangency.

Let $f:\mathbb R^+ \to \mathbb R^+$ be a function such that: $$
x,y > 0 f(x+f(y)) = yf(xy+1).
$$
a) Show that $(y-1)*(f(y)-1) \le 0$ for $y>0$.
b) Find all such functions that require the given condition.

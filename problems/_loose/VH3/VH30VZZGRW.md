Given $\cos \alpha=\tan \beta, \cos \beta=\tan \gamma, \cos \gamma=\tan \alpha$, then $\sin ^{2} \alpha=\sin ^{2} \beta=\sin ^{2} \gamma=\cos ^{4} \alpha=$ $\cos ^{4} \beta=\cos ^{4} \gamma=4 \sin ^{2} 18^{\circ}$.

Let $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ be a square, and $P$ be any point on their plane, then
$$
P A_{1}+P A_{2}+P A_{3}+P A_{4} \geqslant(1+\sqrt{2}) \max _{i} P A_{i}+\min _{i} P A_{i}
$$

The equality holds when $P$ is on the circumcircle of the square.

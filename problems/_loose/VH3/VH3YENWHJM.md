On a circular track with $n$ distinct points, there is a car at each point ready to start. Each car runs at a constant speed of 1 lap per hour. Upon the signal, they all start in a chosen direction simultaneously. If two cars meet, they both change direction and continue at the same speed. Prove that there must be a moment when all cars return to their original starting points.

In an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, let us denote $H$ its ortocenter and $I$ its incentre. Let $D$ be the perpendicular projection of $I$ on the line $B C$, and $E$ be the image of point $A$ in symmetry with center $I$. Furthermore, $F$ is the perpendicular projection of the point $H$ on the line ED. Prove that the points B, H, F and $C$ lie on one circle.

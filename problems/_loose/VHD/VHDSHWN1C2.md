In a class, there are $n$ boys and $n$ girls ($n \geqslant 3$). They sat around a round table such that no two boys and no two girls sit next to each other. The teacher has $2 n$ cards, on which the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 2 n$ are written, each one appearing once. He distributed one card to each student such that the number on any girl's card is greater than the number on any boy's card. Then, each girl wrote down on a piece of paper the sum of the numbers on three cards: her own and those of the boys sitting next to her. For which $n$ could all the $n$ numbers obtained be equal?

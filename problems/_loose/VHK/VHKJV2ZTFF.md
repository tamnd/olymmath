Let $k$ and $l$ be positive integers, prove that there exists a positive integer $M$, such that for all $n>M, \left(k+\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}+\left(l+\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}$ is not an integer.
(Australian Mathematics Competition, 1992)

[5] Segments $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$, and $C C^{\prime}$ with endpoints on the sides of an acute triangle $A B C$ intersect at a point $P$ inside the triangle. On each of these segments, a circle is constructed with the segment as its diameter, and a chord perpendicular to this diameter is drawn through the point $P$. It turns out that the three chords drawn have the same length. Prove that $P$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$.

(G. Galperin)

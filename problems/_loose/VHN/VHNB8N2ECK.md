In the rectangle $ABCD$, the sides are: $AB=CD=5a, BC=DA=3a$, where $a$ is a given length. On the sides, in the same rotational direction, we measure the distances $x=AA'=BB'=CC'=DD'$, so that the parallelogram $A'B'C'D'$ remains inside the rectangle $ABCD$.

$1^{0}$. Calculate the tangent of the acute angle of the parallelogram as a function of $x$ and $a$! Examine and plot the variation of this tangent as $x$ changes.

$2^{0}$. Express the area of the parallelogram as a function of $x$ (and $a$). Determine the value of $x$ if the area is $k^2$ (given). For which values of $k^2$ is there a solution and how many are there?

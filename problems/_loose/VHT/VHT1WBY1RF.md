Given that $\triangle A B C$ is an acute triangle, and points $A, B, C$ have projections on sides $B C, C A, A B$ as $D, E, F$ respectively. The incircles of $\triangle B D F$ and $\triangle C D E$ are $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{C}$, respectively. Circles $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{C}$ touch sides $D F$ and $D E$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively. Line $M N$ intersects circles $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{C}$ again at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove: $M P = N Q$.

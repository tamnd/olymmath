Let $c>1$ be a real number, and let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}}$ be a sequence of real numbers satisfying the conditions:
(a) For any positive integer $n$, $x_{n}>1$;
(b) For any positive integer $n$, $x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}<c$.

Prove that there exists a real number $a>1$, such that for any positive integer $n$, $x_{n}>a^{n}$.

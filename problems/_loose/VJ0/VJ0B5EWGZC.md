Let $A_1$ be the midpoint of the smaller arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of the acute-angled triangle $ABC.{}$ The point $A_1$ is reflected relative to the side $BC,$ and then its image is reflected relative to the bisector of $\angle BAC{}$ resulting in the point $A_2 $ . Similarly, the points $B_2$ and $C_2$ are constructed. Prove that the circumcenter and incenter of the triangle $ABC{}$ lie on the Euler line of the triangle $A_2B_2C_2.$ *Proposed by A. Tereshin*

Let $n$ be a given positive integer, and let $A_{n}=\left\{x \mid 2^{n}<x<2^{n+1}\right.$, and $x=3 m, m \in$ $\left.\mathbf{N}_{+}\right\}$.
(1) When $n$ is odd, find the maximum and minimum numbers in $A_{n}$;
(2) When $n$ is odd, find the sum of all elements in $A_{n}$;
(3) When $n$ is even, find the sum of all elements in $A_{n}$.

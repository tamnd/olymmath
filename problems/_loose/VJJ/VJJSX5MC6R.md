Let $S$ be the set of all functions obtained by finite compositions of sine, cosine, tangent, arcsine, arccosine, and arctangent functions. Does there exist, for any positive rational number $q$, a function $f \in S$ such that $f(1) = q$? If so, provide a proof; if not, provide a counterexample and explain the reasoning.

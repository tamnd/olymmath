Let the line $l: y=x+b$ not intersect the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{25}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$. Through a point $P$ on the line $l$, draw the tangents $P M$ and $P N$ to the ellipse $C$, with points of tangency $M$ and $N$, respectively, and connect $M N$.
(1) When point $P$ moves along the line $l$, prove that the line $M N$ always passes through a fixed point $Q$;
(2) Prove that when $M N \parallel l$, the fixed point $Q$ bisects the segment $M N$.

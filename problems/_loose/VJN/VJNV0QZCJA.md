On a carousel with $n$ seats, a boy rode for $n$ sessions in a row. After each session, he got up and, moving clockwise, sat down on another seat. The number of seats the boy passes by when moving to another seat, including the one he sits on, is called the length of the transition. For which $n$ could the boy have visited each seat over $n$ sessions, if the lengths of all $n-1$ transitions are distinct and less than $n$?

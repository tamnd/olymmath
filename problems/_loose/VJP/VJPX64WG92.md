Given is an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ in which $|BC|:|AC|=3:2$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of side $\overline{AC}$, and $P$ the midpoint of segment $\overline{BD}$. On the line $AC$ there is a point $X$ such that $|AX|=|BC|$, with $A$ between $X$ and $C$. The line $XP$ intersects side $\overline{BC}$ at $E$. The line $DE$ intersects line $AP$ at $Y$. Prove that points $A, X, Y, E$ lie on a circle if and only if $|AB|=|BC|$.

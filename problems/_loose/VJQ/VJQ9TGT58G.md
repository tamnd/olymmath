In $\triangle A B C$, $A C \neq B C$, and $D$ is a point inside $\triangle A B C$ such that $\angle A D B=90^{\circ}+\frac{1}{2} \angle A C B$. A tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ at point $C$ intersects line $A B$ at point $P$; another tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle A D C$ at point $C$ intersects line $A D$ at point $Q$. Prove that $P Q$ bisects $\angle B P C$.

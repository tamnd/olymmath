If $ \{a_k\}$ is a sequence of real numbers, call the sequence $ \{a'_k\}$ defined by $ a_k' = \frac {a_k + a_{k + 1}}2$ the *average sequence* of $ \{a_k\}$ . Consider the sequences $ \{a_k\}$ ; $ \{a_k'\}$ - *average sequence* of $ \{a_k\}$ ; $ \{a_k''\}$ - average sequence of $ \{a_k'\}$ and so on. If all these sequences consist only of integers, then $ \{a_k\}$ is called *Good*. Prove that if $ \{x_k\}$ is a *good* sequence, then $ \{x_k^2\}$ is also *good*.

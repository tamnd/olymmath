Let $\angle R=135^{\circ}$. The perpendicular from $A$ to the nearest side of the angle intersects the smaller circle at point $P$, the perpendicular from $A$ to the second side intersects $B P$ at point $Q$. Finally, let $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$ be the centers of the original circles, and $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of $\triangle A B Q$. Prove that $B O$ is the bisector of angle $O_{1} B O_{2}$.

(Euler's circle) Let $A B C$ be a triangle. We denote $M_{A}, M_{B}, M_{C}$ the midpoints of the sides $[B C],[C A],[A B]$, and $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$ respectively.

1. Show that the six points $M_{A}, M_{B}, M_{C}, H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ are concyclic.
2. Let $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ be the midpoints of the segments $[A H],[B H],[C H]$, where $H$ is the orthocenter of $A B C$. Show that $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ are concyclic with the six points mentioned above.

Consider the two quadratic equations:

$$
x^{2}+2 x+q=0
$$

and

$$
(1+q)\left(x^{2}+2 x+q\right)-2(q-1)\left(x^{2}+1\right)=0
$$

Show that the roots of the second equation are complex if the first equation has two distinct real roots and vice versa.

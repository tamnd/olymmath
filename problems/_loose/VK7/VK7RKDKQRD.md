Given that $\alpha, \beta$ are the two distinct real roots of the equation $4 x^{2}-4 t x-1=0(t \in \mathbf{R})$, and the function $f(x)=\frac{2 x-t}{x^{2}+1}$ has the domain $[\alpha, \beta]$.
( I ) Find $g(t)=\max f(x)-\min f(x)$;
( II ) Prove: For $u_{i} \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)(i=1,2,3)$, if $\sin u_{1}+\sin u_{2}+\sin u_{3}=1$, then $\frac{1}{g\left(\tan u_{1}\right)}+$ $\frac{1}{g\left(\tan u_{2}\right)}+\frac{1}{g\left(\tan u_{3}\right)}<\frac{3}{4} \sqrt{6}$.

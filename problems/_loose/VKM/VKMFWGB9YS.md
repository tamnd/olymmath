Let $A$ be some point in space that does not belong to the plane $\alpha$. Consider all possible planes passing through point $A$ and forming the same angle with the plane $\alpha$. Prove that all lines, along which the planes passing through point $A$ intersect the plane $\alpha$, are tangent to one circle.

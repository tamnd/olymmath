The number of ninth-graders participating in the math olympiad is $45\%$ more than the number of eighth-graders. The students were distributed between two buildings: in the first building, there were fewer than 1000 students, and in the second building, there were more than 1000. How many students took the olympiad in the second building, if $\frac{1}{3}$ of the ninth-graders and $\frac{6}{7}$ of the eighth-graders were in the second building?

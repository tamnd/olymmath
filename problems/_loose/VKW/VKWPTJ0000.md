Prove that for each natural number $n$ the equality $[\sqrt{4 n+1}]=[\sqrt{4 n+3}]$ holds. Here, the brackets [ ] denote the integer part of a number. (Recall that the integer part of a number $x$ is the greatest integer not exceeding $x$. For example, $[3.7]=3$.)

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with incenter $I$ and excenter relative to vertex $A$ denoted by $K$ . Let $D$ be the intersection of the internal angle bisector relative to vertex $A$ with line $BC$ , and let $E$ be the circumcenter of triangle $BDK$ . Also, let $F \ne C$ be the second intersection of line $AC$ with the circumcircle of triangle $BEC$ .

(a) Prove that the points $D, E, F$ are collinear.
(b) Prove that $ID = IF$ .

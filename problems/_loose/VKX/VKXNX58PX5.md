Quadrilateral $A B C D$ is inscribed in circle $S$. Circles $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ touch circle $S$ internally at points $A$ and $C$ respectively. Circle $S_{1}$ intersects sides $A B$ and $A D$ at points $K$ and $N$ respectively, and circle $S_{2}$ intersects sides $B C$ and $C D$ at points $L$ and $M$ respectively. It turns out that lines $K L$ and $M N$ are parallel. Prove that the radii of circles $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ are equal.

Define the *digital reduction* of a two-digit positive integer $\underline{AB}$ to be the quantity $\underline{AB} - A - B$. Find the greatest common divisor of the digital reductions of all the two-digit positive integers. (For example, the digital reduction of $62$ is $62 - 6 - 2 = 54.$)

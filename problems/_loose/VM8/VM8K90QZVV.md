Each side of an equilateral triangle is divided into $k$ equal parts. Through the points on the sides, we draw lines parallel to the sides of the triangle. We draw a zigzag line through the triangle, such that:

a) No triangle appears twice;

b) Each subsequent triangle on the zigzag line shares a side with the previous one.

How many triangles does the zigzag line pass through?

Let $a,b,c$ be real numbers, $z_{1},z_{2},z_{3}$ be complex numbers such that
$\begin{cases}
|z_1|=|z_2|=|z_3|=1\\
\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}+\frac{z_{2}}{z_{3}}+\frac{z_{3}}{z_{1}}=1\\
\end{cases}$
Find $|az_{1}+bz_{2}+cz_{3}|$.

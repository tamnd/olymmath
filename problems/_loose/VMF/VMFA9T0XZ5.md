Try to prove that for each integer $r > 1$, there exists a smallest integer $h(r) > 1$ such that in any partition of the set $\{1,2, \cdots, h(r)\}$ into $r$ groups, there exist integers $a \geqslant 0,1 \leqslant x \leqslant y$, such that the numbers $a+x, a+y, a+x+y$ are contained in the same group of the partition.

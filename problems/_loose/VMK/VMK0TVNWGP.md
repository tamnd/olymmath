Prove the following property: let $I \subset \mathbb{R}, f: I \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function, $\left(u_{n}\right)$ a sequence of elements of $I$ defined recursively by: $u_{0} \in I$ and $\forall$ $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, u_{n+1}=f\left(u_{n}\right)$ and converging to a real number $\ell$. If $f$ is continuous at $\ell$ then $\ell$ is a fixed point of $f$.

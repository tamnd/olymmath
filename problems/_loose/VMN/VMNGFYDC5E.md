In the forest of the purple tree, there are three types of talking animals: foxes, snakes, and turtles. The first lie only on rainy days, the second always lie, and the third always tell the truth. One day, the explorer Berny talks to four animals. Their statements, reported in the order in which they were made, are:

A: "It is raining today."

B: "The animal that spoke before me is lying."

C: "It is sunny today."

D: "The one who spoke before me is lying or I am a fox."

What is the maximum number of turtles Berny could have spoken to?
(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4
(E) It is not possible to determine.

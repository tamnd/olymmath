Given two points $ P,\ Q$ on the parabola $ y = x^2$. Let $ h$ be the $ y$ coordinate of the midpoint of the segment $ PQ$.

(1) Let $ L$ and $ m$ be the length and slope of the segment $ PQ$ respectively. Express $ h$ in terms of $ L,\ m$.

(2) When $ L$ is fixed, find the minimum value of $ h$.

A year is called interesting if it consists of four different digits. For example, 2019 is interesting. It is even the case that 2013 through 2019 are all interesting: as many as seven interesting years in a row.

(a) Determine the next sequence of seven consecutive interesting years and prove that it is indeed the next sequence.

(b) Prove that there is nowhere in the years 1000 to 9999 a sequence of eight consecutive interesting years.

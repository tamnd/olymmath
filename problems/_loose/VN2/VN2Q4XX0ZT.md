In a boys' camp, every participant knows at least $n$ other participants (the acquaintance is always mutual; $n$ is at least 2). We want to seat the boys around a round table so that everyone knows the two neighbors. Prove: at least $n+1$ boys can be seated this way, but it is possible that more cannot be seated, although it is also possible that more than $n+1$ need to sit around the table.

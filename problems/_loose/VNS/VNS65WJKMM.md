Alberto and Barbara have two bags, which contain 2022 and $9^{99}$ candies, respectively. At any time, one of them can eat a candy from their own bag (without having to respect turns or alternation rules), on the condition that, in this case, they must give another candy to the other person, who puts it in their own bag. After some time, neither of them can eat any more candies. It can be stated that, at this point, ..

(A) both are left without candies

(B) Alberto has one candy, Barbara none

(C) Barbara has one candy, Alberto none

(D) both have one candy

(E) one of them (it cannot be predicted which) has one candy, the other none

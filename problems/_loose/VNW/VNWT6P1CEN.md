Consider the cube $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$. Let $E \in (B D)$ such that $B D = 4 \cdot D E$ and $E^{\prime} \in (B^{\prime} D^{\prime})$ such that $D^{\prime} E^{\prime} = 3 \cdot B^{\prime} E^{\prime}$. If $M, N, P$, and $Q$ are the midpoints of the edges $A B, B C$, $D^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, and $A^{\prime} D^{\prime}$, respectively, show that:

a) the planes $(E P Q)$ and $\left(E^{\prime} M N\right)$ are parallel;

b) if $B D^{\prime} \cap \left(E^{\prime} M N\right) = \{T\}$ and $B D^{\prime} \cap (E Q P) = \left\{T^{\prime}\right\}$, then $T T^{\prime} = 2 \cdot B T$.

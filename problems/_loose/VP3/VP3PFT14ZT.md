Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}(n \geqslant 2)$ be $n$ distinct real numbers, $S=a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}$, $M=\min _{1<i<j \leqslant n}\left(a_{i}-a_{j}\right)^{2}$. Prove that: $\frac{S}{M} \geqslant \frac{n\left(n^{2}-1\right)}{12}$.
(1990 China Mathematical Olympiad Problem)

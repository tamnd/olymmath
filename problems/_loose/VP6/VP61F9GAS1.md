On the outside of $\triangle A B C$, construct equilateral triangles $\triangle C B A_{1}, \triangle A C B_{1}, \triangle B A C_{1}$ with each side of $\triangle A B C$ as their base. Let the centroids of these triangles be $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}$, respectively. Also, let the centroids of $\triangle A B_{1} C_{1}, \triangle B A_{1} C_{1}, \triangle C B_{1} A_{1}$ be $G_{1}, G_{2}, G_{3}$, respectively. Then the hexagon $O_{1} G_{3} O_{2} G_{1} O_{3} G_{2}$ is a regular hexagon.

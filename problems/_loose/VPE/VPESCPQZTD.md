Prove that all faces of a tetrahedron are equal if and only if one of the following conditions is satisfied:

a) when completing the tetrahedron, a rectangular parallelepiped is formed;

b) the segments connecting the midpoints of opposite edges are perpendicular;

c) the areas of all faces are equal;

d) the center of mass and the center of the inscribed sphere coincide.

Let's prove that if $a$ and $b$ are positive numbers, $u > 0$, then

$$
\frac{a^{u}+b^{u}}{a^{v}+b^{v}} \geq \frac{a^{u+k}+b^{u+k}}{a^{v+k}+b^{v+k}}
$$

(For the latter problem, see the article in this issue of the problem setter.)

Let $H=\frac{3}{\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}}, G=\sqrt[3]{a b c}, A=\frac{a+b+c}{3}, Q=\sqrt{\frac{\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)}{3}}$.
Prove that when $a, b, c>0$, we have
$$
\frac{A \cdot G}{Q \cdot H} \geq \sqrt[6]{\frac{27}{32}}
$$

A skier can't decide between two routes from $A$ to $B$ of equal length. The first route goes entirely on flat ground, while the second, on the contrary, is half uphill and half downhill. The skier knows that his speed on an uphill is three times slower than on flat ground, but on a downhill, it is three times faster than on flat ground.

-- No matter which path I choose, - he reasons, - the time I will spend is the same anyway.

Is he right, isn't he?

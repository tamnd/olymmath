Suppose there are several juice boxes, one of which is poisoned. You have $n$ guinea pigs to test the boxes. The testing happens in the following way:

- At each round, you can have the guinea pigs taste any number of juice boxes.
- Conversely, a juice box can be tasted by any number of guinea pigs.
- After the round ends, any guinea pigs who tasted the poisoned juice die.

Suppose you have to find the poisoned juice box in at most $k$ rounds. What is the maximum number of juice boxes such that it is possible?

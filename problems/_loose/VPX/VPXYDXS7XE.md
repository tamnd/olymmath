(2004 National College Entrance Examination, Fujian Province) Two people, A and B, participate in an English test. It is known that among the 10 questions available, A can answer 6 of them, and B can answer 8 of them. It is stipulated that each test will randomly select 3 questions from the available questions for testing, and at least 2 questions must be answered correctly to pass.
(I) Find the probability distribution and mathematical expectation of the number of questions A answers correctly, $\xi$;
(II) Find the probability that at least one of A and B passes the test.

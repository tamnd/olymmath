Given $S=\left\{z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}\right\}$ is a set consisting of $n$ $(n \geqslant 9)$ distinct complex numbers, among which exactly $n-3$ are real numbers. Prove: There exist at most two quadratic polynomials $f(z)$ with complex coefficients, such that $f(S)=S$.

Let $P$ be a point inside a circle of radius $R$, $d$ a line passing through $P$ and $d^{\prime}$ the perpendicular to $d$ at $P$. We rotate the lines $d$ and $d^{\prime}$ by an angle $\phi$ around $P$. Show that regardless of the position of the point $P$, the area swept by $d$ and $d^{\prime}$ inside the circle (which has the shape of a cross) will be $\pi R^{2} \frac{4 \phi}{360}$.

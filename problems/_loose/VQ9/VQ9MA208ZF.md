Prove that for any positive $a$, greater than 1, the following inequality holds:

$$
\frac{1+a^{2}+a^{4}+\ldots+a^{2 n}}{a+a^{3}+\ldots+a^{2 n-1}}>\frac{n+1}{n}
$$

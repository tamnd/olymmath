A family of sets $\mathscr{F}$ is called "perfect": if for any three sets $X_{1}, X_{2}, X_{3} \in \mathscr{F}$, the sets $\left(X_{1} \backslash X_{2}\right) \cap X_{3}$ and $\left(X_{2} \backslash X_{1}\right) \cap X_{3}$ are such that at least one is empty. Prove: if $\mathscr{F}$ is a perfect subfamily of a finite set $U$, then $|\mathscr{F}| \leqslant|U|+1$.
(2015, Czech-Polish-Slovak Mathematical Competition)

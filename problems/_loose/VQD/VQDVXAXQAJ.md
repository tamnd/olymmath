Prove that the inequality

$$
\ln x_{1}+\ln x_{2}+\ldots+\ln x_{n}+\ln n!\leq n\left[\ln \left(x_{1}+2 x_{2}+\ldots+n x_{n}\right)-\ln n\right]
$$

holds for any positive real numbers $x_{1} ; x_{2} ; \ldots ; x_{n}$ (with $n \geq 1$). When does the equality hold?

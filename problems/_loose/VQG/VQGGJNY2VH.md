* On the hyperbola $x y=1$, the point with the abscissa $\frac{n}{n+1}$ is $A_{n}$, and the point with the abscissa $\frac{n+1}{n}$ is $B_{n}(n \in \mathbf{N})$. The point with coordinates $(1,1)$ is denoted as $M$. Also, $P_{n}\left(x_{n}, y_{n}\right)$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle A_{n} B_{n} M$. When $n \rightarrow \infty$, find: the limit point coordinates $(a, b)$ of $P_{n}$, where $a=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} x_{n}, b=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} y_{n}$.

At a party, 20 people arrived. It is known that each of them has exactly 14 friends among those who came (friendship is mutual). In addition, during the party, 10 people went out to the balcony, and it turned out that all of them are friends with each other. Prove that all the people who came to the party can be divided into two rooms in such a way that everyone in each room is friends with everyone else.

The chord $MN$ on the circle is fixed. For every diameter $AB$ of the circle consider the intersection point $C$ of the lines $AM$ and $BN$ and construct the line $\ell$ passing through $C$ perpendicularly to $AB$ . Prove that all the lines $\ell$ pass through a fixed point.

(E. Kulanin, Moscow)

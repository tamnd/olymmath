From vertex O, three rays $\mathrm{OA}, \mathrm{OB}$, and $\mathrm{OC}$ emerge, not lying in the same plane, such that angles $\mathrm{AOB}$ and $\mathrm{BOC}$ are equal and acute. Prove that if angle $\mathrm{AOC}$ is obtuse and line $\mathrm{OB}$ is not perpendicular to the plane $\mathrm{AOC}$, then the dihedral angle formed by the planes $\mathrm{AOB}$ and $\mathrm{BOC}$ along edge $\mathrm{OB}$ is obtuse.

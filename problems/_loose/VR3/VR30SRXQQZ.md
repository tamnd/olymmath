Points $M$ and $N$ lie on sides $AB$ and $CD$ such that $AK:KB=DM:MC$ and points $L$ and $N$ lie on sides $BC$ and $DA$ such that $BL:LC=AN:ND$ . The circumcircle of the triangle $CML$ intersects diagonal $AC$ for the second time in point $P$ . The circumcircle of triangle $DNM$ intersects diagonal $BD$ for the second time in point $Q$ . Circumcircles of triangles $AKN$ and $BLK$ intersect for the second time in point $R$ .
Prove that the circumcircle of $PQR$ passes through the intersection of $AC$ and $BD$

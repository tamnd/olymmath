Two vessels, called $A$ and $B$, together have a capacity of exactly 8 liters. A certain amount of water $W$ is distributed between the two vessels such that $A$ is half full and $B$ is completely full. If water is poured from $B$ into $A$ until $A$ is completely full, then $B$ is still one-sixth full. The questions are:

a) the capacity of each of the vessels $A$ and $B$, b) the amount of water $W$.

Determine all the answers to a) and b) that satisfy the given conditions!

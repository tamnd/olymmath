Given a quadratic trinomial $P(x)$. Prove that there exist pairwise distinct numbers $a, b$, and $c$ such that the following equalities hold:

$$
P(b+c)=P(a), P(c+a)=P(b), P(a+b)=P(c)
$$

(N. Agakhanov)

In isosceles $\triangle A B C$, $A B=A C$, point $P$ satisfies $P B<P C, A P \parallel B C$. On the extensions of $P B$ and $P C$, take points $X$ and $Y$ respectively, such that $\angle P X M=\angle P Y M$, where $M$ is the midpoint of $B C$. Prove: $A, P, X, Y$ are concyclic.

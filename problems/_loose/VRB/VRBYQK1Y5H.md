Let $a, b, c$ be three non-zero real numbers such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a^{2}+b+c=\frac{1}{a} \\
& b^{2}+c+a=\frac{1}{b} \\
& c^{2}+a+b=\frac{1}{c}
\end{aligned}
$$

Show that $a=b$ or $a=c$ or $b=c$.

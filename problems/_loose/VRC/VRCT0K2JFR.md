The lines $A M$ and $A N$ are symmetric with respect to the bisector of angle $A$ of triangle $A B C$ (points $M$ and $N$ lie on the line $B C$). Prove that $B M \cdot B N /(C M \cdot C N)=c^{2} / b^{2}$. In particular, if $A S-$ is the symmedian, then $B S / C S=c^{2} / b^{2}$.

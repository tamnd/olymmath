Let ABC be a triangle, and let $M$ be the foot of the median from $A$. Let $\ell_{\mathrm{b}}$ be the bisector of $\widehat{\mathrm{AMB}}$ and $\ell_{\mathrm{c}}$ the bisector of $\widehat{\mathrm{AMC}}$. Finally, let B' be the orthogonal projection of B on $\ell_{b}$, let $C^{\prime}$ be the orthogonal projection of $C$ on $\ell_{c}$, and let $A^{\prime}$ be the intersection point of the lines (AM) and $\left(\mathrm{B}^{\prime} \mathrm{C}^{\prime}\right)$.

Prove that $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}=A^{\prime} C^{\prime}$.

Varya and Miron are playing the following game. There is a pile of $n$ stones on the table. The players take turns, with Varya starting first. On their turn, a player divides any pile that has more than one stone into several equal piles. The player who cannot make a move (when there is exactly one stone in each pile before their turn) wins. For which values of $n$ will Varya win, and for which values will Miron win, if both players play optimally?

Given three sequences $\left(\mathscr{E}_{n}\right),\left(\mathscr{F}_{n}\right)$ and $\left(\mathscr{G}_{n}\right)$ of $\sigma$-subalgebras of the algebra $\mathscr{F}$. Let $\xi$ be a random variable, $\mathrm{E} \xi^{2}<\infty$, and assume that for each $n$ the conditions

$$
\mathscr{E}_{n} \subseteq \mathscr{F}_{n} \subseteq \mathscr{G}_{n} \text { and 1.i.m. } \mathrm{E}\left(\xi \mid \mathscr{E}_{n}\right)=\eta, \text { l.i.m. } \mathrm{E}\left(\xi \mid \mathscr{G}_{n}\right)=\eta
$$

Prove that then l.i.m. $\mathrm{E}\left(\xi \mid \mathscr{F}_{n}\right)=\eta$.

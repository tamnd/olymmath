Polar Friends (from 8th grade, 2 points). At an Antarctic station, there are $n$ polar explorers, all of different ages. With a probability $p$, any two polar explorers will develop a friendly relationship, independently of other affinities or antipathies. When the wintering period ends and it's time to return home, in each pair of friends, the older one gives a friendly advice to the younger one. Find the expected number of those who did not receive any friendly advice.

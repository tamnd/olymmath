Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $|AB|=|AC|>|BC|$ and let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle of this triangle. The line $BI$ intersects the side $\overline{AC}$ at point $D$, and the line through $D$ perpendicular to $AC$ intersects the line $AI$ at point $E$. Prove that the point $J$, which is the reflection of point $I$ across the line $AC$, lies on the circumcircle of triangle $BDE$.

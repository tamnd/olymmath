Find all functions $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that for any real numbers $x$ and $y$, the following inequality holds:
$$
f\left(x^2+2y f(x)\right) + (f(y))^2 \leq f\left((x+y)^2\right)
$$

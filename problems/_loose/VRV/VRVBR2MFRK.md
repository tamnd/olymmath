The 66th problem: Prove that there exist positive integers $\mathrm{n}_{1}<\mathrm{n}_{2}<\ldots<\mathrm{n}_{50}$, such that $\mathrm{n}_{1}+S\left(\mathrm{n}_{1}\right)=\mathrm{n}_{2}+S\left(\mathrm{n}_{2}\right)=\ldots=$ $n_{50}+S\left(n_{50}\right)$. Here $S(n)$ denotes the sum of the digits of the positive integer $n$ in decimal notation.

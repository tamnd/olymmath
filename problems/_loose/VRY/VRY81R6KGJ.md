Let $P$ be a fixed point inside a sphere, and draw three mutually perpendicular rays from $P$ intersecting the sphere at points $A$, $B$, $C$. Point $Q$ is the vertex of the rectangular parallelepiped formed by $P A$, $P B$, $P C$ that is diagonally opposite to $P$. Find the locus of all points $Q$ corresponding to all sets of three such rays drawn from $P$.

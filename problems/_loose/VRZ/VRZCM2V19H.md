\section*{Task 1 - 011221}

In international mail, for rectangular parcels (in the form of a cuboid), the following maximum and minimum dimensions are prescribed:
Maximum dimensions
Length, width, and height together $90 \mathrm{~cm}$, with the greatest length not exceeding $60 \mathrm{~cm}$;
Minimum dimensions Length $10 \mathrm{~cm}$, Width $7 \mathrm{~cm}$.

1) What is the maximum volume a parcel can have? What are the dimensions of length, width, and height in this case?

2) What is the minimum volume a parcel can have? What are the dimensions of the edges in this case? (Justification!)

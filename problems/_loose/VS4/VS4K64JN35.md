Let $A=\{1,2,3,4,5\}, \Gamma_{1}=\{\{1,2\},\{3,4\},\{4,5\}\}$, $\left.\Gamma_{2}=\{\{1\},\{3\},\{1,3,5\}\right\}$, then
$$
\begin{array}{c}
\Gamma_{1} \subseteq P^{*}(A), \Gamma_{2} \subseteq P^{*}(A) . \\
\bigcup_{F \in \Gamma_{1}}^{\bigcup} F=\{1,2,3,4,5\}=A . \\
\underset{F \in \Gamma_{2}}{\bigcup} F=\{1,3,5\} \neq A .
\end{array}
$$

Because
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\bigcup_{F \in \Gamma_{1}} F=\{1,2,3,4,5\}=A . \\
\underset{F \in \Gamma_{2}}{\bigcup} F=\{1,3,5\} \neq A .
\end{array}
$$

So $\Gamma_{1}$ is a cover of $A$ and is a 3-cover of $A$, $\Gamma_{2}$ is not a cover of $A$, but $\Gamma_{2}$ is a 3-cover of the subset $B=\{1,3,5\}$ of $A$.

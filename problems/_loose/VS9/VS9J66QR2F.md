$$ AB=CD=EF \text{and} BC=DE=.FA$$
Prove that if $\sphericalangle FAB + \sphericalangle ABC=\sphericalangle FAB + \sphericalangle EFA = 240^{\circ}$, then $\sphericalangle FAB+\sphericalangle CDE=240^{\circ}$.

On the sides $A B, B C, C A$ of an equilateral triangle $A B C$, find points $X, Y, Z$ (respectively) such that the area of the triangle formed by the lines $C X, B Z, A Y$ is one-fourth the area of triangle $A B C$ and the condition is satisfied:

$$
\frac{A X}{X B}=\frac{B Y}{Y C}=\frac{C Z}{Z A}
$$

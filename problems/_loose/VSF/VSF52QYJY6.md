Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the largest integer $M$ such that for any distinct positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ with sum $M$ there exist integers $b_1,\ldots,b_n \in \{-2,-1,0,1,2\}$ , not all equal to $0$ , such that $\sum_{i=1}^n a_ib_i = 0$ .

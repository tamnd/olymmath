Every evening Ivan Taranov comes to a bus stop at a random time. At this stop, two routes stop - on one of them Ivan can go home, and on the other - to visit his friend Kozjakin. Ivan waits for the first bus and depending on which bus arrives, he goes either home or to his friend. After some time, Ivan noticed that he ends up at Kozjakin's place about twice as often as at home. Based on this, Ivan concludes that one of the buses runs twice as often as the other. Is he right? Can the buses run with the same frequency under the conditions of the problem? (It is assumed that the buses do not run randomly, but according to some schedule.)

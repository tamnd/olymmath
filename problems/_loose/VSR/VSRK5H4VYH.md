$(x, y)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of positive integers $x$ and $y$. Let $a$ and $b$ be two positive integers, $(a, b)=1, p \geqslant 3$ be a prime number, $\alpha=\left(a+b, \frac{a^{p}+b^{p}}{a+b}\right)$, prove that
(1) $(a, a)=1$;
(2) $\alpha=1$ or $\alpha=p$.
(Singapore Mathematical Olympiad, 1985)

Given are two different parallel lines $g$ and $h$. Outside the strip enclosed by them, there are also two different points $A$ and $B$ such that no point of the segment $A B$ lies in this strip and the distance from $A$ to $g$ is smaller than the distance from $A$ to $h$. For each point $P$ on $h$, let $A^{\prime}$ and $B^{\prime}$ denote the intersection of $g$ with $P A$ and $P B$, respectively.

Construct all points $P$ on $h$ for which, with these notations, $\overline{A^{\prime} P}=\overline{B^{\prime} P}$ holds!

Justify the construction; discuss whether all points $P$ with the given property can be obtained and how many such points there can be depending on the position of the given $g, h, A, B$!

To crawl along an edge, she needs one day; at night she rests. Upon reaching the destination, she slides back to the starting point along the cube's diagonal during the following night, to begin a new path the next morning.

She starts on the morning of January 1, 1983. On the morning of which day will she be back at the starting point, after having tried all the paths?

The 11 sailors from Ulysses' crew have been captured by Blackbeard's pirates, who propose a challenge: a number between 1 and 11 will be written on each of their foreheads, without telling them which number it is (several can have the same number). Then, the 11 sailors can look at the numbers assigned to the others but are not allowed to communicate. Finally, each must secretly tell Blackbeard a number, which only he can hear.

In order to regain their freedom, at least one of the sailors must give exactly the number written on his forehead. They can discuss a strategy before the challenge. Do they have a strategy that guarantees their release?

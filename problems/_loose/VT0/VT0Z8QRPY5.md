In a certain country, some cities are connected by two-way direct flights, allowing travel from any city to any other city (including via transfers). It is known that if all the flights forming any odd-length cycle are shut down, it would no longer be possible to travel from any city to any other city. Prove: It is possible to divide the cities of the country into 4 provinces such that each flight connects cities belonging to different provinces.

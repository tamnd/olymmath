If in the prime factorization of a positive integer, the power of each prime factor is greater than 1, then this integer is called a good number. For example, $3^{2} \times 5^{4}, 2^{3} \times 7^{5}$ are good numbers, while $3 \times 5, 2^{2} \times 13$ are not good numbers. Prove: there are infinitely many pairs of consecutive good numbers.

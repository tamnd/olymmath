(40 points) Vasya and Misha write down natural numbers on the board and compute their squares. At some point, it turns out that for three numbers $n, k, l$ the equality $n^{2}+k^{2}=2 l^{2}$ holds. Prove that the number

$$
\frac{(2 l-n-k)(2 l-n+k)}{2}
$$

is a perfect square.

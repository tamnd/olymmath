In a regular triangular pyramid $SABC$ ($S$ is the vertex), point $P$ is the midpoint of the apothem $SD$ lying in the face $SBC$. On the edge $AB$, a point $M$ is taken such that $MB: AB = 2: 7$. A sphere with its center on the line $MP$ passes through points $A$ and $C$ and intersects the line $BC$ at point $Q$ such that $CQ = m$. Find the volume of the pyramid $SABC$ if it is known that the radius of the sphere is $\frac{\frac{m}{\sqrt{3}}}{\sqrt{3}}$.

Let the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ intersect at point $M$. Draw a line through $M$ parallel to $AD$, intersecting $AB$, $CD$ at points $E$, $F$ respectively, and intersecting the extension of $BC$ at point $O$. $P$ is a point on the circle with center $O$ and radius $OM$. Prove that $\angle O P F = \angle O E P$.
(1996 National Junior High School Competition)

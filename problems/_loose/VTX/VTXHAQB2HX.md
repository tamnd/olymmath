Prove that the volume of the pyramid $A B C D$ does not depend on the position of the segments $A B$ and $C D$ on these lines. Find this volume if $A B=a, C D=b$, and the angle and distance between the lines $l$ and $m$ are $\alpha$ and $c$ respectively.

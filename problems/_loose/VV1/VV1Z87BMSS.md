In trapezoid $ABCD$, $BC \parallel DA$, diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $P$. Let the inradii of $\triangle PAB$, $\triangle PBC$, $\triangle PCD$, and $\triangle PDA$ be $r_{1}$, $r_{2}$, $r_{3}$, and $r_{4}$ respectively, and $\frac{1}{r_{1}} + \frac{1}{r_{3}} = \frac{1}{r_{2}} + \frac{1}{r_{4}}$. Prove: $AB + CD = BC + DA$.

Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle and $O$ the center of its circumscribed circle. Let $O_{1}$ be the point on the perpendicular bisector of segment $A B$, lying on the opposite side of line $A B$ from point $O$. Denote the circle with center $O_{1}$ and radius $A O_{1}$ by $\mathcal{K}$. Let the lines $C A$ and $C B$ intersect the circle $\mathcal{K}$ again at points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$. Show: if the segments $A_{1} B$ and $A B_{1}$ intersect on the circumscribed circle of triangle $A B C$, then the quadrilateral $A O_{1} B O$ is cyclic.

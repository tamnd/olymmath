Solve the following system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(4 t^{2}+t+4\right) x+(5 t+1) y & =4 t^{2}-t-3 \ldots \\
(t+2) x+ 2 y & =t \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

Examine the signs of the roots for different values of $t$!

For which values of $t$ is the system of equations determined, indeterminate, or contradictory?

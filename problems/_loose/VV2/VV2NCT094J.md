We know that among the following four statements about the positive integers $a$ and $b$, three are true and one is false:

1) $(a+1)$ is divisible by $b$;
2) $a=2b+5$
3) $a+b$ is divisible by $3$
4) $a+7b$ is a prime number.

Determine the numbers $a$ and $b$.

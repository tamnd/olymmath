Let $P(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+\ldots+a_{n} x^{n}$ be a polynomial of degree $n$ ( $n \geq 1$, integer), and suppose further that $\left|a_{n}\right| \geq\left|a_{1}\right|$ for $i=0,1,2, \ldots n$.

Show: For every root $x_{0}$, $\left|x_{0}\right| \leq n$.

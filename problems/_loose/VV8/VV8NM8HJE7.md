Define the *ratio* of an ellipse to be the length of the major axis divided by the length of its minor axis.

Given a trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ and that $\angle{ADC}$ is a right angle, with $AB=18, AD=33, CD=130,$ find the smallest ratio of any ellipse that goes through all vertices of $ABCD.$

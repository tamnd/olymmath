Consider triangle $ABC$. We draw: the median $AL$ corresponding to side [BC], the bisector $LE$ of angle $\overline{ALC}$, and the bisector $LF$ of angle $\overline{AL} \bar{B}$. $(E \in AC, F \in AB)$ We denote $EF \cap AL=\{M\}$. Prove that for any point $O$ in the plane, we have: $2 \cdot \overline{OM}=\overline{OE}+\overline{OF}$.

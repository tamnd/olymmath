Let graph $P$ be a regular 2016-gon.
(1) If a diagonal of graph $P$ divides $P$ into two parts (the edges of $P$ are not considered as diagonals), and each part contains an even number of edges of $P$, then this diagonal is called a good diagonal. A convex $m$-gon (with $4 < m \leqslant 1008$) whose sides are good diagonals and whose vertices are vertices of $P$ is called a good convex $m$-gon. Calculate the number of good convex $m$-gons;
(2) If a diagonal of graph $P$ divides $P$ into two parts (the edges of $P$ are not considered as diagonals), and each part contains an odd number of edges of $P$, then this diagonal is called a bad diagonal. A convex $m$-gon (with $4 < m \leqslant 1008$) whose sides are bad diagonals and whose vertices are vertices of $P$ is called a bad convex $m$-gon. Calculate the number of bad convex $m$-gons.

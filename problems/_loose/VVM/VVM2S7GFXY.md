We say that a set $E$ of natural numbers is special when taking any two distinct elements $a, b \in E$ it holds that $(a-b)^{2}$ divides the product $ab$.

(a) Find a special set consisting of three elements.

(b) Does there exist a special set consisting of four natural numbers that are in arithmetic progression?

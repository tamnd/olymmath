Let's define *almost mean* of numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ as $\frac{a_1 + a_2 + \ldots + a_n}{n+1}$ . Oleksiy has positive real numbers $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_{2023}$ , not necessarily distinct. For each pair $(i, j)$ with $1 \leq i, j \leq 2023$ , Oleksiy wrote on a board *almost mean* of numbers $b_i, b_{i+1}, \ldots, b_j$ . Prove that there are at least $45$ distinct numbers on the board.

On the shore of a circular lake, there are four piers $K, L, P, Q$. A motorboat departs from pier $K$, and a rowboat from pier $L$. If the motorboat heads straight to $P$, and the rowboat heads straight to $Q$, they will collide at some point $X$ on the lake. Prove that if the motorboat heads to $Q$, and the rowboat heads to $P$, they will reach these piers simultaneously.

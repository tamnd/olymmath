On the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, points $M$, $N$, and $P$ are given, respectively. Prove:

a) if points $M_{1}$, $N_{1}$, and $P_{1}$ are symmetric to points $M$, $N$, and $P$ with respect to the midpoints of the corresponding sides, then $S_{MNP} = S_{M_{1}N_{1}P_{1}}$.

b) if $M_{1}$, $N_{1}$, and $P_{1}$ are such points on sides $AC$, $BA$, and $CB$ that $MM_{1} \parallel BC$, $NN_{1} \parallel CA$, and $PP_{1} \parallel AB$, then $S_{MNP} = S_{M_{1}N_{1}P_{1}}$.

Given a regular prism $K L M N K_{1} L_{1} M_{1} N_{1}$ with base $K L M N$. Planes $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are perpendicular to $L_{1} N$ and pass through vertices $K$ and $N_{1}$, respectively. Let $A$ and $B$ be the points of intersection of planes $\alpha$ and $\beta$ with the diagonal $L_{1} N$, such that $A N < B N$.

a) Find the ratio $L_{1} B: A N$.

b) Suppose additionally that a sphere of radius $\frac{1}{2}$ is tangent to all lateral faces of the prism, as well as to planes $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Find the segment $L_{1} N$ and the volume of the prism $K L M N K_{1} L_{1} M_{1} N_{1}$.

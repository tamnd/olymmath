The numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 2 n-1,2 n$ are divided into two groups of $n$ numbers each. Let $a_{1}b_{2}>\ldots>b_{n}$ be the numbers of the second group in descending order. Prove that

$$
\left|a_{1}-b_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}-b_{2}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{n}-b_{n}\right|=n^{2}
$$

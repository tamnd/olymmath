Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers such that $n > k \geq 4$ and moreover $k(k-1)$ is not divisible by $n-1$ and $k(k-1)(k-2)$ is not divisible by $n-2$. Prove that the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has at least two prime divisors $p$ and $q$ such that $p \mid (n-1)$ and $q \mid (n-2)$.

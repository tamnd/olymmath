Find all values of the parameter $a$ for which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
\left(a\left|y^{3}\right|+a\left|x^{3}\right|-8\right)\left(x^{6}+y^{6}-3 a^{2}\right)=0 \\
\sqrt{x^{6} y^{6}}=a
\end{array}\right.
$$

has eight distinct solutions. (16 points)

In a party of $2020$ people, some pairs of people are friends. We say that a given person's *popularity* is the size of the largest group of people in the party containing them with the property that every pair of people in that group is friends. A person has popularity number $1$ if they have no friends. What is the largest possible number of distinct popularities in the party?

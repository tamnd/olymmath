Find the smallest number $n>4$ for which there exists a set of $n$ people such that any two who know each other have no common acquaintances, and any two who do not know each other have exactly two common acquaintances. (Acquaintance is a symmetric relation: if $A$ knows $B, A \neq B$, then $B$ also knows $A$.)

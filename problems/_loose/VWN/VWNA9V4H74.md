The Rejected Cannon. This simple puzzle in the field of artillery technology you will probably solve without hesitation. It is so simple that a child can understand it. No knowledge of artillery is required to solve the puzzle. Nevertheless, some of my readers will have to ponder it for five minutes.

An inventor proposed a new large gun to a committee whose task was to consider such matters. The inventor claimed that, by loading the cannon once, it could fire 60 shots at a rate of 1 shot per minute. Trials were conducted and it was found that the cannon fired 60 shots in an hour. However, the invention was rejected "due to non-compliance with the technical data specified in the application."

- What nonsense - the inventor exclaimed. - You saw that the rate of fire of the cannon was exactly as I promised.
- Nothing of the kind, - the experts countered, - the rate of fire was different.

Could you explain the mysterious reason for the disagreement? Who was right, the inventor or the experts?

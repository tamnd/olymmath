Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left(1+a_{1}\right)\left(1+a_{1}+a_{2}\right) \cdots\left(1+a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}\right) \\
\geqslant \sqrt{(n+1)^{n+1} a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}} .
\end{array}
$$

(Involving the ratio of areas of right-angled triangles with altitudes) In an acute $\triangle ABC$, $D, E, F$ are the feet of the altitudes from $B C, A C, A B$ respectively, then
(1) $\frac{S_{\triangle A E F}}{S_{\triangle A B C}}=\cos ^{2} A, \frac{S_{B C E F}}{S_{\triangle A B C}}=\sin ^{2} A, \frac{S_{\triangle A E F}}{S_{B C E F}}=\cot ^{2} A$;
(2) $S_{\triangle D E F}=2 S_{\triangle A B C} \cdot \cos A \cdot \cos B \cdot \cos C \leqslant \frac{1}{4} S_{\triangle A B C}$.

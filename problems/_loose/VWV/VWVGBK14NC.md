The territory of the Thirtieth Kingdom consists of all integers. A principality will be called a set of the form $\{a k+b \mid k \in \mathbb{Z}\}$, where $a \neq 0$ and $b$ are some integers (that is, an infinite arithmetic progression in both directions). The tsar wants to divide the entire territory of the kingdom, except for the numbers 3 and 10, into an infinite number of non-overlapping principalities. Is this possible?

(A. A. Teselev)

If one connects the adjacent midpoints of the sides of a regular $n$-gon, a new regular $n$-gon similar to the original one is formed. Let $\Delta A=A-A^{\prime}$ be the difference in the areas of the two $n$-gons.

For which $n$ does $\triangle A=\frac{A}{10}$ hold?

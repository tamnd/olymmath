In cup A, there are $m$ milliliters of red ink, and in cup B, there are $m$ milliliters of blue ink. Pour $a$ milliliters from cup A to cup B $(0<a<m)$, stir it, and then pour $a$ milliliters from cup B back to cup A, then at this moment,
(A) The blue ink mixed into cup A is less than the red ink mixed into cup B.
(B) The blue ink mixed into cup A is more than the red ink mixed into cup B.
(C) The blue ink mixed into cup A is the same as the red ink mixed into cup B.
(D) The relationship between the amount of blue ink mixed into cup A and the amount of red ink mixed into cup B is uncertain.
(1st "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1990)

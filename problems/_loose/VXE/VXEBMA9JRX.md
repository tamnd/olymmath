10.3, $\star$ ** Let $\triangle A B C$ be inscribed in the unit circle $O$, with $A, B, C$ corresponding to the complex numbers $t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}$, respectively, and let $P$ be any point on the circumcircle, corresponding to the complex number $t$. Then the equation of the Simson line of $P$ with respect to $\triangle A B C$ is
$$
t z-s_{3} \bar{z}=\frac{1}{2 t}\left(t^{3}+s_{1} t^{2}-s_{2} t-s_{3}\right),
$$

where $s_{1}=t_{1}+t_{2}+t_{3}, s_{2}=t_{2} t_{3}+t_{3} t_{1}+t_{1} t_{2}, s_{3}=t_{1} t_{2} t_{3}$.

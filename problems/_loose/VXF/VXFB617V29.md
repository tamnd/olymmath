$$
y_{n}=\binom{n}{0} x_{0}+\binom{n}{1} x_{1}+\cdots+\binom{n}{n} x_{n}, \text { for } n \in \mathbb{N}_{0}
$$

is a geometric sequence. Determine $x_{2020}$.

Prove that an integer $n \ge 2$ is a prime if and only if $\phi (n)$ divides $(n - 1)$ and $(n + 1)$ divides $\sigma (n)$.

[Here $\phi$ is the Totient function and $\sigma $ is the divisor - sum function.]

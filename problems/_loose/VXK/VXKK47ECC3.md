From point $A$ in the plane, let the half-lines $e_{0}, e_{1}, \ldots, e_{6}$ form a $30^{\circ}$ angle with each consecutive one, starting with $e_{1}$ with $e_{0}$. Project the point $B_{0}$, which is one unit away from $A$ on $e_{0}$, perpendicularly onto $e_{1}$, and let the projection be $B_{1}$. Similarly, project $B_{1}$ onto $e_{2}$, and let the projection be $B_{2}$, and so on. Calculate the length of the broken line $B_{0} B_{1} \ldots B_{6}$, and the area of the polygon $B_{0} B_{1} \ldots B_{6}$.

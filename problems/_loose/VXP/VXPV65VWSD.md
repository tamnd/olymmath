Let $P$ be a point inside a triangle $A B C$. A line through $P$ parallel to $A B$ meets $B C$ and $C A$ at points $L$ and $F$, respectively. A line through $P$ parallel to $B C$ meets $C A$ and $B A$ at points $M$ and $D$ respectively, and a line through $P$ parallel to $C A$ meets $A B$ and $B C$ at points $N$ and $E$ respectively. Prove

$$
(P D B L) \cdot(P E C M) \cdot(P F A N)=8 \cdot(P F M) \cdot(P E L) \cdot(P D N)
$$

where $(X Y Z)$ and $(X Y Z W)$ denote the area of the triangle $X Y Z$ and the area of quadrilateral $X Y Z W$.

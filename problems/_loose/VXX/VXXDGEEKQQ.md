In a school, there are 30 clubs, each attended by 40 children. For each $i=1,2, \ldots, 30$, let $n_{i}$ denote the number of children attending exactly $i$ clubs. Prove that in the same school, it is possible to organize 40 clubs with 30 children in each such that the numbers $n_{i}$ for these new clubs would be the same. (V. Dolnikov)

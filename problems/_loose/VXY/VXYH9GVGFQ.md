$$
\sqrt{3} \tan A \cdot \tan B - \tan A - \tan B = \sqrt{3} \text{.}
$$
(1) Find the size of $\angle C$;
(2) If $c=2$, and $\triangle A B C$ is an acute triangle, find the range of $a^{2}+b^{2}$.

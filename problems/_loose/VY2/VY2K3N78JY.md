Prove: There exists a positive real number $C$, such that the following conclusion holds: If positive integers $H, N$ satisfy $H \geqslant 3, N \geqslant \mathrm{e}^{C H}$, then among the first $N$ positive integers, selecting no fewer than $C H \frac{N}{\ln N}$ numbers, one can always find $H$ numbers such that the greatest common divisor of any two of these $H$ numbers is equal to the greatest common divisor of these $H$ numbers.

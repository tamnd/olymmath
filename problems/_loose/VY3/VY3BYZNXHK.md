Given the triangle $ABC$ with unequal sides, where $AC$ is the shortest. On the sides $AB$ and $CB$, points $A'$ and $C'$ lie at a distance $AC$ from $A$ and $C$, respectively. Point $P$ is the intersection of the diagonals in the quadrilateral $AA' C' C$. It is to be proven that under these conditions, the areas of triangles $A A' P$ and $C C' P$ can never be equal.

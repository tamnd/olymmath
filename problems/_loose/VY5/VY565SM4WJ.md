Let $a_{i} 、 b_{i}>0(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n+1)$, $b_{i+1}-b_{i} \geqslant \delta>0$ ( $\delta$ is a constant). If $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=1$, prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{i \sqrt*{a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{i} b_{1} b_{2} \cdots b_{i}}}{b_{i+1} b_{i}}<\frac{1}{\delta} .
$$

Given $\triangle A B C$. On the line $A C$ is chosen a point $D$ such that $\overline{C D}=3 \overline{C A}$ (point $A$ is between points $C$ and $D$), and on the line $B C$ is chosen a point $E$, different from $B$, such that $\overline{C E}=\overline{B C}$. If $\overline{B D}=\overline{A E}$, prove that $\measuredangle B A C=90^{\circ}$.

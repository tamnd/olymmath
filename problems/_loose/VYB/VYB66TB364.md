For any positive number $x$, we say that the numbers $x+1$ and $\frac{x}{x+1}$ are children of $x$ and that the two are siblings. For example, $\frac{3}{2}$ and $\frac{1}{3}$ are siblings, as they are children of $\frac{1}{2}$; indeed, $\frac{3}{2}=\frac{1}{2}+1$ and $\frac{1}{3}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{1}{2}+1}$.

a) Find a sibling of $\frac{5}{7}$.

b) Can a number be a child of two different positive numbers? Why?

c) Show that $\frac{1}{2008}$ is a descendant of 1, that is, it is a child of a child of a child... of a child of 1.

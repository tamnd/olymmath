Through the opposite vertices $A$ and $C$ of the quadrilateral $A B C D$, a circle is drawn, intersecting

sides $A B, B C, C D$ and $A D$ at points $M, N, P$ and $Q$ respectively. It is known that $B M=B N=D P=D Q=R$, where $R$ is the radius of the given circle. Prove that in this case the sum of angles $B$ and $D$ of the quadrilateral is $120^{\circ}$.

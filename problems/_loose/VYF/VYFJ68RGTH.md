Consider an $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ and a point $S$ outside its plane. The set of all points belonging to rays with origin at $S$ and passing through the points of the $n$-gon is an $n$-faceted angle. Prove that if the original $n$-gon is convex, then the corresponding $n$-faceted angle is the intersection of $n$ half-spaces defined by $n$ planes intersecting at point $S$. (Such an angle is called a convex polyhedral angle.)

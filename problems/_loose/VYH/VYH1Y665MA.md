Regular Polygons Inversion helps to solve the problem.

In the kingdom of King Dodon, there are 500 cities, each of which has the shape of a regular 37-sided star, with towers located at its vertices. Dodon decided to enclose them with a convex wall such that each segment of the wall connects two towers. Prove that the wall will consist of no fewer than 37 segments. (If several segments lie on the same straight line, they are counted as one.)

Suppose $z_0,z_1,\ldots,z_{n-1}$ are complex numbers such that $z_k=e^{2k\pi i/n}$ for $k=0,1,2,\ldots,n-1$ . Prove that for any complex number $z$ , $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}|z-z_k|\ge n$ .

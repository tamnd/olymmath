14・13 Suppose non-zero digits are selected in such a way that the probability of selecting a digit $d$ is $\lg (d+1)-\lg d$. What is the set of digits from which the probability of selecting a 2 is $-\frac{1}{2}$ of the probability of selecting a digit from the following sets?
(A) $\{2,3\}$.
(B) $\{3,4\}$.
(C) $\{4,5,6,7,8\}$.
(D) $\{5,6,7,8,9\}$.
(E) $\{4,5,6,7,8,9\}$.
(36th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1985)

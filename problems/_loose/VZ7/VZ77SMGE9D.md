On the sides $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots, A_{n-1} A_{n}, A_{n} A_{1}$ of a regular polygon with side length $a$, consider the points $B_{1}, B_{2}, \ldots, B_{n}$, respectively, in the same direction and such that $\left[A_{1} B_{1}\right] \equiv\left[A_{2} B_{2}\right] \equiv \ldots \equiv\left[A_{n} B_{n}\right]=x, x \in(0, a)$. Determine $x$ such that the area of the polygon $B_{1} B_{2} \ldots B_{n}$ is minimized.

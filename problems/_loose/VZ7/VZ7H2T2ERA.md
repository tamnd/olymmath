Given the sides of an acute triangle are $2, 3, x$, then the range of the third side $x$ is
(A) $1<x<5$.
(B) $\sqrt{5}<x<\sqrt{13}$.
(C) $\sqrt{13}<x<5$.
(D) $1<x<\sqrt{5}$.
(E) $\sqrt{5}<x<5$.
(Chongqing, Sichuan Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1983)

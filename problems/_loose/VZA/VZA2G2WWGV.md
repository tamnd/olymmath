"Come here," says Mrs. Pickwick to her three pupils. "Look, I have five caps: three white and two black. Now, while you stand with your eyes closed, I will put one cap on each of you. When you open your eyes again, each of you will be able to see the caps of the others, but of course, not your own or the ones left unused. The first one who can determine the color of their own cap will get a gold coin."

After some time, without exchanging a single word, all the pupils simultaneously declared that they were wearing white caps. How did they reason?

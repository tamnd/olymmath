We consider the set of all tetrahedra for which the following holds:

(1) One of the faces of the tetrahedron is the area of an equilateral triangle.

(2) Of the edges of the tetrahedron, three have the (given) length $a$ and three have the length $a \sqrt{2}$.

a) Show that two non-congruent tetrahedra exist that belong to this set!

b) Give the surface area for each of these tetrahedra!

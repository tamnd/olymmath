Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $D, E, F$ the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$ respectively. We denote $\Gamma$ as the circumcircle of $AEF$. Let $\Gamma_{1}\left(\operatorname{resp} . \Gamma_{2}\right)$ be the circle tangent to $\Gamma$ at $E$ (resp. $\left.F\right)$ passing through $D$. Prove that the second intersection point, which we will denote as $X$, of $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ lies on the line $(B C)$.

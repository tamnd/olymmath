How does the function

$$
y^{2}=\frac{4 a^{2} x^{2}+b^{2}\left(x^{2}-1\right)^{2}}{\left(x^{2}+1\right)^{2}}
$$

change as $x$ varies from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$?

Determine the maximum and minimum values of $y$ if $a > b$.

Furthermore, show that if $y$ is given a value between $a$ and $b$, then the equation in $x$ is a quartic equation with real roots, and finally determine these roots if one of the roots is $a$.

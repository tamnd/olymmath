The fractional part of the mixed number must be less than 1. (For example, $4 \frac{2}{3}$ ). What is the difference between the largest and smallest possible mixed number that can be formed?
(A) $4 \frac{3}{5}$
(B) $4 \frac{9}{20}$
(C) $4 \frac{3}{10}$
(D) $4 \frac{4}{15}$
(E) $4 \frac{7}{20}$

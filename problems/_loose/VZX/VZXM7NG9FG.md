Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of sides $B C$ and $A D$ respectively of quadrilateral $A B C D$. Segments $A M, D M, B N$ and $C N$ divide quadrilateral $A B C D$ into seven parts (six of which are triangles, and the seventh is a quadrilateral). Prove that if the areas of six of these parts are odd natural numbers, then all six of these parts are triangles.

(A. Kuznetsov, D. Shiryayev)

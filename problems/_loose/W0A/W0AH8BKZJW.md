Let $n$ be an integer such that $n \geqslant 3$. Lucie has $n$ white sheets arranged in a circle, and she writes a real number on each sheet. Find all integers $k$ such that $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$ for which the following property is true:

If at least one of the numbers written by Lucie is not zero, there exist $k$ consecutive sheets whose sum of numbers is not zero.

The quadrilateral $A B C D$ is inscribed in a circle with diameter $B D$. Let $M$ be the symmetric point of $A$ with respect to $B D$ and let $N$ be the intersecting point of the straight lines $A M$ and $B D$. The line passing through $N$, which is parallel to $A C$, intersects $C D$ and $B C$ in $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that the points $P, C, Q$ and $M$ are vertices of a rectangle.

At a round table, $2 n$ people are sitting: $n$ physicists and $n$ chemists, some of whom always tell the truth, while the others always lie. It is known that the number of chemist liars is equal to the number of physicist liars. When asked: "Who is your right neighbor?" everyone sitting at the table answered: "Chemist." Prove that $n$ is even.

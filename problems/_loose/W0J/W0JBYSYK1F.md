$A, B, C$ form a triangle $PQR$ with $C \in PQ$ and $B \in PR$. Let $C_{1}$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ in $\Delta ABC$ . Prove that $CC_{1}$ bisects $\widehat{QC_{1}P}$ .

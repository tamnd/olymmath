We want to mechanically drill a cylindrical borehole with a diameter of $80 \mathrm{~cm}$ on a plot of land. The first meter of drilling costs 10 euros, and each subsequent meter costs 4 euros more than the previous one.

A. How long is the longest cable that we can place in the borehole after $1.5 \mathrm{~m}$ of drilling?

B. How much does the drilling of the fifth meter of the borehole cost?

C. How much would we pay for a $5 \mathrm{~m}$ deep borehole if we receive a $5 \%$ discount when paying in cash?

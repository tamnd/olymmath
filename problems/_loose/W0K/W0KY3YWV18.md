In a deck of playing cards, $A, J, Q, K$ represent $1, 11, 12, 13$ respectively. Party A takes 13 hearts, and Party B takes 13 spades, shuffles them separately, and then A and B each play one card in turn, forming 13 pairs of red and black cards. Prove that the product of the differences of these 13 pairs must be an even number.
("Chinese Youth Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Invitational, 1987)

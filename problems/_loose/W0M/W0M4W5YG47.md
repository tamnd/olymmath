Given a triangle $A B C$. Construct two lines $x$ and $y$ such that for any point $M$ on side $A C$, the sum of the lengths of segments $M X_{M}$ and $M Y_{M}$, drawn from point $M$ parallel to lines $x$ and $y$ until they intersect sides $A B$ and $B C$ of the triangle, equals 1.

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, $\Gamma$ its circumcircle, and $B'$ and $C'$ the midpoints of $[AC]$ and $[AB]$, respectively. Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$, and $G$ the centroid of $ABC$. Let $D$ be the intersection of $\Gamma$ and $(GH)$ such that $H$ lies between $D$ and $G$. Finally, let $B''$ and $C''$ be the points of intersection of $\Gamma$ with $\left(B' C'\right)$. Show that $AB'' / AC'' = DB'' / DC''$.

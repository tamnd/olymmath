Given the parabola $y^{2}=4 a x(a>0)$ with focus $F$, a circle centered at point $A(a+4,0)$ with radius $|A F|$ intersects the parabola at points $M$ and $N$ above the $x$-axis.
(1) Prove that point $A$ lies on the ellipse with foci at $M$ and $N$, and passing through $F$;
(2) Let point $P$ be the midpoint of $M N$. Does there exist a value of $a$ such that $|F P|$ is the arithmetic mean of $|F M|$ and $|F N|$? If such a value exists, find $a$; if not, explain why.

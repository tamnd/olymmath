A box (rectangular parallelepiped) is given, on the surface of which (but not inside) an ant is crawling. Initially, the ant is sitting in a corner. Is it true that among all the points on the surface, the point farthest from the ant is the opposite corner? (The distance between two points is considered to be the length of the shortest path connecting them on the surface of the parallelepiped.)

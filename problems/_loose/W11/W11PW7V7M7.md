Prove that it is possible to color each natural number with one of three colors such that the following two conditions are satisfied:

i) For every $n \in \mathbb{N}_{0}$, all natural numbers $x$ such that $2^{n} \leqslant x<2^{n+1}$ are the same color.

ii) There do not exist natural numbers $x, y$, and $z$ of the same color (except for $x=y=z=2$) such that $x+y=z^{2}$.

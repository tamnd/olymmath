Consider the equation

$$
x^{3}+2 y^{3}+4 z^{3}=6 x y z+1
$$

Prove that

a) if $(x, y, z)$ is a solution, then $(2 z-x, x-y, y-z)$ is also a solution;

b) there are infinitely many solutions where $x, y$, and $z$ are positive integers.

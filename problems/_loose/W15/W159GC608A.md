In the plane, circles $k$ and $l$ intersect at points $E$ and $F$. The tangent to circle $l$ constructed at point $E$ intersects circle $k$ at point $H(H \neq E)$. On the arc $E H$ of circle $k$, which does not contain point $F$, choose a point $C(E \neq C \neq H)$ and let the intersection of line $C E$ with circle $l$ be $D(D \neq E)$. Prove that triangles $D E F$ and $C H F$ are similar.

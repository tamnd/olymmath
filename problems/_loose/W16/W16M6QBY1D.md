Person A and Person B start at the same time and place, walking in opposite directions, and each reaches their respective destination A and B after one hour. If they still start from the original place and swap their destinations, then Person A arrives at destination B 35 minutes after Person B reaches destination A. The ratio of Person A's speed to Person B's speed is
(A) $7: 12$.
(B) $12: 17$.
(C) $3: 4$.
(D) $4: 3$.
(China Hubei Province Huanggang Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

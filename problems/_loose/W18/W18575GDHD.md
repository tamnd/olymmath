Let $x, y, z$ be positive integers such that $xy=z^2+2$. Prove that there exist integers $a, b, c, d$ such that the following equalities are satisfied:
\begin{eqnarray*} x=a^2+2b^2\\
y=c^2+d^2\\
z=ac+2bd\\
\end{eqnarray*}

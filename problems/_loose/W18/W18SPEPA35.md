There are 1001 steps on a mountain, and some of them have stones, one per step. Sisyphus takes any stone and moves it to the nearest free step above (i.e., if the next step is free, he places it there, and if it is occupied, he moves it up several steps to the first free one). After this, Hades rolls down one step any stone that has a free step below it. There are 500 stones, and initially, they are on the lower 500 steps. Sisyphus and Hades take turns, with Sisyphus starting first. His goal is to place a stone on the top step. Can Hades prevent him?

Given a triangle $ABC$. Point $A_{1}$ is symmetric to vertex $A$ with respect to line $BC$, and point $C_{1}$ is symmetric to vertex $C$ with respect to line $AB$.

Prove that if points $A_{1}, B$, and $C_{1}$ lie on the same line and $C_{1} B=2 A_{1} B$, then angle $C A_{1} B$ is a right angle.

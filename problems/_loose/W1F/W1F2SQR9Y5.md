Given positive integers $m>1, n$, $m$ is odd and $m \mid n$, let $X$ be a non-empty subset of the set $\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, and $S(X)$ be the sum of all elements in the set $X$. Find the number of all subsets $X$ such that $m \mid S(X)$.

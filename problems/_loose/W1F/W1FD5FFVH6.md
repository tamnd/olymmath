Participants from 100 different countries are taking part in an international competition. Each country has sent 2 participants. Each participant knows their compatriot and exactly one other participant, and this knowledge is mutual. Show that it is possible to divide the participants into two disjoint groups such that in each group there are neither two participants from the same country nor two participants who know each other.

In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$ and the angle between them is $45^{\circ}$. Consider the following 8 points: the midpoints of the two legs, the feet of the three altitudes, the orthocenter, and the midpoints of the segments connecting the three vertices. It is to be proven that these points are the vertices of a regular octagon.

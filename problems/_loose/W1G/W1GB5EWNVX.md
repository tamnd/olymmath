In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, $A(-a, 0), B(a, 0)$, where the constant $a>0$. Moving points $P$ and $Q$ move above the $x$-axis, satisfying $A P \perp P Q, B Q \perp P Q$, and the quadrilateral $A B Q P$ is a convex quadrilateral with a constant area $S$. Find the equation of the boundary of the set of all points on the moving line $P Q$.

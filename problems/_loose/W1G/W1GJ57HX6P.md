A circle $k$ has chords $AB$ and $CD$ intersecting at point $P$. We know that $P$ is inside $k$, but it is different from its center. The tangents to $k$ at $A$ and $B$, and at $C$ and $D$, intersect at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. Show that the line $EF$ intersects the line connecting the points of tangency of any tangents drawn from any point on $EF$ to $k$ at $P$. (H)

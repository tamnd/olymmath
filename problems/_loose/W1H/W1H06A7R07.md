The medians $A M_{a}, B M_{b}$, and $C M_{c}$ of triangle $A B C$ intersect at point $M$. The circle $\omega_{a}$ passes through the midpoint of segment $A M$ and is tangent to side $B C$ at point $M_{a}$. Similarly, the circle $\omega_{b}$ passes through the midpoint of segment $B M$ and is tangent to side $C A$ at point $M_{b}$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the points of intersection of circles $\omega_{a}$ and $\omega_{b}$. Prove that points $X, Y$, and $M_{c}$ are collinear.

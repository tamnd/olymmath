Consider the polynomials $P(x)=x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c, Q(x)=x^{3}+A x^{2}+B x+C$ ( $x$ is the variable, $a, b, c, A, B, C$ are parameters). We know that the three roots of $P$ are positive and that the roots of $Q$ are the reciprocals of the roots of $P$. Prove that $a \cdot A>9, b \cdot B>9$.

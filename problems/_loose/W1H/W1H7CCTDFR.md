(1998 Canadian Olympiad Problem) Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n+1}$ be positive real numbers satisfying the condition $\frac{1}{1+x_{1}}+\frac{1}{1+x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1+x_{n+1}}=1$. Prove that: $x_{1} x_{2} \cdots x_{n+1} \geqslant n^{n-1}$.

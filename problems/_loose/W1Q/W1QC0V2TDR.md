Gulliver found himself in the land of Lilliput with 7,000,000 rubles. He immediately spent all his money on bottles of kefir, which cost 7 rubles per bottle (an empty bottle cost 1 ruble at the time). After drinking all the kefir, he returned the bottles and used all the money he received to buy more kefir. He noticed that both the cost of the kefir and the cost of an empty bottle had doubled. Then he drank all the kefir again, returned the bottles, and used all the money he received to buy more kefir, and so on. Each time he visited the store, both the cost of the kefir and the cost of an empty bottle doubled. How many bottles of kefir did Gulliver drink?

For a tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$ and a point $P$ inside it, the distances from $P$ to the vertices $A_{i}$ and the opposite faces are $l_{i}, d_{i}$, respectively. The distances from the vertices $A_{i}$ to the opposite faces are $h_{i}(i=1,2,3,4), k \geqslant \frac{3}{4}$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{4}\left(\frac{l_{i}}{2 h_{i}+d_{i}}\right)^{k} \geqslant \frac{4}{3^{k}}$.

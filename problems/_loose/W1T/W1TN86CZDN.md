If a natural number $N$ is appended to the right of every natural number (for example, appending 2 to 35 gives 352), and if the new number is always divisible by $N$, then $N$ is called a magic number. Among the natural numbers less than 130, how many magic numbers are there?
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1986)

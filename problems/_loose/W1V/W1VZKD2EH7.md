Define the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 0)$ :
$$
a_{n}=2^{n}+2^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]} \text {. }
$$

Prove: The sequence has infinitely many terms that can be expressed as the sum of several other terms (at least two) in the sequence, and it also has infinitely many terms that cannot be expressed as the sum of several other terms (at least two) in the sequence.

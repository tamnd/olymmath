Consider a circle with radius $r$, center $O$, and a fixed point $P$ inside it. $OP=a$. Take a point $M$ randomly inside the circle, and let $M'$ be the point symmetric to $M$ with respect to $OP$. What is the probability that the circle passing through the points $M, P, M'$ is completely inside the circle $O$ and includes the point $O$? For example, $a=0.5r$.

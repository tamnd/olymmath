The 63rd problem: Given positive integers $a$ and $M, a>1, M \geq 2020$. For a positive integer $x$, let $S_{a}(x)$ denote the sum of the digits of $x$ in base $a$, $F_{a}(x)$ denote the number of digits of $x$ in base $a$, and $f_{a}(x)$ denote the position of the first non-zero digit from right to left in the base $a$ representation of $x$. Find all positive integers $n$ such that there exist infinitely many positive integers $k$ satisfying:
$$
S_{a}(k n)=S_{a}(n) \text {, and. } F_{a}(k n)-f_{a}(k n)>M \text { . }
$$

For a journey of $25 \mathrm{~km}$, she spends 3 liters, so to travel $100 \mathrm{~km}$, Maria will spend $4 \times 3=12$ liters. Therefore, to travel $600 \mathrm{~km}$, the car will consume $6 \times 12=72$ liters. Since each liter costs 0.75 reais, then 72 liters will cost $0.75 \times 72=54$ reais.

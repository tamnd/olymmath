The lengths $a, b$ and $c$ of the edges of a wooden rectangular prism are natural numbers greater than 2, and it also holds that $a=b$. We paint the prism and then cut it into unit cubes. The number of unit cubes that have exactly two painted faces is 16 more than the number of unit cubes that have no painted faces. Determine the lengths of the edges of the prism.

Since neither Vasya nor Misha knew anything, they only guessed the answers. Vasya guessed the correct answers to 6 questions, and Misha to 8 questions, without consulting each other. Find the mathematical expectation of the number of coincidences, that is, the questions where both Vasya and Misha either guessed the correct answers or did not guess them.

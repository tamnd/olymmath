A bookshelf has $m$ levels, each containing $m$ different types of books, with $n$ copies of each type. Now all the books on the bookshelf are to be taken out for cleaning. The cleaning process requires that the categories are not mixed. Try to answer:
(1) How many ways are there for all $m$ types of books to not be on their original levels?
(2) How many ways are there for all $n$ books on each level to not be in their original positions?
(3) How many ways are there for all $m$ levels of books to not be on their original levels, and for all $n$ books on each level to not be in their original positions?

Upon reaching $B$, the car immediately turns back and heads for $A$ along the same route, and meets the motorcyclist $a \mathrm{~km}$ from $B$, and then meets the cyclist $b \mathrm{~km}$ from $B$. The motorcyclist also immediately turns back from $B$ towards $A$ and meets the cyclist $c \mathrm{~km}$ from $B$.

Calculate the distance between $A$ and $B$. (Assume that each movement is uniform.)

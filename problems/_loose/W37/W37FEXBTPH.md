(2) Express $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ in terms of $k$;
(3) Find the minimum value of $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ and the angle $\theta$ between $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ at this time $(0 \leqslant \theta \leqslant \pi)$.

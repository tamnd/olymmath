The sequence $\left\{a_{n} \mid n \in N\right\}$ is defined by the following formula: $a_{1}=1994$, $a_{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{2\left[a_{n}\right]+21}, n \in N$.
Here $\left[a_{n}\right]$ is the greatest integer not exceeding $a_{n}$.
(1) Prove that $a_{12}<1$.
(2) Prove that this sequence is convergent, and find its limit.
(3) Find the smallest positive integer $K$, such that $a_{k}<1$.

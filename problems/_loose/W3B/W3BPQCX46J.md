A $k_{1}$ circle has a diameter $AB$ whose endpoint $A$ is used to draw another circle $k_{2}$ passing through point $B$. At an arbitrary point $C$ on the segment $AB$, a perpendicular is erected to $AB$; one of its intersection points with $k_{1}$ is $D$, and with $k_{2}$ is $E$; the point of tangency of one of the tangents drawn from $E$ to $k_{1}$ is $F$. It is to be proven that $BD=EF$.

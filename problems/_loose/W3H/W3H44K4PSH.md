Given $I, O$ are the incenter and circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$, respectively. Circle $\Gamma_{A}$ passes through points $B, C$, and is tangent to the incircle of $\triangle ABC$. Similarly, define circles $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{C}$. Let circles $\Gamma_{B}$ and $\Gamma_{C}$ intersect at two distinct points $A, A'$. Similarly, define points $B'$ and $C'$. Prove that the lines $AA', BB', CC'$ intersect at a point, and the intersection point lies on the line $OI$. ${ }^{[1]}$
$(2012$, Romanian Master of Mathematics Olympiad)

Let the positive rational numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{2015}$ be such that

$$
\frac{1}{a_{1}+1}+\frac{2}{a_{2}+2}+\ldots+\frac{2015}{a_{2015}+2015}=2000
$$

Show that the sum $\frac{a_{1}}{a_{1}+1}+\frac{a_{2}}{a_{2}+2}+\ldots+\frac{a_{2015}}{a_{2015}+2015}$ is a number of the form $2^{n}-1, n \in \mathbb{N}$.

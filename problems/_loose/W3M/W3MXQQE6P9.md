$\underline{\text { Folkpor }}$ Consider all possible pairs $(a, b)$ of natural numbers, where $a<b$. Some pairs are declared black, and the rest are white.

Can this be done in such a way that for any natural numbers $a$ and $d$, among the pairs $(a, a+d)$, $(a, a+2d)$, $(a+d, a+2d)$, there are both black and white pairs?

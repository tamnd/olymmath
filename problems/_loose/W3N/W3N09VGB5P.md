11、(20 points) Let $e$ be the eccentricity of the conic section $\Gamma$, $F$ be one of its foci, $l$ be the axis of symmetry passing through the focus, $O$ be the vertex on $l$ closer to $F$, and $M$, $N$ be two points on $l$ satisfying $O F \cdot O M + O F \cdot O N = (1 - e) O M \cdot O N$. Prove that for any chord $A B$ (with $A$, $B$ as the endpoints) passing through point $M$ on the curve $\Gamma$, the line $l$ bisects one of the angles formed by $N A$ and $N B$.

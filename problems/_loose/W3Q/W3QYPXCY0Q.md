For given $n$ different numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n} \in \mathbf{N}, n>1$, let $P_{i}=\int_{\substack{j \neq i \\ 1 \leqslant j \leqslant n}}\left(a_{i}-a_{j}\right), i=1,2, \cdots, n$. Prove: for any $k \in \mathbf{N}, \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}^{k}}{p_{i}}$ is an integer.

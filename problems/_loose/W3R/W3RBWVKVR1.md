(a) Let $W X Y Z$ be a rectangle, and locate points $A, B, C, D$ on the sides $W X, X Y, Y Z, Z W$ such that

$$
\frac{W A}{A X}=\frac{X B}{B Y}=\frac{Y C}{C Z}=\frac{Z D}{D W}=1+\sqrt{2} .
$$

Show that $A B C D$ is a parallelogram with $A B / B C=A C / B D$.

(b) Show that, conversely, any parallelogram such that the ratio of two adjacent sides equals the ratio of the diagonals can be obtained from a rectangle in this way.

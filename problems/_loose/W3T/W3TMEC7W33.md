We have a tent in the shape of a cone, whose lateral surface is a circular sector with a radius of $3 \mathrm{~m}$ and a central angle of $120^{\circ}$.

a) Calculate the height of the tent. Provide an exact result.

b) The base of the tent is made of the same material as the lateral surface. One square meter of this material weighs $0.16 \mathrm{~kg}$. How much does the material for the base and the lateral surface of the tent weigh? Round the result to one decimal place.

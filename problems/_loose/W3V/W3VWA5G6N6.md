On a plane, $n>2$ different vectors $\vec{a}_{1}, \vec{a}_{2}, \ldots, \vec{a}_{n}$ of equal lengths are drawn. It turns out that all vectors $-\vec{a}_{1}+\vec{a}_{2}+\ldots+\vec{a}_{n}, \vec{a}_{1}-\vec{a}_{2}+\vec{a}_{3}+\ldots+\vec{a}_{n}$,

$$
\vec{a}_{1}+\vec{a}_{2}+\ldots+\vec{a}_{n-1}-\vec{a}_{n}
$$

also have equal lengths. Prove that $\vec{a}_{1}+\vec{a}_{2}+\ldots+\vec{a}_{n}=\overrightarrow{0}$.

(V. Senderov)

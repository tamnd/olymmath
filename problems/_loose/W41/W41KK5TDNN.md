Let $g$ be a given line. Furthermore, let two points $A$ and $B$ be given, neither of which lies on $g$, and whose connecting segment $A B$ intersects $g$ but is not perpendicular to $g$.

For a polygonal chain $A P Q B$, the following conditions (1), (2), (3) are required:

(1) The points $P$ and $Q$ lie on $g$.

(2) The line through $A$ and $P$ is parallel to the line through $B$ and $Q$.

(3) The segment $P Q$ has the length $t=6 \text{ cm}$.

a) Prove that every polygonal chain $A P Q B$ that satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3) can be obtained by a construction (from the given $g, A, B$ and the given length $t=6 \text{ cm}$)!

b) Describe such a construction!

c) Prove that every polygonal chain $A P Q B$ that can be constructed according to this description satisfies conditions (1), (2), (3)!

d) Choose a line $g$ and points $A, B$ as described above, and construct all those polygonal chains $A P Q B$ that satisfy conditions (1), (2), (3)! (A proof that all required polygonal chains have been constructed is not required.)

a) The first four terms of an infinite arithmetic sequence are:

$$
-\frac{3}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{5}{2}, \frac{9}{2}
$$

Calculate which number is the two hundredth term of this sequence and what is the sum of the first two hundred terms of the sequence.

b) Between the numbers $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{9}{2}$, insert three numbers so that we get a geometric sequence. Determine all possible values of the inserted terms. The values should be exact.

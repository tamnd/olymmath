Just as in the Roman numeral system, for example, $9=\mathrm{IX}=\mathrm{X}-\mathrm{I}$, meaning the I numeral is considered negative because it stands before a larger value numeral, in the Arabic numeral system, certain digits can also be assigned a negative sign. This is typically expressed by placing a line over the digit, for example, $19=20-1=2 \overline{1}$ (twenty minus one), $263=3 \overline{4} 3=3 \overline{3} \overline{7}=1 \overline{8} 7 \overline{7}$.

How many ways can 1969 be written using this method with at most 6 digits?

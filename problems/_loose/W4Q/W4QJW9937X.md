Let $M$, $N$, $P$ be points on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ of $\triangle ABC$, respectively, such that quadrilateral $CPMN$ is a parallelogram. $AN$ and $MP$ intersect at point $R$, $BP$ and $MN$ intersect at point $S$, $AN$ and $BP$ intersect at point $Q$. Let $T$ be the projection of point $Q$ onto side $AB$. Prove: $S_{\triangle NQP} = S_{\text{quadrilateral } TSQR}$.

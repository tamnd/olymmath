In quadrilateral $ABCD$, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at a point $O$ inside the quadrilateral. It is known that $AO=CO$, $DO=3BO$. Points $M$ and $N$ are taken on $AC$ and $CD$ respectively, such that $\frac{AM}{AC}=\frac{CN}{CD}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$. Then, $M, N, B$ are collinear.

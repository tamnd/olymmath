Draw in the complex plane the set of points $(x, y)$, associated with complex numbers $z=x+yi$, for which

$$
\operatorname{Re} z \cdot \operatorname{Im} z<0 \mathrm{and} |z|^{2} \leq \operatorname{Im}\left(z^{2}\right)+1
$$

Calculate the area of this set of points.

Determine for each triplet $(a, b, c)$ of positive real numbers $a, b, c$ all those triplets $(x, y, z)$ of real numbers that satisfy the following system of equations (1), (2), (3):

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2}-(y-z)^{2}=a \\
& y^{2}-(z-x)^{2}=b \\
& z^{2}-(x-y)^{2}=c
\end{aligned}
$$

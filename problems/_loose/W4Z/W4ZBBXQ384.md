Show that there exists a natural number $N$, such that for all integers $n \geqslant N$, there exist integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2020}$ satisfying the conditions:

$-\mathrm{n}=\mathrm{a}_{1}+\mathrm{a}_{2}+\ldots+\mathrm{a}_{2020}$

- $a_{2020}>a_{2019}>\ldots>a_{1} \geqslant 1$;
- For all $1 \leqslant i \leqslant 2019, a_{i}$ divides $a_{i+1}$.

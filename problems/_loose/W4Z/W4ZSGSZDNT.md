In a semicircle constructed over the diameter $A A^{\prime}=2 r$, the trapezoid $A M M^{\prime} A^{\prime}$ must be placed such that

$$
A M+M M^{\prime}+M^{\prime} A^{\prime}=2 l
$$

is satisfied. The distance $x=O P$ must be calculated, where $O$ is the center of the circle and $P$ is the projection of $M$ on the diameter $A A^{\prime}$. Discussion!

$\omega$ is a complex number such that $\omega^{2013} = 1$ and $\omega^m \neq 1$ for $m=1,2,\ldots,2012$ . Find the number of ordered pairs of integers $(a,b)$ with $1 \le a, b \le 2013$ such that $$ \frac{(1 + \omega + \cdots + \omega^a)(1 + \omega + \cdots + \omega^b)}{3} $$ is the root of some polynomial with integer coefficients and leading coefficient $1$ . (Such complex numbers are called *algebraic integers*.)

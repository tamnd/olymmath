Let $a_{1}=a_{2}=\frac{1}{3}$, and for $n=3,4,5, \cdots$, $a_{n}=\frac{\left(1-2 a_{n-2}\right) a_{n-1}^{2}}{2 a_{n-1}^{2}-4 a_{n-2}^{2} a_{n-1}^{2}+a_{n-2}}$.
(i) Find the general term formula for the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$;
(ii) Prove: $\frac{1}{a_{n}}-2$ is a perfect square of an integer.

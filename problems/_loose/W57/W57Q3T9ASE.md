Given $n(n \geqslant 2)$ real-coefficient quadratic functions $f_{i}(x)=x^{2}+a_{i} x+b_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$ whose discriminants $\Delta_{i}$ are all equal. If for any $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n$, the equation $f_{i}(x)+f_{j}(x)=0$ has two distinct real roots. Prove: The equation $\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_{i}(x)=0$ also has two distinct real roots.

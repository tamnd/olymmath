Let $=\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{i}\right\}$ be a family of subsets of set $X$, satisfying $\left|A_{i}\right| \geqslant 2$ and $|A_{i} \cap A_{j}| \neq 1 (i, j=1,2, \cdots, t, i \neq j)$. Prove that the elements of $X$ can be colored with two colors such that the elements of each $A_{i} (1 \leqslant i \leqslant t)$ are not all the same color.

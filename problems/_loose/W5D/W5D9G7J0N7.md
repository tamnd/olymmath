200 people are standing in a circle. Each of them is either a liar or a conformist. Liars always lie. A conformist who stands next to two conformists always tells the truth. A conformist who stands next to at least one liar can either tell the truth or lie. 100 of those standing said: "I am a liar," and the other 100 said: "I am a conformist." Find the maximum possible number of conformists among these 200 people. (R. Zhendarov, S. Berlov)

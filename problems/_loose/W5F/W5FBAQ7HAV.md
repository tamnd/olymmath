Prove that the following inequality holds for all real $a$ and natural $n$ : $$ |a| \cdot |a-1|\cdot |a-2|\cdot ...\cdot |a-n| \ge \frac{n!F(a)}{2n} $$ $F(a)$ is the distance from $a$ to the closest integer.

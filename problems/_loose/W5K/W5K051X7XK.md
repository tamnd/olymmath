An ant, initially placed at one vertex of the large square, is free to walk along the sides of the smaller squares. After walking for a while, having traversed each side of the smaller squares at least once, the ant returns to the starting point. What is the minimum distance, in meters, that the ant will have traveled?
(A) 14
(B) 16
(C) 15
(D) 12
(E) 18

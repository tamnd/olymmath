Renata constructed a trapezoid $P R S T$ with bases $P R$ and $S T$, in which the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied:

- trapezoid $P R S T$ is not a right trapezoid;
- triangle $T R P$ is equilateral;
- triangle $T R S$ is right-angled;
- one of the triangles $T R S, T R P$ has an area of $10 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$.

Determine the area of the other of these two triangles. Find all possible solutions.

(M. Petrová)

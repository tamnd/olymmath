The equation

$$
2^{n+1} \prod_{i=0}^{n} \cos 2^{i} \alpha=1
$$

holds with $n \in \mathbb{N}, \alpha \geq 0$, real. Determine the smallest value of $\alpha$ that satisfies this equation (dependent on $n$).

On a square plate, with a side length of $25 \mathrm{~cm}$, rectangular cookies, with a length of $4 \mathrm{~cm}$ and a width of $2 \mathrm{~cm}$, are placed without exceeding the edges of the plate. The cookies come in packages of 15 pieces each.

a) Show that no matter how we place the cookies from 5 packages, they cannot completely cover the plate.

b) Show that if the cookies from 16 packages are placed on the plate, there are at least 4 cookies that overlap (not necessarily completely).

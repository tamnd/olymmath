a) Let $A A^{\prime}$ and $B B^{\prime}$ be conjugate diameters of an ellipse with center $O$. Draw a perpendicular to the line $O A$ through the point $B$ and mark segments $B P$ and $B Q$ on it, equal to $O A$. Prove that the principal axes of the ellipse are the bisectors of the angles between the lines $O P$ and $O Q$.

b) On a plane, a pair of conjugate diameters of an ellipse is drawn. Using a compass and a straightedge, construct its axes.

A centenarian mathematician couple plans to replace the 12 numbers marking the clock points on a pair of lovers' watches with 24 distinct prime numbers: $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{12}$ and $y_{1}, y_{2}, \cdots$, $y_{12}$, such that the sum of each pair of adjacent primes at corresponding positions on the two clocks is equal to the same square number, i.e.,
$$
x_{k}+x_{k+1}=y_{k}+y_{k+1}=m_{k}^{2}(k=1,2, \cdots, 12) \text {, }
$$

with the convention that $x_{13}=x_{1}, y_{13}=y_{1}$. Please provide one solution for the numbers.

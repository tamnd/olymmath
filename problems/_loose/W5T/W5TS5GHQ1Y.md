At what time did he last wake up, knowing that at that moment he was in Korea and now he is in Italy (remember that the time difference between Italy and Korea is 7 hours ahead)?
(A) 12:04
(B) $12: 18$
(C) $12: 58$
(D) 13:04
(E) $13: 18$.

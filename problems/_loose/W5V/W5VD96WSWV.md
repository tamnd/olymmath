Given that the three medians $A A^{\prime}$, $B B^{\prime}$, and $C C^{\prime}$ of $\triangle A B C$ intersect the nine-point circle at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively, and points $L$, $M$, and $N$ on lines $B C$, $C A$, and $A B$ are the feet of the altitudes of $\triangle A B C$. The three tangents to the nine-point circle at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ intersect lines $M N$, $L N$, and $L M$ at points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ respectively. Prove that points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are collinear. ${ }^{[1]}$

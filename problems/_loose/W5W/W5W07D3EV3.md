The 41st question: Let $n \geq 3$ be a given positive odd number, and it is known that $2^{m} \equiv 1(\bmod n)$, and for all $1 \leq i \leq$ $m-1,2^{i} \equiv 1(\bmod n)$, if $2^{i} \equiv a_{i}(\bmod n)$, where $0 \leq a_{i} \leq n-1, i=1, 2, \cdots, m$, find the sum: $\mathrm{S}=\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{\mathrm{m}}(-1)^{\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{i}}} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{m}-\mathrm{i}}$.

Sully, the power generator specialist at Monster Power Company, has 7 fingers, and he counts in base 7; Mike has 9 fingers, and he counts in base 9. One day, the company's CEO Henry asked about their ages. Sully said: "My age in base 7 is a three-digit number $(\overline{a b c})_{7}$. " Mike said: "My age in base 9 is a three-digit number $(\overline{c b a})_{9}$." Henry said: "As far as I know, you two are the same age." So, their age in decimal is years. (The same letter represents the same digit, different letters represent different digits)

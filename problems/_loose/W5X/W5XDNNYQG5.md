Two lines are in reciprocal position with respect to a triangle if their intersection points with the sides are symmetric with respect to the midpoints of the sides. It is to be proven that if one line passes through the centroid and the center of a tangent circle, then the reciprocal line touches this tangent circle and the Feuerbach circle at their common point.

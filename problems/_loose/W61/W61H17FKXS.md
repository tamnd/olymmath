Prove that if $A B C$ is an equilateral triangle, $M$ is an arbitrary point in the plane, not lying on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$, then there exists a triangle with sides equal to $|M A|,|M B|$, and $|M C|$ (Pompeiu's theorem). Find the angle of this triangle opposite the side equal to $|M B|$, if $\angle A M C=\alpha$.

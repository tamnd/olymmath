Determine the number of all natural numbers $n$ that satisfy the following conditions (1), (2), (3):

(1) The number $n$ is divisible by 5.

(2) The number $n$ and its digit sum both contain no digit zero in their decimal representation.

(3) The digit sum of the digit sum of $n$ is 2.

Let points $A\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right), B\left(\frac{y_{0}^{2}}{p}-x_{0}, y_{0}\right)(p>0)$ be two fixed points on the plane, and point $P$ be a moving point on the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x$. The lines $P A, P B$ intersect the parabola at another point $C, D$ respectively.
Prove: The slope of line $C D$ is a constant.

There is a point $A$, a line $b$ passing through it, a line $f$ passing through it, the first image of line $f$ is $f^{\prime}$, an angle $\alpha$, and two distances, $r$ and $2 s$. Construct a triangle $ABC$ such that one of its vertices is $A$, the side $b$ lies on the line $b$, the angle bisector lies on the line $f$. One of the angles of the triangle is $\alpha$, the perimeter is $2 s$, and the radius of the circumscribed circle is $r$.

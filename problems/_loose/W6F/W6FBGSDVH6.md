Using one straight cut we partition a rectangular piece of paper into two pieces. We call this one "operation". Next, we cut one of the two pieces so obtained once again, to partition *this piece* into two smaller pieces (i.e. we perform the operation on any *one* of the pieces obtained). We continue this process, and so, after each operation we increase the number of pieces of paper by $1$ . What is the minimum number of operations needed to get $47$ pieces of $46$ -sided polygons? [obviously there will be other pieces too, but we will have at least $47$ (not necessarily *regular*) $46$ -gons.]

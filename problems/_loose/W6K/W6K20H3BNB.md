Given a triangle $A B C$. Let's define a point $A_{1}$ on the side $B C$ as follows: $A_{1}$ is the midpoint of the side $K L$ of a regular pentagon $M K L N P$, where vertices $K$ and $L$ lie on $B C$, and vertices $M$ and $N$ lie on $A B$ and $A C$, respectively. Similarly, points $C_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ are defined on the sides $A B$ and $A C$. Prove that the lines $A A_{1}, B B_{1}$ and $C C_{1}$ intersect at one point.

Father, mother, daughter, and son in a family notice:

(1) The product of the day and month of the birthday is 242 for the father, 200 for the mother, and 6 for the daughter.

(Example of such a product: A birthday on July 30 results in $30 \cdot 7 = 210$.)

(2) The sum of the day and month of the birthday equals the age in years for each of the four family members.

(3) The sum of these four age statements is 80.

(4) The product of these four age statements is 59400.

(a) How old are the family members? When do the father, mother, and daughter have their birthdays? Derive the answers to these questions based on the observations (1), (2), (3), (4)! Also, investigate whether there are multiple possibilities for some of the requested information!

(b) Show that the problem (a) can also be solved - with the same result - if one of the observations (1), (2), (3), (4) is omitted!

a) Let $A B C D$ be a square with side length $a=12 \text{ cm}$.

We are looking for three points $F, Q, R$ that lie on the boundary of this square such that the segments $A P, A Q, A R$ divide the square into four equal areas.

Give such points $P, Q, R$ and show that they have the required property!

b) Determine two points $S, T$ on the boundary of the square $A B C D$ such that the segments $A S, A T$ divide this square into three equal areas!

c) Investigate the possibility of a corresponding division of a square (with side length $a$) into $n$ equal areas!

Consider $M$.

Given an isosceles triangle $ABC$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the arc $BAC$ of its circumcircle, and $M$ be the midpoint of side $BC$. Denote by $I_{1}$ and $I_{2}$ the centers of the inscribed circles of triangles $ABM$ and $ACM$ respectively. Prove that the points $I_{1}, I_{2}, A, N$ lie on the same circle.

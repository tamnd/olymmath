MO Space City consists of 99 space stations, with tubular passages connecting any two stations. It is stipulated that 99 of these passages are bidirectional main roads, while the rest are strictly one-way passages. If any four space stations can reach any other station among them through the passages, then the set of these four stations is called a fully connected quartet.

Try to design a scheme for MO Space City to maximize the number of fully connected quartets (please calculate the exact maximum number and prove your conclusion).
(1999 CMO Problem)

In the parallelepiped $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$, the diagonal $A C_{1}$ is equal to $d$. Prove that there exists a triangle, the lengths of whose sides are equal to the distances from the vertices $A_{1}, B$ and $D$ to this diagonal, and that the volume of the parallelepiped is $2 d S$, where $S$ is the area of this triangle.

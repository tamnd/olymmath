Given an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$, point $O$ is its circumcenter, and line $A O$ intersects side $B C$ at point $D$. Moving points $E, F$ are located on sides $A B, A C$ respectively, such that points $A, E, D, F$ are concyclic. Prove that the length of the projection of segment $E F$ on side $B C$ is a constant.

Let $n \geq 1$: in the plane, we place $2n$ points, no three of which are collinear. We color $n$ of them blue and $n$ red. Show that it is possible to draw $n$ segments that do not intersect, each segment connecting a blue point to a red point, in such a way that each point is used exactly once.

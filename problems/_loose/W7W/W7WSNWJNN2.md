Let $\Gamma$ and $\Gamma^{\prime}$ be two circles intersecting at $P$ and $Q$. Let $A$ be a point on $\Gamma$. The tangent to $\Gamma$ at $P$ and the line $(A Q)$ intersect $\Gamma^{\prime}$ at $P^{\prime}$ and $A^{\prime}$ respectively. Show that the lines $(A P)$ and $\left(A^{\prime} P^{\prime}\right)$ are parallel.

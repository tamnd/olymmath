Divide a regular $n$-gon $(n \geqslant 5)$ into the smallest number of:

a) acute triangles,

b) obtuse triangles.

Each side of any triangle in the partition must simultaneously be a side of another triangle in the partition or a side of the polygon.

It is important that no vertex of one triangle divides the side of another triangle. Such a partition is called a usual triangulation.

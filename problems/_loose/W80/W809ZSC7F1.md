Given a triangle $ABC$ and two lines $p$ and $q$ which are not parallel to each other and are not perpendicular to any side of the triangle. Let the normals from points $A, B, C$ to line $p$ be denoted by $p_{A}$, $p_{B}, p_{C}$, respectively, and the normals to line $q$ by $q_{A}, q_{B}, q_{C}$, respectively. Let the intersection points of the lines $p_{A}, q_{A}, p_{B}, q_{B}, p_{C}, q_{C}$ with the lines $q_{B}, p_{B}, q_{C}, p_{C}, q_{A}, p_{A}$ be $K, L, P, Q, N, M$, respectively. Prove that the lines $K L, M N, P Q$ intersect at one point.

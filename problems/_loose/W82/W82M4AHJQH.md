If we draw two quadrilaterals in a circle, one of which is a right-angled quadrilateral and its diagonals are perpendicular to the diagonals of the other, then the 16 intersection points of the sides of the two quadrilaterals lie on four lines.

If from the intersection point of any two of these four lines we draw tangents to the circle, then the chord of contact (the polar of the intersection point) passes through the intersection point of the other two lines.

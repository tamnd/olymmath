Let $n$ be a positive integer. Alexandre fills each cell of an $n \times n$ grid with an integer between $1$ and $n$ inclusive such that no two cells which contain the same number share a row or column. He places a token on the top left cell. He may move the token between two orthogonally adjacent cells if and only if the numbers on the cells differ by $1$ . Find all $n$ for which is it possible for Alexandre to fill the grid in a way such that the token can be moved to any cell of the grid.

*Benny Wang*

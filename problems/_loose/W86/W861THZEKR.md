In the acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AC > AB$, the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle ABC$ is tangent to the line at point $A$ and intersects the extension of line segment $CB$ at point $P$. $X$ is a point on line segment $OP$ such that $\angle AXP = 90^{\circ}$. $E$ and $F$ are points on sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively (on the same side of $OP$), such that
$$
\angle EXP = \angle ACX, \angle FXO = \angle ABX.
$$

If the line $EF$ intersects $\odot O$ at points $K$ and $L$, prove that $OP$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle K LX$.

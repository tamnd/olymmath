A plane intersects the edges $P A, P B, P C$ of tetrahedron $P A B C$ at $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, respectively, and intersects the extensions of the sides $A B, A C, B C$ of the section $A B C$ at $L, N, M$, respectively. Prove that: $\frac{S_{\triangle A N L}}{S_{\triangle L M B}} \cdot \frac{S_{\triangle B M B}}{S_{\triangle B^{\prime} C^{\prime}}} \cdot \frac{S_{\triangle P C A^{\prime}}}{S_{\triangle A^{\prime} N A}}=1$.

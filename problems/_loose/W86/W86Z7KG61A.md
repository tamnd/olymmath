A point on the Cartesian plane with both coordinates being rational numbers is called a rational point. For any positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, can all rational points be colored with $\mathrm{n}$ colors, each point being one color, such that on any line segment with both endpoints being rational points, rational points of each color appear?

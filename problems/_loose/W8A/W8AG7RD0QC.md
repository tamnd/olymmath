At a fair, there is a very inviting game, as it can be played for free; the winner gets a prize. The agreed prize for the first four games is one coin, for the fifth it is two coins. Nicola has a probability of $\frac{2}{3}$ of winning the prize in each game and decides to play 5 games. What is the probability that Nicola wins at least 4 coins?
(A) $5\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5}$
(B) $4\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5}$
(C) $3\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5}$
(D) $2\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5}$
(E) $\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5}$.

(1) $a, b, c$ are distinct;
(2) The algebraic expression $\frac{a^{1993}-b^{1993}}{a-b}+\frac{b^{1993}-c^{1993}}{b-c}+\frac{c^{1993}-a^{1993}}{c-a}$ is an integer.

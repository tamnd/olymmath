Given $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}$, and the equation $a x^{2}+b x+c=0$ has two real roots. If
$$
|a b-a c|>\left|b^{2}-a c\right|+\left|a b-c^{2}\right|,
$$

find the number of roots of the equation $a x^{2}+b x+c=0$ in the interval $(0,2)$.

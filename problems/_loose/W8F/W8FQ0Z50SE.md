Consider $K$ and $L$ as two points on a circle $\Gamma$ with center $O$. Let $A$ be a point on the line $(KL)$ outside the circle. Denote $P$ and $Q$ as the points of tangency of the tangents to $\Gamma$ from $A$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $[PQ]$.

Show that the angles $\widehat{M K O}$ and $\widehat{M L O}$ are equal.

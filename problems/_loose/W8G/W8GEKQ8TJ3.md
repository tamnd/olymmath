Let $a, b, c, d$ be complex numbers, and $a c \neq 0$. Prove:
$$
\frac{\max \{|a c|,|a d+b c|,|b d|\}}{\max \{|a|,|b|\} \cdot \max \{|c|,|d|\}} \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} .
$$

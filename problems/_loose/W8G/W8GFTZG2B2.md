Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfies
$$
a_{n}=n p^{n}\left(n \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}, 0<p<1\right) \text {. }
$$

For the statements:
(1) When $p=\frac{1}{2}$, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a decreasing sequence;
(2) When $\frac{1}{2}<p<1$, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ does not necessarily have a maximum term; $\square$
(3) When $0<p<\frac{1}{2}$, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a decreasing sequence;
(4) When $\frac{p}{1-p}$ is a positive integer, the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ must have two equal maximum terms, the correct ones are ( )
(A)(1)(2)
(B) (3)(4)
(C)(2)(4)
(D)(2)(3)

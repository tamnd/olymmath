Several weights are arranged in a circle, not all of them are of the same weight, and the weights are not necessarily integers. In each triplet of consecutive weights, the weight of one of them is equal to the arithmetic mean of the weights of the weights in the triplet. Prove that the number of weights is divisible by 3.

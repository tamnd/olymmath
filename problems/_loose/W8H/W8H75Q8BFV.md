Perpendiculars are erected from points $K, E, H$, lying on the sides $AB, BC, CA$ of a given triangle $ABC$, respectively, to these sides. Prove that these three perpendiculars intersect at one point if and only if the condition

$$
A K^{2}+B E^{2}+C H^{2}=K B^{2}+E C^{2}+H A^{2}
$$

is satisfied.

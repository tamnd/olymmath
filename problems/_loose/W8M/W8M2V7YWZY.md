Let $a$ be the side length of an equilateral triangle $ABC$ and $P$ be a point inside the triangle such that the distances from $P$ to the vertices are $PA=u$, $PB=v$, and $PC=w$. If $u^{2}+v^{2}=w^{2}$. Prove that: $w^{2}+\sqrt{3} u v=a^{2}$.

For a random variable $\xi$, having a $\mathscr{N}(0,1)$-distribution,

$$
\mathrm{P}(\xi \geqslant x) \sim \frac{\varphi(x)}{x}, x \rightarrow \infty, \text { where } \varphi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2 \pi}} e^{-\frac{x^{2}}{2}} .
$$

Find the corresponding asymptotic for a random variable $\zeta$, having a gamma-distribution with density

$$
f(x)=\frac{I(x>0)}{\Gamma(\alpha)} \cdot x^{\alpha-1} e^{-x}, \alpha>0
$$

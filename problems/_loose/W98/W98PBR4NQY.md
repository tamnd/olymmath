Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}}$ be a sequence of real numbers with the property

$$
2\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)=n a_{n+1}
$$

for any $n \geq 1$.

a) Prove that the sequence $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}}$ is an arithmetic progression.

b) If $\left[a_{1}\right]+\left[a_{2}\right]+\ldots+\left[a_{n}\right]=\left[a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}\right]$, for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, prove that all terms of the sequence are integers. (with $[x]$ denoting the integer part of the real number $x$)

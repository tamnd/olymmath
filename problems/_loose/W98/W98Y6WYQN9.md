On the table, there are three empty jars that used to contain honey. Winnie-the-Pooh, Rabbit, and Piglet take turns placing one nut into one of the jars. Their turn order is determined by drawing lots before the game begins. Winnie can only add a nut to the first or second jar, Rabbit - only to the second or third, and Piglet - to the first or third.

The one whose move results in exactly 1999 nuts in any jar loses.

Prove that Winnie-the-Pooh and Piglet can, by agreeing, play in such a way that Rabbit loses.

We will denote by $M(a, b)$ the least common multiple of two numbers $a$ and $b$, and $D(a, b)$ the greatest common divisor of two numbers $a$ and $b$.

Prove the formula

$$
M(a, b) \cdot D(a, b)=a b
$$

For three numbers, prove the formula

$$
\frac{M(a, b, c) \cdot D(a, b) \cdot D(b, c) \cdot D(c, a)}{D(a, b, c)}=a b c
$$

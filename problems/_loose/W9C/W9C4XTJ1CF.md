In the triangular pyramid $S A B C$, the two equal lateral faces $A S B$ and $C S B$ are perpendicular to the plane of the base, and the face $A S C$ is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\beta$. Find the radius of the sphere circumscribed around the pyramid if the radius of the circle circumscribed around the base is $r$ and $\angle A B C=\alpha$.

Given is a triangle $A B C$. On the sides $\overline{A C}$ and $\overline{B C}$ of triangle $\triangle A B C$, points $M$ and $N$ are marked such that: $|A M|:|M C|=1: m$ and $|B N|:|N C|=1: n$. Determine the natural numbers $m$ and $n$ for which $P_{\triangle A B C}: P_{\triangle M N C}=2: 1$.

\section*{Task 2 - 071042}

For a body in the shape of a pyramid with a square base and congruent side faces, a cuboid container of the smallest possible volume is to be made. The pyramidal body is to be placed in such a way that it touches one of the inner faces of the container either with its base or with one of its side faces. Let $h$ be the height of the pyramidal body and $a$ the side length of its base.

Investigate which of these two positions requires a container with a smaller volume! It is useful to distinguish the cases $h < \frac{a}{2}$ and $h \geq \frac{a}{2}$.

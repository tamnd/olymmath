Prove that if a topological dendrite is homeomorphic to a space $|T|$ (where $T$ is a tree), but does not have this form itself, then it contains an end such that the homeomorphism maps it to a point of $T$ (i.e., not to an end). Can you find a topological dendrite for which this is impossible?

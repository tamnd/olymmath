Given that there are $24$ primes between $3$ and $100$ , inclusive, what is the number of ordered pairs $(p, a)$ with $p$ prime, $3 \le p < 100$ , and $1 \le a < p$ such that the sum
$$a+a^2+a^3+\cdots+a^{(p-2)!} $$is not divisible by $p$ ?

Given the rectangle $A B C D$. If $k_{1}\left(O_{1}, R_{1}\right)$ is the circle passing through $A$ and $D$ and tangent to $B C$, and $k_{2}\left(O_{2}, R_{2}\right)$ is the circle passing through $A$ and $B$ and tangent to $C D$, then

$$
R_{1}+R_{2} \geq \frac{5}{8}(a+b)
$$

where $a=\overline{A B}, b=\overline{A D}$. Prove!

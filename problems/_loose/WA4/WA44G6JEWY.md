In a school, there are $2 n$ courses, and all students' grades are either excellent or good. No two students have identical grades, and it cannot be said that one student is better than another (a student is considered better if they are not worse in any course and better in at least one course compared to another student). Prove that the number of students in the school does not exceed $C_{2 n}^{n}$.

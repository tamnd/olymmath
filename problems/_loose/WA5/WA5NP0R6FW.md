Prove that any quadratic expression $$ Q(x) = Ax^2 + Bx + C $$ (a) can be put into the form $$ Q(x) = k \frac{x(x- 1)}{1 \cdot 2} + \ell x + m $$ where $k, \ell, m$ depend on the coefficients $A,B,C$ and

(b) $Q(x)$ takes on integral values for every integer $x$ if and only if $k, \ell, m$ are integers.

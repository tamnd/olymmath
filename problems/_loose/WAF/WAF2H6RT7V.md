Students of a tenth grade in one of the secondary schools in Kirov were asked which newspapers they read regularly. It turned out that $60 \%$ of the students read the newspaper "Komsomolskaya Pravda", $50 \%$ - "Komsomolskoe Plemya", $50 \%$ - "Kirovskaya Pravda", $30 \%$ - "Komsomolskaya Pravda" and "Komsomolskoe Plemya", $20 \%$ - "Komsomolskoe Plemya" and "Kirovskaya Pravda", $30 \%$ - "Komsomolskaya Pravda" and "Kirovskaya Pravda" and $10 \%$ all three newspapers. What percentage of students in this class read only one newspaper regularly? Only two newspapers? Do not read newspapers regularly?

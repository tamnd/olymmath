Let $x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers satisfying the following system of equations:
$$
\begin{cases}
\sqrt{x}\left(2 + \dfrac{5}{x+y}\right) = 3
\sqrt{y}\left(2 - \dfrac{5}{x+y}\right) = 2
\end{cases}
$$
Find the maximum value of $x + y$.

The following proposition is an analogy from the properties of an equilateral triangle to a general quadrilateral: In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the two pairs of opposite sides are not parallel. The line segment $P_{1} P_{2}$ is located inside the quadrilateral. If the sum of the distances from points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ to the four sides are both equal to $m$, then the sum of the distances from any point on $P_{1} P_{2}$ to the four sides is also equal to $m$. Please review this analogy process and prove this proposition.

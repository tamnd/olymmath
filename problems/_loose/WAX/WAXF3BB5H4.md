Let $\psi$ be a permutation of the set $\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n}\right\}$ where $a_{1}, a_{2}$, $a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n}$ are given positive real numbers. Prove that

$$
\frac{a_{1}}{\psi\left(a_{1}\right)}+\frac{a_{2}}{\psi\left(a_{2}\right)}+\ldots .+\frac{a_{n}}{\psi\left(a_{n}\right)} \geq n
$$

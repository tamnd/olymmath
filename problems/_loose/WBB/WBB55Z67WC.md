Given $\triangle A B C$ with altitudes $A D, B E, C F$, and orthocenter $H$. Point $Q$ is on the circumcircle and $Q R \perp B C$ at point $R$. A line through $R$ parallel to $A Q$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle D E F$ at points $T$ and $S$. $A M \perp R S$ and $H N \perp R S$. Prove: The areas of $\triangle A M S$ and $\triangle H N S$ are equal.

Six mathematicians went fishing. Together they caught 100 fish, and each caught a different number. After fishing, they noticed that any of them could distribute all their fish to the other five fishermen so that the remaining five would have an equal number of fish. Prove that one fisherman can go home with his catch and still, each of the remaining can distribute all their fish to the others so that they end up with an equal number.

Pursuit (II). Ship $O_{1}$ notices another ship $O_{2}$, which at the moment of observation is moving perpendicular to the line $O_{1} O_{2}$. Ship $O_{2}$ does not notice the signals from $O_{1}$ and maintains its original course and speed $v_{2}$. Ship $O_{1}$ wants to attract attention, as it needs help, and with its maximum speed $v_{1}$, which it is capable of, it heads in such a direction to get as close as possible to $O_{2}$. What course should it take? What will be the distance between the two ships at the moment of closest approach, if the initial distance was $d$, and the ratio of speeds $v_{1} / v_{2}$ is $k$ and less than 1?

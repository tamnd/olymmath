A set of distinct positive integers is called *singular* if, for each of its elements, after crossing out that element, the remaining ones can be grouped into two sets with no common elements such that the sum of the elements in the two groups is the same. Find the smallest positive integer $n>1$ such that there exists a singular set $A$ with $n$ items.

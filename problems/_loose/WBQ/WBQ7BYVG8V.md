Prove that $\left(1 + 2\cdot\frac {PA}{PA_{1}}\right)\left(1 + 2\cdot\frac {PB}{PB_{1}}\right)\left(1 + 2\cdot\frac {PC}{PC_{1}}\right)\geq 8$ .

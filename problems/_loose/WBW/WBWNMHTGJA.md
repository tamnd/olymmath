A square sheet of grid paper measuring $100 \times 100$ cells is given. Several non-intersecting broken lines, following the sides of the cells and having no common points, have been drawn. These broken lines are strictly inside the square, and their ends necessarily exit to the boundary. Prove that, besides the vertices of the square, there is still a node (inside the square or on the boundary) that does not belong to any broken line.

On the face $L N M$, a point $P$ is taken at a distance of 2 from the vertex $N$ and at a distance of 1 from the edge $M N$. A light ray is directed from some point $S$, located inside the trihedral angle $N K L M$, to the point $P$. The ray forms an angle $\pi / 4$ with the plane $M N K$ and equal angles with the edges $K N$ and $M N$. The ray is mirror-reflected from the faces of the angle $N K L M$ first at point $P$, then at point $Q$, and then at point $R$. Find the sum of the lengths of segments $P Q$ and $Q R$.

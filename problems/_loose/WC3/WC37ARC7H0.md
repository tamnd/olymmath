In the triangle $ABC$, the orthocenter is $M$ and the feet of the altitudes are $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$; show that $\left(1^{\circ}\right)$

$$
A M+B M+C M=2(R+r)
$$

and

$\left(2^{\circ}\right)$

$$
\frac{A M \cdot C M}{B_{1} M}=\frac{B M \cdot C M}{A_{1} M}=\frac{A M \cdot B M}{C_{1} M}=\text { const. }
$$

if the vertex $A$ moves on the circumference of the circumcircle of the triangle.

of $P$ is less than or equal to 1. Draw the domain of the points $P$ satisfying the condition (i) or (ii) as below, then find the area.

(i) There exists a parabola having the absolute value of the $x$-coordinate of the vertex which is more than or equal to 1 and passes through all points of $A,\ P,\ B$.

(ii) The points $A,\ P,\ B$ are collinear.

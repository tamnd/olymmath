A first-grader made two triangles from six sticks. Then he disassembled the triangles and divided the six sticks into two groups of three sticks: in the first group, there were the three longest sticks, and in the second group, the three shortest sticks. Is it necessarily possible to form a triangle from the three sticks in the first group? And from the three sticks in the second group?

(L. Emelyanov)

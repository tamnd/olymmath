Contours of convex polygons $F$ and $G$ have no common points, and $G$ is located inside $F$. A chord of polygon $F$ - a segment connecting two points of the contour $F$, is called a supporting chord for $G$ if it intersects $G$ only at contour points: it contains either only a vertex or a side of $G$.

a) Prove that there exists a supporting chord whose midpoint belongs to the contour of $G$.

b) Prove that there exist two such chords.

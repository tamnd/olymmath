In Euclidean space, take the point $ N(0,\ 0,\ 1)$ on the sphere $ S$ with radius 1 centered in the origin. For moving points $ P,\ Q$ on $ S$ such that $ NP = NQ$ and $ \angle{PNQ} = \theta \left(0 < \theta < \frac {\pi}{2}\right),$ consider the solid figure $ T$ in which the line segment $ PQ$ can be passed.

(1) Show that $ z$ coordinates of $ P,\ Q$ are equal.

(2) When $ P$ is on the palne $ z = h,$ express the length of $ PQ$ in terms of $ \theta$ and $ h$.

(3) Draw the outline of the cross section by cutting $ T$ by the plane $ z = h$, then express the area in terms of $ \theta$ and $ h$.

(4) Pay attention to the range for which $ h$ can be valued, express the volume $ V$ of $ T$ in terms of $ \theta$, then find the maximum $ V$ when let $ \theta$ vary.

(A.Zaslavsky) Given two triangles $ ABC$ , $ A'B'C'$ . Denote by $ \alpha$ the angle between the altitude and the median from vertex $ A$ of triangle $ ABC$ . Angles $ \beta$ , $ \gamma$ , $ \alpha'$ , $ \beta'$ , $ \gamma'$ are defined similarly. It is known that $ \alpha = \alpha'$ , $ \beta = \beta'$ , $ \gamma = \gamma'$ . Can we conclude that the triangles are similar?

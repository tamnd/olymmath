A pawn is initially on the central square of a $5 \times 5$ chessboard. A move of the pawn consists of moving to a square chosen at random from those that share exactly one vertex with the square it is on. What is the probability that after 12 moves the pawn is on any one of the corners of the chessboard?
(A) $\frac{1}{3}$
(B) $\frac{4}{25}$
(C) $\frac{1}{6}$
(D) $\frac{4}{13}$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}$

Consider all pyramids $A B C S$ that satisfy the following conditions (1), (2), (3):

(1) The base $A B C$ of the pyramid has an area of 1.

(2) It holds that $A B=A C=S B=S C$.

(3) It holds that $B C=S A$.

Investigate whether among all pyramids that satisfy these conditions, there is one with the largest volume. If this is the case, determine the size of the angle $\angle B A C$ for such a pyramid.

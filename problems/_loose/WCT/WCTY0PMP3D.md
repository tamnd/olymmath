Let $a, b$ be constants, $M=\{f(x) \mid f(x)=a \cos x+b \sin x\} ; F$ : maps any point $(a, b)$ in the plane to the function $a \cos x+b \sin x$.
(1) Prove: There do not exist two different points corresponding to the same function;
(2) Prove: When $f_{0}(x) \in M$, $f_{1}(x)=f_{0}(x+t) \in M$, where $t$ is a constant;
(3) For a fixed value $f_{0}(x) \in M$, let $M_{1}=\left\{f_{0}(x+t), t \in \mathbf{R}\right\}$, find the preimage of $M_{1}$ under the mapping $F$, and describe what kind of graph it is.

Given a triangle $ABC$ and an arbitrary point $M$ inside it. Let the lines $MA, MB, MC$ intersect the sides $BC, AC$, and $AB$ respectively, at points $P, Q, R$. Prove that among the ratios $\overline{AM}: \overline{MP}, \overline{BM}: \overline{MQ}$, and $\overline{CM}: \overline{MR}$, there is at least one not greater than 2 and at least one not less than 2.

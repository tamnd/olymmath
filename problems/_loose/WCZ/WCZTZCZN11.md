## Problem 2

At a thermal power station, the gas reserve always remains positive and changes monthly as follows. If the reserve in the current month is $x$ m$^3$, then in the next month it will be $6-x$ m$^3$. Can the gas reserve in some month be exactly the square of the reserve in another month? If this is possible, at what value of the reserve and in which months?

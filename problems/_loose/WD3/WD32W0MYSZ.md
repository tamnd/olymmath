Given 11 weights of different masses (all unique), each weighing an integer number of grams. It is known that no matter how the weights (all or some) are distributed on two scales, as long as the number of weights on each side is not equal, the side with more weights will always be heavier. Prove that at least one of the weights weighs more than 35 grams.

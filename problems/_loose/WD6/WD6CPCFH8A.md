Ketten play the following game: starting from the bottom left corner of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, they take turns moving a piece. The piece can move to the square to its right, the square above it, or the square diagonally to the right and above (if any). The player who moves the piece to the top right corner wins.

In a proper game, does the starting player or their partner win? How should the winning player play?

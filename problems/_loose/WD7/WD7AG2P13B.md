In isosceles $\triangle A B C$, the base $B C$ is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the perimeter. A line parallel to the two legs is drawn from any point $P$ on the base, intersecting the two legs at $E, F$. Then the ratio of the perimeter of quadrilateral $A E P F$ to the perimeter of $\triangle A B C$ is
(A) equal to $\frac{1}{4}$.
(B) equal to $\frac{2}{3}$.
(C) equal to $\frac{3}{4}$.
(D) cannot be determined.
(China Jiangxi Province Nanchang City Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

Let $K\subseteq \mathbb C$ be a field with the operations from $\mathbb C$ s.t.
i) K has exactly two endomorphisms, namely f and g
ii) if f(x)=g(x) then $x\in\mathbb Q$ .
Prove that there exists an integer $d\neq 1$ free from squares so that $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ .

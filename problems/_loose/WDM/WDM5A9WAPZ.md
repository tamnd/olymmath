In the right-angled triangle $A B C$, the side $A B$ is divided into equal parts by the points $C_{0}=A, C_{1}, C_{2}, \ldots, C_{n-1}, C_{n}=B$. Show that:

$$
C C_{0}^{2}+C C_{1}^{2}+C C_{2}^{2}+\ldots+C C_{n-1}^{2}+C C_{n}^{2}=\frac{(n+1)(2 n+1)}{6 n} A B^{2}
$$

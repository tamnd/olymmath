(Mathematics Bulletin, Issue 10, 2003) Let $m \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, x, y, z \in \mathbf{R}_{+}$, and $x y z=1$. Prove that:
$$
\frac{x^{m}}{(1+y)(1+z)}+\frac{y^{m}}{(1+x)(1+z)}+\frac{z^{m}}{(1+x)(1+y)} \geqslant \frac{3}{4}
$$

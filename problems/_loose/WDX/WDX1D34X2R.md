Let $g$ be the multiplicative function given by $$g(p^{\alpha}) = \alpha p^{\alpha-1},$$ for all $\alpha\in\mathbb Z^+$ and primes $p$. Prove that there exist infinitely many integers $n$ such that $$g(n+1) = g(n) + g(1).$$

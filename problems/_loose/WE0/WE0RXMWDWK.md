Find the conditions that the numbers $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$ must satisfy for the system of 6 equations with 4 unknowns

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x_{1}+x_{2}=\alpha_{1} \alpha_{2} \\
x_{1}+x_{3}=\alpha_{1} \alpha_{3} \\
x_{1}+x_{4}=\alpha_{1} \alpha_{4} \\
x_{2}+x_{3}=\alpha_{2} \alpha_{3} \\
x_{2}+x_{4}=\alpha_{2} \alpha_{4} \\
x_{3}+x_{4}=\alpha_{3} \alpha_{4}
\end{array}\right.
$$

to have solutions. Find the values of the unknowns $x_{1}, x_{2}$, $x_{3}, x_{4}$.

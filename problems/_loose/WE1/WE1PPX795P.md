Prove that for natural numbers $p$ and $q$ , there exists a natural number $x$ such that $$ (\sqrt{p}+\sqrt{p-1})^q=\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x-1} $$ (As an example, if $p = 3, q = 2$ , then $x$ can be taken to be $25$ .)

Let's agree to denote by $K$ the expression standing on the left side of the equation of a circle of the form

$$
(x-a)^{2}+(y-b)^{2}-R^{2}=0
$$

Prove that the family of equations of the form $K_{1}+t K_{2}=0$, where $t$ is a variable parameter, defines a so-called pencil of circles - a family of circles having a common radical axis. Write the equation of the pencil in explicit form (in coordinates).

Prove that all circles of the pencil either intersect at two common points (elliptic pencil), or touch each other at one common point (parabolic pencil), or have no common points (hyperbolic pencil). Sketch the pencils of circles of each type.

48***. Using a compass and a straightedge, construct a circle passing through two given points and tangent to a given circle.

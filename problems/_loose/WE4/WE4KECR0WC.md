Before decorating it, Mom weighed the cake on digital scales that round the weight to the nearest ten grams (if the weight ends in 5, the scales round it down). The result was 1440 g. When Mom decorated the cake with identical candles, the number of which was equal to Anya's age, the scales showed 1610 g. It is known that the weight of each candle is an integer number of grams, and if you put one candle on the scales, they will show 40 g. How old can Anya be? List all possible answers and explain why there are no others. (20 points)

Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral such that the rays $[AB)$ and $[DC)$ intersect at $E$, and $[BC)$ and $[AD)$ intersect at $F$. We denote by $X_{1}, X_{2}, X_{3}$, and $X_{4}$ the points of tangency of the incircle of $EBC$, the incircle of $FCD$, the $E$-excircle of $EAD$, and the $F$-excircle of $FAB$ on $(BC)$, $(CD)$, $(DA)$, and $(AB)$ respectively. Show that the points $X_{1}, X_{3}$, and $E$ are collinear if and only if the points $X_{2}, X_{4}$, and $F$ are collinear.

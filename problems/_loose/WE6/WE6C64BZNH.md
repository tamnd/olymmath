Given quadratic trinomials $f_{1}(x)=x^{2}-2 a x+3, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}+x+b, f_{3}(x)=3 x^{2}+(1-4 a) x+6+b$ and $f_{4}(x)=3 x^{2}+(2-2 a) x+3+2 b$. Let the differences of their roots be $A, B, C$ and $D$ respectively. It is known that $|A| \neq|B|$. Find the ratio $\frac{C^{2}-D^{2}}{A^{2}-B^{2}}$. The values of $A, B, C, D, a, b$ are not given.

On Angélica's fruit stand, there are 12 bananas, 1 pineapple, 4 oranges, 2 mangoes, and 3 papayas. The weight of 1 pineapple is the same as the weight of 1 orange, 1 mango, and 1 papaya, combined; the weight of 1 banana is half the weight of 1 papaya; 4 bananas weigh the same as 1 orange and 1 mango, combined; and 1 mango weighs $100 \mathrm{~g}$ more than 1 orange. If 1 pineapple weighs $600 \mathrm{~g}$, then:

a) How much do all the fruits on Angélica's fruit stand weigh?

b) In how many ways can Pedro, Angélica's grandson, choose 2 different fruits for his breakfast, using the fruits from the fruit stand?

c) How many smoothies can be made with these fruits, using $600 \mathrm{~g}$ of fruits? (It is allowed to use repeated fruits, but only whole quantities of fruit).

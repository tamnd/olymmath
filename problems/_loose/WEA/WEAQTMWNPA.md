Let $\tau$ be a collection of subtrees of a tree $T$, and $k \in \mathbb{N}$.
(i) Prove: If the trees in $\mathcal{T}$ have the property that any two of them have a non-empty intersection, then their entire intersection $\cap \mathcal{T}$ is non-empty.
(ii) Prove: Either $\mathcal{T}$ contains $k$ disjoint trees, or $T$ contains a set of at most $k-1$ vertices that intersects with each tree in $\tau$.

Let $k$ be a positive even number, prove that the natural numbers $\{1,2, \cdots, k-1\}$ can be arranged in a row $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{k-1}\right)$, such that the sum of any consecutive terms cannot be divisible by $k$.

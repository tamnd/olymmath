When $x=-1, x=0, x=1, x=2$, the polynomial
$$
P(x)=a x^{3}+b x^{2}+c x+d
$$

takes integer values. Prove that for all integer values of $x$, this polynomial takes integer values.
(14th All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, 1988)

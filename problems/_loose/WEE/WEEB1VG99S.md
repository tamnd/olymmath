For a non-equilateral acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, circle $\Gamma$ is its incircle, and circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ is the excircle opposite vertex $A$, and $\Gamma, \Gamma^{\prime}$ touch side $BC$ at points $P, P^{\prime}$, respectively. Circle $\Gamma_{0}$ passes through points $B, C$ and is tangent to circle $\Gamma$ at point $Q$, and circle $\Gamma_{0}^{\prime}$ passes through points $B, C$ and is tangent to circle $\Gamma^{\prime}$ at point $Q^{\prime}$. If line $PQ$ intersects $P^{\prime}Q^{\prime}$ at point $N$, prove: $AN \perp BC$.

100 points are marked on a circle. These points are numbered from 1 to 100 in some order.

a) Prove that for any numbering, the points can be paired in such a way that the segments connecting the points in pairs do not intersect, and all the sums in the pairs are odd.

b) Is it true that for any numbering, the points can be paired in such a way that the segments connecting the points in pairs do not intersect, and all the sums in the pairs are even?

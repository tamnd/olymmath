Two players alternately write one of the numbers $473,523,573,623,673$, $723,773,823$ or 873 in a free cell of a $3 \times 3$ table, with each number being used only once. The first player is not allowed to write a number in the central cell on their first move. The game ends when one of the players achieves a sum of 2019 in any row, column, or diagonal whose three cells are all filled, and that player wins. If the entire table is filled and no one achieves that sum, the second player wins. Which player has a winning strategy?

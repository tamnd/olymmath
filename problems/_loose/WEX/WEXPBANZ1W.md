66 Let $P$ be any point on the ellipse
$$
\frac{x^{2}}{3+2 \sqrt{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{2+2 \sqrt{2}}=1
$$

(excluding the points of intersection with the $x$-axis), $F_{1}, F_{2}$ be the foci of the ellipse, and $I$ be the incenter of $\triangle P F_{1} F_{2}$. Prove that the line through $I$ parallel to the $x$-axis must bisect the area of $\triangle P F_{1} F_{2}$.

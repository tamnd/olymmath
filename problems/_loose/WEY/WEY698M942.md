On a $10 \times 10$ board, 50 tokens are placed such that no two are on the same square. Specifically, 25 tokens occupy the lower left quarter of the board, and the remaining 25 occupy the upper right quarter. Let $X, Y, Z$ be three consecutive squares (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally). If two tokens are on squares $X$ and $Y$, and square $Z$ is free, the token on square $X$ can be moved to square $Z$, jumping over the token on square $Y$.

Can all 50 tokens be moved to the lower half of the board with a finite sequence of such moves?

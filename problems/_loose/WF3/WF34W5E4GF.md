Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be a regular convex $n$-gon. We divide the side $A_{i} A_{i+1}$ into $(i+1)$ equal parts $\left(i=1,2, \ldots, n\right.$, with $A_{n+1}$ understood to be $A_{1}$) and mark the $i$-th division point from the starting point of the side as $B_{i}$, and the $i$-th division point from the endpoint of the side as $C_{i}$. Prove that the areas of the polygons $B_{1} B_{2} \ldots B_{n}$ and $C_{1} C_{2} \ldots C_{n}$ are equal.

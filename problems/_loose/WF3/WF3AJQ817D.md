How many distinct values does $f(x)$ assume for $x \ge 0$ ? $\textbf{(A)}\ 32 \textbf{(B)}\ 36 \textbf{(C)}\ 45 \textbf{(D)}\ 46 \textbf{(E)}\ \text{infinitely many}$

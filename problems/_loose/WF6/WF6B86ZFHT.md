In one village, there live knights who always tell the truth, and liars who always lie. A traveler asked each resident of the village two questions: "How many knights are there in the village?" and "By how much do the numbers of knights and liars differ?" The traveler knows that there is at least one knight in the village. Can the traveler always determine, from the answers received, who among the villagers is a knight and who is a liar? (S. Berlov)

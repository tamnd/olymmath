In a certain family, the husband and wife have made the following agreement: If the wife washes the dishes on a certain day, then the husband washes the dishes the next day. If, however, the husband washes the dishes on a certain day, then who washes the dishes the next day is decided by a coin toss. Let $ p_n $ denote the probability that the husband washes the dishes on the $ n $-th day of the agreement. Prove that the limit $ \lim_{n\to \infty} p_n $ exists and calculate it. We assume $ p_1 = \frac{1}{2} $.

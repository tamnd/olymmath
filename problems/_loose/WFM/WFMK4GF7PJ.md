The 59th problem: Let $\mathrm{p}$ be a prime number. If for every integer $\mathrm{e}>0$, there exists an integer $N \geq 0$, such that for any integer $\mathrm{m} \geq \mathrm{N}, \mathrm{p}^{\mathrm{e}} \mid \sum_{\mathrm{k}=0}^{\mathrm{m}}(-1)^{\mathrm{k}} \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{m}}^{\mathrm{k}} \mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{k}}$, then the integer sequence $\left\{z_{\mathrm{n}}\right\}_{\mathrm{n}=0}^{+\infty}$ is called "$\mathrm{p}-$ good". Prove: If the sequences $\left\{x_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{+\infty}$ and $\left\{y_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{+\infty}$ are both $p-$ good, then the sequence $\left\{x_{n} y_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{+\infty}$ is also $p-$ good.

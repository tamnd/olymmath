In the plane of triangle $ABC$, a point $M$ is given, the distances $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$ from which to the vertices of the triangle satisfy the equation:

$$
d_{1}^{2} + d_{2}^{2} + d_{3}^{2} = \frac{a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2}}{3}
$$

Prove that the point $M$ is the centroid of the triangle.

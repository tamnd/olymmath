Consider real numbers $A$ , $B$ , \dots, $Z$ such that $$
EVIL = \frac{5}{31}, LOVE = \frac{6}{29}, \text{ and }
IMO = \frac{7}{3}.
$$ If $OMO = \tfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ , find the value of $m+n$ .

It is to be proven: If $a, b, c$ are the side lengths of a triangle, then $a^{2}<2\left(b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$.

Furthermore, it is to be shown that the absolute value of the difference between the two sides of the inequality can be arbitrarily small.

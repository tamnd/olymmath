A line intersects side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ at $C_1$, side $AC$ at $B_1$, and the extension of side $BC$ at $A_1$. Let the reflection of $C_1$ and $B_1$ over the midpoint of their respective sides be $C_2$ and $B_2$, respectively, and let the intersection of lines $B_2 C_2$ and $BC$ be $A_2$. It is to be proven that

$$
\sin \angle B_1 A_1 C : \sin \angle C_2 A_2 B = B_2 C_2 : B_1 C_1.
$$

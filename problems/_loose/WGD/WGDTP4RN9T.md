Let's prove that for every equilateral triangle, there is exactly one number $t$ such that the following holds: No matter how we place a finite number of points on the perimeter of the triangle, there will always be a point on the perimeter whose average distance from the given points is $t$.

Determine the value of $t$ if the side of the equilateral triangle is one unit!

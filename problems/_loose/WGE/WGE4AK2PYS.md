Pepík came up with the following problem:

$$
M+A+M+R+A+D+M+A+T+E+M+A+T+I+K+U=?
$$

He replaced different letters with different digits from 1 to 9 and found out what the result was.

a) What is the largest result Pepík could have obtained?

b) Could he have obtained a result of 50? If so, how?

c) Could he have obtained a result of 59? If so, determine all possible values that the sum $M+A+M$ could have.

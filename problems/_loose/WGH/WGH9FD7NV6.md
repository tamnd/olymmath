Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be an arithmetic progression with common difference $r$. Prove that

$$
\left|a_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{2 n}\right| \geq n|r|, \forall n \geq 1
$$

When does equality hold for a fixed positive integer $n$?

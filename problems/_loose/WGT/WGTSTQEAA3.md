${ }^{+}$ Fix a vertex $v$, is its orbit $U=\{v, \sigma(v), \sigma(\sigma(v)), \ldots\}$ finite or infinite? To determine the position of $U$ in $G$, let $P$ be the path from $v$ to $\sigma(v)$, and consider the union $P \cup \sigma(P) \cup \sigma(\sigma(P)) \cup \cdots$, does this union define an end in some form? Additionally, what can be concluded from the sequence $v, \sigma^{-1}(v), \sigma^{-2}(v), \ldots$?

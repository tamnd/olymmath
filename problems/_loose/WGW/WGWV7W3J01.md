Show that the area of a triangle

$$
T=R r(\sin A+\sin B+\sin C)
$$

and

$$
T=2 R^{2} \sin A \sin B \sin C
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumcircle and $r$ is the radius of the incircle of the triangle.

Let $F, F_{a}, F_{b}$, and $F_{c}$ be the points of tangency of the nine-point circle of triangle $ABC$ with the incircle and the three excircles (where $F_{a}$ is the point of tangency with the circle centered at $I_{a}$, and so on). Let $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}, B_{1}$ and $B_{2}, C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ be the points of intersection of the internal and external angle bisectors of angles $A, B$, and $C$ with the opposite sides, respectively. Prove the similarity of the following triangles: $\triangle F_{a} F_{b} F_{c}$ and $\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}, \triangle F F_{b} F_{c}$ and $\triangle A_{1} B_{2} C_{2}, \triangle F F_{c} F_{a}$ and $\triangle B_{1} C_{2} A_{2}, \Delta F F_{a} F_{b}$ and $\triangle C_{1} A_{2} B_{2}$ (Victor Tebo).

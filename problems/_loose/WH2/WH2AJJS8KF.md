Let $\Gamma$ be a circle with center $O$, and let $A, B, C, D$ be four distinct points on $\Gamma$. Let $M$ be the intersection of $(A C)$ and $(B D)$, and let $N$ be the second intersection of the circumcircle of $O A B$ and that of $O C D$.

Show that $A N M D$ is cyclic.

\section*{Task 4 - 211044}

Several people are playing a game with three dice, on whose sides letters are written instead of the usual numbers. Each face has exactly one letter, and each letter appears only once.

After each roll, the player must try to form a word from the three letters that are facing up.

Investigate whether it is possible to distribute the letters on the dice in such a way that with the so labeled dice, the following words can be formed during the game:

$A U F, B E I, B E N, C U P, G E R, I C H, I D A, I S T, M A N, N O T, T O R, Z U G$

If this is the case, investigate whether the distribution of letters on the dice can be uniquely determined from the given information, i.e., whether for each of the three dice (disregarding the order), it can be uniquely determined which letters are on it! If this is also the case, determine this distribution!

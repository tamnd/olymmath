Lo Jac' le pirate owns a rectangular carpet of unknown dimensions. Unfortunately, a tick has kidnapped his ruler and he has no other device to measure lengths. However, he notices that he can place his carpet (flat) in two rectangular rooms of his Grésillon castle so that each corner of the carpet touches a different wall of the rooms. He also knows that the length and width of his carpet are an integer number of feet, that the widths of these two rooms are 38 feet and 50 feet respectively, and that the lengths of these two rooms are the same (the exact value is unknown). Go fetch an enigma from the cell corresponding to the length of his carpet.

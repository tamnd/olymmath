A $2t$ area convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ has no parallel side pairs. Find the point $P_{1}$ on the line segment $CD$ such that the area of triangle $ABP_{1}$ is $t$, and $P_{1}$ is on the same side of line $AB$ as $C$. Similarly, let $P_{2}, P_{3}, P_{4}$ be the points on lines $AD, AB, BC$ respectively, such that the areas of triangles $BCP_{2}, CDP_{3}, DAP_{4}$ are $t$. Show that the points $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$, and $P_{4}$ lie on a straight line.

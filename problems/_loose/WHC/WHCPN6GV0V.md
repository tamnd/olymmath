Circles $\Gamma, C_{1}$, and $C_{2}$ are in a plane. Circles $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ internally touch circle $\Gamma$ at points $B$ and $C$, respectively, and they externally touch each other at point $D$. Let $A$ be one of the points where the common internal tangent of circles $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ intersects $\Gamma$, and let $K$ and $L$ be the other points of intersection of lines $A B$ and $A C$ with circles $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$, respectively. If $M$ and $N$ are the other points of intersection of line $B C$ with circles $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$, respectively, prove that lines $A D, K M$, and $L N$ intersect at a single point.

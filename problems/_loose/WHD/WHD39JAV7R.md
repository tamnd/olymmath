In quadrilateral $M N P Q$, two non-intersecting circles are placed such that one of them touches the sides $M N, N P, P Q$, and the other touches the sides $M N, M Q, P Q$. Points B and A lie on the sides $M N$ and $P Q$, respectively, and the segment $A B$ is tangent to both circles. Find the length of side $M Q$ if $N P=b$ and the perimeter of quadrilateral $B A Q M$ is greater than the perimeter of quadrilateral $A B N P$ by $2 p$.

Calculate the value of the expression $\left(-4 x y z^{-1}\right)^{-2}$, if $x, y$ and $z$ are unknowns in the system

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{2}{x}-\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=-3 \\
& \frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{y}-\frac{1}{z}=3 \\
& \frac{3}{x}+\frac{2}{y}+\frac{2}{z}=4
\end{aligned}
$$

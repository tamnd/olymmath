Consider the points $A, B, C, D$ such that $B$ is the midpoint of the segment $(A C)$ and $C$ is the midpoint of the segment $(B D)$. Show that:
a) $B C=\frac{A C+B D}{4}$;
b) $\frac{1}{A C}+\frac{1}{B D}<\frac{4}{A D}$.

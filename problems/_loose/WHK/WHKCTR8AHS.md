Prove that there exist pairwise disjoint sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{2014}$, whose union is the set of natural numbers, for which the following condition holds:

For any natural numbers $a$ and $b$, at least two of the numbers $a, b$, $\operatorname{GCD}(a, b)$ belong to one of the sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{2014}$.

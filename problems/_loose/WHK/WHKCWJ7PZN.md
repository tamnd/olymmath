The Fibonacci sequence is defined by $F_{0}=0, F_{1}=1$ and for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \geqslant 2: F_{n}=$ $F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$. Show that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ we have

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} F_{k}^{2}=F_{n} \cdot F_{n+1}
$$

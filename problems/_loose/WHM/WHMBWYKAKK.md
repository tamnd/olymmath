Prove that for any real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{1987}$ and any positive numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{1987}$, the following inequality holds: $\frac{\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{1987}\right)^{2}}{b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{1987}} \leqslant \frac{a_{1}^{2}}{b_{1}}+\frac{a_{2}^{2}}{b_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{1987}^{2}}{b_{1987}}$.
(50th Moscow Olympiad Problem)

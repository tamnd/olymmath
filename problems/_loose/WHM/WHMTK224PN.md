Let the function $f(x)$ be defined on $\mathrm{R}$, for any $a, b \in \mathrm{R}$, we have $f(a+b) + f(a-b) = 2 f(a) \cdot f(b)$, and $f(0) \neq 0$.
(1) Prove that $f(x)$ is an even function;
(2) If there exists a positive number $m$ such that $f(m)=0$, find a value of $T$ that satisfies $f(x+T)=f(x)$ $(T \neq 0)$.

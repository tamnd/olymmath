Suppose we have a board with sixteen squares arranged in four rows and four columns.

(a) Prove that seven chips can be placed, never two in the same square, in such a way that by removing any two rows and any two columns, at least one chip always remains.

(b) Prove that if six chips are placed, never two in the same square, it is always possible to remove two rows and two columns in such a way that all chips are eliminated.

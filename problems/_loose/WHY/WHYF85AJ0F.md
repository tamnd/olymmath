For positive real numbers $ a$ , $ b$ , $ c$ and $ d$ such that $ a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2 = 1$ prove that

$ a^2b^2cd + +ab^2c^2d + abc^2d^2 + a^2bcd^2 + a^2bc^2d + ab^2cd^2 \le 3/32,$

and determine the cases of equality.

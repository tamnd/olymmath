Let $a_{n}$ be a quasi-injective increasing sequence of integers (i.e., there exists $C>0$ such that $a_{n}=k$ has at most $C$ solutions for all $k$). Show that if $a_{n}$ has only a finite number of prime divisors, then there exists $c>1$ such that

$$
c^{n}=O\left(a_{n}\right)
$$

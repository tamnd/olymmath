Let $n$ be a positive integer, and $z_{1}, z_{2}, \cdots, z_{n}, \omega_{1}, \omega_{2}, \cdots, \omega_{n}$ be complex numbers. For any $\varepsilon_{1}, \varepsilon_{2}, \cdots, \varepsilon_{n} \in\{-1,1\}$, the inequality $\left|\varepsilon_{1} z_{1}+\varepsilon_{2} z_{2}+\cdots+\varepsilon_{n} z_{n}\right| \leqslant \mid \varepsilon_{1} \omega_{1}+\varepsilon_{2} \omega_{2}+\cdots+$ $\varepsilon_{n} \omega_{n} \mid$ holds. Prove: $\square$
$$
\left|z_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|z_{2}\right|^{2}+\cdots+\left|z_{n}\right|^{2} \leqslant\left|\omega_{1}\right|^{2}+\left.\left|\omega_{2}\right|^{2}|\cdots+| \omega_{n}\right|^{2} .
$$

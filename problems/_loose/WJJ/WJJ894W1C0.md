The volume of a convex polyhedron is $V$, and the surface area is $S$.
a) Prove that if a sphere of radius $r$ is placed inside it, then $V / S \geqslant r / 3$.

b) Prove that inside it, a sphere of radius $V / S$ can be placed.

c) One convex polyhedron is located inside another. Let $V_{1}$ and $S_{1}$ be the volume and surface area of the outer polyhedron, and $V_{2}$ and $S_{2}$ of the inner one. Prove that $3 V_{1} / S_{1} \geqslant V_{2} / S_{2}$.

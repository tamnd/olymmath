If the continuous function $f(x)\left(x \in \mathbf{R}^{+}\right)$ satisfies $f(0)=0$, and $f^{2}(x+y) \geqslant f^{2}(x)+f^{2}(y), \forall x, y \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$, then $f(x)=a \sqrt{x}, a>0$.

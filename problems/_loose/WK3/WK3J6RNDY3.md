In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, let the ellipse $E: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$, and the line $l: x+y-3a=0$. If the eccentricity of the ellipse $E$ is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and the distance from the origin $O$ to the line $l$ is $3 \sqrt{2}$.
(1) Find the equations of the ellipse $E$ and the line $l$;
(2) If the distances from three points $P, A(0, b), B(a, 0)$ on the ellipse $E$ to the line $l$ are $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$ respectively, prove that $d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}$ can be the lengths of the three sides of a triangle.

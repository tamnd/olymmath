Given: $A B C D$ is a cyclic quadrilateral, $B D<A C$, line $A B$ intersects $C D$ at $E$, line $B C$ intersects $A D$ at $F$. $L$ and $M$ are the midpoints of segments $A C$ and $B D$ respectively. Prove: $\frac{L M}{E F}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{A C}{B D}-\frac{B D}{A C}\right)$.

A non-self-intersecting broken line is located in a given half-plane, with the ends of the broken line lying on the boundary of this half-plane. The length of the broken line is $L$, and the area of the polygon bounded by the broken line and the boundary of the half-plane is $S$. Prove that $S \leq L^{2} / 2 \pi$.

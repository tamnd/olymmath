Consider $n$ Venetian princes each living in a palace. Each palace is connected to every other, either by an alley or by a canal (but not both). Prince Alfonzo decides to visit all his friends in the same evening, but he doesn't want to complicate things and decides to use only one mode of transport. Show that by choosing well, he can visit all the other princes.

A: Let $\triangle A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, and $P$ be any point inside it. Prove that: $\Lambda P^{3} \sin \angle B \Lambda P+$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
B P^{3} \sin \angle C B P+C P^{3} \sin \angle A C P=A P^{3} \sin \angle C A P+B P^{3} \sin \angle A B P+ \\
C P^{3} \sin \angle B C P .
\end{array}
$$

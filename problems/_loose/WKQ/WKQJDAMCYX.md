68 Find the range of real number $a$ such that for any real number $x$ and any $\theta \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$, the following inequality always holds:
$$
(x+3+2 \sin \theta \cos \theta)^{2}+(x+a \sin \theta+a \cos \theta)^{2} \geqslant \frac{1}{8} .
$$

Around a round table, 1994 students are sitting and playing a card passing game. Initially, all $n$ cards are held by one student. Each time, a student who has at least two cards is chosen, and he passes one card to each of the adjacent students. The game ends when and only when each student has at most 1 card. Prove that
(1) when $n \geqslant 1994$, the game can continue indefinitely;
(2) when $n<1994$, the game will inevitably end.

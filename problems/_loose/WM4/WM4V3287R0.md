In the country, there are several cities (more than one); some pairs of cities are connected by roads. It is known that from each city, you can get to any other city by driving along several roads. In addition, the roads do not form cycles, that is, if you leave from some city along some road and then move further without passing through the same road twice, it is impossible to return to the starting city. Prove that in this country, there will be at least two cities, each of which is connected by a road to exactly one city.

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive real numbers satisfying
$$\frac{a}{b} \left( \frac{a}{b}+ 2 \right) + \frac{b}{a} \left( \frac{b}{a}+ 2 \right)= 2022.$$
Find the positive integer $n$ such that $$\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}+\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}=\sqrt{n}.$$

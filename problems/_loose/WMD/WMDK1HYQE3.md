Given a right triangle $A B C$ with hypotenuse $A B$. Let $D$ be the foot of its altitude $z$ from vertex $C$ and $M, N$ the intersections of the angle bisectors of $\angle A D C, \angle B D C$ with sides $A C, B C$. Prove that

$$
2|A M| \cdot|B N|=|M N|^{2} .
$$

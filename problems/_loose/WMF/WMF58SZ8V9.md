One matchmaker declares: "I can marry all the brunettes at the same time so that each of them marries a girl he knows!" The second matchmaker says: "And I can arrange the fate of all the blondes: each will marry a young man she knows!" This dialogue was overheard by a math enthusiast, who said: "In that case, both can be done!" Is he right?

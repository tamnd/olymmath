Let $\left(a_n\right)_{n\ge1}$ be an infinite sequence of positive integers such that for every positive integer $n$ $$a_{n+1}=(n+1)\left(a_n-n+1\right).$$ In terms of $a_1,$ determine the greatest positive integer $k$ such that $\gcd\left(a_i,a_{i+1}\right)=k$ for some positive integer $i\ge2.$

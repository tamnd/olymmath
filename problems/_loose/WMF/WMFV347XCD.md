Let the set $\mathrm{M}$ of natural numbers have $n$ elements, where $n$ is an odd number greater than one. Prove that the number of ordered pairs $(p, q)$ of distinct elements from $\mathrm{M}$ such that the arithmetic mean of the numbers $p, q$ is an element of $\mathrm{M}$, is at most $\frac{1}{2}(n-1)^{2}$.

(Martin Panák, Patrik Bak)

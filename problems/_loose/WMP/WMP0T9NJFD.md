In a test exercise, for an equation with the left side being a 10th-degree polynomial in $x$ with integer coefficients and the right side being 0, a student wrote the solution as $x=\frac{1}{7}$. His neighbor glanced at his notebook and saw only the first two terms of the polynomial: $1975 x^{10}$ and $1974 x^{9}$, and immediately said the answer was incorrect. Why did he say that?
(Kyiv Mathematical Olympiad, 1975)

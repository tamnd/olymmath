The sum of two numbers is 6, and the absolute value of their difference is 8. The quadratic equation with these two numbers as roots is
(A) $x^{2}-6 x+7=0$.
(B) $x^{2}-6 x-7=0$.
(C) $x^{2}+6 x-8=0$.
(D) $x^{2}-6 x+8=0$.
(E) $x^{2}+6 x-7=0$.
(6th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1955)

In the trihedron $O A B C$, the dihedral angles are right angles. On two edges, we mark $O A=O B=a$, while on the third one, the distance $O C=x$. The value of $x$ is to be determined so that the volume of the tetrahedron $O A B C$ is equal to $\frac{F m}{3}$, where $F$ is the surface area of the tetrahedron and $m$ is a given length. For which values of $m$ is there a solution?

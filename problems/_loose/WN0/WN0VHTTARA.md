50 coins are arranged in a straight line. Each coin has a black side and a white side. Initially, the visible sides of all coins are black. An operation consists of flipping (and thus changing the visible color) two adjacent coins. Is it possible that after a finite number of operations, there will be exactly 25 coins with the white side visible?

$A B C D$ is a spatial quadrilateral (points $A, B, C$ and $D$ do not lie in the same plane). Prove that there exist at least eight spheres that touch the lines $A B, B C, C D$ and $D A$. Also prove that if the sum of any two sides of the quadrilateral equals the sum of the other two sides, then there are infinitely many such spheres.

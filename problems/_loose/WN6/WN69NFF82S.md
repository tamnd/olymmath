Shayan is playing a game by himself. He picks **relatively prime** integers $a$ and $b$ such that $1<a<b<2020$. He wins if every integer $m\geq\frac{ab}{2}$ can be expressed in the form $ax+by$ for nonnegative integers $x$ and $y$. He hasn't been winning often, so he decides to write down all winning pairs $(a,b)$, from $(a_1,b_1)$ to $(a_n,b_n)$. What is $b_1+b_2+\ldots+b_n$?

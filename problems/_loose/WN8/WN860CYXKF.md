Let $A B C D$ be a non-overlapping quadrilateral with side lengths $A B=9 \mathrm{~cm}, B C=6 \mathrm{~cm}$, $C D=11 \mathrm{~cm}, A D=8 \mathrm{~cm}$, and with the interior angle at $B$ measuring $110^{\circ}$.

Investigate by construction whether the area of $A B C D$ is uniquely determined by these specifications!

Now, for a rectangle $E F G H$, the following properties are required:

(1) The rectangle $E F G H$ has the same area as the quadrilateral $A B C D$.

(2) $A$ lies on the rectangle side $E H$ between $E$ and $H$, and $C$ lies on the rectangle side $F G$.

(3) The rectangle side $E H$ is perpendicular to $A C$.

Explain and describe how all the points that can serve as vertex $E$ of a rectangle $E F G H$ with the required properties (1), (2), (3) can be constructed!

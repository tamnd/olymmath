Investigate the convergence of the series using the necessary condition and the comparison test:
1) $1+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}+\ldots$;
2) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}+\ldots$
3) $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\ln n}=\frac{1}{\ln 2}+\frac{1}{\ln 3}+\ldots$;
4) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2}}$
5) $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(n+1) 5^{n}}=1+\frac{1}{2 \cdot 5}+\frac{1}{3 \cdot 5^{2}}+\frac{1}{4 \cdot 5^{3}}+\ldots$

In a stationery store, packages of paper are stored in stacks of 60 packages. Each package contains 500 sheets of paper and each sheet of paper has a thickness of $0.1 \mathrm{~mm}$. Ignoring the thickness of the paper used to wrap the packages, we can state that the height of a stack of 60 packages is approximately equal to the height of:
(a) an adult person;
(b) a one-year-old baby;
(c) a common table;
(d) a 10-story building;
(e) a classroom.

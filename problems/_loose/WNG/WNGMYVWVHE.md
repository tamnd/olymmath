Let $A B C$ be a triangle, $\mathcal{C}$ its circumcircle. Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle, $A^{\prime}$ the foot of the angle bisector from $A$, and $S_{A}$ the intersection point of this bisector with $\mathcal{C}$. $S$ is called the South Pole of $A B C$ with respect to $A$.

- Show that $S$ is on the perpendicular bisector of $[B C]$, and then that $B S_{A}=C S_{A}=I S_{A}$.
- Show that $A B S_{A}$ and $B A^{\prime} S_{A}$ are similar.

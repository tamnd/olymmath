In a complete hexagon $A B C D E F$, $A^{\prime}, A^{\prime \prime}$ are the midpoints of $B E, D F$ respectively, $B^{\prime}, B^{\prime \prime}$ are the midpoints of $A E, C F$ respectively, and so on. Prove that the six circles $A A^{\prime} A^{\prime \prime}$, circle $B B^{\prime} B^{\prime \prime}$, ..., circle $F F^{\prime} F^{\prime \prime}$ are concurrent.

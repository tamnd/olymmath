Let $M$ be the centroid of triangle $ABC$, and $X$ be an arbitrary point. On the lines $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$, points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken such that $A_{1} X \parallel AM$, $B_{1} X \parallel BM$, and $C_{1} X \parallel CM$. Prove that the centroid $M_{1}$ of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ coincides with the midpoint of segment $MX$.

3 In a mathematics competition, some of the participants are friends. The friendship relationship is mutual. If any two people in a group of participants are friends, we call this group a "clique" (in particular, a group with fewer than 2 people is also a clique).

It is known that in this competition, the number of people in the largest clique (the clique with the most people) is even. Prove: We can always divide the participants into two classrooms such that the number of people in the largest clique in one classroom is equal to the number of people in the largest clique in the other classroom.

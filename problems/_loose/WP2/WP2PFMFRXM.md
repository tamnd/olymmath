Given an acute triangle $A_{0} B_{0} C_{0}$. Let points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ be the centers of squares constructed on sides $B_{0} C_{0}, C_{0} A_{0}, A_{0} B_{0}$. We do the same with triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. We obtain triangle $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ and so on. Prove that $\Delta A_{\mathrm{n}+1} B_{\mathrm{n}+1} C_{\mathrm{n}+1}$ intersects $\Delta A_{\mathrm{n}} B_{\mathrm{n}} C_{\mathrm{n}}$ exactly at 6 points.

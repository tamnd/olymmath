On a plane, two lines intersect at an angle $\alpha$. A grasshopper is squatting on one of the lines and jumps from one line to the other every second (the intersection point is considered a common point of the two lines). It is known that the distance of each jump is 1, and at any moment, it does not jump back to the position it was in one second ago. After some time, it jumps back to the starting point. Prove that when measured in degrees, the angle $\alpha$ is a rational number.

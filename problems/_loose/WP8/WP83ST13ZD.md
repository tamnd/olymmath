Four. (50 points) Suppose there are roads $A B, C D$ between cities $A, B, C, D$, but no $B C, A D$, then a reform can be made to replace roads $A B$ and $C D$ with roads $B C$ and $A D$. Initially, the country has several cities, some of which are connected by roads, with each city having 100 roads leading out. The minister drew a new road map, where each city also has 100 roads leading out (no two cities are connected by more than one road). Prove: The new road map can be obtained through several reforms.

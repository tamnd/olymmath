We inscribed a circle in a convex deltoid, connected the center of the circle to the vertices of the deltoid, and on each connecting line, we drew a perpendicular line at the respective vertex. Prove that the diagonals of the new quadrilateral determined by the four new lines pass through the center of the circle. (Each vertex of the new quadrilateral is given by the intersection of the perpendiculars drawn at two adjacent vertices of the deltoid.)

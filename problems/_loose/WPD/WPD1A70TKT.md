48 If $\frac{1}{A}+\frac{1}{B}+\frac{1}{C}=\frac{1}{A+B+C}$, then for any odd number $n$,
$$
\frac{1}{A^{n}}+\frac{1}{B^{n}}+\frac{1}{C^{n}}=\frac{1}{A^{n}+B^{n}+C^{n}},
$$

Prove it.

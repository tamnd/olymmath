For an arbitrary polyhedron we denote by $n_{k}$ the number of faces with exactly $k$ vertices. Prove that at least one of the two following sentences is true:

(i) there exists $k$ such that $n_{k} \geq 3$;

(ii) there exists $k \neq h$ such that $n_{k} \geq n_{h} \geq 2$.

The numbers $a, b$, and $c$ lie in the interval $[2,4]$. Prove the inequality:

$$
\frac{2}{a+b^{2}+c^{3}}+\frac{2}{b+c^{2}+a^{3}}+\frac{2}{c+a^{2}+b^{3}} \leqslant \frac{3}{a+b+c} .
$$

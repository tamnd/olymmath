If point $A(a, 0)$ is outside the circle $O: x^{2}+y^{2}=1$, points $P, Q \in$ circle $O$, point $M, P$ are symmetric about point $A$, and $O P \perp O Q$, with $a>0$, then the maximum length of segment $M Q$ must be
(A) $\sqrt{4 a^{2}+4 \sqrt{2} a+2}$.
(B) $\sqrt{4 a^{2}-4 a+2}$.
(C) $\sqrt{a^{2}+a+1}$.
(D) $\sqrt{a^{2}-a+2}$.
(High School Mathematics Competition in Taiyuan City, Shanxi Province, China, 1990)

In the plane, there is a circle $k$ with a radius of $1 \mathrm{~cm}$, and a line $e$ which is $2 \mathrm{~cm}$ away from the center of the circle. Let $A$ be any fixed point in the closed half-plane determined by the line $e$ that does not contain the circle $k$. Consider all parallelograms $A B C D$ where the vertex $B$ lies on the circle $k$, and the vertex $D$ lies on the line $e$. What will be the locus (geometric place) of the vertex $C$ of these parallelograms? (Consider also degenerate parallelograms, which consist of four different points on a line, symmetric with respect to a point, where the midpoints of segments $A C$ and $B D$ coincide.)

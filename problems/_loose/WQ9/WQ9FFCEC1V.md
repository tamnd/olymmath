Let $a, A$ be natural numbers, $A \geqslant a$. Prove:
$$
\frac{a}{A}+\frac{A-a}{A} \cdot \frac{a}{A-1}+\frac{A-a}{A} \cdot \frac{A-a-1}{A-1} \cdot \frac{a}{A-2}+\cdots+\frac{A-a}{A} \cdot \frac{A-a-1}{A-1} \cdots \frac{1}{a+1} \cdot \frac{a}{a}=1 .
$$

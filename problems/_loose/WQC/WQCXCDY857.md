In triangle $ABC$, the relation $2 BC = AB + AC$ holds. Prove that:

a) The vertex $A$, the midpoints $M$ and $N$ of sides $AB$ and $AC$, the center $S$ of the inscribed circle, and the center $O$ of the circumscribed circle lie on the same circle $k$.

b) The line $TS$, where $T$ is the centroid of triangle $ABC$, is tangent to the circle $k$.

Let $ABCD$ be an isosceles trapezoid ($AB \parallel CD$) and let $E$ be an arbitrary point on the arc $AB$ of the circumscribed circle of the trapezoid that does not contain the other vertices of the trapezoid. Perpendiculars are dropped from each of the points $A$ and $B$ to the lines $EC$ and $ED$. Prove that the four feet of these perpendiculars are concyclic.

Given a parallelogram $A B C D$, in which $\angle B A C=40^{\circ}$ and $\angle B C A=20^{\circ}$. On the diagonal $A C$ are marked points $E$ and $G$, and on the side $A D$ are points $F$ and $H$ such that points $B, E$ and $F$ lie on the same line, $\angle A B G=\angle A H G=90^{\circ}$ and $A F=E G$. Prove that $A F=H D$.

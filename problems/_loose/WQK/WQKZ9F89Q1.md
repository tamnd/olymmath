On the sides $[A B],[B C],[C D],[D A]$ of parallelogram $A B C D$, points $M, N, P$ and respectively $Q$ are considered.

a) Show that $\overrightarrow{M N}+\overrightarrow{Q P}$ is collinear with $\overrightarrow{A C} \Leftrightarrow \overrightarrow{N P}+\overrightarrow{M Q}$ is collinear with $\overrightarrow{B D}$.

b) Indicate 4 points $M, N, P, Q$ for which the equivalence from point a) holds.

Alice and Bob have an $8 \times 8$ chessboard in front of them. Initially, all the squares are white. On each turn, Alice chooses a white square and colors it black. Bob then chooses to color one of the adjacent squares (sharing an edge) black or to do nothing. Alice can stop the game whenever she wants. Her goal is to maximize the number of black connected components, while Bob wants to minimize this number. If both players play optimally, how many connected components will there be at the end of the game?

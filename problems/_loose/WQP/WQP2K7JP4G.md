Lin has six non-negative integers (which can be the same) on the six vertices of a regular hexagon. He performs the following operation: erase one number and write the absolute value of the difference of the two adjacent numbers. For which positive integer $n$ is it true that if the sum of the six numbers on the vertices is $n$, it is always possible to perform a series of operations such that all six numbers on the vertices eventually become 0?

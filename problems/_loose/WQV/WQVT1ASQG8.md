Given a cube with an edge length of $n$ cm. We have a long piece of insulating tape 1 cm wide. It is required to cover the cube with the tape, with the tape being able to freely cross over an edge to another face, travel in a straight line parallel to the edge on a face, and not hang off the face. Into how many pieces does the tape need to be cut to cover the cube?

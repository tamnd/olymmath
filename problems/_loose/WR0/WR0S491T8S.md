Let the modulus of complex numbers $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ be no greater than 1. The equation: $\left(w-z_{2}\right)\left(w-z_{3}\right)+\left(w-z_{3}\right)(w-$ $\left.z_{1}\right)+\left(w-z_{1}\right)\left(w-z_{2}\right)=0$ has two roots $w_{1}, w_{2}$. Prove: $\min \left\{\left|w_{1}-z_{i}\right|,\left|w_{2}-z_{i}\right|\right\} \leq 1$, $i=1,2,3$.

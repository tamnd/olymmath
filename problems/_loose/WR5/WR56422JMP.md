Find all values of the parameter $a \in R$, for which the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}+6 x+10 y+34}+\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}+6 x+6 y+18}=2 \\
y^{2}+x^{2}-4 x+10=a
\end{array}\right.
$$

has a unique solution in $R \times R$.

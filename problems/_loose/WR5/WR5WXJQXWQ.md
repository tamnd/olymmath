Let $p\geq5$ be a prime and $S_k=1^k+2^k+...+(p-1)^k,\forall k\in\mathbb{N}.$ Prove that there is an infinity of numbers $n\in\mathbb{N}$ such that $p^3$ divides $S_n$ and $ p $ divides $S_{n-1}$ and $S_{n-2}.$

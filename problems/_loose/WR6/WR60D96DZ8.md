Let $A B C$ be a triangle, and $D$ be an arbitrary point on the plane. The triangle formed by the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from $D$ to the sides of triangle $A B C$ will be called the pedal triangle of point $D$ with respect to triangle $A B C$, and the circle circumscribed around the pedal triangle will be called the pedal circle. Denote by $D_{1}$ the point where the lines symmetric to the lines $A D, B D$, and $C D$ with respect to the angle bisectors of angles $A, B$, and $C$ (respectively) of triangle $A B C$ intersect. Prove that the pedal circles of points $D$ and $D_{1}$ coincide.

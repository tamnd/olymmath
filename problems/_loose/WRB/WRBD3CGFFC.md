Let $ABC$ be a triangle with all angles acute, and such that $AB < AC$. We denote by $D, E$, and $F$ the points of tangency of the incircle of triangle $ABC$ with the segments $[BC], [CA]$, and $[AB]$. Let $G$ be the point on the line $(AB)$ such that the lines $(DG)$ and $(EF)$ are perpendicular. Finally, let $X$ be the point of intersection, other than $A$, between the circumcircles of triangles $ABC$ and $AEF$.

Prove that the points $B, D, G$, and $X$ lie on the same circle.

Find all natural numbers $a, b$ and $c$ such that the roots of the equation

$$
\begin{array}{r}
x^{2}-2 a x+b=0 \\
x^{2}-2 b x+c=0 \\
x^{2}-2 c x+a=0
\end{array}
$$

are natural numbers.

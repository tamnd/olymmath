For a triangle $ABC$, it is required that $a=5.0 \text{cm}$, $s_{a}=6.0 \text{cm}$, and $h_{c}=4.3 \text{cm}$, where $a$ is the length of side $BC$, $s_{a}$ is the length of the median to side $BC$, and $h_{c}$ is the length of the altitude from $C$ to side $AB$.

a) Prove: If a triangle meets these requirements, then it can be constructed from the given lengths 5.0 $\text{cm}$, 6.0 $\text{cm}$, and 4.3 $\text{cm}$!

b) Describe such a construction and create a construction drawing!

c) Prove: If a triangle is constructed according to the description, then it meets the given requirements!

d) Determine whether these requirements uniquely determine a triangle $ABC$ up to congruence!

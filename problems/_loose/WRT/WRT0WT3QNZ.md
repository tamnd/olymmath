Consider a positive integer $n=\overline{a_1a_2...a_k},k\ge 2$ .A *trunk* of $n$ is a number of the form $\overline{a_1a_2...a_t},1\le t\le k-1$ .(For example,the number $23$ is a *trunk* of $2351$ .)
By $T(n)$ we denote the sum of all *trunk* of $n$ and let $S(n)=a_1+a_2+...+a_k$ .Prove that $n=S(n)+9\cdot T(n)$ .

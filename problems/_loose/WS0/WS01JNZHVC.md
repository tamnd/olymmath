Let $A B C$ be a triangle, $M$ the midpoint of the line $B C$ and $D$ a point on the line $A B$ such that $B$ lies between $A$ and $D$. Let $E$ be a point on the other side of the line $C D$ than $B$ so that $\angle E D C=\angle A C B$ and $\angle D C E=\angle B A C$. Let $F$ be the intersection of $C E$ with the line parallel to $D E$ through $A$ and let $Z$ be the intersection of $A E$ and $D F$. Show that the lines $A C, B F$ and $M Z$ intersect at a point.

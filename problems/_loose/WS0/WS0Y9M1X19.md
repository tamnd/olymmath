Six thousand students took an exam and all obtained a score which is an integer between 0 and 8 (0 and 8 included). Vincent decides to replace all scores equal to 1, 2, and 3 with 0 and all scores equal to 5, 6, or 7 with 8. The other scores remain unchanged. After this change, the average of the scores increased by $\frac{1}{10}$.

Prove that there exist two integers $a$ and $b$ such that the difference between the number of students who obtained the score $a$ and the number of students who obtained the score $b$ before the modifications made by Vincent is at least 100.

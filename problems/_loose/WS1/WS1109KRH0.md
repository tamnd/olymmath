In a society of mathematicians, each of them is engaged in at least one of the following areas of mathematics: algebra, analysis, geometry, or logic. Those who are engaged in algebra or logic are also engaged in analysis. Those who are engaged in geometry are also engaged in logic. Those who are engaged in analysis and geometry are also engaged in algebra. In which of these areas are the most, and in which are the fewest, mathematicians engaged?

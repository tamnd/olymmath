a) Prove that $\cos \alpha+\cos (\alpha+x)+\cos (\alpha+2 x)+\ldots$ $\ldots+\cos (\alpha+n x)=\frac{\sin \left(\alpha+\left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right) x\right)-\sin \left(\alpha-\frac{1}{2} x\right)}{2 \sin \frac{1}{2} x}$.

b) Prove that if $\varphi=\frac{2 k \pi}{n+1}$, where the number $k$ is an integer and $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$, then

$$
1+\cos \varphi+\cos 2 \varphi+\ldots+\cos n \varphi=0
$$

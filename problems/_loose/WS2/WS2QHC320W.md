A curve $L$ divides an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with side length $a$ into two equal areas. Prove that the length $l$ of the curve $L$ satisfies the inequality $l \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2 \sqrt[4]{3}} a$.

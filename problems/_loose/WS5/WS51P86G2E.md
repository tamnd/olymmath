Let $ a$ be a postive constant number. Given a positive integer $ n$, take an integer $ m$ such that $ m\leq \frac{na}{\pi}<m+1$.
Find $ \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n}\int_0^{m\pi} \left(a-\frac{t}{n}\right)|\sin t|\ dt$.

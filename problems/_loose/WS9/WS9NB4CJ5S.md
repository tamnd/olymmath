Given that $A$ and $B$ are two points on a horizontal line $l$ with a distance of a positive integer, and $L$ is any broken line with $A$ and $B$ as endpoints. If points $C$ and $D$ are on the broken line $L$, and $C D \parallel A B$, then $C D$ is called a horizontal chord of the broken line $L$. Prove: For any positive integer $n$, there must be a horizontal chord of length $\frac{1}{n}$ on the broken line $L$.

Given a triangle $ABC$, where the base $AB$ is equal to $a$, and its height is $h$; draw a line $DE$ parallel to $AB$ and from $D$ and $E$, draw perpendiculars $DD'$ and $EE'$ to $AB$. Rotate the triangle around the base $AB$. At what distance $x$ should the line $DE \| AB$ be drawn so that the volume of the cylinder generated by the rotation of the parallelogram $DE D' E'$ is equal to the volume of a sphere with radius $R$? Discuss the acceptable values of $x$.

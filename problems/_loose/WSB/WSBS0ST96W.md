On the circumference of a circular disk $k$ with diameter $AB$, determine the point $C$ such that the area of the part of the circle with diameter $AC$ that is not covered by $k$ is maximal.

It is to be proven that in the position of point $C$ determined in this way, the area of the semicircle with diameter $BC$ is equal to the area of triangle $OBC$, where $O$ is the center of $k$.

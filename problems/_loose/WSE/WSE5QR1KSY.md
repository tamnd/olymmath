Given points $A, B, C$ are not on the same line. There is also a line $d$ in space. Take a point $D$ on $d$ and consider the midpoint $K$ of the parallelogram determined by the midpoints of the sides of the spatial quadrilateral (skew quadrilateral) $A B C D$. What path does $K$ trace as $D$ moves along $d$?

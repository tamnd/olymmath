There is a rectangular board $m \times n$, divided into $1 \times 1$ cells. Additionally, there are many domino bones of size $1 \times 2$. The domino bones are placed on the board such that each bone occupies two cells. The board is not completely filled, but in such a way that the bones cannot be moved (the board has edges, so the bones cannot go beyond the board). Prove that the number of uncovered cells
a) is less than $m n / 4$;

b) is less than $m n / 5$

Consider the acute-angled triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, with the notations $\mathrm{AB}=\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{AC}=\mathrm{b}, \mathrm{BC}=\mathrm{a}$ and $\mathrm{AD} \perp \mathrm{BC}, \mathrm{D} \in [BC]$.

a) Show that $\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac{c \cos B}{b \cos C}$.

b) If $A^{\prime}$ is a point such that $\overrightarrow{A A^{\prime}}=(b \cos C) \overrightarrow{A B} + (c \cos B) \overrightarrow{A C}$, prove that the points $A, D, A^{\prime}$ are collinear.

(2) The segment $A D$ is $2 \text{ cm}$ shorter than the segment $A E$.

(3) The segment $C D$ has a length of $C D=5 \text{ cm}$.

(4) The segment $A B$ is $3 \text{ cm}$ longer than the segment $C E$.

a) Construct five such points $A, B, C, D, E$!

b) Determine the lengths of the segments $A D, A B, B C$!

As a solution, it is sufficient to provide:

a) a construction without description and

b) the determination of the segment lengths $A D, A B, B C$ from conditions (1) to (4).

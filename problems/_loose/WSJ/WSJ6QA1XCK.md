Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $E, F$ be two arbitrary points on the sides $(AB)$, respectively $(AC)$. The circumcircle of triangle $AEF$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at point $M$. Let $D$ be the reflection of $M$ over the line $EF$ and $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Prove that $D \in BC$ if and only if $O$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $AEF$.

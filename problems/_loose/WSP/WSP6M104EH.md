A math club with 6 members agree on a real number $ b $ that the incoming seventh member must guess. After they arrived, they received the following clues in sequence:
I. $ b $ is an even number.

II. Adding $ b $ to its cube results in a positive number less than 8000.

III. $ b $ is a real number, and its square is 13.

IV. $ b $ is an integer, divisible by 7.

V. $ b $ is a rational number.

VI. $ b $ is an integer, divisible by 14.

They also informed him that among the pairs I. and II., III. and IV., V. and VI., one is always true and the other is false. - How did the seventh member of the club figure out the number?

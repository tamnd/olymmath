Baron Münchhausen lived in the land $Z$, where the cities were under the control of Chancellor Otto and King Franja (each city was owned by exactly one of them). These cities were connected by some roads (travel was possible in both directions on the roads), with the following conditions: no two cities were connected by more than one road; each road connected a city of Chancellor Otto with a city of King Franja; each city was connected by road to exactly $k$ other cities, $k \geqslant 2$; from any city, it was possible to reach any other city via the roads. The land $Z$ was at war with the land $W$. Baron Münchhausen informed the enemies from the land $W$ that there exists a road such that, if it is destroyed, there will be some two cities that will no longer be able to reach each other using the remaining roads. Is Baron Münchhausen lying?

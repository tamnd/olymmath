The geographical environment of a certain area is remote, severely restricting economic development. A certain local specialty can only be sold locally, and for every million yuan invested by the local government, the profit obtained is $P=-\frac{1}{160}(x-40)^{2}+$ 10 (million yuan). To comply with development policies, the local government, in formulating a ten-year economic development plan, plans to develop this specialty product. The special fiscal allocation for investment in this project is 60 million yuan annually, both before and after development. If the product is to be developed, 30 million yuan must be allocated from the 60 million yuan special fund each year for the first 5 years to build a road, which will take 5 years to complete. After the road is completed, the specialty product can be sold elsewhere, and for every million yuan invested, the profit will be $Q=-\frac{159}{160}(60-x)^{2}+$ $\frac{119}{2}(60-x)$ (million yuan). From the perspective of total profit over ten years, is there any value in developing this project?

If the circumcircle of $\triangle A B T$ is $\odot O, P$ is on the extension of $B A$, and $T B^{\prime}$ is a diameter (Figure 3.10), prove that the following statements are equivalent:
(1) $\angle P T O=\frac{\pi}{2}$.
(2) $\angle P T A=\angle T B A$.
(3) $P T^{2}=P A \cdot P B$.

Let $a, b \neq 0$, the expression $\sqrt{-a^{3}} \sqrt{(-b)^{4}}$ is meaningful. Then the expression equals
(A) $-a b \sqrt{a}$.
(B) $a b \sqrt{-a}$.
(C) $a b \sqrt{a}$.
(D) $-a|b| \sqrt{-a}$.
(2nd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990)

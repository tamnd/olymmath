There is a convex $n$-sided polygon $(n \geqslant 4)$ with all vertices colored in red, green, and blue, where all three colors appear, and any two adjacent vertices are of different colors. Prove that it is possible to divide the polygon into $n-2$ triangles using non-intersecting diagonals within the $n$-sided polygon, such that the three vertices of each triangle are of different colors.

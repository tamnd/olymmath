Given that $a, b, c, k$ are all greater than 0, and
$$
\begin{array}{l}
2 a b c + k\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)=k^{3} . \\
\text { Prove: } \sqrt{\frac{(k-a)(k-b)}{(k+a)(k+b)}}+\sqrt{\frac{(k-b)(k-c)}{(k+b)(k+c)}}+ \\
\sqrt{\frac{(k-c)(k-a)}{(k+c)(k+a)}}=1 .
\end{array}
$$

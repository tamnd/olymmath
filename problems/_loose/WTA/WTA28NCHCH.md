Let $ z_1,z_2,z_3\in\mathbb{C} $ such that
$\text{(i)} \left|z_1\right| = \left|z_2\right| = \left|z_3\right| = 1$
$\text{(ii)} z_1+z_2+z_3\neq 0 $
$\text{(iii)} z_1^2 +z_2^2+z_3^2 =0. $

Show that $ \left| z_1^3+z_2^3+z_3^3\right| = 1. $

In our region, it rains on average only one day out of three. Meteorologists, due to their inherent pessimism, are wrong in their forecasts half the time when the weather is good, but only one in five times when it is rainy.

Every morning, Francine leaves home for the whole day. If she leaves her umbrella at home on a rainy day, she will get wet, and if there is no rain and she takes the umbrella, she will have to carry it around for no reason, although she considers the latter to be half as unpleasant as the former.

"Should I," she naturally asks herself, "listen to the radio every morning and take my umbrella only when the forecast says it's necessary? Or would it be better to take my umbrella every day or, conversely, never take it at all?"

What would you advise Francine?

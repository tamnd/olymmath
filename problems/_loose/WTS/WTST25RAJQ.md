Three cones with vertex $A$ touch each other externally, and the first two are identical, while the angle at the vertex of the third cone is $2 \arcsin \frac{1}{4}$. Each of the cones touches the fourth cone internally, with the vertex at point $A$. Find the angle at the vertex of the first two cones, if it is half the angle at the vertex of the fourth cone. (The angle at the vertex of a cone is defined as the angle between its generators in the axial section.)

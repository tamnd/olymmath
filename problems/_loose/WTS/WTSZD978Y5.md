A XVI. national secondary school student competition II. problem. (L. 1. o)

For a certain circle, we draw a line $e$ through its center, and from a point $P$ outside the circle on this line, we draw tangents to the circle and find the angle bisectors of the tangents and the line $e$. What will be the geometric place of the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from the center of the circle to these angle bisectors, as the point $P$ moves along the part of the line $e$ that lies outside the circle?

The numbers from 1 to $n$ are written on the board. Bob has the right to perform the following action: as long as possible, he erases three distinct numbers $a, b, c$ written such that 3 divides $a+2b$ and $a-c$, and writes on the board $a+b+c$. Can he end up with only one number written in the case where:

$-n=28?$

$-n=27?$

$-n=29?$

Example: If the numbers $1,1,7$ are written, Bob can erase $(1,1,7)$ and rewrite $1+1+7=9$ because $1+2=3$ and $1-7=-6$ are divisible by 3. If the numbers $1,2,1$ are written, he cannot erase $(1,2,1)$ because $1+2 \times 2=5$ is not divisible by 3.

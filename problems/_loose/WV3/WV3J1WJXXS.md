Let $r$ be a rational number. Show that $y=x^{r}$ is convex if $r$ is greater than one or negative; concave if $0<r<1$.

Show that the $r$-th power mean increases as $r$ increases. The geometric mean lies between the $r$-th and $-r$-th power means.

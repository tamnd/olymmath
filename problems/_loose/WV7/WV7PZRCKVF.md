(Equivalent definition of uniform integrability; see definition 4 from V1.I.6.4.) Prove that

$$
\sup _{n} \mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right| I\left(\left|\xi_{n}\right|>c\right) \rightarrow 0 \Leftrightarrow \varlimsup_{n} \mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right| I\left(\left|\xi_{n}\right|>c\right) \rightarrow 0, c \rightarrow \infty
$$

under the condition of finiteness of $\mathrm{E}\left|\xi_{n}\right|, n \geqslant 1$.

Let $m$ be a positive integer greater than 1, and the function $f(x)=\left(\frac{2 x}{x^{2}+1}\right)^{m}$.
(1) When $x \in\left[0, \frac{m-1}{m}\right]$, find the maximum value of $f(x)$;
(2) Prove that for any $x \in\left[0, \frac{m-1}{m}\right]$, $\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{2 m}} f(x)<1$.

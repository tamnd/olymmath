The internal angle bisectors of $\triangle ABC$ intersect its circumcircle at $A', B', C'$, intersect its three sides at $D, E, F$, and intersect the three sides of $\triangle A'B'C'$ at $D', E', F'$.
Prove: (1) $\frac{A'D}{AD'} + \frac{B'E}{BE} + \frac{C'F}{CF} \geqslant 3$;
(2) $\frac{AD'}{A'D} + \frac{BE'}{B'E} + \frac{CF'}{C'F} \geqslant 3$.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $H_{A}$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$. We denote $M_{B}$ and $M_{C}$ as the midpoints of $[AC]$ and $[AB]$, respectively. We then denote $Z$ as the intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $BM_{B}H_{A}$ and $CM_{C}H_{A}$. We denote $M$ as the midpoint of $[M_{B}M_{C}]$. Show that $H_{A}$, $M$, and $Z$ are collinear.

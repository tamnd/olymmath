Let $A B C D$ be a quadrilateral; we call $E$ the intersection (distinct from $A$) of the circles with diameters $A B$ and $A C$, and $F$ the intersection (also distinct from $A$) of the circles with diameters $A C$ and $A D$. Prove that:

(a) if $E \widehat{A} D=90^{\circ}$ then $B C$ is parallel to $A D$

(b) if $E \widehat{A} D=F \widehat{A} B=90^{\circ}$ then $A B C D$ is a parallelogram

(c) if $A B C D$ is a parallelogram then $E \widehat{A} D=F \widehat{A} B=90^{\circ}$.

Flocks of Sheep. Four brothers decided to count their sheep. It turned out that Claude had ten more sheep than Dan. If Claude gave a quarter of his sheep to Ben, Claude and Adam together would have as many sheep as Ben and Dan together. If then Adam gave one third to Ben, Ben would give a quarter of his sheep to Claude, who would then give a fifth of his sheep to Dan, and Ben would then divide a quarter of his sheep equally between Adam, Claude, and Dan, each would end up with an equal number of sheep. How many sheep did each have?

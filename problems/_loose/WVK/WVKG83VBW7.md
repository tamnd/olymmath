Show that if $C \neq 0$ and $A$ and $B$ do not vanish simultaneously, then

$$
A x\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)-\left(A^{2}-B^{2}\right) x y=C
$$

is the equation of a rectangular hyperbola whose asymptotes are

$$
A x+B y=0, B x-A y=0 .
$$

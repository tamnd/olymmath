There are $2n$ beakers and $k < n$ chemical substances. In these beakers, we, in aggregate, have $2k$ grams of each substance and the weight of every substance in each beaker is a non-negative real number. Find the smallest value of $s$ such that we can find $s$ beaker(s) that in aggregate contains at least $2$ grams of each substance.

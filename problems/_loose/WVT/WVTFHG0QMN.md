In the kingdom of Olympia, there are 100 cities, and each pair of them is connected by exactly one one-way road. It turned out that not from every city can one travel to any other city while adhering to the traffic rules. Prove that the king can choose one of the cities and, by changing the directions of all roads entering and exiting it, achieve that from every city to every other city one can travel while adhering to the traffic rules.

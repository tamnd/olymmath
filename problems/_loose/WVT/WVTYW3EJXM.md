Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ satisfying $A B=B C, A D=D C$. $E$ is a point on line segment $A B$, and $F$ is a point on line segment $A D$, such that points $B, E, F, D$ are concyclic. Construct $\triangle D P E$ to be directly similar to $\triangle A D C$, and construct $\triangle B Q F$ to be directly similar to $\triangle A B C$. Prove that points $A, P, Q$ are collinear.

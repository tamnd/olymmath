In the acute triangle $ABC$, with $AB < AC$, $AD$ is the altitude, and $AE$ is the angle bisector, where $D, E \in (BC)$. In the acute triangle $A'B'C'$, with $A'B' < A'C'$, $A'D'$ is the altitude, and $A'E'$ is the angle bisector, where $D', E' \in (B'C')$. It is known that $[AB] \equiv [A'B']$, $[AD] \equiv [A'D']$, and $[AE] \equiv [A'E']$. Prove that triangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ are congruent.

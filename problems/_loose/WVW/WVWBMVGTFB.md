Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid with $A B \| C D$, which satisfies the following conditions:

(1) The lengths of the sides $A B$ and $C D$ are in the ratio $5: 4$.

(2) The midline of the trapezoid has a length of $5.4 \mathrm{~cm}$.

(3) The height of the trapezoid is half the length of the side $A B$.

Determine whether the area of the trapezoid $A B C D$ is uniquely determined by these conditions. If so, give the area of the trapezoid in square centimeters!

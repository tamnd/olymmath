Let $A, B, C$ be fixed points in the plane. A man starts from a certain point $P_{0}$ and walks directly to $A$. At $A$ he turns his direction by $60^{\circ}$ to the left and walks to $P_{1}$ such that $P_{0} A=A P_{1}$. After he performs the same action 1986 times successively around the points $A, B, C, A, B, C, \ldots$, he returns to the starting point. Prove that $A B C$ is an equilateral triangle, and that the vertices $A, B, C$ are arranged counterclockwise.

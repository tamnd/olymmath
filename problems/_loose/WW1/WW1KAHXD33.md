If $a,b>0$ then prove:
$$\left(\frac{a+b}2-\frac{2ab}{a+b}\right)\operatorname{arctan}\left(\sqrt{ab}\right)+\left(\frac{2ab}{a+b}-\sqrt{ab}\right)\operatorname{arctan}\left(\frac{a+b}2\right)+\left(\sqrt{ab}-\frac{2ab}{a+b}\right)\operatorname{arctan}\left(\sqrt{\frac{a^2+b^2}2}\right)\ge0$$

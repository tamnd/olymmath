A. For any real number $x$, let $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$, $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$, and $\lceil x\rceil$ denote the smallest integer not less than the real number $x$. If $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{m}$ $\left(0 \leqslant x_{1}<x_{2}<\cdots<x_{m} \leqslant 10\right)$ are all the real numbers between 0 and 10 that satisfy the equation $\lfloor x\rfloor\{x\}\lceil x\rceil=1$, find the value of $x_{1}+$ $x_{2}+\cdots+x_{m}$.

In a competition, there were 30 shooters. Each competitor shot 16 times at a target divided into two parts, A and B. If a competitor hits part A, they score 10 points, and if they hit part B, they score 5 points. At the end of the competition, it was determined that the number of hits in part B was greater than half of the total number of shots fired, and that the total number of misses was equal to the total number of hits in part A.

Prove that at least two competitors scored the same number of points.

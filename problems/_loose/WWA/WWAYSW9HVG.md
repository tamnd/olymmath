Pirajuba has 10 cities, named $H_{1}, H_{2}, \ldots, H_{10}$, and some of them are connected by two-way roads. It is known that it is possible to travel from $H_{1}$ to $H_{10}$. Show that one of the following situations occurs:

(i) There exists a path connecting $H_{1}$ to $H_{10}$ using at most 3 roads.

(ii) There are 2 cities $H_{i}$ and $H_{j}, 2 \leq i<j \leq 9$, such that every path connecting $H_{1}$ to $H_{10}$ passes through $H_{i}$ or $H_{j}$.

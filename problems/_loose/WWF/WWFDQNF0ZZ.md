Inside the right angle $K L M$, a point $P$ is taken. Circle $S_{1}$ with center $O_{1}$ touches the sides $L K$ and $L P$ of angle $K L P$ at points $A$ and $D$ respectively, and circle $S_{2}$ with center $O_{2}$ of the same radius touches the sides of angle $M L P$, and side $L P$ at point $B$. It turns out that point $O_{1}$ lies on segment $A B$. Let $C$ be the point of intersection of the lines $O_{2} D$ and $K L$. Prove that $B C$ is the bisector of angle $A B D$.

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
(x-p)^{2}=16(y-3+p) \\
y^{2}+\left(\frac{x-3}{|x|-3}\right)^{2}=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

has solutions and solve it for each of the found values of $p$.

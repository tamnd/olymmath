In acute triangle $ABC$, the lines tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $D$. Let $E$ and $F$ be points on $CA$ and $CB$ such that $DECF$ forms a parallelogram. Given that $AB = 20$, $CA=25$ and $\tan C = 4\sqrt{21}/17$, the value of $EF$ may be expressed as $m/n$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Compute $m+n$.

Let $C, B, E$ be three points on a straight line $l$ in that order. Suppose that $A$ and $D$ are two points on the same side of $l$ such that
(i) $\angle A C E=\angle C D E=90^{\circ}$ and
(ii) $\mathrm{CA}=\mathrm{CB}=\mathrm{CD}$.
Let $F$ be the point of intersection of the segment $A B$ and the circumcircle of $\triangle A D C$. Prove that $F$ is the incentre of $\triangle C D E$.

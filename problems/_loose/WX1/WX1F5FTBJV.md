Given the parabola $C: y^{2}=4 x$ with focus $F$, and $M$ is a point on the circle $\Gamma:(x-3)^{2}+y^{2}=8$. A circle $\odot F$ is constructed with $F$ as the center and $F M$ as the radius. The line $l$ is tangent to $\odot F$ at point $M$, and intersects the parabola $C$ at points $A$ and $B$. Lines $l_{1}$ and $l_{2}$ (different from line $l$) pass through points $A$ and $B$ respectively and are tangent to $\odot F$. Prove: $l_{1} \parallel l_{2}$.

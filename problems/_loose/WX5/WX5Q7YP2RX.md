Four circles $O_{l}(i=1,2,3,4)$ touch a certain circle 0 externally (internally) in sequence.

Prove that the segments $t_{l k}$ of the common external tangents of each pair of circles $O_{l}$ are related by the equation:

$$
t_{12} \cdot t_{34}+t_{23} \cdot t_{41}=t_{13} \cdot t_{24}
$$

Verify the validity of this equation for the case when the four given circles touch a line and are located on the same side of the line.

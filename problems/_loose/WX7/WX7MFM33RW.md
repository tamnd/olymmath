+ Prove that the following statements are equivalent for a graph $G$:
(i) $\chi(G) \leqslant k$;
(ii) $G$ has an orientation such that there is no directed path of length $k$;
(iii) $G$ has an acyclic orientation (i.e., an orientation that does not contain directed cycles).

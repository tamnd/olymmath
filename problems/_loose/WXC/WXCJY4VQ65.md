Let $X=X_{t}(\omega)$ and $Y=Y_{t}(\omega),(\omega, t) \in \Omega \times[0,1]-\mathscr{F} \times \mathscr{B}[0,1]$-measurable functions, and $\int_{0}^{1}\left|X_{t}\right| d t, \int_{0}^{1}\left|Y_{t}\right| d t<\infty$ a.s. Suppose that

$$
\left(X_{t_{1}}, \ldots, X_{t_{n}}\right) \text { and } \left(Y_{t_{1}}, \ldots, Y_{t_{n}}\right) \text { are independent }
$$

for any $t_{1}, \ldots, t_{n} \in[0,1](n \geqslant 1)$. Prove that then the quantities $\int_{0}^{1} X_{t} d t$ and $\int_{0}^{1} Y_{t} d t$ are also independent.

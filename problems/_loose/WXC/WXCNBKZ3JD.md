Given is an isosceles triangle $ABC$ such that $|AC| = |BC|$, and a point $D$ on the side $\overline{AB}$ such that $|AD| < |BD|$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from point $D$ to sides $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{BC}$, respectively. The perpendicular bisector of segment $\overline{PQ}$ intersects $\overline{CP}$ at point $E$. The circumcircles of triangles $ABC$ and $PQC$ intersect at points $C$ and $F$.

If points $E$, $F$, and $Q$ are collinear, prove that $\angle ACB$ is a right angle.

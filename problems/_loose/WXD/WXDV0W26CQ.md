Given a sequence of $2 n+1$ numbers such that each member of the sequence is either 1 or -1. Can the numbers in the sequence be divided into two groups (each member of the sequence belongs to only one group) such that the sum of the numbers in one group is equal to the sum of the numbers in the other group.

In Proposition 8.1.1, to prove the existence of a spanning tree, we use Zorn's Lemma "from below" to find a maximal acyclic subgraph. For finite graphs, we can also use induction "from above" to find a minimal connected spanning subgraph. What would happen if we used Zorn's Lemma "from above" to find such a subgraph?
For the following two exercises, considering the cycle space of the given graph (as defined for finite graphs in Section 1.9) might be helpful in solving the problem.

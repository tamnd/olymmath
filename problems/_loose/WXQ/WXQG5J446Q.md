Positive real numbers $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$, for any $n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}$, satisfy
$$
[\alpha n][\beta n]=[\gamma n][\delta n],
$$

and $\{\alpha, \beta\} \neq\{\gamma, \delta\}$. Prove: $\alpha \beta=\gamma \delta$, and $\alpha, \gamma, \delta$ are positive integers.

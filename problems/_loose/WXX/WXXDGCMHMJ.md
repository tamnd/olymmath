In a table of size $m \times n$, numbers are written such that for any two rows and any two columns, the sum of the numbers in the two opposite vertices of the rectangle they form is equal to the sum of the numbers in the other two vertices. Some numbers have been erased, but the remaining numbers can be used to restore the erased ones. Prove that at least $(n+m-1)$ numbers remain.

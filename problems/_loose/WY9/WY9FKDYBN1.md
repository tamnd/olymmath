Given the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{8}+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1$ with the right vertex $C$, and $A$ as any point on the ellipse in the first quadrant. The point $A$'s symmetric point with respect to the origin is $B$. A vertical line is drawn from point $A$ to the $x$-axis, intersecting $B C$ at point $D$. Compare $|A C|^{2}$ with $|C D||C B|$, and provide a proof.

Let $x, y$, and $z$ be real numbers that satisfy the following system:
$$
\begin{aligned}
x+3 y+6 z & =1 \\
x y+2 x z+6 y z & =-8 \\
x y z & =2
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the smallest possible value of $x+y+z$.

In the coordinate plane, from any arbitrary point, in one step, we can either move up or down by twice its abscissa, or move right or left by twice its ordinate. After any step, we cannot immediately step back.

Prove that if we start from the point $(1, \sqrt{2})$, we cannot return to it in a finite number of steps.

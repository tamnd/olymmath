Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\omega$ its circumcircle. Let $S$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ not containing $A$. Let $X \in AB$ and $Y \in AC$ such that $XY \parallel BC$. We take $P$ (resp. $Q$), the second intersection of $SX$ (resp. $SY$) with $\omega$ and $R = PQ \cap XY$.

Show that $AR$ is tangent to $\omega$.

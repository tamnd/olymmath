(2008 National Team Mathematics Training Question) Let $A=\left\{x \mid x \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right.$, in the decimal representation of $x$, every digit is non-zero, and $S(x) \mid x\}$, where $S(x)$ denotes the sum of the digits of $x$.
Prove that for any positive integer $k$, there exists a positive integer in $A$ that has exactly $k$ digits.

Given that $x$ and $y$ are positive real numbers, let $M=\frac{x}{2 x+y}+\frac{y}{x+2 y}$, $N=\frac{x}{x+2 y}+\frac{y}{2 x+y}$. If there exists a constant $t$ such that the maximum value of $M$ is $t$, and the minimum value of $N$ is also $t$,
(1) Guess the value of $t$,
(2) Prove that your guess is correct.

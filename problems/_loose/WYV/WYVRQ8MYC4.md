Perhaps on the beach (one chance in two) or on the tennis court (one chance in four), or maybe in a café (also one chance in four). If Béatrice is on the beach, which is large and crowded, François has one chance in two of not finding her. François searched all three possible meeting places, but he did not find Béatrice.

What is the probability that Béatrice was on the beach, assuming she did not change locations while François was looking for her?

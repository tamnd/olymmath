Through a point $M$, not lying on the sides or their extensions of triangle $A B C$, a secant is drawn, intersecting the sides of this triangle (or their extensions) at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ respectively. Prove the validity of the equality:

$$
\frac{(A B M)}{M \bar{C}_{1}}+\frac{(B C M)}{M A_{1}}+\frac{(C A M)}{M B_{1}}=0
$$

where areas and segments are assumed to be oriented.

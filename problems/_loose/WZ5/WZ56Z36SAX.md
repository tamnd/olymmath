The tangents to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $B$ and $C$ meet at $X$. The line through $H_{1}$ perpendicular to $XH_{1}$ intersects $AB$ and $AC$ at points $Y$ and $Z$, respectively. Prove:
$$
\angle YXB = \angle ZXC .{ }^{[2]}

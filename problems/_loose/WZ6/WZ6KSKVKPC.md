Let $a, b$ be real numbers, and the equation $x^{2}+a x+b=0$ has two real roots. Prove: there exists an integer $n$, such that
$$
\left|n^{2}+a n+b\right| \leqslant \max \left\{\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{a^{2}-4 b}\right\} .
$$

Given a cyclic quadrilateral $A B C D$ with a right angle $A D B$. Through point $C$, a line $l \| A D$ is drawn, and a point $F$ is marked on it such that the angle $B A F$ is equal to the acute angle between the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$, and $F$ and $C$ are on opposite sides of $A B$. Point $X$ is such that $F X C A$ is a parallelogram. Prove that point $X$ lies on $B D$.

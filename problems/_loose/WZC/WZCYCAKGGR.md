Consider the quadrilateral $A B C D$ with non-perpendicular diagonals and sides $A B$ and $C D$ not parallel. Let $O$ be the intersection point of the diagonals, $H_{1}$ the orthocenter of triangle $A O B$, and $H_{2}$ the orthocenter of triangle $C O D$. Denote by $M$ the midpoint of side $[A B]$ and by $N$ the midpoint of side $[C D]$. Show that $H_{1} H_{2}$ is parallel to $M N$ if and only if $A C = B D$.

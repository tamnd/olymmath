In a canteen, there are $100$ apples of total weight $10$ kg, each apple weighs not less than $25$ g. The barman has to cut them into some pieces and distribute these pieces among $100$ students so that each student receives exactly $100$ g. Prove that the barman can attain this aim in such a way that the weight of each piece is at least $25$ g.

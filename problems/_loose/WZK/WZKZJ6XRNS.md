If $x, y$, $z$ are real numbers in the interval $\langle-1,1\rangle$ satisfying the condition $x y+y z+z x=1$, then

$$
6 \sqrt[3]{\left(1-x^{2}\right)\left(1-y^{2}\right)\left(1-z^{2}\right)} \leqq 1+(x+y+z)^{2} .
$$

Prove and determine when equality holds.

In a Mathematical Olympiad competition, students have to take four parts of the exam: algebra, geometry, number theory, and combinatorics. It is known that in any part of the exam, all candidates' scores are pairwise distinct. Find the smallest positive integer $n$, such that among any $n$ students, there exist 10 students who can be arranged in a row, such that the scores in two parts are simultaneously in increasing order.

All pairs of three-digit natural numbers $(x ; y)$ are sought that have the following properties:

1. The numbers of a pair differ in the last two digits exactly by the order of the digits.
2. The difference $x-y$ is 18.
3. Each of the two numbers of a pair is the product of exactly two two-digit prime numbers.

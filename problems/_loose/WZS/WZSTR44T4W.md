In triangle $ ABC$ , $ \overline{CA} = \overline{CB}$ . On $ CB$ square $ BCDE$ is constructed away from the triangle. If $ x$ is the number of degrees in angle $ DAB$ , then

$ \textbf{(A)}\ x\text{ depends upon triangle }ABC \textbf{(B)}\ x\text{ is independent of the triangle}$
$ \textbf{(C)}\ x\text{ may equal angle }CAD \textbf{(D)}\ x\text{ can never equal angle }CAB$
$ \textbf{(E)}\ x\text{ is greater than }45^{\circ}\text{ but less than }90^{\circ}$

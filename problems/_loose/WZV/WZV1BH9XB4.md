In parallelogram $A B C D, M$ is the midpoint of side $[D C], B M \cap A D=\{N\}, C N \cap A B=\{P\}$,

$B M \cap A C=\{T\}$.

a) Prove that BDNC and BDCP are parallelograms;

b) Prove that points $D, T, P$ are collinear.

On the sides of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, points $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ are taken such that the segments $A A_{1}$, $B B_{1}$, and $C C_{1}$ intersect at point $H$. Prove that $A H \cdot A_{1} H = B H \cdot B_{1} H = C H \cdot C_{1} H$ if and only if $H$ is the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$.

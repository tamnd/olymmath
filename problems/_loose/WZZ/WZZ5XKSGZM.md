In $\triangle A B C$, the angle bisector of $\angle C$ intersects side $A B$ and the circumcircle of the triangle at $D, K$, and $I$ is the incenter of $\triangle A B C$.
Prove: (1) $\frac{1}{I D}-\frac{1}{I K}=\frac{1}{I C}$; (2) $\frac{I C}{I D}-\frac{I D}{D K}=1$.
(1988 "Friendship Cup" International Competition Problem)

$ $ $\star$ There are three equal circles $\odot A, \odot B, \odot C$ that are pairwise externally tangent, and there is a smaller circle $\odot O$ that is externally tangent to all of them. On $\odot O$, take any point $P$ and draw tangents to $\odot A, \odot B, \odot C$. Prove: The sum of the lengths of any two of these tangents equals the length of the third tangent.

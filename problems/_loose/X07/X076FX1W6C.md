For each natural number $n$, let $f(n)$ be the number of different possible choices of signs + and - for which $\pm 1 \pm 2 \pm \ldots \pm n=0$. Prove that:

(a) $f(n)=0$ for $n \equiv 1,2(\bmod 4)$;

(b) $f(n) \geqslant 2^{\frac{n}{2}-1}$ for $n \equiv 0,3(\bmod 4)$.

We say a positive integer $n$ is lovely if there exist a positive integer $k$ and (not necessarily distinct) positive integers $d_{1}, d_{2}, \ldots, d_{k}$ such that $n=d_{1} d_{2} \ldots d_{k}$ and

$$
d_{i}^{2} \mid n+d_{i}
$$

for all $i \in\{1, \ldots, k\}$.

(a) Are there infinitely many lovely numbers?

(b) Does there exist a lovely number greater than 1 which is a square of an integer?

(Ivan Novak)

Find all triples $a, b, c$ of natural numbers for which the following conditions hold simultaneously:

$$
n(a b, c)=2^{8}, n(b c, a)=2^{9}, n(c a, b)=2^{11}
$$

where $n(x, y)$ denotes the least common multiple of the natural numbers $x$ and $y$.

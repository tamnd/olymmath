Given 200 different real numbers. We divide the numbers into two groups and sort the elements in each group in ascending order. Thus, we get the sequences $a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{100}$ and $b_{1}<b_{2}<\ldots<b_{100}$. From these same numbers, by forming two new sorted groups of a hundred, we get the sequences $a_{1}^{\prime}<a_{2}^{\prime}<\ldots<a_{100}^{\prime}$ and $b_{1}^{\prime}<b_{2}^{\prime}<\ldots<b_{100}^{\prime}$.

Prove that

$$
\left|a_{1}-a_{1}^{\prime}\right|+\left|a_{2}-a_{2}^{\prime}\right|+\ldots+\left|a_{100}-a_{100}^{\prime}\right|=\left|b_{1}-b_{1}^{\prime}\right|+\left|b_{2}-b_{2}^{\prime}\right|+\ldots+\left|b_{100}-b_{100}^{\prime}\right|
$$

Given three pairwise non-intersecting circles. Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ be the three points of intersection of the common internal tangents to any two of them, and $B_{1}, B_{2}$, $B_{3}$ be the corresponding points of intersection of the external tangents. Prove that these points lie on four lines, three on each ( $A_{1}, A_{2}, B_{3} ; A_{1}, B_{2}, A_{3} ; B_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3} ; B_{1}$, $\left.B_{2}, B_{3}\right)$).

In a triangle $A B C$ with $A B \neq A C$, let $D$ be the projection of $A$ onto $B C$. Furthermore, let $E, F$ be the midpoints of the lines $A D$ and $B C$ respectively and let $G$ be the projection of $B$ onto $A F$. Show that the line $E F$ is the tangent at point $F$ to the circumcircle of the triangle $G F C$.

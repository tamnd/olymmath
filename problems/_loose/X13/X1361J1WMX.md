Given a regular pentagon $A B C D E$ with center $M$. Let $P$ be a point on the segment $E A$, and $Q$ be a point on the segment $A B$. Show:

If there exists a circumcircle for the quadrilateral $M P Q A$, then the sum of the segments $P A$ and $A Q$ is equal to the side length of the pentagon.

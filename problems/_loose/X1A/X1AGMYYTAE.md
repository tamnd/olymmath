A secant line passing through the vertex of a triangle divides it into two triangles, each inscribed in a circle. Prove that if the radii of these circles are equal, then the segment of the secant, enclosed between the vertex and the common external tangent of these circles, is equal to the difference between the semiperimeter of the triangle and its side, which is the segment of the second external tangent of the given circles.

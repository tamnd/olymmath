Define the degree of $n$ as $\operatorname{deg}(n)=\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\ldots+\alpha_{n}$, where $n$ has the standard factorization $n=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} \cdot p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}} \ldots p_{k}^{\alpha_{k}}$, with $p_{i}$ being distinct prime numbers and $a_{i}$ being positive integers.

Try to prove: there exist 2018 consecutive positive integers, among which exactly 1000 numbers have a degree less than 11.

In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AB < AC$. The perpendicular bisector of side $BC$ intersects lines $AB$ and $AC$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$, and $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of segments $BC$ and $PQ$, respectively. Prove that the intersection of lines $HM$ and $AN$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$. ${ }^{[2]}$
(2019, Balkan Mathematical Olympiad)

A set $T$ is called a mischievous set: if for any $u, v \in T$ (where $u, v$ do not have to be distinct), $u+v \notin T$. Prove:
(1) Any mischievous subset of $\{1,2, \cdots, 2006\}$ has at most 1003 elements.
(2) Every set $S$ consisting of 2006 positive integers must contain a mischievous subset with 669 elements.

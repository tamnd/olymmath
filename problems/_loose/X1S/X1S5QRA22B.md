In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, the vertices of $\triangle A B C$ are $B(-1,0)$, $C(1,0)$, and $A$ is a moving point. $H$ and $G$ are the orthocenter and centroid of $\triangle A B C$, respectively. If the midpoint $K$ of segment $H G$ lies on the line $B C$:
(1) Find the equation of the trajectory $E$ of point $A$;
(2) A line passing through point $C$ with a slope of 1 intersects the curve $E$ at point $D$. If there exist two points $P$ and $Q$ on the curve $E$ such that points $P$, $C$, $Q$, and $D$ are concyclic, find the minimum value of the area of the circumcircle of quadrilateral $P C Q D$.

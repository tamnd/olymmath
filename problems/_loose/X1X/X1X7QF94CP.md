Let $ f$ be a linear transformation denoted by $ A = \left( \begin{array}{cc} a & b \\
c & d \end{array} \right)$ and $ g$ by $ B = \left( \begin{array}{cc} a & c \\
b & d \end{array} \right)$.

(1) Show that $ f(\overrightarrow{u})\cdot \overrightarrow{v} = \overrightarrow{u}\cdot g(\overrightarrow{v})$ for any vectots $ \overrightarrow{u},\ \overrightarrow{v}$.

(2) Suppose that the line $ l$ passing through the origin $ O$ is transformed to itself by $ f$.Take a point $ P\neq O$ on $ l$, let $ Q=f(P)$ and $ R=g(P)$. Show that either (a) or (b) of the following contexts holds.

(a) $ Q = R$
(b) 3points $ Q,\ R,\ O$ become the vertices of a right triangle.

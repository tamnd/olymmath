In the triangle $ABC=H$, the ratio of its angles is $1:2:4$. Let the pedal triangle of this triangle be $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}=T_{1}$, and the pedal triangle of $T_{1}$ be $A_{2}B_{2}C_{2}=T_{2}$. Prove that the sides of $T_{2}$ are parallel to the sides of $H$! We call $T_{2}$ the second pedal triangle of $H$. Similarly, we call the pedal triangle of $T_{2}$, $T_{3}$, the third pedal triangle of $H$; the pedal triangle of $T_{3}$, $T_{4}$, the fourth pedal triangle of $H$, and so on. Show that

1) any pedal triangle of $H$ of any order is similar to $H$;
2) starting from the triangle $DEF=G$ where the ratio of the angles is $1:1:8$, no pedal triangle is similar to $G$.

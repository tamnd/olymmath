Two pirates decided to bury their treasure near a river. They marked an oak tree $A$ and a beech tree $B$ to the west of it. Then, on the same side of the riverbank, they drove a stake $K$ not too far from the two trees. Then, from $K$, one of the pirates headed straight for $B$, turned right at $90^{\circ}$, and walked the same distance from $B$ to $Q$ as the stake was from $K$. The other pirate did the same, but he walked the $K A=A P$ path, turning left at $90^{\circ}$ at $A$. Then they walked towards each other on the line $P Q$, and buried the treasure exactly halfway between $P$ and $Q$. A few months later, they returned, but to their great surprise, they found that the stake was no longer in place. Can they still find the treasure?

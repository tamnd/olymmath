A square $17 \times 17$ is cut out from a grid paper. The cells of the square are arbitrarily filled with the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 70$, with one and only one number in each cell.

Prove that there exist four different cells with centers at points $A, B, C, D$ such that $A B=C D, A D=B C$ and the sum of the numbers in the cells with centers at $A$ and $C$ is equal to the sum of the numbers in the cells with centers at $B$ and $D$.

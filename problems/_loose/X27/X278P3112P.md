Consider an isosceles right triangle $ABC$ with hypotenuse $BC$. Describe a semicircle around it with endpoints $B$ and $C$; then draw an arc inside triangle $ABC$ that is tangent to side $AB$ at point $B$ and to side $AC$ at point $C$. The area of the region enclosed between this arc and the described semicircle is equal to the area of the original triangle. Why?

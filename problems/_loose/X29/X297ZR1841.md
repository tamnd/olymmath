It is known about the functions $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ that $p(0)=q(0)>0$ and $p^{\prime}(x) \sqrt{q^{\prime}(x)}=\sqrt{2}$ for any $x \in[0 ; 1]$. Prove that if $x \in[0 ; 1]$, then $p(x)+2 q(x)>3 x$.

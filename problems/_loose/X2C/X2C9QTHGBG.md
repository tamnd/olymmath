For which values of $\gamma$ is the following statement true: If one of the angles of a triangle is $\gamma$, then

$$
\frac{T}{\sqrt{a^{2} b^{2}-4 T^{2}}}+\frac{T}{\sqrt{b^{2} c^{2}-4 T^{2}}}+\frac{T}{\sqrt{c^{2} a^{2}-4 T^{2}}} \geq \frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

where $a, b, c$ are the sides of the triangle, and $T$ is the area of the triangle.

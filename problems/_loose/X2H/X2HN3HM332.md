An isosceles trapezium $ABCD$ is given , such that $AB$ is the longest base.
Let $I$ be the incenter of of $\triangle ABC$ ,and $J$ be the $C$-excenter of $\triangle ACD$ .
Prove that $IJ \parallel AB$.

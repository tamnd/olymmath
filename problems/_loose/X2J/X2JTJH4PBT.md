Let three lines $a, b, c$ be given such that $a \| b$ and $c \perp a$. Let point $A$ be the intersection of lines $a$ and $c$, and point $B$ be the intersection of lines $b$ and $c$. Let point $C$ be on line $b$ and point $D$ be on segment $\overline{A B}$ such that $|\angle D C A|=2 \cdot|\angle B C D|$, and let point $E$ be the intersection of lines $a$ and $C D$. Prove that $|D E|=2 \cdot|A C|$.

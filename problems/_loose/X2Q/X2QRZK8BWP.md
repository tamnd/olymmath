A line $l$ passes through a point $X$ with barycentric coordinates ( $\alpha: \beta: \gamma$ ). Let $d_{\mathrm{a}}, d_{\mathrm{b}}, d_{\mathrm{c}}$ be the signed distances from the vertices $A, B, C$ to the line $l$ (for points lying on opposite sides of the line $l$, the signs are different). Prove that $d_{\mathrm{a}} \alpha+d_{\mathrm{b}} \beta+d_{\mathrm{c}} \gamma=0$.

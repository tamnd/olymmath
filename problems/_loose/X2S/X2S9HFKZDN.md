(England 2003). Let $f$ be a permutation of $\mathbb{N}^{*}$.

a) Prove that there exists an arithmetic progression of three terms $a, a+d, a+2d$, with $d>0$, such that $f(a)<f(a+d)<f(a+2d)$.

b) Does there exist an arithmetic progression of 2004 terms $a, a+d, \ldots, a+2003d$, with $d>0$, such that $f(a)<f(a+d)<\cdots<f(a+2003d)$?

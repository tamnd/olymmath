Quadradágoras was a great admirer of Pythagoras. In his investigations, he discovered two theorems about quadrilaterals:

a) "If a quadrilateral $A B C D$ is such that $\angle A B C=\angle A D C=90^{\circ}$, then $A B^{2}-C D^{2}=A D^{2}-$ $B C^{2}$."

b) "If a quadrilateral $A B C D$ is such that $\angle A C B=\angle A D C=90^{\circ}$, then $A B^{2}=B C^{2}+C D^{2}+$ $A D^{2}$."

Prove these results.

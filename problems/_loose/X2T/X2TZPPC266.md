In an acute triangle $ABC$, a square with side $m$ is inscribed such that two of its vertices lie on side $AB$, and one vertex each on sides $BC$ and $AC$. Let the height of triangle $ABC$ from vertex $C$ be denoted as $h_{c}$, and the side $AB$ as $c$. Prove that $\frac{1}{h_{c}}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{m}$.

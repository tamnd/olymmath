Dwarves go to the stream to fetch water. Each dwarf's bucket has a different volume: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 liters. The dwarves do not lend their buckets to each other and always bring them back full of water.

- Kejchal brings more water in his bucket than Štístko.
- Dřímal would have to go for water three times to bring exactly as much water as Stydlín in one of his buckets.
- Próf's bucket is only 2 liters larger than Štístko's.
- Šmudla himself brings as much water as Dřímal and Štístko together.
- When Prófa and Šmudla go for water, they bring the same amount of water as Rejpal, Kejchal, and Štístko.

How much water will Kejchal and Šmudla bring together?

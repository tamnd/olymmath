Prove that the $p$-arithmetic sum

$$
S=1+2 q+\ldots+P_{2 n}^{n} q^{n}+\ldots+P_{p-1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \frac{p-1}{q^{2}}
$$

equals $0, +1$ or $-1$, and it equals zero only when $q=\frac{1}{4}$.

Now let's establish the connection between Pascal's Triangle and the Fibonacci sequence. This connection, apart from being interesting in itself, will allow us to discover new properties of the Fibonacci sequence.

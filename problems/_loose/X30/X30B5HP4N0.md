Given a triangle $A B C$ with an obtuse angle at vertex $C$. The perpendicular bisector $o_{1}$ of segment $A C$ intersects side $A B$ at point $K$, and the perpendicular bisector $o_{2}$ of segment $B C$ intersects side $A B$ at point $L$. Let the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors $o_{1}$ and $o_{2}$ be $O$. Prove that the center of the incircle of triangle $K L C$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $O K L$.

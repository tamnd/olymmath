Prove that the length of the bisector of a triangle, drawn to the side equal to $a$, can be calculated by the formula

$$
l_{\mathrm{C}}=\frac{2 \sqrt{p(p-a) b c}}{b+c}
$$

where $p=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$.

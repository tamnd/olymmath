Given is an obtuse triangle $\triangle A B C$ where the measures of all angles are expressed in degrees as natural numbers. The angle bisector of the obtuse angle $\angle B A C$ intersects the side $\overline{B C}$ at point $D$, and the foot of the altitude from vertex $A$ is point $N$. Point $D$ is located between points $N$ and $B$. The measure of angle $\angle C B A$ is ten times the measure of angle $\angle D A N$, which is also a natural number when expressed in degrees. What is the largest possible measure of the obtuse angle of this triangle in degrees?

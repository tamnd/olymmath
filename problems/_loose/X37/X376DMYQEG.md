Given an isosceles triangle $A B C$ with base $A C$. The angle $B A C$ is 37 degrees. $X$ is the point of intersection of the altitude from vertex $A$ with the line passing through $B$ parallel to the base, $M$ is a point on the line $A C$ such that $B M=M X$. Find the degree measure of angle $M X B$.

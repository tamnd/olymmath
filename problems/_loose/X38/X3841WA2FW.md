The edge length of the cube $A B C D-A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ is 1. Take a point $M$ on $A_{1} B$ such that $A_{1} M=\frac{1}{3} A_{1} B$, and take a point $N$ on $B_{1} D_{1}$ such that $B_{1} N=\frac{1}{3} B_{1} D_{1}$. Connect $M N$, prove that $M N$ is the common perpendicular of $A_{1} B$ and $B_{1} D_{1}$.

In $\triangle A B C$, if $x \sin A+y \sin B+z \sin C=0$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
(y+z \cos A)(z+x \cos B)(x+y \cos C) \\
+(y \cos A+z)(z \cos B+x)(x \cos C+y)=0 .
\end{array}
$$

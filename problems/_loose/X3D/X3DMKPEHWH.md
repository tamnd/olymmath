Let $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n} \in \mathbb{C}$, and the corresponding points are all on the unit circle. Let $f(z)=\left(z-\alpha_{1}\right)\left(z-\alpha_{2}\right) \cdots(z-\alpha_{n})$. Prove that there exists a complex number $z_{0}$ such that $\left|z_{0}\right|=1,\left|f\left(z_{0}\right)\right| \geqslant 2$.

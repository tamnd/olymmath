Given that a non-negative real number is placed at each vertex of a cube, and the sum of all these numbers is 1. Two players, A and B, play a game: A first selects any face of the cube, B selects another face, and finally, A selects a third face, with the rule that faces parallel to the already selected ones cannot be chosen. Prove that A can always ensure that the number placed at the common vertex of the three selected faces is no greater than $\frac{1}{6}$.

Let $M$ be the set of palindromic numbers of the form $5n+4$, where $n \in \mathbb{N}$. (A natural number is called a palindrome if it is equal to its reverse. For example, the numbers $7, 191, 23532, 3770773$ are palindromic numbers.)

a) If we write the elements of the set $M$ in ascending order, determine which is the 50th number written.

b) Determine the smallest and the largest among the elements of the set $M$ that are written with non-zero digits and have the sum of their digits equal to 2014.

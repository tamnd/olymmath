A two-thousand-digit natural number (where the $a_{i}$ represent Arabic numerals): First write $a_{1}$, then write $a_{2}$, $a_{3}$, ..., and when $a_{i}$ has been written, write $a_{i+1}$ such that the two-digit number $\overline{a_{i} a_{i+1}}$ is a multiple of 17 or 23 $(i=1$, $2, \cdots$, 1999). If, according to the above rule, a two-thousand-digit natural number is written, and among the digits $a_{i}$, the digits 1, 9, 8, 7 all appear, prove that the two-thousand-digit natural number must be a composite number.

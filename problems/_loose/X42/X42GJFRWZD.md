Find three positive integers $a, b, c$ such that
(1) $a < b < c$;
(2) They are pairwise coprime;
(3) The sum of any two numbers is a multiple of the third number.

Prove that the three numbers you find are the only set that can simultaneously satisfy all three conditions.
(Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China Mathematical Competition, 1963)

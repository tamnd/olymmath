(9-11) On the exam, there are three trigonometry problems, two algebra problems, and five geometry problems. Vanya solves trigonometry problems with a probability of $p_{1}=0.2$, geometry problems with a probability of $p_{2}=0.4$, and algebra problems with a probability of $p_{3}=0.5$. To get a grade of three, Vanya needs to solve at least five problems.

a) (1p) What is the probability that Vanya will solve at least five problems?

Vanya decided to focus on problems from one section. In a week, he can increase the probability of solving problems from this section by 0.2.

b) (2p) Which section should Vanya focus on to maximize the probability of solving at least five problems?

c) (2p) Which section should Vasya focus on to maximize the expected number of solved problems?

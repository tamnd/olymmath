In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
$$
\begin{array}{r}
\sin ^{2} A+\sin ^{2} B=1 \\
\Leftrightarrow \frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{1}{b^{2}}=\frac{1}{h_{c}^{2}}
\end{array}
$$
$\Leftrightarrow C=\frac{\pi}{2} $ or $ A-B=\frac{\pi}{2} $ or $ B-A=\frac{\pi}{2}$.

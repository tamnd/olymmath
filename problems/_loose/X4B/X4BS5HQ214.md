Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, where $a_{1}=2$, and $a_{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{a_{n}+2}\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$. Prove:
(1) $a_{n} \leqslant \frac{1}{2^{n-2}}$;
(2) $\frac{2 a_{1}}{a_{1}+2}+\frac{4 a_{2}}{a_{2}+2}+\cdots+\frac{2 n a_{n}}{a_{n}+2}<4$.

Let the lengths of the sides of a cuboid be denoted $ a, b$ and $ c$. Rotate the cuboid in $ 90^\circ$ the side with length $ b$ as the axis of the cuboid. Denote by $ V$ the solid generated by sweeping the cuboid.

(1) Express the volume of $ V$ in terms of $ a,\ b,\ c$.

(2) Find the range of the volume of $ V$ with $ a + b + c = 1$.

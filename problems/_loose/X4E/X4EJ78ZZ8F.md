Several positive numbers, each not greater than 1, are written around a circle. Prove that the circle can be divided into three arcs such that the sum of the numbers written on each arc differs from the sum of the numbers written on any other arc by no more than 1. (If an arc has no numbers, the sum of the numbers written on it is considered to be 0.)

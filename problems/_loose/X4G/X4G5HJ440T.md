A regular hexagonal prism has a base $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{6}$ and a top face $B_{1}, \ldots, B_{6}$, with the center of the latter being O. The side edge $A_{1} B_{1}$ is equal to the diagonal $A_{1} A_{3}$. Take points $C_{1}, C_{3}, C_{5}$ on the side edges $A_{1} B_{1}, A_{3} B_{3}, A_{5} B_{5}$ respectively, such that $A_{1} C_{1}=A_{3} C_{3}=A_{5} C_{5}=A_{1} A_{2}$. Consider the part of the prism below the planes $C_{1} O C_{3}, C_{3} O C_{5}, C_{5} O C_{1}$. Determine the surface area and volume of this part if $A_{1} A_{2}=1$.

On cards, all natural numbers from 1 to $2024^{2}$ are written. The sum of all these numbers is 2024A. Manuel has chosen 2024 cards with numbers whose sum is equal to $A$. Prove that Neva can arrange the remaining cards into 2023 groups such that each group contains 2024 cards and the sum of the numbers on the cards in each group is equal to $A$.

Let $A_1A_2 \dots A_n$ a cyclic $n$ -agon with center $O$ and $P$ , $Q$ being two isogonal conjugates of it (i.e, $\angle PA_{i+1}A_i = \angle QA_{i+1}A_{i+2}$ for all $i$ ). Let $P_i$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle PA_iA_{i+1}$ and $Q_i$ the circumcenter of $\triangle QA_iA_{i+1}$ for all $i$ . Prove that: $a) ~P_1P_2 \dots P_n$ and $Q_1Q_2 \dots Q_n$ are cyclic, with centers $O_P$ and $O_Q$ , respectively. $b)~O, O_P$ and $O_Q$ are collinears. $c)~O_PO_Q \mid \mid PQ.$

Remark: indices are taken modulo $n$ .

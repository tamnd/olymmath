Three given segments $A B, C D, E F$ and three segments of unknown lengths $a, b, c$ should have the following properties:

$A B=a+b=5.6 \text{ cm}; C D=a-b=1.8 \text{ cm}; E F=b+c=6.2 \text{ cm}$.

Draw three such segments $A B, C D, E F$, and determine the unknown lengths $a, b$, and $c$ through a construction (using only a compass and a ruler)!

Explain why your construction yields the unknown lengths $a, b, c$ if they have the required properties!

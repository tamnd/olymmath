(SFRY, 74). For arbitrary $n \geqslant 3$ points $A_{1}$, $A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ on a plane, no three of which lie on the same line, let $\alpha$ denote the smallest of the angles $\angle A_{i} A_{j} A_{k}$ formed by triples $A_{i}, A_{j}, A_{k}$ of distinct points. For each value of $n$, find the greatest value of $\alpha$. Determine for which arrangements of points this value is achieved.

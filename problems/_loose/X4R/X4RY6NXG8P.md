Show that the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{x}{a}+\frac{z}{c}=\lambda\left(1+\frac{y}{b}\right) \ldots \\
& \frac{x}{a}-\frac{z}{c}=\frac{1}{\lambda}\left(1-\frac{y}{b}\right) \ldots \\
& \frac{x}{a}+\frac{z}{c}=\mu\left(1-\frac{y}{b}\right) \ldots \\
& \frac{x}{a}-\frac{z}{c}=\frac{1}{\mu}\left(1+\frac{y}{b}\right) \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

has one and only one solution! Determine this solution!

Note: In equation 4) of the problem, the right-hand side originally featured

$$
\frac{1}{\mu}\left(1-\frac{y}{b}\right)
$$

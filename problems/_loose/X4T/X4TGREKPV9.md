(1985 Beijing Competition Problem) On the line segment $A B$, $2 n$ points are placed symmetrically with respect to its midpoint $M$. Arbitrarily color $n$ of these $2 n$ points red and the other $n$ points blue. Prove: The sum of the distances from all red points to $A$ is equal to the sum of the distances from all blue points to $B$.

Vasya wrote one number on each of 99 cards (among these numbers there could be equal ones) and placed the cards in a circle numbers down. For each pair of adjacent cards, he told Petya what numbers were written on these cards, but did not tell which number was on which card. Could Vasya choose the numbers in such a way that Petya could not definitely determine the number on each card based on this information?

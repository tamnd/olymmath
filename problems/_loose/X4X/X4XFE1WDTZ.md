Anna and Bedřich are playing a card game. Each of them has five cards with values from 1 to 5 (one of each). In each of the five rounds, both lay down one card, and the one with the higher number gets a point. If the cards have the same number, no one gets a point. Used cards are not returned to the game. Whoever has more points at the end wins. What percentage of all possible outcomes of such a game will result in Anna's victory?

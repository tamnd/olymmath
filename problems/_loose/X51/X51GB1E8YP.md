Let's determine the smallest positive value of $a$ for which the equation

$$
\frac{\frac{x-a}{2}+\frac{x-2 a}{3}}{\frac{x+4 a}{5}-\frac{x+3 a}{4}}=\frac{\frac{x-3 a}{4}+\frac{x-4 a}{5}}{\frac{x+2 a}{3}-\frac{x+a}{2}}
$$

has an integer solution for $x$.

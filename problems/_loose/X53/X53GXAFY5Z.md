Given two circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with radii $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$ respectively, where $r_{1}>r_{2}$.

Furthermore, it is assumed that both circles touch each other externally, meaning they have exactly one common internal tangent. This internal tangent intersects one of the common external tangents of both circles at $P$ and the other common tangent at $Q$.

Determine, under these conditions, the length $P Q$ from $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$!

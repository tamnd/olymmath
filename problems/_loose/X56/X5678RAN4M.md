In $\triangle A B C$, the centroid, circumcenter, incenter, and orthocenter are $G, O, I, H$ respectively, the corresponding sides are $a, b, c$, and the circumradius and inradius are $R, r$. Prove:
(1) $O I^{2}=R^{2}-2 R r$;
(2) $O H^{2}=9 R^{2}-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$;
(3) $G I^{2}=r^{2}+\frac{2}{9}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)-\frac{1}{12}(a+b+c)^{2}$;
(4) $H I^{2}=4 R^{2}+2 r^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)$.

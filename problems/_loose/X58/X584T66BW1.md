(2002 Shanghai Mathematics Competition) Given that the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are both arithmetic sequences, $S_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}, T_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}$, and for all positive integers $n, \frac{S_{n}}{T_{n}}=\frac{3 n+31}{31 n+3}$.
(1) Find the value of $\frac{b_{28}}{a_{28}}$;
(2) Find all positive integers $n$ such that $\frac{b_{n}}{a_{n}}$ is an integer.

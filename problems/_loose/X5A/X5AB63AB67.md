Given an orthogonal coordinate system with origin $O$ in the plane. Distinct real numbers are written at the points with integer coordinates. Let $A$ be a nonempty finite set of integer points which is central-symmetric regarding $O$ and $O \notin A$. Prove that there exists an integer point $X$ such that if $A_{X}$ is the image of $A$ under translation defined by $\overrightarrow{O X}$, then at least half of the numbers written at the points of $A_{X}$ are greater than the number written at $X$.

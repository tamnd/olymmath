Let $A, B, C, D$ be four points on a circle in that order. We denote $Q$ as the intersection point of the lines $(A D)$ and $(B C)$, and $P$ as the intersection point of the lines $(A C)$ and $(B D)$. Let $G$ be a point such that the quadrilateral $P C G D$ is a parallelogram.

Show that $\widehat{G Q D}=\widehat{P Q B}$.

Find the number of positive integers $n$ with exactly $1974$ factors such that no prime greater than $40$ divides $n$ , and $n$ ends in one of the digits $1$ , $3$ , $7$ , $9$ . (Note that $1974 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7 \cdot 47$ .)

The product of positive numbers $a, b, c$ and $d$ is 1. Prove the inequality

$$
\frac{a^{4}+b^{4}}{a^{2}+b^{2}}+\frac{b^{4}+c^{4}}{b^{2}+c^{2}}+\frac{c^{4}+d^{4}}{c^{2}+d^{2}}+\frac{d^{4}+a^{4}}{d^{2}+a^{2}} \geqslant 4
$$

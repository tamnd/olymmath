Point $X$ is chosen inside a convex $ABCD$ so that $\angle XBC = \angle XAD, \angle XCB = \angle XDA$ . Rays $AB, DC$ intersect at point $O$ , circumcircles of triangles $BCO, ADO$ intersect at point $T$ . Prove that line $TX$ and the line through $O$ perpendicular to $BC$ intersect on the circumcircle of $\triangle AOD$ .

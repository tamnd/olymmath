If $a, b, c, d$ are four rational numbers, and satisfy the following conditions:
(1) $a+b=c+d$,
(2) $a+db>d>c$.
(B) $d>b>a>c$.
(C) $d>b>c>a$.
(D) $b>d>c>a$.
(Huanggang Region, Hubei Province, China Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

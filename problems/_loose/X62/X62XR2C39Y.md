Let the non-empty subset family $\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}\right\}$ be a partition of the set $M=\{1,2, \cdots, 10\}$, i.e., the non-empty sets $A_{1}$, $A_{2}$, $A_{3}$ are pairwise disjoint, and their union is $M$. If there exists a permutation $i_{1}, i_{2}, i_{3}$ of $1,2,3$ such that
$$
\max A_{i_{k}}>\min A_{i_{k+1}}, k=1,2,3,
$$

where it is defined that $i_{4}=i_{1}$, then $\left\{A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}\right\}$ is called a good partition of $M$. Find the number of all good partitions of $M$.

We call a positive integer $n$ a jackpot number if it has the following property: there exists a positive integer $k$ with at least two digits, all of which are the same (such as 11111 or 888), and for which $n \cdot k$ is again a number where all digits are the same. For example, 3 is a jackpot number because $3 \cdot 222 = 666$.

(a) Determine a 10-digit jackpot number and show that it is indeed a jackpot number.

(b) Show that 11 is not a jackpot number.

(c) Determine whether 143 is a jackpot number and support your answer with a proof.

Let convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ be inscribed in a circle with center $O$ and radius 1 and satisfy $\angle ABC < \angle BCD < 90^\circ$ . Let $P$ be the intersection of lines $BA$ and $CD$ , and $Q$ be the intersection of diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ . Suppose there exists a point $K$ on segment $BC$ such that $P, A, K, D$ are concyclic, and $KB \cdot KC = \frac{1}{2}OP^2$ . The line perpendicular to $BC$ and passing through $Q$ intersects line $OP$ at point $T$ .

- Find all possible values of $OP$ ;
- Find all possible values of $TB+TC$ .

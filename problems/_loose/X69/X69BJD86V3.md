(2004 China National Team Training Selection Contest for IMO) Let $n_{1}, n_{2}, \cdots, n_{k}$ be $k(k \geqslant 2)$ positive integers, and $1< n_{1}<n_{2}<\cdots<n_{k}$, positive integers $a, b$ satisfy
$\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{1}}\right)\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{k}}\right) \leqslant \frac{a}{b}<\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{1}}\right)\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(1-\frac{1}{n_{k-1}}\right)$.
Prove: $n_{1} n_{2} \cdots n_{k} \leqslant(4 k)^{2^{k-1}}$.

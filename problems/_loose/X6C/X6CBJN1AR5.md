Of course, there are other ways to see that some of the numbers in the list are not prime numbers. For example, there is a useful test for divisibility by 3 :
An integer is divisible by 3 if, and only if, the sum of its digits is divisible by 3 .
For example, $1+2+3+4+5+6=21$, and 21 is divisible by 3 . It follows, using this test, that 123456 is divisible by 3 .
So, if the sum of the digits of a positive integer, other than 3 itself, is a number that is divisible by 3 , we can deduce that the integer is not a prime number.

Two congruent regular $2 n$-gons are divided into triangles by connecting their vertices with their respective centers. Each of these triangles is either colored blue or red.

Assume that one of the two $2 n$-gons has as many blue as red triangles. Prove: Under these conditions, it is always possible to superimpose the two $2 n$-gons such that in at least $n$ overlapping pairs of triangles, the two triangles of this pair are identically colored.

**a)** Let $ a,b $ two non-negative integers such that $ a^2>b. $ Show that the equation
$$ \left\lfloor\sqrt{x^2+2ax+b}\right\rfloor =x+a-1 $$
has an infinite number of solutions in the non-negative integers. Here, $ \lfloor\alpha\rfloor $ denotes the floor of $ \alpha. $

**b)** Find the floor of $ m=\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\underbrace{\cdots}_{\text{n times}}+\sqrt{2}}} , $ where $ n $ is a natural number. Justify.

a) Let $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $|x-y|<\frac{1}{n}$, for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$. Prove that $x=y$.

b) Consider $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ an arithmetic progression with strictly positive terms and non-zero common difference. Let $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$, such that $\left[x a_{n}\right]=\left[y a_{n}\right]$ for any $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$. Prove that $x=y$. (Here $[z]$ denotes the integer part of the real number $z$)

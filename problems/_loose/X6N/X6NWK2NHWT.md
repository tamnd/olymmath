In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, there is a point $P$ inside. Extend $AP$, $BP$, and $CP$ to intersect the opposite sides at points $D$, $E$, and $F$ respectively. Let $S_{\triangle DEF}=S_{P}$. Prove: $S_{H} \leqslant S^{\prime} \leqslant S_{1} \leqslant S_{G}$. Here, points $H$, $I$, and $G$ are the orthocenter, incenter, and centroid of $\triangle ABC$ respectively, and $S^{\prime}$ is the area of the triangle formed by the points where the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ touches the sides.

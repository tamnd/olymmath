Let $P(x) = x^2 - 20x - 11$ . If $a$ and $b$ are natural numbers such that $a$ is composite, $\gcd(a, b) = 1$ , and $P(a) = P(b)$ , compute $ab$ .

Note: $\gcd(m, n)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $m$ and $n$ .

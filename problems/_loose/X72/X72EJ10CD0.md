Prove that on a $533 \times 533$ board, colored in a checkerboard pattern, there exist monochromatic cells A and B with the following property: "the number of ways to tile the board without cell B with dominoes is not equal to the number of ways to tile the board without cell A with dominoes." Ways that differ by rotation and reflection are considered different ways.

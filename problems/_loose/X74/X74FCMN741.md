Let $Q$ be the set of $n$-term sequences, each term of which is 0 or 1. Let $A$ be a subset of $Q$ with $2^{n-1}$ elements. Show that there are at least $2^{n-1}$ pairs $(a, b)$ such that $a \in A, b \in Q \backslash A$, and the sequences $a$ and $b$ differ in exactly one term.

From an infinite chessboard, a polygon with sides along the edges of the cells is cut out. A segment of the polygon's perimeter is called black if the cell adjacent to it from inside the polygon is black, and white if the cell is white. Let $A$ be the number of black segments on the perimeter, $B$ be the number of white segments, and let the polygon consist of $a$ black and $b$ white cells. Prove that $A-B=4(a-b)$.

Let $M$ be any interior point of the semicircle $k$ with center $S$ and diameter $A B$. Denote by $k_{A}$ the circle inscribed in the circular sector $A S M$ and by $k_{B}$ the circle inscribed in the circular sector $B S M$. Prove that the circles $k_{A}$ and $k_{B}$ lie in opposite half-planes determined by some line perpendicular to the segment $A B$.

(A circle inscribed in a circular sector touches both radii and the boundary arc.)

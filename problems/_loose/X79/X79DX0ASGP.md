In a computer game, there is a magical flower bed containing 2024 flowers. In each move, the player can pick 4, 9, or 10 flowers. If the player picks 4 flowers, 1 new flower will grow before the next move. If the player picks 9 flowers, 3 new flowers will grow, and if the player picks 10 flowers, 4 new flowers will grow. New flowers will not grow if the player manages to pick all the flowers in the last move.

Can the player pick all the flowers from the flower bed? Justify your answer.

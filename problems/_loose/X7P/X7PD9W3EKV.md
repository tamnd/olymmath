At a preliminary examination, it was announced in advance that only a candidate who gives the correct answer to all three questions can count on admission. An exam proceeded as follows.

Q: We will give you a $19^{\circ}$ angle. Can you divide it into 19 equal parts using only a compass and a ruler?

C: I can.

Q: Good, you will demonstrate it. And a $17^{\circ}$ angle into 17 equal parts?

C: I can.

Q: Good, you will demonstrate it. And a $18^{\circ}$ angle into 18 equal parts?

C: On the same paper?

Q: No, you must immediately submit each completed construction.

C: I cannot divide $18^{\circ}$ into 18 equal parts.

Let real numbers $a, b, c, d$ satisfy
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
a+b+c+d=3, \\
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}=3, \\
a b c+b c d+c d a+d a b=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

Prove:
$$
a(1-a)^{3}=b(1-b)^{3}=c(1-c)^{3}=d(1-d)^{3} .
$$

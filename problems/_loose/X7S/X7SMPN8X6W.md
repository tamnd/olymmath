(A) $1, \frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$.
(B) $4, \frac{25 \sqrt{3}}{3}, \frac{25 \sqrt{3}}{3}, \frac{25 \sqrt{3}}{3}$.
(C) $\sqrt{5}, \frac{\sqrt{30}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{30}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{30}}{2}$.
(D) $2, \frac{6 \sqrt{5}}{5}, \frac{6 \sqrt{5}}{5}, \frac{6 \sqrt{5}}{5}$.

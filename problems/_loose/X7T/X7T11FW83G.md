10 Let $p \geq 2$ be a prime number. Louis and Arnaud alternately choose an index $i \in\{0,1, \ldots, p-1\}$, which has not yet been chosen, and a digit $a_{i} \in\{0,1, \ldots, 9\}$. Louis begins. When all indices have been selected, calculate the following sum:

$$
a_{0}+a_{1} \cdot 10+\ldots+a_{p-1} \cdot 10^{p-1}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} a_{i} \cdot 10^{i}
$$

If this sum is divisible by $p$, Louis wins, otherwise Arnaud wins. Show that Louis has a winning strategy.

Three girls were practicing high jumping before a physical education test. The bar was set at a height of $1,20 \mathrm{M}$.

"I bet," says the first girl to the second, "that my jump will be successful if and only if yours is unsuccessful."

Suppose the second girl said the same to the third, and the third, in turn, said the same to the first. Is it possible for none of the three girls to lose their bet?

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $A_1$ and $A_2$ (resp. $B_1$ and $B_2$; $C_1$ and $C_2$) be points on the line $(BC)$ (resp. $(CA)$ and $(AB)$) such that $\widehat{A_1 A B} = \widehat{A_2 A C}$ (resp. $\widehat{B_1 B C} = \widehat{B_2 B A}$ and $\widehat{C_1 C A} = \widehat{C_2 C B}$). Show that $A_1, B_1$, and $C_1$ are collinear if and only if $A_2, B_2$, and $C_2$ are collinear.

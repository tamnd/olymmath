\section*{Task 1 - 011231}

Two brickworks produce 6 million and 12 million bricks, respectively. They are to supply four construction sites with a demand of 5.2, 3.0, 5.7, and 4.1 million bricks, respectively.

The distances (in km) between the two brickworks and the four construction sites are shown in the following table:

\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
Construction Site & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
Brickwork 1 & 28 & 30 & 37 & 21 \\
Brickwork 2 & 26 & 36 & 18 & 20
\end{tabular}

How many bricks must be transported from the 1st and 2nd brickworks to each of the individual construction sites to minimize the total transportation costs?

It is assumed that the transportation costs are proportional to the distance. Construction site 3 should only be supplied by brickwork 2.

To improve the school work, a total of $P$ suggestions $\left(P \in \mathbf{N}_{+}\right)$ were proposed by all classes in the school. It is known that some classes have the same suggestions, and any two classes have at least one suggestion in common, but no two classes have exactly the same set of suggestions. Prove that the number of classes in the school is no more than $2^{P-1}$.

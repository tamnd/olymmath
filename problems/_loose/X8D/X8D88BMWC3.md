Given the parabola $y=x^{2}+p x+q$ passes through the points $\left(x_{1}, 0\right),\left(x_{2}, 0\right),(1, a),(2, b)$, where $1<x_{1}<x_{2}<2$.
(1) If $x_{1}=\frac{7}{5}, x_{2}=\frac{8}{5}$, find the values of $a$ and $b$;
(2) Prove: If $a \geqslant \frac{1}{4}$, then $b<\frac{1}{4}$.

A dragon has heads, one on each of n necks arranged in a circle. A knight can strike and cut off heads from $k$ adjacent necks with one blow. If the dragon has at least one head left after the blow, it can immediately regrow one of the missing heads. Prove that if for given numbers $n$ and $k$ the knight can rid the dragon of all its heads regardless of how they regrow, he can do so with at most three blows.

The line parallel to (BC) passing through $O$ intersects $[C A]$ at $D$, the line parallel to $(C A)$ passing through $O$ intersects $[A B]$ at $E$, and the line parallel to $(A B)$ passing through $O$ intersects $[\mathrm{BC}]$ at $\mathrm{F}$. What is the value of the following sum of ratios:

$$
\frac{B F}{B C}+\frac{A E}{A B}+\frac{C D}{A C} ?
$$

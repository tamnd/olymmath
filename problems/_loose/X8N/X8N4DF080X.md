Find the period of the repetend of the fraction $\frac{39}{1428}$ by using *binary* numbers, i.e. its binary decimal representation.

(Note: When a proper fraction is expressed as a decimal number (of any base), either the decimal number terminates after finite steps, or it is of the form $0.b_1b_2\cdots b_sa_1a_2\cdots a_ka_1a_2\cdots a_ka_1a_2 \cdots a_k \cdots$ . Here the repeated sequence $a_1a_2\cdots a_k$ is called the *repetend* of the fraction, and the smallest length of the repetend, $k$ , is called the *period* of the decimal number.)

Mr. Chernick bought two new hens at the market, which also laid eggs completely regularly - always one after a few days, with one of them laying twice as often as the other.

Mrs. Chernick calculated that all five hens laid a total of 155 eggs in 60 days. How often did the new hens lay eggs?

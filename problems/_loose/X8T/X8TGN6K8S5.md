Let the angles of a triangle be $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$. Find the smallest and largest values of $\alpha$, rounded to the nearest tenth of a degree, for which $\beta-\gamma=30^{\circ}$, and

$$
10 \sin \alpha \sin \beta \sin \gamma-2 \sin 60^{\circ}\left(\sin ^{2} \alpha+\sin ^{2} \beta+\sin ^{2} \gamma\right)>0
$$

given that the values of $\alpha$ between $23.0^{\circ}$ and $100.0^{\circ}$ satisfy the inequality. (It is advisable to first transform the left side in such a way that during the trial and error process, there is no need to multiply table values and approximate values of irrational numbers.)

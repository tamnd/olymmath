A watch loses $2\frac{1}{2}$ minutes per day. It is set right at $1$ P.M. on March 15. Let $n$ be the positive correction, in minutes, to be added to the time shown by the watch at a given time. When the watch shows $9$ A.M. on March 21, $n$ equals: $\textbf{(A) }14\frac{14}{23} \textbf{(B) }14\frac{1}{14} \textbf{(C) }13\frac{101}{115} \textbf{(D) }13\frac{83}{115} \textbf{(E) }13\frac{13}{23}$

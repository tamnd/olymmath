Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients such that $p(0)=6$. It is known that among the integers $m$ between 1 and 60, exactly 40 are such that $p(m)$ is a multiple of 3; furthermore, it is known that among the integers $m$ between 1 and 60, exactly 30 are such that $p(m)$ is a multiple of 4. How many integers $m$ between 1 and 60 are such that $p(m)$ is a multiple of 6? Note: all intervals that appear in this problem are to be considered inclusive.
(A) 10
(B) 20
(C) 25
(D) 30
(E) 40

A company of several people is called connected if it cannot be divided into two non-empty groups such that people from different groups are not acquainted. In a certain connected company, everyone knows exactly four people, and the four acquaintances of each person form a connected company. Prove that the people of this company can be arranged in a circle so that any two neighbors are acquainted.

Consider the system of equations:

$$
\begin{gathered}
x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots+x_{k}=0 \\
x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+\ldots+x_{k}^{3}=0 \\
\ldots \\
x_{1}^{19}+x_{2}^{19}+\ldots+x_{k}^{19}=0 \\
x_{1}^{21}+x_{2}^{21}+\ldots+x_{k}^{21}=1
\end{gathered}
$$

Prove that for some $k$, this system has a solution.

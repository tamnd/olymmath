Consider the real numbers $x, y, z$. Show that

$$
\frac{x(x+y)}{4 x^{2}+x y+y^{2}}+\frac{y(y+z)}{4 y^{2}+y z+z^{2}}+\frac{z(z+x)}{4 z^{2}+z x+x^{2}} \leq 1
$$

Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ all be positive numbers $(n \geqslant 2)$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}=1$, prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{x_{i}}{\sqrt{1-x_{i}}} \geqslant \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{x_{i}}}{n-1} .
$$

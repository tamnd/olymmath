(Pépin's Theorem) For $n>0$, the $n$-th Fermat number $F_{n}=2^{2^{n}}+1$ is prime if and only if

$$
3^{\left(F_{n}-1\right) / 2} \equiv-1 \bmod F_{n}
$$

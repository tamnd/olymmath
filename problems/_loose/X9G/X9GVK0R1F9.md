Given a tetrahedron $ABCD$. The sphere $S_1$, passing through points $A, B, C$, intersects the edges $AD, BD, CD$ at points $K, L, M$ respectively; the sphere $S_2$, passing through points $A, B, D$, intersects the edges $AC, BC, DC$ at points $P, Q, M$ respectively. It turns out that $KL \parallel PQ$. Prove that the bisectors of the dihedral angles $KMQ$ and $LMP$ coincide.

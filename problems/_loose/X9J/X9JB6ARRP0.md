Around a round table, there are 11 chairs, and at the beginning, 10 children are sitting whose chairs, in the direction of the clock hands, are marked with numbers from 1 to 10. Pavel needs to join them. Once everyone is seated around the table, the children play an elimination game: they start counting in the direction of the clock hands from the child sitting on the chair marked with number 1, and the thirteenth child is eliminated. The remaining children continue the game from where the counting stopped, the thirteenth again is eliminated, and so on, until only one child remains. At which place, or between which two of the ten children who were already sitting at the table, should Pavel sit to win the game?

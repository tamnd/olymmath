A $P_{1} P_{2} \ldots P_{n}$ convex $n$-gon is placed such that the points $Q_{1}, Q_{2}, \ldots, Q_{n-2}$ are inside it, with exactly one of them falling into the interior of any $P_{i} P_{j} P_{k}$ triangle. Prove that if the $n$-gon is divided by drawing all its diagonals, then each $Q_{\ell}$ point falls into a triangular domain.

Based on an idea by Ferenc Sarlós

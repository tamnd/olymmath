A natural number is called a palindrome if it remains unchanged when its digits are written in reverse order (for example, the numbers 4, 55, 626 are palindromes, while 20, 201, 2016 are not). Represent the number 2016 as a product of three palindromes greater than 1 (find all possible options and explain why there are no others).

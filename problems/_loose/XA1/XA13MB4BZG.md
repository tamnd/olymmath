Is it possible to assign a price to each digit from 0 to 9 such that all 10 prices are distinct and there exist 20 consecutive natural numbers, each of which, except the first, is more expensive than the previous one? Here, the price of a natural number is the sum of the prices of the digits in its representation. (I. Rubanov + jury)

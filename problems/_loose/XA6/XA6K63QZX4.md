Let $m$ and $n$ be two strictly positive integers. Let $a_{1}, \cdots a_{m}$ be distinct integers taken from $\{1, \cdots n\}$ satisfying the following condition: if for $1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant m$, we have $a_{i}+a_{j} \leqslant n$, then there exists $k, 1 \leqslant k \leqslant m$ such that $a_{i}+a_{j}=a_{k}$. Show then that:

$$
\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{m}}{m} \geqslant \frac{n+1}{2}
$$

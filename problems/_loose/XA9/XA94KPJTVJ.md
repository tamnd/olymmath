In triangle $ABC$, the feet of the altitudes are $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$; the points symmetric to these with respect to the midpoints of the sides are $A^{\prime \prime}, B^{\prime \prime}, C^{\prime \prime} ;$ show that:

$$
\frac{5{\overline{A^{\prime} B^{\prime}}}^{2}-{\overline{A^{\prime \prime} B^{\prime \prime}}}^{2}}{\overline{A B}^{2}}+\frac{5{\overline{A^{\prime} C^{\prime}}}^{2}-{\overline{A^{\prime \prime} C^{\prime \prime}}}^{2}}{\overline{A C}^{2}}+\frac{5{\overline{B^{\prime} C^{\prime}}}^{2}-{\overline{B^{\prime \prime} C^{\prime \prime}}}^{2}}{\overline{B C}^{2}}=3
$$

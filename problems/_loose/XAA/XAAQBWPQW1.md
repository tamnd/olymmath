Prove that

$$
(1+x)(1+y)(1+z)=(1-x)(1-y)(1-z)
$$

if

$$
x=\frac{a-b}{a+b}, y=\frac{b-c}{b+c}, z=\frac{c-a}{c+a}
$$

and

$$
a \neq-b, b \neq-c, c \neq-a
$$

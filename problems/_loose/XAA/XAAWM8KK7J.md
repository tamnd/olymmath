A chess tournament was held in a round-robin system, where each participant played against every other participant once. Let's call a game incorrect if the winner of the game scored fewer points in total than the loser. (A win gives 1 point, a draw - $1 / 2$, a loss - 0). Can incorrect games make up more than $75 \%$ of the total number of games in the tournament?

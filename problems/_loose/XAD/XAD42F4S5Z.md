Let $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{2011}\right)$ be a permutation (that is a rearrangement) of the numbers $1,2,3, \ldots, 2011$. Show that there exist two numbers $j, k$ such that $1 \leq j<$ $k \leq 2011$ and $\left|a_{j}-j\right|=\left|a_{k}-k\right|$.

On an inclined plane of adjustable inclination $\alpha$, a mass $m$ is placed. There is static friction between the mass and the plane. The coefficient of static friction is $\mu_{0}$. The mass is attached to the upper edge of the inclined plane by a thread. This thread breaks under a load $L_{r}$. The inclination of the plane is now increased (starting from $0^{\circ}$).

At what inclination $\alpha_{r}$ will the thread break?

a) Solve the problem in general terms, i.e., explicitly express $\alpha_{r}=f\left(m ; \mu_{0} ; L_{r} ; g\right)$.

b) Calculate $\alpha_{r}$ numerically for $m=3 \mathrm{~kg}, \mu_{0}=0.3, L_{r}=8 \mathrm{~N}$, and $g=9.8 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}^{2}}$.

Let $n$ be an integer greater than 1, prove that there exists a set $A \varsubsetneqq\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$, such that
(i) $|A| \leqslant 2[\sqrt{n}]+1$;
(ii) $\{|x-y|: x, y \in A, x \neq y\}=\{1,2, \cdots, n-1\}$.

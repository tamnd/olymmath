$ \textbf{(A)}\ b = g

\textbf{(C)}\ b = g - 4
\textbf{(D)}\ b = g - 5
\textbf{(E)}\ \text{It is impossible to determine a relation between }{b}\text{ and }{g}\text{ without knowing }{b + g.}$

A hare is running a 2024-meter race. At the start, it pushes off with its left foot and alternates regularly between its left foot, right foot, and both feet throughout the race. When the hare pushes off with its left foot, it jumps $35 \mathrm{dm}$, when it pushes off with its right foot, it jumps $15 \mathrm{dm}$, and when it pushes off with both feet, it jumps $61 \mathrm{dm}$.

How many jumps will the hare make before reaching the finish line? And which foot will it use to push off before the final jump?

Let the numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be pairwise distinct. Prove that one can choose numbers $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$ such that

$$
\frac{1}{\left(x+a_{1}\right) \ldots\left(x+a_{n}\right)}=\frac{A_{1}}{x+a_{1}}+\ldots+\frac{A_{n}}{x+a_{n}}
$$

On the set $M$ of natural numbers from 1 to 1993, an operation * is defined which assigns to any two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the set $M$ some number $a * b$, also belonging to $M$. It is known that for any numbers $a$ and $b$ from $M$, the equality $(a * b) * a=b$ holds. Prove that there exists a number $a$ such that $a * a=a$.

In an isosceles $\triangle A B C(A C=B C)$ the points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ are midpoints of $B C, A C$ and $A B$ respectively. Points $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ are symmetric points of $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ with respect to $A B$. Let $M$ be the intersecting point of $C A_{2}$ and $A_{1} C_{1}$, and let $N$ be the intersecting point of $C B_{2}$ and $B_{1} C_{1}$. The intersecting point of $A N$ and $B M$ is denoted by $P$. Prove that $A P=B P$.

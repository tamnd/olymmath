Let $\left\{a_{k}\right\}(k \geqslant 1)$ be a sequence of positive real numbers, and there exists a constant $k$, such that $a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}^{2}<k a_{n+1}^{2}$ (for all $n \geqslant 1$).

Prove: There exists a constant $c$, such that: $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}<c a_{n+1}$ (for all $n \geqslant 1$).

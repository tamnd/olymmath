Let the number of positive integers with $n$ decimal digits in which no two adjacent digits (from left to right) form the number 12 be $a_{n}$.
(1) Find $a_{n}$;
(2) Prove that for all $n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}, \frac{a_{n} a_{n+1}-1}{2}$ is a perfect square;
(3) Prove that there exist two sequences of positive integers $\left\{x_{n}\right\},\left\{y_{n}\right\}$ such that for all $n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}$, we have $a_{n}=$ $\frac{x_{n}^{2}+2}{2\left(x_{n}+y_{n}\right)}$.

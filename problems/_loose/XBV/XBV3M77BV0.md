Points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ lie on the sides $B C, A C, A B$ of triangle $A$, respectively, and intersect at point $K$.
Prove that $\frac{K A_{1}}{A A_{1}}+\frac{K B_{1}}{B B_{1}}+\frac{K C_{1}}{C C_{1}}=1$ and $\frac{A K}{A A_{1}}+\frac{B K}{B B_{1}}+\frac{C K}{C C_{1}}=2$.

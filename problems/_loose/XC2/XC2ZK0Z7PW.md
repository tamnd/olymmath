In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular and intersect at $E$. From point $E$, perpendiculars are drawn to sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $P$, $Q$, $R$, and $S$ respectively. Prove that points $P$, $Q$, $R$, and $S$ are concyclic. If the perpendiculars intersect the opposite sides at points $P'$, $Q'$, $R'$, and $S'$ respectively, then these eight points are concyclic.

For natural numbers $a$ and $b$, let $N(a, b)$ be the number of natural numbers $k$ for which

$$
\frac{k^{2}}{a k-b}
$$

is a natural number. Prove that for every pair of natural numbers $(a, b)$, the number $N(a, b)$ is finite, and determine all pairs of natural numbers $(a, b)$ for which the number $N(a, b)$ is odd.

Let $n$ be a positive integer, $f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1} x+\cdots+a_{m} x^{m}$ be an integer-coefficient polynomial, where $m>1$, and
(1) for any $2 \leqslant i \leqslant m$, the number $a_{i}$ is divisible by each prime factor of $n$; (2) $\left(a_{1}, n\right)=1$. Prove: For any positive integer $k$, there exists a positive integer $c$, such that $n^{k} \mid f(c)$.

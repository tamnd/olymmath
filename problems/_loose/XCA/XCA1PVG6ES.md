a) Prove the inequality: $\frac{1}{\sqrt{a b}} \geq \frac{2}{a+b}$, for any strictly positive real numbers $a, b$.

b) Show that: $\sqrt{\frac{x}{y+z+t}}+\sqrt{\frac{y}{z+t+x}}+\sqrt{\frac{z}{t+x+y}}+\sqrt{\frac{t}{x+y+z}} \geq 2$, for any strictly positive real numbers $x, y, z, t$.

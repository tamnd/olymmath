In a country there are finitely many cities and finitely many roads. Each road connects two different cities and each two cities are connected by at most one road. All roads can be traveled in both directions and the road network is set up in such a way that every city can be reached from every other city (possibly via detours). For each city there is also an even number of roads leading away from that city.

The government has now decided to convert all roads into one-way streets. This is to be done in such a way that for each city the number of roads leading out is equal to the number of roads leading in.

(a) Show that this is always possible.

(b) Show that you can still reach any city from any other, no matter how the government implements its plan.

Initially, there are $2024$ green balls and $1$ red ball in a box. Every minute, Kate chooses a random ball from the box. If it is green, she paints it blue and puts it back into the box. If it is blue, she paints it green and puts it back into the box. Finally, if it is red, then she stops the process. What is the expected number of green balls at the end of her process?

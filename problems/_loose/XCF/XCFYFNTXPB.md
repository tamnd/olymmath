In a country with $n$ cities, some of which are connected to each other by roads, and any two cities are connected by no more than one road. Let's call the degree of a city the number of roads that lead out of it. Let $2 \leqslant k \leqslant n$ be some natural number. Prove that in the country there will definitely be $k$ cities, the degrees of which differ from each other by less than $k-1$.

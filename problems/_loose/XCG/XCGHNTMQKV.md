Alberto and Barbara are playing with a die. After a while, they notice that the die is loaded, and the number 1 comes up more frequently than the other 5 numbers (which remain equally probable). They decide that, when a 1 is rolled, that roll is canceled and they roll again. If they continue to roll the die until they get 2 valid rolls, what is the probability that the sum of the 2 valid numbers rolled is 8?
(A) $\frac{3}{25}$
(B) $\frac{1}{6}$
(C) $\frac{1}{5}$
(D) $\frac{6}{25}$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}$.

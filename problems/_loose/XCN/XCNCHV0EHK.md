It is known about the functions $s(x)$ and $t(x)$ that $s(0)=t(0)>0$ and $s^{\prime}(x) \sqrt{t^{\prime}(x)}=5$ for any $x \in[0 ; 1]$. Prove that if $x \in[0 ; 1]$, then $2 s(x)+5 t(x)>15 x$.

Given an equilateral triangle $ABC$. Let's draw the three parabolas whose foci are the vertices $A$, $B$, and $C$, and whose directrices are the lines $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$, respectively. What is the area of the part of the triangle that is not covered by the interior of any of the parabolas? ($AB=1$; by the interior of the parabola, we mean the part of the plane that lies on the same side of the parabola as the focus.)

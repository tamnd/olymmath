Consider an $n \times n$ chessboard. It is required to move a rook from the lower left corner to the upper right corner. You can only move up and to the right, without stepping on the cells of the main diagonal and below it. (The rook is on the main diagonal only at the initial and final moments.) How many such routes does the rook have?

Given the curve $M: x^{2}-y^{2}=m, x>0, m$ is a positive constant. The line $l$ is not perpendicular to the $x$-axis, and intersects the line $y=x$, the curve $M$, and the line $y=-x$ at points $A, B, C, D$ respectively, with $O$ being the origin.

Prove: (1) If $|A B|=|B C|=|C D|$, then the area of $\triangle A O D$ is a constant;
(2) If the area of $\triangle B O C$ is equal to $\frac{1}{3}$ of the area of $\triangle A O D$, then $|A B|=$ $|B C|=|C D|$.

Five different points on a plane are to be connected by lines. All possible connecting lines should always be drawn.

Uwe claims: The five points can be arranged such that there are exactly ten different connecting lines.

Norbert claims: The five points can also be arranged such that there are only five connecting lines.

Fritz claims: The five points can even be arranged such that there is only one single connecting line.

a) Show by drawing one example for each case that all three statements are true!

b) Investigate whether, depending on the arrangement of the five points, other numbers of different connecting lines can also occur, and draw examples for these as well!

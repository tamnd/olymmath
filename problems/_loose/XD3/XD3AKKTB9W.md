\section*{Task 3 - 341033}

Antje and Beate decide, after finding ordinary dice too boring, to replace them with regular octahedra numbered from 1 to 8.

Before they start making these octahedral dice, they agree that (analogous to the dice) the sum of the numbers on any two opposite faces should be 9.

When they compare their self-made octahedra the next day, they notice:

Their octahedra are different from each other, even when the agreement about opposite faces is adhered to, in the sense that no rotation can make the arrangement of the numbers match.

a) Determine how many different arrangements of the numbers there are in the aforementioned sense, under the condition that the agreement about opposite faces is adhered to!

b) Determine how many different arrangements there are in the aforementioned sense, if the agreement about opposite faces does not need to be adhered to!

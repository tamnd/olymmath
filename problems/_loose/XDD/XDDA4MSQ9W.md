Around a round table, $2 n(n>5)$ people are sitting - knights and liars. Liars give a false answer to any question, while knights give a true answer. Each of them knows who is a knight and who is a liar. Each of them answered two questions: "Who is your left neighbor?", "Who is your right neighbor?". A wise man, who knows that there are liars at the table but there are fewer of them than knights, was informed of the number of answers "Knight" and the number of answers "Liar", and he accurately named the number of knights. How many answers "Knight" did the wise man receive? Explain your answer.

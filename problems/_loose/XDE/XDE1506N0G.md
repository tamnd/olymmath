Show that the functions $\log_a(1+a^x)$ and $\sqrt{1+x^2}$ are convex.

Prove that

$$
\sqrt[k]{(1+a_1)(1+a_2) \ldots (1+a_k)} \geq 1+\sqrt[k]{a_1 a_2 \ldots a_k}
$$

and

$$
\sqrt{k^2+(a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_k)^2} \leq \sqrt{1+a_1^2}+\sqrt{1+a_2^2}+\ldots+\sqrt{1+a_k^2}
$$

When can equality hold?

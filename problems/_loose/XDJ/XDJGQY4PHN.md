Let the Thales circles constructed over the sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ be $k_{1}$, $k_{2}$, and $k_{3}$, respectively, and the altitudes corresponding to these sides be $m_{1}$, $m_{2}$, and $m_{3}$. It is to be proven that the intersection points of $m_{2}$ and $k_{2}$, $m_{3}$ and $k_{3}$, and the points of tangency of the tangents drawn from $A$ to $k_{1}$ lie on a circle, and that the center of this circle is $A$.

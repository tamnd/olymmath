Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at two points $A$ and $B$. The tangents to $\Gamma_{1}$ at $A$ and $B$ intersect at $K$. Let $M$ be a variable point on $\Gamma_{1}$, distinct from $A$ and $B$. We denote $P$ as the second intersection point of $(M A)$ and $\Gamma_{2}$, $C$ as the second intersection point of $(M K)$ and $\Gamma_{1}$, and $Q$ as the second intersection point of $(A C)$ with $\Gamma_{2}$.

a) Show that $(P Q)$ passes through a fixed point as $M$ varies.

b) Show that the midpoint of $[P Q]$ lies on the line $(M K)$.

Let $i$ and $j$ be the least numbers satisfying the conditions $a_{n+i} \equiv a_n \pmod {5}$ and $a_{n+j} \equiv a_n \pmod {7}$ for every $n\geq 1$. Which one below cannot be an $(i,j)$ pair?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ (16,4)
\textbf{(B)}\ (3,16)
\textbf{(C)}\ (8,6)
\textbf{(D)}\ (1,48)
\textbf{(E)}\ (16,18)

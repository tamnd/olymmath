In an acute, non-isosceles triangle $A B C$ the midpoints of $A C$ and $A B$ are $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively. A point $D$ lies on $B C$ with $C$ between $B$ and $D$. The point $F$ is such that $\angle A F C$ is a right angle and $\angle D C F=\angle F C A$. The point $G$ is such that $\angle A G B$ is a right angle and $\angle C B G=\angle G B A$. Prove that $B_{1}, C_{1}, F$ and $G$ are collinear.

Let $a>0, n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 3$ and the system $\left\{\begin{array}{c}a^{x_{1}}+a^{x_{2}}+\cdots+a^{x_{n-1}}=n-a^{2} \\ x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n-1}=n-1\end{array}\right.$. Show that if the system has a solution, then $a \leq 1$.

Let $B^{\prime}$ be the point on the circumcircle of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$, diametrically opposite to vertex $B ; I$ be the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C ; M$ be the point of tangency of the inscribed circle with side $A C$. Points $K$ and $L$ are chosen on sides $A B$ and $B C$ respectively, such that $K B=M C$ and $L B=A M$.

Prove that the lines $B^{\prime} I$ and $K L$ are perpendicular.

You arrive on the island and ask one of the locals (let's call him A) if there is gold on his island. In response to your question, A states: "There are treasures on this island if and only if I am a knight."

Our task is divided into two parts:

a) Can we determine whether A is a knight or a liar?

b) Can we determine whether there are treasures on the island?

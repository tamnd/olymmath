\hline e & \cdot & a & = & a

In the illustrated cryptogram, instead of the letters, digits are to be entered into the boxes so that all five given tasks are correctly solved.

The same letters should represent the same digits, and different letters should represent different digits.

As a solution, it is not sufficient, as is usually the case with such "number puzzles," to simply state the sought numbers. It must be demonstrated that the given numbers satisfy all the stated requirements and that they are the only numbers that do so.

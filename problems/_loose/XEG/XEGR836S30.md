A natural number $r$ is a power if it can be expressed in the form $r=t^{s}$, where $t$ and $s$ are natural numbers, $s, t \geqslant 2$. Prove that for every natural number $n$ there exists a set of natural numbers $A$ that satisfies the following conditions:

(i) $A$ has $n$ elements;

(ii) all elements of the set $A$ are powers;

(iii) for all $r_{1}, r_{2}, \ldots r_{k}(2 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$ from $A$, the number $\frac{r_{1}+r_{2}+\ldots+r_{k}}{k}$ is a power.

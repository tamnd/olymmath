In the isosceles triangle $ABC$ ($AB=AC$), a segment $MN$ slides along the base $BC$ at a constant distance. Perpendiculars are drawn from points $M$ and $N$ to $BC$, intersecting $AB$ and $AC$ at points $Q$ and $P$, respectively. Show that the area of the trapezoid $MNPQ$ (for every position of $MN$) is constant and that the geometric locus of the midpoint $D$ of line $PQ$ is a straight line.

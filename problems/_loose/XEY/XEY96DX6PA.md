The first and second groups competed against each other according to the following rules: one squirrel pup from the first group raced against one squirrel pup from the second group. Then another squirrel pup from the first group raced against another squirrel pup from the second group. Finally, the remaining squirrel pups from the first and second groups raced against each other. The group with more wins out of the three races won. Then the second and third groups competed in the same way, followed by the first and third groups. All the squirrel pups ran at the same speed as they did at the beginning when they all competed together. Could it have been that the first group won against the second, the second against the third, and the third against the first? If not - prove it; if yes - show how it could have been.

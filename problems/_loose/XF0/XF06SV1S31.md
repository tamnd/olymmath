Let $f:\mathbb{N} \cup \{0\} \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}$ be defined by $f(0)=0$,
$$f(2n+1)=2f(n)$$
for $n \ge 0$ and
$$f(2n)=2f(n)+1$$
for $n \ge 1$

If $g(n)=f(f(n))$, prove that $g(n-g(n))=0$ for all $n \ge 0$.

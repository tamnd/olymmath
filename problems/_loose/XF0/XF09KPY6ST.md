Let $ A $ be a ring that is not a division ring, and such that any non-unit of it is idempotent. Show that:

**a)** $ \left( U(A) +A\setminus\left( U(A)\cup \{ 0\} \right) \right)\cap U(A) =\emptyset . $

**b)** Every element of $ A $ is idempotent.

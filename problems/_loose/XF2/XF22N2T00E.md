Let $F$ be the left focus of the ellipse $E: \frac{x^{2}}{3}+y^{2}=1$. A line $l$ with a positive slope passes through point $F$ and intersects the ellipse $E$ at points $A$ and $B$. Lines $A M$ and $B N$ are drawn through points $A$ and $B$ respectively, such that $A M \perp l, B N \perp l$, and lines $A M$ and $B N$ intersect the $x$-axis at points $M$ and $N$. Find the minimum value of $|M N|$.

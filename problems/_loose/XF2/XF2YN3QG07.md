Let $n \geqslant 2, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, a_{n+1}$ be positive numbers, and $a_{2}-a_{1}=a_{3}-a_{2}=\cdots=a_{n+1}-a_{n} \geqslant 0$. Prove: $\frac{1}{a_{2}^{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{3}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}} \leqslant \frac{n-1}{2} \cdot \frac{a_{1} a_{n}+a_{2} a_{n+1}}{a_{1} a_{2} a_{n} a_{n+1}}$. (2004 Hong Kong Mathematical Olympiad Problem)

Given a fixed integer $k>0,r=k+0.5$ ,define $f^1(r)=f(r)=r[r],f^l(r)=f(f^{l-1}(r))(l>1)$ where $[x]$ denotes the smallest integer not less than $x$ .
prove that there exists integer $m$ such that $f^m(r)$ is an integer.

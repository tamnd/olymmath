$^{+}$ Can a tree have an unbounded pathwidth?
A sequence of vertices $\left(v_{1}, \ldots, v_{n}\right)$ has a transaction which is a family of disjoint paths from the initial segment $\left\{v_{1}, \ldots, v_{i}\right\}$ to the remaining segment $\left\{v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_{n}\right\}$. The adhesion of a path decomposition $\left(V_{1}, \ldots, V_{n}\right)$ is defined as the number $\max _{i<n}\left|V_{i} \cap V_{i+1}\right|$.

In triangle $ABC$, the internal angle bisector from $A$ intersects the opposite side at $A_{1}$ and the circumcircle of the triangle at $A_{2}$. Similarly, the internal angle bisectors from $B$ and $C$ intersect the opposite sides at $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, and the circumcircle at $B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$, respectively. Prove that

$$
\frac{A A_{1}}{A A_{2}}+\frac{B B_{1}}{B B_{2}}+\frac{C C_{1}}{C C_{2}} \leqq \frac{9}{4}
$$

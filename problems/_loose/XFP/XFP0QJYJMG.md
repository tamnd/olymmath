On 300 cards, all natural numbers from 1 to 300 are written. The cards are evenly distributed between two players. Each of them lays out pairs of cards such that the difference between the numbers on them is a multiple of 25, until it is no longer possible. Prove that they will lay out the same number of such pairs on the table.

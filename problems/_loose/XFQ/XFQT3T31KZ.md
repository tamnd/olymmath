Given an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with orthocenter $H$. Prove that

$$
\overline{A H} \cdot h_{a}+\overline{B H} \cdot h_{b}+\overline{C H} \cdot h_{c}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)
$$

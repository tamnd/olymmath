Given an acute triangle $ABC$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $A$ and $D_{1}, D_{2}$ the images of point $D$ under reflections across the lines $AB, AC$. Further, let $E_{1}$ and $E_{2}$ be points on the line $BC$ such that $D_{1} E_{1} \| AB$ and $D_{2} E_{2} \| AC$. Prove that the points $D_{1}, D_{2}, E_{1}, E_{2}$ lie on the same circle, whose center lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

(Patrik Bak)

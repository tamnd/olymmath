Determine the functions $f: \mathbb{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{*}$ that have the properties:
a) $f(m+n)-1$ divides $f(m)+f(n)$, for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$;
b) $n^{2}-f(n)$ is a perfect square, for all $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$.

In the book "Xu Gu Zhai Qi Suan Fa" written by the Southern Song mathematician Yang Hui, there is such a problem: "When divided by 2, the remainder is 1; when divided by 5, the remainder is 2; when divided by 7, the remainder is 3; when divided by 9, the remainder is 4. What is the original number?
In modern language, it can be stated as: "There is a number that, when divided by 2, leaves a remainder of 1; when divided by 5, leaves a remainder of 2; when divided by 7, leaves a remainder of 3; and when divided by 9, leaves a remainder of 4. What is this number?" Please write the smallest natural number that satisfies these conditions here $ $

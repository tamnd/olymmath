The system rests on the horizontal plane $S$ by the edge $AB$ of the square plate and a suitable point on the sphere. The system is rotated around the vertex $B$ such that the sphere $G$ continuously touches $S$, until the edge $BC$ reaches $S$, and from this position, the system is similarly rotated around the vertex $C$ to the edge $CD$, and so on.

What trace does the system leave on the plane $S$ during this process? Numerical data: the side of the square is 4 units, the radius of the sphere is 1 unit.

The *popularity * of a positive integer $n$ is the number of positive integer divisors of $n$. For example, $1$ has popularity $1$, and $12$ has popularity $6$. For each number $n$ between $1$ and $30$ inclusive, Cathy writes the number $n$ on $k$ pieces of paper, where $k$ is the popularity of $n$. Cathy then picks a piece of paper at random. Compute the probability that she will pick an even integer.

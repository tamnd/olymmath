Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$. Suppose the sequence of positive integers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}(n=1,2, \cdots)$ satisfies $a_{n+2}=\left[\frac{2 a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}\right]+\left[\frac{2 a_{n}}{a_{n+1}}\right]$. Prove: There exists $m \geqslant 3$ $\left(m \in \mathbf{Z}_{+}\right)$, such that $a_{m}=a_{m+1}$.

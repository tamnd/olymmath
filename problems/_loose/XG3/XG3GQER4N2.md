(The Great Triangle, the Circle, and the Euler Line $d^{\prime}$). In a triangle $ABC$, we denote $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ as the midpoints of the sides $BC, CA, AB$, and $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ as the feet of the altitudes from $A, B, C$.

(i) Show that the medians $\left(A A^{\prime}\right),\left(B B^{\prime}\right),\left(C C^{\prime}\right)$ are concurrent at a point $G$, which divides each median into two segments whose length ratio is 2.

(ii) Show that the perpendicular bisectors of each side of the triangle are concurrent at a point $O$, the center of the circumcircle of $ABC$.

(iii) Show that the altitudes $\left(A H_{A}\right),\left(B H_{B}\right),\left(C H_{C}\right)$ are concurrent at a point $H$, called the orthocenter of the triangle $ABC$.

(iv) Let $P_{A}, P_{B}, P_{C}$ be the midpoints of $HA, HB, HC$ respectively. Show that the nine points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}, P_{A}, P_{B}, P_{C}$ lie on the same circle called the nine-point circle, with center $\Omega$, the midpoint of the segment $[OH]$, and radius $R / 2$.

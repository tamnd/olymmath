The game of "superchess" is played on a $100 \times 100$ board, and involves 20 different pieces, each of which moves according to its own rules. It is known that any piece from any position attacks no more than 20 squares (but nothing else is known about the rules, for example, if piece $A$ is moved, we do not know how the set of attacked squares will change). Prove that it is possible to place all 20 pieces on the board so that none of them attack each other.

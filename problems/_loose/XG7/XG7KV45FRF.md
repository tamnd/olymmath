The circle $C_1$ and $C_2$ are externally tangent to each other at $T$. A line passing through $T$ meets $C_1$ at $A$ and meets $C_2$ at $B$. The line which is tangent to $C_1$ at $A$ meets $C_2$ at $D$ and $E$. If $D \in [AE]$, $|TA|=a$, $|TB|=b$, what is $|BE|$?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ \sqrt{a(a+b)}
\textbf{(B)}\ \sqrt{a^2+b^2+ab}
\textbf{(C)}\ \sqrt{a^2+b^2-ab}
\textbf{(D)}\ \sqrt{a^2+b^2}
\textbf{(E)}\ \sqrt{(a+b)b}
$

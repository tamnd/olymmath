Let real numbers $x, y$ satisfy:
$$
x^{3}=3 y^{2} x+5-\sqrt{7}, y^{3}=3 x^{2} y+5+\sqrt{7} \text {. }
$$

Then the value of $x^{2}+y^{2}$ is ( ).
(A) $25-8 \sqrt{7}$
(B) $2 \sqrt{7}$
(C) $12 \sqrt{7}-25$
(D) 4

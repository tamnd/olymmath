(BMO Shortlist 2017, problem N2) Determine all functions $f: \mathbb{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, the number

$$
x f(x)+2 x f(y)+f(y)^{2}
$$

is a perfect square.

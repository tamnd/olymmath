Let $AB$ and $AC$ be two non-collinear rays, and $\odot O$ be a circle tangent to rays $AC$ and $AB$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. Let $R$ be a point on segment $EF$. The line through point $O$ parallel to $EF$ intersects $AB$ at point $P$, and $PR$ intersects $AC$ at point $N$. The line through point $R$ parallel to $AC$ intersects $AB$ at point $M$. Prove that line $MN$ is tangent to $\odot O$.

Let $r$ be the radius of the circle inscribed in triangle $ABC$. Points $D, E, F$ are chosen on the sides $BC, CA$, and $AB$, respectively, such that the radii of the inscribed circles of triangles $AEF, BFD$, and $CDE$ are all equal to $\rho$. If $r'$ is the radius of the circle inscribed in triangle $DEF$, prove that $\rho = r - r'$.

Let $n \geqslant 2$ be an integer. We have placed $3 n^{2}-10 n+10$ circles of radius 1 in the plane. Prove that one of the following assertions is true:

a) there exist $n$ circles such that no one intersects with another;

b) there exist $n$ circles such that each one intersects with the $n-1$ others.

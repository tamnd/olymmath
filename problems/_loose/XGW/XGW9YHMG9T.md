Given a natural number $K>2$ and a set of $N$ cards, on which positive numbers are written. It turned out that from them one can choose several cards (possibly one) with the sum of numbers $K$, several cards with the sum of numbers $K^{2}, \ldots$, several cards with the sum of numbers $K^{K}$. Could it be that $N<K$?

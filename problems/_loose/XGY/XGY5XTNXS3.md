The decimal representation of a natural number $N$ contains each digit from 0 to 9 exactly once. Let $A$ be the sum of five two-digit numbers formed from the first and second, third and fourth, ..., ninth and tenth digits of $\mathrm{N}$, and let $B$ be the sum of four two-digit numbers formed from the second and third, fourth and fifth, ..., eighth and ninth digits of $N$. It turns out that $A$ equals $B$. Can $N$ start with an even digit?

Let $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, \alpha \in \mathbf{R}$, and assume
$$
\begin{array}{c}
f(\alpha)=a b c\left(a^{\alpha}+b^{a}+c^{a}\right), \\
g(\alpha)=a^{a+2}(b+c-a)+b^{a+2}(a-b+c)+c^{a+2}(a+b-c) .
\end{array}
$$

Determine the relationship between $f(\alpha)$ and $g(x)$.
(1994 Taipei Mathematical Olympiad Problem)

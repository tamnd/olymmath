Extend the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ beyond the sides by their own lengths. The points thus obtained, $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$, form triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$, whose area is $t^{\prime}$ and sides are $a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime}$.

Show that

$$
a^{\prime 2}+b^{\prime 2}+c^{\prime 2}-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)=32 t \sin \alpha \sin \beta \sin \gamma
$$

and

$$
t^{\prime}=t[3+8 \cos \alpha \cos \beta \cos \gamma] \text {. }
$$

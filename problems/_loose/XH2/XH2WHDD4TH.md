Let $a, b$ and $c$ be complex numbers. Prove that the following inequalities are equivalent:

1) $\operatorname{Re}[(a-c)(\bar{c}-\bar{b})] \geqslant 0$
2) $\left|c-\frac{a+b}{2}\right| \leqslant \frac{1}{2}|a-b|$.

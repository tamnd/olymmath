An $n \times n$ square is divided into unit squares. One needs to place a number of isosceles right triangles with hypotenuse 2, with vertices at grid points, in such a way that every side of every unit square belongs to exactly one triangle (i.e. lies inside it or on its boundary). Determine all numbers $n$ for which this is possible.

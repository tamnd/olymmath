Let $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2 n}, a_{2 n+1}$ be an increasing arithmetic progression with positive terms. Prove that

$$
\frac{n}{a_{1} a_{2 n+1}}<\frac{1}{a_{1} a_{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{3} a_{4}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{a_{2 n-1} a_{2 n}}<\frac{n}{a_{0} a_{2 n}}
$$

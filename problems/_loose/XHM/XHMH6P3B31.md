Let $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ be the angles of triangle $ABC$, $s$ its semiperimeter, and $R$ the radius of the circumscribed circle. If

$$
3 R^{2}(3-\cos 2 \alpha-\cos 2 \beta-\cos 2 \gamma)=2 s^{2}
$$

prove that the triangle is equilateral.

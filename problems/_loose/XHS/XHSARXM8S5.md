Let the rhombus $A B C D$, with $A B=a$ and $m(\angle A)=60^{\circ}$. From the same side of the plane $(A B C)$, perpendiculars $A F$ and $C E$ are raised such that $A F=C E=c$. Let $\alpha=m(\angle(A C ; B E))$ and $\beta=m(\angle((B F E) ;(A B C)))$.

a) Calculate $\cos \alpha$ and $\cos \beta$.

b) Show that, for any $c>0$, we have $\operatorname{tg} \alpha \cdot \cos \beta=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.

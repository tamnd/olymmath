An equilateral triangle $ABC$ is given. Points $O_{1}, O_{2}$ lie on the sides $AB,AC$ respectively. It is known that the circle centered at $O_{1}$ and passing through $B$ is externally tangent to the circle centered at $O_{2}$ and passing through $C$ at a point $P$ inside the triangle. Find the angle $\angle{BPC}$ .

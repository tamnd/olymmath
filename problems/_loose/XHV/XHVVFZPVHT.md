Let $A B C$ be a triangle where $|A B|<|C A|<|B C|$, and let $D$ and $E$ be points on the rays $B A$ and $B C$ respectively such that $|B D|=|B E|=|A C|$. The circumcircle of triangle $B D E$ intersects the segment $\overline{A C}$ at point $P$, and the line $B P$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ at point $Q(Q \neq B)$. Prove that $|A Q|+|Q C|=|B P|$.

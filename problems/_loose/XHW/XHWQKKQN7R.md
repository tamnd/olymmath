Given that $\odot O_{2}$ is externally tangent to the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle A B C$, and is tangent to the extensions of sides $A C$ and $A B$ at points $D_{2}$ and $E_{2}$, respectively. Prove:
$$
A D_{2}=A E_{2}=\frac{2 b c}{b+c-a},
$$

where, $B C=a, C A=b, A B=c$.

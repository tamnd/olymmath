Let $E$ and $F$ be points on the side $\overline{A B}$ of rectangle $A B C D$ such that $|A E|=|E F|$. The perpendicular to $A B$ at point $E$ intersects the diagonal $\overline{A C}$ at point $G$, and the lines $\overline{D F}$ and $\overline{B G}$ intersect at point $H$. Prove that the areas of triangles $F B H$ and $G H D$ are equal.

The first term of an infinite sequence is $a_{1}=0$. Every subsequent term is formed from the previous one according to the following formula:

$$
a_{n+1}=\frac{1}{5}\left(2 a_{n}^{3}-a_{n}^{2}+3 a_{n}+1\right)
$$

Prove that the sequence has a limit and determine the limit.

The negation of the statement "For all real numbers $a, b$, if $a=0$, then $a b=0$" can be written as: there exist real numbers $a, b$ such that
(A) $a \neq 0$ and $a b \neq 0$.
(B) $a \neq 0$ and $a b=0$.
(C) $a=0$ and $a b \neq 0$.
(D) $a b \neq 0$ and $a \neq 0$.
(E) $a b=0$ and $a \neq 0$.
(17th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1966)

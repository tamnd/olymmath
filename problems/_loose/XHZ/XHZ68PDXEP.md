Determine all the integers $n>1$ such that
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2} \geq x_{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} x_{i}
$$
for all real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$.

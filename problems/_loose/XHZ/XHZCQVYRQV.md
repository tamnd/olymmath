If $n$ is a natural number, and the real number $2^{n} x$ is not equal to an integer multiple of $\pi$, prove that

$$
\frac{1}{\sin 2 x}+\frac{1}{\sin 4 x}+\ldots+\frac{1}{\sin 2^{n} x}=\operatorname{ctg} x-\operatorname{ctg} 2^{n} x
$$

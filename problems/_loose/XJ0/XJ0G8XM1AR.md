Let's determine the value of $n$, given that

$$
\binom{n}{5}=\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4)}{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 \cdot 5}
$$

(which, as we know, is an integer) is of the form $\overline{a b a b a b}$ in the decimal system, where $a$ and $b$ represent digits.

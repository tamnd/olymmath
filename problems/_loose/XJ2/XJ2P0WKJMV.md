Let $ x $ be a positive real number such that the numbers $ x^{-1} $ , $ x $ , and $ x^{2018} $ have the same fractional part: $$ \{x^{-1}\} = \{x\} = \{x^{2018}\}. $$ Prove that $ x = 1 $ .**Note:** If $ x $ is a real number, its fractional part is $ \{x\} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor $ , where $ \lfloor x \rfloor $ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $ x $ .

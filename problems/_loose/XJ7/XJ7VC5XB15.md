The sequence of real numbers $(a_n)_{n\geq 0}$ is such that $a_0 = 1$ , $a_1 = a > 2$ and $ a_{n+1} = \left(\left(\frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}}\right)^2 -2\right)a_n$ for every positive integer $n$ . Prove that $ \sum_{i=0}^k \frac{1}{a_i} < \frac{2+a-\sqrt{a^2-4}}{2}$ for every positive integer $k$ .

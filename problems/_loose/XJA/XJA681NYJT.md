The numbers $a, b, c$ are such that

$$
\frac{a^{2}-b c}{a(1-b c)}=\frac{b^{2}-a c}{b(1-a c)}, a b c(1-b c)(1-a c) \neq 0
$$

If $a \neq b$, prove that

$$
a+b+c=\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}
$$

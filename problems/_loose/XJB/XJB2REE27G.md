A convex hexagon inscribed in a circle has vertices $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{6}$. Prove that the lines $A_{1} A_{4}, A_{2} A_{5}, A_{3} A_{6}$ intersect at a single point if and only if

$$
A_{1} A_{2} \cdot A_{3} A_{4} \cdot A_{5} A_{6}=A_{2} A_{3} \cdot A_{4} A_{5} \cdot A_{6} A_{1}
$$

holds.

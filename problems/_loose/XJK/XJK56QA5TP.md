Let $r$ be a fixed positive real number. It is known that for some positive integer $n$ the following statement is true: for any positive real numbers $a_1,...,a_n$ satisfying the equation $a_1+...+a_n=r(\frac{1}{a_1}+...+\frac{1}{a_n})$ they also satisfy the equation $\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}-a_1}+...+\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}-a_n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}$ ( $a_i\neq\sqrt{r}$ ). Find $n$ .

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=\sqrt{t-t^{2}}-\operatorname{arctg} \sqrt{\frac{1-t}{t}} \\
y=\sqrt{t}-\sqrt{1-t} \cdot \arcsin \sqrt{t}
\end{array}\right.
$$

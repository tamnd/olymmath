12.2.29 $\star_{\star}^{\star \star} A-B C D E$ is a regular pyramid with a square base $B C D E$, and points $F, G, H$ are on $A B, A C, A D$ respectively, such that $A F=A G=A H$.
(1) Prove that $E F$ and $D G$ must intersect at a point $K$, and $B G$ and $E H$ must intersect at a point $L$;
(2) If $E G$ intersects the plane $A K L$ at $M$, prove that $A K M L$ is a square.

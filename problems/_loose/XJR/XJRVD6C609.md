For the number triples $a, b, c$ and $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, the conditions $a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$ and $\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}-\gamma^{2}=2$ are satisfied. Form the number $s=a \alpha+b \beta-c \gamma$, and then the following number triple:

$$
p=a-\alpha s, q=b-\beta s, r=c-\gamma s .
$$

Show that in this case $p^{2}+q^{2}=r^{2}$. - Find a triple of integers $a, b, c$ and a triple of integers $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ that satisfy the conditions, and form $p, q, r$ from them, then form another triple with $p, q, r$ in place of $a, b, c$.

In a row, n different real numbers were written in increasing order. Under them, in the second row, the same numbers were written, but perhaps in a different order. For each pair of numbers written one under the other, their sum was calculated. These sums formed the third row. It turned out that the numbers in the third row are also arranged in increasing order. Prove that the first row coincides with the second.

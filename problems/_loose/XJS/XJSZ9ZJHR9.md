If $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$ are two non-zero complex numbers with equal modulus, then $\left(z_{1}+z_{2}\right)^{2}=\lambda^{2} z_{1} z_{2}$ if and only if $\left|z_{1}+z_{2}\right|=\lambda\left|z_{k}\right|\left(\lambda \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, k=1,2\right)$.

It is known - and can be easily read from the central equation of the ellipse - that points of an ellipse defined by its two axes can be constructed by the following procedure: over both axes, as if they were diameters, we draw circles, then we draw perpendiculars to the appropriate axes through the intersection points of a ray originating from the center with the circles, the intersection of these is a point of the ellipse. Let us give a procedure for constructing points of a hyperbola based on its central equation.

Given circles $\Gamma_{1}, \Gamma_{2}, \Gamma_{3}$ all passing through point $P$. The tangent to circle $\Gamma_{1}$ at point $P$ intersects circles $\Gamma_{2}, \Gamma_{3}$ at the second points $P_{12}, P_{13}$, respectively. Similarly, define points $P_{21}, P_{23}, P_{31}, P_{32}$. Prove: The perpendicular bisectors of segments $P_{12} P_{13}, P_{21} P_{23}, P_{31} P_{32}$ intersect at one point.

Patrick and Slippers. Every day, the dog Patrick gnaws one slipper from the existing supply in the house. With a probability of 0.5, Patrick wants to gnaw a left slipper, and with a probability of 0.5 - a right slipper. If the desired slipper is not available, Patrick gets upset. How many pairs of identical slippers need to be bought so that with a probability of at least 0.8, Patrick does not get upset for a whole week (7 days)?

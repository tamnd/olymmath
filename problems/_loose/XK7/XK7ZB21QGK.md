Consider the representation of a real number $x$ in base 3. $K$ is the set of all such numbers $x$ in $[0,1]$ whose digits are 0 or 2. If
$$
S=\{x+y \mid x, y \in K\},
$$

prove that:
$$
S=\{z \mid 0 \leqslant z \leqslant 2\}=[0,2] .
$$

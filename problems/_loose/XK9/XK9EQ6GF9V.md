Color the vertices of a convex $2 n+1(n \geqslant 2)$-gon such that any two adjacent vertices have different colors. Prove: For any such coloring, the $2 n+1(n \geqslant 2)$-gon can be divided into several triangles by non-intersecting diagonals, such that the endpoints of each diagonal in the triangles have different colors.

In 2019, the Mathematics Olympiad underwent a trial reform: A city held 5 joint competitions in the second year of high school. A student who ranks in the top 20 of the city in 2 out of these 5 competitions can enter the provincial team training and does not need to participate in the remaining competitions. Each student can participate in a maximum of 5 competitions. It is stipulated that if the student does not rank in the top 20 of the city in the first 4 competitions, they cannot participate in the 5th competition. Assume that the probability of a student ranking in the top 20 of the city in each competition is $\frac{1}{4}$, and the results of each competition are independent.
(1) Find the probability that the student will enter the provincial team;
(2) If the student enters the provincial team or completes 5 competitions, let the number of competitions the student participates in be $\xi$. Find the distribution of $\xi$ and the expected value of $\xi$.

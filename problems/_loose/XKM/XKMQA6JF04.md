Prove that the Pell's equation for any natural $d$ (free of squares) has infinitely many solutions in natural numbers. Moreover, if $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right)$ is the fundamental solution, then any solution $\left(x_{n}, y_{n}\right)$ has the form

$$
x_{n}+y_{n} \sqrt{d}=\left(x_{1}+y_{1} \sqrt{d}\right)^{n}
$$

Let $n \geq 2$ be a natural number. What is the maximum number $m \leq n$ ( $m$ depends on $n$ ), for which we can choose $m$ numbers from $1,2, \ldots, n$ with the property that for any two of them, $a, b$, with $a>b$, the number $a-b$ does not divide the number $a+b$?

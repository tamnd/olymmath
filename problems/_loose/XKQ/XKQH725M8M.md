An urn has 99 red balls, 98 green balls, and 3 yellow balls. Ramon and Greg take turns, starting with Ramon, in taking balls randomly from the urn without replacement. Ramon gets one point for each red ball he pulls out, and Greg gets one point for each green ball he pulls out. They do this until there are no more balls left in the urn. The probability that Greg has more points than Ramon at the end of the game is $\frac mn$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $m+n$ ?

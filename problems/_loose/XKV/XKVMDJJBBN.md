Determine all polynomials $P$ of the third degree that have the following three properties:

(i) $P(x)$ when divided by $x^{2}-1$ gives a remainder of $2 x+1$,

(ii) the sum of the roots of the polynomial $P$ is -2,

(iii) the graph of the polynomial $P$ passes through the point $(0,3)$.

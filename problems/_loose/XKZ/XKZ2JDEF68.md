Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle. The perpendicular from $A$ to $A C$ and the perpendicular from $B$ to $A B$ intersect at point $D$. On the side $A D$, an interior point $E$ is taken such that triangles $A B D$ and $C A E$ are congruent. The length of side $A B$ is $10 \mathrm{~cm}$, and $E B \perp B C$. Calculate the perimeter of triangle $A B C$.

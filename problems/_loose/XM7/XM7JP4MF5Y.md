Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, with $AB \neq AC$ and centroid $G$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and consider the circle $\Gamma$ centered at $G$ with radius $GM$. Denote by $N$ the intersection of $\Gamma$ with $BC$ different from $M$. Now let $S$ be the point symmetric to $A$ with respect to $N$, that is, the point on the line $AN$ such that $AN = NS (A \neq S)$.

Prove that $GS$ is perpendicular to $BC$.

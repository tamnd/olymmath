On the $x y$-plane, let $S$ denote the region consisting of all points $(x, y)$ for which
$$
\left|x+\frac{1}{2} y\right| \leq 10, |x| \leq 10 \text { and } |y| \leq 10 .
$$

The largest circle centred at $(0,0)$ that can be fitted in the region $S$ has area $k \pi$. Find the value of $k$.

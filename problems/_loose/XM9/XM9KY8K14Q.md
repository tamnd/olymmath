Indicate (justifying your answer) if there exists a function $f: R \to R$ such that for all $x \in R$ fulfills that

i) $\{f(x))\} \sin^2 x + \{x\} cos (f(x)) cosx =f (x)$ ii) $f (f(x)) = f(x)$ where $\{m\}$ denotes the fractional part of $m$ . That is, $\{2.657\} = 0.657$ , and $\{-1.75\} = 0.25$ .

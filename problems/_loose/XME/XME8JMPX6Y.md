Using the result of problem № 127 (the converse of Archimedes' theorem), prove the identity

$$
\sin \alpha + \sin \beta = 2 \sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2}
$$

under the condition that the sum of the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ does not exceed $180^{\circ}$.

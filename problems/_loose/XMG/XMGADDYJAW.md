Let $n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$ be fixed and the sets $\mathrm{H}_{1}=\left\{A \in M_{n}(\mathbb{R}) / A^{t}=A\right\}$ and

$$
\mathrm{H}_{2}=\left\{A \in M_{n}(\mathbb{R}) / A^{t}=-A\right\}
$$

a). Prove that $\mathrm{H}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{H}_{2}$ are subgroups of the group $\left(M_{n}(\mathbb{R}),+\right)$.

b). Prove that for all $X \in M_{n}(\mathbb{R})$, there exist $A \in H_{1}$ and $B \in H_{2}$ such that $X=A+B$.

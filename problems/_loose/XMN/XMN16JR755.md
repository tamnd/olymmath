A garment factory produces a certain type of winter clothing. In September, the profit from selling the winter clothing (profit per piece = selling price - cost) is 25% of the selling price. In October, the selling price of each piece of winter clothing is reduced by 10% (the cost per piece remains unchanged), and the number of pieces sold increases by 80% compared to September. Therefore, the total profit from selling this type of winter clothing in October is ( ) higher than in September.
A. $2 \%$
B. $8 \%$
C. $40.0 \%$
D. $62\%$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle.

1) Show that the intersection point of the median from $A$ and the median from $B$ is the unique point $M$ such that $\overrightarrow{A M}+\overrightarrow{B M}+\overrightarrow{C M}=\overrightarrow{0}$. Deduce that the medians of a triangle are concurrent. The point of intersection of the medians is called the centroid of the triangle.
2) Show that the altitude from $A$ is the set of points $M$ such that $\overrightarrow{A M} \cdot \overrightarrow{B M} = \overrightarrow{A M} \cdot \overrightarrow{C M}$. Deduce that the altitudes of a triangle are concurrent at a point called the orthocenter of the triangle.

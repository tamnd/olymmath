a) It is known that the domain of the function $f(x)$ is the interval $[-1 ; 1]$, and $f(f(x)) = -x$ for all $x$, and its graph is the union of a finite number of points and intervals. Draw the graph of the function $f(x)$.

b) Can this be done if the domain of the function is the interval $(-1 ; 1)$? The entire number line?

Comments. $1^{\circ}$. In part "a", it is sufficient to draw the graph of any such function.

$2^{\circ}$. Let us recall that an interval does not include its endpoints. However, this is not significant, as the interval is the union of the interval and two points.

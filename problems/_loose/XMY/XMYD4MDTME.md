It is known that from segments of lengths $a$ , $b$ and $c$ , a triangle can be formed. Can it happen that from segments of lengths $$ \sqrt{a^2 + bc}, \sqrt{b^2 + ca}, \sqrt{c^2 + ab} $$ an obtuse triangle can be formed?

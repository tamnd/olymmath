Find out how many solutions may equation
$$
[a^2, b^2] + [b^2, c^2] + [c^2, a^2] = (a^2, b^2)(b^2, c^2)(c^2, a^2)
$$
have in natural numbers, if $[m, n]$ and $(m, n)$ stands respectively for the LCM and GCD of the natural numbers $m$ and $n$.

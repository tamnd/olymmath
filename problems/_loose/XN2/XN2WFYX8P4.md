In the corner of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard, there is a token. Petya and Vasya take turns moving the token, with Petya starting first. Petya makes one move with the token as a queen (only the final cell is considered as passed), and Vasya makes two moves as a king (both cells are considered as passed). It is not allowed to place the token on a cell where it has already been (including the initial cell). The player who cannot make a move loses. Which of the boys can always win, regardless of how the opponent plays?

In $\triangle{ABC}$ , $AB\neq AC$ , $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$ . $X$ is a random point on segment $AM$ . $A'$ is on the the circumcircle of $\triangle{ABC}$ s.t. $AA'||BC$ .

- $\odot (AA'X)$ intersect $AB,AC$ at $F,E$ .
- $A'X$ intersect $BC$ at $P$ .

Prove that $P,M,E,F$ is concyclic.

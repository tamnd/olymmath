Consider the sequence of natural numbers $\left(\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}}\right)_{\mathrm{n} \geq 1}$, where $\mathrm{a}_{1}=2$ and $\mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}+1}=\frac{2 n+3-n!a_{n}}{(n+1)!}, n \geq 1$.
a.) Show that the sequence is convergent.

b.) Calculate $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} n \cdot \ln \left[(n-1) \cdot a_{n}\right]$

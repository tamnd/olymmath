Let $ E $ be any internal point on the side $ AB $ of a convex quadrilateral $ ABCD $. Denote by $ S_1, S_2, T $ the areas of triangles $ ADE $, $ BCE $, and $ CDE $, respectively.
a) Prove that if $ |\measuredangle A| + |\measuredangle B| = 180^{\circ} $, then $ T \geq 2\sqrt{S_1S_2} $.
b) Prove that if $ |\measuredangle A| + |\measuredangle B| > 180^{\circ} $, then $ T > 2\sqrt{S_1S_2} $.

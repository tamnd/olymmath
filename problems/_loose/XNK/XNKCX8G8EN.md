For the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the three pairs of opposite edges have lengths $a$ and $a'$, $b$ and $b'$, $c$ and $c'$, and the radius of its circumscribed sphere is $R$. Prove that $16 R^{2}=a^{2}+a^{\prime 2}+b^{2}+b^{\prime 2}+c^{2}+c^{\prime 2}$ if and only if $a=a^{\prime}, b=b^{\prime}, c=c^{\prime}$.

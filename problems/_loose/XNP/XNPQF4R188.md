Given the complex numbers $z_{i}=1-m \mathrm{i}(m>0), z=x+y \mathrm{i}$ and $w=x^{\prime}+y^{\prime} \mathrm{i}$, where $x, y, x^{\prime}, y^{\prime} \in \mathbf{R}$, $\mathrm{i}$ is the imaginary unit, and for any complex number $z$, we have $w=\bar{z}_{0} \cdot \bar{z},|w|=2|z|$.
(1) Try to find the value of $m$, and write the expressions for $x^{\prime}$ and $y^{\prime}$ in terms of $x$ and $y$.
(2) Consider $(x, y)$ as the coordinates of point $P$, and $\left(x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}\right)$ as the coordinates of point $Q$. The above relationship can be seen as a transformation of points on the coordinate plane: it transforms point $P$ to point $Q$ on the same plane. When point $P$ moves along the line $y=x+1$, find the trajectory equation of point $Q$ after the transformation.
(3) Does there exist a line such that any point on it, after the above transformation, remains on the same line? If it exists, find all such lines; if not, explain the reason.

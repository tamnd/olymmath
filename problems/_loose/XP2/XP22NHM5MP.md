Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral in a counterclockwise direction. Construct $M, N, P, Q$ such that $MBA, NCB, PDC$, QAD are isosceles right triangles in the counterclockwise direction at $\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}, \mathrm{Q}$.

1) Show that MNPQ is a pseudo square, i.e., $\mathrm{MP}=\mathrm{NQ}$ and $M P \perp N Q$.
2) Give a necessary and sufficient condition for MNPQ to be a square.

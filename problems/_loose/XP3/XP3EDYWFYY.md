Let $D$ be a point on the side $[AB]$ of the isosceles triangle $ABC$ such that $|AB|=|AC|$. The parallel line to $BC$ passing through $D$ intersects $AC$ at $E$. If $m(\widehat A) = 20^\circ$, $|DE|=1$, $|BC|=a$, and $|BE|=a+1$, then which of the followings is equal to $|AB|$?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ 2a
\textbf{(B)}\ a^2-a
\textbf{(C)}\ a^2+1
\textbf{(D)}\ (a+1)^2
\textbf{(E)}\ a^2+a
$

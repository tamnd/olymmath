In some cells of a $2 n \times 2 n$ board, there are black and white chips. First, all black chips that are in the same column as some white chip are removed, and then all white chips that are in the same row as any of the remaining black chips are removed. Prove that either the black or the white chips left on the board do not exceed $n^{2}$.

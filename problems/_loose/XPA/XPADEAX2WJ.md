Let $h_{a}, h_{b}, h_{c}, \triangle$ represent the altitudes on the sides $a, b, c$ and the area of $\triangle ABC$, respectively. Prove that for $n \in \mathbf{R}^{+}$,
$$
\left(a h_{b}\right)^{n}+\left(b h_{c}\right)^{n}+\left(c h_{a}\right)^{n} \geqslant 3 \cdot 2^{n} \Delta^{n} .
$$

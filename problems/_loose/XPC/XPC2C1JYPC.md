Let $\Gamma$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$, and circle $\Gamma_{0}$ be internally tangent to circle $\Gamma$, and tangent to side $AB$ at point $P$. Draw a tangent line $CQ$ from point $C$ to circle $\Gamma_{0}$, where $Q$ is the point of tangency, and points $P$ and $Q$ are on the same side of line $BC$. Prove that the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ lies on line $PQ$.

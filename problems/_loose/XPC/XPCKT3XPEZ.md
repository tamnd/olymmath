Let $n$ and $k$ be two integers such that $n \geqslant 3$. Théo is organizing the class representative elections for his class of $n$ students: each student must vote for one of their classmates (everyone is a candidate), and no one votes for themselves. Then Théo groups the students so that if a student is in a group, the student they voted for is not in the same group.

For which values of $k$ is Théo sure to be able to group the students into at most $k$ groups, regardless of how the students vote?

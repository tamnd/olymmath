A biased coin has a probability of $\frac{2}{3}$ of landing heads when flipped. If the coin is flipped 50 times, then the probability that the number of heads is even is
(A) $25\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{50}$.
(B) $\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{3^{50}}\right)$.
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$.
(D) $\frac{1}{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{3^{50}}\right)$.
(E) $\frac{2}{3}$.
(43rd American High School Mathematics Examination, 1992)

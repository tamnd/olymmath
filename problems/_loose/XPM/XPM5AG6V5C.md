Let $n\geqslant 3$ be a positive integer. A circular necklace is called *fun* if it has $n{}$ black beads and $n{}$ white beads. A move consists of cutting out a segment of consecutive beads and reattaching it in reverse. Prove that it is possible to change any fun necklace into any other fun necklace using at most $(n-1)$ moves.

*Note:* Necklaces related by rotations or reflections are considered to be the same.

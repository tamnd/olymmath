Let $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of real numbers.

a) Show that if the sequence $\left(x_{n}^{2}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ is convergent, then the sequence $\left(\cos x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ is convergent.

b) Show that if $x_{n} \in(-1,1), \forall n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$, and the sequences $\left(x_{n}^{2}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ and $\left(\sin x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ are convergent, then the sequence $\left(x_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ is convergent.

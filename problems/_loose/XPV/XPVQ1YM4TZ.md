Let $l_{1}, l_{2}$ and $l_{3}$ be the corresponding lines of similar figures $F_{1}$, $F_{2}$ and $F_{3}$, intersecting at point $W$.

a) Prove that point $W$ lies on the circle of similarity of figures $F_{1}$, $F_{2}$ and $F_{3}$.

b) Let $J_{1}, J_{2}$ and $J_{3}$ be the points of intersection of lines $l_{1}, l_{2}$ and $l_{3}$ with the circle of similarity, distinct from point $W$. Prove that these points depend only on the figures $F_{1}$, $F_{2}$ and $F_{3}$ and do not depend on the choice of lines $l_{1}$, $l_{2}$ and $l_{3}$.

The points $J_{1}$, $J_{2}$ and $J_{3}$ are called the supplementary points of similar figures $F_{1}$, $F_{2}$ and $F_{3}$, and the triangle $J_{1} J_{2} J_{3}$ is called the supplementary triangle.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $X$ be a point inside the triangle. The lines $(BX)$ and $(CX)$ intersect $(AC)$ and $(AB)$ at $D$ and $E$. Furthermore, let $Y$ be the intersection of the diagonals in the quadrilateral $ADXE$. Let $Z$ be the orthogonal projection of $X$ onto the line $(BC)$. Show that the angles $\widehat{DZX}$ and $\widehat{EZA}$ are equal.

For positive integers $n$ and $p$, it holds that $n \geq 6, 4 \leq p \leq \frac{n}{2}$. Color $p$ vertices of a regular $n$-gon red, and the rest blue! Prove that there exist two congruent polygons with at least $\left[\frac{p}{2}\right]+1$ vertices, where all vertices of one polygon are red, and all vertices of the other are blue!

Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, and let $D$, $E$ be the feet of the perpendicular from points $B$, $C$ on the opposite sides respectively.
Let the tangents of $\omega$ at $B$ and $C$ be $l$ and $m$, respectively.
Then prove that $A$ exists on the circumcircle of the triangle bounded by $l$, $m$ and the straight line $DE$.

Let

$$
A=\left\{(x, y) \mid \log \left(1+x^{2}+y^{2}\right) \leqslant 1+\log (x+y)\right\}
$$

and

$$
B=\left\{(x, y) \mid \log \left(2+x^{2}+y^{2}\right) \leqslant 2+\log (x+y)\right\}
$$

be sets of points in the plane. What is the ratio of the area of set $B$ to the area of set $A$?

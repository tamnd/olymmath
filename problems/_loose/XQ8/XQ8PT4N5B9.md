Consider the set of non-ordered pairs of $n$ distinct elements $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$. If there are 3 non-ordered pairs $a_{i} a_{j}, a_{j} a_{k}, a_{k} a_{i}$, they are said to form a triangle. Given that $4 m \leqslant$ $n^{2}$, prove that it is always possible to select $m$ non-ordered pairs such that no triangle is formed among them.

The circle $\beta$ has its center at point $B$ and radius 40. The circles $\alpha$ and $\gamma$, with centers at $A$ and $C$ respectively, have the same radius $r$ and are both externally tangent to $\beta$. The three centers $A, B, C$ are collinear. Knowing that the lines passing through $A$ and tangent to $\beta$ are also tangent to $\gamma$, what can be said about the radius $r$?
(A) $r<72$
(B) $72 \leq r<75$
(C) $75 \leq r<78$
(D) $78 \leq r<81$
(E) $r \geq 81$

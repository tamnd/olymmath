A square table of format $6 \times 6$ is filled with numbers $\pm 1$ in such a way that, for each cell of the table, the product of the number written in that cell and all its neighboring cells is 1. (By neighboring cells, we mean cells that share at least one common vertex.) Prove that the number 1 is written in every cell of the table.

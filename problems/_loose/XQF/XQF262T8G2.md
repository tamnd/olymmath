On the plane, there is a broken line $A_{0} A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ such that

$$
A_{0} A_{1}=A_{1} A_{2}=A_{2} A_{3}=\ldots=A_{n-1} A_{n}=1
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}
60^{\circ} \leqslant \angle A_{3} A_{1} A_{2} \leqslant \angle A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} & \leqslant \angle A_{2} A_{3} A_{4} \leqslant \ldots \\
\ldots & \ldots \angle A_{n-2} A_{n-1} A_{n} \leqslant 120^{\circ}
\end{aligned}
$$

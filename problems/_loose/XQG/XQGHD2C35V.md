Prove that for positive real numbers $a, b, c, d, e, f$ the inequality

$$
\frac{a b}{a+b}+\frac{c d}{c+d}+\frac{e f}{e+f} \leq \frac{(a+c+e)(b+d+f)}{a+b+c+d+e+f}
$$

holds.

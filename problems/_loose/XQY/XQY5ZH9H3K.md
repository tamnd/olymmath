Let $x, y, z \in \mathbf{R}^{+}, A, B, C$ be the three interior angles of $\triangle A B C$, prove that:
$$
x \sin A+y \sin B+z \sin C \leqslant \frac{1}{2}(x y+y z+z x) \sqrt{\frac{x+y+z}{x y z}} .
$$

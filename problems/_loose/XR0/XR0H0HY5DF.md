The fraction $\frac{2 x+3}{2 x-7}$ is given, with $x \in \mathbb{N}, x \geq 4$. Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{1000}$ be the first 1000 natural numbers for which the fraction can be simplified.

a) Calculate $x_{1000}$.

b) Show that $x_{p}+x_{q}-x_{p+q}=1$ for any $p, q \in\{1,2, \ldots, 1000\}$.

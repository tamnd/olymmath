Let $n, k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, n \geq 2$ and $A, B \in M_{n}(\mathbb{C})$ such that $A^{k+1}=B^{k+1}$ and $A^{k} B=B^{k} A$.

Prove that if the matrix $A^{k}+B^{k}$ is invertible, then the matrix $A$ is invertible.

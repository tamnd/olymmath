1 Given that the quadrilateral $A B C D$ has an inscribed quadrilateral $E F G H$ which is exactly a parallelogram. On each side, take $A E^{\prime}=B E, B F^{\prime}=C F$, $C G^{\prime}=D G, D H^{\prime}=A H$. Prove:
(1) $E^{\prime} F^{\prime} G^{\prime} H^{\prime}$ is also a parallelogram;
(2) The parallelogram $E F G H$ has the same area as $E^{\prime} F^{\prime} G^{\prime} H^{\prime}$.
$$
\begin{array}{c}
G=2 \cdot G_{0}-G^{\prime}, \\
H=2 \cdot H_{0}-H^{\prime},
\end{array}
$$

Let $\Gamma_1, \Gamma_2, \Gamma_3$ be three pairwise externally tangent circles with radii $1,2,3,$ respectively. A circle passes through the centers of $\Gamma_2$ and $\Gamma_3$ and is externally tangent to $\Gamma_1$ at a point $P.$ Suppose $A$ and $B$ are the centers of $\Gamma_2$ and $\Gamma_3,$ respectively. What is the value of $\frac{{PA}^2}{{PB}^2}?$

Vasya and Petya are playing the following game. On the board, two numbers are written: $1 / 2009$ and $\frac{1}{2008}$. On each turn, Vasya names any number $x$, and Petya increases one of the numbers on the board (whichever he wants) by $x$. Vasya wins if at some point one of the numbers on the board becomes equal to 1. Can Vasya win, no matter how Petya acts?

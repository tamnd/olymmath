Erika nevertheless claims: If one knows that all these statements are false, the distribution of the balls (for each child, the number of blue and yellow balls) is uniquely determined. The three statements by Dieter are:

(1) The blue balls were distributed to fewer than three children.

(2) Annette received exactly two balls.

(3) Bernd received balls of different colors.

Is Erika right? Justify your answer!

Given a point $P$ and two planes $\left(S_{1}, S_{2}\right)$, and a distance $a$. Fit a line $g$ through the point $P$; let this line intersect the planes at points $A$ and $B$. Construct the line $g$ such that it forms the largest angle with the first plane and for which $\overline{P A} \cdot \overline{P B}=a^{2}$!

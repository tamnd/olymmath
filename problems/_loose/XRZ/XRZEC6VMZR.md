In an isosceles triangle $ABC$ ($AB = BC$), point $O$ is the center of the circumscribed circle. Point $M$ lies on the segment $BO$, and point $M'$ is symmetric to $M$ with respect to the midpoint of $AB$. Point $K$ is the intersection of $M'O$ and $AB$. Point $L$ on side $BC$ is such that $\angle CLO = \angle BLM$. Prove that points $O, K, B, L$ lie on the same circle.

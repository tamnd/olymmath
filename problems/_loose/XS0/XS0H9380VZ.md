To save the planet Cybertron, Optimus Prime established a Space Bridge on Earth consisting of $n(n \geqslant 3)$ energy pillars, which are erected on $n$ points on a plane, with no three points being collinear. The activation method is as follows: arbitrarily select an energy pillar, and emit a laser from it. The energy pillar will rotate the laser counterclockwise until it hits another energy pillar, at which point it stops rotating; the second energy pillar receives the laser, reflects it, and rotates it counterclockwise until the reflected laser hits the next energy pillar, at which point it stops rotating; this continues. If the path of the laser forms a directed cycle, the activation is successful. Prove: there exists a successful activation method, and the number of successful activation methods corresponding to directed cycles is no more than $2 n$.

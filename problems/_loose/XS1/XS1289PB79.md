In 80 cities, there are two types of flight routes being executed: any city is directly connected to at least 7 other cities; any two cities can be connected through a finite number of direct flights. Find the smallest positive integer $k$, such that no matter how the routes satisfying the conditions are arranged, any city can reach any other city with at most $k$ direct flights.

Ana and Barbu draw groups of stars on separate sheets of paper. Ana draws one star, then 3 stars, then 5, and so on, each time an odd number. Barbu draws 2 stars, then 4 stars, then 6, and so on, each time an even number. Show that, regardless of when the two children stop drawing, the total number of stars drawn by Ana cannot be equal to the total number of stars drawn by Barbu.

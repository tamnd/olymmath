Anna and Balázs are playing the following game. They alternately say an integer between 1 and 5, and add it to the sum of the numbers that have been said so far, with the restriction that the player whose turn it is cannot repeat the last number said by the previous player. The player who makes the sum exceed 39 for the first time loses. Anna starts the game. Who will be the winner if both play optimally?

What is the answer if they play the game with numbers between 1 and 3?

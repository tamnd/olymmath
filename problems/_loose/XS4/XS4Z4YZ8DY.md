In an acute $\triangle A B C$ with $C A \neq C B$ and incenter $O$ denote by $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ the tangent points of its excircles to the sides $C B$ and $C A$, respectively. The line $C O$ meets the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$ at point $P$ and the line through $P$ which is perpendicular to $C P$ meets the line $A B$ at point $Q$. Prove that the lines $Q O$ and $A_{1} B_{1}$ are parallel.

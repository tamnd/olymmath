A convex polyhedron $A B C D F E$ has five faces: $C D F, A B E, B C F E, A D F E$ and $A B C D$. Edge $A B$ is parallel to edge $C D$. Points $K$ and $L$ are located on edges $A D$ and $B C$ respectively, such that segment $K L$ divides the area of face $A B C D$ in half. Point $M$ is the midpoint of edge $E F$ and is the vertex of pyramid $M A B C D$, the volume of which is 6. Find the volume of pyramid $E K L F$, given that the volume of polyhedron $A B C D F E$ is 19.

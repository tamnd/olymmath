Consider the points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ on a circle with center $O$ and radius 1, such that $\overrightarrow{O A_{1}}+\overrightarrow{O A_{2}}+\ldots+\overrightarrow{O A_{n}}=\vec{O}$. Prove that $M A_{1}+M A_{2}+\ldots+M A_{n} \geq n$, for any point $M$ in the plane of the circle.

Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral such that triangle $B C D$ is acute and $|A B|=|A D|$. Let $K$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle C A D$ with side $C D$, and $L$ the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle B A C$ with side $B C$. Let $K^{\prime}$ and $L^{\prime}$ be the orthogonal projections of points $K$ and $L$ onto sides $B C$ and $C D$. Prove that points $B, D, L^{\prime}$, and $K^{\prime}$ lie on the same circle.

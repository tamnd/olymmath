Six players $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$, and $F$ are participating in a single round-robin table tennis tournament (each player plays one match against every other player). Every day, three matches are played simultaneously on three tables. It is known that on the first day, $B$ played against $D$; on the second day, $C$ played against $E$; on the third day, $D$ played against $F$; on the fourth day, $B$ played against $C$. Who will $A$ play against on the fifth day? Who will play against whom on the other two tables?

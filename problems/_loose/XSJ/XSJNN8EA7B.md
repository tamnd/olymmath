On the line $\ell$, points $A, B$, and $C$ are marked. Point $B$ lies between points $A$ and $C$ and $A B < B C$. Two circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$, whose radii are greater than $A B$ but less than $B C$, lie on opposite sides of the line $\ell$ and touch it at point $B$. Let $K$ be the point of intersection of the tangent to $\omega_{1}$ drawn from point $A$ and the tangent to $\omega_{2}$ drawn from point $C$, and $L$ be the point of intersection of the tangent to $\omega_{2}$ drawn from point $A$ and the tangent to $\omega_{1}$ drawn from point $C$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A K C L$ is cyclic.

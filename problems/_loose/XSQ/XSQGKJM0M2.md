There are $n$ athletes, numbered $1,2, \cdots, n$, who stand in a line in any order during a gathering. If in each round, some of them can swap positions in pairs, but in the same round, one person can participate in at most one such swap. Prove: At most two rounds of such operations are needed to arrange the queue in the order $1,2, \cdots, n$.
(2004 Jiangxi Province High School Girls' Competition Problem)

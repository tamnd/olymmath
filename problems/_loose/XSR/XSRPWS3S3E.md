Several pipes of the same cross-section, supplying water, and several pipes of another, but also the same cross-section, intended for removing water, are connected to this model. If four inlet and three outlet pipes are opened simultaneously, then after 5 hours, the volume of water in the model will increase by 1000 m ${}^{3}$. If two inlet and two outlet pipes are opened simultaneously for 2 hours, the increase in the volume of water will be $180 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$. How much water does one inlet pipe and one outlet pipe pass in one hour?

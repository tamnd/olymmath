We consider $2025$ points in the plane satisfying the following condition: given any $17$ of them, there exists a circle of diameter $1$ that covers at least $11$ of those $17$ points. Determine the minimum number of circles of diameter $2$ that are guaranteed to cover all $2025$ points.**Note:** A circle covers all points that lie on or inside it. The circles may overlap.

It is known that an given distance $AB$ (drawn with a ruler) can only be bisected in the following way using only a compass (without a ruler): On the arc of the circle centered at $A$ with radius $AB$, measure 3 times the distance $AB$ as a chord from $B$. The point thus obtained, $C$, is used to draw an arc of a circle with radius $CB$. On this arc, measure the distance $BD=BA$ as a chord from $B$. The circle centered at $D$ with radius $DB$ intersects the given segment $AB$ at the midpoint $F$.

a) Prove the correctness of this construction.

b) Construct the midpoint using only a compass if only the points $A$ and $B$ are given, but the segment $AB$ is not drawn.

Paul is playing a game with $N$ marbles and a bag, the marbles forming a pile. On the first turn, Paul removes one marble from the pile to put it in his bag and separates the remaining pile into two non-empty piles. More generally, at each turn, Paul chooses a pile of at least 4 marbles, takes one and puts it in his bag, and separates the remaining pile into two non-empty piles, the game stopping when no more moves are possible. Paul wins if he reaches a situation where all piles consist of 3 marbles, and loses otherwise.

a) Show that if $N=2019$, Paul can win.

b) Show that if $N=2020$, Paul cannot win.

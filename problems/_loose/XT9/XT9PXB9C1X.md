Check the following properties of the symmetric difference operation $\triangle$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(A \triangle B) \triangle C= & A \triangle(B \triangle C), (A \triangle B) \triangle(B \triangle C)=A \triangle C \\
& A \triangle B=C \Leftrightarrow A=B \triangle C
\end{aligned}
$$

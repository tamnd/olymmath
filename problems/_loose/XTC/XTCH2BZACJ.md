(2005 Shanghai College Entrance Examination Question) In the rectangular coordinate plane, it is known that points $P_{1}(1,2), P_{2}\left(2,2^{2}\right)$, $P_{3}\left(3,2^{3}\right), \cdots, P_{n}\left(n, 2^{n}\right)$, where $n$ is a positive integer. For any point $A_{0}$ in the plane, let $A_{1}$ be the point symmetric to $A_{0}$ with respect to $P_{1}$, $A_{2}$ be the point symmetric to $A_{1}$ with respect to $P_{2}$, ..., and $A_{n}$ be the point symmetric to $A_{n-1}$ with respect to $P_{n}$.
(1) Find the coordinates of the vector $\overrightarrow{A_{0} A_{2}}$.
(2) When point $A_{0}$ moves on the curve $C$, the trajectory of point $A_{2}$ is the graph of the function $y=f(x)$, where $f(x)$ is a periodic function with a period of 3, and when $x \in(0,3]$, $f(x)=\lg x$. Find the analytical expression of the function whose graph is the curve $C$ in the interval (1,4$]$.

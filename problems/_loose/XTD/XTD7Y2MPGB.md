Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct prime numbers. Prove that
a) $p^{q}+q^{p} \equiv p+q(\bmod p q)$

b) $\left[\frac{p^{q}+q^{p}}{p q}\right]$ is an even number if $p, q \neq 2$.

Four grasshoppers were sitting at the vertices of a square. Every second, one of the grasshoppers jumps over another to a symmetric point (if $A$ jumps over $B$ to point $A_{1}$, then vectors $\overrightarrow{A B}$ and $\overrightarrow{B A_{1}}$ are equal). Prove that three grasshoppers cannot end up

a) on one straight line parallel to a side of the square;

b) on one arbitrary straight line.

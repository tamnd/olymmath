Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. For any prime $p$ and integers $u, v$, if $p \mid (uv + u + v)$, then $p \mid (f(u) f(v) - 1)$. In this case, $f(x)$ is called "good". Find all good $f(x)$.

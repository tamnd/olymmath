Let positive integers $a, m, n$ satisfy $\sqrt{a^{2}-4 \sqrt{2}}=\sqrt{m}-\sqrt{n}$. Then the values of $a, m, n$
(A) have one set.
(B) have two sets.
(C) have more than two sets.
(D) do not exist.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1996)

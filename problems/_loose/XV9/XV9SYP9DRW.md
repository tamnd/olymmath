In $\triangle A B C$, $A C=C D$, and $\angle C A B-\angle A B C=30^{\circ}$, then the degree measure of $\angle B A D$ is
(A) $30^{\circ}$.
(B) $20^{\circ}$.
(C) $22.5^{\circ}$.
(D) $10^{\circ}$.
(E) $15^{\circ}$.
(8th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1957)

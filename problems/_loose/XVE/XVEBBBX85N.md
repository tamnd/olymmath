Prove that the equation

$$
x^{2}+2 y^{2}=z^{2}+2 t^{2}
$$

has an infinite number of positive integer solutions. Then, find the functions $f: \mathbb{N}^{*} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}^{*}$ such that for all positive integers $m$ and $n$, we have:

$$
f\left(f(n)^{2}+2 f(m)^{2}\right)=n^{2}+2 m^{2}
$$

Let $n$ be a natural number. Simplify the expression

$$
\left(\frac{a+a^{-1} b^{2}}{a-a^{-1} b^{2}}-1\right)^{-1}\left(b^{n}(a-b)^{-1}-b^{n}(a+b)^{-1}\right)
$$

For which values of $a$ and $b$ does the expression have no meaning?

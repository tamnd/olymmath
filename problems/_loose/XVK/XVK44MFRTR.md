(Slovenian National Team Selection Test for the 44th IMO (2003)): Two circles $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ intersect at points $P$ and $Q$, and the common tangent closer to point $P$ touches $\odot O_{1}$ at point $A$ and $\odot O_{2}$ at point $B$. A line tangent to $\odot O_{1}$ at point $P$ intersects $\odot O_{2}$ again at point $C$, and lines $A P$ and $B C$ intersect at point $R$. Prove that the circumcircle of $\triangle P Q R$ is tangent to lines $B P$ and $B R$.

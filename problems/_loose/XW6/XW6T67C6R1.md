For some positive numbers $A, B, C$ and $D$ , the system of equations $$ \begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 = A |x| + |y| = B \end{cases} $$
has $m$ solutions, while the system of equations $$ \begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 +z^2= X |x| + |y| +|z| = D \end{cases} $$
has $n$ solutions. If $m > n > 1$ , find $m$ and $n$ .

( G Galperin)

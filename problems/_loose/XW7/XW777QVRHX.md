a) Show that point $\mathrm{P}_{3}$ is the midpoint of [ $\mathrm{P}_{1} \mathrm{P}_{4}$].

b) Determine the natural number $\mathrm{k}$, for which $\mathrm{P}_{5} \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{k}}=18200$.

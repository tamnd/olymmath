Choose two coprime positive integers written on the blackboard, erase them and write their sum, which is called one operation. Two players, A and B, take turns performing the operation on the blackboard. The player who cannot perform the operation loses. A starts first. Prove: if the original positive integers on the blackboard consist of $2019$ and $2020$ ones respectively, B has a winning strategy.

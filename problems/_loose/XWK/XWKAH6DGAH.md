On two intersecting lines, segments $A B$ and $C D$ are given. Prove that the volume of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ does not change if these segments, without changing their lengths, are moved along these lines. Calculate this volume if the lengths of the segments $m=|A B|, n=|C D|$, the distance $h$ between the lines $A B$ and $C D$, and $\varphi-$ the angle between the lines are given.

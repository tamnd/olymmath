Consider a set $G \subset \mathbb{R}$ that simultaneously satisfies the properties:
a) $1 \in G$;
b) $x \in G \Rightarrow \sqrt{x+2} \in G$;
c) $\sqrt{x+3} \in G \Rightarrow x+4 \in G$;

Show that $\mathbb{N}^{*} \subset G$.

Lucian Dragomir, Gazeta Matematică nr. 12/2014

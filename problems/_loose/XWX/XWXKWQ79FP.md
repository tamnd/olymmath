*Mediovagio* is a computer game that consists in a $3 \times 3$ table in which each of the nine cells has a integer number from $1$ to $n$ . When one clicks a cell, the numbers in the clicked cell and in the cells that share an edge with it are increased by $1$ and the sum is evaluated ${}\bmod n$ . Determine the values of $n$ for which it's possible, with a finite number of clicks, obtain any combination of numbers from an given initial combination.

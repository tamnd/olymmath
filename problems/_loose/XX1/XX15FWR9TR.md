Consider two circles $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ that intersect at $A$ and $B$. The tangent at $A$ to $\Gamma_{1}$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ again at $C$ and the tangent at $B$ to $\Gamma_{2}$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ again at $D$. The line $(B C)$ intersects $\Gamma_{1}$ again at $F$ and the line $(A D)$ intersects $\Gamma_{2}$ again at $E$.

Prove that $D E C F$ is a parallelogram.

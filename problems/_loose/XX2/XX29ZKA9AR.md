(a) Prove that there exists a natural number that is divisible by 2005 and whose sum of digits is equal to 2.

(b) Let $x_{n}$ be the natural number obtained by writing down the natural numbers from 1 to $n$ consecutively (for example, $x_{1}=1, x_{2}=12, x_{3}=123, \ldots$, $x_{13}=12345678910111213, \ldots$). Prove that in the sequence $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots$ there are infinitely many terms that are divisible by 2005.

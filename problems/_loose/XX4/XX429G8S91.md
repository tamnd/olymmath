The mutinous soldiers were caught. Five artillerymen, five grenadiers, five fusiliers, and fifteen privates were arrested. The General sentenced fifteen of them to death, and left it to the Major to arrange them in a row as he pleased, and to shoot every ninth man, starting from the left; when he had gone through the whole row, he was to start again from the left, and continue the count until the row of thirty had been reduced to fifteen, who would then be pardoned. The Major wanted to save the artillerymen, grenadiers, and fusiliers. The question is, in what order did he arrange them?

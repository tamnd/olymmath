In a mathematics competition: (1) The number of competition problems is $n(n \geqslant 4)$; (2) Each problem is solved by exactly 4 people; (3) For any two problems, exactly 1 person solves both problems. If the number of participants is greater than or equal to $4 n$, find the minimum value of $n$ such that there always exists 1 person who solves all the competition problems.
(15th Korean Olympiad Problem)

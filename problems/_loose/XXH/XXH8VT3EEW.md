$A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{s}$ are some subsets of $\{1,2, \cdots, M\}$. Assume that $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{s}$ do not contain each other (i.e., none is a subset of another), and let $a_{i}=\left|A_{i}\right|$. Prove:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{3} \frac{1}{\mathrm{C}_{M}^{a_{M}}} \leqslant 1 .
$$

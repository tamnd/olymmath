Let $S$ be a non-empty set of strictly positive integers satisfying the following property: For all integers $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b} \in \mathrm{S}$, the integer $\mathrm{ab}+1$ also belongs to $S$.

Show that the set of prime numbers that do not divide any of the elements of $S$ is finite.

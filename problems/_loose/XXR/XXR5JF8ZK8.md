If $a$ and $b$ are the legs of a right triangle, then $\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}$ represents the hypotenuse, and if $a$ is a leg and $c$ is the hypotenuse, then $\sqrt{c^{2}-a^{2}}$ represents the other leg. Using this, construct: $a \sqrt{5}, a \sqrt{11}, a \sqrt{13}, a \sqrt{17}, a \sqrt{26}, a \sqrt{34}$.

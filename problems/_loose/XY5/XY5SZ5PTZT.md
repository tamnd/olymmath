Let points $A, B, C$ lie on a circle, and line $b$ be tangent to this circle at point $B$. From point $P$, lying on line $b$, perpendiculars $P A_{1}$ and $P C_{1}$ are dropped to lines $A B$ and $B C$ respectively (points $A_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ lie on segments $A B$ and $B C$). Prove that $A_{1} C_{1} \perp A C$.

(L. Emelyanov)

Furthermore, let the angle at vertex $A$ of the triangle be denoted by $\alpha$, and the distance from the orthocenter to the side $AB$ be denoted by $t$.

The triangle is to be constructed from the given segments $l$ and $t$ and the angle $\alpha$.

Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle with an area of 2, $P$ a point on side $CD$, and $Q$ the point where the incircle of $\triangle PAB$ touches side $AB$. The product $PA \cdot PB$ varies as point $P$ changes. When $PA \cdot PB$ is minimized,
(1) Prove: $AB \geqslant 2BC$;
(2) Find the value of $AQ \cdot BQ$.

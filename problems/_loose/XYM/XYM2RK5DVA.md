From a point $P$ outside the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$, two tangents are drawn to the parabola, touching it at points $M$ and $N$, and $F$ is the focus of the parabola. Prove:
(1) $|P F|^{2}=|M F||N F|$;
(2) $\angle P M F=\angle F P N$.

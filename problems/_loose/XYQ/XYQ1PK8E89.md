We have $3^{2 k}$ indistinguishable coins, one of which is counterfeit and slightly lighter than the genuine ones. Additionally, we have three two-pan balances. It is known that two of the balances are functional, while one is broken (the outcome of the weighing on the broken balance is unrelated to the weight of the coins placed on it, i.e., it can be either correct or distorted in any direction, and the distortion can vary from one weighing to another). It is unknown which balances are functional and which are broken. How can we determine the counterfeit coin in $3 k+1$ weighings?

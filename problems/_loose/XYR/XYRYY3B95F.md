Let $h_a$ , $h_b$ and $h_c$ be the altitudes of a triangle $\triangle ABC$ ejected from the vertices $A$ , $B$ and $C$ , respectively.
Similarly, let $h_x$ , $h_y$ and $h_z$ be the altitudes of an another triangle $\triangle XYZ$ . Show that if $$ h_a : h_b : h_c = h_x : h_y : h_z, $$ then the triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle XYZ$ are similar.

Let $ \alpha ,\ r$ be real numbers such that $ r > 1,\ r\neq 3,\ r\neq 4$.

Find the values of $ \alpha ,\ r$ such that $ \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k = 1}^n \frac {n^{\alpha}}{(n + k)^{r}} = \frac {r - 3}{(r - 1)(r - 4)}$.

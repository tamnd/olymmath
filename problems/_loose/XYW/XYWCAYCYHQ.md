In a right-angled trapezoid $A B C D$, with right angles at vertices $A$ and $B$, there are two circles. One of them is tangent to the lateral sides and to the larger base $A D$, while the other is tangent to the lateral sides, to the smaller base $B C$, and to the first circle.

1) The line passing through the centers of the circles intersects $A D$ at point $P$. Prove that $\frac{A P}{P D}=\sin (\angle D)$.
2) Determine the area of the trapezoid, if the radii of the circles are $\frac{4}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{3}$.

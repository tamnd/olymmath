Let's determine the point $A_{1}$ on the side line $BC$ of triangle $ABC$ such that if the projection of $A_{1}$ onto $CA$ is $B_{1}$, and the projection of $B_{1}$ onto $AB$ is $C_{1}$, then the projection of $C_{1}$ onto $BC$ falls on $A_{1}$. Similarly, let $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ be the points on the side lines $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, such that $A_{2} C_{2} \perp BA, C_{2} B_{2} \perp AC, B_{2} A_{2} \perp CB$. Show that the triangles $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ are congruent.

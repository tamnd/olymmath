We call a function $f: \mathbb{Q}^+ \to \mathbb{Q}^+$ *good* if for all $x,y \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ we have: $$ f(x)+f(y)\geq 4f(x+y). $$ a) Prove that for all good functions $f: \mathbb{Q}^+ \to \mathbb{Q}^+$ and $x,y,z \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ $$ f(x)+f(y)+f(z) \geq 8f(x+y+z) $$ b) Does there exists a good functions $f: \mathbb{Q}^+ \to \mathbb{Q}^+$ and $x,y,z \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ such that $$ f(x)+f(y)+f(z) < 9f(x+y+z) ? $$

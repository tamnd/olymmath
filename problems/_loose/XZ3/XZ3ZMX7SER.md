Three nonintersecting circles $k_{i}\left(O_{i}, r_{i}\right), i=1,2,3$, where $r_{1}<$ $r_{2}<r_{3}$, are tangent to the arms of an angle. One of the arms is tangent to $k_{1}$ and $k_{3}$ at points $A$ and $B$ and the other one is tangent to $k_{2}$ at point $C$. Let $K=A C \cap k_{1}, L=A C \cap k_{2}, M=B C \cap k_{2}$ and $N=B C \cap k_{3}$. The four lines through $C$ and $P=A M \cap B K, Q=A M \cap B L, R=A N \cap B K$ and $S=A N \cap B L$, meet $A B$ at the points $X, Y, Z$ and $T$, respectively. Prove that $X Z=Y T$.

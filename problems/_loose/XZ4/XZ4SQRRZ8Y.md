Let $x, y, z$ all take positive real numbers, and $x+y+z=1$. Prove:
$$
\frac{3 x^{2}-x}{1+x^{2}}+\frac{3 y^{2}-y}{1+y^{2}}+\frac{3 z^{2}-z}{1+z^{2}} \geqslant 0 \text {. }
$$

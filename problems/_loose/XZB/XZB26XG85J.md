Given the vertices of an equilateral triangle $ABC$, we draw the shorter arcs $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ with radius $a$ around each vertex. Points $K$, $L$, and $M$ are points on these three arcs such that $KL = LM = MK$. Prove the following:

1. The centroids of triangles $KLM$ and $ABC$ coincide.
2. The circle with diameter $KL$ passes through point $C$.
3. If $K$ traverses the shorter arc $BC$, then the geometric locus of the midpoints of the sides of triangle $KLM$ is similar to the arc triangle of $ABC$.

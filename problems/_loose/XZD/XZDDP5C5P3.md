We wrote the numbers from 1 to 2009 on a piece of paper. In the second step, we also wrote down twice each number on the paper, then erased the numbers that appeared twice. We repeat this step in such a way that in the $i$-th step, we write down $i$ times each number that is currently visible on the paper, then erase the numbers that appear twice. Prove that after every step, there will be at least 2009 numbers on the paper.

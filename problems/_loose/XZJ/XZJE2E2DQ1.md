The thirty-third question: Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}=n$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{a_{i}}-n \geq$
$$
\frac{8(n-1)\left(1-\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}\right)}{n^{2}}.
$$

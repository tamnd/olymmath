A circle of radius $r$ is inscribed in a triangle, and three circles of radii $r_{1}, r_{2}$, and $r_{3}$ are constructed, each touching two sides of the triangle and the inscribed circle. We will prove that the following inequality holds:

$$
\left(r_{1} r_{2}\right)^{m}+\left(r_{2} r_{3}\right)^{m}+\left(r_{3} r_{1}\right)^{m} \geqslant 3\left(\frac{r}{3}\right)^{2 m}
$$

for $m \geqslant \frac{1}{2}$.

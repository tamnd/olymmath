Given are two intersecting circles $ o(O_1, r_1) $ and $ o(O_2, r_2) $ and a line tangent to them at points $ A $ and $ B $, respectively. Point $ C $ is the common point of these circles closer to the line $ AB $, $ R $ is the radius of the circumcircle of triangle $ ABC $, and $ d = O_1O_2 $. Prove that $ \measuredangle ABC = \measuredangle O_1CO_2 $ if and only if $ d^2 = r_1^2 + r_2^2 + R^2 $.

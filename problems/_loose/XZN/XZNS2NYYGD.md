a) Prove that the number of different sequences $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2 n}$, for which $a_{i}= \pm 1, a_{1} \geqslant 0$, $a_{1}+a_{2} \geqslant 0, \ldots, a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{2 n-1} \geqslant 0$ and $a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{2 n}=0$, is $c_{n}$.

b) A cashier, who has no money at the initial moment, sells tickets for 50 rubles. The queue consists of $2 n$ people, half of whom have only one 100-ruble bill, and the other half have 50 rubles. Prove that the number of different orders of the queue, for which the cashier can give change to everyone, is $c_{n}$.

For $n \in \mathbf{N}, a, b$ being given real numbers, and $x_{0}, x, \cdots, x_{n}$ being real variables, it is known that
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n} x_{i}=a, \sum_{i=0}^{n} x_{i}^{2}=b .
$$

Determine the range of variation for $x_{0}$.

Given a quadrilateral $A B C D$ with an inscribed circle $\odot I$, the line $A D$ intersects $B C$ and $A B$ intersects $C D$ at points $Q$ and $P$, respectively. Points $B$ and $D$ lie on segments $A P$ and $A Q$, respectively. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the incenters of $\triangle P B D$ and $\triangle Q B D$, respectively. The line $P Y$ intersects $Q X$ at point $R$. Prove: $R I \perp B D$.

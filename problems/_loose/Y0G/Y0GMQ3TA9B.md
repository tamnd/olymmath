Given triangle $ABC$ with sides $\overline{AC}=b, \overline{BC}=a$ and the altitude from vertex $C$, $\overline{CD}=h$. Determine the point $M$ on the altitude such that the sum

$$
\overline{AM}^{2}+\overline{BM}^{2}+\overline{CM}^{2}
$$

is minimized.

Show that in any $3 \times 3$ magic square, the sum of the squares of the numbers in the first row is equal to the sum of the squares of the numbers in the third row. (We call an $n \times n$ array of numbers a magic square if the sum of the numbers in each row, each column, and the two diagonals is the same.)

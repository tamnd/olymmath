Given two circles with a common center. The tangent to the smaller circle touches it at point $B$ and intersects the larger circle at points $A$ and $C$ . Point $D$ is the midpoint of the segment $AB$ . A line drawn through point $A$ intersects the smaller circle at points $E$ and $F$ ( $AE < AF$ ). A point $M$ is marked on the segment $AC$ such that $DM = EM$ and $MC = MF$ . Find the ratio $AM : MC$ .

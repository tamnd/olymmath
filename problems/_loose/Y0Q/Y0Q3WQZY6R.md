For a solid circle with radius $R$ and a point $A$ on its circumference, we draw a tangent and a chord $MN$ parallel to the tangent, at a distance $x$ from the tangent. Let the projection of point $M$ onto the tangent be $P$, and the projection of point $N$ be $Q$. Determine the value of $x$ such that the perimeter of the rectangle $PMNQ$ is $2k$, and establish the condition for the possibility of a solution, as well as the number of solutions!

Let $f(x)$ be a function defined in $0<x<\frac{\pi}{2}$ satisfying:

(i) $f\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=0$

(ii) $f'(x)\tan x=\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^x \frac{2\cos t}{\sin t}dt$.

Find $f(x)$.

*1987 Sapporo Medical University entrance exam*

Two corridors, each 1 m high and 1 m wide, run perpendicular to each other on the first and second floors of a building. The separating floor has been removed, creating a 1 m x 1 m hole in the floor of one and the ceiling of the other. What is the maximum length of the beam that can be passed from one corridor to the other through the hole? (Consider the beam as a non-bending segment of zero thickness. The thickness of the floor is also zero, i.e., the floor of the upper corridor and the ceiling of the lower corridor are in the same plane.)

Let $n(n \geqslant 1)$ be the product of $n$ given prime numbers (each prime can appear several times, or not at all) to form $n+1$ positive integers. Prove that among these $n+1$ numbers, some can be selected whose product is a perfect square.
(29th International Mathematical Olympiad Candidate Problem, 1988)

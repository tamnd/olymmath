The winner of a volleyball match is the team that first wins 3 sets. If the final result in sets is 3:0 or 3:1, the winner gets 3 points, and the loser gets 0 points. If the final result is 3:2, the winner gets 2 points, and the loser gets 1 point.

In a volleyball tournament, four teams participated: $A, B, C$, and $D$. Each team played one match against each of the others. After all matches were played, the table looked like this:

| team | points | sets won | sets lost |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $A$ | 9 | 9 | 1 |
| $B$ | 5 | 7 | 6 |
| $C$ | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| $D$ | 2 | 4 | 9 |

Determine the results of all the matches played.

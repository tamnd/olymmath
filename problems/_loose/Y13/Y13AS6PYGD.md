For a given natural number $k{}$ , a convex polygon is called $k{}$ *-triangular* if it is the intersection of some $k{}$ triangles.

- What is the largest $n{}$ for which there exist a $k{}$ -triangular $n{}$ -gon?

- What is the largest $n{}$ for which any convex $n{}$ -gon is $k{}$ -triangular?

*Proposed by P. Kozhevnikov*

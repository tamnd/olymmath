The base of the prism is an $n$-sided polygon. It is required to color all $2n$ of its vertices with three colors such that each vertex is connected by edges to vertices of all three colors.

a) Prove that if $n$ is divisible by 3, then such a coloring is possible.

b) Prove that if such a coloring is possible, then $n$ is divisible by 3.

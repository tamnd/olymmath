Consider a circle $\mathrm{C}(\mathrm{O})$ and a chord $[M N]$ of this circle. In one of the circular segments determined by the chord $[M N]$, two circles $\mathrm{C}_{1}\left(\mathrm{O}_{1}\right)$ and $\mathrm{C}_{2}\left(\mathrm{O}_{2}\right), O_{1} \neq O_{2}$, are inscribed, which are tangent to the circle $\mathrm{C}(\mathrm{O})$ at points $A$ and $B$, and to the chord $[M N]$ - at points $C$ and $D$, respectively. Prove that the points $A, B, C$ and $D$ lie on a circle.

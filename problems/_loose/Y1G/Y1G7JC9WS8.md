Given a rectangular board with $m$ columns and $n$ rows, where $m > n$ and the numbers $m$ and $n$ have the same parity. In the bottom left corner of the board is a white pawn, and in the top right corner is a black pawn. Players $B$ and $C$ take turns moving the pawns according to the rules of chess. Player $B$ starts the game and always plays with the white pawn, while player $C$ always plays with the black pawn. The player who manages to place their pawn under attack by the opponent's pawn wins the game. Which player has a winning strategy?

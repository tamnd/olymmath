If for the sides $a, b$ and their respective opposite angles $\alpha, \beta$ of triangle $ABC$ it holds that

$$
\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right) \sin (\alpha-\beta)=\left(a^{2}-b^{2}\right) \sin (\alpha+\beta)
$$

then triangle $ABC$ is isosceles or right-angled. Prove it!

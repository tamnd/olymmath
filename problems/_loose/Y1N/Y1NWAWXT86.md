For each positive integer $n$ , let $H_n = \frac{1}{1} + \frac{1}{2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{n}$ . If $$ \sum_{n=4}^{\infty} \frac{1}{nH_nH_{n-1}} = \frac{M}{N} $$ for relatively prime positive integers $M$ and $N$ , compute $100M+N$ .

A dragon imprisoned six dwarfs in a cave and said: "I have seven caps of seven colors of the rainbow. Tomorrow morning I will blindfold you and put a cap on each of you, and hide one cap. Then I will remove the blindfolds, and you will be able to see the caps on the heads of the others, but I will not allow you to communicate. After that, each of you will secretly tell me the color of the hidden cap. If at least three of you guess correctly, I will release all of you. If fewer - I will eat you for lunch." How can the dwarfs agree in advance to act in order to save themselves?

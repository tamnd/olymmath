Given a real number $a>1$, construct a bounded infinite sequence $x_{0}, x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots$, such that for every pair of distinct non-negative integers $i, j$, we have $\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right| \cdot|i-j|^{a} \geqslant 1$.

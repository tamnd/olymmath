In a mentoring program at a school, several first-grade students can become friends with one sixth-grade student, but a sixth-grade student cannot be friends with multiple first-grade students. It is known that $\frac{1}{3}$ of the sixth-grade students and $\frac{2}{5}$ of the first-grade students become friends. Then, the proportion of students who have friends among the first and sixth grades is ( ).
(A) $\frac{2}{15}$
(B) $\frac{4}{11}$
(C) $\frac{11}{30}$
(D) $\frac{3}{8}$
(E) $\frac{11}{15}$

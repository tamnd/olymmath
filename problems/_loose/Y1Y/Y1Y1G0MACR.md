Fernando is a cautious person, and the gate of his house has 10 distinct padlocks, each of which can only be opened by its respective key, and each key opens only one padlock. To open the gate, he must have at least one key for each padlock. For security, Fernando has distributed exactly two different keys in the 45 drawers at his workplace, such that two keys in the same drawer open different padlocks, and there are no two drawers containing keys that open exactly the same padlocks.

a) How many possible pairs of keys can be formed with 10 distinct keys?

b) How many copies of each key does Fernando need to have to form all possible pairs of keys?

c) What is the minimum number of drawers that Fernando must open to ensure he has 10 distinct keys and can open the gate to his house?

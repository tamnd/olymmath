## Task 3

After long January holidays, on the first working day, 36 sad office employees decided to play with a ball. The rules of the game are as follows: a sad employee who hits another sad employee with the ball becomes cheerful and no longer feels sad. The employee who is hit is eliminated from the game. After two hours, only one employee remained in the game. By this point, which type of employees had more - those who were eliminated as sad or those who were eliminated as cheerful? Provide an answer and prove it.

Let $x$ and $y$ be integers and let $p$ be a prime number. Suppose that there exist realatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that $$ x^m \equiv y^n \pmod p $$ Prove that there exists an unique integer $z$ modulo $p$ such that $$ x \equiv z^n \pmod p \text{and} y \equiv z^m \pmod p $$

Suppose $x$ and $y$ are real numbers such that $$x^2+xy+y^2=2 \text{and} x^2-y^2=\sqrt5.$$ The sum of all possible distinct values of $|x|$ can be written in the form $ \sum_{i=1}^n\sqrt{a_i}$, where each of the $a_i$ is a rational number. If $ \sum_{i=1}^na_i=\frac mn$ where $m$ and $n$ are positive realtively prime integers, what is $100m+n$?

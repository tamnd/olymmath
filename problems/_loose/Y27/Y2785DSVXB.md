On a circle, $n$ points are marked ($n \geqslant 5$). Petya and Vasya take turns (starting with Petya) to draw one chord connecting pairs of these points that are not adjacent. Any two drawn chords can only intersect at their endpoints. The player who cannot make a move loses. For which $n$ will Petya win regardless of Vasya's actions?

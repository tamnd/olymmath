Let a circle and an equilateral hyperbola intersect at points $ A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}, A_{4} $, where $ A_{1} A_{2} $ are the endpoints of a diameter of the circle.
(1) Prove that the midpoint of segment $ A_{3} A_{4} $ is the center of the hyperbola;
(2) Find the angle between the tangents to the hyperbola at points $ A_{3} $ and $ A_{4} $ and the line $ A_{1} A_{2} $.

$C$ is a moving point outside the line $A B$. Construct squares $C A D F$ and $C B E G$ on the same side of $A B$ with $A C$ and $B C$ as sides. Regardless of the position of point $C$, prove:
( I ) The sum of the distances $D D^{\prime}, E E^{\prime}$ from $D$ and $E$ to the line $A B$ is a constant;
(II) The midpoint $M$ of segment $D E$ is a fixed point.

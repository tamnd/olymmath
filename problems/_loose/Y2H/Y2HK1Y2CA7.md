$P$ is any point on the plane of $\triangle A B C$. Perpendiculars are drawn from $P$ to the lines containing the sides of $\triangle A B C$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $A_{1} 、 B_{1} 、 C_{1}$, respectively. If $O P=d$, prove that $S_{\triangle A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}}=\frac{\left|R^{2}-d^{2}\right|}{4 R^{2}} S_{\triangle A B C}$, where $R$ is the circumradius of $\triangle A B C$, and $O$ is the circumcenter of $\triangle A B C$.

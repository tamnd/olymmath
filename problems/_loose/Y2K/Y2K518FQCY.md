Let $A B C$ be a triangle and $D$ a point on the interior of the segment $B C$. Let $X$ be another point inside the segment $B C$ different from $D$ and let $Y$ be the intersection of $A X$ with the circumcircle of $A B C$. Let $P$ be the second point of intersection of the circumcircles of $A B C$ and $D X Y$. Prove that $P$ is independent of the choice of $X$.

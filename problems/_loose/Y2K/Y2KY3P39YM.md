Prove that for any triangle the equation holds $a \cdot \cos (\beta + \gamma ) + b \cdot \cos (\gamma +\alpha) + c\cdot \cos (\alpha -\beta) = 0$ ,
where $a, b, c$ are the sides of the triangle and $\alpha, \beta,\gamma$ according to their angles sizes of opposite angles.

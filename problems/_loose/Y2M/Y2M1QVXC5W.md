8,9 On the sides of triangle $A B C$, equilateral triangles $B C A 1, C A B 1, A B C 1$ are constructed outside the triangle, and segments $A A 1, B B 1$, and $C C 1$ are drawn. Prove that

a) these segments are equal to each other;

b) these segments intersect at one point;

c) if this point is inside triangle $A B C$, then the sum of the distances from it to the three vertices of the triangle is equal to the length of each of the segments $A A 1, B B 1, C C 1$.

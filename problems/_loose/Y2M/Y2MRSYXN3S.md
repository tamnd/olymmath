Let $x_{1} \ldots x_{n}$ be a finite sequence of real numbers such that:

i) the sum of any 7 consecutive terms is always strictly positive;

ii) the sum of any 13 consecutive terms is always strictly negative.

Which of the following statements is true?

(A) The sequence has at most 18 terms

(B) The sequence can have 19 terms

(C) The sequence has exactly 21 terms

(D) The sequence has at least 18 terms

(E) There exist sequences of arbitrary length that satisfy i) and ii).

Given an angle with vertex B. Take an arbitrary isosceles trapezoid, the lateral sides of which lie on the sides of the given angle. Through two opposite vertices of the trapezoid, draw tangents to the circumscribed circle around it. Denote by $M$ the point of intersection of these tangents. What figure do all such points $M$ form?

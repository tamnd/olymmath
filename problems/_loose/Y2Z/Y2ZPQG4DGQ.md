The product $1 \cdot 2 \cdot \ldots \cdot n$ is denoted by $n!$ and called *n-factorial*. Prove that the product
$$ 1!2!3!\ldots 49!51! \ldots 100! $$
(the factor $50!$ is missing)
is the square of an integer number.

Let $O$ be the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $ABC$, and let $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$ be the points of tangency of this circle with the sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. On the rays $OA_{0}, OB_{0}, OC_{0}$, points $L, M, K$ are taken, respectively, at equal distances from $O$. a) Prove that the lines $AL, BM$, and $CK$ intersect at one point. b) Let $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ be the projections of $A, B, C$ onto an arbitrary line $l$ passing through $O$. Prove that the lines $A_{1}L, B_{1}M$, and $C_{1}K$ intersect at one point (Harano).

Point $K$ lies on the base $AD$ of trapezoid $ABCD$, such that $|AK|=\lambda|AD|$. Find the ratio $|AM|:|AD|$, where $M$ is the point of intersection with $AD$ of the line passing through the points of intersection of the lines $AB$ and $CD$ and the lines $BK$ and $AC$.

Taking $\lambda=1 / n, n=1,2,3, \ldots$, obtain a method for dividing a given segment into $n$ equal parts using a single ruler, if a line parallel to it is given.

(SFRY, 75). On a plane, $n+4$ points are marked, four of which are located at the vertices of a square, and the remaining $n$ lie inside this square. Any of the marked points are allowed to be connected by segments, provided that no constructed segment contains marked points other than its endpoints, and no two of the constructed segments have common points other than, possibly, their endpoints. Find the maximum number of segments that can be constructed in this way.

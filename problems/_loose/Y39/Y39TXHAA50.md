Let $P, Q, R$ and $S$ be polynomials with integer coefficients such that

$$
P\left(x^{5}\right)+x Q\left(x^{5}\right)+x^{2} R\left(x^{5}\right)=\left(1+x+x^{2}+x^{3}+x^{4}\right) S(x)
$$

Prove that $P(x)$ is divisible by $(x-1)$.

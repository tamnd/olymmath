To edit the triangle $ABC$, if the lengths of two sides $AC=b$, $AB=c$ are given, and the angle $AMB=\omega$, where $M$ is the midpoint of segment $BC$, $\omega$ is an acute angle. It is to be proven that the problem is solvable if and only if

$$
b \cdot \tan \frac{1}{2} \omega \leq c < b
$$

In what case does the equality hold?

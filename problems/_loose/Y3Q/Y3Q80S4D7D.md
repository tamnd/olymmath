A positive integer $n$ is called *pretty* if there exists two divisors $d_1,d_2$ of $n$ $(1\leq d_1,d_2\leq n)$ such that $d_2-d_1=d$ for each divisor $d$ of $n$ (where $1<d<n$ ).
Find the smallest pretty number larger than $401$ that is a multiple of $401$ .

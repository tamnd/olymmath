Let $H$ be the set of points in the plane for which the following conditions hold:

1. If $P \in H, Q \in H$ and $R \in H$, then $S \in H$, where $S$ is the reflection of $P$ over the midpoint of the segment $QR$.
2. For any positive $\varepsilon$, there is an $\varepsilon$-radius circle in the plane that contains infinitely many points of $H$ that do not lie on a single line.

Prove that in any circle in the plane, $H$ has infinitely many points.

In the country, there are several cities, some pairs of which are connected by roads, and between any two cities, there exists a unique non-self-intersecting path along the roads. It is known that there are exactly 100 cities in the country from which only one road leads out. Prove that it is possible to build 50 new roads such that after this, even if any road is closed, it will still be possible to travel from any city to any other city.

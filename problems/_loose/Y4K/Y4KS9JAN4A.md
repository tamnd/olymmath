A straddling number is a natural number in which

- each non-zero digit is used exactly twice,
- between two identical non-zero digits, there are exactly as many zeros as the value of those digits.

Examples of straddling numbers include 40001041 and 300103100.

How many seven-digit straddling numbers exist, in which only ones, twos, and zeros appear in their notation?

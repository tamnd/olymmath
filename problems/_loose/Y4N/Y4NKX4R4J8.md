Given a circle plane $(S)$, its center $(O)$, and radius $(r=\sqrt{a(a+b)})$. There are also two points $(P, Q)$ such that $|P Q| \perp S$ and they lie on $O$. $\overline{O P}=a, \overline{O Q}=a+b$.

Let the circle be the common base of two cones with vertices at $P$ and $Q$. For any point $A$ on the circle, there are two generators. What is the geometric locus of the intersection points of the lines perpendicular to these generators and raised at the vertices of the cones?

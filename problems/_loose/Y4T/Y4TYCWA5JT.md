Given two orthogonal diameters $\left[M M^{\prime}\right]$ and $\left[N N^{\prime}\right]$ of two orthogonal circles. Show that among $(M N),\left(M N^{\prime}\right),\left(M^{\prime} N\right)$ and $\left(M^{\prime} N^{\prime}\right)$, two pass through one of the intersection points of the two circles and the others through the other intersection point.

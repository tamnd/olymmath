Given $n \in \mathbf{N}_{+}, k \in \mathbf{N}, P$ represents the set of all $n$-degree complex coefficient polynomials with the leading coefficient 1. When $F \in P$, find
$$
|F(0)|^{2}+|F(1)|^{2}+\cdots+|F(n+k)|^{2}
$$

the minimum value.

a) The continuation of the bisector of angle $B$ of triangle $A B C$ intersects the circumscribed circle at point $M$; $O$ is the center of the inscribed circle, and $O_{b}$ is the center of the excircle touching side $A C$. Prove that points $A, C, O$, and $O_{b}$ lie on a circle with center $M$.

b) Point $O$, lying inside triangle $A B C$, has the property that the lines $A O, B O$, and $C O$ pass through the centers of the circumscribed circles of triangles $B C O, A C O$, and $A B O$. Prove that $O$ is the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $A B C$.

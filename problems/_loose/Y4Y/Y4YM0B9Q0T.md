Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. Suppose that $|AH|=3|BH|$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $P$ be a point such that $|NP|=|NC|$ and $|CP|=|CB|$, and such that $B$ and $P$ lie on opposite sides of the line $AC$.

Prove that $\angle APM = \angle PBA$.

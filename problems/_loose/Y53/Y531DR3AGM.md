The Winkler family has exactly three children.

On January 1, 1975, the oldest child was twice as old as the second, and this child in turn was twice as old as the youngest child. The mother was twice as old as her three children combined. The father was as old as the mother and the youngest child combined. The total age of all five family members was the same as that of the one grandfather, who was 64 years old when the oldest child was born.

How old was each of the mentioned individuals on January 1, 1975?

Note: All age specifications are to be understood in full years of life.

\section*{Problem 3A - 261043A}

Determine all triples $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}\right)$ of real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$ that satisfy the following system of equations (1), (2), (3)!

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3} & =3 \\
x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3}+x_{3}^{3} & =3 \\
x_{1} \cdot x_{2} \cdot x_{3} & =1
\end{aligned}
$$

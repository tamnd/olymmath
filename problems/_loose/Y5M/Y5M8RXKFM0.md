51 Let $n$ be an even number, and select four different numbers $a, b, c, d$ from the integers $1, 2, \cdots, n$ such that $a+c=b+d$. Prove: The number of ways to select (without considering the order of $a, b, c, d$) is $\frac{n(n-2)(2 n-5)}{24}$.

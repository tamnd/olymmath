Let $P$ be an internal point on the hypotenuse $AB$ of the right triangle $ABC$. It is to be proven that

$$
(PA \cdot BC)^{2}+(PB \cdot CA)^{2}=(PC \cdot AB)^{2}
$$

Is the statement also true if $P$ is on the extension of the hypotenuse segment $AB$?

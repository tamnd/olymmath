Let $a$ and $b$ be any two numbers such that $a > b$. Therefore, there exists a positive number $c$ such that

$$
a = b + c
$$

Multiply both sides of the equation by $(a - b)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (a - b) a = (a - b)(b + c) \\
& a^2 - ab = ab - b^2 + ac - bc
\end{aligned}
$$

which simplifies to

$$
a^2 - ab - ac = ab - b^2 - bc
$$

or

$$
a(a - b - c) = b(a - b - c)
$$

from which we get

$$
a = b
$$

However, this contradicts our condition that $a > b$. Where is the mistake?

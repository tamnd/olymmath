A club has $3 n+1$ people, and any two people can engage in one of three activities: tennis, table tennis, and chess. If each person plays tennis with $n$ people, table tennis with another $n$ people, and chess with the remaining $n$ people. Prove: There must be 3 people in the club who engage in all three activities with each other.

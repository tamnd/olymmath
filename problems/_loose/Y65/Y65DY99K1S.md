A ray of light is emitted from a point $A$ on a circle, and when it encounters the circumference during its journey, it reflects according to the law that the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence. The reflection points on the circumference are successively $B, C, D, \cdots$, prove that there are infinitely many points on the circle that the light ray will never reach.

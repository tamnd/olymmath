In the cells of a $2000 \times 2000$ table, numbers 1 and -1 are written. It is known that the sum of all numbers in the table is non-negative. Prove that there exist 1000 rows and 1000 columns of the table such that the sum of the numbers written in the cells at their intersections is at least 1000.

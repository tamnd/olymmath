A regular tetrahedron $ABCD$ has its four vertices on the surface of a sphere with radius $R$, then the length of $AB$ is
(A) $\sqrt{2} R$.
(B) $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3} R$.
(C) $\frac{2 \sqrt{6}}{3} R$.
(D) $\frac{3 \sqrt{6}}{4} R$.
(High School Mathematics Competition, Taiyuan City, Shanxi Province, China, 1990)

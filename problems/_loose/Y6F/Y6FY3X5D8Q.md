An urn contains 8 balls, on which the numbers from 1 to 8 are written. Federica draws two balls in succession, erases the number on the first and replaces it with its double, erases the number on the second ball and replaces it with its quadruple. She then reinserts the two balls into the urn (for example, if Federica has drawn the balls 3 and 7 in this order, she will reinsert into the urn two balls with the numbers 6 and 28). Finally, she draws a ball again: what is the probability that the ball drawn has the number 8?
(A) $\frac{1}{8}$
(B) $\frac{1}{7}$
(C) $\frac{7}{44}$
(D) $\frac{9}{56}$
(E) $\frac{1}{6}$

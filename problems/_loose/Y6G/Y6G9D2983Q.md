Let $\left(a_{n}\right)_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $a_{1}=a_{2}=1$ and $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+\frac{a_{n}}{3^{n}}, n \geq 1$. Show that $a_{n}<2$, for any $n \geq 1$.

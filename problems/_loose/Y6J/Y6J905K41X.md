$$
\frac{n^{2}-n}{2} \leq\{\sqrt{1}\}+\{\sqrt{2}\}+\ldots+\left\{\sqrt{n^{2}}\right\} \leq \frac{n^{2}-1}{2}
$$

where $\{x\}$ denotes the fractional part of $x$.

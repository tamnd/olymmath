If $a<b$, then the radical $\sqrt{-(x+a)^{3}(x+b)}$ is equal to
(A) $(x+a) \sqrt{-(x+a)(x+b)}$.
(B) $(x+a) \sqrt{(x+a)(x+b)}$.
(C) $-(x+a) \sqrt{-(x+a)(x+b)}$.
(D) $-(x+a) \sqrt{(x+a)(x+b)}$.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1986)

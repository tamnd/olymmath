(Jury, Finland, 79). A set $\left(a_{1} ; \ldots ; a_{n}\right)$ of natural numbers satisfying the equation

$$
a_{1}+2 a_{2}+\ldots+n a_{n}=1979
$$

is called even if the number $r$ is even, and is called odd if the number $n$ is odd. Prove that there are as many even sets as there are odd sets.

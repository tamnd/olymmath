We say that a quadruple $(A,B,C,D)$ is *dobarulho* when $A,B,C$ are non-zero algorisms and $D$ is a positive integer such that: $1.$ $A \leq 8$ $2.$ $D>1$ $3.$ $D$ divides the six numbers $\overline{ABC}$ , $\overline{BCA}$ , $\overline{CAB}$ , $\overline{(A+1)CB}$ , $\overline{CB(A+1)}$ , $\overline{B(A+1)C}$ .
Find all such quadruples.

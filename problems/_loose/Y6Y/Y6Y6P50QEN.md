\section*{Task 3 - 121233}

Three schools, one each from Adorf, Bedorf, and Cedorf, conducted a track and field competition at a county sports festival. In each discipline, each school provided exactly one participant. A reporter interviewed a spectator after the competition:

Reporter: "Who won the entire competition?"

Spectator: "Adorf won the long jump, but the entire competition was won by Bedorf with 22 points. Adorf and Cedorf each scored 9 points."

Reporter: "How were the points distributed?"

Spectator: "In each discipline, the first place received a certain number of points, the second place received fewer points, and the third place received even fewer points, but at least one point. This distribution was the same for all disciplines. All point values were integers."

Reporter: "In how many disciplines did the competition take place?"

Spectator: "I don't know."

Reporter: "Who won the shot put?"

Spectator: "I don't know, but shot put was included.

Determine whether the following two questions can be definitively answered based on these (all assumed to be true) statements, and provide all answers that are consistent with these statements!

a) Which of the three schools won the shot put?

b) Which school took second place in the long jump?

(It is known that in each discipline, a clear ranking of the competition participants was determined.)

Prove that for any natural $n \geqslant 3$ there exists a polynomial $f(x)$ of degree $n$ with real coefficients and roots $a_{1}<a_{2}<\ldots<a_{n}$, for which

$$
\int_{a_{1}}^{a_{2}}|f(x)| d x=\int_{a_{2}}^{a_{3}}|f(x)| d x=\ldots=\int_{a_{n}}^{a_{n-1}}|f(x)| d x
$$

Three bogatyrs are fighting the Serpent Gorynych. Ilya Muromets with each blow cuts off half of the Serpent's heads and one more, Dobrynya Nikitich cuts off a third of the heads and two more, Alyosha Popovich cuts off a quarter of the heads and three more. The bogatyrs strike one at a time in any order they choose, cutting off a whole number of heads with each blow. If no bogatyr can strike (the number of heads becomes non-integer), the Serpent devours all three. Can the bogatyrs cut off all the heads of the 41-headed Serpent?

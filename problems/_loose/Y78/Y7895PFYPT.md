Let $I_{A}$ and $I_{B}$ be the centers of the excircles of triangle $ABC$ that touch sides $BC$ and $CA$, respectively, and let $P$ be a point on the circumcircle $\Omega$ of this triangle. Prove that the midpoint of the segment connecting the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $I_{A}CP$ and $I_{B}CP$ coincides with the center of the circle $\Omega$.

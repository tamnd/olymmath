On the sides $[A D]$ and $[D C]$ of the square $A B C D$, equilateral triangles $A E D$ and $D F C$ are constructed such that $E$ is inside the square and $F$ is outside the square. Prove that:

a) the points $B$, $E$, and $F$ are collinear.

b) the point $E$ is the midpoint of the segment $[B M]$, where $\{M\}=B F \cap D C$.

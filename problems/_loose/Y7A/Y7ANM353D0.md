Find the error in the "short proof" of Menger's Theorem (3.3.1) below: Let $ X $ be a minimal $ A-B $ separator. Let $ G_{A} $ denote the subgraph of $ G $ induced by $ X $ and the components of $ G-X $ that intersect $ A $; define $ G_{B} $ similarly. By the minimality of $ X $, there cannot be an $ A-X $ separator in $ G_{A} $ with fewer than $ |X| $ vertices, so by induction $ G_{A} $ contains $ k $ disjoint $ A-X $ paths; similarly, $ G_{B} $ contains $ k $ disjoint $ X-B $ paths. These paths together form the required $ A-B $ paths in $ G $.

Let $a$ be the sum of the numbers:

$99 \times 0.9$

$999 \times 0.9$

$9999 \times 0.9$

$\vdots$

$999\cdots 9 \times 0.9$

where the final number in the list is $0.9$ times a number written as a string of $101$ digits all equal to $9$.
Find the sum of the digits in the number $a$.

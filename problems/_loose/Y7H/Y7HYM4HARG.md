The Hedgehog can meet in the fog either the Gray Mare, or the Gray Mare (female), or his friend the Bear cub. One day, all three came to meet the Hedgehog, but the fog was thick, and the Hedgehog could not see who was who, so he asked them to introduce themselves.

The one who, from the Hedgehog's point of view, was on the left, said: "Next to me is the Bear cub."

The one who stood on the right, stated: "This was told to you by the Gray Mare (female)."

Finally, the one who was in the center, said: "To my left is the Gray Mare."

Determine who stood where, given that the Gray Mare always lies, the Gray Mare (female) - sometimes, and the Bear cub never lies to the Hedgehog?

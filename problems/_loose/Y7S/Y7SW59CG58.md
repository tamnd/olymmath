Let $D$ be the set of all real numbers for which the expression

$$
\sqrt{\log _{2}\left(\cos \frac{\pi x}{\sqrt{2}}\right)}
$$

is defined. Denote

$$
A=\min _{x \in D}\left|2019-x^{2}\right|
$$

Prove that $A$ is a natural number and that it is prime.

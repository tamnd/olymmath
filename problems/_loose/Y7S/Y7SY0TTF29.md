Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $A B>A C$. Its circumcircle is $\Gamma$ and its incentre is $I$. Let $D$ be the contact point of the incircle of $A B C$ with $B C$.
Let $K$ be the point on $\Gamma$ such that $\angle A K I$ is a right angle.
Prove that $A I$ and $K D$ meet on $\Gamma$.

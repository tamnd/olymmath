Given a circle with radius $r$ and points $A, B, C$ on its circumference such that $A B=A C \leqq r$. Let $D$ be a point inside the circle such that $A B D$ is an equilateral triangle. Let the ray $C D$ intersect the circle $k$ at $E$. Prove that $D E=r$.

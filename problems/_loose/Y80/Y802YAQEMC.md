(2008 Moscow Mathematical Olympiad) Each positive integer is colored one of $n$ colors, with each color having infinitely many numbers. It is known that the arithmetic mean of any two different numbers of the same parity is determined only by the colors of those two numbers (for example, the average of a red number and a blue number is always yellow).
(1) Prove that the arithmetic mean of any two numbers of the same color and the same parity must be the same color as those two numbers;
(2) For which $n$ does such a coloring exist?

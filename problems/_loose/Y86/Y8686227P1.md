In $2 k$ rooms there are $6 k$ lamps. Each room contains 3 lamps, and the lamps are themselves grouped in pairs (not necessarily in the same room). Each of these $3 k$ pairs has a unique switch that controls the two lamps of the pair. Is there necessarily a configuration of the switches (on or off) that allows for at least one lamp to be on and one lamp to be off in each room?

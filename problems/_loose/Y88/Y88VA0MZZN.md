Let each side of a regular octagon be colored either blue or yellow. The following operation is allowed: simultaneously modify the coloring of each side, changing a side to blue if its two adjacent sides are of different colors, and to yellow if they are of the same color. Prove that after several steps of operations, all 8 sides will turn yellow, and determine the minimum number of operations needed to turn all sides yellow for all possible initial colorings.

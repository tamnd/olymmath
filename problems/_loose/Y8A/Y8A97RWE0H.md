Cleopatra is playing with a row formed by $n^{2}$ toy soldiers (where $n$ is an integer greater than 30). First, Cleopatra removes from the row all the toy soldiers whose position corresponds to a square (i.e., the $1^{\text{st}}$ soldier, the $4^{\text{th}}$, the $9^{\text{th}}$, and so on). After completing this procedure, Cleopatra forms a new row with the remaining toy soldiers and repeats the process by removing all the toy soldiers whose position in the new row corresponds to a square. This continues in the same way, always with the same procedure. How many toy soldiers could be left when Cleopatra, after completing the procedure several times, gets tired and stops playing?

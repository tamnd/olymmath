Let $k>2$ be an integer. We call a pair of integers $(a,b)$ *$k-$ good* if $$0\leqslant a<k,\hspace{0.2cm} 0<b \hspace{1cm} \text{and} \hspace{1cm} (a+b)^2=ka+b$$ Prove that the number of $k-$ good pairs is a power of $2$ .

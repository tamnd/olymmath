In the quadrilateral $A B C D$, the vertices $A$ and $C$ are translated by the vector $\mathbf{u}$, and the vertices $B$ and $D$ are translated by the vector $\mathbf{v}$ to obtain the quadrilateral $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ (where $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are arbitrary vectors in the plane of the quadrilateral). Prove that the areas of the two quadrilaterals are equal! (We assume that the quadrilaterals $A B C D$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ are not self-intersecting.)

Given is a board of dimensions $2020 \times 2022$. Two fields of this board are said to be adjacent if they share a common side or if they are at the beginning and end of the same row or column. Thus, each field has exactly four adjacent fields.

Viktor, in each step, chooses one field of the board and places five tokens on the board: one on the chosen field and one on each of the adjacent fields. After a finite number of such steps, there are exactly $d$ tokens on each field.

Determine the smallest possible $d$.

On a computer screen, a regular 2011-gon is given, with its vertices colored either black or white. A program performs the following operation: each time, it can select $a$ consecutive vertices (where $a$ is a fixed positive integer less than 2011), and a mouse click will invert the colors of these $a$ vertices, i.e., black points turn white, and white points turn black.
(1) Prove that if $a$ is odd, it is possible to make all vertices white after a finite number of operations, and it is also possible to make all vertices black after a finite number of operations.
(2) When $a$ is even, can all vertices be made the same color after a finite number of operations? Prove your conclusion.

At a dance class, only married couples signed up. Someone observed that the height difference between the two members of each couple is at most $10 \mathrm{~cm}$. The dance teacher arranges the men and women in increasing order of height separately. The dance pairs are formed based on this: the tallest woman dances with the tallest man, and so on. Prove that the height difference between the members of any dance pair is also at most $10 \mathrm{~cm}$.

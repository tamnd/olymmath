João works selling astrological prediction packages. To boost sales of his predictions, he offers discounts if people of the same sign want to hire his services. In the Greek Horoscope, since there are exactly 12 signs, therefore, in a group of 13 people, at least two of them will have the same sign and may be interested in the promotional package.

a) What is the minimum number of people a group must have for him to be certain that there will be at least 3 people of the same sign in the Greek Horoscope?

b) In the Chinese Horoscope, there are also exactly 12 signs. If João wants to be certain that, in a certain group of people, there will be two people with exactly the same signs in both the Greek and Chinese Horoscopes, what is the minimum number of people that such a group must have?

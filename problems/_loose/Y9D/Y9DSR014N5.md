Given a triangle with sides $a, b, c$, and a segment $d$. Let the smaller of $a$ and $d$ (or their common value if they are equal) be denoted by $a_{1}$, and similarly, let $b_{1}$ be the smaller of $b$ and $d$, and $c_{1}$ be the smaller of $c$ and $d$. Can a triangle always be constructed from the segments $a_{1}, b_{1}, c_{1}$?

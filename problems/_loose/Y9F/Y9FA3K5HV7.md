On the blackboard, there is the equation
$$
x^{3}+\ldots x^{2}+\ldots x+\ldots=0,
$$

Two people play a game according to the following rules: Player A first says any number, and Player B then fills this number into any of the 3 blank spaces; then Player A says another number, and Player B fills it into any of the remaining two blank spaces; finally, Player A writes any number in the last remaining blank space. If the resulting equation has 3 distinct integer roots, then Player A wins. Can Player A always win?

Let $S$ be an arbitrary point inside the triangle $ABC$ with sides $a, b, c$. Prove that

$$
S A \cos \frac{A}{2} + S B \cos \frac{B}{2} + S C \cos \frac{C}{2} \geq \frac{a + b + c}{2}.
$$

When does the equality hold?

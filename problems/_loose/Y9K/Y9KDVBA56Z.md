Consider a set $S$ of 4035 points in the plane, no three of which are collinear, with 2017 colored blue and 2018 colored red. Show that it is possible to draw 2017 lines in the plane such that:

(i) no line passes through a point of $S$,

(ii) none of the regions delimited by the 2017 lines contains two points of $S$ of different colors.

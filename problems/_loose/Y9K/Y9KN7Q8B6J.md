There are two groups of numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A_{1} \geqslant A_{2} \geqslant \ldots \geqslant A_{n} \geqslant 0 \\
& B_{1} \geqslant B_{2} \geqslant \ldots \geqslant B_{n} \geqslant 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that

$$
A_{1} B_{1}+A_{2} B_{2}+\ldots+A_{n} B_{n} \geqslant A_{1} B_{n}+A_{2} B_{n-1}+\ldots+A_{n} B_{1}
$$

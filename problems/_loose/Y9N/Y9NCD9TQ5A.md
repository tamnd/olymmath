Let $f(x)$ be a mapping from the set of real numbers to the set of complex numbers, such that for $\mathrm{V} t \in \mathbf{R}$, we have $f(t)=t+\left(4 n^{2}+t n+14\right) \mathrm{i} (n \in \mathbf{Z})$. Prove that there does not exist $t \in \mathbf{R}$ such that the complex number $f(t) \in A$. Here, $A=\{z|| z+2 \mathrm{i} \mid \leqslant 8 \sqrt{3}, z \in \mathbf{C}\}$.

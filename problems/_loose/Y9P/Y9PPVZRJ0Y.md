Andraž and Breda cut two long strips of lengths $a$ and $b$ from a magazine to play with. In this game, the player whose turn it is cuts a piece of length $d$ from any strip. The player who first cannot cut a piece of length $d$ loses the game. Andraž, being a gentleman, lets Breda start the game. Determine how the lengths of the strips affect who will win.

Solve the problems independently. Time allowed: $3 \frac{1}{2}$ hours.

The use of notes, literature, or a pocket calculator is not permitted.

45th Mathematical Competition for High School Students in Slovenia

Idrija, May 12, 2001

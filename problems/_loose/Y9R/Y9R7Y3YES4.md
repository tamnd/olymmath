In the plane, points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ are given such that no three of them are collinear. Let $p_{i j}$ be the line defined by points $A_{i}$ and $A_{j}$. Determine the maximum number of intersection points of the lines $p_{i j}$ and $p_{k l}$, where $i, j, k, l$ are distinct elements of the set $\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$.

Let $\xi$ be a non-negative random variable. Prove that if for a given $\varepsilon>0$ the probability $\mathrm{P}(\xi>\varepsilon)$ does not exceed $a$ for some $a \geqslant 0$, then

$$
\mathrm{E} \xi \leqslant \frac{\varepsilon}{1-\frac{\left(a \mathrm{E} \xi^{2}\right)^{1 / 2}}{\mathrm{E} \xi}}
$$

(assuming that the expression in the denominator is defined and greater than zero).

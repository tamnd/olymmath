Prove that for any positive numbers $a, b, c$ the inequality

$$
\frac{a+b-2 c}{b+c}+\frac{b+c-2 a}{c+a}+\frac{c+a-2 b}{a+b} \geq 0
$$

holds. When does equality hold?

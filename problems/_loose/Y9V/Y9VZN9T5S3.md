In parallelogram $A B C D$, a point $L$ is taken on side $A D$, a point $M$ on diagonal $A C$, and a point $N$ on side $B C$, such that:

$$
5 \cdot \overline{A L}=\overline{A D}, 4 \cdot \overline{A M}=\overline{A C}, 5 \cdot \overline{B N}=2 \cdot \overline{B C}
$$

a) Prove that points $L, M$, and $N$ are collinear.

b) In what ratio does point $M$ divide segment $L N$?

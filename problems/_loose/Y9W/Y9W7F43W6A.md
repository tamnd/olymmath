We say that a quadrilateral $Q$ is *tangential* if a circle can be inscribed into it, i.e. there exists a circle $C$ that does not meet the vertices of $Q$ , such that it meets each edge at exactly one point. Let $N$ be the number of ways to choose four distinct integers out of $\{1, . . . , 24\}$ so that they form the side lengths of a tangential quadrilateral. Find the largest prime factor of $N$ .

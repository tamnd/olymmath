Suppose $f(x)$ satisfies $f\left(x_{1} x_{2}\right)=f\left(x_{1}\right)+f\left(x_{2}\right)\left(x_{1}>0, x_{2}>0\right)$ and has an inverse function, prove: $f^{-1}\left(x_{1}\right)+f^{-1}\left(x_{2}\right) \geqslant 2 f^{-1}\left(\frac{x_{1}}{2}\right) f^{-1}\left(\frac{x_{2}}{2}\right)$.

In the sequence of natural numbers, there are prime numbers that are immediate successors of the square of a natural number.

Examples: $2=1^{2}+1,5=2^{2}+1,17=4^{2}+1,37=6^{2}+1$.

Prove that, in contrast, no prime number (except the prime number 3) has the square of a natural number as its immediate successor!

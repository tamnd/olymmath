In a box, there are 10 red cards and 10 blue cards, each color containing cards labeled with numbers $1, 3, 3^{2}, \cdots, 3^{9}$, one for each number. The total sum of the labels on the cards of both colors is denoted as $S$. For a given positive integer $n$, if it is possible to select some cards from the box such that the sum of their labels is exactly $n$, this is called a "scheme for $n$", and the number of different schemes for $n$ is denoted as $f(n)$. Find $P=$ $\sum_{n=1}^{1000} f(n)$.

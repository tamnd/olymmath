A chicken coop with an area of $240 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$ is to house chickens and chicks, with a desirable free space of $4 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$ for each chicken and $2 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$ for each chick. Additionally, each chick consumes $40 \mathrm{~g}$ of feed per day, and each chicken consumes $160 \mathrm{~g}$ per day, with a maximum daily feed allowance of $8 \mathrm{~kg}$.

(a) Represent the conditions of the problem algebraically.

(b) Graphically represent the conditions above on the Cartesian plane $x O y$.

(c) Can the chicken coop accommodate 20 chickens and 80 chicks? And 30 chickens and 100 chicks?

(d) What is the maximum number of chickens that can be placed in the chicken coop, respecting the desirable spaces and the maximum feed allowance? And of chicks?

For a real number $c \neq 0$ and a natural number $n$, let $a_{k}$ be the coefficient of $x^{k}$ in the expression $(1+c x)^{n}$, and $b_{k}$ be the coefficient of $x^{k}$ in the expression $(1+2 c x)^{n}$. It is known that $a_{1}, a_{3} \text{ and } a_{4}$ are consecutive terms of a geometric sequence, and that $b_{1}, 2 b_{2} \text{ and } 2 b_{3}$ are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence. Determine the numbers $c$ and $n$.

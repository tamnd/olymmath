Given an inscribed quadrilateral $A B C D$, in which $\angle A B C+\angle A B D=90^{\circ}$. On the diagonal $B D$, a point $E$ is marked, such that $B E=A D$. From it, a perpendicular $E F$ is dropped to the side $A B$. Prove that $C D+E F<A C$.

On the plane, there are $N$ points, no three of which lie on the same line. If $A, B, C$ are any three of them, then there is no point inside the triangle $A B C$ from the given points. Prove that these points can be numbered in such a way that the polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ will be convex.

Two guards are walking clockwise along the wall of a round tower, with the first one walking twice as fast as the second. This wall, which has a length of 1, has loopholes. A system of loopholes is called reliable if, at some initial position of the guards, at every subsequent moment at least one of them is near a loophole.

a) What is the smallest length that a loophole can have if a system consisting of only this loophole is reliable?

b) Prove that the total length of the loopholes in any reliable system is greater than $1 / 2$.

c)* Prove that for any number $s > 1 / 2$, there exists a reliable system of loopholes with a total length less than $s$.

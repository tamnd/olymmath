(Quotient by a Normal Subgroup) Let $(G, *)$ be a group and $H \subseteq G$ a normal subgroup of $G$. Show that the quotient $G / H$ is a group under the operation $*$ defined by $X * Y=\{x * y \mid x \in X, y \in Y\}$.

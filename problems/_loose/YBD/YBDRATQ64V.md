A natural number is called a palindrome if it remains unchanged when its digits are written in reverse order (for example, the numbers 4, 55, 626 are palindromes, while 20, 201, 2016 are not). Prove that any number of the form 2016... 2016 (the digit group 2016 is repeated several times) can be represented as the product of two palindromes.

Let the parabola $E: y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ have a focus at $F$, and a line $l$ passing through $F$ and perpendicular to the $x$-axis intersects the parabola $E$ at points $S$ and $T$. A circle with center $P(3,0)$ passes through points $S$ and $T$, and $\angle S P T=90^{\circ}$.
(1) Find the equations of the parabola $E$ and the circle $\odot P$;
(2) Let $M$ be a point on the circle $\odot P$, and a line $l$ passing through $M$ and perpendicular to $F M$ intersects the parabola $E$ at points $A$ and $B$. Prove that $F A \perp F B$.

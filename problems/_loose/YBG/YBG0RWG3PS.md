Given that $ABC$ is an equilateral triangle with side length $a$, and on the sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$, we draw segments $AC'$, $BA'$, and $CB'$, each of length $x$. Prove that:

$1^{\circ}$ The triangle $A'B'C'$ is equilateral.

$2^{\circ}$ $x$ can be chosen in such a way that the side of the triangle $A'B'C'$ will be equal to a given length $l$.

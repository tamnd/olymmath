Let the function $f(x)=\sin ^{n} \alpha x \cos ^{m} \beta x\left(n, m \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, \alpha>\beta>0\right)$, prove:
(i) $f(x)$ is a periodic function if and only if $\frac{\beta}{\alpha}$ is a rational number.
(ii) When $\frac{\beta}{\alpha}$ is a rational number, the smallest positive period of $f(x)$ is equal to $T^{*}$ or $2 T^{*}\left(T^{*}\right.$ is the least common multiple of $\frac{\pi}{\alpha}$ and $\frac{\pi}{\beta}$).

$6 \cdot 53 f, g, h$ are defined as follows:
$f(n)=10 n, n$ is a positive integer;
$g(n)=10 n+4, n$ is a positive integer;
$h(n)=\frac{n}{2}, n$ is a positive even number.

Prove: Every natural number can be represented from 4 by a finite number of operations of some of $f, g, h$.

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}, n \geq 2$, and $A, B \in \mathcal{M}_{n}(\mathbb{R})$. Show that there exists a complex number $z$, with $|z|=1$, having the property that

$$
\operatorname{Re}(\operatorname{det}(A+z B)) \geq \operatorname{det}(A)+\operatorname{det}(B)
$$

where $\operatorname{Re}(w)$, represents the real part of the complex number $w$.

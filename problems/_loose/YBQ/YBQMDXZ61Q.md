Given circles $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ intersect at points $Q$ and $R$, and are internally tangent to circle $T$ at points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$, respectively. $P$ is any point on circle $T$, and line segments $P A_{1}$ and $P A_{2}$ intersect circles $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ at $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, respectively. Prove:
(1) The line tangent to circle $T_{1}$ at point $B_{1}$ and the line tangent to circle $T_{2}$ at point $B_{2}$ are parallel;
(2) $B_{1} B_{2}$ is a common tangent to circles $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ if and only if $P$ lies on line $Q R$. (2006 Italian National Team Selection Exam)

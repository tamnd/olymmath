Let the perimeter of the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ be denoted by $k$, and the sum of the lengths of its two diagonals by $d$. Let $A_1, B_1, C_1, D_1$ be the points that divide the sides $AB, BC, CD, DA$ in the ratio $(1-\lambda): \lambda$, where $1/2 \leqq \lambda \leqq 1$, and let $k_{\lambda}$ denote the perimeter of the convex quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$. Prove that

$$
|2 \lambda d-(2 \lambda-1) k| \leqq k_{\lambda} \leqq (2 \lambda-1) k + 2(1-\lambda) d
$$

Let $A$ be one of the two intersection points of two unequal circles $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ with centers $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively. A common tangent line touches $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ at points $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$; another common tangent line touches $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ at points $Q_{1}$ and $Q_{2}$. $M_{1}$ is the midpoint of $P_{1} Q_{1}$, and $M_{2}$ is the midpoint of $P_{2} Q_{2}$. Prove that $\angle O_{1} \Lambda O_{2}=\angle M_{1} A M_{2}$.

On a plane, 1968 points are marked, which are the vertices of a regular 1968-gon. Two players play the following game: each in turn connects two vertices of the polygon with a segment, adhering to the following rules: you cannot connect two points if at least one of them is already connected to something, and you cannot intersect already drawn segments. The player who cannot make a move according to these rules loses. How should one play to win? Who wins with correct play?

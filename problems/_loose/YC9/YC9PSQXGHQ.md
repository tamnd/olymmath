Let $a, b, c \in \mathbb{C}^{*}$, distinct and having the same modulus, such that

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+a b+a c+b c=0
$$

Prove that $a, b, c$ represent the affixes of the vertices of a right-angled or equilateral triangle.

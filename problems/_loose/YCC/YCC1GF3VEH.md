If $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R} \backslash\{0\}$ and

$$
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{a+b+c}
$$

then among them there are two numbers whose sum is equal to zero. Prove!

For any integer $n\ge 4$ , prove that there exists a $n$ -degree polynomial $f(x)=x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0$ satisfying the two following properties:**(1)** $a_i$ is a positive integer for any $i=0,1,\ldots,n-1$ , and**(2)** For any two positive integers $m$ and $k$ ( $k\ge 2$ ) there exist distinct positive integers $r_1,r_2,...,r_k$ , such that $f(m)\ne\prod_{i=1}^{k}f(r_i)$ .

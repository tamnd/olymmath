(2004 National College Entrance Examination, Liaoning Province) Given the function $f(x)=\ln \left(\mathrm{e}^{x}+a\right)(a>0)$.
(I) Find the inverse function $y=f^{-1}(x)$ of the function $y=f(x)$ and the derivative $f^{\prime}(x)$ of $f(x)$;
(II) Assuming that for any $x \in[\ln (3 a), \ln (4 a)]$, the inequality $\left|m-f^{-1}(x)\right|+\ln \left[f^{\prime}(x)\right]<0$ holds, find the range of the real number $m$.

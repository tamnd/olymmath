Let $b$ be an integer, $b \geqslant 2$ and $z_{n}$ the number of zeros at the end of the representation of $n!$ in base $b$. Show that if $p$ is the largest prime divisor of $b$ then for all $n$ we have that

$$
z_{n}<\frac{n}{p-1}
$$

A female student walks from home to school every day. The streets between her home and school are all east-west and north-south. Let the student's home be at $O(0,0)$ and the school at $P(9,8)$. She always chooses the shortest path (therefore, she does not walk west from east or south from north).
(1) How many different possible paths are there?
(2) If her best friend lives at $A(3,2)$, and she visits her friend every day before going to school, how many different paths are there?
(3) If there is a danger at the intersection $B(6,5)$ (such as a gas leak) and it is prohibited to pass, and she meets her friend at $A$ and goes to school together, avoiding $B$, how many different paths are there?
(4) If the prohibited area is the street $BC$ (the open line segment between $B$ and $C(8,5)$), how many different paths can they take?

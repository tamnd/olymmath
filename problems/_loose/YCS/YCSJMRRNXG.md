Given a regular $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ in the plane, prove that:
(1) If $n$ is even, then for any point $M$ in the plane, the expression $\pm \bar{M} \dot{A}_{1} \pm M \vec{A}_{2} \pm \cdots \pm M \vec{A}_{n}$ can have the signs chosen appropriately so that the resulting sum is the zero vector.
(2) If $n$ is odd, then only for a finite number of points $M$ in the plane can the signs in the above expression be chosen appropriately so that the resulting sum is the zero vector.

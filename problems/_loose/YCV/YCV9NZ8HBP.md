Let $P$ be a point inside triangle $ABC$ such that

$$
\widehat{B P C}-\widehat{B A C}=\widehat{C P A}-\widehat{C B A}=\widehat{A P B}-\widehat{A C B}
$$

Show that $\mathrm{PA} \cdot \mathrm{BC}=\mathrm{PB} \cdot \mathrm{CA}=\mathrm{PC} \cdot \mathrm{AB}$.

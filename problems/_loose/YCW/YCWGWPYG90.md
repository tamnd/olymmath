Students from School No. 57 decided to hold a mini-football championship. Since the goals on the schoolyard are of different sizes, the players want to create a schedule for the games so that:

1) Each team plays against each other exactly once.
2) Each team alternates their games - playing on the poor side, then on the good side of the yard.

a) Is it possible to do this if 10 teams are participating in the tournament?

b) Can the schedule be arranged so that each team plays exactly one game each day?

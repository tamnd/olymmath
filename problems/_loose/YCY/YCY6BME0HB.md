The $m$-th root of the product of any $m$ non-negative numbers is the geometric mean of these $m$ non-negative numbers.
(1) For which positive integers $n$, is there a finite set $S_{n}$ of $n$ distinct positive integers, such that the geometric mean of any subset of $S_{n}$ is an integer?
(2) Is there an infinite set $S$ of distinct positive integers, such that the geometric mean of any finite subset of $S$ is an integer?

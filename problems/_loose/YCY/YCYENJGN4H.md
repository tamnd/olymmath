The natural numbers from 1 to 8 are placed in a table with 2 rows and 4 columns, such that the sum of the numbers in each column and each row is the same:

| 8 | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 7 | 6 | 4 |

The sum in each column is 9, and the sum in each row is 18.

a) Can the first 10 natural numbers be placed in a table with 2 rows and 5 columns, such that the sum of the numbers in each column is the same and the sum of the numbers in each row is the same?

b) Can the first 12 natural numbers be placed in a table with 2 rows and 6 columns, such that the sum of the numbers in each column is the same and the sum of the numbers in each row is the same?

On a piece of paper, 1993 points are marked, some pairs of which are connected by non-intersecting line segments, and no set of these line segments forms a closed polyline. Two players, A and B, take turns placing chess pieces on the marked points, but require that except for the first chess piece placed by A, each subsequent chess piece must be placed on a point adjacent to the previous one (two points connected by a line segment are called adjacent). The player who first cannot place a chess piece loses. Prove that A has a winning strategy.

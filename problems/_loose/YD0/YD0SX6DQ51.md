Let $\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \beta_{2}$ and $\gamma_{2}$ be real numbers such that $\alpha_{1}+\beta_{1}+\gamma_{1} \neq 0$ and $\alpha_{2}+\beta_{2}+\gamma_{2} \neq 0$. Show that $\left\langle\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}\right\rangle=\left\langle\alpha_{2}, \beta_{2}, \gamma_{2}\right\rangle$ if and only if the triplets $\left(\alpha_{1}, \beta_{1}, \gamma_{1}\right)$ and $\left(\alpha_{2}, \beta_{2}, \gamma_{2}\right)$ are multiples of each other.

In each round, A, B, and C simultaneously show one of the gestures: "Rock," "Paper," or "Scissors," and they do so independently. Let $\xi$ be the number of people A wins against in one round. Then the expected value $E \xi$ of the random variable $\xi$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{1}{3}$
(B) $\frac{4}{9}$
(C) $\frac{2}{3}$
(D) 1

The numbers $25$ and $76$ have the property that when squared in base $10$, their squares also end in the same two digits. A positive integer is called *amazing * if it has at most $3$ digits when expressed in base $21$ and also has the property that its square expressed in base $21$ ends in the same $3$ digits. (For this problem, the last three digits of a one-digit number b are 00b, and the last three digits of a two-digit number $\underline{ab}$ are $0\underline{ab}$.) Compute the sum of all amazing numbers. Express your answer in base $21$.

It is known that positive numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy the system:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
x^{2}+y^{2}+x y=529 \\
x^{2}+z^{2}+\sqrt{3} x z=441 \\
z^{2}+y^{2}=144
\end{array}\right.
$$

Find the value of the expression $\sqrt{3} x y+2 y z+x z$.

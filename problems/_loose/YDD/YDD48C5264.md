Rémi writes $n$ strictly positive integers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$ on the board. Every minute, Rémi can replace an integer $a$ written on the board with $\frac{N}{a}$, where $N$ denotes the least common multiple (LCM) of the integers written on the board. Show that Rémi can ensure that all the numbers written on the board are 1s after a finite number of operations.

a) Prove that if $p$ is a prime number, then

$$
\sigma_{k}\left(p^{a}\right)=1+p^{k}+p^{2 k}+\ldots+p^{a k}
$$

b) Prove that if numbers $m$ and $n$ are coprime, then $\sigma_{k}(m n)=\sigma_{k}(m) \sigma_{k}(n)$.

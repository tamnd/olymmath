Prove that there are infinitely many numbers $B$ for which the equation

$$
\left[x^{3 / 2}\right]+\left[y^{3 / 2}\right]=B
$$

has at least 1980 solutions in natural numbers $x, y$ ( $[z]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding the number $z$ ).

András gives four identical toy cars to his friends for Christmas: Bence, Csaba, Dénes, and Elemér. Bence has less money. He only buys 3 stamp sets and the next day he gifts his friends by passing on the car he received. On the third day, Csaba buys 2 chocolates and also passes on the gifts he received. Dénes only needs to buy 1 domino set. Finally, Elemér does not buy any gift but distributes the gifts he received. No boy gave the same type of gift to the one from whom he received a gift. Could it have happened that the gift bought by one boy ended up with two boys at the same time?

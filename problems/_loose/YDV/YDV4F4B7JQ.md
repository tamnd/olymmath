We know that $a, b, c, d$ are real numbers such that

$$
\left|a x^{3}+b x^{2}+c x+d\right| \leqq 1
$$

for all $x$ where $|x| \leqq 1$. Prove that

$$
\left|3 a x^{2}+2 b x+c\right| \leqq 9
$$

for all $x$ with an absolute value less than 1.

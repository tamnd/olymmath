In a scalene $\triangle ABC$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $BC$, and $P$ is a moving point on segment $AM$. The circumcircles of $\triangle ABC$, $\triangle BPM$, and $\triangle CPM$ are denoted as $\odot O$, $\odot O_{1}$, and $\odot O_{2}$, respectively. Let the second intersection points of $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ with $\odot O$ be $D$ and $E$, respectively. The line $DP$ intersects $\odot O_{2}$ again at $X$, and the line $EP$ intersects $\odot O_{1}$ again at $Y$. Prove that as point $P$ moves along segment $AM$, the circumcircle of $\triangle AXY$ always passes through a fixed point $T$ (different from point $A$).

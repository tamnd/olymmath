Let $H$ and $G$ be the orthocenter and centroid of acute $\triangle ABC (AB \neq AC)$, respectively. Line $AG$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at points $A$ and $P$. Let $P'$ be the reflection of point $P$ over line $BC$. Prove:
$$
\angle CAB = 60^\circ \text{ if and only if } HG = P'G.
$$

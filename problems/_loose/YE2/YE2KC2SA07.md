In Rt $\triangle A B C$, $\angle C=90^{\circ}, A C=1$, $B C=\sqrt{5}$, $D$ is the midpoint of side $A B$, and $E$ is a point on side $B C$. When $\triangle A D E$ is folded along $D E$ to get $\triangle A^{\prime} D E$, such that the overlapping area of $\triangle A^{\prime} D E$ and $\triangle B D E$ is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the area of $\triangle A B E$. Then the length of $B E$ is ( ).
(A) $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ or $\frac{\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(D) $\frac{3}{2}$ or $\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$

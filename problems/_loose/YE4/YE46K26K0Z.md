Let $\Omega$ be a circle, and let $\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}$, and $\omega_{3}$ be three circles inside $\Omega$ such that they are tangent to $\Omega$ and tangent to each other pairwise. We then denote $\gamma_{1}, \gamma_{2}$, and $\gamma_{3}$ as three circles tangent to $\Omega$ such that $\gamma_{i}$ is tangent to $\omega_{i+1}$ and $\omega_{i+2}$ (indices are taken modulo 3). For $i=1,2$ or 3, we denote $O_{i}$ as the center of the circle $\omega_{i}$, $T_{i}$ as the point of tangency of $\omega_{i}$ with $\Omega$, $C_{i}$ as the center of the circle $\gamma_{i}$, and $S_{i}$ as the point of tangency of $\gamma_{i}$ with $\Omega$. We then have the following properties:

- The lines $\left(T_{1} C_{1}\right),\left(T_{2} C_{2}\right)$, and $\left(T_{3} C_{3}\right)$ are concurrent.
- The lines $\left(O_{1} C_{1}\right),\left(O_{2} C_{2}\right)$, and $\left(O_{3} C_{3}\right)$ are concurrent.
- The lines $\left(O_{1} S_{1}\right),\left(O_{2} S_{2}\right)$, and $\left(O_{3} S_{3}\right)$ are concurrent.

If $\frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}=\frac{a_{2}}{b_{2}}=\frac{a_{3}}{b_{3}}=\cdots$, then the numerical value of each fraction is:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{a_{1}^{2}+a_{n}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+\ldots}}{\sqrt{b_{1}^{2}+b_{1}^{2}+1_{3}^{2}+\cdots}}
$$

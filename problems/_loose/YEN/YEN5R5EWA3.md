(1) None of the six children have their brother or sister as a table neighbor.

(2) Steffen sits opposite the oldest of the three boys.

(3) To the right of Agnes sits Ines, to the left of Agnes sits Michael.

(4) Kerstin is not Steffen's sister.

Prove that from these statements, both the sibling pairs and the seating arrangement can be uniquely determined, and provide both!

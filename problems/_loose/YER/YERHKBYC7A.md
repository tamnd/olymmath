Two people, A and B, play a number guessing game. First, A thinks of a number, denoted as $a$, then B guesses the number A just thought of, and the number B guesses is denoted as $b$, where $a, b \in \{1,2, \cdots, 6\}$. If $|a-b| \leqslant 1$, then A and B are said to have "close hearts." If two people are randomly chosen to play this game, the probability that they have close hearts is ( ).
(A) $\frac{4}{9}$
(B) $\frac{2}{9}$
(C) $\frac{7}{18}$
(D) $\frac{5}{18}$

September 2007, a language school was founded, where seven educators taught. On 1. September 2010, a new colleague joined these seven teachers, who was exactly 25 years old. By 1. September 2012, one of the teachers left the school, leaving seven teachers again. The average age of the educators at the school was the same on all three mentioned dates.

How old was the teacher who no longer worked at the school on 1. September 2012? What was the average age of the teachers at the school on that day?

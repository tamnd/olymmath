(GDR, 80). Prove that for any numbers $n, k \in \mathbf{N}$, greater than 1, the inequality

$$
\sum_{j=2}^{n^{k}} \frac{1}{j}>k \sum_{j=2}^{n} \frac{1}{j}
$$

holds.

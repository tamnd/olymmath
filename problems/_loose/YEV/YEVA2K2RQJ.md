The numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$ are colored in two colors, blue and red. Prove that if $n=2017$ there exists a coloring such that the equation

$$
8(x+y)=z
$$

has no monochromatic solutions. Determine the smallest $n$ for which it is never possible to color the numbers in such a way that there are no monochromatic solutions.

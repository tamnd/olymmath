Find the maximum value of the positive integer $\mathrm{n}$, such that there exist $\mathrm{n}$ consecutive positive integers satisfying the condition: the sum of the digits of the 1st number is divisible by 1, the sum of the digits of the 2nd number is divisible by 2, $\cdots$, the sum of the digits of the $\mathrm{n}$-th number is divisible by $\mathrm{n}$.

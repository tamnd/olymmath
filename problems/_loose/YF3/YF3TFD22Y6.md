In $\triangle ABC$, if $b=c$, the chord $AP$ intersects side $BC$, and the chord $AQ$ does not intersect side $BC$, prove:
(1) $PB+PC=2PA \cos B$;
(2) $|QB-QC|=2QA \cos B$;
(3) $\frac{PA}{PB+PC}=\frac{QA}{|QB-QC|}$.

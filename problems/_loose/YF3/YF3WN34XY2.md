Do there exist infinitely many triplets of positive integers $(a, b, c)$ such that
the following two conditions hold:
1. $\gcd(a, b, c) = 1$;
2. $a+b+c, a^2+b^2+c^2$ and $abc$ are all perfect squares?

*(Proposed by Ivan Chan Guan Yu)*

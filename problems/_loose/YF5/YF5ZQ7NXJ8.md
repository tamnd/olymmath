Let $B_{0}$ and $C_{0}$ be the midpoints of the sides $A C$ and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$, and $A_{1}$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$. Prove that

a) the second intersection point of the circles $A_{1} B_{0} C$ and $A_{1} C_{0} B$ lies on the circle $A B_{0} C_{0}$;

b) the common chord of the first two circles bisects the segment $B_{0} C_{0}$.

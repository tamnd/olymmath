Let $ a_1,a_2,\dots$ be sequence of real numbers such that $ a_1=1$, $ a_2=\dfrac{4}{3}$, and $$ a_{n+1}=\sqrt{1+a_na_{n-1}}, \forall n \ge 2.$$ Prove that for all $ n \ge 2$, $$ a_n^2>a_{n-1}^2+\dfrac{1}{2}$$ and $$ 1+\dfrac{1}{a_1}+\dfrac{1}{a_2}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{a_n}>2a_n.$$

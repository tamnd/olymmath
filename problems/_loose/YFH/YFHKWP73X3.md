Let $A B C D$ be a triangular billiard table, equipped with elastic edges and pockets at the vertices $A, B, C$ of the triangle, with dimensions such that: $A B = B C = C A = 200 \text{ cm}$. Let $P$ be the point on the side $B C$ such that $B P = 160 \text{ cm}, P C = 40 \text{ cm}$. A player rolls a ball from $A$ towards $P$. Prove that after seven reflections off the sides of the triangle, the ball falls into the pocket $A$.

Additional question (to be addressed only if you have solved all other problems in the test): prove that two points on the billiard table $A B C$ are each crossed three times by the ball.

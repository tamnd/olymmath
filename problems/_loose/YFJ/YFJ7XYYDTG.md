a) Prove that the moment of inertia relative to the center of mass of a system of points with unit masses is equal to $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i<j} a_{i j}^{2}$, where $n-$ is the number of points, $a_{i j}$ - is the distance between points with indices $i$ and $j$.

b) Prove that the moment of inertia relative to the center of mass of a system of points with masses $m_{1}, \ldots, m_{n}$, is equal to $\frac{1}{m} \sum_{i<j} m_{i} m_{j} a_{i j}^{2}$, where $m=m_{1}+\ldots+m_{n}, a_{i j}-$ is the distance between points with indices $i$ and $j$.

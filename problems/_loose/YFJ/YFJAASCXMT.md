Consider all possible $n$-digit numbers composed of the digits 1, 2, and 3. At the end of each of these numbers, a digit 1, 2, or 3 is appended such that to two numbers, which have different digits in all positions, different digits are appended. Prove that there exists an $n$-digit number, in which only one digit is 1, and to which a 1 is appended.

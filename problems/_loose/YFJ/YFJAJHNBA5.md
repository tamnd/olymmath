On the plane, there is a finite set of points $M$ such that no three points lie on the same line. Some of the points are connected to each other by segments in such a way that no more than one segment extends from each point. It is allowed to replace a pair of intersecting segments $A B$ and $C D$ with the pair of opposite sides $A C$ and $B D$ of the quadrilateral $A C B D$. In the resulting system of segments, it is allowed to perform such a replacement again, and so on. Can a sequence of such replacements be infinite?

$\alpha,\beta$ are two different solutions to the equation $4x^2-4tx+1=0(t\in\mathbb{R})$ , the domain of definition of the function $f(x)=\frac{2x-t}{x^2+1}$ is $[\alpha,\beta](\alpha<\beta)$ .**(a)** Find $g(t)=\max f(x)-\min f(x)$ .**(b)** Prove: for $u_i\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)(i=1,2,3)$ , if $\sin u_1+\sin u_2+\sin u_3=1$ , then $\frac{1}{g(\tan u_1)}+\frac{1}{g(\tan u_2)}+\frac{1}{g(\tan u_3)}<\frac{3}{4}\sqrt6$ .

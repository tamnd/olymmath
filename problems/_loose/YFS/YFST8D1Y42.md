Consider the parallelogram $A B C D$ and let $G$ be the centroid of triangle $A B C$. Let point $M$ be on segment $[A D]$ and let point $N$ be on segment $[D C]$. Show that points $G, M, N$ are collinear if and only if $\frac{C N}{N D}-\frac{A M}{M D}=\frac{1}{2}$.

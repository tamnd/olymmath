Given a cube $A B C D A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$ with edge $a$. The bases of the cylinder are inscribed in the faces $A B C D$ and $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$. Let $M$ be a point on the edge $A B$ such that $|A M|=a / 3$, and $N$ be a point on the edge $B_{1} C_{1}$ such that $\left|N C_{1}\right|=a / 4$. A plane passes through points $C_{1}$ and $M$, tangent to the base of the cylinder inscribed in $A B C D$, and through points $A$ and $N$ - a plane tangent to the base inscribed in $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$.

Find the volume of the part of the cylinder enclosed between the planes.

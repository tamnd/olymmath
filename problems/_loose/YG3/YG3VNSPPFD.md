Given an isosceles $ABC$ triangle with $AB=AC$ , let points $X$ and $Y$ lie on side $BC$ with $X$ between $B$ and $Y$ . We denote the circumcircle of triangle $AYB$ by $\omega_{1}$ . The circle $\omega_{2}$ passes through points $C$ and $X$ and is tangent to $AC$ . The points $M$ and $N$ are the intersections of the two circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ . Prove that $\angle{AMX} = \angle{BNX}$ .

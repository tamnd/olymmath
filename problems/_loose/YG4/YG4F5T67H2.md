In an acute $\triangle ABC$, $AD, BE, CF$ are the altitudes of $\triangle ABC$. Construct $AP \perp EF, BQ \perp FD, CR \perp DE$, with the feet of the perpendiculars being $P, Q, R$ respectively. Prove:
$$
p(ABC) \cdot p(PQR) \geqslant (p(DEF))^2,
$$

where $p(T)$ denotes the semiperimeter of triangle $T$.

Let $ A,B\in\mathcal{M} \left( \mathbb{R} \right) $ that satisfy $ AB=O_3. $ Prove that:

**a)** The function $ f:\mathbb{C}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C} $ defined as $ f(x)=\det \left( A^2+B^2+xBA \right) $ is a polynomial one, of degree at most $ 2. $
**b)** $ \det\left( A^2+B^2 \right)\ge 0. $

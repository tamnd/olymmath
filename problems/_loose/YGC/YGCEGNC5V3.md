On the way from city $A$ to city $B$, there are kilometer markers every kilometer. On each marker, one side shows the distance to $A$, and the other side shows the distance to $B$. In the morning, a tourist passed by a marker where one number was twice the other. After walking another 10 km, the tourist saw a marker where the two numbers differed by a factor of three. What is the distance from $A$ to $B$? Provide all possible answers and prove that there are no others.

(11th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) Consider the function $f: \mathbf{Q} \rightarrow \mathbf{Z}$. Prove that there exist two rational numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2} \leqslant \frac{f(a+b)}{2}$.

The numbers $1,2, \ldots, 2006$ are placed along a circle in any order. Two adjacent numbers on the circle can now be swapped repeatedly. After a sequence of such swaps, each of the numbers is diametrically opposed to its initial position. Prove that at least once two numbers with sum 2007 have been swapped.

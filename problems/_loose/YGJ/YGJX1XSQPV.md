The natural numbers $a,b,c,d$ satisfy the equation $a^2 +b^2 +c^2 +d^2 = 7\cdot 4^{2014} $ .
a) Find at least one solution of this equation $(a,b,c,d)$ .
b) Find all solutions of this equation $(a,b,c,d)$ satisfying the condition $a\le b\le c\le d$ .

Let circle $O_{4}$ be externally tangent to circle $O_{1}$, circle $O_{1}$ to circle $O_{2}$, circle $O_{2}$ to circle $O_{3}$, and circle $O_{3}$ to circle $O_{4}$ at points $P_{1}$, $P_{2}$, $P_{3}$, and $P_{4}$, respectively. Prove:
(1) Points $P_{1}$, $P_{2}$, $P_{3}$, and $P_{4}$ are concyclic;
(2) Quadrilateral $\mathrm{O}_{1} \mathrm{O}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3} \mathrm{O}_{4}$ is a tangential quadrilateral of some circle; and
(3) The radius of this circle does not exceed the radius of the circumcircle of quadrilateral $P_{1} P_{2} P_{3} P_{4}$.

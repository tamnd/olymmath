Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of a non-isosceles triangle $ABC$. The circle $\gamma$ touches the lines $AB$ and $AC$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively, and the circle $\omega$ internally at point $T$. The lines $AT$ and $PQ$ intersect at point $S$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $IST$, where $I$ is the incenter of triangle $ABC$, is tangent to $\omega$.

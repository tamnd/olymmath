A sphere of radius $R$ and a circular cone, whose base circle has a radius of $R$ and height $2R$, rest on the same plane. We cut these two bodies with a plane parallel to the base plane. How far from the base plane must the cutting plane be so that

1) the areas of the circles cut from the two bodies are equal to each other;
2) the volume of the frustum of the cone between the base and the cutting plane is $n$ times the volume of the spherical cap between the same planes.

For which values of $n$ is the problem possible?

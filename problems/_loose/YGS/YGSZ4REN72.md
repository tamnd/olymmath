Let $A$ and $B$ be 9-digit numbers. Suppose that by replacing any digit of $A$ with the corresponding (same place value) digit of $B$, we obtain a number divisible by 7. Show that then this also holds in reverse, that is, by replacing any digit of $B$ with the corresponding digit of $A$, we also get a number divisible by 7.

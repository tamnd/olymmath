On a plane, there are 7 blood point searchlights, each capable of illuminating a $90^{\circ}$ sector. If one searchlight is placed within the illuminated sector of another, the latter will cast a shadow, i.e., an infinitely long dark ray on the plane. Prove that it is possible to appropriately place 7 searchlights so that the shadow cast by each one is exactly 7 kilometers long.

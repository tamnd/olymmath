Consider an equally spaced point sequence that divides the line segment between the starting and ending points into $\left(n^{2}+n\right) / 2$ segments. Pick out the two endpoints of the point sequence and any $n-1$ arbitrary intermediate points. Any two of the picked points span a segment. Prove that it is impossible for all the segments thus formed to be distinct if $n \geq 4$.

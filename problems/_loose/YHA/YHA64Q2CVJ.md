In the tetrahedron $A B C D$, points $M$ and $N$ are the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from $A$ to the interior angle bisectors of $\angle A B C$ and $\angle A C D$, respectively.

a) If $A B \cdot C D = B C \cdot A C$, $B M \cap A C = \{E\}$, and $C N \cap A D = \{F\}$, show that $E F \perp C D$. b) Prove that $M N \perp (B C D)$.

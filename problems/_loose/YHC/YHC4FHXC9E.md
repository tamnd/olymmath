On April 1, at the Starbucks cafe that opened in the Ozas shopping center, first-year high school student Hermina purchased a discount card with which, by buying at least one cup of cappuccino coffee every day and treating her classmates to coffee, she promises to buy exactly $420$ cups of coffee by the end of the year (i.e., in $275$ days). Fourth-year high school student Akvilė claims that then there will definitely be several days in a row (perhaps one day) during which Hermina will buy exactly $145$ cups of coffee, and teacher Ben claims that there will definitely be several days in a row (perhaps one day) during which Hermina will buy exactly $125$ cups of coffee.
a) Is fourth-year student Akvilė right?
b) Is teacher Ben right?

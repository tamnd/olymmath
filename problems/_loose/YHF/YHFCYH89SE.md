Let $x,y,a,b$ be positive real numbers such that $x\not= y$ , $x\not= 2y$ , $y\not= 2x$ , $a\not=3b$ and $\frac{2x-y}{2y-x}=\frac{a+3b}{a-3b}$ .
Prove that $\frac{x^2+y^2}{x^2-y^2}\ge 1$ .

Conside a positive odd integer $k$ and let $n_1<n_2<\ldots<n_k$ be $k$ positive odd integers. Prove that:

$$n_1^2-n_2^2+n_3^2-n_4^2+\ldots+n_k^2\ge 2k^2-1$$

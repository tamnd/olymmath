Let the sequence $a(n), n=1,2,3, \cdots$, be defined as follows:
$a(1)=0$, and for $n>1$,
$a(n)=a\left(\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]\right)+(-1)^{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}$.
(a) Find the maximum and minimum values of $a(n)$ for $n<1996$, and give all values of $n$ at which these extrema are attained.
(b) For $n<1996$, how many terms of $a(n)$ have the value 0?

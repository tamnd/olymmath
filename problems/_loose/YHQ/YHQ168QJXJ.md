Let $k, n$ be positive integers greater than 1, and $\mathbf{N}^{*}$ be the set of positive integers. $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{k}$ are pairwise disjoint subsets of $\mathbf{N}^{*}$, whose union is $\mathbf{N}^{*}$. Prove: for some $i \in \{1,2, \cdots, k\}$, there exist infinitely many irreducible polynomials of degree $n$, such that each polynomial has distinct coefficients all in $A_{i}$.

Given a circle $\omega$ with center $I$. Two lines touching $\omega$ at points $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $O$. A point $C$ is chosen on the smaller arc $AB$ so that $AC \neq CB$. Lines $AC$ and $OB$ intersect at point $D$, while lines $BC$ and $OA$ intersect at point $E$. Prove that the circumcenters of triangles $ACE$, $BCD$, and $OCI$ are collinear.

In the rhombus $ABCD$, the acute angle is $60^{\circ}$. On side $AB$, point $M$ is given, and on side $BC$, point $N$ is given, such that

$$
\overline{MB} + \overline{BN} = \overline{AB}
$$

Prove that $\triangle MND$ is equilateral.

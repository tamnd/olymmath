For a triangle $A B C$, let the side lengths be $A B=c, A C=b$ and $B C=a$. The angle bisectors of $\angle C A B$ and $\angle A B C$ intersect at $P$. Through $P$, a line parallel to $A B$ is drawn. It intersects $A C$ at $Q$ and $B C$ at $R$.

Determine the length $Q R$ in terms of the three given side lengths!

There is a billiard table in the form of a polygon (not necessarily convex), where all angles are whole numbers of degrees, and angle $A$ is exactly $1^{\circ}$. At the vertices, there are point-sized pockets, and if the ball hits one of them, it falls into the pocket. A point-sized ball is launched from vertex $A$ and moves inside the polygon, reflecting off the sides according to the law "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection." Prove that it will never return to vertex $A$.

Let $M$ be an arbitrary natural number greater than 3, and let $S$ be the sum of all natural numbers $x$ not exceeding $M$ such that $x^2 - x + 1$ is divisible by $M$. Prove that $S$ is divisible by $M+1$. (In the case when there are no such numbers $x$, we set $S$ to be zero).

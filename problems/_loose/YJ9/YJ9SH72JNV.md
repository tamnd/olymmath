Given the circle $C: x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2}$, points $P$ and $P^{\prime}$ lie on a ray starting from $O$, and satisfy $|O P|\left|O P^{\prime}\right|=r^{2}$, then points $P$ and $P^{\prime}$ are said to be symmetric with respect to the circumference of circle $C$. Then, the equation satisfied by the point $P^{\prime}$, which is symmetric to the point $P(x, y)$ on the hyperbola $x^{2}-y^{2}=1$ with respect to the unit circle $C$: $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$, is ( ).
(A) $x^{2}-y^{2}=x^{4}+y^{4}$
(B) $x^{2}-y^{2}=\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}$
(C) $x^{2}-y^{2}=2\left(x^{4}+y^{4}\right)$
(D) $x^{2}-y^{2}=2\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}$

In the field of real numbers, solve the equation

$$
\frac{x}{x+4}=\frac{5\lfloor x\rfloor-7}{7\lfloor x\rfloor-5}
$$

where $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer that does not exceed the number $x$ (i.e., the floor function of the real number $x$).

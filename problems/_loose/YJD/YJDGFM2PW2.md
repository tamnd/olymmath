Let $M$ be the centroid of $\triangle A B C$. Prove the inequality

$$
\sin \angle C A M+\sin \angle C B M \leq \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

(a) if the circumscribed circle of $\triangle A M C$ is tangent to the line $A B$;

(b) for any $\triangle A B C$.

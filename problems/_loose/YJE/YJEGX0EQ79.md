18-3 (C17, Finland) It is known that a rectangular box can be filled with unit cubes. If it is instead filled with as many volume-2 cubes as possible, and these cubes are aligned with the edges of the box, then the box's volume is exactly filled to $40 \%$. Try to find the volume of a rectangular box with this property $(\sqrt[3]{2}=1.259 \mathrm{~g} \cdots)$.

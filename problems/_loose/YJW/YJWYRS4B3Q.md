Bolyai Farkas gives the following construction for dividing a segment $AB$ into $n$ equal parts, which does not require the construction of parallel lines:

On a line passing through $A$ but different from $AB$, measure off in the same direction the distances $AF = (n-2)u$ and $AK = (n-1)u$ with an arbitrary segment $u$. Then, on the line $KB$, measure off from $K$ towards $B$ the distance $KD = (n-1)KB$. The line $FD$ intersects $AB$ at point $C$, such that $CB = \frac{AB}{n}$. Prove the correctness of this construction!

Let $n$ be a natural number, $n>1$. The squares of a $2 n \times 2 n$ board are colored blue or red such that for any two rows, there are exactly $n$ columns with the property that the two squares at the intersection of that column and the two rows being considered are colored the same color. Prove that the number $n$ must be even.

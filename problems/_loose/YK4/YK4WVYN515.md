Let the focus of the parabola $y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)$ be $F$, $AB$ be a focal chord of the parabola, point $M$ be on the directrix of the parabola, and $O$ be the origin. Prove:
(1) The slopes of the lines $MA$, $MF$, and $MB$ form an arithmetic sequence;
(2) When $MA \perp MB$, $\angle MFO=|\angle BMF-\angle AMF|$.

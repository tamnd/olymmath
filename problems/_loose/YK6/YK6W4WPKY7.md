Let $E$ and $F$ be two $\mathbb{K}$-vector spaces of finite dimensions $n$ and $p$ respectively ($n, p \geq 1$), and let $f: E \longrightarrow F$ be a linear map.

1) In this question only, assume that $n > p$. Can the linear map $f$ be injective?
2) In this question only, assume that $n < p$. Can the linear map $f$ be surjective?

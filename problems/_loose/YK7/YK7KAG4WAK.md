Let $\varepsilon$ be an arbitrarily small positive number. On the number line, at each point $1, 2, \cdots, n, \cdots$, a "pit" centered at that point with a width of $2 \varepsilon$ is removed. A "compass"-style robot starts from point $A$ on the number line and moves forward by $\sqrt{2}$ units along the positive $x$-axis at each step. Prove that the robot's foot (the tip of the compass) will eventually fall into a pit.

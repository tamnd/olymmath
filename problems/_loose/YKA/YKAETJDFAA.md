In Greek mythology, the "many-headed serpent" god is composed of some heads and necks, with each neck connecting two heads. With each strike of a sword, one can sever all the necks connected to a certain head $A$, but head $A$ immediately grows new necks connecting to all the heads it was not previously connected to (each head is connected by only one neck). Only by severing the "many-headed serpent" into two disconnected parts can it be defeated. Find the smallest positive integer $N$ such that any "many-headed serpent" god with 100 necks can be defeated with no more than $N$ strikes.

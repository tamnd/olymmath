Let $S$ be the center of the circumscribed circle, and $H$ be the orthocenter of triangle $A B C$. Furthermore, let $Q$ be a point such that $S$ is the midpoint of segment $H Q$, and let $T_{1}, T_{2}$, and $T_{3}$ be the centroids of triangles $B C Q, C A Q$, and $A B Q$, respectively. Prove that

$$
A T_{1}=B T_{2}=C T_{3}=\frac{4}{3} R
$$

where $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle around triangle $A B C$.

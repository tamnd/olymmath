Given $a, b, c \in \mathbf{R}_{+}$. Prove:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sum \sqrt[4]{\frac{\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)\left(a^{2}-a b+b^{2}\right)}{2}} \\
\leqslant \frac{2}{3}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right) \sum \frac{1}{a+b} .
\end{array}
$$

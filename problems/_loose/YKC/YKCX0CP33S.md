Solve the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 52 x-34 y-z+9 a=0 \ldots \\
& 49 x-31 y-3 z-b=0 \ldots \\
& 36 x-24 y+z+3 a+2 b=0 \ldots
\end{aligned}
$$

Here, $a$ and $b$ represent given positive numbers. Determine what $a$ and $b$ must be relative to each other so that all unknowns are positive, and at what values of $a$ and $b$ the value of $x$ will be smaller.

Sphere $S$ with diameter $A B$ touches plane $\Pi$ at point $A$. Prove that the stereographic projection of the symmetry with respect to a plane parallel to $\Pi$ and passing through the center of sphere $S$, transforms into an inversion with center $A$ and power $A B^{2}$. More precisely, if points $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ are symmetric with respect to the specified plane, and $Y_{1}$ and $Y_{2}$ are the images of points $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ under the stereographic projection, then $Y_{1}$ is the image of point $Y_{2}$ under the specified inversion.

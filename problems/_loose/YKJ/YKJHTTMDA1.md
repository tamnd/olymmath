**10** A circle is**interesting** if its center lies on the Y-axis and is tangent to the hyperbola $x^2-y^2=1$ , whose vertex on the right branch is $A$ . When two**interesting** circles $O_1$ and $O_2$ are externally tangent at $P$ , there exists constant $k$ such that $O_1O_2 = k \cdot AP$ always holds true. Find $k$ .

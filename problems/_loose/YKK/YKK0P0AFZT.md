$$
a=x^{3}-100 x, b=x^{4}-16, c=x+20-x^{2}
$$

is positive (the median of the three given numbers $a, b, c$ is the number $v$ in the triplet $u \leqslant v \leqslant w$, obtained by arranging the given numbers in non-decreasing order).

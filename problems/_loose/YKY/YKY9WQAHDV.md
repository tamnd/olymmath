We inscribe a regular 12-sided polygon $S$ in a given circle, let its area be $t_{1}$. Then we connect every vertex of $S$ to the third subsequent vertex. These chords enclose another (ordinary) 12-sided polygon, let the area of this polygon be $t_{3}$. Similarly, we connect every vertex of $S$ to the fourth subsequent, then to the fifth subsequent vertex, the areas of the two (ordinary) 12-sided polygons thus formed are $t_{4}$ and $t_{5}$, respectively. Show that the arithmetic mean of $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$ is $t_{5}$, and the geometric mean of $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$ is $t_{4}$.

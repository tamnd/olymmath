Prove that in a right-angled triangle, the following holds

$$
\cos ^{2} \frac{\alpha-\beta}{2} \geq \frac{2 a b}{c^{2}}
$$

where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the acute angles, $a$ and $b$ are the lengths of the legs, and $c$ is the length of the hypotenuse.

(2004 Belarusian Mathematical Olympiad Problem) Positive integers $a, b, c$ satisfy the equation $c(a c+1)^{2}=(5 c + 2 b)(2 c + b)$,
(1) Prove: If $c$ is odd, then $c$ is a perfect square;
(2) For some $a, b$, does there exist an even $c$ that satisfies (1);
(3) Prove: Equation (1) has infinitely many positive integer solutions $(a, b, c)$.

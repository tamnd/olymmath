Four. (50 points) Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n} \in \mathbf{R}, n \geqslant 2$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}=1$. Denote $k_{n}=2-2 \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n-1}}$. Prove: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{1-x_{i}^{2}}+k_{n} \sum_{1 \leqslant j<k \leqslant n} x_{j} x_{k} \geqslant n-1$.

On the ray $\left(O B\right.$, points $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{k}, \ldots$ are considered such that $O B=2 \text{ cm}, O A_{1}=1 \text{ cm}$ and $A_{n} A_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2^{n}} \text{ cm}$, for any non-zero natural number $n$.

a) What is the greatest possible length of the segment $A_{1} A_{10}$? What is the smallest?

b) Show that, regardless of their arrangement (under the conditions of the problem), all points $A_{n}\left(n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}\right)$ belong to the segment $(O B)$.

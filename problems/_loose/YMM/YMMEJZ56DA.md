Let $M$ and $N$ be two points on the Thales circle of segment $AB$, different from $A$ and $B$. Let $C$ be the midpoint of segment $NA$, and $D$ be the midpoint of segment $NB$. The circle is intersected by line $MC$ at point $E$ (second intersection), and by line $MD$ at point $F$ (second intersection). What is the value of

$$
M C \cdot C E + M D \cdot D F
$$

if $AB = 2$ units?

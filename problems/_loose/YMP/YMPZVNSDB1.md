The city of Metropolis has $\mathrm{n}$ streets, all of which are straight lines. Each pair of streets has an intersection, and no three streets are concurrent. The city council wishes to decide, at each intersection, which street will have priority over the other. However, to avoid speeding, the council wants to ensure that the same street is never given priority at two consecutive intersections. Prove that this wish is achievable.

In a certain kingdom, there are 3 types of roads: highways, railways, and rural dirt roads. It is known that from each city, exactly 3 roads extend out, and each of the 3 types of roads is exactly 1. Moreover, from any city, it is possible to reach any other city. Prove that it is possible to travel through all cities in the country without traversing any road in different directions more than once.

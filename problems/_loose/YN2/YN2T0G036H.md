Consider a triplet of real numbers $(a, b, c)$. We can perform the following action: choose $x$ and $y$, two of the numbers in the triplet, and replace them with $(x-y) / \sqrt{2}$ and $(x+y) / \sqrt{2}$. Can we go from the initial triplet $(2, \sqrt{2}, 1 / \sqrt{2})$ to the final triplet $(1, \sqrt{2}, 1+\sqrt{2})$?

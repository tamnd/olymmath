Let $a, b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers. Prove that
$$\frac{(a^2+2)^2 - 3}{b^3 + b} +\frac{(b^2+2)^2 - 3}{c^3 + c}+\frac{(c^2+2)^2 - 3}{a^3 + a} \geq 9. $$
When is equality achieved?

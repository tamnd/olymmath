A watch has, in addition to the usual 3 hands, a stopper hand whose axis is the same as that of the hour and minute hands, while the regular second hand (as usual) has its own separate axis. The stopper is started when the second hand points to the $10 \mathrm{s}$ mark. Extending the second hand and the stopper hand in all their positions and marking their intersection point, on what line do these intersection points lie? - What would be the result if the starting position were different?

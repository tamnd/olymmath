Find all positive integers $n$ for which the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$ can be divided into three non-empty disjoint sets with different numbers of elements such that in any pair of sets, the set with the smaller number of elements has a greater sum of its elements. (If a set has one element, we consider it as the sum of all its elements.)

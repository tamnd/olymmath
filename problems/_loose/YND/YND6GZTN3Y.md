Let $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}_{+}^{*}$. Prove that:

$$
\frac{a b c\left(a+b+c+\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}\right)}{\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)(a b+b c+a c)} \leq \frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{9}
$$

Let's denote the radius of the sphere circumscribed around a square-based pyramid by $R$, the radius of the sphere inscribed in the pyramid by $r$, and the distance between the centers of the two spheres by $d$. Show that

$$
d^{2}+(R+r)^{2}=2 R^{2}
$$

What can we say about the maximum value of the ratio $r / R$ based on this? (See F. 2096.)

Positive integers $a, b$ and $c$ are all less than $2020$. We know that $a$ divides $b + c$, $b$ divides $a + c$ and $c$ divides $a + b$. How many such ordered triples $(a, b, c)$ are there?

Note: In an ordered triple, the order of the numbers matters, so the ordered triple $(0, 1, 2)$ is not the same as the ordered triple $(2, 0, 1)$.

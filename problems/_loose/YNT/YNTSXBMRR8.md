Two congruent circles $k_{1}, k_{2}$ are given, each with a radius equal to the distance between their centers. Let the points of intersection be $A$ and $B$. Choose a point $C$ on circle $k_{2}$ such that the segment $B C$ intersects circle $k_{1}$ at a point different from $B$, which we denote as $L$. The line $A C$ intersects circle $k_{1}$ at a point different from $A$, which we denote as $K$. Prove that the line on which the median from vertex $C$ of triangle $K L C$ lies passes through a fixed point independent of the position of point $C$.

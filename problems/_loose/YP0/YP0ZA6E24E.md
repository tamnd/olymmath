$A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$ are the altitudes of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ with the lengths of the corresponding sides $a, b$ and $c$. $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ are the projections of point $A^{\prime}$ onto sides $A B$ and $A C$ respectively; $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$ are the projections of point $B^{\prime}$ onto sides $B C$ and $B A$ respectively; $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ are the projections of point $C^{\prime}$ onto sides $C A$ and $C B$ respectively. Prove that

$$
a^{2} S\left(A^{\prime} A_{1} A_{2}\right)+b^{2} S\left(B^{\prime} B_{1} B_{2}\right)+c^{2} S\left(C^{\prime} C_{1} C_{2}\right)=\frac{S^{3}}{R^{2}}
$$

where $S$ is the area of triangle $A B C$, and $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle around it.

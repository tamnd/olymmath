Let points $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}, \mathrm{C}, \mathrm{D}$ be such that $\mathrm{AB} \| \mathrm{CD}$ and $\mathrm{CD}=\frac{A B}{2}$. Let $\mathrm{AD} \cap \mathrm{BC}=\{\mathrm{E}\}$ and two distinct points F and G symmetric with respect to C, where F, G $\notin$ BC. Show that the lines EG and BF are:

a) parallel if $\mathrm{A}$ and $\mathrm{D}$ are on the same side of the line $\mathrm{BC}$;

b) concurrent at the midpoint of segment $[\mathrm{BF}]$, if A and D are on opposite sides of the line BC.

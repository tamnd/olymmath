Each subsequent number is chosen independently of the previous ones. If it matches any of the already chosen numbers, move clockwise to the first unchosen number.

In the end, $k$ distinct numbers are obtained.

a) (From 6th grade. 1 point). Is it true that the appearance of each specific number in such a sample is equally probable?

b) (From 7th grade. 1 point). Is it true that the appearance of all samples is equally probable?

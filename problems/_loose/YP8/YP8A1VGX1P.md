Given
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{x}{m(n b+k c-m a)}=\frac{y}{n(k c+m a-n b)} \\
=\frac{z}{k(m a+n b-k c)} .
\end{array}
$$

Prove: $\frac{m}{x(b y+c z-a x)}=\frac{n}{y(c z+a x-b y)}$
$$
=\frac{k}{z(a x+b y-c z)} \text {. }
$$

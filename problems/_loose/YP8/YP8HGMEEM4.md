It is known that among the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$, the numbers $1, 1 / 2, 1 / 3, \ldots, 1 / n$ appear exactly once, and among the numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{n}$ - also. It is also known that $a_{1}+b_{1} \geqslant a_{2}+b_{2} \geqslant a_{3}+b_{3} \geqslant \ldots \geqslant a_{n}+b_{n}$. Prove that $a_{m}+b_{m} \leqslant 4 / m$ for all $m$ from 1 to $n$.

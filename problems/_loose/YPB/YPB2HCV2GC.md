Let $p, q$ be distinct primes, and let $n \geqslant 3$ be a positive integer. Find all integers $a$ such that the polynomial $f(x)=x^{n}+a x^{n-1}+p q$ can be factored into the product of two polynomials with positive degrees and integer coefficients.

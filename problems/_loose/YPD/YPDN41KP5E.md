Let $n$ be a positive integer. It is known that there are $2 n$ baskets, each containing red, yellow, and blue balls. Prove that it is possible to select $n+1$ baskets such that the number of red, yellow, and blue balls in them is at least half of all the red, yellow, and blue balls, respectively.

Given a hyperbola: $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a, b>0)$, a fixed point $M(m, 0)(0<|m|<a)$, and a fixed line $l: x=\frac{a^{2}}{m}$, a moving line through point $M$ intersects the hyperbola at points $A$ and $B$. $C$ is any point on the hyperbola, and the lines $C A$ and $C B$ intersect the line $l$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove: The length of the tangent from the intersection point of line $l$ with the $x$-axis to the circle with $P Q$ as its diameter is a constant.

A black bishop and a white king are placed randomly on a $2000 \times 2000$ chessboard (in distinct squares). Let $p$ be the probability that the bishop attacks the king (that is, the bishop and king lie on some common diagonal of the board). Then $p$ can be expressed in the form $\tfrac{m}{n}$ , where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m$ .

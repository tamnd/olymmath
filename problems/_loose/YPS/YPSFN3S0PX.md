The artisan stops working at $12 \mathrm{~h}$, but tells his assistant that he should continue working until he produces the same number of bracelets as him. At what time will the assistant stop working?
(a) $12 \mathrm{~h}$
(b) $12 \mathrm{~h} 30 \mathrm{~min}$
(c) $13 \mathrm{~h}$
(d) $13 \mathrm{~h} 30 \mathrm{~min}$
(e) $14 \mathrm{~h} 30 \mathrm{~min}$

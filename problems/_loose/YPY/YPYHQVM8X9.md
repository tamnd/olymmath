Francesco and Andrea decide to consult the mathematical oracle to find out if they have any lucky pairs $(x, y)$ of numbers (real numbers). To determine the pair (or pairs) of lucky numbers, the oracle asks both Francesco and Andrea for their birth day $(g)$ and month $(m)$, after which it solves the system for each of them:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
13 x-y=181 \\
g x-m y=362
\end{array}\right.
$$

The oracle's response is that Andrea has no lucky pairs of numbers, while Francesco has infinitely many lucky pairs of numbers. Which of the following statements is correct?

(A) Francesco and Andrea were both born in spring

(B) Francesco and Andrea were both born in summer

(C) Francesco and Andrea were both born in autumn

(D) Francesco and Andrea were both born in winter

(E) Francesco and Andrea were born in different seasons.

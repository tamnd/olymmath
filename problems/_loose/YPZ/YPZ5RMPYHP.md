At each lesson, she quizzes one student out of three, and among those questioned, there is always one out of ten who was already called on the previous lesson. One of the students, a notorious slacker, decided that he would only prepare the homework for the next lesson if he wasn't asked in the previous one.

"Which is more likely," he wonders, "to study in vain or to be punished by Miss Blackwick for not studying the lesson?"

The base of the pyramid $A B C D P$ with vertex $P$ is a cyclic quadrilateral inscribed in a circle. It is known that the lines $A D$ and $B C$ intersect at point $K$, outside this circle. The angles $\angle A D P = \angle B C P = 90^{\circ}$, and the angles $\angle A P K$ and $\angle B P K$ are acute. Prove that the line $P K$ is perpendicular to the line $A B$.

Inside an equilateral triangle $A B C$, a point $M$ is marked arbitrarily. Prove that one can choose a point $C_{1}$ on side $A B$, a point $A_{1}$ on side $B C$, and a point $B_{1}$ on side $A C$ in such a way that the lengths of the sides of triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ are equal to the segments $M A, M B$, and $M C$.

Are there 1989 positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{1989}$ such that for every $i=3,4, \ldots, 1989$ we have

$$
a_{i}+S_{i}=\left(a_{i}, S_{i}\right)+\left[a_{i}, S_{i}\right]
$$

where $S_{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{i} a_{j}$ and $(\cdot, \cdot)$ and $[\cdot, \cdot]$ are the greatest common divisor and the lowest common multiple respectively.

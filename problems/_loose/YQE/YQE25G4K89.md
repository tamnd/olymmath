A student wrote a sequence of 16 numbers on the board during math class. A second student in the class writes under each number how often this number appears in the sequence. This gives a second sequence of 16 numbers. A third student writes under each number of the second sequence how often it appears in that sequence. This gives a third sequence of numbers. Subsequently, a fourth, fifth, sixth, and seventh student each create a sequence from the previous one. In the end, it turns out that the first six sequences are all different. The seventh sequence is the same as the sixth sequence.

Give a possible sequence that the first student could have written.

Let $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ be an equipotent partition of $S=\{2,3, \cdots, 2015\}$ (i.e., $S_{1} \cup S_{2}=S$, $S_{1} \cap S_{2}=\varnothing$, and $\left|S_{1}\right|=\left|S_{2}\right|=1007$). Denote the product of all numbers in $S_{1}$ as $a$, and the product of all numbers in $S_{2}$ as $b$. The number $p=a+b$ is called the characteristic number of the equipotent partition of $S$. Prove:
(1) The characteristic number of the equipotent partition of $S$ must be a composite number;
(2) If the characteristic number of the equipotent partition is not a multiple of 2, then the characteristic number is a multiple of $2015^{33}$.
Note: The number of elements in a finite set $M$ is denoted by $|M|$.

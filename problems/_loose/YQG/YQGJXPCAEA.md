The lobster of a positive integer is the number that results when you write its digits in reverse order. Thus, the lobster of 8267 is 7628 and the lobster of 15620 is 2651 (since leading zeros always drop off).

What is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $n$ minus the lobster of $n$ equals 12345678?

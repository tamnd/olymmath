Given is a rectangle $A B C D$. If we decrease the length of each side of the first pair of opposite sides of rectangle $A B C D$ by $3 \mathrm{~cm}$, and increase the length of each side of the second pair of opposite sides of that rectangle by $7 \mathrm{~cm}$, then a new rectangle is formed whose area is equal to the area of rectangle $A B C D$. If we increase the length of each side of the first pair of opposite sides of rectangle $A B C D$ by $3 \mathrm{~cm}$, and decrease the length of each side of the second pair of opposite sides of that rectangle by $5 \mathrm{~cm}$, then a new rectangle is formed whose area is again equal to the area of rectangle $A B C D$. What is the area of rectangle $A B C D$?

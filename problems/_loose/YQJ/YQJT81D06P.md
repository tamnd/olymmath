Given $B_{1}, B_{2}$ are the lower and upper vertices of the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{8}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$, respectively, and a line $l$ passing through the point $A(0,-2 \sqrt{2})$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $P, Q$ (different from points $B_{1}, B_{2}$).
(1) If $\tan \angle B_{1} P B_{2}=2 \tan \angle B_{1} Q B_{2}$, find the equation of the line $l$;
(2) Let $R$ be the intersection point of the lines $B_{1} P$ and $B_{2} Q$. Find the value of $\overrightarrow{A R} \cdot \overrightarrow{B_{1} B_{2}}$.

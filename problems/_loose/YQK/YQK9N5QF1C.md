Suppose function $f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ satisfies
(1) $\forall x,y \geq 0,$ we have $f(x)f(y)\leq y^2f(\frac{x}{2})+x^2f(\frac{y}{2})$ ;
(2) $\forall 0 \leq x \leq 1, f(x) \leq 2016$ .
Prove that $f(x)\leq x^2$ for all $x\geq 0$ .

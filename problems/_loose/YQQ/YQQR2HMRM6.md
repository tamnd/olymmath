The bisector of the angle formed by the non-parallel sides of a trapezoid, extended to their point of intersection, is constructed. Prove that the segment $ t $ of this bisector, enclosed between the bases of the trapezoid, is expressed through its sides by the formula:

$$
t^{2}=\frac{4 b d}{(b+d)^{2}}(p-a)(p-c)
$$

where $ p $ is the semiperimeter, and $ a $ and $ c $ are the lengths of the bases of the trapezoid.

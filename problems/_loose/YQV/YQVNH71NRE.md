For a circle, let the length of the tangent segments drawn from an external point $P$ be $h$, and the midpoint of the segment connecting the points of tangency be $F$. Prove that for a chord $AB$ of the circle, $AP \cdot PB = h^2$ if and only if the line $AB$ coincides with the line through $P$ or $F$.

An investigative laboratory has a special (weightless) room where objects float wherever they are left. The room has the shape of a rectangular prism with a height of $4 \text{m}$ and a base that is a square with a side length of $7 \text{m}$. In the center of the base, there is a supporting pillar in the shape of a rectangular prism with a base that is a square with a side length of $1 \text{m}$ and a height of $4 \text{m}$.

a) In the room, 16 balloons in the shape of spheres with a diameter of $10 \text{cm}$ are scattered, floating. Is there a rectangular prism with edges of $2 \text{m}$, $3 \text{m}$, and $2 \text{m}$ and faces parallel to the walls of the room, in which there must be parts of 2 different balloons?

b) What would happen if there were 17 balloons? In this case, must there be a rectangular prism with edges of $2 \text{m}$, $3 \text{m}$, and $2 \text{m}$ and faces parallel to the walls of the room, in which there must be parts of 2 different balloons?

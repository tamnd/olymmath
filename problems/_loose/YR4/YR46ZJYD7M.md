Let $q_{1}, q_{2}, \ldots, q_{n}(n \geq 2)$ be pairwise distinct prime numbers. Prove that from this assumption it always follows that

$$
\frac{q_{1}^{3}+1}{q_{1}^{3}-1} \cdot \frac{q_{2}^{3}+1}{q_{2}^{3}-1} \cdot \ldots \cdot \frac{q_{n}^{3}+1}{q_{n}^{3}-1}<\frac{36}{25}
$$

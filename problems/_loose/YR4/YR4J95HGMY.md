In $\triangle A B C$, prove:
(1)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a\left(-h_{a}+h_{b}+h_{c}\right)+b\left(h_{a}-h_{b}+h_{c}\right) \\
+c\left(h_{a}+h_{b}-h_{c}\right) \geqslant 6 \Delta ;
\end{array}
$$
(2) $a\left(-t_{a}+t_{b}+t_{c}\right)+b\left(t_{a}-t_{b}+t_{c}\right)+c\left(t_{a}+t_{b}-t_{c}\right)$ $\geqslant 6 \Delta$.

where $h_{a}, t_{a}$ are the altitude and angle bisector from $A$, and so on.

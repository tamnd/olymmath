In an acute triangle $ABC$, $AD$ is the altitude from $A$ to $BC$, and $H$ is the orthocenter. A circle centered at $A$ with radius $\sqrt{AH \cdot AD}$ intersects the circle with diameter $BC$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Prove that $P, Q$, and $H$ are collinear. Furthermore, if $B, P$ and $C, Q$ are on opposite sides of line $AD$, then $BQ, CP$, and $AD$ are concurrent.

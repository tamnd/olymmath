Let's prove that if a quadrilateral is both a cyclic and a tangential quadrilateral (a so-called bicentric quadrilateral), and if we draw unit vectors parallel to its directed sides from a point, then the resultant of these vectors is perpendicular to the line connecting the centers of the circumcircle and the incircle of the quadrilateral, or it is the zero vector.

Calculate the line integral

$$
\int_{L} \frac{y}{3} d x-3 x d y+x d z
$$

along the part of the curve $L$ given parametrically by

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x=2 \cos t \\
y=2 \sin t \\
z=1-2 \cos t-2 \sin t
\end{array} 0 \leq t \leq \frac{\pi}{2}\right.
$$

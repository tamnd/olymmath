17 Let $\left\{A_{n}=\left(a_{n}, b_{n}\right)\right\}$ be a sequence of vectors with integer components. Prove: There exists a positive integer $N$, such that for any vector $A_{n}$, there are $N$ integers $k_{n, 1}, k_{n, 2}, \cdots$, $k_{n, N}$, such that $A_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{N} k_{n, i} A_{i}$.

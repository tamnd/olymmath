Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ be the vertices of a regular $n$-sided polygon inscribed in a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$, and let $P$ be on the extension of $O A_{1}$. Prove that: $\left|P A_{1}\right|\left|P A_{2}\right| \cdots\left|P A_{n}\right|=$ $|O P|^{n}-r^{n}$.

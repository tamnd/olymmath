If $x>0, y>0$. And $\alpha$ is any real number. Let $M=x^{\sin ^{2} \alpha} y^{\alpha x^{2} \alpha}$, $N=x+y$. Then it must be true that
(A) $M>N$.
(B) $M=N$.
(C) $M<N$.
(D) None of the above conclusions are correct.
(China Beijing High School Mathematics Competition, 1985)

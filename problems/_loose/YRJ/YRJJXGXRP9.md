A digit is considered a godchild of a natural number if it is the unit digit of some divisor of that number. For example, the divisors of 56 are 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 14, 28, and 56, so the godchildren of 56 are 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, and 8.

a) What are the godchildren of 57?

b) Find a number that has 7 and 9 as godchildren, but not 3. What are the godchildren of this number?

c) Explain why 2 and 5 are godchildren of any number that has 0 among its godchildren.

d) Explain why 8 is a godchild of any number that has 0 and 9 among its godchildren.

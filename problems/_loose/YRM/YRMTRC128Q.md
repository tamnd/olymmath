Inside triangle $ABC$, there is a point $M$. Prove that

$$
\frac{S_{A C M}+S_{B C M}}{S_{A B M}}=\frac{C M}{C_{1} M}
$$

where $C_{1}$ is the point of intersection of the lines $AB$ and $CM$.

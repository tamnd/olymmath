For any real numbers $p \neq 0, q$ and $k \neq \pm 1$, prove the statement: The equation

$$
x^{2} + p x + q = 0
$$

has two roots in the set of real numbers, one of which is $k$ times the other, if and only if $k p^{2} = (k+1)^{2} q$.

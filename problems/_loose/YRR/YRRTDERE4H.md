Sašo prepared for a cycling marathon for seven weeks. He cycled three times a week. Each subsequent week, he cycled $30 \mathrm{~km}$ more than the previous week. Each week, the first two days he cycled the same distance, and on the third day, he cycled $10 \mathrm{~km}$ more than the first two days. In the first week, he cycled $70 \mathrm{~km}$.

A. How many km did Sašo cycle on the first day of preparation?

B. How many $\mathrm{km}$ did he cycle on the last day of preparation?

C. How many km did he cycle in the seven-week preparation?

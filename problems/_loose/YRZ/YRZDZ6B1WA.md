On a straight line, there are four distinct points in sequence $A, B, C, D$. The point with the minimum sum of distances to $A, B, C, D$
(A) can be a point outside the line $A D$.
(B) is only point $B$ or $C$.
(C) is only the midpoint of segment $A D$.
(D) has infinitely many points.
(China Junior High School Mathematics League, 1987)

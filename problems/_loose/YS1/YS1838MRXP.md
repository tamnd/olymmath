Let $a,b$ and $c$ be positive real numbers . Prove that$$\left | \frac{4(b^3-c^3)}{b+c}+ \frac{4(c^3-a^3)}{c+a}+ \frac{4(a^3-b^3)}{a+b} \right |\leq (b-c)^2+(c-a)^2+(a-b)^2.$$

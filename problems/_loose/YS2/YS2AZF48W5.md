Given $I$ is the incenter of acute $\triangle ABC$, the rays $AI, BI, CI$ intersect the circumcircle $\odot O$ of $\triangle ABC$ at points $A', B', C'$ respectively. Let $AA'$ intersect $BC$, $BB'$ intersect $CA$, and $CC'$ intersect $AB$ at points $D, E, F$ respectively. Denote the circumradius and inradius of $\triangle ABC$ as $R$ and $r$ respectively. Prove:
$$
\frac{AA'}{AD} + \frac{BB'}{BE} + \frac{CC'}{CF} = \frac{R}{r} + 2.
$$

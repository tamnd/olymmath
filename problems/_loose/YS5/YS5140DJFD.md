Limit Points. Let $A B C$ be an isosceles right triangle with a right angle at vertex $C$. Further, let $P_{0}=A$, $P_{1}=$ midpoint of $B C, P_{2 k}=$ midpoint of $A P_{2 k-1}$ and $P_{2 k+1}=$ midpoint of $B P_{2 k}$ for $k=1,2,3, \ldots$. Show that the sequences $\left\{P_{2 k}\right\}$ and $\left\{P_{2 k+1}\right\}$ converge to points that divide the hypotenuse into 3 equal parts.

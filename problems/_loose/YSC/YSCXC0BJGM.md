A rectangular parallelepiped has the measurements of the three edges meeting at one vertex in a geometric mean relationship, and the sum of the two smaller measurements equals the largest one. We connect one vertex of the body to the three neighbors of the opposite vertex. The dihedral angles of the tetrahedron defined by these 4 vertices need to be calculated.

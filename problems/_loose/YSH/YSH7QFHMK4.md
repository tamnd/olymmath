In the tetrahedron $ABCD$, $AB$ is perpendicular to $CD$, and $O$ is an arbitrary point in space. Prove that the sum of the squares of the distances from point $O$ to the midpoints of edges $AC$ and $BD$ is equal to the sum of the squares of the distances from point $O$ to the midpoints of $AD$ and $BC$.

Point $O$ lies inside a convex $n$-gon $A_{1} \ldots A_{n}$ and is connected to the vertices by segments. The sides of the $n$-gon are numbered from 1 to $n$, with different sides having different numbers. The same is done with the segments $O A_{1}, \ldots, O A$.

a) For $n=9$, find a numbering such that the sum of the side numbers for all triangles $A_{1} O A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n} O A_{1}$ is the same.

b) Prove that for $n=10$, such a numbering cannot be achieved.

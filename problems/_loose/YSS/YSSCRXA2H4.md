Suppose there is a convex quadrilateral inside a square, and there is a point $A$ inside the convex quadrilateral. It is known that any 3 points among the 9 points, which include the 4 vertices of the square, the 4 vertices of the convex quadrilateral, and point $A$, are not collinear. Prove that from these 9 points, 5 points can be selected such that they are the 5 vertices of some convex pentagon.

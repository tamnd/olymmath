Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3} ; b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}$ all be positive numbers, prove:
$$
\left(\frac{a_{1}}{b_{1}}+\frac{a_{1}}{b_{2}}+\frac{a_{3}}{b_{3}}\right)\left(\frac{b_{1}}{a_{1}}+\frac{b_{3}}{a_{2}}+\frac{b_{3}}{a_{3}}\right) \geq 9
$$

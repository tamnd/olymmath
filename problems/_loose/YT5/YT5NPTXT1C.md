A $2 n$-gon $A_{1} \ldots A_{2 n}$ is inscribed in a circle. Let $p_{1}, \ldots, p_{2 n}$ be the distances from an arbitrary point $M$ on the circle to the sides $A_{1} A_{2}, A_{2} A_{3}, \ldots, A_{2 n} A_{1}$. Prove that $p_{1} p_{3} \ldots p_{2 n-1}=p_{2} p_{4} \ldots p_{2 n}$.

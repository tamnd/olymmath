Let $x>y>0$ be real numbers that satisfy the equation

$$
x^{y}=y^{x} \text {. }
$$

Show that there exists $s>0$ such that

$$
x=\left(1+\frac{1}{s}\right)^{s+1}, y=\left(1+\frac{1}{s}\right)^{s}
$$

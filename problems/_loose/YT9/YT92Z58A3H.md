On a meadow, there are 3 anthills: $A, B$, and $C$. The distance between anthills $A$ and $B$ is 260 mm, and the distance between anthills $B$ and $C$ is 1200 mm, with $\measuredangle A B C=60^{\circ}$. An ant $a$ starts from anthill $A$ towards anthill $B$, moving in a straight line at a speed of $1 \frac{\text { mm }}{\mathrm{c}}$. At the same moment, an ant $b$ starts from anthill $B$ towards anthill $C$, also moving in a straight line at a speed of $3 \frac{\text { mm }}{\mathrm{c}}$. At what moment will the distance between ants $a$ and $b$ be minimal?

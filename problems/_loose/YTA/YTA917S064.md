Given a triangle $ABC$ with area $P$. From its medians, another triangle is constructed, then from the medians of the second triangle, a third triangle is constructed, and so on. Generally, the $(n+1)$-th triangle is constructed from the medians of the $n$-th triangle. Calculate the sum of the areas of all triangles in the resulting sequence.

Given a right-angled triangle $A B C$ with $\overline{B C}=a, \overline{A C}=b$ and $\overline{\angle A C B}=90^{\circ}$. A semicircle over a segment of $A B$ is positioned such that the sides $B C$ and $A C$ lie on tangents to this semicircle and touch $B C$ and $A C$.

Prove that for its radius $r$, then $\frac{1}{r}=\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}$ holds!

Let a complete directed graph be determined by $n$ known points. That is, for any two points $P_{i}, P_{j}$ in the point set $\left\{P_{1}\right.$, $\left.P_{2}, \cdots, P_{n}\right\}$, there is a definite direction $P_{i} \rightarrow P_{j}$ (or $P_{j} \rightarrow P_{i}$). Prove that there exists a permutation of the known point set
$$
\left[P_{i_{1}}, P_{i_{2}}, \cdots, P_{i_{n}}\right]=\left[a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}\right],
$$

such that the following chain of directed relations holds:
$$
a_{1} \rightarrow a_{2} \rightarrow \cdots \rightarrow a_{n} .
$$

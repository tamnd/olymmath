G21 (7-5, Romania) In $\triangle O A B$, it is known that $\angle A O B$ is an acute angle. Find the locus of $H$ in the following two cases:
(a) When the moving point $M$ is on the line segment $A B$;
(b) When the moving point $M$ is inside $\triangle A O B$.

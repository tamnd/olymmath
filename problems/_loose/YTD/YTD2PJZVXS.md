Let $\mathrm{C}$ and $\mathrm{C}^{\prime}$ be two circles with centers $\mathrm{O}$ and $\mathrm{O}^{\prime}$, external to each other. A common external tangent intersects the two common internal tangents at points $M$ and $N$.

Show that $(O M)$ is perpendicular to $\left(\mathrm{O}^{\prime} \mathrm{M}\right)$ and that $(\mathrm{ON})$ is perpendicular to $\left(\mathrm{O}^{\prime} \mathrm{N}\right)$.

Prove that $$ \frac{1}{a^2+2} + \frac{1}{b^2+2} + \frac{1}{c^2+2} \le \frac{1}{6ab+c^2} + \frac{1}{6bc+a^2} + \frac{1}{6ca+b^2} $$ for all positive real numbers $a$ , $b$ and $c$ satisfying $a^2+b^2+c^2=1$ .

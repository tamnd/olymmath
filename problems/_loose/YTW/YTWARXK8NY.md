Given points $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n}$. Consider a circle of radius $R$ containing some of them. We then construct a circle of radius $R$ centered at the center of mass of the points lying inside the first circle, and so on. Prove that this process will stop, i.e., the circles will start to coincide.

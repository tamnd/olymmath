In a regular eighteen-sided polygon $A_{1} \ldots A_{18}$, diagonals $A_{a} A_{d}, A_{b} A_{e}$, and $A_{c} A_{f}$ are drawn. Let $k=a-b, p=b-c, m=c-d, q=d-e, n=e-f$ and $r=f-a$. Prove that the specified diagonals intersect at one point in any of the following cases:

a) $\{k, m, n\}=\{p, q, r\}$

b) $\{k, m, n\}=\{1,2,7\}$ and $\{p, q, r\}=\{1,3,4\}$;

c) $\{k, m, n\}=\{1,2,8\}$ and $\{p, q, r\}=\{2,2,3\}$.

Note. The equality $\{k, m, n\}=\{x, y, z\}$ means that the specified sets of numbers are the same; the order of their writing is not taken into account.

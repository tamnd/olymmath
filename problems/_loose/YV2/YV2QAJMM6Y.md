Maruška received a magic pouch from her grandmother, which always doubled the amount of gold coins it contained at midnight. On Monday at noon, Maruška put some gold coins into the empty pouch. On Tuesday and Wednesday, she took out 40 gold coins each day and did not put anything in. On Thursday, she took out 40 gold coins again, and the pouch was empty.

How many gold coins did Maruška put into the pouch on Monday?

How many gold coins should she have put into the empty pouch so that she could repeatedly take out 40 gold coins each day, without putting anything in, and the number of gold coins in the pouch would be the same before each withdrawal?

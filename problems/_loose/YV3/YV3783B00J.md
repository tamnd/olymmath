Emil and Ivan are dividing a pile of 25 coins weighing $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 25$ grams respectively. Emil goes first, and subsequently, the one who has collected coins with a greater total weight goes next. If the weights of the coins collected by both are equal, the one who went last goes again. The player whose turn it is selects a coin from the pile, and the opponent decides who should receive it. Can Emil play in such a way that the total weight of the coins he collects at the end of the game is greater than the weight of Ivan's coins, or can Ivan always prevent this?

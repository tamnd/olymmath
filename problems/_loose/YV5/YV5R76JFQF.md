(2005 High School League Liaoning Province Preliminary) Given $a_{1}=1, a_{2}=3, a_{n}=4 a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}(n \geqslant 3)$; $b_{1}=1, b_{2}=3, b_{n}=\frac{b_{n-1}^{2}+2}{b_{n-2}}(n \geqslant 3)$; $c_{1}=1, c_{n+1}=2 c_{n}+\sqrt{3 c_{n}^{2}-2}(n \geqslant 3)$. Prove that for all positive integers $n$, $a_{n}=b_{n}=c_{n}$.

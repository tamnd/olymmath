Koschei the Deathless captured 43 people and took them to an island. Ivan the Tsarevich set out in a two-person boat to rescue them. Koschei told Ivan:

- I'm tired of feeding these freeloaders; let them row back in your boat in good health. Remember: only two can cross from the island to the shore, but one can row back alone. Before the crossing, I will tell each of them about at least 40 other prisoners being werewolves. You choose who I tell about whom. If a prisoner has heard that someone is a werewolf, he will not sit in the boat with that person, but can stay on the shore. I will enchant them so that they remain silent on land, but in the boat, they will tell each other about all the werewolves they know. As long as at least one prisoner remains on the island, you cannot row with them. Only when all 43 are on the other shore can one of them come back for you. If you fail to organize their crossing, you will stay with me forever. $ [8$ points] (A.V. Shapovalov)

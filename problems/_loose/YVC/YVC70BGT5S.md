Above the plane of the rhombus, we construct the regular triangular pyramids $P A M N$ and $R B C D$ with vertices $P$ and $R$, such that $[P A] \equiv[A M]$ and $[R B] \equiv[B C]$.

a) Calculate the length of the segment $[P R]$.

b) If $A P \cap C R=\{S\}$, prove that $S O \perp(A B C)$.

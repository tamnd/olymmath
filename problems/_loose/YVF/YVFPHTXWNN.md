There is a cube and two paints: red and green. Two people play such a game. The one who starts chooses 3 edges of the cube and paints them red. His partner chooses 3 edges from those that are not yet painted and paints them green. After that, the one who starts again paints 3 edges red, and then his partner paints 3 edges green. It is forbidden to repaint an edge in a different color or to paint it twice with the same color. The one who first manages to paint all the edges of any face with their color wins. Is it true that the one who starts will necessarily win with the correct play?

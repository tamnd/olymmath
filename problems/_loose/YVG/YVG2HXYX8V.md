In a state, there are $n$ cities, and an express train runs between each pair of them (in both directions). For any express train, the ticket prices for the "outbound" and "return" trips are the same, and for any different express trains, these prices are different. Prove that a traveler can choose an initial city, leave it, and travel sequentially on $n-1$ express trains, paying less for each subsequent trip than for the previous one. (The traveler can visit the same city multiple times.)

(I. Bogdanov)

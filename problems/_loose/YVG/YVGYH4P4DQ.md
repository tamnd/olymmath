In the country, there are 100 cities, and non-stop flights operate between any two cities. All flights are paid and cost a positive (possibly non-integer) number of tugriks. For any pair of cities A and B, the flight from A to B costs the same as the flight from B to A. The average cost of a flight is 1 tugrik. A traveler wants to fly around some $m$ different cities in $m$ flights, starting and ending in his hometown. Can he always manage to make such a trip, spending no more than $m$ tugriks on tickets, if

$$
\text { a) } m=99 \text {; }
$$

b) $m=100$ ?

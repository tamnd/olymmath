On an $8 \times 8$ board, there are 8 non-attacking rooks. All cells of the board are distributed among the rooks according to the following rule. The cell on which a rook stands is assigned to that rook. A cell that is attacked by two rooks is obtained by the rook that is closer to this cell; if these two rooks are equidistant from the cell, each of them gets half a cell. Prove that the areas of the territories of all rooks are the same.

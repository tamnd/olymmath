There are 8 white cubes of the same size. Marina needs to paint 24 faces of the cubes blue, and the remaining 24 faces - red. After this, Katya assembles them into a $2 \times 2 \times 2$ cube. If the surface of the cube has an equal number of blue and red squares, Katya wins. If not, Marina wins. Can Marina paint the cubes in such a way that Katya cannot achieve her goal?

In the interior of triangle $ABC$, point $D$ is positioned such that

$$
\angle BDC = \angle BAC + 90^\circ
$$

Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $ABD$ and $ACD$ are perpendicular to each other. (Two circles are perpendicular if the tangents at their intersection points are perpendicular to each other.)

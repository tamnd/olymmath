Let $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be positive numbers and $\Sigma_{k}=\Sigma_{k}\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}\right)$. Prove that

$$
\Sigma_{1} \geqslant \sqrt{\Sigma_{2}} \geqslant \sqrt[3]{\Sigma_{3}} \geqslant \ldots \geqslant \sqrt[n]{\Sigma_{n}}
$$

(Maclaurin).

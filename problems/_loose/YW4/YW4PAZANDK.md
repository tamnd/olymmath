Let the prime $p$ satisfy $p \equiv 1(\bmod 3)$, and $q=2\left[\frac{p}{3}\right]$, if $\frac{1}{1 \times 2}+\frac{1}{3 \times 4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{(q-1) q}$ $=\frac{m}{n}$, where $m, n$ are coprime positive integers, prove that $p$ divides $m$.
where, $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding the real number $x$.

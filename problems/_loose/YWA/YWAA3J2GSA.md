Let $A A_{1}, B B_{1}, C C_{1}$ be the altitudes of triangle $A B C$. The lines $A A_{1}, B B_{1}, C C_{1}$ intersect the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ again at points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$, respectively. The Simson lines corresponding to points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ form triangle $A_{3} B_{3} C_{3}$ (where $A_{3}$ is the intersection of the Simson lines corresponding to points $B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$, and so on). Prove that the centroids of triangles $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$ and $A_{3} B_{3} C_{3}$ coincide, and that the lines $A_{2} A_{3}, B_{2} B_{3}$, and $C_{2} C_{3}$ intersect at a single point.

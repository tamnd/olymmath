Conjecture 7.3.6 When $r=5$, Hadwiger's conjecture holds.
Hadwiger's conjecture when $r=6$ is much more difficult than when $r=5$, and it also relies on the Four Color Theorem. Proofs that do not use the Four Color Theorem show that any minimal counterexample is obtained by adding a vertex to a planar graph, but the Four Color Theorem shows that it is not a counterexample at all.

An operation displaying either the sign + or x are made on a computer display repeatedly. In each operation, assume that the probability displaying the same sign as the eve of one succesively in regardless of the course until then is $p$. First the sign x is displayed on the display. Let $P_n$ be the probability such that $n$'s the sign + appears before the three sign X will appear including the first. Note that the operation is over at the stage of appearing $n$'s the sign +.

(1) Express $P_2$ in terms of $p$.

(2) For $n\geq 3,$ express $P_n$ in terms of $p$ and $n$.

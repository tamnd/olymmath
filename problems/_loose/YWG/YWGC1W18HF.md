Inside a convex $n$-gon $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$, a point $O$ is taken such that $\overrightarrow{O A_{1}}+\ldots+\overrightarrow{O A_{n}}=\overrightarrow{0}$. Let $d=O A_{1}+\ldots+O A_{n}$. Prove that the perimeter of the polygon is not less than $4 d / n$ for even $n$ and not less than $4 d n /\left(n^{2}-1\right)$ for odd $n$.

Consider the circle $(O)$ and a point $A$ inside the circle. A variable circle passing through points $A$ and $O$ intersects the circle $(O)$ at points $M$ and $M^{\prime}$.

$1^{0}$. Show that the line $M M^{\prime}$ passes through a fixed point!

$2^{0}$. Show that the bisector of angle $M A M^{\prime}$ is a fixed line!

$3^{0}$. How does the angle $O M A$ change?

Given three points: $A, B, C$, not on the same line. Construct a fourth point, $D$, such that for any line passing through it, the projections of the given points onto this line, denoted as $A^{\prime}$, $B^{\prime}$, $C^{\prime}$, always satisfy the following vector equation:

$$
D C^{\prime}=D A^{\prime}+D B^{\prime}
$$

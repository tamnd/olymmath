The tenth question: Let the set $\mathrm{S} \subseteq\{1,2, \ldots, 100\}$, it is known that for any two distinct elements $\mathrm{a}$ and $\mathrm{b}$ in $\mathrm{S}$, there exists a positive integer $k$ and two distinct elements $c$ and $d$ in $S$ (which can be equal to $a$ or $b$), such that $c<d$, and $a+b=c^{k} d$. Find the maximum number of elements in the set $S$.

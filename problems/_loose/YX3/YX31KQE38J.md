Young Shooters. Vanya and Kolya went to the shooting range. Kolya suggested:

- Vanya, let's shoot in turns. Whoever hits the target first wins.
- That's not fair! You shoot better. Remember, the Absent-Minded Scientist calculated exactly that on average you spend one shot less for one hit than I do.
- Alright, - agreed Kolya. - You shoot first. Then we will have equal chances of winning.
- It's still not fair, - Vanya thought for a moment and said. - Let's take two guns and shoot at two different targets simultaneously. Then a draw can happen, which means the probability that I at least don't lose is greater than the probability of winning when shooting in turns.

a) (recommended for 8th grade, 2 points). Is Kolya right in claiming that if they shoot in turns, but Vanya shoots first, then their chances are equal?

b) (recommended for 8th grade, 3 points). Is Vanya right in claiming that if they shoot at two targets simultaneously, then the probability of not losing is greater for her than the probability of winning when shooting in turns?

A cyclic quadrilateral $ ABCD $ with circumcircle $ \omega $ is given. $ P $ is the intersection of the diagonals $ AC $ and $ BD $. $ X $ is the midpoint of the minor arc $ BC $ of $ \omega $. Points $ Y $ and $ Z $ lie on rays $ AX $ and $ DX $ respectively such that:
$$
2\angle AYB = \angle BXP, 2\angle DZC = \angle PXC.
$$
Prove that $ YZ \parallel AD $.

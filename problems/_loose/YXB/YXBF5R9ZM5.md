In a Mathematical Olympiad competition, students receive scores from four sections: Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, and Combinatorics. Any two students have different scores in all four sections. If a group of students can have their scores in at least two of the four sections simultaneously arranged in increasing order, then the group of students is called "good". Find the smallest positive integer $\mathrm{N}$, such that any $\mathrm{N}$ students will always contain 10 students who are good.

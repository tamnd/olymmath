a) Find the probability that exactly 25 sixes are on the surface of the cube.

b) Find the probability that at least one one is on the surface of the cube.

c) Find the expected value of the number of sixes facing outward.

d) Find the expected value of the sum of the numbers that ended up on the surface of the cube.

e) Find the expected value of the random variable: "The number of different digits that ended up on the surface of the cube."

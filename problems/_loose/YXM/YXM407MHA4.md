Let $D_{n}$ denote the set of all positive divisors of the positive integer $n$. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $D_{n}$ can be written as the union of two disjoint subsets $A$ and $G$, and satisfy: subsets $A$ and $G$ each contain at least three elements, the elements of subset $A$ can be arranged into an arithmetic sequence, and the elements of subset $G$ can be arranged into a geometric sequence.

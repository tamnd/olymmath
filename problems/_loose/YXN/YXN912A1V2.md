Two girls, Ere and Odette, play a game on a $3 \times 3$ chessboard using black and white pieces, with the following rules:
I. They take turns placing pieces.
II. Each turn, a piece is placed on an empty square of the chessboard.
III. When it is a player's turn, they can choose either a white or black piece, and they do not have to always use the same color.
IV. When the chessboard is full, if any row, column, or diagonal has an even number of black pieces, Ere scores 1 point, and if any row, column, or diagonal has an odd number of black pieces, Odette scores 1 point. V. A player must score at least 5 out of 8 points to win.
(1) Is a 4-4 draw possible? Explain.
(2) Describe the winning strategy for the girl who moves first.

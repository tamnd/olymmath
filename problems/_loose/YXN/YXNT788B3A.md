a) Show that it is not possible to place the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 on the edges of a tetrahedron (a three-sided pyramid) such that the sum of the three numbers on each face is the same.

b) Show that the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 10$ can be placed at the four vertices and six midpoints of the edges of a tetrahedron such that the sum of the six numbers at the vertices and midpoints of each face is the same.

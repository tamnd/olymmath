Lilavati. Here is a small puzzle borrowed from "Lilavati" (1150 AD) by Bhaskara

"A beautiful maiden with beaming eyes told me a number. If this number is multiplied by 3, $\frac{3}{4}$ of the product is added, the sum is divided by 7, $\frac{1}{3}$ of the quotient is subtracted, the result is multiplied by itself, 52 is subtracted, the square root is extracted, 8 is added, and the sum is divided by 10, the result is $2$."

With the right approach, solving this puzzle, like many other ancient riddles, is incredibly easy.

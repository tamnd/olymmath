At an international combinatorial conference, a hundred mathematicians are accommodated in a hotel where the rooms are numbered from one to a hundred. The receptionist plans to assign the mathematicians to the rooms corresponding to their arrival order. However, the first arriving guest is forgotten to be given the correct instruction, so they randomly choose one of the rooms. In the end, the receptionist instructs the rest to sequentially occupy the room corresponding to their arrival order; if that room is already occupied, they should randomly choose one of the available rooms. In how many ways can the guests be accommodated in the rooms?

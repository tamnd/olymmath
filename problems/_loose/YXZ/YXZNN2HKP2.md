Let $S$ be a non-empty finite set and $F$ be a family of subsets of $S$ such that
i) $\mathrm{F} \backslash\{S\} \neq \varnothing$,

ii) If $F_{1}, F_{2} \in \mathrm{F}$, then $F_{1} \cap F_{2} \in \mathrm{F}$ and $F_{1} \cup F_{2} \in \mathrm{F}$.

Prove that there exists $a \in S$ which belongs to at most half of the elements of F.

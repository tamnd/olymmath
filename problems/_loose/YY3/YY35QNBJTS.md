On the side $[AB]$ of the square $ABCD$, we construct the isosceles triangle $ABE$ outside such that $m(\angle ABE) = 120^\circ$. The foot of the perpendicular from point $B$ to the angle bisector of $\angle EAB$ is $M$, the foot of the perpendicular from point $M$ to the line $AB$ is $N$, and the intersection of the lines $CN$ and $MB$ is $P$. The centroid of triangle $ABE$ is $G$.

Prove that the lines $PG$ and $AE$ are parallel!

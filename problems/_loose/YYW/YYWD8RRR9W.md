a) On a $2012 \times 2012$ board consisting entirely of white squares, Alice and Bob play the following game: Alice starts by blackening the top-left square. Bob must then blacken a square that has not yet been blackened and is adjacent (i.e., shares a side) to the square Alice just blackened. Then it is Alice's turn to play, and she must blacken a white square adjacent to the square Bob just blackened, and so on. When a player can no longer play, they lose.

Determine which of the two players has a winning strategy.

If the real numbers $x, y, z \in [0, +\infty)$ satisfy the relations:

$$
\frac{x+y}{2} \leq \sqrt{x \cdot y} + 1 \text{ and } \frac{y+z}{2} \leq \sqrt{y \cdot z} + 4
$$

prove that $|\sqrt{z} - \sqrt{x}| \leq 3 \sqrt{2}$.

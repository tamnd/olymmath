Prove that in the equality

$$
N=\frac{N}{2}+\frac{N}{4}+\frac{N}{8}+\ldots+\frac{N}{2^{n}}+\ldots
$$

${ }^{\prime}(N$ - any positive integer) all fractions can be replaced by the nearest integers to them:

$$
N=\left(\frac{N}{2}\right)+\left(\frac{N}{4}\right)+\left(\frac{N}{8}\right)+\ldots+\left(\frac{N}{2^{n}}\right)+\ldots
$$

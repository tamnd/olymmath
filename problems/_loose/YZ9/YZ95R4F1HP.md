From a point $A$ lying outside a circle, two tangents $A T_{1}$ and $A T_{2}$ and a secant $B D$ ( $B$ and $D$ - points of intersection with the circle) are drawn. Prove that the points $A, B, C$ and $D$ form a harmonic set, that is,

$$
\frac{A B}{B C}=\frac{A D}{D \bar{C}}
$$

where point $C$ is the point of intersection of the chord $T_{1} T_{2}$ and the secant.

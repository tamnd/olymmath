Consider 300 collinear points. Three children color these points as follows. The first child colors the first three points green, then skips one, then colors the next three, and skips one again, and so on until the end of the 300 points. The second child performs a similar recoloring from the beginning, but with blue, coloring the first four points, then skipping one, and then the next four, and skipping one again, and so on. The third child recolors with brown, coloring five points at a time and skipping one, and then the next five, and so on. How many points remain uncolored at the end?

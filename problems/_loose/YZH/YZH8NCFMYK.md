Show that the centroid of a right circular cone can be determined as follows: If $C A B$ is the axial section of the cone and the points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen on the side edges $C A$ and $C B$ respectively, such that

$$
C P: P A=C Q: Q B=3: 2,
$$

then the intersection point of the lines $B P$ and $A Q$ gives the centroid.

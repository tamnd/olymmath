(Steiner's chain / porism)

Let $\omega$ and $\Omega$ be two circles. Suppose that the circle $\omega$ is included within the circle $\Omega$. Let $\omega_{0}$ be a circle that is internally tangent to $\Omega$ and externally tangent to $\omega$. We say that $\omega$ and $\Omega$ have the property $P_{n}$ for $\omega_{0}$ if there exist $n-1$ distinct circles $\omega_{i}$ for $i=1 \ldots n-1$ that are tangent to both $\omega$ and $\Omega$ such that $\omega_{i}$ and $\omega_{i+1}$ are tangent (with the convention that the indices are taken modulo $n$). Show that if $\omega$ and $\Omega$ have the property $P_{n}$ for $\omega_{0}$, then they have it for $\omega_{0}^{\prime}$, any circle tangent to $\omega$ and $\Omega$.

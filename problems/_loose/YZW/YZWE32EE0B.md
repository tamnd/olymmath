Let $A B C D$ be a trapezoid with $A B \| C D, A B>C D$. The bisector of angle $\widetilde{A B \bar{C}}$, the bisector of angle $\widetilde{B C D} \bar{D}$, and $A D$ are concurrent at point $M$.

a) Prove that triangle $MBC$ is a right triangle.

b) Prove that $M$ is the midpoint of $[A D]$.

c) Prove that the perpendicular from $A$ to $M B$, the perpendicular from $D$ to $M C$, and $\mathrm{BC}$ are concurrent.

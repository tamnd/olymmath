Let $a$ and $m$ be natural numbers and $x$ be an integer such that $m$ is a divisor of $a^{2} x-a$. Prove that there exists an integer $y$, such that $m$ is a divisor of the numbers $a^{2} y-a$ and $a y^{2}-y$.

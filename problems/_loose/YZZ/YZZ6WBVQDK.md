Let $O_{1}$ be the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $ABC$, and $O_{2}$ be the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $ABD$. The line $O_{1} O_{2}$ intersects $EB$ at $M$ and $EA$ at $N$.

Prove that triangle $EMN$ is isosceles.

The twenty-third question: Given a finite graph $\mathrm{G}$, let $\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{G})$ be the number of 3-complete graphs $\mathrm{K}_{3}$ in $\mathrm{G}$, and $\mathrm{g}(\mathrm{G})$ be the number of 4-complete graphs $\mathrm{K}_{4}$ in $\mathrm{G}$. Find the smallest constant $\mathrm{c}$ such that for every graph $\mathrm{G}$, we have $(\mathrm{g}(\mathrm{G}))^{3} \leq \mathrm{c} \cdot(\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{G}))^{4}$.

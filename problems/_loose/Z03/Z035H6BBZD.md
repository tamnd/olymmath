Four people play the following game. They shuffle a deck of 52 French cards, then the first player draws cards one by one without replacement until they draw an ace. Then the second player takes their turn and draws cards until they also draw an ace, followed by the third, and finally the fourth player. The game is won by the player who ends up with the most cards in their hand, or there is no winner if there is a tie for the lead. Which player has the advantage in this game?

Given a circle and a point $A$ outside it. The tangents from $A$ touch the circle at points $B$ and $C$. Draw a line through $B$ parallel to the line $A C$: this line intersects the circle at $D$. The intersection of the line $A D$ with the circle is $E$. Prove that the line $B E$ bisects the segment $A C$.

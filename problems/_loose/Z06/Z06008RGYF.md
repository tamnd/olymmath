(2002 Bulgarian Spring Mathematical Competition) Given the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, with $O$ as the origin. $A$ is an integer point, and the length of $O A$ is an integer power of an odd prime. Prove: Among the integer points on the circumference of the circle with $O A$ as its diameter, at least half of them satisfy that the distance between any two points is an integer.

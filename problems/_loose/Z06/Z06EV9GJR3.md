Let $f(x)$ be an integer-coefficient polynomial of at least the first degree, for which $f(x)>0$ holds when $x \geq 1$. Prove that in this case, the number

$$
y=0, f(1) f(2) f(3) \ldots
$$

in the decimal system is irrational. (For example, in the case of the polynomial $f(x)=x^{2}$, the number $y$ is as follows: $0,149162536 \ldots$. . )

Two circles are given. A line passing through the center of one of them intersects this circle at points $A$ and $C$, and the other circle at points $B$ and $D$. Prove that if $|A B|:|B C|=|A D|:|D C|$, then the circles are perpendicular, i.e., the angle between the tangents to them at their point of intersection is a right angle.

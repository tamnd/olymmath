The thirty-sixth question, In the equilateral $\triangle A B C$, $D$ is a point on $B C$, $\odot I$ and $\odot J$ are the incircles of $\triangle A B D$ and $\triangle A C D$ respectively. Construct another common tangent of $\odot I$ and $\odot J$ intersecting $A B$ and $A C$ at $M$ and $N$ respectively, and let point 0 be the circumcenter of $\triangle A M N$. Prove: $\mathrm{OI}=\mathrm{OJ}$.

Given an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$, construct a triangle $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ with sides $\sin \angle A, \sin \angle B, \sin \angle C$. With $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}, C^{\prime}$ as centers, draw circles with radii $\cos \angle A, \cos \angle B, \cos \angle C$ respectively. Then the three circles intersect at a point $H$, and $H$ is exactly the orthocenter of $\triangle A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$.

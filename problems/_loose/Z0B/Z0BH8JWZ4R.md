Epitaph (1538).

Two grandmothers with two granddaughters,
Two husbands and their two wives,
Two fathers with two daughters,
Two brothers and their two sisters,
Two mothers with two sons,
Two maidens with their two mothers,
Yet only six people in all
Have ended their days here. Stranger, hold back your wonder
And remember, they did not sin
With incest
Or illegitimate birth.

a) A rectangular plot of land is divided into two rectangular fields by a path. The length of the plot, measured parallel to this path, is $105 \mathrm{~m}$. The width of the first part of the field is $270 \mathrm{~m}$, and the width of the second part of the field is $180 \mathrm{~m}$. The path is $3 \mathrm{~m}$ wide.

Determine the area of the first part of the field and the area of the second part of the field!

b) The entire plot is now combined into one large field by plowing over the path.

Determine the area of the resulting large field!

c) Determine how many meters of wire are needed for an electric fence if the entire field is to be completely enclosed with two wires! The sag and securing of the wire should be accounted for by increasing the double perimeter by one hundredth.

(Round to the nearest meter.)

Hint for a) and b): The areas should be given in hectares, rounded to two decimal places.

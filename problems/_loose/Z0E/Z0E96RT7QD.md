a) Let $a \geq b \geq c \geq 0, d \geq 0$. Show that $\sqrt{a-b}+\sqrt{b-c}+\sqrt{c+d} \leq \sqrt{3(a+d)}$;

b) Let $a, b, c \in[0, \infty)$ with the property $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=1$. Show that

$$
\frac{\sqrt{a}}{1+\sqrt{3} b}+\frac{\sqrt{b}}{1+\sqrt{3} c}+\frac{\sqrt{c}}{1+\sqrt{3} a} \geq \frac{1}{6}
$$

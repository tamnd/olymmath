Let $a, b, c, d$ be four positive real numbers. Prove that
$$\frac{(a + b + c)^2}{a^2+b^2+c^2}+\frac{(b + c + d)^3}{b^3+c^3+d^3}+\frac{(c+d+a)^4}{c^4+d^4+a^4}+\frac{(d+a+b)^5}{d^5+a^5+b^5}\le 120$$

A rectangular board $6\times 9$ consists of $54$ uniform squares, in which of its squares can one checker be placed.
a) Is it possible to place the checkers in such a way that the number of checkers in any two squares $4\times 4$ is different?
b) Is it possible to place the checkers in such a way that the number of checkers in any two squares $5\times 5$ is different?

On a spherical Sun, a finite number of circular spots have been discovered, each occupying less than half of the Sun's surface. These spots are assumed to be closed (i.e., the boundary of the spot belongs to it) and do not intersect with each other. Prove that there will be two diametrically opposite points on the Sun that are not covered by the spots.

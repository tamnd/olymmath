Let $\triangle A B C$ be an acute triangle. Its circumcenter is denoted as $O$, and the circumcenter of $\triangle A C C^{\circ}$ is denoted as $T$. The midpoint of side $A C$ is denoted as $M$. Points $D$ and $E$ are taken on sides $A B$ and $B C$ respectively, such that $\angle B D M - \angle B E M = \angle A B C$. Prove that $B T \perp I E$.

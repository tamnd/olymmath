$$A = \{2, 4, \ldots, 1000\},$$
$$B = \{3, 6, \ldots, 999\},$$

$$D = \{7, 14, \ldots, 994\},$$
$$E = \{11, 22, \ldots, 990\},$$
$$\textrm{and } F = \{13, 26, \ldots, 988\}.$$

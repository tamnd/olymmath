For the square $D E F G$, it is true that point $D$ is a vertex of the rectangle, point $E$ lies on the segment $\overline{A B}$, point $F$ lies on the segment $\overline{B C}$, and $|\angle B E F|=30^{\circ}$. If the area of the square $D E F G$ is $36 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$, calculate the perimeter and the area of the intersection of the rectangle $A B C D$ and the square $D E F G$.

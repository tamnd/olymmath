In the plane of a parallelogram, a point $P$ is given, through which two lines parallel to the sides of the parallelogram are drawn, intersecting them at points $A$ and $C, B$ and $D$ respectively. Prove that the center $M$ of the parallelogram, point $P$, and point $S$ of intersection of the midlines of quadrilateral $A B C D$ lie on the same line, and that $M S=S P$.

Let $D$ be a point inside an acute-angled triangle $A B C$ such that $\angle B A D=\angle D B C$ and $\angle D A C=\angle B C D$. Let $P$ be a point on the circumcircle of the triangle $A D B$. Assume that $P$ is outside the triangle $A B C$. A straight line through $P$ intersects the ray $B A$ in $X$ and the ray $C A$ in $Y$ so that $\angle X P B=\angle P D B$. Show that $B Y$ and $C X$ intersect at $A D$.

For real, distinct, and non-zero numbers $x, y, z, u, v, w$ if $\frac{x}{u}+\frac{y}{v}+\frac{z}{w}=1$ and $\frac{u}{x}+\frac{v}{y}+\frac{w}{z}=0$, prove that $\frac{x^{2}}{u^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{v^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{w^{2}}=1$.

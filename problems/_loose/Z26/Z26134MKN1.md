Find the number of positive integer $ n < 3^8 $ satisfying the following condition.

"The number of positive integer $k (1 \leq k \leq \frac {n}{3})$ such that $ \frac{n!}{(n-3k)! \cdot k! \cdot 3^{k+1}} $ is not a integer" is $ 216 $.

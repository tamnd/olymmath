Let $\triangle A B C$ be a triangle. Prove that there exists a point $D$ on the side $A B$ such that $C D$ is the geometric mean of $A D$ and $B D$ if and only if
$$
\sqrt{\sin A \sin B} \leq \sin \frac{C}{2} .
$$

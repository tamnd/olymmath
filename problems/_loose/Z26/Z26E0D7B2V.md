The polynomial $P_{n}(x)$ is defined by the conditions

$$
P_{0}(x)=x^{3}-4 x \text { and } P_{n+1}(x)=P_{n}(1+x) P_{n}(1-x)-1
$$

Prove that the polynomial $P_{2016}(x)$ is divisible by the polynomial $x^{2016}$. (Dušan Đukić)

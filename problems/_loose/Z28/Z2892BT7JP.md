In one of the vertices of a cube, there are $N$ butterflies, and the other seven vertices are empty. Every minute, from one of the vertices of the cube, one butterfly flies to each of the three adjacent vertices along the edges of the cube, one to the opposite vertex (relative to the center) of the cube, and one flies away and does not return. Find all values of $N$ for which, after some time, the same number of butterflies can be in each vertex of the cube.

Given positive integers $m, n \geqslant 2, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ are integers, and none of them is a multiple of $m^{n-1}$. Prove: There exist integers $e_{1}, e_{2}, \cdots, e_{n}$, not all zero, such that $\left|e_{i}\right|<m(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} e_{i} a_{i}$ is a multiple of $m^{n}$.

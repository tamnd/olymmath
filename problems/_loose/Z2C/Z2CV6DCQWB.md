Test $\mathbf{K}$ Let $a, b, c$ denote the side lengths opposite to $\angle A, \angle B, \angle C$ in $\triangle A B C$. For any two points $P_{1}, P_{2}$ in the plane, the distances from $P_{1}, P_{2}$ to the vertices $A, B, C$ are $a_{1}, a_{2}$, $b_{1}, b_{2}, c_{1}, c_{2}$, respectively. Prove that:
$$
a a_{1} a_{2}+b b_{1} b_{2}+c c_{1} c_{2} \geqslant a b c .
$$

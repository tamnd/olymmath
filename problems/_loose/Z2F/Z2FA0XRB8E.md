Let $I, O, G, H$ be the incenter, circumcenter, centroid, and orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$, respectively. Let $BC=a, CA=b, AB=c$, $p=\frac{1}{2}(a+b+c)$, and $R, r$ be the radii of the circumcircle and incircle, respectively. Prove the following identities:
( I ) $a \cdot I A^{2}+b \cdot I B^{2}+c \cdot I C^{2}=a b c$;
( II ) $I O^{\dot{2}}=R^{2}-\frac{a b c}{a+b+c}=R^{2}-2 R r$;
(III) $I G^{2}=\frac{1}{18 p}\left[2 a\left(b^{2}+c^{2}\right)+2 b\left(c^{2}+a^{2}\right)+2 c\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)-\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)-9 a b c\right]$ $=\frac{2}{3} p^{2}-\frac{5}{18}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\right)-4 R r$
(IV) $I H^{2}=4 R^{2}-\frac{1}{2 p}\left(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+a b c\right)$

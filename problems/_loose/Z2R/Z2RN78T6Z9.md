A sequence of positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots$ is called "good" if and only if for any positive integer $n$, it satisfies the following two conditions:
(1) $a_{n!}=a_{1} a_{2} \cdots a_{n}$, for example,
$$
a_{24}=a_{4!}=a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} a_{4} \text {; }
$$
(2) $a_{n}$ is a positive integer raised to the power of $n$.

Find all good sequences.

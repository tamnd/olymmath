Let $a, b, c>0$, and $a+b+c=A \leqslant 1, \alpha>0$, then $\left(\frac{1}{a}-a\right)^{\alpha}+\left(\frac{1}{b}-b\right)^{\alpha}+\left(\frac{1}{c}-c\right)^{\alpha} \geqslant 3\left(\frac{3}{A}-\frac{A}{3}\right)^{\alpha}$.

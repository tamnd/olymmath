Let $ABC$ be a triangle that is neither right-angled nor isosceles. Denote by $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ the orthogonal projections of $A$ on $(BC)$, $B$ on $(CA)$, and $C$ on $(AB)$, respectively. Let $A_{2}$ be the point of intersection of the lines $(BC)$ and $(B_{1}C_{1})$, $B_{2}$ the point of intersection of the lines $(AC)$ and $(A_{1}C_{1})$, and $C_{2}$ the point of intersection of the lines $(AB)$ and $(A_{1}B_{1})$. Show that the points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$ are collinear.

Bonus question: Show that the line passing through $A_{2}, B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$ is perpendicular to $(OH)$, where $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of $ABC$ and $H$ is its orthocenter.

The sequence $a_{n}$ is defined by $a_{1}\geq 2$ and the recurrence formula
$$a_{n+1}=a_{n}\sqrt{\frac{a_{n}^3+2}{2(a_{n}^3+1)}}$$
for $n\geq 1$. Prove that for every integer $n$, the inequality $a_{n}>\sqrt{\frac{3}{n}}$ holds.

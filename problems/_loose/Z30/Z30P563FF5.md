The vertices of the triangle are denoted by the letters $A, B, C$ in a clockwise direction. The triangle is sequentially rotated clockwise: first around vertex $A$ by an angle equal to angle $A$, then around vertex $B$ by an angle equal to angle $B$, and so on in a cycle (each time the rotation is performed around the current position of the next vertex). Prove that after six rotations, the triangle will return to its original position.

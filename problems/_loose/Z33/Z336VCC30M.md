A circle is divided into three arcs by the points $X, Y$, and $Z$, with the central angles corresponding to these arcs being $60^{\circ}, 100^{\circ}$, and $200^{\circ}$.

If $A, B$, and $C$ are the vertices of a triangle, let $M_{A}$ and $M_{B}$ denote the intersection points of the altitudes from $A$ and $B$ with the circumcircle of the triangle, and $F_{C}$ denote the intersection point of the angle bisector at $C$ with the circumcircle.

Determine all acute triangles $ABC$ for which the points $M_{A}, M_{B}$, and $F_{C}$ coincide with the points $X, Y$, and $Z$ in some order.

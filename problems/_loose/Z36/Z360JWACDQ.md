In a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, $A C=B D=A B$, and $A C \perp B D$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $E$. Let $I$ be the incenter of $\triangle A E B$, and $M$ be the midpoint of side $A B$. Prove that $M I \perp C D$, and $M I=\frac{1}{2} C D$.

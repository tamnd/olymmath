For real numbers $x, y$, let $s(x, y)=\{s \mid s=[n x+y], n \in \mathbf{N}\}$. Prove that if $r>1$ is a rational number, then there exist real numbers $u, v$, such that $s(r, 0) \cap s(u, v)=\varnothing, s(r, 0) \cup s(u, v)=\mathbf{N}$.

David, Kevin, and Michael each choose an integer from the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, 100\}$ randomly, uniformly, and independently of each other. The probability that the positive difference between David's and Kevin's numbers is $\emph{strictly}$ less than that of Kevin's and Michael's numbers is $\frac mn$ , for coprime positive integers $m$ and $n$ . Find $100m + n$ .

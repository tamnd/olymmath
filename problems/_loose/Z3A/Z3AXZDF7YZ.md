(1) Find the equation of the ellipse;
(2) Connect $A P$ and let it intersect the ellipse at point $C$. Draw a vertical line from $C$ to the $x$-axis, intersecting the ellipse at another point $D$. Find the range of $S_{\triangle A B D}$.

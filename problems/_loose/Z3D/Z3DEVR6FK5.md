The younger sister asked the math student Gavrila to fix the swings in the yard. After the repair, they became a flat board (seat) rigidly attached to two parallel rods, the distance between which is $d$. The rods are mounted on a horizontal axis in a cylindrical hinge, meaning they can rotate relative to this axis. One of the rods turned out to be shorter than the other by $a$. At the same time, the normal to the seat lies in the same plane as the rods. The sister sat her favorite doll on the swings, which cannot hold onto the rods but is kept on the seat only by the force of dry friction with a coefficient $\mu$. At what angle can the swings be deflected from the vertical so that the doll does not slide off the seat?

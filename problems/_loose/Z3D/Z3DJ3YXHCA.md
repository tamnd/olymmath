Let $x, y, z$ all be positive numbers. Prove:
$$
\sum \sqrt{y z\left(y^{2}+z^{2}\right)} \geqslant 2 \sum y z \sqrt{\frac{x}{y+z}},
$$

where "$\sum$" denotes the cyclic sum.

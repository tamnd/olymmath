(2008 Anhui Province College Entrance Examination for Science) Let the sequence $\{f(n)\}$ satisfy $f(1)=0, f(n+1)=c f^{3}(n)+$ $1-c, n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$, where $c$ is a real number.
(1) Prove: $f(n) \in[0,1]$ for any $n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$ if and only if $c \in[0,1]$;
(2) Let $0<n+1-\frac{2}{1-3 c}, n \in \mathbf{N}^{+}$.

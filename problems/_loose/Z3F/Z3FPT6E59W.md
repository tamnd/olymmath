$* \star a, b$ are integers greater than 1, numbers of the form $a^{b}$ are called true hanging. Find all positive integers that can be expressed as the sum of a finite number of true hanging numbers.
IN. 10 mo 000
$$
\begin{array}{c}
t=t_{1}+t_{2}+\cdots t_{u}, 1 \leqslant t_{1}<t_{2}<\cdots<t_{u} \leqslant 2^{n-1}, \\
r=r_{1}+r_{2}+\cdots+r_{v}, 1 \leqslant r_{1}<r_{2}<\cdots<r_{v} \leqslant 2^{n-1},
\end{array}
$$

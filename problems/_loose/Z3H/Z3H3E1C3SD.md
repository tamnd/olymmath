Let $\triangle ABC$ be an equilateral triangle with side length $6.$ Three circles of radius $6$ are centered at $A,$ $B,$ and $C.$ Compute the radius of the circle that is centered at the center of $\triangle ABC,$ is internally tangent to these three circles, and lies in the interior of the three circles.

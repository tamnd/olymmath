Let $n$ be a positive integer and $X$ be a set with $n$ elements. Prove that

a) The number of all subsets of $X$ ( $X$ and $\emptyset$ included) is equal to $2^{n}$.

b) There exist $2^{n-1}$ subsets of $X$ each pair of which is with common element.

c) There do not exist $2^{n-1}+1$ subsets of $X$, each pair of which is with common element.

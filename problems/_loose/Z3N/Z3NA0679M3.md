Andrija and Boris have 2014 cards labeled with numbers from 1 to 2014. Andrija has all the cards with even numbers, and Boris has all the cards with odd numbers. Andrija has arranged his cards in a circle in order, from 2 to 2014, in a clockwise direction so that the numbers on the cards are not visible. Boris knows that the cards are arranged in this order and direction, but he does not know where the card with the number 2 is. After that, Boris places one of his cards on each of Andrija's cards, thus forming 1007 pairs of cards. For each pair, the numbers on the cards are compared, and one point is awarded to the player whose card has the higher number.

Determine the largest possible $N$ such that Boris can be sure to score at least $N$ points.

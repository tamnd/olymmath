Given that $F$ is the right focus of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{1+a^{2}}+y^{2}=1(a>0)$, points $M(m, 0), N(0, n)$ are points on the $x$-axis and $y$-axis respectively, and satisfy $\overrightarrow{M N} \cdot \overrightarrow{N F}=0$. If point $P$ satisfies $\overrightarrow{O M}=2 \overrightarrow{O N}+\overrightarrow{P O}$.
(1) Find the equation of the trajectory $C$ of point $P$;
(2) Suppose a line passing through point $F$ intersects the trajectory of point $P$ at points $A, B$. The lines $O A, O B$ intersect the line $y=-a$ at points $S, T$ (where $O$ is the origin), determine whether $\overrightarrow{F S} \cdot \overrightarrow{F T}$ is a constant value. If it is, find this constant value; if not, explain the reason.

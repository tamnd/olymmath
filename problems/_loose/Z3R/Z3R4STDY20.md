Let $a, b, c$ , and $d$ be positive real numbers such that $abcd = 1$ . Prove that
$$\frac{(a-1)(c+1)}{1+bc+c} + \frac{(b-1)(d+1)}{1+cd+d} + \frac{(c-1)(a+1)}{1+da+a} + \frac{(d-1)(b+1)}{1+ab+b} \geq 0.$$
*Proposed by Orif Ibrogimov, Uzbekistan.*

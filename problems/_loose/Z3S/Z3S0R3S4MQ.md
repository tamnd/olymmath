Given a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ which can be inscribed in a circle. Let $F$ be the intersecting point of diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ and $E$ be the intersecting point of the lines $A D$ and $B C$. If $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of $A B$ and $C D$, prove that

$$
\frac{M N}{E F}=\frac{1}{2} \cdot\left|\frac{A B}{C D}-\frac{C D}{A B}\right|
$$

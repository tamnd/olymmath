$48 A=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{2 k}, 1\right\}, k \geqslant 1, a_{i}$ are positive integers, $k=1,2, \cdots, 2 k+1$, prove that for any permutation of $A$:
$$
a_{1_{1}}, a_{i_{2}}, \cdots, a_{i_{2 k+1}}
$$

there is always
$$
\prod_{i=1}^{2 k+1}\left(a_{i}-a_{j}\right)
$$

an even number.

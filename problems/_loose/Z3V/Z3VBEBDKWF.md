A function is defined by $f(x)=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{1}{1+t^{2}}dt.$

(1) Find the equation of normal line at $x=1$ of $y=f(x).$

(2) Find the area of the figure surrounded by the normal line found in (1),
the $x$ axis and the graph of $y=f(x).$

Note that you may not use the formula $\int \frac{1}{1+x^{2}}dx=\tan^{-1}x+Const.$

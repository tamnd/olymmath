Let $k$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $\sqrt{5}$ and let $M$ and $N$ be points on a diameter of $k$ such that $M O=N O$. The chords $A B$ and $A C$, passing through $M$ and $N$, respectively, are such that

$$
\frac{1}{M B^{2}}+\frac{1}{N C^{2}}=\frac{3}{M N^{2}}
$$

Find the length of $M O$.

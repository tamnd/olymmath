Let $n$ be a positive integer. Answer the following questions.

(1) Find the maximum value of $f_n(x)=x^{n}e^{-x}$ for $x\geq 0$.

(2) Show that $\lim_{x\to\infty} f_n(x)=0$.

(3) Let $I_n=\int_0^x f_n(t)\ dt$. Find $\lim_{x\to\infty} I_n(x)$.

Given is an infinite sequence of different natural numbers. Each term of the sequence after the first is equal to either the arithmetic mean or the geometric mean of its two neighboring terms. Is it true that from a certain point onward, all terms of the sequence are equal only to the arithmetic mean of their neighbors, or to their geometric mean?

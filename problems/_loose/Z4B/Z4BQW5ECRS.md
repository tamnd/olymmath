After the failure of his first diabolical plan, the incorrigible François has a new idea. He managed to catch the 42 interns (one of them managed to escape) and subjects them to a new Machiavellian test.

He locks each of them in a cell (once the test begins, they can no longer communicate), and makes them pass through a small room containing a switch (whose initial position they do not know) with two positions: left and right (this is the only information they can leave in the room and therefore the only information they can transmit).

To be released, one of them must affirm that they have all already passed through the room and be correct; if they are wrong, they will be executed by a lethal injection of projective geometry.

They can consult before the start of the "game," what strategy can they adopt?

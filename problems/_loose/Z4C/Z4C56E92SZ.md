We say that $(a,b,c)$ form a *fantastic triplet* if $a,b,c$ are positive integers, $a,b,c$ form a geometric sequence, and $a,b+1,c$ form an arithmetic sequence. For example, $(2,4,8)$ and $(8,12,18)$ are fantastic triplets. Prove that there exist infinitely many fantastic triplets.

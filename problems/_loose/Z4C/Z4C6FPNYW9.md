Prove that among quadrilaterals with given lengths of diagonals and the angle between them, the one with the smallest perimeter is a parallelogram.

$3 *$. a) Each side of the quadrilateral was extended by its length in the process of traversing clockwise. It turned out that the ends of the constructed segments serve as the vertices of a square. Prove that the original quadrilateral is a square.

b) Prove that if the same procedure results in a regular $n$-gon from an $n$-gon, then the original $n$-gon is also regular.

Given two acute-angled triangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ with the points $O$ and $O'$ inside. Three pairs of the perpendiculars are drawn: $[OA_1]$ to the side $[BC]$, $[O'A'_1]$ to the side $[B'C']$, $[OB_1]$ to the side $[AC]$, $[O'B'_1]$ to the side $[A'C']$, $[OC_1] $ to the side $[AB]$, $[O'C'_1]$ to the side $[A'B']$;
it is known that $[OA_1]$ is parallel to the $[O'A']$, $[OB_1]$ is parallel to the $[O'B']$, $[OC_1]$ is parallel to the $[O'C']$
and the following products are equal: $ |OA_1|\cdot|O'A'| = |OB_1|\cdot |O'B'| = |OC_1|\cdot |O'C'|$.
Prove that $[O'A'_1]$ is parallel to the $[OA]$, $[O'B'_1]$ is parallel to the $[OB], [O'C'_1]$ is parallel to the $[OC]$
and $|O'A'_1|\cdot|OA| = |O'B'_1|\cdot|OB| = |O'C'_1|\cdot|OC|$.

A point $D$ has been chosen in the interior of the side $B C$ of an acute triangle $A B C$, and another point $P$ in the interior of the segment $A D$ but not lying on the median from the vertex $C$. The line containing this median intersects the circumcircle of the triangle $C P D$ at a point which we denote by $K(K \neq C)$. Show that the circumcircle of the triangle AKP passes in addition to A through another fixed point which does not depend on the choice of the points $D$ and $P$.

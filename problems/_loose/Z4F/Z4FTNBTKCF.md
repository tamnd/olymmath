From a point lying inside a convex $n$-gon, rays are drawn perpendicular to its sides and intersecting the sides (or their extensions). On these rays, vectors $\boldsymbol{a}_{1}, \ldots, \boldsymbol{a}_{n}$ are laid out, the lengths of which are equal to the lengths of the corresponding sides. Prove that $\boldsymbol{a}_{1}+\ldots+\boldsymbol{a}_{n}=\mathbf{0}$.

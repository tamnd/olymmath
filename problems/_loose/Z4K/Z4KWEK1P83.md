A 'stepped' shape, such as the example shown, is made from $1 \times 1$ squares in the following way.
(i) There are no gaps or overlaps.
(ii) There are an odd number of squares in the bottom row (eleven in the example shown).
(iii) In every row apart from the bottom one, there are two fewer squares than in the row immediately below.
(iv) In every row apart from the bottom one, each square touches two squares in the row immediately below.
(v) There is one square in the top row.

Prove that $36 A=(P+2)^{2}$, where $A$ is the area of the shape and $P$ is the length of its perimeter.

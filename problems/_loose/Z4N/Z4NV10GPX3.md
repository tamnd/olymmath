If a disk is divided into an inner and an outer circle, each equally divided into 10 "cells," and the numbers $1,2,3,4, \cdots, 10$ are filled into the ten cells of both the inner and outer circles (one number per cell, not necessarily in order), and if the inner circle can rotate around the center, prove that at some point during the rotation, the sum of the products of each pair of numbers from the inner and outer circles will be greater than 302.

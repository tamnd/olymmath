In triangle $A B C$ with sides $A B=c, A C=b, B C=a$, let $P$ be the intersection of the angle bisector $C E$ and the median $B D, E \in(A B), D \in(A C)$.

Determine the numbers $x$ and $y$ such that $\overrightarrow{P A}=x \cdot \overrightarrow{P B}+y \cdot \overrightarrow{P C}$.

Let $P$ be any point inside $\triangle A B C$, and let the areas of $\triangle A B C, \triangle B P C, \triangle C P A, \triangle A P B$ be $S, S_{a}, S_{b}, S_{c}$ respectively, and $R$ be the circumradius of $\triangle A B C$. Prove:
$$
\frac{S_{a}}{P A^{2}}+\frac{S_{b}}{P B^{2}}+\frac{S_{c}}{P C^{2}} \geqslant \frac{S}{R^{2}} .
$$

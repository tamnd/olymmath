In the sequence $20,18,2,20,-18, \ldots$ the first two terms $a_{1}$ and $a_{2}$ are 20 and 18 respectively. The third term is found by subtracting the second from the first, $a_{3}=a_{1}-a_{2}$. The fourth is the sum of the two preceding elements, $a_{4}=a_{2}+a_{3}$. Then $a_{5}=a_{3}-a_{4}$, $a_{6}=a_{4}+a_{5}$, and so on.
What is the sum of the first 2018 terms of this sequence?

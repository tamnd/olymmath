a) Prove that the polynomial $P_{n}(x)=(x+1)^{n}-x^{n}-1$ is divisible by $x^{2}+x+1$ if and only if $n=6 k \pm 1$.

b) Prove that $P_{n}(x)$ is divisible by $\left(x^{2}+x+1\right)^{2}$ if and only if $n=6 k+1$.

c) Prove that $P_{n}(x)$ is not divisible by $\left(x^{2}+x+1\right)^{3}$.

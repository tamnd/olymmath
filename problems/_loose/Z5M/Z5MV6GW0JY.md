A line intersects the sides $A B, B C$, and $C A$ of a triangle (or their extensions) at points $C_{1}, B_{1}$, and $A_{1} ; O, O_{a}, O_{b}$, and $O_{c}$ are the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $A B C, A B_{1} C_{1}, A_{1} B C_{1}$, and $A_{1} B_{1} C$; $H, H_{a}, H_{b}$, and $H_{c}$ are the orthocenters of these triangles. Prove that:

a) $\triangle O_{a} O_{b} O_{c} \sim \triangle A B C$.

b) the perpendicular bisectors of segments $O H, O_{a} H_{a}, O_{b} H_{b}$, and $O_{c} H_{c}$ intersect at one point.

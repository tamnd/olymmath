On the table, there are three cones standing on their bases, touching each other. The radii of their bases are 32, 48, and 48, and the vertex angles are $\frac{\pi}{3}$, $\frac{2 \pi}{3}$, and $\frac{2 \pi}{3}$ respectively (the vertex angle of a cone is the angle between its generators in the axial section). A sphere is suspended above the table, touching all the cones. It turns out that the center of the sphere is equidistant from the centers of the bases of all the cones. Find the radius of the sphere.

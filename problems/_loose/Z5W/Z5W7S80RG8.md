For quadrilateral $A B C D$, it holds that $\angle A=\angle C=90^{\circ}$. Let $E$ be a point inside the quadrilateral. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $B E$. Prove that $\angle A D B=\angle E D C$ if and only if $|M A|=|M C|$.

Glafira took another identical siren and positioned herself at the beginning of the bridge on the same shore. Gavrila got into a motorboat, which was located on the shore halfway between the first siren and the beginning of the bridge. It turned out that the sound signals from both sirens reached Gavrila simultaneously. Determine the distance from the starting point where Gavrila will be when he is 40 m away from the shore. Round your answer to the nearest whole number of meters. The riverbank is straight, and the current at each point is directed along the shore.

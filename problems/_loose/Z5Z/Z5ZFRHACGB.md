Numbers $0,1,2,\dots,9$ are placed left to right into the squares of the first row of $10 \times 10$ chessboard. Similarly, $10,11,\dots,19$ are placed into the second row, and so on. We are changing signs of exactly five numbers into the squares of each row and each column. What is the minimum value of the sum of the numbers on the chessboard?

$
\textbf{(A)}\ -10
\textbf{(B)}\ -2
\textbf{(C)}\ 2
\textbf{(D)}\ 10
\textbf{(E)}\ \text{None of the above}
$

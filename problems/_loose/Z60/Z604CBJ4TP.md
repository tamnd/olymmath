Let $ g(k) $ be the greatest prime divisor of the integer $ k $, when $ |k|\geq 2 $, and let $ g(-1) = g(0) = g(1) = 1 $. Determine whether there exists a polynomial $ W $ of positive degree with integer coefficients, for which the set of numbers of the form $ g(W(x)) $ ($ x $ - integer) is finite.

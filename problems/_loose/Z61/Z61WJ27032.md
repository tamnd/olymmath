Let $\mathrm{n}$ be a positive integer, and let $\mathrm{h}(\mathrm{x})=\sum_{\mathrm{k}=0}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{k}} \mathrm{x}^{k}\left(\mathrm{c}_{\mathrm{n}} \neq 0\right)$ be an $\mathrm{n}$-degree polynomial with complex coefficients whose roots all have positive imaginary parts. Let $f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n} \operatorname{Re}\left(c_{k}\right) x^{k}, g(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n} \operatorname{Im}\left(c_{k}\right) x^{k}$. Prove: $f(x) \cdot g(x)$ has all real roots.

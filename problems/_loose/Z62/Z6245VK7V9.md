Let $K_{l}$ be a semicircle with radius $r_{1}$, $K_{2}$ be a circle with radius $r_{2}=0.5 r_{1}$, which touches the diameter and the periphery of $K_{l}$, and $K_{3}$ be a circle with radius $r_{3}$, which touches both the diameter and the periphery of $K_{l}$ as well as the periphery of $K_{2}$.

It is to be proven that under these conditions, for $r_{3}$ it holds that $4 r_{3}=r_{1}$!

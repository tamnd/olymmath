In the distant year 2700, robot Waly needs battery packs to provide energy every day. The store has three types of battery packs, priced at 2 yuan, 3 yuan, and 5 yuan. Waly spends exactly the amount of money equal to the date of the day on battery packs, and he prioritizes buying the more expensive ones. For example, if today is the 12th, he will spend 12 yuan to buy 2 packs of 5 yuan and 1 pack of 2 yuan. On the 1st of every month, he rests and does not need to buy battery packs. How many 5-yuan battery packs did he buy in March of that year?

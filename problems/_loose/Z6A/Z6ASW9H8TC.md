Two planes are given by the equations $A 1 x+B 1 y+C 1 z+D 1=0$ and $A 2 x+B 2 y+C 2 z+D 2=0$. Let $\alpha-$ be the magnitude of the non-obtuse angle formed by the planes. Prove that

$$
\cos \alpha=\frac{\left|A_{1} A_{2}+B_{1} B_{2}+C_{1} C_{2}\right|}{\sqrt{A_{1}^{2}+B_{1}^{2}+C_{1}^{2}} \cdot \sqrt{A_{2}^{2}+B_{2}^{2}+C_{2}^{2}}}
$$

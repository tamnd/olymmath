$a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$ are positive integers with the following properties: 1) the numbers are distinct, 2) none of the numbers is a perfect square, 3) a triangle can be constructed with the square roots of these numbers as side lengths, and the area of this triangle is an integer. - Find a procedure by which any number of essentially different such triples can be formed. Two triples are considered essentially different if none of the numbers in one can be obtained by multiplying the numbers in the other by the same number.

A cylinder is circumscribed around a sphere of radius $R$. Point $P$ is located inside the cylinder on its axis and is $\frac{3}{4} R$ away from the bottom base. Through this point, a plane $\alpha$ is drawn, which has only one common point with the circle of the base. A cone is inscribed in the sphere, with its base lying in the plane $\alpha$ and its vertex located above this plane. Find the volume of the cone.

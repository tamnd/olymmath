The vertices of a convex polygon with $n\geqslant 4$ sides are coloured with black and white. A diagonal is called *multicoloured* if its vertices have different colours. A colouring of the vertices is *good* if the polygon can be partitioned into triangles by using only multicoloured diagonals which do not intersect in the interior of the polygon. Find the number of good colourings.

*Proposed by S. Berlov*

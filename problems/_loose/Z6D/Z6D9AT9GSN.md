Determine the real number $a$ so that $x=\frac{1}{2}$ is a solution to the equation

$$
\left(\frac{x+1}{1-x}-\frac{1-x}{x+1}-\frac{4 x^{2}}{x^{2}-1}\right)\left(\frac{1}{a^{3}+a^{2}}-\frac{1-a}{a^{2}}-1\right)=\frac{1}{2}
$$

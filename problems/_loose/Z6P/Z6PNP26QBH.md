Suppose $A, B \in \mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ are such that $A + B = AB + BA$. Prove that:
a) if $n$ is odd, then $\det(AB - BA) = 0$;
b) if $\det(A) \ne \det(B)$, then $\det(AB - BA) = 0$.

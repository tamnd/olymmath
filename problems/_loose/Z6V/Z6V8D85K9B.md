From a point $A$ located outside a circle, two tangents $A M$ and $A N$ ($M$ and $N$ - points of tangency) and a secant intersecting the circle at points $K$ and $L$ are drawn. Draw an arbitrary line $l$ parallel to $A M$. Let $K M$ and $L M$ intersect $l$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Prove that the line $M N$ bisects the segment $P Q$.

Let $a<b$ be real numbers and $f,g:[a,b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be two functions with the properties:

- The function $f$ has antiderivatives and it is bounded above;

- For any $x,y \in [a,b],$ $x<y$ we have $g(y)-g(x) \ge (y-x) \sup_{t \in [x,y]} f(t).$

Prove that $g$ is integrable.

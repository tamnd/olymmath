Inside a side of the equilateral triangle $A B C$, a point $S$ is given, through which lines $S A_{1}, S B_{1}, S C_{1}$ are drawn, parallel to the sides $A C, A B, B C$ of triangle $A B C$ respectively, such that $A_{1} \cdot B_{1} \cdot C_{1}$ lie on the sides $B C, A C, A B$ respectively. Prove that the sum $S A_{1}+S B_{1}+S C_{1}$ has a constant value that does not depend on the choice of point $S$.

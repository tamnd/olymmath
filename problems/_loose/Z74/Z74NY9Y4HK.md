Let $a$ and $b$ be complex non-zero numbers and $z_1,z_2$ the roots of the polynomials $X^2+aX+b$ . Show that $|z_1+z_2|=|z_1|+|z_2|$ if and only if there exists a real number $\lambda\ge 4$ such that $a^2=\lambda b$ .

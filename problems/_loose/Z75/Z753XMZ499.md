Show using the greedy algorithm that the chromatic number $\chi(G)$ of a finite graph $G$ satisfies

$$
x(G) \leqslant D(G)+1
$$

where $D(G)$ is the maximum degree of a vertex in $G$.

Remark 1.5. It is important to note that the greedy algorithm does not necessarily compute the chromatic number of a graph: it proposes a certain proper coloring, which is not necessarily minimal.

Let $n$ be a given positive integer, $n \geqslant 2, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n} \in(0,1)$. Find
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt[6]{a_{i}\left(1-a_{i+1}\right)}
$$

the maximum value, where $a_{n+1}=a_{1}$.

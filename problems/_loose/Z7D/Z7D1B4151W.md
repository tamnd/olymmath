a) A prize of 1485 DM is to be divided among three participants in the ratio $2: 3: 4$.

How much does each receive?

b) In another game, one participant receives one-fifth of the prize money, which is 150 DM. The remainder is to be divided between the other two participants in the ratio $5: 7$.

How much does each of them receive?

c) In a third game, it was agreed to distribute the prize in proportion to the stakes with which the participants had joined the competition. The total of these stakes of the three participants Anke, Bertram, and Claus was 44 DM, and furthermore: If Anke had bet 6 DM more and Claus had bet twice his stake, all three would have achieved the same winning claim.

How large were the three stakes?

Let the two foci of the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ be $F_{1}, F_{2}$, then there exists a point $P$ on the ellipse such that $\angle F_{1} P F_{2}=\theta(0<\theta<\pi)$ if and only if $\sin \frac{\theta}{2} \leqslant e$ (where $e$ is the eccentricity of the ellipse).

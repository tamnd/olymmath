Show that there are infinitely many positive integers $L$ for which every term of the sequence

$$
a_{0}=0, a_{n+1}=\frac{1}{L-a_{n}} (n=0,1,2, \ldots)
$$

can be written as the quotient of two Fibonacci numbers.

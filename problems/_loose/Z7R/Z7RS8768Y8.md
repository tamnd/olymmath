Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle and $k$ be the circumcircle around it. The point $O$ inside the triangle is such that $\overline{CE} = \overline{CF}$, where $E$ and $F$ are points on $k$ and $E$ lies on $AO$, while $F$ lies on $BO$. Prove that $O$ lies on the angle bisector of the angle at vertex $C$ if and only if the triangle is isosceles with base $\overline{AB}$.

On the coordinate plane, if the coordinates $x_{0}, y_{0}$ of a point $\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ are both integers, then the point is called an integer point. Try to prove: on the coordinate plane, there does not exist a regular $n$-gon ($n \geqslant 7$) whose all vertices are integer points.

Given polynomials $f(x)=x+a_{2} x^{2}+a_{3} x^{3}+\ldots+a_{n} x^{n}$ and $g(x)=x+b_{2} x^{2}+b_{3} x^{3}+\ldots+b_{n} x^{n}$. It turns out that the polynomial $g(f(x))-x$ is divisible by $x^{n+1}$, and the numbers $k!a_{k}$ are integers for $k=2,3, \ldots, n$. Prove that the numbers $k!b_{k}$ are integers for $k=2,3, \ldots, n$.

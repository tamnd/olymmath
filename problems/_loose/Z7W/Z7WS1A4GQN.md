Given an acute triangle $\triangle A B C$ inscribed in $\odot O$, there is a point $Q$ on the circumcircle of $\triangle B O C$ such that $O Q$ is its diameter. Point $M$ lies on the extension of $Q C$, and there is a point $N$ on segment $B C$ such that quadrilateral $A N C M$ is a parallelogram. Prove: The intersection of line $A Q$ and $M N$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle B O C$.

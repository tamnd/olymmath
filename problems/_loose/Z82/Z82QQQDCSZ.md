We understand the height of a pentagon as the distance from a vertex to the opposite side. Let $P$ be a pentagon where each angle is $108^{\circ}$ and each height is different. Show that the vertices of $P$ can be numbered in some direction consecutively so that for the corresponding heights,

$$
m_{1}>m_{3}>m_{4}>m_{5}>m_{2}
$$

holds.

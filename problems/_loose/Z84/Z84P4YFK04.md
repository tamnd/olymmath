On the sides of a convex quadrilateral, rhombi are constructed outwardly, with each having an acute angle of $\alpha$. Moreover, the angles of two rhombi adjacent to one vertex of the quadrilateral are equal. Prove that the segments connecting the centers of opposite rhombi are equal, and the acute angle between these segments is $\alpha$.

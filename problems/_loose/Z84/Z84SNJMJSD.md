(Jury, SRR, 77). Let numbers $a_{i}, \ldots, a_{n}, a_{n+1} \in \mathbf{N}$ be given, and for each value $i=1, \ldots, n$ the numbers $a_{i}$ and $a_{n+1}$ are coprime. Prove that the equation

$$
x_{1}^{a_{1}}+x_{2}^{a_{8}}+\ldots+x_{n}^{a_{n}}=x_{n+1}^{a_{n+1}}
$$

has infinitely many solutions in natural numbers.

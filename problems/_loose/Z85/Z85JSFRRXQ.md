Point, $P_{0}\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ is a fixed point, $x \cos w+y \sin w-P=0$ is any line passing through $P_{0}$.

Prove that $P_{0}$ is the centroid of $\triangle P_{1} P_{2} P_{3}$ if and only if $\delta_{1}+\delta_{2}+\delta_{3}=0$, where $\delta_{i}=$ $x_{i} \cos w+y_{i} \sin w-P(i=1,2,3)$.

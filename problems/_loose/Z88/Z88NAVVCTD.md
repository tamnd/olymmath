1.4 .9 ** Let the function $f(x)$ be a monotonic function on $\mathbf{R}$, satisfying: for any $x, y \in \mathbf{R}$, we have $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ and $f(1)=2$. For what value of the real number $k$ does there exist $t>2$, such that
$$
f\left(k \log _{2} t\right)+f\left(\left(\log _{2} t\right)^{2}-\log _{2} t-2\right)<0 ?
$$

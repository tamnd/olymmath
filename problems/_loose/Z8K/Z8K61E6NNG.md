The 56th question: Consider a permutation $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right)$ of $\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$, and define an operation as: arbitrarily selecting $a_{i}$ and $a_{j}$ to swap their positions. How many operations are needed at least, in the worst case, to transform $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right)$ into $(1,2, \ldots, n)$?

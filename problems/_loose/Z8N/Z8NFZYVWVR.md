a) The sum of the lengths of the edges of any convex polyhedron is greater than three times the diameter. Prove this. (The diameter of a polyhedron is defined as the greatest length of all possible segments with endpoints at the vertices of the polyhedron.)

b) For any two vertices $ A $ and $ B $ of any convex polyhedron, there exist three broken lines, each of which goes from $ A $ to $ B $ along the edges of the polyhedron, and no two of which pass through the same edge. Prove this.

c) If two edges are cut in a convex polyhedron, then for any two of its vertices $ A $ and $ B $, there exists a broken line connecting these two vertices, which goes along the remaining edges. Prove this.

d) Prove that in problem b), it is possible to choose three broken lines, no two of which have common vertices, except for the points $ A $ and $ B $.

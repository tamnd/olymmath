Suppose there exist $2m$ integers $i_1, i_2, \ldots, i_m$ and $j_1, j_2, \ldots, j_m$, of values in $\{1,2,\ldots,1000\}$. These integers are not necessarily distinct.
For any non-negative real numbers $a_1,a_2, \ldots, a_{1000}$ satisfying $a_1+a_2+\cdots +a_{1000}=1$, find the maximum positive integer $m$ for which the following inequality holds
$$a_{i_1}a_{j_1}+a_{i_2}a_{j_2} +\cdots +a_{i_m}a_{j_m}\leq \frac{1}{2.014}.$$

Let $A B C$ be a scalene triangle inscribed in circle $\Gamma$. The internal bisector of $\angle A$ meets $\overline{B C}$ and circle $\Gamma$ at points $D$ and $E$. The circle with diameter $\overline{D E}$ meets $\Gamma$ at a second point $F$. Prove that $\frac{A B}{A C}=\frac{F B}{F C}$.

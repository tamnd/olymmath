A cubic trinomial $x^3 + px + q$ with integer coefficients $p$ and $q$ is said to be *irrational * if it has three pairwise distinct real irrational roots $a_1,a_2, a_3$
Find all irrational cubic trinomials for which the value of $|a_1| + [a_2| + |a_3|$ is the minimal possible.

(E. Barabanov)

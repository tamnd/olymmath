In a country, some cities are connected by one-way flights (there is at most one flight between any two cities). If city $B$ can directly reach city $A$ or reach $A$ with a layover, then $B$ is said to be able to reach $A$. In particular, $A$ can reach $A$. It is known that for any two cities $P$ and $Q$, there exists a city $R$ such that $R$ can reach $P$ and $Q$. Prove: There exists a city $C$ such that $C$ can reach every city.

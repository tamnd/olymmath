Given an infinite sequence of numbers $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$ It is periodic with a period of 100, that is, $a_{1}=a_{101}, a_{2}=$ $a_{102}, \ldots$ It is known that $a_{1} \geq 0, a_{1}+a_{2} \leq 0, a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3} \geq 0$ and generally, the sum $a_{1}+a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}$ is non-negative for odd $n$ and non-positive for even $n$. Prove that $\left|a_{99}\right| \geq\left|a_{100}\right|$.

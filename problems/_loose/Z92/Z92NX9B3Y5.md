At a certain reception, a certain number of people are present, who either know each other or do not know each other (there is at least one acquaintance). For any two people who know the same number of people, it is true that they do not have any common acquaintances. Prove that there is a person at the reception who knows only one other person. (Acquaintance is a symmetric relation.)

Determine all values of parameter $\alpha\in [0,2\pi]$ such that the equation $$ (2\cos\alpha-1)x^2+4x+4\cos\alpha+2=0 $$ has
1) a positive root $x_1$ ,
2) if a second root $x_2$ exists and if $x_2\neq x_1$ , the $x_2\leq 0$ .

Let $n$ be an integer greater than 1 and let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be real numbers such that
$$
\left|x_{1}\right|+\left|x_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|x_{n}\right|=1 \text { and } x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}=0 .
$$

Prove that
$$
\left|\frac{x_{1}}{1}+\frac{x_{2}}{2}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n}}{n}\right| \leq \frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{n}\right) .
$$

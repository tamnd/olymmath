Let VABCD and SABCD be two quadrilateral pyramids with the same base ABCD and with vertices on opposite sides of the plane (ABC). On the 12 edges and 6 vertices of the resulting body, the natural numbers from 1 to 18 are inscribed, one in the middle of each edge and one at each vertex. Is it possible that on each edge of the new body, the number inscribed in the middle of the edge is the arithmetic mean of the numbers inscribed at the endpoints of the respective edge?

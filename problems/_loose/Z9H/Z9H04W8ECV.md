Given the sides of the convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ (in order), and the angle $\varphi$ between the lines containing the sides $A D$ and $C B$, within which the quadrilateral lies. We draw the (internal) angle bisectors, the bisectors at $A$ and $B$ intersect at $M$, and the bisectors at $C$ and $D$ intersect at $N$. What is the distance $M N$? Solve the problem generally, and then with the following data: $A B=70, B C=100, C D=35, D A=75$ units, and the projection of $B C$ onto the line $A D$ is 96 units.

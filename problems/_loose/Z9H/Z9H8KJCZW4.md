If $E, F$ are points on the sides $AB, CD$ of quadrilateral $ABCD$, and $\frac{AE}{EB}=\frac{DF}{FC}=\frac{m}{n}, AD=$ $b, BC=a, AD$ and $BC$ form an angle $\theta$, then $(m+n)^{2} EF^{2}=(a m)^{2}+(b n)^{2}+2 a m \cdot b n \cdot \cos \theta$.

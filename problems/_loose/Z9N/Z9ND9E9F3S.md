Prove that there are infinitely many $n=3 m$, such that the set $X=\{1,2, \cdots, n\}$ has a partition
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{m}\right\}, \\
B=\left\{b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{m}\right\}, \\
C=\left\{c_{1}, c_{2}, \cdots, c_{m}\right\},
\end{array}
$$
satisfying:
$$
a_{i}+b_{1}=c_{i} (i=1,2, \cdots, m) .
$$

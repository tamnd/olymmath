Prove that if $P_{n} / Q_{n} (n \geq 1)$ is a convergent fraction to the number $\alpha$, then at least one of the inequalities $\left|\alpha-\frac{P_{n}}{Q_{n}}\right|<\frac{1}{2 Q_{n}^{2}}$ or $\left|\alpha-\frac{P_{n-1}}{Q_{n-1}}\right|<\frac{1}{2 Q_{n-1}^{2}}$ holds. From this, derive Vahlen's theorem: for any $\alpha$, there are infinitely many fractions $p^{p / q}$ such that $|\alpha-p / q|<1 / 2 q^{2}$.

For the points $C$ on the circle with diameter $AB$, the following statement is true:

"The intersection of the line $AC$ and the tangent at $B$ is $D$; the intersection of the line $BC$ and the line through $D$ parallel to $AB$ is $E$, and finally, the intersection of the line through $E$ perpendicular to $AB$ and the circle (one of the points) is $F$. Then the perpendicular bisector of the segment $DF$ passes through $B$."

Consider the triangles $ABC$ and $DBC$ in different planes. Let points $E, F, P, M$ be such that $P$ is the midpoint of $[AB]$, $F$ is the midpoint of $[DC]$, and $M \in [AC]$, $E \in [BD]$ and satisfy the relation $\frac{AM}{AC} = \frac{DE}{DB} = \frac{1}{3}$. If $EF \cap (ABC) = \{G\}$, $MP \cap (BCD) = \{N\}$, and the distances from points $D$ and $A$ to the line $BC$ are equal, show that $A_{\triangle AMP} = \frac{1}{18} \cdot A_{\triangle DNG}$.

Calculate the volumes of bodies bounded by the surfaces:

a) the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$ and the planes $z=0, x+y+z=2$;

b) the planes $x+y+z=2, 3x+y=2, 3x+2y=4, z=0$, $y=0$;

c) the elliptic paraboloid $z=\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}$ and the planes $x=-1, x=1, y=-1, y=1$;

d) the sphere $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=2R^{2}$, the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=R^{2}$, and the plane $z=0$;

e) the cone $x^{2}+y^{2}=z^{2}$, the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=2y$, and the plane $z=0$.

Given an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ with unequal side lengths, $O$ and $H$ are the circumcenter and orthocenter, respectively. $BE$ and $CF$ are altitudes, and the second intersection point of $AH$ with the circumcircle $\odot O$ is $D$. If the midpoint of $AH$ is $I$, and the lines $EI$ and $FI$ intersect $BD$ and $CD$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively, prove that $MN \perp OH$. ${ }^{[6]}$
(2017, Vietnam Mathematical Olympiad)

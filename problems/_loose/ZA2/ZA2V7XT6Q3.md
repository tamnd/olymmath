Let $n$ be a fixed natural number.
Find all $n$ tuples of natural pairwise distinct and coprime numbers like $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ such that for $1\leq i\leq n$ we have
$$ a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_n|a_1^i+a_2^i+\ldots+a_n^i $$

Two concentric circular disks are placed one above the other, each divided into $n$ congruent sectors. The sectors of the upper disk are filled with real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$, and the sectors of the lower disk are filled with real numbers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$, satisfying: $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}>0, b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}>0$. Prove that it is possible to rotate the sectors to an appropriate position, such that the sum of the products of the numbers in the overlapping sectors is positive.

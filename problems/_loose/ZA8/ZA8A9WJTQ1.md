Let $ABCDT$ be a pyramid with base $ABCD$ ($A$ and $C$ are not adjacent vertices), whose diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $O$, which is the foot of the perpendicular from the apex $T$ to the base. Let $P, Q, R$, and $S$ be the midpoints of segments $AB, BC, CD$, and $DA$, respectively. Four planes are drawn through segments $PQ, QR, RS$, and $SP$, perpendicular to the base of the pyramid, cutting off four triangular pyramids which are then unfolded. Prove that the area of the remaining part of the pyramid is equal to the total area of the four unfolded pyramids.

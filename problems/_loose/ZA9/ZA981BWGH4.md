Once, a team of Knights and a team of Liars met in the park and decided to ride the circular carousel, which can accommodate 40 people (the "Chain" carousel, where everyone sits one behind the other). When they sat down, each person saw two others, one in front of them and one behind them, and said: "At least one of the people sitting in front of me or behind me belongs to my team." One seat remained empty, and they invited another Liar. This Liar said: "Together with me, we can arrange ourselves on the carousel so that this rule is satisfied again." How many people were in the team of Knights? (A Knight always tells the truth, a Liar always lies.)

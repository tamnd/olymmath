17-183 If the three sides of $\triangle A B C$ are $a, b, c$ and simultaneously satisfy
$$
a^{4}=b^{4}+c^{4}-b^{2} c^{2}, b^{4}=c^{4}+a^{4}-a^{2} c^{2} \text {. }
$$

then $\triangle A B C$ is
(A) a scalene triangle.
(B) an equilateral triangle.
(C) a right triangle.
(D) an obtuse triangle.
(E) an isosceles right triangle.
(China Junior High School Mathematics Correspondence Competition, 1985)

Center $O$ of the circumscribed circle of quadrilateral $A B C D$ does not lie on the diagonals of this quadrilateral. Lines $A B$ and $C D$ intersect at point $E$, and lines $A D$ and $B C$ intersect at point $F$.

a) Prove that all six circumcircles of triangles $A B F, C D F, B E C, A D E, B O D$, and $A O C$ intersect at some point $K$.

b) Is it true that point $K$ lies on line $E F$, and that lines $E F$ and $O K$ are perpendicular?

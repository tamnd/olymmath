Given four points $A, B, C$ and $D$ in the plane such that $\overline{A B}=\overline{A C}$ and $\overline{A D}=\overline{B D}$. Let $E$ be a point on the line $A C$, such that $A$ is between $E$ and $C$. If the angles $\alpha=\measuredangle B A E$ and $\beta=\measuredangle A D C$ satisfy $\alpha+\beta=200^{\circ}$, determine the angle $\varphi=\measuredangle B C D$.

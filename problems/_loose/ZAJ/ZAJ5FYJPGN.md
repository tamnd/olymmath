$L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ are two parallel lines in a plane, with $m$ and $n$ points on $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ respectively $(m, n \geqslant 2)$. Connect each point on $L_{1}$ with each point on $L_{2}$ with line segments. It is known that any three of the connected line segments have no common points in the plane region $D$ between $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$. Find the number of different intersection points of these line segments.

Seth has nine stones: three painted blue, three painted red and three painted yellow. The blue stones are labelled 1,2 and 3 , as are the red stones and the yellow stones. He builds a vertical tower with three stones, putting one on top of another.
Three stones form a set if any of the following hold:
(i) They all have the same colour;
(ii) They are all labelled with the same number;
(iii) They all have different colours;
(iv) They are all labelled with different numbers.
In how many ways can he build a tower that avoids creating a set?

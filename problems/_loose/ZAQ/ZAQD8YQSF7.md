On a black box, there is a panel with $K$ light bulbs and a control panel with $K$ two-position switches (toggles). When cycling through the possible states of the control panel, all possible combinations of the light bulbs on the panel light up sequentially. The state of the panel uniquely determines the state of the control panel. It is known that with each toggle switch change, exactly one light bulb turns on or off. Prove that the state of each light bulb depends on the position of exactly one switch (a different one for each light bulb).

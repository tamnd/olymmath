The circumference of a circle is divided into eight arcs by a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$, with four arcs lying inside the quadrilateral and the remaining four lying outside it. The lengths of the arcs lying inside the quadrilateral are denoted by $p, q, r, s$ in counter-clockwise direction starting from some arc. Suppose $p+r=q+s$. Prove that $A B C D$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.

Let $a, b$ and $n$ be three natural numbers, such that $a \neq 0$. Let $d$ be the GCD of $a$ and $b$. Show that $n$ divides $a$ and $b$ if and only if $n$ divides $d$. Deduce, for any integer $c>0$, the relation $\operatorname{GCD}(a c, b c)=c \operatorname{GCD}(a, b)$.

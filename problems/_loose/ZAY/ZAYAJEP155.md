Given an arbitrary acute triangle $A B C$. We are looking for a line $g$ that satisfies the following conditions:

(1) The line $g$ is parallel to $A B$, it intersects the side $A C$ at a point $D$ and the side $B C$ at a point $E$.

(2) For these points, $A D + B E = D E$.

I. Show that a line $g$, if it satisfies conditions (1) and (2), can be constructed for the triangle!

II. Describe such a construction!

III. Show that a line $g$, if constructed according to this description, satisfies conditions (1) and (2)!

IV. Construct an arbitrary acute, non-isosceles triangle $A B C$ and for this, also construct $g$ according to your description!

Define $H_n = 1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}$ . Let the sum of all $H_n$ that are terminating in base 10 be $S$ . If $S = m/n$ where m and n are relatively prime positive integers, find $100m+n$ .

*Proposed by Lewis Chen*

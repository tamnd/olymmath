What will remain of the rectangle? A golden rectangle is a rectangle whose sides $a$ and $b$ are in the golden ratio, i.e., satisfy the equation

$$
a: b=b:(a-b)
$$

Imagine that this rectangle is cut out of paper and lies on the table, facing us with its longer side. Cut off the largest square that can be cut from the rectangle on the left side; the remainder will again be a golden rectangle. Move to the left side of the table to again have the longer side of the rectangle in front of you, and treat the new rectangle in the same way as the previous one. Thus, we walk around the table in a clockwise direction and cut off squares in turn. Each point of the rectangle, except one, will eventually be cut off. Determine the position of that exceptional point.

Given is an acute-angled $\triangle A B C$. Let $A A_{0}, B B_{0}$, and $C C_{0}$ be its altitudes, $A_{1}$, $B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ the midpoints of sides $B C, A C$, and $A B$, respectively, and $O$ the center of the circumscribed circle. Prove that the perimeter of $\triangle A_{0} B_{0} C_{0}$ is less than $2\left(O A_{1}+O B_{1}+O C_{1}\right)$.

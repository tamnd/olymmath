In $\triangle ABC$, $AH$ is the altitude on side $BC$, and $D$ is any point on the line $BC$. $O$, $O_{1}$, $O_{2}$ are the circumcenters of $\triangle ABC$, $\triangle ABD$, $\triangle ACD$ respectively, and $N$, $N_{1}$, $N_{2}$ are the nine-point centers of $\triangle ABC$, $\triangle ABD$, $\triangle ACD$ respectively. Let $O^{\prime}$ be the center of the circle (Salmon circle) passing through $A$, $O$, $O_{1}$, $O_{2}$, and construct $O^{\prime}E \perp BC$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $E$. Prove that $H$, $E$, $N$, $N_{1}$, $N_{2}$ are concyclic.

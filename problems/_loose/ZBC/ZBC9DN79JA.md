Let's call a positive integer "tarka" if, when multiplied by any positive integer $m$, the product contains all 10 digits. We call it $n$-tarka if this property holds for all integers $1 \leq m \leq n$.

a) Show that there is no tarka number.

b) Is there a 1995-tarka number?

In a scalene acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, the centroid $G$ and circumcenter $O$ have their reflections across $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ denoted as $G_{1}$, $G_{2}$, $G_{3}$ and $O_{1}$, $O_{2}$, $O_{3}$, respectively. Prove that $\triangle G_{1} G_{2} C$, $\triangle G_{1} G_{3} B$, $\triangle G_{2} G_{3} A$, $\triangle O_{1} O_{2} C$, $\triangle O_{1} O_{3} B$, and $\triangle O_{2} O_{3} A$ share a common point with the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$.

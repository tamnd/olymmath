In the plane of quadrilateral $ABCD$, a point $M$ is taken. A circle is drawn through points $A, B$, and $M$, and the tangent to this circle at point $M$ intersects side $AB$ or its extension at point $P$. The tangent at point $M$ to the circle drawn through points $B, C$, and $M$ meets side $BC$ or its extension at point $Q$. Similarly, points $R$ and $S$ are constructed on sides $CD$ and $DA$ or their extensions. Prove that if points $P, Q, R, S$ lie on one straight line, then quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. Prove the converse theorem.

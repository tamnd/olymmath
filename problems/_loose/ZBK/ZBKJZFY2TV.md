Given four points on a straight line in the following order: $A, B, C, D$. The line is a diagonal of a regular pentagon, and it intersects one side of the pentagon at point $A$, the line parallel to the previous side at point $B$, and the lines of the sides not adjacent to the first side at points $C$ and $D$. The regular pentagon needs to be constructed.

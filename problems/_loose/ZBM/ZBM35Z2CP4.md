The king has 10 wise men. One day he gave one gold coin to the first wise man, two coins to the second, three to the third, ..., and ten to the tenth. Then he said that every minute the wise men could ask him to give nine of them one gold coin each. If at any point all the wise men have the same number of coins, they can take them. Can the wise men take the gold?

With a chain of domino stones laid out according to the usual rules, it is allowed to perform such an operation: a segment consisting of several consecutive dominoes with the same number of pips on the ends of the segment is selected, flipped as a whole, and inserted back into the same place. Prove that if two chains, assembled from two identical sets of dominoes, have the same number of pips at their ends, then by performing the allowed operations, the order of the dominoes in the second chain can be made the same as in the first.

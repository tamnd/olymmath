In an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AB < AC$, $AD$ is the altitude on side $BC$, $P$ is a point on segment $AD$, $PE \perp AC$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $E$, and $PF \perp AB$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $F$. $O_{1}, O_{2}$ are the circumcenters of $\triangle BDF$ and $\triangle CDE$ respectively. Prove that $O_{1}, O_{2}, E, F$ are concyclic if and only if $P$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$.

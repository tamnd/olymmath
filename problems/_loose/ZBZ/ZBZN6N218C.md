Given an isosceles trapezoid with base $A D$ such that $\overline{A B}=\overline{C D}=a, \overline{A C}=\overline{B D}=b$ and $\overline{B C}=c$. Let $M$ be an arbitrary point on the arc $B C$ of the circumscribed circle around the trapezoid $A B C D$. Express $\frac{\overline{B M}+\overline{M C}}{\overline{A M}+\overline{M D}}$ in terms of $a, b$ and $c$.

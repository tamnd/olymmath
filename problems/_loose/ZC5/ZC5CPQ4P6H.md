It is known that $m, n, k$ are distinct natural numbers greater than 1, the number $\log _{m} n$ is rational, and, moreover,

$$
k^{\sqrt{\log _{m} n}}=m^{\sqrt{\log _{n} k}}
$$

Find the minimum of the possible values of the sum $k+5 m+n$.

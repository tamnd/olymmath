A positive integer is called a perfect power if it can be written in the form $a^{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers with $b \geq 2$. For example, 32 and 125 are perfect powers because $32=2^{5}$ and $125=5^{3}$.

The increasing sequence

$$
2,3,5,6,7,10, \ldots
$$

consists of all positive integers which are not perfect powers. The sum of the squares of the digits of the 1000th number in this sequence is
(A) 42
(B) 26
(C) 33
(D) 18
(E) 21

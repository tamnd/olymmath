Does there exist an integer whose decimal representation contains exactly 300 digits "1", and no other digit different from "0", and which is a perfect square?

An integer $n$ is said to be a perfect square if there exists an integer $\mathrm{k}$ such that $\mathrm{n}=\mathrm{k}^{2}$. For example, $9=3 \times 3$ is a perfect square, while 2 is not.

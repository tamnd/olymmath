Let $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at two points $A$ and $B$. Let $d_{A}$ be a line passing through $A$ and $d_{B}$ be a line passing through $B$. We denote $C$ and $E$ as the points of intersection of $d_{A}$ with $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ respectively, and similarly define $D$ and $F$ as the points of intersection of $d_{B}$ with $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ respectively.

Show that the lines $(C D)$ and $(E F)$ are parallel.

Given the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{6}+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1$ with its right focus at $F$, the line $y=k(x-2)$ through $F$ intersects the ellipse at points $P$ and $Q$ $(k \neq 0)$. If the midpoint of $P Q$ is $N$, and $O$ is the origin, the line $O N$ intersects the line $x=3$ at $M$.
(1) Find the size of $\angle M F Q$;
(2) Find the maximum value of $\frac{|P Q|}{|M F|}$.

Prove that for any positive integer $ m$ there exist an infinite number of pairs of integers $(x,y)$ such that
$(\text{i})$ $x$ and $y$ are relatively prime;
$(\text{ii})$ $x$ divides $y^2+m;$
$(\text{iii})$ $y$ divides $x^2+m.$

Point $K$ lies on segment $A^{\prime} C$, and line $A K$ intersects line $C C^{\prime}$ at point $L$. A line through point $K$ parallel to $B C$ and a line through point $C$ parallel to $B D$ intersect at point $M$. Prove that points $D$, $M$, and $L$ lie on one straight line.

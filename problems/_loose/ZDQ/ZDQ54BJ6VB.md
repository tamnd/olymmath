The Magistrate's Bench. A friend of mine from Singapore asked me some time ago to solve the following problem. On the bench of one magistrate (where exactly, is unknown) sit two Englishmen, two Scots, two Welshmen, one Frenchman, one Italian, one Spaniard, and one American. The Englishmen do not want to sit next to each other, the Scots do not want to sit next to each other, and the Welshmen also do not wish to sit next to each other.

In how many different ways can these 10 members of the magistrate be seated on the bench so that no two people of the same nationality sit next to each other?

Find the smallest positive real number $\lambda$ such that for any real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3} \in\left[0, \frac{1}{2}\right]$ and $b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3} \in(0, \infty)$ with $\sum_{i=1}^{3} a_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{3} b_{i}=1$, we have

$$
b_{1} b_{2} b_{3} \leq \lambda\left(a_{1} b_{1}+a_{2} b_{2}+a_{3} b_{3}\right)
$$

A black stone is placed in the top right corner of an $8 \times 8$ grid. Thereafter, on each turn, one is allowed to place a white stone in any empty cell of the grid, and change the color of the stones already placed in adjacent (having a common vertex) cells (white to black, black to white). Can a situation arise where all cells of the grid are occupied by white stones?

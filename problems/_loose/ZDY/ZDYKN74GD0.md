Kwik, Kwek, and Kwak each lie on exactly two consecutive days of the week and tell the truth the other five days. No two of them lie on the same day. Uncle Donald wants to know which of his nephews ate the candy jar. The nephews themselves know very well which of the three did it. On Sunday, Kwik says it was Kwek. On Monday, Kwik says it wasn't Kwek after all, while Kwak claims that Kwik is certainly innocent. On Tuesday, however, Kwak says that Kwik did it.

Who ate the candy jar?

A) It was Kwik.

B) It was Kwek.

C) It was Kwak.

D) It was Kwik or Kwek, but you can't be sure which of the two.

E) It was Kwik or Kwak, but you can't be sure which of the two.

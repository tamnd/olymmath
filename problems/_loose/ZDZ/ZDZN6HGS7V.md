If for any independent variable $x$ within the domain, there exists a non-zero constant $T$ such that $f(x+T)=f(x)$ always holds, then $T$ is a period of the function $f(x)$. Usually, when we find the period, we are looking for its smallest positive period.
 Find the smallest positive period of the following functions according to the definition of periodic functions:
(1) $f(x)=|\sin x|+|\cos x|$;
(2) $f(x)=\tan \frac{2 x}{3}$.

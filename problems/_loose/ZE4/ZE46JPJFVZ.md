On the Island of Logic, there live 40 knights (who always tell the truth), 25 liars (who always lie), and several sophists. A sophist can only utter phrases that a knight or a liar could not say in their place. For example, standing next to a liar, a sophist can say, "We are both liars" (because if he were a knight, this statement would be false, and if he were a liar, it would be true). One day, a sophist made two statements about the island's inhabitants:
1. "There are exactly ... liars on the island."
2. "There are no more sophists on the island than liars."
Reconstruct the first statement of the sophist. How many sophists are on the island?

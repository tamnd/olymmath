Let $p$ be a prime number.

Let $m$ and $n$ be two integers.

Express $v_{p}(\operatorname{gcd}(m, n))$ and $v_{p}(\operatorname{lcm}(m, n))$ in terms of $v_{p}(m)$ and $v_{p}(n)$.

Deduce a new proof of

$$
\operatorname{gcd}(m, n) \operatorname{lcm}(m, n)=m n
$$

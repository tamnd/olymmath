Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $I$ be the center of its incircle and $I_{A}, I_{B}$, and $I_{C}$ the centers of the $A, B$, and $C$-excircles, respectively. We denote by $l_{A}$ the line passing through the orthocenters of the triangles $IBC$ and $I_{A}BC$. We define the lines $l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$ similarly.

Show that the lines $l_{A}, l_{B}$, and $l_{C}$ are concurrent.

Let $a_{i} \geqslant 0, b_{i}>0(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, $l \in \mathbf{N}, k \in \mathbf{N}_{+}$. Then
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{a_{i}^{l+k}}{b_{i}^{l}} \geqslant \frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i}\right)^{l+k}}{n^{k-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_{i}\right)^{l}} .
$$

In a Thälmann Pioneers summer camp, exactly $70 \%$ of all participants earned the sports badge and exactly $30 \%$ of all participants earned the tourist badge. Previously, no participant owned either of these badges.

For the following statements (1) to (4), all of which refer to this camp, it is to be determined whether they are true or false or whether they cannot be decided based solely on the given information:

(1) Fewer than half of all Pioneers who earned the sports badge also earned the tourist badge.

(2) All participants earned either the sports badge or the tourist badge.

(3) Among the holders of the sports badge, there are more who also earned the tourist badge than those who did not.

(4) If the number of Pioneers who earned the sports badge increased by $10 \%$, there would be more holders of the sports badge than holders of the tourist badge.

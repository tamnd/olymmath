Lines $A P, B P$ and $C P$ intersect lines $B C, C A$ and $A B$ at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ respectively. Points $A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ are chosen on lines $B C, C A$ and $A B$ such that $\overline{B A_{2}}: \overline{A_{2} C}=\overline{A_{1} C}: \overline{B A_{1}}, \overline{C B_{2}}: \overline{B_{2} A}=\overline{B_{1} A}: \overline{C B_{1}}$ and $\overline{A C_{2}}: \overline{C_{2} B}=\overline{C_{1} B}: \overline{A C_{1}}$. Prove that lines $A A_{2}, B B_{2}$ and $C C_{2}$ also intersect at one point $Q$ (or are parallel).

Such points $P$ and $Q$ are called isotomically conjugate with respect to triangle $A B C$.

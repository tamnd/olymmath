Let $x, y, z \in \mathbf{R}, \lambda, \mu, v$ be non-negative real numbers, not all zero, then
$$
\frac{x^{2}}{\lambda x+\mu y+v z}+\frac{y^{2}}{\lambda y+\mu z+v x}+\frac{z^{2}}{\lambda z+\mu x+v y} \geqslant \frac{x+y+z}{\lambda+\mu+v} .
$$

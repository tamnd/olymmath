Two prime numbers $a, b$ are exactly the roots of the integer coefficient equation $x^{2}-21 x+t=0$ for $x$. Then $\frac{b}{a}+\frac{a}{b}$ equals
(A) 2213.
(B) $\frac{58}{21}$.
(C) $\frac{2402}{49}$.
(D) $\frac{365}{38}$.
(2nd "Hope Cup" National Mathematics Invitational Competition, 1991)

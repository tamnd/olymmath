If the integer $n$ when divided by 7 leaves a remainder of $1, 3,$ or $4$, prove:
$$
\cos \left(\frac{n}{7} \pi-\frac{13}{14} \pi\right)+\cos \left(\frac{3 n \pi}{7}-\frac{3}{14} \pi\right)+\cos \left(\frac{5}{7} n \pi-\frac{3}{14} \pi\right)=0 .
$$

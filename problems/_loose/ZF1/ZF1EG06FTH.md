The names of three classmates are Petya, Vasya, and Vitya. Masha knows this, but she doesn't know which boy is which. She can ask them questions that can only be answered with "yes" or "no." Each question is asked to one of the boys, and only he answers it. Natasha knows that Petya will always answer truthfully, Vasya will lie in response to the first question asked to him, Vitya will lie in response to the first and second questions asked to him, and after that, they will all answer truthfully. How can she find out the names of the boys in three questions?

Let the numbers $a$ and $b$ be defined by the equation $a / b=\left[a_{0} ; a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right]$. Prove that the equation $a x-b y=1$ with unknowns $x$ and $y$ has one of the pairs $\left(Q_{n-1}, P_{n-1}\right)$ or $\left(-Q_{n-1},-P_{n-1}\right)$ as a solution, where ${ }^{P_{n-1} / Q_{n-1}}$ is the $(n-1)$-th convergent. What determines which of the pairs is the solution?

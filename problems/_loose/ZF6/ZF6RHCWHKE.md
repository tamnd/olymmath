Prove: There do not exist real numbers $x, y, z$, such that the system of equations
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
x^{2}+4 y z+2 z=0, \\
x+2 x y+2 z^{2}=0, \\
2 x z+y^{2}+y+1=0 .
\end{array}\right.
$$

holds.

Let $ABC$ be a non-degenerate triangle (i.e., $A, B$, and $C$ are distinct), and let $H_{A}, H_{B}, H_{C}$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A, B$, and $C$. We call the orthic triangle of $ABC$ the triangle $H_{A} H_{B} H_{C}$, and we denote by $h$ the transformation that, to each non-right triangle, associates its orthic triangle.

Show that there exists an integer $n$ such that the triangle $h^{n}(A B C)$ will be either right-angled or have a perimeter strictly less than that of $ABC$, where $h^{n}$ denotes the $n$-th iterate of the transformation $h$.

Given three real numbers $x, y$ and $z$, which simultaneously satisfy the equations $(x+y)^{2}+(y+z)^{2}+(x+z)^{2}=94$ and $(x-y)^{2}+(y-z)^{2}+(x-z)^{2}=26$, find:
(1) The value of $x y+y z+x z$;
(2) The value of $(x+2 y+3 z)^{2}+(y+2 z+3 x)^{2}+(z+2 x+3 y)^{2}$.

In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, the ellipse $C: \frac{x^{2}}{3}+y^{2}=1$ has its upper vertex at $A$. A line $l$ intersects the ellipse $C$ at points $P$ and $Q$, and does not pass through point $A$, with $\overrightarrow{A P} \cdot \overrightarrow{A Q}=0$.
(1) Does the line $l$ pass through a fixed point? If so, find the coordinates of the fixed point; if not, explain the reason;
(2) Tangents to the ellipse are drawn through points $P$ and $Q$, intersecting at point $B$. Find the range of the area of $\triangle B P Q$.

We denote by $S(n)$, the sum of all natural divisors of the natural number $n$. A natural number $n$ is called a perfect number if $S(n)=2 \cdot n$.

a) Show that 6 and 28 are perfect numbers.

b) If the numbers $k$ and $2^{k}-1$ are simultaneously prime, prove that the number $n=2^{k-1} \cdot\left(2^{k}-1\right)$ is a perfect number.

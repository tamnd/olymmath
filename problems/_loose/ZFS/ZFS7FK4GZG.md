a) Prove that for any natural number $n$,

$$
1+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{9}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n^{2}} \leq 2-\frac{1}{n}
$$

b) Show that for all $n \geq 2$,

$$
\frac{1}{n^{2}-n}-\frac{1}{n^{2}+n}>\frac{2}{n^{3}}
$$

c) Prove then that for all $n \geq 1$

$$
1+\frac{1}{8}+\frac{1}{27}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n^{3}} \leq \frac{5}{4}
$$

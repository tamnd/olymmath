$\triangle ABC$ is inscribed in circle $O'$, circle $O$ is inscribed in $\triangle ABC$, connect and extend $AO$ to intersect the larger circle at $D$, then it must be true that
(A) $CD = BD = O'D$.
(B) $AO = CO = OD$.
(C) $CD = CO = BD$.
(D) $CD = OD = BD$.
(E) $O'B = O'C = OD$.
(17th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1966)

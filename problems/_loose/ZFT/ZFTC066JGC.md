For every natural number $n$ prove that $$ \left( 1+ \frac12 + ...+ \frac1n \right)^2+ \left( \frac12 + ...+ \frac1n \right)^2+...+ \left( \frac{1}{n-1} + \frac12 \right)^2+ \left( \frac1n \right)^2=2n- \left( 1+ \frac12 + ...+ \frac1n \right) $$ (S. Manukian, Yerevan)

For each point $T$ of the given triangle $ABC$, $m(T)$ and $M(T)$ denote the smallest and the largest, respectively, of the lengths of the segments $TA, TB, TC$.

a) (Czechoslovakia, 74). Find all points $T$ of the triangle $ABC$ for which the value of $m(T)$ is maximized.

b) (Czechoslovakia, 82). Prove that if $\angle BAC \geqslant 90^{\circ}$, then for any point $T$ of the triangle $ABC$ the following inequalities hold:

$$
m(T) \leqslant BC / 2 \leqslant M(T)
$$

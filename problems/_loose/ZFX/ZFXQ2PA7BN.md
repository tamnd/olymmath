In the process of proving multivariate homogeneous inequalities, Muirhead's inequality is often used for local adjustments.

Muirhead's Theorem Given the ternary arrays $\left(a_{1}, a_{2}\right.$, $\left.a_{3}\right)$ and $\left(b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}\right)$ satisfying
(i) $a_{1} \geqslant a_{2} \geqslant a_{3} \geqslant 0, b_{1} \geqslant b_{2} \geqslant b_{3} \geqslant 0$;
(ii) $a_{1} \geqslant b_{1}, a_{1}+a_{2} \geqslant b_{1}+b_{2}$,
$$
a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3} \geqslant b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3} \text {. }
$$

Then for any positive real numbers $x, y, z$, we have
$$
\sum_{s y m} x^{a_{1}} y^{a_{1}} z^{a_{3}} \geq \sum_{s y m} x^{b_{1}} y^{b_{2}} z^{b_{3}} .
$$

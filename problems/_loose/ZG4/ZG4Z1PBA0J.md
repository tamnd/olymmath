Let $N$ be an arbitrary positive integer. Prove that there exists an integer divisible by $N$ which, in the decimal system, is written using only the digits 0 and 1. Moreover, if $N$ is coprime with 10 (i.e., does not divide by 2 or 5), then there exists a number divisible by $N$ composed entirely of ones (if $N$ is not coprime with 10, then, of course, no number of the form $\underbrace{11 \ldots 1}_{n \text{ times}}$ can divide $N$).

If the angle bisectors of $\angle B A D$ and $\angle B C D$ in quadrilateral $A B C D$ are parallel, then the relationship between $\angle B$ and $\angle D$ is
(A) $\angle B=\angle D$.
(B) $\angle B$ and $\angle D$ are supplementary.
(C) $\angle B>\angle D$.
(D) $\angle B<\angle D$.
(China Beijing Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1991)

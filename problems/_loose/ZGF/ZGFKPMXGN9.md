Let the perpendicular bisectors of the sides $AB, BC, CD,$ and $DA$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ be $a, b, c,$ and $d$ respectively; the intersection points of the line pairs $a, b; b, c; c, d$ and $d, a$ are denoted by $A', B', C', D'$ respectively. Show that if the lines $a, b, c,$ and $d$ intersect each other and do not pass through a single point, then the lines $A'C'$ and $B'D'$ are perpendicular to the diagonals of the original quadrilateral.

For $n$ fields of an $n \times n$ table, we say they are scattered if no two are in the same row or column. In each field of this table, a natural number is written such that the sum of the numbers in any $n$ scattered fields is the same, and no row or column contains two equal numbers. It turns out that the numbers along the main diagonal are in increasing order and that their product is the smallest among all products of $n$ scattered numbers.

Prove that the scattered numbers whose product is the largest are located along the secondary diagonal.

Time for work 270 minutes.

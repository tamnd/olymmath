Given a triangle $ABC$ with the shortest side $BC$. On the sides $AB$, $AC$, and on the rays opposite to the rays $BC$, $CB$, choose points $X$, $Y$, $K$, $L$ respectively, such that $|BX|=|BK|=|BC|=|CY|=|CL|$. The lines $KX$ and $LY$ intersect at point $M$. Prove that the centroid of triangle $KLM$ coincides with the incenter of triangle $ABC$.

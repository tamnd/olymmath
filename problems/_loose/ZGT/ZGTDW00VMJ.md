The numbers $x, y, z$ and $t$ lie in the interval $(0,1)$. Prove the inequality

$\sqrt{x^{2}+(1-t)^{2}}+\sqrt{y^{2}+(1-x)^{2}}+\sqrt{z^{2}+(1-y)^{2}}+\sqrt{t^{2}+(1-z)^{2}}<4$.

Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle. Let $Q$ be a random point on $BC$, and let $P$ be the meeting point of $AQ$ and the circumscribed circle of $\triangle ABC$.
Prove that $\frac{1}{PQ}=\frac{1}{PB}+\frac{1}{PC}$.

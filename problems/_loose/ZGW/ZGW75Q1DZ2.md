Let $M$ be a finite subset of the set of integers $\mathbf{Z}$ containing 0, and let $f, g: M \rightarrow M$ be two monotonically decreasing functions, satisfying $g(f(0)) \geqslant 0$. Prove: There exists an integer $p$ in $M$ such that $g(f(p))=p$.

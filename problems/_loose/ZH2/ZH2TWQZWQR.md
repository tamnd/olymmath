A boy says to his friend: You can easily remember my phone number in the following way. There is a sequence of four natural numbers $a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} a_{4}$ in increasing order, where both $a_{2}$ and $a_{3}$ are the harmonic means of their neighbors, and when written next to each other, they form a six-digit number. This six-digit number is my phone number. Which one is it? ( $a_{2}$ is the harmonic mean of $a_{1}$ and $a_{3}$ if

$$
\frac{1}{a_{2}}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{3}}\right)
$$

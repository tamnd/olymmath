Through the midpoint of each diagonal of quadrilateral $ABCD$, a line is drawn parallel to the other diagonal. Prove that if these lines intersect at point $M$, then the areas of quadrilaterals $M M_{1} A M_{4}, M M_{1} B M_{2}, M M_{2} C M_{3}, M M_{3} D M_{4}$ are equal to each other ( $M_{i}$ - midpoints of the sides of the quadrilateral).

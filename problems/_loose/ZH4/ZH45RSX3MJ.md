Select five points $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{5}$ on a plane, and connect each pair of these points with a blue straight line. Assume that no two blue lines are either perpendicular or parallel. For each point $X_{i}(1 \leqslant i \leqslant 5)$, for every blue line $l$ that does not pass through $X_{i}$, there is a red line passing through $X_{i}$ that is perpendicular to $l$. How many intersection points can all the red lines have at most?

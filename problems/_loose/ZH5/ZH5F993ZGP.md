The 68th question: Let the sequence of positive real numbers $\mathrm{x}_{0}, \mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{1995}$ satisfy the following two conditions: (1) $\mathrm{x}_{0}=$ $\mathrm{x}_{1995}$; (2) $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}-1}+\frac{2}{\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}-1}}=2 \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}+\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}}, \mathrm{i}=1, 2, \ldots, 1995$. Find the maximum value of $\mathrm{x}_{0}$ for all sequences that satisfy the conditions.

Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ be finite sets. Prove: When $n \geqslant 3$, $\left.\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|A_{i}\right|+\frac{1}{C_{n}^{3}} \sum_{i \leqslant i<j<k \leqslant n} \right\rvert A_{i} \cap A_{j}$ $\left.\cap A_{k}\left|\geqslant \frac{2}{C_{n}^{2}} \sum_{1 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant n}\right| A_{i} \cap A_{j} \right\rvert\,$
(2005 8th Mediterranean Mathematical Competition Problem)

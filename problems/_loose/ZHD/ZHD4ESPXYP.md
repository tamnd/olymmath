On a "black box" of unknown internal structure, there is a signal panel consisting of $n$ indicator lights and a controller consisting of $n$ switches, each of which has two different states. When all possible state changes are made to the switches on the controller, the indicator lights on the signal panel correspondingly display all possible "on" and "off" combinations, and the on-off state of the indicator lights is uniquely determined by the state of the switches on the controller. It is known that each change in the state of a switch results in exactly one indicator light changing its on-off state. Prove that the state of each indicator light is uniquely determined by the state of one switch (i.e., each light has its own control switch).

In triangle $A B C, m(\angle A B C)=2 m(\angle A C B)$ and $A D \perp B C,(D \in B C)$. Points $E$ and $C$ are located on opposite sides of line $\mathrm{AB}$ such that $\mathrm{BE} \perp \mathrm{AE}$ and $\angle \mathrm{EAB} \equiv \angle \mathrm{ACB}$. The bisector of angle AED intersects line $A C$ at $M$. If $\{H\}=A E \cap B C$, show that:

a) triangles BHA and $\mathrm{AHC}$ are isosceles;

b) MCDE is a parallelogram;

c) The perimeter of parallelogram MCDE is equal to that of triangle $A B C$.

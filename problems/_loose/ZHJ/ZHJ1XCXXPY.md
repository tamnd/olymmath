They have designated seats in three rows, with $n$ seats in each, and they are admitted to the hall one by one, after which they immediately take their seats. Calculate the probability that until the moment the last participant takes their seat, at any time, for any two rows, the difference in the number of participants sitting in them is not greater than 1.

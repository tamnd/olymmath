If a number is equal to the square of some natural number, it is called a perfect square. It is known that $a$ and $b$ are two perfect squares, the unit digit of $a$ is 1, and the tens digit is $x$; the unit digit of $b$ is 6, and the tens digit is $y$. Then
(A) $x, y$ are both odd.
(B) $x, y$ are both even.
(C) $x$ is odd, $y$ is even.
(D) $x$ is even, $y$ is odd.
（2nd "Five Sheep Cup" Junior High School Mathematics Competition, 1990）

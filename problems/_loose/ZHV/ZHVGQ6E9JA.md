Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a simple graph with $n$ vertices and $m$ edges such that no two cycles share an edge. Prove that $2m < 3n$.

*Note*: A *simple graph* is a graph with at most one edge between any two vertices and no edges from any vertex to itself. A *cycle* is a sequence of distinct vertices $v_1, \dots, v_n$ such that there is an edge between any two consecutive vertices, and between $v_n$ and $v_1$.

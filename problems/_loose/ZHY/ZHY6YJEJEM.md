A seller wants to cut a piece of cheese into parts that can be divided into two piles of equal weight. He can cut any piece of cheese in the same ratio $a$ : ( $1-a$ ) by weight, where $0<a<1$. Is it true that for any interval of length 0.001 within the interval $(0,1)$, there exists a value of $a$ such that he can achieve the desired result with a finite number of cuts?

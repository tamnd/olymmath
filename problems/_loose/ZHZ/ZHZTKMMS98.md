In a class of 28 students, all students participate in extracurricular sports, and each student participates in at least one of the following four sports: football, athletics, swimming, and gymnastics, with at least 1 student in each of these sports. No student participates in a sport not listed here.

It is known about the students in this class:

(1) Each student participates in at most two sports.

(2) Exactly 18 students participate in exactly one sport.

(3) Half of the students who do athletics also participate in gymnastics.

(4) Every swimmer participates in two sports, with the other sports being equally represented.

(5) The number of students who only do gymnastics is equal to the number of students who only play football.

(6) The set of students who both do gymnastics and play football is empty.

(7) The number of students who do both gymnastics and athletics is equal to the number of the remaining students who also participate in two sports.

Determine the number of students in this class who participate in

a) football b) athletics c) swimming d) gymnastics!

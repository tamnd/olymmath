Determine all pairs of real numbers $x, y$ that satisfy the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \lfloor x+y\rfloor=2010 \\
& \lfloor x\rfloor-y=p
\end{aligned}
$$

if a) $p=2$, b) $p=3$.

The symbol $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer that is not greater than the given real number $x$ (the so-called floor function of the real number $x$).

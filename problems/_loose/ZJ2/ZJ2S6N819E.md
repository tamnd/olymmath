For every $n$ positive integers we denote $$\frac{x_n}{y_n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}{\frac{1}{k {n \choose k}}}$$
where $x_n, y_n$ are coprime positive integers. Prove that $y_n$ is not divisible by $2^n$ for any positive integers $n$.

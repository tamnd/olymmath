Prove that in any triangle, the following relationship holds:

$$
\frac{r \cdot r_{a} \cdot r_{b} \cdot r_{c}}{R^{4}} \leq \frac{27}{16}
$$

( $R$ is the circumradius, $r$ is the inradius, and $r_{a}, r_{b}, r_{c}$ are the radii of the excircles.)

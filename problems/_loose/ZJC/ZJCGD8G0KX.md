In an acute scalene triangle $ABC$, points $C_{0}$ and $B_{0}$ are the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively, $O$ is the center of the circumscribed circle, and $H$ is the orthocenter. The lines $BH$ and $OC_{0}$ intersect at point $P$, and the lines $CH$ and $OB_{0}$ intersect at point $Q$. It turns out that the quadrilateral $OPHQ$ is a rhombus. Prove that points $A, P$, and $Q$ lie on the same line.

If the winner initially had fewer points than the opponent, the winner additionally receives one point from the loser. In each round, participants with the same number of points compete, but in one of the pairs, a difference of 1 point between opponents is allowed. The tournament ends as soon as a sole leader is determined. How many schoolchildren will finish the tournament with 5 points?

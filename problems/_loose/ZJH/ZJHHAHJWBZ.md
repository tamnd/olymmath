(IMO 2000/1) Let $\Omega_{1}$ and $\Omega_{2}$ be two circles intersecting at $M$ and $N$. Let $\Delta$ be the common tangent to the two circles, which is closer to $M$ than to $N$. $\Delta$ is tangent to $\Omega_{1}$ at $A$ and to $\Omega_{2}$ at $B$. The line through $M$ parallel to $\Delta$ meets $\Omega_{1}$ at $C$ and $\Omega_{2}$ at $D$. Let $E$ be the intersection of lines $(CA)$ and $(BD)$, $P$ the intersection of lines $(AN)$ and $(CD)$, and $Q$ the intersection of lines $(BN)$ and $(CD)$. Show that $EP = EQ$.

For a set $A$ with $n$ elements $\left(n \geqslant 3, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$, some subsets satisfy: each subset contains at least 2 elements, and the intersection of any two different subsets contains at most 2 elements. Let the sum of the cubes of the number of elements in these subsets be $P$. Does there exist $n \geqslant 3$ such that the maximum value of $P$ is equal to the power of some prime number? Provide a reason.

Let $P\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ be a fixed point inside the ellipse $\Gamma$ (different from the center $O$), and draw a line $l$ through point $P$ intersecting the ellipse at points $A$ and $B$. Construct a parallelogram (considered degenerate when $A, O, B$ are collinear) $O A Q B$ with $O A$ and $O B$ as adjacent sides, then the locus of point $Q$ is an ellipse $\Gamma^{\prime}$ centered at $P$ and having the same eccentricity as the ellipse $\Gamma$, and the ellipse $\Gamma^{\prime}$ passes through the center of $\Gamma$.

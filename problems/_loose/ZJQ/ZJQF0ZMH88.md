The lengths of the sides of a polygon are $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$. The quadratic trinomial $f(x)$ is such that $f\left(a_{1}\right)=f\left(a_{2}+\ldots+a_{n}\right)$. Prove that if $A$ is the sum of the lengths of some sides of the polygon, and $B$ is the sum of the lengths of the remaining sides, then $f(A)=f(B)$.

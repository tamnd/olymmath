Alibaba attempts to enter a cave. At the cave entrance stands a drum, with 4 holes on its side. Inside each hole, near the hole's opening, there is a switch, which can be in one of two states: up or down. If all 4 switches are in the same state, the cave door will open. Alibaba is allowed to insert his hand into any two holes, feel the switches to determine their states, and change or leave them as they are. However, after doing so, the drum will spin rapidly, making it impossible to determine which switches were touched once it stops. Alibaba is allowed to repeat this process 10 times. Prove that Alibaba can definitely enter the cave.

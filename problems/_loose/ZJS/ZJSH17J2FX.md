$$
\frac{a}{1-x}-\frac{2}{2-x}+\frac{3}{3-x}-\frac{4}{4-x}+\frac{b}{5-x}=0
$$

be quadratic in $x$, or reducible to a quadratic equation? Solve the equation in each case.

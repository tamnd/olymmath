Let $l_{a}, l_{b}$ and $l_{c}$ be the lengths of the angle bisectors of angles $A, B$ and $C$ of triangle $A B C$, and $m_{a}, m_{b}$ and $m_{c}$ be the lengths of the corresponding medians. Prove that

$$
\frac{l_{a}}{m_{a}}+\frac{l_{b}}{m_{b}}+\frac{l_{c}}{m_{c}}>1
$$

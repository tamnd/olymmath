Given the function $g(x)=(a+1)^{x-2}+1(a>0)$ whose graph always passes through a fixed point $A$, and point $A$ also lies on the graph of the function $f(x)=\log _{\sqrt{3}}(x+a)$.
(I) Find the value of the real number $a$;
(II) When the equation $|g(x+2)-2|=2 b$ has two distinct real roots, find the range of $b$;
(III) Let $a_{n}=g(n+2), b_{n}=\frac{a_{n}-1}{a_{n} \cdot a_{n-1}}, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$, prove that: $b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}<\frac{1}{3},\left(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}\right)$.

Let $A, B \in M_{n}(C)$ be symmetric and $C=A B-B A$. Show that:

a) For $n=2 k+1 , \operatorname{Det} C=0$, where $\mathrm{k} \in \mathbf{N}$.

b) If $P(x)=\operatorname{Det}\left(C+x I_{n}\right)$, then the following relation holds: $P(x)=(-1)^{n} P(-x)$, (a matrix equal to its transpose is called a symmetric matrix)

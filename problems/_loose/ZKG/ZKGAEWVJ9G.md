The Butterfly Theorem can be generalized to lines; to conic sections; and also to equal circles: Equal circles $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ intersect at $A$ and $B$, $O$ is the midpoint of $A B$, a chord $C D$ of $\odot O_{1}$ through $O$ intersects $\odot O_{2}$ at $P$, and a chord $E F$ of $\odot O_{2}$ through $O$ intersects $\odot O_{1}$ at $Q$. Prove that $A B$, $C Q$, and $E P$ are concurrent. Please prove the latter proposition!

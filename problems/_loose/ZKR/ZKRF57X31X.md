In the tetrahedron $S-ABC$, the lateral edges $SA$, $SB$, and $SC$ are mutually perpendicular, $M$ is the centroid of $\triangle ABC$, $D$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and a line $DN$ is drawn parallel to $SC$. Prove:
(1) $DN$ intersects $SM$;
(2) Let the intersection point of $DN$ and $SM$ be $P$, then $P$ is the center of the circumscribed sphere of the tetrahedron $S-ABC$.

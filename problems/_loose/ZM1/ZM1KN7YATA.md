If $x$ is a solution to the equation

$$
\frac{x+1}{1}+\frac{x+2}{2}+\frac{x+3}{3}+\ldots+\frac{x+100}{100}=100
$$

then:
(A) $x=-2$,
(B) $-1 \leq x \leq 1$,
(C) $x=\frac{3}{2}$,
(D) $x=2$,
(E) $x \geq 3$.

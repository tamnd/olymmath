Let $N$ be the number of $2015$ -tuples of (not necessarily distinct) subsets $(S_1, S_2, \dots, S_{2015})$ of $\{1, 2, \dots, 2015 \}$ such that the number of permutations $\sigma$ of $\{1, 2, \dots, 2015 \}$ satisfying $\sigma(i) \in S_i$ for all $1 \le i \le 2015$ is odd. Let $k_2, k_3$ be the largest integers such that $2^{k_2} | N$ and $3^{k_3} | N$ respectively. Find $k_2 + k_3.$ *Proposed by Yang Liu*

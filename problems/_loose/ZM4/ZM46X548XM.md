Set $|A|=n$ . $A_1,A_2,\cdots,A_m$ are subsets of $A$ , and $A_i\not\subseteq A_j$ for any $1\leq i<j\leq m$ . Prove:**(a)** $\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{1}{\text{C}_n^{|A_i|}}\leq1$ .**(b)** $\sum_{i=1}^{m}\text{C}_n^{|A_i|}\geq m^2$ .

Gleb thinks of two positive integers $N, a (a < N)$, and writes them on the blackboard in order. Then he begins to perform the following operation: he divides $N$ by the last number written on the blackboard with a remainder, and writes the remainder on the blackboard. Once a 0 appears on the blackboard, Gleb stops the operation. Can Gleb initially choose such $N$ and $a$ so that the sum of all numbers written on the blackboard is greater than $100N$?

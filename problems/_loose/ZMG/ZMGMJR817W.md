8- 169 Given the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$, let $a_{0}=0$. Define
$$
b_{n}=a_{n}-a_{n-1}, c_{n}=\frac{a_{n}}{n}, n=1,2,3, \cdots
$$

If the sequence $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is monotonically decreasing, prove that the sequence $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ is also monotonically decreasing.

In the tetrahedron $ABCD$, we draw the angle bisectors of the angles at $D$ on the faces $ADB$, $BDC$, and $CDA$, and consider the angles formed pairwise by these three angle bisectors. It is to be proven that either all three angles are obtuse, or all three are acute, or all three are right angles.

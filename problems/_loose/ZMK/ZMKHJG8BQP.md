Suppose the circumcircle $\odot O$ and the incircle $\odot I$ of the bicentric quadrilateral $ABCD$ have radii $R$ and $r$, respectively, and $OI=d$. The incircle $\odot I$ touches $AB, BC, CD, DA$ at $E, F, G, H$, respectively, and $EG$ intersects $FH$ at $K$. Prove:
(1) If $\odot I$ is taken as the inversion circle, then the inversion image of $\odot O$ is the circle passing through the midpoints of the sides of quadrilateral $EFGH$;
(2) $\frac{1}{(R+d)^{2}}+\frac{1}{(R-d)^{2}}=\frac{1}{r^{2}}$.

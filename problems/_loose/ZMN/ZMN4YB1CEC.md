Let $p,q$ be two distinct odd primes. Calculate
$ \sum_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\left \lfloor \frac{qj}{p}\right \rfloor +\sum_{j=1}^{\frac{q-1}{2}}\left \lfloor \frac{pj}{q}\right\rfloor$ .

There are $n$ tourists, among any 3 of whom there must be 2 who do not know each other, and if these $n$ people are arbitrarily divided into two groups to sit in buses, at least one bus will have people who know each other. Prove: There exists a tourist who knows at most $\frac{2}{5} n$ tourists.
(2004 Bulgarian Olympiad Problem)

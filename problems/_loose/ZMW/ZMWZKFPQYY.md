Determine the smallest and largest value of the function

$$
f(x, y, z, t)=\frac{a x^{2}+b y^{2}}{a x+b y}+\frac{a z^{2}+b t^{2}}{a z+b t},(a>0, b>0)
$$

under the conditions $x+z=y+t=1, x, y, z, t \geq 0$.

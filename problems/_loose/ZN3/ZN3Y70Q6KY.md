Positive real numbers $a,b,c$ satisfy $$ 2a^3b+2b^3c+2c^3a=a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2. $$
Prove that $$ 2ab(a-b)^2+2bc(b-c)^2+2ca(c-a)^2 \geq(ab+bc+ca)^2. $$

Given $E(m, n)$ is a fixed point inside the parabola
$$
y^{2}=2 p x(p>0)
$$

two lines passing through $E$ with slopes $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ intersect the parabola at points $A, B, C, D$, and $M, N$ are the midpoints of segments $AB$ and $CD$, respectively.
(1) When $n=0$ and $k_{1} k_{2}=-1$, find the minimum value of the area of $\triangle EMN$;
(2) If $k_{1}+k_{2}=\lambda($ constant $\lambda \neq 0)$, prove that the line $MN$ passes through a fixed point.

Let $a_n$ be a sequence of natural numbers defined by $a_1 = m$ and for $n > 1$ . We call apair $ (a_k, a_{\ell })$ *interesting* if
(i) $0 < \ell - k < 2016$ ,
(ii) $a_k$ divides $a_{\ell }$ .
Show that there exists a $m$ such that the sequence $a_n$ contains no interesting pair.

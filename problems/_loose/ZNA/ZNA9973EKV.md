Let's examine triples $(a, b, c)$ of positive integers satisfying the condition $a b=c^{2}$.

a) For each prime number $p$, provide an example of a triple $(a, b, c)$ such that $a+b-2 c=p$.

b) Prove that for every triple $(a, b, c)$, $a+b+2 c$ is a composite number.

We have a two-armed balance and 101 weights, whose total mass is 200 grams. The mass of each weight, expressed in grams, is an integer.

We place the weights into the pans in decreasing order, such that if the balance is in equilibrium, we place the next weight in the left pan, and if it is not, we place the next weight in the lighter pan.

Prove that after placing all the weights, the balance will be in equilibrium.

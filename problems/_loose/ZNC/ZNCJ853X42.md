Let $\mu-$ be an arbitrary measure, and $\xi$ and $\eta-$ be extended random variables for which $\int_{\Omega}|\xi| d \mu$ and $\int_{\Omega}|\eta| d \mu$ are finite. Then if for all sets $A \in \mathscr{F}$ the inequality $\int_{A} \xi d \mu \leqslant \int_{A} \eta d \mu$ holds, then
$\mu(\xi>\eta)=0$.

If a natural number $A$ is not less than 10, and all its digits are distinct, and any number obtained by removing any one digit from $A$ is a divisor of $A$, then $A$ is called a "Huang Mei number". For example: since 24, 20, and 40 are all divisors of 240, 240 is a "Huang Mei number". How many "Huang Mei numbers" are there? $ $

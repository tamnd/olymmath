Construct tables of the reciprocals $\frac{1}{k}$ in 7-arithmetic, 11-arithmetic, and 13-arithmetic.

As an example, the table of reciprocals in 5-arithmetic is provided below:

| $k$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $\frac{1}{k}$ | 1 | 3 | 2 | 4 |

Show that in any $p$-arithmetic, only the elements 1 and -1 are their own reciprocals.

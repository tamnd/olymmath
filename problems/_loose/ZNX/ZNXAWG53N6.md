In the plane of an equilateral triangle, a random line is given. Prove that the distances $m, n, p$ from the vertices of the triangle to the line and the height of the triangle are related by the equation:

$$
(m-n)^{2}+(n-p)^{2}+(p-m)^{2}=2 h^{2}
$$

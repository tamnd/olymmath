Let $P$ be any point inside a circle $O$ with radius $R$, and draw $n(n \geqslant 2)$ lines $l_{1}$, $l_{2}, \cdots, l_{n}$ through point $P$. If the angle between any two adjacent lines is $\frac{\pi}{n}$, and the $i$-th line $l_{i}$ intersects the circle at points $M_{i}, M^{\prime}{ }_{i}$ $(i=1,2, \cdots, n)$, then $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\left|P M_{i}\right|^{2}+\left|P M^{\prime}{ }_{i}\right|^{2}\right)$ is a constant value $2 n R^{2}$ independent of the point $P$.

In space, there are 5 points, none of which lie in the same plane or on the same sphere. How many planes or spheres exist that are equidistant from these 5 points?

[The distance from a point $M$ to a sphere $\Sigma$ with center $O$ is defined as the length of the shorter of the two segments $M A$ and $M B$, where $A$ and $B$ are the points of intersection of the line $M O$ with the sphere $\Sigma^{1}$).]

Let $\{x_n\}$ be a sequence of natural numbers such that $$(a) 1 = x_1 < x_2 < x_3 < \ldots; (b) x_{2n+1} \leq 2n \forall n.$$ Prove that, for every natural number $k$ , there exist terms $x_r$ and $x_s$ such that $x_r - x_s = k.$

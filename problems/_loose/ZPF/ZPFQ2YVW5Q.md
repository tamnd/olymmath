Consider an $m \times n$ chessboard (consisting of $m$ rows and $n$ columns of squares, where $m \geqslant 3, n \geqslant 3$) where all the small squares are colored either red or blue. If two adjacent (sharing a common edge) small squares are of different colors, they are called a "standard pair." Let the number of "standard pairs" on the chessboard be $S$. Determine whether $S$ is odd or even based on the colors of which squares. Under what conditions is $S$ odd? Under what conditions is $S$ even? Provide a justification.

There were 30 cars participating in the rally. Each of them received a sequential number - numbers from 1 to 30. But just before the start, it turned out that one of the cars could not participate in the race due to its technical condition. Then it turned out that among the remaining 29 numbers, there is a number equal to the arithmetic mean of these 29 numbers. What is the number of the car that dropped out of the race? If the problem has more than one solution, then write the sum of these numbers in the answer.

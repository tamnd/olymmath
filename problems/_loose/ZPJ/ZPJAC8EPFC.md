Prove the following theorem:

"If $k$ is the excircle of triangle $ABC$ touching side $BC$ and $M_{a}$ is the center of $k$, then the size of the angle $\angle B M_{a} C$ depends only on the size $\alpha$ of the angle $\angle C A B$."

For the proof, derive a formula for the size of the angle $\angle B M_{a} C$ in terms of $\alpha$!

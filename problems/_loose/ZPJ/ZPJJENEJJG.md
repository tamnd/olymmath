Péter and Pál play with marbles. Péter has 12 white and 8 black marbles, Pál has 8 white and 12 black marbles, they keep their marbles in separate urns. In each step, simultaneously (without looking), each of them moves one marble from their own urn to the other's. What is the probability that in the $k$-th step, Péter moves a white marble to Pál and Pál moves a black marble to Péter?

a) Prove that if $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n,y_1,y_2,\ldots,y_n$ are positive real numbers satisfying the conditions [list=i]

- $x_1y_1<x_2y_2<\ldots<x_ny_n$ ;

- $x_1+x_2+\ldots+x_k \ge y_1+y_2+ \ldots +y_k,$ for $k=\overline{1,n},$
then $$ \frac{1}{x_1}+\frac{1}{x_2}+\ldots+\frac{1}{x_n} \le \frac{1}{y_1}+\frac{1}{y_2}+\ldots+\frac{1}{y_n}. $$ b) Let $A=\{a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n\}$ be a set of positive integers with the property that for any distinct subsets $B$ and $C$ of $A$ we have $\sum_{x \in B} x \neq \sum_{x \in C} x.$ Prove that $$ \frac{1}{a_1}+\frac{1}{a_2}+\ldots+\frac{1}{a_n}<2. $$

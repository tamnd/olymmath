The perpendicular line $m$ is drawn from $C$ to $CB$, and the projection of $A$ onto $m$ is $A_{1}$. A point $M$ moves along $m$. Perpendiculars are drawn from $A_{1}$ to $MA$ and from $C$ to $MD$, and their intersection is $N$. Prove that the circle centered at $N$ and passing through $E$ is tangent to a fixed line.

From an internal point of a given triangle, three lines are drawn which divide the triangle into 6 parts. Let $S_{1}, S_{2}$, and $S_{3}$ be the areas of any three of these parts. Prove that:
a) $\frac{1}{S_{1}}+\frac{1}{S_{2}}>\frac{4}{S}$

b) $\frac{1}{S_{1}}+\frac{1}{S_{2}}+\frac{1}{S_{3}}>\frac{9}{S}$.

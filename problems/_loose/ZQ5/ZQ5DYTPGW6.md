Given integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{10}$. Prove that there exists a non-zero sequence
$$
\left\{x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{10}\right\} \text {, }
$$

such that all $x_{i} \in\{-1,0,1\}$, and the sum $\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_{i} a_{i}$ is divisible by 1001.
(29th International Mathematical Olympiad Shortlist, 1988)

On the blackboard, there are $n$ real numbers. It is allowed to erase any two numbers, for example $a$ and $b$, and write another number $\frac{1}{4}(a+b)$. This operation is performed $n-1$ times, and finally, only one number remains on the blackboard. Given that the $n$ numbers initially written on the blackboard are all 1, prove that the last remaining number is not less than $\frac{1}{n}$.

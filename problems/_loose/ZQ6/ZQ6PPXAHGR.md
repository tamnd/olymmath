Let $a_{n}=\left\lfloor\sqrt{n^{2}+(n+1)^{2}}\right\rfloor$ for all $n \geqslant 1$. Prove that there are infinitely many integers $n$ such that $a_{n}-a_{n-1}>1$ and $a_{n+1}-a_{n}=1$.

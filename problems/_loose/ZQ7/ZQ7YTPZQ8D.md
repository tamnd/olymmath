6-6 (G19, Poland) Given a tetrahedron $A B C D$, connect the vertex $D$ with the centroid $D_{1}$ of the base $\triangle A B C$. Draw lines through the vertices of $\triangle A B C$ parallel to $D D_{1}$, intersecting the opposite faces at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, respectively. Prove that the volume of the tetrahedron $A B C D$ is three times the volume of the tetrahedron $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$. If point $D_{1}$ is any point inside $\triangle A B C$, does the conclusion still hold?

Find the maximum positive integer $n$, such that there exist $n$ real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ satisfying for any $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant$ $n$, $\left(1+x_{i} x_{j}\right)^{2} \leqslant 0.99\left(1+x_{i}^{2}\right)\left(1+x_{j}^{2}\right)$.

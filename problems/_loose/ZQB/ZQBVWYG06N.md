The ninth question: Given a positive integer $n$ greater than 1, let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be real numbers, and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^{2}=1$. Try to find the maximum value of $\sum_{\mathrm{k}=1}^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{kx}_{\mathrm{k}}{ }^{2}+\sum_{1 \leq \mathrm{i}<\mathrm{j} \leq \mathrm{n}}(\mathrm{i}+\mathrm{j}) \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{j}}$.

Let $x, y, z$ be real numbers that satisfy the equation $x+y+z=x y+y z+z x$. Prove the inequality

$$
\frac{x}{x^{2}+1}+\frac{y}{y^{2}+1}+\frac{z}{z^{2}+1} \geq-\frac{1}{2}
$$

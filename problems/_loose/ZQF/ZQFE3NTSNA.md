Let $G$ be a group of order $n, n \geq 4$, with the property that there exists $m \in \mathbb{N}, 1<m<n$, such that $G$ contains exactly $C_{n-1}^{m-1}$ subgroups of order $m$. Prove that the group $G$ is abelian.

Marius Tămăuceanu, Recreații Matematice 1/2009

A triangle has an inscribed circle with a radius of $r$ inches. If the perimeter of the triangle is $p$ inches and the area is $k$ square inches, then $\frac{p}{k}$ is
(A) independent of the value of $r$.
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{r}$.
(C) $\frac{2}{\sqrt{r}}$.
(D) $\frac{2}{r}$.
(E) $\frac{r}{2}$.
(18th American High School Mathematics Examination, 1967)

Consider the analogous problem for a $4 \times 4$ board. How many different routes are there from square $\mathrm{S}$ to square $\mathrm{T}$ which pass through every other square exactly once?
4.2 Now consider the analogous problem for a $5 \times 5$ board.
4.3 Now consider the general case of an $n \times n$ board.

NOTE
By considering the problem with a $4 \times 4$ board, you should see that the case where $n$ is even is not difficult. However, the case where $n$ is odd is seemingly much more difficult, and we don't know a general formula for this case. Please let us know if you manage to make any progress with this.

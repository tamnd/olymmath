Consider triangle $\triangle A B C$ where $A B < A C$. Let $D \in (A B, B \in (A D)$ and $E \in (A C, C \in (A E)$ such that $[B D] \equiv [C E]$. The perpendiculars from $B$ and $D$ to the angle bisector of $\angle B A C$ intersect line $A C$ at $F$ and, respectively, $G$.

a) Prove that triangle $\triangle A B F$ is isosceles;

b) Prove that $[F G] \equiv [C E]$.

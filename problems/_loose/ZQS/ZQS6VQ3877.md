Let $M(s)=\mathrm{E} e^{s X}$ be the moment generating function of a non-negative random variable $X$. Show that for all $p, s>0$ the following inequality holds:

$$
\mathrm{E} X^{p} \leqslant\left(\frac{p}{e s}\right)^{p} M(s)
$$

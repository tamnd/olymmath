Let the polynomial $f(x)=a_{0} x^{n}+a_{1} x^{n-1}+a_{2} x^{n-2}+\cdots+a_{n-1} x+$ $a_{n}$ have integer coefficients, and suppose there exists an odd number $\alpha$ and an even number $\beta$, such that $f(\alpha)$ and $f(\beta)$ are both odd. Prove that the equation $f(x)=0$ has no integer roots.
(Shanghai Mathematical Competition, China, 1956)

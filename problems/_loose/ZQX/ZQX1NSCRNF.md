Let $a, b, c, d$ be non-negative reals such that $\frac{1}{a+3}+\frac{1}{b+3}+\frac{1}{c+3}+\frac{1}{d+3}=1$ . Show that there exists a permutation $(x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4)$ of $(a, b, c, d)$ , such that $$ x_1x_2+x_2x_3+x_3x_4+x_4x_1 \geq 4. $$

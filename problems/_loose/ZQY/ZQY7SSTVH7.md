Two-digit numbers, if their first digit is the same, can be easily multiplied by subtracting the units from one of them, adding this to the other, multiplying these two numbers, and then adding the product of the units of the original two numbers. (For example, $23 \cdot 28=20 \cdot 31+3 \cdot 8=644$.) Prove the correctness of this computational method!

On an $8 \times 8$ board, there is a beetle on each square. At a certain moment, the distribution of the beetles on the board changes: each beetle crawls either one square to the left or one square diagonally to the right and down. If a beetle cannot make either move without falling off the board, it stays on its square.

How many squares can become unoccupied at most due to this change?

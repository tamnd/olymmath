A chess master will practice for 77 days to participate in a competition, he plans to play at least one game every day, and at most 12 games per week. Prove that there exists a positive integer $n$ such that he plays 21 games in some consecutive $n$ days during these 77 days. If 21 is changed to 22, does the conclusion still hold?

Consider the set $ S$ of all numbers of the form $ a(n)=n^2+n+1, n \in \mathbb{N}.$ Show that the product $ a(n)a(n+1)$ is in $ S$ for all $ n \in \mathbb{N}$ and give an example of two elements $ s,t$ of $ S$ such that $ s,t \notin S$ .

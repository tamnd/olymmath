$$
\frac{1}{2} x+\frac{2}{2^{2}} x^{2}+\frac{3}{2^{3}} x^{3}+\frac{4}{2^{4}} x^{4}+\ldots+\frac{n}{2^{n}} x^{n}+\ldots
$$

at the points $x=1, x=3, x=-2$.

For positive integer $k$, define $x_k=3k+\sqrt{k^2-1}-2(\sqrt{k^2-k}+\sqrt{k^2+k})$. Then $\sqrt{x_1}+\sqrt{x_2}+\cdots+\sqrt{x_{1681}}=\sqrt{m}-n$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.

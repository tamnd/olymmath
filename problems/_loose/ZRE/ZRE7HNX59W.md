Let both segments connecting the centers of opposite sides of a convex quadrilateral $A B C D$ have the same length. Prove that the diagonals $A C$ and $B D$ are perpendicular to each other and that the following equality holds:

$$
|A B|^{2}+|C D|^{2}=|B C|^{2}+|D A|^{2} .
$$

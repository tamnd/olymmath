Prove:

For every system $(a, b, c, d)$ of positive integers $a, b, c, d$ that satisfy the conditions $a \cdot b = c \cdot d$ and $a + b = c - d$, there exists a right-angled triangle whose side lengths, measured in cm, are all integers and whose area, measured in $\mathrm{cm}^{2}$, has the value $a \cdot b$.

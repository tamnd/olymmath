In the tetrahedron $A_{1} A_{2} A_{3} A_{4}$, the circumradius is $R$, and the volume is $V$. The median plane through vertices $A_{k}, A_{j}$ is $S_{k j}(1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4)$. Prove: $\sum_{1 \leqslant k<j \leqslant 4} \frac{1}{S_{k j}} \leqslant \frac{4 \sqrt{6} R}{3 V}$.

A $n$-sided polygon is circumscribed around a circle. A point inside the circle is connected to all vertices and points of tangency with segments. The resulting triangles are sequentially numbered from 1 to $2n$. Prove that the product of the areas of the triangles with even numbers is equal to the product of the areas of the triangles with odd numbers.

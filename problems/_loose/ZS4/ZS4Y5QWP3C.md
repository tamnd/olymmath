Given a parallelogram $K L M N$, where $K L=8, K N=3 \sqrt{2}+\sqrt{6}$ and $\angle K N L=45^{\circ}$. On side $K L$, a point $A$ is taken such that $K A: A L=3: 1$. Through point $A$, a line parallel to $L M$ is drawn, on which a point $B$ is chosen inside the parallelogram, and a point $C$ is chosen on side $K N$ such that $K C=A B$. Lines $L C$ and $M B$ intersect at point $D$. Find the angle $\angle L A D$.

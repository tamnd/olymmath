A $4 \times 4$ chessboard has one square black, and the rest are white. In one move, we can change the color of all squares in a complete column or a complete row to the opposite color. Can we achieve that after some moves, all squares on the chessboard are white?

What can we say in the case where the initial chessboard is $5 \times 5$?

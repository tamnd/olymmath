Given the line $x+y-k=0(k>0)$ intersects the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=4$ at two distinct points $A$ and $B$, and $O$ is the origin, with $|\overrightarrow{O A}+\overrightarrow{O B}| \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}|\overrightarrow{A B}|$. Then the range of $k$ is ( ).
(A) $(\sqrt{3},+\infty)$
(B) $[\sqrt{2},+\infty)$
(C) $[\sqrt{2}, 2 \sqrt{2})$
(D) $[\sqrt{3}, 2 \sqrt{2})$

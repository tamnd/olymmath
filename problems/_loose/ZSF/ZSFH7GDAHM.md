Suppose that $X_1$ is the point on side $BC$ of $\triangle ABC$ such that $\angle AX_1C=120^\circ$ . Moreover, suppose that $Y_1$ is the point on side $AC$ such that $\angle X_1Y_1C=120^\circ$ . Define the sequences of points $\{X_i\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ and $\{Y_i\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ such that $X_i$ is on side $BC$ and $Y_i$ is on side $AC$ , and where $\angle Y_iX_{i+1}C=\angle X_iY_iC=120^\circ$ for all $i\in\mathbb N$ . If $X_1A:X_1B:X_1C=1:3:1$ and $AY_6=\frac{728}{81}$ , then the length of $AB$ can be written in the form $a\sqrt b$ , where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers and $b$ is square-free. What is $a+b$ ?

Let $[a]$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding $a$, for example: $[8]=8, [3.6]=3$.
Some natural numbers can be expressed in the form $[x]+[2 x]+[3 x]$, such as 6 and 3:
$$
\begin{array}{c}
6=\left[\frac{5}{4}\right]+\left[2 \times \frac{5}{4}\right]+\left[3 \times \frac{5}{4}\right], \\
3=[0.8]+[2 \times 0.8]+[3 \times 0.8] .
\end{array}
$$

Among the 2020 natural numbers from 1 to 2020, there are $ $ numbers that can be expressed in the above form.

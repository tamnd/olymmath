Let $a$ be a positive integer, $3 \mid a, f(1)=a$. Let $f(n+1)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}\sqrt{f(n)}, \sqrt{f(n)} \in \mathbf{Z}, \\ f(n)+3, \sqrt{f(n)} \notin \mathbf{Z},\end{array} \right.$. Prove: There exists $M$ such that $f(n) \leqslant M, n \geqslant 1$.

Prove: If a real-coefficient polynomial $P(x)$ takes only non-negative values for all $x \in R$, then it can be expressed as
$$
P(x)=\left[Q_{1}(x)\right]^{2}+\cdots+\left[Q_{n}(x)\right]^{2},
$$

where $Q_{1}(x), \cdots, Q_{n}(x)$ are real-coefficient polynomials.

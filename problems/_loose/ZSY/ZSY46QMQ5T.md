A positive integer is called *good* if it can be written as a sum of two consecutive positive integers and as a sum of three consecutive positive integers. Prove that:

a)2001 is *good*, but 3001 isn't *good*.
b)the product of two *good* numbers is a *good* number.
c)if the product of two numbers is *good*, then at least one of the numbers is *good*.

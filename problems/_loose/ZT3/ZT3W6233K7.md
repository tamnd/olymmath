Let $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ be a rectangular parallelepiped and $\mathrm{M}$ and $\mathrm{N}$ be the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from $A^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ to BD. The lengths of the edges $A B, B C$ and $A A^{\prime}$ are equal to $\sqrt{6}, \sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{2}$, respectively.

a) Prove that $A^{\prime} M \perp C^{\prime} N$.

b) Calculate the measure of the angle between the planes $\left(A^{\prime} M C\right)$ and $\left(A N C^{\prime}\right)$.

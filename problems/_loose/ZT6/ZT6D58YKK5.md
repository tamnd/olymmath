Let the sequence of positive numbers $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ satisfy $\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j} \geqslant \sqrt{n}$, prove that for $n \geqslant 1$, $\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}^{2}>\frac{1}{4} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k}$.

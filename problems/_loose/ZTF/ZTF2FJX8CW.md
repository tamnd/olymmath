From the five numbers $-3, -2, \frac{1}{2}, 1, 3$, a number is randomly selected and denoted as $a$. If the number $a$ makes the system of inequalities
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
\frac{1}{3}(2 x+7) \geqslant 3, \\
x-a<0
\end{array}\right.
$$

have no solution, and makes the fractional equation
$$
\frac{x}{x-3}-\frac{a-2}{3-x}=-1
$$

have a positive integer solution, then the sum of all values of $a$ that satisfy the conditions among these five numbers is ( ).
(A) -5
(B) -2
(C) 1
(D) $\frac{3}{2}$

Prove that for any positive number $x$ and any natural number $n$, the following inequality holds:

$$
\frac{x}{1+x^{2}}+\frac{x}{2^{2}+x^{2}}+\ldots+\frac{x}{n^{2}+x^{2}}<\frac{\pi}{2}
$$

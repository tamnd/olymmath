Let set $M$ consist of $k$ non-overlapping segments on a line, and for any segment of length no more than 1, it can be placed on the line such that both endpoints belong to $M$. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the $k$ segments that make up set $M$ is no less than $\frac{1}{k}$.

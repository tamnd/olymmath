Through the midpoint of segment $CD$, a line is drawn intersecting sides $AC$ and $BC$ at points $M$ and $N$ respectively. It is known that $\angle C = 120^\circ$, the area of triangle $MCN$ is $3\sqrt{3}$, and the distance from point $M$ to line $AB$ is twice the distance from point $N$ to the same line.

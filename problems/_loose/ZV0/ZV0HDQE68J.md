For a regular $2n$-sided polygon, we write an integer on each vertex such that the difference between the numbers written on adjacent vertices is 1. We call a number a peak number if it is greater than both of its neighbors, and a valley number if it is smaller than both of its neighbors. Prove that the sum of the peak numbers minus the sum of the valley numbers equals $n$.

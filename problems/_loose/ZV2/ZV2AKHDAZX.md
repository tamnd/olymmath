Given positive integers $a_1$, $a_2$, $...$, $a_m$ ($m \ge 1$). Consider the sequence $\{u_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, with $$u_n = a_1^n + a_2^n + ... + a_m^n.$$ We know that this sequence has a finite number of prime divisors. Prove that $a_1 = a_2 = ...= a_m$.

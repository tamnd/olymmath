In parallelogram $\mathrm{ABCD}$ we have $\mathrm{AB}=4, \mathrm{BD}=3, \mathrm{BC}=2$. Let $\mathrm{G}=$ the centroid of $\triangle \mathrm{ABD}$, $\mathrm{I}=$ the incenter of $\triangle \mathrm{BCD}$, and $\mathrm{M} \in(\mathrm{BC})$ such that $\overrightarrow{B M}=2 \overrightarrow{M C}$. Show that G, I, M are collinear.

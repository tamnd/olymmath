Given an arbitrary parallelogram $A B C D$ and an arbitrary length $e(e>0)$.

Construct, while retaining the side $A B$, a parallelogram $A B C_{1} D_{1}$ that is equal in area to $A B C D$, lies on the same side of the line through $A$ and $B$ as $A B C D$, and whose diagonal $A C_{1}$ has the given length $e$!

Describe and justify your construction!

Determine whether a parallelogram of the required type can always be uniquely constructed from the given pieces! (An investigation into whether two potentially different parallelograms are congruent is not required here.)

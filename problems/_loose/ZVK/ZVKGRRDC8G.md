(a) $f(1) = 0$ ,
(b) $f(x) > 0$ for all $x > 1$ ,
(c) For all $x, y\ge 0$ with $x + y > 0$ holds $$ f(xf(y))f(y) = f\left( \frac{xy}{x + y}\right) $$

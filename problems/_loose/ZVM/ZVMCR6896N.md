Let $I$ be the centre of the inscribed circle of the non-isosceles triangle $A B C$, and let the circle touch the sides $B C, C A, A B$ at the points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ respectively. Prove that the centres of the circumcircles of $\triangle A I A_{1}, \triangle B I B_{1}$ and $\triangle C I C_{1}$ are collinear.

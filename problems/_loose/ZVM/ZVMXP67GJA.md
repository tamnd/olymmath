The streets of the city of Duzhinsk - simple broken lines, not intersecting each other at internal points. Each street connects two intersections and is painted in one of three colors: white, red, or blue. At each intersection, exactly three streets meet, one of each color. An intersection is called positive if, when walking around it counterclockwise, the colors of the streets appear in the following order: white, blue, red, and negative in the opposite case. Prove that the difference between the number of positive and the number of negative intersections is divisible by 4.

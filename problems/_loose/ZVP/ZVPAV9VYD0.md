Let $N$ be a positive integer such that the sum of the squares of all positive divisors of $N$ is exactly equal to $N(N+3)$. Prove: there exist two indices $i, j$ such that $N=F_{i} \cdot F_{j}$, where $\left\{F_{n}\right\}(n \geqslant 1)$ is the Fibonacci sequence.

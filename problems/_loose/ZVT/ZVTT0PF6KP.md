Igor and Pasha are playing a game, taking turns placing natural numbers at the vertices of a regular hexagon (each can choose any free vertex and place any natural number in it). After six moves, when the game ends, the judge writes the product of the numbers at the two ends of each side of the hexagon on each side. Then all 12 numbers are summed. If the sum is odd, Igor wins, and if it is even, Pasha wins.

It is known that Igor moves first. Who of the players can win regardless of the opponent's actions and how should they act?

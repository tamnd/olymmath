Let $n$ be a positive integer divisible by $4$ . Find the number of permutations $\sigma$ of $(1,2,3,\cdots,n)$ which satisfy the condition $\sigma(j)+\sigma^{-1}(j)=n+1$ for all $j \in \{1,2,3,\cdots,n\}$ .

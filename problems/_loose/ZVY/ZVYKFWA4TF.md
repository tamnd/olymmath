Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $\Omega$ its circumcircle. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $A$. The angle bisector from vertex $A$ intersects the segment $[BC]$ at point $P$ and intersects the circle $\Omega$ again at point $S$. Let $A'$ be the point diametrically opposite to vertex $A$ on the circle $\Omega$. Prove that the lines $(SD)$ and $\left(A'P\right)$ intersect on the circle $\Omega$.

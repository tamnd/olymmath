Construct a triangle $PBC$ on the same side of $BC$ as $A$, such that $\triangle PBC \sim \triangle DEF$. Connect $PD_{1}$. Prove that $EF$, $E_{1}F_{1}$, and $PD_{1}$ are concurrent.

The Sages' Test. A certain ruler, wishing to test three of his sages, said to them: "Before you are five caps: three black and two white. You will each be given a cap to wear. The one who first guesses the color of the cap on his head will receive a reward." Then the sages' eyes were blindfolded and a cap was placed on each of their heads. After the blindfolds were removed, the sages remained silent for a long time. Finally, one of them said: "I have a black cap on me!" How did this sage reason?

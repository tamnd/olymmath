(Reunion of Subgroups) Let $(\Gamma, *)$ be a group and $G, H$ be two subgroups of $\Gamma$. Show that $G \cup H$ is a subgroup of $\Gamma$ if and only if $G \subseteq H$ or $H \subseteq G$.

Definition 69. (Subgroup generated) Let $(G, *)$ be a group and $S \subseteq G$ a subset of $G$. The subgroup of $G$ generated by $S$ is the intersection of all subgroups $H$ of $G$ such that $S \subseteq H$. It is commonly denoted by $\langle S\rangle$ - or $\left\langle s_{1}, s_{2}, \ldots, s_{n}\right\rangle$ if $S=\left\{s_{1}, s_{2}, \ldots, s_{n}\right\}$ is a finite set.

In a right-angled Cartesian $x, y$-coordinate system, the following are given:

The points $M(0 ; 0), F_{1}(2 ; 0), F_{2}(-2 ; 0), A(4 ; 0), B(0 ; 2 \sqrt{3})$, the line $g$ with the equation $x=-8$, the circle $k_{1}$ centered at $M$ passing through $A$, and the circle $k_{2}$ centered at $M$ passing through $B$.

By the ellipse with foci $F_{1}, F_{2}$ and semi-major axis length $\overline{M A}$, we mean the set $E_{1}$ of all points $P(x ; y)$ for which $\overline{F_{1} P}+\overline{F_{2} P}=2 \cdot \overline{M A}$ holds.

By the ellipse with focus $F_{2}$, directrix $g$, and eccentricity 1/2, we mean the set $E_{2}$ of all points $P(x ; y)$ with the following property: If $Q$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to $g$, then $\overline{F_{2} P}=\frac{1}{2} \cdot \overline{P Q}$.

By the ellipse through $A$ with major vertex circle $k_{1}$ and minor vertex circle $k_{2}$, we mean the set $E_{3}$ of all points $P(x ; y)$ that can be obtained by the following construction: Draw any ray from $M$. It intersects $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ at points $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$, respectively. The line parallel to the $y$-axis through $R_{1}$ and the line parallel to the $x$-axis through $R_{2}$ intersect at $P$.

Prove that the three point sets $E_{1}, E_{2}, E_{3}$ are equal to each other!

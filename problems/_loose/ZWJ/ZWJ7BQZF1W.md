(Adapted from the 1994 Shanghai College Entrance Examination) Given
$$
S(t)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}
2\left(1-t+t^{2}-t^{3}\right)\left(0<t<\frac{1}{2}\right), \\
\frac{1}{2}\left(t+\frac{1}{t}\right)\left(t \geqslant \frac{1}{2}\right) .
\end{array}\right.
$$

Determine the monotonic intervals of $S(t)$, and provide a proof.

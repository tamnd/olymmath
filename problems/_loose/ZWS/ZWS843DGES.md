Let the plane angles of a trihedral angle be $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$, and the dihedral angles opposite to them be $A$, $B$, and $C$. Prove that the following equalities hold:
1)

$$
\frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin A}=\frac{\sin \beta}{\sin B}=\frac{\sin \gamma}{\sin C}
$$

(Sine theorem for a trihedral angle),
2) $\cos \alpha=\cos \beta \cos \gamma+\sin \beta \sin \gamma \cos A$ (1st Cosine theorem for a trihedral angle),
3) $\cos A=-\cos B \cos C+\sin B \sin C \cos \alpha$ (2nd Cosine theorem for a trihedral angle).

Let $O$ be the center of an ellipse, $A$ be any point on the ellipse other than the vertices, draw a perpendicular from $A$ to the major axis, with the foot of the perpendicular being $M$, connect $A O$ and extend it to intersect the ellipse at another point $B$, connect $B M$ and extend it to intersect the ellipse at point $C$. Question: Does there exist an ellipse such that $B A \perp C A$?

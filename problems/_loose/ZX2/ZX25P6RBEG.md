If $ x < a < 0$ means that $ x$ and $ a$ are numbers such that $ x$ is less than $ a$ and $ a$ is less than zero, then:

$ \textbf{(A)}\ x^2 < ax < 0 \textbf{(B)}\ x^2 > ax > a^2 \textbf{(C)}\ x^2 < a^2 < 0$
$ \textbf{(D)}\ x^2 > ax\text{ but }ax < 0 \textbf{(E)}\ x^2 > a^2\text{ but }a^2 < 0$

Anthropologists, during their research on the archipelago "Kuku-Muku," discovered the existence of a primitive society with three marital groups $J, G$, and $R$. In this society, the following rules were in effect:
1) A man and a woman can marry if and only if they belong to the same group.
2) Sons of parents who belong to group $J$ belong to group $R$; daughters, on the other hand, belong to group $G$.
3) Sons of parents who belong to group $G$ belong to group $J$; daughters, on the other hand, belong to group $R$.
4) Sons of parents who belong to group $R$ belong to group $G$; daughters belong to group $J$.

a) To which groups can the grandchildren of a married couple from group $J$ belong?

b) Which of the following types of aunts can a person marry:
I. Sister of his mother
II. Sister of his father

III. Widow of his father's brother.

c) In accordance with the rules of the society, can a person marry his granddaughter (considering a granddaughter from a sister, brother, etc., not a granddaughter from a child)?

In a regular $n(n \geqslant 3)$-sided pyramid $P-A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, $O$ is the center of the base regular $n$-sided polygon $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$, and $B$ is the midpoint of the edge $A_{1} A_{n}$.
(1) Prove: $P O^{2} \sin ^{2} \frac{\pi}{n}+P A_{1}^{2} \cos ^{2} \frac{\pi}{n}=P B^{2}$;
(2) Let the angle between the lateral edge and the base of the regular $n$-sided pyramid $P-A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{n}$ be $\alpha$, and the angle between the lateral face and the base be $\beta$. Determine the relationship between $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \cos \angle A_{i} P B$ and $\sin \alpha \cdot \sin \beta$, and prove it.

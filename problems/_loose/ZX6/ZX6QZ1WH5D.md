A regular hexagon and a regular triangle with the same side length have their planes parallel. The nine vertices, when properly grouped, define three additional regular triangles and three squares. Show that the volume of the convex body bounded by these eight faces is also given by the following formula:

$$
V=\frac{m}{6}\left(t_{6}+4 T+t_{3}\right)
$$

where $m$ is the distance between the two parallel faces, $t_{6}$ and $t_{3}$ are the areas of these two faces, and $T$ is the area of the cross-section of the body by a plane perpendicular to the height and passing through its midpoint.

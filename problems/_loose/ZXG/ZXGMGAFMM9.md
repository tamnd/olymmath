All cells of an $n\times n$ table are painted in several colors so that there is no monochromatic $2\times2$ square. A sequence of different cells $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k$ is called a *colorful* if any two consecutive cells are adjacent and are painted in different colors. What is the largest $k{}$ for which there is a colorful sequence of length $k{}$ regardless of the coloring of the cells of the table?

*Proposed by N. Belukhov*

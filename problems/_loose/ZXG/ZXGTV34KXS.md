We place a material point at each vertex of an acute-angled triangle, the mass of the point being proportional to the sine of the angle at the respective vertex. Let us find the position of the center of mass of the point system. What is the geometric meaning of the center of mass? - Examine the question in two further cases: the mass is proportional to the sine of twice the corresponding angle, or the mass is proportional to the tangent of the corresponding angle.

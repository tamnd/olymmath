Let $A_{1} A_{2} \ldots A_{n}$ be a convex polygon and let $p_{i}$ be the length of its orthogonal projection on the line $A_{i} A_{i+1}, 1 \leq i \leq n\left(A_{n+1} \equiv A_{1}\right)$. Prove that if $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{A_{i} A_{i+1}}{p_{i}}=4$, then the polygon is a rectangle.

For any region $D$ on the Cartesian plane, let $N(D)$ denote the number of all lattice points in $D$, and $n$ is a natural number greater than 1.
(1) Let $A$ be the region enclosed by the curve $y=x^{2}$ and the lines $x=n$ and $y=1$ (including the boundaries). Find $N(A)$.
(2) Let $B$ be the region in the first quadrant enclosed by the curve $y=x^{2}$ and the lines $x=1$ and $y=n^{2}$ (including the boundaries). Find $N(B)$.
(3) Find $\sum_{k=1}^{n^{2}}[\sqrt{k}]$.

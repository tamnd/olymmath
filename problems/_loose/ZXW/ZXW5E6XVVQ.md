Given a triangle $ABC$. Let $O$ be the point of intersection of its medians, and $M, N$, and $P$ be points on sides $AB, BC$, and $CA$, respectively, dividing these sides in the same ratio (i.e., $AM: MB = BN: NC = CP: PA = p: q$). Prove that:

a) $O$ is the point of intersection of the medians of triangle $MNP$;

b) $O$ is the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle formed by the lines $AN, BP$, and $CM$.

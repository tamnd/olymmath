$A, B$ are two moving points on the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{9}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$, satisfying $\overrightarrow{O A} \cdot \overrightarrow{O B}=0$.
(1) Prove that $\frac{1}{|\overrightarrow{O A}|^{2}}+\frac{1}{|\overrightarrow{O B}|^{2}}$ is a constant;
(2) A moving point $P$ is on the line segment $A B$, satisfying $\overrightarrow{O P} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}=0$, prove that point $P$ lies on a fixed circle.

A class has $N$ students and they study $M$ subjects, but not every subject (e.g., languages, physical education exemptions, etc.). Let the final grade of the $I$-th student in the $J$-th subject be $X(I, J)$, which is 0 if the student does not study the corresponding subject. Create a block diagram (program) that calculates the average grade for each student and the class average for each subject.

(An identity due to Ramanujan) We will show that $p(5 n+4)$ is divisible by 5 for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

$\triangleright$ Calculate $(q)_{\infty}^{3}$,

$\triangleright$ show that $\left[q^{5 n+4}\right]\left((q)_{\infty}^{4}\right)$ is divisible by 5 for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$\Delta$ show that $\frac{1-q^{5}}{(1-q)^{5}} \equiv 1(\bmod 5)$,

$\triangleright$ deduce the desired relation.

Show that for all $n \geqslant 2$, there exists a set $A=\left\{a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots a_{n}\right\}$ of $n$ distinct integers all $\geqslant 2$ such that for any integer $k$ such that $1 \leqslant k \leqslant n$ the product of the $n-1$ integers in A other than $a_{k}$ is congruent to 1 or -1 modulo $a_{k}$.

Four. (50 points) The plane is completely covered by equilateral triangles with side length 1 (there are no gaps between the triangles, and they do not overlap). Each side is called a grid line, and each vertex is called a grid point. Two grid points connected by a grid line are called adjacent (each grid point has exactly six adjacent grid points). Initially, a chess piece is placed on one grid point. It is allowed to remove a chess piece from a grid point, but at the same time, a chess piece must be placed on each of the two adjacent grid points (if there are no chess pieces on these grid points). Prove: There is always a chess piece on the plane whose distance from the initial chess piece does not exceed 6.

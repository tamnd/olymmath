Prove that for $m \neq n$ the following equalities hold:

a) $\left(a^{m}-1, a^{n}-1\right)=a^{(m, n)}-1(a>1)$;

b) $\left(f_{n}, f_{m}\right)=1$, where $f_{k}=2^{2^{k}}+1-$ Fermat numbers.

(PDR, 79). Prove that for any polynomial $P(x)$ of degree $n>1$, having $n$ distinct real roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$, the following equality holds

$$
\frac{1}{P^{\prime}\left(x_{1}\right)}+\frac{1}{P^{\prime}\left(x_{2}\right)}+\ldots+\frac{1}{P^{\prime}\left(x_{n}\right)}=0
$$

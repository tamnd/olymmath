Who is he?

The philosopher successfully passed all three trials of the first round and was admitted to the second round. This time, the trials were conducted over three days in another room, no smaller than the first, known as the Middle Sanctuary. At the center of the sanctuary, on platinum thrones, sat two figures shrouded in precious cloaks. Those sitting on the thrones uttered one spell each, and the philosopher had to determine who had uttered each spell: a human or a monkey, a knight or a liar. For convenience, we will denote the ones sitting on the thrones as A and B.

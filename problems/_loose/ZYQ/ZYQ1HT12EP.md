Given a plane with two traces and two parallel lines $(a, c)$, and three points $(A, B, C)$ such that $A-\bullet-a$ and $C-\bullet-c$. Construct a line passing through point $B$ and lying in the plane, which intersects $a$ at $X$ and $c$ at $Y$ such that $\overline{A X}: \overline{C Y}=m: n!$ ( $m$ and $n$ are given.)

$(A-\bullet-a$ means: point $A$ lies on line $a$. - The construction $)$

(Problem from the 34th Russian Mathematical Olympiad) Find all integers $n(n>1)$ that satisfy the following condition: there exist not all equal positive integers $b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}$, such that for any given positive integer $k$, there exist positive integers $a, b(a, b>1)$ satisfying $\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(b_{i}+k\right)=a^{b}$.

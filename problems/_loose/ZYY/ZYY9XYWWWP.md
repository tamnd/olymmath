Let $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{n}$ be the vertices of a regular $n$-gon, and let us write the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n$ on these vertices in some order.

a) Prove that the sum of the absolute values of the differences between consecutive numbers is at least $2 n-2$.

b) How many such arrangements are there in which the above sum is exactly $2 n-2 ?(\mathbf{H})$

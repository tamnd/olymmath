$m>1$ is an integer such that $[2m-\sqrt{m}+1, 2m]$ contains a prime. Prove that for any pairwise distinct positive integers $a_1$ , $a_2$ , $\dots$ , $a_m$ , there is always $1\leq i,j\leq m$ such that $\frac{a_i}{(a_i, a_j)}\geq m$ .

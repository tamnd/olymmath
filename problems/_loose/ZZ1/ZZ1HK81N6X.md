A person cuts a meat pie in the following way: first, cut out a sector with a central angle of $\alpha$, flip it over, and embed it back in place. Then rotate the entire meat pie around its center by an angle $\beta$, given that $\beta<\alpha<180^{\circ}$. Next, cut out a sector with a central angle of $\alpha$ at the same place (not rotating with the meat pie), flip it over and embed it back in place, and rotate the entire meat pie in the same direction by an angle $\beta$, and so on. Try to prove that after a finite number of such operations, all points on the meat pie will simultaneously return to their original positions.

Let $\Gamma$ be a circle and $A, B, C, D, E$ and $F$ be points on $\Gamma$. Let $P, Q$ and $R$ be the points of intersection of $(A B)$ and $(D E)$, $(B C)$ and $(E F)$, and $(C D)$ and $(F A)$, respectively.

Show Pascal's theorem, i.e., that $P, Q$ and $R$ are collinear.

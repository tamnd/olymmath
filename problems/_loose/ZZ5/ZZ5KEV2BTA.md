In the Cartesian coordinate system $x O y$, with the origin $O$ as the center, two circles are drawn with radii $a$ and $b$ ($a > b > 0$). Point $Q$ is the intersection of the radius $O P$ of the larger circle with the smaller circle. A perpendicular line $A N \perp O x$ is drawn from point $P$ to the x-axis, with the foot of the perpendicular being $N$. A perpendicular line $Q M \perp P N$ is drawn from point $Q$ to $P N$, with the foot of the perpendicular being $M$. Let the trajectory of point $M$ as the radius $O P$ rotates around point $O$ be the curve $E$.
(1) Find the equation of the curve $E$;
(2) Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be three points on the curve $E$, and satisfy $\overrightarrow{O A} + \overrightarrow{O B} + \overrightarrow{O C} = \overrightarrow{0}$. Find the area of $\triangle A B C$.

There are 8 white pawns on the squares at one edge of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard and 8 black pawns on the squares at the opposite edge. On each move, a player shifts one of his pawns by one or more squares forward (toward the opponent's piece) or backward, but moving a pawn to a square containing the opponent's pawn or over such a square is prohibited. Moves are performed alternately, with white starting. The player who cannot make a move loses. Which player has a winning strategy?

Let the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ be circle $O$, and $P$ be a point inside $\triangle ABC$. Connect $AP$ and extend it to intersect circle $O$ at point $D$; connect $BP$ and extend it to intersect circle $O$ at point $E$; connect $CP$ and extend it to intersect circle $O$ at point $F$. Let the reflection of $A$ over line $EF$ be $A_{1}$, and the reflection of $B$ over line $DF$ be $B_{1}$. Prove that $\triangle PAB \sim \triangle PA_{1}B_{1}$.

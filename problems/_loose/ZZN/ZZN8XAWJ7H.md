Points $A^{\prime}, B^{\prime}$, and $C^{\prime}$ are symmetric to some point $P$ with respect to the sides $B C, C A$, and $A B$ of triangle $A B C$.

a) Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A B^{\prime} C^{\prime}, A^{\prime} B C^{\prime}$, $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C$, and $A B C$ have a common point.

b) Prove that the circumcircles of triangles $A^{\prime} B C, A B^{\prime} C$, $A B C^{\prime}$, and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ have a common point $Q$.
c) Let $I, J, K$, and $O$ be the centers of the circumcircles of triangles $A^{\prime} B C, A B^{\prime} C, A B C^{\prime}$, and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$. Prove that $Q I: O I = Q J: O J = Q K: O K$.

See also problems $28.33, 28.37$.

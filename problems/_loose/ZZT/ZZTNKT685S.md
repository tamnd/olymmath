Let $a, b, c$ be integers such that $a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$. Show that there exists a non-zero integer $d$ such that the equation $(a+d)^{2}+(b+d)^{2}=(c+d)^{2}$ also holds. Based on this, write down the positive integer solutions of the equation $x^{2}+y^{2}=z^{2}$ that can be derived in one step from the equality $9+16=25$.

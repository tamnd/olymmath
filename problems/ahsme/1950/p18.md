(1) $a(x-y)=ax-ay$
(2) $a^{x-y}=a^x-a^y$
(3) $\log (x-y)=\log x-\log y$
(4) $\frac{\log x}{\log y}=\log{x}-\log{y}$
(5) $a(xy)=ax \cdot ay$
$\textbf{(A)}\text{Only 1 and 4 are true} \\\textbf{(B)}\ \text{Only 1 and 5 are true} \\\textbf{(C)}\ \text{Only 1 and 3 are true} \\\textbf{(D)}\ \text{Only 1 and 2 are true} \\\textbf{(E)}\ \text{Only 1 is true}$

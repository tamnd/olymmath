The lengths of two line segments are $a$ units and $b$ units respectively. Then the correct relation between them is:
$\textbf{(A)}\ \frac{a+b}{2} > \sqrt{ab} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac{a+b}{2} < \sqrt{ab} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac{a+b}{2}=\sqrt{ab}\\ \textbf{(D)}\ \frac{a+b}{2}\leq\sqrt{ab} \textbf{(E)}\ \frac{a+b}{2}\geq\sqrt{ab}$

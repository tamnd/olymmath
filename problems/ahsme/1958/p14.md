$\textbf{(A)}\ b = g \textbf{(B)}\ b = \frac{g}{5} \textbf{(C)}\ b = g - 4 \textbf{(D)}\ b = g - 5 \\ \textbf{(E)}\ \text{It is impossible to determine a relation between }{b}\text{ and }{g}\text{ without knowing }{b + g.}$

The roots of $Ax^2 + Bx + C = 0$ are $r$ and $s$. For the roots of
$x^2+px +q =0$
to be $r^2$ and $s^2$, $p$ must equal:
$\textbf{(A)}\ \frac{B^2 - 4AC}{A^2} \textbf{(B)}\ \frac{B^2 - 2AC}{A^2} \textbf{(C)}\ \frac{2AC - B^2}{A^2} \\ \textbf{(D)}\ B^2 - 2C \textbf{(E)}\ 2C - B^2$

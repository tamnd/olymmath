In $\triangle ABC$ with $\overline{AB}=\overline{AC}=3.6$ , a point $D$ is taken on $AB$ at a distance $1.2$ from $A$ . Point $D$ is joined to $E$ in the prolongation of $AC$ so that $\triangle AED$ is equal in area to $ABC$ . Then $\overline{AE}$ is: $\textbf{(A)}\ 4.8 \textbf{(B)}\ 5.4 \textbf{(C)}\ 7.2 \textbf{(D)}\ 10.8 \textbf{(E)}\ 12.6$

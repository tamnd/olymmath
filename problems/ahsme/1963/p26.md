Consider the statements:
$\textbf{(1)}\ p\text{ }\wedge\sim q\wedge r \textbf{(2)}\ \sim p\text{ }\wedge\sim q\wedge r \textbf{(3)}\ p\text{ }\wedge\sim q\text{ }\wedge\sim r \textbf{(4)}\ \sim p\text{ }\wedge q\text{ }\wedge r$
where $p,q$, and $r$ are propositions. How many of these imply the truth of $(p\rightarrow q)\rightarrow r$?
$\textbf{(A)}\ 0 \textbf{(B)}\ 1 \textbf{(C)}\ 2 \textbf{(D)}\ 3 \textbf{(E)}\ 4$

If $n$ is a multiple of $4$, the sum $s=1+2i+3i^2+\cdots+(n+1)i^n$, where $i=\sqrt{-1}$, equals:
$\textbf{(A) }1+i \textbf{(B) }\frac{1}{2}(n+2) \textbf{(C) }\frac{1}{2}(n+2-ni) $
$\textbf{(D) }\frac{1}{2}[(n+1)(1-i)+2] \textbf{(E) }\frac{1}{8}(n^2+8-4ni)$

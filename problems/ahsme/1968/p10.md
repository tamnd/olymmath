Assume that, for a certain school, it is true that
I: Some students are not honest.
II: All fraternity members are honest.
A necessary conclusion is:
$\text{(A) Some students are fraternity members.} \\ \text{(B) Some fraternity member are not students.} \\ \text{(C) Some students are not fraternity members.} \\ \text{(D) No fraternity member is a student.} \\ \text{(E) No student is a fraternity member.}$

$\textbf{(A)} \text{ a circle with one point deleted} \\ \textbf{(B)} \text{ a circle if the distance from } P \text{ to the center of } K \text{ is less than one half the radius of } K; \\ \text{otherwise a circular arc of less than } 360^{\circ} \\ \textbf{(C)} \text{ a semicircle with one point deleted} \\ \textbf{(D)} \text{ a semicircle} \textbf{(E)} \text{ a circle}$

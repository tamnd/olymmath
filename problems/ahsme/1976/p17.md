If $\theta$ is an acute angle, and $\sin 2\theta=a$ , then $\sin\theta+\cos\theta$ equals
$\textbf{(A) }\sqrt{a+1} \textbf{(B) }(\sqrt{2}-1)a+1 \textbf{(C) }\sqrt{a+1}-\sqrt{a^2-a} \\ \textbf{(D) }\sqrt{a+1}+\sqrt{a^2-a} \textbf{(E) }\sqrt{a+1}+a^2-a$

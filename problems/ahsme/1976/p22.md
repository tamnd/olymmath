Given an equilateral triangle with side of length $s$, consider the locus of all points $\mathit{P}$ in the plane of the
triangle such that the sum of the squares of the distances from $\mathit{P}$ to the vertices of the triangle is a fixed number $a$. This locus
$\textbf{(A) }\text{is a circle if }a>s^2 \\ \textbf{(B) }\text{contains only three points if }a=2s^2\text{ and is a circle if }a>2s^2 \\ \textbf{(C) }\text{is a circle with positive radius only if }s^2<a<2s^2 \\ \textbf{(D) }\text{contains only a finite number of points for any value of }a \\ \textbf{(E) }\text{is none of these}$

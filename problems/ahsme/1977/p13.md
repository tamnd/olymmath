If $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ is a sequence of positive numbers such that $a_{n+2}=a_na_{n+1}$ for all positive integers $n$,
then the sequence $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ is a geometric progression
$\textbf{(A) }\text{for all positive values of }a_1\text{ and }a_2 \\ \textbf{(B) }\text{if and only if }a_1=a_2 \\ \textbf{(C) }\text{if and only if }a_1=1 \\ \textbf{(D) }\text{if and only if }a_2=1 \\ \textbf{(E) }\text{if and only if }a_1=a_2=1$

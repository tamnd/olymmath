Consider the graphs $y=Ax^2$ and $y^2+3=x^2+4y$, where $A$ is a positive constant and $x$ and $y$ are real variables. In how many points do the two graphs intersect?
$\mathrm{(A) \ }\text{exactly }4 \mathrm{(B) \ }\text{exactly }2 $
$\mathrm{(C) \ }\text{at least }1,\text{ but the number varies for different positive values of }A $
$\mathrm{(D) \ }0\text{ for at least one positive value of }A \mathrm{(E) \ }\text{none of these}$

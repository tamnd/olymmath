An unfair coin has probability $p$ of coming up heads on a single toss.
Let $w$ be the probability that, in $5$ independent toss of this coin,
heads come up exactly $3$ times. If $w = 144 / 625$, then
$\textbf{(A)}\ p\text{ must be }\tfrac{2}{5} \textbf{(B)}\ p\text{ must be }\tfrac{3}{5} \\ \textbf{(C)}\ p\text{ must be greater than }\tfrac{3}{5} \textbf{(D)}\ p\text{ is not uniquely determined} \\ \textbf{(E)}\ \text{there is no value of } p \text{ for which }w =\tfrac{144}{625}$

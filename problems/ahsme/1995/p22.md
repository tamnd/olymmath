A [pentagon](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Pentagon) is formed by cutting a triangular corner from a [rectangular](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Rectangle) piece of paper. The five sides of the pentagon have lengths $13, 19, 20, 25$ and $31$, although this is not necessarily their order around the pentagon. The area of the pentagon is
$\mathrm{(A) \ 459 } \mathrm{(B) \ 600 } \mathrm{(C) \ 680 } \mathrm{(D) \ 720 } \mathrm{(E) \ 745 }$

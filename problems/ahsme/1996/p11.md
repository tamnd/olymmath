Find the area of the region consisting of all such line segments.
$\text{(A)}\ \frac{\pi} 4 \text{(B)}\ 4-\pi \text{(C)}\ \frac{\pi} 2 \text{(D)}\ \pi \text{(E)}\ 2\pi$

$$f(n) =\begin{cases}n+3 &\text{if n is odd}\\ \ n/2 &\text{if n is even}\end{cases}$$

$\textbf{(A)}\ 3 \textbf{(B)}\ 6 \textbf{(C)}\ 9 \textbf{(D)}\ 12 \textbf{(E)}\ 15$

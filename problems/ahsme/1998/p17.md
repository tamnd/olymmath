Let $f(x)$ be a [function](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Function) with the two properties:

(a) for any two real numbers $x$ and $y$, $f(x+y) = x + f(y)$, and
(b) $f(0) = 2$.
What is the value of $f(1998)$?
$\mathrm{(A)}\ 0 \mathrm{(B)}\ 2 \mathrm{(C)}\ 1996 \mathrm{(D)}\ 1998 \mathrm{(E)}\ 2000$

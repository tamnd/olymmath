In triangle $ABC$, angle $C$ is a [right angle](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Right_angle) and $CB > CA$. Point $D$ is located on $\overline{BC}$ so that angle $CAD$ is twice angle $DAB$. If $AC/AD = 2/3$, then $CD/BD = m/n$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
$\mathrm{(A) \ }10 \mathrm{(B) \ }14 \mathrm{(C) \ }18 \mathrm{(D) \ }22 \mathrm{(E) \ } 26$

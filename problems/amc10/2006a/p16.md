A circle of radius $1$ is [tangent](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Tangent) to a circle of radius $2$. The sides of $\triangle ABC$ are tangent to the circles as shown, and the sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ are congruent. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?
[asy] size(200); pathpen = linewidth(0.7); pointpen = black; real t=2^0.5; D((0,0)--(4*t,0)--(2*t,8)--cycle); D(CR((2*t,2),2)); D(CR((2*t,5),1)); D('B', (0,0),SW); D('C',(4*t,0), SE); D('A', (2*t, 8), N); D((2*t,2)--(2*t,4)); D((2*t,5)--(2*t,6)); MP('2', (2*t,3), W); MP('1',(2*t, 5.5), W);[/asy]
$\textbf{(A) } \frac{35}{2} \textbf{(B) } 15\sqrt{2} \textbf{(C) } \frac{64}{3} \textbf{(D) } 16\sqrt{2} \textbf{(E) } 24 $

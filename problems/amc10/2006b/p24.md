[Circles](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Circle) with centers $O$ and $P$ have radii $2$ and $4$, respectively, and are externally tangent. Points $A$ and $B$ on the circle with center $O$ and points $C$ and $D$ on the circle with center $P$ are such that $AD$ and $BC$ are common external tangents to the circles. What is the area of the [concave](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Concave) [hexagon](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Hexagon) $AOBCPD$?

$\mathrm{(A) \ } 18\sqrt{3} \mathrm{(B) \ } 24\sqrt{2} \mathrm{(C) \ } 36 \mathrm{(D) \ } 24\sqrt{3} \mathrm{(E) \ } 32\sqrt{2}$

For how many such labelings is the sum of the labels on the edges of each of the $6$ faces of the cube equal to $2$?
$\textbf{(A) } 8 \textbf{(B) } 10 \textbf{(C) } 12 \textbf{(D) } 16 \textbf{(E) } 20$

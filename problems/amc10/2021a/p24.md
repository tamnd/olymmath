$\textbf{(A)} ~\frac{8a^2}{(a+1)^2} \textbf{(B)} ~\frac{4a}{a+1} \textbf{(C)} ~\frac{8a}{a+1} \textbf{(D)} ~\frac{8a^2}{a^2+1} \textbf{(E)} ~\frac{8a}{a^2+1}$

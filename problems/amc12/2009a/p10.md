In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $AB = 5$, $BC = 17$, $CD = 5$, $DA = 9$, and $BD$ is an integer. What is $BD$?
<center>

```asy
unitsize(4mm);
defaultpen(linewidth(.8pt)+fontsize(8pt));
dotfactor=4;

pair C=(0,0), B=(17,0);
pair D=intersectionpoints(Circle(C,5),Circle(B,13))[0];
pair A=intersectionpoints(Circle(D,9),Circle(B,5))[0];
pair[] dotted={A,B,C,D};

draw(D--A--B--C--D--B);
dot(dotted);
label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$C$",C,W);
label("$B$",B,E);
label("$A$",A,NE);
```

</center>

\textbf{(A)}\ 11 \textbf{(B)}\ 12 \textbf{(C)}\ 13 \textbf{(D)}\ 14 \textbf{(E)}\ 15

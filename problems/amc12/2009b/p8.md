\textbf{(A)}\ \frac23a + \frac13b \textbf{(B)}\ \frac32a - \frac12b \textbf{(C)}\ \frac32a + b \textbf{(D)}\ \frac32a + 2b \textbf{(E)}\ 3a - 2b

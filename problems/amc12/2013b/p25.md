$$
P(z)=z^n+c_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_2z^2+c_1z+50,
$$
where $c_1,c_2,\cdots, c_{n-1}$ are integers and $P(z)$ has distinct roots of the form $a+ib$ with $a$ and $b$ integers. How many polynomials are in $G$?

$$
|z_1| = |z_2| = ... = |z_n| = 1 \text{ and } z_1 + z_2 + ... + z_n = 0,
$$
then the numbers $z_1, z_2, ..., z_n$ are equally spaced on the unit circle in the complex plane?

\textbf{(A)}\ 1 \textbf{(B)}\ 2 \textbf{(C)}\ 3 \textbf{(D)}\ 4 \textbf{(E)}\ 5

$\text{(A)}\ \frac{366}{31\times 24}$
$\text{(B)}\ \frac{366\times 31}{24}$
$\text{(C)}\ \frac{366\times 24}{31}$
$\text{(D)}\ \frac{31\times 24}{366}$
$\text{(E)}\ 366\times 31\times 24$

Ten balls numbered $1$ to $10$ are in a jar. Jack reaches into the jar and randomly removes one of the balls. Then Jill reaches into the jar and randomly removes a different ball. The [probability](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Probability) that the [sum](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Sum) of the two numbers on the balls removed is [even](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Even) is
$\text{(A)}\ \frac{4}{9} \text{(B)}\ \frac{9}{19} \text{(C)}\ \frac{1}{2} \text{(D)}\ \frac{10}{19} \text{(E)}\ \frac{5}{9}$

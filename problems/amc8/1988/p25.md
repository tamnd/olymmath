A [palindrome](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Palindrome) is a [whole number](https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Whole_number) that reads the same forwards and backwards. If one neglects the colon, certain times displayed on a digital watch are palindromes. Three examples are: $\boxed{1:01}$, $\boxed{4:44}$, and $\boxed{12:21}$. How many times during a $12$-hour period will be palindromes?
$\text{(A)}\ 57 \text{(B)}\ 60 \text{(C)}\ 63 \text{(D)}\ 90 \text{(E)}\ 93$

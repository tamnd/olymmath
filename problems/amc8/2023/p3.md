*Wind chill* is a measure of how cold people feel when exposed to wind outside. A good estimate for wind chill can be found using this calculation: $$ (\text{wind chill}) = (\text{air temperature}) - 0.7 \times (\text{wind speed}), $$ where temperature is measured in degrees Fahrenheit $(^{\circ}\text{F})$ and and the wind speed is measured in miles per hour (mph). Suppose the air temperature is $36^{\circ}\text{F} $ and the wind speed is $18$ mph. Which of the following is closest to the approximate wind chill? $\textbf{(A)}~18 \textbf{(B)}~23 \textbf{(C)}~28 \textbf{(D)}~32 \textbf{(E)}~35$

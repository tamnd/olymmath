Given that $x, y, z$ are integers satisfying the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 7y^4 = z,\\[6pt]
7z^4+ \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{z} \frac{1}{k(2k-1)}}{\sum_{k=0}^{ z-1} \frac{1}{(k+z+1)(2z-k)}}
= \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{174545562} \frac{1}{i(2i-1)}}{\sum_{i=0}^{174545561} \frac{1}{(i+174545563)(349091124-i)}}
\end{cases}
$$
find the maximum value of $x+y+z$.

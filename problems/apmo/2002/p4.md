Let $x, y, z$ be positive numbers such that

$$
\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=1
$$

Show that

$$
\sqrt{x+y z}+\sqrt{y+z x}+\sqrt{z+x y} \geq \sqrt{x y z}+\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}+\sqrt{z}
$$

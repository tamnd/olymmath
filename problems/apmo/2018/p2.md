Let $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ be given by

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{x-2}+\frac{1}{x-4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x-2018}
$$

and

$$
g(x)=\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{1}{x-3}+\frac{1}{x-5}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x-2017} .
$$

Prove that

$$
|f(x)-g(x)|>2
$$

for any non-integer real number $x$ satisfying $0<x<2018$.

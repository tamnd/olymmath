Let $ n > 4$ be a non-negative integer. Given is the in a circle inscribed convex $ n$ -gon $ A_0A_1A_2\dots A_{n - 1}A_n$ $ (A_n = A_0)$ where the side $ A_{i - 1}A_i = i$ (for $ 1 \le i \le n$ ). Moreover, let $ \phi_i$ be the angle between the line $ A_iA_{i + 1}$ and the tangent to the circle in the point $ A_i$ (where the angle $ \phi_i$ is less than or equal $ 90^o$ , i.e. $ \phi_i$ is always the smaller angle of the two angles between the two lines). Determine the sum
$ \Phi = \sum_{i = 0}^{n - 1} \phi_i$
of these $ n$ angles.

Determine all composite positive integers $n$ with the following property: If $1 = d_1 < d_2 < \cdots < d_k = n$ are all the positive divisors of $n$ , then $$ (d_2 - d_1) : (d_3 - d_2) : \cdots : (d_k - d_{k-1}) = 1:2: \cdots :(k-1) $$ (Walther Janous)

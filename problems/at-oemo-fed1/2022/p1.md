Prove that for all positive real numbers $x, y$ and $z$, the double inequality
$$0 < \frac{1}{x + y + z + 1} -\frac{1}{(x + 1)(y + 1)(z + 1)} \le \frac18$$
holds. When does equality hold in the right inequality?

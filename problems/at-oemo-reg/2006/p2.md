Let $ n>1$ be a positive integer an $ a$ a real number. Determine all real solutions $ (x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n)$ to following system of equations:
$ x_1+ax_2=0$
$ x_2+a^2x_3=0$
…
$ x_k+a^kx_{k+1}=0$
…
$ x_n+a^nx_1=0$

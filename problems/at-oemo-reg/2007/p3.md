Let $ a$ be a positive real number and $ n$ a non-negative integer. Determine $ S-T$ , where
$ S= \sum_{k=-2n}^{2n+1} \frac{(k-1)^2}{a^{| \lfloor \frac{k}{2} \rfloor |}}$ and $ T= \sum_{k=-2n}^{2n+1} \frac{k^2}{a^{| \lfloor \frac{k}{2} \rfloor |}}$

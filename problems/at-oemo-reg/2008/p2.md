For a real number $ x$ is $ [x]$ the next smaller integer to $ x$ , that is the integer $ g$ with $ g\leqq<g+1$ , and $ \{x\}=x-[x]$ is the "decimal part" of $ x$ .
Determine all triples $ (a,b,c)$ of real numbers, which fulfil the following system of equations:
$$ \{a\}+[b]+\{c\}=2,9$$$$ \{b\}+[c]+\{a\}=5,3$$$$\{c\}+[a]+\{b\}=4,0$$

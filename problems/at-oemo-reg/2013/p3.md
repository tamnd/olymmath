For non-negative real numbers $a,$ $b$ let $A(a, b)$ be their arithmetic mean and $G(a, b)$ their geometric mean. We consider the sequence $\langle a_n \rangle$ with $a_0 = 0,$ $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = A(A(a_{n-1}, a_n), G(a_{n-1}, a_n))$ for $n > 0.$ (a) Show that each $a_n = b^2_n$ is the square of a rational number (with $b_n \geq 0$ ).
(b) Show that the inequality $\left|b_n - \frac{2}{3}\right| < \frac{1}{2^n}$ holds for all $n > 0.$

Find all natural numbers $ n$ such that the equation:

$ a_{n+1} x^2-2x \sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+...+a_{n+1}^2}+a_1+a_2+...+a_n=0$

has real solutions for all real numbers $ a_1,a_2,...,a_{n+1}$ .

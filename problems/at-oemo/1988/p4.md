Let $ a_{ij}$ be nonnegative integers such that $ a_{ij}=0$ if and only if $ i>j$ and that $ \sum_{j=1}^{1988}a_{ij}=1988$ holds for all $ i=1,...,1988$ . Find all real solutions of the system of equations:

$ \sum_{j=1}^{1988} (1+a_{ij})x_j=i+1, 1 \le i \le 1988$ .

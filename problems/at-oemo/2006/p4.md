Let $ <h_n>$ $ n\in\mathbb N$ a harmonic sequence of positive real numbers (that means that every $ h_n$ is the harmonic mean of its two neighbours $ h_{n-1}$ and $ h_{n+1}$ : $ h_n=\frac{2h_{n-1}h_{n+1}}{h_{n-1}+h_{n+1}}$ )
Show that: if the sequence includes a member $ h_j$ , which is the square of a rational number, it includes infinitely many members $ h_k$ , which are squares of rational numbers.

Let $ n>1$ and for $ 1 \leq k \leq n$ let $ p_k = p_k(a_1, a_2, . . . , a_n)$ be the sum of the products of all possible combinations of k of the numbers $ a_1,a_2,...,a_n$ . Furthermore let $ P = P(a_1, a_2, . . . , a_n)$ be the sum of all $ p_k$ with odd values of $ k$ less than or equal to $ n$ .

How many different values are taken by $ a_j$ if all the numbers $ a_j (1 \leq j \leq n)$ and $ P$ are prime?

Let $a,b,c$ be three integers for which the sum
$$ \frac{ab}{c}+ \frac{ac}{b}+ \frac{bc}{a}$$
is integer.
Prove that each of the three numbers
$$ \frac{ab}{c}, \frac{ac}{b}, \frac{bc}{a}$$
is integer.

(Proposed by Gerhard J. Woeginger)

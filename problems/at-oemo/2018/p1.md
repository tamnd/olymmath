Let $a \ne 0$ be a real number.
Find all functions $f : R_{>0}\to R_{>0}$ with $$ f(f(x) + y) = ax + \frac{1}{f\left(\frac{1}{y}\right)} $$ for all $x, y \in R_{>0}$ .

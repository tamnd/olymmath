Let $ABCD$ be a square. For a point $P$ inside it, a *windmill* centred at $P$ consists of 2 perpendicular lines $l_1$ and $l_2$ passing through $P$ , such that $\bullet ~$ $l_1$ intersects the sides AB and CD at W and Y, respectively, and $\bullet ~$ $l_2$ intersects the side BC and DA at X and Z, respectively.

A windmill is called *round* if the quadrilateral WXYZ is **cyclic.** Determine all points P inside the square such that every windmill centred at P is round.

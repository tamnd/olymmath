Similarly, $q$ meets $C_{1}A_{1}$ at $B_{2}$ , and $r$ meets $A_{1}B_{1}$ at $C_{2}$ . Show that the lines $AA_{2}, BB_{2}, CC_{2}$ meet at some point $X$ , and that $X$ lies on the circumcircle of $ABC$ .

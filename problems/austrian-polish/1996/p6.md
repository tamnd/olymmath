Given natural numbers $n > k > 1$ , find all real solutions $x_1,..., x_n$ of the system $$ x_i^3(x_i^2 + x_{i+1}^2+... +x_{i+k-1}^2) = x_{i-1}^2 $$ for 1 $\le i \le n$ . Here $x_{n+i} = x_i$ for all $ i$ .

Given the monic polynomial
\begin{align*} P(x) = x^N +a_{N-1}x^{N-1} + \ldots + a_1 x + a_0 \in \mathbb{R}[x] \end{align*}
of even degree $N$ $=$ $2n$ and having all real positive roots $x_i$ , for $1 \le i \le N$ . Prove, for any $c$ $\in$ $[0, \underset{1 \le i \le N}{\min} \{x_i \} )$ , the following inequality
\begin{align*} c + \sqrt[N]{P(c)} \le \sqrt[N]{a_0} \end{align*}

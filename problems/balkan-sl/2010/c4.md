Integers are written in the cells of a table $2010 \times 2010$ . Adding $1$ to all the numbers in a row or in a column is called a *move*. We say that a table is *equilibrium* if one can obtain after finitely many moves a table in which all the numbers are equal.
[list=a]
[*]Find the largest positive integer $n$ , for which there exists an *equilibrium* table containing the numbers $2^0, 2^1, \ldots , 2^n$ .
[*] For this $n$ , find the maximal number that may be contained in such a table.
[/list]

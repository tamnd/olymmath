Let $k$ be a positive integer. Find the maximum value of
$$a^{3k-1}b+b^{3k-1}c+c^{3k-1}a+k^2a^kb^kc^k,$$
where $a$, $b$, $c$ are non-negative reals such that $a+b+c=3k$.

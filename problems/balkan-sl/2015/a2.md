Let $a,b,c$ be sidelengths of a triangle and $r,R,s$ be the inradius, the circumradius and the semiperimeter respectively of the same triangle. Prove that: $$ \frac{1}{a + b} + \frac{1}{a + c} + \frac{1}{b + c}
\leq \frac{r}{16Rs}+\frac{s}{16Rr} + \frac{11}{8s} $$ (Albania)

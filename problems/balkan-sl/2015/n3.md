Let $a$ be a positive integer. For all positive integer n, we define $ a_n=1+a+a^2+\ldots+a^{n-1}. $ Let $s,t$ be two different positive integers with the following property:
If $p$ is prime divisor of $s-t$ , then $p$ divides $a-1$ .
Prove that number $\frac{a_{s}-a_{t}}{s-t}$ is an integer.

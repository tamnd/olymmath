The positive real numbers $a, b, c$ satisfy the equality $a+b+c=1$. For every natural number $n$ find the minimal possible value of the expression

$$
E=\frac{a^{-n}+b}{1-a}+\frac{b^{-n}+c}{1-b}+\frac{c^{-n}+a}{1-c}
$$

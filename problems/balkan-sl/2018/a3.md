Show that for every positive integer $n$ we have:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n}\left(\frac{2 n+1-k}{k+1}\right)^{k}=\left(\frac{2 n+1}{1}\right)^{0}+\left(\frac{2 n}{2}\right)^{1}+\cdots+\left(\frac{n+1}{n+1}\right)^{n} \leqslant 2^{n}
$$

Find all positive integers $n$ such that there exist a permutation $\sigma$ on the set $\{1,2,3, \ldots, n\}$ for which
$$\sqrt{\sigma(1)+\sqrt{\sigma(2)+\sqrt{\ldots+\sqrt{\sigma(n-1)+\sqrt{\sigma(n)}}}}}$$
is a rational number.

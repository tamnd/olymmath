A special number is a positive integer $n$ for which there exist positive integers $a, b, c$ and $d$ with

$$
n=\frac{a^{3}+2 b^{3}}{c^{3}+2 d^{3}}
$$

Prove that:
(a) there are infinitely many special numbers;
(b) 2014 is not a special number.

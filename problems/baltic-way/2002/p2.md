Let $a, b, c, d$ be real numbers such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
a+b+c+d & =-2 \\
a b+a c+a d+b c+b d+c d & =0
\end{aligned}
$$

Prove that at least one of the numbers $a, b, c, d$ is not greater than -1 .

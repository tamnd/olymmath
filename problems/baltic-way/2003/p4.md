Let $a, b, c$ be positive real numbers. Prove that

$$
\frac{2 a}{a^{2}+b c}+\frac{2 b}{b^{2}+c a}+\frac{2 c}{c^{2}+a b} \leq \frac{a}{b c}+\frac{b}{c a}+\frac{c}{a b} .
$$

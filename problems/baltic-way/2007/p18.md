Let $a,b,c,d$ be non-zero integers, such that the only quadruple of integers $(x, y, z, t)$ satisfying the equation
$$ax^2+by^2+cz^2+dt^2=0$$
is $x=y=z=t=0$ . Does it follow that the numbers $a,b,c,d$ have the same sign?

Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral such that $\angle ADB=\angle BDC$ . Suppose that a point $E$ on the side $AD$ satisfies the equality
$$AE\cdot ED + BE^2=CD\cdot AE.$$
Show that $\angle EBA=\angle DCB$ .

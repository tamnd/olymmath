Find all quadruples $(a, b, c, d)$ of real numbers that simultaneously satisfy the following equations:

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
a^{3}+c^{3} & =2 \\
a^{2} b+c^{2} d & =0 \\
b^{3}+d^{3} & =1 \\
a b^{2}+c d^{2} & =-6
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

Let $\alpha$ be a non-zero real number. Find all functions $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that

$$
x f(x+y)=(x+\alpha y) f(x)+x f(y)
$$

for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$.

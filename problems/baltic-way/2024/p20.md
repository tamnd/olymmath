Positive integers $a, b$ and $c$ satisfy the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
(a b-1)^{2} & =c\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)+a b+1 \\
a^{2}+b^{2} & =c^{2}+a b
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

(a) Prove that $c+1$ is a perfect square.
(b) Find all such triples $(a, b, c)$.

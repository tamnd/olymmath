Let $a, b, c, n$ be positive integers satisfying $abc = 10^n - 1$. If $S(x)$ denotes the sum of digits of $x$ in decimal notation, prove that

$$S(a) + S(b^2) + S(c^4) \geq \sqrt[3]{243n}.$$

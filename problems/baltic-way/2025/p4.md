Find all functions $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that

$$(f(a - c) + f(b - d))(f(a) + f(b)) = f(ad - bc) + f(f(a) + f(b) - ac - bd)$$

for all real numbers $a, b, c, d$.

Let $n \geq 3$ friends play dodgeball. Initially all players are free. When a free player $A$ knocks out another free player $B$, $A$ imprisons $B$ and frees all players currently imprisoned by $B$. Two knock-outs cannot happen at the same time.

Find the least number of knock-outs after which it is possible that among every two players at least one has freed the other one.

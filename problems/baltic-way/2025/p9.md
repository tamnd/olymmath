A Baltic polyiamond is an $n$-gon with side lengths $n, n-1, \ldots, 2, 1$ in exactly this order, and all internal angles $120^\circ$ or $240^\circ$. Prove that for every Baltic polyiamond, $n$ is divisible by 6.

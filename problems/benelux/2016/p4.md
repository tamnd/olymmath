A circle $\omega$ passes through the two vertices $B$ and $C$ of a triangle $A B C$. Furthermore, $\omega$ intersects segment $A C$ in $D \neq C$ and segment $A B$ in $E \neq B$. On the ray from $B$ through $D$ lies a point $K$ such that $|B K|=|A C|$, and on the ray from $C$ through $E$ lies a point $L$ such that $|C L|=|A B|$. Show that the circumcentre $O$ of triangle $A K L$ lies on $\omega$.

Find all $n$ - tuples of reals $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ satisfying the system:
$$\begin{cases}x_1x_2\cdots x_n=1\\x_1-x_2x_3\cdots x_n=1\\x_1x_2-x_3x_4\cdots x_n=1\\\vdots\\x_1x_2\cdots x_{n-1}-x_n=1\end{cases}$$

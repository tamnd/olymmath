It is given a tetrahedron with vertices $A,B,C,D$ .

(a) Prove that there exists a vertex of the tetrahedron with the following property: the three edges of that tetrahedron through that vertex can form a triangle.
(b) On the edges $DA,DB$ and $DC$ there are given the points $M,N$ and $P$ for which: $$ DM=\frac{DA}n,\enspace DN=\frac{DB}{n+1}\enspace DP=\frac{DC}{n+2} $$ where $n$ is a natural number. The plane defined by the points $M,N$ and $P$ is $\alpha_n$ . Prove that all planes $\alpha_n$ , $(n=1,2,3,\ldots)$ pass through a single straight line.

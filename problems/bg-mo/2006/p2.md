Let $f:\mathbb{R}^+\to\mathbb{R}^+$ be a function that satisfies for all $x>y>0$
$$f(x+y)-f(x-y)=4\sqrt{f(x)f(y)}$$
a) Prove that $f(2x)=4f(x)$ for all $x>0$ ;
b) Find all such functions.

Given $m\in\mathbb{N}$ and a prime number $p$ , $p>m$ , let
$$M=\{n\in\mathbb{N}\mid m^2+n^2+p^2-2mn-2mp-2np \,\, \text{is a perfect square} \} $$
Prove that $|M|$ does not depend on $p$ .

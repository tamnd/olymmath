A real number $f(X)\neq 0$ is assigned to each point $X$ in the space.
It is known that for any tetrahedron $ABCD$ with $O$ the center of the inscribed sphere, we have :
$$ f(O)=f(A)f(B)f(C)f(D). $$
Prove that $f(X)=1$ for all points $X$ .

Let $a \uparrow\uparrow b = \overbrace{a^{a^{a^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{a}}}}}}}^{b}$. That is, $a \uparrow\uparrow b$ is given by the recurrence
$$a \uparrow\uparrow b = \begin{cases} a & b = 1 \\ a^{a \uparrow\uparrow (b-1)} & b \ge 2 \end{cases}$$
What is the remainder of $3 \uparrow\uparrow (3 \uparrow\uparrow (3 \uparrow\uparrow 3))$ when divided by $60$?

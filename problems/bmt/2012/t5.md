Let $p > 1$ be relatively prime to $10$ . Let $n$ be any positive number and $ d$ be the last digit of $n$ . Define $f(n) = \lfloor \frac{n}{10} \rfloor + d \cdot m$ . Then, we can call $m$ a *divisibility multiplier* for $p$ , if $f(n)$ is divisible by $p$ if and only if $n$ is divisible by $p$ . Find a divisibility multiplier for $2013$ .

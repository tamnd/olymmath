Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Let $r$ denote the inradius of $\triangle ABC$. Let $r_a$ denote the $A$-exradius of $\triangle ABC$. Note that the $A$-excircle of $\triangle ABC$ is the circle that is tangent to segment $BC$, the extension of ray $AB$ beyond $B$ and the extension of $AC$ beyond $C$. The $A$-exradius is the radius of the $A$-excircle. Define $r_b$ and $r_c$ analogously. Prove that
$$\frac{1}{r} = \frac{1}{r_a} + \frac{1}{r_b} + \frac{1}{r_c}.$$

It is known that $4$ people $A, B, C$ , and $D$ each have a $1/3$ probability of telling the truth. Suppose that $\bullet$ $A$ makes a statement. $\bullet$ $B$ makes a statement about the truthfulness of $A$ 's statement. $\bullet$ $C$ makes a statement about the truthfulness of $B$ 's statement. $\bullet$ $D$ says that $C$ says that $B$ says that $A$ was telling the truth.
What is the probability that $A$ was actually telling the truth?

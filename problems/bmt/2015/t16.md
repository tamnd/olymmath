Five points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, and $E$ in three-dimensional Euclidean space have the property that $AB = BC = CD = DE = EA = 1$ and $\angle ABC = \angle BCD = \angle CDE = \angle DEA = 90^\circ$. Find all possible $\cos(\angle EAB)$.

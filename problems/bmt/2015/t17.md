There exist real numbers $x$ and $y$ such that
$$x(a^3 + b^3 + c^3) + 3yabc \ge (x + y)(a^2b + b^2c + c^2a)$$
holds for all positive real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$. Determine the smallest possible value of $\frac{x}{y}$.

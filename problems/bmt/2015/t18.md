A value $x \in [0, 1]$ is selected uniformly at random. A point $(a, b)$ is called *friendly* to $x$ if there exists a circle between the lines $y = 0$ and $y = 1$ that contains both $(a, b)$ and $(0, x)$ . Find the area of the region of the plane determined by possible locations of friendly points.

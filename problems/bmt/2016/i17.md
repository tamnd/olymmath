Consider triangle $ABC$ in $xy$-plane where $A$ is the origin, $B$ lies on the positive $x$-axis, $C$ is on the upper right quadrant, and $A = 30^\circ$, $B = 60^\circ$, $C = 90^\circ$. Let the length $BC = 1$. Draw the angle bisector $\ell$ of angle $C$, and let this intersect the $y$-axis at $D$. What is the area of quadrilateral $ADBC$?

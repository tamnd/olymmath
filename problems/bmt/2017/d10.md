Let $\phi (n)$ be the number of positive integers less than or equal to $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$ . Evaluate $$ \sum \limits _{n=1} ^{64} (-1)^{n} \left \lfloor \frac{64}{n} \right \rfloor \phi (n). $$

Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a monotonically increasing function such that
$$\begin{aligned} f\left(\frac{x}{3}\right) &= \frac{f(x)}{2} \\ f(1 - x) &= 2018 - f(x) \end{aligned}$$
If $f(1) = 2018$, find $f\left(\frac{12}{13}\right)$.

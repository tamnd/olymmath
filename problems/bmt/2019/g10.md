A *3-4-5 point* of a triangle $ ABC $ is a point $ P $ such that the ratio $ AP : BP : CP $ is equivalent to the ratio $ 3 : 4 : 5 $ . If $ \triangle ABC $ is isosceles with base $ BC = 12 $ and $ \triangle ABC $ has exactly one $ 3-4-5 $ point, compute the area of $ \triangle ABC $ .

For $k\ge 1$ , define $a_k=2^k$ . Let $$ S=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{2a_k^2-6a_k+5}{\sqrt{(a_k^2-4a_k+5)(4a_k^2-8a_k+5)}}\right). $$ Compute $\lfloor 100S\rfloor$ .

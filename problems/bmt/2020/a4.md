Let $\varphi$ be the positive solution to the equation $$ x^2=x+1. $$ For $n\ge 0$ , let $a_n$ be the unique integer such that $\varphi^n-a_n\varphi$ is also an integer. Compute $$ \sum_{n=0}^{10}a_n. $$

Equilateral triangle $ABC$ has side length $2$. A semicircle is drawn with diameter $\overline{BC}$ such that it lies outside the triangle, and minor arc $\overset{\frown}{BC}$ is drawn so that it is part of a circle centered at $A$. The area of the "lune" that is inside the semicircle but outside sector $ABC$ can be expressed in the form $\sqrt{p} - \frac{q\pi}{r}$, where $p$, $q$, and $r$ are positive integers such that $q$ and $r$ are relatively prime. Compute $p + q + r$.

Sheila is making a regular-hexagon-shaped sign with side length $1$. Let $ABCDEF$ be the regular hexagon, and let $R, S, T$ and $U$ be the midpoints of $FA$, $BC$, $CD$ and $EF$, respectively. Sheila splits the hexagon into four regions of equal width: trapezoids $ABSR$, $RSCF$, $FCTU$, and $UTDE$. She then paints the middle two regions gold. The fraction of the total hexagon that is gold can be written in the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m + n$.

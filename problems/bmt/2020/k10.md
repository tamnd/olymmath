The integral
$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{x}{\tan(x)} \, \mathrm{d}x$$
can be written in the form $a^b\pi\ln c$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are integers such that $c$ is as small as possible. Compute $a + b + c$.

Suppose $f\colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a differentiable function defined by
$$f(x) = f'(2)x^2 + x.$$
Then $f(2)$ can be written in the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m + n$.

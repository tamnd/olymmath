Suppose that a parallelogram has a vertex at the origin of the $2$-dimensional plane, and two of its sides are vectors from the origin to the points $(10, y)$, and $(x, 10)$, where $x, y \in [0, 10]$ are chosen uniformly at random. The probability that the parallelogram's area is at least $50$ is $\ln(\sqrt{a}) + \frac{b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers such that $b$ and $c$ are relatively prime and $a$ is as small as possible. Compute $a + b + c$.

Let $f\colon \mathbb{R}_{>0} \to \mathbb{R}$ (where $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ is the set of all positive real numbers) be differentiable and satisfy the equation
$$f(y) - f(x) = \frac{x^x}{y^y} f\left(\frac{y^y}{x^x}\right)$$
for all real $x, y > 0$. Furthermore, $f'(1) = 1$. Compute $\frac{f(2020^2)}{f(2020)}$.

Non-degenerate quadrilateral $ABCD$ with $AB = AD$ and $BC = CD$ has integer side lengths, and $\angle ABC = \angle BCD = \angle CDA$. If $AB = 3$ and $B \ne D$, how many possible lengths are there for $BC$?

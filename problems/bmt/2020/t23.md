Let $0 < \theta < 2\pi$ be a real number for which $\cos (\theta) + \cos (2\theta) + \cos (3\theta) + ...+ \cos (2020\theta) = 0$ and $\theta =\frac{\pi}{n}$ for some positive integer $n$ . Compute the sum of the possible values of $n \le 2020$ .

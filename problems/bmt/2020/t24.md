For positive integers $N$ and $m$ , where $m \le N$ , define $$ a_{m,N} =\frac{1}{{N+1 \choose m}} \sum_{i=m-1}^{N-1} \frac{ {i \choose m-1}}{N - i} $$ Compute the smallest positive integer $N$ such that $$ \sum^N_{m=1}a_{m,N} >\frac{2020N}{N +1} $$

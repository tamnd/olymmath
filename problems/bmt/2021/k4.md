Compute the area of the region of points satisfying the inequalities $y \le 4 - \frac{x^2}{9}$, $y \ge \frac{x^2}{9} - 4$, $x \le 4 - \frac{y^2}{9}$, and $x \ge \frac{y^2}{9} - 4$.

Suppose the following equality holds, where $a, b, c$ are integers and $K$ is the constant of integration:
$$\int \frac{\sin^a(x) - \cos^a(x)}{\sin^b(x)\cos^b(x)} \, \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\csc^c(x)}{c} + \frac{\sec^c(x)}{c} + K.$$
If $a = 2021$, compute $a + b + c$.

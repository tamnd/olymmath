Let $x_1 = -4$, and for $n \ge 1$, define $x_{n+1} = -4^{x_n}$. Similarly, let $f_1(x) = \sin(\arccos x)$, and for $n \ge 1$, define $f_{n+1}(x) = f_1(f_n(x))$. Compute
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(2^{x_n}).$$
You may assume that this limit exists.

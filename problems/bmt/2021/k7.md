Let $c(x) = \frac{e^x + e^{-2x}}{2}$, defined on the interval $1 \le x \le 2$. Let $c^{-1}(x)$ be the inverse of $c(x)$. Compute
$$\int_{c(1)}^{c(2)} c^{-1}(x) \, \mathrm{d}x.$$

Define
$$f_n(x) = \int_0^x \frac{t^{6n-1}}{1 + t^3} \, \mathrm{d}t$$
for positive integers $n$ and real numbers $0 \le x \le 1$. We can write $f_n(x) = c \cdot \log(p(x)) + h_n(x)$, where $p(x)$ and $h_n(x)$ are polynomials with real coefficients with $p(x)$ monic (coefficient of the highest degree term is $1$), and $c$ is a real number. Compute
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} h_n(1).$$

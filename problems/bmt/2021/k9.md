Emily plays a game on the real line. Emily starts at the number $1$ and starts with $0$ points. When she is at the real number $a$, she chooses a real number $b$ such that $a < b \le 100$. She then moves to $b$ and gains $\frac{4(b - a)}{(a + b)^2}$ points. She repeats this process until she reaches the number $100$. Compute the smallest possible value of $c$ such that Emily's score is always less than $c$.

In triangle $\triangle ABC$, $E$ and $F$ are the feet of the altitudes from $B$ to $\overline{AC}$ and $C$ to $\overline{AB}$, respectively. Line $\overleftrightarrow{BC}$ and the line through $A$ tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$ intersect at $X$. Let $Y$ be the intersection of line $\overleftrightarrow{EF}$ and the line through $A$ parallel to $\overline{BC}$. If $XB = 4$, $BC = 8$, and $EF = 4\sqrt{3}$, compute $XY$.

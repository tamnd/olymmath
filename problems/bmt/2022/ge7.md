Let $f(x) = x^2 + \lfloor x \rfloor^2 - 2x\lfloor x \rfloor + 1$. Compute $f\left(4 + \frac{5}{6}\right)$. (Here, $\lfloor m \rfloor$ is defined as the greatest integer less than or equal to $m$. For example, $\lfloor 3 \rfloor = 3$ and $\lfloor -4.25 \rfloor = -5$.)

Compute the number of ordered triples $(a, b, c)$ of integers between $-100$ and $100$ inclusive satisfying the simultaneous equations $$ a^3 - 2a = abc - b - c $$ $$ b^3 - 2b = bca - c - a $$ $$ c^3 - 2c = cab - a - b. $$

Let $N \ge 3$ be the answer to Problem 21. A regular $N$-gon is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$. Let $D$ be the set of diagonals, where we include all sides as diagonals. Then, let $D'$ be the set of all unordered pairs of distinct diagonals in $D$. Compute the sum
$$\sum_{\{d, d'\} \in D'} \ell(d)^2 \ell(d')^2,$$
where $\ell(d)$ denotes the length of diagonal $d$.

There exists a unique triple of integers $(B,M, T)$ such that $B > T > M$ and $$ 3B^2(3T -M) + 8M^2(B - T) + 3T^2(5M - B) - (2B^3 + 3M^3 + 4T^3) + 15BMT = 2023. $$ Compute $B +M + T$ .

Define a sequence $a_0$ , $a_1$ , $a_2$ , $,...$ recursively by $a_0 = 0$ , $a_1 = 1$ , and $a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + xa_n$ for each $n \ge 0$ and some real number $x$ . The infinite series $$ \sum^{\infty}_{n=0}\frac{a_n}{10^n} = 1. $$ Compute $x$ .

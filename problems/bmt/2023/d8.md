Define a family of functions $S_k(n)$ for positive integers $n$ and $k$ by the following two rules: $$ S_0(n) = 1, $$ $$ S_k(n) = \sum_{d | n} dS_{k-1}(d). $$ Compute the remainder when $S_{30}(30)$ is divided by $1001$ .

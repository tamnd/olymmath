Let triangle $\vartriangle ABC$ have circumcenter $O$ and circumradius $r$ , and let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of ntriangle $\vartriangle BOC$ . Let $F$ be the intersection of $\overleftrightarrow{AO}$ and $\omega$ not equal to $O$ . Let $E$ be on line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ such that $\overline{EF} \perp \overline{AE}$ , and let $G$ be on line $\overleftrightarrow{AC}$ such that $\overline{GF} \perp \overline{AG}$ . If $AC =\frac{65}{63}$ , $BC =
\frac{24}{13}r$ , and $AB = \frac{126}{65}r$ , compute $AF \cdot EG$ .

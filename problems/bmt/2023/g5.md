Triangle $\triangle ABC$ has side lengths $AB = 8$, $BC = 15$, and $CA = 17$. Circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ are externally tangent to each other and within $\triangle ABC$. The radius of circle $\omega_2$ is four times the radius of circle $\omega_1$. Circle $\omega_1$ is tangent to $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{BC}$, and circle $\omega_2$ is tangent to $\overline{BC}$ and $\overline{CA}$. Compute the radius of circle $\omega_1$.

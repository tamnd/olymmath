Let $n$ be the smallest positive integer such that there exist integers, $a$ , $b$ , and $c$ , satisfying: $$ \frac{n}{2}= a^2 \,\, \,\, \frac{n}{3}= b^3 \,\,\ , \,\,\ \frac{n}{5}= c^5. $$ Find the number of positive integer factors of $n$ .

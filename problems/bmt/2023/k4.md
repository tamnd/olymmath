An ice cube melts such that it always remains a cube, and its volume decreases at a constant rate. The initial side length of the cube is $10$ inches, and it takes $50$ minutes for the ice cube to completely melt. When the side length of the ice cube is $4$ inches, what is the rate, in inches per minute, at which the side length of the ice cube is decreasing?

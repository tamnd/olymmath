For a positive number $x$, let $f_0(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ and $f_n(x) = \frac{d^n}{dx^n}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$ for all positive integers $n$. If
$$g(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{f_n(x)},$$
compute $g(1)$.

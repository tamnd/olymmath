Define a sequence $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ by $a_0 = 24$, $a_1 = 23$, and $a_{n+2} = -a_{n+1} + 6a_n$ for $n \ge 0$. Compute
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n}{n6^n}.$$

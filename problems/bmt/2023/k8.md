Let $f : [1, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function such that
$$I(f) = \int_1^\infty \left(\sqrt{2023}\,xe^{-x}f(x) - \frac{1}{4}x^2f(x)^2\right) \mathrm{d}x$$
converges and is maximized over all continuous functions on $[1, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$. Compute $f(1) + I(f)$.

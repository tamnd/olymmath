Call an $n$ -digit integer with distinct digits *mountainous*if, for some integer $1 \le k \le n$ , the first $k$ digits are in strictly ascending order and the following $n - k$ digits are in strictly descending order. How many $5$ -digit mountainous integers with distinct digits are there?

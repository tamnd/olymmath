Let $N_{19}$ be the answer to question 19. For every non-negative integer $k$, define
$$f_k(x) = x(x - 1) + (x + 1)(x - 2) + \cdots + (x + k)(x - k - 1),$$
and let $r_k$ and $s_k$ be the two roots of $f_k(x)$. Compute the smallest positive integer $m$ such that $|r_m - s_m| > 10N_{19}$.

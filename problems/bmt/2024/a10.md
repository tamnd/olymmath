Let $\omega = e^{\frac{2\pi i}{5}}$, where $i = \sqrt{-1}$. If
$$S = \prod_{0 \le a, b, c < 5} \left(\omega^a - \sec\left(\frac{2\pi(b - a)}{5}\right) \cdot \omega^b + \omega^c\right),$$
compute the remainder when $S$ is divided by $101$.

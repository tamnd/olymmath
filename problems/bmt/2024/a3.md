Suppose $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ is an arithmetic sequence, and suppose $g_1, g_2, \ldots$ is a geometric sequence with common ratio $2$. Suppose $a_1 + g_1 = 1$ and $a_2 + g_2 = 1$. If $a_{24} = g_7$, find $a_{2024}$.

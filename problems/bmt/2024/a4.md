For a real number $n$, let $\lfloor n \rfloor$ be the greatest integer less than or equal to $n$ and let $\lceil n \rceil$ be the smallest integer greater than or equal to $n$. For example, $\lfloor 2.5 \rfloor = 2$ and $\lfloor 2 \rfloor = 2$, while $\lceil 2.5 \rceil = 3$ and $\lceil 2 \rceil = 2$. Find the greatest integer $x$ such that $\left\lfloor \frac{x}{20} + 20 \right\rfloor = \left\lceil \frac{x}{24} + 24 \right\rceil$.

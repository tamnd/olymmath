There exist nonzero real numbers $B$, $M$, and $T$ that satisfy the equations:
$$\begin{aligned} 2B + M + T - 2B^2 - 2BM - 2MT - 2BT &= 0, \\ B + 2M + T - 3M^2 - 3BM - 3MT - 3BT &= 0, \\ B + M + 2T - 4T^2 - 4BM - 4MT - 4BT &= 0. \end{aligned}$$
Compute $2B + 3M + 4T$.

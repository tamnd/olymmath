Let $\alpha$ be a positive real number. Over all choices of positive real numbers $w, x, y, z$ satisfying
$$\begin{aligned} wx + yz &= \alpha, \\ wy + xz &= \alpha, \\ wz + xy &= \alpha, \end{aligned}$$
the minimum value of $w + 2x + 3y + 4z$ is $\frac{\alpha}{2}$. Compute $\alpha$.

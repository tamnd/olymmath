Define two sequences of real numbers, $\{a_n\}$ and $\{b_n\}$, such that $a_0 = b_0 = \sqrt{3}$ and for $n \ge 0$:
$$\begin{aligned} a_{n+1} &= a_n + \sqrt{1 + a_n^2} \\ b_{n+1} &= \frac{b_n}{1 + \sqrt{1 + b_n^2}}. \end{aligned}$$
Find the smallest real number $M$ such that $M > |a_i b_i - a_j b_j|$ for any integers $i, j > 0$.

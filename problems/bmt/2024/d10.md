The positive integers $1$ through $9$ are placed in the $9$ cells of a $3 \times 3$ grid. Then, for every pair of cells sharing a side, the sum of the numbers in that pair is recorded in a list. The most number of times any number occurs in the list is $4$ . In how many ways could numbers have been placed in the grid?

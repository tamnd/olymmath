Let a function $f(n)$ satisfy $f(1) = 0$, and for positive integers $n > 1$,
$$f(n) = \begin{cases} f\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \ln 2, & \text{if } n \text{ is even} \\ \frac{f(n-1) + f(n+1)}{2}, & \text{if } n \text{ is odd.} \end{cases}$$
Find the value of
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{2^n} \sum_{k=2^n}^{2^{n+1}} |\ln k - f(k)|.$$

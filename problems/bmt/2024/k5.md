For a real number $n$, let $\lfloor n \rfloor$ be the greatest integer less than or equal to $n$. Compute
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^n \frac{x\lfloor x \rfloor}{n^3} \, \mathrm{d}x.$$

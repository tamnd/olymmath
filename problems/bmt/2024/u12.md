Define an *upright* triangle to be a set of three distinct vertices on Pascal's triangle, where two vertices are on the same row and the third vertex is above both points and shares a diagonal with each of them. For example, the diagram below shows the first four rows of Pascal's triangle, and the three circled numbers are vertices of an upright triangle. Compute the sum of the vertices over all upright triangles on the first $10$ rows of Pascal's triangle.

Let $\lfloor x \rfloor$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Additionally, let $\{x\}$ denote $x - \lfloor x \rfloor$. For example, $\lfloor \pi \rfloor = 3$ and $\{\pi\} = 0.1415\ldots$. Compute the integer $n$ such that there are exactly $2024$ positive solutions $x$ to the equation
$$x^{\lfloor x \rfloor \{x\}} = n.$$

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of obtuse triangle $\triangle ABC$ and $D$ be the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$. A line through $H$ perpendicular to $\overleftrightarrow{DH}$ intersects with $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overleftrightarrow{BC}$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively. Given that $EH = 3$ and $HD = 5$, compute the maximum possible area of triangle $\triangle DEF$.

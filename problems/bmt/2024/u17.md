Let the *power*, $p(n)$, of a positive integer $n$ be the number of fractions of the form $\frac{m}{n}$ that are in simplest form over all positive integers $m$ with $1 \le m \le n$. ($\frac{1}{1}$ is in simplest form.) Let a positive integer $n$ be *weak* if $\frac{p(n)}{n} \le \frac{p(k)}{k}$ for all $1 \le k < n$. Compute the sum of all *weak* positive integers less than $2024$.

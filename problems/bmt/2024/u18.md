Arthur has a four-sided die, of which all faces are initially labeled $1$. Every second, Arthur rolls the die and, if the outcome of the die is $1$, then he changes the number on the top face to $2$, and otherwise, he changes the number to $1$. Let the probability that the sum of the die rolls is at some point $k$ be $p_k$. Let $\lfloor x \rfloor$ denote the largest integer less than or equal to $x$, and let $\{x\} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor$. Compute $\{p_1 + p_2 + \cdots + p_{2024}\}$.

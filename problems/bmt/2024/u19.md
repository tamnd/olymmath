Let $N_{21}$ be the answer to problem 21. Let $b$ and $d$ be real numbers such that the polynomial $P(x) = x^4 - N_{21}x^3 + bx^2 - x + d$ has real roots $p, q, r, s$ and $pq = rs$. Compute the greatest possible integer value of $P(N_{21})$.

Let $N_{19}$ be the answer to problem 19. Danielle picks a real number $p$ uniformly at random from $[0, 1]$. She then creates a magic coin that has probability $p$ of landing on heads and probability $1 - p$ of landing on tails when flipped. Compute the probability that Danielle lands heads exactly $N_{19}$ times in $5N_{19}$ flips of the coin.

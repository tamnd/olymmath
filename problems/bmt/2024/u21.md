Let $N_{20}$ be the answer to problem 20. In triangle $\triangle ABC$, the angle bisector of $\angle B$ intersects $\overline{AC}$ at $D$. The perpendicular bisector of $\overline{BD}$ intersects $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{BC}$ at $X$ and $Y$ respectively. The area of $\triangle BXY$ is $\frac{7}{55}$, $AD = \frac{1}{4}$, and $DC = N_{20}$. Compute the area of $\triangle ABC$.

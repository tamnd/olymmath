Tetrahedron $ABIH$ has points $C$ on $\overline{BH}$, $D$ on $\overline{AH}$, $E$ on $\overline{HI}$, $F$ on $\overline{AI}$, and $G$ on $\overline{BI}$ such that the squares $BCEG$ and $ADEF$ have side length $5$. Quadrilaterals $ABCD$ and $ABGF$ are isosceles trapezoids. Given that $AB = 4\sqrt{2}$ and $CD = 3\sqrt{2}$, compute the volume of solid $ABCDEFG$.

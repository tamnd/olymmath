Submit a positive real number $c$ to at most $6$ decimal places. Define the function $f^1(x) = x^2 + c$, and let $f^k(0) = f^1(f^{k-1}(0))$ for $k \ge 2$. Let $N$ be the smallest positive integer such that $f^N(0) > 2024$. If such an $N$ does not exist, your score is $0$ points. Otherwise, your score is $\max(0, 25 - 3|N - 20|)$ points.

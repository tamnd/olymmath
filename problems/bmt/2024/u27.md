Oliver rolls a standard, fair $6$-sided die $640$ times. Oliver tells Tushar that no 5s were rolled before he rolled a 6 (it is possible he rolled no 5s or 6s). Given this information, Tushar computes $x$, the expected number of 6s rolled by Oliver. Compute $\log_5(\lceil 6x \rceil - 6x)$. Submit your answer as a real number $E$ to at most $3$ decimal places; if the correct answer is $A$, you will receive $\max(0, 25 - \lfloor 4|A - E| \rfloor)$ points.

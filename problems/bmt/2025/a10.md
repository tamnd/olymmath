Complex numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{10}$ satisfy the following property for each $1 \le k \le 10$:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{10} a_i^9 = 10a_k^{10} + a_k^9 - 2^9.$$
Given $a_1 a_2 \cdots a_{10} = -\frac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$, compute the sum of the digits of $m + n$.

A hat initially contains five slips labeled with the numbers $1, 2, 3, 4$, and $5$ (one slip per number). Every minute, Jessica adds a new slip labeled with the number $20$ to the hat. How many slips are in the hat when the average of the numbers in the hat is $5$ times the initial average?

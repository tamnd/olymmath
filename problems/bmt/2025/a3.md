An arithmetic sequence is a sequence of numbers where the difference between any two consecutive terms is the same. Harsh writes an arithmetic sequence where the ratio of the second term to the first term is $4$, and the sum of the third and fourth terms is $34$. Compute the second term of Harsh's sequence.

Let $f(x) = (2x^2 + 7x + 3)(x^2 - 2x - 15)$. The graph of $f(x)$ in the coordinate plane is translated $4$ units to the right and then rotated about the origin by $180$ degrees. The resulting graph is the graph of the function $g(x)$. Compute the sum of the distinct roots of $g(x)$.

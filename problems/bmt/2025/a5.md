Complex numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$ satisfy the equations
$$\begin{aligned} a^3 + b^3 &= 2 - 3ab(a + b), \\ b^3 + c^3 &= 16 - 3bc(b + c), \\ c^3 + a^3 &= 54 - 3ca(c + a). \end{aligned}$$
Compute the sum of all distinct possible real values of $a + b + c$.

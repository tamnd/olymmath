Compute the sum of all distinct real numbers $x$ satisfying $|x| \le \frac{1}{2}$ and
$$\sin\left(\log_{10}\left(\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2}\right)\right) = 0.$$
Note that the argument of the sine function is assumed to be in radians.

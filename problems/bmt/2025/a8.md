Let $a = \log_6(30)$ and $b = \log_{15}(24)$. Then
$$\frac{2ab + 2a - 1}{ab + b + 1} = \log_m(n)$$
for positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that $m$ is as small as possible. Compute $m + n$.

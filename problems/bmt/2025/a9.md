Distinct complex numbers $a, b$, and $c$ each have integer real part and satisfy the equations
$$\begin{aligned} ab + bc + ca &= 300, \\ (a - b)^5 + (b - c)^5 + (c - a)^5 &= 0. \end{aligned}$$
Compute the sum of the three least possible positive real values of $abc$.

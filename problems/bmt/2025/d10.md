Let $S$ be the set of triples of nonnegative integers $(p, q, r)$ such that
$$3^p \cdot 21^q + 9 \cdot 2^r$$
is a perfect square. Compute
$$\sum_{(p,q,r) \in S} 100p + 10q + r.$$

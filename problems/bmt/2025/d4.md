A *substring* of a positive integer $n$ is a positive integer whose digits appear consecutively in the digits of $n$. For example, $10$, $00 = 0$, and $02 = 2$ are two-digit substrings of $1002$, but $12$ and $01 = 1$ are not. An integer $n$ is chosen uniformly at random from the integers between $1000$ and $9999$ inclusive. Compute the probability that all two-digit substrings of $n$ are divisible by three.

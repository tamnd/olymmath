Over all triples of positive integers $(a, b, c)$ satisfying the equation
$$\gcd\left(\operatorname{lcm}(a, b), \operatorname{lcm}(a, c), \operatorname{lcm}(b, c)\right) = 2025,$$
compute the smallest possible value of $a + b + c$.

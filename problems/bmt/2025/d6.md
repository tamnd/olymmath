An increasing sequence of positive integers is *three-hating* if it contains at least one term and alternates between integers that are multiples of $3$ and integers that are not multiples of $3$. For example, $(1)$, $(3, 4, 6)$, and $(3)$ are three-hating while $(3, 6)$ and $(3, 6, 7, 9)$ are not. Compute the number of three-hating sequences in which every term is less than or equal to $12$.

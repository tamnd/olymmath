Let $N = 2025 \times 202^5 \times 20^{25}$. For each positive integer $x$, define $\sigma(x)$ to be the sum of the positive divisors of $x$. Define $h(x)$ to be the sum of all positive divisors $y$ of $x$ such that $\sigma(y)$ is even. For example, $\sigma(9) = 13$ and $h(9) = 3$. Find the number of positive divisors $k$ of $N$ such that $h(k)$ is odd.

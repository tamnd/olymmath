Triangle $\triangle ABC$ has $AB = 3 + \sqrt{2}$ and $AC = 1 + \sqrt{2}$. Point $D$ lies on side $\overline{BC}$ such that $AD = 2 - \sqrt{2}$. The internal angle bisector of $\angle BAD$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $\triangle BAD$ at $X \ne A$, and the internal angle bisector of $\angle CAD$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $\triangle CAD$ at $Y \ne A$. Given $\overline{XY} \parallel \overline{BC}$, compute $XY$.

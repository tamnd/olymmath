Square $ABCD$, regular pentagon $ABEFG$, and regular hexagon $ABHIJK$ all lie in the same plane so that the square lies inside the pentagon, and the pentagon lies inside the hexagon. Compute the degree measure of $\angle DGK$ which is less than $180^\circ$.

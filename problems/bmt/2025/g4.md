Let triangle $\triangle ABC$ be equilateral. Points $D$ and $E$ lie on sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$, respectively, such that $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$ and $\overline{CD} \perp \overline{BE}$. Compute $\frac{AD}{AB}$.

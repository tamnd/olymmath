Circles $I$ and $O$ have radii $1$ and $8$, respectively, and the distance between their centers is $4$. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the set of all circles simultaneously internally tangent to $O$ and externally tangent to $I$. Compute the area enclosed by the curve formed from the centers of all circles in $\mathcal{C}$.

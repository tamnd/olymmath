In three-dimensional coordinate space, a *lattice point* is a point whose coordinates are all integers. A triangular prism has all vertices at lattice points. Its triangular faces are both parallel to the $xy$-plane, and its rectangular faces are all parallel to the $z$-axis. The prism has $34$ lattice points on its edges (including the vertices), $72$ lattice points in the interior of its faces (not including the edges), and $126$ lattice points in its interior (not including the faces). What is the volume of the prism?

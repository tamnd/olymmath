Triangle $\triangle ABC$ has $AB = 4$, $AC = 5$, and $BC = 6$. Points $E$ and $F$ are the midpoints of sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$, respectively. Let $P$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to side $\overline{BC}$, and let $H$ be the midpoint of $\overline{AP}$. The circumcircles of triangles $\triangle HEB$ and $\triangle HFC$ intersect at $K \ne H$. Compute $AK^2$.

Ariel places $4$ green stickers, $4$ orange stickers, and $4$ purple stickers on the faces of two fair $6$-sided dice such that each face has exactly one sticker, and each die has at least one sticker of every color. If these dice are rolled, the probability that two green faces are rolled is $\frac{1}{12}$, and the probability that two orange faces are rolled is $\frac{1}{12}$. What is the probability that two purple faces are rolled?

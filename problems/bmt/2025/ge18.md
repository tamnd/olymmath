Benji writes down the decimal representation of a rational number greater than $10$. He observes that if he replaces the tens digit with $2$, the number decreases by $70\%$. If he instead replaces the tens digit with $8$, the number increases by $14\%$. By what percent would the number decrease if he replaced the tens digit with $5$?

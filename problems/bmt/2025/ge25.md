A *factor tree* for a composite number $n$ is a binary tree with root $n$ and two children, $a$ and $b$, such that $a$ and $b$ are proper divisors of $n$ with $ab = n$, and whose subtrees rooted at $a$ and $b$ are themselves factor trees for $a$ and $b$, respectively. A factor tree for a prime number is the single node containing the number itself.

How many distinct factor trees are there for $120$?

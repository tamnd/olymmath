Ylann has four shirts (red, orange, yellow, and green) and two pairs of pants (black and white). An outfit consists of one shirt and one pair of pants. If Ylann refuses to wear a red shirt with white pants, and he also refuses to wear a yellow shirt with black pants, how many acceptable outfits can he make?

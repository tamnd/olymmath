For each real number $n$, let $\lfloor n \rfloor$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $n$, and let $\lceil n \rceil$ denote the least integer greater than or equal to $n$. Compute
$$\int_0^\infty \frac{(-1)^{\lfloor x \rfloor}}{\lceil x \rceil^2 - \lfloor x \rfloor^2} \, \mathrm{d}x.$$

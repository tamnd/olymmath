Evan wants to grow a garden, so he visits a florist selling $n$ distinct flowers. He repeatedly rolls a fair $n$-sided die with faces labeled from $1$ to $n$ until he rolls a number other than $1$. Evan then calculates $N$, the number of ways he can choose $\min\{k, n\}$ of the flowers, where $k$ is the number of $1$'s rolled (possibly zero). Compute the limit as $n$ goes to infinity of the expected value of $N$.

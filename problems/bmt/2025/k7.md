For positive real numbers $a$, let the circle $C(a)$ be the graph of $x^2 + (y - a)^2 = 4a$ in the coordinate plane. Let $f(a)$ be the fraction of the area of $C(a)$ that lies above the parabola $y = x^2$. Find
$$\lim_{a \to \infty} f(a).$$

An integer $k$ is chosen uniformly at random from the set $\{0, 1, \cdots, 2^{2025} - 1\}$. Compute the probability that
$$\int_0^\infty e^{-2025x} x^k \sin(2025x) \, \mathrm{d}x > 0.$$

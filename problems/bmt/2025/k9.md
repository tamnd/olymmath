Let $f(a, b) = (a^3 - 4a)(b - a) - \frac{(b-a)^2}{2}$. Find the least real number $M$ such that for all finite, strictly increasing sequences $d_1, \ldots, d_n$ with $d_1 = -2$ and $d_n = 3$, the following inequality is satisfied:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} f(d_k, d_{k+1}) \le M.$$

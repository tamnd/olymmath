Arthur chooses three distinct integers between $2$ and $14$, inclusive, to be his winning numbers. If he rolls a fair $6$-sided die and a fair $8$-sided die with faces labeled $1$–$6$ and $1$–$8$, respectively, there is a $\frac{1}{3}$ chance that the sum of the faces rolled is a winning number. What is the largest winning number Arthur could have chosen?

Find the sum of all real numbers $x$ satisfying
$$\left(\sqrt{\frac{x^2 - 3x - 4}{6}}\right)^{x^2 + 3x - 18} = 1.$$
Recall that $\left(\sqrt{-4}\right)^2 = -4$.

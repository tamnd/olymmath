Let $[XYZ]$ denote the area of triangle $\triangle XYZ$. Right triangle $\triangle ABC$ has side lengths $AB = 3$, $BC = 4$, $AC = 5$. Points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are drawn inside $\triangle ABC$ such that:

- $[ABP] : [BCP] : [ACP] = 1 : 1 : 1$,
- $[ABQ] : [BCQ] : [ACQ] = 3 : 4 : 5$,
- $[ABR] : [BCR] : [ACR] = 1 : 28 : 1$.

What is the value of $[PQR]$?

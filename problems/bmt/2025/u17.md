Rectangle $ABCD$ has $AB = CD = \sqrt{3}$ and $AD = BC = 1$. Points $A', B', C', D'$ are constructed such that $AB = A'B$, $BC = B'C$, $CD = C'D$, $AD = AD'$ and the areas of quadrilaterals $A'BCD$, $AB'CD$, $ABC'D$, and $ABCD'$ are all maximized. Find the area of quadrilateral $A'B'C'D'$.

A base-$2025$ palindrome is a positive integer that reads the same forward and backward when written in base-$2025$. For each positive integer $n$, let $P(n)$ be the number of integers between $1$ and $n$, inclusive, that are base-$2025$ palindromes. Find the smallest real number $x$ such that that $x\sqrt{n} \ge P(n)$ for all positive integers $n$.

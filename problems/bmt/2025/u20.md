Let $N_{19}$ be the **leading two digits of the decimal representation** of the answer to problem 19.

The $24$ lines $y = \pm 4x, y = \pm 8x, \ldots, y = \pm 48x$ and the curve $y = N_{19}x^2 + N_{19}$ are drawn in the coordinate plane. How many (possibly infinitely large) distinct regions of the coordinate plane are in the resulting graph?

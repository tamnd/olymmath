Aarush and Brian are watching a sequence of fair coin flips. Both players start with $0$ points. Aarush gains a point when the coin lands on heads, and Brian gains a point when the coin lands on tails. If Brian reaches $2$ points, both players reset their points to $0$. What is the expected number coin flips it takes for Aarush to reach $9$ points for the first time?

There is a unique finite sequence of positive integers $(a_1, \ldots, a_n)$ such that $a_i^2 \le a_{i+1}$ for each $1 \le i < n$ and
$$\prod_{j=1}^{n} \left(1 + \frac{1}{a_j}\right) = 2025.$$
Find $n + a_n$.

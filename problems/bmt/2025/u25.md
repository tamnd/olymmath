Pentagon $ABCDE$ has $AB = 3$, $BC = 4$, $CD = 5$, $DE = 6$, and $EA = 7$, with all vertices lying on a circle. The five diagonals of $ABCDE$ enclose a smaller pentagon. Compute the area of this smaller pentagon. Submit your answer as a positive real number $E$ to at most $5$ decimal places; if the correct answer is $A$, your score for this problem is
$$\operatorname{round}\left(25\exp\left(-\frac{3}{2}\left(\max\left(\frac{E^2}{A^2}, \frac{A}{E}\right) - 1\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}\right)\right).$$

Kiran's coloring sheet has six squares, as shown below. Kiran will color each square a different color: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, or purple. How many different ways can the squares be colored according to the following rules?

- The red square touches the purple square and the orange square.
- The yellow square touches the orange square and the green square.
- The blue square touches the green square and the purple square.

Recall that in an arithmetic sequence, the difference between consecutive terms is constant, and in a geometric sequence, the ratio of consecutive terms is constant. The common difference of the arithmetic sequence $\{a_n\}$ equals the common ratio of the geometric sequence $\{g_n\}$. Given $a_1 = 0$, $g_1 = 1$, and $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 = g_1 + g_2 + g_3$, find the unique positive integer $x$ for which $a_x = g_x$.

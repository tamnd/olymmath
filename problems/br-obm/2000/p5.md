Let $ X$ the set of all sequences $ \{a_1, a_2,\ldots , a_{2000}\}$ , such that each of the first 1000 terms is 0, 1 or 2, and each of the remaining terms is 0 or 1. The *distance* between two members $ a$ and $ b$ of $ X$ is defined as the number of $ i$ for which $ a_i$ and $ b_i$ are different.

Find the number of functions $ f : X \to X$ which preserve the distance.

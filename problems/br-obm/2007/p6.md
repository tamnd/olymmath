Given real numbers $ x_1 < x_2 < \ldots < x_n$ such that every real number occurs at most two times among the differences $ x_j - x_i$ , $ 1\leq i < j \leq n$ , prove that there exists at least $ \lfloor n/2\rfloor$ real numbers that occurs exactly one time among such differences.

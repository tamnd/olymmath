Let $Z$ be the point where line $AX$ meets $t_{b}$ and $Y$ the point where line $BX$ meets $t_{a}$ . Show that the three lines $YZ$ , $t_{x}$ and $AB$ are either concurrent (i.e., all pass through the same point) or parallel.

Let $O$ be the centre of a circle and $A$ a fixed interior point of the circle different from $O$ . Determine all points $P$ on the circumference of the circle such that the angle $OPA$ is a maximum.

[asy]
import graph;

unitsize(2 cm);

pair A, O, P;

A = (0.5,0.2);
O = (0,0);
P = dir(80);

draw(Circle(O,1));
draw(O--A--P--cycle);

label(" $A$ ", A, E);
label(" $O$ ", O, S);
label(" $P$ ", P, N);
[/asy]

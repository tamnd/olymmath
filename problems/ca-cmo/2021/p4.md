$$
\operatorname{gcd}(f(f(x)), f(x+y))=\operatorname{gcd}(x, y)
$$

for all pairs of positive integers $x$ and $y$.
Find all positive integers $m$ such that $f(m)=m$ for all Canadian functions $f$.

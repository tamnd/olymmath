Consider the binomial coefficients $\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}\ (k=1,2,\ldots n-1)$. Determine all positive integers $n$ for which $\binom{n}{1},\binom{n}{2},\ldots ,\binom{n}{n-1}$ are all even numbers.

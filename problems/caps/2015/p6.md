Let $n$ be even positive integer. There are $n$ real positive numbers written on the blackboard. In every step, we choose two numbers, erase them, and replace *each* of then by their product. Show that for any initial $n$ -tuple it is possible to obtain $n$ equal numbers on the blackboard after a finite number of steps.

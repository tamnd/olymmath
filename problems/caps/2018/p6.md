We say that a positive integer $n$ is *fantastic* if there exist positive rational numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $$ n = a + \frac 1a + b + \frac 1b. $$ **(a)** Prove that there exist infinitely many prime numbers $p$ such that no multiple of $p$ is fantastic.**(b)** Prove that there exist infinitely many prime numbers $p$ such that some multiple of $p$ is fantastic.

We consider positive integers $n$ having at least six positive divisors. Let the positive divisors of $n$ be arranged in a sequence $(d_i)_{1\le i\le k}$ with $$ 1=d_1<d_2<\dots <d_k=n (k\ge 6). $$ Find all positive integers $n$ such that $$ n=d_5^2+d_6^2. $$

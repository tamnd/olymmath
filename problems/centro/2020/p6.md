A positive integer $N$ is *interoceanic* if its prime factorization $$ N=p_1^{x_1}p_2^{x_2}\cdots p_k^{x_k} $$ satisfies $$ x_1+x_2+\dots +x_k=p_1+p_2+\cdots +p_k. $$ Find all interoceanic numbers less than 2020.

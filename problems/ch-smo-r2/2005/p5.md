Let $M$ be a set with $n$ elements. Determine the number of ways to select three subsets $A,B,C$ of $M$ such that $$ A \cap B \neq \emptyset, B \cap C \neq \emptyset, C \cap A \neq \emptyset, $$ $$ A \cap B \cap C = \emptyset. $$

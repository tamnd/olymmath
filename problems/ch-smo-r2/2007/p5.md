On a circle $k$ there are five different points $A,M,B,C$ and $D$ in this order and let $MA = MB$. The lines $AC$ and $MD$ intersect in $P$, and the lines $BD$ and $MC$ intersect in $Q$. The line $PQ$ intersects $k$ in $X$ and $Y$. Show that $MX = MY$.

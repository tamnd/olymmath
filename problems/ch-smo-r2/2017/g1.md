Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB \neq AC$ and circumcircle $k$. The tangent of $k$ at $A$ intersects $BC$ at $P$. The angle bisector of $\angle APB$ intersects $AB$ at $D$ and $AC$ at $E$. Show that the triangle $ADE$ is isosceles.

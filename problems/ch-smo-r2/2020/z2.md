Let $p$ be a prime number and $a$, $b$, $c$ and $n$ positive integers with $a, b, c<p$ such that the three assertions $$ p^2 \mid a + (n-1)\cdot b, p^2 \mid b + (n-1)\cdot c, p^2 \mid c + (n-1)\cdot a $$ hold. Show that $n$ is not a prime number.

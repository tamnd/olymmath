Let $a$ and $b$ be fixed positive numbers. Depending on $a$ and $b$, find the smallest possible value of the sum $$ \frac{x^2}{(ay+bz)(az+by)}+\frac{y^2}{(az+bx)(ax+bz)}+\frac{z^2}{(ax+by)(ay+bx)}, $$ where $x,y,z$ are positive real numbers.

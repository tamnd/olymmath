Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $D, E, F$ be the midpoints of $BC, CA, AB$ respectively. The medians $AD$, $BE$ and $CF$ intersect at centroid $S$. At least two of the quadrilaterals $$ AFSE, BDSF, CESD $$ are cyclic. Show that the triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

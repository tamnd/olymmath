Let $ABCDEF$ be a convex hexagon with a circumcircle. Prove that the diagonals $AD, BE$ and $CF$ intersect at a point if and only if $$ \frac{AB}{BC} \cdot \frac{CD}{DE} \cdot \frac{EF}{FA} = 1. $$

Some sides and diagonals of a regular $ n$ -gon form a connected path that visits each vertex exactly once. A *parallel pair* of edges is a pair of two different parallel edges of the path. Prove that
(a) if $ n$ is even, there is at least one *parallel pair*.
(b) if $ n$ is odd, there can't be one single *parallel pair*.

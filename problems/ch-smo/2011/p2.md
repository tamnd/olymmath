Let $\triangle{ABC}$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $D$ , $E$ , $F$ be points on $BC$ , $CA$ , $AB$ , respectively, such that $$\angle{AFE}=\angle{BFD}\text{,} \angle{BDF}=\angle{CDE} \text{and} \angle{CED}=\angle{AEF}\text{.}$$ Prove that $D$ , $E$ and $F$ are the feet of the perpendiculars through $A$ , $B$ and $C$ on $BC$ , $CA$ and $AB$ , respectively.

*(Swiss Mathematical Olympiad 2011, Final round, problem 2)*

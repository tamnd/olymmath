Show that there is no infinite sequence of prime numbers $p_1,p_2,p_3,\dots$ that fulfils $p_{k+1}=2p_k-1$ or $p_{k+1}=2p_k+1$ for every $k$.

*Remark:* The formula might be different for different values of $k$.

Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ be a sequence of integers such that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ $$ \sum_{d\mid n}a_d = 2^n. $$ Prove that for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n$ is a divisor of $a_n$.

*Remark:* For $n=6$ the equation is $a_1+a_2+a_3+a_6=2^6$.

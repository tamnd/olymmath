Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $AB\neq BC$ and circumcircle $k$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the points of intersection of $k$ with the internal and external angle bisector of $\angle CBA$, respectively. Let $AC$ and $PQ$ intersect at $D$. Determine the ratio $AD:DC$.

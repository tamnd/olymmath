Determine the largest positive intger $n$ such that for all real numbers $a,b,c,d$ we have $$ (n+2) \sqrt{a^2+b^2} + (n+1) \sqrt{a^2+c^2} + (n+1)\sqrt{a^2+d^2} \geq n(a+b+c+d). $$

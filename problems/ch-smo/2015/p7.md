Let $a,b,c$ be real numbers such that $$ \frac{a}{b+c}+\frac{b}{c+a}+\frac{c}{a+b} = 1. $$ Determine all values that can be attained by the expression $$ \frac{a^2}{b+c}+\frac{b^2}{c+a}+\frac{c^2}{a+b}. $$

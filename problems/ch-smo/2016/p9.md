Let $n \ge 2$ be a natural number. For an $n$ -element subset $F$ of $\{1, . . . , 2n\}$ we define $m(F)$ as the minimum of all $lcm \, (x, y)$ , where $x$ and $y$ are two distinct elements of $F$ . Find the maximum value of $m(F)$ .

Let $n$ be a natural number and $p, q$ be prime numbers such that the following statements hold: $$ pq | n^p + 2 $$ $$ n + 2 | n^p + q^p. $$ Show that there is a natural number $m$ such that $q|4^mn + 2$ holds.

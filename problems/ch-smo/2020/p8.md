Let $n$ be a positive integer. Let $x_1\leq x_2\leq\ldots\leq x_n$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $x_1+x_2+\ldots+x_n=0$ and $x_1^2+x_2^2+\ldots+x_n^2=1$. Prove that $x_1x_n\leq -1/n$.

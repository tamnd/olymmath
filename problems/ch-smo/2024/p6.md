Let $f\colon \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function such that $$ f(x+y)f(x-y) \ge f(x)^2 - f(y)^2 $$ for every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$. Assume that the inequality is strict for some $x_0,y_0 \in \mathbb{R}$. Prove that $f(x)\ge 0$ for every $x\in\mathbb{R}$ or $f(x)\le 0$ for every $x\in\mathbb{R}§$.

Determine all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}_{>0} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that $$ y\cdot \min\!\Big(f(xy),\ f(x)\Big) = \min\!\Big(f\Big(\frac{x}{y}\Big),\ f(x)\Big) $$ for all $x,y\in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$.

Determine all sequences $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ of rational numbers, such that for all natural numbers $m$, $$ \frac{(x_1)^m + (x_2)^m + \dots + (x_n)^m}{n} $$ is equal to some rational number raised to the power of $m$.

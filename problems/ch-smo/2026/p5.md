We call a strictly increasing sequence $a_1,a_2,\dots, a_{2026}$ of positive integers *indivisible* if for all $2\leq k\leq 2026$, the sum of any $k$ consecutive terms is **not** divisible by $k$.

Prove that an indivisible sequence exists and determine the smallest possible value of the last term in an indivisible sequence.

We call a finite sequence of integers $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_n$ quadratic, if $|a_k-a_{k-1}|=k^2$ holds for $1 \leq k\leq n$.

a) Prove that for any two integers $b$ and $c$, there always exists a positive integer $n$ and a quadratic sequence $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_n$ such that $a_0=b$ and $a_n=c$.

b) Find the smallest positive integer $n$, for which there exists a quadratic sequence $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_n$ such that $a_0=0$ and $a_n=1997$.

Let $f\colon\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\} \rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ be a function, where

- $f(x)-f(y)=f(x)f(1/y)-f(1/x)f(y)$ for all $x,y \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$,

- $f(a)=1/2$ for some $a \in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$.

Determine $f(-1)$.

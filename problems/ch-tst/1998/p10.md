For the function $f\colon\mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ the following holds

- $f(x)\leq 1$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

- $f(x+13/42)+f(x)=f(x+1/6)+f(x+1/7)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

Show that $f$ is periodic.

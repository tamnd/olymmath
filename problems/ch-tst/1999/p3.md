Determine all functions $f\colon\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\} \rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ for which the following holds $$ \frac{1}{x}f(-x)+f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=x \forall x \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}. $$

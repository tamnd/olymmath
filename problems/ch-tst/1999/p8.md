Determine all integers $n$ for which there exists positive real numbers $0<a_1 \leq a_2 \leq \ldots \leq a_n$ with $$ \sum_{k=1}^n a_k=96, \sum_{k=1}^n a_k^2=144, \sum_{k=1}^n a_k^3=216. $$

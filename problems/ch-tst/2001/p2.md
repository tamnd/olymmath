Let $a,b$ and $c$ be the sides of a triangle. Prove the following inequality and determine when equality holds. $$ \sqrt{a+b-c} + \sqrt{c+a-b} + \sqrt{b+c-a} \leq \sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b} + \sqrt{c}. $$

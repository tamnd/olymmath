Let $n$ be a positive integer with at least four different positive divisors. Let the four smallest of these divisors be $d_1, d_2, d_3, d_4$. Find all such numbers $n$ for which $$ d_1^2 + d_2^2 + d_3^2 + d_4^2 = n. $$

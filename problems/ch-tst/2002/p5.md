Find all functions $f\colon\mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, for which:

- $f(x-1-f(x))=f(x)-1-x$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

- The set $\{f(x)/x\mid x \in \mathbb{R},\ x\neq 0\}$ is finite.

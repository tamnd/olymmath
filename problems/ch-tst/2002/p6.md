Let $x_1 , x_2 , x_3 , \ldots$ be a sequence of integers with the properties

- $1 = x_1 < x_2 < x_3 < \ldots,$

- $x_{n+1}\leq 2n $ for $ n \geq 1.$

Show that for every positive integer $k$ there are two indices $i$ and $j$ with $k = x_i - x_j$.

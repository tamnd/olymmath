Prove for each positive real number $a$ and each integer $n\geq 1$, the following inequality holds $$ a^{n} + \frac{1}{a^{n}} - 2 \geq n^{2}\left(a + \frac{1}{a} - 2\right), $$ and determine all equality cases.

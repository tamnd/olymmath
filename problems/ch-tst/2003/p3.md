Find the largest real number $C_1$ and the smallest real number $C_2$ such that for all positive numbers $a,b,c,d,e$ applies $$ C_1<\frac{a}{a+b}+\frac{b}{b+c}+\frac{c}{c+d}+\frac{d}{d+e}+\frac{e}{e+a}<C_2. $$

Find all injective functions $f\colon\mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that for all real numbers $x \neq y$ the following holds $$ f\left(\frac{x+y}{x-y}\right)=\frac{f(x)+f(y)}{f(x)-f(y)}. $$

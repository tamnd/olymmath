Let $X$ be a set with $n$ elements and let $A_1, A_2, \ldots A_n$ be different subsets of $X$. Show: There is an $x \in X$ such that the sets $$ A_1 \setminus \{x\}, A_2 \setminus \{x\}, \ldots , A_n \setminus \{x\} $$ are all different.

Let $n \geq 1$ be a positive integer. Let a regular $4n$-gon of side length $1$ be arbitrarily divided into finitely many parallelograms.

a) Prove that at least one of the parallelograms in the decomposition is a rectangle.

b) Determine the sum of the areas of all rectangles in the decomposition.

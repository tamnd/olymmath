Let $T$ be the set of all triples $(p,q,r)$ of non-negative integers. Determine all functions $f\colon T \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ for which the following holds $$ f(p,q,r)= \begin{cases} 0,&\text{if }pqr=0\\ 1+\frac{1}{6}\{\phantom{+}f(p+1,q-1,r)+f(p-1,q+1,r)\\ \phantom{1+\frac{1}{6}}+f(p-1,q,r+1)+f(p+1,q,r-1)\\ \phantom{1+\frac{1}{6}}+f(p,q+1,r-1)+f(p,q-1,r+1)\},&\text{else.} \end{cases} $$

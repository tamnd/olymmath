Let $n\geq 2$ be a positive integer. Show that the polynomial $$ (x^2-1^2)(x^2-2^2)(x^2-3^2)\cdots (x^2-n^2)+1 $$ cannot be written as a product of two non-constant polynomials with integer coefficients.

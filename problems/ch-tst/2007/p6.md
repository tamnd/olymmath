Let $a,b,c$ be positive real numbers with $a+b+c \geq abc$. Prove that at least two of the following three inequalities are correct: $$ \frac{2}{a}+\frac{3}{b}+\frac{6}{c} \geq 6, \frac{2}{b}+\frac{3}{c}+\frac{6}{a} \geq 6, \frac{2}{c}+\frac{3}{a}+\frac{6}{b} \geq 6. $$

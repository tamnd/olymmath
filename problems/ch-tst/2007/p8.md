Let $ABCDE$ be a convex pentagon with $$ \angle BAC = \angle CAD = \angle DAE \text{and} \angle ABC = \angle ACD = \angle ADE. $$ The diagonals $BD$ and $CE$ meet in $P$. Show that the line $AP$ intersects the side $CD$ at its midpoint.

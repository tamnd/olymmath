Let $GERMANYISHOT$ be a regular dodecagon. The lines $GN$ and $MI$ intersect at point $P$. Prove that

a) the circumcircle of triangle $GIP$ is the same size as the circumcircle of $GERMANYISHOT$.

b) the distance $PA$ is the same length as one side of $GERMANYISHOT$.

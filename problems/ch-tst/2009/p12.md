Let $x,y,z$ be real numbers that fulfil the equation $x+y+z=xy+yz+zx$. Prove the inequality $$ \frac{x}{x^2+1}+\frac{y}{y^2+1}+\frac{z}{z^2+1} \geq -\frac{1}{2}. $$

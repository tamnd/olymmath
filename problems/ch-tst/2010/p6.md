Find all positive real solutions $(a,b,c,d)$ of the equation $$ \frac{a^2-bd}{b+2c+d}+\frac{b^2-ca}{c+2d+a}+\frac{c^2-db}{d+2a+b}+\frac{d^2-ac}{a+2b+c} = 0. $$

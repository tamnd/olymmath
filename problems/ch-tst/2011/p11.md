Let $x_1,\dots,x_{8}\ge 0$ be real numbers such that for $i=1,\dots, 8$ $x_{i}+x_{i+1}+x_{i+2}\le 1$, where $x_{9}=x_{1}$ and $x_{10}=x_2$. Prove the inequality $$ \sum_{i=1}^{8}x_{i}x_{i+2}\le 1 $$ and find all equality cases.

Let $a > 1$ be a natural number and let $f$ and $g$ be polynomials with integer coefficients. Suppose there is a natural number $n_0$ such that $g(n) >0$ for all $n \geq n_0$ and $$ f(n)\mid a^{g(n)} - 1 \text{for all} \ n \geq n_0. $$ Show that $f$ must be constant.

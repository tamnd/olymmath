Let $n\ge 2$ be a positive integer. Depending on $n$, find the largest natural number $d$ such that a permutation $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ of the numbers $1,2,\dots,n$ exists with $$ |a_i-a_{i+1}| \geq d, \ \ \text{for }i=1,2,\dots,n-1. $$

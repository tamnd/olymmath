Let $a,b,c$ be positive real numbers with $abc \geq 1$. Prove the inequality $$ \frac{a^4-1}{ab^3+abc+ac^3} + \frac{b^4-1}{bc^3+abc+ba^3} + \frac{c^4-1}{ca^3+abc+cb^3}\ge 0. $$

Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle and $AD$ the diameter of the circumcircle of a triangle $ABC$. Let $E$ and $F$ be points on the rays $BA$ and $CA$ with $$ BE = CF = \frac{AB+BC+CA}{2}. $$ Show that the lines $EF$ and $DI$ are perpendicular.

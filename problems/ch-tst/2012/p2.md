An integer $m$ is a *true power* if there are positive integers $a$ and $n$ such that $n>1$ and $m=a^n$.

a) Show that there are $2012$ distinct positive integers such that no non-empty subset of them sums to a real power.

b) Show that there are $2012$ distinct positive integers such that every non-empty subset of them sums to a real power.

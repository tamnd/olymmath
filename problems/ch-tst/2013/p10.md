Find the largest $k>0$ such that for all $x,y,z>0$ with $x+y+z\le kxyz$: $$ \frac{1}{x+y+\sqrt{xy}}+\frac{1}{y+z+\sqrt{yz}}+\frac{1}{z+x+\sqrt{zx}}\le\sqrt{\frac{3}{k}}. $$

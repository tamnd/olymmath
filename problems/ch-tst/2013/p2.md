Let $P$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients such that for all $u,v \in \mathbb{N}$: $$ u^{2^{2013}}-v^{2^{2013}} \mid P(u)-P(v). $$ Show that there is a polynomial $Q$ with integer coefficients such that $P(x)=Q(x^{2^{2013}})$ holds.

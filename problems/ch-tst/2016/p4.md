Determine all positive integers $n$ such that for any real numbers $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ the following holds: $$ \left(\frac{x_1^n + \ldots + x_n^n}{n} - x_1 \cdot\ldots\cdot x_n\right) \left(x_1 + \ldots + x_n \right) \geq 0. $$

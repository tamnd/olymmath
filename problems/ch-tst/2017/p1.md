Determine all functions $f\colon \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ for which

- $f(p) > 0$ for all prime numbers $p$,

- $p \mid (f(x) + f(p))^{f(p)} - x$ for all prime numbers $p$ and all $x \in \mathbb{Z}$.

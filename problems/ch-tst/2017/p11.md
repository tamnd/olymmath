Let $B=(-1,0)$ and $C=(1,0)$ be fixed points in the plane. A non-empty, bounded subset $S$ of the plane is called *kind* if the following conditions are met:

- There is a point $T$ in $S$ such that for each other point $Q$ in $S$, the segment $TQ$ is completely inside $S$.

- For each triangle $P_1P_2P_3$, there exists a unique point $A$ in $S$ and a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1, 2, 3\}$ such that the triangles $ABC$ and $P_{\sigma(1)}P_{\sigma(2)}P_{\sigma(3)}$ are similar.

Show that there are two kind subsets $S$ and $S'$ of the set $\{(x,y):x\geq 0,y\geq 0\}$ with the property that the product $BA\cdot BA'$ is independent of the triangle $P_1P_2P_3$, where $A\in S$ and $A'\in S'$ are the points guaranteed by the second condition above for the arbitrary triangle $P_1P_2P_3$.

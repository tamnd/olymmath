Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ be positive real numbers. Show that there exist $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in\{-1,1\}$ such that $$ \sum_{i=1}^na_ix_i^2\geq\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_ix_i\right)^2. $$

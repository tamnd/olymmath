Determine all the pairs $(f,g)$ of functions $f,g \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that for all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$

- $f(x)\geq 0$,

- $f(x+g(y)) = f(x) + f(y) + 2yg(x) - f(y-g(y))$.

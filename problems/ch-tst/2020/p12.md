Let $a,b,c,d$ be positive real numbers such that $a+b+c+d=1$. Prove that $$ \left( \frac{a^2}{a+b}+\frac{b^2}{b+c}+\frac{c^2}{c+d}+\frac{d^2}{d+a}\right)^5 \geq 5^5\left(\frac{ac}{27}\right)^2. $$

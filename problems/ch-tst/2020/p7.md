Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{R}$ such that $0\leq f(x)\leq 2x$ for all $x\geq 0$ and such that for all $x,y\in \mathbb{R}$ $$ f(x+y)=f(x+f(y)). $$

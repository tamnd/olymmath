Let $n$ be a positive integer. Call a sequence of positive integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots , a_n$ *tame* if it satisfies $$ 1 \cdot a_1 \leq 2\cdot a_2\leq \ldots \leq n \cdot a_n. $$ Determine the number of tame permutations of $1,2, \ldots, n$.

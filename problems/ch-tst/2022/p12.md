Let $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ denote the set of positive real numbers. Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}_{>0} \to \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that $$ x + f(yf(x)+1) = xf(x+y) + yf(yf(x)) $$ for all positive real numbers $x$ and $y$.

Determine all positive integers $n\geq 2$ for which there exist $n$ distinct real numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n $ and a real number $r > 0$ such that $$ \big\{a_j-a_i \mid 1 \le i < j \le n \big\} = \big\{ r,r^2,\dots, r^{\binom{n}{2}} \big\}. $$

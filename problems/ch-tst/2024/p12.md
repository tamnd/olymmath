Determine all functions $f\colon\mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ such that $$ \underbrace{f(f(\cdots f}_{bf(a)}(a+1)\cdots)) = (a+1)f(b) $$ holds for all $a,b \in \mathbb{N}$.

Let $n \geq 4$ be an integer and let $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ and $b_1, \ldots, b_n$ be sequences of positive integers such that the $n+1$ products

$$\begin{aligned}a_1a_2 & \cdots a_{n-1}a_n, \\ b_1a_2 & \cdots a_{n-1}a_n, \\ b_1b_2 & \cdots a_{n-1}a_n, \\ & \vdots \\ b_1b_2 & \cdots b_{n-1}a_n, \\ b_1b_2 & \cdots b_{n-1}b_n,\end{aligned}$$

taken in this order, form a strictly increasing arithmetic progression. Determine the smallest possible common difference of this arithmetic progression in terms of $n$.

*Remark:* An arithmetic progression is a sequence of the form $a, a+r, a+2r, \ldots, a+kr$ where $a$, $r$ and $k$ are integers and $r$ is called the common difference.

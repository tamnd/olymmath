Determine all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}_{>0} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that $$ x\left(f(x)+f(y)\right) \ge f(y) \left(f(f(x)) + y\right) $$ for all $x,y\in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$.

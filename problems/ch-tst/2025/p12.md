Find all polynomials $P \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ for which there exists a non-constant polynomial $Q \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ satisfying the following: For all integers $a,b$ we have $P(b-a) \mid Q(b) - Q(a)$.

Let $f\colon \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} \to \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ be a function **that takes on infinitely many values**. Assume that for all non-negative integers $m$ and $n$ that satisfy $$ f(m+n) = \max\Big\lbrace f(0),\ f(1),\ \dots,\ f(m+n)\Big\rbrace, $$ we have $f(m+n) = f(m) + f(n)$.

Prove that there exist positive integers $A,B,C$ and $D$ such that for all non-negative $n$ we have $f(An+B) = Cn+D$.

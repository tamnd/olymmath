Find all real numbers $a$ for which there exist different real numbers $b, c, d$
different from $a$ such that the four tangents drawn to the curve $y = \sin (x)$ at the points $(a, \sin (a)),
(b, \sin (b)), (c, \sin (c))$ and $(d, \sin (d))$ form a rectangle.

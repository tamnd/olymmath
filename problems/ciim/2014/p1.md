Let $g:[2013,2014]\to\mathbb{R}$ a function that satisfy the following two conditions:

i) $g(2013)=g(2014) = 0,$ ii) for any $a,b \in [2013,2014]$ it hold that $g\left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right) \leq g(a) + g(b).$ Prove that $g$ has zeros in any open subinterval $(c,d) \subset[2013,2014].$

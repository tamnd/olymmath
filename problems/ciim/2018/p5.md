Consider the transformation $$ T(x,y,z) = (\sin y + \sin z - \sin x,\sin z + \sin x - \sin y,\sin x +\sin y -\sin z). $$ Determine all the points $(x,y,z) \in [0,1]^3$ such that $T^n(x,y,z) \in [0,1]^3,$ for every $n \geq 1$ .

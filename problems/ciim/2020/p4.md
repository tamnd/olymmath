For each polynomial $P(x)$ with real coefficients, define $P_0=P(0)$ and $P_j(x)=x^j\cdot P^{(j)}(x)$ where $P^{(j)}$ denotes the $j$ -th derivative of $P$ for $j\geq 1$ .
Prove that there exists one unique sequence of real numbers $b_0, b_1, b_2, \dots$ such that for each polynomial $P(x)$ with real coefficients and for each $x$ real, we have $P(x)=b_0P_0+\sum_{k\geq 1}b_kP_k(x)=b_0P_0+b_1P_1(x)+b_2P_2(x)+\dots$

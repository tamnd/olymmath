Let $\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ be the set of positive integers.**a)** Prove that there is only one function $f:\mathbb{Z}^{+} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}^{+}$ , strictly increasing, such that $f(f(n))=2n+1$ for every $n\in \mathbb{Z}^{+}$ .**b)** For the function in **a**. Prove that for every $n\in \mathbb{Z}^{+}$ $\frac{4n+1}{3}\leq f(n)\leq \frac{3n+1}{2}$ **c)** Prove that in each inequality side of **b** the equality can reach by infinite positive integers $n$ .

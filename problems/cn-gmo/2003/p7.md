Let the sides of a scalene triangle $ \triangle ABC$ be $ AB = c,$ $ BC = a,$ $ CA =b,$ and $ D, E , F$ be points on $ BC, CA, AB$ such that $ AD, BE, CF$ are angle bisectors of the triangle, respectively. Assume that $ DE = DF.$ Prove that

(1) $ \frac{a}{b+c} = \frac{b}{c+a} + \frac{c}{a+b}$

(2) $ \angle BAC > 90^{\circ}.$

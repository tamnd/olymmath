Let $ m$ and $ n$ be positive integers with $ m > n \geq 2.$ Set $ S = \{1, 2, \ldots, m\},$ and $ T = \{a_l, a_2, \ldots, a_n\}$ is a subset of S such that every number in $ S$ is not divisible by any two distinct numbers in $ T.$ Prove that
$$ \sum^n_{i = 1} \frac {1}{a_i} < \frac {m + n}{m}.
$$

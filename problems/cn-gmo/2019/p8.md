For a tournament with $8$ vertices, if from any vertex it is impossible to follow a route to return to itself, we call the graph a *good* graph. Otherwise, we call it a *bad* graph. Prove that $(1)$ there exists a tournament with $8$ vertices such that after changing the orientation of any at most $7$ edges of the tournament, the graph is always a*bad* graph; $(2)$ for any tournament with $8$ vertices, one can change the orientation of at most $8$ edges of the tournament to get a *good* graph.
(A tournament is a complete graph with directed edges.)

Let $O$ be the circumcenter of triangle $\triangle ABC$, where $\angle BAC=120^{\circ}$. The tangent at $A$ to $(ABC)$ meets the tangents at $B,C$ at $(ABC)$ at points $P,Q$ respectively. Let $H,I$ be the orthocenter and incenter of $\triangle OPQ$ respectively. Define $M,N$ as the midpoints of arc $\overset{\frown}{BAC}$ and $OI$ respectively, and let $MN$ meet $(ABC)$ again at $D$. Prove that $AD$ is perpendicular to $HI$.

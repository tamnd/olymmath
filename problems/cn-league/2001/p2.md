If nonnegative reals $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ satisfy
$$
\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2 + 2\sum_{1 \leq k < j \leq n} \sqrt{\frac{k}{j}}x_kx_j = 1
$$
what are the minimum and maximum values of $\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$ ?

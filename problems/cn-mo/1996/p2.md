Let $n$ be a natural number. Suppose that $x_0=0$ and that $x_i>0$ for all $i\in\{1,2,\ldots ,n\}$ . If $\sum_{i=1}^nx_i=1$ , prove that
$$1\leq\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{x_i}{\sqrt{1+x_0+x_1+\ldots +x_{i-1}}\sqrt{x_i+\ldots+x_n}} < \frac{\pi}{2} $$

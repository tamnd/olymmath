$$(n + 1)a_1^n + na_2^n + (n - 1)a_3^n|(n + 1)b_1^n + nb_2^n + (n - 1)b_3^n$$
holds for all positive integers $n$ . Prove that there exists $k\in N$ such that $ b_i = ka_i$ for $ i = 1,2,3$ .

Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ are real numbers, prove that;
$$\sum_{i=1}^na_i^2-\sum_{i=1}^n a_i a_{i+1} \le \left\lfloor \frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor(M-m)^2.$$
where $a_{n+1}=a_1,M=\max_{1\le i\le n} a_i,m=\min_{1\le i\le n} a_i$ .

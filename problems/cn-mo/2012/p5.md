Consider a square-free even integer $n$ and a prime $p$ , such that
1) $(n,p)=1$ ;
2) $p\le 2\sqrt{n}$ ;
3) There exists an integer $k$ such that $p|n+k^2$ .
Prove that there exists pairwise distinct positive integers $a,b,c$ such that $n=ab+bc+ca$ .

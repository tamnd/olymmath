Given any positive integer $n$ , let $a_n$ be the real root of equation $x^3+\dfrac{x}{n}=1$ . Prove that
(1) $a_{n+1}>a_n$ ;
(2) $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{(i+1)^2a_i} <a_n$ .

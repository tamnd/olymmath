Given a sequence $\{ a_n\}_{n\in \mathbb{Z}^+}$ defined by $a_1=1$ and $a_{2k}=a_{2k-1}+a_k,a_{2k+1}=a_{2k}$ for all positive integer $k$.
Prove that, for any positive integer $n$, $a_{2^n}>2^{\frac{n^2}{4}}$.

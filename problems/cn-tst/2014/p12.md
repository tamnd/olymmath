Let $k$ be a fixed even positive integer, $N$ is the product of $k$ distinct primes $p_1,...,p_k$ , $a,b$ are two positive integers, $a,b\leq N$ . Denote $S_1=\{d|$ $d|N, a\leq d\leq b, d$ has even number of prime factors $\}$ , $S_2=\{d|$ $d|N, a\leq d\leq b, d$ has odd number of prime factors $\}$ ,
Prove: $|S_1|-|S_2|\leq C^{\frac{k}{2}}_k$

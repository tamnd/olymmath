Determine the greatest real number $ C $ , such that for every positive integer $ n\ge 2 $ , there exists $ x_1, x_2,..., x_n \in [-1,1]$ , so that $$ \prod_{1\le i<j\le n}(x_i-x_j) \ge C^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}} $$ .

Let $p$ and $c$ be an prime and a composite, respectively. Prove that there exist two integers $m,n,$ such that $$ 0<m-n<\frac{\textup{lcm}(n+1,n+2,\cdots,m)}{\textup{lcm}(n,n+1,\cdots,m-1)}=p^c. $$

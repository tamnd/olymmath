A sequence $a_1, a_2,\dots, a_n$ of positive integers is *alagoana*, if for every $n$ positive integer, one have these two conditions
I- $a_{n!} = a_1\cdot a_2\cdot a_3\cdots a_n$ II- The number $a_n$ is the $n$ -power of a positive integer.
Find all the sequence(s) *alagoana*.

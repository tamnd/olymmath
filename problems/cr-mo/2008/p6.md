Let $ O$ be the circumcircle of a $ \Delta ABC$ and let $ I$ be its incenter, for a point $ P$ of the plane let $ f(P)$ be the point obtained by reflecting $ P'$ by the midpoint of $ OI$ , with $ P'$ the homothety of $ P$ with center $ O$ and ratio $ \frac{R}{r}$ with $ r$ the inradii and $ R$ the circumradii,(understand it by $ \frac{OP}{OP'}=\frac{R}{r}$ ). Let $ A_1$ , $ B_1$ and $ C_1$ the midpoints of $ BC$ , $ AC$ and $ AB$ , respectively. Show that the rays $ A_1f(A)$ , $ B_1f(B)$ and $ C_1f(C)$ concur on the incircle.

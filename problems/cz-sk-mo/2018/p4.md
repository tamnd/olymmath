Let $a,b,c$ be integers which are lengths of sides of a triangle, $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$ and all the values $$ \frac{a^2+b^2-c^2}{a+b-c}, \frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{b+c-a}, \frac{c^2+a^2-b^2}{c+a-b} $$ are integers as well. Show that $(a+b-c)(b+c-a)(c+a-b)$ or $2(a+b-c)(b+c-a)(c+a-b)$ is a perfect square.

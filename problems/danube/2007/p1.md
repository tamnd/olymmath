Let $ n\ge2$ be a positive integer and denote by $ S_n$ the set of all permutations of the set $ \{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ . For $ \sigma\in S_n$ define $ l(\sigma)$ to be $ \min_{1\le i\le n-1}\left|\sigma(i+1)-\sigma(i)\right|$ . Determine $ \max_{\sigma\in S_n}l(\sigma)$ .

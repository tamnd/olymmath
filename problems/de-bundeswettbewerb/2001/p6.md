For each $ n \in \mathbb{N}$ we have two numbers $ p_n, q_n$ with the following property: For exactly $ n$ distinct integer numbers $ x$ the number $$ x^2 + p_n \cdot x + q_n$$ is the square of a natural number. (Note the definition of natural numbers includes the zero here.)

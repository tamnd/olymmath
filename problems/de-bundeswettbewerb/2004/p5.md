Let $k$ be a positive integer. A natural number $m$ is called *$k$ -typical* if each divisor of $m$ leaves the remainder $1$ when being divided by $k$ .

Prove:

**a)** If the number of all divisors of a positive integer $n$ (including the divisors $1$ and $n$ ) is $k$ -typical, then $n$ is the $k$ -th power of an integer.

**b)** If $k > 2$ , then the converse of the assertion **a)** is not true.

Let $n$ be an integer that is greater than $1$ . Prove that the following two statements are equivalent:
(A) There are positive integers $a, b$ and $c$ that are not greater than $n$ and for which that polynomial $ax^2 + bx + c$ has two different real roots $x_1$ and $x_2$ with $| x_2- x_1 | \le \frac{1}{n}$ (B) The number $n$ has at least two different prime divisors.

Many people use the social network "BWM". It is known that: By choosing any four people of that network there always is one that is a friend of the other three.
Is it then true that by choosing any four people there always is one that is a friend of everyone in "BWM"?**Note:** If member $A$ is a friend of member $B$ , then member $B$ also is a friend of member $A$ .

Let $a,b$ be positive even integers. A rectangle with side lengths $a$ and $b$ is split into $a \cdot b$ unit squares.
Anja and Bernd take turns and in each turn they color a square that is made of those unit squares. The person that can't color anymore, loses.
Anja starts. Find all pairs $(a,b)$ , such that she can win for sure.**Extension:** Solve the problem for positive integers $a,b$ that don't necessarily have to be even.**Note:** The *extension* actually was proposed at first. But since this is a homework competition that goes over three months and some cases were weird, the problem was changed to even integers.

**(a)** [Problem for class 11]

Let r be the inradius and $r_a$ , $r_b$ , $r_c$ the exradii of a triangle ABC. Prove that $\frac{1}{r}=\frac{1}{r_a}+\frac{1}{r_b}+\frac{1}{r_c}$ .

**(b)** [Problem for classes 12/13]

Let r be the radius of the insphere and let $r_a$ , $r_b$ , $r_c$ , $r_d$ the radii of the four exspheres of a tetrahedron ABCD. (An *exsphere* of a tetrahedron is a sphere touching one sideface and the extensions of the three other sidefaces.)

Prove that $\frac{2}{r}=\frac{1}{r_a}+\frac{1}{r_b}+\frac{1}{r_c}+\frac{1}{r_d}$ .

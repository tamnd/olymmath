A positive integer $n$ is called *smooth* if there exist integers $a_1,a_2,\dotsc,a_n$ satisfying
$$a_1+a_2+\dotsc+a_n=a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdot \dotsc \cdot a_n=n.$$
Find all smooth numbers.

Given two real numbers $p$ and $q$ , we study the following system of equations with variables $x,y \in \mathbb{R}$ :
\begin{align*} x^2+py+q&=0,
y^2+px+q&=0.
\end{align*}
Determine the number of distinct solutions $(x,y)$ in terms of $p$ and $q$ .

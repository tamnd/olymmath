Suppose that real numbers $x,y$ and $z$ satisfy the following equations:

\begin{align*}
x+\frac{y}{z} &=2,
y+\frac{z}{x} &=2,
z+\frac{x}{y} &=2.
\end{align*}

Show that $s=x+y+z$ must be equal to $3$ or $7$ .

*Note:* It is not required to show the existence of such numbers $x,y,z$ .

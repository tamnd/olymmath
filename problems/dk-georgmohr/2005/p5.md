For which real numbers $p$ does the system of equations

$$\begin{aligned}
x_1^4 + \frac{1}{x_1^2} &= p x_2, \\
x_2^4 + \frac{1}{x_2^2} &= p x_3, \\
&\vdots \\
x_{2004}^4 + \frac{1}{x_{2004}^2} &= p x_{2005}, \\
x_{2005}^4 + \frac{1}{x_{2005}^2} &= p x_1
\end{aligned}$$

have just one solution $(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{2005})$, where $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{2005}$ are real numbers?

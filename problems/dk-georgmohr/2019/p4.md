Georg writes a positive integer $a$ on a blackboard. As long as there is a number on the blackboard, he does the following each day: $\bullet$ If the last digit in the number on the blackboard is less than or equal to $5$ , he erases that last digit.
(If there is only this digit, the blackboard thus becomes empty.) $\bullet$ Otherwise he erases the entire number and writes $9$ times the number.
Can Georg choose $a$ in such a way that the blackboard never becomes empty?

A *miracle number* is a number formed from a positive integer as follows: First one adds 3 to the number, then the result is multiplied by 4, then 5 is added to the new result, and finally one multiplies by 6. A *wonder number* is a number formed from a positive integer as follows: First the number is multiplied by 3, then 4 is added to the result, then the new result is multiplied by 5, and finally one adds 6.

a) Prove that the number 318 is a miracle number, but not a wonder number.

b) Do there exist numbers which are both miracle numbers and wonder numbers?

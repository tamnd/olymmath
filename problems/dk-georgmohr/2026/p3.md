For a positive integer $n > 1$ one considers the $n - 1$ fractions

$$\frac{1}{n},\ \frac{2}{n},\ \frac{3}{n},\ \dots,\ \frac{n-1}{n}.$$

All the fractions are reduced as much as possible, and then one adds up their numerators. The result is called $f(n)$. For example $f(6)$ is determined by considering the fractions $\frac{1}{6}, \frac{2}{6}, \frac{3}{6}, \frac{4}{6}, \frac{5}{6}$, which are reduced to $\frac{1}{6}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{2}{3}, \frac{5}{6}$, yielding $f(6) = 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 5 = 10$.

Determine whether $f(2^{1001} \cdot 1001)$ is even or odd.

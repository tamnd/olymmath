A *lattice point* is a point in the plane with integer coordinates. Georg goes for a walk in the plane. He starts at $(0, 0)$ and walks from lattice point to lattice point in steps of length 1 horizontally or vertically. He ends the walk in $(0, 0)$ after more than two steps and has not visited any point more than once. Georg notices that there exists a lattice point $P$ which he has not visited so that every lattice point $Q$ along his walk satisfies that no lattice points other than $P$ and $Q$ lie on the line segment $PQ$.

Prove that 4 divides the number of steps in Georg's walk.

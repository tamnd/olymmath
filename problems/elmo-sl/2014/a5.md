Let $\mathbb R^\ast$ denote the set of nonzero reals. Find all functions $f: \mathbb R^\ast \to \mathbb R^\ast$ satisfying $$ f(x^2+y)+1=f(x^2+1)+\frac{f(xy)}{f(x)} $$ for all $x,y \in \mathbb R^\ast$ with $x^2+y\neq 0$ .

Let $p>13$ be a prime of the form $2q+1$ , where $q$ is prime. Find the number of ordered pairs of integers $(m,n)$ such that $0\le m<n<p-1$ and
$$3^m+(-12)^m\equiv 3^n+(-12)^n\pmod{p}.$$

*Alex Zhu.*

<details><summary>Note</summary>The original version asked for the number of solutions to $2^m+3^m\equiv 2^n+3^n\pmod{p}$ (still $0\le m<n<p-1$ ), where $p$ is a Fermat prime.</details>

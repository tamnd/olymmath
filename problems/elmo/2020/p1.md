Let $\mathbb{N}$ be the set of all positive integers. Find all functions $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that $$ f^{f^{f(x)}(y)}(z)=x+y+z+1 $$ for all $x,y,z \in \mathbb{N}$ .

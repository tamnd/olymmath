Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the number of sequences $a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2n}$ of integers in the range $[0, n]$ such that for all integers $0 \le k \le n$ and all nonnegative integers $m$, there exists an integer $k \le i \le 2k$ such that $\lfloor k/2^m \rfloor = a_i$.

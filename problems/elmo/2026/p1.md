Let $P_1$, $P_2$ and $P_3$ be distinct points on a circle $\Omega$. For all positive integers $1 \le i \le 23$, define $P_{i+3}$ as the reflection of $P_i$ over line $P_{i+1}P_{i+2}$. Prove that $P_{20}P_{26}$ is tangent to $\Omega$.

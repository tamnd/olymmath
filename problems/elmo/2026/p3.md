Determine all integers $n \ge 2$ such that there exists an integer polynomial $P(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ of degree at most $0.9n$ such that the range of $P$ modulo $n$ is the same as the set of zeros of $P$ modulo $n$.

*The range of $P$ modulo $n$ is the set of all integers $0 \le y \le n - 1$ such that there exists a positive integer $x$ where $P(x) - y$ is a multiple of $n$.*

*The set of zeros of $P$ modulo $n$ is the set of all integers $0 \le x \le n - 1$ such that $P(x)$ is a multiple of $n$.*

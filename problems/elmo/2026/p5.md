Let $\mathbb{N}$ denote the set of all positive integers. Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that
$$f\left(f(n)^m\right) = 2^{f(m)^n}$$
for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$.

Let $n$ be a positive integer. There are $n(n+1)$ points chosen on the circumference of a circle, and each of these points is colored one of $n$ colors such that each color appears exactly $n+1$ times. Elmo wishes to choose $n$ non-overlapping arcs of the circle such that both endpoints of each arc are selected points of the same color, and this color is different for each of the $n$ chosen arcs.

Prove that Elmo can select the arcs in at least $n+1$ distinct ways.

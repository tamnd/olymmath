A convex polygon of $n$ sides is considered. All its diagonals are drawn and we suppose that any three of them can only intersect on a vertex and that there is no pair of parallel diagonals. Under these conditions, we wish to compute

a) The total number of intersection points of these diagonals, excluding the vertices.

b) How many points, of these intersections, lie inside the polygon and how many lie outside.

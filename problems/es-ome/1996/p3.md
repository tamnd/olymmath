Consider the functions $ f(x) = ax^{2} + bx + c $, $ g(x) = cx^{2} + bx + a $, where a, b, c are real numbers. Given that $ |f(-1)| \leq 1 $, $ |f(0)| \leq 1 $, $ |f(1)| \leq 1 $, prove that $ |f(x)| \leq \frac{5}{4} $ and $ |g(x)| \leq 2 $ for $ -1 \leq x \leq 1 $.

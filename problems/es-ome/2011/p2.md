Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be positive real numbers. Prove that
$$ \frac{a}{b+c}+\frac{b}{c+a}+\frac{c}{a+b}+\sqrt{\frac{ab+bc+ca}{a^2+b^2+c^2}}\ge\frac52$$
and determine when equality holds.

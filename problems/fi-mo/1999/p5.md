An ordinary domino tile can be identified as a pair $(k,m)$ where numbers $k$ and $m$ can get values $0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$ and $6.$
Pairs $(k,m)$ and $(m, k)$ determine the same tile. In particular, the pair $(k, k)$ determines one tile.
We say that two domino tiles *match*, if they have a common component.
*Generalized n-domino tiles* $m$ and $k$ can get values $0, 1,... , n.$ What is the probability that two randomly chosen $n$ -domino tiles match?

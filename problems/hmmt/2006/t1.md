$$
\cdot \longleftarrow \uparrow^{\downarrow}
$$

![](https://cdn.mathpix.com/cropped/2025_01_24_9d8d7899d21870636906g-01.jpg?height=80&width=359&top_left_y=1863&top_left_x=921)

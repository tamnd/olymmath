Find all functions $f: \mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) f(y) & =f(x)+f(y)-f(x y) \\
1+f(x+y) & =f(x y)+f(x) f(y)
\end{aligned}
$$

for all rational numbers $x, y$.

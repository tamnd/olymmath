Triangle $A B C$ obeys $A B=2 A C$ and $\angle B A C=120^{\circ}$. Points $P$ and $Q$ lie on segment $B C$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
A B^{2}+B C \cdot C P & =B C^{2} \\
3 A C^{2}+2 B C \cdot C Q & =B C^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Find $\angle P A Q$ in degrees.

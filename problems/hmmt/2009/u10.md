Let $a, b$, and $c$ be real numbers. Consider the system of simultaneous equations in variables $x$ and $y$ :

$$
\begin{aligned}
a x+b y & =c-1 \\
(a+5) x+(b+3) y & =c+1
\end{aligned}
$$

Determine the value(s) of $c$ in terms of $a$ such that the system always has a solution for any $a$ and $b$.

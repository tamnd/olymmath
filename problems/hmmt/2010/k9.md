$$
\left(x+x^{\prime}\right)^{2}+x \cdot x^{\prime \prime}=\cos t
$$

with $x(0)=x^{\prime}(0)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{5}}$. Compute $x\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$.

Suppose that there are real numbers $a, b, c \geq 1$ and that there are positive reals $x, y, z$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
a^{x}+b^{y}+c^{z} & =4 \\
x a^{x}+y b^{y}+z c^{z} & =6 \\
x^{2} a^{x}+y^{2} b^{y}+z^{2} c^{z} & =9
\end{aligned}
$$

What is the maximum possible value of $c$ ?

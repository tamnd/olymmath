Let $a_{0}=-2, b_{0}=1$, and for $n \geq 0$, let

$$
\begin{aligned}
& a_{n+1}=a_{n}+b_{n}+\sqrt{a_{n}^{2}+b_{n}^{2}} \\
& b_{n+1}=a_{n}+b_{n}-\sqrt{a_{n}^{2}+b_{n}^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Find $a_{2012}$.

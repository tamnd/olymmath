Let $N=30^{2015}$. Find the number of ordered 4-tuples of integers $(A, B, C, D) \in\{1,2, \ldots, N\}^{4}$ (not necessarily distinct) such that for every integer $n, A n^{3}+B n^{2}+2 C n+D$ is divisible by $N$.

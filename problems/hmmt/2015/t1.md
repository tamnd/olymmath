The complex numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy

$$
\begin{aligned}
x y z & =-4 \\
(x+1)(y+1)(z+1) & =7 \\
(x+2)(y+2)(z+2) & =-3
\end{aligned}
$$

Find, with proof, the value of $(x+3)(y+3)(z+3)$.

$$
\prod_{k=0}^{10} \cos \left(2^{k} \theta\right) \neq 0
$$

and

$$
\prod_{k=0}^{10}\left(1+\frac{1}{\cos \left(2^{k} \theta\right)}\right)=1 ?
$$

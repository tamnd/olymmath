Let $w, x, y$, and $z$ be positive real numbers such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 & \neq \cos w \cos x \cos y \cos z \\
2 \pi & =w+x+y+z \\
3 \tan w & =k(1+\sec w) \\
4 \tan x & =k(1+\sec x) \\
5 \tan y & =k(1+\sec y) \\
6 \tan z & =k(1+\sec z)
\end{aligned}
$$

(Here $\sec t$ denotes $\frac{1}{\cos t}$ when $\cos t \neq 0$.) Find $k$.

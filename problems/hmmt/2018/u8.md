$$
\log _{2}\left(\log _{4} x\right)+\log _{4}\left(\log _{16} x\right)+\log _{16}\left(\log _{2} x\right)=0
$$

Compute

$$
\log _{2}\left(\log _{16} x\right)+\log _{16}\left(\log _{4} x\right)+\log _{4}\left(\log _{2} x\right)
$$

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers with $a>b$. Suppose that

$$
\sqrt{\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}}+\sqrt{\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}}
$$

is a integer.
(a) Must $\sqrt{a}$ be an integer?
(b) Must $\sqrt{b}$ be an integer?

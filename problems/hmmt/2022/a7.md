Let $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right),\left(x_{2}, y_{2}\right),\left(x_{3}, y_{3}\right),\left(x_{4}, y_{4}\right)$, and $\left(x_{5}, y_{5}\right)$ be the vertices of a regular pentagon centered at $(0,0)$. Compute the product of all positive integers $k$ such that the equality

$$
x_{1}^{k}+x_{2}^{k}+x_{3}^{k}+x_{4}^{k}+x_{5}^{k}=y_{1}^{k}+y_{2}^{k}+y_{3}^{k}+y_{4}^{k}+y_{5}^{k}
$$

must hold for all possible choices of the pentagon.

Suppose $a, b$, and $c$ are complex numbers satisfying

$$
\begin{aligned}
a^{2} & =b-c, \\
b^{2} & =c-a, \text { and } \\
c^{2} & =a-b .
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute all possible values of $a+b+c$.

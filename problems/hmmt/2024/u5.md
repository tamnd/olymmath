Let $a, b$, and $c$ be real numbers such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
a+b+c & =100 \\
a b+b c+c a & =20, \text { and } \\
(a+b)(a+c) & =24
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute all possible values of $b c$.

She has three solutions of different bases available: $10 \%$ rubidium hydroxide $(\mathrm{Rb}(\mathrm{OH})), 8 \%$ cesium hydroxide $(\mathrm{Cs}(\mathrm{OH}))$, and $5 \%$ francium hydroxide $(\mathrm{Fr}(\mathrm{OH})$ ).

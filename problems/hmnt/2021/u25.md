Let $x, y, z$ be real numbers satisfying

$$
\begin{aligned}
2 x+y+4 x y+6 x z & =-6 \\
y+2 z+2 x y+6 y z & =4 \\
x-z+2 x z-4 y z & =-3
\end{aligned}
$$

Find $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}$.

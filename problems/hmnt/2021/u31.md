For positive integers $n$, let $f(n)$ be the product of the digits of $n$. Find the largest positive integer $m$ such that

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{f(n)}{m^{\left\lfloor\log _{10} n\right\rfloor}}
$$

is an integer.

Suppose $a, b$, and $c$ are real numbers such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
a^{2}-b c & =14 \\
b^{2}-c a & =14, \text { and } \\
c^{2}-a b & =-3
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute $|a+b+c|$.

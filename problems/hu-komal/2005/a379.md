Find all real numbers $\lambda$ for which there exists a non-zero polynomial $P$, such that $\frac{P(1)+P(3)+P(5)+\dots+P(2n-1)}{n} = \lambda P(n)$ for all $n$. List all such polynomials for $\lambda$=2.

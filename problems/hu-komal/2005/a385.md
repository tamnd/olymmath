Find all sequences $a_1$,$a_2$,... of non-negative numbers, such that the sum $S=\sum_{k=1}^\infty a_k$ is finite and $\sum_{k=1}^\infty a_{kn} = \frac{S}{n^\alpha}$ for all positive integers $n$.

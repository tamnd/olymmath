Consider one of the two arcs of a rectangular hyperbola centred at $O$, and choose an arbitrary point $P$ on it. Let $Q$ and $R$ denote the intersections of the hyperbola and the circle of radius 2$OP$ drawn about $P$. Prove that $\angle$ QPR $=120^o$.

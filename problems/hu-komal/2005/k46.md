The values of a gold artefact in Aurum is proportional to the square of its mass. A 100-dollar piece is stolen by thieves. The thieves cut it up into smaller pieces of equal mass to prepare pendants of them, whose total value is 10 dollars. The pendants are bought up by a jeweller who assembles them into bracelets (of not necessarily the same mass). How much are the individual bracelets worth, given that each bracelet is made of a whole number of pendants, and their total value is 46 dollars?

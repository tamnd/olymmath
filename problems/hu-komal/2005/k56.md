Eskimos greet each other by rubbing their noses together, and they greet the members of an arctic expedition by a tap on the hand. The members of the expedition say hello to each other as well as to the Eskimos. At a certain event, ``Hello'' was said 415 times. How many nose rubbings and how many taps on the hand were there? (Everyone greeted everyone else in the appropriate way.)

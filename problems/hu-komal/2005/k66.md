There are only 5 candies left on the Christmas tree: two of them filled with jelly and the other three filled with almond paste. The wrapping of the candies does not show the flavour. Hanga decided to eat one of them every day. Which is more probable: that she will eat the second jelly-filled candy on the third day, or that she will eat the third almond paste candy on the fourth day?

Let {$r$} denote the number obtained by taking away from $r$ the greatest integer not greater than $r$. Prove that the equation {$x^3$}+{$y^3$}={$z^3$} has infinitely many solutions, such that $x$, $y$ and $z$ are rational numbers, but none of them are integers.

Mihály Tímár (the hero of a famous Hungarian novel) is in a trouble. The red crescent that marked the sack containing the treasure has come off. He knows, however, that the heaviest of the four sacks hides the treasure. From three measurements, he has found out that the first sack together with the second one are lighter than the other two, the first and third together are equal in weight to the other two, and the first and fourth sacks together are heavier than the other two. Which sack hides the treasure?

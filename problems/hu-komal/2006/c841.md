Paul the postman was delivering letters in a long street. First he walked down the street on one side (where the odd-numbered blocks were) and then he walked back on the other side (delivering the letters to the even-numbered blocks). On the odd side he spent one third as much time standing in front of the letter boxes as he spent by walking back on the even side. On his way back on the even side his total standing time in front of the letter boxes was one third as much as his time in motion down the street on the odd side. Finally, it turned out that delivering the letters took the same time on each side. Find the ratio of his average walking speed (not counting the stops) on the odd side to his average walking speed back on the even side.

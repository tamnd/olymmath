2006 small circles are placed in a large circle, so that each touches the next one in a row on the outside, the centres of the small circles all lie on the same diameter of the large circle, and the sum of the diameters of the small circles equals the diameter of the large circle. Compare the total perimeter of the small circles and the perimeter of the large circle.

We have two cubes of not necessarily the same size with integer edges. They are placed on top of each other, so that the upper cube has a whole face touching a face of the lower cube. The volume of the solid obtained in this way expressed incube units is the same as the measure of the surface area in square units. How long are the edges of the original cubes?

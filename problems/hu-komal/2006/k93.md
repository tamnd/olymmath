Sherlock Holmes was investigating a crime in the Musgrave property famous for a beautiful elm tree. Looking for the hidden treasure, he was to ``walk ten and ten steps to the north, five and five steps to the east, two and two steps to the south, and one and one step to the west.'' Holmes found out, however, that one of the directions was wrong, one should walk in the opposite direction instead (for example, south instead of north). Since he did not know which was wrong, he tried all possibilities. Each walk ended within the park of the Musgraves. Given that Holmes' steps are 80 cm long and the park is a rectangle with sides in the north-south and east-west directions, find the minimum possible area of the park.

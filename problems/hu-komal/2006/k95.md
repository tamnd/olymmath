A one-digit number is written in each field of a 3×3 chessboard. A chessman is moved in an L-shaped path from one field to another (the way that a knight moves). Such an L consists of four fields. In how many different ways is it possible to fill out the 3×3 table with (not necessarily different) one-digit numbers so that the sum of the four numbers in every such L-shaped move is the same?

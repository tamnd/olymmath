Lines $FD$ and $AC$intersect at $P$, lines $DE$ and $AB$ intersect at $Q$. The midpoints of segments $EP$ amd $FQ$ are $M$ and $N$, respectively. Prove that $MN$ and $OI$ are perpendicular.

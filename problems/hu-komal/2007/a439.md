Prove that $\frac{a_1^2}{1-a_1} + \frac{a_2^2}{a_1-a_2} + \frac{a_3^2}{a_2-a_3} + \ldots + \frac{a_n^2}{a_{n-1}-a_n} > \frac12(a_1+2a_2+3a_3+\ldots+na_n)-1$ whenever $1>a_1>a_2>\ldots>a_n>0$.

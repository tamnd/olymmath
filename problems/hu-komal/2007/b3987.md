Let $n \ge$4 be an integer, and let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ denote nonnegative real numbers. Prove that ${(a_1+a_2+a_3)}^2 {(a_2+a_3+a_4)}^2\cdot \ldots \cdot {(a_{n-1}+a_n+a_1)}^2{(a_n+a_1+a_2)}^2$ $\ge 2^n{(a_1+a_2)}^2{(a_2+a_3)}^2\cdot \ldots \cdot {(a_{n-1}+a_n)}^2{(a_n+a_1)}^2.$ Discuss also the case of equality.

The charge of money withdrawal from an ATM is made up of two terms. There is a flat charge independent of the amount withdrawn and another part that is proportional to it. For $40 000$ forints (Hungarian currency, HUF) withdrawn the charge is 221 forints, and for $100 000$ it is 485 forints. What is the charge for $85 000$ forints?

There are two tribes living in Binumeria. The difference between the two tribes is that they write numbers in notations using different bases. A member of each tribe was asked how many members their tribe had. Each of them answered $10 011$. How many inhabitants are there in Binumeria, given that each tribe consists of more than 100 but less than 1000 members?

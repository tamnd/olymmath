``Maci sajt'' is a brand name of processed cheese that comes in a round box containing eight individually wrapped sectors that just fit in the box. Stevie ate two sectors for breakfast, and arranged the remaining ones in the box as shown in the figure. His mom did not like that. She said that Stevie was squeezing the cheese pieces. Stevie insisted that there was no squeezing, the pieces just fit. Who was right?

Given a continuous function $f\colon \mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ such that for any real number $a$>0, the sequence $f(a),f(2a),f(3a),\ldots$ converges to 0. Show that $\lim\limits_{x\to\infty}f(x)=0$.

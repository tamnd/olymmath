A billiards ball at corner $A$ of a rectangular billiards table ABCD is struck. It starts moving along the angle bisector. It rebounds from the sides $CD$, $BC$ and finally $AB$, and collides head-on with a ball that is at the centre of the rectangle. In what other direction is it possible to strike the ball at point $A$, so that it rebounds from three different sides and then hits the ball at the centre head-on?

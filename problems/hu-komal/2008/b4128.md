The line through $P$ parallel to $AB$ intersects the sides $BC$ and $AD$ at $K$ and $L$, and the line through $Q$ parallel to $BC$ intersects the sides $AB$ and $CD$ at $M$ and $N$. Show that the areas of the triangles PNM and QKL are equal.

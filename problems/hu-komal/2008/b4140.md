$E$ is the midpoint of the arc $BC$ on the circumscribed circle of a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD . The midpoint of arc $DA$ is $F$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the centres of the inscribed circles of triangles ABC and ABD , respectively. Show that $PQ$ is parallel to $EF$.

<!-- S2 -->
$k$ is the circumscribed circle of a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD . The centre of the inscribed circle of triangle ABC is $P$, and that of triangle ABD is $Q$. Let $E$ denote the midpoint of arc $BC$, and let $F$ denote the midpoint of arc $DA$ of the circle $k$. Prove that $PQ$ is parallel to $EF$.

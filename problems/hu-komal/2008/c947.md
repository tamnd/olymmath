A rabbit is sitting at a point $A$, at a distance of 160 m from a straight railway track. The perpendicular projection of $A$ onto the track is $T$. A train is approaching $T$ at a speed of 30 m/s. The distance of the front of the train from point $T$ is 300 m initially. The rabbit can run at 15 m/s. Can he cross the track in any direction before the train comes?

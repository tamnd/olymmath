Consider all possible seven-digit numbers formed by using each of the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 once. $a$) Show that the difference of any pair of such seven-digit numbers is divisible by 9. $b$) How many pairs of different numbers can be selected out of them, such that one is a factor of the other?

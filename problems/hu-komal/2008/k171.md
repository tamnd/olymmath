Four classes of 8th-grade students took part in a mathematics competition organized by a school. Each team scored a different number of points. A mathematics teacher added up the scores of two teams in every possible way and revealed that the largest sum was 128, and the smallest sum was 82. He also disclosed that the team in the second place was only 4 points ahead of the team in the third place. What was the difference between the scores of the teams in the first and four places?

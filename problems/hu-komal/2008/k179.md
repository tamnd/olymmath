He spent 1500 forints (HUF, Hungarian currency) on lunch, 1000 forints on presents, 1250 forints on movie tickets, 500 forints on hiring films on DVD. His spendings on chewing gum were represented by a 20-degree sector of the pie chart. He had no other expenses in addition to those mentioned above. How much did he spend on chewing gum in October?

A box contains red, blue and green marbles. At least twelve marbles need to be drawn from the box to make it certain that there will be a red one among them, and at least seventeen need to be drawn to make sure that there is a red one and also a green one among them. At least seven need to be drawn to be certain that there is a marble among them that is not blue. At least how many marbles need to be drawn to make sure that at least two of them are green?

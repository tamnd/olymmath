A 11×11 cm square can be tiled with two different sets of 5 rectangles whose sides in centimetres are integers, with each length from 1 to 10 cm occurring exactly once. (Tiling means that the rectangles are not allowed to reach over the edge of the square, and they cannot even partly overlap.) Find both tilings.

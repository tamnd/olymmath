In a graph, no vertex is connected to all of the others. For any pair of vertices not connected there is a vertex adjacent to both. The sum of the squares of the degrees of vertices is $n^2-n$ where $n$ is the number of vertices. What is the length of the shortest possible circuit in the graph?

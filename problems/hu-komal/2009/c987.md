The lengths of the sides of a triangle cut out of paper are 8 cm, 10 cm and 12 cm. The triangle is folded along a line through the common vertex so that the shortest side overlaps with the longest side. A double-layer part and a single-layer part are obtained. Prove that the single-layer part is an isosceles triangle.

The perpendicular drawn at $B$ intersects the circle of diameter $AD$ at $P$ and $Q$. The perpendicular drawn at $C$intersects the circle of diameter $BD$ at $K$ and $L$. Show that the centre of the circle passing through points $P$, $K$, $L$ and $Q$ is point $B$.

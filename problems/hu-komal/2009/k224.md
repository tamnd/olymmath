There are dice of six and four faces (cubes and tetrahedra) on a table. Their faces are numbered with dots, 1 to 6 and 1 to 4, respectively. The number of all dots on the dice is 323. If we had as many six-sided dice as we have of the four-sided dice and vice versa, the number of dots would be 185. How many dice of each kind are there on the table?

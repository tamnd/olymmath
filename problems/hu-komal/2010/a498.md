Let $p$($x$) be a polynomial with integer coefficients, and let $w$ be a complex number of unit absolute value. Prove that if the number $c$=$p$($w$) is purely real, then there is a polynomial $q$($x$) with integer coefficients for which $c=q\left(w+\frac1w\right)$.

Two sailboats are travelling in perpendicular directions on the lake Balaton at the same speed of 10 km/h. At a given time instant, while both are approaching the intersection of their paths, the respective distances of sailboats $A$ and $B$ are 1 km and 2 km from that point. A man travelling in boat $A$ wants to swim to boat $B$ . He swims at a uniform speed of 2 km/h. When should he jump in the water in order to minimise the time spent in the water?

Let $a$ and $b$ denote positive integers. Given that $p$ is a polynomial of integer coefficients that has both a value divisible by $a$ and a value divisible by $b$ at integer points. Prove that there is an integer point where the value of $p$ is also divisible by the least common multiple of $a$ and $b$.

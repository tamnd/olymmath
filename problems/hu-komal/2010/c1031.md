In the lottery, five out of the numbers 1 to 90 are drawn. Ticket holders mark five numbers on each ticket. A man bought two tickets and marked ten different numbers on them altogether. Given that four out of his ten numbers were drawn, what is the probability that he had a ticket with $a$) four numbers drawn? $b$) two numbers drawn?

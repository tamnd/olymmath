In a three-digit number, the digit in the ones' place is 3 smaller than the digit in the hundreds' place. $a$) Find the largest number that meets the condition. $b$) How many such numbers are there altogether? $c$) Find all numbers that meet the above condition, for which by subtracting the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits the result is 297.

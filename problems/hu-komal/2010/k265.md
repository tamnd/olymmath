A man commutes to work by train. Every day in the afternoon, he arrives to the railway station with the same train, and his wife drives him home. The wife leaves home just in time to arrive at the station when her husband's train comes in. One day, the man arrived at the station an hour earlier, and started to walk home along the road on which his wife drove him home every day. The wife left home at the usual time. When she saw her husband on the road, she immediately stopped, picked him up and turned around. They arrived home 20 minutes earlier than usual. Calculate the time in minutes that the husband walked along the road.

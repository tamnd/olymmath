Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ denote positive integers. Given that $\frac{a}{b}<\frac{c}{d}$ , show that $\frac{a+c}{b+d}$ always lies between $\frac{a}{b}$ and $\frac{c}{d}$ .

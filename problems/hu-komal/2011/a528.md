Let $a$, $b$ and $n$ be positive integers and let $C$ be a finite set of integers. Suppose that every positive integer can be represented in the form $ax^n$+$by^n$+$c$with some positive integers $x$, $y$ and $c \in C$. Find the possible values of $n$.

The positive real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ satisfy $a$+$b$+$c$+$d$= abc + abd + acd + bcd . Prove that ${(a+b)(c+d)}+(a+d)(b+c) \ge 4\sqrt{(1+ac)(1+bd)}.$

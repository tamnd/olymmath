A vertical wall of a building is a square of side 20 metres. The angles subtended by the vertical sides of the square at two points on the (level) ground are measured. The results are $40^o$ and $38^o$ at one point, and $32^o$ and $40^o$ at the other point. Find the distance between the two points.

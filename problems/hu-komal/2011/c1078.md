Define ``words'' as strings of at most ten characters made out of the 26 letters of the English alphabet. (A ``word'' may as well consist of a single letter, and two ``words'' are considered different if they differ in at least one letter.) Prove that the number of all possible ``words'' obtained in this way is divisible by 27.

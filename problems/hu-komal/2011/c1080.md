A steerable airship has two engines and a given supply of fuel. If both engines are operated, the airship covers 88 kilometres per hour. If the first engine were used only, the fuel would last 25 hours longer, but the distance covered per hour would only be 45 km. If the second engine were used only, the fuel would last 16 hours longer than with two engines, and the distance covered per hour would only be 72 km. In which case can the airship travel the longest distance?

Ann's grandmother made a birthday cake for her grandchild. Before decorating it with almond paste figurines, she weighed the cake on her digital scales that display weights rounded to the nearest tenth of a kilogram. The reading was 3.4 kg. When she put as many identical almond paste figurines on the cake as the age of Ann in years, the reading changed to 3.6 kg. The true weight of each figurine is an integer multiple of 10 grams, but any one of them alone reads 0.1 kg on the scales. How old may Ann be and what may be the weight of the figurines?

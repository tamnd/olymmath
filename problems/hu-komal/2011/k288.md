We want to make a set of triangular dominoes. The stones are regular triangles with an integer from 0 to 5 in each vertex. If a stone has three different numbers on it, then they increase in clockwise order. How many domino stones will there be? (There may also be three identical numbers on a stone, or two identical and one different number.)

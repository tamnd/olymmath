We have drawn a rectangle on squared paper, such that (its sides are lattice lines and) it consists of $n$ small lattice squares. Prove that if the half of the number of lattice points on the boundary of the rectangle is added to the number of lattice points in its interior, and 1 is subtracted from the sum then the result will be $n$.

The three interior angles of a triangle followed by the three exterior angles, in the appropriate order, form six consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence. Find the measures of the angles of the triangle in degrees. (In an arithmetic sequence, the difference of the consecutive terms is constant, that is, the sequence increases uniformly.)

From the third term onwards, the terms of a sequence are obtained by subtracting from the previous term the term preceding that. (Thus the third term = the second term - the first term, the fourth term = the third term - the second term, and so on.) The first term is 2, the sum of the first 2012 terms is 2012. What is the second term?

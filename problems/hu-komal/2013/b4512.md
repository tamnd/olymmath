A digit is written on each face of two cubes such that the number of any day of a month is obtained by placing the two cubes next to each other in an appropriate way. (One-digit numbers are written as usual, e.g. 01. A 9 may also be obtained by turning a 6 around.) In how many different ways can this be done if the relative positions of numbers on the same cube are not considered?

If a token is inserted in a slot machine, it will roll a regular die and show the result. Then the player may choose to either accept the prize, which is 100 times the number rolled, and thereby end the game, or to insert a second token. In the latter case, the machine rolls the die again, and then the prize is 100 times the product of the two numbers rolled. The game then ends, there is no more choice offered. How much should a token cost so that, in the long run, the slot machine make a profit for its owner?

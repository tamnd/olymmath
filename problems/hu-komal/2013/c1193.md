An isosceles triangle of apex angle $120^o$ is drawn on each side of a unit square, on the outside of the square. An equilateral octagon is obtained. In comparison, consider the regular octagon in which the side of the square formed by connecting every other vertex is unity. Find the ratio of the area of the equilateral octagon to the area of the regular octagon.

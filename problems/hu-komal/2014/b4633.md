There are some points marked in the interior of a triangle, such that no three of them (including the vertices) are collinear. The points are connected to each other and to the vertices of the triangle so that the resulting line segments should not intersect, and the triangle should be divided into the largest possible number of smaller triangles. Prove that the number of small triangles formed is odd.

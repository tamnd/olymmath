For every positive integer $n$ , let $P(n)$ be the greatest prime divisor of $n^2+1$. Show that there are infinitely many quadruples $(a,b,c,d)$ of positive integers that satisfy $a<b<c<d$ and $P(a)=P(b)=P(c)=P(d)$.

The diagonals of a quadrilateral $ABCD$ are perpendicular, they intersect at $E$ . From point $E$ , drop a perpendicular onto the line of each side. Consider the intersection of each perpendicular with the opposite side. Prove that the four points all lie on a circle centred at a point of the line segment connecting the midpoints of the diagonals.

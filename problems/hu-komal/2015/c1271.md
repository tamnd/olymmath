Consider circumscribed circle of a right-angled triangle. Draw the semicircle containing the triangle, and draw tangents to it, parallel to the legs. The parallels together with the line of the hypotenuse form a similar right-angled triangle. Find the angles of the triangle if the area of the outer triangle is 6 times the area of the inner triangle.

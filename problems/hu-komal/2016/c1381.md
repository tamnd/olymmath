Let $M$ denote the intersection of the line of the altitude drawn from vertex $A$ of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with the Thales' circle of $BC$. Let $N$ be the intersection of the altitude from vertex $C$ with the Thales' circle of $AB$. Prove that $|BM|=|BN|$.

A man has five chequered shirts, out of which two are brown and one is blue. He has seven brown shirts, three of which are striped. He has three blue shirts, none of which is striped. In addition, he has got eight red shirts, half of which are striped. He regularly wears three pairs of trousers: one pair has blue stripes on it, one is brown with a checquered pattern, and one is a striped gray pair. He never wears blue and brown together, and he never wears striped with checquered either. How many different outfits (of one shirt and one pair of trousers) may he wear?

In a school trip, we asked each participant how many classmates of his or hers were present. Every student answered the question. Ten of them said 4, twelve said 3, six said 2, and four said 1. The class teacher of every child was also there and no other teachers took part in the trip. How many students and how many teachers were present in the school trip?

The numbers from 1 to 14 are written next to the vertices of a regular heptagon such that the sum is the same on each side (that is, for the two vertices lying on the side). $a)$ What is this sum? Show a valid construction. $b)$ May there be a vertex without any number written next to it? $c)$ What may be the sum of the numbers written next to a particular vertex?

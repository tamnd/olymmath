Prove that the inequality $\frac1{\sqrt{2x}} + \frac1{\sqrt{2y}} + \frac2{\sqrt{x+y}} + 2 \ge \frac4{\sqrt{x+2}} + \frac4{\sqrt{y+2}}$ holds for all pairs $(x,y)$ of positive real numbers.

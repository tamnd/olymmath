In a triangle $ABC$, $AB=BC$. $D$ is an interior point of the triangle such that $\angle ADC= 2 \angle ABC$. Prove that the distance of point $B$ from the exterior angle bisector of $\angle ADC$ is the arithmetic mean of the line segments $AD$ and $DC$.

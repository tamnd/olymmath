$1\times 1$ squares are placed on a rectangle of dimensions $a\times b$, with sides parallel to the sides of the rectangle. Prove that the maximum number of such squares without overlaps is $[a] \cdot [b]$ (where $[x]$ stands for the greatest integer not greater than the number $x$).

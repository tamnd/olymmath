In a 50-metre running race, if Martin gives a 4-metre advantage to Bill, he will just catch up with him at the finish line. If Bill gives 15 metres of advantage to Henry in a 200-metre race, they will finish side by side. How many metres of advantage may Martin give to Henry in a 1000-metre race so that the two of them finish together? (Assume that each of the three runners maintains the same constant running speed throughout the races.)

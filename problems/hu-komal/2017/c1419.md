In an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, consider the two circular sectors, each bounded by an arc $AB$, with one arc passing through the foot of the altitude drawn from $A$, and the other arc passing through the orthocentre of the triangle. Prove that if $\angle ACB =45^\circ$ then the areas of the two circular sectors are equal.

A bag contains the numbers 1 to 200, written on cards. Andrew and Bill take turns drawing number cards one by one until the bag is empty. At the end, each of them adds his numbers together. Given that the first number drawn by Andrew is 3 and Bill's first number is 170, by what maximum amount may Andrew's sum exceed Bill's sum at the end?

Five bystanders are trying predict the outcome of a football game. They make the following statements about the two teams, Team Fox and Team Wolf. Aron: Team Fox will score more than Team Wolf. Team Wolf will score at least 2 goals. Bob: The game will not be a draw. Team Fox will score 1 goal. Cecil: The winning team will win by 2 goals. At half-time, Team Wolf will have scored more. Dan: Team Wolf will not score any goal. Team Fox will win the game. Eve: In the second half of the game, Team Fox will score twice as many goals as Team Wolf. The game will be a draw. We know that finally there was no own goal during the game, and everyone had one true and one false statement in the above list. What is the final result of the game?

We call a positive integer lively if it has a prime divisor greater than $10^{10^{100}}$. Prove that if $S$ is an infinite set of lively positive integers, then it has an infinite subset $T$ with the property that the sum of the elements in any finite nonempty subset of $T$ is a lively number.

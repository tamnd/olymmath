Let $F_n$ be the $n$th Fibonacci number (${F_1=F_2=1}$, ${F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}}$). Construct infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $n$ divides $F_{F_n}$ but $n$ does not divide $F_n$.

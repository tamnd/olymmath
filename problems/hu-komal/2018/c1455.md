The currency used on a distant island consists of coins of unusual denominations. The basic units are three different one-digit numbers, and there are their multiples, too: ten times, a hundred times, and also a thousand times their value. The price of one kilo of coconut may be paid with two identical coins plus a third coin of different value. In order to pay for a kilo of passion fruit, which costs twice as much, the third coin needs to be replaced by the coin with 10 times its value. Given that no coin has a denomination of 1 and the largest denomination is 7000, what other coins are used on the island?

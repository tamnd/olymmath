The positive integers 1 to $n$ are written in the fields of the upper row of a $2 \times n$ table, in increasing order. The same numbers are written in the lower row, in decreasing order. How many positive integers $n$ smaller than 50 are there for which every number in the upper row is relatively prime to the number directly below?

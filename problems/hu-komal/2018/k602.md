Andrew and Paul are playing a game. The winner is always awarded $x$ points and the loser always gets $y$ points (where $x>y$ are integers). There is no draw. After a few rounds, we observe that Andrew has 30 points and Paul has 25 points since Paul has only won twice. How many points are awarded to the winner?

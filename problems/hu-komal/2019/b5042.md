The convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is not a trapezium, and diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are equal in length. Let $M$ denote the intersection of the diagonals. Show that the other intersection (different from $M$) of the circles $ABM$ and $CDM$ lies on the angle bisector of angle $BMC$.

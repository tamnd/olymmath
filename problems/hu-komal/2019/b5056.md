Consider the quadratic function $f(x)=x^2+bx+c$ defined on the set of real numbers. Given that the zeros of $f$ are some distinct prime numbers $p$ and $q$, and $f(p-q)=6pq$, determine the primes $p$ and $q$, and determine the function $f$.

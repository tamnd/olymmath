The circumscribed circle of a square is reflected in each side. Let $T$ denote the area of the circle that touches these reflections in the interior of the square. Let $t$ denote the area of the circle that touches one reflection and the circumscribed circle, both from the inside. Determine the smallest possible value of $\frac{T}{t}$.

Four identical rectangular sheets of paper are placed on the table to form a larger rectangle without gaps or overlaps. The area of the resulting rectangle is $1200~\mathrm{cm}^{2}$. Given that it is not possible to transfer any rectangle to any other only by translation, find the perimeter of the large rectangle.

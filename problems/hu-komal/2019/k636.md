Find all possible values of the digits $x$ and $y$ for which every nonzero digit occurs the same number of times in the prime factorization of the eight-digit number $\overline{xyxyxyxy}$ in decimal notation. (In making the prime factorization, identical prime factors are not written as a power but written down as separate factors.)

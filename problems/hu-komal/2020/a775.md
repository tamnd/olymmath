Let $H \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ such that if we reflect any point in $H$ across another point of $H$, the resulting point is also in $H$. Prove that either $H$ is dense in $\mathbb{R}^3$ or one can find equidistant parallel planes which cover $H$.

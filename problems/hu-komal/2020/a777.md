Finite graph $G(V,E)$ on $n$ points is drawn in the plane. For an edge $e$ of the graph let $x(e)$ denote the number of edges that cross over edge $e$. Prove that $\sum\limits_{e\in E} \frac{1}{x(e)+1}\le 3n-6.$

The intersection of two congruent regular pentagons is a decagon with sides of $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{10}$ in this order. Prove that $a_1a_3+a_3a_5+a_5a_7+a_7a_9+a_9a_1 = a_2a_4+a_4a_6+a_6a_8+a_8a_{10}+a_{10}a_2.$

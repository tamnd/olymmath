In England, a man lost his wedding ring, and set out to search for it with the help of a friend and a metal detector. They did not find the ring, but they did find some gold coins from the time of King Henry VIII, delivering $100 000$ pounds to the two friends. The one-pound coins were preserved in very good condition, and during the 500 years elapsed, their average annual increase in value was between 1.42% and 1.43%. How many coins may they have found?

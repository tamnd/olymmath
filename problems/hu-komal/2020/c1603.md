The altitude drawn from vertex $A$ of an isosceles triangle $ABC$ intersects the leg $BC$ at $T$. Let $M$ denote the orthocentre, and let $O$ be the centre of the inscribed circle. Prove that if line $OT$ is parallel to the base $AB$, then $MC=2AM$.

For all positive integers $n$ let $r_n$ be defined as $r_n=\sum_{t=0}^{n} {(-1)}^t \binom{n}{t} \frac{1}{(t+1)!}.$ Prove that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} r_n=0$.

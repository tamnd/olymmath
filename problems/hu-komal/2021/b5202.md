Two rational numbers are said to be acquainted to each other if they can be represented in the forms $p/q$ and $r/s$, respectively ($p$, $q$, $r$, $s$ are integers), such that $|ps-qr|=1$. If two rational numbers are acquainted to each other, how many common acquaintances may they have?

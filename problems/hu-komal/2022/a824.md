An infinite set $S$ of positive numbers is called thick, if in every interval of the form $\big[1/(n+1), 1/n\big]$ (where $n$ is an arbitrary positive integer) there is a number which is the difference of two elements from $S$. Does there exist a thick set such that the sum of its elements is finite?

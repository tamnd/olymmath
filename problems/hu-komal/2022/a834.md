Let $A_1A_2\ldots A_8$ be a convex cyclic octagon, and for $i=1,2\ldots,8$ let $B_i=A_iA_{i+3}\cap A_{i+1}A_{i+4}$ (indices are meant modulo 8). Prove that points $B_1,\ldots,B_8$ lie on the same conic section.

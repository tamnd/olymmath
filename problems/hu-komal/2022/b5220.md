Let $n$ be a positive integer. Prove that it is possible to select $n$ perfect squares from the numbers $1$ to $2^{n+2}$ such that the sums obtained by adding an arbitrary subset of the selected numbers (including sums of single terms and the sum of all the numbers) are all distinct.

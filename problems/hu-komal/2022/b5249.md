Let $T_0$ denote the area of the triangle formed by the points of tangency of the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$ on the sides, and let $T_1$ denote the area of the triangle formed by the centres of the escribed circles. Show that the geometric mean of $T_0$ and $T_1$ equals the area of triangle $ABC$.

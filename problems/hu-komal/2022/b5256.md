In the lottery game, five numbers are drawn out of ninety every week. Andrew fills out a single lottery ticket with the same five numbers in each of the 52 weeks of the year. Belle uses a different scheme. She plays only once a year with 52 tickets simultaneously: she fills them out in pairwise different ways. Is it true that both of them have the same chance of having a ticket with five correct numbers?

A flea starts out from point $(a,b)$ of the coordinate plane, where $a$, $b$ are positive integers. With each jump, the flea will move one unit to the left or downwards. It keeps jumping until it reaches either the $x$ axis or the $y$ axis. What fraction of the possible sequences of jumps terminate on the $x$ axis?

In the interior of a regular triangle $ABC$ consider the points $P$ where side $AB$ subtends an angle of $135^{\circ}$. Prove that the line segments $PA$, $PB$, $PC$ can always form a triangle, and one angle of such triangles is always the same, independently of the position of point $P$.

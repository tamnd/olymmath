Two circles touch each other externally. The line passing through the centres of the circles intersects the circles again at points $A$ and $B$. The points of tangency on one of the common external tangents of the circles are $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Prove that the lines $AP$ and $BQ$ intersect on the common internal tangent of the circles.

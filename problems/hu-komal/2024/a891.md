Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Points $B'$ and $C'$ are located on the interior of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $M$ denote the second intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $ABC$ and $AB'C'$, while let $N$ denote the second intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $ABC'$ and $AB'C$. Reflect $M$ across lines $AB$ and $AC$, and let $l$ denote the line through the reflections. a) Prove that the line through $M$ perpendicular to line $AM$, the line $AK$, and $l$ are either concurrent or all parallel. b) Show that if the three lines are concurrent at $S$, then triangles $SBC'$ and $SCB'$ have equal areas.

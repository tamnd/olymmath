Find the smallest possible degree of polynomial $f$ of one variable, if it satisfies the following property: the range of $f$ and $f\circ f$ are different, while the range of $f \circ f$ and $f\circ f\circ f$ are the same. (The symbol $\circ$ denotes function composition.)

We have filled in the squares of the $8\times 8$ chessboard with the positive integers from $1$ to $64$ in increasing order, starting from the top left corner and proceeding row by row. Is it possible to delete two numbers from two adjacent squares (two squares sharing an edge or a vertex) such that the sum of the remaining numbers is exactly $2024$?

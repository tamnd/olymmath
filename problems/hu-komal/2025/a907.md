2025 light bulbs are operated by some switches. Each switch works on a subset of the light bulbs. When we use a switch, all the light bulbs in the subset change their state: bulbs that were on turn off, and bulbs that were off turn on. We know that every light bulb is operated by at least one of the switches. Initially, all lamps were off. Find the biggest number $k$ for which we can surely turn on at least $n$ light bulbs.

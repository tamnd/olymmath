Let $\mathbb{N}^*=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. Find al the functions $f: \mathbb{N}^*\rightarrow \mathbb{N}^*$ such that:

(1) If $x<y$ then $f(x)<f(y)$.
(2) $f\left(yf(x)\right)=x^2f(xy)$ for all $x,y \in\mathbb{N}^*$.

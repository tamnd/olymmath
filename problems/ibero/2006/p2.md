For n real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots , a_{n},$ let $d$ denote the difference between the greatest and smallest of them and $S = \sum_{i<j}\left |a_i-a_j \right|.$ Prove that
$$(n-1)d\le S\le\frac{n^{2}}{4}d$$
and find when each equality holds.

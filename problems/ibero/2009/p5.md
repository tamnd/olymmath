Consider the sequence $ \{a_n\}_{n\geq1}$ defined as follows: $ a_1 = 1$ , $ a_{2k} = 1 + a_k$ and $ a_{2k + 1} = \frac {1}{a_{2k}}$ for every $ k\geq 1$ . Prove that every positive rational number appears on the sequence $ \{a_n\}$ exactly once.

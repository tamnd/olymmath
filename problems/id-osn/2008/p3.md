Find all natural number which can be expressed in
$ \frac{a+b}{c}+\frac{b+c}{a}+\frac{c+a}{b}$
where $ a,b,c\in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy
$ \gcd(a,b)=\gcd(b,c)=\gcd(c,a)=1$

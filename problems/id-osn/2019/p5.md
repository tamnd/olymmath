Given that $a$ and $b$ are real numbers such that for infinitely many positive integers $m$ and $n$ ,
$$ \lfloor an + b \rfloor \ge \lfloor a + bn \rfloor $$
$$ \lfloor a + bm \rfloor \ge \lfloor am + b \rfloor $$
Prove that $a = b$ .

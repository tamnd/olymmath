A function $f$ is defined on the natural numbers $\mathbb{N}$ and satisfies the following rules:

(a) $f(1)=1$;

(b) $f(2n)=f(n)$ and $f(2n+1)=f(2n)+1$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$.

Calculate the maximum value $m$ of the set $\{f(n):n\in\mathbb{N},1\le n\le1989\}$, and determine the number of natural numbers $n$, with $1\le n\le1989$, that satisfy the equation $f(n)=m$.

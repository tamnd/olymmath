Suppose $P$ is a point in the interior of a triangle $ABC$, that $x,y,z$ are the distances from $P$ to $A,B,C$, respectively, and that $p,q,r$ are the perpendicular distances from $P$ to the sides $BC,CA,AB$, respectively. Prove that

$$xyz\geq 8pqr,$$

with equality implying that the triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

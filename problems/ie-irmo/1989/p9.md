Let $a$ be a positive real number, and let

$$b=\sqrt[3]{a+\sqrt{a^2+1}}+\sqrt[3]{a-\sqrt{a^2+1}}.$$

Prove that $b$ is a positive integer if, and only if, $a$ is a positive integer of the form $\frac12 n(n^2+3)$, for some positive integer $n$.

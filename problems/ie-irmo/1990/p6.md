Let $n$ be a natural number, and suppose that the equation

$$x_1x_2+x_2x_3+x_3x_4+x_4x_5+\cdots+x_{n-1}x_n+x_nx_1=0$$

has a solution with all the $x_i$’s equal to $\pm1$. Prove that $n$ is divisible by 4.

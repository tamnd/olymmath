Let $n>2$ be an integer and let $m=\sum k^3$, where the sum is taken over all integers $k$ with $1\leq k<n$ that are relatively prime to $n$. Prove that $n$ divides $m$. (Note that two integers are relatively prime if, and only if, their greatest common divisor equals 1.)

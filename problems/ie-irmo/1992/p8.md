Let $a,b,c$ and $d$ be real numbers with $a\neq 0$. Prove that if all the roots of the cubic equation
$$az^3+bz^2+cz+d=0$$
lie to the left of the imaginary axis in the complex plane, then
$$ab>0,\quad bc-ad>0,\quad ad>0.$$

The real numbers $\alpha$, $\beta$ satisfy the equations
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha^3-3\alpha^2+5\alpha-17&=0,\\
\beta^3-3\beta^2+5\beta+11&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Find $\alpha+\beta$.

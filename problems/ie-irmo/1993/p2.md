A natural number $n$ is called good if it can be written in a *unique* way simultaneously as the sum $a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_k$ and as the product $a_1a_2\ldots a_k$ of some $k\geq 2$ natural numbers $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k$. (For example $10$ is good because $10=5+2+1+1+1=5.2.1.1.1$ and these expressions are unique.) Determine, in terms of prime numbers, which natural numbers are good.

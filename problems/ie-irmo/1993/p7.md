Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_n$ be $2n$ real numbers, where $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ are distinct, and suppose that there exists a real number $\alpha$ such that the product

$$(a_i+b_1)(a_i+b_2)\cdots(a_i+b_n)$$

has the value $\alpha$ for $i=1,2,\ldots,n$. Prove that there exists a real number $\beta$ such that the product

$$(a_1+b_j)(a_2+b_j)\cdots(a_n+b_j)$$

has the value $\beta$ for $j=1,2,\ldots,n$.

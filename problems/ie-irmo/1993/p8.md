For nonnegative integers $n,r$, the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{r}$ denotes the number of combinations of $n$ objects chosen $r$ at a time, with the convention that $\binom{n}{0}=1$ and $\binom{n}{r}=0$ if $n<r$. Prove the identity

$$\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\binom{n-r+1}{d}\binom{r-1}{d-1}=\binom{n}{r}$$

for all integers $n$ and $r$, with $1\le r\le n$.

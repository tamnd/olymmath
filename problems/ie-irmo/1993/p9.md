Let $x$ be a real number with $0<x<\pi$. Prove that, for all natural numbers $n$, the sum

$$\sin x+\frac{\sin 3x}{3}+\frac{\sin 5x}{5}+\cdots+\frac{\sin(2n-1)x}{2n-1}$$

is positive.

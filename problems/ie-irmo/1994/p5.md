Let $f(n)$ be defined on the set of positive integers by the rules: $f(1)=2$ and
$$f(n+1)=(f(n))^2-f(n)+1,\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$
Prove that, for all integers $n>1$,
$$1-\frac{1}{2^{2^{n-1}}}<\frac{1}{f(1)}+\frac{1}{f(2)}+\cdots+\frac{1}{f(n)}<1-\frac{1}{2^{2^n}}.$$

Let $ f(n)$ be defined for $ n \in \mathbb{N}$ by $ f(1)=2$ and $ f(n+1)=f(n)^2-f(n)+1$ for $ n \ge 1$. Prove that for all $ n >1:$

$ 1-\frac{1}{2^{2^{n-1}}}<\frac{1}{f(1)}+\frac{1}{f(2)}+...+\frac{1}{f(n)}<1-\frac{1}{2^{2^n}}$

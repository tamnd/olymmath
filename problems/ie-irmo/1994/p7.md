Let $p,q,r$ be distinct real numbers that satisfy the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=p(4-p),\\
r&=q(4-q),\\
p&=r(4-r).
\end{aligned}
$$

Find all possible values of $p+q+r$.

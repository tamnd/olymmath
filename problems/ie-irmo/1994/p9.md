Let $w,a,b$ and $c$ be distinct real numbers with the property that there exist real numbers $x,y$ and $z$ for which the following equations hold:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x+y+z&=1,\\
xa^2+yb^2+zc^2&=w^2,\\
xa^3+yb^3+zc^3&=w^3,\\
xa^4+yb^4+zc^4&=w^4.
\end{aligned}
$$

Express $w$ in terms of $a,b$ and $c$.

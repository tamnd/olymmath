For each integer $n$ such that $n=p_1p_2p_3p_4$, where $p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4$ are distinct primes, let
$$
d_1=1<d_2<d_3<\cdots<d_{15}<d_{16}=n
$$
be the sixteen positive integers that divide $n$. Prove that if $n<1995$, then $d_9-d_8\ne 22$.

Suppose that $a$, $b$ and $c$ are complex numbers, and that all three roots $z$ of the equation
$$
x^3+ax^2+bx+c=0
$$
satisfy $|z|=1$ (where $|\,|$ denotes absolute value). Prove that all three roots $w$ of the equation
$$
x^3+|a|x^2+|b|x+|c|=0
$$
also satisfy $|w|=1$.

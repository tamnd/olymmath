We are given three points $P,Q,R$ in the plane. It is known that there is a triangle $ABC$ such that $P$ is the mid-point of the side $BC$, $Q$ is the point on the side $CA$ with $|CQ|/|QA|=2$, and $R$ is the point on the side $AB$ with $|AR|/|RB|=2$. Determine, with proof, how the triangle $ABC$ may be constructed from $P,Q,R$.

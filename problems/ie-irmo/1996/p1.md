For each positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ denote the highest common factor of $n!+1$ and $(n+1)!$ (where $!$ denotes factorial). Find, with proof, a formula for $f(n)$ for each $n$. [Note that “highest common factor” is another name for “greatest common divisor”.]

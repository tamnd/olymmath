Let $ S(n)$ denote the sum of the digits of a natural number $ n$ (in base $ 10$). Prove that for every $ n$, $ S(2n) \le 2S(n) \le 10S(2n)$. Prove also that there is a positive integer $ n$ with $ S(n)=1996S(3n)$.

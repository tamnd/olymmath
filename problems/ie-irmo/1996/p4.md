Let $F$ be the mid-point of the side $BC$ of a triangle $ABC$. Isosceles right-angled triangles $ABD$ and $ACE$ are constructed externally on the sides $AB$ and $AC$ with right-angles at $D$ and $E$ respectively. Prove that $DEF$ is an isosceles right-angled triangle.

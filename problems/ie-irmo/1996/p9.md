Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $D$, $E$, $F$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $A$, $B$, $C$ onto the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$, respectively. Let $P$, $Q$, $R$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $A$, $B$, $C$ onto the lines $EF$, $FD$, $DE$, respectively. Prove that the lines $AP$, $BQ$, $CR$ (extended) are concurrent.

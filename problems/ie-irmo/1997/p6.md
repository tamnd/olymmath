Given a positive integer $n$, denote by $\sigma(n)$ the sum of all positive integers which divide $n$. [For example, $\sigma(3)=1+3=4$, $\sigma(6)=1+2+3+6=12$, $\sigma(12)=1+2+3+4+6+12=28$].

We say that $n$ is *abundant* if $\sigma(n)>2n$. (So, for example, $12$ is abundant). Let $a,b$ be positive integers and suppose that $a$ is abundant. Prove that $ab$ is abundant.

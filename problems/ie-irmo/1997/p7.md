$ABCD$ is a quadrilateral which is circumscribed about a circle $\Gamma$ (i.e., each side of the quadrilateral is tangent to $\Gamma$.) If $\angle A=\angle B=120^\circ$, $\angle D=90^\circ$ and $BC$ has length $1$, find, with proof, the length of $AD$.

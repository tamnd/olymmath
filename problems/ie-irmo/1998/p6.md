Find all positive integers $n$ that have exactly 16 positive integral divisors $d_1$, $d_2$, $\ldots$, $d_{16}$ such that
$$1=d_1<d_2<\cdots<d_{16}=n,$$
$d_6=18$ and $d_9-d_8=17$.

Prove that if $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive real numbers, then

(a)
$$\frac{9}{a+b+c}\leq 2\left(\frac{1}{a+b}+\frac{1}{b+c}+\frac{1}{c+a}\right),$$
and

(b)
$$\frac{1}{a+b}+\frac{1}{b+c}+\frac{1}{c+a}\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right).$$

A sequence of real numbers $x_n$ is defined recursively as follows: $x_0,x_1$ are arbitrary positive real numbers, and
$$x_{n+2}=\frac{1+x_{n+1}}{x_n},\quad n=0,1,2,\ldots.$$
Find $x_{1998}$.

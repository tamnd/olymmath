$ABCDEF$ is a convex (not necessarily regular) hexagon with $AB=BC$, $CD=DE$, $EF=FA$ and

$$\angle ABC+\angle CDE+\angle EFA=360^\circ.$$

Prove that the perpendiculars from $A$, $C$ and $E$ to $FB$, $BD$ and $DF$, respectively, are concurrent.

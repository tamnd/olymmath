Show that there is a positive number in the Fibonacci sequence that is divisible by $1000$.

[The Fibonacci sequence $F_n$ is defined by the conditions:
$$
F_0=0,\ F_1=1,\ F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}\text{ for }n\geq 2
$$
So, the sequence begins $0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13,\ldots$ ]

Let $D$, $E$ and $F$, respectively, be points on the sides $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$, respectively, of a triangle $ABC$ so that $AD$ is perpendicular to $BC$, $BE$ is the angle-bisector of $\angle B$ and $F$ is the mid-point of $AB$. Prove that $AD$, $BE$ and $CF$ are concurrent if, and only if,
$$
a^2(a-c)=(b^2-c^2)(a+c),
$$
where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are the lengths of the sides $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$, respectively, of the triangle $ABC$.

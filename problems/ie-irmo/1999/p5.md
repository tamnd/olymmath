Three real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a<b<c$, are said to be in arithmetic progression if $c-b=b-a$.

Define a sequence $u_n$, $n=0,1,2,3,\ldots$ as follows: $u_0=0$, $u_1=1$ and, for each $n\geq1$, $u_{n+1}$ is the smallest positive integer such that $u_{n+1}>u_n$ and $\{u_0,u_1,\ldots,u_n,u_{n+1}\}$ contains no three elements that are in arithmetic progression.

Find $u_{100}$.

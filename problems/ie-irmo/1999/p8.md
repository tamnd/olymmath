Let $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ be positive real numbers whose sum is 1. Prove that

$$\frac{a^2}{a+b}+\frac{b^2}{b+c}+\frac{c^2}{c+d}+\frac{d^2}{d+a}\geq\frac12,$$

with equality if, and only if, $a=b=c=d=1/4$.

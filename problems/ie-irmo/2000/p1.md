Let $S$ be the set of all numbers of the form $a(n)=n^2+n+1$, where $n$ is a natural number. Prove that the product $a(n)a(n+1)$ is in $S$ for all natural numbers $n$. Give, with proof, an example of a pair of elements $s,t\in S$ such that $st\notin S$.

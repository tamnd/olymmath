Let $p(x)=a_0+a_1x+\cdots+a_nx^n$ be a polynomial with non-negative real coefficients. Suppose that $p(4)=2$ and that $p(16)=8$. Prove that $p(8)\leq 4$ and find, with proof, all such polynomials with $p(8)=4$.

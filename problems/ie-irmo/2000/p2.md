Let $ABCDE$ be a regular pentagon with its sides of length one. Let $F$ be the midpoint of $AB$ and let $G,H$ be points on the sides $CD$ and $DE$, respectively, such that $\angle GFD=\angle HFD=30^\circ$. Prove that the triangle $GFH$ is equilateral. A square is inscribed in the triangle $GFH$ with one side of the square along $GH$. Prove that $FG$ has length

$$
t=\frac{2\cos 18^\circ(\cos 36^\circ)^2}{\cos 6^\circ},
$$

and that the square has sides of length

$$
\frac{t\sqrt{3}}{2+\sqrt{3}}.
$$

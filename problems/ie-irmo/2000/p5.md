Consider all parabolas of the form $y=x^2+2px+q$ ($p,q$ real) which intersect the $x$- and $y$-axes in three distinct points. For such a pair $p,q$ let $C_{p,q}$ be the circle through the points of intersection of the parabola $y=x^2+2px+q$ with the axes. Prove that all the circles $C_{p,q}$ have a point in common.

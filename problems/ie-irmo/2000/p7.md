Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral and $R$ the radius of the circumcircle. Let $a,b,c,d$ be the lengths of the sides of $ABCD$ and $Q$ its area. Prove that
$$R^2=\frac{(ab+cd)(ac+bd)(ad+bc)}{16Q^2}.$$
Deduce that
$$R\geq\frac{(abcd)^{3/4}}{Q\sqrt{2}},$$
with equality if and only if $ABCD$ is a square.

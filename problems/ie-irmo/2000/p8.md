For each positive integer $n$ determine with proof, all positive integers $m$ such that there exist positive integers $x_1<x_2<\cdots<x_n$ with
$$\frac{1}{x_1}+\frac{2}{x_2}+\frac{3}{x_3}+\cdots+\frac{n}{x_n}=m.$$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with sides $BC,CA,AB$ of lengths $a,b,c$, respectively. Let $D,E$ be the midpoints of the sides $AC,AB$, respectively. Prove that $BD$ is perpendicular to $CE$ if, and only if,
$$b^2+c^2=5a^2.$$

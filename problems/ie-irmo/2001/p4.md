Prove that

(a) $$\frac{2n}{3n+1}\leq\sum_{k=n+1}^{2n}\frac{1}{k},$$

and

(b) $$\sum_{k=n+1}^{2n}\frac{1}{k}<\frac{3n+1}{4(n+1)},$$

for all positive integers $n$.

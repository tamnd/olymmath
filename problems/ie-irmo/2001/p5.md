Let $a,b$ be real numbers such that $ab>0$. Prove that
$$\sqrt[3]{\frac{a^2b^2(a+b)^2}{4}}\leq\frac{a^2+10ab+b^2}{12}.$$
Determine when equality occurs.

Hence, or otherwise, prove for all real numbers $a,b$ that
$$\sqrt[3]{\frac{a^2b^2(a+b)^2}{4}}\leq\frac{a^2+ab+b^2}{3}.$$
Determine the cases of equality.

Let $ABC$ be an acute angled triangle, and let $D$ be the point on the line $BC$ for which $AD$ is perpendicular to $BC$. Let $P$ be a point on the line segment $AD$. The lines $BP$ and $CP$ intersect $AC$ and $AB$ at $E$ and $F$ respectively. Prove that the line $AD$ bisects the angle $EDF$.

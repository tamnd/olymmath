Let $ABC$ be a triangle whose side lengths are all integers, and let $D$ and $E$ be the points at which the incircle of $ABC$ touches $BC$ and $AC$ respectively. If $\bigl||AD|^2-|BE|^2\bigr|\leq 2$, show that $|AC|=|BC|$.

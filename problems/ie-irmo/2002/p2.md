(a) A group of people attends a party. Each person has at most three acquaintances in the group, and if two people do not know each other, then they have a mutual acquaintance in the group. What is the maximum number of people present?

(b) If, in addition, the group contains three mutual acquaintances (i.e., three people each of whom knows the other two), what is the maximum number of people?

Let the sequence $a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,\ldots$ be defined by
$$a_1=1,\quad a_2=1,\quad a_3=1$$
and
$$a_{n+1}a_{n-2}-a_na_{n-1}=2,$$
for all $n\geq 3$. Prove that $a_n$ is a positive integer for all $n\geq 1$.

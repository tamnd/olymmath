A $3\times n$ grid is filled as follows : the first row consists of the numbers from $1$ to $n$ arranged from left to right in ascending order. The second row is a cyclic shift of the top row. Thus the order goes $i,i+1,\ldots,n-1,n,1,2,\ldots,i-1$ for some $i$. The third row has the numbers $1$ to $n$ in some order, subject to the rule that in each of the $n$ columns, the sum of the three numbers is the same.

For which values of $n$ is it possible to fill the grid according to the above rules? For an $n$ for which this is possible, determine the number of different ways of filling the grid.

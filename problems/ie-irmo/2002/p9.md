For each real number $x$, define $[x]$ to be the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$.

Let $\alpha=2+\sqrt{3}$. Prove that

$$\alpha^n-[\alpha^n]=1-\alpha^{-n},\text{ for }n=0,1,2,\ldots$$

Let $T$ be a triangle of perimeter $2$, and let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be the lengths of the sides of $T$.

(a) Show that
$$
abc+\frac{28}{27}\ge ab+bc+ac.
$$

(b) Show that
$$
ab+bc+ac\ge abc+1.
$$

Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $D,E$ and $F$, respectively, be points on the sides $BC,CA$ and $AB$, respectively—none of which coincides with a vertex of the triangle—such that $AD,BE$ and $CF$ meet at a point $G$. Suppose the triangles $AGF,CGE$ and $BGD$ have equal area. Prove that $G$ is the centroid of $ABC$.

Determine the number of different arrangements $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{10}$ of the integers $1,2,\ldots,10$ such that

$$a_i>a_{2i}\quad\text{for }1\leq i\leq 5,$$

and

$$a_i>a_{2i+1}\quad\text{for }1\leq i\leq 4.$$

Suppose $a,b$ and $c$ are non-negative real numbers. Prove that

$$\frac{1}{3}\bigl[(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2\bigr]\leq a^2+b^2+c^2-3\sqrt[3]{a^2b^2c^2}\leq(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2.$$

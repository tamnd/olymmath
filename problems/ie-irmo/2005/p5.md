Let $ a,b,c$ be nonnegative real numbers. Prove that:

$ \frac{1}{3}((a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2) \le a^2+b^2+c^2-3 \sqrt[3]{a^2 b^2 c^2 } \le (a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2.$

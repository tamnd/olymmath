Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $X$ be a point on the side $AB$ that is not $A$ or $B$. Let $P$ be the incentre of the triangle $ACX$, $Q$ the incentre of the triangle $BCX$ and $M$ the midpoint of the segment $PQ$. Show that $|MC|>|MX|$.

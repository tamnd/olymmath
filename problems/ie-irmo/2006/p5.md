Determine, with proof, all functions $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ such that $f(1)=1$, and

$$
f(xy+f(x))=xf(y)+f(x)
$$

for all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$.

Notation: $\mathbb{R}$ denotes the set of real numbers.

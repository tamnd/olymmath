Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the greatest common divisor of the numbers

$$
\binom{2n}{1},\ \binom{2n}{3},\ \binom{2n}{5},\ \ldots,\ \binom{2n}{2n-1}.
$$

Notation: If $a$ and $b$ are nonnegative integers such that $a\geq b$, then

$$
\binom{a}{b}=\frac{a!}{(a-b)!b!}.
$$

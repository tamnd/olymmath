Suppose that $x$, $y$ and $z$ are positive real numbers such that $xyz\geq1$.

(a) Prove that

$$
27\leq(1+x+y)^2+(1+y+z)^2+(1+z+x)^2,
$$

with equality if and only if $x=y=z=1$.

(b) Prove that

$$
(1+x+y)^2+(1+y+z)^2+(1+z+x)^2\leq3(x+y+z)^2,
$$

with equality if and only if $x=y=z=1$.

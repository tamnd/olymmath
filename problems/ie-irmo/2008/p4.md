How many sequences $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{2008}$ are there such that each of the numbers $1,2,\ldots,2008$ occurs once in the sequence, and $i\in\{a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_i\}$ for each $i$ such that $2\leq i\leq2008$?

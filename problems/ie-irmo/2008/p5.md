A triangle $ ABC$ has an obtuse angle at $ B$. The perpindicular at $ B$ to $ AB$ meets $ AC$ at $ D$, and $ |CD| = |AB|$.
Prove that $ |AD|^2 = |AB|.|BC|$ if and only if $ \angle CBD = 30^\circ$.

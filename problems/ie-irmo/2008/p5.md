A triangle $ABC$ has an obtuse angle at $B$. The perpendicular at $B$ to $AB$ meets $AC$ at $D$, and $|CD|=|AB|$. Prove that
$$
|AD|^2=|AB|\cdot|BC|\quad\text{if and only if}\quad\angle CBD=30^\circ.
$$

Find $a_3,a_4,\ldots,a_{2008}$ such that $a_i=\pm1$ for $i=3,\ldots,2008$ and

$$
\sum_{i=3}^{2008}a_i2^i=2008,
$$

and show that the numbers $a_3,a_4,\ldots,a_{2008}$ are uniquely determined by these conditions.

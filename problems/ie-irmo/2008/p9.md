Given $k\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ and a positive integer $n$, let $f_k(n)$ be the number of sequences $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, where $x_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ for $i=1,\ldots,n$, and

$$
x_1+\cdots+x_n\equiv k\pmod{4}.
$$

(a) Prove that $f_1(n)=f_3(n)$ for all positive integers $n$.

(b) Prove that

$$
f_0(n)=\frac{3^n+2+(-1)^n}{4}
$$

for all positive integers $n$.

Suppose that $x,y$ and $z$ are positive numbers such that $$ 1=2xyz+xy+yz+zx $$ Prove that
(i) $$ \frac{3}{4}\le xy+yz+zx<1 $$ (ii) $$ xyz\le \frac{1}{8} $$ Using (i) or otherwise, deduce that $$ x+y+z\ge \frac{3}{2} $$ and derive the case of equality.

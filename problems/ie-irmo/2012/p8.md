Suppose $a,b,c$ are positive numbers. Prove that $$ \left(\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{a}+1\right)^2\ge (2a+b+c) \left(\frac{2}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right) $$ with equality if and only if $a=b=c$ .

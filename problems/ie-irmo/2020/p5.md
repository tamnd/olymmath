Let $a,b,c>0$. Prove that

$$
\sqrt[7]{\frac{a}{b+c}+\frac{b}{a+c}}
+\sqrt[7]{\frac{b}{c+a}+\frac{c}{a+b}}
+\sqrt[7]{\frac{c}{a+b}+\frac{a}{b+c}}
\geq 3.
$$

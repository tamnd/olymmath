Let $\mathbb{N}$ denote the strictly positive integers. A function $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ satisfies the following for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$:

$$
f(1)=1
$$

$$
f(f(n))=n
$$

$$
f(2n)=2f(n)+1.
$$

Find the value of $f(2020)$.

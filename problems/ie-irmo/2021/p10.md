Let $P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{2021}$ be 2021 points in the quarter plane $\{(x, y): x \geq 0, y \geq 0\}$ . The centroid of these 2021 points lies at the point $(1,1)$ .

Show that there are two distinct points $P_{i}, P_{j}$ such that the distance from $P_{i}$ to $P_{j}$ is no more than $\sqrt{2} / 20$ .

Let $\triangle$ ABC be a triangle with circumcentre *O*. The perpendicular line from *O* to *BC* intersects line *BC* at *M* and line *AC* at *P*, and the perpendicular line from *O* to *AC* intersects line *AC* at *N* and line *BC* at *Q*. Let *D* be the intersection point of lines *PQ* and *MN*. construct the parallelogram *PCQJ* with *PJ* || *CQ* and *QJ* || *CP*.
Prove the following:

a) The points *A*, *B*, *O*, *P*, *Q*, *J* are all on the same circle.
b) line *OD* is perpendicular to line *CJ*.

Find all functions $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ with the property that

$$
f(x)f(y)=(xy-1)^2f\left(\frac{x+y-1}{xy-1}\right)
$$

for all real numbers $x,y$ with $xy\neq 1$.

The positive integers $a,b,c,d$ satisfy

(i) $a+b+c+d=2023$

(ii) $2023\mid ab-cd$

(iii) $2023\mid a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$.

Assuming that each of the numbers $a,b,c,d$ is divisible by $7$, prove that each of the numbers $a,b,c,d$ is divisible by $17$.

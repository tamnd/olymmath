Let $\mathbb{Z}_{+}=\{1,2,3,4,\ldots\}$ be the set of all positive integers. Find, with proof, all functions $f:\mathbb{Z}_{+}\mapsto\mathbb{Z}_{+}$ with the property that
$$
f(x+f(y)+f(f(z)))=z+f(y)+f(f(x))
$$
for all positive integers $x,y,z$.

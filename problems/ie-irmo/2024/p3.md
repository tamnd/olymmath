Let $\mathbb{Z}_+=\{1,2,3,4,\ldots\}$ be the set of all positive integers. Determine all functions $f:\mathbb{Z}_+\mapsto\mathbb{Z}_+$ that satisfy
$$
f(mn)+1=f(m)+f(n)\text{ for all positive integers }m\text{ and }n;
$$
$$
f(2024)=1;
$$
$$
f(n)=1\text{ for all positive }n\equiv22\pmod{23}.
$$

A game of coins is played as follows: You start with $1$ head and $1$ tail on a table. At each turn, you can perform any one of the following moves:

a) You can turn over all the coins on the table.

b) You can triple the number of heads and tails at the table.

c) If there are at least $4$ tails on the table, you can turn over $4$ tails.

d) If there are at least $5$ tails on the table, you can turn over $3$ of the tails and discard $2$ of the tails.

Knowing that at the end of the game you have $2024$ heads, what are all possible numbers of tails at the end of that game?

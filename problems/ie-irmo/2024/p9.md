Let $K,L,M$ denote three points on the sides $BC$, $AB$ and $BC$ of $\triangle ABC$, so that $ALKM$ is a parallelogram. Points $S$ and $T$ are chosen on lines $KL$ and $KM$ respectively, so that the quadrilaterals $AKBS$ and $AKCT$ are both cyclic.

Prove that $MLST$ is cyclic if and only if $K$ is the midpoint of $BC$.

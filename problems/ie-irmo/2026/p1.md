The points $A,B,C,D$ are on four different sides of a square of side $r$, and $a=|AB|$, $b=|BC|$, $c=|CD|$, $d=|DA|$. Prove that

$$
\operatorname{area}(ABCD)\geq r^2-\frac14(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)
$$

and that $ABCD$ is a rectangle in case of equality.

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle with circumcentre $O$. Lines $BO$ and $AC$ intersect at $J$. Lines $CO$ and $AB$ intersect at $L$. The circumcircle of $\triangle BOC$ intersects line $AO$ for the second time at $P$. The circumcircles of $\triangle PCJ$ and $\triangle PBL$ intersect for the second time at $Q$.

Prove that the line $PQ$ passes through the orthocentre $H$ of $\triangle ABC$.

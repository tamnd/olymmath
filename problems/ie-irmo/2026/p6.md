Aisling and Brendan play the following game with 2026 stones.

- Aisling divides the 2026 stones into three non-empty piles.
- Brendan removes one stone from each of two piles, adds one of those stones to the third pile and discards the remaining stone.
- Aisling and Brendan then continue in turns, starting with Aisling, selecting two piles from which one stone is removed, adding one of those stones to the third pile and discarding the last stone.
- A pile can reduce to zero stones, and stones can be added back to a zero pile, but no pile can become negative.
- The first player unable to move loses the game.

Determine a strategy for Aisling which ensures that she will win the game.

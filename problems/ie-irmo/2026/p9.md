Let $\mathbb{Z}$ denote the set of all integers, $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ the positive integers (excluding zero), and $c\in\mathbb{Z}$. Find all functions $f:\mathbb{Z}_{>0}\mapsto\mathbb{Z}$ such that for all integers $x>y>0$:

$$f(x-y)+f(x+y)=2f(x)+cf(y).$$

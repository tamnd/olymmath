Prove the inequality**a.)** $
\left( a_{1}+a_{2}+...+a_{k}\right) ^{2}\leq k\left(
a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+...+a_{k}^{2}\right) , $ where $k\geq 1$ is a natural number and $a_{1},$ $a_{2},$ $...,$ $a_{k}$ are arbitrary real numbers.**b.)** Using the inequality (1), show that if the real numbers $a_{1},$ $a_{2},$ $...,$ $a_{n}$ satisfy the inequality

$$
a_{1}+a_{2}+...+a_{n}\geq \sqrt{\left( n-1\right) \left(
a_{1}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}+...+a_{n}^{2}\right) },
$$

then all of these numbers $a_{1},$ $a_{2},$ $\ldots,$ $a_{n}$ are non-negative.

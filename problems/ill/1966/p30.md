Let $n$ be a positive integer, prove that :

(a) $\log_{10}(n + 1) > \frac{3}{10n} +\log_{10}n ;$

(b) $ \log n! > \frac{3n}{10}\left( \frac 12+\frac 13 +\cdots +\frac 1n -1\right).$

Prove the following inequality:
$$\prod^k_{i=1} x_i \cdot \sum^k_{i=1} x^{n-1}_i \leq \sum^k_{i=1}
x^{n+k-1}_i,$$
where $x_i > 0,$ $k \in \mathbb{N}, n \in
\mathbb{N}.$

$(MON 4)$ Let $p$ and $q$ be two prime numbers greater than $3.$ Prove that if their difference is $2^n$, then for any two integers $m$ and $n,$ the number $S = p^{2m+1} + q^{2m+1}$ is divisible by $3.$

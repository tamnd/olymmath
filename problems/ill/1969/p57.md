Given triangle $ ABC $ with points $ M $ and $ N $ are in the sides $ AB $ and $ AC $ respectively.
If $ \dfrac{BM}{MA} +\dfrac{CN}{NA} = 1 $, then prove that the centroid of $ ABC $ lies on $ MN $.

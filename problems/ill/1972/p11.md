The least number is $m$ and the greatest number is $M$ among $ a_1 ,a_2 ,\ldots,a_n$ satisfying $ a_1 +a_2 +...+a_n =0$. Prove that
$$ a_1^2 +\cdots +a_n^2 \le-nmM$$

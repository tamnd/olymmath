Given the expression
$$P_n(x) =\frac{1}{2^n}\left[(x +\sqrt{x^2 - 1})^n+(x-\sqrt{x^2 - 1})^n\right],$$
prove: $(a) P_n(x)$ satisfies the identity
$$P_n(x) - xP_{n-1}(x) + \frac{1}{4}P_{n-2}(x) \equiv 0.$$ $(b) P_n(x)$ is a polynomial in $x$ of degree $n.$

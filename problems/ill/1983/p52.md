Let $(F_n)_{n\geq 1} $ be the Fibonacci sequence $F_1 = F_2 = 1, F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_n (n \geq 1),$ and $P(x)$ the polynomial of degree $990$ satisfying
$$ P(k) = F_k, \text{ for } k = 992, . . . , 1982.$$

Prove that $P(1983) = F_{1983} - 1.$

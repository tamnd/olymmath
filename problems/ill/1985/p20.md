Let $T$ be the set of all lattice points (i.e., all points with integer coordinates) in three-dimensional space. Two such points $(x, y, z)$ and $(u, v,w)$ are called *neighbors* if $|x - u| + |y - v| + |z - w| = 1$ . Show that there exists a subset $S$ of $T$ such that for each $p \in T$ , there is exactly one point of $S$ among $p$ and its *neighbors*.

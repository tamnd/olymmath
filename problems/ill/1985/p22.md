The positive integers $x_1, \cdots , x_n$, $n \geq 3$, satisfy $x_1 < x_2 <\cdots< x_n < 2x_1$. Set $P = x_1x_2 \cdots x_n.$ Prove that if $p$ is a prime number, $k$ a positive integer, and $P$ is divisible by $pk$, then $\frac{P}{p^k} \geq n!.$

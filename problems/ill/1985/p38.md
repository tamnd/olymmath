The tangents at $B$ and $C$ to the circumcircle of the acute-angled triangle $ABC$ meet at $X$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Prove that

(a) $\angle BAM = \angle CAX$, and

(b) $\frac{AM}{AX} = \cos\angle BAC.$

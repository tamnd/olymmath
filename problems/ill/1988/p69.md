Let $ Q$ be the centre of the inscribed circle of a triangle $ ABC.$ Prove that for any point $ P,$
$$ a(PA)^2 + b(PB)^2 + c(PC)^2 = a(QA)^2 + b(QB)^2 + c(QC)^2 + (a + b + c)(QP)^2,
$$

where $ a = BC, b = CA$ and $ c = AB.$

Let $ \{a_k\}^{\infty}_1$ be a sequence of non-negative real numbers such that:
$$ a_k - 2 a_{k + 1} + a_{k + 2} \geq 0
$$

and $ \sum^k_{j = 1} a_j \leq 1$ for all $ k = 1,2, \ldots$. Prove that:
$$ 0 \leq a_{k} - a_{k + 1} < \frac {2}{k^2}
$$

for all $ k = 1,2, \ldots$.

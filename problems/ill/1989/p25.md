The integers $ c_{m,n}$ with $ m \geq 0, \geq 0$ are defined by

$$ c_{m,0} = 1 \forall m \geq 0, c_{0,n} = 1 \forall n \geq 0,$$

and

$$ c_{m,n} = c_{m-1,n} - n \cdot c_{m-1,n-1} \forall m > 0, n > 0.$$

Prove that $$ c_{m,n} = c_{n,m} \forall m > 0, n > 0.$$

Let $ a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$ which are not perfect squares. Prove that if
$$ x^2 - ay^2 - bz^2 + abw^2 = 0$$
has a nontrivial solution in integers, then so does
$$ x^2 - ay^2 - bz^2 = 0.$$

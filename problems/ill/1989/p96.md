Let $ f : \mathbb{N} \mapsto \mathbb{N}$ be such that

**(i)** $ f$ is strictly increasing;
**(ii)** $ f(mn) = f(m)f(n) \forall m, n \in \mathbb{N};$ and
**(iii)** if $ m \neq n$ and $ m^n = n^m,$ then $ f(m) = n$ or $ f(n) = m.$

Determine $ f(30).$

Let $ f(0) = f(1) = 0$ and

$$ f(n+2) = 4^{n+2} \cdot f(n+1) - 16^{n+1} \cdot f(n) + n \cdot 2^{n^2}, n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$$

Show that the numbers $ f(1989), f(1990), f(1991)$ are divisible by $ 13.$

Let $ p(x)$ be a cubic polynomial with rational coefficients. $ q_1$, $ q_2$, $ q_3$,... is a sequence of rationals such that $ q_n = p(q_{n + 1})$ for all positive $ n$. Show that for some $ k$, we have $ q_{n + k} = q_n$ for all positive $ n$.

An *Egyptian number* is a positive integer that can be expressed as a sum of positive integers, not necessarily distinct, such that the sum of their reciprocals is $1$ . For example, $32 = 2 + 3 + 9 + 18$ is Egyptian because $\frac 12 +\frac 13 +\frac 19 +\frac{1}{18}=1$ . Prove that all integers greater than $23$ are *Egyptian*.

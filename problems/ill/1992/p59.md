Let a regular $7$ -gon $A_0A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5A_6$ be inscribed in a circle. Prove that for any two points $P, Q$ on the arc $A_0A_6$ the following equality holds:
$$\sum_{i=0}^6 (-1)^{i} PA_i = \sum_{i=0}^6 (-1)^{i} QA_i .$$

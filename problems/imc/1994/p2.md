Let $f \in C^1(a,b)$, $\lim_{x\to a+} f(x) = +\infty$, $\lim_{x\to b-} f(x) = -\infty$ and $f'(x) + f^2(x) \ge -1$ for $x \in (a,b)$. Prove that $b-a \ge \pi$ and give an example where $b-a=\pi$.

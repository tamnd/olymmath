Given a set $S$ of $2n-1$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, different irrational numbers. Prove that there are $n$ different elements $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n \in S$ such that for all non-negative rational numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ with $a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n > 0$ we have that $a_1x_1+a_2x_2+\cdots+a_nx_n$ is an irrational number.

Suppose that $(f_{n})_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is a sequence of continuous functions on the interval $[0,1]$ such that $$ \int_{0}^{1}f_{m}(x)f_{n}(x) dx=
\begin{cases}
1& \text{if} n=m
0 & \text{if} n\ne m
\end{cases} $$ and $\sup\{|f_{n}(x)|: x\in [0,1] \text{and} n=1,2,\dots\}< \infty$ .
Show that there exists no subsequence $(f_{n_{k}})$ of $(f_{n})$ such that $\lim_{k\to \infty}f_{n_{k}}(x)$ exist
for all $x\in [0,1]$ .

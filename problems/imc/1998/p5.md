Let $P$ be an algebraic polynomial of degree $n$ having only real zeros and real coefficients.

a) Prove that for every real $x$ the following inequality holds:

$$(n-1)(P'(x))^2 \ge nP(x)P''(x). \tag{2}$$

b) Examine the cases of equality.

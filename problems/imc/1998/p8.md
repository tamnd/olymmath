Let

$$\mathcal{P} = \left\{f : f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^3 a_k x^k,\ a_k \in \mathbb{R},\ |f(\pm 1)| \le 1,\ |f(\pm \frac{1}{2})| \le 1\right\}.$$

Evaluate

$$\sup_{f\in\mathcal{P}} \max_{-1\le x\le 1} |f''(x)|$$

and find all polynomials $f \in \mathcal{P}$ for which the above “sup” is attained.

Suppose that a function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies the inequality

$$\left|\sum_{k=1}^n 3^k\big(f(x+ky) - f(x-ky)\big)\right| \le 1 \tag{1}$$

for every positive integer $n$ and for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$. Prove that $f$ is a constant function.

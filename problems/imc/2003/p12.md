Let $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be the sequence defined by

$$a_0 = 1, \quad a_{n+1} = \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{k=0}^n \frac{a_k}{n-k+2}.$$

Find the limit

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=0}^n \frac{a_k}{2^k},$$

if it exists.

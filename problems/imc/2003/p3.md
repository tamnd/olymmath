Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ real matrix such that $3A^3 = A^2 + A + I$ ($I$ is the identity matrix). Show that the sequence $A^k$ converges to an idempotent matrix. (A matrix $B$ is called idempotent if $B^2 = B$.)

Prove that if $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is three times differentiable, then there exists a real number $\xi \in (-1,1)$ such that

$$\frac{f'''(\xi)}{6} = \frac{f(1) - f(-1)}{2} - f'(0).$$

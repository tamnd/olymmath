Let $H$ be an infinite-dimensional real Hilbert space, let $d > 0$, and suppose that $S$ is a set of points (not necessarily countable) in $H$ such that the distance between any two distinct points in $S$ is equal to $d$. Show that there is a point $y \in H$ such that

$$\left\{ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{d}(x - y) : x \in S \right\}$$

is an orthonormal system of vectors in $H$.

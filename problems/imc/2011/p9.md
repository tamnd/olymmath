Let $f$ be a polynomial with real coefficients of degree $n$ . Suppose that $ \frac{f(x)-f(y)}{x-y}$ is an integer for all $0 \leq x<y \leq n$ . Prove that $a-b | f(a)-f(b)$ for all distinct integers $a,b$ .

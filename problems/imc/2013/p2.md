Let $ {f:{\cal R} \to {\cal R}}$ be a twice differentiable function. Suppose $ {f\left( 0 \right) = 0}$ . Prove there exists $ {\xi \in \left( { - \frac{\pi }{2},\frac{\pi }{2}} \right)}$ such that $$ {f''\left( \xi \right) = f\left( \xi \right)\left( {1 + 2{{\tan }^2}\xi } \right)}.$$

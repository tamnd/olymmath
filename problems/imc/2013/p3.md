There are $ {2n}$ students in a school $ {\left( {n \in {\Bbb N},n \geqslant 2} \right)}$ . Each week $ {n}$ students go on a trip. After several trips the following condition was fulfiled: every two students were together on at least one trip. What is the minimum number of trips needed for this to happen?

Suppose that $ {{v_1},{v_2},...,{v_d}}$ are unit vectors in $ {{{\Bbb R}^d}}$ . Prove that there exists a unitary vector $ {u}$ such that $ {\left| {u \cdot {v_i}} \right| \leq \frac{1}{{\sqrt d }}}$ for $ {i = 1,2,...,d}$ .**Note.** Here $ { \cdot }$ denotes the usual scalar product on $ {{{\Bbb R}^d}}$ .

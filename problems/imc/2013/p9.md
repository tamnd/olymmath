Does there exist an infinite set $ {M}$ consisting of positive integers such that for any $ {a,b \in M}$ with $ {a < b}$ the sum $ {a + b}$ is square-free?**Note.** A positive integer is called square-free if no perfect square greater than $ {1}$ divides it.

*Proposed by Fedor Petrov, St. Petersburg State University.*

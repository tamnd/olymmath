For any positive integer $m$ , denote by $P(m)$ the product of positive divisors of $m$ (e.g $P(6)=36$ ). For every positive integer $n$ define the sequence $$ a_1(n)=n, a_{k+1}(n)=P(a_k(n)) (k=1,2,\dots,2016) $$ Determine whether for every set $S\subset\{1,2,\dots,2017\}$ , there exists a positive integer $n$ such that the following condition is satisfied:

For every $k$ with $1\leq k\leq 2017$ , the number $a_k(n)$ is a perfect square if and only if $k\in S$ .

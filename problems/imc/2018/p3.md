Determine all rational numbers $a$ for which the matrix $$ \begin{pmatrix}
a & -a & -1 & 0
a & -a & 0 & -1
1 & 0 & a & -a
0 & 1 & a & -a
\end{pmatrix} $$ is the square of a matrix with all rational entries.

*Proposed by Daniël Kroes, University of California, San Diego*

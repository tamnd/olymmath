Let $(a_n)_{n=0}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $a_0=0$ and $$ a_{n+1}^3=a_n^2-8 \text{for} n=0,1,2,… $$ Prove that the following series is convergent: $$ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{|a_{n+1}-a_n|}. $$ *Proposed by Orif Ibrogimov, National University of Uzbekistan*

A four-digit number $YEAR$ is called *very good* if the system
\begin{align*}
Yx+Ey+Az+Rw& =Y
Rx+Yy+Ez+Aw & = E\
Ax+Ry+Yz+Ew & = A
Ex+Ay+Rz+Yw &= R
\end{align*}
of linear equations in the variables $x,y,z$ and $w$ has at least two solutions. Find all very good $YEAR$ s in the 21st century.

(The $21$ st century starts in $2001$ and ends in $2100$ .)

*Proposed by Tomáš Bárta, Charles University, Prague*

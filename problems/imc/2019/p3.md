Let $f:(-1,1)\to \mathbb{R}$ be a twice differentiable function such that $$ 2f'(x)+xf''(x)\geqslant 1 \text{ for } x\in (-1,1). $$ Prove that $$ \int_{-1}^{1}xf(x)dx\geqslant \frac{1}{3}. $$ *Proposed by Orif Ibrogimov, ETH Zurich and National University of Uzbekistan and Karim Rakhimov, Scuola Normale Superiore and National University of Uzbekistan*

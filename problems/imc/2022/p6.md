Let $p \geq 3$ be a prime number. Prove that there is a permutation $(x_1,\ldots, x_{p-1})$ of $(1,2,\ldots,p-1)$ such that $x_1x_2 + x_2x_3 + \cdots + x_{p-2}x_{p-1} \equiv 2 \pmod p$ .

Let $n$ be a positive integer. Suppose that $A$ and $B$ are $n \times n$ matrices with real entries such that

$$A^\top A + BB^\top = AB + BA,$$

where $X^\top$ denotes the transpose of matrix $X$.

Does this imply that $AB = BA$?

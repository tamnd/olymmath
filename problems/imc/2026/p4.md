Let $x_1 > 0$. Define the sequence $\{x_n\}$ by the recurrence

$$x_{n+1} = \arctan\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n}{n}\right) \quad \text{for all } n \ge 1.$$

Find $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n \sqrt{\ln n}$, where $\ln x$ denotes the natural logarithm of $x$.

(a) Is there a differentiable function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that

$$f'(f(x)) = x$$

for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

(b) Is there a differentiable function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that

$$f'(f(x)) = |x|$$

for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

For a continuous function $f : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$, let $S(f)$ be the union of all straight segments in the plane joining points $(x, 0)$ and $(f(x), 1)$, where $x \in [0,1]$. Let $A(f)$ be the area of $S(f)$. Find the infimum of $A(f)$ over all continuous $f$.

Let $n \ge 5$, and suppose that $A = (a_{ij})$ is a real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix such that

$$a_{ii} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad a_{ij} \in \{-1, 1\} \text{ for } i \neq j.$$

Assume that the scalar products of any two distinct rows of $A$ have the same value. Let $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n$ be the eigenvalues of $A$. Prove that

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} |\lambda_i| \ge 2n - 2$$

and determine all matrices for which equality holds.

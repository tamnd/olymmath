Let $a_1, a_2, \dots$ be an infinite sequence of positive real numbers satisfying

$$a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_{2n-1} = a_n^2$$

for all positive integers $n$. Prove that $a_n \ge 2n - 1$ for all positive integers $n$.

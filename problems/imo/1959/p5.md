(ROM) A segment $A B$ is given and on it a point $M$. On the same side of $A B$ squares $A M C D$ and $B M F E$ are constructed. The circumcircles of the two squares, whose centers are $P$ and $Q$, intersect in $M$ and another point $N$. (a) Prove that lines $F A$ and $B C$ intersect at $N$. (b) Prove that all such constructed lines $M N$ pass through the same point $S$, regardless of the selection of $M$. (c) Find the locus of the midpoints of all segments $P Q$, as $M$ varies along the segment $A B$.

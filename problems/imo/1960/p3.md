(ROM) A right-angled triangle $A B C$ is given for which the hypotenuse $B C$ has length $a$ and is divided into $n$ equal segments, where $n$ is odd. Let $\alpha$ be the angle with which the point $A$ sees the segment containing the middle of the hypotenuse. Prove that $$ \tan \alpha=\frac{4 n h}{\left(n^{2}-1\right) a}, $$ where $h$ is the height of the triangle.

(CZS) A cube $A B C D A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ is given. (a) Find the locus of all midpoints of segments $X Y$, where $X$ is any point on segment $A C$ and $Y$ any point on segment $B^{\prime} D^{\prime}$. (b) Find the locus of all points $Z$ on segments $X Y$ such that $\overrightarrow{Z Y}=2 \overrightarrow{X Z}$.

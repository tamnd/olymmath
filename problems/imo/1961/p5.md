(CZS) Construct a triangle $A B C$ if the following elements are given: $A C=b, A B=c$, and $\measuredangle A M B=\omega\left(\omega<90^{\circ}\right)$, where $M$ is the midpoint of $B C$. Prove that the construction has a solution if and only if $$ b \tan \frac{\omega}{2} \leq c<b . $$ In what case does equality hold?

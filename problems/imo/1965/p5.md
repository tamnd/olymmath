(ROM) Given a triangle $O A B$ such that $\angle A O B=\alpha<90^{\circ}$, let $M$ be an arbitrary point of the triangle different from $O$. Denote by $P$ and $Q$ the feet of the perpendiculars from $M$ to $O A$ and $O B$, respectively. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of the triangle $O P Q$. Find the locus of points $H$ when: (a) $M$ belongs to the segment $A B$; (b) $M$ belongs to the interior of $\triangle O A B$.

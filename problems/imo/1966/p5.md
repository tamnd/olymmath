(CZS) Solve the following system of equations: $$ \begin{aligned} & \left|a_{1}-a_{2}\right| x_{2}+\left|a_{1}-a_{3}\right| x_{3}+\left|a_{1}-a_{4}\right| x_{4}=1, \\ & \left|a_{2}-a_{1}\right| x_{1}+\left|a_{2}-a_{3}\right| x_{3}+\left|a_{2}-a_{4}\right| x_{4}=1, \\ & \left|a_{3}-a_{1}\right| x_{1}+\left|a_{3}-a_{2}\right| x_{2}+\left|a_{3}-a_{4}\right| x_{4}=1, \\ & \left|a_{4}-a_{1}\right| x_{1}+\left|a_{4}-a_{2}\right| x_{2}+\left|a_{4}-a_{3}\right| x_{3}=1, \end{aligned} $$ where $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$, and $a_{4}$ are mutually distinct real numbers.

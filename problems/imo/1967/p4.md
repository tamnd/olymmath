(BUL 4) Suppose medians $m_{a}$ and $m_{b}$ of a triangle are orthogonal. Prove that: (a) The medians of that triangle correspond to the sides of a right-angled triangle. (b) The inequality $$ 5\left(a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}\right) \geq 8 a b $$ is valid, where $a, b$, and $c$ are side lengths of the given triangle.

(BUL 5) Let $h_{n}$ be the apothem (distance from the center to one of the sides) of a regular $n$-gon $(n \geq 3)$ inscribed in a circle of radius $r$. Prove the inequality $$ (n+1) h_{n+1}-n h_{n}>r . $$ Also prove that if $r$ on the right side is replaced with a greater number, the inequality will not remain true for all $n \geq 3$.

(BEL 3) Consider a regular $2 n$-gon and the $n$ diagonals of it that pass through its center. Let $P$ be a point of the inscribed circle and let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be the angles in which the diagonals mentioned are visible from the point $P$. Prove that $$ \sum_{i=1}^{n} \tan ^{2} a_{i}=2 n \frac{\cos ^{2} \frac{\pi}{2 n}}{\sin ^{4} \frac{\pi}{2 n}} $$

(CZS 3) Let $M$ be an interior point of the tetrahedron $A B C D$. Prove that $$ \begin{aligned} & \overrightarrow{M A} \operatorname{vol}(M B C D)+\overrightarrow{M B} \operatorname{vol}(M A C D) \\ & +\overrightarrow{M C} \operatorname{vol}(M A B D)+\overrightarrow{M D} \operatorname{vol}(M A B C)=0 \end{aligned} $$ ( $\operatorname{vol}(P Q R S)$ denotes the volume of the tetrahedron $P Q R S)$.

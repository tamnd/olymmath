(FRA 1) In the triangle $A B C$ let $B^{\prime}$ and $C^{\prime}$ be the midpoints of the sides $A C$ and $A B$ respectively and $H$ the foot of the altitude passing through the vertex $A$. Prove that the circumcircles of the triangles $A B^{\prime} C^{\prime}, B C^{\prime} H$, and $B^{\prime} C H$ have a common point $I$ and that the line $H I$ passes through the midpoint of the segment $B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$.

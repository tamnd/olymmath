(BUL 2) Consider a sequence of polynomials $P_{0}(x), P_{1}(x), P_{2}(x), \ldots$, $P_{n}(x), \ldots$, where $P_{0}(x)=2, P_{1}(x)=x$ and for every $n \geq 1$ the following equality holds: $$ P_{n+1}(x)+P_{n-1}(x)=x P_{n}(x) $$ Prove that there exist three real numbers $a, b, c$ such that for all $n \geq 1$, $$ \left(x^{2}-4\right)\left[P_{n}^{2}(x)-4\right]=\left[a P_{n+1}(x)+b P_{n}(x)+c P_{n-1}(x)\right]^{2} $$

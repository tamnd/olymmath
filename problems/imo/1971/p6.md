(HUN 7) Let $n \geq 2$ be a natural number. Find a way to assign natural numbers to the vertices of a regular $2^{n}$-gon such that the following conditions are satisfied: (1) only digits 1 and 2 are used; (2) each number consists of exactly $n$ digits; (3) different numbers are assigned to different vertices; (4) the numbers assigned to two neighboring vertices differ at exactly one digit.

(BUL 6) Let a tetrahedron $A B C D$ be inscribed in a sphere $S$. Find the locus of points $P$ inside the sphere $S$ for which the equality $$ \frac{A P}{P A_{1}}+\frac{B P}{P B_{1}}+\frac{C P}{P C_{1}}+\frac{D P}{P D_{1}}=4 $$ holds, where $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$, and $D_{1}$ are the intersection points of $S$ with the lines $A P, B P, C P$, and $D P$, respectively.

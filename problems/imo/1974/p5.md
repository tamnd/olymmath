I 5 (GBR 3) Let $A_{r}, B_{r}, C_{r}$ be points on the circumference of a given circle $S$. From the triangle $A_{r} B_{r} C_{r}$, called $\triangle_{r}$, the triangle $\triangle_{r+1}$ is obtained by constructing the points $A_{r+1}, B_{r+1}, C_{r+1}$ on $S$ such that $A_{r+1} A_{r}$ is parallel to $B_{r} C_{r}, B_{r+1} B_{r}$ is parallel to $C_{r} A_{r}$, and $C_{r+1} C_{r}$ is parallel to $A_{r} B_{r}$. Each angle of $\triangle_{1}$ is an integer number of degrees and those integers are not multiples of 45 . Prove that at least two of the triangles $\triangle_{1}, \triangle_{2}, \ldots, \triangle_{15}$ are congruent.

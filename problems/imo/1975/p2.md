(CZS) ${ }^{\mathrm{IMO} 1}$ Let $x_{1} \geq x_{2} \geq \cdots \geq x_{n}$ and $y_{1} \geq y_{2} \geq \cdots \geq y_{n}$ be two $n$-tuples of numbers. Prove that $$ \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_{i}-y_{i}\right)^{2} \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_{i}-z_{i}\right)^{2} $$ is true when $z_{1}, z_{2}, \ldots, z_{n}$ denote $y_{1}, y_{2}, \ldots, y_{n}$ taken in another order.

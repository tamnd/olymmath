Define f(x) = 1 - a cos x - b sin x - A cos 2x - B sin 2x, where a, b, A, B are real constants. Suppose that f(x) ≥ 0 for all real x. Prove that $a^2$ + $b^2$ ≤ 2 and $A^2$ + $B^2$ ≤ 1.

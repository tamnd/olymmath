(BUL 1) The set $M=\{1,2, \ldots, 2 n\}$ is partitioned into $k$ nonintersecting subsets $M_{1}, M_{2}, \ldots, M_{k}$, where $n \geq k^{3}+k$. Prove that there exist even numbers $2 j_{1}, 2 j_{2}, \ldots, 2 j_{k+1}$ in $M$ that are in one and the same subset $M_{i}$ $(1 \leq i \leq k)$ such that the numbers $2 j_{1}-1,2 j_{2}-1, \ldots, 2 j_{k+1}-1$ are also in one and the same subset $M_{j}(1 \leq j \leq k)$.

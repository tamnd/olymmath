(CZS 2) Let $T_{1}$ be a triangle having $a, b, c$ as lengths of its sides and let $T_{2}$ be another triangle having $u, v, w$ as lengths of its sides. If $P, Q$ are the areas of the two triangles, prove that $$ 16 P Q \leq a^{2}\left(-u^{2}+v^{2}+w^{2}\right)+b^{2}\left(u^{2}-v^{2}+w^{2}\right)+c^{2}\left(u^{2}+v^{2}-w^{2}\right) $$ When does equality hold?

(GDR 2) For every integer $d \geq 1$, let $M_{d}$ be the set of all positive integers that cannot be written as a sum of an arithmetic progression with difference $d$, having at least two terms and consisting of positive integers. Let $A=M_{1}, B=M_{2} \backslash\{2\}, C=M_{3}$. Prove that every $c \in C$ may be written in a unique way as $c=a b$ with $a \in A, b \in B$.

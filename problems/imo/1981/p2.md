Take r such that 1 ≤ r ≤ n, and consider all subsets of r elements of the set {1, 2, ... , n}. Each subset has a smallest element. Let F(n,r) be the arithmetic mean of these smallest elements. Prove that: F(n,r) = (n+1)/(r+1).

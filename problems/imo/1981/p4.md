(CAN) Let $\left\{f_{n}\right\}$ be the Fibonacci sequence $\{1,1,2,3,5, \ldots\}$. (a) Find all pairs $(a, b)$ of real numbers such that for each $n, a f_{n}+b f_{n+1}$ is a member of the sequence. (b) Find all pairs $(u, v)$ of positive real numbers such that for each $n$, $u f_{n}^{2}+v f_{n+1}^{2}$ is a member of the sequence.

A3 (USS 4) ${ }^{\mathrm{IMO} 3}$ Consider the infinite sequences $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ of positive real numbers with the following properties: $$ x_{0}=1 \text { and for all } i \geq 0, x_{i+1} \leq x_{i} $$ (a) Prove that for every such sequence there is an $n \geq 1$ such that $\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+$ $\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}} \geq 3.999$. (b) Find such a sequence for which $\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{x_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{x_{n-1}^{2}}{x_{n}}<4$ for all $n$.

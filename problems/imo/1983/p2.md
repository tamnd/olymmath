(BEL 1) Let $n$ be a positive integer. Let $\sigma(n)$ be the sum of the natural divisors $d$ of $n$ (including 1 and $n$ ). We say that an integer $m \geq 1$ is superabundant (P.Erdös, 1944) if $\forall k \in\{1,2, \ldots, m-1\}, \frac{\sigma(m)}{m}>\frac{\sigma(k)}{k}$. Prove that there exists an infinity of superabundant numbers.

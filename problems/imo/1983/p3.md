(BEL 3) $)^{\mathrm{IMO} 4}$ We say that a set $E$ of points of the Euclidian plane is "Pythagorean" if for any partition of $E$ into two sets $A$ and $B$, at least one of the sets contains the vertices of a right-angled triangle. Decide whether the following sets are Pythagorean: (a) a circle; (b) an equilateral triangle (that is, the set of three vertices and the points of the three edges).

(BRA 1) Consider the set of all strictly decreasing sequences of $n$ natural numbers having the property that in each sequence no term divides any other term of the sequence. Let $A=\left(a_{j}\right)$ and $B=\left(b_{j}\right)$ be any two such sequences. We say that $A$ precedes $B$ if for some $k, a_{k}<b_{k}$ and $a_{i}=b_{i}$ for $i<k$. Find the terms of the first sequence of the set under this ordering.

(CAN 3) Let $c$ be a positive integer. The sequence $\left\{f_{n}\right\}$ is defined as follows: $$ f_{1}=1, f_{2}=c, f_{n+1}=2 f_{n}-f_{n-1}+2 (n \geq 2) $$ Show that for each $k \in \mathbb{N}$ there exists $r \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $f_{k} f_{k+1}=f_{r}$.

(NET 3) ${ }^{\mathrm{IMO} 3}$ The weight $w(p)$ of a polynomial $p, p(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_{i} x^{i}$, with integer coefficients $a_{i}$ is defined as the number of its odd coefficients. For $i=0,1,2, \ldots$, let $q_{i}(x)=(1+x)^{i}$. Prove that for any finite sequence $0 \leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{n}$, the inequality $$ w\left(q_{i_{1}}+\cdots+q_{i_{n}}\right) \geq w\left(q_{i_{1}}\right) $$ holds.

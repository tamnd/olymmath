(AUS 1) ${ }^{\mathrm{IMO} 2}$ Each of the numbers in the set $N=\{1,2,3, \ldots, n-1\}$, where $n \geq 3$, is colored with one of two colors, say red or black, so that: (i) $i$ and $n-i$ always receive the same color, and (ii) for some $j \in N$, relatively prime to $n, i$ and $|j-i|$ receive the same color for all $i \in N, i \neq j$. Prove that all numbers in $N$ must receive the same color.

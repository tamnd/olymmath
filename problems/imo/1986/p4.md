(CZS 3) Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $p$ be a prime number, $p>3$. Find at least $3(n+1)$ [easier version: $2(n+1)]$ sequences of positive integers $x, y, z$ satisfying $$ x y z=p^{n}(x+y+z) $$ that do not differ only by permutation.

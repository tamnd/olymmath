(GRE 4) Show that if $a, b, c$ are the lengths of the sides of a triangle and if $2 S=a+b+c$, then $$ \frac{a^{n}}{b+c}+\frac{b^{n}}{c+a}+\frac{c^{n}}{a+b} \geq\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n-2} S^{n-1}, n \geq 1 $$

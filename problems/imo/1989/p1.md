Let $A A_{1}$ meet the lines that bisect the two external angles at $B$ and $C$ in point $A^{0}$. Define $B^{0}$ and $C^{0}$ similarly. If $S_{X_{1} X_{2} \ldots X_{n}}$ denotes the area of the polygon $X_{1} X_{2} \ldots X_{n}$, prove that $$ S_{A^{0} B^{0} C^{0}}=2 S_{A C_{1} B A_{1} C B_{1}} \geq 4 S_{A B C} . $$

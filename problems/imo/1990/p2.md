(CAN 1) Given $n$ countries with three representatives each, $m$ committees $A(1), A(2), \ldots A(m)$ are called $a$ cycle if (i) each committee has $n$ members, one from each country; (ii) no two committees have the same membership; (iii) for $i=1,2, \ldots, m$, committee $A(i)$ and committee $A(i+1)$ have no member in common, where $A(m+1)$ denotes $A(1)$; (iv) if $1<|i-j|<m-1$, then committees $A(i)$ and $A(j)$ have at least one member in common. Is it possible to have a cycle of 1990 committees with 11 countries?

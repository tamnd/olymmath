$(\mathbf{C Z S} 1)^{\mathrm{IMO} 2}$ On a circle, $2 n-1(n \geq 3)$ different points are given. Find the minimal natural number $N$ with the property that whenever $N$ of the given points are colored black, there exist two black points such that the interior of one of the corresponding arcs contains exactly $n$ of the given $2 n-1$ points.

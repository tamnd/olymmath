Let $ {\mathbb Q}^ +$ be the set of positive rational numbers. Construct a function $ f : {\mathbb Q}^ + \rightarrow {\mathbb Q}^ +$ such that
$$ f(xf(y)) = \frac {f(x)}{y}
$$
for all $ x$ , $ y$ in $ {\mathbb Q}^ +$ .

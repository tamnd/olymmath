(PHI 3) Let $A B C$ be any triangle and $P$ any point in its interior. Let $P_{1}, P_{2}$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to the two sides $A C$ and $B C$. Draw $A P$ and $B P$, and from $C$ drop perpendiculars to $A P$ and $B P$. Let $Q_{1}$ and $Q_{2}$ be the feet of these perpendiculars. Prove that the lines $Q_{1} P_{2}, Q_{2} P_{1}$, and $A B$ are concurrent.

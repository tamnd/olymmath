(PRK 1) Let $S$ be any point on the circumscribed circle of $\triangle P Q R$. Then the feet of the perpendiculars from $S$ to the three sides of the triangle lie on the same straight line. Denote this line by $l(S, P Q R)$. Suppose that the hexagon $A B C D E F$ is inscribed in a circle. Show that the four lines $l(A, B D F), l(B, A C E), l(D, A B F)$, and $l(E, A B C)$ intersect at one point if and only if $C D E F$ is a rectangle.

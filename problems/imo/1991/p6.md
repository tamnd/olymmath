(USS 4) ${ }^{\mathrm{IMO}}$ Prove for each triangle $A B C$ the inequality $$ \frac{1}{4}<\frac{I A \cdot I B \cdot I C}{l_{A} l_{B} l_{C}} \leq \frac{8}{27} $$ where $I$ is the incenter and $l_{A}, l_{B}, l_{C}$ are the lengths of the angle bisectors of $A B C$.

(CHN 1) Let $\mathbb{R}^{+}$be the set of all nonnegative real numbers. Given two positive real numbers $a$ and $b$, suppose that a mapping $f: \mathbb{R}^{+} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{+}$ satisfies the functional equation $$ f(f(x))+a f(x)=b(a+b) x $$ Prove that there exists a unique solution of this equation.

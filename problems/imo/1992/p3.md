(CHN 2) The diagonals of a quadrilateral $A B C D$ are perpendicular: $A C \perp B D$. Four squares, $A B E F, B C G H, C D I J, D A K L$, are erected externally on its sides. The intersection points of the pairs of straight lines $C L, D F ; D F, A H ; A H, B J ; B J, C L$ are denoted by $P_{1}, Q_{1}, R_{1}, S_{1}$, respectively, and the intersection points of the pairs of straight lines $A I, B K$; $B K, C E ; C E, D G ; D G, A I$ are denoted by $P_{2}, Q_{2}, R_{2}, S_{2}$, respectively. Prove that $P_{1} Q_{1} R_{1} S_{1} \cong P_{2} Q_{2} R_{2} S_{2}$.

Let S be a finite set of points in three-dimensional space. Let $S_x$, $S_y$, $S_z$ be the sets consisting of the orthogonal projections of the points of S onto the yz-plane, zx-plane, xy-plane respectively. Prove that: $|S|^2$ ≤ $|S_x$| $|S_y$| $|S_z$|, where |A| denotes the number of points in the set A. [The orthogonal projection of a point onto a plane is the foot of the perpendicular from the point to the plane.]

(CAN 2) Let triangle $A B C$ be such that its circumradius $R$ is equal to 1. Let $r$ be the inradius of $A B C$ and let $p$ be the inradius of the orthic triangle $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ of triangle $A B C$. Prove that $p \leq 1-\frac{1}{3}(1+r)^{2}$. Remark. The orthic triangle is the triangle whose vertices are the feet of the altitudes of $A B C$.

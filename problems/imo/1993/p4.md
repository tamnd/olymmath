(SPA 2) In the triangle $A B C$, let $D, E$ be points on the side $B C$ such that $\angle B A D=\angle C A E$. If $M, N$ are, respectively, the points of tangency with $B C$ of the incircles of the triangles $A B D$ and $A C E$, show that $$ \frac{1}{M B}+\frac{1}{M D}=\frac{1}{N C}+\frac{1}{N E} $$

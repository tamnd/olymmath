Let m and n be positive integers. Let $a_1$, $a_2$, ... , $a_m$ be distinct elements of {1, 2, ... , n} such that whenever $a_i$ + $a_j$ ≤ n for some i, j (possibly the same) we have $a_i$ + $a_j$ = $a_k$ for some k. Prove that: ($a_1$ + ... + $a_m$)/m ≥ (n + 1)/2.

A5 (POL) Let $f(x)=\frac{x^{2}+1}{2 x}$ for $x \neq 0$. Define $f^{(0)}(x)=x$ and $f^{(n)}(x)=$ $f\left(f^{(n-1)}(x)\right)$ for all positive integers $n$ and $x \neq 0$. Prove that for all nonnegative integers $n$ and $x \neq-1,0$, or 1 , $$ \frac{f^{(n)}(x)}{f^{(n+1)}(x)}=1+\frac{1}{f\left(\left(\frac{x+1}{x-1}\right)^{2^{n}}\right)} $$

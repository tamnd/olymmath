Let $ a$ , $ b$ , $ c$ be positive real numbers such that $ abc = 1$ . Prove that
$$ \frac {1}{a^{3}\left(b + c\right)} + \frac {1}{b^{3}\left(c + a\right)} + \frac {1}{c^{3}\left(a + b\right)}\geq \frac {3}{2}.
$$

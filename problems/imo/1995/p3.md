Determine all integers n > 3 for which there exist n points $A_1$, ... , $A_n$ in the plane, no three collinear, and real numbers $r_1$, ... , $r_n$ such that for any distinct i, j, k, the area of the triangle $A_iA_jA_k$ is $r_i$ + $r_j$ + $r_k$.

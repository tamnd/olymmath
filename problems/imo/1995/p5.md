Let ABCDEF be a convex hexagon with AB = BC = CD and DE = EF = FA, such that ∠BCD = ∠EFA = $60^o$. Suppose that G and H are points in the interior of the hexagon such that ∠AGB = ∠DHE = $120^o$. Prove that AG + GB + GH + DH + HE ≥ CF.

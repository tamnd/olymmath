A6 (JAP) Let $n$ be an integer, $n \geq 3$. Let $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n}$ be real numbers such that $x_{i}<x_{i+1}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-1$. Prove that $$ \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \sum_{i<j} x_{i} x_{j}>\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(n-i) x_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{j=2}^{n}(j-1) x_{j}\right) . $$

Let $\mathbb{N}_0$ denote the set of nonnegative integers. Find all functions $f$ from $\mathbb{N}_0$ to itself such that

$$
f(m + f(n)) = f(f(m)) + f(n) \qquad \text{for all } m, n \in \mathbb{N}_0.
$$

A3 (GRE) Let $a>2$ be given, and define recursively $$ a_{0}=1, a_{1}=a, a_{n+1}=\left(\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{a_{n-1}^{2}}-2\right) a_{n} $$ Show that for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$, we have $$ \frac{1}{a_{0}}+\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{k}}<\frac{1}{2}\left(2+a-\sqrt{a^{2}-4}\right) . $$

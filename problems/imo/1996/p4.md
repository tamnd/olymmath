A4 (KOR) Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be nonnegative real numbers, not all zero. (a) Prove that $x^{n}-a_{1} x^{n-1}-\cdots-a_{n-1} x-a_{n}=0$ has precisely one positive real root. (b) Let $A=\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}, B=\sum_{j=1}^{n} j a_{j}$, and let $R$ be the positive real root of the equation in part (a). Prove that $$ A^{A} \leq R^{B} $$

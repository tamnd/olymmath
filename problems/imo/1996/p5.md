Let ABCDEF be a convex hexagon such that AB is parallel to DE, BC is parallel to EF, and CD is parallel to FA. Let $R_A$, $R_C$, $R_E$ denote the circumradii of triangles FAB, BCD, DEF respectively, and let p denote the perimeter of the hexagon. Prove that: $R_A$ + $R_C$ + $R_E$ ≥ p/2.

Let p, q, n be three positive integers with p + q < n. Let $x_0$, $x_1$, ... , $x_n$ be integers such that $x_0$ = $x_n$ = 0, and for each 1 ≤ i ≤ n, $x_i$ - $x_{i-1}$ = p or -q. Show that there exist indices i < j with (i, j) not (0, n) such that $x_i$ = $x_j$.

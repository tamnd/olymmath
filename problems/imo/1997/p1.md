(BLR) $)^{\mathrm{IMO}} \mathrm{An}$ infinite square grid is colored in the chessboard pattern. For any pair of positive integers $m, n$ consider a right-angled triangle whose vertices are grid points and whose legs, of lengths $m$ and $n$, run along the lines of the grid. Let $S_{b}$ be the total area of the black part of the triangle and $S_{w}$ the total area of its white part. Define the function $f(m, n)=\left|S_{b}-S_{w}\right|$. (a) Calculate $f(m, n)$ for all $m, n$ that have the same parity. (b) Prove that $f(m, n) \leq \frac{1}{2} \max (m, n)$. (c) Show that $f(m, n)$ is not bounded from above.

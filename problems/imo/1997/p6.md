(IRE) (a) Let $n$ be a positive integer. Prove that there exist distinct positive integers $x, y, z$ such that $$ x^{n-1}+y^{n}=z^{n+1} . $$ (b) Let $a, b, c$ be positive integers such that $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime and $c$ is relatively prime either to $a$ or to $b$. Prove that there exist infinitely many triples $(x, y, z)$ of distinct positive integers $x, y, z$ such that $$ x^{a}+y^{b}=z^{c} . $$ Original formulation: Let $a, b, c, n$ be positive integers such that $n$ is odd and $a c$ is relatively prime to $2 b$. Prove that there exist distinct positive integers $x, y, z$ such that (i) $x^{a}+y^{b}=z^{c}$, and (ii) $x y z$ is relatively prime to $n$.

(POL) Let $A B C D$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Let $E$ and $F$ be variable points on the sides $A B$ and $C D$, respectively, such that $A E: E B=C F$ : $F D$. Let $P$ be the point on the segment $E F$ such that $P E: P F=A B$ : $C D$. Prove that the ratio between the areas of triangles $A P D$ and $B P C$ does not depend on the choice of $E$ and $F$.

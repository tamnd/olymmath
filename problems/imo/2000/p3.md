C3 (COL) Let $n \geq 4$ be a fixed positive integer. Given a set $S=$ $\left\{P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{n}\right\}$ of points in the plane such that no three are collinear and no four concyclic, let $a_{t}, 1 \leq t \leq n$, be the number of circles $P_{i} P_{j} P_{k}$ that contain $P_{t}$ in their interior, and let $$ m(S)=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n} $$ Prove that there exists a positive integer $f(n)$, depending only on $n$, such that the points of $S$ are the vertices of a convex polygon if and only if $m(S)=f(n)$.

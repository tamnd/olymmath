A5 (BUL) Find all positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ such that $$ \frac{99}{100}=\frac{a_{0}}{a_{1}}+\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n}} $$ where $a_{0}=1$ and $\left(a_{k+1}-1\right) a_{k-1} \geq a_{k}^{2}\left(a_{k}-1\right)$ for $k=1,2, \ldots, n-1$.

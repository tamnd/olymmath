N5 (IRN) Let $m, n \geq 2$ be positive integers, and let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be integers, none of which is a multiple of $m^{n-1}$. Show that there exist integers $e_{1}, e_{2}, \ldots, e_{n}$, not all zero, with $\left|e_{i}\right|<m$ for all $i$, such that $e_{1} a_{1}+e_{2} a_{2}+\cdots+e_{n} a_{n}$ is a multiple of $m^{n}$.

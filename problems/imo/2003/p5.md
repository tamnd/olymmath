Given n > 2 and reals $x_1$ ≤ $x_2$ ≤ ... ≤ $x_n$, show that (∑$_{i,j}$ $|x_i$ - $x_j$| )$^2$ ≤ (2/3) ($n^2$ - 1) ∑$_{i,j}$ ($x_i$ - $x_j$)$^2$. Show that we have equality iff the sequence is an arithmetic progression.

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with circumcenter $o$ . The points $P$ and $Q$ are interior points of the sides $C A$ and $A B$ respectively. Let $K$ , $L$ , $M$ be the midpoints of $\overline{{B P}}$ $\overline{{C Q}}$ , $\overline{{P Q}}$ . Suppose that $\overline{{P Q}}$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle K L M$ . Prove that $O P = O Q$ .

Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{Z}_{>0}\to \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ such that for positive integers $a$ and $b$ , the numbers

$$a, f(b), f(b + f(a) - 1)$$

are the sides of a non-degenerate triangle.

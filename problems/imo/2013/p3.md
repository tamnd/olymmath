Let the excircle of triangle $A B C$ opposite the vertex $A$ be tangent to the side $B C$ at the point $A_{1}$ . Define the points $B_{1}$ on $C A$ and $C_{1}$ on $A B$ analogously, using the excircles opposite $B$ and $C$ , respectively. Suppose that the circumcenter of triangle $A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $A B C$ . Prove that triangle $A B C$ is right-angled.

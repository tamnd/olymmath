Let $A B C$ be an acute triangle with orthocenter $H$ , and let $W$ be a point on the side $\overline{{B C}}$ , between $B$ and $C$ . The points $M$ and $N$ are the feet of the altitudes drawn from $B$ and $C$ , respectively. Suppose $\omega_{1}$ is the circumcircle of triangle $B W N$ and $X$ is a point such that $\overline{{W X}}$ is a diameter of $\omega_{1}$ . Similarly, $\omega_{2}$ is the circumcircle of triangle $C W M$ and $Y$ is a point such that $\overline{{W Y}}$ is a diameter of $\omega_{2}$ . Show that the points $X$ , $Y$ , and $H$ are collinear.

Let $a_{0}< a_{1}< a_{2}< \dots$ be an infinite sequence of positive integers. Prove that there exists a unique integer $n\geq 1$ such that

$$a_{n}< \frac{a_{0} + a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{n}}{n}\leq a_{n + 1}.$$

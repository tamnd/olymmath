Let $P$ and $Q$ be on segment $B C$ of an acute triangle $A B C$ such that $\angle P A B =$ $\angle B C A$ and $\angle C A Q = \angle A B C$ . Let $M$ and $N$ be points on $\overline{{A P}}$ and $\overline{{A Q}}$ , respectively, such that $P$ is the midpoint of $\overline{{A M}}$ and $Q$ is the midpoint of $\overline{{A N}}$ . Prove that $\overline{{B M}}$ and $\overline{{C N}}$ meet on the circumcircle of $\triangle A B C$

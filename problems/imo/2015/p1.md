We say that a finite set $S$ of points in the plane is balanced if, for any two different points $A$ and $B$ in $S$ , there is a point $C$ in $S$ such that $AC = BC$ . We say that $S$ is center-free if for any three different points $A$ , $B$ and $C$ in $S$ , there are no points $P$ in $S$ such that $PA = PB = PC$ .

(a) Show that for all integers $n \geq 3$ , there exists a balanced set consisting of $n$ points.
(b) Determine all integers $n \geq 3$ for which there exists a balanced center-free set consisting of $n$ points.

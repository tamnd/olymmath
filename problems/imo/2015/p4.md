Triangle $ABC$ has circumcircle $\Omega$ and circumcenter $O$ . A circle $\Gamma$ with center $A$ intersects the segment $BC$ at points $D$ and $E$ , such that $B$ , $D$ , $E$ , and $C$ are all different and lie on line $BC$ in this order. Let $F$ and $G$ be the points of intersection of $\Gamma$ and $\Omega$ , such that $A$ , $F$ , $B$ , $C$ , and $G$ lie on $\Omega$ in this order. Let $K = (BDF) \cap \overline{AB} \neq B$ and $L = (CGE) \cap \overline{AC} \neq C$ and assume these points do not lie on line $FG$ . Define $X = \overline{FK} \cap \overline{GL}$ . Prove that $X$ lies on the line $AO$ .

In convex pentagon $A B C D E$ with $\angle B > 90^{\circ}$ , let $F$ be a point on $\overline{{A C}}$ such that $\angle F B C = 90^{\circ}$ . It is given that $F A = F B$ $D A = D C$ $E A = E D$ , and rays $\overline{{A C}}$ and $\overline{{A D}}$ trisect $\angle B A E$ . Let $M$ be the midpoint of $\overline{{C F}}$ . Let $X$ be the point such that $A M X E$ is a parallelogram. Show that $\overline{{F X}}$ $\overline{{E M}}$ $\overline{{B D}}$ are concurrent.

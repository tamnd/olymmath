Find all integers $n \geq 3$ for which there exist real numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots a_{n + 2}$ satisfying $a_{n + 1} = a_1$ , $a_{n + 2} = a_2$ and $$ a_ia_{i + 1} + 1 = a_{i + 2}, $$ for $i = 1, 2, \dots, n$ .

A site is any point $(x,y)$ in the plane for which $x,y\in \{1,\ldots ,20\}$ . Initially, each of the 400 sites is unoccupied. Any and Ben take turns placing stones on unoccupied sites, with Amy going first; Amy has the additional restriction that no two of her stones may be at a distance equal to $\sqrt{5}$ . They stop once either player cannot move. Find the greatest $K$ such that Amy can ensure that she places at least $K$ stones.

Let $a_{1}$ , $a_{2}$ , ... be an infinite sequence of positive integers, and $N$ a positive integer. Suppose that for all integers $n\geq N$ , the expression

$$\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}} +\frac{a_{2}}{a_{3}} +\dots +\frac{a_{n - 1}}{a_{n}} +\frac{a_{n}}{a_{1}}$$

is an integer. Prove that $(a_{n})$ is eventually constant.

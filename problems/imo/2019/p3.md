A social network has 2019 users, some pairs of which are friends (friendship is symmetric). If $A$ , $B$ , $C$ are three users such that $A B$ are friends and $A C$ are friends but $B C$ is not, then the administrator may perform the following operation: change the friendships such that $B C$ are friends, but $A B$ and $A C$ are no longer friends.

Initially, 1009 users have 1010 friends and 1010 users have 1009 friends. Prove that the administrator can make a sequence of operations such that all users have at most 1 friend.

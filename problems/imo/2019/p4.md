Solve over positive integers the equation

$$k! = \prod_{i = 0}^{n - 1}(2^{n} - 2^{i}) = (2^{n} - 1)(2^{n} - 2)(2^{n} - 4)\dots (2^{n} - 2^{n - 1}).$$

Let $A B C$ be a triangle with incenter $I$ and incircle $\omega$ . Let $D$ , $E$ , $F$ denote the tangency points of $\omega$ with $\overline{{B C}}$ , $\overline{{C A}}$ , $\overline{{A B}}$ . The line through $D$ perpendicular to $\overline{{E F}}$ meets $\omega$ again at $R$ (other than $D$ ), and line $A R$ meets $\omega$ again at $P$ (other than $R$ ). Suppose the circumcircles of $\triangle P C E$ and $\triangle P B F$ meet again at $Q$ (other than $P$ ). Prove that lines $D I$ and $P Q$ meet on the external $\angle A$ -bisector.

Show that the inequality

$$\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\sum_{j = 1}^{n}\sqrt{|x_{i} - x_{j}|}\leq \sum_{i = 1}^{n}\sum_{j = 1}^{n}\sqrt{|x_{i} + x_{j}|}$$

holds for all real numbers $x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots , x_{n}$ .

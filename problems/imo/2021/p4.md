Let $\Gamma$ be a circle with center $I$ , and $ABCD$ a convex quadrilateral such that each of the segments $AB$ , $BC$ , $CD$ and $DA$ is tangent to $\Gamma$ . Let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of the triangle $AIC$ . The extension of $BA$ beyond $A$ meets $\Omega$ at $X$ , and the extension of $BC$ beyond $C$ meets $\Omega$ at $Z$ . The extensions of $AD$ and $CD$ beyond $D$ meet $\Omega$ at $Y$ and $T$ , respectively. Prove that

$$AD + DT + TX + XA = CD + DY + YZ + ZC.$$

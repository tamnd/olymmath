Let $A B C D E$ be a convex pentagon such that $B C = D E$ . Assume that there is a point $T$ inside $A B C D E$ with $T B = T D$ $T C = T E$ and $\angle A B T = \angle T E A$ . Let line $A B$ intersect lines $C D$ and $C T$ at points $P$ and $Q$ , respectively. Assume that the points $P$ , $B$ , $A$ , $Q$ occur on their line in that order. Let line $A E$ intersect $C D$ and $D T$ at points $R$ and $S$ , respectively. Assume that the points $R$ , $E$ , $A$ , $S$ occur on their line in that order. Prove that the points $P$ , $S$ , $Q$ , $R$ lie on a circle.

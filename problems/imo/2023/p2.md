Let $A B C$ be an acute-angled triangle with $A B< A C$ . Let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of $A B C$ . Let $S$ be the midpoint of the arc $C B$ of $\Omega$ containing $A$ . The perpendicular from $A$ to $B C$ meets $B S$ at $D$ and meets $\Omega$ again at $E\neq A$ . The line through $D$ parallel to $B C$ meets line $B E$ at $L$ . Denote the circumcircle of triangle $B D L$ by $\omega$ . Let $\omega$ meet $\Omega$ again at $P\neq B$ . Prove that the line tangent to $\omega$ at $P$ meets line $B S$ on the internal angle bisector of $\angle B A C$ .

Given the equation
$$ x^4 + px^3 + qx^2 + rx + s = 0$$
has four real, positive roots, prove that
(a) $ pr - 16s \geq 0$
(b) $ q^2 - 36s \geq 0$
with equality in each case holding if and only if the four roots are equal.

Let $a, b, c$ be three real numbers such that $1 \geq a \geq b \geq c \geq 0$. Prove that if $\lambda$ is a root of the cubic equation $x^{3}+a x^{2}+b x+c=0$ (real or complex), then $|\lambda| \leq 1$.

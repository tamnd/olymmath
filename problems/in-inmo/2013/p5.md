In an acute triangle $A B C, O$ is the circumcenter, $H$ is the orthocenter and $G$ is the centroid. Let $O D$ be perpendicular to $B C$ and $H E$ be perpendicular to $C A$, with $D$ on $B C$ and $E$ on $C A$. Let $F$ be the midpoint of $A B$. Suppose the areas of triangles $O D C, H E A$ and $G F B$ are equal. Find all the possible values of $\widehat{C}$.

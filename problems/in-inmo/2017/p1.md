In the given figure, $A B C D$ is a square paper. It is folded along $E F$ such that $A$ goes to a point $A^{\prime} \neq C, B$ on the side $B C$ and $D$ goes to $D^{\prime}$. The line $A^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ cuts $C D$ in $G$. Show that the inradius of the triangle $G C A^{\prime}$ is the sum of the inradii of the triangles $G D^{\prime} F$ and $A^{\prime} B E$.

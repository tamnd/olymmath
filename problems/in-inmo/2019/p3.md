Let $m, n$ be distinct positive integers. Prove that

$$
\operatorname{gcd}(m, n)+\operatorname{gcd}(m+1, n+1)+\operatorname{gcd}(m+2, n+2) \leq 2|m-n|+1
$$

Further, determine when equality holds.

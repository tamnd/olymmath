Let $p$ be an odd prime number and $a, b, c$ be integers so that the integers

$$
a^{2023}+b^{2023}, b^{2024}+c^{2024}, c^{2025}+a^{2025}
$$

are all divisible by $p$. Prove that $p$ divides each of $a, b$, and $c$.

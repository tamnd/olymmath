Find the number of $10$ -tuples $(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_9,a_{10})$ of integers such that $|a_1|\leq 1$ and
$$a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2+\cdots+a_{10}^2-a_1a_2-a_2a_3-a_3a_4-\cdots-a_9a_{10}-a_{10}a_1=2.$$

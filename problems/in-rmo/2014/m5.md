Let $a,b,c$ be positive real numbers such that
$$ \cfrac{1}{1+a}+\cfrac{1}{1+b}+\cfrac{1}{1+c}\le 1. $$
Prove that $(1+a^2)(1+b^2)(1+c^2)\ge 125$ . When does equality hold?

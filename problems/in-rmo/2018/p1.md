Let $ABC$ be a triangle with integer sides in which $AB<AC$ . Let the tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ at $A$ intersect the line $BC$ at $D$ . Suppose $AD$ is also an integer. Prove that $\gcd(AB,AC)>1$ .

Each vertex of an $m\times n$ grid is colored blue, green or red in such a way that all the boundary vertices are red. We say that a unit square of the grid is properly colored if: $(i)$ all the three colors occur at the vertices of the square, and $(ii)$ one side of the square has the endpoints of the same color.
Show that the number of properly colored squares is even.

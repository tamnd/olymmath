Let $a,b$ be integers with $0<a<b$ . A set $\{x,y,z\}$ of non-negative integers is *olympic* if $x<y<z$ and if $\{z-y,y-x\}=\{a,b\}$ . Show that the set of all non-negative integers is the union of pairwise disjoint olympic sets.

Let $ABC$ and $PQR$ be two triangles such that
(a) $P$ is the mid-point of $BC$ and $A$ is the midpoint of $QR$.

(b) $QR$ bisects $\angle BAC$ and $BC$ bisects $\angle QPR$

Prove that $AB+AC=PQ+PR$.

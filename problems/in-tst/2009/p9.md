Let
$ f(x)=\sum_{k=1}^n a_k x^k$ and $ g(x)=\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{a_k x^k}{2^k -1}$ be two polynomials with real coefficients.
Let g(x) have $ 0,2^{n+1}$ as two of its roots. Prove That $ f(x)$ has a positive root less than $ 2^n$ .

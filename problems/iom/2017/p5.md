Let $x $ and $y $ be positive integers such that $[x+2,y+2]-[x+1,y+1]=[x+1,y+1]-[x,y]$ .Prove that one of the two numbers $x $ and $y $ divide the other.

(Here $[a,b] $ denote the least common multiple of $a $ and $b $ ).

We have finite number of distinct shapes in plane. A "*convex Kearting*" of these shapes is covering plane with convex sets, that each set consists exactly one of the shapes, and sets intersect at most in border.
![Image](http://aycu30.webshots.com/image/4109/2003791140004582959_th.jpg)
In which case Convex kearting is possible?
1) Finite distinct points
2) Finite distinct segments
3) Finite distinct circles

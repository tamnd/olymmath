Suppose that $ p$ is a prime number. Prove that for each $ k$, there exists an $ n$ such that:
$$ \left(\begin{array}{c}n\\ \hline p\end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}{c}n+k\\ \hline p\end{array}\right)$$

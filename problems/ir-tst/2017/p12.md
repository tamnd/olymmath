Let $k>1$ be an integer. The sequence $a_1,a_2, \cdots$ is defined as: $a_1=1, a_2=k$ and for all $n>1$ we have: $a_{n+1}-(k+1)a_n+a_{n-1}=0$ Find all positive integers $n$ such that $a_n$ is a power of $k$ .

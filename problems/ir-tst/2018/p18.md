Consider quadrilateral $ABCD $ inscribed in circle $\omega $ . $P\equiv AC\cap BD$ . $E$ , $F$ lie on sides $AB$ , $CD$ respectively such that $\hat {APE}=\hat {DPF} $ . Circles $\omega_1$ , $\omega_2$ are tangent to $\omega$ at $X $ , $Y $ respectively and also both tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle PEF $ at $P $ . Prove that: $$ \frac {EX}{EY}=\frac {FX}{FY} $$ *Proposed by Ali Zamani*

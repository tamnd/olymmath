We call a number $n$ *interesting*if for each permutation $\sigma$ of $1,2,\ldots,n$ there exist polynomials $P_1,P_2,\ldots ,P_n$ and $\epsilon > 0$ such that: $i)$ $P_1(0)=P_2(0)=\ldots =P_n(0)$ $ii)$ $P_1(x)>P_2(x)>\ldots >P_n(x)$ for $-\epsilon<x<0$ $iii)$ $P_{\sigma (1)} (x)>P_{\sigma (2)}(x)> \ldots >P_{\sigma (n)} (x) $ for $0<x<\epsilon$ Find all *interesting* $n$ .

Given $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ prove that there exist infinite pairs of distinct natural numbers such that
\begin{align*}
n+s(2n)=m+s(2m)
kn+s(n^2)=km+s(m^2).
\end{align*}
( $s(n)$ denotes the sum of digits of $n$ .)

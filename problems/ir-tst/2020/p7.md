We call a monic polynomial $P(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ *square-free mod n* if there <u>dose not</u> exist polynomials $Q(x),R(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ with $Q$ being non-constant and $P(x) \equiv Q(x)^2 R(x) \mod n$ . Given a prime $p$ and integer $m \geq 2$ . Find the number of monic *square-free mod p* $P(x)$ with degree $m$ and coeeficients in $\{0,1,2,3,...,p-1\}$ .

*Proposed by Masud Shafaie*

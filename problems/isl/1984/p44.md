Let $a,b,c$ be positive numbers with $\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c}= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
Prove that the system of equations
$$\sqrt{y-a}+\sqrt{z-a}=1$$
$$\sqrt{z-b}+\sqrt{x-b}=1$$
$$\sqrt{x-c}+\sqrt{y-c}=1$$
has exactly one solution $(x,y,z)$ in real numbers.

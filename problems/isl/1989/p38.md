A sequence of real numbers $ x_0, x_1, x_2, \ldots$ is defined as follows: $ x_0 = 1989$ and for each $ n \geq 1$

$$ x_n = - \frac{1989}{n} \sum^{n-1}_{k=0} x_k.$$

Calculate the value of $ \sum^{1989}_{n=0} 2^n x_n.$

The expressions $ a + b + c, ab + ac + bc,$ and $ abc$ are called the elementary symmetric expressions on the three letters $ a, b, c;$ symmetric because if we interchange any two letters, say $ a$ and $ c,$ the expressions remain algebraically the same. The common degree of its terms is called the order of the expression. Let $ S_k(n)$ denote the elementary expression on $ k$ different letters of order $ n;$ for example $ S_4(3) = abc + abd + acd + bcd.$ There are four terms in $ S_4(3).$ How many terms are there in $ S_{9891}(1989)?$ (Assume that we have $ 9891$ different letters.)

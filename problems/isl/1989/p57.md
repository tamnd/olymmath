Let $ v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_{1989}$ be a set of coplanar vectors with $ |v_r| \leq 1$ for $ 1 \leq r \leq 1989.$ Show that it is possible to find $ \epsilon_r$ , $1 \leq r \leq 1989,$ each equal to $ \pm 1,$ such that $$ \left | \sum^{1989}_{r=1} \epsilon_r v_r \right | \leq \sqrt{3}.$$

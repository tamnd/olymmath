Let $ n$ be a positive integer, $ X = \{1, 2, \ldots , n\},$ and $ k$ a positive integer such that $ \frac{n}{2} \leq k \leq n.$ Determine, with proof, the number of all functions $ f : X \mapsto X$ that satisfy the following conditions:

**(i)** $ f^2 = f;$
**(ii)** the number of elements in the image of $ f$ is $ k;$
**(iii)** for each $ y$ in the image of $ f,$ the number of all points $ x \in X$ such that $ f(x)=y$ is at most $ 2.$

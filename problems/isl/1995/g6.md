$$ GA_1 \cdot GA_2 \cdot GA_3 \cdot GA_ \cdot4 \leq GA'_1 \cdot GA'_2 \cdot GA'_3 \cdot GA'_4$$

and

$$ \frac{1}{GA'_1} + \frac{1}{GA'_2} + \frac{1}{GA'_3} + \frac{1}{GA'_4} \leq \frac{1}{GA_1} + \frac{1}{GA_2} + \frac{1}{GA_3} + \frac{1}{GA_4}.$$

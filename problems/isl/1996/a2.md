Let $ a_1 \geq a_2 \geq \ldots \geq a_n$ be real numbers such that for all integers $ k > 0,$ $$ a^k_1 + a^k_2 + \ldots + a^k_n \geq 0.$$

Let $ p =\max\{|a_1|, \ldots, |a_n|\}.$ Prove that $ p = a_1$ and that

$$ (x - a_1) \cdot (x - a_2) \cdots (x - a_n) \leq x^n - a^n_1$$ for all $ x > a_1.$

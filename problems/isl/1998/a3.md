Let $x,y$ and $z$ be positive real numbers such that $xyz=1$ . Prove that

$$
\frac{x^{3}}{(1 + y)(1 + z)}+\frac{y^{3}}{(1 + z)(1 + x)}+\frac{z^{3}}{(1 + x)(1 + y)}
\geq \frac{3}{4}.
$$

Let $ M$ and $ N$ be two points inside triangle $ ABC$ such that
$$ \angle MAB = \angle NAC \text{and} \angle MBA = \angle NBC.
$$

Prove that
$$ \frac {AM \cdot AN}{AB \cdot AC} + \frac {BM \cdot BN}{BA \cdot BC} + \frac {CM \cdot CN}{CA \cdot CB} = 1.
$$

Let $O$ be the circumcenter and $H$ the orthocenter of an acute triangle $ABC$. Show that there exist points $D$, $E$, and $F$ on sides $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ respectively such that
$$ OD + DH = OE + EH = OF + FH$$
and the lines $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ are concurrent.

The sequence $a_0$ , $a_1$ , $a_2,$ $\ldots$ is defined as follows: $$a_0=2, a_{k+1}=2a_k^2-1 \text{for }k \geq 0.$$ Prove that if an odd prime $p$ divides $a_n$ , then $2^{n+3}$ divides $p^2-1$ .

Let $k$ be a fixed integer greater than 1, and let ${m=4k^2-5}$. Show that there exist positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that the sequence $(x_n)$ defined by
$$x_0=a, x_1=b, x_{n+2}=x_{n+1}+x_n \text{for} n=0,1,2,\dots,$$
has all of its terms relatively prime to $m$.

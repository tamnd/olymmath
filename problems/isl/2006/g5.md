In triangle $A B C$, let $J$ be the centre of the excircle tangent to side $B C$ at $A_{1}$ and to the extensions of sides $A C$ and $A B$ at $B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, respectively. Suppose that the lines $A_{1} B_{1}$ and $A B$ are perpendicular and intersect at $D$. Let $E$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C_{1}$ to line $D J$. Determine the angles $\angle B E A_{1}$ and $\angle A E B_{1}$. (Greece)

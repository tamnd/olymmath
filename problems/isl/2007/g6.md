Determine the smallest positive real number $k$ with the following property. Let $A B C D$ be a convex quadrilateral, and let points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ and $D_{1}$ lie on sides $A B, B C$, $C D$ and $D A$, respectively. Consider the areas of triangles $A A_{1} D_{1}, B B_{1} A_{1}, C C_{1} B_{1}$, and $D D_{1} C_{1}$; let $S$ be the sum of the two smallest ones, and let $S_{1}$ be the area of quadrilateral $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} D_{1}$. Then we always have $k S_{1} \geq S$. (U.S.A.)

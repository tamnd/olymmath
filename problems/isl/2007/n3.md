Let $X$ be a set of 10000 integers, none of them is divisible by 47 . Prove that there exists a 2007-element subset $Y$ of $X$ such that $a-b+c-d+e$ is not divisible by 47 for any $a, b, c, d, e \in Y$. (Netherlands)

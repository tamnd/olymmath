Let $a, b, c, d$ be positive real numbers such that $$ a b c d=1 \text { and } a+b+c+d>\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{d}+\frac{d}{a} $$ Prove that $$ a+b+c+d<\frac{b}{a}+\frac{c}{b}+\frac{d}{c}+\frac{a}{d} $$

Let $n$ be a positive integer. Show that the numbers $$ \left(\begin{array}{c} 2^{n}-1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right), \left(\begin{array}{c} 2^{n}-1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right), \left(\begin{array}{c} 2^{n}-1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right), \ldots, \left(\begin{array}{c} 2^{n}-1 \\ 2^{n-1}-1 \end{array}\right) $$ are congruent modulo $2^{n}$ to $1,3,5, \ldots, 2^{n}-1$ in some order.

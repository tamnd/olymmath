A grasshopper jumps along the real axis. He starts at point 0 and makes 2009 jumps to the right with lengths $1,2, \ldots, 2009$ in an arbitrary order. Let $M$ be a set of 2008 positive integers less than 1005 - 2009. Prove that the grasshopper can arrange his jumps in such a way that he never lands on a point from $M$. Variant 2. Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer. A grasshopper jumps along the real axis. He starts at point 0 and makes $n+1$ jumps to the right with pairwise different positive integral lengths $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n+1}$ in an arbitrary order. Let $M$ be a set of $n$ positive integers in the interval $(0, s)$, where $s=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n+1}$. Prove that the grasshopper can arrange his jumps in such a way that he never lands on a point from $M$.

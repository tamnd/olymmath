Let $A B C$ be a triangle. The incircle of $A B C$ touches the sides $A B$ and $A C$ at the points $Z$ and $Y$, respectively. Let $G$ be the point where the lines $B Y$ and $C Z$ meet, and let $R$ and $S$ be points such that the two quadrilaterals $B C Y R$ and $B C S Z$ are parallelograms. Prove that $G R=G S$.

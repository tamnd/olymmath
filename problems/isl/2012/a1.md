Find all the functions $f: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ such that $$ f(a)^{2}+f(b)^{2}+f(c)^{2}=2 f(a) f(b)+2 f(b) f(c)+2 f(c) f(a) $$ for all integers $a, b, c$ satisfying $a+b+c=0$.

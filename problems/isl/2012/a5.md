Find all functions $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ that satisfy the conditions $$ f(1+x y)-f(x+y)=f(x) f(y) \text { for all } x, y \in \mathbb{R} $$ and $f(-1) \neq 0$.

Prove that in any set of 2000 distinct real numbers there exist two pairs $a>b$ and $c>d$ with $a \neq c$ or $b \neq d$, such that $$ \left|\frac{a-b}{c-d}-1\right|<\frac{1}{100000} $$ (Lithuania)

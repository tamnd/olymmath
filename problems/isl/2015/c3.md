For a finite set $A$ of positive integers, we call a partition of $A$ into two disjoint nonempty subsets $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ good if the least common multiple of the elements in $A_{1}$ is equal to the greatest common divisor of the elements in $A_{2}$. Determine the minimum value of $n$ such that there exists a set of $n$ positive integers with exactly 2015 good partitions. (Ukraine)

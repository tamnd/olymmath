Determine all positive integers $M$ for which the sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, defined by $a_{0}=\frac{2 M+1}{2}$ and $a_{k+1}=a_{k}\left\lfloor a_{k}\right\rfloor$ for $k=0,1,2, \ldots$, contains at least one integer term. (Luxembourg)

Let $n$ be an odd positive integer. In the Cartesian plane, a cyclic polygon $P$ with area $S$ is chosen. All its vertices have integral coordinates, and all squares of its side lengths are divisible by $n$. Prove that $2 S$ is an integer divisible by $n$.

A hunter and an invisible rabbit play a game in the Euclidean plane. The hunter's starting point $H_{0}$ coincides with the rabbit's starting point $R_{0}$. In the $n^{\text {th }}$ round of the game ( $n \geqslant 1$ ), the following happens. (1) First the invisible rabbit moves secretly and unobserved from its current point $R_{n-1}$ to some new point $R_{n}$ with $R_{n-1} R_{n}=1$. (2) The hunter has a tracking device (e.g. dog) that returns an approximate position $R_{n}^{\prime}$ of the rabbit, so that $R_{n} R_{n}^{\prime} \leqslant 1$. (3) The hunter then visibly moves from point $H_{n-1}$ to a new point $H_{n}$ with $H_{n-1} H_{n}=1$. Is there a strategy for the hunter that guarantees that after $10^{9}$ such rounds the distance between the hunter and the rabbit is below 100 ? (Austria)

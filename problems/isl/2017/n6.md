Find the smallest positive integer $n$, or show that no such $n$ exists, with the following property: there are infinitely many distinct $n$-tuples of positive rational numbers ( $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ ) such that both $$ a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n} \text { and } \frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}} $$ are integers. (Singapore)

A point $T$ is chosen inside a triangle $A B C$. Let $A_{1}, B_{1}$, and $C_{1}$ be the reflections of $T$ in $B C, C A$, and $A B$, respectively. Let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of the triangle $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1}$. The lines $A_{1} T, B_{1} T$, and $C_{1} T$ meet $\Omega$ again at $A_{2}, B_{2}$, and $C_{2}$, respectively. Prove that the lines $A A_{2}, B B_{2}$, and $C C_{2}$ are concurrent on $\Omega$. (Mongolia)

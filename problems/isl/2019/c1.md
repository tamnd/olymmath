The infinite sequence $a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ of (not necessarily different) integers has the following properties: $0 \leqslant a_{i} \leqslant i$ for all integers $i \geqslant 0$, and $$ \binom{k}{a_{0}}+\binom{k}{a_{1}}+\cdots+\binom{k}{a_{k}}=2^{k} $$ for all integers $k \geqslant 0$. Prove that all integers $N \geqslant 0$ occur in the sequence (that is, for all $N \geqslant 0$, there exists $i \geqslant 0$ with $a_{i}=N$ ).

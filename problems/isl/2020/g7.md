Let $P$ be a point on the circumcircle of an acute-angled triangle $A B C$. Let $D$, $E$, and $F$ be the reflections of $P$ in the midlines of triangle $A B C$ parallel to $B C, C A$, and $A B$, respectively. Denote by $\omega_{A}, \omega_{B}$, and $\omega_{C}$ the circumcircles of triangles $A D P, B E P$, and $C F P$, respectively. Denote by $\omega$ the circumcircle of the triangle formed by the perpendicular bisectors of segments $A D, B E$ and $C F$. Show that $\omega_{A}, \omega_{B}, \omega_{C}$, and $\omega$ have a common point. (Denmark)

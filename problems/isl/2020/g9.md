Prove that there exists a positive constant $c$ such that the following statement is true: Assume that $n$ is an integer with $n \geqslant 2$, and let $\mathcal{S}$ be a set of $n$ points in the plane such that the distance between any two distinct points in $\mathcal{S}$ is at least 1 . Then there is a line $\ell$ separating $\mathcal{S}$ such that the distance from any point of $\mathcal{S}$ to $\ell$ is at least $c n^{-1 / 3}$. (A line $\ell$ separates a point set $\mathcal{S}$ if some segment joining two points in $\mathcal{S}$ crosses $\ell$.)

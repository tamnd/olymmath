Show that for all real numbers $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ the following inequality holds: $$ \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \sqrt{\left|x_{i}-x_{j}\right|} \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \sqrt{\left|x_{i}+x_{j}\right|} $$

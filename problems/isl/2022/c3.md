In each square of a garden shaped like a $2022 \times 2022$ board, there is initially a tree of height 0 . A gardener and a lumberjack alternate turns playing the following game, with the gardener taking the first turn: - The gardener chooses a square in the garden. Each tree on that square and all the surrounding squares (of which there are at most eight) then becomes one unit taller. - The lumberjack then chooses four different squares on the board. Each tree of positive height on those squares then becomes one unit shorter. We say that a tree is majestic if its height is at least $10^{6}$. Determine the largest number $K$ such that the gardener can ensure there are eventually $K$ majestic trees on the board, no matter how the lumberjack plays. (Colombia)

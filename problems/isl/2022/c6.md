Let $n$ be a positive integer. We start with $n$ piles of pebbles, each initially containing a single pebble. One can perform moves of the following form: choose two piles, take an equal number of pebbles from each pile and form a new pile out of these pebbles. For each positive integer $n$, find the smallest number of non-empty piles that one can obtain by performing a finite sequence of moves of this form. (Croatia)

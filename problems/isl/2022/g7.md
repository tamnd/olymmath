Let $A B C$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}$ be two triangles having the same circumcircle $\omega$, and the same orthocentre $H$. Let $\Omega$ be the circumcircle of the triangle determined by the lines $A A^{\prime}, B B^{\prime}$ and $C C^{\prime}$. Prove that $H$, the centre of $\omega$, and the centre of $\Omega$ are collinear. (Denmark)

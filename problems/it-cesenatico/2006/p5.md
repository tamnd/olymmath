$$(a_1+a_2+\dots+a_n)^2\ge 4(a_1a_2+a_2a_3+\cdots+a_na_1).$$
a) Find all $n\ge 3$ such that the inequality is true for positive reals.
b) Find all $n\ge 3$ such that the inequality is true for reals.

Let $X = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$ . We want to color, using $k$ colors, all subsets of $3$ elements of $X$ in such a way that, two disjoint subsets have distinct colors.
Prove that:
(a) $4$ colors are sufficient;
(b) $3$ colors are not sufficient.

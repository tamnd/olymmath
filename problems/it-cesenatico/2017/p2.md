Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer. Consider the solutions of the system $$ \begin{cases}
n=a+b-c
n=a^2+b^2-c^2
\end{cases} $$ where $a,b,c$ are integers. Show that there is at least one solution and that the solutions are finitely many.

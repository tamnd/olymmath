Let $A$ and $P$ are the points of intersection of the circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ with centers $O$ and $K$, respectively. Let also $B$ and $C$ be the symmetric points of $A$ with respect to $O$ and $K$, respectively. A line through $A$ intersects the circles $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ at the points $D$ and $E$, respectively. Prove that the centre of the circumcircle of the triangle $D E P$ lies on the circumcircle $O K P$.

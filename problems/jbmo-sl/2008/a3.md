Let the real parameter $p$ be such that the system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
p\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)=\left(p^{2}-1\right) x y \\
|x-1|+|y|=1
\end{array}\right.
$$

has at least three different real solutions. Find $p$ and solve the system for that $p$.

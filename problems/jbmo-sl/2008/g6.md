Let $A B C$ be a triangle with $A<90^{\circ}$. Outside of a triangle we consider isosceles triangles $A B E$ and $A C Z$ with bases $A B$ and $A C$, respectively. If the midpoint $D$ of the side $B C$ is such that $D E \perp D Z$ and $E Z=2 \cdot E D$, prove that $\widehat{A E B}=2 \cdot \widehat{A Z C}$.

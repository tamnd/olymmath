Real numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy

$$
0<x, y, z<1
$$

and

$$
x y z=(1-x)(1-y)(1-z) .
$$

Show that

$$
\frac{1}{4} \leq \max \{(1-x) y,(1-y) z,(1-z) x\}
$$

Let $A L$ and $B K$ be angle bisectors in the non-isosceles triangle $A B C(L \in B C, K \in A C)$. The perpendicular bisector of $B K$ intersects the line $A L$ at point $M$. Point $N$ lies on the line $B K$ such that $L N \| M K$. Prove that $L N=N A$.

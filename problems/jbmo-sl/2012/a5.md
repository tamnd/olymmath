Find the largest positive integer $n$ for which the inequality

$$
\frac{a+b+c}{a b c+1}+\sqrt[n]{a b c} \leq \frac{5}{2}
$$

holds for all $a, b, c \in[0,1]$. Here $\sqrt[1]{a b c}=a b c$.

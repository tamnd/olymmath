Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, and $P$ a point on the circumcircle of the triangle $A B C$ and distinct from $A, B$ and $C$. If the lines through $P$ and parallel to $B C, C A, A B$ intersect the lines $C A, A B, B C$ at $M, N$ and $Q$ respectively, prove that $M, N$ and $Q$ are collinear.

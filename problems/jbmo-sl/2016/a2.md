Given positive real numbers $a, b, c$, prove that

$$
\frac{8}{(a+b)^{2}+4 a b c}+\frac{8}{(a+c)^{2}+4 a b c}+\frac{8}{(b+c)^{2}+4 a b c}+a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2} \geq \frac{8}{a+3}+\frac{8}{b+3}+\frac{8}{c+3}
$$

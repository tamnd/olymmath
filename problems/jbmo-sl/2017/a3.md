Let $a, b, c, d$ be real numbers such that $0 \leq a \leq b \leq c \leq d$. Prove the inequality

$$
a b^{3}+b c^{3}+c d^{3}+d a^{3} \geq a^{2} b^{2}+b^{2} c^{2}+c^{2} d^{2}+d^{2} a^{2}
$$

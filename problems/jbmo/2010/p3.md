Let $A L$ and $B K$ be angle bisectors in the non-isosceles triangle $A B C$ ( $L$ lies on the side $B C, K$ lies on the side $A C$ ). The perpendicular bisector of $B K$ intersects the line $A L$ at point $M$. Point $N$ lies on the line $B K$ such that $L N$ is parallel to $M K$. Prove that $L N=N A$.

Let $n$ be an integer , $n \ge 2$. There are $n \times n$ squares, and an integer between $1$ and $n$ inclusive can be written in each square. An integer between $1$ and $n$ inclusive appears once in each row and column. At this time, find the maximum possible number of pairs of two squares that are diagonally adjacent to each other and have the same number written on them. However, when two sets of cells are said to be diagonally adjacent to each other, they do not share edges but share vertices.

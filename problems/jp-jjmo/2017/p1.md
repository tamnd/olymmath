Let $n$ be a positive integer. There are $n$ chairs lined up at equal intervals on the circumference of the circle, each of which has a person sitting on it. Each person moved clockwise around the circumference of the circle $0$ laps or more and less than $1$ lap and sat down on one chair. At this time, find the maximum number of different types of lengths that the person moved. However, it is not necessarily the case that people sit in different chairs before and after moving (if they are the same, the moved length is $0$), and it is assumed that exactly one person sits on each chair, even after the movement.

There are five points on the $xy$ plane, each of which has a different $x$ - coordinate and a different $y$ - coordinate. The area of each rectangle whose diagonal line connects $2$ of the $5$ points and whose sides are parallel to the $x$ - axis or $y$ - axis is greater than or equal to $1$. Let $M$ be the maximum area of these rectangles. Find the smallest possible value for $M$.

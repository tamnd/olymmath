Let $k\geq 2$ be an integer, and set $A=2^{k}-1$. First, write a positive odd number $l\geq A/2$ on the blackboard, and repeat the following operation $k-1$ times : If the previous integer is $n$, rewrite $n$ to the largest odd number that divides $A-n$ on the blackboard.
Show that $l$ will be written again.

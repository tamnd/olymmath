Let $n\geq 3$ be an odd integer. One plays a game with $n\times n$ cells.
・Firstly, pick exactly one cell and place a piece.
・Then select one of the adjacent cells (vertically, horizontally or diagonally) that has not been visited by the piece before, and moves the piece. Repeat this operation until the piece cannot be moved.
Find the minimum possible number of times a piece has moved vertically or horizontally (i.e. not diagonally) when it has visited all the cells.

Suppose that $ABC$ and $PQR$ are triangles such that $A,P$ are the midpoints of $QR,BC$ respectively, and $QR,BC$ are the bisectors of $\angle BAC,\angle QPR$. Prove that $AB+AC=PQ+PR$.

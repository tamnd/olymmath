Denote by $ S(n)$ the sum of decimal digits of a positive integer $ n$. Show that there exist $ 2002$ distinct positive integers $ n_1, n_2, \cdots, n_{2002}$ such that $ n_1 + S(n_1) = n_2 + S(n_2) = \cdots = n_{2002} + S(n_{2002})$.

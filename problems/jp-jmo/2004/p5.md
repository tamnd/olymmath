In a land any towns are connected by roads with exactly other three towns. Last year we made a trip starting from a town coming back to the town by visiting exactly one time all towns in land. This year we are plan to make a trip in the same way as last year's trip. Note that you can't take such order completely same as last year's one or trace the order only reversely. Prove that this is possible.

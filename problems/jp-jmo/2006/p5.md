For any positive real numbers $x_1,\ x_2,\ x_3,\ y_1,\ y_2,\ y_3,\ z_1,\ z_2,\ z_3,$ find the maximum value of real number $A$ such that if
$$ M = (x_1^3+x_2^3+x_3^3+1)(y_1^3+y_2^3+y_3^3+1)(z_1^3+z_2^3+z_3^3+1) $$
and
$$ N = A(x_1+y_1+z_1)(x_2+y_2+z_2)(x_3+y_3+z_3), $$
then $M \geq N$ always holds, then find the condition that the equality holds.

Find all functions $f,$ defined on the positive real numbers and taking real numbers such that
$$f(x)+f(y)\leq \frac{f(x+y)}{2},\ \ \frac{f(x)}{x}+\frac{f(y)}{y}\geq \frac{f(x+y)}{x+y}$$
for all $x,\ y>0.$

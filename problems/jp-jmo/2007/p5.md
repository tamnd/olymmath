For real positive numbers $x,$ the set $A(x)$ is defined by
$$A(x)=\{[nx]\ | \ n\in{ \mathbb{N}}\},$$
where $[r]$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding real numbers $r.$ F ind all irrational numbers $\alpha >1$ satisfying the following condition.
Condition: If positive real number $\beta$ satisfies $A(\alpha)\supset A(\beta),$ then $\frac{\beta}{\alpha}$ is integer.

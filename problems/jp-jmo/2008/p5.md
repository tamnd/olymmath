Can it be existed positive integers $ n$ such that there are integers $ b$ and non zero integers $ a_i\ (i=1,\ 2,\ \cdots n)$ for rational numbers $ r$ which satisfies $ r=b+\sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{a_i}?$

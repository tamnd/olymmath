Let $ x,\ y,\ z$ be positive real numbers.

Prove that
$$ \frac {1 + yz + zx}{(1 + x + y)^2} + \frac {1 + zx + xy}{(1 + y + z)^2} + \frac {1 + xy + yz}{(1 + z + x)^2}\geq 1$$

Let $n,\ k$ be positive integers with $n\geq k$. There are $n$ persons, each person belongs to exactly one of group $1$, group $2,\ \cdots$, group $k$ and more than or equal to one person belong to any groups. Show that $n^2$ sweets can be delivered to $n$ persons in such way that all of the following condition are satisfied.

$\bullet$ At least one sweet are delivered to each person.

$\bullet$ $a_i$ sweet are delivered to each person belonging to group $i\ (1\leq i\leq k).$

$\bullet$ If $1\leq i<j\leq k$, then $a_i>a_j.$

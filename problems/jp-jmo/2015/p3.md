A sequence $\{a_n\}_{n\geq 1}$ of positive integers is called ascending if $a_n$ satisfies $a_n<a_{n+1}$ and $a_{2n}=2a_n$.

(1) Let $\{a_n\}$ b e ascending. If $p$ is a prime greater than $a_1$, then prove that there exists a multiple of $p$ in the sequence.

(2) Let $p$ be an odd prime. Prove that there exists a sequence $\{a_n\}$ which is ascending and has no multiple of $p$.

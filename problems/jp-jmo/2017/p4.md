Let $n \geq 3$ be an integer. There are $n$ people, and a meeting which at least $3$ people attend is held everyday. Each attendant shake hands with the rest attendants at every meeting. After the $n$ t h meeting, every pair of the $n$ people shook hands exactly once. Prove that every meeting was attended by the same number of attendants.

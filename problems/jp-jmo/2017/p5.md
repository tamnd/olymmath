Let $x_{1}, x_{2},\cdots,x_{1000}$ be integers, and $ \sum_{i=1}^{1000} x_{i}^{k}$ are all multiples of $2017$ for any positive integers $k \leq 672$. Prove that $x_{1}, x_{2},\cdots,x_{1000}$ are all multiples of $2017$.
Note: $2017$ is a prime number.

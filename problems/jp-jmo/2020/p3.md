Find all functions $f:\mathbb{Z}^{+}\to\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ such that
$$m^{2}+f(n)^{2}+(m-f(n))^{2}\geq f(m)^{2}+n^{2}$$
for all pairs of positive integers $(m,n)$.

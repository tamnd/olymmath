Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer. $3n$ distinct points are plotted on the circle, where A and B perform the following operation : Firstly, $A$ picks exactly 2 points which haven't been connected yet and connects them by a segment. Secondly, B picks exactly 1 point with no piece and place a piece. Prove that, after consecutive $n$ operations, despite how B acts, A can make the number of segments no less than $ \frac{n-1}{6}$, which connect a point with a piece and a point with no piece.

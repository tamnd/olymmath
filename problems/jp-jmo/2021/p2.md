Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer. Players $A$ and $B$ play a game using $n\times 2021$ grid of square unit cells. Firstly, $A$ paints each cell either black of white. $B$ places a piece in one of the cells in the uppermost row, and designates one of the cells in the lowermost row as the goal. Then, $A$ repeats the following operation $n-1$ times:
・When the cell with the piece is painted white, $A$ moves the piece to the cell one below.
・Otherwise, $A$ moves the piece to the next cell on the left or right, and then to the cell one below.
Find the minimum possible value of $n$ such that $A$ can always move a piece to the goal, regardless of $B$ ' s choice.

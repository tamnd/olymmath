Given that $g(n) = \frac{1}{{2 + \frac{1}{{3 + \frac{1}{{... + \frac{1}{{n - 1}}}}}}}}$ and $k(n) = \frac{1}{{2 + \frac{1}{{3 + \frac{1}{{... + \frac{1}{{n - 1 + \frac{1}{n}}}}}}}}}$ , for natural $n$ . Prove that $\left| {g(n) - k(n)} \right| \le \frac{1}{{(n - 1)!n!}}$ .

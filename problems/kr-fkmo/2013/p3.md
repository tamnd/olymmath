For a positive integer $n \ge 2 $ , define set $ T = \{ (i,j) | 1 \le i < j \le n , i | j \} $ . For nonnegative real numbers $ x_1 , x_2 , \cdots , x_n $ with $ x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n = 1 $ , find the maximum value of
$$ \sum_{(i,j) \in T} x_i x_j $$
in terms of $n$ .

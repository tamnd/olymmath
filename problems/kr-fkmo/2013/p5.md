Two coprime positive integers $ a, b $ are given. Integer sequence $ \{ a_n \}, \{b_n \} $ satisties
$$ (a+b \sqrt2 )^{2n} = a_n + b_n \sqrt2 $$
Find all prime numbers $ p $ such that there exist positive integer $ n \le p $ satisfying $ p | b_n $ .

For any permutation $ f : \{ 1, 2, \cdots , n \} \to \{1, 2, \cdots , n \} $ , and define
$$ A = \{ i | i > f(i) \} $$
$$ B = \{ (i, j) | i<j \le f(j) < f(i) \ or \ f(j) < f(i) < i < j \} $$
$$ C = \{ (i, j) | i<j \le f(i) < f(j) \ or \ f(i) < f(j) < i < j \} $$
$$ D = \{ (i, j) | i< j \ and \ f(i) > f(j)\} $$
Prove that $ |A| + 2|B| + |C| = |D| $ .

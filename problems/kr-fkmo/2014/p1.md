Suppose $x$ , $y$ , $z$ are positive numbers such that $x+y+z=1$ . Prove that
$$
\frac{(1+xy+yz+zx)(1+3x^3 + 3y^3 + 3z^3)}{9(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}
\ge
\left(
\frac{x \sqrt{1+x} }{\sqrt[4]{3+9x^2}}
+ \frac{y \sqrt{1+y} }{\sqrt[4]{3+9y^2}}
+ \frac{z \sqrt{1+z}}{\sqrt[4]{3+9z^2}}
\right)^2. $$

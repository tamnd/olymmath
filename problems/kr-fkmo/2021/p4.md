There are $n$ ( $\ge 2$ ) clubs $A_1,A_2,...A_n$ in Korean Mathematical Society. Prove that there exist $n-1$ sets $B_1,B_2,...B_{n-1}$ that satisfy the condition below.
(1) $A_1\cup A_2\cup \cdots A_n=B_1\cup B_2\cup \cdots B_{n-1}$ (2) for any $1\le i<j\le n-1$ , $B_i\cap B_j=\emptyset, -1\le\left\vert B_i \right\vert -\left\vert B_j \right\vert\le 1$ (3) for any $1\le i \le n-1$ , there exist $A_k,A_j $ ( $1\le k\le j\le n$ )such that $B_i\subseteq A_k\cup A_j$

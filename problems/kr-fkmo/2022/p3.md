A function $g \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is given such that its range is a finite set. Find all functions $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ that satisfies $$ 2f(x+g(y))=f(2g(x)+y)+f(x+3g(y)) $$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ .

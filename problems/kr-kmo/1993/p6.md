The circle through $A, D, E$ meets $AC, AB$ again at $F, G$ respectively. Let $H\not = B$ be a point on $AB$ with $BG = GH$ . Prove that triangles $EBH$ and $ABC$ are similar and ﬁnd the ratio of their areas.

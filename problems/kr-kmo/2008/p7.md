Prove that the only function $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ satisfying the following is $f(x)=x$ .
(i) $\forall x \not= 0$ , $f(x) = x^2f(\frac{1}{x})$ .
(ii) $\forall x, y$ , $f(x+y) = f(x)+f(y)$ .
(iii) $f(1)=1$ .

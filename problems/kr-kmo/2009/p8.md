For a positive integer $n$ , define a function $ f_n (x) $ at an interval $ [ 0, n+1 ] $ as
$$ f_n (x) = ( \sum_{i=1} ^ {n} | x-i | )^2 - \sum_{i=1} ^{n} (x-i)^2 . $$
Let $ a_n $ be the minimum value of $f_n (x) $ . Find the value of
$$ \sum_{n=1}^{11} (-1)^{n+1} a_n . $$

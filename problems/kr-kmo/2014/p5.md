Let's say the intersection point of $ AC, BD $ be $ E $ .
Let's say point $ F $ which lies on $ \overrightarrow{ME} $ satisfies $ \overline{ME}\times \overline{MF}=\overline{MA}^{2} $ .

Prove that $ \angle LFM=\angle MFN $ . :)

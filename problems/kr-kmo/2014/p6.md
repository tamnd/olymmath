How many one-to-one functions $f : \{1, 2, \cdots, 9\} \rightarrow \{1, 2, \cdots, 9\}$ satisfy (i) and (ii)?
(i) $f(1)>f(2)$ , $f(9)<9$ .
(ii) For each $i=3, 4, \cdots, 8$ , if $f(1), \cdots, f(i-1)$ are smaller than $f(i)$ , then $f(i+1)$ is also smaller than $f(i)$ .

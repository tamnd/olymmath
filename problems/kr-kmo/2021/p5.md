A real number sequence $a_1, \cdots ,a_{2021}$ satisfies the below conditions. $$ a_1=1, a_2=2, a_{n+2}=\frac{2a_{n+1}^2}{a_n+a_{n+1}} (1\leq n \leq 2019) $$ Let the minimum of $a_1, \cdots ,a_{2021}$ be $m$ , and the maximum of $a_1, \cdots ,a_{2021}$ be $M$ .
Let a 2021 degree polynomial $$ P(x):=(x-a_1)(x-a_2) \cdots (x-a_{2021}) $$ $|P(x)|$ is maximum in $[m, M]$ when $x=\alpha$ . Show that $1<\alpha <2$ .

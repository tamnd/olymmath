Show that the areas of triangles $P_0OP_1,P_0OP_2,P_1OP_2$ form a geometric sequence in that order if and only if there exists a real number $x$ , such that $$ a_0x^2+a_1x+a_2=b_0x^2+b_1x+b_2=0 $$

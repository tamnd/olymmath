J has several cheetahs in his dresser, which has 7 drawers, such that each drawer has the
same number of cheetahs. He notices that he can take out one drawer, and redistribute all of the
cheetahs (including those in the removed drawer) in the remaining 6 drawers such that each drawer
still has an equal number of cheetahs as the other drawers. If he has at least one cheetah, what is
the smallest number of cheetahs that he can have?

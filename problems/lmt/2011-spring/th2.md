The Epic Cycle of Ancient Greek poems includes five poems, the Cypria, the Aethiopis, the
Iliupersis, the Nosti, and the Telegony. Each of these poems consists of some number of books
of verse. The average number of books making up the last four poems is 3.5. When the Cypria
is included among them, the average number of books among the five poems is 5. How many
books of verse make up the Cypria?

Toothless the fish-loving dragon is at (0,0) and wants to get back to his home, located at (6,6).
There is a pond full of fish at (2,5). If Toothless decides to only fly east one unit or north one
unit at a time, then how many paths can he take that will allow him to catch some fish before
returning home?

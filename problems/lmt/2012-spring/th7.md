Ali, Bob, Cam, Dan, and Eve play Knights and Knaves and have the following conversation.
Who is (are) the knave(s)? (Note: Either includes both. For example, if both Bob and Dan
are knaves, then Ali is still correct.)
Ali: Either Bob or Dan is a knave.
Bob: Either Ali or Eve is a knight.
Cam: Either Bob or Eve is a knave.
Dan: Either Ali or Cam is a knave.
Eve: Exactly two of us are knaves.

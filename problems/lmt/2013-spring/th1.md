Surya decides to sell gourmet cookies at LMT. If he sells them for 25 cents each, he sells 36
cookies. For every 4 cents he raises the price of each cookie, he sells 3 fewer cookies. What is
the smallest possible price, in cents, that Surya’s jealous friends can change each cookie to so
that Surya has no customers?

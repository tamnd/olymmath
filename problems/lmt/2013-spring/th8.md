Bill, Bob, and Ben write their favorite numbers on a sheet of paper. Bob points out that each
number has its digits sum to 4, Bill points out that they are all divisible by the same prime
number greater than 10, and Ben points out that none of them have 0’s. Find the sum of Bill’s,
Bob’s, and Ben’s favorite numbers, given no two of them have the same favorite number.

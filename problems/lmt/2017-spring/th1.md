Bob wants to build a bridge. This bridge has to be an arch bridge which reaches down on each side 10 feet and
crosses a 20 foot gap. If the arch is shown by a semi circle with radius 9, and the bridge is 5 feet wide, how many
cubic feet of material does Bob need?

A deck of cards contains 4 suites with 13 numbers in each suit. Evan and Albert are playing a game with a deck of
cards. First, Albert draws a card. Evan wins if he draws a card with the same number as Albert’s card or the same suit
as Albert’s card. What is the probability that Evan wins?

Jason is creating a structure out of steel bars. First, he makes a cube. He then connects the midpoints of the faces
to form a regular octahedron. He continues by connecting the midpoints of the faces of this octahedron to form
another, smaller cube. Find the ratio of the volme of the smaller cube to the volume of the larger cube.

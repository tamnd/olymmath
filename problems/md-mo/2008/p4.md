Let $ n$ be a positive integer. Find all $ x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ that satisfy the relation:
$$ \sqrt{x_1-1}+2\cdot \sqrt{x_2-4}+3\cdot \sqrt{x_3-9}+\cdots+n\cdot\sqrt{x_n-n^2}=\frac{1}{2}(x_1+x_2+x_3+\cdots+x_n).$$

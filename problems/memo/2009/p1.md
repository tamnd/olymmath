Find all functions $ f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ , such that
$$ f(xf(y)) + f(f(x) + f(y)) = yf(x) + f(x + f(y))$$
holds for all $ x$ , $ y \in \mathbb{R}$ , where $ \mathbb{R}$ denotes the set of real numbers.

Let $ a$ , $ b$ , $ c$ be real numbers such that for every two of the equations
$$ x^2+ax+b=0, x^2+bx+c=0, x^2+cx+a=0$$
there is exactly one real number satisfying both of them. Determine all possible values of $ a^2+b^2+c^2$ .

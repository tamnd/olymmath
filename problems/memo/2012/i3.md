The line through $ C $ parallel to $ AD $ meets the segments $ BD $ and $ AB $ in $ E $ and $ F $ , respectively. Let $ O $ be the circumcenter of the triangle $ BEF $ . Suppose that $ \angle ACO = 60^{\circ} $ . Prove the equality
$$ CF = AF + FO .$$

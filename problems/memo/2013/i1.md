Let $ a, b, c$ be positive real numbers such that
$$ a+b+c=\frac{1}{a^2} + \frac{1}{b^2} + \frac{1}{c^2} . $$
Prove that
$$ 2(a+b+c) \ge \sqrt[3]{7 a^2 b +1 } + \sqrt[3]{7 b^2 c +1 } + \sqrt[3]{7 c^2 a +1 } . $$
Find all triples $ (a,b,c) $ for which equality holds.

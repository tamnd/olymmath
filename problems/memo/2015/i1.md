Find all surjective functions $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ such that for all positive integers $a$ and $b$ , exactly one of the following equations is true:
\begin{align*}
f(a)&=f(b), <br />
f(a+b)&=\min\{f(a),f(b)\}.
\end{align*}
*Remarks:* $\mathbb{N}$ denotes the set of all positive integers. A function $f:X\to Y$ is said to be surjective if for every $y\in Y$ there exists $x\in X$ such that $f(x)=y$ .

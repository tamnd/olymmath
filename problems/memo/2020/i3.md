Let $ABC$ be an acute scalene triangle with circumcircle $\omega$ and incenter $I$. Suppose the ortho-center $H$ of $BIC$ lies inside $\omega$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the longer arc $BC$ of $\omega$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the shorter arc $AM$ of $\omega$.

Prove that there exists a circle tangent to $\omega$ at $N$ and tangent to the circumcircles of $BHI$ and $CHI$.

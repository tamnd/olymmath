We call an integer $ n > 1$ *good* if, for any natural numbers $ 1 \le b_1, b_2, \ldots , b_{n-1} \le n - 1$ and any $ i \in \{0, 1, \ldots , n - 1\}$ , there is a subset $ I$ of $ \{1, \ldots , n - 1\}$ such that $ \sum_{k\in I} b_k \equiv i \pmod n$ . (The sum over the empty set is zero.) Find all good numbers.

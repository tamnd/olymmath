James drew a regular $100$-gon $A_1 A_2 \ldots A_{100}$ (numbered clockwise) on the last year’s Náboj (23rd March, 2012) and randomly placed a chip on one of the vertices. Every following day, he moved the chip clockwise by the number of vertices indicated by the number of the current vertex (from $A_3$ he would move it to $A_6$, from $A_{96}$ to $A_{92}$). Now the chip lies on $A_{100}$. What was the probability this would happen?

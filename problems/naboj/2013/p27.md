We have a hat with several white, gray, and black rabbits. When a magician starts taking them randomly out of the hat (without returning them), the probability of him taking a white rabbit before a gray one is $\frac{3}{4}$. The probability of him taking a gray rabbit before a black one is $\frac{3}{4}$ as well. What is the probability of him taking a white rabbit before a black one?

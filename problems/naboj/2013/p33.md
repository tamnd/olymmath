Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be an acute triangle with $\mathrm{AB} =4\pi$, $\mathrm{BC} =4\pi +3$, $\mathrm{CA} =4\pi +6$. Denote by $D$ the foot of the $A$-altitude. Find $\mathrm{CD} -\mathrm{BD}$.

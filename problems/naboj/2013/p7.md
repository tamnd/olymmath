Point $D$ lies on the diameter $\mathrm{AB}$ of the semicircle $k$. A perpendicular to $\mathrm{AB}$ through point $D$ intersects $k$ at point $C$. The lengths of the arcs $\mathrm{AC}$ and $\mathrm{CB}$ of $k$ are in the ratio $1:2$. Find $\mathrm{AD} :\mathrm{DB}$.

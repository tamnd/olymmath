For non-zero digits $X$ and $Y$, denote $\overline{\mathrm{XY}}$ the number whose decimal representation is $\mathrm{XY}$. Assuming non-zero digits $A$, $B$, $C$ satisfy $\overline{\mathrm{AA}} +\overline{\mathrm{BB}} +\overline{\mathrm{CC}} =\overline{\mathrm{ABC}}$, determine $\overline{\mathrm{ABC}}$.

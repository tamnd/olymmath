Find all pairs of positive integers $a$, $b$ such that $\mathrm{ab} =\gcd (a,b)+\mathrm{lcm} (a,b)$, where $\gcd (a,b)$ and $\mathrm{lcm} (a,b)$ are the greatest common divisor and the least common multiple of $a$ and $b$, respectively.

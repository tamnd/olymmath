Consider a $2014\times 2014$ grid of unit squares with the lower left corner in the point $(0,0)$ and upper right corner in $(2014,2014)$. A line $p$ passes through the points $(0,0)$ and $(2014,2019)$. How many squares of the grid does $p$ cross?

Note: A line crosses a square if they have at least two points in common. For example, the diagonal crosses $2014$ squares of the grid.

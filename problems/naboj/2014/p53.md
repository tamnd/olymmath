I chose two numbers from the set $\{1,2,\ldots ,9\}$. Then I told Peter their product and Dominic their sum. The following conversation ensued:

- Peter: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Dominic: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Peter: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Dominic: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Peter: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Dominic: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Peter: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Dominic: “I don’t know the numbers.”

- Peter: “Now I know the numbers.”

What numbers did I choose?

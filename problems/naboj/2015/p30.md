In a parallelogram $\mathrm{ABCD}$, the point $E$ lies on side $\mathrm{AD}$ with $2\cdot \mathrm{AE} =\mathrm{ED}$ and the point $F$ lies on side $\mathrm{AB}$ with $2\cdot \mathrm{AF} =\mathrm{FB}$. Lines $\mathrm{CF}$ and $\mathrm{CE}$ intersect the diagonal $\mathrm{BD}$ in $G$ and $H$, respectively. Which fraction of the area of parallelogram $\mathrm{ABCD}$ is covered by the area of the pentagon $\mathrm{AFGHE}$?

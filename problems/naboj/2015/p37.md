In an isosceles triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ with base $\mathrm{AB}$, let $D$ be the intersection of angle bisector of $\angle ACB$ with $\mathrm{AB}$ and $E$ the intersection of angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ with $\mathrm{BC}$. Given that $\mathrm{AE} =2\cdot \mathrm{CD}$, find $\angle BAC$.

A circle $\omega_3$ of radius $3$ is internally tangent to circles $\omega_1$, $\omega_2$ of radii $1$ and $2$, respectively. Moreover, $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ are externally tangent. Points $A$, $B$ on $\omega_3$ are chosen so that the segment $\mathrm{AB}$ is a common external tangent of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$. Find the length of $\mathrm{AB}$.

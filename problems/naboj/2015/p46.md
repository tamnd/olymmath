Let the points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$, $F$ lie clockwise in this order on a circle $\omega$. Assume further that $\mathrm{AD}$ is a diameter of $\omega$, $\mathrm{BF}$ intersects $\mathrm{AD}$ and $\mathrm{CE}$ in $G$ and $H$, respectively, $\angle FEH=56^\circ$, $\angle DGB=124^\circ$, and $\angle DEC=34^\circ$. Find $\angle CEB$.

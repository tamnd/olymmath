Students were given a three-digit number $\overline{\mathrm{abc}}$ with $0<a<b<c$. The task was to multiply it by $6$ and then swap the tens digit with the hundreds digit. Alex made a mistake and performed the swap before the multiplication, but the result turned out to be correct! Find $\overline{\mathrm{abc}}$.

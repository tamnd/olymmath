A sequence $(a_n )$ is given by $a_1 =1$, and $a_n =\lfloor \sqrt{a_1 +a_2 +\cdots +a_{n-1}} \rfloor$ for $n>1$. Determine $a_{1000}$.

The symbol $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the integral part of $x$, i.e. the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

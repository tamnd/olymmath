Let $\mathrm{ABCD}$ be a rhombus and $M$, $N$ points on the segments $\mathrm{AB}$, $\mathrm{BC}$ different from $A$, $B$, $C$ such that $\mathrm{DMN}$ is an equilateral triangle and $\mathrm{AD} =\mathrm{MD}$. Find $\angle ABC$ (in degrees).

Equilateral triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ is inscribed in circle $\omega$. Point $X$ is on the (shorter) arc $\mathrm{BC}$ of $\omega$ and $T$ is the intersection of $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{CX}$. If $\mathrm{AX} =5$ and $\mathrm{TX} =3$, find $\mathrm{BX}$.

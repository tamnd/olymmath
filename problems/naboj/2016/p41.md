Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be an equilateral triangle. An interior point $P$ of $\mathrm{ABC}$ is said to be shining if we can find exactly $27$ rays emanating from $P$ intersecting the sides of the triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ such that the triangle is divided by these rays into $27$ smaller triangles of equal area. Determine the number of shining points in $\mathrm{ABC}$.

We construct a sequence of integers $a_0 ,a_1 ,a_2 \ldots \,$ in the following way: If $a_i$ is divisible by three, let $a_{i+1} =a_i /3$; otherwise let $a_{i+1} =a_i +1$. For how many different positive integers $a_0$ does the sequence reach the value $1$ for the first time in exactly eleven steps (i.e. $a_{11} =1$, but $a_0 ,a_1 ,\ldots ,a_{10} \ne 1$)?

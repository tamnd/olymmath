Assume further that $A$, $D$, $E$ are inner points of line segments $\mathrm{HI}$, $\mathrm{FG}$, $\mathrm{BC}$, respectively, and $\angle AED=53^\circ$. Determine the size of $\angle JKL$ (in degrees).

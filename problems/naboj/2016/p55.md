Let ${(a_n )}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers such that $9\mid a_{3k-2}$, $14\mid a_{3k-1}$, and $19\mid a_{3k}$ for all positive integers $k$. Find the smallest possible value of $a_{2016}$.

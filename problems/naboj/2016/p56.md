Let $P$ be a point inside triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$. Points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on the segments $\mathrm{BC}$, $\mathrm{CA}$, $\mathrm{AB}$, respectively, such that the lines $\mathrm{AD}$, $\mathrm{BE}$, $\mathrm{CF}$ intersect in $P$. Given that $\mathrm{PA} =6$, $\mathrm{PB} =9$, $\mathrm{PD} =6$, $\mathrm{PE} =3$, and $\mathrm{CF} =20$, find the area of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$.

Eight-digit palindromes are numbers of the form $\overline{\mathrm{abcddcba}}$ where $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$ are not necessarily distinct digits. How many eight-digit palindromes have the property that we can delete some digits in such a way that the resulting number would be $2017$?

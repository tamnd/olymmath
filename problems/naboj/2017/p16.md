A factory owner hires $100$ employees. Each work team leader earns €$5\, 000$ per month, each labourer €$1\, 000$ per month and each part-time worker earns €$50$ per month. In total, the factory owner pays €$100\, 000$ monthly to his employees and there is at least one employee of each type. How many work team leaders are there in his factory?

All the $2017$ seats around a very large round table are occupied by superheroes and villains. The superheroes always tell the truth, whereas the villains always lie. Each person sitting at the table reported that he or she was sitting between a superhero and a villain. For unknown reasons, exactly one superhero made a mistake. How many superheroes are there?

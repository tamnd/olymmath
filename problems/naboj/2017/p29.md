A bus, a truck, and a motorcycle move at constant speeds and pass a stationary observer in this order at equal time intervals. They pass another observer farther down the road at the same equal time intervals, but in different order: this time the order is bus, motorcycle, truck. Find the speed of the bus in $\text{km/h}$, if the speed of the truck is $60\, \text{km/h}$, and the speed of the motorcycle is $120\, \text{km/h}$.

Paul’s cattle grazes on a triangular meadow $\mathrm{ABC}$. Since his spotted cows and those without spots did not go well together, Paul built a twenty-meter long fence perpendicular to the side $\mathrm{AC}$, starting at point $P$ on this side and ending in $B$, dividing the meadow into two right-angled triangles. However, this was soon met with protests from the spotted cows (grazing in the part with point $A$), which pointed out that $\mathrm{AP} :\mathrm{PC} =2:7$ and demanded a fairer division. Consequently, Paul replaced the fence with a new one, parallel to the old one and with ends on the borders of the meadow, but dividing the meadow into two parts of equal area. What was the length of the new fence in meters?

For a composite positive integer $n$, let $\xi (n)$ denote the sum of the least three divisors of $n$, and $\vartheta (n)$ the sum of the greatest two divisors of $n$. Find all composite numbers $n$ such that $\vartheta (n)={(\xi (n))}^4$.

Note: By a divisor we mean a positive, not necessarily proper divisor.

Let $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$ be a right-angled triangle with right angle at $C$. Let $D$ and $E$ be points on $\mathrm{AB}$ with $D$ between $A$ and $E$ such that $\mathrm{CD}$ and $\mathrm{CE}$ trisect $\angle ACB$. If $\mathrm{DE} :\mathrm{BE} =8:15$, find $\mathrm{AC} :\mathrm{BC}$.

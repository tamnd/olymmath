Positive integers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy $a\ge b\ge c$ and

$$a+b+c+2\mathrm{ab} +2\mathrm{bc} +2\mathrm{ca} +4\mathrm{abc} =2017.$$

Find all possible values of $a$.

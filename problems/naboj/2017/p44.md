Four brothers, Allan, Bert, Charlie, and Daniel, had collected a large amount of hazelnuts in a forest. At night, Allan woke up with the irrestible urge to eat, so he decided to eat some of them. After counting the hazelnuts, he found out that if one hazelnut was removed, the rest could have been divided into four equal parts, so he discarded the extra hazelnut and ate his share (one fourth of the rest); then he returned to bed. Later that night, Bert woke up because of hunger, too, and found out that there was again an extra nut, which he removed and ate one fourth of the remaining nuts. Until morning, exactly the same happened to Charile and Daniel. When all brothers met in the morning, they found out that the number of the remaining nuts was still such that it was divisible by four after removing one nut. What was the minimal number of hazelnuts the brothers could have collected?

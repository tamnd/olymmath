Square $\mathrm{ABCD}$ has inscribed circle $\omega$, which touches the square at points $W$, $X$, $Y$, $Z$ lying on sides $\mathrm{AB}$, $\mathrm{BC}$, $\mathrm{CD}$, $\mathrm{DA}$ respectively. Let $E$ be an inner point of the (shorter) arc of $\omega$ between $W$ and $X$ and $F$ the intersection of $\mathrm{BC}$ and $\mathrm{EY}$. Provided that $\mathrm{EF} =5$ and $\mathrm{EY} =7$, determine the area of triangle $\mathrm{FY} C$.

In the cryptogram

$$W\, E\cdot \mathrm{LIKE} =N\, \mathrm{ABOJ}$$

different letters stand for different digits. Furthermore, we are given $S(W\, E)=11$, $S(\mathrm{LIKE} )=23$, and $S(N\, \mathrm{ABOJ} )=19$, where $S(n)$ denotes the sum of digits of a positive integer $n$. None of the three numbers may start with zero. Find the five-digit number $N\, \mathrm{ABOJ}$.

Determine all pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ such that all the roots of both the equations

$$\begin{array}{llll}x^2 -\mathrm{ax} +a+b-3 & =0, & & \\ x^2 -\mathrm{bx} +a+b-3 & =0 & & \end{array}$$

are also positive integers.

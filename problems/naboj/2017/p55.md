Triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ with $\mathrm{AB} =3$, $\mathrm{BC} =7$, and $\mathrm{AC} =5$ is inscribed in circle $\omega$. The bisector of angle $\angle BAC$ meets side $\mathrm{BC}$ at $D$ and circle $\omega$ at a second point $E$. Let $\gamma$ be the circle with diameter $\mathrm{DE}$. Circles $\omega$ and $\gamma$ meet at $E$ and a second point $F$. Determine the length of $\mathrm{AF}$.

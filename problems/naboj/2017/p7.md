Express the quotient

$$\frac{0.\overline{2}}{0.\overline{24}}$$

as a fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms with positive integers $a$ and $b$.

Note: The overline denotes periodic decimal expansion, for example $0.\overline{123} =0.123123\ldots$.

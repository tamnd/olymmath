Naomi ordered eight tennis balls and one handball at an online sports shop. The balls (of a perfect spherical shape) were packed in a cubic box so that each tennis ball was tangent to three of the six faces of the box and to the handball. The radius of a handball is $10\, \mathrm{cm}$ and the radius of each tennis ball is $5\, \mathrm{cm}$. Find the length of the edge of the box in centimeters.

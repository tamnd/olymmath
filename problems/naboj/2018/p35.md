In an equilateral triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, a light ray coming from $B$ hit $\mathrm{AC}$ at point $D$ satisfying $\mathrm{DC} :\mathrm{AC} =1:2018$, and it reflected such that the angle of incidence was equal to the angle of reflection. Then it reflected again every time it reached a side of $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$. How many times did it reflect (including the first reflection) until it reached some vertex of $\triangle \mathrm{ABC}$?

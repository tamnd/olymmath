Let $\mathrm{ABCDEFGHI}$ be a regular nonagon with circumcircle $\varrho$ and center $O$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the (shorter) arc $\mathrm{AB}$ of $\varrho$, $P$ the midpoint of $\mathrm{MO}$, and $N$ the midpoint of $\mathrm{BC}$. Let lines $\mathrm{OC}$ and $\mathrm{PN}$ intersect at $Q$. What is the measure of $\angle NQC$ (in degrees)?

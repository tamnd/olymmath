Let $a(1),a(2),\ldots ,a(n),\ldots \,$ be an increasing sequence of positive integers satisfying $a(a(n))=3n$ for every positive integer $n$. Compute $a(2018)$.

Note: A sequence is increasing if $a(m)<a(n)$ whenever $m<n$.

Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a triangle with $\mathrm{AB} =5$, $\mathrm{AC} =6$ and $\omega$ its circumcircle. Let $F$, $G$ be points on $\mathrm{AC}$ such that $\mathrm{AF} =1$, $\mathrm{FG} =3$, and $\mathrm{GC} =2$, and let $\mathrm{BF}$ and $\mathrm{BG}$ intersect $\omega$ in $D$ and $E$, respectively. Given that $\mathrm{AC}$ and $\mathrm{DE}$ are parallel, what is the length of $\mathrm{BC}$?

Find rational numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ such that

$$\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{2} -1} =\sqrt[3]{a} +\sqrt[3]{b} +\sqrt[3]{c} .$$

Note: A rational number is a quotient of two integers.

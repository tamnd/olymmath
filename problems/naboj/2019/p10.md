Fiona has two cubes, one of side length $9\, \mathrm{cm}$ composed of white unit cubes (i.e. cubes of side length $1\, \mathrm{cm}$), and one of side length $10\, \mathrm{cm}$ composed of black unit cubes. With these unit cubes she wants to build up a cube of side length $12\, \mathrm{cm}$. At least how many $\mathrm{cm}^2$ of the surface have to be black?

Let $\mathrm{ABCD}$ be a rectangle with $\mathrm{AB} =8\, \mathrm{cm}$ and $\mathrm{BC} =6\, \mathrm{cm}$. Further let $X$ and $Y$ be the intersections of the perpendicular bisector of $\mathrm{AC}$ with $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{CD}$, respectively. What is the length of $\mathrm{XY}$ (in centimeters)?

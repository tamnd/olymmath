Let $\mathrm{ABCD}$ be a parallelogram and let $T$ be an interior point of $\mathrm{AD}$ such that the straight line through $T$ and $C$ is the bisector of $\angle BCD$. Further, let $E$ be a point on $\mathrm{AB}$ such that $\angle AET=40^\circ$. If $\angle CTE=75^\circ$, what is $\angle CDA$ (in degrees)?

Find all positive integers $n$ such that

$$\lfloor \frac{n}{5} \rfloor +\lfloor \frac{n}{7} \rfloor +\lfloor \frac{n}{35} \rfloor =2019.$$

Note: The expression $\lfloor x\rfloor$ denotes the integer part of $x$, i.e. the greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

Paul has a rectangular $5$ by $3$ bar of chocolate. He has put extra sugar on the top left square to make it sweeter. He proceeds to eat the chocolate in the following way: In each step he randomly chooses either eating the rightmost column, or the lowest row, both options having the same probability $1/2$. He repeats these steps until he has eaten the whole chocolate. What is the probability that in the very last step, he eats only the sweeter square?

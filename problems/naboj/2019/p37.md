Tom and Jerry have two polynomials $T(x)=x^2 +2x+10$ and $J(x)=x^2 -8x+25$. When each of them substituted their favourite positive integer $t$, $j$ into their respective polynomial they got the same result, i.e. $T(t)=J(j)$. Find all possible values of $|t-j|$.

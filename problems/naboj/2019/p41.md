On a bicycle tour of length $110\, \mathrm{km}$ from Passau to Linz, Heiko and Eva have to overcome three climbs. During their first stop, Heiko, who is excellent at mental arithmetic, says: “If you multiply the three distances from Passau to the peak of each climb, you get a multiple of $2261$.” After thinking about that for a while, Eva responds: “You also get a multiple of $2261$ if you multiply the distances measured from Linz instead of Passau.” After riding $80\, \mathrm{km}$ from the start they make a second stop and Heiko remarks: “Now there is only one climb ahead before we arrive in Linz.” Assuming that all the distances are road distances in kilometres and that they are integers, find the distances from Passau to the peak of each climb in kilometres.

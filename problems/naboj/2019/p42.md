Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a right-angled triangle with right angle at $C$ such that $\mathrm{AC} =4-\sqrt{3}$ and $\mathrm{BC} =\sqrt{3}$. Furthermore, let $D$ and $E$ be points such that $\mathrm{ABDE}$ is a square not containing point $C$ in its interior, and let $J$ be a point on $\mathrm{DE}$ such that $\angle ACJ=45^\circ$. Finally, let $K$ be a point on $\mathrm{CJ}$ such that $\mathrm{AK} \parallel \mathrm{BC}$. What is the area of the triangle $\mathrm{JKE}$?

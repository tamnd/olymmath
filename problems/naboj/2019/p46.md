Let $\mathrm{ABC}$ be a right-angled triangle with $\angle ACB=90^\circ$, $\mathrm{AC} =15$, $\mathrm{BC} =20$. Let $D$ be the point on the line $\mathrm{AB}$ such that $\mathrm{CD} \perp \mathrm{AB}$. The circle $t$ inscribed in the triangle $\mathrm{ACD}$ touches the line $\mathrm{CD}$ at $T$. Another circle $c$ also touches the line $\mathrm{CD}$ at $T$, and it touches the line segment $\mathrm{BC}$. Denote the two intersections of the circle $c$ with the line $\mathrm{AB}$ by $X$ and $Y$. What is the length of $\mathrm{XY}$?

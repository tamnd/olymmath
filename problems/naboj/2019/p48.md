One hundred children stand in a row. The first child has $4$ grams of chocolate, the second child has $8$ grams of chocolate and so on until the $100$th child has $400$ grams of chocolate. The first child gives one third of their chocolate to the second child (so the second child now has $\frac{28}{3}$ grams of chocolate). The second child gives one third of their chocolate to the third child and so on until the $99$th child gives one third of their chocolate to the $100$th child. How many grams of chocolate will the $100$th child have in the end?

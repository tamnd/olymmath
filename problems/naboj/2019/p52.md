Let $a_1 ,a_2 ,a_3 ,\ldots \,$ be a sequence of positive real numbers. Starting from the second term $a_2$ each number is half the sum of the arithmetic and geometric mean of the two neighbour numbers. Find $a_{333}$ if it is known that $a_1 =\frac{2}{7}$ and $a_{11} =\frac{7}{2}$.

Note: The geometric mean of positive reals $x$ and $y$ is defined as $\sqrt{\mathrm{xy}}$.

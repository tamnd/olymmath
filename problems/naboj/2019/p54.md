Let $P$ be a point inside a triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$. Denote by $A^\prime$, $B^\prime$, $C^\prime$ the intersections of $\mathrm{AP}$, $\mathrm{BP}$, $\mathrm{CP}$ with $\mathrm{BC}$, $\mathrm{CA}$, $\mathrm{AB}$, respectively. Assume that

$$A^\prime P=B^\prime P=C^\prime P=3$$

and

$$\mathrm{AP} +\mathrm{BP} +\mathrm{CP} =25.$$

Find $\mathrm{AP} \cdot \mathrm{BP} \cdot \mathrm{CP}$.

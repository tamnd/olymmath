Fourteen points $A_1 ,\ldots ,A_{14}$ were chosen on a circle $c$ counter-clockwise in this order so that no three distinct segments with endpoints among the given points meet in the interior of $c$. Christine drew all these segments, but seeing that the picture had got too messy, she decided to erase all the sides and all the diagonals of heptagons $A_1 A_3 A_5 A_7 A_9 A_{11} A_{13}$ and $A_2 A_4 A_6 A_8 A_{10} A_{12} A_{14}$. Into how many regions do the remaining segments divide the interior of $c$?

Real numbers $x_1 ,x_2 ,\ldots ,x_{2020}$ have these properties:

- Whenever we sum all of the numbers except a single $x_i$ with $i$ odd, the result is $2$.

- Whenever we sum all of the numbers except a single $x_i$ with $i$ even, the result is $0$.

What is the value of the sum $x_1 +x_2 +\cdots +x_{2020}$?

Two players are playing a game, alternating moves. Each move consists of changing a positive integer $n$ into another positive integer in the range $[\frac{n}{3} ,\frac{n}{2} ]$. A player who cannot move loses. For how many starting numbers in the range $[1,1000]$ is the first player able to win with optimal strategy?

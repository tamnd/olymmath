Seven trolleybuses run between fourteen stations on one line. Each trolleybus starts in one of the stations and moves in one direction until it reaches the end of the line, which is either the first or the last station on the line, where it turns around and continues in the other direction. All trolleybuses maintain constant speed and they pass exactly one station in a minute. MSc Birne has placed them in such a way, that1.there is at most one trolleybus at each station, and2.there will be at most one trolleybus at each station in one minute, no matter in what directions the trolleybuses go.

In how many ways could that have been done? The trolleybuses are considered identical.

Let us define $x\star n=(2-x)^n +x^3 -6x^2 +12x-5$ for any real number $x$ and a positive integer $n$. Determine the sum of all real numbers $a$ solving equation

$$(\ldots (a\star 2020)\star 2019)\star \cdots \star 2)\star 1=a.$$

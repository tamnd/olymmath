Consider functions $f:\mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{N}$ satisfying the condition

$$f(m+n)\ge f(m)+f(f(n))-1$$

for all $m,n\in \mathbb{N}$. Find the arithmetic mean of all possible values of $f(2020)$.

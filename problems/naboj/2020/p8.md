Four mathematicians sat around a table and have ordered a large bowl of pretzels. Daniel left for the toilet. Each minute, Adam, Beatha and Cyril took one pretzel, divided it into three equal pieces and ate it. After some time, Daniel returned to the table and they all continued eating one pretzel each minute, but Daniel got to eat $2/5$ of each pretzel, while the others got to eat $1/5$. After some time, Adam noted that Daniel ate exactly the same portion as himself. What is the ratio of time in which Daniel was absent to the time he was present?

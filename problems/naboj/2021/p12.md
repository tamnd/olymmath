Let $n$ be a positive integer. Now consider the increasing sequence $S_n$ starting with $1$ and having constant difference $n$ between one term and the next term. For instance, $S_2$ is the sequence $1,3,5,\ldots$ For how many values of $n$ does $S_n$ contain the number $2021$?

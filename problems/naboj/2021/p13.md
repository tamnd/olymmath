There is a straight $90$-meter-long corridor with ten windows, each two neighbouring being $10$ meters apart. Tommy placed seven robots by seven different windows, and switched them all on at the same time. When switched on, each robot moves at a constant speed of $10$ meters in a minute in one of the two directions until it reaches the end of the corridor where it instantly turns and continues in the same manner in the opposite direction. Tommy was measuring the time until the first moment when every robot met all the others. Determine the largest possible value he could have measured in seconds.

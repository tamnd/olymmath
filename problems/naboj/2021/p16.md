In the football stadium the substitutes’ bench has fourteen single chairs. The new management of the team consisting of coach, assistant coach, manager and physiotherapist wants to become acquainted with all the players. Therefore, during the game they want to sit on the bench among the ten substitute players in such a way that each member of the management sits between two players. In how many ways can the members of the management choose their four chairs to achieve this?

Note: Using two different orders on the same four chairs counts as two different ways.

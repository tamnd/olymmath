For a fixed positive integer $a$ we construct an acute-angled triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ such that $\mathrm{BC} =a$ and lengths $h_b$, $h_c$ of the corresponding altitudes are integers as well. Given that the greatest possible area of such triangle is $101.4$, determine $a$.

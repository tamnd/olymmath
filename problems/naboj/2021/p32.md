Ludwig computed the sum of $1000$ positive integer numbers and got the value $1\, 200\, 500$. If these numbers are arranged in ascending order, then the difference between two consecutive numbers is either $2$ or $7$. The smallest of the numbers is $101$. Now he wants to maximize the largest number within this sum by keeping all other conditions fulfilled. What is the maximum value possible for this number?

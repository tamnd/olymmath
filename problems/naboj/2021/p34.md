Let $\mathrm{ABCDE}$ be a regular pentagon and $F$ be the intersection of diagonals $\mathrm{AD}$ and $\mathrm{BE}$. The isosceles triangle $\mathrm{AFE}$ can be completed to a regular pentagon $\mathrm{AFEXY}$, let us denote it $p$. There is another regular pentagon, $q$, the vertices of which are the intersections of all the five diagonals of $\mathrm{ABCDE}$. Given that $\mathrm{AF} =1$, what is the largest distance between a vertex of $p$ and a vertex of $q$?

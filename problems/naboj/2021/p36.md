Tom and Mary want to buy a house. They look for a perfect place, but their definitions of “perfect” differ. They found $10$ offers and decided to try the following decision process: they both rank the houses randomly (a draw is not allowed) and if the top-three houses of Mary and Tom have exactly one house in common, they buy this house. What is the chance that this process succeeds?

Let us define polynomials

$$p(x)=ax^{2021} +bx^{2020} +\cdots +ax^{2k-1} +bx^{2k-2} +\cdots +bx^2 +\mathrm{ax} +b$$

and

$$q(x)=ax^2 +\mathrm{bx} +a,$$

where $a$ and $b$ are positive real numbers. We know that $q(x)$ has precisely one real root. Find the sum of all real roots of $p(x)$.

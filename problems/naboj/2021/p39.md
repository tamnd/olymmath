Four people are sitting in a room, each of them speaks exactly three of these five languages: Czech, German, English, Polish, and Hungarian. They don’t speak any other language. We can see that in total there are $10000$ ways to assign the languages to people. In how many of these scenarios can someone give a talk in a language that all of them understand?

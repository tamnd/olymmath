The Scrabboj game set consists of a $5\times 1$ board and a bag of distinguishable tiles. On each tile exactly one letter out of $N$, $A$, $B$, $O$, $J$ is written. How many different sets of Scrabboj are there for which the total number of ways to compose the word $\mathrm{NABOJ}$ is equal to $1440$?

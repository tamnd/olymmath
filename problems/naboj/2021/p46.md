The Cube City of Tomorrow has a map that looks like a cubic grid $4\times 4\times 4$. Every point with integral coordinates is called a crossroad and every two crossroads of distance $1$ are connected by a straight road. The crossroad in the middle of the city, $(2,2,2)$, is closed due to maintenance. David wants to go from the crossroad $(0,0,0)$ to the crossroad $(4,4,4)$ via the shortest possible path along roads. How many possible paths are there?

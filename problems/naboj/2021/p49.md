Find the smallest positive real number $x$ with the following property: There exists at least one triple of positive real numbers $(s,t,u)$ such that

$$\begin{array}{llll}s^2 -\mathrm{st} +t^2 & =12, & & \\ t^2 -\mathrm{tu} +u^2 & =x, & & \end{array}$$

and no two triples with this property can differ in the last coordinate only.

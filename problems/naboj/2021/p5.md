Once upon a time there was a wise King. His castle was in the centre of four concentric circular walls of radii $50$, $100$, $150$, $200$ and the land surrounded by the largest wall was used as the castle grounds (including the land inside the other walls). There were peaceful times, so he decided to tear down all four walls and build only one circular wall, again with his castle as the center, of maximal possible radius from the material of the old ones. What is the ratio of the area of the new castle grounds and the area of the old one (as a number greater or equal to one)?

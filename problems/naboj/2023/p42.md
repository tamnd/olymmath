Let $x_1 =2020$ and let $x_n$ be equal to $x_{n-1}$ multiplied by the smallest prime $p$, which is not a divisor of $x_{n-1}$, and divided by all primes smaller than $p$. Find the number of different prime divisors of $x_{2020}$.

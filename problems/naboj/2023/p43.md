The medians of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ dissect it into six sub-triangles. The centroids of these sub-triangles are vertices of hexagon $\mathrm{DEFGHI}$. Find the area ratio between the hexagon $\mathrm{DEFGHI}$ and the triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$.

Let $O$ be the circumcenter of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$. Let further points $D$ and $E$ lie on the segments $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{AC}$, respectively, so that $O$ is the midpoint of $\mathrm{DE}$. If $\mathrm{AD} =8$, $\mathrm{BD} =3$, and $\mathrm{AO} =7$, determine the length of $\mathrm{CE}$.

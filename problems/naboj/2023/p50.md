In the cryptogram

$$\begin{array}{llllllllll} & & & R & E & D \\ + & & B & L & U & E \\ + & G & R & E & E & N \\ & & & & & & & & & \\ \\ \\ \\ = & B & R & O & W & N\end{array}$$

different letters represent different digits. None of the four numbers may start with zero. Furthermore, we know that $\mathrm{BLUE}$ is a perfect square. Find the five-digit number $\mathrm{BROWN}$.

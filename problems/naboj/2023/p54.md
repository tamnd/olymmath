Positive real numbers $x$, $y$, $z$ satisfy

$$\begin{array}{llll}x^2 +\mathrm{xy} +y^2 & =1, & & \\ y^2 +\mathrm{yz} +z^2 & =2, & & \\ z^2 +\mathrm{zx} +x^2 & =3. & & \end{array}$$

Find the value of $\mathrm{xy} +\mathrm{yz} +\mathrm{zx}$.

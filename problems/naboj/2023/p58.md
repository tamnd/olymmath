A point $P$ is located in the interior of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$. If

$$\mathrm{AP} =\sqrt{3} , \mathrm{BP} =5, \mathrm{CP} =2, \mathrm{AB} :\mathrm{AC} =2:1, \text{and} \angle BAC=60^\circ ,$$

what is the area of triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$?

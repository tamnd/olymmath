A botanist planted two plants, $P_1$ and $P_2$, of the same species and measured their heights. After a week, during which the plants had grown up by the same percentage, he measured again and noticed that $P_1$ was as big as $P_2$ had been a week before and $P_2$ was by $44\%$ bigger than $P_1$ had been a week before. By what percentage did the plants grow during the week?

In the Slovak folk song Kopala studienku (“She was digging a well”), a girl checks whether her well is equally deep and wide. By definition, a well is a right cylinder, whose height is the depth of the well and whose base diameter is the width of the well. She knows that she needs a week to dig a well of the desired width but only $\frac{1}{3}$ of its required depth, while Janko Matúška needs a week to dig a well that is sufficiently deep but half as wide as needed. The effort is proportional to the amount of earth removed. How many days will they need to dig a proper well together?

Positive integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ satisfy the equations

$$\begin{array}{llll}\sqrt{a^2 +b^2 -172} & =c, & & \\ \sqrt{c^2 +b^2 -220} & =a. & & \end{array}$$

What is the largest possible value of their sum $a+b+c$?

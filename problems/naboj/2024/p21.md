We call the $n$-th birthday a square birthday if $n>1$ and whenever a prime number $p$ divides $n$, then also $p^2$ divides $n$. For example, $n=8=2^3$ works, while $n=56=8\cdot 7$ does not. This year, Grandpa Jefo celebrated his $196$th birthday. How many square birthdays has he lived through?

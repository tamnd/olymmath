Consider a quadrilateral $\mathrm{ABCD}$ with side lengths $\mathrm{AB} =5$, $\mathrm{BC} =3$, and $\mathrm{CD} =10$. The measure of the internal angle at $B$ is $240^\circ$, while the one at $C$ is $60^\circ$. Calculate the length $\mathrm{AD}$.

Fero has coins with values of $1\, \mathrm{ct}$, $2\, \mathrm{ct}$, and $5\, \mathrm{ct}$. He has $33$ of the $1\, \mathrm{ct}$ type, $106$ of the $2\, \mathrm{ct}$ and $31$ of the $5\, \mathrm{ct}$ type. He wants to split them into two piles with the same number of coins and the same value and give one of the piles to his sister. In how many ways could he do this? Coins of the same value are considered indistinguishable.

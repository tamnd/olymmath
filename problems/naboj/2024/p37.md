By $\lfloor x\rfloor$ we denote the largest integer that is not greater than the real number $x$. Let $a_1 ,a_2 ,\ldots \,$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $a_1 =\sqrt{3}$ and for each $n\ge 1$, we have

$$a_{n+1} =\lfloor a_n \rfloor +\frac{1}{a_n -\lfloor a_n \rfloor} .$$

What is the value of $a_{2024}$?

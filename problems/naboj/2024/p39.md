Let $x\parallel y$ denote a concatenation of two positive integers, that is, a number obtained by first writing out the digits of $x$ as they appear in $x$ and then doing the same for $y$; for example, $3\parallel 4=34$, $24\parallel 5=245$, and $20\parallel 24=2024$. A positive integer $n$ is called threevisible if there exist three pairwise distinct positive integers (without leading zeros) $a$, $b$, and $c$ such that $n=a\parallel b\parallel c$ and $a$ divides $b$ and $b$ divides $c$. What is the largest threevisible five-digit number?

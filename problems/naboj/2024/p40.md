By writing $S_x$, a string of digits with a subscript $x$ (some positive integer larger than all used digits), we indicate that base $x$ should be used to interpret the string. For example $242_7 =2\cdot 7^2 +4\cdot 7+2=128_{10} =10000000_2$. Find the sum of all integers $x>5$ for which the statement

$$2024_x \text{ is divisible by} 15_x$$

holds true.

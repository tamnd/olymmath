Adam, Bea, Charles, Daniel, and Erik are attending two courses. Adam and Bea attend only the first course, Charles and Erik attend only the other one, while Daniel attends both. Roberta knows that each course is attended by three students, but not by which three. So she asks everyone to point a finger randomly at a classmate from any of their courses (i.e., Daniel will choose each of the other four people with probability $\frac{1}{4}$, etc.). What is the probability that it will be possible for Roberta to figure out that Daniel is the one attending both courses?

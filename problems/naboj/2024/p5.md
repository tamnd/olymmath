There are three channels on your TV: $1$st, $2$nd, and $3$rd. From each channel, you can switch only to a channel with the number differing by one, e.g., from the $1$st only to the $2$nd. You start watching the second channel and then switch the channel $11$ times. How many different sequences of channels can you obtain?

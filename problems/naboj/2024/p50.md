Ondra got bored of traditional operations like addition and multiplication. So he made up his own operation, starification. This operation, denoted by $a\star b$, is defined on real numbers and has the following properties:1.$(a+b)\star c=(a\star c)+(b\star c)$,2.$a\star (b+c)=(a\star b)\star c$.

Given that $3\star 2=54$, find the value of $5\star 4$.

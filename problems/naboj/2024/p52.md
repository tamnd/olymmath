Helena learned about moving averages. She took her favourite sequence, the Fibonacci sequence ${\{F_k \}}_{k=0}^\infty$, which satisfies $F_n =F_{n-1} +F_{n-2}$ with $F_0 =0$ and $F_1 =1$, and made a sequence ${\{m_k \}}_{k=6}^{2024}$ of moving averages, which satisfies $m_k =\frac{F_k +F_{k-1} +\cdots +F_{k-6}}{7}$. How many terms of the sequence ${\{m_k \}}_{k=6}^{2024}$ are integers?

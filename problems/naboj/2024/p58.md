Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $10$ with integer coefficients such that it has only real roots and $P(x)$ divides the polynomial $P(P(x)+2x-4)$. Find the value of $\frac{P(2024)}{P(206)}$.

Note: Here we say that a polynomial $P(x)$ divides a polynomial $Q(x)$ if $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ have integer coefficients and there exists a polynomial $R(x)$ with integer coefficients such that $Q(x)=R(x)\cdot P(x)$.

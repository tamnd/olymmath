Joe is standing in the circle of $2024$ people labeled clockwise by numbers $1,2,\ldots ,2024$. They are playing with a frisbee. Person in place $1$ throws it to the person in place $3$, then they throw it to the person in place $5$, and so on. Each person throws the disc to the person next to the one left of them (so they skip one person). The skipped person is now angry that they have not played and leave the circle. This process repeats until the last two people are playing. If Joe wants to be one of those two people in the end, where should he stand in the beginning? Find the sum of numbers of these positions.

The real numbers $a>b>1$ satisfy the inequality

$${(\mathrm{ab} +1)}^2 +(a+b)^2 \le 2(a+b)(a^2 -\mathrm{ab} +b^2 +1).$$

Determine the minimum possible value of

$$\frac{\sqrt{a-b}}{b-1} .$$

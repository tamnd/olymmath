Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be distinct non-zero integers such that

$$\frac{(x-1)^2}{z} +\frac{(y-1)^2}{x} +\frac{(z-1)^2}{y} =\frac{(x-1)^2}{y} +\frac{(y-1)^2}{z} +\frac{(z-1)^2}{x} .$$

Find the minimum possible value of $|64x+19y+4z|$.

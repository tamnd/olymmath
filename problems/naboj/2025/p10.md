We call a four-digit number intriguing if it has the following property: when its hundreds digit is removed, the resulting three-digit number is one-ninth of the original four-digit number. For example, the number $2025$ is intriguing, since $225=\frac{1}{9} \cdot 2025$. Find the largest intriguing four-digit number.

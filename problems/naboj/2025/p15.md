Let $\gcd (a,b)$ and $\mathrm{lcm} (a,b)$ denote the greatest common divisor and the least common multiple of integers $a$ and $b$, respectively. Evaluate the following expression:

$$\mathrm{lcm} (2025,\mathrm{lcm} (2024,\gcd (2023,\gcd (2022,\ldots \mathrm{lcm} (4,\gcd (3,\gcd (2,1)))\ldots \, )))).$$

The operations $\gcd$ and $\mathrm{lcm}$ alternate every two steps, with a total of 1012 $\gcd$ computations and 1012 $\mathrm{lcm}$ computations in the entire expression. For example, if there were only two occurrences of each operation, the expression would be $\mathrm{lcm} (5,\mathrm{lcm} (4,\gcd (3,\gcd (2,1))))$.

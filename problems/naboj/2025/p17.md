Tyler ran an $18\, \mathrm{km}$ circuit. He started at a steady pace but, feeling fatigued at some point, he slowed his pace by $25\%$ for the remainder of the run. After completing the run, Tyler checked his smart watch and discovered that he spent twice as much time running at the slower pace as he did at the faster pace. What distance (in kilometers) did Tyler cover before slowing down?

In a village of 60 people, each person belongs to one of three types: truth-tellers, who always tell the truth; liars, who always lie; and normal people, who are free to answer however they prefer. Everyone in the village knows everyone else’s type. An outsider asked all the villagers two questions:1.“Are there at least 31 truth-tellers?” – and received exactly 43 positive replies.2.“Are there at least 31 liars?” – and received exactly 39 positive replies.

What is the minimum number of normal people in the village?

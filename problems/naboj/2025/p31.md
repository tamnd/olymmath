Consider the sequence of all strictly increasing $4$-tuples with entries in the set $\{0,1,2,\ldots ,15\}$, arranged in lexicographic order:

$$(0,1,2,3),(0,1,2,4),(0,1,2,5),\ldots ,(12,13,14,15).$$

That is, $(a_1 ,a_2 ,a_3 ,a_4 )$ appears earlier than $(b_1 ,b_2 ,b_3 ,b_4 )$ in this sequence if and only if

$$a_1 <b_1 \text{or} a_1 =b_1 ,a_2 <b_2 \text{or} a_1 =b_1 ,a_2 =b_2 ,a_3 <b_3 \text{or} a_1 =b_1 ,a_2 =b_2 ,a_3 =b_3 ,a_4 <b_4 .$$

Find the position of the tuple $(2,4,7,14)$ in the sequence.

A ring of twelve squares is formed by removing the four central squares from a $4\times 4$ board. In how many ways can four squares be chosen in the ring such that at least one square is selected from each side of the ring?

Note: Each corner square belongs to two sides. Choices differing only by symmetry (rotations or reflections of the ring) are considered distinct.

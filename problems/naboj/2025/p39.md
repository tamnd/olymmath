In the following computation, different letters stand for different non-zero digits.

$$\begin{array}{llllll} & N & N & N & N & N \\ & + & A & A & A & A \\ & & + & B & B & B \\ & & & + & O & O \\ & & & & + & J \\ & - & 2 & 0 & 2 & 5 \\ & & & & & \\ = & N & A & B & O & J\end{array}$$

Determine the largest possible value of the five-digit number $\mathrm{NABOJ}$.

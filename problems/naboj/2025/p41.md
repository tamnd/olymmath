Determine the number of pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers satisfying $a\le b$ and such that $\gcd (a,b)$, together with $a$ and $b$, can be arranged into an arithmetic sequence whose sum is $2025$.

Note: An arithmetic sequence is a sequence of numbers where the difference between one number and the next one is always the same.

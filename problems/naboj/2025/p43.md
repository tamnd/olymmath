$$\begin{array}{llll}2a+2b-\mathrm{ab} & =2025, & & \\ 2b+2c-\mathrm{bc} & =47, & & \\ 2c+2d-\mathrm{cd} & =5. & & \end{array}$$

Find the value of $2a+2d-\mathrm{ad}$.

For how many positive integers $n$ less or equal to $200$ has the equation

$$5\lfloor \frac{x}{5} \rfloor -n\lfloor \frac{x}{n} \rfloor =1$$

at least one integer solution $x$ with $1\le x\le 200$?

Note: The symbol $\lfloor t\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to the real number $t$.

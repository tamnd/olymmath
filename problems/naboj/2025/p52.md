Let $f$ be a function that assigns a non-negative integer to each pair of non-negative integers and is defined by the following conditions:1.For every $x$: $f(x,x)=0$.2.For every $x,y$: $f(x,y)=f(y,x)$.3.For every $x,y$: $f(2x,2y)=f(x,y)$.4.For every $x,y$: $f(2x+1,2y+1)=f(x,y)$.5.For every $x,y$: $f(2x+1,2y)=f(x,y)+1$.

Find the sum of all non-negative integers $t$, $t\le 60$, satisfying $f(20,t)=2$.

Mark received a sequence ${\{a_n \}}_{n=1}^\infty$ as a gift, defined by the initial terms $a_1 =1$, $a_2 =3$, and the recurrence relation

$$a_{n+1}^2 +3a_n^2 -4a_{n-1}^2 =4a_n \cdot (a_{n+1} -a_{n-1} )+2n-1$$

for all $n\ge 2$. However, the sequence is not uniquely determined by this relation. To resolve any ambiguity, Mark calculated the terms step by step, always choosing the largest value whenever more than one possibility arose. Determine the value of $a_{13}$.

Let $x$ be a positive integer such that

$$\mathrm{lcm} (x,2^5 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 7^2 )=2^6 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 7^2 \text{and} \mathrm{lcm} (x,2^8 \cdot 3^4 \cdot 5^3 \cdot 7)=2^8 \cdot 3^4 \cdot 5^3 \cdot 7^2 .$$

How many distinct values can $\gcd (x,2^2 \cdot 3^4 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 7^3 )$ take?

Note: $\gcd (a,b)$ and $\mathrm{lcm} (a,b)$ denote the greatest common divisor and the least common multiple of integers $a$ and $b$, respectively.

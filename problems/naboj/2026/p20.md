In the equation

$$\frac{M\cdot A\cdot T\cdot H}{N\cdot A\cdot B\cdot O\cdot J} =G\cdot A\cdot M\cdot E,$$

each letter represents a digit, distinct letters represent distinct digits, and the symbol $\cdot$ denotes multiplication. How many different values can the product $M\cdot A\cdot N\cdot G\cdot O$ take?

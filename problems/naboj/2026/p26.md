For communication with an orbital satellite, six distinct channels are chosen from the set $\{1,2,\ldots ,13\}$, and only the set of channels matters – two selections are considered the same if they contain the same six channels, regardless of order. To achieve the best transmit rate, the selection must include at least one pair of channels whose numbers differ by an odd integer. How many such sets of channels are possible?

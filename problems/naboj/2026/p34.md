A flea jumps along the circumference of a circle. Its first jump subtends a central angle of $1^\circ$ (that is, the angle between the radii to the endpoints of the jump is $1^\circ$). Each subsequent jump is in the same direction and subtends $2^\circ$, then $3^\circ$, and so on. On which jump does the flea first land back at its starting point?

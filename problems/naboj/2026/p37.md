Let $\mathrm{ABCD}$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with $\angle ADB=48^\circ$ and $\angle BDC=56^\circ$. A point $X$ is chosen inside triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$ such that $\angle XCB=24^\circ$, and the ray $\mathrm{AX}$ bisects $\angle BAC$. Find the measure of $\angle CBX$ in degrees.

Note: A cyclic quadrilateral is a quadrilateral whose four vertices all lie on a single circle.

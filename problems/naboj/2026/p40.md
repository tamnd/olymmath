Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be real numbers such that $a+b+c+d=2$ and $\frac{a^2}{a+2b} +\frac{b^2}{b+2c} +\frac{c^2}{c+2d} +\frac{d^2}{d+2a} =2026$. Find the value of

$$\frac{b^2}{a+2b} +\frac{c^2}{b+2c} +\frac{d^2}{c+2d} +\frac{a^2}{d+2a} .$$

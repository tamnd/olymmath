In how many ways can a $3\times 3$ table be filled with digits $0,1,\ldots ,9$ such that each cell contains exactly one digit, no digit is used more than once, and the six sums formed by the three rows and three columns are either all even or all odd? An example of a valid table in which all row and column sums are even is shown below:

$$\begin{array}{lll}1 & 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 4 & 7 \\ 6 & 0 & 8\end{array}$$

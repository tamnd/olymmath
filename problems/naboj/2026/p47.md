Consider two rooms, one full of dwarves, each $1.2\, m$ tall, and the other full of cyclopes, each $4\, m$ tall. After 35 cyclopes and 24 dwarves moved from their room to the other one, the average height in both the rooms became exactly the same. What was the smallest total number of creatures for this to happen?

Adele is a master of algebraic manipulation, so she has taken all the expressions $\pm a\pm b\pm c\pm d$ (altogether, $16$ expressions for all the possible sign combinations) and multiplied them together, obtaining a polynomial in the variables $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$. She then discarded all terms in which at least one of the variables was missing. What was the sum of the coefficients of the remaining terms?

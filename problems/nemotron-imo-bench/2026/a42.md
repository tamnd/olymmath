Prove that for all non-degenerate subintervals $ I_{1},\dots, I_{k} $ of the interval $ [0, 1]$
we have $$ \sum \frac{1}{\left | I_{i}\cup I_{j} \right |} \geq k^{2},$$ where the summation is over all pairs $ (i, j) $ of indices such that $I_i\cap I_j\neq \emptyset$.

Let $m\geq n\geq 2022$ be integers and let $a_1,\ldots, a_n$ and
$b_1,\ldots, b_n$ be real numbers. Prove that there are at most $3n\sqrt{m\log n}$ pairs
$(i,j)$ with $1\leq i,j\leq n$ and
$$|a_i+b_j-ij|\leq m.$$

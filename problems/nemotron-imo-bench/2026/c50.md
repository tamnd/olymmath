Prove that there are $c,n_0>0$ such that for any set
$A$ consisting of $n>n_0$ integers we have
$$|A-A|-|A+A|\leq n^2-cn^{8/5},$$
where $A\pm A=\{a\pm b| a,b\in A\}$.

Let $a_1,\ldots, a_n$ be positive integers such that
$$\frac{1}{a_1}+\ldots+\frac{1}{a_n}\leq \frac{1}{2}.$$
The government of Optimistica publishes each year an annual report, with $n$ economic indicators, the values of the
$i$th indicator being in the set $\{1,\ldots, a_i\}$. We say the report is optimistic if at least $n-1$ indicators have
equal or higher values than in the previous report. Prove that the government can publish annual reports in an infinitely long sequence.

$A', B', C'$ respectively. Prove that
$$\frac{BA'}{BC}+\frac{CB'}{CA}+\frac{AC'}{AB}=\frac{3}{2}$$
if and only if at least two of the triangles $PAB, PBC, PCA$ have the same area.

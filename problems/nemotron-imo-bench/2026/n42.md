For every positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$, $g(n)$ be the minimal positive integers such that
$$1+\frac{1}{1!}+\frac{1}{2!}+\dots +\frac{1}{n!}=\frac{f(n)}{g(n)}.$$ Is there a positive integer $n$ for which $g(n)>n^{0.999n}$?

Let $n>1$ be an integer. Prove that $2n-1$ is a prime number if and only if
for any $n$ pairwise distinct positive integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots ,
a_n$ there exist $i, j \in \{1, 2, \ldots , n\}$ such that
$$\frac{a_i+a_j}{\gcd(a_i,a_j)} \geq 2n-1.$$

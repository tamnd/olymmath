A quadruple $(a; b; c; d)$ of positive integers with $a \leq b \leq c \leq d$ is called good if we can colour each integer red, blue, green or purple, in such a way that $i$ of each $a$ consecutive integers at least one is coloured red; $ii$ of each $b$ consecutive integers at least one is coloured blue; $iii$ of each $c$ consecutive integers at least one is coloured green; $iiii$ of each $d$ consecutive integers at least one is coloured purple.
Determine all good quadruples with $a = 2.$

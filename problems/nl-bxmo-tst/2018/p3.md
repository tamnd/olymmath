Let $p$ be a prime number.
Prove that it is possible to choose a permutation $a_1, a_2,...,a_p$ of $1,2,...,p$ such that the numbers $a_1, a_1a_2, a_1a_2a_3,..., a_1a_2a_3...a_p$ all have different remainder upon division by $p$ .

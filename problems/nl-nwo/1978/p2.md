One tiles a floor of $a \times b$ d m $^2$ with square tiles, $a,b \in N$. Tiles do not overlap, and sides of floor and tiles are parallel. Using tiles of $2\times 2$ d m $^2$ leaves the same amount of floor uncovered as using tiles of $4\times 4$ d m $^2$. Using $3\times 3$ d m $^2$ tiles leaves $29$ d m $^2$ floor uncovered. Determine $a$ and $b$.

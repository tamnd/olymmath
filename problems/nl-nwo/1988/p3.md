For certain $a,b,c$ holds: $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{a+b+c}$ Prove that for all odd $n$ holds, $$ \frac{1}{a^n}+\frac{1}{b^n}+\frac{1}{c^n}=\frac{1}{a^n+b^n+c^n}. $$

<!-- S2 -->
Show that if $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{1}{a + b + c},$ then also $$\frac{1}{a^n} +\frac{1}{b^n} +\frac{1}{c^n} =\frac{1}{a^n + b^n + c^n},$$
provided $n$ is an odd positive integer.

The lines $AD , BE$ and $CF$ intersect in $S$ within a triangle $ABC$ .
It is given that $AS: DS = 3: 2$ and $BS: ES = 4: 3$ . Determine the ratio $CS: FS$ .

[asy]
unitsize (1 cm);

pair A, B, C, D, E, F, S;

A = (0,0);
B = (5,0);
C = (1,4);
S = (14*A + 15*B + 6*C)/35;
D = extension(A,S,B,C);
E = extension(B,S,C,A);
F = extension(C,S,A,B);

draw(A--B--C--cycle);
draw(A--D);
draw(B--E);
draw(C--F);

dot(" $A$ ", A, SW);
dot(" $B$ ", B, SE);
dot(" $C$ ", C, N);
dot(" $D$ ", D, NE);
dot(" $E$ ", E, W);
dot(" $F$ ", F, dir(270));
dot(" $S$ ", S, NE);
[/asy]

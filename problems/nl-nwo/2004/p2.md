Two circles $A$ and $B$ , both with radius $1$ , touch each other externally.
Four circles $P, Q, R$ and $S$ , all four with the same radius $r$ , lie such that $P$ externally touches on $A, B, Q$ and $S$ , $Q$ externally touches on $P, B$ and $R$ , $R$ externally touches on $A, B, Q$ and $S$ , $S$ externally touches on $P, A$ and $R$ .
Calculate the length of $r.$ [asy]
unitsize(0.3 cm);

pair A, B, P, Q, R, S;
real r = (3 + sqrt(17))/2;

A = (-1,0);
B = (1,0);
P = intersectionpoint(arc(A,r + 1,0,180), arc(B,r + 1,0,180));
R = -P;
Q = (r + 2,0);
S = (-r - 2,0);

draw(Circle(A,1));
draw(Circle(B,1));
draw(Circle(P,r));
draw(Circle(Q,r));
draw(Circle(R,r));
draw(Circle(S,r));

label(" $A$ ", A);
label(" $B$ ", B);
label(" $P$ ", P);
label(" $Q$ ", Q);
label(" $R$ ", R);
label(" $S$ ", S);
[/asy]

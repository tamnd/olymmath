Three circles $C_1,C_2,C_3$ , with radii $1, 2, 3$ respectively, are externally tangent.
In the area enclosed by these circles, there is a circle $C_4$ which is externally tangent to all three circles.
Find the radius of $C_4$ .

[asy]
unitsize(0.4 cm);

pair[] O;
real[] r;

O[1] = (-12/5,16/5);
r[1] = 1;
O[2] = (0,5);
r[2] = 2;
O[3] = (0,0);
r[3] = 3;
O[4] = (-132/115, 351/115);
r[4] = 6/23;

draw(Circle(O[1],r[1]));
draw(Circle(O[2],r[2]));
draw(Circle(O[3],r[3]));
draw(Circle(O[4],r[4]));

label(" $C_1$ ", O[1]);
label(" $C_2$ ", O[2]);
label(" $C_3$ ", O[3]);
[/asy]

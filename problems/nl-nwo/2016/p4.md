In the acute triangle $ABC$, the midpoint of side $BC$ is called $M$. Point $X$ lies on the angle bisector of $\angle AMB$ such that $\angle BXM = 90^\circ$. Point $Y$ lies on the angle bisector of $\angle AMC$ such that $\angle CYM = 90^\circ$. Line segments $AM$ and $XY$ intersect in point $Z$.

Prove that $Z$ is the midpoint of $XY$.

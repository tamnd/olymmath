In acute-angled triangle $ABC$ with $|BC| < |BA|$, point $N$ is the midpoint of $AC$. The circle with diameter $AB$ intersects the bisector of $\angle B$ in two points: $B$ and $X$.

Prove that $XN$ is parallel to $BC$.

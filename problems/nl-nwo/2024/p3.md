Given are two positive integers $a$ and $b$ with the property that

$$
\frac{b^3}{a^4}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\frac{a^3}{b^2}
$$

are both integers greater than $1$.

What is the smallest possible value for the sum $a+b$?

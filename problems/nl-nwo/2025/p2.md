Consider the equation

$$n!-(n-1)n=4p^2$$

where $n$ is a positive integer and $p$ is a prime number. (Here $n!$ represents the product of the numbers 1 to $n$. For example, $4!=1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4=24$.)
Find all solutions $(n,p)$.

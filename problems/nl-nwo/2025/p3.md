Amber has a square consisting of $10 \times 10$ square boxes. One at a time, she randomly chooses one of the hundred squares and fills it with a number. The number she puts in a square is always equal to the total number of squares already filled in that square’s row and column. So in the first square she chooses she writes a 0, in the second square a 0 or a 1, depending on whether it is in a different row and a different column or not, et cetera. So in each square there will be a number from 0 to 18 inclusive.
After Amber has written a number in each square, she adds up all the numbers. What is/are the possible result(s) of this addition?

Nikki is making a sequence of numbers. The first number is $13$. She creates each subsequent number by alternately concatenating $23$ and $27$ to the right hand side of the previous number in the sequence. So the sequence starts as follows:

$$
13,\quad 1323,\quad 132327,\quad 13232723,\quad 1323272327,\quad 132327232723,\quad \ldots
$$

Prove that $13$ is the only prime number in her sequence.

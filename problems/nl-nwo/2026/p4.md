In the trapezium $ABCD$, $AB$ is parallel to $CD$. We denote the midpoint of side $BC$ by $M$. We denote the point of intersection of $AM$ and $BD$ by $P$. It is also given that $\frac{|AB|}{|CD|}=\frac{|PD|}{|PB|}$. Calculate $\frac{|AB|}{|CD|}$.

The country of Islandia consists of $n\geq 4$ islands. There is a bridge between every two islands. The Ministry of Transport has issued instructions to paint every bridge red or blue, in accordance with the following rule: for any set of three islands, out of the three bridges between these islands an *odd* number must be painted blue. For all $n\geq 4$, determine, in terms of $n$, the number of ways to fulfil the instructions of the Ministry.

a) Find all primes $p, q, r$ and natural numbers $n \in \mathbf{N}$ such that $\frac1p + \frac1q + \frac1r = \frac1n$.

b) Find all integers $x, y, z \in \mathbf{Z}$ such that $x^3 + 5y^3 = 9z^3$.

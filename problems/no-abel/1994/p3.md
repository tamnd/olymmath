a) Let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{1994} > 0$ be real numbers. Prove that
$$\left(\frac{x_1}{x_2}\right)^{\frac{x_1}{x_2}} \left(\frac{x_2}{x_3}\right)^{\frac{x_2}{x_3}} \cdots \left(\frac{x_{1993}}{x_{1994}}\right)^{\frac{x_{1993}}{x_{1994}}} \ge \left(\frac{x_1}{x_2}\right)^{\frac{x_2}{x_1}} \left(\frac{x_2}{x_3}\right)^{\frac{x_3}{x_2}} \cdots \left(\frac{x_{1993}}{x_{1994}}\right)^{\frac{x_{1994}}{x_{1993}}}.$$

b) Prove that there exists no function $f : \mathbf{Z} \to \mathbf{Z}$ (both arguments and results are integers) such that $f(f(x)) = x + 1$ for all $x$.

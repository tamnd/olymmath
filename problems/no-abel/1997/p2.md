a) Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, and let $P$ be a point in its interior. Let $Q$, $R$ and $S$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ respectively. Show that the sum $PQ + PR + PS$ is independent of the choice of $P$. (5 points)

b) Let $A$, $B$ and $C$ be three different points on a circle such that $AB = AC$. Let $E$ be a point on the line segment $BC$, and let $D$ be the point of intersection between the circle and the extension of $AE$ ($D \ne A$). Show that the product $AE \cdot AD$ is independent of the choice of $E$. (5 points)

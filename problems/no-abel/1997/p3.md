a) Each subset of $97$ out of $1997$ given real numbers has positive sum. Show that the sum of all the $1997$ numbers is positive. (3 points)

b) In a school with $91$ students distributed in $3$ classes every student took part in a competition. For every sample of $6$ students of the same sex at least $2$ achieved the same number of points. Show that there are $4$ students who are in the same class, are of the same sex, and who achieved the same result in the competition. (7 points)

a) Determine a function $f(x)$ such that $f(t^2 + t + 1) = t$ for all real numbers $t \ge 0$.

b) Prove that for all real numbers $a, b, c, d$ and $e$, the ineuqality
$$a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2 + e^2 \ge a(b + c + d + e)$$
applies.

a) Find all integers $m$ and $n$ such that $2m^2 + n^2 = 2mn + 3n$.

b) Given positive integers $a$, $b$ and $c$ such that $a^3$ is divisible by $b$, $b^3$ is divisible by $c$, and $c^3$ is divisible by $a$, prove that $(a + b + c)^{13}$ is divisible by $abc$.

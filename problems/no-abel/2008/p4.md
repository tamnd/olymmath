Note that the two subproblems (a) and (b) are unrelated, and the triangles in these subproblems do not need to be the same.

(a) Three distinct points $A$, $B$, and $C$ lie on a circle with centre at $O$. The triangles $AOB$, $BOC$, and $COA$ have equal area. What are the possible magnitudes of the angles of the triangle $ABC$?

(b) A point $D$ lies on the side $BC$, and a point $E$ on the side $AC$, of the triangle $ABC$, and $BD$ and $AE$ have the same length. The line through the centres of the circumscribed circles of the triangles $ADC$ and $BEC$ crosses $AC$ in $K$ and $BC$ in $L$. Show that $KC$ and $LC$ have the same length.

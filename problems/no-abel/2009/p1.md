(a) Show that there exist infinitely many integers that cannot be written as the difference between two perfect squares.

(b) Show that the sum of three consecutive perfect cubes can always be written as the difference between two perfect squares.

(A perfect square is an integer raised to the second power. A perfect cube is an integer raised to the third power.)

(a) Show that $\left(\dfrac{2010}{2009}\right)^{2009} > 2$.

(b) Let $x = 1 - 2^{-2009}$. Show that $x + x^2 + x^4 + x^8 + \cdots + x^{2^m} < 2010$ for all positive integers $m$.

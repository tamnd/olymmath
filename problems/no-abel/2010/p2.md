(a) Show that $\dfrac{x^2}{1-x} + \dfrac{(1-x)^2}{x} \ge 1$ for all real numbers $x$, where $0 < x < 1$.

(b) Show that $abc \le (ab + bc + ca)(a^2 + b^2 + c^2)^2$ for all positive real numbers $a$, $b$ and $c$ such that $a + b + c = 1$.

(a) Find all positive integers $k$ and $l$ such that $k^2 - l^2 = 1005$.

(b) Let $n > 2$ be an integer. Show that it is possible to choose $n$ points in the plane, not all of them lying on the same line, such that the distance between any pair of points is an integer (that is, $\sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$ is an integer for all pairs $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ of points).

(a) The positive numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ satisfy $a_1 = 1$ and $(m+n)a_{m+n} \le a_m + a_n$ for all positive integers $m$ and $n$. Show that $1/a_{200} > 4 \cdot 10^7$.

(b) Find all functions $f$ from the real numbers to the real numbers such that $f(xy) \le \frac12\bigl(f(x) + f(y)\bigr)$ for all real numbers $x$ and $y$.

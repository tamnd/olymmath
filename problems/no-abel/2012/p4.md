(a) Two positive numbers $x$ and $y$ are given. Show that
$$\left(1 + \frac{x}{y}\right)^3 + \left(1 + \frac{y}{x}\right)^3 \ge 16.$$

(b) Positive numbers $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ are given so that
$$b_1 + b_2 + \cdots + b_n \le 10.$$
Further, $a_1 = b_1$ and $a_m = s a_{m-1} + b_m$ for $m > 1$, where $0 \le s < 1$. Show that
$$a_1^2 + a_2^2 + \cdots + a_n^2 \le \frac{100}{1 - s^2}.$$

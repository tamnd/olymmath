(a) An ordered quadruple $(P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4)$ of corners in a regular $2013$-gon is called crossing if the four corners are all different, and the line segment from $P_1$ to $P_2$ intersects the line segment from $P_3$ to $P_4$. How many crossing quadruples are there in the $2013$-gon?

(b) A total of $a \cdot b \cdot c$ cubical boxes are joined together in a $a \times b \times c$ rectangular stack, where $a, b, c \ge 2$. A bee is found inside one of the boxes. It can fly from one box to another through a hole in the wall, but not through edges or corners. Also, it cannot fly outside the stack. For which triples $(a, b, c)$ is it possible for the bee to fly through all of the boxes exactly once, and end up in the same box where it started?

(a) Find all triples $(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ satisfying the equations
$$\begin{aligned} x^2 + 4y^2 &= 4zx, \\ y^2 + 4z^2 &= 4xy, \\ z^2 + 4x^2 &= 4yz. \end{aligned}$$

(b) Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that
$$x^2 f\bigl(yf(x)\bigr) = y^2 f(x) f\bigl(f(x)\bigr)$$
for all real numbers $x$ and $y$.

A " walking sequence " is a sequence of integers with $a_{i+1} = a_i \pm 1$ for every $i$.Show that there exists a sequence $b_1, b_2, . . . , b_{2016}$ such that for every walking sequence $a_1, a_2, . . . , a_{2016}$ where $1 \leq a_i \leq1010$, there is for some $j$ for which $a_j = b_j$.

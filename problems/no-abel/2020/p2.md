(a) Find all natural numbers $k$ such that there exist natural numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{k+1}$ with
$$a_1! + a_2! + \cdots + a_{k+1}! = k!.$$
Note that we do not consider $0$ to be a natural number.

(b) Assume that $a$ and $b$ are natural numbers with $a \ge b$ so that $\sqrt{a + \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}}$ is a natural number. Show that $a$ and $b$ have the same parity.

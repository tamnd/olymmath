(a) The midpoint of the side $AB$ in the triangle $ABC$ is called $C'$. A point on the side $BC$ is called $D$, and $E$ is the point of intersection of $AD$ and $CC'$. Assume that $AE/ED = 2$. Show that $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$.

(b) The triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$. The centre of the circumcircle is called $O$, and the base point of the normal from $O$ to $AC$ is called $D$. The point $E$ lies on $AO$ with $AE = AD$. The angle bisector of $\angle CAO$ meets $CE$ in $Q$. The lines $BE$ and $OQ$ intersect in $F$. Show that the lines $CF$ and $OE$ are parallel.

(a) A tetrahedron $ABCD$ satisfies $\angle BAC = \angle CAD = \angle DAB = 90^\circ$. Show that the areas of its faces satisfy the equation
$$\operatorname{area}(BAC)^2 + \operatorname{area}(CAD)^2 + \operatorname{area}(DAB)^2 = \operatorname{area}(BCD)^2.$$

(b) The tangent at $C$ to the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ intersects the line through $A$ and $B$ in a point $D$. Two distinct points $E$ and $F$ on the line through $B$ and $C$ satisfy
$$|BE| = |BF| = \frac{\bigl||CD|^2 - |BD|^2\bigr|}{|BC|}.$$
Show that either $|ED| = |CD|$ or $|FD| = |CD|$.

(a) Determine all positive integers $n$ such that $2022 + 3^n$ is a perfect square.

(b) Find all primes $p$ and positive integers $n$ satisfying
$$n\,5^{n - n/p} = p!\,(p^2 + 1) + n$$
(note: $p! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot \cdots \cdot p$)

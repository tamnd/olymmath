(a) A triangle $ABC$ with circumcircle $\omega$ satisfies $|AB| > |AC|$. Points $X$ and $Y$ on $\omega$ are different from $A$, such that the line $AX$ passes through the midpoint of $BC$, $AY$ is perpendicular to $BC$, and $XY$ is parallel to $BC$. Find $\angle BAC$.

(b) Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ have pairwise parallel sides: $EF \parallel BC$, $FD \parallel CA$, and $DE \parallel AB$. The line $m_A$ is the reflection of $EF$ through $BC$, similarly $m_B$ is the reflection of $FD$ through $CA$, and $m_C$ the reflection of $DE$ through $AB$. Assume that the lines $m_A$, $m_B$ and $m_C$ meet in a common point. What is the ratio between the areas of triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$?

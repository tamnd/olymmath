(a) Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}^+ \to \mathbb{R}^+$ satisfying
$$f\left(\frac1x\right) \ge 1 - \frac{\sqrt{f(x)\,f\left(\frac1x\right)}}{x} \ge x^2 f(x)$$
for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^+$, where $\mathbb{R}^+$ is the set of positive real numbers.

(b) Do there exist $2022$ polynomials with real coefficients, each of degree equal to $2021$, so that the $2021 \cdot 2022 + 1$ coefficients in their product are equal?

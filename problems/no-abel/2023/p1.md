(a) In the triangle $ABC$, $X$ lies on the side $BC$, $Y$ on the side $CA$, and $Z$ on the side $AB$ with $YX \parallel AB$, $ZY \parallel BC$ and $XZ \parallel CA$. Show that $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ are the midpoints of the respective sides of $ABC$.

(b) In the triangle $ABC$, points $D$ and $E$ lie on the side $BC$, with $CE = BD$. $M$ is the midpoint of $AD$. Show that the centroid of $ABC$ lies on $ME$.

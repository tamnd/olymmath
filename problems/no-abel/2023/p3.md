(a) Find all nonnegative integers $n$, $a$, and $b$ satisfying
$$2^a + 5^b + 1 = n!\,.$$

(b) Find all integers $a$ and $b$ satisfying
$$\begin{aligned} a^6 + 1 &\mid b^{11} - 2023b^3 + 40b \quad \text{and} \\ a^4 - 1 &\mid b^{10} - 2023b^2 - 41. \end{aligned}$$
(Given integers $u$ and $v$, the notation $u \mid v$ means that there exists some integer $c$ so that $uc = v$.)

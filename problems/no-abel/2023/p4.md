(a) Assuming $a$, $b$, and $c$ are the side lengths of a triangle, show that
$$\frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{ab} + \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{bc} + \frac{c^2 + a^2 - b^2}{ca} > 2.$$
Also show that the inequality does not necessarily hold if you replace $2$ (on the right hand side) by a bigger number.

(b) Find all functions $f\colon \mathbb{R}_+ \to \mathbb{R}_+$ satisfying
$$f\bigl(f(x) + y\bigr) = f(y) + x \quad \text{for all } x, y \in \mathbb{R}_+.$$
(Here $\mathbb{R}_+$ is the set of all real numbers $x > 0$.)

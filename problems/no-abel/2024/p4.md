(a) The triangle $ABC$ with $AB < AC$ has an altitude $AD$. The points $E$ and $A$ lie on opposite sides of $BC$, with $E$ on the circumcircle of $ABC$. Furthermore, $AD = DE$ and $\angle ADO = \angle CDE$, where $O$ is the circumcentre of $ABC$. Determine $\angle BAC$.

(b) The pentagons $P_1P_2P_3P_4P_5$ and $I_1I_2I_3I_4I_5$ are cyclic, where $I_i$ is the incentre of the triangle $P_{i-1}P_iP_{i+1}$ (reckoned cyclically, that is $P_0 = P_5$ and $P_6 = P_1$). Show that the lines $P_1I_1$, $P_2I_2$, $P_3I_3$, $P_4I_4$, and $P_5I_5$ meet in a single point.

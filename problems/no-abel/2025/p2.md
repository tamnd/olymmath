(a) A teacher asks each of eleven pupils to write a positive integer with at most four digits, each on a separate yellow sticky note. Show that if all the numbers are different, the teacher can always select two or more of the eleven stickies so that the average of the numbers on the selected notes is not an integer.

(b) Which positive integers $a$ have the property that $n! - a$ is a perfect square for infinitely many positive integers $n$?

(a) In the triangle $ABC$, the foot of the altitude from $B$ is $E$, and the foot of the altitude from $C$ is $F$. The lines orthogonal to the line $EF$ through $B$ and $C$ intersect $EF$ in $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Show that $EP = FQ$.

(b) An acute triangle $ABC$ has circumcentre $O$. The lines $AO$ and $BC$ intersect in $D$, while $BO$ and $CA$ intersect in $E$, and $CO$ and $AB$ intersect in $F$. Show that, if the triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are similar (with vertices in that order), then $ABC$ is equilateral.

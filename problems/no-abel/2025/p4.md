(a) Find all polynomials $P$ with real coefficients satisfying
$$P\left(\frac{1}{1 + x}\right) = \frac{1}{1 + P(x)}$$
for all real numbers $x \ne -1$.

(b) Determine the largest real number $C$ such that
$$\frac1x + \frac1{2y} + \frac1{3z} \ge C$$
for all real numbers $x, y, z \ne 0$ satisfying the equation
$$\frac{x}{yz} + \frac{4y}{zx} + \frac{9z}{xy} = 24.$$

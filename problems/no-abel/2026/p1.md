(a) Determine all pairs of positive integers $a$, $b$ for which both $a^2 + 2b + 1$ and $b^2 + 2a + 1$ are perfect squares.

(b) Write $S(n) = 1^1 + 2^2 + \cdots + n^n$ for every positive integer $n$. Show that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that the first digit of $S(n)$ equals the first digit of $n$, while simultaneously the final digit of $S(n)$ equals the final digit of $n$.

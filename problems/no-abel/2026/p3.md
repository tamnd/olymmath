(a) Find all infinite sequences $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ of positive integers, such that for every positive integer $n$ there exists a positive integer $k$ satisfying
$$k = \frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{a_{n+k}}.$$

(b) Given $4052$ distinct non-zero integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2026}, b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_{2026}$, let $M$ be the number of pairs of indices $(i, j)$ such that $1 \le i < j \le 2026$ and
$$2(a_ib_j + a_jb_i)^2 = (a_i^2 + b_i^2)(a_j^2 + b_j^2).$$
What is the largest value of $M$, given all permitted choices of the $4052$ numbers?

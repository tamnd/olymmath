(a) Let $D$ be an interior point of the triangle $ABC$. Let $E$ be the point on the line segment $AC$ such that $\angle ADE = \angle ABC$, and $F$ be the point on the line segment $AB$ such that $\angle ADF = \angle ACB$. Show that the circumcircles of $ABC$ and $DEF$ are mutually tangent.

(b) Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $E$ and $F$ be the points satisfying
$$\angle BCE = \angle EAC = \angle CBF = \angle FAB = 90^\circ.$$
Let $D$ be the point of intersection of the lines $BC$ and $EF$. Show that the line $AD$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$.

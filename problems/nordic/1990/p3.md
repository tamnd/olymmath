Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $P$ be an interior point of $A B C$. We assume that a line $l$, which passes through $P$, but not through $A$, intersects $A B$ and $A C$ (or their extensions over $B$ or $C$ ) at $Q$ and $R$, respectively. Find $l$ such that the perimeter of the triangle $A Q R$ is as small as possible.

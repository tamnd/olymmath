Find all solutions of the system of equations

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
s(x)+s(y) & =x \\
x+y+s(z) & =z \\
s(x)+s(y)+s(z) & =y-4
\end{aligned}\right.
$$

where $x, y$, and $z$ are positive integers, and $s(x), s(y)$, and $s(z)$ are the numbers of digits in the decimal representations of $x, y$, and $z$, respectively.

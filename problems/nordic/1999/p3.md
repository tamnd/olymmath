The infinite integer plane $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}=\mathbb{Z}^{2}$ consists of all number pairs $(x, y)$, where $x$ and $y$ are integers. Let $a$ and $b$ be non-negative integers. We call any move from a point $(x, y)$ to any of the points $(x \pm a, y \pm b)$ or $(x \pm b, y \pm a) a(a, b)$-knight move. Determine all numbers $a$ and $b$, for which it is possible to reach all points of the integer plane from an arbitrary starting point using only $(a, b)$-knight moves.

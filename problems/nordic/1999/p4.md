Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers and $n \geq 1$. Show that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& n\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right) \\
& \geq\left(\frac{1}{1+a_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{1+a_{n}}\right)\left(n+\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

When does equality hold?

Let $A B C$ be a triangle and let $D$ and $E$ be points on $B C$ and $C A$, respectively, such that $A D$ and $B E$ are angle bisectors of $A B C$. Let $F$ and $G$ be points on the circumcircle of $A B C$ such that $A F$ and $D E$ are parallel and $F G$ and $B C$ are parallel. Show that

$$
\frac{A G}{B G}=\frac{A C+B C}{A B+C B}
$$

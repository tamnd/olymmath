The real numbers $a, b, c$ are such that $a^{2}+b^{2}=2 c^{2}$, and also such that $a \neq b, c \neq-a, c \neq-b$. Show that

$$
\frac{(a+b+2 c)\left(2 a^{2}-b^{2}-c^{2}\right)}{(a-b)(a+c)(b+c)}
$$

is an integer.

Let $n$ be a positive integer greater than 2. Find all functions $f : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ satisfying

$$\big(f(x + y)\big)^n = f(x^n) + f(y^n), \quad \text{for all integers } x, y.$$

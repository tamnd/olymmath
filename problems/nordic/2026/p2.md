Consider the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} x^2 = y + 1, \\ xy = x + y. \end{cases}$$

Show that if $(x, y) = (x_0, y_0)$, where $x_0$ and $y_0$ are real numbers, is a solution to the above system of equations, then there is also a solution $(x, y) = (x_1, y_1)$, where $x_1$ and $y_1$ are real numbers and $y_1 x_0 = 1$.

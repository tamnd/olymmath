Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral such that $BA = BC$. The internal angle bisectors of $\angle DBA$ and $\angle CBD$ intersect the perpendicular bisectors of $AD$ and $CD$ in $E$ and $F$, respectively.

Prove that the circumcircles of $\triangle DAC$ and $\triangle DEF$ are tangent.

*Note: A convex quadrilatral is a quadrilateral where all angles are smaller than $180^\circ$.*

*Note: The internal angle bisector of an angle is the line segment that divides the angle into two equal parts.*

Suppose that $x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},\ldots x_{n}$ are real numbers between 0 and 1 with sum $s$ . Prove that

$$\sum_{i = 1}^{n}{\frac{x_{i}}{s + 1 - x_{i}}} + \prod_{i = 1}^{n}(1 - x_{i})\leq 1.$$

a) Determine if there are matrices $A,B,C\in\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})$ such that $A^2+B^2=C^2$.

b) Determine if there are matrices $A,B,C\in\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})$ such that $A^4+B^4=C^4$.

Note: The notation $A\in \mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})$ means that $A$ is a $2\times 2$ matrix with integer entries and $\det A=1$.

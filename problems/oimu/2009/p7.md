Let $G$ be a group such that every subgroup of $G$ is subnormal. Suppose that there exists $N$ normal subgroup of $G$ such that $Z(N)$ is nontrivial and $G/N$ is cyclic. Prove that $Z(G)$ is nontrivial. ( $Z(G)$ denotes the center of $G$ ).**Note**: A subgroup $H$ of $G$ is subnormal if there exist subgroups $H_1,H_2,\ldots,H_m=G$ of $G$ such that $H\lhd H_1\lhd H_2 \lhd \ldots \lhd H_m= G$ ( $\lhd$ denotes normal subgroup).

Let $a$ , $b$ and $c$ be real numbers such that $a^2+b^2=c^2$ , solve the system:
$$ z^2=x^2+y^2 $$
$$ (z+c)^2=(x+a)^2+(y+b)^2 $$
in real numbers $x, y$ and $z$ .

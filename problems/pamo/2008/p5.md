A set of positive integers $X$ is called *connected* if $|X|\ge 2$ and there exist two distinct elements $m$ and $n$ of $X$ such that $m$ is a divisor of $n$ .
Determine the number of connected subsets of the set $\{1,2,\ldots,10\}$ .

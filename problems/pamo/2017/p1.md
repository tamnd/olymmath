We consider the real sequence $(x_n)$ defined by $x_0=0, x_1=1$ and $x_{n+2}=3x_{n+1}-2x_n$ for $n=0,1,...$ We define the sequence $(y_n)$ by $y_n=x_n^2+2^{n+2}$ for every non negative integer $n$ .
Prove that for every $n>0$ , $y_n$ is the square of an odd integer

Let $ \mathbb{R} $ denote the set of real numbers. Find all functions $ f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} $ such that

$$
f(x^2) - y f(y) = f(x+y)(f(x) - y)
$$

for all real numbers $ x $ and $ y $.

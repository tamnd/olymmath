A $2025 \times 2025$ grid is filled with the integers $1, 2, \ldots, 2025^2$ in no particular order, each used exactly once. Mpho chooses a cell and places a token on it. He then repeatedly moves the token to another cell of the grid subject to the following rules: the destination cell lies inside the $7 \times 7$ square centred at the token's current cell, and the number in the destination cell is larger than the number in the current cell. Determine the greatest integer $M$ such that, no matter how the integers are arranged, Mpho can always make at least $M$ moves.

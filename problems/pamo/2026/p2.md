Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle with orthocenter $H$ and $AD$ $BE$ $CF$ be its altitudes.
Let $G$ be the intersection of $EF$ and $AD$ and $I$ be the antipode of A with respect to $(ABC)$. Let $J$ be the intersection of $AI$ and $BC$.

Show that $HI$ and $GJ$ are parallel.

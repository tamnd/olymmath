Find all polynomials $P(x)$ with integers coefficients such that:

- $P(n)>0$ for all positive integers $n$
- for all positive integers $n , k$ and primes $p$ that divide $3^n-2^n$ we have:
  $p^k \mid 3^n - 2^n \iff p^k \mid 3^{P(n)} - 2^{P(n)}$

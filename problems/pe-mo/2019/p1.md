Determine for what $n\ge 3$ integer numbers, it is possible to find positive integer numbers $a_1 < a_2 < ...< a_n$ such $\frac{1}{a_1}+\frac{1}{a_2}+...+\frac{1}{a_n}=1$ and $a_1 a_2\cdot\cdot\cdot a_n$ is a perfect square.

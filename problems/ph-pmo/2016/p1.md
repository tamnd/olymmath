The operations below can be applied on any expression of the form $ax^2+bx+c$. $(\text{I})$ If $c \neq 0$, replace $a$ by $4a-\frac{3}{c}$ and $c$ by $\frac{c}{4}$. $(\text{II})$ If $a \neq 0$, replace $a$ by $-\frac{a}{2}$ and $c$ by $-2c+\frac{3}{a}$. $(\text{III}_t)$ Replace $x$ by $x-t$, where $t$ is an integer. (Different values of $t$ can be used.)

Is it possible to transform $x^2-x-6$ into each of the following by applying some sequence of the above operations? $(\text{a})$ $5x^2+5x-1$ $(\text{b})$ $x^2+6x+2$

Let $AG$ intersect $BD$ at $H$, let $DG$ intersect $AC$ at $I$, and let $EG$ intersect $AD$ at $J$. If $FHGI$ is cyclic and $$JA \cdot FC \cdot GH = JD \cdot FB \cdot GI,$$ prove that $G$, $F$ and $E$ are collinear.

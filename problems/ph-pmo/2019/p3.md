Find all triples $(a, b, c)$ of positive integers such that $a^2 + b^2 = n\cdot lcm(a, b) + n^2$ $b^2 + c^2 = n \cdot lcm(b, c) + n^2$ $c^2 + a^2 = n \cdot lcm(c, a) + n^2$ for some positive integer $n$ .

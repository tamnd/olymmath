Let $n$ be a positive integer. For any $\mathcal{S}\subseteq\{1,2,\cdots,n\}$, let $f(\mathcal{S})$ be the set containing all positive integers at most $n$ that have an odd number of factors in $\mathcal{S}$. How many subsets of $\{1,2,\cdots,n\}$ can be turned into $\{1\}$ after finitely many (possibly zero) applications of $f$?

For a real number $x$, let $\lfloor x\rfloor$ be the greatest integer not exceeding $x$. The sequence $\{a_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ of real numbers is defined as follows:
$$a_1=1,\ \text{and}\ a_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2\lfloor a_n\rfloor-a_n+1}\ \text{for all}\ n\ge 1.$$
Find, with proof, the value of $a_{2024}$.

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocenter $H$, circumcenter $O$, and circumcircle $\Omega$. Points $E$ and $F$ are the feet of the altitudes from $B$ to $AC$ and $C$ to $AB$, respectively. Let line $AH$ intersect $\Omega$ again at point $D\ne A$. The circumcircle of $DEF$ intersects $\Omega$ again at $X$, and $AX$ intersects $BC$ at $I$. The circumcircle of triangle $IEF$ intersects $BC$ again at $G$. If $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, prove that lines $MX$ and $OG$ intersect at a point on $\Omega$.

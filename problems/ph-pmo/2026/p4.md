Let $\mathbb{N}_0$ be the set of nonnegative integers. Find all nondecreasing functions $f:\mathbb{R}^+\to\mathbb{N}_0$ such that
$$f(x)=f\left(\frac{x}{n}\right)+f\left(\frac{x+1}{n}\right)+f\left(\frac{x+2}{n}\right)+\cdots+f\left(\frac{x+n-1}{n}\right)$$
for all positive real numbers $x$ and positive integers $n$.

(A function $f$ is *nondecreasing* if $f(x)\le f(y)$ for all $x$ and $y$ in the domain such that $x\le y$.)

Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $k$ satisfying the following property: there exist positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, not all equal, such that
$$\frac{1}{a_1(a_1+1)}+\frac{1}{a_2(a_2+1)}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_k(a_k+1)}=1.$$

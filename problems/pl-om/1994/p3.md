$k$ is a fixed positive integer. Let $a_n$ be the number of maps $f$ from the subsets of $\{1, 2, ... , n\}$ to $\{1, 2, ... , k\}$ such that for all subsets $A, B$ of $\{1, 2, ... , n\}$ we have $f(A \cap B) = \min (f(A), f(B))$ . Find $\lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{a_n}$ .

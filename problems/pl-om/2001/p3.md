A sequence $x_0=A$ and $x_1=B$ and $x_{n+2}=x_{n+1} +x_n$, where $A$ and $B$ are real numbers, is called a Fibonacci type sequence. Call a number $c$ a repeated value if $x_t=x_s=c$ for $t$ different from $s$ .
Prove one can choose $A$ and $B$ so that the sequence has more than $2000$ repeated values, but one cannot choose them so that it has infinitely many repeated values.

In a tournament, $n$ players participate. Each player plays each other exactly once, with no ties. A player $A$ is said to be *champion* if, for every other player $B$ , one of the following two situations occurs:
(a) $A$ beat $B$ ;
(b) $A$ beat a player $C$ who in turn beat $B$ .
Prove that in such a tournament there cannot be exactly two champions.

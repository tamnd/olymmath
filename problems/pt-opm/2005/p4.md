A natural number $n$ is said to be *abundant* if the sum of its divisors is greater than $2n$ . For example, $18$ is abundant because the sum of its divisors, $1 + 2 + 3 + 6 + 9 + 18$ , is greater than $36$ . Write every even number greater than $46$ as a sum of two numbers abundant.

How many pairs of 2007-digit numbers $a_1a_2\cdots a_{2007}$ and $b_1b_2\cdots b_{2007}$ are there such that $a_1b_1+a_2b_2+\cdots a_{2007}b_{2007}$ is even? Express your answer as $a*b^c+d*e^f$ for integers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, $e$, and $f$ with $a\nmid b$ and $d\nmid e$.

For how many permutations $(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{2007})$ of the integers from 1 to 2007 is there exactly one $i$ between 1 and 2006 such that $a_i>a_{i+1}$? Express your answer as $a*b^c+d*e^f$ for integers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, $e$, and $f$ with $a\nmid b$ and $d\nmid e$.

Triangle $ABC$ has $AC=3$, $BC=5$, $AB=7$. A circle is drawn internally tangent to the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $C$, and tangent to $AB$. Let $D$ be its point of tangency with $AB$. Find $BD-DA$.

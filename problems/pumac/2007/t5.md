Integers $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{100}$ satisfy
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{x_1}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{x_2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sqrt{x_{100}}}=20.$$
Find $\prod_{i\ne j}(x_i-x_j)$.

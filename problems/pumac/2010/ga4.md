In regular hexagon $ABCDEF$, $AC$, $CE$ are two diagonals. Points $M$, $N$ are on $AC$, $CE$ respectively and satisfy $AC:AM=CE:CN=r$. Suppose $B$, $M$, $N$ are collinear, find $100r^2$.

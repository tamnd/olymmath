Unit square $ABCD$ is divided into four rectangles by $EF$ and $GH$, with $BF=\frac{1}{4}$. $EF$ is parallel to $AB$ and $GH$ parallel to $BC$. $EF$ and $GH$ meet at point $P$. Suppose $BF+DH=FH$, calculate the nearest integer to the degree of $\angle FAH$.

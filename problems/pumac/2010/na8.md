A consecutive pythagorean triple is a pythagorean triple of the form $a^2 + (a+1)^2 = b^2$ , $a$ and $b$ positive integers. Given that $a$ , $a+1$ , and $b$ form the third consecutive pythagorean triple, find $a$ .

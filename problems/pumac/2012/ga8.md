Cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ has side lengths $AB = 2, BC = 3, CD = 5, AD = 4$ .
Find $\sin A \sin B(\cot A/2 + \cot B/2 + \cot C/2 + \cot D/2)^2$ .
Your answer can be written in simplest form as $a/b$ . Find $a + b$ .

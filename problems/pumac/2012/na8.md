Find the largest possible sum $m+n$ for positive integers $m,n\le 100$ such that $m+1\equiv 3\pmod 4$ and there exists a prime number $p$ and nonnegative integer $a$ such $\frac{m^{2^n-1}-1}{m-1}=m^n+p^a$.

There is a sequence with $a(2)=0$, $a(3)=1$ and $a(n)=a\left(\left\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor\right)+a\left(\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil\right)$ for $n\ge 4$. Find $a(2014)$. [Note that $\left\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor$ and $\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil$ denote the floor function (largest integer $\le\frac{n}{2}$) and the ceiling function (smallest integer $\ge\frac{n}{2}$), respectively.]

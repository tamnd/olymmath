Real numbers $x,y,z$ satisfy the following equality:
$$4(x+y+z)=x^2+y^2+z^2$$
Let $M$ be the maximum of $xy+yz+zx$, and let $m$ be the minimum of $xy+yz+zx$. Find $M+10m$.

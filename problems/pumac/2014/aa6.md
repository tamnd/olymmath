Given that $x_{n+2}=\dfrac{20x_{n+1}}{14x_n}$, $x_0=25$, $x_1=11$, it follows that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x_{3n}}{2^n}=\frac{p}{q}$ for some positive integers $p,q$ with $GCD(p,q)=1$. Find $p+q$.

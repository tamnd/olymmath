$x,y,z$ are positive real numbers that satisfy $x^3+2y^3+6z^3=1$. Let $k$ be the maximum possible value of $2x+y+3z$. Let $n$ be the smallest positive integer such that $k^n$ is an integer. Find the value of $k^n+n$.

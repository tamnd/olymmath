Let $O$ be the center of a circle of radius $26$, and let $A,B$ be two distinct point on the circle, with $M$ being the midpoint of $AB$. Consider point $C$ for which $CO=34$ and $\angle COM=15^o$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of $CO$. Suppose that $\angle ACB=90^o$. Find $MN$.

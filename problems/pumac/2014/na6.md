Given $S=\{2,5,8,11,14,17,20\ldots\}$. Given that one can choose $n$ different numbers from $S$, $\{A_1,A_2,\ldots A_n\}$, s.t. $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{A_i}=1$. Find the minimum possible value of n.

Find all number sets $(a,b,c,d)$ s.t. $1<a\le b\le c\le d$, $a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{N}$, and $a^2+b+c+d$, $a+b^2+c+d$, $a+b+c^2+d$ and $a+b+c+d^2$ are all square numbers. Sum the value of $d$ across all solution set(s).

A gambler has \$25 and each turn, if the gambler has a positive amount of money, a fair coin is flipped and if it is heads, the gambler gains a dollar and if it is tails, the gambler loses a dollar. But, if the gambler has no money, he will automatically be given a dollar (which counts as a turn). What is the expected number of turns for the gambler to double his money?

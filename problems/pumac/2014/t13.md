There is a right triangle $\triangle ABC$, in which $\angle A$ is the right angle. On side $AB$, there are three points $X,Y$, and $Z$ that satisfy $\angle ACX=\angle XCY=\angle YCZ=\angle ZCB$ and $BZ=2AX$. The smallest angle of $\triangle ABC$ is $\frac{a}{b}$ degrees, where $a,b$ are positive integers such that $GCD(a,b)=1$. Find $a+b$.

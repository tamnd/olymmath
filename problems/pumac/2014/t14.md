Define function $f_k(x)$ (where $k$ is a positive integer) as follows:
$$f_k(x)=(\cos kx)(\cos x)^k+(\sin kx)(\sin x)^k-(\cos 2x)^k$$
Find the sum of all distinct value(s) of $k$ such that $f_k(x)$ is a constant function.

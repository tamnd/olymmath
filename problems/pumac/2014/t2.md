Given a Pacman of radius $1$, and mouth opening angle $90^o$, what is the largest (circular) pellet it can eat? The pellet must lie entirely outside the yellow portion and entirely inside the circumcircle of the Pacman. Let the radius be equal to $a\sqrt{b}+c$ where $b$ is square free. Find $a+b+c$.

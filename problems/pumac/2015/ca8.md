In a tournament with $2015$ teams, each team plays every other team exactly once and no ties occur. Such a tournament is *imbalanced*if for every group of $6$ teams, there exists either a team that wins against the other $5$ or a team that loses to the other $5$ . If the teams are indistinguishable, what is the number of distinct imbalanced tournaments that can occur?

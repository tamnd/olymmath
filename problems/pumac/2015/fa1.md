Alice is placing bishops on a 2015-by-2015 chessboard such that no two can attack one another. (Bishops attack each other if they are on a diagonal.) Her friend Bob notices that he is not able to place down a larger number of bishops such that any two still cannot attack one another. If there are $\prod p_i^{\alpha_i}$, with $\alpha_i>0$ and $p_i>0$ prime for all $i$, ways Alice could have placed her bishops, find $\sum p_i+\alpha_i$.

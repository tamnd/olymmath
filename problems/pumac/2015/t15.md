Let $S$ be the set of ordered integer pairs $(x, y)$ such that $0 < x < y < 42$ and there exists some integer $n$ such that $x^6-y^6 \mid n^2+2015^2$ . What is the sum $\sum_{(x_i, y_i) \in S}x_iy_i$ ?

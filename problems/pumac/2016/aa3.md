For positive real numbers $x$ and $y$, let $f(x,y)=x^{\log_2 y}$. The sum of the solutions to the equation
$$4096f(f(x,x),x)=x^{13}$$
can be written in simplest form as $\frac{m}{n}$. Compute $m+n$.

The George Washington Bridge is $2016$ meters long. Sally is standing on the George Washington Bridge, $1010$ meters from its left end. Each step, she either moves $1$ meter to the left or $1$ meter to the right, each with probability $\frac{1}{2}$. What is the expected number of steps she will take to reach an end of the bridge?

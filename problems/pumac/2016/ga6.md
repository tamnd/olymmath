In isosceles triangle $ABC$ with base $BC$, let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Let $P$ be the intersection of the circumcircle of $\triangle ACM$ with the circle with center $B$ passing through $M$, such that $P\ne M$. If $\angle BPC=135^\circ$, then $\frac{CP}{AP}$ can be written as $a+\sqrt{b}$ for positive integers $a$ and $b$, where $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b$.

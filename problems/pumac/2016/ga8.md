Let $\triangle ABC$ have side lengths $AB=4$, $BC=6$, $CA=5$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ and let $P$ be the point on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ such that $\angle MPA=90^\circ$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $B$ to $AC$, and let $E$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. Let $PD$ and $PE$ intersect line $BC$ at $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Compute the square of the area of $\triangle AXY$.

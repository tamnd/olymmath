Let $k=2^6\cdot 3^5\cdot 5^2\cdot 7^3\cdot 53$. Let $S$ be the sum of $\frac{\gcd(m,n)}{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}$ over all ordered pairs of positive integers $(m,n)$ where $mn=k$. If $S$ can be written in simplest form as $\frac{r}{s}$, compute $r+s$.

Let $n=2^8\cdot 3^9\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 7^{11}$. For $k$ a positive integer, let $f(k)$ be the number of integers $0\le x<n$ such that $x^2\equiv k^2\pmod{n}$. Compute the number of positive integers $k$ such that $k\mid f(k)$.

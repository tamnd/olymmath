Suppose $P(x)=x^{2016}+a_{2015}x^{2015}+\ldots+a_1x+a_0$ satisfies
$$P(x)P(2x+1)=P(-x)P(-2x-1)$$
for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. Find the sum of all possible values of $a_{2015}$.

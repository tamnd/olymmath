In triangle $ABC$, let $S$ be on $BC$ and $T$ be on $AC$ so that $AS\perp BC$ and $BT\perp AC$, and let $AS$ and $BT$ intersect at $H$. Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of $\triangle AHT$, $P$ be the center of the circumcircle of $\triangle BHS$, and $G$ be the other point of intersection (besides $H$) of the two circles. Let $GH$ and $OP$ intersect at $X$. If $AB=14$, $BH=6$, and $HA=11$, then $XO-XP$ can be written in simplest form as $\frac{m}{n}$. Find $m+n$.

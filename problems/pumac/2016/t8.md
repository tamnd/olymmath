Alice has $100$ balls and $10$ buckets. She takes each ball and puts it in a bucket that she chooses at random. After she is done, let $b_i$ be the number of balls in the $i$th bucket, for $1\le i\le 10$. Compute the expected value of $\sum_{i=1}^{10}b_i^2$.

Compute the number of ordered pairs of integers $(a, b)$ , where $0 \le a < 17$ and $0 \le b < 17$ , such that $y^2 \equiv x^3 +ax +b \pmod{17}$ has an even number of solutions $(x, y)$ , where $0 \le x < 17$ and $0 \le y < 17$ are integers.

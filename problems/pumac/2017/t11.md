For a sequence of $10$ coin flips, each pair of consecutive flips and count the number of "Heads-Heads", "Heads-Tails", "Tails-Heads", and "Tails-Tails" sequences is recorded. These four numbers are then multiplied to get the *Tiger number* of the sequence of flips. How many such sequences have a *Tiger number* of $24$ ?

Call a positive integer $n$ *tubular* if for any two distinct primes $p$ and $q$ dividing $n, (p + q) | n$ . Find the number of tubular numbers less than $100,000$ . (Integer powers of primes, including $1, 3$ , and $16$ , are not considered *tubular*.)

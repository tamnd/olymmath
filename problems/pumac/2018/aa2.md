If $a_1,a_2,\ldots$ is a sequence of real numbers such that for all $n$,
$$\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k\left(\frac{k}{n}\right)^2=1,$$
find the smallest $n$ such that $a_n<\frac{1}{2018}$.

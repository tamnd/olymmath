Let $x_0,x_1,\ldots$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $x_n=\frac{1+x_{n-1}}{x_{n-2}}$ for $n\ge 2$. Find the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(x_0,x_1)$ such that the sequence gives $x_{2018}=\frac{1}{1000}$.

Suppose real numbers $a,b,c,d$ satisfy $a+b+c+d=17$ and $ab+bc+cd+da=46$. If the minimum possible value of $a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$ can be expressed as a rational number $\frac{p}{q}$ in simplest form, find $p+q$.

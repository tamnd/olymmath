For $k\in\{0,1,\ldots,9\}$, let $\epsilon_k\in\{-1,1\}$. If the minimum possible value of $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{9}\sum_{j=0}^{i-1}\epsilon_i\epsilon_j2^{i+j}$ is $m$, find $|m|$.

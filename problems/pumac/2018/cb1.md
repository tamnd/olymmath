You have four fair $6$-sided dice, each numbered from $1$ to $6$ (inclusive). If all four dice are rolled, the probability that the product of the rolled numbers is prime can be written as $\frac{a}{b}$, where $a,b$ are relatively prime. What is $a+b$?

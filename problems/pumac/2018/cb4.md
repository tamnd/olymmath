Let $N$ be the number of sequences of natural numbers $d_1,d_2,\ldots,d_{10}$ such that the following conditions hold: $d_1\mid d_2,\ldots,d_9\mid d_{10}$ and $d_{10}\mid 6^{2018}$. Evaluate the remainder when $N$ is divided by $2017$.

Triangle $ABC$ has $\angle A=90^\circ$, $\angle C=30^\circ$, and $AC=12$. Let the circumcircle of this triangle be $W$. Define $D$ to be the point on arc $BC$ not containing $A$ so that $\angle CAD=60^\circ$. Define points $E$ and $F$ to be the foots of the perpendiculars from $D$ to lines $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $J$ be the intersection of line $EF$ with $W$, where $J$ is on the minor arc $AC$. The line $DF$ intersects $W$ at $H$ other than at $D$. The area of the triangle $FHJ$ is in the form $\frac{a}{b}(\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{d})$ for positive integers $a,b,c,d$, where $a,b$ are relatively prime, and the sum of $a,b,c,d$ is minimal. Find $a+b+c+d$.

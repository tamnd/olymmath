Let $\omega$ be a circle. Let $E$ be on $\omega$ and $S$ be outside $\omega$ such that line segment $SE$ is tangent to $\omega$. Let $R$ be on $\omega$. Let line $SR$ intersect $\omega$ at $B$ other than $R$, such that $R$ is between $S$ and $B$. Let $I$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\angle ESR$ with the line tangent to $\omega$ at $R$; let $A$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\angle ESR$ with $ER$. If the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle EIA$ is $10$, the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle SAB$ is $14$, and $SA=18$, then $IA$ can be expressed in simplest form as $\frac{m}{n}$. Find $m+n$.

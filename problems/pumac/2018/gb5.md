Consider rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB=30$ and $BC=60$. Construct circle $T$ whose diameter is $AD$. Construct circle $S$ whose diameter is $AB$. Let circles $T$ and $S$ intersect at $P$, so that $P\ne A$. Let $AP$ intersect $BC$ at $E$. Let $F$ be the point on $AB$ so that $EF$ is tangent to the circle with diameter $AD$. Find the area of triangle $AEF$.

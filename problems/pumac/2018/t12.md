In right triangle $\triangle ABC$, a square $WXYZ$ is inscribed such that vertices $W$ and $X$ lie on hypotenuse $AB$, vertex $Y$ lies on leg $BC$, and vertex $Z$ lies on leg $CA$. Let $AY$ and $BZ$ intersect at some point $P$. If the length of each side of square $WXYZ$ is $4$, the length of the hypotenuse $AB$ is $60$, and the distance between point $P$ and point $G$, where $G$ denotes the centroid of the triangle, is $\frac{a}{b}$, compute the value of $a+b$.

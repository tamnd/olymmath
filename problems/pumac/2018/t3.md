The value of
$$\frac{\log_3 5\log_2 5}{\log_3 5+\log_2 5}$$
can be expressed as $a\log_b c$, where $a,b$, and $c$ are positive integers, and $a+b$ is as small as possible. Find $a+2b+3c$.

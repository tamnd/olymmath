Let triangle $\triangle MNP$ have side lengths $MN=13$, $NP=89$, and $PM=100$. Define points $S,R,B$ as midpoints of $MN,NP,PM$ respectively. A line $\ell$ cuts lines $MN,NP,PM$ in points $I,J,A$, respectively. Find the minimum value of $(SI+RJ+BA)^2$.

Let $\omega=e^{\frac{2\pi i}{2017}}$ and $\zeta=e^{\frac{2\pi i}{2019}}$. Let $S=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb{Z}\mid 0\le a\le 2016,\ 0\le b\le 2018,\ (a,b)\ne(0,0)\}$. Compute $\displaystyle\prod_{(a,b)\in S}(\omega^a-\zeta^b)$.

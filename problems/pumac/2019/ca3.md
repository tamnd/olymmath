Marko lives on the origin of the Cartesian plane. Every second, Marko moves $1$ unit up with probability $2/9$, $1$ unit right with probability $2/9$, $1$ unit up and $1$ unit right with probability $4/9$, and he doesn't move with probability $1/9$. After $2019$ seconds, Marko ends up on the point $(A,B)$. What is the expected value of $A\cdot B$?

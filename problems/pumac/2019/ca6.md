The Nationwide Basketball Society (NBS) has $8001$ teams, numbered $2000$ through $10000$. For each $n$, team $n$ has $n+1$ players, and in a sheer coincidence, this year each player attempted $n$ shots and on team $n$, exactly one player made $0$ shots, one player made $1$ shot, $\ldots$, one player made $n$ shots. A player's field goal percentage is defined as the percentage of shots that the player made, rounded to the nearest tenth of a percent. (For instance, $32.45\%$ rounds to $32.5\%$.) A player in the NBS is randomly selected among those whose field goal percentage is $66.6\%$. If this player plays for team $k$, what is the probability that $k\ge 6000$?

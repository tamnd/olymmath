Let $\Delta ABC$ be a triangle with circumcenter $O$ and orthocenter $H$. Let $D$ be a point on the circumcircle of $ABC$ such that $AD\perp BC$. Suppose that $AB=6$, $DB=2$, and the ratio $\frac{\operatorname{area}(\Delta ABC)}{\operatorname{area}(\Delta HBC)}=5$. Then, if $OA$ is the length of the circumradius, then $OA^2$ can be written in the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m,n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m+n$.

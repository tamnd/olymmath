Suppose we choose two numbers $x,y\in[0,1]$ uniformly at random. If the probability that the circle with center $(x,y)$ and radius $|x-y|$ lies entirely within the unit square $[0,1]\times[0,1]$ is written as $\frac{p}{q}$ with $p$ and $q$ relatively prime nonnegative integers, then what is $p^2+q^2$?

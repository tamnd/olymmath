Let $BC=6$, $BX=3$, $CX=5$, and let $F$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Let $AX\perp BC$ and $AF=\sqrt{247}$. If $AC$ is of the form $\sqrt{b}$ and $AB$ is of the form $\sqrt{c}$ where $b$ and $c$ are nonnegative integers, find $2c+3b$.

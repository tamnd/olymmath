Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid such that $AB\parallel CD$ and let $P=AC\cap BD$, $AB=21$, $CD=7$, $AD=13$, $[ABCD]=168$. Let the line parallel to $AB$ trough $P$ intersect circumcircle of $BCP$ in $X$. Circumcircles of $BCP$ and $APD$ intersect at $P,Y$. Let $XY\cap BC=Z$. If $\angle ADC$ is obtuse, then $BZ=\frac{a}{b}$, where $a,b$ are coprime positive integers. Compute $a+b$.

Let $\gamma$ and $\Gamma$ be two circles such that $\gamma$ is internally tangent to $\Gamma$ at a point $X$. Let $P$ be a point on the common tangent of $\gamma$ and $\Gamma$ and $Y$ be the point on $\gamma$ other than $X$ such that $PY$ is tangent to $\gamma$ at $Y$. Let $PY$ intersect $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $B$, such that $A$ is in between $P$ and $B$ and let the tangents to $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $B$ intersect at $C$. $CX$ intersects $\Gamma$ again at $Z$ and $ZY$ intersects $\Gamma$ again at $Q$. If $AQ=6$, $AB=10$ and $\frac{AX}{XB}=\frac{1}{4}$. The length of $QZ=\frac{p}{q}\sqrt{r}$, where $p$ and $q$ are coprime positive integers, and $r$ is square free positive integer. Find $p+q+r$.

Suppose we have a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ such that $\angle B=110^\circ$ and the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ has a center at $D$. Find the measure, in degrees, of $\angle D$.

*Note:* The circumcircle of a $\Delta ABC$ is the unique circle containing $A$, $B$ and $C$.

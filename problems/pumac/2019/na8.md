The integer $107$ is a prime number. Let $p=107$. For an integer $a$ such that $p\nmid a$ let $a^{-1}$ be the unique integer $0\le a^{-1}\le p^2-1$ such that $p^2\mid aa^{-1}-1$. Find the number of positive integers $b$, $1\le b\le\frac{p^2-1}{2}$ such that there exists an integer $a$, $0\le a\le p^2-1$ such that $p^2\mid b^2-(a+a^{-1})$.

In quadrilateral $ABCD$, angles $A,B,C,D$ form an increasing arithmetic sequence. Also, $\angle ACB=90^\circ$. If $CD=14$ and the length of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$ is $9$, compute the area of $ABCD$.

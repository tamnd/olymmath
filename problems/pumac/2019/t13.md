Let $e_1,e_2,\ldots e_{2019}$ be independently chosen from the set $\{0,1,\ldots,20\}$ uniformly at random. Let $\omega=e^{\frac{2\pi}{i}2019}$. Determine the expected value of $|e_1\omega+e_2\omega^2+\ldots+e_{2019}\omega^{2019}|^2$.

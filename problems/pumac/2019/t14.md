Consider a grid of black and white squares with $3$ rows and $n$ columns. If there is a non-empty sequence of white squares $s_1,\ldots,s_m$ such that $s_1$ is in the top row and $s_m$ is in the bottom row and consecutive squares in the sequence share an edge, then we say that the grid percolates. Let $T_n$ be the number of grids which do not percolate. There exists constants $a,b$ such that $\frac{T_n}{ab^n}$ approaches $1$ as $n$ approaches $\infty$. Then $b$ is expressible as $(x+\sqrt{y})/z$ for squarefree $y$ and coprime $x,z$. Find $x+y+z$.

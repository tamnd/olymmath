Let $f(x)=x^3+3x^2+1$. There is a unique line of the form $y=mx+b$ such that $m>0$ and this line intersects $f(x)$ at three points, $A,B,C$ such that $AB=BC=2$. Find $\lfloor 100m\rfloor$.

For all sets $A$ of complex numbers, let $P(A)$ be the product of the elements of $A$. Let $S_z=\left\{1,2,9,99,999,\frac{1}{z},\frac{1}{z^2}\right\}$, let $T_z$ be the set of nonempty subsets of $S_z$ (including $S_z$), and let $f(z)=1+\sum_{s\in T_z}P(s)$. Suppose $f(z)=6125000$ for some complex number $z$. Compute the product of all possible values of $z$.

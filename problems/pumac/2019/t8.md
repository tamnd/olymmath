The curves $y=x+5$ and $y=x^2-3x$ intersect at points $A$ and $B$. $C$ is a point on the lower curve between $A$ and $B$. The maximum possible area of the quadrilateral $ABCO$ can be written as $\frac{A}{B}$ for coprime $A,B$. Find $A+B$.

The sum $\sum_{k=1}^{2020} k \cos \left(\frac{4 k \pi}{4041}\right)$ can be written in the form $$ \frac{a \cos \left(\frac{p \pi}{q}\right)-b}{c \sin ^{2}\left(\frac{p \pi}{q}\right)} $$ where $a, b, c$ are relatively prime positive integers and $p, q$ are relatively prime positive integers where $p<q$ . Determine $a+b+c+p+q$ .

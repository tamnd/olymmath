Let $f(z)=\frac{a z+b}{c z+d}$ for $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{C}$ . Suppose that $f(1)=i, f(2)=i^{2}$ , and $f(3)=i^{3}$ . If the real part of $f(4)$ can be written as $\frac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m, n$ , find $m^{2}+n^{2}$ .

Let $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ be externally tangent circles with radii $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{1}{8}$ , respectively. The line $\ell$ is a common external tangent to $\Gamma_{1}$ and $\Gamma_{2}$ . For $n \geq 3$ , we define $\Gamma_{n}$ as the smallest circle tangent to $\Gamma_{n-1}, \Gamma_{n-2}$ , and $\ell$ . The radius of $\Gamma_{10}$ can be expressed as $\frac{a}{b}$ where $a, b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $a+b$ .

Let $f(p)$ denote the number of ordered tuples $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{p}\right)$ of nonnegative integers satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^{p} x_{i}=2022$ , where $x_{i} \equiv i(\bmod p)$ for all $1 \leq i \leq p$ . Find the remainder when $\sum_{p \in \mathcal{S}} f(p)$ is divided by 1000, where $\mathcal{S}$ denotes the set of all primes less than 2022 .

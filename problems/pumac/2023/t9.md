The real quartic $P x^{4}+U x^{3}+M x^{2}+A x+C$ has four different positive real roots. Find the square of the smallest real number $z$ for which the expression $M^{2}-2 U A+z P C$ is always positive, regardless of what the roots of the quartic are.

The Minkowski sausage is constructed as follows. $M_0$ is the line segment from $(0,0)$ to $(1,0)$. $M_{i+1}$ is constructed by replacing each segment in $M_i$ with eight segments, each of length $1/4^{i+1}$ (see figure below, where we have provided $M_0$ through $M_3$). Let $M_\infty$ be the limiting shape of $M_0,M_1,\ldots$. The area of the smallest convex polygon which encloses $M_\infty$ can be written as $\frac{a}{b}$ for relatively prime positive integers $a$ and $b$. Find $a+b$.

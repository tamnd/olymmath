Max writes an integer on the board. Then every minute, if the number $k$ on the board is even, he replaces it with $\frac{k}{2}$; otherwise, he replaces it with $\frac{3k+1}{2}$. If Max writes down the number $1$ after at most $5$ minutes, how many different integers could he have started with?

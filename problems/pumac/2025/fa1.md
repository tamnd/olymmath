Let $\lambda^*$ be the maximum value of $\lambda$ such that for all cubic polynomials $P(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx+c$ whose zeros are all real and nonnegative, then $P(x)\ge\lambda(x-a)^3$ for all $x\ge 0$. Prove that $\lambda^*=-\frac{1}{27}$.

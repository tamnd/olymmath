Let $ABC$ be an acute scalene triangle with orthocenter $H$, circumcenter $O$ and circumcircle $\Omega$. It is given that $OH\parallel BC$. Line $OH$ meets side $AB$ at $U$ and side $AC$ at $V$. Let $R$ be on $\Omega$ such that $\angle ARH=90^\circ$. Let the circumcircle of triangle $ROH$ meet $\Omega$ a second time at $S$. Show that lines $SO$ and $RH$ meet on the circumcircle of triangle $RUV$.

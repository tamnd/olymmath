Two circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ have radii $3$ and $2$, respectively, and intersect at two distinct points $X$ and $Y$. The line through $X$ perpendicular to $\overline{XY}$ intersects $\omega_1$ at $A\ne X$ and $\omega_2$ at $B\ne X$. Given that $AB=4$, find the area of triangle $ABY$.

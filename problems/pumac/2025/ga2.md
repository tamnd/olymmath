Consider a right triangular prism with bases $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle A'B'C'$, where $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle A'B'C'$ and $AC=10$. Point $M$ lies on $\overline{AB}$ such that $AM=5$ and $MB=4$. Let $N$ lie on $\overline{A'C'}$ such that $\overline{BN}$ is parallel to the plane of $\triangle A'CM$. Find $A'N$.

Let $ABCD$ be an isosceles trapezoid with $\overline{AB}\parallel\overline{CD}$, $AB=20$, and $CD=22$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AD$ and $BC$, respectively. Ray $NM$ meets the circumcircle of $ABCD$ at $K$. If the lengths of $KM$ and $AM$ are positive integers, find the sum of all possible values of $KM$.

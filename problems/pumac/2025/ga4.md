Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle BAC=45^\circ$, $AB=2\sqrt{2}$ and $AC=3$. Let $X$ be the reflection of $B$ over $\overline{AC}$ and let $Y$ be the reflection of $C$ over $\overline{AB}$. Lines $XY$ and $BC$ intersect at $S$. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$ and let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle XHY$. Find $SO$.

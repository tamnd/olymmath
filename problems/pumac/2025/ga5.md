Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle BAC=60^\circ$, $AB=10$, $AC=13$, and orthocenter $H$. Let $\omega_1$ be the circle through $H$ tangent to $\overline{BC}$ at $B$, and let $\omega_2$ be the circle through $H$ tangent to $\overline{BC}$ at $C$. Let $\overline{CH}$ intersect $\omega_1$ again at $U$ and let $\overline{BH}$ intersect $\omega_2$ again at $V$. Let $N$ be the point on the same side of $\overline{BC}$ as $A$ such that triangle $BNC$ is equilateral. Find $\frac{NU}{NV}$.

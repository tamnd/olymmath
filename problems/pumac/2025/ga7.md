Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AC=33$, $BC=16$, $AB=28$ and incenter $I$. Let $U$ and $V$ be points on sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively such that $AU=AV=20$. Let $P$ be the reflection of $B$ over $U$ and let $Q$ be the reflection of $C$ over $V$. The circumcircles of triangles $BIP$ and $CIQ$ intersect at a point $S\ne I$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $\overline{BC}$. Find $MS$.

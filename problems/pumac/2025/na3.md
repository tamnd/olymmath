Compute the sum of all primes $p<50$ such that there exist linear multinomials $P$ and $Q$ with integer coefficients such that $x^2-2xy-y^2\equiv P(x,y)\cdot Q(x,y)\pmod{p}$.

*A linear multinomial is an expression of the form $ax+by+c$.*

Consider the equations
$$\begin{aligned}x+y+z&=A,\\ x^2+y^2+z^2&=B,\\ x^3+y^3+z^3&=C.\end{aligned}$$
An ordered triple $(A,B,C)\in\mathbb{R}^3$ is called "nice" if the corresponding system above has exactly three real solutions for $x$. For fixed $(A,B)\in\mathbb{R}^2$, the set of real $C$ such that the system is "nice" is a finite union of finite intervals; denote the sum of their lengths as $\ell(A,B)$. Find $\sum_{A=3}^{5}\sum_{B=3}^{5}\ell(A,B)$.

A 24-cell is a region that can be formed by the following procedure: taking convex hull of $8$ vertices formed by permuting integer coordinates $(\pm 1,0,0,0)$ and $16$ vertices at all half-integer coordinates $(\pm 1/2,\pm 1/2,\pm 1/2,\pm 1/2)$. If we rotate and scale the 24-cell so that each coordinate has absolute value at most $1$ in such a way that the 24-cell has greatest hypervolume, how many vertices $(x,y,z,w)$ of the 24-cell satisfy $x=1$?

Let $x$ be the unique real number which satisfies
$$x=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt{x}}-\sqrt{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x-1}}}.$$
Find $\lfloor x\rfloor$.

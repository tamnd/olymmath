And it came to pass that Jeb owned over a thousand chickens. So Jeb counted his
chickens. And Jeb reported the count to Hannah. And Hannah reported the count
to Joshua. And Joshua reported the count to Caleb. And Caleb reported the count
to Rachel. But as fate would have it, Jeb had over-counted his chickens by nine
chickens. Then Hannah interchanged the last two digits of the count before
reporting it to Joshua. And Joshua interchanged the first and the third digits of the
number reported to him before reporting it to Caleb. Then Caleb doubled the
number reported to him before reporting it to Rachel. Now it so happens that the
count reported to Rachel was the correct number of chickens that Jeb owned.
How many chickens was that?

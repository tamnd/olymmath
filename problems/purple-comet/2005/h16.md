A tailor met a tortoise sitting under a tree. When the tortoise was the tailor’s age,
the tailor was only a quarter of his current age. When the tree was the tortoise’s
age, the tortoise was only a seventh of its current age. If the sum of their ages is
now 264, how old is the tortoise?

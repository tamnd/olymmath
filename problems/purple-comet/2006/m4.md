At the beginning of each hour from 1 o’clock AM to 12 NOON and from 1 o’clock PM to 12
MIDNIGHT a coo-coo clock’s coo-coo bird coo-coos the number of times equal to the number
of the hour. In addition, the coo-coo clock’s coo-coo bird coo-coos a single time at 30 minutes
past each hour. How many times does the coo-coo bird coo-coo from 12:42 PM on Monday until
3:42 AM on Wednesday?

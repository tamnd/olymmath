4. A lumberman wishes to cut down a tree whose trunk is cylindrical and whose material is uniform. He will cut a notch, the two sides of which will be planes intersecting at a dihedral angle $ \theta $ along a horizontal line through the axis of the cylinder. If $ \theta $ is given, show that the least volume of material is cut out when the plane bisecting the dihedral angle is horizontal.

$$
y=c \cosh \frac{x}{c}
$$
a line $ L $ is drawn perpendicular to the tangent to the catenary at a point $ P $. Prove that the length of $ L $ intercepted by the axes is equal to the ordinate $ y $ of the point $ P $.

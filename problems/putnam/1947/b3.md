9. Let $ x, y $ be Cartesian coordinates in the plane. $ I $ denotes the line segment $ 1 \leq x \leq 3, y=1 $. For every point $ P $ on $ I $, let $ P^{*} $ denote that point that lies on the segment joining the origin to $ P $ and such that the distance $ P P^{*} $ is equal to $ 1 / 100 $. As $ P $ describes $ I $, the corresponding point $ P^{*} $ describes a certain curve $ C^{*} $. Let $ l(I), l\left(C^{*}\right) $ be the lengths of $ I $ and $ C^{*} $ respectively. Which one of $ l(I), l\left(C^{*}\right) $ is greater? Prove your answer.

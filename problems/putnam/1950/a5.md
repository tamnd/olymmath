5. A function $ D(n) $ of the positive integral variable $ n $ is defined by the following properties: $ D(1)=0, D(p)=1 $ if $ p $ is a prime, $ D(u v)=u D(v)+ $ $ v D(u) $ for any two positive integers $ u $ and $ v $. Answer all three parts below.
(i) Show that these properties are compatible and determine uniquely $ \boldsymbol{D}(n) $. (Derive a formula for $ \boldsymbol{D}(n) / n $, assuming that $ n=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}} p_{2}{ }^{\alpha_{2}} \cdots p_{k}{ }^{\alpha_{k}} $ where $ p_{1}, p_{2}, \ldots, p_{k} $ are different primes.)
(ii) For what values of $ n $ is $ D(n)=n $ ?
(iii) Define $ D^{2}(n)=D[D(n)] $, etc., and find the limit of $ D^{m}(63) $ as $ m $ tends to $ \infty $.

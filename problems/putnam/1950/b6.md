6. Consider the closed plane curves $ C_{i} $ and $ C_{o} $, their respective lengths $ \left|C_{i}\right| $ and $ \left|C_{o}\right| $, the closed surfaces $ S_{i} $ and $ S_{o} $, and their respective areas $ \left|S_{i}\right| $ and $ \left|S_{o}\right| $. Assume that $ C_{i} $ lies inside $ C_{o} $ and $ S_{i} $ inside $ S_{o} $. (Subscript $ i $ stands for "inner," $ o $ for "outer.") Prove the correct assertions among the following four, and disprove the others.
(i) If $ C_{i} $ is convex, $ \left|C_{i}\right| \leq\left|C_{o}\right| $.
(ii) If $ S_{i} $ is convex, $ \left|S_{i}\right| \leq\left|S_{o}\right| $.
(iii) If $ C_{o} $ is the smallest convex curve containing $ C_{i} $, then $ \left|C_{o}\right| \leq\left|C_{i}\right| $.
(iv) If $ S_{o} $ is the smallest convex surface containing $ S_{i} $, then $ \left|S_{o}\right| \leq\left|S_{i}\right| $.

You may assume that $ C_{i} $ and $ C_{o} $ are polygons and $ S_{i} $ and $ S_{o} $ polyhedra. (Why?)

6. A man has a rectangular block of wood $ m $ by $ n $ by $ r $ inches $ (m, n $, and $ r $ are integers). He paints the entire surface of the block, cuts the block into inch cubes, and notices that exactly half the cubes are completely unpainted. Prove that the number of essentially different blocks with this property is finite. (Do not attempt to enumerate them.)

4. A homogeneous solid body is made by joining a base of a circular cylinder of height $ h $ and radius $ r $, and the base of a hemisphere of radius $ r $. This body is placed with the hemispherical end on a horizontal table, with the axis of the cylinder in a vertical position, and then slightly oscillated. It is intuitively evident that if $ r $ is large as compared to $ h $, the equilibrium will be stable; but if $ r $ is small as compared to $ h $, the equilibrium will be unstable. What is the critical value of the ratio $ r / h $ which enables the body to rest in neutral equilibrium in any position?

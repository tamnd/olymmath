6. A projectile moves in a resisting medium. The resisting force is a function of the velocity and is directed along the velocity vector. The equation $ x $ $ =f(t) $ gives the horizontal distance in terms of the time $ t $. Show that the vertical distance $ y $ is given by
$$
y=-g f(t) \int \frac{d t}{f^{\prime}(t)}+g \int \frac{f(t)}{f^{\prime}(t)} d t+A f(t)+B
$$
where $ A $ and $ B $ are constants and $ g $ is the acceleration due to gravity.

6. Prove that, if $ x $ and $ y $ are positive irrationals such that $ 1 / x+1 / y=1 $, then the sequences $ [x],[2 x], \ldots,[n x], \ldots $ and $ [y],[2 y], \ldots,[n y], \ldots $ together include every positive integer exactly once. (The notation $ [x] $ means the largest integer not exceeding $ x $.)

Let $N(n)$ denote the smallest positive integer $N$ such that $x^N =e$ for every element $x$ of the symmetric group $S_n$ , where $e$ denotes the identity permutation. Prove that if $n>1,$ $$ \frac{N(n)}{N(n-1)} =\begin{cases} p \;\text{if} n \text{is a power of a prime } p
1 \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases} $$

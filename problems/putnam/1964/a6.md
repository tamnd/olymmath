Let $S$ be a finite subset of a straight line. Say that $S$ has the *repeated distance property* if every value of the distance between two points of $S$ (except the longest) occurs at least twice. Show that if $S$ has the *repeated distance property* then the ratio of any two distances between two points of $S$ is rational.

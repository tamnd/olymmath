Show that if $\lambda > \frac{1}{2}$ there does not exist a real-valued function $u(x)$ such that for all $x$ in the closed interval $[0,1]$ the following holds: $$ u(x)= 1+ \lambda \int_{x}^{1} u(y) u(y-x) dy. $$

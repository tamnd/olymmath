A-2. Let $ P(x, y)=x^{2} y+x y^{2} $ and $ Q(x, y)=x^{2}+x y+y^{2} $. For $ n=1,2,3, \ldots $, let $ F_{n}(x, y)=(x+y)^{n}-x^{n}-y^{n} $ and $ G_{n}(x, y)=(x+y)^{n}+x^{n}+y^{n} $. One observes that $ G_{2}=2 Q, F_{3}=3 P, G_{4}=2 Q^{2}, F_{5}=5 P Q, G_{6}=2 Q^{3}+3 P^{2} $. Prove that, in fact, for each $ n $ either $ F_{n} $ or $ G_{n} $ is expressible as a polynomial in $ P $ and $ Q $ with integer coefficients.

A-6. Suppose $ f(x) $ is a twice continuously differentiable real valued function defined for all real numbers $ x $ and satisfying $ |f(x)| \leqq 1 $ for all $ x $ and $ (f(0))^{2}+\left(f^{\prime}(0)\right)^{2}=4 $. Prove that there exists a real number $ x_{0} $ such that $ f\left(x_{0}\right)+f^{\prime \prime}\left(x_{0}\right)=0 $.

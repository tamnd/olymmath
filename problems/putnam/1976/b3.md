B-3. Suppose that we have $ n $ events $ A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n} $, each of which has probability at least $ 1-a $ of occurring, where $ a<1 / 4 $. Further suppose that $ A_{1} $ and $ A $, are mutually independent if $ |i-j|>1 $, although $ A_{1} $ and $ A_{i+1} $ may be dependent. Assume as known that the recurrence $ u_{k+1}=u_{k}-a u_{k-1}, u_{0}=1, u_{1}=1-a $, defines positive real numbers $ u_{k} $ for $ k=0,1, \ldots $. Show that the probability of all of $ A_{1}, \ldots, A_{n} $ occurring is at least $ u_{n} $.

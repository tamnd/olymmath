B-4. For a point $ P $ on an ellipse, let $ d $ be the distance from the center of the ellipse to the line tangent to the ellipse at $ P $. Prove that $ \left(P F_{1}\right)\left(P F_{2}\right) d^{2} $ is constant as $ P $ varies on the ellipse, where $ P F_{1} $ and $ P F_{2} $ are the distances from $ P $ to the foci $ F_{1} $ and $ F_{2} $ of the ellipse.

Problem A-6
Let $ f(x, y) $ be a continuous function on the square
$$
S=\{(x, y): 0<x<1,0<y<1\} .
$$

For each point $ (a, b) $ in the interior of $ S $, let $ S_{(a, b)} $ be the largest square that is contained in $ S $, is centered at $ (a, b) $, and has sides parallel to those of $ S $. If the double integral $ \iint f(x, y) d x d y $ is zero when taken over each square $ S_{(a, b)} $ must $ f(x, y) $ be identically zero on $ S $ ?

Problem A-6
Let $ 0<p_{i}<1 $ for $ i=1,2, \ldots, n $. Show that
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\left|x-p_{i}\right|}<8 n\left(1+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}+\cdots+\frac{1}{2 n-1}\right)
$$
for some $ x $ satisfying $ 0<x<1 $.

Problem B-6
For $ k=1,2, \ldots, n $ let $ z_{k}=x_{k}+i y_{k} $, where the $ x_{k} $ and $ y_{k} $ are real and $ i=\sqrt{-1} $. Let $ r $ be the absolute value of the real part of
$$
\pm \sqrt{z_{1}^{2}+z_{2}^{2}+\cdots+z_{n}^{2}}
$$

Prove that $ r<\left|x_{1}\right|+\left|x_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|x_{n}\right| $.

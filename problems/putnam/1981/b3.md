Problem B-3
Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $ n $ with the property that if $ p $ is a prime divisor of $ n^{2}+3 $, then $ p $ is also a divisor of $ k^{2}+3 $ for some integer $ k $ with $ k^{2}<n $.

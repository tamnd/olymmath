Problem B-6
Let $ K(x, y, z) $ denote the area of a triangle whose sides have lengths $ x, y $, and $ z $. For any two triangles with sides $ a, b, c $ and $ a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime} $, respectively, prove that
$$
\sqrt{K(a, b, c)}+\sqrt{K\left(a^{\prime}, b^{\prime}, c^{\prime}\right)} \leqslant \sqrt{K\left(a+a^{\prime}, b+b^{\prime}, c+c^{\prime}\right)}
$$
and determine the cases of equality.

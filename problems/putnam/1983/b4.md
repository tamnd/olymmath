**Problem.** Let $f:\mathbb{R}_0^+\rightarrow\mathbb{R}_0^+$ be a function defined as $$ f(n)=n+\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor~\forall~n\in\mathbb{R}_0^+. $$ Prove that for any positive integer $m,$ the sequence $$ m,f(m),f(f(m)),f(f(f(m))),\ldots $$ contains a perfect square.

Problem B-6
Let $ k $ be a positive integer, let $ m=2^{k}+1 $, and let $ r \neq 1 $ be a complex root of $ z^{m}-1=0 $. Prove that there exist polynomials $ P(z) $ and $ Q(z) $ with integer coefficients such that
$$
(P(r))^{2}+(Q(r))^{2}=-1
$$

Problem A-3
Let $ n $ be a positive integer. Let $ a, b, x $ be real numbers, with $ a \neq b $, and let $ M_{n} $ denote the $ 2 n \times 2 n $ matrix whose ( $ i, j $ ) entry $ m_{i j} $ is given by
$$
m_{i j}=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
x & \text { if } i=j, \\
a & \text { if } i \neq j \\
b & \text { if } i \neq j
\end{array} \text { and } i+j \text { is even }, ~ i+j\right. \text { is odd. }
$$

Thus, for example, $ M_{2}=\left(\begin{array}{llll}x & b & a & b \\ b & x & b & a \\ a & b & x & b \\ b & a & b & x\end{array}\right) $. Express $ \lim _{x \rightarrow a} \operatorname{det} M_{n} /(x-a)^{2 n-2} $ as a polynomial in $ a, b $, and $ n $, where $ \operatorname{det} M_{n} $ denotes the determinant of $ M_{n} $.

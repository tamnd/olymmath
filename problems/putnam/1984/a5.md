Problem A-5
Let $ R $ be the region consisting of all triples ( $ x, y, z $ ) of nonnegative real numbers satisfying $ x+y+z \leqslant 1 $. Let $ w=1-x-y-z $. Express the value of the triple integral
$$
\iiint_{R} x^{1} y^{9} z^{8} w^{4} d x d y d z
$$
in the form $ a!b!c!d!/ n! $, where $ a, b, c, d $, and $ n $ are positive integers.

For a positive real number $r$, let $G(r)$ be the minimum value of $|r - \sqrt{m^2+2n^2}|$ for all integers $m$ and $n$. Prove or disprove the assertion that $\lim_{r\to \infty}G(r)$ exists and equals 0.

For all real $x$, the real-valued function $y=f(x)$ satisfies $$ y''-2y'+y=2e^x. $$

(a) If $f(x)>0$ for all real $x$, must $f'(x) > 0$ for all real $x$? Explain.

(b) If $f'(x)>0$ for all real $x$, must $f(x) > 0$ for all real $x$? Explain.

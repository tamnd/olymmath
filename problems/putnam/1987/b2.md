Let $r,s$ and $t$ be integers with $0 \leq r$, $0 \leq s$ and $r+s \leq t$. Prove that $$ \frac{\binom s0}{\binom tr} + \frac{\binom s1}{\binom{t}{r+1}} + \cdots + \frac{\binom ss}{\binom{t}{r+s}} = \frac{t+1}{(t+1-s)\binom{t-s}{r}}. $$

Prove that there exists a *unique* function $f$ from the set $\mathrm{R}^+$ of positive real numbers to $\mathrm{R}^+$ such that $$ f(f(x)) = 6x-f(x) $$ and $$ f(x)>0 $$ for all $x>0$.

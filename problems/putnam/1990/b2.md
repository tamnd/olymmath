Prove that for $|x| < 1$, $|z| > 1$, $$ 1 + \sum_{j=1}^\infty (1 + x^j)P_j = 0, $$ where $P_j$ is $$ \frac{(1 - z)(1 - zx)(1 - zx^2) \cdots (1 - zx^{j-1})} {(z - x)(z - x^2)(z - x^3) \cdots (z - x^j)}. $$

Let $(x_n)_{n \geq 0}$ be a sequence of nonzero real numbers such that $x_n^2 - x_{n-1}x_{n+1} = 1$ for $n=1,2,3,\dots$. Prove there exists a real number $a$ such that $x_{n+1} = ax_n - x_{n-1}$ for all $n \geq 1$.

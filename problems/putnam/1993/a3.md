Let ${\cal P}_n$ be the set of subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$. Let $c(n, m)$ be the number of functions $f: {\cal P}_n \to \{1, 2, \dots, m\}$ such that $f(A \cap B) = \min\{f(A), f(B)\}$. Prove that $$ c(n, m) = \sum_{j=1}^m j^n. $$

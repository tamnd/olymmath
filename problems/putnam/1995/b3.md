To each positive integer with $ n^2 $ decimal digits, we associate the determinant of the matrix obtained by writing the digits in order across the rows. For example, for $ n = 2 $, to the integer 8617 we associate $ \det\begin{pmatrix} 8 & 6 \\ 1 & 7 \end{pmatrix} = 50 $. Find, as a function of $ n $, the sum of all the determinants associated with $ n^2 $-digit integers. (Leading digits are assumed to be nonzero; for example, for $ n = 2 $, there are 9000 determinants.)

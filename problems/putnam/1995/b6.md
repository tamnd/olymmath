For a positive real number $ \alpha $, define
$$
S(\alpha) = \{ \lfloor n\alpha \rfloor : n = 1,2,3,\ldots \}.
$$
Prove that $ \{1,2,3,\ldots\} $ cannot be expressed as the disjoint union of three sets $ S(\alpha), S(\beta) $ and $ S(\gamma) $. [As usual, $ \lfloor x \rfloor $ is the greatest integer $ \leq x $.]<|end_of_box|>

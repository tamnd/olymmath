If $ p $ is a prime number greater than 3 and $ k = \lfloor 2p/3 \rfloor $, prove that the sum
$$
\binom{p}{1} + \binom{p}{2} + \cdots + \binom{p}{k}
$$
of binomial coefficients is divisible by $ p^2 $.

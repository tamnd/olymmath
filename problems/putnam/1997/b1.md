Let $\{x\}$ denote the distance between the real number $x$ and the nearest integer. For each positive integer $n$, evaluate
$$F_n = \sum_{m=1}^{6n-1} \min\left( \left\{ \frac{m}{6n} \right\}, \left\{ \frac{m}{3n} \right\} \right).$$
(Here $\min(a,b)$ denotes the minimum of $a$ and $b$.)

Let $a_{m,n}$ denote the coefficient of $x^n$ in the expansion of $(1 + x + x^2)^m$. Prove that for all [integers] $k \geq 0$,
$$0 \leq \sum_{i=0}^{\lfloor 2k/3 \rfloor} (-1)^i a_{k-i,i} \leq 1.$$

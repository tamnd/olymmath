Let $\mathcal F$ be a finite collection of open discs in $\mathbb R^2$ whose union contains a set $E\subseteq \mathbb R^2$. Show that there is a pairwise disjoint subcollection $D_1,\ldots, D_n$ in $\mathcal F$ such that $$E\subseteq \cup_{j=1}^n 3D_j.$$ Here, if $D$ is the disc of radius $r$ and center $P$, then $3D$ is the disc of radius $3r$ and center $P$.

Let $ \mathscr{F} $ be a finite collection of open discs in $ \mathbb{R}^2 $ whose union contains a set $ E \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2 $. Show that there is a pairwise disjoint subcollection $ D_1, \ldots, D_n $ in $ \mathscr{F} $ such that
$$
E \subseteq \bigcup_{j=1}^n 3D_j.
$$
Here, if $ D $ is the disc of radius $ r $ and center $ P $, then $ 3D $ is the disc of radius $ 3r $ and center $ P $.

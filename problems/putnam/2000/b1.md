Let $a_j, b_j, c_j$ be integers for $1 \leq j \leq N$. Assume for each $j$, at least one of $a_j, b_j, c_j$ is odd. Show that there exist integers $r, s, t$ such that $r a_j + s b_j + t c_j$ is odd for at least $4N/7$ values of $j$, $1 \leq j \leq N$.

Let $S$ denote the set of rational numbers different from $\{-1, 0, 1\}$. Define $f : S \to S$ by $f(x) = x - 1/x$. Prove or disprove that
$$\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} f^{(n)}(S) = \emptyset,$$
where $f^{(n)}$ denotes $f$ composed with itself $n$ times.

Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer. How many ways are there to write $n$ as a sum of positive integers, $$ n = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_k, $$ with $k$ an arbitrary positive integer and $a_1 \le a_2 \le \cdots \le a_k \le a_1 + 1$? For example, with $n=4$ there are four ways: 4, 2+2, 1+1+2, 1+1+1+1.

Let $ n $ be a fixed positive integer. How many ways are there to write $ n $ as a sum of positive integers,
$$
n = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_k,
$$
with $ k $ an arbitrary positive integer and $ a_1 \leq a_2 \leq \cdots \leq a_k \leq a_1 + 1 $? For example, with $ n = 4 $ there are four ways: $ 4 $, $ 2+2 $, $ 1+1+2 $, $ 1+1+1+1 $.

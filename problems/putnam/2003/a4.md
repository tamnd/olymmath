Suppose that $ a, b, c, A, B, C $ are real numbers, $ a \neq 0 $ and $ A \neq 0 $, such that
$$
|ax^2 + bx + c| \leq |Ax^2 + Bx + C|
$$
for all real numbers $ x $. Show that
$$
|b^2 - 4ac| \leq |B^2 - 4AC|.
$$

Let $ n $ be a positive integer. Starting with the sequence $ 1, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{3}, \ldots, \frac{1}{n} $, form a new sequence of $ n-1 $ entries $ \frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{12}, \ldots, \frac{2n-1}{2n(n-1)} $ by taking the averages of two consecutive entries in the first sequence. Repeat the averaging of neighbors on the second sequence to obtain a third sequence of $ n-2 $ entries, and continue until the final sequence produced consists of a single number $ x_n $. Show that $ x_n < 2/n $.

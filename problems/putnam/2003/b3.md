Show that for each positive integer $ n $,
$$
n! = \prod_{i=1}^n \text{lcm}\{1, 2, \ldots, \lfloor n/i \rfloor\}.
$$
(Here lcm denotes the least common multiple, and $ \lfloor x \rfloor $ denotes the greatest integer $ \leq x $.)

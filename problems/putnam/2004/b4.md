Let $n$ be a positive integer, $n \geq 2$, and put $\theta = 2\pi/n$. Define points $P_k = (k, 0)$ in the $xy$-plane, for $k = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Let $R_k$ be the map that rotates the plane counterclockwise by the angle $\theta$ about the point $P_k$. Let $R$ denote the map obtained by applying, in order, $R_1$, then $R_2, \ldots$, then $R_n$. For an arbitrary point $(x,y)$, find, and simplify, the coordinates of $R(x,y)$.

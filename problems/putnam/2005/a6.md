Let $n$ be given, $n \geq 4$, and suppose that $P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_n$ are $n$ randomly, independently and uniformly chosen points on a circle. Consider the convex $n$-gon whose vertices are the $P_i$. What is the probability that at least one of the vertex angles of this polygon is acute?

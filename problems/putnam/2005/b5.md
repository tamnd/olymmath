Let $P(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ denote a polynomial with real coefficients in the variables $x_1, \ldots, x_n$, and suppose that $\left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_1^2} + \cdots + \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_n^2} \right) P(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = 0$ (identically) and that $x_1^2 + \cdots + x_n^2$ divides $P(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$. Show that $P = 0$ identically.

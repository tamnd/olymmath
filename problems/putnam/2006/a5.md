Let $n$ be a positive odd integer and let $\theta$ be a real number such that $\theta/\pi$ is irrational. Set $a_k = \tan(\theta + k\pi/n)$, $k = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Prove that
$$\frac{a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n}{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}$$
is an integer, and determine its value.

Prove that, for every set $X = \{x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\}$ of $n$ real numbers, there exists a non-empty subset $S$ of $X$ and an integer $m$ such that
$$\left| m + \sum_{s \in S} s \right| \leq \frac{1}{n+1}.$$

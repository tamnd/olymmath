Start with a finite sequence $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ of positive integers. If possible, choose two indices $j < k$ such that $a_j$ does not divide $a_k$, and replace $a_j$ and $a_k$ by $\gcd(a_j, a_k)$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a_j, a_k)$, respectively. Prove that if this process is repeated, it must eventually stop and the final sequence does not depend on the choices made. (Note: $\gcd$ means greatest common divisor and $\operatorname{lcm}$ means least common multiple.)

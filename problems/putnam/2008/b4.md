Let $p$ be a prime number. Let $h(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients such that $h(0), h(1), \ldots, h(p^2 - 1)$ are distinct modulo $p^2$. Show that $h(0), h(1), \ldots, h(p^3 - 1)$ are distinct modulo $p^3$.

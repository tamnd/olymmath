Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers. Say that a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ is $k$-limited if $|\sigma(i) - i| \leq k$ for all $i$. Prove that the number of $k$-limited permutations of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ is odd if and only if $n \equiv 0$ or $1 \pmod{2k+1}$.<|end_of_box|>

Let $d_n$ be the determinant of the $n \times n$ matrix whose entries, from left to right and then from top to bottom, are $\cos 1, \cos 2, \ldots, \cos n^2$. (For example,
$$
d_3 = \begin{vmatrix}
\cos 1 & \cos 2 & \cos 3 \\
\cos 4 & \cos 5 & \cos 6 \\
\cos 7 & \cos 8 & \cos 9
\end{vmatrix}.
$$
The argument of $\cos$ is always in radians, not degrees.) Evaluate $\lim_{n \to \infty} d_n$.

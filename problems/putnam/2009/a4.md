Let $S$ be a set of rational numbers such that

(a) $0 \in S$;

(b) If $x \in S$ then $x+1\in S$ and $x-1\in S$; and

(c) If $x\in S$ and $x\not\in\{0,1\}$, then $\frac{1}{x(x-1)}\in S$.

Must $S$ contain all rational numbers?

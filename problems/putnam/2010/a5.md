Let $ G $ be a group, with operation $ * $. Suppose that
(i) $ G $ is a subset of $ \mathbb{R}^3 $ (but $ * $ need not be related to addition of vectors);
(ii) For each $ \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \in G $, either $ \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{a} * \mathbf{b} $ or $ \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = 0 $ (or both), where $ \times $ is the usual cross product in $ \mathbb{R}^3 $.

Prove that $ \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = 0 $ for all $ \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \in G $.

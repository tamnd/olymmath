Let $ A $ be an $ n \times n $ matrix of real numbers for some $ n \geq 1 $. For each positive integer $ k $, let $ A^{[k]} $ be the matrix obtained by raising each entry to the $ k $th power. Show that if $ A^k = A^{[k]} $ for $ k = 1, 2, \ldots, n+1 $, then $ A^k = A^{[k]} $ for all $ k \geq 1 $.<|end_of_box|>

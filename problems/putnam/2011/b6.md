Let $p$ be an odd prime. Show that for at least $(p+1)/2$ values of $n$ in $\{0, 1, 2, \ldots, p-1\}$,
$$\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} k! n^k$$
is not divisible by $p$.<|end_of_box|>

Prove that, for any two bounded functions $g_1, g_2: \mathbb{R} \to [1, \infty)$, there exist functions $h_1, h_2: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that, for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $$ \sup_{s \in \mathbb{R}} (g_1(s)^x g_2(s)) = \max_{t \in \mathbb{R}} (x h_1(t) + h_2(t)). $$

Prove that, for any two bounded functions $g_1, g_2 : \mathbb{R} \to [1, \infty)$, there exist functions $h_1, h_2 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that, for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,
$$\sup_{s \in \mathbb{R}} \left( g_1(s)^x g_2(s) \right) = \max_{t \in \mathbb{R}} \left( x h_1(t) + h_2(t) \right).$$

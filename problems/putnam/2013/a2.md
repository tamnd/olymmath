Let $ S $ be the set of all positive integers that are not perfect squares. For $ n \in S $, consider choices of integers $ a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_r $ such that $ n < a_1 < a_2 < \cdots < a_r $ and $ n \cdot a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdots a_r $ is a perfect square, and let $ f(n) $ be the minimum of $ a_r $ over all such choices. Show that the function $ f $ from $ S $ to the integers is one-to-one.

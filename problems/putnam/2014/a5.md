Let
$$P_n(x) = 1 + 2x + 3x^2 + \cdots + nx^{n-1}.$$
Prove that the polynomials $P_j(x)$ and $P_k(x)$ are relatively prime for all positive integers $j$ and $k$ with $j \neq k$.

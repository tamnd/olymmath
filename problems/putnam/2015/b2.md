Given a list of the positive integers $1,2,3,4,\ldots$, take the first three numbers $1,2,3$ and their sum $6$ and cross all four numbers off the list. Repeat with the three smallest remaining numbers $4,5,7$ and their sum $16$. Continue in this way, crossing off the three smallest remaining numbers and their sum, and consider the sequence of sums produced: $6,16,27,36,\ldots$. Prove or disprove that there is some number in the sequence whose base $10$ representation ends with $2015$.

Let $P_n$ be the number of permutations $\pi$ of $\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ such that
$$|i - j| = 1 \text{ implies } |\pi(i) - \pi(j)| \leq 2$$
for all $i,j$ in $\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$. Show that for $n \geq 2$, the quantity
$$P_{n+5} - P_{n+4} - P_{n+3} + P_n$$
does not depend on $n$, and find its value.

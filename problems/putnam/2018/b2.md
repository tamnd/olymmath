Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $f_n(z) = n + (n-1)z + (n-2)z^2 + \cdots + z^{n-1}$. Prove that $f_n$ has no roots in the closed unit disk $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| \leq 1\}$.

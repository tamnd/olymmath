\sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^{\lfloor k(\sqrt{2} - 1) \rfloor} \geq 0.
$$
(As usual, $ \lfloor x \rfloor $ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $ x $.)<|end_of_box|>

Let $p$ be a prime number greater than $5$. Let $f(p)$ denote the number of infinite sequences $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ such that $a_n \in \{1, 2, \ldots, p-1\}$ and $a_n a_{n+2} \equiv 1 + a_{n+1} \pmod{p}$ for all $n \geq 1$. Prove that $f(p)$ is congruent to $0$ or $2 \pmod{5}$.

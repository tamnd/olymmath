Suppose that $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ are real numbers between $0$ and $1$ that are chosen independently and uniformly at random. Let $S = \sum_{i=1}^k \frac{X_i}{2^i}$, where $k$ is the least positive integer such that $X_k < X_{k+1}$, or $k = \infty$ if there is no such integer. Find the expected value of $S$.

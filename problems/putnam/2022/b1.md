Suppose that $P(x) = a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + \cdots + a_n x^n$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, with $a_1$ odd. Suppose that $e^{P(x)} = b_0 + b_1 x + b_2 x^2 + \cdots$ for all $x$. Prove that $b_k$ is nonzero for all $k \geq 0$.

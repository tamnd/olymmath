Let $ v_1, \ldots, v_{12} $ be unit vectors in $ \mathbb{R}^3 $ from the origin to the vertices of a regular icosahedron. Show that for every vector $ v \in \mathbb{R}^3 $ and every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, there exist integers $ a_1, \ldots, a_{12} $ such that $ \|a_1v_1 + \cdots + a_{12}v_{12} - v\| < \varepsilon $.

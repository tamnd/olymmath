Let $ \mathbb{N} = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots\} $. Find the largest real constant $ r $ such that there exists a function $ g: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} $ such that
$$
g(n + 1) - g(n) \geq (g(g(n)))^r
$$
for all $ n \in \mathbb{N} $.<|end_of_box|>

A *magic square* is a table
![Image](https://cdn.artofproblemsolving.com/attachments/7/9/3b1e2b2f5d2d4c486f57c4ad68b66f7d7e56dd.png)
in which all the natural numbers from $1$ to $16$ appear and such that: $\bullet$ all rows have the same sum $s$ . $\bullet$ all columns have the same sum $s$ . $\bullet$ both diagonals have the same sum $s$ .
It is known that $a_{22} = 1$ and $a_{24} = 2$ . Calculate $a_{44}$ .

For $ a_i \in \mathbb{Z}^ +$ , $ i = 1, \ldots, k$ , and $ n = \sum^k_{i = 1} a_i$ , let $ d = \gcd(a_1, \ldots, a_k)$ denote the greatest common divisor of $ a_1, \ldots, a_k$ .
Prove that $ \frac {d} {n} \cdot \frac {n!}{\prod\limits^k_{i = 1} (a_i!)}$ is an integer.

*Dan Schwarz, Romania*

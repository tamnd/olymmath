Demonstrate that if $ z_1,z_2\in\mathbb{C}^* $ satisfy the relation:
$$ z_1\cdot 2^{\big| z_1\big|} +z_2\cdot 2^{\big| z_2\big|} =\left( z_1+z_2\right)\cdot 2^{\big| z_1 +z_2\big|} , $$
then $ z_1^6=z_2^6 $

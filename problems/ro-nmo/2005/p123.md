Let $f:[0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)$ a continous function such that $\lim_{n\to\infty} \int^x_0 f(t)dt$ exists and it is finite. Prove that
$$ \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac 1{\sqrt x} \int^x_0 \sqrt {f(t)} dt = 0. $$

Let $ n \in \mathbb N$ , $ n \geq 2$ . Determine $ n$ sets $ A_i$ , $ 1 \leq i \leq n$ , from the plane, pairwise disjoint, such that:

(a) for every circle $ \mathcal C$ from the plane and for every $ i \in \left\{ 1,2,\ldots,n \right\}$ we have $ A_i \cap \textrm{Int} \left( \mathcal C \right) \neq \phi$ ;

(b) for all lines $ d$ from the plane and every $ i \in \left\{ 1,2,\ldots,n \right\}$ , the projection of $ A_i$ on $ d$ is not $ d$ .

We define a *pseudo-inverse* $B\in \mathcal M_n(\mathbb C)$ of a matrix $A\in\mathcal M_n(\mathbb C)$ a matrix which fulfills the relations
$$ A = ABA \text{ and } B=BAB. $$
a) Prove that any square matrix has at least a pseudo-inverse.

b) For which matrix $A$ is the pseudo-inverse unique?

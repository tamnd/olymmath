Let $ a>0$ and $ f: [0,\infty) \to [0,a]$ be a continuous function on $ (0,\infty)$ and having Darboux property on $ [0,\infty)$ . Prove that if $ f(0)=0$ and for all nonnegative $ x$ we have
$$ xf(x) \geq \int^x_0 f(t) dt ,$$ then $ f$ admits primitives on $ [0,\infty)$ .

Let $ f: [0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be a derivable function, with a continuous derivative $ f'$ on $ [0,1]$ . Prove that if $ f\left( \frac 12\right) = 0$ , then
$$ \int^1_0 \left( f'(x) \right)^2 dx \geq 12 \left( \int^1_0 f(x) dx \right)^2.$$

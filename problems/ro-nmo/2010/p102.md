Consider $v,w$ two distinct non-zero complex numbers. Prove that
$$|zw+\bar{w}|\le |zv+\bar{v}|,$$
for any $z\in\mathbb{C},|z|=1$ , if and only if there exists $k\in [-1,1]$ such that $w=kv$ .

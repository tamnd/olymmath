Fie $a \in (0, 1)$ şi $\mathcal{C}$ mulţimea funcţiilor crescătoare $f : [0, 1] \to [0, \infty)$, astfel încât $\int_0^1 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = 1$. Să se determine:

**(a)** $\max\limits_{f \in \mathcal{C}} \int_0^a f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$,

**(b)** $\max\limits_{f \in \mathcal{C}} \int_0^a \big(f(x)\big)^2\,\mathrm{d}x$.

Given $a\in (0,1)$ and $C$ the set of increasing functions
$f:[0,1]\to [0,\infty )$ such that $\int\limits_{0}^{1}{f(x)}dx=1$ . Determine: $(a)\underset{f\in C}{\mathop{\max }}\,\int\limits_{0}^{a}{f(x)dx}$ $(b)\underset{f\in C}{\mathop{\max }}\,\int\limits_{0}^{a}{{{f}^{2}}(x)dx}$

Find all continuous functions $ f:\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R} $ that satisfy: $ \text{(i)}\text{id}+f $ is nondecreasing $ \text{(ii)} $ There is a natural number $ m $ such that $ \text{id}+f+f^2\cdots +f^m $ is nonincreasing.

Here, $ \text{id} $ represents the identity function, and ^ denotes functional power.

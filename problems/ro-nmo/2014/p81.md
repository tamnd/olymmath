Fie $a, b, c \in (0, \infty)$. Demonstraţi inegalitatea:
$$\frac{a - \sqrt{bc}}{a + 2(b + c)} + \frac{b - \sqrt{ca}}{b + 2(c + a)} + \frac{c - \sqrt{ab}}{c + 2(a + b)} \ge 0.$$

Let be a ring $ A, $ having the property that any element in the set $ A\setminus U(A) $ is nilpotent. Show that

a) $ axa=0, \forall x\in A, \forall a\in A\setminus U(A) . $
b) $ \mathbb{N}\ni |A|\ge 2 \implies\left(\exists d\in A\setminus \left( U(A)\cup\{ 0 \}\right) d'\in A\setminus U(A)\implies dd'=d'd=0\right) . $

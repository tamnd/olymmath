Let $a>0$ and $\mathcal{F} = \{f:[0,1] \to \mathbb{R} : f \text{ is concave and } f(0)=1 \}.$ Determine $$ \min_{f \in \mathcal{F}} \bigg\{ \left( \int_0^1 f(x)dx\right)^2 - (a+1) \int_0^1 x^{2a}f(x)dx \bigg\}. $$

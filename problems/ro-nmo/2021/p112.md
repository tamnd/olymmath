Pentru $n \ge 2$ numere reale nenule $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$, nu neapărat distincte, definim matricea $A = (a_{ij})_{1 \le i, j \le n} \in \mathcal{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$ prin $a_{ij} = \max\{a_i, a_j\}$, $\forall\, i, j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. Arătaţi că $\operatorname{rang}(A) = \operatorname{card}\{a_k \mid k = 1, 2, \ldots, n\}$.

Let $n \ge 2$ and $ a_1, a_2, \ldots , a_n $ , nonzero real numbers not necessarily distinct. We define matrix $A = (a_{ij})_{1 \le i,j \le n} \in M_n( \mathbb{R} )$ , $a_{i,j} = max \{ a_i, a_j \}$ , $\forall i,j \in \{ 1,2 , \ldots , n \} $ . Show that $\mathbf{rank}(A) $ = $\mathbf{card} $ $\{ a_k | k = 1,2, \ldots n \} $

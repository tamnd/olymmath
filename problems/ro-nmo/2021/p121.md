Determinaţi funcţiile continue $f : [0, 1] \longrightarrow [0, \infty)$ care verifică relaţia
$$\int_0^1 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \cdot \int_0^1 f^2(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \cdot \ldots \cdot \int_0^1 f^{2020}(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = \left( \int_0^1 f^{2021}(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \right)^{1010}.$$

Let $ p > 2$ be a prime number. Find the least positive number $ a$ which can be represented as
$$ a = (X - 1)f(X) + (X^{p - 1} + X^{p - 2} + \cdots + X + 1)g(X),
$$
where $ f(X)$ and $ g(X)$ are integer polynomials.

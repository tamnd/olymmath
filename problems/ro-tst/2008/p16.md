Let $ n \geq 3$ be a positive integer and let $ m \geq 2^{n-1}+1$ . Prove that for each family of nonzero distinct subsets $ (A_j)_{j \in \overline{1, m}}$ of $ \{1, 2, ..., n\}$ there exist $ i$ , $ j$ , $ k$ such that $ A_i \cup A_j = A_k$ .

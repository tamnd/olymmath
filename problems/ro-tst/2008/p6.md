Let $ n \geq 3$ be an odd integer. Determine the maximum value of
$$ \sqrt{|x_{1}-x_{2}|}+\sqrt{|x_{2}-x_{3}|}+\ldots+\sqrt{|x_{n-1}-x_{n}|}+\sqrt{|x_{n}-x_{1}|},$$
where $ x_{i}$ are positive real numbers from the interval $ [0,1]$ .

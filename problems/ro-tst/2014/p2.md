Let $n \ge 2$ be an integer. Show that there exist $n+1$ numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{n+1} \in \mathbb{Q} \setminus \mathbb{Z}$ , so that $\{ x_1^3 \} + \{ x_2^3 \} + \cdots + \{ x_n^3 \}=\{ x_{n+1}^3 \}$ , where $\{ x \}$ is the fractionary part of $x$ .

Let $a$ be a real number in the open interval $(0,1)$ . Let $n\geq 2$ be a positive integer and let $f_n\colon\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be defined by $f_n(x) = x+\frac{x^2}{n}$ . Show that
$$\frac{a(1-a)n^2+2a^2n+a^3}{(1-a)^2n^2+a(2-a)n+a^2}<(f_n \circ\ \cdots\ \circ f_n)(a)<\frac{an+a^2}{(1-a)n+a}$$ where there are $n$ functions in the composition.

Let $f(n)$ be number of numbers $x \in \{1,2,\cdots ,n\}$ , $n\in\mathbb{N}$ , such that $gcd(x, n)$ is either $1$ or prime. Prove $$ \sum_{d|n} f(d) + \varphi(n) \geq 2n $$ For which $n$ does equality hold?

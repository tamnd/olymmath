Prove that we can find numbers $\mathbf{a}_1$ , ..., $\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$ such that $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{ij}} = \mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{i}} - \mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{j}}$ .

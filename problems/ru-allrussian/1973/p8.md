At time 1, n unit squares of an infinite sheet of paper ruled in squares are painted black, the rest remain white. At time $\mathrm{k + 1}$ , the color of each square is changed to the color held at time k by a majority of the following three squares: the square itself, its northern neighbour and its eastern neighbour. Prove that all the squares are white at time $\mathrm{n + 1}$ .

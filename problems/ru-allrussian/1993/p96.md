Inside a circle, there is a convex quadrilateral, the extensions of whose sides intersect it at points $A_{1}, A_{2}, B_{1}, B_{2}, C_{1}, C_{2}, D_{1}$ and $D_{2} 960$. Prove that if $A_{1} B_{2}=B_{1} C_{2}=C_{1} D_{2}=D_{1} A_{2}$, then the quadrilateral formed by the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}, C_{1} C_{2}, D_{1} D_{2}$ can be inscribed in a circle.

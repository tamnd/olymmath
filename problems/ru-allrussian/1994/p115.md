Given a sequence of natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}, \cdots$, where $a_{1}$ is not divisible by 5, and for each $n$, the equation
$$
a_{n+1}=a_{n}+b_{n} \text {, }
$$

holds, where $b_{n}$ is the last digit of $a_{n}$. Prove: the sequence contains infinitely many powers of 2.

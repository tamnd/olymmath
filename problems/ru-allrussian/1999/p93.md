Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in circle $S$. Let $A_{0}$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ of circle $S$ not containing point $A$, and $C_{0}$ be the midpoint of the arc of circle $S$ not containing point $C$. Circle $S_{1}$ with center $A_{0}$ is tangent to $BC$, and circle $S_{2}$ with center $C_{0}$ is tangent to $AB$. Prove that the center $I$ of the inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$ lies on one of the common external tangents to circles $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$.

In an acute scalene triangle $ABC$, the bisector of the angle between the altitudes $AA_1$ and $CC_1$ intersects the sides $AB$ and $BC$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. The bisector of angle $B$ intersects the segment connecting the orthocenter $H$ of triangle $ABC$ with the midpoint $M$ of side $AC$ at point $R$. Prove that points $P, B, Q$, and $R$ lie on the same circle.

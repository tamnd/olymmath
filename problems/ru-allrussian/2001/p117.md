The $2001$ towns in a country are connected by some roads, at least one road from each town, so that no town is connected by a road to every other city. We call a set $D$ of towns *dominant* if every town not in $D$ is connected by a road to a town in $D$ . Suppose that each dominant set consists of at least $k$ towns. Prove that the country can be partitioned into $2001-k$ republics in such a way that no two towns in the same republic are connected by a road.

Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is circumscribed around a circle. The bisectors of the external angles $A$ and $B$ intersect at point $K$, the bisectors of the external angles $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $L$, the bisectors of the external angles $C$ and $D$ intersect at point $M$, and the bisectors of the external angles $D$ and $A$ intersect at point $N$. Let $K_{1}$, $L_{1}$, $M_{1}$, $N_{1}$ be the points of intersection of the altitudes of triangles $ABK$, $BCL$, $CDM$, $DAN$ respectively. Prove that quadrilateral $K_{1} L_{1} M_{1} N_{1}$ is a parallelogram.

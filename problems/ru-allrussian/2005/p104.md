Circles $\sigma_{B}, \sigma_{C}$ are the excircles of triangle $ABC$ (touching sides $AC$ and $AB$ and the extensions of the other two sides, respectively). Circle $\omega_{B}$ is symmetric to $\sigma_{B}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $AC$, and circle $\omega_{C}$ is symmetric to $\sigma_{C}$ with respect to the midpoint of side $AB$. Prove that the line passing through the points of intersection of circles $\omega_{B}$ and $\omega_{C}$ bisects the perimeter of triangle $ABC$.

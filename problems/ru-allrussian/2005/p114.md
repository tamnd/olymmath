Let positive integers $x, y, z (x>2, y>1)$ satisfy the equation $x^{y}+1=z^{2}$. Let $p$ denote the number of distinct prime factors of $x$, and let $q$ denote the number of distinct prime factors of $y$. Prove: $p \geqslant q+2$.

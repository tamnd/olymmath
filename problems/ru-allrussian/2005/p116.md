Can 12 rectangular parallelepipeds $P_{1}, P_{2}, \ldots, P_{12}$, whose edges are parallel to the coordinate axes $O x, O y, O z$, be placed in space such that $P_{2}$ intersects (i.e., has at least one common point) with each of the remaining, except $P_{1}$ and $P_{3}$, $P_{3}$ intersects with each of the remaining, except $P_{2}$ and $P_{4}$, and so on, $P_{12}$ intersects with each of the remaining, except $P_{11}$ and $P_{1}$, and $P_{1}$ intersects with each of the remaining, except $P_{12}$ and $P_{2}$? (The surface of the parallelepiped belongs to it.)

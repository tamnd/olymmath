For the tetrahedron $SABC$, the incircle $I$ of the face $ABC$ touches the edges $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$ at points $D$, $E$, and $F$, respectively. Points $A'$, $B'$, and $C'$ are taken on the edges $SA$, $SB$, and $SC$ such that $AA' = AD$, $BB' = BE$, and $CC' = CF$. Let $S'$ be the antipodal point of $S$ on the circumsphere of the tetrahedron. Given that $SI$ is the altitude of the tetrahedron. Prove: The distances from point $S'$ to points $A'$, $B'$, and $C'$ are equal.

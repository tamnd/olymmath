Let $k>10$. Prove: In the expression
$$
f(x)=\cos x \cos 2 x \cos 3 x \cdots \cos 2^{k} x
$$

one $\cos$ can be replaced by $\sin$, such that the resulting $f_{1}(x)$, for all real numbers $x$, satisfies
$$
\left|f_{1}(x)\right| \leqslant \frac{3}{2^{k+1}} \text {. }
$$

An acute triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle $\Omega$. The tangents to $\Omega$ at points $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $P$. Points $D$ and $E$ are the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from point $P$ to the lines $AB$ and $AC$. Prove that the orthocenter of triangle $ADE$ is the midpoint of segment $BC$.

(P. Kozhevnikov)

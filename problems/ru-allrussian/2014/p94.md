Segments $BP$ and $AC$ intersect at point $S$. Let $AD$ be the altitude of triangle $BP$. The circumcircle $\omega$ of triangle $CSD$ intersects the circle $\Omega$ again at point $K$. Prove that $\angle CKM = 90^{\circ}$.

A football match was played between a team of liars (who always lie) and a team of truth-lovers (who always tell the truth). The match was 10 on 10 players. After the match, each player was asked: "How many goals did you score?" Some of the participants answered "one," Misha said "two," some answered "three," and the rest said "five." Is Misha lying if the truth-lovers won with a score of $20: 17$?

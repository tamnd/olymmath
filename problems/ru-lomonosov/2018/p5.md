In the park, landscaping work was carried out for two years: old trees were cut down and new ones were planted. The project leaders claim that the average growth in the number of trees over two years is $15 \%$. Environmentalists say that the number of trees has decreased by $10 \%$ over two years. Could both of these statements be true? (If the number of trees increased in a year, the growth is considered positive, if it decreased - then negative. The leaders calculate the average growth over two years as $(a+b) / 2$, where $a$ is the growth for the first year, $b$ is for the second.)

A cottage village has dimensions $n \times m$ of identical square plots. Owners began to fence their plots one by one. The cost of the fence between any two adjacent plots was 10 thousand rubles, and it was fully covered by the neighbor who fenced their plot first (expenses were not shared between neighbors, meaning some could have spent nothing at all). In the end, all plots were fenced on all four sides. Could it be that in the end, an equal number of residents spent 0, 10, 30, and 40 thousand rubles on the fence, while the rest spent 20 thousand rubles?

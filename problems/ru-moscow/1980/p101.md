Consider the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{n}, \ldots$ of increasing natural numbers. It is known that $a_{n+1} \leq 10 a_{n}$ for all natural $n$.

Prove that the infinite decimal fraction $0, a_{1} a_{2} a_{3} \ldots$, obtained by concatenating these numbers, is non-periodic.

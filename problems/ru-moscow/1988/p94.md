Let $x$ and $y$ be natural numbers. Consider the function $f(x, y)=\frac{1}{2}(x+y-1)(x+y-2)+y$. Prove that the set of values of this function includes all natural numbers, and that for any natural number $i=f(x, y)$, the numbers $x$ and $y$ are uniquely determined.

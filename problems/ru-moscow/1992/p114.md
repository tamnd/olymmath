Inside a tetrahedron, there is a triangle whose projections on the 4 faces of the tetrahedron have areas $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$, $P_{4}$. Prove that a) in a regular tetrahedron $P_{1} \leq P_{2}+P_{3}+P_{4}$; b) if $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}, S_{4}$ are the areas of the corresponding faces of the tetrahedron, then $P_{1} S_{1} \leq P_{2} S_{2}+P_{3} S_{3}+P_{4} S_{4}$.

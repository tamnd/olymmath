The device for comparing numbers $\log _{a} b$ and $\log _{c} d(a, b, c, d>1)$ operates according to the following rules: if $b>a$ and $d>c$, it transitions to comparing the numbers $\log _{a} \frac{b}{a}$ and $\log _{c} \frac{d}{c}$. If $b<a$ and $d<c$, it transitions to comparing the numbers $\log _{d} c$ and $\log _{b} a$. If $(b-a)(d-c) \leq 0$, it provides the answer.

a) Show how the device will compare the numbers $\log _{25} 75$ and $\log _{65} 260$.

b) Prove that it will compare any two unequal logarithms in a finite number of steps.

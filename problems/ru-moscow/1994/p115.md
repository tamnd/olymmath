Consider a convex quadrilateral ABCD. The pairs of its opposite sides are extended to intersect: AB and CD - at point P, CB and DA - at point Q. Let $l_{A}, l_{B}, l_{C}$, and $l_{D}$ be the bisectors of the external angles of the quadrilateral at vertices A, B, C, and D, respectively. Let $l_{P}$ and $l_{Q}$ be the external bisectors of angles $APD$ and $AQB$ (i.e., the bisectors of the angles that complete these angles to a straight angle). Denote by $M_{AC}$ the intersection of $l_{A}$ and $l_{C}$, by $M_{BD}$ the intersection of $l_{B}$ and $l_{D}$, and by $M_{PQ}$ the intersection of $l_{P}$ and $l_{Q}$. Prove that if all three points $M_{AC}, M_{BD}$, and $M_{PQ}$ exist, then they lie on the same line.

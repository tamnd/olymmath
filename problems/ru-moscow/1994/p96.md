In a square of grid paper $10 \times 10$, one ship $1 \times 4$, two $1 \times 3$, three $1 \times 2$, and four $1 \times 1$ need to be placed. Ships should not share any points (even vertices) with each other, but they can touch the borders of the square. Prove that

a) if they are placed in the order specified above (starting with the larger ones), the process can always be completed, even if at each step only the current ship is considered, without thinking about the future;

b) if they are placed in the reverse order (starting with the smaller ones), a situation may arise where the next ship cannot be placed (provide an example).

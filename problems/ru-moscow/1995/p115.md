For what largest $n$ can one invent two infinite in both directions sequences $A$ and $B$ such that any segment of sequence $B$ of length $n$ is contained in $A$, $A$ has a period of 1995, and $B$ does not have this property (is non-periodic or has a period of a different length)?

Comment. The sequences can consist of arbitrary symbols. The question refers to the minimal period.

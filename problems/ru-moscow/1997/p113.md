On the board, three functions are written:

$$
f_{1}(x)=x+\frac{1}{x}, f_{2}(x)=x^{2}, f_{3}(x)=(x-1)^{2}
$$

You can add, subtract, and multiply these functions (including squaring, cubing, ...), multiply them by any number, add any number to them, and perform these operations on the resulting expressions. Obtain the function $1 / x$ in this way. Prove that if any of the functions $f_{1}, f_{2}, f_{3}$ is erased from the board, it is impossible to obtain $1 / x$.

A regular triangle $ABC$ cut out of plywood was placed on the floor. Three nails were hammered into the floor (one next to each side of the triangle) in such a way that the triangle cannot be rotated without lifting it off the floor. The first nail divides side $AB$ in the ratio $1:3$, counting from vertex $A$, the second nail divides side $BC$ in the ratio $2:1$, counting from vertex $B$. In what ratio does the third nail divide side $AC$?

Some of the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{200}$ are written in blue pencil, and the rest in red. If all the red numbers are erased, the remaining numbers are all natural numbers from 1 to 100, written in ascending order. If all the blue numbers are erased, the remaining numbers are all natural numbers from 100 to 1, written in descending order. Prove that among the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{100}$, all natural numbers from 1 to 100 inclusive are contained.

A grasshopper jumps along the segment $[0,1]$. In one jump, it can go from a point $x$ either to the point $x/\sqrt{3}$ or to the point $x/\sqrt{3}+\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$. A point $a$ is chosen on the segment $[0,1]$.

Prove that, starting from any point, the grasshopper can, after several jumps, end up within a distance of $1 / 100$ from the point $a$.

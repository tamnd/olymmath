Find all such positive integers $\mathrm{k}$, for which the number

$1 \ldots 12 \ldots 2-2 \ldots 2$

is a square of an integer.

(In the first addend (minuend), there are a total of 2000 digits, of which the last $\mathrm{k}$ digits are "2", and the rest are "1";

the second addend (subtrahend) consists of 1001 consecutive digits "2")

In a non-isosceles triangle $ABC$, point $I$ is the center of the inscribed circle, $I'$ is the center of the circle that touches side $AB$ and the extensions of sides $CB$ and $CA$; $L$ and $L'$ are the points where side $AB$ touches these circles. Prove that the lines $IL'$, $I'L$, and the altitude $CH$ of triangle $ABC$ intersect at one point.

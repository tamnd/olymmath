Lyosha thought of a two-digit number (from 10 to 99). Grisha is trying to guess it by naming two-digit numbers. It is considered that he has guessed if he named one digit correctly and the other digit is off by no more than one (for example, if the number 65 is thought of, then 65, 64, and 75 are correct, but 63, 76, and 56 are not). Come up with a method that guarantees Grisha success in 22 attempts (no matter what number Lyosha thinks of).

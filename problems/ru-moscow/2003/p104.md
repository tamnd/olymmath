Let $M$ be the point of intersection of the medians of triangle $ABC$. On the perpendiculars dropped from $M$ to the sides $BC$, $AC$, and $AB$, points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$ are taken respectively, such that $A_1 B_1 \perp MC$ and $A_1 C_1 \perp MB$. Prove that $M$ is also the point of intersection of the medians in triangle $A_1 B_1 C_1$.

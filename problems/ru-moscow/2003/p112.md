Given a polynomial $P(x)$ of degree 2003 with real coefficients, and the leading coefficient is 1. There is an infinite sequence of integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ such that $P\left(a_{1}\right)=0, P\left(a_{2}\right)=a_{1}$, $P\left(a_{3}\right)=a_{2}$, and so on. Prove that not all numbers in the sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots$ are distinct.

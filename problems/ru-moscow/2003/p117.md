Given the equality

$$
\left(a^{m_{1}}-1\right) \ldots\left(a^{m_{n}}-1\right)=\left(a^{k_{1}}+1\right) \ldots\left(a^{k_{l}}+1\right)
$$

where $a, n, l$ and all exponents are natural numbers, and $a>1$. Find all possible values of the number $a$.

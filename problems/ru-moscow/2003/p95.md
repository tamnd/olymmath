A right triangle $ABC$ with hypotenuse $AB$ is inscribed in a circle. Let $K$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ not containing point $A$, $N$ be the midpoint of segment $AC$, and $M$ be the point of intersection of ray $KN$ with the circle. Tangents to the circle at points $A$ and $C$ intersect at point $E$. Prove that $\angle E M K=90^{\circ}$.

Before a clairvoyant lies a deck of 36 cards face down (4 suits, 9 cards of each suit). He names the suit of the top card, after which the card is revealed to him. Then he names the suit of the next card, and so on. The task of the clairvoyant is to guess the suit as many times as possible. The backs of the cards are asymmetrical, and the clairvoyant can see which of the two positions the top card is in. The clairvoyant's assistant knows the order of the cards in the deck, cannot change it, but can arrange the back of each card in one way or another. Could the clairvoyant have agreed with the assistant, when the latter still did not know the order of the cards, to ensure guessing the suit of at least

a) 19 cards;

b) 23 cards?

In space, there are 200 points. Each pair of them is connected by a segment, such that no two segments intersect at interior points. Each segment is colored in one of $k$ colors. Peter wants to color the given 200 points in the same colors such that no two points are simultaneously colored in the color of the segment connecting them. Is this always possible if

a) $k=7$?

b) $k=10$?

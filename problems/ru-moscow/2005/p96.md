Given an acute-angled triangle $ABC$ and a point $P$ that does not coincide with the orthocenter of the triangle. Prove that the circles passing through the midpoints of the sides of triangles $PAB$, $PAC$, $PBC$, and $ABC$, as well as the circle passing through the projections of point $P$ onto the sides of triangle $ABC$, intersect at one point.

Given a triangle $ABC$ and points $P$ and $Q$ lying on its circumscribed circle. Point $P$ is reflected over the line $BC$ to obtain point $P_a$. The intersection point of the lines $QP_a$ and $BC$ is denoted as $A'$. Points $B'$ and $C'$ are constructed similarly. Prove that the points $A', B'$, and $C'$ lie on the same line.

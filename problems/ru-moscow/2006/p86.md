Vanya receives exactly one grade ("3", "4", or "5") for each of seven subjects every week. He considers a week successful if the number of subjects with improved grades exceeds the number of subjects with worsened grades by at least two. It turned out that $n$ weeks in a row were successful, and in the last of these weeks, the grade for each subject exactly matched the grade of the first week. What could the number $n$ be?

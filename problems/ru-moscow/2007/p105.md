Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle with center at $O$. $X$ is an arbitrary point inside triangle $ABC$ such that $\angle XAB = \angle XBC = \phi$, and $P$ is a point such that $PX \perp OX$, $\angle XOP = \phi$, and the angles $\angle XOP$ and $\angle XAB$ are similarly oriented. Prove that all such points $P$ lie on a single straight line.

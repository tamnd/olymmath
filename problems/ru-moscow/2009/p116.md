Prove that for any partition of one hundred "two-digit" numbers 00, 01, ..., 99 into two groups, some numbers in at least one of the groups can be arranged in a sequence such that any two adjacent numbers in this sequence differ by 1, 10, or 11, and in at least one of the two digits (units or tens) all 10 different digits appear.

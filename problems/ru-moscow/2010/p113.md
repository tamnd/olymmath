Prove that if the numbers $x, y, z$ for some values of $p$ and $q$ are solutions to the system

$$
y=x^{n}+p x+q, z=y^{n}+p y+q, x=z^{n}+p z+q,
$$

then the inequality $x^{2} y+y^{2} z+z^{2} x \geq x^{2} z+y^{2} x+z^{2} y$ holds.

Consider the cases a) $n=2$; b) $n=2010$.

*(O. N. Kosukhin)*

On a flat horizontal platform, there are five projectors, each emitting a laser beam at one of two acute angles $\alpha$ or $\beta$ to the platform and can only rotate around a vertical axis passing through the vertex of the beam. It is known that any four of these projectors can be turned so that all four emitted beams intersect at one point. Is it necessarily possible to turn all five projectors so that all five beams intersect at one point?

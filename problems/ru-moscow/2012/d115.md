Let $S(n, k)$ denote the number of coefficients in the expansion of the polynomial $(x+1)^{n}$ in powers of $x$ that are not divisible by $k$.

a) Find $S(2012,3)$.

b) Prove that $S\left(2012^{2011}, 2011\right)$ is divisible by 2012.

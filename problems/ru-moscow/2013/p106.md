Let $I$ be the center of the inscribed circle of a non-isosceles triangle $ABC$. Let $A_1$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ that does not contain point $A$, and let $A_2$ be the midpoint of the arc $BAC$. The perpendicular dropped from point $A_1$ to the line $A_2 I$ intersects the line $BC$ at point $A'$. Points $B'$ and $C'$ are defined similarly.

a) Prove that the points $A', B'$, and $C'$ lie on the same line.

b) Prove that this line is perpendicular to the line $OI$, where $O$ is the center of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$.

One hundred wise men want to travel by a train consisting of 12 cars from the first to the 76th station. They know that at the first station, two inspectors will board the train in two cars. After the fourth station, on each section, one of the inspectors will move to the adjacent car, and they will "walk" in turns. A wise man can see an inspector only if the inspector is in the adjacent car or the car next to the adjacent one. At each station, each wise man can run along the platform no further than three cars (for example, from the 7th car, a wise man can reach any car from 4 to 10 and board it). What is the maximum number of wise men who can avoid being in the same car with an inspector at any time, regardless of how the inspectors move? (The wise men do not receive any information about the inspectors other than what is stated in the problem. The wise men agree on a strategy in advance.)

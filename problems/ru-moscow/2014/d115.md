The surface of the convex polyhedron $A_{1} B_{1} C_{1} A_{2} B_{2} C_{2}$ consists of eight triangular faces $A_{i} B_{j} C_{k}$, where $i, j, k$ vary from 1 to 2. A sphere with center at point $O$ touches all these faces. Prove that the point $O$ and the midpoints of the three segments $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}$ and $C_{1} C_{2}$ lie in the same plane.

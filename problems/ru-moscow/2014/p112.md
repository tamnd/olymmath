Find all values of $a$ for which there exist such $x, y$ and $z$ that the numbers $\cos x, \cos y$ and $\cos z$ are pairwise distinct and
form an arithmetic progression in the given order, and the numbers $\cos (x+a), \cos (y+a)$ and $\cos (z+a)$ also form an arithmetic progression in the given order.

*(D. V. Goryashin)*

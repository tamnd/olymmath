The radical of a natural number $N$ (denoted $\operatorname{rad}(N)$) is the product of all distinct prime divisors of $N$. For example,

$\operatorname{rad}(120)=2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5=30$. Does there exist a triple of pairwise coprime natural numbers $A, B, C$, such that $A$ $+B=C$ and $C>1000 \operatorname{rad}(A B C)$?

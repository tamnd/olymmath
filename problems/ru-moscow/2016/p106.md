In a round-robin hockey tournament, 2016 teams participated. According to the tournament regulations, 3 points are awarded for a win, 0 points for a loss, and in case of a draw, extra time is played, with the winner receiving 2 points and the loser 1 point. At the end of the tournament, Ostap Bender was informed of the number of points scored by each team, based on which he concluded that no fewer than $N$ matches went to extra time. Find the maximum possible value of $N$.

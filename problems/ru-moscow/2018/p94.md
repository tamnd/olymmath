Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ with pairwise non-parallel sides. A point $P$ is chosen arbitrarily on side $AD$, distinct from $A$ and $D$. The circumcircles of triangles $ABP$ and $CDP$ intersect again at point $Q$. Prove that the line $PQ$ passes through a fixed point, independent of the choice of point $P$.

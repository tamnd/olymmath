A natural number divisible by 7 is printed on the computer screen, and the cursor is located between some two adjacent digits of this number. Prove that there exists such a digit that, if it is typed into this gap any number of times, all the resulting numbers will also be divisible by 7. For example, all numbers 259, 2569, 25669, 256669, ..., as well as 2359, 23359, 233359, ... are divisible by 7.

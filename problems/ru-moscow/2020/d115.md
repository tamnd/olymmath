A grasshopper is jumping along the number line. Points $-a$ and $b$ are marked on the number line, where $a$ and $b$ are positive numbers, and their ratio is irrational. If the grasshopper is at a point closer to $-a$, it jumps to the right by a distance of $a$; if it is at the midpoint of the interval $[-a, b]$ or closer to $b$, it jumps to the left by a distance of $b$. Prove that, regardless of the grasshopper's initial position, it will eventually be at a point whose distance from 0 is less than $10^{-6}$.

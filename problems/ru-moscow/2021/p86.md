In a certain state, there are 32 cities, every two of which are connected by a road with one-way traffic. The Minister of Communications, a secret villain, decided to organize traffic in such a way that after leaving any city, it would be impossible to return to it. To achieve this, he can change the direction of traffic on one of the roads every day, starting from June 1, 2021. Prove that he can achieve his goal by 2022 (that is, he has 214 days).

In a volleyball championship with 16 teams, a single round-robin format was used (each team played against each other exactly once, and there are no draws in volleyball). It turned out that some two teams won the same number of matches. Prove that there will be three teams that won against each other in a cycle (i.e., $A$ won against $B$, $B$ won against $C$, and $C$ won against $A$).

We have perfectly accurate two-pan scales and a set of 50 weights, the weights of which are equal to $\operatorname{arctg} 1$, $\operatorname{arctg} \frac{1}{2}$, $\operatorname{arctg} \frac{1}{3}$, ..., $\operatorname{arctg} \frac{1}{50}$. Prove that it is possible to choose 10 of them and distribute 5 weights on each pan of the scales so that equilibrium is established.

*(M. A. Evdokimov)*

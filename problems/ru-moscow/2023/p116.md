Let's call a triplet of numbers a "triplet" if one of them is equal to the arithmetic mean of the other two. Given an infinite sequence $\left(a_n\right)$, consisting of natural numbers, it is known that $a_1 = a_2 = 1$ and for $n > 2$, the number $a_n$ is the smallest natural number such that among the numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ there are no three forming a triplet. Prove that $a_n \leqslant \frac{n^2 + 7}{8}$ for any $n$.

*(B. Butyrin)*

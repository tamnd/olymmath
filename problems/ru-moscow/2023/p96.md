Let's call a triplet of numbers a "triplet" if one of them is equal to the arithmetic mean of the other two.

The sequence $(a_n)$ is constructed as follows: $a_0 = 0$, $a_1 = 1$, and for $n > 1$, $a_n$ is the smallest natural number greater than $a_{n-1}$ such that among the numbers $a_0$, $a_1$, ..., $a_n$ there are no three numbers that form a triplet. Prove that $a_{2023} \leqslant 100\,000$.

*(B. Butyrin)*

Let $ A$ and $ B$ be two disjoint sets in the interval $ (0,1)$ . Denoting by $ \mu$ the Lebesgue measure on the real line, let $ \mu(A)>0$ and $ \mu(B)>0$ . Let further $ n$ be a positive integer and $ \lambda =\frac1n$ . Show that there exists a subinterval $ (c,d)$ of $ (0,1)$ for which $ \mu(A\cap (c,d))=\lambda \mu(A)$ and $ \mu(B\cap (c,d))=\lambda \mu(B)$ . Show further that this is not true if $ \lambda$ is not of the form $ \frac1n$ .

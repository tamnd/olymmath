Let $ f(x)$ be a polynomial of second degree the roots of which are contained in the interval $ [-1,+1]$ and let there be a point $ x_0\in [-1.+1]$ such that $ |f(x_0)|=1$ . Prove that for every $ \alpha \in [0,1]$ , there exists a $ \zeta \in [-1,+1]$ such that $ |f'(\zeta)|=\alpha$ and that this statement is not true if $ \alpha>1$ .

Find the complex numbers $ z$ for which the series
$$ 1 + \frac {z}{2!} + \frac {z(z + 1)}{3!} + \frac {z(z + 1)(z + 2)}{4!} + \cdots + \frac {z(z + 1)\cdots(z + n)}{(n + 2)!} + \cdots$$
converges and find its sum.

Prove that, for every pair $ n$ , $r$ of positive integers, there can be found a polynomial $ f(x)$ of degree $ n$ with integer coefficients, so that every polynomial $ g(x)$ of degree at most $ n$ , for which the coefficients of the polynomial $ f(x)-g(x)$ are integers with absolute value not greater than $ r$ , is irreducible over the field of rational numbers.

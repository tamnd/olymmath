Prove that the infinite series
$ 1-\frac{1}{x(x+1)}-\frac{x-1}{2!x^2(2x+1)}-\frac{(x-1)(2x-1)}{3!(x^3(3x+1))}-\frac{(x-1)(2x-1)(3x-1)}{4!x^4(4x+1)}-\cdots$
is convergent for every positive $ x$ . Denoting its sum by $ F(x)$ , find $ \lim_{x\to +0}F(x)$ and $ \lim_{x\to \infty}F(x)$ .

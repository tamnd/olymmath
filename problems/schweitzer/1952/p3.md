Prove:If $ a=p_1^{\alpha_1}p_2^{\alpha_2}\cdots p_{n}^{\alpha_n}$ is a perfect number, then
$ 2<\prod_{i=1}^n\frac{p_i}{p_i-1}<4$ ;
if moreover, $ a$ is odd, then the upper bound $ 4$ may be reduced to $ 2\sqrt[3]{2}$ .

Let $ G$ be anon-commutative group. Consider all the one-to-one mappings $ a\rightarrow a'$ of $ G$ onto itself such that $ (ab)'=b'a'$ (i.e. the anti-automorphisms of $ G$ ). Prove that this mappings together with the automorphisms of $ G$ constitute a group which contains the group of the automorphisms of $ G$ as direct factor.

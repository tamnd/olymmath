Given a positive integer $r>1$ , prove that there exists an infinite number of infinite geometrical series, with positive terms, having the sum 1 and satisfying the following condition: for any positive real numbers $S_{1},S_{2},\dots,S_{r}$ such that $S_{1}+S_{2}+\dots+S_{r}=1$ , any of these infinite geometrical series can be divided into $r$ infinite series(not necessarily geometrical) having the sums $S_{1},S_{2},\dots,S_{r}$ , respectively. **(S. 6)**

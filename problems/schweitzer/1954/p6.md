Prove or disprove the following two propositions:
(i) If $a$ and $b$ are positive integers such that $a<b$, then in any set of $b$ consecutive integers there are two whose product is divisible by $ab$
(ii) If $a,b$ and $c$ are positive integers such that $a<b<c$, then in any set of $c$ consecutive integers there are three whose product is divisible by $abc$. (N.8)

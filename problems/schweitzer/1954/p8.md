Prove the following generalization of the well-known Chinese remainder theorem: Let $R$ be a ring with unit element and let $A_{1},A_{2},\dots . A_{n} (n\geqslant 2)$ be pairwise relative prime ideals of $R$ . Then, for arbitrary elements $c_{1},c_{2}, \dots , c_{n}$ of $R$ , there exists an element $x\in R$ such that $x-c_{k} \in A_{k} (k= 1,2, \dots , n)$ . **(A. 17)**

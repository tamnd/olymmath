Lep $p$ be a connected non-closed broken line without self-intersection in the plane $\varphi $ . Prove that if $v$ is a non-zero vector in $\varphi $ and $p$ has a commom point with the broken line $p+v$ , then $p$ has a common point with the broken line $p+\alpha v$ too, where $\alpha =\frac{1}{n}$ and $n$ is a positive integer. Does a similar statemente hold for other positive values of $\alpha$ ? ( $p+v$ denotes the broken line obtained from $p$ through displacemente by the vector $v$ .) **(G. 1)**

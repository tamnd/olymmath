Show that to any elliptic paraboloid $\varphi_1$ there may be found an elliptic paraboloid $\varphi_2$ (other than $\varphi_1$ ) and an affinity $\phi$ which maps $\varphi_1$ onto $\varphi_2$ and has the following property: If $P$ is any point of $\varphi_1$ such that $\phi(P) \neq P$ , then the straight line connecting $P$ and $\phi(P)$ is a common tangent of the two paraboloids. **(G. 18)**

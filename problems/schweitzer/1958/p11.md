Let $a_n = (-1)^n (n= 1, 2, \dots , 2N)$ . Denote by $A_{N}(x)$ the number of the sequences $1 \leq i_1 < i_2< \dots <i_N \leq 2N$ such that $a_{i_1}+a_{i_2}+ \dots +a_{i_N}< x \sqrt{\frac{N}{2}} (-\infty < x < \infty)$ . Show that $\lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{A_{N}(x)}{\binom{2N}{N}} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{u^2}{2}} du$ .**(N. 16)**

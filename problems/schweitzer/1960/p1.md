Consider in the plane a set $H$ of pairwise disjoint circles of radius 1 such that, for infinitely many positive integers $n$ , the circle $k_n$ with centre at the origin and of radius $n$ contains at least $cn^2$ elements of the set $H$ . Prove that there exists a straight line which intersects infinitely many of the circles of $H$ . Show further that if we require only that the circles $k_n$ contain o(n²) elements of $H$ , the proposition will be false. **(G. 5)**

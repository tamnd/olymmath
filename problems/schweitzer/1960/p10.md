A car is used by $n$ drivers. Every morning the drivers choose by drawing that one of them who will drive the car that day. Let us define the random variable $\mu (n)$ as the least positive integer such that each driver drives at least one day during the first $\mu (n)$ days. Find the limit distribution of the random variable $\frac {\mu (n) -n \log n}{n}$ as $n \to \infty$ . **(P. 9)**

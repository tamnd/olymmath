Let $f$ be a bounded real function defined on the unit cube $H$ of the $n$ -dimensional space and, for a given $y$ , let $A_y$ and $B_y$ denote the parts of the interior of $H$ on which $f>y$ and $f<y$ , respectively. Show that $f$ is integrable in the Riemannian sense if and only if for every $y$ almost all points of $A_y$ and $B_y$ are inner points. **(R. 9)**

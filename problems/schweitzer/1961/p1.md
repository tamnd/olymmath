Let $a$ ( $\neq e$ , the unit element) be an element of finite order of a group $G$ and let $t$ ( $\geq 2$ ) be a positive integer. Show: if the complex $A= \{ e,a,a^2, \dots , a^{t-1} \} $ is not a group, then for every positive integer $k$ ( $2 \leq k \leq t$ ) the complex $B= \{ e. a^k, a^{2k}, \dots , a^{(t-1)k} \} $ differs from $A$ . **(A. 16)**

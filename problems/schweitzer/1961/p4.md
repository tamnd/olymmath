Let $f(x)$ be a real- or complex-value integrable function on $(0,1)$ with $\mid f(x) \mid \leq 1 $ . Set $ c_k = \int_0^1 f(x) e^{-2 \pi i k x} dx $ and construct the following matrices of order $n$ : $ T= (t_{pq})_{p,q=0}^{n-1}, T^{*}= (t_{pq}^{*})_{p,q =0}^{n-1} $ where $t_{pq}= c_{q-p}, t^{*}= \overline {c_{p-q}}$ . Further, consider the following hyper-matrix of order $m$ : $
S= \begin{bmatrix}
E & T & T^2 & \dots & T^{m-2} & T^{m-1}
T^{*} & E & T & \dots & T^{m-3} & T^{m-2}
T^{*2} & T^{*} & E & \dots & T^{m-3} & T^{m-2}
\dots & \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots
T^{*m-1} & T^{*m-2} & T^{*m-3} & \dots & T^{*} & E
\end{bmatrix} $ ( $S$ is a matrix of order $mn$ in the ordinary sense; E denotes the unit matrix of order $n$ ).
Show that for any pair $(m , n) $ of positive integers, $S$ has only non-negative real eigenvalues. **(R. 19)**

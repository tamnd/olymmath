Let $ f$ be a finite real function of one variable. Let $ \overline{D}f$ and $ \underline{D}f$ be its upper and lower derivatives, respectively, that is, $$ \overline{D}f=\limsup_{{h,k\rightarrow 0}_{{h,k \geq 0}_{h+k>0}}} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x-k)}{h+k}$$ ,
$$ \underline{D}f=\liminf_{{h,k\rightarrow 0}_{{h,k \geq 0}_{h+k>0}}} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x-k)}{h+k}.$$ Show that $ \overline{D}f$ and $ \underline{D}f$ are Borel-measurable functions. [A. Csaszar]

Denote by $ M(r,f)$ the maximum modulus on the circle $ |z|=r$ of the transcendent entire function $ f(z)$ , and by $ M_n(r,f)$ that of the $ nth$ partial sum of the power series of $ f(z)$ . Prove that the existence of an entire function $ f_0(z)$ and a corresponding sequence of positive numbers $ r_1<r_2<...\rightarrow +\infty$ such that $$ \limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}
\frac{M_n(r_n,f_0)}{M(r_n,f_0)}=+\infty$$ [P. Turan]

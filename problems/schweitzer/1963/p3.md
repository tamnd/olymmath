Let $ R=R_1\oplus R_2$ be the direct sum of the rings $ R_1$ and $ R_2$ , and let $ N_2$ be the annihilator ideal of $ R_2$ (in $ R_2$ ). Prove that $ R_1$ will be an ideal in every ring $ \widetilde{R}$ containing $ R$ as an ideal if and only if the only homomorphism from $ R_1$ to $ N_2$ is the zero homomorphism. [Gy. Hajos]

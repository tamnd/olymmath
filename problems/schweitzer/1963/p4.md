Call a polynomial positive reducible if it can be written as a product of two nonconstant polynomials with positive real coefficients. Let $ f(x)$ be a polynomial with $ f(0)\not=0$ such that $ f(x^n)$ is positive reducible for some natural number $ n$ . Prove that $ f(x)$ itself is positive reducible. [L. Redei]

Let $ H$ be a set of real numbers that does not consist of $ 0$ alone and is closed under addition. Further, let $ f(x)$ be a
real-valued function defined on $ H$ and satisfying the following conditions: $$ f(x)\leq f(y)\ \mathrm{if} x \leq y$$ and $$ f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y) \;(x,y \in H)\ .$$ Prove that $ f(x)=cx$ on $ H$ , where $ c$ is a nonnegative number. [M. Hosszu, R. Borges]

Let $ \varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,...,\varepsilon_{2n}$ be independent random variables such that $ P(\varepsilon_i=1)=P(\varepsilon_i=-1)=\frac 12$ for all $ i$ , and define $ S_k=\sum_{i=1}^k \varepsilon_i, 1\leq k \leq 2n$ . Let $ N_{2n}$ denote the number of integers $ k\in [2,2n]$ such that either $ S_k>0$ , or $ S_k=0$ and $ S_{k-1}>0$ . Compute the variance of $ N_{2n}$ .

Let $ p$ be a prime and let $$ l_k(x,y)=a_kx+b_ky \;(k=1,2,...,p^2)\ .$$ be homogeneous linear polynomials with integral coefficients. Suppose that for every pair $ (\xi,\eta)$ of integers, not both divisible by $ p$ , the values $ l_k(\xi,\eta), 1\leq k\leq p^2 $ , represent every residue class $ \textrm{mod} p$ exactly $ p$ times. Prove that the set of pairs $ \{(a_k,b_k): 1\leq k \leq p^2 \}$ is identical $ \textrm{mod} p$ with the set $ \{(m,n): 0\leq m,n \leq p-1 \}.$

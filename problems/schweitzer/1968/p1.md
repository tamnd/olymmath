Consider the endomorphism ring of an Abelian torsion-free (resp. torsion) group $ G$ . Prove that this ring is Neumann-regular if and only if $ G$ is a discrete direct sum of groups isomorphic to the additive group of the rationals (resp. ,a discrete direct sum of cyclic groups of prime order). (A ring $ R$ is called Neumann-regular if for every $ \alpha \in R$ there exists a $ \beta \in R$ such that $ \alpha \beta \alpha=\alpha$ .)

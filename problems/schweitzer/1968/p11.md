Let $ A_1,...,A_n$ be arbitrary events in a probability field. Denote by $ C_k$ the event that at least $ k$ of $ A_1,...,A_n$ occur. Prove that $$ \prod_{k=1}^n P(C_k) \leq \prod_{k=1}^n P(A_k).$$

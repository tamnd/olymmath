Let $ K$ be a compact topological group, and let $ F$ be a set of continuous functions defined on $ K$ that has cardinality greater that continuum. Prove that there exist $ x_0 \in K$ and $ f \not=g \in F$ such that
$$ f(x_0)=g(x_0)=\max_{x\in K}f(x)=\max_{x \in K}g(x).$$

Let $ \Psi=\langle A;...\rangle$ be an arbitrary, countable algebraic structure (that is, $ \Psi$ can have an arbitrary number of finitary operations and relations). Prove that $ \Psi$ has as many as continuum automorphisms if and only if for any finite subset $ A'$ of $ A$ there is an automorphism $ \pi_{A'}$ of $ \Psi$ different from the identity automorphism and such that $$ (x) \pi_{A'}=x$$ for every $ x \in A'$ .

For every natural number $ r$ , the set of $ r$ -tuples of natural numbers is partitioned into finitely many classes. Show that if $ f(r)$ is a function such that $ f(r)\geq 1$ and $ \lim _{r\rightarrow \infty} f(r)=+\infty$ , then there exists an infinite set of natural numbers that, for all $ r$ , contains $ r$ -triples from at most $ f(r)$ classes. Show that if $ f(r) \not \rightarrow +\infty$ , then there is a family of partitions such that no such infinite set exists.

*P. Erdos, A. Hajnal*

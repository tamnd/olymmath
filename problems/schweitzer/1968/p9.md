Let $ f(x)$ be a real function such that
$$ \lim_{x \rightarrow +\infty} \frac{f(x)}{e^x}=1$$
and $ |f''(x)|\leq c|f'(x)|$ for all sufficiently large $ x$ . Prove that $$ \lim_{x \rightarrow +\infty} \frac{f'(x)}{e^x}=1.$$

*P. Erdos*

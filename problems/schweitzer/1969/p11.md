Let $ A_1,A_2,...$ be a sequence of infinite sets such that $ |A_i \cap A_j| \leq 2$ for $ i \not=j$ . Show that the sequence of indices can be divided into two disjoint sequences $ i_1<i_2<...$ and $ j_1<j_2<...$ in such a way that, for some sets $ E$ and $ F$ , $ |A_{i_n} \cap E|=1$ and $ |A_{j_n} \cap F|=1$ for $ n=1,2,... .$

*P. Erdos*

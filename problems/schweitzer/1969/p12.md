Let $ A$ and $ B$ be nonsingular matrices of order $ p$ , and let $ \xi$ and $ \eta$ be independent random vectors of dimension $ p$ . Show that if $ \xi,\eta$ and $ \xi A+ \eta B$ have the same distribution, if their first and second moments exist, and if their covariance matrix is the identity matrix, then these random vectors are normally distributed.

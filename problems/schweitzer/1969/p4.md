Show that the following inequality hold for all $ k \geq 1$ , real numbers $ a_1,a_2,...,a_k$ , and positive numbers $ x_1,x_2,...,x_k.$
$$ \ln \frac {\sum\limits_{i = 1}^kx_i}{\sum\limits_{i = 1}^kx_i^{1 - a_i}} \leq \frac {\sum\limits_{i = 1}^ka_ix_i \ln x_i}{\sum\limits_{i = 1}^kx_i} .
$$
*L. Losonczi*

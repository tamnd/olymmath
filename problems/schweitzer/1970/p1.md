We have $ 2n+1$ elements in the commutative ring $ R$ : $$ \alpha,\alpha_1,...,\alpha_n,\varrho_1,...,\varrho_n .$$ Let us define the elements $$ \sigma_k=k\alpha + \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i\varrho_i^k .$$ Prove that the ideal $ (\sigma_0,\sigma_1,...,\sigma_k,...)$ can be finitely generated.

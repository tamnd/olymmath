Let $ \vartheta_1,...,\vartheta_n$ be independent, uniformly distributed, random variables in the unit interval $ [0,1]$ . Define $$ h(x)= \frac1n \# \{k: \vartheta_k<x\ \}.$$ Prove that the probability that there is an $ x_0 \in (0,1)$ such that $ h(x_0)=x_0$ , is equal to $ 1- \frac1n.$

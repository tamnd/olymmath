Let $ \pi_n(x)$ be a polynomial of degree not exceeding $ n$ with real coefficients such that $$ |\pi_n(x)| \leq \sqrt{1-x^2}
\;\textrm{for}\ \;-1\leq x \leq 1 \ .$$ Then $$ |\pi'_n(x)| \leq 2(n-1).$$

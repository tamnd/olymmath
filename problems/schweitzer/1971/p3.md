Let $ 0<a_k<1$ for $ k=1,2,... .$ Give a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence, for every $ 0<x<1$ , of a permutation $ \pi_x$ of the positive integers such that $$ x= \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_{\pi_x}(k)}{2^k}.$$

*P. Erdos*

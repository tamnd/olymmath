We throw $ N$ balls into $ n$ urns, one by one, independently and uniformly. Let $ X_i=X_i(N,n)$ be the total number of balls in
the $ i$ th urn. Consider the random variable $$ y(N,n)=\min_{1 \leq i \leq n}|X_i-\frac Nn|.$$ Verify the following three statements:

(a) If $ n \rightarrow \infty$ and $ N/n^3 \rightarrow \infty$ , then $$ P \left(\frac{y(N,n)}{\frac 1n \sqrt{\frac Nn}}<x \right)
\rightarrow 1-e^{-x\sqrt{2/ \pi}} \;\textrm{for all}\ x>0 \ .$$

(b) If $ n\rightarrow \infty$ and $ N/n^3 \leq K$ ( $ K$ constant), then for any $ \varepsilon > 0$ there is an $ A > 0$ such that $$ P(y(N,n) < A) > 1-\varepsilon .$$

(c) If $ n \rightarrow \infty$ and $ N/n^3 \rightarrow 0$ then $$ P(y(N,n) < 1) \rightarrow 1.$$

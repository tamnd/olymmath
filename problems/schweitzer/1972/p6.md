Let $ P(z)$ be a polynomial of degree $ n$ with complex coefficients, $$ P(0)=1, \;\textrm{and}\ \;|P(z)|\leq M\ \;\textrm{for}\ \;|z| \leq 1\ .$$ Prove that every root of $ P(z)$ in the closed unit disc has multiplicity at most $ c\sqrt{n}$ , where $ c=c(M) >0$ is a constant depending only on $ M$ .

*G. Halasz*

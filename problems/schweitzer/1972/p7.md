Let $ f(x,y,z)$ be a nonnegative harmonic function in the unit ball of $ \mathbb{R}^3$ for which the inequality $ f(x_0,0,0) \leq \varepsilon^2$ holds for some $ 0\leq x_0 \leq 1$ and $ 0<\varepsilon<(1-x_0)^2$ . Prove that $ f(x,y,z) \leq \varepsilon$ in the ball with center at the origin an radius $ (1-3\varepsilon^{1/4}).$

*P. Turan*

Let $ f(n)$ be that largest integer $ k$ such that $ n^k$ divides $ n!$ , and let $ F(n)= \max_{2 \leq m \leq n} f(m)$ . Show that $$ \lim_{n\rightarrow \infty} \frac{F(n) \log n}{n \log \log n}=1.$$

*P. Erdos*

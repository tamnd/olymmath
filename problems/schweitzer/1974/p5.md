Let $ \{f_n \}_{n=0}^{\infty}$ be a uniformly bounded sequence of real-valued measurable functions defined on $ [0,1]$ satisfying $$ \int_0^1 f_n^2=1.$$ Further, let $ \{ c_n \}$ be a sequence of real numbers with $$ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n^2= +\infty.$$ Prove that some re-arrangement of the series $ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_nf_n$ is divergent on a set of positive measure.

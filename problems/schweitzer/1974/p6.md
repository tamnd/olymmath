Let $ f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n/(x+n^2), \;(x \geq 0)\ ,$ where $ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |a_n|n^{- \alpha} < \infty$ for some $ \alpha > 2$ . Let us assume that for some $ \beta > 1/{\alpha}$ , we have $ f(x)=O(e^{-x^{\beta}})$ as $ x \rightarrow \infty$ . Prove that $ a_n$ is identically $ 0$ .

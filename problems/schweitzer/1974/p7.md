Given a positive integer $ m$ and $ 0 < \delta <\pi$ , construct a trigonometric polynomial $ f(x)=a_0+ \sum_{n=1}^m (a_n \cos nx+b_n \sin nx)$ of degree $ m$ such that $ f(0)=1, \int_{ \delta \leq |x| \leq \pi} |f(x)|dx \leq c/m,$ and $ \max_{-\pi \leq x \leq \pi}|f'(x)| \leq c/{\delta}$ , for some universal constant $ c$ .

Let $ a<a'<b<b'$ be real numbers and let the real function $ f$ be continuous on the interval $ [a,b']$ and differentiable in its interior. Prove that there exist $ c \in (a,b), c'\in (a',b')$ such that $$ f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a),$$ $$ f(b')-f(a')=f'(c')(b'-a'),$$ and $ c<c'$ .

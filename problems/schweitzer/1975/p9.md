Let $ l_0,c,\alpha,g$ be positive constants, and let $ x(t)$ be the solution of the differential equation $$ ([l_0+ct^{\alpha}] ^2x')'+g[l_0+ct^{\alpha}] \sin x=0, t \geq 0,\ \;-\frac{\pi}{2} <x< \frac{\pi}{2},$$ satisfying the initial conditions $ x(t_0)=x_0, x'(t_0)=0$ . (This is the equation of the mathematical pendulum whose length changes according to the law $ l=l_0+ct^{\alpha}$ .) Prove that $ x(t)$ is defined on the interval $ [t_0,\infty)$ ; furthermore, if $ \alpha >2$ then for every $ x_0 \not= 0$ there exists a $ t_0$ such that $$ \liminf_{t \rightarrow \infty} |x(t)| >0.$$

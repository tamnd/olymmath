Let $ H$ denote the set of those natural numbers for which $ \tau(n)$ divides $ n$ , where $ \tau(n)$ is the number of divisors of $ n$ . Show that

a) $ n! \in H$ for all sufficiently large $ n$ ,

b) $ H$ has density $ 0$ .

*P. Erdos*

Let $ 0 \leq c \leq 1$ , and let $ \eta$ denote the order type of the set of rational numbers. Assume that with every rational number $ r$ we associate a Lebesgue-measurable subset $ H_r$ of measure $ c$ of the interval $ [0,1]$ . Prove the existence of a Lebesgue-measurable set $ H \subset [0,1]$ of measure $ c$ such that for every $ x \in H$ the set $$ \{r : x \in H_r\ \}$$ contains a subset of type $ \eta$ .

*M. Laczkovich*

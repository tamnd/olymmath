Let $ D$ be a convex subset of the $ n$ -dimensional space, and suppose that $ D'$ is obtained from $ D$ by applying a positive central dilatation and then a translation. Suppose also that the sum of the volumes of $ D$ and $ D'$ is $ 1$ , and $ D \cap D'\not= \emptyset .$ Determine the supremum of the volume of the convex hull of $ D \cup D'$ taken for all such pairs of sets $ D,D'$ .

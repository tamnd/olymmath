Let $ f$ be a real function defined on the positive half-axis for which $ f(xy)=xf(y)+yf(x)$ and $ f(x+1) \leq f(x)$ hold for every positive $ x$ and $ y$ . Show that if $ f(1/2)=1/2$ , then $$ f(x)+f(1-x) \geq -x \log_2 x -(1-x) \log_2 (1-x)$$ for every $ x\in (0,1)$ .

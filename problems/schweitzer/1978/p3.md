Let $ 1<a_1<a_2< \ldots <a_n<x$ be positive integers such that $ \sum_{i=1}^n 1/a_i \leq 1$ . Let $ y$ denote the number of positive integers smaller that $ x$ not divisible by any of the $ a_i$ . Prove that $$ y > \frac{cx}{\log x}$$ with a suitable positive constant $ c$ (independent of $ x$ and the numbers $ a_i$ ).

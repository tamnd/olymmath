Let $ \mathbb{Q}$ and $ \mathbb{R}$ be the set of rational numbers and the set of real numbers, respectively, and let $ f : \mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function with the following property. For every $ h \in \mathbb{Q} , x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ , $$ f(x+h)-f(x) \rightarrow 0$$ as $ x \in \mathbb{Q}$ tends to $ x_0$ . Does it follow that $ f$ is bounded on some interval?

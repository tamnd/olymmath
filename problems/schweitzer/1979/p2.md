Let $ \Gamma$ be a variety of monoids such that not all monoids of $ \Gamma$ are groups. Prove that if $ A \in \Gamma$ and $ B$ is a submonoid of $ A$ , there exist monoids $ S \in \Gamma$ and $ C$ and epimorphisms $ \varphi : S \rightarrow A, \;\varphi_1 : S \rightarrow C$ such that $ ((e)\varphi_1^{-1})\varphi=B$ ( $ e$ is the identity element of $ C$ ).

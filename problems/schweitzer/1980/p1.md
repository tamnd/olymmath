For a real number $ x$ , let $ \|x \|$ denote the distance between $ x$ and the closest integer. Let $ 0 \leq x_n <1 (n=1,2,\ldots)\ ,$ and let $ \varepsilon >0$ . Show that there exist infinitely many pairs $ (n,m)$ of indices such that $ n \not=
m$ and $$ \|x_n-x_m \|< \min \left( \varepsilon , \frac{1}{2|n-m|} \right).$$

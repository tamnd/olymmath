Let $ f(x)$ be a nonnegative, integrable function on $ (0,2\pi)$ whose Fourier series is $ f(x)=a_0+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k \cos (n_k x)$ , where none of the positive integers $ n_k$ divides another. Prove that $ |a_k| \leq a_0$ .

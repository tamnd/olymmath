Let $ P$ be a probability distribution defined on the Borel sets of the real line. Suppose that $ P$ is symmetric with respect to the origin, absolutely continuous with respect to the Lebesgue measure, and its density function $ p$ is zero outside the interval $ [-1,1]$ and inside this interval it is between the positive numbers $ c$ and $ d$ ( $ c < d$ ). Prove that there is no distribution whose convolution square equals $ P$ .

*T. F. Mori, G. J. Szekely*

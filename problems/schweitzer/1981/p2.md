Consider the lattice $ L$ of the contradictions of a simple graph $ G$ (as sets of vertex pairs) with respect to inclusion. Let $ n \geq 1$ be an arbitrary integer. Show that the identity $$ x \bigwedge \left( \bigvee_{i=0}^n y_i \right) = \bigvee_{j=0}^n
\left( x \bigwedge \left( \bigvee_{0 \leq i \leq n, i\not=j\ } y_i \right)\right)$$ holds if and only if $ G$ has no cycle of size at least $ n+2$ .

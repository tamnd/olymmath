Let $ K$ be a convex cone in the $ n$ -dimensional real vector space $ \mathbb{R}^n$ , and consider the sets $ A=K \cup (-K)$ and $ B=(\mathbb{R}^n \setminus A) \cup \{ 0 \}$ ( $ 0$ is the origin). Show that one can find two subspaces in $ \mathbb{R}^n$ such that together they span $ \mathbb{R}^n$ , and one of them lies in $ A$ and the other lies in $ B$ .

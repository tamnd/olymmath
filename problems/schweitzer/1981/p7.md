Let $ U$ be a real normed space such that, for an finite-dimensional, real normed space $ X,U$ contains a subspace isometrically isomorphic to $ X$ . Prove that every (not necessarily closed) subspace $ V$ of $ U$ of finite codimension has the same property. (We call $ V$ of finite codimension if there exists a finite-dimensional subspace $ N$ of $ U$ such that $ V+N=U$ .)

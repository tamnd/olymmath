Let $ G(V,E)$ be a connected graph, and let $ d_G(x,y)$ denote the length of the shortest path joining $ x$ and $ y$ in $ G$ . Let $ r_G(x)= \max \{ d_G(x,y) : y \in V \ \}$ for $ x \in V$ , and let $ r(G)= \min \{ r_G(x) : x \in V\ \}$ . Show that if $ r(G) \geq 2$ , then $ G$ contains a path of length $ 2r(G)-2$ as an induced subgraph.

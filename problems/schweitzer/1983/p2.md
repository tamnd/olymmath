Let $ I$ be an ideal of the ring $ R$ and $ f$ a nonidentity permutation of the set $ \{ 1,2,\ldots, k \}$ for some $ k$ . Suppose that for every $ 0 \not= a \in R, aI \not= 0$ and $ Ia \not=0$ hold; furthermore, for any elements $ x_1,x_2,\ldots ,x_k \in I$ , $$ x_1x_2\ldots x_k=x_{1f}x_{2f}\ldots x_{kf}$$ holds. Prove that $ R$ is commutative.

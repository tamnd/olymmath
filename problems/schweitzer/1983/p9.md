Prove that if $ E \subset \mathbb{R}$ is a bounded set of positive Lebesgue measure, then for every $ u < 1/2$ , a point $ x=x(u)$ can be found so that $$ |(x-h,x+h) \cap E| \geq uh$$ and $$ |(x-h,x+h) \cap (\mathbb{R} \setminus E)| \geq uh$$ for all sufficiently small positive values of $ h$ .

Let $k$ be an arbitrary cardinality. Show that there exists a tournament $T_k = (V_n , E_n)$ such that for any coloring $f: E_n \to k$ of the edge set $E_n$ , there are three different vertices $x_0 , x_1 , x_2 \in V_n$ such that $x_0 x_1 , x_1 x_2 , x_2 x_0 \in E_n$ and $\left | \{ f(x_0 x_1), f(x_1 x_2), f(x_2 x_0)\} \right |\leq 2$ (A *tounament* is a directed graph such that for any vertices $x, y \in V_n, x \neq y$ exactly one of the relations $xy \in E_n$ holds.)

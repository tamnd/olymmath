Prove that the series $\sum_p c_p f(px)$ , where the summation is over all primes, unconditionally converges in $L^2[0,1]$ for every $1$ -periodic function $f$ whose restriction to $[0,1]$ is in $L^2[0,1]$ if and only if $\sum_p |c_p|<\infty$ . (*Unconditional convergence* means convergence for all rearrangements.) [G. Halasz]

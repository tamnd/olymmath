Let $a_0=0$ , $a_1, \ldots, a_k$ and $b_1, \ldots, b_k$ be arbitrary real numbers.
(i) Show that for all sufficiently large $n$ there exist polynomials $p_n$ of degree at most $n$ for which $$ p_n^{(i)} (-1)=a_i,\,\,\,\, p_n^{(i)} (1)=b_i,\,\,\,\, i=0, 1, \ldots, k $$ and $$ \max_{|x|\leq 1} |p_n (x)|\leq \frac{c}{n^2}\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, (*) $$ where the constant $c$ depends only on the numbers $a_i, b_i$ .

(ii) Prove that, in general, (*) cannot be replaced by the relation $$ \lim_{n\to\infty} n^2\cdot \max_{|x|\leq 1} |p_n (x)| = 0 $$ [J. Szabados]

Let $\{a_n\}$ be a bounded real sequence.
(a) Prove that if X is a positive-measure subset of $\mathbb R$ , then for almost all $x\in X$ , there exist a subsequence $\{y_n\}$ of X such that $$ \sum_{n=1}^\infty (n(y_n-x)-a_n)=1 $$ (b) construct an unbounded sequence $\{a_n\}$ for which the above equation is also true.

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space. The bounded linear operator $A$ is called *positive* if $\langle Ax, x\rangle \geq 0$ for all $x\in H$ . Let $\sqrt A$ be the positive square root of $A$ , i.e. the uniquely determined positive operator satisfying $(\sqrt{A})^2=A$ . On the set of positive operators we introduce the $$ A\circ B=\sqrt A B\sqrt B $$ operation. Prove that for a given pair $A, B$ of positive operators the identity $$ (A\circ B)\circ C=A\circ (B\circ C) $$ holds for all positive operator $C$ if and only if $AB=BA$ .

Let $K\subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be compact. Prove that the following two statements are equivalent to each other.
(a) For each point $x$ of $K$ we can assign an uncountable set $F_x\subseteq \mathbb{R}$ such that $$ \mathrm{dist}(F_x, F_y)\ge |x-y| $$ holds for all $x,y\in K$ ;
(b) $K$ is of measure zero.

Let $ Z_1, Z_2\dots, Z_n$ be $ d$ -dimensional independent random (column) vectors with standard normal distribution, $ n - 1 > d$ . Furthermore let
$$ \overline Z = \frac {1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^n Z_i, S_n = \frac {1}{n - 1}\sum_{i = 1}^n(Z_i - \overline Z)(Z_i - \overline Z)^\top$$
be the sample mean and corrected empirical covariance matrix. Consider the standardized samples $ Y_i = S_n^{ - 1/2}(Z_i - \overline Z)$ , $ i = 1,2,\dots,n$ . Show that
$$ \frac {E|Y_1 - Y_2|}{E|Z_1 - Z_2|} > 1,$$
and that the ratio does not depend on $ d$ , only on $ n$ .

Consider the space $ \{0,1 \} ^{N} $ with the product topology (where $\{0,1 \}$ is a discrete space). Let $ T: \{0,1 \} ^ {\mathbb {N}} \rightarrow \{0,1 \} ^ {\mathbb {N}} $ be the left-shift, ie $ (Tx) (n) = x (n+1) $ for every $ n \in \mathbb {N} $ .
Can a finite number of Borel sets be given: $ B_ {1}, \ldots, B_ {m} \subset \{0,1 \} ^ {N} $ such that $$ \left \{T ^ {i} \left (B_ {j} \right) \mid i \in \mathbb {N}, 1 \leq j \leq m \right \} $$ the $ \sigma $ -algebra generated by the set system coincides with the Borel set system?

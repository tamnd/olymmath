Is there any sequence $(a_n)_{n=1}^{\infty}$ of non-negative numbers, for which $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n^2<\infty$ , but $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{a_{kn}}{k} \right)^2=\infty$ ?

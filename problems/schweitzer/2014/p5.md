Let $ \alpha $ be a non-real algebraic integer of degree two, and let $ \mathbb{P} $ be the set of irreducible elements of the ring $ \mathbb{Z}[ \alpha] $. Prove that
$$ \sum_{p\in \mathbb{P}}^{{}}\frac{1}{|p|^{2}}=\infty $$

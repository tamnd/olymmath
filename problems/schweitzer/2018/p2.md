A family $\mathcal{F}$ of sets is called *really neat* if for any $A,B\in \mathcal{F}$ , there is a set $C\in \mathcal{F}$ such that $A\cup B = A\cup C=B\cup C$ . Let $$ f(n)=\min \left\{ \max_{A\in \mathcal{F}} |A| \colon \mathcal{F} \text{ is really neat and } |\cup \mathcal{F}| =n\right\} . $$ Prove that the sequence $f(n)/n$ converges and find its limit.

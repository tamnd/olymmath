Let $I$ be a nonempty open subinterval of the set of positive real numbers. For which even $n \in \mathbb{N}$ are there injective function $f: I \to \mathbb{R}$ and positive function $p: I \to \mathbb{R}$ , such that for all $x_1 , \ldots , x_n \in I$ ,
$$ f \left( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{x_1+\cdots+x_n}{n}+\sqrt[n]{x_1 \cdots x_n} \right) \right)=\frac{p(x_1)f(x_1)+\cdots+p(x_n)f(x_n)}{p(x_1)+\cdots+p(x_n)} $$
holds?

The systems of equations
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l}
2x_1 - x_2 = 1
-x_1 + 2x_2 - x_3 = 1
-x_2 + 2x_3 - x_4 = 1
-x_3 + 3x_4 - x_5 =1
\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots
-x_{n-2} + 2x_{n-1} - x_n = 1
-x_{n-1} + 2x_n = 1
\end{array} \right.
$$
has a solution in positive integers $x_i$ . Show that $n$ must be even.

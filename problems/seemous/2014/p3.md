Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb{C}) $ and $a\in \mathbb{C} $ such that $A-A^*=2aI_n $ , where $A^*=(\overline{A})^T $ and $I_n$ is identity matrix.
(i) Show that $|\det A|\ge |a|^n $ .

(ii) Show that if $|\det A|=|a|^n $ then $A=aI_n$ .

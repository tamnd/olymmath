Consider $A\in \mathcal{M}_{2020}(\mathbb{C})$ such that $$ (1)\begin{cases}
A+A^{\times} =I_{2020},
A\cdot A^{\times} =I_{2020},
\end{cases} $$ where $A^{\times}$ is the adjugate matrix of $A$ , i.e., the matrix whose elements are $a_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}d_{ji}$ , where $d_{ji}$ is the determinant obtained from $A$ , eliminating the line $j$ and the column $i$ .
Find the maximum number of matrices verifying $(1)$ such that any two of them are not similar.

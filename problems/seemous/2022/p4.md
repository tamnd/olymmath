Let $\mathcal{F}$ be the family of all nonempty finite subsets of $\mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}.$ Find all real numbers $a$ for which the series $$ \sum_{A \in \mathcal{F}} \frac{1}{\sum_{k \in A}a^k} $$ is convergent.

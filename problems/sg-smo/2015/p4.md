Let $f_0, f_1,...$ be the Fibonacci sequence: $f_0 = f_1 = 1, f_n = f_{n-1} + f_{n-2}$ if $n \geq 2$ .
Determine all possible positive integers $n$ so that there is a positive integer $a$ such that $f_n \leq a \leq f_{n+1}$ and that $a( \frac{1}{f_1}+\frac{1}{f_1f_2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{f_1f_2...f_n} )$ is an integer.

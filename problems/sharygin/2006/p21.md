On the sides $AB, BC, CA$ of triangle $ABC$ , points $C', A', B'$ are taken.
Prove that for the areas of the corresponding triangles, the inequality holds: $$ S_{ABC}S^2_{A'B'C'}\ge 4S_{AB'C'}S_{BC'A'}S_{CA'B'} $$ and equality is achieved if and only if the lines $AA', BB', CC'$ intersect at one point.

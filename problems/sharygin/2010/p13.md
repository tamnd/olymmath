Let us have a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ such that $AB = BC$. A point $K$ lies on the diagonal $BD$, and $\angle AKB + \angle BKC = \angle A + \angle C$. Prove that $AK \cdot CD = KC \cdot AD$.

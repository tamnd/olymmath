A circle $\omega_c$ centered at $I_c$ is tangent to $BA$ at point $C_1$ , and is also tangent to the lines $\ell$ and $AC$ . Prove that the orthocenter of triangle $A_1BC_1$ lies on the line $I_aI_c$ .

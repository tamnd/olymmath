Let $A_1$ , $B_1$ and $C_1$ be the midpoints of sides $BC$ , $CA$ and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ , respectively. Points $B_2$ and $C_2$ are the midpoints of segments $BA_1$ and $CA_1$ respectively. Point $B_3$ is symmetric to $C_1$ wrt $B$ , and $C_3$ is symmetric to $B_1$ wrt $C$ .
Prove that one of common points of circles $BB_2B_3$ and $CC_2C_3$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ .

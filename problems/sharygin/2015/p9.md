Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle. Construct points $A', B', C'$ on its sides $BC, CA, AB$ such that:
- $A'B' \parallel AB$ ,
- $C'C$ is the bisector of angle $A'C'B'$ ,
- $A'C' + B'C'= AB$ .

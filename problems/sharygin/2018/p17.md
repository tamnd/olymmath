The common internal tangent to $\alpha$ and $\beta$ meets the arc $A_1B_1$ not containing $C_1$ at point $C_2$ . Points $A_2$ , $B_2$ are defined similarly. Prove that the lines $A_1A_2, B_1B_2, C_1C_2$ concur.

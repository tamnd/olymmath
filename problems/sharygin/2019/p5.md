Let $A, B, C$ and $D$ be four points in general position, and $\omega$ be a circle passing through $B$ and $C$. A point $P$ moves along $\omega$. Let $Q$ be the common point of circles $ABP$ and $PCD$ distinct from $P$. Find the locus of points $Q$.

Circles $\Omega$ and $\omega_a$ are the circumcircle and $A$ -excircle of some triangle $ABC$ . Let $ I_b, I_c$ be the centers of two remaining excircles, and $A_b, A_c$ be the touching points of the extensions of $AB, AC$ with $\omega_a$ . Prove that the meeting point of the lines $A_bI_b$ and $A_cI_c$ , does not depend on the triangle $ABC$ .

Let $AA_1$ and $CC_1$ be bisectors of a triangle $ABC$ , and $B_0$ be the midpoint of the arc $AC$ on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ , not containing $B$ . The circumcircles of triangles $AA_1B_0$ and $CC_1B_0$ meet the lines $BC$ and $AB$ at points $P$ and $Q$ respectively. Prove that the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ lies on $PQ$ .

A triangle $ABC$ is given. A line $m_1$ meets $BC$ , $CA$ , $AB$ at points $A_1$ , $B_1$ , $C_1$ respectively, and a line $m_2$ meets $BC$ , $CA$ , $AB$ at points $A_2$ , $B_2$ , $C_2$ , so that $A_1$ and $A_2$ are symmetric about the midpoint of $BC$ , $B_1$ and $B_2$ are symmetric about the midpoint of $CA$ , $C_1$ and $C_2$ are symmetric about the midpoint of $AB$ . Prove that $m_1 \perp m_2$ if and only if $m_1$ and $m_2$ are two Simson lines of triangle $ABC$ (for some two points on the circumcircle of $ABC$ ).

*Proposed by L. Emelyanov*

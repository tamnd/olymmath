A triangle $ABC$ is given. Let $D$ be an arbitrary point on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ , lying outside the triangle. The lines $BD$ and $AC$ meet at $C'$ , and the lines $CD$ and $AB$ meet at $B'$ . A point $M_a$ is the midpoint of $BC$ , and $M$ is the second common point of circles $(BB'D)$ and $(CC'D)$ . Prove that the circumcenter of $DMM_a$ lies on a fixed line.

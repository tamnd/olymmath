Prove that among any $42$ numbers from the interval $[1,10^6]$, you can choose four numbers so that for any permutation $(a, b, c, d)$ of these numbers, the inequality
$$25 (ab + cd) (ad + bc) \ge 16 (ac + bd)^ 2$$
holds.

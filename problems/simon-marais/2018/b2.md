Let $S$ be the set of real numbers that can be expressed as $\sqrt{m} - \sqrt{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers.

Do there exist real numbers $a < b$ such that the open interval $(a, b)$ contains only finitely many elements of $S$?

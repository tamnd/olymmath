Let $a, b,$ and $c$ be real numbers, and let $P$ be the polynomial

$$P(x) = x^6 + ax^4 + bx^2 + c.$$

Suppose that there is a unique circle $\Gamma$ in the complex plane such that all of the roots of $P$ lie on $\Gamma$. Prove that $b^3 = a^3c$.

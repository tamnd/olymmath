Determine all continuous functions $f : \mathbb{R} \setminus \{1\} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ that satisfy

$$f(x) = (x + 1)f(x^2),$$

for all $x \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-1, 1\}$.

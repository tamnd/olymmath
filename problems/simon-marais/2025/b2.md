Let $W$ be the interior of a convex quadrilateral in $\mathbb{R}^2$ which has no two sides parallel. Prove that there exist two triangles in $\mathbb{R}^2$ whose interiors $S$ and $T$ satisfy

$$W = \{s + t \mid s \in S, t \in T\}.$$

Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $2011$ such that $P(1)=0$, $P(2)=1$, $P(4)=2$, $\ldots$, and $P(2^{2011})=2011$. Compute the coefficient of the $x^1$ term in $P(x)$.

Let $ABC$ be any triangle, and $D,E,F$ be points on $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{CA}$, $\overline{AB}$ such that $CD=2BD$, $AE=2CE$ and $BF=2AF$. $\overline{AD}$ and $\overline{BE}$ intersect at $X$, $\overline{BE}$ and $\overline{CF}$ intersect at $Y$, and $\overline{CF}$ and $\overline{AD}$ intersect at $Z$. Find $\frac{Area(\triangle ABC)}{Area(\triangle XYZ)}$.

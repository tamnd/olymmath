Let sequence $A$ be $\{\frac{7}{4},\frac{7}{6},\frac{7}{9},\ldots\}$ where the $j^{\text{th}}$ term is given by $a_j=\frac{7}{4}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{j-1}$. Let $B$ be a sequence where the $j^{\text{th}}$ term is defined by $b_j=a_j^2+a_j$. What is the sum of all the terms in $B$?

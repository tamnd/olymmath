A frog is jumping on the number line, starting at zero and jumping to seven. He can jump from $x$ to either $x+1$ or $x+2$. However, the frog is easily confused, and before arriving at the number seven, he will turn around and jump in the wrong direction, jumping from $x$ to $x-1$. This happens exactly once, and will happen in such a way that the frog will not land on a negative number. How many ways can the frog get to the number seven?

Call a nonnegative integer $k$ sparse when all pairs of 1’s in the binary representation of $k$ are separated by at least two zeroes. For example, $9=1001_2$ is sparse, but $10=1010_2$ is not sparse. How many sparse numbers are less than $2^{17}$?

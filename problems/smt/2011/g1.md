Triangle $ABC$ has side lengths $BC=3$, $AC=4$, $AB=5$. Let $P$ be a point inside or on triangle $ABC$ and let the lengths of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $BC, AC, AB$ be $D_a, D_b, D_c$ respectively. Compute the minimum of $D_a+D_b+D_c$.

Two parallel lines $l_1$ and $l_2$ lie on a plane, distance $d$ apart. On $l_1$ there are an infinite number of points $A_1,A_2,A_3,\cdots$, in that order, with $A_nA_{n+1}=2$ for all $n$. On $l_2$ there are an infinite number of points $B_1,B_2,B_3,\cdots$, in that order and in the same direction, satisfying $B_nB_{n+1}=1$ for all $n$. Given that $A_1B_1$ is perpendicular to both $l_1$ and $l_2$, express the sum $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\angle A_iB_iA_{i+1}$ in terms of $d$.

We have a unit cube $ABCDEFGH$ where $ABCD$ is the top side and $EFGH$ is the bottom side with $E$ below $A$, $F$ below $B$, and so on. Equilateral triangle $BDG$ cuts out a circle from the cube’s inscribed sphere. Find the area of the circle.

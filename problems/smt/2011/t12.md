Find the boundary of the projection of the sphere $x^2+y^2+(z-1)^2=1$ onto the plane $z=0$ with respect to the point $P=(0,-1,2)$. Express your answer in the form $f(x,y)=0$, where $f(x,y)$ is a function of $x$ and $y$.

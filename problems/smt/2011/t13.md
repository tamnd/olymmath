Compute the number of pairs of 2011-tuples $(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{2011})$ and $(y_1,y_2,\ldots,y_{2011})$ such that $x_k=x_{k-1}^2-y_{k-1}^2-2$ and $y_k=2x_{k-1}y_{k-1}$ for $1\le k\le 2010$, $x_1=x_{2011}^2-y_{2011}^2-2$, and $y_1=2x_{2011}y_{2011}$.

Find the smallest $\alpha>0$ such that there exists $m>0$ making the following equation hold for all positive integers $a,b\ge 2$:

$$
\left(\frac{1}{\gcd(a,b-1)}+\frac{1}{\gcd(a-1,b)}\right)(a+b)^\alpha\ge m.
$$

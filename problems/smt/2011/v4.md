Let $T_n$ denote the number of terms in $(x+y+z)^n$ when simplified, i.e. expanded and like terms collected, for non-negative integers $n\geq 0$. Find
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{2010}(-1)^kT_k=T_0-T_1+T_2-\cdots-T_{2009}+T_{2010}.
$$

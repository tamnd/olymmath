The function $f(x)$ is known to be of the form $\prod_{i=1}^{n}f_i(a_i x)$, where $a_i$ is a real number and $f_i(x)$ is either $\sin(x)$ or $\cos(x)$ for $i=1,\ldots,n$. Additionally, $f(x)$ is known to have zeros at every integer between $1$ and $2012$ (inclusive) except for one integer $b$. Find the sum of all possible values of $b$.

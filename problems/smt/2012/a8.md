For real numbers $(x,y,z)$ satisfying the following equations, find all possible values of $x+y+z$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2y+y^2z+z^2x&=-1\\
xy^2+yz^2+zx^2&=5\\
xyz&=-2
\end{aligned}
$$

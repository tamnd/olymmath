In preparation for the annual USA Cow Olympics, Bessie is undergoing a new training regime. However, she has procrastinated on training for too long, and now she only has exactly three weeks to train. Bessie has decided to train for $45$ hours. She spends a third of the time training during the second week as she did during the first week, and she spends a half of the time training during the third week as during the second week. How much time did she spend training during the second week?

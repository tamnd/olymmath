Given a number $n$ in base $10$, let $g(n)$ be the base-$3$ representation of $n$. Let $f(n)$ be equal to the base-$10$ number obtained by interpreting $g(n)$ in base $10$. Compute the smallest positive integer $k\geq 3$ that divides $f(k)$.

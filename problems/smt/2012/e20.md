A permutation of the first $n$ positive integers is quadratic if, for some positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that $a+b=n$, $a\ne 1$, and $b\ne 1$, the first $a$ integers of the permutation form an increasing sequence and the last $b$ integers of the permutation form a decreasing sequence, or if the first $a$ integers of the permutation form a decreasing sequence and the last $b$ integers of the permutation form an increasing sequence. How many permutations of the first $10$ positive integers are quadratic?

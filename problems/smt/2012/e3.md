The Tribonacci numbers $T_n$ are defined as follows: $T_0=0$, $T_1=1$, and $T_2=1$. For all $n\geq 3$, we have $T_n=T_{n-1}+T_{n-2}+T_{n-3}$. Compute the smallest Tribonacci number greater than $100$ which is prime.

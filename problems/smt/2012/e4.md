Steve works $40$ hours a week at his new job. He usually gets paid $8$ dollars an hour, but if he works for more than $8$ hours on a given day, he earns $12$ dollars an hour for every additional hour over $8$ hours. If $x$ is the maximum number of dollars that Steve can earn in one week by working exactly $40$ hours, and $y$ is the minimum number of dollars that Steve can earn in one week by working exactly $40$ hours, what is $x-y$?

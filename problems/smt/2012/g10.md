A large flat plate of glass is suspended $\sqrt{2/3}$ units above a large flat plate of wood. (The glass is infinitely thin and causes no funny refractive effects.) A point source of light is suspended $\sqrt{6}$ units above the glass plate. An object rests on the glass plate of the following description. Its base is an isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel DC$, $AB=AD=BC=1$, and $DC=2$. The point source of light is directly above the midpoint of $CD$. The object’s upper face is a triangle $EFG$ with $EF=2$, $EG=FG=\sqrt{3}$. $G$ and $AB$ lie on opposite sides of the rectangle $EFCD$. The other sides of the object have $EA=ED=1$, $FB=FC=1$, and $GD=GC=2$. Compute the area of the shadow that the object casts on the wood plate.

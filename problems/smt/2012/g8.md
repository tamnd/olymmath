Let $ABC$ be a triangle with side lengths $5$, $6$, and $7$. Choose a radius $r$ and three points outside the triangle $O_a$, $O_b$, and $O_c$, and draw three circles with radius $r$ centered at these three points. If circles $O_a$ and $O_b$ intersect at $C$, $O_b$ and $O_c$ intersect at $A$, $O_c$ and $O_a$ intersect at $B$, and all three circles intersect at a fourth point, find $r$.

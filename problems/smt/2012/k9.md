Find the ordered pair $(\alpha,\beta)$ with non-infinite $\beta\neq 0$ such that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt[n^2]{1!2!\cdots n!}}{n^\alpha}=\beta$ holds.

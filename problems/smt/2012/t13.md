How many positive integers $n$ are there such that for any natural numbers $a,b$, we have $n\mid(a^2b+1)$ implies $n\mid(a^2+b)$? (Note: The symbol $\mid$ means “divides”; if $x\mid y$ then $y$ is a multiple of $x$.)

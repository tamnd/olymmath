Find constants $\alpha$ and $c$ such that the following limit is finite and nonzero:

$$
c=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{e\left(1-\frac{1}{n}\right)^n-1}{n^\alpha}.
$$

Give your answer in the form $(\alpha,c)$.

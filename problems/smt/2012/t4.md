We say that the pair of positive integers $(x,y)$, where $x<y$, is a $k$-*tangent pair* if we have
$$\arctan\frac{1}{k}=\arctan\frac{1}{x}+\arctan\frac{1}{y}.$$
Compute the second largest integer that appears in a 2012-tangent pair.

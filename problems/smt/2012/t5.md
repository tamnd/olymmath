Regular hexagon $A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5A_6$ has side length 1. For $i=1,\ldots,6$, choose $B_i$ to be a point on the segment $A_iA_{i+1}$ uniformly at random, assuming the convention that $A_{j+6}=A_j$ for all integers $j$. What is the expected value of the area of hexagon $B_1B_2B_3B_4B_5B_6$?

A plane in 3-dimensional space passes through the point $(a_1,a_2,a_3)$, with $a_1$, $a_2$, and $a_3$ all positive. The plane also intersects all three coordinate axes with intercepts greater than zero (i.e. there exist positive numbers $b_1,b_2,b_3$ such that $(b_1,0,0)$, $(0,b_2,0)$, and $(0,0,b_3)$ all lie on this plane). Find, in terms of $a_1,a_2,a_3$, the minimum possible volume of the tetrahedron formed by the origin and these three intercepts.

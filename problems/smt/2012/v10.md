We say that two polynomials $F(x)$ and $G(x)$ are equivalent mod $5$ if and only if $F(x)-G(x)=5\cdot H(x)$ for some integer polynomial $H(x)$. We say that $F(x)$ has $n$ as a root mod $5$ if and only if $5\mid F(n)$. How many inequivalent integer polynomials mod $5$ of degree at most $3$ do not have any integer roots mod $5$?

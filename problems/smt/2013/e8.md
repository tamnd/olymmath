According to Moor’s Law, the number of shoes in Moor’s room doubles every year. In $2013$, Moor’s room starts out having exactly one pair of shoes. If shoes always come in unique, matching pairs, what is the earliest year when Moor has the ability to wear at least $500$ mismatched pairs of shoes? Note that left and right shoes are distinct, and Moor must always wear one of each.

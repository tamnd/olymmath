A board has 2, 4, and 6 written on it. A person repeatedly selects (not necessarily distinct) values for $x$, $y$, and $z$ from the board, and writes down $xyz+xy+yz+zx+x+y+z$ if and only if that number is not yet on the board and is also less than or equal to 2013. This person repeats this process until no more numbers can be written. How many numbers will be written at the end of this process?

Rational Man and Irrational Man both buy new cars, and they decide to drive around two racetracks from time $t=0$ to time $t=\infty$. Rational Man drives along the path parametrized by

$$x=\cos(t)$$

$$y=\sin(t)$$
and Irrational Man drives along the path parametrized by

$$
x=1+4\cos\frac{t}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

$$
y=2\sin\frac{t}{\sqrt{2}}.
$$

Find the largest real number $d$ such that at any time $t$, the distance between Rational Man and Irrational Man is not less than $d$.

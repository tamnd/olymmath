Charles is playing a variant of Sudoku. To each lattice point $(x,y)$ where $1\le x,y<100$, he assigns an integer between 1 and 100, inclusive. These integers satisfy the property that in any row where $y=k$, the 99 values are distinct and are never equal to $k$; similarly for any column where $x=k$. Now, Charles randomly selects one of his lattice points with probability proportional to the integer value he assigned to it. Compute the expected value of $x+y$ for the chosen point $(x,y)$.

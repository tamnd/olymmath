A positive integer $b\geq 2$ is *neat* if and only if there exist positive base-$b$ digits $x$ and $y$ (that is, $x$ and $y$ are integers, $0<x<b$ and $0<y<b$) such that the number $x.y$ base $b$ (that is, $x+\frac{y}{b}$) is an integer multiple of $x/y$. Find the number of *neat* integers less than or equal to 100.

Robin is playing notes on an 88-key piano. He starts by playing middle C, which is actually the 40th lowest note on the piano (i.e. there are 39 notes lower than middle C). After playing a note, Robin plays with probability $\frac{1}{2}$ the lowest note that is higher than the note he just played, and with probability $\frac{1}{2}$ the highest note that is lower than the note he just played. What is the probability that he plays the highest note on the piano before playing the lowest note?

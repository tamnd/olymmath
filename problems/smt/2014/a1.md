Alice and Bob are painting a house. If Alice and Bob do not take any breaks, they will finish painting the house in 20 hours. If, however, Bob stops painting once the house is half-finished, then the house takes 30 hours to finish. Given that Alice and Bob paint at a constant rate, compute how many hours it will take for Bob to paint the entire house if he does it by himself.

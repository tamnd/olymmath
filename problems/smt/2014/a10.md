Let $x_0=1,x_1=0$, and $x_i=-3x_{i-1}+x_{i-2}$ for $i\geq2$. Let $y_0=0,y_1=1$, and $y_i=-3y_{i-1}+y_{i-2}$ for $i\geq2$. Compute

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{2013}\frac{(x_i y_{2014}-y_i x_{2014})^2}{y_{2014}^2}.
$$

You may give your answer in terms of at most ten values of the $x_i$ and/or $y_i$ (but must otherwise simplify completely).

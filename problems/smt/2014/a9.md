Let $b_n$ be defined by the formula

$$
b_n=\sqrt[3]{-1+a_1\sqrt[3]{-1+a_2\sqrt[3]{-1+\cdots a_{n-1}\sqrt[3]{-1+a_n}}}}
$$

where $a_n=n^2+3n+3$. Find the smallest real number $L$ such that $b_n<L$ for all $n$.

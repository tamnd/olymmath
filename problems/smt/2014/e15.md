Given a regular $2014$-gon, we construct $2014$ isosceles triangles on the exterior of the polygon such that each isosceles triangle has an edge of the polygon as its base and has legs formed by the extensions of the two adjacent sides. Compute in degrees the largest angle of one such triangle.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB=12$, $BC=5$, $AC=13$. Let $D$ and $E$ be the feet of the internal and external angle bisectors from $B$, respectively. (The external angle bisector from $B$ bisects the angle between $BC$ and the extension of $AB$.) Let $\omega$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle BDE$; extend $AB$ so that it intersects $\omega$ again at $F$. Extend $FC$ to meet $\omega$ again at $X$, and extend $AX$ to meet $\omega$ again at $G$. Find $FG$.

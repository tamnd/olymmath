For a given $x>0$, let $a_n$ be the sequence defined by $a_1=x$ for $n=1$ and $a_n=x^{a_{n-1}}$ for $n\geq 2$. Find the largest $x$ for which the limit $\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n$ converges.

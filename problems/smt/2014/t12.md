Find the smallest $L$ such that

$$\left(1-\frac{1}{a}\right)^b\left(1-\frac{1}{2b}\right)^c\left(1-\frac{1}{3c}\right)^a\leq L$$

for all real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$ greater than $1$.

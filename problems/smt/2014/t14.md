Convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ has sidelengths $AB=7$, $BC=9$, $CD=15$. A circle with center $I$ lies inside the quadrilateral, and is tangent to all four of its sides. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of $AC$ and $BD$, respectively. It can be proven that $I$ always lies on segment $MN$. If $I$ is in fact the midpoint of $MN$, find the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$.

Marc has a bag containing $10$ balls, each with a different color. He draws out two balls uniformly at random and then paints the first ball he drew to match the color of the second ball. Then he places both balls back in the bag. He repeats until all the balls are the same color. Compute the expected number of times Marc has to perform this procedure before all the balls are the same color.

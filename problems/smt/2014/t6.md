A composition of a natural number $n$ is a way of writing it as a sum of natural numbers, such as $3=1+2$. Let $P(n)$ denote the sum over all compositions of $n$ of the number of terms in the composition. For example, the compositions of 3 are 3, $1+2$, $2+1$, and $1+1+1$; the first has one term, the second and third have two each, and the last has 3 terms, so $P(3)=1+2+2+3=8$. Compute $P(9)$.

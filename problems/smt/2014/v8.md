$a_0,a_1,\ldots$ is a sequence of positive integers where $a_n=n!$ for all $n\leq 3$. Moreover, for all $n\geq 4$, $a_n$ is the smallest positive integer such that

$$
\frac{a_n}{a_i a_{n-i}}
$$

is an integer for all integers $i$, $0\leq i\leq n$. Find $a_{2014}$.

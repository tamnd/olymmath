Let $a_1,\ldots,a_{2018}$ be the roots of the polynomial

$$
x^{2018}+x^{2017}+\cdots+x^2+x-1345=0.
$$

Compute

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{2018}\frac{1}{1-a_n}.
$$

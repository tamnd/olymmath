Let $(a,b,c,d,e)$ be an integer solution to the system of equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
a+d&=12\\
b+ad+e&=57\\
c+bd+ae&=134\\
cd+be&=156\\
ce&=72
\end{aligned}
$$

Find all possible values of $b+d$.

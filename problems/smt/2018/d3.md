A dice is labeled with the integers $1,2,\ldots,n$ such that it is $2$ times as likely to roll a $2$ as it is a $1$, $3$ times as likely to roll a $3$ as it is a $1$, and so on. Suppose the probability of rolling an odd integer with the dice is $\frac{17}{35}$. Compute $n$.

A sequence is defined as follows. Given a term $a_n$, we define the next term $a_{n+1}$ as

$$
a_{n+1}=
\begin{cases}
\frac{a_n}{2} & \text{if }a_n\text{ is even}\\
a_n-1 & \text{if }a_n\text{ is odd}
\end{cases}
$$

The sequence terminates when $a_n=1$. Let $P(x)$ be the number of terms in such a sequence with initial term $x$. For example, $P(7)=5$ because its corresponding sequence is $7,6,3,2,1$. Evaluate $P(2^{2018}-2018)$.

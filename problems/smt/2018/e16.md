A turtle and rabbit both stand at the corner of a cube with side length $18$ meters and race to get to the opposite corner of the cube. The turtle can swim through the interior of the cube at a rate of $6$ meters/minute. The rabbit can run at a rate of $9$ meters/minute, but must go along the faces of the cube. The rabbit arrives at the opposite corner first. How long must he wait for the turtle to arrive?

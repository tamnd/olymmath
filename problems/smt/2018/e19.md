A $3\times 3$ magic square is a grid of distinct numbers whose rows, columns, and diagonals all add to the same integer sum. Connie creates a magic square whose sum is $N$, but her keyboard is broken so that when she types a number, one of the digits $(0-9)$ always appears as a different digit (e.g. if the digit $8$ always appears as $5$, the number $18$ will appear as $15$). The altered square is shown below. Find $N$.

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
9 & 11 & 10\\
\hline
18 & 17 & 6\\
\hline
14 & 11 & 15\\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

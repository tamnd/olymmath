Lake Donald is invaded by $1000$ ducks with red or blue feathers and red or blue heads. If $538$ of the ducks have red feathers, $318$ ducks have blue heads and $250$ ducks have both blue heads and blue feathers. How many ducks have both red heads and red feathers?

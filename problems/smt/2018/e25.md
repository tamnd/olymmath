Let $f(x)=x^3-n_1x^2+(n_2-k^2)x-(n_3-k^4)$. Suppose that $n_1$, $n_2$, and $n_3$ form a geometric sequence with common ratio $k$ and that the roots of $f$ are nonzero and form an arithmetic sequence with common difference also $k$. Find $k$.

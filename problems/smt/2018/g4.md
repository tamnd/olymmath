Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{12}$ be the vertices of a regular dodecagon $D_1$ (12-gon). The four vertices $a_1,a_4,a_7,a_{10}$ form a square, as do the four vertices $a_2,a_5,a_8,a_{11}$ and $a_3,a_6,a_9,a_{12}$. Let $D_2$ be the polygon formed by the intersection of these three squares. If we let $[A]$ denotes the area of polygon $A$, compute $\frac{[D_2]}{[D_1]}$.

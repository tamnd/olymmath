Let $ABC$ be a right triangle with $\angle ACB=90^\circ$, $BC=16$, and $AC=12$. Let the angle bisectors of $\angle BAC$ and $\angle ABC$ intersect $BC$ and $AC$ at $D$ and $E$ respectively. Let $AD$ and $BE$ intersect at $I$, and let the circle centered at $I$ passing through $C$ intersect $AB$ at $P$ and $Q$ such that $AQ<AP$. Compute the area of quadrilateral $DPQE$.

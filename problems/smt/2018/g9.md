Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral with $3AB=2AD$ and $BC=CD$. The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $X$. Let $E$ be a point on $AD$ such that $DE=AB$ and $Y$ be the point of intersection of lines $AC$ and $BE$. If the area of triangle $ABY$ is $5$, then what is the area of quadrilateral $DEYX$?

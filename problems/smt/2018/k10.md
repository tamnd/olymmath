*Fact:* The value $\ln(2)$ is not the root of any polynomial with rational coefficients.

For any nonnegative integer $n$, let $p_n(x)$ be the unique polynomial with integer coefficients such that
$$p_n(\ln(2))=\int_1^2(\ln(x))^n\,dx.$$

Compute the value of the sum
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{p_n(0)}.$$

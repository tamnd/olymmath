Let $f(k)$ be a function defined by the following rules:

(a) $f(k)$ is multiplicative. In other words, if $\gcd(a,b)=1$, then $f(ab)=f(a)\cdot f(b)$,

(b) $f(p^k)=k$ for primes $p=2,3$ and all $k>0$,

(c) $f(p^k)=0$ for primes $p>3$ and all $k>0$, and
(d) $f(1)=1$.

For example, $f(12)=2$ and $f(160)=0$. Evaluate

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{f(k)}{k}.
$$

Consider all increasing arithmetic progressions of the form $\frac{1}{a},\frac{1}{b},\frac{1}{c}$ such that $a,b,c\in\mathbb{N}$ and $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$. Find the sum of all possible values of $\frac{1}{a}$.

Suppose that $a,b,c,d$ are positive integers satisfying

$$
\begin{aligned}
25ab+25ac+b^2&=14bc\\
4bc+4bd+9c^2&=31cd\\
9cd+9ca+25d^2&=95da\\
5da+5db+20a^2&=16ab
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute $\frac{a}{b}+\frac{b}{c}+\frac{c}{d}+\frac{d}{a}$.

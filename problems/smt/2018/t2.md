Cindy has a collection of identical rectangular prisms. She stacks them, end to end, to form $1$ longer rectangular prism. Suppose that joining $11$ of them will form a rectangular prism with $3$ times the surface area of an individual rectangular prism. How many will she need to join end to end to form a rectangular prism with $9$ times the surface area?

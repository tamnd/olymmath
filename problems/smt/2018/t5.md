A point $(x_0,y_0)$ with integer coordinates is a primitive point of a circle if for some pair of integers $(a,b)$, the line $ax+by=1$ intersects the circle at $(x_0,y_0)$. How many primitive points are there of the circle centered at $(2,-3)$ with radius $5$?

Consider any $5$ points placed on the surface of a cube of side length $2$ centered at the origin. Let $m_x$ be the minimum distance between the $x$ coordinates of any of the $5$ points, $m_y$ be the minimum distance between $y$ coordinates, and $m_z$ be the minimum distance between $z$ coordinates. What is the maximum value of $m_x+m_y+m_z$?

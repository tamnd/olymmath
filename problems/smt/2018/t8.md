Eddy has two blank cubes $A$ and $B$ and a marker. Eddy is allowed to draw a total of $36$ dots on cubes $A$ and $B$ to turn them into dice, where each side has an equal probability of appearing, and each side has at least one dot on it. Eddy then rolls dice $A$ twice and dice $B$ once and computes the product of the three numbers. Given that Eddy draws dots on the two dice to maximize his expected product, what is his expected product?

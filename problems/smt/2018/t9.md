Let $ABCD$ be a square. Point $E$ is chosen inside the square such that $AE=6$. Point $F$ is chosen outside the square such that $BE=BF=2\sqrt{5}$, $\angle ABF=\angle CBE$, and $AEBF$ is cyclic. Compute the area of $ABCD$.

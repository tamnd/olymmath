Let $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ be the set of nonnegative real numbers. Consider a continuous function $f:\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}\to\mathbb{R}$ which satisfies

$$
f(x^2)+f(y^2)=f\left(\frac{x^2y^2-2xy+1}{x^2+2xy+y^2}\right)
$$

for $x,y$ positive real numbers with $xy>1$. Given that $f(0)=2019$ and $f(1)=\frac{2019}{2}$, compute $f(3)$.

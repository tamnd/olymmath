Let $f(x)=36x^4-36x^3-x^2+9x-2$. Then let the four roots of $f(x)$ be $r_1$, $r_2$, $r_3$, and $r_4$. Find the value of

$$
(r_1+r_2+r_3)(r_1+r_2+r_4)(r_1+r_3+r_4)(r_2+r_3+r_4).
$$

Let $f(X)$ be a complex monic quadratic with real roots $\frac{1}{3},\frac{2}{3}$. (The polynomial $f(X)$ is of the form $X^2+bX+c$ where $b,c,X$ are complex numbers.) If $|z|=1$, what is the sum of all possible values of $f(z)$ such that $f(z)=\overline{f(z)}$?

The equation

$$
(x-1)(x-2)(x-4)(x-5)(x-7)(x-8)=(x-3)(x-6)(x-9)
$$

has distinct roots $r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_6$. Evaluate

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{6}(r_i-1)(r_i-2)(r_i-4).
$$

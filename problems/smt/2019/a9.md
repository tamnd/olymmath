Suppose we have a strictly increasing function $f:\mathbb{Z}^+\to\mathbb{Z}^+$ where $\mathbb{Z}^+$ denotes the set of positive integers. We also know that both

$$
f(f(1)),f(f(2)),f(f(3)),\ldots
$$
and

$$
f(f(1)+1), f(f(2)+1), f(f(3)+1), \ldots
$$

are arithmetic sequences. Given that $f(1)=1$ and $f(2)=3$, find the maximum value of

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{100} f(j).
$$

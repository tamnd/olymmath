Connie finds a whiteboard that has magnet letters spelling $\mathit{MISSISSIPPI}$ on it. She can rearrange the letters, in which identical letters are indistinguishable. If she uses all the letters and does not want to place any $I$s next to each other, how many distinct rearrangements are possible?

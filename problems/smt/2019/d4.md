In your drawer, you have two red socks and a blue sock. You randomly select socks, without replacement, from the drawer. However, every time you take a sock, another blue sock magically appears in the drawer. You stop taking socks when you have a pair of red socks. At this time, say you have $x$ socks total. What is the expected value of $x$?

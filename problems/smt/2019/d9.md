Edward has a $3\times 3$ tic-tac-toe board and wishes to color the squares using 3 colors. How many ways can he color the board such that there is at least one row whose squares have the same color and at least one column whose squares have the same color? A coloring does not have to contain all three colors and Edward cannot rotate or reflect his board.

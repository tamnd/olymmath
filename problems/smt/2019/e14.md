A cat chases a mouse down on the $xy$ plane. The cat starts at the origin and the mouse at $(1,0)$. The mouse runs straight towards the mouse hole at $(1,3)$. The cat runs towards the place at which it will catch the mouse. If the cat runs at $5$ units/sec and the mouse at $3$ units/sec, how far away from the hole was the mouse when it was caught?

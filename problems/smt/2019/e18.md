Peter has $18$ colored gumballs composed of $3$ red, $4$ blue, $5$ yellow, and $6$ green where same colored gumballs are indistinguishable. What is the probability that if he chooses four gumballs at random, the gumballs that he chooses consist of at least two colors and at most three colors?

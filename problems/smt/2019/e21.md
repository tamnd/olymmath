At the start of stage $0$, the Meta-Meme-Machine has a pool of $i=7$ images and a pool of $t=31$ textboxes. In each stage, it creates $i\times t$ memes by making all pairs of an image plus a textbox. The pool of images at the start of the next round consists of all previous $i$ images as well as the $i\times t$ memes. There are still $t$ textboxes at the start of the next round. What is the first stage $s$ starting with a pool of more than $7$ million images?

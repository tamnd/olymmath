A positive number greater than $1$ is *exponent-happy* if when written in the form $p_1^{e_1}p_2^{e_2}\ldots p_k^{e_k}$ for distinct primes $p_1,p_2,\ldots,p_k$, we have that $\gcd(e_1,e_2,\ldots,e_k)=1$. How many positive numbers between $2$ and $5000$ inclusive are *exponent-happy*?

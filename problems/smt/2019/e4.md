The blue train heads north at $60$ miles per hour from Blueville. The red train starts an hour later and heads south at $80$ miles per hour from Redtown. If Redtown is $200$ miles north of Blueville, what is the ratio of the distance the blue train travels to the distance the red train travels before the collision?

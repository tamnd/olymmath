Let $O$ be the center of the circumcircle of right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle ACB=90^\circ$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of minor arc $\widehat{AC}$ and let $N$ be a point on line $BC$ such that $MN\perp BC$. Let $P$ be the intersection of line $AN$ and the Circle $O$ and let $Q$ be the intersection of line $BP$ and $MN$. If $QN=2$ and $BN=8$, compute the radius of the Circle $O$.

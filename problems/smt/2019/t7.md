Let $f(x)=\sin^8(x)+\cos^8(x)+\frac{3}{8}\sin^4(2x)$. Let $f^{(n)}(x)$ be the $n$th derivative of $f$. What is the largest integer $a$ such that $2^a$ divides $f^{(2020)}(15^\circ)$?

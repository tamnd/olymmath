Let $\mathbb{R}^n$ be the set of vectors $(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n)$ where $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ are all real numbers. Let $\|(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\|$ denote $\sqrt{x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2}$. Let $S$ be the set in $\mathbb{R}^9$ given by
$$
S=\{(x,y,z):x,y,z\in\mathbb{R}^3,1=\|x\|=\|y-x\|=\|z-y\|\}
$$
If a point $(x,y,z)$ is uniformly at random from $S$, what is $E[\|z\|^2]$?

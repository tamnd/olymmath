Let $f(x)$ be the unique integer between $0$ and $x-1$, inclusive, that is equivalent modulo $x$ to $\left(\sum_{i=0}^{2}\binom{x-1}{i}((x-1-i)!+i!)\right)$. Let $S$ be the set of primes between $3$ and $30$, inclusive. Find $\sum_{x\in S}f(x)$.

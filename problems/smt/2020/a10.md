Let $f(a,b)$ be a third degree two-variable polynomial with integer coefficients such that $f(a,a)=0$ for all integers $a$ and the sum

$$
\sum_{\substack{a,b\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}\\a\neq b}}\frac{1}{2^{f(a,b)}}
$$

converges. Let $g(a,b)$ be the polynomial such that $f(a,b)=(a-b)g(a,b)$. If $g(1,1)=5$ and $g(2,2)=7$, find the maximum value of $g(20,20)$.

Two cars driving from city A to city B leave at the same time. The first car drives at some constant speed during the whole trip. The second car travels at a speed 12 km/hr slower than the first car until the halfway point between city A and city B. After the halfway point, the second car travels at a constant speed of 72 km/hr. Both cars end up reaching city B at the same time. Calculate the speed of the first car in km/hr, given that it was faster than 40 km/hr.

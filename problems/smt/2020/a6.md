Let $f:A\to B$ be a function from $A=\{0,1,\ldots,8\}$ to $B=\{0,1,\ldots,11\}$ such that the following properties hold:

$$
f(x+y\pmod 9)\equiv f(x)+f(y)\pmod{12}
$$

$$
f(xy\pmod 9)\equiv f(x)f(y)\pmod{12}
$$

for all $x,y\in A$. Compute the number of functions $f$ that satisfy these conditions.

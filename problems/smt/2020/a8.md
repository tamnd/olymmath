For how many integers $n$ with $3\leq n\leq2020$ does the inequality

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor}\binom{n}{4k+1}9^k
>
3\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor(n-3)/4\rfloor}\binom{n}{4k+3}9^k
$$

hold?

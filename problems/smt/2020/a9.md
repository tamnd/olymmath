A sequence of numbers is defined by $a_0=2$ and for $i>0$, $a_i$ is the smallest positive integer such that $\sum_{j=0}^{i}\frac{1}{a_j}<1$. Find the smallest integer $N$ such that $\sum_{i=N}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\log_2(a_i)}<\frac{1}{2^{2020}}$.

William has a bag of white, milk, and dark chocolate bars. Each minute he reaches into the bag, selects a chocolate bar at random, and eats it. Given that there are 17 milk chocolate bars, 12 dark chocolate bars, and 19 white chocolate bars, what is the probability that William runs out of milk chocolate bars first and dark chocolate bars second?

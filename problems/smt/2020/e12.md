A certain $10$-sided die has the number $1$ on one side, the number $2$ on two sides, the number $3$ on three sides, and the number $4$ on the the remaining $4$ sides. Nathan and David each roll this die once. If the die is equally likely to land on any of the $10$ sides, what is the probability that the number Nathan rolled is greater than the number David rolled?

Suppose $ABCD$ is a square with points $E,F,G,H$ inside square $ABCD$ such that $ABE$, $BCF$, $CDG$, and $DAH$ are all equilateral triangles. Let $E',F',G',H'$ be points outside square $ABCD$ such that $ABE'$, $BCF'$, $CDG'$, and $DAH'$ are also all equilateral triangles. What is the ratio of the area of quadrilateral $EFGH$ to the area of quadrilateral $E'F'G'H'$?

A cat chases a mouse on the integer lattice. The mouse starts at $(0,0)$ and wants to get to the mouse hole at $(5,5)$. The cat starts at $(-1,-3)$. The mouse can only travel along the grid at $1$ unit/sec, whereas the cat can travel on diagonals and at $2$ unit/sec. How long will have the cat been waiting at the hole for the mouse?

Farmer Joe will plant carrots to cover a rectangle in the first quadrant with a vertex at the origin and sides parallel to the $x$ and $y$ axes. However, he can not grow carrots on his neighbor’s land. If the border between his and his neighbor’s land is along the curve $y=-\ln(2x)$, what is the maximum area of carrotland Farmer Joe can create?

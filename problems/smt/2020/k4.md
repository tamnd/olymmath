The Chebyshev Polynomials are defined as

$$T_n(x)=\cos(n\cos^{-1}(x)),$$

for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$. Compute the following infinite series:

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{-1}^{1}T_{2n+1}(x)\,dx.$$

If the series diverges, your answer should be ”D.”

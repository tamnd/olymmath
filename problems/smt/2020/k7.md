Define $f_1(x)=x$ and for every integer $n\geq 2$, we define $f_n(x)=x^{f_{n-1}(x)}$. Compute

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_e^{2020}\frac{f_n'(x)}{f_n(x)f_{n-1}(x)\ln x}-\frac{f_{n-1}'(x)}{f_{n-1}(x)}\,dx.$$

Define $a_n=\underbrace{\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\cdots}}}}_{\text{n square roots}}$. For example $a_1=\sqrt{2}$ and $a_2=\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}$. Find the value of

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}4^n(2-a_n).
$$

Let $S=\{1,2,\ldots,100\}$. Compute the minimum possible integer $n$ such that, for any subset $T\subseteq S$ with size $n$, every integer $a$ in $S$ satisfies the relation $a\equiv bc\pmod{101}$, for some choice of integers $b,c$ in $T$.

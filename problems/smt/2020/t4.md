Let $C$ be the circle of radius $2$ centered at $(4,4)$ and let $L$ be the line $x=-2$. The set of points equidistant from $C$ and from $L$ can be written as $ax^2+by^2+cxy+dx+ey+f=0$ where $a,b,c,d,e,f$ are integers and have no factors in common. What is $|a+b+c+d+e+f|$?

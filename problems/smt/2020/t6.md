Let $A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_{2020}$ be a regular $2020$-gon with a circumcircle $C$ of diameter $1$. Now let $P$ be the midpoint of the small-arc $A_1-A_2$ on the circumcircle $C$. Then find:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{2020}|PA_i|^2
$$

A certain party of $2020$ people has the property that, for any $4$ people in the party, there is at least one person of those $4$ that is friends with the other three (assume friendship is mutual). Call a person in the party a *politician* if they are friends with the other $2019$ people in the party. If $n$ is the number of politicians in the party, compute the sum of the possible values of $n$.

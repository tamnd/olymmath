Find the sum of all possible values of $a$ such that there exists a non-zero complex number $z$ such that the four roots, labeled $r_1$ through $r_4$, of the polynomial
$$
x^4-6ax^3+(8a^2+5a)x^2-12a^2x+4a^2
$$
satisfy $|\Re(r_i)|=|r_i-z|$ for $1\leq i\leq 4$. Note, for a complex number $x$, $\Re(x)$ denotes the real component of $x$.

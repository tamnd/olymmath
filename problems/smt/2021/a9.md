Let $m,n\in\mathbb{R}$ and
$$
f(m,n)=m^4(8-m^4)+2m^2n^2(12-m^2n^2)+n^4(18-n^4)-100
$$
Find the smallest possible value for $a$ in which $f(m,n)\leq a$, regardless of the input of $f$.

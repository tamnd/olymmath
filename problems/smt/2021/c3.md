Consider sequences that consist of $0$’s and $1$’s. The probability that a random sequence of length $2021$ contains an equal number of occurrences of ‘$01$’ and ‘$10$’ is $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m,n$ are positive, relatively prime integers. Find $m+n$.

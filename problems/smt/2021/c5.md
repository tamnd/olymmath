Alice and Bob play a game in which they take turns rolling a fair die. The winner of a round is the first player to roll a $6$. Whoever loses rolls first in the next round. Alice rolls first in round $1$. The probability that Alice rolls first in round $4$ is $\frac{A}{B}$, where $A$ and $B$ are relatively prime, positive integers. Find $A+B$.

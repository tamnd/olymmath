A frog starts at $(0,0)$ and must return to his home at $(13,7)$. There is a river located along the line $y=x-6$. At each step, the frog can only move exactly one unit up or one unit to the right along the lattice points of the plane. If the frog cannot cross the river (but is allowed to move to points on the river), the number of paths the frog can take to his home is $N$. Compute $\frac{N}{120}$.

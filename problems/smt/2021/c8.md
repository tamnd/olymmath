Oliver and Xavier are playing a game on an $n$ by $n$ grid of squares. Initially, all cells of the grid are unoccupied. A *turn* is defined as Oliver placing an $O$ on any currently unoccupied square then Xavier placing an $X$ on any remaining unoccupied square. If, at the end of such a turn, there exists a row or column where there are at least $3$ more $O$’s than $X$’s in that given row or column, then Oliver wins. Otherwise, if they fill the board and there does not exist such a row or column, Xavier wins. Find the minimum value of $n$ such that Oliver is guaranteed to win if they both play optimally.

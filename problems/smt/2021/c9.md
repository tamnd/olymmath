Alice plays the violin and piano, and would like to create a practice schedule. She will only practice one instrument on a given day, she can have break days when she does not practice any instrument, and she wants to make sure she does not neglect any instrument for more than two days (e.g. if she does not practice piano for two days, she must practice piano the next day). Following these rules, how many ways can she schedule her practice for eight days?

Let $ABC$ be a triangle and $D$ be a point on side $BC$. Let $O$ be the midpoint of $AD$. The circle centered at $O$ passing through $A$ intersects $AB$ and $AC$ at $E$ and $F$ (both not $A$), respectively. If $O$ lies on $EF$ and $\angle ABC$ is five times $\angle ACB$, compute $\angle ABC$ in degrees.

January 1, 2021 is a Friday. Let $p$ be the probability that a randomly chosen day in the year 2021 is a Friday the 13th given that it is a Friday. Let $q$ be the probability that a randomly chosen day in the year 2021 is a Friday the 13th given that it is the 13th of a month. What is $\frac{p}{q}$?

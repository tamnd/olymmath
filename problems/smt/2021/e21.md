Three spheres are centered at the vertices of a triangle in the horizontal plane and are tangent to each other. The triangle formed by the uppermost points of the spheres has side lengths $10$, $26$, and $2\sqrt{145}$. What is the area of the triangle whose vertices are at the centers of the spheres?

Carla has $100$ stacks of pennies. The stacks have $1$ penny, $2$ pennies, $3$ pennies, up to $100$ pennies. Carla makes a move by adding one penny to each of any $99$ stacks. What is the least number of moves Carla can make such that all $100$ stacks have the same amount of pennies?

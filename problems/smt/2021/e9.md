How many ways are there to color every square of an eight-by-eight grid black or white such that for every pair of rows $r$ and $s$, we have that either $r_i=s_i$ for all $1\leq i\leq 8$, or $r_i\neq s_i$ for all $1\leq i\leq 8$?

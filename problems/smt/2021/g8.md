In triangle $\triangle ABC$, $AB=5$, $BC=7$, and $CA=8$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$, respectively, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. The area of triangle $MEF$ can be expressed as $\frac{a\sqrt{b}}{c}$ for positive integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ such that the greatest common divisor of $a$ and $c$ is $1$ and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Compute $a+b+c$.

Find the sum of all possible values of $abc$ where $a,b,c$ are positive integers that satisfy

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\gcd(b,c)+3,\\
b&=\gcd(a,c)+3,\\
c&=\gcd(a,b)+3.
\end{aligned}
$$

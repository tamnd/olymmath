Find the least positive integer $k$ such that there exists a set of $k$ distinct positive integers $\{n_1,n_2,\ldots,n_k\}$ that satisfy the equation

$$
\prod_{i=1}^{k}\left(1-\frac{1}{n_i}\right)=\frac{72}{2021}.
$$

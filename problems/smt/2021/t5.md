A potter makes a bowl, beginning with a sphere of clay and then cutting away the top and bottom of the sphere with two parallel cuts that are equidistant from the center. Finally, he hollows out the remaining shape, leaving a base at one end. Assume that the thickness of the bowl is negligible. If the potter wants the bowl to hold a volume that is $\frac{13}{27}$ of the volume of the sphere he started with, the distance from the center at which he makes his cuts should be what fraction of the radius?

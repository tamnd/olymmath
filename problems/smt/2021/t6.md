Let $AB$ be a line segment with length $2+\sqrt{2}$. A circle $\omega$ with radius $1$ is drawn such that it passes through the end point $B$ of the line segment and its center $O$ lies on the line segment $AB$. Let $C$ be a point on circle $\omega$ such that $AC=BC$. What is the size of angle $CAB$ in degrees?

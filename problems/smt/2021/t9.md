For positive integers $n$ and $k$ with $k\leq n$, let

$$f(n,k)=\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}j\binom{k-1}{j}\binom{n-k+1}{k-j}.$$
Compute the sum of the prime factors of

$$
f(4,4)+f(5,4)+f(6,4)+\cdots+f(2021,4).
$$

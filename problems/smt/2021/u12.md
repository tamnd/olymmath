Parabola $P$, $y=ax^2+c$ has $a>0$ and $c<0$. Circle $C$, which is centered at the origin and lies tangent to $P$ at $P$’s vertex, intersects $P$ at only the vertex. What is the maximum value of $a$, possibly in terms of $c$?

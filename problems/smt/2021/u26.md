Suppose points $A,B,C,D$ lie on a circle $\omega$ with radius 4 such that $ABCD$ is a quadrilateral with $AB=6, AC=8, AD=7$. Let $E$ and $F$ be points on $\omega$ such that $AE$ and $AF$ are respectively the angle bisectors of $\angle BAC$ and $\angle DAC$. Compute the area of quadrilateral $AECF$.

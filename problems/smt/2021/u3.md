There are $5$ people standing at $(0,0)$, $(3,0)$, $(0,3)$, $(-3,0)$, and $(-3,0)$ on a coordinate grid at a time $t=0$ seconds. Each second, every person on the grid moves exactly $1$ unit up, down, left, or right. The person at the origin is infected with covid-19, and if someone who is not infected is at the same lattice point as a person who is infected, at any point in time, they will be infected from that point in time onwards. (Note that this means that if two people run into each other at a non-lattice point, such as $(0,1.5)$, they will not infect each other.) What is the maximum possible number of infected people after $t=7$ seconds?

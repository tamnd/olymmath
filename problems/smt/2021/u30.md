Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n=4$. Compute the maximum possible value of $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\sqrt{a_n}}{2^n}$ (assume this always converges).

Lines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ have slopes $m_1$ and $m_2$ such that $0<m_2<m_1$. $\ell'_1$ and $\ell'_2$ are the reflections of $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ about the line $\ell_3$ defined by $y=x$. Let $A=\ell_1\cap\ell_2=(5,4)$, $B=\ell_1\cap\ell_3$, $C=\ell'_1\cap\ell'_2$ and $D=\ell_2\cap\ell_3$. If $\frac{4-5m_1}{-5-4m_1}=m_2$ and $\frac{(1+m_1^2)(1+m_2^2)}{(1-m_1)^2(1-m_2)^2}=41$, compute the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$.

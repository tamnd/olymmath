Compute the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{\binom{2n}{2}}
=\frac{1}{\binom{2}{2}}-\frac{1}{\binom{4}{2}}+\frac{1}{\binom{6}{2}}-\frac{1}{\binom{8}{2}}-\frac{1}{\binom{10}{2}}+\frac{1}{\binom{12}{2}}+\cdots.
$$

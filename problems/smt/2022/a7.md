Let $M=\{0,1,2,\ldots,2022\}$ and let $f:M\times M\to M$ such that for any $a,b\in M$,
$$
f(a,f(b,a))=b

and $f(x,x)\ne x$ for each $x\in M$. How many possible functions $f$ are there (mod 1000)?

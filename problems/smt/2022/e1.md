There are natural numbers $a$ and $b$, where $b$ is square-free, for which we can write
$$
20+\frac{1}{20+\frac{1}{20+\frac{1}{20+\cdots}}}=a+\sqrt{b}.
$$
What is $a+\sqrt{b}$?

Suppose that $x$ and $y$ are complex numbers satisfying the relations
$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2+y^2&=11\\
x^3+y^3&=20\\
x^4+y^4&=23\\
x^5+y^5&=-25.
\end{aligned}
$$
Compute $x^6+y^6$.

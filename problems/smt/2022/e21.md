Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle with $\overline{AB}=20$ and $\overline{AC}=22$ and circumcircle $\omega$. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of the triangle, and let $\overline{AA'}$ be a diameter of $\omega$. Suppose that $\overline{CA'}$ intersects $\overline{AH}$ at $T$. If $BHCT$ is cyclic then the sum of all possible lengths of $\overline{BC}$ can be expressed in the form $a\sqrt{b}$ where $a$ and $b$ are integers and $b$ is square-free. What is $a\sqrt{b}$?

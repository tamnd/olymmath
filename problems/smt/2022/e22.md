Let $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ be a right regular triangular prism with triangular faces $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$ and edges $\overline{AA_1},\overline{BB_1},\overline{CC_1}$. A sphere is tangent to sides $\overline{AB},\overline{BC},\overline{AC}$ at points $M,N,P$ and to the plane that the triangle $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$ is in at point $Q$. Let $\angle MPQ=45^\circ$ and the distance between lines $\overline{MP}$ and $\overline{NQ}$ be equal to $1$. Find the the side length of the base of the prism.

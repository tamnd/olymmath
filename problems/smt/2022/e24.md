Consider the sequence of integers $\{a_n\}_{n\geq 1}$ constructed in the following way. $a_1\geq 1$, and for $n\geq 2$ we have $a_n=(a_{n-1}^2+337a_{n-1})$ modulo $2022$. We define the period of a sequence to be the smallest integer $k$ such that there is an integer $N$ such that for all $n\geq N$ we have $a_{n+k}=a_n$. Determine the sum of all possible periods of $a_n$.

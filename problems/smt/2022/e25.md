Suppose that $a,b,c$ are real numbers which satisfy $a^2+b^2+c^2=2022$. Let $x=\sqrt{2022-c^2}$ and $y=\sqrt{2022-2ac}$. Find the minimum value of

$$
\frac{xy\cdot(x+y+c)}{b^2c}.
$$

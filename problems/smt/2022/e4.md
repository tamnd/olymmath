Consider an acute angled triangle $\triangle ABC$ with sides of length $a,b,c$. Let $D,E,F$ be points (distinct from $A,B,C$) on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ such that: $AD\perp BC$, $BE\perp AC$, $CF\perp AB$. What is the ratio of the area of the hexagon $AECDBF$ to the area of the triangle $\triangle ABC$?

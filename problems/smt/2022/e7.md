On December $9$, $2004$, Tracy McGrady scored $13$ points in $33$ seconds to beat the San Antonio Spurs. Given that McGrady never misses and that each shot made counts for $2$, $3$, or $4$ points, how many shot sequences could McGrady have taken to achieve such a feat assuming that order matters?

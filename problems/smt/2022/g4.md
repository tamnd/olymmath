Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle A=\frac{135^\circ}{2}$ and $\overline{BC}=15$. Square $WXYZ$ is drawn inside $ABC$ such that $W$ is on $AB$, $X$ is on $AC$, $Z$ is on $BC$, and triangle $ZBW$ is similar to triangle $ABC$, but $WZ$ is not parallel to $AC$. Over all possible triangles $ABC$, find the maximum area of $WXYZ$.

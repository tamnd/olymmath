Let $\Gamma$ and $\Omega$ be circles that are internally tangent at a point $P$ such that $\Gamma$ is contained entirely in $\Omega$. Let $A, B$ be points on $\Omega$ such that the lines $PB$ and $PA$ intersect the circle $\Gamma$ at $Y$ and $X$ respectively, where $X, Y\ne P$. Let $O_1$ be the circle with diameter $AB$ and $O_2$ be the circle with diameter $XY$. Let $F$ be the foot of $Y$ on $XP$. Let $T$ and $M$ be points on $O_1$ and $O_2$ respectively such that $TM$ is a common tangent to $O_1$ and $O_2$. Let $H$ be the orthocenter of $\triangle ABP$. Given that $PF=12, FX=15, TM=18, PB=50$, find the length of $AH$.

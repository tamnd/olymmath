You are given that $\log_{10}2\approx0.3010$ and that the first (leftmost) two digits of $2^{1000}$ are 10. Compute the number of integers $n$ with $1000\le n\le2000$ such that $2^n$ starts with either the digit 8 or 9 (in base 10).

Let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and let $E$ and $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $B$ and $C$, respectively, onto the opposite sides. $EF$ intersects $BC$ at $P$. The line passing through $O$ and perpendicular to $BC$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at $L$ (on the major arc $BC$) and $N$, and intersects $BC$ at $M$. Point $Q$ lies on the line $LA$ such that $OQ$ is perpendicular to $AP$. Given that $\angle BAC=60^\circ$ and $\angle AMC=60^\circ$, compute $OQ/AP$.

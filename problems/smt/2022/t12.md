Let $T$ be the isosceles triangle with side lengths $5,5,6$. Arpit and Katherine simultaneously choose points $A$ and $K$ within this triangle, and compute $d(A,K)$, the squared distance between the two points. Suppose that Arpit chooses a random point $A$ within $T$. Katherine plays the (possibly randomized) strategy which given Arpit’s strategy minimizes the expected value of $d(A,K)$. Compute this value.

For a regular polygon $S$ with $n$ sides, let $f(S)$ denote the regular polygon with $2n$ sides such that the vertices of $S$ are the midpoints of every other side of $f(S)$. Let $f^{(k)}(S)$ denote the polygon that results after applying $f$ a total of $k$ times. The area of
$$
\lim_{k\to\infty}f^{(k)}(P)
$$
where $P$ is a pentagon of side length 1, can be expressed as $\frac{a+b\sqrt c}{d}\pi^m$ for some positive integers $a,b,c,d,m$ where $d$ is not divisible by the square of any prime and $d$ does not share any positive divisors with $a$ and $b$. Find $a+b+c+d+m$.

Consider the function
$$
f(m)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(n-m)^2}{(2n)!}.
$$
This function can be expressed in the form $f(m)=\frac{a_m}{e}+\frac{b_m}{4}e$ for sequences of integers $\{a_m\}_{m\ge1},\{b_m\}_{m\ge1}$. Determine
$$
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{2022b_m}{a_m}.
$$

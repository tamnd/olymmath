In $\triangle ABC$, let $G$ be the centroid and let the circumcenters of $\triangle BCG,\triangle CAG$, and $\triangle ABG$ be $I,J$, and $K$, respectively. The line passing through $I$ and the midpoint of $BC$ intersects $KJ$ at $Y$. If the radius of circle $K$ is 5, the radius of circle $J$ is 8, and $AG=6$, what is the length of $KY$?

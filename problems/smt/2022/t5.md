Let $a$, $b$, and $c$ be the roots of the polynomial $x^3-3x^2-4x+5$. Compute

$$\frac{a^4+b^4}{a+b}+\frac{b^4+c^4}{b+c}+\frac{c^4+a^4}{c+a}.$$

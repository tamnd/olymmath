Carol writes a program that finds all paths on a $10$ by $2$ grid from cell $(1,1)$ to cell $(10,2)$ subject to the conditions that a path does not visit any cell more than once and at each step the path can go up, down, left, or right from the current cell, excluding moves that would make the path leave the grid. What is the total length of all such paths? (The length of a path is the number of cells it passes through, including the starting and ending cells.)

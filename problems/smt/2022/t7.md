Consider the sequence of integers $a_n$ defined by $a_1=1$, $a_p=p$ for prime $p$ and

$$a_{mn}=ma_n+na_m$$

for $m,n>1$. Find the smallest $n$ such that $\dfrac{a_{n^2}}{2022}$ is a perfect power of $3$.

Consider the set $S$ of functions $f:\{1,2,\ldots,16\}\to\{1,2,\ldots,243\}$ satisfying:

(a) $f(1)=1$

(b) $f(n^2)=n^2f(n)$,

(c) $n\mid f(n)$,

(d) $f(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n))f(\gcd(m,n))=f(m)f(n)$.

If $|S|$ can be written as $p_1^{e_1}\cdot p_2^{e_2}\cdots p_k^{e_k}$ where $p_i$ are distinct primes, compute $p_1e_1+p_2e_2+\cdots+p_ke_k$.

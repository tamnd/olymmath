A ring of six identical spheres, in which each sphere is tangent to the spheres next to it, is placed on the surface of a larger sphere so that each sphere in the ring is tangent to the larger sphere at six evenly spaced points in a circle. If the radius of the larger sphere is $5$, and the circle containing the evenly spaced points has radius $3$, what is the radius of each of the identical spheres?

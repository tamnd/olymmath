Given a sequence of coin flips, such as ‘HTTHTHT...’, we define an *inversion* as a switch from H to T or T to H. For instance, the sequence ‘HTTHT’ has $3$ inversions.

Harrison has a weighted coin that lands on heads $\frac{2}{3}$ of the time and tails $\frac{1}{3}$ of the time. If Harrison flips the coin $10$ times, what is the expected number of inversions in the sequence of flips?

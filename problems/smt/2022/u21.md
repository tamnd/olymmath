Let $\triangle ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocenter $H$, circumcenter $O$, and circumcircle $\Gamma$. Let the midpoint of minor arc $BC$ on $\Gamma$ be $M$. Suppose that $AHMO$ is a rhombus. If $BH$ and $MO$ intersect on segment $AC$, determine

$$
\frac{[AHMO]}{[ABC]}.
$$

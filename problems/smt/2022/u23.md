Let $r$ be the answer to problem 22. Let $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ be circles of each of radius $r$, respectively. Suppose that their centers are also separated by distance $r$, and the points of intersection of $\omega_1,\omega_2$ are $A$ and $B$. For each point $C$ in space, let $f(C)$ be the incenter of the triangle $ABC$. As the point $C$ rotates around the circumference of $\omega_1$, let $S$ be the length of the curve that $f(C)$ traces out. If $S$ can be written in the form $\frac{a+b\sqrt{c}}{d}\pi$ for $a,b,c,d$ nonnegative integers with $c$ squarefree and $\gcd(a,b,d)=1$, then compute $a+b+c+d$.

Let $m$ be the answer to problem 23. Suppose that $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{m-1}$ are each chosen randomly and independently from the set $\{1,2,\ldots,m\}$. Then, let $e_n$ be the expected value of $\sqrt[n]{\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}x_i^n}$. Compute $\left\lfloor\lim_{n\to\infty}e_n\right\rfloor$.

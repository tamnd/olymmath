Arpit is hanging Christmas lights on his Christmas tree for the holiday season. He decides to hang $12$ rows of lights, but if any row of lights is defective then the Christmas tree will not be lit. If the tree is not lit when he plugs in his lights, how many subsets of rows of lights can be broken for the lights to not work?

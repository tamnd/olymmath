Let $(1+2x+4x^2)^{2020}=a_0+a_1x+\cdots+a_{4040}x^{4040}$. Compute the largest exponent $k$ such that $2^k$ divides
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{2020}a_{2n-1}.
$$

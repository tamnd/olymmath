Suppose that $p(x), q(x)$ are monic polynomials with nonnegative integer coefficients such that

$$
\frac{1}{5x}\geq\frac{1}{q(x)}-\frac{1}{p(x)}\geq\frac{1}{3x^2}
$$

for all integers $x\geq 2$. Compute the minimum possible value of $p(1)\cdot q(1)$.

Colin has a peculiar 12-sided dice: it is made up of two regular hexagonal pyramids. Colin wants to paint each face one of three colors so that no two adjacent faces *on the same pyramid* have the same color. How many ways can he do this? Two paintings are considered identical if there is a way to rotate or flip the dice to go from one to the other. Faces are adjacent if they share an edge.

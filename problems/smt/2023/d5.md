Ryan chooses five subsets $S_1,S_2,S_3,S_4,S_5$ of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$ such that $|S_1|=1$, $|S_2|=2$, $|S_3|=3$, $|S_4|=4$, and $|S_5|=5$. Moreover, for all $1\leq i<j\leq 5$, either $S_i\cap S_j=S_i$ or $S_i\cap S_j=\emptyset$ (in other words, the intersection of $S_i$ and $S_j$ is either $S_i$ or the empty set). In how many ways can Ryan select the sets?

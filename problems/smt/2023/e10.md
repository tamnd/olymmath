Consider the rectangle with a length of $2$ and a width of $1$. Pick one of the two diagonals of the rectangle. Observe that this diagonal separates the rectangle into two right-angled triangles, $R_1$ and $R_2$. Reflect $R_1$ in the diagonal to obtain $R_1'$. Compute the area of the intersection of $R_1'$ and $R_2$.

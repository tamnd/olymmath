Let $A_1A_2\ldots A_{12}$ be a regular dodecagon. Equilateral triangles $\triangle A_1A_2B_1$, $\triangle A_2A_3B_2$, $\ldots$, and $\triangle A_{12}A_1B_{12}$ are drawn such that points $B_1$, $B_2$, $\ldots$, and $B_{12}$ lie outside dodecagon $A_1A_2\ldots A_{12}$. Compute the ratio of the area of dodecagon $B_1B_2\ldots B_{12}$ to the area of dodecagon $A_1A_2\ldots A_{12}$.

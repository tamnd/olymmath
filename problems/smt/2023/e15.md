Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be three points on a line (in that order), and let $X$ and $Y$ be two points on the same side of line $AC$. If $\triangle AXB\sim\triangle BYC$ and the ratio of the area of quadrilateral $AXYC$ to the area of $\triangle AXB$ is $111:1$, compute $\frac{BC}{BA}$.

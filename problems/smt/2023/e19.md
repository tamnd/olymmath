Bernie has an infinite supply of Nerds and Smarties with the property that eating one Nerd increases his IQ by $10$ and eating one Smartie increases his IQ by $14$. If Bernie currently has an IQ of $99$, how many IQ values between $100$ and $200$, inclusive, can he achieve by eating Nerds and Smarties?

William has a large supply of candy bars and wants to choose one of among three families to give the candy to. Family A has $13$ children, family B has $11$ children, and family C has $7$ children. The children in family C each require an even number of candy bars. If William attempts to distribute the candy bars equally among the children in families A, B, and C, there are $7$, $5$, and $8$ candy bars left over, respectively. What is the least number of candy bars that William could have?

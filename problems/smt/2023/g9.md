Triangle $\triangle ABC$ is isosceles with $AC = AB$, $BC = 1$, and $\angle BAC = 36^\circ$. Let $\omega$ be a circle with center $B$ and radius $r_\omega = \frac{P_{ABC}}{4}$, where $P_{ABC}$ denotes the perimeter of $\triangle ABC$. Let $\omega$ intersect line $AB$ at $P$ and line $BC$ at $Q$. Let $I_B$ be the center of the excircle with of $\triangle ABC$ with respect to point $B$, and let $BI_B$ intersect $PQ$ at $S$. We draw a tangent line from $S$ to $\odot I_B$ that intersects $\odot I_B$ at point $T$. Compute the length of $ST$.

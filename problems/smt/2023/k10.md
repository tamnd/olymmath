Define the double factorial via $(2n-1)!!=(2n-1)(2n-3)\cdots 1$. Compute the unique pair $(a,c)$ with $c>0$ and $a\in(0,\infty)$ such that
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{c^n(4n-1)!!}{(2n-1)!!(2n-1)!!}=a.
$$

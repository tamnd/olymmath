A stone is bouncing on a pond. It starts at height 1. Each time it bounces on the pond, its height $x$ changes to a uniformly random height between 0 and $x$. If the height ever drops below $\frac{1}{10}$, the next time it hits the pond it will sink. What is the expected number of times the stone will bounce before sinking (not counting the sinking as a bounce)?

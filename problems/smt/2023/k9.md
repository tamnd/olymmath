Let $\triangle ABC$ be an equilateral triangle with side length 4. Let $P$ be a point chosen uniformly and at random in the interior of $\triangle ABC$. Determine the probability that a square of side length 1 with a corner at $P$ can be rotated to lie entirely within $\triangle ABC$.

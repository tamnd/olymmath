We call a time on a 12 hour digital clock *nice* if the sum of the minutes digits is equal to the hour. For example, $10{:}55$, $3{:}12$ and $5{:}05$ are nice times. How many nice times occur during the course of one day? (We do not consider times of the form $00{:}XX$.)

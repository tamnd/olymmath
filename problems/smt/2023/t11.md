Define the Fibonacci sequence by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, and $F_i=F_{i-1}+F_{i-2}$ for $i\geq 2$. Compute

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{F_{F_{n+1}+1}}{F_{F_n}\cdot F_{F_{n-1}-1}}.
$$

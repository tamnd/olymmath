Let $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ be points in the plane with integer coordinates such that no three of them are collinear, and where the distances $AB$, $AC$, $AD$, $BC$, $BD$, and $CD$ are all integers. Compute the smallest possible length of a side of a convex quadrilateral formed by such points.

Suppose the real roots of $p(x)=x^9+16x^8+60x^7+1920x^2+2048x+512$ are $r_1,r_2\ldots,r_k$ (roots may be repeated). Compute

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{k}\frac{1}{2-r_i}.
$$

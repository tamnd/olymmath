Along Stanford’s University Avenue are 2023 palm trees which are either red, green, or blue. Let the positive integers $R$, $G$, $B$ be the number of red, green, and blue palm trees respectively. Given that

$$
R^3+2B+G=12345,
$$

compute $R$.

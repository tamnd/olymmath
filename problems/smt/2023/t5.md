A computer virus starts off infecting a single device. Every second an infected computer has a $7/30$ chance to stay infected and not do anything else, a $7/15$ chance to infect a new computer, and a $1/6$ chance to infect two new computers. Otherwise (a $2/15$ chance), the virus gets exterminated, but other copies of it on other computers are unaffected. Compute the probability that a single infected computer produces an infinite chain of infections.

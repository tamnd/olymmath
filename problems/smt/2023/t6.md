In the language of *Blah*, there is a unique word for every integer between 0 and 98 inclusive. A team of students has an unordered list of these 99 words, but do not know what integer each word corresponds to. However, the team is given access to a machine that, given two, not necessarily distinct, words in *Blah*, outputs the word in *Blah* corresponding to the sum modulo 99 of their corresponding integers. What is the minimum $N$ such that the team can narrow down the possible translations of “1” to a list of $N$ *Blah* words, using the machine as many times as they want?

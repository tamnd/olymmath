Let $A$ be the area enclosed by the relation

$$
x^2+y^2\leq 2023.
$$
Let $B$ be the area enclosed by the relation

$$
x^{2n}+y^{2n}\leq\left(A\cdot\frac{7}{16\pi}\right)^{n/2}.
$$

Compute the limit of $B$ as $n\to\infty$ for $n\in\mathbb{N}$.

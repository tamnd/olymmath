To convert between Fahrenheit, $F$, and Celsius, $C$, the formula is $F=\frac{9}{5}C+32$. Jennifer, having no time to be this precise, instead approximates the temperature of Fahrenheit, $\widehat{F}$, as $\widehat{F}=2C+30$. There is a range of temperatures $C_1\le C\le C_2$ such that for any $C$ in this range, $|\widehat{F}-F|\le 5$. Compute the ordered pair $(C_1,C_2)$.

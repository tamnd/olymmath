Ten square slips of paper of the same size, numbered $0,1,2,\ldots,9$, are placed into a bag. Four of these squares are then randomly chosen and placed into a two-by-two grid of squares. What is the probability that the numbers in every pair of blocks sharing a side have an absolute difference no greater than two?

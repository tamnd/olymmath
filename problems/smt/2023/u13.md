Let $\triangle ABC$ be an equilateral triangle with side length $1$. Let the unit circles centered at $A$, $B$, and $C$ be $\Omega_A$, $\Omega_B$, and $\Omega_C$, respectively. Then, let $\Omega_A$ and $\Omega_C$ intersect again at point $D$, and $\Omega_B$ and $\Omega_C$ intersect again at point $E$. Line $BD$ intersects $\Omega_B$ at point $F$ where $F$ lies between $B$ and $D$, and line $AE$ intersects $\Omega_A$ at $G$ where $G$ lies between $A$ and $E$. $BD$ and $AE$ intersect at $H$. Finally, let $CH$ and $FG$ intersect at $I$. Compute $IH$.

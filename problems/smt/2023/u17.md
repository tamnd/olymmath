Consider a triangle $\triangle ABC$ with angles $\angle ACB=60^\circ$, $\angle ABC=45^\circ$. The circumcircle around $\triangle ABH$, where $H$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$, intersects $BC$ for a second time in point $P$, and the center of that circumcircle is $O_c$. The line $PH$ intersects $AC$ in point $Q$, and $N$ is center of the circumcircle around $\triangle AQP$. Find $\angle NO_cP$.

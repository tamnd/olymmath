$A_1A_2\ldots A_{12}$ is a regular dodecagon with side length $1$ and center at point $O$. What is the area of the region covered by circles $(A_1A_2O)$, $(A_3A_4O)$, $(A_5A_6O)$, $(A_7A_8O)$, $(A_9A_{10}O)$, and $(A_{11}A_{12}O)$? $(ABC)$ denotes the circle passing through points $A$, $B$, and $C$.

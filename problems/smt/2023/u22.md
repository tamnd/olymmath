Consider the series $\{A_n\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$, where $A_0=1$ and for every $n>0$,

$$
A_n=A_{\left[\frac{n}{2023}\right]}+A_{\left[\frac{n}{2023^2}\right]}+A_{\left[\frac{n}{2023^3}\right]},
$$

where $[x]$ denotes the largest integer value smaller than or equal to $x$. Find the $(2023^{3^2}+20)$-th element of the series.

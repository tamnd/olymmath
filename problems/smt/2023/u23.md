The side lengths of triangle $\triangle ABC$ are $5$, $7$ and $8$. Construct equilateral triangles $\triangle A_1BC$, $\triangle B_1CA$, and $\triangle C_1AB$ such that $A_1,B_1,C_1$ lie outside of $\triangle ABC$. Let $A_2,B_2$, and $C_2$ be the centers of $\triangle A_1BC$, $\triangle B_1CA$, and $\triangle C_1AB$, respectively. What is the area of $\triangle A_2B_2C_2$?

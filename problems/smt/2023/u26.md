A year is said to be *interesting* if it is the product of $3$, not necessarily distinct, primes (for example $2^2\cdot 5$ is interesting, but $2^2\cdot 3\cdot 5$ is not). How many interesting years are there between $5000$ and $10000$, inclusive?

For an estimate of $E$, you will get $\max\left(0,25-\left\lceil\left|\frac{E-X}{10}\right|\right\rceil\right)$ points, where $X$ is the true answer.

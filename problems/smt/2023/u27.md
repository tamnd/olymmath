Sam chooses $1000$ random lattice points $(x,y)$ with $1\leq x,y\leq 1000$ such that all pairs $(x,y)$ are distinct. Let $N$ be the expected size of the maximum collinear set among them. Estimate $\lfloor 100N\rfloor$. Let $S$ be the answer you provide and $X$ be the true value of $\lfloor 100N\rfloor$.

You will get $\max\left(0,25-\left\lceil\left|\frac{S-X}{10}\right|\right\rceil\right)$ points for your estimate.

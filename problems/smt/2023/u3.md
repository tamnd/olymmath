The number of ways to flip $n$ fair coins such that there are no three heads in a row can be expressed with the recurrence relation

$$S(n+1)=a_0S(n)+a_1S(n-1)+...+a_kS(n-k)$$

for sufficiently large $n$ and $k$ where $S(n)$ is the number of valid sequences of length $n$. What is

$$\sum_{n=0}^{k}|a_n|?$$

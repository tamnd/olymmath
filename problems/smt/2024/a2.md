Suppose that for some angle $0\leq\theta\leq\pi/4$, the roots of $x^2+ax+\frac{3}{10}$ are $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$. If the roots of $p(x)=x^2+cx+d$ are $\sin(2\theta)$ and $\cos(2\theta)$, what is the value of $p(1)$?

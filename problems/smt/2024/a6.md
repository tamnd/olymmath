Let $f(x,y)=xy$ and $g(x,y)=x^2-y^2$. If a counterclockwise rotation of $\theta$ radians about the origin sends $g(x,y)=a$ to $f(x,y)=b$, compute the value of $\frac{a}{b\tan\theta}$.

Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,$ and $a_4$ be non-negative real numbers such that $a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2+a_4^2=1$. Compute the minimum possible value of
$$6a_1^3+8a_2^3+12a_3^3+24a_4^3.$$

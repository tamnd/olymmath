Let $X$ be a $2024$ digit perfect square. Let $a(X)$ be the $1012$ digit number formed from reading the first $1012$ digits of $X$, in order, and let $b(X)$ be the $1012$ digit number formed from reading the last $1012$ digits of $X$, in order. Given that $X$ is the unique choice that maximizes $a(X)-b(X)$, find the sum of digits of $X$.

We define the *spillage* of a number as $s(x)=\left\lfloor\frac{x}{100}\right\rfloor$, that is, the largest integer that is at most $\frac{x}{100}$. The *spillage* of a list of numbers $[a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n]$ is the sum of left to right *spillages*: $s(a_1)+s(a_1a_2)+s(a_1a_2a_3)+\cdots+s(a_1a_2\cdots a_n)$. Let $M$ be the minimum possible *spillage* of $[1,2,\ldots,10]$ over all the permutations of this list. How many of these permutations achieve $M$?

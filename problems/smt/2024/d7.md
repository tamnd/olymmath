Let $1\leq A\leq 119$ and $1\leq B\leq 139$ be two integers such that $\frac{A}{60}$ and $\frac{B}{70}$ are fractions in simplest form, yet, when adding $\frac{A}{60}$ and $\frac{B}{70}$ by rewriting both fractions with their lowest common denominator and adding the resulting numerators, the new fraction can be simplified. Find how many ordered pairs $(A,B)$ are possible.

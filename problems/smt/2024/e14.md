Let $0.\overline{d}_b = 0.ddd\ldots_b$ denote a repeating decimal written in base $b$. If $0.\overline{4}_a + 0.\overline{7}_b = 1$ for positive integers $a$ and $b$ such that $a \ne b$, what is the minimum possible value of $a+b$?

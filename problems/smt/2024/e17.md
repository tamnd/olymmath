Nacho is building a sandcastle. Each time he adds a scoop of sand, he has a $\frac{5}{6}$ chance that the sandcastle will increase by $1$ inch in height. Nacho is a clumsy engineer, so each time the height doesn’t increase, the sandcastle topples and loses $\frac{1}{3}$ of its current height. Suppose Nacho starts his sandcastle at height $H$. What $H$ should he choose so that after any number of scoops, the expected height of his sandcastle is still $H$?

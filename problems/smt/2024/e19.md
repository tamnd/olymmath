Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots$ be a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers such that $a_{3k-2}$ is divisible by $8$ and $a_{3k}$ is divisible by $9$ for all positive integers $k$. Find the largest possible positive integer $i$ such that $a_i>2024$ and $a_{i-1}\leq 2024$.

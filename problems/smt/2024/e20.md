I have $3$ red balls, $3$ blue balls, and $3$ yellow balls. These $9$ balls are randomly arranged on a $3\times3$ grid. Let a 3-in-a-row denote when $3$ balls of the same color are aligned in a line in the grid (a row, column, or diagonal consisting of $3$ balls of the same color). What is the expected number of 3-in-a-rows that will show up in the grid?

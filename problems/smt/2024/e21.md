How many positive integers $n$ are there such that the powers of $2024$ (mod $n$) repeat in a cycle of length $2$? In other words, how many positive integers $n$ are there such that

$$
2024^0\pmod{n}\equiv2024^2\pmod{n}\equiv2024^4\pmod{n}\ldots
$$

and

$$
2024^1\pmod{n}\equiv2024^3\pmod{n}\equiv2024^5\pmod{n}\ldots
$$

but

$$
2024^0\pmod{n}\not\equiv2024^1\pmod{n}?
$$

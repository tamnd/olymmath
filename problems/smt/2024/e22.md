Consider a quadratic function $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ with distinct positive roots $r_1$ and $r_2$, and a second polynomial $Q(x)=cx^2+bx+a$ with roots $r_3$ and $r_4$. John writes four numbers on the whiteboard: $r_1$, $r_2$, $4r_3$ and $4r_4$. What is the smallest possible integer value of the sum of the numbers John wrote down?

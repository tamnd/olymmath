Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the region in the $xy$-plane bounded by $y=0$, $y=x$, and $y=2-x$. $\mathcal{A}$ includes the area enclosed by these boundaries, as well as the boundaries themselves. What is the maximum possible radius of a circle that lies in $\mathcal{A}$?

Call two positive integers similar if their prime factorization have the same number of distinct prime divisors, and when ordered in some way, the exponents match. For example, 250 and 24 are similar because $250=5^3\cdot 2$, and $24=2^3\cdot 3$. How many positive integers less than or equal to 200 are similar to 18 (including itself)?

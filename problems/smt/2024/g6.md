Let $ABCDE$ be a regular pentagon with side length $1$. Circles $\omega_B$, $\omega_C$, and $\omega_D$ are centered at $B$, $C$, and $D$, respectively, each with radius $1$. $\omega_B$ intersects $\omega_C$ inside $ABCDE$ at point $F$, and $\omega_C$ intersects $\omega_D$ inside $ABCDE$ at point $G$. Compute the ratio of the measure of $\angle AFB$ to the measure of $\angle AGB$.

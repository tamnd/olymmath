Let $f_0(x)=\max(|x|,\cos(x))$, and $f_{k+1}(x)=\max(|x|-f_k(x),f_k(x)-\cos(x))$. Compute

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}(f_{n-1}(x)+f_n(x))\,dx.
$$

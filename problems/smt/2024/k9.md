The integral $\int_0^{f(2024)} f^{-1}(x)\,dx$, where $f(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$, can be written in the form $A(1-e^{-B})$ for positive rational constants $A$ and $B$. Compute $A+\lfloor\log_{10}B\rfloor$.

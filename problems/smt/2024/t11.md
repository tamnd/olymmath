Floria is picking flowers on a vast prairie with six different colors of flowers, where each color is equally likely to be picked at random, independent of previously picked flowers. Out of the flowers she picks, Floria wants to make a bouquet of $7$ flowers, such that all $6$ colors are used and there is a pair of identically colored flowers.

Let $O$ be the center of a unit circle and $d$ be a diameter of this circle. Consider a point $X\ne O$ with $XO\perp d$. Let points $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be the points on line $XO$ such that $\overline{X_iO}=\frac{1}{2^i}$. Let $p_i$ be the chord passing through $X_i$ with $p_i\perp XO$ and let $C_i$ and $B_i$ be the endpoints of $p_i$. If $A_i$ is the area of the triangle $OB_iC_i$, compute

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} A_i^2.
$$

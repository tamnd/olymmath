There are two birds currently sitting on a tree. At the end of every passing hour, there is a $1-\frac{1}{n}$ probability that one of the birds on the tree at that time leaves the tree (where $n$ is the number of birds sitting on the tree that past hour). Similarly, at the end of every passing hour there is an independent probability of $\frac{1}{n}$ that one new bird will come sit on the tree. What is the expected number of hours that will pass until there are four birds on the tree for the first time?

For any integer $n\geq 2$ with prime factorization $n=p_1^{a_1}p_2^{a_2}\ldots p_k^{a_k}$, we let $f(n)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i a_i$. For example, since $90=2^1\cdot3^2\cdot5^1$, we have $f(90)=2\cdot1+3\cdot2+5\cdot1=13$. Let $m$ be the minimum value that $f(n)$ can take on for all integers $n>2024$. Find the smallest integer $k\geq2$ such that $f(k)=m$.

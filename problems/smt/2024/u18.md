Consider the following rule for moves on the two-dimensional integer lattice: for each coordinate $(b,c)$ that you are on, move to $(b+1,c)$ if $0=x^2+bx+c$ has no real solution, and move to $(b,c+1)$ otherwise. If you begin at $(0,0)$, what coordinates do you land on after $2024$ moves?

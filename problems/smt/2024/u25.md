Frank composes a random 15-note melody where each note is either A, B, or C. What is the probability that no sequence of 5 consecutive notes occurs more than once in the melody he composes? (For example, in the melody ABCABCABCABCABC the sequence ABCAB occurs more than once.)

For an estimate of $E$, you will get $\max(0,25-\lceil 500|E-X|\rceil)$ points, where $X$ is the true answer.

A prime number is said to be *toothless* if none of its digits are 2, 4, or 8. Estimate the number of toothless primes with at most 8 digits.

For an estimate of $E$, you will get $\max\left(0,25-\left\lceil\frac{|E-X|}{4000}\right\rceil\right)$ points, where $X$ is the true answer.

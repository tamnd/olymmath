Let $f(x)=4x+a$, $g(x)=6x+b$, and $h(x)=9x+c$. Let $S(a,b,c)$ be the set of the $3^{20}$ functions formed by all possible compositions of $f$, $g$, $h$ a total of 20 times, and let $R(a,b,c)$ be the number of distinct roots over all functions in $S(a,b,c)$. Compute the second smallest possible value of $R(a,b,c)$ as $a,b,c$ range over all reals.

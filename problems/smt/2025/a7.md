The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ for all positive integers $n\geq 3$. Let $S=\{2^{F_2},2^{F_3},\ldots,2^{F_{27}},2^{F_{28}}\}=\{2,4,\ldots,2^{196418},2^{317811}\}$ be a set. If

$$
P=\prod_{x\in S}\prod_{y\in S}x^{\log_3 y}
$$

is the product of $x^{\log_3 y}$ across all ordered pairs of elements $(x,y)$ in $S$, including when $x=y$, compute $\sqrt{\log_2 P}$.

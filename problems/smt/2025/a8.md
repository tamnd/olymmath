Let $Z$ be the set of all complex numbers $z$ such that $|z|=1$ and

$$
z^8+iz^6-(1+i)z^5+(1-i)z^3+iz^2-1=0,
$$

with the polar angle $\theta_z$ (the angle in $[0,2\pi)$ that $z$ makes with the positive real axis, measured counterclockwise) lying in the interval $[0,\pi)$. Compute the sum of all distinct values of $\theta_z$ for $z$ in $Z$.

Define a sequence $\{a_n\}$ by $a_1=5$ and $a_{n+1}=\frac{5a_n+\sqrt{21a_n^2+4}}{2}$ for $n\geq 1$. Compute the value of

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{a_na_{n+1}}.
$$

Professor Matrix Tessellation Laplace arranges all of the integers from 1 to 99999 (inclusive) in the order that maximizes the gigantic number formed by concatenating them. In this order, the number 2025 appears as the $i$-th integer from left to right. Find $i$.

(Formally, he sets the permutation $\sigma=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{99999})$ of $\{1,2,\ldots,99999\}$ such that the value of the concatenation $\overline{\sigma_1\sigma_2\ldots\sigma_{99999}}$ is maximized. Find the index $i$ such that $\sigma_i=2025$.)

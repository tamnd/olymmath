For any permutation $\sigma=(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_8)$ of the set $\{1,2,\ldots,8\}$ and some index $1\le k\le 8$, define

$$
L_k(\sigma)=\operatorname{lcm}(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k)
$$

where $\operatorname{lcm}(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k)$ denotes the least common multiple of $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k$. An index $2\le k\le 8$ is called *increasing* if $L_k(\sigma)>L_{k-1}(\sigma)$. Let $T(\sigma)$ be the total number of increasing indices of $\sigma$. Compute the expected value of $T(\sigma)$ when $\sigma$ is chosen uniformly at random among all permutations of $\{1,2,\ldots,8\}$.

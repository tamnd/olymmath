Let $\alpha$ be the permutation of $\{0,1,\ldots,12\}$ satisfying $\alpha(0)=0$, $\alpha(n)=n+1$ for odd $n$, and $\alpha(n)=n-1$ for positive even $n$. Let $f(n)$ be the unique polynomial of minimal degree with coefficients in $\{0,1,\ldots,12\}$ such that $13\mid f(n)-\alpha(n)$ for all $n\in\{0,1,\ldots,12\}$. If $f(n)$ has degree $d$ and can be expressed as $f(n)=\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_i n^i$, find $100a_d+a_1$.

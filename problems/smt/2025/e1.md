Ethan likes to build sandcastles on the beach. Let $t,h,e,b,a,c$ be integers such that $b+e\ne 0$ and

$$
\frac{t^h e}{b+e}=a-ch.
$$

If $b=0,c=1,t=2$, and $h=3$, compute $a$.

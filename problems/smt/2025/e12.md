Compute the number of subsets $A$ of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$ which have the property that more than half of the elements in $A$ are prime. For example, $A=\{2,3,4\}$ satisfies the condition. (A prime number is a positive integer greater than $1$, whose only positive divisors are $1$ and itself.)

An integer $m>1$ is called *elementary* if its first digit is 1, its last digit is 1, and every other digit is 0. Let $S$ be the set of the 2025 smallest elementary integers. Compute the greatest possible size of a subset $T$ of $S$ such that any two distinct elements in $T$ are pairwise coprime.

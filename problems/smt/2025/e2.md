An isosceles triangle has legs of length $9!+8!$, a base of length $2(9!-8!)$, and a height of length $m\cdot 8!$ perpendicular to the base for some positive integer $m$. Compute $m$.

(Here, $n!$ represents the factorial of $n$, or the product of all positive integers up to $n$. For example, $3!=3\times 2\times 1=6$ and $2!=2\times 1=2$.)

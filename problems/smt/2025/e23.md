Compute the number of integers between 1 and 2025 inclusive that *cannot* be represented as $x(\lceil x\rceil+\lfloor x\rfloor)$ for any positive real number $x$.

(Recall that $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest integer not exceeding $x$ and $\lceil x\rceil$ is the smallest integer not less than $x$.)

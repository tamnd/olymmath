Let $s_0$ be a square. One diagonal of $s_0$ is chosen, and a square $s_1$ is drawn with this diagonal as one of its sides. There are four possible such squares $s_1$; one is chosen out of these four uniformly at random. Choose $s_2$ in the same manner (so that it has a side which is a diagonal of $s_1$), and continue similarly with $s_3,s_4,\ldots$. Let $p$ be the probability that square $s_{20}$ shares an interior with $s_0$ (in other words, the area of their intersection is positive). Estimate $p$ in the decimal form $0.abcdefgh$, where $a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h$ are decimal digits each between 0 and 9, inclusive.

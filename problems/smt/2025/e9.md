Professor Matrix Tessellation Laplace writes the following equation on a blank blackboard:

$$z=0.$$

One by one, each of his $2025$ students edits the equation by adding a new term:

$$x_1y_1+z=0$$

$$x_2y_2+x_1y_1+z=0$$

$$\vdots$$

When all $2025$ students are done, compute the number of characters written on the blackboard. These include digits, variable names, operations, and the equal sign (there is always only one equation on the board). For example, $x_{12}y_{12}+z=0$ has $10$ characters.

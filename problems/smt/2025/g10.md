Let $AFDC$ be a rectangle. Construct points $E$ and $B$ outside of $AFDC$ such that $AB=BC=DE=EF=45$ and $ABCDEF$ is a convex hexagon. Let $\mathcal{E}$ be an inscribed ellipse tangent to sides $AB,BC,CD,DE,EF,FA$ at points $U,V,W,X,Y,$ and $Z$, respectively. Points $F_1$ and $F_2$ with $F_1$ closer to $B$ are the foci of $\mathcal{E}$ satisfying $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle F_1WF_2\cong\triangle DEF$. Let $Q$ be on line $CD$ such that $F_1Y\perp ZQ$. Compute the area of quadrilateral $F_1QYZ$.

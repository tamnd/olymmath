Given real constants $a,b,c$, define the cubic polynomials
$$
\begin{aligned}
A(x)&=ax^3+abx^2-4x-c\\
B(x)&=bx^3+bcx^2-6x-a\\
C(x)&=cx^3+cax^2-9x-b.
\end{aligned}
$$
Given that $A$, $B$, and $C$ have local extrema at $b$, $c$, and $a$, respectively, compute $abc$. (A local extremum is either a local maximum or a local minimum.)

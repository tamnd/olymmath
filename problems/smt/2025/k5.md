Consider the set of all continuous and infinitely differentiable functions $f$ with domain $[0,2025]$ satisfying $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=0$, $f'(2025)=1$, and $f''$ being strictly increasing on the interval $[0,2025]$. Compute the smallest real number $M$ such that for all functions $f$ in this set, $f(2025)<M$.

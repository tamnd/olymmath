Consider the function

$$f(x)=\frac{(2025+x)\ln(2025+x)}{x^3-6x^2+11x-6}$$

defined for all real numbers except $x=1,2,$ and $3$. For a positive integer $n$, let $d_n$ denote $\frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}$, where $f^{(n)}(x)$ is the $n^{\text{th}}$ derivative of $f(x)$. Compute $\lim_{n\to\infty}d_n$.

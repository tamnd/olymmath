For positive integers $p$ and $q$, there are exactly $14$ ordered pairs of real numbers $(a,b)$ with $0<a,b<1$ such that both $ap+3b$ and $2a+bq$ are positive integers. Compute the number of possible ordered pairs $(p,q)$.

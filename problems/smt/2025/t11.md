Let $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ be circles such that $\omega_2$ passes through the center of $\omega_1$ and is also internally tangent to it. In addition, let $AB$ be a chord of $\omega_1$ tangent to $\omega_2$ at some point $T$. Given that $AT=9$ and $BT=16$, compute the radius of $\omega_1$.

For each positive integer $n$, define the coloring $C_n$ as a coloring of all lattice points (i.e. points with integer coordinates) on the plane such that any two points $A=(A_x,A_y)$, $B=(B_x,B_y)$ have the same color if and only if $A_x+A_y$ and $B_x+B_y$ have the same remainder when divided by $n$. A positive integer $n$ is called *broken* if in the coloring $C_n$ there exist two points of different color and Euclidean distance a positive integer divisible by $n$. Find the sum of all broken positive integers less than or equal to $50$.

Let $S$ be the set of all strings of length $15$ formed from five $1$s, $2$s, and $3$s. Say a string in $S$ is *threnodic* if:

- No two adjacent characters are the same, and
- Through a sequence of removals of contiguous substrings $123$, $231$, and $312$, the string can be deleted (note that the intermediate strings can have adjacent equal characters).

Compute the number of threnodic strings in $S$.

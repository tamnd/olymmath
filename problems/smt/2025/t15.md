Let $\triangle ABC$ be an acute triangle with circumcircle $\omega_1$, and let $D$ be a point on segment $BC$. Circle $\omega_2$ is tangent to segment $AD$, segment $BD$, and $\omega_1$. Circle $\omega_3$ is tangent to segment $AD$, segment $CD$, and $\omega_1$, and both circles are on the same side of $BC$ as $A$. If circles $\omega_2$ and $\omega_3$ have radii $5$ and $7$, respectively, with centers $13$ units apart, compute the sum of all possible lengths of the inradius of $\triangle ABC$.

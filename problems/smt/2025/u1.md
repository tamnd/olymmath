Your team is participating in the Guts round! You see a problem which consists of 5 Yes/No questions and peculiar scoring. You will submit $n$ of the 5 problems and receive $n(n-1)$ points if they’re all right and 0 points if any of them is wrong. Suppose your team is 100% certain about the answers for two of the questions and has no clue for the other three (50% chance of getting each right). Compute the average number of points your team will get if they submit answers to the questions to maximize this average.

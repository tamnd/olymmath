Aristotle is thinking of a monic quadratic polynomial $P(x)=x^2+ax+b$ with roots $r$ and $s$. He tells Plato $r$ and $a$ and tells Socrates $r$ and $b$. However, Plato mistakenly believes that $a$ is the constant term, and Socrates believes that $b$ is the coefficient of $x$. Plato and Socrates independently compute $s$, and their (incorrect) results multiply to 1. If $s=5$, compute $r$.

A sequence of real numbers $\{a_n\}$ satisfies $a_0=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ and $a_n=\frac{1}{a_0}\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a_k^2}$ for $n\geq 1$. Define the sequence $\{b_N\}$ by

$$
b_N=\frac{\sum_{n=1}^{2N}a_n}{\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n}
$$

for $N\geq 1$. Compute the smallest value of $N$ such that $b_N\geq 2025$.

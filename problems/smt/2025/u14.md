Sophie has a right triangle $T$ with sides $6$, $8$, and $10$. Let $\mathcal{P}$ be the set of all points $P$ within $T$ such that there is a circle of radius $1$ entirely contained within $T$ with $P$ lying on its circumference. Compute the area of $\mathcal{P}$.

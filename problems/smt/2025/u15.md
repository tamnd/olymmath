Define a function $f(n)$ by

$$
f(n)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\lfloor\frac{n}{i}\right\rfloor,
$$

where $n$ is a positive integer. Compute the number of integers $n$ between $1$ and $2025$, inclusive, such that $f(n)$ is odd.

Let $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ be circles with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$, and radii $2$ and $3$, respectively. Let $\omega_3$ be a third circle centered at $O_3$, intersecting $\omega_1$ at point $X$, $\omega_2$ at point $Y$, such that $O_1X\perp XO_3$ and $O_2Y\perp YO_3$. Additionally, $\omega_3$ intersects $O_1O_2$ at distinct points $M$ and $N$, and $O_1O_2=7$. Over all possible circles $\omega_3$, compute the maximum possible value of $MN$.

Call an ordered quintuple $(p,q,r,s,t)$ of integers good if $-3\leq p,q,r,s,t\leq 3$, and there exists a nonzero solution $(a,b,c,d,e)\neq(0,0,0,0,0)$ to the following system of equations:

$$
\begin{cases}
a+bp+cp^2+dp^3+ep^5=0\\
a+bq+cq^2+dq^3+eq^5=0\\
a+br+cr^2+dr^3+er^5=0\\
a+bs+cs^2+ds^3+es^5=0\\
a+bt+ct^2+dt^3+et^5=0.
\end{cases}
$$

Here, $a,b,c,d,e$ are real numbers. Compute the number of good quintuples.

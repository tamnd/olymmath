Let $I$ and $O$ be the incenter and circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$, respectively, and let $\omega$ be its incircle. Suppose that the tangents from $O$ to $\omega$ meet $\omega$ at $E$ and $F$, and suppose $EF$ meets $BC$ at $D$. If the inradius of $\triangle ABC$ is $2$ and $OI=OD=\sqrt{10}$, find $BC^2$. (There are two possible configurations. Either answer will be accepted.)

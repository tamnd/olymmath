Call a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$ almost-convex if there exists at most one triple $a,b,c\in\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$ such that $a<b<c$, and

$$
\sigma(b)>\frac{\sigma(c)-\sigma(a)}{c-a}(b-a)+\sigma(a).
$$

Compute the number of almost-convex permutations of $\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$.

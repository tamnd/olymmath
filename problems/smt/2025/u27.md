We define the $m$-expansion of a multiset of numbers (a multiset is a set where duplicates are allowed) as the maximum sum of squares among all of the subsets of size $m$ (formally, the expansion is $\max_{S:\,|S|=m}\sum_{i\in S}a_i^2$).

Suppose that $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{170}$ are real numbers such that $\sum_{i=1}^{170}a_i^2=170$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{170}a_i^4=510$. Compute the smallest possible $85$-expansion of any set $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{170}$ satisfying these properties.

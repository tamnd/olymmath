The nonzero digits $A$, $B$, and $C$ are chosen such that the three-digit number $\overline{ABC}$ is a multiple of 25, and the three-digit number $\overline{CBA}$ is a multiple of 36. Compute the product of the three digits, that is, $A\times B\times C$.

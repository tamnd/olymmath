In a dance party there are $n$ girls and $n$ boys, and some $m$ songs are played. Each song is danced to by at least one pair of a boy and a girl, who both receive a *malai* each. Prove that for all positive integers $k \le n$ , it is possible to select $k$ boys and $n - k$ girls so that the $n$ selected people received at least $m$ malai in total.

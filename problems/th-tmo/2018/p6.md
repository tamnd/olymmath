Let $A$ be the set of all triples $(x, y, z)$ of positive integers satisfying $2x^2 + 3y^3 = 4z^4$.
a) Show that if $(x, y, z) \in A$ then $6$ divides all of $x, y, z$.
b) Show that $A$ is an infinite set.

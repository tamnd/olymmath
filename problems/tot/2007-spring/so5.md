a) The cake has the shape of an obtuse triangle $\Delta_{1}$, where the obtuse angle is twice as large as one of the acute angles. The cake box has the shape of a triangle $\Delta_{2}$, and $\Delta_{1}$ and $\Delta_{2}$ are symmetric with respect to some line. How can the cake be cut into two pieces that can fit into the box (without flipping the cake over so that the frosting is facing down)?

b) The same problem for a cake with the shape of a triangle with angles $20^{\circ}, 30^{\circ}, 130^{\circ}$.

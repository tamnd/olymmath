The test consists of 30 questions, each with two answer options (one correct, the other incorrect). In one attempt, Vitya answers all the questions, after which he is told how many questions he answered correctly. Can Vitya act in such a way as to guarantee that he will know all the correct answers no later than:

a) after the 29th attempt (and answer all questions correctly on the 30th attempt);

b) after the 24th attempt (and answer all questions correctly on the 25th attempt)?

(Initially, Vitya does not know any of the answers, and the test is always the same.)

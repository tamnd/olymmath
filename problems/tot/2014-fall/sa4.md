The incircle of triangle $ABC$ touches the sides $BC, CA, AB$ at points $A', B', C'$ respectively. The lines $AA', BB'$ and $CC'$ intersect at point $G$. The circumcircle of triangle $GA'B'$ intersects the lines $AC$ and $BC$ again at points $C_A$ and $C_B$. Similarly, points $A_B, A_C, B_C, B_A$ are defined. Prove that the points $A_B, A_C, B_C, B_A$, $C_A, C_B$ lie on a single circle.

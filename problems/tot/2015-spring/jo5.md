Given $2 n+1$ numbers ( $n$ - natural), among which one number is 0, two numbers are 1, two numbers are 2, ..., two numbers are $n$. For which $n$ can these numbers be written in one line so that for each natural $m$ from 1 to $n$, there are exactly $m$ other numbers between the two numbers equal to $m$?

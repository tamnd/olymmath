Given a regular $2 n$-gon $A_{1} A_{1} \ldots A_{2 n}$ with center $O$, where $n \geq 5$. Diagonals $A_{2} A_{n-1}$ and $A_{3} A_{n}$ intersect at point $F$, and $A_{1} A_{3}$ and $A_{2} A_{2 n-2}$ intersect at point $P$.

Prove that $P F=P O$.

There is an iron weight of 6 kg, sugar, and weightless bags in unlimited quantities, as well as non-standard scales with two pans: the scales are in balance if the loads on the left and right pans are in the ratio of $3: 4$. In one weighing, you can place any available weights on the scales and add a bag with a certain amount of sugar to one of the pans to balance the scales (such bags of sugar can be used in subsequent weighings). Is it possible to measure out 1 kg of sugar?

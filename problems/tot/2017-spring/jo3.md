From the vertex $A$ of an acute-angled triangle $ABC$, a billiard ball was launched along the angle bisector of $\angle A$, which then reflected off the side $BC$ according to the law "the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection" and continued to roll in a straight line, no longer reflecting from anything. Prove that if $\angle A=60^{\circ}$, then the trajectory of the ball passes through the center of the circumscribed circle of triangle $ABC$.

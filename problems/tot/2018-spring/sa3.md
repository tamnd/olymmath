In a $10 \times 10$ table, 100 different numbers are written. In one move, you can choose any rectangle composed of cells and rearrange all the numbers in it symmetrically relative to its center ("rotate the rectangle by $\left.180^{\circ} "\right)$. Is it always possible to achieve, in 99 moves, that the numbers increase from left to right in each row and from bottom to top in each column?

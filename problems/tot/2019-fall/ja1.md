Let us call the number of factors in the prime decomposition of an integer $n > 1$ the complexity of $n$ . For example, *complexity* of numbers $4$ and $6$ is equal to $2$ . Find all $n$ such that all integers between $n$ and $2n$ have complexity
a) not greater than the complexity of $n$ .
b) less than the complexity of $n$ .

(Boris Frenkin)

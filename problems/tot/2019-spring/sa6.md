For each five distinct variables from the set $x_1,..., x_{10}$ there is a single card on which their product is written. Peter and Basil play the following game. At each move, each player chooses a card, starting with Peter. When all cards have been taken, Basil assigns values to the variables as he wants, so that $0 \le x_1 \le ... \le x_10$ . Can Basil ensure that the sum of the products on his cards is greater than the sum of the products on Peter's cards?

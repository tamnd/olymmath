A parallelogram $ABCD$ is split by the diagonal $BD$ into two equal triangles. A regular hexagon is inscribed into the triangle $ABD$ so that two of its consecutive sides lie on $AB$ and $AD$ and one of its vertices lies on $BD$ . Another regular hexagon is inscribed into the triangle $CBD{}$ so that two of its consecutive vertices lie on $CB$ and $CD$ and one of its sides lies on $BD$ . Which of the hexagons is bigger?

Given $2N$ real numbers. It is known that if they are arbitrarily divided into two groups of $N$ numbers each then the products of the numbers of each group differ by $2$ at most. Is it necessarily true that if we arbitrarily place these numbers along a circle then there are two neighboring numbers that differ by $2$ at most, for
a) $N=50$ ; (3 marks)
b) $N=25$ ? (5 marks)

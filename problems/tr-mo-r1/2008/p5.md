(i) $40^o$, $60^o$, $80^o$
(ii) $10^o$, $10^o$, $160^o$
(iii) $110^o$, $35^o$, $35^o$
(iv) $50^o$, $30^o$, $100^o$
(v) $90^o$, $40^o$, $50^o$
(vi) $80^o$, $20^o$, $80^o$

a) 1 b) 2 c) 3 d) 4 e) 5

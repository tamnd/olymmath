For every positive integer $N$, let $\sigma(N)$ denote the sum of the positive integer divisors of $N$. Find all integers $m \geq n \geq 2$ satisfying $$ \frac{\sigma(m)-1}{m-1}=\frac{\sigma(n)-1}{n-1}=\frac{\sigma(m n)-1}{m n-1} . $$

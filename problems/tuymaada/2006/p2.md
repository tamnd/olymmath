We call a sequence of integers a *Fibonacci-type sequence* if it is infinite in both ways and $a_{n}=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$ for any $n\in\mathbb{Z}$ . How many *Fibonacci-type sequences* can we find, with the property that in these sequences there are two consecutive terms, strictly positive, and less or equal than $N$ ? (two sequences are considered to be the same if they differ only by shifting of indices)

*Proposed by I. Pevzner*

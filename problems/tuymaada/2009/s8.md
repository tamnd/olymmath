Determine the maximum number $ h$ satisfying the following condition: for every $ a\in [0,h]$ and every polynomial $ P(x)$ of degree 99 such that $ P(0)=P(1)=0$ , there exist $ x_1,x_2\in [0,1]$ such that $ P(x_1)=P(x_2)$ and $ x_2-x_1=a$ .

*Proposed by F. Petrov, D. Rostovsky, A. Khrabrov*

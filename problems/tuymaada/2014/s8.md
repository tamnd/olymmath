Let positive integers $a,\ b,\ c$ be pairwise coprime. Denote by $g(a, b, c)$ the maximum integer not representable in the form $xa+yb+zc$ with positive integral $x,\ y,\ z$ . Prove that
$$ g(a, b, c)\ge \sqrt{2abc}$$

*(M. Ivanov)*

<details><summary>Remarks (containing spoilers!)</summary>1. It can be proven that $g(a,b,c)\ge \sqrt{3abc}$ .
2. The constant $3$ is the best possible, as proved by the equation $g(3,3k+1,3k+2)=9k+5$ .</details>

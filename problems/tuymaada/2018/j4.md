Prove that for every odd positive integer $d > 1$ and every positive integer $m$ the sequence $a_n = 2^{2^n} + d$ contains two terms $a_k$ and $a_\ell$ ($k \neq \ell$) such that their greatest common divisor is greater than $m$.

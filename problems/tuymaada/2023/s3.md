Prove for integral $n \ge 2$ the inequality

$$\frac{\sqrt[3]{\frac{1}{n+1}} + \sqrt[3]{\frac{2}{n+1}} + \dots + \sqrt[3]{\frac{n}{n+1}}}{n} \le \frac{\sqrt[3]{\frac{1}{n}} + \sqrt[3]{\frac{2}{n}} + \dots + \sqrt[3]{\frac{n-1}{n}}}{n-1}.$$

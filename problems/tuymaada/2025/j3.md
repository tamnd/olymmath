In a zoo shop, four aviaries arranged in a circle contain $222$ parrots each. Sometimes, a zoologist takes one parrot from an aviary and lets it go; together with it, she lets go either one parrot from the opposite aviary, or two parrots from the aviary on the left, or three parrots from the aviary on the right. At some moment, only one aviary still contains parrots. What is the least possible number of the remaining parrots?

All sides of a triangle $ABC$ are pairwise different. Its angle bisectors $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $CC_1$ meet at point $I$. The incircle of the triangle $ABC$ is tangent to the side $BC$ at $A_2$. The circle $\omega_a$ contains $A_1$, $A_2$, and the midpoint of $AI$. The circles $\omega_b$ and $\omega_c$ are defined similarly. Prove that the centres of $\omega_a$, $\omega_b$, $\omega_c$ are collinear.

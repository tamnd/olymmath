Circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ pass through a point $A$ and touch a line $\ell$ at $B_1$ and $B_2$, respectively. A variable line $m$ passes through $A$ and meets $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ again at (variable) points $P_1$ and $P_2$, respectively. The rays $P_1B_1$ and $P_2B_2$ intersect at a point $P$. Prove that the tangent to the circumcircle of $PP_1P_2$ at $P$ passes through a point independent of the choice of $m$.

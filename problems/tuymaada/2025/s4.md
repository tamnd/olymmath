Positive numbers $a_1, \dots, a_n$ and real numbers $b_1, \dots, b_n, c, d$ satisfy

$$[a_1x + b_1] + \dots + [a_nx + b_n] = [cx + d]$$

for all real $x$. Prove that $a_1, \dots, a_n$ cannot be all distinct.

Each of $2025$ persons arranged in a circle is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. Each of these $2025$ persons said: *There are exactly three knights among my two left neighbours, my two right neighbours and me.* What is the maximum possible number of knights among these persons?

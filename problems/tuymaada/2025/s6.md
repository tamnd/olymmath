In a sequence $(x_n)$, the number $x_1$ is positive and rational, and

$$x_{n+1} = \frac{\{nx_n\}}{n} \quad \text{for} \quad n \ge 1$$

($\{a\}$ denotes the fractional part of $a$). Prove that this sequence contains only finitely many non-zero terms and their sum is an integer.

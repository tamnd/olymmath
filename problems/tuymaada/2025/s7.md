Each vertex of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is reflected across the two sides not containing it (vertex $A$ is reflected across $BC$ and across $CD$, and so on). Prove that some $4$ of $8$ points thus obtained are vertices of a cyclic or circumscribed quadrilateral.

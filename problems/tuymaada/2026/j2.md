In an acute triangle $ABC$, the bisector $BL$ is drawn. The points $P$ and $Q$ are projections of $L$ onto $AB$ and $BC$, respectively. The points $X$ and $Y$ are projections of $A$ and $C$, respectively, onto $BL$. Prove that the lines $PY$ and $QX$ meet on the side $AC$.

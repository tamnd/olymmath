There are $n$ cities in Eastasia, some of them are connected by direct two-way flights. It is possible to fly from any city to any other with no more than one transfer. Let $d_k$ be the number of cities connected with $k$-th city by direct flights. Prove that

$$d_1^2 + d_2^2 + \dots + d_n^2 \ge n(n-1).$$

Points $C_1$ and $B_1$ lie on the legs $AB$ and $AC$ respectively of a right triangle $ABC$ so that the midpoint of $B_1C_1$ is equidistant from $B$ and $C$. The lines $BB_1$ and $CC_1$ intersect at $P$ and meet the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $B_2$, $C_2$ respectively. Point $Q$ on the circumcircle is such that $AQ \perp PQ$. The half-line $QP$ meets the segment $BC$ at $R$. Prove that the circle passing through $B_2$, $C_2$, and $R$, contains the midpoint of $BC$.

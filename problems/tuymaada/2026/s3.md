Positive numbers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ satisfy the inequalities $a^2 + b^2 \le 1$ and $(c^2 + d^2)^2 + 2c^2d^2 \le 1$. Prove that

$$a^2c^2 + b^2d^2 + \frac{2}{3}(ad + bc)^2 \le 1.$$

The Sultan informed two sages that tomorrow he would place $k + 4$ glasses along the edge of a round table, four of them with wine and the rest with poison. It is impossible to distinguish one from the other by sight. The sages will have to drink four glasses taking turns (first — second — first — second). The glasses will be filled in the presence of the first sage, thus he will know where the poison is. However, after that he will not be permitted to give any information to the second sage; that is, the second sage will only know what glasses the first one drank. The sages can coordinate their actions today. What is the maximum $k$ such that the sages can certainly drink the four glasses with wine?

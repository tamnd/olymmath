We call an infinite sequence of real numbers *progressive*, if for each positive integer $n$ there is a positive integer $k$ such that $a_n + a_k = a_{n+k}$. Prove that every numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_m$ (in this order) are $m$ initial terms of some progressive sequence.

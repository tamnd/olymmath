We call a positive number *unary*, if its fractional part is $\frac{1}{m}$ for some integer $m$. Let $q$ be a rational number. Prove that if $q^k$ is unary for every positive integer $k$ then $q = \frac{1}{n}$ for some positive integer $n$.

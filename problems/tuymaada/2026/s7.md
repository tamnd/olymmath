Some flies sit on the faces of a cube (in inner points). Two spiders, Spike and Dyke, play the following game. The spiders take turn, Spike starts the game. In a move, a spider chooses a vertex of the cube and eats any number of flies (at least one in total) from the faces adjacent to this vertex. The player that cannot move loses. In the beginning the numbers of flies on the faces are 20, 21, 22, 25, 27, 29, as shown in the figure. Which spider has a winning strategy?

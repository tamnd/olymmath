$ \sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+...+\sqrt{2}}}}+\sqrt{6+\sqrt{6+\sqrt{6+...+\sqrt{6}}}}<5$

where there are $ n$ twos and $ n$ sixs on the left hand side.

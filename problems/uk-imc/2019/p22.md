Four of the following six-digit integers are always divisible by 7, regardless of the values of the digits $P$ and $Q$.

Which of the following is not necessarily a multiple of 7?

A ‘$PQQPQQ$’ B ‘$PQPQPQ$’ C ‘$QPQPQP$’ D ‘$PPPPPP$’ E ‘$PPPQQQ$’

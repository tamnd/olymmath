A triangle with all three sides the same length, but three different interior angles. A quadrilateral with all four sides the same length, but four different interior angles. A pentagon with all five sides the same length, but five different interior angles. A only the pentagon B only the quadrilateral C the quadrilateral and the pentagon D all three E none of them

Dean wishes to place the positive integers $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 9$ in the cells of a $3 \times 3$ square grid so that:

(i) there is exactly one number in each cell;

(ii) the product of the numbers in each row is a multiple of four;

(iii) the product of the numbers in each column is a multiple of four. Is Dean's task possible? Prove that your answer is correct.

A train leaves K for L at 09:30 while another train leaves L for K at 10:00. The first train arrives in L 40 minutes after the trains pass each other. The second train arrives in K 1 hour and 40 minutes after the trains pass.

Each train travels at a constant speed.

At what time did the trains pass each other?

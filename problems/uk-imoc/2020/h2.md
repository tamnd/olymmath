In the UK, 1p, 2p and 5p coins have thicknesses of 1.6 mm, 2.05 mm and 1.75 mm respectively.

Using only 1p and 5p coins, Joe builds the shortest (non-empty) stack he can whose height in millimetres is equal to its value in pence. Penny does the same but using only 2p and 5p coins.

Whose stack is more valuable?

In the four-digit number 4753, three two-digit numbers are formed by successive pairs of digits $(47, 75, 53)$. Exactly two of these two-digit numbers are prime. Find all four-digit numbers in which all four digits are prime, and all three two-digit numbers formed by successive digits are prime.

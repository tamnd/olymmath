Mike is doing a one-hour cycling challenge. He has a computer which predicts how far he will cycle in the rest of the hour based on his average speed so far.

After cycling 1 km in $t$ minutes, he checks the distance the computer predicts he will cycle in the remaining time and it shows $d$ km.

In the next 36 minutes, he cycles 15 km. He checks the computer again and finds it still predicts he will cycle $d$ km in the remaining time.

Find the distance shown on the computer each time he looks.

A keypad is a 3 by 3 grid with the digits 1 to 3 in order on the top row, 4 to 6 in order on the second row and 7 to 9 in order on the third row.

Seb has the keypad upside-down (i.e rotated through $180^\circ$) and types his three-digit PIN as if the keypad were the right way up. The number he types in turns out to be $k$ times his original PIN where $k$ is a positive integer. Find all possible PINs and prove there are no others.

There are two types of tile to tile a floor. One is a square tile 5 by 5 and the other is a rectangular tile 4 by 9. The floor is 140 long by 80 wide. It is to be completely tiled with no gaps, none of them overlapping and none going outside the edges of the floor using the minimum number of tiles possible. Demonstrate how to do this and prove that it cannot be done with fewer tiles.

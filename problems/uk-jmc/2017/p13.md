Consider the following three statements.

(i) Doubling a positive number always makes it larger.

(ii) Squaring a positive number always makes it larger.

(iii) Taking the positive square root of a positive number always makes it smaller. Which statements are true?

A All three B None C Only (i) D (i) and (ii) E (ii) and (iii)

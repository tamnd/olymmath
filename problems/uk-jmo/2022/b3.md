You start with a regular pentagon $ABCDE$. Then you draw two circles: one with centre $A$ and radius $AB$, and the other with centre $B$ and radius $BA$. Let the point inside the pentagon at which these two circles intersect be $X$.

What is the size of $\angle DEX$?

The positive integers from 1 to $n (n \ge 2)$ inclusive are to be spaced equally around the circumference of a circle so that:

(a) no two even numbers are adjacent;

(b) no two odd numbers are adjacent; What is the smallest value of $n$ for which the above is possible?

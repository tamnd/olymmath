Blackbeard and Brownbeard each have a pile of gold coins. One night, Blackbeard takes 3 gold coins from Brownbeard’s pile and adds them to his pile, and Brownbeard responds by taking half of the coins in Blackbeard’s pile and adding them to his pile. The next night, Brownbeard takes 3 coins from Blackbeard’s pile and adds them to his pile, and Blackbeard responds by taking half of the coins in Brownbeard’s pile and adding them to his pile. Both pirates now have the same number of gold coins as they had at the beginning.

Prove that the total number of gold coins is divisible by 3.

Let $S = \{a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n\}$ where the $a_i$ are different positive integers. The sum of the elements of each non-empty proper subset of $S$ is not divisible by $n$. Show that the sum of all elements of $S$ is divisible by $n$. Note that a proper subset of $S$ consists of some, but not all, of the elements of $S$.

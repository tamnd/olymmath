Does there exist a positive integer $N$ which is a power of 2, and a different positive integer $M$ obtained from $N$ by permuting its digits (in the usual base 10 representation), such that $M$ is also a power of 2? Note that we do not allow the base 10 representation of a positive integer to begin with 0.

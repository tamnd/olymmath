Find all functions $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ that satisfy the inequality $$ f(y)-\left(\frac{z-y}{z-x} f(x)+\frac{y-x}{z-x} f(z)\right) \leq f\left(\frac{x+z}{2}\right)-\frac{f(x)+f(z)}{2} $$ for all real numbers $x<y<z$.

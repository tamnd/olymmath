Let $\triangle A B C$ be a non-equilateral, acute triangle with $\angle A=60^{\circ}$, and let $O$ and $H$ denote the circumcenter and orthocenter of $\triangle A B C$, respectively. (a) Prove that line $O H$ intersects both segments $A B$ and $A C$ at two points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. (b) Denote by $s$ and $t$ the respective areas of triangle $A P Q$ and quadrilateral $B P Q C$. Determine the range of possible values for $s / t$.

Let $\omega$ be the incircle of a fixed equilateral triangle $A B C$. Let $\ell$ be a variable line that is tangent to $\omega$ and meets the interior of segments $B C$ and $C A$ at points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. A point $R$ is chosen such that $P R=P A$ and $Q R=Q B$. Find all possible locations of the point $R$, over all choices of $\ell$.

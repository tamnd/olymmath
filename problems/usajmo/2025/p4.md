Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $a_0 \geq a_1 \geq \dots \geq a_n \geq 0$ be integers. Prove that

$$\sum_{i = 0}^{n}i\binom{a_{i}}{2}\leq \frac{1}{2}\binom{a_{0} + a_{1} + \cdots + a_{n}}{2}.$$

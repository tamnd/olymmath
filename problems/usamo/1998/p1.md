Suppose that the set $\{1,2, \ldots, 1998\}$ has been partitioned into disjoint pairs $\left\{a_{i}, b_{i}\right\}$ $(1 \leq i \leq 999)$ so that for all $i,\left|a_{i}-b_{i}\right|$ equals 1 or 6 . Prove that the sum $$ \left|a_{1}-b_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}-b_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|a_{999}-b_{999}\right| $$ ends in the digit 9.

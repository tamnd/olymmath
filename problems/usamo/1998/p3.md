Let $a_0,a_1,\cdots ,a_n$ be numbers from the interval $(0,\pi/2)$ such that $$ \tan (a_0-\frac{\pi}{4})+ \tan (a_1-\frac{\pi}{4})+\cdots +\tan (a_n-\frac{\pi}{4})\geq n-1. $$ Prove that $$ \tan a_0\tan a_1 \cdots \tan a_n\geq n^{n+1}. $$

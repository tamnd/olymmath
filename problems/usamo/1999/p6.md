Let $F$ be a point on the (internal) angle bisector of $\angle DAC$ such that $EF \perp CD$ . Let the circumscribed circle of triangle $ACF$ meet line $CD$ at $C$ and $G$ . Prove that the triangle $AFG$ is isosceles.

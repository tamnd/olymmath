Let $A B C$ be a triangle such that $$ \left(\cot \frac{A}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(2 \cot \frac{B}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(3 \cot \frac{C}{2}\right)^{2}=\left(\frac{6 s}{7 r}\right)^{2} $$ where $s$ and $r$ denote its semiperimeter and its inradius, respectively. Prove that triangle $A B C$ is similar to a triangle $T$ whose side lengths are all positive integers with no common divisors and determine these integers.

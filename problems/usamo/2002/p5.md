Show that we can link any two integers m, n greater than 2 by a chain of positive integers m = $a_1$, $a_2$, ... , $a_{k+1}$ = n, so that the product of any two consecutive members of the chain is divisible by their sum. [For example, 7, 42, 21, 28, 70, 30, 6, 3 links 7 and 3.]

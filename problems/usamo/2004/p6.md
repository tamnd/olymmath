A circle $\omega$ is inscribed in a quadrilateral $A B C D$. Let $I$ be the center of $\omega$. Suppose that $$ (A I+D I)^{2}+(B I+C I)^{2}=(A B+C D)^{2} $$ Prove that $A B C D$ is an isosceles trapezoid.

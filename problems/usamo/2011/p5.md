Let $P$ be a point inside convex quadrilateral $A B C D$. Points $Q_{1}$ and $Q_{2}$ are located within $A B C D$ such that $$ \begin{array}{ll} \angle Q_{1} B C=\angle A B P, & \angle Q_{1} C B=\angle D C P, \\ \angle Q_{2} A D=\angle B A P, & \angle Q_{2} D A=\angle C D P . \end{array} $$ Prove that $\overline{Q_{1} Q_{2}} \| \overline{A B}$ if and only if $\overline{Q_{1} Q_{2}} \| \overline{C D}$.

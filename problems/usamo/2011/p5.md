Let $P$ be a given point inside quadrilateral $ABCD$ . Points $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ are located within $ABCD$ such that
$$\angle Q_1BC=\angle ABP, \angle Q_1CB=\angle DCP, \angle Q_2AD=\angle BAP, \angle Q_2DA=\angle CDP.$$ Prove that $\overline{Q_1Q_2}\parallel\overline{AB}$ if and only if $\overline{Q_1Q_2}\parallel\overline{CD}$ .

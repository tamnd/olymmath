Let $A$ be a set with $|A|=225$, meaning that $A$ has 225 elements. Suppose further that there are eleven subsets $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{11}$ of $A$ such that $\left|A_{i}\right|=45$ for $1 \leq i \leq 11$ and $\left|A_{i} \cap A_{j}\right|=9$ for $1 \leq i<j \leq 11$. Prove that $\left|A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{11}\right| \geq 165$, and give an example for which equality holds.

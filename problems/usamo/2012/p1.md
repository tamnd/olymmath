Find all integers $n \geq 3$ such that among any $n$ positive real numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$, $a_{n}$ with $$ \max \left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right) \leq n \cdot \min \left(a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}\right), $$ there exist three that are the side lengths of an acute triangle.

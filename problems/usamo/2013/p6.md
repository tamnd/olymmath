Let $A B C$ be a triangle. Find all points $P$ on segment $B C$ satisfying the following property: If $X$ and $Y$ are the intersections of line $P A$ with the common external tangent lines of the circumcircles of triangles $P A B$ and $P A C$, then $$ \left(\frac{P A}{X Y}\right)^{2}+\frac{P B \cdot P C}{A B \cdot A C}=1 $$

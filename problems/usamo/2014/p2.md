Find all $f: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ such that $$ x f(2 f(y)-x)+y^{2} f(2 x-f(y))=\frac{f(x)^{2}}{x}+f(y f(y)) $$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $x \neq 0$.

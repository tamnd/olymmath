Find all real numbers $c>0$ such that there exists a labeling of the lattice points in $\mathbb{Z}^{2}$ with positive integers for which: - only finitely many distinct labels occur, and - for each label $i$, the distance between any two points labeled $i$ is at least $c^{i}$.

Find all polynomials $P$ with real coefficients such that $$ \frac{P(x)}{y z}+\frac{P(y)}{z x}+\frac{P(z)}{x y}=P(x-y)+P(y-z)+P(z-x) $$ for all nonzero real numbers $x, y, z$ obeying $2 x y z=x+y+z$.

Let $n \geq 4$ be an integer. Find all positive real solutions to the following system of $2 n$ equations: $$ \begin{aligned} a_{1} & =\frac{1}{a_{2 n}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}, & a_{2} & =a_{1}+a_{3}, \\ a_{3} & =\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{4}}, & a_{4} & =a_{3}+a_{5}, \\ a_{5} & =\frac{1}{a_{4}}+\frac{1}{a_{6}}, & a_{6} & =a_{5}+a_{7}, \\ & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_{2 n-1} & =\frac{1}{a_{2 n-2}}+\frac{1}{a_{2 n}}, & a_{2 n} & =a_{2 n-1}+a_{1} . \end{aligned} $$

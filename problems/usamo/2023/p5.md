Let $n \geq 3$ be an integer. We say that an arrangement of the numbers $1,2, \ldots, n^{2}$ in an $n \times n$ table is row-valid if the numbers in each row can be permuted to form an arithmetic progression, and column-valid if the numbers in each column can be permuted to form an arithmetic progression. For what values of $n$ is it possible to transform any row-valid arrangement into a column-valid arrangement by permuting the numbers in each row?

A convex polygon $ A_1,A_2,\cdots ,A_n$ is inscribed in a circle with center $ O$ and radius $ R$ so that $ O$ lies inside the polygon. Let the inradii of the triangles $ A_1A_2A_3, A_1A_3A_4, \cdots , A_1A_{n - 1}A_n$ be denoted by $ r_1,r_2,\cdots ,r_{n - 2}$ . Prove that $ r_1 + r_2 + ... + r_{n - 2}\leq R(n\cos \frac {\pi}{n} - n + 2)$ .

Let be given four positive real numbers $ a$ , $ b$ , $ A$ , $ B$ . Consider a sequence of real numbers $ x_1$ , $ x_2$ , $ x_3$ , $ \ldots$ is given by $ x_1 = a$ , $ x_2 = b$ and $ x_{n + 1} = A\sqrt [3]{x_n^2} + B\sqrt [3]{x_{n - 1}^2}$ ( $ n = 2, 3, 4, \ldots$ ). Prove that there exist limit $ \lim_{n\to + \propto}x_n$ and find this limit.

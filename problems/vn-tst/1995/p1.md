Let be given a triangle $ ABC$ with $ BC = a$ , $ CA = b$ , $ AB = c$ . Six distinct points $ A_1$ , $ A_2$ , $ B_1$ , $ B_2$ , $ C_1$ , $ C_2$ not coinciding with $ A$ , $ B$ , $ C$ are chosen so that $ A_1$ , $ A_2$ lie on line $ BC$ ; $ B_1$ , $ B_2$ lie on $ CA$ and $ C_1$ , $ C_2$ lie on $ AB$ . Let $ \alpha$ , $ \beta$ , $ \gamma$ three real numbers satisfy $ \overrightarrow{A_1A_2} = \frac {\alpha}{a}\overrightarrow{BC}$ , $ \overrightarrow{B_1B_2} = \frac {\beta}{b}\overrightarrow{CA}$ , $ \overrightarrow{C_1C_2} = \frac {\gamma}{c}\overrightarrow{AB}$ . Let $ d_A$ , $ d_B$ , $ d_C$ be respectively the radical axes of the circumcircles of the pairs of triangles $ AB_1C_1$ and $ AB_2C_2$ ; $ BC_1A_1$ and $ BC_2A_2$ ; $ CA_1B_1$ and $ CA_2B_2$ . Prove that $ d_A$ , $ d_B$ and $ d_C$ are concurrent if and only if $ \alpha a + \beta b + \gamma c \neq 0$ .

For any nonnegative integer $ n$ , let $ f(n)$ be the greatest integer such that $ 2^{f(n)} | n + 1$ . A pair $ (n, p)$ of nonnegative integers is called nice if $ 2^{f(n)} > p$ . Find all triples $ (n, p, q)$ of nonnegative integers such that the pairs $ (n, p)$ , $ (p, q)$ and $ (n + p + q, n)$ are all nice.

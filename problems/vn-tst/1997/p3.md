Find the greatest real number $ \alpha$ for which there exists a sequence of infinitive integers $ (a_n)$ , ( $ n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$ ) satisfying the following conditions:
1) $ a_n > 1997n$ for every $ n \in\mathbb{N}^{*}$ ;
2) For every $ n\ge 2$ , $ U_n\ge a^{\alpha}_n$ , where $ U_n = \gcd\{a_i + a_k | i + k = n\}$ .

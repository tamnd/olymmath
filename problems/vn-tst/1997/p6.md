Let $ n$ , $ k$ , $ p$ be positive integers with $ 2 \le k \le \frac {n}{p + 1}$ . Let $ n$ distinct points on a circle be given. These points are colored blue and red so that exactly $ k$ points are blue and, on each arc determined by two consecutive blue points in clockwise direction, there are at least $ p$ red points. How many such colorings are there?

Let an integer $ n > 3$ . Denote the set $ T=\{1,2, \ldots,n\}.$ A subset S of T is called *wanting set* if S has the property: There exists a positive integer $ c$ which is not greater than $ \frac {n}{2}$ such that $ |s_1 - s_2|\ne c$ for every pairs of arbitrary elements $ s_1,s_2\in S$ . How many does a *wanting set* have at most are there ?

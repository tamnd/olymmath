There are $ 6n + 4$ mathematicians participating in a conference which includes $ 2n + 1$ meetings. Each meeting has one round table that suits for $ 4$ people and $ n$ round tables that each table suits for $ 6$ people. We have known that two arbitrary people sit next to or have opposite places doesn't exceed one time.
1. Determine whether or not there is the case $ n = 1$ .
2. Determine whether or not there is the case $ n > 1$ .

Show that sin x/sin X = sin y/sin Y = sin z/sin Z and that x + y = $180^o$ iff X + Y = $180^o$. (2) ABCD is a tetrahedron with ∠BAC = ∠CAD =∠DAB = $90^o$. Points A and B are fixed. C and D are variable. Show that ∠CBD + ∠ABD + ∠ABC is constant. Find the locus of the center of the insphere of ABCD.

Given a regular 103-sided polygon. 79 vertices are colored red and the remaining vertices are colored blue. Let $A$ be the number of pairs of adjacent red vertices and $B$ be the number of pairs of adjacent blue vertices.
a) Find all possible values of pair $(A,B).$ b) Determine the number of pairwise non-similar colorings of the polygon satisfying $B=14.$ 2 colorings are called similar if they can be obtained from each other by rotating the circumcircle of the polygon.

If $a,b,c$ are nonnegative real numbers, then
\[{ 3(a^2+b^2+c^2) \geq (a+b+c)(\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{bc}+\sqrt{ca})+(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2 \geq (a+b+c)^2.}\]

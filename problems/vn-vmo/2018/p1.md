The sequence $(x_n)$ is defined as follows: $$ x_1=2, x_{n+1}=\sqrt{x_n+8}-\sqrt{x_n+3} $$ for all $n\geq 1$ .
a. Prove that $(x_n)$ has a finite limit and find that limit.
b. For every $n\geq 1$ , prove that $$ n\leq x_1+x_2+\dots +x_n\leq n+1. $$

Let $$ a_n = \frac{1\cdot3\cdot5\cdot\cdots\cdot(2n-1)}{2\cdot4\cdot6\cdot\cdots\cdot2n}. $$ (a) Prove that $\lim_{n\to \infty}a_n$ exists.
(b) Show that $$ a_n = \frac{\left(1-\frac1{2^2}\right)\left(1-\frac1{4^2}\right)\left(1-\frac1{6^2}\right)\cdots\left(1-\frac{1}{(2n)^2}\right)}{(2n+1)a_n}. $$ (c) Find $\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n$ and justify your answer

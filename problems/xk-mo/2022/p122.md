Let be given $n$ positive integer. Lets write with $a_n$ the number of positive integer pairs $(x,y)$ such that $x+y$ is even and $1\leq x\leq y\leq n$ . Lets write with $b_n$ the number of positive integer pairs $(x,y)$ such that $x+y\leq n+1$ and $1\leq x\leq y\leq n$ . Prove that $a_n=b_n$ .

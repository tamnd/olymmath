Determine all polynomials $a(x)$ , $b(x)$ , $c(x)$ , $d(x)$ with real coefficients satisfying the simultaneous equations
\begin{align*}
b(x) c(x) + a(x) d(x) & = 0
a(x) c(x) + (1 - x^2) b(x) d(x) & = x + 1.
\end{align*}

Let $ A = \{(a_1,\dots,a_8)|a_i\in\mathbb{N}$ , $ 1\leq a_i\leq i + 1$ for each $ i = 1,2\dots,8\}$ .A subset $ X\subset A$ is called sparse if for each two distinct elements $ (a_1,\dots,a_8)$ , $ (b_1,\dots,b_8)\in X$ ,there exist at least three indices $ i$ ,such that $ a_i\neq b_i$ .
Find the maximal possible number of elements in a sparse subset of set $ A$ .

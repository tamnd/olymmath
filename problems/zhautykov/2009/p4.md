On the plane, a Cartesian coordinate system is chosen. Given points $ A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4$ on the parabola $ y = x^2$ , and points $ B_1,B_2,B_3,B_4$ on the parabola $ y = 2009x^2$ . Points $ A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4$ are concyclic, and points $ A_i$ and $ B_i$ have equal abscissas for each $ i = 1,2,3,4$ .
Prove that points $ B_1,B_2,B_3,B_4$ are also concyclic.

Points $M, N, K$ lie on the sides $B C, C A, A B$ respectively, of a triangle $A B C$, and are different from its vertices. The triangle $M N K$ is called beautiful if the triangles $M N K$ and $A B C$ are similar (with the vertices respectively in this order). Show that if in the triangle $A B C$ there are two beautiful triangles with a common vertex, then $\triangle A B C$ is right-angled.

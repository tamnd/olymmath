In an alphabet of $n$ letters, a syllable is any ordered pair of two (not necessarily distinct) letters. Some syllables are considered indecent. A word is any sequence, finite or infinite, of letters, that does not contain indecent syllables. Find the least possible number of indecent syllables for which infinite words do not exist.

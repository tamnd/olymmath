# Coverage

This report is made by `om coverage`. The register gives the expected positions of each competition. C0 is the share of expected positions with a problem record. C1 is the share with a canonical statement of class H1 or H2. C2 is the share with an H1 statement. A candidate is an expected position with no record and with at least one seed row from a wave 0 dataset. Spec 14 gives the definitions.

The gap register (`registers/gaps.yaml`) gives the reason of an expected position with no record: link-only by rule, blocked by robots.txt, or no allowed source. A position that the register does not name is open. A position with a seed candidate is open, also when the register says it has no allowed source. The allowed positions are the expected positions less the positions that are link-only, blocked or with no allowed source. "C0 allowed" is the share of the allowed positions with a problem record.

# Match rate

This report is made by `om matchrate` (spec 05.4). A seed row is matched when its competition, year and position give an appearance that has a record. Else it is compared with the 19305 problem records with a statement by E, the cosine of the TF-IDF weights of their words and TeX, and a row whose nearest record has E of at least 0.7 is matched. A row with E from 0.5 to 0.7 is near, and a person must look at it to tell. The other rows can be new problems. The acceptance of milestone 6 asks for less than 1% new rows.
